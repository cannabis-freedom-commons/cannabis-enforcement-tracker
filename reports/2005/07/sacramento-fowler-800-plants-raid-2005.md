Sacramento County sheriff's detectives served state search warrants at four
locations on 7 July 2005, including the Alternative Specialties marijuana
distribution centre on Folsom Boulevard, after owner Louis Wayne Fowler, 51,
of Rio Linda was denied a county business licence. [@1] Finding evidence of
federal drug violations, they called in the DEA, which served two federal
warrants and found two indoor growing operations holding about 800 plants
worth some $500,000. [@1,2]

Fowler was arrested carrying a loaded handgun and a loaded assault pistol
and was booked into Sacramento County Jail on state felon-in-possession
charges. [@1] He was then charged federally with manufacturing a controlled
substance, possessing firearms in furtherance of drug trafficking, and being
a felon in possession of firearms, in a case prosecuted by the U.S.
Attorney's Office in Sacramento. [@1]

The raid capped a four-month investigation by the sheriff's office and the
county district attorney's office into the unlicensed business. [@1] By May
2007 Fowler was free pending trial, with a status hearing scheduled for 1
June before Judge Edward Garcia in Sacramento federal court. [@2]
