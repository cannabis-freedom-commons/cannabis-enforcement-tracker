Police in Wa in the Upper West Region arrested four people in possession of about seven tonnes of dry leaves suspected to be cannabis. [@1] Officers stopped a DAF truck, registration UW 3706 C, at a police barrier on the Wa-Kumasi road at about 5 pm on Saturday; the truck was heading toward Burkina Faso through Babile. [@1] The suspected cannabis was packed in 95 maxi bags concealed with 300 bags of cement. [@1]

Those held were the driver, his two mates and 38-year-old Alhassan Salifu, believed to own the consignment; police said the suspects were in custody assisting investigations. [@1] Salifu allegedly offered officers 10 million cedis to let the truck go, which they refused. [@1]
