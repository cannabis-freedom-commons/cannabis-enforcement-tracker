Sarnia police and the Ontario Provincial Police drug enforcement unit shut down
a grow operation in an industrial building in the city's south end on Tuesday,
finding marijuana with a street value of $3.4 million. [@1] Officers found
about 2,800 plants, from seedlings to full-grown plants a metre tall, along
with 13.6 kg of dried marijuana ready for sale. [@1] They also recovered
lights, irrigation equipment and air exchangers valued at $75,000 plus an
undisclosed amount of cash, in what police called one of the largest drug finds
in Sarnia police history. [@1]

The weeks-long investigation began with a Crime Stoppers tip. [@1] Police
would not name the building's owners or its exact location, saying only that
it was not derelict and stood on the fringe of Sarnia's Chemical Valley
industrial area. [@1] Douglas Bingham, 35, and Sarah Shipley, 23, both of
Sarnia, were charged with production of a controlled substance for the purpose
of trafficking and were due in Sarnia court the next day. [@1]

## Related searches

In connection with the bust, officers also executed search warrants that
Tuesday at two residences just outside Sarnia, finding a small amount of drugs
and other evidence tied to the growing operation. [@1] No arrests were made at
the houses, and police would not elaborate. [@1] Police said there was no
indication the operation had cross-border connections. [@1]
