Iraqi national Mattar bin Hussein bin Bakhit al-Khazaali was beheaded after being
convicted of smuggling hashish into Saudi Arabia.[@1] The execution took place in
the northern town of Arar, close to the Iraqi border, according to the Interior
Ministry statement.[@1]

The beheading was carried out on Saturday 1 January 2005 and announced alongside the
execution of Pakistani national Mohammed Amin Abdullah Jan, who was beheaded in the
Red Sea port city of Jeddah for smuggling heroin.[@1] The ministry statement said
al-Khazaali was the second Iraqi executed in Arar in ten days, after Qaied bin Kamal
al-Zayadi was beheaded there on 22 December for hashish.[@1] Press tallies in the
announcement put beheadings at 35 in 2004 against 52 in 2003, most for drug
smuggling.[@1]
