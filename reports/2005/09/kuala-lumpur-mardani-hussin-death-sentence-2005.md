The Kuala Lumpur High Court sentenced Mardani Hussin, a 29-year-old Indonesian labourer from Aceh, to death by hanging for trafficking cannabis. [@1] Hussin had reportedly entered Malaysia without a valid permit in April 2002 and committed the offence six months later. [@1]

He was charged with trafficking 927 grams of cannabis in the capital on 22 October 2002. [@1] He was riding a motorcycle when police stopped him, searched him and seized a parcel containing the drug. [@1]

Sentencing him, High Court judge Abdull Hamid Embong told him many of his countrymen came to Malaysia to earn a proper living and were surely ashamed of his act. [@1]
