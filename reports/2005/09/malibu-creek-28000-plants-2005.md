Thirty million dollars worth of marijuana was removed from Malibu Creek State Park on
Tuesday 27 September 2005 by the Lost Hills Sheriff's Department. [@1] About 28,000 plants
spread along a half-mile stretch across a ridgeline had been spotted from a helicopter the
previous week, according to Sergeant Ken Davidson. [@1] The plants had been growing in a
remote part of the park for four to five months and stood four to six feet tall. [@1]

Deputies began hauling the plants out at 8 a.m. on Tuesday and completed the removal the
same day. [@1] No arrests were made in connection with the grow, the Sheriff's Department
said. [@1]

## Federal investigation

By February 2006 the investigation had passed to federal authorities, Davidson said, with
the Malibu/Lost Hills narcotics division having done the removal work. [@2] The follow-up
account put the haul's worth at $28 million and again described about 28,000 plants across
a half-mile stretch of the park. [@2]
