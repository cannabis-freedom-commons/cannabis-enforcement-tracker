Police arrested three men and seized more than two tons of hashish from an oil
truck in Karachi on Sunday, an official said. [@1] The 2,100 kilograms of hashish
were hidden in specially built chambers in the truck, Karachi police official
Sanaullah Abbasi said. [@1] The seizure was made at a roadblock set up following
a tip-off. [@1]

## Arrests

The truck's driver and two other men in the vehicle, all Pakistanis, were arrested
and were to be formally charged after an investigation, Abbasi said. [@1] A fourth
man in the truck escaped. [@1] Drug trafficking can carry the death penalty in
Pakistan. [@1]
