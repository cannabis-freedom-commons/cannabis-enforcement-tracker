On the afternoon of 27 May 2005 the RCMP stopped a vehicle on the Alaska
Highway south of Whitehorse, detained its two passengers and seized the
car.[@1] A search by the RCMP Police Dog Service Unit found 101.25 kilograms
(225 pounds) of marijuana hidden in a secret compartment inside a skidoo
deck, equal to roughly 33,750 to 55,625 joints taken off the streets.[@1]
The seizure was the largest in Yukon's history at the time, and the
Whitehorse Star later listed it among the year's top ten stories.[@1,2]

Kurtis Ray Croy, 33, and Kyle MacDonald-Wolochatiuk, 30, both of Squamish,
British Columbia, were arrested without incident and each charged with one
count of possession of marijuana for the purpose of trafficking.[@1] The
Star recorded the seizure as 101 kilograms of marijuana taken from a car
headed toward Whitehorse.[@2]
