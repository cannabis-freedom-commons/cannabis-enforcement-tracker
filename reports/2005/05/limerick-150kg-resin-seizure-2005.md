Police in Limerick seized 150 kilos of cannabis resin with an estimated market
value of over €1 million on 13 May 2005, according to a US Embassy Dublin
counternarcotics summary. [@1] The embassy put the local police's total
narcotics seizures for 2005 at more than €5 million. [@1]
