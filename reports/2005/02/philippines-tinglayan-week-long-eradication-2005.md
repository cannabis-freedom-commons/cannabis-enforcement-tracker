Cordillera police seized and torched marijuana plants valued by police at more than P270 million during week-long operations in the mountains of Tinglayan, Kalinga. [@1] The marijuana haul was presented as cutting the source of supply in the Cordillera region, which police said accounts for 85 percent of domestic supply. [@1]

Cordillera police director Chief Superintendent Jesus Verzosa said one raided farm in Sitio Kasablotan, Barangay Loccong, Tinglayan yielded about 5,000 marijuana plants valued at P1 million. [@1] Eight more marijuana plantations overrun in Tinglayan the previous Thursday held destroyed plants valued at P251 million, and marijuana leaves pounded into brick form worth P25 million were confiscated the same day. [@1] The Dangerous Drugs Board pegged brick-form marijuana at P25,000 per kilo and unprocessed leaves at P2,500 per kilo. [@1]
