Elements of the Regional Anti-Illegal Drugs Special Operations Task Group (RAIDSOTG),
Regional Mobile Group 1 and the Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency, working with
Santol municipal police, destroyed about 170,000 fully grown marijuana plants of the
so-called miracle variety near the tri-boundary of La Union, Benguet and Ilocos Sur over
a three-day operation that weekend. [@1] The force acted after task group commander
Chief Superintendent Claudio Caneso Cabreros received a tip about two marijuana farms in
the area and directed his deputy Senior Superintendent Vicente Tandiama Raval Jr. and
special operations chief Superintendent Rolando Bonifacio Diaz to run the
operation. [@1]

The teams uprooted 35,000 fully grown plants on a 4,000-square-metre plot and 135,000
more on an 8,500-square-metre plot at the same place, 12,500 square metres (1.25
hectares) in all. [@1] The Dangerous Drugs Board placed the value of the destroyed
plants at 34 million pesos. [@1] Bamboo tubes crisscrossed the plantation to irrigate
the plants. [@1] Most of the plants were burned at the site because they were hard to
carry out, with the burning witnessed by village officials and documented by the raiding
team. [@1]
