A joint operation by Ghana's Narcotics Control Board and the police destroyed three acres of cannabis in Akatsi in October 2005 and took two Ghanaians and two Jamaicans into custody. [@1] The same cable reports a police campaign against cannabis farms that drove a nearly 18-fold rise in seizures in 2005, the year with the most drug-trafficking arrests on record. [@1] Cannabis, known locally as Indian hemp, is widely cultivated on rural farmland and usually harvested in September and October, when enforcement teams step up surveillance. [@1]
