The Federal Supreme Court in Abu Dhabi upheld the commutation of hashish
trafficker Mirza Khan's death sentence to ten years in prison, a Dh50,000
fine and deportation to follow. [@1,2] Khan had been caught by police in
Sharjah with three kilograms of hashish in his possession while he was
searching for a buyer, and prosecutors charged him with importing hashish
for trafficking, seeking punishment under Islamic Shariah law. [@1,2] The
Sharjah court of first instance unanimously sentenced him to death, but the
Court of Appeal revoked that verdict, reducing the charge to possession and
consumption and the punishment to ten years, and the Supreme Court left the
reduced punishment standing. [@1,2]
