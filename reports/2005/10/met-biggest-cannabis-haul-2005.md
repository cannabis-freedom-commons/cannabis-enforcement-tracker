On the morning of Thursday 6 October 2005, customs officers and the Metropolitan Police
found more than five tonnes of cannabis hidden inside a cargo of furniture and artefacts
imported from Mexico at a port on England's east coast. [@1] The haul, with an estimated
street value of £12 million, was described as the biggest cannabis seizure the force had
made. [@1,2]

Six men aged 26 to 52 and two women aged 31 and 42 were arrested at addresses across
London early that Thursday, and a 43-year-old man was later arrested in Essex, making
nine arrests in total. [@1,2] Police said the raids were part of a long-planned operation
against gangs suspected of supplying drugs in London. [@1]
