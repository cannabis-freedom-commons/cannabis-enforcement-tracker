Nine citizens of Tajikistan were detained in connection with the
drug-trafficking crimes the Interior Ministry recorded that week. [@1] Three of
the nine people detained were women. [@1] The ministry recorded eight crimes
connected with illegal drug trafficking between 28 November and 5 December 2005.
[@1]

Its press centre said officers had taken about 30 kg of drugs out of illegal
circulation over that period. [@1] The haul was broken down as more than 7.6 kg
of heroin and about 22 kg of marijuana. [@1] Only the marijuana portion of about
22 kg is counted as cannabis here; the heroin and the combined 30 kg total are
not. [@1]

The breakdown was given to the Asia-Plus news agency by the Interior Ministry's
press centre, which reported from Dushanbe. [@1] The ministry presented the
figures as a weekly national result for Tajikistan, not a single district case.
[@1]
