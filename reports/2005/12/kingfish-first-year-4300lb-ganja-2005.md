One year after its launch against organised crime, Jamaica's Operation Kingfish had seized about 4,300 pounds of ganja and 12 metric tonnes of cocaine, task-force head Assistant Commissioner Glenmore Hinds told the Jamaica Information Service. [@1] The operation had also destroyed 7 hectares of fully grown ganja plants, about 225,000 seedlings and 13 nurseries, and had seized illegal weapons and destroyed three drug-trafficking airstrips. [@1]

National Security Minister Peter Phillips gave the same first-year account in a national broadcast, saying over 12 metric tonnes of cocaine, unspecified quantities of hash oil and over 4,000 pounds of compressed ganja had been seized. [@2] He said the task force had arrested 235 people for murders, firearms, drugs and ammunition offences and apprehended 32 wanted persons. [@2] Hinds said key players in the criminal networks were among those arrested and called the year a precise meeting of the task force's mandate. [@1]
