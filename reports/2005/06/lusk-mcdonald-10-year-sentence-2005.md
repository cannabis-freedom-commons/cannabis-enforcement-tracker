Colm McDonald, a 38-year-old haulier from Racecourse Common in Lusk, County Dublin, was
jailed for 10 years by Judge Joseph Matthews at Dublin Circuit Criminal Court. [@1] He had
pleaded guilty to possession of 978.8 kg of cannabis worth an estimated €12.42 million and
30.08 kg of cocaine worth about €3.1 million. [@1,2]

## Peat briquettes as cover

Gardaí uncovered the drugs during a surveillance operation, following the lorry along the
main Dublin to Belfast road to Bentley's, a container yard at Treen Hill in Lusk. [@1]
Officers found the cannabis packed in 36 bales in a hidden compartment under the floor of
the lorry, with evidence of recent welding in the container section. [@1,2] The cocaine was
found in a suitcase hidden in McDonald's garage after officers watched it being removed from
the lorry. [@1]

The lorry had ostensibly been used for transporting peat briquettes. [@1,2] McDonald drove
it from Rosslare to France on 14 April 2003, where the briquettes were dropped off, then on
to Spain before returning through Rosslare with the drugs on 19 April 2003. [@1] His
co-accused, Julian Gilloughley, who had travelled with him, was jailed for five years, later
increased to seven years on the prosecution's appeal. [@1]
