Spain's Interior Ministry said the British-registered Squilla was stopped on Sunday 5 June
2005 as part of Operation Bouzas, a joint Spanish-British inquiry running for three months
after a British request through Europol. [@1] The boat was intercepted in the Atlantic
Ocean off western Spain and taken to Cadiz in southern Spain. [@1] Officers found eight
tonnes of cannabis, which the authorities believed was destined for Scotland and worth an
estimated 24 million pounds. [@1,2]

Four of the boat's crew, two Scots, a Spaniard and an Estonian, were arrested, and police
searched addresses across Scotland, arresting two men in Glasgow and a third in Perth.
[@1] The Scottish Drug Enforcement Agency, which led the operation with Spanish
cooperation, called the seizure a major blow to a Scotland-based organised crime group.
[@1] The fishing vessel had reportedly left Britain weeks earlier, called in Galicia and
headed for Morocco before being boarded on its return journey. [@2]
