Lynn Wood, who ran the Cannabis Café in Saint John with her husband, was sentenced to one year in jail on 1 June 2005 for distributing cannabis from the shop. [@1] Judge Murray Cain refused house arrest, finding she was a risk to re-offend, so the mother of three would give birth to her fourth child in a prison hospital. [@1] Wood maintained that all cannabis sales had been for medical purposes. [@1]

The court had convicted Wood of drug trafficking in February 2005 and rejected her defence that the café was a compassion club selling only to medical users, with the judge calling that argument a sham. [@2] Sentencing had first been set for 12 May. [@2] The café had stopped selling marijuana after the June bust but remained open. [@2]
