## The boarding

The British-registered fishing boat Squilla was stopped in the Atlantic off western Spain on Sunday 5 June 2005, ending Operation Bouzas, a surveillance operation run over the previous three months and coordinated by the Scottish Drug Enforcement Agency. [@1] The joint Spanish-British operation followed a British request for cooperation through Europol, and a Spanish customs vessel intercepted the trawler, which Scottish officers had tailed since it left Morocco bound for the United Kingdom. [@1, 2]

Officers found eight tonnes of cannabis worth an estimated £24 million stowed under the Squilla's decks, a haul the authorities believed was destined for Scotland. [@1, 2] Four crew members were arrested aboard, two Scots aged 49 and 43 with a Spaniard and an Estonian, while searches at addresses across Scotland brought two further arrests in Glasgow and one in Perth. [@1] The vessel was taken to Cadiz in southern Spain. [@1]

Detective Superintendent Campbell Corrigan, the agency's head of operations, called the seizure a "major body blow" to a Scotland-based organised crime group, and Spain's Interior Ministry said it could not rule out more arrests in Scotland and Spain. [@1]
