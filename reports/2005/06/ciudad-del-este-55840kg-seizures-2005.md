Paraguay's Senad anti-drug secretariat seized a total of 55,840 kilograms of
marijuana in two unconnected seizures at checkpoints in Ciudad del Este during
the last week of June 2005, with dogs from its U.S.-funded canine unit finding
the drug. [@1] In the same week's operations the dogs also uncovered 726 grams
of cocaine at a checkpoint in Mariscal Estigarribia, and Senad agents arrested
four people. [@1] A 1 July 2005 U.S. embassy roundup reported the busts under
the heading "Drug Busts Offer Hope" in its account of the week's events. [@1]
