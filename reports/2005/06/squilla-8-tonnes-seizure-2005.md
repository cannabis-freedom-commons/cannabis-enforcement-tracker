Scottish police said they had broken up a major smuggling ring after a fishing boat carrying an estimated 24 million pounds worth of cannabis was stopped off Spain. [@1] The British-registered Squilla was intercepted in the Atlantic Ocean off western Spain and taken to Cádiz in southern Spain. [@1] Spain's Interior Ministry said the boat had been stopped on the Sunday as part of Operation Bouzas, a joint Spanish-British operation begun three months earlier after a British request through Europol. [@1]

The four crew members on board, including two Scots aged 49 and 43, were arrested, along with three more people in Glasgow and Perth. [@1] Officers put the haul at eight tonnes and said they believed the drugs were destined for Scotland. [@1] A later Scotsman account of the wider Operation Folklore investigation described the Squilla as a converted trawler owned by John Gorman that was intercepted off western Spain in June 2005 carrying cannabis worth 24 million pounds. [@2]
