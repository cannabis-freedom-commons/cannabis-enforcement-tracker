The National Cannabis Crime Operation, run between November 2004 and April
2005, destroyed about 108,000 cannabis plants across New Zealand. [@1] The
operation led to 564 arrests, the seizure of 46 firearms and the recovery of
an estimated $230,000 worth of stolen property. [@1] Detective Senior
Sergeant Scott McGill credited the public, whose tip-offs located plots and
offenders, and dedicated the results to the memory of Detective Hughes and
pilot Chris Scott, who died in a plane accident in Queenstown while working
on the operation. [@1]

Detective Travis Hughes of Queenstown and pilot Chris Scott of the Bay of
Plenty were killed when their single-engined Cessna 172 crashed in the
Gibbston Valley area during a routine cannabis reconnaissance flight. [@2]
Police named the two men on the morning of 30 January 2005; the Cessna had
been flying in good weather with no indication then of the cause, and a
Transport Accident Investigation Commission inspector was due at the crash
site. [@2]
