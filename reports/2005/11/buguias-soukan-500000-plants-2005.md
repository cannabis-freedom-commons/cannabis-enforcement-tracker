Elements of the Anti-Illegal Drugs Special Operations Task Force (AIDSOTF)
uprooted some 500,000 marijuana plants valued at about 100 million pesos in a
plantation in Benguet believed to be owned by New People's Army (NPA) rebels.
[@1] AIDSOTF director Marcelo Ele Jr. reported that his men found the plantation
in a far-flung area about 7,120 feet above sea level at the tri-boundaries of
Benguet, Mountain Province and Ifugao. [@1]

Supported by the Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency, the task force raided the
plantation at Sitio Soukan, Barangay Lam-ayan, Buguias, Benguet. [@1] Of the
500,000 plants uprooted, 300,000 were fully grown with fruiting tops, 150,000
were half-grown and 50,000 were seedlings; the raiding team also found a manual
on insect sprayers and insecticides. [@1] The task force report stated that the
plantation was maintained and cultivated by local communist terrorists as their
source of funds. [@1]

## Burned on site

No one was arrested in the operation. [@1] Because the police had no capability
to airlift the huge volume of uprooted plants to Camp Crame in Quezon City, Ele
said, they burned the plants in Barangay Lam-ayan; only four sacks were
presented by Interior Secretary Angelo Reyes and PNP chief Arturo Lomibao at a
press conference at Camp Crame. [@1] Reyes congratulated the participating
policemen, saying the momentum of the anti-illegal-drugs campaign would keep
going and accelerate. [@1]
