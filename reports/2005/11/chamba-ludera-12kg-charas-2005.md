A Himachal police CIA team acting on a tip-off intercepted a private vehicle at the
Ludera checkpoint in Chamba district on 14 November 2005 and seized 12 kg of charas from
it. [@1] Superintendent of Police J.P. Singh said the three carriers, Parveen Kumar,
Mohinder Singh and Balwant Singh, the driver of the vehicle, were arrested on the spot
and the vehicle (DL8C-0816) was impounded. [@1]

All three arrested men are residents of Amritsar in Punjab, and cases were registered
against them under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act. [@1] Singh valued
the seized charas at Rs 24 lakh. [@1]
