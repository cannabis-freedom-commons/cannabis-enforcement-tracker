La Prensa reported that a second marijuana shipment of the week had been
captured the previous day by the PTJ drug division. [@1] The seizure took place
at the mouth of the Río Chico in the corregimiento of Pacora, where two
Colombian nationals were detained. [@1] DIAaDIA likewise placed the trafficking
attempt on Pacora's coast at the Río Chico for introducing Colombian-origin
drugs into Panama. [@2] The operation began two days earlier after the PTJ
received information, and officers detected a blue double-cab pickup in the
early hours of the previous day and found drugs on inspection. [@1] Both
detained men had rented the blue pickup used to move the marijuana. [@2] The
vehicle used belonged to a rental agency in the capital city. [@1] PTJ
criminal-investigations chief Wilfredo Hurtado said one detainee lived in San
José, Costa Rica, and the other in Colombia. [@1] DIAaDIA reported instead that
one of the two Colombians lived in Panama and the other in Costa Rica. [@2] The
two Colombians were placed at the disposal of the drug prosecutor's office. [@2]

Hurtado said the seizure comprised 169 packages containing 334 kilograms of
marijuana. [@1] A second account gave the same total of 334 kilos of marijuana.
[@2] That account said officers found about 167 bundles making up the kilos.
[@2] The article said more than 500 kilos of drugs had been seized that week on
Panama province coasts. [@1]

The PTJ said it would ask immigration authorities for documentation on the
migratory movements of the two detained Colombians. [@1] Hurtado said the
packaging suggested the load was bound north toward the United States. [@2]
