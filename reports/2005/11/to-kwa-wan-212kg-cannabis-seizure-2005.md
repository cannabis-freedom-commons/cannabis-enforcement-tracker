Police charged a 32-year-old man with drug trafficking after seizing HKD9.5 million in cannabis in To Kwa Wan, the biggest haul since 2002. [@1] Officers ambushed a building on Tam Kung Road at 4.45pm on 11 November 2005 and intercepted the man as he left carrying 1 kg of herbal cannabis. [@1] Another 211 kg was found in his flat in vacuum-packed plastic bags. [@1]

The cannabis had been imported from the Mainland. [@1] The man was due to appear in the Kowloon City Magistracy the following Monday. [@1]
