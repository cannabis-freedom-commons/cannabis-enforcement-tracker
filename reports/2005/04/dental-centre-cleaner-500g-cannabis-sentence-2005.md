Vanmaichelvan s/o Barsathi, 35, a supervisor with a cleaning company, faced a capital
charge of trafficking 749.17 grams of cannabis and a second charge over 458.91 grams
of cannabis mixture, both allegedly committed on 2 April 2004 in a cleaner's store at
the National Dental Centre to which his work gave him access. [@1] He and S. Manogaran,
accused of abetting him, claimed trial and were tried together. [@1]

On the fourth day of the trial the prosecution amended the cannabis charge down to
499.99 grams, just under the 500-gram threshold for the mandatory death sentence;
Vanmaichelvan then pleaded guilty and admitted hiding the drugs above a false ceiling
in store 8-1 on the eighth floor, where officers recovered blocks and loose vegetable
matter in a paper bag and foil wrappings. [@1] He told officers the drugs belonged to
an accomplice who had left them with him four or five days earlier. [@1]

## Sentence appeal fails

The judge sentenced him to 26 years' imprisonment with 15 strokes of the cane, near
the minimum of 20 years given the trade of a reduced charge for his plea. [@1] On
appeal Vanmaichelvan disavowed the mitigation his own counsel had offered, saying he
had agreed to whatever was put to him to secure the deal and spare his life. [@1]
The court found the sentence was not crushing and dismissed the appeal on
22 April 2005. [@1]
