City police detectives acted on a tip that a drug transaction was about to
take place at the Minang Asih Restaurant on Jalan Raya Buncit in South
Jakarta and moved in on Friday evening 29 April 2005, arresting four
suspected members of a drug syndicate. [@1] Officers confiscated 176
kilograms of cannabis in their possession, city police spokesman Senior
Commander Tjiptono said the next day. [@1]
