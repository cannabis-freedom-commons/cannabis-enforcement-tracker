Gardaí were still questioning four men on the night of 25 August 2005 over the discovery of
1.5 tonnes of cannabis resin valued at €10 million, one of the biggest seizures in the State in
recent years. [@1] Three of the men were Irish and the fourth was a truck driver from Valencia in
Spain. [@1]

A Spanish-registered vehicle was being forensically examined after the drugs were intercepted at
a commercial property at Carbury, County Kildare, on the Tuesday afternoon. [@1] Gardaí believed
the vehicle had been in the country for several days and that the Irish dealers behind the shipment
had failed to organise a drop-off point after it arrived; the yard where it was being unloaded
belonged to a man previously unknown to gardaí. [@1]

Among those held were a man from the midlands, an associate in his twenties, and a
Blanchardstown drug dealer known to gardaí for over a decade whose associates included former
members of the Dublin-based Westies gang. [@1] The four were held at Garda stations in Newbridge
and Kildare town under Section 2 of the Criminal Justice Drug Trafficking Act. [@1] A later US
diplomatic cable recorded the same seizure as 1.2 tons of resin leading to the detention of three
Irish men and a Spaniard. [@2]
