Bureau of Land Management (BLM) officials found a large sensimilla marijuana garden on public land in
Delta County during an annual aerial search on the afternoon of Wednesday 4 August 2005. [@1]
Special Agent in Charge John Silence said the plants had a street value of more than $6 million and
that the site appeared to have been cultivated for several years. [@1]

A team of BLM and U.S. Forest Service law enforcement officers, the Colorado National Guard and the
Delta County Sheriff’s Office removed about 3,000 pounds of marijuana from the location. [@1] Because
of the volume, BLM used a wildland fire helicopter to haul the material away for destruction. [@1]
The article does not say whether anyone was present or arrested at the garden. [@1]

Since 1989 the agency has flown public lands in Colorado—about 8.3 million acres—to locate illegal
crops, Silence said, to protect users and employees from hidden cultivation sites. [@1]
