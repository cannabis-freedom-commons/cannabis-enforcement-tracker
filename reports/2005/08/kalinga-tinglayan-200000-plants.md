Anti-narcotics agents and Kalinga police torched marijuana worth about ₱40 million after a two-day
eradication operation in Tinglayan this week in August 2005. [@1] Philippine Drug Enforcement
Agency regional director Danilo Flordeliza said teams found 7.4 hectares of plantations in Sitio
Kasablutan, Barangay Loccong, and Sitio Kaum-uma, Barangay Tulgao West, totaling about 200,000
fully grown plants. [@1,2]

## Access and ceremony

Flordeliza said villagers tipped off the raiders, who walked four hours from Tinglayan town to reach
the hills. [@1] Part of the haul was brought to Tabuk, Kalinga’s capital, where Governor Dominador
Belac and provincial prosecutor Arsenio Ande led a ceremonial burning on 17 August with local
officials and journalists present. [@1,2] Philstar’s Tagalog edition reported the same hectares,
plant count and Tabuk ceremony. [@2]

## Demand

The regional PDEA office told Philstar that demand for marijuana had risen relative to shabu, with
pushers shifting toward cannabis; the opened reports do not quantify that claim beyond the
Tinglayan eradication figures. [@2]
