Officers of PJ Nikšić and the Administration stopped a truck with Nikšić licence
plates owned by 47-year-old Branislav Mijatović at the Vraćenovići border
crossing while 30-year-old Vladica Bojović from Nikšić was driving it. [@1] The
criminal investigation established that Bojović, with the involvement of owner
Mijatović, intended to transport the drugs to Bosnia and onward to countries in
Western Europe. [@1]

In a joint operation by officers of PJ Nikšić and the Administration, officers
found and seized around 33 kilograms of marijuana in the truck driven by
Bojović. [@1] The search could not be carried out at the border crossing, so the
truck was returned to Nikšić for a detailed examination. [@1] In Nikšić,
officers found three PVC sacks containing 25 packages with 30 kilograms of
marijuana hidden in a specially built bunker in the trailer. [@1]

The truck was travelling toward Bosnia before continuing toward countries in
Western Europe. [@1] The Nikšić police announced the seizure and the findings of
the criminal investigation into the intended smuggling route. [@1]
