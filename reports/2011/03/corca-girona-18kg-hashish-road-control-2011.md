Catalonia's police said a routine road check on the road between Corçà and
Girona had found nearly 18 kilograms of cannabis resin in a car. [@1] The police
put the resin's estimated street value at 25,500 euros and reported the arrest
of the driver, whose nationality was not released. [@1] The driver, 32, was held
in pretrial detention and then appeared in court accused of drug trafficking and
an offence against public health. [@1]
