Security forces in Kandahar province's Zharay district captured several
suspected insurgents linked to a drug cache. [@1] Military officials reported
the Zharay action in a Washington statement datelined 28 March 2011. [@1] The
statement lists the Zharay action among other operations yesterday, placing the
capture on 27 March 2011. [@1]

The operations linked to that Zharay cache resulted in seizure of 1,500 pounds,
about 680 kg, of marijuana. [@1] The same Zharay cache operation also yielded
350 pounds of marijuana seeds. [@1] Officials described those captured only as
several suspected insurgents and tied them to the drug cache rather than naming
them. [@1]

The U.S. Army published the account on 29 March 2011 from the International
Security Assistance Force Joint Command. [@1] The wider roundup also describes a
coalition airstrike in Paktika's Bermal district that killed more than 10
insurgents, showing the Zharay seizure was one item in a multi-province
operational summary. [@1] The same summary separately lists weapons and drug
stockpiles found throughout Afghanistan in other actions. [@1]
