Police in northern Tajikistan stopped a Mercedes in the Isfisor jamoat of
Bobojonghafurov district on 10 March 2011 as part of drug-search operations and
detained its 33-year-old driver, identified only as F.G. [@1] A search of the
boot found 29 kg of hashish, which the Interior Ministry said F.G. had bought
together with a citizen identified as S.B. in order to sell it. [@1] The
ministry said the driver told officers he had bought the hashish from C.B. to
resell, and a criminal proceedings was opened against the detained men while the
investigation continued. [@2] Criminal proceedings were opened in the case and
an investigation was under way. [@2]

That seizure of 29 kg was one of three large hashish hauls reported in
Tajikistan within days, after 33 kg was intercepted near Tursunzoda on 9 March
and a further batch was reported in Tursunzoda district on 8 March. [@1]
