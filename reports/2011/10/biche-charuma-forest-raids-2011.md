Police and soldiers destroyed marijuana worth more than TT$271 million in the Charuma Forest near Biche in early October 2011, during Trinidad and Tobago's state of emergency. [@1,2] In raids on Tuesday 4 October, officers destroyed thousands of fully grown marijuana trees and found cured marijuana worth TT$4.5 million in one operation, then seized a larger haul plus arms and ammunition in a second raid with the Defence Force; no one was arrested. [@1] About two days later, officers from the Eastern Division Task Force, the Organised Crime and Narcotics Unit and the Defence Force destroyed further fields valued at TT$115 million. [@2]

## Emergency context

The 4 October raids came on the 45th day of the state of emergency, during which police said 3,853 people had been arrested, adding that operations targeted drugs and arms as well as suspects. [@1] Both announcements gave police estimates of street value. [@1,2]
