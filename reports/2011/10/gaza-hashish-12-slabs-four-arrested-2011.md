Gaza anti-narcotics police detained two men identified only by the initials M-J
and A-N. [@1] In a separate operation, anti-narcotics police in the central area
arrested two men identified only by the initials M-M and R-L after an ambush the
police had set for them. [@1] All four men were referred to an investigation
inspector for completion of the legal procedures against them. [@1]

In the Gaza case, officers seized 10 furush of hashish, 1,070 Atramal pills
valued together with the hashish at 1,500 dollars, and two bottles of beer. [@1]
In the central case, officers seized 2 farsh of hashish valued at 2,000 dollars
and 8,970 Atramal pills valued at 13,000 dollars. [@1] Together the two
operations account for the reported totals of 12 hashish slabs and 10,040 pills.
[@1]

Farsh, plural furush, is the unit the report uses for slabs of hashish. [@1] The
first seizure was reported in Gaza and the second was reported separately by the
central anti-narcotics police in the central Gaza Strip. [@1]
