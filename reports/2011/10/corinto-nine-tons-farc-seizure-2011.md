Colombian anti-narcotics police seized nine tons of marijuana found on Saturday in a house near the village of Corinto, in the southwestern department of Cauca. [@1,2] Colonel Carlos Rodríguez, a deputy director of the anti-narcotics police, said the drugs belonged to the 6th Front of the FARC guerrillas and were meant for export, with Central America as the stated destination. [@1,2]

Inside the country-style residence officers found 129 bags that tested positive for cannabis and its derivatives, with a net weight of 9,000 kilos. [@2] Police estimated the street value of the stash at 15.3 million dollars, calling it a significant blow to the guerrillas' finances; Diálogo separately cited a black-market value of 795,000 dollars. [@1,2]
