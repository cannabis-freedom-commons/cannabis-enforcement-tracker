Police in Jamaica seized roughly 200 pounds (90 kilograms) of marijuana in a raid in the rural southwestern parish of St Elizabeth. [@1] A police statement issued on Sunday said the packaged marijuana was seized during the raid, and that no arrests had been made. [@1]

The report noted the United States State Department's assessment that Jamaica is the Caribbean's largest producer and exporter of marijuana, grown mostly on mountain hillsides or hidden among other crops. [@1]
