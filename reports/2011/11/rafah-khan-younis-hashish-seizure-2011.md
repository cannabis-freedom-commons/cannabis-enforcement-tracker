Gaza anti-narcotics police set an ambush for a dealer in Khan Younis governorate
and arrested a man identified only as M.A. [@1] Officers caught M.A. in Khan
Younis with one slab of hashish in his possession. [@1] During his initial
questioning, he said he had bought hashish slabs from people in Rafah city. [@1]

Acting on that statement, police set a second ambush for the Rafah sellers and
seized three more hashish slabs in Rafah governorate. [@1] The operation
therefore accounted for four slabs in total, one seized in Khan Younis and three
seized in Rafah. [@1] Farsh, plural furush, is the unit the report uses for
slabs or parcels of hashish. [@1]

Police kept the seized quantity and detained the accused to complete the legal
procedures against them. [@1] The report names no charges and gives no court
outcome, saying only that the accused remained detained for completion of the
procedures. [@1]
