Police stopped an interstate bus at the Kala Amb border post with Haryana on 17 November 2011 and found 10.5kg of charas in a bag carried by two of its passengers. [@1,2] Security had been tightened in the area because senior BJP leader L. K. Advani's Rath Yatra was passing through, and officers were routinely checking buses at the border post when they grew suspicious of the couple's bag. [@1,2]

Investigating officer Ranjit Singh named the pair as Mukesh Kumar, 51, and Lalita Devi, 35, and said both were arrested with the contraband the same day. [@1,2] The bag held candles of charas wrapped in polythene sheets, covered with newspaper and sealed with cello tape. [@1,2]

The two told police they were coming from Katra in Jammu and were headed to Haridwar. [@1,2] Police booked the pair under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act. [@2]
