A federal grand jury in the District of Maryland indicted six people on charges
related to the Brown Organization, a marijuana distribution network allegedly
operating across Maryland, Pennsylvania, New York, Arizona and Jamaica. [@1] The
six charged are Jean Brown, 42, of Jamaica; Hubert Downer, a.k.a. “Doc,” 51, of
Jamaica; Dean Myrie, a.k.a. “Journey,” of Jamaica; Dmytro Holovko, a.k.a. “The
Russian,” 53, of Hillside, New Jersey; Jason Carnegie, 41, of Lauderhill,
Florida; and Anthony Hendrickson, 71, of Gardena, California. [@1]

The seven-count indictment alleges the organization conspired to distribute and
possess with intent to distribute at least 1,000 kilograms of marijuana from
2000 onward. [@1] The indictment also alleges that in December 2009, Brown,
Downer and Myrie kidnapped and murdered Michael Knight to further the
organization’s drug business. [@1] Downer is separately charged with being a
felon and an illegal alien in possession of a firearm, and with illegally
re-entering the United States after a previous deportation following a felony
conviction. [@1]

Hendrickson was arrested on November 4, 2011, in Los Angeles; Brown, Downer,
Holovko and Carnegie had been previously arrested. [@1] Brown, Downer and
Holovko are in custody, Carnegie was released under pretrial supervision, and
Myrie remains a fugitive. [@1] The investigation was conducted by ICE-HSI, the
Baltimore County Police Homicide/Missing Persons Unit and the Anne Arundel
County Police Department. [@2]
