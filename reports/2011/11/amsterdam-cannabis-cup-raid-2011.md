On Wednesday 23 November 2011, about 100 Dutch police officers raided the
24th annual High Times Cannabis Cup expo at the Borchland Sportcentrum in
Amsterdam, separating hundreds of attendees from exhibitors to check vendors
against the 500-gram limit for licensed vendors under the Opium Act.
[@1,2] It was the first police raid in the Cup's 24-year history. [@1,2]

## Detained vendor, friendly search

One vendor, Mila, was reported arrested and released over excess hash, though
other accounts say no arrests were made, and no attendees were reported
arrested in any account. [@1,2,3] Small quantities of cannabis were
confiscated. [@2] Officers had watched the expo in plain clothes the day
before after seeing booths give out cannabis, and the search itself was
described as gentle and polite, with participants told they were not its
target. [@1,3]

The Cup's concert and awards went ahead that night at the Melkweg club as
planned. [@1,2] The raid was read as a show of force after conservatives took
power in the Netherlands and shut down cannabis tourism in the south of the
country. [@1]
