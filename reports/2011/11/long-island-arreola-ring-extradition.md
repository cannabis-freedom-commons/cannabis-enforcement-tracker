Bruno Garcia-Arreola, a Mexican national accused of leading an organisation that sent tons of
marijuana from Mexico to the United States, was extradited from Mexico to face a federal
indictment in New York in November 2011. [@2] He had been a fugitive for eight years after
fleeing a 2003 Long Island marijuana and money-laundering case, and was due to be arraigned
before a federal judge in Central Islip on 10 November 2011. [@1,2]

## The 2003 takedown

In May 2003 a federal grand jury in Central Islip indicted Barry Hazan and Warren Hazan, and on
2 June 2003 the authorities unsealed an indictment against Bruno Arreola, charging all three
with running a wholesale marijuana distribution and money-laundering ring. [@1] Investigators
seized 4,000 pounds (about 1.8 tonnes) of marijuana and 11 million dollars in drug proceeds from
locations on Long Island. [@1,2] Each defendant was charged with conspiring to traffic more than
1,000 kilograms of marijuana and with money laundering. [@1]

The Hazans were arraigned in May 2003 and pleaded not guilty; Barry Hazan was released on a 1
million dollar bond while Warren Hazan stayed in custody. [@1] Arreola was arrested on a warrant
in Las Vegas, where agents had lured him by private plane from Mexico, but was released on a
20,000 dollar unsecured bond over prosecutors' objections and failed to appear in court. [@1,2]
He was then also charged with bail jumping and was finally apprehended in Mexico in April 2011
by United States Marshals with Interpol and Mexican officers. [@1,2] If convicted of the drug
conspiracy charge he faced at least ten years in prison and up to life. [@2]
