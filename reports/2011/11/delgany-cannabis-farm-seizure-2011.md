Gardaí from Bray carried out a search of an industrial unit near Delgany in
the course of an ongoing investigation on the afternoon of 30 November
2011. [@1] Inside they found a substantial cannabis cultivation operation:
approximately 1,000 cannabis plants, put at an estimated street value of up
to €400,000 subject to analysis, were seized. [@1]

A man in his thirties was arrested at the scene and detained at Bray Garda
station under section 2 of the Criminal Justice (Drug Trafficking) Act
1996, under which he could be held for up to seven days. [@1,2]
