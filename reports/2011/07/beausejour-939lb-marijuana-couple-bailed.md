Julian and Moeita Adjodha were arrested and charged on 7 April 2011 over
marijuana found at their Beausejour business compound. [@1] The
forty-five-year-old man and his 41-year-old wife were both charged with
possession of controlled drugs and possession with intent to supply. [@1] Julian
Adjodha was also charged separately with importing controlled drugs. [@1] The
couple first appeared before a magistrate on 8 April represented by local
attorneys Lorne Theophilus and Gerald Williams. [@1] Julian and Moeita Adjodha
were granted bail on 11 April after spending the weekend behind bars. [@1] The
bail application came before Justice Kenneth Benjamin on that Monday. [@1] Lorne
Theophilus appeared for the defence while Tina Mensah appeared for the Crown.
[@1]

The case concerns 93.9 lbs of marijuana found concealed on the Beausejour
business compound on 17 March. [@1] The drugs were hidden in sealed metal
barrels containing dishwashing liquid and were valued at EC$75,200. [@1] Bail
was set at $10,000 on each of the five charges or suitable security with one
surety on each charge. [@1] They had to surrender their passports and were
prohibited from leaving the state without court permission. [@1] The couple also
had to report to the Gros Islet Police Station every Monday and Friday before
6pm. [@1]

Julian and Moeita Adjodha appeared in the High Court on 1 July for a sufficiency
hearing. [@2] A third defendant appeared in the box with them. [@2] He is Einan
Etan, who was charged on 14 April with aiding and abetting importation of
controlled drugs. [@2] The Crown sought an adjournment to obtain outstanding
statements and reports. [@2]
