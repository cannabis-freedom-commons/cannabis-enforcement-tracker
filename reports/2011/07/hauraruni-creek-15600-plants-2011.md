Police ranks conducted a narcotics eradication operation at Hauraruni Creek on the Soesdyke-Linden Highway on 11 July 2011. [@1] The operation unearthed a field of about thirteen acres under cultivation with approximately 15,600 cannabis plants ranging between three and twelve feet in height. [@1]

The site also held eight nurseries containing approximately 40,000 seedlings, three tarpaulin camps, 20 kg of dried cannabis and 2 kg of cannabis seeds. [@1] The plants, seedlings, dried cannabis and seeds were destroyed. [@1]

## No arrests

No one was arrested during the operation, and the account names no suspects behind the field and camps. [@1]
