Tokushima prefectural police arrested a 39-year-old physician of Kyoto
University Hospital's internal medicine department on suspicion of possessing
cannabis at about 3:30 am on 17 July 2011. [@1] Officers who stopped him after
he entered a night-restricted road in front of JR Tokushima station found about
0.15 grams of dried cannabis in his trouser pocket and in the dashboard of the
car he was driving. [@1] He admitted the cannabis was for his own use, and
police said they would investigate how he had obtained it. [@1]

Hospital director Michiaki Mishima apologised on 20 July 2011, saying the
arrest of a hospital physician should never have happened, that the hospital
would establish the facts quickly and deal with the matter strictly, and that
it would reinforce guidance to all staff. [@2]
