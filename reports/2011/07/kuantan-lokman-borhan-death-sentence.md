Kuantan High Court sentenced tour guide Lokman Borhan, 54, to death on 5 July
2011 for trafficking 895.08 grams of cannabis about a year earlier. [@1]
Judicial Commissioner Mariana Yahya found the defence had not raised reasonable
doubt against the prosecution's case. [@1] Lokman was charged over drugs at
house number 11, Lorong Kubang Buaya 47, Taman Kubang Buaya 3, at 9.20pm on 7
August 2010. [@1]

The court also gave him four years in jail from the date of arrest for
possessing five packets holding 22.39 grams of suspected cannabis at the same
place and time. [@1] Nine prosecution witnesses and two defence witnesses
testified at the trial, which the Bernama news agency reported. [@1]
