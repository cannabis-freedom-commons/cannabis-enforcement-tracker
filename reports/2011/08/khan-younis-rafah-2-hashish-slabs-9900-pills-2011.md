Khan Younis anti-narcotics police in the southern Gaza Strip seized two slabs of
hashish from a dealer described as a hashish promoter. [@1] Officers made the
seizure in an ambush near Gaza International Airport at the Amer project after
targeting the dealer. [@2] Police retained the seized hashish and detained the
dealer for completion of the legal procedures against him. [@2]

In a separate related action, Rafah anti-narcotics officers seized 9,900
narcotic pills from a citizen identified only as M-T. [@1] The Rafah seizure
happened during a mission in Rafah governorate and was reported alongside the
Khan Younis ambush. [@1]

Safa published its account of both seizures on 11 August 2011 and describes the
Khan Younis operation as taking place on Thursday. [@2] Safa places the Khan
Younis seizure in the southern Gaza Strip on Thursday and attributes the account
to a police statement. [@2] The 9,900 pills were narcotic pills rather than
cannabis, so only the two hashish slabs count as the cannabis component of this
event. [@2]
