Benešov police charged a young man from the Benešov district with unlawful drug
production after he grew marijuana on his parents' farm. [@1] Police said he
used the dried cannabis for smoking and for making ointments and teas. [@1] He
admitted the offence of unlawful cultivation of plants containing a narcotic or
psychotropic substance. [@2] He was investigated at liberty rather than taken
into custody. [@2] If convicted he faces up to six months in prison. [@1]

Acting on a Benešov judge's order, officers carried out a house search on the
farm on the previous Wednesday. [@1] The search seized 133 cannabis plants
measuring 0.3 to 3 metres together with dried crushed cannabis. [@1] A second
account confirms officers seized 133 cannabis plants, some up to three metres
tall, plus stocks of dried crushed material from the parents' land and house.
[@2] Police secured the plants for chemical analysis at a specialist facility in
Kladno to determine their THC content. [@1] The 133 plants far exceeded the
statutory larger-than-small amount threshold of more than five plants. [@2]

Police said he had grown marijuana since the previous year in at least 15
unidentified spots around Klimětice in the Příbram district from seeds obtained
in Sedlčany. [@1] About twenty of those plants were harvested, dried, crushed
and kept in his room in a house in the Benešov district for personal smoking and
for making ointments and teas. [@1] Seeds from that earlier crop were then used
to grow the 133 plants later seized on his parents' farm. [@1]
