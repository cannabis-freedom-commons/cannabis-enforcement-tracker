Manuchehr Najafi, son of Farman, was hanged in Tehran's Evin prison on the morning of 12 January 2011 for buying and selling 9 kilos of hashish. [@1] The Abdorrahman Boroumand Center memorial records his killing by hanging in Tehran on that date on drug-trafficking charges. [@2]

Najafi was one of eight men hanged that morning; seven had been convicted of drug trafficking and one of rape, according to Iranian agency reporting. [@1] With these executions, the count for the first twelve days of 2011 reached 36 people. [@1]
