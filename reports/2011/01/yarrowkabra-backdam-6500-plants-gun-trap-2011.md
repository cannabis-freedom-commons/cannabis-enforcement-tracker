Police conducted a drug eradication operation at Yarrowkabra Backdam on the Soesdyke-Linden Highway and found seven fields, about six acres in size, holding approximately 6,500 marijuana plants between two and seven feet in height. [@1] A nursery containing 3,500 seedlings and seven camps stood on the same site. [@1]

Officers also found 50 kg of dried cannabis and 3 kg of seeds at the farm. [@1] On a trail leading to the farm they found an improvised shotgun with one live 12-gauge cartridge, set as a gun trap. [@1] The plants and camps were destroyed. [@1]

## No arrests

No one was arrested during the operation, and the account names no suspects behind the fields, the stored cannabis or the gun trap. [@1]
