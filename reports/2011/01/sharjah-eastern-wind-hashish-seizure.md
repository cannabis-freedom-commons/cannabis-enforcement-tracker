Sharjah police and the coastguard arrested fourteen men and seized 2.5 tonnes of hashish from a boat off the Khor Fakkan coast in an operation codenamed Eastern Wind. [@1,2] The twelve Iranians and two Pakistanis had stuffed the hashish into bags used to pack cooking flour and were waiting for a chance to slip past UAE territorial waters. [@1,2]

Police said a tip-off revealed the gang was hiding the drugs on the boat and looking for a buyer, so one officer posed as a buyer and contacted the gang's head several times before the squad cordoned off the area and stopped the fourteen as they tried to flee. [@1] Sharjah's police chief, Major-General Humaid Al Hadidi, called it the biggest hashish haul in 20 years, since three tonnes seized on the same Khor Fakkan coast in 1991. [@2]
