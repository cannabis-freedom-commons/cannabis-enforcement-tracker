Municipal guards and Civil Police officers went to a chácara, a small country
property, in Colônia Murici in the rural area of São José dos Pinhais, in the
Curitiba metropolitan region, on Thursday 20 January 2011 after anonymous
tip-offs. [@1] They arrested the owner of the property, Antonio Olinek, 38, in
the act. [@1] Officers estimated that about 50,000 cannabis plants had been sown
among a maize crop on the property, some up to three metres tall. [@1] Olinek
told officers he had received the seeds from some young people and said he had
no intention of selling the product. [@1]

The plants were removed on Friday 21 January and held until the decision to burn
them. [@1] The burning of the roughly 50,000 plants began at about 14:00 on
Monday 24 January 2011 in São José dos Pinhais. [@1] A truck carried about 1.2
tonnes of the cannabis plants to a company inside the city whose name was
withheld for security reasons. [@1] Sixteen security officers, among them
municipal guards, Civil Police officers and police chiefs, accompanied the
incineration. [@1]
