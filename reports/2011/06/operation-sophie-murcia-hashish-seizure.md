Spain's Customs Surveillance service closed Operation Sophie in Murcia with
4,320 kilos of hashish seized, five people arrested and a fishing vessel
taken. [@1] The agency put the haul's value on the illicit market at
6,177,000 euros. [@1]

The operation began on 9 December 2010, when air and sea patrols spotted the
12-metre fishing vessel S Siroco sailing in a way deemed suspicious for
fishing. [@1] The Cartagena-based patrol boat Abanto intercepted it at 4.30
pm, 24 miles south of Cartagena. [@1] On boarding, officers found 141 burlap
bales, commonly used to transport hashish, in the hold and other parts of
the boat. [@1]

Officers seized the vessel and arrested the five crew members, including the
skipper, taking them to Cartagena that afternoon. [@1] Some of those
arrested had previously been investigated by Customs Surveillance for
maritime drug trafficking. [@1] The operation was closed a few days before
the 13 June 2011 announcement, once the inquiries aimed at dismantling the
criminal organisation were complete. [@1] The drugs, the detainees and the
vessel were handed over to the duty Investigating Magistrate's Court in
Cartagena. [@1]
