Combined Afghan and coalition forces discovered a drug cache in the Maiwand
district of Kandahar province. [@1] The Maiwand cache consisted of 3,307 pounds
of hashish, 661 pounds of marijuana and 146 pounds of opium. [@1] The same
statement reported that the forces discovered a second drug cache of 220 pounds
of hashish in the Zharay district of Kandahar province. [@1]
