Officers of the Customs and Excise Department, the Plipdeco port police, the Criminal Intelligence Unit, the Drug Enforcement Agency and the Organised Crime, Narcotics and Firearms Bureau searched the Port of Point Lisas from about 5.55 pm on Thursday 15 September 2011. [@1] The search team, led by Superintendent Johnny Abraham and Assistant Superintendent Corbette with Couva police support, opened a 40-foot container said to hold frozen chicken parts and found 38 bags of compressed marijuana weighing more than 921 kilos. [@1,2]

Customs communications manager Alicia Charles said the haul had a street value of $7.4 million, calculated at about $8,000 a kilo, while a Police Service release late the next day quoted $30 million as confirmed by a narcotics expert. [@1,2] Charles said the seizure was part of an ongoing joint operation between the Customs and Excise Division and Point Lisas port security that began at the start of the year, under which containers fitting a particular profile were singled out for inspection. [@1] A prominent businessman of central Trinidad, to whom the container had been assigned, was assisting police with inquiries, and sources said it was not the first container of drugs assigned to him seized by law enforcers. [@1] The seizure was the fourth major drug bust at the port within six months. [@1]
