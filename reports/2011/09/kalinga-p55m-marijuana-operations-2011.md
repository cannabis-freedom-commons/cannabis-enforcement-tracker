Joint operatives of the Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency (PDEA) and the Kalinga Police Provincial Office confiscated a total of P5.5 million worth of marijuana plants in separate operations in the region in the week before police reported them on Monday 5 September 2011. [@1]

Kalinga police director Senior Superintendent Ernesto Gaab said officers first seized 20 bricks of dried marijuana leaves, priced at P500,000, at Sitio Foyfoyan in Barangay Ambato-Legleg, Tinglayan town. [@1] The bricks had been abandoned by still unidentified dealers riding tandem on a motorcycle who fled to avoid pursuit when they ran through a checkpoint. [@1]

In a separate incident, PDEA agents uprooted around 25,000 fully grown marijuana plants in large plantations on the outskirts of Sitio Talaca in Barangay Agbannawag, Tabuk City. [@1]
