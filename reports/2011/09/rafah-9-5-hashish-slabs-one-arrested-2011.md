Anti-narcotics officers in Rafah governorate arrested a man identified only as
Y.M. in an ambush they had set. [@1] Questioned after the arrest, he confessed
to what had been attributed to him. [@1] He was referred to the police inspector
for completion of the legal procedures against him. [@1]

The General Department for Combating Drugs in Rafah seized 9.5 farsh of hashish
valued at 9,500 dollars. [@1] The seizure followed information reaching the
department that a person intended to smuggle narcotics. [@1]

The department said it deploys its men everywhere to catch smugglers and dealers
and had recently imposed control over all crossings through which drugs could be
brought into the Strip. [@1]
