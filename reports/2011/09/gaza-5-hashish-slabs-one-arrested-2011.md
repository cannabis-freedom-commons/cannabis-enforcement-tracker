Gaza anti-narcotics police set an ambush with a force from the public order and
intervention units. [@1] Colonel Imad al-Amsi, the director of the
anti-narcotics department, led the operation. [@1] Officers arrested a man
identified only as M-D and found the drugs in his possession. [@1] He was
referred to the police inspector to complete the legal procedures against him.
[@1]

Officers seized 5 farsh of hashish and 1,100 Atramal pills in an ambush set with
a force from the public order and intervention units. [@1] The 1,100 Atramal
pills were seized alongside the hashish and are not counted in the cannabis
totals. [@1]
