Police ranks conducted a drug eradication operation at Kamuni on the Demerara River and found an eight-acre field under cannabis cultivation, about 3.24 hectares. [@1] The field held about 12,000 cannabis sativa plants at an average height of five feet. [@1]

The site also held four nurseries with approximately 80,000 seedlings, 40 kilogrammes of dried cannabis and a tarpaulin camp. [@1] Everything the operation uncovered, the plants, the seedlings, the dried stock and the camp, was destroyed. [@1]

## No arrests

No one was arrested during the operation, and the account names no suspects behind the field and nurseries. [@1]
