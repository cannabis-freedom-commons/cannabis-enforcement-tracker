Officers of the National Drug Enforcement Agency (NDEA) and the Police Intervention Unit (PIU) were on duty at the Sibanor checkpoint on 12 May 2011 when a Toyota pickup with registration number 6325F failed to stop. [@1] The information was passed to PIU officers at the Faraba Sutu checkpoint, where the vehicle failed to stop again, and officers fired two warning shots before the driver diverted through the back way to Kembujeh village. [@1] The driver passed through the Jah Oil company to fill the tank and abandoned the vehicle on seeing the officers. [@1] Officers found nine bags and 86 bundles of suspected cannabis in the vehicle. [@1] Four people were arrested: the vehicle's owner Musa Samura, Modou Camara, a soldier attached to Yundum Barracks, Mamat Secka and Ebrima Jallow. [@1] The driver, Mamadi Jarjue, Mustapha Secka and Omar remained at large. [@1]
