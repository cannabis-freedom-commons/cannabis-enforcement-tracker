Palestinian police anti-narcotics officers in Gaza seized about 100 green banjo
seedlings, a large quantity of dried banjo seedlings, and banjo seeds kept for
planting. [@1] The Al-Tuffah anti-narcotics unit made the seizure with the units
for Al-Rimal and the beach, teams named for neighbourhoods of Gaza City and its
coast. [@1] They said the cannabis was in the possession of a man identified
only by the initials A.D. [@1]

Officers secured the seized quantity and referred the accused to the police
investigation inspector to complete the legal procedures against him. [@1] The
figure of about 100 covers only the green seedlings; the dried seedlings are
described as a large quantity, and the seeds only as a quantity held for
cultivation. [@1] No other person is named in the police account of the seizure.
[@1] The joint operation was the work of the Palestinian police anti-narcotics
service, which said it had both living plants and material ready to grow more.
[@1]
