Interior Ministry officers stopped an Opel Vectra driven by a 39-year-old man
identified only as M.B. in Dushanbe on 25 May 2011 on suspicion of drug
trafficking. [@1] A search of the car found and removed more than 45 kg of
hashish. [@1] Investigators said the driver had bought the batch from a
32-year-old man identified only as A.K. [@1]

On the same day officers seized more than 73 kg of narcotics in the northern
Sughd region. [@2] That total included 40.5 kg of hashish seized in Chkalovsk.
[@1] Officers seized more than 10 kg of hashish in Bobojon Ghafurov district.
[@1] In Qairoqqum they seized more than 9 kg of hashish and 13.4 kg of
marijuana. [@1] Near the capital officers detained a 45-year-old woman from
Qurghon Teppa in Khatlon province with 3 kg of heroin, a separate find counted
in the ministry's overall total. [@2]

The Interior Ministry said its officers took more than 120 kg of narcotics
nationwide in the preceding 24 hours. [@1] Criminal cases were opened over all
the finds and the detainees were placed in custody. [@1]
