Police at Nairobi's Jomo Kenyatta International Airport seized hashish packed
in 88 slabs in 20 cartons that had been disguised as vegetables for export,
and detectives said the consignment was destined for the United Kingdom. [@1]
Airport CID chief Joseph Ngisa said officers suspected something was not right
with the cartons and seized them early on Tuesday. [@1]

The truck driver who had just delivered the cargo at the airport was arrested,
while two other men believed to be loaders who had accompanied him escaped,
police said. [@1] JKIA police chief Philip Tuimur said officials from the
Government Chemist and from weights and measures were invited to assist the
investigation as detectives prepared to arraign the suspect in court, and he
said the street value of the drugs had not been established. [@1] A detective
involved in the investigation said police had the details of the person due to
receive the cargo in London and were relying on counterparts there to trace
him. [@1] Police said they were increasingly concerned at the high cases of
drug trafficking at the airport, a major regional hub. [@1]
