Police ranks conducted a narcotics eradication operation at Moblissa Backdam on the Linden-Soesdyke Highway over the weekend of 28-29 May 2011 and found two fields with about 4,000 cannabis plants ranging between two and five feet in height. [@1] The site also held two nurseries containing approximately 20,000 seedlings and two tarpaulin camps. [@1]

The plants, seedlings and camps were destroyed. [@1] Two people were arrested and were in police custody assisting with the investigations. [@1]
