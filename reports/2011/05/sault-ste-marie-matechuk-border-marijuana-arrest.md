Jordan Matechuk, a long-snapper for the Canadian Football League's Hamilton
Tiger-Cats, was stopped at the International Bridge border crossing into the
United States on 31 May 2011. [@1] According to a release from U.S. Customs and
Border Protection, officers seized 1.25 grams of marijuana from him after an
inspection. [@1] The same search turned up 543 anabolic steroid pills and 262
millilitres of liquid anabolic steroids, which are outside this tracker's
cannabis scope. [@1]

Matechuk was arrested and turned over to the Sault Ste Marie Police Department.
[@1] Customs spokesperson Devin Chamberlain said anabolic steroids are a
Schedule III controlled substance that it is illegal to possess without a
prescription. [@1] No charges or court outcome related to the marijuana were
reported. [@1]
