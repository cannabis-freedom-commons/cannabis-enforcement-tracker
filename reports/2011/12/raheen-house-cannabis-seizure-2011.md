Gardaí from Roxboro Road station searched a house in the Raheen area of
Limerick early on the morning of 7 December 2011. [@1] A woman in her
twenties was arrested at the scene and cannabis herb with an estimated
street value of up to €160,000, subject to analysis, was seized; she was
detained at Roxboro Road station under section 2 of the Criminal Justice
(Drug Trafficking) Act 1996. [@1]

The woman was charged in connection with the seizure and was due before
Limerick District Court at 10.30am on 8 December. [@1]
