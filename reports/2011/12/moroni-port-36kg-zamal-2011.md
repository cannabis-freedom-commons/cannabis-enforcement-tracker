More than 36 kg of zamal, the local word for cannabis, was seized at the port of
Moroni and burned at the Moroni court. [@1] Ivory anti-drug sources confirmed
the seizure on Friday and said the cannabis was burned at the Moroni court on
Saturday. [@2]

Brigade chief Youssouf Soilih Msouri said the two parcels had landed at Moroni
port on 19 September. [@1] The shipment came from Dar es Salaam and was to be
delivered to a man named Chamsoudine Allaoui, according to the same source. [@1]
The parcels were opened in the presence of the chief clerk of the Moroni
prosecutor's office, Me Chifaenti Maka. [@1]

The report says tightening of drug controls on major trafficking routes has
turned the Comoros archipelago into a transit hub toward Europe, Mauritius and
Réunion. [@1]

HabarizaComores republished the same account on the same day. [@2]
