Tajik police detained a 32-year-old unemployed man in Shuroobod district, on the
border with Afghanistan, on 31 December 2011. [@1] A search of his person found
8.5 kilograms of cannabis, and a search of his home found a further 27
kilograms. [@1] The interior ministry said the operation by police from the
Shouroobod district station led to the seizure of more than 35 kilograms of
hemp. [@2]

The detained man told investigators he had bought the cannabis from two local
residents, one of them a forester at the Dashti-Jum forestry enterprise. [@1]
The accounts name no charges, say whether the two sellers were detained, or give
a court outcome. [@1] The same Interior Ministry briefing reported a separate
case days earlier, in which the conductor of the Dushanbe-Moscow passenger train
was held with about 5 kilograms of heroin in his luggage. [@1]
