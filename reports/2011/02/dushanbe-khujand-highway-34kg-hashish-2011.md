Officers of Tajikistan's State Committee for National Security stopped a KamAZ
lorry on the Dushanbe-Khujand highway, the main road between the capital and the
northern Sughd region. [@1] Radio Ozodi, RFE/RL's Tajik service, placed the stop
at the Khusheri tract on the same highway and said the lorry had been detained
while a drug group tried to move the drugs. [@2]

Officers removed the drugs from a specially built hiding place in the vehicle,
80 kg in all by the Asia-Plus account. [@1] Nozirjon Buriev, head of the
committee's public-relations centre, gave Asia-Plus a breakdown of about 22 of
heroin, 34 kg of hashish and 24 of raw opium, and 34 kg is the cannabis weight
recorded here. [@1] Radio Ozodi, citing the same official, reported 22 kg of
heroin, more than 34 kg of raw opium and 23 kg of hashish for the same seizure,
its hashish figure differing from Asia-Plus. [@2]

Three alleged traffickers had been arrested over the seizure, and Buriev said
their names were withheld in the interests of the investigation. [@1]

He said operations to identify the remaining members of the group were
continuing. [@1]
