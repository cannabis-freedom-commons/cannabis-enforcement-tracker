Police discovered a two-hectare dagga plantation in Atteridgeville, Pretoria, on Thursday 10 February 2011, with an estimated street value of R5 million, about US$690,000. [@1,2] The find, in the Brazzaville informal settlement, was described by the city as larger than two rugby fields and the biggest such plantation seen in Tshwane. [@1,2]

About 60 municipal parks officials began cutting the crop down at 09:00 that morning to burn it, a job expected to take the whole day. [@1,2] The discovery followed a tip-off from local residents. [@1] No arrests were reported. [@2]
