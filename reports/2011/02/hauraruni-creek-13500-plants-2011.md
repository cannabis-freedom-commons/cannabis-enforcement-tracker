Police ranks conducted a narcotics eradication operation at Hauraruni Creek on the Soesdyke-Linden Highway on 8 February 2011 and found 13 cannabis fields, about 13.5 acres in size, with approximately 13,500 plants ranging between six and ten feet in height. [@1] The site also held 20 nurseries with about 100,000 seedlings. [@1]

In addition, 15 tarpaulin camps with 30 kilogrammes of dried marijuana were found at the fields. [@1] The plants, seedlings and dried marijuana were destroyed. [@1]

## No arrests

No arrest was made during the operation, and the account names no suspects behind the fields and nurseries. [@1]
