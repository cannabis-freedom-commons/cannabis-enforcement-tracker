Houston police stopped an 18-wheeler supposedly carrying only lettuce on the
East Freeway near Market Street on a Sunday afternoon and found 1,995 pounds
of marijuana hidden among the produce. [@1,2] Officers pulled the truck over
in the 13400 block of the East Freeway around 4:45 p.m. for a routine traffic
stop and truck inspection. [@1,2]

Inside, officers found the marijuana packed in plastic-wrapped bundles amid
boxes of leafy greens, alongside several boxes that really did hold lettuce.
[@1,2] Police put the street value of the load at about $3.1 million. [@1,2]

The truck's driver and lone occupant, 36-year-old Paulino Guerra, was arrested
and charged with felony possession of marijuana. [@1,2]
