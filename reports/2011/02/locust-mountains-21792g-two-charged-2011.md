Williams was 24 years old. [@1] He had escaped custody in Dominica in late
November 2010. [@1] Williams was brought before the Serious Offences Court on
February 28, 2011, on drug charges. [@1] He was jointly charged with Abasia
Humphrey of Bayroad, Georgetown, with possession of 21,792 grams of cannabis on
February 27, 2011, in the Locust Mountains. [@1] Both men pleaded not guilty to
the charge. [@1] Humphrey was also charged with having a .22 semi-automatic
pistol and five rounds of .22 ammunition on February 26 at the same location,
and he pleaded not guilty. [@1]

Humphrey was granted bail of $30,000, ordered to report to the Georgetown Police
Station on Mondays and Fridays, and ordered to surrender all travel documents.
[@1] Williams was given bail of $20,000 and ordered to report to the Georgetown
Police Station on Mondays. [@1] After bail was set, the Director of Public
Prosecutions indicated to the court that Williams was involved in other court
matters. [@1] Magistrate Sonya Young noted that her hands were tied since she
had already set bail and advised that the matter could be taken to the High
Court. [@1] The drug and gun matter was adjourned to May 9, 2011. [@1]

Williams had earlier been charged with two other Dominicans over a burglary at
Greens Supermarket in Loubiere, Dominica. [@1] Williams and Martin Luther Jno
Baptiste appeared before Magistrate Candia George in Dominica on November 24,
2010, on a joint burglary charge, while Eustace Joseph was charged with handling
stolen goods. [@1] Williams was not granted bail in the Dominica matter, as he
was considered a flight risk. [@1]
