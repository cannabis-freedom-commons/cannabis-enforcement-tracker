A Bermuda Supreme Court jury acquitted former customs officer Bromwin Thompson
of one count of conspiring to import cannabis on 8 April 2011 and could not
reach a decision on the other conspiracy count. [@1] The counts of corruption
and of possessing illegal drugs with intent to supply were dropped before the
verdict, leaving only the two conspiracy counts. [@1]

Thompson, 36, of Granaway Heights in Southampton, had been charged with two
counts of conspiring to import cannabis and one of possessing illegal drugs with
intent to supply. [@1] The trial heard evidence implicating Thompson with
Shannon Berkley, 42, in the importation of cannabis. [@1]

Bernews reported that the evidence said FedEx packages addressed to two
restaurants had been used to send the drugs, and that the restaurant management
said they had not ordered the packages. [@1] Bernews reported that the total
value of the drugs involved was said to be around $54,000. [@1] A seven-woman,
five-man jury deliberated through the day, was redirected to reach a majority
verdict on the undecided count, and did not decide it. [@1]

On 28 May 2010 Thompson and Shannon Berkeley had been charged in the
Magistrates' Court over an alleged $100,000 plot to bring cannabis and cannabis
resin to the island, and both elected trial by judge and jury at the Supreme
Court. [@2] Magistrate Khamisi Tokunbo released both on $30,000 bail after that
hearing, with a pretrial hearing set for 18 June. [@2]
