Police raided the Montagu farmhouse of Jeremy Acton, leader of the Dagga Party of South Africa, early on Friday morning, 29 April 2011. [@1,2] Acton was away in Robertson at the time; the officers questioned one of his workers and took him to the police station. [@1,2]

All the officers found were a few cannabis seeds. [@1,2] They also took Acton's pipes and photographed his marijuana graphics and a meditation poem, Acton said. [@1,2] Acton had earlier taken party pamphlets to the Montagu police to explain his campaign to legalise cannabis, and said he believed the police wanted to stop his efforts. [@1]

## An election campaign

Acton's party was registered in Langeberg Municipality to contest the 18 May 2011 local government elections, its first. [@1,2] Acton and a fellow party executive already had dagga possession cases pending from earlier busts by local police. [@2]
