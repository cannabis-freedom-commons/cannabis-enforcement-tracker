Webster Edmund, 37, of Layou was sentenced to two years and five weeks in jail
at the St. Joseph court on 7 April 2011, after pleading guilty to four charges.
[@1] He had been brought to court on ten charges three days after he and a
missing Layou teenager were found. [@1]

On 7 April 2011 the St. Joseph court dealt with only four charges: unlawful
possession of 67 grams of marijuana, possession of marijuana with intent to
supply, possession of a firearm and possession of ammunition. [@1] Edmund had
previous dealings with the law before these charges. [@1] The four charges were
among ten brought after Edmund and the teenager were found in the Hillsborough
Estate area, three days before his first court appearance. [@2] The court heard
that Edmund had held the teenager for 40 days, and he had pleaded guilty only to
the drug charges at that stage. [@2]

Edmund was later tried separately for kidnapping and sexual offences, and on 15
March 2013 the High Court sentenced him to a total of eight years and nine
months in jail. [@3] That later account says his marijuana sentence was five
weeks, and that a separate two-year four-month term in 2001 was for unlawful
possession of a firearm and ammunition. [@3]
