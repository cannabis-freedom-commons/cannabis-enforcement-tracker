Police in Antananarivo stopped a car in Antsobolo, in the centre of the capital,
on Tuesday 26 April 2011 as the traffickers were about to deliver their
merchandise to Andranomena, on the Ivato airport road. [@1] The three occupants
of the car were detained, the vehicle was impounded and the cannabis, packed in
two large sacks, was seized. [@1]

The newspaper's page title gives the haul as 200 kg of cannabis. [@1]

The driver told the Anosy court, where the three appeared the day before the
article was published, that one sack weighing about 100 kg was worth 500,000
ariary, about 185 euros. [@1]
