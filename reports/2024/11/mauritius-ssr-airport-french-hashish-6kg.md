Officers of the Customs Anti-Narcotics Section brought a registered piece of
luggage belonging to an incoming male French national in for scanning at SSR
International Airport on 4 November 2024, and the images showed
abnormalities. [@1] Taken to the section's office, the passenger refused to
open the case, claiming it was not his and belonged to someone who had already
left the airport. [@1] A preliminary check found the luggage registered in his
name, and the lock was forced open in his presence. [@1]

Under the zipper on one side of the suitcase lay three rectangular parcels
wrapped in cellophane; one was cut slightly open and held a substance suspected
to be a dangerous drug. [@1] A presumptive field test for cannabis came back
positive. [@1] The revenue authority put the seizure at 6 kg of hashish worth
around Rs 30 million and handed the passenger to the Anti-Drug and Smuggling
Unit for further enquiry. [@1]
