Four people, three relatives and a friend, were charged with possession of
controlled drugs with intent to supply in the Belize City Magistrate's Court.
[@1] They appeared in court on Wednesday, November 27, 2024. [@4] The accused
were Leon Anthony Lodge, 24, his mother Juliet Diane Richards, 51, his deaf-mute
sister Shanice Brown, 31, and his friend Eldy Jane Taylor, 32, a chef. [@1]

Police searched a home at #34 Hemsley Street in the Fabers Road area of Belize
City. [@3] The search took place on November 27, 2024, and turned up 85.1 grams
of cannabis. [@3] The drugs were in a barrel in the back room of the house. [@2]
Lodge admitted the cannabis was his and told the court it was for him and his
friends to smoke; he said he stored a larger quantity to avoid frequent
purchases. [@1] He said his mother and sister did not smoke and were unaware of
the drugs. [@1] Taylor told the court she was there to smoke. [@2]

The magistrate explained that the offence carried a fine of up to $10,000 and a
possible three-year prison term and gave Lodge the chance to reconsider his plea
and seek legal counsel. [@1] Lodge refused representation and kept his guilty
plea, so the magistrate entered a not-guilty plea on his behalf when proceedings
resumed in the afternoon. [@1] All four were granted bail of $2,000 each on
condition that they attend every court date and avoid similar charges. [@1] They
were ordered to return to court on January 9, 2025. [@2]

Lodge posted bail that day, but his mother, sister and Taylor were remanded to
the Belize Central Prison. [@1] Richards and Brown met their bail on November
29, 2024. [@4] Taylor spent five nights on remand before she met her bail. [@3]
She was released on the morning of December 3, 2024. [@3]
