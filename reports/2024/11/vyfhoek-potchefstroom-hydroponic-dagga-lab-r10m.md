Police and security teams searching a small holding in Vyfhoek, Potchefstroom,
found a hydroponic dagga laboratory and illegal weapons and explosives, and
arrested a 29-year-old man. [@2] The search warrant was obtained and executed on
Thursday, 14 November 2024. [@3]

The team was made up of the Drug Task Team, the BIU Security Company and the
Provincial Organised Crime Unit. [@1] North West police spokesperson Colonel
Amanda Funani said officers found two rifles, one of them self-made, ammunition
and three self-made explosive devices: two hand grenades and a small pipe bomb.
[@1] Funani said police also found a hydroponic dagga laboratory with dagga and
dagga plants, which they valued at an estimated street value of R10 million.
[@1] Besides the laboratory, where the plants were being cultivated, police
found other dagga plants in flower pots. [@2]

The suspect, Michael Cilliers, 29, appeared in the Potchefstroom Magistrate's
Court on Tuesday, 19 November, on charges of unlawful possession of firearms and
explosives and of cultivating and dealing in dagga. [@1] He was remanded in
custody, and the case was postponed to Wednesday, 4 December 2024. [@3]

The operation was described by a local outlet as a sophisticated dagga
cultivation operation valued at R10 million, found during a raid at a
smallholding in Vyfhoek on 14 November 2024. [@4] Lieutenant General Sello
Kwena, the North West provincial police commissioner, commended the operation
and said it showed criminal activity would not be tolerated. [@1]
