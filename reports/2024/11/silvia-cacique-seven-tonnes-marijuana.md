Colombian army troops seized seven tonnes of marijuana stored in a depot in the
caserío (hamlet) of Cacique, part of the municipality of Silvia in Cauca
department, in a joint operation with the National Police. [@1] Brigadier
General Federico Mejía, commander of the army's Third Division, called the haul
the largest seizure made by troops since 2017. [@1] The army attributed the
stash to the FARC dissidents led by alias Iván Mordisco and said selling
marijuana was the group's main source of financing. [@1] Authorities added that
the load belonged to the Iván Mordisco dissidents' Dagoberto Ramos substructure.
[@2]

Troops of Light Cavalry Group No. 8 and Infantry Battalion No. 7 went to the
site after military intelligence located the storage point in an operation
mounted the previous night. [@2] No one was arrested in the operation, and the
seized material was handed to the authorities, the army said. [@1]

According to the army, the seizure took national marijuana seizures past the
Defence Ministry's 98-tonne target for 2024, to 112 tonnes. [@2]
