Officers from the Western Division Serious Organised Crime Intelligence
Department (SOCID) conducted two raids in the Malua Range of Navosa after
receiving information about alleged marijuana cultivation. [@1,2]

More than 1,800 plants believed to be marijuana were found at one farm, and
more than 900 plants ranging from 10 cm to 2.5 metres were seized at a second
farm. [@1,2] Assistant Commissioner of Police Operations Livai Driu called the
seizure significant for disrupting the supply chain as operations focus on
cultivation and distribution. [@1,2]

Driu commended the sharing of information that led to the discovery. [@1] The
team spent a night in the highlands to carry out the operation in the difficult
terrain. [@2]
