Colombia's national police director, general William Salamanca, announced on 26
November 2024 that three people had been captured and 1,184 kilos of marijuana
seized aboard a Go-Fast boat. [@1] The operation took place south of Isla
Jicarón, in Panama's Veraguas province. [@1] Salamanca said the three detainees
were of Nicaraguan and Costa Rican origin and were handed to Panamanian
authorities. [@1] According to Salamanca, the crew was taking the load from La
Tola, Nariño, on Colombia's Pacific coast, towards the United States via Panama
and Mexico. [@1]

Colombia's national police, the Attorney General's Office, the U.S. Drug
Enforcement Administration (DEA) and Panama's Servicio Nacional Aeronaval
(Senan), the aeronaval service, took part in the operation. [@1] The same boat
also carried 704 kilos of cocaine hydrochloride, which is not counted in this
report. [@1]

The article reports a separate Senan operation off Punta Mala, Los Santos
province, in which three Colombians were detained with 1,027 packages of drugs
of undetermined weight and type. [@1]
