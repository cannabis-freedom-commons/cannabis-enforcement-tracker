Brice T., 21, who lives in Luxembourg, flew from Dubai to Tirana with a large
blue suitcase and was arrested at the Albanian capital's airport on 10 November
2024, a Sunday, after security forces opened his luggage. [@1] Inside they found
48 parcels of marijuana weighing 26.7 kg in total. [@1] The Albanian State
Police said it detained a 21-year-old resident of Luxembourg, identified as B.
T., and seized 26 kilograms and 700 grams of suspected cannabis from his
luggage. [@2] Police said the seizure was 26 kilograms and 700 grams of
suspected cannabis held as material evidence. [@2]

The State Police said its Rinas police station, Rinas customs authorities and
the Tirana regional narcotics investigation section carried out the operation.
[@2] Police said the check was planned after intelligence that a traveller from
Dubai to Rinas had hidden cannabis in his luggage. [@2] The State Police said
work continued to identify and catch other people implicated, and sent the case
file to the Tirana prosecutor's office. [@2] According to the police, the
21-year-old was travelling from Dubai to Tirana with a suitcase holding 26.7 kg
of cannabis and was arrested after the plane landed. [@3]

Brice T. told investigators he had travelled via Thailand to Dubai and then
Albania, and that a man in Thailand asked him to carry the suitcase to Tirana.
[@1] He said he assumed the suitcase contained only clothes. [@1] According to
his account, someone was to meet him at Rinas airport to take possession of the
suitcase. [@1] Virgule carried the article from Luxemburger Wort, which
published it the day after the police announcement. [@1]
