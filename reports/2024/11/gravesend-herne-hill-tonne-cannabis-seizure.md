Officers stopped a Ford Transit van in Gravesend on 13 November 2024 after a report of
suspicious activity and found an estimated 500 kg of packed cannabis inside. [@1]

Two men were arrested at the scene, and inquiries the same day led detectives to search a
property in Herne Hill in south-east London. [@1] About 800 kg more cannabis was seized there,
taking the total over one tonne, about 1,300 kg, with a street value estimated above
£1 million. [@1]

## Charged and remanded

Police said both men were charged with possession of cannabis with intent to supply. [@1]
They were remanded in custody and due to appear at Maidstone Crown Court on a date to be
arranged. [@1]
