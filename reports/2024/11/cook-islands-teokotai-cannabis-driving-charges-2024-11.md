Eric Greig Teokotai's case was before the Cook Islands High Court, where he was
facing charges of careless driving causing injury, EBA Blood and possession of
cannabis. [@1] The court told him to find a lawyer and seek legal advice because
of the seriousness of his case, and he was to do so before entering a plea. [@1]

His case was adjourned to November 21. [@1] His callover was one of seven
different criminal cases that were also adjourned by the High Court in the same
sitting. [@1]

He had not entered a plea when the case was put off, with his plea to follow
once he had spoken to a lawyer. [@1]
