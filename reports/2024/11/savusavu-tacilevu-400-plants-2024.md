The Savusavu Operations team raided a farming area in Savusavu on Tuesday 26 November 2024 and seized more than 400 plants believed to be marijuana, in what police described as part of a bigger attempt to target known cultivation areas. [@1,2] The Tacilevu farming area was named as the site of the raid. [@2] Police chief of operations Livai Driu said the owner of the farm was yet to be known as investigations continued. [@1]
