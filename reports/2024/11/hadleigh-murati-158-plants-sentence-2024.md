Officers from Suffolk Police's Sentinel South team raided a house in Ann Beaumont Way, Hadleigh, on 19 August 2024 and found 158 cannabis plants growing in three converted upstairs rooms and a divided loft. [@1,2] They caught Jani Murati, 37, of no fixed abode but from the Ipswich area, using a ladder to reach the loft space as they entered. [@1,2] The plants, at different stages of growth across five separate growing areas, pointed to a long-term harvesting operation. [@2]

## Sentence

Police assessed that the crop could have produced between 4.4kg and 13.2kg of cannabis, with a potential street value of between £17,000 and £80,000 if sold in kilogram quantities. [@1,2] Officers seized £538 in cash, a mobile phone and cultivation equipment alongside the plants, which were destroyed. [@1,2]

Murati admitted producing cannabis at Ipswich Magistrates' Court on 20 August and was sentenced to 10 months in jail at Ipswich Crown Court on 25 November. [@1,2]
