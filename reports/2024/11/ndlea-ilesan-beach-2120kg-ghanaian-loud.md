NDLEA operatives intercepted 2,120 kg of Ghanaian Loud, a strong strain of
cannabis the agency says is produced in Ghana, at Ilesan beach in Lagos on
Thursday 28 November 2024. [@1,2]

A suspect, Onibogi Muftau, was arrested in connection with the seizure, and
four vehicles meant to convey the shipment from the waterfront were recovered.
[@1,2]
