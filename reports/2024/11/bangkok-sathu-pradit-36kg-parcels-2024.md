Officers of the Office of the Narcotics Control Board (ONCB) arrested a British man and a Dutch man at a Bangkok condominium on 5 November 2024 after tracing a suspicious parcel sent from Britain. [@2] The parcel, seized on 4 November, held 210g of MDMA, and officers watched the two men collect it. [@1,2] A raid on their Sathu Pradit condominium found no drugs, but the men led officers to a rented house in Khlong Toei and another condominium in Phra Kanong. [@2] The house held 30kg of cannabis in vacuum-sealed bags ready for delivery, with documents for shipment to Britain mislabelled as pillows, plus about 1.4kg of cannabis glandular trichomes. [@1,2] The Phra Kanong flat held 6kg more cannabis in vacuum-sealed bags. [@2] The ONCB said British police had seized growing amounts of Thai cannabis that year and announced closer joint work against the route. [@1,2]
