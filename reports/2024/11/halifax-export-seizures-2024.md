The Canada Border Services Agency announced on 26 November 2024 that officers had seized 425 kg of suspected cannabis in three interceptions at ports of entry in Nova Scotia between 22 October and 14 November. [@1] On 22 October, officers examining a marine container destined for the Caribbean found 574 packages holding 347 kg of suspected cannabis and one package with about 1 kg of suspected hashish, and turned the evidence over to the RCMP Federal Serious and Organized Crime unit. [@1]

On 13 November, officers at Halifax Stanfield International Airport intercepted two suitcases with about 49 kg of suspected cannabis bound for London and arrested Quinn Alexander Cole for smuggling it out of Canada. [@1,2] Cole was charged under the Cannabis Act with exporting, possession for the purpose of selling and possession for the purpose of distributing, and was due in Dartmouth Provincial Court on 22 January 2025. [@1] On 14 November, officers at the same airport intercepted two more suitcases with about 30 kg bound for London and arrested Alexander James Mahar, who faces the same charges and was due in court on 28 January 2025. [@1]
