At least 50,800 marijuana plants worth P10.28 million were uprooted and
destroyed in Kalinga and Benguet provinces, law enforcement authorities said on
23 November 2024. [@1]

The Philippine National Police Drug Enforcement Group (PDEG) found about 38,000
plants on a 3,800-square-metre plantation in Barangay Buscalan, Tinglayan town,
on Friday 22 November. [@1] Police sprayed the site with herbicide to stop its
reuse, and PDEG director Brig. Gen. Eleazar Matta said the cultivators were
not present during the raid. [@1]

Separately in Bakun, Benguet, the Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency uprooted
12,800 marijuana plants and 3,050 seedlings valued at P2.68 million. [@1]
