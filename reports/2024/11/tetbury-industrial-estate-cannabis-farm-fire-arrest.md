A fire broke out at a suspected cannabis farm inside an industrial unit on the
Tetbury Industrial Estate on Cirencester Road at about 12:45 GMT. [@1] Three men
were seen running away from the fire, and one of them was detained nearby. [@1]
Gloucestershire police officers arrested a 26-year-old man on suspicion of drugs
manufacture and the production of a class B drug, namely cannabis. [@1] The
police said the fire had been extinguished, and officers stayed at the scene
overnight because extensive damage had made the building unsafe. [@1] Officers
searched for the two other men with a dog unit and the National Police Air
Service, and no one else had been found. [@1] Stroud Times reported that the two
other men fled across farmland and that no one else had been found. [@2] The two
men being sought were described as wearing a black hooded top with grey jogging
bottoms, and black shorts with a red hooded top. [@2]

Wiltshire 999s reported that more than 45 firefighters worked for several hours
to put out the blaze, which had flames coming from the roof. [@3]
Gloucestershire Fire and Rescue Service said nine fire engines attended from
Nailsworth, Stroud, Cirencester, Wotton-under-Edge, Cheltenham West, Gloucester
South and Fairford. [@3] Cirencester Road was closed until about 4pm and the
closures had since been lifted. [@3] The Wilts and Gloucestershire Standard
reported that an additional two fire engines were sent from Dorset and
Wiltshire. [@4] The fire service told residents to close their windows and doors
and to avoid the area. [@5] Police asked anyone with information to come
forward, quoting incident 218 of 15 November. [@4]
