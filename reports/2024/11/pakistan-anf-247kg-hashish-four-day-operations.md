Pakistan's Anti-Narcotics Force recovered more than 260 kg of drugs in operations across the country over four days, its spokesperson said in a statement on Saturday 30 November 2024. [@1,2] The haul comprised 247.3 kg of hashish, 5.049 kg of heroin, 10 kg of crystal meth and 83 g of weed, valued by the ANF at about 24.4 million rupees on the black market. [@1,2]

Operations on Kuchlak Road and Saryab Road in Quetta recovered 30 kg of hashish and led to six arrests. [@1,2] Officers also seized 176.4 kg of hashish hidden in a house in Saranan, Pishin district, and 32 kg near the coast in Gwadar, where a warehouse holding 5 kg of heroin and 5 kg of crystal meth was uncovered. [@1,2]

Fourteen suspects were arrested across the operations and cases were registered against them. [@1,2]
