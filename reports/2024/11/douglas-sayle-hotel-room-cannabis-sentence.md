Ethan Sayle, 22, was sentenced at Douglas Courthouse for cannabis he had kept in
a hotel room on the Isle of Man. [@1] He received a 14-month prison sentence
suspended for two years. [@1] Deemster Graeme Cook said he had been "very
foolish". [@1] The deemster, the Isle of Man's judge, said he saw "good reason"
to suspend the sentence because Sayle was young, had a job and was drug-free.
[@1] Sayle pleaded guilty to possession of cannabis with intent to supply,
possession of ketamine, driving while disqualified and driving without
insurance. [@1] Sayle, of Clifton Terrace in Douglas, was also ordered to pay
£500 in court costs. [@1]

Police stopped him at about 01:00 on 12 May 2023 as he drove a black BMW on Peel
Road because of a faulty light. [@1] He was arrested when officers found £25 of
ketamine and the hotel room key in his car. [@1] Officers found two wraps of
white powder in the vehicle along with the key to the room he had been staying
in at the Palace Hotel in Douglas. [@1] Hotel management searched the room and
found drug paraphernalia and £4,364 worth of cannabis. [@1] The court heard the
cannabis found in the room was worth £4,364. [@1]

Sayle was stopped again in September 2024 after officers detected a smell of
cannabis in his car. [@1] No drugs were found in the vehicle, and he was
arrested for driving while disqualified and driving without insurance after
being banned from the roads in November 2023. [@1] His defence advocate told the
court Sayle was now "drug-free" and that his "chaotic lifestyle has stopped".
