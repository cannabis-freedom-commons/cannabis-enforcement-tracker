Spain's Civil Guard seized 4,723 kg of hashish from two inflatable boats
intercepted between the islands of Gran Canaria and Fuerteventura on 2
November 2024, acting on intelligence from Morocco's Directorate of
Territorial Surveillance. [@1,2] A maritime surveillance system located the
boats sailing without lights about 80 km off the coast, carrying 131 bales
of hashish with two people aboard each boat. [@1,2]

The four crew members were arrested and taken with the drugs to Las Palmas de
Gran Canaria, where a court ordered all four held in custody on 4 November.
[@1] Morocco's DGST provided the intelligence behind the interception, in
what the reports describe as close police cooperation between the two
countries. [@1,2] The operation followed an expected November attempt to land
Moroccan shipments on Gran Canaria's coast. [@1]
