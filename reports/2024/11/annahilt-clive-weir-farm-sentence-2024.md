Clive Weir, from the Annahilt area, was handed a three-year sentence at Craigavon Crown
Court: 18 months in prison and 18 months on licence. [@1,2] He had pleaded guilty to
cultivating cannabis and to using and converting criminal property. [@1,2]

On Friday 3 February 2023, detectives investigating organised drug crime searched Weir's
address and found a substantial cannabis farm in a silo a few yards from his back door.
[@1,2] Two men, Anh Nguyen and Quy Nguyen, were found living in a caravan inside the
building and were arrested; both were later jailed for a year in prison and a year on
licence for cultivation of cannabis. [@1,2] Specialist search teams needed four days to
dismantle the farm because of its size and complexity. [@1,2]

Weir was not at the property when the farm was found but was later interviewed about his
involvement. [@2] Police said the quantities suggested an operation capable of making
millions of pounds over several harvests, with the hallmarks of organised criminal gang
involvement. [@2]
