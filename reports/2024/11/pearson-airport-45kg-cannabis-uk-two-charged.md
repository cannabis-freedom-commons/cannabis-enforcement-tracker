Two women were arrested at Toronto Pearson International Airport after border
officers found cannabis in their luggage before scheduled flights from Toronto
to the U.K. in November 2024. [@1] Krystle Lee Paula Cormack, 40, was arrested
and charged with export contrary to section 11(2) of the Cannabis Act over 21.95
kilograms of cannabis. [@3] Channelle Monique Atherton, 32, was arrested and
charged with export contrary to section 11(2) of the Cannabis Act over 22.5
kilograms of cannabis. [@3] Both accused were released on an undertaking and
were expected to appear at the A. Grenville and William Davis Courthouse in
Brampton on February 12, 2025. [@3]

Canada Border Services Agency officers found 45 kilograms of cannabis in their
luggage during a baggage check at Toronto Pearson International Airport before
their scheduled flight to London on November 14. [@1] The RCMP put the street
value of the cannabis at approximately $175,000. [@2] The cannabis was seized
and the travellers were arrested following the discovery. [@2]

Outbound baggage examinations uncovered two separate cases of cannabis concealed
in luggage. [@3] The RCMP and the CBSA cautioned travellers that it is illegal
to bring any amount of cannabis onto any international flight. [@3]
