Uganda Police raided cannabis growers, sellers and smokers in Mbale, Wakiso
and Iganga districts between 21 and 23 November 2024, arresting 16 suspects
and destroying a cannabis garden. [@1,2]

On 21 November, police found a cannabis garden allegedly owned by 52-year-old
Oguti Robert at Nangirima Upper village in Mbale District, destroyed the
plants and arrested him. [@1,2] The following day, officers raided a bar in
Gogonya, Wakiso District, around 11:00 AM: the owner fled, while Nakawunde
Lydia and Kawoya were caught smoking marijuana. [@1,2] The bar was suspected
of distributing marijuana under the guise of a legitimate establishment and
was found holding significant stocks of the drug. [@1,2]

In Iganga District, police arrested twelve suspects in Busei A and Nampirika.
[@1,2] On 23 November, an intelligence-led operation in Bengazi Ghetto in
Kyengera Town Council, Wakiso District, detained 42-year-old Kyalimpa Evelyn,
who was found with a sack of marijuana rolls and confessed to her involvement.
[@1,2]

Police spokesperson ACP Kituuma Rusoke announced the operations at a press
briefing at police headquarters in Naguru on 25 November. [@2] No charges or
court outcomes for the 16 suspects have been reported. [@1,2]
