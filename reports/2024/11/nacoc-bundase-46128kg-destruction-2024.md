Ghana's Narcotics Control Commission (NACOC) destroyed 151,899.54 kg of illicit drugs
at the Bundase Military Camp in the Ningo-Prampram District on Friday 8 November
2024. [@1,2] The stocks, seized from suspects between 2021 and 2024, included
46,128.29 kg of cannabis alongside codeine, heroin, cocaine, speedball, meth and
MDMA. [@1,2]

The supervised burning involved the Environmental Protection Agency, the police, the
military, national security officers and the Ghana Standards Authority. [@2] NACOC
Director-General Kenneth Adu-Amanfoh said destroying the drugs was an essential step
to protect citizens and showed the commission's commitment to the rule of law. [@1,2]
