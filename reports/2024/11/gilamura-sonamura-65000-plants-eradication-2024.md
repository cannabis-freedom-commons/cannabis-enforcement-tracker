The Border Security Force, working with Tripura Police and forest officials,
destroyed about 65,000 mature ganja plants spread over 10 hectares near
Gilamura village on 13 November 2024. [@1,2] Acting on specific information
about cultivation in the forest area of the village under Sonamura police
station in Sepahijala district, the joint team cut, uprooted and burned the
mature crop. [@1,2] The BSF said it was working with sister agencies toward a
drug-free society. [@1]
