Law-enforcement officers in Gaoua seized more than 5,000 litres of contraband
alcohol and 90 kilogrammes of chanvre indien, the French term for cannabis. [@1]
The operation was carried out with the support of the Groupement des Compagnies
Républicaines de Sécurité (GCRS) in Gaoua. [@1]

Authorities put the total value of the intercepted products at 25 million CFA
francs and said they were intended for the local market ahead of the year-end
holidays. [@1] Investigations uncovered a traffickers' network operating from
the commune of Malba and supplying artisanal gold-mining sites around Gaoua,
Kampti and Galgouli. [@1] Police said the success came from close collaboration
with the local population and thanked residents for their vigilance and
contribution to fighting illicit trafficking. [@1]

The operation was presented as showing the determination of Burkina Faso's
security forces to combat crime and protect public health. [@1] Authorities said
the operation should deter traffickers and strengthen security in the region.
