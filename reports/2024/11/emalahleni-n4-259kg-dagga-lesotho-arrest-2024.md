Mpumalanga police arrested a 49-year-old Lesotho national during Operation
Shanela after finding dagga in his bakkie on the N4 near Witbank. [@1] Members
of the Middelburg flying squad unit stopped the white Toyota Hilux double cab on
the N4 Road between the Middelburg toll gate and the Nelson Mandela Drive
off-ramp near Witbank on Thursday. [@1] A search of the vehicle found 13 bags of
dagga weighing about 259 kilograms, which police valued at about R260,000 on the
street. [@1] The driver, the vehicle's only occupant, was arrested and charged
with possession of dagga and dealing in dagga. [@1] He was expected to appear
before the eMalahleni Magistrate's Court on Monday. [@1] The arrest was made on
Thursday 14 November 2024, and the man was due in the eMalahleni Magistrate's
Court on Monday 18 November. [@3]

Mpumalanga provincial police spokesperson Brigadier Donald Mdhluli gave the
account of the stop and search. [@1] Acting provincial commissioner Major
General Zeph Mkhwanazi commended the Middelburg flying squad members for their
vigilance and dedication. [@1] A second account of the police statement says the
officers spotted the bakkie during a patrol and that its rear left tyre seemed
to be flat. [@2] It records the same 13 bags, about 259 kg and a street value of
roughly R260,000, and the same charge of possession of and dealing in dagga.
