Customs officers searched two young women arriving at Roland-Garros airport in
Saint-Denis, La Réunion, on a flight from Paris on Sunday 10 November 2024, and
arrested them. [@1]

Officers found 17 kg of cannabis resin and 6 kg of cocaine carried on the two
women, whom the reports describe as mules, the couriers of drug-trafficking
networks. [@1] The resale value of the seized substances was estimated at
900,000 euros. [@1] If the weight is confirmed, the seizure would be one of the
largest drug hauls ever made on La Réunion. [@1]

Réunion La 1ère reports that the two women were handed to the drugs unit of the
Saint-Denis judicial police, the STPJ. [@2] They were brought before the
Saint-Denis court on Wednesday 13 November 2024 for the opening of a judicial
investigation and a likely remand in custody. [@1] The Saint-Denis prosecutor's
office opened a judicial investigation, and the two were remanded in custody
while the investigation continues. [@4]

Free Dom describes the two as young Réunionnaises in their twenties, while the
other accounts do not say where they are from. [@4] Imaz Press, reporting as the
arrests were confirmed, put the cannabis resin at about fifteen kilos, below the
17 kg published by Réunion La 1ère, Ouest-France and Free Dom. [@3]
