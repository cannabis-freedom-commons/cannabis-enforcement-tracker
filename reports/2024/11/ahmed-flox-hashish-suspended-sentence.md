Egyptian actor Ahmed Flox was linked in recent years to two criminal cases, one
alleging insult, defamation and harassment of basketball player Yasmin Ezzat and
the other alleging drug possession for consumption. [@1] In November 2024, he
faced a separate case of possessing hashish for consumption after a cigarette
containing the drug was found in his possession. [@1] He was caught while
authorities were carrying out procedures to enforce the prison sentence issued
in the other case, and the drug-possession incident was then sent to trial on a
charge of possessing drugs for consumption. [@1]

The case was registered as No. 13886 of 2023 with the Matrouh police station
felonies, after the Matrouh security directorate received a report that Flox had
been caught while wanted in another case with hashish for consumption in his
possession. [@2] A security force went to where he was staying inside a tourist
village and, on catching him as a precaution, found a cigarette box holding
several cigarettes, one of which contained hashish. [@2] He admitted that he
possessed it for consumption, a report of the incident was filed, and the
prosecution referred him to court. [@3]

The Matrouh Criminal Court of Appeal upheld the judgment against Flox of six
months' imprisonment with suspension of execution and a fine of 10,000 Egyptian
pounds in the hashish-possession case. [@2]
