Niger's national police announced in a statement dated 17 January 2024 that
officers had seized large quantities of drugs in Konni department, in the Tahoua
region, and in the Zinder region. [@1] An operation by the Konni branch of
Niger's Central Office for the Suppression of Illicit Drug Trafficking (OCRTIS)
led officers to arrest a man at his home in Konni department, where the search
turned up 1.035 kg of cannabis. [@1] The same search found 294.5 tramadol
tablets, 55 diazepam D5 tablets and 251 exol tablets, none of them cannabis.
[@1] Investigators said the man was the local receiver for an international
cannabis network whose main supplier worked at Amarawa, near Illela in Nigeria,
with another branch at Kalmalo in Nigeria, delivering to customers in Konni.
[@1]

Three Nigerian nationals were arrested at Illela on 12 January 2024 carrying
17.795 kg of cannabis on a motorcycle towards Konni. [@1] The cannabis was
hidden among sacks holding cans of smuggled fuel to deceive the defence and
security forces along the border. [@1]

In a separate case in the Zinder region, the OCRTIS regional service arrested
four Nigerien nationals found with cannabis. [@1] The first of the four had 45
balls weighing 23.555 kg intended for sale at Djado, the second was carrying 275
grams of cannabis, and the other two had 67 paper cones weighing 420 grams. [@1]
The first two were arrested after police received information in the Gangaran
Djida neighbourhood of Zinder, and the other two were arrested at the barrier.
[@1]
