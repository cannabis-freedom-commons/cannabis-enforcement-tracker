Two people who had entered Nicaragua from Honduras on a motorcycle fled when
they detected army troops in the community of Santa Rosa, in the municipality of
Jalapa, department of Nueva Segovia, abandoning the load they were carrying.
[@1] The seizure was carried out on 14 January 2024 by the Army's 1st Regional
Military Command during an operational service patrol. [@1]

The abandoned load was a white sack containing 33 packages of marijuana inside.
[@1] The Army said the operation was carried out under the state's “Muro de
Contención” (Containment Wall) strategy. [@1] The pair had entered the country
through an unauthorized crossing point. [@1] The seized drug was handed over to
the corresponding authorities. [@1]

The Army's 1st Regional Military Command page records the same seizure, with the
same place, date, sack and package count. [@2] The troops complied with the
health ministry's COVID-19 protection measures during the seizure. [@1] No
arrests were made at the scene because both carriers escaped. [@1]
