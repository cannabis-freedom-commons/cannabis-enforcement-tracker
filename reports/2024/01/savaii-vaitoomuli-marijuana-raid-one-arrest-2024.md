Samoa Police, Prisons and Corrections Services arrested and charged a
34-year-old man after a drug raid in Savai'i on Tuesday 9 January 2024. [@1] A
police media release said the raid was led by officers of the Vaitoomuli
outpost. [@1] According to the release, police seized fresh marijuana plants,
containers filled with dried marijuana leaves, marijuana cigarettes and dried
marijuana plants from the property. [@1]

The suspect was remanded in custody until 6 February 2024 for mention, a court
listing at which a case is called without a plea. [@1] Radio Samoa named the
village as Vailoa Palauli in Savai'i and said the man would face charges of
possessing and growing marijuana in court on 6 February. [@2]

The police commissioner could not confirm the volumes of drugs confiscated. [@1]

No mention was made of whether cash was found on the premises. [@1]
