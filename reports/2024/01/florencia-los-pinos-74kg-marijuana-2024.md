The National Police seized about 74 kilograms of marijuana in 149 packages found
in a car abandoned in the Los Pinos neighbourhood of Florencia, Caquetá. [@1]
The car was abandoned in Los Pinos, a neighbourhood of Florencia, the capital of
Caquetá department. [@1]

The drugs were hidden inside a private vehicle whose driver fled when he noticed
the police checkpoints. [@1] Officers from the National Community Policing Model
were checking people and vehicles near the El Cunduy prison when they saw a
vehicle turn around and not continue its journey. [@1] The officers began a
pursuit and located the abandoned vehicle in the Los Pinos neighbourhood. [@1]
On inspection they found several packages wrapped in black bags containing
pressed marijuana. [@1]

The immobilised vehicle and the seized marijuana were placed at the disposal of
the Prosecutor's Office of the URI of Florence, the Immediate Reaction Unit.
[@1] The case was opened for the crime of manufacturing, trafficking and
possession of narcotics. [@1] Police said the action prevented the sale and
consumption of more than 74,000 doses of the substance. [@1]

The police account reports no arrest and does not identify the driver who fled.
