California Department of Cannabis Control officers served a search warrant at
Gro More Garden Supply on Clovis Avenue in Fresno, just north of Clinton Avenue.
[@1] The raid was carried out on the morning of Wednesday 10 January 2024, and
officers were seen carrying bags and boxes of marijuana out of the rear
warehouse of the building, which held several suites. [@1] Officers from the
California Department of Fish and Wildlife and the Fresno Police Department were
also at the scene. [@1]

The city seized 811 unlicensed marijuana plants, Fresno City Attorney Andrew
Janz announced. [@2] In all, 526 pounds (about 238.6 kilograms) of unlicensed
cannabis was seized, which police valued at an estimated $868,725. [@1] The city
also seized hundreds of pounds of illegal cannabis. [@2] Pacific Gas and
Electric shut off power to the building because of electrical hazards and code
violations, including unpermitted modifications. [@2]

The operation was run by the city's code enforcement team with the Fresno Police
Department, the Department of Cannabis Control, the Department of Fish and
Wildlife and the Department of Tax and Fee Administration. [@2]

A neighbouring business owner said the block had smelled of "skunk weed for a
very long time" and that she was not surprised by the raid. [@1]
