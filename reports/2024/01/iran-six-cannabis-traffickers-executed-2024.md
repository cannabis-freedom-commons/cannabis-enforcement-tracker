Three were hanged at a prison in the northwestern province of Ardabil for buying
and transporting heroin and opium. [@1] The other six were executed separately
on trafficking charges naming methamphetamine, heroin and cannabis. [@1] The
accounts were attributed to Iran's official IRNA news agency. [@1]

Iran lies on a major opium-smuggling route between Afghanistan and Europe and
has one of the world's highest rates of domestic opiate use. [@1] Iranian
authorities have launched multiple campaigns against drug abuse and trafficking
and regularly announce large seizures of opiates smuggled from neighboring
Afghanistan. [@1] Iran says executions are carried out only after exhaustive
legal proceedings and are a necessary deterrent against drug trafficking. [@1]

Amnesty International reported that Iranian authorities had executed at least
173 people convicted of drug-related offenses during the first five months of
2023. [@1] Amnesty says Iran executes more people per year than any other nation
except China. [@1] The Norway-based Iran Human Rights group said that Iran had
executed more than 700 people in 2023, the highest figure in eight years. [@1]
