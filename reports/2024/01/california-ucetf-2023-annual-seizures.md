In 2023 the state's Unified Cannabis Enforcement Taskforce (UCETF) seized
189,854.02 pounds (about 86.1 tonnes) of unlicensed cannabis and eradicated
317,834 plants across California, its first full calendar year of operation.
[@1,2] Officers served 188 search warrants, seized 119 illegally possessed
firearms and took $223,809 in cash, valuing the cannabis products at
$312,880,014.35. [@1]

In the fourth quarter alone the task force served 24 warrants and seized
13,393.65 pounds of cannabis worth about $22.3 million, alongside 20,320
plants, 26 firearms and $35,195 in cash. [@1,2] Alameda County led the year at
$77.8 million seized, followed by Siskiyou, Mendocino, Los Angeles and Kern
counties. [@1,2]

Governor Gavin Newsom created UCETF in 2022 to align state enforcement and
increase coordination between state, local and federal partners. [@1,2] It is
co-chaired by the Department of Cannabis Control and the Department of Fish
and Wildlife and draws on more than 20 state agencies and departments. [@1]
