On 24 January 2024, gendarmes from Tubuai seized 298 cannabis plants in the
commune of Mataura. [@1] The gendarmerie of French Polynesia announced the
operation on social networks as a new seizure of paka at Tubuai in Mataura. [@1]
Four individuals were implicated in connection with the seizure of the 298
plants. [@1]

The operation was carried out by gendarmes from Tubuai in Mataura. [@1] The
account gives the seizure total as 298 pieds of cannabis, the French term for
cannabis plants. [@1] The same account names Mataura as the commune where the
plants were found and seized. [@1]

In the same update, the gendarmerie said it had already seized and destroyed 848
cannabis plants in total since the start of 2024. [@1] That 848-plant figure was
presented as the cumulative French Polynesia total, not as the Mataura seizure
alone. [@1] The Mataura seizure was described as a new seizure within that
ongoing 2024 enforcement activity. [@1] Paka is the term the gendarmerie used in
its announcement for the plants seized at Mataura. [@1]
