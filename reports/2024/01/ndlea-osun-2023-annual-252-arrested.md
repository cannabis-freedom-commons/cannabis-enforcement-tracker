NDLEA Osun State Commander Nnadi Chidi said the command arrested 252
suspects, 214 males and 38 females, and seized 1,909.692 kg of illicit drugs
between January and December 2023. [@1]

The command secured the convictions of 57 accused persons, sentenced to
between three months and five years in prison, with 47 cases still
pending. [@1]

Drug supply reduction efforts led to the discovery and destruction of 9.5
hectares of cannabis farms holding 23.759 tonnes of cannabis sativa in
Ifedayo and Oriade Local Government Areas. [@1,2] The command also gave
brief counselling to 79 people who use drugs and ran War Against Drug Abuse
sensitisation in religious houses, schools, motor parks, markets and
workplaces. [@1,2] Chidi rejected claims that Osun was the second-highest
cannabis-producing state in the South West, saying the National Bureau of
Statistics 2018 report ranked it fifth for drug use and cultivation. [@1]
