A court in Bella Unión sentenced a 28-year-old man over an unlicensed cannabis
plantation and harvest on his property in Artigas department, the Interior
Ministry said. [@1]

Inspectors from the IRCCA, Uruguay's cannabis regulation and control institute,
went to a property on Ruta 30 and found cannabis plants, 14 sacks of harvested
branches with flower to process and buds packed in sacks ready to be
transported. [@1] With the Bella Unión prosecutor's office, the anti-drug
brigade, a tactical reserve group and forensic police then raided the property
and detained one adult man. [@1] Officers seized 28 cannabis plants and 11 sacks
holding branches with flowers for processing, along with a .38 revolver with a
holster, four cartridges, two spent cases, an iPhone, a Ceibal laptop with
charger, five security cameras with a DVR and a hard drive. [@1]

The single first-instance judge in Bella Unión convicted the man, identified
only as G.M.B.C., of a preparatory act of the crime of drug trafficking in the
form of supply, together with unlawful possession and carrying of a firearm.
[@1] He was sentenced to 24 months in prison, of which three months are
effective and the remaining 21 months are to be served under probation,
Uruguay's libertad a prueba regime. [@1] The probation conditions require him to
keep a supervised address, report once a week to police for 21 months and carry
out community service one day a week for six months, spending the first three
months confined to the home around the clock under police control. [@1] El
Pueblo Digital of Salto published the same Artigas police statement the
following day. [@2]
