Paul Rowan, of Garston in Liverpool, posted two parcels containing cocaine and
cannabis to the Isle of Man in 2020. [@1] He was jailed for 10 years and eight
months at Douglas Courthouse on 26 January 2024 after admitting two counts of
importing cocaine and one of importing cannabis. [@1] The case was heard at the
island's Court of General Gaol Delivery, where Isle of Man judges known as
deemsters pass sentence. [@3]

Customs staff intercepted the first parcel at the island's postal sorting office
in Braddan on 13 March 2020, and it held 984.9 grams of cannabis and 56.4 grams
of cocaine. [@2] The BBC puts the street value of that cannabis at £19,698 and
of the cocaine at £5,460. [@1] A second parcel intercepted on 14 July 2020 held
£6,350 of cocaine concealed inside an adapted book. [@1] Isle of Man Today
reported that the charges were framed as being concerned in importing cocaine
and cannabis, and that the cocaine amounts were 56.4 and 63.5 grams. [@3]

His fingerprints on the outside of the packages, and calls to the sorting office
from numbers that claimed to belong to different people, linked both parcels to
him. [@1] He was arrested in the United Kingdom in November 2023 on a warrant
and brought back to the Isle of Man. [@1] The court accepted a basis of plea
that he owed £4,000 to Liverpool drug dealers and had been threatened into
posting the parcels, and his car was set alight after he refused to send a
third. [@3] His advocate, Jim Travers, said he had been the "muggins with all
the risk on his shoulders". [@3] The BBC reported his advocate saying he had
been the "one in danger" because of threats from people further up the chain.
[@1]

Deemster Graeme Cook jailed him for five years and six months for the first
offence and five years and two months for the second, making 10 years and eight
months in total. [@3] Rowan appealed, and in July 2024 the appeal court ruled
the sentence "manifestly excessive" and cut it to eight years. [@4] The appeal
judges said the total had to reflect the criminality, and that hiding the drugs
in a hollowed-out book had not been sophisticated. [@4] In March 2024 it was
reported that he had lodged an appeal against the sentence. [@5]
