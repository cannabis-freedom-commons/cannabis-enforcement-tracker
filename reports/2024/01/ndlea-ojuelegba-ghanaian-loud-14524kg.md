Officers of Nigeria's National Drug Law Enforcement Agency (NDLEA) trailed two trucks
and a J5 bus loaded with jumbo bags of Ghanaian Loud, a potent strain of cannabis, and
stopped them at Ojuelegba in Lagos in the early hours of Sunday 28 January 2024. [@1,2]
The three vehicles together held 14,524.8 kilogrammes of cannabis smuggled in from
Ghana. [@1,2]

Two drivers jumped from the moving vehicles as officers blocked them, while the third,
66-year-old Nasiru Ojomu, was arrested. [@1,2] An NDLEA photograph of the arrest shows
Ojomu beside the seized bags with a placard giving the place as Ojuelegba Bridge in
Lagos and the date as 28 January 2024. [@2] The agency said Ojomu worked for Suleiman
Jimoh, a wanted Mushin-based drug baron also known as Temo, and that several earlier
shipments worth billions of naira had been linked to Jimoh, who had gone into hiding
as the manhunt continued. [@1,2]

The seizure was announced in a statement by NDLEA spokesman Femi Babafemi on
4 February, and agency chairman Buba Marwa commended the Lagos command and other
formations for the week's arrests and seizures. [@1,2]
