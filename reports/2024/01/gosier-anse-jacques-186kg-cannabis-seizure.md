On the night of 13 to 14 January 2024, Guadeloupe's Direction Territoriale de la
Police Nationale (DTPN), the territorial police directorate, set up surveillance
on the beaches of Le Gosier after a tip from the maritime gendarmerie and
customs. [@1] At 1:15 a.m. on 14 January, officers of the BST (Brigade
Spécialisée de Terrain), a specialist field unit, and the BAC (Brigade
Anti-Criminalité), an anti-crime brigade, arrested six people on Anse à Jacques
beach in Le Gosier. [@1] Officers caught the six in the act of landing drugs
from a boat, which police seized with its cargo. [@2]

Police reported seizing 186 kilograms of cannabis herb in the operation. [@1]
The same cargo included 10.9 kilograms of cocaine, mentioned here only as
context and excluded from cannabis figures. [@2] The OFAST detachment (Office
Anti-Stupéfiants), the French anti-drug office, of the Territorial Judicial
Police Service took over the investigation. [@1]

Police described the seizure as the second large drug seizure by police services
since the start of 2024. [@1] Eight days earlier, in the night of 5 to 6
January, 366 kilograms of cocaine had been seized on Petit Havre beach in Le
Gosier and several people arrested. [@1] That earlier case was transferred to
the Juridiction Interrégionale Spécialisée in Fort-de-France, the interregional
specialist court for serious organised crime. [@1]
