One resident was detained in connection with the drug seizure. [@2] He lived in
Eski Jomboy mahalla, Jomboy district, and was identified as A.A., born in 1983.
[@2] Samarkand regional law-enforcement authorities searched his home and
auxiliary household rooms in the courtyard during an operation in Jomboy
district. [@2]

The search found 31 kilograms 855 grams of marijuana, equivalent to 31.855 kg,
and the authorities formally documented the substances discovered there. [@2]
The marijuana was accompanied by 2 grams of opium and two psychotropic tablets;
those separately identified non-cannabis substances are excluded from the
cannabis weight. [@2] Gazeta.uz reported 31.8 kg of marijuana from the same
house and household premises, alongside the 2 grams of opium and two
psychotropic tablets. [@1] UZA's account gave the more precise amount as 31
kilograms 855 grams, listing it as marijuana and the opium and tablets
separately. [@2]

The seizure figure therefore records marijuana alone, rather than a combined
weight for all substances found in the home. [@2]
