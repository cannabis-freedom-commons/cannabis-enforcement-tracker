On 23 January 2024, joint personnel of the National Narcotics Agency (BNN),
the military and the police burned a marijuana field in Teupin Rusep village,
Sawang district, North Aceh. [@1,2]

Officers found about 22,000 ready-to-harvest plants spread over 2 hectares,
which they estimated at 10 tonnes, and destroyed the crop by burning. [@1,2]
The captions name no arrests. [@1,2]
