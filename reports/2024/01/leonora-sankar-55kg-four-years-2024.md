Ranks of the Customs Anti Narcotic Unit (CANU) arrested two people on 15
January 2024 after finding 55.2 kg of cannabis at Block 1 Unity, Hubu Parika
on the East Bank of Essequibo. [@1] The discovery of several large bags
containing brick-like parcels was made during an operation at the location.
[@1,2]

Those held were Latchman Sankar, known as Jack, 39, and Sanya Ramrattan, known
as Shena, 36, of Sisters Village, West Bank Demerara. [@1,2] Officers escorted
both to CANU headquarters with the narcotic, which tested positive for
cannabis and amounted to 102 parcels with a street value of about G$20
million. [@1,2] Ramrattan was later released without being charged. [@2]

Three days later Sankar appeared at the Leonora Magistrate's Court before
Magistrate Zamilla Ally-Seepaul and admitted having more than 55 kg of
cannabis in his possession. [@2] He was sentenced to four years' imprisonment
and fined $49.6 million. [@2]
