A joint force of Sepahijala district police, the 11th Battalion of the Tripura
State Rifles and KTDS and ACRRTC units destroyed about 84,000 immature ganja
plants across 42 acres (about 17 hectares) of forest land on 2 January 2024.
[@1]

The plants were uprooted and destroyed on the spot in a drive against illegal
cultivation in the district. [@1]
