Detectives from the Area 3 Narcotics Division seized over 500 pounds of ganja in
Slipe and Clandestine districts, St Elizabeth, on Wednesday 31 January 2024.
[@1] At about midday, lawmen searched the two districts. [@1] In Slipe, part of
the ganja was found in a house occupied by a man, and the other part was later
found in bushes in Clandestine. [@1]

A 38-year-old farmer of Slipe district, Alton McPherson, was arrested after a
question-and-answer session in the presence of his attorney. [@1] McPherson was
charged with possession of and dealing in ganja. [@1] His court date had not
been finalised. [@1]

Over 500 pounds is about 227 kilograms. [@1]
