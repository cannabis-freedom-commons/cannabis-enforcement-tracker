Rivera departmental police stopped a Paraguayan-registered pickup at the Manuel
Díaz border crossing between Rivera and Tacuarembó at about 19:00 on Sunday 21
January 2024. [@2] The two occupants, Paraguayan men aged 33 and 51, were
detained. [@2] Police said the pickup had entered Uruguay from Paraguay over the
Quaraí bridge in Artigas and then taken route 30 towards Montevideo, and that it
had not gone through the migration controls. [@1]

The Interior Ministry put the haul at 139 kilograms of pressed marijuana. [@2]
Police said the drug was packed in 239 bricks placed above the engine and in the
double bottom of the pickup's luggage compartment. [@1] The Interior Ministry
said the men admitted under questioning that the load was to be handed over in
Playa Pascual, in San José department, and that they had skipped the migration
checkpoints for that reason. [@2] Teledoce reported that Rivera police
headquarters said the drug came packed in 239 bricks above the engine and in the
double bottom of the luggage compartment of a Paraguayan-registered pickup. [@3]
Dnegocios, citing the Interior Ministry, reported the same stop at the Manuel
Díaz crossing and the same sentence of two years and six months for the two men.
[@4]

Both men were convicted as the perpetrators of possession, importation and
transport of prohibited narcotic substances. [@2] Both were sentenced to two
years and six months in prison. [@2] They were taken to the prosecutor's office
for the legal proceedings, and the Rivera police account names prosecutor
Alejandra Domínguez on the case. [@1] The Interior Ministry said the men had
avoided the migration checkpoints and were handed to the courts, which imposed
the sentence. [@4]
