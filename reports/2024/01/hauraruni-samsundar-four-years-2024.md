Customs Anti-Narcotic Unit officers raided a cannabis farm at Hauraruni
Village off the Soesdyke-Linden Highway on 25 January 2024 and arrested
Nazelena Alli, 33, and Nishart Samsundar, 20, both of Timehri North, East
Bank Demerara. [@1,2]

At two camps the officers found about 200 cannabis plants and some 5,000
nursery plants, seized 1.976 kg of cannabis and a 12-gauge cartridge, and
destroyed the plants together with about 100 pounds of leaves, seeds and
stems. [@1,2]

On 31 January Samsundar appeared before Magistrate Scarce at the Diamond
Magistrate's Court and pleaded guilty to possession of narcotics for the
purpose of trafficking and to cultivating a prohibited plant. [@2] He was
sentenced to four years' imprisonment and fined G$1,778,400 for the
trafficking offence and four years for cultivation, the terms to run
concurrently. [@2]
