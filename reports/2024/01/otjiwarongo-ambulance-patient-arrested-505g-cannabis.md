A 38-year-old male patient was arrested near Otjiwarongo after police found
cannabis in his possession while he was being transported in an ambulance. [@1]
The man was travelling from Windhoek to Outjo in the ambulance, which was
stopped and searched. [@1]

Police said 505 grams of cannabis valued at N$25 250 was found in his
possession. [@1] The suspect was expected to appear in the Otjiwarongo
Magistrate’s Court on Monday. [@1]
