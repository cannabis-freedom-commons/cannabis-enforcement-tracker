Customs investigators arrested a 38-year-old woman on Thursday evening 18
January 2024 as she arrived at Johan Adolf Pengel airport on a KLM flight from
the Netherlands. [@1,2,3] The woman, who lives in France, was stopped by the
Douanerecherche at Zanderij with a suitcase stuffed with hashish. [@1,2,3] A
thorough search of her checked baggage uncovered more than 25 kg of hashish,
which officers seized on the spot. [@1,2,3]

After the discovery the passenger was handed over to the Narcotica Brigade,
which took up the investigation. [@1,2,3] Following consultation with the
Public Prosecution Service she was remanded in custody, the police confirmed.
[@1,2,3]
