On 29 February 2024, officers of the Bobo-Dioulasso Mobile Customs Brigade
seized 740 slabs of chanvre indien, the French term for cannabis, during a road
check. [@2] The cannabis was hidden in specially prepared hiding places inside a
seven-tonne vehicle stopped on the Bobo-Ouaga axis. [@2] Burkina Faso customs
communication likewise described 740 slabs concealed in a well-prepared hiding
place in a seven-tonne vehicle on the Bobo-Dioulasso–Ouaga axis. [@3]

Customs valued the seized cannabis at 52,500,000 CFA francs. [@1] The drugs were
concealed inside the walls of the vehicles and were found through the vigilance
and professionalism of customs investigators acting on intelligence. [@1]

The Director General of Customs congratulated the Western region customs
hierarchy and the Bobo-Dioulasso Mobile Brigade staff for the operations. [@1]
Director General Adama Ilboudo called for closer cooperation between the public
and customs officers to strengthen the fight against terrorism and ensure public
safety. [@1] On the same axis on 26 February 2024 the same brigade had
separately seized 590 kg of amphetamine valued at 6,320,000 CFA francs, which is
not counted in the cannabis figures. [@2]
