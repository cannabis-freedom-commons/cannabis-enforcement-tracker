Cairo detectives arrested 13 alleged drug traders in districts across the
capital before the drugs could be passed to users. [@2] The Cairo security
directorate described the action as an expanded security campaign against
traders in narcotic substances and pills. [@1] Those confronted admitted
possessing the seized drugs to trade and keeping firearms and bladed weapons to
defend their activity, and police said legal procedures were taken. [@1]

In al-Zawiya al-Hamra, officers seized 10 kg of hashish with one man, along with
a pump-action shotgun and two cartridges. [@1] In al-Sharabiya, officers seized
1 kg of hashish with one man, along with a bladed weapon described as a matwa.
[@1] In al-Shorouk, officers seized 1 kg of hashish with two men, while another
man was held with an unspecified quantity of hashish and heroin. [@1] In
al-Matariya, officers seized 1 kg of hashish with one man, and in al-Zawiya
al-Hamra they seized 1.2 kg of hashish with another man. [@1] In Nasr City
Third, officers seized 4 kg of hydro with one man, alongside ice, a bladed
weapon and a precision scale. [@1] In al-Maadi, officers seized 1 kg of hashish
with two men. [@1] The separately stated cannabis seizures total 19.2 kg of
hashish and hydro. [@2]

The same sweep also produced non-cannabis seizures that are not counted here,
including 2 kg of ice in Dar al-Salam, other ice quantities, firearms, bladed
weapons and 12,030 Parkinol pills. [@1] The headline total of 25 kg covers all
drugs together, so it is not recorded as cannabis, and voodoo is excluded as a
synthetic cannabinoid. [@1]
