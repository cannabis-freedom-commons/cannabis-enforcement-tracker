Penobscot County sheriff's deputies raided an illegal marijuana grow operation
in Corinna on the morning of Friday, 9 February 2024. [@1] Deputies acted after
neighbours complained of being pressured to sell their properties, chemical
smells, material being buried at the property and out-of-state vehicle traffic
at all hours. [@1]

Inside the home, deputies found about 3,770 marijuana plants alongside exposed
high-voltage wires throughout the interior and living conditions the sheriff's
office described as inhumane. [@1] A later national account confirmed that
officials found about 3,700 marijuana plants inside the Corinna grow, located
just 50 yards from the town offices. [@2]

The sheriff's office presented the raid as part of a rising surge of large
illegal marijuana operations across Maine, noting a leaked federal memo
estimating 270 large-scale illegal grows in the state connected to organised
crime groups in China. [@1]
