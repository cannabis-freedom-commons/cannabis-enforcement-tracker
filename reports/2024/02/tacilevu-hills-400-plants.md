Police conducted a raid in Tacilevu Hills in Fiji's Northern Division, seizing
close to 400 plants believed to be marijuana. [@1] No arrests had been made at
the time the seizure was reported. [@1]

Divisional Police Commander North, Senior Superintendent of Police Kemueli
Baledrokadroka, called the discovery and seizure a positive outcome of efforts
to disrupt the sale and distribution of the illicit drug. [@1] Baledrokadroka
said community support is crucial in the campaign against all forms of illicit
drugs. [@1] He thanked community leaders and members of the public who continue
to share information about those involved in the illegal trade. [@1]
