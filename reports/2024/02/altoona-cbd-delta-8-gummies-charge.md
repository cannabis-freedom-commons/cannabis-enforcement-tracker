Thomas Bryan, 56, of Altoona was charged in February after a nearly year-long
investigation that began in March 2023 when two sons bought the gummies. [@1]
The two bought and ate Primo Vibes Live Resin Delta 8 gummies from CBD Altoona
on W. Plank Road. [@1] Bryan was charged with multiple counts of possession with
intent to deliver, possession of a controlled substance and use or possession of
drug paraphernalia. [@1] He was arraigned on February 20 and released on 40,000
dollars unsecured bail. [@1]

An agent with the Attorney General's office made a controlled purchase of the
same item at CBD Altoona two weeks after the intoxication report. [@1] The
purchased gummies were sent to the state police lab and tested positive for
Delta 8 THC, which state police described as a Schedule I controlled substance
in Pennsylvania. [@1] In January the agent confirmed the shop was still
advertising Delta 8 and had a sign outside saying the store was giving out free
samples of Delta 8 and CBD. [@1] A few days later the agent made another
controlled buy and was sold what an employee called the strongest stuff they
had. [@1] Pennsylvania State Police served a search warrant at the shop the next
day and seized 552 items. [@1] A sample of each manufacturer was sent to be
tested. [@1]

In Pennsylvania there is no clear definitive law on Delta 8 and its legality,
while under federal law the products are legal and Pennsylvania has legalized
hemp derivatives, cannabinoids and isomers except Delta 9 THC. [@1]
