Police arrested 42 people in drug-related operations across the Northern
Division between 17 January and 12 February 2024. [@1] During the same period,
police seized more than 20,000 marijuana plants across the Northern Division.
[@1] Fiji Police Assistant Commissioner Operations Livai Driu said more green
plants, seeds and dried leaves believed to be marijuana were seized in the past
week. [@1]

Driu attributed the recent successful raids and arrests largely to increased
sharing of information. [@1] He said only cultivators and distributors benefited
from the illicit drug trade. [@1] He added that while a few got rich, the rest
of society suffered the health and social implications. [@1] Police said the
special operations aimed to identify new farms and revisit known cultivation
areas. [@1]
