Portugal's National Republican Guard (GNR) seized around three tons of hashish from a semi-rigid vessel intercepted on the Guadiana River. [@1] The operation, run with Spain's Guardia Civil, began when surveillance systems spotted the vessel entering the river mouth with its lights off, and a coastal patrol from Olhão moved to board it. [@1]

The vessel held 80 bales of hashish; officers also seized the boat, fuel cans and navigation equipment, preventing the drugs from being landed. [@1] When the patrol closed in, the suspects escaped on foot into Spanish territory, and the case was passed to prosecutors in Vila Real de Santo António. [@1]
