Officers of the Antioquia Police Department captured two men, aged 27 and 34, in
an operation in the rural area of La Pintada, Antioquia. [@1] The men were
transporting 2 kilograms of marijuana. [@1] The Traffic and Transportation unit
made the arrest after intercepting the motorcycle the men were riding. [@1] A
search of the vehicle found 2,000 grams of marijuana. [@1]

The two men were handed to the Prosecutor's Office's URI South Envigado for the
crime of trafficking, manufacturing or carrying narcotics. [@1]

The National Police said it was continuing its fight against drug trafficking to
protect public health and community safety. [@1]
