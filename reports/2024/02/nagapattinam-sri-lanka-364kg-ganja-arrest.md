Tamil Nadu Police arrested three men in Nagapattinam district on Sunday 18
February 2024 and seized over 364 kg of ganja meant to be smuggled to Sri
Lanka. [@1,2] The arrested men were identified as K. Sathiyaseelan, 37, of
Velankanni, M. Mahendran, 38, of Naluvedhapathi and T. Sugumar, 29, of
Pushpavanam. [@1,2]

Nagapattinam Superintendent of Police Harsh Singh said the accused were
planning to smuggle the ganja to Sri Lanka by the sea route in a fibre glass
boat, which the police seized. [@1,2] Vettaikaraniruppu Police registered a
case against the three accused under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic
Substances Act. [@1,2]
