A Water and Forests patrol arrested the traffickers, including a woman already
known to police, at the village of Toudoun Doki, 5 km from the town of Tahoua.
[@1] Those arrested were handed over to officers of the Central Office for the
Repression of Drug Trafficking (OCRTIS), Tahoua branch, for investigation. [@1]

Water and Forests officers in the Tahoua region seized more than 100 kg of
cannabis, which they valued at more than 10 million CFA francs, together with
two motorbikes, three mobile phones and a sum of money. [@1] The governor of the
Tahoua region, Colonel-Major Oumarou Tawayé, visited the scene to congratulate
the Water and Forests officers for their bravery and professionalism. [@1]

He praised the work of the defence and security forces in fighting drug
trafficking and terrorism, which he described as closely linked. [@1] The
governor called for stronger cooperation between the security forces and
citizens to report and prevent such activity. [@1]
