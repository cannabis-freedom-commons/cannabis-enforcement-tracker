Southern Division officers destroyed about 1,600 cannabis trees and several
seedlings during an eradication exercise in the Moruga district on Friday
16 February 2024, acting on intelligence information. [@1,2]

The officers proceeded to the La Lune Forest, where two fields occupying
about three lots of land held the trees and seedlings before they were
located and destroyed. [@1,2] Police put the street value of the destroyed
plants at TT$1.6 million. [@1,2] The three arrests reported alongside the
exercise were for possession of one gram of cocaine each in a separate
anti-crime sweep, not for the cannabis fields. [@1,2]
