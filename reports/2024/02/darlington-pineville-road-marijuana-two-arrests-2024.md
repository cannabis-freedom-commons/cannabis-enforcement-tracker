The Darlington County Sheriff's Office arrested two men during a drug bust
earlier that week. [@1] The sheriff's office seized drugs during a search
warrant at a home on Pineville Road on Tuesday, 27 February 2024. [@1] The
Darlington County Combined Drug Unit executed the warrant off Pineville Road as
part of an ongoing narcotics investigation. [@1]

Deputies said they recovered approximately 2,799 grams of marijuana, or about
2.799 kg. [@1] The seizure also included methamphetamine, crack cocaine, heroin,
fentanyl, cocaine, 82 cases of various THC gummies and two pill presses. [@1]
WMBF reported that the investigation had been going on for nearly two years when
the drugs were recovered. [@2]

Omarion Porter was arrested and charged with trafficking fentanyl and possession
with intent to distribute marijuana, crack cocaine and cocaine. [@1] Robert
Sweet was arrested and charged with possession with intent to distribute cocaine
and marijuana as third offenses, plus trafficking crack cocaine, fentanyl,
heroin and methamphetamine. [@1] WBTW identified the men as Robert Leon Sweet
and Omarion Dinton Porter, both of Darlington, and reported that they were
booked into the W. Glenn Campbell Detention Center. [@3] WMBF reported Porter
bonded out for $260,000 while Sweet remained in jail. [@2] The sheriff's office
said its narcotics investigation was ongoing and more charges may follow. [@1]
