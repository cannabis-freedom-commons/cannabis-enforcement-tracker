An unlicensed cannabis store in Moncton, H.C.A. Wellness Inc., was fined $20,000 on 27 February 2024 following a raid in 2022, while charges against two men connected to the company were withdrawn. [@1,2] Peace officers with New Brunswick's Department of Justice and Public Safety, backed by the Codiac Regional RCMP, had raided the storefront on 13 October 2022 and seized more than a dozen kilograms of cannabis, 1.5 kg of hash, vape pens and cartridges, live resin, RSO and edibles, plus about $10,400 in cash, a haul valued at about $136,000. [@1,2]

Donald Matthew Scott, 42, of Fredericton, and G. James McEwen, 45, were both charged with possessing cannabis for the purpose of selling. [@1,2] On 27 February 2024 officers laid a new charge against the company instead, and the counts against both men were withdrawn that day. [@1,2] The case was reported as a first: a New Brunswick business fined for selling illegal cannabis. [@1]
