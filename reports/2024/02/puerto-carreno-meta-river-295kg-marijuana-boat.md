On 27 February 2024 Colombia's Navy announced the seizure of 295 kilograms of
marijuana from a Venezuelan boat on the Meta river at Puerto Carreno, Vichada.
[@2] A Marine Infantry river-combat boat stopped and inspected the
Venezuelan-registered boat after tracking and investigation work. [@1] Officers
first found 12 bundles holding plant material resembling marijuana when they
inspected the Venezuelan-registered boat. [@1] The Navy said the launch had no
sailing authorisation and was violating merchant-marine rules. [@1] The crew was
two Venezuelan men and one Venezuelan woman, and officers took the boat to the
main dock at Puerto Carreno, where they found 357 rectangular packages inside
the bundles. [@3]

Prosecution CTI officers ran a homologated preliminary identification test that
confirmed the 295 kilograms were marijuana. [@1] Officers detained the three
crew members and handed them, the boat and the drug to the Attorney General's
office for custody and later destruction. [@3] The Navy valued the load at about
2 million dollars on the illegal international market. [@1]
