Moldovan police reported detaining three natives of Cantemir district, two of
whom had not yet turned 18, on suspicion of drug trading. [@1] According to
police, the three-person Cantemir group traded marijuana and Ecstasy. [@2] The
detentions were reported on 23 February 2024. [@1] With two of the three
detainees under 18, the group therefore included two minors and one adult. [@1]

As part of the same case, officers searched the suspects' homes in Cantemir
district. [@1] Those home searches turned up banned substances, which police
estimated at about 100,000 lei. [@1] That 100,000-lei estimate covered all the
seized substances together. [@1] No cannabis-only valuation was published, so no
value is recorded for marijuana alone. [@1]

The investigation into the three-person Cantemir group was still continuing when
these detentions were reported. [@2] Police said those eventually found guilty
in the case faced three to seven years in prison. [@1] The marijuana allegation
forms a separate cannabis enforcement component alongside the Ecstasy allegation
in this mixed case. [@2]
