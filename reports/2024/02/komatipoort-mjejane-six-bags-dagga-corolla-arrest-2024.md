Mpumalanga traffic officers and a South African Police Service member arrested a
31-year-old motorist at Mjejane, near Hectorspruit and Komatipoort, on Wednesday
14 February 2024. [@1] The officers were working at a vehicle control checkpoint
when they saw a white Toyota Corolla overtake on a barrier line and ordered it
to stop, but it sped off. [@1] A high-speed chase ended on Coopersdal Road,
where the officers cornered the car; Lowvelder put the arrest at about 17:50.
[@2] The car carried false registration number plates. [@1]

Six large bags of dagga were loaded on the back seat and in the boot. [@1]
Police said the bags were marked with specific names and addresses, and that
preliminary investigation showed they were destined for Gauteng. [@1] The dagga
and the Toyota Corolla were seized for further investigation. [@1] The driver
was charged with reckless and negligent driving and with dealing in dagga, the
charge the republished account on News365.co.za also carried. [@3] He was due to
appear in the Komatipoort Magistrate's Court on Friday 16 February 2024. [@1]

At the time of publication the outcome of that court appearance was not known.
[@2] Mpumalanga police commissioner Lieutenant General Semakaleng Daphney
Manamela commended the officers for their collective effort in the arrest and
the confiscation of the dagga. [@1] The account came from provincial police
spokesperson Colonel Donald Mdhluli and was republished the same day by
News365.co.za. [@1]
