On Tuesday 13 February 2024, Western Cape Provincial Traffic officers intercepted a heavy motor vehicle in Laingsburg and found 42 bags of compressed dagga weighing about two tonnes, with an estimated value of R4 million. [@1,2] The truck was also carrying a shipment of counterfeit clothing. [@1,2]

Two people were arrested and charged with possession of an illegal substance and dealing in counterfeit goods. [@1,2] Mobility MEC Ricardo Mackenzie praised the team, saying its officers go out every single day, around the clock, to make the Western Cape safer for all. [@1,2]

The bust came during a week of heightened road operations: between 12 and 18 February, provincial traffic officers ran 248 roadblocks and checkpoints, stopped and checked 27,699 vehicles, and made 96 arrests, most of them for driving under the influence of alcohol. [@1] Neither report says where the dagga was grown or where the truck was headed. [@1,2]
