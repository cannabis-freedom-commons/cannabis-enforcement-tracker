The Drug Enforcement Commission (DEC) announced on 1 February 2024 that a
meticulously executed series of targeted raids across Zambia had seized more
than five tons of cannabis and various narcotics. [@1,2]

In Kalumbila District of North-Western Province, officers arrested Rodwell
Kamboro, 44, and Lemon Kamboro, 78, of the Mulenga area, jointly charged with
unlawful cultivation of 3.12 tons of fresh cannabis. [@1,2] In Chavuma
District, two suspects were arrested for unlawful cultivation of fresh cannabis
and trafficking in loose psychotropic substances totalling over 44 kg combined.
[@1,2]

In Northern Province, operations in Kasama and Mpulungu districts seized over
one ton of fresh cannabis plants and over six kilograms of loose cannabis,
with eight suspects arrested and charged. [@1,2] In Kapiri Mposhi District,
officers separately arrested a Somali national for trafficking over five
kilograms of khat-miraa believed imported from Tanzania, a non-cannabis
seizure announced in the same statement. [@1,2]
