A joint party of the Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB), India's border-guard force, and
police arrested a Nepali citizen on the India-Nepal border and seized contraband
worth Rs 5 crore, a senior officer said on 22 February 2024. [@1] The
Maharajganj superintendent of police, Somendra Meena, identified the arrested
man as Basant Khatri, 24, from Dang district in Nepal. [@1]

Khatri was arrested in Sonauli with 9.898 kg of charas that police valued at Rs
5 crore in the international market. [@1] Times of India's account of the same
arrest, credited to PTI, also named Khatri and gave the quantity as 9.898 kg of
charas valued at Rs 5 crore. [@2]

The accused was booked under the relevant sections of the Narcotic Drugs and
Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act, police said. [@1] ABP Live described the
arrest as one of three seizures in the Sonauli police station area that
Thursday, the others being about 71 kg from an SUV at the main Sonauli gate and
38 kg near the ICP, with eight people arrested in all. [@3]

ETV Bharat also placed the Khatri arrest among three separate recoveries in the
Sonauli police station area on Thursday, putting the total at 119 kg of charas
worth Rs 90 crore in the international market. [@4] ETV Bharat said the three
recoveries together brought the day's total to 119 kg of charas and eight
arrests in the Sonauli police station area. [@4]
