Nepal Police arrested 14 people with about 150 kg of ganja at Jirogpoint in
Dharan-13, Sunsari, on Sunday, the district's police said. [@2] The 14 held are
from Purnia in Bihar, India, Police Superintendent Navin Krishna Bhandari said,
and they included two 17-year-olds and an 18-year-old. [@2]

A team from the temporary police post at Amarhat in Dharan checked vehicles
coming from Dhankuta towards Dharan and found 50 kg of ganja in one and 99 kg in
the other. [@4] The total came to 149 kg of ganja, DSP Bhandari said. [@2] Five
more people travelling in a third Indian vehicle, a BR 10 AJ 4486 car, were
taken into custody after police found it was involved in the transport. [@4]

Lokpath reported the check followed a specific tip. [@4] District Police Office
Sunsari said further investigation into the ganja trafficking was under way.
[@2]

The NDTV wire account of the same arrests reported that at least 14 Indian
nationals were arrested in three separate drug smuggling cases in Nepal that
Sunday and that 149 kg of the "contraband" was recovered. [@1] The same wire
copy called the haul hashish. [@1] NDTV said all of them were arrested in Dharan
city. [@1]

Udghosh Daily recorded the arrest as taking place on Sunday morning and gave the
same total of 149 kg of ganja. [@3]
