Federal police officers of the Cipolletti anti-drug division raided a house on
calle Las Margaritas al 600, in a rural neighbourhood of Cipolletti, Río Negro,
on 2 February 2024 and arrested a 33-year-old Argentine man. [@1] The raid was
ordered by the Federal Court of General Roca, whose secretariat was in the
charge of Dr. Juan Ignacio Oller, after an investigation had established that
the man was growing, storing and selling marijuana from the property. [@1]

Officers seized 30 cannabis sativa plants more than 1.5 metres tall, which had
no authorisation from REPROCAN, the national register of people authorised to
grow cannabis for controlled use. [@1] Police said they also seized two bags
holding 417 grams of crushed marijuana, a bag of buds of the same substance, two
mobile phones and 8,700 pesos that came from selling the drugs. [@1] The man was
held at the disposal of the judge in a case opened as "Infracción a la ley de
drogas", an offence against the drugs law. [@1]

A local Río Negro outlet published the same account with the same figures and
called the location a municipal rural neighbourhood. [@2]
