Five people were arrested when the Indore zonal team of the Narcotics Control Bureau (NCB)
intercepted a truck at a dhaba on the Bemetara–Simga Road in Chhattisgarh's Bemetara district
on 15 February 2024. [@1,2] Officers searching the truck found 628.42 kg of ganja hidden
underneath sacks of rice bran used to conceal the contraband. [@1,2]

## An interstate network

The team also seized the truck along with a car that had been escorting the consignment. [@1,2]
The ganja had been sourced from Sonepur district in Odisha and was destined for Sagar district
in Madhya Pradesh, according to the NCB release. [@1,2]

Zonal director Ritesh Ranjan said it was the third major ganja seizure by the Indore unit in
the last 15 days of 2024. [@1,2]
