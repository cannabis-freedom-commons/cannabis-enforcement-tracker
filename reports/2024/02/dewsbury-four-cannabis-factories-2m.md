West Yorkshire Police seized cannabis worth an estimated 2 million pounds in a series of raids
on four properties around Dewsbury between 15 January and 2 February 2024. [@1] More than
1,700 plants were found, and police said the raids followed information from local residents.
[@1]

The largest seizure came on 25 January at a disused commercial building at Wharf Mill in
Ravensthorpe, where at least 1,500 plants were growing after a community tip-off; police said
the premises had been converted for large-scale production and the seizure took a substantial
amount of potential profit from organised criminals. [@1,2] Officers had found another 100
plants at a second Wharf Mill site the week before. [@2] Police said every raided farm
potentially yields new clues and forensic information about those involved. [@1]
