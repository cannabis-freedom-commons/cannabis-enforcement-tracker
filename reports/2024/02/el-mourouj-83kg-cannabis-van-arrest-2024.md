The inquiry began after information that a person coming from Kasserine was
transporting drugs in a fruit-and-vegetable van. [@1] Police ambushed the
suspect in El-Mourouj and arrested him in his vehicle. [@1] A search of the
vehicle uncovered 832 slabs of cannabis weighing 83 kg. [@1] The drugs were
packed in three bags found during the vehicle search. [@3] The Ben Arous court
prosecutor ordered the suspect held in pre-trial detention and sent the case to
the El-Gorjani judicial-police narcotics brigade for questioning and further
investigation. [@3]

The shipment was headed for the Bir el-Kassaa area of El-Mourouj. [@3] The
dealer was preparing to resell the drugs in Greater Tunis. [@1] Kapitalis
reported the ministry's estimated value as nearly 3 million Tunisian dinars.
[@1]

Tunisia's Interior Ministry announced the seizure on Tuesday 6 February 2024.
[@2] Tunisian outlets call the cannabis zatla, a local term for cannabis. [@3]
