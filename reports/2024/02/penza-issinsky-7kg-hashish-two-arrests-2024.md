Officers of the Penza Oblast branch of the Russian Interior Ministry, backed by
regional Rosgvardia troops, stopped a car in the regional capital and detained
the two men inside on suspicion of drug trafficking. [@1] The two detainees were
a man born in 1991 from Moscow Oblast and a foreign national born in 1999. [@1]

A search of the car turned up 27 packets of an unknown substance, which testing
identified as 5 kg of mephedrone and 3 kg of hashish. [@1] In Issinsky district
of Penza Oblast police then found a cache the suspects' accomplices had set up
and seized about 4 kg of hashish from it, confirmed by examination. [@1]

According to police, the accomplices collected the large consignment from a
stash in the Moscow region on the instructions of an unidentified coordinator
and planned to sell the drugs through caches across Penza Oblast without direct
contact with buyers. [@1] A criminal case was opened under part 3 of article 30
and part 5 of article 228 of the Criminal Code, covering an attempted
large-scale drug offence. [@1] Both suspects were placed in pre-trial detention.
