The Donegal Divisional Drugs Unit, assisted by local members from the
Ballyshannon district, searched an industrial premises in Donegal town on the
afternoon of Wednesday 21 February 2024. [@1,2]

Inside they found a large-scale growhouse with cannabis plants at various
stages of maturity. [@1,2] The seizure comprised 500 cannabis plants and
13.5 kg of cultivated cannabis herb, which gardai valued at approximately
631,500 euro. [@1,2] The suspected drugs were to be sent to Forensic Science
Ireland for analysis. [@1,2]
