Essex Police forced entry to an industrial unit in Nobel Square, Pitsea, at
08:30 on Saturday 10 February 2024 and found more than 350 cannabis plants
at various stages of growth with hydroponic equipment and lights. [@1,2]

Basildon Community Policing Team carried out the drugs warrant and seized
the plants for destruction; no one was present at the unit and no arrests
had been made. [@1,2] District Commander Dan McHugh said tackling drugs and
their cultivation was a priority for his teams and credited information
from the public. [@1]
