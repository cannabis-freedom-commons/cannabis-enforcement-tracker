Gendarmes of the Montecarlo section of Squadron 10 and the Eldorado unit for
complex crimes were on a foot patrol at Puerto Delicia, on Argentina's border
with Paraguay, when they saw a group of people throwing large bundles from boats
at the mouth of the Aguaray Guazú stream. [@1] Seeing the officers, the people
fled towards the Paraguayan bank, and a search of the area turned up 33 bundles.
[@1] No one was detained at the scene. [@2]

Because the ground was steep and muddy, the Prefectura Naval Argentina, the
coast guard, sent two boats to carry out the officers and all the packages. [@1]
At the unit, officers counted 671 rectangular bricks that tested positive for
cannabis sativa with the force's Narcotest field kits, weighing 981 kilos 705
grams in all. [@1] The federal court and federal prosecutor's office in Eldorado
ordered the drug seized under Argentina's national drug law 23.737. [@1]
Investigations continued to identify those responsible for the load. [@2]
