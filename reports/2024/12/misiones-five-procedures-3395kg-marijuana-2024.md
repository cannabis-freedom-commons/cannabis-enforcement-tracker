Gendarmería Nacional Argentina seized more than three tonnes of marijuana in
five procedures across Misiones province. [@2] At the Santa Ana toll post on
National Route 12, officers stopped a long-distance bus travelling from Misiones
to Buenos Aires. [@1] The detector dog Laika reacted to a passenger's bag and
suitcase during the onboard check. [@1] From that luggage officers extracted 15
shapeless packages holding 20 kilos of cannabis sativa. [@1] The Posadas federal
court and prosecutor ordered the bus-load seizure and the detention of the
36-year-old passenger. [@1]

Near Garuhapé on Provincial Route 220, after vehicles in a rural area fled in
different directions, a search recovered 72 large bundles holding 3,279
rectangular packages with 2,221 kilos 600 grams of cannabis sativa from hills,
an abandoned house and a roadside. [@1] Officers of the Apóstoles group of
Squadron 8 opened three large parcels flagged by the detector dog León. [@1]
Inside, in front of witnesses, they found 97 rectangular packages of compacted
plant matter that field Narcotest testing identified as cannabis sativa, seizing
604 kilos 140 grams. [@1] At Puerto Viejo in Puerto Esperanza, after people
carrying flashlights fled into the dense monte when challenged, a search found
12 bundles of buds plus two more with 67 rectangular packages, totalling 400
kilos 454 grams of cannabis sativa. [@1] On foot patrol along the Paraná near
Puerto Laharrague, by the international border with Paraguay, officers found
seven stockpiled bundles holding 149 kilos 16 grams of marijuana. [@1] After
searching the area, officers saw a small boat arriving at the neighbouring
country's shore. [@1] The five procedures together yielded 3,395 kilos 210 grams
of cannabis sativa. [@1]

The Garuhapé load was seized with the intervention of the Oberá federal court
and prosecutor. [@1] The Eldorado federal court and prosecutor intervened in
both Squadron 10 operations at Puerto Esperanza and Puerto Laharrague. [@1]
