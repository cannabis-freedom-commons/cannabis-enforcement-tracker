South African National Defence Force soldiers of the Tshwane Regiment, deployed
under the Joint Tactical Headquarters KwaZulu-Natal, arrested four suspected
dagga smugglers in the Dukuza-Moyeni area of Upper Tugela. [@1] The operation
fell under KwaZulu-Natal Operation Corona, the military's border tasking, and
followed a tip-off about dagga smuggling at a house. [@1] Soldiers and the Upper
Tugela South African Police Service carried out a cordon and search operation at
the house. [@1]

Four male suspects were apprehended at the scene. [@1] Officers recovered 21
bags of dagga, three cellphones and R3 300 in cash. [@1] The dagga weighed 293.8
kilograms, which the SANDF valued at R4 a gram for an estimated total of R1 175
000. [@1] Three of the four suspects are Lesotho nationals and one is a South
African citizen. [@1]

The army said troops stationed on the KwaZulu-Natal border with Lesotho remain
alert to intercept illegal activity in the area. [@1]
