Police seized 60 pounds of cannabis in a suitcase at V.C. Bird International
Airport on Tuesday 17 December 2024. [@1] A suitcase was found holding 60
vacuum-sealed packages during a joint operation by Customs and the Police. [@2]
The 60 pounds is about 27.22 kg of cannabis. [@3]

The cannabis was valued at $480,000 and was taken to the police station pending
further investigations. [@1] Antigua News Room published the police media
release of the seizure under the heading 60 Pounds of Cannabis Seized at
Airport. [@2]

The next day the police Narcotics Department and Customs announced a separate
seizure of a black suitcase with 28 packages weighing 33.4 pounds during
investigations into the first matter. [@3]

Antigua News reported the same seizure, with the cannabis valued at $480,000.
