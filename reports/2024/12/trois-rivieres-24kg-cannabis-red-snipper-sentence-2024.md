All three of the defendants were of Dominican origin and were found guilty of
importing 24 kilos of cannabis herb into Guadeloupe on a beach at
Trois-Rivières. [@1]

On 6 April, between 8 and 10 pm, the boat the Red Snipper landed at Anse Duquéry
beach in Trois-Rivières. [@1] Following intelligence, investigative services
laid an ambush for the three men, 30-year-old Joewin Thomas among them. [@1]

Sentences of six years, three years and eighteen months were imposed, all with
the defendants kept in custody. [@1]
