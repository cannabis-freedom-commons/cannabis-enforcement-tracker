Justice Ambrose Lewis-Allagoa of the Federal High Court, Lagos, convicted and
sentenced 51-year-old Gbenga Abiodun and 58-year-old Bashir Mohammed for
trafficking 2,986.2 kg of cannabis sativa. [@1] Both men had pleaded guilty to
the charges brought by the National Drug Law Enforcement Agency (NDLEA). [@1]

Abiodun was arraigned on one count of dealing in 2,760 kg of cannabis. [@1]
Prosecutor Juliana Negedu told the court he was arrested with the weed on 25
October 2024 at Igunnuko Shrine, Alpha Beach, Lekki, Lagos. [@1,2] NDLEA
spokesman Femi Babafemi said the jumbo bags of Ghanaian Loud were recovered
there that Friday and two suspects were arrested. [@2]

Mohammed was arraigned on two counts covering 57.6 kg at Ashipa, Seme Border,
on 19 September 2024 and 168.6 kg at Aketegbo, Seme Border in Badagry West, on
18 September 2024. [@1] His counsel said he was a driver arrested where the
drug was found and asked the court for mercy as a first-time offender. [@1]

The judge sentenced the men to five years' imprisonment on each count and
ordered a N5 million fine option in lieu of the jail term. [@1]
