Police and Excise Department officers destroyed ganja plantations across wide areas of Gajapati district, Odisha, in a joint operation with Forest and Revenue Department officers. [@1] The teams cleared plantations on over 82 acres (about 33 hectares) of land in the villages of Garada, Sikilmaha and Gunjipadar in Raipanka panchayat, under Adaba police limits. [@1] The cannabis had been grown illegally on forest land, police said. [@1]

The officers destroyed around 1.64 lakh (164,000) ganja plants as part of Operation Green Clean. [@1] They estimated the market value of the destroyed crop at about Rs 17 crore. [@1] As many as seven platoons of armed police were deployed for the operation. [@1] The police and the Excise Department asked villagers not to take up illegal ganja cultivation. [@1]
