Antrim Crown Court sentenced two men on 13 December 2024 over a sophisticated
cannabis farm discovered in Ballymena in September 2023. [@1] Lin Wang, 47,
received 24 months for cultivating cannabis and possessing Class B drugs with
intent to supply. [@1] Robert McMitchell, 60, from Newtownabbey, received
200 hours of community service after pleading guilty to permitting production
of Class B drugs on his premises and possessing criminal property. [@1]

## A factory fed from the mains

On 27 September 2023 officers searched business premises owned by McMitchell on
Broughshane Street, Ballymena. [@1] Inside they found a large cultivation spread
over four rooms, with more than 300 plants at various stages of growth. [@1]
The premises had no electricity supply of its own: a feed had been dug up from
the mains under the road outside. [@1] Specialist police resources were needed
to dismantle the operation. [@1]

Wang was arrested in an adjoining premises; police believed he maintained the
cultivation. [@1] Officers also searched two further premises in Ballymena,
finding what they believed was a partly dismantled grow in one and cultivation
equipment stored in the other. [@1]

## Organised crime links

Police also recovered a large amount of cash in sterling and euros, which is
subject to ongoing criminal-property proceedings. [@1] A detective inspector
called the operation clearly well organised, with all the hallmarks of
organised-crime-group involvement, and said those running it were motivated by
greed. [@1] Police believed Chinese and Western Balkan nationals were involved,
and said the farm could have made hundreds of thousands of pounds over several
harvests. [@1]
