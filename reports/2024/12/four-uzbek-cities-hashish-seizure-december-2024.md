Law enforcement officers carried out operations in Tashkent, Fergana, Urgench
and Akhangaran and seized more than 10 kilograms of hashish and opium, brought
in particular from Tajikistan. [@1] UzA, the national news agency, said the
officers had stopped illegal trafficking of more than 10 kilograms of drugs.
[@3]

In Akhangaran, officers stopped a Lacetti driven by a 27-year-old resident of
Kokand and found 4,986 grams of hashish in the cabin. [@2] The car was stopped
and searched in Akhangaran as it travelled from Tashkent toward Fergana. [@2]
His 37-year-old brother, previously convicted, was following in a Malibu, and
both men were detained. [@2] The brothers had bought the hashish from Tajik
traffickers for 30,000 dollars, for delivery in Piskent district, and planned to
resell it in Kokand. [@2]

In Fergana, officers detained a 52-year-old resident of Fergana district,
previously convicted of a drug offence, and found 987 grams of hashish in a toy
box. [@2] He had obtained the drugs from a 40-year-old resident of Margilan, who
was also detained. [@2] Officers found a further 1,980 grams of hashish and 941
grams of opium in that man's Cobalt. [@2] A pre-trial check found that a citizen
of Tajikistan had earlier smuggled those drugs into Uzbekistan. [@2]

In Urgench, in Khorezm region, a 43-year-old resident of Oltinsoy district in
Surkhandarya planned to sell 501 grams of opium and 459 grams of hashish,
brought from Tajikistan, for 7,800 dollars. [@2] He was caught while taking a
3,000-dollar advance. [@2] In Tashkent's Yangihayot district, officers detained
a 42-year-old resident of Yashnobod district, previously convicted in a drug
case, and found 297 grams of hashish and 9 grams of opium in a suitcase of work
tools. [@2] The drugs found in these stops were seized under procedural rules.
[@1]
