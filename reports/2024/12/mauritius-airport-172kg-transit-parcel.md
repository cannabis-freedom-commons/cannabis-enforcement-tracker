Customs officers of the Mauritius Revenue Authority's Anti-Narcotics Section
seized 17.2 kg of cannabis in a parcel in transit on 8 December 2024, in an
operation carried out at the airport. [@1] The parcel had been declared as
personal effects and was flagged on risk criteria and the history of earlier
seizures of similar parcels. [@1]

Inside, officers found 16 packets of cannabis hidden in a box. [@1] A
presumptive test confirmed the drug and a Mauritius Revenue Authority sniffer
dog also reacted positively. [@1] The cannabis was secured and handed to the
Anti-Drug and Smuggling Unit (ADSU) at the airport for further investigation.
[@1]

ION News reported that the cannabis was bound for the Malagasy market and was in
transit to Mauritius from South Africa. [@2] ION News gave the inspection date
as Monday 9 December 2024 and put the haul at 16 sachets weighing 17.2 kilos in
total. [@2] ION News valued the seizure at about Rs 20 million in its headline
and dated its report 10 December 2024. [@2] According to ION News sources at the
ADSU headquarters, a report was to be sent to the South African and Malagasy
authorities to strengthen cooperation against drug trafficking and
information-sharing among agencies in the Indian Ocean. [@2] News Moris
republished the customs account in English, saying agents found 16 wrapped
packages of cannabis hidden inside a box. [@3]
