Security forces in Mogadishu arrested four suspects after finding them with loads of
hashish in the capital's Gubadley district. [@1] The Somali Police Force said the
suspects were arrested in possession of seven truckloads of hashish, which they
intended to distribute to various secret outlets in the city to be sold. [@1] The
suspects were also in possession of firearms. [@1]

A second account of the same operation says four men were arrested during a targeted
security operation in Gubadleey on Monday 30 December 2024, with a substantial
quantity of hashish and an AK47 rifle seized from one of the suspects. [@2] The
origin of the drugs was not established. [@1]
