The Melaka High Court sentenced former factory van driver Ramlan Mohammed
Salleh, 56, to life imprisonment after finding him guilty of trafficking
470.33 grams of cannabis. [@1] Judge Datuk Mohd Radzi Abdul Hamid held the
defence had failed to raise reasonable doubt, noting Ramlan never called
Shafiq, the man who had allegedly borrowed his car, as a witness. [@1]

Ramlan and another person were accused of trafficking the cannabis in a car
at Taman Cheng Jaya in the Melaka Tengah district at 6pm on 6 February 2020.
[@1] They were charged under Section 39B(1)(a) of the Dangerous Drugs Act
1952, which carries death or life imprisonment, with at least 15 strokes of
the cane if death is not imposed. [@1]

Ten witnesses testified for the prosecution, while Ramlan was the sole
defence witness. [@1]
