Sri Lanka Army intelligence officers, Thanamalwila Police and local Army
personnel raided cannabis plantations at three locations in the Unakanda,
Hambegamuwa area of Thanamalwila, acting on intelligence inputs. [@1]

The joint team destroyed 58,320 plants on 1.5 acres, 3,722 plants on half an
acre and 1,950 plants on a quarter acre, a total of 63,992 plants across 2.25
acres. [@1] All of the uprooted plants were burned on site so they could not be
processed or distributed further. [@1]

The report names no arrests and no dried cannabis recovered at the sites. [@1]
