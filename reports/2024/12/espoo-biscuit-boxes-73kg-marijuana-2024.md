In October 2024 Finnish Customs placed a man arriving at Helsinki Airport from
Bangkok under surveillance, followed him to Espoo where he met another man,
and seized about 13 kg of marijuana from the arrival's suitcase. [@1,2] Both
men were apprehended. [@1,2]

The inquiry led to a rented storage space in Espoo holding 60 kg of marijuana
hidden in biscuit boxes, while empty boxes indicated some 30 kg more had
passed through it. [@1,2] Customs suspects the organisation smuggled about
100 kg into Finland in total and had already distributed around 30 kg of it in
southern Finland. [@1,2] Three foreign nationals, none permanently resident in
Finland, were remanded on suspicion of several aggravated narcotics offences;
the preliminary investigation was completed and sent to the prosecutor for
consideration of charges. [@1,2]

Customs valued the seized marijuana at more than 2 million euros, and said its
marijuana seizures were heading for a record of over 830 kg in the first nine
months of 2024, nearly double the previous year's total. [@1,2]
