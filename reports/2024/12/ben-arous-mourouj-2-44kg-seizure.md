The National Guard's central anti-drug brigade in Ben Arous, backed by
specialist intervention units, carried out a large targeted security operation
against suspected traffickers. [@1] The suspects had established hideouts in the
El Mourouj 2 district where they concealed and distributed large cannabis
shipments. [@3] Officers arrested one suspect during the operation and placed a
second suspect on a wanted list for drug trafficking and money laundering. [@2]

The operation seized 440 slabs of cannabis weighing 44 kg in total, together
with sums of money and bladed weapons, according to the National Guard
directorate-general. [@1] Alchourouk headlines the same seizure as 440 slabs of
“Zatla” with cash seized and one person held. [@4]

The public prosecution at the Tunis 2 Court of First Instance ordered the
detention of one suspect and a wanted notice for the second man for drug
trafficking and sale, money laundering and other offences, while opening the
required judicial proceedings. [@1]
