New South Wales Police charged five people following an investigation into
cannabis supply in Sydney's south-west. [@1] Police Transport Command officers
began investigating in June, focusing their efforts on Yagoona. [@1] On 5
December 2024, officers stopped a vehicle on Brodie Street in Yagoona and
arrested two men aged 31 and 28. [@1] Officers searched the vehicle and seized
two kilograms of suspected methylamphetamine. [@1] Later that night, police
searched a property on Wycombe Street in Yagoona. [@1] At the property, they
arrested two women, aged 29 and 55, and a 57-year-old man. [@1] During the
search, officers seized over 60 kilograms of cannabis, 63 cannabis plants, a
hydroponic setup, and about $140,000 in cash. [@1] Authorities estimated the
cannabis had a street value of more than $1.2 million. [@1]

Both men were taken to Bankstown Police Station and each charged with eight
offences. [@1] The 31-year-old was charged with offences including commercial
drug supply, cultivating a commercial quantity of cannabis, dealing with
proceeds of crime, and participating in a criminal group. [@1] The 28-year-old
was charged with the same offences. [@1] They were refused bail at Bankstown
Local Court on 6 December to appear before Burwood Local Court on 12 February.
[@1] The 57-year-old man and 55-year-old woman were charged with commercial drug
supply, commercial cannabis cultivation, dealing with proceeds of crime, and
participating in a criminal group. [@1] The 29-year-old woman faced charges
including commercial drug supply, commercial cannabis cultivation, and
participating in a criminal group. [@1] All three appeared in Bankstown Local
Court on 6 December, where both women received conditional bail to appear at
Burwood Local Court on 12 February. [@1] The 57-year-old man was refused bail
before Burwood Local Court granted him conditional bail on 9 December to
reappear on 12 February. [@1]

Subsequent inquiries led police to raid three properties on the New South Wales
South Coast. [@1] On 6 December, South Coast Police District officers searched
homes on Morton Parade and Burradoo Crescent in Nowra, seizing 196 cannabis
plants and dismantling a hydroponic setup. [@1] On 18 December, police searched
a home on Elderberry Avenue in Worrigee, seizing 231 cannabis plants and a
hydroponic setup. [@1] With 490 cannabis plants seized in total, authorities
estimated the total street value of all seized drugs at $4.2 million. [@1]
