A Chomutov investigator started prosecuting a 30-year-old man for unauthorised
cultivation of plants containing a narcotic or psychotropic substance. [@1] The
man is known as a long-term marijuana user. [@2] He faces a charge for
unauthorised cultivation of plants containing a narcotic or psychotropic
substance. [@2] He was detained after an anonymous tip about unusual activity in
his garden. [@2] Detectives then investigated the tip, including carrying out a
house search. [@1]

At his home, in the garden of a family house in Chomutov, he had set up a
fóliovník, a foil-covered polytunnel greenhouse, where he grew cannabis without
permission instead of vegetables. [@1] Police seized 17 cannabis plants during
the search. [@1] Some of the plants reached two metres tall. [@2] The polytunnel
was completely filled with the plants. [@1] An expert examination of the seized
plants found they contained a total of 48 grams of THC, tetrahydrocannabinol.
[@1]

Someone with no sympathy for his hobby reported the grower to police
anonymously. [@1] He had not managed to harvest the crop before police arrived,
so uniformed officers harvested the mature plants. [@1]
