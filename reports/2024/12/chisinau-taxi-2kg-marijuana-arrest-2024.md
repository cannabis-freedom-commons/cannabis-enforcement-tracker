Police stopped a taxi at the entrance to Chisinau carrying a 55-year-old man and
a 35-year-old woman who had travelled from Vulcanesti district. [@3] The
defendants were placed in preventive detention for 25 and 30 days respectively.
[@2]

A search of the car boot found about 9 kg of synthetic drugs. [@3] Later
searches of the driver's home and the woman's flat seized an additional 2 kg of
mephedrone and PVP together with 2 kg of marijuana. [@2] Police put the total
drugs removed from circulation at about 13 kg. [@2] Police valued the combined
haul at 7.5 million lei. [@1]

Police said the drugs had been smuggled into Moldova from Ukraine and collected
from a cache in a forest strip in Vulcanesti district. [@1] The court of
Chisinau's Chekany sector ordered the preventive detention. [@3] The detention
followed a request from PCCOCS, the Prosecutor's Office for Combating Organized
Crime and Special Cases. [@2] Police said the suspects face up to 15 years in
prison. [@1] Police said work to identify accomplices, including those who had
placed the drugs in the hiding spot, was continuing. [@2]
