Saudi Arabia's General Directorate of Border Guard thwarted several drug
smuggling attempts in the southern Jazan, Asir and Najran regions, seizing 1.3
tonnes of hashish. [@1,2] The 28 December 2024 Saudi Press Agency release also
reports 1,388,622 narcotic pills and 136 tonnes of khat taken in the same
operations. [@1,2]

The operations led to 1,823 arrests: 959 Ethiopians, 849 Yemenis, three
Somalis and Eritreans, and 12 Saudis. [@1,2] The release urges the public to
report smuggling on the 911 and 995 hotlines and says all reports are treated
with strict confidentiality. [@1]
