Police arrested a 39-year-old farmer carrying a suitcase full of marijuana and a
26-year-old unemployed man with marijuana leaves at Narain Jetty on 24 December
2024. [@1]

The search at the jetty was carried out by the Fiji Police Force together with
the Fiji Dog Detector Unit, the force's detector-dog team. [@1]

Both men were held in police custody while investigations continued. [@1]
