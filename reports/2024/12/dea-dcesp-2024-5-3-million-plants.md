Federal agents and their state and local partners uprooted and destroyed about
5.3 million cultivated cannabis plants in 2024 under the Drug Enforcement
Administration's Domestic Cannabis Eradication/Suppression Program. [@1] The
programme's enforcement actions in 2024 led to 5,764 marijuana-related arrests.
[@1]

The 2024 total was slightly below the 5.7 million plants reported for 2022 and
the 5.5 million for 2021. [@1] Thirty-five states took part in the eradication
programme last year. [@1]

The DEA counted 16,330 cultivation sites eradicated in 2024, including 14,904
outdoor sites and 1,426 indoor ones. [@2] The DEA isolated 15 of the year's grow
operations as what it labels Chinese grows. [@1]

Officers also seized 3,125 weapons during the 2024 operations. [@2] Federal
marijuana arrests fell from 8,501 in 2011 to fewer than 5,000 in 2020 before
rising again, while the FBI counted 217,150 state and local marijuana arrests in
2023. [@1] NORML deputy director Paul Armentano said considerable time and
resources still go to enforcing and prosecuting under federal marijuana
prohibition. [@1]
