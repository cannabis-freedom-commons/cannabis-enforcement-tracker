Armed Police Force officers seized about 64 kg of cannabis from the border area
of Soniahi in Bhokraha Narsingh Rural Municipality-8, Sunsari, on the night of
Monday 9 December 2024. [@1] The load was being taken from Nepal to India when
the Border Outpost of the force's Sunsari-based No. 4 Battalion stopped it,
Superintendent Somenanda Bajracharya, the battalion chief, said. [@1] Earlier, a
Border Outpost of the same battalion at Sahebgunj seized an auto-rickshaw
carrying about 35 kg of cannabis from the Chimdi Bazaar area in Barju Rural
Municipality-6. [@1]

The battalion says it seized 1,502 kg of cannabis between mid-July and early
December 2024, against 1,103 kg in the previous fiscal year 2023/24. [@1] The
confiscated cannabis was handed over to the Nepal Police for legal action. [@1]

Bajracharya said the force had stepped up regular patrols, checkpoints and
operations based on confidential information to stop cannabis reaching India
through the open border. [@1] The article links the border seizures to a
smuggling network that produces cannabis in the hill districts of Dhankuta,
Bhojpur and Udayapur in Koshi Province and moves it through southern Sunsari.
