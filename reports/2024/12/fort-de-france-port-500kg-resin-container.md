French anti-drug investigators identified a man held in a prison in the Paris
region as the organiser of a two-way trafficking route between Martinique and
mainland France. [@1] The network sent cocaine that reached Martinique onward to
mainland France, and moved cannabis the other way toward the Caribbean island.
[@1] The detained man was ranked among the 50 biggest French narcotics
traffickers and is said to have designed the scheme around ties to cannabis
dealers in the Paris region. [@1]

In December 2024, during a check, close to 500 kg of cannabis resin was
intercepted at the port of Fort-de-France in Martinique, inside a container that
had come from the port of Le Havre. [@1] Police also found a few luxury cars,
weapons and 30,000 euros in cash. [@1] After tracing the operation to its
organiser, officers of the Office anti-stupéfiants (OFAST), France's central
anti-narcotics office, found and seized more than half a million euros in his
bank accounts. [@1] Already in prison in other cases, the man was placed under
formal investigation. [@1]
