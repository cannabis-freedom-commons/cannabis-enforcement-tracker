Operatives of the Marine Command of Nigeria's National Drug Law Enforcement
Agency (NDLEA) intercepted two boats loaded with 1,960 kg of Ghanaian Loud, a
strong strain of cannabis, at Eleko beach in the Lekki area of Lagos at 2:30am
on Christmas Eve, Tuesday 24 December 2024. [@1,2]

The six foreign nationals bringing the consignment from Ghana were arrested
during the midnight operation. [@1,2] They were named as two Ghanaians,
Godsway John, 38, and Freedom Kelvin, 33, and four Beninese nationals, Chegoun
Hounsou, 23, Gadabor Nyameto, 47, Adantg Sasa, 34, and Ayao Kayivi, 21. [@1]

The seizure was announced by NDLEA spokesman Femi Babafemi in a statement
issued on Sunday 29 December, in which agency chairman Brig. Gen. Mohamed Buba
Marwa (retired) commended the Marine Command and other units for their end of
year operations. [@1]
