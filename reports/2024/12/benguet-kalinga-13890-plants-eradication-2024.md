Anti-narcotics operatives of the Cordillera police command torched P5.2 million worth of cannabis in Benguet and Kalinga provinces on Tuesday 10 December 2024. [@1] In remote Barangays Kayapa in Bakun and Tacadang in Kibungan, both in Benguet, they found 9,890 fully grown plants, 800 seedlings and 20,000 grams of dried stalks, leaves and fruiting tops worth P4.4 million. [@1]

On the same day, police with PDEA-Cordillera operatives seized 4,000 harvestable marijuana shrubs worth P800,000 in remote Barangay Ngibat, Tinglayan, Kalinga. [@1] Cordillera police director Brig. Gen. David K. Peredo said all the plants were uprooted and burned on site, and that operatives were still hunting other plantation sites and those behind the farming. [@1]
