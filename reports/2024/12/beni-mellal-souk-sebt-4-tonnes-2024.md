Early on Wednesday morning the Beni Mellal judicial police, working with the General Directorate of Territorial Surveillance (DGST), disrupted an international drug trafficking operation in Beni Mellal.[@1] Officers seized nearly 4 metric tons of cannabis resin, known locally as chira, at a farm in a remote area near Souk Sebt Ouled Nemma.[@1]

The cache was hidden in a warehouse on the property.[@1] Police arrested a 35-year-old suspect found at the scene and confiscated two cargo transport vehicles and a large sum of money believed to be proceeds of the drug trade.[@1]

The arrested man was placed in custody under a judicial investigation directed by the competent prosecutor's office.[@1] Authorities said they were continuing to search for additional members of the trafficking network.[@1]
