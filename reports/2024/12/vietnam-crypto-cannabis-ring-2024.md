Anti-narcotics police announced on 20 December 2024 that they had broken up a cannabis ring that took payment through unverified bank accounts and cryptocurrency. [@1] Ten people were arrested and 550 kg of dried cannabis and 10 kg of resin were seized, along with packaging disguised as tea and food. [@1] The alleged ringleader told police he had arranged seven shipments, about two tonnes, in two months. [@1] The first bust came in Bình Dương on 15 November 2024. [@1]
