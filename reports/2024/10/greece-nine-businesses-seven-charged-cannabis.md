Greece Police raided nine businesses in Monroe County on Wednesday, 30 October
2024, in an investigation into illegal cannabis sales that began in May. [@1]
Seven men were arrested and all seven were charged with criminal possession of
cannabis and conspiracy. [@1] Chief Michael Wood said the investigation had run
the better part of six months and grew from illegal sales at the Red Eye Smoke
Shop on West Ridge Road. [@2] All seven suspects were released on appearance
tickets. [@2]

The searches covered nine shops, six homes and five vehicles across Greece,
Irondequoit, Webster, Henrietta, Ogden and Rochester. [@3] Police said none of
the businesses were licensed to sell cannabis. [@1] Officers seized 129 pounds
(about 58.5 kg) of bulk cannabis, 826 pounds of THC products and 108 pounds of
psilocybin products. [@1] The searches also turned up $628,000 in currency and
$459,083 from bank accounts, and five vehicles were searched and seized. [@1]

The New York State Office of Cannabis Management closed all the businesses that
were searched. [@1] The Red Eye Stoneridge Plaza location had been raided in
April 2023 for illegal cannabis sales. [@1] The Greece Town Board voted in
December 2021 to opt out of allowing cannabis dispensaries to open in the town.
[@1] Police said additional charges were pending and the investigation was
continuing. [@1]
