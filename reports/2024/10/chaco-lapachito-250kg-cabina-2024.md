Gendarmería officers of the Sección Vial “Lapachito”, part of Escuadrón 14 “Las
Palmas”, stopped a general-cargo truck that had come from Paraguay and was
heading for Buenos Aires province at kilometre 1,056 of Ruta Nacional 11 in
Chaco, in the early hours of 3 October 2024. [@1] The officers noticed bags and
a backpack in the cab and searched them with the anti-drug dog “Lennin”, which
reacted to the bundles, in the presence of witnesses. [@1] The Ministry of
National Security's own account says the officers were on a control operation at
kilometre 1,056 and pulled the Paraguay-bound truck over for inspection. [@2]

Opening the bundles, the gendarmes counted 270 packages wrapped in clear nylon
holding a plant substance that a Narcotest field test found positive for
cannabis sativa. [@1] The load weighed 250 kilograms 500 grams, the ministry's
own release says. [@2] Radio 3 Cadena Patagonia, crediting argentina.gob.ar,
reported that the officers found 270 packages holding a total of 250 kilos 500
grams of marijuana. [@3]

The Federal Court No. 2 of Resistencia ordered the drug seized and the driver of
the truck detained. [@1] The Ministry of National Security release says the same
court ordered the seizure and the driver's detention. [@2] Radio 3 Cadena
Patagonia reported that the driver, visibly nervous during the inspection, was
arrested and accused of breaking Law 23.737 on narcotics. [@3] Radio 3 Cadena
Patagonia reported that the Federal Court No. 2 of Resistencia ordered the
substance seized and the driver immediately detained, accused of breaching the
narcotics law. [@3]
