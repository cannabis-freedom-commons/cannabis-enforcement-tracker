Victoria Police's Major Drug Squad uncovered more than 6,000 cannabis plants at a
rural property in Leongatha South, in Gippsland, on Tuesday 22 October 2024. [@1,2]
Police counted 6,525 plants, from seedlings to mature plants, weighing 1.7 tonnes in
total, and estimated their street value at more than AUD 26 million. [@1,2] Police
called it one of the biggest hauls seen in Victoria in recent times. [@1,2]

Officers found a sophisticated hydroponic set-up with several commercial-sized hot
houses, and believed the property was rented although they had not spoken to its
owners. [@1,2] It took three days and more than 15 police to dismantle and remove the
plants, which were burned in an open area at the property. [@2] Fire authorities
helped clear the burnt material because the smoke was considered hazardous. [@2]

Five Vietnamese nationals, four men and one woman, were arrested at the scene and
charged with cultivating and trafficking a commercial quantity of cannabis. [@1,2]
They were remanded to face the Latrobe Magistrates' Court in March. [@1]
