Special agents of Paraguay's National Anti-Drug Secretariat (Senad) from its
Bella Vista Norte regional office entered a rural area of Amambay department to
destroy marijuana plantations. [@1] The operation was led in the field by
anti-drug prosecutor Celso Morales. [@1] Agents destroyed seven hectares of
marijuana in its final stage of growth and seven camps used to produce and
process the drug. [@1]

The teams also found marijuana drying on the site, about 1,700 kg of it. [@1]
Senad said more than 21 tonnes of marijuana were destroyed in the operation.
[@1]

The raid followed geolocation of the area and planning of the teams that entered
the site. [@1]

Morales said intelligence indicated about 40 people were working the plantation,
and the operation made no detentions. [@2] Because of the size of the
plantations and the difficult access, Morales asked the Joint Task Force (FTC)
for support to protect those taking part. [@2] Morales said a large group that
had worked elsewhere in Amambay appeared to have moved into Bella Vista Norte,
leaving forest reserves destroyed. [@2]
