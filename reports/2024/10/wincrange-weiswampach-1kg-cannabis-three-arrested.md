On Tuesday, 29 October 2024, judicial police officers carried out three
court-ordered searches in the communes of Wincrange and Weiswampach in northern
Luxembourg. [@1] The searches were conducted by officers of the judicial
police's northern narcotics section, who seized about one kilogram of cannabis.
[@1] Officers also seized nearly €15,000 in cash during the operation. [@2]
Various drug paraphernalia were also seized alongside the cannabis and cash.
[@2]

The Diekirch public prosecutor's office ordered the three subsequent arrests for
offences under the narcotics law. [@1] Three people were arrested for offences
under the narcotics law. [@1] On 30 October, the day after the searches, the
three arrestees were presented to the competent investigating judge. [@1] The
judge issued an arrest warrant against the suspects after their appearance. [@1]
All three defendants were placed in pre-trial detention. [@1]
