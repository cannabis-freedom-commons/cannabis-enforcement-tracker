Gendarmes of the Anti-Drug Investigations Section "Santiago del Estero" and
other Gendarmería Nacional units carried out 10 simultaneous searches in the
provincial capital, La Banda and Añatuya. [@1] The operations dismantled a
criminal organisation that the force said distributed drugs to cities in
Santiago del Estero province. [@1] The searches came after a year of
investigation, and the force said they were ordered because a shipment was about
to move to supply other cities. [@1] Eight people were detained in the searches.
[@1]

Officers seized 301 kilos 712 grams of marijuana. [@1] The searches also
produced 78 grams of cocaine, which is not cannabis and is left out of the
cannabis figures. [@1] Gendarmería also took 48 mobile phones, three firearms,
six motorcycles, four cars, 2,767,150 Argentine pesos, three precision scales
and case documents. [@1]

The Juzgado Federal Nº 2 of Santiago del Estero, the local federal court,
ordered the searches. [@2] El Liberal reported that gendarmes found and weighed
301 kg of marijuana in a house on Avenida del Libertador at number 1000 in the
provincial capital, described as the base of an alleged distributor. [@3]

In other homes the same raids produced 809 grams of marijuana bud, one marijuana
seedling, a further 88 grams of cocaine, seven cars and 11 motorcycles, almost 3
million pesos in cash and 6 million pesos in cheques, 58 phones, six firearms
and six scales, in El Liberal's tally. [@3]
