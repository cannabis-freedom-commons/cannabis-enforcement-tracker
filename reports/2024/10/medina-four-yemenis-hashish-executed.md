Saudi Arabia executed four Yemeni men in the Medina region on 22 October 2024 for smuggling hashish.
[@1,2] The interior ministry named them as Yahya Mohammed Yahya Lutfullah, Ali Abdullah Ahmed Azib,
Ahmed Yahya Masoud Ali and Salem Ibrahim Nahari. [@1,2] It said they were arrested, tried, and
sentenced to discretionary death after the Supreme Court upheld the convictions. [@1,2]

## Part of a larger execution day

The ministry announcement republished by Ajel deals only with these four hashish smugglers in Medina.
[@1] Other executions announced the same week included murder cases; the opened sources here do not
describe those defendants. [@1,2]
