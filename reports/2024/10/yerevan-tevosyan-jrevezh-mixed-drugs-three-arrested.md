Officers of the criminal police department against illegal drug trafficking,
working with the Abovyan investigative division, the Kotayk police division and
Yerevan patrols, acted on operational information. [@2] On 30 October 2024
police detained two men aged 52 and 53 on Tevosyan Street in Yerevan on
suspicion of illegally acquiring, keeping and using drugs, and took them with
their GAZ-3110 car to the Kotayk police division. [@1]

A search of the 52-year-old man found marijuana. [@1] The 53-year-old admitted
under the weight of the facts that the Subutex found in the car he had bought
for 10,000 drams from an acquaintance in Jjrvezh village, whose identity the
police already knew. [@1] Inside a criminal proceeding opened on the case,
police detained a 56-year-old Jjrvezh resident on suspicion of illegally selling
drugs. [@1] He was brought to the division in the Mercedes he was driving. [@2]

A search at the 56-year-old's home the same day found 1.4 kg of marijuana, 15 g
of methamphetamine, 2.5 g of cocaine, 1 g of heroin, 2 g of hashish, 1.5 Subutex
tablets, 170 ml of methadone and 110 psychotropic tablets, according to the
Armenian Interior Ministry's press service. [@1]

The preliminary investigation was continuing. [@1] Armenian law presumes a
suspect or accused innocent until guilt is proved under a court decision that
has entered into force. [@1]
