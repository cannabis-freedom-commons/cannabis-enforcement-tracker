On Thursday 24 October 2024 the anti-drug brigade of Colonia department police
raided three branches of a hemp-producing company in Nueva Helvecia, Colonia
Valdense and San José. [@4] The company held the licences needed to produce hemp
but, according to the police, was selling CBD and THC preparations locally
outside the law. [@2] Police detained three people at the three sites: two men
aged 27 and 31 and a woman aged 46. [@1] The searches followed a year of
investigation by the departmental anti-drug brigade, which grew out of Operation
Demeter, 12 raids in Nueva Helvecia, nearby resort towns and Montevideo in
September 2023 in which 10 people were held. [@1]

The Interior Ministry said officers seized 1,140 kilograms of hemp biomass, 337
kilograms of plain plant material, 5 kilograms of mouldy plant material and 2
kilograms of plant material with THC. [@1] Police also seized 250 kilograms of
cannabis seeds in different varieties. [@1] Officers took away jars holding BHO,
droppers of cannabis oil, scales, two vehicles, mobile phones and cash in
Uruguayan, US and Paraguayan currency. [@2]

The three were brought before the third-turn criminal court in Rosario and
convicted. [@3] The older man received two years and ten months in prison and
the second man two years, both for continuous supply of narcotic substances.
[@3] The woman was sentenced to twelve months for aggravated concealment, to be
served entirely on probation. [@3]
