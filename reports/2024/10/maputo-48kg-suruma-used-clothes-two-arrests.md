Mozambique's Republican Police (PRM) detained two people, one Mozambican and one
Tanzanian, with about 50 kilograms of cannabis sativa, known locally as suruma.
[@1] The drug was hidden in bundles of used clothes and was bound for Tanzania.
[@1] Officers seized 45 small bundles totalling 48 kilograms of the drug. [@1]
The operation ended at the Mozambican suspect's home, where the drug was being
packed into clothing sacks to mislead the authorities. [@1]

The suspect said this was the third time he had made this kind of run but that
on the earlier trips he did not know he was carrying drugs, believing it to be
humanitarian aid. [@1] He was detained after being contacted by agents of the
National Criminal Investigation Service (SERNIC). [@1]

PRM spokesperson in Maputo Marta Pereira said the case belonged to a larger
trafficking network and that the amount seized was only a fraction of it. [@1]
She said investigations were continuing to catch more people involved. [@1]
