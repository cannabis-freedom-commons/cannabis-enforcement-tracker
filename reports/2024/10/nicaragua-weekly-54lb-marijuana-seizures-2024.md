Nicaragua's National Police detained 29 people for dangerous crimes across the
country between 30 September and 6 October 2024. [@1] Seven of them were
arrested for drug trafficking in districts of Managua, identified as Milton
Alejandro López Mercado, Pedro Jacobo Mercado Dávila, José Campos Salazar, Jaime
Aguilar Arteaga, José Canales Aguilar, Cristopher Salvador Avendaño and Gabriel
Antonio Escoto Espinoza. [@1] Commissioner General Jaime Vanegas, the police
inspector general, announced the weekly results to the Nicaraguan people. [@1]

In the Managua cases police seized 20 pounds of marijuana, along with 4
kilograms and 894 grams of cocaine, a car and a motorcycle. [@1] In Kukra Hill
police captured Héctor Hebberth López for drug trafficking with 26 pounds of
marijuana, while in Quilalí, Nueva Segovia, Carlos Javier Quintero López was
arrested with two pounds of marijuana. [@3] In Jinotega police captured Maynor
Joel Balladares López in Pantasma and Santos Moisés Pineda Pravia in San José de
Bocay with 6 pounds of marijuana and 1 motorcycle, while in Quilalí Carlos
Javier Quintero López was detained with 2 pounds of marijuana. [@1]

Two other people detained in Bluefields, Eliud Omar Alvarado Ballesteros and
Jorvin Alberto Fajardo Martínez, had only 301.4 grams of cocaine seized from
them, which is not counted in the cannabis total. [@1] Canal 2 corroborated the
same 30 September to 6 October operation as 29 arrests for homicide, robbery
with intimidation and drug trafficking under the citizen-security plan. [@2]
