Since 7 October 2024, Senegal's military has conducted an extensive crackdown
on hemp cultivation and illegal logging in northern Sindian's Djibidione,
Dieye-Djondji, Massaran, Balla Bassene and Niale areas near the Gambian
border. [@1]

The operation answers long-standing pleas from residents, as formerly
conflict-displaced areas repopulate and trafficking draws young people away
from school and lawful farming. [@1]

Leading the effort, the commando battalion supported by the 25th
Reconnaissance and Support Battalion had already destroyed more than 50
hectares of hemp crops, with an estimated market value of more than 2 billion
CFA francs. [@1]

The army said it would eradicate the fields, dismantle armed groups protecting
cannabis farms, apprehend traffickers and restore agricultural stability. [@1]
