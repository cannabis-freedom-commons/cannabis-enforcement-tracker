Greek police destroyed more than 8,300 illegally grown cannabis plants found inside a corn farm in central Greece, one of the largest such finds in the country. [@1] Officers uprooted about 1,300 of the plants and destroyed another 7,000 on the spot, some up to 3.5 metres tall, because their roots were entangled with the corn. [@1]

A 44-year-old farm owner was arrested on charges of illegal cannabis cultivation and drug trafficking as part of a criminal group. [@1] Police estimated the group aimed at an illegal profit of 15 million euros from the crop. [@1]

Greece legalised cannabis for medical use in 2017, but growing it for recreational purposes remains illegal. [@1]
