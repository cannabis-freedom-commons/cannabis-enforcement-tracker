The Drug Law Enforcement Agency of The Gambia (DLEAG) arrested three people
in a sting operation that uncovered nine cannabis plantation farms between
Kunjenjeh and Kanilai villages in the West Coast Region. [@1,2] The operation
began on 5 October 2024, with DLEAG officers first finding eight farms and a
backup team from the Police Intervention Unit (PIU) finding one more. [@1,2]

The three suspects were held in custody while investigations continued.
[@1,2] DLEAG spokesperson Ousman Saidybah said the agency would leave no
stone unturned in freeing the country from drugs, and thanked the police
leadership, the PIU team and the armed forces chief for their support.
[@1,2]

Saidybah warned that planting cannabis violates the Drug Control Act 2003,
which carries fines of at least 300,000 dalasis for individuals on
conviction. [@1]
