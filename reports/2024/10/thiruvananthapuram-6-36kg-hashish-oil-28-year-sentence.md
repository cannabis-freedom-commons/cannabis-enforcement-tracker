The Thiruvananthapuram Additional District and Sessions Court sentenced three
men to 28 years of rigorous imprisonment on Friday, 25 October 2024 for
smuggling and selling hashish oil. [@2] The convicts were Rosari Ronaldo, 45, of
Thoothukudi in Tamil Nadu, and Thankamani natives Binoy Thomas, 50, and T N
Gopi, 74. [@1] Judge K P Anil Kumar convicted them of conspiracy and drug
smuggling under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act. [@1] Each
offence carried 14 years, but the terms run concurrently, so the three will
serve 14 years. [@1] The court fined each of them Rs 2 lakh, with one more year
in prison if the fine is not paid. [@1]

Excise officers arrested the three on 1 September 2018 at the parking area of a
garment store in Thiruvananthapuram while they were trying to sell hashish oil
to Maldivians. [@2] The three were carrying 6.36 kg of hashish oil when excise
officers arrested them. [@1] A team led by then Thiruvananthapuram Excise Circle
Inspector T. Anikumar caught them while they were transferring the contraband.
[@2] The hashish oil was valued at Rs 6 crore and was to be smuggled to the
Maldives. [@1] The oil was sourced from Ullas, an Idukki native settled in
Andhra Pradesh who owns ganja plantations there, according to the New Indian
Express. [@1]

Rosari had worked as an agent at Thoothukudi port, where he was involved in
smuggling hashish oil to the Maldives and Sri Lanka. [@1] He shifted his
operation to Thiruvananthapuram after one of his consignments was caught and
central agencies and Tamil Nadu police intensified their search for him. [@1]
Former Assistant Excise Commissioner (Enforcement) A.R. Zulfikar submitted the
charge sheet within 180 days of the incident. [@2] The prosecution examined 13
witnesses and presented 48 material objects and 91 exhibits. [@2] The court
accepted the prosecution's submission to confiscate the vehicles used in the
crime and Rs 6.75 lakh in cash linked to the smuggling operation. [@2] It also
directed that the seized hashish oil be preserved because the investigation was
ongoing. [@2] The first two accused had been in judicial custody for six years,
while the third was granted temporary bail after five years. [@2]
