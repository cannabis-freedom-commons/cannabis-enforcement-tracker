On 31 October 2024 a surveillance frigate of the French armed forces in the
southern Indian Ocean zone detained a coaster about 180 km north of La Réunion
and seized drugs from it. [@1] The frigate belonged to the Armed Forces in the
Southern Indian Ocean Zone (FAZSOI), France's joint military command for the
region. [@2]

Réunion's prefecture put the haul at nearly 200 kg of drugs and named cannabis
and cannabis resin alongside heroin and methamphetamine in the cargo. [@2] The
prefecture said the operation showed France's determination to fight drug
trafficking, which it called a factor destabilising its regional partners. [@2]
It said the crews of the FAZSOI units and all the state services associated with
the operation had shown professionalism. [@2]

The state services said maritime enforcement was striking transnational criminal
organisations by removing a significant source of money from the informal
market. [@3] The announcement followed an earlier seizure of 1.6 tonnes of
methamphetamine in the same waters in early 2024. [@2]
