Three Namibian nationals were found with 90kg of dagga in Atlantis in the
Western Cape. [@1] The suspects are aged 40, 41 and 43 and face charges of
possession of dagga. [@1] The suspects were detained at the Philadelphia police
station. [@1] They were expected to appear in the Atlantis Magistrate’s Court.
[@1]

Members of the Atlantis Crime Prevention Unit acted on information about three
men carrying large bags and acting nervously at a petrol station on the N7. [@2]
Officers found the 90kg of dagga hidden in the area where trucks park. [@1] The
operation took place on Wednesday, October 2 at about 10.50pm. [@1]

Further investigation found that the dagga had been transported from Namibia.
[@1] Provincial police spokesperson Anelisiwe Manyana gave the account of the
arrests and seizure. [@1]
