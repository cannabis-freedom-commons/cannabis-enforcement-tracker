Staff at the Federal Training Centre in Laval's Saint-Vincent-de-Paul
neighbourhood seized contraband and unauthorized items including hashish on
October 4, 2024. [@2] Correctional Service Canada credited the vigilance of its
staff for the discovery. [@2] The Laval News describes the centre as a medium-
and minimum-security federal prison in Saint-Vincent-de-Paul, while the agency
calls it a multi-level security federal institution. [@1]

The seized items included hashish. [@2] Correctional Service Canada put the
total estimated institutional value of the seizure at $64,031. [@2]

The agency says it uses ion scanners and drug-detector dogs to search buildings,
personal property, inmates and visitors. [@2] It says it is heightening measures
to prevent contraband from entering its institutions to help ensure a safe and
secure environment for everyone, and works in partnership with police against
those who try to introduce contraband. [@2] It says it set up a telephone tip
line for all federal institutions to receive information about security
activities including drug use or trafficking. [@2] It says calls to the
toll-free tip line remain anonymous and shared information is protected. [@2]
