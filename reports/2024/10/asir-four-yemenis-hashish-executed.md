Saudi Arabia executed seven people on Wednesday 23 October 2024, five of them
for drug trafficking, according to the official Saudi Press Agency (SPA).
[@1,2]

Four of them, Yemeni nationals named as Yahya Lutfullah, Ali Azib, Ahmed Ali
and Salem Nahari, were put to death in the southern province of Asir for
smuggling hashish into the country. [@1,2]

Also that day a Pakistani man was executed for drug trafficking, and two Saudi
nationals were executed for murder. [@1,2] The seven deaths brought the
kingdom's 2024 execution total to 236 by that date. [@2]
