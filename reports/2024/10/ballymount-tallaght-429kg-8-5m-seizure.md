On 11 October 2024 the Garda National Drugs and Organised Crime Bureau and the Tallaght Drug
Unit, supported by Revenue customs officers, intercepted two vehicles shortly after 3pm and
searched a business premises in Ballymount, Tallaght, as part of investigations into organised
crime under Operation Tara. [@1,2]

They seized 429 kilograms of cannabis with an estimated street value of €8.5 million, subject
to analysis by Forensic Science Ireland. [@1,2]

Four men aged 31, 45, 52 and 58 were arrested and detained under drug-trafficking powers.
[@1,2] They were charged and due to appear in the Criminal Courts of Justice on the morning of
14 October. [@3]
