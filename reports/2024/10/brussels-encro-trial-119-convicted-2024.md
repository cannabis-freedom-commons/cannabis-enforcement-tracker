The court convicted 119 defendants, 115 people and the four companies, with
sentences from 14 months to 17 years, and acquitted nine people. [@2] Eridan
Munoz Guerrero, the Albanian described as manager of cocaine-processing labs in
Brussels and arrested in October 2021, was sentenced to 14 years, when 19 had
been sought. [@2]

Besides clandestine cocaine laboratories, cannabis plantations were also found
in warehouses in Belgium. [@4] The cannabis element sits inside a case over a
wide international traffic in cocaine and cannabis that Belgian police
dismantled in 2021-2022 with forces from Italy and Germany. [@1]

The file was named Encro because investigators relied heavily on decrypting the
Encrochat and Sky ECC messaging services. [@4] The Belgian federal police broke
up the network at the end of 2021 after months of inquiry based on Sky ECC data,
operating between South America and Europe with Antwerp as a gateway. [@3]

Defence lawyer Gilles Vanderbeck called the case a publicity stunt by the
federal prosecutor and said people had been artificially linked although they
had no connection. [@2] The 220,000-page file was impossible to read in full,
lawyers said, and the judgement is open to appeal. [@3]
