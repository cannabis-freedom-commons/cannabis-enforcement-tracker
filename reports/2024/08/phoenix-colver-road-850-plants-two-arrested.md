Two people were arrested after police found an illegal cannabis grow site in the
4400 block of Colver Road in Phoenix, a town in southern Oregon's Jackson
County. [@1]

The Jackson County Sheriff's Office said police seized 850 plants, 40 pounds
(about 18.1 kilograms) of processed marijuana and an assortment of THC and
psilocybin mushroom products. [@1]

The two suspects, 47-year-old Steven Mialocq and 34-year-old Alyssa Monique
Valverde, both from Phoenix, each faced three felony counts. [@1] Police said
the counts included unlawful possession, distribution and manufacture of
marijuana. [@1]

Jackson County Code Enforcement went to the scene during the search warrant and
issued $44,000 in fines to the responsible party. [@1] The release said the
fines were for building code violations, non-permitted electrical installations
and failure to obtain land use approval for marijuana production. [@1]
