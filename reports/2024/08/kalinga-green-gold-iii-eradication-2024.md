Joint operatives of the National Bureau of Investigation (NBI) and the
Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency (PDEA) destroyed two marijuana plantation
sites in Tinglayan, Kalinga, on 16 August 2024. [@1,2] The operation, part of
Oplan Green Gold III, was carried out by the NBI Bayombong District Office
together with PDEA-Kalinga and other units. [@2] It began after PDEA-Kalinga
asked the NBI to investigate a reported plantation in the province. [@1]

The two sites spanned 15,400 square metres and held marijuana worth P15.4
million. [@1,2] The plants were uprooted, inventoried and burned on-site by
the joint operatives. [@1] NBI Director Jaime Santiago vowed the agency would
remain steadfast in its campaign to eradicate illegal drugs. [@1]

The NBI Bayombong office later listed the raid among its 2024 accomplishments
at a July 2025 flag-raising ceremony at the Nueva Vizcaya provincial
capitol. [@2] The reports name no arrests of cultivators at the sites. [@1,2]
