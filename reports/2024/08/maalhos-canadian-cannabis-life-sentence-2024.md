A Canadian national arrested for drug possession at a guesthouse in B. Maalhos
was sentenced to life imprisonment by the Maldivian courts. [@1] Scott William
Armstrong was arrested from his beachside hotel room in Maalhos on 21 July 2019
after a report that he was selling drugs there. [@3] Police searched his
guesthouse room in July 2019 under a warrant obtained on a tip-off and
discovered illegal drugs. [@1] He was charged with cannabis trafficking under
Articles 108 and 138 of the Drug Act. [@3] He pleaded not guilty but was
convicted on the police evidence. [@1] The court found him guilty beyond
reasonable doubt on the prosecution evidence. [@3]

He surrendered two pieces of cannabis weighing 26.76 grams kept in a container
in the room's minibar. [@3] Police also found another piece of cannabis weighing
0.12 grams in a container inside his luggage. [@3] The court imposed a life term
of 25 years and ordered him to pay MVR 100,000 within a year. [@3] Five months
and nine days spent in detention were counted as time served, leaving 24 years,
six months and 21 days to serve. [@3]

Under Maldivian law, more than 14 grams of cannabis found on a drug addict is
treated as intent to sell. [@3] Prosecutors said he stayed alone in a locked
room and showed police himself where the drugs were hidden. [@3] The defence
said other people, including hotel staff, could also enter the room where the
drugs were found. [@2]
