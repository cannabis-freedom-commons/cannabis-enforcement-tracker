Officers of Panama's National Border Service and the Public Ministry intercepted
three sacks containing 5,900 golden cylindrical capsules filled with liquid in
Clarita, Chepo district. [@1] Patrolling border service units ordered the
suspects carrying the sacks to halt, but they escaped and fled into the jungle.
[@1] Officers who initially suspected the sacks held cocaine consulted Colombian
counterparts, who advised that the substance appeared to be liquid marijuana for
medicinal or recreational use. [@1]

Senafront general director Jorge Gobea stated that the absence of regulatory
markings indicated the containers came from clandestine laboratories. [@1]
Authorities estimated that the 5,900 capsules would have an illegal market value
of approximately 500,000 balboas. [@1] Prosecutors sent samples to a laboratory
for forensic analysis to confirm the composition of the substance. [@1] The
Public Ministry opened investigations to identify the criminal organisations
behind the shipment and determine its destination and intended use. [@1] The
interdiction was carried out under Operación Manto Oscuro as part of the broader
Campaña Darién security effort. [@1]
