Belarusian customs canine officers detected seven drug cases among scheduled-bus
passengers on the border in the week before 20 August 2024. [@1] All seven bus
passengers were detained, and criminal cases were opened against them for
unlawfully and intentionally moving narcotics across the EAEU customs border.
[@1]

A Latvian citizen travelling on the Riga–Gomel bus hid marijuana among his
personal belongings, and a service dog found the packet as he underwent customs
control. [@1] Entering Belarus from Latvia at the Grigorovshchina checkpoint,
the man had declared that he was carrying no prohibited or restricted goods
subject to customs declaration. [@1] Vitebsk customs officers searched his
belongings and found a crumpled paper packet containing greenish-brown plant
material. [@1] Days later at the same checkpoint, officers found marijuana on a
Kazakhstani citizen travelling the same route after a service dog alerted to
narcotics in his suitcase. [@1] He had hidden a plastic bag of the drug inside a
pair of trainers. [@1]

The agency said the seven passengers hid marijuana, hashish and methadone in
their personal belongings. [@1] It said the drugs were carried for personal use
rather than commercial purposes, warning that such conduct could lead to loss of
liberty. [@1] A forensic examination by the Vitebsk department of the State
Forensic Examination Committee confirmed that the material the men carried was
marijuana. [@1]
