One person was arrested when the Indore zonal team of the Narcotics Control Bureau (NCB)
intercepted a truck at Titarpani Toll Plaza on the Lakhnadon–Lalitpur road (NH-26) in
Madhya Pradesh's Sagar district on the night between Thursday and Friday. [@1,2] Officers
searching the truck found 170 kg of ganja hidden beneath fish-feed sacks in its rear, and
seized the truck with the contraband. [@1,2]

## Watched on intelligence

Zonal director Ritesh Ranjan said the team had kept the traffickers' movements under
surveillance on specific intelligence before stopping the truck. [@1,2] The consignment had
been sourced from Devgaon in Maharashtra and was destined for Ayodhya in Uttar Pradesh,
he said. [@1,2]

Ranjan said it was the Indore unit's eleventh seizure of the year, with more than 3,000 kg
of drugs valued at about Rs 9.5 crore taken in 2024. [@1,2]
