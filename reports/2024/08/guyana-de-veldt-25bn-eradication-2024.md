Police and Coast Guard ranks destroyed more than G$2.5 billion worth of cannabis at De Veldt on the Berbice River on 16 August 2024. [@1,2] The eradication exercise ran from 05:00 to 12:30 and was described as one of the biggest narcotics operations in Berbice. [@2]

At a suspected stash house, the ranks found 1,300 pounds (about 590 kg) of dried cannabis, 100 pounds of cannabis seeds, 520 twelve-gauge cartridges and a .22 rifle with 19 matching rounds. [@1,2] The house, the drugs and the farms were photographed and then destroyed by fire, while the cartridges and the rifle were lodged at the Central Police Station. [@1,2]

Officers then found three abandoned farms of about three acres each. [@2] One held forty trays of cannabis seedlings about two inches tall, and the other two held 39,000 and 50,000 plants between six and twelve inches tall. [@1] Police put the street value of everything destroyed at more than G$2.5 billion and arrested no one in the operation. [@1,2]
