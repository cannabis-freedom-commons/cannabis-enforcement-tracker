Customs officers of the Dakhla-Oued Eddahab regional authority seized 4,198
kg of hashish, locally known as chira, at the El Guerguerat border post with
Mauritania on Monday, 19 August 2024. [@1,2]

The drug was pressed into tablets and hidden in custom-built compartments in
a truck's secure storage areas, found during a meticulous search carried out
with the national security services. [@2] The crossing sits about 380 km
south of Dakhla on the road route between Morocco and Mauritania. [@1]

The next day officers using scanners found 46 kg of cocaine hidden in the
refrigeration system of a second transport truck, a separate seizure
recorded here only for context and left out of the cannabis figures. [@1,2]
