On 10 August 2024, Revenue officers, assisted by Gardaí, stopped and searched an unaccompanied freight unit that had disembarked a ferry from Bilbao, Spain, at Rosslare Europort. [@1,2] Using risk profiling with an x-ray scanner and detector dog Jasper, they found about 783 kg of herbal cannabis and 70 kg of cannabis resin, with an estimated value of €16,086,000. [@2]

No arrests had been made at the time; Gardaí said the drugs were sent to Forensic Science Ireland for analysis and that investigations were continuing. [@1]
