South Western and Central Divisions police seized a gun and a quantity of
marijuana and arrested six people in exercises between July 31 and August 1,
2024. [@1] In the South Western Division, officers held a warrant exercise
between 1 am and 8 am on July 31 and arrested six people aged between 30 and 68
on outstanding affiliate and time-allowed commitment (TAC) warrants. [@1]

The exercise was coordinated by Snr Supt Teewarie, Supt Cumberbatch, ASPs
Forbes, Haynes and Corrie and Insp Harripersad, and included warrant officers
from the Point Fortin, Cedros, La Brea and Guapo police stations. [@1] Central
Division Task Force (CDTF) officers went to Church Street, Couva, between 2 pm
and 8 pm on August 1 and found a quantity of marijuana in a black plastic bag
while searching an area. [@1] The officers then went to California, a community
near Couva, where they found and seized a rifle. [@1] The Central Division
exercise was coordinated by Snr Supt Simon, Supt Glodon, ASP Ablacksingh and
Insp Sylvan and included members of CDTF Area South and the Canine Unit. [@1]
