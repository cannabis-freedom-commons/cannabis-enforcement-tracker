Attorney General Gentner Drummond's Organized Crime Task Force seized and
eradicated more than 9,000 marijuana plants and 456 pounds (about 206.8
kilograms) of unlawful marijuana at a grow facility in Seminole. [@1]

The State Fire Marshal's Office and Oklahoma LP Gas inspected the facility on
25 July, finding multiple administrative violations as well as a significant
amount of unlawful marijuana. [@1] Acting on that inspection, the task force
executed a search warrant at the facility on 1 August with the Oklahoma
Medical Marijuana Authority, the Seminole Police Department and the National
Guard Counter-Drug Unit, and seized the illegal drugs. [@1]

Drummond said illegal operations have plagued Oklahoma since medical marijuana
legalization in 2018 and that the task force is making significant strides in
driving out the bad actors behind them. [@1] The release asks anyone with
information about an illegal grow to report it to the task force. [@1] No
arrests at the Seminole facility were reported. [@1]
