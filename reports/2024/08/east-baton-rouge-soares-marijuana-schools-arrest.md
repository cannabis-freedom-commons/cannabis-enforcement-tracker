East Baton Rouge Sheriff's Office agents arrested Jordan Soares, 23, and said he
was an area marijuana trafficker. [@1] The office's Gang Intelligence and
Enforcement Operation said agents made two undercover purchases of marijuana
from Soares and identified two locations they said he used for his operation.
[@1] Agents said they seized more than 23 pounds (about 10.4 kilograms) of
marijuana, 100 marijuana vape cartridges, pain pills and other drugs, more than
$5,000 in cash, and several guns including a rifle. [@1]

Investigators said Soares was also recruiting and using children to distribute
marijuana in schools in the parish. [@1] Detectives would not say which schools
or which school system they accused Soares of trafficking drugs into, and East
Baton Rouge Schools said it carries out random campus searches. [@1] The
Director of EBR Schools Security, Sgt. Rodney Walker, said the searches are
meant to prevent and deter contraband on campuses. [@1]

Investigators said witnesses came forward and led them to Soares. [@1] Soares
was released on a $40,000 bond and his next court date was scheduled for 10
October. [@1]
