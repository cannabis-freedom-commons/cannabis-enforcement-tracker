Acting on a tip-off, a Rapid Action Battalion team raided the house of Mosharaf Hossain in Fulbari village under Godagari upazila in Rajshahi district at around 1.30 am. [@1] The team recovered 112 kilograms of ganja from the house and arrested Hossain, 38, together with Muhammad Sharif, 27, of Ghorashal village under Muradnagar upazila in Comilla district, red-handed. [@1]

The arrested men and the seized ganja were handed over to the police, and two separate cases were filed with the respective police stations. [@1]
