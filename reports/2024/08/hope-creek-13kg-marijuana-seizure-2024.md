The Dangriga Operation Team of the Belize Police Department carried out an
anti-drug operation in Hope Creek Village on the morning of 8 August 2024. [@1]
Officers seized about 31.67 pounds of marijuana, or 13.735 kilograms, lying in a
bushy area. [@1] No one was in the area during the search, so police labelled
the marijuana as found property. [@1]
