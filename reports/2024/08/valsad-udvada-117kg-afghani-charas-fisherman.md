Valsad police seized 11.74 kg of Afghani charas at Udvada in Pardi taluka on
Monday 12 August 2024, after a fisherman found an abandoned bag behind a local
kindergarten school and informed the police on Monday evening. [@1] The bag held
11 packets carrying inscriptions in Arabic, Pardi police said. [@1] Police
described the contents as Afghani charas, hashish produced from live cannabis
grown in Afghanistan, and called the find the first of its kind on the South
Gujarat coastline. [@1] Pardi police inspector G R Gadhvi said they had
coordinated with Dwarka police, which seized contraband with similar
inscriptions from a coastal area on 8 June. [@1]

The newspaper put the international market value of the Afghani charas at Rs 50
lakh per kilogram, an official estimate. [@1] The same article reported that
around 35 kg of Afghani charas was seized on Monday and Tuesday from Dungri and
Pardi in Valsad district and from Hazira in Surat. [@1]
