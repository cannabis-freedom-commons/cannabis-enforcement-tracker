Prefectura Naval Argentina seized more than 27 kilograms of marijuana on the
bank of the Parana River at Corpus, in Misiones province. [@1] The force valued
the load at more than 68 million pesos. [@1]

Officers found a bundle hidden among the undergrowth at Puerto Natural EFA, a
natural river port on the Parana, and found it held 38 panes of pressed
marijuana. [@2] The Prefectura described the find as a result of sweeping the
ground as part of its drug-seizure control and prevention work. [@1] The
Misiones outlets reported the operation as part of constant controls on the
border zone to prevent crime. [@2]

The Federal Prosecutor's Office in Obera, headed by Viviana Vallejos, and the
court of the same city, headed by Marcos Gallandat Luzuriaga, took charge of the
case. [@1] The ministry release and the Misiones outlets give the same weight,
place and package count, and name no arrests. [@3]
