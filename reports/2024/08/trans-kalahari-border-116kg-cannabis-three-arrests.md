Police arrested a 44-year-old truck driver and two other men at the
Trans-Kalahari Border Post in the Omaheke region at about 02h15 on Thursday.
[@1] Omaheke police spokesperson chief inspector Fina Sakajengenga said the
driver was at the wheel of a Maritz Transport truck from South Africa and was
helped by a Namibia Revenue Agency (Namra) official to bring cannabis through
the checkpoint from Nomansland. [@1]

The cannabis weighed 116 935 grams, which police valued at N$5,8 million. [@1]
The other two suspects were a 27-year-old Namra official and a 30-year-old
Tanzanian national. [@1]

All three men were charged and were due to appear in the Gobabis magistrate's
court on Monday. [@1]
