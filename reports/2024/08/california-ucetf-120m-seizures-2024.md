Governor Gavin Newsom announced on 7 August 2024 that California's Unified Cannabis Enforcement Taskforce (UCETF) had seized more than $120 million worth of unlicensed cannabis since January. [@1,2] The operations destroyed nearly 37 tons of unlicensed cannabis, including 122,914 illegal plants, and state officers also seized 22 firearms. [@1,2]

In recent months the task force ran operations in 11 counties: Mendocino, Tuolumne, Shasta, Orange, Kern, Alameda, Yuba, Trinity, Los Angeles, Butte and Humboldt. [@1] Newsom said the state would not tolerate illegal operations that threaten the economy and the health of communities, and the task force's enforcement chief said its partners were disrupting the unlicensed market. [@1,2]
