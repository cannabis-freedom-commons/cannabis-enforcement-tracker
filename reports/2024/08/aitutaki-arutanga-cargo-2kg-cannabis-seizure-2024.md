Cook Islands Customs officers found just over 2 kg of cannabis in cargo from New
Zealand during a search at the port in Arutanga on Aitutaki on Wednesday 7
August 2024. [@2] Cook Islands Customs seized over two kilograms of cannabis at
Arutanga Harbour, and police opened an investigation into a drug supply to
Aitutaki. [@1] Police were notified of the seizure and Rarotonga detectives were
flown to the island the next day with search warrants. [@2]

Detective Inspector Areumu Ingaua, commander of the Criminal Investigation
Branch, credited the Customs service's diligence in securing the border and said
police were confident of a positive outcome. [@2] The ministry said the
operation was a joint effort by Customs in Aitutaki and Rarotonga and the
police, with further updates to follow. [@2]
