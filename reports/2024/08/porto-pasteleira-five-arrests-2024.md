Officers of the PSP's Porto Metropolitan Command detained five men in Porto on
Wednesday on suspicion of drug trafficking. [@1] The detainees were men aged 25
to 47. [@1] Four were detained in Bairro da Pasteleira and one in Bairro do
Cerco, neighbourhoods in Porto. [@1] The five detainees were to be brought
before the judicial authorities for the application of coercive measures. [@1]

Police presented the main seizure as almost 2,500 doses of heroin, cocaine and
hashish together with more than 18,000 euros in cash. [@1] In detail, police
reported seizing 1,863 doses of cocaine, 101 doses of heroin and 461 doses of
haxixe, or hashish, a cannabis resin, along with 18,226 euros in cash from the
detainees. [@1] In a separate seizure on a public road in Bairro de São Tomé,
officers seized another 32 doses of haxixe and 35 doses of liamba, a term for
cannabis herb, as well as 530 euros in cash. [@1]

The PSP described the São Tomé seizure as occurring during a crime-visibility
and prevention operation to combat the direct sale of narcotics. [@1] The Porto
Metropolitan Command announced the detentions and seizures in a statement. [@1]
