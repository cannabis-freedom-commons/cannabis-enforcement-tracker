Prosecutors of the PCCOCS and officers of the National Investigations
Inspectorate detained two men from Cahul in a cannabis seizure intended for
sale, and on the request of the PCCOCS the Chișinău court at Ciocana ordered
that they be held in preventive arrest for 30 days. [@2] The two suspects were
aged 35 and 41. [@1]

Law enforcement officers seized six kilograms of marijuana during searches in
Cahul. [@1] Police put the value of the marijuana seized at more than 1.2
million lei. [@1] The PCCOCS said the pair specialised in growing cannabis
plants with a high concentration of THC, the main psychotropic substance in
cannabis. [@2] They bought cannabis seeds outside Moldova and sold the
higher-quality cannabis buds to dealers in Chișinău and Romania, with the rest
going to dealers in Cahul. [@2]

At the home of the 41-year-old, in searches at both men's homes carried out with
the support of the special police unit Fulger, officers found equipment for
dividing and selling the drug. [@2] Officers also seized a hunting weapon and
live cartridges held without permits. [@2] The PCCOCS said the two men benefit
from the presumption of innocence during the continuing criminal investigation.
[@2]
