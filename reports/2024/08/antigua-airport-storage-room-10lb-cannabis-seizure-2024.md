Customs and police officers seized ten pounds of cannabis during a joint drug
operation at V.C. Bird International Airport on Friday 30 August 2024. [@1] Nine
vacuum-sealed packages of the substance were found inside a cardboard box during
the search of a storage room. [@1]

The ten pounds is about 4.54 kg of cannabis. [@1] The Observer put the street
value of the cannabis at $60,000. [@1] The packages were taken to the police
station while further investigation continued. [@1]

Antigua News published the same account of the airport seizure on 31 August.
