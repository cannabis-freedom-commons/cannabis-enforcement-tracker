Gardai arrested a teenage boy in County Louth following the seizure of illegal
THC oil estimated to be worth over EUR 180,000. [@1] Gardai went to an incident
involving the boy in Dundalk after receiving a report on Friday morning. [@1]
The boy left the scene but gardai chased and caught him. [@1]

Officers seized approximately seven litres of suspected THC oil valued at EUR
181,000, alongside drug paraphernalia and an Armani watch. [@1] The boy was
taken to a Garda station in the Dundalk area. [@1]

He has since been released without charge. [@1] A file will now be prepared for
the Garda Youth Diversion Office. [@1] The seized drugs will be sent to Forensic
Science Ireland for analysis. [@1] Investigations were continuing. [@1]
