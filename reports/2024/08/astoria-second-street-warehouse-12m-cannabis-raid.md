Officers from the NYPD's 114th Precinct in Astoria responded around 3 a.m. on 8
August 2024 to a report of a burglary in progress at a warehouse on Second
Street. [@2] The two men, John Betancourt, 36, of Ridgewood, and James Pennisi,
38, of Chester, Pennsylvania, tried to flee on foot when police arrived and were
arrested. [@5] A criminal complaint alleges that officers recovered 27 one-pound
packages of marijuana outside the warehouse and a further 77 pounds of marijuana
in three garbage bags beside a neighbouring building, about 104 pounds (about
47.2 kg) in all. [@5]

Officers found two men fleeing the warehouse with large bags and, after
arresting them, found nearly 100 pounds of cannabis flower inside the bags. [@3]
The NYPD obtained a search warrant and officers from both departments descended
on the warehouse on the afternoon of 8 August 2024. [@3] Inside the warehouse
officers found cannabis flower, pre-rolls, THC edibles, THC vape products, THC
concentrate and jars of THC oil, along with psilocybin mushroom chocolate bars
and two machines used to manufacture homemade vape products. [@3] None of the
products was regulated and the warehouse had no state licence to sell them,
according to Sheriff's Office Lieutenant Francesca Rosa. [@3]

The city's sheriff, Anthony Miranda, described the building as a distribution
point supplying locations in the city and beyond. [@4] The sheriff first valued
the confiscated products at $5 million. [@7] The Sheriff's Office later put the
total seized at $12 million, $7 million higher than the estimate it reported the
morning after the raid. [@4] Lieutenant Francesca Rosa of the Sheriff's Office
said the seizure was one of the largest the office had made, though not the
largest. [@6] Officials described it as one of the biggest marijuana raids in
New York City's history. [@1] Betancourt and Pennisi were arraigned in Queens
Criminal Court on multiple drug and drug-related charges and held in lieu of
bail. [@5] The Sheriff's Office padlocked the warehouse after the raid and its
investigation remained open. [@4] Rosa said it was still unclear whether the two
arrested men were connected to the warehouse operation or had truly been
burglarising the property. [@3]

The seizure came during Operation Padlock to Protect, the sheriff's campaign
with the NYPD and other city agencies against unlicensed cannabis shops,
launched on 30 May 2024. [@4] The Queens Daily Eagle reported that the seizure
equalled 30 percent of all cannabis the city confiscated between June and August
2024. [@4]
