Gendarmería Nacional Argentina officers stopped a Brazilian-flagged refrigerated
lorry on National Route 12 near Montecarlo, in northern Misiones, and found
7,949 kilos 250 grams of cannabis sativa hidden in its semi-trailer. [@2] The
trailer's load seal had been broken, and the anti-narcotics dog Loana reacted to
the load in front of witnesses. [@2] The Federal Court of Eldorado ordered the
load section opened, and inside officers counted 346 bundles holding 8,827
rectangular packages. [@2] The unit's criminalistics and forensic officers ran
Narcotest field tests, which were positive for cannabis sativa. [@3]

The court ordered the arrest of the Brazilian driver, held incommunicado, along
with the seizure of the drug and the lorry. [@3] The case was handled by the
Federal Court of Eldorado under Argentina's narcotics law 23.737. [@2]

The gendarmes of Squadron 10 El Dorado ran a cordon operation around Montecarlo
after investigating a lorry suspected of smuggling, with the Montecarlo section,
the Eldorado road section and the Eldorado complex-crimes investigations unit
taking part. [@3] Gendarmería called it the largest marijuana seizure of the
current administration to that date. [@1] The lorry was a refrigerated
international freight vehicle used for smuggling that had come from Brazil. [@6]

La Voz del Interior reported the seizure took place on Wednesday 3 July 2024,
when officers inspected a freight vehicle travelling from Brazil. [@4] Infobae
reported that the investigating magistrate ordered the driver's detention along
with the seizure of the drug and the truck, for infringement of law 23.737. [@5]
