Argentina's federal police detained three men and seized almost three kilograms
of marijuana in the province of Entre Ríos. [@1] The DUOF Paraná unit, a federal
operational division, opened the inquiry at the request of the Federal
Prosecutor's Office of First Instance of Paraná under Leandro Ardoy and found
that several people used a social network to organise motorcycle trips from
Paraná to Concepción del Uruguay to move narcotics covertly. [@1] El Entre Ríos
reported that the group shared fuel costs for the motorcycle runs that carried
the drugs, and that the judicial case began after investigators saw the
movements on social media. [@2]

The Federal Court of First Instance No 1 of Paraná, under Leandro Damián Ríos,
ordered the field work to continue and an officer was designated an Agente
Revelador, an undercover operative, who made covert contact with the suspects to
receive a parcel in Concepción del Uruguay. [@1] El Entre Ríos placed the
handover at the corner of Isaías Torres and Pablo Scéliga streets in Concepción
del Uruguay. [@2] The parcel was opened at judicial headquarters, found to hold
a bag with 109 grams of marijuana buds, reconditioned as received, and released
under a court order authorising an Entrega Vigilada, a controlled delivery. [@1]
With help from the DUOF unit in Concepción del Uruguay, investigators ran
discreet inquiries in both cities and identified two addresses, one in Paraná
and one in Concepción del Uruguay. [@1] Infobae, citing Uno Entre Ríos, reported
that the searches of the two homes were carried out on Saturday 6 July 2024.
[@3]

Searches at the two addresses ended with the detention of three men and the
seizure of a parcel and three nylon wrappers holding almost three kilograms of
marijuana buds. [@1] Infobae described the three detainees as Argentine adults
believed to be the heads of the organisation, which sold marijuana to order and
delivered it by motorcycle. [@3] Officers also seized 94,980 pesos, three
motorcycles, four mobile phones and a precision scale, among other items of
interest to the case. [@1] The three detainees, Argentine adults, were placed at
the disposal of the intervening magistrate with the seized items for violating
the drug law. [@1] Infobae said the detainees would be charged under Argentina's
drug law, which it numbered 27.737; the current national narcotics law is
23.737. [@3]
