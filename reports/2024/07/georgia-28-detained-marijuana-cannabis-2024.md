Georgia's Interior Ministry said 28 people were detained on drug charges after
operational and investigative measures carried out with the Prosecutor General's
Office. [@1] Sixteen of those detained were described as drug dealers. [@1]
Those arrested included Georgian and foreign citizens. [@2]

Sixteen people were detained for illegal sale, attempted sale and preparation
for sale of large and especially large amounts of narcotics, including
marijuana. [@1] Twelve other people, including four foreigners, were detained
for illegal acquisition and storage of large amounts of drugs and psychotropic
substances including marijuana, illegal import into Georgia, and illegal growing
and cultivation of narcotic-containing plants. [@1] Police made several
controlled drug purchases from those held on sale charges under judges' orders
and secretly audio- and video-recorded the purchases. [@1] Searches of the
detainees, their homes and places they indicated recovered large quantities of
drugs as evidence, listed as cocaine, heroin, Alpha-PVP, methadone, MDMA,
mephedrone, lysergide, ketamine, buprenorphine, Subutex, ecstasy,
methamphetamine, marijuana and cannabis plants. [@1]

The investigation is proceeding under articles 260, 261, 262, 265, 18-260,
19-260, 273 prima and 19-273 prima of Georgia's Criminal Code. [@1] The alleged
offences carry up to 20 years in prison or life imprisonment. [@1]
