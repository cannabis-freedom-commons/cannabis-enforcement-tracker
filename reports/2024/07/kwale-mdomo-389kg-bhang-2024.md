Acting on a tip-off, Coast Anti-Narcotics Unit detectives laid an ambush at Mdomo centre on the Samburu-Kinango road and intercepted a heavily laden Toyota Ipsum, registration KBJ 679M.[@1,2] A search found seven green gunny sacks holding long khaki rolls of bhang weighing 389.45 kg, with an estimated street value of Sh11,683,500.[@1,2]

The driver jumped out and vanished into a nearby thicket when officers closed in.[@1,2] The co-driver, Elisha Omondi, 32, was cornered and arrested at the scene.[@1,2] Detectives said a second convoy suspect also escaped.[@2]

## Custody and route

The seized bhang was kept as exhibits and Omondi was taken to Mombasa Urban Police Station, while detectives pursued the fugitive driver.[@1] The van driver was later reported transferred to port police in Mombasa for arraignment at Mariakani Law Courts on 22 July 2024.[@2] Police said the consignment had come from suppliers in Tanzania through the Isebania border point in Migori County and was bound for a known distributor in Diani.[@2]
