Matthew Woods, Kurt Hughes and Daniel Moody were sentenced for plotting to
import cocaine and cannabis into the Isle of Man hidden in a car. [@1] Woods,
24, received 18 years and nine months for smuggling almost £100,000 of cocaine
and about £78,000 of cannabis to the island on 6 June 2022. [@1] Hughes, 58,
whose Liverpool-based garage was used to hide the drugs in the vehicle, received
10 years and eight months. [@1] Moody, 36, received four years for helping to
package the cannabis. [@1]

Hughes serviced a BMW, stored it in his garage overnight, and it was collected
in the morning. [@1] Spotswood was jailed for 15 years last August for playing a
leading role in an organised crime gang that imported cocaine and cannabis to
the island. [@1] Hughes pleaded guilty to charges of being concerned in the
importation of cocaine and the importation of cannabis. [@1] Moody was found
guilty of playing a part in importing the cannabis, while Woods was convicted of
being concerned in the importation of both cocaine and cannabis following a
trial. [@1]

Woods was also sentenced for a separate charge of being concerned in the
importation of cocaine in August 2021 and for causing grievous bodily harm in
March 2023 while on bail for the drugs offences. [@1] Deemster Graeme Cook said
Woods was high up the chain and had played a part in coordinating the operation
through phone communication. [@1] Moody and Hughes were also given exclusion
orders forbidding them from returning to the Isle of Man for five years after
release. [@1] The three were sentenced on Wednesday. [@1]
