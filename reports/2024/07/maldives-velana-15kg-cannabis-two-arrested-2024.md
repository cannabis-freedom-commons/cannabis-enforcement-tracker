Maldives Customs officers found over 15 kg of cannabis in the luggage of a
44-year-old Malaysian national who arrived at Velana International Airport on
Emirates flight EK656 from Dubai on the morning of 27 July 2024. [@1,2]
Police valued the seizure at 12.3 million rufiyaa, and initial tests confirmed
the substance was cannabis. [@1,2]

The police Drug Enforcement team then ran a controlled delivery, which led to
the arrest of a second suspect, a 41-year-old Malaysian man, that night.
[@1,2] Both men were remanded for five days by the Criminal Court while the
investigation continued. [@1,2]

Police said the investigation pointed to an international trafficking network
with links to people from four countries, and called the haul the largest
cannabis seizure in the Maldives that year. [@1,2]
