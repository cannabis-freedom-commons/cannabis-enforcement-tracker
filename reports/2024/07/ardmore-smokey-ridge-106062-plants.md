Oklahoma Bureau of Narcotics agents served a search warrant at 1420 Smokey Ridge
Road in Ardmore on Tuesday 23 July 2024. [@3] Agents seized 106,062 marijuana
plants and 1,120 pounds of processed marijuana, about 508 kg. [@3] Wen Bin
Huang, 34, and Wen Zhao Huang, 37, were arrested and faced charges including
aggravated trafficking of marijuana and aggravated cultivation of a controlled
dangerous substance. [@1]

The raid was not the first on Smokey Ridge Road; the bureau said it had already
served a warrant, arrested people and seized plants there in a previous
investigation. [@1] Bureau spokesman Mark Woodward said a newly activated
licence for the property brought it back onto the bureau's radar, that the
operators were tied to the group arrested before, and that this was part of a
shell game in which another group takes over after a bust. [@1] The bureau's
investigation into the grow began in November 2023. [@2] KTEN put the weight of
marijuana seized at 1,100 pounds; the bureau's release and KOKH reported 1,120
pounds, the figure used here. [@1]

Woodward said agents found buyers taking more than 50 pounds of marijuana from
the farm per visit and operators loading as much as 1,000 pounds at a time into
refrigerated semi-trucks bound for black markets in New York City, Chicago and
parts of Florida. [@2] Carter County Sheriff Chris Bryant said Ardmore sits
halfway between Dallas and Oklahoma City and can serve as a meet-up point for
illegal drug movements out of the county. [@1]
