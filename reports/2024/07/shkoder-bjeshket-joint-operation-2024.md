Albanian and Montenegrin police destroyed 11,358 cannabis plants in the Kelmend area of Malësi e Madhe, on Albania's border with Montenegro, in an operation the two forces presented as their first joint action against cannabis. [@1,2] A man from Shkodër caught red-handed at the site was arrested, and officers seized a firearm with ammunition and a shotgun, both held without a permit. [@1,2] The plants were spread across three parcels in difficult mountain terrain. [@2]

## A joint press conference

Officers from Shkodër and Podgorica and anti-drug units from both countries took part, and the result was announced at a joint press conference. [@2] Montenegro's deputy police director said joint operations with Albania against drug trafficking would continue, describing cross-border criminal groups as profit-driven and both police forces as reliable partners. [@2] Shkodër's police director said cannabis cultivation in the county had fallen, though Shkodër remained on the red map. [@2]

Police said 229,000 cannabis plants had been destroyed across Albania since January. [@1]
