Detectives arrested four people at the Fisheries Estate in Bamburi, Mombasa
County, on 12 July 2024 and recovered more than half a tonne of cannabis
sativa that had recently arrived from Tanzania. [@1] The haul was valued at
about Sh16.5 million. [@1]

The suspects were named as Dorris Adika, her son David Omondi, her niece
Belinda Achieng and 61-year-old storekeeper Rhoda Vonza. [@1] A police
spokesperson said the suspects had been monitored for a while before the
arrest. [@1]

They were expected to be arraigned at the Shanzu Law Courts on drug
trafficking charges. [@1] Police linked the seizure to a larger trafficking
network moving cannabis from Tanzania into Kenya, a connection drawn again a
week later when a van carrying about half a tonne of bhang was stopped in
Kwale. [@2]
