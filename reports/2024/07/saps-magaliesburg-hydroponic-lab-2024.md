Members of the South African Police Service (SAPS) in Gauteng dismantled a
clandestine hydroponic cannabis laboratory worth about R40 million on a small
holding in Magaliesburg, West Rand district, on 18 July 2024. [@1,2] Officers
had operationalised information about the suspected laboratory, investigated,
and executed a search warrant for the property. [@1,2]

The search uncovered a fully operational cannabis laboratory. [@1,2] Police
seized dagga plants, processed products, chemicals and equipment, as well as a
signal jamming device, live rounds of ammunition, a gas pistol and an air
rifle. [@1,2] The team drew on organised crime, crime intelligence
counter-narcotics, forensic chemistry, intervention, tactical response, K9 and
emergency medical units. [@1,2]

Seven suspects were arrested: a Scottish national, a Russian national, a
Tanzanian national, two Lesotho nationals and two South Africans. [@1,2] They
were due before the Krugersdorp Magistrate's Court on 22 July 2024 on charges
including dealing in and cultivating dagga, possession of unlicensed
ammunition and contravening the Electronic Communications Act. [@1,2]
Provincial commissioner Lieutenant General Tommy Mthombeni said it was the
third clandestine laboratory found in a few weeks. [@1,2]
