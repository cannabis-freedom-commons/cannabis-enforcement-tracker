The National Drug Law Enforcement Agency's Ondo command said it had destroyed about 56 hectares of suspected hemp farmland at various locations in the state. [@1,2] Commander Kayode Raji gave the figure on 8 July 2024 in Akure while decorating 51 newly promoted officers. [@1,2]

The most recent site, about 17 hectares, took officers three days to clear, and its main planter was in custody and would be charged in court soon. [@1,2] Raji also said the command had seized more than 18,000 kilograms of assorted drugs, mainly hemp, and had arrested over 140 suspects with 41 convictions that year. [@1,2]
