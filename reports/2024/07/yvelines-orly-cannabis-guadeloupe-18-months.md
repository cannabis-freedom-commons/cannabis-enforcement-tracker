A court in the Yvelines sentenced a 51-year-old man on 18 July 2024 for
organising cannabis shipments from the Paris region to Guadeloupe. [@1] The
sentence was 18 months in prison, nine of them suspended. [@1] He had been
arrested at Orly airport the day before the ruling as he prepared to travel to
the French West Indies. [@1] Sending cannabis from mainland France to Guadeloupe
ran against the usual direction of the trade. [@1]

The arrest followed a judicial police investigation in Plaisir, in the Yvelines.
[@1] Trappes customs officers had earlier searched a parcel at a delivery
company and found close to 2 kg of drugs bound for Guadeloupe. [@1] Analysis of
communications then led investigators to detain a mother and her daughter at
their home in the Paris region. [@1] The father then took full responsibility
for the trafficking. [@1] He admitted buying cannabis in Seine-Saint-Denis and
packing it for shipment. [@1]

Officers found about 1.5 kg of cannabis, herb and resin, packed in a chocolate
box in his luggage. [@1] He said the drugs he meant to carry were to repay an
earlier seizure owed to those who ordered the shipments. [@1] The mother and
daughter, who knew nothing of the trafficking, were cleared. [@1]
