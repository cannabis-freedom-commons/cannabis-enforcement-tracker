Police dismantled a criminal group that sold synthetic drugs in Chisinau,
Ungheni, Straseni and Balti districts. [@1] The group comprised four men and two
women from the capital who distributed drugs through dead drops. [@1] The
suspects aged 19 to 29 were detained following prosecutorial and special
investigative measures. [@1] A criminal case was opened and 30-day arrest
warrants were issued for the accused. [@2] If found guilty they face 5 to 10
years in prison. [@1]

House searches seized 500 grams of PVP-type synthetics, 400 grams of
amphetamine, 350 grams of mephedrone, 200 grams of hashish and marijuana, plus
small precision scales and packaging packets. [@1] Police estimated the value of
all the seized drugs at one million lei. [@1]

The drugs were sold to consumers through internet shops created in the Telegram
app. [@1] Ziarul de Garda reported the group was dismantled by officers of the
Anti-Drug Directorate of the National Investigation Inspectorate. [@2] The group
was documented over one month as operating with dead-drop distributors. [@2]
