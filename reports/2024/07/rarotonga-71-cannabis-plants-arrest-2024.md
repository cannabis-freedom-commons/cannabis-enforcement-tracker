Cook Islands police arrested a 49-year-old man caught with 71 cannabis plants.
[@1] Police strategic and media advisor Trevor Pitt said the man was arrested
and charged on Friday over what he described as a well-constructed and organised
crop of cannabis seedlings. [@1] He was remanded into prison on Saturday after
appearing in court. [@1]

He was to appear in court again the following week. [@1]

The same police roundup reported three rental-car crashes on Rarotonga roads
over the past weekend. [@1] It also reported the separate arrest of a man
charged with assaulting a child and a woman after a domestic incident on
