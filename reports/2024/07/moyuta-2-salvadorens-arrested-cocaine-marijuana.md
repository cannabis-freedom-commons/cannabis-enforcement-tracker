The detainees were 36-year-old Belter Estefani Moge Arévalo and 30-year-old
Óscar Gustavo Quintanilla Martínez, riding a blue Yamaha motorcycle with
Salvadoran plates M365600. [@1]

The SGAIA anti-narcotics unit's field tests on both packages returned
presumptive positive results for cocaine and marijuana. [@1] Police
investigations say the men had been entering Guatemala through blind spots,
uncontrolled points along the border, and both will answer for their acts before
the competent court. [@2]

Authorities did not confirm whether the drugs were being moved from El Salvador
or whether the detainees will be deported. [@1] Article 40 of Guatemala's
anti-narcotics law sets a 6 to 10 year prison term for anyone found guilty of
illicit drug trafficking. [@2]
