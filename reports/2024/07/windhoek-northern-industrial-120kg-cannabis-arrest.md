The Namibian Police and the Namibia Revenue Agency intercepted a truck driver
allegedly transporting cannabis in Windhoek’s Northern Industrial Area on
Wednesday. [@1] The stop followed an intelligence-led operation in Windhoek.
[@1] Three Zimbabwean nationals aged 54, 45 and 34 were arrested in connection
with the incident. [@1] The three suspects were expected to appear in the
Windhoek Magistrate’s Court on Thursday. [@1]

Officers searched the driver’s truck and found cannabis weighing 120.6 kilograms
and worth N$6 million. [@1] Police spokesperson Deputy Commissioner Kauna
Shikwambi and Namra spokesperson Yarukeekuro Ndorokaze issued the account in a
joint statement the day before publication. [@1]

Police and Namra said they remain dedicated to stopping the illegal importation
and distribution of controlled substances in order to protect society. [@1] The
statement said these initiatives highlight the importance of the two
institutions safeguarding society from the negative impacts of drugs. [@1] The
statement asked the public to remain vigilant, report suspicious activity to law
enforcement and abide by laws protecting national safety. [@1]
