Police in Gokwe South arrested a three-person dagga syndicate on the night of 3
July 2024 after a tip-off from residents of Kuruwa Village under Chief
Nemangwe. [@1,2] Officers found Brian Kuruwa, 36, watering a dagga plantation
along the Svisvi River banks and arrested him, uprooting 67 plants from his
garden. [@1,2]

Further searches recovered a 50-kilo sack of loose dagga at the home of
Talkmore Muzondiwa, 30, and three 40-kilo bags hidden in the bedroom hut of
Shylet Magigani, 32. [@1,2] In total the police reported 6,500 live plants and
170 kilos of dried packaged dagga. [@1,2] During the raids Muzondiwa's husband,
kraal head Joseph Kuruwa, 43, mobilised villagers to fight the police, and
officers fired warning shots to overcome them. [@1]

The three appeared before a Gokwe magistrate on 4 July, were convicted of
cultivation and possession of dagga, and were sentenced the same day. [@1,2]
Brian Kuruwa received four years with labour with one year suspended,
Muzondiwa 24 months with eight months suspended, and Magigani an effective
three years. [@1,2]
