Police in Homa Bay County have uprooted and destroyed more than 40 acres of bhang
planted in the Gwasi hills, in ongoing operations within the Gwasi forest, a gazetted
water tower spanning over 6,000 acres in Suba South Constituency. [@1,2]

The current phase is led by Suba Sub-County Deputy County Commissioner Sebastian Okiring,
who said officers had worked across parts of the hills over about two weeks. [@1,2]
Officers reached the latest five-acre plot on Saturday after a tip from members of the
public, taking several hours to reach the fields. [@1,2] Two camps have been set up at the
base of the hills in the Kinchororio and Kithereka areas to clear the remaining crop, and
officers are working with other agencies and the Kenya Forest Service to plant trees where
bhang was uprooted and to guard against re-encroachment. [@1,2]
