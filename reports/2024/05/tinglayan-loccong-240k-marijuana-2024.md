## The operation

A joint force of Cordillera police, the Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency, the Air Force, the Coast Guard, the Army and police counter-intelligence overran an 8,000-square-metre marijuana plantation in a communal forest in Barangay Loccong, Tinglayan, Kalinga, on 1 May 2024. [@1,2] Officers uprooted 240,000 fully grown marijuana plants and set them on fire at the site. [@1,2] Police valued the plantation at P48 million. [@1,2]

PRO-Cordillera director Brig. Gen. David Peredo called the raid a milestone in the region's campaign against marijuana cultivation. [@1,2]

## No arrests reported

The reports name no growers and no arrests at the plantation. [@1,2]
