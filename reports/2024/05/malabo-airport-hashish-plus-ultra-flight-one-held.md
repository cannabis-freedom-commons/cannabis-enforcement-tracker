Gendarmerie officers at Malabo airport found a hashish consignment in luggage on
a Plus Ultra Líneas Aéreas flight that had arrived from Madrid. [@1] The
detection at the airport led the authorities to open an investigation into a
trafficking network. [@1]

The authorities made public the detention of one person, who told customs
officers it was the third time he had collected the drug. [@1] That person was
described as the one who collected the merchandise and handed it to the
recipients, who distributed it to customers or end users. [@1]

The Equatorial Guinean authorities did not give the exact origin of the
substance or identify who sent it. [@1] The scheme routed oversized luggage
through a separate check-in desk and onto the baggage belt, so that it skipped
the cargo terminal, customs and police controls. [@1]

The route was operated with Plus Ultra aircraft and crews from its start in 2021
until November 2025. [@1] Spain's UDEF financial crime unit later entered the
airline's offices and detained several senior executives. [@1]
