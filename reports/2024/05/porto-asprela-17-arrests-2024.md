Porto's Polícia de Segurança Pública dismantled a trafficking network selling
drugs around the Asprela university area on Monday 27 May 2024. [@1] Officers
detained 17 people, 15 men and two women aged between 20 and 52. [@1] All those
detained lived in the city of Porto and were heard by the judicial authorities
on Tuesday 28 May. [@1] An earlier police account given while the operation was
still under way put the detention count at 18. [@2]

The operation involved 60 home searches and six non-home searches in the
Paranhos area, supported by rapid-intervention teams and the Special Police Unit
detachment. [@1] Officers reported seizing more than 30 kg of drugs including
hashish, liamba and amphetamines, alongside firearms and more than 58,000 euros
in cash. [@1] Police described the haul as more than 64,000 individual doses,
mainly hashish, while liamba, amphetamines such as ecstasy, cocaine and heroin
were also sold. [@1] Officers also seized 58,214 euros in banknotes, seven
bladed weapons, seven firearms and ammunition, plus other drug-dealing
paraphernalia. [@1] A same-day police account described the seizure as a
significant quantity of drugs, fundamentally hashish, plus some synthetic drugs.
[@2]

Police identified Bairro de São Tomé as the business epicentre, close to
faculties including the Porto Engineering Institute and the Faculty of
Psychology and Education Sciences. [@1] The Jornal de Notícias, cited in the
report, described university students as the main clients. [@1] The
investigation had lasted three years and extended to searches in Agra do Amial,
Carriçal and Regado. [@1]
