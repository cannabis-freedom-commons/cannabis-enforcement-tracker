Egypt's General Administration for Drug Control arrested people accused of
trading and promoting drugs in three police districts of Port Said. [@1] The
three entries name 14 people in all, two of them women, detained in the
Al-Zohour, Al-Dawahi and Awal El-Ganoub police districts of Port Said. [@1]

Three men in Al-Zohour were holding 4 kg of hashish, a kilogram each of Ice,
heroin and hypothyl, a quantity of voodoo, and a shotgun with bullets. [@1] A
man in Al-Dawahi was holding 3 kg of hashish, and a man in Al-Zohour 2 kg of
hashish. [@1] Three men in Awal El-Ganoub held 5 kg of hashish and a kilogram of
heroin. [@1] The article also records 7 kg of hypothyl, a drug it reports by
weight but does not describe, in two Al-Zohour cases. [@1]

Police put the value of all the seized drugs at about 3.5 million Egyptian
pounds. [@1] Police said legal measures had been taken and the suspects were
being brought before the Public Prosecution. [@1]
