Four men were jailed at Leeds Crown Court on 22 May 2024 for running a cannabis
factory in Huddersfield. [@1,2] Police found 670 cannabis plants worth an estimated
£510,000 inside a former commercial building on Blackmoorfoot Road during a raid
in March. [@1,2] Enea Bajrami, 25, and Ervin Jamaku, 22, were each sentenced to one
year and three months; Florin Cena, 23, received one year; and Denald Pashja, 26,
received eight months, all for producing a class B drug. [@1,2]

## Police reaction

Inspector Andy Barraclough of West Yorkshire Police called the site a significant
operation that could have produced drugs worth many hundreds of thousands of
pounds. [@1] He said closing the factory and convicting the four men producing
cannabis there would have dealt a blow to the organised criminals who financed it
and expected revenue from it, and he thanked members of the public whose
information supports action against cannabis production in Huddersfield. [@1,2]
The Cannabis Law Report carried the same account of the case. [@2]
