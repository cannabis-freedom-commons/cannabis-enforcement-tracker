On 7 May 2024 the Garda National Drugs and Organised Crime Bureau and
Revenue Customs Service ran two intelligence-led operations seizing two
consignments of herbal cannabis, together about 184 kilos with an estimated
value of €3,680,000, one in Wexford and one in Dublin. [@1,2]

In Wexford officers seized 139 kilos of cannabis herb worth about
€2,780,000 and arrested a man in his 60s, who was detained in the
south-eastern region under drug-trafficking legislation. [@1,2] In a second
operation the same day, officers seized 45 kilos worth €900,000 and arrested
a man in his 50s, detained at a west Dublin station under the same
legislation. [@1]

Both men were charged on 9 May: the man in his 50s over the €900,000 seizure
was due before Blanchardstown District Court, and the man in his 60s over
the €2,780,000 seizure before Gorey District Court, both that morning. [@3]
All of the drugs were subject to further analysis as investigations
continued. [@1]
