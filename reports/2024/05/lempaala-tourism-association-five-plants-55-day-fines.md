Pirkanmaan käräjäoikeus, the district court of the Pirkanmaa region, sentenced
the man who was responsible for the premises of the Lempäälän seudun
matkailuyhdistys, a local tourism association in Lempäälä, to 55 day-fines for a
drug offence. [@1] Police found five cannabis plants during a house search in
the premises the association rented. [@1] A lamp had been used to help the
plants grow. [@1] According to the court, the association's representative had
grown hemp for use as a narcotic or as a raw material for one. [@1] The
defendant is now 51 years old and the offence took place in Lempäälä on 27 May
2024. [@1] The court dated the offence to 27 May 2024 in Lempäälä. [@1]

The prosecutor asked for a punishment for the drug offence and for a victim
surcharge. [@1] The defendant admitted the act in the preliminary investigation.
[@1] The defendant did not come to court, but had been summoned by a notice that
the case could be heard and decided in his absence. [@1] The court found that
this evidence supported the charge and the admission. [@1]

The court found no reason to doubt that the admission was valid and convicted
the defendant of the drug offence. [@1] The court sentenced him to 55 day-fines
for the drug offence. [@1] The fine was set at 55 day-fines of six euros each,
making 330 euros. [@1] He must also pay an 80-euro victim surcharge. [@1]
