On 26 May 2024, gendarmes from the Mewoulou post in Yaoundé's 6th subdivision
arrested suspected drug trafficker Nté Eric and seized three bags of cannabis
weighing 45 kilograms in total. [@1] The arrest took place at about 4 p.m.
behind the Etoug-Ebe bilingual high school after a search carefully organized by
the officers. [@1] He was presented to the press in Yaoundé on 4 June 2024,
where he said he had turned to the illegal trade to support his poor and sick
mother. [@2] The former Yaoundé taxi driver said he took up the activity, which
he considered more profitable, to care for his poor and very ill mother. [@1]

He said he noticed people buying and using cannabis at the Omnisport stadium,
decided it was a lucrative market, and began selling there at 3,000 francs per
packet. [@1] He said he had already raised 815,000 francs toward the one million
he needed for her care. [@1] The interim commander of the Yaoundé II gendarmerie
company reported that the three confiscated bags weighed 45 kilograms in total.
[@3] Acting on information provided by Eric, the authorities opened an
investigation to arrest his accomplices. [@3]
