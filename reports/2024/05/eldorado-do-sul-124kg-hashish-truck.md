Brazil's Federal Police stopped a truck in Eldorado do Sul, in the Porto Alegre
metropolitan area of Rio Grande do Sul, in the early hours of Saturday 11 May
2024. [@1] Officers found 124 kilograms of hashish in the vehicle, a drug
Subrayado described as made from marijuana buds. [@1] The truck was registered
in Yaguarón, Paraguay, and had been caught by Uruguayan police in 2021 bringing
contraband timber for prefabricated houses to the Lago Merín beach resort. [@1]

A migration official also reported the driver for threats at that time, and the
truck was held in Uruguay for 90 days. [@1] Subrayado reported that the driver
and owner of the vehicle was a police officer in Montevideo and kept carrying
freight between Brazil and Uruguay. [@1]

The truck driver was arrested in the act for drug trafficking and taken to
Federal Police headquarters in Porto Alegre. [@2]
