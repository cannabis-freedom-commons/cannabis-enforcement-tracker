Macau's Judiciary Police arrested a 23-year-old Thai university student at Macau
International Airport on 7 May 2024 as he collected his luggage, suspecting him
of smuggling cannabis into the city and on to Hong Kong. [@1]

Investigators at the airport spotted him moving with the arriving crowd and
avoiding their gaze, then took him to the customs X-ray machine, where the image
showed the unusual contents of his suitcase. [@2] Officers found 31
vacuum-sealed packets of marijuana buds in transparent plastic bags, 22 of them
hidden inside courier bags, weighing 10,448 grams in total. [@2] The suitcase
also held four bottles of gummies containing tetrahydrocannabinol (THC), 60
sweets weighing about 112 grams, the first THC gummies found in Macau. [@2]
Macau police put the estimated street value of the seizure at 10.8 million
patacas. [@1] A canine unit from the public security police also checked the
suitcase and signalled on it before officers opened it. [@4]

Under questioning the student admitted a drug gang had paid him to collect the
drugs at an airport in Thailand and carry them to Macau. [@1] He said he was to
take the golden shuttle bus across the Hong Kong–Zhuhai–Macau Bridge and hand
the load on in Hong Kong. [@3] He refused to give more details about his methods
or his employer, but Cheng Pou reported that he had so far received no payment
and did not know how much he was promised. [@4] Police said they were still
tracing the gang and its leaders. [@3] Officers referred him to the Public
Prosecutions Office (檢察院) on suspicion of illegally selling narcotic drugs and
psychotropic substances. [@1] A senior investigator, Lei Ka Wo, announced the
case at a special press conference in the city. [@2] The 23-year-old was
surnamed Kham Kham Kaew. [@2]
