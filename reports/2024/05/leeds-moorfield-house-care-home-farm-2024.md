Six Albanian men were jailed at Leeds Crown Court on 16 May 2024 for growing
cannabis plants worth up to £446,000 in the derelict former Moorfield House
care home in Moortown, Leeds. [@1,2] Alitor Deda, 30, Andi Teta, 33, and Ali
Qerfozi, 42, were each sentenced to two years; Erijon Gjoka, 21, received 18
months; and Edison Markeci, 21, and Agostin Marku, 36, received 16 months
each. [@2]

Officers raided the Grade II-listed building on 4 December 2023 and found 811
plants growing in six rooms, capable of producing 44 kg of cannabis, with
growing equipment worth up to £45,000. [@1,2] Officers also found baseball
bats and machetes alongside the high-powered lamps. [@1] The men had worked
at the site as gardeners. [@2] Sergeant Simon Green said the farm was clearly
run as an organised criminal enterprise, and its removal stopped nearly half
a million pounds of drugs reaching the streets. [@1]
