Paraguay's National Anti-Drug Secretariat (Senad) ran Operation Frente Sur in Caazapá National Park in two incursion phases between 2 and 11 May 2024, announcing the results on 13 May. [@1,2] Agents detected and destroyed 53 drug camps holding a total of 145,300 kg of ready marijuana, and eradicated 111 hectares of cannabis crops. [@1,2]

Senad calculated that the operation took about 478 tonnes of marijuana out of circulation, representing some $14 million in lost profit for trafficking financiers. [@1,2] The agency said the operation struck criminal structures that had spent years expanding production and stockpiling drugs inside the park's forests. [@1]

The 16,000-hectare park is part of the Upper Paraná Atlantic Forest, and the national forestry institute Infona had recorded 99.5 hectares deforested there for illicit crops between 2020 and 2022. [@1] No arrests were reported. [@2]
