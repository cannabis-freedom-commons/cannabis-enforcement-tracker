Officers of the Civil Guard's Huelva Maritime Service seized 293 bales of hashish
weighing 11,400 kg off the Huelva coast and arrested seven people. [@1]

Agents surprised the occupants of six semi-rigid boats, three of them with four
engines each, sailing close to the coast; the crews fled at speed and threw the
bundles overboard before being intercepted, and officers recovered them from the
sea. [@1]
