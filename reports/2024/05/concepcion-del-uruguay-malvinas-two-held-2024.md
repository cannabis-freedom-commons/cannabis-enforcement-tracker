Personnel of the Argentine Naval Prefecture detained a man and a woman and
seized cocaine and marijuana in three searches in Concepción del Uruguay, Entre
Ríos. [@1] A 43-year-old man and a 47-year-old woman were held at a police
facility in connection with the investigation. [@2] The searches took place in
three houses in Barrio Malvinas Argentinas on the afternoon of Thursday 23 May
2024. [@2]

The ministry said the two were linked to the sale of narcotics and that
marijuana, cocaine, cash, eight mobile phones, a precision scale and material of
interest were seized. [@1] Local reporting added that officers seized cocaine
portioned for sale, marijuana, cash in national and foreign currency, mobile
phones and digital scales. [@2] They also seized a .38 revolver, a 9 mm pistol
and ammunition. [@2]

The searches were carried out under Entre Ríos provincial law 10.566 on
narcomenudeo, local retail drug dealing, read with the national drug law 23.737.
[@2] The searches were ordered by the city's Juzgado de Garantías, the
provincial guarantee court, under Judge Alejandrina Herrero, with personnel of
the Entre Ríos provincial police taking part. [@1] The case was being run by the
Toxicology Division of the Uruguay departmental police headquarters together
with the naval prefecture. [@2] Provincial media said the afternoon deployment
involved the Toxicology Division with support from the Special Guard and the
naval prefecture, and that three houses in the neighbourhood were involved. [@4]

Provincial outlet ElEntreRíos reported the operation with the prefecture
credited as its source. [@3]
