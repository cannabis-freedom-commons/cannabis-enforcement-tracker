Gendarmes of the Sección Monte Caseros, part of Escuadrón 7 Paso de los Libres
"Cabo Misael Pereyra", were carrying out roadside prevention work at kilometre
363 of Ruta Nacional 14, at the Piedritas toll booth, when they stopped two
trucks with foreign plates carrying hydrocarbons. [@1] The two fuel tankers had
set out from Asunción, Paraguay, and were bound for the city of Campana in
Buenos Aires province. [@1]

The paperwork presented by the drivers showed no irregularities, but a thorough
inspection of the vehicles found 103 plastic packages stowed in the side lockers
of each truck, holding plant buds with a strong smell resembling marijuana. [@1]
The unit's criminalistics and forensic studies staff ran Narcotest field tests
on the samples, which came back positive for Cannabis Sativa with a total weight
of 45 kilos 822 grams. [@1] The two trucks were also put through the scanner at
the Complejo Terminal de Cargas (COTECAR), the cargo terminal scanner, whose
high-contrast images confirmed they hid no larger quantities of drugs elsewhere.
[@3]

The Federal Court and the Federal Prosecutor's Office of Paso de los Libres
ordered both drivers detained and the drug and both trucks seized. [@1] Radio
Libertad reported that the two drivers were being held incommunicado. [@3] The
Ministry of National Security's own release on the same operation said
Gendarmería also held both drivers, but it published no value for the load. [@2]

Noticias Chajarí reported that the seizure happened near the Corrientes town of
Juan Pujol and that the two trucks were Paraguayan-flagged, crediting Diario El
Litoral de Corrientes. [@4]
