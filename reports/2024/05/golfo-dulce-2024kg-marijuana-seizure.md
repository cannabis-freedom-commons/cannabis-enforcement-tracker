Costa Rica's National Coast Guard Service confirmed the seizure, which was made
through joint patrolling with the United States. [@1] Officers seized about two
and a half tonnes of drugs about 163 kilometres offshore from Golfo Dulce on the
southern Pacific coast. [@1] The boat was carrying 2,024 packages of marijuana
and 485 packages of cocaine of about one kilogram each, apparently of Colombian
origin. [@1] At the naval base officers counted 2,024 one-kilo packages of
marijuana and 485 one-kilo packages of cocaine, for a total of 2,509 kilos of
drugs. [@2] Two Costa Rican brothers and a Nicaraguan crewed the boat, and all
three were detained. [@1] The detainees are two Costa Rican brothers surnamed
Irias, aged 47 and 31, the elder with a judicial record for drug transport in
2019. [@2] The third occupant is a Nicaraguan surnamed Membreño aged 23 who was
in the country with irregular migration status. [@2]

The interception happened on the previous Sunday, and the sea transfer took more
than 15 hours because of weather and distance. [@1] The boat was detected on
Sunday 204 kilometres northwest of the entrance to Golfo Dulce thanks to flights
by a United States maritime patrol aircraft. [@2] After the alert, Special
Operations Group and Coast Guard officers set out toward the signalled location.
[@2] The fast boat was about 10 metres long with three 200-horsepower outboard
motors. [@1] La Nación measured the intercepted vessel at about 10.9 metres,
grey with blue, equipped with three 200-horsepower outboard motors. [@2] It had
no visible name or registration and was driven by the elder of the Irias
brothers. [@2]

The seized boat, load and detainees were taken to Golfito in the early hours of
Monday and placed at the disposal of the Public Ministry and the Drug Control
Police. [@2] Security Minister Mario Zamora said the type of drug found
indicated it was meant to be sold in Costa Rica. [@1] He said the presence of
marijuana suggested its destination was the country and the crew composition
showed it was coming for the domestic market. [@2]
