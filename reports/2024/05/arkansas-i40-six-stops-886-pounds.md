Arkansas State Police's Interstate Criminal Patrol, the force's highway
interdiction unit, seized 886 pounds of marijuana and one handgun in six traffic
stops on Interstate 40 over 10 days. [@1]

Troopers transported driver Jalen Gillispie, 24, of Spencer, Oklahoma, and
passenger Daviauna Partee, 21, of Tulsa, Oklahoma, to the Lonoke County
Detention Center after the 29 April stop. [@1] Gillispie was charged with
possession of a controlled substance, simultaneous possession of drugs and
firearms and possession of drug paraphernalia, and Partee with possession of a
controlled substance with purpose to deliver. [@1] Troopers arrested Carlos
Smith, 53, of Louisville, Kentucky, on 30 April and charged him with possession
with purpose to deliver a controlled substance, unauthorized usage of property
and improper lane change. [@1] Mark Williams, 71, was taken into custody on 1
May and charged with possession with intent to deliver, possession of drug
paraphernalia, speeding and expired tags. [@1] Marlita Williams, 44, of Hope
Mills, North Carolina, was taken into custody on 2 May and charged with
possession of a controlled substance with purpose to deliver, possession of drug
paraphernalia and careless and prohibited driving. [@1]

Five of the six stops were in Lonoke County and one in Conway County, and the
loads ranged from 31 pounds to 300 pounds. [@2] At the 25 April stop, troopers
found 75 pounds of marijuana in three large duffle bags inside a transported
vehicle at the 164-mile marker, then another 68 pounds in a later search, 143
pounds in all. [@1] The 29 April stop at the 175-mile marker yielded 120 pounds
of vacuum-sealed marijuana in six duffle bags and a 9mm handgun. [@1] Troopers
found 31 pounds of marijuana in a Kia Sportage at the 170-mile marker in Lonoke
on 30 April. [@1] At the 102-mile marker in Conway County on 1 May, troopers
found 70 pounds of marijuana hidden in trash bags in the trunk of a Toyota
Corolla. [@1] A 2 May search of a Nissan van at the 178-mile marker in Lonoke
County found boxes and suitcases holding about 222 pounds of marijuana. [@1] On
3 May troopers stopped a Sprinter van at the 171-mile marker and found 11 boxes
of vacuum-sealed marijuana weighing about 300 pounds. [@1] The six marijuana
seizures added together come to the 886 pounds the force reported for the
period. [@3] The same statement said the Interstate Criminal Patrol team had
already confiscated over 4,000 pounds of illegal marijuana in Arkansas in 2024.
[@3]

Colonel Mike Hagar said the team's success in intercepting large amounts of
illegal drugs in less than two weeks was a testament to its dedication. [@1] The
vehicles were moving between Oklahoma City, Atlanta, Memphis and Los Angeles
toward East Point, Georgia. [@1]
