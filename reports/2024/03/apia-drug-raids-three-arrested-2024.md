Samoa police conducted three drug raids in Apia last week, netting marijuana and
methamphetamine along with drug utensils, while cash and firearms were also
seized from one location. [@1] Three people were arrested at the other two raid
locations, where marijuana and methamphetamine were found. [@1] A 31-year-old
man of Fasitoouta and Faleasi'u was arrested at a home at Tulaele during a
police investigation of a domestic dispute after methamphetamine and utensils
were discovered. [@1] Two men aged 38 and 33 were arrested at a home in Alafua
and jointly charged with possession of narcotics, and have been remanded in
custody until 11 March 2024. [@1]

At Fugalei Market, drug sniffing dogs found marijuana and methamphetamine, but
the owners were not found and are believed to have dropped the drugs when they
saw the police team. [@1] Reports from the public that drugs were being traded
at the market prompted the raid, and police said they will reopen an office at
the market to monitor illicit drug trafficking. [@1]

Deputy police commissioner Papalii Monalisa Tia'i-Keti said police were
concerned that drugs were found at Fugalei Market just a short distance away
from an all girl's primary school. [@1]
