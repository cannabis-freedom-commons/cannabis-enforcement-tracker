Kimbe police arrested eight people after a boat they stopped during a routine
patrol was found carrying 17 packages of marijuana. [@2] The boat fled when the
officers approached and was caught at Taveliai Village, where the eight
passengers were detained. [@2] A search of the boat found 16 unopened packages
and one opened package of marijuana. [@2]

Post Courier reported about 100 kilograms of marijuana in the 17 bags on the
dinghy with eight passengers. [@1] Post Courier put the load's value at K2
million and called it, on police estimates, the largest marijuana haul in the
country's history. [@1] PNG News Update reported the load as worth about K2.37
million on the street and said the marijuana would be presented as evidence in
court. [@2] Police also confiscated a homemade gun from the suspects. [@2]

Provincial police commander Superintendent Peter Barkie, whose province is West
New Britain, praised the patrol's vigilance and said the seizure disrupted drug
trafficking in the province. [@2] Barkie did not disclose the suspects'
identities or the charges they might face. [@2]
