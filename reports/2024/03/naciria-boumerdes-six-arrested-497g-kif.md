The brigade acted after receiving information about a criminal drug-trafficking
network active in Naciria commune. [@2] Investigations identified, located and
arrested the suspects. [@2] A later overview confirms that six traffickers were
arrested by the National Gendarmerie at Naciria in Boumerdes. [@1]

The later overview describes the haul as nearly 500 grams of kif traité plus
bladed weapons, mobile phones and cash. [@1]

The suspects were referred to the competent courts. [@2] The Gendarmerie
presented the operation as part of its fight against organised crime in its
Boumerdes area. [@2] The overview presents the dismantling as evidence that
interior networks are often fed by cross-border trafficking channels. [@1]
