NDLEA officers went to Okaka in Yenagoa at about 7 p.m. on 20 March 2024 to arrest a notorious female dealer known as Rebecca, after officers had made a pre-purchase of cannabis sativa and crack cocaine from her on 15 March. [@1,2] A mob of cult members and local youths attacked the team with bottles, stones and improvised explosive devices, seizing exhibits and freeing the dealer, and the officers exchanged gunfire with them. [@1,2]

A bricklayer identified as Moses Akada, who was returning home from work, was struck by a bullet in the chaos and died; the mob later dumped his body in front of the NDLEA state command headquarters. [@1,2] The officers arrested one 14-year-old boy, Abraham Aderin, who had sparked the mob, and brought the situation under control with police and Operation Doo-Akpo patrol teams. [@1] The NDLEA state commander expressed sadness over the death while defending the operation. [@1]
