Ecuadorian Army soldiers captured three men in the La Paz sector of Montúfar
canton in Carchi province during a search operation. [@1] Soldiers stopped and
inspected a taxi whose occupants behaved suspiciously and arrested them for
moving a hidden marijuana load. [@1] Two of the detained people were Colombian
nationals and the third was Ecuadorian. [@1]

The Armed Forces said military personnel detained a vehicle with three
occupants, two of them Colombian, who were carrying 15 kg of illicit substances
(marijuana), 2 cellphones and cash. [@1] The vehicle inspection found 92 US
dollars, 100,180 Colombian pesos, three cellphones with chargers, two watches
and two black bags holding about 15 kg of a substance that tested positive for
marijuana. [@1] The seized items were placed at the disposal of the competent
authorities for the corresponding legal process. [@1] The Carchi Governorate
said the evidence and the apprehended people were brought before the
corresponding authorities for the respective procedures. [@1]

Colombian and Ecuadorian authorities were reported to be on alert over
clandestine criminal movements at the two countries’ border crossings, with
security reinforced at the boundaries. [@1]
