Anti-narcotics police, soldiers and prosecutors cleared illegal plantations in
Malacatillo, La Vega and Nueva Candelaria, in Tajumulco, San Marcos, on 19 March
2024. [@1]

The operation destroyed 13,573 marijuana plants. [@1] The National Civil Police
valued the destroyed marijuana at about 5,089,875 quetzales. [@1] The same
operation uprooted 1,165,975 poppy plants, which the police valued at 29,149,375
quetzales. [@1] TV Azteca Guatemala, citing the same joint operation, gave the
same 13,573 marijuana plants valued at 5,089,875 quetzales and the same
1,165,975 poppy plants. [@2]

No arrests were reported in either account. [@1]

The authorities are the SGAIA, the anti-narcotics analysis division of the
National Civil Police, the Guatemalan Army and the Public Prosecutor's Office.
[@2]

TV Azteca Guatemala described the operation as the first eradication with
figures in the millions reported that year. [@2] Authorities said the
eradication work would continue in the highlands of San Marcos department. [@2]
