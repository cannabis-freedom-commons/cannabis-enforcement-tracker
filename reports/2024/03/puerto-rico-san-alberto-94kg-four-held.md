Prefectura Naval Argentina officers detained four men after two occupants began
unloading bags at a house in paraje Helvecia, Puerto Rico, Misiones. [@1]
Officers stopped the second car, acting as a “puntero”, about 100 metres away
and detained its two occupants. [@1] They had tracked the two cars for about 60
kilometres along National Route 12 and nearby local roads before one entered
Helvecia. [@1]

The operation followed earlier inquiries into suspected drug trafficking near
Puerto San Alberto, where patrol officers took position at kilometre 1,744 of
the Paraná River. [@1] Officers saw several men unload bundles from a boat into
a vehicle, which sped away with a second car acting as a “puntero,” a scout car
intended to warn accomplices of road checkpoints. [@1] A search found 101
packages containing more than 94 kilograms of marijuana. [@1] El Marplatense
reported the same package count and weight and said Prefectura seized the two
vehicles. [@2]

The Federal Court of First Instance in Oberá ordered detention of the four men
and seizure of the drugs. [@1] The Ministry of Security said its investigation
was intended to combat drug trafficking. [@1]
