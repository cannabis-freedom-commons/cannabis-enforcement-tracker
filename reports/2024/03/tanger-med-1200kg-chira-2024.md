Moroccan security forces working with customs officers at Tanger Med port
foiled an international drug trafficking attempt in the early hours of
Saturday, seizing 1.2 tonnes of hashish. [@1,2] The cannabis resin, known
locally as chira, was hidden in compartments specially built into the metal
chassis of a Morocco-registered international goods truck. [@1,2]

The truck's 28-year-old Moroccan driver was arrested at the port. [@1,2]
He was placed under investigation by the judicial authorities under the
supervision of the prosecutor's office, which aims to identify everyone
involved and any national or international links of the trafficking
operation. [@1,2]
