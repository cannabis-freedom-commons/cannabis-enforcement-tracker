On 12 March 2024 the judicial court in Narbonne sentenced a Belgian lorry driver
to five years in prison with continued detention, a lifetime ban from French
territory and a customs fine of 890,025 euros. [@1] Customs officers had handed
the driver to the Perpignan judicial police when their procedure ended. [@1] The
driver, a Belgian national in his forties, was stopped while driving a Belgian
refrigerated heavy-goods vehicle on the A9. [@1] During the check he told
officers he had come from Spain and was heading to northern France to deliver
various goods to a supermarket chain. [@1] Officers judged those explanations
vague and ordered a full check of the vehicle combination. [@1]

A customs sniffer dog then guided officers during the inspection. [@1] In the
cab, the pallet doors and the load, the dog team found 11 sports bags holding
cannabis herb and resin. [@1] A complete search of the lorry recorded 430.5 kg
of cannabis resin and 16.5 kg of cannabis herb. [@1] The Narbonne customs
operation recovered 447 kg of cannabis in total on the A9 at Bages in the Aude
on 9 March 2024. [@1]
