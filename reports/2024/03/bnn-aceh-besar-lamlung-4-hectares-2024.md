Indonesia's National Narcotics Agency (BNN) destroyed a four-hectare marijuana
plantation spread over three sites in Aceh Besar regency, as announced in Aceh
Besar on Wednesday 6 March 2024 by Brigadier General Ruddi Setiawan, BNN's
deputy for narcotics eradication. [@1]

A combined force of 170 personnel from BNN, BNN's Aceh office, the military
and police, the prosecutor's office, customs, and the agriculture and forestry
departments took part in the eradication, which the agency linked to
post-election security operations. [@1] Two of the sites were partly harvested
fields at altitudes of 129 and 109 metres in Lamlung village, Indrapuri
district, and the wet marijuana plants destroyed weighed about seven tons.
[@1]

The agency said the plantations were found after public information about
marijuana shipments from Aceh to Lampung. [@1] BNN arrested a suspect
identified only as RZ and confiscated 12 sacks of dried marijuana. [@1]
