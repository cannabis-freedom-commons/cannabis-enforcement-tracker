Three men arrested after a drug bust at a residential compound in George Town's
industrial area were released on bail after being charged with possession of
ganja with intent to supply. [@1] A 57-year-old George Town resident was also
charged with possession of criminal property over cash seized during the arrest.
[@1] He and two Bodden Town men aged 28 and 29 appeared in court and were bailed
to return later that month. [@1]

Police seized around 450lbs of suspected ganja in several packages in a bust
near Seymour Drive in George Town's industrial area. [@2] Officers conducting
enquiries smelled ganja from an address with several individual apartments and
saw a man trying to discard something. [@2] Officers detained the man and found
he had a bag containing suspected ganja. [@2] A search of his apartment under
the Misuse of Drugs Act found more packages of ganja as well as over CI$5,000
and over US$800 in cash. [@2] A further search at the rear of the apartment
recovered additional packages, bringing the total to around 450lbs. [@2] The
57-year-old George Town man was arrested on suspicion of possession with intent
to supply and possession of criminal property. [@2] Two Bodden Town men aged 28
and 29 who were at the location were also arrested on suspicion of possession
with intent to supply. [@2]
