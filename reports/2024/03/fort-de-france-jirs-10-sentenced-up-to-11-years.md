Ten people, nine of them Martiniquais and one a national of Saint Lucia, were
convicted in March 2024 of importing cocaine, crack and cannabis from Saint
Lucia to Martinique by sea. [@1] The Fort-de-France correctional court's
interregional specialist section, the JIRS, heard the case on 28 March. [@1]

Police had seized a total of 1.3 tonnes of drugs, a dozen handguns and sums of
money. [@1] The court found the ten guilty and sentenced them to prison. [@2]
The prison terms ranged from two to eleven years. [@1]

The court barred the ten from carrying weapons and confiscated the seized items.
[@1] All ten were kept in detention except one who had fled, against whom the
court issued an arrest warrant. [@1] Only one of the ten defendants appealed the
sentences. [@1]
