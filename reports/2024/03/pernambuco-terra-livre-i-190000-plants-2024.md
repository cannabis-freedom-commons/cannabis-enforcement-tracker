The Federal Police announced the results of its latest cannabis eradication
operation in the sertão of Pernambuco, run between 21 February and 2 March 2024.
[@1] The operation destroyed about 190,000 cannabis plants and 19,000 seedlings
growing on 34 plots. [@1] Officers also seized 71 kilograms of cannabis ready
for consumption. [@1] The Federal Police did not say whether anyone was
arrested. [@1]

The plots lay on islands of the São Francisco river and on the mainland around
Orocó, Triunfo, Salgueiro, Cabrobó, Belém do São Francisco, Betânia, Flores,
Carnaubeira da Penha, Sertânia and Dormentes. [@1] The Federal Police estimated
that the destroyed plants, if harvested and pressed, could have produced 63
tonnes of the drug. [@1] Federal, civil, military and penal police took part,
working on land and on the water with inflatable boats and an aircraft of the
state air tactical group, GTA. [@1] Officers worked with land and river
incursions using inflatable boats and an aircraft of the GTA, the state public
security secretariat's air tactical group. [@1]

Gazeta Brasil and O DEBATE ON, republishing Portal Novo Norte, carry the same
Federal Police statement and name the operation Terra Livre I. [@3] The
statement says the raids are part of the strategy of the Federal Police's
general coordination against drugs, weapons, crimes against property and
criminal factions, based in Brasília, and of its Pernambuco regional office.
[@3] Gazeta Brasil's report of the same operation was first published on 8 March
2024. [@2]
