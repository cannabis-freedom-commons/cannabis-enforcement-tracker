On 25 April 2019, Marie-Nanette Taryn Julie of Les Canelles was found
cultivating twenty-one cannabis plants at her residence on Mahé. [@1]

Charged first with cultivation, she pleaded guilty on 30 November 2023 to the
alternative count of possessing the twenty-one plants, and the court noted she
was a first-time offender. [@1]

On 26 March 2024 the Supreme Court sentenced her to one year's imprisonment,
suspended for two years, and a fine of SCR10,000 payable by 31 December 2024,
with six months' imprisonment in default of payment. [@1] She was given 30
working days to appeal. [@1]
