Somerset County Sheriff's deputies executed a drug search warrant at a residence
on Waterville Road in Skowhegan at 9:40 a.m. on Friday, 1 March 2024. [@1] Three
people were in the house during the search and all three were arrested: Yao Bin
Cheng, 61, Ying Xia Liao, 59, and Xi Qiang Zhao, 56, all of Brooklyn, New York.
[@1] Each was charged with drug trafficking and cultivation of marijuana and
held at the Somerset County Jail on $10,000 cash bail each. [@1]

Inside the home officers seized 4,760 marijuana plants at various stages of
growth, more than $7,000 in suspected drug proceeds, a 2022 Toyota Tacoma pickup
and drug paraphernalia. [@1] Sheriff Dale Lancaster said the operation was one
of the largest his office had broken up, that the home had sparse living
quarters, and that deputies saw possible black mould and electrical problems.
[@3]

Detectives from the Waterville Police Department, Homeland Security agents and
the Drug Enforcement Administration took part, with help from the Somerset and
Kennebec County district attorneys, the U.S. Attorney's Office and the Maine
Office of Cannabis Policy. [@1] The Skowhegan code enforcement officer and fire
department were told of possible building code violations at the home. [@3]
Lancaster called the raid part of a wider investigation into illegal grows in
Somerset County. [@3] Their initial court appearance was scheduled for 5 June
2024 in the Somerset County Unified Court. [@2]

Xi Qiang Zhao struck a plea deal with prosecutors in December 2024, pleading
guilty to a misdemeanour that was later dismissed under a deferred disposition
agreement. [@4] Yao Bin Cheng's trial was called off in September 2026 after his
lawyer told a judge he had returned to China and did not intend to come back,
and the judge issued a no-bail arrest warrant and ordered forfeiture of his
$10,000 cash bail. [@4] Cheng still faces felony drug trafficking and marijuana
cultivation charges should he return to the United States. [@5]
