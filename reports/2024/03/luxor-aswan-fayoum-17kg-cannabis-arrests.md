Egypt's Public Security sector working with the Luxor, Giza, Aswan and Fayoum
security directorates reported results from a drive against people holding and
trading drugs and unlicensed firearms and ammunition. [@1] In Luxor, police
arrested a man described as having a criminal record. [@1] Police arrested a
second man described as having a criminal record in Luxor. [@1] In Aswan, police
arrested four people, two of whom were described as having criminal records.
[@1] In Fayoum, police arrested an unemployed man described as having a criminal
record. [@1]

The first Luxor case involved 2 kg of hashish along with a quantity of shabu, a
homemade gun and a bullet. [@1] The second Luxor case involved 2 kg of hashish
along with a homemade gun and a bullet. [@1] The Aswan case involved 10 kg of
bango, the Egyptian term for herbal cannabis, and 2 kg of hashish. [@1] Those
Aswan suspects also held two automatic rifles, two homemade guns, two magazines
and bullets, plus a pickup truck, a tuk-tuk and a precision scale. [@1] The
Fayoum case involved 1 kg of hashish along with quantities of voodoo and heroin,
an automatic rifle and bullets. [@1]

Police estimated the financial value of all the seized drugs together at about
560,000 Egyptian pounds. [@1] Police said legal measures were taken. [@1] In
Giza in the same sweep, two unemployed men with criminal records were caught
with a shotgun, bullets, a homemade gun, heroin and a scale, with no cannabis
reported. [@1]
