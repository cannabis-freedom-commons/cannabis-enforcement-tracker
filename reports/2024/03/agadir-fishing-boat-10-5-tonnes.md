Moroccan police seized 10 metric tons and 542 kilograms of chira, a potent form
of cannabis resin, from a fishing boat used for smuggling in Agadir on
Sunday. [@1] Thirteen suspects were arrested in the operation. [@1,2]

## A joint sea operation

The seizure was a joint operation by local police, the Royal Navy and the
Royal Gendarmerie, acting on statements from the General Directorate of
National Security (DGSN) and the General Directorate of Territorial
Surveillance (DGST). [@1] Police said investigations were under way into the
arrested suspects' possible links with international drug trafficking
networks. [@1,2]

Morocco is a major cannabis producer and has allowed regulated cultivation,
export and use of cannabis for medical or industrial purposes since 2021,
while recreational use remains banned. [@2]
