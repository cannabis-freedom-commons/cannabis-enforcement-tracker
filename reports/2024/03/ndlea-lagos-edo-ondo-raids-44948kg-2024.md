In a Sunday statement on 24 March 2024, NDLEA spokesman Femi Babafemi said operatives
had seized more than 44,948.1 kilogrammes of illicit drugs, arrested eight suspects
and seized 11 cartel vehicles in massive raids across Lagos, Edo and Ondo states over
the previous week. [@1,2]

In Ondo State, 7,687.8 kilogrammes of cannabis were destroyed in a warehouse in Ala
forest, Akure, with another 670 kilogrammes evacuated on Saturday 23 March. [@1,2]
In Edo State, 14,310.8625 kilogrammes were razed on 5.7 hectares of farms in
Ohosu-Ugbogui forest in Ovia South West on Tuesday 19 March, where five suspects
aged 37 to 67 were arrested. [@1,2] In Uzebba-Avbiosi forest on Thursday 21 March,
over 6,500 kilogrammes were destroyed on 2.6 hectares and three suspects were
arrested. [@1,2] A further 5,000 kilogrammes were razed in a warehouse in Okpuje
forest in Owan West with military support on Friday 22 March. [@2]

In Lagos, a 20 March operation in Ajah seized 10,534 kilogrammes of Ghanaian Loud, a
cannabis strain, along with the 11 vehicles. [@1,2] NDLEA chairman Buba Marwa
commended the Lagos, Edo, Ondo and other commands involved in the week's seizures. [@1]
