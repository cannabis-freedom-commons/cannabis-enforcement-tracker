The Area 2 Narcotics Division seized 919 pounds of compressed ganja in Norwich,
Port Antonio, in Portland parish. [@1] Officers carried out the operation in the
community at about 2:00 am on Friday, 29 March 2024. [@1]

The 919 pounds, about 417 kilograms, were packed in 18 knitted bags hidden in
shrubs along the coast. [@1] Police estimated the street value of the ganja at
JM$9,190,000. [@1]

No one was arrested in connection with the seizure. [@1] A later account,
published on 1 April 2024, repeated the same police figures of 18 bags, 919
pounds and the JM$9,190,000 estimate. [@2] Detectives asked anyone with
information to contact the Narcotics Division on 876-923-5729, the police
emergency number 119 or Crime Stop at 311. [@1]
