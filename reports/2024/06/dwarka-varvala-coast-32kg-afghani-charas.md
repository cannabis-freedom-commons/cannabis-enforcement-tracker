Gujarat Police recovered high-quality Afghani charas worth Rs 16 crore lying
abandoned on the coast of Devbhumi Dwarka district on Friday, 7 June 2024. [@1]
A team of the Sagar Suraksha Dal, a group of volunteer fishermen who keep watch
on the coast, found three plastic bags holding 30 packets while patrolling near
Varala village. [@2] Each packet weighed nearly one kilogram. [@1] Forensic
officials who examined the packets confirmed the contents as high-quality charas
weighing 32.05 kg in all. [@1] Gujarat Police said the charas was produced from
live cannabis grown in Afghanistan. [@3]

The Forensic Science Laboratory reached the spot and confirmed the packets
contained charas. [@2] No one was arrested in the find, and the force said it
was searching the coast for more packets. [@2]

In April, the same Dwarka police had recovered one kilogram of charas worth Rs
52 lakh from the coast in Varvala village. [@1]
