The Court of Appeal in Putrajaya on 26 June 2024 commuted the death sentence of Faizal Zainal Abidin, 46, a former odd-job worker, to 30 years' jail for trafficking 345 kg of cannabis eight years earlier. [@1,2] A three-member bench chaired by Justice Che Ruzima Ghazali, sitting with Justices Azmi Ariffin and Azhahari Kamal Ramli, also ordered 24 strokes of the rotan, the maximum allowed. [@1,2]

Faizal had been found guilty of trafficking 190 kg of cannabis at a house in Subang Jaya and 155 kg at a car park nearby on 12 March 2016, and the Shah Alam High Court sentenced him to death on 29 September 2022. [@1] The drugs had been brought from Sungai Petani, Kedah, by a syndicate; police trailed Faizal's drug-laden car and caught him after watching a rented house he had occupied since January 2015. [@1]

His lawyer conceded the conviction but asked the bench to use its discretion now that the 2023 reform ended the mandatory death penalty, while the deputy public prosecutor urged the court to keep the death sentence given the excess weight. [@1] The jail terms run concurrently from 13 March 2016 with the whipping consecutive, giving 30 years and 24 strokes in total. [@1,2]
