Prefectura Naval Argentina seized almost 600 kilograms of marijuana in Puerto
Rico, Misiones. [@1] The force answers to the national Security Ministry and the
account describes the haul as a multimillion-peso cargo. [@1] The operation grew
out of the force's constant patrols to protect the river border and prevent
crime. [@1] Officers at an observation post spotted two boats loaded with
bundles crossing at kilometre 1728,5 of the Paraná River at Puerto Urfer, and
watched one of them return to its country of origin. [@1] The sighting occurred
at kilometre 1728,5 of the Paraná River, identified in the account as Puerto
Urfer. [@1]

The border guard vessel GC-195 Guaraní and another force launch immediately
headed to the area and found a boat beached on the shore with bundles inside it
and scattered around it. [@1] The subsequent search recovered 23 bundles holding
539 marijuana bricks weighing almost 600 kilos. [@1]

The authorities put the value of that load at more than 1,300 million pesos.
[@1] The Federal Prosecutor's Office in Oberá, Misiones, took over the case.
