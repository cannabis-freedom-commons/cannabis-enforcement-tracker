Moroccan police seized 18.243 tonnes of cannabis resin in the coastal town of
Sidi Rahal south of Casablanca on Monday, arresting five people as they
unloaded the drug from a truck into rapid inflatable boats. [@1,2]

## A watched truck-to-boat transfer

The Casablanca prefectural judicial police and the General Directorate of
Territorial Surveillance had kept the suspects under surveillance as they
moved the shipment overland in a freight truck, planning to load it onto
high-speed boats under cover of darkness for export by sea. [@1,2] Officers
intercepted the group in the act and impounded the cannabis along with fuel,
marine engines, vessels, communications equipment and the land vehicle. [@2]

Prosecutors ordered an investigation into the arrested group's domestic and
foreign trafficking ties so accomplices could be brought to justice. [@1,2]
The seizure followed other large interceptions that year, including 10.5 tonnes
taken from a fishing boat in Agadir in March. [@1]
