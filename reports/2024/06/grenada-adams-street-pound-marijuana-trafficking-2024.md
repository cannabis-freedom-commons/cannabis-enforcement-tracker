Grenada police and Mississippi Bureau of Narcotics agents served a search
warrant at a home in the 1000 block of Adams Street in Grenada on Wednesday 5
June 2024, police chief George Douglas said. [@1] Officers arrested 41-year-old
Eric Conley of Grenada, who was booked in the Grenada County Jail and held with
no bond. [@1]

The search found more than 3,000 dosage units of fentanyl, about 10 grams of
cocaine, about one pound (0.45 kg) of marijuana, a Glock 9 mm handgun and about
$10,000 in cash, Chief Douglas said. [@1] WCBI's same-day account gave the same
findings: 3,000 dosage units of fentanyl, 10 grams of cocaine, a pound of
marijuana, a gun and $10,000 in cash. [@2] The fentanyl and cocaine found in the
same search are outside this tracker's cannabis scope and are not counted in its
figures. [@2]

Conley was charged with aggravated trafficking of a controlled substance, a
single charge covering the drugs found. [@1] GPD Sergeant Wilburn opened the
investigation from the department's investigation division, and police said the
case remained under investigation. [@1] A week later the Grenada Star reported
that Conley remained in jail with no bond and that no further information was
available. [@3]
