By 20 June 2024, the Jinghe County Public Security Bureau said it had cleared
and destroyed more than 50,000 wild cannabis plants so far that year. [@1] The
countywide total was reported alongside a recent clearance near farmland in
Huang Gong Bei Village, Mangding Township. [@1]

During patrols and household visits, the county bureau's anti-drug squad and a
local police room found suspected drug plants beside an irrigation channel.
[@1] Officers identified them as wild cannabis, removed more than 2,000 plants
and checked nearby areas for seedlings. [@1] The squad chief said similar
clearances were carried out with village teams and local police rooms during
the April-to-October growing season. [@1]

County police also reported distributing more than 1,300 drug-prevention
booklets and educating more than 2,800 people that year. [@1] Officers gave
residents information on drug harms and how to identify cannabis plants during
their fieldwork, and the bureau said it would continue the patrols and public
education. [@1] The report gives no total area or weight for the plants cleared.
