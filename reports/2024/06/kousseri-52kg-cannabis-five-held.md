Far North gendarmes arrested five men found with 52 kg of cannabis intended for
Kousseri. [@1] The men held were Sali Oumaté, 32, Djimé Gambo, 39, Ali Mamati,
34, Alhadji Oumaté, 45, and Aboubakar Kourama, 41. [@1] Officers arrested the
suspects at a local small-car travel agency, and they were being held in a cell
of the Far North Gendarmerie Legion. [@1] The suspects were held in the Far
North Gendarmerie Legion, and Legion commander Colonel Joseph Nguele saluted the
cooperation between residents and the national gendarmerie. [@2]

The 52 kg had come from Douala toward Logone-et-Chari, hidden in a Peugeot 504
for sale in Kousseri. [@1] Kousseri is a border town opposite N’Djamena, the
capital of neighbouring Chad. [@1] Interrogating the driver led officers to
further suspects, including the owner of the parcel staying at Mémé, a locality
35 km from Maroua. [@2]

The gendarmerie said the load was meant to supply terrorist groups and was
intercepted through cooperation between gendarmes and local residents. [@2]
Presenting the results to the press, the gendarmerie called the fight against
drug trafficking and terrorism an absolute priority. [@1] Colonel Joseph Nguele
said officers were determined to keep working to eradicate the trafficking and
ensure citizens' safety. [@1]
