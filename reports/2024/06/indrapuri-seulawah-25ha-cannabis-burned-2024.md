Aceh's National Narcotics Board (BNN) officers, with soldiers and police, burned a cannabis crop in the Seulawah mountains at Indrapuri village in Aceh Besar Regency on Saturday 1 June 2024. [@1,2] Officers found 2.5 hectares of crops about three months old and ready for harvest, and destroyed the plants by burning them. [@1,2]

The grower was not caught. [@1,2]
