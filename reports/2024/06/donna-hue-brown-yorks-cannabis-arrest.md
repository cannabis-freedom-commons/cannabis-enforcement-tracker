Police arrested and charged 42-year-old Donna-Hue Brown of Yorks for unlawfully
having 3.5 pounds of cannabis in her possession, possession of cannabis with
intent to transfer, and being concerned with supplying cannabis. [@1]

Brown was taken into custody on Thursday, June 6, after police executed a search
warrant on her property and found the cannabis. [@1] She was expected to appear
in court the next week. [@1]

Antigua News independently carried the same account that Brown was arrested and
charged over 3.5 pounds of cannabis and taken into custody on June 6 after a
search warrant. [@2]
