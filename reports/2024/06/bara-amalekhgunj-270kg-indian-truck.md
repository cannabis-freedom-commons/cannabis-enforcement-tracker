Bara police arrested a 19-year-old Indian driver on 18 June 2024 after finding 270 kg of marijuana hidden in his truck at Amalekhgunj in Jeetpur Simara sub-metropolitan city. [@1]

Officers stopped the Indian truck, registration plate NL 01 N 3063, travelling from Hetauda towards Pathlaiya for a routine security check at 12.30 p.m. [@1] A search uncovered the marijuana concealed inside, and the driver, Antyas Kumar Giri of Dhanhar Dihuli, Ramgarhwa in Bihar, was arrested. [@1]

The truck was impounded for further investigation, according to Deputy Superintendent of Police Dadhiram Nyaupane of Bara. [@1]
