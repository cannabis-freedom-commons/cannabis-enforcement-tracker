Agents of the national police Drug Enforcement Group together with other police units raided a marijuana plantation in Barangay Ngibat, Tinglayan, Kalinga, on Sunday 30 June 2024. [@1,2] The plot measured 5,250 square metres, and the team worked it from 10 a.m. to 6:30 p.m., about eight hours. [@1,2]

## Planned raid, no arrests

They uprooted and destroyed up to 54,500 marijuana plants, which the police valued at P10.9 million. [@1,2] PDEG director Brig. Gen. Eleazar Matta said the operation had been meticulously planned and executed. [@2] No one was arrested during the raid. [@1]
