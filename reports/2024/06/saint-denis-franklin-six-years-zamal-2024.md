Jean-Hubert Celerine, alias Franklin, and Jeremy Decide, alias Nono, were at the
centre of a cannabis trafficking case between Mauritius and Reunion. [@1] Both
men had first been convicted in absentia in July 2021 by the correctional court
of Saint-Denis in Reunion for their role in that network. [@1] Franklin had then
received seven years in prison, while Nono had also received a multi-year prison
term. [@1] After extradition to Reunion in 2024, Franklin was retried and
sentenced to six years in prison, a 50,000 euro fine and a definitive
territorial ban. [@1]

Franklin was extradited from Mauritius on 2 April after detention for money
laundering and opposed the in-absentia judgment on arrival in Reunion. [@2] His
retrial due on 2 May was postponed to 6 June because his lawyer was absent. [@2]
On 6 June he was again sentenced to six years after conviction for complicity in
detention, acquisition, smuggling transport and export of drugs. [@2] The court
also imposed a 50,000 euro fine and a territorial ban. [@2]

He was accused of moving large quantities of zamal by boat at night in 2017 and
2018 from the port of Sainte-Rose in Reunion to the west coast of Mauritius with
accomplice Jeremy-Desire Decide, alias Nono. [@2] Investigators said that after
an arrest at Gillot airport in February 2018, about one hundred kilos of zamal
ready for delivery were seized from a farmer. [@2] Nono, also extradited from
Mauritius, had been sentenced on 5 October to four years after contesting his
in-absentia conviction. [@2]
