Just after 4am on Tuesday 11 June 2024, officers were called to reports of four
males trying to force entry to the derelict Grand Bingo & Social Club on
Camphill Road in Nuneaton. [@1] The lock on the front door of the building had
reportedly been snapped. [@1] Two males who allegedly tried to escape on foot
were arrested. [@1] The two arrested men, aged 19 and 24 and both from Coventry,
were held on suspicion of burglary (non-dwelling) and going equipped. [@1]
Police said the pair were not believed to be directly associated with the grow,
and had allegedly tried to gain access to disrupt or steal from the operation.
[@1]

A closer inspection of the property revealed a cannabis grow in progress inside
the address, with around 112 plants in total across two rooms. [@1] Officers
also reportedly found a cutlass-style sword, a large hatchet, a tomahawk,
several meat cleavers, a 10-inch “zombie knife”, a six-inch kitchen knife and
three crowbars inside the property. [@1]

PC 2103 Smith said the discovery of a full house at the cannabis grow meant the
action had hit the gangs operating such grows in their pockets. [@1]
