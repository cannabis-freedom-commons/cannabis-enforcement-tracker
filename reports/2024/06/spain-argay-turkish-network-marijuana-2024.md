## The raids

More than 400 French, Spanish and Turkish officers searched 28 locations in Granada, Málaga and Seville on 11 and 12 June 2024, breaking up a Turkish-led network accused of moving large quantities of marijuana and heroin from Spain to Germany and Eastern Europe. [@1,2,3] The searches seized more than two tonnes of marijuana alongside cash, firearms and ten luxury cars. [@1,2,3] Europol coordinated the operation, which followed about nine months of joint work by Spanish, French and Turkish narcotics police. [@1,2]

## The arrests

Officers captured the suspected leader, Kerim Dağlıoğlu, known as Emre Karaca, a man of Turkish origin living in Spain and wanted on a Turkish international arrest warrant. [@1,2] AFP reported 36 suspects arrested across ten nationalities, while Türkiye's interior minister put the total at 42 including the leader. [@1,2,3]

Officers also dismantled six indoor marijuana plantations. [@3] The 28 searches comprised 20 in Granada, 6 in Málaga and 2 in Sevilla. [@3] Officers seized a dozen luxury vehicles and more than EUR 100,000 in cash. [@3] Officers seized three firearms and many cartridges. [@3]
