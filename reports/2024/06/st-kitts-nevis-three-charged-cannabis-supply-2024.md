Police in St Kitts and Nevis charged three men with possession of cannabis with
intent to supply. [@2] The accused are Davin Phipps of Lower Westbourne Ghaut in
St Kitts, Xavier Dore of Pot Works in Nevis and Antonio Pascal of Cotton Ground
in Nevis. [@1] Police said the three men were charged after being found with
cannabis. [@3]

Phipps’s alleged offence was committed on 27 June 2024 on St Kitts, while Dore’s
and Pascal’s alleged offences were committed on 26 June 2024 in Pot Works,
Nevis. [@2] Phipps was charged at the Basseterre Police Station on 28 June 2024,
and Dore and Pascal were each charged at the Newcastle Police Station on Nevis.
[@1] The police force published the same charging details in a press release
dated 1 July 2024. [@4]

Possession and cultivation with intent to sell outside what is licensed remains
illegal under legislation passed in 2023, while the government builds out the
cannabis industry. [@1] Certain cannabis cultivation, possession and use for
religious and sacramental purposes is legalised under the current regime. [@1]
Rastafari groups, recognised as a protected religious group, may cultivate and
use cannabis within their registered places of assembly. [@1] Licensed
individuals may possess limited amounts of cannabis and cultivate up to five
plants within secure private premises. [@1] Cannabis sales are restricted to
licensed entities approved by the Medicinal Cannabis Authority. [@1]
