The Civil Guard dismantled an international marijuana trafficking organisation
under Operation Lumbre, arresting 28 people and investigating two more for
membership of a criminal organisation, drug trafficking and electricity
fraud. [@1] Officers carried out 16 searches in Almería, Málaga, Toledo and
Zaragoza, seizing large amounts of cash and several imitation firearms. [@1]

The inquiry began in mid-2023 after earlier drug operations pointed to members
with prior convictions in other European countries. [@1] The network bought
marijuana from growers in Almería, then used a Toledo contact to source cheaper
supplies there, vacuum-packing the buds at a farm south of Madrid and moving
them abroad in trucks fitted with double bottoms. [@1]

In a first phase, officers stopped trucks already en route to other European
countries and searched seven premises across Almería, Málaga and Toledo plus a
lorry travelling through Zaragoza, finding more than 100 kg of vacuum-packed
buds soaked in bleach hidden in a double bottom and arresting 11 people. [@1] A
second phase brought 17 more arrests, with the two investigated people accused
of running the organisation's own plantations, buying third-party crops and
arranging transport through front men, while nine searches in Almería and
Toledo uncovered nine indoor grows with nearly 1,500 plants. [@1]
