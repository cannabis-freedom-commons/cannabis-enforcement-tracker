Assistant Commissioner of Police Operations Livai Driu said a team of officers from the Savusavu Police Station raided the Vusasivo area and seized more than 4,000 plants believed to be marijuana. [@1] The report names no arrests, charges or court outcomes for the seizure. [@1]

The same 7 June 2024 police roundup reports separate arrests in Nausori, Raiwai and Nadi, including a vehicle search in Nadi in which white powder believed to be cocaine was found; those are different events left out of this report. [@1]
