Two Zimbabwean men, aged 24 and 26, were arrested at Walvis Bay on Friday, 7
June 2024, while allegedly selling cannabis to an undercover police officer.
[@1] Erongo police spokesperson chief inspector Ileni Shapumba said the arrest
followed an intelligence-driven operation that also recovered illicit
cigarettes. [@1] A Namibian man, 25, was arrested for alleged cannabis
possession at Kuisebmond in Walvis Bay the next day. [@1]

Police seized four parcels and two big bankies of majad cannabis weighing 655
grams, plus 4,108 packets of illicit cigarettes, from the Walvis Bay pair. [@1]
Shapumba valued that discovery at N$88,710, a figure police gave for the
cannabis and cigarettes together. [@1] Police said the two arrested men had no
legal documents with them. [@1]

The Kuisebmond arrest was made by police officers and police reservists working
together. [@1] Their haul was one medium and 96 small bankies of skunk cannabis
weighing 187 grams. [@1] Police estimated that skunk at N$9,350. [@1]
