Federal police detained an Argentine man and a Paraguayan woman, both 38, after
stopping a car in Posadas, Misiones, and finding cannabis sativa inside it. [@1]
The officers were from the División Unidad Operativa Federal Posadas, the
federal police's local operational unit, and were carrying out routine controls
against federal crime. [@1] Officers signalled a hire car carrying a passenger
to stop at the intersection of Hungría and Juan Ambrosetti streets. [@1]
Crónica's headline called the two an enamoured couple travelling with more than
8 kilos of marijuana, and its subtitle described the man driving his own car
with the woman pretending to be his passenger. [@3]

Officers said the woman looked around nervously and that a strong smell of
marijuana came from inside the vehicle. [@1] In a search carried out in front of
witnesses, officers found 7.9 kilos of cannabis sativa in 41 packages of
different sizes. [@1] MisionesOnline, reporting on 21 June, counted 31 packages
of marijuana hidden in parcel boxes and said the two people aboard were
detained. [@2] MisionesOnline said the packages were found after officers
followed the vehicle, and that the authorities were still investigating where
the drug came from and possible links to regional trafficking networks. [@2]
Police seized the vehicle, 30,000 pesos and two mobile phones, and staff of the
Posadas scientific unit took part in the procedure. [@1]

The national security ministry said the operation was carried out on its
instructions as part of the fight against drug trafficking. [@1] The two
detainees and the seized items were left at the disposal of the federal criminal
and correctional court of first instance in Posadas for an offence under the
drug law. [@1]
