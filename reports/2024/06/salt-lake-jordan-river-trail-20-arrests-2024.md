More than 20 people were arrested in a two-day illicit drug interdiction
operation along the Jordan River Trail that SLCPD held on Saturday, June 15, and
Wednesday, June 19, 2024, working with the Utah Department of Corrections Adult
Probation and Parole unit. [@1]

The agencies arrested more than 20 people and seized marijuana, methamphetamine,
over 500 fentanyl pills, more than $2,000 in cash, and two illegally possessed
guns. [@2] Police said the overall objective of the operation was to identify,
locate, and arrest drug dealers, traffickers, and buyers along the Jordan River
Trail. [@1]

SLCPD's increased enforcement along the Jordan River Trail and nearby
communities began in October 2023 due to increased criminal activity, and
further operations along the trail are expected to continue. [@1]
