Argentina's National Gendarmerie detained three members of the Marcos Estrada
Gonzalez narco clan, two in Peru with Peruvian police and one in Buenos Aires
Province. [@1] Peruvian police located and detained Jorge Enrique Andia Arnao
(62), alias Monillo, and Ronald Jilmer Huertas (35), alias Jota, between 8 and 9
June, both wanted under an INTERPOL red notice. [@1] Gendarmerie officers
geolocated and detained the third member, Carlos Martin Alvarado Andio (36),
alias Caballito, near the Laferrere train station in La Matanza and moved him to
the unit's premises. [@1]

The three adult Peruvian citizens distributed, accounted for and supervised
cocaine and marijuana and guarded the money collected at the gang's kioscos.
[@1] Gendarmeria's Cinturon Sur complex-crimes unit had been investigating the
organization operating in Barrio Padre Ricciardelli (ex 1-11-14) since August
2021 under orders from Federal Court No. 12 with PROCUNAR's involvement. [@1]

Officers seized documentation in the operations that is considered important for
continuing the investigations. [@1] Cronica confirms that two of the wanted men
were detained by Peru's National Police in that country while the third was
captured in Laferrere, La Matanza. [@3] Argentina's justice system is arranging
the extradition of the two citizens detained in Peru. [@2]
