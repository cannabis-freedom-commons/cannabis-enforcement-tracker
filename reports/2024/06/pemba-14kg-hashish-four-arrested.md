Mozambique's National Criminal Investigation Service (SERNIC) detained four
people in Pemba, Cabo Delgado, in possession of 14 kilograms of haxixe, or
hashish. [@1] The four, all nationals, were caught red-handed. [@2] The
detention took place on 22 June 2024. [@2] The accused deny involvement and say
they were detained unjustly. [@2] One said he had only accepted a lift from the
car's owner to be dropped at his garage. [@2] Another, who described himself as
a mechanic, said he had lent the car, gave investigators the owner's number and
had never opened the car. [@2]

Officers intercepted the accused while they were travelling in two vehicles
carrying the drug. [@1] Both vehicles have been seized. [@1] SERNIC spokesperson
Sumail Sabila announced the detention at a Wednesday press conference in Pemba.
[@3] He said a case file had already been drawn up and sent to the Attorney
General's Office for further action. [@3]

SERNIC said it had been investigating the detainees for a month, but they had
not revealed the origin or destination of the drugs. [@2] Sabila said this was
the second seizure SERNIC had made that year. [@3] The owner of the drugs
remains unidentified. [@2]
