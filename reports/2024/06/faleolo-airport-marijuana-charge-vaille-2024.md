Customs officials at Faleolo International Airport detained a 44-year-old man
from Vailele on Saturday after finding marijuana in his possession when he
arrived from American Samoa. [@1] The discovery was made during a routine
inspection of incoming passengers, and police sent a team from their Faleolo
outpost after customs alerted them. [@1] The man arrived on the Talofa Airways 3
pm flight from American Samoa, and the Police Commissioner, Auapa'au Logoitino
Filipo, confirmed the flight. [@1] During questioning the man was found to be
carrying US$8,000 in cash as well as the marijuana, and he said his trip to
American Samoa was to sell "koko Samoa", a traditional Samoan cocoa beverage.
[@1]

The 44-year-old was formally charged with possession of marijuana. [@1] The
reported amount of cash raised additional suspicions about his activities. [@1]
He was released on bail on condition that he sign at the police station every
Friday, surrendered all his travel documents and must appear in court on 24
June. [@1]
