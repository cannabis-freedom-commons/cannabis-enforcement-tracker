Pradhannagar police recovered a large quantity of ganja from a four-wheeled
vehicle in the Gurungbasti area under Pradhannagar police station on Sunday
night. [@1] Police arrested one trafficker in connection with the ganja
smuggling. [@1] Police identified the arrested man as Ranjan De. [@1] Police
said De is a resident of Balurghat. [@1] He was produced before the Siliguri
subdivisional court on Monday. [@1]

Pradhannagar police carried out the operation in Gurungbasti in Siliguri after
receiving information from a secret source. [@1] Officers stopped a vehicle on
the basis of specific information and searched it. [@1] The search recovered 35
kg of ganja, the local term for cannabis. [@1] The ganja had been hidden in a
secret chamber of the vehicle. [@1] Police seized the vehicle used for the
smuggling. [@1] The report put the seized ganja's current market value at more
than one lakh rupees. [@1]

Police said the seized ganja had been brought from Cooch Behar. [@1] Police said
there was a plan to smuggle it to Balurghat. [@1] Police filed a case against De
under the NDPS Act, India's narcotics law. [@1] Police said they were examining
who else was involved in the trafficking. [@1] The Siliguri Police
Commissionerate said it was continuing its drive against drugs. [@1]
