Officers with the Josephine Marijuana Enforcement Team seized more than 2,600
marijuana plants during a raid on a property in Wolf Creek, Oregon, on Tuesday
25 June 2024. [@1] The search warrant was executed in the 300 block of Sunny
Glen Way, with Josephine County Public Health & Building Safety assisting the
team. [@1]

The Josephine County Sheriff's Office said inspectors cited electrical, water
and solid waste violations, which could result in forfeiture of the property.
[@1] The primary suspect in the illegal grow was not on the property, and police
said the person would face charges including possessing and manufacturing
marijuana and unlawful appropriation of water if located. [@1] No arrests were
reported after the search. [@1]
