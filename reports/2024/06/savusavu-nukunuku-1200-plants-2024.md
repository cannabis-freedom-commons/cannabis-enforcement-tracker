Assistant Commissioner of Police Operations Livai Driu said a Savusavu Police Station team operated on 5 June 2024 at a farm in the Nukunuku area of Natewa, Savusavu, uprooting and seizing more than 1,200 plants believed to be marijuana. [@1,2] The owner of the farm was yet to be established as investigations continued. [@1,2]

The same police roundup reported a 20-year-old farmer arrested by Tukavesi Police for alleged unlawful possession of dried leaves believed to be marijuana, a separate case from the Nukunuku farm seizure. [@1,2]
