Drug enforcement officers arrested an Iraqi expatriate in the Raqqa area on
charges of drug trafficking involving hashish. [@1] Two drug enforcement
officers were seriously injured while carrying out that arrest in the Raqqa
area. [@1] During the investigation, a second Iraqi man living in an apartment
in the Jabriya area confessed to involvement in the trafficking. [@1]

Police obtained permission from the public prosecution and raided the Jabriya
apartment. [@1] Officers found the second suspect in Jabriya with a quarter of a
kilo of hashish. [@1] The second suspect resisted the officers during the raid
and broke one of the apartment windows. [@1] Two detectives sustained injuries
as a result of the suspect's resistance. [@1]

Officers eventually brought the resisting suspect under control and detained
him. [@1] He was then referred to the public prosecution for further legal
proceedings, with no court outcome reported in this account. [@1]
