A businessman escaped the gallows on 20 June 2024 after Malaysia's Federal Court commuted
his death sentence to 30 years in prison for trafficking 9.528 kg of cannabis. [@1] A
three-judge panel headed by Justice Zabariah Mohd Yusof upheld Mohammad Firdaus Mohsin's
trafficking conviction and dismissed his appeal, but set aside the death sentence and
ordered 12 strokes of the rotan. [@1]

Firdaus, 40, had been convicted by the Shah Alam High Court on 3 December 2021 of
trafficking the drug at about 10pm on 28 March 2017 along Jalan Bukit in front of the
Kajang KTMB train station in Hulu Langat, Selangor. [@1,2] A witness saw him carrying
bags and placing them beside himself in the back seat of a car. [@1]

The Court of Appeal had upheld the conviction and death sentence on 2 August 2023, with
Justice Hadhariah Syed Ismail ruling there was no merit in the appeal and the conviction
was safe. [@2] Deputy public prosecutor Zulkpli Abdullah appeared for the prosecution,
while Firdaus was represented by Muhammad Rafique Rashid Ali and Nur Mustahir Md Nor. [@1]
