Spain's Tax Agency Customs Surveillance unit and the National Police raided an indoor cannabis plantation in the Palmete neighbourhood of Seville on 4 April 2024, dismantling a criminal organisation based there. [@1] Officers acting on a court search warrant seized 205 cannabis plants in an advanced stage of flowering, several bags of buds ready for sale and more than 6,000 euros in cash, along with a crossbow, a harpoon gun and several bladed weapons. [@1] The growers had wired the property with unauthorised electrical connections, creating a fire risk as well as defrauding the power supplier. [@1] Five people were arrested on suspicion of drug trafficking and electricity fraud and brought before the judicial authorities once police procedures were complete. [@1]
