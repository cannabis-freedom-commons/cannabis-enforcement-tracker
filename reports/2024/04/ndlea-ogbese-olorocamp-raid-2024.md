In a Sunday statement on 21 April 2024, NDLEA spokesman Femi Babafemi said
operatives had raided the Oloro camp at Ogbese in Akure North Local Government
Area of Ondo State. [@1,2]

Officers arrested 50-year-old Linus Odogwu at the camp and destroyed a total
of 25,000 kilogrammes of cannabis growing on 10 hectares of farmland, while
recovering 89.5 kilogrammes of processed cannabis. [@1,2]
