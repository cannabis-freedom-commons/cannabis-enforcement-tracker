Seet Poh Jing, a former property sales agent with Huttons Asia, was sentenced
to the mandatory death penalty for trafficking cannabis in Singapore. [@1,2]
In reasons issued on 2 April 2024, Justice See Kee Oon of the Appellate
Division convicted him on one charge of trafficking no less than 4,509.2
grams of cannabis mixture. [@1,2]

Central Narcotics Bureau officers arrested Seet, then 28, on 28 June 2018
after he led them to a car holding five blocks of vegetable matter that
proved to be cannabis mixture he called weed. [@1,2] Forensic examination of
two of his mobile phones found mass-sent messages advertising the drug. [@1,2]

Seet claimed the cannabis was for research and development toward a
cannabidiol business, and relied on a psychiatric report of a drug-induced
hypomanic episode behind grandiose plans for a cannabis venture. [@1,2] The
judge rejected both, calling the research claim a concocted afterthought at
odds with Seet's post-arrest statements, which spoke of helping friends and
set out a weed pricing strategy without mentioning research. [@1,2]
