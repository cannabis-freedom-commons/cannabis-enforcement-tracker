Detectives acting on a tip-off intercepted a suspicious Nissan vehicle in Subukia while it was headed toward Nakuru. [@1,2] A search uncovered nine bales of marijuana hidden beneath sacks of hay. [@1,2]

Faced with arrest, the 32-year-old driver Evans Oluoch Rogo led officers to two accomplices at a hotel in Nakuru city. [@2] Officers arrested Ali Abdikadir Ali, 30, and Ortofa Dida, 28, and searched their saloon car, recovering 36 more bales of the herb. [@1,2]

In total, police seized 45 bales weighing 138 kg, valued at about Sh3 million, and referred the three suspects to the Rift Valley Regional Headquarters. [@1,2]
