Revenue officers stopped a van that had arrived at Dublin Port from the United
Kingdom on the afternoon of Wednesday 3 April 2024, acting on risk profiling
and with the assistance of detector dog James. [@1] Concealed in
vacuum-packed packages among a consignment of bottled water and garden
furniture, they found 55.3 kilograms of herbal cannabis with an estimated
value of €1,106,000, pending analysis. [@1,2]

Gardaí from the Dublin Metropolitan Region North Central divisional drugs unit
arrested a man in his 50s at the scene. [@1,2] He was detained under Section 2
of the Criminal Justice (Drug Trafficking) Act 1996 at a Dublin Garda station,
where he could be held for up to seven days, while investigations continued.
[@1,2]
