On 15 March 2023 at about 5 pm, plain-clothes officers raided the 'api (allotment)
of Sione Songo'imoli at Popua, where several people had gathered in and around
two motor vehicles, and arrested a number of them over suspected illicit drugs.
[@1,2]

Heneli Manuofetoa was found beside a green Toyota sedan with a packet of
cannabis weighing 0.41 g on the ground behind the car, and the judge accepted
Officer Fifita's evidence that Manuofetoa had thrown it there. [@1,2] 'Aisea
Havea and Kaniteli Guttenbeil sat in a white Mazda holding an open black
shoulder bag between them with eight packets of cannabis weighing 3.33 g, a
smoking pipe and a lighter. [@1,2]

After a trial on 5 and 6 March 2024, Justice Cooper convicted all three on 14
March 2024. [@1,2] Manuofetoa received five and a half months' imprisonment,
concurrent with the 32-month term he was already serving, and the cannabis was
ordered forfeited and destroyed. [@1,2]

Havea and Guttenbeil, who had asked for pre-sentence reports, were sentenced on
5 April 2024. [@2] Havea's two-month suspended sentence was activated and he
must then serve six months for the cannabis and utensil counts, served
concurrently. [@2] Guttenbeil's ten-month suspended sentence was activated and
he must then serve nine months for the cannabis count, with six months for the
utensils concurrent, after breaching conditions that included a drug-awareness
course. [@2]
