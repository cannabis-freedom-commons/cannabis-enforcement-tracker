Guinea-Bissau prosecutor Eduardo Mancanha was detained at Lisbon Airport on
Sunday 21 April 2024 while preparing to board a flight to Bissau. [@1]
Guinea-Bissau's Public Prosecution Service said he had about 200 grams of
hashish, known in Portuguese as haxixe, in his luggage. [@1] The council
statement said he had spent more than three years in Portugal on service leave
to study when officers caught him in flagrante delicto with 200 grams of a
product presumed to be drugs. [@1] Portuguese police apprehended him at Lisbon
airport. [@2] He was in Portugal on leave to prepare a doctorate in law. [@2]

The Superior Council of the Public Prosecution Service preventively suspended
him, citing the gravity of the conduct under the prosecution magistrates'
statutes. [@1] The council also opened disciplinary proceedings and named deputy
prosecutor-general Quintino Inbequi to conduct the inquiry. [@1]

Guinea-Bissau's prosecution office said it had been informed through appropriate
channels on Sunday the 21st of a magistrate's detention for alleged drug
trafficking. [@1] The prosecution leadership then convened the deputy
prosecutors-general and informed them of the magistrate's imprisonment in
Lisbon. [@1]
