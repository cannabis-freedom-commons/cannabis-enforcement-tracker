The Oklahoma Medical Marijuana Authority completed a three-day enforcement operation in
Muskogee County on 25 April 2024 with the Attorney General's Organized Crime Task Force, the
US Department of Homeland Security, the county sheriff and police and the Muscogee (Creek)
Nation Lighthorse. [@1,2]

Compliance inspectors carried out 15 regulatory inspections while investigators pursued
suspected administrative and criminal violations at licensed marijuana businesses. [@1] The
operation produced five emergency licence suspensions and four search warrants executed by law
enforcement partners. [@1]

The warrants yielded about 32,000 cannabis plants, 2,000 pounds of harvested marijuana, more
than $207,000 in cash and six firearms. [@1] The Attorney General's announcement of the same
busts described the processed seizure as 5,000 pounds of untraceable marijuana. [@2]
