On Tuesday 23 April 2024 Revenue customs officers seized approximately 120
kilograms of herbal cannabis with an estimated value of €2,400,000 in the
Meath area, in an intelligence-led joint operation with the Garda National
Drugs and Organised Crime Bureau (GNDOCB). [@1] Two men in their 50s were
arrested by gardaí and detained at a County Meath station under Section 2 of
the Criminal Justice (Drug Trafficking) Act 1996. [@1]

In follow-up searches in County Laois on Wednesday 24 April, the GNDOCB
seized cash in excess of €200,000, €140,000 of suspected cannabis, €10,000 of
suspected MDMA tablets, a substantial amount of suspected counterfeit
prescription tablets and a sophisticated pill-making facility including a pill
press. [@2] A man in his 50s and a woman in her 40s were arrested and detained
at a County Laois station under the same drug-trafficking powers, while the
two men arrested in Meath remained in custody. [@2] All seized items were to
be sent to Forensic Science Ireland for examination. [@2]
