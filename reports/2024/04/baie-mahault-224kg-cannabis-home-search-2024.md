Gendarmes arrested a man in his sixties at his home in Baie-Mahault, Guadeloupe,
on Tuesday 9 April 2024. [@1] The arrest came a day after officers on a street
check in the town stopped a young woman who had just left his home. [@1] The
authorities described him as a man in his sixties known locally for offences
under drug legislation. [@1]

A search of his home found 2.24 kilograms of packaged cannabis, three precision
scales and 1,010 euros in small notes. [@1] The man was placed in garde à vue,
French police custody, where he admitted the facts. [@1] He said the cannabis
served both his own consumption and resale, and that he had been selling
narcotics since 2007. [@1] He was summoned to appear in court the following
June. [@1]

The check that triggered the case found 8.5 grams of cannabis on the woman,
which officers seized. [@1] She said spontaneously that she had just bought the
drugs while her mother waited in the street. [@1] Both women said after being
taken to the gendarmerie brigade that they bought drugs at that address from
time to time. [@1]
