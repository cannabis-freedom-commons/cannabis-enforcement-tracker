Gary Bowie, 31, was sentenced at Douglas Courthouse for trying to have a parcel
containing £9,000 of cannabis, addressed to someone else, delivered to his own
home. [@1]

Customs and excise officials intercepted the parcel, sent to an address in
Ramsey from the United States, when it arrived on the island on 4 May and found
it contained cannabis. [@1] Bowie, claiming to be the addressee, went to the
post office and asked for the parcel to be delivered to his own address on
Brookhill Road. [@1] He was arrested on 15 May after accepting the parcel when
it was delivered to his home. [@1]

Told officers he had been offered £300 to obtain the package, but said he knew
what was in it and had intended to keep it for himself. [@1] He pleaded guilty
to attempting to possess cannabis with the intent to supply. [@1] Deemster
Graeme Cook handed him a 14-month prison sentence, suspended for 18 months,
saying it was "good news" that the cannabis had been "taken from the street".
[@1] Cook said that suspending the sentence was "appropriate in relation to what
custody could do to you". [@1]
