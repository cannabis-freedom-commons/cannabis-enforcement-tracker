Spain's Civil Guard seized 2,345 kilograms of hashish from a rubber dinghy off
the coast of Gran Canaria after a tip from Morocco's territorial surveillance
agency, the DGST. [@1,2] Officers on the patrol boat Rio Tambre approached
the seven-metre dinghy, which was sailing without lights, and found 66 bales
of hashish aboard. [@2]

The two men aboard, aged 36 and 26, were arrested for a crime against public
health and placed in provisional detention. [@1,2] The dinghy had sailed from
Morocco to land on the island's coast. [@2] Officers also seized the boat,
two outboard motors, a satellite phone and GPS and mobile phones; the Las
Palmas organised-crime unit kept the investigation open, so further arrests
were not ruled out. [@1,2]
