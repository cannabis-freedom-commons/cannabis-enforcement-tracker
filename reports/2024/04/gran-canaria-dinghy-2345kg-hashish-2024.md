Spain’s Guardia Civil arrested the two crew members, aged 26 and 36, of an inflatable boat off Gran Canaria and seized 2,345 kilograms of hashish. [@1,2] Morocco’s territorial surveillance agency, the DGST, had tipped off the Guardia Civil that a boat from Morocco intended to land its cargo on the island’s coast. [@1,2]

The Las Palmas judicial police and maritime service deployed on land and at sea. [@1,2] Officers aboard the patrol boat Río Tambre approached the seven-metre dinghy after spotting it sailing without lights near the coast. [@1,2] They found 66 bales of hashish and seized the dinghy, two outboard motors, a satellite phone, two GPS units and several mobile phones. [@1,2]

The two crew members were arrested on suspicion of an offence against public health. [@1,2] Las Palmas Investigating Court No. 1 ordered both held in pretrial detention. [@1,2] The Guardia Civil said the investigation remained open and further arrests were possible. [@1,2]
