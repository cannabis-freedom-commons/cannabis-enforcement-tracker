A day after the Wiruni operation, police carried out another eradication exercise at Kaiwa
in the Upper Berbice River area between 05:30 and 12:30 hours on Saturday 13 April 2024,
and destroyed two cannabis farms. [@1,2]

The first farm was about five acres in size with some 1,000 seedlings planted on beds,
alongside a nursery holding about 35,000 seedlings; a large camp there held about 34.019
kilograms of dried cannabis valued at G$30,617,100. [@1,2] A second farm about 100 feet
away covered two acres with about 2,000 plants, estimated at 2,200 kilograms and valued at
G$338,800. [@1,2] The farms, the nursery, the camp and the narcotics were all destroyed.
[@1,2]

No one was arrested, according to the police. [@1]
