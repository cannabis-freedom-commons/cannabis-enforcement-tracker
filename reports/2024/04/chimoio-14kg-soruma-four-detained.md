Police in Chimoio, the capital of Manica province in central Mozambique,
detained four people accused of trafficking, selling and consuming drugs. [@1]
The four detainees are between 25 and 45 years old and include two women. [@1]

Officers found 14 kilograms of cannabis sativa, known locally as soruma, in
their possession. [@1] Mateus Mindu, spokesman for the provincial command of
Mozambique's Republic Police (PRM), said the group tried without success to
bribe the officers with 21,000 meticais, about 330 US dollars. [@1]

Mindu said a separate file on active corruption would be opened against those
involved. [@1] The case file was handed to the Public Prosecutor's Office for
the next steps. [@1] Police said the drug appeared to have been produced outside
Manica province and that they believed the group has more members. [@1] Mindu
said information still being checked indicated the drug was produced outside
Mozambique, and that police wanted to identify and hold all members of the
network responsible. [@1]

One of the women in custody said she received the drug as an order in Chimoio,
that it came from outside Manica, and that she had sold soruma before. [@1] She
denied trying to bribe the police, saying officers found 5,000 meticais at her
home and asked her for more money before presenting 21,000 meticais as an
attempted bribe. [@1] A second detainee said officers dug inside his home and
found some soruma buried there, and that he used it rather than selling it. [@1]
He said he had smoked soruma for three years and never sold it. [@1]
