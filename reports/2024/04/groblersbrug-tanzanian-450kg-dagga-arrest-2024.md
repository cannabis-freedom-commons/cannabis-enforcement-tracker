Members of SA Police Service's border policing unit in Limpopo arrested a
58-year-old Tanzanian man for attempting to smuggle dagga at the Groblersbrug
port of entry linking South Africa and Botswana. [@1] Police had received a
tip-off about a Tanzanian man allegedly trying to transport dagga across the
border, driving a white Toyota Hilux with Gauteng registration numbers. [@1]

The vehicle was stopped and searched at the checking point, where 19 bags of
dagga weighing around 450 kilograms were found, with an estimated street value
of R1,890,000. [@1] The Toyota Hilux, worth over R400,000, was also confiscated
as part of the ongoing investigation. [@1] The Tanzanian man was detained at
Witpoort police station in Limpopo, and the 19 bags of dagga were confiscated.
[@1]

He was set to appear before the Phalala Magistrate's Court in Limpopo, facing
charges of dealing in dagga. [@1]
