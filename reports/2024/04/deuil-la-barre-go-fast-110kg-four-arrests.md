Four men were arrested on the evening of Friday 5 April 2024 in Deuil-la-Barre,
Val-d'Oise, by officers of the Versailles recherche and intervention brigade
(BRI) and the financial BRI of Nanterre. [@1] A second outlet, actu.fr, reports
the same interception at about 22:00 on Friday 5 April 2024 in the Galathée
district. [@2]

The four suspects, who had left Spain that morning, were stopped around 22:00 in
the Galathée district as they were about to unload the drugs. [@1] The cannabis,
close to 110 kg of herb, was carried in a Peugeot 5008, and two other vehicles
travelled in the convoy from Spain. [@1] Actu.fr states that 110 kg of cannabis
herb was seized during the arrests. [@2] Actu17 put the load's resale value for
dealers at close to one million euros. [@1]

Investigators of the Versailles anti-narcotics office (OFAST) had followed the
alleged organiser, a 43-year-old repeat offender, for several months. [@1] He is
suspected of organising this go-fast; police identified the three vehicles used
and the three accomplices. [@1]

A source close to the investigation said the men were arrested calmly, as the
5008 was full of cannabis herb. [@1] The four were placed in garde à vue, French
police custody pending investigation, at the OFAST offices in Versailles, where
the measure can last up to 96 hours. [@1] They were due to be brought before an
investigating judge in Pontoise for a possible formal investigation. [@1]
Actu.fr likewise reported the planned hearing before a Pontoise investigating
judge. [@2]
