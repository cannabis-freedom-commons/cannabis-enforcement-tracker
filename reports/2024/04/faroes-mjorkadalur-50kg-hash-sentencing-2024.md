The Court of the Faroe Islands sentenced two men to two years and three months
and two years and seven months in prison for smuggling about 50 kilograms of
hash to the islands. [@1] Police called it the single largest cannabis case the
court had ever dealt with, valuing the haul at 12.5 to 15 million DKK. [@1] A
third man, a Lithuanian citizen, was acquitted of the smuggling counts but
sentenced to four months for assault and related charges, deported and banned
from the islands for three years. [@1]

Police arrested and detained all three in August 2023 after seizing nearly 30
kg of cannabis at the Mjorkadalur detention centre. [@1] Investigators found
that hash was part of a 50 kg consignment smuggled into the Faroes in May 2023.
[@1] The court heard the two Faroese men had helped bring the drugs in and were
to sell them, while the Lithuanian had come to collect money and had held
captive, threatened and assaulted people while looking for cannabis believed
stolen. [@1] One of the jailed men received the shorter term after pleading
guilty and cooperating. [@1] The court also ordered confiscation of BMW cars
and smartphones bought with drug money along with 200,000 DKK in cash. [@1]
