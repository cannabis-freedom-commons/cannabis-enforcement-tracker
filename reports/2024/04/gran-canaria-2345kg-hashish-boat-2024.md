Spain's Guardia Civil seized 2,345 kilograms of hashish after intercepting an
inflatable boat off the coast of Gran Canaria on 29 April 2024. [@1,2] Morocco's
DGST territorial-surveillance service had tipped off the Guardia Civil that a boat
coming from Morocco intended to land on the island's coast. [@1,2]

Officers from the Las Palmas Judicial Police organic unit and the Provincial Maritime
Service deployed by land and sea, and the patrol boat Rio Tambre approached a small
boat sailing without lights near the coast. [@2] Officers found 66 bales of hashish
weighing more than 2,300 kilograms aboard the seven-metre pneumatic boat, and also
seized its two outboard motors, a satellite phone, two GPS devices and several mobile
phones. [@2]

The boat's two crew members, aged 36 and 26, were arrested for a crime against public
health. [@2] The investigation remained open, with further arrests not ruled out. [@2]
