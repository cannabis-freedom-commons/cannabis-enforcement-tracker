Customs Surveillance officers of the Tax Agency and Civil Guard officers
dismantled seven cannabis production facilities in the Granada towns of
Cijuela and Chimeneas on 1 April 2024, as part of Operation Chume. [@1] In
all seven properties they found the equipment for growing and producing
cannabis, along with 2,180 Cannabis sativa plants at various stages of
flowering. [@1]

Investigators, helped by technicians from the electricity supplier, had found
at least 20 illegal double connections to the grid in homes in the two towns;
high consumption readings in seven of them pointed to large-scale growing, and
the searches followed a court order. [@1] Seven people were arrested for drug
cultivation and production and for electricity fraud, while 13 more people
were investigated for electricity fraud. [@1]
