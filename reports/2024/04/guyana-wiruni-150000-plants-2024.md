A party of Guyana Police led by an Assistant Superintendent conducted an eradication exercise
at Wiruni on the Berbice River between 03:00 and 16:05 hours on Friday 12 April 2024. [@1,2]

On the first farm, covering about 20 acres, the ranks found a makeshift camp and a drying
area holding about 1,200 pounds (about 544 kg) of dried cannabis, which police valued at
G$489,879,360. [@1,2] The second farm covered about 10 acres and held about 150,000 plants,
valued at G$50,820,000. [@1,2] Both farms were destroyed by fire. [@1,2]

No arrests were made during the exercise, according to the police. [@1]
