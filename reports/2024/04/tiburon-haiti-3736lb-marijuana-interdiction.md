The Resolute crew and Coast Guard Cutter Mohawk interdicted one go-fast vessel
and apprehended four suspected smugglers. [@1] The interception took place off
Haiti’s southwest coast, where the Coast Guard seized 3,736 pounds of marijuana
and valued the load at more than $3.5 million. [@2] The four men were Miguel
Orr, Rayvon Smith, Sherland Campbell and Romaine Betty, all from Jamaica. [@3]
Trial evidence placed the interception on or about 30 April 2024, approximately
14 nautical miles off Tiburon, Haiti. [@4]

Laboratory testing of samples from the recovered bales returned positive results
for marijuana, prosecutors said. [@5] A federal grand jury indicted all four men
on a conspiracy charge involving possession with intent to distribute a
controlled substance aboard a vessel subject to U.S. jurisdiction. [@3]

On 16 January 2026, a federal jury returned guilty verdicts against Miguel Orr
and Romaine Betty after a multi-day trial. [@4] Orr was found guilty of
conspiracy and possession with intent to distribute aboard a vessel subject to
U.S. jurisdiction, while Betty was found guilty of possession with intent to
distribute. [@4] Sentencing for Orr and Betty was scheduled for 22 May 2026
before Chief Judge Robert A. Molloy. [@5]
