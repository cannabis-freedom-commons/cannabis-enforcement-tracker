At around 09:00 on Sunday, 29-year-old M.B. entered the Turkish Republic of
Northern Cyprus through Ercan Airport, where customs officers examined his
luggage in a routine inspection. [@1] During that entry check at Ercan Airport,
officers discovered substances believed to be cannabis weighing 13 kilograms in
the luggage of M.B. as he entered the TRNC. [@1] The report identifies the
detained person as 29-year-old M.B. [@1] Customs officials conducted the luggage
inspection at Ercan Airport on entry. [@1]

The Police Press Office said customs officials conducting the inspection seized
substances believed to be cannabis totalling approximately 13 kilograms from
M.B.'s luggage. [@1] Police said the approximately 13-kilogram total was made up
of 26 separate parcels of about half a kilogram each. [@1] Officers seized all
26 packages from M.B.'s luggage. [@1] Police described all of the seized
material as substances believed to be cannabis. [@1]

Police confirmed that M.B. was arrested. [@1] Police said the investigation into
the case was still continuing. [@1]
