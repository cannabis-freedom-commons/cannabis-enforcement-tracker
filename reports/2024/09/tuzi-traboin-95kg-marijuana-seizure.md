Employees of the Border Police Sector at Border Police Station Podgorica checked
a vehicle and the person driving it in the Nabom Traboin area of Tuzi after
several days of activities conducted under their jurisdiction in that area. [@1]
The driver was 20-year-old MU from Tuzi. [@1] The prosecutor at the High State
Prosecutor's Office in Podgorica was notified of the incident. [@1] By order of
that prosecutor, MU was deprived of his liberty on suspicion of having committed
the criminal offense of production, possession and placing unauthorized narcotic
drugs on the market. [@1]

In the trunk of the checked vehicle, police found four large PVC packages
containing marijuana. [@1] Those four packages together had a total weight of
about 95 kilograms. [@1] The drugs had been transferred from the territory of
Albania into the territory of Montenegro and had then been taken over by the
suspect MU. [@1] Measures and actions were being taken to identify and prosecute
the person suspected of having carried out that cross-border transfer from
Albania. [@1]
