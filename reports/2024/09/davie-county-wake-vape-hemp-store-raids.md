Davie County Sheriff J.D. Hartman said some CBD products being sold in the
county were actually marijuana. [@1] A 4-year-old child in Davie County had
overdosed a couple of weeks earlier on a packaged CBD product found in the home.
[@1] No arrests were made at 601 Tobacco and Vape off Yadkinville Road in
Mocksville and at Tanglewood Tobacco in Bermuda Run, but officers were working
with the district attorney's office to review the evidence for possible charges.
[@1]

Officers raided two such stores, confiscating products and cash. [@1] Hartman
called the effort Operation Wake and Vape and said he hoped it would alert the
public that some stores were selling marijuana rather than hemp products. [@1]
Some products sold at the stores were advertised as containing more than 100
times the allowed THC limit, and tests proved that true. [@1]

Special agents of the Trademark Enforcement Division of the NC Secretary of
State's Office joined the raids and seized several items of counterfeit
merchandise. [@1] Officers acted on tips and made undercover buys before sending
the products to a private lab for testing of THC content. [@1] Seized items were
packaged in a manner that could easily be mistaken for candy by children. [@1]
