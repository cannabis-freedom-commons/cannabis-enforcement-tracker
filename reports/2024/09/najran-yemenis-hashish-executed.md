Saudi Arabia executed two Yemeni nationals in Najran on 14 September 2024. [@1,2] The interior
ministry said Saqr Salem Abd Rabeh Al-Ayashi and Mohammed Ali Ahmed Sarhan smuggled and received
hashish, were arrested, convicted, and put to death after appeal and Supreme Court approval. [@1,2]

## Discretionary sentences

Both sources republish the ministry statement describing the penalty as taazir, a discretionary
death sentence under Saudi law for the proved drug offences. [@1,2] The announcements warn that
anyone who smuggles or deals drugs faces the same legal consequences. [@1]
