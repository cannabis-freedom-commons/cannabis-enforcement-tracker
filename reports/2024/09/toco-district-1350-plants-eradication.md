Officers of the Special Investigations Unit (SIU) held a cannabis eradication exercise in the
Toco district on Thursday 26 September 2024, according to the Trinidad and Tobago Police
Service (TTPS). [@1] Officers of the Coastal and Air Support Unit supervised by Insp. Ronnie
Rampallard, the SIU Training Team and other specialist sections supported the exercise. [@1]

The team found a camp on 0.051 hectares of land holding 1,350 fully grown cannabis trees,
22.7 kilos of dried cannabis and a drying rack, and destroyed them. [@1] Police put the total
street value of the destroyed drugs at TT$1,577,000 and said investigations were
continuing. [@1]

The account names no arrests.
