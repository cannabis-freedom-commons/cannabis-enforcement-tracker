Police in Dysselsdorp, outside Oudtshoorn in the Western Cape, arrested two
people, aged 33 and 57, during a search of a house on Belelie Street on Friday
13 September 2024. [@1] Officers at the house seized 12 mandrax tablets, 31
bankies of dagga and five beer crates of 750 ml bottles. [@1] A bankie is a
small plastic packet in which dagga is commonly sold in South Africa, and the
reports give no weight for the dagga. [@3]

The two were charged with dealing in drugs and with dealing in liquor without a
licence. [@1] The search was part of a wider clamp-down on unregistered liquor
outlets in the Garden Route District on that day. [@1] Police said those
arrested would appear in the Oudtshoorn Magistrate's Court once charged. [@1]
Sergeant Christopher Spies, a police spokesperson, gave the account of the
Dysselsdorp search and arrests. [@1]

The same day's clamp-down also produced arrests at a house in Bontebok Avenue,
Bridgeton, and one in Thabo Mbeki Square, Bongulethu, on liquor charges only.
[@2]

Police described the operation as part of their effort to eradicate substance
abuse in vulnerable communities of the Western Cape. [@2]
