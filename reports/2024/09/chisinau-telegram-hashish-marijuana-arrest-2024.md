Officers in Chisinau detained a 31-year-old man on suspicion of selling drugs
via the Telegram messaging app. [@1] The operation was carried out by officers
of the National Investigation Inspectorate's Directorate for Combating Organised
Crime. [@2]

A search at his home in Chisinau uncovered 1,100 grams of hashish and 300 grams
of marijuana, as well as 500 grams of PVP, mephedrone and mobile phones. [@2]
Authorities estimated the total black-market value of all the seized substances
at 700,000 lei. [@1]

The suspect was placed in preventive detention for 72 hours and faces between
seven and 15 years in prison. [@1]
