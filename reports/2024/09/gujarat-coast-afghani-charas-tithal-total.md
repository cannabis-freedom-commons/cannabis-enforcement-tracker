Valsad police seized a packet of Afghani charas lying abandoned on the banks of
Tithal beach on Sunday. [@1] Officers found the packet on the ground beside a
protection wall near the Sai Baba temple on Sunday evening after local passersby
tipped them off, and forensic science laboratory officials checked the material.
[@1] The seized charas weighed 1.18 kg and police valued it at Rs 58.60 lakh.
[@1]

Valsad rural police registered a case under sections of the NDPS Act, the
Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, against unknown persons and
began a probe. [@1]

In the previous 22 days, police in Surat, Navsari and Valsad had seized 93
packets of high-purity Afghani charas valued at over Rs 56.46 crore from
different seashores. [@1] On August 12 and 13, Valsad police recovered 32
packets of Afghani charas in beach areas near protection walls at Udvada and
Dhanti-Bhagal village in Pardi taluka, each weighing 1.18 kg and together worth
around Rs 19 crore. [@1] Gujarat Samachar reported that 21 packets of charas
were found in Valsad by the local crime branch and special operations group,
with writing in Urdu on them. [@2] It said three suspicious packets found during
coastal checks in Surat contained approximately 3 kg of Afghani charas worth Rs
1.50 crore. [@2] Gujarat Samachar said smugglers wrap the packets in six layers
so the parcels survive being thrown into the water. [@2]

The Indian Express described Afghani charas as produced from live cannabis grown
in Afghanistan. [@3] An earlier Indian Express account of the first Udvada
seizure put it at 11.746 kg and the international market value of a kilogram at
Rs 50 lakh. [@3] That account called the Udvada find the first such consignment
on the South Gujarat coastline. [@3]
