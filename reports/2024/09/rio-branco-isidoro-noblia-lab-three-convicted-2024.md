Customs officers on a routine check in Río Branco, Cerro Largo department, tried
to stop two vehicles but one accelerated away and the second turned around and
fled back towards the city. [@1] Cerro Largo police helped the customs staff and
found one of the cars abandoned in a dead-end street in the city, carrying
10.460 kilos of marijuana buds. [@1]

By judicial order officers raided a farm, or chacra, in Isidoro Noblía with the
Unidad Investigativa II of the Cerro Largo police and found a clandestine
laboratory for growing and producing marijuana buds. [@1] There they seized 212
cannabis plants, 4.178 kilograms of marijuana buds and six vehicles registered
in Brazil. [@1]

Three men were detained; two were convicted and sentenced to two years and four
months in prison, and the third to 20 months in prison to be served under the
probationary regime known as libertad a prueba. [@1]
