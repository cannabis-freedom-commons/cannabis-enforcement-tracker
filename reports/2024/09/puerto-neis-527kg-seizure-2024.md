Argentine Naval Prefecture officers seized a marijuana load in Puerto Rico,
Misiones. [@1] During river patrols to prevent crime, officers spotted a rowing
boat grounded on the Argentine bank at kilometre 1,731.5 of the Paraná River, in
an area known as Puerto Neis, with rectangular packages inside. [@1] Officers
found similar packages scattered nearby, bringing the total number of pieces
found to 22. [@1]

Maritime authority personnel inspected the packages and confirmed that they
contained 475 “panes” of marijuana, meaning pressed bricks. [@1] The final
weight was 527 kilograms, and the Prefecture valued the load at more than 1.2
billion pesos. [@1] The 22 pieces counted at the scene were the packages found
in and around the boat; 475 was the count of cannabis bricks contained within
them. [@1] The Federal Prosecutor’s Office in Oberá, headed by Viviana Alejandra
Vallejos, intervened in the case. [@1]
