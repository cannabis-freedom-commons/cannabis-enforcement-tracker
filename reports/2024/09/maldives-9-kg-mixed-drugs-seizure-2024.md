Maldives Customs seized 9 kg of drugs from a 65-year-old Maldivian man who
arrived on a Sri Lankan Airlines flight on Wednesday. [@1] Customs found 9 kg of
drugs in an air cargo shipment brought to Maldives on Wednesday and in a post
package. [@1]

During first-stage testing, the seized substances were identified as synthetic
cathinone, heroin, cannabis and ecstasy. [@1] Customs found 349 grams of heroin
in the man's possession. [@1] The post package contained 582 grams of synthetic
cathinone. [@1] Together with the cannabis and ecstasy, the total weight of the
seized drugs is 9 kg, Customs said. [@1]

Customs handed the man and the seized drugs to police for further investigation.
