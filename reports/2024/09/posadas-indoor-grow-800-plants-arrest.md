The Guardia Civil, Spain's military-status police force, arrested a 35-year-old
resident of Posadas, an Andalusian town in the province of Córdoba, on suspicion
of drug trafficking and of cultivating and processing drugs. [@1] Officers
seized 800 cannabis plants in an advanced stage of flowering and growth and
dismantled the indoor plantation. [@1]

During a public-security patrol near the town, officers noticed a strong smell
of marijuana coming from a house where a compressor-like noise was heard, and
the working refrigeration confirmed their suspicion of an indoor grow. [@2] The
grower was tracked down as the occupant of the apparently empty house, and a
search of the house found the plantation and a bag of 20 grams of crack cocaine
prepared for sale. [@2]

The plantation sat on the upper floor behind a door, with ventilation and air
conditioning, eleven 720-watt LED lamps and two large carbon filters to suppress
the smell. [@2] The plants were weeks away from finishing flowering, when they
would have been pulled up and dried for sale, the Guardia Civil said. [@1] The
man was placed at the disposal of the courts together with the plants and the
cocaine; the reports name no charge, plea or court outcome. [@1]
