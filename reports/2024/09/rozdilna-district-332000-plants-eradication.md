Police in Odesa found a huge cannabis plantation near the border with Moldova, in a village
of the Rozdilna district. [@1] The plot covered nearly 7 hectares, about ten football fields,
on land of Ukraine's forest fund used by a state forestry enterprise. [@1]

The land was ploughed and the crop regularly cultivated, with the rows thinned out. [@1]
Police counted 332,000 cannabis plants on the plot and found a tractor with a trailer
there. [@1] All the plants were destroyed. [@1]

Officers were collecting evidence to bring everyone involved in the illegal cultivation to
justice. [@1] Criminal proceedings were opened under the article on illegal production and
distribution of drugs. [@1]
