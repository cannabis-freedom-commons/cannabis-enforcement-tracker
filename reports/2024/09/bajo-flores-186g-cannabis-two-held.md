Two Peruvian citizens were detained after gendarmes found them selling drugs on
the streets of the former Villa 1-11-14 in the Autonomous City of Buenos Aires.
[@1] Personnel of Squadron "1-11-14" of the Agrupación "Cinturón Sur", a
grouping of Argentina's National Gendarmerie, were walking through the Barrio
Padre Rodolfo Ricciardelli, the official name of that settlement, when they saw
two men selling narcotics at retail on the public street. [@1] The Unidad de
Flagrancia Oeste ordered both men, both Peruvian nationals, detained and ordered
the substances seized, together with other items of interest. [@1]

At the scene the gendarmes searched the men and swept the area, and recovered
203 wrappers of a yellow granulated substance, already portioned and ready for
sale, that tested positive for pasta base and weighed 55.3 grams. [@1] They also
counted 29 wrappers of cocaine weighing 9.7 grams and 50 doses of cannabis
sativa weighing 186.6 grams. [@1] Taken together, the packages were 282 wrappers
of cocaine, paste base and marijuana. [@1]

The cannabis in the seizure was those 50 street doses of cannabis sativa, kept
separate from the paste base and the cocaine found in the same stop. [@1]
