Detectives assigned to the St Elizabeth Police Division seized 216 pounds
(about 98 kg) of compressed ganja during a snap raid in Burnt Savannah on
Tuesday, 3 September 2024. [@1,2]

Officers on patrol saw a group of men who ran as law enforcement approached.
[@2] Between 5:30 and 6:30 p.m., officers searched an abandoned building and
found the drug packed in four white knitted bags. [@1,2]

Police believe the ganja was packaged for export and valued it at about
J$2.16 million. [@1,2] No one was arrested in relation to the seizure. [@1,2]
