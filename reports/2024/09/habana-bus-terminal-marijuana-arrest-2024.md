Authorities detained one person at a bus terminal in Havana who was carrying a
wrapper containing marijuana. [@1] The detention occurred at the Terminal
Nacional de Ómnibus, where officers found a wrapper of marijuana on the accused.
[@2] Authorities did not publish the amount of marijuana in the wrapper. [@1]
The detainee was described as presumably Cuban. [@1] Prosecutors considered a
sanction of more than 10 years in prison for the accused. [@2]

The marijuana detention was announced together with an operation against químico
in Marianao, in Havana, in which 22 people were detained. [@1] Interior Ministry
agents had seized the synthetic drug known as químico a few weeks earlier in the
Coco Solo-Zamora council in Marianao and detained 22 citizens linked to its
distribution. [@2] In the same announcement, two foreigners detected with
cocaine inside their bodies at José Martí International Airport faced requested
15-year prison terms. [@1] Authorities said confiscation applies not only to the
substances but also to related assets such as plots, cars or other linked
vehicles. [@1] In Havana courts, 96% of people tried for drug trafficking or
possession had received sanctions, with 95.8% sentenced to between 10 and 20
years, according to experts cited in the announcement. [@1]

Cuba's anti-drug chief has described químico as a synthetic cannabinoid with 45
variants entering as powder, liquid, mixed with acetone, impregnated in paper or
in plant material. [@1]
