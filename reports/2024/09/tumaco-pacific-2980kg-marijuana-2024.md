On the night of 26 September 2024, Colombia's Navy intercepted a suspicious boat
in Pacific waters near Tumaco, Nariño. [@1] The boat was crewed by three
Colombian nationals and was carrying a total load of 3,735 kilograms of drugs.
[@1] Navy units on maritime control detected the boat by radar, and a Tumaco
Coast Guard unit pursued it to a maritime interdiction. [@1] Inspectors found
148 bales of different shapes and sizes containing illicit substances aboard the
boat. [@1] The three crew members, aged 33, 42 and 52, were captured and taken
with the cargo and the boat to the Tumaco Coast Guard Station. [@1]

The CTI's Preliminary Homologated Identification Test confirmed the packages
held 2,980 kilograms of marijuana and 755 kilograms of cocaine hydrochloride.
[@1] Authorities valued the seizure at 44 million dollars, more than 186 billion
Colombian pesos, saying it stopped about 2.3 million doses reaching the
international market. [@1]

The operation was part of the Navy's integral maritime security strategy on the
Pacific coast, described as a key drug-trafficking route toward Central America
and other destinations. [@1]
