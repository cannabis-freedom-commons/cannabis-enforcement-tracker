Customs officers at Laem Chabang Port in Si Racha seized more than 1.4 tonnes
of cannabis buds bound for the United Kingdom, director Direk Kocharak
announced at a press conference on 25 September 2024. [@1,2] Acting on
information from international agencies, officers working with the Seaport
Interdiction Task Force, the UNODC-WCO Container Control Programme and the UK
Border Force detained two suspicious export declarations for inspection. [@1]

On 11 September officers opened three pallets declared as rubber fitness mats
and found modified wooden boxes holding 153.3 kg of cannabis buds worth 5
million baht. [@1,2] On 16 September they opened four crates declared as fabric
gloves and found compressed cannabis, buds and rolled joints weighing 1.3
tonnes and worth 20 million baht. [@1,2] Together the 1,453.3 kg was valued at
25 million baht domestically and an estimated 100 million baht once exported.
[@1,2] Those responsible face charges of false export declarations under
sections 202, 244 and 252 of the Customs Act. [@2]
