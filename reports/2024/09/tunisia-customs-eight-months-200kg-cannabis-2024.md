Tunisia's customs service seized 200 kg of cannabis, known locally as zatla, at
the country's border crossings in the first eight months of 2024, its spokesman
Colonel Chokri Jabri said. [@1] Jabri announced the eight-month figures on
Tuesday 3 September 2024 in Mosaïque FM's morning programme "Sbeh Ennes". [@1]
The same customs total counted more than 31 kg of cocaine and 745,000 narcotic
tablets, listed separately from the cannabis. [@1] The Mosaïque FM report of the
same phone interview gives the cannabis weight as 200 kg of zatla, the Tunisian
name for cannabis resin, alongside more than 31 kg of cocaine and 745,000
narcotic tablets. [@2]

In a separate case, customs units at Tunis-Carthage airport seized more than 6.5
kg of cocaine the day before, on Monday 2 September 2024, hidden in the soles of
12 shoes and in the lining of a suitcase. [@1] The cocaine was carried by a
woman passenger from sub-Saharan Africa who had arrived from an Arab country,
and the representative of a private clinic who met her was also arrested. [@1]
