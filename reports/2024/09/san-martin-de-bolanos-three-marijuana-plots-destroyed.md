Guardia Nacional members on security and crime-prevention patrols in the
community of Rancho Viejo, in the municipality of San Martín de Bolaños,
Jalisco, spotted a plot holding plants of different heights with characteristics
similar to marijuana. [@1] Officers secured the area and mounted a search for
the people responsible for the crop, with no positive results, but found two
more stretches of land growing plants with the same characteristics. [@1]

The three plantings together covered an estimated 13,000 square metres, about
1.3 hectares, split into plots of about 1,300, 4,700 and 7,000 square metres.
[@1] With authorisation, the guards destroyed the plants of apparent marijuana
by manual labour and incineration. [@1]

The National Guard said its members located and destroyed the three suspected
marijuana plantings as part of Mexico's National Security Strategy. [@1] The
guards reported the discovery to the Federal Public Ministry's agency in the
state before carrying out the destruction. [@1] The agency described the
operation as part of its work to curb the production and distribution of
substances affecting public health. [@1]
