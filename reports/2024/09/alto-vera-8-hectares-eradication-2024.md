Agents of the Special Forces of Paraguay's Secretaría Nacional Antidrogas
(Senad), the national anti-drug agency, from its regional department No. 3 in
Itapúa found four parcels of marijuana plantations at Compañía Alto Verá Km 4,
in the district of Alto Verá, Itapúa department. [@1] The eradication procedure
began at 07:00 that day. [@1] TVS Encarnación reported the operation was headed
by the regional department No. 3 of Senad, based in Encarnación, with special
forces personnel. [@2] Enrique Fornerón, an agent of Itapúa's specialised
anti-drug trafficking unit, led the incursion, according to ABC Color, while TVS
described him as the prosecutor supervising the procedure. [@1]

In total, 8 hectares of marijuana plantations were destroyed. [@1] That total
came from four parcels of two hectares each, TVS Encarnación reported. [@2] TVS
gave the site's coordinates as 26°46'50.2"S and 55°49'58.9"W. [@2] Neither
account names anyone arrested or charged in the operation. [@1]

ABC Color recalled that on 16 August a car carrying more than 300 kg of pressed
marijuana from Alto Verá district was seized in Encarnación on its way to
Argentina. [@1]
