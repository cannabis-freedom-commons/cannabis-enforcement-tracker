Twenty-eight-year-old Jennifer Marroquin Valencia of Los Angeles, California,
appeared unrepresented in the Belize City Magistrate's Court on 6 September 2024
to answer a single count of importation of a controlled drug. [@1] She pleaded
guilty at the first opportunity. [@1] She told the court she was from Los
Angeles, where marijuana is legal, and was unaware of Belizean law. [@2]

She was caught with 11.1 grams of marijuana when she arrived at the Philip
Goldson International Airport the previous day, 5 September 2024. [@1] The court
fined her $1,000 plus a $5 cost-of-court fee, ordered to be paid forthwith
because she is not a Belizean citizen. [@2]

Two other Americans, 49-year-old Roderick Boone and 37-year-old Lee Whyms, had
been caught at the same airport in late August in similar importation cases.
[@2]
