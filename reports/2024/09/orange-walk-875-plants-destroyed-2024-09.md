Police officers carried out an operation in Orange Walk District on Monday 16
September 2024 between 4:00 a.m. and 10:00 a.m. [@1] The operation led to the
discovery of a plantation holding 875 marijuana plants. [@1] The plants found
measured between 2 and 6 feet in height. [@1] No one was present in the area
when police arrived, and all the plants were uprooted and destroyed by fire.
[@1]

7 News Belize reported that police reached the plantation not long after 4:00
a.m. that morning and found the same total of 875 marijuana plants. [@2] The
station noted that marijuana in consumption amounts may be legal in Belize but
growing or selling the plant remains against the law. [@2] The outlet's second
account said officers descended on the farm shortly after 4:00 a.m. and found no
one in the vicinity, after which the plants were uprooted and burned. [@3]
