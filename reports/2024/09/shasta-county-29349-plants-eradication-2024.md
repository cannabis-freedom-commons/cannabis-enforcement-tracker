The Shasta County Sheriff's Office ran a large-scale marijuana eradication
operation from September 9 to September 13, 2024. [@1] Deputies served 34 search
warrants on 42 parcels within Shasta County during the five days. [@1] The
sheriff's Marijuana Eradication Team reported finding 29,349 marijuana plants,
796 pounds of processed marijuana and 11 firearms. [@1]

The sheriff's office said many of the properties were believed to be tied to
drug trafficking organizations. [@1] It said illegal grows harm people who
cultivate legally, damage the environment and attract a criminal element to the
county. [@1] The sheriff's office led the operation with the Shasta Interagency
Narcotics Task Force, Cal Fire Law Enforcement, the Shasta County District
Attorney's Office, the California Department of Fish and Wildlife and the
California Department of Justice's Eradication Prevention of Illicit Cannabis
programme. [@1] Cal Fire issued two citations for fire-related offences during
the operation. [@1]
