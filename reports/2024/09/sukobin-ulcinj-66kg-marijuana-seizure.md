Montenegro's Border Police seized 66 kilograms of skunk marijuana in the area of
the village of Sukobin in Ulcinj after two suspected carriers fled toward
Albania and abandoned their bags when they saw police teams. [@1,3] The Albanian
police were searching for the two fugitives after the incident. [@1,3]

Police had acted on operational information that drugs were being trafficked
from Albania to Montenegro in that village area and that two unknown persons
with large bags were moving near the border. [@1,3] A search of the area found
three bags with 119 packages of skunk marijuana weighing about 66 kilograms, as
announced by the Montenegrin Police Directorate. [@1,3] Officers using a drone
spotted the two people before they managed to leave toward Albania. [@2]

The Ulcinj border police located and seized about 66 kilograms of narcotics
suspected to be marijuana in the Sukobin area. [@2] The drugs were found in the
area of Sukobin village in Ulcinj after being smuggled from Albania toward
Montenegro. [@2] The High State Prosecutor's Office in Podgorica was notified of
the incident. [@2,3]
