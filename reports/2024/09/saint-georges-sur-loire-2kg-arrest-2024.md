Gendarmes arrested a 19-year-old man from the Central African Republic at
Saint-Georges-sur-Loire, in Maine-et-Loire, on 4 September 2024. [@1] He was
arrested while in possession of 2 kg of cannabis. [@1]

The arrest followed a road check at which the suspect at first refused to comply
with the officers' instructions. [@1] He then fled by car, pursued by gendarmes
of the surveillance and intervention platoon, the PSIG, of Angers. [@1] During
the chase he tried to get rid of the drug by throwing it out of his vehicle's
window, and the officers saw him do it. [@1]

Once caught, the young man was placed in garde à vue, French police custody.
[@1] During questioning he reportedly said he had acted under duress for a
trafficker based in Rezé, near Nantes. [@1] He faces a prison sentence of up to
10 years. [@1]
