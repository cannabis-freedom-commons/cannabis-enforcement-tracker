Almir Cahani, a 31-year-old Albanian national, was jailed for 28 months at Selkirk
Sheriff Court on 2 September 2024 for producing cannabis at the former Buccleuch
Hotel in Hawick. [@1,3] Police found him hiding in an attic after he used bolt
cutters to get into the building, which had been secured with double doors. [@1,3]
The raid on the Trinity Street hotel, carried out in October 2023, uncovered 77 kg
of cannabis worth up to £771,000 alongside 410 plants that had not fully
matured. [@1,2,3] Cahani pleaded guilty at Jedburgh Sheriff Court to producing the
class B drug and being concerned in its supply between 19 September and 5 October
2023, while not-guilty pleas to breaching the Electricity Act and to culpable and
reckless conduct were accepted by the Crown. [@1] His sentence was backdated to
6 October 2023, when he was first remanded. [@1]

## The co-accused

Dritan Katana, 41 and also from Albania, received the same 28-month sentence after
admitting similar charges, and was warned he faces deportation on release. [@1]
Unlike Katana, Cahani told the court he hoped to remain in the country and appears
to have successfully claimed asylum in the United Kingdom. [@1] Cahani's solicitor
said he had effectively acted as a gardener, had been locked in the building for
around two weeks before the raid, and that the grow appeared much older. [@1]
Sheriff Kevin McCarron told Cahani the offence was very serious and had to attract
a substantial custodial sentence. [@1]
