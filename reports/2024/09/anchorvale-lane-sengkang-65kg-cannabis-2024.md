On the morning of 17 September 2024, Central Narcotics Bureau officers arrested a 26-year-old man
in the car park of a residential estate near Anchorvale Lane and then raided a flat in the same
estate, where four other men were arrested. [@1] All five are Singaporeans aged 19 to 39, held for
suspected drug trafficking. [@1,2]

The flat yielded about 6.15 kg of cannabis, 777 g of 'Ice', 281 g of Ecstasy, 693 LSD stamps and
1,378 Erimin-5 tablets, and a search of one suspect's car in the estate added another 375 g of
cannabis plus more 'Ice', Ecstasy and Erimin-5. [@1] Officers also recovered a small amount of
'Ice', LSD stamps and Erimin-5 tablets from one suspect's official residence near Tampines North
Drive 1. [@1] In total the operation netted about 6.5 kg of cannabis, a figure a wire-service
account corroborated, and CNB valued the whole cocktail of drugs at about S$333,000, enough for
some 1,480 abusers for a week. [@1,2]

Three children aged 11, 13 and 15 were in the raided flat, and arrangements were made for their
welfare before they were placed in the care of next of kin. [@1] Investigations were continuing. [@1]
