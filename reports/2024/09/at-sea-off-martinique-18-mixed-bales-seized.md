On 25 September 2024, the French Navy surveillance frigate Germinal intercepted
an unflagged fast boat on the high seas about 850 km off Martinique. [@1] The
crew acted on information transmitted by Colombia and the United States Joint
InterAgency Task Force-South, which coordinates counter-trafficking assets in
the Caribbean. [@1]

Officers recovered 18 bales containing cocaine and marijuana with a combined
weight of 526 kg. [@2] The 526 kg figure covers cocaine and marijuana together,
not cannabis alone. [@3] In agreement with the Fort-de-France specialised
interregional jurisdiction, all of the seized drugs were destroyed. [@2]

French forces described the operation as their 12th drug seizure in the Antilles
in 2024. [@1] They said more than 28 tonnes of cocaine had been intercepted
since the start of the year, a total covering cocaine rather than cannabis
alone. [@2]
