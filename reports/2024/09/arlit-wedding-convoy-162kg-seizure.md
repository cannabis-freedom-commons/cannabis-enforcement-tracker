On 28 September 2024, officers of Niger's Central Office for the Repression of
Drug Trafficking (OCRTIS) intercepted a convoy in Arlit in the north of the
country. [@1] The traffickers had concealed the cannabis inside a wedding
procession to try to evade controls. [@1] OCRTIS is the Office central de
répression du trafic des stupéfiants, the specialist anti-drug office that
carried out the intervention in the town of Arlit. [@1]

The seizure totalled 162 kg of cannabis. [@1] The consignment held 157 bricks of
cannabis, probably from a neighbouring country. [@2] The load was taken at Arlit
in the north of the country on 28 September. [@2]

Arlit divisional commissioner Ibrahim Tassiou reported the seizure and called it
another foiled attempt, saying his teams had intercepted the cargo despite the
wedding-procession ruse. [@1] Arlit prefect Ousman Almoustapha thanked the
officers for their commitment and said the action helped protect Niger's young
people from the scourge of drugs. [@1] He also highlighted the cooperation
between the different actors that made the success possible. [@1]
