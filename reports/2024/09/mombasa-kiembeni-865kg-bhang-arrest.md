Officers from Kadzandani Police Station in Nyali raided a house at Green
Estate, Kiembeni, on Friday 20 September 2024 after a tip-off from members of
the public. [@1,2] They uncovered 16 sacks of cannabis, known locally as
bhang, weighing 864.9 kg with an estimated street value of Sh25,407,000. [@1,2]

Police arrested 30-year-old Cynthia Anyango, who was found in the house, which
is believed to belong to a suspected trafficker who escaped before the
officers arrived. [@1,2] She was taken in to assist the investigation and help
trace the main suspect. [@1,2]

The Directorate of Criminal Investigations said the seized bhang was kept as an
exhibit while the hunt for the trafficker continued. [@1,2]
