Belize Police Department officers destroyed a marijuana plantation in the
Rhaburn Ridge area after acting on information they received. [@1] Commissioner
of Police Chester Williams said Rhaburn Ridge lies in the Belize District but
falls under the Orange Walk police jurisdiction. [@1]

The destruction was announced together with a drug operation in Sarteneja,
Corozal District, where police found 25 parcels of suspected cocaine weighing
28.38 kilograms at a home and detained four people. [@2] Separately, police
reported a drug bust in the Chunox Village area of Corozal District where six
parcels of suspected cocaine were found at a house and a number of people were
detained. [@1] Williams said one of those detained over the Chunox parcels
pleaded guilty in court while the case was withdrawn against the other. [@1]

Williams said the police operations in the north are continuing with resources
the government of Belize has funded. [@1] Love FM carried the same briefing,
quoting Williams saying the police recently destroyed a marijuana plantation in
the Rhaburn Ridge area. [@2] Mikey Live, a Caribbean news site, reproduced the
same Rhaburn Ridge account in its report on the prime minister denying Belize is
a major drug-producing country. [@3]
