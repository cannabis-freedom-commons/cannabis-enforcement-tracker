Police in the Brussels-Midi zone carried out 13 searches on the morning of
Wednesday 4 September 2024 in an inquiry into drug trafficking around place
Saint-Antoine in Forest, a Brussels municipality. [@1] Eleven people were
arrested in the operation. [@1] Nine of the detainees were brought before an
investigating judge, who charged them with unlawful possession of narcotics and
selling narcotics as an association. [@1] The judge placed eight of them under
arrest warrant and released one under conditions. [@1]

Officers seized 21 kg of cannabis, 260 sachets of cocaine, 60,000 euros in cash,
a fake pistol and two cars. [@1] Police also recovered two motorcycles that had
been reported stolen. [@1]

Place Saint-Antoine had long been known as a place where drugs were sold, and in
the night of 12 to 13 March 2024 three people were wounded by gunfire in the
nearby rue de Fierlant in what was described as a settling of scores between
rival traffickers. [@1] On the day of the operation Sudinfo reported 13
searches, two of them backed by the police special response unit, and 12
suspects detained, including an unaccompanied minor. [@2] La Libre, citing its
sister outlet La Capitale, reported 15 searches, 12 arrests and 40,000 euros in
cash, while the police zone said its final figures would follow. [@3] RTL's
video report of the operation described 13 searches, 11 arrests, 21 kilos of
cannabis, 260 sachets of cocaine, 60,000 euros in cash, a fake pistol and two
stolen motorcycles. [@4]
