## Oplan Wasay eradication

Combined operatives of the Kalinga police office uprooted and torched about P1.6 million worth of
fully grown marijuana plants in Kalinga on Tuesday 10 September 2024. [@1,2] At least 8,000 plants
stood on an 800-square-metre lot discovered through "Oplan Wasay" (Axe). [@1] The operation was
led by Tinglayan town police with the Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency's Cordillera office,
joined by the Kalinga intelligence and drug enforcement units, PDEA Kalinga, the 2nd Kalinga
mobile force company and the regional mobile force battalion. [@1,2]

## No one caught

After documenting the haul, officers destroyed the plants at the site, and no one was caught as
the grower behind the plantation. [@1] The day-long Cordillera operations also netted shabu and
an arrest in Ifugao and Benguet, reported separately from this eradication. [@2]
