Portugal's Judicial Police, working with Spain's UDYCO drugs unit and National Police after a two-month joint investigation, detained five men and seized about 2,000 kilos of hashish and two vehicles on 8 November 2014. [@1]

Officers followed a large cement truck that crossed the International Bridge over the Rio Guadiana toward Tavira to Cumenda, where the drugs were hidden in a property owned by an absent foreigner. [@1] Police moved in as the hashish was being loaded onto the vehicle and arrested all five people involved. [@1]

The detainees, aged 30 to 58, included Romanian, Dutch and Spanish nationals, and they appeared in court on an application to be remanded in custody. [@1] The property owner was believed possibly unaware that drugs were stashed on his land. [@1]
