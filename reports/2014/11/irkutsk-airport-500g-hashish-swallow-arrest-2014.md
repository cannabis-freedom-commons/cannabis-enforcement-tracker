Drug-control officers detained a 21-year-old native of Kyrgyzstan at Irkutsk
airport on suspicion of carrying drugs inside his body. [@1] The young
swallower-courier had flown to Irkutsk from Bishkek. [@1] Irkutsk drug-control
officers said they stopped the 21-year-old in the airport for intracavitary
hashish transport and recovered about 500 grams in total. [@2]

Officers seized 79 capsules of hashish weighing about 500 grams from the
courier. [@1] A Bishkek account confirms officers found 500 grams of hashish in
the 21-year-old Kyrgyzstani man's stomach. [@3] On questioning he said it was
his second visit to Irkutsk after first coming in September to seek work, when
an acquaintance offered him courier work. [@1]

He was told to return to Bishkek and bring drugs to Irkutsk for payment, then he
swallowed 79 tape-wrapped pressed hashish parcels. [@2] A criminal case was
opened against the courier and he was placed in pre-trial detention. [@1] A
request for a character reference was sent to his home country to inform a later
court decision. [@1]
