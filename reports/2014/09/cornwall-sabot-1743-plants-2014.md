Between 8 and 11 September 2014, the Cornwall Regional Task Force (CRTF) with
the Canadian Armed Forces ran the year's Project SABOT sweep across Stormont,
Dundas and Glengarry counties and the Prescott-Russell counties. [@1] Officers
seized 1,743 cannabis plants, found through aerial patrols and tips from
concerned residents, before the crop could be harvested. [@1] The OPP drug
enforcement unit and emergency response team, the Cornwall and Akwesasne Mohawk
police services and RCMP detachments from Kingston and Ottawa assisted in the
operation. [@1]

Project SABOT is the annual RCMP and armed-forces programme against outdoor
cannabis cultivation, running since 1989 with military aircraft, surveillance
equipment and personnel. [@1] The task force's Inspector Tim Kimpan said the
proactive sweeps hit the activities and finances of organised crime. [@1]
