Detectives from the Busko-Zdrój county police headquarters detained a
26-year-old man they suspected of growing and selling cannabis. [@1] The suspect
lived in one of the villages of Gmina Busko-Zdrój, the district that takes its
name from the town of Busko-Zdrój in Świętokrzyskie Voivodeship. [@1]

In one of the rooms the man used, officers found four cannabis seedlings in a
wardrobe, lit by a grow lamp that gave them good growing conditions. [@1] In the
same room they found two plastic bags holding 125 grams of green-brown plant
material, which they provisionally identified as marijuana. [@1] Elsewhere on
the property they found five more cannabis bushes about a metre tall. [@1]

Police seized the plants, the dried material and the cultivation equipment, nine
cannabis plants in all. [@1] The 26-year-old provisionally admitted growing the
cannabis and said he did it for his own use. [@1] Police said the offence he is
suspected of carries up to three years in prison, and the account came from the
Busko-Zdrój county police headquarters. [@1]
