Tajikistan's drug-control agency detained a 20-year-old Afghan citizen on the
Tajik Pamir for smuggling hashish. [@1] The detention operation took place near
the town of Khorog, about 520 kilometres south-east of Dushanbe. [@1] The man
had illegally crossed the state border along the Pyanj river on a homemade craft
made from a car inner tube, using a primitive paddle. [@1] Officers were waiting
in ambush for him on the Tajik bank of the river. [@1] He was charged with
especially large-scale drug trafficking, illegal crossing of the state border
and contraband. [@1] If found guilty, he faced 12 to 20 years' imprisonment.
[@1]

Officers found 20 briquettes of hashish weighing 18 kilograms in total on him at
detention. [@1]

Tajikistan's Drug Control Agency, the AKN under the president of Tajikistan, was
the authority that announced the detention. [@1] A second account repeats the
same agency report of 20 briquettes weighing 18 kilograms and the same three
charges. [@2]
