Timothy Sturgis, 42, shot and killed himself on the evening of 2 September after a two-hour standoff at his property just outside Ashville, Ohio, where he was growing marijuana.[@1,2] A state helicopter working with the Bureau of Criminal Investigation's Marijuana Eradication and Clandestine Lab unit had spotted the plants, and three detectives from the Pickaway County Sheriff's Office and the US 23 Major Crimes Task Force approached on foot.[@1] When confronted, Sturgis pointed an AK-47 rifle at his own head and, after the standoff, fired a single shot.[@1,2] Sheriff Robert Radcliffe said the grow was moderate, with more plants inside the house and weapons in every room, and that deputies had hoped Sturgis would put the gun down.[@2]
