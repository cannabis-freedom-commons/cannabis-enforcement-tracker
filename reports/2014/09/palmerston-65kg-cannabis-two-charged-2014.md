Northern Territory Police searched a storage shed, two hotel rooms and a home in Woodroffe, Palmerston, on Thursday and Friday, 25 and 26 September 2014. [@1] In one seizure, officers found about 65 kg of cannabis packed in 144 individual pound bags ready for sale, along with a large amount of cash. [@1] A further search turned up more cash and cannabis, two firearms and three large boxes of fireworks, bringing the seized cash to $133,000, which police described as believed proceeds of crime. [@1]

Police arrested a 51-year-old South Australian man in a hotel room on the Friday and a 30-year-old man at a home on Sibbald Crescent in Woodroffe. [@1] Both men were charged with possessing a commercial quantity of cannabis and supplying a commercial quantity of cannabis, and were due to appear in the Darwin Magistrates Court on Monday 29 September. [@1]
