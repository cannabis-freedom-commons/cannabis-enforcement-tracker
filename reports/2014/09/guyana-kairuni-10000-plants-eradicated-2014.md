Police narcotics branch investigators carried out a drug eradication operation
at Kairuni along the Soesdyke-Linden Highway on Saturday 13 September 2014. [@2]
Officers found a field of about two acres, roughly 0.81 hectares, growing some
10,000 cannabis sativa plants about one foot tall. [@1] The police said a
nursery with seedlings and an abandoned camp stood at the site, and that the
plants were all destroyed. [@3]

Two men were arrested and were in police custody assisting with the
investigations. [@1] The account came from a statement by the Guyana Police
Force, which the outlets published with their reports the next day. [@1]
Kaieteur News reported that police destroyed the two acres of marijuana during a
raid in the Soesdyke/Linden Highway area. [@3]
