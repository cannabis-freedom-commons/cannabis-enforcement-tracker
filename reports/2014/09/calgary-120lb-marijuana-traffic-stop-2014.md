Calgary police seized roughly 120 pounds (about 54.4 kg) of processed
marijuana from two vehicles stopped in a northwest Calgary parking lot on 25
September 2014. [@1] Officers acting on a tip earlier in the year had found
that a large quantity of marijuana was being transported from British Columbia
to Alberta. [@1] The drugs were packed in dozens of heat-sealed packages
hidden in black garbage bags, with an estimated street value of $535,500. [@1]

Sandra Pearl Dalpe, 61, of Chestermere was charged with trafficking cannabis
and possession for the purpose of trafficking, while Douglas Paul Hryniw, 51,
of Calgary was charged with possession for the purpose of trafficking. [@1]
The seizure was announced on 3 October 2014. [@1]
