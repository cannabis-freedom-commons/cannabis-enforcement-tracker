Police ranks conducted a drug eradication operation at the La Harmonie Backdam on the Demerara River on 4 September 2014 and found a field of cannabis sativa under cultivation. [@1] The field was about three acres in size, some 1.21 hectares, with about 3,000 plants ranging in height from four to seven feet. [@1]

The plants were destroyed. [@1]

## No arrests

No one was arrested during the operation, and the account names no suspects behind the field. [@1]
