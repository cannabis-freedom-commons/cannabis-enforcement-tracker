On 12 September 2014, an Accra circuit court remanded Police Chief Inspector
Paul Akpabli with driver Peter Kamasah and driver's mate Daniel Ekualiho over
283 slabs of suspected cannabis found in a bus stopped between Kpeve and Dzodze
in the Volta Region. [@1] The three faced two counts, conspiracy to commit
crime and possession of narcotic drugs without lawful authority, and all
pleaded not guilty. [@1, 2] The court remanded them into police custody to
reappear on 25 September. [@1]

The prosecution said the bus, loaded with seven fertiliser sacks of compressed
slabs, was stopped on 10 August on its way from Kpeve to Dzodze. [@1] Kamasah
and Ekualiho told police they had been contracted by a man of Dzodze, who gave
them 400 cedis and Akpabli's phone number, and that Akpabli met them at Kpeve,
took the money, loaded the sacks at Kpeve-Tornu and was to escort the bus to
Dzodze before they were arrested halfway. [@1, 2] Police retrieved 1,100 cedis
from the inspector, including the 400 cedis in 20-cedi notes the pair said they
had handed over, and cited phone calls between the driver and Akpabli on the
day. [@1, 2]
