Paraguayan anti-narcotics police found 25,704 kg of pressed marijuana ready for distribution, plus another 850 kg of loose marijuana in 34 bags, inside a private property in the Aguara neighbourhood of Capitán Bado, Amambay, on the afternoon of 18 September 2014. [@1] The procedure was led by the Pedro Juan Caballero anti-drugs prosecutor Katia Uemura, and the area's correspondent reported the stockpile was ready to supply dealers across the nearby Brazilian border. [@1,2]

Earlier that day, SENAD agents seized about 4,500 kg of marijuana, in pressed and loose form with packaging equipment, at rural camps in Itakyry, Alto Paraná, and destroyed it on the spot on prosecution orders. [@1,2] Together the two operations took more than 30 tonnes out of circulation, a haul worth millions that was likely destined for Brazilian criminal groups. [@2]
