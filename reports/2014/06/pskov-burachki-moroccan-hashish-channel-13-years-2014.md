Russia's federal drug control service said officers of the Pskov drug police had
taken about 91 kilograms of Moroccan hashish out of circulation. [@1] The
service's head, Viktor Ivanov, announced the result to journalists after an away
meeting of the State Anti-Drug Committee. [@1] Ivanov said the drugs never
reached consumers, putting them at about 300,000 doses worth more than 60
million roubles. [@1] The hashish was of Moroccan origin and travelled through
10 countries, among them Portugal, France, Germany, Belgium, Poland and Belarus,
he said. [@1] A group of 12 people organised the supply channel and drug police
worked on it for a year and a half, according to Ivanov. [@1]

At the end of January 2014, drug control officers and customs staff at the
Burachki border checkpoint in Sebezhsky district stopped 46 kilograms 515 grams
of hashish from being brought into the Customs Union. [@1] The hashish was
hidden in the fuel tank of a Volkswagen Sharan driven by a Moldovan citizen who
was travelling into Russia from Latvia. [@1] Prosecutors in Sebezhsky district
said the hashish was found in the car's tank as the accused crossed from Latvia
into Russia near Burachki on the evening of 25 January 2014, and that the case
had been sent to court. [@2] Prosecutors put the quantity at 46,515 grams of
hashish. [@2] A court sentenced the driver, named as Kirill Goncharov, to 13
years in prison for smuggling narcotics on an especially large scale under part
4 of article 229.1 of the Criminal Code. [@1]

The organiser of the supply channel, also a Moldovan citizen, was detained some
time later in a raid that took an even larger amount of hashish. [@1] He was
arrested in the car park of an industrial estate run by the individual
entrepreneur Voytov in the village of Gorki, Leninsky district, Moscow Oblast,
where his Opel Astra held two checked bags of hashish wrapped with a chocolate
scent to defeat sniffer dogs. [@1] The hashish bars in the car weighed more than
38 kilograms; officers also found 85 more packages weighing more than 40
kilograms in a trailer on the same lot and about 12 kilograms of hashish in a
Moscow garage he used. [@1] Another member of the group, also a Moldovan
citizen, was detained, and the drug police carried out all the arrests in a
single night. [@1]

The Pskov investigative department opened a criminal case over the Moscow
arrests for preparation of an especially large sale of narcotics under part 1 of
article 30 and part 5 of article 228.1 of the Criminal Code. [@1] Officers also
seized 315,000 roubles and 2,750 euros from the group. [@1] The Pskov operation
was run jointly with the second department of the drug control service's second
directorate in Moscow. [@1]
