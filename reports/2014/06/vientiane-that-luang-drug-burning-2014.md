A haul of seized drugs went up in smoke at the That Luang esplanade in Vientiane in June 2014 to mark the 27th anniversary of the International Day against Drug Abuse and Illicit Trafficking. [@1]

The contemporary account put the haul at more than 3.8 million amphetamine tablets, 238.5 kg of heroin, 47.8 kg of raw opium, 1,480 kg of dried cannabis, 83.5 kg of morphine and 22.3 kg of crystal methamphetamine (ya ice). [@1] A year later the state news agency described the same anniversary burning very differently: three tonnes of dried cannabis, 49.2 grams of ice, 334 grams of heroin, 32.2 kg of Ameflu and more than 5.4 million amphetamine tablets. [@2]

The 2014 account also said the authorities had closed 1,581 drug cases and arrested 2,140 offenders that year. [@1]
