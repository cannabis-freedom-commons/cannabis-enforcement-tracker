Moroccan police seized nearly 30 tonnes of hashish in Casablanca in two busts on 6 and 7 June 2014, one of the largest hauls in the country in years. [@1,2] A day earlier, officers had found 16.7 tonnes in a shipping container at the port. [@2] On 7 June they raided a warehouse in the port city and seized another 12 tonnes of the drug, known locally as chira. [@1,2] Police treated both finds as one consignment. [@2]

The container was apparently bound for Libya, and judicial police chief Abdelhak Khayyam said an investigation had opened into a large-scale operation sending drugs to an Arab country through Casablanca's port. [@2] Police arrested two men, including the suspected leader of the smuggling ring. [@2]
