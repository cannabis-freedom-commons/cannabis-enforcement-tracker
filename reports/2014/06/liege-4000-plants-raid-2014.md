On 10 June 2014, the Lille research section of the French gendarmerie and the
federal judicial police of Liège jointly raided a network of indoor cannabis
plantations in Belgium. [@1] Fourteen suspects were jailed over the network,
seven in France and seven in Belgium. [@2] The suspects were accused of taking
part in an indoor cannabis production and export network. [@1]

Investigators seized nearly 4,000 cannabis plants across eight plantations, all
of them located in Belgium. [@2] The indoor growing took place in the Liège
region, and the cannabis was destined for the market of Boulogne-sur-Mer and its
surroundings. [@2] The seizure was described as quite exceptional in its number
of plants, with a market value estimated at about 600,000 euros. [@2] During the
searches, Belgian investigators also seized weapons, drugs, a wave jammer,
20,000 euros in cash and several vehicles. [@1]

A judicial investigation had been opened in July 2013, and Belgian and French
investigators joined forces in March 2014 to track the traffickers. [@1]
According to the French gendarmes, the network's two main instigators lived in
Boulogne-sur-Mer and in Belgium. [@1] Twenty Minutes' Lille edition confirmed
the same eight-plantation, 14-jailing account, attributing the approximate
arrest split of seven in France and about fifteen in Belgium to the joint 10
June operation. [@3] The Belgian outlet La Libre Belgique also reported the
exceptional seizure and the split of seven jailed in France and seven in
Belgium. [@4]
