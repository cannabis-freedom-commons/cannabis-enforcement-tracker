Police and Narcotics and Affairs of Border (NAB) officers burned 4,110.84 kilograms of seized ganja at Pungdongbam Mamang Chingkhong in Imphal East district on 28 June 2014. [@1,2] The stock had been seized by NAB and Manipur Police from various places in the state between 1997 and 2013 and was set on fire in a bonfire at Kangla Haobi Lampak ground under Lamlai police station. [@1,2]

A disposal committee headed by Kh Chandramani, Deputy Inspector General (Range IV), supervised the burning after a court order permitted disposal of the long-held stock. [@1] NAB Superintendent S. Gautam, Additional SP Hussne Jamal and Lamlai station officers were present at the bonfire. [@1,2] Officers said the ganja was worth more than Rs 20 lakh in the local market and had been registered across 32 cases. [@1,2]

The same committee still held 19.88 kilograms of opium and 5.5 kilograms of heroin awaiting court permission for disposal, which fall outside this cannabis report. [@1,2] The opened reports describe no arrests, charges or trial outcomes connected to the 32 ganja cases. [@1,2]
