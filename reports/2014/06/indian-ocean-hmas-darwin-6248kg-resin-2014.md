The Australian frigate HMAS Darwin, operating in support of the British-led
Combined Task Force 150 (CTF-150), boarded a dhow in the Indian Ocean and
discovered 6,248 kilograms of cannabis resin hidden within the vessel's hull. [@1,2]
The find was made on 28 June 2014 after boarding teams searched the dhow for more
than 12 hours. [@2]

The task force called the haul the largest maritime seizure of hashish in the
Indian Ocean to date. [@1,2] It was the eighth drugs seizure of Darwin's
deployment to the Middle East. [@2] The resin carried an estimated
street value in Britain of about 18 million pounds. [@1]

## Why the navy cares

CTF-150's commander, Royal Navy Commodore Jeremy Blunden, congratulated the
ship's company for setting new standards in counter-narcotics work in the Indian
Ocean. [@2] Darwin's commanding officer, Commander Terry Morrison, said the ship
remained focused on the war against funding for terrorist organisations. [@2]
