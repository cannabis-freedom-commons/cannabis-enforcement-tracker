Police burned 105 kg of cannabis that had been seized in Buganda commune at
Karurama, about 2 km from the capital of Cibitoke province, around noon on
Friday 20 June. [@1]

A police source said the drugs came from the Democratic Republic of the Congo.
[@1] The same source described the Rusizi border as porous and said the movement
of goods and people was not controlled. [@1]

Residents of Buganda accused some local administrators and law-enforcement
officers of involvement in the drug trade. [@1] One elderly resident said some
police officers and grassroots administrators invested in the trade, which went
on openly. [@1] Cibitoke police commissioner OPP1 Eugène Bizindavyi warned that
coercive measures would be taken against any police officer conniving with drug
traffickers. [@1]

Residents nevertheless welcomed the police action and called for such operations
to be extended to other communes of the province to discourage the trade. [@1]
