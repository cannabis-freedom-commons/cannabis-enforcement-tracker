Two drugs busts in 48 hours netted Libyan police and customs officials around 15 tons of hashish at two ports. [@1,2] The first discovery came on 9 June aboard a Liberian-flagged container vessel docked in Benghazi, where police acting on a tip-off searched three refrigerated containers and found around five tons of cannabis. [@1,2]

In the second bust on 10 June at Khoms, the local CID with members of the public prosecutor's office swooped on the Maersk Regensburg, which had arrived from Senegal. [@1,2] Sniffer dogs detected containers holding around ten tons of hashish at the bottom of crates loaded with vegetables and fruit, including watermelons, allegedly consigned to a local food importer. [@1,2] Both consignments were to be destroyed in front of the public prosecutors. [@1,2]
