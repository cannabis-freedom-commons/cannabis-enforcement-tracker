Officers of the transport police directorate of Tajikistan's Interior Ministry
detained 32-year-old Miravali Davlatov, a resident of Hamadoni district in the
south of the country, on 4 June 2014 during drug-search operations. [@1] The
Interior Ministry said a personal search of the detainee found and seized 4 kg
of raw opium, which is not counted as cannabis here. [@1]

In further checks, officers searched an Opel Astra belonging to Ismoilkhon
Ibronov, another Hamadoni resident, and found more than 12 kg of hashish in its
boot. [@1]

A criminal case was opened on these facts and an investigation was under way.
