Mali's Mobile Intervention Brigade of Customs seized 172 packages of cannabis
weighing more than 300 kg at Kati, about fifteen kilometres from Bamako. [@1]
The operation took place during the night from Tuesday to Wednesday before 7
February 2014, as a load bound for Senegal moved along the Bamako-Dakar road
toward Dakar. [@2] Officers of the brigade had discreetly followed the vehicle
to the exit of Kati on the Bamako-Dakar road after it was loaded with cannabis
that night. [@1] The packages were carefully concealed under the chassis of a
Senegal-registered container platform trailer. [@1]

A public-transport van was to take over moving the load once it reached its
destination. [@2] A few kilometres farther on, a Mali-registered minibus
appeared to recover the cannabis packages. [@1] The drivers and their
accomplices, identified as Senegalese, used the night to slip into the brush,
abandoning the vehicles and their cargo. [@1] Officers took both vehicles and
their contents to the brigade's courtyard in Bamako. [@1]

Coulibaly said the seizure suggested Mali was becoming a transit hub for
narcotics to various destinations. [@1] The seizure came only days after the
seizure of 400 kg of cannabis from a shop in the commercial centre of Bamako's
central market. [@1]
