On 14 February 2014 members of the Public Order Police deployed at Sileza in
Emanguzi, northern KwaZulu-Natal, raided a homestead in an information-driven
operation. [@1] They found 163 bags of dagga stashed in one house on the
premises, weighing 1,723.67 kg with an estimated street value of R1.72
million, and three .458 Winchester Magnum rounds plus a firearm breech block
in another house. [@1]

The 41-year-old woman who owned the premises was arrested and detained at
Emanguzi police station on charges of dealing in drugs and illegal possession
of ammunition, with an appearance at the Emanguzi Magistrate's Court set for
17 February 2014. [@1] KwaZulu-Natal provincial commissioner Mmamonnye
Ngobeni commended the borderline officers for intercepting smugglers
operating across the region's borders. [@1]
