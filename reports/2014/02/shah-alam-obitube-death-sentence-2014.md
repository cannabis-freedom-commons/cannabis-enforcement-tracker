The Shah Alam High Court sentenced 28-year-old Nigerian student Prince Bartholomew Obitube to death on 26 February 2014 after finding him guilty of trafficking cannabis. [@1,2] Obitube, a student at a private college in Kuala Lumpur, received the mandatory death sentence under the Dangerous Drugs Act. [@1,2]

Police from the Gombak Narcotic Crime Investigation Department arrested Obitube at Desa Satu Apartment in Gombak on 20 August 2011 after a tip-off, following a trial of about three years. [@1,2] Officers said he tried to run away with a backpack containing the drugs when they moved in on the apartment area. [@1,2]
