Police arrested three people across Greater Jakarta in late February 2014 after finding more than
half a ton of marijuana in trucks travelling from Aceh to Java. [@1,2] Alleged couriers A.N. and
A.J. were arrested in Beji, Depok, with 84 kilograms; police said the pair had already moved more
than 100 kilograms to West Jakarta and to campuses including the University of Indonesia in Depok
and Pancasila University in South Jakarta. [@1] The pair typically carried at most six kilograms
at a time and were paid Rp 100,000 per kilogram. [@1]

After arresting the duo, police raided a warehouse in Cikupa, Tangerang, in the early hours of
Thursday morning, seizing 350 kilograms and arresting a third suspect, T.F. [@1] Authorities
said the marijuana was bound for Jakarta-area college students. [@2] Investigators would not
specify where in Aceh it had been grown. [@1]
