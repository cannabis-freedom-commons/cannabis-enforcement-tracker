Police seized nine small cannabis plants from an attic at a family home in
Belvaux, south of Luxembourg. [@1] The room was equipped with lighting,
air-conditioning and ventilation, according to police. [@1] In another instance,
a police house search in the Luxembourg City station district resulted in the
discovery of further plants in an attic as well as a bong, grinder, 14.9 grams
of marijuana and other drugs paraphernalia. [@1]

Police warned in a statement that in addition to it being illegal to grow
cannabis in Luxembourg, the construction of home-made growing stations such as
these also poses a significant fire hazard. [@1] Police seized the plants after
finding two growing stations in Luxembourg homes. [@1]
