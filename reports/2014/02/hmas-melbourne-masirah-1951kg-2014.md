The Royal Australian Navy frigate HMAS Melbourne and the Pakistani frigate PNS Alamgir, both deployed under tasking to the Combined Maritime Forces (CMF), jointly intercepted a suspect dhow east of Masirah Island, Oman. [@1,2] The Pakistani ship detected the dhow early on 12 February 2014, and the Australian boarding team embarked it the following day. [@1]

The boarding team found 62 bags holding almost 4,000 bricks of suspected cannabis resin, amounting to 1,951 kilograms, stacked in a hidden compartment within the vessel's fishing hold. [@1,2] The two navies seized and destroyed the haul, which they estimated had a street value of about A$113 million. [@1]

Australia's Commodore Daryl Bates, then commander of CMF's Combined Task Force 150, said the operation showed how well the task force's navies were working together against terrorism and related trafficking in the Middle East and Indian Ocean regions. [@1]
