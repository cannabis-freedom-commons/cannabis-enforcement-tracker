Soldiers from the 9th/12th Royal Lancers and 3rd Battalion The Mercian Regiment
supported an Afghan Uniform Police clearance operation in Yakchal, east of the
town of Gereshk. [@1] Insurgents often moved through the Yakchal area, which had
previously been a source of improvised explosive devices used against British,
American and Afghan forces. [@1] The operation met only a limited response from
insurgents, who were taken by surprise by its speed and efficiency. [@1]

British-mentored Afghan police officers seized 400 kilos of hashish during the
operation in Helmand province. [@1] After completing the operation, the Afghan
police found more than 400 kilograms of drugs which would have been used to fund
attacks on security forces across Helmand. [@1] British forces found
anti-personnel mines, ammunition and improvised explosive device components.
[@1] One British troop recovered over 300 rounds of 7.62-millimetre ammunition
and 10 mines. [@1]

The operation took place with Afghan security forces firmly in the lead while
British forces were drawing down ahead of the end of combat operations later
that year. [@1]
