Bikram Singh Soni, 32, was arrested on 7 February 2014 at his home in the 1100
block of Dover Way in Monterey Park. [@1] Officers arrested Soni on suspicion of
cultivating marijuana. [@1] Soni was held in lieu of $165,000 bail pending a
scheduled arraignment in Alhambra Superior Court. [@1]

Officers seized more than 300 marijuana plants in the raid. [@1] Police said the
plants amounted to about 55 pounds of marijuana. [@1] Soni’s two-story residence
had been completely converted into an indoor growing operation. [@1] Soni rented
the home and was its sole resident. [@1] The owners were believed to be unaware
of the growing operation. [@1]

Officers from the Monterey Park Police Department’s Special Response Team had
watched the home for about a month and arrested Soni at about 2:25 p.m. [@1] The
investigation began after anonymous tips about suspicious activity in the
neighborhood. [@1] Officers confirmed people coming and going only at night,
blinds always shut, and the odor of marijuana from the home. [@1] Officials
red-tagged the home because of damage from its conversion into an indoor growing
operation. [@1] Walls had been removed and the electrical system had been
jerry-rigged. [@1] Officials were also investigating Soni for stealing
electricity from Southern California Edison by bypassing the home’s meter. [@1]
