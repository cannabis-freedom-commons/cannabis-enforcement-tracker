Tajik police detained three men during an operation in Vose district in the
southern Khatlon Region on suspicion of illegally storing drugs. [@1] The three
were Sharifov Sheravgan, born 1965 and a resident of Vose district, Nazriyev
Rustam, born 1981 and a resident of Dushanbe, and Kurbonov Bakhtiyor, born 1973
and a resident of Farkhor district. [@1] A body search of a sports bag belonging
to Sharifov turned up 13 kg 764 grams of heroin, a strong narcotic. [@1] The
Interior Ministry of Tajikistan published the account on its official website,
as cited by the news agency Asia-Plus. [@1]

In a separate operation in Kurgan-Tyube, officers searched an Opel Astra
belonging to Saidkarim Khayotov, born 1959 and a resident of Kulob, and found 1
kg 377 grams of hashish, the cannabis component recorded here. [@1] The seized
drugs came to more than 15 kg in all, a combined total that mixes the heroin and
the hashish. [@1] Criminal cases were opened on these facts and an investigation
was under way, with no court outcome reported. [@1]
