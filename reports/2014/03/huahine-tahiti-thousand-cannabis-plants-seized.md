The growers from the different plantations were all summoned before the Papeete
criminal court. [@1] On Huahine, gendarmes found 865 plants spread across
several points of the island. [@1]

On Huahine they also found 200 grams of dried cannabis in a freezer. [@1] Other
seizures totalling more than a hundred plants were made at Toahotu on Tahiti's
peninsula and at Paea. [@1] Altogether, more than a thousand paka plants, the
local name for cannabis, were seized during that week. [@1]
