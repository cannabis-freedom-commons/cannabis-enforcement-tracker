Joint Task Force-Bravo and the US Embassy military liaison office in Belmopan joined Belizean military and police in a marijuana eradication operation across Belize from 15 to 20 March 2014. [@1,2,3] The Belizean government destroyed 34 marijuana fields holding about 57,283 mature plants, with an initial estimated value of $29 million. [@1,2,3] The teams also found and destroyed 25 pounds of marijuana seeds. [@2,3]

The Americans provided aviation support, on-call casualty evacuation and caving-ladder training over land and water for the 52 Belizean personnel who took part. [@1,2,3] US Army Captain Fernando Martinez, an Alpha Company commander with the 1-228th Aviation Regiment, described preparing landing zones for the helicopters that carried the ground teams. [@2]
