Police raided a house on Henley Road on 14 February 2014 and found about 1,500 cannabis plants growing across thirteen rooms. [@1,2] Officers spent the weekend removing plants and equipment from the property. [@2] Police said the drugs could have had a street value as high as £500,000. [@1,2]

Blerim Mema, 21, and Graci Fekolari, 29, both of Henley Road, were charged with production of cannabis and abstraction of electricity and remanded to appear at crown court on 25 February. [@2] They admitted producing cannabis at Ipswich Crown Court and were each jailed for 30 months on Friday 28 March 2014. [@1] The two denied electricity counts were allowed to lie on file. [@1]
