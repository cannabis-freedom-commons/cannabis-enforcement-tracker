Gendarmes serving with the Rikitea brigade discovered a plantation of paka
growing on the island of Mangareva. [@1] The owner of the cultivated parcel was
identified by the gendarmes and summoned to attend the gendarmerie brigade. [@1]
He was expected to answer charges of possession, use and resale of narcotic
products before the next sitting of the tribunal forain. [@1]

The field operation was carried out by two mobile gendarmes acting with the
support of a municipal police officer. [@1] Working as a team, the officers
uprooted and seized the cannabis plants they found growing in the district of
Rikitea. [@1] The seizure totalled 233 cannabis plants taken from the cultivated
plot. [@1]

Paka is the local name used for the plantation crop. [@1] The uprooted crop
consisted of cannabis plants, confirming the paka plantation was a cannabis
crop. [@1] The discovery, uprooting and seizure therefore concerned the same
plantation during a single intervention in Rikitea. [@1]
