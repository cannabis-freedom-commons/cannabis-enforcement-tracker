During a two-phase operation in Kanibadam, a 28-year-old local man was detained
by the Sughd Regional Administration of the Drug Control Agency of Tajikistan,
from whom one cellophane bag of hashish was seized. [@1] Preliminary
investigation established that the detained man was trying to sell hashish in
the city of Kanibadam. [@1]

A criminal case was opened under Article 200 part 1 of the Criminal Code of
Tajikistan (illegal trafficking of narcotic substances or psychotropic
substances with the intent to sell), and the investigation is ongoing. [@1] The
detained man was placed in the Administrative Detention Center of the Sughd
Regional Administration of the Drug Control Agency. [@1]
