Denver police officers, some in hazmat suits, raided multiple marijuana-growing operations on Tuesday as part of an investigation into illegal marijuana sales in Minnesota, assisted by federal agents. [@1] The officers hauled dozens of high-intensity grow lights and thousands of plants out of the operations, and a federal official said as much as $1 million was found in large bags during the searches. [@1] Investigators believed the people involved came to Colorado from Minnesota, where marijuana stayed illegal outside a restrictive medical program. [@1]

## Six locations

Local reporting counted marijuana plants, money and cars seized at six locations throughout Denver. [@2] The Denver Police Department tweeted only that the investigation involved illegal drug activity, while the DEA's Denver group supervisor James Gothe called it a large and successful investigation. [@2] The raids came eleven months after local and federal authorities had swept a dozen large marijuana businesses in Denver and Boulder. [@1]
