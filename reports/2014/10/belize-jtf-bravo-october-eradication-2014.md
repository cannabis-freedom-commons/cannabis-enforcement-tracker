Belizean soldiers and police, lifted by two US Army UH-60 Blackhawk helicopters and a CH-47 Chinook from Joint Task Force-Bravo in Honduras, swept marijuana fields across Belize from 29 September to 3 October 2014. [@1] The Belize Defence Force, Coast Guard, Anti-Drug Unit and Mobile Interdiction Team destroyed more than 52,800 marijuana plants, 10 pounds of seeds and 1,000 pounds (about 454 kg) of processed marijuana, which the report estimated at more than $27 million on the street. [@1]

The operation continued the partnership between the United States and Belize against criminal groups working in the country. [@1] An Amandala editorial quoting its 22 October report of the same drive confirms the two Blackhawks, the Chinook and the seed and processed-marijuana seizures, but puts the haul higher at more than 58,000 plants worth $54 million. [@2]
