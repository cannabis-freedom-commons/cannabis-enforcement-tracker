Argentina's Naval Prefecture, the coast guard, and the Buenos Aires provincial
police intercepted a Paraguayan-flagged vessel called the Papu Sur at kilometre
290 of the Paraná river, off Vuelta de Obligado in San Pedro partido. [@1]
Officers detained the captain and eight other crew members, all Paraguayan
nationals. [@1] A search of the containers found more than two tonnes of
marijuana, the national security ministry said. [@1] Security Secretary Sergio
Berni said the scan had confirmed 2,000 kg of marijuana so far, with more than
120 containers still to be checked. [@1]

Investigators said the boat had left Puerto Fénix near Asunción bound for Buenos
Aires, carrying a drug cargo. [@1] Federal judge Alberto Santamarina of Lomas de
Zamora ordered the operation after a multi-month investigation. [@1] Berni said
the provincial police had been investigating a transnational drug gang and
called the interception a new smuggling route into Argentina. [@1]
