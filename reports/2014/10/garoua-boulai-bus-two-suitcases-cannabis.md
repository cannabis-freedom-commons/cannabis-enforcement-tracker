Police in Garoua-Boulaï seized two suitcases of chanvre indien, the French term
for Indian hemp or cannabis, from the luggage hold of a bus on 12 October 2014.
[@1] The suitcases were in a 70-seat bus of the agency Touristique Voyage
travelling from Bertoua to Ngaoundéré. [@1] Officers found the parcels while
searching the baggage hold at the stop on the way out of Garoua-Boulaï. [@1]

Adamou Alioum, the divisional commissioner who heads the National Security
delegation for the East, said the load would first be weighed to establish its
exact weight and value. [@1] All the passengers were taken to the Garoua-Boulaï
public security police station, and none of them admitted owning the suitcases.
[@1] The commissioner said a careful search of one suitcase turned up a notice
convening the general assembly of the Association Bamessingué, signed by its
president Joseph Kaffo, which the judicial police (DRPJ) would use in the case.
