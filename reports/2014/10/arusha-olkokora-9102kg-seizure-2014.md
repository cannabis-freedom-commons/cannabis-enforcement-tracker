Tanzanian police said on 30 October 2014 that they had seized 9,102 kg of cannabis in an ongoing crackdown in the north of the country. [@1,2] Arusha Regional Police Commander Liberatus Sabas said the seizure followed an operation in Olkokora village on the slopes of Mount Meru, Tanzania's second-highest mountain. [@1,2] The operation brought together police officers, anti-narcotics officials and other security agencies. [@1,2]

Police said 267 bags of cannabis were seized on Tuesday and Wednesday, hidden in mountain gorges and in some people's homes. [@1,2] During the operation police set fire to a two-acre farm of bhang, about 0.81 hectares. [@1,2] Sabas said no one had been arrested as the investigation continued. [@1,2]

## An ongoing northern crackdown

The announcement presents the Olkokora operation as part of an ongoing crackdown in the northern part of the country. [@1,2] Sabas tied the seizure to police work around Arusha, on the slopes of the country's second-highest mountain. [@1,2] Because both opened pages relay the same police account, this report treats the weight, bag count and farm size as the commander's account rather than independently verified findings. [@1,2]
