Gardai from the Letterkenny drugs unit searched a premises on Navenny Street, Ballybofey on the evening of 21 January 2014 as part of an investigation into cannabis cultivation in Co. Donegal. [@1] They found cannabis plants plus other items made and adapted for growing cannabis, which Gardai valued at €360,000 pending analysis. [@1,2] A man in his thirties and three women were arrested at the scene and detained at Letterkenny Garda station under section 2 of the Criminal Justice (Drug Trafficking) Act 1996. [@1,2]

The three women were due before Letterkenny District Court on the morning of 23 January 2014 charged in connection with the seizure, while the man remained in custody where he could be held for up to seven days. [@2] Gardai later charged the man as well, and he was due before Letterkenny District Court at 10.30am on Friday 24 January 2014. [@3]
