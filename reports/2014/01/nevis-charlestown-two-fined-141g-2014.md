On Tuesday, 14 January, four cannabis cases were on the docket at Charlestown
Magistrate Court, but only two were heard and the rest were rescheduled for the
following week. [@1] Kemar Francis of Bath Village and Zalima Herbert of Mount
Lily were convicted of cannabis offences and ordered to pay fines in the first
trial of the day. [@1] The two cases were unrelated matters. [@1] Magistrate
Yasmine Clarke convicted Francis of possession of cannabis and fined him $2,000.
[@1] Herbert was convicted of possession of cannabis with intent to supply on
her guilty plea and was fined $1,500. [@1]

Police Prosecutor Stephen Hector said Constable Brown was on patrol on 7 January
when he saw Francis near Mem’s Pizzeria and engaged him in a stop and search on
suspicion of drugs. [@1] Francis tried to evade Brown and threw something he
claimed was dust into the nearby bushes. [@1] Police searched the area but
initially found nothing, then took Francis to Charlestown Police Station and
cautioned him. [@1] Hector said officers returned to the restaurant the next
morning and found a black plastic bag containing 104 grams of cannabis in the
bushes. [@1] Herbert, 28, was at her boyfriend’s house on 6 December when a
search warrant was executed at 7:25 a.m. and she was the only occupant. [@1] The
search yielded 37.3 grams of cannabis stored in three drink mix containers. [@1]
One container held 11 dime bags and seeds were also found in the seized amount.
[@1] In about a month police in Nevis had seized at least 140 grams, or nearly
five ounces, of cannabis. [@1]

Herbert’s boyfriend Dwayne Ferlance pleaded not guilty and the prosecutor
withdrew the charges against him, leaving Herbert to face the charges alone.
[@2] Herbert was ordered to pay the $1,500 fine within three months or serve six
months in prison in default. [@2]
