Customs and Border Protection officers at the Mariposa commercial facility seized nearly 7,400 pounds of marijuana on Wednesday 22 January 2014 from a tractor-trailer whose tomato load hid 315 bales of the drug. [@1,2] A narcotics detection canine had alerted officers to the Freightliner rig, driven by Cuauhtemoc Lopez-Cabrera, 30, of Nogales, Sonora, Mexico, and the agency valued the load at an estimated $3,699,000. [@1,2]

Lopez-Cabrera was arrested and turned over to U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement's Homeland Security Investigations, and the drugs and the tractor-trailer were seized. [@1,2] "CBP officers in Nogales made use of several layers of enforcement to identify and locate the marijuana," said Nogales Port Director Guadalupe Ramirez. "Experience and dedication of our personnel work as a containment net to prevent these drugs from entering our nation." [@1]
