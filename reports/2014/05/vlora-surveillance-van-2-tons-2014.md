Vlora police in southern Albania seized two tons of marijuana early on Thursday 15 May 2014 after stopping a van they had under surveillance at 5 a.m. [@1] Officers found the drugs inside and arrested the driver, Albanian media reported. [@1]

Police said they believed the drugs had come from Lazarat and were to be shipped to Italy. [@1] The van stop was the largest of several cross-border seizures described that week between Albania and Greece. [@1]
