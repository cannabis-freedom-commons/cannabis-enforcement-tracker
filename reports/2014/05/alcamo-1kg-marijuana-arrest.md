Alcamo police arrested 21-year-old local Pietro Giacalone in flagrante for
unlawful possession of a narcotic substance on a Wednesday evening. [@1] The
stop came during a specially prepared operation by the Investigative Section.
[@1] Officers had watched the young man as he suspiciously carried a bulky sack
along Via Vincenzo Spica in central Alcamo. [@1]

A personal search found him carrying about one kilogram of marijuana. [@1] An
extended search of his home found small scales and material suitable for
packaging drug doses. [@1] After validation of the arrest, the investigating
judge placed Giacalone under house arrest. [@1]

The Alcamo commissariat presented the case as part of its tightened fight
against drug trafficking in Alcamo under Operation Pre-Crime guidelines devised
by Trapani police chief Carmine Esposito. [@1] Giacalone was already known to
the commissariat for prior cases of possession for personal use. [@1] He was
also accused over the 4 March 2011 robbery of the LIDL supermarket on state road
SS113. [@1]
