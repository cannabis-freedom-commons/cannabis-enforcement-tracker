The Rwanda National Police recovered over 80 kg of cannabis from a house in Nyakabanda cell of Niboye sector, Kicukiro, and took two brothers, D'Amour Iyakaremye and Kimaki Mbagariye, into custody after the seizure on Thursday 15 May 2014. [@1] Iyakaremye had reportedly been imprisoned about five times before in connection with drugs, and his wife was serving a sentence at Kigali's central prison after conviction for trafficking cannabis. [@1]

The seizure was announced alongside a police call for the public to help fight drug trafficking, which the force linked to porous borders and other crimes. [@1] Four other people, including three motorcycle operators, were caught separately transporting illicit gin, and their motorcycles were confiscated. [@1]
