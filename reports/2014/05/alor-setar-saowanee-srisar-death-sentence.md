On 12 May 2014 the Alor Setar High Court sentenced Thai national Saowanee Srisar, 33, to death
for trafficking 23,123 grams of cannabis. [@1,2] Srisar, a divorcee formerly on the housekeeping
staff of a Bangkok hotel, was caught at the Shahab Perdana express bus terminal at about
8.45pm on 21 January 2012. [@1,2] The cannabis was kept in two bags, one black and one
blue. [@1]

Judicial Commissioner Datuk Mohd Zaki Abdul Wahab held that the prosecution had proved
possession and that the accused had failed to raise doubt against its case. [@1,2] A conviction
for trafficking under section 39B of the Dangerous Drugs Act 1952 carries a mandatory death
sentence. [@1,2] The Court of Appeal record confirms the charge: 23,123 grams of cannabis at
Shahab Perdana on 21 January 2012. [@3] After sentencing she stayed calm, smiled and hugged court
staff and police personnel before being led away. [@1]
