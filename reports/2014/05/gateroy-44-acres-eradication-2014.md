Police ranks conducted a drug eradication operation at Gateroy on the Berbice River and found 44 acres of land under cannabis cultivation, about 17.8 hectares. [@1] The fields held approximately 145,000 cannabis sativa plants, ranging in height from one foot to fourteen feet. [@1]

Beyond the standing crop, the officers came upon sixteen camps across the site and about 8,000 kilograms of dried marijuana already gathered. [@1] Everything the operation uncovered, the plants in the fields, the camps and the dried stock, was found and destroyed. [@1]

## No arrests

No one was arrested during the operation, and the account names no suspects or cultivators behind the fields and camps. [@1] The report itself is dated 4 May 2014 and gives no separate date for the raid. [@1]
