HSI and CBP Border Patrol officers, assisted by the Willacy County Sheriff's
Office, seized more than 9,000 pounds of marijuana from a grow field near
Raymondville on 7 August 2014. [@2] The agencies discovered two acres of
marijuana plants hidden in a grow field about 10 minutes from Raymondville. [@2]
Raymondville lies about 50 miles north of the Mexican border. [@1]

After two days of hand-picking the plants, agents reported a total seizure of
11,500 plants. [@1] ICE estimated the local street value of the seized marijuana
at $10 million. [@2] HSI San Antonio Special Agent in Charge Janice Ayala called
it the largest grow field of marijuana seized in the area. [@2]

Willacy County Sheriff Larry Spence described the operation's primitive
sophistication, saying it reminded him of Vietnam. [@1] ICE described the case
as an ongoing investigation, with no arrests announced in either account. [@2]
