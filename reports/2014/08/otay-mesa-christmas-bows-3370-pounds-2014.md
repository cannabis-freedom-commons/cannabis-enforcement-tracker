On 28 August 2014, at about 3.30 pm, a 28-year-old Mexican citizen drove a 1998 Kenworth tractor-trailer into the Otay Mesa Cargo Facility with cargo manifested as bows, and an officer referred him for a closer examination. [@1] Officers ran the rig through the port's imaging system, saw anomalies in the cargo, probed one of the boxes and pulled out a green-leafy substance that field-tested positive for marijuana. [@1,2] They then extracted 209 large packages holding 3,370 pounds (about 1,529 kg) of marijuana from inside the boxes, valued at $1.5 million. [@1,2]

The driver was booked into the Metropolitan Correctional Center to await arraignment. [@1,2] Customs officers seized the marijuana together with the tractor and trailer. [@1,2]
