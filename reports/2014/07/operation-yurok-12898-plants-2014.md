From 21 to 24 July 2014, the Humboldt County Sheriff's Office joined the Bureau of Indian Affairs, the California Department of Justice, California Fish and Wildlife, the Marin County Sheriff's Office, the Humboldt County District Attorney's Office, the Yurok Police Department and the Eureka Police Department to eradicate marijuana grows on Yurok Tribal Land and adjacent properties. [@1,2] Thirty-five officers served twenty Humboldt County Superior Court search warrants and nine Tribal Court search warrants over the four days. [@1,2]

Officers seized 12,898 marijuana plants, over 300 pounds (about 136 kilos) of processed marijuana and seven firearms. [@1,2] They reported extensive environmental damage at several grow sites along with California Fish and Wildlife violations. [@1,2]

## No arrests

No arrests were made during the four-day operation, though the sheriff's office said several suspects had been identified and arrest warrants would be requested. [@1,2]
