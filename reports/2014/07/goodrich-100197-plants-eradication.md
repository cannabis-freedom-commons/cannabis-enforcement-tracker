A deer hunter maintaining a deer lease off Old Highway 35 near Goodrich, about 70 miles north of Houston, stumbled onto a sophisticated outdoor grow on Saturday 26 July 2014 and called police. [@1,2] Deputies arriving that day found evidence the growers had just left elaborate campsites with food, clothing, equipment, generators and an irrigation system pumping water from a nearby creek through hoses into the fields. [@1,2]

## Clearance

On Monday 28 July, more than 70 officers from up to 12 agencies, guided by helicopters and growers' trails through the undergrowth, worked with Polk County Jail inmates to pull up the plants across 13 fields found at first, later put at 100,197 plants among 29 fields. [@1,2] Chief Deputy Byron Lyons of the Polk County Sheriff's Office said deputies suspected the growers were from outside the area and were fingerprinting the campsites to identify them. [@1,2] The Chronicle put the crop's estimated value at about $175 million, and KTRK reported one person taken into custody. [@1]
