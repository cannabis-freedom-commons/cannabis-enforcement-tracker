Police in Forth Valley seized cannabis worth an estimated £235,000 in a raid on premises on Main Street in Shieldhill, acting on information supplied by a member of the public. [@1] The raid uncovered a large-scale cannabis cultivation facility. [@1]

A 48-year-old man was arrested during the operation. [@1] Police said the investigation was ongoing and that further arrests could follow. [@1]
