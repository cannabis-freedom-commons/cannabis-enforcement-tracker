The United States Coast Guard intercepted a vessel about 200 nautical miles
southwest of Cabo Rojo in the Caribbean Sea and arrested five suspected
traffickers. [@1] The Coast Guard cutter Northland reported the suspicious
68-foot vessel An-Nur and found the marijuana on board. [@2] The five men
arrested were Michael Dacosta, Shawn A. Ally, Nolan E. Connelly, Ron D. Griffith
and Owen K. Brakes. [@1] All five are citizens of Guyana and were placed in the
Metropolitan Detention Center in Guaynabo. [@1] They were handed to federal
authorities in Ponce on 8 July and charged with drug trafficking on 17 July.
[@1] Magistrate Sylvia Carreño Col authorized the complaint charging them with
conspiracy to possess a controlled substance on board a vessel subject to United
States jurisdiction. [@1] Those convicted face sentences of 10 years to life in
prison. [@1]

The boarding team found 3,591 pounds (about 1,630 kilograms) of marijuana hidden
on the cargo vessel. [@3] Officials estimated the wholesale value at 3.5 million
dollars. [@2] The Northland handed the drugs and the detained men to Customs and
Border Protection and Federal Bureau of Investigation agents. [@2]

Federal prosecutor Jennifer Hernández called it the largest drug load seized by
authorities in the previous 10 months. [@1] Since October 2013 the Coast Guard
had seized 17,591 kilograms of cocaine and 4,341 pounds of marijuana in 25
interdictions under Operation Unified Resolve, worth more than 444 million
dollars. [@2] The 270-foot cutter Northland, homeported in Virginia, had been
assigned to Caribbean waters a week earlier when it made what was then the
year's largest marijuana seizure. [@1]
