High Court judge Datin Amelia Tee Hong Geok Abdullah sentenced 37-year-old Nigerian college student Uchechukwu Nelson Ohaechesi to be hanged on 21 July 2014 after finding him guilty of trafficking 26.5 kilos of cannabis four years earlier. [@1,2] Ohaechesi committed the offence beside an overhead bridge staircase on the Kajang-bound Taman Connaught highway in Cheras, Kuala Lumpur, at about 7.45pm on 17 October 2010. [@1]

The judge ruled that Ohaechesi had failed to raise reasonable doubt against the prosecution's case and that his defence amounted to an afterthought, pure fabrication and untrue. [@1] Under section 39B of Malaysia's Dangerous Drugs Act 1952, trafficking in 200 grams or more of cannabis carries a mandatory death sentence. [@2]
