Gardai from the Roscommon/Longford drug unit, assisted by Granard gardai, searched a house at Dalystown, Granard, on 14 July 2014 as part of an investigation into the sale and supply of drugs in the Longford area. [@1,2] They found 118 cannabis plants with an estimated street value of 94,000 euro and a kilogram of cannabis herb valued at 20,000 euro, both subject to analysis. [@1] Four men aged 28, 29, 33 and 33 were arrested and detained under section 4 of the Criminal Justice Act 1984 at Roscommon, Longford and Granard stations. [@1,2] All four were charged and due before Longford District Court on the morning of 15 July. [@1]
