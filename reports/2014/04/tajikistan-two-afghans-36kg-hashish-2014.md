Officers of Tajikistan's Interior Ministry drug-trafficking department detained
two Afghan nationals in Tajikistan during a special operation. [@1] The two were
identified as Kunduz residents, 39-year-old Saidikrom valadi Saidislom and
32-year-old Khairmahmad Muhammad Muso. [@1] Two other men, 40-year-old Dushanbe
resident Dilshod Lakaev and 36-year-old Farkhor native Jamshed Yusufov, were
also arrested for alleged drug trafficking. [@1]

More than 36 kg of hashish was confiscated from the two Afghan detainees. [@1]
Asia-Plus said about 8 kg of opium was seized from the other two men. [@1]
Avesta described the same seizure from Lakaev and Yusufov as about 8 kg of
hashish. [@2]

Criminal cases were opened into the seizures and an investigation was under way.
[@2]
