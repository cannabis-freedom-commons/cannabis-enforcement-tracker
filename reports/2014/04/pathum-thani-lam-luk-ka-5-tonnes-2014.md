Pathum Thani police raided a warehouse in Lam Luk Ka District on 30 April 2014 and found approximately five tonnes of marijuana packed for shipment to the Netherlands. [@1,2] The cannabis was neatly wrapped in moisture-control bags, and some of it was packed in boxes labelled "towel". [@2] NNT reported the haul weighed 5,000 kilograms and was worth up to 500 million baht. [@1] Officers confiscated the drugs together with the packaging equipment and recovered fingerprints they said could lead to the perpetrators. [@1]

Police identified the warehouse owner as Sirisak Boonsiri, 47. [@1,2] NNT reported he was present when officers arrived, and that his 34-year-old wife, who was also there, denied knowing the packages held cannabis and said her husband had been in the export business for more than two years. [@1] Coconuts, citing police, said instead that Sirisak was not on the premises when the raid took place. [@2] A World Federation Against Drugs summary of the case confirms the five-tonne seizure in Lam Luk Ka and its Netherlands destination. [@3]
