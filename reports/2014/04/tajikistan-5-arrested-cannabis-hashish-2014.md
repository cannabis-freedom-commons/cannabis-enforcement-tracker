Tajikistan's Ministry of Internal Affairs reported that in the past 24 hours,
more than 1 kg of narcotics was seized from illegal circulation across three
locations. [@1] In Tursunzade, police detained three suspects during an
anti-narcotics operation; a personal search of one of them yielded 856 g of
cannabis. [@1]

In Dushanbe's Sino-2 district, a resident of the capital was detained with 182 g
of hashish. [@1] In Ganchisho district, police detained a 30-year-old man from
B. Gafurov district with 136 g of hashish. [@1]

Criminal cases were opened on all the above facts and an investigation is
underway. [@1]
