As part of an ongoing operation into the illegal sale and supply of controlled
drugs in the Dublin Metropolitan Region, Gardai from Crumlin seized cannabis
herb with an estimated value of 100,000 euro, pending analysis, in a planned
search of a premises in Coolmine, Dublin 15. [@1]

A 24-year-old man and a 23-year-old woman were arrested and detained at
Sundrive Road Garda Station under Section 4 of the Criminal Justice Act 1984.
[@1] Both were later released from custody without charge, and files were
being prepared for the Director of Public Prosecutions. [@1]
