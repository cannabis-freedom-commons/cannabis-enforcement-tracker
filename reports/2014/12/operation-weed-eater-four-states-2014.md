Nigeria's National Drug Law Enforcement Agency ran a twelve-day sting,
Operation Weed Eater, across Ekiti, Edo, Delta and Kogi states with one
hundred officers drawn from several state commands. [@1,2] Officers destroyed
cannabis plantations at Ise Forest Reserve in Ise/Orun and at Ode Camp in
Gboyin, both in Ekiti State, and at Utese Forest Reserve in Edo State.
[@1,2]

Further interceptions included a forty-foot container loaded with 19,675 kg
found on a truck along Amai Road in Obiaruku, Delta State, and two trucks
carrying 2,103 kg seized in Lokoja, Kogi State. [@1] In Ekiti alone the
agency said 1.5 million kg of cannabis was destroyed on two plantations.
[@1] The NDLEA put the total haul at 1,514,278 kg with a street value of
N15.1 billion. [@1,2]

Officers arrested Uche Obi, described as the operational head of the Akunnubas
cannabis syndicate and known as "King Maker", along with seven other members
of the syndicate and two truck drivers. [@1,2] The agency said the cartel
controlled much of the cannabis business in the South-West and South-South,
hired cheap labour including school-age children for its farms, and moved the
processed drug by motorcycle to covert warehouses and then by truck for
interstate distribution. [@1]
