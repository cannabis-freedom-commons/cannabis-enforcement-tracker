Gardaí from Clare and Limerick searched a house at Querrin, Kilkee, County
Clare, on the afternoon of Friday 19 December 2014 as part of an investigation
into drug dealing in both counties. [@1] They found 34 kg of cannabis herb,
which gardaí valued at €680,000 pending laboratory analysis. [@1,2] A
24-year-old man was arrested at the scene and detained at Kilrush Garda station
under drug-trafficking legislation. [@1,2]

Gardaí said the man would appear before Limerick District Court at 2pm charged
over the seizure. [@2] No other arrests were reported. [@1,2]
