The High Court in Alor Setar sentenced 34-year-old Nigerian Oluigbo Eric Chimeze to death on Monday 27 February 2012 after finding him guilty of drug trafficking two years earlier. [@1,2] Judicial Commissioner Mohd Zaki Abdul Wahab ruled the prosecution had proved its case beyond reasonable doubt. [@1,2]

Oluigbo had distributed 22.159 kilogrammes of cannabis at a traffic light in Napoh, near Alor Setar, while heading toward Bukit Kayu Hitam at about 6.45 a.m. on 1 September 2010. [@1,2] He was charged under Section 39B of the Dangerous Drugs Act 1952, which carries a mandatory death sentence on conviction. [@1,2] Counsel B. Murthy represented the accused while deputy public prosecutor Noor Fadzila Ishak appeared for the prosecution. [@2] Death sentences in Malaysia are carried out by hanging. [@2]
