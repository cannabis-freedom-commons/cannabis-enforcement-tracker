As part of an operation targeting cannabis cultivation around Mullingar,
gardaí carried out a planned search of an industrial unit on the outskirts of
the town at about 2.30pm on 4 February 2012. [@1] Inside the structure they
uncovered a grow house holding cannabis plants with an estimated street value of
up to €500,000 pending analysis. [@1]

## Two charged

Two men aged 37 and 42 were arrested at the scene and detained at Mullingar
Garda station under section 4 of the Criminal Justice Act 1984. [@1] Both were
charged in connection with the investigation and were due before a special
sitting of Mullingar District Court on the morning of 6 February 2012. [@2]
