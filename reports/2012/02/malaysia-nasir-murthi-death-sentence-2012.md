A High Court sentenced two friends, 55-year-old Muhamad Nasir Hussain and 48-year-old contractor R. Murthi, to death for trafficking 20,698.4 grams of cannabis in 2007. [@1] The pair were alleged to have trafficked the cannabis at Jalan Besar between 3.45 pm and 4.15 pm on 20 March 2007. [@1]

Justice Datuk Zamani Abd Rahim found the defence of both accused to be merely a denial. [@1] Deputy Public Prosecutor Tan Guat Cheng prosecuted the case, while Nasir was represented by V. M. Ravichandran and Murthi by R. S. N. Rayer. [@1]
