UK Border Agency officers stopped 45-year-old Portuguese driver Luis Antonio Lopes De Faria at the
inward freight controls of Dover Eastern Docks on 21 February 2011 and searched the
Dutch-registered lorry he was driving. [@1,2] Inside a load of mixed goods, six pallets held boxes
containing 1.75 tonnes of cannabis with an estimated street value of over £5 million. [@1]

He was arrested and charged with attempted importation, pleaded not guilty at Folkestone
Magistrates' Court on 25 February 2011, then changed his plea to guilty at Canterbury Crown Court
on 10 January. [@1] The court sentenced him to eight years in prison on 21 February 2012. [@1]
Assistant Director Malcolm Bragg said the case showed the strength of port controls and that the
sentence should warn off other smugglers. [@1]
