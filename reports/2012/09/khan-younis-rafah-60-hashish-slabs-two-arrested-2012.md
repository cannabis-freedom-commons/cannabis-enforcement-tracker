The General Department for Combating Drugs in the Palestinian Police said it
seized 60 farsh of hashish in Rafah governorate, in the southern Gaza Strip.
[@1] The Khan Younis anti-narcotics department said it arrested two men,
identified only as R.R. and R.F., with 60 farsh of hashish in their possession
as they tried to smuggle it into the Gaza Strip. [@1] Officers seized the
quantity of hashish and held the two men, and the case was referred to a police
investigation inspector to complete the legal procedures against them. [@1]

Farsh, plural furush, is the unit Gaza's police use for slabs of hashish. [@1]
The same account, with the same figures and the same police director, was
published by the Safa news agency on 9 September 2012. [@2]

Major Ahmed al-Qadra, head of the General Department for Combating Drugs, said
his department would spare no effort and work day and night against the drug
problem. [@1] Al-Qadra said the force would strike with an iron hand at anyone
trading or distributing drugs in the Gaza Strip. [@1]
