Federal prosecutors indicted 27 people on 19 September 2012 over a hydroponic grow-house ring in the Houston area, after officers seized about 13,900 marijuana plants a month earlier. [@1] On 21 August officers executed warrants on 60 properties across Harris, Fort Bend and Montgomery counties; 40 held active grow houses with 100 to 850 plants each, and 24 suspects were arrested. [@1,2] Officers also seized growing equipment from the houses and from a suspected hydroponic storefront and warehouse. [@1,2]

## Charges

The defendants were charged with conspiring to possess with intent to distribute a controlled substance involving more than 1,000 marijuana plants, plus conspiracy to maintain a place for making drugs. [@2] A conviction on the drug conspiracy charge carried at least ten years and up to life in federal prison. [@2] Investigators said 14 more grow houses had already been searched earlier in the same inquiry, bringing its total to about 19,000 plants. [@2]
