Tajik police arrested a 30-year-old Afghan citizen in Dushanbe on 8 September
2012 on suspicion of drug trafficking. [@1] Officers confiscated 70 kilograms of
hashish from the Afghan man. [@1]

Police also arrested a 35-year-old Dushanbe resident, Umedjon Qayumov, on
suspicion of involvement in the same crime. [@1] The Interior Ministry press
service, which reported the operation, gave no further details of the crime.
