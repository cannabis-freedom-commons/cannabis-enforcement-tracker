Mozambican police detained three men in Angoche district and Nampula city for
hashish trafficking. [@1] Two of the three detained men are father and son. [@1]
One of the three is a repeat offender who had previously been caught with 28 kg
of drugs and returned to society after winning his court case. [@1] The three
detainees each gave their own version of involvement and all appeared repentant.
[@1]

Police seized about 187 kilograms of hashish from the three men. [@1] Officers
first seized 7 kg in Nampula city when a 23-year-old was caught selling in the
Muhala-belenenses neighbourhood, then traced 180 kg hidden by two other members
of the same group in Angoche, recovering it and detaining its owners. [@1] At
seizure the 187 kg was disguised in small 1 kg packages whose owners claimed
were animal vitamins, which examination showed was hashish. [@1]

Nampula police spokesman Inácio João Dina said the origin of the drug was still
unknown. [@1] The detainees indicated the hashish reaches Angoche by a boat
anchored miles offshore and is then moved to Angoche city by contracted boats.
[@1] Dina described it as a large drug-trafficking circuit of which the seized
drugs are only a small part, with the onward destination after Angoche still
unknown. [@1] Police said the seizure resulted from operational work intensified
in recent days mainly in Nampula city amid rising crime. [@1] Police said
investigative work was continuing. [@1]
