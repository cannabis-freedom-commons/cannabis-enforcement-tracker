Denpasar District Court sentenced British national Paul Beales, 40, to four years in prison for possessing hashish. [@1,2,3] The sentence was reported on 20 December 2012, days after it was handed down earlier that week. [@1,2] Beales had been charged with possession of drugs, which carries a twelve-year sentence under Indonesia's strict narcotics law. [@1]

The AFP account put the amount at 3.6 grams of hashish found at his house and said the court cleared him of the more serious charge of drug trafficking. [@3] Beales was arrested at the end of May 2012 in a police sting mounted after fellow Briton Lindsay Sandiford was caught with cocaine at Bali's airport. [@2,3]

In the same proceedings, Rachel Dougall was sentenced to twelve months in prison for failing to report a crime, with the court noting she was a mother and had cooperated with investigators. [@1,2,3]
