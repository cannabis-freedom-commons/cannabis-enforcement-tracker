Customs officers seized 30 grams of marijuana at the Integrated Control Area of
the General Artigas international bridge in Paysandú. [@1] Officers carried out
the check on the morning of Friday 28 December jointly with sanitary-barrier
staff, stopping an Argentine-registered car carrying a Uruguayan man, an
Argentine man and the first man's son. [@1] The drug and the vehicle were
seized, while the two men and the adolescent were placed at the disposal of
Paysandú's second-turn judge, Karen Ramos. [@1] The judge ordered that the
Narcotics Brigade be informed of the situation. [@1]

The driver was also carrying medicinal herbs, seasonings, seeds and unpackaged
rice, alongside a herb that looked and smelled like Cannabis sativa and was
confirmed as cannabis by testing. [@1] The driver's 15-year-old son, who
admitted being a consumer, was carrying a grinder containing one gram of dried,
chopped cannabis ready for consumption, plus cigarette rolling papers. [@1]

The driver, a 38-year-old Uruguayan living in Córdoba, said he was travelling to
Treinta y Tres to visit his mother and other relatives. [@1] He said the
marijuana was for his personal consumption and also to prepare infusions to
treat an illness affecting his mother. [@1]
