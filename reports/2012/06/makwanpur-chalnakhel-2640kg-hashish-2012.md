Nepal police seized 2,640 kilograms of crude hashish at Chalnakhel in Setidevi, Makwanpur district, in what the force called the biggest narcotic consignment ever caught in the country. [@1] Officers found the drug packed in 88 plastic bags and said it was worth millions in the local market, with a far higher value internationally. [@1]

Five men were arrested during the operation: Hasta Bahadur Rumba, Tirtha Bahadur Rai, Bire Lama, Chandra Bahadur Gole and Kumar Lama. [@1] Police described them as links in a network that collected raw material from farmers in Makwanpur and delivered it to a refinery in Chalnakhel, from where the hashish was planned to be exported to India and China. [@1]

Months earlier a television station had broadcast a report on illegal hashish farming and processing in Makwanpur, including a closed school whose periphery was used for processing, and police had been monitoring the area since amid alleged negligence by local personnel. [@1]
