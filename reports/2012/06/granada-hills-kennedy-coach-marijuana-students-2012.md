Los Angeles police arrested Pedro Cruz Trujillo, a 24-year-old assistant
baseball coach at John F. Kennedy High School in Granada Hills, on 25 June 2012,
police said. [@2] The arrest followed an investigation that began after a staff
member at the school overheard students talking about the incident and told
police. [@1]

Police suspect he drove six students aged 14 to 16 to a Dodgers game on 18 May
2012 and that he and some of the students smoked marijuana in his car on the way
there and back. [@1]

CBS News reported that Trujillo was held on $100,000 bail. [@1] The Los Angeles
Daily News reported that each count carried $100,000 bail, a total of $600,000,
and that Trujillo was still in custody on Tuesday. [@3] A consent search of his
SUV found pipes, bongs and marijuana, according to the LAPD's Devonshire
division commander. [@3]

The Los Angeles Unified School District removed Trujillo from the Kennedy campus
in May when the case was reported, and he was fired after his arrest. [@3]
Trujillo had worked at the Granada Hills school for about two years and had no
previous arrest record or complaints against him, police said. [@3] Police said
they were working with the school to establish whether there had been other
similar incidents. [@2]
