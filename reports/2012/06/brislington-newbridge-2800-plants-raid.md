Avon and Somerset police discovered a cannabis factory with 2,800 plants at a unit on the Newbridge Trading Estate in Brislington, Bristol. [@1] The force said it was the largest farm it had found, and the BBC reported the crop was worth £2.5 million. [@1]

The unit also held thousands of pounds worth of growing equipment and living areas for the people paid to grow the drugs. [@1] Force drugs strategy manager Paul Bunt called it a very sophisticated and highly organised production site whose seizure would directly affect cannabis supply in Bristol. [@1] He added that the trade was illegal and police took it very seriously, even though some saw cannabis as a lower-level drug. [@1]

The find was part of Operation Viscount, the force's campaign against cannabis cultivation in the area, and all the plants were to be destroyed before reaching buyers. [@1] Police said investigations were continuing. [@1]
