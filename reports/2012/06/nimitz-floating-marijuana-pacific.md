On 16 June 2012 the crew of the aircraft carrier USS Nimitz, working with the cruiser USS Princeton, two helicopter squadrons and the Mexican navy, retrieved 186 bales of marijuana floating in the Pacific, apparently destined for the United States. [@1,2] Nimitz lookouts had spotted two small boats about four nautical miles away; when a Seahawk helicopter closed in, the boats began dumping dark bags overboard and fled east at about 25 knots. [@1] Princeton sent rigid-hull inflatable boats to recover the bags, about 100 pounds each, and a Mexican naval ship helped recover the rest. [@1,2] The bales were transferred to the Coast Guard cutter Edisto for transport ashore. [@1,2]

## Scale

At about 100 pounds a bale, the 186 bales weighed about 18,600 pounds (about 8.4 tonnes). [@1] The Nimitz strike group had been conducting carrier qualifications off the California coast when the lookouts made the sighting. [@1] No arrests were reported; the dumping crews fled. [@1]
