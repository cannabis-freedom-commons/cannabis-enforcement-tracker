The Royal Canadian Mounted Police announced on 18 April 2012 the results of Project Celsius, a joint investigation that seized more than 43 tonnes of hashish worth an estimated $860 million. [@1,2] The hashish came mostly from Pakistan and was intercepted in Pakistan, Belgium, Italy and Canada after travelling in marine containers toward Montreal and Halifax, Nova Scotia. [@1,2]

The investigation began in 2009 and 2010, when officers found drugs hidden in containers at the ports of Halifax and Montreal. [@1] The RCMP-led inquiry then ran for about two years with the collaboration of law enforcement authorities in Pakistan, Italy, Belgium and the United States. [@1,2] Officers raided the Port of Montreal on 18 April 2012 and arrested nine suspects over a series of hashish shipments totalling 43.3 metric tonnes. [@2]

Police said the seizure equalled more than half of all the hashish sent to Canada, or confiscated there, in all of 2009. [@1] Officers of the RCMP and the Canada Border Services Agency later received Quebec Police Awards for the operation. [@2]
