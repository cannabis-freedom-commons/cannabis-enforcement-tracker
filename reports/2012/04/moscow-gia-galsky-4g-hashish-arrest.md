Moscow police detained Abkhazia-born criminal authority Georgy Zarandia, known
as Gia Galsky, who allegedly controlled apartment-theft and bag-snatching groups
in the capital. [@1] The Central Federal District police headquarters identified
him as a previously convicted leader of the criminal milieu and a so-called
thief in law. [@1] Police said Zarandia was born in 1979 in Nabakevi village in
Abkhazia's Galsky district and was living in Moscow without a fixed residence.
[@1] The police account was conveyed to Interfax by the press service of the
Main Directorate of the Interior Ministry for the Central Federal District. [@1]

During the detention he presented officers with a forged passport in the name of
Yury Krasnov. [@1] A personal search during the detention seized 4 grams of
hashish from him, police said. [@1] Police said Zarandia had held thief-in-law
status for only a short time after allegedly being crowned in summer 2011. [@1]

According to operative information, his crowning took place in Kaluga Oblast
with the participation of Gela and Gizo Kordava, Vladimir Zhurakovsky known as
Vova Pukhly, Merab Mzarelua and Roland Gegechkori. [@1] Police said he
controlled criminal groups engaged in apartment burglaries and bag-snatching in
Moscow. [@1]
