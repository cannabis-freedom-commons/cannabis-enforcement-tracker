At the Basseterre Magistrate's Court in Basseterre, St Kitts, a juvenile was
convicted of possession of cannabis with intent to supply. [@1] For that
conviction, the court placed the juvenile on probation for six months with one
surety. [@1] The juvenile's case was dealt with on St Kitts at the court in
Basseterre. [@1]

The magistrate ordered the juvenile to keep the peace and to be of good
behaviour as conditions of the probation. [@1] The magistrate imposed a curfew
on the juvenile and ordered the juvenile to find gainful employment. [@1]

The cannabis offence behind the conviction was committed on Thursday, 12 April
2012. [@1]
