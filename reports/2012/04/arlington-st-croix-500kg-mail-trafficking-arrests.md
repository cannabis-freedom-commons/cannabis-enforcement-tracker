Federal authorities identified Neil Nick Rene, 35, as the ringleader and primary
participant in a drug-trafficking organization that shipped hundreds of
kilograms of marijuana from Arlington to the U.S. Virgin Islands. [@1]
Authorities arrested Rene and five others, including his 42-year-old ex-wife
Laura Louise Ryan-Rene, in North Texas in the week before publication. [@1] The
people arrested in North Texas had already been released on bond when the
article was published. [@1] Eleven others were arrested in St. Croix on the
Thursday the article was published, in connection with the same case, including
a territorial court marshal. [@1]

Federal documents said the organization had been sending packages of marijuana
to the Virgin Islands since at least November 2009. [@1] The group sent more
than 120 Express Mail parcels from at least five post office stations in
Arlington to at least six addresses in St. Croix. [@1] Authorities put the total
weight of the marijuana at more than 500 kilograms. [@1] U.S. Attorney Sarah R.
Saldaña said the arrests culminated a more than two-year investigation into
shipping large quantities of marijuana from Arlington, Texas, to St. Croix. [@1]

The investigation was spearheaded by the Dallas and Fort Worth offices of the
U.S. Postal Inspection Service, U.S. Customs and Border Protection, the Drug
Enforcement Administration and Internal Revenue Service-Criminal Investigation.
[@1] Rene had previously pleaded guilty in 1995 and was sentenced to five years
in federal prison in a Dallas marijuana distribution case, returned to prison in
fall 2000 after his supervised release was revoked, and was re-released in fall
2001. [@1] The case drew attention because Rene's teenage sister Lisa Rene had
been kidnapped from an Arlington apartment and killed in 1994 by men angry at
Rene over a drug dispute. [@1]
