Albanian police seized two tonnes of cannabis believed to be bound for Greece
at Kakome Bay, about 240 kilometres south of Tirana, on the night of Wednesday,
25 April 2012. [@1,2] The occupants of the vehicle fled after spotting officers
following them, and the drugs were recovered without an immediate arrest. [@1,2]

A police statement days later said five suspects considered the main traffickers
had been arrested. [@1] They were described as owners of a tourism resort and
guardsmen who had allegedly tried to delay police work that evening. [@1]

Police linked the load to smuggling routes into Greece, with the wire account
adding that Albania had become a main supplier of marijuana to Europe and that
more than three and a half tonnes of cannabis had been seized since the start
of the year. [@1,2]
