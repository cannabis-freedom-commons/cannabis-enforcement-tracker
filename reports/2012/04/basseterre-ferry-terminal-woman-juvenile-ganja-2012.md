Plain-clothes Delta Squad operatives arrested a young woman and a male juvenile
on 5 April 2012 on charges of possession of cannabis and possession of cannabis
with intent to supply another. [@1] Police identified the woman as 22-year-old
Hilisha Leader, and said she, the juvenile and an infant were at the St. Kitts
Ferry Terminal in Basseterre waiting to board a vessel to Nevis when the
incident occurred. [@1] The operatives had been tasked with monitoring the
ferries for suspicious activities and had observed the two acting in a
suspicious manner. [@1] Both persons were charged with possession of cannabis
and possession of cannabis with intent to supply another. [@1]

Officers found compressed marijuana with a street value of EC$60,000 in the
possession of Leader and her juvenile accomplice. [@1] A secondary search
disclosed another EC$15,000 worth of marijuana, bringing the total value of
marijuana seized in the arrest to EC$75,000. [@1] The account was given by
Police Press and Public Relations Officer Sergeant Stephen Hector. [@1]

Police said the seizure reflected the continued vigilance of the Royal St.
Christopher and Nevis Police Force in implementing its strategy of taking back
the streets from criminals. [@1]
