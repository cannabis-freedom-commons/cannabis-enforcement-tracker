Berkeley Patients Group, described as a medical marijuana industry leader
serving thousands of patients, closed its storefront at 2747 San Pablo
Avenue at the end of business on 30 April 2012 and did not reopen it.
[@1] The dispensary had operated since October 1999 and fell victim to the
ongoing federal crackdown. [@2,3]

US Attorney Melinda Haag had threatened the previous fall to seize the
property, and the landlords served an eviction notice effective the next day.
[@2] The dispensary had negotiated a stay through the spring after Haag's
November 2011 warning letters to landlords, but the pressure forced it to
shut its doors, in what was called a major blow to the state's medical
marijuana industry. [@2,3]

The dispensary later reopened that fall at a new Berkeley location on San
Pablo Avenue. [@4]
