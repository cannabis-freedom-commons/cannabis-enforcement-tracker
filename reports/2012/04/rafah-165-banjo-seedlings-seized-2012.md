The General Directorate of Anti-Narcotics Police seized 165 banjo seedlings, a
local name for cannabis, in a separating border agricultural area east of Rafah
in the southern Gaza Strip. [@1] The department said it received information
about quantities of banjo seedlings in a piece of farmland, after which an
anti-narcotics patrol moved to the site and seized an estimated 165 seedlings.
[@2]

The department's public relations and media director, Captain Saadi al-Sheikh
Khalil, praised the efforts of the police and the anti-narcotics administration
in fighting what he called this dangerous scourge. [@2] Captain al-Sheikh Khalil
said the anti-narcotics department would spare no effort and would work day and
night to protect the lives of young people. [@1] He said the operation was part
of the General Directorate's continuing efforts to make the Gaza Strip an area
free of drugs. [@1]
