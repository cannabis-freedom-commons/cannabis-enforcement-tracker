Luxembourg police arrested a suspected drug dealer at the e-Lake music festival
in Echternach in August 2012 and found cocaine and marijuana on him. [@1]
Officers at the festival followed a man who was repeatedly approached by young
people there. [@1]

The search of the man found 27 packets or pellets of cocaine, 14 grams of
marijuana and a substantial amount of cash, police said. [@2] Police said the
man had been dealing drugs actively on the festival grounds. [@2]

Criminal investigators from Grevenmacher also checked a second man who kept
close to the first and found eight bags of marijuana on him. [@2] The suspected
dealer was arrested on the order of the public prosecutor's office in Diekirch.
[@3]

The e-Lake festival ran on 10 and 11 August 2012, and the police detained the
dealer on the festival car park by the lake. [@4] The festival organisers
thanked the police for the arrest and presented it as a result of their
anti-drug policy at the event. [@4]
