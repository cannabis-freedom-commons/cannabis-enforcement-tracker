Police began investigating the UPS facility on Foster Avenue in Brooklyn after a
tip, and the case ended with two of its drivers and a third man arrested. [@1]
Undercover officers watching the facility on a Thursday morning grew suspicious
when driver Ruben Jordan loaded a box onto a truck he was not assigned to. [@1]
Officers kept the truck under watch after Jordan went off duty and left, and
another driver, Alexis Herve, drove it to a dead-end street where Jordan, 44,
was waiting with another man and a minivan. [@1]

Herve, 52, handed the package to Jordan, who put it in the minivan, and police
moved in. [@1] Inside the package officers found three five-gallon Home Depot
buckets stuffed with marijuana. [@1] The sources put the weight at nearly 40
pounds and the street value at almost $60,000. [@1] Police also recovered tens
of thousands of dollars from the front seat of the minivan. [@1]

The two drivers and the man who was not a UPS employee were arrested and all
three faced drug charges. [@1]
