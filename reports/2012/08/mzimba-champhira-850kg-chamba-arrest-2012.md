Malawi police arrested a 36-year-old Zambian man, Febe Moyo, and seized 17 bags
of cannabis, known locally as chamba and as Indian hemp, at Champhira in Mzimba
district. [@1] Police put the weight at 50 kg a bag, a total of 850 kg. [@1]

Mzimba police spokesperson Gift Nyirongo said detectives at Champhira police
post laid an ambush after a hired driver told them he had been enlisted to carry
the drug to Lundazi in Zambia and that they would set off at midnight. [@1]
Nyirongo said officers arrested Moyo around midnight and seized the 17 bags,
each weighing 50 kg. [@1]

Moyo, from Chipembe village in traditional authority Mpezeni in Zambia's Chipata
district, was charged with being found in possession of cannabis sativa under
section 19(1) of the Dangerous Drugs Act. [@1] Police said he would appear in
court soon. [@1] The article also reported an earlier June 2012 case in Mzimba,
in which a Zambian woman, Hildah Phiri, was held with six bags of chamba
weighing 90 kg each and remanded at Mzimba Prison; that is a separate event.
