On 13 August 2012, officers attached to the Drug Unit and the Special Services
Unit conducted a joint eradication exercise on a marijuana plantation at Errand
Estate, Dennery. [@1] The account of the operation was issued by the RSLPF press
office. [@1] No one was arrested during the operation. [@1]

During the operation, officers destroyed a total of 2,304 grown marijuana
plants. [@1] Officers also destroyed 500 seedlings, bringing the total number of
plants destroyed to 2,804. [@1] Officers also recovered a spent shotgun round at
the site. [@1]
