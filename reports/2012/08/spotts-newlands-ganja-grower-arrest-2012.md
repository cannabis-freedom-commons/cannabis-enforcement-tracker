Police arrested one man during a ganja operation in the Spotts-Newlands area of
Grand Cayman. [@1] The man was 32 years old. [@1] Officers seized an unknown
amount of ganja during the operation. [@1] The operation took place in the
afternoon of 14 August 2012. [@2] The Royal Cayman Islands Police Service said
he was arrested under the Misuse of Drugs Law. [@2] The stated grounds were
cultivation and possession of ganja. [@2] He was also arrested on suspicion of
consuming a controlled drug. [@2]

He was released on police bail on Tuesday. [@1]

Officers from Bodden Town Police Station, OSU and X-Ray 1 carried out the
operation. [@2] Police described it as part of an ongoing island-wide initiative
focused on proactively tackling criminal and traffic-related offences. [@2]
Acting Chief Inspector Brad Ebanks, OiC of the Eastern Districts, warned that
the RCIPS was watching those involved in crime. [@2] Ebanks said police would
pay close attention to those who commit such offences. [@1] He added that people
involved in illegal activities could get an unexpected police visit. [@1]
