Kenya Revenue Authority officers seized more than 900 kilograms of bhang worth about Sh9 million at the Tanzanian border on Thursday. [@1] The bhang, packed in 31 sacks, was being transported in a truck that had just crossed into Kenya when officers intercepted it during a routine inspection and found it concealed in one of the truck's compartments. [@1]

KRA spokesman Kennedy Onyonyi said the truck driver ran away when officers searched the vehicle, and police in Namanga launched a manhunt for him. [@1] Following the seizure, officers tightened security at the border to curb bhang trafficking from Tanzania, which is seen as the main source of the drug consumed locally. [@1] An anti-narcotics officer said the bhang is usually transported to Nairobi before being repackaged and taken to Mombasa. [@1]
