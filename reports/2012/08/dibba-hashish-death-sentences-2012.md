The Dibba Criminal Court sentenced an Emirati, identified as W.A., and an Iranian, identified as H.A., to death after a five-man gang was caught planning to bring narcotics through the Dibba Al-Hisn land border checkpoint. [@1] H.A. was sentenced in absentia. [@1] Two accomplices, S.M. and A.A., received ten years each plus a Dh20,000 fine, and a fifth gang member, M.A., received four years. [@1] The gang was arrested with 18 kg of hashish and 20,000 tramadol tablets. [@1,2]

## Appeal

The Khorfakkan Court of Appeal upheld the death sentence against the Iranian, H.A., fined him Dh50,000 and ordered the drug haul confiscated. [@2] It commuted the Emirati's death sentence, identifying him as W.S., to life imprisonment for smuggling, with four more years for possessing and consuming narcotics. [@2] The court acquitted a third accused, M.M., of smuggling but jailed him for four years for drug consumption, and cleared S.M. and A.A. of possessing and using drugs, overturning their ten-year terms and fines. [@2]
