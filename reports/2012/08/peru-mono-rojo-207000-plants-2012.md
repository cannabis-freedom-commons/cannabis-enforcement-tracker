Peruvian police destroyed more than 50 tonnes of marijuana in a five-day operation, locating a record 207,000 plants in two central regions of the country. [@1,2] Interior Minister Wilfredo Pedraza said the haul was seventeen times what police had destroyed in all of 2011. [@1]

## Operation Moño Rojo

The operation, dubbed Moño Rojo (Red Bow), began on 27 July and covered sites in La Libertad and Huánuco. [@1] Officers burned the 207,000 plants they uncovered across the two regions. [@1,2] The destroyed crop was valued at about 15 million dollars on the United States market, a record for the Peruvian National Police. [@1]

Pedraza presented the operation as evidence that the government's intensified fight against drug trafficking was working. [@1] Peru is better known for cocaine, and analysts said little was known about its marijuana production. [@2]
