Moroccan customs seized seven tonnes of cannabis resin bound for Britain at the port of Agadir in the southwest. [@1] The drugs had been hidden inside a container of frozen fish and were uncovered during a scan. [@1]

The customs service put the street value of the haul at seven million euros (about $8.7 million), and described it as one of the largest seizures in Morocco that year. [@1] Morocco is a major hashish producer, and customs officers said they used sophisticated detection equipment against narcotics trafficking. [@1]
