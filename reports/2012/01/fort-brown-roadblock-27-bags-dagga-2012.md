An alert team of traffic officers and police arrested five people suspected of dealing in dagga at a roadblock outside the Fort Brown police station near Grahamstown on Tuesday. [@1] The suspects were aged between 31 and 56. [@1]

Officers first stopped a white VW Polo with four occupants, and a traffic officer overheard one of the occupants speaking on a cellphone saying the other party must not come through. [@1] A blue Isuzu bakkie was then seen pulling over to the side of the road before making a U-turn and driving back towards Fort Beaufort; two police officers gave chase but could not trace it at first. [@1] On their way back to the station they stopped a man standing by the roadside who could not give a good account of himself, and a search of the area revealed the abandoned bakkie containing 27 bags of dagga with an estimated street value of R500,000. [@1]

Grahamstown police cluster commander Brigadier Vakala Moyake praised the Fort Brown warrant officers and the four traffic officers for their alertness and quick action. [@1]
