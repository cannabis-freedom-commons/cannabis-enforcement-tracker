On Tuesday 31 January 2012, members of the Drug Unit based in Castries conducted
an eradication exercise on four marijuana plantations located in a forest area
at Anse-La-Raye. [@1] The exercise targeted those four plantations found
together in the same Anse-La-Raye forest area. [@1] No one was arrested during
that Anse-La-Raye forest operation. [@1]

Officers destroyed approximately 16,841 grown marijuana plants during that
eradication exercise. [@1] Police put the estimated street value of those 16,841
grown plants at EC$942,050 in Eastern Caribbean dollars. [@1] Officers also
destroyed 10,000 marijuana seedlings in the same Anse-La-Raye operation in
addition to the grown plants. [@1] Officers also destroyed 200 pounds of
harvested marijuana in the same operation alongside the growing plants and
seedlings. [@1]

The 10,000 seedlings and the 200 pounds of harvested marijuana were reported as
additional quantities beyond the approximate 16,841 grown plants. [@1] All of
the plant counts and the street value were attributed to that 31 January forest
operation by the Castries-based Drug Unit, which reported no arrests for this
eradication. [@1]
