Mauritanian authorities intercepted a boat off Nouadhibou in northwestern
Mauritania and arrested the two Moroccan nationals found on board. [@1] The
vessel was coming from Dakhla in Western Sahara, described as arriving from
Morocco. [@1] The interception account was attributed to a security source in
Nouadhibou. [@2]

Officers seized 2.5 tonnes of hashish concealed aboard the boat. [@1] The source
described the load as a cargo of 2.5 tonnes of hashish hidden inside the craft.
[@1] The boat was boarded as it was about to enter Mauritanian territorial
waters. [@1] The seizure point was reported as off Nouadhibou in the country's
northwest. [@2] The two people arrested were the Moroccan nationals travelling
aboard that vessel from the Dakhla area. [@1]

An investigation was opened following the seizure. [@1] Mauritania said it had
put in place a system for monitoring its borders intended to combat drug
trafficking. [@2]
