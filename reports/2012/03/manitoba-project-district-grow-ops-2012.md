In February 2012 police across Manitoba launched Project District, a five-week operation that dismantled ten marijuana grow operations and seized 3,300 plants, along with about C$34,000 in cash and growing equipment. [@1] Officers from RCMP "D" Division joined the Ste. Anne Police Department in the sweep, part of the RCMP's national Marijuana Grow Initiative against grow operations run by organized crime. [@1,2]

Thirteen people faced charges including production and trafficking of a controlled substance, theft of electricity and possession of proceeds of crime. [@1] Officers found children living in two of the grow-ops, and fire officials later deemed six of the properties unfit for habitation until inspected and remediated. [@1] Public Safety Minister Vic Toews and MP James Bezan publicly commended the two police services for the seizure. [@2]
