Anti-narcotics police in Khan Younis in the southern Gaza Strip seized 12.5
slabs of hashish, also called farsh, a quantity of cannabis seeds known locally
as banj, and drug-use paraphernalia. [@1] The Khan Younis anti-narcotics
director, Major Emad al-Ghalban, said the force raided the Khan Younis home of a
man identified only as (M.A.) after a tip that drugs were kept there. [@1]

The search of the house turned up 6.5 slabs of hashish, cannabis seeds and
drug-use paraphernalia. [@1] In a separate case, officers set up a tight ambush
in Rafah city after a tip that two men were to hand over and receive a quantity
of drugs, and seized two men identified only as (M.Sh.) and (M.R.) as they drove
a taxi. [@1] The search of the taxi found 6 slabs of hashish. [@1] Police held
the seized items and referred the Khan Younis case to a police investigation
inspector to complete the legal procedures against the suspect. [@1] The Rafah
case went to a Rafah police investigation inspector for the same purpose, so the
two operations accounted for three people held in total. [@1]

The Safa news agency reported the same two seizures, also crediting Major Emad
al-Ghalban, and said the two Rafah suspects were arrested. [@2] Safa's account
of the house search gives the same figures and refers the Khan Younis case to
the police investigation inspector. [@2] Safa published its account on Saturday
3 March 2012, while the Al-Ray article carries the date 4 November 2012. [@2]
