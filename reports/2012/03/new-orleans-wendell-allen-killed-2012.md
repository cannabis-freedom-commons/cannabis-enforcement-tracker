New Orleans police officer Joshua Colclough shot dead unarmed 20-year-old Wendell Allen with a single bullet to the chest during a marijuana raid in the Gentilly neighbourhood on the evening of 7 March 2012. [@1,3] Officers of the 3rd District narcotics unit had forced their way into Allen's home with a search warrant issued after a days-long marijuana-dealing investigation. [@1,3] Five children aged between 1 and 14 were inside, and family members said officers held guns to the children's faces as they entered. [@1]

## What police found

Officers said they recovered marijuana along with bags, digital scales and a handgun from the house, and arrested two men for possession with intent to distribute. [@1] The first account put the haul at about five ounces; later accounts settled on 4.5 ounces (about 128 grams). [@1,2] No weapon was found on Allen. [@3]

## Indictment

In August 2012 a state grand jury indicted Colclough on one count of manslaughter, rejecting a second-degree murder charge, after he failed to appear at a hearing where he had been expected to plead guilty to negligent homicide. [@2,3] He turned himself in for booking the next day. [@3]
