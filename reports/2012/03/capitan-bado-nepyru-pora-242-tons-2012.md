Agents of Senad, Paraguay's anti-drug agency, destroyed a total of about 242 tons of marijuana after ten days of continuous raids around the town of Capitán Bado, in the Brazilian-border department of Amambay. [@1,2] The operation, named "Ñepyrû Porâ", closed with the destruction of several marijuana production and storage centres. [@1]

The teams pushed into the wooded hills of the Colono, Piray and Suzuki settlements, where they found 79 hectares of cannabis in 22 plots, estimated to hold 237 tons of the plant. [@1,2] At the storage camps they found 5,415 kilos of chopped and bagged marijuana ready for pressing, along with four wooden presses used to make the bricks. [@1,2] Senad put the traffickers' loss at about 7.26 million US dollars, and prosecutor Valeriana Ferreira accompanied the operation. [@1,2]
