Agents assigned to the Carrizo Springs Station seized two marijuana loads within two hours on 3 March 2012. [@1] The loads weighed 11,973 pounds (about 5,431 kg) and 6,692 pounds (about 3,036 kg), and both had been camouflaged with oilfield supplies. [@1] They were found within a short distance of one another. [@1]

## A sector record

The two loads were the largest and third largest marijuana seizures in Del Rio sector history. [@1] Together they weighed 18,665 pounds (about 8,467 kg), and a second account of the same record day confirms that total from two tractor-trailer stops. [@1,2] CBP valued the combined haul at $15 million, the largest single-day marijuana seizure the sector had recorded. [@1]
