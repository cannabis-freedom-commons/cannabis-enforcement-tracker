The High Court in Alor Setar sentenced 33-year-old Nigerian Sylvester Okole to death by hanging after convicting him of trafficking cannabis. [@1,2] Judicial Commissioner Mohd Wahab found the prosecution had established the case beyond reasonable doubt and imposed the mandatory death sentence under the trafficking charge. [@2]

Okole was charged with trafficking 11.577 kg of cannabis, described as Indian hemp, at Platform 8 of the Shahab Perdana bus terminal. [@1,2] Local police officers arrested him there at about 9pm on 7 October 2010. [@1]
