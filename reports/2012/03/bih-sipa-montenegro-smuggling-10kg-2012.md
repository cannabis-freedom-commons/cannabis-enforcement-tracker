Police officers in Bosnia and Herzegovina arrested three suspects during an
overnight operation mounted to suppress illicit drug trafficking and abuse. [@2]
State investigators announced that two of the arrested individuals were citizens
of Montenegro and one was a citizen of Bosnia and Herzegovina. [@2] The
Montenegrin nationals were arrested in Bosnia and Herzegovina on suspicion of
smuggling ten kilograms of marijuana. [@1] Investigators from the State
Investigation and Protection Agency seized 10.664 kilograms of marijuana during
the operation. [@1] Following criminal processing, the three detainees were
scheduled to be handed over to the Prosecutor's Office of Bosnia and Herzegovina
for further legal proceedings. [@1]

The operation was carried out in cooperation with the Ministry of Internal
Affairs of Republika Srpska, the Ministry of Internal Affairs of Sarajevo Canton
and the Ministry of Internal Affairs of Central Bosnia Canton. [@1] Police
officers from the State Investigation and Protection Agency continued their
operational and investigative work on the case following the initial arrests and
drug seizure. [@2]
