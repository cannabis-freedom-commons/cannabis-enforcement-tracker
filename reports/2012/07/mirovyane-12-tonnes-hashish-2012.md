Bulgarian police seized 12 tons of hashish from a warehouse in Mirovyane, a village near the capital Sofia. [@1] Staminir Florov, the director of the General Directorate for Combating Organized Crime (GDBOP) within the Interior Ministry, announced the seizure on Thursday, valuing the cannabis resin at about 360 million euros (445 million US dollars) and saying it was destined for the European Union market. [@1]

## A Moroccan shipment via the Black Sea

The hashish came from Morocco, and the authorities believe fishermen transported it via the Black Sea. [@1] The seizure beat a 4-tonne cannabis seizure days earlier in Vidin, a city on the Romanian border, and the authorities believe the two shipments may be linked. [@1] Bulgaria had emerged as a major cannabis producer over the previous three years, according to the International Narcotics Control Board, and the UN Office on Drugs and Crime reported production and use rising in the country, traditionally supplied with Moroccan cannabis. [@1]
