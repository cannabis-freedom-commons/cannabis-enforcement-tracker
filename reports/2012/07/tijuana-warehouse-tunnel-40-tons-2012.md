On Thursday 12 July 2012, Mexican soldiers found an incomplete drug tunnel
inside a warehouse in Tijuana and seized more than 40 tons of marijuana
(about 36,300 kg) stockpiled at its entry. [@1,2] The passage ran nearly 400
yards, including more than 100 yards into United States territory, but no
exit had been built on the American side. [@1,2] It was lit and ventilated
and fitted with a railcar system, and soldiers also found four moving trucks
full of marijuana plus a trailer of dirt and excavation tools in the
warehouse. [@1]

The find was one of four border tunnels discovered in a single week, and
press reports linked the week's sophisticated passages to the Sinaloa
cartel's expanding control of the border region. [@1,2] The Associated Press
noted that raids the previous November on two San Diego-Tijuana tunnels had
netted a combined 52 tons of marijuana. [@1]
