On 9 July 2012, a joint operation by the Criminal Investigation Department, Task
Force and Crime Patrol Unit of the Nevis Division found three firearms, 222
rounds of ammunition, two magazines and 104 dime bags of cannabis sativa in
bushes at an unstated location on Nevis. [@1] Officers executed a search warrant
at the Low Street, Charlestown premises of Ibol Tyson, which resulted in the
find of a quantity of crack cocaine. [@1]

Tyson and one Kinshenell Benjamin of St. Paul’s Village, St. Kitts were formally
arrested and charged with possession of crack cocaine. [@1] One individual was
taken into custody to assist police with their investigation in relation to the
firearms and cannabis find. [@1]
