A 43-year-old man from Morne Prosper pleaded not guilty to three cannabis
charges and one charge of possessing ammunition. [@1] Franklyn McMorris Jno
Baptiste was granted EC$14,000 bail. [@1]

Police arrested him on 8 July 2012. [@1] He was charged with possessing three
live rounds of 12-gauge ammunition. [@1] He was charged with possessing 2,310
grams of cannabis with intent to supply. [@1] He was also charged with
possessing and cultivating one grown cannabis plant. [@1]

He was to return to court for trial on 15 October 2012. [@1] His bail conditions
required him to report to the Roseau police station twice a week, on Mondays and
Fridays. [@1] The court warned him against committing any offence while on bail.
[@1]

Online News From Dominica republished a shortened copy of the Dominica News
Online report on the same day, linking back to the original post. [@2]
