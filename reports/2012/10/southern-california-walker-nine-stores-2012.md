Twelve of 14 people named in a 14-count federal indictment were arrested on the morning of 25 October 2012 over a chain of nine marijuana stores across Orange and Los Angeles counties. [@1,2] The indictment described a conspiracy led by owner John Melvin Walker, 56, of San Clemente, which distributed at least a ton of marijuana through the storefronts. [@1] The nine stores stretched from Long Beach and Dana Point to Santa Ana, Costa Mesa, Whittier and San Juan Capistrano, and most had already been searched in 2010 and 2011. [@1,2]

## Money and guns

The stores generated tens of millions of dollars, with one shop alone allegedly profiting about $2.4 million in 2009, and the indictment accused Walker of hiding the income from tax authorities and ordering records destroyed. [@1,2] When authorities had seized firearms from Walker's home the previous year they also took about $390,000 in cash; Walker, previously convicted on cocaine and marijuana charges, was charged over a handgun, a shotgun and an AK-47. [@1,2] Two defendants were still being sought, and all 14 faced a conspiracy count carrying ten years to life. [@1]
