Tajikistan's Drug Control Agency, the anti-drug agency under the president,
carried out a multi-stage operation to seize a very large party of narcotics
from illegal circulation. [@1] In Kumsangir district of Khatlon region, agents
stopped an Opel Astra driven by a 52-year-old Tajik citizen, previously not
convicted and unemployed, suspected of drug smuggling. [@1]

Searching the car, officers found and seized 122 pressed tablets in the shape of
books, containing a brown substance with a distinctive smell. [@1] The agency's
forensic examination found the seized substances to be hashish weighing 85 kg,
each tablet stamped and marked in Arabic script, which the source said showed
the drug's high quality. [@1]

The agency said the hashish was meant to be smuggled from Afghanistan through
Tajikistan to CIS countries. [@1] A criminal case was opened under article 200
part 4 of the Criminal Code of Tajikistan, which covers illegal trafficking of
narcotic drugs or psychotropic substances for sale in especially large
quantities. [@1] The drug trafficker was detained and investigative steps were
under way to establish other people involved in the case. [@1]
