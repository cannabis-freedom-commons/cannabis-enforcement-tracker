Seventh District watchstanders tasked the Coast Guard crew to assist the
Jamaican-flagged fishing vessel Captain Richard after learning it was disabled
and adrift in the Caribbean Sea without fuel more than 200 miles from the
nearest port. [@2] The combined crew took the 60-foot Jamaican-flagged fishing
vessel Captain Richard in tow after finding it disabled and adrift in the
Caribbean Sea without fuel more than 200 miles from the nearest port. [@2] The
Coast Guard boarded the Captain Richard after receiving permission from the
Government of Jamaica to conduct a law enforcement boarding. [@2] The crew of
the Captain Richard was detained after the discovery of the marijuana aboard the
vessel. [@3]

The boarding team found several hundred packages wrapped in plastic wrap and
tape, and the contents later tested positive for marijuana. [@2] About 6,500
pounds of marijuana was seized in the joint effort of the crews of the Coast
Guard Cutters Mohawk and Tahoma and offloaded in Key West on 16 October 2012.
[@2] The Coast Guard put the estimated wholesale value of the marijuana at $6.2
million. [@1]

A joint crew of Tahoma personnel with six Mohawk crewmembers operated aboard the
Mohawk while the Tahoma underwent a nine-month overhaul at the Coast Guard Yard
in Baltimore. [@2] The seizure came about two weeks after more than $1 million
in marijuana washed ashore near West Palm Beach. [@1]
