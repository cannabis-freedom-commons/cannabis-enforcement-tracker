Spain's Tax Agency seized 3,373 kilos of hashish in Huelva and stopped a further 3,000 kilos reaching the Málaga coast in three operations announced on 25 October 2012. [@1] Three people were arrested and three vessels were seized. [@1]

In the first operation on 9 October, a customs patrol boat found an unmanned pleasure craft moored at a jetty inside the Huelva estuary carrying bales of the type used for hashish, towed it to the Huelva base and weighed 973 kilos. [@1] South of Marbella, customs air and sea units spotted three semi-rigid inflatable boats heading for the Málaga coast, but the smugglers threw more than 100 bales, about 3,000 kilos, overboard and fled; officers intercepted one dinghy and arrested its three crew members. [@1] In a third operation in the early hours of 14 September off Huelva, run jointly with the National Police, officers seized a 12-metre inflatable with three outboard engines after spotting it sailing without lights, along with 2,400 kilos.
