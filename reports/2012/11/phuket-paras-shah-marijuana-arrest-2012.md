Nepal's former crown prince Paras Shah was arrested in Thailand and charged with
possession of marijuana. [@1] Police said Shah spent a night in jail on a charge
of possessing an illegal drug and was later released on bail of 10,000 baht.
[@1] A senior Phuket police officer said Shah was arrested with a woman at a
hotel on the resort island but did not identify her. [@1] The officer said hotel
staff had called police to break up a quarrel between a man and a woman, and
officers found the drugs while mediating the dispute. [@1]

Police placed the incident last month in the tourist resort of Phuket. [@1] The
man involved turned out to be the Nepalese former crown prince. [@1] The officer
who described the arrest spoke on condition of anonymity. [@1]

Nepalese newspaper The Himalayan Times said the Nepalese embassy in Bangkok was
informed about the case on October 27. [@1] The Himalayan Times account said the
Nepalese embassy in Bangkok and the Thai embassy in Nepal had remained mum over
the matter. [@1] The same account noted Shah had earlier been involved in
similar escapades in Nepal. [@1]
