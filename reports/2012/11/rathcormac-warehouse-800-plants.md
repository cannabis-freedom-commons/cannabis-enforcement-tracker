Gardai from the Garda National Drugs Unit in Dublin Castle, Anglesea Street and Fermoy detectives searched a warehouse at Rathcormac, Fermoy, Co. Cork on 19 November 2012 as part of an operation against cannabis cultivation and supply in the Munster region. [@1,2] During the search they found about 800 cannabis plants with a total street value of €640,000 pending analysis. [@1,2] The scene was sealed off for technical examination. [@1,2]

Five men in their twenties and thirties were arrested and detained at Fermoy, Midleton and Cobh Garda stations under section 2 of the Criminal Justice (Drug Trafficking) Act 1996. [@1,2] All five were later charged and due to appear before Midleton District Court at 4.30pm in connection with the seizure. [@2]
