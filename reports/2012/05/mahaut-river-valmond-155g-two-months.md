Betham Valmond, a resident of Sineku in Dominica's Carib Territory, was
sentenced to two months in prison after pleading guilty to possessing cannabis
with intent to supply. [@1] He was arrested and charged on 12 May 2012 at Mahaut
River, also in the Carib Territory. [@1]

Police on patrol said they saw him walking along the main road with a black
backpack and then entering a yard, calling out offers of weed for $5, $10 or
$20. [@1] The officers approached him, searched him and recovered 155 grams of
marijuana in the bag. [@1] Valmond told police he was selling the drug so that
he could "set up his shack". [@1]

Magistrate Candia George reminded him that he had been given several chances in
the past for various offences when he asked the court for leniency. [@1] Before
sentencing, she told him that although he might be making a quick buck for
himself he was destroying the lives of others and should stop. [@1] Online News
From Dominica republished the Dominica News Online account the same day with a
link to the original post. [@2]
