A 32-year-old man from Bath Estate and Massacre was given until 30 October 2012
to pay two separate drug fines totalling EC$4,000 or face 13 months in jail.
[@1] Curvel St. Rose appeared before Chief Magistrate Evalina Baptiste on 14 May
2012 and pleaded guilty to possession of cannabis and possession with intent to
supply 210 grams. [@1]

On 12 May 2012 at about 10:45pm, officers of the Task Force on patrol near the
Minya's 7-11 supermarket in Canefield saw him parking a scooter and looking
suspicious. [@1] He denied having drugs and a search of his person found
nothing, but a search of the scooter found a clear plastic bag with 210 grams of
cannabis that police valued at EC$500. [@1] He was arrested and charged, and
told police the drug had been given to him by a man he had done a mechanical job
for. [@1]

He repeated the account in court, and the magistrate told him she did not
believe his story. [@1] Baptiste told him he took drugs in place of payment for
his mechanical work, and said young people selling the drug were supplying
schoolboys who took it from the age of 10. [@1] She fined him EC$1,000 for
possession of cannabis, payable by 30 October 2012 or six months in jail, and
EC$3,000 for possession with intent to supply. [@1]

The account of the sentencing was carried the same day by Online News From
Dominica, which reprints Dominica News Online articles. [@2]
