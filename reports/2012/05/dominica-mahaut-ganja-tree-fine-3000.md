Fergerson Joseph, a 49-year-old man of Mahaut in Dominica, pleaded guilty to
possession and cultivation of cannabis in the Roseau court of Chief Magistrate
Evalina Baptiste on 21 May 2012. [@1] Mahaut police said they went to Joseph's
home on 19 May 2012 after a report and found a marijuana tree growing in a white
pail in his yard. [@1] Asked to explain, he told the officers the tree was a
"kali tree" kept to make tea instead of coffee. [@1]

Asked to mitigate on his own behalf because he had no lawyer, the first-time
offender told the court, “your honour, I don’t know what to say”. [@1] The
magistrate noted that he had no previous convictions and had not wasted the
court's time, and that the maximum penalty for cultivation was EC$100,000. [@1]
Baptiste said the prevalence of the "illicit and destructive" drug was a matter
of grave concern to her. [@1]

Joseph was fined EC$2,200 for cultivation and a further EC$800, both to be paid
by 21 October 2012, with a seven-month jail term if he defaulted on the
cultivation fine. [@1]
