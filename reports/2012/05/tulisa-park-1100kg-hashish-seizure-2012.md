Members of the Hawks, SAPS crime intelligence and customs officers from the
South African Revenue Service seized 1.1 tons of narcotics, identified as
hashish with a street value of R36 million, in Tulisa Park, Johannesburg, in
the late afternoon of Saturday 26 May 2012. [@1,2]

The seizure followed information from a member of the public and weeks of
investigation that identified a storage facility in southern Johannesburg run
as part of a freight service storing and dispatching consignments across the
country and overseas. [@1,2] Officers found the drugs in fraudulently labelled
packs, including a popular brand of pet food as well as tobacco and coffee
packages, and said they believed the consignment was destined for the
international market. [@1,2] Forensic officers tested the drugs at the scene
and the initial results indicated hashish. [@1,2]

Officers also found a large tortoise kept with the consignment without food or
water, identified by the NSPCA wildlife unit as a 50-year-old female leopard
tortoise; officers named her Nonhlanhla and handed her to the unit. [@1,2] No
arrests had been made, and the Hawks were still following up information. [@1,2]
