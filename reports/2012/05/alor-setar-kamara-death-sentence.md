The High Court in Alor Setar sentenced Nigerian student James Kamara, 23, to death on 14 May 2012 after finding him guilty of trafficking 18.81 kilogrammes of cannabis. [@1,2] Judicial Commissioner Mohd Zaki Abdul Wahab imposed the mandatory sentence of death by hanging under Section 39B of the Dangerous Drugs Act 1952. [@1,2]

Kamara, who held a student visa and studied at a private higher-learning institution in Kuala Lumpur, was convicted over cannabis found at the Shahab Perdana bus terminal in Alor Setar at about 9.05pm on 13 September 2010. [@1,2] The prosecution was brought by the Royal Malaysian Customs Department, and the court held that Kamara and his defence team had failed to raise a reasonable doubt against the prosecution's case. [@2]

Deputy Public Prosecutor Nur Sarah Dina Baharudin appeared for the prosecution, while Burhanudeen Abdul Wahid represented Kamara. [@1]
