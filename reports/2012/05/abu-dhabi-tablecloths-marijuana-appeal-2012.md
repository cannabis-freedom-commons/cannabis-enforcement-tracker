Four men who tried to smuggle marijuana into the country stitched into
embroidered tablecloths appeared before an Abu Dhabi appeal court on 31 May
2012. [@1] The two cloths were brought into the courtroom, where an expert
explained that hashish pieces hidden between the threads of the embroidery were
clearly visible under a magnifying glass. [@1]

The criminal court had convicted the four of using the drug, and had sentenced
one of them to life in prison for dealing; he appealed that conviction. [@1]
All four appealed against sentences of four years in jail followed by
deportation for drug use. [@1] The court adjourned the hearing until 14 June,
when the arresting officer was to testify and the lawyer for the defendant
convicted of dealing was to present his defence. [@1]
