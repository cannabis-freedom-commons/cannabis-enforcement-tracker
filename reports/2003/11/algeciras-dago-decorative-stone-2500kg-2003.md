The Guardia Civil searched a trailer of a lorry that had arrived at the port of
Algeciras from Morocco, with the assistance of the police dog Dago, and found
2,500 kilos of hashish hidden inside its cargo of decorative stone items
supposedly destined for local gardens and terraces. [@1] Officers smashed open the
stone ornaments identified by the dog, each of which held five kilos of hashish. [@1]

Dago, a German Shepherd based with the Guardia Civil in Algeciras, had detected
13,126 kilos of hashish so far that year. [@1] His Algeciras partner, another
German Shepherd called Reno, had sniffed out 328 kilos of hashish hidden on a yacht
in August. [@1]
