The Dubai Supreme Court handed a death sentence to two of six drug smugglers who set fire to a boat carrying more than 800 kilos of cannabis as the Anti-Narcotics Squad approached them off the Dubai coast. [@1] The rest of the gang received life sentences. [@1]

The cable notes that a 1995 law stipulates capital punishment for drug trafficking in the UAE, but that no executions for drug offences had ever taken place and sentences were usually commuted to life imprisonment. [@1]
