On the morning of Wednesday 26 November 2003 customs officers stopped a truck as it arrived at
Poole Ferryport from Cherbourg and found a tonne of cannabis resin hidden in a pallet of ceramic
tiles. [@1] The haul carried a street value of up to £2.5 million. [@1]

The driver, a man from Winsford in Cheshire, was questioned by Customs and Excise and released on
bail pending further enquiries. [@1] A Customs spokesman said the seizure showed Poole should not
be seen as a soft touch, adding that keeping cannabis illegal remained part of the drugs strategy
alongside action against Class A drugs. [@1]
