A British haulier was remanded in custody in southern France after customs officers seized
four tonnes of cannabis in his truck. [@1] Officers near Montpellier used sniffer dogs to
detect the drugs, which were hidden among pallets of fruit juice and construction tiles.
[@1] The driver, who was not named, denied knowledge of the drugs. [@1]
