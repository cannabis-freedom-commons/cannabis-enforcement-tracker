Saudi Arabia beheaded Saudi national Dhaher bin Thamer al-Shimry on 14 September
2003 after his conviction for smuggling marijuana into the kingdom, according to
a Ministry of Interior statement quoted from the Associated Press.[@1] The
execution took place in the eastern province of Hafr al-Baten.[@1] He was the
41st person beheaded in the kingdom that year, most of them for drug
trafficking.[@1]
