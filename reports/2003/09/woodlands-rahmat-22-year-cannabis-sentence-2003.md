Rahmat bin Abdullah, a 59-year-old taxi driver, and Kalaiselvan Nallathamby, a
45-year-old Malaysian, were originally charged with conspiracy to traffic
1,063 g of cannabis, a capital charge. [@1] The prosecution reduced both
charges, and each man pleaded guilty to trafficking 499.9 g of cannabis and to
consuming cannabis. [@1]

## A rubbish-bin pickup in Woodlands

Rahmat telephoned Kalaiselvan at 9.50 a.m. on 25 January 2003 and asked him to
retrieve a plastic bag of cannabis hidden in a rubbish bin at Blk 728 Woodlands
Circle, which Kalaiselvan unpacked at his nearby flat. [@1] That afternoon the
two drove off in Rahmat's taxi, which Central Narcotics Bureau officers
intercepted at 3.20 p.m. at Woodlands Drive 61; a later search of the flat
found the trafficking drugs in a cupboard, and both men's urine tested positive
for controlled drugs. [@1]

## 22 years each, plus the cane for one

Justice Choo Han Teck sentenced both men to 22 years' imprisonment with effect
from their remand on 27 January 2003, plus one year's jail each on the
consumption charges, all running concurrently. [@1] The second accused received
an additional 15 strokes of the cane on the trafficking charge, while the
59-year-old first accused was spared caning because of his age. [@1]
