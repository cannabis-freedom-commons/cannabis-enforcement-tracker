Kaua'i police seized 3,285 marijuana plants in a three-day eradication effort in uninhabited areas of the island, under the Project Green Harvest program. [@1,2] The operation ended Thursday 18 September 2003, officials said the next day. [@1,2] No arrests were made during the operation. [@1,2] The effort was conducted with the US Drug Enforcement Administration, the Army, the state and all four county police departments. [@1]
