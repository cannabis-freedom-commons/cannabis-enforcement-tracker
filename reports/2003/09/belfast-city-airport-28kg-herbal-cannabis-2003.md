Customs anti-smuggling officers seized around 28 kilos of herbal cannabis at Belfast City Airport on Sunday 7 September 2003. [@1,2] The haul carried an estimated street value of £112,000. [@1]

Edward Lee Earsnshaw, 43, a South African national, appeared at Belfast Magistrates Court on Monday 8 September charged with illegally importing prohibited substances. [@1] He was remanded in custody for four weeks. [@1] Customs senior investigation officer John Whiting said removing the cannabis showed Customs' commitment to the multi-agency fight against drugs.
