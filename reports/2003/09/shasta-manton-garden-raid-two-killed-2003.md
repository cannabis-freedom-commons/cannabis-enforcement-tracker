Two suspected growers were fatally shot by drug agents raiding a large
marijuana garden near Manton in the eastern part of Shasta County early on
Tuesday 16 September 2003. [@1,3] Agents said they were met with gunfire when
they arrived at the garden just before 7 a.m., and the two men died in the
resulting gun battle with 40 to 50 officers conducting the raid. [@3] None of
the officers was injured. [@3]

California's Campaign Against Marijuana Planting (CAMP) said its personnel had
assisted the Shasta County Sheriff's Department with the eradication of 27,262
marijuana plants at the site. [@2] The two suspects were shot and killed when
they assaulted local law enforcement officers, and three further suspects
remained at large after the shooting. [@2] Officers seized three weapons at the
garden: a .22 caliber pistol, a second pistol and a 7.62 mm SKS assault
rifle. [@2]

The state Attorney General said the Shasta and Butte County gardens raided that
week each held between 8,000 and 30,000 plants and were believed to be under
the control of Mexican drug organizations. [@1] Three men had fled the Manton
scene and were being sought in the Manton and Shingletown areas as armed and
dangerous, and the reports name none of the dead or missing men and give no
later arrests or charges from this raid. [@3]
