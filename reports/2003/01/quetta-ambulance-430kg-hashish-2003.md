Police in Quetta, capital of Balochistan province, intercepted an ambulance belonging to a
private non-governmental organisation on Sunday 19 January 2003 and recovered 430 kg of
hashish packets hidden in secret cavities of the vehicle. [@1,2] The consignment had been
smuggled from neighbouring Afghanistan and was heading towards Sindh province in
southeastern Pakistan. [@1,2] Four suspects were apprehended, and police said further
investigations were under way. [@1]

An AFP photograph taken in Quetta that day shows a policeman handing one of the recovered
hashish packets to a colleague. [@2]
