Joint military and anti-narcotics operatives burned marijuana plants worth more than ₱20 million
after uprooting them on Mt Chumanchil, the known plantation in Tinglayan, Kalinga, the previous
week. [@1] Citing regional Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency reports, the account says combined
elements of the Army's 21st Infantry Battalion and narcotics agents uprooted more than 100,000
fully grown plants and destroyed them at the site. [@1]

Senior Inspector Edgar Apalla, officer-in-charge of the PDEA Cordillera office, said no marijuana
cultivator was apprehended during the eradication. [@1] He said arresting cultivators was one of
the operation's objectives but they remained elusive. [@1]

## The wider drive

The account notes the burning followed calls at a recent Luzon-wide drugs summit in Baguio for an
all-out drive against the Cordillera marijuana trade, including criminal charges against growers
and owners of land planted with marijuana. [@1] It describes the Cordillera as the country's top
marijuana source, supplying almost 70 percent of national output. [@1]
