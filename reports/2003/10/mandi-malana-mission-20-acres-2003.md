The anti-narcotics task force of the Narcotics Control Bureau's Chandigarh unit and the
Mandi police destroyed illicitly cultivated cannabis on 20 acres (about 8.1 hectares) in
Gadgusain, Didar, Jhamaj, Bagrath, Shillibaghi and adjoining remote areas between 26 and
30 October 2003, in the last leg of Mission Malana-II. [@1] Ten NCB officials and ten
Mandi police officials, assisted by 30 labourers, carried out the operation in the area
adjoining the Banjar sub-division of Kullu district. [@1]

NCB Superintendent O.P. Sharma and the Mandi district police chief told PTI that the teams
booked eight people for cultivating cannabis on private land under Section 20 of the
Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act. [@1]
