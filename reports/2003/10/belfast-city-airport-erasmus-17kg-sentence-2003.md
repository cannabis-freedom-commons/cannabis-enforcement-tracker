On 10 October 2003 Belfast Crown Court jailed South African Gabriel Jacobus Frederick Erasmus, 49, for two and a half years after he pleaded guilty to attempting to smuggle cannabis through Belfast City Airport. [@1] Customs officers had intercepted him on 20 January on arrival from Johannesburg via London Heathrow. [@1] Inside his suitcase officers found packages glued to the underside of a board hidden beneath the lining, holding almost 17 kilos of compressed herbal cannabis with a street value of 50,000 pounds. [@1] Customs' head of investigation for Northern Ireland Jane Salt said removing the cannabis from the criminal supply chain showed Customs' commitment to the multi-agency fight against drugs. [@1] In sentencing, Judge Burgess said the message must go out that Northern Ireland courts would punish drug smuggling heavily. [@1]

## Remand

Erasmus, then 48, appeared at Belfast Laganside Courts the day after the seizure charged with illegally importing prohibited substances and was remanded in custody until 28 January. [@2] The earlier report put the seizure at around 17 kilos of herbal cannabis with an estimated street value of 53,000 pounds. [@2] Customs' head of anti-smuggling for Northern Ireland Colin McAllister said the seizure showed the same commitment to detecting cannabis smugglers alongside work against Class A drugs. [@2]
