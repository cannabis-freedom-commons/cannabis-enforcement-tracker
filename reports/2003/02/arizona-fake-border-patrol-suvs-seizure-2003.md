Border Patrol and Customs agents stopped two sport utility vehicles, a 2000 Dodge Durango and a 2001 Ford Expedition, painted to resemble Border Patrol units with the agency's logos and painted-on government plates, before dawn near Palominas, about 70 miles southeast of Tucson. [@1,2] Inside, agents found large bundles holding about 1,900 pounds (about 862 kg) of marijuana. [@1,2]

Agents arrested the driver of one vehicle, an undocumented Mexican national held in federal custody pending prosecution on drug smuggling charges, while the second driver fled. [@1,2] Officials said it was the first time traffickers had been caught using fake government vehicles, and presented the joint operation as a preview of cooperation under the new Department of Homeland Security. [@1,2]
