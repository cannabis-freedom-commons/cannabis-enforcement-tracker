Agents of the specialised drug and organised crime unit UDYCO Costa del Sol arrested
Joseph Henry B., a 29-year-old British man, for allegedly shipping hashish to
England. [@1] His company, based in an industrial park in Mijas Costa, exported
high-voltage batteries to Britain through France, and police say the hashish was
hidden in the bottom of those containers. [@1]

Officers grew suspicious because the Mijas warehouse saw little activity yet made
periodic shipments through an international transport company; they intercepted one
container and found 700 kilos of hashish hidden in its base. [@1] Police also seized
two stolen vehicles believed used to carry the drugs to the warehouse, one still
holding the sacks in which the hashish had arrived from Morocco. [@1]

## Investigation continues

Police said they would continue working with Interpol and Europol to identify the
rest of the network, which they believed may also have trafficked stolen heavy
vehicles. [@1]
