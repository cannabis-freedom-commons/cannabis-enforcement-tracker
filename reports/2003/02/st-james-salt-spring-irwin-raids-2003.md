St James police ended two days of raids in Salt Spring and Irwin Heights with
one man shot dead, four men arrested, four guns, about 150 rounds of
ammunition and 192 parcels of marijuana seized. [@1] Police named the dead
man as 19-year-old Roger Haughton of Irwin Heights, killed in an exchange of
gunfire, with a Raikel .380 pistol whose serial number had been erased and a
loaded magazine allegedly taken from the scene. [@1]

The raids began on a Saturday afternoon when armed police with a search
warrant searched a building in the Go Peace area of Salt Spring. [@1] That
search yielded a Glock 26 pistol, an AK47 assault rifle, 57 7.62mm
cartridges, 80 9mm cartridges, nine Smith and Wesson cartridges, a
bullet-proof vest, a suit of army fatigues, 27 packets of compressed ganja,
three buckets holding 15 gallons of hash oil and two motor vehicles. [@1]

After the shooting, police searched another Irwin Heights house, seizing a
home-made shotgun with two 12-gauge cartridges and arresting three occupants,
Livingston Ricketts, 26, Claude Lloyd, 25, and Donavon Hamilton, 25, who were
charged with illegal possession of a firearm. [@1] A further house in Irwin
Heights occupied by Byron O'Conner was searched and 165 parcels of ganja were
allegedly found in a room; O'Conner was arrested and charged with possession
of and dealing in ganja. [@1] The divisional commander, Superintendent Newton
Amos, said he was pleased with the operations and their results. [@1]
