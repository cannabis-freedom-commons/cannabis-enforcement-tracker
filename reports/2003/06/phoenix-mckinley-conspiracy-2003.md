At 6:30 a.m. on 24 June 2003 Phoenix police served five search warrants and arrested four
Arizonans, including Cardinals fullback Dennis McKinley, in the final phase of a conspiracy
investigation into marijuana imported from Mexico. [@1,2] The Arizona Attorney General’s office said
the inquiry had already produced indictments against 34 suspects and seizures of more than 1,500
pounds of marijuana, two tractor-trailers, nine vehicles, eleven guns and about $407,601 in cash.
[@1]

## Warehouse network

Investigators said the group stored marijuana smuggled from Mexico in a Phoenix warehouse, repacked
it and shipped it to New York, Chicago, Detroit, Cleveland and other destinations. [@1,2] The case
began after a August 2002 shootout during a drug robbery in Phoenix; surveillance later tied McKinley
to leasing the warehouse for about $3,000 a month, though police said he was not a suspect in the
shooting. [@1,2] McKinley was booked into Maricopa County jail on conspiracy charges, and the
Cardinals cut him the same day. [@2]

The Attorney General’s release said further searches and arrests were planned in other parts of the
United States; it does not name every defendant beyond the four taken into custody that morning.
[@1]
