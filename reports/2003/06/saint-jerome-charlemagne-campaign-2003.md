Project Charlemagne, a joint RCMP and local police task force targeting indoor hydroponic growers in municipalities north of Montreal, had seized 148,400 cannabis plants since September 2001, RCMP Sergeant Richard Martel said in early June 2003. [@1] The RCMP-led, multi-agency task force works out of its Saint-Jérôme detachment with officers from five local forces, the Sûreté du Québec and Kanesatake Mohawk police, and focuses on illegal marijuana cultivation and production in the area. [@1,2]

Police had announced 23 busts in 2003 alone, and more than 400 people had been arrested overall. [@1] Beyond the plants, the task force had netted 768 pounds of buds, 622 kilograms of shake, 1.5 kilograms of hash and $226,969 in cash. [@1]

## Disputed values

Marijuana Party leader Marc-Boris St-Maurice said police consistently overstate crop values about tenfold, making each grower look worse in court and justifying calls for harsher penalties. [@1]
