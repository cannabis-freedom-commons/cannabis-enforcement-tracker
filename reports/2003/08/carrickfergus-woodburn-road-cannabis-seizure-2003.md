Drug squad officers of the Police Service of Northern Ireland searched a
lorry on Woodburn Road in Carrickfergus, County Antrim, on Tuesday 5 August
2003 and seized cannabis with an estimated street value of £2 million.
[@1] Superintendent George McCauley, head of the PSNI drug squad, called the
find a tremendous success and said it was the single largest seizure of
cannabis ever made in Northern Ireland. [@1] The Northern Ireland minister
Jane Kennedy later confirmed in Parliament that £2 million of cannabis seized
that August was the largest seizure of the drug in Northern Ireland. [@3]

Four men were initially questioned by police over the find. [@1] The next
day Stephen Spence, 28, from Belfast, Stephen Martin, 40, from Carrickfergus,
and Nigel Carter, 36, from Fleetwood in England appeared before Belfast
Magistrate's Court after being arrested in connection with the seizure,
which the court report valued at £2.5 million. [@2] Police believed the
drugs were bound for the hands of loyalist paramilitaries. [@2] All three
were released on bail on condition they report regularly to the PSNI and to
police in Fleetwood. [@2]
