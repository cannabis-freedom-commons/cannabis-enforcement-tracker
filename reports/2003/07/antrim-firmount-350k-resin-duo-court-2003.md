A man and a woman, both aged 30, were arrested after police raided a house at
Firmount Drive in Antrim that weekend. [@1] Officers found cannabis resin worth
an estimated £350,000. [@1]

The pair were expected to appear in Coleraine Magistrates Court on 28 July 2003
charged with possession of Class B drugs and possession with intent to
supply. [@1] Two other people arrested in the same operation were released
pending a written report. [@1]
