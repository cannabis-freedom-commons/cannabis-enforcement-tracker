Big Island police destroyed 8,092 marijuana plants, from seedlings to 12 feet
tall, in a five-day eradication effort that ended Saturday 19 July 2003 in
Hamakua, Puna, North Hilo and South Hilo. [@1,2] No arrests were made in the
sweep. [@1,2]

## Mountain View search

Officers executing a search warrant at a home in Mountain View, Puna, seized
1,103 more plants, from seedlings to six feet tall; no arrest was made and the
investigation continued. [@1,2] Honolulu and Maui police officers, the Hawaii
National Guard and the United States Drug Enforcement Administration joined Big
Island officers in the operation. [@1,2]
