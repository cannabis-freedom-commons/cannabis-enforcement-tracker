National Police forces found 1,300 kilos of hashish hidden in jars of olives in a
Marbella warehouse. [@1,2] The discovery was made by Nerón, a rottweiler trained to
sniff out drugs, and the cargo was destined for delivery to the UK. [@1,2]

In a joint operation, police from Madrid and Marbella found that the hashish was
being bought in Morocco, stored in Marbella, packaged as olives and forwarded to
Madrid for future shipment to Britain. [@1,2] The investigation led to the arrest
of Britons Ciaran S., Simon H. and Bernard C., along with Humberto C. [@1,2]
