Officers from Customs and Excise and Staffordshire Police, taking part in Britain's biggest
anti-VAT-fraud swoop, found cannabis resin hidden in a lorry at Blythe Business Park in
Staffordshire. [@1] The discovery came during one of 70 raids on premises across the country
and at properties in Spain, carried out by 350 officers against an estimated £120 million
mobile-phone VAT fraud. [@1,2]

## Three arrests

Thirty-three men and six women were arrested across the operation as a whole. [@1] At the
Blythe lorry, three men were arrested, and a 32-year-old appeared at Newcastle magistrates
court charged with supplying drugs. [@1] The later account puts the haul at two tonnes of
resin worth £12 million; earlier same-week reports had put it at one tonne worth about
£5 million. [@1,2]
