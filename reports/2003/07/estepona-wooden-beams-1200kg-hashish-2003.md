Police raided a warehouse in the Martín Méndez industrial estate in Estepona used
by a gang of British drug-runners, chosen for its proximity to the motorway and to
the local beach where the drugs were landed in bales. [@1,2] The gang used
hollowed-out wooden beams to transport the drugs to the UK. [@1,2]

Officers found 1,200 kilos of hashish at the site, along with enough prepared
beams to move the cargo. [@1,2] They arrested three Britons, one a 16-year-old
minor. [@1,2]
