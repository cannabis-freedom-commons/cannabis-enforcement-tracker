Joseph Michael Tully, 63, of Granemore Road in Keady appeared before Armagh
Magistrates Court on 7 April 2003 charged with possessing cannabis and possessing
it with intent to supply. [@1] The charges followed the discovery of cannabis
resin worth an estimated £750,000. [@1]

Drugs squad officers and uniformed police uncovered the haul during the search of
a vehicle in south Armagh on Sunday 6 April. [@1] The brief reports no later
outcome of the case. [@1]
