A High Court found Indonesian labourer Parlan Dadeh, 33, guilty of trafficking in 436.2 grams of cannabis and sentenced him to death on 29 April 2003. [@1,2] He had been charged over cannabis found in a black plastic bag tucked inside his jeans in front of a restaurant in Jalan Tok Kangar, Juru, Central Seberang Prai, on 8 November 2000. [@1,2]

At the appeal, his counsel argued Parlan might not have known the concealed packet held drugs, while the prosecution said he must have known what was hidden at his waist. [@1,2] Court of Appeal judges Gopal Sri Ram, Mohd Ghazali Mohd Yusoff and Zulkefli Ahmad Makinudin held that only Parlan could have placed the bag inside his jeans and that the trial judge had been right to find he knew its contents. [@1,2] The court dismissed his appeal against conviction and sentence, leaving him an automatic appeal to the Federal Court. [@1,2]
