A Malaysian court sentenced Indonesian labourer Ruslan Bendadeh, 28, to
death on 29 April 2003 for trafficking 436.2 grams of marijuana. [@1]
Bendadeh was convicted of trying to distribute the drug at a food outlet in
Penang on 8 November 2000. [@1] High Court Commissioner Su Geok Yiam said
she could impose no lighter sentence because the death penalty was
mandatory for the offence, but she allowed the sentence to be put on hold
while Bendadeh appealed. [@1]
