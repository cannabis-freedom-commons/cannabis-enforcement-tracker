Police arrested a man after seizing cannabis worth more than £25,000 during a
search of a house on the Ballymena Road in Ballymena. [@1] Officers found more
than thirty cannabis plants and 0.5 kg of cannabis resin in the house. [@1]

Officers also found a handgun and equipment used for growing cannabis plants
during the search. [@1] The 4ni.co.uk item reporting the search is dated 30
April 2003 and gives no separate date for it. [@1]
