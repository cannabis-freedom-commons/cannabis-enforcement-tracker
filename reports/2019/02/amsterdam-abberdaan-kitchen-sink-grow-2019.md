Police in Amsterdam found about 1,000 cannabis plants through a cupboard under a
kitchen sink at a property in the western port region. [@1] The Nieuw West
police division shared the find on its Facebook page on the Thursday before the
report, saying one suspect had been arrested and investigations were
continuing. [@1,2]

The grow sat inside a commercial building on the Abberdaan. [@1,2]
