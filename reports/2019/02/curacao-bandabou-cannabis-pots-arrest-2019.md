In the early morning of Saturday 23 February 2019, the Rescue and Coordination
Center of the Coast Guard spotted a suspicious vessel southwest of Curaçao,
sailing from the south towards the coast. [@1] The centre ordered the Coast
Guard helicopter and land patrol to the area and alerted the police and customs.
[@1] Once there, the units detained 12 undocumented people of Venezuelan origin,
11 men and one woman. [@1] Officers found a firearm that may belong to one of
the detained people. [@1]

During the intensive search, officers came across an area with many cannabis
plants. [@1] The plants each stood in a pot on the site. [@1] A person was
arrested in connection with the cannabis plantation but has since been released.
[@1] The KPC, Curaçao's police corps, is investigating both cases. [@1]
