Takeo provincial police were hunting the growers of more than 1,000 cannabis
plants on six fields in Chroy village in Preah Bat Chonchum commune, Kiri Vong
district. [@1] The account was given by Kiri Vong district deputy police chief
Hiem Srim. [@1] The growers had escaped and Srim said he did not know how many
of them there were. [@1] Srim said a team of experts was investigating and
searching for the suspects so they could be punished accordingly. [@1] Commune
police chief In Savuth said he did not take part in the bust. [@1]

Provincial and district police raided the fields on the preceding Sunday after
receiving reports from villagers. [@1] The six fields totalled at least 979sqm.
[@1] Officers destroyed 1,398 marijuana plants and one water storage during the
raid. [@1]

Srim said the hunt was launched because the suspects would not stop planting
cannabis around the radio tower in Chroy village even after an earlier
crackdown. [@1]
