Federal Police officers from the Regional Security and Federal Forces divisions
located and destroyed about 54 tonnes of suspected marijuana grown on a
plantation in the municipality of Álamos, Sonora. [@1] Desk and field
investigations and tactical overflights led the officers to the crop near the
ejido El Cajón del Sabino, where they deployed an operation on the ground. [@1]
The officers drove about 40 kilometres along a dirt track to reach an irregular
plot about 150 metres long and 60 metres wide, roughly 9,000 square metres in
area, planted with green plants with the characteristics of marijuana and fitted
with an irrigation system. [@1]

The federal officers cut the plants by hand and then burned about 135,000 of
them. [@1] After destroying the crop, the officers took representative samples
and handed them to the competent authority to continue the investigation. [@1]
The account is the Security Secretariat's bulletin 124/19, published as a
statement on 7 February 2019, and it names no arrests. [@1]

Quadratín México republished the same bulletin on 7 February 2019, with the 54
tonnes, the 9,000 square metres and the 135,000 plants. [@2]
