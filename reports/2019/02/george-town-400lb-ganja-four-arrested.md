Four men were arrested after police found a significant quantity of suspected
ganja in George Town in the early hours of 14 February 2019. [@1] Shortly after
5:00am on 14 February, officers on patrol in the capital saw a Honda vehicle
speeding along Walkers Road. [@1] The driver ignored police signals to stop and
continued racing along the road. [@1] Shortly afterwards, officers found the
vehicle empty on Rock Hole Road, with its occupants having fled. [@1]

Officers then searched the vehicle and the surrounding area. [@1] The search
recovered several large garbage bags nearby. [@1] Those bags contained suspected
ganja, estimated at over 400 pounds (about 181.4 kg). [@1]

Three of the men were from George Town and were aged 26, 30 and 41. [@1] The
fourth man, aged 31, was from Bodden Town. [@1] All four were arrested on
suspicion of being concerned in the importation of ganja. [@1] They remained in
custody as investigations continued, police said. [@1]
