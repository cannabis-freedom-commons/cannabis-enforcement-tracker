Zambia's Drug Enforcement Commission arrested four small-scale farmers in
North-Western and Luapula provinces for unlawful cultivation of cannabis plants.
[@2] Daniel Mwemba, 51, a small-scale farmer of the Shafulundu area in Solwezi,
was arrested for cultivating cannabis plants weighing 295 kg. [@2] Mwemba was
jointly charged with David Lewila, 47, of the same abode for unlawful
cultivation of 408 kg of cannabis concealed in maize and potato fields. [@2]
Bupe Ngandwe, 42, a small-scale farmer of Paison Village in Luapula Province,
was arrested for unlawful cultivation of 410 kg of fresh cannabis plants. [@2]
Christopher Mwewa, 45, a small-scale farmer of Kolala Village in North-Western
Province, was arrested for unlawful cultivation of 115 kg of fresh cannabis
plants. [@2]

The Solwezi cannabis was concealed in maize and potato fields, and Mwewa's crop
was inter-cropped with maize. [@1] The Commission put the four farmers' combined
cannabis at over 1,225 kg. [@2] DEC deputy public relations officer Kamufisa
Manchishi disclosed the Solwezi arrests in a statement. [@1]

Drug Enforcement Commission spokesperson Theresa Katongo announced the four
arrests. [@2] All the suspects were in police custody and were expected to
appear in court soon. [@2]
