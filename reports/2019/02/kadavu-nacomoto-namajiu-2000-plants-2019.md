Fiji police uprooted more than 2,000 plants believed to be marijuana from
various farms on Kadavu as teams began revisiting known cultivation locations.
[@1] The farms were located at the villages of Nacomoto, Namajiu, Mataso and
Rakiraki. [@1]

Police spokesperson Ana Naisoro said the raid followed information received from
members of the public. [@1] The police vessel M.V. Veiqaravi was deployed to
Kadavu the day before the 10 February 2019 report to transport the plants to
Suva for analysis. [@1] The owners of the farms were known to police and
investigations were continuing. [@1]

Police separately confirmed two other drug-related arrests, with one person
remaining in custody pending investigations and the other charged with
possession of illicit drugs. [@1]
