Police raided the home of a 50-year-old suspect in Drauniivi village in Ra in
the early morning of 8 February 2019. [@1] Officers from the Western Division
Taskforce and the K9 Unit, the force's dog unit, carried out the raid. [@1]

Officers seized more than 200 rolls of dried leaves wrapped in aluminium foil
believed to be marijuana. [@1] The rolls were allegedly found stashed in a
bucket and buried underground, police spokesperson Ana Naisoro said. [@1] Police
also found $4,000 in cash during the raid. [@1] Fijivillage reported that over
200 rolls of dried leaves believed to be marijuana and the $4,000 cash were
seized at the home of a 50-year-old man in the village. [@2]

Police investigations were continuing, with no charge or court outcome reported.
[@1] The same Fijivillage report separately described a raid on a farm at
Dakunikoro, Pacific Harbour, where officers seized more than 30 plants believed
to be marijuana, a different event left out of this report. [@2]
