Fiji police uprooted 476 plants believed to be marijuana from separate farms on
Kadavu over the two days before 7 February 2019. [@1] Police said 325 of the
plants came from a farm at Nacomoto Village. [@1] The other 151 plants were
uprooted from a farm near Namajiu Village. [@1]

The farms' owners had not been arrested when police issued the report. [@1]
