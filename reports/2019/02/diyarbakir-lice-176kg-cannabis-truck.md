The Diyarbakır provincial gendarmerie's anti-smuggling and organised crime unit
stopped a truck on the Lice–Diyarbakır highway on 18 February 2019. [@1] Four
people were taken into custody in the operation. [@1] The unit acted on
intelligence that a suspect had rented a vehicle to send drugs to Gaziantep.
[@1] Officers had identified the truck's plate before the stop. [@1] A narcotics
dog alerted during a search at the roadside, and the truck was taken to the
provincial gendarmerie command for a detailed inspection. [@1]

Hidden among hay bales loaded in the truck, officers found 176 kg 340 g of kubar
esrar, a cannabis product. [@1] The Diyarbakır governor's office said the drug
was packed ready for sale. [@3] It said the cannabis was seized and held on the
public prosecutor's instructions. [@3]

The governor's office said the operation was part of judicial and administrative
work to protect public order, which it said would continue without interruption.
[@3] The names of the four suspects were not released. [@2]
