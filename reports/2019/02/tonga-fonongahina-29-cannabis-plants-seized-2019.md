Police seized 29 cannabis plants from a bush allotment at Fonongahina, near
Sia'atoutai on Tongatapu, on 12 February 2019. [@1]

The police Drugs Enforcement Taskforce made the seizure at around 10:00pm. [@2]

No suspect had been arrested, and police said the investigation continued. [@2]

Deputy Commissioner Pelenatita Vaisuai said police were committed to disrupting
and detecting the supply of drugs, and to holding those responsible to account.
[@2]

Police asked anyone with information about drug offending to contact the nearest
police station or ring 23417 or 922. [@2]
