Lithuanian police announced on 13 February 2019 that they had seized 1.5 tons of hashish worth around 20 million euros, the largest seizure in the country's history. [@1,2] The cargo, hidden in a truck, was intercepted in a joint operation of the criminal police and prosecutors near the Lithuanian and Latvian border after a long, secret investigation into an international trafficking network. [@1]

Police chief Linas Pernavas said the hashish of Moroccan origin had come through Spain and was bound for Russia. [@1,2] Officers arrested two Lithuanian citizens and one Belarusian, aged 40 to 50, and said more suspects could face 10 to 15 years in prison. [@1] One of the suspects owned a local cargo company; police described Lithuania as a transit country for narcotics moving from Morocco, Britain and the Netherlands toward Moscow and St Petersburg. [@1,2]
