A 49-year-old businessman of St. John known to produce coconut oil was charged
after 48 pounds of marijuana were found concealed in bags of coconut that had
arrived at St. George's Port from St. Vincent & The Grenadines. [@1] Jeremiah
Harris of Black Bay was arrested the previous Friday and slapped with three
drug-related charges connected with the marijuana, which police said had an
estimated street value of $108,864. [@1]

Police apprehended Harris at his home in Black Bay and confiscated a smaller
quantity of marijuana there. [@1] Chief Magistrate Tamara Gill granted Harris
bail in the sum of $80,000 with two sureties and special conditions. [@1] The
court ordered Harris to report to the Grand Roy Police Station every Monday and
Friday between 6:00 a.m. and 6:00 p.m., to surrender all travel documents, and
to request court permission to leave the jurisdiction. [@1] Harris was due to
reappear in court on 6 September. [@1]
