Nicaragua's National Police said it arrested 52 people for high-danger crimes
across the country in the week from Monday 10 to Sunday 16 June 2019. [@1] The
police's own statement says the same figures were reported under the country's
security plan for the previous week. [@2] General commissioner Sergio Gutiérrez,
second chief of the Managua police, presented the results. [@2]

The detainees were also counted as 25 people held for robbery with intimidation,
one for murder, four for homicide and 11 as leaders of criminal groups. [@1] Six
alleged drug suppliers in the capital, Lester Merlo, Eliud Noguera, Carlos
Reyes, Francisco Guevara, Jairo Guido and William Bustillo, were found with 15
pounds (about 6.8 kg) of marijuana. [@2] Separately, María Auxiliadora Calero,
42, was arrested in Matagalpa with 12 pounds (about 5.4 kg) of marijuana. [@2]
Officers also seized 3 pounds (about 1.4 kg) of marijuana and a motorcycle from
Francisco Zeledón Ortega in San José de Bocay, Jinotega. [@2]

Across the arrests, police reported seizing 39 pounds (about 17.7 kg) of
marijuana, 20 firearms, three motorcycles, one car and three pickups. [@1] Canal
4 reported the same 52 detentions and 39 pounds of marijuana from the 17 June
presentation. [@3]

Among the arrests was Germán Aurelio Gutiérrez Zamora, 33, held for a murder
committed in La Libertad, Chontales, on 16 June 2010. [@2] Police said José
Alberto Orozco Rostrán, 26, Eduardo Javier Canales, 32, and Henry Martínez
Fuerte, known as El Mono, carried out a homicide in the Las Torres neighbourhood
of Managua with a home-made firearm. [@2] Gustavo Adolfo Jaén Delgado, 44, and
Christian Adolfo Jaén Delgado, 39, were held for robbery with intimidation in
Masaya, with a Makarov pistol and a car. [@2]
