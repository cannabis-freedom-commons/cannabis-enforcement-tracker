An Afghan appeal court sentenced 30 drug smugglers to between one and 16 years
in prison. [@1] The Counter-Narcotics Justice Center (CNJC), the centre that
houses Afghanistan's special narcotics courts, said in a statement that the 30
had been charged in 25 drug-smuggling cases. [@1] The CNJC said a primary court
had convicted the smugglers before the appeal court passed sentence. [@1] The
cases against the 30 involved 427 kilograms of hashish, the cannabis component
recorded here. [@1] The same cases also involved more than 24 kilograms of
heroin, more than 29 kilograms of morphine, more than 17 kilograms of other
drugs and 823 kilograms of tablet-K, a synthetic opioid tablet. [@1]
