Vincentian police held three people, including a Grenadian, in connection with
the seizure of 400 lbs of marijuana. [@1] The three were 26-year-old Grenadian
Javed Travis Chetram of Lagoon Road in St. George, 32-year-old Vincentian
national Key-Roy Davito Edwards and British national Kevin William Slauter. [@1]
Members of the St. Vincent Coast Guard apprehended the men on a Wednesday night
of the previous week in Vincentian waters. [@1]

Law-enforcement officers intercepted a yacht belonging to the British national
in St. Vincent and found the drugs on board, although the material still had to
be tested. [@1] The seizure took place at sea in Vincentian waters rather than
during a land raid. [@1] The reported 400 lbs of marijuana is equivalent to
about 181.4 kg. [@1]

The three suspects were expected to appear in a Vincentian court that week to
face drug-related charges. [@1] Chetram was described as already known to law
enforcement because of previous encounters over similar offences, while no plea
or outcome in this case was reported. [@1]
