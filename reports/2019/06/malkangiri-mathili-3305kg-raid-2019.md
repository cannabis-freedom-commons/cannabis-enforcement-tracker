A 75-member police team led by Sub-divisional Police Officer Risikesh Khilari intercepted ganja traffickers in the forests of Mathili police station area in Malkangiri district. [@1,2] The raid began on the night of 6 June and ran into the early hours of 7 June 2019, netting 3,305 kg of ganja and 34 arrests. [@1,2]

## A second night in the forest

The night before, about 100 traffickers had escaped after a scuffle with officers who raided the forests on a tip-off, leaving one officer injured. [@1] Of the 34 arrested, 17 were from Malkangiri, 11 from Nabarangpur, five from Koraput and one from Chhattisgarh, where police suspect the consignment was headed. [@1,2]
