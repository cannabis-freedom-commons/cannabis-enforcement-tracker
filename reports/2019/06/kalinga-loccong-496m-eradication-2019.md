Joint operatives of the national police and the Philippine Drug Enforcement
Agency destroyed 240,500 fully grown marijuana plants in Tinglayan, Kalinga,
in a three-day operation from 27 to 29 June 2019. [@1] The eradication,
covered by Oplan Green Pearl Charlie, targeted drug-affected barangays in
Tinglayan, particularly Barangay Loccong. [@1]

The plants covered 14,200 square metres across nine plantation sites and
were valued at about 49.6 million pesos, police Drug Enforcement Group
director Brig. Gen. Ignatius Ferro said. [@1] No cultivator was arrested
during the operation, but Ferro said troops would keep monitoring the area.
[@1] He said the operation warned growers to stop and reminded Loccong's
anti-drug council to strengthen its campaign, noting that cultivation is
banned under the Comprehensive Dangerous Drugs Act of 2002. [@1]
