Cape Verde's Polícia Judiciária detained a man in São Salvador do Mundo, on
Santiago island, on Friday 31 May 2019. [@1] The detainee was 58 years old and
lived in the locality of Pico Acima. [@1] Officers of SCITE, the Central Section
for Investigation of Drug Trafficking, made the detention while executing a
search-and-seizure warrant. [@1] The suspect was brought before the competent
judicial authorities on Saturday 1 June for a first judicial interrogation and a
decision on personal coercive measures. [@1] The court ordered him held in
preventive detention, the pretrial custody measure. [@1]

In the operation officers seized 162 kg of cannabis and a firearm. [@1] He was
found in possession of the cannabis and the firearm at the time of the
detention. [@1] A photograph credited to the Polícia Judiciária showed the
seized cannabis stored in drums. [@1]

The Polícia Judiciária announced the detention in a press note issued on a
Tuesday. [@1] SCITE's full Portuguese name is Secção Central de Investigação de
Tráfico de Estupefacientes, identifying the case as a drug-trafficking
investigation. [@1]
