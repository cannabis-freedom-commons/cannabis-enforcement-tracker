Police on patrol in George Town shortly after 1:30am on Monday 3 June 2019
approached three men behaving suspiciously at a parking lot off Shedden Road.
[@2] When the officers reached the men they smelled a strong scent of ganja
coming from their direction. [@2] A search of the men found small portions of
ganja on two of them. [@2]

Officers searched the car the men were standing next to under the Misuse of
Drugs Law. [@2] Several packets of ganja and drug paraphernalia were found
inside the car. [@2] The car's owner, a 35-year-old man from East End, was
arrested on suspicion of possession of ganja with intent to supply and other
related offences. [@2]

The other two men, aged 34 and 21 and both from West Bay, were arrested on
suspicion of possession and consumption of ganja. [@2] Cayman News Service
reported the two West Bay men as arrested for possession of ganja. [@1] Police
said the matter was under investigation. [@2]
