Makawanpur police arrested two people on the morning of Friday 14 June 2019 in
possession of 54 kilograms of marijuana in Hetauda Sub-Metropolitan City-15,
Makawanpur district. [@1] The two were identified as Rohit Shrestha, 28, of
Simara-2 in Bara district, and Sanjay Mahato, 44, of Raxaul in India. [@1]

A police team led by Police Inspector Arun Kumar Kuswaha intercepted the truck,
Na 3 Kha 5340, on its way to Pathlaiya from Hetauda, where the two were
travelling with the drug. [@1] Makawanpur District Police Superintendent Mukesh
Kumar Singh said the marijuana was found in 27 packets inside two sacks hidden
in the vehicle. [@1]

Police said the truck was detained and the marijuana seized for further
investigation. [@1]
