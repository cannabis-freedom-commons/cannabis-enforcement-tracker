The operation led to the arrest of three suspects described as having criminal
records, and bladed weapons were seized. [@1] One of the suspects was the
subject of a national wanted notice for organizing illegal immigration and human
trafficking operations. [@1]

The shipment weighed more than twelve tonnes of cannabis. [@1] Moroccan police
announced the seizure of a hashish shipment in the Nador region in the northeast
of the country. [@1] Police said the drugs were being transported aboard a truck
carrying a false registration plate. [@1] The seizure was described as one of
the biggest seizures of the year. [@1]

Morocco is one of the world's main producers and exporters of cannabis resin,
according to the UN Office on Drugs and Crime. [@1]
