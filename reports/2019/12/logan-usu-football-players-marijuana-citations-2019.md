Logan City police went to Millennial Towers Student Housing, at 657 E. 1000
North just west of Maverik Stadium, at 11:32 p.m. on 14 December 2019 after a
call about several people smoking marijuana in a student flat. [@2] Officers
could smell marijuana and found several people smoking in a room, and a seventh
person was found hiding in a nearby bedroom. [@2] Police found no drugs or drug
paraphernalia at the scene, but the smell gave probable cause; the people
refused consent to a urine test until officers obtained a warrant, and the tests
showed marijuana in their systems. [@3]

All were cited and released at the scene, with none arrested. [@2] Quarterback
Jordan Love, wide receiver Sean Carter, running back Gerold Bright and track
athlete Elani Rice were charged on Tuesday, 17 December 2019 with possession of
a controlled substance, marijuana or spice, a class B misdemeanor. [@1] The
fourth person cited was Elani Rice, an athlete on the university's track and
field team, who was also cited for underage alcohol possession. [@2] Utah State
University said citations had been issued and that its student conduct and
athletics procedures would be followed. [@1]

All four were cited on 17 December and their arraignment was set for 30
December. [@4] A lawyer for Love said initial reports were wrong to say he had
been arrested, and that no marijuana or other illegal substances were recovered
at the scene. [@3]
