Karshma "Buju" Richards spent the weekend in police custody before appearing
before a Roseau magistrate on 2 December 2019. [@1] He was charged with
possession and cultivation of 288 marijuana trees. [@1]

He pleaded not guilty to the charge. [@1] The magistrate granted him EC$12,000
bail. [@1] The matter was adjourned to 16 March 2020. [@1]

Richards had come to public prominence in the days before his arrest through his
criticism of the Skerrit-led Dominica Labour Party administration. [@1]
