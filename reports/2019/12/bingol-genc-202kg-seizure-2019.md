Turkish security forces seized 202 kilograms (445 pounds) of marijuana in the
country's east on Dec. 31, according to the provincial governor's office. [@1]
The drugs were recovered from a stream bed in Genç in Bingöl province. [@1]
Officers conducted the seizure as a sniffer dog-aided operation. [@1] The
dispatch was credited to Anadolu Agency in Bingöl. [@1]

A statement said the drugs were seized in the stream-bed operation. [@1] It
added that the fight against illicit drugs would continue across the province.
[@1] The governor's office was cited as the authority for the seizure account.
[@1]

The same account said Turkey had confiscated over 40 tons of marijuana and
seized about 40 million cannabis roots in its anti-drug efforts this year. [@1]
The seizure was also described in rounded terms as more than 200 kg of marijuana
in eastern Turkey. [@1] The pounds equivalent of the seizure was given as 445
pounds. [@1]
