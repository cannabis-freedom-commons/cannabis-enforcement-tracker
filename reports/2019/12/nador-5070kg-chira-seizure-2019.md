Judicial police in Nador seized five tonnes and 70 kilograms of chira early on
Thursday morning. [@2] The resin was wrapped in 169 packages. [@2] The police
said they had aborted a large drug-trafficking attempt that Thursday. [@1] The
driver fled, and searches were under way to identify and arrest him. [@3]

The DGSN said the chira was concealed in a vehicle. [@1] Officers took the load
from a Moroccan-registered utility vehicle on the Rocade de Nador, the city's
ring road, after a pursuit by the judicial police. [@3] A communiqué account
described the drug as cannabis resin and placed the city in north-eastern
Morocco, dating the seizure Thursday 5 December. [@3]

A judicial investigation was opened under the competent prosecutor to identify
accomplices and any national or international ramifications. [@2] The DGSN
presented the operation as part of its efforts against criminal networks
involved in international drug trafficking. [@3] The DGSN said its services were
continuing their fight against drug traffickers in the north. [@1]
