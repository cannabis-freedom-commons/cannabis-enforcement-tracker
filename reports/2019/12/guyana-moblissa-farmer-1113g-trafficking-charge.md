Michael Melville, a farmer in Moblissa on the Linden Soesdyke Highway, was
arrested when police found marijuana at his home on 7 December 2019. [@2] He was
charged with possession of 1,113 grams of marijuana for the purpose of
trafficking. [@2]

Police went to the home after receiving information that he was involved in
trafficking. [@1] While the marijuana was not found in the house, it was
discovered in the yard hidden under a zink sheet. [@1] Stabroek News reported
that the cannabis was found wrapped in a sheet in a black bag at the back of the
yard. [@3] Melville denied having knowledge of the marijuana and said his friend
had been left to look after the house. [@2]

The prosecutor objected to bail on the grounds of the prevalence of the offence
and the penalty the charge attracts. [@2] The Chief Magistrate released him on
GYD 200,000 bail and required him to report to the Amelia’s Ward Police Station
every Friday. [@2] He was instructed to make his next court appearance on 16
January 2020. [@2]
