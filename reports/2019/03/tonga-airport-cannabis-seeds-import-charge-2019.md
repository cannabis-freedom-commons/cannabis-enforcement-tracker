Police said that Tonga's Drug Enforcement Taskforce charged a 34-year-old man
from Tofoa over cannabis seeds brought into the country at Fua'amotu
International Airport on 30 March 2019. [@1] Quarantine officers intercepted
more than 242 grams of cannabis seeds. [@2] Police did not reveal the flight or
the country of origin. [@1]

Deputy Police Commissioner Pelenatita Fe'ao Vaisuai said a search warrant was
executed at the accused's home in Tofoa. [@2] Officers allegedly seized over 85
grams of cannabis liquid there, along with an unlicensed firearm and ammunition.
[@2] He was charged with unlawfully possessing and importing illicit drugs,
possessing a firearm and ammunition without a licence, and a false declaration
on his arrival card. [@2]

The man was due to appear at the Magistrate's Court in Nuku'alofa on 29 April
2019. [@2]
