Four people tried to bring a boatload of marijuana into the Dominican Republic
near Cabo Pequeño and fled when two Dominican Navy units moved towards them.
[@1] The individuals fled by abandoning their boat after being spotted by two
naval units of the Dominican Navy heading for them. [@1] The Dominican Navy
seized 645 kilograms of marijuana, about 1,422 pounds, off Cabo Pequeño near
Isla Beata. [@1] Diario Libre reported that the bales were aboard a
foreign-registered boat of about 25 feet that the crew abandoned after naval
units chased them. [@2] Diario Libre reported that the load was contained in 27
sacks and weighed about 1,422 pounds. [@3] Diario Libre's account describes the
marijuana as presumed, without a laboratory confirmation being reported. [@2]

Naval and land units cordoned off the area to search for the four people, who
fled after noticing the soldiers. [@2] The navy handed the marijuana and other
evidence to the National Directorate of Drug Control (DNCD) and to
representatives of the Public Ministry of Pedernales province. [@4] The
marijuana was transferred to a naval unit and taken to Santo Domingo, and the
boat was towed to the naval base on Beata Island. [@1]

The navy said the pursuit was part of security measures ordered by the general
commander of the navy during the reinforcement of the maritime border. [@3] The
interception followed the increased presence of armed forces along Haiti's land
and sea border with the Dominican Republic against smuggling, drug trafficking,
firearms and illegal Haitian migrants. [@1] The seizure was the fifth the
Dominican Navy had carried out since the start of 2019 in support of the DNCD.
[@1]
