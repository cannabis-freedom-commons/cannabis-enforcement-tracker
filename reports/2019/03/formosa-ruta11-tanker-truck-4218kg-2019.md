Gendarmería Nacional Argentina seized 4,218 kilos 112 grams of marijuana hidden
in a tanker truck in Formosa province, and one person was detained. [@1]
Officers of the Puerto Velaz section stopped the tanker during checks on
National Route 11 at kilometre 1,103; the truck had come from Formosa and was
bound for the city of Reconquista in Santa Fe. [@1]

The gendarmes noticed that the screws holding the hatches on top of the tanker
had recently been changed. [@1] With authorisation from Federal Court No. 2 of
Formosa, under Judge Fernando Carabajal, and in front of witnesses, officers
took the vehicle to the headquarters of Squadron 15 Bajo Paraguay and opened the
hatches. [@1] Inside they found 5,085 rectangular packages wrapped in packing
tape. [@1] Field Narcotest tests by criminalistics and forensic staff were
positive for marijuana, with a total weight of 4,218 kilos 112 grams. [@1]

The judge ordered the drug seized and the driver detained; the release says the
driver came from Santa Fe province. [@1]
