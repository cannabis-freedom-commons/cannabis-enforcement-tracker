Customs officers held a Maldives national at Cochin International Airport at
Nedumbassery with hashish on Tuesday 5 March 2019. [@2] The arrested man was
identified as 32-year-old Mohammed Sobah of GA Vilingili in the Maldives. [@2]
Sobah had arrived in Kochi three days earlier and was stopped as he was due to
depart for Male on Indigo 1787. [@2] Airport screening flagged the checked-in
bags, a dog squad was used to sniff out the drugs, and physical checking found
hashish wrapped in plastic inside a false cavity in the bag. [@2]

Officers weighed the hashish at 3.120 kg. [@2] The New Indian Express estimated
the haul was worth around Rs 10 crore in the international market. [@2] Maldives
Independent put the estimated street value at more than US$1 million. [@1]
Customs registered a Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act case and
referred further investigation to the Narcotics Control Bureau. [@2]

Investigators said Sobah planned to divert the hashish to Bahrain from the
Maldives. [@2] From initial interrogation, officers suspected he was part of a
drug cartel supplying banned narcotic products to Middle East countries. [@2]
Indian police accounts cited in the coverage described the Maldives and Sri
Lanka as transit hubs used to smuggle hashish oil onward to Europe and Latin
America. [@1]
