Federal Police officers found and burned three cannabis plantations in the
municipality of Álamos, in the mountains of Sonora, and about 357,500 marijuana
plants were destroyed by incineration on them. [@1]

The three cannabis plots covered an area of about 24,500 square metres. [@1] The
incinerated cannabis plants weighed an estimated 88,500 kilograms, the
equivalent of the 88 tonnes the police announced. [@1] While inspecting the
area, the officers found another plot of about 5,000 square metres holding some
40,000 poppy plants, which they destroyed by beating them down (vareo). [@1]

The operation followed desk and field investigative work and tactical
overflights, after which officers moved in overland into the sierra. [@1]

Officers took representative samples for investigation and handed them to the
federal public prosecutor's office, and the bulletin names no arrests. [@1]

The federal Security Secretariat published the account as bulletin 203/19 on 9
March 2019. [@1]
