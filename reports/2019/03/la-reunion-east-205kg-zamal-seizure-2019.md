A man in his fifties, identified as the supplier of a drug network operating in
the east of La Réunion, was arrested on 7 March 2019 and placed in police
custody. [@1]

The search of his home found 200 zamal plants, a total of 205 kilograms of
cannabis, and dried cannabis branches prepared for sale. [@1] The police
estimated the value of the seized drugs at 200,000 euros. [@1]
