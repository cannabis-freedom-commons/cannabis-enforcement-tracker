Joint troops of the Tinglayan municipal police, its drug enforcement unit, the
Provincial Mobile Force Company of the Kalinga police and agents of the
Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency in Cordillera held a three-day marijuana
eradication in Barangay Loccong, Tinglayan, from 13 to 15 March 2019. [@1] Run
under Oplan Linis Barangay VIII, the operation found five plantation sites
covering 5,650 square metres, and uprooted and burned on site 107,600 fully
grown marijuana plants. [@1] The police valued the destroyed plants at 21.52
million pesos. [@1]

## Fire in the reforestation site

The environment department of Cordillera later said the March burning in
Loccong involved 70 million pesos worth of uprooted plants, and appealed to
anti-drug teams to protect trees when they set plantations ablaze. [@2] Its
regional director said the fire spread about 100 metres to a pine reforestation
site under the national greening programme, razing 23 hectares with about 1,000
saplings, and that residents needed about two days to put it out. [@2]
