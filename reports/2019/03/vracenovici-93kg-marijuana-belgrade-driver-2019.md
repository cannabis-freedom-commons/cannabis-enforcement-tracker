Border police officers from Nikšić and customs officers at the Vraćenovići
crossing stopped a Mercedes ML270 jeep with Republic of Serbia plates towing a
trailer around 7 pm on 14 March 2019, as it left Montenegro. [@2] During the
check, using a videoscope, they found 91 PVC packages of dry plant matter
suspected to be marijuana, with a total gross weight of about 93 kg, hidden in
compartments in the trailer floor. [@1] The police arrested Jovan Božić (33)
from Belgrade, who was driving the vehicle, on suspicion of unauthorized
production, possession and distribution of narcotic drugs. [@1]

After the scene investigation, police seized the drugs, the jeep and trailer,
and 1,500 euros found with him that they suspected was earned through crime.
[@1] Police said they would bring him, with a criminal complaint, before the
state prosecutor at the Higher State Prosecution in Podgorica. [@2] The Police
Administration and the Customs Administration described it as the largest drug
seizure at the Vraćenovići crossing in the preceding period. [@1]
