A 20-year-old man was arrested in Fayette County, Texas, after authorities found
him in possession of tetrahydrocannabinol oil. [@1] Dylan Carlton of College
Station was arrested and charged with possession of a controlled substance. [@1]

The stop happened around 10 p.m. on Friday 22 March 2019, when Deputy Meagher
pulled over a 2017 Acura for speeding on S. Kirtley Road. [@1] The car turned
westbound on State Highway 71 before it pulled over. [@1] Two people were in the
vehicle. [@1]

After questioning the two occupants, the deputy found 80 grams of
tetrahydrocannabinol oil in cartridges. [@1] Carlton was arrested after he
claimed responsibility for the oil. [@1] He was then taken to the Dan R. Beck
Justice Center. [@1] The Fayette County Sheriff's Office released a booking
photograph of Carlton. [@1]
