Georgian officers detained three Armenian citizens at the Zemo Larsi (Upper
Lars) checkpoint on the Georgian-Russian border. [@1] Those detained were the
bus driver and two more passengers, according to the Armenian embassy in
Georgia. [@2] All other passengers on the bus were being questioned as part of
the preliminary investigation. [@1]

Customs officers at the Kazbegi crossing inspected a passenger bus crossing the
Georgian-Russian border after becoming suspicious of the vehicle. [@2] The
search found 149 grams of cocaine and 64 grams of hashish in the bus. [@2] The
bus, driven by an Armenian citizen, was travelling from Russia to Armenia in
transit through Georgia. [@1]

The case was handed to the Kazbegi criminal police of the Interior Ministry for
further action. [@2] The Interior Ministry opened a criminal case on unlawful
import, export or transit of narcotics, which carries six to nine years in
prison. [@2] The Armenian embassy said all passengers were provided with food
and basic necessities while diplomats worked to speed their return to Armenia.
[@2]
