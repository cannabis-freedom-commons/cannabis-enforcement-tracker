About 80 officers, using drones, raided six plantations in the Faleatiu hills and destroyed more than 10,000 cannabis plants; 11 people were arrested. Police valued the crop at about ST$10 million at ST$1,000 a plant. The area had been raided in 2015 and 2016, and officers reported guns being pointed at them. [@1]

Samoa Police Service said the roughly 10,000 plants seized in the November 2019 Faleatiu raid were destroyed the following month, after court proceedings. [@2]
