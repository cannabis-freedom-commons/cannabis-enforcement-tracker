Prefectura Naval Argentina (PNA), Argentina’s coast guard, seized 8,110
kilograms of marijuana hidden in forest near Puerto Rico, Misiones. [@1] The
cache was near Puerto Natural Luján, at kilometre 1,754 of the Paraná River,
about 140 kilometres from Posadas. [@1] Investigators said the cargo was being
held (“enfriando,” or temporarily stored) until distribution handlers came to
collect it. [@1] The investigation identified the area where the marijuana was
being held. [@1]

Search teams found 11,954 “ladrillos,” or pressed bricks, concealed in dense
vegetation. [@1] Ground patrols entered the woodland, while the river security
deployment was supported by border-guard vessel GC-197 “Timbú.” [@1] El Litoral
likewise described Prefectura’s operation as involving searches by land and
water. [@2]

The Prefectura’s public statement put the cargo’s commercial value above
500,900,000 pesos. [@1] Security Minister Patricia Bullrich and Prefectura chief
Eduardo Scarzello supervised the operation. [@1] The case was handled by the
Federal Prosecutor’s Office in Oberá, whose secretary general, Arlindo Otto
Kurtz, was named in the statement. [@1]
