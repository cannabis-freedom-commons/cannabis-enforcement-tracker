Police in Namibia's Oshana region arrested a 30-year-old woman and her
29-year-old boyfriend at Omashaka informal settlement in Ondangwa, during a
month-long operation called Usalama. [@1] The police crime report says the
couple, suspected of dealing in drugs, flushed the drugs down a toilet bowl when
officers arrived. [@1]

Operation members disconnected the sewage pipes and recovered the drugs from the
sewage pond. [@1] The couple were found with four bankies of cannabis valued by
police at N$250, alongside 52 units of crack cocaine worth a stated street value
of N$5 200. [@1] Police also confiscated 40 packs of YES cigarettes valued at
N$800 and 20 bottles of Amarula cream from the couple's house. [@1]

The two face charges of dealing in drugs, defeating the course of justice and
contravening the Customs Act. [@1] They were held at Ondangwa Police Station and
were expected to appear in the Ondangwa Magistrate's Court the next day. [@1]
Police said their investigations continued. [@1]
