Police in Himachal Pradesh's Kullu district arrested three people in two separate checks and seized a total of about 1kg of charas, superintendent of police Gaurav Singh said. [@1] Hyderabad residents Priyanka Verma, 25, and Mohammad Amir, 26, were arrested with 501 grams of charas at Jai Nullah. [@1] Mumbai resident Akash Kundran, 23, was arrested with 515 grams of charas the previous day while police were patrolling near Chillmor. [@1]

All three were booked under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, and investigations were continuing. [@1]
