Police completed a two-week special operation against a cannabis-group supply
channel running from Zhambyl region to Almaty. [@1] For two weeks officers kept
an Almaty resident suspected of storing and selling hashish under operational
surveillance. [@1] Search measures identified the supplier as a native of Shu
who transported the drugs from Zhambyl region. [@1] The supplier was detained in
Bayserke village in Ili district of Almaty region. [@1] On the evening of the
same day police detained the Almaty resident who was directly selling the drugs.
[@1] Both detainees were placed in a pre-trial detention facility and a
pre-trial investigation was opened under part 3 of Article 297. [@1] The charge
covers illegal manufacture, processing, acquisition, storage, transport for
sale, forwarding and sale of narcotic drugs, and carries ten to fifteen years'
imprisonment with confiscation of property. [@2]

A personal search of the supplier's travel bag found two plastic wraps carrying
five kilograms of hashish. [@1] A personal search of the Almaty suspect seized a
paper wrap containing hashish. [@1] A service search dog found another seven
kilograms of hashish in a refrigerator in his house. [@1] The total weight of
the seized drugs was 12 kg, which is an especially large size. [@1]

Police said about 20 tonnes of drugs are seized nationally each year, mostly
marijuana and hashish of the cannabis group. [@2]
