The Pakistan Navy, working with the Anti-Narcotics Force (ANF), seized
about 1,200 kilograms of hashish from a vessel in an intelligence-based
operation in the waters near Pasni, Balochistan. [@1,2] The navy announced
the seizure on 30 November 2019. [@1]

The navy said the hashish was being transported to a foreign destination
through the sea. [@1] The boarding team turned the cache over to the ANF
for disposal. [@1,2] The official handout photo shows navy and ANF
personnel with the seized green sacks. [@1]
