Police at the Ariamsvlei border post arrested a 30-year-old man and a
45-year-old woman, both Namibian citizens. [@1] A search of the Colt Mitsubishi
they were driving from South Africa into Namibia found 58 parcels of compressed
cannabis. [@1]

The cannabis weighed 37,53 kilograms, and police put its street value at N$1 125
900. [@1] The two were arrested around 11h40, the deputy commissioner Chrispin
Mubebo, the Namibian Police's crime investigations coordinator for the ||Kharas
region, told Nampa. [@1]

The two were held at the Ariamsvlei Police Station and were expected to appear
in the Karasburg Magistrate's Court on charges of drug dealing the next day.
[@1] Police said investigations continued. [@1]
