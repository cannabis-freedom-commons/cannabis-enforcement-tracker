The Ethiopian Ministry of Revenue seized 474 kg of cannabis from a freight truck in Humera town while it was being smuggled to neighbouring Sudan. [@1] The head of the ministry Humera area branch, Melese Yimam, told the state-run Ethiopian News Agency that the cannabis was found inside the truck about four kilometres from the Sudanese border. [@1]

Security forces and customs staff had trailed the truck for some time before seizing it, and the cannabis was hidden below a consignment of food and edible oil the truck was officially carrying. [@1] The driver and his assistant disappeared shortly before the seizure and police were on a manhunt for them. [@1]
