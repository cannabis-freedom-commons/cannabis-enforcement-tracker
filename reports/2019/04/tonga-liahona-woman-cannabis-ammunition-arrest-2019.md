Police in Tonga arrested a 32-year-old woman from the Liahona area on 10 April
2019 after finding cannabis and live ammunition with her. [@1] Officers seized
the cannabis, 31 cannabis seeds and the ammunition from her vehicle. [@2] The 20
packs of cannabis weighed 10.20 grams in all. [@2]

Deputy Police Commissioner Pelenatita Fe'ao Vaisuai said the accused had been
charged with possession of the illicit drugs and ammunition. [@2] The woman was
remanded in police custody to appear at the Magistrates Court at a later date.
[@2]

Vaisuai commended Houma's Community Patrol Volunteers for their vigilance and
for working closely with police in the arrest. [@2] The commissioner said the
trust and confidence of communities let police work with them to hold to account
people involved in supplying and distributing illicit drugs. [@2]
