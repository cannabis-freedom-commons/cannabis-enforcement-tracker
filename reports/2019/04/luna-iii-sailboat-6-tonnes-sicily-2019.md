Italian finance police seized more than six tonnes of hashish from a sailboat off Sicily, customs authorities announced on Saturday 13 April 2019. [@1] The drugs were found on the 12-metre Spanish-flagged sailing yacht Luna III, stopped about 160 km south of the Sicilian coast carrying 6,000 kg of hashish. [@1,2,3]

Spanish authorities had originally flagged the boat as suspicious. [@1] Three Spaniards were arrested on board. [@2] The seizure was part of a joint operation in which the Spanish Tax Agency's Customs Surveillance unit took part alongside the Italian authorities. [@3] The Guardia di Finanza said Spain, France, Italy and Greece had been on high alert against hashish trafficking from Morocco, and that the seizure was the fifth such interception in the waters between Sicily and Tunisia since the previous summer. [@1] Authorities valued the load at about 60 million euros. [@2]
