Customs and Border Protection officers at the Port of San Luis referred a
tractor-trailer for additional inspection on Wednesday afternoon, 24 April 2019,
as its 26-year-old driver, a resident of San Luis Rio Colorado in Sonora,
Mexico, tried to enter the United States. [@1,2] The trailer was empty, but a
narcotics detection canine alerted officers to a scent coming from it, and a
search found nearly 170 packages of marijuana hidden within the trailer's
roof. [@1,2] The drugs weighed more than 1,995 pounds, about 905 kilograms,
with an estimated value of nearly 998,000 dollars. [@1,2] The agency's
photograph shows the trailer's ceiling torn open with packages visible
inside. [@1]

## Seizure and arrest

Officers seized the drugs and the vehicle, and arrested the driver. [@1,2] He
was turned over to U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement's Homeland Security
Investigations. [@1,2]
