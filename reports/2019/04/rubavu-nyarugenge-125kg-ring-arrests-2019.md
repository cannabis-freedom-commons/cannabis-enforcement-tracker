On 25 April 2019, Rwanda National Police Anti-Narcotics Unit officers working with district
stations arrested two suspected members of one drug trafficking ring in coordinated operations
in Rubavu and Nyarugenge districts. [@1,2] Charlene Bazihira, 19, was tracked and arrested in
Gasutamo Village, Mbugangali Cell, Gisenyi Sector at about 1:30pm just after crossing into
Rwanda from the Democratic Republic of Congo with 1,230 pellets of cannabis. [@1,2] Police said
she worked for Evelyne Uwineza, who ran a narcotics business from Buhoro Village, Matabaro Cell,
Kimisagara Sector in Nyarugenge, and arrested Uwineza later the same day. [@1,2]

In a separate Rubavu operation officers recovered ten bales of cannabis weighing over 125 kg,
though the owners escaped arrest. [@1,2] Western region spokesperson CIP Innocent Gasasira said
the fugitive owners were known and would be traced to face the law. [@1,2] He described Rubavu
as a major trafficking route and credited information-sharing with residents for breaking the
supply chain. [@1,2]
