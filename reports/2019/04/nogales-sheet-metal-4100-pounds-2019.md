Customs and Border Protection officers at the Mariposa commercial facility in
Nogales referred a tractor-trailer driven by a 45-year-old Mexican national for
inspection on Saturday afternoon, 20 April 2019, as he tried to enter the United
States. [@1,2] A narcotics detection canine alerted officers to the load, and a
search found nearly 600 bundles of marijuana concealed inside a shipment of
rolled sheet metal. [@1,2] The drugs weighed more than 4,100 pounds, about
1,860 kilograms, which the agency valued at nearly 2.1 million dollars. [@1,2]
The agency's photograph shows the load as large rolls of sheet metal chained to
a flatbed trailer. [@1]

## Seizure and arrest

Officers seized the tractor-trailer together with the drugs, and arrested the
driver. [@1,2] He was turned over to U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement's
Homeland Security Investigations pending further investigation. [@1,2]
