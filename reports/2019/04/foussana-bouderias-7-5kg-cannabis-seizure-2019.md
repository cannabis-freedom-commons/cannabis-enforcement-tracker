National guard officers of the Bouderias border brigade, in the Foussana
district of Kasserine governorate, seized cannabis in the evening between Sunday
and Monday, 22 April 2019. [@1] The cannabis, known locally as zatla, was found
in a plastic bag at the border area of Bouderias. [@1] Two people who had been
holding it escaped the national guard units by crossing into Algerian territory.
[@1]

A security source said on Monday 22 April 2019 that more than 7 kg of zatla had
been seized at Bouderias. [@2] Nessma reported the haul as 7.5 kg of Indian
hemp, zatla, that a person had been carrying and threw away as security officers
approached. [@2] Preliminary investigations indicated the cannabis was held by a
person of Algerian nationality who intended to hand it to another person, a
Tunisian. [@2] Investigations were continuing to identify the two suspects. [@2]
Zoom Tunisia, citing a security source, reported that the national guard's
Bouderias border brigade in Foussana, Kasserine governorate, seized the cannabis
on Sunday evening. [@3] The Algerian national fled and investigations were
continuing to identify the Tunisian suspect. [@3]

Foussana lies in Kasserine governorate, the mountain frontier zone where
cannabis resin smuggled from Algeria is regularly intercepted and zatla is the
local term for cannabis resin. [@3] Officers recovered the cannabis after its
carrier abandoned it in the mountain border strip that runs along the Algerian
border. [@3]
