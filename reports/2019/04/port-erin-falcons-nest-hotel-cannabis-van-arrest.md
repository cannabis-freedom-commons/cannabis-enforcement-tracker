Police seized cannabis bush with a street value of £120,000 that had been
smuggled to the Isle of Man in a van. [@1] The drugs were found in a Citroen
Berlingo van that had travelled on the Liverpool ferry. [@3] Officers arrested a
46-year-old man from Liverpool at the Falcon's Nest Hotel in Port Erin at about
01:00 BST on 26 April 2019. [@1] The man was arrested on suspicion of drug
importation and remained in custody, a police spokesman said. [@1] Police said
targeting those linked to the matter in the UK was a priority and that their
investigations were continuing. [@2] The arrested man told police he was on the
island to tender for electrical work but could not give the address of the site.
[@5]

At Douglas Courthouse on 30 April 2019 he pleaded not guilty to unlawfully
bringing six kilograms of cannabis to the island and to possessing the drug with
intent to supply it. [@6] High Bailiff Jayne Hughes remanded him in custody,
with no application for bail, to appear again by video link from the Isle of Man
Prison in Jurby. [@6] At further hearings in May the court kept him in custody
and listed his next appearance for 4 June 2019. [@4] The case concerned six
kilos of cannabis, the weight the prosecution put on the haul. [@3]
