Sudanese police seized 15 tonnes of hashish in a western part of Omdurman, the twin city of Khartoum, on 1 April 2019, in what state media reports on 3 April called one of the biggest hauls in recent years. [@1,2] General Hussein Nafia, chief of the anti-narcotics police, told the official SUNA news agency it was the work of an international network, without saying whether anyone had been arrested. [@1,2]

Prime Minister Mohamed Tahir Eila visited a police building in Khartoum where the seized drugs were put on display. [@1,2] Officials acknowledged a rise in drug trafficking and consumption across the country in recent years, especially among young people. [@1,2]
