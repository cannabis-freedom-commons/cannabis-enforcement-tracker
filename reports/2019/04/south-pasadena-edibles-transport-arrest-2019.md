On 24 April 2019 at about 3:15 p.m., a South Pasadena police officer stopped a
vehicle at Mission Street and Fremont Avenue. [@1] The officer smelled a strong
odor of marijuana coming from inside the vehicle when speaking with the driver.
[@1] Four suspects ranging from 18 to 24 years old sat on the curb while
officers inspected the vehicle for over an hour. [@1] The driver admitted to
transporting marijuana edibles in the vehicle and intending to mail them at the
post office. [@1] Police arrested the suspect for unlawful transportation of
marijuana. [@1]

Police said the vehicle held a large quantity of edibles with an estimated value
of over $15,000. [@1] Five squad cars and several officers responded to the
white Dodge Charger in the parking lot of Carrow's Restaurant at about 3:15 p.m.
on 24 April. [@1]

Responding officers at the scene declined to say what they were investigating
until a full report was filed. [@1] In an official statement released the
following day, the South Pasadena Police Department said the incident had been a
drug bust after a routine traffic stop. [@1] Police added that even without
intent to sell, a person is prohibited from giving away more than 28.5 grams of
marijuana and must not furnish it to a minor. [@1]
