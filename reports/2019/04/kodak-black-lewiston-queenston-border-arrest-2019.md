Rapper Kodak Black, born Bill Kapri, and three companions were arrested after
allegedly trying to cross the U.S.-Canada border near Buffalo with guns and
marijuana. [@1] The group was stopped at the Lewiston-Queenston International
Bridge. [@1] State police said Kodak, 21, and Jeantony Saintmelus, 22, were
booked on suspicion of criminal possession of a weapon and unlawful possession
of marijuana, while Stetson J. President, 24, and Madarrow D. Smith, 20, were
booked on suspicion of criminal possession of a weapon. [@1]

U.S. Customs and Border Protection said the group admitted possessing marijuana
and firearms during initial inspection, and after a secondary inspection found
the weapons were possessed unlawfully in New York State, the marijuana, firearms
and four people were turned over to the New York State Police. [@1] Kodak was
driving a Cadillac Escalade with temporary California registration, and state
police said the gun in it was a Glock 9mm pistol. [@1] Saintmelus was driving a
Porsche alongside the Escalade, and police said three guns and marijuana were
found in that car. [@1] Kodak posted bail on Thursday while his three
co-defendants remained in custody into early afternoon, according to a Niagara
County sheriff's official. [@1]

The House of Blues in Boston said the Kodak Black shows were postponed due to
unforeseen circumstances, with new dates to be announced. [@1]
