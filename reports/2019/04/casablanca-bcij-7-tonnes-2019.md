Moroccan authorities arrested seven suspects, including one described as the
presumed head of the network. [@2] The Central Bureau of Judicial Investigations
(BCIJ) is a police service specialising in counterterrorism and serious crime.
[@1] The BCIJ reported seizing seven tonnes of cannabis resin. [@1]

The seizure was carried out during an operation on Monday and Tuesday in
Casablanca, in the west of the country. [@1] The seized resin was aboard a
freight transport truck and two utility vehicles near the port of Casablanca,
described as the kingdom's economic capital. [@1] The BCIJ acted in cooperation
with the General Directorate for Territorial Surveillance (DGST) to thwart the
trafficking operation in Casablanca. [@3] Officers also seized MAD 204,630
during the operation. [@3]

The BCIJ said the shipment was intended for export by sea, without specifying a
destination. [@1] The BCIJ said the operation was part of its efforts against
organised crime, especially international drug trafficking. [@3] The BCIJ said
investigations would continue to determine possible national and international
links of the suspected criminal network. [@1]
