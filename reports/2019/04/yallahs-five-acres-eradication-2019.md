Officers from the police Narcotics Division and the Mobile Reserve carried out searches in and around the Yallahs section of St Thomas on Monday 22 April 2019. [@1] They found three ganja fields and destroyed a collective five acres (about two hectares) of the crop. [@1]

Ten pounds of ganja seeds were destroyed as well. [@1] The report names no arrests from the Yallahs operation. [@1]
