Toronto's Organized Crime Enforcement drug squad executed 29 search warrants
across four southern Ontario cities on 25 April 2019 in an investigation
codenamed Project Dos. [@1,2] Eight people were arrested and charged with 27
drug offences, including possession for the purpose of trafficking,
possession of the proceeds of crime and conspiracy. [@1,2] A superintendent
told a 9 May news conference the network was a high-level trafficking ring
capable of generating millions of dollars in profit. [@1]

Officers seized 3,905 illegally grown cannabis plants, which CBC rounds to
around 4,000. [@1,2] They also confiscated 29 kilograms of cocaine, smaller
amounts of ketamine, hundreds of kilograms of precursor chemicals such as
ephedrine, and about 308,000 dollars in cash. [@1,2] Police put the street
value of all the drugs seized at up to 17 million dollars. [@1,2] The raids
took place in Toronto, Vaughan, Waterloo and Stoney Creek, with most of the
arrests in Toronto. [@1]
