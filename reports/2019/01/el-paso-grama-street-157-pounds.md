Officers from the El Paso Police Department arrested six people on 23 January
2019 after finding 140 bundles of marijuana inside a central El Paso home. [@1]
Police identified the arrested individuals as Alexis Ileene Lopez, 21, Fredrick
Green, 20, Jasmine Solomon, 21, Dominick James Mullins, 20, Kenny Alfredo Perez,
28, all of El Paso or Fort Bliss, and Jesus Humberto Garciduenas, 22, of Ciudad
Juárez, Mexico. [@3] All six suspects were booked into the El Paso County Jail
on a charge of possession of marijuana over 50 pounds and under 2,000 pounds.
[@2] Bonds for each of the six detainees were set at $50,000. [@1]

Officers had responded to the residence in the 800 block of North Grama Street
around 2:45 p.m. following a call reporting a burglary in progress. [@3] Upon
arriving at the address, officers found the front door of the home open and a
vehicle parked in the driveway with its rear compartment open. [@1] An
investigation revealed that the six individuals were inside the house alongside
140 wrapped bundles of marijuana weighing 157.30 pounds (about 71.35 kg). [@1]
