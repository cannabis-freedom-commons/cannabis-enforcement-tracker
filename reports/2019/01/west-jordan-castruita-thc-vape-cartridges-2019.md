Police in West Jordan, Utah, carried out a drug bust on Friday 4 January 2019 in
which officers seized hundreds of THC cartridges intended for vaping pens. [@1]
Investigators said the man behind the operation was Juan Cruz Castruita,
described as the leader of a gang called the Polo Getto Boys. [@1] Police said
the gang used the messaging app Snapchat to distribute the product. [@1]

Sergeant Holt of the police said ordinary vape oils contain nicotine, while
vaping a THC oil is basically the same as smoking marijuana. [@1] Police linked
their concern to the popularity of vaping among high school students, citing a
national study the previous month that found one in three seniors had vaped.
[@1] Sergeant Holt said the effect on schools, if the cartridges reached them,
was huge and far-reaching. [@1]
