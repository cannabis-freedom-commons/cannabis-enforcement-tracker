The resin lay in a specially built hollow compartment aboard a
Morocco-registered international haulage truck found in the Boukhalef district
of Tangier. [@1] Le360 reported that judicial police officers found the drugs
aboard two goods trucks on Wednesday 9 January in the Boukhalef-Al Irfane
district, in an operation by the Tangier judicial police anti-drug squad, known
in Morocco as the brigade anti-drogue. [@2] The DGSN, the Direction générale de
la sûreté nationale, announced the seizure on Thursday in a statement. [@1]

The haul followed the discovery on Saturday 5 January of a first cargo of 13
tonnes and 750 kilograms of cannabis resin at the Tanger Med port. [@1] The DGSN
put the two seizures together at 28 tonnes 994 kilograms of drug. [@1] TelQuel,
publishing the Agence France-Presse report, said the drug had been hidden in a
truck in a district of the port of Tangier, while other accounts place the truck
in the Boukhalef neighbourhood. [@3]

Preliminary findings pointed to a cross-border criminal network, suspected of
setting up shell companies to cover its activities and of keeping links with
criminal networks in Morocco or Europe. [@1] The Tangier judicial police said
they would keep working with the DGST, the kingdom's domestic intelligence
service, under the public prosecutor's supervision to arrest everyone involved
and uncover the network's trafficking and money-laundering activities. [@1] None
of the reports named anyone arrested or charged over either seizure. [@4]
