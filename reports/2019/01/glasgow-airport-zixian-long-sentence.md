Edinburgh art student Zixian Long, 21, was caught with 10 kg of cannabis in a shrink-wrapped suitcase at Glasgow Airport on Hogmanay 2017 after returning from Barcelona. [@1,2] Border Force officers found the haul, which a detective said was worth £100,000 broken into street deals or £40,000 wholesale. [@1]

Long, who is Chinese and had moved to Britain about ten years earlier, said through an interpreter that her mother had been offered a free trip for bringing back luggage from Barcelona and had asked her along. [@1] A Chinese man dropped the suitcase at their Barcelona hotel, and her mother asked her to carry it home. [@1]

Her mother Xiaodan Wang, 51, had been stopped at Edinburgh Airport the day before, on 30 December 2017, with 10 kg of cannabis from the same Barcelona trip; both lived in Glasgow's East End. [@2] Long denied importing class B drugs and being concerned in their supply, but was convicted after trial at Paisley Sheriff Court and jailed for two years on 8 January 2019, backdated to her remand that December. [@1,2]
