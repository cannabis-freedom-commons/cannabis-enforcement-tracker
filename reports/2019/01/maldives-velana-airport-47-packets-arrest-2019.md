Two foreign nationals were arrested with 47 packets of cannabis in a joint
operation by the police drug enforcement department and the Maldives Customs
Service. [@1] The first suspect, a 21-year-old Bangladeshi man, was arrested on
arrival at Velana International Airport on Saturday morning after customs
officers grew suspicious of his behaviour, scanned his luggage and found 31
packets in a sealed package. [@1]

Police said the second suspect, also 21, was arrested later on Saturday at a
residence in the capital Malé, where officers found 26 packets of cannabis after
searching the home with the permission of its local owner. [@1] The criminal
court remanded both suspects to 15 days in police custody. [@1]

Customs said 32kg of drugs were seized in 83 cases in 2018, a 232 percent
increase from the previous year. [@1] Heroin and hashish oil, a liquid cannabis
extract, are the most commonly used drugs in the Maldives, and customs officers
reported finding about 3kg of cannabis in the Bangladeshi man's luggage. [@1]
