Libya's customs department seized about 8 tonnes of hashish inside two
containers at the port of Misrata on Sunday 6 January 2019. [@1] The drugs
were packed in 260 bags, according to a statement from Misrata's Customs
Department. [@1] A maritime security review also lists the Misrata hashish
seizure among incidents at sea in the week of 7-13 January. [@2]

The customs department said all the seized narcotics were destroyed. [@1]
No arrests were reported. [@1]
