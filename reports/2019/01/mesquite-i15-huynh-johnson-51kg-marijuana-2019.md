A Mesquite police officer stopped a car for a traffic violation on Interstate 15
at about 3 a.m. on 8 January 2019. [@1] The officer saw evidence that the
occupants were trafficking illegal drugs and began an investigation. [@2]

A search of the car found almost 60 pounds of marijuana buds and 53 pounds of
concentrated THC marijuana oils and edible candies. [@1] Mesquite police said
the stop happened about 3 a.m. on 8 January and that the officer stopped the car
for a traffic violation. [@3]

Police identified the two men as Anthony Huynh, 21, of Peoria, Illinois, and
Jordan Johnson, 24, of West Peoria, Illinois. [@2] Both men were taken to the
Clark County Detention Center in Las Vegas. [@3] Each man was charged with one
felony count of trafficking marijuana above 50 pounds and a separate felony
count of trafficking marijuana concentrate above 1 pound of concentrated THC.
[@3]

A Mesquite police sergeant said officers believed the men bought the marijuana
in California and were heading back to Illinois, and did not know whether they
bought it legally or illegally. [@1] Cannabis is legal in Nevada, but adults may
possess only one ounce of marijuana and an eighth of an ounce of concentrated
marijuana, and may not take it across state lines. [@1]

The two men were arrested on 8 January 2019. [@4]
