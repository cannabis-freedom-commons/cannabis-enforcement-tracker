Gendarmería Nacional officers seized 4,115 kilos 468 grams of marijuana, which
the force calls cannabis sativa, from a truck near Hipólito Yrigoyen, Misiones.
[@1] The driver jumped from the moving truck near a checkpoint and got away on
foot in the dark. [@2]

Members of Escuadrón 11 San Ignacio were manning a check on kilometre 1429 of
Ruta Nacional 12 when the truck approached, stopped and began reversing. [@1]
The unmanned truck kept going, broke through a guardrail and fell into a ravine
about 8 metres deep. [@1] On reaching the wreckage, officers found bundles of
marijuana packages scattered on the slope. [@1] The truck was a blue Mercedes
Benz 1114, abandoned at the bottom of the slope. [@2] The federal court in Oberá
ordered the drug and the truck seized so that investigators could keep working
on the route the load had taken. [@1]

Misiones Online republished the Gendarmería account with the same place,
checkpoint, ravine and weight. [@3] Clarín reported the check was part of the
Operativo Conjunto Abierto de Frontera, a federal border-control programme, and
that the truck was stopped very close to the border with Paraguay. [@2] Security
minister Patricia Bullrich described the procedure as everyday cooperation
between federal forces, local authorities and criminal intelligence. [@2]
