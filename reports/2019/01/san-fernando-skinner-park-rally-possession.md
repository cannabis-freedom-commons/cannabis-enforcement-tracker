Police arrested Makaan Grant, 27, of Curepe for possession of cannabis during
the All Mansions of Rastafari's national cannabis rally at Skinner Park, San
Fernando, on Sunday 27 January 2019. [@1] Rastas chanted for equal rights and
justice after the arrest, and eyewitnesses claimed the arresting officer was
intoxicated on duty and called for him to be breathalysed. [@1] The arrest
prompted a short-lived clash between police and rally participants before the
crowd cooled and returned to the space set aside for the event. [@1]

Close to 50 uniformed officers, several marked police vehicles and a bus were
stationed around the rally, and officers walked throughout the crowd. [@1] It
was AMOR's second national cannabis rally and the first after a
government-organised public consultation on decriminalising cannabis; the first
was held in October at Woodford Square, Port-of-Spain. [@1] Bongo Grease of AMOR
said organisers faced attempts to shut the rally down over a missing dancehall
licence and cancelled some scheduled entertainers to keep the event on. [@1]

Grant was arrested at about 6 pm at the height of the rally. [@2] A police
prosecutor said a corporal saw Grant walking on the outer perimeter of Skinner
Park smoking a cigarette of unusual length, and Grant replied, "Boss, is just a
lil smoke I have." [@2] Grant pleaded guilty to having 0.6 grammes of marijuana.
[@2]

In the San Fernando Magistrates' Court the next morning, magistrate Alicia
Chankar ordered Grant to perform 80 hours of community service, starting the
following Monday and finishing by 30 June. [@2] Chankar told Grant that
marijuana possession, despite the heated debate, is still illegal, and that
walking and smoking in full view of people was a blatant violation. [@2] Grant
told the magistrate he smoked the herb in accordance with his Rastafarian belief
and culture, and a member of AMOR was present for the hearing. [@2]

The Port-of-Spain rally the Guardian calls the first had passed off differently
in police terms, and the discourse around it was the decriminalisation
consultation rather than a court case. [@1]
