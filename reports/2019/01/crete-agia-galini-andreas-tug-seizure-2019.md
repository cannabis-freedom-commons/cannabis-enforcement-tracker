The Hellenic Coast Guard apprehended the tug Andreas L in the waters off Crete
and found an estimated 6.5 tonnes of cannabis in a hidden compartment. [@1,2]
About 258,000 pills of the banned stimulant fenethylline were found in the
same compartment, and the vessel was held at the small port of Agia
Galini. [@2]

The coast guard said the investigation was ongoing and gave no further
details. [@2] The same tug had been caught in December 2017 with 10 tonnes of
hashish aboard. [@1]
