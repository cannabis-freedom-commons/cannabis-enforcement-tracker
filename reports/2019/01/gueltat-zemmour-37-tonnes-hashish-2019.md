Morocco's Royal Armed Forces seized 3.7 tonnes of Moroccan hashish in an
operation on Tuesday evening near the border with Mauritania, as reported
Wednesday by the Moroccan press agency MAP. [@1] The army said it foiled a
drug-trafficking operation at the defence line at Gueltat Zemmour after firing
warning shots at two vehicles. [@1] The seized load consisted of 148 packages of
cannabis resin, totalling 3.7 tonnes. [@1]

A second French-language account likewise places the Tuesday-evening operation
at Gueltat Zemmour near the Mauritanian border. [@2] That account says the drugs
were in the two vehicles stopped by the army's warning shots. [@2]
English-language coverage likewise reports 3,700 kilograms of cannabis resin
seized at Guelta Zemmour west of Morocco's defence wall. [@3]

Gueltat Zemmour lies near the Mauritanian border in Western Sahara, a former
Spanish colony mostly controlled by Morocco but claimed by the Polisario
movement. [@1] Morocco is the world's leading producer and a major exporter of
cannabis resin, most of it sold in Europe, according to the UN Office on Drugs
and Crime. [@1]
