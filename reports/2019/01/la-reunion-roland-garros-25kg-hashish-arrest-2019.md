Customs officers at Roland-Garros airport, the airport on Réunion that locals
call Gillot, arrested two people who were carrying 25 kg of cannabis resin in
their suitcases. [@1] The 25 kg of resin was hidden in liquid-laundry drums
packed inside the luggage. [@1]

The two women were taken into custody and then charged with drug trafficking by
the judge of freedoms and detention, the French judge who rules on pre-trial
detention. [@1] They say they did not know what the suitcases contained and
consider themselves to have been set up. [@1]

The man who was to receive the drugs, a young man from Saint-Paul, was arrested
the following evening. [@1] He already appears in the case file on the cannabis
resin intercepted at Roland-Garros airport the previous December. [@1]

In December, Réunion customs seized 19 kg of cannabis resin and 484 g of heroin
hidden in laundry drums. [@1] LINFO.re later dated the interception to 25
January 2019, when two people were arrested at Gillot with 25 kg of resin hidden
in laundry drums. [@2]

Réunion La 1ère described three men transporting 25 kg of resin in laundry drums
as charged and jailed after that 25 January interception. [@3] In the December
case, airport customs officers scanning a passenger's two bags found ten
liquid-laundry drums concealing the fraud goods. [@4]
