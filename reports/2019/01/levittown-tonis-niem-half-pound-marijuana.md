Nassau County Police arrested two men from Levittown after a traffic stop on
Polaris Drive on Monday, January 14, 2019 at 8:14pm. [@1] Officers had observed
five occupants in a 2014 Honda that failed to stop at a stop sign on Polaris
Drive. [@2] The stop and the investigation that followed led to the recovery of
a loaded .38 handgun and approximately a half pound of a substance believed to
be marijuana inside the vehicle. [@1]

Police said the two occupants of the vehicle confessed to possession and
admitted to further weapons and drugs at a storage facility and a residence.
[@1] Search warrants executed at Jordan Tonis's Levittown residence and a
storage locker at 4040 Hempstead Turnpike in Bethpage recovered about 298
alleged Xanax pills, 254 individually packaged e-cigarette cartridges containing
alleged concentrated THC, approximately $1,670 in US currency, a large digital
scale, a counting machine and a vacuum sealing machine. [@1]

Jordan Tonis, 21, was charged with two counts of Criminal Possession of a
Controlled Substance, Assault 2nd degree, Criminal Possession of Marijuana and
five counts of Criminal Contempt for violating a Nassau County Stay Away Order.
[@1] David Niem, 19, of Levittown was charged with three counts of Criminal
Possession of a Weapon, Criminal Possession of a Controlled Substance, Criminal
Possession of a Firearm and Menacing. [@1] Tonis was arraigned on Wednesday,
January 16, 2019 in First District Court at 99 Main Street, Hempstead. [@2] Niem
would be arraigned as soon as medically practical. [@2]
