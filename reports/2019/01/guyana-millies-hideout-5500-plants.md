On Saturday 12 January 2019, Guyana Police went to 8 Miles, Millie’s Hideout, in
the Upper Demerara River and destroyed a two-acre marijuana farm holding about
2,500 plants, estimated at 675 kilogrammes. [@1] At that site they also
destroyed several nurseries with about 3,000 plants, four makeshift camps and
about 200 kilogrammes of dried cannabis. [@1] About seven miles away they
destroyed a second cultivation of about 1,500 plants (225 kg) together with a
nursery of 1,500 plants and a camp holding 100 kg of dried cannabis. [@1] Police
said the plants, camps, dried cannabis and other items were photographed and
destroyed by fire, and that no one was found on the farms. [@1]

The destruction came the day after the Liberty and Justice Party (LJP) promised
to decriminalise cultivation of marijuana and hemp; the article is chiefly about
decriminalisation politics, not the police operation. [@1] LJP leader Lenox
Shuman said at the party’s launch that Guyana’s jails are packed with people
caught with small amounts of marijuana while successive governments have failed
to decriminalise it. [@1]
