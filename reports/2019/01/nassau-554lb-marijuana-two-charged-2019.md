Nheru Newton, 44, of Wellington Street, and Edgar Bonimy, 39, of Golden Gates,
were arraigned in a Nassau magistrate's court before Judge Subusola Swain. [@1]
The two men were alleged to have been found in possession of 554 pounds (about
251 kg) of suspected marijuana on 9 January 2019. [@1] The drugs were given an
estimated street value of $554,000. [@1]

Both men were charged with possession of dangerous drugs with intent to supply
and conspiracy to possess dangerous drugs with intent to supply. [@1] Both
pleaded not guilty and were denied bail. [@1] They were remanded to the Bahamas
Department of Correctional Services and are due to return to court on 5 April.
[@1]

Police were also searching for a third man, Ernest Knowles Jr, in connection
with the drug find. [@1]
