The Royal Australian Navy frigate HMAS Ballarat found and destroyed 3,100 kg of hashish during a night-time boarding of a dhow late on 8 January 2019. [@1] Operating under the Canadian-led Combined Task Force 150, the frigate was patrolling the "Hash Highway", the stretch of the northern Arabian Sea where dhows move drugs toward their markets. [@1,2]

The boarding party discovered the narcotics hidden in void spaces around the vessel. [@1] The haul, worth about A$155 million, was the ship's third drugs seizure in just over two weeks and the task force's sixth since the current team took command on 6 December 2018. [@1,2] The commanding officer said the seizure reflected a continued narcotics trade with the potential to fund terrorist groups across the region. [@1]

A fishing vessel believed to be engaged in illegal activity had been directed to submit to a flag-verification boarding in international waters. [@2] The seized hashish was laid out on the frigate's flight deck and then prepared for disposal at sea. [@1]
