A Narcotics Control Bureau team confiscated 15 kg of hashish at the post office in Sundhara, Kathmandu, on Wednesday afternoon as the post office prepared to send it to Hong Kong. [@1] The hashish was concealed in 148 packets of incense of the kind Buddhist devotees use while performing puja. [@1] Police identified the arrested woman as Kanchi Maya Ghale of Chisang Rural Municipality-8 in Nuwakot. [@1]
