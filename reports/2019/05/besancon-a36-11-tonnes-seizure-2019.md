French judicial police seized 11 tonnes of cannabis in the Besancon area on Friday 10 May 2019, intercepting several vehicles including a truck on the A36 motorway toward Paris. [@1] Police arrested four suspects during the operation, including an Italian national. [@1]

The operation was run by the judicial police of Strasbourg and the central office against drug trafficking, and capped a judicial investigation opened in 2017 with what the interior minister called exemplary cooperation from Moroccan and Spanish investigation teams. [@1] The Nancy prosecutor's office said the investigation had revealed large-scale trafficking between Morocco, Spain, France and Belgium. [@1] Interior Minister Christophe Castaner publicly congratulated the officers on the record seizure. [@1]

The 11-tonne haul was the largest cannabis seizure in France since 1999, when police had taken 23.5 tonnes in Boulogne-sur-Mer in northern France. [@1]
