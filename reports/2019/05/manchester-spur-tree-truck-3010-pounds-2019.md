Two men were taken into police custody after the Narcotics Police seized more
than 3,000 pounds of marijuana on the Spur Tree main road in Manchester on 29
May 2019. [@1]

Officers were conducting operations in the area at about 3:40 pm when they
signalled a GMC motor truck to stop. [@1] The driver complied and the truck was
searched, yielding 62 knitted plastic bags with several packages of compressed
ganja weighing approximately 3,010 pounds. [@1] Police valued the ganja at more
than J$12 million on the street. [@1]

The truck and the drugs were seized and both men were taken into custody. [@1]
Police withheld the men's identities pending formal interviews in the presence
of their attorneys. [@1]

The account came from the police Corporate Communications Unit, the
constabulary's public information office. [@1]
