Joint teams from PDEA-Kalinga, the Tinglayan and Lubuagan municipal police, the Kalinga police
mobile force companies, the drug enforcement unit and the provincial intelligence branch hiked
mountain paths to reach ten plantation sites in Butbut Proper, Tinglayan, covering 2,931 square
metres. [@1,2] The three-day operation of 27–29 May 2019, run under PDEA's Oplan Linis Barangay XI
and the police's Oplan Green Pearl Bravo, uprooted and burned 28,300 fully grown marijuana plants
and 1,000 seedlings on site. [@1,2]

## Value and no arrests

PDEA-CAR regional director Edgar Apalla valued the destroyed plants at about 5.6 million
pesos. [@1] No cultivators were found at the sites; Apalla's office said the growers likely fled
when they heard the teams hiking up, a pattern seen across Cordillera plantation raids. [@1]
