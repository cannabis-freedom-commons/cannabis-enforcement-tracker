A three-year joint operation by Spanish and Italian police seized 2.7 tonnes of hashish in Andalusia and led to 44 arrests. [@1,2] More than 70,000 euros in cash and illegal firearms were also seized. [@1,2] Police reporting put the cash at €75,500 and counted 34 searches, 2.5 kg of marijuana, 12 firearms and 14 cars, with several more suspects placed on a wanted list. [@3]

## Who was arrested

Those held were mostly suspected members of the Italian 'Ndrangheta and the Spanish Los Castañas clan. [@1,2] They face charges including belonging to a criminal organisation, money laundering, bribery, possession of illegal weapons and possession of false documents. [@1,2] Italian police have in recent years claimed particular success against the Calabrian 'Ndrangheta. [@1]
