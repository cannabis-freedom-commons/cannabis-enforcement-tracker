Gardaí from Ashbourne and the Ballymun Drugs Unit searched a property in Gormanston, Co. Meath on Wednesday 8 May 2019 in an intelligence-led operation. [@1,2] They seized cannabis herb with an estimated street value of €2.5 million along with two hand grenades. [@1,2] The Defence Forces Explosive Ordnance Disposal team was called to the scene and made the devices safe. [@1,2]

One man was arrested at the location. [@1] He was expected before the Criminal Courts of Justice on Parkgate Street, Dublin, on the afternoon of Friday 10 May 2019, charged in connection with the seizure. [@3] Gardaí said investigations were continuing. [@1]
