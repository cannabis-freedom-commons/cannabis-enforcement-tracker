On the evening of Friday 24 May 2019, during a routine patrol, the police marine
unit stopped a local canoe named "Corrie" about one hundred yards off Mathurin
Point in Vieux Fort. [@2] Officers found and seized sixty-three pounds (about
28.6 kg) of what appeared to be cannabis on board. [@2] Vieux Fort resident
Martinez Fontenelle was arrested and charged with possession of a controlled
drug. [@2] He was taken to the Second District Court and granted bail in an
undisclosed amount. [@3]

The second occupant jumped overboard to try to escape, and searches of the area
found him neither then nor later. [@2] On Tuesday 28 May 2019 police said a male
body, believed to be the second occupant of the canoe, had been recovered at a
beach along Mathurin Point. [@2] Police said formal identification had not been
made and that the link to the canoe could not be confirmed at that time. [@2]
The Royal Saint Lucia Police Force announced the interception and the cannabis
find in a statement on 30 May 2019. [@2]

Andre De Caires, chairman of the Cannabis Movement, an advocacy group pushing
for legal cannabis in Saint Lucia, called the interception "unfortunate" and
said the case showed the harm of the possession laws. [@1] "It’s very
unfortunate that ganja does not kill users, but illegal possession of it does,"
De Caires said, adding that many people's lives had been destroyed and people
had died because of the illegality of cannabis. [@1] De Caires said the group
was in talks with Commerce Minister Bradley Felix, the government's point man on
cannabis, who had asked for a budget for a planned national education programme
that the group intended to submit that week. [@1]
