A mobile customs unit near Tangier seized four tonnes of cannabis resin on the
night of 7 to 8 May 2019. [@3] Morocco's MAP news agency reported the seizure,
citing the customs unit that carried out the operation. [@2] A customs statement
said the resin was aboard a utility vehicle carrying a falsified registration
plate. [@2] The haul was about four tons of hashish, or cannabis resin. [@1] The
agency's account, carried by Vijesti, added that the drugs were in a truck with
false plates. [@1]

According to UN data, Morocco is one of the largest producers and exporters of
hashish. [@1] In 2018, Moroccan police seized 52 tons of cannabis resin, which
is used to make hashish. [@1]
