Over the weekend before 29 May 2019, officers of the Special Operations Response
Team (SORT) searched a house on Maingot Road in Tunapuna and arrested the two
men found inside. [@1] The two arrested men were taken to Tunapuna Police
Station, where they were questioned. [@1] Police said the two men were assisting
with other investigations while charges of possession of arms and ammunition
were pending against them. [@1]

Inside the Maingot Road house, officers found an Uzi submachine gun fitted with
a magazine containing 32 rounds of 9mm ammunition. [@1] Acting on information
received after the house search, police later searched a bushy area in Tunapuna
and recovered three separate packets of marijuana weighing 622.5 grams in total.
[@1]

Police made no arrest in connection with the three packets of marijuana found in
the bushy area, and investigations into that find were continuing. [@1] The gun
seizure and the later bushy-area cannabis recovery were reported as parts of the
same weekend SORT exercise in Tunapuna. [@1]
