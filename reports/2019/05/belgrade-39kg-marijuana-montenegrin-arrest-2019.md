Members of the Belgrade police arrested N.B., a 48-year-old citizen of
Montenegro, on suspicion of producing and selling drugs. [@1] The suspect was
driving the vehicle that officers stopped and inspected. [@1] Officers ordered
the suspect held for up to 48 hours following the arrest. [@1] After that
detention period, police were to bring him before the High Public Prosecutor's
Office in Belgrade together with a criminal report. [@1]

Officers found 39 packages of marijuana concealed in the boot of the car, under
the bonnet and inside its structural cavities. [@1] Police seized a total of 39
kilograms of marijuana from the suspect in the operation. [@1]

The marijuana had been distributed through hidden areas of the same car rather
than carried in plain view. [@1] The criminal report presented to the Belgrade
prosecutors concerned the production and sale of drugs. [@1]
