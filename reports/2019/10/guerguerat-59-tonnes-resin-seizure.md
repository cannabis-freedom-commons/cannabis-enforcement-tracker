Moroccan authorities seized 5.9 tonnes of cannabis resin at the Guerguerat border crossing with Mauritania on Monday 14 October 2019, one of the largest catches of that year. [@1] The resin was concealed in a truck heading to West Africa. [@1]

The driver was arrested along with two other people. [@1] Police had to fire their weapons to stop the suspects' car. [@1]

The seizure followed the interception of two tonnes of cannabis resin in Fez days earlier, with three arrests there. [@1] Police and customs also seized 2.2 tonnes of resin hidden in an international transport truck at Tanger-Med port the same Monday, arresting its Spain-bound driver. [@1] Moroccan authorities seized 19.2 tonnes of cannabis in the whole of 2018. [@1]
