Border Guard Bangladesh seized drugs and smuggled goods worth about Tk72.39 crore in October 2019 in raids across border areas. [@1,2] The seized drugs included 624 kg of marijuana alongside about 10 lakh yaba tablets, tens of thousands of phensidyl bottles, foreign liquor, heroin and other tablets. [@1,2]

BGB detained 292 people for involvement in smuggling, including 46 Bangladeshis, 13 Indian nationals and five Nigerians held for crossing borders, and recovered pistols, revolvers, guns and bullets. [@1,2] Legal action was taken against those held. [@2]
