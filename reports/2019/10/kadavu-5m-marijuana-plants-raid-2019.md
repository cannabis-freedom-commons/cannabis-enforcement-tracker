Police uprooted and seized more than $5 million worth of marijuana plants in a
raid on drug farms in one of the villages on Kadavu the month before the reports
were published. [@1] Acting Commissioner of Police Rusiate Tudravu confirmed the
raid and said officers had conducted drug farm raids in areas where no arrests
were made. [@1] Tudravu said the force's focus was to destroy marijuana plants
from farms. [@1]

Tudravu, given as Deputy Commissioner of Police in a second account of the raid,
said villagers had asked why police carried out the raid without arresting
anyone. [@2] He said police gave no prior notification of raids because it would
let cultivators dispose of the illegal substances, and that the uprooted plants
had since been brought to Suva. [@2] Tudravu said the police raid was carried
out on reliable information and that other farm raids had also produced no
arrests. [@2] He said community policing teams were making ongoing follow-up
visits to villages where raids had been carried out. [@2] Tudravu said police
sometimes approach a village's headman but in other situations carry out the
raid beforehand, depending on the situation on the ground. [@2]
