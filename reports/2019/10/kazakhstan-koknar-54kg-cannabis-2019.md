Police in East Kazakhstan and Zhambyl regions blocked a marijuana and hashish
supply channel from the southern region to the eastern part of the country
during Operation Koknar last week. [@1] After lengthy work, police identified a
previously not convicted Zhambyl resident who had organised marijuana supplies
to other regions for further sale. [@1] The man stored marijuana at a relative's
home in Tuymekent village in Zhambyl region, where he was detained. [@1] A
sanctioned personal search of the seller seized two cell phones and 28,500 tenge
received for selling marijuana. [@1]

A search of the house in Tuymekent with a dog handler and service dog found four
bags of dried marijuana weighing more than 30 kilograms and a packet of hashish
weighing 2.4 kilograms in an outbuilding, which the detainee had intended to
send to East Kazakhstan Region. [@1] Near the Tatti-Sary-Koby highway police
detained a 49-year-old native of Merke district with one plastic bag of
marijuana weighing more than 12 kilograms. [@1] In Ust-Kamenogorsk police
detained a local resident for drug sale, finding 20 polymer wraps with
psychotropic substance in a hiding place. [@2] Over the week anti-drug units
identified and registered 33 drug-trafficking crimes across the country. [@1]
They included 17 sales, five cases of storage in an especially large amount, six
cases of smuggling and one case of cultivation of narcotic plants, and officers
closed one drug den. [@1] Police seized more than 54 kilograms of drugs in
total, including 52 kilograms of marijuana and 2.4 kilograms of hashish, with
the remaining heroin and synthetic amounts left out of the cannabis total. [@1]

Both outlets attribute the operation to the Interior Ministry press service and
describe it as part of Operation Koknar. [@2] Citizens sent 298 reports of drug
trafficking in a month to the anti-drug department's WhatsApp hotline, mostly
about graffiti advertising drug-sale sites or Telegram channels, which were
being checked. [@1]
