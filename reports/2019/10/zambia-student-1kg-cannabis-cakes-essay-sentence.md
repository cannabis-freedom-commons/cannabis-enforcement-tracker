Chikwanda Chisendele was a first-year civil engineering student at the
Copperbelt University. [@1] Police arrested Chisendele on a Monday. [@1] He was
21 years old. [@1] He was convicted of planning to sell cannabis cakes. [@1] A
judge sentenced him to write a 50-page essay on drug use. [@1] He was also
ordered to write letters of apology to his university, his parents and Zambia's
Drug Enforcement Commission before 15 November. [@1] The judge gave him a
two-year suspended jail term, meaning he would not spend time in jail unless he
committed another offence. [@1]

He was caught with cannabis-laced cakes weighing more than 1kg (2.2lb),
according to police. [@1] Zambia's Drug Enforcement Commission said it had
intensified efforts to catch people baking cakes containing cannabis. [@1] The
agency warned universities to be alert to cakes and scones laced with cannabis
being trafficked among students. [@1]

Under Zambian law, marijuana is classed as a dangerous drug and is illegal to
possess. [@1]
