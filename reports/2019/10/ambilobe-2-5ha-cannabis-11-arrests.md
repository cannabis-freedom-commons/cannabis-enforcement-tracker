During the operation at Ambilobe, gendarmes came across eleven suspects,
including three women. [@1] They were all carrying bags filled with Indian hemp.
[@1] Officers arrested the eleven suspects and then placed them in provisional
detention. [@1]

In the same operation the gendarmes discovered 2.5 hectares of Indian hemp
plantation and nearly 800 kg of cannabis. [@1] The discovery was made during the
night of Wednesday to Thursday 10 October in the margins of the operation. [@1]
All of the drugs that were recovered were destroyed in full with the agreement
of the prosecutor’s office. [@1]

The territorial gendarmerie company based at Ambilobe, in the north-west of the
island, found the large Indian hemp plantation. [@1] The north-west of
Madagascar, described as known for growing and selling Indian hemp, had been
combed through by the authorities. [@1] The operation had been launched after
months of national gendarmerie investigations in the districts of Ambanja and
Ambilobe. [@1] The crop is Indian hemp, also described as cannabis. [@1]
