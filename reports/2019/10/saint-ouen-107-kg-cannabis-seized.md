In three days, police intercepted more than 107 kilos of cannabis in Saint-Ouen,
in Seine-Saint-Denis. [@1] Two people were arrested in connection with the
seizures. [@1]

Officers seized more than 107 kg of cannabis and hashish during the previous
week. [@1] They also seized more than 13,000 euros in cash and at least two
handguns. [@1] According to Le Parisien, the seizures followed intelligence
gathered by officers. [@1]
