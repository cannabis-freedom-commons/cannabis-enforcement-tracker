On 21 and 22 October 2019 a joint RSIPF team uprooted more than 5,500 marijuana plants at several locations in Mbarande, North East Guadalcanal. No one was arrested at the time, and police asked village chiefs, church leaders and youth leaders to help identify the growers. The same area had lost more than 4,000 plants in a raid on 24 June 2019, when a man in his 20s was arrested and police put the crop's street value at SBD 500,000 to SBD 1 million. [@1,2]

Police carried four truckloads of uprooted marijuana plants from the gardens beside the road to Hells Point, east of Honiara. [@3] Police burned the plants of one garden close to Koivo Village where they stood, one and a half kilometres from the nearest vehicle access. [@3] Three gardens lay on the other side of the Mbarande Bridge and one big garden further down, about a kilometre's drive from Koivo Village. [@3]
