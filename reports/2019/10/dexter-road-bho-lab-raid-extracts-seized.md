On 23 October 2019, the Oregon State Police Northwest Region Marijuana team
executed a search warrant at a rural property in the 38000 block of Dexter
Road near Dexter. [@1,2] Inspectors from the Oregon Liquor and Cannabis
Commission's medical marijuana division had found the illegal extraction
operation during a compliance check of the site, which was registered as an
Oregon Medical Marijuana Program grow but was not licensed to process cannabis.
[@1,2]

The extraction lab was running in a makeshift outdoor structure with unsanitary
conditions, using butane hash-oil machines to make concentrated cannabis oil.
[@1,2] Officers seized about 216 pounds (about 98 kg) of cannabis extracts and
two butane hash-oil machines valued at $120,000. [@1,2]

Ten firearms, one of them reported stolen, were seized from the property along
with items relating to the manufacture and sale of counterfeit vape cartridges.
[@2] The federal Drug Enforcement Administration's Eugene office and the Lane
County Sheriff's Office assisted in the raid. [@1,2] Charges of unlawful
possession of a marijuana item, unlawful delivery of a marijuana item and
unlawful manufacture of a marijuana item against two suspects were forwarded to
the Lane County district attorney for consideration. [@1,2]
