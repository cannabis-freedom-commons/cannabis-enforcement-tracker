Gendarmerie officers stopped a Turkmen citizen on a bicycle in Göynük, in the
Kemer district of Antalya province, on 3 October 2019, and found drugs in a pack
of wet wipes in the bicycle basket. [@2]

The package held 31 packets of 110 grams each of skunk, a cannabis product,
prepared for sale, together with two 3-gram packets of Afghan hashish and a
stimulant. [@2] The man was detained and was arrested at the courthouse on 4
October after questioning. [@2]

Azattyq reported the arrest on 7 October, citing the Turkish outlet
Haberler.com, and said it had not yet learned the details of the case or the
measures taken against the man from the Antalya police department. [@1] The
reporting gives no court outcome, no charge and no prison sentence for the man.
[@2]
