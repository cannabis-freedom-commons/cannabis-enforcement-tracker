Covina police arrested Isaac Martinez at an apartment in Azusa on 17 October
2019 and booked him on suspicion of drug-related charges. [@1] Officers found a
2-year-old child in the apartment, and the child was placed in the care of the
county department of child and family services. [@1] Martinez was held on
$100,000 bail, posted bond and was released early Friday morning according to
county jail records. [@1] A second man was also at the apartment during the
arrest, whom police declined to identify. [@2]

During the raid officers found approximately 25 pounds of marijuana, about 11.34
kg, in the apartment. [@1] Officers also found about 12,000 pills that appeared
to be Xanax and $1,200 in cash in the apartment. [@1] Officers also found 7.5
ounces of cocaine in the same raid. [@1] Police also recovered a machine used to
produce and press pills at the apartment. [@1]

The investigation began about two weeks earlier after a student at a Covina-area
high school was caught with what looked like Xanax pills without a prescription.
[@1] Investigators tracked a possible source of the drugs to an apartment in the
400 block of North San Gabriel Avenue in Azusa. [@1] Investigators said they
believed Martinez had made several sales to students at the Covina high school
and were probing whether he was part of a larger dealing enterprise. [@1] Police
said the pills found in the apartment had not yet been tested to confirm their
contents. [@1]
