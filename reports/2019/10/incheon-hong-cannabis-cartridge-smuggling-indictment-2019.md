Incheon District Prosecutors' Office indicted an 18-year-old woman without
detention on 18 October 2019 on a charge of violating the Narcotics Control Act
over cannabis and other drugs brought into South Korea. [@2] The case was
assigned to the 15th criminal division of Incheon District Court, but no first
hearing date had been set. [@2] The woman was a daughter of Hong Jeong-wook, a
former lawmaker of the Hannara Party, the predecessor of the Liberty Korea
Party. [@2] Prosecutors said they could not disclose the specific allegations
because the suspect was a minor. [@1]

On 27 September 2019 she arrived at Incheon International Airport on a flight
from Honolulu, Hawaii, having brought liquid cannabis cartridges and other drugs
in her luggage. [@2] JTBC reported that she hid the cartridges and other drugs
in a travel bag and in her clothes pockets. [@1] An X-ray check during arrival
immigration screening flagged her, and the accounts also say she brought in LSD,
a paper drug placed on the tongue, and stimulants. [@2]

She admitted all the allegations in questioning but said she had no intention of
distributing the cannabis she brought in. [@2] Prosecutors had urgently arrested
her and sought a detention warrant, but a court refused it, citing no risk of
destroying evidence or fleeing and noting she was a minor with no criminal
record. [@2] The detention warrant was sought on 30 September 2019 and refused,
and the EToday account says she was also accused of having smoked the cannabis
several times abroad. [@3] The Fact reported that prosecutors requested a
detention warrant for her on 30 September 2019 and the court refused it, taking
into account that she was a minor with no prior record. [@4]
