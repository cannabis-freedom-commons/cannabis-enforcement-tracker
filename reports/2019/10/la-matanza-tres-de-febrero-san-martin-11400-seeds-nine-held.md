Gendarmería Nacional officers searched 10 properties in the Buenos Aires
districts of La Matanza, Tres de Febrero and San Martín on the orders of the
Juzgado Federal en lo Criminal y Correccional Nº 2. [@1] The raids followed
seven months of investigative work against an organisation that sold marijuana
seeds. [@1] Officers detained nine members of the organisation, six men and
three women. [@1]

Officers seized 11,400 marijuana seeds in bulk and 867 high-potency blisters.
[@1] The searches also produced 1 kilo 914 grams of "creepy" marijuana, buds and
1.2 litres of cannabis oil. [@1] Gendarmes also took six cars, cash in five
currencies, grow lights, an electronic scale and a banknote counter. [@1]

One searched property was fitted out to look like a company office but served as
a "búnker", a fortified base for the group, with an alarm system. [@1] The
organisation flew seeds in from Spain, with stopovers in Chile or Uruguay, and
sold them through a Facebook page, delivering by hand or by post. [@1] The seeds
were "feminised" so that buyers would grow female plants, which Gendarmería said
produce more buds than male plants. [@1]

The Juzgado Federal Nº 2, without naming its judge, ordered the detentions and
the seizure of the seeds, buds and other evidence. [@1] MDZ Online reported the
same operation the same day with identical figures, describing the organisation
as a "multinational" of marijuana seeds. [@2]
