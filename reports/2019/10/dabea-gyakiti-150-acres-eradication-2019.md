The police destroyed 150 acres of cannabis farms, with a street value of more than a million dollars, by spraying them at Dabea-Gyakiti near Akosombo in the Eastern Region. [@1] Four suspects, including the owners of the farms and labourers whose names were withheld for security reasons, were arrested to assist in investigations. [@1]

The two-day operation was led by Superintendent David Selom Hukportie, the director of the Drug Law Enforcement Unit of the CID headquarters, together with the Narcotic Control Board. [@1] Hukportie told the Ghanaian Times the operation followed police intelligence that uncovered large-scale cultivation of cannabis on the mountains and in the enclaves of the area. [@1]
