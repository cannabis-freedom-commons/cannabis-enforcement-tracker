Inspectors with Sonoma County's Permit and Resource Management Department found unpermitted commercial cannabis cultivation on five properties south of Santa Rosa on 28 August 2019. [@1,2] The county called it the largest non-permitted commercial cannabis cultivation ever found there, with 63,241 plants in total. [@1,2]

Most of the plants were on two properties, one holding 26,650 plants on 2.5 acres and the other 36,685 plants on 3.3 acres, department spokeswoman Maggie Fleming said. [@1] Some plants were interspersed with corn, and both properties sit in endangered California tiger salamander habitat. [@1] The previous largest illegal cultivation shut down in the county had held 13,000 plants. [@1]

## A year of complaints

County inspectors had responded to 195 complaints of unpermitted cannabis activity since January, abating about 161,000 plants before this find. [@1] Trade press noted the county framed the five-property bust as its record illegal grow operation. [@2]
