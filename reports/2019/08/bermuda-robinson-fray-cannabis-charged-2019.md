Zadun Robinson, 20, of Sandys, and Kanhai Fray, 32, of Southampton, were charged
with possessing cannabis with intent to supply on 28 August 2018 in St George's.
[@1] Robinson was also charged with possessing THC, the psychoactive component
of cannabis, on the same date. [@1]

The court heard that Robinson was alleged to have 7.136 kilograms of cannabis
and 91.6 grams of THC. [@1] Fray was alleged to have 6.238 kilograms of
cannabis. [@1] The drugs were given a total street value in court of $473,450
for Robinson and $311,905 for Fray, $785,355 together. [@1]

Both men were also charged with conspiracy to import drugs between an unknown
date and 28 August 2018. [@1] They were both due to appear in the Magistrates'
Court on the day of the report; Fray failed to appear and a warrant for his
arrest was issued. [@1] Senior magistrate Juan Wolffe released Robinson on
$25,000 bail with orders to surrender his travel documents and report to
Hamilton Police Station three times a week. [@1] No pleas were entered because
the case must be heard in the Supreme Court, and it was adjourned to the October
arraignments session. [@1]
