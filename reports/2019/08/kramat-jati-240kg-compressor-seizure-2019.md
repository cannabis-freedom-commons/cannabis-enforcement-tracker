Indonesia's National Narcotics Agency (BNN) arrested six alleged couriers and seized 240 kg of marijuana hidden inside compressor cylinders and nitrogen cylinders in Kramat Jati, East Jakarta. [@1,2] Officers trailed the suspects through Thursday 8 August 2019 and arrested four that afternoon at a rented house used as a storehouse behind the state elementary school SDN Kramat Jati 02 Pagi, catching the other two alleged couriers later. [@1,2] The BNN confiscated the marijuana together with the cylinders and a toolbox kept in the house. [@1,2]

## From Aceh, a prison link

The marijuana had been brought from Aceh and was to be distributed in Jakarta and West Jakarta. [@1,2] BNN eradication deputy head Insp. Gen. Arman Depari said the six faced possible death sentences for possessing and transporting a large amount, and that investigators would establish whether they were fully involved in a distribution syndicate or only helping it. [@1,2] The agency said it believed the distribution from Aceh was controlled by inmates of the Cirebon Class I Penitentiary in West Java, and that the storehouse belonged to a former drug convict who had served a four-year sentence. [@1,2]
