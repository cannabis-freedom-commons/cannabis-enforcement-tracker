Officers of Niger's Central Office for the Repression of Illicit Drug
Trafficking, acting from the office's branch in Konni department in
south-central Niger, dismantled an international drug-trafficking network. [@1]
The operation seized 111 kg of cannabis resin and police arrested two
individuals. [@1] China.org.cn carried the same account of the Konni operation
credited to Niger's public radio. [@2]

According to Abba Adam, the head of the Konni branch, the drugs were found after
an investigation and had been hidden inside car tyres. [@1] The Aviseur
International reprint repeats that the drugs seized after investigation had been
hidden in car tyres. [@3] Adam said other individuals were still being sought
and that investigators were on the right track to catch them. [@1]

Abba Lélé, prefect of Konni department, went to the scene to congratulate and
encourage the police officers for the result, which he said was achieved with
the collaboration of the population. [@1] Konni department borders Nigeria. [@1]
