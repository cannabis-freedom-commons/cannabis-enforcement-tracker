Officers of the Directorate of Revenue Intelligence's Visakhapatnam regional unit seized 1,015
kilograms of ganja from a Madhya Pradesh-registered truck on 28 August 2019. [@1,2] The truck was
stopped on the Chennai-Srikakulam highway after specific intelligence, and though its container
looked empty, interrogation of the driver revealed a false partition holding 365 brown
packets. [@1] The local crime blotter places the interception at NAD Junction in Visakhapatnam
city. [@2]

The DRI valued the haul at Rs 1,52,25,000 on the grey market. [@1,2] The cannabis was being
brought from the Visakhapatnam Agency tribal areas and was destined for Madhya Pradesh. [@1,2]
The seizure was made under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, and the truck
driver was arrested. [@2]
