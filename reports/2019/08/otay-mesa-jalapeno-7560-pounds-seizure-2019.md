CBP officers at the Otay Mesa cargo facility in San Diego seized almost four tons of marijuana concealed in a shipment of jalapeño peppers on 15 August 2019, in a load valued at about $2.3 million. [@1,2] Officers stopped the truck on the Thursday evening, a canine team alerted to the palletised peppers, and a search found a leafy-green substance that field-tested positive for marijuana. [@1,2]

Officers pulled 314 large wrapped packages of marijuana, weighing 7,560 pounds (about 3.43 tonnes), from among the red and green peppers. [@1,2] CBP seized the truck and the drugs; Otay Mesa Port Director Rosa Hernandez said the seizure kept millions of dollars from a transnational criminal organisation. [@1,2]
