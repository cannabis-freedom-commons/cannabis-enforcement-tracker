Security Minister Patricia Bullrich and Naval Prefect chief Eduardo Scarzello
presented the results of an anti-drug operation in Puerto Libertad that ended
with the seizure of almost 5,000 kilograms of marijuana in the border zone with
Paraguay. [@1] Prefectura Naval Argentina detected the load in a refuge the
previous week, where it was hidden to be collected later. [@1] Bullrich visited
the exact spot where the drug was found to show the results, saying officers had
worked in a hard-to-reach zone where traffickers were cooling the drug to take
it to urban centres. [@1]

Intelligence work by Prefectura officers led them to a natural refuge at
kilometre 1,865 of the Paraná River, where the packages lay under a curtain of
branches and leaves. [@1] Officers recovered 4,951 kilograms and 330 grams of
marijuana, which the force said could supply about 15 million doses. [@1]
Scarzello said the marijuana had been cooled, meaning stored, to later move it
to urban centres for sale. [@1]

Officials said the load was waiting for the rest of the gang to distribute it.
[@1] The Federal Court of Eldorado, under Miguel Ángel Guerrero, took charge of
the case. [@1]
