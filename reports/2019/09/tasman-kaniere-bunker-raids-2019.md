Tasman police ended a months-long covert investigation into commercial cannabis cultivation on the West Coast with the arrest of a 72-year-old Ngahere man on 9 September and a search of a large rural property at Kaniere days later. [@1] At Kaniere officers found a very large underground bunker holding a sophisticated cannabis growing operation, along with a large number of firearms. [@1]

## Two men charged

The Ngahere man was charged with possession of cannabis for supply and due in Christchurch District Court. [@1] A 63-year-old man arrested at Kaniere was charged with cultivating and selling cannabis and appeared in Greymouth District Court on 18 September. [@1] Police restrained assets estimated at $5 million. [@1]

Later trial coverage of the same raids reported officers had found more than 800 plants, not counting seedlings or harvested cannabis, in a 240-square-metre bunker buried three metres underground and entered through a shipping container. [@2,3]

Milne, 68, and Harris, 77, were found guilty in March 2024 and were jailed on 24 October 2024. [@4] Judge Paul Kellar sentenced Milne to six years in prison for cultivating, possessing for sale and selling cannabis. [@3] Police said the bunker could produce about 136 kilograms of cannabis a year with a street value of more than $1 million. [@3]
