Two men were charged in Bermuda's Magistrates' Court on 2 September 2019 with
possessing cannabis and cannabis resin together worth an estimated $550,000.
[@1] Charles Butterfield, 33, was charged with possessing cannabis and cannabis
resin with intent to supply in St George's on 20 December 2018. [@1] Kinte
Smith, also 33, was charged with possessing cannabis resin in St George's
between an unknown date and 20 December 2018. [@1]

The court heard that the two, both from St George's, were alleged to have more
than five kilograms of cannabis resin with an estimated street value of
$500,000. [@1] It was further alleged that Smith had about one kilogram of
cannabis valued at $50,000. [@1] Neither man had to enter a plea because the
case must be heard in the Supreme Court. [@1]

Magistrate Maxanne Anderson released the pair on $10,000 bail and ordered them
to hand over their passports, adjourning the case to the October arraignments
session at the Supreme Court. [@1] Police had searched Butterfield's home at
Ferry Close in St George's under a warrant on 20 December 2018, and he told
officers the drugs were his. [@2] An analyst found 5,029.2 grams of cannabis
resin and a further 1,005.4 grams of cannabis in heat-sealed packages in the
house, valued in court at $502,950 and $50,250. [@2]

Smith was arrested on 22 July 2019 and charged on one count of handling a
controlled drug, namely cannabis resin. [@2] On the Supreme Court indictment
Butterfield faced two counts of possessing a controlled drug with intent to
supply, cannabis resin and cannabis, while Smith faced the single handling
count. [@2]
