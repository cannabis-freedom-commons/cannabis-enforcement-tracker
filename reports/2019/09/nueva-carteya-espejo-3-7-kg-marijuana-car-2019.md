Guardia Civil officers from Nueva Carteya stopped a car at a check point for
people and vehicles on the N-432 road in the municipality of Espejo, Córdoba
province. [@1] Officers noticed an intense smell of marijuana coming from the
car and searched it with the help of a sniffer dog from the command's canine
unit. [@1] The search found packages hidden in different parts of the car
containing 3 kilograms and 700 grams of marijuana, which officers seized. [@1]

The driver, a 34-year-old man from Salamanca province, was arrested as the
suspected author of drug trafficking. [@1] The detainee, the seized drug and the
case file were handed to the judicial authority. [@1] The Guardia Civil accused
him of a crime against public health, through drug trafficking, and said the
action fell under the national security plans applied in the province. [@2]
