A St Petersburg court found 19-year-old American student Audrey Eliza Lorber
guilty of illegal drug possession and fined her 15,000 rubles, about $235. [@1]
Because she had been held in pre-trial detention, the court released her from
serving the sentence and from custody. [@3] Lorber, a film student at Pace
University in New York who lives on Staten Island, was travelling in St
Petersburg with her mother. [@3]

She was searched at Pulkovo Airport and found with 19.05 grams of cannabis in
her belongings. [@1] She had a US medical-marijuana document, and the St
Petersburg court system said that arrangement did not apply on Russian
territory. [@1] The St Petersburg court system wrote on its Telegram channel
that she was fined after being found guilty of drug possession. [@1]

A St Petersburg district court, the Moscow District Court, convicted Lorber
under Part 1 of Article 228 of the Russian Criminal Code for illegal possession
of drugs. [@2] She had been held in a pre-trial detention centre since late
July. [@2] Lorber admitted her guilt to the court. [@2] Russian authorities said
the US patent she carried for marijuana use under a medical programme had no
effect in Russia. [@2]
