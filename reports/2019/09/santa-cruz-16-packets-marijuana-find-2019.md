Police on a patrol in Santa Cruz, Trinidad, found 16 packets of marijuana at the
Arnold Dwarika Recreation Grounds. [@1] Police said the patrol began at midday
and ended at 4 pm. [@1] Officers found the drugs in plastic packets at the
grounds. [@1]

Police said no one was held in relation to the find. [@1] Santa Cruz CID were
continuing enquiries. [@1]

The exercise was led by Inspector Marshall and supervised by Corporal Maharaj.
[@1] Constables Mykoo, De Francois and Walters also took part. [@1]
