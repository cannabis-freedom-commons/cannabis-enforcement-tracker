Two men, aged 31 and 32, who were cultivating the plantation were arrested, and
officers said they were carrying a shotgun, a homemade muzzle-loading type known
locally as a batebucha. [@1] On the afternoon of Thursday, 12 September 2019,
officers of the 27th Battalion of the Military Police destroyed 8,000 cannabis
plants on a vicinal rural road at Taquariz in the municipality of Presidente
Juscelino, Maranhao. [@1] The two men were presented at the regional police
station in Rosario after their arrest. [@1]

The battalion commander, Major Pereira, said the battalion's Advanced Service
Group had identified four cannabis plots in scrubland near the settlement. [@1]
On receiving that information the patrol teams went straight to the site to
support the Advanced Service Group team in eradicating the crop. [@1]

The plantation was spread across four plots, along with several smaller
seedbeds. [@1] Police said the crop was already at harvesting point when the
officers counted 8,000 plants and burned them on the spot. [@1]
