Argentina's security minister Patricia Bullrich travelled to Paraguay to jointly
lead Operation Integration with Paraguay's anti-drug chief Arnaldo Giuzzio. [@1]
The eradication in the San Rafael reserve in Itapua was carried out jointly by
Argentina's Gendarmerie and Paraguayan security forces. [@1] The results of the
joint 10-day operation were presented at a press conference at Encarnacion
airport by Giuzzio, Bullrich and anti-drug prosecutor Raquel Bordon. [@2]

Officials said the eradication would total about 345,000 kilos of marijuana,
representing more than 1,000 million doses valued at more than 300 million
dollars. [@1] Bullrich said Argentina seizes about 180 tonnes a year, so the 345
tonnes destroyed in 10 days equalled about a year and a half of Argentine
seizures. [@2]

The crops were located by geolocation in the reserve's wooded zone through
information exchanged by Argentina's Security Ministry, the Gendarmerie and
Paraguay's Senad. [@2] Giuzzio said the operation confirmed serious
environmental damage because forests had been cleared to plant marijuana. [@2]
Official calculations put the yield of the crops at about 3,000 kilograms per
hectare. [@1] Authorities estimated the cultivated area in Paraguay's
centre-north at about 6,000 hectares of wooded zones and forest reserves. [@1]
