Police in Samburu, Kwale County, arrested two men and seized about seven sacks of bhang with a street value of Sh5 million on 4 September 2019. [@1] Jared Omondi and Caleb Otieno were caught trying to smuggle the drugs into the country from neighbouring Tanzania. [@1,2]

The pair drove a vehicle with a fake registration number and were bound for Mombasa. [@1] After defying an order to stop, they changed cars during a police pursuit before being caught. [@2] Mombasa criminal investigations officer Anthony Muriithi said the consignment came from the Migori border area and was destined for Likoni and other parts of the coast, calling it the biggest bhang seizure in Mombasa that year. [@1,2]

The suspects were expected to be arraigned in court later that Wednesday. [@1]
