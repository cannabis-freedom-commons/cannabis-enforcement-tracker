Rodney Tomasi was acquitted of possessing cannabis. [@1] The cannabis acquittal
was because evidence from a search was ruled inadmissible. [@1] He was also
acquitted of offering a bribe to police officers because that charge was
unproven. [@1]

The Supreme Court issued the ruling on July 17. [@1]
