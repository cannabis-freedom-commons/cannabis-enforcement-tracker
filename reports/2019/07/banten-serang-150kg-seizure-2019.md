Banten narcotics officers captured two suspected syndicate members, identified only as FN
and IG, who were crossing the Banten area with 150 kilograms of marijuana hidden in a
car's baggage compartment under a welded iron plate. [@1,2] Satnarkoba Polda Banten
members and local Muspida officials watched as officers checked the packages before their
destruction at the BNN Banten building in Serang on 31 July 2019. [@1] BNN officers fed
the packages into the incinerator, and the two suspects were presented alongside the
evidence at the BNN office. [@2,3]
