Police in Migori County destroyed 1,720 kilograms of bhang at Lichota in Suna West after obtaining
a court order on 4 July 2019 covering exhibits whose cases were complete. [@1] County Commander
Joseph Nthenge led the burning with security officials and officers from the environmental, public
health and judicial services. [@1]

## A year of seizures

The bhang had been seized by police across the county over the previous year, with Suna East and
Suna West accounting for 1,130 kilograms and smaller amounts from Nyatike, Kuria West and
Uriri. [@1] Nthenge said most of the bhang reached Migori in transit from Tanzania's Mara region,
and warned vehicle owners that hired-out cars caught carrying narcotics would be forfeited. [@1]

## Value confirmed later

A Kenya News Agency account two months later confirmed the same July destruction at Lichota,
valuing the burned bhang at Sh17.2 million. [@2]
