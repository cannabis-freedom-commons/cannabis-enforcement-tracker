The driver's fine came to 23,640 dollars, the court in Quito said. [@2]

El Tiempo reported 24 people killed in the crash and three convicted in Ecuador
that July. [@1] Sixteen people had been arrested for drug trafficking in the
case by August 2019. [@1]

Eight of them had been convicted: three in Ecuador in July 2019 and five in
Colombia. [@1] Colombian prosecutors said Juan Gabriel Céspedes González
coordinated the July 2018 shipment, loaded in Cali with Chile as its final
destination, where the marijuana would triple in value. [@1] The Colombian
convictions were against members of a band called Los Mercaderes de la Frontera,
which prosecutors said kept sending drug-loaded buses south by the same method.
[@1] In Ibagué, Jairo Espinosa Méndez, known as 'Gafas', and his wife Lina
Ximena Pinzón Cárdenas were each sentenced in 2019 to 10 years and 10 months.
[@1] Ana Lucía Salamanca Mendieta received four years in prison and Érika
Tatiana Pinzón Cárdenas two years and eight months. [@1] Luis Adolfo Castro
Villanueva, arrested in August 2018 guarding about 100 kilos of marijuana, was
the first member convicted, drawing five years and four months in April 2019.
[@1] José Ómar Araque Ortiz, arrested in Pereira in September 2018, was
sentenced in 2019 to six years and three months. [@1] Claudia Orozco, who
promoted the cut-price tour in Ecuador, was held in a Bogotá prison on an
Interpol red notice requested by an Ecuadorian judge. [@1]
