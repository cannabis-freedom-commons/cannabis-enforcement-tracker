Hai Van Le, also known as Trung Dung Le, a Vietnamese national born in 1981,
pleaded guilty at Aberdeen Sheriff Court on 21 January 2019 to being concerned
with others in supplying cannabis and to producing cannabis between 19 June
and 3 July 2018. [@1,2] He and a co-accused were found during a search of a
property in Aberdeen where a significant growing operation was in progress:
440 juvenile and 587 mature cannabis plants, with a potential combined street
value of between £225,000 and £831,000. [@1]

The sheriff sentenced him to three years in prison, discounted from four
years for the guilty plea. [@1] Le said a gang that trafficked him into
Britain had forced him to look after cannabis plants for around ten years
without pay, but the Home Office had rejected his trafficking claim and the
sheriff found his position fell short of coercion. [@1] On 10 July 2019 Lords
Brodie and Turnbull refused his appeal against the sentence, calling the
three-year term possibly lenient. [@1,2]
