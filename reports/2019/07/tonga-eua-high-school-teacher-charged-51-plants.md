A 42-year-old male teacher at 'Eua High School, on Tonga's 'Eua island, has been
charged with cultivating cannabis. [@1] He was arrested on 27 June 2019 while
watering the plants, which grew inside and around the school compound. [@4,5]

Police seized 51 cannabis plants in all, 33 from inside the school compound and
18 from an area immediately outside it. [@2] The deputy commissioner, Pelenatita
Vaisuai, said 51 cannabis plants had been found growing within the school
grounds as part of an ongoing drugs operation. [@3]

The accused, a teacher from Tufuvai on 'Eua, has not been named and was still
detained in police custody. [@2] He was expected to appear in the Magistrate's
Court in 'Eua on 3 July 2019 while the investigation continued. [@2] Vaisuai
said the find was a significant result of an operation meant to help the
community feel safe and reduce drug-related harm. [@4]
