Police from the Dobele precinct dismantled two sites where cannabis was being
grown in Penkule and Ķegums parishes early in July 2019, the Zemgale Regional
Administration said. [@1] The operation was carried out within a criminal case
that had already been opened. [@1]

During searches at a privately owned non-residential building, officers found 69
cannabis seedlings and 54 recently planted bushes in a purpose-built greenhouse.
[@1] Police seized a total of 2,054 grams of undried marijuana there. [@1]

In further investigative steps police shut down a second site in Ķegums, where
the growing operation had been set up inside a residential house and a
greenhouse standing next to it. [@1] The search of the private house yielded 16
bushes and the greenhouse five more, and officers also found several packages of
already dried material. [@1]

Police detained two men born in 1973 and 1994. [@1] The younger man, described
as a user of the substance, was found carrying 2.7317 grams of marijuana at the
time of his detention. [@1] All the seized substances were sent for expert
examination. [@1] Both men were given a preventive measure not involving
deprivation of liberty, and investigators were to check whether either of them
was linked to other crimes. [@1] The State Police had opened criminal
proceedings in the case. [@1]
