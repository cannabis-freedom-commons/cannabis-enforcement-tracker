Police in Savannakhet province seized a large marijuana crop that a villager had
planted clandestinely inside a rubber plantation in Somsa-ath village, in Kaysone
Phomvihane city. [@1,2] The raid took place on the Monday before the 1 August 2019
report, which was 29 July 2019. [@1,2]

Officers netted 166 growing marijuana plants and another 154 harvested plants that
had already been dried, 320 plants in total. [@1,2] The reports name no suspects,
describe no arrests and give no weight for the dried plants. [@1,2]
