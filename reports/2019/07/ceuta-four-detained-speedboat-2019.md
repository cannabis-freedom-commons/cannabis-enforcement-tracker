The Civil Guard's Maritime Service detained four people after a chase that ended
with their semirigid narcolancha, powered by three engines, aground on a Ceuta
beach on the morning of Sunday 28 July 2019. [@2]

Mowatine reported that Spanish media said the four men, aged 42, 38, 33 and 24,
were suspected of smuggling hashish from Morocco to Spain aboard a Phantom-type
fast boat. [@1] The four fled when they saw the patrol, ran their boat aground
and were arrested on land by coast patrols. [@2]

El Faro de Ceuta said no drugs were aboard the boat, which was being
investigated, and that the men were for now accused of disobedience. [@2]
Mowatine reported that Spain's judicial police placed the suspects under police
custody to investigate them and establish where the boat came from. [@1]

La Vanguardia, reporting the same four arrests, said they were accused of
contraband, disobedience and resisting officers. [@3] La Vanguardia described
narcolanchas as semirigid boats used to transport hashish from Morocco to
mainland Spain. [@3]
