On the morning of Tuesday, 30 July 2019, detectives with the Illegal Marijuana
Enforcement Team (IMET) served search warrants at two neighbouring properties
in the 900-block and 600-block of Upper River Road near Gold Hill. [@1,2]
Detectives found indoor and outdoor growing operations with more than 1,000
marijuana plants, which they described as a black-market collective with no
permits to grow or process marijuana for recreational, commercial or medical
use. [@1,2]

Detectives also found a laboratory producing butane honey oil, a concentrated
form of cannabis. [@1,2] An Oregon State Police team was called in to help
safely dismantle the lab. [@1]

Two suspects present during the warrants were arrested: Michael Glen Newmann,
66, of the 900-block of Upper River Road, and Joshua Carey of Homedale, Idaho.
[@1,2] Newmann was jailed on charges related to the unlawful possession,
manufacture and delivery of marijuana and butane honey oil, while Carey was
jailed for unlawful manufacture of marijuana. [@1,2] IMET detectives were
assisted by the Medford Area Drug and Gang Enforcement team and Jackson County
sheriff's deputies. [@1]
