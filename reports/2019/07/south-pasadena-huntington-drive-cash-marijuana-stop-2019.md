A South Pasadena police officer stopped a driver in the 1100 block of Huntington
Drive at about 1 a.m. on 9 July 2019 after noticing a broken tail light. [@1]
Approaching the car, the officer smelled burnt marijuana, and the driver, the
only person inside, admitted he had been smoking marijuana while driving. [@1]
The driver told officers there were "a few pounds" of marijuana in the trunk,
and with his consent they opened it. [@1]

South Pasadena Police Cpl. Craig Phillips said officers found almost three
pounds of marijuana and $116,000 in cash in the trunk, which the man could not
explain. [@1] The man, a 30-year-old Los Angeles resident whose name police did
not release, was taken to the police station jail. [@1] Officers later searched
his home under a warrant and seized "large amounts of marijuana and firearms".
[@1]

Phillips said the man was facing multiple possible felony charges. [@1]
SGVCityWatch reported that the vehicle was stopped at about 1 a.m. in the 1100
block of Huntington Drive and that the driver was booked on suspicion of
illegally transporting marijuana for sale. [@2] The same account said officers
found large amounts of marijuana and illegal firearms in the home. [@2]
