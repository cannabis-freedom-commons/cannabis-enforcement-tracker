Two Jamaican men, aged 33 and 59, were on board a drug canoe that police found
drifting about 50 miles east of Grand Cayman on Wednesday 25 July 2019. [@2]
Officers aboard the RCIPS Joint Marine Unit vessel MV Mark Luke came alongside
the boat at about 4:30pm and found a substantial amount of ganja in numerous
packages. [@2] Police seized about 1,000 pounds of ganja (about 469 kg) and the
vessel, and arrested both men on suspicion of drug trafficking. [@2]

Police said the drugs had an estimated street value of more than CI$1 million.
[@1] Rovan Johnson, 33, and Albert Campbell, 59, appeared in court on Monday 29
July 2019 charged with possession of more than 1,000 pounds of ganja intended
for importation to Cayman. [@1] Both men were remanded in custody until 19
August 2019, when they were expected to answer the charges. [@1]

Cayman News Service described the seizure as one of the biggest hauls in recent
years by the police service. [@1]
