Moroccan police seized 27.3 tonnes of cannabis resin, a record haul, from three trucks at the Tanger Med port on the evening of Sunday 7 July 2019. [@1] The trucks were believed to be transporting industrial equipment to Europe, the General Directorate of National Security (DGSN) said in a statement the next day. [@1]

The resin was divided among 16 containers in the trailers of the three trucks. [@1] The drivers and their three assistants, all Moroccan, were arrested and taken into police custody. [@1] Tanger Med in northern Morocco is one of Africa's largest ports and sits within sight of the Spanish coast. [@1]
