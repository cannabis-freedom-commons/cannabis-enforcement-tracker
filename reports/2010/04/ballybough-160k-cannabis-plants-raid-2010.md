Gardaí from Pearse Street seized cannabis plants and arrested two males following a search in the Ballybough area of Dublin on the afternoon of 2 April 2010. [@1] Detective gardaí arrested the two males, both in their thirties, after searching a house in the area, seizing approximately €160,000 worth of cannabis plants, subject to analysis. [@1] The scene was preserved for a technical examination, and the two men were detained at Pearse Street Garda station under Section 2 of the Criminal Justice Drug Trafficking Act 2006. [@1]
