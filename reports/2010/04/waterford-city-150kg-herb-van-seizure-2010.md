Gardaí and officers from Revenue's Customs Service seized 150kg of cannabis herb in Waterford City on the morning of 20 April 2010. [@1] The herbal cannabis was seized when a van was stopped in the city centre, and subject to analysis the drugs were said to carry a street value of up to €1.8 million. [@1]

A man in his fifties, an Irish national, was arrested at the scene and detained at Waterford Garda station under Section 2 of the Criminal Justice Drug Trafficking Act 1996, under which he could be held for up to seven days. [@1]
