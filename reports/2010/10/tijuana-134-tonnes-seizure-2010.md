Heavily armed soldiers and police seized a record marijuana haul from three homes in Tijuana
early on 18 October 2010 after a shootout with suspected traffickers. [@1,2] Police had noticed
a suspicious convoy on the city's roads, and after armed escorts of a marijuana-laden truck
exchanged fire and were subdued, they told officers where the rest of the drugs were stored.
[@2] One officer and one suspect were injured in the exchange of fire, and 11 suspects were
arrested. [@1,2]

The marijuana, wrapped in more than 15,000 packages and held in shipping containers, was bound
for the United States across the border near San Diego. [@1,2] Army Gen. Alfonso Duarte Mujica
put its Mexican street value at 4.2 billion pesos, about $340 million, a figure that would have
risen had the drugs crossed the border. [@2] The first estimate of 105 tonnes grew as counting
continued, and on 20 October the authorities gave a final weight of 134 tonnes. [@1,2]
Government security spokesman Alejandro Poire called it the biggest seizure of marijuana packed
for sale in the country's history and said it probably belonged to the Sinaloa cartel. [@1]
