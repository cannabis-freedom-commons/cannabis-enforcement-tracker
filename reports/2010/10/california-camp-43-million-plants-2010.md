California's Campaign Against Marijuana Planting (CAMP) reported 4,320,314
plant seizures in the 2010 season, slightly less than the all-time record of
4,463,917 set in 2009. [@1,2] CAMP estimated the destroyed crop's wholesale
value at $17.2 billion, working out to $4,000 per plant and over a pound per
plant. [@1,2]

At that rate the marijuana destroyed amounted to more than four times
California's estimated annual consumption of one million pounds a year, and
CAMP's haul had grown tenfold since 2002-2003. [@1,2] The leading counties
were Mendocino with 572,680 plants, Tuolumne with 411,004, Lake with 374,958,
Shasta with 325,480 and Tehama with 312,574. [@1,2]
