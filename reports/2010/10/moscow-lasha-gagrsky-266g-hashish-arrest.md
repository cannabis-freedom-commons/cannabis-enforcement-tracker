Officers of the Moscow criminal investigation department detained crime figure
Lasha Jologua, known as Lasha Gagrsky. [@1] The 39-year-old Jologua was detained
on Wednesday outside house No. 11, building 3, on Shvernika Street in the
Akademichesky neighbourhood. [@1] Life News described Jologua as the right hand
of crime figure Aslan Usoyan and said Moscow MUR operatives caught him on
Wednesday evening in south-west Moscow. [@2]

During a search of his clothing, officers found 26.6 grams of hashish. [@1] Life
News reported that officers found 26 grams of a narcotic substance believed to
be hashish on Gagrsky. [@2]

Moscow detectives had received information that a consignment of hashish had
reached Jologua and placed him under surveillance. [@1] He planned to pass it in
parts to thieves in law held in pre-trial detention. [@1] On questioning, Lasha
Gagrsky confirmed that he was a thief in law, saying he had just left home for
the hairdresser when he was detained. [@1] After the detention Jologua was taken
to a lockup while investigative actions were carried out and a decision on
opening a criminal case was pending. [@2]
