The Turkish gendarmerie confiscated more than six tons of hashish in the southeastern province of Diyarbakır on Monday 25 October 2010, the Anatolia news agency reported. [@1] Gendarmerie forces staged operations in seven villages in the districts of Lice and Hazro and seized 6.1 tons of the drug. [@1]
