Gardaí from the Wicklow Division and the Garda National Drugs Unit searched a house in the Ashford area on 28 October 2010 as part of an ongoing operation and uncovered a cannabis growing operation. [@1,2] The raided property was a large rented rural farmhouse at the Cullenmore bends outside the village of Ashford, and officers seized cannabis plants at various stages of growth with a potential street value of €500,000 subject to analysis. [@1,2,3]

The factory held over 1,000 cannabis plants along with humidifiers, heaters and other drug-related equipment. [@3] Garda sources believed an Asian gang was using the factory as a moneymaking operation to fund wider criminal activity. [@3] People suspected of running the enterprise were at the property when officers arrived but escaped into the surrounding area despite pursuit by the Garda helicopter, and no arrests had been made. [@3]
