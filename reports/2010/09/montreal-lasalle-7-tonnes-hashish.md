The Royal Canadian Mounted Police seized about seven tonnes of hashish on Sunday, 12 September 2010, from an abandoned container in Montréal’s LaSalle borough. [@1,2,3,4,5] Police had received a tip about the container after it sat in a storage lot for several days and obtained a warrant to search it. [@3,4,5]

The container, marked HJCU 8291013, had been left on a trailer with no vehicle attached. [@2,5] Officers found dark, laptop-sized bricks of cannabis resin wrapped in denim clothing and packed into cardboard boxes. [@1,3,4,5] RCMP Corporal Luc Thibault described it as one of the largest hashish seizures his detachment could remember. [@1,4]

Police reported no arrests and asked the public for help tracing the load. [@2,3,4,5] Thibault declined to estimate the value because prices varied and the quality had yet to be determined. [@4] The Canadian Press calculated about C$70 million from a 2007 RCMP price survey, while TVA reported an estimate of nearly C$49 million. [@4,5]
