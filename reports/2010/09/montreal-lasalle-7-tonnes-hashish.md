Police in Montreal found about seven tonnes of hashish, or around seven million grams, packed into
an abandoned trailer in the city's west end. [@1] The dark, laptop-sized bricks had been shrouded in
denim clothing and stuffed into cardboard boxes, cramming the entire trailer. [@1]

The container, number HJCU 8291013, had been left on a tractor-trailer in a LaSalle storage centre
when the RCMP's Montreal drug section seized it on 12 September 2010. [@2] Police had received a call
about the suspicious container after it sat in the lot for several days, and obtained a search warrant
before opening it. [@1]

RCMP spokesman Cpl. Luc Thibault called the find "a big one" and one of the largest hashish seizures
his detachment could remember. [@1] No arrests were made, and investigators asked the public for tips
to advance the case. [@1,2] Thibault would not speculate on the street value of the load; the report
noted that a 2007 RCMP price survey put hash at $10 a gram, which would price the trailer's contents
at about $70 million. [@1]
