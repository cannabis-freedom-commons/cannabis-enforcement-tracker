U.S. Border Patrol agents assigned to the Casa Grande Station seized 2,000 pounds (907 kg) of marijuana and three vehicles on Thursday after following off-road tire tracks through the desert.[@1] When agents reached the vehicles they found all three loaded with cellophane-wrapped bundles, 160 bundles in total, which the agency valued at about $1.6 million.[@1]

The seizure was part of a heavy year for the Tucson Sector, which had taken more than 943,000 pounds of marijuana worth over $754 million since 1 October 2009.[@1]
