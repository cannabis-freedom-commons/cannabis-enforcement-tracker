Detectives with a Portuguese special drug unit found more than 6.6 tons (6 metric
tons) of Moroccan hashish during an operation in southeastern Portugal on the weekend
of 4–5 September 2010. [@1] Armed officers stopped a truck carrying just over three
tonnes of hashish and found a similar amount at a different location. [@1,2]

Two British citizens, aged 38 and 40, were arrested on suspicion of belonging to an
international trafficking ring and remanded in custody by a magistrate; their
identities were not published. [@1] The operation followed a year-long investigation
into a suspected British-led ring based in the Algarve, and police said an
international investigation was continuing. [@1,2]
