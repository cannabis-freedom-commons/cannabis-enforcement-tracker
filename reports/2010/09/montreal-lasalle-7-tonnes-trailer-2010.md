Police in Montreal found about seven tonnes of hashish packed into an abandoned trailer in a storage lot in the west-end borough of LaSalle on the weekend of 11-12 September 2010. [@1,2] Officers acted on a call from a member of the public and moved in with a search warrant. [@1]

The whole trailer was crammed with boxes of dark, laptop-sized hash bricks shrouded in denim clothing. [@1,2] RCMP spokesman Corporal Luc Thibault called the find one of the biggest hashish seizures his detachment could remember and would not put a dollar figure on the stash. [@1,2] A 2007 RCMP drug report had pegged the average street price of hash at 10 dollars per gram, which would value seven tonnes at about 70 million dollars, though Thibault would not speculate on this load. [@2]

Reports suggested the hashish originated in Pakistan, one of the world's leading producers of the resin. [@1] No arrests had been made, and police asked the public for help with the investigation. [@1]
