Police seized approximately 3,000 cannabis plants worth more than £1 million in a raid on an industrial unit on the Fisher Street Industrial Estate in Tipton. [@1] Officers also seized ventilation and lighting equipment and began deconstructing the growing operation. [@1] Two Vietnamese nationals, a 32-year-old man and a 16-year-old youth, both of no fixed address, were arrested during the raid and have been charged and remanded in custody. [@1]
