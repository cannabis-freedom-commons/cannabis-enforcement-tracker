The High Court in Alor Setar sentenced Rizal Sham Ahmad, 32, and Md Fitri Md Saad, 33, to death on 26 September 2010 after finding them guilty of trafficking in 2.8 kilograms of cannabis. [@1,2] Justice Datuk Zamani A. Rahim ruled that the prosecution had proven its case beyond reasonable doubt. [@1,2]

The two men, a factory van driver and a labourer, had been charged under Section 39B(1)(a) of the Dangerous Drugs Act 1952 over cannabis found at the car park of the Aneka Shopping Complex in Gurun at about 4pm on 7 February 2006, more than four years before the verdict. [@1,2] That charge carries a mandatory death sentence on conviction. [@1] The judge found that the two men had given contradictory statements that the court could not accept. [@1,2]

Rizal Sham was represented by counsel M. Chandran and Md Fitri by A. Ashok, while Deputy Public Prosecutor Norshuhada Mohd Yatim appeared for the prosecution. [@1]
