Canada Border Services Agency officers at the Port of Montréal found more than
1,700 kg of hashish, with an estimated street value of over C$36 million, in a
shipping container from South Africa on 27 January 2010; the agency announced
the seizure on 18 February. [@1] The drugs were divided into 864 packets
concealed in the false bottoms of wooden crates holding statues and masks, and
18 of the 19 inspected crates contained hashish. [@1]

## Inspection history

Officers had examined the same container in July 2009 and returned it to its
country of origin because its wood packaging failed Canada's plant-health
requirements. [@1] This time officers flagged the goods for routine inspection,
confirmed the hashish in an in-depth physical examination, and handed the drugs
to the Royal Canadian Mounted Police. [@1]
