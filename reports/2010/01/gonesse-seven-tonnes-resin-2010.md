French police seized seven tonnes of cannabis resin from a shipment arriving
from Morocco on Sunday 31 January 2010. [@2] The load had an estimated street
value of 35 million euros. [@2] Seven people were arrested after an
investigation that ran between Morocco and Spain. [@1]

## Display and destruction

The seizure was presented at Nanterre, in Hauts-de-Seine, in early February.
[@2] On 23 February 2010, officers unloaded the packages from a truck at
Gonesse, a suburb north of Paris, before destroying them. [@1]
