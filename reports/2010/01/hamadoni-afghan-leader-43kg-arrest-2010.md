Tajikistan's Drug Control Agency arrested a 31-year-old Afghan national in
Hamadoni district, Khatlon, on 22 January 2010 as the suspected leader of a
smuggling group. [@1] The man was detained in a staged special operation in the
border district of Hamadoni. [@2] Officers seized hashish and cannabis weighing
more than 43 kilograms in total at his detention. [@2] Asia-Plus reported that
more than 43 kilograms of cannabis were confiscated from him. [@1]

A criminal case was opened under Tajikistan's Penal Code provisions on drug
trafficking for sale, smuggling and illegal crossing of the state border. [@1]
The agency's investigative department opened that case, and the suspect was
placed in its temporary detention facility. [@2] An investigation was under way,
and officers were working to identify and prosecute others involved in the same
case. [@1]

The agency said the group had long smuggled drugs from Afghanistan through
Central Asia to Russia. [@1] Avesta, citing the agency's public relations
centre, described the group as one trafficking drugs, and it also reported the
43 kilograms figure. [@3]
