Mohsen bin Mohammed bin Saleh al-Mokhles was beheaded by the sword in Dammam in
the east of the kingdom for trafficking in hashish, under a Saudi Interior
Ministry statement carried by the official SPA news agency. [@1] The Hands Off
Cain account is sourced to an Agence France-Presse wire dated 24 January 2010. [@1]
A British executions database lists the same beheading under 25 January, with the
crime given as drugs. [@2]
