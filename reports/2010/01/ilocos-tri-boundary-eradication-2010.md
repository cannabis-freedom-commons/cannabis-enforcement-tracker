Joint elements of the PDEA, the police, the Armed Forces and the National Bureau of Investigation had been uprooting marijuana since 20 January in the mountain hinterlands at the tri-boundaries of Ilocos Sur, La Union and Benguet. [@1] PDEA Region 1 director Roberto Opena said on Sunday that the drive had so far destroyed about three million fully grown plants plus seedlings and seeds growing on some 14 hectares, calling it the biggest single marijuana haul in the country. [@1] The agency valued the destroyed plants at P1 billion under the Dangerous Drugs Board's guideline of P300 per kilo, and said one person had been arrested at a plantation site, with troops ferried in by two Air Force helicopters. [@1]

## An earlier phase

The regional weekly's account of the drive's first three days, which ended on Friday, put the wipe-out at 765,700 fully grown plants, 16,900 seedlings and 370 grams of seeds worth P153.8 million across 15 sites on the Kibungan-Sugpon-Santol boundaries. [@2] It named the arrested cultivator as Juliano Tapaya, 48, of Sapdaan village in Santol, and said two companions escaped as troops scoured the area. [@2] The operation ran under the police's Master Plan Sangbanat and the PDEA's Oplan Farmville. [@2]
