Drug police in Russia's Far East joined with the Federal Space Agency
(Roscosmos) to spot cannabis fields in the Primorye Territory, regional
drugs-control chief Lt Gen Alexander Rolik told a Vladivostok press
conference. [@1] Roscosmos sent coordinates of cannabis fields to
anti-narcotics officers, who destroyed the plants and returned confirmation
data for the agency's database; Rolik said contact began in late 2009. [@1]
Drug police destroyed 48 hectares of cannabis plants during 2009. [@1] In
September they seized four metric tons of marijuana in two forest caches and a
truck, and discovered an illegal hash-oil plant in the woods. [@1] Rolik said
the discoveries pointed to a net of organised criminal groups trafficking
cannabis-based drugs in Primorye cities and nearby regions. [@1] He added that
satellites had also helped other regions, including neighbouring Amur and the
Urals, spot cannabis plants. [@1]
