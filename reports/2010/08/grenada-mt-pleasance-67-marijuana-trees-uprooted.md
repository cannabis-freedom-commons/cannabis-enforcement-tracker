Officers of the Royal Grenada Police Force's Special Services Unit carried out
an eradication operation in the Mt Pleasance area of St Patrick's parish on
Tuesday 24 August 2010. [@1] They found a number of marijuana plants between 1
foot and 8 feet tall. [@1]

The force's press release said 67 marijuana trees were uprooted in the
operation. [@1] No arrests had been made in connection with the discovery,
police said. [@1]
