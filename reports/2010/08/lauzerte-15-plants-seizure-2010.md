Gendarmes arrested a man in his forties at his home in a small village in the
canton of Lauzerte and placed him in custody at the gendarmerie brigade for drug
possession and use. [@1] Released the next day, he was summoned to appear before
the Montauban correctional court for possession and use of narcotics. [@1]

Gendarmes seized about fifteen flowering cannabis plants grown in a dedicated
room fitted with ventilation and air conditioning, sodium lamps and a
humidifier. [@1] The plants held nearly a kilogram of cannabis herb that was
almost ready for harvest. [@1] Laboratory analysis measured an exceptionally
high THC level of 15 percent in the plants. [@1]

A patrol discovered the grow by chance while conducting door-to-door inquiries
over a burglary, after noticing a strong smell coming from a shed and asking the
owner to open it. [@1] The growing equipment was seized and was to be destroyed
along with the plants, which were stored at the brigade. [@1]

The brigade commander said the canton was very prone to such offences because
its large area, scattered homes and low population density gave growers
discretion, adding that most were private individuals growing for their own use.
