The Osun State Command of the National Drug Law Enforcement Agency (NDLEA) destroyed more than 155 hectares of Indian hemp farms worth N5 billion at different locations across the state, state commander Wosilat Audu said in a statement in Osogbo reported on 8 February 2010. [@1] In a separate operation, an agency patrol working with other security agents discovered a fresh cannabis farm where 600 kg of Indian hemp worth about N150 million was recovered from one Steven Enenwe. [@1]

For context, the statement gave the command's 2009 totals of 208 suspects arrested over cultivation and possession, of whom 45 had been convicted and 33 cases were pending. [@1]
