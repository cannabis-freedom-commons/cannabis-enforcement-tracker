Guatemalan police forces, army troops and U.S. DEA agents destroyed 319 million opium plants and
250,000 marijuana plants in a four-day operation in the Ixchiguán and Tajumulco municipalities of
San Marcos department, near the Mexican border. [@1,2] The EFE news agency reported the operation
on 5 February. [@1] The National Civil Police described San Marcos as a "sanctuary" of opium
cultivation. [@1]

## A mixed operation

The two outlets put the value of the destroyed crops together at an estimated $780 million, a
combined figure for both drugs that cannot be divided between them. [@1,2] This report records only
the cannabis component, the 250,000 marijuana plants, and attributes none of that shared valuation
to cannabis. [@1,2]
