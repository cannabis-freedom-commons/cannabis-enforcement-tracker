Police in the southwestern department of Cauca captured a record 20 tons of marijuana belonging to the FARC, the authorities said on Wednesday 24 February 2010. [@1] Officers presented the seized packages to the press in Santander de Quilichao the same day; according to the authorities it was the largest marijuana seizure in Colombia's history. [@2,3]

General Gustavo Adolfo Ricaurte, who headed Colombia's southwestern police force, said the bust had effectively dismantled the FARC's central financial hub in the Cauca region. [@1] He put the value of the seized marijuana at $2.5 million and called the site the guerrillas' financial centre, "where they cultivated marijuana, pressed and exported it". [@1]

## A separate 6.5-ton seizure that month

Police had seized 6.5 tons of marijuana in transit through Valle del Cauca in early February, but it was not known whether that shipment was also part of a FARC operation. [@1] The FARC's 30th Front trafficked drugs in Cauca and was believed to work with the Tijuana cartel in Mexico. [@1]
