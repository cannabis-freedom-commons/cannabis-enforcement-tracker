Mexican soldiers seized 12 tons of cannabis in Puerto San Felipe, near the border
with the United States, and displayed the stacked packages in Tijuana on 7
February 2010. [@1] Soldiers stood guard over rows of plastic-wrapped bales laid
out for the presentation. [@1]

The seizure was one of two large February trailer cases in Baja California: about
15 tons were found in a similar case the same month, making some 30 tons seized in
under a month. [@2]
