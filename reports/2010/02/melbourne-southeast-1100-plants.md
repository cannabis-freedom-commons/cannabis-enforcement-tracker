More than 100 detectives raided 16 houses in Hampton Park, Narre Warren, Berwick and Endeavour Hills
over two days in early February 2010 at the end of a four-month Drug Task Force investigation. [@1,2]
Police said most targets held commercial hydroponic cannabis crops. [@1,2]

## Seizures and arrests

Officers seized more than 1,100 plants with an estimated street value of at least A$9 million plus
$250,000 cash. [@1,2] Thirteen people were arrested. [@2] Detective Inspector Doug Fryer said the
operation removed more than half a tonne of cannabis from circulation and would hurt an organised
syndicate supplying the trade. [@1,2]

## Courts

The Sydney Morning Herald reported on 5 February that eight of the thirteen arrested were due at
Melbourne Magistrates Court that day, one person was charged and bailed for a later date, and four
were released pending further inquiries. [@1] The Berwick Star News said eight defendants appeared
in court on 5 February and that police were proceeding with confiscation applications on several
properties. [@2]
