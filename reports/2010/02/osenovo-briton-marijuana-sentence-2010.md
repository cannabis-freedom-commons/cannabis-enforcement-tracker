Police raided a house in Osenovo near Varna in July 2009, inhabited by three UK
citizens, and found 8.826 kg of marijuana with an estimated street value of
BGN 35,304. [@1] UK citizen Jody Macon admitted the cultivation charge and
reached an agreement with Varna District Court. [@1]

On 18 February 2010 the court handed Macon a three-year suspended sentence and
a BGN 9,900 fine. [@1] The complicity case against the other two Britons,
Michelle Wood and John Teague, was dropped for lack of evidence. [@1]
