Tonga Police separately charged two men with cultivating and possessing cannabis
plants found at their homes in Lavengatonga and Vaini, on Tongatapu. [@1] Deputy
Police Commander Taniela Faletau reported on 21 July 2010 that a 35-year-old man
had been charged with cultivating and possessing seven cannabis plants at his
home. [@2]

Three days later, a 23-year-old man was charged with the same offences after
four cannabis plants were found at his home in Vaini. [@2] The plants found at
the two homes ranged in height from 4 to 10 or 11 inches. [@2] Both men were
kept in police custody pending bail and were to appear at the Nuku'alofa
Magistrate's Court in August. [@2]
