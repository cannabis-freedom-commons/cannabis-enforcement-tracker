The George Town High Court sentenced thirty-year-old food outlet assistant Sharifurahim Abdullah to death for trafficking in 23 kg of cannabis. [@1,2] Judicial commissioner Mohd Amin Firdaus Abdullah imposed the sentence for the offence committed behind a house in Kampung Permatang Rawa, Bukit Mertajam, at about 9 p.m. on 31 March 2006. [@1,2]

Both outlets trace the case to the New Straits Times of 31 July 2010, which reported the sentencing as having happened the day before. [@1,2]
