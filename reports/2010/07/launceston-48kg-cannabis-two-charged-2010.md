Launceston police seized 48 kilograms of cannabis from premises in two of the
city's suburbs after a week-long investigation by Northern Drug Investigation
Services and the Launceston CIB. [@1,2] Police called it one of the largest
cannabis seizures in Tasmania. [@1,2]

Police put the haul's street value at between $300,000 and $350,000. [@1] Two
men from Rosevears, aged 29 and 32, were charged with trafficking and summonsed
to appear in the Launceston Magistrates Court at a later date. [@1] Police said
further investigations were continuing. [@1]
