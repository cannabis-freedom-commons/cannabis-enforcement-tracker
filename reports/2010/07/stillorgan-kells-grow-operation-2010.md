Gardaí investigating drug dealing in the Blackrock area stopped and searched a car on Drummartin Link Road, Stillorgan, at about 1 p.m. on 26 July 2010. [@1,3] They recovered 5 kg of cannabis grass from the boot and arrested the 33-year-old male driver. [@1,3,4]

Later that day officers searched a house on Mount Anville Wood, Stillorgan, where they found the entire upstairs in use as a cannabis factory, with a pot-grow system, high-density lighting, reflective wall coverings and ventilation in each of four bedrooms. [@1,3,4] About 500 cannabis plants and several more kilos of cannabis grass were discovered there, and a 33-year-old woman was arrested at the scene. [@1,3,4] Two follow-up searches in Clondalkin and Crumlin produced no seizures or arrests. [@1,3]

In the early hours of 27 July, officers searched a house on Headford Road, Kells, Co Meath, as part of the same investigation and seized 400 cannabis plants, 3 kg of cannabis herb and cultivation equipment; the house had a bypassed electricity supply and an air extraction system. [@2,4] Two males aged 16 and 21 were arrested and detained at Kells Garda Station. [@2]

The Garda put the value of the Stillorgan seizures at about €600,000 and the Irish Times valued the combined seizure of almost 1,000 plants at over €800,000, describing both premises as elaborate indoor factories. [@1,4]
