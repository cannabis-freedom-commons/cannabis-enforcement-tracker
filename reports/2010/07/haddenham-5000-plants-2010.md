Cambridgeshire Police raided a building at Hill Row, Haddenham, near Ely, in a morning operation on
8 July 2010 and found about 5,000 cannabis plants. [@1] The force estimated the farm’s street value at
£1.75 million and said it was the largest cannabis cultivation site officers had uncovered in the
county. [@1]

Four men from Cambridgeshire were arrested on suspicion of producing cannabis and were being
questioned. [@1] They were described as a 41-year-old from Haddenham, two men aged 24 and 29 from
Huntingdon, and a 40-year-old from Warboys. [@1] The BBC report does not describe the building layout
or whether electricity had been bypassed. [@1]
