The Eastern Regional Police Command destroyed 46 bags of Indian hemp weighing about 564,000 grams that had been seized from Huhunya in the Yilo Krobo District. [@1] The destruction at Krobo Nkwanta near Koforidua followed a directive from a Koforidua Circuit Court, and was witnessed by personnel of the Police Professional and Intelligence Bureau and court officials including the registrar. [@1]

Police said 25 of the bags came from a 2008 swoop in which 23 suspects, including Kwaku Paddy and Philip Teye, were arrested; 21 were later exonerated and released, while Paddy and Teye were arraigned for cultivation and possession of narcotic drugs. [@1] Paddy died while on remand, with a post-mortem finding tuberculosis as the cause, and the Attorney General later advised that the charges against Teye, the only suspect left, be dropped. [@1] The other 21 bags had been seized in 2009 with no suspects arrested, and police said both consignments were burned to free the exhibit room. [@1]
