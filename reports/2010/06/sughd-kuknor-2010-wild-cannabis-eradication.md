Officers of the anti-drug department and city and district police found and destroyed over 27,000 wild cannabis bushes across northern Tajikistan in five days as part of Operation Kuknor-2010, the Sughd regional police directorate told Asia-Plus on 21 June 2010. [@1] The itemised finds add up to 29,962 bushes at nine sites. [@1]

The largest stands were about 10,000 bushes on land of a farming association brigade in Bobojon Ghafurov district and 8,853 wild Indian cannabis bushes in the S. Ayni recreation park in Chkalovsk. [@1] Police also cleared 2,500 bushes in Asht district, 2,400 in Spitamen district, 1,500 at a cemetery in Zafarobod district, 1,200 on a Zafarobod village's land, 1,223 at a Qayroqqum rest home, 1,327 in a Chkalovsk park and 959 at a Mastchoh district cemetery. [@1] Every stand found was destroyed. [@1]
