Gardaí from the Blanchardstown Drug Unit carried out a planned search in sheds at the rear of a house on Blakestown Road on the afternoon of 8 June 2010. [@1] They discovered an elaborate high-tech cannabis growing operation, with plants provisionally valued at up to €450,000, along with nine stun guns. [@1,3] The Irish Times put the crop at between 1,000 and 1,200 plants in a sophisticated indoor facility. [@3]

A man in his 20s was arrested at the scene and detained at Blanchardstown Garda Station under section 2 of the Criminal Justice (Drug Trafficking) Act 1996. [@1,3] He was charged on the evening of 11 June and was due before Dublin District Court the next morning. [@2]
