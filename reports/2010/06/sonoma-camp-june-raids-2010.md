Sonoma County drug agents working with the state's Campaign Against Marijuana Planting destroyed 150,667 immature plants over six days in late June 2010, more than the county's entire 2009 take. [@1] Three of those days with CAMP personnel yielded 145,467 plants from raids near The Geysers, Lake Sonoma and the Red Slide area of Mill Creek, while the county task force alone destroyed another 5,200 plants on private land, including in a vineyard. [@1]

Sgt. Chris Bertoli, who runs the county Narcotics Task Force, estimated the crop could have yielded smokable bud worth $300 million at $2,000 a pound. [@1] The county had seized 108,486 plants with CAMP plus another 40,000 on its own the year before, and Bertoli credited more calls from fed-up landlords and neighbors alongside ever-larger gardens. [@1]
