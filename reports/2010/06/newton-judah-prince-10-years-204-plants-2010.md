A federal judge in Wichita sentenced Judah Prince, 50, formerly of Newton, Kansas, to 10 years in prison on 30 June 2010 for growing 204 marijuana plants in his home. [@1] A jury had found Prince guilty of one count of manufacturing marijuana and 26 counts of using a false address on federal paperwork about his eligibility to buy firearms. [@1,2] Prosecutors said the marijuana count alone carried 10 years to life. [@2]

## The 2008 search

On 9 January 2008 agents of the federal Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms and Explosives served a search warrant at Prince's Newton home with a Newton police detective. [@2,3] Inside they found the marijuana plants growing in the house, including more than 200 plants in the basement, and seized more than 35 firearms, among them rifles, shotguns and pistols. [@1,2,3] Officers had first seen several plants and growing equipment in plain view, then obtained a second warrant from a Harvey County judge before seizing the contraband. [@3]
