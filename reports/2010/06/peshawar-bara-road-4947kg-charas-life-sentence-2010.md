ANF officers acting on prior information stopped truck JU 0810/Sindh coming from the Bara tribal
territory at Gilani Manzil on Bara Road near the Bara Qadeem check-post on the evening of 29
July 2009. [@1,2] A search found 198 plastic sacks of charas hidden under wild grass in the
truck's body; weighed at 4,947 kg and bound for Punjab, the load led to the arrest of driver Ali
Muhammad. [@1,2]

The Special Court (CNS) in Peshawar convicted Ali Muhammad on 9 June 2010 and sentenced him to
life imprisonment with a Rs200,000 fine, with two more years in prison in default. [@2] The
forensic laboratory confirmed the substance was charas, and the agency put its
international-market price at about six billion rupees. [@1,2]
