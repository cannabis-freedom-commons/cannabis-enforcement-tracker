Gardaí stopped a car in the Castleconnell area of County Limerick on the
morning of 8 May 2010 and discovered what they believed was cannabis herb,
subject to analysis, with an estimated street value of €240,000. [@1] The
driver, a man in his thirties, was arrested at the scene and brought to
Henry Street Garda station in Limerick, where he was detained under section
2 of the Criminal Justice (Drug Trafficking) Act 1996. [@1]

The Sunday Tribune put the seizure at nearly €250,000 and reported that the
man was being questioned at Henry Street station. [@3] He was subsequently
charged in connection with the seizure and was due to appear before
Limerick District Court at 1pm on 9 May. [@2]
