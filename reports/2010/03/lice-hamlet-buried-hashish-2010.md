Turkish security forces seized 8.5 tons of hashish in the country's southeast on
Thursday and detained six people, media reports said. [@1] The haul was found
buried in a small hamlet in Diyarbakır's Lice district in a dawn operation by
gendarmerie forces, the CNNTürk news channel said. [@1]

## Possible militant link examined

Investigators were examining whether the drugs were linked to armed Kurdish
militants waging a long campaign against the Turkish military, the Anatolia news
agency said. [@1] Turkey accuses the outlawed Kurdistan Workers' Party (PKK) of
narcotics and people smuggling; the PKK is listed as a terrorist organisation by
Turkey, the United States and the European Union. [@1]
