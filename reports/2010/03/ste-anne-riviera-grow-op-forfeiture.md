On 8 July 2009, Steinbach RCMP executed a search warrant at a residence in Lake Riviera Estates, in the RM of Ste. Anne, seizing about 600 marijuana plants as well as packaged marijuana with an estimated street value of about $600,000. [@1] A man and woman from Winnipeg were arrested and released for an August court date on several drug charges. [@1]

On 11 March 2010 the province announced that the house at 8 Riviera Drive in Lake Riviera Estates, three miles east of Ste. Anne, had been forfeited to the province by court order under the Criminal Property Forfeiture Act, the first order under the legislation. [@2] RCMP officers had seized more than 580 plants there in July 2009, and the province listed the house for sale at $279,900, directing proceeds to victim compensation, crime prevention and enforcement programs. [@2]
