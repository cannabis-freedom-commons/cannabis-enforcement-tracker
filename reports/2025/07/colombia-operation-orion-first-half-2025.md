Colombian Navy commander Juan Ricardo Roz Obregón presented the first-half 2025
results of the multinational Operation Orion, which gathered 62 allied
countries. [@1] The operation has run since 2018 and is coordinated by
Colombia's Navy. [@1] Authorities detained 1,120 people allegedly linked to
criminal activities and seized 130 boats during the period. [@1]

Authorities reported 2,320 tons of various drugs seized or with their transit
interrupted. [@1] Of those, 1,770 tons of marijuana had their flow interrupted
and 211 tons of marijuana were seized. [@1] The same breakdown lists 12 tons of
hashish, alongside 327 tons of cocaine, 452 kilograms of heroin and 118
kilograms of methamphetamines. [@1]

Authorities seized an autonomous semisubmersible carrying drugs, described as
the first such find in Colombia. [@1] The vessel was found in the Caribbean Sea
near the Colombian city of Santa Marta. [@1] The commander said the find
reflects a shift toward more sophisticated unmanned systems that increase
evasion capacity and pose a growing challenge to maritime security. [@1] The
Orion report counted 10 such semisubmersibles with radar-evading and
partial-autonomy technology seized at different points across the Americas. [@1]
