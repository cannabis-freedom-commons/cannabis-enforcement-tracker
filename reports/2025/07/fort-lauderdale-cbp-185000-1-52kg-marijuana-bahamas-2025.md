U.S. Customs and Border Protection officers seized $185,000 in cash and more
than a kilogram of marijuana in what the agency described as a smuggling attempt
out of Fort Lauderdale. [@1] Carlos C. Martel, CBP's director of field
operations for its Miami and Tampa field offices, announced the seizure in a
post on X on Wednesday. [@1]

Martel said the officers recovered 1.52 kilograms of marijuana. [@1] Martel said
the contraband was packed in a cooler, writing that "crime doesn't travel well…
especially when it's packed in a cooler." [@1] The post included redacted images
of the cash, drugs and the vessel where the seizure was made. [@1]

The agency gave no information on exactly where the seizure took place or
whether anyone was arrested. [@1] A Brevard County news station, recounting
Martel's statement, reported that officers intercepted $185,000 and more than a
kilogram of marijuana. [@2]
