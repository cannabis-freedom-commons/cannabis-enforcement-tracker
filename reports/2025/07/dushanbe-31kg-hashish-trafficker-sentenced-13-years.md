SCNS officers in Tajikistan detained Mustafuo Saifullo Saifullozoda, born 1994,
a native of Kulob and resident of Arbobkhotun mahalla in Dushanbe's Sino
district, on suspicion of involvement in illegal drug trafficking, with drugs
seized in especially large quantities. [@1]

During the investigation it was established that Saifullozoda, in prior
agreement with his criminal accomplice, an Afghan citizen named Khodji, about 45
years old, received narcotics from a hidden cache in exchange for a large sum of
money and transported them to Dushanbe in a personal Toyota Corolla, state
number 00-10 RU 01 TJ. [@1] In Dushanbe, Saifullozoda hid the narcotics in a
cache on the arable lands of Djuibodom mahalla. [@1]

The Drug Control Agency's forensic examination confirmed that the seized
substances were of the hashish type, with a total weight of 31 kilograms. [@1]
The Investigative Department of the SCNS in Khatlon Region opened a criminal
case under Article 200, Part 4 of the Criminal Code of Tajikistan, and a
preliminary investigation was conducted. [@1] The Ismoili Somoni District Court
of Dushanbe sentenced Saifullozoda Mustafa Saifullo to 13 years of imprisonment.
[@1]

Operational-investigative actions continue in relation to the Afghan national
named Khodji. [@1]

Asia-Plus confirms the SCNS statement and reports that Saifullozoda, 30 years
old, concealed the 31 kg of hashish in a hiding place in the agricultural lands
of the Juyibodom neighbourhood in Dushanbe and was apprehended during a special
operation, the drugs seized by authorities. [@2]

An investigation was launched by the SCNS's office in Khatlon province under
Article 200 (4) of Tajikistan's Criminal Code, dealing with the illegal
trafficking of narcotic or psychotropic substances, and investigative actions
are ongoing in relation to the Afghan suspect. [@2]

Avesta reports that Saifullozoda, a native of Kulob and resident of Dushanbe's
Sino district, was detained by national security officers on suspicion of
involvement in illegal drug trafficking, with drugs confiscated in especially
large quantities. [@3]

The Ismoili Somoni District Court of Dushanbe sentenced Saifullozoda Mustafa
Saifullo to 13 years in prison. [@3]
