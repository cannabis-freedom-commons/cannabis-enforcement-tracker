Officers of the police departments of Tbilisi, Adjara, Kvemo Kartli and Imereti
detained 16 people on drug charges after operational and investigative measures.
[@1] Police said those detained were accused of illegal acquisition and storage,
sale, attempted sale and facilitation of sale in an especially large amount,
illegal cultivation of plants containing narcotic substances and illegal use
without a doctor's prescription. [@2] The alleged crimes carry up to 20 years in
prison or life imprisonment. [@2]

On the basis of a judge's order, police carried out controlled purchases of
drugs and secretly recorded them with audio and video. [@1] Searches of the
accused persons' belongings and homes, a specially equipped greenhouse for
cultivating plants containing narcotic substances and locations they indicated
during investigative experiments recovered batches intended for sale described
as especially large. [@2] The seizure list included methamphetamine, cocaine,
buprenorphine, clephedrone, methadone, alpha-PVP, marijuana and the cannabis
plant. [@2] Police seized the illegally sold drugs, materials needed for
packaging and cultivation of narcotic substances and cash as physical evidence.
[@1]

The investigation is proceeding under articles 260, 260 terzia, 19-260 terzia,
260 kvarta, 261, 265 prima and 273 of Georgia's Criminal Code. [@2]
