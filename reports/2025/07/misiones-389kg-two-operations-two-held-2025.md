Gendarmería Nacional detained two adult men after officers of Squadron 12
Bernardo de Irigoyen and the Comandante Fontana reconnaissance unit intercepted
one of two cars that fled a control on Ruta Provincial Nº 17 at kilometre 41 in
Misiones. [@1] The car carried five bundles holding 226 rectangular packages of
marijuana, weighing 148 kilos 578 grams, on the rear seat and in the boot. [@1]
The federal courts and prosecutors' offices of Oberá and Eldorado ordered the
drug and the vehicle seized and both detainees held. [@2]

In the other operation, a foot patrol of the Jardín América section of Squadron
11 San Ignacio found 11 large bundles in vegetation on the bank of the Paraná at
Puerto Falso Rutilo. [@1] A Narcotest field test identified the contents as
cannabis sativa, weighing 240 kilos 570 grams in 338 rectangular packages. [@1]
The load was being kept among the vegetation, waiting for traffickers to move it
into the country's drug distribution circuit. [@1]

The Gendarmería put the haul at more than 380 kilos of marijuana, and the two
stated weights sum to 389 kilos 148 grams. [@1] The government estimated the
value of the haul at 1,201,470,440 pesos. [@1] Both operations were carried out
under Plan Guaçurarí, a National Security Ministry plan to prevent crime on the
border with Brazil. [@1]
