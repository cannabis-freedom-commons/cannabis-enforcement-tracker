Abai Region police searched the residence of a 33-year-old resident of Almaty
Region during the Karasora-2025 and Darmek preventive operations. [@1] Officers
found and seized four sacks containing green plant material and a polymer bag
containing a green powder-like substance. [@1]

A forensic narcotics examination identified the seized material as dried
marijuana weighing more than 105 kilograms and hashish weighing more than 7
kilograms. [@1] During questioning, the detained man confessed and explained
that he had independently gathered wild cannabis, then dried it and stored it
for the purpose of further sale. [@1] He was placed in a temporary detention
facility after his detention. [@1]

A pre-trial investigation was opened into the case under the Criminal Code of
Kazakhstan. [@2] Police said they are continuing measures to detect and stop
drug-trafficking offences and appealed to citizens to report such facts by
calling 102. [@3]
