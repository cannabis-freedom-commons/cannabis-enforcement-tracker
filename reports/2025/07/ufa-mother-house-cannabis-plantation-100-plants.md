Drug-control officers of Bashkortostan's Interior Ministry identified three Ufa
residents who police say grew cannabis and sold drugs. [@1] A 28-year-old Ufa
man set up a cannabis plantation in his mother's house on the instructions of a
curator for an online drug shop, made the drug from the plants and handed
packages to two couriers. [@1] In the room, detectives found about 100 cannabis
bushes, 5 kg of dried "dope grass" (durman-trava, a slang term for marijuana)
and equipment used for the operation. [@1] Detained, the grower admitted he had
received instructions on caring for, harvesting and processing the plants from
accomplices. [@1]

Police later detained two women who are mothers of several children and had
joined the same drug shop as couriers in 2024. [@1] The two friends were
detained while delivering the drug to customers. [@1] Searching the car the
couriers drove and their flats, police found and seized three types of drugs
weighing about 350 grams in total: hashish, mephedrone and cocaine. [@1] The
hashish is part of that combined 350-gram figure, and officers published no
separate weight for it. [@2]

A criminal case for illegal drug trafficking was opened, and all three suspects
were remanded in custody. [@1] The investigation in Ufa was still under way when
Bashkortostan's Interior Ministry announced the arrests on 1 July 2025. [@3]
