Police officers joined investigators from the Erebuni and Nubarashen districts
and Yerevan patrol officers to act on verified information about illicit drug
trafficking. [@1] According to the police press service, on 7 July at 15:20 on
Mazmanyan Street in Yerevan they detained two residents of Dzorakhpyur and a
woman from Yerevan. [@1] The three were taken to the Erebuni police division
together with the Ford car driven by the 40-year-old man. [@1]

A personal search of the driver found a pipette with ash-like traces and a
folding knife. [@1] Under an opened criminal case and by court order, officers
then searched the 40-year-old man's house. [@1] The house search found 1 kg 850
g of marijuana and 256 g of hashish, alongside opium, methamphetamine, methadone
tablets and two automatic-rifle cartridges. [@1] Police put the market value of
all the drugs taken out of circulation at about 216 million drams. [@1]

All three detainees were placed in a detention isolator and forensic
examinations were ordered. [@1] The circumstances are being established in a
preliminary investigation. [@1]
