NDLEA operatives destroyed 28,054.053 kg of skunk, a potent local strain of cannabis,
on three large plantations spanning over 11 hectares in the Ewere Uzebba forest of Owan
West Local Government Area, Edo State, on 8 July 2025. [@1,2,3] Officers also found 82 kg
of processed cannabis inside a hut on the illicit farms. [@1]

As the team left the forest, armed members of the cannabis cultivating syndicate seeking
revenge opened heavy gunfire on them. [@1,2] The officers returned fire with superior gun
power and brought the situation under control without casualties, though one operational
vehicle was riddled with bullets. [@1,2]

Edo State commander Mitchel Ofoyeju disclosed the operation to journalists in Benin,
praising his officers' courage and saying the command would not be intimidated by the
attack. [@1]
