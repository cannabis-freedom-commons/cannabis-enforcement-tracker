Arber Dashi, 27, of Bromford Rise, Wolverhampton, was sentenced to 39 months in
prison at Ipswich Crown Court on 25 July 2025 after pleading guilty to being
concerned in cannabis production. [@1] He was the fourth member of a West
Midlands organised crime group running a production line across Suffolk worth
over £200,000, taking the gang's combined sentences past ten years. [@1]

The first factory occupied a three-storey house on London Road, Ipswich,
converted into seven growing areas holding 373 plants, and officers raiding it
in April 2024 found a man hiding in the basement. [@1] A second factory in
Langer Road, Felixstowe, held 81 plants and came to light after officers
stopped a car carrying fertiliser and growing equipment. [@1] Fingerprints and
phone messages tied Dashi to both sites, and arresting officers in the West
Midlands found him with a Peugeot key for a car holding pesticides and
cannabis-growing chemicals. [@1]

Three accomplices were already serving time: Bojken Muhametanji received 20
months while Esat Dashi and Thien Le got 33 months each in January. [@1]
Sergeant Dave Logan said the case showed how specialist teams had shut two
large grows and taken out key members of the cultivation gang. [@1]
