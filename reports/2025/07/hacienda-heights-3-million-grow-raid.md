Los Angeles County sheriff's deputies seized more than $3 million in marijuana
from an illegal grow in Hacienda Heights on Wednesday, July 16, after executing
a search warrant. [@1] The sheriff's Industry Station County Special Assignment
Team served the warrant at the unlicensed grow. [@2]

Sheriff's officials worked with Southern California Edison to look for unusual
spikes in energy use, which led them to the grow. [@1] Officials allege the grow
bypassed the utility's panel and meter to use about $18,000 in electricity each
month over several years. [@1] Authorities estimate the grow stole around
$500,000 worth of electricity over the years. [@1] The sheriff's department said
electricity theft and illegal grows create fire hazards and contribute to
environmental degradation. [@1]

Captain Glenn Emery of the Sheriff's Industry Station said the operation sent a
clear message that criminal activity would not be tolerated in Los Angeles
County. [@1] The investigation is ongoing and no arrests have been announced.
[@1] The home has been shut down, and the sheriff's department described the
search and seizure as part of an ongoing effort against illegal grows in
unincorporated areas of the county. [@2]
