Macao's Judiciary Police arrested a mainland Chinese couple in Macao on
suspicion of drug trafficking after cannabis was found concealed in the man's
tailored underwear. [@1] Anti-narcotics officers intercepted the man and his
wife on the evening of July 17 after they arrived from Bangkok, Thailand. [@1]
Investigators said the man habitually used drugs and had bought the narcotics in
Bangkok, while his wife allegedly helped smuggle them through Macao toward the
Chinese mainland. [@1] Both suspects were referred to the prosecutors' office
for investigation on charges of trafficking narcotics and psychotropic
substances. [@1]

Officers recovered five packets of cannabis flower weighing about 321.9 grams
and 11 packets of cannabis residue in the man's modified underwear, about 333.8
grams in total. [@1] In his luggage police seized four boxes of cookies in
ordinary biscuit tins suspected to contain cannabis weighing 2,818.8 grams and a
packet of cannabis-laced gummies weighing 829.4 grams. [@1] Laboratory tests
confirmed cannabis and tetrahydrocannabinol in the seized items, which police
valued at MOP 656,000 (about $81,145). [@1]

The operation was carried out by anti-narcotics officers of the drug-crime
investigation unit targeting inbound flights on the basis of intelligence. [@1]
