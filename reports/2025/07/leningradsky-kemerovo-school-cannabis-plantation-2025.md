Police in the settlement of Leningradsky near Kemerovo found a large cannabis
plantation inside the building of a former boarding school. [@1] Those behind
the operation had bought the former boarding-school building specifically to use
it for illegal cultivation. [@1] The organisers were described as residents of
Krasnoyarsk Krai and of a Central Asian country, and they were detained and
placed in a pre-trial detention centre to await trial. [@1] The detention report
states that they were held in custody pending trial. [@1]

Inside the building, the growers had fitted out greenhouses with modern heating,
irrigation and ventilation systems. [@1] During the subsequent search, officers
found more than 1,800 cannabis plants growing in the school building. [@1]
Officers also seized 32 kilograms of ready-to-use marijuana during the same
search. [@1]

Police said they seized the large batch of cannabis cultivated in the abandoned
school, and a criminal case on drug production was opened over the operation.
[@1] The account identifies the seized crop as cannabis grown in the abandoned
school. [@1]
