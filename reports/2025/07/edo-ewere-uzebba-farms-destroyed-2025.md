NDLEA operatives destroyed no fewer than 28,054.053 kilograms of skunk, a potent local strain of cannabis, on three farms measuring more than 11 hectares in the Ewere Uzebba forest of Owan West Local Government Area on Tuesday 8 July 2025. [@2] They also recovered 82 kilograms of already processed cannabis from a hut on the farms. [@1,2] Edo commander Mitchell Ofoyeju confirmed the figures when he briefed journalists in Benin City. [@1]

## Ambush on the way out

As the operatives exited the forest, armed members of the cannabis cultivating syndicate opened heavy gunfire on them. [@1,2] The officers repelled the attack with superior firepower and came out unhurt, though one operational vehicle was riddled with bullets. [@1,2] Ofoyeju commended his officers' courage and said the command would not be intimidated by the cartel's hostility. [@1]
