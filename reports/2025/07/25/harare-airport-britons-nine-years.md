British nationals Tihaise Darlin Elisha and Taylor Tamara Simone were sentenced at the Harare
Magistrates' Court to nine years in prison each, with three years suspended on condition of good
behaviour, for unlawful dealing in dangerous drugs. [@1,2,3] Reporting in early August 2025 said
each would serve an effective six years. [@2,3]

Police arrested the pair on 1 May 2025 at Robert Gabriel Mugabe International Airport after they
landed on Ethiopian Airlines flight ET 873 from Thailand. [@2,3] Officers at arrivals terminal 2
searched four suitcases and found 124 half-kilogram compressed satchets of dagga weighing 66.415 kg,
which police valued at more than US$600,000. [@2] The National Prosecuting Authority told
NewZimbabwe that detectives had flagged suspicious behaviour before the search. [@3]

BBC News said more than 66 kg was hidden in the luggage and noted the UK Foreign Office was
supporting the two detainees. [@1] NewZimbabwe quoted prosecutors as saying 53 packets were found
in red and brown bags tagged with the defendants' names, while giving a total weight of 65.415 kg.
[@3]
