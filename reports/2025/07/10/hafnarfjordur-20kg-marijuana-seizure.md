Capital Region police said they arrested six people during the previous week as part of an inquiry
into drug imports. [@1,2] Officers carried out seven house searches and seized 20 kilograms of
marijuana that had been hidden in freight shipments. [@1,2]

The drugs were found both during customs inspections and when police searched an industrial premises
in Hafnarfjörður. [@1,2] Three of the six arrestees were held in custody for a time. [@1,2] Police
said the investigation was progressing well and gave no further detail on charges. [@1,2]

RÚV reported the announcement on 10 July 2025, repeating the police statement that more than 20 kg
had been seized. [@1] The national police website published the same account the same day. [@2]
