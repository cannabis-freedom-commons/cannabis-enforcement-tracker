A gendarmerie patrol found a cannabis load being transported in Antakalanabe, in
Befandriana-Nord district. [@1] The surprised traffickers fled, leaving the load
behind. [@1] No suspect was arrested. [@1] Gendarmes seized several sacks of
chanvre indien, or cannabis, estimated at about 250 kilograms. [@1] The
operation took place during the night from Tuesday into Wednesday. [@1]

The load was intercepted around midnight after targeted surveillance in a sector
identified as a sensitive point for cannabis trafficking. [@2] The
woven-polyethylene sacks were found on the ground, secured and taken to the
local gendarmerie post. [@2] The cannabis was to be destroyed at the post under
a judicial decision. [@2]

The cannabis likely came from plantations hidden in remote areas near Ambanja
and Ambilobe. [@1] The surrounding bush was difficult to access, and growers
used tracks that were lightly monitored. [@1] Gendarmes monitored routes used to
carry cannabis toward Befandriana-Nord, Antsohihy and Antsiranana, and onward to
Mahajanga and Antananarivo. [@1] The gendarmerie said the traffickers' routes
remained under surveillance. [@1] The gendarmerie was preparing targeted
operations to trace the plantations and dismantle the network. [@1]
