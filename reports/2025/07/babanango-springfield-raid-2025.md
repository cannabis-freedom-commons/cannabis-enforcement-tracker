A multidisciplinary intelligence-driven operation in the early hours of Wednesday 16 July 2025 uncovered about five hectares of dagga plantation disguised as a vegetable farm at Springfield in the Babanango policing precinct. [@1] Officers from the Zululand District, the Vryheid and Ulundi K9 units, Vryheid Search and Rescue, Home Affairs immigration officers, the Zone 3 Task Team and the Ulundi municipality took part after intelligence pointed to the site. [@1]

Fourteen suspects aged between 19 and 40, ten of them foreign nationals, were arrested and were to appear in the Babanango Magistrate's Court. [@1] Officers also recovered pieces of South African National Defence Force uniform, machetes and processed cannabis from the suspects. [@1] Police estimated the recovered dagga to be worth hundreds of thousands of rand. [@1]
