Police detained three people at a house on Jirón Riobamba in the Lima district
of San Martín de Porres. [@1] The detainees were Enrique Cabrera Quichis, known
as Nano, aged 27, Jonathan Flores Huaraca, known as Verde, aged 26, and a
17-year-old girl. [@1] General Isaac Candia, head of the Callao police region,
confirmed the names of the detainees. [@1] Preliminary reports said the girl was
not in the house by chance but was allegedly involved in the group's logistics
and in contacts with buyers through online apps and platforms. [@1] Officers of
the Playa Rímac police station carried out the intervention with intelligence
agents of the Callao region. [@1] The raid followed the street detention of
Cabrera with 4 kilograms of marijuana hidden in a bag after he left his house
with the drug. [@2] Police said Cabrera had a prior record for unlawful drug
trafficking in 2021. [@2]

Officers seized 120 kilograms of marijuana in the house. [@1] Agents found large
marijuana packages hidden in different rooms of the property. [@1] According to
the police report, the drug was carefully packed and ready for distribution.
[@1] Police put the load's value on the illicit market at more than 300,000
soles, a figure the newspaper also reported as approximately that amount. [@1]
Investigators said the group allegedly used digital channels to offer the drug,
and La República reported it was to be sold to different points of the capital.
[@1]

The Public Ministry and the family prosecutor's office were to assess the girl's
legal situation, including whether an aggravating factor applied for her use by
a criminal organisation. [@1] The detainees were taken to the Playa Rímac police
station for legal proceedings. [@2] The case went to the Public Ministry, which
was to evaluate the evidence from the house and the phones and computers seized
from the detainees. [@1]
