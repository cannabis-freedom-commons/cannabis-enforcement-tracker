Sri Lanka Customs officers arrested a 37-year-old Canadian woman at Bandaranaike
International Airport on the morning of 22 July 2025 for attempting to smuggle
hashish into the country. [@1] Newswire, reporting the same Customs account,
said she was arrested early that morning at the airport in Katunayake. [@2] The
woman had arrived from Montreal, Canada via Doha on Qatar Airways flight QR 662.
[@1]

Customs officers conducting a routine inspection discovered 18,123 grams (18.123
kilograms) of hashish concealed in her baggage. [@1] Newswire gave the amount as
18.1 kilograms. [@2] Officials estimated the seized hashish had a street value
of around Rs. 181 million. [@2]

The Narcotics Control Unit of the Sri Lanka Customs Department was conducting
initial investigations. [@1] The suspect and the seized narcotics were to be
handed over to the Police Narcotics Bureau (PNB) for further investigation and
legal proceedings. [@1]
