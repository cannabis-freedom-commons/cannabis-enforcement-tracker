Officers of the Trinidad and Tobago Police Service carried out a cannabis
eradication exercise in Moriah, Tobago, on Thursday 31 July 2025. [@1] The
operation ran between 6 am and 8 am. [@2] PC Thorpe led a party of officers
about 360 metres into a forested area off Northside Road, Castara. [@2] The
operation was coordinated by Inspector Bacchus and Acting Inspector Stewart,
strategically led by W/Ag. Sgt. Gordon-Harris and Ag. Sgt. McMillan, and
supervised by W/Cpl. Alleyne-Pantin, Ag. Cpl. Ferguson and Cpl. Broomes of the
Special Investigations Unit. [@2] Officers of the Tobago Divisional Task Force
executed the operation with additional assistance from other members of the
Special Investigations Unit. [@2]

Inside the forest, officers found a single cannabis field occupying about one
lot of land. [@2] The field held an estimated 250 fully grown cannabis plants.
[@2] Police put the estimated worth of the cannabis destroyed at $125,000. [@2]
A further search of the surrounding area uncovered a makeshift campsite and a
drying rack presumed to have been used to process the cannabis. [@2] The
officers destroyed the plants and the structures on site in line with
established procedures. [@2]
