‘Aisea Pese Havea, 42, is serving one year and eight months in prison for
cultivating cannabis plants seized at a home in Ha’ateiho. [@1] He pleaded
guilty to cultivating three cannabis plants weighing 2,300 grams, possessing
2.11 grams of cannabis, and possessing a homemade bong. [@2] He was also found
with four .22 calibre bullets without a firearms licence. [@2] He was serving a
six-month suspended sentence from a prior case when he was arrested. [@3]

Police received a report in February that Tonga College student Salesi Fangaake
was found with cannabis leaves. [@2] The student admitted picking the leaves
from a taro plantation behind his home in Ha’ateiho on neighbour Joe Masima’s
allotment. [@2] The Drugs Squad then went to Masima’s residence, where he and
his wife were present. [@2] Police searched the backyard taro plantation and
found Havea lying on a bed in a makeshift shelter beside three cannabis plants.
[@3] Questioned about the plants, Havea admitted they were his marijuana. [@2]
Officers also recovered a homemade bong, 2.11 grams of cannabis and four .22
calibre bullets. [@3]

Presiding judge Petunia Tupou KC said the defendant was not young and had
breached the conditions of a suspended sentence. [@2] Havea was convicted and
sentenced to one year and eight months in prison. [@2] The probation officer
recommended he attend the Alcohol and Drug Abuse Course with the Salvation Army
as part of his sentence. [@2]
