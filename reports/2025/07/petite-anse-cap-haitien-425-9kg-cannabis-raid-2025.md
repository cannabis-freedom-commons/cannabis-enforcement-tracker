Cap-Haïtien police detained three people during a 3:00 a.m. raid on a residence
in the Petite-Anse coastal community on 27 July 2025. [@1] The three suspects
were arrested inside the house and police identified them as women aged 60, 38
and 34. [@1] Officers seized 425.9 kg of cannabis and 525,245 gourdes, about
US$4,000, and the Haitian Times called it one of the largest cannabis busts in
the area that year. [@1]

Police also confiscated three motorcycles, a bicycle, a solar panel, a
television and several mobile phones. [@1] The operation was carried out jointly
by the Office for the Fight against Drug Trafficking (BLTS), the Departmental
Judicial Police Service (SDPJ), the Departmental Unit for the Maintenance of
Order (UDMO) and the Intervention Brigade (BI). [@1] Le P'tit Journal Haïti
reported that the three suspects were being held in police custody awaiting a
hearing by the departmental judicial police. [@2] The BLTS said it was still
investigating the case and had not commented further on the raid. [@1]

UN News, in a November 2025 review of Haitian trafficking trends, recorded 426
kg of cannabis confiscated at Petite-Anse near Cap-Haïtien two weeks after a
record cocaine seizure at Île de la Tortue. [@3]
