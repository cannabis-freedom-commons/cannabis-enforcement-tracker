Guinea's national police directorate announced the seizures in a communique
published on 18 July 2025. [@1] The driver of the cannabis vehicle fled before
officers intervened and remained actively sought. [@3]

The cannabis seizure took place at Salguidi in the Maférinyah sub-prefecture of
Forécariah prefecture. [@1] Officers found six bundles of chanvre indien,
cannabis, weighing 315 kg in total aboard a Toyota Prado registered AI-3132-04
that was stopped after a tail by the Special Intervention Brigade of the Police,
the BSIP. [@1] In the same announcement, police reported 14 packages of various
pharmaceutical products intercepted in the Sincéry quarter of Dabola town. [@2]
Those pharmaceutical products were aboard a Ford minibus travelling from Siguiri
toward Kounsitel, a rural commune in Gaoual prefecture. [@2]

The intercepted vehicles and seized cargoes were handed to the competent
judicial authorities of the prefectures concerned. [@2] The cannabis vehicle and
its load were placed at the disposal of the Forécariah Court of First Instance
for further proceedings. [@3] Police described the Forécariah cannabis seizure
as a record seizure in the Salguidia area of Maférinyah. [@1]
