Governor Gavin Newsom announced on 10 July 2025 that California seized $476
million of unlicensed cannabis between April and June through the Unified
Cannabis Enforcement Task Force (UCETF). [@1,2] The quarter's operations
eradicated 413,302 plants and seized 185,873 pounds (about 84.3 tonnes) of
processed cannabis, serving 214 warrants, taking 77 firearms and making 93
arrests. [@1,2] The task force is co-led by the Department of Cannabis
Control and the Department of Fish and Wildlife. [@1,2]

The quarter included the task force's largest operation to date: a May sweep
across 4,600 square miles of the Central Valley in which 200 officers served
71 warrants, taking 105,700 plants and 22,057 pounds valued at $123.5 million
plus nine firearms. [@1,2] Newsom said the state would not tolerate growers
who undermine the legal market, and enforcement chiefs said operations would
continue until unlicensed growing ends. [@1,2] Since 2019 the state has
seized over 1.9 million pounds of illegal cannabis with an estimated street
value of $3.6 billion. [@2]
