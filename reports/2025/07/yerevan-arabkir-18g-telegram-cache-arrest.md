Police from Yerevan's Arabkir district station detained a 24-year-old resident
of the town of Berd on Nairi Zaryan Street and took him to the station, a police
press service statement said. [@1] Operational information indicated the man had
left a packet of drugs near garages on Azatutyan Avenue, the avenue's name also
spelled Azatutyun in the same report. [@1]

A search of the young man turned up seven packets of a substance resembling
marijuana and a pipette holding the same substance. [@1] The total weight of
what was found was 18 grams, or 0.018 kilograms. [@1] The man had bought the
drug for sale from a user of one of the Telegram messaging app's channels. [@1]

In a search carried out under the criminal case, officers found the packet the
young man had left by a garage in the yard of a building on Azatutyan Avenue.
[@1] The investigation was continuing. [@1]
