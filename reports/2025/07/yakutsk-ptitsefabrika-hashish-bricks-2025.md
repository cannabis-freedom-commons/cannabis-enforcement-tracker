Drug-control officers of the Yakutsk city interior directorate stopped a
30-year-old local man suspected of illegally distributing banned substances.
[@1] Police detained him in the Ptitsefabrika microdistrict on the edge of
Yakutsk while he was setting up hidden stash caches in a forest area. [@1]
Yakutia-Daily, citing the same police operation, said officers detained the
30-year-old while he was setting up hidden stashes in the forest of the
Ptitsefabrika microdistrict on the edge of Yakutsk. [@2]

A personal search of his backpack found five wraps containing an unidentified
dark plant substance. [@1] Officers later recovered 35 pressed bricks with
similar contents from caches he had set up and from a freezer during a search of
his flat. [@1] An examination identified the seized material as the narcotic
hashish weighing more than 4.5 kilograms. [@1] Yakutia-Daily reported the
combined haul as 35 bricks whose contents proved to be hashish weighing more
than 4.5 kilograms, including what officers found in the flat's freezer. [@2]

Operatives said the detained man acted as a so-called storehouse, receiving
criminal goods on the instructions of an unknown curator for later contactless
sale. [@1] A criminal case was opened against the suspect for an attempted
large-scale drug sale under part 3 of article 30 and point A of part 4 of
article 228.1 of the Russian Criminal Code. [@1]
