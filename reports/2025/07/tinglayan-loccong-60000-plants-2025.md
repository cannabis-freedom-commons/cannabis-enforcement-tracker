The Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency's Cordillera regional intelligence and operations team, together with its regional special enforcement team and local police, raided a marijuana plantation in Barangay Loccong, Tinglayan, Kalinga, on 14 and 15 July 2025. [@1]

The operation uprooted approximately 60,000 fully grown marijuana plants from an area of around 4,000 square metres. [@1] All the plants were incinerated on site in line with established procedures. [@1] No one engaged in the cultivation was caught during the operation. [@1]
