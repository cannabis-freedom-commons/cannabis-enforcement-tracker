The Bangkok Metropolitan Police Bureau raided a cannabis shop at the entrance of Soi Intamara 44 in Din Daeng on 9 July 2025, acting on a Criminal Court warrant issued the previous day. [@1,2] The operation was led by Police Lieutenant General Siam Boonsom with Police Major Generals Nopasilp Poonwasit and Pallop Aemonla. [@1,2] The shop was selling cannabis products without the required authorisation. [@1,2]

Officers arrested 28-year-old Worapong at the shop. [@1,2] They seized 371.32 grams of psychedelic mushrooms together with cannabis products: 157 boxes of cannabis-infused electronic cigarettes, 61 bags of cannabis-infused jelly, 83 cannabis brownies and 18 bottles of cannabis-related products. [@1] The mushrooms are not cannabis and are counted only in the text, not the figures. [@1]
