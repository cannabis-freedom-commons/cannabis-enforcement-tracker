Police and soldiers destroyed a cannabis field in Castara, Tobago, on 11 July
2025. [@1] Officers estimated they destroyed about 626 cannabis trees and a
quantity of dried marijuana. [@1] Police valued the haul at TT$5,012,800. [@1]

A police statement issued on 12 July said the discovery was part of an
intelligence-led eradication exercise in the Moriah Police Station district.
[@1] The exercise ran from 5.45 am to 7.25 am, spearheaded by W/ASP Williams and
Insp Bacchus with W/Sgt Gordon-Harris and acting Sgt McMillan. [@1] W/Cpl Alfred
and Ag Cpl Ferguson led the exercise, which included the Tobago Divisional Task
Force, the Canine Unit and regiment members under captain Akil Mc Kenna. [@1]

Acting on information, PC Antoine led the party about 900 metres into a forested
area near the Springs of Castara, where they detected a strong scent of
marijuana. [@1] A search uncovered the growing trees and 1,500 grammes of dried
marijuana. [@1] Officers destroyed the trees and the dried plants by cutting and
burning them on site. [@1]
