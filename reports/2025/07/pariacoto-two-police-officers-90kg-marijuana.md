Two serving National Police non-commissioned officers and a civilian were
detained on 8 July 2025 in Pariacoto district, Huaraz province, with about 90 kg
of marijuana in their vehicles. [@1] The three were the PNP officers Benedicto
Paredes Espinoza, 29, and William Albino Nación, 40, both stationed in Huánuco
region, and civilian Piero Jesús Delgado Vía, 27. [@2] Officers found 16
packages of marijuana weighing 90 kg in total, carrying the drug north from
Huánuco towards Chimbote. [@2]

The stop was made at a spot known as El Milagro by personnel of the Pariacoto
police station, acting on intelligence about a drug shipment from central Peru.
[@2] Huaraz prosecutor Guillermo Lizarzaburu Palma said the load appeared to be
Cannabis sativa and that he was seeking preventive detention. [@3] Lizarzaburu
said the detainees were held initially at Pariacoto police station and then
taken to the National Police anti-drug unit in Huaraz for the proceedings. [@2]

Lizarzaburu said the officers were providing security for the civilian's load;
the Huaraz criminal investigation chief, Nichols Javier Mori Oriundo, said the
civilian identified them as his escorts. [@4] Chimbote's discipline office
temporarily suspended the two officers from duty while the investigation
continues. [@4] Huaraz Noticias said the hearing was set for Monday 21 July at
8:15 a.m. before judge Ventocilla Padilla Ramón Fischer at the transitional
preparatory investigation court. [@5]
