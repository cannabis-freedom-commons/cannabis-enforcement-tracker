A 23-year-old man from Saint-Brieuc was sentenced at Saint-Brieuc on 11 July
2025 to three years in prison, 18 months of them suspended, with immediate
incarceration, for drug trafficking and illegal possession of weapons. [@2]
Ouest-France reported that the 23-year-old was jailed in Brittany for drug
trafficking on Friday 11 July 2025. [@1] He was arrested at his home in Rennes
on Wednesday 9 July 2025. [@2] Two of his resellers, a couple, were arrested at
the same time, so that three people in all were placed in police custody. [@2]
None of the three had a criminal record. [@2] The couple has not yet been tried
and will be summoned to court. [@2]

He admitted the offences and was tried under comparution sur reconnaissance
préalable de culpabilité (CRPC), the French guilty-plea procedure. [@2] The
searches turned up 9 kg of cannabis and police seized three vehicles, two
Volkswagen Golf GTIs and a Mercedes E-Class. [@2] The county court ordered the
confiscation of 2,500 euros in cash seized during the same searches. [@2]

Ouest-France reported that police had watched him since March 2025, suspecting
him of running a trade in cannabis herb and resin in Côtes-d'Armor and
Ille-et-Vilaine. [@1] The Saint-Brieuc police drug squad began the investigation
in March 2025 from a tip passed by a police service during another inquiry, the
Saint-Brieuc prosecutor's office told Le Télégramme on 11 July 2025. [@2]
Investigators found that he supplied himself with cannabis herb and resin from
northern France and Belgium. [@2] He sold cannabis across Côtes-d'Armor and
Ille-et-Vilaine with the help of resellers. [@2] He had recently started a
car-rental business so that his lifestyle would not draw attention. [@2]
Ouest-France reported that he was arrested near Rennes on Wednesday 9 July 2025
and sentenced that Friday at Saint-Brieuc. [@1]
