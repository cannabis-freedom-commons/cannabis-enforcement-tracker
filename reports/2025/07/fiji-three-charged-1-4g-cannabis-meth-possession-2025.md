A Tongan national and two locals appeared in court in Fiji charged with
possession of methamphetamine and cannabis. [@1] The three accused were named as
Mafua Afimeimounga, Mohammed Aziz and Merelita Kusuna. [@1] The court appearance
took place on the afternoon of 11 July 2025. [@1]

Each of the three was charged over 1.4 grams of cannabis, named in the same
charge alongside 2.260 grams of methamphetamine. [@1] The reported
methamphetamine amount in the same charge was 2.260 grams. [@1] The court also
heard that Afimeimounga faces a separate but similar charge related to
methamphetamine possession. [@1]

Bail was denied for all three accused. [@1] The prosecution opposed bail on the
grounds that methamphetamine remains a serious national concern in Fiji. [@1]
The prosecution also raised the risk of the accused absconding. [@1] All three
opted for Legal Aid representation and were advised to file a formal bail
application. [@1] The case was adjourned and will be called again on 25 July.
