Officers of the Firearm and Narcotics Investigation Division found a Nissan
Caravan motor vehicle in Rio Nuevo, St Mary, during an intelligence-led
operation on the night of Saturday 12 July 2025. [@1] The operation began at
about 10:00 pm, according to the Port Maria police. [@1]

A search of the vehicle found 37 bags filled with compressed ganja weighing
about 1,200 pounds. [@1] The Gleaner reported the same figures, giving the ganja
weight as 1,200 pounds in 37 bags. [@2] The 1,200 pounds convert to about 544.31
kilograms. [@2] Officers also seized four large barrels of gasoline from the
vehicle. [@1]

No one was arrested in connection with the find. [@1] The Gleaner likewise
reported that no one was arrested in relation to the discovery. [@2]
