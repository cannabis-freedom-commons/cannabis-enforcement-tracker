Two people were arrested after police seized suspected cannabis plants in the
Strabane area. [@1] The two arrested people remained in custody. [@1]

The seizure was made during a planned search operation on Wednesday. [@1] Police
put the estimated street value of the seized suspected plants at £1.4 million.
[@1] Police said they had dismantled what is believed to be one of the biggest
suspected cannabis operations in the north west in recent years. [@1] Detective
Inspector Pyper said row after row of suspected cannabis plants filled two
floors of the building in a large, sophisticated suspected operation. [@1]
Searches remained ongoing and paraphernalia connected to drugs cultivation and
supply had also been seized. [@1]

Detectives from the Police Service of Northern Ireland's Organised Crime Branch
carried out the operation while investigating suspected drugs criminality linked
to the INLA. [@1] Derry City and Strabane Commander Gillian Kearney said
large-scale drug operations are linked to organised crime gangs who will do
anything to make money with no care for the harm they cause in communities. [@1]
