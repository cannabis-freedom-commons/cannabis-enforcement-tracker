Butte County sheriff's deputies went to the 13000 block of Doe Mill Road in
Forest Ranch at about 12:38 a.m. on 29 July 2025 after a report of shots fired.
[@1] They found no gunshot victims, but a protective search of the property
uncovered a marijuana cultivation and processing operation. [@1] Live marijuana
plants were growing in two greenhouses and at an outdoor grow site on a nearby
parcel, and the sheriff's office said processed marijuana in various stages of
preparation was found inside the residence. [@2] Deputies detained several
people at the scene and then obtained a search warrant for the property. [@2]

During the search deputies seized 1,483 marijuana plants, processed marijuana
and two firearms, according to the sheriff's office. [@1] Five men were arrested
on suspicion of illegal cultivation of marijuana: a 38-year-old from Chico, a
23-year-old from Oaxaca, Mexico, a 26-year-old from Bakersfield, a 41-year-old
from Mexico and a 56-year-old from Sinaloa, Mexico. [@2] The sheriff's office
said the investigation was ongoing and that more details would follow. [@1]
