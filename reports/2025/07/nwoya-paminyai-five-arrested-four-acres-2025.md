A joint operation by the Uganda Police Force and the Uganda People's Defence
Forces (UPDF) arrested five suspects and destroyed four acres of marijuana in
Paminyai Subcounty, Nwoya District. [@1]

Aswa West regional police spokesperson ASP David Ongom Mudong said the suspects
are believed to be part of a trafficking syndicate supplying marijuana to South
Sudan through illegal border routes and to consumers in Gulu City. [@1] The
three-day crackdown targeted cannabis cultivators and traffickers said to be
using Nwoya and neighbouring districts as transit corridors. [@1]

Mudong called the operation part of a broader effort to dismantle narcotics
networks and said the forces would sustain it until all such cells are
neutralised. [@1] The Criminal Investigations Department is compiling case
files for the Resident State Attorney, and the suspects are expected to face
legal action. [@1]
