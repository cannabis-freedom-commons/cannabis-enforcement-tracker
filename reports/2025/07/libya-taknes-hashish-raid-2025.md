Security forces raided a network in the Green Mountain near Taknes, about 127 km east of Benghazi, at dawn on 19 July. [@1] They seized five tonnes of hashish and broke up what officials called a cannabis cultivation and processing operation, arresting several people. [@1] Authorities described it as the first such raid against large-scale drug cultivation and production in the region. [@1]
