Border police found six cannabis fields holding 526 plants in Ograzhden mountain
in Petrich municipality, the Interior Ministry said. [@1] The finds were made in
a special operation against the sowing, growing and distribution of narcotics in
the area covered by the Zlatarevo border police unit. [@1]

On 19 July officers found two fields holding a total of 330 plants that tested
positive for cannabis and weighed a total of 757 kg in the land of Zoychene,
Petrich municipality. [@1] Two Bulgarian citizens from the region were held at
the scene over the growing of the crop. [@1]

On 22 July, in a separate field in the land of the village of Krandzhilitsa, the
Zlatarevo officers found 55 plants that tested positive for cannabis, weighing
82.5 kg of green mass. [@1] On 23 July, in the land of the village of Dragush,
officers found three fields holding 141 cannabis plants weighing 245.5 kg of
green mass. [@1] The total weight of the cannabis came to 245.5 kg of green
mass. [@1]

After samples were taken for the investigation, the plants were uprooted, tied
into bundles and destroyed by a commission set up for the purpose. [@1]
