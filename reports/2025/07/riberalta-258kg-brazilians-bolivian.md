Two Brazilians, Ananias A.A. and Tiarles A.R., and a Bolivian, Gabriel N.I.,
were arrested in Riberalta, in the Beni department of northeastern Bolivia,
after prosecutors and the Bolivian police found them handling sacks of
marijuana. [@1] Beni's departmental prosecutor, Gerardo Balderas, said the three
were being investigated for trafficking controlled substances. [@1]

The operation took place at about 4:50 a.m. in Riberalta, where officers watched
several nervous men carry light-blue jute sacks towards a room. [@2] Two
motorcycles, one black and one red, were parked near the building. [@2] Inside
the room officers found nine jute sacks of brick-shaped packages wrapped in
adhesive tape, and field tests came back positive for marijuana. [@2]

The marijuana weighed 258 kilos and 650 grams, according to the weighing of the
seized substance. [@1] The three men were waiting to be taken before a judge to
have their pre-trial detention decided, according to the prosecutor's account
published the day after the arrest. [@2] The prosecutor's office said the
detainees were waiting to be brought before a court that decides custody, and
the seizure was found in a house in Riberalta. [@3]
