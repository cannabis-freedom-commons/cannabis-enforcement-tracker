Officers of the INI organized-crime directorate, with the Ciocana
criminal-investigation body and Chișinău-Ciocana prosecutors, searched the home
of a 25-year-old suspect. [@2] The suspect, a 25-year-old resident of Stăuceni,
was detained for 72 hours on suspicion of drug trafficking. [@1] Police said the
man coordinated the group's unlawful activity. [@3]

At his home in the town of Stăuceni and in a BMW, officers seized 1,200 grams of
cannabis and 0.5 kg of hashish. [@2] Police put the haul's value on the illicit
market at about 700,000 lei. [@1] Officers also seized a mobile phone containing
information important to the criminal case. [@2]

Investigators said the suspects obtained different types of drugs through the
Telegram app and electronic payment networks. [@2] The money was then
transferred to current accounts in Moldovan commercial banks registered in the
names of front persons. [@2] A criminal case was opened, and if the court finds
the man guilty he faces 7 to 15 years in prison. [@1] Law-enforcement officers
said the investigation was continuing to identify the group's remaining members.
