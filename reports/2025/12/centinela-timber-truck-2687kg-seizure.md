Gendarmería Nacional Argentina seized cannabis sativa hidden in a truck carrying
eucalyptus and pine wood during a check at kilometre 785 of National Route 14 at
Paraje Centinela, Misiones. [@1] The operation was directed by the Ministry of
National Security. [@1]

Authorities described it as the third large marijuana seizure detected in the
littoral region using the same concealment method. [@1] The drug was carried in
a semi-trailer boarded up with eucalyptus and pine wood. [@1] In the early
morning, officers of Reinforced Section San José, under Squadron 8 Alto Uruguay,
were conducting checks at kilometre 785 of National Route 14 at Paraje Centinela
when they inspected a general-cargo truck. [@1] The truck was travelling from 9
de Julio in Misiones to San Fernando in Buenos Aires province. [@1] The vehicle
was occupied by two men of Argentine nationality. [@1] At dawn, officers stopped
a transport that had departed from 9 de Julio, Misiones. [@2]

The mobile scanner found anomalies consistent with hidden packages among the
wood load, and narcotics detection dog León reacted positively. [@1] Detector
dog León gave a positive marking that confirmed the officers' suspicions. [@2]
With the intervention of the Federal Court and Federal Prosecutor of Posadas,
the truck was taken to the unit for a more thorough inspection. [@1] Officers
found six large pressure-built sawn-wood crates fitted between bales of
machimbre, holding 2,836 rectangular and shapeless packages containing cannabis
sativa. [@1] Field Narcotest tests confirmed the substance, with a total weight
of 2,687 kilos 46 grams. [@1]

Authorities ordered seizure of the drug, the vehicle with its trailer and three
mobile phones. [@1] The two truck occupants were detained for violating Law
23.737, with the participation of the International Centre for Analysis of
Complex Crime on the Border. [@1]
