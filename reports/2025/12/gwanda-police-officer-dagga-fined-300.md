Knowledge Maposa, 32, a Zimbabwe Republic Police officer stationed in Gwanda,
was convicted of unlawful possession of dangerous drugs at the Gwanda
Magistrates' Court and fined US$300. [@1] The court sentenced him to pay the
US$300 fine or serve three months in prison in default of payment. [@1] The
Sunday Mail named the offence as unlawful possession of dangerous drugs and said
the conviction came "last week". [@3]

On 29 December 2025, detectives in Gwanda received a tip-off that a police
officer was keeping dangerous drugs in his room inside police premises. [@1] The
next day, officers searched Maposa's room and recovered dagga with a street
value of ZiG120, together with a J-pipe used for smoking crystal
methamphetamine, and arrested him. [@1] No weight was given for the dagga; the
National Prosecuting Authority described it only by its street value. [@2]

After the sentencing, the National Prosecuting Authority of Zimbabwe said it
would keep working with police and the courts to arrest offenders and secure
convictions. [@1] Pindula News reproduced the same prosecuting authority account
of the tip-off, the search, the ZiG120 value and the J-pipe. [@2] Ziyah News
reported that the National Prosecuting Authority issued its statement on the
Monday after the sentence and said Maposa was arrested at the scene and later
charged. [@4] Ziyah News said the case followed a Beitbridge regional court's
remand of another Gwanda policeman accused of being found with large quantities
of dagga. [@4]
