Belgian customs intercepted some nine tonnes of narcotics at Brussels Airport in 2025,
including eight tonnes of cannabis, more than double the seizures of 2024. [@1,2] The figures
were presented at the annual press conference of Belgium's General Administration of Customs
and Excise (AAD&A). [@1,2] Customs chief Kristian Vanderwaeren said the rise was driven by a
massive influx of cannabis from Thailand, the United States and Canada, where higher European
prices make the trade more profitable. [@1,2]

The drugs arrived by freight cargo, small parcels sent by post and passenger luggage. [@1,2]
Officers took 1,767 kilograms of cannabis from travellers, about four times the 2024 amount,
and 429 kilograms from postal parcels. [@2] Vanderwaeren admitted large quantities still slip
through undetected. [@1,2]
