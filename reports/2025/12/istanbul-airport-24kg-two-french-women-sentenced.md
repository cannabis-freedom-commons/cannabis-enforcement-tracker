An Istanbul court sentenced two young Frenchwomen, Ibtissem, 22, and Mariam, 23,
to ten years in prison each on 23 December 2025 for carrying nearly 25 kilos of
cannabis from Thailand. [@1] The pair had been arrested on 28 February during a
stopover at Istanbul airport. [@1] They were carrying two suitcases holding two
lots of 12 kilos of the drug. [@1] Each woman was also fined 100,000 Turkish
lira, about 1,990 euros. [@1]

The judge convicted them of drug transport rather than drug dealing, which would
have carried a heavier sentence. [@1] Their lawyers recalled that cannabis
trafficking is punishable by 20 years in Turkey and cocaine trafficking by at
least 30 years. [@1] The defence said its aim now was to obtain the women's
transfer to a French prison. [@1] Turkey, a transit country for drugs between
Asia, the Middle East and Europe, deals severely with drug trafficking. [@1]

At the first hearing in September, the women said a friend and his accomplice
had given them the suitcases in Bangkok to hand to the friend's mother in
Belgium. [@1] Both women have always maintained that they did not know what they
were carrying. [@1] Ibtissem's aunt said the childhood friend was in prison in
Amiens, which the two women had not known. [@1] They have spent ten months in
Silivri prison, west of Istanbul. [@1]
