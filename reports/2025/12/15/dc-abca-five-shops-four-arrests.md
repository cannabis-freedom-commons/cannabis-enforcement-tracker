Between 10 and 15 December 2025 the Alcoholic Beverage and Cannabis Administration (ABCA) and the
Metropolitan Police Department padlocked five unlicensed cannabis retailers and arrested four
people. [@1,2] ABCA named Capitol CBD, District Hemp Botanicals, Flowerz, Grass & Co and Seman Kwik
Stop, saying each posed a public-health risk through illegal sales. [@1]

The wave brought to 93 the number of illegal cannabis businesses padlocked since civil closure
powers took effect on 15 July 2024. [@1,2] At Capitol CBD on 6 P Street NE, agents seized 5.5
pounds of marijuana and assorted THC products and arrested Patrick Gray, 51, of Silver Spring,
Maryland. [@1] District Hemp Botanicals at 1323 Connecticut Avenue NW yielded packaged marijuana,
pre-rolled cigarettes and 16,262.7 grams of THC edibles; Raliwa Keleta, 48, of Northwest
Washington, was arrested. [@1]

At Flowerz on 318 Massachusetts Avenue NE, ABCA listed 68.3 pounds of marijuana, 11.4 pounds of
pre-rolled cigarettes and very large quantities of THC beverages, gummies and concentrates; Alexandra
Hynson Keleta, 30, of Alexandria, Virginia, was arrested. [@1,2] Grass & Co on 1730 Columbia Road
NW yielded 8.9 pounds of marijuana and smaller THC inventories; Hussain Khalid, 49, of Ellicott
City, Maryland, was arrested. [@1] Seman Kwik Stop on 4801 Georgia Avenue NW was padlocked without
a cannabis seizure. [@1,2]

ABCA's shop-by-shop marijuana weights total about 97 pounds (roughly 44 kg); Outlaw Report noted
additional THC products at each site beyond that herb total. [@1,2] Two hemp operators told
Washington City Paper they believed they complied with federal law; that claim was not resolved in
these accounts. [@2]
