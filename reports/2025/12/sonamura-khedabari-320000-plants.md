On 29 December 2025, Sepahijala District Police carried out a joint operation with the Border Security Force, Tripura State Rifles, Forest Department and other agencies in forest areas around Khedabari, Anandapur and Ghatigarh under Sonamura Police Station. [@1]

The operation destroyed 35 cultivation plots containing approximately 320,000 mature cannabis plants across about 65 acres, which the report converted to an estimated market value of INR64 million. [@1]
