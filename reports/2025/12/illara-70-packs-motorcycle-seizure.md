A night patrol of the Illara border police station, the Commissariat frontalier
de Police d'Illara, intercepted a motorcycle carrying a parcel containing
cannabis, known locally as chanvre indien, during the night of 9 to 10 December.
[@1] The team, out on night surveillance, spotted a motorcyclist transporting a
bulky load in the Illèchi quarter near the border. [@1] Officers tried to stop
the rider for a check because his behaviour drew their attention, but he
abandoned the motorcycle and fled into the bush under cover of darkness. [@1] No
arrest was made at the scene, and police opened an investigation to find the
rider. [@1]

Officers recovered the motorcycle and the parcel and took them to the police
station. [@1] Examination of the parcel there counted 70 wrapped packs of
cannabis. [@1]

The account was relayed from the digital platforms of the Police républicaine,
Benin's national Republican Police. [@1] Investigators are working to establish
whether the rider acted alone or with other people. [@1] The Illara border
station appealed to residents to report any information useful for identifying
the suspect or detecting possible storage points for prohibited products in the
area. [@1]
