Macau's Judiciary Police arrested an 18-year-old Malaysian man on suspicion of
trafficking 6,279 grams of cannabis buds valued at 6.27 million patacas. [@1]
Police spokesman Chao Teng Hin said the suspect, surnamed Goh, said he was
jobless. [@1]

Officers stopped Goh at Macau's airport as he entered the city with a suitcase
of cannabis buds and prepared to leave in a car the group had arranged. [@1]
Twelve vacuum-sealed packets of cannabis buds wrapped in silver foil were found
in the suitcase, and police put their street value at 6.27 million patacas. [@1]

Police said intelligence and cross-border risk checks identified a trafficking
group planning to send a courier, known in Cantonese as "airplane pig", with
drugs from Thailand via the Philippines to Macau for onward distribution in Hong
Kong and neighbouring regions. [@1] Chao said Goh was recruited in Malaysia,
collected the suitcase in Thailand and flew on to the Philippines before
arriving in Macau, where he planned to pass the drugs on to Hong Kong
immediately. [@1] The driver of the car meant to carry Goh was initially ruled
out as an accomplice, and police later confirmed the seized drugs were cannabis.
[@1]

Under questioning Goh admitted trafficking the buds and said he had been
promised 10,000 Malaysian ringgit, about 19,300 patacas. [@1] He was transferred
to the Public Prosecutions Office facing a drug-trafficking charge. [@1]
