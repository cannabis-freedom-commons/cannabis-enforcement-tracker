Kabul's primary court for the prevention of narcotics and alcoholic beverages
gave disciplinary sentences to 15 offenders, the Supreme Court said. [@1] All 15
were arrested on charges of selling and trafficking intoxicating tablets (Zikap
and "K-A"), alcoholic beverages and hashish. [@1] The court's Pashto-language
statement, carried by Pajhwok, names the same tablets and alcohol and calls the
cannabis charas, the local term for hashish. [@2] The primary court sentenced
the 15 to prison terms of seven months to four years, plus 10 to 39 lashes each.
[@1] Kabul Tribune reported the same 15 convictions, the same seven months to
four years and the same 10 to 39 lashes from the Supreme Court statement. [@3]

Kabul Tribune adds that the primary court issued the decisions and the Supreme
Court confirmed them before they were enforced. [@3] Both accounts put the
hashish in one ruling with synthetic tablets and alcohol, and neither gives a
weight of cannabis. [@3]
