Malagasy customs officers intercepted a Latvian national on Saturday 13 December
at Fascène international airport in Nosy Be as he was about to board a flight to
Réunion. [@1] His luggage aroused the suspicions of customs officers, who had it
opened immediately in his presence before tests confirmed the contents were
drugs. [@2] The man was immediately detained and placed in custody. [@1] The
Malagasy authorities opened an investigation to establish the origin of the
drugs and whether he acted alone or for an international trafficking network.
[@1]

During the customs check, officers found about 3 kg of cocaine and 5.5 kg of
hashish hidden in his baggage. [@1] The cannabis component recorded here is the
5.5 kg of hashish, while the cocaine and the 8.5 kg combined drug total are
excluded from cannabis figures. [@2] He was due to board Air Austral flight UI
432 from Nosy Be to Réunion. [@1] Testing confirmed a total of 8.5 kg, split as
5.5 kg of hashish and about 3 kg of cocaine. [@2]

Customs interceptions of passengers carrying drugs at the airport are not new,
and baggage is also screened by other means such as scanners. [@2] Customs also
has sniffer dogs specially trained to detect drugs. [@2]
