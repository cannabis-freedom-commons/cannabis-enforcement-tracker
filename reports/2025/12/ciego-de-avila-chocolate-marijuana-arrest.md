Two people were detained in Ciego de Ávila with a load of several pounds of
marijuana that they intended to move to Havana. [@1] The detention was reported
through the officialist Facebook profile Elizabeth Ferrer. [@1] The operation
resulted from coordination among law-enforcement forces from different
territories. [@1] Investigations were continuing in order to determine the scope
of the alleged network involved in the case. [@1]

The marijuana was found hidden inside sacks that apparently contained chocolate.
[@1] A second account likewise says the drug was hidden in sacks simulating
chocolate and that its destination was Havana. [@2] The second account says the
case information was disclosed through Elizabeth Ferrer’s officialist Facebook
profile. [@2] The second account describes the operation as involving
coordination among law-enforcement forces from several regions of the country.
[@2]
