Military police in Maranhão destroyed 5,100 cannabis plants in an illegal
plantation in the early hours of Thursday 18 December 2025. [@1] The plantation
was at povoado Mundico, in the rural area of Santa Helena, a town 115 km from
São Luís, the state capital. [@1]

Officers found the crop after an anonymous tip and walked about 2.5 km through
dense, hard-to-reach forest to reach it. [@1] No suspects were found at the
site, but police saw signs that people had been living there, such as leftover
food and tools used to grow the drug. [@1]

Officers destroyed the plants on the spot and registered the case at the Santa
Helena police station, which will handle the investigation. [@1]
