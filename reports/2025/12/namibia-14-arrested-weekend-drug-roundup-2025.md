Namibian police arrested 14 people across the country over the weekend of 19 to
21 December 2025 on charges related to possessing and dealing in illicit drugs.
[@1]

Police spokesperson Deputy Commissioner Kauna Shikwambi said five men aged
between 23 and 33 were arrested at Rehoboth on Friday after police found them
selling cannabis and half a Mandrax tablet from their residence, following a
tip-off. [@1] Shikwambi said the Rehoboth drugs were estimated to be worth more
than N$100 000 and that police confiscated more than N$10 000 in cash believed
to be proceeds of drug sales. [@1] At Karasburg on Saturday, a 41-year-old man
was arrested after a search of his home around 02h00 found Mandrax tablets, and
he was expected to appear in the Karasburg Magistrate's Court on Monday. [@1]

At Soweto in Katutura, police arrested two 22-year-old suspects around 17h25
after finding crack cocaine and Mandrax tablets at their residence, drugs valued
at N$4 640. [@1] A 37-year-old Namibian man was arrested at Keetmanshoop at
about 00h50 on Saturday after a body search found cannabis “ballies” on him.
[@1] In the Maltahöhe district, a 60-year-old man was arrested on Friday after a
search of his home found Mandrax tablets and bankies of skunk cannabis, and
Shikwambi said the pensioner remained in police custody. [@1] An 18-year-old was
arrested later that evening at Maltahöhe after being found with cannabis in a
search at 19h20. [@1] At Outjo, two suspects aged 37 and 45 were arrested on
Friday after police on patrol searched their residence and found a plastic bag
of skunk cannabis and bankies valued at N$3 250, and they were expected in the
Outjo Magistrate's Court on Monday. [@1] A 42-year-old man was arrested on
Friday on the M0070 Omitara–Otjinene gravel road after police found him carrying
a plastic bag of cannabis. [@1]

Police said investigations in all the cases were ongoing. [@1]
