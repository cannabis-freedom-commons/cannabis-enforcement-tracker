On the morning of December 17, 2025, the OIJ — Costa Rica's Judicial
Investigation Agency — carried out a search in Orotina, where they detained two
men suspected of drug sales under the country's psychotropic law. [@1] According
to the authorities, the investigation began about a month earlier, when OIJ
received confidential information that the two men were engaged in drug sales.
[@1] Drug sales were carried out from a house located in central Orotina,
specifically in the community called El Mastate. [@1] The two detained men were
surnamed Vásquez, age 41, and Sosa, age 37. [@1]

During the early-morning raid, OIJ seized marijuana, mobile phones, and cash,
which were incorporated as evidence into the judicial proceedings. [@1] After
the operation ended, the two detainees were placed at the disposal of the Public
Ministry, which will determine their legal status as the investigation proceeds.
[@1]
