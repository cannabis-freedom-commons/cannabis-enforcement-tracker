Police arrested and formally charged 18-year-old Ocean Maat Lolita Hinds of
Lammings, St Joseph, over offences alleged to have been committed on Friday,
December 5, 2025. [@1] The Barbados Police Service said its Narcotics Unit made
the arrest and brought the charges. [@2]

Police put the suspected cannabis at 22.80 kilograms, with an estimated street
value of $182,400. [@1] The CBC account states the street value as $182,400.00
in Barbados currency. [@2]

Hinds was scheduled to appear in the District ‘B’ Magistrates’ Court on Monday,
December 8, 2025. [@1]
