Pune police arrested five persons from different cities over the past month in a
drug-peddling case. [@1] The five were identified as Swaraj Anant Bhosale,
Tushar Chetan Verma, Sumit Santosh Dedwal, Akshay Sukhlal Meher and Malay Rajesh
Deliwala, aged 21 to 28. [@2] Verma was arrested first in Khadki during routine
patrolling. [@3] A case against him was registered under the Narcotic Drugs and
Psychotropic Substances Act. [@3] All the accused were held in police custody
under the NDPS Act. [@2]

Police seized 1.194 kg of ganja and an electronic weighing scale from Verma.
[@3] The accused had rented a flat in a densely populated area of
Pimpri-Chinchwad where they had set up a soil-based hydroponic ganja cultivation
unit. [@2] Police dismantled the illegal setup and seized live plants, ganja
stock and cultivation equipment from the premises. [@3] Police said nine
different types of narcotics seized included ganja and hydroponic OG Kush,
charas, cannabis gummies and CBD oil alongside MD, mushrooms, LSD papers and
pills. [@2] Police put narcotics and related material seized in the case at
approximately Rs 3.45 crore. [@2] Police also froze Rs 7.80 lakh in bank
accounts, crypto wallets and foreign currency linked to the accused. [@2]

The investigation uncovered links to an international drug-peddling network in
Thailand and Bhutan using the dark web and hawala payments in cryptocurrency.
[@1] Dark web and cryptocurrency were used to bring OG-Kush ganja and other
substances into India, with drugs imported from Thailand and Bhutan to Assam and
then to Pune hidden in courier services. [@1] The operation, code-named
Operation Alakh Niranjan, was carried out under the supervision of senior Pune
police officers. [@3] Further investigation was under way and police said more
arrests were likely. [@3]
