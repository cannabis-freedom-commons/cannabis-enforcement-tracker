Units of Tunisia's National Guard broke up a network that marketed drugs and
seized more than 420 kg of cannabis. [@1] Two members of the network were
arrested and a third fled and was being sought, while seven other members were
covered by wanted notices, according to a statement published on Thursday by the
National Guard's general directorate. [@1]

The same statement said the network's members were in contact with a group of
people based in one of the European countries. [@1] Réalités Magazine reported
that two members of the criminal organisation were arrested while a third
managed to flee and was being intensively sought. [@3] On the instructions of
the Nabeul prosecutor's office, the National Guard's central anti-drug brigade
at El Aouina was assigned to continue the investigation. [@3]

The operation led to the seizure of more than 420 kilograms of cannabis and of
two vehicles used in the network's illegal activities. [@3] The first findings
of the investigation indicated the network was run by a group of people based in
a European country who coordinated its trafficking and distribution operations
from there. [@3]

The brigade was also ordered to keep the arrested suspects in detention and to
issue wanted notices for seven other people involved in the case. [@3] The
statement was published on Thursday on the official page of the National Guard's
general directorate. [@3] Kapitalis reported that the National Guard's general
directorate announced the dismantling of an international drug-trafficking
network on Thursday 4 December 2025. [@2] Kapitalis said the investigation had
identified seven other people who were now the subject of search warrants in
Tunisia. [@2]
