The Pierce County Sheriff's Office Special Investigations Unit dismantled an
illegal marijuana grow operation and took seven people into custody. [@1] On 5
December 2025, three adult women and four adult men were taken into custody.
[@1] The seven are now facing charges of unlawful manufacturing of a controlled
substance and unlawful use of a building for drug purposes. [@1]

Deputies executed search warrants at three separate locations: the 8100 block of
113th St. Ct E, the 22000 block of 45th Ave. E, and the 7500 block of 184th St.
E. [@1] The sheriff's office said each site revealed extensive grow operations.
[@1] The searches led to the confiscation of over 2,000 marijuana plants and all
associated equipment. [@1] Law enforcement also seized more than $16,000 in cash
and two firearms. [@1]

The sheriff's office said that while marijuana is legal in Washington state,
unlicensed grow operations remain illegal and pose potential dangers to the
community. [@1]
