The investigative department of the interior ministry division for Khabarovsky
district opened three criminal cases against a 50-year-old resident of the
village of Knyaze-Volkonskoye with repeated previous convictions, under part 3
of article 30 with part 4 of article 228.1 and part 2 of article 228 of the
Russian Criminal Code. [@1] Drug-control officers of the Khabarovsk Krai
interior ministry department stopped his activity together with district police
colleagues and with power support from Rosgvardia fighters. [@1] He was placed
in custody as a measure of restraint. [@1]

During a search of the house and outbuildings belonging to him, officers found
and seized more than 35 kilograms of marijuana, over 18 grams of hashish oil,
more than 5 kilograms of cannabis parts and about 50,000 rubles earned from
selling the drug. [@1] He also kept a 16-gauge smoothbore shotgun and 94
cartridges of 16 mm calibre without permits. [@1] In the same place officers
found a loose substance in two metal cans with a total mass of 181 grams, which
experts identified as smokeless hunting gunpowder. [@1] The material on the
illegal storage of the weapon, ammunition and explosives was separated into a
separate proceeding, and an inquiry is under way. [@1]

According to the detainee's testimony, last spring he bought cannabis seeds from
an unknown person and planted them in a forest belt near his house, then
harvested the crop and made the drug at home for his own use. [@1] The
investigation believes, however, that he supplied fellow villagers and residents
of nearby settlements with the drug. [@1] He said he had found the weapon,
cartridges and gunpowder about 10 years ago while fishing and had kept the find,
supposedly to defend himself against wild animals. [@1]
