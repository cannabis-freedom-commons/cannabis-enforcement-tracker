On 5 December 2025, Police Regional Office–Cordillera (PRO-CAR) teams ran
coordinated marijuana eradication operations across Kalinga and Benguet
provinces. [@1]

In Kalinga, operatives found three plantations holding 16,000 fully grown
marijuana plants valued at P3.2 million in Barangay Butbut Proper, Tinglayan,
working with the 2nd Kalinga Provincial Mobile Force Company, the Tinglayan
police, provincial drug enforcement and intelligence units and PDEA-CAR. [@1]

In Benguet, combined forces found four plantations with 7,210 fully grown
plants and 18,400 seedlings carrying a standard drug price of P2.178 million
in Barangay Kayapa, Bakun and Barangay Badeo, Kibungan. [@1]

All the plants were uprooted and burned on site, and police said
investigations continue to identify other cultivation sites and the people
behind the plantations. [@1]
