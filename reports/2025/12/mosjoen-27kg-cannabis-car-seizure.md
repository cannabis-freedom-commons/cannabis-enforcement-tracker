On 15 December 2025, Nordland police announced that they had seized 27 kilograms of cannabis from a car parked in Mosjøen the previous month. [@1,2] A man in his 30s was arrested, charged with an aggravated drug offence and remanded in custody for up to four weeks. [@1,2]

Police estimated the cannabis's street value at about four million Norwegian kroner and said the case formed part of their work against criminal networks. [@1] They were trying to identify other people involved, but said they did not know whether the cannabis was meant for the Nordland market. [@1]

The man admitted bringing a smaller amount of drugs but said he did not know about all the cannabis in the car. [@1,2] Vesterålnytt reported that police believed the cannabis had been loaded in Oslo for transport north. [@2] It also reported that police searched the man's home in eastern Norway and seized electronic devices and small amounts of other drugs. [@2] Helgelands Blad's photo caption identifies its police evidence image as packages found in the Mosjøen car. [@3]

The sources do not state the exact day the car was searched in November. [@1,2] The police statement described an ongoing investigation; the sources reviewed here give no later court outcome. [@1,2]
