On 22 December 2025 Mexico's Federal Attorney General's Office (FGR) announced that it had taken notice of the destruction of 17 cannabis plots in the rural municipality of Mascota, Jalisco. [@1,2] The FGR said Defence Ministry personnel found the plots during ground patrols conducted under Operation Perla. [@1,2]

Authorities destroyed about 116,900 cannabis plants by burning and uprooting them, and seized 470 kilograms of marijuana from the sites. [@1,2] The FGR said no one was detained. [@1,2]

The FGR said representative samples from the plots were placed at the disposal of the Federal Public Ministry, which would continue assembling the investigation file. [@1,2]
