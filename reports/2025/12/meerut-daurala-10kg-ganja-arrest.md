Daurala police in Meerut arrested a 24-year-old youth and recovered 10 kilograms
of illegal cannabis from him at the Sakauti underpass during a night patrol and
check on 1 December 2025. [@1] The arrested man was identified as Udit Kumar,
son of Ashok Kumar, a resident of Daha village in the Doghat police station area
of Baghpat district. [@1] Amar Ujala adds that the arrest was made late on
Monday night, 1 December 2025, during a check near the Sakauti underpass. [@2]
Police said the youth's movements looked suspicious while they were checking
vehicles near the underpass, and that when they stopped him the cannabis was
found with him. [@2]

Daurala police station registered a case against him, number 379/25, under
section 8/20 of the NDPS Act, India's main narcotics law. [@1] Police took the
cannabis and the suspect to the station. [@2] After questioning, he was produced
in court, which sent him to jail. [@2] The arrest was made under a drive ordered
by the Meerut senior superintendent of police to catch criminals and keep repeat
offenders off the streets, police said. [@1]

According to the Bhaskar English account, the arrested man already faced a case
at Doghat police station in Baghpat, number 178/22, under sections 323, 504 and
506 of the Indian Penal Code. [@1] The team that made the arrest included
Sakauti outpost in-charge Sub-Inspector Kaushal Kishore and Daurala police
station Sub-Inspector Akashdeep. [@1]
