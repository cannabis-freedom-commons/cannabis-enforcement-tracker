On 27 December 2025, Sepahijala District Police carried out an anti-narcotics operation with BSF, Tripura State Rifles, Forest Department teams and other agencies in forest areas around Kamalnagar, Batadola, Dhanirampur and Bijoy Nagar under Sonamura Police Station. [@1]

The operation destroyed 38 cultivation plots containing approximately 360,000 mature cannabis plants across about 75 acres of forest. [@1]
