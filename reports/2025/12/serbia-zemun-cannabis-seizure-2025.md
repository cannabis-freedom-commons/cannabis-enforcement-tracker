Belgrade's Higher Public Prosecutor filed an indictment against two defendants on 5 December 2025 in a case involving two tonnes of cannabis seized in Zemun. [@1]

The prosecutor had opened the investigation on 21 August 2025 after the cannabis was found packaged for further sale. [@1] The office confirmed both steps in a statement published on 6 February 2026. [@1]
