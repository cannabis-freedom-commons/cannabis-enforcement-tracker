Greenland police and customs officers arrested a man at 19:30 on Friday 5 December 2025 as he was
on his way out of Nuuk on a coastal passenger ship, with more than a kilogram of hash on him.
[@1,2] The next day, Saturday 6 December, the district court gave him a straksdom, an immediate
sentence, of five months' unconditional detention in the anstalt, Greenland's prison. [@2] The
case was closed and he was released to serve the sentence later. [@2]

The arrest was a joint operation between the police and the customs service, and KNR reported the
police account that the man had faced five months' unconditional detention. [@1] Neither report
names the man or gives his age, and neither says where he had got the hash or where he was
travelling to. [@1,2]
