On 27 December 2025, Pecharthal Police and personnel from the 15th Battalion of the Tripura State Rifles conducted an anti-narcotics operation in the Kuli Basti forest near Machmara Tea Garden, Unakoti district. [@1,2]

The teams destroyed approximately 15,000 to 17,000 mature cannabis plants spread across eight plots. [@1,2]
