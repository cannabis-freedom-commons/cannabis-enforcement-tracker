The Risk Analysis Unit of customs officers and Civil Guard border officers at Algeciras seized more than 2,100 kilogrammes of hashish from lorry trailers arriving from Tangier in Morocco. [@1,2] On 9 November, cabotage officers checking a trailer found unusual welds and finishes in its underbody beams, and a drug-detection dog marked the suspect area. [@1,2] Inspectors found the hashish resin hidden inside the beams and lower structures as a false bottom designed to defeat routine checks. [@1,2]

Daily vehicle monitoring then turned up five more trailers with the same concealment, bringing the total to six intercepted lorries and 2,133 kilogrammes seized. [@1,2] The six drivers were arrested on suspicion of offences against public health and handed to the courts with the seized drugs. [@1,2]
