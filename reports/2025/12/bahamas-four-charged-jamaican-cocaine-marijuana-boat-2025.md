Four men, three Bahamian and one Jamaican, were denied bail in The Bahamas on
charges from an alleged attempt to smuggle cocaine and marijuana into the
country. [@1] Prosecutors allege that Bahamians Howard Rolle, James Jones and
Zenard Rolle and Jamaican national Nicholas Duckenson, of Negril, planned to
bring in cocaine valued at US$18.4 million and marijuana valued at US$7,000.
[@1]

The US Coast Guard intercepted the Bahamian-registered go-fast boat carrying the
drugs off the coast of Cuba on 7 December. [@1] Police seized 2,250 pounds of
cocaine, seven pounds of marijuana and a .40 Smith & Wesson pistol from the
vessel. [@1]

At their arraignment before Senior Magistrate Raquel Whyms the four denied
possession of drugs with intent to supply, conspiracy to import drugs with
intent to supply, conspiracy to possess drugs with intent to supply and
possession of an unlicensed firearm. [@1] Bail was denied and their trial is
scheduled for 16 April. [@1]
