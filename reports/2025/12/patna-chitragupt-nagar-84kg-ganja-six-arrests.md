Patna police arrested six people and seized 84 kg of ganja that officers valued
at about Rs 1 crore. [@1] Chitragupt Nagar police and the district intelligence
unit carried out the operation together. [@1] Police said the gang used Patna as
a transit point to send ganja to Delhi and Varanasi. [@1]

Acting on a tip-off, officers stopped a car and caught five men trying to flee
with 34 kg of ganja hidden in two trolleys and a sack. [@1] The five men were
all from Vaishali district and told police they ran a supply chain from a rented
flat in Ashok Nagar. [@1] A raid on the flat led to the arrest of the alleged
mastermind and the recovery of a further 50 kg of ganja, four mobile phones and
two walkie-talkies. [@1] Three vehicles, including luxury SUVs, were seized, and
the flat had been used for packing and distribution. [@1]

City SP (East) Parichay Kumar said the gang bought ganja in Agartala and
Guwahati at Rs 18,000 to Rs 20,000 per kg and moved it by train in trolley bags.
[@1] Couriers were paid Rs 2,000 a trip, and the gang sold the ganja in Delhi
and Varanasi at Rs 80,000 to Rs 90,000 per kg. [@1] Police said the gang had
operated for months, planned to open an office in Delhi, and suspected that 10
to 15 more people were involved. [@1]
