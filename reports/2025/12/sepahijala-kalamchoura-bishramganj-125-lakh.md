Two joint drives in Sepahijala district destroyed more than 1.25 lakh cannabis plants on 6 December 2025. [@1]

Between noon and 2:30 pm, a team led by Inspector Arup Debbarma of Kalamchoura police station, with sub-inspectors, station staff, 9th Battalion TSR men and Boxanagar forest officials, worked the Nayajala gram panchayat area at Dopuriabhan Yasiadong Para. [@1] That team uprooted about 25,000 premature plants across twelve plots in difficult forest terrain. [@1]

From 11 am to 4 pm, a second team led by Inspector Ajit Debbarma of Bishramganj police station, with district branch staff, women police and 11th Battalion TSR men, moved through Latiacherra, Ram Krishna Para, Bhubhan Para, Chikan Cherra, Jogiram Para and Madhu Para. [@1] That team dismantled about a lakh fully matured plants across eleven plots. [@1]

The account presents the drives as part of an ongoing crackdown on cultivation networks in remote forest zones. [@1] It names no growers, and no arrests were reported. [@1]
