Police and Tripura State Rifles (TSR) officers seized 94 kg of dried cannabis from a Maruti car during a highway check in the Amtali area. [@1] The car was signalled to stop but sped toward Bishalgarh, and a police team led by Anti-Narcotics SP Shyamananda Sharma, Amtali SDPO Paramita Pandey and Amtali police station chief Paritosh Das chased it down and intercepted it at Chaumuhani Bazaar. [@1]

Police identified the driver as Akash Barman of Konaban under Madhupur police station, who tried to escape but was arrested. [@1] A forensic team examined the seizure in the presence of Bikramnagar tehsildar Alok Chakraborty and Sadar subdivision administration representatives, and police confirmed the recovery of 94 kg of cannabis. [@1] Barman was being interrogated at Amtali Police Station while investigators probed whether others were involved in the smuggling network. [@1]
