Livardo Rogelio Hinds was spared a further prison term when Justice Elwood
Watts, in Barbados' No. 2A Supreme Court, ordered him to pay fines on the spot
instead of serving the rest of his sentence. [@1] He also admitted to having
1.09 kilogrammes of cannabis. [@2] For the cannabis, Justice Watts ordered him
to pay $2 000 or serve 18 months. [@1] He was also fined $20 000 for the gun and
$13 000 for the ammunition or jailed, and all the fines were due immediately and
were paid. [@1]

The judge said possession of a firearm in Barbados ought to bring an automatic
custodial sentence, but noted Hinds' medical challenges and that prosecution and
defence both agreed a custodial sentence should not be imposed. [@1] Justice
Watts had taken a starting point of eight years for the firearm and ammunition,
citing the extended magazine and Hinds' previous convictions, before discounts
for the guilty plea and 512 days on remand left a term of three years 339 days.
[@1] State Counsel Dr Zoe King prosecuted the case and defence attorney Shadia
Simpson represented Hinds. [@1]

Police executing a search warrant at a Glendairy Road, Bridge Road, St Michael,
home found Hinds on a sofa beside a clothes basket holding a bag with four
transparent bags of vegetable matter suspected to be cannabis. [@1] Beneath the
bag in the basket were a 9mm Luger semi-automatic pistol with an extended
magazine, a ski mask and a firearm holder; Hinds first denied the gun but said
the cannabis was his. [@1]

Hinds pleaded guilty before Justice Watts on 10 November 2025, and his case was
adjourned to 18 November. [@2] Nation News reported that Hinds was remanded to
Dodds Prison and that police had executed the warrant at the residence of
another man, where a second man was also present. [@3]
