In December 2025, Raikia Police Station officer Ashutosh Paik led an anti-cannabis operation with the Phulbani Excise Department and Forest Department in the Naudimahao forest area of Raikia block, Kandhamal district. [@1]

The teams uprooted more than 100,000 cannabis plants across a 25-acre plantation, and the report estimated the destroyed crop at about INR20 million. [@1]
