Sabarkantha police arrested a 38-year-old man from Lakhiya village after a drone
check confirmed he was growing cannabis on land in front of his house. [@1] The
Special Operation Group (SOG), Gujarat's anti-narcotics unit, seized 558 green
cannabis plants weighing 226.237 kilograms. [@1] Police put the market value of
the seized green cannabis at about Rs 1.13 crore. [@1] The raid followed a
tip-off while the SOG team was patrolling the Poshina area under SOG PI D.C.
Parmar and PSI P.M. Zala. [@1] Drone surveillance was used to trace the
plantation and confirm the exact location before officers moved in. [@1] The
raid was carried out in the presence of government-appointed panch witnesses.
[@1] A case was registered against the accused at Poshina Police Station under
the NDPS Act and further legal proceedings were initiated. [@1]

The report also describes two separate December cannabis cases, one in Ranpur,
Botad and one in Sayla, Surendranagar, which are not covered here. [@1]
