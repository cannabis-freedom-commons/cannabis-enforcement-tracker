Moroccan police and customs foiled an attempt to smuggle 8,196 kilograms of cannabis
at the Mediterranean port of Tanger Med on Thursday 25 December 2025. [@1,2] A joint
security operation found the drugs hidden in a Morocco-registered international freight
truck that was about to depart by sea toward a European port. [@1,2]

The seizure was announced in a statement of the General Directorate of National Security. [@1]
The 50-year-old Moroccan driver was arrested and placed under judicial investigation to
identify local and international trafficking links. [@1,2]
