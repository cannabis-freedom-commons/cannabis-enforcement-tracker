A joint team of the Nepal Police and the Armed Police Force destroyed illegal
cannabis cultivation on more than 35 bighas of land in the Chure region of
Lalbandi Municipality, Sarlahi. [@1] The operation cleared cannabis planted on
15 bighas and 9 kaththas at Pratatol and Hattisudhe in Lalbandi Municipality-14.
[@1] Officers also destroyed large tracts of illegal cultivation at Dobhan Shir
and other nearby areas of the Chure hills. [@1] The operation continued until
late Sunday evening. [@1]

More than 180,000 cannabis plants were uprooted and destroyed. [@1] Deputy
Superintendent of Police Saroj Rai, the information officer at the District
Police Office in Sarlahi, said the action was part of an ongoing anti-narcotics
drive against illegal drug cultivation in the district. [@1] Rai said such
cultivation has been detected repeatedly in the Chure region. [@1]

Rai said security agencies had by then destroyed more than 70 bighas of illegal
cannabis cultivation in the Chure belt of Sarlahi, and that the operation would
continue. [@1] Police said further investigation was under way to identify those
involved in the cultivation. [@1]
