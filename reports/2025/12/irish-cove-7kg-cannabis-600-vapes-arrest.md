RCMP Northeast Traffic Services in Cape Breton arrested a man for Cannabis Act
offences after a traffic stop in Irish Cove. [@2] On December 27, an officer
stopped a Dodge Ram 1500 on Highway 4 after observing it travelling at 113 km/h
in a posted 80 km/h zone. [@2] Through the roadside investigation, the officer
learned the driver, a 20-year-old man of New Waterford, was transporting a large
quantity of illicit cannabis, and safely arrested him. [@2] CTV News reported
that the New Waterford man was arrested after the traffic stop led to several
cannabis-related charges. [@1]

Police seized the truck as part of the investigation. [@2] A search of the
vehicle found more than seven kilograms of dried cannabis and 600 cannabis
vapes. [@1] An RCMP handout photo shows dried cannabis in clear plastic bags
displayed alongside a box holding hundreds of cannabis vapes. [@2]

The man faces several charges under the Cannabis Act for possessing illicit
cannabis for the purpose of distribution. [@2] He was released pending an
appearance in Sydney Provincial Court on May 5. [@2] The investigation is
ongoing. [@2]
