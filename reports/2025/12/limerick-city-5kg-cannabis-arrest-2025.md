Gardaí and Revenue Customs officers seized approximately 5 kg of herbal cannabis in Limerick city on 22 December 2025 during an intelligence-led joint operation. [@1,2] The operation brought together the Garda National Drugs and Organised Crime Bureau, Limerick Divisional Drug and Detective Units and Revenue Customs Service. [@1,2] Revenue officers made the seizure, which Revenue valued at approximately €100,000. [@2]

Officers arrested one woman, aged in her 20s, who was detained at a Garda station in County Limerick under Section 2 of the Criminal Justice (Drug Trafficking) Act 1996. [@1,2] The next day Gardaí said the arrested woman had been charged and would appear before Limerick District Court on a date in late January 2026. [@3]
