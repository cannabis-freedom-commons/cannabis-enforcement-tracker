Dylan Minton, 18, lured the victim to meet him behind Queen Elizabeth II High
School in Peel on 4 September 2024, on the pretence that he would collect drugs
from her - but instead stabbed her in the abdomen, arm and back. [@1] He pleaded
guilty at Douglas Courthouse to charges including wounding with intent and being
concerned in the supply of cocaine, and was sentenced to six years and one month
in jail. [@1] Minton was jailed for three years and nine months for wounding
with intent and a consecutive two years and four months for the drug offences,
making six years and one month in total. [@2] Minton pleaded guilty to wounding
with intent, being concerned in the supply of cocaine, being concerned in the
supply of cannabis, possession with intent to supply of cannabis, possession of
cannabis and possession of criminal property. [@1] Deemster Graeme Cook said
while under current laws Minton, who was 17 at the time, would be considered an
adult on the island, but under new laws set to come into effect early next year
he would be considered a juvenile. [@1]

A subsequent investigation into Minton's phone, found messages which showed that
Minton was concerned in the supply of up to 25g of cocaine, and up to 1,500g of
cannabis between 22 June and 1 September that year. [@1] He had also been
arrested in the early hours of 31 May after smelling like cannabis, and a search
of him found £3,912 of cannabis, and £1,035 of cash. [@1]

Minton was arrested later that night, initially claiming someone made him do it,
but later telling police that he himself was stabbed. [@3] When interviewed, the
victim said she had arranged to meet Minton with a view to selling him cannabis.
[@2] Meanwhile, the female victim was taken to hospital after suffering nine
stab wounds, two of which were serious. [@2] Mr Kane said the prosecution did
not believe a third person had been present, but could not completely rule it
out and therefore accepted the basis of plea. [@2] Jailing him for six years and
one month, Deemster Graeme Cook said it was a sustained and frenzied attack
which had a potentially horrific outcome. [@1]
