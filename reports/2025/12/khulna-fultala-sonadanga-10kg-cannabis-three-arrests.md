Police arrested three people with 92 bottles of banned liquid syrup ESKuF and 10
kilograms of cannabis from Fultala upazila in Khulna on 1 December 2025. [@1]
Those arrested were Md. Robiul Islam alias Rubel, 40, Mohammad Naim alias Bhulu,
50, and Runa Begum, 45. [@1] District police announced the arrests in a press
release. [@1]

On a tip-off, a police team arrested Rubel with 92 bottles of the contraband
syrup ESKuF during a check post operation at Damodar area under Fultala upazila
around 5:30 am. [@1] Following Rubel's confessional statement, police conducted
a special drive at Basupara Orphanage intersection under Sonadanga Model Police
Station of the KMP. [@1] Officers arrested Bhulu and Runa there around 7.45 am
with 10 kilograms of cannabis. [@1]

During preliminary interrogation, the detainees admitted to being professional
drug dealers who collected narcotics from various sources and stored them for
sale. [@1] A case was filed under the Narcotics Control Act 2018 with Fultala
Police Station. [@1]
