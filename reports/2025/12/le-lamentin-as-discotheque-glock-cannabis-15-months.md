Fort-de-France's criminal court sentenced a 25-year-old man to 15 months in
prison on 1 December 2025 for illegally possessing a firearm and drugs. [@3] The
court also fined him 1,500 euros and banned him from possessing a firearm for
ten years. [@3] He returned to detention after the hearing and had ten days to
appeal. [@3] He was presented in immediate appearance on 20 October and remanded
in pre-trial detention until a trial set for 1 December. [@1,4]

Police and mobile gendarmes, acting on the public prosecutor's requisition, ran
the operation from 5am to 7:30am on Saturday 18 October 2025 outside the AS
Discothèque in Le Lamentin. [@2] They seized four weapons, including a pellet
pistol and a dummy pistol. [@2] Two of the weapons were found in vehicles,
including an automatic pistol. [@2] Two suspects were placed in police custody
after the checks. [@2] Small quantities of cannabis resin and herb were found
during the operation. [@2] One suspect was remanded in custody pending
investigation. [@2] The other suspect was due to be tried for weapons
possession. [@2]

In the vehicle where the defendant was, gendarmes found a loaded Glock with 9mm
cartridges hidden in a pizza box on the passenger seat and seized 110 g of
cannabis resin. [@3] He denied owning the weapon and drugs. [@3] He said his car
had been unlocked and a third party could have left them there. [@3] He had a
previous conviction in June 2020 for unauthorised possession of a category B
weapon. [@1] He was sentenced to one year in prison for that offence. [@3] His
lawyer argued that CCTV footage and fingerprint analysis did not directly link
him to the items. [@3] The court accepted the prosecution's position that
nothing showed the items were not his. [@3]
