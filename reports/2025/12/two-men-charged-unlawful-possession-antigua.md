Roshawn Elliott, 32, of Potters Village, was formally charged with unlawful
possession of cannabis after police found him with cannabis on 31 December in
Potters. [@1] Terrance “Funk” Roberts, 48, of Federation Road, Grays Farm, was
formally charged with unlawful possession of cannabis after police found him
with cannabis on 30 December 2025 along Fort Road. [@1]

The two cases arose from separate police patrols conducted in different
locations across the island. [@1] Elliott was found with 1.1 pounds of cannabis,
about 0.5 kg. [@1] Roberts was found with 267.8 grammes of cannabis. [@1]

The Royal Police Force of Antigua and Barbuda said it continues to conduct
targeted operations and patrols to deter criminal conduct and enforce the laws.
[@1] Police encouraged members of the public to cooperate and report suspicious
activity to the nearest police station. [@1]
