The OFAST Caraïbes anti-drug office opened an investigation in June 2024 after
receiving a tip about a structured trafficking operation. [@1] Investigators
suspect the network operated mainly between Île-de-France and Martinique in 2024
and 2025. [@1] Five suspects, four men and one woman aged 23 to 39, were charged
in Paris with importing and trafficking narcotics as an organised gang. [@1] The
group is suspected of importing more than one tonne of cannabis resin by sea to
the Caribbean, with an estimated value of nearly eight million euros. [@1] The
article says a kilo of cannabis sells for about 3,000 euros in the Paris region
compared with nearly 8,000 euros in the overseas territories. [@1]

The suspected head of the network is Axel N., 32, who allegedly ran several
companies based in Le Lamentin in Martinique. [@1] Axel N. was already in prison
at Bois-d’Arcy in Yvelines for similar offences and violence against prison
officers and allegedly kept directing operations from his cell. [@1] He was
allegedly assisted by William E., 33, who handled logistics and moving the
goods. [@1] In August 2024, 421 kg of cannabis was sent to the Antilles hidden
in pallets of glue and tiles. [@1] A commercial unit in Corbeil-Essonnes served
as the network’s rear base, while sites including the Garonor logistics zone in
Aulnay-sous-Bois were used to package the drug. [@1] The resin was hidden inside
refrigerators placed on pallets intended for export. [@1]

On 17 November 2025 ten pallets of household appliances were loaded into a
container bound for Martinique, the ship left Le Havre on 28 November and docked
at Fort-de-France on 7 December. [@1] On arrival, officers seized 650 kg of
cannabis resin and arrested several accomplices on the spot. [@1] Further
arrests followed in mainland France and the Antilles, including Fort-de-France,
Aulnay-sous-Bois, Villejuif, Morsang-sur-Orge, Indre and the Bois-d’Arcy prison.
[@1] Searches recovered 27,000 euros in cash, several Rolex-type luxury watches
and another 5 kg of cannabis resin. [@1] The five suspects were held in custody
in Versailles and then charged, with three men remanded in pre-trial detention
and the woman and one other suspect released under judicial supervision. [@1]
The investigation is continuing to identify possible local branches and
establish the exact scale of the traffic between mainland France and Martinique.
[@1]
