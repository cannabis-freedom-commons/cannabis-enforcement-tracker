Albanian police announced on 19 December 2025 that Megiljan Deda, 31, had been
arrested in Montenegro and extradited to Albania. [@1] Police said the arrest
came from information exchanged between specialists of Interpol Tirana and
Interpol Podgorica. [@1] The First Instance Court in Lezha had ordered him held
in prison in April 2025 on a charge of cultivating narcotic plants committed in
collaboration. [@1]

Lezha police had declared him wanted after the April 2025 Operation Vacuum, in
which three co-defendants were caught in greenhouses at Rrabosht and Gocaj. [@3]
That police notice was published on 3 September 2025, when Interpol Tirana was
still working with Montenegro to complete his extradition. [@3]

Montenegro was named as the country of arrest, and the 19 December police
statement reported the extradition as done through Interpol and published his
name. [@2] A second outlet reported the same police account on 19 December 2025,
including the three bags of about 34 kg of cannabis seized in Balldre in April.
[@2]
