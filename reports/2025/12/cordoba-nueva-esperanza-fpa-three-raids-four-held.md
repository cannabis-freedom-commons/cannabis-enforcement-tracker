Four people, two men and two women, were detained in barrio Nueva Esperanza in
the city of Córdoba. [@1] The raid was the culmination of three months of
investigation by operational groups of the Fuerza Policial Antinarcotráfico,
Córdoba province's anti-narcotics police force. [@1] Officers dismantled one
sales point and two storage properties. [@1] One of the detainees is a Peruvian
national, and the group was described as narcomenudistas, small-scale retail
drug dealers. [@1] ENREDACCIÓN described the group as a narcomenudista band
operating in the north-west of Córdoba city on the boundary with Villa Allende.
[@4]

The searches ordered by the Ministerio Público Fiscal, the provincial
prosecution service, were carried out on calle Bernardo Houssay, Manzana 25, in
barrio Nueva Esperanza, in front of a small football pitch. [@1] Canal C
reported that the football pitch in front of the homes served the investigated
group as a reference point. [@3] Narcotics detector dogs helped officers find
the drugs hidden in different parts of the homes. [@1] Officers seized 12 doses
of marijuana, alongside 1,139 doses of cocaine that are excluded from the
cannabis figures here. [@1] They also seized 948,700 pesos and 2,500 dollars, a
shotgun, 11 cartridges of calibre 22, two digital scales and other evidence.
[@1] One of the homes held a kiosco de pantalla, a shopfront used as a front for
selling drugs. [@1]

The Fiscalía de Lucha contra el Narcotráfico del primer Turno, the first-turn
anti-narcotics prosecutor, ordered the seized items sent on and the four
detainees transferred to judicial headquarters for an offence under National
Narcotics Law No. 23.737. [@1] ElDoce.tv reported that the four detainees were
placed at the disposal of that prosecutor's office and accused of breaking law
23.737. [@2]
