Police, forest officials and paramilitary forces destroyed about 8 lakh premature cannabis plants in coordinated operations at Kalamchoura and Bangshibari in Sepahijala district on 2 December 2025. [@1]

From 7 am to 2 pm, the officer in charge of Kalamchoura police station led station staff, TSR men and women personnel, CRPF and forest officials through the North Kalamchoura gram panchayat, Pasania and Takkamura Tila forest areas. [@1] That team uprooted about 5.5 lakh premature plants across 48 plots. [@1]

At the same time, from 7 am to 11 am, the officer in charge of Bishalgarh police station led a second team through Raghunandan Para and Jagatrambari under the Bangshibari council area. [@1] That team destroyed about 3 lakh premature plants across roughly 70 plots. [@1]

The account presents the two drives as part of ongoing joint measures against cannabis cultivation and trafficking in the state. [@1] It names no growers, and no arrests were reported. [@1]
