The Police Regional Office Cordillera (PRO-CAR) and the Philippine Drug
Enforcement Agency eradicated marijuana plantations worth an estimated P11.25
million across Kalinga and Benguet provinces on 24 December 2025. [@1]

In Kalinga, a joint team of the provincial mobile force, Tinglayan, Tanudan,
Balbalan and Rizal police, the provincial drug enforcement and intelligence
units and PDEA-Kalinga found five plantations with about 45,000 fully grown
marijuana plants worth P9 million in Barangays Loccong, Buscalan, Butbut Proper
and Tulgao East, all in Tinglayan municipality. [@1]

In Benguet, personnel of the Kibungan and Bakun police, the provincial mobile
force, provincial drug and intelligence units, regional intelligence units and
PDEA-CAR found seven plantations with an estimated 11,250 fully grown plants
worth P2.25 million in Barangay Tacadang, Kibungan and Barangay Kayapa,
Bakun. [@1]

All the plants were uprooted and burned on site after documentation. [@1] Police
said investigations continue to identify other plantation sites and the people
behind the cultivation. [@1]
