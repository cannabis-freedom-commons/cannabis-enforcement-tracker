Officers of the Metropolitan Police Bureau, joined by officials of the Department of Thai
Traditional and Alternative Medicine, raided a warehouse in Soi Bang Kradi, Samae Dam, in
Bangkok's Bang Khun Thian district on 16 December 2025 after residents complained about a strong
smell of cannabis. [@1,2] A search confirmed a large indoor cultivation facility, and senior
metropolitan police officers had obtained a search warrant from the Thon Buri District Court
before the raid. [@1]

Inside, officers found Nguyen Duc Anh, 28, Do Van Bay, 27, and Dang Sy Tai, 35, who admitted
cultivating cannabis on the premises. [@1,2] Police seized 1,685 plants, each estimated to yield
about 100 grams of dried flowers for about 168.5 kilograms in total, valued at about 1.69 million
baht. [@1,2] The suspects had registered a company to run a cannabis-related business but had not
obtained the licences to grow or trade the controlled herb, and investigators say the operation ran
entirely without official approval. [@1,2]

The three men were initially charged with jointly cultivating, processing or selling a controlled
herb for commercial purposes without permission, and were handed over with the seized plants to
investigators at Samae Dam police station. [@1] Police said further inquiries were under way to
determine whether the operation was linked to a broader network. [@1]
