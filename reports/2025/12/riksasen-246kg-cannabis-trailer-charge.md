A man from Lithuania has been charged with importing a quarter of a tonne of
cannabis into Norway. [@1] He is in his 50s and the amount is given as about 246
kilograms of cannabis. [@2] The import took place at Riksåsen in Kongsvinger,
the border crossing from Sweden into Norway, on 23 December 2025, lille julaften
(the day before Christmas Eve). [@2]

State prosecutor Camilla Ek Sørensen wrote in the indictment that the drugs were
hidden in a cavity in the floor of the truck and trailer he was driving. [@2]
The case is due to be heard in Romerike og Glåmdal district court from 2 to 4
December. [@2]
