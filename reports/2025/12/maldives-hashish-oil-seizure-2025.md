Customs officers inspecting sea freight on 25 December 2025 found more than 40 kg of hashish oil hidden in bottles labelled as chocolate syrup. Police estimated the street value at over MVR 30 million and arrested two men, aged 47 and 57, on 26 and 27 December; both were remanded for five days. Hashish oil is a cannabis concentrate and is treated as a Schedule 1 drug. [@1,2]

Customs officers scanned and tested the shipment on Thursday night and found the bottles packed in 10 boxes. [@2] The arrested 57-year-old is Mohamed Aadil from Gaafu Dhaalu Thinadhoo. [@2] Aadil was arrested on suspicion of smuggling drugs in a shipment imported under Raas Maldives aboard the vessel MSS Doora and attempting to distribute the drugs after clearance. [@2] Raas Maldives had emailed Customs Sea Cargo Examination Unit saying Ahmed Ali would represent the company to clear the shipment. [@2] Raas Maldives is registered under Aadil's name and he serves as managing director, with the company registered 16 years ago. [@2] The court kept Aadil in custody over his alleged involvement and concerns that release could risk public safety, destruction of evidence or influence on witnesses. [@2]
