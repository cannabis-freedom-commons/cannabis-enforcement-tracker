On 23 December 2025, Jatrapur Police and Tripura State Rifles personnel conducted an early-morning anti-narcotics operation in Sepahijala district. [@1,2]

The teams destroyed cannabis plants in 12 plots in the Southern Taichhama forest of Jagatrampara ADC village, then destroyed 10 more plots in the South Paharpur forest area near Kathalia. [@1,2] The two reports put the total at nearly or over 100,000 mature plants. [@1,2]
