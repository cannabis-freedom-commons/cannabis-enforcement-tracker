Colombian National Police officers and army troops seized nearly 1,300 kilograms
of marijuana hidden in a riverside cache in the Dos Islas sector of rural
Carurú, Vaupés. [@2] The operation was carried out on 19 December 2025. [@3] The
cache lay about five hours by river from the urban centre of Carurú. [@2]

Police valued the load at 2.86 billion pesos and described it as about one
tonne. [@1] The seizure was made by the police Criminal Investigation Section
and Police Intelligence Section together with army units, supported by
intelligence and verification work in the area. [@1] Preliminary investigations
said the marijuana was bound for Brazil as part of a transnational smuggling
route. [@2] The authorities attributed the route to residual organized armed
groups (GAO-r) under alias Iván Mordisco. [@2]

Police said the operation hit the groups' illegal income and kept large
quantities of drugs from reaching international markets. [@1] Colonel Felipe
Andrés Ardila Valderrama restated the National Police's commitment to fighting
drug trafficking and asked residents to keep passing information through
official channels. [@1]
