A man and a woman aged 32 to 44 were detained in flagrante delicto by the
Judicial Police in Achadinha de Baixo in Praia. [@1] The two were detained on
the afternoon of Friday 19 December during the execution of home
search-and-seizure warrants. [@1] Police described both as strongly suspected of
drug trafficking and of conversion and concealment of assets and valuables. [@1]
After a first judicial interrogation, the Praia district court ordered pretrial
detention for both, the most severe coercive measure. [@1] They were to await
further proceedings in São Martinho civil prison. [@1]

The operation seized 403.329 grams of cannabis. [@1] Officers also seized eleven
individual doses of cocaine ready for sale, plus plastic sachets used to package
drugs. [@1] Police also seized 130,403 escudos in cash, a knuckle-duster, mobile
phones, gold artefacts and other items of evidential interest. [@1]

The searches were carried out under warrants requested by the Public Prosecutor
and issued by the Praia court's criminal investigation judge. [@2] The Judicial
Police said the operation formed part of inland drug-trafficking enforcement.
[@2] Police said investigations directed at the detainees continue. [@3]
