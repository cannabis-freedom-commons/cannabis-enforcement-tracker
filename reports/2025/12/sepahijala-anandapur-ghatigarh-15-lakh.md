On 30 December 2025, Sepahijala district police with BSF and TSR battalions, forest officials and other agencies destroyed about 15 lakh matured cannabis plants across roughly 180 acres of forest at Anandapur and Ghatigarh. [@1]

The operation began at 7:35 am on Tuesday and ran under Sonamura police station, led by Inspector Tapas Das, with men from the 81st and 49th BSF battalions, the 5th, 9th, 7th and 11th TSR battalions, Sonamura forest teams and the 14th women TSR battalion. [@1] Officers destroyed 150 plots and put the destination market value of the crop at about Rs 75 crore. [@1]

Das briefed the media the same day and said such operations would continue the next day. [@1] No growers were named, and no arrests were reported. [@1]
