On 12 December 2025 at about 2:50 p.m., Indian Head RCMP officers on patrol on Highway 1 west of Indian Head came across Saskatchewan Highway Patrol officers who had stopped a tractor-trailer unit, and stopped to help. [@1] Officers searched the vehicle and found about 111 kilograms of illegal cannabis and about 874 grams of cocaine, which the RCMP seized. [@1,2] A man was arrested at the scene. [@1]

The RCMP identified the accused as 49-year-old Krenar Guci of Port Coquitlam, British Columbia. [@1] He was charged with possession of cocaine for the purpose of trafficking and possession of cannabis for the purpose of distributing, and was scheduled to appear in Indian Head Provincial Court on 17 March 2026. [@1]

The RCMP detachment commander said the joint work of the RCMP and the Highway Patrol kept more than 100 kilograms of illicit drugs from reaching their destinations. [@1] The cannabis figures in this report cover the cannabis only; the cocaine is noted here because it was seized in the same stop. [@1]
