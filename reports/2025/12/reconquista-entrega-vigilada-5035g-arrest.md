An adult came to a private-mail branch in Reconquista, Santa Fe, to collect the
flagged parcel, and gendarmes identified and detained him and placed him at the
disposal of the intervening magistrate. [@1] The Federal Prosecutor's Office and
Federal Court No. 2 of Formosa had ordered the drug seized and replaced with
inert material and the parcel sent on under controlled delivery to identify the
recipient. [@1]

Officers of the Puerto Velaz section of Squadron 15 Bajo Paraguay inspected a
parcel-transport load during checks on National Route 11 at kilometre 1,103 in
General Lucio V. Mansilla, Formosa. [@1] A scanner check of one box showed
irregular bodies like those used to pack drugs, and opening it revealed
rectangular bags holding 5 kilos 035 grams of cannabis sativa in bud form. [@1]

With the Avellaneda complex-crimes investigations unit, the force posted
officers at the private-mail branch in Reconquista to await the recipient. [@1]
Infoqom reported the joint Formosa–Santa Fe operation ended with the detention
in Reconquista of the man who came to collect the parcel carrying more than five
kilograms of marijuana already detected by the Gendarmerie. [@2] The outlet
described the detention as dismantling an interprovincial drug-distribution
manoeuvre under the Formosa magistrate's direction. [@2]
