Denson Emroy Bailey, 53, of Culloden Road, St Michael, appeared before Chief
Magistrate Ian Weekes in the District 'A' Criminal Court No. 1 on Friday. [@1]
He pleaded guilty to trafficking and importing cannabis. [@1] He was fined
$6,000 over the offences. [@1]

Officers of The Barbados Police Service Narcotics Unit arrested and charged him
over offences committed on December 11. [@1] The suspected cannabis weighed an
estimated 3.1 kilogrammes. [@1] Police put its street value at approximately
$24,800. [@1]
