Kazakhstan's interior ministry said police recorded almost 3,000 drug offences,
including 828 sales, in the first four months of 2025. [@2] Over the same period
officers seized about 3.5 tonnes of drugs, among them about 2 tonnes of
marijuana and 303 kg of hashish. [@2] The 3.5 tonnes also included 259 kg of
synthetic drugs, and almost 6 tonnes of precursors were taken out of
circulation. [@1] Police said they shut down 83 underground drug laboratories
and broke up 20 organised criminal groups. [@2]

Officers detained 1,783 people in the four months, of whom 669 were arrested and
held. [@1] More than 11,000 websites and 12,000 bank accounts and cards worth
more than 1 billion tenge were blocked, according to the head of the interior
ministry's drug crime committee. [@3]

In one April case in Kosshy, a man from Akmola Region was detained with 6.5 kg
of the synthetic drug a-PVP found in a search of his car, and his alleged
accomplices in North Kazakhstan Region were then detained with another kilogram.
[@2] In April police in Astana detained a resident of Aktobe holding 2.5 kg of
synthetic drugs; all the suspects in these cases were placed in temporary
detention centres. [@4]
