Two police operations in Carchi and Guayas seized more than 140 kg of drugs.
[@1] In the Tulcán action, police seized 82.4 kg of marijuana. [@1] In Posorja,
Guayas, police seized 59 kg of cocaine in the same roundup. [@1] The National
Police said six people were captured across both operations. [@1]

Officers also seized two vehicles, six mobile terminals, one port body camera
and one firearm during the actions. [@1] A second account confirmed that police
seized 82.4 kilograms of marijuana in Tulcán, Carchi, and 59 kilograms of
cocaine in Posorja, Guayas. [@2] It reported that six people were apprehended
over both interventions for alleged involvement in the offences. [@2] It also
reported that two vehicles, six mobile phones, a body camera used at the port
and a firearm were retained. [@2]
