Troops of the 26th Cacique Pigoanza Infantry Battalion and the 6th Light Cavalry Group of the Ninth Brigade, working with the anti-narcotics police, seized 1,084 kg of marijuana at a checkpoint on the I-24 highway at Puerto Seco in Gigante, Huila. [@1,2]

Officers stopped an NPR-type truck and found the marijuana camouflaged in boxes, hidden under another load of merchandise. [@1,2] They arrested two people, who face charges of trafficking, manufacture or carrying narcotics. [@1,2]

Investigators said the shipment had come from the department of Cauca and was worth more than 1.2 billion pesos in Colombia and about 6.9 billion pesos abroad. [@1,2] It was attributed to the Hernando Gonzalez Acosta Group of the Isaias Pardo Central Bloc, a structure the Army links to financing through drug trafficking. [@1,2]
