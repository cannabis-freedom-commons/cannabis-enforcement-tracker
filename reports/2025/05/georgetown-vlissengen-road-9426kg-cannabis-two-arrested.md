Two men travelling in a car along Vlissengen Road in Georgetown were arrested
after 9.4 kilogrammes of cannabis was found inside the vehicle. [@1] Officers of
the Customs Anti-Narcotic Unit (CANU), Guyana's anti-drug agency, intercepted
the motor car during an operation on Vlissengen Road, Georgetown, on Saturday 17
May 2025. [@2] CANU said its officers had acted on information received before
carrying out the operation. [@1]

A search of the vehicle found several parcels containing leaves, seeds and stems
suspected to be cannabis. [@1] The two men in the car were arrested and escorted
to CANU headquarters with the suspected narcotic. [@1] CANU said the material
tested positive for cannabis and weighed 9.426 kilograms. [@2] News Room
reported the weight as 9.4 kilogrammes. [@2]

CANU published a photograph of the parcels found in the operation. [@2]
Investigations were ongoing, and neither account named the men or reported
charges. [@2]
