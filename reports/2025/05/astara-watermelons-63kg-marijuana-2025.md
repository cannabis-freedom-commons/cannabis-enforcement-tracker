Officers of the Astara Customs Department of Azerbaijan's State Customs
Committee found a large quantity of drugs hidden in watermelons during a customs
operation. [@1] Customs officers inspected a vehicle carrying a load of “fresh
watermelons” from Iran to Russia in transit through Azerbaijan. [@1] A
stationary X-ray scanner flagged suspicious lines in the cargo during the
initial screening. [@3]

During a further detailed check, a trained sniffer dog reacted to 42 boxes. [@3]
The boxes held 84 watermelons containing foil-wrapped packages of dried
marijuana concealed inside the fruit. [@1] The State Customs Committee's
reported total was 63 kilograms 417 grams, including packaging, for the dried
marijuana. [@2] The concealed fruit load was described as an unusual attempt to
smuggle narcotics across the customs border. [@2] The packages were sealed
inside the melons and wrapped to avoid suspicion during the customs check. [@3]

An investigation into the incident was under way. [@1]
