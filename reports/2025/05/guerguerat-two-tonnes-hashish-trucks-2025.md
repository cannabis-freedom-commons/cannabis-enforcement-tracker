On Friday 2 May 2025, security and customs officers at the Karkarat (Guerguerat) border post south of Dakhla stopped two trucks with Moroccan plates and found two tonnes of hashish hidden in a load of construction materials. [@1] The drug was bound for a sub-Saharan African country, and trained dogs took part in the joint inspection that uncovered it. [@1]

## The inquiry

The two drivers, aged 40 and 47, were arrested and placed under preliminary investigation under the supervision of the public prosecutor. [@1] Investigators set out to identify other people involved and to trace the network's extensions within Morocco and at regional and international level. [@1] The seizure was presented as part of the continuous efforts of the services at the post against international drug trafficking and transnational organised crime. [@1]
