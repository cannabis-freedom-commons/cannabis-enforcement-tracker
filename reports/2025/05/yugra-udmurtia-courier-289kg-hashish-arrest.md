Drug-control officers of the Yugra interior ministry directorate, with
Nefteyugansk traffic police and regional Rosgvardia support, stopped the
unlawful activity of a 57-year-old resident of Udmurtia suspected of involvement
in illegal distribution of banned substances. [@1] Officers stopped his Kia
Carnival on the 13th kilometre of the road giving access to Surgut. [@1] At the
moment of detention he tried to flee and physically resisted the police
officers. [@2]

Officers found six wraps of dark plant material hidden under the boot lining of
the car. [@1] Testing identified the seized material as the narcotic hashish
with a total weight of 2 kilograms 891.28 grams. [@2] According to operatives,
acting on instructions from an unidentified curator he had met online, he had
brought the drug to Yugra for later contactless sale. [@1]

A criminal case was opened against the suspect for attempted large-scale drug
sale under part 3 of article 30 and point “g” of part 4 of article 228.1 of the
Russian Criminal Code. [@1] At the investigator’s request, the court placed him
in custody as a measure of restraint. [@1]
