Officers of Gendarmería Nacional Argentina's Squadron 13 "Iguazú" opened six
parcels sent through a courier company for delivery to Buenos Aires province.
[@1] The El Dorado federal court and the federal prosecutor's office issued the
order to open them. [@2] Radio Yguazú reported that the parcels were opened
under an order in a judicial investigation. [@3] Crónica, citing the security
ministry's statement, said the gendarmes had waited for a consignment to arrive
with instructions to search several packages. [@4]

Inside, in front of witnesses, the officers found 173 transparent plastic
containers that appeared to hold plant material. [@1] Radio Yguazú described
vacuum-packed buds of cannabis sativa in the containers. [@3] A Narcotest field
test was positive for marijuana, with a total weight of 97 kilos 486 grams
(97.486 kg). [@1]

The magistrate handling the case ordered the drug seized under Argentina's drug
law 23.737. [@1] Radio Yguazú reported that the El Dorado federal court and
prosecutor ordered the drug seized and the case file opened. [@3] Radio Yguazú
put the load's estimated value at 285,555,225 pesos, which it attributed to
official sources. [@3] The investigation into where the drug came from and who
sent it was still under way, Radio Yguazú reported. [@3]
