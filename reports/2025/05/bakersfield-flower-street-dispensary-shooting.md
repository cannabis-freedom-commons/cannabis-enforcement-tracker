Five people were charged in connection with a robbery at an east Bakersfield
marijuana dispensary that left two men dead. [@3] The shooting happened at about
9:40 p.m. on May 27, 2025, at a dispensary in the 1600 block of Flower Street.
[@5] The men killed were Damien Joseph Bullard, 26, and Anthony Victor Ochoa,
37. [@1] Five suspects were taken into custody in the case. [@5] Thompson was
arrested in Las Vegas and booked into a local jail there while awaiting
extradition to Kern County. [@7]

Turner, Brookins, Thompson and Young were each charged with two counts of
murder, while Ruiz was charged only with robbery as the alleged getaway driver.
[@3] Terrance Thompson is charged with two counts of murder, robbery and gang
participation. [@4] He was the third and final adult suspect in the case to
enter a not guilty plea. [@4] One of the suspects was armed with a black
AR-style rifle, according to sheriff's reports. [@3] One of the teens was
identified from surveillance video and arrested, and a large bag of marijuana
was seized. [@1] A ski mask and a backpack believed to have been worn by Young
during the robbery were also seized. [@1]

Ramahs Turner admitted to all counts and enhancements against him in juvenile
court during a hearing in the week before November 11, 2026. [@1] The longest he
can be held in custody is until his 25th birthday, because the court found he
did not meet the requirements for transfer to adult court. [@1] His disposition
hearing, in effect a sentencing hearing in juvenile court, is scheduled for
November 19. [@1]

Prosecutors waived the death penalty against Thompson and Young, who now face a
maximum of life in prison without the possibility of parole if convicted. [@6]
Prosecutors are seeking to move Brookins to adult court, where he would face
life in prison. [@2] The cases against the other four defendants remain pending.
[@1]
