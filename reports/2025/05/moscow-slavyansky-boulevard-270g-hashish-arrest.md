Drug-control officers of the Russian Interior Ministry, together with Moscow's
Western District police and Volgograd Region police, identified and stopped a
previously convicted 31-year-old Moscow resident engaged in trafficking drugs
and potent substances. [@1] The man lived in a flat on Slavyansky Boulevard in
Moscow, which officers inspected. [@1] Police said he had received the drugs
from his handlers to distribute through dead drops and to sell to small- and
large-scale wholesale buyers. [@1] The suspect was detained and remanded in
custody. [@1]

Officers seized 270 grams of hashish divided into four wraps in the flat. [@1] A
second account of the same police release gives the same seizure as 270 grams of
hashish in four wraps. [@2] They also seized more than 60 kilograms of
pregabalin, part stored in capsules and part in plastic containers. [@1]
Officers also seized empty medicinal casings, a capsule-filling machine,
electronic scales, a laptop and other items. [@1]

The operation followed operational intelligence developed by the drug-control
police with their Moscow and Volgograd colleagues. [@1] An investigator of the
Western District police opened criminal cases against him on attempted drug sale
and potent-substance trafficking charges. [@1] The investigation into his
activity was continuing. [@1]
