Police in Yakutia tracked four alleged drug dealers aged 22 to 33 before
mounting detention operations. [@1] When the suspects drove along Larionova
Street in Yakutsk, officers tried to stop their car. [@1] The suspects ignored
the officers' demands and drove off. [@1] During the resulting pursuit, two of
the fugitives jumped out of the car and fled. [@1] Officers detained the other
two during the pursuit with SOBR force support from the regional Rosgvardiya.
[@1] The republic's drug-control directorate and the FSB jointly detained the
group of alleged dealers aged 22 to 33. [@3]

A search of the two detained men found 227 packets of hashish weighing about 1
kg. [@1] Police also seized a traumatic pistol and a knuckleduster from the
pair. [@1] Police later published a wanted notice for the two men who had fled.
[@1] After seeing the notice, the two fugitives surrendered and handed over a
bundle of the same drug weighing about 400 grams. [@1] The two seizures together
amounted to about 1.4 kg of hashish with a non-lethal pistol and a
knuckleduster. [@3]

Investigators say the group worked for a shadow online shop, selling banned
substances in Yakutsk through dead-drop caches. [@1] They collected the sale
goods from hidden stashes whose locations were shared by an unidentified
handler. [@1] The four became suspects in a criminal case opened by the republic
interior ministry's investigators under the attempted large-scale sale
provision. [@2] A court remanded two of the men in custody and released the
other two on travel restrictions. [@3] The investigation into the group was
still continuing. [@1]
