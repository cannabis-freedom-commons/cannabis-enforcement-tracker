Tashkent police said citizens growing narcotic plants at home were detained in
the Mirabad district of the city. [@1] District criminal investigators searched
one house on received operational information and found bushes of a narcotic
plant there. [@1] The plants were cannabis that a mahalla resident was growing
inside his house. [@2]

The grower had arranged ventilation and heating for the indoor grow and kept the
plants in plastic pots. [@2] Officers seized 12 cannabis bushes 60 to 170 cm
tall from that house. [@2] Officers also found dried plant material in the same
house that had been dried and prepared for use. [@1] In the same district,
officers found that a neighbour was growing one more cannabis bush and tried to
get rid of it during the house check, but the attempt failed. [@2]

The seized plants were sent for examination and criminal cases were opened in
both cases. [@2] Police noted that cultivation of prohibited crops carries up to
10 years in prison, while drug manufacture, possession and other sale-related
acts carry up to 20 years. [@1]
