Surprised in the middle of a transport operation, the traffickers panicked,
scattered and abandoned their load before disappearing into the dark. [@1]
Despite an immediate search of the surroundings, the fugitives escaped the
security forces, so no arrests were reported. [@1]

The abandoned load was nearly 200 kilos of Indian hemp left behind by
traffickers in the middle of the night. [@1] The cannabis was packed in twelve
polyethylene sacks abandoned on the spot. [@2] The load was taken to the
Antsohihy territorial gendarmerie brigade, where it is kept sealed pending
incineration on the prosecutor's orders. [@2]

The interception happened around 10 p.m. on the night of Monday 26 to Tuesday 27
May, when a gendarmerie patrol found twelve sacks full of cannabis. [@1] The
scene was at Ambodimanary, a remote sector of the Sofia region. [@1] The
cannabis is grown in forest zones that are difficult to access and is then
carried manually to the main roads. [@1] From there the goods head toward large
towns such as Ambanja, Ambilobe and Antsiranana, where resale prices are
considerably higher. [@1]
