Two men were arrested at Katutura on Saturday after being found in possession of
cannabis valued at N$188 500. [@1] According to a cited crime report, police
apprehended the two suspects at around 11h50 at Tjikati Street in Katutura. [@1]
A third person who was with the two suspects at Tjikati Street managed to escape
during the police apprehension. [@1]

Police found the cannabis in the form of four parcels with a combined weight of
37.7 kilograms. [@1] The crime report estimated the value of the seized cannabis
at N$188 500. [@1] The arrests and the seizure were attributed to police action
reported in the cited crime report. [@1]

The two suspects were expected to appear before the Katutura Magistrate’s Court
on Monday, 19 May following the Saturday arrests. [@1] Police investigations
into the Katutura cannabis case were continuing at the time the account was
published. [@1] The arrests took place on the Saturday immediately before the
account dated Sunday 18 May 2025. [@1]
