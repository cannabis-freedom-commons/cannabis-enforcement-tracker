Police arrested a 70-year-old man after finding a cannabis cultivation inside a
warehouse on the Gateside Industrial Estate in Cowdenbeath. [@2] The man was
released pending further enquiries. [@3] A later report to the Cowdenbeath Area
Committee said three men had been reported to the procurator fiscal, Scotland's
prosecutor, over the cultivation. [@1]

Police valued the plants at an estimated street value of £3.2 million. [@2]

The Gateside seizure was described as the largest seizure in the Cowdenbeath
area's 2025/26 drug recoveries, which police put at more than £3.739 million
across heroin, cocaine and cannabis. [@1] Police said cannabis accounted for the
main bulk of recoveries over the 12 months. [@1] Police linked such activity to
serious and organised crime and human trafficking, saying vulnerable people can
be forced or coerced into tending cultivation sites. [@1] Police urged the
public to report suspected drug dealing to Police Scotland or Crimestoppers.
