Prison officers at the La Reforma prison complex in San Rafael de Alajuela
intercepted a cat as it tried to enter the compound. [@1] Officers noticed the
animal wandering round the perimeter with something stuck to its abdomen. [@1]
After catching it, the guards found it was carrying a small handmade bag with 80
grams of cannabis and 190 grams of heroin. [@1]

Costa Rica's Public Prosecutor's Office opened an investigation to find who
trained the cat and which smuggling network used it as a prison courier. [@1]
