Police in Risaralda arrested a 37-year-old man on the charge of trafficking,
manufacturing or carrying narcotics. [@1] Officers of the Risaralda Transit and
Transportation Section stopped a truck travelling from southern Colombia towards
Bogotá at a checkpoint on the national highway that links Valle del Cauca with
the city of Pereira. [@1] Caracol Radio reported that transit officers detained
the truck on the national road connecting Valle del Cauca department with
Pereira and found the drug when they inspected its load. [@2]

A search of the load found 55 packages of marijuana hidden inside several
electrical transformers. [@1] The marijuana weighed 100 kilograms and police
valued it at about 200 million pesos. [@1] Infobae described the marijuana as
cripi-type and said the transformers looked functional. [@3] The driver's
nervousness during the inspection raised officers' suspicions and led them to
search the cargo closely, Infobae reported. [@3] Officers found 55 packages
wrapped in adhesive tape inside the transformers, all containing marijuana. [@3]

The 37-year-old driver was captured at the scene and placed at the disposal of
the Fiscalía. [@3] Caracol Radio reported that the load had come from the south
of the country and was headed for Bogotá. [@2] Infobae called the concealment
method a new strategy by criminal groups seeking to evade controls on
trafficking corridors between south-west and central Colombia. [@3]
