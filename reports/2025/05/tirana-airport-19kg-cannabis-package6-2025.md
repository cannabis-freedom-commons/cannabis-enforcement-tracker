Tirana police arrested 39-year-old Dh. Sh., a resident of Kras village in
Tepelena, at Rinas in flagrante delicto during the anti-drug operation codenamed
Package 6. [@2] The man had arrived from Thailand carrying a suitcase with 19 kg
and 400 g of cannabis sativa with a high THC percentage, which officers seized
as material evidence. [@2] Police said he had brought the suitcase from Thailand
intending to sell the drugs in Albania. [@2]

The same detection was described by UNODC as 20 kilograms of cannabis sativa
concealed in a passenger's luggage arriving on an international flight at Tirana
International Airport in May 2025. [@1] The reported precise weight was 19.4 kg
of cannabis with a high THC percentage hidden in his suitcase. [@2] Police put
the value of the seized narcotics at 388 thousand euros. [@2]

The operation was carried out by Tirana narcotics investigators with the Rinas
police commissariat under the direction of the Tirana prosecution after
information from the national Narcotics Investigation Directorate. [@2] Airport
Control Units are dedicated frontline teams of customs, border police and other
law enforcement officers who target high-risk passengers and cargo. [@1] UNODC
said the Tirana unit operates within the EU-UNODC Joint Action on targeted
border controls at ports and airports in South-Eastern Europe. [@1]

Investigators said inquiries continue to identify others who may have been
involved. [@2] The procedural materials were sent to the Tirana prosecution for
further action. [@2]
