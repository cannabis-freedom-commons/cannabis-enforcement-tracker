Cuban police arrested a young man in Salvador Rosales, a community in Songo-La
Maya municipality, Santiago de Cuba province, as he counted seeds from an
illegal marijuana crop. [@1] The National Revolutionary Police, Cuba's national
police force, carried out the detention. [@2] Periódico Cubano reported the same
operation in Salvador Rosales, saying the young man was found attending a
plantation reported to exceed 2,000 plants. [@3]

The crop held more than 2,000 marijuana plants. [@1] The Interior
Ministry-linked Facebook page Héroes del Moncada reported the case. [@1] Local
residents alerted the authorities to the illegal crop. [@2] Besides the main
suspect, several other people accused of taking part in the harvest,
distribution and trafficking of the marijuana were also arrested and are being
processed in criminal proceedings. [@2] Periódico Cubano reported that the other
people implicated in harvesting and distributing the crop were arrested and are
under criminal prosecution. [@3] Residents of the community backed the
operation, and the authorities cited their help as a key part of it. [@3]

No names or information on where the seized crop went have been released. [@1]
The authorities said the operation is part of the government's Zero Tolerance
policy against drugs. [@1]
