A court in Nancy sentenced two men to three years in prison each and a customs
fine of 730,000 euros over a cannabis shipment stopped at the Gye toll plaza.
[@1] Customs officers had arrested the two men, a 22-year-old Belgian resident
and a 28-year-old man from Moselle, at Gye a few days earlier. [@1]

Officers found 200 kg of cannabis resin and 8 kg of cannabis herb in the cars.
[@1] The seizure was made at the Gye toll plaza in the early morning of 23 May,
and the officers said they were alerted by the behaviour of the younger man.
[@1] The Belgian, described as the man who opened the way, was seen leaving his
vehicle to meet the second driver, a man from Moselle aged 28 at the wheel of a
German saloon car. [@1] Customs officers found the drugs hidden in that
German-registered car. [@1]
