Police dismantled an illegal indoor cannabis cultivation site at a rented house in Atenas canton, Alajuela province, acting on community complaints about suspicious activity linked to cannabis use there. [@1] Officers confirmed the house was being used to grow marijuana indoors and called in specialists from the Drug Control Police (PCD) to process the evidence. [@1]

Authorities seized about 244 marijuana plants, 267 grams of processed cannabis and indoor cultivation tools, all secured as evidence. [@1] A man at the residence, identified by the surname Trejos, was taken into custody and transferred to the San Jose Public Ministry to await a judge's decision on possible pretrial detention. [@1]

The Ministry of Public Security said the case was the fourth cannabis grow house dismantled in Costa Rica in 2025. [@1]
