On Thursday night, Berhampore police intercepted two men travelling on the
Berhampore–Domkal state road at Nishadbag in Berhampore and took both men into
custody. [@2] Police identified the detained men as Swapan Barman, a resident of
Cooch Behar, and Rabiul Sheikh, a resident of Madhupur in the Nowda area. [@2]
Police said the two men would be produced before the court on the same day the
seizure was reported. [@2]

Officers searching the pair recovered a total of 24 kg of ganja from their
possession during the overnight operation. [@2] Berhampore police station police
carried out the Thursday-night action that yielded the 24 kg consignment and the
two detentions. [@1]

The interception point sits on the state road linking Berhampore with Domkal,
the route the two men were using when caught. [@2] Police said their preliminary
assessment was that the seized ganja had come from Siliguri and was being
transported onward to Nowda. [@2] That single Thursday-night interception
produced both the 24 kg recovery and the two detentions. [@1]
