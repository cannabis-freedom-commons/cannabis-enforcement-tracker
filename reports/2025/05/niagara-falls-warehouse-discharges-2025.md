Ontario Provincial Police's Joint Forces Cannabis Enforcement Team seized 15,000 cannabis plants and charged six people in a search-warrant raid on a warehouse on Don Murie Street in Niagara Falls on 17 April 2024. [@1,3] Police began the investigation after a strong smell of cannabis was noted outside what appeared to be an abandoned warehouse, and seized more than 2,500 clone plants plus more than 13,000 vegetative and mature budding plants with a potential street value of about $8 million. [@1,3] The six charged were released and ordered to appear in St. Catharines court. [@1]

## Workers discharged

In court in St. Catharines on 1 May 2025, three of the men, Arthit Seesai, Xueqi Gao and Jeffrey Lian, pleaded guilty to cultivating cannabis without authorization under federal law and were granted conditional discharges. [@2] Their lawyer argued they were only workers who tended the plants, not connected to ownership of the operation. [@2] The raid had involved the OPP's West Region Emergency Response Team, its chemical, biological, radioactive, nuclear and explosive team, the Provincial Asset Forfeiture Unit and Niagara Regional Police. [@1,2]
