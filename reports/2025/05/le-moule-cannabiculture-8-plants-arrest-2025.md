Gendarmes of the Détachement de surveillance et d'intervention (DSI) of Le
Moule, a gendarmerie surveillance and intervention unit, detained a suspect
during a call-out over threats made with a firearm. [@1] Searching his home for
the weapon, the officers found a cannabis plantation in full production. [@1]

France-Antilles gave the dried part as 460 grams in its text, while the caption
of its first photo stated 420 grams. [@1] After the plants were pulled up and
weighed, the gendarmerie put the total seizure at more than 3 kg of cannabis.
[@1]

Investigators were trying to establish the extent of the suspect's illicit
activity, including whether he was selling his production or growing for his own
use. [@1] RCI reported that the inquiry was continuing to determine whether the
suspect was involved in other offences. [@2] The gendarmerie said the case
showed its vigilance against drug trafficking in Guadeloupe and that
cultivating, holding and selling drugs are strictly banned and systematically
prosecuted. [@1]
