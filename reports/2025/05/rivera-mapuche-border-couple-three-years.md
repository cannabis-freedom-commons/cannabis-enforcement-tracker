A Rivera court sentenced a 25-year-old Brazilian man and a 27-year-old Uruguayan
woman to three years in prison for negotiating prohibited narcotics, the charge
known in Uruguay as suministro de sustancias estupefacientes. [@3] The two lived
in Santana do Livramento, the Brazilian city facing Rivera across the border.
[@1]

A Plateia says they supplied several bocas de venta, Uruguay's term for open-air
drug-selling points, on the Uruguayan side of the frontier. [@3] The
investigation was run by the northern region of the National Police's
investigations directorate as Operation Mapuche. [@1] Officers stopped the
couple once they had crossed into Uruguay, at Aparicio Saravia and Ignacio Núñez
streets, in the Recreo neighbourhood. [@3] The search found 997 grams of
marijuana, 255 grams of pasta base, a cocaine paste, and 154 grams of cocaine.
[@1] Officers also took 968 reais, a motorcycle and a mobile phone, and the
third-turn prosecutor ordered the couple held. [@1]

With the pair detained, the Uruguayan police passed the information to the
intelligence unit of the Military Police in Santana do Livramento, which
searched a storage site. [@1] There Brazilian police seized a further 1,816
grams of marijuana, 2,740 grams of cocaine and 1,247 grams of pasta base. [@1]
The same Brazilian find included a pistol magazine, 79 rounds of 9 mm and .45
ammunition, 41,480 reais and 1,332 pesos. [@1] Diario Norte, the Rivera daily,
reported the operation as a joint Uruguayan-Brazilian action and said the
investigations were continuing on both sides of the border. [@2]
