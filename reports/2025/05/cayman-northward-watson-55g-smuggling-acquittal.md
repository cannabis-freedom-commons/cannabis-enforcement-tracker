Senior prison officer Derron Watson was arrested on 28 February 2024 for
allegedly smuggling drugs into HMP Northward. [@1] The arrest was disclosed in a
joint press release from the RCIPS, the Cayman Islands Prison Service and the
Department of Public Safety Communications. [@1] On his arrival at the prison
compound, Watson was confronted and searched by senior officials. [@1] During
the search an unknown quantity of vegetable matter suspected to be ganja was
retrieved. [@1] He was then transported to the CI Prisoner Detention Centre,
where he remained in custody pending further police investigation. [@1]

Following the 28 February joint operation at HMP Northward, Watson was charged
with misconduct in public office and supplying drugs (ganja). [@2] Watson later
appeared in Grand Court and pleaded not guilty to one charge of misconduct in
public office. [@3] He was accused of smuggling the ganja into the prison for
one inmate. [@3]

In May 2025 Watson was acquitted of misconduct in public office over the
allegation that he smuggled 55 grams of ganja into HMP Northward. [@4] He denied
willing smuggling, claiming he had been coerced into carrying the drugs by
unknown armed gunmen. [@4] The jury returned a not-guilty majority verdict after
a trial lasting just over a week. [@4] The judge commented that Watson,
suspended on full pay since he was charged, may not be suitable for
reinstatement to his former job. [@4]
