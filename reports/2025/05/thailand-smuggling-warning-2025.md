As cases of travellers smuggling cannabis rose, the health minister, Somsak Thepsutin, said it was against Thai law to take cannabis out of the country without permission from the authorities, and that shops would soon be allowed to sell only to customers with a prescription. In March 375 kg had been seized in 22 suitcases at Samui airport and 13 foreigners arrested. [@1,2]

Thai officials said the Samui suspects were instructed to carry the suitcases from Samui to Singapore and then to the U.K., where they would be paid 2,000 pounds upon completing the job. [@2]
