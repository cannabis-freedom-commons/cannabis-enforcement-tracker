Officers stopped Chyna Jada Browne-Frater, a 22-year-old British woman, at Kotoka International Airport on 18 May 2025 as she prepared to board a British Airways flight to London. [@1,2] She had been placed on an immigration stop list after the Narcotics Control Commission (NACOC) received intelligence from foreign counterparts about a syndicate moving Thai-grown cannabis to Britain. [@2,3] Officers traced a suitcase bearing her name, offloaded it from the aircraft and opened it in her presence, finding 32 vacuum-sealed slabs. [@1,3] A field test was positive for THC, and the haul of about 17.92kg was valued at $232,960. [@3]

Browne-Frater told investigators her boyfriend, known only as Joey, had packed the case with local drinks and spices. [@1,3] She was charged with attempted exportation, unlawful possession and conspiracy, and an Accra court remanded her in custody as a flight risk pending laboratory analysis. [@1] A 41-year-old Ghanaian businessman, Joey Mclaren Hammond of East Legon, was then arrested as the alleged accomplice and remanded into NACOC custody, with reappearance set for 16 June 2025. [@3]
