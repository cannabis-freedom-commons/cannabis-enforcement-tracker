On 7 May 2025, NDLEA officers acting on intelligence intercepted a fuel truck
and three other vehicles loaded with 246 bags of skunk, a strain of cannabis,
weighing 3,047 kg on the Suleja-Kaduna road in Niger State. [@1]

Four suspects were arrested with the exhibits: Christopher Onyema, 47; Benedict
Etineruba Young, 54; Chukwudi Ujue Jerry, 30; and Mohammed Abdullahi Danasabe.
[@1] Apart from the fuel truck, three other vehicles were recovered from the
suspects: a Honda Odyssey bus, a Gulf bus, and another Honda Odyssey bus. [@1]
