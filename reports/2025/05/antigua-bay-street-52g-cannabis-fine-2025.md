Dorian Wilkins, a 27-year-old resident of Bay Street in Villa, was convicted and
fined for cannabis possession in St John's Magistrates' Court in late May 2025.
[@1] Acting Chief Magistrate Dexter Wason fined him $1,000 and ordered the drugs
confiscated and destroyed. [@1] The court gave him 30 days to pay and set 90
days in jail if he fails to do so. [@1]

On May 25, officers on mobile patrol on Bay Street in Villa stopped him and
searched a bag he was carrying. [@1] Police found a transparent plastic bag
holding 52 grams of cannabis, which they valued at $520. [@1] Wilkins was
arrested, taken to Police Headquarters and later charged. [@1]

He was also charged with possession with intent to transfer; he pleaded not
guilty and the prosecution withdrew that charge. [@1] The self-employed car and
house washer told the court he had the drugs for his personal use. [@1]
