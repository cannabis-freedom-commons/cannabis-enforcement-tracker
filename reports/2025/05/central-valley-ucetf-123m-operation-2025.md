California's Unified Cannabis Enforcement Task Force (UCETF) seized 105,700
illicit cannabis plants and 22,057 pounds (about 10 tonnes) of processed
cannabis worth USD 123.5 million in a week-long operation across Kern, Kings
and Tulare counties in May 2025, announced as the task force's largest
operation to date. [@1,2] During the week of 5 May, more than 200 officers and
staff from 15 state, local and federal partners served 71 search warrants
across about 4,600 square miles. [@1,2] Officers confiscated nine firearms,
detained numerous people and made several arrests, with charges to be filed
through local district attorneys. [@1,2] At eight locations they found banned
or restricted pesticides that threaten native species and the health of anyone
consuming the tainted cannabis. [@1]

Governor Gavin Newsom said the state would not tolerate growers who undermine
the legal industry and harm the environment. [@1] The operation adds to USD 534
million seized by the task force in 2024; since 2022 it has served 500 warrants
and removed more than 800,000 plants and 220 tons of processed cannabis. [@1]
