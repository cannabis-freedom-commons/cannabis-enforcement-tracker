Honduras's Public Ministry said its anti-drug directorate, the DLCN, reported
that authorities seized 1,652 pounds (about 749 kg) of marijuana in the previous
18 months. [@1] In the same period the DLCN said 221,453 marijuana plants were
destroyed. [@1] The DLCN put the market value of all the drugs seized in the
period at 1,092 million lempiras, or 42 million dollars. [@1] The figures
covered a mixed haul: 4,296 kilograms of cocaine were seized and 574,508 coca
bushes destroyed, neither of which is counted as cannabis here. [@1]

The DLCN, a specialised unit of the Public Ministry, reported the figures on
Tuesday 20 May 2025. [@1] The anti-drug directorate published no break-up of the
cannabis by department, operation or date. [@2]

Honduras remains a transit corridor for drugs moving from South America to the
United States, including through the Caribbean coast and La Mosquitia. [@1]
