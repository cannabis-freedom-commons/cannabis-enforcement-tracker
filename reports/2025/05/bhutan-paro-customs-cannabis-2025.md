The customs service also seized more than 7 kg of dried cannabis. [@1]

By May 26, 2025, the Royal Bhutan Police recorded 21 drug busts with 32 arrests,
up from 10 cases and 15 arrests in all of 2024. [@1]

The three heroin interceptions at Paro International Airport came in the same
wave of seizures reported for the first five months of 2025. [@1] Police said
the surge in brown sugar trafficking was part of a wider drug crisis, with most
of those arrested for drug offences between 2023 and 2024 aged under 24. [@1]

The National Council's Social and Cultural Affairs Committee listed marijuana
among the substances most commonly abused by young Bhutanese in a recent report.
