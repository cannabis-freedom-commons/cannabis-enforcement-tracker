On 26 May 2025, police from Canelones and Montevideo carried out three
simultaneous raids in the Conciliación neighbourhood of Montevideo under
Operation Valldemosa, detaining six men and dismantling a drug-trafficking group
that operated between both departments. [@2] Following a court hearing, three of
the detainees were convicted and sentenced to prison terms ranging from two
years and ten months to three years and eight months for supplying narcotics and
weapons offences. [@1]

An anti-drug unit investigation had identified a leader who directed the
distribution of illicit substances and used others to move them between
Montevideo and Canelones, leading the 1st Shift Prosecutor's Office of Las
Piedras to obtain search warrants for three properties in Montevideo's 19th
Police Precinct that served as the group's centre of operations. [@1]

In the first raid at a house on María Orticochea street, officers detained a
39-year-old man identified as P.S.M.O. and a 49-year-old man identified as
O.R.R., and seized doses of marijuana and cocaine base paste, more than half a
kilogram of a yellowish-white substance, an automatic pistol, a revolver,
ammunition, scales, police and security clothing, phones, and US dollars and
Uruguayan pesos. [@1] At a second house on the same street, police detained
three men aged 41 (N.B.R.), 37 (F.B.R.) and 67, discovered two marijuana
processing laboratories, and seized cannabis plants, growing equipment, packages
of high-quality marijuana, surveillance cameras, phones, cash and a pickup
truck. [@1] In the third raid, officers detained a 37-year-old man identified as
M.E.S. and confiscated a car, cash, cocaine, cocaine base paste and mobile
phones. [@1]

The court formalised the investigation, sentenced N.B.R. to three years and
eight months in prison and M.E.S. to two years and ten months for supplying
narcotics, sentenced P.S.M.O. to three years in prison for supplying narcotics
and illegal weapons trafficking and possession, ordered the release of F.B.R.
and O.R.R., and ordered the forfeiture of the seized goods. [@1]
