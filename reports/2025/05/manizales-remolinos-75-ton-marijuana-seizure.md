Units of the Transit and Transportation Directorate's Criminal Investigation
Section, with Caldas transit units, seized 7.5 tons of marijuana on the
Remolinos-Puente Indial road in the municipality of Manizales. [@1] Two people
were arrested during the operation for drug trafficking, manufacturing or
possession. [@1]

The marijuana was transported in a public-service tractor-trailer concealed
among boxes of refrigerated empanadas. [@1] Officers found 608 hidden packages
of marijuana inside the inspected vehicle. [@2] The two arrested men were aged
38 and 42, and the vehicle and the seized drug were placed under the custody of
the Manizales sectional prosecutor's office. [@2]

The truck was travelling the Yumbo-Barranquilla route, but investigators
established that the shipment's true destination was Medellin. [@1]
Investigators attributed the load to the Jaime Martinez Front of the Central
General Staff, a FARC dissident group under alias Mordisco. [@1] Police
estimated the shipment's value at 2.5 billion pesos and said the seizure
prevented about 12 million doses from reaching the illegal market. [@1]

The Transit and Transportation Directorate said it had seized a total of 40,242
kilos of marijuana on the country's roads so far in 2025. [@1]
