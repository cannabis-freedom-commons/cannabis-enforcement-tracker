After placing a Onex locker under round-the-clock surveillance, officers took an
Armenian citizen to the State Revenue Committee near the Yerevan
scientific-research institute of mathematical machines on suspicion of drug
smuggling, then detained him under criminal proceedings opened in the
Investigative Committee after a personal search. [@1] In cooperation with police
officers in Stepanavan, two more Armenian citizens were detained on suspicion of
acquiring and selling drugs and were also held under Investigative Committee
proceedings after personal searches. [@1] The detentions followed the State
Revenue Committee's discovery of 970 grams of marijuana in an international
courier parcel, as reported by the committee's press service. [@1]

Officers had examined the parcel, sent to Armenia from the United States, in the
customs-control zone of the Onex temporary-storage warehouse. [@1] Besides
personal belongings and clothing, the parcel contained marijuana weighing 970
grams, and its recipient was expected to collect it from a Onex locker. [@1] The
drug was intended for sale through Telegram. [@1] According to operational
information, it was to be picked up near the Vanadzor-Stepanavan tunnel. [@1]

Work was continuing to identify other people involved in the drug trafficking.
