Argentina's Airport Security Police (PSA) apprehended four adults and three
minors who were moving at high speed through the southern Buenos Aires province
with hundreds of doses of marijuana and portioning materials. [@1] The seven
detentions happened in two operations carried out to guarantee neighbours'
safety. [@1]

The first stop happened in Ezeiza near the Ezeiza-Cañuelas highway, when
officers spotted a car speeding along Provincial Route 205 and running several
red lights. [@1] Officers used audible and visual signals to stop the car,
identified the driver and companion, and, noticing a strong smell inside the
vehicle, carried out an exhaustive inspection. [@1] Inside a backpack and a
waist pouch officers found a precision scale and 82 wraps of plant material that
field-tested positive for marijuana weighing 106.6 grams, and they seized the
items together with the occupants' mobile phones. [@1] The two people detained
in that stop were placed at the disposal of Instruction and Trial Unit No. 4
under Dr. María Garrido, who ordered them charged with simple possession of
narcotics. [@1]

The second stop took place in La Unión, Ezeiza, when police saw a van with a
blurred licence plate travelling along La Plata street toward El Dorado carrying
several people. [@1] After stopping the van, officers checked the driver and
four other passengers and, in the presence of witnesses, searched the vehicle,
finding two jars of green herbs in a door and a revolver in the boot, later
confirmed as cannabis weighing 38.2 grams. [@1] Those detainees, including three
minors, were taken with the seized items to Ezeiza's 5th police station, with
Youth Criminal Responsibility Court No. 2 of Lomas de Zamora and Decentralised
Instruction Unit No. 2 of Ezeiza intervening. [@1]
