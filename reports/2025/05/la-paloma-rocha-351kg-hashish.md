A police pursuit in eastern Uruguay ended with the capture of four Brazilian
citizens, the seizure of several vehicles and the confiscation of 351 kilograms
of hashish. [@1]

The drug was found inside a pickup truck whose occupant tried to flee in the
seaside resort of La Paloma, Rocha department. [@1] Police mounted the operation
after receiving information from different sources about three vehicles carrying
Brazilians driving suspiciously through the resort of San Antonio, a few
kilometres east of La Paloma. [@1] Inside the pickup truck officers found 309
packages of hashish, a drug derived from cannabis. [@1]

A second vehicle was caught in La Paloma and a third at the checkpoint at the
entrance to the Chuy customs post, the town on the border between Uruguay and
Brazil. [@1] The news programme Telenoche put the value of the drug at around
600,000 dollars. [@1]
