Judicial police in Agadir arrested a 66-year-old man on the evening of 2 May 2025 at an
agricultural estate in the Oulad Kroum area on the outskirts of Oulad Teima. [@1,2] Officers
searching the farm found 105 packages of hashish weighing 4,328 kilograms, loaded onto a utility
vehicle. [@1,2] They also seized a small car and a rubber boat; police believe the boat was meant to
move the drugs by sea toward international smuggling routes. [@1,2]

The operation targeted what police describe as an international drug trafficking network and was
carried out with the country's intelligence services. [@1,2] The suspect was placed in custody
pending further investigation under the supervision of the public prosecutor, as officers work to
identify others involved. [@1,2]
