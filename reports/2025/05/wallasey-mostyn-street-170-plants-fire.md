Merseyside Fire and Rescue was called to a house on Mostyn Street in Wallasey on
1 May 2025 after reports of a fire. [@1] After the fire was put out and the
house was deemed safe, officers found a large cannabis farm hidden inside the
property. [@1] An investigation was under way to locate the people involved in
growing the cannabis, with police appealing for information. [@1]

Officers recovered 170 plants, while many more plants were believed destroyed in
the fire. [@1] Police believed up to four growing rooms were destroyed in the
fire. [@1]

Matt Brown, who leads Merseyside Police's Cannabis Dismantling Team, said
cannabis farms endanger nearby residents, especially when criminals tamper with
wiring and overload plugs, creating risks of fire and flooding. [@1] He said
organised crime damages communities through intimidation and violence, with
those involved thinking of no one but themselves. [@1] Police said telltale
signs of cannabis being grown inside a property include strange smells, sealed
windows and birds gathering on the roof in cold weather. [@1]
