Former Aberdeen Mayor Maurice Howard, 41, was sentenced to eight years in prison
for violating the terms of his release on two prior felony convictions. [@1]
Howard, 41, had been elected twice and served five years as mayor of Aberdeen, a
city in Monroe County. [@1]

His prior convictions were for embezzlement in 2021 and for possession of
marijuana in late 2024. [@1] In April 2025, Starkville police charged Howard
with conspiracy to commit a felony in connection with the sale of illegal
marijuana products at a Starkville smoke shop. [@1] Maurice Howard was charged
with conspiracy to commit a crime. [@2] A Starkville police spokesperson said
the sale of narcotics prompted the arrest warrant. [@2]

Starkville police said the charge was related to his business, The Gas Station
on Highway 12. [@2] Howard had previously been arrested in a similar
investigation in Aberdeen in December 2023. [@2]
