Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency teams and police uprooted and burned
marijuana plants in a plantation in Barangay Loccong, Tinglayan, Kalinga, on 27
June 2025. [@1] The operation was led by PDEA Cagayan Valley, with PDEA-Kalinga,
Kalinga police anti-drug enforcers, mobile groups and Tinglayan police as
support units. [@1] The joint operation ran from 4:30 a.m. to 11:25 p.m. on 27
June. [@2]

The teams uprooted about 34,500 fully grown marijuana plants and then burned the
2.3-hectare plantation. [@1] PDEA put the destroyed plants at about 34,500
growing in some 2,300 square metres of land, with an estimated value of P6.9
million. [@3] PDEA Region 2 said the destroyed plants were worth an estimated
P6.9 million. [@2]

No cultivator or caretaker was arrested at the site. [@1] Investigators were
still trying to establish who owned the plants. [@1]

PDEA said the success of anti-drug operations depended on community cooperation
as well as the work of government agencies. [@3] Saksi Ngayon reported that the
high-impact operation ran on 27 June and destroyed about 34,500 plants on 2,300
square metres. [@4]
