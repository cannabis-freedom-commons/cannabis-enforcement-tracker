NDLEA Edo State Commander Mitchell Ofoyeju, presenting the command's first
monthly operational scorecard in Benin City on 9 June 2025, said
intelligence-led operations in May led to 39 arrests, comprising 18 males and
21 females. [@1,2,3]

Two cannabis farms measuring a combined 0.782961 hectares were destroyed in
the Uzebba and Ohosu forests across Owan West and Ovia South West, with the
Uzebba farm covering 0.265874 hectares and the Ohosu farm 0.517087
hectares. [@1,2]

The command seized 1,303.8858 kg of illicit drugs in total, of which
1,133.337 kg was cannabis sativa, alongside psychotropic substances,
methamphetamine, cocaine, codeine cough syrup and pentazocine. [@2] The
highlight was the interception of seventy-four and a half bags of cannabis
sativa weighing 1,115 kg at Oke community in Ohunwude Local Government Area,
for which 33-year-old Isaac Israel and 19-year-old Precious Zion were
arrested. [@1,2,3] In a separate raid at Oluku in Benin City, 18-year-old
Nigerien Mohammed Isah was arrested with four grams of methamphetamine, 54
grams of cannabis, six grams of tramadol and 28 grams of Exol-5. [@3]
