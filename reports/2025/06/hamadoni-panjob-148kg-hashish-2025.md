Four of the group were Tajik citizens and one was an Afghan citizen. [@3] The
first suspect, Bobojon Abdulhak, born in 1991 and living in Dushanbe, was held
with 139 parcels of drugs in his car. [@1] Securing service officers found
another 41 parcels of drugs hidden in the same district. [@1] Investigators said
he had bought the drugs from Afghan smugglers for 20,000 US dollars to send them
on to Dushanbe and Sughd region and out of the country. [@1] The other four
detainees were named as Afghan citizen Akbari Hijrat Akbar, 1994, from Kabul and
living in Vahdat, and Tajik citizens Kosimzoda Safarali, Ibrohimov Rahmonali and
Nazirov Dilovar. [@2] Asia-Plus reported that the accomplices were arrested in
Dushanbe and Khujand. [@3]

The forensic department of the Drug Control Agency under the president put the
total weight of the seizure at 178 kg 845 g. [@2] Of that total, 148 kg 129 g
was hashish, the cannabis product recorded here. [@2] The remaining 30 kg 333 g
was opium, a separate substance not counted in the cannabis figure. [@2] The
security service called the crime the work of an organised transnational group
of drug couriers made up of Tajik and Afghan citizens. [@2] RFE/RL's Azattyk
Asia reported the operation was announced after the seizure and that the vehicle
belonged to a taxi service. [@4]

Asia-Plus said the exact date of the detentions remained unknown and no comment
came from the detainees or their relatives. [@3] Criminal cases were opened and
investigative measures were continuing. [@1]
