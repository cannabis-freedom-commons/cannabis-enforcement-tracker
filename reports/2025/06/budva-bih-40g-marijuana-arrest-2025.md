Budva police arrested M.C., a 29-year-old citizen of Bosnia and Herzegovina, and
found cocaine and marijuana. [@1] M.C. had been running away from police
officers before officers caught up with him. [@1] He was arrested by order of
the Higher State Prosecutor's Office in Podgorica on suspicion of unauthorized
production, possession and distribution of narcotic drugs. [@1] After being
caught, he was handed over for further processing to officers of the Budva
Security Department, where he was identified as M.C., aged 29, a citizen of
Bosnia and Herzegovina. [@1]

Officers found on him two PVC bags containing a white powdery substance
resembling cocaine, weighing about five grams. [@1] Officers also found on him a
PVC bag containing a plant substance resembling marijuana, weighing about 40
grams. [@1] Police also found money in the amount of 130 euros and a mobile
phone on him. [@1] A search of the apartment used by M.C. found scales for
precise measurement, two so-called marijuana grinders with traces of narcotics
and two hand sprays with an irritating effect. [@1] Officers also found in the
apartment several PVC bags intended for further packaging of narcotics and other
items. [@1] The Police Directorate announced the arrest and the seizures. [@1]
