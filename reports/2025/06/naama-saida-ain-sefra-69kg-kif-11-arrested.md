Algeria's Central Service for Combating Organized Crime dismantled a
drug-trafficking criminal network during the previous week. [@1] Officers
arrested 11 network members, including two women. [@1] All of those arrested
were brought before the public prosecutor at the specialized penal pole at the
Sidi M'hamed court. [@1] The acting unit was the Saoula-based Central Service
for Combating Organized Crime, reporting through the General Directorate of
National Security. [@2]

The operation seized 69.45 kg of processed kif (kif traité) originating in
Morocco. [@2] The network brought Moroccan kif into Algerian territory. [@1] It
then moved the drugs from the western wilayas toward the central wilayas of the
country. [@1] Officers intercepted a 4x4 on the road linking Saïda and Aïn
Sefra, a commune in Naâma Province. [@1] The two people aboard were arrested and
the drugs hidden in the vehicle were seized. [@2] Investigators then arrested
nine more suspects and seized four vehicles along with 404 million centimes.
[@1]

The operation followed intensive investigations in which SCLO investigators
identified the trafficking network. [@1] Investigators said the network
specialized in transporting and illicitly trafficking drugs arriving from
Morocco from the western wilayas to the central ones. [@2]
