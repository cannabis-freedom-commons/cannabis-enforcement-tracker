Customs officers at Mumbai's Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport
arrested two passengers and the man waiting outside to receive their
consignment. [@1] The three individuals were booked under the Narcotic Drugs and
Psychotropic Substances Act, 1985. [@1] Investigators said the case pointed to
an international drug trafficking syndicate and that they were tracing the
network. [@1]

Officers acting on specific intelligence stopped two passengers who had arrived
from Bangkok on Wednesday 18 June 2025. [@1] A search of their luggage found
high-grade cannabis grown hydroponically, without soil, packed in vacuum-sealed
packages. [@1] Officers seized 24.96 kilograms of the cannabis. [@1] Customs
estimated its illicit market value at Rs 24.66 crore, about Rs 25 crore. [@1]
That consignment of a total of 24.96 kg was worth Rs 24.66 crore in the illicit
market. [@2]

Interrogation of the two passengers led officers to the third man waiting
outside the airport to collect the consignment, and he too was apprehended. [@3]

It was the third case that month of passengers smuggling cannabis from Thailand
into Mumbai. [@1] Earlier that month customs at the same airport arrested three
Indian nationals in two separate cases with more than eight kilograms, 8.6 kg,
of cannabis brought from Thailand. [@1]
