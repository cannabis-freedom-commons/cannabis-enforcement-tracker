Police in Marikina City arrested a 30-year-old man in a buy-bust operation in
Barangay Malanday on the evening of Tuesday 3 June 2025. [@1] The National
Capital Region Police Office said the man was identified only by the alias
“Ace”. [@1] Daily Tribune reported that Ace was on the street-level individual
watchlist and was arrested after a transaction with a police poseur-buyer. [@2]

Operatives of the Marikina City Police Station’s Station Drug Enforcement Unit
carried out the operation in coordination with the Philippine Drug Enforcement
Agency. [@2] Officers seized seven sachets and 10 medium-sized vacuum-sealed
packs of suspected kush, a high-grade cannabis flower, according to police. [@1]
They also took 10 cartridges and 12 vacuum-sealed packs of suspected marijuana
oil, along with one white box and P1,000 in marked buy-bust money. [@2]

Police said the kush weighed about 300 grams and was valued at P450,000, while
the 22 millilitres of marijuana oil were valued at P1,320. [@1] The two seizures
together were valued at P451,320. [@1]

The suspect was held at the Marikina City Police Station custodial facility
pending charges under Republic Act 9165, the Dangerous Drugs Act. [@1] The
seized items were turned over to the Eastern Police District Forensic Unit for
laboratory examination. [@1]
