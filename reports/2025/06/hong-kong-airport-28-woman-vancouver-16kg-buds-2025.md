Hong Kong police arrested a 28-year-old finance worker at Hong Kong
International Airport on Saturday 28 June 2025 after she arrived on a flight
from Vancouver, Canada. [@1] Officers from the narcotics bureau stopped her in
the airport arrivals hall in the afternoon and examined her two carry-on
suitcases. [@2]

The suitcases held 16 kg of suspected cannabis buds along with about 15 kg of
suspected "Ice", the street name for methamphetamine, packed in eight bags. [@1]
Police put the total street value of the 31 kg of drugs at about HK$12 million.
[@2]

She was arrested on suspicion of trafficking in a dangerous drug and detained
for investigation. [@2] Police said she was to be charged and brought to West
Kowloon Magistrates' Courts on 30 June 2025. [@2] Officers said she spent five
days in Vancouver before carrying the drugs back, having taken HK$20,000 to work
as a drug courier after being recruited through an online dating app, and that
they believed she was in debt. [@5]

Inspector Ng Ka-wai of the narcotics bureau said trafficking in a dangerous drug
carries a maximum penalty of a HK$5 million fine and life imprisonment on
conviction. [@3]

The 15 kg of suspected Ice and 16 kg of suspected cannabis buds were split into
eight bags hidden in two suitcases. [@4]
