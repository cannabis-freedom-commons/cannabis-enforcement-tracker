Tajik national-security officers and Border Troops personnel stopped a
drug-trafficking case in the service zone of the 9th Panjob border outpost in
Panjob jamoat, Mir Said Ali Hamadoni district, Khatlon Region. [@2] They
detained Tajik citizen Zarifov Sharofuddin Karimovich, born in 1969 and living
in Panjob village, as a member of a transnational drug group. [@2] Tajik
law-enforcement officers also reported the detention as a major attempt to
smuggle more than 90 kilograms of banned substances from Afghanistan, carried
out with armed accomplices. [@1]

A search of his Opel car with registration plate 2794 VS 03 TJ found four blue
packets holding drugs in 90 packs and one plastic container in the boot. [@2]
Zarifov told investigators he stayed in contact through an Afghan mobile-network
SIM card with Afghan citizen Khol, aged 45 to 50, a member of the transnational
group. [@2] Under their arrangement, Khol and seven armed group members carried
the drugs across the state border into Tajikistan. [@2] In the Panjob border
zone, Khol met Zarifov and handed him the drugs, then returned to Afghanistan
with his accomplices. [@2] Zarifov planned to hide the drugs in a secret place,
but security officers and border guards detained him. [@2]

Forensic examination put the haul at 79.722 kg of hashish, 9.860 kg of opium and
975 g of methamphetamine, with a total weight of 90.557 kg. [@2] Investigators
opened a criminal case under several articles of the Criminal Code, and a
preliminary investigation was under way. [@2] A second account said a criminal
case had been opened against the suspect and an investigation was under way.
[@1]
