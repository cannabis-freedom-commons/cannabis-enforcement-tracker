Fletcher Nelson, 24, of Union Mills, was sentenced to two years and one month at
Douglas Courthouse for money laundering and possession of cannabis with intent
to supply. [@1] During the arrest for money laundering on 24 March, officers
found a blue holdall containing 3lb (1.4 kg) of cannabis in packages at Nelson's
home in Snugborough Avenue, Union Mills, Braddan. [@1] The cannabis had a street
value of £27,000. [@1]

Nelson helped move more than £36,000 of criminal cash off the Isle of Man over a
16-month period. [@1] His bank account statements showed 25 credits totalling
£36,822 between April 2022 and August 2023, despite him having no income and
receiving no benefits. [@1] £36,210 was transferred out to 23 different accounts
using different reference terms, including car, puppy, holiday and tickets. [@1]
The arrest was part of a police operation targeting organised crime groups. [@1]

Although Nelson told officers the drugs were for personal use, messages on his
mobile phone showed evidence of dealing. [@1] Nelson pleaded guilty to two
counts of money laundering and possession of cannabis with intent to supply.
[@1] Nelson's defence advocate said he had a significant drug debt of over
£2,000, and that the difference between the money going in and out of his
account meant the actual benefit was clearly very small. [@1] Deemster Graeme
Cook said Nelson was trying to make money out of drug activity. [@1]
