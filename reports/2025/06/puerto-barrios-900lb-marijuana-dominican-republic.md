Guatemala's National Civil Police said on 24 June 2025 that investigators had
seized about 900 pounds of marijuana intended for shipment to the Dominican
Republic. [@1] The seizure took place at the commercial port of Santo Tomás de
Castilla in Puerto Barrios, Izabal, on Guatemala's Atlantic coast. [@4] Officers
found the drug in 30 cardboard boxes at the port facility. [@1] Each box held
about 30 pounds of marijuana. [@1] The load was detected during a scanner
inspection, after which investigators coordinated with the anti-narcotics
prosecution office to verify the contents. [@1]

The boxes were declared as electronic spare parts, according to the shipping
policy. [@1] Police valued the seizure at about 3 million quetzals, almost
390,000 US dollars. [@2] Anti-narcotics analysis officers detected the drug,
packed in blocks, during a routine review. [@2]

The anti-narcotics prosecution office was notified and is coordinating further
steps to expand the investigation and establish responsibility. [@3] The
proceedings were still under way when the accounts were published, with no
arrests reported. [@2]
