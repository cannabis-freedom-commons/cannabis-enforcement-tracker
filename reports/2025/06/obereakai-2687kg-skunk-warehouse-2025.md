NDLEA operatives raided a warehouse in Obereakai, Odukpani LGA, Cross River
State on Thursday 12 June 2025. [@1] They arrested 47-year-old Friday Achibong
Joseph at the warehouse during the raid. [@1] They also arrested 21-year-old
Abraham Anthony Willy and 24-year-old Utibe David Okon at the same warehouse.
[@1]

Officers recovered a total of 2,687kg of skunk from the warehouse. [@1] Skunk is
described as a strain of cannabis. [@1] The Obereakai warehouse recovery
amounted to 2,687kg of skunk, described as a potent strain of cannabis. [@2]

The 2,687kg figure is the NDLEA's figure for the warehouse recovery. [@1] The
raid combined arrests and a seizure at the same Obereakai site, with three
people detained and the cannabis haul taken there. [@1] The warehouse was in
Obereakai, Odukpani LGA, Cross River State. [@1] In Cross River, NDLEA officers
raided a warehouse on Thursday in Obereakai, Odukpani LGA. [@2] At least three
suspects were arrested when operatives raided the Obereakai warehouse. [@1]
