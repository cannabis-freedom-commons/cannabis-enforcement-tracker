Police in Parsa arrested 36-year-old Buddhi Bahadur Syangtan with 319 kilograms
of hashish being smuggled by truck from Kathmandu to Birgunj. [@1] Syangtan is a
resident of Thaha Municipality-12 in Makawanpur district. [@1] A joint team from
the Simara-based police unit and the District Police Office, Parsa, tracked the
suspicious truck on a special tip-off and found it parked at Parwanipur Chowk in
Birgunj on Wednesday evening. [@1] The Parsa District Police Office presented
the arrestee at a press conference on Thursday. [@2]

Officers found 13 tightly packed bundles of hashish concealed inside white
plastic sacks in the truck's rear cargo section, weighing 319 kilograms in
total. [@1] Police intercepted truck Province 2–03–001 Kha 6348 en route to the
Sirsiya Dry Port in Birgunj. [@2] Officers also seized Rs 192,000 in cash and a
mobile phone from the suspect. [@2] The driver admitted transporting the hashish
from Dhading and intending to smuggle it into India through the Dry Port customs
checkpoint in Birgunj. [@2] Police estimated the market value of the 319
kilograms at approximately Rs 10 million. [@2]

The Parsa District Court granted a five-day remand for further investigation
after Syangtan was presented before it. [@2] Parsa police chief Gautam Mishra
said the seizure may be the largest hashish seizure recorded by the Parsa
District Police to date. [@2] Police said legal proceedings are under way under
narcotics control laws. [@1]
