Niger police arrested six young Nigeriens aged 20 to 30 in Arlit after trapping
them with a simulated drug purchase. [@2] The operation was led by the DCLTIS,
Niger's central office against illicit drug trafficking, in the dunes around
Arlit. [@1] Officers staged a transaction to unmask a network described as
operating with disconcerting assurance. [@1] Officers also confiscated luxury
vehicles linked to the trade, described as gleaming SUVs, and the young suspects
were placed in custody. [@1] The six suspects were still awaiting judgment when
the case was reported. [@1]

Officers seized 122 bricks of cannabis that had been carefully concealed. [@2]
The haul weighed 113 kilograms, which police valued at more than 20 million CFA
francs. [@1] Arlit lies about 200 kilometres from Agadez and is known for its
uranium mines and regional transit role. [@1]

Police said the network aimed to flood illicit markets, potentially beyond
Niger's borders. [@1] The Agadez region is a long-standing transit corridor
between sub-Saharan Africa and the Maghreb for illegal goods including drugs.
[@1] The account notes that a kilogram of cannabis can resell for up to 300,000
CFA francs on some markets. [@1]
