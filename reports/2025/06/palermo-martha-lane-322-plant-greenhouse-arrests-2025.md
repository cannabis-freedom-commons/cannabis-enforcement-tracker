Butte County Sheriff's Office detectives and patrol deputies executed a search
warrant at a residence in the 7000 block of Martha Lane in Palermo on 18 June
2025. [@1] The search followed an aerial surveillance flyover by a sheriff's
detective who documented a large greenhouse containing live, growing marijuana
plants. [@1]

Detectives found approximately 322 live marijuana plants in a large greenhouse
at the back of the property. [@1] Three people were arrested on the property:
Wilfred St. Louis, 54, Marlon Shortridge, 44, and William St. Louis, 18. [@1]
Wilfred St. Louis was arrested on a felony charge of maintaining a place for the
purpose of selling a controlled substance and of being a prohibited person in
possession of ammunition. [@1] William St. Louis was arrested for being a
prohibited person in possession of a firearm and ammunition. [@1] Marlon
Shortridge was arrested for felony child endangerment. [@1]

Detectives found a 7-year-old child living in deplorable conditions in a trailer
next to the grow site. [@1] The child was placed in the custody of the Butte
County Children's Services Division. [@1] The sheriff's office said the
investigation was ongoing and asked anyone with information to contact its
Felony Investigations Unit. [@1]
