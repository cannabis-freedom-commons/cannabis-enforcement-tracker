Nassau police arrested three people after two searches for suspected marijuana
in the capital, a 45-year-old man and two women aged 50 and 23. [@1]

Officers attached to the Drug Enforcement Unit executed a search warrant around
11:00 a.m. Tuesday at a residence on Miami Street. [@1] With assistance from the
K-9 Unit, police found suspected marijuana inside a vehicle parked at the
property and arrested the man. [@1] A second search at a residence on Lumber
Road, believed to be connected to the suspect, uncovered a larger amount of
suspected marijuana hidden in a bedroom. [@1]

Two women, aged 50 and 23, were taken into custody at the Lumber Road address.
[@1] Officers seized more than $6,000 in cash there, which police suspected was
proceeds from drug sales. [@1] In total police recovered about 1.4 pounds (0.635
kg) of marijuana and estimated its street value at $2,600. [@1] Investigations
were continuing, and the report names no charges or court dates. [@1]
