Louis Henri Thomas was jailed for seven years at the Court of General Gaol
Delivery in Douglas for cocaine dealing and for trying to import cannabis. [@2]
Thomas had admitted all the offences and was sentenced to a further 18 months in
custody, consecutive to the cocaine terms, for attempting to import cannabis.
[@2]

Isle of Man Today reported that the parcel held 898 g of cannabis with a street
value of £17,966. [@2] Thomas absconded after being bailed on the cocaine
charges and was arrested when he came to collect a dummy package at the Post
Office sorting office on 31 January 2025, the court heard. [@2] The BBC reported
that Thomas tried to import £17,966 of cannabis through the post and that the
parcel had been intercepted at the Braddan sorting office. [@1]

Thomas had been introduced to undercover police officers during Operation
Nightjar, the island constabulary's operation against street dealing in
Douglas's night-time economy, and sold them wraps of cocaine on three occasions
in August 2023. [@1]

Deemster Graeme Cook told Thomas that drugs brought misery to many individuals
and families and that crime did not pay. [@2] The BBC reported that Thomas was
27, while Isle of Man Today gave his age as 29. [@1]
