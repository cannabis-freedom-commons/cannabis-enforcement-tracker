Horry County police officers from the Narcotics and Vice unit, with the SWAT
team, searched a home on Spring Street in the Loris area of unincorporated Horry
County shortly after 5 a.m. on 30 June 2025. [@1] The search led to the arrest
of a 48-year-old man, who faces at least 10 drug and weapons charges. [@1]

WPDE ABC15 reported that the charges included trafficking 400 grams or more of
cocaine, three counts of trafficking crack cocaine, trafficking fentanyl,
trafficking 10 pounds or more of marijuana, manufacturing and distributing crack
cocaine, and weapon offences. [@2] Officers seized about 4,663 grams, or 10
pounds, of marijuana products and flower, which the department listed alongside
large amounts of cocaine, fentanyl, heroin, methamphetamine and pills. [@1] The
same search produced 2 cell phones, a Glock 17 9mm pistol, about $21,599 in cash
and a 2018 Range Rover, which police also recorded as seized. [@1]

The department said the man remained in custody at the J. Reuben Long Detention
Center when it published the statement. [@1] WMBF News reported that he remained
in custody at the detention centre and that the investigation had led to his
arrest. [@3]
