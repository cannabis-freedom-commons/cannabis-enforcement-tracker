Sati’o Matakaiongo, 43, was described as a repeat offender. [@1] He was serving
18 months imprisonment for possession of cannabis. [@1] His 23-year-old
co-accused received a suspended sentence in the same case. [@1] Both sentences
were imposed in the Fasi Magistrate’s Court. [@1]

The case involved 43.07 grams of cannabis seized in Popua. [@1]

Matakaiongo had earlier been found guilty of cannabis possession in Popua. [@2]
That earlier guilty finding concerned possession in Popua in April of that year.
