Officers from the central Dassa-Zoumè district police station stopped a cannabis
transport on 26 June 2025, seizing 61 slabs carried by two people on motorcycles
near Fita village in Kpingni arrondissement. [@2] The two riders fled when they
saw the security setup. [@3] They abandoned two unregistered Bajaj motorcycles
and a sack disguised as a fertilizer parcel. [@1] A search of the sack revealed
suspect substances later identified as cannabis. [@1] An official weighing with
the Collines departmental agriculture, livestock and fisheries directorate
confirmed 34 kilograms. [@1]

The interception happened on an isolated rural track described as close to
informal transit zones. [@1] The parcel was handed to the central office against
illicit drug trafficking and precursors, OCERTID, for judicial follow-up and
technical analysis. [@1]

A sweep of the area with local young residents failed to catch the two fleeing
suspects, and efforts to find and arrest them continue. [@2] La Nouvelle Tribune
also reported the seizure as 34 kilograms of cannabis quantified with the DDAEP.
[@3]
