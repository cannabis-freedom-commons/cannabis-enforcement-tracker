Jone Vakarisi, Sekove Tikoiserea, Paula Draunidalo, Nikotimo Vasuturaga, Jokini
Deni and Elia Marau are charged after allegedly being found with 139.6 grams of
marijuana in Lakeba, Lau, on 23 June 2025. [@1] Jone Vakarisi and three others
who were allegedly found with marijuana pleaded not guilty, while one man
pleaded guilty. [@1]

Elia Marau pleaded guilty to a charge of being in possession of illicit drugs.
[@1] Marau told the court that he pleaded guilty of his own free will and was
not coerced. [@1] A bench warrant was issued against Nikotimo Vasuturaga for
failing to appear in court. [@1]

Vakarisi told the court that he is currently in Kadavu. [@1] The prosecution was
ordered to clarify the charges against the six because one of them has pleaded
guilty. [@1] The matter was adjourned to 20 January next year to fix a hearing
date. [@1]
