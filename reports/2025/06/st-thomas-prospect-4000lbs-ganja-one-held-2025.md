Police in St Thomas took a man they believe to be a Bahamian national into
custody after finding ganja at an abandoned premises in Prospect. [@1,3] The
search was carried out by investigators of the Firearms and Narcotics
Investigation Division (FNID) with Jamaica Defence Force personnel and the St
Thomas Police Division. [@1]

More than 750 parcels of compressed ganja were found inside the premises. [@1]
Police put the seized ganja at 4,000 pounds, worth more than J$12 million in
their estimate. [@1] The Gleaner reported that a search of an unoccupied
building in Prospect found knitted bags of ganja weighing 4,079 pounds. [@2,3]
Several boats, drums of gasoline and other items were also seized in the
operation. [@1] The man found at the premises was 70 years old, and the Gleaner
reported 154 knitted bags holding 765 packages of ganja. [@2]

Bahamian officials in Jamaica were notified of the detention, and the man's name
was withheld while the investigation continued. [@2] Acting Senior
Superintendent Patrae Rowe, director of FNID, said the seizure struck at the
illegal guns-for-drugs trade, which he said ganja helps to fund. [@1]

A joint police and military team conducted the operation in the Blue Mahoe scheme about 11:15 pm under a search warrant issued under the Dangerous Drugs Act. [@3]
