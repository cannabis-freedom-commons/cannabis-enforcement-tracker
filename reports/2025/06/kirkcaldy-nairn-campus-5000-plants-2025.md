Around 12.40pm on Wednesday 18 June 2025, officers with a warrant searched a derelict building at Nairn Campus on High Street, Kirkcaldy, and uncovered a large cannabis cultivation. [@1,2] About 5,000 plants were recovered, with the harvested product weighing over 1,000kg. [@1] Police Scotland called it the largest cultivation found in Scotland to date, quoting Detective Inspector Samantha Davidson calling it "the most significant we've seen in Scotland". [@1] The force first put the street value at about £7 million and later revised it to almost £12 million once the crop was weighed. [@2,1] The previous Scottish record was a £6.3 million grow found on the same street in May 2022. [@2] Enquiries were continuing, with no arrests announced. [@1]
