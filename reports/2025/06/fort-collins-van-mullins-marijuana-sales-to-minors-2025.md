Fort Collins police officers and a Loveland Police SWAT team arrested Garett Van
Mullins on Thursday 19 June 2025 as part of an investigation into underage
marijuana sales. [@1] Police say they believe Van Mullins used social media
accounts under the names "420 Minister" and "I Am Foundation Church" to sell
marijuana. [@1]

He faces a charge of unlawful distribution of marijuana to a minor, a class 3
felony. [@1] The Fort Collins Police Department described the arrest as part of
an ongoing investigation into underage marijuana sales. [@1]

Police asked anyone with information, or any parent who believes their teenager
bought from him, to contact the department's marijuana enforcement officer. [@1]
