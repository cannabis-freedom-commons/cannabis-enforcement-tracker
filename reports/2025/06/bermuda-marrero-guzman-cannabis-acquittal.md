The jury reached its unanimous verdict before Puisne Judge Juan Wolffe after
nearly five hours of deliberation, and she was dismissed from the court. [@1]
She arrived on an Air Canada flight from Toronto on 20 June 2025 and was stopped
by customs at LF Wade International Airport after her checked suitcase was
scanned and showed anomalies. [@2] A customs officer, Ryan Marshall, told the
court he saw her head straight for the baggage belts and the exit, so sent her
for secondary inspection. [@2]

Officers cut off the case's combination lock because she said she did not know
the code, and found 29 heat-sealed packages containing 12,876g of cannabis. [@2]
The suitcase held 12,876 grams of cannabis, which prosecutors valued at $1.3
million on the island. [@1] Prosecutor Khadija Beddeau argued she acted as a
drug courier, telling the jury she was burdened with debt approaching $20,000
and had been offered $7,000 by a man called Anton to carry the case into
Bermuda. [@1]

Her defence counsel, Susan Mulligan, argued she believed the luggage contained
designer clothes and had no knowledge of the drugs. [@1] The trial was her
second in the Supreme Court for the offences, the first having ended with the
jury discharged. [@1] When she first appeared in Magistrates' Court in June
2025, Magistrate Craig Attridge remanded her in custody to the August
arraignments. [@3] A Toronto Sun account of the trial reported that 57
fingerprints were found on the heat-sealed bags and that none of the prints
analysed matched her. [@4]
