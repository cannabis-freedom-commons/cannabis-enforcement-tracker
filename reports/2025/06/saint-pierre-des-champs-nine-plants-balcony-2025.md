Gendarmes of the Lagrasse brigade found nine cannabis plants on the balcony of a
private home in Saint-Pierre-des-Champs, in the Corbières massif, and seized
them immediately. [@1] They came across the plants on 17 June 2025 during a foot
patrol in the commune, alerted by the characteristic smell of cannabis. [@1] The
patrol took place in a commune in the Corbières massif, about 40 kilometres from
both Carcassonne and Narbonne. [@1]

The grower was questioned over the facts by the local brigade and said the
plants were only for his own consumption. [@1] L'Indépendant places the facts on
Tuesday 17 June 2025 and names Lagrasse as the gendarmerie brigade that carried
out the seizure. [@2]
