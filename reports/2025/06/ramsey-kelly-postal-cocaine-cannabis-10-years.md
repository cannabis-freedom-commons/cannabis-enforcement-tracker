A man who had cocaine and cannabis sent through the post while sofa-surfing was
jailed for 10 years. [@1] The man was 25-year-old Lewis Jack Moulder Kelly. [@2]
Kelly pleaded guilty to importing cocaine, importing cannabis and possessing
cannabis with intent to supply. [@1] Deemster Graeme Cook said the other man's
initial arrest for the offences was an aggravating factor. [@1] The Deemster
said long sentences were warranted because the island was trying to rid itself
of drugs. [@1]

The package for Lewis Kelly was intercepted at the postal sorting office in
Braddan on 14 October. [@1] The parcel had been sent from Manchester and was
addressed to Louis Oakey at Bircham Avenue in Ramsey. [@1] The 25-year-old had
also been caught with 58 wraps of cannabis at his partner's home two days
earlier. [@1] The drug was found in an ice cream tub in 58 individual wraps at
an address in Ramsey. [@3] The wraps were found with scales and two grinders at
his partner's home on 12 October after police executed a warrant. [@1] A
fingerprint on one wrap matched Kelly. [@1]

Kelly told police he had a drug debt of between £10,000 and £20,000. [@2] He
said he believed the parcel would contain only cannabis and not cocaine. [@2]
Kelly also used the name Oakey when claiming disability living allowance. [@1]
Kelly handed himself in and admitted making the delivery searches on the other
man's phone, saying he had sold his own phone. [@1] Police had arrested the
other occupant after finding phone searches about parcel deliveries from the
United Kingdom to the island. [@1] The cocaine purity of between 82 and 84
percent was described as extremely high. [@2]

His defence advocate said he had built up a drug debt of between £15,000 and £20,000 and had thought the package would only contain cannabis. [@1] The deemster said Kelly may have been "duped" by those sending the drugs to the island, but that did not earn any sympathy from him. [@1] Douglas Courthouse was told the parcel was addressed to an address on Bircham Avenue in Ramsey and was sent from Manchester. [@1]
