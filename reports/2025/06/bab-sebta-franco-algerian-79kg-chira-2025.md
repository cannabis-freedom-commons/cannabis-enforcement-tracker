Customs officers at the Bab Sebta border post, working in close coordination
with National Security officers as part of continuing efforts against drug
trafficking, stopped a light Citroën arriving from Moroccan territory for a
thorough check on the evening of Monday 16 June 2025. [@1] A Franco-Algerian man
was arrested on Monday evening at Bab Sebta with cannabis concealed in his
vehicle. [@1] He is a French national of Algerian origin suspected of being the
principal author of the smuggling attempt, and he was accompanied by a woman
described as his suspected accomplice. [@1]

Officers found 79 kilograms of cannabis resin, known locally as chira, carefully
concealed in the vehicle. [@1] A second account of the same interception
confirms the car was a Citroën registered abroad and arriving from Morocco. [@2]
The same account confirms the check uncovered 79 kg of chira carefully
concealed. [@2]

The competent authorities immediately opened a judicial investigation under the
supervision of the public prosecutor to identify the networks involved in the
cross-border trafficking attempt. [@1] The prosecutor ordered the inquiry to
identify possible cross-border relays behind the attempt. [@2]
