Interior Minister Ali Yerlikaya announced that gendarmerie teams seized
12,389,000 cannabis and skunk plants together with 127 kilograms of cannabis
in Lice district, Diyarbakır. [@1,2] The plants were enough to produce more
than 100 tonnes of powdered cannabis, according to the minister's
statement. [@1,2]

The operation deployed 143 teams with 1,780 personnel, supported by an S-70
utility helicopter, an ATAK attack helicopter, two gendarmerie drones and
remote-sensing systems. [@1] Twenty-two suspects were caught in the
operation, and all of them were remanded in custody by the courts to which
they were brought. [@1]
