Bella May Culley, an 18-year-old woman from County Durham in England, was
arrested in Georgia with 14 kilograms of cannabis. [@1] If convicted, she could
receive up to 20 years in prison or life imprisonment in Georgia, according to
reports cited by the author. [@1]

The author, a criminologist, says drug mules usually do not know what they are
carrying or how much, and that harsh sentences therefore do not deter them. [@1]
The article names her case alongside that of Charlotte May Lee, a 21-year-old
from south London held in Sri Lanka with 46 kilograms of synthetic cannabis, who
has yet to be charged. [@1]
