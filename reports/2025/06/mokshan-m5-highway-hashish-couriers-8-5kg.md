Officers of the Penza drug-control directorate together with Mokshan district
traffic inspectors stopped a car carrying suspected drug-trafficking suspects on
the M-5 Ural highway near the settlement of Mokshan. [@1] The two detained men
are residents of Belgorod and Voronezh oblasts born in 1995 and 2001. [@1]

During the vehicle inspection, police found and seized packages of hashish with
a total mass of 8.5 kg hidden under the boot lining. [@1] According to the
detainees, they had collected the drug from a dead-drop cache in Moscow Oblast.
[@1] They were transporting the hashish to Samara and Chelyabinsk oblasts with
the intention of setting up caches for further sale. [@1]

A criminal case was opened on the signs of attempted large-scale illegal sale of
narcotics under Article 30(3) and Article 228.1(4)(g) of the Russian Criminal
Code. [@1] The investigation into the case is under way. [@1] The regional
drug-control chief said the hashish was divided into small 50-100 gram parcels
intended for dead drops in oblasts neighbouring Penza. [@2] The same chief said
the two men had come to police attention a month earlier because they had laid
caches with drugs in Penza Oblast. [@2]
