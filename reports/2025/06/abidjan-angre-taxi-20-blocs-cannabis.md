Officers of the 34th district police station in Abobo Baoulé stopped a metered
taxi at the Angré crossroads in Abidjan during a routine check on 24 June 2025.
[@1] Inside the vehicle they found 20 blocks of cannabis packed in sachets. [@2]
The co-driver ran off when he saw the officers and escaped arrest. [@4] The taxi
driver was detained and taken to the Abidjan police prefecture for
investigation, the national police said on 25 June. [@1]

The check fell under Operation Épervier 10, an operation set up to strengthen
surveillance in strategic zones of the Abidjan district. [@4] Officers opened an
investigation to find the accomplice who fled and to identify where the cannabis
came from and where it was destined. [@4] Wakat Séra said the national police
announced the seizure on its official page on 25 June. [@3] Abidjan 24, citing
Afrique-sur7, said Operation Épervier 10 was launched at the end of 2024. [@5]
