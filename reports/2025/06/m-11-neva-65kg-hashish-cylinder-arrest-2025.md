Drug-control officers of the St Petersburg and Leningrad Oblast police stopped a
rented Lada Largus at the 681st kilometre of the M-11 Neva motorway and detained
its 36-year-old driver, a resident of Leningrad Oblast, on suspicion of drug
distribution. [@1] The stop took place in the Pushkinsky district of St
Petersburg, where Pushkin traffic police assisted the anti-drug directorate.
[@3]

In the boot officers found a hydraulic accumulator cylinder, and inside it a
cache of 12 polymer packages. [@1] The driver said he installed water-supply
systems at country houses and knew nothing of the cylinder's contents. [@3] The
packages weighed about 6.5 kilograms in total, and examination found the wraps
contained hashish. [@3] The police statement said a total of more than 6 kg was
seized and that one package held almost 1 kg of hashish, with the remaining
wraps still to be examined. [@1] Officers detained the man for two days under
Article 91 of the Criminal Procedure Code. [@3]

The next day the driver was charged as accused Vladimir Kudryashov, and the
Pushkinsky District Court remanded him in custody until 23 August. [@2] He
opposed the arrest and asked for house arrest or a ban on certain actions
instead. [@2] Police opened a criminal case for attempted large-scale drug sale
under Article 30(3) and Article 228.1(4)(g) of the Criminal Code and were
working to establish the source of the drug and its distribution channels. [@1]
Igorglavk said investigators were establishing where the drug was bought and how
it was to be sold on. [@3]
