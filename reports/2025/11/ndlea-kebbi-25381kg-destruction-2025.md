The Kebbi State Command of the National Drug Law Enforcement Agency destroyed 25,381.495 kilogrammes of seized drugs, valued at several billions of naira, at Kolar in Birnin Kebbi on Saturday. [@1,2] Cannabis sativa made up the bulk at 16,951.105 kilogrammes, alongside 8,093.381 kilogrammes of tramadol, 336.978 kilogrammes of Akuskura described as THC, 0.027 kilogrammes of cocaine and a trace of methamphetamine. [@1,2]

Deputy Director of General Operations Muhammed Usman, representing Chairman Mohamed Buba Marwa, said the public destruction followed an order of the Federal High Court in Birnin Kebbi under Section 3(1) of the NDLEA Act. [@1,2] Marwa said the exercise showed the agency's commitment to a drug-free society and warned those in illicit drug activity. [@1,2]
