DRI officers acting on intelligence intercepted two passengers shortly after they arrived from Bangkok at Mumbai's CSMIA on Sunday. [@1,2] A detailed baggage search recovered 21 food packets, including noodles and biscuits, with hydroponic weed concealed inside ordinary packaging. [@1,2] Field tests with the NDPS kit confirmed the substance was narcotic. [@1,2]

The haul weighed 42.34 kg, which DRI valued at about Rs 42 crore, and was seized under the NDPS Act. [@1,2] Both passengers were arrested. [@1,2] DRI said the seizure was part of continuing Operation Weed Out, under which 292.9 kg had been seized at Indian airports. [@1,2]
