A Texas Department of Public Safety trooper pulled over a vehicle for speeding
in a school zone in San Antonio on Thursday 20 November 2025. [@1] Three adult
men were in the vehicle, and officers found more than 100 THC vape cartridges,
258 grams of THC and 4.91 ounces (about 139 grams) of marijuana inside. [@1]
Multiple handguns were also found during the search. [@1] All three men were
taken into custody. [@1]

The Texas Department of Public Safety announced the bust on its Facebook page,
and the account was published by the San Antonio broadcaster KABB on 22 November
2025. [@1]
