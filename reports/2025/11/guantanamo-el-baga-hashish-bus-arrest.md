A joint Ministry of the Interior operation in Guantánamo province detained one
person carrying hashish while he travelled on an intermunicipal bus. [@1] The
interception happened aboard a Yutong bus covering the Baracoa to Guantánamo
route. [@2] The man, a Baracoa resident, was stopped at the El Bagá police
checkpoint in the municipality of San Antonio del Sur. [@1] Witnesses said a
drug-detection dog taking part in the checkpoint found the drug, identified as
hashish, a cannabis derivative. [@1]

The detainee was already serving a sentence for drug trafficking and was on
temporary prison leave when he was stopped again. [@1] The officialist profile
Guantánamo y su verdad reported that he was already sanctioned for drug
trafficking, so the new detention came while he held a prison benefit. [@1]

Both outlets place the case within Cuba's official Zero Tolerance campaign
against drug trafficking, promoted as #ContraLasDrogasSeGana. [@1] The detention
and its disclosure through officialist channels were presented as reaffirming
the state's hard line on drug use and trafficking, including the synthetic
cannabinoids known in Cuba as químico and papelillo, which are not part of this
case. [@1] The second outlet linked the case to a wider judicial offensive of
exemplary sentences and to tighter controls at entry points and on
intermunicipal transport. [@2]
