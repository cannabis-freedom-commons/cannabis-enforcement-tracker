The Volta Regional Police Command burned 600 fertilizer sacks of Indian hemp at the Klefe dumping site in Ho on Monday 17 November 2025. [@1,2] The consignment weighed 47,530 kg, with an estimated face value of about GH¢4.2 billion. [@1,2]

The stock had been seized in 2022 in the case The Republic versus Yao Kwadwo, also known as Wallako, and two others. [@1,2] The Ho Circuit Court, presided over by His Honour Felix Datsumor, ordered the destruction after the exhibits were tendered in evidence. [@1,2] The destruction was announced in a statement signed by Chief Inspector Francis Kwaku Gomado. [@2]

The exercise was supervised by officials of the Circuit Court, the Attorney General's office, the Narcotics Control Commission and the Environmental Protection Agency, with the police and national security present. [@1,2]
