Gendarmería Nacional officers of the Sección Seguridad Vial Lapachito, part of
Escuadrón 14 Las Palmas, stopped a general-cargo truck at kilometre 1,060 of
Ruta Nacional 11 in paraje Lapachito, Chaco, during a dynamic control operation.
[@1] The truck had left Mariano Roque Alonso in Paraguay and was heading for
Córdoba province. [@1] Officers noticed inconsistencies in the seal ropes
holding the tarpaulin of the semi-trailer and a strong smell of cannabis coming
from the load. [@1] The anti-drug dog Olivia reacted to the load, and with the
Federal Court No. 1 of Resistencia informed, the truck was taken to Escuadrón 14
for a full inspection. [@1]

With staff of the customs agency ARCA's Barranqueras branch, the gendarmes found
115 boxes hidden among wire coils, holding 2,384 rectangular and amorphous
packages. [@1] A narcotest field test was positive for cannabis sativa, with a
total weight of 2,697 kilograms 926 grams. [@1]

The judge ordered the drug and three mobile phones seized and the Paraguayan
driver detained, where he remained held incommunicado. [@1] Gendarmería's own
release, published three days earlier, describes the same stop and gives the
same 2,697 kilograms 926 grams total. [@2] The local outlet TN24 reported that
the driver was placed at the disposal of the courts and that investigators were
working to establish where the load was to be delivered. [@3]
