Two members of an organised drug group were stopped in Bukhaydin district,
Fergana region, and found carrying 10 kilograms of hashish, about 20,000 doses.
[@2] The two had run the business for about a year, police said, collecting
hashish left in hiding places at coordinates passed on by a man living in
Kyrgyzstan. [@2] They planned to distribute the hashish among residents of
Fergana, Namangan and Andijan regions. [@2]

Two more men were stopped in Bostan district, Andijan region, with 1 kilogram of
hashish, about 2,000 doses, for sale in the region. [@2] Police said those two
were also part of the same organised crime group and sold drugs in Andijan
region. [@2] The interior ministry announced both detections together as the
break-up of one organised drug group. [@2] The interior ministry put the hashish
at 11 kilograms across the two regions, about 22,000 doses. [@2]

A regional news agency reported police detaining several groups and taking 12.5
kilograms of drugs, which it said amounted to about 25,000 doses in Tashkent
region and Fergana and Andijan regions. [@1] The Fergana group had for more than
a year been taking drugs from hiding places at given addresses and passing them
to residents of the three regions. [@1] In Andijan region, locals were caught
holding 1 kilogram of hashish intended for sale there. [@1]
