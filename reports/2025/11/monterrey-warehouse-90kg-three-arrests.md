Monterrey municipal police detained two men and a woman after an alleged robbery
of an iPhone 17, which led to the search of a warehouse where almost 90 kilos of
marijuana were found. [@1] The detained people were identified as Jairo Rivaldo
R., aged 25, Bárbara M., aged 23, from Colombia, and Liván O., aged 26,
originally from Cuba. [@1] The street detention happened on Monday afternoon at
the corner of 15 de mayo and Juan Álvarez in central Monterrey as part of
Programa Escudo operations. [@1] Police said the three were accused of robbing a
person at gunpoint of the phone, which was valued at 34,000 pesos. [@1]

Investigators then located a warehouse at that spot, and the Fiscalía General de
Justicia de Nuevo León obtained a cateo, a court-ordered search, which
ministerial agents carried out to find the drugs. [@1] Inside the building
officers found 89 bags of dry green plant with characteristics similar to
marijuana, with an approximate weight of 90 kilos. [@1] Officers also seized a
digital scale, a sealer, plastic bags, an identity card and passports. [@1]

The warehouse was secured by the Ministerio Público investigator specializing in
Narcomenudeo for the corresponding investigation, while the detainees awaited
their legal situation. [@1]
