Guangzhou Post Office Customs seized two bags of cannabis-infused candies in the inbound mail
channel, according to a “Customs Release” WeChat notice reported by Southern Net. [@1] The parcel
had been declared as a phone charger (“手机充电器”). [@1]

Officers X-rayed the mail item and found the image abnormal. [@1] Inside was a black storage box
holding two bags of colorful cube-shaped hard candies, 17 pieces in total. [@1] A field reagent
test on the candies was positive for cannabis. [@1]

The candies were sent to a technical institution, which confirmed they contained THC and were
cannabis products weighing 283 g. [@1] Customs reminded the public that smuggling drugs carries
criminal liability regardless of quantity. [@1]
