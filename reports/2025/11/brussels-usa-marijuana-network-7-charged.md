Federal judicial police in Brussels uncovered a structured international
drug-trafficking network after two pallets of marijuana from the United States
were intercepted. [@2] The operation was led by the drugs section of the
Brussels Federal Judicial Police in close collaboration with the Brussels
prosecutor's office under the direction of an investigating judge. [@2] Officers
carried out 27 searches mainly in Brussels and deprived 15 people of liberty.
[@2] Seven people were charged with criminal organisation in connection with
importing narcotics, and five of them were placed under arrest warrant. [@3] The
28 November overview describes the case as a cannabis-importation network from
the United States dismantled in Brussels with seven people charged by the
Brussels federal judicial police. [@1] Analysis of seized telephones showed the
detainees acted for a criminal organisation active in Brussels and beyond. [@2]
The group had organised logistical support for vehicles, money transfers and
delivery management. [@2]

One of the main suspects was reported to be on the run in the United Arab
Emirates. [@3] The searches seized a large sum of money, several firearms and
different types of drugs. [@2] The prosecutor's office called the operation a
major step against trafficking structures active in the Brussels area. [@2]
