The Border Security Force, working with PR Bari police station staff and officials of the Deputy Collector and Magistrate's office in Belonia, searched a house in Uttar Awasthipara village, Dakshin Krishnapur, Radhanagar, South Tripura district, and recovered 168 kg of ganja. [@1,2] The search took place on 7 November 2025 and was reported the next day. [@1,2]

In a separate operation the same day, BSF troops apprehended three Bangladeshi nationals in the Indian village of Megliband, Katlamura, under Sidhai police station in Mohanpur subdivision of West Tripura district, as they allegedly planned to cross into Bangladesh. [@1,2] The BSF said the operations showed its continued effort against cross-border crime and drug trafficking in Tripura with state agencies. [@2]
