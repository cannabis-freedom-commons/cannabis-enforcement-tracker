West Midlands Police officers carried out a drugs warrant at an address on
Goldthorn Road in Wolverhampton and recovered 30 cannabis plants. [@1] Officers
seized the crop on Monday morning and arrested a man on suspicion of cultivation
of cannabis. [@1]

The man was also held on suspicion of illegally abstracting electricity and of
possessing a banned breed of dog, and West Midlands Police said he remained in
custody. [@1] Birmingham Live reported that West Midlands Police estimated the
plants' street value at about £30,000, working from an average of £1,000 a
plant. [@2] Birmingham Live reported that the warrant was a section 23 misuse of
drugs warrant and that police photos showed about a dozen plants growing in pots
under a hydroponic set-up and several clear jars of cannabis. [@2] Ettingshall,
Blakenhall and Spring Vale Police said on X that officers conducted the warrant
that morning and that one male was in custody for cultivation of cannabis,
abstracting electricity and possession of banned dogs. [@2]
