Siedia Cherrell Bell, 38, of Fishers, was charged with a Level 6 felony count of
neglect of a dependent and a Class B misdemeanor of reckless supervision by a
child care supervisor over a 12 November incident. [@2] The case was filed in
Hancock Superior Court 2. [@2] Bell was arrested on Thursday, and a judge set a
$1,000 cash bond at a Friday morning initial hearing. [@2] She is due back in
court in March for a pretrial conference, Hancock County Prosecutor Brent Eaton
said. [@2]

A mother contacted police after her 3-year-old daughter came home not feeling
normal on 12 November from Tots R' Us, a day care in the 3700 block of Shadeland
Avenue on Indianapolis's east side. [@1] The day care is at 3709 Shadeland Ave.
[@1] The child told her mother she had eaten a gummy that made her feel weird.
[@1] An at-home drug test was positive for THC, and a hospital drug screen was
positive for cannabinoids. [@1] Court documents do not say how much of the gummy
the child ingested. [@1] Her family said she recovered fully from the THC
exposure. [@1]

A day-care employee told the mother she had firsthand knowledge the child
consumed THC gummies taken from Bell's jacket pocket. [@1] The day the child
allegedly ate the gummy, the day-care owner took her from the Indianapolis day
care to the owner's home in Greenfield because of structural issues, and IMPD
handed the investigation to Greenfield police. [@1] The probable-cause affidavit
says the mother reported that Bell had THC gummies in her jacket and had laid
the jacket on the child at a Greenfield home. [@2] An Indiana Metropolitan
Police Department detective contacted Greenfield police on 17 November about the
neglect report. [@2]

A Department of Child Services case worker met Bell on 21 December. [@1] Bell
admitted having marijuana gummies in her jacket pocket but said she had removed
them before the child had access to the jacket. [@1] She told officers she had
moved the marijuana candies to her glove compartment before the children were in
her vehicle. [@2] She told a DCS worker that she had told the girl's mother
there was no way the child got the THC gummies out of her pocket. [@1] Online
court records showed an active warrant for her arrest when the charge was first
reported. [@1]
