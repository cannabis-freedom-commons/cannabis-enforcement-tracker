On Tuesday 18 November 2025, some 500 personnel of the district police, Tripura State Rifles,
CRPF, district administration and the Forest Department destroyed about 12 lakh semi-mature
cannabis plants across 120 plots in the remote forests of Sepahijala district. [@1,2,3] The
six-hour drive ran from 8:00 AM to 2:00 PM and followed specific intelligence gathered by
Tripura Police. [@1,3]

The plots lay at Sutarmura, Toktumadum, Chitta Rambari, Ukyamura, Tulamura Kamalnagar and
Ghatigar, under the Bishramganj, Bishalgarh, Kalamchura, Sonamura and Melagarh police
stations. [@3] Superintendent of Police Bijoy Debbarma, who led the operation, said the
destruction of the crop crippled a major supply chain and warned that such cultivation would
not be tolerated. [@1,2,3]

Police estimated the street value of the destroyed plants at Rs 50 crore, and a preliminary
investigation suggested the crop was intended for interstate trafficking. [@1,3] All detected
plants were uprooted and destroyed on site. [@2,3] No arrests were reported in the accounts
opened. [@1,2,3]
