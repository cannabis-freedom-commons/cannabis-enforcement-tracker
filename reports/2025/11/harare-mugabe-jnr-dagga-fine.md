Robert Mugabe Jnr, 33, pleaded guilty to unlawful possession of drugs at the
Harare Magistrates' Court. [@1] Magistrate Lisa Mutendereki fined him US$300,
ordering him to pay or serve four months in prison if he defaulted. [@2] The
court also gave him three months' imprisonment, wholly suspended on condition
that he commits no similar offence within five years. [@1] He was convicted on
Thursday 13 November after asking the court for leniency, saying he was a single
father of two. [@2]

Court papers said police stopped him in a silver Honda Fit travelling the wrong
way along Second Street Extension in Harare and found two sachets of dagga, a
pack of Rizla papers and a white dagga crusher in his sling bag. [@2,6] Dagga is
the southern African name for cannabis. [@1]

Mugabe was arrested in October and later released on $300 bail, and a warrant
issued after he missed a routine remand date was cancelled before the trial.
[@3] He had first appeared in court on 2 October and was remanded in custody
pending a bail ruling. [@5,6,8] He was released on Friday after paying bail of $300.
[@4] At that first appearance police said five people in a "syndicate linked to
him" had also been arrested and that they found 25 sachets of marijuana and six
ecstasy pills. [@4,6]

Police charged Mugabe over 2 g of cannabis with a street value of $30. [@4] His
lawyer, Ashiel Mugiya, denied the charge and said the drugs came from a car
carrying three other people, with only 0.02 g of cannabis found. [@4,6] The 13
November ruling followed a warrant and missed hearing in a case that had drawn
attention because Mugabe is the son of Zimbabwe's late president Robert Mugabe.
[@4]

Robert Mugabe Junior, 33, was arrested on Wednesday, 1 October 2025, after he was stopped and searched by officers who spotted him driving into oncoming traffic. [@7]

One of former Zimbabwean President Robert Mugabe's sons has been detained on drug possession charges, with the police saying he is part of a syndicate of illegal substance peddlers. [@8]

Police stopped Mugabe, 33, on Tuesday night as he drove a silver Honda Fit the wrong way along 2nd Street Extension in central Harare. [@9] Officers reportedly found two small sachets of dagga, papers and a dagga crusher in a sling bag he was carrying. [@9] Mugabe was remanded in custody at Harare Central Prison after the Harare Magistrate's Court postponed his bail hearing to Friday, and by then had spent two nights in custody. [@9]
