Gendarmes of the Núcleo section of the 22 "San Antonio de los Cobres" Squadron
arrested one man on the Argentine side of the border with Chile, near the "XVI -
X" boundary marker and the Salín volcano, while his companion drove away. [@1]
Officers signalled two pickups that were entering at high speed from Chile to
stop, but one of the drivers evaded the control. [@1] The arrested man was the
passenger of the second pickup, a Toyota Hilux without plates, and was caught as
he tried to flee on foot. [@1]

On the way to the Socompa post with the arrested man, the patrol saw a vehicle
coming down an internal road of an abandoned mining project. [@1] There they
found a second Toyota Hilux, dark red and also without plates, abandoned with
its doors open. [@1] In the pickup's bed were bundles packed in jute-type sacks
holding a brown vegetable substance with a strong penetrating smell, similar to
marijuana. [@1] A Narcotest on the load came back positive for "cannabis
sativa", with a total weight of 361 kilograms 769 grams, according to the
Gendarmería statement. [@1]

The Salta Federal Prosecutor's Office ordered both pickups and the drug seized
and the man involved arrested. [@1]

The operation took place in the mining area of San Antonio de los Cobres, near
the Chilean border, at 3,775 metres above sea level. [@2]

Gente de Salta reported that the investigation was continuing to identify the
rest of the organisation's members. [@2]

Salta outlets reproduced the Gendarmería account, likewise reporting a satellite
phone and communication antenna seized from the first pickup. [@3]
