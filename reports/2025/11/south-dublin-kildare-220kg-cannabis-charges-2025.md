As part of an intelligence-led operation involving the Garda National Drugs and Organised Crime Bureau and Revenue Customs Service, 220 kg of herbal cannabis with an estimated value of €4.4 million was seized on Friday 14 November 2025. [@1,2,4] The 220 kg was seized by Revenue Customs Service, and a further 10.5 kg of herbal cannabis with an estimated market value of €210,000, plus €10,000 in cash, was seized by Gardaí in follow-up searches in South Dublin and Co. Kildare. [@1,2]

Four men, all aged in their 30s, were arrested and detained in Garda stations in Dublin and Kildare under section 50 of the Criminal Justice Act 2007. [@1,2,4] Three of them were later charged and were expected to appear before Blanchardstown District Court on the morning of Monday 17 November 2025, while the fourth was released without charge pending a file to the Director of Public Prosecutions. [@3]
