On 22 November 2025, officers of the Konni departmental branch of the Central
Office for the Repression of Illicit Drug Trafficking (OCRTIS) intercepted a
suspect at the Cerassa checkpoint after a tip about a network active on the
Togo-Benin-Niger route. [@1] The suspect is a Nigerien national. [@1] Officers
found 20 balls of Amnesia-type cannabis weighing 11.34 kg concealed in his
personal effects. [@1]

Investigators said he had stocked the cannabis at Tchitaoua in Togo before
travelling north to supply buyers, including in Agadez and at the Chirfa
gold-mining site where he worked as a labourer. [@1] Police said the network's
method was to get off the bus with the luggage before Cerassa, a barrier known
for strict controls, take a kabou-kabou motorcycle taxi and rejoin the vehicle
further on at Tsernaoua. [@1] The ruse was meant to avoid the systematic search
of passengers but failed against OCRTIS intelligence work and surveillance. [@1]

The Konni Departmental Directorate of the National Police said its objective was
to cut traffickers' routes and protect young people, and it called for closer
cooperation from the public. [@1] Authorities said citizens' involvement remains
essential in fighting a trade that undermines social cohesion. [@1]
