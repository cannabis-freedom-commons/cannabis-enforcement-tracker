Officers of the Anti-Gang and Organised Crime Police Directorate (Dipampco)
detained one man in barrio Cosmul, San Manuel, Cortés, on 2 November 2025. [@1]

Police identified him only by the aliases “Danger” and “Peligro” and said he had
been an active Mara Salvatrucha (MS-13) member for about a year, holding the
rank of “traka”, a street-level seller and distributor of drugs. [@1] Officers
seized 51 small plastic bags of dried plant material suspected to be marijuana.
[@1] The same search turned up 77 bags of white powder suspected to be cocaine,
cash and a mobile phone, none of which are counted as cannabis in this report.
[@1]

Police said the man is suspected of drug trafficking to the detriment of public
health and sent him to the competent prosecutor's office. [@1] The National
Police announced the arrest on its Facebook page under the headline “Traka of
the MS-13 falls in Cortés”, saying suspected cocaine and marijuana ready for
distribution and sale were seized. [@2] The same post said the seizure was
suspected cocaine and marijuana ready for distribution and sale. [@2]
