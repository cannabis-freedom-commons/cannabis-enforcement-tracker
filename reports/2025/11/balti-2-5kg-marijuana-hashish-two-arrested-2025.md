Police detained a 34-year-old Chișinău driver who was carrying the drugs. [@2]
They later detained a 25-year-old Chișinău resident whom police said had
supplied the driver. [@2] Police announced the seizure at the entrance to Bălți
on Monday, 10 November. [@3] The National Inspectorate of Investigations'
Northern directorate and prosecutors from PCCOCS's Northern office reported that
the load was in a BMW 7-series. [@2] The driver was carrying about 2.5 kilograms
of hydroponic marijuana and hashish. [@2]

The drugs were packed in 140 sachets intended for sale in Bălți. [@1] Police
valued the seizure at about 1,000,000 lei. [@2] Investigators suspected the men
were part of a group selling drugs in Bălți, Telenești and nearby districts
through orders received on Telegram. [@2] Police said the group was seeking
additional dealers as it expanded. [@3]

At prosecutors' request, a Bălți court placed the driver in pre-trial detention.
[@2] Prosecutors charged the second suspect with complicity in drug trafficking
and said they would seek pre-trial detention. [@2] Police seized the BMW as a
vehicle used to transport drugs, with a view to possible future confiscation by
the state. [@2]
