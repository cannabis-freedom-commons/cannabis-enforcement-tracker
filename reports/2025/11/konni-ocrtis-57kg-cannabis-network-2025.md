Niger's Central Office for the Repression of Illicit Drug Trafficking (OCRTIS)
arrested two suspected members of a cross-border cannabis network in the Konni
department of Tahoua Region. [@1]

The first seizure was made on 30 October 2025 by a patrol of the local urban
corps service in Konni, which found 44.225 kg of cannabis resin, packed for
transport. [@1] The same patrol also found 25,000 Exol tablets and 3,800 doses
of 225 mg tramadol, which are not counted as cannabis. [@1] The case was handed
to OCRTIS, whose investigators identified a Nigerien national known for
suspicious border trips and arrested him on 6 November 2025. [@1]

Officers then intercepted a second suspect, a Nigerian national, as he made a
delivery by motorcycle at Saléoua, a hamlet of the rural commune of Guidan Idder
in Malbaza. [@1] He was carrying 14 packages of an "amnesia" cannabis variety
weighing 12.81 kg in total, according to the account. [@1] ActuNiger describes
the second courier's 14 packages as balls of amnesia cannabis and his motorcycle
as a Kasea 125. [@2] ActuNiger dates the first seizure at Konni to 30 October
2024, while the Journal du Niger dates it to 30 October 2025. [@2]

The cumulative cannabis seizures in the case exceeded 57 kg, alongside the tens
of thousands of psychotropic pills, according to the Journal du Niger. [@1] The
two suspects were held in police custody while the investigation continued. [@1]
