A detainee and one of his relatives were brought before the Fort-de-France
prosecutor and convicted over half a kilo of cannabis resin found on him at the
Ducos prison visiting room in Martinique. [@1] The woman received 12 months in
prison, six of them firm. [@1] The detainee, who was checked with the drugs,
will serve an extra year of detention for these facts. [@1]

No prosecution is being pursued against the 52-year-old prison officer, whose
police custody was lifted. [@1] Investigators established no link between the
officer and the trafficking. [@1]

Half a kilo of cannabis resin had been found on the detainee at the visiting
room of the Ducos prison after his family's visit. [@1] Officers found the name
of a prison officer working in the same building as the detainee on the envelope
he was carrying. [@1] RCI reported on 21 November 2025 that a prison officer had
been in police custody since Thursday 20 November, the day a detainee was
checked with almost 500 grams of cannabis resin after a family visit. [@2]
Gendarmes arrested the 52-year-old officer at about 14:00 the previous day,
handcuffed him and searched his locker and his vehicle. [@2]
