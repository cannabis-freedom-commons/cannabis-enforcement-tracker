The criminal court in Aix tried Wissam B. separately after his co-defendants and
sentenced him to five years in prison, including two years to be served in
custody. [@1] The court heard that he played a leading role in a
drug-trafficking operation in the Jean-Jaurès housing estate in Port-de-Bouc.
[@1] He had escaped the June 2023 roundup because he was on holiday in Algeria,
the country where he was born. [@1] Two people questioned in the case, including
his younger brother, pointed to his role in the trafficking. [@1]

The police investigation had begun in March after tip-offs about drug
trafficking in that estate, which surveillance then corroborated as organised
trafficking. [@1] The police operation launched on 26 June 2023 and the
resulting searches seized 17.725 kg of cannabis resin and 5.8 kg of herbal
cannabis, alongside 585 g of cocaine, a small quantity of synthetic drugs and
8,000 euros in cash. [@1] Several other people arrested in the roundup were
tried a few days later in immediate-appearance hearings and received various
penalties. [@1]
