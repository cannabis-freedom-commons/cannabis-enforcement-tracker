Cook Islands Police arrested a 36-year-old man at Rarotonga International
Airport on Friday 21 November 2025 and charged him with importing cannabis. [@1]
Customs officers found more than one kilogram of cannabis in two packages in his
possession when he arrived as a passenger on a flight, and referred the matter
to the police. [@1] He arrived as a passenger on a flight on the Friday and
Customs referred the matter to the police, who arrested him. [@1]

Police media advisor Trevor Pitt said the accused was still remanded in custody
after appearing in court on the Saturday. [@1] The same police roundup reported
the theft of a rented motorcycle in Avarua and four weekend crashes, separate
incidents left out of this report. [@1]
