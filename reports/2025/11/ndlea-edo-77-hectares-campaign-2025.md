The Edo State command of Nigeria's National Drug Law Enforcement Agency (NDLEA)
destroyed nine cannabis farms covering 77.5122197 hectares between January and
November 2025. [@1,2] The agency put the estimated yield of the destroyed farms
at 193,491.1 kg of cannabis. [@1,2]

State Commander Mitchell Ofoyeju disclosed the figures on Wednesday in Benin City
during a meeting with the Edo State Commissioner for Agriculture and Food
Security, Dr Jerry Uwangue. [@1,2] Ofoyeju called for the affected farmlands to
be confiscated by the state government, saying the land would have helped ease
food insecurity if properly used. [@1]

The meeting agreed that the NDLEA and the agriculture ministry would formalise a
collaboration framework, including identifying vulnerable farming communities and
training farmers to switch to crops such as cassava, maize and cocoa under the
agency's Alternative Development Programme. [@2]
