Customs officers found 18 pouches holding 9.94 kilos of suspected marijuana kush
in an unclaimed green suitcase at the arrival area of NAIA Terminal 3 on Friday
28 November 2025. [@3,4] The suitcase contained 18 pouches with 9.94 kilos of the
suspected kush. [@1] The PNP Aviation Security Group estimated the haul at P14.9
million. [@1] A Bureau of Customs x-ray operator flagged the luggage after
spotting a suspicious image during routine screening, and a PDEA canine team
confirmed an illegal substance before customs personnel opened it. [@1]

Investigators traced the bag to a female foreign passenger who had arrived from
Bangkok and was booked on a connecting flight to Macau. [@1,4] The passenger had
already boarded her Macau flight at 7:16 p.m. and evaded arrest. [@1,4] Probers
confirmed that the foreigner departed for Macau and evaded arrest. [@2]

The seized items were inventoried by PDEA for turnover to its national office.
[@1] The Philippine Star reported that the contraband will be turned over to the
PDEA for proper disposition. [@2]

The Filipino-language account says the suspect boarded the Macau flight at 7:16
p.m. and authorities could no longer catch her. [@3]

A Bureau of Customs report to NAIA District Collector Yasmin Mapa said an x-ray operator first spotted the suspicious image during routine screening. [@4] The find was reported to the NAIA Inter-Agency Drug Interdiction Task Group, and a PDEA K9 team with a narcotics detection dog then verified the presence of illegal substances. [@4] The luggage was screened by customs x-ray before the PDEA dog unit was called, and the airport police aviation security group took part under its director, PBGen. Dionisio B. Bartolome. [@4]
