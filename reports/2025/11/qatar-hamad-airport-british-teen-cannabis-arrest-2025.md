Lino Neil, an 18-year-old from Drymen near Stirling, was detained at Hamad
International Airport in Doha on 21 November 2025 while changing planes on
his way back from Thailand to Dublin. [@1,2] Cannabis was found in his
suitcase, and he was accused of drug trafficking under Qatar's strict drug
laws. [@1,2]

Neil says a British expatriate in Thailand controlled and threatened him and
forced him to carry the suitcase, and his family says he was exploited by
gangsters. [@1,2] He spent Christmas in a crowded airport cell with 80 other
inmates before being moved to Doha's Central Prison, where suspected drug
couriers are typically held. [@1,2]

His mother said he had lost weight and was terrified, and his brother said
the family only learned of the arrest after Neil missed his connecting flight
home. [@1,2] A court hearing was scheduled for 27 January 2026, at which Neil
was expected to argue he had been coerced. [@1,2]
