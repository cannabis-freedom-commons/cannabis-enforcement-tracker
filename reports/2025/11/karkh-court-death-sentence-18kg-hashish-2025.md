The Karkh Criminal Court in Baghdad sentenced three convicted drug traffickers to death,
Iraq's judiciary said in a statement. [@1,2]

The three convicts were caught with 18 kilograms of hashish, which the court said they
intended to trade and sell among users. [@1,2] The sentences were issued under Article
27/First of the Narcotics and Psychotropic Substances Law No. 50 of 2017, which allows the
death penalty or life imprisonment for importing, transporting or cultivating narcotics.
[@1,2]

The ruling came as Baghdad pressed a wider crackdown on the drug trade. [@1] Iraq's
interior ministry said security forces had dismantled 1,201 drug trafficking and
distribution networks over three years, including 171 international networks, and that
2,318 sentences had been issued on drug charges, including 300 death sentences and 1,147
life terms. [@1,2]
