Steinkopf police officers on Wednesday-morning patrols near Steinkopf spotted a
suspicious vehicle loaded with boxes at the back. [@2] Officers searched the
vehicle and found roughly 64.8 kilograms of dagga packed in boxes. [@2] Police
arrested two men, both aged 33, at the scene. [@2]

Police placed the estimated street value of the dagga at R7.7 million. [@2]
Police confiscated the vehicle allegedly used in the commission of the crime.
[@2] The two suspects were expected to appear in court soon on charges of
dealing in drugs. [@2]

The South African Police Service said officers intercepted a vehicle on the N7
road in the Northern Cape and uncovered dagga worth R7.7 million. [@1] That
police account says two suspects were arrested and the vehicle used in the crime
was confiscated. [@1] Police counted the N7 interception as part of intensified
operations that resulted in the arrest of 17 817 suspects from 03 to 09 November
2025. [@1]
