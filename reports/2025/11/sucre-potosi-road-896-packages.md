Anti-drug agents intercepted a van carrying 896 packages of marijuana on the
Potosí–Sucre road, according to the police report. [@1] EL DEBER reported that
officers seized 45 jute sacks in which the marijuana was packed. [@1] Officers
of Bolivia's FELCN, the Special Force against Drug Trafficking, stopped the
private van at a static vehicle checkpoint on the Potosí–Sucre highway. [@3] The
FELCN personnel involved were from Sucre. [@2]

Inside the vehicle they found 45 light-blue jute sacks holding 896 beige
packages of a greenish herb. [@3] A field test was positive for marijuana, and
the packages were seized as a controlled substance. [@3] The packages were
wrapped in beige tape, the newspaper reported. [@2]

The controlled substance was seized, but it was not known whether the driver or
anyone else had been detained. [@2] Police said this was the start of an
investigation and that a press conference was expected to give more details of
the operation. [@2] The FELCN unit in Sucre said it runs intensive operations on
the region's interdepartmental routes, where large marijuana seizures had been
reported frequently in the previous two months. [@2]
