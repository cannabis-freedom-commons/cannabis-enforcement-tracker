Edo State Commander Mitchell Ofoyeju, presenting the command's October scorecard in Benin City, said intelligence-led operations arrested 39 suspected traffickers, 27 men and 12 women, and seized 2,477.59 kilogrammes of narcotics. [@1,2] Three cannabis farms in Ugbodo Forest in Ovia North East, covering 6.711192 hectares with an estimated yield of 16,777.98 kilogrammes, were destroyed in the same period. [@1,2]

Cannabis accounted for 2,476.87 kilogrammes of the seized drugs, with the rest made up of tramadol, nitrazepam, bromazepam, diazepam, Swinol, Danabol, abandoned methamphetamine and codeine syrup. [@1,2] Ofoyeju said the prosecution unit secured eleven convictions in October, filed eleven new cases, and had 121 cases pending at the Federal High Court in Benin City. [@1,2] Twenty-two people with drug abuse problems received counselling, and sensitisation programmes ran in schools, the National Youth Service Corps and army and community groups. [@1,2]
