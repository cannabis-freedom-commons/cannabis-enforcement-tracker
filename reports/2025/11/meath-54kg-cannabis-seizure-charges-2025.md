As part of an intelligence-led operation, about 54 kg of herbal cannabis worth an estimated €1,080,000 was seized in Co. Meath on Thursday 20 November 2025. [@1,2,4] The seizure was made in a joint operation conducted by the Garda National Drugs and Organised Crime Bureau, the Meath Divisional Drugs Unit and Revenue's Customs Service. [@1,2]

Gardaí arrested two men, both aged in their 30s, who were detained at a Garda station in Co. Meath under section 2 of the Criminal Justice (Drug Trafficking) Act 1996. [@1,2,4] Both men were charged the next day and were expected to appear before the Criminal Courts of Justice on the morning of Friday 21 November 2025; Gardaí said they could not comment further as the matter was before the courts. [@3]
