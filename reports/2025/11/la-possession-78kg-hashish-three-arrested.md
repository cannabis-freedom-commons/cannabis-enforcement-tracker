Police in La Possession, Réunion, arrested a 38-year-old drug dealer and his
21-year-old accomplice in the street on 3 November 2025 after months of
investigation. [@1] The operation was run by the narcotics squad of the Réunion
territorial judicial police in a preliminary inquiry opened by the Saint-Denis
prosecutor's office. [@1]

Investigators said the dealer ran a sustained resale business at many points
across the island, with the younger man from Le Port handling deliveries. [@1]
Searches found a hiding place where officers seized 7.8 kilos of cannabis resin,
along with about 5 kilos of cocaine and 1.9 kilos of ecstasy tablets. [@1] The
seizures also included weighing equipment, packaging, 46,520 euros in cash and
two vehicles. [@1]

The main suspect's partner was also arrested, bringing the number of suspects to
three. [@1] After extended police custody, the three suspects were brought
before the Saint-Denis prosecutor's office on 7 November 2025. [@1]
