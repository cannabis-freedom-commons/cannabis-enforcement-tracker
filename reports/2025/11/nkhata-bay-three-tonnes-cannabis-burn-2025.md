Police in Nkhata Bay District burned three tonnes of cannabis on 11 November
2025 in an exercise ordered by the local court. [@1]

Deputy district police spokesperson Devetor Mithi said the cannabis had been
confiscated from various people across the district between September 2024 and
August 2025. [@1] He said the burning followed a ruling by the Second Grade
Magistrate's Court in Nkhata Bay. [@1] According to Mithi, destroying the drugs
aims to curb the illegal sale and trafficking of cannabis in the district. [@1]
