A 36-year-old man from Mechelen appeared in the correctional court charged with
possession of narcotics after police found hashish on him and cocaine at his
home. [@1]

On 11 November 2025 a police patrol in the Bruul in Mechelen noticed him on an
electric scooter, and the patrol followed him because he looked around
repeatedly and was known from earlier offences. [@1] Officers stopped him in the
Stationsstraat, where he took back his phone and left a voice message in Berber
that appeared to be meant for his brother. [@1] He had almost 5 grams of hashish
on him, and a house search found 42 grams of hashish, about 4,000 euros in cash
and two user amounts of cocaine. [@1] A drugs dog deployed in the search reacted
at the toilet sink, strengthening the suspicion that drugs had been flushed
away. [@1]

The prosecutor noted that the cocaine lay on the floor next to the toys of the
man's 6-year-old daughter, though the child had not touched the drugs. [@1] The
man said he had bought the hashish in Molenbeek and gave some away occasionally
in a café. [@1] He said he did not know where the cocaine came from and thought
it might belong to a woman friend who visited him sometimes. [@1]

He said the cash came from undeclared work as a roofer, and the prosecutor said
he had since made progress and asked for a one-year suspended prison sentence.
[@1] His lawyer, Thibaud Delva, said his client was ashamed and regretted what
had happened, and asked for a community-service sentence. [@1] The court will
give its judgment on 7 December. [@1]
