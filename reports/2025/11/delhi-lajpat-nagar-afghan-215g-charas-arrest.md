An Afghan national was arrested with more than 215 grams of charas in New
Delhi's Lajpat Nagar area. [@1] The accused was identified as Najib Ahmed, who
allegedly came to India in 2014 and stayed on after his visa expired in 2015.
[@1] Police said he had been supplying drugs in parts of south and southeast
Delhi for some time and had no previous criminal record. [@2] The Statesman
identified the accused as 40-year-old Najib Ahmad living in the Sant Nagar area
of East of Kailash. [@3]

Police said they received information that a foreign national was actively
supplying charas in the Lajpat Nagar area. [@1] Acting on the tip-off, police
formed a raiding team and laid a trap at the identified location on November 21,
where the suspect arrived on a scooter and was apprehended. [@2] A search led to
the recovery of 215.74 grams of high-quality charas. [@2] The seized charas was
described as a cannabis-based narcotic. [@3] Police also seized a scooty
allegedly used for the transportation and supply of drugs. [@3] An FIR was
registered in connection with the seizure under relevant sections of the
Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act. [@2] Police said further
investigation is in progress to trace the source of the narcotics and identify
potential associates. [@3]
