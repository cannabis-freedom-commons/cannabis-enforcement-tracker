Revenue officers seized 204.04 kg of suspected cannabis resin, with an estimated value of €1,224,240, at Rosslare Europort on 27 November 2025. [@2] The drugs were found when an accompanied freight unit that had disembarked a ferry from France was stopped, scanned and searched following routine profiling, with the assistance of Revenue detection dog Mollaidh. [@2]

A man in his 40s was arrested by Gardaí and detained for questioning, under section 2 of the Criminal Justice (Drug Trafficking) Act 1996, at a Garda station in the South East region. [@1,2] Gardaí said the seizure followed a search of a vehicle that had disembarked from a ferry the previous day, and that investigations were ongoing. [@1]
