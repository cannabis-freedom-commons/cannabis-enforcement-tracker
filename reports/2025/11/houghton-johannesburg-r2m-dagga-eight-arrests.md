Johannesburg Metro Police Department officers raided a hijacked residential
property in Houghton, Johannesburg in an intelligence-led operation. [@1] The
Tactical Response Unit, CCTV Crime Analysis and Tracing Team and support units
carried out the raid. [@1] Officers said the illegally occupied house had been
repurposed as a drug packaging and distribution centre. [@2] Police arrested
eight suspects on charges including possession and dealing in narcotics and
illegal occupation of the property. [@2] Those found included tenants and
omalayisha cross-border couriers, and police said each person’s role was still
to be established. [@3]

Inside officers found processed dagga valued at about R2 million, alongside
packaging material allegedly used to prepare consignments. [@3] Officers also
seized a large amount of prescription cough syrup containing codeine, a Schedule
5 controlled substance. [@1]

The consignment had been packaged and prepared for transport to Bulawayo,
Zimbabwe via informal cross-border couriers known as malayisha. [@4] Traffickers
planned to move the drugs via cross-border buses and informal Malayisha delivery
vehicles operating between South Africa and Zimbabwe. [@1] Investigators found
tenants were paying rent into a bank account registered under an unknown name,
raising concerns about organised criminal involvement. [@2] Public Safety MMC Dr
Mgcini Tshwaku praised the operation and said additional raids were planned as
investigations continue. [@3] The city urged the public to report hijacked
buildings or drug activity to the JMPD on 011-758-9650. [@3]
