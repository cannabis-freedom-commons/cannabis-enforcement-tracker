Bella May Culley was 18 when she was arrested in May 2025 at Tbilisi International Airport with about 12 kilograms of herbal cannabis and 2 kilograms of hashish in her luggage. [@1,3,4] Culley was from County Durham in England, and her family had reported her missing in Thailand before she was found in Georgia. [@2,3] She faced a possible life sentence, and a criminologist writing about her case noted reports that she could receive up to 20 years or life. [@1,2,3]

Culley told the court she had been tortured and forced to carry the drugs. [@2,4] In July the court kept her in pre-trial detention after her lawyer offered 50,000 lari in an early plea proposal. [@2]

On 3 November 2025, Judge Giorgi Gelashvili of Tbilisi City Court found Culley guilty under a plea agreement. [@1,4] The court fined her 500,000 lari and sentenced her to five months and 24 days in prison, which she had already served at the Rustavi women's prison. [@1,4] Culley, who was 19 and pregnant at the time, was released. [@1,4]
