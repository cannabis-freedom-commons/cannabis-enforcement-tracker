Law enforcement authorities intercepted nearly EC$1 million worth of illegal
drugs at Port Castries on Saturday, November 15, uncovering compressed foreign
cannabis and cannabis resin concealed in barrels. [@1] Officers recovered 22.76
kilograms of compressed cannabis valued at $500,000 from Shed 7. [@1] Officers
recovered an additional 4.32 kilograms of cannabis and resin worth $400,000 from
Shed 6. [@1]

All recovered items were photographed, tested and secured. [@1] The operation
was carried out by the Police Gangs, Narcotics, and Firearms Unit and the
Customs and Excise Department. [@1] Inspector Shervon Matthieu, the officer in
charge of the unit, said investigations were continuing into the consignees
linked to the intercepted barrels. [@1]

A second account confirmed a joint operation at Port Castries on Saturday,
November 15 that uncovered a substantial quantity of narcotics. [@2] That
account said the operation involved Customs, the Gangs and Narcotics Unit and
Saint Lucia Air and Sea Ports Authority personnel. [@2] That account quoted a
customs officer describing a large find in several barrels and an apparent
deliberate attempt to move illegal substances through standard cargo channels.
[@2] It placed the find in a broader sustained crackdown on the illegal
importation of drugs, firearms and contraband through Port Castries, Port Vieux
Fort and courier services. [@2]
