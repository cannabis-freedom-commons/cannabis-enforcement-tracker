The Karkh Criminal Court in Baghdad sentenced two convicted drug traffickers to
death after convicting them of drug trafficking, Iraq's judiciary said in a
statement. [@1]

The two convicts were caught with 102 kilograms of Captagon and an additional 96
kilograms of hashish, which the court said they intended to trade and sell among
users. [@1] The Captagon is excluded from the cannabis figures, and only the
separately stated 96 kilograms of hashish is recorded. [@1]

The ruling came as Baghdad continued a crackdown on the country's drug trade.
[@1] According to The New Region, Iraq has evolved from a transit route into a
significant consumption market, with extensive borders with Syria, Iran, Saudi
Arabia and Kuwait. [@1] Iraqi authorities arrested around 14,500 suspects on
drug-related charges in 2024 and issued death sentences for 144 suspected drug
traffickers, official data cited by the outlet showed. [@1] In the first three
months of 2025, authorities detained at least 1,365 suspected Iraqi traffickers
and 194 foreign nationals, according to the General Directorate of Narcotics and
Psychotropic Substances. [@1]
