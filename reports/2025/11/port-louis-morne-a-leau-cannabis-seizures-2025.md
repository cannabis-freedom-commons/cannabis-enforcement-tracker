At the prosecutor's request, the Le Moule gendarmerie company carried out a
series of anti-crime operations in Port-Louis and Morne-à-l'Eau, two communes in
northern Grande-Terre, Guadeloupe. [@1] The controls took place during the week
before 25 November 2025 and were targeted at illicit trafficking in northern
Grande-Terre. [@2]

The deployment brought together the Le Moule PSIG and DSI units (the Gendarmerie
Surveillance and Intervention Platoon and the Surveillance and Intervention
Detachment) plus a canine team from Pointe-à-Pitre, in a coordinated
arrangement. [@1]

Officers found several packets of cannabis and cocaine, alongside a replica of a
Glock-type pistol. [@1]

The gendarmerie said the operations showed its determination to fight drug sales
and the circulation of weapons in Guadeloupe. [@2] Guadeloupe La 1ère also
reported that simple roadside checks the previous weekend led to four motorists
being stopped for use, possession and transport of narcotics, and nine for
driving under the influence of drugs, all of whom had their licences withdrawn.
[@2]
