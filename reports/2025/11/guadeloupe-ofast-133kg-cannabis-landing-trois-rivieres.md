Officers of the Guadeloupe detachment of OFAST, France's anti-narcotics office,
backed by the territorial judicial police, stopped a drugs landing on the
island's south coast on the night of 2 to 3 November 2025. [@2] They arrested
ten people. [@3] Three vehicles carrying the bales were stopped in the act in
Trois-Rivières and Capesterre-Belle-Eau. [@3]

The police said the haul was 131.061 kg of cannabis herb, 2.44 kg of cannabis
resin and 1 kg of cocaine, 134.5 kg of drugs in all. [@2] Surveillance had
identified an inter-island import route between Dominica and Guadeloupe that
also carried people in an irregular situation. [@2] Searches after the arrests
found ammunition of several calibres, drugs packaged for resale and a satellite
phone. [@2]

Three of those arrested, from Saint-Martin, Dominica and Guadeloupe, were
brought before the Pointe-à-Pitre prosecutor and appeared in immediate
proceedings on 7 November; that hearing was postponed until December. [@2] Two
others were sentenced to six months in prison with a five-year ban from French
territory. [@2] Four more were placed in an administrative detention centre, and
one person's status was still being settled. [@2] France-Antilles, covering the
Pointe-à-Pitre correctional court on 10 November, reported that twelve people
had been arrested while 54 kg of cannabis herb was unloaded on a Trois-Rivières
beach, and that only three would be tried later because the case had been
adjourned. [@1] A television report said one of those three asked for time to
prepare a defence, so the case was put off to 12 December, and that the three
remained in pre-trial detention. [@4]
