The Federal High Court sitting in Benin City, presided over by Justice
Babatunde Olaide Quadiri and Justice (Prof) Chuka Austine Obiozor, sentenced
eleven drug offenders, nine men and two women, to a combined 79 years in
prison with hard labour. [@1,2] NDLEA Edo State Commander Mitchell Ofoyeju
announced the October convictions in a statement on Thursday 6 November
2025. [@1,2]

Among the standout cases was wanted drug baron Alaba Monday, 49, caught with
115 kilograms of cannabis, who received 15 years with a N3 million fine.
[@1,2] Cannabis cultivator Abel Edah, found with 5 kilograms and a 0.678
hectare farm, received a concurrent two-year sentence on each count with a
N5 million fine option per count. [@1,2]

Samuel Samson and Daniel Peter, sentenced for unlawful possession and
cultivation of cannabis sativa, each received two years or a N5 million fine
option. [@1,2] Emmanuel Nwosu received 15 years with a N750,000 fine option
for possessing 276 grams of cannabis, while Atari Israel received two years
with a N2.5 million fine for possessing and transporting 9 kilograms of
cannabis, and the court ordered the forfeiture of his navy-blue Audi station
wagon. [@1,2]
