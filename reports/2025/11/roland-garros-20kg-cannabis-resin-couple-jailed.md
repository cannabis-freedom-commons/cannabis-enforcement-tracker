The Saint-Denis judicial court tried the defendants by immediate appearance on
Wednesday 26 November 2025. [@1] A couple living in Saint-Nazaire were among the
suspects. [@1] The woman was sentenced to two years of firm prison and her
husband to three years of firm prison. [@1] The two other defendants were
sentenced to thirty-six months of prison with probationary suspension. [@1] The
four convicted people must jointly pay a 302,700-euro customs fine. [@2] The
couple's infant, a few months old, remains placed with child-welfare services.
[@1] The mother travelled with her three-month-old baby, who was entrusted to
child welfare. [@2]

On 2 October 2025 a joint operation at Roland Garros Airport led to four people
being detained. [@1] Officers seized 20.1 kg of cannabis resin in the operation.
[@3] Police, gendarmerie and customs officers checked more than 500 passengers
on flights AF648 and AF652. [@2] Customs estimated the market value of the resin
at 302,700 euros. [@2]

The four defendants were tried for drug trafficking and criminal association
before the Saint-Denis correctional court. [@2] The prosecutor had requested
three years in prison for the woman and her husband. [@3] The husband's lawyer
called the penalty acceptable. [@1] He added that cannabis is not cocaine and
that three years for commissioning a 20 kg resin import did not seem excessive.
[@1]
