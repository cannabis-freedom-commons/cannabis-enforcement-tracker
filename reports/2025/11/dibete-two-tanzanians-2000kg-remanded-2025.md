Police at the Dibete roadblock found 69 bundles of suspected dagga weighing more
than 2,000 kilos inside a VW panel van on 17 November 2025. [@1] Two Tanzanian
nationals travelling in the van, Robert Manumbu, 28, and Meshack Anthony
Kalinga, 29, were arrested in what police called a major drug bust. [@1]

The two men appeared before the Mahalapye Magistrate's Court on 19 November on
a single count of trafficking in narcotic drugs and were remanded in custody as
investigations continue. [@1] They are due back in court on 11 December. [@1]

Botswana Police Service spokesperson Assistant Commissioner Near Bagali said the
arrest was part of intensified efforts under Operation Powerless, a
multi-agency collaboration between the police, the Botswana Defence Force and
other law enforcement partners. [@1]
