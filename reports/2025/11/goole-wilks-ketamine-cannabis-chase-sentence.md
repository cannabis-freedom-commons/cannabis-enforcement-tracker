Jack Wilks, 21, of Kelleythorpe near Driffield, East Yorkshire, was jailed for
19 months at Hull Crown Court. [@2] He was arrested on 25 November 2025 after
failing to stop for police at Holme-on-Spalding Moor, south of York. [@3] Police
charged him with inflicting grievous bodily harm without intent, dangerous
driving, driving without insurance, driving while disqualified and possession
with intent to supply a Class B controlled drug. [@3]

He drove at 50mph in a 30mph limit, through red lights and on the wrong side of
the road before stopping in a dead end in Goole. [@1] In the car police found
108 grams of ketamine worth £2,160 and cannabis valued by police at £225, the
court heard. [@2] A police sergeant was left with a 3cm wound near her right
eyebrow that needed 10 stitches, and permanent facial scarring. [@2]

Wilks admitted wounding the officer, dangerous driving, driving while
disqualified, having no insurance and possessing ketamine and cannabis, pleading
guilty on the day of a scheduled trial. [@2] He was banned from driving for
three years and must pass an extended retest before he can drive legally again.
