Golby pleaded guilty to possession with intent to supply cocaine and cannabis
and possession of criminal cash. [@1] Deemster Graeme Cook jailed him for seven
years. [@1] He was given six months in jail for each offence of possessing
cannabis with intent to supply and possession of criminal cash which will run
concurrently. [@2] Golby, 43, appeared for sentencing at the Court of General
Gaol Delivery in Douglas on Friday. [@2]

Police raided John Golby's home in Close Moore, Douglas, and found £13,250 of
cocaine on 2 August. [@1] Officers also found £5,656 of cannabis, scales and
about £3,000 in cash stashed above the kitchen hob. [@1] Isle of Man
Constabulary officers executed a search warrant at Golby's address and seized a
paint tub containing drugs. [@2] Police also found a tick list in the bedroom
showing people owed Golby about £8,000. [@2] He was arrested at about 23:20 BST
that night and refused to comment in police interviews the following day. [@1]

His defence said he worked as a painter but was addicted to alcohol, cocaine and
gambling, which had become a vicious cycle for him. [@1] His advocate said he
had worked as a painter and decorator until a car accident a year earlier left
him unable to work and his addiction took over. [@2] He was not high in the
chain, his defence argued, and people would go to his address to pick up the
drugs. [@1] The deemster told Golby he had been dealing before and knew full
well what the consequences would be. [@2]
