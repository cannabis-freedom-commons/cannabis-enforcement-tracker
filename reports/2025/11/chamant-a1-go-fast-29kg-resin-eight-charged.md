Eight men aged 23 to 58 were arrested in an investigation into drug trafficking
and importation, after 241 kg of ecstasy tablets and 29 kg of cannabis were
seized at a toll booth on the A1 motorway in the Oise department. [@1] Police
suspect the group of running a cannabis and ecstasy traffic from Argenteuil, in
the Val-d'Oise, since April, the Pontoise prosecutor's office said. [@1]

The ecstasy consignment came from Belgium and the cannabis from Morocco,
directed at the Paris region. [@3] Officers intercepted the convoy at the
Chamant toll booth, described as the Saint-Chamant toll on the A1, in the night
of 19 to 20 November 2025. [@2] The convoy had a lead car and a second vehicle
carrying the drugs, and about 40 officers were deployed, including the research
and intervention brigades of Versailles and Creil. [@2]

Searches of the suspects' homes produced 29 kg of cannabis resin, which police
valued at 87,000 euros, and 10,845 euros in cash. [@2] Part of the resin was
meant for a dealer held at the Osny remand prison, police suspect, reportedly
brought in with the help of an employee of an outside company. [@2] The eight
suspects were held at the Cergy police station and brought before the Pontoise
prosecutor's office, and seven were placed in pre-trial detention. [@2]
