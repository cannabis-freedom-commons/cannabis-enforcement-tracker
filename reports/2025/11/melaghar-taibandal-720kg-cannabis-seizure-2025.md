Acting on a tip-off, a joint team of Melaghar police station personnel, the Tripura State Rifles and the Special Task Force raided the forest area of a Forest Department rubber plantation at Taibandal Gamai Chhara on 9 November 2025. [@1,2] The team found large drums and packets buried underground which, on inspection, held dry cannabis. [@1,2]

The seized cannabis was brought to Melaghar police station, where it was weighed at 720 kilograms. [@1,2] Local people praised the police for the operation against the drug trade. [@1,2] Neither outlet reported any arrest or named any suspect. [@1,2]
