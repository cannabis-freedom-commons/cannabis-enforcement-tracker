Gendarmes searched two properties in the cities of San Francisco, Córdoba, and
Frontera, Santa Fe. [@1] One citizen was detained. [@1] Members of the San
Francisco complex-crimes investigations unit carried out two raids in private
homes investigated in a case under drug law 23.737. [@1] The San Francisco
anti-narcotics prosecution office issued the judicial orders for the raids. [@1]

Officers seized a total of 23 marijuana plants of different sizes. [@1] Officers
seized 16 grams of shredded cannabis and 4 grams of cannabis buds, along with
lamps, mobile devices, chemical products and relevant documents. [@1]
Investigators found that marijuana plants were grown with chemical products
intended to aid growth and secure harvests. [@1] The cannabis was then offered
through social networks and sold retail. [@1]

The courts ordered that the person involved remain detained. [@1] The operation
was supported by the Rafaela investigations unit, the Rafaela road-security
squadron and the San Francisco road-security section. [@1]

In a separate road check at Santa Elena, Tulumba, officers inspected a transport
from Orán, Salta, bound for San Luis. [@1] That check found a bag with 6 kilos
of coca leaves and 8 foreign tires without legal backing. [@1]
