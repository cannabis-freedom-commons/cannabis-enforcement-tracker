Zenhastel Odel Hector Jr., a resident of Half-Moon Heights in Frigate Bay, St
Kitts, was arrested and charged with multiple offences relating to the
importation and possession of cannabis. [@1] On 22 September 2025, officers from
the Anti-Narcotics Unit of the Royal St. Christopher and Nevis Police Force
responded to a report from the St. Kitts and Nevis Customs and Excise Department
at Robert L. Bradshaw International Airport regarding a suspected drug
importation. [@1] Upon investigation, officers discovered six vacuum-sealed
packages containing vegetable material suspected to be cannabis inside Hector's
luggage. [@1] The suspected cannabis was processed by the Crime Scene Unit,
taken into police custody, and confirmed as cannabis shortly thereafter. [@1]

On 9 October 2025, police charged Hector with importation of cannabis and
possession of cannabis with intent to supply. [@1] The St. Kitts and Nevis
Customs and Excise Department additionally charged him under customs regulations
with presenting a false declaration, importation of cannabis, and intent to
deceive a customs officer. [@1] The police force said it continues to work
jointly with national security agencies to combat the illegal importation and
distribution of controlled substances across the federation. [@2]
