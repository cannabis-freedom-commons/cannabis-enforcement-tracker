Military police destroyed a cannabis plantation of about 12,000 plants on the
morning of Tuesday 21 October 2025 in indigenous land in Pesqueira, in
Pernambuco's Agreste region. [@1] The plantation covered about two hectares.
[@1]

According to the Military Police, investigations by the 8th Independent Military
Police Company (8ª CIPM) showed that a crop near Aldeia Lagoa was growing the
drug. [@1] Officers who went to the site were met with gunfire from men guarding
the plantation and returned fire. [@1] The suspects escaped through the
vegetation in the exchange of fire and were not caught. [@1] Police found a
further 92 kilograms of cannabis already harvested and ready for consumption,
stored in eleven plastic sacks. [@1] The crop was burned on the land itself,
except for a small sample kept for presentation at the Civil Police station.
[@1]

Diario de Pernambuco reported that officers of the 8th CIPM went to the site and
found a place where the drug was grown and sold. [@2] Diario de Pernambuco
reported that the crop was being grown in the indigenous area of the
municipality, near Aldeia Lagoa. [@2] Portal Agreste Violento reported that
surveillance, operations, rural patrol and GATI teams took part; GATI is the
tactical motorised support group of the Pernambuco Military Police. [@3] Portal
Agreste Violento reported that the Civil Police in Pesqueira will investigate
the origin of the crop and try to identify those responsible. [@3]
