In a joint operation, officers of the State Investigation Agency (AEI) and
personnel of the Mexican Army destroyed three marijuana plantings in Urique
municipality. [@1] The three plots, with areas of 800, 500 and 300 square
metres, were found some 300 metres from the paved road at the 45+800-kilometre
mark of the road that leads to Las Estrellas in Ocampo municipality. [@1]

The plants, which had been cut and destroyed by incineration, were more than one
metre tall; the site was documented with geographic coordinates and a photo
series for the corresponding investigation. [@1] The Western District Attorney's
Office reiterated its commitment to work for society in the investigation and
prosecution of crimes in coordination with other police forces and government
institutions. [@1]

At the same time, in Urique municipality, State Investigation Agency officers
and the Mexican Army destroyed three marijuana plantings by incineration, with
areas of 800, 500 and 300 square metres respectively. [@2]

Both operations were carried out in response to citizen complaints about the
presence of illegal drug plantings in these regions of the state. [@2]
