Gendarmería Nacional officers of the Malargüe Squadron 29 searched two homes, in
Malargüe and in San Rafael, under a federal case for suspected infringement of
Argentina's drug law, Law 23.737. [@1]

In the two homes the officers seized 387 marijuana seedlings of different sizes,
721 grams of cannabis sativa buds and crushed leaf, 10,000 seeds, two precision
scales, pipes, mobile phones and documents. [@1] Two people were arrested in the
house searches, and the case is before the San Rafael decentralised prosecutors'
office and Federal Court No. 1 of San Rafael. [@1]

A second operation on a secondary road near Paraje La Junta stopped a rented
vehicle carrying a driver, a woman and three minors, and the sniffer dog Mambo
indicated six bags of a brown substance. [@1] Officers seized 2 kilograms 21
grams of marijuana from the vehicle and detained the two adults, while the three
minors were handed over to relatives under a written record. [@1] On a passenger
bus, two packages found under the seats held another 202.5 grams of cannabis
sativa. [@3]

Ser y Hacer de Malargüe said one of the two searched homes was in Malargüe town
and the other at Paraje La Junta. [@2]
