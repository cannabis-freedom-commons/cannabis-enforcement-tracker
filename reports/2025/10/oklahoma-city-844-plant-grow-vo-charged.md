On 7 October 2025, investigators served a search warrant at a marijuana grow in
the 3700 block of South Bryant Avenue in Oklahoma City. [@1] Following that
search, Dung Tuan Vo was charged with one count of cultivation of marijuana in
connection with the Bryant Avenue grow. [@1] He was also charged with one count
of use of a firearm while committing a felony. [@1]

According to the probable cause affidavit, officers discovered 844 marijuana
plants in various stages of growth during the search of the property. [@1]
Agents with the Oklahoma Bureau of Narcotics took a sample of the plants from
the property for examination. [@1] Testing of that sample confirmed the plants
were cannabis, which authorities describe as a Schedule I substance. [@1]
Authorities said Vo had illegally grown the marijuana at the Oklahoma City
property. [@1]
