On the night of Tuesday 14 October 2025, police stopped a 26-year-old man in an
alcohol check in Escaldes-Engordany and found several small wraps of cocaine in
his vehicle, along with more than 500 euros in mostly 60-euro bundles. [@1,2]
Officers said they already suspected he might be involved in drug dealing. [@1,2]

After he agreed to a home search, a canine team found three sealed packages
testing positive for cocaine, scales, dozens of small bags used for dosing and
21.7 g of cannabis and 10.4 g of hashish in the flat. [@1,2] Police said he was
in an irregular situation in Andorra and had tested positive for drugs at the
roadside, adding a collective-security charge to the public-health case. [@1,2]

He was handed to a judge on the morning of 16 October 2025. [@2]
