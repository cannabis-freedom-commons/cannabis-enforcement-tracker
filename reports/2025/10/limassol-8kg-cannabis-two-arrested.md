Two men aged 35 and 26 were arrested on Thursday afternoon in Limassol after
eight kilos of cannabis were found in their possession. [@2] Police observed the
35-year-old parking outside the younger man's home, where he received a nylon
bag containing 3.4 kilograms of cannabis. [@1] Officers also found a grinder,
two mobile phones, and a hand-rolled joint in his car. [@1] He was arrested
immediately. [@1]

Shortly after, the 26-year-old was stopped while carrying two more nylon bags.
[@1] Inside, officers discovered 4.5 kilograms of cannabis, a mobile phone, and
two boxes of disposable gloves. [@1] Police stopped the older man and carried
out a check of the car, which revealed 3.390kg of cannabis. [@2] The man was
stopped by the police who carried out a search and found the nylon bags
contained 4.520kg of cannabis. [@2] Police found seven grammes of cannabis in
the 35-year-old's home, and from the 26-year-old's home they seized two
additional mobile phones. [@2]

Police said all items seized as evidence will undergo further examination as
investigations continue. [@2]
