Laurel County sheriff's deputies arrested two adults on Saturday night, 4
October 2025, after being sent to a drug overdose complaint involving two
juveniles at a home on Robert E. Cox Road, about nine miles south of London,
Kentucky. [@1] Tammy Lynn Cundiff, 30, and Brian Taylor Smith, 29, were arrested
and lodged in the Laurel County Correctional Center. [@1] Cundiff is the parent
of the two children involved. [@1]

Deputies said the two adults made brownies with marijuana and brownies without
marijuana, and that the children ate the brownies containing marijuana. [@1] The
children's father took them to Baptist Health Hospital in Corbin for treatment
of a possible overdose. [@1] Social services was notified and conducted an
investigation at the scene. [@1] Cundiff was charged with public intoxication
involving controlled substances and two counts of endangering the welfare of a
minor. [@1] Smith was charged with public intoxication involving controlled
substances, two counts of endangering the welfare of a minor and possession of
drug paraphernalia. [@1]

A FOX 56 account, republished by Yahoo News, also reported that two people were
arrested after the overdose of two juveniles and that both were charged with
public intoxication involving controlled substances and two counts of
endangering the welfare of a minor. [@2]
