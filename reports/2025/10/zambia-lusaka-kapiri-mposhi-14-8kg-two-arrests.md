Zambia's Drug Enforcement Commission arrested two women in separate operations
for trafficking more than 14 kilogrammes of cannabis. [@1] DEC public relations
officer Allan Tamba said the two suspects were Catherine Musonda, aged 41, held
for 8.6 kilogrammes and Rhoda Chifwaila, aged 52, held for 6.2 kilogrammes of
cannabis. [@1] In Lusaka District in Lusaka Province, the Commission formally
charged and arrested 41-year-old Catherine Musonda. [@1] Her arrest was in
connection with trafficking 8.6 kilogrammes of compressed cannabis. [@1]

In a separate operation in Mutanuka area of Kapiri Mposhi District in Central
Province, 52-year-old Rhoda Chifwaila was arrested for trafficking 6.2
kilogrammes of dry loose cannabis. [@2] Together the two operations accounted
for a total of 14.8 kilogrammes of cannabis in Lusaka and Kapiri Mposhi
districts. [@2] The drugs were seized and both suspects were detained in lawful
custody to appear before competent courts. [@2]

Tamba reminded the public that cannabis contains potent elements which, when
abused, can cause serious mental disorders, memory and attention deficits,
depression and cardiovascular ailments. [@2] He urged the public to keep
reporting drug trafficking so collaborative prevention, detection and
prosecution can help create resilient, healthier and safer communities for all.
[@2]
