Officers arrested a 41-year-old suspect at the house, and he has been in
pretrial detention since being brought before the investigating judge. [@1] The
Trier police press spokesman, Marc Fleischmann, confirmed that investigators had
shut down a commercial cannabis plantation. [@3] Fleischmann said officers found
several hundred cannabis plants in various stages of growth in the house. [@2]

The newspaper Trierischer Volksfreund reported that around 700 cannabis plants
were seized in the house. [@1] The village lies about five kilometres as the
crow flies from the Luxembourg border, near Grevenmacher. [@2] The house stands
in the middle of the village, and residents had considered it suspicious for
some time. [@2] The suspect was said to have lived quietly and to have had
little contact with the neighbourhood. [@2]
