Burkina Faso's national gendarmerie dismantled a local cannabis production and
sales network operating in several villages of Guiaro department, in Nahouri
province in the Centre-Sud region. [@1] The Guiaro territorial brigade acted
after residents reported smokers and cannabis crops in remote areas. [@1]

Investigators arrested a grower identified only as O. in Boli village at 21h26
on 8 October 2025 for production, sale and consumption of cannabis. [@1] A
second man identified only as I. was arrested on 11 October for sale and
consumption. [@1] A third man identified only as Z., who lived in Koumbili, was
arrested on 12 October for importation, cultivation, sale and consumption of
cannabis. [@1] Three people were arrested between 8 and 12 October 2025 in the
villages of Boli and Koumbili. [@3]

All three men admitted the facts, saying they grew and resold the cannabis for
local consumption. [@2] On the instructions of the Faso prosecutor at the Pô
High Court, gendarmes destroyed the identified fields. [@2] The gendarmerie said
cannabis use was taking insidious root in Guiaro commune in the south of the
country. [@1]

Investigations are continuing to identify possible accomplices and assess the
scale of the phenomenon. [@2] The first investigation in Boli village followed
information judged credible. [@1] The gendarmerie said the offences are punished
under article 382-1 of Burkina Faso's criminal code and invited citizens to
report suspicious behaviour on its hotlines. [@2]
