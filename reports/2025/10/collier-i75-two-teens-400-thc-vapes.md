Two 17-year-olds were arrested early Tuesday morning after Florida Highway
Patrol troopers found 400 THC vape cartridges during a traffic stop on
Interstate 75 near Immokalee Road in Collier County, Florida. [@1] Troopers said
the teens were driving an Audi SUV at 85 miles per hour near mile marker 114 on
Interstate 75 when they were pulled over for speeding just before 3 a.m. [@1]

During the stop, officers noticed several brightly colored boxes in the car,
which turned out to contain hundreds of THC vapes. [@1] Troopers counted 400 THC
vape cartridges in the boxes. [@1] THC, short for tetrahydrocannabinol, is the
main intoxicating compound in cannabis, and the cartridges are concentrated
cannabis oil rather than dried flower or plants. [@1] Both teens were booked
into the Lee County Jail. [@1]

The Florida Highway Patrol warned parents to stay alert, noting that many THC
vapes are packaged to look like candy, posing a risk to children and teens. [@1]
