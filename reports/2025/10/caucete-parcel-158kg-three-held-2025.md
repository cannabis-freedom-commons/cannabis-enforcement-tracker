Gendarmería Nacional officers in San Juan arrested three people as they
collected a parcel that held 15 kilos 800 grams of marijuana. [@1] The arrests
formed part of an investigation into a criminal organisation that transported
and sold drugs from Misiones to other Argentine provinces. [@1] Officers of the
force's San Juan unit for complex crimes and judicial proceedings waited in
place as the three arrived to take delivery. [@1] The parcel's stated
destination was Caucete department, east of the provincial capital. [@1] The
suspects came to a shipping company in the San Juan provincial capital to
collect the package, whose final destination was Caucete department. [@4]

Tiempo de San Juan reported that the parcel came from Liniers, Buenos Aires,
declared as costume jewellery and holding four pressed bundles of marijuana.
[@2]

In front of witnesses officers seized 15 kilos 800 grams of cannabis sativa,
62,000 pesos in cash, four mobile phones used for the criminal operations and a
car. [@1] The Federal Prosecutor's Office in San Juan directed that all the
items be seized and that the three people be held on suspicion of breaching
Argentina's drug law, Ley 23.737. [@1] The operation was coordinated by the
force's units in Eldorado, Misiones, where the organisation's shipments began,
and in San Juan. [@1] Radio 3 Cadena Patagonia repeated the agency's account of
three detainees, 15.8 kilos of cannabis sativa, 62,000 pesos, four phones and a
car. [@3]
