A joint intelligence-led operation involving the Transnational Organised Crime
Unit, Trinidad and Tobago's Vetted Unit and South Western Division police took
place in Palo Seco. [@1] The operation followed the arrest on Tuesday of a key
associate of a drug-trafficking network, after which officers kept a remote area
along Quarry Road under surveillance. [@1] Authorities said the network planned
to move remaining drugs from a hidden stash to avoid detection, so surveillance
teams stayed in place overnight before launching a coordinated search and
lockdown on Wednesday. [@1] Officers found the drugs at a concealed stash site
after overnight surveillance of the remote area. [@2]

The search produced 1,177.4 kilogrammes of Colombian creepy marijuana packed in
41 crocus bags, which police valued at $129,216,117. [@1] Officers also found
117.14 kilogrammes of cocaine packed in five crocus bags, valued at
TT$163,283,788.80. [@2] Police put the combined value of the narcotics seized on
1 October at approximately $292,499,906. [@1] With Tuesday's seizure included,
police put the two-day total at TT$314,666,605 worth of illegal drugs. [@1]
Officers transported the narcotics to Siparia Police Station as investigations
continued. [@1]

Police described the seizure as a major blow to a prominent drug-trafficking
network operating in the South Western Division. [@1] The Ministry of Homeland
Security commended the Vetted Unit and supporting officers and said the
operation demonstrated the Government's commitment to dismantling organized
criminal networks. [@2]
