A Mauritanian customs unit at the Rosso border post seized a large quantity of
cannabis from traffickers. [@1] The customs service announced the operation on
its official Facebook page. [@1] Customs said the seizure reflected the constant
vigilance and professional dedication of its officers posted at border posts.
[@1]

A video released by customs showed the cannabis rolled up and hidden in plastic
baskets. [@1]

MADAR, a Nouakchott-based news agency, carried the same customs Facebook
announcement. [@2] MADAR described the haul as Indian cannabis and said customs
qualified it as considerable. [@2]
