A woman from La Croix was arrested at Port Castries on 29 October 2025 when
officers inspecting a shipment found 5.38 kilograms of suspected cannabis. [@1]
She had arrived at the port to clear the shipment, and officers opened a box in
her presence and found six packages of suspected cannabis concealed inside. [@1]
The Royal Saint Lucia Police Force put the street value of the cannabis at
EC$161,000. [@1] Police said the interception was part of an intelligence-led
operation on 29 October 2025. [@2]

When she appeared before the First District Court on 3 November 2025 she was
charged with possession of cannabis, possession with intent to supply and
importation of a controlled substance, and was granted EC$14,000 bail. [@2] Her
bail was set at EC$14,000 cash or suitable surety and she is due back in court
on 9 December 2025. [@2]

Inspector Shervon Mathieu, head of the police Gangs, Narcotics and Firearms
Unit, said the force remained committed to intercepting narcotics destined for
Saint Lucian communities and bringing suspects before the courts. [@1] The case
was one of a series of interceptions at the island's ports in the weeks before,
part of what police described as intelligence-led efforts against organised
crime and border security. [@1]
