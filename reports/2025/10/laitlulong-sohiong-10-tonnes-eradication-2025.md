A multi-force team destroyed about 10 tonnes of illegally cultivated cannabis spread across five hectares at Laitlulong village in the Sohiong area of East Khasi Hills district. [@1,2] The joint operation brought together the district police, the Forest Department, the Excise Department and the state Anti-Narcotics Task Force, who systematically uprooted cannabis from the whole area. [@1,2]

Police sources said the five-hectare plantation belonged to several households and stressed that the cultivation was illegal. [@2] No arrests were reported. [@1,2]
