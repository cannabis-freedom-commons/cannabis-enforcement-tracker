Officers of the counter-narcotics unit of the Kostanay regional police
department detained a 47-year-old man in a garage complex in Kostanay on
suspicion of storing narcotics on an especially large scale. [@1] The detention
was carried out as part of the Karasora-2025 operational and preventive
campaign. [@2] The detained man was a local resident of Kostanay. [@2] During a
search of the detained man, officers found and seized wraps containing green
plant material with a distinctive smell. [@1] Police found more of the same
substance when they searched the garage where the suspect was staying. [@1]

A medical examination found traces of narcotic use in the man's body. [@1] A
forensic chemical examination identified the seized substance as hashish with a
total weight of about 1 kilogram. [@1] The combined weight covered the wraps
taken from the man and the substance found in the garage. [@2] Police opened a
criminal case for storing narcotics on an especially large scale, and further
investigative measures were under way. [@2]
