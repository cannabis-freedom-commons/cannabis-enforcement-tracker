Police in Kamsar, in Guinea's Boké region, seized a bale of Indian hemp weighing
62 kg during a routine patrol on Monday 13 October 2025. [@1] The patrol was in
the Bagataye sector of the Kamsar-centre district, in the rural commune of
Kamsar. [@2] The detachment was the Special Intervention and Protection Brigade
(BSIP), a unit of the central police station of Kamsar. [@3]

A woman was holding the bale, which police said was about to be put into
circulation, and officers arrested her on the spot. [@1] The National Police
Directorate said she was to be brought before the competent judicial authorities
on Tuesday 14 October 2025. [@1] The police gave no name for the suspect and
published no estimate of the bale's street value. [@4]

Another account of the police statement, published a day later, put her
appearance before the courts on Wednesday 15 October 2025. [@3] Guinéenews
described the seizure as part of continued efforts to slow the spread of drug
trafficking in the region. [@2]
