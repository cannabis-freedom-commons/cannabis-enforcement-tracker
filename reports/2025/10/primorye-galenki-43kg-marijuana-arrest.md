Detectives of the criminal investigation unit of the Oktyabrsky municipal
district police department acted on a tip that a 59-year-old resident of Galenki
village in Primorsky Krai might be involved in trafficking plant-based drugs.
[@1] The tip was confirmed during undercover police operations. [@1]

Officers searched the man's homestead and found and seized more than 43
kilograms of marijuana. [@1] The man gave a confession and said he had gathered
and dried Manchu hemp himself, knowing that cannabis is banned in Russia. [@1]

An investigator of the Oktyabrsky district police department opened a criminal
case under part 2 of article 228 of the Russian Criminal Code, and the
investigation was continuing. [@1] Izvestia, citing the press service of the
regional interior ministry, reported that laboratory testing confirmed the
substance was cannabis. [@2] The regional police statement said the case
concerned storage of drugs on an especially large scale and that the charge
carries up to 10 years in prison. [@2]

Все новости, reprinting aif.ru, reported that the seized substance had a total
mass of more than 43 kilograms and that the man said he had gathered and dried
the wild Manchu hemp himself. [@3]
