National Guard members and Mexican Army soldiers on security and
crime-prevention patrols in the municipality of Tamazula de Gordiano, Jalisco,
spotted five cultivated plots of marijuana plants of different heights. [@1] The
five plots together covered 14,600 square metres, about 1.46 hectares. [@2]

The troops secured the area and mounted a search in the surroundings without
locating anyone responsible. [@1] No arrests were reported over the plots. [@1]

The troops told the federal public prosecutor's office in Jalisco about the find
and destroyed the plants, described as possibly marijuana, by manual labour and
burning. [@1] The National Guard said the operation was carried out under the
Mexican government's National Security Strategy. [@1] Reforma reported from
Guadalajara that the five cultivated plots in Tamazula de Gordiano were found by
National Guard and army personnel. [@3] Nayarit Noticias republished the
National Guard's statement, crediting the force's press office, on 16 October
2025. [@2]
