Adamawa State NDLEA Commander Aliyu Abubakar, presenting the command's
September 2025 scorecard at its Yola headquarters, said operatives intercepted
two siblings moving 500 blocks of compressed cannabis sativa from Akure toward
Mubi when their Honda Pilot was stopped in Lamurde on an intelligence report.
[@1,2] A search of the dealers' premises in Mubi recovered 40 more blocks.
[@1,2]

For the month, the command said it arrested 58 drug suspects, 56 men and two
women, and seized 366.808 kg of cannabis sativa alongside 209.8 kg of
psychotropic substances and 11.6 kg of methamphetamine and other drugs.
[@1,2] Operatives also confiscated four vehicles and a motorcycle said to have
transported the illicit substances. [@1,2]

In Mijilu village in Mubi North Local Government Area, officers destroyed a
six-hectare cannabis farm and arrested five suspects connected to it. [@1,2]
