Three teenagers were charged after Ashland police stopped their car and found
marijuana and firearms, including a stolen handgun. [@1] Ashland police stopped
the car at 11:14 p.m. on Friday, 31 October, for driving 95 mph in a 70-mph
zone. [@1] The officer saw several burnt marijuana cigars in the car while
speaking to the driver, and the driver and both passengers were under 21. [@1]

A search of the car found about 35 grams of marijuana, empty bags and scales.
[@1] Searching the driver, Deshaun R. Whitley, 19, of Jefferson City, police
found a Taurus 9 mm semi-automatic pistol reported stolen in Shawnee, Kansas.
[@1] The passenger Tyler E. Wright, 18, of Kansas City, hid a 5.56 caliber AR-15
pistol under clothes on the floorboard. [@1]

Prosecutors charged Whitley with receiving stolen property, delivering 35 grams
or less of marijuana, and misdemeanor speeding by 20 to 25 mph. [@1] They
charged Wright with delivering 35 grams or less of marijuana and unlawfully
possessing or transporting an illegal weapon, and charged the third person in
the car, Caleb K. Duncan, 19, of Independence, with delivering 35 grams or less
of marijuana and misdemeanor underage possession of liquor. [@1] All three were
being held in the Boone County Jail. [@1]
