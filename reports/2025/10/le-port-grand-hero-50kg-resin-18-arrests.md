Gendarmes arrested 18 people on 25 October 2025, 10 of them in Réunion and 8 in
mainland France, in an operation involving more than 250 officers. [@2] On the
afternoon of 26 October 2025, officers seized 50 kilos of cannabis resin aboard
the Grand Hero at Port Est. [@1] Investigators found exactly 497 bars of shit
hidden inside several of the cars carried on the ship. [@1] Investigators
estimated the resin's market value at more than one million euros. [@1] The
gendarmerie's account says the drug was hidden in the tyres of the vehicles.
[@1] Dockers said about 10 kg of resin was hidden inside each car. [@3]

The searches also found 162,640 euros in cash, 12 vehicles, a hydraulic press,
drones, computers, phones, a handgun and luxury goods. [@2]

Seventeen people were brought before the investigating judge from 28 October
2025. [@2] Those arrested in Réunion were placed in pre-trial detention while
those arrested in mainland France were issued an arrest warrant. [@2] Among the
suspects was a docker suspected of acting as a facilitator, who was arriving on
the island by plane when the operation began, and his wife was also questioned.
[@1]

The investigation ran for more than a year, starting from the discovery on 5
October 2024 of 8 kg of MDMA in crystal form hidden in the spare wheel of a new
vehicle landed at Réunion from Le Havre. [@2] A judicial inquiry was opened on
22 May 2025. [@2] A national investigation cell was set up in Réunion on 1
August 2025 to coordinate the work of about ten investigators. [@2]

The Grand Hero left Le Havre on 1 October, called at Tangier, the Canary Islands
and South Africa, and docked in Réunion on 26 October around 14h. [@3] The
gendarmerie communiqué, as carried by Imaz Press, confirms the discovery of 497
bars of cannabis resin in several vehicles. [@4]
