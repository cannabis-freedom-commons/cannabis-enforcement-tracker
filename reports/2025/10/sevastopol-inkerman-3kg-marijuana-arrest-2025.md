Crime-investigation officers from the Inkerman police division and drug-control
officers from the Balaklavsky district interior ministry department detained a
previously convicted 37-year-old Inkerman resident suspected of attempting to
sell a large batch of marijuana. [@1]

Officers found two cannabis bushes connected to an irrigation system on the
household plot belonging to the detained man. [@1] In one of the sheds they
found a growbox fitted with ventilation, electric heating, LED lamps and
temperature sensors for growing and drying plants. [@1] In the gazebo they found
containers of prepared cannabis, electronic scales and packaging materials, plus
a grinder and a homemade smoking device with brown residue. [@1] Forensic
specialists of the Sevastopol interior ministry expert centre established that
the seized substance was marijuana weighing about three kilograms. [@1] Gazeta
Krym described the cannabis as stored in a basin, buckets and three-litre jars.
[@2] KP Sevastopol reported that police found about three kilograms of marijuana
at the home of the 37-year-old Inkerman resident. [@4]

The detained man told officers the cannabis bushes had grown on his plot by
themselves and that he only collected, dried and stored the harvest for personal
use. [@1] He told Gazeta Krym that the crop had grown in a swamp without his
care. [@2] Police said the drugs were intended for onward sale to third parties.
[@1] STV described the Inkerman resident as having set up a makeshift operation
for growing and processing cannabis on his household plot. [@3]

Investigators opened a criminal case for attempted large-scale drug sale under
the Russian Criminal Code, and the man was detained under article 91 of the
criminal procedure code pending a custody ruling. [@1] Gazeta Krym reported that
he now faces 10 to 20 years in prison. [@2] KP Sevastopol wrote that the man
faces up to 20 years in prison. [@4]
