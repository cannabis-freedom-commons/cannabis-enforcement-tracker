Six suspected members of a transnational organised drug group were detained in
Fergana, Andijan and Tashkent regions. [@1] The detainees were suspected of
transporting drugs as part of an international group. [@1] A second account
confirms that six members of the organised criminal group were detained in the
same three regions. [@2]

A joint operation by the Interior Ministry's operative-search department, the
State Security Service and the Customs Committee seized 111 kg of drugs in
total, including 81 kg of hashish and 30 kg of opium. [@1] The Interior Ministry
said the cross-border group had distributed drugs for several years. [@1] On 13
September, a Fergana region resident was detained while receiving a batch of
drugs. [@1] Two suspects were caught in Andijan region as they removed 10 kg of
hashish from a hiding place. [@1] Two group members living in Andijan region
were detained in a cafe while buying 1 kg of opium for 6,000 US dollars. [@1] In
Angren, a previously thrice-convicted man was caught while receiving 5 kg of
opium. [@1]

Criminal cases were opened and an investigation is under way. [@1] Efforts are
continuing to detain the group's remaining suspected members. [@1]
