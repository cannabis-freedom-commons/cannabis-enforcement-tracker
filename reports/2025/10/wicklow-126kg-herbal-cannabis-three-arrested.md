Gardaí and Revenue officers seized 12.6 kg of herbal cannabis with an estimated value of €252,000 in Co. Wicklow on Friday 3 October 2025, in an intelligence-led joint operation. [@1,2] The operation brought together the Garda National Drugs and Organised Crime Bureau, the Bray Drugs Unit, the Bray and Arklow District Detective Units and Revenue's Customs Service. [@1,2]

Revenue officers first seized about 6.6 kg of herbal cannabis worth an estimated €132,000, and a follow-up search recovered about 6 kg more, bringing the total to 12.6 kg valued at €252,000. [@1,2]

Gardaí arrested a man in his 20s and two women in their 20s and 50s, who were detained in Garda stations in Co. Wicklow under section 2 of the Criminal Justice (Drug Trafficking) Act 1996. [@1] Investigations were continuing. [@1]
