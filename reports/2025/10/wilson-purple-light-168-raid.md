On Monday 20 October 2025, Oklahoma Attorney General Gentner Drummond’s Organized Crime Task
Force raided Purple Light 168 LLC near Wilson in Carter County, a medical marijuana grow the
Oklahoma Bureau of Narcotics and Dangerous Drugs Control said was operating without proper
registration. [@1,2] Agents eradicated about 15,000 marijuana plants and “hundreds of pounds”
of processed marijuana, according to Drummond’s office. [@1,2]

Task force agents working with the Drug Enforcement Administration, Homeland Security
Investigations, ICE, the Oklahoma National Guard, the Carter County Sheriff’s Office and local
fire and county crews also seized body armour and illegal Chinese pesticides applied to the
plants. [@1,2] Several undocumented workers at the site were taken into ICE custody; Drummond
said further criminal arrests were pending while the investigation continued. [@1,2]

Drummond credited the task force with shrinking Oklahoma’s licensed grow count since he took
office and said partnering with ICE helped target labour on illegal farms. [@1] KXII noted that
more arrests were possible. [@2]
