A Canadian of Brampton, Ontario was arrested Sunday night at the Norman Manley
International Airport in Kingston. [@1] The 20-year-old was held after two
pounds of vegetable matter resembling ganja was found in his suitcase. [@1]
Airport personnel conducted a search of his suitcase and allegedly found ganja
weighing two pounds. [@2]

He was subsequently arrested and later charged. [@2] He was charged with
possession of ganja, dealing in ganja, and taking steps preparatory to exporting
ganja. [@2] A court date is being finalised. [@2] An investigation is ongoing.
