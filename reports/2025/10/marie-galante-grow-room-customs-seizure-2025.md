A private home on Marie-Galante was searched on 22 October 2025 and found to
hold a cannabis growing set-up, with 283 grams of herb and 13 cannabis plants.
[@1] Officers also took dried branches and a complete grow room from the home.
[@3] Customs officers checked a shop on the island and recorded the illegal
holding of narcotics and manufactured tobacco said to have been smuggled in.
[@1] That check yielded 20 grams of cannabis herb, 2,193 cigarettes and 1 kg of
loose tobacco, known locally as high leaf. [@1]

The operation was run by the Groupe d'action de l'État sur le littoral, a state
coastal action group set up under the prefect of Guadeloupe, together with
gendarmes of Grand-Bourg, the Pointe-à-Pitre nautical brigade, motorcyclists of
the departmental flow-control squadron, a dog team and customs officers of
Marie-Galante. [@1] RCI reported that the operation ran under the authority of
the prefect of Guadeloupe. [@2] At sea, the nautical brigade checked about ten
commercial, pleasure and fishing vessels and the ferries linking the island with
Grande-Terre. [@1]

The authorities presented the operation as part of their effort to reinforce the
state's presence on the coastline and fight all forms of crime, on land and at
sea. [@3]
