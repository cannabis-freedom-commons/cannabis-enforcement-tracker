Namibian police arrested five people in separate operations at Aroab, Aranos,
Tses and Omatako over the weekend of 3 to 5 October 2025, on suspicion of
possessing and dealing in cannabis and other illegal substances. [@1] Police
spokesperson Kauna Shikwambi said the arrests came during routine roadblocks and
patrols targeting drug offences. [@1]

At Aroab on Friday afternoon, officers at a mini roadblock on the C16 gravel
road in front of the Aroab Police Station found two large parcels of cannabis,
described as skunk, in packets of chips inside a public transport minibus and
arrested a 27-year-old man. [@1] Police valued the two parcels at N$10 000, and
the man was expected in the Keetmanshoop Magistrate's Court on Monday. [@1]

At Aranos, police arrested a 38-year-old man near the Shoprite shopping centre
after finding drugs hidden in a 2.5 kg bag of white sugar. [@1] Shikwambi said
the Aranos haul held one and a half Mandrax tablets, 48 “bankies” of skunk
cannabis and loose cannabis, valued at N$2 580. [@1] Later that night at Aroab,
police arrested a 42-year-old man after finding a small cannabis plantation at
his plot in the Groenhoek area, and seized two plants about 30 centimetres tall.
[@1] He was to appear in the Keetmanshoop Magistrate's Court. [@1]

At Tses, a stop-and-search led to the arrest of a 24-year-old man found with a
quarter of a Mandrax tablet, one “bankie” of cannabis and another container
holding cannabis seeds. [@1] Shikwambi said he faces charges of dealing in and
possessing dependence-producing substances. [@1] At Omatako, in the Maroelaboom
area, a night patrol arrested a 32-year-old man with 22 small zip-lock bags of
cannabis and one large bag of skunk cannabis, valued at N$1 100. [@1]

All five suspects remained in custody for appearances in their respective
magistrate courts that week while investigations continued. [@1]
