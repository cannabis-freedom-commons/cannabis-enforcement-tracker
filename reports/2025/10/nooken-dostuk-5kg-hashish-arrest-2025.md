Kyrgyzstan's State Committee for National Security reported on 6 October 2025
that a foreign national had been detained with 5 kg of hashish in Nooken
district, Jalal-Abad region. [@1] The detained man is identified only by the
initials A.A.A. [@1] He is accused of buying 5 kg of hashish inside Jalal-Abad
region and planning to move it into Uzbekistan. [@2]

Security officers detained him on 2 October 2025 near the Kyrgyz-Uzbek state
border, in the Lola area of Dostuk village, Nooken district. [@1] He was caught
as he tried to carry the drugs across the border. [@3]

The State Committee for National Security said an investigation was under way.
