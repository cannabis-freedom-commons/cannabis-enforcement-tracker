A 26-year-old miner was arrested at Mango Landing, Guyana, on Tuesday 21 October
2025, after being found in possession of 215 grams of marijuana. [@1] According
to a Guyana Police Force statement, the suspect was a 26-year-old miner of
Guyhoc Park, Georgetown, also known as Plaisance, East Coast Demerara. [@1]

Police searched several persons and their belongings during the operation; the
suspect was found holding a white plastic container which police suspected had a
false bottom, and upon opening it they found leaves, seeds and stems suspected
to be cannabis. [@1] Police informed the suspect of the offence, took him into
custody and escorted him to the Mahdia Police Station, where the suspected
narcotics were weighed in his presence at 215 grams and lodged as evidence. [@1]

Investigations were ongoing at the time of reporting. [@1]

The Times of Suriname republication of the Kaieteur News account confirms the
arrest at Mango Landing and the 215-gram figure. [@2]
