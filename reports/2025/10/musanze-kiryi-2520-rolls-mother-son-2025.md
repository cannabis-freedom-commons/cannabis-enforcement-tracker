Police in Musanze District arrested a young man and his mother after finding them with 2,520 rolls of cannabis, allegedly meant for sale in the community. [@1] Officers made the arrest on Monday night in Kiryi Village, Kigombe Cell, Muhoza Sector, acting on a tip from local residents. [@1]

Northern Province police spokesperson IP I. Ngirabakunzi credited community members with the information that led to the arrests, calling residents the police's primary partners in ensuring public safety. [@1] The two suspects were held at Muhoza Police Station pending further investigation and transfer to the Rwanda Investigation Bureau. [@1] No weight for the rolls and no names for the pair were published. [@1]
