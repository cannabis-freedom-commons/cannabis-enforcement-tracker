Drug-control officers of the Komi Republic's interior ministry established that
two Syktyvkar residents were involved in a drug-trafficking offence. [@1]
Officers stopped an Audi A4 in a forest in Ust-Vymsky district, and the two
suspects inside were a previously convicted 25-year-old from Syktyvkar and his
26-year-old accomplice. [@1] A search of the car found pressed bars of plant
material, which an expert identified as hashish weighing more than 3 kilograms
in total. [@1] Investigators then found that the pair had laid 28 stashes in
Ukhta district, and officers located all of them and seized more than 2.5
kilograms of the same drug. [@1]

A criminal case for attempted large-scale group drug sale was opened against
both suspects, and both were remanded in custody. [@1] The same outlet said the
28 stashes were in Ukhta itself rather than in its district. [@2] BNK, relaying
the republican interior ministry, reported that the couriers brought three
kilograms into Ust-Vymsky district and were caught in the act there. [@3]
