On 10 October 2025 the Armed Forces seized a large marijuana plantation in a
mountainous sector of Olancho in eastern Honduras. [@1] The operation against
the Los Veliz criminal structure was carried out jointly with the Armed Forces
and the FESCCO organised-crime prosecutors. [@2] Officers carried out eight
house raids in the municipalities of Gualaco, San Esteban and Salamá. [@2] Two
men were detained as suspected members of the structure. [@2] One of them, Osmin
Renán Padilla Martínez, already had an arrest warrant for drug trafficking and
criminal association. [@2]

Inspectors found about 30,000 suspected marijuana plants on a four-manzana plot.
[@2] The plants stood over one metre tall. [@2] The four manzanas cover about
2.8 hectares. [@1] They also found a rustic installation used to dry the bushes.
[@2] The Armed Forces had first reported more than 40,000 bushes seized in the
same mountainous area. [@1]

The DLCN said the investigation began with a tip about a criminal organisation
broken off from Los Cachiros. [@2] The report says the structure kept extensive
marijuana plantations in the San Esteban mountains. [@2] It says the group
extended its drug-trafficking activity to the departments of Colón, El Paraíso
and Choluteca. [@2] Anti-drug prosecutors brought charges against members of the
structure for drug trafficking and criminal association. [@2]
