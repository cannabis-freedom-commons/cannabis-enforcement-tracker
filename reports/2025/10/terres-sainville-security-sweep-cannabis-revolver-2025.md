Police in Fort-de-France ran a security sweep in the Terres-Sainville
neighbourhood on Thursday 2 October 2025, the second such operation in the
district in three days. [@1] Several dozen officers were deployed, including a
dog handler from the canine unit. [@1]

Officers found a revolver and also recovered crack, cannabis herb and cannabis
resin. [@1] The operation was run by the Direction territoriale de la police
nationale, the national police's territorial directorate for Martinique,
reinforced by a mobile gendarmerie platoon, with the stated aim of fighting
street crime, the circulation of weapons and drug trafficking. [@2] Officers
checked 37 people and 21 motorbikes and scooters. [@1] France-Antilles reported
four scooters, a .44-type revolver and drugs (crack, cannabis herb and cannabis
resin) seized in the operation. [@2] Police took four scooters off the road.
[@1]

Police and gendarmes deployed late in the day and covered several roads and
sensitive points of the district, carrying out identity checks and targeted
checks of two-wheelers. [@3] La 1ère listed the judicial seizures as a .44
revolver plus drugs: crack, cannabis herb and cannabis resin. [@3] Police placed
the operation in a strategy of regular presence meant to reduce street crime,
cut off drug dealing and take weapons off the streets, and announced further
operations in the coming weeks. [@3]
