Customs officers intercepted two Senegalese women arriving from Paris in the
Arrivals zone of Blaise Diagne International Airport around 12:30 on 18 October
2025. [@2] Customs said the two travellers were acting as couriers for a
criminal network operating on the Paris-Dakar route. [@1] Officers ran a
controlled delivery with the OCRTIS anti-drug office to the Liberté 3
neighbourhood in Dakar and arrested two other suspected members of the same
network. [@3] The four detainees were referred to the Mbour prosecutor for
further judicial proceedings. [@3]

Officers of the airport Tourism Brigade seized 120 packets of hashish weighing
11.8 kilograms from the women's suitcases. [@1] The resin was concealed inside
Nesquik cocoa-powder pots packed in their luggage and was found during a routine
baggage check. [@1] The operation was carried out by the Tourism Brigade under
the AIBD Customs Subdivision. [@1]

The same unit had seized 59 packets weighing 32.686 kg on 2 October 2025, less
than three weeks earlier. [@2]
