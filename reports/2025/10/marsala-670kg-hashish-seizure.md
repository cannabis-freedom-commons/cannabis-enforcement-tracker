Police said the men’s criminal responsibility would be established only after
any final judgments. [@2]

On the night of 20 October 2025, a joint patrol noticed suspicious activity off
Marsala as a high-powered inflatable boat headed out to sea. [@1] Officers
tracked the inflatable into international waters, where it met a
Tunisian-flagged fishing vessel and large blue packages were transferred to it.
[@2] After the transfer, the inflatable headed back toward Marsala and was
stopped in Italian waters after a high-speed chase. [@2] Its driver threw some
packages overboard, but officers recovered them and found more than 160 kg of
hashish. [@2]

The financial police then stopped the fishing boat in international waters;
during its escape, the crew abandoned 11 packages containing more than 600 kg of
hashish. [@2] Officers boarded the fishing boat a few miles from Tunisia’s
territorial limit, and both vessels were seized. [@2] Police reported seizing
more than 670 kg of hashish and described the haul as the largest hashish
seizure in Trapani waters for decades. [@2]
