Edo State Commander Mitchell Ofoyeju, presenting the command's September scorecard in Benin City, said intelligence-led operations destroyed six cannabis plantations covering 26.43 hectares and led to 54 arrests, comprising 43 men and 11 women. [@1,2] The destroyed farms were put at an estimated yield of 66,078.57 kilogrammes of skunk cannabis. [@1,2]

The plantations stood in Ugbogui Forest in Ovia South West, Ataroro Forest in Owan West, Urohi Forest in Esan West and Ogu Forest in Igueben. [@1,2] The command separately seized 1,506.57 kilogrammes of narcotics, made up of 1,502.26 kilogrammes of cannabis and 4.27 kilogrammes of psychotropic substances including tramadol, nitrazepam, Swinol, Danabol, methamphetamine, cocaine and heroin. [@1]

Officers intercepted a truck on Wareke-Auchi Road carrying 82 bags of cannabis concealed in charcoal sacks and weighing 1,025 kilogrammes, arresting Kabiru Abdullahi, 35, and Anas Safiyanu, 20, both of Zamfara State. [@1] At Wire Road in Benin City, officers arrested 43-year-old Etiosa Bazuaye with 98 pinches of crack cocaine hidden in a wooden stool. [@1] Ofoyeju said one conviction was secured in September, four new cases were charged and 118 cases were ongoing, while 32 people were counselled and rehabilitated. [@1]
