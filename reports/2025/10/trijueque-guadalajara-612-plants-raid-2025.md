Officers of the Tax Agency's Customs Surveillance Service in Castilla-La Mancha, working with Civil Guard officers from Sigüenza, dismantled an indoor cannabis plantation in the municipality of Trijueque, Guadalajara, on 15 October 2025. [@1] The operation, known as Colombia-Capetri, had begun in the middle of the year after checks in the area, and continued as a joint investigation. [@1]

The property looked like a residential building from the outside but held three rooms fitted out for growing plants, with air-conditioning, air extraction, lighting, heating, an irrigation system and fertilisers. [@1] Officers seized 612 cannabis plants at various stages of growth and flowering, equivalent to 66 kilograms of cannabis. [@1] They also found an illegal connection to the electricity supply worth more than 13,000 euros. [@1]

Three people were arrested during the raid, and a fourth was arrested on 29 October in Torrejón de Ardoz in connection with the case. [@1] All four face charges of offences against public health, drug trafficking and electricity fraud. [@1]
