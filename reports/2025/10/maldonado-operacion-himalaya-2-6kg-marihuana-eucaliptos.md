Officers seized 4 bricks of marijuana weighing a total of 2.593 kilograms,
alongside 2.703 kilograms of cocaine, a .22 revolver, four 9 mm pistol
magazines, and 1,020 cartridges of various calibres. [@1]

No one was detained at the property. [@1] Also seized were four hand-held radio
units and binoculars, items the sources describe as typical equipment for drug
bands to maintain contact and carry out remote surveillance. [@1]

Forensic police documented evidence at the site, and a drug-detection dog helped
locate the seized substances. [@1]
