Customs officers at Blaise Diagne International Airport arrested the foreign
dual-national owner of two suspect suitcases on 2 October 2025. [@1] The man had
arrived from an Asian country and was travelling toward a country bordering
Senegal via the airport. [@2] Officers handed the suspect over to the Mbour
prosecutor for further judicial proceedings. [@2]

The Customs Tourism Brigade seized 59 packets of cannabis resin, commonly called
hashish, weighing a total of 32.686 kg from the two accompanied suitcases. [@1]
Officers had targeted and isolated the two accompanied bags using objective
targeting criteria and investigation techniques, and their passage through the
scanner revealed suspicious signs. [@1] When the owner opened the suitcases,
officers found the drug packets concealed inside. [@1] Laboratory analysis by
the National Laboratory of the Technical and Scientific Police Division
confirmed that the substance was hashish. [@2]

The customs directorate said the seizure formed part of its operations against
international drug trafficking. [@1] It restated its determination to confront
transnational organized crime and combat illicit trafficking in all its forms.
