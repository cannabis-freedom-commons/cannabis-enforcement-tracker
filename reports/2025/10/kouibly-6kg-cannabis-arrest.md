Police arrested one person and seized six kilograms of cannabis in Kouibly, in
western Ivory Coast, on Monday 6 October 2025. [@1] The operation was carried
out by the Man regional branch of the DPSD, Ivory Coast's drug police, as part
of the national Operation Épervier. [@1]

According to the Directorate General of the National Police, officers acted on
information about drug trafficking in a plantation where the suspect was
packaging cannabis intended for sale. [@1] Officers seized six kilograms of
cannabis, which was held as evidence, and apprehended the alleged dealer. [@1]
Police surprised a man packaging cannabis in a remote plantation at Kouibly.
[@2] That plantation was used as a processing point for cannabis intended for
resale. [@2] Officers found several carefully wrapped bundles prepared for sale
at the site. [@2]

Police took the suspect under guard to the police station for the judicial
investigation. [@2] Police said investigators are continuing work to trace the
full supply chain of the trafficking network still active in the area. [@2]

The national police restated its commitment to intensify action against drug
trafficking and use in the west and across the country. [@1]
