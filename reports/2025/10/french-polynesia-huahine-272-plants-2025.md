On 8 October 2025, gendarmes seized and destroyed 272 cannabis plants in
Huahine. [@3] The operation was led by Huahine brigade gendarmes with air
support from the Dauphin helicopter of flotilla 35F. [@4] Guided from the air,
officers found two grow sites on islets east of the island and in the Maroe
valley. [@2] The plants were hidden in dense vegetation in shelters and
greenhouses fitted with solar panels. [@2]

The 272 plants were spread over two grow sites, some under greenhouse cover.
[@1] The 272 plants represented about 42 kilograms of herb seized and destroyed.
[@2] Officers seized the crop and destroyed the entire production on site. [@3]

Those responsible had not been identified and investigations were continuing.
[@2] An investigation was under way to identify those responsible for the
illegal crop. [@4] The forces said they remained fully mobilised against drug
trafficking. [@3]
