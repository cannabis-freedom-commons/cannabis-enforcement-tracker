Drug-control officers of the Novocherkassk inter-municipal police department
detained two Oktyabrsky District residents aged 25 and 51 on suspicion of
storing drugs during the Mak-2025 preventive operation. [@1] A search at the
accomplices' home found and seized over 60 kg of marijuana, which expert
examination confirmed was a narcotic drug. [@1]

Police established that the men had grown drug-containing plants in Oktyabrsky
District. [@1] After the harvest they used a house belonging to one of them to
store and process the plant material and prepare it for sale in small batches.
[@1] Police said the growers planned to sell the output in small batches across
the region. [@2]

Both suspects confessed during questioning. [@1] A criminal case was opened
against them for unlawful production and sale of drugs on an especially large
scale. [@2] Both men were remanded in custody. [@2]
