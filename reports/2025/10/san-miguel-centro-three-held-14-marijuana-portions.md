Police in San Miguel Centro arrested three men in two drug operations. [@2]
Víctor Alexander Rivera Romero, 29, was detained at avenida Roosevelt Sur and 7ª
Avenida Sur. [@2] Police said he had eight portions of marijuana, one portion of
cocaine, a digital scale, a notebook and two mobile phones. [@2]

William Orestes Calderón Solorzano and Óscar René González Garay were arrested
in a second operation at the 3rd and 4th stage of the colonia Pacífica. [@2]
Police seized six portions of marijuana, six 40 mm shell casings, two digital
scales, three mobile phones, $70 and a vehicle from them. [@2]

The two operations produced 14 portions of marijuana in total; the reports give
no weight for them. [@2] The cocaine recovered from Rivera is counted separately
from the marijuana. [@1] The National Civil Police announced the arrests on
Saturday 25 October 2025. [@2] Police said the detentions had happened in the
preceding hours. [@2] All three will be referred to the courts for illicit drug
trafficking. [@1]
