The Colombo Crime Division arrested a major trafficker accused of smuggling
Kerala ganja from India to Sri Lanka by boat. [@1] The operation was described
as a significant breakthrough against the smuggling route. [@1] The account
presents the suspect as a large-scale trafficker working the India-to-Sri Lanka
boat route. [@1] The suspect had been under surveillance for a considerable
period before officers acted. [@1] Officers apprehended the suspect in the
Jaffna area. [@1]

Authorities reported that more than 10 kilograms of Kerala ganja were seized
during the operation. [@1] The seized substance was described throughout the
account as Kerala ganja. [@1] The seizure occurred during the same operation as
the Jaffna arrest. [@1]

The arrest was made possible following revelations from a man identified only as
'Anandan'. [@1] Anandan had previously supplied a boat used in the escape of a
key suspect in the Ganemulla Sanjeewa shooting incident at the Aluthkade Court
Complex. [@1] That earlier Aluthkade case provided the lead that directed
investigators to the trafficking suspect. [@1]
