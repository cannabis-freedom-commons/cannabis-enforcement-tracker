Zambia's Drug Enforcement Commission (DEC) seized more than eight tonnes of cannabis in joint operations with the police, immigration and wildlife officers in Eastern and Central provinces, the Commission announced in a statement issued in Lusaka on Thursday 16 October 2025. [@1] In Mambwe District, officers arrested seven suspects in Muzambwe village in the Mfuwe area, where they found 8.1 tonnes of fresh cannabis plants and 101 kilograms of dry cannabis allegedly cultivated and stored for illicit trade. [@1] In Nchelenge District, officers arrested a woman aged 37 with 70.7 kilograms of dry cannabis concealed in dry banana bark and packed in polythene sacks ready for distribution. [@1] DEC spokesperson Allan Tamba said the arrests followed sustained surveillance and intelligence work against cultivation and trafficking networks, and that all the suspects were in custody to appear in court.
