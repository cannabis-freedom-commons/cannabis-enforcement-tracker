Namibian police arrested seven people for possession of cannabis and mandrax at
different places over one weekend in October 2025. [@1] National police
spokesperson Deputy Commissioner Kauna Shikwambi said three suspects aged 25, 25
and 30 were arrested in a stop and search at Takarania location in Mariental
with skunk and pure cannabis valued at a combined N$2 900. [@1]

At Omashaka in Ondangwa, a 20-year-old was arrested at their house with seven
bankies of cannabis weighing eight grams and worth N$400, and was to appear in
the Ondangwa Magistrate's Court on 20 October. [@1] At Block E in Rehoboth, a
37-year-old man was arrested on Friday during an undercover operation with four
bankies of cannabis skunk and several mandrax tablets valued at N$2 380. [@1] A
33-year-old man was arrested at Ondangwa with 11 parcels of cannabis worth N$32
750 and was to appear in the Ondangwa Magistrate's Court on Monday. [@1]

Separately, border officials found three bags under a bridge at the Ariamsvlei
Border Post at about 01h20 on Sunday containing 59 parcels of cannabis, 55 grams
of loose cannabis and 4 073 mandrax tablets. [@1] The Namibian put the whole
Ariamsvlei haul at about N$4 million, with cannabis worth over N$3 million and
mandrax at about N$500 000, while 99FM reported a combined street value of about
N$3.5 million. [@1]

99FM reported that officials noticed suspicious shoe tracks leading under the
bridge, that no suspects were found in the area and that the tracks had mixed
with others, making identification difficult. [@2] Chief Inspector Elifas
Kuwinga of the Namibian Police Force confirmed the Ariamsvlei discovery and said
the drugs were seized and registered as found property while investigations
continued. [@3]
