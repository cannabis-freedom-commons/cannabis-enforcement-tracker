Acting on a tip-off, a Rapid Action Battalion team raided the house of Ahidur Rahman Sawdagar in the Chikanchhara area under Baganbazar Union of Bhujpur police station on Sunday evening. [@1] The team recovered 51 kilograms of ganja kept in three plastic bags hidden under a bed, with an estimated market value of around 800,000 taka. [@1]

Three men were arrested: Md Nurannabi, 43, Md Abul Bashar, 27, and Md Abdul Karim, 30, all residents of the Chikanchhara area. [@1] They were later handed over to Bhujpur police station for legal action, as confirmed by RAB-7 assistant director for media ARM Mozaffar Hossain. [@1]
