NDLEA operatives raided Proxy Nightclub at 7 Akin Adesola Street, Victoria
Island, Lagos, in the early hours of Sunday 26 October 2025, disrupting a party
the agency called a drug party. [@1] Over 100 people were detained for
screening, including the club owner Mike Eze Nwalie Nwogu, known as Pretty Mike,
and his manager Joachin Millary. [@2] The agency said undercover operatives had
been embedded in the party from about 11pm on Saturday 25 October and moved in
at about 3am on Sunday. [@3]

The NDLEA's own roundup said cartons of Loud and laughing gas were recovered
from guests at the party and from the club's store. [@6] The agency later put
the haul at 384.882 kilograms of Canadian Loud, a strong cannabis strain, and
other substances from the club's store. [@2] The agency also announced a
forfeiture suit against the club in the Lagos court. [@4] It said those held
would face charges including conspiracy, possession of narcotics and permitting
the premises to be used for drug activity. [@5]

The NDLEA said all attendees initially arrested were profiled, counselled and
released within hours, while Pretty Mike and his manager remained in custody.
[@2] The agency later said guests had been invited by flyers, and warned that
clubs and hotels hosting drug parties risk forfeiting their properties to the
federal government. [@2]
