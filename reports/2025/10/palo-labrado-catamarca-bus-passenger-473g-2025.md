Members of Sección Núcleo of Escuadrón 67 “Catamarca” of the Gendarmería
Nacional stopped a passenger bus on Ruta Nacional No. 38 at Palo Labrado in the
Paclín department. [@1] The bus was travelling from the city of Orán in Salta
province to Mendoza province. [@1] During the passenger inspection, officers
found that an adult citizen was carrying two rectangular packages, one hidden at
waist level and the other under his seat. [@1] The Federal Court of Catamarca
intervened and ordered the substance and other items of interest seized and the
man detained. [@1]

The packages held a greenish-brown plant substance giving off an odour
characteristic of marijuana. [@1] Field Narcotest testing was positive for
cannabis sativa, with an approximate weight of 473 grams. [@1]
