A truck driver was arrested on Wednesday night after police intercepted his
vehicle and found a large quantity of ganja in Morant Bay, St Thomas. [@1] The
driver, a haulage contractor from Clarendon, was taken into custody, and both
the ganja and the truck were seized. [@1] He was named as Fabian Watson, 33, of
Mount Zion, Rock River in Clarendon. [@2] A subsequent check found the ganja
weighed over 1,500 pounds (about 680 kg). [@1] Police said a search of the
vehicle uncovered more than 150 parcels of compressed ganja along with over
$180,000 in cash. [@2]

About 10:33 p.m. on Wednesday, 1 October 2025, the Special Operations team of
the Firearms and Narcotics Investigation Division (FNID), supported by the St
Thomas police, intercepted the truck on Church Street in Morant Bay. [@3] Watson
was on Friday charged with possession of ganja, dealing in ganja, trafficking
ganja, taking steps preparatory to export ganja, possession of criminal property
and conspiracy. [@3] The charges were six breaches of the Dangerous Drugs Act.
[@2] A date was to be set for him to appear in court. [@3]

Police put the estimated street value of the ganja at approximately $4.7
million. [@3] Acting Senior Superintendent Patrae Rowe, director of FNID, said
the division continues to collaborate with both local and international
stakeholders to apprehend individuals involved in drug trafficking. [@2] Rowe
said ganja is being used in the guns-for-drugs trade, with guns taken into the
country to commit murders. [@2] The seizure was the latest in a series of recent
ganja busts in St Thomas. [@1]
