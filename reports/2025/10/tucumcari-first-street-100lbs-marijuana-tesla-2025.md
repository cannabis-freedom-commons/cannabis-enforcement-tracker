Tucumcari police stopped a car with California plates in the 2600 block of South
First Street on the afternoon of 10 October 2025 for suspicion of drunk driving.
[@1] The stop was at the McStop on South First Street, and officers served a
search warrant on the Tesla before finding the drugs. [@2] According to Chief
Lorenzo Emillio, officers found about 100 pounds (45 kg) of marijuana and an
unspecified amount of suspected cocaine in the car. [@1]

Caleb Morgan Cook, 24, and Oliver Doyle Bozarth, 25, were arrested. [@2] Both
men were from California. [@2] Both were charged with driving while intoxicated,
possession of a controlled substance, trafficking marijuana and conspiracy. [@1]
Police said the drugs were to be delivered to a location within 150 miles of
Tucumcari. [@2]

Chief Lorenzo Emillio said last week's operations were among the first results
of an increased drive to be active police officers in the city. [@2] Police said
more details would be released as the investigation continued. [@2] No court
step was reported; both accounts cited the Tucumcari Police Department. [@1]
