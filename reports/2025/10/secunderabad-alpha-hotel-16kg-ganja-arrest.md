Two men from Odisha who were transporting ganja for Mumbai-based traders were
arrested by Telangana Excise and Prohibition officials in Secunderabad. [@1] The
accused were identified as Murali Nayak and Kala Charan, who had procured the
ganja from a supplier in Odisha. [@3] The pair had travelled from Odisha to
Hyderabad aboard the Konark Express. [@2] They were waiting near Alpha Hotel to
hand over the consignment when they were apprehended. [@1]

The Excise State Task Force C team intercepted them near Alpha Hotel and seized
three bags of ganja, which officials valued at around Rs 8 lakh. [@1] Officers
seized over 16 kilograms of ganja from the two men. [@1]

The seized ganja and the two arrested men were handed over to the Secunderabad
excise station for further investigation. [@1] A case was registered and further
investigation was under way. [@2]
