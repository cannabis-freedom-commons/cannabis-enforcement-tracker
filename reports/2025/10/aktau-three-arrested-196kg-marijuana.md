In Aktau, police detained three suspects accused of storing and selling
narcotics. [@1] A criminal case was opened over the seizure. [@1] A court
ordered the three suspects held in a pre-trial detention facility for two
months. [@2]

Searches at several addresses found large batches of marijuana together with
electronic scales, packing materials and a pneumatic weapon. [@1] Officers also
found a hidden store in the basement of a house where the detainees lived, with
four boxes holding bags of marijuana weighing more than 100 kg. [@1] Police put
the total weight of the seized substance at 196 kg. [@1] A forensic chemical
examination confirmed that the seized substance was marijuana. [@2]

The operation was carried out by the anti-extremism department of the Mangystau
Region police, coordinated with the regional prosecutor's office and supported
by the SOBR special unit. [@1] Officers used a service dog named Kira during the
operation. [@1] Police said the operation took place under the Pravoporyadok
preventive campaign to identify people involved in drug trafficking. [@1]
