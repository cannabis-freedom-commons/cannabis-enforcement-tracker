Police received a report that a 19-year-old resident of Varser village was
illegally acquiring and selling drugs. [@1] Officers detained the youth on 11
October near Varser village and took him to the Sevan police department. [@1]

A search of the detainee found 43 tape-sealed packets containing 82 grams of a
yellow-brown substance. [@1] Officers also found two pipettes with traces of ash
on him. [@1] The youth said he had collected the packets of marijuana in the
village of Tsaghkunk in order to place them for sale. [@1]

According to police, he had already placed some of the packets he had collected
before he was detained. [@1] In the course of the criminal proceedings, officers
recovered 8 more packets containing 18 grams of the yellow-brown substance,
bringing the total to 51 packets and 100 grams. [@1] The circumstances were
still being clarified when the account was published. [@1]
