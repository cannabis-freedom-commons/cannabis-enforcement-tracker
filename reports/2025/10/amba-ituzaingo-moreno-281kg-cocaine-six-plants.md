Argentina's federal police detained five people in eight searches across the
Buenos Aires metropolitan area on 2 October 2025, after following a Peruvian
citizen who an investigation said supplied illicit substances to narco bands.
[@2] The investigation by the Procuraduría de Narcocriminalidad (PROCUNAR) had
identified a Peruvian citizen said to be supplying illicit substances to narco
bands. [@1] Officers of the Departamento Federal de Investigaciones (DFI) of the
Policía Federal Argentina (PFA) watched his car stop near calle Comandante
Peredo at 500 in Ituzaingó to meet the presumed buyer, and both men were
detained in the street. [@1] The Juzgado Federal de Villa Mercedes in San Luis,
under judge Juan Carlos Nacul, validated the stop and ordered the vehicle
searched; officers found 60 compact bricks totalling more than 65 kilograms of
high-purity cocaine hydrochloride. [@1]

With the buyer, an Argentine aged 44, confirmed as a comprador previo, the court
ordered eight searches of properties linked to the seller's family and to the
rest of the structure under investigation. [@1] At a house on calle Don Bosco in
Barrio Trujuy, Moreno, officers found another 200 bricks weighing 216 kilograms
of cocaine hydrochloride. [@1] The remaining searches covered five homes in the
city of Buenos Aires, two more on Comandante Peredo in Ituzaingó and one on
Brandsen in Bosques, Florencio Varela. [@1] In those searches three more men
were detained, and officers seized several doses of marijuana, six Cannabis
Sativa plants, 2,280,000 pesos, 1,300 US dollars, two pistols, a revolver,
ammunition, four cars and 18 mobile phones. [@1] TN reported the same seizures
and the same three further detentions in the house searches. [@3] The
prosecutor's office put the cocaine seized in the eight searches at 281,872
kilograms, a total that is not cannabis and is not counted here. [@2]

All five detainees were adults and were left with the seized items at the
disposal of the intervening judge, accused of infringing Argentina's national
drug law. [@1] TN reported that all the detainees were left at the judge's
disposal facing charges under the national drug law. [@3] The Public
Prosecutor's Office described the five detainees as a Peruvian, a Uruguayan and
three Argentines. [@2] The organisation ran from August 2023 to October 2025,
selling cocaine hydrochloride to resellers in the AMBA and in San Luis, Buenos
Aires and Santa Fe through mulas who carried the drug overland. [@2] Judge Nacul
took statements from the five on 3 October 2025, and all refused to testify.
[@2]
