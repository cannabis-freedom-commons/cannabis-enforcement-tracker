In Huíla province, a lawyer was detained this week and later made a formal
suspect by the public prosecutor after the Lubango prison service accused him of
bringing two wraps of liamba and two packs of cigarettes into the city prison.
[@1] Prison officers said they frustrated the attempted introduction on
Wednesday the 15th. [@2] The two inmates were named as José Modesto Hininguali
and Albano Ngalang Kahongo, who had just had a consultation with lawyer Anselmo
Sacanje, holder of licence 11664 and member of the Angolan Bar Association. [@2]
The lawyer had gone to the prison for his usual visit to two detained clients.
[@1] After the visit, prison officers searched inside the prison and found the
two inmates holding two wraps of liamba and two packs of cigarettes. [@1] When
questioned, the detainees said they had obtained the products through the
lawyer. [@1]

The Lubango prison service detained the lawyer and called in the Serviço de
Investigação Criminal (SIC) to prepare the criminal case. [@1] A second account
says a small operation led to the lawyer's detention and his presentation to the
SIC for criminal proceedings. [@2] After being presented to the public
prosecutor's office, the lawyer was made a formal suspect and released after
bail was imposed. [@1]

On Friday the 17th, the Huíla branch of the Angolan Bar Association went to the
Lubango prison to look into the situation and safeguard the detained lawyer's
rights. [@1] The association said its competent internal mechanisms would be
used to establish any disciplinary responsibility arising from the facts under
investigation. [@1]
