Officers of the Anti-Drug and Smuggling Unit, working with customs, intercepted
a passenger arriving at Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam International Airport on the
evening of Wednesday 15 October 2025 on flight MK289 from Madagascar. [@1] The
passenger, a 29-year-old driver from Antananarivo, was carrying a cardboard box
bearing a name and a Mauritian telephone number. [@1] Inside the box,
investigators found an electric blender whose base held two sachets filled with
cannabis leaves, about 140 grams in all. [@1]

Investigators then carried out a controlled delivery, watching the parcel until
someone came to collect it. [@1] The next morning, Thursday 16 October, a second
suspect came to collect the parcel at Vallée-Pitot, and was arrested on the
spot. [@1] A search of the second suspect's home at Vallée-Pitot found no other
illicit product. [@1]

Both suspects were placed in detention on the order of Superintendent
Imembaccus, the courier at the Vacoas detention centre and the recipient at the
Piton centre. [@1] The investigation was continuing to establish the reach of
the network and to identify any accomplices. [@1]
