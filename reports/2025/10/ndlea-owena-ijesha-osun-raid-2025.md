On Thursday 2 October 2025, NDLEA officers raided the Owena/Ijesha forest reserve in Osun
State, where they destroyed 14,000 kilogrammes of cannabis on 5.6 hectares of plantation and
recovered 142 kilogrammes of the same substance. [@1,2] Three suspects were arrested:
Chuimieze Shedrack, 28, Sunday John, 25, and Solomon Okopko, 27. [@1,2]

NDLEA spokesman Femi Babafemi disclosed the raid in an agency statement issued on Sunday
5 October. [@1,2] The reports name no charges or court outcome for the three suspects. [@1,2]
The same statement describes separate NDLEA operations in Edo and Abuja. [@1,2] It also
describes an Ogun raid where 112 kilogrammes of cannabis were seized with other drugs. [@2]
Those are different events left out of this report.
