Kilisitina Finau, aged 34, is serving two years in prison on charges including
possession of 484.43 grams of cannabis. [@1] Her 23-year-old co-offender,
Lesieli Makasini, was found guilty of possessing a lesser amount of cannabis and
received a suspended sentence. [@1]

The case was heard at the Nuku'alofa Supreme Court under Lord Chief Justice
Bishop. [@1]
