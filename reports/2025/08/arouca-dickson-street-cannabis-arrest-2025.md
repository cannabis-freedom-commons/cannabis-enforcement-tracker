Trinidad and Tobago police arrested one man for trafficking cannabis during a
roving operation in the North-Central Division on Friday, 15 August 2025. [@1]
The operation was run by officers of the North-Central Division Task Force
(East) with the Arouca CID and ran from 5 pm to 8 pm. [@1] It covered Dickson
Street, St Michael Road, Market Street, Thomas Trace and the corner of Lopinot
Road and Manan Street in Arouca, a town in Tunapuna-Piarco. [@1]

Around 6:30 pm officers saw a group of men gathered on Dickson Street near a
spot identified for drug activity and identified themselves as police as they
approached. [@1] One man was seen passing two foil packets through a gate while
saying "look the 100 and 20 piece". [@1] PC Lalsingh detained the suspect, a
resident of St Clair Extension, Trincity, and a search of his person found two
foil packets containing cannabis and a quantity of cash. [@1] Police told him of
the offence of trafficking in cannabis, arrested him and cautioned him under the
Judges' Rules, the common-law rules on questioning suspects. [@1] He was taken
to Arouca Police Station, where investigations continued under PC Mohammed. [@1]

The exercise ran under ACP Simon and Snr Supt Gift with ASP Bharath, Insp Pierre
and PC Rochford, the police service said. [@2] The Trinidad and Tobago Police
Service said it remains committed to proactive policing and community safety.
