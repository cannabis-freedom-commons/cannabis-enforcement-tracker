A 32-year-old man was arrested in Dundee, KwaZulu-Natal, after police found 90
bags of compressed dagga in his car. [@1] Police said they had intelligence that
a car was entering the country from Eswatini through Paulpietersburg carrying
dagga. [@1] Officers from the Glencoe K9 Unit, the Zululand District Task Team
and Dundee Crime Prevention looked for the car and found it on the R33 road
heading towards Blood River. [@1] The driver refused to stop and a chase
followed until police stopped the car in Dundee. [@2]

The bags of dagga were on the passenger seat, the back seat and in the boot of
the car. [@1] Police put the street value of the 90 bags at an estimated R4
million. [@2] The man was charged with dealing in dagga. [@1] He was expected to
appear in the Dundee Magistrate's Court on Wednesday, 6 August 2025. [@1]

KwaZulu-Natal police arrested the man on Tuesday night, 5 August 2025. [@3]
