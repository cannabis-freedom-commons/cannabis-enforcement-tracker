Police in Jhalawar district, Rajasthan, stopped a truck carrying iron rods at a
blockade near the Dudhalia outpost under Dag police station in the middle of the
night and found 103.6 kg of cannabis hidden among the rods. [@1] Officers
arrested the truck's driver, identified in the reporting as 35-year-old Zahir
Khan, and his associate, 28-year-old Vinod Sharma, at the scene. [@2]

Jhalawar Superintendent of Police Amit Kumar said the consignment had been
brought from Odisha for delivery in Jhalawar, and that the seized cannabis was
worth about ₹1 crore. [@1]

All four were booked under the NDPS Act and were being questioned to identify
others in the network. [@1] Police seized the truck and the luxury car used in
the operation, and the article put the combined value of the drugs and vehicles
at about ₹2 crore. [@1] The PTI account adds that police also booked a woman
tour-travel operator for providing a private-numbered vehicle to the soldier to
escort the truck. [@2]
