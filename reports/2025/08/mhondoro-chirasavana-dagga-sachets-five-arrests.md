Police in Mhondoro, Mashonaland West, arrested five people at Chirasavana
Business Centre in Mamina on 22 August 2025 on suspicion of unlawful possession
of dagga. [@1] Zimbabwe Republic Police national spokesperson Commissioner Paul
Nyathi confirmed the arrests and the drugs recovered. [@1]

The five were found in possession of 171 small sachets and one plastic bag of
dagga, the local name for cannabis. [@1] Officers also recovered a machete, an
axe, two Columbian knives and one chain whip in the same arrest. [@1]

The Zimbabwe Republic Police published its own account of the arrests on X the
next day, naming the same five suspects and the same 171 sachets and plastic bag
of dagga. [@2]

The police post said the machete, axe, two Columbia knives and chain whip were
recovered from the same group. [@2]
