The South African Police Service reported that 16,571 suspects were arrested
between 4 and 10 August 2025 during Operation Shanela across the country's nine
provinces. [@1] Among the arrests, 2,065 wanted suspects were arrested for
serious crimes including drug trafficking, 1,390 were arrested for assault with
intent to cause grievous bodily harm, and 796 were arrested for drug dealing
while 2,336 others were arrested for drug possession. [@1] Other breakthroughs
in the operations included the interception of a vehicle entering the country
from Eswatini via Paulpietersburg, carrying 90 bags of dagga, with a 32-year-old
suspect arrested by police. [@1]
