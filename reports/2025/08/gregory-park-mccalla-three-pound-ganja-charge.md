Portmore police charged a 71-year-old farmer with possession of ganja and
dealing in ganja after an incident on Queens Avenue in Gregory Park, St
Catherine, on Saturday. [@1] The farmer is Austin McCalla, also called “Bigga
Man”, of Bellas Gate, Old Harbour, in the parish. [@1]

The Portmore police said that at about 2:30 pm a police team observed McCalla at
a stall making a purchase, with a black bag in his right hand. [@1] On seeing
the police, McCalla allegedly threw the bag to the ground, and the officers
approached, accosted and searched him before retrieving the bag. [@1] The police
said the bag was searched, and a large quantity of vegetable matter resembling
ganja was reportedly found. [@1] The contents of the bag weighed over three
pounds, about 1.36 kg. [@1] McCalla was arrested and subsequently charged with
possession of ganja and dealing in ganja. [@1]

His court date is being finalised. [@1] The account of the arrest and the
charges came from the Portmore police. [@1]
