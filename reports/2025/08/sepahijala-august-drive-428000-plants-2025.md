On 31 August 2025, Sepahijala district police in joint operations with the Border Security
Force (BSF), Tripura State Rifles battalions, forest officials and other agencies destroyed
illicit cannabis cultivation across the district from early morning. [@1,2] In total, more
than 428,000 ganja saplings and plants were destroyed, which the reports called one of the
district's largest one-day destruction drives. [@1,2]

In the Jatrapur police station area, officers destroyed 12 nursery plots holding nearly
200,000 saplings in the deep forest at South Moheshpur and Katalia under the Sonamura
subdivision. [@1,2] A second drive under Sonamura police station, with 81 BN BSF, the 9th and
7th BN TSR and forest teams, cleared 19 plots spread over 35 acres (about 14.2 hectares) at
Matinagar, Bijoy Nagar, Khatamura and Batadola, uprooting about 228,000 plants. [@1,2] No
arrests, charges or case registrations over the destroyed plantations were reported. [@1,2]
