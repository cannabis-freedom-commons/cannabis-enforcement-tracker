A 16-year-old and a 17-year-old from Osaka Prefecture were arrested at Fukuoka
Airport on 30 August 2025 for smuggling liquid containing the cannabis compound
THC from Thailand. [@1,2] Customs officers found about 14 kg of the liquid,
around 7 kg in each boy's suitcase, hidden inside retort curry boxes during an
inspection. [@1,2]

The pair had travelled together to Thailand two days earlier and returned
separately via Bangkok's Suvarnabhumi Airport. [@2] Both admitted transporting
the drugs for compensation. [@1,2] Moji Customs confirmed the haul was the
largest seizure of cannabis liquid in its jurisdiction. [@1]
