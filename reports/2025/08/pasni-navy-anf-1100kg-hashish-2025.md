In an intelligence-led operation off the Pasni coast of Balochistan, the Pakistan Navy together with the Anti-Narcotics Force seized a narcotics shipment worth an estimated 38 million dollars. [@1, 2] About 1,250 kilograms of drugs were intercepted before they could reach international markets through the Arabian Sea. [@1, 2]

The consignment consisted of approximately 1,100 kilograms of hashish, 50 kilograms of crystal methamphetamine and 100 kilograms of heroin, taken from smugglers moving the cargo across the Arabian Sea. [@1, 2] Officials handed the narcotics to the ANF for legal proceedings and further investigation. [@1, 2]
