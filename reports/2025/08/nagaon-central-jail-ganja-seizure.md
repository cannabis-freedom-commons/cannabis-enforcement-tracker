Nagaon Deputy Commissioner Debashish Sharma and Senior Superintendent of Police
Swapnaneel Deka made a surprise visit to Nagaon Central Jail on Friday 29 August
2025. [@1] Two personnel identified as Patangi and Hazarika allegedly supplied
drugs to a convict named Salim, who officials said ran a large-scale drug
network from within the prison. [@1] Sharma said Patangi and Hazarika brought
drugs from outside and handed them to Salimuddin, adding that staff hide
everything beforehand when tipped about inspections. [@1] Jail Superintendent
Mukul Bhuyan was accused of shielding the illegal activities. [@1]

The follow-up account says the inspection team seized a significant haul from
Canteen No. 4 including energy drinks, fried fish, drugs, gutkha packets and
ganja. [@2]

Sharma said strict action would be taken, the government had been informed, and
he had personally witnessed the practices. [@1] The District Commissioner
reported the matter to the authorities for a detailed investigation. [@1] The
Assam government suspended superintendent Mukul Bhuyan for indiscipline after
the contraband discovery. [@2] The suspension order under the Assam Services
(Discipline and Appeal) Rules, 1964 followed a report from the Inspector General
of Prisons. [@2] The Deputy Commissioner chaired a review meeting and directed a
detailed probe with instructions that those found guilty be punished under the
law. [@2]
