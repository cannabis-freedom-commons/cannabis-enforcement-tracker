On the night of 6 August 2025 a specialised police team led by an Inspector
carried out a targeted cordon and search operation at Puruni Landing on the
Mazaruni River. [@2] Acting on intelligence, the team went to the home of
37-year-old Hudley Khan, also known as Allan, where his reputed wife,
32-year-old Venezuelan national Yolimar Del Valle Rivas, was present. [@2] Both
people present were arrested, told of the offences against them and taken to the
Bartica Police Station. [@2]

Officers asked to search for illegal firearms, narcotics and ammunition, and
Khan said he had narcotics in his room. [@1] Khan directed police to a
multi-coloured bag holding 23 bulky brown parcels and one transparent plastic
bag of leaves, seeds and stems suspected to be cannabis. [@2] At the station the
suspected cannabis weighed 12,542.5 grams, about 12.5 kg. [@2] Officers also
seized one plastic bag of a whitish rock-like substance suspected to be
methamphetamine. [@2] The suspected methamphetamine weighed 108.8 grams. [@2]
Police documented $1,120,810 in cash suspected to be narcotics proceeds and 5.9
pennyweights of raw gold. [@2]

Khan admitted owning the narcotics in a video-recorded interview at the scene
but later declined further questioning, and both suspects remained in custody
while investigations continued. [@2] The accounts in both outlets came from a
Guyana Police Force statement. [@1]
