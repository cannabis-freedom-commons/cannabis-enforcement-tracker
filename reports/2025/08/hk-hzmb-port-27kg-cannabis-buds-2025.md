Two male passengers aged 61 and 52 arrived in Hong Kong from Macao by private
car through the Hong Kong-Zhuhai-Macao Bridge Hong Kong Port on 12 August 2025.
[@1] Customs officers clearing them found about 13.5 kg of suspected cannabis
buds hidden in plastic vacuum bags in each of their suitcases. [@1] Officers
seized about 27 kilograms of suspected cannabis buds in total, which Customs
valued at about HK$5.5 million. [@1]

Both men were arrested and each was charged with one count of trafficking in a
dangerous drug. [@1] The case was listed for mention at West Kowloon
Magistrates' Courts on 14 August 2025. [@1]

Customs said trafficking in a dangerous drug carries a maximum penalty on
conviction of a HK$5 million fine and life imprisonment. [@2]
