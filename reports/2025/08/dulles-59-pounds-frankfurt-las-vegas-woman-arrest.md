A 42-year-old woman from Las Vegas, Nevada, was arrested at Washington Dulles
International Airport on 3 August 2025 after officers found more than 59 pounds
of marijuana in her checked baggage. [@1] Metropolitan Washington Airports
Authority police charged her with transporting a controlled substance into
Virginia and possession with intent to distribute, both felonies. [@2]

U.S. Customs and Border Protection officers found the marijuana in two
hard-sided suitcases while inspecting baggage being loaded onto a
Frankfurt-bound flight. [@2] The suitcases carried 50 vacuum-sealed packages
holding a green leafy substance that field tested positive for marijuana. [@2]
CBP identified the passenger from the baggage tags, detained her at the
departure gate and took her and her luggage to an inspection station for a
secondary examination. [@2] CBP said the shipment could have sold for two to
three times as much in Europe, depending on potency. [@2] CBP officers handed
the woman and the marijuana over to airport police. [@2]

CBP said its officers keep encountering travelers carrying suitcases of
marijuana bound for Europe, often for trafficking organizations. [@2] CBP said
marijuana remains illegal under federal law and that exporting it from the
United States is prohibited, even where states have decriminalised it. [@2]
