Customs officers at Roland Garros Airport on Réunion arrested a traveller
arriving from Paris on Thursday 7 August 2025. [@1,2] The traveller, described as
a mule, had arrived on a flight from Paris Charles de Gaulle carrying a large
quantity of drugs. [@1] Questioning in the case was expected to take place
during the course of that Thursday. [@1]

The interception was described as an important seizure for the Réunion customs
officers on duty that day. [@1] Officers seized 31 kg of cannabis resin from the
traveller in the airport interception. [@1,2] Officers also seized 5 kg of cocaine
from the same traveller in the same interception. [@1] The overall load had
initially been described as nearly 35 kilos of drugs before the separate weights
were given. [@1]

The traveller was carrying the drugs when customs officers stopped him at the
airport after the Paris flight landed. [@1] The cannabis component recorded here
is the 31 kg of resin, while the cocaine is mentioned only to explain the mixed
seizure and is excluded from cannabis figures. [@1]

Investigations by the territorial judicial police service, the STPJ, identified two other people linked to the trafficking. [@2] Three people in total were arrested and placed in police custody, two were remanded in pre-trial detention and the third was released without charge. [@3] The national police said its STPJ investigators were working relentlessly to dismantle trafficking of every kind. [@4]
