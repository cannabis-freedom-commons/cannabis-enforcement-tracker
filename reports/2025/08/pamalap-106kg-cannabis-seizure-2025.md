Guinea's border police searched suspect homes in the border locality of Pamalap,
Alphaya sector, on 15 August 2025. [@1]

Officers seized a total of 106 kilos of cannabis during the Pamalap operation.
[@1] Officers also recovered several cannabis seeds in the same operation. [@2]
Officers found cannabis plants and destroyed them on the spot. [@2]

Police reported the seizure figures in a national police directorate communique
as part of a continuing offensive against drug trafficking and criminal
networks. [@1]
