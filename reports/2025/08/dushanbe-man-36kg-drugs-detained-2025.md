Officers of the Ismoili Somoni District Department of Internal Affairs disrupted
large-scale drug trafficking as a result of operational measures. [@1] A
37-year-old man from the Bobojon Gafurov district of the Sughd region, Ayzam
Akhmedov, was detained while attempting to sell drugs. [@1] Police seized more
than 36 kilograms of drugs from the suspect, including hashish, opium and
methamphetamine. [@2]

A criminal case was opened against the suspect under Article 200 of the Criminal
Code of the Republic of Tajikistan, which covers illegal trafficking of narcotic
substances. [@1] Preliminary investigation is ongoing at the time of the report.
