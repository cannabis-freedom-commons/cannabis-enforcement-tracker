Gardai seized cannabis plants worth more than 750,000 euro after searching a premises in Drogheda, County Louth, on Tuesday 5 August 2025. [@1,2]

Officers from the Dublin Crime Response Team and Drogheda found a large-scale cannabis cultivation factory in the search, part of an ongoing operation against the sale and supply of drugs. [@1,2] The seized plants were sent to Forensic Science Ireland for analysis. [@1,2]

Two men in their forties were arrested at the scene and detained at stations in the north east under section 2 of the Criminal Justice (Drug Trafficking) Act 1996. [@1,2] Assistant Commissioner Cliona Richardson said dismantling the sophisticated cultivation operation would affect the ability of those involved to cause harm in communities. [@1,2]
