Officers of the organised-crime department of the criminal-investigation
directorate of the Interior Ministry for the Republic of Bashkortostan detained
a previously convicted 46-year-old resident of Sterlitamak who grew
drug-containing plants for sale. [@1] The officers acted on operational
information about his activity. [@1]

During a search of his private house, investigators found and seized about 14
kilograms of cannabis and poppy straw, a combined figure the police statement
does not split between the two substances. [@1] In three greenhouses on the
man's land, police found 118 cannabis bushes whose drug mass, converted to dry
weight, came to about 35 kilograms. [@1] They also seized cultivation equipment:
spades, rakes and watering cans. [@1]

The man admitted he had grown "durman-travu" to sell to drug-dependent people
hand to hand; the term is a Russian colloquial name for drug plants. [@1]
Investigators and inquiry officers of the Sterlitamak interior ministry opened
criminal cases under part 3 of article 30 with point "g" of part 4 of article
228.1, and part 1 of article 231, of the Russian Criminal Code: attempted
large-scale drug sale and illegal cultivation of drug-containing plants. [@1] A
court placed the suspect in custody as a preventive measure. [@1]

The Bashkortostan state television company reported the man's detention and the
seizure of the cannabis in the greenhouses. [@2]
