SERNIC seized about 200 kilograms of cannabis sativa, known locally as suruma,
at the Mboza checkpoint in Moatize district, Tete. [@1] In connection with the
case, SERNIC detained the driver and the conductor of the vehicle. [@2] The
owners of the consignment remained at large while the driver and conductor were
held. [@3] The suspects said they had only agreed to carry a parcel handed over
by a client at the Tete city bus terminal for delivery to a guesthouse in
Ulongue town. [@3]

The cannabis was travelling in interdistrict semi-collective passenger
transport, hidden in bales of second-hand clothing known locally as calamidade.
[@2] The load was presented as calamidade clothing allegedly bound for Angonia
district. [@3] SERNIC spokesperson Celina Roque said the cannabis came from the
Kingdom of Eswatini and was ultimately bound for Angonia. [@3] SERNIC estimated
the consignment could have yielded almost six million meticais to the criminal
network. [@3]

The seizure resulted from search actions carried out by the authorities at the
checkpoint. [@3] The minibus used for the transport was also seized after its
passengers were transferred to other vehicles. [@3]
