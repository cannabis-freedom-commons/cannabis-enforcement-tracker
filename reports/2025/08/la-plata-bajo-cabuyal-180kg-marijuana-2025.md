Ninth Brigade troops of the 26th Cacique Pigoanza Infantry Battalion, working with the Huila police special operations group (GOES), found 180 kg of marijuana in an abandoned car during patrols at Bajo Cabuyal, a rural area of La Plata, Huila. [@1,2]

Officers opened 35 large packages wrapped in black vinyl tape holding a plant substance resembling marijuana, and weighing set the shipment at 180 kg. [@1,2] At half a gram per average dose, the find equalled about 360,000 doses kept from reaching schools and neighbourhoods, the command said. [@1,2] The reports name no suspects. [@1,2]
