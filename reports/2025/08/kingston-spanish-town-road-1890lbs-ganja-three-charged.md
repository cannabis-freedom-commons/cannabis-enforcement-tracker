Three men were arrested in an operation by the Firearms and Narcotics
Investigation Division on Spanish Town Road, Kingston 11, on 15 August 2025.
[@2] Police said the accused were found on their way to one of the country's
ports of entry with 1,890 pounds of compressed ganja seized on 15 August 2025.
[@1]

Officers intercepted two of the men in a Toyota Hiace bus in which knitted bags
said to contain the ganja were found. [@2] The third man was arrested in a motor
car, which police said was piloting the other two towards the Norman Manley
International Airport. [@2]

The three are jointly charged with conspiracy to export ganja, possession of
criminal property, trafficking and dealing in ganja, taking steps preparatory to
export ganja, and possession of ganja. [@1] On 27 August 2025 Parish Judge
Maxine Ellis granted bail of $1 million each to two of the accused in the
Kingston and St Andrew Parish Court, imposing strict conditions. [@2] Those two
must surrender their travel documents, report regularly to the police, and had
stop orders imposed against them. [@2]

The remaining accused, Leon Hines, had not been granted bail by late November
and appeared before Parish Judge Paula Blake Powell from lockup. [@1] The
prosecution told the November court sitting that a forensic certificate for the
drugs was outstanding, and the men were ordered to return on 6 January 2026 for
case management. [@1] At the August bail hearing the prosecutor opposed bail
strongly, citing the seriousness of the offences and caution statements in which
some of the accused admitted involvement in the trafficking operation. [@2]
