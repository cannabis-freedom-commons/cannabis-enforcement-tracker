Montenegro's border police arrested S.P., a 48-year-old Serbian citizen, and
I.V., a 36-year-old Montenegrin, in Ulcinj and found marijuana on them. [@1] The
two were taken to the misdemeanor court for a violation of the Drug Abuse
Prevention Act, and S.P. was then served a decision revoking his residence
permit with a ban on entering the country. [@2] The Ulcinj action processed nine
people for violations of the Law on Foreigners. [@1]

At the Božaj crossing on the way into Montenegro, border officers and customs
found three handmade cigarettes with marijuana and a package of plant matter
resembling marijuana on K.K., a 29-year-old Turkish citizen travelling as a bus
passenger. [@1] K.K. was arrested for a violation of the Drug Abuse Prevention
Act. [@1] On Velika Plaza, the long beach at Ulcinj, Mobile Unit officers
checked S.M., 37, and found a bag of marijuana weighing over 50 grams on him.
[@1] A misdemeanor charge was filed against S.M. over that marijuana. [@1]

The Border Police Sector said the controls, run with the Customs Administration
and the prosecutors' offices, processed 18 people in all, 13 of them foreign
citizens. [@1] The same announcement also covered a tobacco and cigarette case
at Božaj, a vehicle stop near Bijelo Polje and cigarette seizures in Bar, none
of them involving cannabis. [@1]
