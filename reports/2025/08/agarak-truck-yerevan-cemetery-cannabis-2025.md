Officers of Armenia's State Revenue Committee searching a truck arriving from
Iran at the Agarak border checkpoint found a plastic bag holding 282 grams of
methamphetamine and a metal container filled with 33 grams of hashish oil. [@1]
An Iranian citizen was detained on suspicion of drug smuggling and taken to the
Syunik department of the Investigative Committee, where a criminal case was
opened. [@1] The drugs were to be left at one of Yerevan's cemeteries, where
Revenue Committee officers set an ambush, and a married couple who are Armenian
citizens were detained after they collected the drugs from the hiding place.
[@1] Investigators said the couple were users of a Telegram platform selling
drugs and planned to sell the drugs they collected through it. [@1]

A search of the Iranian citizen's truck found another 650 grams of
methamphetamine in hiding places. [@1] Officers also found a package of
methamphetamine at a cache site in Yerevan and the investigator seized it by
law. [@1] In all, the operational and investigative work found and took out of
illegal circulation more than 1.5 kg of various types of drugs. [@1]

Armenia's Investigative Committee press service said packages with 1.5 kg of
various drugs were found in a truck at the Agarak checkpoint on the
Armenian-Iranian border and that an Iranian citizen was detained. [@2] The same
press service account said the couple were detained after the drugs were taken
from the cache and that a criminal case was opened. [@2] Operational measures
continue to identify other people possibly involved in circulating the drugs and
to find packages already placed around Yerevan. [@1]
