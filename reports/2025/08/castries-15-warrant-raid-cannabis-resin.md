Royal Saint Lucia Police Force officers arrested several people during a
pre-dawn operation in Castries on Sunday 24 August 2025. [@1] Police said the
arrests were made on suspicion of narcotics trafficking, firearms trafficking
and violent crimes. [@1] The Royal Saint Lucia Police Force mounted a
large-scale, multi-agency operation targeting what authorities described as
high-risk areas. [@1] A simultaneous traffic enforcement effort stopped dozens
of vehicles, issued multiple tickets and impounded several cars, and one person
was arrested during the checks. [@1]

The operation ran from 4:00 a.m. to 10:00 a.m. and combined targeted house
searches with widespread traffic enforcement. [@1] Officers executed 15 search
warrants during the exercise. [@1] Officers seized significant quantities of
cocaine and cannabis resin, one firearm with ammunition, electronic devices and
a large sum of cash. [@1] The operation focused on the Darling Road and Conway
areas and involved the Drug Squad, K9 Unit, Special Services Unit, Gangs,
Narcotics and Firearms Unit, Police Mobile Unit and Traffic Department. [@2]

The operation came exactly one week after a wave of deadly gun violence in
Castries left two men dead and several others injured. [@2] Police said
investigations into all matters related to the operation were ongoing. [@1]
