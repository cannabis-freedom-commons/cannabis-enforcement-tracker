Two former employees of the South Carolina Department of Juvenile Justice were
arrested on 27 August 2025 and charged with providing contraband to minors in
state custody. [@1] A department spokesperson said Jordan Major, 33, of
Lexington County, was charged with furnishing contraband to a juvenile in DJJ
custody and with misconduct in office. [@1] Authorities said Major supplied
minors with edible THC gummies. [@1] Major was suspended without pay after being
identified as a suspect and later separated from the agency. [@1] He turned
himself in on Wednesday at the Alvin S. Glenn Detention Center in Columbia. [@1]

A second former employee, Shala Dunham, 38, of Orangeburg, was also arrested
that day and charged with providing contraband to minors. [@1] Investigators
said she supplied vaping devices to youths while employed at the agency. [@1]
Dunham was suspended without pay once identified as a suspect and subsequently
left the department. [@1] She also surrendered at the Alvin S. Glenn Detention
Center. [@1] ABC Columbia reported that both had been relieved of their duties.
[@2]

The Department of Juvenile Justice did not release further details and said the
cases remained under investigation. [@1]
