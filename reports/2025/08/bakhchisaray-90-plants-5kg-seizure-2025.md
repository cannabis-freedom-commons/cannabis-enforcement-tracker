Crimea’s drug-control directorate and Bakhchisaraysky district police found a
cannabis plantation on the household plot of a 50-year-old man from an unnamed
village during Operation Mak-2025, an operational-preventive police operation.
[@1] Officers seized more than 90 bushes of plants that looked like hemp and
about five kilograms of dried plant parts and seeds from the private property.
[@1] Forensic testing showed the green plants belonged to the cannabis genus and
contained a narcotic substance banned from cultivation and circulation. [@1]

The owner said he had planted the crop himself at the beginning of May that year
and tended it by periodically weeding and watering. [@1] He said he cultivated
the plants for personal use. [@1]

Police opened two criminal cases against the suspect under provisions covering
illegal cultivation and large-scale drug possession. [@1] The authorities placed
him under a written undertaking not to leave and to behave properly. [@1] First
Sevastopolsky reported that he faced up to 10 years in prison. [@2]
