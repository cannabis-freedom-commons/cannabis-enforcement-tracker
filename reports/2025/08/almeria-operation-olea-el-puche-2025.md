National Police officers dismantled a heavily armed family drug gang in Almeria
in an operation dubbed Olea, making five arrests. [@1,2] The clan ran indoor
cannabis farms and stored and moved hashish from properties tied to one family
in El Puche and on a farm in El Acebuche. [@1,2] The investigation stays open
and more arrests are expected. [@1]

## Power cuts gave the grows away

Officers opened the probe after repeated power cuts in the El Puche
neighbourhood pointed to illegal electricity siphoning, typical of indoor
grows. [@1] Surveillance led them to several properties used for cultivation,
stash storage, weapons concealment and laundering cash. [@1] The raids were led
by the Provincial Judicial Police with the backing of Investigating Court
No. 6. [@1]

Police seized about two tonnes of hashish in 49 bales and 740 kg of marijuana,
including 1,745 plants at various stages of growth weighing 176 kg. [@1,2]
They also took a vehicle, packaging materials, a frequency jammer, a taser,
satellite GPS equipment, a tracking beacon and 11,445 euros in cash. [@1]

## A war arsenal

What stood out was the weaponry: around 20 firearms, all loaded and ready to
fire, including a 12mm semi-automatic shotgun, a Fabarm pump-action shotgun, a
Smith & Wesson semi-automatic rifle, pistols and an Uzi submachine gun. [@1,2]
A government representative called it the third biggest recent hit against drug
trafficking in the province, after operations in Pechina and Huercal de
Almeria. [@1] The national police chief in Almeria described the gang as highly
active and dangerous. [@1]

A follow-up account said police sources told Diario de Almeria that at least
some of the firearms matched makes and models sent by NATO countries to
Ukraine, a possible diversion investigators were still examining. [@2]
