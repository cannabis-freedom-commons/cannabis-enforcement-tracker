Shemroy Robertson, called “Minutes”, was found guilty of aiding the commission
of trafficking cannabis. [@1] Robertson appeared before Senior Magistrate Fabayo
Azore at the Vigilance Magistrates’ Court. [@1] The court sentenced him to two
years imprisonment and fined him $30,000 for aiding the commission of
trafficking 1.174 kilograms of cannabis. [@1]

According to the Customs Anti Narcotic Unit, the conviction stemmed from an
operation conducted on June 17, 2021. [@1] During the operation officers
intercepted a minibus at Ann’s Grove Public Road, East Coast Demerara, and
arrested a suspect with that quantity of cannabis. [@1] Robertson was
subsequently arrested in relation to the operation on June 28, 2021. [@1]

He had previously been arrested and charged on January 15, 2016 along with one
other suspect for possession of cocaine. [@1] The other suspect was released
while Robertson was ordered to stand trial in the High Court and granted bail
which was still pending. [@1] He had also previously been arrested and charged
along with three others on March 6, 2017, but the charges against him were
dropped. [@1]
