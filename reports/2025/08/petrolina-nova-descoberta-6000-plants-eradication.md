Military Police officers in Pernambuco uprooted 6,000 cannabis plants on Tuesday
12 August 2025 in Petrolina, in the state's Sertão region. [@1] The plantation
was found at Nova Descoberta, a rural hamlet of the municipality, during an
operation the police named Operação Saturação. [@1]

According to the Military Police, officers also seized 10 kilograms of cannabis,
a .38 revolver, six rounds of .38 ammunition, a 5.5 mm air rifle and a mobile
phone. [@1] The police said all the material was taken to the Civil Police
station for the legal measures to be taken. [@1] No one had been arrested by the
time the report was published. [@1]

The Petrolina outlet Edenevaldo Alves published the same police tally for
Operação Saturação, adding that the seized phone was a Redmi handset. [@2]
Edenevaldo Alves listed 6,000 plants eradicated and 10 kilograms of cannabis
seized, with six intact .38 rounds. [@2]
