Three American women arrived in Belize on a JetBlue flight from New York on
Wednesday, August 20, 2025, and were stopped at the Philip Goldson International
Airport. [@2] Police found 17.35 grams of marijuana on Arisa Philson, 12.41
grams on Iyahna Rayside and 3.0 grams on Alliah Kemp, about 32.76 grams in all.
[@1] The women were arrested at the airport and spent the night at the Ladyville
Police Station. [@3]

The next morning, 21 August 2025, the three appeared in Court #1 before
Magistrate Ludlow Black and all pleaded guilty to importing a controlled drug.
[@2] The magistrate explained that Belizean law allows personal possession of up
to 10 grams of cannabis but treats importing it as a separate offence. [@1]
Citing their guilty pleas, lack of prior convictions and the small amounts, the
magistrate imposed a non-custodial sentence. [@1] Each woman was fined $300 plus
$5 in court costs, $915 in total, to be paid immediately or serve three months
in prison. [@1] They paid their fines, were released and went on with their
vacation, joining a fourth friend who had not been detained. [@1]
