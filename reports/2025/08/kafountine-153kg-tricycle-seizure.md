The operation took place on the night of Wednesday 27 August 2025. [@3] No
arrest was reported: the rider escaped and the gendarmes were still trying to
identify him. [@1]

Officers found 153 kilograms of chanvre indien, the local French term for Indian
hemp, packed in black waterproof sacks inside the vehicle. [@2] They seized the
blue tricycle along with the drugs. [@2] The seizure was made in Kabar, an
outlying quarter of the commune of Kafountine. [@2] Investigators were still
searching for the owner of the load. [@1]

The authorities say Senegalese law punishes drug trafficking severely with heavy
prison terms and the systematic confiscation of the means used to transport it.
[@1]
