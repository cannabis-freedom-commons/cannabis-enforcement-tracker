Police in the Armenian city of Gyumri arrested a 24-year-old foreign national on
suspicion of placing drug packages around the city for sale. [@1] The Police
Ministry's press service said officers had received information that the young
foreigner was buying drugs and selling them in different districts of Gyumri by
leaving them as packages. [@1]

At 20:00 on 22 August 2025, seeing police approaching him in Gorky central park,
he ran off and threw several packets into a house courtyard on Teryan Street
before officers detained him. [@1] A search of the man found 11 packets holding
about 14 grams of marijuana. [@1] He told police he had bought the drug through
the Telegram app for onward sale. [@1]

A criminal case was opened, and officers carrying out checks in it found five
more packets placed in the park and eight thrown away during the chase. [@1] The
foreigner was arrested and police said the circumstances were still being
established. [@1]
