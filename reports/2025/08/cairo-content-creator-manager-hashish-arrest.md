Cairo security forces arrested a content creator resident in Cairo Governorate
together with his business manager, finding an unlicensed firearm with the
creator. [@1] Dar El-Hilal reported that the creator was detained with his
manager inside a well-known cafe in New Cairo after procedures were regularised
and under a prosecution permit. [@2] Officers found the two men with an
unspecified quantity of hashish and ice, in addition to the unlicensed firearm.
[@2] When confronted, both men admitted that they possessed the drugs for
personal use. [@1] Legal measures were taken against both men and they were
referred to the competent authorities for further action. [@1]

The case began with several complaints saying the creator published videos on
social media that violated family values and misused social-media platforms.
[@1] Both men admitted that they published those videos on the creator's
social-media page in order to increase views and earn money. [@1]
