Moldovan police detained a 46-year-old priest from Chisinau together with a
26-year-old man and his 18-year-old cohabiting partner on suspicion of
participating in a drug-trafficking network. [@1] Police announced on 8 August
2025 that the three suspects had been detained and placed under preliminary
arrest for 30 days. [@1] According to officers, the 26-year-old man and his
partner had rented an apartment in Chisinau that they used both as their
dwelling and as a storage place for drugs. [@1]

During a search of that rented apartment on 5 August, officers found 13 packets
of marijuana together with electronic scales, packaging materials and telephones
that officers said had been used in the criminal activity. [@1] In the priest's
house, officers found hashish and marijuana, another set of electronic scales,
homemade implements for consuming drugs and several mobile telephones. [@1]

Under the preliminary police version, the suspects distributed drugs in Chisinau
and Straseni by using the Telegram application. [@1] If found guilty, all three
face imprisonment of from 5 to 10 years. [@1]
