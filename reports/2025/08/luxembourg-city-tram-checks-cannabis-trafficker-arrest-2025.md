On 27 August 2025 police carried out a large-scale check in public transport in
Luxembourg City, concentrated on the tram line linking the main station to the
Glacis and on the areas around the stops. [@1] Officers controlled 62 people and
issued one fine and one report for an offence against the drug legislation. [@1]

Near the Hamilius stop a man tried to evade the check, and the suspicious
movements officers saw suggested he was trying to hide drugs. [@1] Officers
seized several packets of cannabis, and a scanner test came back positive. [@1]
The public prosecutor’s office ordered the arrest of the suspect, described as a
presumed drug trafficker. [@1] The operation deployed 17 police officers with a
dog unit to increase the police presence in public transport and improve
passengers’ safety. [@1]

The checks also produced four reports for foreigners in an irregular situation,
and one person was taken to the retention centre for illegal stay and an
immigration offence. [@1]
