Saudi Arabia executed four Somali nationals and three Ethiopians in the
southern region of Najran on Saturday 2 August 2025 for smuggling hashish into
the kingdom, the Saudi Press Agency announced. [@1,2] They were seven of eight
people put to death that day amid a surge in capital punishment, particularly
for drug convictions. [@1,2]

The executions brought the 2025 total to 230, of which 154 were for
drug-related offences, according to an AFP tally of official announcements.
[@1,2] At that pace the kingdom was on track to exceed the record 338
executions of the previous year. [@1,2] Observers link the rise to the
anti-drug campaign launched in 2023; the death penalty for drug offences had
been paused for about three years before resuming at the end of 2022. [@1,2]
