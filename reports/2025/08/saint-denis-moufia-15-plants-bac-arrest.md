Police of the anti-crime brigade, the Bac, arrested a resident of the Moufia
neighbourhood in Saint-Denis on Tuesday 19 August 2025 and accused him of
growing zamal, the Creole name for cannabis on Réunion. [@1] Officers searched
his home and found 15 cannabis plants in two growing rooms, along with equipment
used to tend them. [@1]

The suspect was placed in police custody and admitted the facts, the police
account reported by local media said. [@1] Two other Réunion outlets, LINFO.re
and Imaz Press Réunion, reported the same 19 August arrest, the two growing
rooms, the 15 plants and the cultivation equipment. [@2] Police said the arrest
followed the discovery of a cannabis crop at the man's home in the Moufia area.
[@2]

LINFO.re said the man was summoned before the officer in charge of the case, the
officier de police judiciaire, as part of the proceedings against him. [@2]
Zinfos974, citing the police, reported that the man would later be summoned
before the police court, the tribunal de police. [@1]

Imaz Press Réunion also reported that police had searched his home and found the
two growing rooms, 15 plants and cultivation equipment. [@3]
