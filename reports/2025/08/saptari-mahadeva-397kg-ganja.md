Police recovered approximately 400 kilograms of cannabis from Mahadeva in
Saptari, in a seizure by a joint team of the Armed Police Force's proposed
Border Security Unit and Nepal Police. [@1] The cannabis was found hidden in
bushes in an abandoned state in Dakaneshwari Municipality-5, Mahadeva. [@1] On
23 Shrawan, a joint team of the proposed Border Security Company at Pato and
Nepal Police seized 397 kilograms of ganja from Dakneshwori Municipality-5,
Mahadeva, in Saptari district. [@2]

On Friday, 8 August, a joint team of the Armed Police Force Nepal's proposed
Border Security Company and the Nepal Police recovered approximately 397 kg of
marijuana. [@3] The marijuana was found abandoned in the bushes near the Pokhair
Canal at Mahadeva, Dakneshwori Municipality-5, in Saptari district. [@3] Acting
on a special tip-off, a joint security team led by Armed Police Deputy
Superintendent Narendra Singh Dhami found the marijuana packed in 17 bags. [@3]
Armed Police Force spokesperson Shailendra Thapa said the contraband was
concealed in 17 pokas, or bags, and that security forces recovered 397 kilograms
of cannabis. [@1]

Authorities were investigating those involved in the illegal storage and
possible trafficking of the seized cannabis, and no arrests had been made so
far. [@1] The recovered marijuana was handed over to the concerned authorities
for necessary legal action, according to DSP Shailendra Thapa, assistant
spokesperson. [@3]
