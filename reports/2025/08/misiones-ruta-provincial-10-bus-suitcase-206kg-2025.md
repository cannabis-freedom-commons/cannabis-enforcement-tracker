Gendarmes of the "Azara" group of Squadron 8 "Alto Uruguay" stopped a
long-distance bus at a roadside check on Provincial Route 10 in Misiones
province. [@1] The bus had set out from San Pedro, Misiones, and was heading for
Retiro in Buenos Aires. [@1] While the hold was being searched, the
anti-narcotics dog "León" reacted strongly to a suitcase. [@1]

Forensic staff opened the suitcase and found fruit and vegetables, and under
them a double bottom holding eight packages. [@1] Field Narcotest tests came
back positive for marijuana, with a total weight of 2 kilos 060 grams. [@1]
Before opening it, the gendarmes put the luggage through a scanner and saw
foreign bodies inside. [@1]

MisionesOnline reported that the officers noticed the luggage had no
identification of its owner. [@3] The Federal Court and the Federal Prosecutor's
Office of Posadas, the federal court and prosecutor in the provincial capital,
ordered the whole drug load seized. [@2] The court also ordered inquiries to
continue to find whoever was responsible for the load. [@1] The Posadas federal
justice ordered the drug seized and the inquiry continued to find whoever was
responsible. [@4]
