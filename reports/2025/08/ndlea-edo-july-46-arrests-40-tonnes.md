NDLEA Edo State Commander Mitchell Ofoyeju, presenting the command's July 2025
scorecard in Benin City, said intelligence-led operations including raids on
Ihama Road and other drug flashpoints led to 46 arrests, comprising 38 males
and 8 females. [@1,2]

The command said it took 39,983.294 kg of narcotics out of circulation, of
which 39,977.644 kg was cannabis, alongside small quantities of psychotropic
substances, methamphetamine, cocaine and codeine syrup. [@1,2]

Seven cannabis farms measuring 15.852 hectares across Oke Forest in Uhunmwonde
and Ewere Forest in Uzebba, Owan West, were destroyed. [@1,2]

Among those arrested was 49-year-old Alaba Monday of Owan West, described as a
suspected drug baron and cultivator wanted since 2023, caught inside one farm
with eleven bags of processed cannabis weighing 115 kg. [@1] Roadside arrests
included 40-year-old Yusuf Isuku with 10.265 kg of skunk concealed in garri on
Wareke Road, Auchi, and 48-year-old Josiah Friday with 36 kg of cannabis on
Igara-Auchi Road. [@1]
