Tunisian customs officers found cannabis hidden in parcels at the cargo terminal
of Tunis-Carthage airport and arrested one person. [@1] The parcels were
declared as clothing and shoes arriving from abroad. [@1] Officers became
suspicious of certain shoe parcels and found the cannabis packed inside the shoe
soles. [@2] The exact weight of the cannabis seized was 96.3 kg. [@3]

All goods arriving from abroad are scanned and searched, customs said. [@2] The
parcels' recipient, a foreigner preparing to leave the country, was identified
and arrested. [@2] Officers drew up an offence report against him for importing
banned goods without authorisation. [@2] After consulting prosecutors, customs
handed the suspect and the drug to the security services for further
investigation. [@2]

Customs spokesman Colonel-Major Chokri Jabri announced the seizure in a phone
interview with Mosaïque FM on 27 February 2025. [@2] In the same interview Jabri
said customs had seized seven kilos of cocaine and more than one hundred kilos
of cannabis at border points since the start of the year. [@1]
