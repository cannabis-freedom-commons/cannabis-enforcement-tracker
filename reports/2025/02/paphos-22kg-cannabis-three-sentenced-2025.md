Shortly after 1.30pm on 23 February 2023, police stopped a car driven by a
23-year-old man in Paphos and found 22 packages holding 22 kg of dry cannabis;
he was arrested at once. [@2] Information from him led officers to a
21-year-old woman, arrested with about 400 g of cocaine, a gun and 25 illegal
fireworks in her car, and to the cannabis supplier, a 43-year-old man who was
also arrested. [@2] That evening three more suspected members of the drug
ring, two men aged 43 and 28 and a woman aged 43, were arrested, bringing the
day's arrests to six. [@2]

On 25 February 2025 the Paphos district court sentenced three of the men,
aged 31, 45 and 46, for importing large amounts of drugs: the 31-year-old
received 15 years in prison and his two accomplices 13 years each. [@1] The
court case rested on the February 2023 seizure of over 22 kg of cannabis plus
the cocaine, pistol, fireworks and large sums of money found in the same
operation. [@1,2]
