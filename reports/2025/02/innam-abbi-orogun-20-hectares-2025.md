NDLEA operatives supported by soldiers stormed a forest in the
Innam-Abbi/Orogun community of Ndokwa West Local Government Area, Delta State,
on Thursday, 27 February 2025. [@1,2] They destroyed over 50,000 kg of
cannabis growing on a 20-hectare farm, setting the crop ablaze. [@1,2]

Five suspects were arrested during the raid: Augustine Subaru, 45; Ukoba
Malachukwu, 53; Odalonu Emmanuel, 40; Esuabom Macaulay, 35; and Itoje Safe,
54. [@1,2] The reports do not name the farm's owner or say what charges
followed the arrests. [@1,2]

The agency announced the raid on 2 March 2025 as part of a nationwide weekly
roundup of arrests and seizures credited to NDLEA commands across the
country. [@1,2]
