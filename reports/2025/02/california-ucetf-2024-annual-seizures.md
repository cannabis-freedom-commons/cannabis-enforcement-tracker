In 2024 the state's Unified Cannabis Enforcement Taskforce (UCETF) seized and
destroyed over $254 million in unlicensed cannabis and products across
California. [@1,2] Officers seized 154,000 pounds (about 69.9 tonnes) of
illegal cannabis, eradicated 236,000 plants, confiscated 39 firearms, took
$748,146 in cash and made 35 arrests. [@1]

The task force served 150 search warrants in 46 cities across 20 counties.
[@1] Under Operation Grab Bag it also removed 2.2 million pieces of illegal
packaging, much of it marketed to children, alongside counterfeit labels
bearing forged state seals. [@1]

Governor Gavin Newsom created UCETF in 2022 to align state enforcement and
coordination between state, local and federal partners. [@1] Since its
inception it has seized nearly $600 million in unlicensed cannabis,
eradicated 583,000 plants and confiscated 167 firearms across 380 operations.
[@1]
