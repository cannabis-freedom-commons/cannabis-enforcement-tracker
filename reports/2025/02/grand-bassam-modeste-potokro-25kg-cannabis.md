Agents of Ivory Coast's DPSD, the drugs squad of the national police, found 25
blocks of cannabis weighing 25 kg hidden in dense bush at Modeste Potokro, a
locality on the Port-Bouët – Grand-Bassam road. [@1] The discovery was made on
Saturday 15 February 2025 during an operation mounted under Épervier 10, a
police drive against drug trafficking. [@1] Officers secured the area and opened
an investigation to trace the network and identify those responsible for the
shipment. [@1] The blocks were carefully wrapped and intended for the black
market. [@1]

No arrest was reported, and the accounts name no suspect. [@3] The DPSD squad
acted on precise information and searched the spot in vegetation along the
lagoon shore. [@2] Afrik Soir said officers secured the load and took it to the
competent services. [@2] Koaci said the DPSD intercepted the 25 blocks hidden in
dense bush and that the area was secured immediately. [@3] The Ivorian press
agency AIP credited the account to Police Secours, a police platform, in a post
on its Facebook page on 24 February 2025. [@1]
