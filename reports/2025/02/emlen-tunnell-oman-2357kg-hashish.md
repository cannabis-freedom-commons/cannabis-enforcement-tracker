On 7 February 2025, a crew from the U.S. Coast Guard cutter Emlen Tunnell seized
illegal drugs from a stateless vessel in the Arabian Sea. [@1] The boarding team
found 2,357 kg of hashish aboard. [@2] The cutter was patrolling off Oman as
part of Combined Task Force 150 when its crew spotted a suspicious dhow on a
well-known drug-smuggling route. [@2] Stars and Stripes described the haul as
nearly 5,300 pounds. [@1]

New Zealand took command of CTF 150 on 15 January. [@1] The interception was the
task force's first drug seizure under New Zealand's leadership. [@1] Royal New
Zealand Navy Commodore Rodger Ward was CTF 150's commander. [@2] Ward said he
was pleased the task force had achieved its first successful bust after only a
few weeks in command. [@2]

CTF 150 is one of five operational task forces under the Combined Maritime
Forces, based in Bahrain and focused on maritime security in the Middle East,
especially disrupting drug-smuggling operations. [@2] Ward said every bust
reduced the flow of finances to terrorist organisations and contributed to
maritime security and protection of the international rules-based system. [@3]
