The Gambia's Drug Law Enforcement Agency, backed by the police Police
Intervention Unit, arrested eight adult male Gambian nationals in a pre-dawn
operation on Saturday 8 February 2025 in Nyangbolong village, Foni Bintang
District, West Coast Region. [@2] The agency said the operation was aimed at
cannabis cultivators and dealers and resulted in the seizure of more than 200
bundles of suspected Cannabis sativa, 13 containers of 20 litres filled with
cannabis seeds intended for cultivation, and three unlicensed hunting rifles.
[@2]

The drugs agency said 35-year-old Abdoulie Sonko was held with 13 bags holding
165 bundles of suspected cannabis and 11 containers of cannabis seeds, and that
two unlicensed hunting guns were seized at his property. [@2] According to the
agency, Edrisa Sonko, 39, was found with nine bundles hidden in a blue plastic
container, Alagie Jammeh, 26, and Modou Sanyang, 35, had three bundles each, and
Desere Sanyang, 40, and Duwa Badjie were arrested for jointly possessing eight
bags of suspected cannabis. [@2] The agency said 49-year-old Yusupha Sonko was
held with one bundle, one parcel and other quantities of suspected cannabis, and
50-year-old Nfally Nyassi with a parcel and several quantities. [@2] Operatives
also found a bag holding nine suspected cannabis plants and two 20-litre
containers filled with cannabis seeds at a cashew farm. [@2]

The three hunting rifles were handed to the police station at Sibanor for
further investigation. [@2] The suspects were in the custody of DLEAG, the Drug
Law Enforcement Agency The Gambia, while the investigation continued. [@2] The
agency's management thanked the Inspector General of Police, the assistant
inspector in charge of operations and the Police Intervention Unit command for
their support in operations of this kind. [@2] APA reported that the operation
dismantled a large cannabis growing and trafficking network in the west of the
country and that the suspects were expected to be brought before a court in the
following days. [@1]

Block TV also reported that eight people were arrested over the weekend and that
more than 200 bundles of suspected Cannabis sativa, more than a dozen containers
of cannabis seeds and three unlicensed hunting rifles were seized. [@3]
