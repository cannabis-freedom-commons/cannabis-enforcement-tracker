Tonga Police investigated reports of illegal drug distribution near a school
zone. [@1] A suspected 16-year-old had been reported for distributing cannabis
leaves around a school area. [@2] The investigation led officers to a residence
in Ha'ateiho. [@2] A 43-year-old man and the 16-year-old were arrested and
charged with possession of illicit drugs. [@2]

Officers seized three cannabis plants with a total weight of 2,300 grams. [@2]
They also seized four ammunition bullets and drug-related utensils. [@2]

Police issued a warning urging the community to remain vigilant after the
arrests near the school. [@3] Police said investigations remain ongoing. [@2]
