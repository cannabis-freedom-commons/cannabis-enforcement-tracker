Cook Islands police arrested a man in February 2025 after Atiu police found
cannabis plants near a makeshift nursery located inland on the island. [@1] Cook
Islands News reported in January 2025 that the arrested man was found with 40
cannabis plants, a case police used to warn cultivators across Rarotonga and the
Pa Enua, the Cook Islands' outer islands. [@2] Several of the plants were
confiscated and destroyed, and police warned residents against cultivating
cannabis. [@1]

The man was transferred to Rarotonga, where his court case was still pending in
June 2025. [@1] Senior Constable Tuaine Rakei, who had been in charge of the
Atiu police station for 18 years, said the arrest was the first cannabis
cultivation case he had encountered on the island. [@1] Rakei said he and his
single colleague, the island's only two police officers, were now routinely
inspecting the area so that cultivation did not escalate. [@1]

Rakei said both officers go around to inspect and monitor such activity, and he
put the island's population at 383. [@1] He said crime on Atiu is mostly minor,
chiefly traffic offences, domestic disputes and occasional thefts. [@1]
