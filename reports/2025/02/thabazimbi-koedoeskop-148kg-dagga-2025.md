Thabazimbi police and Koedoeskop farmers stopped a white Scania truck with two trailers in the Koedoeskop area around 10:00 on 13 February 2025 in an intelligence-led operation. [@1,2] A search found 106 small bags, 10 large bags and two refuse bags of loose dagga weighing about 148.5kg, with an estimated street value of R500,000. [@1,2] Two Zimbabwean men aged 25 and 39 were arrested, and the truck they used was seized. [@1,2] Limpopo provincial commissioner Thembi Hadebe welcomed the arrests. [@1,2] The men were expected before the local magistrate's court soon on possession and dealing charges. [@1,2]
