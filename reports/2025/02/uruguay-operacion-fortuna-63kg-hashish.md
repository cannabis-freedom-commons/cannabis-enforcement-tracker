Three raids in the departments of Montevideo, Canelones and Rocha led to the
detention of 13 people, including three Brazilians and 10 Uruguayans whom
authorities linked to the organization in different roles. [@1] The General
Directorate for the Repression of Illicit Drug Trafficking (DGRTID) and the
Carrasco Customs risk-analysis division identified the group as transnational
and said it brought narcotics into Uruguay by air from California and Florida.
[@1]

Investigators intercepted 11 postal shipments containing hidden drugs and seized
approximately 63 kilograms of hashish during Operation Fortuna. [@1] The
Ministry said the hashish was mailed and concealed in electronic devices. [@1]
An earlier la diaria account said hashish from California was sent by air in
postal parcels concealed inside high-definition speakers to Carrasco Airport.
[@3] Officers also seized three vehicles, 257,555 Uruguayan pesos, 300 dollars,
mobile phones and assorted documents. [@1]

After court proceedings, the Organized Crime Court's First Turn formalized an
investigation against 12 detainees for offences related to exporting, importing,
transporting and storing prohibited drugs. [@1] The court ordered the 12 to
preventive detention for 120 days, while the remaining detainee was released.
[@1] la diaria reported that 12 people were formally charged and 63 kilograms of
hashish seized in Operation Fortuna. [@2] The Ministry estimated the seized
hashish was destined for Brazil and valued the total at about 3 million reais,
based on an approximate price of 45,000 reais per kilogram. [@1]
