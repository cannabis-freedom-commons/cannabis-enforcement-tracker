Federal Drug Enforcement Administration agents and Wake County Sheriff's Office
deputies raided a home on Hartsfield Drive near Wake Forest on the morning of
Wednesday 26 February 2025. [@1] The Wake County Sheriff's Office said it and
partner agencies had "conducted a large-scale operation targeting drug
traffickers operating in our county", and that the operation resulted in the
arrests of 17 men and women. [@1] Agents carried multiple guns and evidence bags
out of the house. [@1]

Two of the 16 people charged in the raid face marijuana counts alongside cocaine
charges: Aleshia Walker, 52, and Aaron Walker, 54, each with possession with
intent to manufacture, sell or deliver marijuana and felony possession of
marijuana. [@1] A 17th person, 30-year-old Juwan Clifton, was arrested earlier
in February and charged with possession with intent to sell or deliver marijuana
among other counts. [@1]

Charges were brought with the DEA and a group of local, state and federal
agencies, including the US Marshals Service, the Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco,
Firearms and Explosives, the State Bureau of Investigation, the Wake County
District Attorney's Office, Raleigh, Wake Forest and Henderson police and the
Franklin County Sheriff's Office. [@1]

Most suspects appeared virtually in court on Thursday; Aaron and Aleshia Walker
had already posted bond and most had a next hearing set for 18 March 2025. [@2]
