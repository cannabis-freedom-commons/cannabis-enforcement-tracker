The Colombian navy detained three Nicaraguans and two Costa Ricans whose boat
was carrying more than three tonnes of marijuana and cocaine in the Pacific.
[@3] A navy vessel detected the suspicious boat and naval units stopped it after
the crew tried to flee. [@2] The boat was located near Buenaventura, Colombia's
main Pacific port. [@1]

Five crew members and 138 sacks and rectangular packages were found aboard. [@1]
Tests at the Buenaventura Coast Guard Station found 2,215 kg of marijuana and
1,204 kg of cocaine hydrochloride. [@2]

The five detainees were aged between 23 and 41 and were handed to the Attorney
General's Office for prosecution. [@2] The navy said the two parallel operations
kept more than US$100 million from criminal organisations, a figure covering
both the maritime seizure and a separate Chocó depot find. [@1]

In the parallel Chocó operation, troops found an illegal depot in a wooded area
of the Abega sector, rural Bahía Solano, holding 786 kg of marijuana and 670 kg
of cocaine, with no arrests reported. [@2]

The detainees were presented to prosecutors in Colombia to face the
corresponding charges. [@3]
