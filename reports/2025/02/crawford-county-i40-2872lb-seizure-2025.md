At about 12:45 p.m. on Monday 3 February 2025, Arkansas State Police
troopers pulled over a tractor-trailer for a traffic violation at mile
marker 2 eastbound on Interstate 40 in Crawford County. [@1,2] Searching
the trailer, they found seven pallets holding 107 boxes of illegal
marijuana, 2,872 pounds in all. [@1,2]

The driver, 31-year-old Zhiping Mu of Kent, Washington, was arrested and
taken to the Crawford County Detention Center. [@1,2] He was charged with
trafficking a controlled substance and possession of drug paraphernalia and
held on a $150,000 bond. [@1,2]

State Police Director Colonel Mike Hagar said the seizure showed the
agency's commitment to stopping drug trafficking on the state's highways. [@1,2]
