Egypt's General Administration for Drug Control arrested suspects in a series of
operations against drug import and trafficking in Alexandria and Sharqia, the
Interior Ministry said. [@1] In Alexandria, officers arrested one man in the Bab
Sharq police district with 12 kg of hashish. [@1] Three more men, two of them
with criminal records, were arrested in the Second Amiriya district with 32 kg
of hashish. [@1]

In Sharqia, two men, one with a criminal record, were arrested in the Minya
al-Qamh police district with 15 kg of hashish. [@1] A woman was arrested in the
Zagazig police district with 5 kg of hashish, a pistol, a number of bullets and
a car. [@2] Police valued the seized items at about 6 million Egyptian pounds.
[@1]

The General Administration for Drug Control is part of a sector for combating
drugs and unlicensed weapons and ammunition. [@1] It said legal measures had
been taken against the suspects as part of the Interior Ministry's campaign
against drug trafficking. [@1]
