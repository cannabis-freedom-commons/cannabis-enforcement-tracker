Uzbekistan’s State Security Service (SGB) and Ministry of Internal Affairs
detained 228 people during a two-day Operation Qoradori sweep. [@1] The
operation’s first stage ran nationwide on 3–4 February 2025. [@2] Gazeta.uz
translated Qoradori as “Black Poppy,” the name of the national anti-drug
operation. [@1]

UZA reported 653 special measures targeting individuals and criminal groups
involved in illegal trafficking of narcotic drugs and potent medicines. [@2]
Authorities opened 222 criminal cases and reported bringing 228 people to
criminal responsibility. [@2] The State Security Service said operational
measures to stabilize the crime situation would continue in other directions.
[@2]

Authorities reported seizing 112 kg 257 g of narcotic substances, including
heroin, opium, hashish and synthetic drugs. [@2] UzNews reported the same
combined weight and named heroin, opium, hashish and synthetic drugs among the
seized substances. [@3] Gazeta.uz rounded the mixed-drug total to 112.3 kg. [@1]
UZA also reported the seizure of 8,601 units of potent medicines. [@2] Hashish
was included in the same mixed narcotic seizure as heroin, opium and synthetic
drugs. [@2]
