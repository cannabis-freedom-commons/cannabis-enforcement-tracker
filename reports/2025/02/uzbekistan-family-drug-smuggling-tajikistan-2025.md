In Surkhandarya, Namangan and Andijan regions, several drug dealers involved in
smuggling drugs from Tajikistan were detained, and a total of nearly 50 kg of
heroin, opium and hashish was seized. [@1]

The man and his family members were importing and selling prohibited substances
from Tajikistan. [@1] His 69-year-old father, previously convicted of a
drug-related crime, had over 9 kg of hashish, $137,500 and 570 million som of
unknown origin found on him. [@2] The previously convicted 45-year-old brother
of the drug dealer, who lives in Uzun district, had $160,000 seized from him.
[@2]

In Yangikurgan district of Namangan region, officers stopped a bus heading from
Yangikurgan to Namangan for inspection and found 19.9 kg of hashish in the bags
of two passengers, which local residents had brought in from Kyrgyzstan. [@2]

In Marhamat district of Andijan region, border guards detained a previously
convicted citizen who tried to cross the state border, with 4.9 kg of opium and
nearly 10 kg of hashish found on him, which had been previously passed to him by
a Kyrgyz citizen. [@2]

Criminal cases were opened under Article 246 (Smuggling) and Article 273
(unauthorized production, acquisition, storage and other acts with drugs, their
analogs or psychotropic substances with intent to sell, as well as their sale),
and the investigation is ongoing. [@2]
