Federal police in Gualeguaychú, in Argentina's Entre Ríos province, detained a
27-year-old Argentine man after stopping his motorcycle for an identity check
and searching him at the intersection of Asisclo Méndez and Lavayén streets.
[@1] The officers were from the División Unidad Operativa Federal (DUOF)
Gualeguaychú, the federal police's local operational unit, patrolling with the
DUOF Concepción del Uruguay during an Operativo de Prevención Federal, a federal
crime-prevention drive ordered by the national security ministry. [@2]

The search, carried out in front of witnesses, found 55 nylon wrappings of
marijuana weighing close to 200 grams among the man's belongings. [@2] Officers
seized the marijuana together with two mobile phones, the motorcycle and other
items of interest to the case. [@2] The ministry's account gives the haul as 55
wrappings of marijuana without a weight, and also lists two mobile phones and a
motorcycle as seized. [@1]

The man was left at the disposal of the on-duty Unidad Fiscal de Gualeguaychú,
the local prosecutor's office headed that day by Dr. Natalia Bartolo, for an
offence under Argentina's drug law. [@1] El Día placed the arrest in
Gualeguaychú's Hipódromo neighbourhood and described the case as one of
narcomenudeo, local retail drug dealing, without naming a charge or court. [@2]
The same ministry statement reported a separate operation in Rosario, Santa Fe,
in which federal police arrested a 44-year-old man and a 34-year-old woman
selling cocaine on Rivadavia street between Oroño and Balcarce and seized 95
wrappings of cocaine. [@1]
