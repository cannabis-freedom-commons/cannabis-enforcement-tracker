Police arrested and charged 36-year-old Javin Maynard of Cedar Grove with
unlawful possession of five .25 rounds of ammunition and unlawful possession of
37.5 grams of cannabis. [@1] On Friday, February 28, police officers executed a
warrant at his residence and found the illegal items in his possession. [@1] He
was taken into custody and was expected in court on the day of the charging
report. [@1]

Antigua News then reported that Maynard appeared before Magistrate Ngaio Emanuel
and admitted the cannabis charge but not the ammunition charge. [@2] He was
remanded to prison until May 21, when he was to return to court for trial. [@2]
Antigua News Room reported the same hearing, saying he would return to court on
May 21 for trial after admitting unlawful cannabis possession and denying the
ammunition charge. [@3] Antigua News Room also recorded that police found the
37.5 grams of cannabis and five .25 rounds at his Cedar Grove home during a
February 28 search. [@3]

The Antiguan Trumpet reported that Maynard, represented by attorney Lawrence
Daniel, entered a guilty plea to cannabis possession before Magistrate Ngaio
Emanuel. [@4] The Trumpet account says that despite the guilty plea to the drug
offence, his not guilty plea on the ammunition charge resulted in remand until
the May court date, when he was to stand trial. [@4]
