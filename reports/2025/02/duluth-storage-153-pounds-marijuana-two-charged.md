Two men were arrested on 14 February 2025 after Gwinnett County Sheriff's Office
deputies and federal agents identified a suspect car at a Duluth storage
facility. [@3] They were named as Jian Yi Wu and Hieu M. Quach. [@2] After the
arrests, officers searched the storage facility and a hotel in Duluth. [@2] The
searches found 153 pounds (69.4 kg) of marijuana and more than $20,000 in cash.
[@2] The Gwinnett Daily Post reported that deputies seized the 153 pounds of
marijuana, $20,000 in cash and arrested the two men. [@1]

Wu was charged with trafficking in marijuana, described as 2,000 to 10,000
pounds, and with possession of Schedule I and Schedule III controlled
substances. [@2] Quach was charged with trafficking in marijuana, described as
2,000 to 10,000 pounds. [@2] VGCSA refers to Georgia's Violation of the Georgia
Controlled Substances Act. [@2] Both men were held at the Gwinnett County Jail.
[@2]

The Gwinnett Metro Task Force worked the case with the Drug Enforcement
Administration. [@3] The task force draws officers from local police departments
and the sheriff's office and works vice crimes including drug trafficking,
prostitution and gambling. [@3] The sheriff's office said the investigation was
ongoing. [@3]
