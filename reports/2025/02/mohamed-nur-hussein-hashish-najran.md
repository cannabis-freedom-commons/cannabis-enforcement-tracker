Saudi Arabia executed Mohamed Nur Hussein Jaal, a Somali national, in the Najran region on 16 February
2025. [@1] The interior ministry said he had smuggled hashish into the kingdom, was convicted after
investigation and appeal, and was put to death once the Supreme Court upheld the sentence and a royal
order authorised it. [@1]

## Family protest

Reporting on the ministry announcement said Hussein Jaal was beheaded in Najran after a specialised
court sentenced him to death for the smuggling charge. [@2] His family told SMS Somalia TV that the
execution was unjust, that he lacked legal representation, and that they had little chance to
appeal. [@2] Saudi authorities said the penalty reinforced their stance against drug trafficking.
[@2]

## Context

Amnesty International, citing the same February ministry notice, recorded the Najran execution among
a sharp rise in drug-related death penalties in early 2025. [@3] The opened sources do not say how
much hashish was involved. [@1,2]
