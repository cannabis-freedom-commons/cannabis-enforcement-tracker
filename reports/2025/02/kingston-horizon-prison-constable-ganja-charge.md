District Constable Robert Campbell, 25, from Point Hill in St Catherine was
charged with dealing in ganja and bringing prohibited articles into a
correctional centre. [@1] Campbell was stationed at Detention and Courts. [@1]
He was charged on Thursday following a ruling by the Office of the Director of
Public Prosecutions, after a file on the case was prepared and submitted to it.
[@1]

Campbell was held with the items around 7:00 am on the previous Friday while in
the barrack room of the Horizon Adult Remand Centre on Spanish Town Road in
Kingston. [@1] He was changing his clothes and getting ready for work when a
small parcel fell from his pocket and he quickly stepped on it. [@1] The
corporal supervising him became suspicious, retrieved the object, and it was
found to be vegetable matter resembling ganja. [@1]

A search of his knapsack in his presence found several parcels of vegetable
matter resembling ganja, vegetable matter resembling tobacco, a USB cable,
$20,650 in cash, a pack of Rizzla and a lighter. [@1] The policeman was taken
into custody after the search. [@1]
