Gendarmes dismantled a drug-trafficking network running between Réunion and
Mauritius, gendarmes announced on 11 February 2025. [@2] Nine people were
arrested in the case and the principal suspects were placed in detention. [@1]

Gendarmes seized 224 kilograms of zamal, the Réunion Creole word for cannabis,
in the case. [@1] Investigators put the market value of the seized drugs at 5
million euros. [@1] Seven people were placed under formal investigation in the
case. [@1]

On 16 December 2024, gendarmes of the GIGN branch arrested three people at the
Sainte-Marie jetty as they prepared to sail to Mauritius. [@1] Their boat was
carrying 162 kg of zamal and two firearms hidden in its hold. [@1] Two firearms
were found during the Sainte-Marie boat seizure. [@2] The case began with an
investigation opened in August 2024 by the anti-narcotics branch (A-OFAST) of
Saint-Denis and the Saint-Benoît research brigade. [@2] That investigation
produced a first seizure of 62 kg of zamal. [@2]

On 4 and 5 February 2025, three more people linked to the case were questioned
by investigators under a judicial investigation opened at the Saint-Denis court.
[@1] In that final operational phase, gendarmes seized 7,000 euros in cash,
several zamal plants, a handgun and a vehicle. [@1] The operation ended a
network organised between Réunion and Mauritius that had been structured over
several months. [@3]
