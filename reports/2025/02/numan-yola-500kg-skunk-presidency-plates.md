NDLEA operatives arrested Shuaibu Nuhu Isa, 55, known as Don, and Zidon
Zurga, 50, on 25 November 2024 along the Numan-Yola road with 500.800 kg of
skunk, a strain of cannabis, packed in 626 blocks. [@1,2]

The drug was concealed in a pilot Toyota Hilux and a Prado SUV; the Hilux
wore Lagos plate NT 829 AAA while the Prado carried fake presidency plate
01B-266 FG. [@1]

Justice Bala Usman of the Federal High Court in Yola convicted the pair on 7
February 2025 and sentenced each to 35 years on two counts, a combined 70
years, or a N25 million fine each, ordering interim forfeiture of both
vehicles. [@1,2] The consignment ran from Ondo State toward Mubi, and the
interception was a joint effort with the military, praised on site by
Governor Ahmadu Umaru Fintiri. [@2] NDLEA chairman Mohamed Buba Marwa said
the convictions and forfeitures would strengthen deterrence against drug
networks. [@1]
