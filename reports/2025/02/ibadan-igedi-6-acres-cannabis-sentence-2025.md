A Federal High Court in Ibadan sentenced 70-year-old Fidelis Igedi to a total
of 30 months in a correctional centre for cultivating six acres (about 2.43
hectares) of cannabis sativa. [@1]

Justice N.E. Maha convicted him on the evidence of the National Drug Law
Enforcement Agency (NDLEA) and his guilty plea, imposing 15 months on each of
two counts to run concurrently from his arrest. [@1]

NDLEA prosecutor Daniel Otunla told the court that Igedi was arrested on 13
August 2024 in a bush in Oluyole Local Government Area of Ibadan, where
operatives found him on the six-acre plantation with a white sack holding 7.3
kg of the dried substance. [@1]

The charges were unlawful cultivation and dealing under sections 19(A) and (C)
of the NDLEA Act, which prescribes up to life imprisonment. [@1] The judge said
she tempered the sentence because of Igedi's age, ill health and first-offender
status and warned that a return to the trade would not be spared. [@1]
