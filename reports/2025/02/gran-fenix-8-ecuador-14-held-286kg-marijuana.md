On 13 February 2025, Ecuador's National Police, through its Fuerza Investigativa
Anticriminal, carried out operation Gran Fenix 08 in Sucumbios, Orellana,
Tungurahua, El Oro, Guayas, Los Rios and Manabi against a criminal structure
linked to the armed groups Los Lagartos and Comandos de Frontera, dedicated to
large-scale international drug trafficking. [@2] Officers carried out 15
searches and detained 14 people under arrest warrants, including two high-value
targets, Darwin G., alias Patron, detained in Manabi, and Marcelo Q., alias
Cerapo, detained in Sucumbios, plus an intermediate-value target, Juan S., alias
Raton, captured in Santo Domingo de los Tsachilas. [@2] The two high-value
detainees were described by the Ecuadorian authorities as the principal leaders
of these organisations. [@1]

In the operation police seized 28,600 grams of marijuana, alongside cocaine, and
also retained 37 vehicles and seized 6 firearms. [@2] Press accounts of the same
searches reported 28.1 kilos of marijuana found in the properties, with the same
counts of six firearms and 37 vehicles. [@1] Police said the organisation had
set up multiple bases on farms and in front businesses to consolidate its
operations. [@1]

El Comercio corroborated the same results for operation Gran Fenix 8: 14
detainees and 28.1 kilos of marijuana alongside cocaine, firearms and vehicles.
[@3]
