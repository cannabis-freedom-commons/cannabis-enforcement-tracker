Three Castries men — proprietor Earland Charles of Carille, Darren Jason Charles
of La Clery and Daniell Didier of Ravine Chabot — were each granted bail when
they appeared in court on a Monday on multiple charges. [@1] They had been
arrested on Friday, 28 February 2025, when officers of the Gang, Narcotics, and
Firearms Unit of the Royal Saint Lucia Police Force ran a two-phase operation in
the northern policing division. [@1]

During patrols in Castries, officers searched 36 people and took drugs from
various shops on Jeremie Street. [@1] A search warrant executed at Liberation
Bar, at the corner of Carl Crescent Street and Jeremie Street, led to the
discovery of 245 grammes of cannabis and 178 grammes of cannabis resin. [@1]
Officers also found EC$3,852 and US$153 in cash at the bar. [@1] The three men
were arrested in connection with those recoveries. [@1]

Search warrants were then executed at the home of each suspect, and the search
at Darren Charles's residence uncovered 21 rounds of .380 ammunition. [@1]
Darren Charles was charged with possession of cannabis, possession of cannabis
with intent to supply, possession of cannabis resin with intent to supply and
possession of ammunition, and was granted a total of $16,900 bail. [@1] The
other two were granted $15,000 bail and charged with possession of cannabis,
possession of cannabis with intent to supply, possession of cannabis resin and
possession of cannabis resin with intent to supply. [@1] Authorities said
Earland Charles had previously been charged with drug possession offences in
November 2024, in matters still before the court. [@1]
