Argentina's federal police detained five people in three anti-drug procedures in
San Luis, Buenos Aires and Neuquén provinces, announced on 16 February 2025, in
which doses of cocaine and marijuana, firearms and ammunition were seized. [@1]
The release's opening summary instead says three people were detained and
mentions only San Luis and Buenos Aires province, before its three sections
describe five detainees including two in Neuquén. [@1] At Mercedes, San Luis,
the Villa Mercedes federal operational unit closed a drug sales point and
detained the man running it, who was already serving house arrest in another
case under Argentina's narcotics law, Law 23.737. [@1] Villa Mercedes federal
judge Juan Carlos Nacul ordered the search of the house, where officers seized
80 grams of high-purity cocaine hydrochloride, two phones, a pickup, a car, a
.22 revolver with 12 cartridges and 336,000 pesos in low-denomination notes.
[@1] No cannabis was listed in the Mercedes seizure, which involved cocaine, a
weapon and cash. [@1]

A second inquiry by the federal police's western metropolitan operations
division led to five searches of homes accused of storing and distributing drugs
in the Moreno district, with lookouts posted nearby to protect the sales. [@1]
Guarantee Court No 1 of the Moreno–General Rodríguez judicial department ordered
the five searches, in which officers detained the man and woman said to lead the
organisation. [@1] The Moreno searches produced 260 grams of marijuana and three
cannabis sativa plants alongside nearly 3 kilos of high-purity cocaine
hydrochloride, a car and eight phones. [@1]

At a roadside check on Provincial Route 65 near Villa Traful, Neuquén, officers
stopped a car and the sniffer dog Ara marked a bag inside the passenger
compartment. [@1] The Zapala federal prosecutor's office, under Juan Manuel
García Barrese, ordered the case opened before witnesses, revealing about 600
grams of pressed marijuana split into five nylon wrappings, a second bag with 30
grams of cocaine and a digital precision scale. [@1] The magistrate ordered the
drug, the phones and the car seized, notified the two Argentine occupants, a man
and a woman, and ordered the investigation to continue under the national drug
law. [@1]
