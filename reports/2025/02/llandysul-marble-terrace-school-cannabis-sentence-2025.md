Alfred Perkola, 44, from Ealing in London, was jailed for three years and nine
months and Aldi Gjegjaj, 25, of Salford, for three years at Swansea Crown Court on
26 February 2025, after both pleaded guilty to conspiracy to supply
cannabis. [@2] On 4 July 2024 Dyfed-Powys Police stopped two vehicles travelling
together just after the M4, a Volvo and a hire van, and found nearly 100 kg of
cannabis worth about £1 million inside, along with mobile phones. [@1,2] GPS data
from a tracking device on the van led officers to Marble Terrace in
Llandysul. [@2]

At the address stood the boarded-up three-storey Hen Ysgol Gynradd, the old primary
school, smelling strongly of cannabis. [@2] Inside, officers found a sophisticated
setup with living quarters and around 1,500 plants in various stages of growth
across two floors, watched over by CCTV cameras. [@1,2] Police put the total street
value of the bud and plants seized at up to £1,960,000. [@1] Detective Chief
Inspector Rich Lewis called it a very successful operation built on joint work
between departments and other forces. [@1] DC Steve Thomas said the sentences were
an excellent result that had taken nearly £2 million of drugs out of a supply chain
bound for the streets. [@2]
