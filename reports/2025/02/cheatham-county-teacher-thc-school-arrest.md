Cheatham County officers went to Sycamore High School in Pleasant View on
February 14, 2025, after staff reported a teacher who appeared possibly
intoxicated. [@1] The teacher was identified as Brandon Peters, a Sycamore High
School teacher. [@2] Upon arrival, the responding officer gave Peters field
sobriety tests at the school. [@1] A drug recognition expert was then called to
the school and found Peters was under the influence of THC. [@1] He was arrested
for possession of a Schedule VI drug on school property, a drug-free zone, and
for public intoxication. [@2] The Cheatham County School District said Peters
had been employed since 2024 and terminated him on Friday, February 14. [@1]

A search of his vehicle at the school yielded eight carts of THC. [@1] The
sheriff’s office said deputies also found a small amount of marijuana in
addition to the eight THC vape pens. [@2] Peters told officers he had taken his
wife’s medication, which he described as an antidepressant. [@1]

The district said a review of his personnel file showed no prior issues. [@1]
Peters has since bonded out of jail. [@2] The district said it is fully
cooperating with the Cheatham County Sheriff’s Office while the investigation
continues. [@1]
