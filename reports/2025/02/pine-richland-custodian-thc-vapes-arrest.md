A Pine-Richland High School custodian was arrested Thursday for allegedly
selling nicotine, THC vape cartridges and alcohol to students on school grounds.
[@1] Keon Ency Barrett, who the district identified as an evening custodial
employee, is charged with delivery of scheduled substances, criminal use of a
communication facility and two counts of corruption of minors. [@1] The
23-year-old Saxonburg man was arrested by Northern Regional Police Department’s
school resources officers and taken to Allegheny County Jail. [@1] The evening
custodian was arrested and placed on immediate administrative leave pending the
outcome of the police investigation and further proceedings. [@2]

One student told police he paid Barrett $20 in November to purchase two cases of
Twisted Tea from Sheetz, the criminal complaint alleged. [@1] A second student
told police he smoked from Barrett’s THC vape pen in a cafeteria restroom during
a varsity basketball game, police said. [@1] That student then messaged with
Barrett through Snapchat about buying a THC pen and Twisted Tea from him. [@1]
The unidentified student told police he gave Barrett $10 in exchange for the
drugs and alcohol, which they retrieved from Barrett’s car. [@1] Police said
they confirmed the student’s account through Snapchat messages and security
footage. [@1]

According to police, at least one student reported that Barrett had sold them
alcohol and another reported Barrett had sold them a THC vape. [@3] Police also
said that a student informed them Barrett had let them use his THC vape pen at a
school basketball game. [@3] The district said that student and staff safety and
well-being is a priority for the district and encouraged anyone with information
to contact Northern Regional police or use the Safe2Say Something reporting
system. [@2] Barrett’s preliminary arraignment took place Friday, and his
preliminary hearing before Judge Matthew Brungo is scheduled for Feb. 26. [@1]
DeWick said the department’s investigation is “continuing into the extent of
activity that had been going on at the school.” [@1]
