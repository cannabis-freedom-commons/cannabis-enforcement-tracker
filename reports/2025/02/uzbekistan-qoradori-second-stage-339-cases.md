Uzbekistan's State Security Service and Interior Ministry ran the nationwide
second stage of Operation Qoradori, translated as Black Poppy, on 5-7 February
2025. [@1] Authorities opened 269 criminal cases against 339 people and detained
53 wanted people. [@1]

Authorities said the stage disrupted the sale of 28.8 kg of narcotics, listed as
heroin, opium, hashish and synthetic substances, alongside 9421 potent and
psychotropic tablets. [@1] Authorities also reported finding 18 illegal firearms
and 126 bladed weapons. [@1] Kun.uz corroborated the same second-stage totals of
about 29 kg of mixed narcotics with the same four substance categories, 9421
tablets, and 18 firearms and 126 bladed weapons. [@2]

In one episode, officers acting on a Telegram tip about a 1 kg synthetic-drug
dead drop in Yukorichirchik district detained two suspects as they retrieved it.
[@1] A search of one suspect's house in Mirabad district found another 200 g of
synthetic drug plus electronic scales, tape and packaging for dead drops. [@1]
