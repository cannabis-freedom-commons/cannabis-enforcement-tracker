Bahamian law enforcement officers arrested two Bahamian men in their mid-to-late
30s after finding suspected marijuana on a go-fast vessel at a marina in the
Lucaya area of Grand Bahama. [@1] The discovery was made around noon on
Wednesday 26 February 2025. [@1] The two men, aged 35 and 38, were taken into
custody and were assisting police with their investigations. [@1]

Chief Superintendent Will Hart, officer in charge of Criminal Management in the
Northern Bahamas, said officers received information shortly after noon that a
speedboat was being fuelled near a Lucaya marina. [@1] Officers from the Drug
Enforcement Unit and Bahamas Customs responded and intercepted the vessel. [@1]
Hart said the officers boarded and searched the vessel and found a large
quantity of suspected marijuana weighing an estimated 500-plus pounds, with a
street value of $1.5 million. [@1]

The headline value of $1.5 million was described in the article's summary as
over $1 million. [@1]
