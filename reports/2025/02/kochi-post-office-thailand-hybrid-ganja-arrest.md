Kochi Customs arrested a Kakkanad man after a parcel of hydroponic ganja, also
called hybrid ganja, arrived from Thailand through the International Post Office
in Kochi. [@1] On 18 February 2025 officers visited the Postal Appraising
Section, examined the parcel and seized the cannabis. [@1] The parcel also
contained cornflakes and chocolates, and the ganja was hidden inside the
cornflakes packets. [@2] Officers found 1.04 kg of hybrid ganja, which Customs
called the largest drug seizure at the International Post Office in recent
years, and valued it at Rs 1.04 crore. [@2]

Officers of the Headquarters Preventive Unit of the Commissionerate of Customs
(Preventive), Kochi, made the arrest. [@1] Samples were drawn in the presence of
a judicial magistrate and the cannabis was deposited at the Customs warehouse.
[@1] The receiver was not identified at first, so a team of the Headquarters
Preventive Unit worked with the Customs Postal Section and the Department of
Posts and sent a dummy parcel through normal delivery. [@1] The address the
receiver gave was fake, so officers kept watch at locations he had mentioned.
[@1]

On 20 February 2025, while a postman was delivering the parcel, Customs officers
located the receiver's home and arrested him. [@1] A search of the house found
about 30 g of hydroponic weed and 50 g of ganja, bringing the cannabis found in
the case to about 1.12 kg. [@1] His mobile phone held what officers called
evidence of distribution chains and possible supply networks. [@1]

Customs said the man confessed, and he was produced before the Judicial
Magistrate Court, Ernakulam, and remanded in judicial custody for 14 days. [@1]
Customs said it was also examining the involvement of other people in the case.
[@2]
