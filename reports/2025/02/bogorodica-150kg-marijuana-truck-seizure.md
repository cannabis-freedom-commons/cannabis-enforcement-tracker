A joint police and customs operation prevented an attempt to traffic marijuana
into North Macedonia. [@1,2] A detailed inspection at the Bogorodica border
crossing found 150 kilograms of marijuana hidden in a truck with Skopje licence
plates. [@1,2] Investigations were continuing to identify all the individuals
involved and the organisation behind the trafficking network. [@1,2]

Preliminary information said the truck had entered from Kosovo. [@1,2] The truck
was planned to transit through North Macedonia en route to Greece, with Turkey
as its final destination. [@1,2] The operation was an interinstitutional
collaboration between the Ministry of Internal Affairs and the Customs
Directorate under a cooperation memorandum signed in November 2024. [@1,2]

The seized narcotics were to undergo forensic analysis and further expert
evaluation. [@1,2] Estimates put the black-market worth of the quantity at over
1.5 million euros. [@1]
