Argentine Naval Prefecture officers seized almost one tonne of marijuana that
had been brought in by water from Paraguay and was found at Puerto Neis, on the
bank of the Paraná River in Puerto Rico, Misiones. [@1] The load was hidden in
two boats, and after searching the area thoroughly the officers found more
bundles scattered along the coast in the early hours. [@1]

The marijuana was divided into 40 packages containing 1,114 pressed bricks. [@1]
The Federal Prosecutor's Office in Oberá, Misiones, led by Viviana Vallejos with
Arlindo Kurtz as secretary, ordered the seizure of the drug and the
investigation to find those responsible. [@1]

The officers were acting under border operations ordered by the Security
Ministry to fight drug trafficking in the frontier zone. [@1] The force said the
finding happened at dawn, when officers discovered the drug hidden in the two
boats and then found additional bundles scattered along the coast. [@2]
Prefectura said the load had entered by river from Paraguay and was found at
Puerto Neis, on the Paraná, being “chilled”, a method criminal organisations use
to leave cargo hidden until it is later distributed locally. [@2]
