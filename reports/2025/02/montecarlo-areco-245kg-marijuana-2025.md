Prefectura Naval Argentina seized 245 kilograms of marijuana in Montecarlo,
Misiones, in a night operation coordinated by the Security Ministry. [@1]
Officers using a night-vision monocular spotted a motorboat crossing from the
Paraguayan bank into Argentine territory with several bundles aboard. [@1] The
crossing was at kilometre 1784 of the Paraná River, at a natural port known as
Areco. [@1] The Misiones daily El Territorio reported the seizure in Montecarlo
the same evening, under the same ministry account. [@2] When officers approached
and combed the bank they found eight packages scattered around. [@1] The
packages held 316 bricks of marijuana weighing 245 kilograms in total. [@3]

The force put the value of the load at more than 657 million pesos. [@3] Radio
Up, a Misiones outlet, described the haul as one of the largest seizures in the
region so far this year. [@4] The case is with the Federal Prosecutor's Office
in Eldorado, led by Liliam Edith Delgado, and the Federal Court in Eldorado,
under Miguel Ángel Guerrero. [@3] Radio Up said the two offices would
investigate to find those responsible for the trafficking. [@4] No arrests,
detainees or crew members are reported, and the accounts do not say whether the
boat was stopped. [@1]
