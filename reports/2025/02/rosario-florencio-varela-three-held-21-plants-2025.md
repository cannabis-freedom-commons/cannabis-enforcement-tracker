Argentina's federal police detained three people suspected of selling drugs in
two separate operations, one in Rosario and one in Buenos Aires province. [@1]
In Rosario, Santa Fe, officers of the federal force's special investigations
operational unit detained a man who delivered narcotics in small amounts by car.
[@1] He sold drugs in the Villa Banana, Triángulo Moderno and Bella Vista
neighbourhoods. [@1] The officers followed him to a cocaine delivery to a client
and detained him, seizing the drug and the vehicle. [@1] The prosecutor Noelia
Navone backed the investigation and Judge Rodrigo Santana of the first-instance
criminal court ordered a search of the man's home. [@1] At the house officers
found portioned marijuana buds ready for sale, 39 cannabis sativa seeds and 30
rounds of .32 ammunition, besides high-purity cocaine, a tablet, a phone and
500,000 pesos. [@1] The man, 33 and Argentine, was left at the disposal of the
judge for an offence under Argentina's drug law. [@1] Crónica reported the same
Rosario arrest, stating that the police operation took place in the city of
Rosario, Santa Fe. [@4] Conclusión likewise reported the detention of a
33-year-old man by federal police personnel during a surveillance operation.
[@3]

In the second operation, officers of the Metropolitan South operations division
of the federal force's dangerous-drugs superintendency broke up two retail drug
points in Buenos Aires province. [@1] The Florencio Varela decentralised drug
prosecutor's office under Giovanni Maggio established that two units of a local
housing complex were storing and distributing marijuana and cocaine. [@1] El
Sindical reported that the inquiry began in November 2024 from an anonymous
complaint telephoned to the 911 emergency line. [@2] That complaint accused a
woman and her daughter of selling narcotics in the "Pepsi" neighbourhood of
Bosques, in the municipality of Florencio Varela. [@2] Guarantee Court No 5 of
Florencio Varela, under Judge Juan Busteros, ordered searches of the homes, and
officers detained two Argentine adult women. [@1] The searches found more than
half a kilo of marijuana in compact brick form, 21 cannabis sativa plants,
cocaine doses, a .22 revolver with ammunition, 98,650 pesos in low-denomination
notes, four precision scales and phones. [@1] Both women were left with the
seized items at the disposal of the intervening judge for an offence under the
drug law. [@1]

The ministry reported the two procedures on 15 February 2025, and Crónica
carried the Rosario case the next day. [@1]
