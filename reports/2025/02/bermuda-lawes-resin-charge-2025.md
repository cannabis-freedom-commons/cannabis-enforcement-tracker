Ty Lawes, a 19-year-old from Pembroke, was charged in Bermuda's Magistrates'
Court with smuggling more than $1 million worth of cannabis resin into the
island. [@1] The charge accuses him of importing cannabis resin on 20 July 2023
and possessing cannabis resin with intent to supply on the same date. [@1] He
was not required to enter a plea because the case will be heard by the Supreme
Court. [@1]

The court heard the case involved more than 16kg of cannabis resin, worth
between $1 million and $3.3 million. [@1] The hearing took place the day before
the report was published, on 25 February 2025. [@1]

Magistrate Auralee Cassidy released him on $50,000 bail with a surety. [@1] She
ordered him to surrender his travel documents, report regularly to Hamilton
Police Station and not leave the island without the court's permission. [@1] The
case is expected to appear before the Supreme Court on 1 April. [@1]
