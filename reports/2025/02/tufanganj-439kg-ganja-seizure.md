A joint team of the Special Task Force of Kolkata Police and Coochbehar District
Police intercepted a heavy goods vehicle at the Raidhak Bridge Naka Point under
Tufanganj Police Station. [@1] The operation was based on intelligence inputs.
[@1] The driver was 39-year-old Gill Gurdeo Singh, a resident of Vadodara in
Gujarat. [@1] The vehicle bore registration number GJ 06 AU 8470. [@1]

Police searched the vehicle under videography and in the presence of the Joint
Block Development Officer of Tufanganj. [@1] Police found 439.7 kg of ganja,
known locally as ganja, concealed in hidden chambers at the base of the vehicle.
[@1] Singh could not give a satisfactory explanation for the consignment and was
immediately arrested. [@1]

Preliminary interrogation indicated that the ganja had been sourced from Manipur
and was intended for delivery in Jharkhand. [@1] A case under the Narcotic Drugs
and Psychotropic Substances Act was registered at Tufanganj Police Station. [@1]
Further investigations were under way to pursue the inter-state supply chain.
