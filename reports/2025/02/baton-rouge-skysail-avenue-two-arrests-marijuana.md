East Baton Rouge Parish sheriff's deputies arrested Jordan Daigre, 35, and
Deangelo Adams, then 18, after an anonymous tip led them to investigate Daigre
as an alleged Baton Rouge drug dealer. [@1] Deputies watched Daigre and his home
on Skysail Avenue for several hours and obtained a search warrant. [@2] On 19
February 2025 SWAT agents went to the house to execute the warrant, and Daigre
refused to come out. [@1] SWAT video showed Daigre flushing what appeared to be
narcotics down a bathroom toilet, and Adams was caught after he ran from the
back of the house. [@1] Deputies also found that Daigre already had a warrant
through the East Baton Rouge Sheriff's Office. [@1]

The search of the Skysail Avenue home turned up 1.04 pounds of marijuana (about
472 grams), alongside methamphetamine, cocaine, fentanyl, MDMA and prescription
pills. [@1] The seized items included a stolen 9 mm Glock handgun, eight digital
scales, a money counter, cutting agent, plastic bags and $386. [@1]

Daigre was booked on charges including possession with intent to distribute a
Schedule I drug (marijuana), possession with intent to distribute cocaine,
methamphetamine and other drugs, possession of a firearm by a convicted felon,
obstruction of justice and resisting an officer. [@1] The charges also included
possessing a controlled dangerous substance in the presence of a juvenile,
possession of drug paraphernalia and possession of a stolen firearm. [@1] Adams
was booked on a cocaine possession charge and resisting an officer. [@1] The
WAFB account gives Adams's age as 18, while WBRZ reported he was 19. [@2]

WBRZ reported that the sheriff's gang intelligence unit received the tip about
Daigre in early February 2025. [@2]
