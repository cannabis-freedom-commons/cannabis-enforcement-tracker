Guatemala's National Civil Police (PNC) reported detaining a Salvadoran citizen
who was carrying drugs on an interdepartmental bus stopped at a checkpoint in
Retalhuleu department. [@1] Police identified the detainee as Marcos "N", who
was travelling as a passenger on a Galgos Sur bus from Tecún Umán, San Marcos,
toward El Salvador. [@1]

During the inspection at the inter-agency checkpoint at kilometre 185.3 of the
CA-2 highway in Nuevo San Carlos, anti-narcotics officers of the SGAIA unit
found three packages of marijuana weighing four kilograms in his luggage, along
with half a kilogram of a drug known locally as cristal, meaning crystal
methamphetamine. [@1] SGAIA is the anti-narcotics analysis and information
directorate of the national civil police, which staffs this inter-agency control
point on the CA-2 route. [@1] The official report valued the haul at more than
20,000 quetzales, without saying how much of that figure was the marijuana. [@1]

La Hora reported that the Salvadoran was travelling as a passenger on a Galgos
Sur unit from San Marcos and that police presumed he was heading to his country
of origin. [@2] The same police statement, issued on 27 September 2025, also put
the marijuana at four kilograms and the other drug at half a kilogram. [@2]
