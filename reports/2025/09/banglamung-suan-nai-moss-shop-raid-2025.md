Local authorities raided a cannabis shop named "Suan Nai Moss" on Chaiyaphruek 2 Road in Huai Yai subdistrict, Bang Lamung, at about 4:36 p.m. on 2 September 2025. [@1,2] The raid, led by Bang Lamung District Chief Patcharapat Sritanyanon, followed complaints to the district's Damrongdhama Centre and an undercover sting that found the shop openly selling cannabis, tramadol and illicit tobacco. [@1,2]

Officers seized 20.245 kilograms of dried cannabis and cannabis buds. [@1,2] They also confiscated 52 bottles of cough and antihistamine syrups, 18 strips of tramadol, 105 packs of untaxed cigarettes and 32 e-cigarettes, and found more cannabis and e-cigarettes hidden in vehicles parked behind the shop. [@1,2]

The shop's operator, identified only as 29-year-old Mr. Ek, was detained and handed over to Huai Yai Police Station for legal proceedings. [@1,2] Although cannabis is decriminalised in Thailand, its sale requires a licence and documentation, which the shop did not have. [@1] The shop had previously operated solely as a boiled-kratom-drink vendor and had been raided several times before. [@1,2]
