Directorate of Revenue Intelligence officers, acting on specific intelligence, intercepted an Indian woman arriving from Dubai at Rajiv Gandhi International Airport in Hyderabad on 20 September 2025. [@1,2] Her luggage held packets of a greenish, lumpy substance that tested positive as hydroponic cannabis: 6 kilograms in the bag she carried and 6 more kilograms in a delayed checked-in bag that reached Hyderabad the same day. [@1,2]

The 12-kilogram haul was valued at nearly Rs 12 crore on the international market. [@1] The DRI arrested the woman under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, and further investigations were under way. [@1,2]
