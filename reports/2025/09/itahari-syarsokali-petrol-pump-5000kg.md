The Armed Police Force seized 158 sacks holding about 5,000 kg of cannabis from
two trucks parked at the Syarsokali Petrol Pump in Itahari-5, Sunsari. [@1] The
larger truck, Na 4 Kha 2901, held 124 sacks and the mini-truck, Ba 5 Kha 1169,
held 34. [@4] Both trucks carried vegetables and the sacks of cannabis were
hidden inside the load. [@2] APF spokesperson Senior Superintendent Sudhir Jung
Thapa said the 21-member team was led by Armed Police Inspector Ashok Neupane.
[@4]

The trucks and the cannabis were taken into control and the load was being
weighed ahead of being handed to the District Police Office in Sunsari. [@1] The
drivers and helpers of both trucks fled as the police moved in. [@2] Police said
they were searching for the men and that further investigation was under way.
[@2] Thapa said the people involved fled the scene after seeing the police and
were being searched for. [@4]

Superintendent of Police Suman Kumar Timilsina of the Sunsari District Police
Office said police had unconfirmed reports of around 5,000 to 6,000 kg of
cannabis being seized from Itahari by the APF. [@1] Security sources told The
Rising Nepal that this was the first time such a large quantity, more than 5,000
kg in two trucks, had been hauled in Sunsari. [@1] Kenjo Khabar described
trafficking across the border area as rising while police posts were displaced
during the Gen-Z movement, Nepal's September 2025 protests. [@3]
