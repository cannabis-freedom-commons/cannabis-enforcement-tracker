Huang and Chen rented a factory in an unnamed Anhui county in March 2023 to grow cannabis for
sale. [@1,2] By the end of that year they had picked, dried and trimmed about 3.4 kilograms of
finished cannabis and packed it into 170 bags of 20 grams each. [@1,2]

In March 2024 they put the bags in suitcases and drove to Shandong, using rental cars and leaving
the cannabis at night in spots around Qingdao and Weihai to avoid detection. [@1,2] In April 2024
they sold it through an online dating platform: Chen handled the advertising and dealt with the
platform's staff, while Huang took payment in virtual currency and cashed it out. [@1,2] About 60
bags, roughly 1.2 kilograms, were sold for 110,000 yuan — 70,000 to Huang and 40,000 to
Chen. [@1,2]

The Lixin County Procuratorate indicted Huang on 18 August 2025 and added a money-laundering
charge over the conversion of the drug money into virtual currency and back. [@1,2] On
10 September 2025 the Lixin County Court sentenced him to four years and six months in prison and
a 400,000-yuan fine on the trafficking, transport and money-laundering counts; the judgment has
taken effect. [@1,2] The Supreme People's Procuratorate featured the case among ten typical drug
cases published on 25 June 2026, holding that converting drug proceeds into virtual assets is
self-laundering. [@1]
