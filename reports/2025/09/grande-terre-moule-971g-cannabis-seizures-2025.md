Gendarmes of the Le Moule company ran several anti-drug operations in communes
across Grande-Terre over the weekend. [@1] On Friday evening, 19 September, they
checked a known dealing spot at Douville in Sainte-Anne and seized 64 grams of
cannabis. [@1] Officers recovered 50 of those grams from an individual who tried
to flee and was caught. [@2]

On Saturday in Morne-à-l'Eau, a road check and search of a vehicle uncovered 501
grams of loose cannabis flowers. [@1] On Sunday, 21 September, gendarmes of the
DSI, the gendarmerie's surveillance and intervention detachment, intervened in
Saint-François. [@2] Patrolling rue de la Liberté, they intercepted a scooter.
[@2] The rider was carrying 125 grams of cannabis packed in small bags ready for
sale. [@2]

Across the whole week, the Le Moule company seized 971 grams of cannabis in
total. [@1] The same week also yielded 49 grams of cocaine and 10 grams of
crack, which are mentioned only to explain the mixed seizure and are excluded
from the cannabis figure. [@2]
