Police officers at the Djinnack advance post in Bignona department intercepted a
cart concealing 150 kilos of cannabis, known locally as chanvre indien. [@1]
They arrested the cart's convoyeur, 33-year-old Djibril Diamé, in flagrante
delicto. [@2]

A few hours later a crowd of local residents including an imam gathered outside
the police post to demand his release. [@1] Some protesters issued threats and
put strong pressure on the officers deployed at the post. [@1] To avoid
escalation the authorities released the suspect under summons. [@2] Police
nevertheless kept the seized load secured under seal pending further judicial
proceedings. [@1]

Libération, as cited by both outlets, reported that an alleged accomplice aged
only 8 had also been identified, raising questions about the use of minors in
trafficking. [@1] Sources close to the investigation said further arrests could
follow. [@1] The outlets place the case in a longer series of seizures
confirming the centrality of Casamance in the illicit cannabis trade. [@1]
