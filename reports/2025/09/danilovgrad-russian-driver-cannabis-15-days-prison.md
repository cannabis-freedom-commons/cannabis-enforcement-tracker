The Minor Offences Court in Podgorica, Danilovgrad Department, sentenced a
Russian citizen identified only as M.K. to 15 days in prison for driving under
the influence of cannabis. [@1] The court said the offence fell under Article
317a, paragraph 1, item 3, read with Article 182 of the Law on Road Traffic
Safety. [@1] Judge spokesperson Ana Bokan said the offence happened on 22
September 2025, when the man took part in public traffic in a part of
Danilovgrad municipality with cannabis in his body. [@1] The cannabis was
detected by testing carried out by police officers. [@1]

The same decision imposed a protective measure banning M.K. from driving a
category B motor vehicle anywhere in Montenegro for six months. [@1] The court
said the sentence and the ban followed a weighing of mitigating and aggravating
circumstances on the defendant's side. [@1]

Vijesti's Montenegrin-language version of the report carries the same account of
the sentence, the date and the six-month driving ban. [@2]
