One 56-year-old man was arrested in a joint Hawks and Durban Metro operation at
a farm in Camperdown, KwaZulu-Natal. [@1]

Police searched the farm and found 4,165 cannabis plants, 70 buckets of
processed cannabis, and paraphernalia to a street value of about R30 million.
[@2] Police also confiscated six firearms and more than 165 live rounds of
ammunition, and Forensic Science Laboratory experts processed the scene. [@1]

The suspect is to be charged with cultivation of cannabis, dealing in cannabis,
and unlawful possession of firearms and live rounds of ammunition, and is
expected to appear before the Camperdown Magistrate's Court. [@3] KwaZulu-Natal
Hawks Provincial Head Major General Lesetja Senona welcomed the arrest and
praised the officers for the good work. [@1]
