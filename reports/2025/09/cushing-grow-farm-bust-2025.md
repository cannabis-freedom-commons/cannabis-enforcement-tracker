The Oklahoma Bureau of Narcotics (OBN) seized more than 12,000 marijuana plants from a grow
farm in Cushing, according to a News 9 report published on 11 September 2025.[@1] Agents
also seized nearly 300 pounds (about 136 kg) of processed marijuana at the site.[@1]

## An investigation into illegal cultivation

OBN said the bust was part of an investigation into illegal activities and multiple
violations at the farm.[@1] The report does not name the farm or its licence status, and it
gives no separate date for the raid itself.[@1]

## Seven detained, two handed to ICE

Seven people were detained during the operation, two of whom were placed into the custody of
federal Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE).[@1] No arrests, names or charges were
reported.[@1]
