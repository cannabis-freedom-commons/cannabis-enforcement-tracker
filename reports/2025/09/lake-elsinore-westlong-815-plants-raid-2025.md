Deputies from the Lake Elsinore Sheriff's Station Special Enforcement Team
served a search warrant in the 33000 block of Westlong Street in an
unincorporated part of Lake Elsinore on Thursday, 4 September 2025. [@2] The
search followed complaints about illegal marijuana cultivation at the residence
received by the station a day earlier. [@1]

The main residence and a second dwelling on the property had been converted into
cannabis grow sites, and deputies seized 815 marijuana plants in various stages
of growth. [@2] MyNewsLA reported the seizure as approximately 815 plants. [@3]
Southern California Edison crews came to the residence to disconnect power to an
electrical system that had been illegally altered. [@1]

No one was arrested; the sheriff's office said the property was vacant when
deputies arrived. [@1] The sheriff's office described the investigation as
ongoing and asked anyone with information to contact Deputy Olson at the Lake
Elsinore station. [@2]
