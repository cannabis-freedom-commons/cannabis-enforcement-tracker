More than 130 Sûreté du Québec officers, assisted by Montreal police and the
Ontario Provincial Police, dismantled a cannabis trafficking ring linked to
Asian organized crime on 17 September 2025. [@1] The operation began with the
SQ's Contraband Investigation Service in St-Pierre-les-Becquets and widened
to more than a dozen searches across the Montreal area, Vaudreuil-Dorion and
Markham, Ontario. [@1,2]

By late afternoon police reported seizing just over a tonne of packaged
cannabis, 1,110 plants, 50 sticks of dynamite, 100 one-ounce gold bars and
$320,000 in cash. [@1] SQ spokesperson Laurie Avoine said medical
personal-production certificates appeared to have been used fraudulently to
give the network cover. [@1] Two men were arrested in connection with the
operation. [@2]
