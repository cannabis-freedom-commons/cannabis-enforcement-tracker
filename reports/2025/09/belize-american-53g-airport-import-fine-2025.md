A 31-year-old American man from New York was arrested at the Philip Goldson
International Airport on September 10, 2025, after arriving on an American
Airlines flight. [@1] He spent the night at the Ladyville Police Station, the
night of his 32nd birthday. [@2]

Officers found 53 grams of marijuana in his luggage. [@1] He appeared
unrepresented the next day in the Belize City Magistrate's Court, where he
pleaded guilty to importation of a controlled drug. [@1]

The offence carries a minimum $10,000 fine and up to three years in prison, but
the magistrate spared him the minimum mandatory penalty. [@1] The magistrate
started from a $3,000 fine and deducted $1,000 for his early guilty plea, citing
his early plea and lack of prior convictions in Belize. [@2] He was fined $2,000
plus a $5.00 cost of court, to be paid immediately or serve one year in prison.
