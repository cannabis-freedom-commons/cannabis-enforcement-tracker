Gendarmes, customs officers and sailors watching Martinique's coastline seized
more than 55 kg of cannabis herb during an attempted illegal landing in the
south of the island at about 22:00 on Thursday 4 September 2025. [@1]
France-Antilles describes the seizure as the fruit of a joint operation by the
gendarmerie, the national navy and the customs service. [@3]

The people landing the load fled in their boat, which had come from the Saint
Lucia Channel, and no arrests were reported. [@2] Investigations continue under
the authority of the prosecutor's office. [@1]

The seizure fell under Operation Scotopélia, a surveillance operation launched
in the first quarter of 2025 that places officers at entry points into
Martinican territory and is named after a fishing owl. [@1] France-Antilles says
Scotopélia, in place since the start of 2025, targets trafficking in drugs,
weapons and stolen goods and other illegal trafficking. [@3]
