A joint team led by the Valparaíso focus prosecutor with the Navy's Dipolmar
unit, Carabineros OS7, Customs and Peru's National Police detected and seized
848.4 kilograms of creepy marijuana. [@1] The drug had arrived by sea from
Colombia. [@1] Officers found it hidden in a container inside the installations
of the Port of San Antonio. [@2] Investigators said its final destination was
the Dominican Republic. [@2]

The load was packed in 40 boxes holding a total of 762 packages. [@2] The
authorities called the joint action Operación Independencia and linked its
detection to cooperation with Peruvian authorities against the Colombia-origin
load. [@1] Authorities set its commercial value at 3,187,147,260 Chilean pesos.
[@2] Customs said profiling had flagged an elevated risk, so officers opened and
inspected the cargo directly without using the scanner truck. [@1] The
prosecutor described the creepy marijuana as highly addictive with a significant
health impact. [@1]

The prosecutor said the seizure kept Chilean ports from being used as a transit
route from producer countries to consumer destinations. [@3] Carabineros zone
chief Patricia Vásquez called the seizure of more than 830 kilos valued at more
than 3,000 million pesos a joint success with the Maritime Police. [@1]
