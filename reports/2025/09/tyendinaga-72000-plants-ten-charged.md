Officers from the Ontario Provincial Police Organized Crime Enforcement Bureau
and the Tyendinaga Police Service, with other police branches and Canada
Border Services, executed search warrants on 23 and 24 September 2025 at four
illicit cannabis production sites on Tyendinaga Mohawk Territory. [@1,2]

Police seized more than 72,000 illicit cannabis plants with an estimated value
above $122 million, plus three firearms, ammunition and other items. [@1,2]
Officers also stopped an unlawful diversion of water from the Bay of Quinte to
the sites and seized offence-related property valued at about $530,000. [@2]

Ten people, six of them not members of Tyendinaga Mohawk Territory, were
arrested and charged under the Criminal Code and the Cannabis Act. [@1,2] They
were released and ordered to appear before the Ontario Court of Justice in
Belleville on 5 November 2025. [@2] Police said the sites were controlled by
non-Indigenous organized criminal networks aided by a limited number of
community members, after an investigation begun in April 2025. [@1,2]
