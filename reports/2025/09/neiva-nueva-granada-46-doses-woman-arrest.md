National Police officers in Neiva carried out a search and seizure operation in
the Nueva Granada neighborhood of Commune 8, arresting a woman and seizing 46
doses of marijuana. [@1] The Criminal Investigation Section (SIJIN) acted with
support from the Special Operations Group (GOES). [@1] The operation formed part
of the strategy against local drug trafficking. [@1]

The arrested woman was accused of facilitating the sale of marijuana in the
Nueva Granada neighborhood of Neiva. [@1] Officers seized 46 doses of marijuana
during the search. [@1] Investigators said the captured woman was allegedly the
driving force behind the sale and marketing of narcotics in this sector. [@1]
Police said that illicit activity mainly affected minors in the city of Neiva.
[@1]

The captured woman and the seized substance were placed at the disposal of the
competent authority for the crime of trafficking, manufacturing or carrying
narcotics. [@1] Lieutenant Colonel Alex Andrés Muñoz Ausecha serves as acting
commander of the Neiva Metropolitan Police. [@1] He said officers continue
working against drug trafficking affecting public safety and community life and
remain safe, accessible and present to protect the community. [@1]
