Three foreign nationals were detained in Tumbes as they tried to bring drugs
into Peru from Ecuador. [@1] Police intercepted them in two separate operations
at the Centro Binacional de Atención Fronteriza, known as Cebaf. [@1] In the
first case, Ecuadorian Hugo Barco was carrying a styrofoam box that apparently
served to transport black clams, a local seafood. [@1] When authorities
inspected the box they found several bags with marijuana at the bottom, and the
drug was said to be bound for Lima. [@1]

Hours later, two Russian nationals were detained after authorities found six
kilos of high-purity cocaine hydrochloride hidden in their suitcases. [@1] Both
Russians had also entered Peru from Ecuador and could not justify the contents
of their luggage. [@1] The drugs were seized immediately and the detainees were
handed to the Public Ministry for the investigation to continue. [@1] The two
Russians were detained at the Cebaf entry control when their suitcases went
through the sensors and the field test showed six and a half kilos of cocaine
hydrochloride, according to Andina. [@2] Andina names the pair as Zevs Kosmos
and Aleksandr Livinskii, both 47, travelling in a lead-grey Kia with Ecuadorian
plate PDI-3090. [@2] Andina describes the two Russians as members of a group it
calls the "Los Rusos de la Droga" clan. [@2] Andina says the detainees were
taken to the National Police criminalistics division in Tumbes, where the
provincial anti-drug prosecutor's office attended. [@2]

The vehicle used by the Ecuadorian was also held as part of the preliminary
proceedings. [@1] Both detentions took place at the Cebaf on the Peru-Ecuador
border, a strategic zone used for migration and trade control. [@1] The
authorities said they would keep reinforcing controls on the northern border
because more foreigners were trying to bring illegal substances into the
country. [@1] The three detainees remained in custody while the inquiries
continued. [@1] The prosecutor's office had already opened an investigation to
establish whether there were more people involved and what the final destination
of the drugs would be. [@1]
