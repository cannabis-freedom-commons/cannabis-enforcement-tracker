The Liberia Drug Enforcement Agency seized 1,929 kilograms of compressed marijuana in
Brewerville, on the outskirts of Monrovia, on 18 September 2025. [@1,2]

Fitzgerald Biago, then head of the LDEA interim team, told a press conference that the
intelligence-led operation was carried out with the Liberia National Police and found the drugs
in a building. [@1,2] GNN said the raid was in District #17. [@2] Biago valued the haul at
L$27,850,760, about US$146,604. [@1,2]

Two suspects were arrested: Umar Mohammed, 38, a Nigerian known as “Degote”, and Abdullah
Kamara, 18, a Sierra Leonean. [@1,2] Both were held by the LDEA while investigators tried to
establish whether the marijuana entered by land border or by water, and were to be charged and
sent to court when the investigation ended. [@1,2]
