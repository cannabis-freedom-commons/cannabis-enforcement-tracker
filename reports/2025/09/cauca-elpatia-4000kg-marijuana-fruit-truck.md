Units of the National Police and the National Army stopped a fruit truck on the
Mojarras–Popayán road at kilometre 31+100 in El Patía, Cauca, and arrested the
driver. [@2] The truck was covering the Santander de Quilichao–Ipiales route,
police said. [@3] Officers found 3,920 rectangular packages of marijuana hidden
among boxes of passion fruit and oranges in the vehicle. [@2]

The police inspection and weighing procedure put the gross weight of the
shipment at 4,000 kilograms. [@2] Police valued the load at close to 4 billion
pesos on the national market. [@2] Laboratory analysis confirmed that the
material was marijuana. [@5]

The driver, the drug and the truck were handed to the 37th Specialized
Prosecutor's Office against drug trafficking. [@3] The director of the National
Police, General Carlos Fernando Triana, said the load belonged to the Dagoberto
Ramos structure. [@4] EFE reported that the load was bound for Ecuador and was
to be delivered in Ipiales, on the border. [@6] The National Police said the
result formed part of its registration and background checks on the main roads
of the department. [@1]
