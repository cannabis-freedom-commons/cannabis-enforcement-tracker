The Royal Eswatini Police Service Anti-Narcotics Unit, working jointly across all four regions, raided a homestead at Nyonyane in the Mliba police station area on Monday 15 September 2025 and arrested a 38-year-old man found with 2,169 kg of dagga. [@1,2] Officers uncovered a homestead being used as a storage hub and retrieved 277 bags of dagga along with additional unprocessed stock. [@1,2]

Police put the street value of the haul at E5.4 million, calling it one of the largest seizures in recent times. [@1,2] The suspect was expected to appear before the Mbabane Magistrate's Court to face charges. [@1,2]
