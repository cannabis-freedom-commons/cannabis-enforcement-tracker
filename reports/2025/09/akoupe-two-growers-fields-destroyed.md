The regional branch of the DPSD, the Ivorian drug-squad police, arrested two
cannabis growers in the commune of Akoupé during the night of Thursday 18 to
Friday 19 September 2025. [@1] The two men arrested were A.Y.M., aged 38, and
Y.B.C., aged 59. [@1] Police said both suspects were placed at the disposal of
the justice system to answer for their acts. [@1]

Officers destroyed two cannabis fields during the same operation in Akoupé. [@1]
The operation also seized four bags of bulk cannabis and 172 cannabis cuttings,
with police putting the total weight at 45 kilograms. [@1] The reported
45-kilogram total covered both the four bags and the 172 cuttings together. [@1]

The arrest took place under Operation Vacances sécurisées 10 (Secure Holidays
10), a security operation announced by the Directorate General of the National
Police. [@1] Police presented the action as part of a stepped-up National Police
effort against drug trafficking and use through that operation. [@1]
