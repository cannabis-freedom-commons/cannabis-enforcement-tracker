On the evening of Monday 1 September 2025, about twenty police officers,
including the anti-crime brigade and a dog unit, operated in the Cour Zamia
neighbourhood of Pointe-à-Pitre, Guadeloupe. [@2] The operation came under the
authority of Thierry Devimeux, the newly arrived prefect of Guadeloupe, and was
carried out at the request of Pointe-à-Pitre public prosecutor Caroline Calbo.
[@1] Officers from different services were mobilised, including the anti-crime
brigade, the specialised field brigade, the departmental intervention company
and the border police. [@1]

Police were targeting drug trafficking, the carrying of weapons, theft and
handling stolen goods, and ran 34 identity checks and 4 vehicle checks. [@1] Two
Dominican nationals in an irregular situation were arrested. [@1] The dog unit
found about 150 grams of cannabis herb packed for retail sale at two separate
spots. [@1] Guadeloupe La 1ère reported that about 250 grams of cannabis herb,
conditioned for resale, were seized. [@2]

The operation was carried out under the departmental plan to restore everyday
security, known by its French initials PADRSQ, and the reinforced-security-city
scheme. [@1] The prosecutor described Cour Zamia as a drug-dealing spot. [@2]
