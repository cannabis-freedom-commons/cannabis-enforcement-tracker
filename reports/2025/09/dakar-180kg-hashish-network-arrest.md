Dakar’s Regional Narcotics Brigade arrested two people in Dakar on 10 September
2025 after 180 kg of hashish was seized in Morocco. [@1] The Dakar arrests
followed operational intelligence after Moroccan authorities intercepted a
refrigerated truck heading toward Senegal. [@1] A scanner search revealed the
concealed drugs, leading to the arrest of a Senegalese transporter and a
Moroccan transporter. [@1] Moroccan authorities arrested a Senegalese national
and a Moroccan national who were in charge of transporting the drugs. [@2]
Dakar’s brigade chief was then informed of the presence of two other suspects
linked to the seizure. [@1] The two individuals arrested in Dakar were placed in
custody while the investigation continues. [@1]

The 180 kg consisted of 60 kg seized in a truck in Morocco bound for Senegal and
120 kg seized in a Moroccan depot. [@1] Police seized 150,000 FCFA and three
mobile phones in the case. [@2]

Police say the detained suspects confirmed a vast hashish-trafficking network
moving drugs through Senegal toward Burkina Faso and Côte d’Ivoire. [@1] The
traffickers used trucks operating on legal commercial routes to camouflage the
illicit transport. [@1] Once in Senegal, the hashish was to be sent onward to
supply Burkina Faso and Côte d’Ivoire. [@1]
