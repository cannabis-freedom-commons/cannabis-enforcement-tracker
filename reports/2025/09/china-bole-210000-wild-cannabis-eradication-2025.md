A railway police patrol in Bole, Xinjiang, found a stand of wild cannabis and
reported a count of about 210,000 plants. [@1,2] Multiple departments joined
the police to remove the plants. [@1,2] People's Daily Client published its
video report on 9 September 2025; its images identify Bole and display the
approximate count. [@1]

The later county-media report says police inquiries found that no one had
contracted or leased the site where the plants grew. [@2] It does not identify
a grower or explain how the plants came to be there. [@2] The footage shows
officers pulling and stacking plants in brush and at the edge of a wooded
field. [@1]

Neither report gives a separate date for the patrol or removal; the later
article, published on 19 September, says only that it happened “recently.”
[@1,2] The reports name Bole but give no more precise location, acreage or
plant weight. [@1,2] Neither names a suspect or reports an arrest or charge.
