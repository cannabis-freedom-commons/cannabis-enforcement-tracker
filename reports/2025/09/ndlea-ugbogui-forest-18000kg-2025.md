Edo State Command officers of Nigeria's National Drug Law Enforcement Agency (NDLEA)
destroyed a cannabis plantation at Ugbogui Forest in Ovia South West Local Government
Area on Thursday 11 September 2025, acting on intelligence about cultivation there. [@1,2]
The farm covered 6.79 hectares, and the command put the destroyed crop at over 18,000
kilogrammes. [@1,2]

Officers also recovered harvested and processed cannabis packed in bags and weighing
1,176 kilogrammes. [@1,2] Four men found taking refuge in tents on the farm were
arrested: Ebenezer Wang, 48, of Uyo in Akwa Ibom State; Oshare John, 45, of Ughelli
South in Delta State; Emmanuel Monday, 29, of Eket in Akwa Ibom State; and David
Sunday, 30. [@1,2]

State Commander Mitchell Ofoyeju said using farmland to grow cannabis threatens food
security in the state. [@1,2] The suspects were under investigation and expected to be
charged in court. [@2]
