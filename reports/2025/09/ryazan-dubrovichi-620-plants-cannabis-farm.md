Criminal-investigation officers of the Ryazan Oblast Interior Ministry and
Ryazansky district police detained a 32-year-old Ryazan resident who had set up
a country farm to grow cannabis on a large scale and make marijuana for sale.
[@1] Detectives detained the suspect in Ryazan after he sold 21 grams of
plant-based drug to a test buyer. [@1] Officers seized more than 100 grams of
cannabis from his car and from his flat on Solotchinskoye shosse. [@1]

On a rented plot near Dubrovichi village he had installed five container cabins,
and inside them officers found 620 cannabis plants, containers with 9 kg 621 g
of finished drug and 9 kg 470 g of cannabis plant parts still drying. [@1]
Police said the batch was the largest of plant-based drugs taken out of illegal
circulation by law enforcement in the region in 2025. [@1] According to the
detectives he grew elite cannabis varieties in the greenhouses and drove to the
plot every day to tend the plants. [@1] An investigator of the Ryazansky
district police opened criminal cases, and the suspect was placed in custody for
the duration of the investigation. [@1] Ryazannews, citing the regional Interior
Ministry, reported the man was unemployed and had no previous convictions. [@2]
RZN.info, also citing the regional Interior Ministry press service, said
officers seized all the cannabis plants, weighing 25 kg 540 g, and added them to
the case file. [@3] RZN.info reported that the regional Interior Ministry press
service had earlier described the suspect as unemployed and previously
convicted. [@3]

The Ryazansky district prosecutor's office approved an indictment against a
33-year-old local resident. [@4] Prosecutors accuse him of illegally cultivating
drug-containing plants, selling drugs in a significant amount and attempting to
sell drugs on a large scale. [@4] According to the investigation he grew
cannabis on a rented plot in Dubrovichi between February and August 2025 and,
after cutting parts of the plants, made a drug for onward sale. [@4] Prosecutors
said 620 drug-containing plants were seized and that they weighed more than 25
kg when dried. [@4] The offences carry 10 to 20 years in prison, and the case
was sent to Ryazansky district court on its merits. [@4] The criminal case was
sent to Ryazansky district court for consideration on the merits. [@4]
