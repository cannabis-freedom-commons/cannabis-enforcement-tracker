Bogotá metropolitan police reported the capture of a 33-year-old woman accused
of trying to send marijuana camouflaged in a parcel bound for San Andrés. [@1]
The detention occurred near El Dorado International Airport during routine
checks by officers in the cargo warehouses. [@1] Officers intervened after
noticing the suspicious attitude of the person dispatching the box. [@1]

During inspection officers found 40 rectangular polyethylene-wrapped packages
hidden inside a stove containing a dry substance with characteristics and odour
similar to marijuana. [@1] The load weighed approximately 21 kilograms. [@1] The
woman was captured at the scene and placed with the seized material at the
disposal of the Attorney General's Office for alleged trafficking, manufacture
or possession of narcotics. [@1]

El Dorado airport police commander Wilson Torres said the operation resulted
from daily inspection, search and control procedures at the air terminal in
coordination with the United States HSI interagency unit. [@1]
