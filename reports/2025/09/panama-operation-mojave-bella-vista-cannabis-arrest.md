Panama's drug prosecutors and the National Police's intelligence directorate,
the DNIP, ran Operation Mojave on 12 September 2025 to dismantle a criminal
group trafficking drugs that arrived from abroad in courier parcels. [@1]
Officers searched the cargo area of Tocumen International Airport and found a
box sent from the United States and addressed to Panama's Bella Vista
corregimiento holding twelve vape devices loaded with Delta-8 THC with a total
weight of 584.54 grams. [@1]

The same operation included a controlled purchase that tested positive and a
search of a business premises, where officers arrested a Panamanian citizen
after finding dried plant matter presumed to be marijuana in different
containers, gummies with a presumed illicit substance, a vape device and an
envelope of pink powder. [@1]

The Public Ministry's statement grouped the Mojave arrest with three people
taken into custody during a week of seizures totalling more than 1.6 tonnes of
drugs of different types across Panamanian ports, a vehicle carrying 400
packages and the La Nueva Joya prison. [@1] Mi Diario gave the same three-person
total and described the courier network behind Mojave as moving synthetic drugs.
[@2] The prosecuting agency said it would keep pursuing objective and
transparent investigations and bring cases before the courts as the Constitution
and the law require. [@1]
