Gendarmes in Guadeloupe carried out two anti-drug operations on 17 September
2025, one in Saint-François and one in Sainte-Anne, seizing hard drugs and
cannabis. [@1] In Saint-François, an operation was conducted on rue de la
Liberté, a street known as a drug-dealing spot. [@1] The Détachement de
surveillance et d'intervention (DSI) of Le Moule, a gendarmerie surveillance and
intervention unit, seized 107.3 grams of cannabis and 19.9 grams of cocaine
there. [@1]

In Sainte-Anne, a similar operation on rue Lethière seized 9.39 grams of
cocaine, 6.84 grams of crack, 57.05 grams of cannabis herb and 15 grams of dried
cannabis leaf. [@1]

The gendarmes said both actions were part of their daily commitment to securing
neighbourhoods and stemming trafficking that affects residents' daily lives.
[@1] Both operations fell under the Plan d'action départemental de restauration
de la sécurité au quotidien, a departmental plan to restore everyday security.
[@1]

In Saint-François the cannabis haul was 107 grams and the cocaine haul nearly 20
grams, in figures the report rounds. [@2]

The Sainte-Anne operation was described as a second coup de filet (crackdown) in
the same sweep. [@2]

The gendarmerie gave the same precise weights: 107.3 grams of cannabis and 19.9
grams of cocaine at Saint-François. [@3]

At Sainte-Anne the gendarmerie listed 9.39 grams of cocaine, 6.84 grams of
crack, 57.05 grams of cannabis herb and 15 grams of dried cannabis leaf. [@3]

RCI reported that the aim of the operations was to dismantle networks and
identify drug-dealing points. [@2]
