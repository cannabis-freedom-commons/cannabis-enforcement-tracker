Uzbekistan's State Security Service reported seizing batches of drugs smuggled
from Afghanistan in Fergana region and Karakalpakstan, and said several people
were remanded in custody. [@1] In the Fergana case a 42-year-old businessman
from Margilan and his 36-year-old accomplice were detained as they received
9,896 grams of hashish. [@1] The hashish had been carried from Afghanistan into
Surkhandarya region by couriers, then moved and hidden in a stash on a field
plot in Yazyavan district for sale in the Fergana Valley. [@1] The businessman,
who tried to flee the scene, was found with 14,500 US dollars and 6.25 million
som, and 110 grams of opium, 1,200 dollars and 28 million som were found at the
home of a third suspect. [@1] Daryo.uz attributed the operation to State
Security Service officers, gave the businessman's birth year as 1983 and his
accomplice's as 1989, and said officers also seized 9 kg 896 g of hashish and
electronic scales. [@2]

In the other case a 62-year-old resident of Turtkul district in Karakalpakstan,
previously convicted of drug trafficking, and a 47-year-old resident of
Khazarasp district in Khorezm region ordered a large consignment from Afghan
drug traders. [@1] The two men were detained as they took 6,149 grams of opium
and 9,814 grams of poppy straw from a stash in Turtkul district. [@1]

Criminal cases were opened against all the detainees under article 246 on
smuggling and article 273 on drug trafficking, and they were remanded in
custody. [@1] Daryo.uz gave the Turtkul district resident's birth year as 1963
and the Khorezm region resident's as 1978, and said the State Security Service
was still working to identify their accomplices. [@2]
