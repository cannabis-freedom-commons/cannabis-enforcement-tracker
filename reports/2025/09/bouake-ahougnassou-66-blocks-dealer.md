Bouaké's anti-drug unit arrested one man suspected of selling drugs in the
Ahougnassou quarter of Bouaké during the night of Sunday 7 to Monday 8 September
2025. [@1] The intervention was carried out at about 00:15. [@1]

A search of the suspect's home found 66 blocks of cannabis, hidden in the house.
[@1] The Cellule Anti-Drogue is the national gendarmerie's specialist drug
squad. [@4] A source close to the investigation said the suspect belonged to a
local, possibly regional, distribution network. [@1] The blocks were said to
amount to several kilograms meant to supply resale circuits in several Bouaké
quarters. [@1]

The suspect was placed in custody while police investigated possible accomplices
and the supply chain. [@1] Police said the suspect would be presented before the
competent courts to answer for his acts. [@3]

Fraternité Matin reported that the search was made possible by local
intelligence and awareness campaigns. [@2] The arrest followed information
gathered by the security forces, according to a security note seen by
Abidjan.net. [@3]

The seizure was described as part of coordinated security operations against
drug trafficking in the Gbêkê region, of which Bouaké is the capital. [@2] The
regional gendarmerie described the operation as part of its strategy to dry up
drug distribution circuits and strengthen security in large cities. [@4]
Authorities called on residents to report suspicious behaviour to the security
forces. [@1]
