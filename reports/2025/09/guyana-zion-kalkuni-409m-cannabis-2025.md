The Guyana Police Force's Narcotics Branch and Regional Division 6 found and
destroyed more than GY$409 million worth of cannabis in two operations in the
Upper Berbice River area on Sunday 28 September 2025. [@2] At Zion, Upper
Berbice River, officers found about two acres of land that was being prepared
for cultivation, with a makeshift camp and drying area a short distance from the
fields. [@2] The Zion camp measured about 30 by 20 feet, had a living and
kitchen area and held about 350 pounds (about 159 kg) of dried cannabis. [@2]
Police burned the Zion camp and its dried cannabis, which they valued at
GY$142,881,480. [@2]

Between 05:00 and 10:20 on the same Sunday, the same units carried out a
separate eradication exercise at Kalkuni, Upper Berbice River, on a plot of
about two acres. [@2] The Kalkuni plot held about 3,000 cannabis plants between
two and seven feet tall, which police valued at GY$1,016,400. [@2] A makeshift
camp with a drying area at Kalkuni contained about 650 pounds (about 295 kg) of
dried cannabis, valued at GY$266,367,720. [@2] A search of the Kalkuni camp also
turned up two twelve-gauge shotguns with no visible serial numbers and nineteen
twelve-gauge cartridges. [@2] Police took possession of both firearms and sent
them to the Narcotics Branch for analysis by its ballistics section. [@2] The
Kalkuni camp, its dried cannabis and the plants were all destroyed by fire. [@2]

The police statement also reported finding two unlicensed twelve-gauge shotguns
and ammunition alongside the destroyed cannabis. [@1]
