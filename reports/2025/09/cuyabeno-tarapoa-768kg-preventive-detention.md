A judge in Cuyabeno, Sucumbíos, ordered pretrial detention for two Colombian men
arrested on 8 September 2025 after a military operation seized 800 blocks of
marijuana. [@1] Prosecutors charged Edgar Alexis G. C. and Omar Daniel S. N.
with allegedly transporting 800 packages of marijuana in a pickup. [@2] The
soldiers operated on a road in Tarapoa, in the Río Aguas Negras area of Cuyabeno
canton. [@3] The operation ran from the night of 7 September into the early
hours of 8 September. [@4]

At the hearing on the arrest and filing of charges, the prosecutor presented the
case to a judge. [@3] The Fiscalía said the weighing record gave the load a net
weight of 768 kg, and a preliminary field test identified it as marijuana. [@2]
The Defence Ministry estimated the cargo's illicit-market value at about one
million dollars. [@1] The judge also ordered the pickup held and prohibited its
sale, and froze the men's bank accounts. [@2]
