Gomel customs officers, working with staff of the Central district police
department and the Dobrush district traffic police, detained a 35-year-old
resident of Zhlobin with marijuana. [@1] The man was a bus passenger travelling
to Russia for work and intended to take the drug out of Belarus with him. [@1]

Plastic bags holding a partly ground brown-green plant substance were found when
officers examined his personal belongings. [@1] The State Committee of Forensic
Expertise for Gomel Oblast confirmed the substance was marijuana weighing 60
grams. [@1] The man, who already has a conviction under part 3 of article 328 of
the Criminal Code, said he had gathered and dried the plants himself. [@1]

The Gomel regional newspaper Petrikovskiye novosti republished the customs
statement, crediting the Gomel customs press service. [@2] Gomel customs opened
a criminal case against him under part 1 of article 14 and part 1 of article
328-1 of the Criminal Code, which carry a maximum of seven years in prison. [@1]
