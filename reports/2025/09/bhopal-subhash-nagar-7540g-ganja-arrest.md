Bhopal's Crime Branch caught a man accused of supplying ganja in the city and
seized 7.540 kg of it from him at a closed petrol pump near Subhash Nagar bridge
in MP Nagar. [@1] Police identified the man as 30-year-old Ranjeet Sen, a
resident of Barkhedi Ekta Chowk in the Jehangirabad police-station area of
Bhopal. [@1] The plastic sack he was carrying held eight brown packets of ganja,
weighed together at 7.540 kg. [@1] Officers also seized an Android mobile phone
from him. [@1] Police valued the ganja and the phone together at about Rs 1.6
lakh. [@1] The team acted on a tip-off and found him at the closed petrol pump,
where it matched him to the informer's description before searching him. [@1]

Sen told police he bought ganja cheaply in Odisha, India's neighbouring eastern
state, and sold it at higher prices in the Chhola Mandir and Nishatpura areas of
Bhopal. [@1] Police said his trade was making glassware. [@2] He had an earlier
case at Gautam Nagar police station registered in 2024 under sections 294, 323,
327 and 506 of the Indian Penal Code, a fighting offence rather than a drugs
offence. [@2]

Police registered a case against him under sections 8 and 20 of the NDPS Act,
India's main anti-drug law. [@1] Officers said they were still investigating the
wider supply chain and possible accomplices. [@1] The Crime Branch said it had
seized 258.441 kg of ganja from 48 accused people in Bhopal during 2025. [@1]
