CBSA officers examining an outbound container at the Port of Halifax on
5 September 2025 found four wooden crates of vacuum-sealed cannabis behind a
declared load of wire mesh bins. [@1,2] The shipment was bound for a business
in the United Kingdom. [@1,2] Officers counted 1,479 packages weighing
801.5 kg and handed the cannabis to the RCMP's federal policing unit in Nova
Scotia for investigation. [@1]

Although cannabis is legal in Canada, taking it across the border is a serious
criminal offence, punishable by up to five years under the Customs Act and up
to 14 under the Cannabis Act. [@1]
