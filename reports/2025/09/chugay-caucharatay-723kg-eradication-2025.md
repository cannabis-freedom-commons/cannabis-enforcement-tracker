Peru's National Police anti-drug officers carried out five interventions in the
hamlet of Caucharatay, in Chugay district, Sánchez Carrión province, La
Libertad. [@3] Twenty-seven officers took part in the operations between 22 and
26 September 2025 at five separate points. [@3]

On 22 September officers eradicated and burned 29,000 plantings on one hectare
of cannabis. [@2] The next day they destroyed 43,000 more marijuana seedlings on
a hectare and a half. [@2] In all, the operation covered more than two and a
half hectares of illegal marijuana fields. [@3]

Officers seized and destroyed 71 sacks holding 723 kilograms and 800 grams of
marijuana. [@2] Police put the value of the destroyed marijuana at 206,760
soles. [@2] A separate Infobae account of the same Chugay operation reported
49,000 cannabis plants eradicated and 200 kilograms of the drug burned. [@1] The
same account put the value of the crops destroyed at more than 500,000 soles.
[@1]

No arrests were reported because those responsible fled minutes before officers
arrived. [@1] Police said investigations continue to identify and dismantle
those responsible for the cultivation. [@3]
