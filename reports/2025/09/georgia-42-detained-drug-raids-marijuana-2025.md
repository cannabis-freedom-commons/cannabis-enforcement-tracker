Georgia's Interior Ministry detained 42 people on drug charges in a police
operation across the country over the previous 24 hours, deputy interior
minister Aleksandre Darakhvelidze said at a briefing. [@1] Those held include
Georgian citizens and foreign nationals. [@1] According to the deputy minister,
most of those detained are large-scale drug dealers, while the rest are accused
of illegally buying and keeping drugs in large quantities. [@2]

Under a court order, investigators carried out covert measures including audio
and video recording, which they said confirmed that most of those held were
involved in a drug-selling scheme. [@1] The dealers left drugs in set places for
later sale and sent buyers the information through an internet application, the
deputy minister said. [@1] Police seized especially large batches of various
drugs intended for sale on the black market, including heroin, cocaine,
alpha-PVP, buprenorphine, Suboxone, methadone, ketamine, mephedrone, MDMA and
marijuana. [@1] The crimes carry a penalty of up to 20 years in prison or life
imprisonment. [@1]

The interior ministry's press service distributed footage of special forces
storming drug dealers' homes. [@2] The Interior Ministry says it carries out
such raids systematically and had detained more than 1,500 people on drug
charges in the previous two months, a significant part of them drug dealers.
[@2]
