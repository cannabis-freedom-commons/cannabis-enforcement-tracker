On 22 December 2025 the Lille prosecutor announced the dismantling of a large
network importing cocaine and cannabis through the Norman ports after an
investigation of nearly three years. [@1] The investigation began in March 2022
in Rouen into the importation and resale of cannabis and cocaine. [@2] After the
international dimension emerged, the Lille specialised jurisdiction took over
the case with two investigating magistrates. [@2] Investigators identified a
Rouen-based network that moved drugs through the ports of Le Havre and
Moulineaux using dockers' information on container movements. [@2]

On 11 December 2023 officers found 23 kilos of cannabis resin in the storage
unit of one of the identified traffickers. [@2] Nine people were then arrested
and all were charged. [@2] Six of them were placed in pretrial detention and
three dockers were placed under judicial supervision. [@2] The suspected head
was located in Casablanca, Morocco, accused of organising all the imports by
road and sea of cannabis resin and herb from Morocco and cocaine from Colombia.
[@2] Those goods were sent toward the ports of Le Havre and Moulineaux in
Normandy and Dunkerque in the north. [@2]

He was arrested in Morocco on 22 February 2025 and handed to the French
authorities on 23 September 2025. [@2] He was charged the next day on ten counts
including organising and directing a group for drug trafficking, importation and
sale, aggravated money laundering and active and passive corruption. [@2] He was
placed in pretrial detention. [@2] In the same case investigators reported 183
kilos of cocaine seized in June 2023 in a container at Le Havre and further
cocaine arrivals of 400 kilos and one tonne by drop-off in September 2023. [@2]
