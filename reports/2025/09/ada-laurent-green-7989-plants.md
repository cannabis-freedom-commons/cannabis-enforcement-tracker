The grow is southeast of Ada in the 18000 block of County Road 3588. [@1]
Oklahoma Bureau of Narcotics agents, working in their Marijuana Enforcement
Teams, served a search warrant at Laurent Green LLC in Ada on Thursday, 4
September 2025. [@2]

The agents seized 7,989 marijuana plants during the raid. [@2] They also seized
54 pounds (about 24.5 kg) of processed marijuana. [@2]

OBN agents arrested one person at the site. [@2] Immigration and Customs
Enforcement agents took two other people into custody. [@1] OBN said more
arrests were possible as the investigation continued. [@2]

The investigation concerned illegal marijuana cultivation at the site, which was
alleged to have obtained its OBN registration by fraud. [@3] The Oklahoma Bureau
of Narcotics is the state's drug enforcement agency. [@1]
