Officers conducted a house search and eradication exercise in Garrand,
Babonneau, on 24 September 2025. [@2] Kenny Akim Eleodore was arrested and later
charged with possession, possession with intent to supply and cultivation. [@2]
Glen Elijah Marius, also of Garrand, was arrested and later charged with
possession and possession with intent to supply. [@2] Both men were taken before
the First District Court on 29 September 2025. [@2] Eleodore was remanded into
custody while Marius was granted EC$35,000 bail. [@2]

Officers eradicated 4,451 cannabis plants at Eleodore's plantation, which police
valued at EC$445,100. [@2] A search of his house recovered 45 pounds of dry
green plant material believed to be cannabis, put at EC$45,000 street value.
[@2] At Marius's premises officers recovered 23 pounds of green plant material
believed to be cannabis, put at EC$23,000. [@2] Officers found and eradicated
another plantation of 4,905 plants during the same exercise. [@2] All plants and
plant material were photographed, packaged and sealed under standard evidential
procedures. [@2]

The Herbal Cooperative condemned the operation, saying two farmers were arrested
for cultivating a few thousand cannabis plants. [@1] Chairman Andre de Caires
described the police action as nothing less than an assault. [@1] De Caires
called the incident a setback for reform while the Cannabis and Hemp Industry
Bill remained untabled. [@1] HTS News 4orce also reported that officers seized
and destroyed cannabis valued at hundreds of thousands of dollars and arrested
two men whom the group described as farmers. [@3] Adults may possess up to 30
grams and grow four plants per household, while commercial cultivation without
authorisation remains illegal. [@1]
