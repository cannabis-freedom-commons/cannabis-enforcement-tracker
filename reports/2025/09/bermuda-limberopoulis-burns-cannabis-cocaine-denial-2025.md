Josephine Limberopoulis, 34, pleaded not guilty in Bermuda's Magistrates' Court
to having cocaine and cannabis with intent to supply, and her husband Salih
Burns, 32, had already denied the charges at an earlier appearance. [@1]

The couple, from Paget, are alleged to have had five grams of cocaine and almost
69 grams of cannabis in Pembroke on 2 August 2024. [@1] Burns also denied a
separate charge of having a folding saw with a blade longer than three inches in
public on the same date. [@1]

Magistrate Maria Sofianos adjourned the case until 15 October and released both
defendants on $8,000 bail. [@1]
