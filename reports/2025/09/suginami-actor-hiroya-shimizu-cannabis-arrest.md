Tokyo Metropolitan Police arrested 26-year-old actor Hiroya Shimizu on September
3, 2025, on suspicion of marijuana possession. [@1] The department suspects
Shimizu of violating the Narcotics Control Law by jointly possessing marijuana.
[@1] Shimizu reportedly admitted the charge. [@1] A woman in her 20s who lives
with Shimizu was also arrested on the same charge. [@1] Officers found cannabis
and rolling paper at the home, and Shimizu was taken into custody two hours
after the search began. [@2]

Shimizu and the woman are suspected of possessing dried marijuana at their home
in Tokyo's Suginami Ward before July 10. [@1] Police began investigating Shimizu
in January after receiving a tip that he was using marijuana. [@1] Officers
searching the residence on the morning of the arrest found a substance
resembling marijuana with rolling papers likely used for smoking on a table.
[@1] Forensic analysis confirmed the substance was about 0.4 gram of dried
marijuana. [@1] Police are investigating where Shimizu obtained the drug and the
route through which it was supplied. [@1]

Shimizu told investigators he first tried marijuana while studying in Los
Angeles at age 20 and used it more frequently after returning to Japan. [@1] He
said he was offered marijuana rolled in papers like a cigarette at a home party
in Los Angeles. [@1] After returning to Japan, he said he began using the drug
again at around age 21 or 22 to experience euphoria and relaxation. [@1] He told
investigators his use increased about two years ago when his television
appearances and income rose. [@1] He added that he had smoked marijuana several
times a month since the beginning of the year. [@1] His agency, Office Saku,
said it learned of the arrest from breaking reports and apologized for the
concern caused. [@1] Broadcaster TBS said it was working to edit Shimizu's
scenes out of his ongoing drama program in light of the news. [@1]
