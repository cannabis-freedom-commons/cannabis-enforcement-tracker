Officers from the Counter-Terrorism and Organised Crime Investigation Branch,
supported by the Firearms and Narcotics Investigation Division, intercepted two
vehicles along the Pamphret main road in St Thomas on Friday, September 26,
2025. [@1] Officers searched the two vehicles and found 1,600 pounds of
compressed ganja packed in 33 knitted bags. [@1] The two occupants, 41-year-old
Rayon Chambers of Whitehouse, Westmoreland, and 30-year-old Tekashi Foster of
Slipe, St Elizabeth, were arrested at the scene. [@1]

Both men were charged with possession of ganja, dealing in ganja, taking steps
preparatory to export ganja, and trafficking ganja. [@1] Their court date was
still being finalised when the charges were announced. [@1] Police described the
seizure as the second major ganja seizure in St Thomas in just over a week, part
of intensified efforts to disrupt the illegal guns-for-drugs trade. [@1]

A second outlet corroborated that more than 1,600 pounds of ganja were seized
during the weekend police operation on the Pamphret main road in St Thomas, with
two men taken into custody. [@2] That account likewise reported that two motor
cars were intercepted and searched, revealing 33 knitted bags containing
compressed ganja. [@2]
