Police seized 12 kilograms of marijuana hidden in a parcel carried on a
public-service vehicle heading for Cartagena. [@1] The seizure took place at a
roadside prevention post on the San Onofre-Cartagena highway, where officers on
a routine inspection noticed a smell coming from the load. [@1] The drug was in
12 rectangular packages and was identified as marijuana of the creepy variety.
[@1] The package had been sent from Cali, in Valle del Cauca. [@2] The operation
was carried out by the Traffic and Transport Section of the National Police in
Bolívar, the department that includes the town of San Onofre. [@2]

Investigators said the marijuana was destined for retail sale points in
Cartagena, where it would be distributed in small amounts. [@2]

Caracol Radio described the parcel as having been sent through a courier
company. [@2]

The marijuana was handed to the Attorney General's Office to continue the
judicial process. [@1]

El Heraldo reported that the packages were covered in black plastic and were
found when officers searched the parcel more closely. [@3]

The commander of the Bolívar police, Colonel Alejandro Reyes Ramírez, said the
operation reflected the force's commitment to dismantling drug distribution
networks. [@2]
