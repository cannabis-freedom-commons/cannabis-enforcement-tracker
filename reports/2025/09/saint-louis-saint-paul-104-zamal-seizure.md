Réunion gendarmes seized 104 zamal plants, presented in the headline as
cannabis, in the communes of Saint-Louis and Saint-Paul. [@1] The Saint-Louis
discovery was made by that commune's autonomous territorial brigade, while the
Saint-Paul plantation was uncovered by that commune's community of brigades.
[@3] The three accounts describe only the plants and dried material taken and
name no suspects, arrests, charges or court outcomes. [@1]

At Saint-Louis, officers discovered 8 zamal plants as well as 1.49 kg of dried
material. [@2] Réunion La 1ère identifies that Saint-Louis dried material as
1.49 kg of dried zamal leaves. [@3] At Saint-Paul, officers uncovered a
plantation of 96 zamal plants. [@2] Added together, the Saint-Louis and
Saint-Paul finds totalled 104 seized zamal plants and 1.49 kg of dried material.
[@2]

The gendarmerie publicised the seizures in a Facebook post recalling the dangers
of drugs. [@2] It said such plantations hide very real risks, from early use
among young people affecting their health, schooling and future, to drug-driving
crashes and trafficking that weakens families and feeds insecurity. [@1]
