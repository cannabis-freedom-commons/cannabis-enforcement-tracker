Police in the Zhetysu Region stopped a channel used to move narcotics and
detained a 51-year-old man in the settlement of Shamalgan in the Almaty Region.
[@2] Officers of the region's anti-drug unit, working with the regional
prosecutor's office under the Qarasora-2025 operation, established that the man
stored and transported drugs from Almaty to the Zhetysu Region. [@2]

A search of his home and of a livestock farm in Merke district, Zhambyl Region,
found plant material with the smell of cannabis. [@2] A forensic examination
confirmed the material was dried marijuana weighing more than 10 kilograms. [@2]
The regional report said the man kept the dried cannabis on his own farm
warehouse. [@1] The police account described the storage place as a livestock
farm, a fazenda, and said the searches covered both his home and that farm. [@3]

The man was placed in custody and investigative measures continued. [@2] Police
estimated the seized amount could have drawn more than ten thousand people into
drug use. [@2] The police department said the offence of acquiring, storing,
transporting for sale, sending or selling narcotics carries seven to twelve
years in prison with confiscation of property. [@2]

A week before, police had identified a whole family selling drugs and took 20
kilograms of marijuana from them. [@1] The detained family members were a
married couple with their son and a 51-year-old resident of Shu, all described
as members of one criminal group, and they too were placed in a pre-trial
detention centre. [@1] The head of the region's anti-drug police said more than
140 drug offences had been identified since the start of the year, with more
than 100 people arrested and every case sent to court. [@1]
