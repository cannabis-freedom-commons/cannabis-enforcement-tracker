Three people from Moruga were in custody for separate offences after police held
a joint anti-crime and road-traffic exercise with regular and municipal police
in the district on 17 September 2025. [@1] They included a 22-year-old
unemployed man from St Mary's Village, held for possession of 2.02 kilogrammes
of marijuana for the purpose of trafficking. [@1] They also included a
54-year-old man from Penal Rock Road, held for drunk driving. [@1] Police also
arrested a man from La Lune Village on inquiry for a report of larceny. [@1]

In an overgrown area in Basse Terre Village, police found six packets of
marijuana weighing 186.1 grammes, with no arrest in connection with that
discovery. [@1]

The exercise, which brought together municipal police from the Princes Town and
Rio Claro stations with regular police from the Southern Division, took place
between 10 am and 3 pm. [@1] Supt Soker, acting Supt Guzman, ASP Morales and
Insp Ragbir coordinated the exercise, while acting ASP Maharaj, Sgt Othello,
acting Sgt Ali and Cpls Hyburn and Pariman led it. [@1] Members of the K9 Unit
also took part in the exercise. [@1] Officers issued 20 fixed penalty notices
for various traffic offences during the operation. [@1]
