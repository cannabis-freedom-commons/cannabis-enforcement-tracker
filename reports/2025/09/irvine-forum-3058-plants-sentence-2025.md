Three Albanian men were jailed at the High Court in Glasgow on Wednesday 3
September 2025 for running an industrial-scale cannabis farm in the former
Irvine Forum shopping centre in North Ayrshire. [@1,2]

Police raiding the site in September 2024 found 3,058 cannabis plants, reported
to be worth between £600,000 and £1.8 million. [@1,2]

Elton Skenderi, 29, Gjovalin Toma, 31, and Eduard Daja, 39, had pleaded guilty
to offences under the Misuse of Drugs Act on 6 August. [@1,2] Daja also admitted
breaching a deportation order imposed in July 2023 after an earlier
cannabis-production conviction at Leeds Crown Court. [@1,2]

Judge Lord Mulholland sentenced Skenderi to five years and three months and
Toma to six years, while Daja received six years plus a consecutive nine months
for the deportation breach. [@1,2]

The judge rejected the claim that the men were victims of trafficking or modern
slavery, telling them they were cannabis farmers pure and simple. [@1,2]
