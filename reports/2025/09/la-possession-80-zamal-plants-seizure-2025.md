Gendarmes of the autonomous territorial brigade of La Possession seized 80 zamal
plants, the Réunion name for cannabis, in the courtyard of a family home in La
Possession. [@1] The discovery came during an intervention by the gendarmes at
the home of Possession residents. [@1]

Le Quotidien de La Réunion reports that the gendarmes seized all of the plants
found at the property. [@2] The gendarmerie publicised the seizure in a Facebook
post, saying it illustrated the daily mobilisation of gendarmes against illicit
cultivation and drug trafficking. [@1]

The authorities did not state the exact date of the seizure. [@1] Neither
account reports an arrest, a charge, a custody decision or a court outcome over
the 80 plants or the family at whose home they were found. [@2]
