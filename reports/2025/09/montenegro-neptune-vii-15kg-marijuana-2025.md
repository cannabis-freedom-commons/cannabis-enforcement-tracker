A vehicle search during INTERPOL's Operation Neptune VII border checks in
Montenegro led to the seizure of 15 kg of marijuana. [@1] Operation Neptune VII
ran from July to September 2025 as a border-security operation coordinated by
INTERPOL with Europol, Frontex and the World Customs Organization, targeting
Mediterranean maritime routes, airports and land borders. [@1] The operation ran
for about two weeks in each of the 20 participating countries. [@1]

INTERPOL said Operation Neptune VII produced a total of 220 kg of marijuana,
alongside silver bullion, USD 130,000 in cash and stolen luxury vehicles. [@1]
Montenegro was one of 20 participating countries, alongside Albania, Bosnia and
Herzegovina, Croatia, Greece, Italy, Spain and others. [@1]

The operation's main stated aim was counter-terrorism border control, and
INTERPOL said it also produced significant seizures tied to other serious
crimes. [@1] INTERPOL said drugs, high-value goods and cash are often used to
fund criminal and terrorist activity. [@1]
