A 23-year-old man from Enmore, East Coast Demerara, appeared at the Georgetown
Magistrate's Court on 10 September 2025 charged with possession of
cannabis-infused gummies for the purpose of trafficking. [@1] He pleaded not
guilty before Acting Chief Magistrate Faith McGusty. [@1]

The charge alleged that on Thursday 23 May 2025, at Qua-mina Street, Georgetown,
he had in his possession 3.75 lbs (about 1.7 kg) of gummies infused with
cannabis for the purpose of trafficking. [@1] His attorney asked the court to
release him on self-bail, arguing that he was not a flight risk. [@1] The
prosecutor objected, noting that the accused had another matter before another
court and asking the court to consider the amount of narcotics involved. [@1]

Magistrate McGusty granted bail of GY$50,000 and adjourned the matter to 24
September. [@1]
