Officers from the Southend Local Policing Team and Southend CID raided an industrial unit in Terminal Close, Shoeburyness, on 15 April 2025 and found more than 700 cannabis plants at various stages of cultivation. [@1,2] The unit on the industrial estate had been set up as a sophisticated large-scale grow, with lighting systems, fans and mobile phones found at the scene. [@1] Endri Metaj, 40, was arrested in the raid. [@1]

## Sentences

Metaj was charged with being concerned in the production of cannabis and pleaded guilty shortly before his trial was due to begin at Basildon Crown Court. [@1] On 26 September he was sentenced to 20 months in prison, ordered to pay a £187 victim surcharge, and the court approved the forfeiture and destruction of the plants and related equipment. [@1] A second man, aged 25, admitted producing cannabis before Southend magistrates the day after the raid and was jailed for 35 weeks. [@1]
