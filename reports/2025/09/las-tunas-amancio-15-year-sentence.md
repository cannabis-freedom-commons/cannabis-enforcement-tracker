The First Criminal Chamber of the Provincial People's Court of Las Tunas sentenced a resident of the Amancio municipality to 15 years in prison for trafficking Cannabis indica, known as marijuana. [@1,2] The case dated back to September 2024, when he was arrested in the city of Las Tunas carrying a considerable amount of national currency and three packages of chopped plant material, which he threw a few metres away at the moment of arrest. [@1,2] Scent traces on the packages matched a sample taken from him, and his urine tested positive for marijuana. [@1,2] The court treated his earlier conviction for a similar offence as aggravating, and added confiscation of the money from the drug sales and a ban on leaving the country while he serves the sentence. [@1,2] Both the defendant and the prosecution may appeal. [@1,2]

The trial was public and exemplary, held as part of the Fourth National Exercise for Crime Prevention and Control under the government's zero-tolerance drug policy. [@1,2]
