Spain's National Police announced on Tuesday 30 September 2025 that it had seized more
than 11 tonnes of hashish in two operations in Málaga and Algeciras, conducted in
coordination with Morocco's General Directorate for National Security (DGSN). [@1,2]

On 5 September officers intercepted a van that had arrived at the port of Tangier and
continued to Málaga, finding 1,825 kilogrammes of hashish concealed inside furniture
and hidden floor compartments. [@1,2] Four suspects were arrested, and officers also
seized cash, four vehicles and eight mobile phones. [@1,2]

On 10 September investigators tracked a refrigerated lorry that had disembarked at
Algeciras the day before to Málaga, where they found 9.3 tonnes of hashish hidden
among pallets of melons. [@1,2] The lorry, its trailer and two escort vehicles used as
lookouts were seized, and six people were arrested. [@1,2]

The investigations, which began after intelligence exchanges between the Moroccan DGSN
and Spanish police, were overseen by the National Court's anti-drugs prosecutor and the
Campo de Gibraltar courts. [@1,2]
