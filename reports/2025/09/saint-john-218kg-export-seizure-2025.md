Border officers at the Port of Saint John examined an outbound marine container
on 16 September 2025 after a referral from the CBSA's Export Team in Halifax,
Nova Scotia. [@1,2] The shipment was falsely declared as empty plastic bottles
and was bound for a business in the United Kingdom. [@1,2] Inside, officers
found 16 large cardboard boxes holding 399 vacuum-sealed packages of suspected
cannabis weighing 218 kilograms. [@1,2] The cannabis and all the evidence were
transferred to the RCMP's Eastern Region Federal Policing unit in New Brunswick
for further investigation. [@1,2]

Although cannabis is legal in Canada, moving it across the border is a serious
criminal offence, punishable by up to five years' imprisonment under the
Customs Act and up to 14 years under the Cannabis Act. [@1] The agencies say
cannabis smuggling supports organized crime. [@1]
