Officers of the Interior Ministry's Main Directorate for the North Caucasus
Federal District and Pyatigorsk police stopped the activity of a 47-year-old
local man suspected of contactless sale of drugs. [@1] The man was detained
while preparing a hiding place for a prohibited substance. [@1] The resident
said he had started working as a drug dealer at the start of 2025 and that an
anonymous curator had promised to pay him for distributing the drugs. [@1]

A search of his person recovered the stash he had just placed. [@1] Police later
found 30 more caches holding drugs prepared for further sale. [@1] At the man's
home officers found more than 1.4 kilograms of synthetic drugs and over 300
grams of hashish, along with packaging materials and scales. [@1] AiF Stavropol
relayed the police account that officers seized more than 1.4 kilograms of
synthetic substances and over 300 grams of hashish from the 47-year-old
Pyatigorsk man. [@2] AiF Stavropol reported that police later found 30 more
caches and seized the main part of the drugs at his home, over 1.4 kg of
synthetics and 300 g of hashish with packaging and scales. [@2]

Investigators of the Zheleznovodsk city police department opened a criminal case
for attempted sale of narcotics under part 3 of article 30 and part 5 of article
228.1 of the Russian Criminal Code. [@1] He was detained under article 91 of the
Russian Code of Criminal Procedure. [@1] He faces up to 20 years in prison,
which is the maximum penalty for the charge rather than a sentence. [@1]
