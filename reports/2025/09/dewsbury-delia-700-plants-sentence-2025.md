Officers raided a house on Bradford Road in Dewsbury in May 2025 after receiving reports that the property was being used to produce drugs. [@1] Inside they found 700 cannabis plants in various stages of growth, along with 24-year-old Kristean Delia. [@1]

## Sentence

Delia admitted producing a Class B drug and was jailed for a year. [@1] Inspector Charlotte Nicholls of West Yorkshire Police said the team had located the large-scale drugs factory by investigating community intelligence about suspicious activity, shutting down another criminal enterprise in local communities. [@1] She said every such closure takes money from those behind drug-related violence and county lines crime, and urged residents to keep reporting suspected criminality. [@1]
