Special units of the Maranhão public security forces destroyed more than 36,000
cannabis plants in the municipality of Centro do Guilherme, about 400 km from
the state capital São Luís, on Wednesday 19 March 2025. [@1] The teams located
and destroyed two large cannabis plantations in the municipality and burned the
seedlings on the spot. [@2] Jovem Pan São Luís reported that the two
plantations' seedlings were incinerated after the cultivation areas were
identified. [@3]

The Maranhão Military Police said the 19 March action was the first day of an
intensified drive against crime in municipalities of the Gurupi and Turi
regions, which have illegal cannabis cultivation. [@1] The destroyed plants were
estimated by the authorities to be enough to produce three tonnes of cannabis.
[@1] On Thursday 20 March the teams were working in the neighbouring
municipality of Centro Novo, a second stage of the same drive. [@2] The Maranhão
public security secretariat said further incursions would follow in the coming
days to identify and break up criminal schemes. [@2]

Lieutenant-colonel Onildo, commander of Special Missions of the Maranhão
Military Police, said the eradication and incineration of the plants stopped the
drugs reaching the streets and struck a blow against drug trafficking. [@1]
Besides the Air Tactical Centre (CTA), a military police aviation unit, the
action involved the Maranhão Military Police's special operations battalion
(Bope), its riot battalion (BPChoque), the Rotam mobile tactical patrol unit and
the canine unit. [@1] The deployment falls under Operation Renoe (Rede Nacional
de Operações Ostensivas Especializadas), an initiative of the National Public
Security Secretariat (Senasp) of the Ministry of Justice and Public Security.
[@1]
