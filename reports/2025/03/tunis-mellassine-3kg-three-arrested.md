National Guard officers dismantled a drug-trafficking network and arrested three
of its members in the Mellassine quarter of Tunis. [@1] The arrests took place
in Mellassine, a quarter of Tunis. [@1] The operation was conducted by the
Sub-Directorate for the Fight against Crime in Ben Arous. [@1] The
Directorate-General of the National Guard was cited as the authority behind the
account of the operation and the seizure. [@1]

Officers seized 3 kg of cannabis, divided into 30 slabs, during the same
Mellassine operation. [@1] The National Guard directorate announced that seizure
on Friday 28 March 2025. [@1] Officers also seized two vehicles that the
traffickers had used for their movements. [@1] They additionally seized sums of
money that were described as proceeds generated by that same trafficking
activity. [@1]

The three men held after the operation were described as dealers. [@1] The
public prosecution ordered that all three dealers be placed in detention. [@1]
The prosecution also ordered that the investigation into the trafficking network
continue. [@1]
