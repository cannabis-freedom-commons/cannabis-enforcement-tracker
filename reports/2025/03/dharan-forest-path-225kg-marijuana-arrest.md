Acting on a tip-off about illegal drug smuggling, a police team from the Area
Police Office Dharan, the Ward Police Office and the temporary police post in
Jorsakhuwa was deployed to arrest the smugglers. [@1] On Thursday morning the
team intercepted porters carrying marijuana through an inner forest footpath,
smuggling it from Bishnupaduka to Railway, Dharan-17, via Chimphule Khola in
Dharan-20. [@1] When police tried to stop and check them, the porters abandoned
their loads and fled into the jungle. [@1]

During the pursuit, police fired three warning shots into the air and
apprehended 43-year-old Hari Kumar Rai from Dhankuta. [@1] Officers confiscated
nine plastic bags holding a total of 230 kg of marijuana, of which 225 kg was
without plastic packaging, Police Inspector Diwas Kunwar of the Area Police
Office Dharan said. [@1]

The arrestee is in police custody for further investigation while officers are
still trying to locate the suspects who fled. [@1]
