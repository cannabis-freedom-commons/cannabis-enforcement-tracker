Officers conducted a drive-through exercise in the Port-of-Spain and North
Eastern Division. [@1] At approximately 6:00 PM, the officers proceeded to the
Morris Marshall Recreational Grounds, where they searched the pavilion. [@1]

of cannabis was discovered and seized. [@1]

article's 12 March 2025 publication. [@1] The packets were weighed and totalled
95 grams. [@1] The details were attributed to a release by the Trinidad and
Tobago Police Service. [@1] PC Thompson is continuing the inquiries, and the
