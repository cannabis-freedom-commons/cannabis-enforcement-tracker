Moroccan police seized 5.7 tonnes of cannabis resin near the Atlantic coastal
town of Moulay Bousselham on Thursday evening, 6 March 2025. [@1,2] The
operation, reported by the official MAP news agency via Xinhua, was carried
out in coordination with the General Directorate of Territorial Surveillance
(DGST), the domestic intelligence service. [@1,2]

One suspect was caught red-handed attempting to traffic the resin in a
foreign-registered truck, and a woman was arrested later in a separate
operation in Kenitra. [@1] Both were held over alleged links to a criminal
network active in international drug and psychotropic substance smuggling, and
a judicial investigation was opened to identify accomplices and map the
network. [@1,2]
