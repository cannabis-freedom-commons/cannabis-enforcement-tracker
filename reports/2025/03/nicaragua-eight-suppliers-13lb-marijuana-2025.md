National Police announced its weekly results covering 10 to 16 March 2025. [@1]
Five of the detainees were held as alleged drug suppliers in districts of
Managua. [@1] Police detained Juan Francisco Centeno Montenegro and Brandón Jose
Palacios Reyes for alleged drug trafficking in Jinotega. [@1] Police detained
Jeyson Mendoza William for alleged drug trafficking in Puerto Cabezas, also
called Bilwi. [@1] The marijuana cases were part of a wider roundup in which
police said 20 people were captured for dangerous crimes across the country.
[@1]

Police said the five Managua detainees were found with 7 pounds and 207 grams of
marijuana, together with 273 grams of cocaine, 41 grams of crack, a car, a
motorcycle, a mobile phone and cash. [@1] In Jinotega, police seized 5 pounds of
marijuana and two motorcycles from the two detainees. [@1] In Puerto Cabezas,
police seized 1 pound of marijuana from Mendoza William. [@1]

Subcomisionada Karen Obando, co-head of the National Police public relations
division, gave the figures. [@1] In the same weekly roundup in Rivas, two men
were held with 3 kilos and 180.6 grams of cocaine and a motorcycle, amounts left
out of the cannabis total here. [@1]
