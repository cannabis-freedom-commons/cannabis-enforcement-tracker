Two Nicaraguans and a Jamaican were arrested after being found with over 1,400
pounds of ganja and a fishing vessel off the coast of Westmoreland on Monday.
[@1] The three men aboard the vessel were arrested during the operation. [@1]
Their identities have not been revealed. [@1] The three suspects were detained
on reasonable suspicion while FNID investigators pursued charges under the
Dangerous Drugs Act and the Criminal Justice (Suppression of Criminal
Organizations) Act. [@1]

A pre-dawn operation by the Firearms and Narcotics Investigation Division
(FNID), supported by the Jamaica Defence Force Coast Guard, intercepted the
fishing vessel off Westmoreland and searched it. [@1] During the search officers
found 1,480 pounds (672.6 kilograms) of the drug. [@1]

FNID head Senior Superintendent Samuel Blake said the interdiction underscored
the constabulary's growing intelligence capabilities for pursuing organised
firearms and narcotics traffickers and the value of collaboration. [@1] Blake
said the seizure potentially prevented the influx of prohibited weapons into
Jamaica, describing the syndicate's method as exchanging narcotics for firearms,
specifically AK-47 rifles. [@1]
