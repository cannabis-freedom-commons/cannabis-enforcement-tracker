Peru's Supreme Court upheld the ten-year prison sentence, 240 days' fine and
permanent firearms ban given to Johan Alejandro Uchatuma Albites for illegally
possessing explosives and a firearm with ammunition, together with drug
trafficking. [@1] The Supreme Court's permanent criminal chamber ruled on 24
March 2025 on the appeal of the 8 November 2023 judgment of the First Permanent
Criminal Appeals Chamber of Ate, which had confirmed the conviction at first
instance. [@1]

Police of the anti-extortion unit Divise and the Divreing stopped him in
Huaycán, in the district of Ate, on 7 March 2022 during the "Anti-extortions
2022" operations, when he was in the passenger seat of a Toyota Yaris with plate
AUL-527. [@1] The car search found, under the passenger seat where he sat, 36
grams of marijuana together with 49 grams of coca paste and one gram of cocaine
hydrochloride. [@1] Officers also found three homemade fuse bombs and a revolver
with a filed-off serial number and three .38 cartridges under that seat. [@1]

His defence argued that the drugs and weapons had been planted because no
prosecutor or defence lawyer was present at the detention, search and drug
seizure. [@1] The court held that Peruvian law does not require a prosecutor or
defence lawyer at such preliminary police steps, which are pre-constituted acts
carried out in urgent circumstances and in flagrante delicto, and found no
support for the claim that police planted the material. [@1] The court did not
enter the sufficiency of the evidence itself, because that review belongs to the
appellate court that had ratified the conviction. [@1]

The charges at trial were possession of explosives, possession of a firearm and
ammunition, and drug trafficking under the former micro-commercialisation drug
offence. [@1] The first-instance court also ordered him to pay 6,000 soles in
civil damages. [@1]
