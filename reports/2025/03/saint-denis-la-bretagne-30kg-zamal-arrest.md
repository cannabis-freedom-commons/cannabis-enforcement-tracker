The anti-drug and underground-economy brigade learned on Monday that a resident
of La Bretagne in Saint-Denis was growing cannabis herb. [@1] Officers
identified and arrested the man and placed him in police custody. [@1]

A search of his home found several cannabis plants totalling more than 30 kg.
[@1] Officers also found equipment for reselling his crop during the same home
search. [@1]

The man will have to answer for his actions before the courts. [@1]
