Gendarmes carried out an anti-drug operation on the island of Moorea on 20 March
2025, led by the Windward Islands Company of the gendarmerie. [@1] A Dauphin
helicopter of the 34F flotilla flew over the island and identified a suspect
zone, with two gendarmes of the Moorea brigade aboard and a team of three
gendarmes on the ground. [@1]

The operation seized 375 cannabis plants, representing eight kilograms of herb.
[@1] One person was identified and implicated according to the gendarmerie's
Facebook page. [@1]

The operation followed the seizure of 13 pots of cannabis at Tahiti's ferry
terminal from two passengers arriving from Moorea. [@1]
