Police arrested a 34-year-old man from Enterprise during intelligence-led
operations in the Central Division. [@1] Officers searched a residence on
Enterprise Street in Chaguanas between 5:30 PM and 8:30 PM and found four
plastic packets of cannabis weighing about 1.8 kilograms. [@1] The man was taken
into custody at the Chaguanas Police Station. [@1]

Officers found a black plastic bag hidden under a parked forty-foot container
trailer west of the Uriah Butler Highway near the Caroni Bird Sanctuary. [@1]
The bag held ten brown plastic packets of cannabis weighing about 5.4 kilograms,
with a police-estimated street value of $100,000 TTD. [@1] Police put the street
value of the Enterprise Street cannabis at $35,000 TTD. [@1]

Senior Central Division officers supervised the operations with help from the
TTPS K-9 Unit and the Trinidad and Tobago Defence Force Special Operations
Force. [@1] Investigations into the Caroni seizure were ongoing under Cpl.
Carter. [@1] Investigations into the Enterprise Street seizure were continuing
under PC Ramnath of the Special Operations Unit. [@1]
