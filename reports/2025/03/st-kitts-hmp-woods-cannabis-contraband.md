Meldesha Woods of Stapleton Village, St Kitts, was charged with conveying
contraband into His Majesty's Prison in Basseterre over an offence committed
there on 7 March 2025. [@4] She was officially charged the following day at the
Basseterre Police Station. [@3] Officers stopped her as she tried to carry
vegetable material into the prison, and further examinations confirmed the
material was cannabis and tobacco. [@2] She was charged at the Basseterre Police
Station on 8 March 2025. [@1]

Police named Woods as the person caught attempting to smuggle contraband into
the prison. [@3] SKNVibes reported that in January another person, Steadroy
Jules of St Paul, had been charged with attempting to smuggle cannabis, tobacco
and rolling papers into the same prison. [@3] It also reported that in November
2024 a prison officer had been charged with conveying cannabis into the prison.
[@3] The Royal St. Christopher and Nevis Police Force and prison officers at the
Basseterre facility had been trying to stop contraband deliveries. [@3]

NevisPages reported Woods, a resident of Stapleton Village, as charged with the
same offence. [@5]
