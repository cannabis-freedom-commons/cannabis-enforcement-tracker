Sakhalin Oblast drug-control officers said they broke up a local group selling
marijuana hand to hand and detained five people: two dealers, two consumers and
a supplier. [@1]

Police said a Yuzhno-Sakhalinsk resident born in 1959 conspired with an
acquaintance born in 1968 to pass him large batches at one price for resale at a
higher price. [@1] In February, the retail dealer sold several packets holding
more than 120 grams of marijuana to a 58-year-old Yuzhno-Sakhalinsk resident,
who was detained after the deal. [@1] The same day, officers detained a local
man born in 1978 who had bought seven cannabis packages weighing more than 270
grams, with the drug found at his home. [@1]

Searches at one dealer's home and at the other's workshop found more than 12.5
kg of marijuana. [@1] Police then detained the supplier, a resident of
Krasnopolye village in Uglegorsky district born in 1960. [@1] Officers found and
seized more than 4.7 kg of marijuana at his property. [@1] The supplier told
police he had grown cannabis in a forest, harvested and dried the crop and
stored it for distribution. [@1]

The regional investigative unit opened criminal cases against the supplier,
dealers and consumers under part 4 of article 228.1 and part 2 of article 228 of
Russia's Criminal Code. [@1] Sakhalin police seized more than 17 kg of marijuana
in total, Aif-Sakhalin reported. [@2]
