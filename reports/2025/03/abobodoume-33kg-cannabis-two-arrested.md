Ivory Coast's national police arrested two people at Abobodoumé in the commune
of Yopougon. [@1] The two suspects were identified as K.A., aged 36, and B.M.,
aged 28. [@1] The Tripostal branch of the anti-narcotics police acted after
being alerted to possible drug transactions in the neighbourhood. [@1] A team
went to the scene to verify the information and intervene. [@1]

Officers seized 33 kg of cannabis, which police estimated to be worth 6,945,000
FCFA on the market. [@1] Officers also confiscated a black Apsonic motorcycle
during the intervention. [@1] The account is attributed to a note from the
Directorate General of the National Police consulted on 6 March 2025. [@1]

Police said the operation formed part of Operation Épervier 10. [@1] The DPSD is
the Direction de la Police des Stupéfiants et des Drogues, Ivory Coast's
anti-narcotics police. [@1]
