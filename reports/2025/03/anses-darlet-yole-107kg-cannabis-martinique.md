Customs officers, with the navy and national police, caught a drug landing in
Anses d'Arlet in southern Martinique on Friday 28 March 2025. [@1] On land
customs officers detained a man who had abandoned his car with a boot full of
suspect packages. [@1] The packages weighed 107.24 kg of cannabis herb. [@1]

In a night operation, RAID police officers on a new interceptor vessel boarded
the yole — a Martinican open sailing boat — suspected of having unloaded the
cargo. [@1] Officers arrested two more men, from Saint Lucia and Saint Vincent.
[@1] All three suspects were held in custody at the offices of Ofast, France's
anti-narcotics office. [@1]

After 96 hours of questioning, the man detained on land was tried that week and
sentenced to one year in prison and a 160,000 euro fine. [@1] The two men picked
up on the yole were freed but served with an obligation to leave French
territory. [@1]
