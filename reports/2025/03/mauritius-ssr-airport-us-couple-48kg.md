Customs officers of the revenue authority's anti-narcotics section seized about
48 kg of cannabis, valued at Rs 58,020,000, from a couple arriving at Sir
Seewoosagur Ramgoolam International Airport on 1 March 2025. [@1] The man, a
photographer and film director from California, and the woman, a photographer
and salesgirl also from California, had arrived on Emirates flight EK703 from
New York via Dubai with three pieces of luggage between them. [@1]

Officers picked the couple for inspection in the Green Channel after profiling,
and scans showed abnormalities in their luggage. [@1] Each suitcase held vacuum
packed sachets sealed inside clothes, together 90 packets of compressed dried
leaf matter suspected to be cannabis; a field test came back positive and
sniffer dogs Oscar and Scarlet reacted to the exhibits. [@1] The pair said
someone had given them the three suitcases at the airport in New York to bring
to Mauritius. [@1] Both were arrested by Anti-Drug and Smuggling Unit officers,
who ran a controlled delivery operation. [@1]

The man's mother names him as Kristopher Gorr, a cinematographer hired to film
a wedding in Mauritius, and says a man delivered two suitcases said to hold
camera equipment minutes before his airport ride, leaving no time to check
them. [@2] She says Gorr and the other photographer, Natalie, travelled
together from Los Angeles through Arizona, New York and Dubai, and that he was
stunned when officers cut open a package in Natalie's bag and pulled out
cannabis. [@2] She says he has been held in Beau Bassin prison without trial
and faces up to 45 years for drug trafficking. [@2]
