National Police officers arrested a man during a traffic stop after finding more
than six kilograms of marijuana hidden in his luggage. [@1] Officers from the La
Espriella Police Substation made the capture during surveillance and control
activities. [@1] The detained man was known by the alias Fumador. [@1]

He was caught carrying a suitcase containing seven plastic bags with more than
six kilos of marijuana. [@1] Officers found the drugs on a public bus on the
Tumaco-Pasto highway. [@1]

Police called on the community to report any suspicious activity that could
affect public safety. [@1]
