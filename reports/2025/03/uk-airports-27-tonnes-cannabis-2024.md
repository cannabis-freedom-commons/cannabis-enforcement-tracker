Border Force and the National Crime Agency (NCA) seized 26,924 kg of cannabis
from around 750 smugglers flying into UK airports in 2024. [@1,2] That was
nearly five times the 5,609 kg seized in 2023, when 134 suspects were arrested,
and far above the two tonnes seized in 2022. [@1]

Heathrow accounted for the largest share, with 430 arrests and 14,617 kg
seized, followed by Manchester with about 135 arrests and 4,815 kg, and
Birmingham with 40 arrests and 1,410 kg. [@1] Of the couriers, 460 had arrived
from Thailand, 108 from Canada and 63 from the United States, with the rest
from other countries; by nationality about 290 were UK citizens, alongside 162
Malaysians, 86 Canadians and 52 Americans. [@1,2]

The NCA judges the trend is fuelled by organised crime groups with access to
cannabis grown legally overseas, recruiting couriers to carry it to Britain
where it fetches higher profits. [@1] The agency had warned travellers from
Thailand, Canada and the United States in August 2024 that they faced jail
sentences if caught. [@1,2] NCA director of threat leadership Alex Murray said
Border Force had been excellent at detecting the couriers and that targeting
them was crucial to breaking the supply chain. [@1]
