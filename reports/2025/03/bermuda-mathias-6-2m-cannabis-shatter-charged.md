David Mathias, 67, and Danielle Mathias, 36, were charged in Bermuda's
Magistrates' Court with importing cannabis and THC or shatter. [@1] Neither
entered a plea because the case must be heard in the Supreme Court. [@1]
Carrington Mahoney, for the Crown, said the substances were allegedly imported
on 10 May 2020. [@1] The Crown put the street value of the substances at an
estimated $6.2 million. [@1] Mr Mathias is from Warwick and Ms Mathias from
Devonshire. [@1]

Senior magistrate Maxanne Anderson released the pair on $50,000 bail with a
surety until the May arraignment session. [@1] She also ordered them to
surrender all travel documents and to report to Hamilton Police Station twice a
week. [@1] TNN Bermuda identified the two as father and daughter appearing in
court on 28 March 2025. [@2] TNN reported that the Crown put the shatter at an
estimated street value of $6 million and the cannabis at nearly $41,000. [@2]
TNN said the pair must report to Hamilton Police Station every Monday and
Thursday between 8am and 6pm. [@2] TNN gave the younger defendant's age as 37
and said the case was adjourned to the arraignment session on 1 May. [@2]
