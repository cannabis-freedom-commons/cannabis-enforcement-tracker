From 10 to 16 March 2025, the Police Regional Office Cordillera (PRO-CAR) ran
42 anti-drug operations across the region, according to its Regional Operations
Division as carried by Baguio's Zigzag Weekly. [@1]

Officers destroyed 129,720 fully grown marijuana plants and 150 seedlings, and
seized 30,016.13 grams of dried marijuana leaves and fruiting tops plus 5,000
grams of dried stalks, with an overall standard drug price of P30,345,953.20.
[@1] Small amounts of suspected shabu were also seized in Baguio and Apayao.
[@1]

The operations led to 11 arrests: three high-value individuals and eight
street-level suspects. [@1] The largest cannabis hauls were in Benguet, worth
P19,450,000, and in Kalinga, worth P9,716,000 with two street-level suspects
held, followed by smaller seizures in Mountain Province, Baguio, Ifugao and
Apayao. [@1] Regional director Brig. Gen. David K. Peredo Jr. commended the
operatives and the communities whose cooperation aided the campaign. [@1]
