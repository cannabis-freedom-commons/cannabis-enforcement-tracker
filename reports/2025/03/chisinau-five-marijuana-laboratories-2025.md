Moldovan police detained three residents of Chișinău and Criuleni district, aged
36, 40 and 46, who had organised five underground drug-production laboratories.
[@1] The three men were detained for 72 hours. [@1] Four of the laboratories
were set up in the suspects' own homes and one in a rented warehouse. [@1] The
special operation uncovered five marijuana-growing laboratories fitted with
modern equipment. [@1] The operation was carried out with the support of the
Fulger special forces unit. [@1]

In 10 searches officers seized 169 marijuana plants still in the growth stage
and about 1.5 kg of dried drug product. [@1] The searches also produced growing
and processing equipment, cash from drug sales, bank cards issued by Russian
banks, a car used in the criminal activity, a GSM signal jammer and a modified
firearm. [@1] Nokta put the value of the drugs and raw material seized at more
than 7 million lei. [@1] A second account said 35 kg of marijuana, estimated at
over 7 million lei on the black market, was taken from the five clandestine
laboratories in Chișinău and Criuleni. [@2]

IPN described the three suspects as aged between 36 and 46, suspected of running
the illegal activity with state-of-the-art equipment. [@3] IPN reported that
officers also seized cultivation and processing equipment, money from drug
sales, bank cards issued in Russia, a GSM signal jammer and a modified homemade
weapon. [@3] The three suspects were held for 72 hours and faced 7 to 15 years
in prison if charged and found guilty of drug trafficking. [@3] Police said the
investigation was continuing and officers were checking the detainees' possible
links to other criminal groups. [@1]
