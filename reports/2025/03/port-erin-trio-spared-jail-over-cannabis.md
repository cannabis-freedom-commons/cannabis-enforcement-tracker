Some 465g of cannabis was found in various wraps and containers in Finley
Corrin's home in Port Erin along with two cannabis plants, during a police raid
on 4 August 2023. [@1] Officers had been following up reports of a smell of
cannabis coming from the house. [@1] A total of 117 of the cannabis wraps that
had been found, had been stashed in a backpack separated into two bin bags,
Douglas Courthouse heard. [@1]

Corrin, Maxwell and Baker, who were all at the house at the time of the search,
were arrested and £2,000 of criminal cash was found on Baker. [@1] All three men
pleaded guilty to possession of cannabis with intent to supply, with Corrin also
pleading guilty to the cultivation of cannabis and Baker admitting possession of
criminal property. [@1]

Delays in the court process had given all three young men a chance to turn their
lives around before they were sentenced on Thursday at the Court of General Gaol
Delivery. [@2] Deemster Graeme Cook told the trio they had all been immature,
young fools. [@1] "There is too much cannabis on this island and you played a
part in that, albeit a small part," he said. [@1] Corrin has been handed a
14-month sentence suspended for two years, Baker an 18-month sentence suspended
for two years and Maxwell a 16-month sentence suspended for two years. [@1]
