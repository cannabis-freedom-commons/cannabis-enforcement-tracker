Police arrested three men for alleged possession of cannabis and mandrax tablets
during an operation near Uis. [@1] Officers intercepted the suspects at around
01h20 about 26km before Uis while they were travelling in a white Isuzu 260.
[@1] The three suspects are Namibian men. [@2]

The suspects were found with 20 parcels of cannabis and two plastic bags
containing 2,037 mandrax tablets. [@1] Police said the value of the drugs was
yet to be determined. [@1]

Police spokesperson Kauna Shikwambi said the main suspect had been under
surveillance while travelling on public transport from Windhoek via Omaruru to
collect his stock. [@1] She said the main suspect was then picked up by the
other two suspects from Omaruru toward his destination. [@1] Police
investigations into the matter were continuing. [@1]
