Prosecutor Rocío Valdez of the anti-drug unit in Itapúa led the raid on a rural
property in the Compañía Tarumá area of Alto Verá district, Itapúa, where
marijuana plots were found. [@1] Police from the Investigations Department and
the Special Operations Group (GEO) of the National Police supported the
operation. [@1] The work began at about 7:00 on Tuesday 11 March 2025, after the
plots were found in a wooded part of the property. [@1]

The prosecutor ordered the plantation destroyed. [@1] Some three hectares of
marijuana were destroyed, the plants ready for harvest. [@1] The plants were cut
up in full and carried an estimated 6,000 kilograms, according to the
authorities. [@1] The operation participants put the market loss at about 900
million guaraníes. [@1] Itapúa en Noticias reported the raid happened on the
morning of Tuesday 11 March 2025. [@2]

No arrests were reported. [@2]
