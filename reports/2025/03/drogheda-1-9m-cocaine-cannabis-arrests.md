Gardaí, the Irish police, intercepted a vehicle in Drogheda, County Louth, at
about 09:00 local time on Wednesday 12 March 2025. [@1] Officers then searched a
business premises in Drogheda and two residential properties in Drogheda and
north Dublin. [@1] Two men, aged in their 30s and 40s, were arrested on
suspicion of facilitating and enhancing drug trafficking for a criminal
organisation. [@1] The two men were detained at a Louth Garda station. [@1]

Police said the operation seized cocaine and cannabis with a combined estimated
value of €1.9m, about £1.6m. [@1] The simultaneous search operation resulted
from ongoing investigations and collaboration between the Garda National Drugs
and Organised Crime Bureau and the Police Service of Northern Ireland. [@1]

After the operation, Detective Chief Superintendent Seamus Boland said gardaí
are committed to targeting organisations that attempt to exploit the land border
for serious criminal activity. [@1] He added that drug traffickers and those who
facilitate the trade remain priority targets because of the destruction,
violence and intimidation they cause in communities. [@1]
