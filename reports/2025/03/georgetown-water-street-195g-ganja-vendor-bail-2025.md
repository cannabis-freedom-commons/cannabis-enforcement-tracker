Jermaine Saran, a 23-year-old vendor, was granted GY$200,000 bail at the
Georgetown Magistrates' Court on a possession of narcotics charge. [@1] He was
arrested on 22 March 2025 at Water Street, Georgetown, for possessing 195.5
grams of marijuana for the purpose of trafficking. [@1] Saran pleaded not guilty
after Acting Chief Magistrate Faith McGusty read the charge to him. [@1]

His lawyer, Dominic Bess, told the court that Saran was at his stall at Stabroek
Market, market and shopping area in central Georgetown, when police searched a
stall about 20 yards away and found the marijuana. [@1] Bess said his client had
been held on suspicion of having 140 grams but was now charged over 195.5 grams,
and that there was no confirmation the material found was marijuana. [@1] The
prosecution said Saran had been under observation for selling an unknown
substance to numerous people from a push cart, and that a search of the cart in
his stall found leaves, seeds and stems suspected to be marijuana. [@1]

Magistrate McGusty granted him bail and set his return to court for 23 April
2025. [@1] Kaieteur News, the Georgetown daily that carried the account on 27
March, was credited as the source of the Surinamese republication. [@2]
