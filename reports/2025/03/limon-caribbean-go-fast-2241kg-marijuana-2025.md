On 9 March 2025 a U.S. Coast Guard vessel spotted a suspicious 30-foot go-fast boat with no
registration or flag crossing Costa Rica's Caribbean coast and passed its coordinates to the Costa
Rican Coast Guard, which intercepted the craft about 143 nautical miles off Limon in Limon
Province. [@1]

The boat's three Nicaraguan crew members were arrested on suspicion of drug trafficking. [@1]
Towed to the Limon Coast Guard station, the craft was found to hold about 2,241 packages of
marijuana weighing roughly one kilogram each, or more than two tonnes. [@1] The boat, the drugs,
fuel and navigation equipment were confiscated, and the suspects were transferred to the Limon
Public Ministry to await a judge's decision on pre-trial detention for international drug
trafficking. [@1]

The operation was carried out under the Joint Patrol Treaty between Costa Rica and the United
States. [@1] Costa Rica seized nearly 32 tonnes of cocaine and about 15 tonnes of marijuana in
2024, according to the ministry. [@1]
