Four men were convicted and one woman was released in Durazno after police ran
Operation Relámpago, which seized marijuana and other drugs, a car, weapons,
phones and about a million pesos in cash. [@1]

The operation was run by the Durazno department's anti-drug brigade, which had
intelligence that two people were coming into the department to supply local
distributors. [@1] On Saturday 22 March 2025 police intercepted the car carrying
the two men and found two bricks of pasta base, a cocaine paste, weighing 2.160
kg. [@1] Four raids followed, two of them finding nothing; the other two led to
three arrests and 51 bricks of marijuana weighing 25 kg. [@1] In the raids
police also seized 1,000,000 pesos, a .32 revolver, two air rifles, two two-way
radios and eight mobile phones. [@1]

The detained 33-year-old was sentenced to four years in prison as the
perpetrator of a continuing offence of dealing in prohibited drugs in an
aggravated form. [@1] The 43-year-old was sentenced to three years and one month
for assisting a person active in drug dealing. [@1] The 42-year-old and the
31-year-old were each sentenced to four years for aggravated transport of
prohibited drugs. [@1] A court ordered the forfeiture of the cash and seized
items to the state, including 749,192 pesos. [@3] Durazno Digital reported that
the four convictions were final after the court hearing, and that police also
seized the car used by the group. [@2]

The money and property seized were ordered confiscated to the state after the
judgement. [@2] The Durazno police chief, Gabriel Lima, confirmed to the local
outlet that the operation broke up a drug distribution network. [@2] Durazno's
police command presented the results of the operation at a press conference the
following Monday. [@4]

The seized property included the car the group travelled in, which the force
kept. [@3]
