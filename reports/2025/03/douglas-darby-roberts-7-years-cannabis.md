Darby Roberts, 25, was jailed for seven years at Douglas Courthouse for drug
supply. [@1] He had pleaded guilty to possessing cocaine, MDMA and cannabis with
intent to supply. [@1] He was jailed for seven years for possessing cocaine with
intent to supply, five years for the MDMA count and four years for the cannabis
count, all to run concurrently. [@3]

Darby Roberts was stopped by police on Main Road in Greeba at about 4.40pm on 18
October because of the speed he was driving. [@2] Police had stopped Roberts'
Ford Transit van at Greeba on 18 October over concerns of excessive speed. [@3]
A search of his car found 180 MDMA tablets and cocaine that was later valued at
£3,150. [@1] Police then found £114,292 of cannabis in his property, stashed in
sealed bags in a hatch, along with £1,292 of cash hidden in rubber gloves and an
envelope and electronic weighing scales. [@1] In total officers found 31.5 grams
of cocaine valued at £3,150, 5.7 kilos of cannabis valued at £114,294 and 180
MDMA tablets valued at between £1,800 and £2,700. [@3]

The prosecutor told the court that police had identified a number of messages
from people seeking drugs. [@1] Interviewed the following day, Roberts said the
cocaine was for personal use but gave no comment on the cannabis and cash. [@1]
He insisted his partner had nothing to do with the drugs found. [@3]

The court heard Roberts had no previous convictions and that references
described him as a hard-working man who had given back to the community through
charity work. [@1] Roberts, of Ballawattleworth Estate, had no previous
convictions and showed genuine remorse. [@3] Deemster Graeme Cook said he
struggled to understand why Roberts became involved in this sort of activity and
that Roberts was someone who should have known better. [@1]
