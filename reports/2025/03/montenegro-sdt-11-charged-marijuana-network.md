Montenegro's Special State Prosecutor's Office (SDT) filed an indictment against
11 people for drug trafficking, weapons offences and ordering a murder. [@1]
Prosecutors allege that Milan Janković of Bar and Toni Junčaj of Tuzi formed the
group, which also included Albanian citizen Nikolino Pepaj alongside Montenegrin
defendants. [@1] In July 2023, the Special Police Department (SPO) arrested
Janković, Junčaj, Milan Milović, Alen Đolević, Filip Krstonijević and Spasoje
Zlatičanin on suspicion of creating a criminal organization and smuggling drugs
from Albania through Montenegro toward Bosnia and Herzegovina, while Slađan
Đinović was also arrested. [@3]

According to the indictment, Milan Janković and Toni Junčaj organized the
criminal group in 2021 and 2022 across Montenegro, Albania, Serbia, Bosnia and
Herzegovina, Croatia, Austria and the Netherlands. [@1] The SDT alleges that the
group recruited members and organized marijuana transfers from Montenegro to
neighbouring countries and EU member states using encrypted messaging phones.
[@1] Prosecutors accuse the organizers of recruiting couriers to transport
marijuana or cocaine, arranging houses to store and repackage drugs, and
supplying money and encrypted phones for purchases. [@2] Milović is additionally
accused of buying 12 pistols of various brands and calibres plus a Heckler &
Koch USP Compact pistol in Bar in May 2021 without weapons documents and of
inciting an unattempted murder. [@1]

In March 2025, a panel of the Higher Court in Podgorica confirmed the SDT
indictment against the 11 defendants using data from the ANOM application. [@4]
Confirmation followed prosecution appeals after the Higher Court initially
excluded USB evidence from the encrypted ANOM app before the Appellate Court
overturned that ruling. [@4] Prosecutors said it was the first confirmed
indictment in Montenegro in which electronic evidence from the formerly
encrypted ANOM application was accepted as legally valid. [@3]
