Nicholas Kirton is the captain of Canada's national cricket team. [@1] Police
detained Kirton on landing in Barbados on Sunday. [@1] He was taken into custody
after arriving at Grantley Adams International Airport. [@2] He was charged with
possession, trafficking, importation and intent to supply cannabis. [@1] Kirton
pleaded not guilty to the drug-related charges in a Barbados court. [@1] He
appeared in court on Friday morning and was granted bail. [@1] The court ordered
him to return to Barbados for a hearing on 2 June. [@1]

The investigation related to 20 pounds (about 9.07 kg) of cannabis. [@2] The
cannabis was estimated to be worth around BDS$71,000. [@3]

Cricket Canada said it was actively monitoring the situation and closely
following developments. [@1] Canada's national men's team was preparing for the
North America Cup starting on 18 April in the Cayman Islands. [@1]
