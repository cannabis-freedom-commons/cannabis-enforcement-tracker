On 4 March 2025, two suitcases holding 79 packages of marijuana were left
unclaimed in the arrivals area of Punta Cana International Airport. [@3] The
suitcases had arrived on a flight from Toronto. [@2] One suitcase held 45
packages and the other 34, wrapped in clear and black plastic. [@4] Agents of
the DNCD and the airport security corps CESAC, coordinated by the Public
Ministry, seized the packages at the airport in La Altagracia province. [@4]

The DNCD said Zachery Crowford and Casandra Diprieto were arrested on judicial
orders as they tried to leave through the same airport and were investigated
over those suitcases. [@3] A later account identified the couple as Cassandra
DiPietro and Zach Crawford and said the tag on Cassandra's bag had been switched
onto a bag full of marijuana. [@1] That account said they were led away to face
charges of importing 79 packages of Canadian marijuana. [@1]

The couple were stuck in the Dominican Republic for 100 days. [@1] They remained
for months even after the charges were dropped, after authorities determined the
marijuana-filled suitcases did not belong to them. [@1]
