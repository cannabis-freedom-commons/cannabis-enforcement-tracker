A 27-year-old German man was arrested on 31 March 2025 after police in Gozo raided a residence on Triq ix-Xjuf ir-Riħ in Nadur, MaltaToday and TVM reported. [@1,2] Officers had kept the address under surveillance, obtained a warrant and entered the property in a joint operation by several police sections, the outlets said. [@1,2]

Inside, police found a room fitted with apparatus used to grow cannabis, about 20 plants of various sizes, cannabis seeds and other cultivation items, MaltaToday reported. [@1] TVM gave the same plant count and said the amount exceeded what Maltese law allows for personal cultivation. [@2]

Duty magistrate Simone Grech was informed and appointed an inquiry, both outlets said. [@1,2] The man remained in police custody and was expected to be charged in the coming days, MaltaToday reported. [@1]

MaltaToday carried a Malta Police photograph of the plants. [@1]
