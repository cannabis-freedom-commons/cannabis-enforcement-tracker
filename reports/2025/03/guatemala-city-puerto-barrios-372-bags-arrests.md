Police detained 20-year-old Noel “N”, who sold drugs in zone 11 of Guatemala
City, in operations against retail drug dealing, or narcomenudeo. [@1] The
National Civil Police described him as a suspected member of the Mara
Salvatrucha, or MS-13, gang. [@1] Police said he operated around 10 Avenida and
9 Calle in Colonia Roosevelt, where they searched a property. [@2] In a separate
raid officers detained 43-year-old Wendy “N” in a cantina in Puerto Barrios,
Izabal. [@1] Wendy “N” was placed at the disposal of a competent judge to
resolve her legal situation. [@2]

Inside the zone 11 property officers found 207 bags with marijuana and seven
plastic colmillos, small containers, holding cocaine. [@2] They also found a
Serpento motorcycle without a circulation plate during the same search, the
police said on social media. [@1] In the Puerto Barrios cantina officers found
165 bags of marijuana and 915 quetzales in cash that authorities believe came
from selling the drug. [@2] Together the two operations yielded 372 bags of
suspected marijuana and two arrests, police reported. [@2]
