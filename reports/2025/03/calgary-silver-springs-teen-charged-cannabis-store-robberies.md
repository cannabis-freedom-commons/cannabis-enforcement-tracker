Calgary police arrested a 15-year-old male shortly after three robberies at
cannabis stores over the weekend of 1 March 2025. [@1] The first robbery was at
13th Floor Cannabis, 8060 Silver Springs Blvd. N.W., at about 8:40 p.m. on
Saturday, March 1, when a disguised suspect threatened an employee with a weapon
while demanding cannabis products and fled on foot. [@2] The second robbery was
at Cowboy Vape & Smokes, 4624 Varsity Dr. N.W., at about 8:25 p.m. on Sunday,
March 2, when the same suspect threatened an employee, demanded cannabis
products and fled on foot. [@2] The third robbery was again at 13th Floor
Cannabis at about 9:50 p.m. that night, when the suspect demanded cash and
cannabis products while threatening an employee with a weapon. [@2] A search
warrant was subsequently executed at a residence in the community of Silver
Springs, where stolen items believed to have been taken during the robberies
were recovered. [@2]

The 15-year-old male was charged with one count of robbery and two counts of
robbery with a firearm; he cannot be named under the Youth Criminal Justice Act.
[@2] He is scheduled to appear in court on 24 March 2025. [@1]
