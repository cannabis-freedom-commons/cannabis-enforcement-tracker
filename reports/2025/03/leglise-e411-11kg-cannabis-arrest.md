A Federal Road Police patrol intercepted a suspect vehicle on the E411 near
Léglise in the direction of Luxembourg. [@3] Officers turned on their blue
lights and asked the driver to stop. [@1] The driver immediately pulled over to
the side of the road. [@1] Officers deprived him of liberty and brought him
before a juge d’instruction, the investigating judge who leads criminal
inquiries. [@1] After questioning, he was placed in détention préventive,
pre-trial detention. [@1]

A search of the vehicle found 11 kg of cannabis in its boot. [@1] The Police
Fédérale de la Route, Belgium’s federal road police, announced the seizure on
its Facebook page on Wednesday 19 March. [@2]

The patrol had been stationed on the E411 near Léglise toward Luxembourg when
the car drew its attention. [@1] The driver averted his gaze nervously as he
passed the patrol. [@1] Officers following the car noted stressed driving and
unusual behaviour and concluded something was wrong. [@1] The car was travelling
on the E411 in Belgium a short distance from the Grand Duchy border. [@1]
