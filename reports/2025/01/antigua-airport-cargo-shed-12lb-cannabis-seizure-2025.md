Police and customs officers found six vacuum-sealed packages of cannabis inside
a cardboard box during a routine search of the Customs Cargo Shed at V.C. Bird
International Airport. [@1,3] The search took place on the Friday before the
release, 17 January 2025. [@2] The release was issued by the police force's
Strategic Communications office, known as Stratcom. [@2]

The packages weighed 12 pounds, about 5.4 kilograms, and were taken to the
police. [@1,3] Police valued the cannabis at approximately $72,000. [@1,3] No arrest
was reported and the police said further investigations were ongoing. [@2,3]
