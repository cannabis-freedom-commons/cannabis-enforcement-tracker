Assam Rifles, with Tripura Police and the Tripura Forest Service, destroyed
cannabis plantations spread over 16.2 hectares in the Boxanagar Forest Range
under the Sonamura sub-division of Sepahijala district. [@1,2] The 9 January
2025 operation eradicated about 16,500 plants, which the force valued at
roughly Rs 70 lakh. [@1,2] The plantations lay in the general area of
Anandnagar police station inside the Boxanagar Forest Range. [@1]

Assam Rifles presented the drive as part of a broader mission to stamp out
illicit cultivation and promote a drug-free region, with support from the
local administration. [@1,2] The Rs 70 lakh valuation is the force's own
estimate. [@1,2] The accounts report no arrests and no seizure of processed
ganja. [@1,2]
