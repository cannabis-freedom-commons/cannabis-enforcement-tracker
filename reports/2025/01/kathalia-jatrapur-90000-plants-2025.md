Assam Rifles, with Tripura Police and the Tripura Forest Service, destroyed
about 90,000 cannabis plants spread across some 110 acres (44.5 hectares)
in the Kathalia area under Jatrapur police station in the early hours of
Thursday 16 January 2025. [@1,2]

Officials valued the destroyed crop at about Rs 3.5 crore. [@1,2] The
accounts report no arrests and no seizure of processed ganja. [@1,2]
