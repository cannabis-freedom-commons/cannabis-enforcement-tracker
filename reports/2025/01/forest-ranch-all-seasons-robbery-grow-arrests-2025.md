Butte County sheriff's deputies arrested three men after a 911 call led them to
an armed robbery at an illegal marijuana grow on All Seasons Drive in Forest
Ranch. [@1] Dispatch received the call at about 10:41 a.m. on Wednesday, 22
January 2025, from 26-year-old Daniel Sarmientogodoy of Los Angeles, who said he
"was in a little bit of trouble and there is a robbery going on nearby." [@2]
Deputies traced the phone to Summer Place Road in Forest Ranch and found an
illegal assault rifle near Sarmientogodoy, and arrested him. [@1] Detectives
then established that several people had carried out an armed robbery at a
marijuana grow on All Seasons Drive, and deputies and a sheriff's helicopter
searched for hours before finding 27-year-old Jorge Llantada of Van Nuys and
26-year-old Gerardo Chequerbazanez of Los Angeles, both of whom were arrested.
[@1] Chequerbazanez was in possession of an illegal assault rifle when he was
contacted, the sheriff's office said. [@2]

Three other people who worked at the marijuana grow were contacted, and
detectives found evidence that at least one of them conspired with the three
robbers to steal marijuana from the All Seasons Drive property. [@1] Detectives
believe the marijuana owner interrupted the robbery, causing the robbers to flee
the property. [@2] On the morning of 23 January 2025, detectives served a search
warrant at the All Seasons Drive property and found approximately 2,000 pounds
of marijuana (about 907 kilograms) in cardboard boxes. [@1]

The sheriff's office said the investigation was ongoing and that it expected
more arrests. [@2]
