A serving Fiji police officer was charged with one count of unlawful possession
of illicit drugs. [@1] The officer was held in custody to be produced at the
Suva Magistrate court the next day. [@1]

Police found 6,037 branches of dried leaves believed to be marijuana at the
officer's house last Wednesday. [@1] The discovery was made after police raided
the house. [@1] Police said the dried leaves tested positive for cannabis
sativa, identified in the report as marijuana. [@1] A photograph published with
the report showed part of the 6,037 marijuana plants found at the house of the
police officer. [@1]

RNZ independently reported that a Fiji police officer had been charged with
possessing illegal drugs. [@2] RNZ confirmed that the officer was found with
6,037 branches of dried leaves after a search conducted at his home the previous
week. [@2] RNZ confirmed Fiji police's account that the dried leaves tested
positive for cannabis sativa. [@2]
