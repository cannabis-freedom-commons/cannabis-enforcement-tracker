A 19-year-old fisherman who worked as a watchman on Turneffe Island was
convicted in Belize City Magistrate's Court on two counts of possession of
controlled drugs with intent to supply. [@1] Cameron Flowers, a resident of
Mopan Street, Belize City, was caught with 61 grams and 209 grams of marijuana
on the island. [@1] He appeared unrepresented before Chief Magistrate Jayani
Wegodapola and pleaded guilty to both counts. [@1] He said the marijuana was for
personal use during his extended stays on the remote island, but the amounts
exceeded the 60-gram threshold, which makes possession with intent to supply the
automatic charge. [@1]

As a first-time offender he avoided the usual mandatory minimum penalty of
$10,000 and/or three years in prison. [@1] The prosecutor acknowledged that he
had no prior convictions and did not seek the full sentence, but asked the court
to weigh the substantial quantities found on him. [@1] In mitigation he told the
court that Turneffe Island lies 37 miles from Belize City and that the isolation
of his work left him few chances to replenish supplies. [@1] Chief Magistrate
Wegodapola fined him $800 plus $5 court costs for the first count, involving 61
grams of marijuana. [@1]

For the second count, involving 209 grams, she fined him $1,000 plus $5 court
costs. [@1] He has until March 14, 2025, to pay both fines, which total $1,810.
[@1] If he misses the deadline he faces five months' imprisonment on the first
count and an additional year on the second. [@1]
