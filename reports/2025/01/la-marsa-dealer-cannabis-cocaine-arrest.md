Tunisia's Directorate General of National Security, the DGSN national police
directorate, announced on Tuesday 7 January 2025 that a drug trafficker had been
arrested in the act in La Marsa, a northern suburb of Tunis. [@1] The
directorate said its La Marsa criminal investigation squad, with the east and
west La Marsa police posts, acted after information that a man was selling drugs
from an apartment he had rented in the area. [@3] Officers moved in after
coordinating with the public prosecution and arrested the suspect at the
address. [@3]

Three other people waiting outside his home to buy drugs from him were arrested
at the same time, bringing the number held to four. [@2] A search of the home
found four plastic wrappers of cocaine, pieces of cannabis of varying size, an
electronic scale, narcotic tablets, 1,500 dinars in cash and a box containing a
drug liquid. [@3]

Tunisia's public prosecutor ordered all four people held in detention. [@1] No
charges had been brought against any of the four people. [@1] The specialized
security squad was still investigating the case. [@3]
