The West Gendarmerie Legion intercepted a cannabis load in the Ndé department
during a surveillance-and-control operation. [@2] The load comprised 78 100-kg
sacks totalling 7.8 tonnes. [@2] The truck carried 78 sacks of cannabis
concealed among fresh produce. [@1] Colonel Abba Saïdou, commander of the West
Gendarmerie Legion, presented the cargo to Governor Awa Fonka Augustine and the
press in Bafoussam. [@1] The truck's driver and convoy assistant fled during a
systematic search. [@1]

The operation was conducted by Captain Abdouraman Oumarou, head of the legion's
office for combating major crime. [@1] Abba Saïdou said the load came from the
neighbouring Northwest Region and was bound for Yaoundé and the northern
regions. [@2] Investigators identified Kumbo in the Northwest Region as the
truck's starting point and said it was to pass through Njimom, Foumban, Foumbot
and Bangangté before reaching Yaoundé. [@1] One-quarter of the load was destined
for the capital, while another part was intended for Kousseri and onward
transport to other countries. [@1]

A later account describes the January seizure as a convoy carrying 78 sacks
totalling 7.8 tonnes confiscated in the Ndé department. [@4] Actu Cameroun
reported each sack held 100 kg but gave an exact total of 7,870 kg. [@3]
Governor Awa Fonka Augustine described the cannabis as an industrial-scale
production capable of supplying the entire West Region for a year. [@3] The
gendarmerie said the seizure formed part of Opération Tentacules. [@3]
