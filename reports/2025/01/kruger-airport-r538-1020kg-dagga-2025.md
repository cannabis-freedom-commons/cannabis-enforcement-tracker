Officers of the White River Flying Squad stopped a white Toyota Quantum with two
occupants, aged 23 and 30, during a routine patrol on the R538 near Kruger Mpumalanga
International Airport on 26 January 2025. [@1] A search found 50 compressed bags of
dagga weighing about 1,020 kg, with an estimated street value of R1.2 million. [@1,2]

Both men were arrested and charged with dealing in dagga, and were expected to appear
in the White River Magistrate's Court. [@1] One suspect, a citizen of eSwatini without
legal documentation to be in South Africa, faces an additional charge under the
Immigration Act. [@1]

Mpumalanga's MEC for Community Safety, Security and Liaison, Jackie Macie, applauded
the police for the arrests, made under acting provincial commissioner Major General
Zeph Mkhwanazi's leadership. [@2]
