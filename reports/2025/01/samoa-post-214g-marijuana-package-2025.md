Samoa's Ministry of Customs and Revenue intercepted a suspicious package that
arrived through Samoa Post and found 214 grams of marijuana inside. [@1] The
interception took place during the week Samoa marked International Customs Day
2025. [@1]

Customs officers used X-ray scanning equipment and intelligence-driven checks to
identify the drugs before they reached local communities. [@2] The intercepted
drugs were handed to the Ministry of Police, Prison and Corrections Service for
further investigation and legal action. [@2] The package was handed over to the
police ministry for investigation and legal proceedings. [@3]

A man appeared in Samoa's Supreme Court and pleaded not guilty to possession and
importation of narcotics over the marijuana brought in through Samoa Post. [@4]
The court granted him bail on strict conditions that included surrendering all
his travel documents and living at Afiamalu until his next appearance, and
Justice Fepuleai Ameperosa Roma told him not to contact police witnesses. [@4]
The judge set the hearing for the week beginning 19 May 2025. [@4]

Customs chief executive Fonoti Talaitupu Li'a-Taefu said the week's drug busts
showed the service's ongoing commitment and that it would keep strengthening
border control with local and international partners. [@1] The marijuana
interception came two days after customs officers seized a shipment of
methamphetamine at the Matautu wharf, a separate case in which a businessman and
two staff were arrested. [@1]
