Customs officers at the Obala post in Cameroon's Centre region stopped a public
transport bus on the Bafoussam–Yaoundé road on 13 January 2025 and found 100 kg
of cannabis hidden in its luggage holds. [@1] The cannabis was divided into five
parcels, concealed in the holds of the bus. [@2] The load came from Bafoussam in
the West region and was headed for Yaoundé, the capital. [@2]

According to the state broadcaster CRTV, the drug was carefully wrapped to
escape checks, and the transport company's managers could not at first justify
why the parcels were on board. [@2] An investigation was opened to identify who
ordered and who was to receive the load. [@2]

Xinhua, citing CRTV, reported the same 13 January seizure of 100 kg of cannabis
in five parcels on a bus from the West region bound for Yaoundé. [@3]
