Customs officers at Chennai international airport arrested three people, one
woman and two men from the same family, after intercepting them with hydroponic
ganja smuggled in from Thailand. [@1] The three had arrived in Chennai from
Bangkok on 26 January 2025. [@2] Acting on specific intelligence, the Chennai
Customs air intelligence unit intercepted the trio when they landed. [@3]

Officers found 23.48 kg of hydroponic ganja hidden in packages of frozen fruits.
[@2] Customs valued the seizure at Rs 23.5 crore. [@2] The Times of India
reported the overall seizure as weighing 23.5 kg, the same figure as its rupee
value. [@3] NDTV reported that the ganja was concealed in soft drink powder
packets, a detail that conflicts with the frozen-fruit packaging described
elsewhere. [@1] Hydroponic ganja is cannabis grown in water rather than soil,
sold in India under street names including OG, sugarcone and Kush. [@1]

The three told investigators that someone at Bangkok airport had handed them the
packages to deliver in Chennai, and each was paid Rs 15,000 for the job. [@3]
All three were arrested under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances
Act, 1985 and remanded in judicial custody. [@2] Customs warned travellers not
to accept parcels from strangers at foreign airports. [@3] The Times of India
said Indian law does not treat about 20 kg of hydroponic ganja as a commercial
quantity, which makes bail easier to obtain. [@3]
