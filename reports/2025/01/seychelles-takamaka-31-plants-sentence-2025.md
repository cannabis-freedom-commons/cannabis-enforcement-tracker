On 10 March 2020, Earonn Mario Markus Humphrey of Quatre Borne, Takamaka had
thirty-one cannabis plants, ranging from 6 cm to 104 cm, on his farm at
Takamaka, Mahé. [@1]

Charged with cultivation, he pleaded guilty to possessing the plants, and the
court convicted him on his plea when the case was heard on 18 December 2024.
[@1]

On 31 January 2025 the Supreme Court sentenced him to two years' imprisonment,
suspended for two years, plus a fine of SCR14,000 payable in monthly
instalments of SCR1,000. [@1]
