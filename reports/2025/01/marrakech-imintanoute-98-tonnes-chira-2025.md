Officers of the Marrakech police prefecture, working with their counterparts in Safi,
foiled an attempt to traffic 9.8 tonnes of cannabis resin, known locally as chira,
to another country on Tuesday 21 January 2025. [@1] The operation took place in the
Addher area of the rural commune of Kouzemt near Imintanoute, on precise information
from the General Directorate of Territorial Surveillance (DGST). [@1,2]

Six suspects aged between 29 and 59 were caught preparing an international drug
trafficking operation by sea, including an official and a former elected
representative linked to the network. [@1,2] Officers seized the resin at several
sites along with a commercial vehicle bearing a false number plate, an inflatable
boat and a marine engine. [@1]

The suspects were taken into custody for investigation under the supervision of the
competent public prosecutor, to establish the full extent of the case and identify
possible accomplices. [@1]
