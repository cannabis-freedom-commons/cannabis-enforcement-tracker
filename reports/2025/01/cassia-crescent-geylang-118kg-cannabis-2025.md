On the afternoon of 4 January 2025, Central Narcotics Bureau officers arrested a 27-year-old man
and a 25-year-old man at a car park near Cassia Crescent in Geylang. [@1] A third suspect, aged
25, fled on seeing the arrests but was caught after a short pursuit, and officers seized an
offensive weapon from him and handed it to the police. [@1]

The 27-year-old was carrying about 1,855 g of heroin, 1,032 g of 'Ice', 98 g of Ecstasy tablets
and 300 Erimin-5 tablets, while the 25-year-old had about 136 g of 'Ice' and 21 g of cannabis on
him. [@1] Officers then raided the 27-year-old's suspected hideout, a flat in the same area, where
they recovered more 'Ice', heroin, Erimin-5 tablets and a small amount of Ecstasy, and arrested a
25-year-old woman suspected of drug abuse. [@1] A search of the car the first two men were arrested
next to recovered about 11.78 kg of cannabis along with heroin, 'Ice', ketamine, Ecstasy tablets
and 1,200 Erimin-5 tablets. [@1]

All four suspects are Singaporeans. [@1] CNB put the street value of the total haul at more than
S$1 million, enough to feed about 4,590 abusers for a week. [@1] Trafficking more than 500 g of
cannabis can carry the mandatory death sentence in Singapore. [@1] Investigations were continuing. [@1]
