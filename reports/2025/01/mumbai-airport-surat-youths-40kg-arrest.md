Customs officers arrested four young residents of Surat at Mumbai's Chhatrapati
Shivaji Maharaj International Airport for allegedly attempting to smuggle
hydroponic cannabis from Bangkok. [@2] The four were identified as Manavkumar
Dilip Pipliya, Ravibhai Dilip Pipliya, Jigar Naresh Panchani and Brijesh Sanjay
Mendapara. [@1] A second account gives the same four names in shorter form as
Manavkumar Pipaliya, Ravi Pipaliya, Jigar Panchani and Brijesh Mendpara, all
residents of Surat. [@2] A case was registered under the Narcotic Drugs and
Psychotropic Substances Act, 1985. [@2] The four accused were remanded to
custody. [@2]

Officers found 42 transparent plastic packets containing hydroponic cannabis
hidden inside multiple trolley bags stuffed with clothes and personal
belongings. [@2] Customs seized 40 kilograms of hydroponic marijuana that the
youths attempted to smuggle from Bangkok. [@1] A field testing kit confirmed the
substance as hydroponic cannabis, a high-potency variant of marijuana. [@2]
Customs estimated the haul, weighing over 40 kg, to be worth Rs 40 crore in the
illegal drug market. [@2]

All four had flown to Bangkok on January 27 and returned to India on January 30.
[@1] Customs officers grew suspicious of the youths' travel timing and purpose
before searching their baggage. [@1] Preliminary investigations indicate they
were acting as drug couriers for an international syndicate operating between
Bangkok and India. [@2] Authorities said they were working to track down the
mastermind behind the operation, which could be linked to a global network. [@1]
