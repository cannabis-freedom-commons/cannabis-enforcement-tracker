Acting on intelligence, officers of the Jamaica Constabulary Force's Firearms
and Narcotics Investigation Division (FNID) and the Jamaica Defence Force
Coast Guard intercepted a vessel at South Bowden Wharf in Port Morant, St
Thomas, late at night. [@1,2]

Officers searched the vessel, described in the later report as a canoe with
the men aboard, and found bags of compressed ganja weighing about 1,832
pounds (about 831 kg). [@1,2] Five men were taken into custody; their
identities were initially withheld pending investigation. [@1]

The accused, Moses Pitt, 48, Rusheyne Drummonds, 22, Preston Stephens, 38,
Andre Barracks, 38, and Anthonio Pitt, 50, were charged with possession of
ganja, dealing in ganja, trafficking ganja, taking steps preparatory to
export ganja and conspiracy to traffic ganja. [@2] They appeared before the
St Thomas Parish Court on Friday, 3 January 2025, and were remanded in
custody until 28 January. [@2]

FNID head Senior Superintendent Samuel Blake said the joint operation showed
the commitment of Jamaican law enforcement to combating drug trafficking and
related crimes. [@2]
