The Carabineros and Environmental Protection Unit of the Nariño police carried
out an operation in the village of Santa María in the municipality of Buesaco.
[@1] During routine patrol, search and control operations, officers were
supported by a canine unit trained in narcotics detection. [@1] The discovery
occurred when an individual, upon noticing the police presence, abandoned the
substance and fled. [@1] No arrest was reported in the police account. [@1] The
canine alerted to the presence of narcotics, allowing officers to collect the
illicit material and turn it over to the appropriate authorities for analysis
and inclusion in the corresponding legal proceedings. [@1]

Police said the seizure totalled 1,282 doses of marijuana. [@1] Police described
the amount as more than one kilo of marijuana and said the operation prevented
its distribution and consumption. [@1]

The operation was carried out by the Carabineros and Environmental Protection
Section (SECAR) of the Nariño Police Department (DENAR) as part of its fight
against drug trafficking. [@1] The National Police said it will continue to
strengthen its presence in rural areas, ensuring the safety and well-being of
remote communities. [@1] Officers encouraged citizens to report suspicious
activity through the 123 hotline or directly to police units in their area. [@1]
