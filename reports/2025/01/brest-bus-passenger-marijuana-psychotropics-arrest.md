Brest customs officers detained an express-bus passenger who had arrived from
Poland with three plastic packets containing marijuana, amphetamine and 4-SMS.
[@1] Entering Belarus through the Brest checkpoint, the Belarusian citizen chose
the green corridor for crossing the border. [@1] He told Brest customs officers
that he had nothing to declare and no prohibited or restricted items. [@1] A
service dog signalled possible concealed prohibited substances by reacting to
the man's backpack. [@1] Searching his toiletry bag, officers found a green
chewing-gum pack concealing a parcel wrapped in tape. [@1]

Examination by the Brest regional department of the State Committee of Forensic
Examinations identified the seized items as marijuana, amphetamine and the
psychotropic 4-SMS. [@1] Together the substances weighed 3 grams, a combined
total covering the marijuana and both psychotropics. [@2] Customs described the
detention as the disruption of an unlawful import of prohibited substances from
Poland. [@2] Brest Customs opened a criminal case under part 2 of article 328-1
of the Criminal Code, an offence punishable by up to 10 years' imprisonment.
[@2]
