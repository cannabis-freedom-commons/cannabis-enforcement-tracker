Detectives from the Herford criminal investigation department searched an industrial building in Hiddenhausen on Tuesday 7 January 2025. [@1] The building had housed a food-processing company, and witnesses had reported that hemp was being grown there as a plantation for marijuana production. [@1] The search was carried out under a warrant as part of a drug investigation led by the Bielefeld public prosecutor's office. [@1]

Officers seized more than 250 hemp plants, more than 70 kilograms of marijuana and almost 1 kilogram of hashish from the premises. [@1] Police put the street value of the drugs at at least half a million euros. [@1] The investigation into suspected gang and commercial production of and trafficking in narcotics in not insignificant quantities is continuing. [@1]
