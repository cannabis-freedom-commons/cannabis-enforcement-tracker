Officers of the Vadodara district Rural Special Operation Group, a patrolling
police unit, stopped a suspicious moped in the Karjan police station area and
found ganja in its storage compartment. [@1] Police arrested Shakil Iqbalbhai
Malek, a resident of Brahman Faliyu in Kanbha, Karjan, Vadodara. [@1]

Police seized 1.920 kg of ganja from the scooter. [@1] The seized goods,
including the Activa scooter, a mobile phone, cash, an electronic weighing scale
and a bag, were worth Rs 50,810 according to police. [@1]

Under questioning, the accused said he had bought the stock from a person named
Vinod. [@1] Police declared Vinod wanted and began efforts to arrest him. [@1]

The SOG registered a case under the NDPS Act at Karjan police station. [@1]
Further investigation was handed to the inspector of Shinor police station. [@1]
