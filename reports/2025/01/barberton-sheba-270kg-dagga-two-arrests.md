Mpumalanga police officers deployed under Operation Vala Umgodi, an operation
against illicit mining, arrested two men for alleged dealing in dagga. [@1] The
two suspects, aged 30 and 36, were arrested in Barberton on the evening of
Monday. [@1] Officers patrolling the area spotted a white Isuzu bakkie with two
occupants coming from the direction of Low's Creek towards Sheba Siding, and
stopped it after the driver tried to turn. [@1]

Police searching the vehicle recovered 10 bags loaded with dagga weighing more
than 270 kilogrammes. [@1] Police put the estimated street value of the dagga at
around R1 million. [@1]

Police said the two men were taking the dagga from the border of the Kingdom of
eSwatini to Sheba when they were intercepted. [@1] The two suspects were
apprehended and charged. [@1] The two arrested men were expected to appear in
court soon. [@1]
