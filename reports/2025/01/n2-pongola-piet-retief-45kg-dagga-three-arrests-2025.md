Mpumalanga police arrested three suspects aged between 32 and 52 after allegedly
finding dagga on the N2 Road between Pongola and Piet Retief. [@1] Officers from
the Mahamba port of entry stopped a red Mercedes-Benz minibus carrying two men
and a woman during routine patrols. [@1] After searching the vehicle and finding
eight rolls of dagga wrapped in black plastic bags, officers arrested the three
occupants and charged them with dealing in dagga. [@1] The two men are South
African citizens while the woman is an eSwatini national without valid
documentation to be in South Africa. [@1] The suspects were expected to appear
in the Piet Retief Magistrate's Court on 20 January 2025 for a formal bail
application. [@1] Police said additional charges for contravening South Africa's
Immigration Act could follow. [@1]

Police said the confiscated dagga weighed at least 45 kilograms, with an
estimated street value of about R67,500. [@1] The minibus, which police said
belongs to the 37-year-old male suspect, was impounded for further
investigation. [@1]

Preliminary investigations indicated the suspects were travelling from eSwatini
toward KwaZulu-Natal. [@1] Mpumalanga acting provincial commissioner Major
General Zeph Mkhwanazi welcomed the arrests and warned against trafficking
illegal substances. [@1]
