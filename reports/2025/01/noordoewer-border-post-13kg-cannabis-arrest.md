Namibia Revenue Agency officials at the Noordoewer border post stopped a
41-year-old South African man and searched his bag, finding cannabis. [@1] He
was arrested at about 07h00 on Sunday at the border post, after he left his
suitcase hidden, went to stamp his passport and came back for the bag. [@1]

The bag held 2.1 kg of cannabis described as skunk, valued at N$105 250, and
10.9 kg described as majat, valued at N$109 100. [@1] Police put the total value
of the cannabis at more than N$200 000. [@1]

He was charged with one count of dealing in cannabis and was scheduled to appear
in the Noordoewer Periodic Court on Tuesday. [@1] Deputy commissioner Nicodemus
Mbango, the Namibian Police's regional crime investigations coordinator, gave
the account in a crime report on Sunday. [@1] Police said their investigations
into the matter were continuing. [@1]
