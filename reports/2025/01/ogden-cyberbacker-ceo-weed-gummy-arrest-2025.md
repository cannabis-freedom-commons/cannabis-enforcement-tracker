Craig Goodliffe is the CEO of Cyberbacker. [@1] Goodliffe was arrested in Ogden,
Utah, for allegedly giving his 8-year-old son marijuana candy. [@1] His arrest
followed a welfare-check request from his mother, who was concerned he was
having a manic episode. [@1] Officers found Goodliffe in a state of hysteria,
talking about magic rituals. [@1] He told police he had taken a large amount of
marijuana, Viagra and caffeine. [@1] He admitted giving his son a weed gummy he
hoped would put him to sleep while he watched pornography. [@1] Officers said
the child was less than coherent and later tested positive for THC at hospital.
[@1]

The house held 9 pounds (about 4.08 kg) of various marijuana products that were
unmarked and easily accessible to children. [@1] Near the contraband, officers
found urine and feces on the floor, and Goodliffe said he had defecated on the
floor. [@1] The arrest led to charges of child endangerment, purchase of a
firearm by a restricted person and intoxication. [@1]

Cyberbacker provides real-estate professionals with administrative support and
virtual-assistant services. [@1] Goodliffe had founded a Keller Williams
affiliate in Utah before heading Cyberbacker. [@1] HousingWire published this
account on 14 January 2025. [@1] The article said the Ogden arrest happened the
previous week. [@1]
