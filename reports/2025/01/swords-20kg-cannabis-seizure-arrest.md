An Garda Síochána, the Irish police, carried out a search in Swords, County
Dublin, on Saturday. [@1] A man in his 40s was arrested following the seizure.
[@1] Gardaí said the seized drugs will be sent for forensic analysis. [@1]

During the search officers seized 40 vacuum-sealed bags of cannabis with a
combined weight of 20kg. [@1] Gardaí valued the cannabis at €400,000. [@1]
Officers also seized €7,000 in cash during the same search. [@1]

Assistant Commissioner Paul Cleary described the seizure as another step towards
dismantling the gang involved and said Gardaí have now seized over €2 million in
illegal drugs from that gang. [@1]
