Macau's Judiciary Police arrested a 49-year-old local resident surnamed Pinho, a
clerk, for smuggling cannabis from Bangkok hidden in three pairs of sneakers.
[@1] Officers approached the flight from Thailand at Macau International Airport
under a risk-management screening and intercepted him for a luggage check at
6:40 p.m. on Saturday, 25 January 2025. [@2]

In his backpack officers found three old sneakers packed with the drug wrapped
in shopping bags and cling film, and three e-cigarettes containing cannabis oil.
[@3] The haul weighed 800 grams in all and was made up of 10 packets of cannabis
buds plus the three oil e-cigarettes. [@1] Police said the cannabis had an
estimated street value of 803,000 patacas. [@1] A search of his flat in Taipa
turned up nine packs of rolling papers. [@1]

Pinho refused to cooperate with questioning but tested positive for cannabis
use, and the Chinese-language accounts say a urine test confirmed it. [@3] The
investigation into where the cannabis came from continues. [@2]
