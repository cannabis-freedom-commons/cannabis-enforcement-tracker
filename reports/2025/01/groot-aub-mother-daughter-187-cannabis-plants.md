Namibian police arrested a 55-year-old mother and her 24-year-old daughter near
Groot-Aub, south of Windhoek, according to the weekly crime report they released
on Sunday. [@1] The police said they found 187 cannabis plants weighing over
35kg on the property, which they valued at an estimated N$1.7 million. [@1]

The Namibian Sun reported that the two made a first appearance in the Rehoboth
Magistrate’s Court facing charges of drug dealing and possession. [@2]

The Issue later described the case as two women arrested in January for
cultivating a cannabis plantation at Groot Aub in the Khomas region. [@3] That
summary repeated the 187 plants, the 35-kilogramme weight and the police
estimate of N$1.7 million. [@3]
