Mauritania's National Gendarmerie rapid-intervention unit foiled an attempted
cannabis-smuggling run 25 kilometres east of Nouakchott on Wednesday, 23 April
2025. [@1] The unit that acted was the Gendarmerie's Unité d'intervention
rapide. [@1] Officers stopped a car that had come from the city of Boghé and was
travelling toward the capital. [@1] Officers arrested the driver of the vehicle
at the scene. [@1] Later inquiries led to the arrest of the sender of the load,
who lives in Boghé, and its intended recipient in Nouakchott. [@1]

The operation followed in-depth field investigations by the gendarmerie. [@1]
After stopping the car, officers carried out a thorough search of the vehicle.
[@1] They found the drug carefully concealed inside the car. [@1] The
gendarmerie identified the seized drug as cannabis and described the haul as an
important quantity. [@1] The three arrests linked the load's dispatch in Boghé
to its intended receipt in Nouakchott. [@1]
