Acting on a tip-off received on 13 April 2025 that a container truck from Guwahati was carrying a large quantity of ganja toward Kushinagar, Special Task Force teams with local police stopped it near Swastik Dhaba at Abhinayakpur in the Kasya area on Sunday evening. [@1,2] Inside hidden compartments in the container they found 120.26 kilograms of ganja and arrested three members of the smuggling syndicate. [@1,2]

Police identified the men as Ajay Yadav of Mau district, driver Vipin Kumar Pal and conductor Suhail Khan, both of Etawah district. [@1,2] Yadav said Sandeep Yadav of Mau had sent him to Guwahati with a promise of Rs 15,000 for bringing the truck back, while Pal said he was promised Rs 50,000 for driving the consignment from Guwahati to Mau. [@1] Police valued the haul at about Rs 72 lakh, seized the container truck with registration HR-55 AK 1767, and filed a case under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act. [@2,1]
