Police Regional Office Cordillera (PRO CAR) teams discovered and destroyed
marijuana plants worth PhP2,358,000 in eradication operations across Kalinga
and Benguet provinces on 19 April 2025. [@1]

In Kalinga, joint operatives of Rizal and Tinglayan police stations, the 1st
Kalinga Provincial Mobile Force Company, the provincial drug enforcement unit
and PDEA-Kalinga found 9,000 fully grown plants and 1,200 seedlings worth
PhP1,848,000 in Buscalan, Tinglayan and Romualdez, Rizal. [@1]

In Benguet, joint forces of Bakun and Kibungan police, provincial drug and
intelligence units, regional intelligence units and PDEA-CAR found 2,550 fully
grown plants worth PhP510,000 in Badeo, Kibungan and Kayapa, Bakun. [@1]

All plants were uprooted and burned on site, with samples kept for the
regional forensic unit, while investigations continue to find the growers. [@1]
