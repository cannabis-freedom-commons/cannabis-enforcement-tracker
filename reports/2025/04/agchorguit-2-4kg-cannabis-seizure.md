Mauritania's gendarmerie brigade on duty at Agchorguit seized drugs on Wednesday
9 April 2025 during a routine check of a vehicle arriving from the town of
Boghé. [@1] The National Gendarmerie announced the seizure in a tweet. [@1] The
parcel was addressed to a recipient in Nouakchott, and the gendarmes foiled an
attempt to bring the drug into the capital. [@1]

The operation took place at 11:30. [@1] Officers seized 2.4 kg of cannabis found
on the driver of a Toyota Avensis Verso. [@1]

Rimnow's original account likewise says the Agchorguit brigade seized the drug
during a routine check of a vehicle from Boghé on Wednesday 9 April, as
announced in the National Gendarmerie's tweet. [@2] Rimnow likewise reports the
11:30 operation and the seizure of 2.4 kg of cannabis from the driver of a
Toyota Avensis Verso. [@2]
