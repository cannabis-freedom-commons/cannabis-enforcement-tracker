Surat Railway Police seized more than 4 kilograms of marijuana from an
unattended school bag found on the Rajkot–Secunderabad Express at Surat railway
station. [@2] Police registered a case against an unknown person under the
Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act. [@1]

The discovery was made during a surprise check of the station and of trains
arriving and departing from it. [@1] Train 22718 had stopped at platform 1 at
night, and officers were checking its sleeper accommodation with sniffer dog
Drake. [@1] A black school bag was found unclaimed in the corridor between
coaches S/09 and S/10. [@1] Drake barked when the bag was checked, and handler
Head Constable Pratapsinh interpreted this as a signal that narcotics were
inside. [@1] Police questioned nearby passengers, but no owner was found. [@1]

The bag contained 4.450 kilograms of marijuana, which police valued at ₹44,500.
[@1] Police said the unknown person abandoned the bag out of fear of being
caught. [@1] Surat Railway Police began searching for the owner of the unclaimed
bag. [@1] Further investigation was under way to trace those responsible. [@2]
