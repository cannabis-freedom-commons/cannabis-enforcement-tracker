Goa Police arrested Israeli national Yaniv Benaim alias Atala from his residence
and seized charas and cocaine. [@1] The Anti-Narcotics Cell raided Atala’s
residence at Siolim village in North Goa on Friday night. [@1] Atala had been in
the news in 2010 after his statements exposed the alleged police-politician-drug
nexus in Goa. [@1]

Officers seized 110 grams of charas in the raid. [@1] Officers also seized 50
grams of cocaine in the same raid. [@1] Police collectively valued the seized
charas and cocaine at over Rs 9 lakh. [@1] The Times of India separately put the
value of the narcotics at Rs 8.7 lakh. [@2]

Police said Atala had been under the Anti-Narcotics Cell radar for many days
before the team visited his rented residence at Siolim. [@2] Atala was in police
custody while Goa police questioned him for further leads. [@2] Atala had been
staying in Goa since 2006, was arrested in 2010 in an alleged
police-politician-drug mafia nexus case, and was extradited from Peru in 2011.
[@2]
