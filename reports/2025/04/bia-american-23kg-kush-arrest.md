A 31-year-old American citizen was arrested for allegedly smuggling a suitcase
full of “kush” cannabis into Sri Lanka from Thailand. [@1] The man, who works
for a land trading company in the United States, was held at the Bandaranaike
airport in Colombo on Tuesday morning after arriving on a SriLankan Airlines
flight. [@1] He was detained while trying to leave the airport through a
designated business corridor at the arrival terminal. [@1] He has not been
named. [@1]

The Narcotics Control Unit of Sri Lanka Customs recovered a total of 23kg of
cannabis concealed in around two dozen packets in his suitcase. [@1] Customs
officials estimated the street value of the seized drugs at about £582,000. [@1]

The American was one of more than half a dozen foreigners arrested in the first
four months of the year while attempting to bring cannabis into Sri Lanka from
Thailand. [@1] Additional Director General of Sri Lanka Customs Seevali Arukgoda
said decriminalisation of cannabis in Thailand had been followed by a growing
trend of smuggling of the drug into Sri Lanka in significant quantities. [@1]
