Saudi Arabia executed two citizens in the Makkah region on 22 April 2025 for promoting hashish.
[@1] The interior ministry named them as Rakan bin Abdulaziz bin Nasser Al-Dosari and Majed bin
Ahmed bin Rada Al-Otaibi. [@1] It said security forces had arrested them, courts found the charge
proved, and the discretionary death sentence became final after appeal and Supreme Court approval
before a royal order carried it out. [@1]

## Same day as other executions

Amnesty International noted that on 22 April the Saudi Press Agency also announced an execution of a
Pakistani national for heroin trafficking in the same batch of statements. [@2] The ministry text
republished by CNN covers only the two Makkah hashish cases above. [@1,2]
