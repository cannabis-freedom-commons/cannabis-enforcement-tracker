Callum Knight-Hook was given a 12-month jail term, suspended for 18 months, with
a supervision order for the same duration. [@1] The 28-year-old appeared at the
Court of General Gaol Delivery for sentencing on Friday. [@2] Callum
Knight-Hook, 28, from Darragh in Port Erin, had earlier appeared before
magistrates at Douglas Courthouse. [@3] No plea had been entered to two counts
of possession with intent to supply and one count of possessing criminal
property at that earlier hearing. [@3] Police executed a search warrant at his
home on 9 March 2024. [@2] His home was in Darragh, Port Erin, where officers
found him in the living room with a package of cannabis nearby. [@1]

After being arrested and handcuffed, he was seen trying to dispose of white
powder on a counter. [@1] When challenged by officers he said the powder was
ketamine, not cocaine. [@1] The search found 84.4 g of cannabis with a street
value of £1,688 and £3,400 in cash. [@2] Officers also found six grammes of
ketamine. [@2] Knight-Hook initially told officers he bought the cannabis in
bulk for personal use. [@1] His phone later revealed messages showing he had
been selling cannabis to other people. [@1]

Knight-Hook later admitted possessing cannabis with intent to supply, possessing
ketamine and possessing criminal property. [@2] The cannabis offence drew eight
months suspended for 18 months with supervision, with a concurrent two-month
term for ketamine. [@2] He also received a consecutive four-month term for
possessing criminal property, also suspended for 18 months. [@2] Deemster Graeme
Cook said Knight-Hook would benefit more from probation than immediate custody.
[@2] His advocate said family issues and mental-health problems from cannabis
use had affected him, and that he was now receiving medicinal cannabis on
prescription. [@2]
