Officers of the Financial Administration stopped a shipment in Sežana on
28 April 2025 that had travelled from Thailand to the Netherlands by ship and
then by lorry to Slovenia. [@1] The shipment was declared as tea — nine
pallets, or 140 cartons — imported by a Danish company for a Dutch recipient.
[@1,2] After a suspicious sample emerged among tested packets, criminal
investigators isolated the suspect packet and the National Forensic Laboratory
confirmed it was cannabis. [@1,2] A further check uncovered 925 kilograms of
cannabis packed in smaller parcels of around 300 grams each. [@1,2]

Police valued the 925 kilograms at about 2,312,500 euros on the black market.
[@1] The findings went to the Koper district prosecutor's office, which opened
pre-trial proceedings, while investigators worked to identify the origin of
the drugs and their intended recipient. [@1] The shipment's unusual route —
from the Netherlands to a Slovenian customs warehouse and back toward the
Netherlands — raised questions about whether the declared recipient was the
real one or had been abused for smuggling. [@2] No one had been charged when
the case was presented on 15 May 2025. [@2]
