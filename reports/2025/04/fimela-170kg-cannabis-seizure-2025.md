The traffickers abandoned six packages of chanvre indien hidden at the scene and
fled. [@1] No arrests were reported, and an investigation was opened to identify
those responsible. [@2]

The seizure was made in Fatick department, between the villages of Loul Sessène
and Fayil. [@1] Dakaractu gave the same total of 170 kilogrammes. [@1]

The brigade intercepted the consignment during the night. [@1]
