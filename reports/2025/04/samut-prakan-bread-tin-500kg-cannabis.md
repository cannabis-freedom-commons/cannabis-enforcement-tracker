At about 2 p.m. on 23 April 2025 officers from Bang Kaeo Police Station, with Samut Prakan
immigration police, searched a luxury house in King Kaew, Rachathewa subdistrict, Bang Phli
district, Samut Prakan, under a provincial court warrant. [@1,2]

They arrested six people — three Thai women, one Thai man, one Chinese man and one Lebanese
national — and seized more than 500 kilograms of dried cannabis flowers packed in plastic bags,
along with cannabis-filled e-cigarettes and bottles of cannabis oil. [@1] Police said the house
lacked the permits needed to process or sell controlled cannabis and that repackaging breached
Thailand's controlled-herb rules. [@1,2]

Officers found cannabis hidden in bread tins, instant-noodle cups, ready-to-eat food containers
and supplement cans for parcel shipment, and loaded the haul to the station in nearly ten pickup
trucks. [@1] Pol. Col. Adirek Thongkamkaew said the raid followed a tip about unlicensed online
sales and that investigators were tracing finances and possible international links. [@1]
