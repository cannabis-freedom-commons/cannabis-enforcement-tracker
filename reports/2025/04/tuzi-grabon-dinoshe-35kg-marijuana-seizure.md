Montenegrin border police found 35 kg of marijuana in the trunk of a vehicle
belonging to a person from Tuzi, suspected of having been trafficked from
Albania. [@1] The driver E. M., 24, was arrested. [@1]

The Police Directorate announced that the border police checked the vehicle on
the Grabon–Dinoshë road and found the drugs in bags in the trunk, 62 packages of
marijuana. [@1] The state prosecutor at the High State Prosecutor's Office in
Podgorica was notified of the incident. [@1] The driver was arrested on
suspicion of the criminal offence of unauthorised production and trafficking of
drugs. [@1]
