The Fayette County Sheriff’s Office arrested 13 people after a three-week
investigation and 10 narcotics-related search warrants. [@1] The search warrants
were executed in Hawkeye, Arlington, West Union and Clermont. [@1] Investigators
found and seized about a half pound of marijuana, half an ounce of
methamphetamine and numerous pills. [@1] The sheriff’s office said 37 charges
had been filed in the investigation. [@2] The sheriff’s office conducted the
investigation with the Iowa State Patrol, the Clayton County Sheriff’s Office,
the Fayette Police Department and the West Union Police Department. [@2]

Caleb Hammel, 18, of Clermont, was charged with first-offence marijuana
possession and possession of drug paraphernalia, both misdemeanours. [@1] Hannah
Bodley, 22, of Hawkeye, Taryn Weaver, 40, of Arlington, and Dakota Howard, 31,
of Arlington, each faced the same two first-offence marijuana charges. [@1]
Matthew Burnell, 48, of Hawkeye, was charged with third-offence marijuana
possession, an aggravated misdemeanour, and possession of drug paraphernalia.
[@1]

Matthew Helgerson, 30, and Desirae Jordan, 28, both of West Union, were each
charged with child endangerment and first-offence marijuana possession. [@1]
Jennifer Herman, 43, of Hawkeye, was charged with third-offence marijuana
possession and possession of drug paraphernalia. [@1] James Cornwell, 42, of
Arlington, was charged with third-offence methamphetamine and third-offence
marijuana possession, both Class D felonies, and possession of drug
paraphernalia. [@1] Brian Coonradt, 61, of Hawkeye, was arrested twice in one
week, and each search warrant at his home produced methamphetamine and marijuana
possession counts and paraphernalia charges. [@1] Alex Dettbarn, 32, of
Arlington, was charged with intent to deliver marijuana and another controlled
substance, failure to affix a drug tax stamp, marijuana possession and
possession of drug paraphernalia. [@1]

Melissa Shaw, 40, of Clermont, was charged with intent to deliver
methamphetamine, two counts of felony child endangerment and a drug tax
violation, with no marijuana count. [@1] Audie Wood, 51, of West Union, was
charged with first-offence methamphetamine possession, with no marijuana count.
[@1]

The sheriff’s office said additional charges were pending. [@1]
