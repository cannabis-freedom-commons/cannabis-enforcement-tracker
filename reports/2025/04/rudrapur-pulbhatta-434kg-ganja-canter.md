The Kumaon unit of the Uttarakhand Special Task Force, with the Udham Singh
Nagar police and Pulbhatta police station staff, arrested a truck driver in the
Pulbhatta police station area of Rudrapur on the intervening night of 10 and 11
April 2025. [@3] He was identified as Raju Ali, a resident of Bilwa village in
Lakhimpur Kheri district of neighbouring Uttar Pradesh. [@2]

Officers stopped the canter and found 4.34 quintals of ganja, about 434
kilograms, hidden in compartments built into the vehicle; police seized the
truck, registration UK06CB 4534. [@3] Senior Superintendent of Police Navneet
Singh Bhullar said it was the largest cannabis seizure in Uttarakhand to date.
[@3] The Bhaskar English account put the value of the ganja at about Rs 2 crore,
while Hindustan Times, citing police, gave an illegal-market value of about Rs
1.08 crore and Pioneer Edge said more than Rs 1 crore. [@1]

Police said the consignment was brought from Jharkhand on the instructions of a
man named Suresh Gupta and was meant for sale in Rudrapur, Gadarpur and Bazpur
in Udham Singh Nagar district. [@3] Bhullar said the accused works as a driver,
hauling goods for Uttarakhand traders to Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, West Bengal,
Jharkhand and Odisha, and sometimes carries drugs back on his return trips. [@1]
Police said they were making efforts to arrest Gupta and had identified several
other drug peddlers from the interrogation, against whom action would be taken.
[@3]

The STF said head constable Govind Singh played a key role in the operation.
[@2] The Hindi-language police account gave the driver's age as 35. [@4] Police
listed Suresh Gupta, who arranged the shipment, as wanted with no known address.
[@4]
