A 22-year-old male Grade 11 pupil of a school at Otjiwarongo was arrested on
Friday on a charge of possessing or dealing in drugs. [@1] Senior inspector
Maureen Mbeha, the Namibian Police's head of community affairs in the
Otjozondjupa region, said the pupil was arrested in a police action at a house
where he lives in Orwetoveni. [@1] Nampa reported that the arrest was made at
about 18h00. [@2]

Mbeha said the pupil was found with three large parcels of skunk cannabis. [@2]
She told Nampa the parcels weighed about 756 grams and had an estimated street
value of N$37 800. [@1]

The suspect was to make a first appearance in the Otjiwarongo Magistrate's Court
on Monday. [@1]
