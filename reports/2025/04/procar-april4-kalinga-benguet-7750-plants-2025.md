Police Regional Office Cordillera (PRO CAR) teams destroyed marijuana plants worth PhP1,550,000.00 in eradication operations across Kalinga and Benguet provinces on 4 April 2025. [@1]

In Loccong, Tinglayan, Kalinga, joint operatives of the Kalinga police, Regional Mobile Force Battalion 15 and PDEA-Kalinga found 6,500 fully grown plants worth PhP1,300,000.00. [@1] In Tacadang, Kibungan, Benguet, joint operatives of the Benguet police, the regional intelligence division and PDEA-CAR found two plantation sites with 1,250 fully grown plants worth PhP250,000.00. [@1]

All plants were uprooted and burned on site, with samples kept for forensic examination, while investigations continue to find the growers. [@1] Officers also arrested a 31-year-old high-value suspect in Upper San Vicente, Baguio City on a drugs warrant the same day. [@1]
