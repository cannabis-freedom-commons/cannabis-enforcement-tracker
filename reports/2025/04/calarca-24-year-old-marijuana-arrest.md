Members of the National Police arrested a man in the municipality of Calarcá for
the crime of trafficking, manufacturing or carrying narcotics. [@1] The man was
24 years old. [@1] Officers were carrying out routine registration and control
activities on a public road at Block 14 of the Balcones de la Villa
neighbourhood when they stopped him. [@1]

Police said the man acted suspiciously when he noticed their presence. [@1]
Officers then stopped him for verification and questioning. [@1] When officers
searched him, they found he was carrying a black plastic bag containing
marijuana. [@1]

A police summary of the case said the individual intended to sell the narcotic
substance. [@1] Officers read him his rights as a detained person and placed him
at the disposal of the competent authority to answer for his actions. [@1] The
force's Calarcá news page filed the arrest under its 'For a country without
drugs' heading. [@1]
