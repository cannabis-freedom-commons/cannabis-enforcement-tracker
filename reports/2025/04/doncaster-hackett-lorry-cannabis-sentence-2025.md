On 10 April 2025, Kirk Hackett, 30, from Dungannon, was jailed for three years
and nine months at Sheffield Crown Court after admitting possession with intent
to distribute a Class B drug. [@3] Hackett was arrested by National Crime Agency
officers after his lorry was stopped on the A1 near Doncaster, where cannabis
was found concealed beneath the trailer floor. [@2] South Yorkshire Police
searched the lorry on behalf of the NCA. [@4] Officers removed the trailer floor
and found a coil well, designed to carry cylindrical items, being used to
conceal the cannabis. [@4]

Investigators said Hackett had travelled from Larne port to Cairnryan two days
earlier and then drove to Sheffield, where he had collected the drugs. [@4] He
was heading to collect animal feed as a cover load before returning to Northern
Ireland. [@4] A search of the cab found a drill, drill bits, screws matching
those used to install the false floor, and six false number plates. [@4] The NCA
said the lorry also had false registration plates and a false insurance
certificate, making a police stop less likely. [@4]

Hackett appeared at Doncaster Magistrates' Court charged with possession with
intent to supply and was remanded in custody. [@1] Hackett pleaded guilty at
Sheffield Crown Court on 24 October 2024. [@5] The seizure formed part of an NCA
investigation into a suspected crime network based in Northern Ireland. [@1]
