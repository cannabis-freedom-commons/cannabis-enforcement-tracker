The Guardia Civil arrested three people, two of them women, in Pozoblanco during
Holy Week on suspicion of drug trafficking. [@1] Officers identified the
occupants of a car, two women, at a checkpoint for people and vehicles set up
during a public-security service at one of the entrances to the town. [@1] The
women seemed suspicious during the identity check, so officers searched the car.
[@1]

In a handbag belonging to one of the women, officers found 530 grams of cocaine
and 190 grams of hashish and seized them. [@1] The Guardia Civil said the drugs
were meant to be sold in the town during Holy Week. [@2] Both occupants of the
car were arrested on suspicion of drug trafficking. [@1] Later investigations
identified the person the drugs were to be delivered to, who was also arrested.
[@1]

The detainees, the seized drugs and the case file were placed at the disposal of
the courts, which ordered two of the three detainees to prison. [@2] The local
weekly El Quincenal de Los Pedroches gave the same figures of three arrests, 530
grams of cocaine and 190 grams of hashish. [@3]
