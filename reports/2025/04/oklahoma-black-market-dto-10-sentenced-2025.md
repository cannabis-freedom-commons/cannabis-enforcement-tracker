Ten defendants in a black-market marijuana trafficking organisation were sentenced to a
combined 463 months (more than 38 years) in federal prison after a four-year investigation
by Homeland Security Investigations. [@1,2] The drug trafficking organisation first operated
out of Michigan, growing, brokering and distributing black-market marijuana across the
country, then moved to Ada, Oklahoma in early 2022 after Michigan law enforcement actions,
running a grow there and stash houses in Oklahoma City for out-of-state shipments. [@1,2]

## What was seized and who was sentenced

Investigators seized about 3,500 pounds (about 1,588 kg) of marijuana, some 5,000 marijuana
plants, $409,883.04 in cash, real property worth $4,669,671.64 across ten properties, and
ten vehicles. [@1,2] The group laundered its proceeds through wire transfers, funnel and
straw bank accounts, vehicle purchases and cashier's checks and real estate buys. [@1]

On 23 August 2024 a federal jury found Naiqing Lin and Fei Xie guilty of drug conspiracy
after a three-day trial. [@1] At the final sentencing hearing on 29 April 2025, US District
Judge Joe Heaton sentenced Naiqing Lin, 37, to 37 months in prison plus five years of
supervised release. [@1,2] Leaders Fei Xie and Naigang Lin received 120 months each; the
other sentences ranged from four months to 46 months, with one defendant given five years of
supervised release for money laundering conspiracy. [@1] The case was investigated by
IRS Criminal Investigation, Homeland Security Investigations and the Drug Enforcement
Administration as an Organized Crime Drug Enforcement Task Force case. [@1]
