Sierra Leone Police found a cannabis farm in Kpatema Village, Kpadebu Section, Kakua Chiefdom,
Bo District, in a joint operation by the Operations Support Division and Bo West Division. [@1]

The farm had planted cannabis, old pits and seeds, which officers said showed cultivation had
been going on there for some time. [@1] The operation was led with Assistant Inspector General
Brima Kanneh, Regional Police Commander South; Superintendent Alhaji Mustapha Senesie of the
Regional Operations Office; Superintendent Jabaty, Regional Coordinator South of the OSD; and
Inspector Almamy Mansaray, head of the Transnational Organised Crime Unit (TOCU) South. [@1]

Police arrested a suspect. [@1] The TOCU head said there would be a deeper investigation and
that the suspect would be charged. [@1] AIG Kanneh thanked residents for helping the police and
said the force aimed to keep the Southern Region free of drugs. [@1]
