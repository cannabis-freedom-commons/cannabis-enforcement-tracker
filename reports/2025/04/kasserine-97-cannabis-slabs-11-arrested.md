National Guard units based in Kasserine dismantled several drug-trafficking
networks in a series of operations across several regions. [@1] Officers
arrested 11 suspects during those operations, a group that included foreign
nationals. [@1] The operations were directed by the central anti-drug brigade
based in Ben Arous. [@1] They were carried out in Greater Tunis, in Monastir, in
Sousse and in Kasserine. [@1] One of the networks operated internationally, with
part of it operating from abroad. [@1]

Officers seized 9.7 kilograms of cannabis, corresponding to 97 slabs, alongside
3.6 kilograms of cocaine. [@1] They also seized sums of money and four vehicles
that had been used to transport the drugs. [@1]

The Directorate General of the National Guard announced the results in a
statement published on Tuesday 1 April 2025. [@1] That statement described the
seizures as significant quantities of cocaine and cannabis from the dismantled
networks. [@1] The 9.7-kilogram cannabis amount stands separate from the cocaine
amount and forms the cannabis component of this mixed seizure. [@1]
