The outlet reported the haul as more than 300 kg of Indian hemp. [@1]

His companion, who apparently could not swim, was arrested at the landing. [@1]
The man then tried to ride off on a motorcycle parked on the bank, but officers
led by the commissioner stopped him. [@1] He was arrested with the cannabis
cargo and taken to the police station. [@1]

He was handed to the courts and was expected to appear before Criet, Benin's
court for economic crimes and terrorism, in the following days. [@1]

Days earlier about fifteen bags of soybeans bound for Niger had been confiscated
in the same area. [@1] Authorities restated their commitment to tighten
surveillance of crossing points and to disrupt smuggling routes. [@1]
