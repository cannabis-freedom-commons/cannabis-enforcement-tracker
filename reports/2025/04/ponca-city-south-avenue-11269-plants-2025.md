Oklahoma's Organized Crime Task Force and partner agencies raided a marijuana grow facility at 3880 W South Avenue in Ponca City on 9 April 2025, arresting five people and confiscating more than 11,000 illegal plants. [@1,2]

The sweep yielded 11,269 plants and 477 pounds (about 216.4 kg) of processed marijuana. [@1,2] The raid was a joint effort of the task force with the Kay County Sheriff's Office, Ponca City police, the district attorney's drug task force, the Oklahoma National Guard, the FBI and immigration officers. [@1,2]

Investigators said the site showed no sign of legal operation: it held no active medical-marijuana licence, no narcotics-bureau registration and no legally tagged plants. [@1,2] Attorney General Gentner Drummond said the marijuana was likely headed for the black market. [@1,2]

Those arrested, aged 37 to 62, were each charged with aggravated manufacturing of marijuana and trafficking a controlled substance. [@1,2] One of them was found with clothing resembling a Chinese People's Liberation Army uniform. [@1]
