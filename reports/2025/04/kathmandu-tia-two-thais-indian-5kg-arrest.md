Nepal Police's Narcotics Control Bureau arrested three foreign nationals at
Tribhuvan International Airport in Kathmandu: two Thai women and one Indian man.
[@1] Narcotics Control Bureau said the contraband was brought to Kathmandu on
Thai Airways flight TG319 from Bangkok, which landed at the airport around 1 pm
on Tuesday. [@1] Officers found the cannabis with a 27-year-old Thai national as
she approached the X-ray machine in the arrival area's red channel, Narcotics
Control Bureau spokesperson SP Narayan Prasad Adhikari said. [@1]

Police put the load at 5 kilograms and 200 grams of marijuana. [@2] The two
other suspects were identified as a 24-year-old Thai woman and a 31-year-old
Indian man. [@1] Police said further investigation showed the two others were
involved in the same smuggling arrangement and were then arrested. [@2]

Police believe the main handler was based in Thailand, with Thai travellers
carrying cannabis into Nepal and Indian nationals moving it on to cities in
India. [@1] The group is also suspected of using Nepal-India routes to send
drugs to Gulf countries in the past. [@1]

Republica reported two days earlier that the 27-year-old Thai woman had been
arrested at the customs area with six kilograms of marijuana in her luggage and
handed to the Narcotics Control Bureau in Koteshwor. [@3]
