Paraguay's National Anti-Drug Secretariat (Senad) and Brazil's Federal Police destroyed 253 hectares of cannabis plantations in a ten-day joint effort, the 49th phase of Operation New Alliance on the two countries' border. [@1] Officers also seized and destroyed more than 100.1 tonnes of harvested marijuana, and dismantled over 170 pressing camps along with seed stocks. [@1]

Senad said the operation worked through Itapopó, María Auxiliadora, Alpasa and Ñe'ã, a hard-to-reach zone on the border with Brazil's Mato Grosso do Sul state, where Brazilian criminal groups fund many of the farms and send the crop to Brazilian cities. [@1] Counting both the cleared fields and the harvested drug, Senad estimated the phase kept 859 tonnes of marijuana out of circulation. [@1]

Senad valued the blow at about USD 25.7 million at Paraguayan prices and USD 128 million at Brazilian market prices. [@1]
