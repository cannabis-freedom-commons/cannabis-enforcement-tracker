Examining the load, the officers noticed a suspicious structure in X-ray images
produced by a scanner. [@1,3] The suspect crate was unloaded and opened for a
closer check, confirming that it held a large quantity of marijuana. [@1] The
lorry was registered abroad and in transit through Luxembourg, and it was
carrying 42 kilos of marijuana, customs announced on the Thursday. [@2] The
customs administration announced the find in a statement issued on 24 April.
[@1]

To make sure no other drugs were in the rest of the load, officers called in
Balto, a drug-detection dog. [@1,3] The dog indicated another crate, in which four
kilos of marijuana were found. [@1,3] In total, 42 kg of cannabis were seized.
[@1,3]

A detailed report was drawn up and the Luxembourg public prosecutor was informed
of the discovery. [@1,3] The prosecutor ordered the drugs confiscated. [@1,3]
