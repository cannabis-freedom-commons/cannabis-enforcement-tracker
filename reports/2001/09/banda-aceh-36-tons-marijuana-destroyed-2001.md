Police in Aceh province destroyed some 3.6 tons of dried marijuana on Saturday, evidence seized in drug raids between 1996 and 2001.[@1] The destruction took place in Banda Aceh, Aceh.[@1] Comr. Laksa Widiyana said the evidence had been collected in Pidie, Sabang, East Aceh, North Aceh, Southeast Aceh and South Aceh.[@1]

Widiyana said much more marijuana could not be destroyed because it was linked to cases still under investigation or being tried.[@1] He said Aceh Police were trying to break the cycle of narcotics circulation in the province.[@1] He added that marijuana grows well in Aceh and that security officers would strive to destroy the plants so the young generation would not be poisoned by drugs.[@1]
