Ashok Kumar Giri, 45, was sentenced to death for trafficking five blocks of marijuana weighing 2.37 kilograms, his lawyer S.S. Dhillon said after the High Court verdict. [@1] Mohamed Abdul Nasser Mohamood, arrested with him, was acquitted. [@1]

Both men had been jointly tried over five parcels of cannabis weighing 2,370.5 grams nett, which Giri delivered to Nasser in a car at the slip road of Aljunied Road leading to the MRT station at about 11.30 am on 23 October 2000. [@2] Central Narcotics Bureau officers watching the scene arrested Nasser as he carried the plastic bag toward the station and seized Giri before he could drive away. [@2,3]

## Trial and appeal

The High Court acquitted Nasser but convicted Giri and imposed the mandatory death sentence. [@2,3] Giri appealed, arguing among other things that his statements were wrongly admitted, but the Court of Appeal dismissed the appeal on 3 September 2001. [@2]
