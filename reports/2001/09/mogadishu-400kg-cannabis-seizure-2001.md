Mogadishu's police chief said his officers had seized over 400 kg of cannabis in the city
over the previous two weeks and arrested about a dozen people on drug charges. [@1] The
seizures came out of special anti-crime operations launched a month earlier, with most of
the cannabis found in Hamar Jajab and Hodan in south Mogadishu and Jaaqshiid in the
north. [@1]

Chief Abdi Hasan Awale Qeybdid said drug abuse and trafficking had grown so rampant that
he had set up a special task force to fight the menace. [@1] He blamed much of the city's
crime on drug abuse, saying many rapes were directly related to it. [@1]

## What happened next

Qeybdid said those arrested would be brought to court soon. [@1] He appealed to
international aid agencies working in Somalia to help with training, drugs education and
equipment, saying the police needed assistance to succeed. [@1]
