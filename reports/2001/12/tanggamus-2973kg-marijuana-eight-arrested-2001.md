## The seizure

Lampung police arrested eight suspects as they tried to load marijuana onto a military truck in Sumber Agung Pekon Margomulya village, Tanggamus regency, on 9 December 2001.[@1] The confiscated marijuana totalled 2,973 kilograms and was held at the Tanggamus police precinct.[@1] Police valued the haul at Rp6 billion, about US$600,000.[@1,2]

## Who was held

Those arrested included two servicemen and a civilian staff member from the Indonesian military headquarters in Cilangkap, Jakarta, alongside a truck driver and other civilians from Tangerang, Jakarta, North Aceh and Tanggamus.[@1] The two servicemen were to be handed to the military police for further investigation.[@1,2]

## The route

Police said the suspects tried to smuggle the marijuana from restive Aceh province to Java island by truck.[@1,2] Lampung's governor urged police to destroy most of the seized marijuana at once, keeping only samples for investigation, lest it be diverted.[@1] Police in Jambi had also confiscated hundreds of kilograms of marijuana from Aceh and arrested dozens of suspects in the months before.[@1]
