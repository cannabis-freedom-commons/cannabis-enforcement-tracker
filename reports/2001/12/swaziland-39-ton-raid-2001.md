In 2001, police in Swaziland seized 3.9 tons of compressed cannabis in a single raid. [@1,2] Police believed the consignment was destined for export to the United Kingdom. [@1,2]

The Institute for Security Studies put the cargo's British street value at about 15.6 million pounds, while IRIN later reported it as over 20 million US dollars. [@1,2] The best-quality Swazi harvest was typically compressed into one- or two-kilogram blocks and smuggled via South Africa to Europe and the UK. [@1]
