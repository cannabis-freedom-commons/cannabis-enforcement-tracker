U.S. Customs agents seized 6,310 pounds (2,860 kg) of marijuana from a Panama-flagged cargo ship
entering the Port of Miami, agency officials said on 6 July 2001. [@1] Federal agents found the
drug late on Tuesday 3 July in 111 white plastic bags stacked at the rear of a container that was
carrying medical supplies and machinery. [@1]

Customs said the marijuana was worth about $6 million at wholesale prices. [@1] The vessel had
stopped in Jamaica before sailing to Miami from Colón, Panama. [@1] No one was charged in connection
with the seizure, which the spokeswoman said was the agency’s largest at the seaport so far that
year. [@1]
