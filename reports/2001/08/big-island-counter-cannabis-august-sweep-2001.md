Hilo vice officers confiscated 14,927 marijuana plants ranging from seedlings to twelve feet tall during a five-day operation that ended Friday 24 August 2001. [@1] Big Island police were joined in the operation by members of the Statewide Marijuana Eradication Task Force. [@1]

The sweep covered the Hamakua, North Hilo, South Hilo, Puna and Ka'u districts, concentrating on Honomu, Pepeekeo, Pahoa, Fern Forest, Leilani Estates, Hawaiian Paradise Park, Keaau, Orchidland, Ainaloa, Kukuihaele, Paauilo, Ninole and Ookala, among other areas. [@1]

The operation was part of the department's continuing Counter-Cannabis Field Operation. [@1] No incidents were reported in the operation, and no arrests were made. [@1]
