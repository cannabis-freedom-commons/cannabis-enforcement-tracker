A South African Police Service briefing to parliament's safety and security committees on 30 May 2001 listed a cannabis eradication operation in Swaziland that month, covering 108 hectares valued at R7.5 million. [@1]

Most Swazi cannabis was grown in the north of the country, which is steep and hilly and not easily accessible by road, and joint operations with South African helicopter support located plantations there for destruction. [@2]
