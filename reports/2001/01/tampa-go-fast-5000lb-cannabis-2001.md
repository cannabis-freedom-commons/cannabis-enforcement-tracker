On 10 January 2001 the Portsmouth-based cutter Tampa and three other Coast Guard boats ended a nine-hour chase by seizing a go-fast boat in the southeastern Bahamas carrying 5,000 pounds (about 2,268 kg) of marijuana and 300 pounds of hashish oil. [@1,2] Officers fired on the speeding boat and arrested three men. [@1]

The Virginian-Pilot described the chase as unfolding near Cuba, with the Tampa joined by the Florida-based cutters Monhegan, Matagorda and Padre. [@1,2] Tampa's commanding officer, Cmdr. Jim Howe, presented the joint surface-and-air effort as a new "Wolf Pack" tactic of hunting smugglers with several units working together. [@1]
