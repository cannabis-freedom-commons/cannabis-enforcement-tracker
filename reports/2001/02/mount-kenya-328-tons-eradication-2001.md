In February 2001 Kenyan law enforcement officers destroyed more than 328 tons
of cannabis grown in the area of Mount Kenya. [@1] A later United Nations
review described the 2001 and 2002 operations as highly publicized, successful
targeted raids on 14 farms around the mountain, which together destroyed 461
tons of cannabis. [@2]

## Plantations inside the forest belt

Fairly large-scale cannabis cultivation took place in the central highlands
around Mount Kenya, with as much as 1,500 hectares estimated in the area. [@2]
Some grew in the lower farmlands concealed among traditional crops, and smaller
plots lay higher up in areas regarded as national wildlife reserve. [@2] Despite
the 2001 and 2002 raids, police noted that cultivation had increased again when
they ran targeted raids in 2004. [@2]
