Simon Withers and Wayne Green were each jailed for four years at Manchester Crown
Court after admitting turning a small flat in Northern Moor into a cannabis
nursery. [@1] Police found heating, watering and ventilation systems in the
Ossington Court flat and estimated the potential yield of the seized plants at more
than £12,000. [@1] The judge said the equipment had been installed with skill and
expertise, and that anyone setting up such a nursery had to expect a sentence
reflecting society's disapproval of the drug. [@1]

Withers, 30, of Epsom Avenue, Sale, and Green, 31, of Barrack Street, Hulme, had
pleaded guilty at the first opportunity to cultivating cannabis and possessing it
with intent to supply. [@1] They were arrested after police saw them arrive at the
flat minutes after raiding it. [@1] Their solicitor said both men were cannabis users
who had planned to supply known users and had not realised the seriousness of what
they were doing. [@1]
