Wild cannabis plants were found growing over about 15 acres (about six hectares) in the Shantivan and Shaktisthal areas of Delhi on 22 and 23 March 2001. [@1] The Narcotics Control Bureau destroyed the plants. [@1]

The details come from the government's written answer to Unstarred Question No. 6096 in the Lok Sabha, answered on 27 April 2001 by Minister of State for Finance Gingee N. Ramachandran. [@1] Asked who was responsible and what punishment was proposed, the minister replied that the growth was wild, so responsibility could not be fixed on any individual. [@1]
