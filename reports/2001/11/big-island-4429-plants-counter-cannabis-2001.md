Big Island vice officers with the Statewide Marijuana Eradication Task Force confiscated 4,429 marijuana plants during a five-day operation that ended Friday 9 November 2001. [@1,2] The plants ranged in height from seedlings to three feet tall. [@2]

The sweep covered the Hamakua, North Hilo, South Hilo, Puna and Ka'u districts and was part of the department's continuing Counter-Cannabis Field Operation. [@1,2] Neither account reports any arrests. [@1,2]
