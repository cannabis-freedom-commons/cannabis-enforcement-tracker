Spain's Guardia Civil intercepted a lorry from Morocco at Algeciras Port in Cádiz carrying more than 32 tonnes of hashish, the largest amount of drugs ever seized in one bust on dry land. [@1] The 32,376 kilos of hashish resin had been hidden amongst a cargo of melons, and police valued the haul at 50 million euros on the street. [@1,2]

The melons were purely cover: shipped from the port of Tangiers, they occupied only the first two rows of pallets while the rest of the space held hashish. [@2] The lorry was to take the drugs to Perpignan, just over the French border, and the police operation was named Operación Melonar. [@1] The Moroccan lorry driver faced a long time behind bars given the sheer quantity involved. [@1]

The Gazette noted a larger 1996 bust of 36 tonnes from a Panamanian ship off Marín in Galicia, with 17 arrests, but that seizure was made at sea. [@1]
