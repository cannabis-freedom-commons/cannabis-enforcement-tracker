Birmingham Crown Court jailed Vietnam-born Tung Vu, 34, for two years and
eight months for producing cannabis. [@1] The former economics student admitted
the charge after telling police he thought he had been looking after Chinese
herbal plants. [@1]

Officers knocked on his door in Grassmere Road, Handsworth, on 3 January 2012
while making enquiries about an unrelated robbery and were struck by the smell
of cannabis. [@1] Inside they found almost 230 plants, capable of producing
harvests worth up to £400,000 a year, growing in a bedroom, while a second
bedroom and the loft were being converted to grow more. [@1]

Passing sentence, the judge said one harvest alone would be valued at
£102,600, with an annual yield of between £300,000 and £400,000, and told Vu
that there were 228 plants and he must have known their significance. [@1]
Police also found his belongings with plane tickets and £120 in cash, along
with growth accelerant, face masks and gloves. [@1] Vu said he had been in
Birmingham only a few days after travelling from Bedford with another
Vietnamese man. [@1]
