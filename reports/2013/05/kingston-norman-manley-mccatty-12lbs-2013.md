Police charged Orville McCatty with possession of marijuana and with attempting
to export an illegal substance after a search of his luggage at Norman Manley
International Airport in Kingston. [@1] Investigators from the Transnational
Crimes and Narcotics Division seized 12.1 pounds (about 5.49 kg) of marijuana
from the passenger's luggage at the airport on 9 May 2013. [@1]

The man was taken into custody over the seizure and was charged afterwards. [@1]
Police said McCatty was checking in for an Insel Airline flight bound for
Curacao when officers searched his luggage, including his laptop bag, and found
the marijuana. [@1] The police valued the marijuana at J$36,000. [@1]
