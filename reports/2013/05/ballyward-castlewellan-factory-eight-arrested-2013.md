Police searched 11 locations across Northern Ireland on Tuesday 21 May 2013 in an operation against a suspected organised crime gang, and found a concealed cannabis factory in rural Ballyward near Castlewellan. [@1,2] Officers arrested seven men and a woman in the raids; five remained in custody while the other three were released on police bail. [@1] Police estimated the street value of the drugs at £350,000. [@1,2]

Officers also seized seven vehicles valued at more than £100,000, including two BMWs and a Mercedes. [@1,2] The second outlet confirms two BMWs and a Mercedes were taken alongside the £350,000 drugs find at the County Down factory. [@2]
