Canterbury Crown Court sentenced Seamus Keenan, a 48-year-old lorry driver from Newry, County Down, to three years in prison on 20 May 2013 for trying to smuggle about 107 kilograms of cannabis resin into Britain. [@1,2] Keenan had pleaded guilty at the same court in July 2012. [@1]

Border Force officers stopped his Irish-registered lorry at Dover's Eastern Docks on 31 January 2011 and scanned its load of a dump truck and excavator spare parts including a counterweight. [@1,2] On closer examination they found the drugs hidden inside the counterweight. [@1,2] The haul had an estimated potential street value of more than 300,000 pounds. [@1,2]

Malcolm Bragg of Border Force said the force was at the forefront of stopping drugs entering the country and was determined to protect society from activity with a destructive impact on local communities. [@1]
