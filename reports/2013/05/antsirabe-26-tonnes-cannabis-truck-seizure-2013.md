Police in Antsirabe stopped a lorry carrying cannabis in the night of Sunday 5
May 2013 and seized nearly 2.6 tonnes of the drug. [@1]

The 172 sacks of cannabis were hidden among 10 sacks of charcoal and 13 sacks of
dried grain. [@1] LINFO.re reported that the consignment was spread over about a
hundred sacks and quoted the FIP commander, Pascal Randriamahasoa, on the sacks
of charcoal and grain. [@2] The cannabis had been dried and packed at 15
kilograms a sack, according to the FIP commander. [@2] The police said the load
was meant to supply the market of the capital, Antananarivo. [@1]

Two people in the lorry were arrested when police intervened, and a third fled.
[@1] LINFO.re reported that three people, including the lorry driver, were
detained, and that a fourth suspect escaped on foot. [@2]
