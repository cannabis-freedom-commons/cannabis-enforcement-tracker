Officers of the Georgian Revenue Service Customs Department and the patrol
police detained Armenian citizens at the Sadakhlo checkpoint on the
Georgian-Armenian border after finding drugs on them. [@1] A search of the first
detainee, identified only as G. A., found marijuana in a plastic bag in his left
trouser pocket, which he said he had bought in Armenia. [@1] Officers also found
three Subutex tablets and 13 Lyrica tablets in G. A.'s luggage. [@2] G. A. told
officers that a second Armenian citizen, I. A., had arrived in Georgia with him.
[@1]

Officers found a paper-wrapped white tablet marked Subutex in I. A.'s right
sock. [@1] The Revenue Service press service reported the detentions on Tuesday,
7 May 2013, which is used as the incident date. [@1] All case materials were
handed to the patrol police for further action. [@2]
