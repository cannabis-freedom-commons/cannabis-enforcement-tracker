Spanish police found 52 tonnes of hashish in an industrial workshop on a trading estate in Córdoba and arrested three Spanish men. [@1] The drug was packed in 1,700 parcels weighing 30 kilos each, with an estimated street value of 80 million euros. [@1] Police called it the biggest hashish seizure ever made in Spain and a European record. [@1,2]

## How the raid happened

The police acted on the account of a lorry driver who had arrived at the premises to return a rented forklift truck and found himself bound and gagged, with two other men held the same way and armed men searching the store for the drug. [@1] When officers reached the building, its main door was blocked from the inside with concrete blocks; once inside, they found the parcels. [@1] The Gazette notes its account is reconstructed from a Spanish original it describes as sketchy. [@1]

The same spring brought another supersized find: 32 tonnes of hashish in a truck carrying melons from Morocco. [@2]
