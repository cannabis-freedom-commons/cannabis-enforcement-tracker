Waitemata police executed a search warrant on the morning of 3 July 2013 at an industrial premises on Parkhead Place, Albany, as part of an ongoing investigation. [@1] Inside they found a sophisticated cannabis growing operation of at least 1,100 plants at various stages of maturity, plus a further 1,200 seedlings. [@1]

The operation used 24,000 litres of water and between $5,000 and $7,000 worth of electricity each month, the field crime manager said. [@1] One man was assisting police with their enquiries. [@1]
