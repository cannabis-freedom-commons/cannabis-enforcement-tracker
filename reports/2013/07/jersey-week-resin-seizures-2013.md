On Friday 5 July 2013, Jersey Customs and Immigration officers found almost 40 kg of cannabis resin concealed in a car that had arrived on the ferry from Poole, and a 27-year-old man was charged with drug trafficking offences over that seizure. [@1,2] On Tuesday 9 July, after a separate long-term operation, officers found 72 kg of cannabis resin in a commercial vehicle in St Lawrence, and a 26-year-old man was charged with drug trafficking offences while enquiries continued. [@1,2]

The two seizures had a combined street value of more than £1.3 million. [@1,2] David Nurse, the Director of Law Enforcement for Customs and Immigration, said officers had worked hard to bring both operations to a successful conclusion and that the seizures had seriously disrupted syndicates trying to import commercial quantities of drugs into the island. [@1]
