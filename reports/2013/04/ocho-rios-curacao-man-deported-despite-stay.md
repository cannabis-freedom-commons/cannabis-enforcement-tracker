Shurendy Quant, a Curacao man, was detained in Ocho Rios, St. Ann, while in a
taxi transporting ganja. [@1] He was detained with the driver and two other
passengers; the driver was charged with possession of ganja and later released,
while the two other passengers were released without charge. [@1] Quant was
detained without any reason being given. [@1]

His lawyers, Carolyn Reid and Chuck Cameron, made a habeas corpus application
seeking his release, and the matter was adjourned until Monday, April 8. [@1] A
deportation order was presented to the court, and an application for a stay was
subsequently filed in the Supreme Court. [@1] On Wednesday the authorities
consented to the stay, but despite this Quant was deported on Thursday. [@1]

The legal team is heading to court Friday to seek judicial review and determine
who will be held responsible for the flagrantly disobeyed court order. [@1]
