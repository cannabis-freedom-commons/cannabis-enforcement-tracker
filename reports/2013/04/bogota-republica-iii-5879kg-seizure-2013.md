Police seized 5,879 kg of marijuana and captured six people in three operations in different parts of Bogotá on Tuesday 23 April 2013. [@1,2] Police called it the biggest confiscation of marijuana in the capital in ten years and valued the load at more than a million dollars on the street. [@1,2]

The largest find was 3,821 kg hidden between thick walls in a warehouse in Bosa, where two men were captured. [@2] In Engativá police stopped a truck carrying 1,808 kg and captured its driver, and in Usaquén in the north they took 250 kg from a vehicle and captured its three occupants. [@2] Colombia Reports, citing the police report, describes the raids as targeting micro-trafficking structures in the Bronx area under Operación República III. [@1]

Police said the marijuana came from the violent department of Cauca, where the FARC guerrillas are strong and allegedly owned the load, and that it was meant for places selling in small retail quantities. [@2] The operations followed President Juan Manuel Santos's 1 April order to wipe out 24 big open drug markets, known as ollas, in 20 cities within 60 days. [@2]
