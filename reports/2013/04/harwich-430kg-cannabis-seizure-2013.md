Border Force officers seized about 430 kg of cannabis at the Port of Harwich
shortly after 5.30am on Thursday 4 April 2013. [@1,2] The drug was found in the
trailer of a lorry that had arrived at the port on a ferry from the Hook of
Holland. [@1,2]

Border Force valued the seizure at more than £1.7 million. [@1,2] Two Dutch
men, aged 72 and 48, were arrested on suspicion of illegally importing a Class B
drug and were bailed until 13 May while investigations continued. [@1,2]
Charlotte Mann of Border Force said the seizure had stopped a sizeable amount of
cannabis reaching the streets of Britain. [@1,2]
