Galway's Divisional Drug Unit, assisted by the Regional Support Unit and local
gardaí, searched a business premises in the Tuam district on the morning of 20
April 2013 as part of Operation Nitrogen, the Garda operation targeting cannabis
growing in the west of Ireland. [@1,3] They uncovered a sophisticated cannabis
growing operation with about 3,000 plants at various stages of growth, plus
growing paraphernalia, valued at an estimated €2.5 million pending analysis. [@1,3]

## Four held

Two men in their mid-40s and early 50s were detained at Mill Street Garda
station in Galway under section 2 of the Criminal Justice (Drug Trafficking) Act
1996. [@1,3] As the investigation continued, Galway drugs officers arrested two
more men in their 30s in the Tuam area later the same day, while the first two
remained in detention. [@2,3] All four were held at Mill Street station, and
Gardaí said photographs of the seized grow would be made available. [@1,2,3]
