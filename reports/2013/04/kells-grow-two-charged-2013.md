In an intelligence-led operation, the Kells Garda drugs unit assisted by the
Regional Support Unit searched a business premises in the Kells area on the night
of 19 April 2013. [@1] They discovered a large cannabis cultivation operation of
plants and herb, subject to analysis, with an estimated street value of €1.5
million. [@1,3]

## Two arrested

A 53-year-old man and a 43-year-old woman were arrested at the scene and detained
at Kells Garda station under section 2 of the Criminal Justice (Drug
Trafficking) Act 1996. [@1,3] Both were expected to appear before Ardee District
Court on the morning of 22 April 2013. [@2] The Irish Times reported the Kells
seizure alongside a separate €2.5 million grow uncovered in Tuam the next day, a
different case recorded on its own. [@3]
