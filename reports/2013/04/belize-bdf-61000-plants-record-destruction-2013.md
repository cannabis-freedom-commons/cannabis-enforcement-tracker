Twenty-four Belize Defence Force soldiers and police officers destroyed a record
quantity of marijuana plants in a two-day operation. [@1] A BDF release said the
teams destroyed more than 61,000 plants, 220 pounds of compressed marijuana and
10 pounds of marijuana seeds. [@1]

The operation was concentrated in the southwestern Orange Walk District and the
Cayo District. [@1] According to the BDF, the marijuana in its various forms had
a combined street value of 12.5 million Belize dollars. [@1] The BDF said those
61,000 plants destroyed in 48 hours were twice the 30,000 destroyed in all of
the previous year and 50 per cent more than the 41,000 destroyed in 2011. [@1]

The operation had air support from the US Southern Command, with Joint Task
Force Bravo assisting and US soldiers seen in Chinook helicopters in footage the
BDF provided. [@1] The BDF attributed the result to improved information sharing
and enhanced intelligence gathering. [@1]
