Between 19 and 21 August 2013, four police services from across southwestern Ontario and the Canadian Armed Forces worked together in a multi-jurisdictional cannabis eradication programme. [@1] The project, dubbed O-SABOT, drew on the RCMP Kitchener, Toronto West and Toronto North detachments, the Canadian Armed Forces, the Ontario Provincial Police, the West Grey Police Service and the Hanover Police Service. [@1] Officers shared information and intelligence to identify outdoor grow sites, then seized and destroyed close to 2,700 cannabis plants. [@1]

## Part of a national programme

Project O-SABOT is the Ontario portion of the Canada-wide eradication programme Operation SABOT, aimed at cutting the supply of cannabis to communities. [@1] The release presents the joint operation as part of the RCMP Marihuana Grow Initiative launched in September 2011 to counter production run by organised crime groups. [@1] The release says the group effort required the services to share information and intelligence in order to identify the grow sites. [@1] It calls the joint operation an example of the collaboration envisioned under that Initiative and a needed step in building a coordinated, collective response across the country. [@1]
