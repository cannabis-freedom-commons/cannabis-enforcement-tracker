Police arrested and charged three men in connection with a cannabis seizure in
Layou on 25 August 2013. [@1] The three were Ezra Edmond and Ezlee Edmond of
Layou and Tony King of St. Joseph. [@1]

During the police operation in Layou, officers seized 2,336 grams of cured
marijuana, 438 grams of marijuana seeds and 2,184 marijuana trees. [@1] Online
News From Dominica republished the Dominica News Online account on 27 August
2013 under the headline 'Three charged for marijuana'. [@2]
