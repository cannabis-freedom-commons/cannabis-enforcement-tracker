Two people aged 51 and 26, both residents of Almayate, were arrested on suspicion of crimes against public health after a raid on a marijuana plantation in Archidona. [@1] Officers confiscated 2,072 marijuana plants, most of them over a metre tall, which had been camouflaged with sweet corn. [@1] The plantation lay in an area described as quite difficult to access. [@1]
