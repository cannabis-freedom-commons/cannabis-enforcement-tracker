Phoenix Suns forward Michael Beasley was arrested early on 6 August 2013 after
Scottsdale police stopped his car on a traffic violation near Scottsdale and
McCormick roads. [@2] The officer smelled marijuana coming from the car and a
search of the driver's area recovered three cigarettes suspected to contain
marijuana, described in another account as three joints. [@1]

Scottsdale police said the marijuana was impounded, Beasley was booked and he
was released from custody. [@2] He was released after being cited for
investigation of one felony count each of marijuana possession and possession of
drug paraphernalia. [@1] Police said they would await laboratory results before
formal charges were filed. [@3]

Phoenix New Times noted that marijuana possession is a felony in Arizona, which
made the stop his most serious run-in with police. [@2] The Phoenix Suns said
they had no comment on the arrest. [@3] USA TODAY Sports reported that Beasley
was arrested on suspicion of marijuana possession and released from custody.
