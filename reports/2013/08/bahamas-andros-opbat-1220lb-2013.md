Bahamian police arrested nine suspects after 1,220 pounds (about 553 kg) of
marijuana was found on Andros Island and flown to Nassau. [@1] A joint task
force from Operation Bahamas, Turks and Caicos, known as OPBAT, seized the
marijuana in the vicinity of Andros Island. [@1] The Coast Guard put the
wholesale value of the haul at an estimated $1.1 million. [@1]

The task force included the U.S. Drug Enforcement Administration, the U.S. Coast
Guard, the Bahamas Drug Enforcement Unit and the Royal Bahamas Police Force.
[@1] A Coast Guard aircrew stationed at the Atlantic Undersea Test and
Evaluation Center, the U.S. Navy's AUTEC facility on Andros, spotted the stash
from an MH-60T Jayhawk helicopter. [@1] The aircrew guided investigators to a
remote site in the island's mangroves. [@1] The Jayhawk crew then hoisted the
marijuana and carried it to Bahamian law enforcement in Nassau, on New
Providence Island. [@1] The seizure took place on 28 August 2013, a Wednesday.
[@2]

Commander Mark Driver, the Coast Guard's OPBAT director, said the seizure
prevented nearly $1.1 million of illegal narcotics from harming Bahamian
citizens or crossing international borders. [@1] OPBAT is a partnership of the
DEA, the Coast Guard and the government of the Bahamas against drug smuggling to
and from the Bahamas. [@1]
