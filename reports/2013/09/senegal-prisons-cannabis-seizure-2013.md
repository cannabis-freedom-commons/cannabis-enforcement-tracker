Senegalese Justice Minister Sidiki Kaba gave a mid-term review of an operation
to secure detention facilities across Senegal. [@1] Searches of detainees’ cells
found bladed weapons, mobile phones and cannabis, called chanvre indien in the
source. [@1] The investigation findings he cited said detainees held nearly 250
mobile phones along with cannabis. [@1] Bladed weapons were seized especially in
remand and correctional facilities in the Dakar region. [@1]

Kaba called the findings unacceptable and promised to remedy them. [@1] He said
the new authorities launched the security drive after learning of practices that
endangered some detainees. [@1] He described detention conditions as deplorable
and said authorities were accelerating the sale of Rebeuss prison for transfer
to Sébikotane, 40 km from Dakar. [@1] He said any act harming human dignity must
stop in prisons. [@1]

He linked the push to bring prisons up to international standards to an inquiry
into alleged mistreatment of detainees at the Camp pénal de Liberté 6 in Dakar.
[@1] Senegalese rights groups had separately reported alleged harassment, abuse
and violence against people held at Liberté 6. [@1]
