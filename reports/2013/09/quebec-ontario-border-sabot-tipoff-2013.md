While patrolling the border area during their September 2013 eradication
operation, Ontario officers told the Sûreté du Québec about another substantial
grow operation along the Quebec-Ontario border. [@1] Quebec officers eradicated
2,960 plants there and seized 20 kg of hash and 41 kg of cannabis buds, and
charges were laid. [@1] The Quebec operation grew out of the same week's
Ontario patrols, in which the Cornwall force seized 7,547 plants across four
eastern Ontario counties. [@1]
