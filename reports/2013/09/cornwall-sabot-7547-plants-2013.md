Between 16 and 20 September 2013, the Cornwall Regional Task Force (CRTF) with
the Canadian Armed Forces (CAF), the Ontario Provincial Police (OPP) and the
Cornwall Community Police Service ran a cannabis eradication operation across
Prescott-Russell, Stormont, Dundas and Glengarry counties. [@1] Officers visited
several sites and seized 7,547 cannabis plants in what the task force presented
as its share of the yearly Canada-wide Operation SABOT. [@1] Officers arrested
one man who tried to flee as police approached, with charges pending further
investigation. [@1]

## A national programme

Operation SABOT is a yearly Canada-wide eradication programme run with the
RCMP, the armed forces and provincial and municipal police, using aerial patrols
and tips from residents to find outdoor grow operations before harvest. [@1]
The task force's Inspector Tim Kimpan said the seizures would leave less
cannabis for sale in local communities. [@1] The release thanks the OPP drug
unit, the Sûreté du Québec, the Cornwall police, CAF elements and the RCMP's
regional criminal intelligence section for their help. [@1]
