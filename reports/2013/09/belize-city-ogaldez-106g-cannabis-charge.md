Police officers visited a residence at #22 Allan Pitts Crescent in Belize City
on the morning of Tuesday, September 3, 2013, to conduct a search for illegal
firearms. [@1] No illegal firearms were discovered on the premises, inside the
house, or on the person of the resident during the inspection. [@1] During the
search of the house, officers instead discovered suspected cannabis concealed
inside a pillowcase. [@1] When weighed by police, the seized plant material
amounted to 106 grams. [@1]

Resident Barbara Ogaldez, 38, was formally charged with drug trafficking when
she appeared before Magistrate Leslie Hamilton that afternoon. [@1] Ogaldez
entered a plea of not guilty to the drug trafficking charge. [@1] Court records
examined during the arraignment revealed that Ogaldez had an outstanding balance
of $330 owed to the court. [@1] Magistrate Hamilton exercised his discretion and
granted Ogaldez until Monday, September 2, to pay half of that outstanding fine.
[@1]

The court offered her bail in the amount of $1,000 along with one surety of the
same amount. [@1] Because she was unable to meet the bail conditions, Ogaldez
was remanded to the Belize Central Prison. [@1] She was ordered to return to the
magistrate's court for her next hearing on October 18, 2013. [@1]
