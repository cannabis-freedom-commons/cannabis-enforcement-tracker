On the morning of 9 September 2013, Humboldt County sheriff's deputies assisted
by CalFire, the California Department of Fish and Wildlife and the county drug
task force served a Superior Court search warrant on a 160-acre parcel off State
Route Highway 36 near mile marker 31.54 at Bridgeville. [@1,2] Deputies
detained twelve suspects across the property while a thirteenth fled into the
brush, and hours later officers recaptured the fugitive, identified as Yul
Cashman, with a police dog after he ignored orders to surrender. [@1]

In a 60-by-30-foot shop with living quarters used to process and dry marijuana,
officers found about 375 pounds (about 170 kg) of hanging harvested marijuana,
and in four 40-by-80-foot greenhouses they found 685 budding plants six to
seven feet tall, all of which were removed. [@1,2] Officers also seized a
semiautomatic 9 mm handgun from a suspect's vehicle, $10,422 in cash, scales
and packaging material indicating sales for profit. [@1,2]

Officers reported major environmental damage, including clearcut trees, illegal
roads cut into the hillside and a pump draining Little Larabee Creek dry
downstream, a federally protected steelhead stream. [@1,2] Thirteen people were
arrested at the first site on cultivation and possession-for-sale charges, with
several also held on immigration and customs holds, and Cashman was additionally
charged with resisting arrest after the dog bite. [@1]

While processing the first site, officers found a second grow on an adjoining
parcel, obtained a second warrant, and at about 1 p.m. seized 1,026 plants one
to five feet tall from three greenhouses along with ammunition and further
environmental violations including raw sewage draining into the creek. [@1]
Three more suspects were arrested there, including Michael Kenneth Metelits, 42,
of Fortuna, who faced additional streambed-alteration, water-pollution and
timber charges and was reported wanted in Virginia for shipping marijuana from
Fortuna. [@1]
