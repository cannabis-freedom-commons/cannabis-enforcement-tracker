Italy's Guardia di Finanza had been monitoring the Tanzania-registered cargo ship Gold Star after a
tip that it carried a large drug load. [@1] When Italian and Maltese government boats approached it
for inspection on Friday afternoon, the nine crew members set fire to the cargo and jumped
overboard. [@2]

## Rescue, arrests and tow

All nine crew members were rescued from the sea and arrested. [@2] The vessel burned for more than
a day before the flames were put out around noon on Saturday. [@1,2] The Maltese armed forces handed
the ship to Italian authorities that afternoon, and the tug Nos Taurus towed it to Syracuse, Sicily,
where the rescued men were questioned. [@1,2]

## A 30-ton load

The search of the hold revealed 30 tons of hashish resin, which the maritime account says had been
loaded in Turkey. [@1] The Times of Malta estimated the shipment's street value at 300 million
euros. [@2]
