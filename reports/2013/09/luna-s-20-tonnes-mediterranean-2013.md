The French navy intercepted the Tanzania-flagged cargo ship Luna-S in international waters between Italy and Algeria, and announced on 12 September 2013 that it had seized about 20 tons of cannabis. [@1,2] A senior French customs official called it the biggest seizure by the French state in the Mediterranean, and the haul was valued at up to 50 million euros. [@2,3]

As the patrol ship approached south of Sardinia, the eight Syrian crew members set the cannabis on fire in an act officials described as prepared sabotage. [@2,3] It took several hours to bring the blaze under control, and the navy managed to save about 10 of the 20 tons. [@3] The Luna-S had left Morocco days earlier and appeared headed for Egypt, while Tanzania, as flag state, authorised France to stop the ship and try the suspects. [@1,2] The vessel was towed to Toulon and its eight crew taken into custody, facing up to 10 years in prison in France. [@1,3]
