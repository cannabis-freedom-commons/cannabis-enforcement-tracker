Gendarmes carried out several cannabis seizures and plant destructions in French
Polynesia during the day on 9 September 2013. [@1] Acting on intelligence,
gendarmes and municipal police in Papara on Tahiti found 160, 256 and 172
cannabis plants at the homes of three men aged 63, 33 and 22. [@1] The three men
were questioned and, on the instructions of the Papeete prosecutor's office,
summoned to a later court hearing. [@1]

On Taha'a, in the Îles Sous-le-Vent, the local BTA, a gendarmerie brigade, found
two plantations totalling 1,800 cannabis plants. [@1] Those plants were
destroyed by burning, and the owner or owners of the illegal crops were still
being identified. [@1]

Across Tahiti and Taha'a the day's seizures came to nearly 2,400 plants, 2,388
counted site by site. [@1]
