Police ranks conducted a narcotics eradication operation at Yarrowkabra on the Soesdyke-Linden Highway on 18 September 2013. [@1] Nine fields of cannabis plants, comprising approximately 85,000 plants ranging in height between three and ten feet, were found and destroyed along with seven nurseries with seedlings and three camps. [@1]

No arrest was made during the operation. [@1]
