Damián Delvalle Sanabria, known as the 'rey de la marihuana,' was captured on
the afternoon of Friday 6 September 2013 in a joint operation by Paraguay's
National Police, prosecutors and SENAD. [@2] The raid targeted a house in the
Buena Vista neighbourhood of Encarnación owned by his partner Mariela Rivarola,
a former adviser to the Yacyretá binational hydroelectric entity. [@2] SENAD
investigators determined that the 51-year-old had been residing in the luxury
mansion in Encarnación. [@3] No weapons or drugs were found in the residence.
[@3] Several other people were in the house, including two men with
drug-trafficking histories, but they were not detained and their identities were
not released. [@3] He was then transferred to SENAD's operations base in
Asunción. [@3]

SENAD had first detained him in November 2010 with more than two tonnes of
marijuana, and he escaped in May 2011 after allegedly paying US$150,000 for a
falsified release order. [@1] Investigators said he directed a criminal group
moving marijuana to neighbouring countries and linked his group to 800 kilos
recently seized on the Argentina-Paraguay border. [@1] Argentina sought his
extradition, including in connection with the 2003 kidnapping of the son of a
former Argentine minister. [@1]
