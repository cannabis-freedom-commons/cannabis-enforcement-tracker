Police in //Karas Region arrested a 38-year-old Namibian truck driver in
connection with the seizure. [@1] The driver was expected to appear in the
Karasburg Magistrate's Court on a charge of possession of dagga. [@1]

Police seized 276 kg of dagga with a stated street value of N$830 460. [@1] The
dagga, packed as 1 098 parcels in 10 drums, had been hidden under a bridge on
the B1 road between Karasburg and Ariamsvlei. [@1] Police believe the driver hid
the 10 drums there after fearing exposure. [@1] The truck had entered Namibia
through the Ariamsvlei border post and was placed under surveillance after
police received a tip-off that it was carrying dagga. [@1]

//Karas regional commander Armas Shivute commended the officers for the seizure.
[@1] Shivute said that without the confiscation the dagga would have ended up in
the streets. [@1]
