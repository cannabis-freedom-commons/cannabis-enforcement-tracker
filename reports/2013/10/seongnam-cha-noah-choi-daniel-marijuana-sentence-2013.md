The Seongnam Branch of the Suwon District Court sentenced singer Choi Daniel,
21, to one year in prison for buying, brokering and smoking cannabis and took
him into custody in the courtroom. [@2] The court also ordered him to forfeit
7.16 million won (7,160,000), the amount stated in its sentence. [@2]

The court said selling and brokering cannabis required a strong penalty to stop
the drug spreading and to stop more people taking it up. [@2] It said it chose a
prison term because he had taken the trial seriously and repented, and it took
his circumstances into account in sentencing. [@2] Choi was accused of receiving
cannabis from an English instructor and others on 16 occasions between August
2012 and February 2013 and passing it to broadcaster Bianca Mobley and others,
and of smoking it several times. [@2]

Actor Cha Seung-won's son Cha Noah, 24, a former professional gamer, was
sentenced to six months in prison suspended for two years for cannabis smoking.
[@2] The Suwon District Court's Seongnam Branch criminal division 1, headed by
judge Ham Seok-cheon, issued the sentences at a hearing on the morning of 17
October 2013. [@3] The court gave no separate explanation of its reasons for
Cha's sentence. [@3] Cha had been indicted without detention, and the trial
court noted that his offence was a smaller number of smoking occasions than
those of the others tried with him. [@3] Prosecutors had sought ten months in
prison for Cha at the closing hearing, and his family submitted a petition for
leniency before it. [@3]

Cha Noah was also indicted without detention on cannabis-smoking charges in
March, alongside broadcaster Bianca Mobley and Choi Daniel. [@1] The court said
Cha and the other smokers' offences could not be considered light, but it was
sparing them punishment this time in view of their deep reflection. [@1]
