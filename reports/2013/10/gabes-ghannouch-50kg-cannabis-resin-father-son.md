National Guard units in Gabès stopped a drug-trafficking attempt on Friday 11
October 2013 and detained two men, a father and his son, who were in a car near
Ghannouch beach. [@1]

Officers seized 50 kg of cannabis resin from the car, the Interior Ministry said
in a statement made public on Saturday 12 October. [@1] The 50 kg was packed in
200 parcels. [@1]
