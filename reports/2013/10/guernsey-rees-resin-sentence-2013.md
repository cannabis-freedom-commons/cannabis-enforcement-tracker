On 14 January 2013, Customs officers stopped Edward Vernon Rees, a 57-year-old man from Cardiff, as he arrived in Guernsey from Poole and searched his Ford Mondeo after a detector dog gave a positive indication on its rear doors. [@1,2] Inside the rear offside door frame they found 24 nine-ounce bars of cannabis resin weighing 6.02 kg, with a potential resale value of £120,400 to £150,500. [@1] Rees, who had been claiming sickness benefit in Britain, was arrested, answered no comment in interview and was remanded in custody. [@1]

Rees pleaded not guilty and maintained his innocence through a trial held from 16 to 18 September 2013, at the end of which the Royal Court returned a unanimous guilty verdict for importing the resin. [@1] On 30 October, Judge Finch sentenced him to eight years in prison, calling the story Rees gave nonsensical and adding that British criminals like Rees were not welcome on the island; the sentence runs from the date of his arrest. [@1,2]
