On 15 October 2013, Eufaula officer John Phillips stopped a car driven by Joshua Kelly, 30, with
Cameron Massey, 26, riding in the passenger seat. [@1,2] An informant had said a car matching its
description was carrying a load of marijuana through town, and Phillips cited an improper lane
change and obscured registration tags. [@1,3]

Phillips handcuffed Kelly after he twice stepped out of the car and grew loud and aggressive, then
called for backup; Chief Ralph Conner arrived and ordered the 6-foot-8 Massey to keep his hands on
the dashboard. [@1,2] When Massey leaned toward the driver's side and the car began rolling forward,
Conner fired once and Phillips fired four more times, killing the unarmed Massey. [@1,2,3] Officers
later found seven pounds (about 3.2 kg) of marijuana in the trunk. [@3]

Body-camera footage from Conner, published by BuzzFeed News in January 2016, shows Conner aiming at
Massey before the engine started and firing as the car rolled forward. [@1,2] A grand jury declined
to indict the officers in 2014, and Massey's family filed a civil suit in July 2015. [@1,2]
