Anti-smuggling and organized-crime police and gendarmerie teams seized 23 tons of marijuana in the district of Lice, Diyarbakır, the largest amount of drugs ever confiscated in a single operation in the nation's history. [@1,2] The drugs were stored in large containers, packed for sale in the winter and buried in the ground. [@1] Because the area sees heavy terrorist activity, 24 teams backed by air support cordoned it off and raided seven addresses. [@1]

Police took the stash, reportedly worth 6 million lira, to the Diyarbakır police department's storage area by truck. [@1] Briefing reporters, provincial governor Cahit Kıraç said 198 operations had been launched since May with 47,893,000 cannabis plants destroyed, and that the settlement process with the PKK had let security forces return to stopping drug crime. [@1]
