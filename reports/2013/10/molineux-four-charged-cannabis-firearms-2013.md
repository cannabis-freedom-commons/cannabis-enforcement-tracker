The four accused are Nichole Henry, Shantel Charles, Richie Henry and Delvin
Benjamin, all of whom live in Molineux. [@1] The four, including two women, were
arrested and charged over three firearms, more than 100 rounds of ammunition and
a quantity of cannabis sativa. [@1] They were jointly charged on three counts of
possession of firearm, two counts of possession of ammunition, possession of
cannabis and possession of cannabis with intent to supply. [@1] A joint
operation was launched on Saturday 26 October on the premises of the four
accused by the Royal St. Christopher and Nevis Police Force, the St. Kitts-Nevis
Defence Force and Her Majesty’s Customs and Excise Department’s Enforcement
Division. [@1]

The operation was intelligence- and surveillance-driven with assistance from
Crime Stoppers, which led to the execution of search warrants on the premises of
the four arrested people. [@1] The search found one M16 assault rifle, one DPMS
assault rifle and one CZ-52 pistol, with 103 rounds of ammunition, a BSA red-dot
scope and a tin of lubricating oil. [@1] Police did not state the amount of
cannabis found. [@1]

Commissioner Celvin ‘CG’ Walwyn congratulated police officers and community
members who had cooperated with information on firearms finds. [@1] Residents in
the Molineux area said they were appalled that two females were involved but
praised the police for removing more guns from the streets. [@1] Police said the
Molineux find brought to five the firearms taken off the streets in seven days,
after one find in Cotton Ground, Nevis and one in Upper Cayon. [@1]
