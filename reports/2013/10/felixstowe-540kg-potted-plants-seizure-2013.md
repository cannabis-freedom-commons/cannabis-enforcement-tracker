Border Force officers seized 540 kg of herbal cannabis at the Port of Felixstowe
at 11pm on Friday 4 October 2013. [@1] The drug was found in 68 boxes spread
among a legitimate cargo of potted plants in a trailer that had been shipped
from the Netherlands. [@1]

Border Force put the seizure's estimated street value at more than £2.7
million. [@1] A 52-year-old man and a 41-year-old man, both from the
Netherlands, were arrested on suspicion of illegally importing a Class B drug
and were bailed while investigations by the National Crime Agency's Border
Policing Command continued. [@1] Border Force regional director Phillip Holliday
said seizures like this showed officers were at the forefront of the fight to
keep illegal drugs off the streets. [@1]
