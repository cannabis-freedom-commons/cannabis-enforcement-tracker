Nine people were arrested after U.S. and Bahamian authorities seized 3,150
pounds (about 1,429 kg) of marijuana from a boat and a drop point near Hatchet
Point on Eleuthera Island in the Bahamas. [@2] Three of the nine were arrested
at the drop point, where a helicopter aircrew found 45 bales of marijuana
weighing about 1,500 pounds. [@2]

The other six were arrested later, four on a second boat bringing fuel to the
stranded go-fast vessel and two more on the go-fast itself, where officers found
an additional 2,150 pounds of marijuana. [@2]

A U.S. Customs and Border Protection aircraft crew spotted the 35-foot go-fast
vessel with two outboard engines dropping off packages near Hatchet Point. [@2]
The go-fast vessel headed for Nassau but ran out of fuel, and a second boat was
seen bringing it fuel before agents from the U.S. Drug Enforcement
Administration and the Bahamas Drug Enforcement Unit caught both. [@1] The Coast
Guard, which gave the accounts, said the total load weighed 3,150 pounds and put
the wholesale value at more than $3 million. [@1] The boat, the smugglers and
the marijuana were taken to Nassau. [@2] The operation was run by Operation
Bahamas, Turks and Caicos, known as OPBAT, a joint task force of U.S. and
Bahamian agencies. [@2]
