Spanish Customs Surveillance intercepted the trawler Avenir de Safi II about 50 miles south-east of Cartagena on the afternoon of Saturday 12 October 2013 and found nearly nine tonnes of hashish resin hidden in its cold store. [@1] Customs aircraft had spotted the flagless vessel 49 nautical miles out, showing poor maintenance and sailing in waters unusual for its type. [@1]

Officers from the patrol vessel Abanto boarded the trawler and found about 300 bales of the type commonly used to transport hashish, which pending official weighing could hold around 9,000 kilos. [@1] The six crew members, all of Moroccan origin, were arrested immediately, and the vessel was towed to the port of Cartagena. [@1] The detainees, the vessel and the drugs were handed to the investigating magistrate's court acting as duty court in Cartagena. [@1]

The agency presented the seizure, under Operation Lamed, as the third large hashish interception on the eastern Mediterranean route since June 2013, bringing that route's running total past 40 tonnes. [@1]
