Branch Four of the Islamic Revolutionary Court in Shiraz condemned B. Kh., 45, after his arrest on the Shiraz-Sepidan road with 294 kilograms of hashish and one and a half kilograms of extracted heroin known as crack. [@1,2,3] Alongside the death sentence, the court imposed life imprisonment, a 360-million-rial fine and 50 lashes, and the ruling was carried out at the central prison on the morning of 1 October 2013 after his plea for forgiveness was rejected. [@1,2,3]
