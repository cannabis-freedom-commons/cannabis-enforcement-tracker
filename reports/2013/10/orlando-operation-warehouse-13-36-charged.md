Orlando-area law enforcement agencies announced charges of racketeering and
trafficking in marijuana against 36 people. [@1] The Metropolitan Bureau of
Investigation, which led the operation, said 50 people had been arrested or
charged. [@4] Many suspects were arrested on Wednesday and Thursday in raids
that included the group's distribution hub in Orange County. [@3] The three
alleged ringleaders, named by investigators, were arrested earlier in October.
[@3] Roman, Reed and a third suspect, Mark Frazer, were found to have multiple
identities and aliases, and all three were held in the Orange County Jail, MBI
said. [@1] Radio Jamaica reported that about 36 people were arrested between
Wednesday and Thursday morning. [@6] A UPI account said some suspects were
arrested in a series of raids, including one on the group's alleged distribution
center. [@5]

Investigators said the organization smuggled thousands of pounds of marijuana
from Mexico into the United States and then to central Florida. [@1] The group
used the U.S. mail, private parcel services, freight shipping and private
vehicles to move the drug, authorities said. [@1] Homes in Pine Hills were used
as stash houses, and warehouses in west Orlando took deliveries and served as
distribution bases. [@4] One warehouse, which ran as a glass repair shop by day,
was identified as the base of the operation. [@4] Investigators seized more than
3,200 pounds of marijuana, $200,000 in cash, 10 firearms and six vehicles,
authorities said. [@1] ClickOrlando put the cannabis haul at more than 3,212
pounds. [@2] The Orlando Sentinel reported 15 guns seized and said more seizures
were expected. [@3] The group was linked to homicides in Florida and Jamaica,
armed robberies and other violent crimes, MBI said. [@3] Agents began targeting
the group in September 2011. [@3] In July, agents stopped an RV in a resort
parking lot and found about 1,230 pounds of marijuana hidden inside. [@3] The
Sentinel linked the RV to a nearly 3,000-pound marijuana seizure in July that
was brought into Orlando. [@3] WESH reported the July interception as more than
2,000 pounds. [@1]

Suspects face charges of racketeering and conspiracy to commit racketeering,
trafficking in cannabis and money laundering. [@6] MBI Director Larry Zwieg said
Roman was laundering the bulk of the millions of dollars the ring made. [@3]
Zwieg said the money-laundering investigation was ongoing. [@3] Investigators
anticipated more arrests as the investigation continued. [@2]
