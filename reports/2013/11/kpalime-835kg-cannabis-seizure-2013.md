Police had arrested three suspected cannabis traffickers by the time of the
presentation. [@2] Kpalimé lies about 120 km north of Lomé. [@1]

Police commissioner Makiliwè Kassim Katanga said the cannabis had been seized at
the Togo–Ghana border post at Kamétonou, at Klomayo’Ndi and Sévénocopé, and
within Kpalimé. [@1] The cannabis was displayed in square parcels wrapped with
tape at the entrance to the police office. [@2]

The three arrested people had already been transferred to Lomé civil prison by
the time police presented the cannabis to the press. [@1] Katanga urged
residents to report suspicious people in their neighborhoods, cooperate with the
police and help eliminate insecurity in Kpalimé. [@2] He gave 117 and 115 as
telephone numbers residents could use to alert officers. [@2]
