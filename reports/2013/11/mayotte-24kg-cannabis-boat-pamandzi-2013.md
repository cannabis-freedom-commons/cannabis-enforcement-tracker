Mayotte's maritime gendarmerie intercepted a clandestine boat in the lagoon east
of the island late last week, coming from the north-west coast of Madagascar.
[@2] The boat, fitted with a 140 hp engine, carried 22 passengers, most of them
Comorian. [@3] Gendarmes found 24 kg of cannabis on board. [@1]

Two people were placed in garde à vue, the French police custody that can last
up to 48 hours before a prosecutor decides on charges or release. [@2] Gendarmes
at Pamandzi, in the south of Mayotte, were put in charge of the investigation.
[@3] LINFO.re said the Pamandzi gendarmerie had opened a smuggling and
drug-importation investigation against the two men. [@1]

The other passengers were to be sent back to the border, mostly to the Comoros,
once exams were complete. [@1] The three accounts trace the story to
Madagascar's L'Express de Madagascar, which reported that the passengers had
been given medical checks before the administrative removals. [@3]
