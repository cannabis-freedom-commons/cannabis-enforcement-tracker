U.S. Customs and Border Protection officers at the Calexico East cargo facility
intercepted almost two tons of marijuana concealed inside a shipment of
plastic pallets on 13 November 2013. [@1] Officers stopped a 2011 Peterbilt
tractor-trailer carrying a manifested shipment of plastic pallets at about
7:40 p.m., and imaging anomalies plus a detector-dog alert led them to 240
wrapped packages of marijuana hidden inside hollowed-out pallets. [@1]

Officers seized a total of 3,498 pounds (about 1,587 kg) of marijuana with an
estimated street value of $1.5 million. [@1] The driver, a 43-year-old Mexican
citizen living in Mexicali, Baja California, was turned over to Homeland
Security Investigations agents for further processing, and CBP seized the
narcotics and the conveyance. [@1]
