A tractor-trailer of Mexican squash hiding a 10.1-million-dollar shipment of marijuana was stopped at the port of Nogales by Customs and Border Protection officers at the Mariposa commercial facility. [@1] Pedro Corona-Moreno, 32, of Hermosillo, Sonora, was sent to a routine inspection of his produce load as he entered the United States, and a canine alerted to marijuana once the shipment was offloaded. [@1,2]

## A state record

Officers pulled 881 bundles weighing 20,375 pounds (about 9.24 tonnes) from the load, the largest marijuana seizure on Arizona's border with Mexico. [@1,2] It beat the previous state record of about 14,000 pounds seized that January. [@1,2]

The tractor-trailer, the drugs and the produce were seized, and Corona-Moreno was arrested and turned over to Immigration and Customs Enforcement's Homeland Security Investigations. [@1,2] Tucson field chief Roderick Blanchard credited his officers' diligence for the continued success at the ports. [@1]
