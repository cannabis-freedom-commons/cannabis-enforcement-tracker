The Criminal Court sentenced Hussain Humam of Henveiru Lobby House to six years'
imprisonment on two counts of using cannabis. [@1] Humam had been arrested in
connection with the murder of MP Dr Afrasheem Ali. [@1] His trial in the
Afrasheem murder case was still going on, with Humam the only person charged.
[@1]

In May 2012 Humam was arrested by a patrolling officer on suspicion of being
intoxicated, and police urine testing was positive for cannabis. [@1] Humam
confessed in court to using cannabis in that case. [@1] In October 2012 Humam
was arrested near Jalapeno Restaurant on suspicion of being intoxicated. [@1] He
was taken to Male Custodial, where his urine again tested positive for cannabis.
[@1] Humam again confessed to using cannabis in court during that trial. [@1]
The court found he had violated article 113a of the 2011 Drug Act and sentenced
him under article 113b. [@1]

A month earlier the same court had sentenced Humam to one year's imprisonment
for refusing to provide a urine sample for a drug test. [@1] Humam was also
charged in a separate case over allegedly attacking a police officer, a charge
he denied. [@1]
