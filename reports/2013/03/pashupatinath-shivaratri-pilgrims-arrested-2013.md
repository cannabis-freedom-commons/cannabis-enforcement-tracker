Nepalese police arrested 67 people at the Pashupatinath temple in Kathmandu on Sunday 10 March 2013 for smoking cannabis and disturbing the peace during the Shivaratri festival. [@1] Most of those held were found intoxicated with cannabis, and those arrested were pilgrims, Kathmandu police spokesman Uttam Subedi told Agence France-Presse. [@1]

Cannabis is illegal in Nepal, but holy men known as sadhus are allowed to smoke it during the annual celebration honouring the Hindu god Shiva. [@1] Thousands of pilgrims travel to the temple complex from Nepal and India for Shivaratri, and police deployed thousands of officers as more than 150,000 pilgrims were expected. [@1] The roundup was the biggest arrest of pilgrims for drug use at the festival to date. [@1,2]
