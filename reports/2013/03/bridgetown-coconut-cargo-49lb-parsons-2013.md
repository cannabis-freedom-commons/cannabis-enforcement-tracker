Dennis Lawrence Parsons, a 35-year-old musician from Fountain in St Vincent,
appeared at the District A Court in Bridgetown, Barbados, on Wednesday 20 March
2013. [@1] Members of the Drug Squad had arrested and charged him the previous
day, Tuesday 19 March. [@1] A Royal Barbados Police press release said he was
charged with possession of 49 pounds of cannabis, trafficking of cannabis,
possession with intent to supply and importation of the drug. [@1]

On 19 March 2013, officers of the Customs and Excise Department searched a
shipment of coconuts that had entered Barbados as cargo on an inter-island
vessel from St Vincent. [@1] Packages of cannabis were found with the
consignment, and the Barbadian Drug Squad was called in to continue the
investigation. [@1] TrakkerNews, reporting from Bridgetown, said 49 pounds were
seized aboard the inter-island vessel and that customs officials searching the
cargo notified the police after finding packages of the drug in the consignment.
[@2] The 49 pounds come to about 22.2 kilograms of cannabis. [@2]

Parsons and a woman were both taken into custody, but the woman was released and
Parsons alone was charged. [@1] TrakkerNews identified him as a 35-year-old
Vincentian national facing charges of possession, possession with intent to
supply, trafficking and importation. [@2]
