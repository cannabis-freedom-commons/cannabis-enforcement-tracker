Divisional Tasking Unit police from Wangaratta and Wodonga joined Whitfield police and
forensic officers from Melbourne to raid a 35-hectare vineyard outside Cheshunt at 9.30am on
Monday 18 March 2013. [@1] Between 4,000 and 6,000 almost-mature cannabis plants were growing
under neglected vines, hidden from the eighth row back in the 49-row vineyard while
cannabis-free rows and a farmhouse fronted the road. [@1] Officers began pulling up the crop, a
job expected to take days, for later destruction once weighed. [@1]

## Two charged

James Stammers, 65, and Alam Noor, 38, both of New South Wales, were charged at Benalla
Magistrates Court and remanded in custody without applying for bail. [@1] The pair had rented
the vineyard for six to nine months without arousing the suspicions of locals. [@1] Sergeant
Damien Loiterton called it the biggest crop he had seen in 10 years and said the find followed
a protracted investigation, with the plants about a month from harvest. [@1] The crop's
reported street value was £12 million. [@1] Stammers and Noor were due back at
Wangaratta Magistrates Court on 13 June. [@1]
