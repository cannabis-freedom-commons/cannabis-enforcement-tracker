Units of the National Police's traffic and transport directorate stopped a truck at the Betania toll on the Buga–Tuluá dual carriageway on Tuesday 4 June 2013 and found pressed marijuana camouflaged under a shipment of passion fruit. [@1] The stash weighed exactly 3,531 kg, had allegedly come from the department of Cauca, and was bound for Bogotá. [@1]

Officers detained the truck driver and a 17-year-old travelling with him as a helper. [@1] Colombia Reports described the same interception as a 3.9-ton seizure at a checkpoint between Buga and Andalucía, with both drivers arrested. [@2] The detainees, the truck and the marijuana were handed to the Fiscalía, to be prosecuted for trafficking, manufacture or possession of narcotics. [@1]
