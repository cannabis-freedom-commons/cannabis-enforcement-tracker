Judicial police of Valle del Cauca (Sijin) found a truck carrying 2,356 kg of pressed marijuana in Palmira on Sunday 2 June 2013. [@2] Colombia Reports, citing the police, rounds the haul to nearly 2.6 tons and says one person was captured in the operation. [@1]

The seizure was the first of two large marijuana hauls from trucks in Valle del Cauca within three days, followed by the interception of 3,531 kg hidden under passion fruit near Buga on 4 June, a separate event reported on its own. [@1,2] Those detained and the seized marijuana were handed to the Fiscalía, to be prosecuted for trafficking, manufacture or possession of narcotics. [@2]
