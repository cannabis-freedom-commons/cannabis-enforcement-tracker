At the port of Bari, Guardia di Finanza officers seized just over 136 kilograms
of marijuana from a Macedonian-plated truck that had just landed from the motor
vessel Sveti Stefan, which had come from Montenegro and was bound for
Switzerland. [@2] Officers from the customs agency's anti-fraud office assisted
the financial police in the seizure. [@2] The vehicle was a curtain-sided
semi-trailer travelling without cargo. [@3] The 134 packets of marijuana were
packed to fit into cavities hollowed out of each of the wooden side boards. [@2]
The operation was carried out with the help of sniffer-dog units. [@2]

The truck's 33-year-old Macedonian driver was arrested and jailed in Bari. [@3]
He was to answer a charge of international drug trafficking, and the truck was
seized. [@3] Investigators said the marijuana would have earned criminal
organisations at least 1.3 million euros if sold at retail. [@3] The truck had
been travelling from Montenegro toward Switzerland when officers stopped it in
Bari. [@1]
