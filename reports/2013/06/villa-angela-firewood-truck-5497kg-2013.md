Gendarmería Nacional, Argentina's national gendarmerie, detained one man and
seized 5,497 kg of marijuana hidden in a truck carrying firewood in Chaco
province. [@3] The operation was carried out by the Villa Ángela section of
Squadron 1 Roque Sáenz Peña on National Route 95 near the city of Villa Ángela.
[@3] The operation took place within Escudo Norte, the anti-drug operation
designed by the Security Ministry. [@1]

Officers found 252 jute bags of rectangular blocks wrapped in packing tape
hidden under the wood, with a total weight of 5,497 kg of marijuana. [@1] The
truck was a model usually used to transport cattle and was carrying timber from
Formosa province bound for Buenos Aires province. [@1] Officers stopped the
livestock-style truck during a routine check after noticing the load of stacked
firewood, according to the force's account. [@4] A second Chaco account likewise
reports that officers found the 252 jute bags hidden under the timber. [@2]

The federal judge of Presidencia Roque Sáenz Peña, Zunilda Niremperger,
authorised a full search of the truck, and a court under her ordered the seizure
of the drug and the driver's detention. [@1] The truck was inspected inside a
service station on National Route 95 near the entrance to Villa Ángela. [@1]
