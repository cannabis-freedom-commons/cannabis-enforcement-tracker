Trebinje police officers, in a continuation of operation Mreža 2013, arrested
five people and seized over 35 kilograms of marijuana. [@3] The arrested men
were Odisej Fofilos (31) from Vareš, Vanja Kenjić (22) from East Sarajevo, and
Đuro Perović (27), Robert Ljiljak (36) and Marko Božinović (37), all described
as from Tivat in Montenegro. [@2] They were arrested on the Ljubinje-Stolac road
at Žegulje for allegedly trying to smuggle the marijuana with two citizens of
Bosnia and Herzegovina. [@1]

Around 15:00 police stopped a Bosnian-registered Dacia driven by Fofilos at
Žegulje. [@2] A search of the Dacia's trunk found and seized 30 packages of
marijuana. [@2] On the Ljubinje-Trebinje road police also stopped a
Tivat-registered Polo carrying driver Đuro Perović and Robert Ljiljak. [@2] The
second vehicle stopped was a Croatian-registered Mercedes driven by Marko
Božinović. [@2] A later search of the Mercedes found and seized one more package
of marijuana. [@2]

Police put the black-market value of the 35 kilograms at about 350,000
convertible marks. [@2] Police continued the criminal investigation of the five
arrested men and documented the offence of unauthorised production and
trafficking of narcotic drugs. [@2]
