Highway police in Valle del Cauca found about 6,000 kg of pressed marijuana inside the container of a truck stopped at kilometre 44 in El Bolo, rural Palmira. [@1,2] The truck had left northern Cauca, and the driver carried two manifests, one for Ibagué and one for Bogotá. [@2] Regional commander General Francisco Patiño said the interception kept millions of doses from circulating in the cities. [@1,2]

## Concealment and value

The marijuana was packed in more than a hundred plastic sacks stashed among wooden pallets, in a truck with Bucaramanga plates TBW 069. [@2] Police estimated the load was worth more than 3.6 billion pesos, about 1.8 million dollars. [@1,2] El Tiempo reported the seizure brought the 2013 tally on Valle roads to 41 tonnes; Colombia Reports, citing Semana, linked the load to a boom in pot cultivation in north-eastern Cauca for the domestic market. [@1,2]
