Miami-Dade narcotics detectives arrived at 15415 Southwest 57th Street in the Lakes of the Meadow development of West Kendall at about 7pm on Tuesday evening to check a tip about a possible marijuana grow house. [@1,2] As they approached the rented house, the occupants opened fire, officers returned fire, and three men fled on foot while the house caught fire for reasons yet unknown. [@1]

The shootout ran for an estimated 30 minutes and drew police units from across the county for an all-night manhunt that kept the neighbourhood locked down. [@1,2] Police arrested 29-year-old Brian Kelly Howell within 18 hours; the body of 50-year-old Dell Peter DiGiovanni was found hanging some ten blocks away in an apparent suicide, and a third body lay in the charred home, still unidentified the next evening. [@1] Police were still seeking a third suspect, 27-year-old Michael Steven DiGiovanni. [@1]
