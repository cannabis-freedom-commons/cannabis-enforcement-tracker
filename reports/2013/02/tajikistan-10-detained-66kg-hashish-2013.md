Tajikistan's State Committee for National Security (GKNB), the country's
security service, reported that 10 people had been detained on suspicion of
especially large-scale drug trafficking, including five officers of the Interior
Ministry, the security committee and the border troops. [@1] Several servicemen
of the republic's border troops were detained for alleged involvement in the
trafficking after the committee said in its official statement that they had
helped move the drugs. [@1] A second consignment was seized in Dushanbe, where
Interior Ministry personnel were detained on suspicion of drug dealing. [@1]

The GKNB said several anti-trafficking operations in Dushanbe and Sughd region
recovered about 140 kg of drugs in total, including 45 kg of heroin and 66 kg of
hashish, with only the hashish counted as cannabis here. [@1] In Sughd region,
officers found the drugs inside a passenger car. [@1]

Operatives established that the heroin, described as of Afghan origin, had been
carried across the Tajik-Afghan border in Panj district of Khatlon region. [@1]
