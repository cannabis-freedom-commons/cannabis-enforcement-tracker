Venezuela's National Guard found more than five tonnes of marijuana hidden in a fuel tanker at a checkpoint in Ureña, on the Colombian border, with the find announced on 24 February 2013. [@1] The drugs sat in a concealed bottom section of the tanker, which had come from Colombia, according to the government's anti-drugs office. [@1] Interior and Justice Minister Néstor Reverol announced the seizure, and one person was detained. [@1] Ureña lies in Táchira state, an area the report describes as having high levels of contraband flow with Colombia. [@1]
