Larry Feldman, 63, a long-time volunteer with the Wesley College football team, was arrested on 13 February 2013 after four pounds of marijuana (about 1.8 kg) he had mailed to himself from California arrived at his Dover home. [@1,2] He had sent the package to himself by FedEx, which brought the Tracy, California, police department, the Delaware State Police and the Drug Enforcement Administration into the case. [@1]

Feldman was charged with possessing a controlled substance in a high quantity, with possession of marijuana and with possession of drug paraphernalia, including a marijuana pipe, rolling papers and a digital scale. [@1] State police forcibly entered his home when no one answered the door. [@1] Wesley's head football coach and athletic director said Feldman kept the practice schedule and turned on the field lights, and that the arrest would leave no one to replace him. [@1]
