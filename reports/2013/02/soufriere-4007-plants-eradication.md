On Saturday 23 February 2013, officers attached to the Soufriere Police Station
conducted an eradication exercise on a marijuana plantation at St Phillips, Fond
St Jacques, Soufriere, Saint Lucia. [@1] The plantation lay in the Fond St
Jacques community of the Soufriere district. [@1]

According to police, the officers uprooted a total of 4,007 fully grown
marijuana plants. [@1] In addition, officers recovered three bags of compressed
marijuana at the site. [@1] Police destroyed the uprooted plants and the three
bags of compressed marijuana by fire. [@1]

No one was arrested in relation to the incident. [@1] The eradication exercise
therefore removed both growing plants and processed marijuana from the
plantation and left no one in custody. [@1]
