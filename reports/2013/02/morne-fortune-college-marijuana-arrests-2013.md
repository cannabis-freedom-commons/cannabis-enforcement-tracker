The son of a former government minister was among those arrested by police for
marijuana possession last week. [@1] The arrests took place on the compound of
the Sir Arthur Lewis Community College at Morne Fortune, in an area known to
school officials for ganja smoking. [@1] After the arrests police remained
silent, while citizens questioned whether preferential treatment would be shown
to the young man. [@1] The case involved a schoolchild, and police said they
were sensitive about releasing the names of schoolchildren in such cases. [@1]
Francois said there had been similar past cases where students of the college
were arrested and their names were withheld. [@1] Police decided to withhold the
name in this case on the basis that a schoolchild was involved. [@1] Approaches
had been made about the case on the basis that it involved a schoolchild and
just one spliff. [@1]

The area involved was one where students met frequently to smoke marijuana and
gamble, and police had been called there several times before and had made
arrests. [@1] Principal Urban Dolor said college regulations clearly do not
tolerate any illegal activity including drug use. [@1] Dolor said staff inform
police when students are found with drugs, police decide whether to charge, and
the college takes its own action in each matter. [@1] He said the school tries
to detect habitual use early and offers counselling and guidance. [@1]

In recent years there had been a noticeable increase in marijuana and alcohol
use by school students, with some students arrested for possessing ganja and
peddling it. [@1]
