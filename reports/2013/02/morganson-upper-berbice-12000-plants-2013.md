Police ranks conducted a drug eradication operation at Morganson on the Upper Berbice River and found a five-acre field under cannabis cultivation, about 2.02 hectares. [@1] The field held approximately 12,000 cannabis sativa plants, ranging in height from one foot to twelve feet. [@1]

Alongside the standing crop, the officers found about 2,000 kilogrammes of dried marijuana already gathered. [@1] The plants and the dried stock were destroyed. [@1]

## No arrests

No arrest was made during the operation, and the account names no suspects behind the field. [@1]
