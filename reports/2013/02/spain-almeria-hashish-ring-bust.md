## The arrests

On the afternoon of 6 February 2013, UDYCO investigators of the Spanish National Police arrested five key suspects caught red-handed loading a German-registered trailer truck with 88 bundles of hashish bound for northern Europe. [@1,2] The network stored the drugs in a warehouse between Murcia and Almería; the bundles weighed 25–30 kg each, totalling about 2.6 tonnes, which Europol valued at about €4 million on the illegal market. [@1,2] Two of those arrested were a Dutch father and son, two were Spanish nationals and one was an Irish citizen; a sixth suspect escaped and remained at large. [@1]

## A wider network

Europol said the Almería-based network was part of a wider multi-agency operation, with related investigations running for months in Germany, led by Lower Saxony investigators and customs, and in Norway, led by the Oslo police district with customs support, alongside investigators from Denmark, the Netherlands, Sweden and the United Kingdom. [@1] Intelligence moved through Europol's SIENA network, which also funded the investigators' joint meetings. [@1]
