Belgian police and customs seized seven tonnes of cannabis hidden in a shipment of coconuts from Africa in a cargo container near the small town of Ypres in the west, close to the French border. [@1,2] The drug, found the previous week, had an estimated street value of 14 million euros. [@1]

Shortly afterwards a similar container holding more of the drug was found in Antwerp, the country's commercial heart and second-biggest port in Europe, though the authorities gave no figure for that second haul. [@1] The government called the combined finds a record seizure by customs working with the police. [@1]
