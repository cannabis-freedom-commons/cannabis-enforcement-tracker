Solomon Islands police and the Participating Police Force raided cannabis
plantations at Roroni Community in the Guadalcanal Plains area of Guadalcanal in
early December 2013. [@1] The raid followed cooperation between the community
and police, according to a statement from the Police Media Unit relayed by
Solomon Times. [@1] Police said the uprooted plants were being held at the
police station to help the investigation and the gathering of evidence. [@1]

A total of 6,492 cannabis plants were taken from several locations, which the
broadcaster valued at an estimated street value of 13 million Solomon Islands
dollars. [@2] Solomon Times put the street value of the Roroni plants at 14
million Solomon Islands dollars. [@1] Supervising Police Commissioner Walter
Kola called the haul a record cannabis seizure by the Royal Solomon Islands
Police Force. [@2]

After forensic samples were taken, police destroyed the plants in a controlled
burn on 12 December 2013. [@2] Kola said police had identified a number of
persons of interest in what he described as a major ongoing investigation. [@2]
The joint police force warned that anyone caught planting, selling or using the
drug would face severe prosecution in court. [@1]
