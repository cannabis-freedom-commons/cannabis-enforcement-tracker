Police at Norman Manley International Airport in Kingston arrested seven people
during routine checks on 9 December 2013. [@1] Personnel from the Canine
Division and the Transnational Crimes and Narcotics Division made the seizures.
[@1] Police withheld the identities of those arrested until they are charged.
[@1]

About 10 o'clock yesterday morning members of the Transnational Crimes and
Narcotics Division accosted and searched a man boarding a flight to Barbados.
[@1] His luggage had a false compartment with ganja weighing approximately 5.9
pounds. [@1] The man and the female who accompanied him to the airport were
taken into custody. [@1] An hour later the team conducted a stop and search
operation near the airport roundabout. [@1] The driver of a white Isuzu Elf
motor truck was signalled to stop and five boxes of yams containing ganja
weighing approximately 129 pounds were found. [@1] Three men were taken into
custody and the motor truck was seized. [@1] Later that afternoon officers
attached to the Canine Division searched the luggage of a male who had checked
in to board a flight during routine security checks in the baggage area. [@1]
Ganja amounting to 18.6 pounds was found and the male was taken into custody.
[@1] Within hours officers searched the luggage of another male checking in on a
flight to Curacao and found ganja weighing about 2.8 pounds in a tin of cheese.
[@1] In total police seized approximately 156.4 pounds of ganja and a motor
truck over a six-hour period on Monday. [@2] The street value of the drug was
estimated at J$782,000. [@2]

The seizures ran from about 10:00 a.m. to about 2:30 p.m., with the third
seizure about 12:20 p.m. [@2] Police appealed to the public to comply with the
laws and warned those involved in drug trafficking that they will be caught.
[@1]
