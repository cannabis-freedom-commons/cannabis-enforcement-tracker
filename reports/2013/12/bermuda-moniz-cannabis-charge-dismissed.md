Thomas Moniz, 19, the son of Health Minister Trevor Moniz, appeared in Bermuda's
Magistrates' Court on 31 December 2013 charged with possessing cannabis and drug
paraphernalia. [@1] He denied having 2.1 grams of cannabis and a blue grinder in
his possession on 2 September in Devonshire parish. [@1] He elected to be tried
in the Magistrates' Court and was set to appear again later that week. [@1]
Magistrate Khamisi Tokunbo released him on $2,000 bail with one surety in the
meantime. [@1]

When he returned to court on 2 January 2014 the Crown offered no evidence
against him. [@2] Senior Magistrate Archibald Warner then dismissed the charges.
[@2] The Royal Gazette described the dismissal as the first ruling of 2014. [@2]
Bernews recorded that he had pleaded not guilty on 31 December to cannabis
possession and to having a drug grinder. [@3]

Attorney-General Mark Pettingill said the magistrate's ruling fell well within
normal guidelines for a police caution. [@4] Pettingill said the matter was for
the Department of Public Prosecutions, while the police made the initial
decision in such cases. [@4]
