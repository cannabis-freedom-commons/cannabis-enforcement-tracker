U.S. District Judge Percy Anderson sentenced Aaron Sandusky, 42, of Rancho Cucamonga, to 10 years in federal prison on 7 January 2013 for running a marijuana business in the Inland Empire. [@1,2] Sandusky, the former president of G3 Holistics, had been convicted the previous fall on federal narcotics charges for distributing hundreds of pounds of marijuana. [@1]

A federal jury that heard two days of evidence convicted Sandusky of conspiracy and possession with intent to distribute marijuana, finding each count involved at least 1,000 marijuana plants and triggering a 10-year mandatory minimum. [@1,2] The jury could not reach verdicts on four other counts charging him with maintaining drug-involved premises at G3 stores in Upland, Colton and Moreno Valley and at a grow in Ontario, which the court dismissed at the government's request. [@1,2] The case followed October 2011 warnings that the stores violated federal law; Sandusky closed Colton and Moreno Valley but kept Upland open even after two search warrants, forfeiture suits over the property and nearly $11,500 in seized cash.
