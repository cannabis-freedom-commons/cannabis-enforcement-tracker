Gardai arrested two men, both in their sixties, in connection with the discovery of cannabis plants in Co. Donegal on the night of 24 January 2013 at about 10.30pm. [@1] As part of a planned operation involving Gardai from Ballyshannon assisted by a member of the Regional Support Unit, officers searched a house in Ballintra and seized about 300 cannabis plants. [@1] The plants carried an estimated potential street value of about 240,000 euro, pending analysis. [@1]

The two men were detained at Ballyshannon Garda station under section 2 of the Criminal Justice (Drug Trafficking) Act 1993. [@1]
