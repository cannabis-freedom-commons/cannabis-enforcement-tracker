Officers attached to the Soufriere Police Station conducted an eradication
exercise on a marijuana plantation at Ravine Clear, Soufriere, on Wednesday 16
January 2013. [@1] During that Ravine Clear exercise, officers destroyed 16,008
marijuana seedlings growing on the plantation. [@1] During the same Ravine Clear
exercise, officers destroyed 2,260 grown marijuana plants found alongside the
seedlings. [@1] At Ravine Clear, police also seized a white bucket containing
compressed marijuana. [@1]

On Tuesday 22 January 2013, the same Soufriere officers conducted eradication
exercises on two other marijuana plantations at Migny in Fond St Jacques,
Soufriere. [@1] At those two Migny plantations, officers destroyed 8,500
seedlings and 3,650 fully grown marijuana plants. [@1]

No one was arrested in relation to either the Ravine Clear exercise or the later
Migny exercises. [@1]
