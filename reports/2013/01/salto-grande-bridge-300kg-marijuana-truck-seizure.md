A Paraguayan-registered truck trying to enter Uruguay over the Salto Grande
bridge was stopped at the border crossing on the afternoon of Friday 18 January
2013. [@1] Argentine customs officers found 300 kilograms of marijuana hidden
among the truck's cargo. [@1]

The two people travelling in the vehicle were detained and held incommunicado.
[@1] The detainees were to answer before the Argentine court in Concepción del
Uruguay, which handled the procedure. [@1]

The report describes Salto as a route used for years by Paraguayan traffickers
to move drugs toward the port of Montevideo and onward to other South American
countries and Europe. [@1]
