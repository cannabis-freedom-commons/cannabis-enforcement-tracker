Three men aged 41, 50 and 67 from Cahul district were suspected of circulating
marijuana. [@2] Officers carried out five searches at their homes. [@2] One
suspect was detained for 72 hours and then placed in detention for 30 days. [@1]
The other two suspects were investigated while remaining at liberty. [@3]

Officers found and seized about two kilograms of marijuana. [@2] During the
searches police also found grinding tools, a polymer-glass device used for
consuming drugs, small electronic scales and a firearm of unknown model. [@3]
Officers also found several glass jars and zip-lock packets in which the
suspects kept the drugs. [@1] The estimated value of the seized drugs was given
as 490,000 lei. [@3] Moldova 1 put the haul at drugs worth about 500,000 lei
taken in several searches at the three men's homes. [@1]

Police opened a criminal case and the suspects faced three to seven years in
prison. [@1] Police said they were continuing special investigative measures to
establish and involve other people in the criminal activity. [@2]
