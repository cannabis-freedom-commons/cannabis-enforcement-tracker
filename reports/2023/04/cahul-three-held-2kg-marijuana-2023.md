Police investigating suspected large-scale marijuana sales carried out five searches at homes belonging to three men aged 41, 50 and 67 from Cahul district. [@2,3] They seized approximately two kilograms of marijuana, including cannabis stored in glass jars and resealable packets. [@1,2,3,4] Police also found equipment for grinding and consuming cannabis, small electronic scales and a firearm of unidentified make. [@2,3,4]

TV8 reported an estimated value of 490,000 Moldovan lei, while Moldova 1 and Radio Moldova put it at about 500,000 lei. [@1,2,3,4] Police held one suspect for 72 hours before he was remanded for 30 days. [@1,2,3,4] The other two were investigated while remaining at liberty. [@2,3] The alleged offence carried three to seven years in prison, and police said they were still looking into whether other people were involved. [@1,2,3,4]
