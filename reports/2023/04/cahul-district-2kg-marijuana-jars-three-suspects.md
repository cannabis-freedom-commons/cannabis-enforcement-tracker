Police carried out several searches in the homes of three men from Cahul
District who were suspected of selling marijuana in especially large amounts.
[@1] Law-enforcement officers conducted the operation in the suspects' houses
and reported the discovery of stored drugs on those premises. [@1] One of the
three suspects was initially detained for 72 hours and was then remanded in
custody for 30 days. [@1] The other two suspects remained under investigation
after the searches while the first suspect stayed in custody. [@1]

During the searches officers seized about two kilograms of marijuana. [@1] The
seized marijuana included several jars and packets containing marijuana. [@1]
Officers also seized a homemade device for drug use during the same house
searches. [@1] Officers also seized small electronic scales and a firearm during
the same searches. [@1] Police valued the drugs seized from the three homes at
about 500,000 Moldovan lei. [@1]

Police opened a criminal case over the alleged large-scale sale, in which the
three suspects face three to seven years of imprisonment. [@1]
