A 30-year-old Indian national was arrested in Nepal's Chitwan district on
Friday 7 April 2023 for ferrying a cache of hashish in a truck. [@1,2] Acting
on a tip-off, police raided the truck, owned by Kundan Patel, a resident of
East Champaran district in Bihar. [@1,2]

Police recovered 181 kg and 500 grams of hashish from the truck. [@1,2] Police
said the cache was being ferried toward India. [@1]
