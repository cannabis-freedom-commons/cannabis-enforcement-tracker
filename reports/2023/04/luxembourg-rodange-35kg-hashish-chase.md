Luxembourg customs officers set out to subject a car arriving from France to a
customs check near the Franco-Luxembourg border. [@2] The 19-year-old driver
disobeyed the officers' instructions and fled, so the customs officers set off
in pursuit of the car. [@1] During the flight he broke traffic rules by driving
at excessive speed and running a red light, then caused a traffic accident at
Rodange where officers caught him. [@1] Nobody was injured in the crash and only
material damage to the fleeing car remained. [@2] Officers arrested the
19-year-old driver and presented him to the liberty judge. [@1]

Officers confiscated both the drugs and the vehicle involved in the chase. [@1]
During the subsequent search of the vehicle, service dog Balto sniffed out the
hashish, which had been hidden in a professionally built smuggling cache in the
front compartment and the underbody of the vehicle. [@1] The seizure totalled
3.5 kg of hashish found in the vehicle after the troubled border control. [@1]
The customs administration only disclosed the 5 April control on Thursday 29
June, saying the wait was for reasons linked to the case's procedural
investigation. [@1]
