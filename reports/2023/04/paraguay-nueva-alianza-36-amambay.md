Paraguay's National Anti-Drug Secretariat (Senad) and Brazil's Federal Police
closed the 36th phase of Operation Nueva Alianza on 3 April 2023, after raids
by air and land in the forests of Amambay that began on 26 March. [@1,2] Under
prosecutor Celso Morales, tactical teams moved through Cadete Boquerón,
Trabuco, Karapã'i, Cerro Kuatia, Colonia Piray, Colonia Estrella, Tatú and
María Auxiliadora, dismantling 131 drug camps. [@1] Inside the camps they found
82,680 kilograms of ready marijuana and 750 kilograms of seeds, and eradicated
334 hectares of plantations in growth and harvest. [@1,3] Senad estimated that
about 1,084,000 kilograms of marijuana were taken out of circulation, most
bound for Brazil where a kilogram sells for about USD 150, putting the loss to
traffickers at about USD 162.7 million. [@1,3] The agency's statement names no
arrests. [@1]

Amambay has long been Paraguay's marijuana production hub, with nearly 70
percent farmland and little state presence, and most of its crop moves across
the porous border to Brazil. [@2] The Nueva Alianza operations run under a
cooperation agreement between Senad and the Federal Police against organised
crime in the border region. [@1]
