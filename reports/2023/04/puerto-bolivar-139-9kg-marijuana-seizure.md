Colombia's Navy, working with the Colombian Air Force, carried out a joint
operation against drug trafficking in the Caribbean Sea. [@1] Surface units and
naval aviation detected and pursued a boat crewed by a Colombian man and a
Dominican man. [@1] The two men were transporting more than 342 kilograms of
drugs in a go-fast boat heading for a port in the Dominican Republic. [@1] After
the pursuit, a Navy coast guard unit intercepted the boat 19 nautical miles
north of Puerto Bolívar, in La Guajira department. [@1]

Inside the boat, officers found 16 sacks holding 488 rectangular packages of the
kind traffickers use to move illicit drugs, along with a pistol and ammunition.
[@1] The ammunition amounted to 11 cartridges of different calibers. [@2]
Preliminary identification tests (PIPH) confirmed 202.9 kilograms of cocaine
hydrochloride and 139.9 kilograms of marijuana. [@1] The substances were tested
once the packages were taken ashore. [@3] The Navy said the operation prevented
the sale of 507,000 doses of cocaine and 23,000 doses of marijuana on the
international illegal market. [@1] Authorities said the combined cocaine and
marijuana seizure kept 7.7 million dollars from reaching the trafficking
organisations operating in the region. [@3]

The two detainees and the seized material were handed to the CTI, the Attorney
General's Office's investigative body, for prosecution. [@1] The Navy's
Caribbean Naval Force said it would continue joint operations against drug
transport along maritime corridors. [@1]
