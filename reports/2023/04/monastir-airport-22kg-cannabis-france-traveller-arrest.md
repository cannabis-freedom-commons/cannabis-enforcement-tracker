Customs officers at Monastir airport arrested a 35-year-old Tunisian man who had
arrived from France and had been living there since 2012. [@1] Officers seized
22 kilograms of cannabis from the traveller, which he had hidden in chocolate
boxes and coffee packets. [@1] A second suspect living in Tunisia, described as
the traveller’s accomplice, was also arrested. [@1] Two further suspects were
identified and are wanted under search warrants. [@1]

Farid Ben Jha, spokesperson for the Monastir and Mahdia courts, announced the
case in a statement to the media on Thursday 13 April 2023. [@1] During
questioning, the detained traveller said he had obtained the 22 kilograms
through another man who also lives in France. [@1] He said that man had asked
him to carry the drugs into Tunisia and hand them to a third suspect who lives
in Monastir. [@1] The seizure points to cannabis being smuggled by air from
France into Tunisia, concealed in ordinary food packaging. [@1]
