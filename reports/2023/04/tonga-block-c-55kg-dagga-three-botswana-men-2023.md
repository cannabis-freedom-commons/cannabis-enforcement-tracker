Tonga SAPS officers arrested three Botswana nationals on the night of 20 April
2023 after finding dagga in their car. [@1] The vehicle held three men aged
between 33 and 46. [@1] All three suspects were arrested and the vehicle was
impounded for further investigation. [@1]

Officers on patrol spotted a white Toyota Grande with Botswana number plates in
Block C near Tonga. [@1] Police said they could smell dagga but could not locate
it in the car. [@1] Officers took the vehicle to the police station and found
over 408 rolls of dagga weighing 55 kg stashed in the door panels. [@1] A second
account also describes the case as three suspects nabbed with 55 kg of dagga.
[@2]

Police said the consignment was destined for Botswana. [@1] The three men were
expected to appear in the Nkomazi Magistrate's Court on 24 April 2023 for
dealing in dagga. [@1]
