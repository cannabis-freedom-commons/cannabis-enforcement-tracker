Moroccan national security and customs officers seized 5,438 kg of cannabis
resin on Saturday aboard an international freight truck at the Tanger Med
port. [@1,2]

A Directorate General of National Security (DGSN) statement said the chira, in
plate form, was hidden in specially fitted cavities in the truck's trailer and
chassis as it prepared to sail for Spain. [@1,2]

The 45-year-old Moroccan driver was arrested and taken into custody after
investigators found he had tried to use falsified export documents; a judicial
investigation was opened to find other suspects linked to the smuggling.
[@1,2]

Morocco is the world's largest producer of cannabis resin according to the
United Nations, and its police seized nearly 100 tonnes in 2022. [@1]
