The Verviers criminal court sentenced three defendants for drug-law offences
linked to cannabis plantations in Dison, Herstal and Verviers. [@2] The main
defendant, a man born in 1973, was involved in all three plantations. [@1] The
court found he headed a criminal organisation and gave him five years in prison.
[@1] His two co-defendants received prison terms of three years and 18 months
respectively. [@1]

The investigation began on 22 December 2021 with suspicions about a cannabis
plantation on rue Albert 1er in Dison. [@1] A search there uncovered equipment,
fertiliser and lamps, plus five grow rooms holding a total of 2,350 cannabis
plants. [@1] The investigation then uncovered a second grow site on rue Pépin in
Herstal and a third on avenue Mullendorf in Verviers. [@1] At the Verviers site,
investigators found two grow rooms with 1,369 pots filled with potting soil.
[@1]

The court ordered the confiscation of 63,700 euros in illegal proceeds as well
as three vehicles belonging to the main defendant. [@1] The main defendant was
also ordered to pay 6,621 euros in procedural costs. [@1] The two co-defendants
were ordered to pay procedural costs of 2,495 euros and 1,288 euros
respectively. [@1]
