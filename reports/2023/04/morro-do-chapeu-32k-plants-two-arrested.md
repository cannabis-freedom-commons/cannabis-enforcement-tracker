Teams from Cipe Caatinga, Rondesp Chapada and the 24th Independent Military
Police Company eradicated a cannabis plantation at the Mônica settlement in
Morro do Chapéu, in Bahia's Chapada Diamantina, on the morning of Thursday 6
April 2023. [@2] The plantation stood at the Mônica settlement, a village in the
municipality of Morro do Chapéu. [@1] The officers pulled the crop out of the
ground and burned it on the site. [@1]

Two men accused of growing and maintaining the crop were arrested at the scene.
[@1] The two were arrested in the act and taken to the Morro do Chapéu
territorial police station. [@2] Police also destroyed an improvised camp the
growers had used at the plantation. [@1]

Major Ednaldo Siqueira Vieira, commander of Cipe Caatinga, said officers
gathered intelligence before reaching the plantation. [@1] The Bahia Public
Security Secretariat said police found about 320,000 cannabis plants across the
state in the first quarter of 2023. [@1] The operation was carried out jointly
by Cipe Caatinga, Rondesp Chapada and the 24th Independent Military Police
Company. [@3]
