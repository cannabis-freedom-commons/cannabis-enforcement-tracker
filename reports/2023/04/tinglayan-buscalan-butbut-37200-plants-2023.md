Police and anti-drug agents uprooted and burned about 37,200 fully grown
marijuana plants in four plantations in Tinglayan, Kalinga, on 29 and 30 April
2023. [@1] Police valued the destroyed plants at about 7.4 million pesos, the
standard value officials put on the crop. [@1] Police Captain Ruff Manganip, the
Kalinga police information officer, said about 22,500 of the plants were found
in two plantations in Barangay Buscalan. [@1] A further 14,700 fully grown
plants were discovered in two plantations in Barangay Butbut, giving 37,200
plants in all. [@1]

Manganip said the plants had been grown on public land that is treated as a
communal forest. [@1] Police said they were still investigating who cultivated
the plants, and no caretakers were arrested. [@1]
