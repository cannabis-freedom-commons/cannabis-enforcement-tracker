The detainees were taken to the MNB department in Kerki, where after questioning
they gave information about two people for whom the load was intended. [@1] Two
Kelif residents born in 1968 and 1975 were later detained and questioned to
identify a distribution network on the Turkmen side. [@1]

Turkmen special services had learned of the planned drug shipment on 19 April
and prepared a detention operation. [@1] At 3 a.m. the smugglers crossed the
river by boat between Shortepa district in Balkh region of Afghanistan and Kelif
village in Koytendag etrap of Lebap velayat. [@1] Servicemen of the Kerki border
detachment and officers of an MNB special unit waited for them on motorboats at
the Turkmen bank. [@1] After the officers switched on searchlights and ordered
the men to raise their hands, the smugglers jumped into the water and tried to
swim away but were detained. [@1] Operational information indicated there should
have been three smugglers, with one account saying the third stayed on the
Afghan bank and another saying he escaped. [@1]

The report places the crossing at Kelif village in Koytendag etrap of Lebap
velayat. [@1]
