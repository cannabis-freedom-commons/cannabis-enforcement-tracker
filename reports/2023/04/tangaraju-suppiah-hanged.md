Tangaraju s/o Suppiah, 46, was hanged at dawn on 26 April 2023 at Changi Prison despite clemency
pleas from his family, activists and the United Nations. [@1] The Central Narcotics Bureau had
scheduled the capital sentence for that date after he was convicted of abetting the trafficking of
1,017.9 grams of cannabis, more than twice the 500-gram threshold for a mandatory death sentence.
[@2]

## Conspiracy without the drugs

In September 2013 he was not found with the cannabis or during the delivery. [@1] The High Court
found that he had abetted a courier, Mogan Valo, by conspiracy to deliver 1,017.9 grams to him,
including by using two phone numbers linked to the delivery. [@3] He denied owning one of the
phones and said he had lost the other. [@1] The judge concluded he was not a mere courier eligible
for the alternative life sentence, and prosecutors did not certify substantive assistance against
trafficking networks. [@3]

## Appeals and protests

He was sentenced to death on 9 October 2018 and the Court of Appeal dismissed his appeal on 14
August 2019. [@2] A petition to the president failed, and a last-minute review application was
dismissed on 25 April 2023. [@1,2] His relatives collected his body outside the prison after the
hanging. [@1] Singapore authorities said he had had a fair trial with legal counsel throughout.
[@1,2]
