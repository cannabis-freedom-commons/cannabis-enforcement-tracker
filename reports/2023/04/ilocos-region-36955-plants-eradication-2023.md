The Police Regional Office 1 (PRO-1) said its officers uprooted and burned at least 36,955 fully grown marijuana plants in separate operations across Pangasinan, La Union and Ilocos Sur between 14 and 23 April 2023. [@1]

## Seizures by province

In La Union, officers also destroyed at least 15,500 seedlings and 20,000 seeds, while in Ilocos Sur 10kg of marijuana leaves and 360 seedlings were burned. [@1] The haul was valued at more than P9 million, and the report names no arrests. [@1]
