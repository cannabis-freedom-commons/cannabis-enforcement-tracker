Polish and Spanish police, with Europol support, took down an international
network that smuggled marijuana from Spain to Poland hidden in refrigerated
lorries carrying fruit, vegetables and potatoes. [@1,2]

On 23 April 2023, coordinated raids in both countries led to 14 arrests in
Poland and 6 in Spain, with cash worth more than half a million euros seized in
various currencies. [@1,2] Over the previous year, actions against the network
brought 42 arrests in total, including the ringleaders, and seizures of over
260 kilograms of marijuana, amphetamine and cocaine with a street value of
about 2.9 million euros. [@1,2]

The lorries ran from Spain to Poland through the Netherlands and Germany, and
investigators found links between the network's members and football hooligan
groups. [@1] In an earlier strike in the same investigation, Poland's Central
Bureau of Investigation arrested seven suspects in April 2022 after finding
158 bags of marijuana in potato trucks from Spain, plus 51 more bags at their
homes; investigators put the network's marijuana smuggling at 3 tonnes worth
about 30 million euros. [@1]

In May 2022, Polish prosecutors in Tarnow had already charged 14 defendants
over cannabis packaged in the Alicante area and trucked to Poland in fruit and
vegetable lorries, in proceedings aided by the FBI and Europol. [@3] No court
outcomes for any of the suspects have been reported. [@1,2,3]
