Colombia's Navy intercepted a boat crewed by three foreign men in the southern
Pacific. [@2] The three men were detained in the operation and their names and
nationalities were not published. [@2] Officers took the detainees, the boat and
the load to the Coast Guard Station at Tumaco in the southwest for verification
by prosecution investigators. [@2] The detainees must answer before judicial
authorities for alleged trafficking, manufacture or possession of narcotics.
[@3]

Officers found 96 sacks of marijuana aboard the intercepted boat. [@1] Technical
tests by prosecution investigators set the load's net weight at 2,915 kilograms
of marijuana. [@2] Authorities estimated the load's approximate cost at 18
million dollars on the international illegal market. [@3] The boat was
travelling at high speed in the southern Pacific when coastguard units located
it. [@2]

The maritime interdiction was carried out by coastguard units with international
support in the general southern Pacific area. [@2] Lieutenant Colonel Lincoln
Jaimes, chief of staff of Anti-Narcotics Task Force No. 72 Poseidon, said Navy
personnel intercepted the vessel. [@2] The marijuana load was apparently bound
for Central America. [@1] The Navy announced the seizure that Sunday. [@2]
