Kenado Thomas, a 35-year-old Turks and Caicos pilot from South Caicos, was
arrested by local police on 23 April 2023 at Norman Manley International Airport
in Kingston when packages reportedly totalling 18 pounds of marijuana were found
in his luggage as he prepared to depart on an interCaribbean Airways flight.
[@1] He was granted bail in the Kingston and St Andrew Parish Court in the sum
of $2 million Jamaican dollars (about $12,900 US dollars), with up to two
sureties. [@2] He was charged with possession, dealing in, conspiracy to export
and attempting to export marijuana, and was ordered to surrender his travel
documents and remain in Jamaica while his case was set to return to court on 12
June 2023. [@1]

On 1 November 2024, now 37, Thomas pleaded guilty in the Kingston and St Andrew
Parish Court and was fined $15,000 for possession of ganja and $260,307 for
attempting to export ganja, a total of $275,307, with no prison term imposed.
[@3] The charges of conspiracy to export ganja and dealing in ganja were
dismissed. [@3]
