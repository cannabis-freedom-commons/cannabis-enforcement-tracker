Two detainees in a cell at the Ducos men's remand unit (maison d'arrêt des
hommes) were the target of a cell search on the morning of Wednesday 26 April
2023. [@1] Prison officers searched the cell in living unit 20 from about 9 a.m.
after suspecting it held contraband. [@1] In the two prisoners' personal effects
the officers found 100 grams of cannabis resin and herb and a smartphone. [@1]

The two detainees face a disciplinary procedure before the prison's disciplinary
commission. [@1] The prison's management referred the case to the Fort-de-France
public prosecutor for any legal follow-up. [@1]

The FO Justice prison union said the cannabis would have entered the prison
through family visiting rooms. [@1] The union's Patrick Louvounou said the
prison should continue searches and plan sector searches with the gendarmerie's
dog unit, and that the penitentiary law bars officers from body searches. [@1]
FO Justice also asked the prison administration and the Mission pour les
Outre-Mer to formally recognise the officers' work. [@1]
