A police patrol in Cessange stopped a driver early on Monday afternoon because
of his irresponsible driving. [@1] The driver took a rapid drug test, which came
back positive. [@1]

During the search that followed, the driver handed over a bag containing almost
a kilogram of marijuana. [@1] Officers also seized a large amount of cash and a
precision scale. [@1] The man was arrested on the orders of the public
prosecutor's office and presented to an examining magistrate. [@1]

The German-language account attributes the details to the Grand-Ducal Police and
says the drugs and other items relevant to the crime were seized on the
prosecutor's orders. [@2] The police statement says the driver handed officers a
bag holding almost 1 kilogram of marijuana. [@2]

In the early evening, police separately arrested a person at Place de la Gare in
Luxembourg City and seized 63 pellets of cocaine and heroin. [@1] That second
suspect was arrested on the prosecutor's orders and presented to an examining
magistrate on Tuesday morning. [@1]
