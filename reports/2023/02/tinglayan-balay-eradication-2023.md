The Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency (PDEA) and the Philippine National
Police (PNP) ran a marijuana eradication campaign at two sites in Sitio Balay,
Tinglayan, Kalinga, on 18 to 20 February 2023. [@1]

They uprooted full-grown marijuana plants estimated at P17.8 million and
torched them on site. [@1] The operation was reported by PDEA Cordillera
chief Julius Paderes at a 20 February regional development council meeting in
Baguio City, alongside other February operations including drug-den raids in
Baguio, Lagawe and Bontoc. [@1]
