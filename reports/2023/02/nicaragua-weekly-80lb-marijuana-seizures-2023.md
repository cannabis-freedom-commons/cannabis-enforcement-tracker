Police detained Mario Antonio Salgado Castro in El Tuma-La Dalia, Matagalpa,
seizing 38 pounds of marijuana and two motorcycles. [@1] In El Cuá, Jinotega,
police arrested Pablo Martínez Hernández, Juan Junior Herrera Silva and Ana
Yarely Siles Martínez, seizing 21 pounds of marijuana and two motorcycles. [@1]
In Jalapa, Nueva Segovia, José Miguel Hernández Cárdenas was detained with 10
pounds of marijuana. [@1] In Estelí, Mileydi Tatiana Sánchez Medina was captured
with six pounds of marijuana. [@1] In Managua, Eddy Orellana Lizano and Leyton
Williams Clair were detained with four pounds of marijuana. [@2] In León, José
Miguel García Vásquez and Benito José Lezama Reyes were detained with one pound
of marijuana. [@2]

Police put the week's marijuana total at 80 pounds, about 36.29 kilograms. [@2]
The same weekly results also included cocaine seizures, which are not counted in
the cannabis total. [@2]

The seizures were part of citizen-security operations carried out from 6 to 12
February 2023. [@2] Commissioner Major César Cuadra announced the results on
behalf of the National Police. [@1]
