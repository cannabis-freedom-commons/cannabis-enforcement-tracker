Spain's Customs Surveillance Service (SVA) seized 5,400 kg of hashish resin from
a fishing vessel off Isla Cristina, in Huelva province, in an operation announced
on 4 February 2023. [@1] Officers found 150 bales of hashish in the hold of the
Portuguese-flagged vessel Praia de Montegordo. [@1,2]

## Night-time transfer intercepted

The operation began in the early hours of 1 February, when a customs aircraft
spotted an inflatable boat with powerful engines running without lights about
15 miles off Isla Cristina and heading for the coast. [@1,2] Air and naval units
then shadowed the speedboat in secret until it came alongside the fishing vessel
and the two crews began moving the cargo. [@1] The Huelva-based SVA patrol boat
Alcatraz boarded the fishing vessel just as the transfer was under way. [@1,2]

The four crew members, one Portuguese and three Spanish nationals, were arrested
on suspicion of smuggling and crimes against public health. [@1,2] The vessel
was taken to the port of Huelva, where a full search confirmed the 150 bales
weighing 5,400 kg. [@1]

## Case handed to the courts

The drugs, the detainees, the vessel and the rest of the evidence were placed at
the disposal of the duty judge of Ayamonte Investigating Court No. 4. [@1] The
Tax Agency said the investigation remained open and further arrests could not be
ruled out. [@1]
