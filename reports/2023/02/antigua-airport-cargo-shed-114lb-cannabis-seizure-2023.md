Police and customs officers searching the Cargo Transit Shed at V. C. Bird
International Airport found two E-containers and a cardboard box holding 114
packages of cannabis. [@1] The cannabis weighed 114 pounds, about 51.7 kg, and
was valued at $684,000. [@1] The Observer reported that it was the third
cannabis seizure by the two law-enforcement agencies within 72 hours. [@1] No
arrest had been made in that seizure or in the other two. [@1]

Two days earlier, on Tuesday 21 February, police and customs found a cardboard
box with five packages of cannabis, weighing five pounds and valued at $30,000,
at the airport's Intransit Cargo Shed. [@4] On Monday 20 February, twelve
vacuum-sealed packages of cannabis were found hidden in two cardboard boxes at
the General Post Office. [@4] The Observer said the three seizures that week
together came to 131 pounds of cannabis with an estimated value of $786,000.
[@1]

Antigua News Room published the same account of the cargo-shed search on the
same day. [@2] Antigua News also carried the same account of the airport
seizure. [@3]
