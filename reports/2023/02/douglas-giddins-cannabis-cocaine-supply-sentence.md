William Giddins was jailed for three years and 11 months. [@1] Douglas
Courthouse heard that Giddins had admitted selling both drugs for some time.
[@1] Police saw Giddins trying to hand a plastic wrapper to another man in a
doorway in the early hours of 25 June. [@1] The doorway was at a shop in an
alley off Howard Street, where officers were patrolling at about 02:20 BST. [@1]
Police also found cannabis on Giddins when they searched him. [@1] A search of
his bedroom uncovered a rucksack under his desk containing snap bags and digital
scales. [@1]

Giddins initially admitted taking drugs but denied selling them. [@1] After his
finances were questioned, he told police he had dealt cocaine for several weeks
and cannabis for several months. [@1] He pleaded guilty to being concerned in
the supply of cocaine and cannabis, and to possession of cocaine and ketamine.
[@1]

Prosecutors told the BBC that the cannabis sold was worth up to £9,680. [@1]
Isle of Man Today reported that the cannabis supplied weighed 363–484 g and was
valued at £7,380–£9,680. [@2] While on bail, he was found with personal amounts
of cannabis and cocaine on 12 February 2023. [@2] Prosecutor James Robinson
described Giddins as a low-level dealer who had not profited significantly. [@2]
