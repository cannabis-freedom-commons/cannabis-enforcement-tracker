Isle of Man police said Operation Artemis, which ran from February to October
2022, seized 119lb (54kg) of cannabis with a street value of £1m. [@1] The force
said cannabis had been hidden in concealed compartments in the seats or consoles
of five cars. [@1] Police said the operation also found drugs posted to the
island in wrapping paper and hidden in tyres on a shipping pallet. [@1] Det Insp
Jamie Tomlinson said almost £250,000 set to leave the island was intercepted,
including £11,940 found in a pram. [@1]

The force made 22 arrests of residents of the Isle of Man and the UK, which led
to 14 people being convicted of drugs and money-laundering offences. [@1] Chief
Constable Gary Roberts said a "long-standing criminal group" had been
"dismantled" and that the operation exposed the exploitation of vulnerable and
young people on the island. [@1] Roberts said the drugs seized came mainly to
the island via sea links and "emanated largely from Liverpool". [@1]

Manx Radio reported that the 14 people convicted were imprisoned for a combined
39 years and four months. [@2] Manx Radio gave the street value of the cannabis
as more than £1,080,884.21 and the criminal cash recovered as more than
£247,170.00. [@2] Manx Radio reported that the cash recovered was destined for
Merseyside and that five vehicles fitted with hidden compartments were seized.
[@2]

Energy FM reported that Operation Artemis targeted organised criminal groups
based in the United Kingdom and on the Isle of Man which supplied large amounts
of cannabis. [@3] Energy FM recorded 22 arrests, 14 convictions and a combined
39 years and four months in prison, and that one long-standing group had been
dismantled. [@3]
