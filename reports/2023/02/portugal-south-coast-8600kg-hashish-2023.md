Portuguese marines operating from the patrol vessel NRP Drago intercepted two
high-powered semi-rigid boats in international waters about 70 nautical miles
(130 km) south of mainland Portugal. [@1,2] An Air Force maritime patrol had
tracked the boats' suspicious movements before the Navy closed in and boarded
them. [@1,2] The operation seized around 8,600 kg of hashish and led to the
arrest of eight men aged between 34 and 40. [@1,2]

The seized boats were escorted to the port of Faro, the nearest port, so the
investigation could continue on land. [@1] The detainees were to be presented
to the judicial authority, and the Judiciary Police kept the investigation.
[@1] The joint operation brought together the Navy, the Air Force, the
Maritime Authority and the Judiciary Police; a second boat was stopped about
80 nautical miles off the coast. [@1,2]
