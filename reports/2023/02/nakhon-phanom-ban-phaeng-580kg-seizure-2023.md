A Mekong Riverine Unit navy patrol seized 580 kg of dried cannabis in Ban Phaeng district of Nakhon Phanom on Monday 27 February 2023. [@1] The seizure followed a tip-off: a patrol chased a black Chevrolet pickup, whose driver abandoned it near Noen Khanung village in tambon Ban Phaeng and fled. [@1]

The pickup carried a bag with 400 bricks of compressed dried cannabis with flowers and buds, about 1 kg each, plus another bag holding 180 kg. [@1] The unit commander, Capt Kasidi Klinseesuk, said the cannabis was believed smuggled across the Mekong river from Laos and bound for foreign markets. [@1]

He said cannabis was no longer listed as an illegal narcotic but a permit was required to possess its flowers and buds for medical dispensing. [@1] Smuggling cannabis into the country violated the Customs Act and the Plant Quarantine Act, he said. [@1]
