Jean-Hubert Celerine, alias Franklin, was arrested in Mauritius on 7 February
2023. [@1] Officers of the Mauritian anti-corruption commission arrested him at
his home in Rivière-Noire and took him to the Bambous court. [@1] Le Mauricien
confirmed that the Rivière-Noire resident was arrested after questioning
following the freezing of his assets and would be taken to the Bambous court.
[@2] He was then placed in detention until 20 February, with a further court
appearance scheduled for 14 February. [@1]

He was not then accused of drug trafficking but was suspected of laundering 25
million rupees through real estate. [@1] Mauritius Supreme Court had ordered his
bank accounts frozen on 31 January 2023 after his lifestyle attracted attention.
[@1]

Franklin had been convicted in absentia on 2 July 2021 by the Saint-Denis
correctional court and sentenced to seven years in prison for transport,
possession, offer and acquisition of narcotics, with an arrest warrant issued
against him. [@1] He was described as the alleged organiser of zamal boat
traffic between the two islands from January 2017 to April 2018. [@1] On 14
January 2023 he held a press conference denying Reunion drug involvement and
claiming mistaken identity. [@1]

After Mauritian media published details of the Reunion conviction on 5 February
2023, he sought a Supreme Court injunction to block extradition to Reunion,
which the judge rejected. [@1]
