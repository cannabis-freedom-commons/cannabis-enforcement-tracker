Michael Fitzsimmons, a 39-year-old from Liverpool, was arrested after police
found drugs worth thousands of pounds at a holiday property in Port Erin on 23
October, before the February 2023 sentencing. [@1] Police went to the
Airbnb-registered property in Erin Way at about 19:00 BST after a report of a
domestic incident. [@1] Fitzsimmons and his girlfriend, who was on the island
with him, denied they had been arguing and said they had been shouting at a dog.
[@1] Asked about the strong smell of cannabis when they opened the door, he said
he had "just smoked a joint" and that there was one more in the property. [@1]

A search found a red plastic bag on a chair holding £1,744 of cannabis and a
further £38 worth in a brown box. [@1] Officers also found wraps of ketamine
valued at up to £3,180, digital weighing scales and four mobile phones. [@1]
About £700 in cash was in a black bag, with a further £1,100 found in a coat.
[@1]

Fitzsimmons pleaded guilty to possession of cannabis with intent to supply,
possession of ketamine with intent to supply and possession of criminal
property. [@1] Douglas Courthouse jailed the 39-year-old for 21 months. [@1]
Deemster Graeme Cook also made an exclusion order banning him from returning to
the island for five years after his release. [@1]
