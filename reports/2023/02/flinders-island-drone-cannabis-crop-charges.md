Officers from the Northern Drug Investigation Services executed several search
warrants on Flinders Island earlier in the week, acting on information about
cannabis cultivation and distribution in the area. [@1,2] Police also used a drone
to conduct reconnaissance and pinpoint the illegal cannabis crop. [@1,2]

One of the searches netted a crop of cannabis plants along with other drug-related
items. [@1,2] One man will be proceeded against for cannabis cultivation, firearms
offences and wildlife offences, and another for cannabis possession and sales,
while two other people face minor drug charges. [@1,2] Officers also ran two
traffic operations on the island targeting drugs, alcohol and vehicle roadworthiness
while the searches were under way. [@1,2]
