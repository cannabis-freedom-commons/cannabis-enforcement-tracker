French customs officers arrested a 32-year-old Romanian lorry driver at the Tavel rest area on
16 February 2023 after a sniffer dog found 50 kilograms of cannabis hidden in a specially built
cache among his load of vegetables. [@1,2]

The driver said it was his first time carrying anything illegal, while the prosecutor noted he
had driven the same truck for seven years and requested six years in prison with a permanent
ban from France. [@1,2]

At an immediate hearing on Tuesday 21 February the court sentenced him to four years in prison,
a €270,000 customs fine instead of the €500,000 sought, and a ten-year ban from France. [@1,2]
