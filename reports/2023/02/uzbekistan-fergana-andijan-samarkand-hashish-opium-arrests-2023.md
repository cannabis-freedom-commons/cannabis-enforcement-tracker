Uzbek security service officers and police broke up drug trafficking in Fergana,
Andijan and Samarkand regions. [@2]

Officers stopped a car in Altyaryk district, Fergana region, with two men
carrying 1.841 kg of hashish that a courier from Tajikistan had smuggled in and
hidden in Pap district, Namangan region. [@1] Both had served sentences for
theft and returned to crime after their release, the security service said. [@1]

A Margilan man was detained selling 964 grams of hashish brought from Kyrgyzstan
for 8,000 dollars. [@1] Two residents of Bulakbashi district were detained in
Khodjaabad district, Andijan region, selling 630 grams of hashish smuggled from
Kyrgyzstan for 5,800 dollars. [@1]

In Samarkand, officers detained a previously convicted resident of Taylak
district selling 352 grams of opium brought from Tajikistan for 5,000 dollars.
[@1] Criminal cases were opened under the relevant articles of Uzbekistan's
Criminal Code and investigators were still working on all four. [@1]

Gazeta.uz said the security service seized almost 4 kg of drugs in all. [@2]
