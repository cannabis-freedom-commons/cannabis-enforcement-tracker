Twiggs County sheriff's deputies stopped a Dodge Charger on Interstate 16 and
arrested the three men inside after finding marijuana in the car. [@1] The
deputies noticed the Charger was driving too slowly in the left lane and had
windows that appeared too darkly tinted. [@1]

Deputies found loose marijuana in the passenger-side compartments and two trash
bags between the back seat and a speaker in the trunk. [@1] The sheriff's office
said the two bags held 31 pounds of marijuana, about 14.1 kilograms. [@1]

22-year-old Sharif Golden, 23-year-old Derrick Dupont and 23-year-old Johnny
Harris were charged with trafficking marijuana and other violations. [@1] 41NBC
reported that the stop happened on a Tuesday, 28 February 2023. [@2] The three
men were booked into the Twiggs County Jail, and two of them had extensive
criminal histories including RICO cases on illegal narcotics, according to
41NBC. [@2]

41NBC reported that Golden faced drug trafficking, drug possession and driving
while license withdrawn charges, Harris faced drug trafficking, and Dupont faced
drug trafficking and permitting an unauthorised person to drive. [@2]
