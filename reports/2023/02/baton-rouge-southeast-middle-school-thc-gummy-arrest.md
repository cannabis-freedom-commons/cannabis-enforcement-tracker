East Baton Rouge Sheriff's Office deputies arrested a 12-year-old sixth grader
who brought THC gummies to Southeast Middle School in Baton Rouge and shared
them with three other sixth graders. [@1] One student was taken to a hospital
after getting sick at school on Monday afternoon, and the child was expected to
be OK. [@1] The 12-year-old was booked into juvenile detention on charges of
distributing Schedule I drugs, bringing drugs into a school zone, and possession
of marijuana. [@1] The three other students were issued summonses for possession
of contraband on school property and released to their parents. [@1] Deputies
said the gummies were shared allegedly at the request of the three other
students. [@2] The East Baton Rouge Parish School System said one student was
transported off campus by EMS and another was vomiting, and that all parents of
students involved were contacted. [@2]
