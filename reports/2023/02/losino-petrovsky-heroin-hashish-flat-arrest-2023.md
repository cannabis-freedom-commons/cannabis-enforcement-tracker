Police detained a native of one of the former Soviet republics in the Moscow
Oblast town of Losino-Petrovsky on suspicion of drug trafficking, a case
announced by Interior Ministry spokesperson Irina Volk. [@1]

Officers of the Interior Ministry's Main Directorate for Drug Control, with
Moscow Oblast police and the Shchyolkovo police directorate, detained the
suspect, who police say organised a warehouse for large drug consignments at his
temporary home. [@1] In a rented flat in Losino-Petrovsky officers found more
than 51 kilograms of heroin and five kilograms of hashish, the cannabis product
in the case. [@1] Volk said the drugs had been hidden in cots and on the kitchen
and balcony of the flat. [@2] Volk said the suspect lived in the
Losino-Petrovsky urban okrug, north-east of Moscow. [@2] Police said the drugs
were meant for sale through hidden stash points across Moscow Oblast. [@1]

Volk put the whole consignment at about 80,000 single doses and said its
black-market value could reach 150 million roubles, a figure covering the heroin
and hashish together. [@1] A court placed the suspect in custody. [@1]
