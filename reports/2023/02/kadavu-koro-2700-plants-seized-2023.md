Fiji police officers seized more than 2,700 plants believed to be marijuana from
a farm near Kadavu Koro during drug raids carried out in the week before 26
February 2023. [@2] Chief Operations Officer Acting Assistant Commissioner of
Police Livai Driu said the seizures were made throughout the country, reflecting
the tempo of police drug operations. [@1]

During the same week of drug raids, officers seized white substances believed to
be methamphetamine in Nakasi. [@1] In the Northern Division, particularly in the
Labasa area, marijuana was seized following raids conducted by the Labasa
Taskforce team. [@3] Police said the week's drug raids resulted in seizures of
white drugs believed to be methamphetamine as well as plants believed to be
marijuana. [@3]

Driu said successful raids had been conducted in recent weeks as police received
more information from members of the public. [@1] He said that while teams were
pursuing new information on those involved in the illicit drug trade, similar
efforts were directed to revisiting well-known areas of cultivation and trade.
[@2]
