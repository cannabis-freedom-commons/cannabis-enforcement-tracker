Customs Anti-Narcotics Unit officers, acting on information, searched a property
on Norton Street, Wortmanville, Georgetown, on 21 February 2023 and found a
brick-like parcel that tested positive for cocaine. [@1] Agents then searched a
car bearing registration PHH 9387 on the same street, in the presence of its
driver and another occupant, and found three brick-like parcels of suspected
cannabis. [@1] The parcels tested positive for cannabis and weighed about 3.3
pounds (1.542 kilograms), which CANU valued at GY$500,000. [@1] Four people were
arrested in the operation: Andre Clarke, 50, of Norton Street, Wortmanville, and
Eon Martin, 45, of Pike Street, Kitty, over the cocaine find, and Chatterpaul
“Dutchie” Singh, 39, of First Street, Cummings Lodge, and Anesia Isfehani, 24,
of Crown Dam Industry, both East Coast Demerara, with the cannabis. [@1]

A Georgetown court sentenced Singh to four years in prison and fined him
GY$1,387,800 for trafficking in the 3.3 pounds of marijuana. [@2]

The cocaine parcel, found at the property rather than in the car, weighed 2.55
pounds (1.158 kilograms) and CANU put its street value at GY$1.1 million. [@1]
