Ahead of the 25 February 2023 general elections, operatives of Nigeria's National Drug Law
Enforcement Agency (NDLEA) stormed the Ujiogba Forest Reserve in Esan West LGA, Edo State,
where cartels had warehoused hundreds of jumbo bags of skunk for distribution. [@1,2]

The special operation of Wednesday 22 February followed credible intelligence, but could not
proceed until Strike Force officers cleared roadblocks set up by the drug lords' armed guards
and overpowered them in a gun duel. [@1,2] Two rifles, spent short-gun cartridges and an
empty AK-47 shell were recovered, and 24-year-old Calvary Essien was arrested. [@2]

After the gunfight, officers descended on a large warehouse built of wood and zinc holding
100 jumbo bags weighing 3.2 tons, 5,000 bags weighing 65 tons and 137 bags of cannabis
seeds weighing 4.66 tons, 72.286 tons in all. [@2] They also destroyed five hectares of
cannabis plantation in the reserve. [@1,2] Seven bags of cannabis and one bag of seeds were
kept for investigation and prosecution while the rest was set ablaze. [@2]
