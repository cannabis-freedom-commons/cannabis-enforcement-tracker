Ghana's Narcotics Control Commission (NACOC) burned 50,000 kilograms of seized
cannabis at the Bundase Military Training Camp in the Ningo-Prampram District of
the Greater Accra Region on 8 February 2023, at the end of a two-day destruction
exercise. [@1,2] The cannabis, valued at GH₵50 million, had been seized between
2021 and January 2023, mostly in the Volta and Eastern regions, where suspects
escaped arrest while their trucks, vans and cars were impounded at the
Commission's headquarters in Accra. [@1,2] The destruction followed a court
order, as section 108 of the Narcotics Control Commission Act (Act 1019)
requires, and officials from the court, police, military and Environmental
Protection Agency took part. [@1,2]

NACOC's acting director for public affairs and international relations, Francis
Opoku Amoah, said some suspects were standing trial while others had escaped
arrest, and warned drivers and vehicle owners that vehicles used by smugglers
would be seized. [@2] He added that cultivating cannabis in the country was
still illegal and culprits would be arrested and prosecuted. [@2]
