Morocco's National Judicial Police Brigade (BNPJ), working with the General
Directorate for Territorial Surveillance (DGST), stopped a drug shipment late in
the evening of Tuesday 26 September 2023 and seized 4,168 kilograms of chira,
the Moroccan name for cannabis resin. [@3] According to a statement from the
National Directorate of Security (DGSN), the operation took place on the
motorway linking Bouznika and Mohammedia. [@1]

The driver of the van carrying the seized drugs tried to drive against the flow
of motorway traffic to escape the pursuing police, then left the vehicle and
fled. [@1] Le360 reported on 27 September that the driver was still being
actively sought. [@2]

Searches of the van found 110 packages of cannabis resin weighing 4 tonnes and
168 kilograms in total. [@1] An investigation was opened under the supervision
of the competent public prosecutor's office to arrest everyone involved in the
criminal band's activity. [@1]
