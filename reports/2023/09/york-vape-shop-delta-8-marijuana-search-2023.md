The York-Poquoson Sheriff's Office and the Tri-Rivers Drug Task Force searched
the York Tobacco & Vape Shop in the Patriots Square shopping center in York
County, Virginia, on 15 September 2023. [@2] Sheriff Ron Montgomery said the
investigation began about a month earlier, after complaints from citizens and
concerned parents that illegal products were being sold at the shop, including
vapes to underage children. [@2]

Deputies seized Delta 8 pre-made blunts, 26 containers of prepackaged marijuana,
84 liquid THC packages and synthetic marijuana during the search. [@3]
Authorities said Delta 8 THC is illegal in Virginia after a law took effect on 1
July 2023. [@1] A store employee said Bezeid Khaomani, who was named in court
documents, no longer worked at the shop. [@1] Court documents say Khaomani sold
marijuana out of the store, and the records say authorities carried out
surveillance on the employee during illegal drug transactions. [@1]

Criminal charges were pending against two employees of the shop, and the
sheriff's office gave no weight for the marijuana seized. [@2] The only lawful
sales of marijuana in Virginia at the time were for medical use. [@1]
