Two people aged 35 and 43 were arrested at the Nerston port of entry on the
South Africa-Eswatini border after allegedly being found with dagga. [@1] The
two suspects were arrested for dealing in dagga. [@1] One suspect is a
35-year-old South African citizen and the other is a 43-year-old Zimbabwean
national without documentation to be in the country. [@1] Police said they were
working with home affairs officials to ascertain the Zimbabwean suspect's
status. [@1]

Border-policing officers on routine patrol stopped and searched a suspicious
Toyota Etios around 4.20pm on the R65 Nerston-Westoe Road while it was
travelling from Nerston toward Westoe. [@1] The car had Gauteng registration
plates and tinted windows. [@1] Officers found 38 bags of dagga weighing about
180.40 kilogrammes inside the vehicle. [@1] Police estimated the value of the
dagga at about R757,680. [@1]

The two men were expected to appear before the Amsterdam Magistrate's Court on
Monday after their Friday arrest. [@1] Mpumalanga provincial commissioner
Lieutenant General Semakaleng Daphney Manamela commended the officers for
intercepting the vehicle before the dagga could reach its destination. [@1]
