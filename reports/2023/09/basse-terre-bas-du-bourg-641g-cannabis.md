National police ran an identity-check operation in the Bas-du-Bourg district of
Basse-Terre on the evening of 21 September 2023 in the presence of the
Basse-Terre public prosecutor. [@1] Officers arrested four people during that
identity check in Bas-du-Bourg. [@1] The four were Dominican nationals in an
irregular situation on the territory and were held by border police officers.
[@1] Public-security police officers also issued a fixed criminal fine, known in
France as an amende forfaitaire délictuelle, to a person found carrying a small
quantity of cannabis herb. [@1]

On the ground nearby, officers discovered several abandoned plastic bags holding
14 packets of contraband cigarettes together with the drugs. [@1] Those bags
held 641 grams of cannabis herb and 23.58 grams of cannabis resin, along with
53.52 grams of crack, 119.03 grams of cocaine and 1.68 grams of methamphetamine.
[@1]

All of the products were seized for destruction. [@1] The national police
restated that fighting drug trafficking is a priority. [@1]
