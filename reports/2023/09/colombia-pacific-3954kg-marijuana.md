Colombian Navy units found two boats in different areas of the central Pacific,
one crewed by three men and the other by two, carrying bundles. [@2] The men,
the boats and the cargo were taken to port in the District of Buenaventura,
Valle del Cauca, where judicial authorities took charge of the case. [@1] Tests
in Buenaventura confirmed marijuana, 1,536 kg on one boat and 1,603 kg on the
other, and the five men were placed at the disposal of the authorities. [@2]

Two men on a third boat jumped into the sea when they saw the Navy, and a search
by two surface units and an aircraft from the Aerospace Force's Air Combat
Command No. 7 did not find them. [@2] That boat held 235 kg of cocaine
hydrochloride and 815 kg of marijuana, and the search for the two men continued.
[@2] In a fourth operation, a crew beached its boat and fled, leaving eight
sacks holding 240 kg of cocaine hydrochloride. [@2]

Across the four operations the Navy reported seizing 4,427 kg of drugs, of which
3,954 kg was marijuana and 473 kg cocaine hydrochloride. [@3] The Navy said the
cargo was being carried through Pacific waters towards Central America and that
the operations kept more than US$41 million from trafficking organisations. [@2]
The Navy said the four operations prevented two million doses of both drugs from
being sold around the world. [@1]
