California Department of Fish and Wildlife officers raided an unlicensed cannabis warehouse on the 300 block of Adeline Street in Oakland on 28 September 2023. [@1,2]

Officers eradicated 41,082 cannabis plants and destroyed 1,841 pounds (about 835 kg) of processed cannabis, which the agency valued at $36,930,300 retail. [@1,2] Three firearms were also recovered at the scene. [@1,2]

No one was arrested, though suspects were interviewed, and the department said the investigation was continuing. [@1,2] The agency called it one of the Bay Area's largest cannabis enforcement actions of the year by retail value. [@1,2]
