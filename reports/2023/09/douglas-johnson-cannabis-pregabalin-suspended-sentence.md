Adam Johnson, 26, of Cronk Y Voddy on the Isle of Man, was given an 11-month
prison sentence suspended for 18 months at Douglas Courthouse after admitting
cannabis supply, pregabalin supply, money laundering and drug-driving. [@1] He
pleaded guilty to possession of cannabis with intent to supply, two counts of
possession of Pregabalin with intent to supply, money laundering, and driving
while over the prescribed limit. [@1] Deemster Cook told him: "I'm going to give
you an opportunity to prove to everybody that you can leave crime behind you and
get on with the rest of your life." [@1] Johnson was also made subject to an
18-month supervision order and was banned from driving for two years. [@1]

Officers on patrol on the main road in Greeba stopped him on 8 December while he
was driving a Volkswagen Golf under the influence of drugs, pulling him over at
the Glen Helen car park. [@1] He appeared agitated and the car smelled of
cannabis, and he failed a roadside drug wipe. [@1] A blood sample later tested
positive for benzoylecgonine, a metabolite of cocaine. [@3]

A search of the car found blister packs holding 220 Pregabalin tablets worth
£880. [@1] Police valued the cannabis found at his home at £3,638; Manx Radio
reported the weight as 181.9 grams. [@2] The search of his home also found a
further 79 Pregabalin tablets, worth £316, and £5,465 in cash. [@1] His defence
advocate said there was nothing to suggest his driving had been particularly
poor and described him as a young man who still needed help with his drug
problem. [@1] Johnson must take an extended test before regaining his licence.
