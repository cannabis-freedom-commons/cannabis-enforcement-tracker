Grodno customs officers seized more than 38 kg of hashish concealed in the fuel
tank of a passenger car that had been brought from Lithuania as cargo. [@1] A
Lithuanian carrier was moving two passenger cars, a 2023 BMW X5 and a 2019 Opel
Insignia, on a truck through the Benyakoni crossing into the Eurasian Economic
Union. [@1] Customs officers sent the truck with the transported vehicles for
examination with an inspection-scanning complex. [@1] The resulting scan clearly
indicated hidden additions inside the fuel tank of one of the cars. [@2]

During the subsequent search, officers removed 29 briquettes of pressed
dark-brown substance from the fuel tank of the Opel Insignia. [@1] Forensic
experts at the Lida interdistrict department of the State Committee of Forensic
Examinations for the Grodno region confirmed the substance was hashish. [@1]

Grodno Regional Customs opened a criminal case under part 2 of article 328-1 of
the Criminal Code on illegal movement of narcotic drugs across the border. [@1]
The charge carries up to 10 years of imprisonment. [@1]
