On 6 September 2023 soldiers of Nicaragua's 6th Regional Military Command seized
and burned 8,000 marijuana plants in the community of Pauniwás, Wiwilí
municipality, Jinotega department. [@1] The army said the plants stood two
metres tall and were sown among a maize crop. [@1] During the operation troops
detained Nicaraguan citizens Bismark López Martínez, 36, and Adolfo Martínez,
32, the army saying the two cared for the plot. [@1] The army said both
detainees were handed over to the corresponding authorities. [@1]

The wire agency EFE reported that the two men were handed to the competent
authorities for the judicialisation of the case, but not the evidence, because
it had been incinerated. [@2] EFE said the operation brought the army's
marijuana seizures in the preceding 12 days to 20,000 plants, with six people
detained in connection with the plots. [@2]
