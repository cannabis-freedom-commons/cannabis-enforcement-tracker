Four Israelis were arrested for cannabis possession at a Ukrainian police
checkpoint on the road to Uman. [@2] Ukraine police held the four at the border
crossing near the city of Vinnytsia, about an hour and a half from Uman,
according to Israel's Foreign Ministry. [@3] One of the four was travelling with
his eight-year-old son, who stayed in police custody with his father, Channel 12
reported. [@1] The four were in Ukraine for the annual Jewish pilgrimage to
Uman, where thousands of mainly ultra-Orthodox Jews gather ahead of Rosh
Hashanah in a city described as one of the birthplaces of the Hasidic movement.
[@1]

The arrests came as Ukrainian police set up checkpoints on the roads to Uman and
at the grave complex of Rabbi Nachman of Breslov, after reports of extensive
drug use at the Rosh Hashanah celebrations. [@2] That year's celebrations were
to take place under tightened security because of the Russian war on Ukraine.
[@1] Israel's consul and the Foreign Affairs Ministry were handling the cases,
according to Israeli media. [@2] Cannabis remained illegal in Ukraine at the
time, although the country had taken steps to legalise its medical use, while
Israel had decriminalised possession of small amounts. [@1]
