Border Police drug officers working with PCCOCS prosecutors detained four people
from Chișinău and southern Moldova over a marijuana capture, the prosecutors
being the Procuratura pentru Combaterea Criminalității Organizate și Cauze
Speciale. [@2] The four detained men were aged between 34 and 45. [@2] Two
Chișinău residents were caught in the act after repeatedly selling drugs to an
undercover agent. [@2] The other two, from the south of the country, were
detained over the cultivation of the plants the Chișinău men received from them.
[@3] Radio Moldova reported that four people from Chișinău and the south of the
country were arrested in the case. [@1]

Officials said one detained man works as a dental technician, one as a
restaurant cook, one as an IT specialist, and the fourth is a defendant in
another drug-trafficking case PCCOCS prosecutors have already sent to court.
[@2] Border Police valued the marijuana taken out of circulation at about
2,500,000 lei on the black market. [@2] Radio Moldova put the seized marijuana
at roughly 2.5 million lei and said it had been grown in Chișinău, Comrat,
Basarabeasca and Leova. [@1] Realitatea.md reported that the marijuana was taken
out of circulation in Chișinău, Comrat, Basarabeasca and Leova. [@3] The
marijuana plants were grown both in greenhouses and in spots adapted for the
purpose in forest belts near the border. [@2]

The searches also recovered feminised cannabis seeds, genetically modified to
make cultivation easier, that had been smuggled out of the European Union. [@2]
At PCCOCS's request, Chișinău district court judges at the Ciocana seat ordered
all four held in pretrial detention for 30 days. [@2] Radio Moldova reported
that all four were placed in custody for 30 days at the prosecution's request.
[@1] Investigations continued, and the four retained the presumption of
innocence. [@2]
