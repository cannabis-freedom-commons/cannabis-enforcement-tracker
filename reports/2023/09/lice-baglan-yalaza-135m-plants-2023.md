Gendarmerie teams seized 2,121 kilograms of cannabis and destroyed
13,514,550 cannabis plants in the rural Bağlan and Yalaza neighbourhoods of
Lice district, Diyarbakır, the provincial governorate announced. [@1] The
plants comprised 12,759,450 cannabis plants and 755,100 skunk plants, while
the seized cannabis consisted of 715.5 kilograms of powdered cannabis and
1,405.5 kilograms of herbal cannabis. [@1]

The operation, named Jandarma Uzman Çavuş Ali Şirin, deployed 74 teams
totalling 1,120 personnel, including Lice commando units,
gendarmerie special operations and police special operations teams, village
guards, and anti-smuggling and counterterrorism units. [@1] Teams searched
129 separate locations using six heavy machines and 30 motorised trimmers,
and the seized drugs were taken into custody. [@1]

The governorate's statement, carried by the state news agency, names no
arrests and no suspects. [@1]
