Gouré departmental police detained one person during a police descent on a house
at Guidiguir where a large quantity of drugs was believed to be stored. [@1] The
operation was carried out by the Direction Départementale de la Police Nationale
of Gouré, the departmental police directorate for Gouré in Niger’s Zinder
region. [@1] On 22 September 2023 the directorate publicly presented the
resulting seizure to Gouré’s administrative, judicial and customary authorities.
[@1]

The seizure presented on that occasion consisted of 33 balls of cannabis taken
from the house at Guidiguir. [@1] Police gave the weight of those 33 balls as
about 17.049 kg in their communiqué. [@1] Guidiguir, where the house was
located, lies about 60 km from the town of Gouré. [@1] Police said they acted
after receiving information indicating that a house at Guidiguir was being used
to store an important quantity of drugs. [@1]

The descent was carried out on Monday 18 September 2023, and it both recovered
the cannabis and led officers to arrest the one individual. [@1] The
presentation brought together local administrative, judicial and customary
representatives to witness the seized cannabis balls. [@1]
