Officers of the network police department at Turksib station identified and
detained two residents of Zhambyl Region at the Karasu railway stop in Merki
district during operational search measures. [@1] A check of the two men's car
found 23 kilograms 140 grams of dried marijuana, an especially large amount,
which officers seized. [@1] The department head, Bakytzhan Malybayev, announced
the seizure. [@1]

A pre-trial investigation was opened under part 4 of article 296 of Kazakhstan's
Criminal Code, on unlawful storage and transport of drugs in an especially large
amount. [@1] Other information about the case was withheld under article 201 of
Kazakhstan's Code of Criminal Procedure as contrary to the interests of the
investigation. [@1]

The Interior Ministry's media outlet Polisia.kz carried the same statement on 19
September 2023, quoting Malybayev on the 23 kilograms 140 grams of dried
marijuana found in the car. [@2]
