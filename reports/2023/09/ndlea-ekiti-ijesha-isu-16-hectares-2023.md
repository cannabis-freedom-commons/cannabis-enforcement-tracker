Between Monday 11 and Wednesday 13 September 2023, NDLEA operatives stormed thick forests at
Ijesha Isu-Ekiti in the Ikole Local Government Area of Ekiti State, where they destroyed
40 tons (40,000 kg) of cannabis plants covering 16 hectares of farmland. [@1,2]

Agency spokesman Femi Babafemi disclosed the operation in a statement issued on Sunday
24 September, part of a weekly roundup in which the agency said it intercepted more than
four tons of other illicit drugs across seven states. [@1,2]
