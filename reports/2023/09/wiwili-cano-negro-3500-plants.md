On 16 September 2023 soldiers of Nicaragua's 6th Regional Military Command
seized and burned 3,500 marijuana plants in the community of Caño Negro, Wiwilí
municipality, Jinotega department. [@1] The army said the plants were 1.5 metres
tall and had been sown among a maize crop. [@1] During the operation troops
detained Nicaraguan citizen Cristian de Jesús Poveda Tinoco, 33, whom the army
described as the person who cared for and maintained the plantation. [@1]

The army said the detainee was handed over to the corresponding authorities.
[@1]

The operation was carried out under the state strategy "Muro de Contención"
(Containment Wall), the government's anti-drug campaign. [@1] The army said the
protective measures ordered by the Health Ministry (MINSA) against COVID-19 were
followed during the seizure and burning. [@1] The army information note was
dated 19 September 2023 at the headquarters of the 6th Regional Military
Command, three days after the operation. [@1]
