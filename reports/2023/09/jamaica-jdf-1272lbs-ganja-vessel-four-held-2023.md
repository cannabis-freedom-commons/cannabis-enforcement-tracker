The Jamaica Defence Force seized 1,272.9 pounds of marijuana during a joint Air
Wing and Second District Coast Guard operation on Thursday. [@1] In the wee
hours of Thursday morning, service members in a JDF helicopter spotted a vessel
in Jamaica's waters while on patrol. [@1] The Second District JDF Coast Guard
was alerted and intercepted the vessel. [@1] The four occupants of the vessel
were taken into custody. [@1] Two of the detained men were identified as
Nicaraguans. [@1]

A detailed board and search of the vessel found 20 large bags of marijuana. [@1]
The seized marijuana weighed 1,272.9 pounds, about 577.38 kg. [@1] The JDF said
money from this quantity of marijuana could be used to purchase approximately 15
rifles or 30 pistols. [@1]

The army called the seizure significant, saying it puts a major dent in the
drugs-for-guns trade. [@1] According to the army, it has been making significant
investments in its air and maritime assets to increase its capacity to safeguard
Jamaica's blue economy. [@1] The JDF urged fisherfolk and other seafarers to use
maritime spaces in accordance with the law and to report illegal activities to
the JDF. [@1]
