U.S. Customs and Border Protection officers at the World Trade Bridge in
Laredo, Texas, seized more than two tons of marijuana in a single enforcement
action on Thursday 14 September 2023. [@1,2] A CBP officer had referred a 2023
Freightliner tractor trailer manifesting home goods for secondary
inspection. [@1,2]

A thorough examination using a non-intrusive inspection system found 177
packages holding a total of 4,466 pounds of alleged marijuana inside the
trailer, about 2,025.74 kg. [@1,2] CBP put the street value of the drugs at
9,904,204 dollars. [@1,2]

CBP seized the narcotics and Homeland Security Investigations special agents
are investigating the seizure. [@1,2] Laredo Port of Entry Director Albert
Flores said officers had come up big and noted that marijuana seizures had
recently been rarer than harder narcotics. [@1,2]
