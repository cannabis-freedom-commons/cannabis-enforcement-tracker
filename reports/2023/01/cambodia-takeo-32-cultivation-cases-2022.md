Cambodian authorities found 32 marijuana cultivation cases in 2022 and arrested
five Cambodian growers, Lieutenant General Nak Yuthea, deputy secretary-general
of the National Authority for Combating Drugs, said. [@1] Nak Yuthea said Takeo
province, and Kiri Vong district in particular, is the country's largest area
for growing marijuana. [@1]

He said all 32 cases involved local people, and that growers with a few plants
are educated and released while those growing on a large scale for retail have
their plants destroyed and face prosecution. [@1] The government's anti-drug
campaign report for 2022 counted 6,290 drug crimes and 14,784 people arrested,
of nine nationalities. [@1] According to the report, 32 of the cases were for
cultivation and 2,007 for usage, and 10 cases were being processed by the
courts. [@1] The report said police confiscated more than 14 tons of marijuana,
over 60,000 cannabis plants and more than 500 tons of chemical ingredients. [@1]
The Anti-Drugs Police Department report of the same 2022 figures, released on 1
January 2023, said 259 of the detainees were foreigners of nine nationalities.
[@3] A second Khmer Times report on the 2022 national figures gave the dried
marijuana seized as 45 kilogrammes. [@2]
