Anti-narcotics police seized a large quantity of hashish from a warehouse in
Beit Ummar, north of Hebron, on Wednesday 4 January 2023. [@1] Al-Quds reported
the same Wednesday seizure from a warehouse in Beit Ummar. [@2]

Police said they acted on information that a drug dealer was keeping drugs in
the warehouse and obtained a search warrant from the Public Prosecution to
search it and seize what was inside. [@1] Police spokesman Colonel Louay
Arzeiqat said the warehouse belonged to one of the most dangerous drug dealers
in Hebron and had a tunnel underneath used to store drugs. [@1]

The search found 100 slabs of hashish weighing 10 kilograms, plus a quantity of
marijuana-plant seeds and a group of narcotic pills. [@1] Al-Quds gave the same
seizure figures of 100 hashish slabs weighing 10 kilograms, with marijuana seeds
and narcotic pills also found. [@2]

The dealer had managed to flee, and police said search and investigation efforts
were continuing to arrest him. [@1]
