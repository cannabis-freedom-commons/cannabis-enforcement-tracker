Highway police in Córdoba stopped a car on Ruta Nacional 158 near the entrance
to San Francisco, a city in Córdoba province, and found sealed packages of
pressed plant material inside. [@2] The smell led the officers to call in
Gendarmería Nacional, Argentina's federal gendarmerie, which ran the procedure
with the Córdoba provincial police. [@1] A second car, loaded with similar
packages, was found; the gendarmes counted 307 bricks of pressed plant material
in the first vehicle and 279 rectangular packages in the second. [@1]

Forensic tests by the Villa María highway squadron proved positive for
marijuana, and the total seized was 500 kilos 910 grams. [@1] La Voz del
Interior put the haul at 501 kilograms, carried in two white Chevrolet cars, a
Corsa and an Onix, and said the drivers were heading to Córdoba Capital to stock
drug sales points known locally as quioscos. [@2] The Federal Court of San
Francisco ordered that the two adults be held and that the minor be taken to an
institution in Córdoba city. [@1] La Voz reported three people detained: the
Corsa's 27-year-old driver from Alta Gracia, Córdoba, and a 23-year-old and a
16-year-old from the Yapeyú neighbourhood of Santa Fe city. [@2] The Corsa had
been reported stolen to the justice system of Rosario, and the vehicles were
seized along with the drug. [@2]

The federal magistrate issued four search warrants for addresses in Córdoba and
Santa Fe cities, which gendarmerie units carried out. [@1] The searches turned
up mobile phones and a shotgun. [@1] La Gaceta published the gendarmerie's
statement that more than 500 kilograms of marijuana were seized in San Francisco
with three people detained. [@3]
