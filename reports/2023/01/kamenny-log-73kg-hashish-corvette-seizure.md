Belarusian customs officers intercepted a Lithuanian driver in a 1976 Chevrolet
Corvette on the Belarusian side of the Kamenny Log-Miadininkai checkpoint on the
Belarus-Lithuania border. [@1] The driver was crossing the Belarusian-Lithuanian
border and used the red corridor to file a declaration for temporary import of
his car into the EAEU. [@1] He declared no other goods requiring written
declaration. [@1] His behaviour aroused the customs officers' suspicion. [@1]
Customs sent the vehicle for examination using an inspection-scanning complex.
[@1]

The intercepted load weighed 73 kg of hashish. [@1] A scan and follow-up search
found 134 briquettes of pressed dark-brown substance hidden in a structural
cavity in the rear of the car, closed off by a specially fitted false wall. [@1]
The substance was seized and sent for expert examination to the Lida
interdistrict department of the State Committee of Forensic Examinations for the
Grodno region. [@1] The expert conclusion confirmed that the substance was the
dangerous narcotic drug hashish. [@1]

The Grodno Regional Customs opened a criminal case under Part 2 of Article 328-1
of the Belarusian Criminal Code on illegal movement of narcotic drugs across the
border. [@1] The article carries a penalty of up to ten years of imprisonment, a
possible punishment rather than a sentence already imposed. [@1]
