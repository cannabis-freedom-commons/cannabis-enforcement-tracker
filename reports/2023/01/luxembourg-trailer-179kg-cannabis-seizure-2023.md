At the end of January 2023, Luxembourg customs officers checked a lorry trailer
travelling to Ghent in Belgium. [@1] A sniffer dog alerted the officers during
the check. [@1] A search of the trailer after the alert found around 179 kilos
of cannabis. [@1]

Customs confiscated the cannabis and launched an investigation. [@1] The trailer
check was described as one of several successful Luxembourg customs operations
against drug crime since the start of 2023. [@1]
