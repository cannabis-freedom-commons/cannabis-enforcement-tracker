A suspect found inside his farm house at Dogo Settlement, Nananu, Tailevu was
arrested and taken into custody at Korovou Police Station. [@1] The raid was
conducted at Dogo Settlement, Nananu, Tailevu. [@3] Officers from the Eastern
Division Taskforce, the Criminal Investigations Department and the K9 Unit
carried out the raid. [@2]

Officers seized more than 100 plants along with dried leaves and branches, all
believed to be marijuana. [@1] That Eastern Division Task Force raid earlier
last week discovered more than 100 plants, dried leaves and branches believed to
be marijuana. [@4]

Police linked the operation to community policing efforts and increased
information sharing between police and the community. [@1] Acting Assistant
Commissioner Driu said community support is vital in curbing the illicit drug
trade and that similar efforts are directed towards white drugs, not only
marijuana. [@1]
