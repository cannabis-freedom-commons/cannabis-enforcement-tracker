Two local residents, Gairat Zoidov and Rahmonali Mavlonov, were detained on
suspicion of this crime. [@2] Tajik police said they seized 8 kg of narcotics in
Pyandzh district, Khatlon Region, during operational search activities, an
announcement the Interior Ministry's press center issued. [@1]

In the second case, officers found about 747 g of methamphetamine in the trunk
of a Mercedes-Benz car owned by Mavlonov. [@1]

The English version of the report states that two suspects were arrested. [@2]

A criminal case was opened under article 200 part 4 of the Criminal Code, which
covers illegal trafficking in drugs and psychotropic substances and carries up
to five years of imprisonment. [@1]

During a search of the house of one suspect, Gairat Zoidov, officers seized 7 kg
231 g of hashish. [@1]

Police said the two detainees had been engaged in regular drug trafficking. [@1]
