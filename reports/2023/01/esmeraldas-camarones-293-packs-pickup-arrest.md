Police detained a man on the morning of Sunday 22 January 2023 in an operation
in the Camarones sector on the E15 highway north of Esmeraldas. [@2] The driver
was travelling in a pickup truck and tried to evade the police checkpoint before
several police units stopped him. [@2] Police identified the detained man only
as Erick S. [@2] He was placed at the disposal of the judicial authority, as
stated by Joffre López, chief of tactical operations of the Esmeraldas police
subzone. [@2]

Police found that the man had 293 packages of substances subject to inspection.
[@2] The packages were camouflaged in the trunk of a pickup truck. [@2] Testing
showed the load was positive for marijuana and weighed 438,800 grams. [@2]
Infobae described the same weekend seizure as 293 packages of marijuana in a
pickup trunk in Esmeraldas, equivalent to 438,800 grams. [@1]

Police said the drugs had apparently come from somewhere on the northern border
for distribution inside the country. [@2] Esmeraldas is the Ecuadorian province
that borders Colombia. [@1]
