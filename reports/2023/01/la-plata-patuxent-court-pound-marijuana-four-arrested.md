The Charles County Sheriff's Office's Neighborhood Enforcement Team was
investigating a suspicious vehicle on Patuxent Court in La Plata at 3:12 p.m. on
4 January 2023 when officers found a loaded handgun with an extended magazine.
[@1] In the vehicle, officers also found a pound of marijuana, packaging
materials and cash. [@1]

Four men from La Plata and Bel Alton, aged 20 to 27, were arrested and charged
with possession of a controlled dangerous substance with intent to distribute,
illegal possession of a regulated firearm and related gun charges. [@1] The
sheriff's office said every occupant of the vehicle was barred from possessing
firearms. [@1] One local outlet described the arrests as the result of a traffic
stop. [@2]

The four appeared before a District Court Commissioner on 5 January 2023, and
all were released on bond: two on recognisances and two after paying bail. [@1]
