Gerehu police raided a school compound in Port Moresby on Monday 16 January
2023 and uprooted seven marijuana plants. [@1,2] Officers found the plants
behind an elementary school classroom at Tete Settlement in Gerehu Stage Two
at around 5pm; the classroom had been vacant during the school holiday.
[@1,2] The plants were growing in a small vegetable garden among cassava,
banana and leafy vegetables, and more than five officers took part in the
operation. [@2]

Two suspects were arrested at the scene and detained at Gerehu police station
with the uprooted plants. [@1,2] Police said the suspects would be questioned
intensively before any charges were laid, with suggestions they could be
connected to a drug ring. [@2] National Capital District and Central police
commander Anthony Wagambie Jr commended the officers and warned city residents
involved in cultivating, smuggling or selling cannabis to stop. [@1,2] He
said penalties under the new drug legislation reached ten years in jail or a
fine of more than K10,000. [@1]
