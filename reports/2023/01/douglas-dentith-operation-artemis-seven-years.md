Garry Dentith, 41, described by the court as playing a "leading role" in an
organised crime group selling cannabis on the Isle of Man, was jailed for seven
years and three months. [@1] Dentith, of Princes Street in Douglas, pleaded
guilty at Douglas Courthouse to five counts of money laundering, being concerned
in the importation of cannabis, and being concerned in its supply. [@1] Sam
Leigh, 31, of Brown Close in Barnsley, admitted bringing the cannabis to the
island and was jailed for 32 months. [@1] Andrew McGill, 56, of Brunswick Road
in Douglas, admitted possessing cannabis and being concerned in its supply and
was jailed for 30 months. [@1]

Dentith was arrested after police raided his flat in October, and officers found
£12,900 in cash in an attic room along with plastic bags with cash amounts
written on them. [@1] McGill was arrested when police raided his home on
Brunswick Road in Douglas the same day and found the cannabis hidden in the
eaves of his attic. [@1] Officers recovered 8.5 kilograms of cannabis from
McGill's home along with scales, tick lists and vacuum packaging material. [@2]
McGill admitted possessing 8.5 kg of cannabis with a street value of £171,270
with intent to supply, the court heard. [@3] Leigh was stopped at Douglas's Sea
Terminal on 25 September 2022 as he prepared to sail to Liverpool, and a search
of his vehicle found almost £50,000 hidden under a passenger seat along with an
empty hide. [@2] The BBC reported that Leigh had brought the cannabis to the
island in a hidden compartment in a car. [@1]

McGill's advocate told the court he was a "gamer living a hermit lifestyle" who
had received £300 for his part in the operation. [@2] Deemster Graeme Cook told
Dentith he had played a leading role and must, in the judge's judgement, have
strong connections with organised crime in Liverpool. [@2] Dentith's advocate,
Paul Rodgers, argued that the leading role in the operation belonged to the
dealer in England. [@3] The three had been investigated as part of Operation
Artemis, a police operation that saw officers arrest people at the Sea Terminal
in 2022. [@2,4] Dentith pleaded guilty to five money-laundering counts concerning
£192,855 of criminal proceeds. [@3]
