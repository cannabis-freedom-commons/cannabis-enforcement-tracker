The Radhanagar Battalion of the Assam Rifles, Agartala sector, destroyed large-scale cannabis cultivation in forest land at Ramdhula Para in Unakoti district on 9 January 2023. [@1,2] The joint operation with the Sub-Divisional Police Officer of Kumarghat and Pecharthal police station followed inputs about the plantation from the force's own sources and the police. [@1]

The team destroyed about 12,000 plants growing on two acres (about 0.8 hectares) of forest land. [@1] State police valued the destroyed crop at about Rs 24 lakh. [@1] Police said they were working to find the cultivators; no arrests were reported. [@1]
