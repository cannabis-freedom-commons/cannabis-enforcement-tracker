About a ton of plants believed to be marijuana was uprooted from seven farms on
Kadavu Island and destroyed under approved court destruction orders. [@1] Livai
Driu, the police's Chief Operations Officer and Acting Commissioner of Police,
and a team of senior officers visited officers and drug operation teams based at
the Kadavu Police Station the previous week and witnessed the destruction. [@1]

Driu thanked the officers based in Kadavu and those involved in the drug
operations, noting the operations had been long with teams on rotation
throughout last year. [@1]

He called on the officers to strengthen community policing engagements on the
island because it was the most effective way to curb the illicit drug trade
there. [@1]
