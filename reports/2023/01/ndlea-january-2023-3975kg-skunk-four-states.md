Nigeria's National Drug Law Enforcement Agency (NDLEA) announced on 8 January
2023 that a week of interdiction operations had seized almost four tonnes of
skunk cannabis in Kaduna, Kano and Lagos States, with 11 suspects arrested.
[@1,2,3] Officers recovered 3,672 kg of cannabis sativa from a warehouse at
Rido village in Kaduna on 3 January, 73 kg from a suspect at Semugu in Kano on
7 January, and over 230 kg in raids on Festac Town and Lagos Island. [@1,2]
Outlets tallying all four-state raids report 3,975 kg of skunk and 12 people
detained, including five women. [@3]

At Lagos airport and port, officers intercepted 37.5 kg of cannabis
consignments concealed for export and import. [@1,2,3] A freight agent was
held on 6 January trying to export 1.5 kg hidden in foodstuffs to Dubai, and a
courier-company parcel of 11.5 kg hidden in a giant wooden sculpture from
Ghana bound for London was stopped. [@1,2] At Tincan port, 24.5 kg of cannabis
indica from Montreal hidden in a used Toyota Sienna was seized with one
accomplice arrested, while the importer stayed at large and an N8 million
bribe offered to officers was secured for prosecution. [@1,2]

Officers also seized tramadol pills in the same raids, which are outside this
tracker's cannabis figures. [@1,2,3] No court outcomes were reported. [@1,2]
