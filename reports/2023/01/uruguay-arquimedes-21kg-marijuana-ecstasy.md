Police in Uruguay carried out several raids in Montevideo, Canelones and Rivera
in an operation called Arquímedes aimed at an organization selling and
distributing drugs around the country. [@2] Four men and one woman were detained
and left at the disposal of the Second Turn drug prosecutor's office. [@3]

Officers seized more than 21 and a half kilograms of marijuana, part of a haul
that also included cocaine, ammunition and items used to sell the drugs. [@1]
Police also seized 2,000 ecstasy pills and 17,500 pesos. [@1]

The raids were led by the Montevideo departmental anti-drug brigade with support
from the General Directorate for Repression of Illicit Drug Trafficking, after
an investigation of several months. [@2] The Interior Ministry, whose statement
the outlets published, said the operation was meant to break up an organization
selling and distributing narcotics in different parts of the country. [@1]
