Michael Hoff appeared before Chief Justice Patrick Keane at the High Court in
Avarua on a charge of possession of cannabis, to which he had previously pleaded
guilty. [@1] Hoff was stopped for driving with excess breath alcohol on 23 July
2022 and taken to the cells. [@1] When he was body searched on being taken to
Aorangi Prison, police found a small plastic receptacle containing two tinfoils
of cannabis in his underwear. [@1]

Chief Justice Keane said Hoff told the court he had found the cannabis and $20
in cash on the side of the road, spent the cash on food and forgotten about the
drug. [@1] The judge noted it was Hoff's first drug-related offence and that his
two other convictions were unrelated and unaggravated. [@1] Chief Justice Keane
called it an exceptional offence and told Hoff he was a responsible husband and
father who should not be defined by it. [@1] Police supported a sentence of 12
months' supervision, and the Chief Justice agreed and sentenced Hoff to 12
months' supervision. [@1]
