The French surveillance frigate Ventôse intercepted a suspect fishing boat on
the high seas west of the Grenadines on 23 January 2023. [@2] The interception
occurred during a Caribbean maritime surveillance patrol when the frigate closed
on the suspicious vessel. [@2] French sailors recovered 12 bales holding a total
of 320 kg of cannabis herb that tested positive after recovery. [@1] The French
sailors took the fishing vessel's crew into custody. [@1]

The fishing boat had no identification marks and did not respond to radio
messages. [@1] As the navy surveillance frigate approached, the fishing vessel's
crew threw about twenty bales into the water, twelve of which tested positive
for cannabis after being recovered. [@1]

The seized cannabis was to be destroyed in the presence of French authorities
under international law. [@1] The vessel's identification documents established
its provenance and links to a cross-border trafficking network, but the ministry
did not publicly disclose that provenance. [@2]
