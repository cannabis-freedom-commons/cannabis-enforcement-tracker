Operatives of Nigeria's National Drug Law Enforcement Agency (NDLEA) stormed a
cluster of cannabis syndicates in the Opuje forests of Edo State and destroyed
warehouses and tents storing 317,417 kg (317.4 tonnes) of cannabis in a
two-day operation. [@1,2]

Reaching the forest on Wednesday 18 January took hundreds of operatives hours
of trekking, and armed youths ambushed them with bonfires as they left the
community the next day; the agency reported no casualties, only heavy losses
for the cartels. [@2,3]

## Four arrests

Four suspects were arrested in connection with the drugs: Omoruan Theophilus,
37, described as a police impersonator who posed as an inspector to ferry
drugs from the forest to the cities, alongside Aigberuan Jacob, 42, Ekeinde
Anthony Zaza, 53, and Naomi Patience Ohiewere, 42. [@1,2,3]

The agency describes the Opuje community in Owan West Local Government Area as
notorious for cannabis cultivation, where cartels cut down the forest
reserves' economic trees, cultivate cannabis on a large scale and guard the
warehouses with armed youths. [@1,2]
