The Metropolitan Police of Bucaramanga captured an 18-year-old man caught in the
act with hallucinogens in the municipality of Lebrija. [@1] The arrest was made
through the Hunter Plan in the fight against drug trafficking. [@1] Officers on
patrol with the National Community Policing Model by Quadrants stopped the man
along the national highway at the entrance to the El Pesebre neighborhood. [@1]

The man was traveling on a motorcycle and was found in possession of
approximately 1000 grams of marijuana. [@1] Q'hubo described the detainee as a
jíbaro caught while traveling on a motorcycle with a kilogram of marijuana in
Lebrija. [@2]

The captured man and the seized narcotics were placed at the disposal of the
Attorney General's Office. [@1] Prosecutors were to present him before the
competent authority or a judge to determine his legal situation. [@2] Police
said they would continue preventive plans and asked the community to report
crime to line 123. [@1]
