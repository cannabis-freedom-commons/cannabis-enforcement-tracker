Bosnian police arrested six people on 19 June 2023, the eighth action day of a
three-year operation against a large criminal network trafficking drugs and
weapons, laundering money and corrupting officials. [@1] The arrests brought to
38 the number of people detained over three years for links to the organised
crime group, Europol said. [@1]

Eight of the 38 held had positions in Bosnia and Herzegovina's national
administration, according to the operation's published results. [@1] One
high-value target arrested in Germany was extradited to Bosnia and Herzegovina.
[@1] Europol said the operation's seizures included more than 300 kg of
cannabis, alongside real estate and cash worth over 6 million euros in total.
[@1]

Bosnian outlet Avaz carried the same operation results the day they were
published, including the 38 arrests, the eight officials and the 300 kg of
cannabis. [@2]

The investigation began in 2021 and focused on a network producing and
trafficking drugs across the Western Balkans, the European Union and South
America. [@1] Europol said leads came from analysing suspects' messages on the
encrypted ANOM and SkyECC apps, the latter shut down in 2021. [@1] Bosnia's
Federal Ministry of Interior, the Republika Srpska interior ministry, the Border
Police and the State Prosecutor's Office took part, with Germany's Federal
Criminal Police Office and prosecutors. [@1]
