Acting on a tip-off, a Mahasamund police team intercepted a truck within the limits of Singhod police station. [@1] Mahasamund Superintendent of Police Dharmendra Singh said officers recovered 500 kg of cannabis from the truck. [@1] Police valued the seizure at about Rs 1.25 crore. [@1]

The accused were identified as Delhi residents Sudhir Kumar Yadav and Ramkumar Shahshankar, who were arrested under the NDPS Act for alleged cannabis peddling. [@1]
