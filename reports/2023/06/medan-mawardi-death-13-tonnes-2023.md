The Medan District Court in North Sumatra sentenced Mawardi, a 24-year-old man
from Gayo Lues, Aceh, to death on 6 June 2023 after finding him guilty of
carrying 1.3 tonnes of marijuana. [@1,2,3] The verdict met the prosecutors'
demand. [@1,2]

Presiding judge Yusafrihardi Girsang said in the online trial that Mawardi had
violated several articles of Law 35/2009 on narcotics, found no mitigating
factors, and held that the severity reflected the defendant's violation of the
government's anti-drug efforts. [@1,2] Mawardi, present by teleconference, said
he would appeal. [@1,2]

Mawardi had been arrested in Medan on 12 December 2022 carrying the 1.3 tonnes
from Gayo Lues to Medan. [@2] He said he took the job, which would have earned
him Rp 2 million, to pay for his mother's medical treatment. [@2,3]
