Two Nicaraguan men were detained on Sunday while carrying more than a tonne of
marijuana through waters off Sierpe de Osa. [@1] Both suspects held Costa Rican
refugee cards. [@1] Vice-minister Manuel Jiménez Steller said neither suspect,
surnamed Homfret Henry, 34, and Pineda Rocha, 42, had a criminal record in the
country. [@1] The same surnames and ages were given in a second account of the
interception. [@2]

At a press conference on Monday morning, the vice-minister confirmed a final
weighing of 1,196 kilograms of marijuana in 55 bundles. [@1] The interdiction
also confiscated the vessel, 15 empty fuel drums and two outboard motors. [@1]
The Public Security Ministry confirmed the 1,196-kilogram load and said it
apparently came from Colombia. [@2] The boat was stopped in a joint operation by
authorities from Colombia, Costa Rica and the United States. [@2]

The suspects tried to throw the cargo of 44 bundles into the sea but were
stopped by coast guard officers after a short chase. [@2] After their arrest the
two crew members were handed over with the seized load to the coast guard naval
base at Puerto Quepos. [@2] The two men were placed at the Public Ministry's
disposal, and the vice-minister said their situation was being verified. [@1]
Asked when the men had been granted refugee status, the vice-minister declined
to give that detail. [@1]
