Qiaokuer Border Police Station of the Ili Border Management Detachment reported
that officers had cleared more than 50,000 wild cannabis plants in the days
before 22 June 2023. [@1] The report describes a multi-day operation at the
third company of an unnamed regiment, where plants were growing beside several
unused courtyards. [@1]

On 18 June, officers on routine patrol found plants they suspected were
cannabis and identified them by comparing them at the scene. [@1] The station
organized officers to uproot and destroy the plants to prevent harm to people
working and living in the area. [@1]

Officers later took samples to nearby residents and explained the types and
harms of drugs and the laws against illegal planting. [@1] They urged residents
to report wild drug plants or suspected cultivation. [@1] The report does not
give the regiment's name, site area, plant weight or any arrest or charge.
