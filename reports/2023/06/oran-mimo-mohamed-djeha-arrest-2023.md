Mohamed Djeha, known as Mimo and described as one of France's biggest drug
traffickers, was arrested in Algeria after years on the run. [@1] Algerian
police made the arrest on Thursday in Oran in north-west Algeria. [@1] The
arrest took place on 15 June near Oran. [@3] Investigators called him the number
one for drug trafficking in Marseille who also organised imports of cannabis or
cocaine for other French regions and listed him as an OFAST priority target.
[@2]

Born on 21 September 1981 in Bejaia, the 41-year-old known as Mimo, Papipaolo or
Suarez had been wanted since 2019 after a ten-year prison sentence for
laundering drug-trafficking money in Marseille's La Castellane. [@1] In May that
year he was also sentenced in his absence to 30 years by the Aix-en-Provence
court for ordering a 2017 murder on the A55 motorway near Marseille. [@1] French
judicial authorities reported the facts to Algeria so he could be tried there,
ruling out extradition because he holds Franco-Algerian nationality and Algeria
does not extradite its nationals. [@1] The arrest followed an OFAST visit to
Algeria in May and a visit by the French police chief and central judicial
police director in June to discuss cooperation on his case. [@1]

Many neighbourhoods in France's second city are described as blighted by drug
trafficking, mostly cannabis, with increasingly deadly fights over selling
points. [@1] Since 2018 and the first trial, Mimo had no longer lived in France
and was known to travel between Dubai, Algeria and Morocco. [@1] Investigators
only recently became certain he had settled in Algeria and was avoiding Dubai
for fear of arrest and transfer to French justice. [@1]
