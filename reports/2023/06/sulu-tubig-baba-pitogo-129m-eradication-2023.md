Operating units of the Kalingalan Caluang police, PDEA-BARMM and Special Action Force
troops, backed by the police provincial office, intelligence units and the Armed Forces
of the Philippines, raided a clandestine marijuana farm in Sitio Tubig Baba, Barangay
Pitogo, on 6 June 2023. [@2] The plantation of about 15,000 square metres held fully
grown marijuana plants with an estimated value of PHP12,900,000.00, which the joint
force uprooted and destroyed on site. [@1,2]

The farm was allegedly cultivated by a certain Maddi Khan, alias Maddi, with his
cohorts. [@2] Three samples of the uprooted plants were turned over to the Sulu
Provincial Crime Laboratory for examination, and cases for violation of Section 16 of
Republic Act 9165 are being prepared against arrested suspects. [@2] PNP chief
Benjamin Acorda Jr. praised residents of Kalingalan Caluang for their support during the
operation. [@2]
