Prefectura Naval Argentina officers seized 263 kilos of marijuana in a procedure
in Puerto Rico, Misiones, as part of border-zone controls to prevent crime. [@1]
The operation took place during the early hours at kilometre 1742 of the Paraná
River, where a force patrol found the drug load hidden inside a boat. [@1]

The 263 kilos were divided into 14 sacks. [@1] Officers seized the marijuana on
the instructions of the Federal Prosecutor's Office in Oberá, led by Viviana
Vallejos. [@1] The Prefectura said the marijuana had a market value above 130
million pesos. [@1] The Misiones outlet reported that the operation took place
in the early hours of that Monday. [@2]
