A passenger arriving at Rarotonga International Airport was arrested on a
Saturday morning and later appeared in the Cook Islands Criminal Court charged
with possession of cannabis. [@1] He appeared before Justice of the Peace John
Whitta in the Criminal Court on Thursday. [@1] The arrest followed a baggage
check by Cook Islands Customs, and he had been remanded before being granted
bail to appear in court. [@1]

His lawyer, Michelle Tangimama, asked for him to be allowed to return to New
Zealand for the birth of his child on 3 July and for his children. [@1]
Tangimama said he had flown to Rarotonga for a family funeral and was arrested
at the airport for possession of cannabis. [@1] The lawyer said the case was to
be called before the Chief Justice or a judge but the defence had not yet
received disclosure. [@1] She argued her client should be allowed to travel back
to New Zealand and return in time for the next hearing. [@1]

Police prosecutor Senior Sergeant Fairoa Tararo did not oppose bail but asked
for a bond to be put in place. [@1] Justice of the Peace Whitta adjourned the
matter to 1 September and set a $500 surety before police would release the
defendant's passport. [@1]
