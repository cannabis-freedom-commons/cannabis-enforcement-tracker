Customs Anti-Narcotics Unit officers intercepted Surinamese national Virjanand
Narain during an operation at Eliza Mary, Corriverton, Berbice, on 3 June 2023.
[@2] Narain was arrested and escorted with the suspected narcotics to CANU
headquarters in Berbice, where they were weighed and tested. [@2]

Hash is a drug made by compressing and processing parts of the cannabis plant.
[@1] The seized drugs comprised 101.5 grammes of hashish alongside ecstasy, and
both substances tested positive. [@4] He answered a charge of possession of a
narcotic for the purpose of trafficking involving 101.5 grammes of hashish
described as cannabis resin. [@3]

Narain appeared before the New Amsterdam Magistrate's Court on 5 June 2023. [@5]
He pleaded guilty and was sentenced to three years' imprisonment with a fine of
GY $688,350. [@3]
