Customs Anti-Narcotic Unit officers searched Paul Lewis's home at No. 30
Village, West Coast Berbice, on Wednesday 7 June 2023 and found 22 parcels of
cannabis. [@2] Lewis, 44, was arrested and taken to CANU headquarters with the
parcels. [@2] The narcotics tested positive for cannabis and weighed about 41.79
kg (92.13 lb), which CANU valued at about $12.5 million. [@1]

On Thursday 29 February 2024 Magistrate Peter Hugh found Lewis guilty at the
Fort Wellington Magistrate's Court. [@3] The court jailed him for two years and
six months and fined him $3.5 million. [@3] Guyana Times reported that the
conviction concerned possession of 3.954 kilograms of ganja, far less than the
41.79 kg CANU announced, and did not explain the difference. [@3]
