Gendarmes of the Akam-Essatouk district brigade in Woleu department arrested
Franck Eneme Ndong. [@1] He had come from Equatorial Guinea holding 18 bundles
of cannabis he planned to sell in Oyem, called Israël, with two accomplices
still at large. [@1] An investigating magistrate placed him under a detention
order. [@2] He was sent to the Peloton for purchase and possession. [@2]

He had left Oyem with two friends, including one known as Chicago. [@1] The
three men went to Equatorial Guinea to stock up on Indian hemp. [@1] Their
supplier sold them 33 bundles at 10,000 CFA francs each. [@1] They invested
330,000 CFA francs in total and planned to resell at 20,000 CFA francs per
bundle. [@1] Fifteen bundles were said to be with the accomplice. [@2]

Around 10 pm they tried to reach the town on foot to avoid police patrols. [@1]
Residents of Adzap-B’Endeng village chased them after noticing their behaviour.
[@1] They fled in panic into the forest, but Franck Eneme Ndong was caught. [@1]
Gendarmes sent to the scene found 18 bundles of Indian hemp hidden in his
backpack. [@1]

Illegal drug import is punishable by up to 20 years in prison and a fine of five
times the seizure value. [@1]
