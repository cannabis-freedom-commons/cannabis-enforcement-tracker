The Penobscot County Sheriff's Office Special Response Team raided an illegal grow operation in
Carmel at about 8 a.m. on 29 June 2023, seizing more than 3,400 marijuana plants and 111 pounds
of processed marijuana. [@1] Deputies searched a residence on Route 2, and the sheriff's office
said the unlicensed operation's methods and process were dangerous to those involved and the
surrounding area. [@1]

The sheriff's office said the operation was organised by people who live in Maine while several
people from outside Maine were arrested, without immediately naming them. [@1] Later reports
named four men, residents of New York and Massachusetts, and quoted a sheriff's spokesperson
saying the operation was not licensed, permitted or following safety protocols. [@2] The
sheriff's office said the large unlicensed operation undermines licensed medical and
recreational growers in the state. [@1] The Town of Carmel, which has not opted in to allow
commercial cultivation, welcomed the enforcement of its local rules. [@2]
