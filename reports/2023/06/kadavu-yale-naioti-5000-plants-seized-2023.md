More than 5,000 plants believed to be marijuana were discovered on a farm in the
Naioti terrains in Yale, Kadavu. [@1] Assistant Commissioner of Operations Livai
Driu said the find was seized and that police were trying to identify the owner
of the farm. [@1] Driu said a special drug operations team was on the island
pursuing information about alleged cultivation of the drug, as police target
cultivators and suppliers. [@1]

He said arrests were being made in urban centres for users while operations
against cultivators and suppliers ran concurrently throughout the Divisions.
[@1] Driu said police were doing all they could to curb the illegal drug trade
but needed public support, pointing to increased admissions to St Giles Hospital
for drug and substance abuse. [@1] The Fiji Police Force said it hoped the
harmful effects of drugs would be discussed more in community social and
religious groups. [@1] Members of the public were urged to pass information on
alleged drug activity to Crimestoppers on 919 or through the Fiji Police's
social media pages. [@1]
