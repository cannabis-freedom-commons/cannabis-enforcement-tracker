In a Sunday statement on 18 June 2023, NDLEA spokesman Femi Babafemi said
operatives had destroyed 30.9 hectares of cannabis farms in Delta, Edo and
Ondo states over the previous week. [@1,2]

In Delta, 18 hectares of cannabis farms were destroyed on 12 June in Umuchime
village, Ogume, in Ndokwa West Local Government Area, by operatives supported
by soldiers. [@1,2] Four suspects arrested on the farms were named as Kingsley
Atuola, 40; Okikiolu Adekunle, 44; Osai Doris, 40; and Ublenu Joy, 28. [@1]

In Edo, operatives stormed Awakpa forest in Owan West Local Government Area
on 14 June and arrested five men found in a hut on a 2.5-hectare cannabis
farm: Malachy Amaechi, Nanmua Peter, Ojo Momoh, Afam Luke and John Danladi.
[@1,2] That farm was destroyed and 102 kilogrammes already harvested were
recovered; another farm of about 1.29 hectares was also destroyed, bringing
the Edo raid total to about 3.98 hectares. [@1]

In Ondo, 30-year-old John Emmanuel was arrested at Ofosu with 81 kilogrammes
of cannabis on 15 June, while Gideon Akoh was arrested at Ala forest the same
day, where nine hectares of farms were destroyed and 12 kilogrammes of
harvested cannabis recovered. [@1]
