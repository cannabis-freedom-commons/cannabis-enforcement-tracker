Bahamian law enforcement officials arrested two men in Grand Bahama after the
discovery of suspected marijuana. [@1] The two men, a 61-year-old Freeport
resident and a 59-year-old male resident of Andros, were in custody assisting
police in their investigations. [@1]

Shortly before 3pm on Thursday, the Drug Enforcement Unit, Bahamas Customs and
the K9 unit carried out a joint operation at Freeport Harbour. [@1] Acting on a
tip, officers went to the harbour, where the K-9 unit was deployed and alerted
officials to a box during searches of the premises. [@1] A man was seen
collecting the box and getting into a silver Nissan van, which officers
intercepted. [@1] The box contained 2.5 pounds of suspected marijuana, which
police estimated had a street value of $2,500. [@1]

Investigations into the matter were continuing. [@1]
