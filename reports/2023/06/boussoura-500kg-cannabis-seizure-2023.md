Officers seized the cannabis from three individuals, including one Sierra
Leonean. [@1] The three were immediately placed in custody. [@1] They remained
in special-services custody pending referral to the judicial services. [@2]

Guinea’s special services presented 500 kg of cannabis to the press on 21 June
2023. [@1] The seizure had been made at the Boussoura landing in Matam commune.
[@1] A second account confirms the special services presented press with
cannabis seized at Boussoura port in Matam commune. [@2] The seizure weighed
about 500 kg and took place during the night of 14-15 June. [@2]

Special-services chief Abdoul Malick Koné said his services would never relent
in their efforts. [@1] He described cannabis as the raw material used to make
the more toxic drug known as Kush. [@2]
