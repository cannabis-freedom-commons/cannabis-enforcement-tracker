Kazakhstan ran the nationwide Qarasora operation, the interior ministry's annual
anti-cannabis campaign, from 1 June 2023 against cultivation, harvesting and
transport of hemp out of the southern regions and against organised groups
distributing drugs. [@4] Special units of the interior ministry, the National
Guard and the Defence Ministry took part, and officers used drones and satellite
surveys to map where drug-bearing plants grow. [@1] In the first month the
interior ministry reported 221 drug offences, including 83 sales, 29 cases of
large-scale illegal possession, 82 cultivation offences and four drug
laboratories. [@1]

In the Zhambyl region a local resident was detained after preparing 232 kg of
marijuana, and a search of his barn found a sack with 24.5 kg of hashish and 12
kg of dried marijuana. [@1] In Rudny, Kostanay region, a resident was held with
2.5 kg of hashish, along with a service pistol, a sawn-off hunting rifle, more
than 200 cartridges and money in tenge and US dollars. [@1] Two residents of the
Shu district in Zhambyl region were detained in Almaty after bringing 90 kg of
marijuana and 2 kg of hashish there to sell. [@1] In Konaev, Almaty region, a
local resident was detained for growing more than a thousand cannabis bushes
weighing about 50 kg at his home. [@1] In Ekibastuz police took two grow boxes
and 11 pots with planted cannabis from a woman, seizing 110 drug-bearing plants
weighing 40 kg as evidence. [@1] In Turkestan region officers destroyed two
opium poppy plantations of 10,600 bushes and more than 840 cannabis bushes,
putting the combined weight of the drug-bearing plants at about 230 kg. [@1]

The interior ministry said officers took more than a tonne of drugs out of
circulation, including 614 kg of marijuana and 29 kg of hashish, a cannabis
total of 643 kg. [@3] That list also included 4 kg of synthetic drugs and about
1 kg of heroin, which are excluded from the cannabis figures here. [@2]
