In a joint operation, Spanish and Danish authorities arrested five individuals,
members and aspiring members of a criminal organisation linked to the Hells
Angels motorcycle gang for drug trafficking; two were apprehended in Malaga,
Spain, and three were arrested in Denmark. [@1] The accused had allegedly
smuggled over 3.5 tonnes of hashish into Denmark, hidden within shipments of
live animals transported by the criminal organisation, and the hashish was
subsequently distributed to small and medium-scale drug traffickers. [@1] During
the operation, Danish and Spanish authorities seized 59,000 euros in a house in
Spain and hashish in Denmark. [@1] In December 2022, as a result of an
investigation started years ago, law enforcement agents identified two
individuals in the southern Spanish city of Marbella, believed to be members of
the criminal organisation. [@1] One was a Danish former member of the Hells
Angels biker gang and the other was an aspiring member; their role was to
contact hashish suppliers in Morocco, arrange the drug's import into Spain, and
then ship it to Denmark. [@1] Both individuals were considered high value
targets for the Danish authorities and had previous records in Spain, including
a 2022 arrest linked to a dispute with a Polish criminal organisation and other
drug trafficking charges. [@1] On 31 May 2023, Europol specialists and
investigators from Denmark and Spain conducted a coordinated operation, during
which agents from both countries participated in multiple raids; the searches
resulted in the seizure of several computer and telephone devices used by the
criminal organisation to secure its communications. [@1]
