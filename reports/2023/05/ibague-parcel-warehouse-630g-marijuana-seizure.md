The Metropolitan Police of Ibagué, through its Carabineros and canine-guides
group, carried out an operation against crime and drug trafficking involving a
parcel warehouse used to store and distribute drugs allegedly arriving from
Antioquia department. [@1] The detector dog Perla, an antinarcotics specialist,
signalled on a box sent from Medellín to Ibagué. [@1]

The headline described the haul as 630 doses of marimba, a local term for
marijuana, hidden in a parcel shipment. [@1] Officers seized five packages
containing 630 grams of marijuana. [@1] They also seized 11 glass capsules
containing cannabis oil, whose status was still under investigation after the
find in the warehouse. [@1]

The seized substances were placed at the disposal of the Attorney General's
office. [@1] Authorities said such operations would be intensified in several
sectors of Ibagué, the capital of Tolima, as a preventive security measure. [@1]
Authorities asked residents to report drug trafficking on the 167 anti-drugs
line and other offences on line 123 or to the nearest police post. [@1]
