On 6 May 2023, the Gaya departmental branch of Niger's Central Office for the
Repression of Illicit Drug Trafficking (OCRTIS) broke up a drug-trafficking
network operating between Benin and Niger. [@2] Officers arrested three Nigerien
nationals in the operation. [@2] The cannabis was found in a house in the
Koira-Tegui district of Gaya that was lived in by one of the traffickers. [@2]
The suspects and the seized goods were presented to the press in the presence of
Prosecutor General Chaibou Moussa. [@2]

Officers seized 917 bricks of cannabis weighing 812.5 kg, about one tonne. [@1]
Police put the purchase value of the cannabis at 36,680,000 CFA francs. [@2]
Officers also seized two motorcycles and 435,000 CFA francs in cash. [@1]

Police said Gaya department had gradually become the epicentre of large-scale
drug trafficking brought into Niger by river using pirogues. [@1] Once brought
in across the River Niger, the drugs were collected and stored in riverside
villages before being carried by motorcyclists toward Nigeria or into Niger's
interior. [@1] Officers said most of the seized drugs were intended to supply
local demand. [@1]
