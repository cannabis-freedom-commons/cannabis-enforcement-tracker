The Pará Civil Police operation named Inimigo Oculto located and destroyed about
9,000 cannabis plants on Tuesday 30 May 2023. [@1] The plants were growing in an
area surrounded by dense forest and protected by traps in the Umirizal
community, in the rural zone of Terra Alta in north-eastern Pará. [@1]

At the site officers seized a homemade 28-gauge firearm and one round of
ammunition. [@1] By the early afternoon of Wednesday 31 May no one had been
arrested. [@1] A sample of the vegetation was also seized and sent for analysis
to the forensic science police headquarters in Belém. [@1]

Thiago Dias, director of the Guamá regional superintendency, said further steps
would be taken to identify the cultivators and everyone involved in the
production. [@1]
