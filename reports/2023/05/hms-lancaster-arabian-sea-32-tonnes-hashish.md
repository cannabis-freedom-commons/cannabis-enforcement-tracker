The Royal Navy frigate HMS Lancaster boarded a suspicious vessel in the Arabian
Sea at last light and seized 3.2 tonnes of hashish, which the navy calls
cannabis resin. [@1] The boarding took place at last light on Monday 8 May 2023,
days after the ship helped evacuate civilians through Port Sudan. [@2]

Led by the ship's Royal Marines detachment from 42 Commando, the boarding party
found packages on the deck and, with a team of sailors, recovered nearly 200
packages weighing around 11 kg each. [@2] The search found almost 200 packages,
each containing around 11 kg of cannabis resin, for a total of 3.22 tonnes. [@3]
The haul was transferred to the warship during the night and the drugs were
later destroyed. [@3]

The Royal Navy put the drugs' value at £2.5 million on the UK wholesale market.
[@1] The task force's own public statement recorded the seizure on 8 May, put
the weight at 3,000 kg and the value at US$6 million, and did not say whether
anyone on the vessel was detained. [@4]

The Lancaster was serving with Combined Task Force 150, a multinational naval
force based in Bahrain that the Royal Navy commanded from January to July 2023.
[@1] It was the ship's first drugs seizure since it arrived in the Middle East
the previous autumn to replace HMS Montrose. [@2]
