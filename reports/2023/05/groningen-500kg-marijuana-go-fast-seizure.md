The Dutch navy patrol ship Zr. Ms. Groningen intercepted a go-fast vessel in the
Caribbean during the night from Monday to Tuesday. [@1] The Groningen had been
on patrol for less than 24 hours when the Coast Guard alerted it to the
suspicious go-fast. [@1]

The Groningen launched its two fast boats to intercept the vessel. [@1] The four
smugglers aboard the go-fast surrendered immediately. [@1] About 500 kilos of
marijuana were confiscated in the operation. [@1]

The suspects and the seized drugs were handed over to the Coast Guard, which
already disposed of the narcotics. [@1] Since May the Groningen had been serving
as a station ship in the Caribbean on counter-drug operations, Coast Guard
support and humanitarian aid. [@1]
