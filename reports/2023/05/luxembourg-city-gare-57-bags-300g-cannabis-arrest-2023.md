A police patrol intercepted a man suspected of drug trafficking in the Gare
neighbourhood of Luxembourg City on the afternoon of Monday 15 May 2023. [@1]
The man spotted the officers and fled, and the patrol caught him after a short
chase. [@1]

At the police station officers seized 57 plastic bags holding a total of 300
grams of cannabis, five mobile phones and €707 in cash. [@1] During his flight
the man had thrown away 16 cocaine pellets and a further 22 grams of cannabis.
[@1]

The public prosecutor's office ordered that he be held in detention, and he was
arrested on its orders. [@2] He appeared before an examining magistrate on
Tuesday, and the magistrate issued an arrest warrant against him. [@2]
