Gendarmes of Escuadrón 57 “Santo Tomé” opened boxes that had been detected in
checks on Ruta Nacional N° 14, acting on the orders of the Federal Court of Paso
de los Libres and before witnesses. [@1] Inside the parcels the gendarmes found
nine packages holding a vegetable substance. [@1] They also noticed a strong
smell of marijuana coming from footwear, and found three more packages hidden
inside the soles. [@1] A Narcotest field test was positive for “cannabis
sativa”, with a total weight of 11 kilos 12 grams. [@1]

In Chaco, officers of the Sección Seguridad Vial “Lapachito”, part of Escuadrón
14 “Las Palmas”, stopped a remís, a hired car, on Ruta Nacional N° 11 at
kilometre 1,060 that had come from La Leonesa and was heading for Resistencia.
[@1] Searching the boot, the officers found a handbag belonging to the female
passenger, hidden among clothes, holding a package with 5 kilos 775 grams of
marijuana. [@1] The Juzgado Federal N° 1 of Resistencia ordered the drug seized
and the woman involved detained. [@1]

The two seizures together came to 16 kilos 787 grams of marijuana, matching the
release's headline figure of 16 kilos. [@1] Chaco Ahora republished the
Gendarmería account under the heading that Gendarmería seized drugs in Chaco and
Corrientes in the preceding hours. [@2] Chaco Ahora states that the drug in the
Chaco case was being brought by remís from La Leonesa to Resistencia. [@2]
