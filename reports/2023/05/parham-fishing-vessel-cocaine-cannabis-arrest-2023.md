A 59-year-old Golden Grove man and a 29-year-old man of Nut Grove were arrested
by the police after being found in possession of 10.9 pounds of cocaine and one
pound of cannabis. [@1] Around 1:00 am on Friday 26 May, the police intercepted
a fishing vessel occupied by both men at Parham wharf during a drug operation.
[@1] The vessel was searched and 10.9 pounds of cocaine were found aboard; the
vessel was seized and the men were taken into custody. [@1]

Search warrants were also executed on their properties, and one pound of
cannabis was found at the home of the Golden Grove man. [@1] The fishing vessel,
occupied by both men, was intercepted at Parham wharf during a drug operation,
and the vessel was seized. [@1]

In a later court account, both men on the boat, named Cleveland Telemaque and
Elni Barnarld, were taken to the police station and subsequently charged for the
drugs that weighed 10.9 pounds in total. [@2]
