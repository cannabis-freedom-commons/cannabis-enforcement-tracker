National Drug Law Enforcement Agency (NDLEA) officers acting on intelligence
laid an ambush along Eleko Beach Road in Lekki, Lagos, and flagged down two
long trucks at about 4.51am on Thursday 4 May 2023. [@1,2] The trucks,
escorted by armed men, sped off instead of stopping, and an exchange of
gunfire lasting about 30 minutes followed before the officers overpowered the
escorts. [@1,2]

The drivers and armed escorts fled into the bush, abandoning the trucks and
202 bags holding 8,852 kg of Canadian Loud, an imported cannabis strain: 149
jumbo bags weighing 6,548 kg in the red truck and 53 bags weighing 2,304 kg
in the blue one. [@1,2] NDLEA spokesman Femi Babafemi said officers were on
the trail of the importer who shipped the consignment into the country. [@1,2]

No arrests at the scene were reported. [@1,2]
