U.S. Customs and Border Protection officers in Philadelphia found seven
vacuum-sealed bags of marijuana concealed inside an air fryer in an outbound
international parcel on 2 May 2023. [@2] The package was addressed to Bermuda
and had been mailed from an address in Ocean County, New Jersey. [@4]

The marijuana weighed a combined 3.49 kilograms, or seven pounds and 11 ounces,
and CBP put its U.S. street value at about $17,000. [@2] CBP said the same load
would be worth about $150,000 in Bermuda, where it said high-potency U.S.
marijuana fetches prices many times higher than at home. [@2] CBP said no arrest
had been reported in the parcel case and that an investigation continued. [@1]
CBP released photographs of the seven vacuum-sealed bags of marijuana found in
the air fryer. [@3]

The consignment was seized a week after CBP officers in Philadelphia stopped a
traveller trying to take 72 pounds of marijuana in his checked luggage to
Dublin, Ireland, a different case. [@1] Rene Ortega, acting area port director
for CBP's Area Port of Philadelphia, said officers were seeing a stream of bulk
marijuana carried in baggage or shipped out of the country. [@2] Ortega said
officers would keep seizing marijuana they encounter and seek consequences for
couriers, because possession and distribution remain illegal under federal law.
[@2]
