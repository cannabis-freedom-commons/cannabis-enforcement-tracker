Cuban Border Guard Troops and the United States Coast Guard seized 17 containers
holding 340.7 kilograms of marijuana in waters off Banes, Holguín province. [@1]
The two people aboard the suspect speedboat were later detained by the United
States authorities, according to Cuba's Interior Ministry. [@1]

Cuba's Interior Ministry said the Miami Coast Guard's Seventh District
telephoned the Border Guard Troops at 11:07 that morning about a white boat with
two outboard motors and two crew seen five miles north of the province and
suspected of drug trafficking. [@2] Two Border Guard surface units found the
boat at 12:15, chased it, and saw bundles thrown from the launch into the sea
during the pursuit. [@2] Cuban guards followed the boat as far as 18.8 miles
north of Faro Lucrecia, where they saw a Miami Coast Guard helicopter overhead.
[@1] Cuba's Interior Ministry said the US Coast Guard cutter Dependable 626 was
in the area and that its crew intercepted the boat. [@2]

The marijuana thrown into the sea was recovered and laboratory tests confirmed
it was marijuana. [@2] The containers were taken to the Holguín naval squadron
and handed to the provincial operational guard, which is continuing
investigations to bring those involved to criminal responsibility. [@2] The
Interior Ministry framed the case as proof of cooperation with the United States
against drug trafficking and of Cuba's stated zero tolerance for the trade. [@2]
Cubanet reported that Cuban authorities had broken up a drug-trafficking group
in April 2023 and seized more than 300 kilograms of marijuana, cocaine and
amphetamines, detaining 26 people. [@1]
