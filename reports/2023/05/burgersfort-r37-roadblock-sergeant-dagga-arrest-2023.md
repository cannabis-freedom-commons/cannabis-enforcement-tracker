Police in Burgersfort arrested a 46-year-old police sergeant and two Eswatini
nationals for alleged possession and transportation of dagga on Thursday 18 May
2023 at about 11:00. [@2] Officers were running a stop-and-search operation at a
roadblock along the R37 next to L50 Farm when they stopped a white Volkswagen
Polo with Mpumalanga registration plates carrying three occupants. [@2] They
asked to search the car after smelling an unpleasant odour inside, and found
three big parcels wrapped in brown tape on the back seat. [@2] One of the
suspects confirmed the parcels held dagga, and police arrested the three on the
spot for possession and transportation of dagga. [@2]

The driver was a 46-year-old sergeant stationed at Barberton SAPS in Mpumalanga
province. [@2] His two accomplices, aged 30 and 35, were foreign nationals from
Eswatini holding valid passports. [@2] Police said they confiscated dagga with
an estimated street value of R62 000. [@1]

The three suspects face charges of possession and transportation of dagga and
were expected to appear before the Burgersfort Magistrates Court soon. [@3] The
Citizen reports the account through Limpopo SAPS spokesperson Colonel Malesela
Ledwaba; dagga is the South African term for cannabis. [@1]
