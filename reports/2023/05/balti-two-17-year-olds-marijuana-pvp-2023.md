Moldovan police and prosecutors detained two 17-year-old students from Fălești
on suspicion of drug trafficking after searches at flats they rented in Bălți.
[@1] The searches recovered about 0.5 kg of marijuana, drug-use equipment and
bags used for selling doses. [@3] Officers also seized about 1.5 kg of PVP,
known locally as "salt", a synthetic stimulant that is outside the cannabis
seizure figures here. [@1] TV8 reported that police put the combined value of
the seized drugs at 1.5 million lei. [@2]

Officers from the National Investigations Inspectorate's northern directorate
and the PCCOCS prosecutor's office took part, according to police. [@3] One
suspect was caught in the act with a spade and drugs he had just dug up from a
cache at a location given by other members of the group, to divide into smaller
amounts for clients. [@1] Police said he had already sold drugs to undercover
officers, evidence attached to the criminal case. [@3] The second suspect was
detained after the searches, and both were placed in pre-trial detention in
Bălți at the request of PCCOCS prosecutors. [@3]

Police said the pair were instructed by other members of an organised criminal
group to change flats every three or four days to hide the activity. [@3] They
arranged sales with clients through Telegram, police said. [@2] One suspect was
in the tenth grade and the other studied at a secondary vocational school,
according to police. [@1] Police said the investigation continued into other
members of the group, while the two suspects kept the presumption of innocence.
[@3]
