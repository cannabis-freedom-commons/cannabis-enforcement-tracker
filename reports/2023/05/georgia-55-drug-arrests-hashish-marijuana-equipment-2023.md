Georgia's Interior Ministry announced on 18 May 2023 that special operations
carried out at different times in Tbilisi and the country's regions had detained
55 people on drug charges, including 37 drug dealers. [@1] The English-language
account describes the same announcement as operational activities carried out
across the country resulting in 55 arrests including 37 drug dealers. [@2] The
investigation found that the detainees were placing drugs for sale at specific
locations through internet applications and social networks and then sending
buyers the names and coordinates. [@3]

The ministry said officers made several controlled purchases of drugs from those
detained and secretly audio- and video-recorded the transactions. [@2] Searches
of the detainees, their cars, homes and places they indicated recovered
especially large amounts of various drugs, including cocaine, heroin, Subutex,
hashish, MDMA and Alpha-PVP. [@1]

Police also seized small electric scales, equipment for growing marijuana,
sticky tape and money believed to be proceeds of drug sales. [@1] One of those
detained was also found with a firearm, 26 cartridges and two cartridge
magazines. [@1] Those detained face up to 20 years in prison or life
imprisonment if their guilt is proven. [@1]
