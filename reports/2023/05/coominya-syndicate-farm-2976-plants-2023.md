Detectives from Queensland's State Drug Squad searched a rural property at
Coominya, about 80 kilometres west of Brisbane, on 15 May 2023 and seized
cannabis with an estimated street value of $23.1 million. [@1,2] Inside 19
greenhouses, each 70 metres long, they found 2,976 cannabis plants, alongside
close to 1,600 seedlings in two hydroponic growth rooms and more than 32 kg
of dried cannabis in a house and a shipping container. [@1,2]

No one was on the property at the time of the raid, but on 18 May detectives
arrested a 26-year-old man at a Heathwood home. [@1,2] He was charged with
one count of producing a commercial quantity of cannabis and was due before
Richlands Magistrates Court on 6 June. [@1,2]

Police linked the farm to an alleged national syndicate buying up isolated
rural Queensland properties for large-scale commercial cannabis production
and distribution across Queensland, Victoria, New South Wales and the
Northern Territory. [@2] Detective Inspector Brad Phelps said twelve
similarly sized farms had been dismantled so far and police were working
with other jurisdictions to destroy the group. [@1,2]
