SAPS members from the Mahamba port of entry caught a 31-year-old suspect in the
act and arrested him at about 11.30am on Monday 15 May 2023. [@1] The arrested
man was a 31-year-old Eswatini national stopped at the Mahamba Border Post in
Shiselweni after dagga was found in a gas cylinder in his possession. [@2]
Police described him as an undocumented foreign national from the Kingdom of
Eswatini carrying a gas cylinder that was sealed but fully loaded with dagga.
[@1] He was arrested and charged with possession of dagga and with being in
South Africa without valid documentation. [@1] He was charged with possession of
dagga and an immigration-related charge. [@2] He was expected to appear in the
Piet Retief Magistrate’s Court. [@1]

Officers had spotted the suspect carrying the gas cylinder inside a plastic bag.
[@1] After checking the sealed cylinder, officers suspected it held something
other than gas and cut it open. [@1] Mpumalanga police put the street value of
the dagga at around R18 000. [@1] Police described the seized drug as dagga, the
local name for cannabis. [@1]

Mpumalanga police spokesperson Colonel Donald Mdhluli said police had
strengthened security measures against dagga trafficking from neighbouring
countries including the Kingdom of Eswatini. [@1] He said security had been
tightened on South Africa’s borderline where false vehicle compartments were
uncovered recently. [@1] He described the sealed gas cylinder as a new tactic
discovered by police officers. [@1]
