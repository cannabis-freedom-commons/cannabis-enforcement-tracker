At about 1pm on Monday 29 May 2023, Revenue Customs officers seized cannabis
worth an estimated €600,000 at Dublin Airport after finding it in passenger
luggage from an inbound flight; analysis of the drug was still pending. [@1]
The cannabis weighed 30 kg and was packed in two suitcases on a flight from
the United States. [@2]

Gardaí from the Ballymun Drugs Unit arrested a man in his early 40s at the
airport, and gardaí from the Coolock Drugs Unit arrested a woman in her late
30s in Dublin city centre that afternoon. [@1] The pair were named in court
as Phung Sien Tran (41), caught with the cannabis in his luggage and said to
be on his way to meet his co-accused at the Clayton Hotel in Dublin 4, and
Chavy Oeun (37), arrested at a south Dublin hotel. [@2]

Both were charged with unlawful importation and possession of cannabis and
having it for sale or supply under the Misuse of Drugs Act. [@1,2] They were
granted legal aid, remanded in custody pending directions from the Director
of Public Prosecutions, and were due back in court the following week; bail
was objected to over flight-risk concerns. [@2]
