Vincent Sims, 24, was caught when postal sorting office staff became suspicious
about his inquiries about a parcel on 21 April 2022. [@1] The package had been
sent by special delivery and was X-rayed by staff before being reported to the
police. [@1] Two vacuum-sealed packets of cannabis wrapped in brown paper and
polka-dot wrapping paper were hidden inside the taped-shut box of a board game.
[@1] Sims was arrested when he later attempted to collect the parcel from the
sorting office in Braddan. [@1]

He pleaded guilty to importing the cannabis and attempting to possess it with
intent to supply. [@1] Deemster Graeme Cook sentenced Sims to 12 months in jail,
suspended for 18 months, with a supervision order for the same period. [@1] The
cannabis had a street value of more than £2,200. [@1]
