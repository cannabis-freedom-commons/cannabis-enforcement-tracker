National Guard members on patrol near the municipality of Ixtlahuacán del Río,
Jalisco, found and destroyed two plots of plants with the characteristics of
marijuana. [@1]

The two plots together covered about 825 square metres, about 0.08 hectares.
[@1] The first plot measured about 600 square metres and held plants about 1.2
metres tall at an average density of four plants per square metre. [@1] The
second plot measured about 225 square metres, with four plants per square metre
and plants about one metre tall. [@1] Guards searched for those responsible for
the sowings without positive results and then destroyed the plants by manual
labour and incineration. [@1]

Samples of the possible cannabis plants were handed to the Federal Public
Ministry's agency in the state to continue the investigation. [@1] The National
Guard said the find came during inspection, surveillance and crime-prevention
patrols near the municipality, as part of the National Security Strategy. [@1]
Netnoticias reported the same two plots and 825 square metres on the outskirts
of Ixtlahuacán del Río, with its lead photo credited only as Cortesía. [@2]
