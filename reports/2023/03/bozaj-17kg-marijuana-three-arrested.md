Albanian police arrested three Albanian citizens near the Božaj border crossing
for allegedly trying to smuggle marijuana from Albania into Montenegro. [@1] The
three were identified only by initials and ages as KG, aged 20, JK, aged 19, and
EO, aged 22. [@1] KG was driving the vehicle in which the group was travelling,
while JK and EO were travelling as passengers. [@1]

During a search of the vehicle, officers found 16 packages and a plastic bag
inside a suitcase. [@1] Police said the contents were suspected to be cannabis,
with the packages and bag together totalling a suspected 17 kilograms. [@1] The
seized load in the vehicle therefore totalled a suspected 17 kilograms of
cannabis. [@1]

The stop took place near Božaj, the Albania-Montenegro border crossing named by
police as the interception point. [@1] The alleged direction of travel was from
Albania into Montenegro by vehicle. [@1] Officers linked all three occupants of
the vehicle to the alleged cross-border smuggling attempt. [@1]
