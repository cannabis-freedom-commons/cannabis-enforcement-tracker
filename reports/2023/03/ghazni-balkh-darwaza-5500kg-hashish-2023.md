Security forces in Ghazni province seized 5,500 kilograms of hashish and
arrested 10 people in connection with the seizure. [@1] The hashish and the
detainees were shown to journalists in the courtyard of the police headquarters
in Ghazni city on Wednesday, 15 March 2023. [@1] Ghazni police spokesman Abu
Khalid Sarhadi said the hashish was loaded in 10 pick-up and mazda vehicles,
which were intercepted at the Balkh Darwaza area of Ghazni city the previous
day. [@1] Sarhadi said the detainees told investigators they had brought the
hashish from districts of Ghazni and were taking it onward; the account stops at
that point. [@1]

The Taliban interior ministry said that over the past week anti-drug police in
Ghazni had detained 10 suspects and recovered 5,500 kg of hashish in various
parts of the province. [@2]
