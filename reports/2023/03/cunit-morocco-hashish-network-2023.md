Spain's Tax Agency and Catalonia's Mossos d'Esquadra have dismantled a hashish-trafficking
organisation that moved the drug by road from Morocco to France and central Europe from a
distribution centre in Cunit, Tarragona.[@1]

The investigation began after Deltebre local police tried to stop an SUV in Riumar on 20 January
2022; the driver fled, was caught nearby, and officers found 784 kilograms of hashish in 21 bales
inside, arresting the sole occupant.[@1] Later, on 29 January, officers found a lorry in Manresa
carrying 360 kilograms of hashish hidden in its floor and arrested its driver, who was remanded in
custody.[@1]

Two days later, eleven raids and searches across Cunit, Segur de Calafell, Amposta, Masdenverge and
Castelló led to nine arrests, adding to the two drivers already held; the group's two leaders were
among the nine.[@1] The raids turned up 152,000 euros in cash, 1.7 kilograms of hashish and six
vehicles, and investigators say the group laundered its proceeds through fake employment records
and property deals.[@1] The case went before Investigating Court No. 3 in Tortosa, which remanded
three detainees in custody and released the rest on bail while the investigation continues.[@1] In
total the investigation seized 1,146 kilograms of hashish.[@1]
