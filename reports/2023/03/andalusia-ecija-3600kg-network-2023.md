Spain's Customs Surveillance Service and the Civil Guard, working with
Europol, arrested 14 members of a hashish trafficking network that bought
drugs in Huelva, stored them in Málaga and distributed them from Seville to
several European countries. [@1] Twelve of the detainees were remanded in
custody for drug trafficking, membership of a criminal organisation and money
laundering. [@1]

The inquiry began in September 2021 around Écija, Seville, the network's
epicentre, with a second branch in Málaga province handling procurement in
Ayamonte, storage and transport to Écija. [@1] In February 2022 officers
stopped a France-bound lorry in Granada carrying 950 kg hidden in its legal
cargo, and that October seized 1,000 kg more from another lorry in La Carolina,
Jaén. [@1] In January 2023 they seized two vans, one with about 600 kg on the
A-92 near Puebla de Cazalla and one with just over 900 kg in Pizarra,
Málaga. [@1]

On 21 February, Écija court No. 2 authorised 16 house searches, netting more
than 1.5 million euros in cash, including 1,085,000 euros hidden in a
ringleader's rented vehicle in Écija and 136,000 euros in a false-bottomed car
stopped in Málaga that morning, plus three firearms, high-end vehicles and
cover documentation. [@1] In total the operation seized 3,600 kg of
hashish. [@1]
