Police and customs officers seized cannabis worth almost three-quarters of a
million dollars at V.C. Bird International Airport in three separate joint
narcotics operations on Wednesday 22 March 2023. [@1] In one shipment, officers
found 57 vacuum-sealed packages of cannabis inside a cardboard box at the
Customs Cargo Shed. [@1] That shipment weighed 57 pounds, about 25.9 kg, and was
estimated at $342,000. [@1] Two other shipments of 31 vacuum-sealed packages and
15 vacuum-sealed packages held 31 pounds and 15 pounds of cannabis respectively,
with a combined estimated value of $278,700. [@1] Officers also seized a portion
of cannabis-infused candies during the operations. [@1]

The cannabis was taken to the police station while police continued their
investigation, and no arrest was reported. [@1] The Observer published the same
account on the same day under its Breaking News and The Big Stories sections.
[@2] Antigua News Room published the same account and the same figures on the
same day. [@3]
