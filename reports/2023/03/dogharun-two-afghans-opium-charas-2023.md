Iran's border forces arrested two Afghans at the Dogharun customs post as they
entered the country and charged them with drug smuggling, the state IRNA news
agency reported. [@1] The load was 10 kilograms of opium and more than four
kilograms of marijuana, the IRNA account said. [@1] The director of Dogharun
customs, Sayed Hassan Rahimizadeh, named in Afghan reporting, said the men were
trying to bring 10 kilograms of opium and about four kilograms of charas, the
local word for hashish, into Iran. [@2]

Dogharun (also written Dogharon) is Iran's customs and border crossing opposite
Islam Qala in Herat province, Afghanistan. [@1] In Iran, people accused of drug
trafficking face severe punishment, and Afghan reporting said dozens of
traffickers had been executed in the previous few years. [@1] The two men's
names, the charges filed against them and any court outcome were not published.
[@2]
