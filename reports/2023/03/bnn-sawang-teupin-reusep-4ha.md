Officers of Indonesia's National Narcotics Agency (BNN) destroyed a four-hectare
cannabis field in Teupin Reuseup village, Sawang district, North Aceh, on
Wednesday 8 March 2023. [@1,2]

The field held more than 40,000 stems, about 20 tons of cannabis, which the
officers uprooted and burned on the spot. [@1,2] The operation brought together
140 combined personnel of the military, police and customs, and BNN's deputy for
eradication, Brigadier General Roy Hardi Siahaan, joined the destruction. [@1]
