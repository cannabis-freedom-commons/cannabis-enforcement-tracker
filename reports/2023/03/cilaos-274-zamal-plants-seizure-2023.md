Gendarmes of the Cilaos brigade seized 274 zamal plants, the Réunion name for
cannabis, at the home of a man already known to the courts. [@2] The operation
followed the exploitation of a piece of intelligence, the gendarmerie said in a
statement published on its social media accounts. [@3] The plants were growing
in the garden at the back of the house, close to the family swimming pool and
the children's trampoline. [@2]

The owner was placed in police custody after the seizure. [@3] On the decision
of the Saint-Pierre prosecutor's office he was released from custody with a
summons for a guilty-plea hearing, the French procedure known as comparution sur
reconnaissance préalable de culpabilité. [@1] He is due to be judged at the
Saint-Pierre judicial court on 15 September 2023. [@1]

The 274 seized plants are to be destroyed by the gendarmerie. [@1]
