Maldonado department police raided a property in the Punta Negra resort, in the
municipality of Piriápolis, where a report said marijuana was being grown
without authorisation. [@1] The search warrant was issued by the 4th-shift
prosecutor's office of Maldonado, and the brigade carried out the procedure on
27 March 2023 with inspectors of the IRCCA, Uruguay's cannabis regulation and
control institute. [@1] Police detained five people at the property: a
57-year-old woman and four men aged 66, 64, 31 and 21, identified only by their
initials. [@1] Officers seized 34 kilograms of marijuana buds, 65 marijuana
plants, several containers holding 600 grams of hashish, 33,130 Uruguayan pesos
and two precision scales. [@1]

Police also seized equipment used to process and maintain the crop, including a
marijuana dryer, four electrical panels, a water pump, twelve solar panels, four
light fixtures, eight batteries and three cookers. [@1] Firearms and ammunition
were also seized, along with a crossbow, an air pistol, a rifle, 181 cartridges,
a telescopic sight, binoculars, a laptop, mobile phones, seven exterior video
cameras and a pick-up truck. [@1] The prosecutor ordered the woman's release,
and the four men were taken to the criminal court. [@1]

At a hearing in the 11th-shift criminal court the investigation was formalised
against the four men for the alleged offence of drug trafficking, two of them as
perpetrators and two as co-perpetrators. [@1] The man aged 66 was also charged
with internal trafficking of firearms and ammunition in real reiteration with
the drug offence. [@1] The court ordered total house arrest for 60 days as a
precautionary measure for all four men, together with the retention of their
travel documents. [@2] Maldonado Noticias reported that none of the four men was
charged with planting, growing and harvesting under article 3 of law 14.294.
