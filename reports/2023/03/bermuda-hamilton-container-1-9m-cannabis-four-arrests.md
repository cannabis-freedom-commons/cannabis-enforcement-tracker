Bermuda police and HM Customs ran a joint operation on 15 March 2023. [@2] Four
people were arrested in the operation. [@2] Officers from the Bermuda Police
Service drugs unit, with the financial crime unit and the tactical division,
executed warrants at a business in the City of Hamilton and at homes in
Devonshire and Pembroke. [@1] A shipping container was seized during the
operation. [@2]

A search of the container found several packages of suspected cannabis, which
police valued at $1.9 million in Bermuda dollars. [@2] An Island Construction
truck was hired to take the container to a secure location for the search. [@1]
Police said nobody associated with Island Construction was arrested or
interviewed over the operation. [@1]

Detective Superintendent Sherwin Joseph, who leads the crime division, said the
operation had disrupted what he called a well-organised crime group in Bermuda
and dealt a severe blow to its established illegal importation pipeline. [@1]
Joseph thanked HM Customs for its assistance. [@1] He said the drugs unit would
continue an investigation at a critical stage and that no further information
would be given at the time. [@1]
