An Arab-Israeli photographer, Fidaa Kiwan, was released from the United Arab
Emirates and flew home to Israel on Sunday, 26 March 2023. [@1] Israel called
her pardon a "special gesture" to President Isaac Herzog, who had asked the UAE
to release her. [@1] She had been arrested in Dubai in April 2021 while visiting
the city for work. [@1]

According to prosecutors, she was found with 500 grams of cannabis and 50 grams
of cocaine. [@1] She said the cocaine was not hers. [@1] Her initial death
sentence was reduced to life in prison on appeal. [@1]

President Herzog urged his UAE counterpart, Sheikh Mohammed bin Zayed al-Nahyan,
to pardon her. [@1] After the release, Herzog thanked the UAE president and
wished him "a month of kindness and peace" for Ramadan. [@1]

Israel and the UAE normalised relations under a US-brokered deal in 2020, the
Abraham Accords. [@1]
