As part of an intelligence-led operation targeting organised crime in the
Limerick area, officers from the Garda National Drugs and Organised Crime
Bureau, the Limerick Divisional Drugs Unit and the Revenue Customs Service
carried out a joint operation on Wednesday 1 March 2023. [@1]

During the operation Revenue officers seized approximately 10 kilograms of
suspected cannabis herb, which Gardaí valued at an estimated €200,000. [@1]
Gardaí arrested a man aged in his 20s following the seizure, and he was
detained under Section 2 of the Criminal Justice (Drug Trafficking) Act 1996
at Henry Street Garda station. [@1] Gardaí said investigations were ongoing. [@1]
