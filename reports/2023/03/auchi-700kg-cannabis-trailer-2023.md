NDLEA officers intercepted a DAF trailer marked ZUR 378 XJ (Kebbi) in Auchi,
Etsako West local government area, on Friday 24 March 2023. [@1,2] Hidden
under bags of fertiliser in the trailer were 69 bags of cannabis sativa
weighing 700 kg. [@1,2]

The bags of fertiliser were to be delivered in Funtua, Katsina State, while
the cannabis consignment was to be dropped in Kaduna. [@1,2] Both the driver
of the truck, Babangida Mande, and his assistant, Mandir Abdullahi, were taken
into custody. [@1,2] The interception was announced by NDLEA spokesperson Femi
Babafemi in a statement issued on Sunday 26 March 2023 as part of a wider
roundup of the agency's interceptions that week. [@1,2]
