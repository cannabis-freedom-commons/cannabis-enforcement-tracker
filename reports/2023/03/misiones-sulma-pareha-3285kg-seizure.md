On the morning of 6 March 2023, officers of Squadron 49 San Vicente ran a
road-prevention operation on Provincial Route 212 about six kilometres from
National Route 14 at Paraje Sulma. [@1] Officers saw three pickups, a Toyota
Hilux and two Ford Rangers, driving with their lights off, whose occupants tried
to flee when they noticed the checkpoint. [@2] Two of the vehicles collided with
each other during the evasive manoeuvres and were left by the roadside, while
the third pickup was found abandoned about 700 metres away. [@1] Officers
secured the area and found 161 rectangular bundles distributed inside the cabins
and cargo beds of the three vehicles. [@1] In the presence of witnesses,
officers confirmed the load was cannabis sativa weighing 3,094 kilos 890 grams
in 4,397 packages. [@1] The Oberá federal court ordered the detention of two men
and the seizure of the drug and the vehicles. [@1]

That afternoon, members of the Núcleo and Esperanza sections of Squadron 10
Eldorado patrolled the mountainous Paraje Pareha area of Colonia Victoria. [@1]
During the patrol, officers noticed vehicle tracks and people's footprints on a
path suggesting possible illicit manoeuvres. [@1] After combing the area, they
found 13 bundles of different sizes hidden in the vegetation, similar to those
used to transport drugs. [@1] In the presence of witnesses and using narcotest
field tests, officers counted 252 bricks holding a total of 191 kilos 20 grams
of marijuana. [@1] The Eldorado federal court, with the federal prosecutor's
knowledge, ordered the seizure of the substance and items of interest to the
case. [@1]

The Gendarmerie presented the two finds as a single Misiones result of more than
three tonnes of marijuana. [@1]
