The Anti-Narcotics Cell of Pune city police arrested a suspected drug peddler
and seized 2 kg of charas from him. [@1] The suspect was identified as Pappu
Ansari, also known as Ranga Pardeshi and Aurangzeb. [@1] Officers acted on a
tip-off that a peddler would arrive in the Uruli Kanchan area with a large
quantity of charas and intercepted him on the route to Pandharstal village. [@1]
Charas is the cannabis resin often called hash, made by processing material from
the cannabis plant. [@1]

Police valued the seized charas at more than Rs 24 lakh in the illegal market.
[@1] An officer from the cell said the investigation showed the suspect had
obtained the charas from Nepal and was about to sell it to peddlers in Pune.
[@1] The same officer said police had started an inquiry into the suspect's
supply and distribution network. [@1]

Officials said the suspect was already wanted in a narcotics smuggling case
registered at Yerawada police station in February of the previous year. [@1]
