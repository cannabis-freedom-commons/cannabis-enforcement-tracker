Macau's Judiciary Police arrested a local man for smuggling cannabis oil from
Zhuhai to Macau hidden in a seasoning packet inside a cup of instant noodles.
[@1] The suspect was 31 years old, surnamed Cheong, and told police he was
jobless. [@1] Police said that on Sunday afternoon they learned a suspected drug
package had arrived at a logistics company in Gongbei and had been picked up by
Cheong. [@1] After Cheong returned to Macau, officers intercepted him in a car
park near the checkpoint. [@1] The Judiciary Police date the interception to 26
March 2023, after the man took several packaged items from the Gongbei parcel
onward to Macau via Gongbei Port. [@2]

Officers found cannabis oil hidden in a seasoning packet in a cup of instant
noodles amongst other snacks in the package. [@1] The oil disguised as
cup-noodle seasoning weighed around 5.61 grams. [@2] The Judiciary Police said
officers later seized an e-cigarette and two suspected drug-related e-cigarette
cartridges from the man's motorcycle, valuing all the drugs together at
approximately MOP8,000. [@2] The Macau Post Daily instead said the e-cigarette
and two cartridges containing cannabis oil were seized from Cheong's flat and
vehicle. [@1] Cheong admitted buying 5 ml of marijuana oil for his own
consumption through a social networking platform for MOP2,000. [@2]

The parcel had been delivered from Thailand to Zhuhai and was suspected of
containing controlled substances for a Macao recipient. [@2] The Judiciary
Police then ran a joint investigation with Zhuhai police units, the
Anti-Smuggling Bureau of Gongbei Customs and the Macao Customs Service. [@2]
Under questioning, Cheong admitted that he had smoked cannabis habitually while
living outside Macau in the past. [@1] He was transferred to the Public
Prosecutions Office for illegal trafficking, illegal consumption and improper
possession of utensils, while police kept tracing the origin and others
involved. [@2] Va Kio Daily described the seizure from the noodle seasoning
packet as five millilitres of cannabis oil. [@3]
