Officers from Dumfries CID, acting under warrant, searched a property on Annan
Road in Dumfries on Tuesday 28 March 2023 and found a cannabis cultivation
there. [@1] Police put the estimated street value of the cultivation at
£21,000. [@1]

A 38-year-old man was arrested and charged with drugs offences. [@1] He was
released on an undertaking and was to appear in court at a later date. [@1]
Detective Inspector Graeme Robertson of Dumfries CID said the operation showed
the force's commitment to removing harmful substances from local communities. [@1]
