Venezuelan authorities announced on 3 March the seizure of 457 kg of marijuana
packed in 838 panels on the coast of Falcon state in the Caribbean Sea. [@1] The
armed forces said the 838 marijuana panels were found abandoned during a coastal
patrol in Falcon state and weighed 456.725 kg. [@2]

Seizures of marijuana shipments on Venezuela's coast and along its border zone
position Venezuela as an important corridor for a particular type of Colombian
cannabis flooding regional markets. [@1] Regional demand for Colombian-produced
marijuana, especially creepy marijuana with high THC levels, is driving these
large-scale seizures in Venezuela. [@1] Once the marijuana enters Venezuelan
territory, carriers move it to the states of Falcon, Sucre, or Nueva Esparta to
be loaded onto boats bound for Caribbean markets. [@1]

Venezuela is not a large-scale cannabis cultivating country, so much of the drug
moving through and seized in the country is produced and dispatched from
Colombia, specifically from the mountains of northern Cauca department. [@1] To
cross into Venezuela, creepy marijuana grown in the mountains of northern Cauca
uses cities such as Bogota or Medellin as storage points before distribution
toward border departments, above all Norte de Santander, La Guajira and Vichada.
[@1]
