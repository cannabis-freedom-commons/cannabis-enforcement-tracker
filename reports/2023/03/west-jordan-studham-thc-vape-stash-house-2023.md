West Jordan police arrested 22-year-old Anthony Studham after a search of what
they called a stash house, accusing him of holding thousands of drugs ready for
distribution. [@1] Police said the arrest followed a months-long surveillance
operation on the stash house. [@1] Studham's arrest came after police said they
discovered he was selling drugs on social media. [@1]

Inside the house officers found 10,000 THC vape cartridges, several pounds of
marijuana, thousands of edibles, several thousand magic mushrooms, 15 guns and
$70,000 in cash. [@1] He was arrested on five felonies, including possession
with intent to distribute, money laundering and being a restricted person who
has a weapon. [@1]

West Jordan police officer Ramon Ramirez said the arrest prevented further crime
by getting a man described as a main distributor off the streets. [@1] Chelsea
Ivie, a woman in recovery who works with the treatment provider Odyssey House,
said arrests like Studham's mean one less person who holds lives in their hands.
