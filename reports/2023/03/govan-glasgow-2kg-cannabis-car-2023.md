Police stopped a car in the Govan area of Glasgow on Sunday 19 March 2023 and
found over 2 kg of herbal cannabis inside it. [@1] Police put the estimated
street value of the cannabis at £7,600. [@1]

The 27-year-old man who was driving the car was arrested in connection with
the find. [@1] A Police Scotland spokesperson said he was arrested for drug
offences and released on report. [@1]
