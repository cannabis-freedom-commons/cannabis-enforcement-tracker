Brazil's Federal Police and Paraguay's National Anti-Drug Secretariat (Senad)
began the 36th phase of Operation Nueva Alianza (Nova Aliança), a joint
eradication drive against cannabis cultivation in the border region, on Monday
20 March 2023. [@2] By 22 March, their second day of work, the teams had
eradicated more than 159 tonnes of cannabis plants, the Federal Police said,
standing on some 53 hectares of plantations. [@2] The Federal Police put the
area destroyed in the opening days at 53 hectares, and G1 reported that more
than 50 hectares of cannabis plantations had been destroyed. [@1] Six camps that
traffickers used as bases were destroyed in the same days. [@2] Officers also
burned 660 kilograms of cannabis ready for distribution and 15 kilograms of
cannabis seeds. [@2]

The Federal Police said the cannabis was destroyed in Paraguayan territory, and
that its role was to pay the whole cost of the operation, fly teams by aircraft
to hard-to-reach cultivation sites, and send Brazilian observers to watch the
work. [@3] The operation was run with Paraguay's Public Ministry and the Joint
Task Force (FTC), a Paraguayan body, at its side. [@2] G1 reported an estimate
that the losses to criminals already exceeded USD 5 million, based on the price
the cannabis would fetch in Brazil. [@1] According to the authorities of both
countries, 80 percent of the cannabis grown in Paraguay is sold to criminal
factions operating in Brazil. [@1]

No arrests were reported during these first days of the phase. [@2]
