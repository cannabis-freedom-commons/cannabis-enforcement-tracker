Police stopped a fish-transport truck in San Mateo, Esmeraldas, on the morning
of 26 March 2023 and detained its driver. [@2] A judge in Esmeraldas ordered
pretrial detention for the driver, identified as Clever Riol A. V., over alleged
large-scale trafficking of controlled substances. [@2] The judge opened a 30-day
preliminary investigation after the flagrancy hearing. [@2] The court ordered
the seizure, retention and prohibition on sale of the truck used in the
incident. [@2]

Officers found the drugs in a hidden compartment discovered during a detailed
search of the truck. [@2] Inside were 524 rectangular packages wrapped in brown
packing tape and black plastic with green plant material, plus three similar
packages with white powder. [@2] Weighing and preliminary field tests identified
511,680 grams of marijuana and 2,730 grams of cocaine. [@2] Police initially
described the load as 514 kilos of drugs concealed in the rear of the truck.
[@1] El Comercio reported the packages included both marijuana and cocaine and
that the driver was detained. [@1]

Prosecutors are pursuing the case as large-scale trafficking under article 220
of the COIP, which provides for ten to thirteen years of imprisonment. [@2]
