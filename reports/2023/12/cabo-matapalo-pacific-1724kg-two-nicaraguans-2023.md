Costa Rica's coast guard captured a drug boat 24 kilometres off Cabo Matapalo in
the southern Pacific in the early hours of Friday. [@1] The two men aboard, both
Nicaraguan nationals, were detained. [@1] The Tico Times identified them as
44-year-old captain Wilson and 27-year-old sailor Julias. [@2] Both were placed
at the disposal of the prosecution service. [@1]

Officers found about 1,724 kilos of marijuana and 36 kilos of cocaine on the
vessel. [@1] The boat, named Lexus with foreign registration CP-01-3333, was 38
feet long and fitted with two 250Hp outboard engines. [@2]

A United States maritime patrol aircraft had spotted the craft on Thursday
afternoon and alerted the Costa Rican coast guard, which pursued it. [@1] The
chase lasted about 12 hours before the capture. [@2] The boat also carried 17
tins holding about 500 litres of fuel. [@2]
