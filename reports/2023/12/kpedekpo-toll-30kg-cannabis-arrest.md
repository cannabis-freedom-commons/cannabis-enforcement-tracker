Police arrested a motorcyclist at the toll and weighing station in Kpédékpo, on
the Kpédékpo–Zagnanado road, on Sunday 3 December 2023. [@3] Kpédékpo is in
Zagnanado commune, in Zou department. [@1] The motorcyclist was carrying a
passenger and a suspicious parcel, according to the police account. [@1]
Officers stopped the motorcycle and searched the parcel after it aroused their
suspicions. [@1] The passenger fled during the search, leaving the motorcyclist
behind at the scene. [@3]

Officers found 30 kilograms of carefully wrapped cannabis, described in French
as chanvre indien, inside the parcel. [@1] The Kpédékpo arrondissement police
station had launched daily security searches across its jurisdiction. [@3]
Police described the security operation as an effort to protect people and
property at the end of the year. [@2]

The motorcyclist was handed to OCERTID, the Central Office for the Repression of
Illicit Drug and Precursor Trafficking, for further judicial proceedings. [@4]
