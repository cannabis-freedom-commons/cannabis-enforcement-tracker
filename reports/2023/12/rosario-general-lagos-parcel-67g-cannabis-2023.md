Gendarmes of the Rapid Deployment Special Group "Grl D Martín Miguel de Güemes"
opened, with Rosario customs staff, a parcel impounded earlier at the roadside
control of the General Lagos toll booth, and found two wrappers holding 67 grams
of Cannabis Sativa buds hidden inside clothing and a computer tower. [@1]

The gendarmes then opened other parcels seized in earlier operations and found
299 stainless-steel thermoses of 1,000 ml, made abroad and without documents
proving their legal origin. [@1] They also handed 385 tyres of different makes,
made abroad, to customs staff; the tyres had been seized for breaking the
Customs Code. [@1]

The federal courts Nos. 3 and 4 of Rosario handled the cases and ordered the
drug confiscated and the thermoses and tyres handed to AFIP-DGA, Argentina's tax
and customs agency, under law 22.415, the customs code. [@1]

RedBoing, a Rosario news site, carried the release the same day under a headline
naming the General Lagos toll booth and giving the same 67 grams of buds. [@2]
03442 Noticias, an Entre Ríos outlet, republished the force's release on 10
December 2023 with the same figures and the same courts. [@3] Ceibal Jujuy's
version carries the same three paragraphs, from the 67 grams to the order of the
federal courts, without adding any figure. [@4]
