Chitwan district police arrested two Indian nationals at Aanptari in Bharatpur
Metropolitan City-1 on Tuesday morning, 19 December 2023, during a routine check
of trucks. [@2] The two were identified as Anil Giri, 45, and Rajpal, 30, both
from Sambhal in Uttar Pradesh. [@1] Anil was the truck's driver and Rajpal his
helper. [@1]

Police recovered about 380 kg of cannabis, which the official Nepal Police
statement calls ganja and other outlets call marijuana. [@4] The NDTV wire
account, carried by India's Press Trust of India, described the same 380 kg as
hashish. [@1] The Himalayan Times put the haul at 396 kg of marijuana. [@3]

The load was packed in 28 packets and hidden under a false bottom built into the
front side of the truck. [@1] Police stopped the Indian truck, registration JH
02 W 9631, while it was carrying the drug from Muglin toward Narayangadh. [@3]
The Himalayan Times said a police team headed by SP Rameshwor Karki made the
arrests, and that DSP Bijaya Raj Pandit, spokesperson for the District Police
Office Chitwan, briefed reporters. [@3]

Nepal Police said it was still carrying out the necessary investigation. [@4]
Chitwan police said further investigation into the matter was under way. [@2]
