Officers and patrol sergeants of the Salt Lake City Police Department’s Pioneer
Patrol Division arrested 14 people in two operations to reduce criminal activity
along the Jordan River Trail. [@2] The first operation took place on Monday, 18
December 2023, near 1200 West North Temple, where four people were arrested for
possession or use of a controlled substance. [@2] The second operation was held
on Thursday, 21 December 2023, led by the division’s graveyard shift with a K-9
unit and a bike squad, and ended with nine people arrested and one released on a
criminal citation. [@2] Officers said the intent of both operations was to
identify drug dealers along the trail. [@2] On the Thursday operation officers
contacted 35 people along the trail. [@2]

During the Thursday operation police recovered 70 blue fentanyl pills, about
$400, a knife and other drug paraphernalia. [@2] Eight people were booked into
the Salt Lake County Metro Jail. [@2] Among those booked was Carlos
Ferrara-Lopez, 33, held on a charge of possession of a controlled substance
(marijuana or spice). [@2] The other charges in the police list were for
controlled substances, drug paraphernalia, failing to stop for officers,
outstanding warrants and providing false information, with no further cannabis
count. [@2] Officers cited and released a 27-year-old man for criminal
trespassing and violating city park curfew. [@1]

KUTV’s account of the same police statement lists the same bookings and the same
seizure of pills, cash and paraphernalia. [@1] Chief Mike Brown said the
department would not allow people to jeopardize community safety by letting
drugs be distributed. [@2] Police said the charges are only allegations and
every arrested person is presumed innocent. [@2]
