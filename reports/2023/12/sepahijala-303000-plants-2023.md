Sepahijala District Police, together with Tripura State Rifles (TSR) contingents, the Forest Department and the CRPF's 140th Battalion, destroyed about 303,000 immature ganja plants in a joint operation on 23 December 2023. [@1] The plants covered multiple locations spanning 151.5 acres (about 61.3 hectares) of forest land. [@1]

The coordinated action brought together district police, TSR personnel from the 7th, 9th, 11th and 15th battalions, both men and women, alongside forest officials and the CRPF. [@1] It aimed to curb illegal cultivation and trade in the region, and officials said such joint drives also protect the ecological balance of the forest lands. [@1]
