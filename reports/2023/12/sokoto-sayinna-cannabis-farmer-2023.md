The NDLEA Sokoto Command arrested suspected cannabis farmer Anas Sani in
Sayinna village, Tambuwal Local Government Area, commandant Adamu Iro told
journalists in Sokoto on Saturday, 2 December 2023. [@1,2] Iro said it was
the first such case in the history of the state. [@1,2]

Sani was arrested following a tip-off after planting a foreign variety of
cannabis sativa inside his maize farm, which NDLEA operatives stormed and
uprooted. [@1,2] Media Talk Africa described the arrest as groundbreaking for
Sokoto State. [@1]

Iro said the suspect would face prosecution after a laboratory analysis of the
uprooted leaves. [@1,2] He thanked residents for their support, urged them to
remain vigilant, and warned that cultivating Indian hemp and dealing in
illicit drugs attract heavy punishment. [@1,2]
