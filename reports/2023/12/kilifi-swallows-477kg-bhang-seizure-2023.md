Directorate of Criminal Investigations officers found George Nitti, 37, and Raphael Mbithi, 37, with 477 kilograms of marijuana in the Swallows area of Kilifi South on 6 December 2023. [@1,2] Police put the street value of the bhang at about Sh14 million. [@1,2]

The driver ignored repeated orders to stop, so officers fired shots that deflated the car's rear right tyre before arresting the two men. [@1] The National Police Service said a Toyota Harrier with registration KDK020B used to carry the drug was impounded. [@2]

The suspects were held in custody awaiting arraignment while the DCI investigated. [@1,2]
