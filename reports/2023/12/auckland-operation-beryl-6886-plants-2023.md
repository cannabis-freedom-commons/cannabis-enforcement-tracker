Ten people were arrested and 6,886 cannabis plants seized as New Zealand
police, business ministry and immigration officers dismantled an Auckland drug
cultivation and money laundering syndicate run by Vietnamese nationals.
[@1,2] As part of Operation Beryl, officers executed 53 search warrants across
the Waitematā and Counties Manukau districts, finding 42 significant cannabis
grows, mostly sophisticated indoor setups in residential properties, plus
about 10 kg of packaged cannabis. [@1,2] Police put the street value at
NZ$16–25 million and said up to NZ$100,000 in cash was also seized. [@1,2]

Twelve people face deportation: nine migrants unlawfully in New Zealand and
three more to be deported that weekend, with further deportations under
investigation. [@2] Officers said some syndicate members had come to New
Zealand specifically to join organised crime groups, and the inquiry also led
to investigations of accredited employers over migrant exploitation. [@2]
Those arrested were due before the Waitākere, North Shore and Counties
Manukau courts in the coming days. [@1]
