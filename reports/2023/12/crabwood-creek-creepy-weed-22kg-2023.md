The Customs Anti-Narcotics Unit (CANU) and the Guyana Revenue Authority (GRA)
searched a location in the Crabwood Creek area, Corentyne, Berbice, on Friday 29
December 2023. [@1] During the joint search officers found several parcels of
suspected cannabis and several cases of uncustomed alcohol. [@1] CANU said the
narcotic tested positive for imported cannabis, which it calls “creepy weed”.
[@1]

CANU said the shipment weighed 48.5 pounds, or 22 kilogrammes. [@1] CANU
estimated the street value of the marijuana at GY$12.5 million, which it
attributed to the higher THC content. [@1] CANU head James Singh said the
marijuana most likely originated in Colombia. [@1] CANU said the cannabis was
intercepted before it could be further smuggled to neighbouring Suriname. [@1]

The operation also took cases of Corona and Heineken beer that had been smuggled
from Suriname. [@1] CANU said joint operations with the GRA were being carried
out across the country to prevent narcotics trafficking and contraband
smuggling. [@1]
