Officers of the Zheleznogorsk inter-district interior ministry division and the
Kursk Oblast branch of the FSB broke up a cannabis-growing operation run by a
29-year-old man from Perm Krai. [@1] More than a year earlier he had bought a
disused building in Zheleznogorsk district to grow farm produce for sale to
supermarket chains, fitting it with air conditioning, water supply and lighting.
[@1] When that business did not bring the expected profit, he began growing
drug-containing plants in the same building to make marijuana for sale, and
brought in an acquaintance from his home region. [@1]

When detectives moved in on the grow house, which was watched by perimeter
cameras, the organiser locked the entrance doors, set the building on fire and
tried to escape through a back exit with his partner. [@1] During the searches
officers seized 157 cannabis plants and about 5 kg of marijuana prepared for
sale. [@1]

Investigators opened criminal cases over attempted sale of drugs in especially
large amounts and over illegal cultivation of drug-containing plants, and both
suspects were remanded in custody during the investigation. [@1]
