Sri Lankan police said they arrested 2,121 suspects in an island-wide special
operation in the 24 hours ending at 6 a.m. on 18 December 2023, the first full
day of Operation Yukthiya. [@2] The 2,121 suspects were 2,020 men and 101 women.
[@2] Police obtained detention orders against 12 suspects and sent 133 people to
rehabilitation. [@2]

Officers recovered 178 kilograms of cannabis, which News First described as the
dominant drug seized in the sweep. [@2] Police also reported recovering 30,550
cannabis plants during the same 24 hours. [@2]

The Police Special Task Force and the Air Force ran the operation on the orders
of Acting Inspector General of Police Deshabandu Tennakoon. [@2]

Most of those arrested came from the Western Province. [@1] Police said 116 of
the 3,340 people listed in their Special Branch and Narcotics Bureau databases
for the operation had been arrested. [@1]

The sweep combined the police, including its Narcotics and Special Bureau, with
the armed forces and the Police Special Task Force. [@1]
