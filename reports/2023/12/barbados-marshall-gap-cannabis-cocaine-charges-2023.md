A man and a woman from 3rd Avenue, Marshall Gap, New Orleans, in the parish of
St Michael, appeared before Magistrate Alison Burke in Barbados' District 'A'
Traffic Court on cannabis and cocaine charges. [@1] Rohan Hewitt, 41, and
Shekinah Shiaeka Cornibert, 37, denied possession, intent to supply and
trafficking of 194 grammes of cannabis valued at $970. [@1] They also denied
possession of 233 grammes of cocaine valued at $11 650, on 12 December 2023.
[@1]

Cornibert faces a separate set of charges of possession, intent to supply and
trafficking of 39.15 kilogrammes of cannabis valued at $313 200, on the same
date. [@1] She was not required to plead to that indictable offence. [@1]

Hewitt was granted $8 000 bail, while a bail amount of $100 000 was imposed on
Cornibert. [@1] The two were to return to court on 28 June 2024. [@1]
