Keon Hewlett, a resident of College Housing in Basseterre, St Kitts, was
arrested and charged with cannabis importation and possession of cannabis with
intent to supply on two warrants. [@2] The offences were committed at the Robert
L. Bradshaw International Airport on 2 December 2023, and the charges were laid
at the Basseterre Police Station on 4 December 2023. [@2] Police said Hewlett
was caught trying to evade officers while importing illicit substances on
Saturday 2 December. [@1]

The St. Kitts and Nevis Customs and Excise Department added four charges:
submitting a false declaration to a customs officer, importing a quantity of
Cannabis Sativa, importing restricted goods, namely Cannabis Sativa, and
knowingly attempting to fraudulently evade customs. [@2] Hewlett was charged on
Monday 4 December 2023 at the Basseterre Police Station with a number of
drugs-related offences. [@1] VON Radio reported that the charges were laid two
days after the airport offences, which it dated to Saturday 2 December. [@3]

NevisPages published the same customs charges on 6 December 2023, the day
SKNVibes reported the case. [@4] The reports name no weight for the cannabis,
give no street value and do not say whether Hewlett was granted bail or has
appeared in court. [@1]
