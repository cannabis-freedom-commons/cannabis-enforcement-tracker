Officers of the rapid-response group of Belarus's Operational Customs, acting
jointly with the drug-control unit of the Leninsky district police department in
Minsk, detained two Belarusian men who had been growing marijuana. [@1] The
29-year-old detainee had set up a specially equipped room inside his residential
flat for growing cannabis bushes. [@1]

At the moment of detention, officers seized two of those cannabis bushes from
the younger man. [@1] Officers also seized his smoking devices and the equipment
used to grow the marijuana. [@1] That equipment consisted of a grow box, mineral
fertilisers, a timer, fans and measuring instruments. [@1] The second detainee,
a 33-year-old man, was found with smoking devices and a jar of marijuana ready
for consumption, which officers seized. [@2]

Customs said the two detainees face up to five years' imprisonment for drug
trafficking and for illegally sowing and growing plants containing narcotic
drugs. [@1] The Investigative Committee opened a criminal case under article 328
of the Criminal Code. [@2]
