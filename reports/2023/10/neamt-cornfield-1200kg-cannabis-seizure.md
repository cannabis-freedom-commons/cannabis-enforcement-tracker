In Neamț County, DIICOT prosecutors and organized-crime police detained three
traffickers, one of whom had grown the cannabis while the other two stole from
him to sell it onward. [@1] The two men had learned of the cornfield crop and
repeatedly took plants from the grower, storing them in an abandoned house to
sell onward. [@2] They were caught red-handed the previous Sunday while
transporting some of the stored plants, and one of them was caught only after
police fired warning shots into the air. [@2] All three were brought before the
Neamț Tribunal with a proposal for 30 days of preventive arrest. [@2]

Searches recovered 246 cannabis plants weighing 1,200 kilograms from the
farmland, 59 kilograms of cannabis from the greenhouse crop and about 18.5
kilograms from the abandoned building. [@2] DIICOT and the Neamț police
described the 1,200-kilogram field seizure as a record quantity. [@1]

The grower had set up two crops for sale that year, one in a greenhouse behind
his home and a second on nearby farmland hidden inside the corn. [@2] The
investigation was documented by the Neamț organized-crime service together with
DIICOT's Neamț territorial office against three people on risk-drug trafficking
and unlawful-possession allegations. [@3]
