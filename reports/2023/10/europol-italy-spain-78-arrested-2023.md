Europol supported Italy's Financial Guard and the Catalan Mossos d'Esquadra, with Eurojust,
in dismantling a large criminal network behind several tonnes of cannabis trafficked into
the EU, also striking the money-laundering network clearing its proceeds. [@1,2,3]

The action day on 17 October brought 78 arrests, 58 in Italy and 20 in Spain, across 104
house searches, 89 in Italy and 25 in Spain. [@1,2,3] Officers seized about 350 kilos of
cannabis, 327 of hashish and 33 of marijuana, alongside weapons, electronic equipment,
documents and assets worth 845,000 euros. [@1,2,3] Moroccan nationals based in Spain arranged
the drug deliveries into Spain and onward to distributors in Italy. [@1,3]

Between 2019 and 2021 alone, authorities in Italy and Spain had already seized more than
six tonnes of cannabis and hashish from the network, as well as cannabinoid-based
electronic cigarette refills; criminal investigations opened in Italy in 2019 and in Spain
in 2022 and converged as cooperation progressed. [@1,2,3] The suspects coordinated logistics
over the encrypted platforms Sky ECC and Encrochat, and settled shipments through an
informal hawala-like banking system known as fei-ch'ien, run mainly by Chinese nationals.
[@1,2,3] Investigators found two separate but leader-linked networks, one for trafficking
and one for laundering, and financial and asset-recovery inquiries remain open. [@1,2,3] The
network had already been hit in November 2022, when a coordinated action arrested 36
suspects in Italy and six more were detained in the Netherlands and Spain on European
arrest warrants. [@1,2,3]
