On Thursday 26 October 2023, officers from the Anti-Gang and Firearms
Investigation Task Force, working with the Bahamas Customs Department and the
Department of Immigration, carried out a joint operation at the Lynden Pindling
International Airport in Nassau. [@1]

Authorities confiscated suspected marijuana worth an estimated $131,000. [@1]
During an inspection of several incoming packages at an intake area used by a
local courier company, officers found multiple vacuum-sealed bags of suspected
marijuana concealed inside three boxes of furniture. [@1]

No arrests were made in connection with the seizure. [@1] Police said they would
continue to interdict contraband, including dangerous drugs and firearms, in
cooperation with other agencies, and to deter traffickers from importing such
contraband into the Bahamas. [@1]
