Twenty-seven individuals were arrested in connection with hashish and opium
smuggling in the Dur Baba district of eastern Nangarhar province. [@1] Border
forces detained the 27 alleged drug smugglers in the Landa Khwala area of the
district. [@1] The detainees intended to smuggle the drugs across the border
into Pakistan. [@1]

The detentions were confirmed by the spokesman for the 201st Khalid bin Walid
Military Corps in the eastern zone. [@1] Wahidullah Mohammadi provided details
of the operation to Pajhwok Afghan News. [@1] Mohammadi said the detainees had
confessed to their crime after being referred to judicial organs. [@1]
