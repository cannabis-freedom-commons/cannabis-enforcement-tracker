The Tax Agency's Customs Surveillance Service and the National Police seized 2,200 kilos of hashish from a storehouse in a hamlet of Las Cabezas de San Juan in Seville that served as a logistics hub for drugs moved up the Guadalquivir river. [@1]

The investigation began in the summer after officers spotted suspicious movements near the river, identifying people who collected large hashish consignments from riverside hiding places and stored them in a country house away from the town centre. [@1] When the premises were raided and searched, officers found the 2,200 kilos of hashish alongside two cannabis plantations of large-sized plants and two shotguns with a large quantity of ammunition. [@1]

Four people were arrested on suspicion of drug trafficking and unlawful possession of weapons, and everything seized and arrested was brought before Investigating Court No. 2 in Lebrija, which led the investigation. [@1] Further arrests were not ruled out. [@1]
