North Aceh police found a five-hectare cannabis plantation in Sawang Sub-district on Friday 6 October 2023, following the arrests of two local residents. [@1] Officers raided the home of a 38-year-old garden keeper identified as D in Sawang Village that day, then went with him to the plantation area and destroyed the crop. [@1] Both suspects were held in custody for further investigation. [@1]

Police said the plantation came to light through a 32-year-old courier identified as A, who was caught in Sawang Village on 8 September attempting to transport 18 kilograms of dried marijuana. [@1] He told investigators he had obtained the package from D, who in turn said the marijuana came from the plantation owned by A. [@1] The police chief estimated 40,000 plants could grow on five hectares at 8,000 seeds per hectare, a hypothetical rate rather than a counted total. [@1]
