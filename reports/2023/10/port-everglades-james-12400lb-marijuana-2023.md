The crew of the U.S. Coast Guard Cutter James offloaded more than 33,200 pounds
of cocaine and 12,400 pounds of marijuana at Port Everglades, Florida, on
Thursday 26 October 2023. [@1] The marijuana amounted to about 5,625 kilograms,
and the Coast Guard valued the cocaine and marijuana together at approximately
$448 million. [@1]

The drugs came from 13 separate interdiction cases in international waters of
the Eastern Pacific Ocean and the Caribbean Sea. [@1] Several Coast Guard ships
took part, supported by U.S. Customs and Border Protection's Air and Marine
Operations and the Joint Interagency Task Force South. [@1] CBS12 reported that
the seizures were made by a group of Coast Guard and Navy crews including the
cutters Thetis, Vigilant and Valiant, two Coast Guard tactical law enforcement
teams, the destroyer USS Farragut and a Helicopter Interdiction Tactical
Squadron. [@2]

Vice Adm. Kevin Lunday, commander of the U.S. Coast Guard Atlantic Area, called
the offload a matter of national security and part of an interagency and
international effort against maritime drug smuggling into the United States.
[@1] gCaptain reported that the Coast Guard's 11th District in California and
7th District in Miami led the law enforcement operations in the Eastern Pacific
and Caribbean, with the Joint Interagency Task Force South in Key West detecting
and monitoring drug transit. [@3]
