On 10 October, North Kazakhstan police detained a 25-year-old Petropavlovsk man
in a rented flat. [@2] Officers found more than 100 polymer packets of
dose-packaged plant material in the flat. [@1] An expert examination identified
the substance as hashish. [@1] The total weight of the seized hashish exceeded 2
kg. [@1] Police said the man had received the batch through a so-called “klad”,
a hidden stash left for pickup. [@1] The suspected seller was placed in custody
while an investigation continued. [@1]

The case was registered in the unified register of pre-trial investigations
under part 3 of article 297 of Kazakhstan's Criminal Code. [@2] Police noted
that for hashish an especially large amount starts at 200 grams and that the
charge carries up to 15 years' imprisonment. [@1]

Since the start of the year, the region's police said they had seized more than
730 kg of drugs, including over 708 kg of marijuana and un-dried hemp. [@2]
