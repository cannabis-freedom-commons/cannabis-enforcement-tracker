Inter-Agency Task Force (IATF) officers arrested a 23-year-old man at his home
during a warrant exercise on Tuesday, 24 October 2023. [@2] Officers arrived
around 4:00 am at the suspect's residence on Quarry Road in East Dry River, Port
of Spain, to execute a search warrant. [@2] According to the Police Service, the
warrant exercise ran between 4:00 am and 6:00 am on 24 October 2023. [@1]

When officers searched the premises, they found and seized a quantity of
marijuana weighing 363.5 grams, or about 0.36 kilograms. [@2] A Trinidad and
Tobago Police Service (TTPS) statement specified the seized money as an amount
of TT$27,492.00 in Trinidad and Tobago currency. [@1] The accused man was
cautioned and conveyed to the Besson Street Police Station along with the seized
items. [@1]

He was subsequently charged with possession of cannabis. [@2] Officers of the
Financial Investigations Bureau (FIB) are conducting investigations into the
cash that was seized during the warrant exercise. [@2] Police said
investigations into the matter were ongoing. [@1]
