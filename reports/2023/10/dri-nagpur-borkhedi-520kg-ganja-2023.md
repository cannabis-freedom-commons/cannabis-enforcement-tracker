DRI officers in Nagpur intercepted a truck at Borkhedi Toll near Nagpur on the morning of 9 October 2023. [@1,2]

On examining the truck, officers found a specially built-in cavity between the driver's cabin and the cargo portion. [@1,2] The cavity held 520 kg of ganja packed in 242 packages, which DRI valued at Rs 1.04 crore. [@1,2]

Two people travelling in the truck were apprehended and subsequently arrested under the provisions of the NDPS Act. [@1,2] DRI said further investigation was in progress. [@1,2]
