Civil Guard officers arrested a 24-year-old driver at the entrance to La
Guijarrosa, in Córdoba province, after finding 1.3 kilos of hashish pollen in
his car. [@1] The stop happened at about 17.30 on 6 October 2023, at an alcohol
and drug checkpoint on the A-379 regional road. [@1]

Officers pulled over a Citroën C5 and, while breath-testing the driver, noticed
a strong smell of hashish from inside the vehicle. [@1] A search of the car
found a bag holding 13 packets of about 100 grams of hashish pollen each. [@1]
The driver was arrested on suspicion of an offence against public health for
drug trafficking; he was born in Fernán Núñez and lives in Santaella, both in
Córdoba province. [@1]

Officers also found 900 euros in cash and two high-end mobile phones among his
belongings. [@1] The Civil Guard said he could face a prison sentence of six
months to three years and a fine of six to twelve months. [@1]
