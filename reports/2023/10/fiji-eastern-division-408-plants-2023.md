Four hundred and eight marijuana plants were uprooted in Fiji's Eastern Division
in the month before the roundup and in the first two weeks of October 2023, the
Fiji Police Force said. [@1] Divisional Police Commissioner Eastern Josua Vodo
said more than 300 packs of marijuana had also been seized in the division. [@1]

Vodo thanked members of the public for tips that helped police combat marijuana
cultivation. [@1] He said the taskforce continued to be deployed to the Eastern
Division because of the information coming from community members and villagers.
[@1] Vodo said police were seizing drugs and raiding homes, and that community
policing teams visit villages to discuss the consequences, especially for
children. [@1]
