The Wake County Sheriff's Office said two 3-year-olds were sent to the hospital
after ingesting gummies with narcotics in them. [@1] After 3-year-old twins were
sickened by THC gummies, their 24-year-old mother faces criminal charges of
misdemeanor child abuse and felony possession of a controlled substance. [@1]
According to the warrant for her arrest, Sydney Greysen Stoker had the gummies
in her room at 1025 Fair Long Road in Wake Forest where her twins were able to
get access to them. [@1]

The twins' grandmother was taking the twins to the hospital for treatment on 29
October when her car broke down on Calvin Jones Highway in Wake Forest and she
called for assistance. [@1] The grandmother told first responders that the
children had taken gummies that had THC in them. [@1]

Dr. Mike Steiner, pediatrician in chief at UNC Children's Hospital, said 3,000
to 4,000 children under the age of 6 are expected to unintentionally ingest THC
gummies that year. [@1] He added that approximately 10% of those children will
end up in the ICU with depressed sensorium. [@1]
