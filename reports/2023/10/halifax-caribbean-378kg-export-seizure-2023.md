Border officers at the Port of Halifax found 377 kg of suspected cannabis and
1 kg of suspected hashish in a marine container bound for the Caribbean in
late October 2023. [@1,2] The drugs sat in two large boxes inside a container
declared as personal and household goods: 765 packages of cannabis and one
package of hashish. [@1] CBSA intelligence officers assisted the examination,
and the haul was handed to the Nova Scotia RCMP for further investigation. [@1]

The seizure followed a similar August find at the same port, when officers
pulled 165 kg of suspected cannabis from another Caribbean-bound container. [@1]
