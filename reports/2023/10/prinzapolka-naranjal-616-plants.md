Nicaraguan soldiers of the Ecological Battalion Bosawas seized 616 marijuana
plants in the community of Naranjal, Prinzapolka municipality, North Caribbean
Coast Autonomous Region, on 9 October 2023. [@1] The army said the plants were
1.5 metres tall each and had been sown in a mountainous area. [@1] Alongside the
plants, troops seized 1.5 pounds of processed marijuana ready for sale and 6
ounces of seeds, with 1.5 pounds equal to about 0.68 kilograms. [@1]

Troops retained Nicaraguan citizen Osmar Úbeda Zelaya, 30, whom the army
described as the person who looked after and maintained the plantation. [@1] The
detainee, the plants, the drugs and the seeds were handed over to the
corresponding authorities. [@1]

Canal 4, reproducing the same army press note, said Ecological Battalion
officers seized about 616 marijuana plants in the community of Naranja in
Prinzapolka municipality in the North Caribbean region. [@2] TN8, citing the
same army statement, said members of the Ecological Battalion Bosawas of the
Eighth Military Region occupied a marijuana plantation and captured a person in
the community of El Naranjal under Prinzapolka jurisdiction in the North
Caribbean Coast Autonomous Region. [@3] TN8 said the operation seized 616
marijuana plants in the community of El Naranjal. [@3] TN8 said the soldiers
also seized 1.5 pounds of processed marijuana ready for sale and 6 ounces of
seeds. [@3] TN8 named the retained man as Nicaraguan citizen Osmar Úbeda Zelaya,
30, who looked after and maintained the plants. [@3]

The operation was carried out under the state strategy Muro de Contención, the
government's Containment Wall anti-drug campaign. [@1] The army said the
protective measures ordered by the Health Ministry against COVID-19 were
complied with during the operation. [@1] The information note, numbered
023/2023, was dated 10 October 2023 at the headquarters of the Ecological
Battalion Bosawas. [@1]
