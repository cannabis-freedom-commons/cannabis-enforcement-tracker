Interior Minister Taulant Balla said on 23 October 2023 that 25 people had been arrested or proceeded against and 11,729 cannabis plants destroyed during October under the national Operation Focus. [@1] Balla said the State Police continues to systematically strike groups and criminal activity linked to cultivating and distributing narcotics. [@1]

The statement also says criminal cultivation activity in indoor settings was uncovered during the month. [@1] It gives no district breakdown and names no individual suspects. [@1]
