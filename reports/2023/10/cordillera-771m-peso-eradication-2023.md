The Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency in the Cordillera Administrative Region (PDEA-CAR)
destroyed about P771 million worth of marijuana plants in 200 eradication operations from
January to 16 October 2023. [@1] Regional director Julius Paderes said 112 operations were
conducted in Benguet, 79 in Kalinga, six in Mountain Province and three in Ifugao. [@1]

## Mostly plants, mostly Kalinga

Most of what was destroyed were plants worth P688 million, alongside P2.3 million in
seedlings, P4.5 million in dried leaves, P70 million in stalks, P2.4 million in bricks
and P12,500 in seeds. [@1] Kalinga recorded the highest value destroyed at P624.5 million,
even though most operations took place in Benguet. [@1]

Paderes said teams return to the same places because new plantations can appear near
earlier raid sites, with the goal of fully eradicating cultivation. [@1] The police, the
Armed Forces of the Philippines, the Coast Guard and the National Bureau of Investigation
joined PDEA-CAR in the operations. [@1]
