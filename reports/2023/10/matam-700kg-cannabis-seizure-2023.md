Officers of the Matam central police station detained two suspected drug
traffickers in the operation. [@2] The parcels and the concession's senior
representative were presented to the press on Monday 23 October 2023. [@2]
Police said the suspects and the six bags would be placed at the disposal of the
prosecutor at the Mafanco court of first instance. [@2] The concession head
denied ownership and said the drugs belonged to his nephew Mohamed Camara, who
lives in Dabondy and does not work. [@1] He said he had been asleep when his
nephew brought the parcels in and that he asked him to remove them after
learning they held cannabis, before police arrived that morning. [@1]

Officers seized six bags holding about 700 kilograms of cannabis in a household
at Matam on the morning of Monday 23 October 2023. [@1] The seizure was made by
agents of the Matam central police station. [@1] A second account describes the
place as the Soumah family concession known as Mafereinyah. [@2] The operation
took place at Tougui Wondy in Matam commune. [@2] The team continued the same
investigation at about 3 a.m. on the basis of intelligence, finding the six bags
in the concession of Fodé Soumah managed by the eldest son called Maférinyah.
[@2]

Commissioner Mohamed Lamine Conté said the seizure followed a requisition from
the Matam mayor to clear coastal areas of cannabis-use shelters. [@1] The
commissioner said the six bags were seized on the basis of intelligence in the
concession of Fodé Soumah called Maferinyah. [@1]
