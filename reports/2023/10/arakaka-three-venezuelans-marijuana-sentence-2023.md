On 20 October 2023 at about 5:27 PM, lawmen at the Arakaka Police Station
arrested three Venezuelan men. [@3] The men were identified as Eliyer Vera
Venez, Romel Masim and Jiminez Rojas. [@1] Ranks had received information that
three men had visited a shop to charge their phones and were seen with firearms
in their waistbands. [@2] Shortly after, Reny Melville arrived at the shop and
picked up the suspected Venezuelan nationals. [@2] They left on an ATV toward
Melville residence at Waterfront, Arakaka in the North West District. [@2] The
ranks requested permission to conduct a search in and around his home, to which
he agreed. [@2] The ranks entered the house, where they found three men lying on
the floor, communicating in Spanish. [@2]

Police found a Glock 19 pistol, two Beretta 92 A1 pistols and matching
ammunition in a haversack under a bed. [@1] At the outpost the suspected drugs
weighed 66.4 grams of cocaine and 64.7 grams of marijuana in the presence of the
individuals. [@1] The police took the three Venezuelans into custody, but
Melville escaped while the officers searched his home. [@1]

On 24 October 2023 one of the three Venezuelans pleaded guilty and was sentenced
to six years imprisonment in total. [@3] For the marijuana he was sentenced to
three years imprisonment and fined GY$58,230 for possessing 64.7 grammes for
trafficking. [@3] Romel Marin and Alirio Jimenez pleaded not guilty and their
cases were adjourned to November 13 for trial. [@3]
