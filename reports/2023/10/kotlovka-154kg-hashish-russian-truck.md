Grodno customs officers stopped a 44-year-old Russian citizen at the Kotlovka
border crossing on the border with Lithuania as he tried to bring 154 kg of
hashish into the Eurasian Economic Union. [@1] Investigators say he acted as
part of a group that had agreed in advance and meant to deliver the consignment
to Russia. [@1] The truck was empty and the hashish was hidden in specially
built compartments in the truck. [@1]

A customs sniffer dog confirmed that the hiding places held drugs. [@1] After
dismantling the decorative lining of the cab floor and the front part of the
semi-trailer, officers removed 231 bricks of pressed dark-brown substance. [@1]
An expert of the Lida interdistrict department of the State Committee of
Forensic Examinations for the Grodno region confirmed that the substance was
hashish. [@1]

Grodno Regional Customs opened a criminal case against the driver under part 2
of article 328-1 of the Criminal Code. [@1] The man was detained under article
108 of the Criminal Procedure Code. [@1] The charge carries up to 10 years in
prison, a possible penalty rather than a sentence the man has received. [@1]

The state news agency BelTA reported the case, citing Grodno Regional Customs.
[@2] BelTA described the man as a carrier who drove through the Kotlovka
crossing with the drugs hidden in the empty truck. [@2] Minskaya Pravda, the
Minsk regional news agency, reported that the hashish was found with an
inspection-scanning complex and that a sniffer dog confirmed the drug. [@3]
