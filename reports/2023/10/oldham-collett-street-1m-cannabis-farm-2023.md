Greater Manchester Police raided an empty industrial unit on Collett Street in
Derker, Oldham, on the morning of Wednesday 18 October 2023. [@1,2] Officers
found hundreds of cannabis plants inside the property. [@1,2]

The force said the farm was believed to be worth more than one million
pounds. [@1,2] Two men in their thirties were arrested on suspicion of
cultivating cannabis and were held in custody for questioning. [@1,2]

Chief Superintendent Phil Hutchinson said community support had enabled the
warrant and called the discovery a huge find. [@1,2] He said the seizure brought
the total street value of cannabis farms seized by the Oldham team in the
previous four weeks to more than four million pounds. [@1,2] He added that a
web of organised criminality stood behind the recent seizures. [@1,2]
