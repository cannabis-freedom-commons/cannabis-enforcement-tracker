Military police destroyed a planting of 4,000 cannabis plants at Bebedouro, a
locality in Campo Formoso in northern Bahia, on Friday 20 October 2023. [@1] The
Bahia Public Security Secretariat, the SSP-BA, announced the operation on the
afternoon of Saturday 21 October. [@1]

Officers also seized 17 kilograms of cannabis and two firearms at the site, and
no one was arrested. [@1] According to the SSP-BA, teams from the 54th and 75th
Independent Military Police Companies, known in Brazil as CIPMs, destroyed the
crop, together with Rondesp Norte, a Military Police unit. [@1] The planting was
found after investigations by intelligence units, with support from drones. [@1]

The commander of the 54th CIPM, major Gil Santana, said the joint work was
essential. [@1] The force said the action against organised crime was part of
Operação Terra Limpa, which it said had eradicated 820,000 cannabis plants in
2023. [@1]

CN1 Camaçari Notícias republished the g1 account on 22 October 2023 with the
same plant, drug and firearm figures and the same statement that no one was
arrested. [@2]
