Officers from the Wroclaw provincial police's anti-football-hooligan unit
received information that a 29-year-old resident of Trzebnica County, linked to
the football-hooligan milieu, was growing cannabis in a field next to his
property. [@2] He hid the plants far from roads and paths among growing corn,
believing nobody would notice them. [@1] When police checked the field, only
three large bushes remained, so they also searched the man's property. [@1] The
man first insisted the plants were not his, but admitted the truth after
officers found equipment in an outbuilding that could be used to process drugs.
[@1]

In the attic of a workshop building he had created special chambers for drying
selected cannabis parts quickly before portioning the finished marijuana. [@1]
Police said the prepared drug was carefully weighed and labelled in glass
containers. [@1] In total, police seized more than 4.5 kilograms of finished
marijuana and the three bushes found among the corn. [@3]

The 29-year-old was held in police custody, questioned, and charged with
possessing a significant quantity of drugs and cultivation, which carries up to
10 years in prison. [@1] His case was left for a court to decide, with no
outcome reported at the time of publication. [@1]
