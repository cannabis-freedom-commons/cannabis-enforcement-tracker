Police and Tripura State Rifles (TSR) personnel destroyed about 30,000 cannabis plants across several plots at Ramdula para, under Pecharthal police station, on the morning of 12 October 2023. [@1] The raid began at 10 AM and was led by the Kumarghat sub-divisional police officer in charge, with the deputy superintendent of police (probationer), the Pecharthal officer in charge and additional police and TSR personnel. [@1]

The report describes the drive as part of the effort against illegal ganja cultivation and distribution in the area. [@1] It names no arrests, growers or follow-up proceedings. [@1]
