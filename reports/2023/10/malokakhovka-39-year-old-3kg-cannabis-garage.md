Police officers acting on operational information detained a 39-year-old
resident of the village of Malokakhovka in Kakhovsky district of Kherson Oblast
on suspicion of illegal drug trafficking. [@1] The search took place at his
private household, in the garage. [@1] There officers found four cardboard boxes
and one metal and one plastic container filled with plant material smelling of
marijuana. [@1] The Kherson Oblast directorate's press service put the total
weight at about three kilograms and said the material was at various stages of
drying. [@1]

The man gave a confessional statement and said he had kept the cannabis both for
personal consumption and for further sale. [@1] Everything seized was sent for
examination to the forensic centre of the Russian Interior Ministry's Kherson
Oblast directorate. [@1] Officials said they were deciding whether to open a
criminal case on signs of crimes under articles 228 and 228.1 of the Russian
Criminal Code, which cover illegal possession and illegal sale of drugs. [@1]
