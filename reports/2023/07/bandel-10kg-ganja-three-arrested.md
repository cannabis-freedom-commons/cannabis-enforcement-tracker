Three people were arrested at Bandel, described as including a woman. [@1] Three
persons were arrested with 10 kg of marijuana on a Saturday. [@1] Police
identified one of the three people held as Sagar Tamang, who was in the car with
the ganja. [@1] Sources identified the driver of the car as Purushottam Jha.
[@1]

The crime branch stopped the vehicle near the Keota checkpoint in Bandel and
recovered 10 kg of ganja. [@1] Police estimated the market value of the seized
ganja at several lakhs of rupees. [@1]

The Special Branch said it had received reliable information that people were
coming to Bandel road with marijuana and had formed a team and laid a trap. [@1]
Police said the trio hired a taxi from Howrah station and told the driver they
would first go to Sealdah and then to Bandel station in Hooghly. [@1] After
reaching Bandel, the car was to be taken on to Jirat Balagarh. [@1]
