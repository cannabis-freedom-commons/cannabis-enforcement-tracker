Police in Mostar arrested four people after officers raided a laboratory for
growing marijuana, as announced at a 4 July press conference. [@1] Those
arrested were two men, one of them a German citizen, and two women, and the
police report was submitted to the competent HNK prosecutor's office. [@1]
Police identified those held only by initials as B.Ć. born in 1998 with dual
German and Bosnian citizenship, Z.D. born in 1992, E.H. born in 2000 and K.P.
[@2] Police filed a crime report against the two men with the competent
prosecutor's office in Mostar, while the two women remained under investigation.
[@2] Police said the arrested people were previously unknown to the police and
had no prior convictions. [@1]

The operation, codenamed Lab, was carried out on 2 and 3 July by the Department
for Special Purposes of the HNK Interior Ministry with the Mostar Police
Administration intervention group and criminal police, under a Mostar Municipal
Court order. [@1] At three locations inspectors found a laboratory with
sophisticated equipment and seized 444 cannabis stems, two automatic rifles and
cultivation equipment and material. [@1] Officers searched three sites in the
Sjever, Stari grad and Jug areas of Mostar, where one equipped laboratory had
not been put into operation before the raid. [@2] Alongside the 444 cannabis
stems, officers seized two automatic rifles, bottles with seeds and all material
for growing, protecting and feeding small seedlings. [@2] Of the 444 stems, 198
were seedlings up to 30 centimetres in pots and 246 were cut or adult plants in
the final production stage. [@2]

Police described it as one of the largest drug seizures in the recent period in
Bosnia and Herzegovina. [@1] Police had not established the value of the seized
drugs and growing equipment when the results were announced. [@2] Police said
further investigation would establish the value of the confiscated drugs and
equipment, how long the group had been active and how much it had produced and
sold. [@1]
