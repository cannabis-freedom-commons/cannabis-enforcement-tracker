The Edo State command of Nigeria's National Drug Law Enforcement Agency (NDLEA)
destroyed two cannabis farms in Upokhoten forest near Uzzebba village in Owan West
Local Government Area on 28 July 2023, cutting the plantations and setting them
ablaze after a three-hour trek to the site. [@1]

Assistant Commander Ondotimi Bebetu said the destruction followed intelligence-led
surveillance that had led to the arrest of three suspects two weeks earlier. [@1]
He put the first farm at 6.28 hectares, equal to about 15.69 tonnes of cannabis, and
the second at 0.68 hectares, equal to 1.7 tonnes, totalling 17.4 tonnes destroyed. [@1]
Daily Trust's account of the same operation gives the same measurements, totalling
6.96 hectares and more than 17 tonnes. [@2]

Bebetu said the real owners were big-time barons who hired local boys to establish
the farms, and that the arrested suspects would be handed to the agency's legal unit
to advise on prosecution. [@1] One of those arrested on the farm, Monday Nduul from
Benue State, said he had been brought in as a labourer to weed and had not known it
was a cannabis farm. [@1] Assistant Commander James Olarewaju said Edo had become a
spotlight for year-round cannabis plantations and the command was determined to cut
the supply chain through farm destruction. [@1]
