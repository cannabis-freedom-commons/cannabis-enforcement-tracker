Three people were arrested in Juab County during a drug investigation and booked
into the Juab County Jail for investigation of drug possession. [@1] The three
were Jordan Lindsay Mendoza, 35, Tyler Lester Perry, 20, and Gavino Santana
Rich, 18. [@1] An attempted murder allegation was added to each of the three
suspects' booking sheets on the Tuesday after their arrests. [@1]

The case began about 5:30 a.m. on that Friday, when Tristan Scott Edmonson, 27,
entered a convenience store in Nephi bleeding and confused, saying he had been
shot at in a field. [@1] Santaquin police stopped a vehicle registered to
Mendoza travelling north on I-15 and took Mendoza and the two men into custody,
finding AR-15 style weapons in plain view in the rear with bandanas covering
parts of the guns. [@1]

A search warrant obtained for the Highlander turned up 1 kilo of cocaine, and
Edmonson was arrested for investigation of drug possession with intent to
distribute and marijuana possession. [@1] GPS data from the rental Highlander
showed it stopping in the small town of Rocky Ridge in Juab County, which
investigators believed was around the time of the shooting. [@1] After an
extensive search, deputies found where the shooting happened, with foot
impressions in the dirt matching the shoes of Perry and Rich and four shell
casings. [@1]
