Throughout June 2023, all 43 police forces across England and Wales worked with the
National Crime Agency and Immigration Enforcement to search large-scale cannabis
grows in Operation Mille, the most significant national operation of its kind ever
run across UK law enforcement. [@1,2] About 11,000 officers coordinated 1,013
warrants and searches against organised crime groups that use cannabis cultivation
as a key source of illicit revenue. [@1,2]

Police seized 182,422 plants with an estimated street value of GBP 115-130 million
and arrested 967 people for offences including cultivation, drugs supply, money
laundering and weapons possession. [@1,2] Officers also seized 20 firearms, GBP
636,000 in cash and cocaine, put at 26 kilogrammes by the NPCC and 20 kilogrammes by
the BBC. [@1,2] More than 450 of those arrested had since been charged. [@2]

NPCC serious and organised crime lead Steve Jupp said the operation disrupted a
significant amount of criminal activity and that cannabis networks were also linked
to Class A drug importation, modern slavery and wider violence and exploitation. [@1]
He said the intelligence gathered would inform future law enforcement work across
the country. [@1]
