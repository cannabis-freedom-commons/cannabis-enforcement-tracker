California's Unified Cannabis Enforcement Taskforce (UCETF) served 24 search warrants on
unlicensed commercial cannabis cultivation sites in Siskiyou County between 11 and 13 July
2023. [@1,2] The sites were in the Whitney Creek, Harry Cash and Shasta Vistas areas. [@1,2]

## Largest operation to date

Officers eradicated 67,045 illegal cannabis plants and seized 8,019.75 pounds (about
3,637.7 kg) of processed cannabis, which the governor's office valued at an estimated
$68.5 million. [@1,2] They also seized a dozen firearms. [@1,2]

The operation targeted growers that harm the environment and the public through unlawful
pesticides and toxic chemicals, according to the governor's office, which called it the
task force's largest operation to date. [@1]
