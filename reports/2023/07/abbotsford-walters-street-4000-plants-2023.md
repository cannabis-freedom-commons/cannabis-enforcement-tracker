On 13 July 2023, the BC Conservation Officer Service and the Abbotsford Police
Department searched a rural property in the 7400 block of Walters Street in
Abbotsford and found a large-scale marijuana grow operation. [@1] The
conservation officers had asked Abbotsford officers to attend the search to keep
the peace during a separate conservation investigation. [@3] The police drug
enforcement unit then investigated and determined the grow was producing more
cannabis than legally allowed. [@1] The unit obtained a second search warrant
under the Cannabis Act to search the property. [@1]

During that search officers seized approximately 4,000 marijuana plants. [@1]
Officers also seized about two kilograms of suspected fentanyl and about one
kilogram of suspected cocaine alongside the plants. [@2] Police seized six
firearms including two handguns, three sawed-off shotguns, a rifle and extended
loaded magazines. [@1] Police found 12 more lawfully owned firearms and seized
them for public safety and safekeeping, bringing the reported total to 18 guns.
[@1]

Police said the property was authorised to grow cannabis for medical purposes
but was overproducing outside its legal confines. [@2] Police said there was no
threat to public safety related to the incident and the drug enforcement unit
was continuing the investigation. [@1] No charges had been laid at the time the
seizures were reported. [@3]
