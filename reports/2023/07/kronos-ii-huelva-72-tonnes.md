Spain's Customs Surveillance Service and the Civil Guard dismantled a family-clan
network smuggling hashish by sea along the Huelva coast, the Tax Agency announced
on 10 July 2023. [@2] Operation Kronos II had begun the previous November after
investigators found consignments moving through Ayamonte and Isla Cristina. [@2]

The network hid bales of hashish in false bottoms of pleasure craft, then moved
them by off-road vehicle to coastal caches and houses for distribution. [@2]
Officers foiled five stashes and seized 7,200 kg of hashish, 15 boats and 14
vehicles in total. [@2]

Two April-May seizures accounting for 2,600 kg of that total had been reported
earlier: 1,353 kg at a storage site near the Pozo del Camino marshes, with two
loaded firearms and 16 vehicles seized, and 39 bales weighing 1,300 kg abandoned
after a chase at Punta del Moral in Ayamonte, where two people were arrested.
[@1,2]

In the early hours of 27 June officers carried out 14 raids on homes and
warehouses in Ayamonte, Isla Cristina and Villablanca and arrested eight
suspected members; three were remanded in custody and five released on bond.
[@2] Officers also seized air guns, a taser, night-vision gear, ten luxury
watches and 17,075 euros in cash, and the investigations stayed open. [@2]
