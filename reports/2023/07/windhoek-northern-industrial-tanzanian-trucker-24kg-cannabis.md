Namibian Police arrested a 37-year-old Tanzanian man in Windhoek's northern
industrial area on Sunday at about 20h00. [@1] A truck coming in from South
Africa was intercepted and searched, and three big parcels of cannabis were
found inside. [@1]

The police put the value of the parcels at N$1.2 million, and Informanté, citing
the police media chief, reported the load as about 24,460 grams (24.46 kg) of
cannabis skunk worth N$1,223,000. [@1] Informanté, citing police media chief
inspector Elifas Kuwinga, reported a controlled delivery and said police
investigations were ongoing. [@2] Informanté reported that the arrest was made
at Northern Industry, the name the police used for the same area. [@2]

The man was due to appear in the Windhoek Magistrate's Court on Tuesday, two
days after the arrest. [@1]
