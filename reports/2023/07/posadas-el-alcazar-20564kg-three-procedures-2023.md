Gendarmería Nacional Argentina reported seizures of more than 20 kilograms of
marijuana in three separate procedures in Misiones, all on the orders of the
magistrate handling each case. [@1] The first procedure took place at the bus
terminal in the city of Posadas, where officers of the Núcleo section of
Escuadrón 50 Posadas, a Gendarmería squadron, were carrying out checks. [@1]
Inspecting a long-distance bus, the officers smelled a strong characteristic
smell of marijuana and searched a female passenger's handbag, finding
rectangular packages of plant material weighing 9 kilos 985 grams in total. [@1]
MisionesOnline reported that the officers grew suspicious of two adult women
travelling with minors in the early hours. [@2] Both women were released subject
to the case, MisionesOnline reported. [@2]

In a second operation, officers of the El Arco road section opened a courier
parcel holding three bricks of cannabis sativa that weighed 2 kilos 134 grams.
[@1] In a third procedure, officers of the El Alcázar group, part of Escuadrón
11 San Ignacio, were deployed on Ruta Provincial N° 11 near the locality of El
Alcázar when two men on a motorcycle approached the checkpoint. [@2] The two men
sped away when they saw the force, fleeing towards Colonia Tarumá, and threw
away a bundle holding 8 kilos 445 grams of marijuana. [@2] The release does not
say whether the motorcyclists were caught, and it reported no arrest in the
parcels case. [@1]

MisionesOnline reported that field tests identified the material found on the
bus as cannabis sativa and put a price on each of the three loads. [@2] The
three seizures together came to 20 kilos 564 grams of marijuana, matching the
release's headline of more than 20 kilograms. [@1] The magistrate handling each
case, as the release calls the responsible federal judge, ordered the three
loads seized. [@1]
