A special team of Nagapattinam Police arrested six men on 31 July 2023 who were
suspected of belonging to an international ganja smuggling gang and seized
contraband bound for Sri Lanka. [@1] Police named the six as V. Ravi, 53, of
Agasthiyampalli; M. Lakshmanan, 43, of Kodiyakadu; M. Ravi, 28, and K.
Vedhamani, 27, of Thethakudi; M. Kumar, 44, of Kunnam in Perambalur district;
and J. Mayakrishnan, 29, of Madurai. [@1] ABP Nadu identified the Kodiyakadu
accused, Lakshmanan, as a DMK functionary. [@3] Vedaranyam police registered a
case against the six under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act,
and they were arrested and remanded in judicial custody. [@1] The accused were
produced before the criminal magistrate court at Vedaranyam and lodged in
Nagapattinam prison. [@3]

Acting on a tip-off, the special team ran a surprise vehicle check near the
Kuravappulam Railway level crossing late on Sunday night and intercepted a
four-wheeler. [@1] Police found over 300 kg of ganja packed in 12 bags, meant to
be smuggled to Sri Lanka, and confiscated it. [@1] Questioning revealed the
ganja had been bought in Andhra Pradesh and driven via Madurai to Vedaranyam for
smuggling to Sri Lanka. [@2] Police also seized the four-wheeler and two
two-wheelers used in the operation. [@1] Maalaimalar put the weight at about 300
kg and its value at about 60 lakh rupees. [@2] ABP Nadu reported the haul's
Indian value at about 60 lakh rupees and its possible overseas value at about
six crore rupees. [@3]

Superintendent of Police Harsh Singh told The Hindu that a special police team
had a tip-off that a large quantity of ganja was entering Nagapattinam district
from the Odisha-Andhra border areas. [@1] He said the drugs were meant to go
illegally to Sri Lanka by sea, with Nagapattinam as a transit point. [@1] Singh
inspected the seized contraband at Vedaranyam police station on 31 July. [@1]
Police said further investigations were under way. [@1]
