Joint teams of the Police Regional Office Cordillera, the Philippine Drug
Enforcement Agency (PDEA)-Cordillera and the PNP Drug Enforcement Group ran 29
marijuana eradication operations in Benguet and Kalinga from 16 to 23 July
2023. [@1,2] They destroyed 693,615 fully grown marijuana plants, 35,560
seedlings and 75,000 grams of dried leaves and stalks, worth PHP149,145,400.
[@1,2]

Ten people were arrested during the same eight-day period, but in separate
anti-drug operations in Baguio, Benguet and Abra, caught with 10.69 grams of
shabu (methamphetamine) worth PHP72,692, not cannabis. [@1,2] Regional director
Brig. Gen. David Peredo Jr. said the volume of marijuana found was sharply
lower than in Herodotus 1 and 2, which he read as proof the operations were
working. [@1,2]
