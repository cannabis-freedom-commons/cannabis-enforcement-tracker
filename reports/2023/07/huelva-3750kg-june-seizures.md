Customs officers, National Police and Civil Guard seized 3,750 kg of hashish in
two operations eight days apart on the Huelva coast, the Tax Agency said on 1
July 2023. [@1]

On the evening of 1 June officers chased an overloaded van through the Isla
Canela marshlands until the driver abandoned it and fled; inside were 78 bales
weighing about 2,700 kg, and the van proved stolen in Badajoz province with bent
number plates. [@1]

On the evening of 9 June the SIVE surveillance system spotted a drug-smuggling
speedboat south of Punta Umbría transferring packages to a six-metre pleasure
craft, which officers followed to the Astur salt flats; its crew also fled,
leaving 30 bales weighing about 1,050 kg. [@1] Both cases went to the courts in
Ayamonte and Huelva, and further arrests were not ruled out. [@1]
