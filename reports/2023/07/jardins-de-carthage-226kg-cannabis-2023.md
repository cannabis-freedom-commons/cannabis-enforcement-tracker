Police dismantled an international drug-trafficking network operating through an
apartment in Jardins de Carthage. [@1] Investigators identified the apartment
owner, located him and arrested him in an ambush in Gammarth in the northern
suburbs of Tunis. [@1] The owner was arrested with seven other people of
different nationalities, and two additional suspects were also arrested. [@1]
The public prosecutor ordered the nine suspects held in detention while the
investigation continued. [@1]

Police seized 2,262 slabs of cannabis weighing 226 kg in the Jardins de Carthage
apartment. [@1] The Interior Ministry valued the seized cannabis at six million
Tunisian dinars. [@1] The Interior Ministry announced the case on Monday 31 July
2023. [@1] Investigators had opened the inquiry the previous Saturday after
information that suitcases containing dozens of kilos were being brought into
the apartment. [@1]
