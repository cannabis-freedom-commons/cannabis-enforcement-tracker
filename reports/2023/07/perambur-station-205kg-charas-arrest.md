Greater Chennai Police arrested three people at Perambur railway station on
Thursday. [@1] The arrested people were named as Upendra Kumar, 30, of Bihar,
and Ramchander, 35, and Muskanja, 20, both from Nepal. [@1] The three had
reached the city by the Sanghamitra Express from Bihar and were waiting for an
autorickshaw when they were intercepted. [@1] A special team supervised by
Deputy Commissioner of Police I. Eswaran of Pulianthope had mounted surveillance
around the station after a tip-off. [@1]

Police seized 20.5 kg of charas smuggled from Nepal with a market value of ₹1
crore. [@1] Officers said those arrested were found in possession of 20 kg of
charas and 300 mg of ganja oil. [@1] Charas is separated resin obtained from the
cannabis plant. [@1]

Joint Commissioner of Police R. V. Ramya Bharathi said the contraband was
sourced from Nepal and that police were investigating where it was headed and
who was behind the racket. [@1] She said 1,800 kg of ganja, 12 kg of
methamphetamine and other drugs worth ₹28 crore had been seized in the city that
year. [@1]
