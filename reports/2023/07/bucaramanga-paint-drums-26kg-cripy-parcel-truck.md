Police of the Traffic and Transport Section of the Metropolitan Police of
Bucaramanga found 26,000 grams of marijuana hidden in four paint drums in a
parcel truck, in an operation on the La Fortuna–Bucaramanga road at kilometre
52. [@1] Inspecting the vehicle, officers found four paint tubs and inside them
48 packages of a substance that by its characteristics resembles cripy
marijuana, a pressed, stronger-smelling variety. [@3]

Major Carlos Ernesto Vergara Vélez, head of the Mebuc traffic police, said the
drug was pressed and meant to supply the Bucaramanga metropolitan area. [@1] The
parcel had been sent from Cali to the Bucaramanga metropolitan area. [@1]
Vanguardia reported that police had intelligence that the load was coming from
Cali and stopped the truck after an informant described it. [@4]

No one was arrested, because the drug was moving as a parcel, and the marijuana
was handed to the Attorney General's Office while investigators work to find
those who sent it. [@4] Melodía en Línea reported that police said they would
keep investigating to secure the arrest of those responsible for the shipment.
[@2]
