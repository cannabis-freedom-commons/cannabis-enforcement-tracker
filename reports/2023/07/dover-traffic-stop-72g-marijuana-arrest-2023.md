The Dover Police Department arrested Jordan Patrick, 29, of Magnolia, after a
traffic stop early on 26 July 2023. [@1] Officers stopped his car at College
Road and Saulsbury Road at 1:01 a.m. after it failed to stop at a red light and
continued east on College Road at high speed. [@1] A records check showed
Patrick had a capias, an arrest warrant, and officers took him into custody
without incident. [@1]

A search of Patrick and his car found a 9mm handgun, 72 grams (0.072 kg) of
marijuana and 14 grams of mushrooms, which police said tested positive for
methamphetamine. [@2]

Patrick was taken to the Dover police station, where he was processed and
arraigned. [@1] He was charged with possession of marijuana other than for
personal use, possession of a firearm during the commission of a felony,
possession of methamphetamine in a Tier 2 quantity, carrying a concealed deadly
weapon, failure to stop at a red light and driving at an unreasonable speed.
[@1] He was released on $23,603 unsecured bail. [@1]
