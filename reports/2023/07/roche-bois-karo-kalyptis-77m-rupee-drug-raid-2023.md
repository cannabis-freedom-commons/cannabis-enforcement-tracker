The Flying Squad of the Anti-Drug and Smuggling Unit raided a house in Karo
Kalyptis, in the Roche-Bois suburb of Port Louis, on Monday 31 July 2023 and
broke up one of the neighbourhood's largest drug sales points. [@2] Three
suspected dealers were arrested and were to be taken to court on 1 August 2023.
[@2] Officers found the drugs in a sports bag that one of the suspects threw out
of a back window as they entered. [@3]

The raid, carried out in the early hours of the morning, seized cannabis,
cannabis resin, heroin and synthetic drugs. [@4] The cannabis found was 493
grams of cannabis herb and 411 grams of cannabis resin. [@3] The bag also
contained 3.2 kg of heroin granules, about 2.9 kg of synthetic drug and doses of
both, which are not counted as cannabis here. [@3] Police valued the whole mixed
haul at Rs 77 million (more than $1.7m at the time), a figure covering the
heroin and synthetics as well as the cannabis. [@1]

All three were provisionally charged in the Port Louis court on 1 August 2023
with drug dealing and returned to detention, according to Top FM. [@5] The
provisional charges were drug dealing with aggravating circumstances and
possession of a dangerous drug for the purpose of distribution with an averment
of trafficking. [@3] Officers said they had watched the sales points for more
than three weeks beforehand, working in plain clothes. [@2] Karo Kalyptis, a
cluster of shacks in Roche-Bois, is described as the epicentre of the island's
drug trade, a neighbourhood where lookouts often warn dealers of police visits
in advance. [@1]
