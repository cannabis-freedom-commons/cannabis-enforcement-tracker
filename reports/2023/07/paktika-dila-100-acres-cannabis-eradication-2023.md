Counter-narcotics police destroyed cannabis plants grown on about 100 acres of
land in Dila district of southeastern Paktika province, a police spokesman said
on Sunday. [@1] Police spokesman Mohammadullah told Pajhwok Afghan News that the
eradication took place during a campaign launched a week earlier in Dila
district. [@1] "So far cannabis plant on 100 acres land has been cleared and the
campaign is still ongoing because Dila is a large district," the spokesman said.
[@1]

The Ministry of Interior wrote on its Twitter page that cannabis and poppy crops
had been destroyed on more than 100 acres of land in Paktika and Parwan
provinces. [@2] The ministry said the operations took place in Dila district of
Paktika and Saidkhel district of Parwan, and that the campaign was still going
on. [@2]

The Ministry of Interior also reported that anti-narcotics police had arrested
eight people for selling drugs and alcohol in Kabul, Uruzgan, Khost and Parwan
provinces, a separate matter. [@2] The Islamic Emirate has banned the
cultivation, purchase and sale of any kind of drugs in the country, and
violators are dealt with legally. [@2]
