On 21 August 2023, joint personnel of Indonesia's National Narcotics Board
(BNN), the military and the police destroyed a marijuana field in Blang Manyak
village, Sawang district, North Aceh. [@1,2]

Officers found more than 11,000 ready-to-harvest plants spread over 1.2
hectares, which they estimated at about 5 tonnes, and burned the crop.
[@1,2] BNN narcotics director Kombes Pol. Guntur Aryo Tejo led the operation
and set fire to the crops. [@2]

The captions name no arrests. [@1,2]
