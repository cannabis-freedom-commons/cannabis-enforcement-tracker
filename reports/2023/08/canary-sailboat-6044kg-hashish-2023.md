Officers of Spain's Customs Surveillance Service boarded a sailing vessel 95
miles south of the Canary Islands and found 6,044 kilograms of hashish packed
in 169 bales, arresting its two crew. [@1] The Maritime Analysis and Operations
Centre (MAOC-N) in Lisbon said the vessel may have loaded the resin off
Morocco after sailing from southern Portugal and was believed bound for
Brazil. [@2]

The boarding, from the Customs patrol boat Condor, capped an investigation
running since 2020 into a Dutch-origin network that ran its Spanish logistics
from a town in Alicante. [@1] Police then carried out six raids in Alicante,
Murcia, Madrid and Gran Canaria, arresting five people in total and seizing
more than 35,000 euros in cash, boats and a vehicle. [@1] Among those held was
the network's leader, wanted by Germany, where prosecutors are seeking a
15-year sentence. [@1]

Officers searching a warehouse in Murcia also dismantled a laboratory for
making synthetic-drug tablets, with its presses and raw materials. [@1] MAOC-N
said the seizure points to Moroccan hashish being traded directly with
Brazilian groups for cocaine. [@2]
