Officers of the National Police Brigade, working with the Casablanca-Settat
regional brigade, carried out an operation in the Roudani area of Casablanca
against families accused of drug trafficking and seized several types of drugs.
[@1] The operation targeted the (Z) family and the (Ould H) families, described
as well known for involvement in drug trafficking. [@1] Officers detained five
people during the operation, including members of the (Z) and (Ould H) families.
[@1] Those detained were being investigated at the National Brigade headquarters
on Roudani Street to establish details of their alleged trafficking and
networks. [@1]

The drugs seized in the operation included boufa, qarqubi and hashish. [@1] The
operation was based on precise information provided by territorial surveillance
officers. [@1]

The operation was described as part of a wide-scale cleansing campaign in the
Roudani area aimed at drugs and their dealers. [@1] Police said continuing
search efforts were aimed at identifying other places and people involved in the
drug-trafficking activity. [@1]
