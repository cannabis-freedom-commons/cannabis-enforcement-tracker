A 56-year-old truck driver was arrested in Middelburg, Mpumalanga, for allegedly
trying to smuggle dagga into South Africa from eSwatini. [@1] Police said he was
intercepted in Middelburg on Tuesday. [@1]

Mpumalanga police spokesperson Brigadier Selvy Mohlala said officers had
received information that a coal-loaded truck was carrying bags of dagga
underneath the coal, and that the load was coming from eSwatini. [@1] Officers
stopped the truck described in the tip-off and searched it with the help of a
Bidvest Security sniffer dog, and the driver was asked to offload it. [@1]
Police recovered 104 bags of dagga, each with names written on it. [@1]

Mohlala said the dagga weighed 500 kg and had an estimated street value of R1
million. [@1] The driver was arrested and charged with dagga trafficking. [@1]
He was due to appear in the Middelburg Magistrate's Court on Thursday. [@1]

Mpumalanga provincial commissioner Lieutenant General Semakaleng Manamela
welcomed the arrest and thanked community members for the information. [@1] She
said in a statement that dagga traffickers' time had come to an end, especially
on Mpumalanga roads. [@1]
