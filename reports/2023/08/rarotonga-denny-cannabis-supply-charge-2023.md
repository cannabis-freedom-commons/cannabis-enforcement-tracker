A new charge of supplying a Class C controlled drug was laid against Christopher
Denny when his case was called in the Criminal High Court in Avarua on 1 August
2023. [@1] Denny also faces charges of possessing cannabis leaves and possessing
utensils. [@1] Denny was arrested and charged with possessing cannabis leaves on
16 February 2023 and appeared in the Criminal Court the next day. [@1] A justice
of the peace granted him bail with conditions that he surrender his passport and
not leave the Cook Islands without the High Court's permission. [@1]

Defence lawyer Mark Short said he needed to review the evidence from the
disclosures. [@1] Chief Justice Patrick Keane said the Crown would rely on text
messages, which were being analysed by an expert from New Zealand. [@1] The
matter was adjourned to 27 October 2023. [@1]

In January 2023 Denny relaunched Cook Islands Security, a company he had founded
in 2018 to protect the tourism industry, after closing it in December 2022. [@1]
