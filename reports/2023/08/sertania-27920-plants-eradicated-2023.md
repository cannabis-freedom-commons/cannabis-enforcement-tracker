Military Police of Pernambuco uprooted and burned 27,920 cannabis plants at
Sítio Lambedor, in the rural zone of Sertânia in the state's sertão, on Tuesday
1 August 2023. [@1] Officers went to the property after residents reported that
several men were lodging there and that a truck carrying irrigation pipes had
arrived. [@1]

The Military Police said officers entered the caatinga and found the suspects
watering the plantation, and that the men drew firearms and fired at them before
fleeing into the vegetation. [@1] No arrests were reported and the suspects
remained at large when the accounts were published. [@1] Another outlet
reporting the operation said officers searched for the suspects but could not
arrest them. [@2]

Alongside the plants, officers destroyed 5,880 planting holes. [@1] On the plot
they found and seized a mobile phone, 46.7 kilograms of cannabis ready for
consumption and 2 kilograms of cannabis seed for planting. [@1] The seized
material was delivered to the Civil Police station in Sertânia. [@2]

A local account reproducing the Military Police's own release puts the number of
suspects at about six. [@3] That account says the suspects fired at the
officers, who returned fire. [@3] It names the units involved as the Military
Police's reserved-service team, NIS-1/3ºBPM and Malhas da Lei, acting on
denunciations to the 2nd Military Police Company in Sertânia. [@3]
