Two men from Parika, East Bank Essequibo, were charged on Tuesday with
possession of cannabis in separate cases. [@1] Kwame Silas, a 46-year-old
labourer, was arrested on Saturday by officers of the Leonora Police Station
with 49 grammes of cannabis. [@1] He appeared before Magistrate Zamilla
Ali-Seepaul at the Leonora Magistrate Court, pleaded not guilty and was placed
on $150,000 bail. [@1] His matter was postponed to October 23. [@1]

Andy Sam, a 21-year-old labourer of Parika Old Road, was arrested on Friday by
the same police station with 53 grammes of marijuana. [@1] He appeared before
the same magistrate, pleaded guilty and was fined $30,000 and sentenced to three
months in prison. [@1]
