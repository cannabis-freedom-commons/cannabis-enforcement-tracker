Members of the Rustenburg Flying Squad arrested two suspects aged 37 and 41 near Mooinooi
on Thursday 17 August 2023 for dealing in drugs. [@1,2] Acting on a tip-off about a
vehicle carrying drugs, the officers followed a white truck from the Brits Toll Plaza and
stopped and searched it at the Bapong Weighbridge close to Mooinooi. [@1,2]

Upon the discovery of 64 bags of dagga in the truck, the driver and passenger were
arrested immediately. [@1,2] North West police spokesperson Colonel Adele Myburgh put the
street value of the dagga at R5 million. [@1,2]

The men were expected to appear in the Brits Magistrate's Court on charges of dealing in
drugs, and one of them faced an additional charge of being in South Africa illegally. [@1,2]
Provincial police commissioner Lieutenant General Sello Kwena applauded the officers'
swift response. [@1]
