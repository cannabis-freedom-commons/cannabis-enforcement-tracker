Ofast investigators searched a well-established moving company in Martinique.
[@1] The search took place on the morning of Wednesday 16 August 2023. [@1]
Inside a container shipped from the port of Le Havre by CMA-CGM that had spent
several days at Pointe des Grives, officers found 151 kilos of cannabis resin.
[@1] The resin was hidden inside household appliances. [@1]

Officers watching transatlantic freight traffic had been alerted by suspicious
details about the cargo. [@1] After a discreet check confirmed a large quantity
of drugs was inside, police let the container travel on to the forwarder, which
had no part in the illicit shipment. [@1] No one came to the moving firm to
claim the appliances. [@1] Ofast seized the drugs for destruction and opened an
investigation to trace those who ordered the shipment. [@1]

Six days earlier, on 10 August, customs had found 610 kilos of cannabis resin in
a container at the port of Fort-de-France. [@1,3] In this case the traffickers
used the rip-off method, forcing the container to insert the drugs at the last
moment for accomplices to recover in transit. [@1,3] A later RCI dossier confirms
that Ofast found 151 kilos of cannabis resin hidden in household appliances in a
container from Le Havre using the rip-off method. [@2]
