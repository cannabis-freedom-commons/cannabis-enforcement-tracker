On 5 October 2022 detectives from the Drug and Serious Crime Group, with Bundaberg, Wide Bay and Childers police, raided a property beside the Bruce Highway at Isis Central and found 51 greenhouses, each about 80 metres long, holding 46,894 cannabis plants and seedlings with generators and hydroponic equipment. [@1,2] Police arrested twelve men and two women, aged 25 to 52, at the site, each charged with producing and possessing cannabis and possessing items used in production, and said all the plants were destroyed and the equipment dismantled. [@1]

In the Bundaberg District Court on 31 August 2023, Duy Khanh Bui, 30, and Simon Tran, 51, pleaded guilty to producing a dangerous drug and were each sentenced to three years in prison with convictions recorded and 330 days already served, making them eligible for parole in October and December 2023. [@2] The court heard the two men were recruited with twelve others from across Australia, repaired the house and built the greenhouses, and were paid a wage of $3,000 a month with no share in the crop's profit. [@2] The crop before the court consisted of 43,246 potted plants weighing 14,339 kg plus 16 kg of seedlings, which the prosecutor said could be worth more than $100 million at full maturation. [@2]
