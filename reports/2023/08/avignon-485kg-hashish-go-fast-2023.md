Two men were arrested in Avignon in the night of Monday to Tuesday, 7 to 8
August 2023, with 485 kg of cannabis in the boots of two cars coming fast from
Spain. [@4] Officers of the BRI, a specialist police intervention unit, stopped
them during a go-fast from Spain, after one of the cars was found in a hotel car
park in Avignon. [@4] The Montpellier Brigade de recherche et d'intervention
reported intercepting the go-fast on the A9 motorway that same night. [@1]
Investigators from the Lille judicial police had followed the two vehicles from
the Spanish border while watching a suspected trafficking network for several
months. [@1] The cars were a Renault Mégane and an Audi A7, spotted after they
crossed from Spain, and the first was located in a hotel car park in Avignon.
[@2] When BRI officers arrived, one suspect tried to flee and mimed drawing a
weapon, and an officer fired once without injuring anyone. [@2]

The vehicles held several suitcases of cannabis weighing 485 kg, which police
valued at 4 million euros for resale. [@1] The load was 13 Moroccan suitcases
carrying 485 kg of cannabis resin. [@2] Interior Minister Gérald Darmanin
welcomed the arrest of two people and the seizure of 485 kg of cannabis, which
he valued at nearly 4 million euros. [@2] The same night's interception in the
Vaucluse yielded 485 kilos of cannabis resin estimated at four million euros.
[@3]

After custody the two men were handed to the investigating service, indicted for
drug trafficking and imprisoned. [@1] Officers found thirteen Moroccan suitcases
of cannabis in the cars, and the two men were indicted at the end of that week.
[@4]
