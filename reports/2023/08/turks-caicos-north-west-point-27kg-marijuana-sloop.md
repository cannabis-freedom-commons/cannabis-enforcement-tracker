Officers of the Royal Turks and Caicos Islands Police Force Marine Unit and the
Customs Department seized marijuana with an estimated street value of US$500,000
from an illegal migrant vessel off North West Point, Providenciales. [@1] At
about 10:10 p.m. on Tuesday 29 August 2023, Maritime Operations personnel
reported they were pursuing an illegal vessel carrying migrants in the vicinity
of North West Point, Providenciales. [@1] The vessel failed to comply with law
enforcement instructions and ran aground, and several of the migrants jumped
into the water and ran off. [@1] Marine Unit officers removed one man and a
female minor from the vessel during rescue efforts. [@1] A joint operation
remained under way to apprehend the migrants who had fled. [@1] Marine officers
retrieved 52 packages floating in the water near the grounded vessel. [@1] A
subsequent search of the vessel by Customs officials found 61 more similar
packages. [@1]

The Collector of Customs, Linda Malcolm, said the Customs Enforcement Unit found
27 kilograms of cannabis hidden on board the vessel when it was searched at the
commercial dock on Providenciales. [@1] No arrest had been made, as there were
no occupants on board when the abandoned vessel was found. [@1] The drugs were
secured and handed over to the police, and investigations were continuing. [@1]

Magnetic Media reported that the police released the find on 5 September 2023
and that the two-day search produced 113 packages in total. [@2] Acting
Commissioner of Police Rodney Adams credited the Customs Department, the Border
Force, the TCI Regiment and the Immigration Task Force with assisting the
operation. [@1]
