Samoa Police arrested five suspects and seized 5,000 marijuana plants during a
drug raid at Faleatiu in August 2023. [@1] Officers searching the location also
found marijuana seeds and unregistered firearms. [@1] The operation was one of
the police service's major raids at Faleatiu village. [@2] Police uprooted the
marijuana plants and brought them to main headquarters following the raid. [@2]
Police also confiscated unregistered guns and other materials alongside the
marijuana plants. [@2]

Police estimated the street value of the seized plants to be worth 250,000 talā.
[@1] The Samoa Police, Prisons, and Corrections Services (SPPCS) carried out the
search after the village raised concerns about ongoing issues involving
narcotics. [@2] Authorities described the search as successful, saying it
resulted in the seizure of the plants, seeds and firearms and the arrest of the
five suspects. [@1]

The SPPCS said it would continue to combat drug-related activities in Samoa
because their use can have severe medical and criminal consequences. [@1] Police
conveyed sincere gratitude to the public for ongoing assistance and support in
ensuring safety for all. [@1] Officials urged residents to come forward with any
information about drug-related activities in their community and inform police
immediately or contact a nearby station. [@1]
