An investigation by the General Directorate of Customs (DGA) into a criminal
group moving narcotics toward the Buenos Aires metropolitan area led to the
seizure of 1,700 kg of marijuana hidden in a truck carrying eucalyptus wood.
[@1] The truck was intercepted at night at the El Arco post on National Route
12 in Misiones Province by the National Gendarmerie, with a sniffer dog
confirming narcotics hidden in the timber load. [@1]

The search found 1,150 rectangular bricks of marijuana and 76 plastic packages
of cannabis flowers. [@1] The driver was arrested by order of federal judge
Federico Villena of Lomas de Zamora, and documentation and cell phones were
also seized. [@1]
