On 15 August 2023 Hong Kong Customs inspected a seaborne consignment declared as chickpeas
arriving from Canada at the Kwai Chung Customhouse Cargo Examination Compound and found about
120 kilograms of suspected cannabis buds concealed in ten carton boxes, with an estimated
market value of about HK$28 million. [@1,2]

After follow-up investigation and a controlled delivery, officers arrested two local men aged
31 and 46 in Tin Shui Wai on 17 August for suspected connection with the case. [@1,2] Both
were jointly held on one count of attempting to traffic in a dangerous drug and were to appear
at Tuen Mun Magistrates' Courts on 19 August. [@1,2] Customs notes that trafficking in a
dangerous drug carries a maximum penalty of a HK$5 million fine and life imprisonment. [@1]
