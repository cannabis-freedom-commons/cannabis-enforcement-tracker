Officers of the anti-drug cell of the national gendarmerie in Bouaké arrested a
woman in the Dar Es Salam quarter of Bouaké on Sunday 20 August 2023 and seized
70 blocks of cannabis. [@1] She was caught in the act of delivering drugs she
had hidden in second-hand clothing, known in Côte d'Ivoire as friperie. [@1] The
gendarmerie said the seller was detained as she was making the delivery, with
the cannabis concealed in sacks of second-hand clothes. [@1]

Abidjan.net reported the seizure as coming from official sources, and the
gendarmerie confirmed the account through its own statement. [@1]
