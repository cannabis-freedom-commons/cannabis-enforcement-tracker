Military Police and Federal Police officers destroyed and burned a cannabis
plantation in the rural zone of Betânia, in Pernambuco's Sertão, on Thursday 17
August 2023, in an operation the police named Operação Polígono IV. [@1] The
plantation was at Sítio Angico, which officers reached after pushing through the
caatinga, the dry scrubland of the semi-arid north-east. [@2]

They found a cultivated plot with 416 planting holes holding 3,130 cannabis
plants. [@1] The officers also found 5 kg of the drug ready for consumption, all
of which was burned at the site except a small sample. [@2]

The operation was carried out by the Interior Policing Specialised Battalion,
known by its Portuguese initials BEPI, together with the Federal Police. [@3]
The Military Police said the operation was one of a series seeking cannabis
plantations in the Pernambuco Sertão, with Angico its target this time. [@1] The
police accounts name no one arrested and no charge or court step in the case.
[@2]
