Erongo police commander commissioner Nikolaus Kupembona announced the seizure on
Friday as a breakthrough in anti-drug operations in the region. [@1] Police
seized more than 30kg of cannabis with a street value of over N$1,5 million from
seven separate suspects. [@1] The seven suspects have appeared in court, while
others await their court appearances, according to the commissioner. [@1]

The haul included cannabis seed intended for cultivation in Namibia. [@1]
Kupembona said the amount of seed was enough to plant a cannabis farm. [@1] He
did not disclose how the drugs reached Swakopmund and Walvis Bay. [@1]

Kupembona credited effective policing and community tip-offs for the results.
[@1]

NBC reported seven Namibian nationals in custody after separate arrests. [@2]

Erongo's drug squad raided a house in Kuisebmond, Walvis Bay, and found cannabis
valued at N$1.3 million. [@2] In Swakopmund, a 31-year-old man was accused of
receiving a parcel from Germany containing cannabis seeds worth N$97 000 and
appeared in the Swakopmund Magistrates Court. [@2] An undercover operation in
Walvis Bay led to the arrest of a 25-year-old man who was sentenced to three
years' direct imprisonment for selling cannabis valued at about N$60,000. [@2]
Four more suspects arrested in two separate cases were reportedly found with
cannabis worth N$27,000. [@2]

Kupembona said minors were being used as conduits for drug distribution while
young adults dealt drugs for income. [@2]
