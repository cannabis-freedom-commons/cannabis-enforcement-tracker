Border Force officers found 250 kilograms of cannabis in a shipping container at Southampton Docks on 26 July 2023, hidden in a consignment of imported metal tool chests and cabinets. [@1] The National Crime Agency (NCA) opened an investigation into the failed importation. [@1] On Tuesday 15 August, NCA officers arrested a man in his 30s from Grays in Essex in connection with the seizure; he was interviewed and released pending further enquiries. [@1]
