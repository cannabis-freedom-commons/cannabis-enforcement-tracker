Customs officers at Fort-de-France found 610 kg of cannabis resin in several
bags hidden inside a container on the morning of Thursday 10 August 2023. [@1]
The Fort-de-France customs brigade made the find during container checks at the
Pointe des Grives port terminal. [@1] The container had come from Le Havre. [@1]
The 610 kg haul was the largest cannabis resin seizure of the year, and possibly
the largest ever made at Fort-de-France. [@1]

The seizure resulted from investigations by the DNRED, France's national customs
intelligence and investigation directorate, rather than a chance find. [@1]
Investigators believe the resin was loaded at Le Havre with the rip-off
technique, in which traffickers put drugs into a container of legal goods after
it has been closed, for accomplices at the arrival port to recover them. [@1]

The resin was handed to the OFAST anti-narcotics office and was to be destroyed,
and an investigation was opened to identify those who ordered and were to
receive it. [@1] Anti-drug services have recorded a resurgence of trafficking
between French ports, notably Le Havre and Fort-de-France. [@1]
