Kalinga police recovered P5.88 million worth of marijuana, already
semi-processed into brick form, along the national highway at Sitio Jangayoy,
Barangay Dangoy, in Lubuagan town on Monday. [@1,2]

The 2nd Kalinga Provincial Mobile Force Company had received a tip that an
unidentified male rider was moving marijuana bricks from Tinglayan toward
Tabuk City, and a second tip that a rider had unloaded baggage beside the road
at Jangayoy. [@1,2]

Officers searched along the national road and found an army duffle bag partly
covered with leaves and branches holding 49 bricks of dried marijuana leaves
weighing about 49,000 grams. [@1,2]

The bricks were inventoried on site in the presence of a Dangoy village
official, brought to the Lubuagan police station and then to the Kalinga
provincial forensic unit for examination, while a manhunt for the courier
continued. [@1,2]
