Three men were sentenced at Laganside Crown Court over a large-scale cannabis farm
uncovered in the Carrickfergus area, in a hearing reported on 17 November 2023. [@1]
Edward Cambridge, 52, and Samuel Harrison, 64, pleaded guilty to cultivating cannabis,
possessing a class B controlled drug and being concerned in its supply. [@1] They were
sentenced to 18 months in custody with 18 months on licence. [@1]

Connor Harrison, 23, pleaded guilty to offering to supply a class B controlled drug and
was given six months in prison suspended for 18 months. [@1] Police had searched
premises in the Carrickfergus area and uncovered more than 100 cannabis plants with a
cultivation system. [@1] Detective Inspector Corrigan said the sentences showed police
and partner organisations remained committed to disrupting drug supply, and asked the
public to pass information to police or the Crimestoppers charity. [@1]
