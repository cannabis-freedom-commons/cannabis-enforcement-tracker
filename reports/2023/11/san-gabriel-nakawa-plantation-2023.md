The Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency's Ilocos office and the La Union Police Provincial Office
raided a 1,950-square-metre marijuana plantation at Sitio Nakawa in Lon-oy village, San Gabriel,
on the morning of Tuesday 7 November 2023. [@1,2] Officers uprooted about 10,500 seedlings and
8,500 fully grown plants, which PDEA Ilocos director Joel Plaza said were all burned on site
during the joint operation. [@1,2]

PDEA valued the destroyed crop at P2,120,000. [@1,2] No suspect was arrested, though regional
information officer Maripe De Guzman said a suspect had been identified and was still being
verified. [@1] Charges were to be filed under Section 16 of the Comprehensive Dangerous Drugs
Act. [@1]
