Central Division officers went to a Southern Main Road, Caroni, address, where a
warrant was executed on the home of a 21-year-old described as a priority
offender. [@1] The exercise ran from 3 pm to 6 pm and involved the Central
Division Task Force, Area South and the Caroni K9 Unit, with attention paid to
drug blocks in the Couva and Freeport area. [@1]

A search of the man's bedroom found four rectangular packages wrapped in red and
brown plastic, each holding marijuana. [@1] The packages weighed 2.1 kg and
police put their street value at TT$65,000. [@1] The man was arrested and the
packages were seized and taken to the Couva Police Station. [@1]

The report says the exercise was coordinated by Snr Supt Simon, Supt
Montrichard, Supt Gyan, ASP Ablacksingh and Insp Stewart, and supervised by Sgt
Persad. [@1]
