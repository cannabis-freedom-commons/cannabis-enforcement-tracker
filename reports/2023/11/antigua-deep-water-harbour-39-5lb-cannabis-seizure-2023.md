Officers from the Narcotics Department and the Customs Enforcement Unit carried
out a significant drug interdiction operation at Deep-Water Harbour on Thursday
at about 10:30 a.m. [@1] The operation resulted in the confiscation of a
substantial quantity of cannabis, totalling thirty-nine and a half (39.5)
pounds, about 17.9 kilograms. [@1] The estimated street value of the seized
cannabis is $316,000. [@1] Antigua News Room published the same account on the
same day, giving the same weight of 39.5 pounds and a street value of
$316,000.00ECC, Eastern Caribbean dollars. [@2]

As of the latest update, no arrests have been made in connection with the
operation. [@1] At present, the confiscated substance is undergoing a detailed
investigation to uncover additional information about its origin and intended
destination. [@1] Ongoing investigations are actively in progress. [@1] Further
updates are anticipated as the investigation unfolds. [@1]
