Western Cape Provincial Traffic Officers stopped a heavy motor vehicle for a routine
inspection on the N1 between Laingsburg and Leeu-Gamka on Saturday 25 November 2023. [@1,2,3]
While inspecting the truck's two trailers, the officers found 40 bags of compressed
cannabis weighing two tonnes, with an estimated value of R16 million. [@1,2,3]

The driver was arrested and detained at the Laingsburg police station. [@1,2] The provincial
mobility minister, Ricardo MacKenzie, praised the officers and urged road users to support
their work heading into the festive season. [@1,2,3] The police oversight minister, Reagen
Allen, said intercepting the shipments kept the drugs out of communities. [@1,2]

The bust came a week after traffic officers and SAPS Crime Intelligence intercepted a
R7 million dagga shipment on the N1 at Worcester after a tip-off, a separate event left
out here. [@1,2,3]
