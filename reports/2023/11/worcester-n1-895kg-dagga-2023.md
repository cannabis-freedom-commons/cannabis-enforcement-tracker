Members attached to the Worcester SAPS acted on information received from the public and
pulled over a light-duty vehicle suspected of carrying a large consignment of dagga in its
trailer. [@1] Police searched the vehicle and seized 895 kg of dagga concealed in five
plastic containers in the trailer, which they valued at R7.1 million. [@1]

The two occupants, aged 34 and 36, were arrested and detained on a charge of dealing in
drugs. [@1] Provincial police spokesperson Lieutenant Colonel Malcolm Pojie described the
stop, and later coverage confirmed it as a joint operation by Provincial Traffic Officers
and SAPS Crime Intelligence on the N1 at Worcester made possible by a tip-off. [@1,2,3]

A week later, traffic officers stopped a truck on the N1 near Laingsburg and seized a
further two tonnes of cannabis, a separate event left out here. [@2,3]
