Before dawn on 24 November 2023, gendarmes saw two cars stopped on the shoulder
of Ruta Nacional 105 near the Garupá roundabout, in Misiones, while three people
unloaded bundles from them. [@1] The officers ordered them to halt and arrested
the three, who had tried to flee. [@1] In front of witnesses they counted 72
bundles, whose contents tested positive for cannabis sativa and weighed 36
kilograms 25 grams. [@1] The three were held incommunicado, and the magistrates
handling each case ordered the drugs seized. [@1]

In another procedure, officers of the El Arco road section, part of Escuadrón 50
Posadas, stopped a long-distance bus travelling from Posadas to Formosa at
kilometre 1,327 of Ruta Nacional 12. [@1] In the baggage hold they smelled
marijuana and found a plastic package holding 80 grams of cannabis sativa in a
bag. [@1]

At the Santa Ana toll, Escuadrón 11 San Ignacio stopped a car carrying two
people, and after a drug dog marked one passenger's pockets the gendarmes found
105 grams of cocaine in a field test. [@1] The magistrates in each case ordered
the drugs and all items of interest to the case seized. [@1] La Gaceta, a
Tucumán newspaper, carried the same Gendarmería account, reporting 72 bundles
with a positive reaction for cannabis sativa and a weight of 36 kilograms 25
grams. [@2]
