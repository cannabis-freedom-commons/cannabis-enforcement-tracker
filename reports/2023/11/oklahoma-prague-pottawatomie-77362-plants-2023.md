Agents of the Attorney General's Organized Crime Task Force (OCTF) and Oklahoma
Medical Marijuana Authority (OMMA) inspectors served search warrants on 16
November 2023 at six medical marijuana grow operations near Prague, in
Pottawatomie County. [@1,2]

The sweep yielded 77,362 untraceable and untagged marijuana plants, nearly
2,000 pounds (about 907 kg) of untraceable and untagged harvested marijuana,
and several firearms. [@1,2] The District 23 Drug Task Force assisted in the
operation. [@1]

Attorney General Gentner Drummond said the task force was dedicated to rooting
out illegal marijuana operations, warning cartels and crime syndicates that
endanger public safety. [@1] The seizure came only a week after the task force
seized more than 36 tons of black-market marijuana in a single day elsewhere in
the state. [@1]
