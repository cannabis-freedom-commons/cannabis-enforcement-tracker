Three men admitted drug offences at Laganside Crown Court over a large-scale
cannabis farm uncovered in the Carrickfergus area. [@1]

Edward Cambridge, 52, and Samuel Harrison, 64, pleaded guilty to cultivating
cannabis, possessing a class B controlled drug and being concerned in the
supply of a class B controlled drug. [@1] They were sentenced to a total of
18 months in custody and 18 months on licence. [@1]

Connor Harrison, 23, pleaded guilty to offering to supply a class B
controlled drug and being concerned in an offer to supply such a drug. [@1]
He was sentenced to six months in prison, suspended for 18 months. [@1]

Police had searched premises in the Carrickfergus area and found more than
100 cannabis plants and a cultivation system. [@1] Welcoming the sentences,
Detective Inspector Corrigan said information from the public was crucial to
tackling drugs and removing suppliers from the streets. [@1]
