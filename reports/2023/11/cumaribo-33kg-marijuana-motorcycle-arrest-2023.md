Colombian police and army troops arrested a 22-year-old man riding a motorcycle
over Puente Taro Taro, in the Alcaraván neighbourhood of Cumaribo, Vichada. [@1]
Officers were checking people and vehicles at a checkpoint in the Alcaraván
neighbourhood when they stopped and searched him. [@1] The Army's own statement
put the arrest at Puente Taro Taro, in the Alcaraván neighbourhood of Cumaribo
municipality. [@2]

He was carrying 39 bricks of marijuana weighing 33 kilograms in a sack. [@1]
Judicial police testing identified the load as creepy marijuana, a common name
in Colombia for pressed, high-grade cannabis. [@2] Police valued the load at
more than 100 million pesos. [@1]

Officers seized his motorcycle and a mobile phone. [@1] The man and the seized
items were handed to the competent authority, and he faces charges of
possessing, trafficking or manufacturing narcotics while a judge decides his
legal situation. [@1]

El Morichal reported the operation as a joint action by the Army with the
Aerospace Force and the National Police. [@3]
