An 18-year-old motorist appeared in the High Court at Avarua on Friday 24
November 2023 charged with careless driving, possession of cannabis and
possession of a bong. [@1] The court heard that on 3 June 2023 he was driving
when he suffered an epileptic fit after feeling dizzy, resulting in a car crash.
[@1] Following the crash, police searched his vehicle and found cannabis and a
bong. [@1]

Police said the defendant entered an early guilty plea and admitted that the
cannabis and bong were for his personal use. [@1] Defence lawyer Mona Ioane
explained that his client suffered from a mental health issue and that the crash
resulted from an epileptic seizure. [@1] Ioane told the court his client
accepted full responsibility for the incident. [@1]

Justice Christine Grice accepted that the careless driving was caused by the
medical episode and asked whether a discharge would be appropriate. [@1] Police
did not oppose a discharge on the traffic offence. [@1] Justice Grice emphasised
that the drug offences were serious matters. [@1] The judge convicted and fined
him $500 for possessing cannabis and $200 for possessing a bong, plus $50 in
court costs, and discharged him without conviction for careless driving. [@1]
