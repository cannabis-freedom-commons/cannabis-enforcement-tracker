Argentine Naval Prefecture officers detained one suspected drug trafficker in
Puerto Rico, Misiones, during border patrols to prevent crime. [@1] Officers
watching Puerto Río y Sol saw a group handling bundles at kilometre 1739 of the
Paraná River, who fled to a house 100 metres from the shore when they noticed
the maritime authority. [@1] Officers went to the house and detained one of the
suspects. [@1]

After searching the house, officers seized more than 61 kilograms of marijuana
packed in 60 vacuum-sealed packages, plus more than 3,000 seeds of the same
drug. [@1] The detained 40-year-old man also had 25 grams of marijuana buds
among his belongings. [@1] In the same place officers seized electronics and
clothing brought into the country through an unauthorised crossing, including
routers, printers, video cards, notebooks, processors, ink cartridges and
cellphones. [@1]

The Federal Court of Oberá, Misiones, under Dr Marcos Gallandat Luzuriaga, took
charge of the case and ordered seizure of everything found. [@1] The authorities
put the market value of everything seized at more than 70 million pesos. [@1]
