On 28 November 2023, gendarmes seized 78 zamal plants and 1,400 grams of herbal
cannabis in the heights of Sainte-Marie. [@1] Officers on patrol found several
zamal plants in a private garden near abandoned vehicle carcasses in
Sainte-Marie. [@1]

The seized cannabis belonged to a 41-year-old man. [@1] The man was heard in a
free interview without detention in connection with the case. [@1] He is to face
a penal decision in the case. [@1]
