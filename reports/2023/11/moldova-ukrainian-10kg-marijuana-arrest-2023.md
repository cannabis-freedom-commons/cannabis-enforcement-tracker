A 19-year-old Ukrainian citizen described as an active member of a group
smuggling drugs into Moldova and selling them through online stores was arrested
for 30 days. [@2] Officers of the Anti-Drug Directorate and the analytical
investigations directorate of the National Inspectorate of Investigations,
together with criminal investigators of the Riscani Police Inspectorate and
prosecutors of the Chisinau Prosecutor's Office's Riscani branch, documented his
activity. [@2] Investigators established that he had arrived in Moldova in 2022
and rented an apartment that he adapted as a drug warehouse. [@2]

He received various types of drugs from Ukraine, repackaged them into smaller
doses of 100 to 1,000 grams and placed them in hiding places where other dealers
collected them for sale through smaller online stores. [@2] After he was caught
in the act, searches of the car he was driving and his rented home recovered
small-scale scales and mobile phones used in the offence, along with 10 kg of
marijuana buds, 1.5 kg of mephedrone, 700 grams of PVP salt and more than 300
Ecstasy tablets. [@2] The same search is also reported as finding 10 kg of
marijuana with 1.5 kg of mephedrone, 700 g of salt and more than 300 Ecstasy
tablets in the apartment. [@1]

Police said the drugs taken out of circulation were worth more than 3.5 million
lei on the black market. [@2] The offence carries 7 to 15 years' imprisonment
under the law in force. [@2]
