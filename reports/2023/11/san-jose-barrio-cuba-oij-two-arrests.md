Agents of Costa Rica's Judicial Investigation Agency (OIJ) arrested two men in
Barrio Cuba, San José, on the morning of 23 November 2023. [@1] One detainee was
24 years old and the other 34. [@1] The arrests came as OIJ officers raided two
structures in Barrio Cuba, San José. [@1] The OIJ was supported by the municipal
police of San José and of Goicoechea. [@1]

The raids and arrests followed several months of investigation by agents of the
OIJ's drug trafficking section, the Sección de Estupefacientes. [@1] During the
raids officers seized doses of cocaine, marijuana and ketamine. [@1] Officers
also seized firearm ammunition and cash that could be the proceeds of drug
sales, according to the OIJ. [@1]

Both men were held as suspects of an offence under Costa Rica's Psychotropic
Substances Law (Ley de Psicotrópicos) for selling drugs. [@1] The detainees were
placed under the orders of the Public Ministry, which was to determine their
legal situation. [@1]
