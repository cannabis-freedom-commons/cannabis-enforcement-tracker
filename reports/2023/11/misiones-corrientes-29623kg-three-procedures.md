Argentina's National Gendarmerie reported three separate marijuana seizures in
Misiones and Corrientes totalling more than 29 kilos. [@1] The Squadron 47 check
described as happening yesterday stopped a long-distance bus at kilometre 1,274
of National Route 12, so 2 November 2023 is used as the incident date. [@1]

Two passengers stood out for wearing too many warm clothes and acting nervous,
so officers asked both to get off the bus. [@1] In front of witnesses, officers
found packages strapped to their bodies that smelled strongly of marijuana. [@1]
Forensic staff field-tested the samples with Narcotest, confirming cannabis
sativa weighing 4 kilos 94 grams. [@1] Both bus passengers were detained. [@1]

A Squadron 11 San Ignacio foot patrol on trails in the coastal sector of Puerto
Rico saw several people moving through the brush with bundles on their backs.
[@1] When ordered to halt, they dropped the load and fled into the adjoining
wooded hills. [@1] A search then found two bundles holding 16 kilos 405 grams of
marijuana, seized on the orders of the Oberá federal court and prosecutor. [@1]
Squadron 50 Posadas officers, acting on a federal court order, opened a courier
parcel and found 16 plastic packages of cannabis sativa weighing 9 kilos 124
grams. [@1]

The responsible magistrates in each case ordered the detention of those
involved, formal records and seizure of the drug for infringing Law 23.737 on
narcotics. [@1]
