An Abidjan port police unit went to Grand-Bassam on 27 November 2023 to check a
tip about cannabis blocks kept in an unfinished house. [@2] The officers found
59 cannabis blocks weighing 590 kg, seized them and placed them under seal. [@2]
The Ivorian national police directorate announced the seizure in an information
note seen by Abidjan.net. [@1] Grand-Bassam is a seaside town south of Abidjan.
[@1]

An investigation was opened to establish where the merchandise came from and who
was involved. [@2]

Abidjan.net recalled that police had also seized 320 cannabis blocks at Azuretti
in Grand-Bassam on 9 December 2020. [@1] Abidjan.net presented the operations as
part of the fight against drugs in Côte d'Ivoire. [@1]
