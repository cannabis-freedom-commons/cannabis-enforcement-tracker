Police in Liège arrested a driver and her passenger on the evening of 21
November 2023 after a patrol saw their car speeding on the boulevard Fosse
Crahay in Liège. [@1] The two occupants, both already known to police, were
carrying 70 packets holding a total of 276 grams of cannabis in lunch boxes,
according to the Liège police zone. [@1] Both suspects were arrested and
officers searched a home with a dog trained to detect drugs and money. [@1]

The search found 25.3 kilograms of cannabis and 783 grams of cocaine, which
police valued together at 39,150 euros. [@1] Officers also found 37,780 euros in
various notes, a revolver and ammunition. [@1] Police seized a precision scale,
a banknote counter, several mobile phones and equipment for packaging the drugs.
[@1] The home also held more than 600 bottles of alcohol, according to La DH,
which played no part in the cannabis counts. [@2]

Because of the scale of the discovery, the judicial brigade of the Liège police
took over the case under the supervision of the Liège prosecutor's office. [@1]
The Liège criminal brigade was still investigating, and police called the
operation a remarkable success in the fight against drug trafficking. [@2]
TodayInLiege reproduced the police statement and the inventory of bottles
officers found, including 420 of Desperados and 24 of Ricard. [@3]
