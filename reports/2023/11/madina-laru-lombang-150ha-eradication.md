North Sumatra police chief Irjen Pol Agung Setya Imam Effendi led officers to 150 hectares of
cannabis spread over 18 sites in the hills of Laru Lombang village, Tambangan, on Saturday
11 November 2023. [@1,2] The force found the fields with satellite imagery verified by drone
instead of foot patrols, then flew in by helicopter with the Kodam I/Bukit Barisan commander,
Mayjen TNI Mochammad Hasan, to lead the destruction. [@1,2]

Officers walked six to ten hours to reach the hill and mountain sites, which police called the
largest cannabis field found by officers in the anti-narcotics war. [@1,2] A joint TNI-Polri team
secured field owners and couriers carrying harvested cannabis for sale during the operation. [@2]

Police said the case grew from the earlier arrest of three couriers from Padang, aged 16, 18 and
23, caught with 15 kg of cannabis on a road in East Panyabungan. [@1] The three allegedly acted on
the orders of an inmate known as ZR alias Kijok in a Padang prison, who was then taken to North
Sumatra police for questioning. [@1,2] Police said the Laru Lombang harvest was sold in Padang
under the inmate's control. [@2]
