Moroccan police seized 4.635 tonnes of cannabis in Casablanca on Saturday,
acting on the official MAP news agency's account carried by both reports.
[@1,2]

The drugs were concealed in 123 large packages ready to be exported by sea
when officers intercepted them in Morocco's largest city. [@1,2]

Two suspects were arrested over alleged links to a drug trafficking network,
and an investigation was opened into their possible role in a criminal network
moving drugs and psychotropic substances by sea. [@1,2]
