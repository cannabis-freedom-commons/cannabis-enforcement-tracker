Police found three tonnes of cannabis at premises in Newtownards, County Down, in a joint operation by the Police Service of Northern Ireland and HM Revenue and Customs officers. [@1] Police put the estimated street value at £18 million and called it the largest cannabis seizure ever made in Northern Ireland. [@1] Assistant Chief Constable Peter Sheridan praised the officers involved for striking a major blow against drug gangs. [@1]

## Charges

Two men were arrested after the seizure on Tuesday 10 October. [@2] Stephen James McGivern, 31, of Oak Crescent, Sunderland, and Martin Kerfoot, 39, of Seacliff Road, Bangor, appeared at Newtownards Magistrates Court, sitting in Downpatrick, charged with possession of the Class C drug with intent to supply. [@2] The pair were remanded in custody until 27 October. [@2]
