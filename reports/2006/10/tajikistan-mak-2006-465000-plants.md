Tajikistan's Drug Control Agency seized about 1,160 kg of drugs, including more
than 405 kg of heroin, from illegal circulation in the first nine months of
2006. [@1] Officers intercepted 98 cases of illegal trafficking in narcotic
drugs and psychotropic substances in that period. [@1] The agency later broke
the 1,161-kg total down as 405 kg of heroin, 520 kg of raw opium and 235 kg of
cannabis-group drugs. [@2]

During the year's special operation Mak-2006, officers identified and destroyed
about 465,000 bushes of wild-growing cannabis. [@1] Officers also seized three
explosive devices, 914 pieces of various ammunition and 14 firearms from
criminals. [@1]

The agency said 40 joint operations with Tajik, CIS and Afghan security services
since the start of the year had seized more than a tonne of drugs, including 446
kg of heroin. [@1] Its director Rustam Nazarov said the same nine-month result
covered 98 intercepted trafficking cases, 235 kg of cannabis-group drugs and
about 465,000 destroyed wild cannabis bushes. [@2]
