Malaysia's Federal Court in Putrajaya dismissed electrician Mohd Azhar Saad's appeal against his death sentence for trafficking in almost 1 kg of cannabis thirteen years earlier. [@1,2] The decision on 17 October 2006 left the death sentence in place after the court rejected counsel Datuk K. Kumaraendran's contention that the Court of Appeal had erred in law. [@1,2]

In November 1994 the High Court had found Azhar guilty of trafficking the drug at the Bukit Kayu Hitam immigration complex in Kedah on 6 July 1993. [@1,2] The courts accepted a statement he made to a lance corporal at the time of his arrest. [@1,2] His appeal to the Court of Appeal had already been dismissed in August 2001. [@1,2]
