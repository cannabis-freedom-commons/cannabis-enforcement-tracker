Afghan police and NATO-led International Security Assistance Force (ISAF) troops seized 9.2 tonnes of hashish from a truck stopped at a checkpoint on Highway 1 near Qalat, the capital of Zabul province on the Kandahar-Kabul road. [@1,2] ISAF called it the largest seizure of its kind, and the truck's drivers and passengers were taken into custody. [@1,2]

NATO spokesman Mark Laity said tackling the drug trade was essential because it fuelled corruption and the insurgency, while noting it could not be solved by military means alone. [@2] The same roundup also netted 55 kg of opium from a car in Farah City, with its driver and a passenger detained; that opium is not counted in this report's cannabis figures. [@1,2]
