Nineteen police forces across England and Wales raided suspected indoor cannabis factories in
Operation Keymer, a coordinated crackdown running from 25 September to 5 October 2006. [@1,2]
The Association of Chief Police Officers said criminal gangs saw home growing as low-risk and
high-profit, with 200 plants turning over about £30,000 in three months, and warned of fire
and electrocution risks from electricity supplies the growers bypassed. [@1] Police asked the
public to watch for covered windows, visits at odd hours, gardening equipment left outside and
the pungent smell of growing cannabis. [@1]

Officers closed factories, arrested suspects and moved against criminal profits under the
Proceeds of Crime Act. [@1,2] Announcing the results, police said fifteen forces had destroyed
28,000 cannabis plants and 54 kilograms of prepared cannabis worth more than £2.5 million,
arrested 133 people including one wanted for murder, and seized £160,000 in cash together with
four houses, five cars and antiques. [@3]
