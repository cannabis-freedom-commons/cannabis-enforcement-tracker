Police seized two and a half tonnes of cannabis with a street value of about
10 million pounds in Studley, Warwickshire. [@1] The seizure was made in a
joint operation between Warwickshire and Greater Manchester Police on Tuesday
morning. [@1]

## One arrest, no charge reported

A 31-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of conspiracy to supply a
controlled drug. [@1] He was questioned at Leamington Spa police station and
released on police bail pending further forensic inquiries. [@1]
