The Drug Enforcement Administration’s Oakland resident office and Berkeley Police Department said
they arrested seven people on federal marijuana cultivation charges after dismantling four
sophisticated indoor grows. [@1] Berkeley officers served search warrants on 15 March 2006 at sites
in Berkeley, Oakland, Castro Valley and Brentwood, then worked with the DEA on additional searches
the next week in Oakland and Antioch. [@1]

## Seizures

Law enforcement reported seizing more than 5,800 live plants, about 117 pounds of processed
marijuana, 12 firearms including one assault rifle, and approximately $220,000 in U.S. currency.
[@1,2] Berkeley police had opened the investigation in November 2005 and linked several people and
addresses to the organization before the March raids. [@1]

## Charges

The DEA named Jonathan J. Ford of Oakland and six other men from Oakland, Castro Valley, Brentwood
and San Leandro on complaints under 21 U.S.C. §841(a)(1). [@1] The opened releases do not report
sentences or pleas.
