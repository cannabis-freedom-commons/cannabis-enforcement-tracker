Wayne County deputies found more than 6,200 marijuana plants near the intersection of Airport and Lancaster roads on the Friday before the 20 August 2006 report. [@1] The sheriff's helicopter, flying a routine patrol from its base near the Goldsboro-Wayne County Municipal Airport, spotted the plants around noon; off-duty and on-duty SWAT members, sheriff's deputies and Goldsboro police officers then walked narrow trails through brush and trees to reach the plots. [@1] The first clearing held more than 350 plants, and officers worked through seven plots to a total of more than 6,200 plants. [@1] The site, about a mile from Norwayne Middle School, included a campsite and a makeshift shack hidden by trees and a canopy where growers were believed to have stayed through the summer. [@1]

Sheriff Carey Winders valued the find at a potential 15 million dollars had the plants matured, calling it the second-largest seizure in county history. [@1] A September follow-up confirmed the August seizure and said no arrests in the county's summer marijuana cases had been made. [@2]
