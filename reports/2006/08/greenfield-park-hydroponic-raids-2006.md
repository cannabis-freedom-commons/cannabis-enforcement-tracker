Police in south-western Sydney raided two houses in Greenfield Park on the
morning of Monday 7 August 2006, seizing cannabis worth about one million
Australian dollars.[@1] Both houses stood on Smithfield Road and were raided
at about 11.30am, when officers found elaborate hydroponic set-ups.[@1]

Officers seized 360 cannabis plants along with a large quantity of processed
cannabis.[@1] Nobody was home at either house and no charges were laid, but
police said investigations were continuing.[@1] The raids were carried out by
the Wetherill Park Target Action Group as part of a crackdown on hydroponic
cannabis cultivation.[@1]
