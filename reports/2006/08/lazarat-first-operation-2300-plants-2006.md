Albanian police destroyed more than 2,300 marijuana plants in the southern village of Lazarat in what the Tirana Times described as the first great operation in the village. [@1] Officers found the plants in different parcels and abandoned houses in Lazarat, about 20 kilometres from the Kakavie checkpoint on the border with Greece, but made no arrests. [@1]

Lazarat had been an untouched area for police. [@1] Two years earlier, an Italian helicopter surveying cannabis plantations in the area had been shot at by local residents; one person was arrested much later. [@1] The paper noted that post-communist Albania had become a frequent transit point for smugglers moving people, drugs and weapons into the European Union, and that the international community had told Albania it must curb trafficking to advance toward the EU and NATO. [@1]
