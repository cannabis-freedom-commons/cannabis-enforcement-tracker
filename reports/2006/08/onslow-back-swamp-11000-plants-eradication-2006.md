County officers cut down about 11,000 marijuana plants growing in fields in the Back Swamp area near the Duplin County border on 24 August 2006, the largest seizure the Onslow County Sheriff's Department had made. [@1] A North Carolina Highway Patrol helicopter from Kinston spotted the fields off Futrell Loop Road around noon on Thursday and called in the State Bureau of Investigation and the sheriff's department. [@1] Heavy rain had flooded the swampy ground, so officers used all-terrain vehicles to reach what Sheriff Ed Brown called an extensive business-like operation rather than a casual grow. [@1]

No one was at the field, though clothing and other items left behind might help identify whoever ran it, and no arrests had been made. [@1] Brown said the haul would be a major disruption to whoever was selling it. [@1] Twenty officers spent five hours cutting the plants down with weedeaters and machetes and hauling them out. [@1]
