Heavily armed police and soldiers raided a house at 18 Bay Farm Road in Kingston at about 6
p.m. on 29 September 2006 and seized about 3,380 kg (7,436 pounds) of compressed, packaged
ganja from two houses on the premises. [@1,2] The weed sat in 170 boxes and was being readied
for export. [@1] Officers valued it at J$30.6 million on the Jamaican market and US$22.95
million in the United States. [@1]

The occupant and his associates fled before the raid; one man, said to be the occupant's
brother, was detained but had not been arrested or charged at press time. [@1,2] Police also
found pharmaceutical items including Viagra on the premises. [@1] Senior Superintendent Carlton
Williams called it an intelligence-led strike in an ongoing crackdown on the marijuana trade,
and detectives linked the bust to the drug-for-guns trade between Jamaica and Haiti. [@1,2]
The find followed a 2,677 kg (5,890-pound) seizure in eastern Kingston three days earlier,
then described as the largest single find of the decade; the Bay Farm Road haul surpassed
it. [@1,2]
