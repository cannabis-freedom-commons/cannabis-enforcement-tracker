More than 30 police officers entered Vatubalavu village in the Navosa highlands
and destroyed 9,000 marijuana plants, root crops and small huts the villagers
used as resting houses in the plantations. [@1] Radios, pots, plates, bedding
and mats inside the huts were destroyed and burnt. [@1]

Village chief Ratu Jone Rokovesa said the raid broke an agreement made in June
with police commissioner Andrew Hughes and the church: a six-month Operation
Valoma under which villagers would uproot the plants themselves with police
help at the end of the month, but officers came months early. [@1] When
villagers complained at the new police headquarters, a senior officer said the
head office had no knowledge of the directive and told them to write to Hughes,
while some villagers wanted the police to pay for the damage. [@1] Attempts to
get comment from the police were unsuccessful. [@1]
