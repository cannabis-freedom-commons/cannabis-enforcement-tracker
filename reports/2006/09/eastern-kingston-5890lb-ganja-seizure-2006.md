Narcotics police and soldiers seized 5,890 pounds of compressed ganja in
eastern Kingston on Tuesday 26 September 2006. [@1] The Gleaner put the find
at 2,677 kilograms and called it the largest single ganja seizure of the
decade to that point. [@2]

The seizure was the first of two massive finds that week. Three days later,
Operation Kingfish and narcotics agents seized a still larger 7,650-pound
haul at Bay Farm Road in Kingston, recorded in a separate report. [@1,2]
Police believed both busts were connected to the drug-for-guns trade between
Jamaica and Haiti. [@1]

Neither opened account names anyone arrested in the Tuesday operation. [@1,2]
The Gleaner described the back-to-back finds as signs of an ongoing
crackdown on the marijuana trade, with narcotics officers watching the
ganja-growing parishes for the source of the weed. [@2]
