New Zealand police arrested 500 people involved in growing and dealing cannabis during the 2005-2006 season and destroyed about 118,000 plants, about 10,000 more than the year before. [@1] Detective Senior Sergeant Scott McGill said the results showed police commitment to reducing drug availability, despite unseasonably bad weather during the operation. [@1]

Officers seized 54 firearms and recovered an estimated $100,000 in stolen property during the season. [@1] Police also shut down nine methamphetamine laboratories found alongside cannabis inquiries. [@1]

Police seized 77 kg of dried cannabis, compared with 75 kg the year before. [@1] McGill said members of the public made a major contribution by providing information that led to plots being located and offenders arrested, and planning for the next year's operation was under way. [@1]
