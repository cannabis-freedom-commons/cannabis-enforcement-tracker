Police were conducting operational measures to establish the owner of the cache
found in Hamadoni district. [@1] About 5 kg of cannabis was found on the night
from Saturday to Sunday in the cabin of a car belonging to Dushanbe resident
Yusufali Gadoev. [@1]

The largest part of the weekend seizures was made on 11 February in the border
Hamadoni district of Khatlon region. [@1] Over that weekend, law-enforcement
officers in the republic seized over 33 kg of drugs. [@1] During a patrol round
in a village of the Ittifok dehkan farm, militia officers found more than 28 kg
of drugs. [@1] Of that Hamadoni cache, over 23 kg was cannabis and about 5 kg
was heroin. [@1] From 4 to 12 February, officers recorded 18 drug-trafficking
cases in Tajikistan and seized over 50 kg of drugs in total, including 13 kg of
heroin. [@1]

The information came from UBNON, the Interior Ministry's drug-enforcement
department. [@1] Hamadoni is a border district in Khatlon region. [@1] Ittifok
is a dehkan farm, in one of whose villages the cache was found during the
patrol. [@1] Only the separately stated cannabis weights are counted as cannabis
here, and the combined drug totals and heroin weights are left out. [@1]
