Officers recorded 15 drug-trafficking cases in Tajikistan during the week
reported on 20 February 2006. [@1] Police took about 40 kg of hashish and
marijuana out of circulation that week. [@1]

The combined weekly haul of drugs was about 145 kg, of which more than 72 kg was
heroin. [@1] A further 32 kg of the haul was opium. [@1]

The figures came from UBNON, the drug-enforcement department of Tajikistan's
Interior Ministry. [@1] From the start of 2006, officers in Tajikistan had
seized 526 kg of drugs, 339 kg of it heroin. [@1]
