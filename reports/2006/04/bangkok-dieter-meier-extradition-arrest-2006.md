Thai immigration police announced on 12 April 2006 that they had arrested German national Dieter Meier in Bangkok on narcotics charges. [@1] A German court had issued arrest warrants for Meier and seven accomplices for smuggling marijuana 351 times between 1997 and 2005, with the cannabis weighing 6,661 kilograms and valued at 221 million baht (about US$5.5 million). [@1]

Immigration Bureau commissioner Suwat Thumrongsiskul told a press conference that Meier had fled to Thailand to escape prosecution and married a Thai woman, but Thai police tracked him down. [@1] He said Meier would be deported to Germany. [@1]
