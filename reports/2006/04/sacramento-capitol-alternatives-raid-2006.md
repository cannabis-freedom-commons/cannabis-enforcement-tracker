DEA agents with the active participation of the Sacramento Police Department raided the Capitol Alternatives dispensary at 2020 16th Street in Sacramento on the morning of 19 April 2006. [@1] Earlier the same morning, the DEA, FBI and Sacramento Sheriff had raided the home of one of the dispensary's operators, seizing paperwork and medicine without making an arrest. [@1] Officers held everyone inside the dispensary while supporters gathered to protest outside. [@1]

## No charges, club reopened

No charges were made over the raid, and the club reopened the next day at another location. [@2] Like other California pot shops of the time, the dispensary had to operate as a collective or cooperative of medicinal cannabis patients under state law even though federal law treated possessing or selling marijuana as a crime. [@2] Advocates called the enforcement action a direct attack on patients and providers of medical marijuana. [@1]
