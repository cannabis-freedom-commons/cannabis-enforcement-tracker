Tajik drug police arrested Muhammad Said Amriddin, a 35-year-old citizen of
Afghanistan, in Dushanbe at the end of the week before 17 April 2006 on
suspicion of involvement in drug trafficking. [@1] Officers of the Interior
Ministry department for combating drug trafficking, the drug police, detained
him in Dushanbe's Ismoili Somoni district with 1.6 kilograms of hashish. [@1]

A search of the apartment Amriddin was renting found four kilograms of cannabis,
the drug police said. [@1] Police said the flat belonged to Zoir Beknazarov, and
that he and his brother Tolibjon intended to sell the drugs in Dushanbe. [@1]
The Beknazarov brothers were wanted by police at the time of the report. [@1]
