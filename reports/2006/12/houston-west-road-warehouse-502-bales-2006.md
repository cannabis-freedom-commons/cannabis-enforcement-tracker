An anonymous tip led Harris County sheriff's deputies to a warehouse in the 7300 block of West
Road in northwest Houston late on Wednesday 27 December 2006, where they seized one of the
largest marijuana loads the county had seen. [@1] Deputies stopped a truck leaving the warehouse
with its headlights off and found a small amount of cocaine and marijuana in the cab, then
searched the building and found 502 bundles of marijuana packed in wooden crates. [@1]

The haul weighed nearly 7,000 kilograms and carried an estimated street value of $25 million to
$40 million, the National Drug Intelligence Center reported. [@2] A truck driver was charged
with drug trafficking over the seizure. [@1] Sheriff's Lieutenant J.D. Glesmann told the
Chronicle he had never seen a marijuana load as big. [@1]

## Transshipment point

The warehouse appeared to be a distribution point where bulk loads were broken down for
transport east, possibly through Atlanta to the East Coast, according to Stan Furce, a former
DEA agent heading Houston's High-Intensity Drug Trafficking Area office. [@1] The seizure
confirmed Houston's role as a principal transshipment area for marijuana: Texas Department of
Public Safety figures show Harris County marijuana seizures more than doubled from 887 kilograms
in 2005 to 1,906 kilograms in 2006. [@2]
