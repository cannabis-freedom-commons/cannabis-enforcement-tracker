Moroccan customs services used a mobile scanner to seize a record quantity of
11 tons of hashish aboard two TIR trucks in December 2006. [@1] A US
diplomatic cable describing the same record seizure says officials seized a
record 11 metric tons of cannabis resin in Tangier that month, crediting the
mobile x-ray scanner. [@2] The national wire's account places the seizure at
Nador. [@1]
