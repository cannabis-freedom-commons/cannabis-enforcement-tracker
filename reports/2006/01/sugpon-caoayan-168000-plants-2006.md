Elements of the Special Operations Group, Sugpon police and the Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency
(PDEA) destroyed marijuana across at least 24 sites totalling 5.5 hectares in sitios Nava, Nagawa,
Nacneng, Bekes and Mount Kuba in Barangay Caoayan, Sugpon, Ilocos Sur. [@1] Senior Superintendent
Marlou Cortez Chan of the regional intelligence division said troops uprooted about 168,000 fully
grown plants worth ₱33.6 million and seedlings worth ₱2.8 million, for a total value of ₱36.4
million by police estimates. [@1]

## Weapons and absent suspects

The team also confiscated a .30-calibre M1 Garand rifle, four sticks of dynamite, twelve blasting
caps, a hand grenade, detonating cord and eight clips of Garand ammunition. [@1] Suspected cultivators
Santos and Pepito Alkadis were not present, but police said they would face charges under the
dangerous-drugs and anti-firearms laws. [@1] The report does not describe arrests or burns at the
scene beyond the uprooting. [@1]
