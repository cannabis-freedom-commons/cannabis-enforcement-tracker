Pakistani national Ijaz Ahmad Nadar Khan was beheaded by sword in the western Saudi city of Mecca after being sentenced to death for receiving contraband hashish, according to an Interior Ministry statement carried by the official Saudi Press Agency.[@1] An unnamed Nigerian was beheaded with him after being convicted of introducing cocaine into the kingdom concealed in his stomach.[@1,2]

The two executions were announced together and bring that year's reported execution total in Saudi Arabia to at least 26.[@1] Under Saudi Arabia's strict interpretation of Islamic law, drug trafficking is among the offences punishable by death, and executions are generally carried out in public.[@1]
