In a joint operation involving police from Lesotho and South Africa in July 2006, 47 tons of cannabis herb were seized. [@1]

The seizure, equal to 47,000 kilograms, was recorded by the International Narcotics Control Board in its annual report for 2006. [@1] The report gives no separate account of where the cannabis was found, whether anyone was arrested, or what happened to the haul after it was seized. [@1]
