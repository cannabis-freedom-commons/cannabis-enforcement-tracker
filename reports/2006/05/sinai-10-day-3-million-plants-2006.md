Egyptian police and military forces held a ten-day operation against cannabis plantations in the mountains and plateaus of central and southern Sinai in May 2006, an effort the Egyptian press described as a war on the growers. [@1] Some 500 personnel took part, and the official account reported 450 plantations destroyed along with three tons of bango, a local variety of marijuana, and three million plants. [@1]

Prohibited crops had reappeared in Sinai in the 1990s after declining under Israeli occupation, becoming the target of regular destruction campaigns. [@1] The International Crisis Group, reporting the May 2006 operation in January 2007 from the Egyptian press, noted that researchers have long questioned the plausibility of official Sinai eradication figures. [@1]
