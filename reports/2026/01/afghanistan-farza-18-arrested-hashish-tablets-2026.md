Farza district's security command in Kabul province announced the arrest of 18
people on charges of trafficking narcotics and addictive tablets. [@1] The
arrests were made during a ten-day operation in the district. [@1] District
control officer Khalid said officers arrested the 18 people together with seven
vehicles used to move drugs from one province to another. [@1]

The district police chief said officers found and seized more than 32,000 Zeegap
tablets, over 13,000 K tablets and more than 23 kilograms of hashish. [@1] One
detainee said the drugs were brought from Pakistan to Jalalabad and then
distributed to various provinces and districts, including Kabul. [@1]

Security chief Iqbal Ahmad Enteqam said the public cooperated with police and
that people report anything suspicious they observe. [@1] Enteqam said
investigations found the detainees admitted importing the drugs from neighboring
countries. [@1] Security officials said poppy cultivation and drug production in
the district are now nearly nonexistent, while most synthetic drugs are smuggled
from neighboring countries. [@1] One detainee said he previously used drugs
himself and was later offered a salary to help distribute them. [@1]
