On 7 January 2026, a joint operation by Assam Rifles, the Border Security Force, Tripura Police, Tripura State Rifles and the Forest Department destroyed approximately 600,000 young cannabis plants across 65 plots and about 200 acres in Dhanpur, Induria, Kacchakhala and Dhanmura in Sepahijala district. [@1,2,3,4,5] The four villages fall under the Sonamura, Melagarh and Kalamchoura police station areas. [@5]

Authorities estimated the market value of the destroyed crop at approximately INR360 million. [@1,2,3,4,5] Brighter Kashmir described simultaneous raids across the four locations, while Northeast Herald reported that the day-long operation continued until late Wednesday evening. [@1,3]

Officials said around 30 lakh marijuana plants worth Rs 145 crore had been destroyed in Sepahijala district in ten days, after earlier drives on 30 December and 3 January accounted for over 23 lakh plants across 414 acres and about Rs 108 crore. [@4,5] A police official said several people involved in illegal cultivation were arrested under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, 1985, without giving a number. [@4] A police official said forest land and other government land had been encroached upon and illegally used for cannabis cultivation. [@5] The same account says dry ganja produced in Tripura is smuggled to states including Bihar and Uttar Pradesh, where it fetches a higher price. [@1] Residents, including women, said they cultivate ganja in hilly and inaccessible areas as part of their livelihood. [@5]
