On 7 January 2026, a joint operation by Assam Rifles, the Border Security Force, Tripura Police, Tripura State Rifles and the Forest Department destroyed approximately 600,000 young cannabis plants across 65 plots and about 200 acres in Dhanpur, Induria, Kacchakhala and Dhanmura in Sepahijala district. [@1,2,3]

Authorities estimated the market value of the destroyed crop at approximately INR360 million. [@1,2,3] Brighter Kashmir described simultaneous raids across the four locations, while Northeast Herald reported that the day-long operation continued until late Wednesday evening. [@1,3]
