Officers of Squadron 56 “Gualeguaychú” stopped a bus carrying football
supporters toward Uruguay at the Puerto Unzué section. [@1] The stop took place
at kilometre 38 of Ruta Nacional 136. [@1] The check was carried out as part of
sports-security controls. [@1]

Officers first found drug wrappers in the bathroom area, which led them to
extend the search to compartments inside the bus. [@1] An officer noticed screws
at different depths on the air-conditioning housing. [@1] Behind the wiring
panel officers found a hidden 9mm pistol. [@1] The pistol had its magazine
fitted with 15 rounds ready to fire. [@1] The operation also seized doses of
cocaine and marijuana alongside the pistol. [@1]

The Federal Court of Gualeguaychú intervened and ordered the seizure of the
weapon and drugs for breach of the drugs and arms laws. [@1]
