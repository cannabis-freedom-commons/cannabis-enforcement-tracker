Two residents of Stolbtsy District in Belarus's Minsk Region, aged 40 and 56,
ran an illegal cannabis-growing operation in a room at the older man's home, the
Interior Ministry said. [@1] The two men fitted out a room and sowed cannabis
seeds there. [@1]

Drug-control officers of Minsk Region found and seized 41 cannabis plants from
the grow room, which the Ministry called a cannabis farm. [@1] The Interior
Ministry's own statement in Minsk Region's police channel describes the same
discovery and seizure of 41 plants. [@2] Officers found and seized 41 cannabis
plants in the case, Radio Mir reported. [@3]

Investigators opened a criminal case over the grow, BelTA reported. [@1] Both
men had previously been convicted of the same kind of offence. [@1] Both
suspects had earlier convictions for a similar crime, the Interior Ministry
said. [@2] Police reminded the public that sowing or growing plants or mushrooms
containing narcotic or psychotropic substances is banned in Belarus, and that
the maximum penalty under that article is up to 15 years in prison. [@1]
