Police detained Elieth del Rosario Sanchez Arriola, 30, and Walter de Jesus Jaen
Jimenez, 34, after profiling a collective-transport bus on the Jalapa-Esteli
route. [@1] The stop took place at the police checkpoint at kilometre 231 on the
Ocotal-Mozonte road. [@1] Police said both detainees had criminal records for
trafficking in narcotics, psychotropic and controlled substances and were
remitted with the seized evidence for judicial proceedings. [@1] A second
account names the same pair, aged 30 and 34, as detained by the National Police
in Mozonte, Nueva Segovia, with five pounds of marijuana. [@2]

Officers searching the detainees under the law seized a black bag belonging to
them containing five packages wrapped in adhesive bags with green herb. [@1] A
field test was positive for marijuana and police gave the weight as 5 pounds,
about 2.27 kg. [@1] The second account likewise says the pair carried five
adhesive-tape-wrapped packages in a bag aboard the Jalapa-Esteli passenger bus.
[@2] It also describes the contents as green herb that tested positive for
marijuana with a weight of five pounds. [@2]

The operation occurred on Thursday 29 January 2026 at 2:00 in the afternoon.
[@1] Departmental police spokeswoman Lieutenant Karla Duarte said the couple
were arrested that Thursday afternoon at the kilometre 231 checkpoint on the
Ocotal-Mozonte road. [@2] Police presented the result at a press conference at
the Commander Thomas Borge Martinez station in Ocotal, Nueva Segovia. [@1] The
seized drugs and the detainees were placed at the order of the Public Prosecutor
to be charged before the corresponding authorities. [@2]
