The Paris tribunal judiciaire convicted the 11 people tried over a cannabis
delivery network run by three brothers from the Val-de-Marne, at the end of a
trial that ran for fifteen days. [@1]

Afif B., 36, who was tried in his absence after being released when the trial
was postponed in 2024, was sentenced to 15 years in prison. [@1] His two
brothers and their eight co-defendants received sentences ranging from a
one-year suspended prison term to 11 years firm. [@1] The court also imposed
fines running from 30,000 euros to 5 million euros. [@1]

The nine men and two women, aged 28 to 38, were tried for running an
industrial-scale "go fast" drug delivery system between 2018 and 2020, a term
for convoys that carry drugs at speed by car. [@1] Led by the three brothers,
the network brought large quantities of cannabis from Morocco between 2018 and
2020 to Paris, the Paris suburbs and the provinces. [@1]

At the trial, Afif B. said he had sold "bédo", slang for cannabis, for twenty
years and delivered across the whole of France, describing himself as a
wholesaler rather than a retailer. [@1]
