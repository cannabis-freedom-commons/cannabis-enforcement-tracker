Guinea's national police anti-drug units arrested two women in a hashish case
that began with an airport interception. [@1] Mme Da Costa Quinta was arrested
on 19 December 2025 at Conakry's Ahmed Sékou Touré International Airport with
159 slabs of hashish weighing 16.8 kg. [@1] Questioned by investigators of the
Central Anti-Drug Office (OCAD), she named Mme Fatoumata Sy as the owner of the
drugs and said she had twice carried large quantities of drugs for her. [@1]
Acting on that account, OCAD's research brigade followed and watched Sy for
about a month before arresting her on 19 January 2026. [@2]

Police describe hashish as a cannabis derivative with high concentration. [@1]
Sy acknowledged making several trips to Mali, Senegal, Tunisia, Morocco and Côte
d'Ivoire. [@1]

Police said she could face prosecution for detention, transport and
international trafficking of cocaine and psychotropic substances under articles
812 and 821 of the penal code, although the seizure described in the same
statement was hashish. [@1] Both suspects were to be brought before the Mafanco
Court of First Instance. [@2]
