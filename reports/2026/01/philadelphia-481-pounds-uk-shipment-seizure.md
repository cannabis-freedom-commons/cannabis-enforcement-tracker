U.S. Customs and Border Protection officers intercepted nearly 500 pounds of
marijuana near Philadelphia on 6 January 2026 while it was being shipped from
Georgia to the United Kingdom. [@1] During routine export inspections, officers
examined a shipping container holding 80 boxes manifested as clothing. [@1] The
clothing had been shipped from Decatur, Georgia, to an address in Oxford,
England. [@1]

Officers opened one box and found marijuana in a vacuum-sealed bag. [@1] They
then checked all 80 boxes and found that seven boxes held vacuum-sealed bags of
marijuana. [@1] Officers counted 405 vacuum-sealed packages of marijuana in
total. [@1] The packages carried labels possibly identifying different strains,
including Runtzberry, Joker Candy, Galactic Gas, Mega Cherries and Blue Zooties.
[@1] The marijuana weighed a combined 218 kilograms, about 481 pounds. [@1]
Officers seized the marijuana. [@1] CBP put the street value in the United
States at $2.4 million. [@1] The agency said high-potency marijuana could fetch
two to three times that amount in Europe. [@1]

CBP area port director for Philadelphia Cleatus P. Hunt Jr. said large marijuana
loads in containerized cargo had become rare and suggested traffickers may be
shifting bulk-smuggling tactics. [@1] He said seizing bulk loads hits
transnational criminal groups in the wallet by depriving them of illicit gains.
[@1]
