At a 23 January 2026 briefing, Urumqi Railway Public Security said it had jointly cleared more than 238,000 wild cannabis plants outside railway protection fences across Xinjiang during 2025. [@1,2] The figure was presented as an annual result for railway-safety work across the region, rather than the count from one site or a single raid. [@1,2] Both outlets report the same briefing and give a rounded minimum of 23.8 wan (238,000) plants; the tracker records 238,000 as the minimum, not an exact total. [@1,2]

Xinjiang Daily says the bureau was bringing railway-safety environmental management into broader social governance. [@1] Both accounts also report more than 5,200 railway-protection outreach sessions during 2025. [@1,2] Xinjiang Daily separately says railway police stopped 32 instances of burning near railway lines that year. [@1] These are distinct safety activities and are not additional cannabis-clearance figures. [@1,2] Neither account lists the counties or stations involved, the dates or number of individual clearance operations, the area searched, or the weight of the plants. [@1,2] The photographs on both pages show the January 23 government briefing rather than the clearance itself. [@1,2]
