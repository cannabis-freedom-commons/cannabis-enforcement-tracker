Police in Bishkek's Sverdlovsky district detained a man found carrying cannabis
in a quantity the city interior department classified as especially large. [@1]
Plainclothes officers had received information that a man was collecting,
storing and distributing drugs around the junction of Zhibek Zholu avenue and
Kalinin street. [@1]

On Kalinin and Ozernaya streets, in the Vostok residential area, officers
stopped a KIA Picanto, whose driver, identified only as A.A., handed over a
white bag of plant material with a characteristic smell. [@2] The seized
material weighed 2 kilograms 14 grams. [@1] A preliminary forensic chemical
examination identified the substance as cannabis, that is marijuana, listed
among prohibited substances. [@2]

The Sverdlovsky district investigation service opened a criminal case under
article 283 of Kyrgyzstan's Criminal Code, which covers unlawful manufacture of
drugs and psychotropic substances without intent to sell. [@1] The driver, born
in 2000, was held in the temporary detention centre of Bishkek's main interior
department. [@2] Investigators said further investigative actions were
continuing. [@1]

The city interior department cited illegal drug circulation as a serious crime
carrying strict criminal liability and urged the public to report drug storage,
manufacture or sale to the nearest police station or on 102. [@2] The accounts
of the case were published by the news site 24.kg and Caravan-Info on 23 January
2026, and the 24.kg text was republished by the aggregator FOR.kg the same day.
