Bandera County sheriff's deputies searched a property in the Polly Peak
Subdivision on Saturday, 31 January 2026. [@2] The search was carried out by the
sheriff's criminal interdiction unit under a search warrant. [@1]

Deputies seized more than 2 pounds of marijuana and 168 grams of
tetrahydrocannabinol, or THC. [@2] They also seized 7 grams of methamphetamine
and more than $30,000 in cash. [@1]

Randy Stokes was arrested on a charge of possessing between 4 ounces and 5
pounds of marijuana, a state jail felony, and on two counts of possession with
intent to deliver, for between 4 and 200 grams of methamphetamine and the same
range of THC. [@2] Ean Stokes was arrested on an outstanding felony warrant for
possessing between 1 and 4 grams of THC, and also faces the marijuana and
intent-to-deliver THC charges. [@2] Both men were booked into the Bandera County
Jail. [@1]

Sheriff Josh Teitge praised the deputies in a Facebook post on 2 February. [@2]
