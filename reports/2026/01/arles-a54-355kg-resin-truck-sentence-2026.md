Customs officers of the Nîmes internal surveillance brigade (BSI) stopped a
Spain-registered articulated lorry at the Arles toll plaza on the A54 motorway,
in the Spain-to-Italy direction. [@1] The control took place around midnight on
the night of 29 January 2026. [@1] A customs sniffer-dog team gave a positive
indication on the left pallet-carrier of the trailer. [@1]

Behind sacks of sawdust officers found many heat-sealed parcels, and on-the-spot
tests confirmed the contents as cannabis resin pressed into soap-bar form. [@1]
The search brought the total seized to 355 kilograms of cannabis resin. [@1] The
load was handed to OFAST, the French anti-narcotics office in Marseille, for the
rest of the proceedings. [@1] The driver was held in customs detention and taken
to the brigade. [@1]

The truck was carrying mainly textiles from Barcelona to Italy, declared as
goods for the retailer Lidl. [@1] The driver, a 53-year-old Dominican national
resident in Spain, was jailed for three years and given a 710,000-euro customs
fine. [@2] The court also barred him from French territory for five years and
ordered the confiscation of all the drugs and of the lorry. [@2] He was judged
in Marseille on Wednesday 3 February. [@2]

According to the Montpellier regional customs directorate, a customs dog picked
up a suspicious smell around the pallet trucks in the load of clothing. [@2]

The regional customs directorate said a seizure this size happens only two or
three times a year, with a kilo of cannabis worth about 2,000 euros. [@2]

Police & Réalités, citing the Nîmes daily La Gazette de Nîmes, reported that six
customs officers based in Nîmes carried out the control. [@3]
