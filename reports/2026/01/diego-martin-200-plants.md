On 4 January 2026, police in Diego Martin, Trinidad and Tobago, found a clearing off Salandy Street during Operation Trident and destroyed approximately 200 cannabis plants. [@1,2]

The officers walked nearly a mile uphill, uprooted the plants and burned them; the operation involved units from the Western Division Gang Intelligence Unit, West End Criminal Investigations Department and West End Charge Room. [@1,2]

The exercise ran between 11 am and 2.30 pm, with officers proceeding on foot along a track off Salandy Street over approximately one mile of steep terrain. [@2] The plantation held around 200 fully grown and juvenile cannabis plants. [@2] Senior Superintendent Williams sanctioned the operation, which was led by Superintendent Gyan and W/Assistant Superintendent Aguellera. [@2] Coordination was provided by Inspectors Grant and Perry, with supervision from Corporals Garcia, Khan and Acting Corporal Joseph. [@2]
