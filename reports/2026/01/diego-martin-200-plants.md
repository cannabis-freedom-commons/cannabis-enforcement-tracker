On 4 January 2026, police in Diego Martin, Trinidad and Tobago, found a clearing off Salandy Street during Operation Trident and destroyed approximately 200 cannabis plants. [@1]

The officers walked nearly a mile uphill, uprooted the plants and burned them; the operation involved units from the Western Division Gang Intelligence Unit, West End Criminal Investigations Department and West End Charge Room. [@1]
