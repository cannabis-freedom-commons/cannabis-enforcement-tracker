The Comorian coast guard intercepted a fishing boat in Comorian waters in the evening of Saturday
17 January 2026 and seized 149 kg of cannabis. [@1,2,4] Several people were detained and handed to
the judicial authorities. [@1,2,4] Among them was a repeat facilitator known to the security
services, who the coast guard said was involved in organised networks, including bringing African
migrants into Comoros irregularly. [@1,2,3,4]

La Gazette des Comores estimated the cannabis at about 100,000 Comorian francs per kilogram, or
about 14.9 million francs (around €30,000) for the cargo. [@3] The coast guard said the seizure
showed its increased surveillance of the archipelago's maritime borders. [@1,2]

On 21 January the coast guard said it had burned the 149 kg at the Palais de Justice in Anjouan
under the authority of the public prosecutor, with the court clerk present. [@5] It said the
destruction was meant to protect the population, especially young people. [@5]
