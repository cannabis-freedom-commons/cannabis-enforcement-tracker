Junagadh police arrested a man for cultivating cannabis plants in Mendarda
taluka. [@1] Officers seized 3 kilograms 360 grams of fresh cannabis worth
approximately Rs 1.68 lakh. [@1]

The operation was conducted by the Special Operations Group under the guidance
of Junagadh Range IG Nilesh Jajadia and SP Subodh Odedara. [@1] Acting on
information received by SOG officers, police raided a property at Lakkadvera
Nesh on the Haripur-Bhalchhel road. [@1] The accused, 47-year-old Bapumiya
Ismailmiya Bukhari, had cultivated the cannabis behind his residence, adjacent
to the kitchen wall. [@1] During questioning he admitted growing the plants
because of his personal habit of consumption. [@1]

A case was registered at Mendarda police station and further investigation was
underway. [@1]
