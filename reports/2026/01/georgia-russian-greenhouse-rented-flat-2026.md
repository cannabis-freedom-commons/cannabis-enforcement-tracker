Police in Georgia detained a 46-year-old Russian citizen on suspicion of growing
cannabis. [@1] Investigators say the man fitted a special greenhouse inside a
rented temporary flat where he unlawfully grew plants containing narcotic
substances. [@2] A criminal case was opened over unlawful drug storage and
unlawful sowing, growing or cultivation of narcotic-containing plants. [@1]

During a search, officers seized what the Interior Ministry described as an
especially large amount of dried marijuana and cannabis plants as physical
evidence. [@1] Officers also seized materials needed to cultivate the plants and
to package or process narcotics. [@2] The ministry said the man had grown the
narcotic-containing plants on an especially large scale in the temporary flat.
[@2]

The investigation is proceeding under Articles 260 and 265 of Georgia's Criminal
Code, which cover unlawful acquisition or storage of drugs and unlawful
cultivation of narcotic plants. [@2] Those articles carry punishment of up to 20
years in prison or life imprisonment. [@2]
