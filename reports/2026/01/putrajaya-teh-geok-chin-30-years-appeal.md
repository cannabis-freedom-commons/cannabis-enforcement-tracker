The Court of Appeal in Putrajaya upheld 30 years' imprisonment and 12 strokes of the rotan for Teh Geok Chin, a tiler found guilty of trafficking 1,024.6 g of cannabis. [@1,2] Justices Hashim Hamzah, Mohamed Zaini Mazlan and Azmi Ariffin dismissed his appeal against conviction and sentence. [@1,2]

Teh, 47, was sentenced by the High Court in October 2023 for an offence at an apartment in Jalan Muafakat, Larkin, Johor Bahru, at 12.10 a.m. on 18 April 2019. [@1,2] He was first charged with Nguyen Thi Thanh Tuyen, who was later acquitted, and changed his plea to guilty after the Attorney General's Chambers accepted his representation. [@2] Because he pleaded guilty, the law barred him from appealing the conviction and let him contest only the sentence. [@1,2] The court found the charge was read to him in Mandarin and the guilty-plea procedure was followed. [@1,2]

On sentence, the court said 30 years was the minimum the High Court could impose, since its only options were death or life imprisonment with 12 strokes. [@1,2] Justice Zaini's 34-page judgment of 18 December 2025 added a 17-point guideline for trial judges taking guilty pleas in grave cases. [@2]
