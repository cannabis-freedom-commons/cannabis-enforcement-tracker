Liam Waldrum, 29, was given a 22-month prison sentence suspended for two years at the Court of
General Gaol Delivery for being concerned in importing cannabis. [@1,2] Isle of Man Today reported
that Deemster Graeme Cook also imposed two years' supervision. [@2]

A brown parcel addressed to Waldrum's home in Union Mills was intercepted at the Isle of Man Post
Office on 8 May 2025 after a police dog indicated that it was suspicious. [@1,2] It contained
4,007.5 grams of cannabis with a street value of £81,150, Isle of Man Today reported; the BBC put the
value at £81,000. [@1,2] A search of his home found a small amount of cannabis in a bedside table,
and police seized his phone and weighing scales. [@1]

Waldrum answered no comment in interview but later pleaded guilty to importing cannabis. [@1] He
told the court that he had not ordered the drugs and had agreed to let his address be used in
exchange for an ounce of cannabis, worth about £500; the BBC reported the offer as £560. [@1,2] His
advocate said he worked as a supermarket lorry driver, saw his child twice a week, and would lose his
job and home in prison. [@1,2]
