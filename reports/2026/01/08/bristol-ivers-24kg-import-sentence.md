Jamie Ivers, 34, of Field Road, St George, appeared at Bristol Crown Court on 8 January 2026 and
was sentenced to three years and nine months in prison after pleading guilty to seven offences.
[@1,2] The South West Regional Organised Crime Unit (SWROCU) had linked him to packages totalling
24 kilos of cannabis imported from the United States between May and November 2025, which police
valued at £200,000. [@1,2]

Officers executed warrants at two Field Road addresses and at a stash house in Craftes Court,
Speedwell. [@1,2] SWROCU said Ivers assaulted an officer during the searches, where officers found
more than 2.5 kilos of cannabis ready for supply and £16,000 in cash, along with a zombie-style
machete at the stash house. [@2] At the time Avon and Somerset Police were also investigating him
for dangerous driving and perverting the course of justice. [@1,2]

Detective Sergeant Stuart Cumine said Ivers had supplied class B drugs at bulk and street level in
Bristol, particularly East Bristol. [@1,2] The guilty pleas covered importation, possession with
intent to supply, possession of criminal property, possessing an offensive weapon in a private
place, assaulting an emergency worker, dangerous driving and perverting the course of justice.
[@2]
