Gendarmes of the Brigade Territoriale d'Aéré Lao arrested three Senegalese men
at about 5 am on 26 January 2026 during a road check. [@1] The three men were
travelling in a Peugeot 407. [@3]

The search of the car found fourteen one-kilogram packets of Indian hemp hidden
in a bag of livestock feed on the front passenger seat, 14 kilograms in total.
[@1] Public broadcaster RTS reported the same fourteen packets, the same hiding
place and the same 14-kilogram total. [@2] The gendarmes confiscated the Peugeot
407 used to transport the drug and a Haojue motorcycle they said probably
belonged to the suspects. [@1] RTS reported that the car and the motorcycle were
seized along with the drug. [@2]

The three men were placed in custody and are to face prosecution for drug
trafficking. [@1] The operation was part of the national plan against drug
trafficking run by the Gendarmerie nationale. [@2] Dakaractu published the
Gendarmerie nationale's own note describing the same road check and the same
fourteen packets in the livestock-feed bag. [@3] Actusen reported that the
Brigade Territoriale d'Aéré Lao broke up the trafficking network on 26 January
2026 at about 5 am, citing the Gendarmerie nationale. [@4]
