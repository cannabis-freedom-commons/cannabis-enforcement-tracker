Uzbek law-enforcement agencies carried out a series of special operations
against narcotics illegally brought in from Tajikistan. [@1] In Kokand, State
Security Service officers together with customs and internal-affairs colleagues
searched the home of a local resident born in 1984 who was suspected of drug
trafficking. [@1] In the kitchen of that house, officers found and seized 4.572
kg of opium and 1.058 kg of hashish. [@1] Officers also seized 9.98 kg of opium
previously hidden in Dangara district after Tajik dealers sent its geolocation
via WhatsApp. [@1]

A Kokand resident born in 1969 had organized a drug laboratory at home, where
officers seized another 7.226 kg of opium. [@1] In another Kokand episode,
officers stopped a Lacetti and found 5.022 kg of opium said to have been bought
for $34,000. [@1] The suspected local seller and his accomplice, a Tajik
citizen, were detained, and officers seized another 2.362 kg of opium at the
Tajik citizen's house in Uzbekistan district. [@1] Officers seized 2.362 kg of
opium in the house of the Tajik citizen in Uzbekistan district. [@1] At a Nexia
check in Kokand, officers found 982 g of hashish in the bag of a passenger born
in 1992, taken from a cache previously set up by Tajik couriers. [@1]

In Fergana, a man born in 2000 was detained while attempting to sell 989 g of
hashish smuggled from Tajikistan. [@1] Criminal cases were opened over all the
episodes, the suspects were placed in custody, and investigative actions are
continuing. [@1] NOVA24.UZ, citing the SSS press service, reported that the
operations intercepted a total of 32 kg of narcotics from Tajikistan. [@2]
