Customs officers and border guards stopped a Lacetti that had entered from
Tajikistan at the Ravat post and examined the car with technical inspection
equipment. [@1] The car was driven by a citizen born in 1979 who lives in
Uzbekistan district. [@3] Investigators said the driver agreed for 2,000 US
dollars to carry the prohibited substances across the border and bury them in
the garden near his house. [@2] A customer who is a citizen of Tajikistan was to
collect the buried drugs afterwards. [@1] The driver was placed in custody as a
restraint measure while the case is investigated. [@3]

Officers found hidden wraps in the fuel tank and opened the tank in the presence
of attesting witnesses. [@1] The search recovered 10 kg 739 g of hashish and 412
g of marijuana concealed from inspection in the tank. [@3] Officers also seized
35 g of opium and 6,945 Royal tablets, a potent medicinal drug, alongside the
cannabis. [@2] All the seized substances were taken as evidence. [@1]

The operation took place at the Ravot border post in Besharik District of
Fergana Region. [@3] Authorities opened a criminal smuggling case under Article
246 of the Criminal Code. [@1] Investigators are working to establish the
driver’s criminal links and inquiries continue. [@3]
