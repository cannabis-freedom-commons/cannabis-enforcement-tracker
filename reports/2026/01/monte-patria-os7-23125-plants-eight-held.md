Eight people were detained in the raids in Ovalle and Monte Patria and all were
placed in preventive detention after formalisation. [@1] Four of them were
detained at the plantation in the Rapel sector of Monte Patria, where they were
guarding and working the crop. [@1] Ovalle's acting regional prosecutor, Rodrigo
Gómez, said the four were two Haitian nationals, a Bolivian and a Chilean. [@1]
The Chilean was 40 years old, the Bolivian 26 and in Chile irregularly, and the
two Haitians were 41 and 36, the younger with an outstanding warrant for
assault. [@2]

Officers seized more than 23,125 live cannabis plants still growing, which
Carabineros valued at 111,000 million pesos. [@1] The uprooting across several
ravines inside Monte Patria took more than six hours, backed from the air by the
Sección Aérea and on the ground by GOPE and territorial police. [@1] A second
operation on the road between Ovalle and Monte Patria, moving from Tulahuén
towards Talhuén, seized more than 40 kg of processed marijuana and four more
people were detained. [@1] The Limarí press reported the two consecutive
operations together as the biggest cannabis seizure since the OS7 section was
created in the region, with two criminal organisations dismantled. [@3]

It was the largest cannabis seizure for OS7 Limarí since the unit was set up in
the region in January 2024, and the third territorial operation this year after
two plantation finds in Los Vilos. [@1] The cultivation investigation was given
a 60-day deadline and the trafficking case 40 days, with no court ruling on
guilt reported. [@1] OS7 Limarí marked two years in operation this month, a
period in which it says it has seized more than six tonnes of drugs. [@1]
