Sri Lankan police arrested three Indian nationals for allegedly possessing 50 kg
of cannabis at Colombo international airport, as officials described the case on
Tuesday. [@1] The suspects included two women, and police said the group had
arrived at Bandaranaike International Airport on a SriLankan Airways flight from
Bangkok. [@1] Police identified the two women suspects as Mumbai residents aged
between 25 and 27 who worked as teachers. [@1]

Police said the three suspects had attempted to flee through the green channel
when officers arrested them at the airport. [@1] Officers found them carrying 50
kg of Kush cannabis when they were stopped. [@1] Police valued that 50 kg at LKR
500 million, a figure also reported as over Rs 14.5 crore. [@1]

The police narcotics bureau said the 50 kg haul was the largest drug haul ever
caught at the airport. [@1] Officials separately described the same haul as the
largest narcotics haul at Colombo international airport. [@1]
