Troops of the Cacique Pigoanza Infantry Battalion No. 26 of the National Army's
Ninth Brigade, in coordination with the Huila Police, seized more than 28
kilograms of narcotics during a road checkpoint in the municipality of Paicol.
[@1] The checkpoint was set up on route I-24, described as a strategic road in
western Huila, to check vehicles and passengers moving through the corridor.
[@1] During the inspection of a public-service bus, officers found a travel
suitcase holding 25 packages of illicit substances. [@1] A woman was captured
during the operation as allegedly linked to transporting the stash. [@2]

Preliminary tests showed the load was about 28 kilograms of marijuana plus 250
grams of coca paste, which authorities said kept about 28,000 doses off the
streets. [@1] Authorities put the illegal-market value of the seized drugs at
24,080,000 pesos. [@2] The seized substances were placed at the disposal of the
competent judicial authorities for prosecution and destruction while
investigations continue into the drugs' origin, final destination and criminal
networks. [@1]

Authorities said traffickers keep using public buses and passenger luggage to
move drugs and evade controls. [@1] The authorities said the road controls and
military operations will continue permanently at strategic points in Huila. [@1]
