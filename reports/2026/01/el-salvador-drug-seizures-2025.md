Defence minister René Merino Monroy presented the security cabinet's figures for 2025: 25,011.05 kg of drugs seized, against 17,224.8 kg in 2024. Cocaine made up 24,612.59 kg of the total; marijuana accounted for 394.52 kg, valued at about 1.39 million dollars. [@1,3]

The Salvadoran government said security forces seized a little more than 25 tonnes of drugs in 2025, most of it cocaine, valued at more than $618.7 million. [@2] Marijuana, crack and methamphetamine completed the drug haul of the past year, whose total value rises above $620 million, according to the Salvadoran presidency. [@2] Defence Minister René Francis Merino said the National Navy located most of the drug in Salvadoran Pacific waters. [@2] In 2024 the authorities seized more than 17.2 tonnes of drugs valued at $422.7 million. [@2]

Defence minister René Merino said 22.5 tonnes of the 2025 total were taken by the National Navy in 16 operations at sea. [@3] The ministry's table also listed 0.23 kg of hashish, valued at $8,050, alongside the marijuana. [@3] Merino said the 2025 seizures passed 2024's by almost eight tonnes of drugs. [@3] The year's summary left out heroin, ecstasy, tusi and fentanyl, which the previous year's table had included. [@3]
