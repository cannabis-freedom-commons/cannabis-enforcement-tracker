Togo’s National Police intervened in an abandoned house after residents alerted
officers to suspect sacks left inside an unoccupied dwelling. [@1] Officers sent
to the scene carried out a thorough search of the building. [@1] The alert came
from residents of Kligué-Kondji after suspect sacks were found in the abandoned
house. [@2] No arrests had been reported, while police said investigations were
continuing to identify those responsible and dismantle the network behind the
consignment. [@1]

Inside, officers found three maize sacks containing 216 pains of cannabis
carefully concealed. [@1] Traces of the substance found on the floor suggested
the house was used as a storage place in drug trafficking. [@1] Cannabis powder
found spread on the floor confirmed to police that the house had served as a
cannabis storehouse. [@2]

The seizure occurred on 10 January 2026 at around 11 a.m. in a district of the
town of Kpalimé. [@2] The abandoned house is in Kloto Prefecture in
south-western Togo. [@1] Authorities praised residents’ vigilance, stressing
cooperation between citizens and security forces in fighting drug trafficking in
Togo. [@1]
