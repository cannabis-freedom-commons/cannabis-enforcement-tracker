The criminal court of first instance in Ieper convicted fifteen defendants of
cultivating and possessing cannabis grown in the cellars beneath the pigsties of
a farm in Staden, West Flanders. [@1] In July 2023 police raided the farm, where
the farming family had converted a cellar under the pigsties and a barn to grow
cannabis plants between April 2021 and July 2023. [@1] Seventeen defendants had
to answer the charges in the case. [@1]

The family played the central role: invoices for the structural conversion work
were paid through the family's companies, and other defendants advised, moved
material from the Netherlands, did electrical work and trimmed the harvest. [@3]
The lead defendant, one of the family members, was sentenced to four years in
prison, half suspended, a 24,000-euro fine, half suspended, and had 50,000 euros
confiscated. [@1] The second defendant received a 150-hour community service
order, an 8,000-euro fine and 20,000 euros confiscation. [@1] The third
defendant received three years in prison, eighteen months suspended, an
8,000-euro fine and 30,000 euros confiscation. [@1]

The ex-advocate Pol Vandemeulebroucke, ninth defendant, was given a 120-hour
community service order and an 8,000-euro fine for bringing the farming family
into contact with another defendant experienced in setting up cannabis
plantations. [@2] At the barbecue on the farm, Vandemeulebroucke and that
defendant discussed the construction, cultivation and harvest of a cannabis
plantation in great detail, the court found. [@2] Other defendants received
prison terms ranging from eight months to three years, partly suspended, along
with fines of 8,000 to 24,000 euros, part suspended. [@2]

The submitted HLN account presents this Eigen Kweek case, with Vandemeulebroucke
among the convicted, as continuing on appeal. [@4]
