Gendarmes of Grupo Talamuyuna, under Squadron 58 La Rioja, seized a marijuana
shipment being carried on a passenger bus. [@1] The checkpoint was at kilometre
434 of National Route 38. [@1] The bus was travelling the San Salvador de Jujuy
to Mendoza route. [@1] Officers found a black handbag with orange details inside
the bus. [@1] The bag lay between seats 19 and 20, both of which were
unoccupied. [@1] A strong marijuana smell led officers to open the bag before
witnesses. [@1]

The bag held three packages wrapped in ochre tape. [@1] Field Narcotest testing
confirmed the contents as cannabis sativa (marijuana) weighing 3.294 kg. [@1]
The Federal Court of La Rioja ordered full seizure of the drug, identification
of the drivers and a detailed passenger list, and allowed the bus to continue
its route. [@1] The security ministry's account says the operation was part of
Plan Verano prevention work on tourist routes during the high season. [@2]
Mendoza Today confirms officers found three ochre-taped packages inside the bag
after the search. [@3] No arrest or detention was reported; the published orders
mention only seizure, identification of the drivers and listing the passengers.
