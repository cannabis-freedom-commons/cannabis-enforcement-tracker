Mexico's federal security secretary, Omar García Harfuch, announced on 13
January 2026 that six alleged members of the Venezuelan criminal organisation
Tren de Aragua had been arrested in Mexico City. [@1] The authorities linked the
six to extortion, human trafficking and drug trafficking, including the
distribution of narcotics in the capital. [@1]

The federal security ministry said they were linked to extortion, human
trafficking and drug trafficking, which are among the lines of investigation
opened by the agencies in the operation. [@2] Officers from the Defence
Ministry, the Navy, the federal security ministry, the Mexico City security
ministry, the attorney general's office and the National Guard took part in the
operations. [@2] After fixed, mobile and discreet surveillance of buildings in
colonia Valle Gómez, Venustiano Carranza, a control judge granted warrants and
officers searched two homes. [@2]

In those two searches officers detained five people, including one identified as
Lesli Valeri N, described as the person who collected proceeds from sexual
exploitation and controlled trafficked women. [@2] The searches in Valle Gómez
also turned up doses of methamphetamine and marijuana, mobile phones, a short
firearm, cash and computer equipment. [@2] The two searches in Valle Gómez
yielded doses of methamphetamine and marijuana, cash, a short firearm, phones,
computers and a notebook listing women from whom extortion payments were
collected. [@3]

In Iztapalapa, a separate arrest order was carried out against a man identified
as Bryan N, described as the organisation's financial operator, who already
faced an arrest warrant for human trafficking and organised crime. [@2] Bryan N
was accused of providing properties to shelter trafficking victims and house
gang members. [@2] The federal security ministry said seven further arrest
warrants for human trafficking and organised crime were served on people already
held in prisons in Mexico City, Puebla and Nayarit. [@3]

The six detainees and the seized items were handed to the Public Prosecutor's
Office, which was to decide their legal situation, and the searched properties
were sealed and placed under police guard. [@3]
