Israel Police officers seized 42.5 kilograms of material suspected to be cannabis from an apartment in Kfar Shalem, in south Tel Aviv, on 7 January 2026 and arrested the two people who lived there. [@1,2] The suspects were a man of 52 and a woman of 41. [@1,2]

Detectives from the Tel Aviv South station in the Ayalon district and officers of the Tel Aviv Special Patrol Unit came with a search warrant. [@2] Inside the apartment they found sacks in a storage room, 2,110 shekels in cash and other evidence that police said pointed to drug dealing. [@1,2] Forensic investigators were called to examine the scene. [@2]

The two were taken for further investigation. [@1,2] Israel Hayom reported that at the end of the investigation a prosecutor's declaration was filed against the 52-year-old resident. [@3]
