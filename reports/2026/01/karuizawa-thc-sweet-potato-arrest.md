Kyoto Prefectural Police arrested unemployed 26-year-old Hayato Kato, who lives
in Karuizawa, Nagano Prefecture, on 27 January 2026 on suspicion of violating
Japan's Narcotics Control Act. [@1] According to the prefectural police, in
November 2025 Kato heated cannabis plants at his home in Karuizawa, extracted
the illegal compound THC, mixed it into unsalted butter, and then made 8 sweet
potatoes for profit. [@1]

Kato had been selling the sweet potatoes for 6,500 yen each on the messaging app
Telegram, which he used because of its secrecy. [@2] Kato had already been
arrested and charged for profit-motivated possession of cannabis and other
offences, and this new charge emerged from the same investigation. [@2] In
interrogation Kato denied the charges, saying the items were not made by him and
that he had no profit motive. [@2]
