Shawn Okeefe Bridgeman, 52, of Drax Hall Hope, St George, and Lenox Persid, 53,
of Mount Wilton, St Thomas, appeared before Magistrate Ward-Sargeant in the
Holetown Magistrates' Court. [@1] They were not required to plead to the
indictable offences. [@1] Both men were granted bail of $500,000 each with one
surety. [@1] Their cases were transferred to the District 'D' Magistrates' Court
for Tuesday, January 13. [@1]

The Barbados Police Service Serious Organised Crime Unit charged the men with
possession, trafficking and intent to supply cannabis. [@1] The alleged offences
were committed on December 12, 2025. [@1] Police put the suspected cannabis at
395.3 kilogrammes with an estimated street value of $1,581,200. [@1]
