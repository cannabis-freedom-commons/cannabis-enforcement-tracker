Michael Keylon Miller-Jimerson, 26, was sentenced on 6 January 2026 in Pierce
County Superior Court to 15 years in prison for second-degree murder in the 2022
robbery of the World of Weed dispensary in Tacoma. [@1] He had pleaded guilty to
second-degree murder for directing teenagers to rob the dispensary, where an
employee was shot and killed. [@1]

During the 19 March 2022 armed takeover of the shop at 3202 E. Portland Ave., a
15-year-old boy, Marshon Jones, fatally shot 29-year-old employee Jordan Brown
while Jones and Montrell Hatfield robbed the store. [@1] A third teenager stood
lookout while Miller-Jimerson waited in a car outside in case his accomplices
needed a backup getaway driver. [@1] The teens had escaped house arrest in King
County days earlier by disabling their ankle monitors. [@1]

Judge Susan Adams imposed an agreed recommendation from prosecutors and the
defense that was below Miller-Jimerson's standard sentencing range of about 21
to 30 years. [@1] Deputy prosecuting attorney Sunni Ko said the state
recommended the shorter term because it would need the testimony of Jones and
Hatfield, who refused to cooperate, to prove Miller-Jimerson acted as an
accomplice. [@1] Ko said the U.S. Attorney's Office for Western Washington
arranged a global disposition under which the federal sentence would run
concurrently with the state term. [@1]

Jones and Hatfield pleaded guilty to first-degree murder and multiple counts of
first-degree robbery in February 2024 and were each sentenced to 25 years in
state custody. [@1] Dennis Brown, the victim's father, said it mattered that the
adult involved in his son's murder was held accountable. [@1] Dennis Brown said:
None of it will bring Jordan back. [@1]
