National Police detained six alleged drug suppliers in districts and
municipalities of Managua during a wider anti-crime sweep. [@2] Police presented
the sweep as part of its weekly results covering 5 to 11 January 2026. [@2] The
same sweep also held suspects accused of homicide and robbery with intimidation,
force and violence. [@1]

As evidence from the Managua operation, police listed 2 pounds of marijuana,
about 0.907 kg, together with 130 grams of cocaine, 72 grams of crack and cash.
[@1] A second police account likewise lists 2 pounds of marijuana plus 130 grams
of cocaine, 72 grams of crack and cash as the Managua evidence. [@2] National
Police spokeswoman Comisionada Karen Obando announced the results. [@2]

Police used the term abastecedores de drogas for the six, meaning alleged drug
suppliers. [@1] The operation spanned districts and municipalities across the
capital area rather than a single named site. [@1] Police described the actions
as continuing work to guarantee protection and well-being of people, families
and communities. [@1]
