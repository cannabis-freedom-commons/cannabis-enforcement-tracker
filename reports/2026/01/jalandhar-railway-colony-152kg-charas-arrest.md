Jalandhar police arrested 25-year-old Mohammad Tanveer, a post-graduate student
from Bihar with alleged links to a Nepal drug trafficking racket, and recovered
15.2 kg of charas from him. [@1] Tanveer was produced before a court and
remanded in police custody for further investigation into the trafficking
network, according to Ravinder Kumar, Station House Officer of Navi Baradari
police station. [@1] Tanveer is an MA Sociology student and had previously been
involved in smuggling liquor from Punjab to Bihar, Kumar said. [@1] Kumar said
the arrest was made during a checking drive near the Railway Colony. [@1]

The police team noticed a young man approaching from the Bashirpura side who
became visibly nervous on spotting the officers. [@1] According to Kumar, the
suspect threw a white sack and tried to flee before the team overpowered him.
[@1] Questioned by police, Tanveer reportedly said he had brought around 15 kg
of charas from Nepal and concealed it in a suitcase. [@1]

Tanveer allegedly sourced the drugs from Nepal and was waiting in Jalandhar to
finalise a buyer transaction. [@2] Police said Tanveer remained in custody while
investigators verified his statements and traced the supply network and other
people involved. [@2] The South Asia Terrorism Portal recap says the FIR for the
case was registered on 25 December 2025 at Cyber Crime Police Station,
Chandigarh. [@2]
