Three men arrested in connection with an alleged €10.1 million cannabis
seizure in Co Louth appeared before a special sitting of Dundalk District
Court. [@1][@2]

Gardaí seized 506 kilograms of cannabis worth €10,120,000 after searching a
unit at Newtownstalaban, Drogheda, on Friday, January 30, 2026, arresting two
men in their 30s and one in his 50s. [@1,3][@2]

Graham Sommers, 37, of Moscow Drive, Liverpool; Brian Coleman, 38, of
Glasanaon Road, Finglas, Dublin 11; and Alan O'Neill, 56, of Coolfore House,
Hill of Rath, Drogheda, are each charged with unlawful possession of cannabis
and having the drug for sale or supply at the unit on Friday. [@1,3] The
defendants made no reply when the charges were put to them after caution on
Saturday, there were no bail applications, and Judge Stephanie Coggans
remanded them in custody to appear by videolink on Wednesday, February 4.
[@1]

The cannabis had been concealed in boxes of lettuce. [@3,4] Gardaí received confidential information about importation by a transnational criminal organisation on a Spanish-registered truck on a ferry from Cherbourg to Rosslare. [@3,4] Around 2pm the truck reversed into the warehouse where it was unloaded by forklift. [@3,4] When gardaí swooped at 4.15pm, the defendant and two other men were at the open back doors of a white van and wearing gloves. [@3,4] The defendant was arrested at the scene and brought to Drogheda Garda Station. [@3,4] Over a series of interviews, he eventually made full admissions, telling investigators that he had a €100,000 drugs debt. [@3] He had been paying off the debt at €200 per-week and by “doing someone else’s bidding” and had reduced what he owed to €48,000. [@3] The prosecuting garda detective said that he wasn’t the target of the operation. [@3] Counsel described Brian Coleman as a cog in the machine, coming in at a low level under a drugs debt. [@3] Judge Dara Hayes said that there will be a lengthy sentence as the case was adjourned for mention to January 11 for a probation report and for the outcome of a separate matter before Dublin Circuit Court. [@3,4]

Brian Coleman had come forward on signed pleas of guilty to possession, possession for sale or supply and possession for sale or supply of cannabis with a value of €13,000 or more at Newtownstalaban, Drogheda, on January 30 last. [@4] One of the men they found loading a van in a warehouse in Co. Louth appeared via video link for sentencing. [@4]
