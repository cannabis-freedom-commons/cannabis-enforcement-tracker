Siskiyou County sheriff's deputies arrested Shel Saunders, 55, of Fort Bragg,
after a traffic stop on a van carrying 1,363 pounds (about 618 kg) of illegally
cultivated marijuana flower and $253,870 in cash. [@1] Saunders was driving a
sprinter van holding 74 bags of marijuana and $253,870 in U.S. currency when
deputies pulled him over for a traffic violation. [@1] The sheriff's office said
the marijuana came from the Mount Shasta Vista area, an unincorporated community
in eastern Siskiyou County. [@2]

A probable-cause search found no paperwork or licence showing the marijuana was
legally grown or certified for sale on the legal market. [@1] Deputies also
found a .40 calibre Smith & Wesson handgun in the van's engine compartment. [@1]
The sheriff's office said it believed the cash came from sales of the illicit
marijuana. [@1]

Saunders was booked into the Siskiyou County jail on charges of transporting and
possessing marijuana for sale. [@2] The sheriff's office released its account of
the case on 27 January, eleven days later; Mendocino Coast News reported the
arrest took place on 16 January and noted the press release gave no date. [@3]
The same outlet said a check of the county's jail-population search showed
Saunders was no longer in custody and raised questions about the reported
weight, which it said the photo did not support; no court outcome has been
reported. [@3]
