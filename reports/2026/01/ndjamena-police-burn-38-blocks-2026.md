Chad's National Police incinerated seized prohibited goods on 15 January 2026.
[@3] The incineration took place at a site in Gassi, in the commune of
N'Djamena's 7th arrondissement. [@1] The action was supervised by Prosecutor
Moussa Abdelkerim Saleh, deputy police chief Mourhal Sahir, the anti-drug chief
and deputy mayor Florentine Ngaoudjé. [@1] The goods had been stored at the
Central Anti-Drug Office before destruction. [@3]

The destroyed lot included 38 blocks and 165 balls of cannabis. [@3] The same
inventory lists 10 sachets of “Colorado” alongside the cannabis, plus
adulterated alcohol, diazepam, tramadol, Exol, pregabalin and aphrodisiacs. [@2]
The lot also included 186 mobile phones, seized passports and identity cards,
and counterfeit notes including 217 100-dollar bills. [@2] The outlets give a
combined overall valuation of 101 625 000 CFA francs for all the destroyed goods
together. [@2]

Prosecutor Saleh said no one who endangers public health, the national economy
and citizens' security can escape the law. [@2] He said perpetrators,
co-perpetrators and accomplices will be systematically tracked down, arrested
and brought before the courts. [@3] Police presented the destruction as a
renewed call for citizen vigilance and cooperation with internal security
forces. [@2]
