On 28 January 2026, Assam Rifles carried out an anti-narcotics operation in the Boxanagar area of Sepahijala district, about 41 kilometres south of Agartala, in coordination with the district police, Tripura State Rifles and the Forest Department. [@1,2] The teams destroyed approximately 145,000 cannabis saplings cultivated across more than 58 acres, or roughly 23.47 hectares. [@1,2]

Northeast Herald reported that officials estimated the destroyed crop at about ₹14.5 crore. [@1] NorthEast Now said the destruction followed intelligence inputs, was conducted in the presence of civil administration and police representatives, and resulted in no arrests. [@2]
