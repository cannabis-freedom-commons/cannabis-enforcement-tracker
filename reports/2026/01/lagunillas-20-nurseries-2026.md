Bolivia's anti-drug forces destroyed 20 marijuana nurseries near the community
of Lagunillas, in Rafael Bustillos province, northern Potosí department, the
deputy ministry for controlled substances (Viceministerio de Sustancias
Controladas) reported. [@1] The operation was carried out in the early hours of
Monday 19 January 2026. [@1] Officers reached the site along a bridle path and
found sophisticated irrigation machinery in the nurseries. [@1]

Each nursery covered about 700 square metres, a total of roughly 1.4 hectares,
with marijuana plants up to three metres tall at four plants per square metre.
[@1] The deputy ministry calculated each nursery's potential at 11.9 tonnes, a
total of 238 tonnes of marijuana destroyed, of which 71.4 tonnes were the net
weight in its natural state before processing for trafficking. [@1] The deputy
ministry put the economic damage to drug trafficking at 10.7 million dollars.
[@1]

The whole plantation was incinerated, with the Public Prosecutor's Office
present, and during the burning local residents set off dynamite near the
intervention area. [@1] The deputy ministry said more than 60 active nurseries,
many with advanced irrigation and high yields, had already been recorded in the
same region in 2025, which it said suggested a consolidated criminal
organisation rooted in national drug-trafficking circuits. [@1]
