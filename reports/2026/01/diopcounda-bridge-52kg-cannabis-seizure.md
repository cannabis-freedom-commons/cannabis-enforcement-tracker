Gendarmes from the Sédhiou brigade seized 52 kilograms of chanvre indien, the
name used in Senegal for cannabis, of the variety called green, from a courier
at the Diopcounda bridge. [@1] The bridge lies between the commune of Dianah Ba,
in Sédhiou department, and the commune of Karantaba, in Goudomp department. [@1]
The correspondent described that crossing as a strategic route for traffickers
trying to reach Guinea-Bissau. [@1]

The stop followed information that a courier was coming from North Sindian and
travelling toward Guinea-Bissau. [@1] Besides the cannabis, the gendarmes seized
the motorcycle used for the illicit transport. [@1] The suspect was immediately
placed in garde à vue, police custody, for the investigation, and was then
referred to the prosecutor. [@1]

After meeting the prosecutor, the trafficker was placed under a mandat de dépôt,
a remand warrant, at the Maison d'arrêt et de correction of Sédhiou, the local
detention and correctional centre. [@1] The correspondent presented the seizure
as part of closer checks on this cross-border route. [@1]
