A mobile patrol of the Mauritanian National Gendarmerie seized an important
quantity of cannabis rolling leaves on Wednesday evening as they were on their
way to Nouakchott. [@1] The rolling leaves were found on one person at the
entrance to the village of Khouara, on the Tiguint-Rosso road, while that person
was trying to smuggle them toward Nouakchott. [@1]

The gendarmerie said the operation formed part of its efforts to control
Mauritania's roads and to fight smuggling and smugglers. [@1] Sahara Médias
reported the seizure on 15 January 2026, the day after it happened, under a
headline describing an important quantity of cannabis seized on the
Rosso-Nouakchott axis by a mobile brigade of the national gendarmerie. [@2]
