Police arrested two people at Suresh Street, Chaguanas, during an anti-crime
operation in Area North of the Central Division on Friday. [@1] One arrested
person was held for possession of cocaine and the second for possession of
cocaine for the purpose of trafficking. [@1] The arrested individuals were taken
to a police station in the Central Division to assist with investigations. [@1]

In a separate search, officers searched a bushy area off Tobago Road Extension,
Enterprise. [@1] Officers found four rectangular packages wrapped in brown tape
at that location. [@1] The packages contained cannabis with a combined weight of
three kilograms. [@1] The cannabis was seized and taken into police custody.
[@1]

Police said enquiries are continuing in relation to the seizures and arrests.
[@1]
