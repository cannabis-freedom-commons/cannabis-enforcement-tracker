On 29 January 2026, India Today NE reported that Assam Rifles carried out two coordinated anti-narcotics operations in Tripura, including one near Kamalnagar and Bijoy Nagar in Sepahijala district valued at ₹27 crore. [@1] Hindustan Times identified the same operation as a joint raid in the forest areas of Kamalanagar, Krishnadola, Dulunga and Bijoy Nagar, from 7:45 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., during which personnel destroyed 41 plantation plots and approximately 180,000 cannabis plants across nearly 65 acres. [@2]

The operation involved Sonamura Police Station, the 81st Battalion BSF, the 5th, 9th, 11th and 14th TSR battalions, the 14th Battalion Women TSR, the 35th Battalion Assam Rifles and other security agencies. [@2] Sentinel Assam separately reported the Kamalnagar–Bijoynagar destruction and the estimated ₹27-crore value, with participation from Sepahijala police, the Forest Department, BSF and TSR. [@3]
