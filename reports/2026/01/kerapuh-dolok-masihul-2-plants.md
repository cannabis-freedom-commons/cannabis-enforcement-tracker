On 6 January 2026, Serdang Bedagai police went to a house in Dusun II, Kerapuh, after receiving information about cannabis cultivation and found two approximately two-metre plants behind the house. [@1,2]

Police arrested three men, identified in the reports by their initials, and took them and the plants to Dolok Masihul police for further examination under Indonesia's narcotics law. [@1,2]
