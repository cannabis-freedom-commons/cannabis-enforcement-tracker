Alcohol Law Enforcement officers arrested Ahmed Almotaser, 42, and Mohammed
Ahmed, 45. [@1] The investigation brought charges against store owner Ahmed
Almontaser, 42, and his business partner Mohammed Ahmed, 45, both from Clayton.
[@1] Four of five men named in marijuana-conspiracy charges had already been
arrested. [@2] Yaser Abduljalil Mohammed Al Wesabi, Saleh Ali Saleh Hasan and
Thabet Alahmedi were also named as conspirators. [@2] Al Wesabi and Hasan were
also arrested, while Alahmedi had not been arrested when that account was
published. [@2] Two employees who were in control of the shops when they were
searched were issued criminal summonses. [@2] Court records show both men have
bonded out of jail. [@1] Almontaser, Ahmed and Al Wesabi were released on total
bonds of $155,000, $120,000 and $20,000. [@2]

Search warrants say six minors were sick for days after taking illegal THC
gummies. [@1] The minors bought the gummies in December at Prime Vape Shop on
Highway 42 in Johnston County. [@1] Alcohol Law Enforcement received the
complaint on 10 December 2025. [@2] An undercover purchase at Prime Vape tested
at 0.37% THC in the gummies and 19% THC in the flower, both above the 0.3% legal
limit. [@1] Undercover agents also tried to buy the flower at Clayton Tobacco
Shop on US-70, another store Almontaser owns. [@1] Flower from that shop also
tested above the legal limit. [@1] Two of those flower products measured 11.78%
THC and 14.55% THC. [@2] A clerk told an undercover agent the flower would test
legal when they get it, but is not legal when you smoke it. [@1]

Agents with the North Carolina Alcohol Law Enforcement Division executed search
warrants at five locations in Johnston County. [@3] One address was Prime Vape &
Tobacco, 5533 N.C. Highway 42 West, Unit A-18, Garner. [@3] Another was Clayton
Tobacco Shop, 11514 U.S. 70 Business (Clayton Boulevard), Clayton. [@3] Agents
seized more than 17 grams of loose marijuana from the Clayton store, among other
items. [@1] Searches of properties owned by Almontaser and Ahmed also took other
substances, cash and guns. [@1] The searches also found over 3,400 grams of
cathinone, known as khat, which is not cannabis and is not counted here. [@2]
The conspiracy charges include a school-zone count because Clayton Tobacco Shop
is within 1,000 feet of Clayton High School. [@2]

Almontaser faces 32 charges total and Ahmed faces 19 drug-related charges. [@1]
A court-records account lists 28 felony charges and 7 misdemeanor charges
against Almontaser. [@2] A representative for Ahmed denies any wrongdoing. [@1]
Both men were expected back in court on Thursday, March 5, at 9 a.m. [@1]
