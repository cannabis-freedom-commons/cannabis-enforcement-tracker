Judicial investigators from the OIJ in San Carlos raided three homes in Boca
Arenal and barrio Managuita before dawn on 27 January 2026, at about 4:00 a.m.,
as part of an investigation under the psychotropics law. [@1] Officers detained
seven people, six men and one woman, on suspicion of selling drugs in the area.
[@1] The detainees were three men surnamed Mairena, aged 20, 24 and 48, a man
surnamed Porras, 35, two men surnamed Pineda, aged 34 and 57, and a woman
surnamed Sequeira, 49. [@1]

The investigation had begun about a month earlier after agents received
information that the suspects were apparently selling crack, marijuana and
cocaine both by express delivery and from the raided houses. [@1] CRHoy linked
the three raids to cases called Cañal, Las Latas and Mecánico. [@3] CRHoy
reported that the group was investigated for alleged retail sale of cocaine,
crack and marijuana. [@3] During the operation agents seized drugs and other
evidence relevant to the investigation, including cash and apparent drugs. [@4]
The San Carlos municipal police and the Directorate of Criminal Intelligence and
Analysis assisted in the operation. [@1]

The seven detainees were placed at the disposal of the Public Ministry, which
will determine their legal situation as the case proceeds. [@1] Diario Extra
independently gave the same account of seven detainees, six men and one woman,
suspected of selling crack, marijuana and cocaine by express delivery and from
the targeted properties. [@2]
