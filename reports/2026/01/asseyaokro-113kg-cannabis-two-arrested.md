Ivory Coast's national police arrested two men at Asseyaokro village in Bouaké
on 14 January 2026, as announced that day by the Directorate General of the
National Police. [@1] The two suspects were identified as Z.K.I., 34, and
Z.S.H., 32. [@1] A search on the spot recovered 113 kilograms of cannabis and a
12-gauge hunting rifle, according to the DGPN. [@1] Both men were handed to the
competent services for the investigation and the court proceedings provided by
law. [@1]

The Bouaké branch of the DPSD, Ivory Coast's drugs and narcotics police, carried
out the operation after local residents reported suspicious cannabis activity in
the village. [@1] Police presented the operation as part of the national
police's continuing efforts against drug trafficking and use to protect public
safety and health. [@1]

Ivoirematin also reported that police arrested the same Z.K.I., 34, and Z.S.H.,
32, at Asseyaokro after residents denounced suspicious cannabis activity. [@2]
Ivoirematin also reported the seizure of 113 kilograms of cannabis and the
discovery of a 12-gauge hunting rifle in the village search. [@2]

Ivoirematin said the operation was led by the Bouaké DPSD under Opération
Épervier XI after a tip from residents. [@2]

Informateur.ci placed the arrests in Asseyaokro village in the commune of Bouaké
and said the DGPN made the information public on 14 January 2026. [@3]

Informateur.ci said officers put the area under surveillance before a targeted
descent on the indicated premises. [@3]

Informateur.ci described the haul as about 113 kilograms of carefully packaged
cannabis plus a 12-gauge hunting rifle found in the suspects' premises. [@3]

Informateur.ci said the two men are suspected of involvement in cannabis
cultivation, possession and sale in the area and must answer before the courts.
[@3]
