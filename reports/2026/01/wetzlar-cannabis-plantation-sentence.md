A man rented a house in Wetzlar to build and operate a cannabis plantation. [@2]
He received electricity and gas for the house from summer 2019 to summer 2021.
[@2] He was arrested in June 2021 for growing marijuana. [@3] Limburg Regional
Court sentenced him to a combined term of three years and four months for the
cultivation. [@3]

At the end the plantation filled almost all rooms of the house. [@3] He had
broken the meter seals and blocked its turntable to hide the load. [@2] The
ticker noted the estimated use equals what an average four-person household uses
in more than 60 years. [@1]

The court said a supplier whose meter has been bypassed may estimate the
unrecorded consumption. [@2] It said the penalty for unauthorised use is meant
to prevent further abuse. [@2] Only a complaint seeking leave to appeal to the
Federal Court of Justice remains possible. [@3] Criminal findings said early
setbacks and a heavy fly infestation delayed harvests until the end of 2020.
[@2]
