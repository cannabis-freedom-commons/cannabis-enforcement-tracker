Guatemala's National Civil Police (PNC), through its Subdirectorate General for Anti-Narcotics Information Analysis (SGAIA), carried out the first cannabis eradication of 2026 near Las Cruces, Petén, on Thursday 1 January, according to the Ministry of the Interior's later chronology. [@2] Officers eradicated and incinerated 23,439 cannabis plants at the site. [@1,2] Police assessed the crop at Q8,789,625. [@1,2]

Prensa Libre reported the same count and valuation in an article dated 1 January; it placed the operation in areas adjoining Las Cruces and embedded a PNC post dated 2 January. [@1] The Ministry's 10 January roundup also records the first destruction on 1 January near the municipality and gives the same count and value. [@2] Neither account provides an exact field address or cultivated area, and neither states whether anyone was arrested. [@1,2] The Ministry's article later lists separate eradications on other January dates; those are outside this report. [@2]

The plant count and valuation are official police estimates, not independent measurements. [@1,2]
