Ahmedabad Special Operations Group (SOG) Crime Branch arrested an international
narcotics smuggler who had escaped a Nepalese prison in Gujarat. [@1,2] The
fugitive was identified as Dharmesh Rasiklal Chunara, who had been on the run
since a jailbreak in Kathmandu. [@1]

Chunara is a resident of the Thakkarbapa Nagar area in Ahmedabad and was
intercepted at Kathmandu Airport in July 2024. [@1] He had arrived on flight
TG-319 from Bangkok carrying 13 kilograms of high-grade hybrid cannabis, or
ganja. [@1,2] Police put the haul's estimated street value at Rs 13 crore, and he
was imprisoned in Kathmandu's Bhadra Jail under the NDPS Act. [@1,2]

In September 2025 Nepal was rocked by Gen-Z protests, during which protesters
stormed and vandalized Bhadra Jail. [@1,2] Several inmates, including Chunara,
broke through the facility's defenses and fled in the resulting anarchy. [@1]
Nepal shared a list of fugitive Indian nationals with the Sashastra Seema Bal in
Delhi, which was circulated to state police, and a dedicated team was formed
under CID Crime guidance to track him. [@1] Police Sub-Inspector JB Desai's team
used human intelligence and technical analysis to pinpoint Chunara's location in
Ahmedabad after initial records lacked his full address. [@1] On interrogation
after being intercepted in Ahmedabad, Chunara admitted slipping away in the
prison riot and crossing the Sonauli border back into India to hide in his
hometown. [@1] The Ahmedabad SOG Crime Branch took him into custody and is
initiating legal procedures following the recapture. [@1]

Officers learnt he was hiding at his mother-in-law's house in the Ahmedabad area and moved in to arrest him. [@2] Although no fresh criminal case had been registered against him in Ahmedabad, police said his role in international drug smuggling is under active scrutiny. [@2] Officers are probing whether earlier consignments made it into India and whether he was part of a larger cross-border narcotics network. [@2] Authorities shared details of multiple Indian escapees, two of them from Gujarat including one from Surat, and efforts are on to trace the others now that he is in custody. [@2] Police said he will be handed over to central agencies, after which formal procedures will begin to extradite him back to Nepal to face trial. [@2]
