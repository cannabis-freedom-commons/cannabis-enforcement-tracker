The suspect is originally from and lives in El Paraíso department. [@2] Officers
caught him during a pursuit after he allegedly tried to flee when he noticed the
officers. [@2] He was riding a motorcycle when he lost control of the vehicle
and fractured an arm. [@2] He was then taken to a health centre for medical
care. [@2] The National Police said it had captured an alleged member of Los
Nikson in El Paraíso. [@1] He and the seized evidence were to be referred to the
local Public Ministry prosecution to continue the legal process. [@2]

They also seized a pistol with its magazine and 10 unfired cartridges, along
with a motorcycle of Nicaraguan origin. [@1]

The competent authorities are to determine his responsibility through the
process established by law. [@2]
