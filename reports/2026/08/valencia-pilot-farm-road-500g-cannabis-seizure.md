The operation took place between 7 pm and 11.30 pm on August 3. [@1] Police
seized about 500 grammes of cannabis. [@1]

The officers acted on intelligence that illegal firearms had been hidden in
vacant fruit stalls along the Eastern Main Road in Valencia. [@1] At about 10.15
pm they searched a shed near Pilot Farm Road. [@1]

No arrests were reported up to the time of the police release. [@1]
Investigations are continuing as police work to determine who concealed the
firearm, ammunition and cannabis at the location. [@1]
