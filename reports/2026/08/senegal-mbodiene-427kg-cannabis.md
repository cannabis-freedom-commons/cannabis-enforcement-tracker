A cart driver was arrested and a second man escaped after gendarmes seized 427 kg of cannabis on the coast at Mbodiène on August 29, 2026. [@1,2] The operation followed intelligence about a planned landing, and units from Joal, Nianing and Mbour joined the effort to intercept the shipment. [@1,2]

## The interception

The gendarmerie said a pirogue reached shore at about 10 p.m., then a cart arrived to move the cargo. [@1] Officers spotted it about 500 metres from the crossroads between Mbodiène and Ndianda, carrying fifteen sacks and two people armed with machetes. [@1,2] One man fled after officers called on them to stop; the cart driver resisted before gendarmes subdued and arrested him. [@1,2] The gendarmerie reported that the cannabis weighed 427 kg. [@1,2]

## Investigation

The gendarmerie said the investigation was continuing. [@1,2] Neither report identifies the shipment's owner or destination, and no later charge or court outcome is given. [@1,2]
