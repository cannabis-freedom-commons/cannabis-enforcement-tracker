Nabeul's anti-drug brigade dismantled a drug-trafficking network operating in
Hammamet and arrested two members who were actively sought for drug cases. [@1]
The public prosecutor at the Grombalia Court of First Instance ordered the two
suspects to be held in custody while the investigation continues. [@1]
Investigators said efforts are continuing to identify and arrest the network's
other accomplices. [@1]

The arrests followed a search of one suspect's home in Hammamet carried out
after officers obtained the required legal and judicial authorisations. [@2]
During that search officers seized cannabis that was packaged and ready for
resale, described in one account as cannabis resin known locally as zatla. [@3]
In the same search officers also seized a quantity of ecstasy tablets and a
pistol. [@2]

A security source reported the operation to the TAP news agency. [@3] La Presse
identifies the arresting unit as the Nabeul regional anti-drug brigade under the
Gorjani sub-directorate. [@3] A judicial investigation was opened to establish
the full membership of the network and bring any remaining suspects to justice.
[@2]
