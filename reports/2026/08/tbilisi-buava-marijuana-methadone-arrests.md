Georgia's Interior Ministry detained three people in the investigation into the
death of Abkhaz blogger Daur Buava, who it said died after using drugs. [@1] One
of the three, a Ukrainian citizen, was arrested on Saturday and charged with
abandoning a person in a life-threatening situation. [@3] Investigators said the
pair used drugs together and both felt unwell. [@1] The arrested woman,
identified only as V.K., left Buava alone, locked the flat and drove away
without telling anyone about his condition. [@1] She was charged under Article
128 of Georgia's Criminal Code, which covers leaving a person in danger. [@1]

Two other people were arrested on Sunday, a day after the woman, on drug-related
charges. [@3] Their arrests followed searches of two homes in Tbilisi carried
out under the investigation into Buava's death. [@2] During the searches police
seized several types of narcotics, including marijuana and methadone, in 10
packages. [@3] The ministry said one of the detainees had given narcotics free
of charge to the woman, who used drugs with Buava before leaving him in a
life-threatening condition. [@2]

Buava was found dead in his rented flat in Tbilisi's Didi Dighomi district on 27
August, days after his death, after neighbours were alerted by a strong odour.
[@2] Investigators said Buava lived alone in the rented flat in Didi Dighomi.
[@3] A chemical examination found that Buava had used methadone and drunk
alcohol, and methadone was also found in his home, the prosecutor's office said.
[@3] Buava's lawyers dispute the investigation's drug-death account and have
commissioned an alternative expert examination. [@3]
