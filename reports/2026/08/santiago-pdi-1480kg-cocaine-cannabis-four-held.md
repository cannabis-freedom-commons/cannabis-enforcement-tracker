Chile's investigative police (PDI) broke up three drug-trafficking bands
bringing drugs from Bolivia and detained four people during searches in the
Santiago Metropolitan Region. [@1] The detainees were two Chilean citizens and
two Bolivians. [@1] The three procedures were called Operación “Mar de Drake
II”, “Norte Rojo II” and “Capitanes”. [@1] One of the Chilean detainees was
Sebastián Soto Guerra, 33, described as a member of the band “Los Pájaros de La
Legua” and the father-in-law of Boca Juniors footballer Carlos Palacios. [@1] He
was caught with 75 kilos of coca paste and had been using a vehicle registered
to the footballer to move the drug. [@1]

Police seized 1,480 kilos of drugs, which included cannabis sativa. [@1] Police
valued the seized drugs at 18,015,300,000 pesos, about USD 19.7 million. [@1]
PDI director general Eduardo Cerna said officers also seized five vehicles, two
handguns and a rifle, and froze more than eight bank accounts. [@1] Security
minister Martín Arrau said breaking up national and foreign bands and taking
their money and weapons reduced the groups that frighten neighbourhoods. [@1]

One of the properties searched in the Santiago commune of La Florida is
registered to the footballer's partner. [@1] The Metropolitan South regional
prosecutor, Héctor Barros, said police would not rule out investigating the
footballer, who could be charged or called as a witness. [@1]
