Under the national 'Frontera Segura' plan, FELCN officers carried out three
interdiction operations along the Bioceanic route in Santa Cruz, concentrated in
the border municipalities of Roboré, Puerto Suárez and the Puerto Quijarro bus
terminal. [@1] In the first operation, officers inspected a passenger bus and
identified two occupants holding five packages of cocaine hydrochloride. [@1]
The two suspects were immediately apprehended and the cocaine load was seized.
[@1]

In a second operation focused on parcel inspections, officers found 16 packages
of marijuana concealed inside a boxing bag and a metal safe. [@1] In a third
operation at the Puerto Quijarro bus terminal, officers found a cardboard box
containing a metal safe, and on opening it in the presence of the driver,
identified four additional packages of marijuana. [@1] Red Uno reported the same
three operations, describing the first as a search of an interprovincial bus in
which officers found two passengers carrying five rectangular packages of
cocaine, and immediately apprehending both suspects. [@2] eju.tv, reprinting El
Deber, reported the same three operations and noted the marijuana packages
field-tested positive. [@3]
