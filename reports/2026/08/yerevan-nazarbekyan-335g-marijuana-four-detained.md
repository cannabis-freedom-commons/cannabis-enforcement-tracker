Police said they had received operative information that four men, two residents
of Yerevan and two residents of Kotayk Province, were illegally acquiring and
selling drugs. [@1] Police detained all four on 29 July on Nazarbekyan Street in
Yerevan and took them, together with the car driven by a 33-year-old Yerevan
resident, to the Mashtots department of the Community Police. [@1]

The men admitted that they had acquired the drugs through the Telegram app in
order to sell them. [@1] A criminal case was opened, and a search of the Neta
car found a packet of a marijuana-like mass weighing 335 grams. [@1]

On 1 August a search of the home of a 29-year-old Yerevan resident found 30
tape-wrapped packets with a combined mass of more than 20 grams of a
marijuana-like substance. [@1]
