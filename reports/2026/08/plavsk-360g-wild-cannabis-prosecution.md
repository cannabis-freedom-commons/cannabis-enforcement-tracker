Prosecutors in Plavsky District approved the indictment against a 43-year-old
local man accused of buying and keeping drugs in a large amount without
intending to sell them. [@1] Investigators say that by 16 August 2026 the man
had collected parts of wild cannabis plants in Plavsky District. [@1] He is said
to have carried the cannabis with him in a polymer bag for his own use. [@1]

Police officers stopped him and seized 360 grams of the drug during a personal
search. [@1] The case has been sent to the Plavsky inter-district court for
trial on the merits. [@1]

A second Tula outlet reports that the charge is illegal acquisition and storage
of drugs in a large amount, citing the Tula Oblast prosecutor's office. [@2]
That account dates the collection of the wild cannabis to 16 August 2026 and
says police found and seized the 360 grams when they searched him. [@2] The case
was sent to the Plavsky inter-district court for consideration on the merits,
the outlet reports. [@2]
