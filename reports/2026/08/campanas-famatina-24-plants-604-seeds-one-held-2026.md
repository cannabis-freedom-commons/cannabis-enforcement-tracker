Gendarmeria officers searched a home in barrio Tinti, in Campanas in the
Famatina department of La Rioja, under a court order following an investigation
into alleged marijuana cultivation and sale. [@1] An adult was detained in
connection with the case. [@1] The Federal Court of La Rioja and the Federal
Prosecutor's Office of La Rioja took charge of the case. [@1]

Officers seized 24 cannabis sativa plants and seedlings during the search of the
home. [@1] Officers also seized 604 marijuana seeds in the same search. [@1]
They also took a grinder, a digital precision scale, items intended for
cultivation and a computer. [@1]

The operation was led by the Chilecito complex-crimes investigation section with
support from Squadron 24 units, the La Rioja flight section, Squadron 58 La
Rioja and the provincial police anti-narcotics unit. [@1]
