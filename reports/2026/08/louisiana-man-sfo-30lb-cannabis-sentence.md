Rafe Paul Grant, a 50-year-old man from Louisiana, has been sentenced to jail
after staff at San Francisco International Airport found 30 pounds of marijuana
in his suitcase before he tried to board a flight to the Netherlands, San Mateo
County prosecutors said Thursday. [@1]

In February 2025, Grant checked his bag at SFO with a ticket on a KLM Royal
Dutch Airlines flight to Amsterdam. [@1] A random bag check found the drugs, and
a U.S. Customs and Border Protection agent detained him. [@1]
