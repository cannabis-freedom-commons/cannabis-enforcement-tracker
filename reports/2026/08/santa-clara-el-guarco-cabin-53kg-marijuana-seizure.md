Costa Rica's Judicial Investigation Agency raided a cabin used as a drug
storehouse in Santa Clara, El Guarco, Cartago, on the evening of Monday 3 August
2026. [@2] Four men aged 34 to 39, surnamed Abarca, Fuentes, Orozco and Araya,
were caught inside the cabin with the drugs. [@2] Officers found 1,092 one-kilo
packages of cocaine and 53 packages of marijuana inside the cabin. [@2] Agents
also found 2.5 million colones and several mobile phones in the cabin. [@2] They
also seized three vehicles, including a medium cargo truck, all fitted with
secret compartments for moving drugs. [@2]

Colombia's National Police said the same Cartago seizure, made through its
Anti-Narcotics Directorate with DEA support and Costa Rica's specialized
anti-drugs unit, totalled 1,092 kilos of cocaine and 53 kilos of marijuana. [@1]
The investigation began with confidential information about a large load moving
from the south of the country toward San Jose with an intermediate storehouse.
[@2] The agency described the cabin as part of a trafficking route used to hide
and cool large stashes closer to the capital before moving them onward. [@2]
Colombia's police presented the operation under its Esmeralda Plus strategy.
[@1] The same load was described as bound for international markets. [@1] The
police said the seizure cost traffickers more than USD 36.4 million and stopped
millions of doses reaching buyers. [@1]
