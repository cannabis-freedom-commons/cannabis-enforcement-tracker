Border Guard Bangladesh's Rangpur Battalion (51 BGB) carried out two separate
anti-smuggling operations on the Rangpur border, recovering Indian cannabis and
netting and seizing six smuggled cows. [@1] The same account is carried by
Alokito Bangladesh, which dates it to 21 August 2026. [@2] At about 10 p.m. on
20 August, a Gomnati border outpost patrol acting on a tip-off operated about
200 yards inside Bangladesh near border pillar 789/9-S in the Uttar Gomnati
area. [@1] The smugglers sensed the patrol and fled, abandoning cannabis and
netting they had brought from India. [@1]

A search of the spot recovered 100 grams of Indian ganja and 40 feet of current
netting. [@1] At about 4 a.m. on 21 August, a Panbari border outpost patrol
learned that smugglers were trying to enter Bangladesh from India with cows and
took position about 250 yards inside Bangladesh at Swadhin Bangla Chattar near
pillar DAMP 4/9-S. [@1] When several people arrived from India with six cows,
the patrol challenged them and they fled back toward India, leaving the animals,
which were taken to Panbari border outpost. [@1]

BGB put the six Indian cows at about 450,000 taka and put the combined seizure
value of the two operations at the same 450,000 taka. [@1] BGB said such
anti-smuggling operations on the border would continue. [@1]
