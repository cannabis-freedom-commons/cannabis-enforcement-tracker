The Edo State Command of the NDLEA destroyed two cannabis plantations at Ugboku in the Igbanke Forest, Orhionmwon Local Government Area, with a combined estimated yield of 4,628.98 kg, state commander Mitchell Ofoyeju disclosed on 17 August 2026. [@1,2] The operation followed credible intelligence and an operational order from the agency chairman, Brig. Gen. Mohamed Buba Marwa, to dislodge drug locations nationwide. [@1,2]

The first farm measured 0.699792 hectares with an estimated yield of 1,749.26 kg, and 40-year-old Agholor Elebojie was arrested there. [@1,2] The second farm measured 1.151889 hectares with an estimated yield of 2,879.72 kg, and officers recovered two bags of processed cannabis weighing 12.4 kg from it. [@1,2]

In linked city operations, officers arrested 28-year-old Kingsley Anigala at Oluku on 14 August with 25.857 kg of cannabis and 93 g of methamphetamine, and nine suspects at Abuja Quarters in Benin City on 13 August. [@1,2] Activist Omoyele Sowore criticised the destruction and called on Nigeria to legalise and regulate cannabis instead. [@1]
