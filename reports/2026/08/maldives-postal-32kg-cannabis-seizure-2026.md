Maldives Customs seized 3.2kg of cannabis that was allegedly being smuggled into
the country through the postal service. [@1] Officers found the drugs on
Saturday during a search of a postal package suspected of containing drugs. [@1]

Customs said the package was discovered during a routine inspection of a
suspicious postal package and further examination confirmed cannabis. [@2]

Authorities released no further details and it remains unclear whether any
arrests were made in connection with the shipment. [@2] Authorities urged the
public to remain vigilant because innocent people have previously faced criminal
allegations after agreeing to collect suspicious packages for others. [@2]
