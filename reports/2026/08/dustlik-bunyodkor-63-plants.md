Law-enforcement officers found 63 wild cannabis plants on 5 August 2026 on a farmland plot in Bunyodkor mahalla, Dustlik District, Jizzakh Region. [@1,2]

The plot belonged to a 57-year-old local resident, and the plants were described as growing wild. [@1,2] After procedural documents were prepared, officials destroyed all 63 plants under the legal procedure. [@1,2]
