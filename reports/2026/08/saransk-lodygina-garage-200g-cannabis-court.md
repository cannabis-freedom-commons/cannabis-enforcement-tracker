trial in Mordovia. [@1] Police searched his garage on Lodygina Street in Saransk
on 26 August. [@1] Inside the garage, officers found three wraps of marijuana
and a plastic bottle with burn marks. [@1] On an abandoned plot near the
building, police seized 10 bushes of a drug-containing plant, which the ministry
said belonged to him. [@1]

as 200 grams. [@2] The man told police he had accidentally come across a patch
of wild hemp some time earlier and pulled out several stems. [@2] He said he
brought the rest of the harvest to the garage, dried it and packaged it for
personal use. [@2]
