The five suspects were arrested late on the night of Wednesday 19 August in the
Ramgarhtal area. [@1] A joint ANTF and UP ATS team intercepted an Ertiga bearing
registration number GJ-15 AA-8841 near the LIC office on Deoria Bypass late on
Wednesday, August 19, and officers recovered hydroponic OG cannabis from the
accused during a search of the vehicle. [@1] Police identified the arrested men
as Suraj Singh and Satyam Singh of Karmahi village in Kandhai, Pratapgarh,
Prince Patel of Grid Road, Navsari, Ratnam Trivedi of Katargam, Surat, and
Bhavin of Daman Chowk, Bhavnagar. [@1] The ANTF Gorakhpur unit in-charge lodged
a first information report for alleged drug trafficking at Ramgarhtal police
station. [@1]

Police recovered 3.085 kg of hydroponic cannabis, which the ANTF estimated to be
worth around Rs 1.40 crore. [@1] Officers also seized the Ertiga car, five
mobile phones and Rs 30,390 in cash. [@1] According to sources, the high-value
cannabis was allegedly being smuggled into Gujarat and supplied to clubs and
discos, where it was sold to young people at premium prices. [@1]

During interrogation, the accused allegedly said the hydroponic cannabis sourced
from Thailand was being transported to Gujarat via Delhi. [@1] In Surat and
Bhavnagar, the cannabis was allegedly divided into smaller packets and sold to
users at high prices, while investigators suspect supplies were delivered to
clubs and discos based on demand. [@1] During questioning, the accused allegedly
told investigators that a youth had supplied the OG cannabis in a vehicle
bearing a Deoria registration number. [@1] The supplier's identity and address
have not yet been established, while ATS and ANTF teams have found indications
pointing to foreign links in the suspected smuggling network. [@1] Investigators
are now trying to establish how the cannabis allegedly travelled from Thailand
to India and identify the channels used to transport it further. [@1] The
accused's mobile phones are being examined to trace their contacts and the wider
network. [@1]
