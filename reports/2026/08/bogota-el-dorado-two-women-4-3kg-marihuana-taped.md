Police at Bogotá's El Dorado International Airport stopped two women carrying
drug packages taped to their bodies, one of them Colombian and the other a
foreign national. [@1] Security cameras at El Dorado detected unusual movement
in two passengers, which led officers to intervene. [@1]

Officers found 18 packages taped to the women's bodies whose contents added up
to 5,445 grams of cocaine and 4,315 grams of marijuana. [@1] El Espectador
reported the same split and the same handover of the two women and the drugs to
the Attorney General's Office. [@2]

The packages were stuck to the passengers' bodies and were found during a
personal search. [@1] The load was destined for Cali, a domestic route inside
Colombia. [@1]

Both women were handed to the Attorney General's Office, accused of
manufacturing, trafficking in and carrying narcotics. [@1] The police said
technological surveillance in air terminals was key to preventing the transfer
of the substances and securing the seizure. [@1] Authorities were investigating
whether a wider network stood behind the attempted transfer. [@1]

On 16 September 2026 two Venezuelan women were arrested at the same airport with
marijuana and cocaine strapped to their bodies, and a report on that second case
described the August detention as an identical earlier procedure. [@3]
