The Anti-Drug Division of the Bolivarian National Police seized 600 parcels of
marijuana, given as 300 kilograms, in a sector called El Sindicato near Mauroa,
on the border between the states of Falcón and Zulia. [@1] In total, the
officers took 12 bales of marijuana from the two men. [@1]

Two citizens, Yorfran Enríquez Meléndez Chirinos, aged 26, and Darwin Rafael
Mosquera, aged 29, were detained during the operation. [@1] The two men were
allegedly entrusted with moving the stash on two motorcycles, which officers
retained. [@1]

The commission also collected two AR-15 assault rifles, a .45 calibre Glock
pistol, magazines and ammunition. [@1] The case was placed at the disposal of
the Public Prosecutor's Office. [@1]
