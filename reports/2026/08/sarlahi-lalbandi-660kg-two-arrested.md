A police team on regular patrol in Sarlahi district arrested two people with
more than 660 kilograms of marijuana. [@1] The Nepal Police newsroom identified
the arrested pair as Nar Bahadur Thing, 63, and Jesun Maya Thing, 52. [@1] Both
arrested people are residents of Lalbandi Municipality-14. [@1] The pair were
arrested with 660 kilograms of marijuana on Wednesday. [@1]

Police figures put the total Sarlahi seizure at 660 kilograms of marijuana. [@1]
Police recovered 560 kilograms of marijuana from Nar Bahadur's house. [@1]
Police recovered another 100 kilograms of marijuana from Jesun Maya's cowshed.
[@1] Together, the 560 kilograms and the 100 kilograms make up the reported
total of 660 kilograms. [@1]

The marijuana was stored at a house and a cowshed when officers recovered it.
[@1] The Nepal Police newsroom is the authority cited for the names, ages,
address and amounts in this case. [@1] The two Lalbandi residents were held with
the drugs during the regular patrol operation. [@1]
