Operation Ultramar ran from 15 to 28 August 2026 as a coordinated
counternarcotics effort by the Dominican Republic, the United States, Colombia
and the Netherlands against maritime drug trafficking in the Central Caribbean.
[@1]

The operation produced two interdictions on 19 and 20 August, removing a
combined 4,687 kilograms of cocaine and more than 22.5 kilograms of marijuana,
and eight suspected traffickers were detained. [@1]

On 19 August, U.S. maritime patrol aircraft detected a suspected go-fast vessel
more than 100 nautical miles south of the Dominican Republic; Dominican forces
then intercepted and boarded the vessel, seized the contraband, and detained
three suspected traffickers. [@1]

The 19 August interdiction seized 1,399 kg of cocaine and more than 22.5 kg of
marijuana from a 32-foot, unregistered Eduardoño-type boat equipped with two
250-horsepower outboard engines. [@1]

On 20 August, a second go-fast vessel detected more than 150 nautical miles
southwest of Puerto Rico led to the detention of five suspected traffickers and
the seizure of 3,288 kg of cocaine; the unseaworthy vessel was then safely sunk.
[@1]

The DNCD said the interdictions were a 'decisive blow to transnational organized
crime networks' that 'consolidates the country as an efficient and strategic
operational partner in the fight against drug trafficking and related crimes.'
[@1]

Operation Ultramar is a component of Campaign MARTILLO, the JIATF-S-coordinated
multinational campaign designed to disrupt illicit trafficking networks
throughout the region. [@1]
