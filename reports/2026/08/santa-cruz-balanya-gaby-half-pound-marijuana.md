Investigators from Dipanda, the National Division against the Criminal
Development of Gangs, working with the Ministerio Público, arrested Gabriela
“N”, 33, known as Gaby, in zone 1 of Santa Cruz Balanyá, Chimaltenango. [@1] The
PNC said she was linked to the sale and distribution of drugs. [@1] La Red 106.1
reported that preliminary inquiries linked her to the Solo Para Locos clique of
the Barrio 18 gang, which it said operates in the region. [@2] Nuestro Diario,
citing PNC spokesman Ángel Mux, described her as a suspected supplier of drugs
to that clique. [@3] Nuestro Diario published her name as Gabriela Morales. [@3]

During the search, officers found 28 small bags of marijuana and another bag
with about half a pound of marijuana, about 227 grams, plus 12 wraps of cocaine
and nine bags of crack rocks. [@1] They also seized a digital scale, a telephone
and Q1,658.70 in cash, which the PNC said was the proceeds of drug sales. [@1]
The PNC's own account listed the same find in the home: 28 bags of marijuana, a
further bag of about half a pound, 12 cocaine wraps, nine bags of crack, a
digital scale, a telephone and Q1,658.70 from drug sales. [@4]

La Red reported that she was placed before the courts with the seized items so
her legal situation could be resolved. [@2] Nuestro Diario said the
investigation was still seeking to establish her role in the structure and
whether the property was used to store and distribute drugs. [@3]
