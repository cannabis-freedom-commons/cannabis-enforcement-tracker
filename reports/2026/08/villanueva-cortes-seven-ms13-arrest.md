Officers of the Anti-Extortion and Terrorist Associations Division (DAET),
Honduras's anti-extortion police division, broke up an active Mara Salvatrucha
(MS-13) cell operating in Villanueva, Cortés. [@2] The tactical operation led to
the capture of seven suspected members of the group, including two formally
identified ringleaders. [@2] The operation took place in aldea Suyapa, barrio El
Campo, in Villanueva, Cortés. [@3] Among those held was 43-year-old Jorge
Alexander Cárcamo Valladares, alias El Clon. [@3] Also held was Jesús Enrique
Castillo Dubón, alias El Sarco, described as second-in-command of the local
structure. [@3] Police identified several detained women as caleteras, a term
the report explains as people tasked with hiding weapons and drugs for the
group. [@3] The seven detainees were placed at the disposal of the Public
Ministry prosecution. [@2] They face formal accusations of drug trafficking,
illegal possession of permitted firearms, possession of prohibited ammunition
and criminal association. [@2]

During the operation officers found more than 2,600 wraps of suspected
marijuana. [@3] The marijuana included rectangular packages sealed with red
grease to evade canine checks as well as individual doses. [@2] Officers also
seized more than 3,600 plastic tips containing suspected white powder believed
to be cocaine. [@3] Police valued the seized drugs at three million lempiras.
[@1] Officers also seized two short firearms, magazines with prohibited-calibre
ammunition, several mobile phones and a luxury pickup allegedly used to move the
drugs. [@2]

Police said the operation was part of actions against structures dedicated to
illicit activities in northern Honduras. [@3] Judicial authorities must still
establish each suspect's role in the structure and clarify the origin and
destination of the seized drugs. [@3]
