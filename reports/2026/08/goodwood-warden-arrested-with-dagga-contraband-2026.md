Western Cape Minister of Police Oversight and Community Safety Anroux Marais
welcomed the arrest of a prison warden at the Goodwood Prison in Cape Town. [@1]
The arrest followed the discovery of drugs, mobile phones and other illicit
goods in the warden's vehicle during a joint operation last weekend. [@1]

Officers found 600 mandrax tablets, 100 grams of tik, 300 grams of dagga, 23
cellular telephones and other illicit goods in his possession. [@1]

Marais said there should be zero tolerance for officials who facilitate criminal
activities. [@1]
