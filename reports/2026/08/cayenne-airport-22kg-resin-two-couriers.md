A woman arriving at Félix-Éboué international airport in French Guiana from
Paris-Charles-de-Gaulle on 16 August 2026 was stopped by the Cayenne customs
internal-surveillance brigade, which found bars of cannabis resin in three LEGO
toy boxes in her checked baggage. [@1] The resin in the LEGO boxes weighed 5.316
kg and customs valued it at 48,000 euros. [@2] The next day, 17 August, a second
check on a flight from Orly found vacuum-sealed sachets of cannabis resin in two
boxes of gift packaging, 16.877 kg valued at about 152,000 euros. [@1] The two
seizures at the airport came to 22.193 kg of cannabis resin within 48 hours.
[@1] The customs service announced the seizures in a statement published on
Thursday 27 August 2026. [@2]

The first courier, the woman stopped on 16 August, was sentenced to three years
in prison, two of them suspended, an 8,000 euro fine and a five-year ban on
staying in French Guiana. [@1] The courier stopped on 17 August, a passenger
arriving from Orly, was sentenced to four years in prison, two of them
suspended, a 20,000 euro fine and a two-year ban from Félix-Éboué airport. [@2]
In 2025 customs officers in French Guiana seized 466 kg of cocaine, 70 kg of
cannabis and 1,213 kg of tobacco. [@1] Customs said its latest seizures in 2026
showed cannabis resin being imported from Europe. [@1]
