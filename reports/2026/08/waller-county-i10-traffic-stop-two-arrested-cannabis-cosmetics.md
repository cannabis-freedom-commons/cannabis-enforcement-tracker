Two wanted suspects with a combined 11 outstanding warrants were arrested during
a traffic stop on Interstate 10 in Waller County, Texas, on 27 August 2026. [@1]
The Waller County Sheriff's Office did not identify the two people arrested in
its 29 August announcement. [@1]

Deputies say they found marijuana and a large quantity of cosmetics believed to
have been stolen in the vehicle. [@1] The sheriff's office did not say how much
marijuana was recovered or where along I-10 the stop took place. [@1] ABC13
Houston reported that cosmetics and cannabis were found inside the suspects'
vehicle. [@2]

Sealy police used Flock automated license plate reader technology to learn the
suspects' whereabouts and direction of travel, then relayed the information to
Waller County deputies. [@1] Deputies stopped the vehicle heading eastbound on
I-10 and took both occupants into custody. [@1]

The stop led to the recovery of about 786 cosmetic items, which the sheriff's
office valued at about $9,600. [@1] Investigators linked the merchandise to
thefts from roughly 18 Walgreens and CVS stores in the San Antonio area, and the
items were being processed for return to the retailers. [@1]
