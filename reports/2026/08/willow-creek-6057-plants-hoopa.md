Humboldt County sheriff's deputies arrested a man while serving a search warrant
at an illegal cannabis cultivation site in the Willow Creek area on 6 August
2026. [@1] The Marijuana Enforcement Team, the sheriff's cannabis unit, searched
an area south of Hoopa with help from the Trinity County Sheriff's Office and
the California Department of Fish and Wildlife. [@1] The sheriff's office said
the Trinity County Sheriff's Office and the California Department of Fish and
Wildlife assisted in serving the warrant. [@2]

Deputies eradicated about 6,057 growing cannabis plants and seized and destroyed
more than 480 pounds (about 218 kg) of cannabis shake. [@2] They also found and
seized one rifle at the property. [@2] Four people were found working at the
site; investigators determined they were not being properly compensated as state
law requires, and deputies documented unsafe, inadequate living conditions. [@2]
The Northern California Coalition to Safeguard Communities provided resources
and support to the four workers. [@2]

The California Department of Cannabis Control had licensed the parcel for
commercial cannabis cultivation, but the licence was suspended, including
because the property lacked the required Humboldt County permits. [@2] Deputies
identified the parcel as actively cultivating in violation of state law through
aerial surveillance. [@2] Elvecio Machado, 54, of Lafayette, California, was
booked into the Humboldt County Correctional Facility on six charges. [@2] The
six charges were felony marijuana cultivation, maintaining a place for illicit
drugs, disobeying a court order, streambed alteration, littering in a waterway
and failure to properly pay employees. [@3]

Assisting agencies recorded water diversion, water pollution and rubbish-dumping
violations, carrying civil fines of up to 8,000 dollars a day per violation, or
up to 20,000 dollars for water pollution and for rubbish in or near a waterway.
[@2] The sheriff's office said further violations and civil fines were expected
from the assisting agencies. [@2]
