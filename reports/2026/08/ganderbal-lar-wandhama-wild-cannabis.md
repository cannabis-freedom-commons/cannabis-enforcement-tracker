On 27 August 2026, J&K Police in Ganderbal and the Excise Department conducted a joint Wild Cannabis Destruction Drive at Lar, Wandhama and adjoining areas under the “Nasha Mukt Jammu & Kashmir” campaign. [@1,2]

During the drive, teams identified wild cannabis growing along roadsides and at other locations, then systematically uprooted and destroyed the plants. [@1,2]

The opened accounts give no quantity, area, plant count, arrest or later case outcome for the operation. [@1,2]
