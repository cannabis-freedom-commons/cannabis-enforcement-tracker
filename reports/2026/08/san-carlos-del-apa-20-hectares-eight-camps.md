Agents of the Senad's Regional Department No. 7 in Concepción, with support from
the CODI and the Public Ministry, made a rural incursion into Cerranía San Luis
in the district of San Carlos del Apa, Concepción department. [@1] The incursion
took place on the Wednesday before Última Hora's 21 August report, that is 19
August 2026. [@2]

Officers eradicated and burned 20 hectares of growing marijuana spread over five
plots, and dismantled eight precarious camps holding equipment used to produce
the drug. [@1] Two bags holding a total of about 40 kilograms of marijuana seeds
were also destroyed. [@1]

The Senad put the potential production of the 20 hectares at 60,000 kilograms of
marijuana kept out of the trafficking circuit. [@1] Using the drugs' estimated
value on the Brazilian market, it put the loss to criminal organisations at
about USD 9 million. [@1] The operation ran from about 11:45 to 16:00, led by
Senad special agents with Special Forces personnel, CODI and SAP 2 under
Lieutenant Colonel Sebastián Campo. [@2] Anti-drug prosecutor Pablo Zárate of
the specialised narcotics unit and assistant prosecutor Enrique Huerta were
present. [@2]
