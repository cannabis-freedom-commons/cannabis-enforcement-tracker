On 11 August 2026, the 38th Battalion of the Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB) conducted an anti-narcotics operation in the Bomja village area under the Area of Responsibility of its G-Coy Bomja unit in Tawang district. [@1]

The team found wild cannabis spread across about one acre, uprooted the plants and destroyed them to prevent misuse or illegal harvesting. [@1]
