Toronto police came upon an illegal cannabis production operation on 4 August
while executing a search warrant at a commercial unit on Rutherford Road in
connection with a separate investigation. [@1] The Ontario Provincial Police
cannabis enforcement team was called in and determined through Health Canada
that the property had no registrations or licences to operate. [@1] The
operation was discovered on 4 August at a commercial unit on Rutherford Road
South, as Toronto police executed a search warrant for a separate investigation.
[@2]

A day later, with a search warrant, investigators returned to the property and
recovered nearly 4,000 cannabis plants, more than 23 kilograms of processed
cannabis and $600 in cash. [@1] Investigators seized nearly 4,000 cannabis
plants in various stages of growth, more than 23 kilograms of processed cannabis
and $600 in Canadian currency. [@2] Police valued the seized cannabis at more
than $1.5 million. [@1]

Forty-three-year-old Ha Van Thang and 38-year-old Van Trieu Ngo were arrested
and charged with possession for the purpose of distributing and unauthorized
cultivation under the Cannabis Act. [@1] Both men were released from custody and
are scheduled to appear in court in Toronto on 3 September. [@2] Investigators
found significant safety hazards commonly associated with illegal cannabis
production, including risks related to electrical infrastructure and building
safety. [@1] The Office of the Fire Marshal and other agencies were called to
assess the property and police said there was ultimately determined to be no
ongoing threat to public safety. [@2]
