Police in Lilongwe arrested two men on 10 August 2026 after finding 190
kilograms of cannabis in a house in Area 25. [@1] The suspects are Shadreck
Maganga, 41, from Chipoka Village under Traditional Authority Mwanyenga in
Phalombe district, and Ben David Mkosi, 36, from Yotamu Ziba Village under
Traditional Authority Chindi in Mzimba district. [@1]

Officers from the Central West Regional Police team and Kanengo detectives acted
on information that people were keeping and selling illicit drugs in the area and
raided a house in Sector 7. [@1] A search found 10 bales and 23 packets of
Cannabis Sativa weighing 190 kilograms. [@1] The men could not produce documents
authorising them to possess or sell the drug, police said, and were charged with
being found in possession of cannabis without a licence. [@1]

During interrogation, Maganga allegedly told police that the cannabis originated
in Eswatini and entered Malawi in refrigerated trucks, was repackaged into empty
fuel tankers and was to be transported to Tanzania. [@1] The seized cannabis was
sent to Chitedze Research Station for laboratory analysis. [@1] Police said the
men would appear in court after the necessary legal processes were completed. [@1]
