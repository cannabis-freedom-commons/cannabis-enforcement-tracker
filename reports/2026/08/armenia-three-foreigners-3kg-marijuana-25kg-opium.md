Armenia's Investigative Committee said foreign nationals living in Armenia
helped smuggle 25.5 kg of opium and 3 kg of marijuana into the country on an
especially large scale. [@1] One of the foreigners tried to smuggle 13.8 kg of
opium hidden in electric relays in two parcels to Germany and one parcel to
Great Britain, but was stopped by State Revenue Committee officers. [@1]
Detained M.K. went with SRC officers to his residence and handed over packages
with 8,677 grams of opium and 2,680 grams of marijuana plus a fireproof safe
with suspected drugs, which were seized. [@1] Two other alleged group members,
foreign citizens R.Sh. and M.A., were detained at the same time and taken to the
investigating authority. [@1]

Detention was ordered as the restraint measure against the accused. [@2]
Searches at the foreigners' residence and inspections of the fireproof cabinet
uncovered a significant amount of controlled substances. [@2] The authorities
put the market value of the seized opium and marijuana at about 1,193,445,000
drams. [@1] On the prosecutor's decision, public criminal prosecution was
brought against R.Sh. and M.K. for illegal drug circulation for sale and
attempted smuggling, and against M.A. on additional smuggling-facilitation
counts under articles 393, 44-399 and 46-399. [@1]

The preliminary investigation is continuing to identify other people who
acquired especially large quantities of drugs in Armenia. [@1]
