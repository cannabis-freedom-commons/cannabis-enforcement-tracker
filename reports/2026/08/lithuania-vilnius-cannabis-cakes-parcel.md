In May 2026 customs officers at Lithuania's postal logistics centre in Vilnius checked a suspicious
parcel from Canada declared as 10 cakes and 10 chocolates. [@1,2] It smelled strongly of cannabis.
[@1] Cutting open one of the cakes, they found a plastic bag of dried cannabis; the other cakes
contained it too. [@1] The sweets weighed about 5 kg and the cannabis filling about 0.5 kg. [@1]

## Two suspects

The parcel was seized and the customs laboratory confirmed the drug, opening an investigation into
drug smuggling led by prosecutors in Vilnius. [@1,2] In June, customs investigators detained two
suspects: a Ukrainian citizen in Vilnius, who resisted and was subdued with gas and handcuffs, and a
Belarusian citizen in Kupiškis. [@1] Searches found cannabis seeds, crystal methamphetamine, ecstasy
tablets and three cannabis seedlings in pots, along with packaging materials, magnetic bags and two
air guns. [@1]

The Belarusian was released after questioning on a written undertaking not to leave the country; a
Vilnius court remanded the Ukrainian in custody for three months. [@1] The investigation continues
under provisions on drug smuggling, illegal possession and resisting an officer. [@1]
