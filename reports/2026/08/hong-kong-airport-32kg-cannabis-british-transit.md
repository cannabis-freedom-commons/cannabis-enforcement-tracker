On 28 August 2026, Customs searched two suitcases carried by a 42-year-old British passenger transiting through Hong Kong from Bangkok to London. [@1,2]

Officers found 29 packages of suspected cannabis buds weighing about 32 kg in total. [@1,2] Customs estimated their market value at HK$7.5 million. [@1,2]

The passenger was arrested and charged with trafficking in a dangerous drug, and his case was scheduled to appear at Kowloon City Magistrates’ Courts on 29 August. [@1,2]
