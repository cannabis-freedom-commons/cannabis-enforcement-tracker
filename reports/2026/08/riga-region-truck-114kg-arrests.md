Officers of the State Police's Administration for Combating Organised Crime and
Serial Crimes tracked an organised criminal group suspected of large-scale drug
trafficking during operational work against illegal trafficking of narcotic and
psychotropic substances. [@1] The two suspects, born in 1977 and 1978, were
detained on 26 August. [@1] Both detainees were kept in custody. [@1]
Investigators concluded that the detainees had agreed with at least two
accomplices to form an organised group to acquire, store and sell drugs. [@1]
Police said the roles and duties of each group member were strictly divided.
[@1]

As part of the investigation, police carried out procedural measures including
searches in the Riga region. [@1] Officers found a Mercedes Benz truck
containing 114 kg of dried marijuana. [@1] The State Police said its
investigation was continuing. [@1]
