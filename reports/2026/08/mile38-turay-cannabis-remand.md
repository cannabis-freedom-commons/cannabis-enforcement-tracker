Driver Samuel Turay was intercepted on 9 June 2026 at the Mile 38 police
checkpoint in Sierra Leone's North West Region. [@1] Officers found 600.9
grams of loose cannabis sativa in his possession without legal authorisation,
which later tested positive for tetrahydrocannabinol. [@1] The prosecution
says he knowingly transported the cannabis from 55 Junction Waterloo to the
Mile 38 checkpoint in Port Loko District. [@1]

Turay faces two counts under the National Drugs Control Act 2008: unlawful
possession of cannabis sativa and transporting prohibited drugs. [@1] He
pleaded not guilty to both counts. [@1]

## Remand

High Court Justice Adrian Fisher ordered Turay remanded at Freetown's Male
Correctional Facility and denied him bail. [@1] Lead prosecutor A. Jalloh
asked for a brief adjournment to bring state witnesses, and proceedings are
scheduled to resume on 12 October 2026. [@1]
