Customs arrested two mainland Chinese women, aged 49 and 51, at Hong Kong International Airport on 18 August 2026. [@1,2]

The women had arrived from Bangkok via Manila, and officers found about 27 kg of suspected cannabis buds in their checked baggage. [@1,2] Customs estimated the seizure’s market value at HK$6.3 million. [@1,2]

The women were jointly charged with one count of trafficking in a dangerous drug and were due to appear at West Kowloon Magistrates’ Courts on 20 August. [@1,2]
