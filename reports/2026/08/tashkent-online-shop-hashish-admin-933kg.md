Interior Ministry officers working with the State Security Service shut down an
online drug shop in Tashkent and seized nearly 10 kilograms of drugs, the
ministry said. [@2] The detained man was the shop's 37-year-old administrator
and curator, a native of Tashkent. [@2] The ministry said he had earlier been
convicted under Articles 164 and 278 of Uzbekistan's Criminal Code. [@1]

Officers searching his car and home found 9.33 kilograms of hashish and 0.545
kilograms of methamphetamine, which the ministry said were kept for later sale.
[@2] The ministry put the combined 9.875 kilograms at 64,817 single doses, about
0.15 grams each. [@2]

The ministry said drug offences of this scale carry up to 20 years in prison
under Uzbek law. [@2] The operation fell under Kora-Dori 2026, a nationwide
preventive campaign the ministry said was continuing at an intensified pace.
[@1]
