Turkey's Interior Ministry said that gendarmerie teams had raided drug sellers
and illegal cannabis growers in 28 provinces, and that 96 suspects were caught.
[@1] Of the 96 suspects, 69 were remanded in custody and 27 were placed under
judicial control, the ministry said. [@1] The ministry said the suspects sold
drugs through social media and messaging apps, grew illegal cannabis and skunk,
and ran the drug trade in an organised way. [@1]

The ministry said 2,200,000 rooted cannabis and skunk plants were destroyed.
[@1] Officers also seized 104 kilograms of drugs and 294,000 drug pills, a
combined figure the ministry did not break down by substance. [@1]

The raids were run by provincial gendarmerie commands under the Gendarmerie
General Command's anti-narcotics department and public prosecutors, with 1,326
narcotics and public-order teams and 7,956 personnel taking part. [@2] The
ministry published the statement on its social media accounts and said
operations against drug makers and sellers would continue without pause. [@2]
The ministry said in the statement that its fight against street sellers, drug
makers and illegal drug cultivation would continue without interruption. [@3]
