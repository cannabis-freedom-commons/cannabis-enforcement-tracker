Police and municipal officials in Georgievsk, Stavropol Krai, found a large
patch of wild cannabis during operation Mak-2026. [@1] The patch covered 800
square metres, or 0.08 hectares. [@1] The cut crop weighed more than 190
kilograms and officers burned it. [@1] The Stavropol Krai Interior Ministry
press service announced the find. [@1]

Pobeda 26 put the destroyed material at about 190 kilograms growing on the 800
square metres. [@2] The work fell in the third stage of Mak-2026, a nationwide
interdepartmental operation against drugs of plant origin. [@3] The operation
aims to stop plant-based drugs spreading and to close down cannabis growing
sites. [@3]

Police asked residents to report wild cannabis they find. [@2] The aggregator
Prospect carried the same police account on 3 September. [@4]
