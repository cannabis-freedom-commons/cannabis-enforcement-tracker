RAB-13 arrested four women for holding ganja in Lalmonirhat. [@1] The arrested
women are Roksana, 40, Marjina, 45, Rawshanara, 37, and Moni, 35. [@1] Roksana
is from Alamnagar Muslimpara in Rangpur city, Marjina is from Gopal in Kaunia,
Rangpur, and Rawshanara and Moni are from the Nowpara Rail Gate Lichu Bagan area
of Rangpur city. [@1]

An operational team of RAB-13, Rangpur carried out the raid on the paved road in
front of Ma Market at Kakina Zero Point crossing under Kaliganj police station
in Lalmonirhat district. [@1] The raid took place at 4:30 pm on 22 August 2026
on the basis of specific intelligence information. [@1] The team seized 11 kg of
ganja in the operation. [@1]

In initial questioning, the arrested women were said to have been involved in
the drug trade for a long time. [@1] RAB filed a case against them under the
Narcotics Control Act as complainant. [@1] The arrested suspects and the seized
material were handed over to the concerned police station for further legal
action. [@1]
