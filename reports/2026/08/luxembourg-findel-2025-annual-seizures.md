Luxembourg customs' 2025 seizure figures come from passenger checks in terminals
A and B and the general-aviation terminal at Findel Airport. [@1] Customs
authorities said narcotics topped the seized goods, with record 2025 seizures of
cannabis, as hashish and marijuana, and cocaine. [@1]

The Finance Ministry's annual activity report for the customs administration put
the 2025 haul at nearly 2.6 tonnes of marijuana and 215.192 kg of hashish. [@1]
The same report listed about 873.804 kg of cocaine, 525.9 kg of ketamine and
275.5 kg of methamphetamine. [@1] The seizures also included 16.566 kg of
heroin, 43 grams of hallucinogenic mushrooms and 93.4 grams of new psychoactive
substances, with 2.8 grams and 11 tablets of MDMA intercepted. [@1] Officers
also seized 895 cannabis seeds, seven joints and two electronic cigarettes
containing THC, the psychoactive substance from cannabis. [@1]

Authorities identified two new trafficking routes from Latin America and
confirmed the North American route already detected at other major European
freight airports. [@1] Beyond drugs, officers seized 28 mobile phones and two
vehicles, with 13 items for selling narcotics including scales and tracking
devices and 11 grinders for drug use, plus a taser and four bladed weapons. [@1]
Officers seized 4,318.77 euros from drug trafficking and, in money-movement
checks, confiscated 279,870 euros, 3,385 Turkish lira and 345 US dollars. [@1]
Customs carried out 152 documentary and physical checks on cosmetic and
pharmaceutical products, of which 112 revealed non-compliance. [@1]
