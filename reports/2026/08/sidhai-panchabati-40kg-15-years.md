## The sentence

A special court in Agartala sentenced 59-year-old Ranjit Saha of Panchabati, under Sidhai police station, to 15 years of rigorous imprisonment on Monday, 31 August 2026. [@1] The Special Judge for West Tripura also imposed a fine of Rs 1.5 lakh, with six more months of simple imprisonment if the fine is not paid. [@1] The court convicted him in case SPL (NDPS) 177/2025, registered as Sidhai police station case 31/2025. [@1]

## The 2025 raid

Sidhai police raided Saha's residence in Panchabati between about 2:35 AM and 3:15 AM on 27 May 2025 after receiving secret information. [@1] Officers recovered 40 kg of dry ganja in the presence of independent witnesses after completing the required legal formalities. [@1] Police said Saha fled from the spot during the raid. [@1] Sub-Inspector Sujit Reang of Sidhai police station filed the chargesheet on 21 November 2025. [@1]
