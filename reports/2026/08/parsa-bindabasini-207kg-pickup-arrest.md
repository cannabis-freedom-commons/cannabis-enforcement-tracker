Police arrested 48-year-old Krishna Lama of Hetauda Sub-Metropolitan City-13,
Makawanpur, on Tuesday evening, 4 August 2026. [@1] The arrest took place at
Bindabasini Rural Municipality-5 in Parsa district, where police stopped the
vehicle he was driving. [@3]

Lama was driving a Tata Yodho pickup with registration number Province 3-01-024
Cha 5385, and police seized it along with the marijuana. [@2] Officers found 207
kilograms of marijuana concealed inside cement bags in the pickup. [@2] The
check was made at around 6:30 pm on Tuesday evening. [@2] The District Police
Office Parsa named its spokesman and information officer, Deputy Superintendent
of Police Chakra Bahadur Mall, as the source of the account. [@2]

Police said further investigation into the case is under way. [@1] Galaxy Khabar
reported that police put the weight of the material found, which looked like
ganja, at about 207 kilograms and were investigating the driver for a drug
offence. [@4]
