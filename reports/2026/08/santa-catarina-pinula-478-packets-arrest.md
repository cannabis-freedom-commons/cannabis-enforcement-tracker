Investigators of the División Especializada en Investigación Criminal (DEIC),
Guatemala's specialist criminal-investigation division, captured two suspected
drug distributors on the highway to El Salvador. [@1] The detainees were
identified as 33-year-old Gloria “N”, a Guatemalan woman, and 23-year-old Luis
“N”, a Colombian man. [@1] A second account gives their full names as Gloria
Estefany De León de León and the Colombian Luis Aníbal Gutiérrez Rendon, aged 33
and 23 respectively. [@2]

Officers intercepted the pair at kilometre 13 of the Carretera a El Salvador, in
the jurisdiction of Santa Catarina Pinula, while they were travelling inside a
van. [@2] Guatemala's National Civil Police (PNC) said the two were transporting
478 packets containing marijuana inside the van when DEIC investigators captured
them at that spot. [@1] The second account identifies the van as a Mazda CX5
with registration plate P-919LKH carrying the 478 marijuana packets. [@2]

Officers also seized four mobile phones from the pair, which will undergo
analysis to determine whether they strengthen the investigation into suspected
membership of a drug-distribution gang. [@2] Both accounts describe the pair as
suspected distributors rather than convicted offenders. [@1]
