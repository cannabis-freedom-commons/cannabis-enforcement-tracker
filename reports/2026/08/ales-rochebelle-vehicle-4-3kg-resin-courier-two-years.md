On Monday 31 August 2026, national police motorcyclists in Alès, in the Gard,
stopped a car whose number plates did not comply with the rules. [@2] Officers
noticed a strong smell of cannabis and saw an open bin bag holding yellow
packaging sachets on the passenger seat. [@2] The driver fled and was caught in
a building on the quai de Bilina where he lived with his parents, and police
first put the haul at 5 kg of cannabis resin. [@1] The young man, a 20-year-old
Alès resident previously unknown to the courts, was arrested in the Rochebelle
district, one of four dealing spots police have identified in the town. [@3]
Weighed at the courthouse, the bin bag held 4.3 kg of cannabis resin, and a jute
sack in the same car held 1.4 kg of cocaine. [@2]

After his police custody the suspect was brought before the Alès public
prosecutor, who ordered him held in provisional detention until a hearing on
Thursday 3 September under France's comparution immédiate procedure, a
fast-track hearing used for clear-cut cases. [@1] At the hearing the prosecutor
accused him of acting as a courier, a mule, carrying the drugs for people
investigators had not identified. [@2] The man said he had been forced, that
threats against his family frightened him, and that the goods had been left in
his car. [@2] The prosecutor asked for three years in prison, one suspended with
probation, and for the man to stay in detention. [@2]

On 3 September 2026 the Alès court gave him two years in prison, one of them
suspended with probation, and did not keep him in detention. [@2] A police
source said the force was active against drug dealing in the town, and the
September accounts put the number of people jailed in drug cases in August at
19. [@3]
