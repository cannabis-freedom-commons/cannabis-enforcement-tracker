Police and local-government officials in Jumgal District, Naryn Region, mowed and burned about 2.5 tonnes of wild cannabis on roughly 2 hectares at Marinka and Kindik, areas belonging to the Min-Kush rural district. [@1]

The 11 August 2026 report said the work was part of Operation Kara Kuura and involved the Jumgal district police, Naryn regional police drug-enforcement service and Min-Kush rural administration. [@1] Police said raids to find and destroy wild cannabis would continue. [@1]
