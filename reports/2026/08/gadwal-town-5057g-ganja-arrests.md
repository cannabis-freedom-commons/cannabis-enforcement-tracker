Two men were arrested by Gadwal Town police and 5.057 kilograms of cannabis was
seized in Gadwal town, Telangana, on 30 August 2026. [@1] Police were checking
vehicles at Melacheruvu crossroads that Sunday evening when officers stopped the
two suspicious men and found cannabis packets. [@3] Police identified them as B.
Bisanna from Shanigepalli village in Itikyala mandal and Gokari from
Vithalapuram village in Maldakal mandal. [@2] Deputy Superintendent of Police Y.
Mogulaiah announced the case details at a press conference at Gadwal Town police
station on Monday. [@3]

Mogulaiah said the men were bringing cannabis to supply in Gadwal. [@3] Officers
seized two mobile phones and a Bajaj Platina motorcycle along with the cannabis.
[@2] The seized motorcycle bore registration number AP-22-S-2561. [@2]
Investigators found that the pair had been supplying cannabis in Gadwal,
Erravalli and Kothakota. [@3] Police registered a case, arrested the men and
remanded them to custody. [@3] Police were investigating where the cannabis had
come from and where the men were taking it. [@2]
