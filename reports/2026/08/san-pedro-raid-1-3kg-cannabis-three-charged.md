Three San Pedro residents were jointly charged with drug trafficking following a
police raid in the Boca del Rio area. [@1] Police arrested and jointly charged
three people, including 26-year-old Horace Anthony Crawford and 24-year-old
Bridget Alcer alongside another 26-year-old San Pedro resident. [@1] Each of the
three faces three counts of possession of controlled drugs with intent to supply
to another. [@1] The two named accused are 26-year-old Horace Anthony Crawford
and 24-year-old Bridget Alcer. [@1] The account identifies the third accused
only as another 26-year-old San Pedro resident. [@1]

Police said the operation was conducted at a residence on 21 August 2026. [@1]
The residence search resulted in the discovery of 1,312 grams of suspected
cannabis. [@1] The same search resulted in the discovery of 21 grams of
suspected cocaine. [@1] The same search also resulted in the discovery of 28
grams of suspected crack cocaine. [@1]

The police account reports the cannabis, cocaine and crack finds together as the
outcome of the single residence operation. [@1]
