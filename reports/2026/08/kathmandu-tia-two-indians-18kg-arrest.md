Two Indian nationals who had arrived from Bangkok via Hong Kong were arrested on
Tuesday night at Tribhuvan International Airport in Kathmandu in possession of
18 kilograms of marijuana. [@1] The arrestees were identified as 36-year-old
Sanjeev Kumar and 26-year-old Rajiv Kumar, both residents of Punjab, India. [@1]
According to Superintendent of Police Durga Raj Regmi, spokesperson for the
Narcotics Control Bureau, the duo was intercepted at the airport’s arrival
customs area. [@1]

Officers seized the 18 kilograms of marijuana carried by the pair at Tribhuvan
International Airport. [@1] The suspects had arrived in Nepal aboard a Cathay
Pacific flight. [@1] Police said the marijuana they carried on that flight was
produced in Thailand. [@1] Upon landing, the suspects attempted to flee by
abandoning their luggage at the arrival customs area to avoid arrest. [@1]

Preliminary investigation found the pair had planned to smuggle the contraband
into India through a local Nepali agent a few days later. [@1] Both individuals
were taken into custody and remain held for further investigation. [@1]
