Police arrested eight foreign nationals at Tribhuvan International Airport in
Kathmandu. [@1] The group comprised five citizens from Myanmar and three people
from Punjab, India. [@1] The five Myanmar nationals arrived from Bangkok on Thai
Airways flight TG-319, and police said a check of their bags recovered cannabis.
[@2] Nepal Live gave their names as जार नि लिन, इ इ थ्वे, आङ ब्वार, चुए म्याट
थ्वाल सेइन and जिन म्यो आङ. [@2] The three Indian nationals arrived from Phuket
on Batik Airways flight OD-541 and were identified as Khushdeep, Gurdeep Singh
and Kamini from Punjab. [@2]

Officers found a total of 39.288 kilograms of high-grade Thai marijuana hidden
in the travellers' luggage. [@1] The Drug Control Bureau said the arrests came
during routine profiling targeting international drug-smuggling routes. [@1] The
bureau said it had increased passenger profiling and surveillance after
information that cannabis with high THC content was being brought in by air.
[@2] Police also seized eight passports and eight mobile phones from the
detainees. [@2]

Bureau officials said an investigation into drug-transport offences was under
way. [@1] The bureau's co-spokesperson and Deputy Superintendent Jeevan Niraula
said the eight remained under further investigation. [@2]
