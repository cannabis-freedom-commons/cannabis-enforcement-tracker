Lapland District Court sentenced a man to five months of suspended imprisonment for growing cannabis in Tervola and failing to report two deactivated rifles. [@1] He admitted the offences and agreed to have the case decided in written proceedings. [@1]

The drug offence ran from 14 October 2025 to 8 March 2026. [@1] The prosecutor said the man grew at least two cannabis plants to sell or otherwise distribute the cannabis. [@1] Police seized 276 grams of cannabis flowers and shake grown from those plants. [@1]

The man also sold at least 20 grams of cannabis to unidentified people at ten euros per gram, for a reported total of 200 euros in criminal proceeds. [@1] The court ordered the proceeds forfeited to the state and set a 120-euro victim surcharge. [@1]

The separate firearms offence occurred on 6 March 2026. [@1] The man had two deactivated military rifles but had failed to notify police of acquiring them within the required 30 days. [@1] Tilannemedia clarified that the charge concerned the missing notification, not the mere possession of deactivated rifles. [@1] The court said it considered the amount and type of cannabis and the intent to distribute it for financial gain. [@1]
