Elite Guatemalan Army units and civil security forces carried out a large-scale
surprise search at the El Boquerón preventive detention centre in Santa Rosa.
[@1] The joint operation was run by specialized penitentiary units, the National
Civil Police and the Army, with K-9 canine support. [@2] The inspection extended
over several hours across different sectors of the prison. [@1] The operation
was part of a state strategy to weaken privilege and internal-control structures
operating in the Guatemalan prison system. [@1]

Authorities listed five bags of suspected marijuana among the items seized. [@1]
They also listed one marijuana cigar and three jars of suspected marijuana. [@1]
The seizure included ten pipes for inhaling marijuana. [@1] Officers also found
one machine for making marijuana cigarettes. [@1] The inventory also recorded
seven jars for grinding drugs. [@1]

The same inventory recorded 6,450 envoltorios de papos alongside the cannabis
items. [@1] It separately listed bags of suspected cocaine. [@1] The Interior
Ministry report also counted sharp objects, speakers, USB and micro-SD devices,
mobile phones and SIM cards. [@1] The Defence Ministry said such actions
strengthen control of prisons and help maintain order and security. [@2]
