The Oklahoma Bureau of Narcotics Marijuana Enforcement Team conducted a raid on
a marijuana grow in Okmulgee, Oklahoma. [@1] On Thursday, OBN MET agents carried
out a search warrant at 18 Drops LLC, located near Gun Club Road and North 280
Road. [@1] According to OBN, the search followed an investigation into alleged
illegal cultivation and a fraudulent OBN registration obtained through what
investigators called a straw-ownership scheme. [@1] OBN said five people were
taken into ICE custody and more arrests are expected as the investigation
continues. [@1]

During the search, agents seized 7,358 cannabis plants at the property. [@1]
Agents also seized 398 pounds of processed marijuana, about 180.5 kg. [@1]

Agents of the Oklahoma Department of Agriculture, Food and Forestry located
illegal pesticides at the property and obtained a separate search warrant so the
pesticides could also be seized. [@1]
