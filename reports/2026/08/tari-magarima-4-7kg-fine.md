Police and Defence Force personnel stopped a 26-year-old man from Jangome
Village in Tari Urban, Tari-Pori District, at the Magarima check point on
4 August 2026 and found 60 rolls of suspected marijuana weighing about
4.7 kilograms. [@1,2] He was handed to the police Investigative Task Force the
next day, formally arrested and held at the Tari police station. [@1,2]

He appeared before the Tari District Court for the first time on 10 August 2026,
where the magistrate found him guilty and ordered him to pay a K30,000 fine. [@1,2]
If he does not pay, he will serve 20 years in prison. [@1,2]

Hela provincial police commander Inspector Arau Mairi said the case should send
a message. "Let this case send a strong message that the wheel of justice is
turning," he said. [@1,2] He also warned young people to stay away from drugs,
illegal firearms and tribal fights. [@1,2] Police did not name the man. [@1]
