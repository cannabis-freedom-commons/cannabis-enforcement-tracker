A police officer was stopped at the Burnside checkpoint in Coronie, Suriname,
on 24 August 2026 and arrested after officers found suspected marijuana in his
vehicle. [@1,2,3]

The vehicle held two suitcases with plastic-wrapped, compressed blocks. [@1,2,3]
Closer inspection showed dried leaves, stems and seeds that closely resembled
Cannabis sativa, police said. [@1,3]

Police identified the driver as a 38-year-old officer, giving his initials as
L.R.; Dagblad Suriname gave them as R.L. [@1,2] He was taken to a police
station and, after consultation with the Public Prosecutor's Office, held in
custody pending the investigation. [@1,2,3]

The vehicle and the drugs were seized, and the case was transferred to the
Police Affairs Investigation Department (OPZ), the police unit that investigates
cases against officers. [@1,3]

Dagblad Suriname said he was taken to the police station in Totness; the police
statement said the station in Coronie. [@1,2] Police did not give the weight of
the cannabis or a value for it. [@1,2]
