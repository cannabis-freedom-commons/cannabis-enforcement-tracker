Ecuadorian Army personnel carried out an offensive reconnaissance military
operation in the canton of Pedro Carbo, in the province of Guayas, acting on
military intelligence. [@1] The soldiers searched several houses because they
knew that members of the Los Águilas organised crime group, which the Army said
was engaged in illicit activities, were present. [@1] The Army described both
men arrested as presumed members of the Los Águilas group. [@2] The two men
arrested were identified as Ángel E. Loor C., alias El Loco, and Arnold D. Muñoz
S., alias Arnold. [@2] The Army said the two were involved in micro-trafficking.
[@2] The Army also linked them to contract killings, extortion and robbery of
trucks on the roads. [@2]

The search of the houses produced 5 large bags of suspected marijuana. [@1]
Soldiers also recorded 4 envelopes of suspected cocaine, 105 bags of suspected
heroin, a video-surveillance camera and a mobile phone. [@2] The evidence and
the two detainees were handed to the competent authorities for the corresponding
legal procedures. [@1]
