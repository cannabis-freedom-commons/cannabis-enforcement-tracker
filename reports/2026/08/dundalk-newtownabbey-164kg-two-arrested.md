Two men were arrested following an investigation that uncovered 119kg (262lb) of
cannabis in Dundalk, County Louth, after a car was stopped on Wednesday. [@1] A
man in his 40s has been charged after the cross-border drugs bust. [@1] He is
due to appear before Cavan District Court on Friday 28 August 2026 at 2:00pm.
[@3] A man in his 30s was arrested and is being held by the PSNI. [@2]

Revenue Customs Service, the Garda National Drugs and Organised Crime Bureau and
the PSNI Organised Crime Unit seized a total of 119 kg of cannabis with an
estimated value of €1,666,000 as part of a joint operation. [@2] Follow up
searches in Newtownabbey, County Antrim, turned up a further 45kg of cannabis,
half a kilogram of suspected cocaine, 22 imitation firearms and £25,000 in cash.
[@1]

Gardaí said a man in his 40s was arrested on suspicion of drug trafficking after
a vehicle was stopped in the Dundalk area. [@2] Police and gardaí, Ireland's
police, said investigations were ongoing. [@1]
