Officers of Porto-Novo's 4th district police station intervened at a home in the
Houssouko quarter on the evening of Monday 17 August 2026. [@1] The operation
followed tip-offs that the home of a couple living in the quarter served as a
distribution point for illicit substances. [@1] Both suspects were arrested on
the spot and placed in police custody. [@1]

During the search of the house, police found and seized dried plant matter
described as chanvre indien, the French term for cannabis. [@1] Officers also
seized several Tramadol tablets, known locally as kolo, in the same search. [@1]
Investigators said the husband worked by day as a motorcycle-taxi driver and
allegedly used that work to distribute the goods. [@1] They said his partner
handled management, packaging and guarding of the stock kept at the home. [@1]

Police said the two would be brought before the public prosecutor once the
inquiry procedures were completed. [@1] La Nouvelle Tribune confirms that a
couple were arrested on 17 August 2026 in Houssouko by the 4th district police
station. [@2] It reports that police found presumed cannabis and Tramadol
tablets at their home. [@2] That account states that the exact quantities seized
were not specified in the police information. [@2]
