Investigators of the Drugs section of the Brussels Federal Judicial Police, the
PJF, Belgium's federal detective branch, followed the cargo until it was
collected by its intended recipients. [@1] Police intervened on the evening of
27 August 2026 and arrested eight people as the recipients recovered the
shipment. [@1] Four of those arrested were subsequently placed under arrest
warrant, the Belgian pre-trial detention order. [@1]

Officers seized more than 500 kilos of cannabis concealed in two pallets sent
from Canada. [@1] The two pallets had arrived by plane in Belgium on 24 August
2026 with paperwork declaring clothing. [@1] Customs checks of the pallets
uncovered more than half a tonne of cannabis. [@1] The Drugs section of the
Brussels PJF then took over the file from customs to pursue the recipients. [@1]

Alongside the cannabis, officers seized four vehicles, a signal jammer used to
block communications and several mobile phones. [@1] Investigators said
exploitation of the seized phones should help identify the different people
involved in the import and determine their respective roles. [@1] The
investigation is continuing to identify any other suspects and to establish the
full extent of the criminal organisation involved. [@1]
