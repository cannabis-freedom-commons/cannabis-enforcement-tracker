Between January and July 2026, Azerbaijani authorities recorded 4,963 drug
crimes during comprehensive operational and investigative measures against drug
addiction and trafficking, according to the working group of the State
Commission on combating drug addiction and illegal drug circulation. [@1] Of
those cases, 1,250 involved illegal sale of drugs, 3,613 illegal acquisition and
storage, 97 illegal sowing and cultivation of drug-containing plants, and three
other crimes in the category. [@1] Compared with January-July 2025, the volume
of other narcotics seized in the same period of 2026 fell by 13 kg 395.167 g, or
9.7%. [@1]

Officers took 6 tons 223 kg 519.057 g of narcotic drugs and psychotropic
substances out of circulation in the detected cases, which the authorities
valued at 187 million 200 thousand manat. [@1] Marijuana accounted for 5 tons
307 kg 260.329 g of that total and hashish for 247 kg 630.147 g, alongside 257
kg 073.146 g of heroin, 124 kg 912.418 g of opium and psychotropic substances.
[@1] Officers also found and seized 12,715 cannabis bushes, 34,983 methadone
tablets and quantities of potent and toxic substances. [@1]

In operations organised by the Interior Ministry with other agencies, 3,960,601
wild drug-containing plants weighing 327 tons 716 kg were found and destroyed on
1,167.2 hectares. [@1]
