Officers of the Rose-Belle branch of the Anti-Drug and Smuggling Unit carried
out a targeted operation at the home of a 29-year-old man on Tuesday. [@4] The
search took place at Résidence La Bourgate in Beau-Vallon, at the home of
29-year-old Haidar R., and was led by Superintendent Ghoora with Chief Inspector
Appasamy and Sergeant Charun. [@2] The operation followed intelligence work
indicating that a new product alongside traditional drugs was popular in the
south of the island. [@2] Officers arrested the 29-year-old man and placed him
in detention. [@4]

Officers found 5.22 grams of cannabis in the home along with a jelly-like
substance suspected to be cannabis gummies. [@2] Police also recovered several
red gummy sweets suspected of containing tetrahydrocannabinol, known as THC, the
main psychoactive substance in cannabis. [@4] The sweets, also called gom
gandia, were seized at Mahébourg. [@1] The treats were sold discreetly at Rs 500
per piece. [@3]

The ADSU said the gummies remain relatively rare on the local market. [@2] The
seized gummies were sealed and sent to the Drug Chemistry Section of the
Forensic Science Laboratory to confirm whether they contain THC. [@4] Unlike
smoked cannabis, the products can be consumed orally and discreetly without
odour or special equipment. [@2] After the seizure, ADSU headquarters asked
field units to gather more intelligence on the products and seize them when
found. [@2]
