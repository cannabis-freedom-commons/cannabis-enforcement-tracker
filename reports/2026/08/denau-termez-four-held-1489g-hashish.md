Uzbekistan's State Security Service reported stopping several cases of illegal
drug trafficking in Surkhandarya region. [@1] Opium and hashish were seized and
several people were detained in operations in Denau and Termez. [@1]

In Denau, officers searched the flat of a 46-year-old man who had previously
been convicted of drug trafficking. [@1] In a second case in the same district,
two men born in 1986 and 1966, both previously punished for drug trafficking,
were detained as they tried to sell 491 g of hashish to a stand-in buyer for 85
million soums. [@1]

The Termez case took place at the international trade centre by the border with
Afghanistan. [@2] In Termez, an Afghan citizen born in 2003 was detained at the
city's International Trade Center after bringing 506 g of opium into Uzbekistan.
[@1] He had swallowed four packets of the drug to hide them and then tried to
sell it for 2,500 dollars to an undercover officer, who caught him in the act.
[@1] The service said the man had used his own body to conceal and transport the
drugs. [@1]

Criminal cases were opened on all three cases and investigative actions are
under way. [@3] The detained men may face up to 15 years in prison. [@1]
