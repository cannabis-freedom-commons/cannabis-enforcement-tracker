Chad's Public Security Directorate presented 45 detained people in N'Djamena on
3 August 2026. [@1] The operation ran from 17 July to 3 August 2026, and its
results were detailed by Commissioner Mounne Eugene Dezoumbe of the national
police. [@1] The arrests came from joint work by national police intelligence,
the Ngueli border post, public-security stations, community policing and the
national gendarmerie. [@1] The suspects were divided into four groups by type of
offence, covering armed robbery and kidnapping, theft and fraud, forgery and
counterfeiting, and drugs. [@1] The drug group comprised two suspects, one
pursued for drug trafficking and the other for fraudulent sale of veterinary
products. [@1]

Police reported seizing eight automatic pistols along with three vehicles and
two motorcycles. [@1] They also seized 21,891,000 FCFA in cash, 10 million in
counterfeit notes and a computer used for forgery. [@1] Officers also seized a
large quantity of psychoactive substances described as Tramadol and cannabis,
with no separate cannabis amount given. [@1] Dezoumbe said all the suspects
would be sent to face justice. [@1]
