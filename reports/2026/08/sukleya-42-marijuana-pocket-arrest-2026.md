Criminal-investigation officers of the capital's militia detained a 42-year-old
local man in Sukleya, a village in the Slobodzeya district, during an operations
search. [@2] A search of his shorts pocket found and seized a polythene bag
holding a fold of newspaper. [@1] Inside was a green, dry, chopped plant mass
believed to be marijuana. [@1] He gave contradictory accounts: first that he had
found the fold while walking around the village, later that he had picked the
plant himself in a field nearby. [@2] At the time of his detention he was in a
state of drug intoxication and admitted that he had used marijuana. [@2]

The seized material was sent for chemical and biological examination. [@1]
Depending on the weight established, the Sukleya resident could face up to three
years in prison, a fine of up to 6,300 Transnistrian roubles or administrative
arrest of up to 30 days. [@1] For using drugs he will face administrative
liability under article 6.13 of the Transnistrian Administrative Offences Code,
which provides a fine of up to 3,150 roubles or administrative arrest of up to
15 days. [@1] A pre-investigation check into the case is under way. [@1]

The Interior Ministry press centre added that detecting and suppressing
drug-trafficking offences remains one of the priorities of the internal affairs
bodies. [@2]
