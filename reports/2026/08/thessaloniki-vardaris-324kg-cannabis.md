Police arrested two foreign nationals aged 58 and 43 as suspected members of the
network in the Thessaloniki area. [@4] The operation took place on the morning
of 14 August 2026. [@4] SELEC later reported that two members had been arrested
in Greece and one in North Macedonia, bringing reported arrests across the joint
case to three. [@2] SELEC also said another foreign national had been identified
and was being sought. [@2] An arrest warrant had been issued for a third
suspect, and the crackdown followed a tip-off. [@1]

Authorities seized 324 kilograms and 474 grams (324.474 kg) of raw cannabis.
[@3] The cannabis was concealed in a load of paper for recycling. [@2] The
organization used international freight trucks and specially constructed
compartments to hide drugs in legitimate recycling-paper loads and provide cover
for the vehicles' movements. [@2]

SELEC described the group as hierarchical, with professional infrastructure,
moving raw cannabis from Albania to Türkiye via Greece in international freight
trucks. [@2] Investigations to identify and locate accomplices in Albania and
Türkiye were continuing. [@2]
