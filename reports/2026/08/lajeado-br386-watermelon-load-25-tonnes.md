Federal Highway Police officers stopped a truck with licence plates from
Cascavel in Parana on the BR-386 in Lajeado, Rio Grande do Sul. [@1] The suspect
is 44 years old and a native of Pernambuco. [@1] The man was arrested for drug
trafficking and taken to the judicial police with the vehicle and the seized
drugs. [@1] The stop and arrest took place on Monday the 10th in Lajeado. [@1]

The truck was loaded with watermelons. [@1] After inspections with the support
of sniffer dogs, officers found 2.5 tonnes of marijuana hidden under the
watermelon and hay load in the truck bed. [@1] The report describes the haul as
more than 2.5 tonnes of marijuana concealed in the watermelon shipment. [@1] The
load had been collected in Goias and was destined for Porto Alegre, according to
the Federal Highway Police. [@1]

The Federal Highway Police is the force credited with the stop, inspection and
seizure. [@1] The published account places the sniffer-dog find in Lajeado in
Rio Grande do Sul. [@1] The truck bed held watermelons and hay above the
concealed marijuana. [@1]
