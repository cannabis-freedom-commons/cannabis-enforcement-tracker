Police were called shortly after midnight on Saturday 8 August 2026 to a group
of people behaving suspiciously on rue du Fort Dumoulin in Luxembourg City. [@2]
Officers found and checked eight people, most of them adolescents. [@2] The
group was stopped between Hamm and the Pulvermühle, near the Sportlycée, in the
capital. [@3]

During the checks police seized a knife, a quantity of cannabis, pepper spray, a
balaclava, adhesive tape, a screwdriver, a cable and an electric scooter from
the group. [@1] The police statement describes the drug seized as a quantity of
marijuana. [@2] Officers also found a scooter at the scene that had already been
reported stolen, and an investigation is under way into whether it is linked to
the group. [@2]

The public prosecutor's office, the Parquet, was informed and ordered the
seizure of the illegal items and of a mobile phone. [@1] Police drew up an
official report on the incident. [@4] The Grand-Ducal Police gave the call time
as 00:06 and published the account the same day, in German and English on its
portal. [@2]
