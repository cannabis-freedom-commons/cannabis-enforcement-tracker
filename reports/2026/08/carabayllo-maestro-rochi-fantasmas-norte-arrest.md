Peru's National Police captured 68-year-old Luis Eduardo Vera Sansoni, known as
Maestro Rochi, in Carabayllo as the alleged leader of the criminal group Los
Fantasmas del Norte. [@1] Escuadrón Verde officers entered a house in the El Sol
urbanisation in Carabayllo as part of the operation against Los Fantasmas del
Norte. [@1] Besides Vera Sansoni, officers detained two other alleged members of
the group, a 30-year-old man and a 16-year-old girl. [@2] Police identified the
30-year-old as Marlon Mendoza Salinas, known as Gil. [@1] Police described
Mendoza Salinas as allegedly in charge of transporting and delivering drug
packets. [@1] He denied belonging to the group and said he delivered orders for
the retailer Temu. [@1]

Police sources put the haul at seven bricks of coca paste and cocaine
hydrochloride, more than a kilo and a half of marijuana and 10,000 ketes. [@2]
The operation also seized a firearm, ammunition, two magazines and a scale. [@1]
The raided house stood in the seventh stage of the El Sol urbanisation. [@3]

Police said the group was dedicated to drug trafficking and used the house to
store substances for distribution across northern Lima. [@1] The Escuadrón Verde
chief said Vera Sansoni had prior drug-trafficking history and previous spells
in Lurigancho prison. [@1] The Escuadrón Verde located the house after
surveillance work. [@1] The case went to the Dirandro anti-drugs directorate in
San Isidro to determine each detainee's responsibility and the scope of the
group. [@1]
