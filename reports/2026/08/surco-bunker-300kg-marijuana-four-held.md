Police in Surco, a district of Lima, detained four people in a raid on a
suspected drug bunker. [@1] Officers found more than 300 kilos of marijuana, a
variety known in Peru as 'cripy', in the property. [@1] The police account says
the marijuana is thought to have been brought to Lima from the Alto Huallaga, a
coca- and cannabis-growing valley region. [@1]

Officers broke down the door to enter the property, where they found white and
raffia bags. [@2] According to preliminary information, three of the detainees
are Peruvian and one is Venezuelan, and one is suspected of leading the band.
[@2] The National Police said several of those detained had earlier arrests for
drugs, robbery and handling stolen goods. [@2]

A neighbour told reporters that around midday a truck arrived and its occupants
unloaded large bundles that were then carried into the apartment. [@2] ATV.pe
reported that the marijuana was to be sold wholesale, in small quantities and
even by delivery. [@2]
