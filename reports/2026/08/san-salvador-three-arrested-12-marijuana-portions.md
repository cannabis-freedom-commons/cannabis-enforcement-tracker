El Salvador's National Civil Police detained three people during a search of a
house in San Salvador on Saturday 15 August 2026. [@1] The detainees were
identified as Elmer Geovanni Ortiz Flores, Pedro Gálvez González and María Elena
Ortiz Flores. [@3] A National Civil Police dog handler and its K9 agent
supported the house search. [@2]

Officers seized 12 large portions of marijuana, each wrapped in black plastic.
[@2] The search also turned up a digital scale and three mobile phones. [@4]

Security Minister Gustavo Villatoro said the seizures would let authorities dig
deeper into distribution networks in the capital and nearby areas. [@1] The
three are to be prosecuted for unlawful drug trafficking. [@3] Villatoro said
they could face prison terms of several decades if convicted, a possible
sentence rather than one imposed. [@2] Villatoro said El Salvador has no room
for criminals or drug sellers and that all would be put where they belong,
behind bars. [@2] Officials said a formal accusation for unlawful drug
trafficking would be presented against the detainees. [@1]
