On 12 August 2026, a joint team working under Sonamura police searched forest land at Dhanirampur ADC village, Bijoynagar ADC village and Batadola in Sepahijala district. [@1,2] The operation included Sonamura police, the 81st Battalion of the Border Security Force, the 11th Battalion of the Tripura State Rifles and forest officials. [@1]

The team found about 27 cannabis seed beds and destroyed approximately 800,000 saplings on site after completing the stated legal formalities. [@1,2] NewsEyes says the plants were spread across the 27 plots and that no arrest was made. [@2]

The operation also destroyed about 20 kilograms of cannabis seeds and 200 kilograms of dried cannabis leaves. [@1,2]
