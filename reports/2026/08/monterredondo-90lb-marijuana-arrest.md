The operation took place in aldea Monterredondo on the road connecting Olancho
with Tegucigalpa. [@2] There officers detained 34-year-old Darwin Ramón Antúnez
Pérez, known as El Gordo. [@2] Police describe him as a suspected member of an
independent criminal structure that moves drugs from Colón toward central and
southern Honduras. [@2] The interception followed months of intelligence,
surveillance and tracking of the group's operations. [@2]

Officers found the consignment hidden in a compartment in the grey pickup he was
driving. [@2] The hiding place was the spare tire, where officers found more
than 45 packages wrapped in transparent plastic. [@2] The National Police
announced the DAET capture of an individual with 90 pounds of marijuana hidden
in a vehicle on the road to Tegucigalpa. [@1] Police gave the preliminary weight
as about 90 pounds (about 40.82 kg) of suspected marijuana, pending laboratory
analysis of its nature and composition. [@2] Officers also seized several mobile
phones and cash with the drug. [@2]

Authorities present the capture as part of a wider investigation rather than an
isolated case. [@2] Specialist teams are still working to identify suppliers,
carriers, receivers and distributors and any links to other criminal
organisations. [@2] The detainee and the seized items were placed at the
disposal of the competent authorities for the courts to determine criminal
responsibility. [@2]
