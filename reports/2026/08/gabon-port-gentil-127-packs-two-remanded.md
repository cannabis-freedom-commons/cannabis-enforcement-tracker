Hendrick Mbina and Patrice Moussavou Mouloungui, alias Ibrahim, were placed in
pretrial detention at Port-Gentil central prison on 28 August 2026. [@1] The two
men had been arrested on the night of 23 August during an operation led by
investigators. [@1] They were presented to an investigating judge on 28 August
before being remanded. [@1] Both men remain presumed innocent while the
proceedings continue. [@1]

The search and investigation found 127 packs of cannabis, known locally as
chanvre indien, plus pieces of paper suspected of being used to prepare the
drug. [@1] Mbina admits holding cannabis but denies any trafficking. [@1] He
says he bought it from itinerant sellers and that it was for his personal use.
[@1] Living at Ndougou for farming, he says he uses cannabis during plantation
work. [@1] He insists that he only consumes cannabis and never trafficked. [@1]

Moussavou also rejects the accusations. [@1] He says the cannabis found at the
scene was not in his room and belonged to his elder brother. [@1] He said
investigators found only six shotgun cartridges in his room. [@1] The case
centres on the seizure at Ndougou in Ogooué-Maritime province. [@1] The
investigation must now establish the packs’ origin and destination and each
man’s exact role. [@1]
