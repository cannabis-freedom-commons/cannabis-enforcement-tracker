On 15 August 2026 border guards at the Brest checkpoint detained a Russian
citizen suspected of trying to bring hashish from Poland into Belarus. [@1]
Belarus's State Border Committee said guards in the Brest crossing stopped an
attempt to move a narcotic from Poland into Belarus that day. [@2] The man was
crossing the border in a minibus. [@1] During the inspection he went to the
driver and quietly dropped the packet under the vehicle's wheels. [@1] Border
guards recorded him doing it. [@3] He was detained and placed in the temporary
detention facility of the Brest border group. [@1]

A border detail working with a service dog found the paper packet beside the
passport-control booth. [@2]

Investigators opened two criminal cases against the man, for illegal drug
trafficking and for moving drugs across the border. [@1] The cases were brought
under Part 1 of Article 328 and Part 1 of Article 328-1 of Belarus's Criminal
Code. [@3]
