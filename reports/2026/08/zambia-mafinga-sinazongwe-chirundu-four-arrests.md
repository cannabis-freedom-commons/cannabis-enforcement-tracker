Zambia's Drug Enforcement Commission arrested four suspects and seized cannabis
in coordinated operations across Muchinga, Southern and Lusaka provinces. [@2]
In the Thendere area of Chilumbi village in Mafinga District, a joint DEC and
Zambia Police team arrested 59-year-old Binwell Chanda for trafficking 18.5
kilogrammes of dry loose cannabis wrapped in banana leaves and concealed in
polythene bags. [@4] In Sinazongwe District, officers arrested 51-year-old
peasant farmer Selina Mwalama of Mwanajoke Village, who was found with 470 balls
and loose cannabis weighing 4.6 kilogrammes. [@4] During the same operation,
officers apprehended 28-year-old Lucky Siabusu, an assistant shopkeeper and
relative of Mwalama, for allegedly attempting to bribe officers with K5,000 to
secure her release. [@4] Siabusu was handed over to the Anti-Corruption
Commission for further investigation. [@4] At the Chirundu One-Stop Border Post,
officers arrested 37-year-old Congolese national Modeste Wetu Kankonde for
trafficking 2.3 kilogrammes of cannabis. [@4] Kankonde was intercepted while
travelling from South Africa and was reportedly destined for the Democratic
Republic of Congo. [@4]

The combined haul was described as more than 25 kilogrammes of cannabis, with
the three stated weights totalling 25.4 kilogrammes. [@1] DEC public relations
officer Allan Tamba said in a statement issued in Lusaka on Monday that the
intelligence-led operations were part of intensified efforts to disrupt illicit
drug supply chains. [@3] All four suspects remained in lawful custody awaiting
court processes. [@3]

The operations were presented as part of the commission's broader campaign
against drug trafficking networks operating across Zambian regions. [@1] Tamba
warned that border-control operations had been intensified and that people
engaged in transnational drug trafficking would be arrested and prosecuted. [@3]
