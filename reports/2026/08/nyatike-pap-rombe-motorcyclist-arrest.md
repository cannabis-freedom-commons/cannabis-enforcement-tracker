Officers from Macalder Police Station arrested one suspected drug trafficker in
the Pap Rombe area of Nyatike Sub-County, Migori County on 2 August 2026. [@2]
The officers were on routine surveillance in the Pap Rombe area when they
intercepted a person on a motorcycle carrying a suspicious sack. [@2] The
motorcyclist had been signalled to stop but failed to comply, prompting a swift
pursuit that ended with officers intercepting and detaining him shortly
afterwards. [@1]

A thorough search of the rider and his luggage uncovered several rolls of
suspected cannabis sativa tightly wrapped and concealed beneath layers of
clothing. [@1] Officers impounded the motorcycle and secured the recovered rolls
as exhibits for prosecution. [@1] The suspect was held at Macalder Police
Station pending court arraignment. [@1]

The National Police Service described the operation as part of its continuing
action against drug abuse, narcotics and illicit brews in Migori County. [@2] It
said officers remain steadfast in that effort and appealed for public
information through police stations and its emergency and reporting lines. [@2]
