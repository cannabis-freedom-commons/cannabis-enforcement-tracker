Curacao police arrested a 26-year-old man at Hato International Airport in
Willemstad. [@1] Police identified the suspect only by the initials J.R.A.H.P.
[@1] The suspect was born in Curacao. [@1] He is suspected of violating the
Opium Ordinance. [@1] After the arrest, he was brought before an assistant
public prosecutor, who ordered that he remain in custody pending further
investigation. [@1]

The arresting authority was the Curacao Police Force, known as KPC. [@1] The
arrest took place at approximately 10:25 p.m. on Monday, August 17, shortly
after the suspect arrived on Curacao. [@1] Police allege that he attempted to
enter the island carrying a quantity of hashish from the Netherlands. [@1]
Police did not disclose the amount of hashish allegedly found in the suspect's
possession. [@1] Police provided no details about how the drugs were discovered.
[@1] The investigation into the case is continuing. [@1]
