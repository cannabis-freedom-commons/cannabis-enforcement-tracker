Officers of the Anyama police district station arrested two young people at the
cattle market in Anyama at about 4:30 a.m. on Sunday 23 August 2026, acting on a
tip. [@1] A search of the two found several boulettes of cannabis, pressed balls
of the drug, and medicines presented as of inferior quality or falsified. [@1]

Police took both to the station; they are K.A., 19, who lives in Anyama's
Ancienne Gendarmerie quarter, and T.M., 16, unemployed and living in the
Contrôle quarter. [@1] The two were brought to the prosecutor's office at the
Abobo court of first instance at 8 a.m. on Monday 24 August 2026. [@1]

The arrest was made under Vacances sécurisées 11, the Ivorian holiday-period
security drive, which aims to strengthen police presence and curb activity that
disturbs public order. [@1] Police said the find led them to examine where the
products came from and whether the two were selling them. [@1]

A second Ivorian account gives the same two ages and reports that the pair were
held at the disposal of the judicial authorities after being taken to the
station. [@2]
