Syria's Anti-Narcotics Department arrested two people suspected of possessing
narcotics after a security operation in Aleppo city's Al-Suryan neighbourhood.
[@2] The force said it set a tight ambush that caught two men identified only by
the initials M.M. and A.K. [@2] The two detainees were referred to the competent
judiciary for legal proceedings against them. [@1]

Anti-narcotics officers seized 61 kilograms of hashish during the operation in
Aleppo's Al-Suryan neighbourhood. [@1] Officers also recovered about 220,000
Captagon tablets with a total weight of 37 kilograms. [@1] The seizure
additionally included 700 grams of a narcotic substance identified as H-boz.
[@1]
