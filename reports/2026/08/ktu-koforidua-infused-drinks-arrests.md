NACOC arrested ten people over cannabis-infused products during Koforidua Technical University's SRC Week celebrations. [@1,2,3,4]

Ghana's Narcotics Control Commission (NACOC) arrested ten people, nine men
and one woman, on the Koforidua Technical University campus on Friday night
during the Students' Representative Council (SRC) Week celebrations, in what
the commission called an intelligence-led operation. [@1,3] Officers recovered
wraps of suspected cannabis along with cups of "abele" ice cream, bottles of
pineapple juice, sobolo drinks, fresh palm wine and other beverages believed
to have been infused with cannabis and prepared for distribution to
celebrants. [@1,3,4] Preliminary field tests on the exhibits indicated the
presence of cannabis. [@1,4]

NACOC said the operation, which ran from Friday 31 July into Saturday 1 August
2026, broke up an organised network producing and selling cannabis-infused
products on campus, allegedly led by 23-year-old Priscilla Tetteh with nine
other young men. [@3,4] The commission called the trade a threat to public
health and young people's safety and warned that producing or selling food and
drinks containing controlled substances is illegal. [@3] The ten suspects
remained in NACOC custody assisting investigations, with no formal charges
announced. [@1,3]
