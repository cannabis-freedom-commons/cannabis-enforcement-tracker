A months-long drug investigation ended on Wednesday 5 August 2026 with a traffic
stop on Mall Road in St. Clairsville, Ohio. [@3] During the stop, detectives
arrested Donald Daniels, 36, of Cleveland, and seized 132 grams of marijuana and
approximately 110 grams of suspected crack cocaine/cocaine. [@1] Daniels was
taken into custody without incident. [@4]

Belmont County Sheriff James G. Zusack said the quantity of narcotics seized
qualifies Daniels for a Major Drug Offender Specification under Ohio law. [@1]
Daniels was booked into the Belmont County Jail on one count of possession of
drugs and one count of trafficking in drugs, and was being held on a $1 million
bond. [@2]

Detectives with the Belmont County Sheriff’s Office Criminal Interdiction Unit
and the Ohio Organized Crime Investigations Commission conducted the traffic
stop and arrested Daniels. [@2] Zusack commended the Criminal Interdiction Unit
for its continued efforts to prevent illegal narcotics from reaching Belmont
County communities. [@2]
