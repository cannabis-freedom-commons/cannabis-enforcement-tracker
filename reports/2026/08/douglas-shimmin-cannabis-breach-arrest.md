Stephen George Shimmin was arrested on 29 August 2026 in Nelson Street in
Douglas, initially for breaching his bail conditions. [@1] A police search of
his pocket found 2.9 grams of cannabis, valued by police at £58. [@1] Shimmin
admitted possessing the cannabis. [@1] Sentencing was adjourned until 13
November 2026 at the Court of General Gaol Delivery, where he is due to be
sentenced for provoking behaviour, a telecommunications offence, common assault,
criminal damage and threatening behaviour. [@1] He was on bail at the time of
the arrest. [@1]

The possession put him in breach of a suspended sentence imposed in December
2025 after he received more than £30,000 worth of cannabis in the post. [@1]
That sentence was 20 months in prison suspended for 20 months with supervision,
after he pleaded guilty to two counts of being concerned in importing cannabis.
[@2] Customs and Excise officers intercepted two parcels of cannabis addressed
to him at a Douglas post office, one containing 592.5 grams worth £11,870 and
one sent from Thailand containing 999 grams worth £19,980. [@2]
