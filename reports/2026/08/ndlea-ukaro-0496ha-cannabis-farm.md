NDLEA operatives in Edo raided a cannabis plantation in Ukaro Forest, Owan West Local Government Area, on 18 August 2026 and arrested Imohimi Ugbeje, 50, Usihoro Saturday, 27, and Bala Sunday, 23. [@1,2]

The farm covered approximately 0.496 hectares and had an estimated potential yield of 1,240.9 kilograms. [@1,2] NDLEA destroyed the plantation and recovered cannabis plant stems plus half a bag of processed cannabis weighing 5.5 kilograms. [@1,2]

The articles also describe separate NDLEA operations on 19 August in Edo, including a warehouse raid and an urban cannabis arrest; those events are left out of this report. [@1,2]
