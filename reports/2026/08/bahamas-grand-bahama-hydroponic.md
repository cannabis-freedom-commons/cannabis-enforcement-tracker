Officers of the Bahamas Drug Enforcement Unit arrested three men aged 24, 25 and 26 after searching a residence on Scott Street in Grand Bahama, police reported. [@1,2]

The search took place shortly after 9:40 pm on Friday 7 August 2026. [@1,2] Officers, assisted by the Mobile Division, executed a search warrant at the home. [@1] They found about 1.3 pounds (0.59 kg) of suspected hydroponic marijuana and $3,295 in cash. [@1,2] The Tribune reported that police valued the cannabis at $2,060 and seized the money as suspected proceeds of crime. [@1]

The three men were arrested. [@1,2] The report did not name them or say whether they had been charged. [@1,2]

The Grand Bahama search was one of three drug operations on New Providence, Grand Bahama and Bimini on Thursday 6 and Friday 7 August in which four people were arrested and more than $21,000 of suspected marijuana products were seized, the Tribune reported. [@1]
