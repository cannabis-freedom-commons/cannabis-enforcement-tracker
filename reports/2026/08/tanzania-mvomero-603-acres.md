The Drug Control and Enforcement Authority (DCEA) reported destroying 603 acres of cannabis
farms in Mvomero district, Morogoro region, and seizing 49 bags of processed cannabis. [@1,2]
The 603 acres equal about 244 hectares. [@1,2] Both reports place the farms in four villages:
Misengele, Lugenge, Kisaki and Nyarutanga. [@1,2]

The operation also led to the arrest of 13 suspects and the dismantling of 21 temporary camps
used by growers to manage the remote farmland. [@1,2] Tanzania Insight attributes the
announcement to DCEA Commissioner General Aretas Lyimo, who described the action as part of an
ongoing effort to combat drug production. [@2] The Guardian and Tanzania Insight published
their accounts on 18 and 17 August respectively, but neither gives the date when officers
entered the farms. [@1,2]

The sources do not count the plants destroyed or give the weight of the crops or the 49 bags.
[@1,2] They also do not name the 13 suspects or report what happened to them after arrest.
