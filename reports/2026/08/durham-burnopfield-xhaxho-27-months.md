## The sentence

Erik Xhaxho, 24, was sentenced to 27 months in prison after being caught tending a
professional-scale cannabis grow in a former shop in Burnopfield, County Durham.
[@1,2] He had been deported to Albania in 2024 after serving a ten-month sentence in
Scotland for tending a cannabis farm in an apparently empty detached house in
Kirkcaldy, Fife, that January. [@2]

## The raid

Police executed a search warrant at the Burnopfield premises on 26 June and found
Xhaxho inside, with 200 cannabis plants and 518 seedlings spread across the ground
floor shop and the flat above. [@1,2] The operation used professional heating and
lighting equipment, and expert analysis put the potential yield at 20 to 60 kg, worth
an estimated £57,000 to £207,000. [@1,2]

## Return and removal

Xhaxho told the court he had returned to the United Kingdom to pay off debts, and he
accepted that he would be deported a second time. [@2] Judge Richard Bennett said the
set-up was plainly commercial and that Xhaxho, given his previous offence, would have
known its scale. [@2] The judge warned him that coming back again would likely mean a
sentence measured in years rather than months. [@2]
