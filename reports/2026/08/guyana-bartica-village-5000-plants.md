Police conducted the eradication at Bartica Village, Upper Berbice River, on Thursday 6 August 2026, between 06:00 and 12:30. [@1,2] They destroyed a field estimated at two acres containing about 5,000 suspected plants, three to six inches tall. [@1,2] A makeshift camp measured about 10 by 10 feet, and a search there found a 12-gauge shotgun and six cartridges. [@1,2]

Officers uprooted and destroyed the plants, burned the camp, and took the firearm and ammunition to Central Police Station. [@1,2] No arrests were made and police said the investigation continued. [@1,2] News Room describes the location as Bartica Village, Upper Berbice River, while Guyana Times adds Region 10, Upper Demerara-Berbice. [@1,2] Both articles repeat the police account, so the approximate count and area have no independent measurement, and neither identifies who owned the farm or firearm. [@1,2] The News Room page publishes one photograph of the cleared field and another showing the seized shotgun and cartridges. [@1]
