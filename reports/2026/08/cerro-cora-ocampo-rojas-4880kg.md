Special agents of Paraguay's National Anti-Drug Secretariat (Senad) from its
Regional Department No. 1 in Pedro Juan Caballero raided a rural property in
Colonia Capitán Raúl Ocampo Rojas, in the Cerro Corá district of Amambay
department. [@1] The operation began at about 08:00 on Friday 14 August 2026,
with support from the Senad's special forces. [@2]

The officers found and destroyed three hectares of cannabis still in the growth
stage. [@1] They also burned 4,880 kilograms of harvested cannabis: branches
drying on wires, branches stored under a tarpaulin and bags of chopped
marijuana. [@1] InfoNortePY put the drying material at 3,250 kilograms on wires
and 850 kilograms under the tarpaulin, plus 26 bags holding about 780 kilograms
of chopped marijuana. [@2] The same account said 26 bags held about 780
kilograms of chopped marijuana, and that the harvested drug totalled about 4,880
kilograms before the plantation left standing was counted. [@2]

Officers destroyed a camp and seven zarandas, sieves used to process the drug.
[@1] The Senad counted about 14 tonnes taken out of circulation by adding the
potential harvest of the eradicated three hectares to the processed cannabis
found on the property. [@1] The agency estimated the value of the destroyed drug
on the Brazilian market at about USD 2 million. [@1]

Nobody was arrested during the raid. [@2] The prosecutor's office in Pedro Juan
Caballero accompanied the operation through specialised anti-narcotics unit No.
1, led by prosecutor Celso René Morales. [@1] All the evidence was destroyed on
site after photographic and audiovisual recording, and a prosecutor's record of
the raid was drawn up. [@1]
