Sugauli police in Motihari district of Bihar arrested a Nepali youth with ganja
during an anti-drug campaign. [@1] The arrested youth was identified as Rajan
Mukhiya, son of Ramakant Mukhiya, a resident of Jitpur Sub-Metropolis-1 in Bara
district. [@1] Sugauli station in-charge Anish Kumar Singh said police mounted
special surveillance and action on the basis of secret information on Tuesday.
[@1] Officers took Rajan into custody and recovered 750 grams of ganja from his
possession. [@1] Dainik Bhaskar reported the youth was arrested with 750 grams
of ganja near the main market in Sugauli town on Wednesday. [@2] Bhaskar
reported the action was carried out by a police team under station in-charge
Anish Kumar Singh watching suspicious activity. [@2] Bhaskar identified the
detained youth as Rajan Mukhiya, son of Ramakant Mukhiya, resident of Jitpur in
Bara district of Nepal. [@2]

Police seized the recovered ganja and initiated legal proceedings against the
arrested youth. [@1] Sugauli police said further investigation into the incident
is under way. [@1] Investigators are questioning him to establish where the
ganja was brought from and to whom it was to be delivered. [@2]

Ratopati reported the youth had entered Indian territory with ganja from the
Jitpur area and was arrested about 35 kilometres inside India. [@1] Ratopati
described trafficking of drugs and illegal goods in the border areas as a
continuing challenge because of the open Nepal-India border. [@1]
