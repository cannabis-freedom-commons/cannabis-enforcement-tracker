Allen police arrested musician Xavier Landum, known as BigXthaPlug. [@1] The
arrest took place on Friday 21 August 2026. [@2] Officers booked Landum into the
Collin County Jail on charges of driving while intoxicated, possession of less
than two ounces of marijuana and unlawful possession of a firearm. [@2] Landum
was released from jail on Saturday. [@1]

Driving while intoxicated and possession of less than two ounces of marijuana
are Class B misdemeanors, while unlawful firearm possession is a Class A
misdemeanor. [@1] The new charges are separate from the case stemming from last
year's arrest. [@1] Landum was free on bond in the earlier Dallas case when he
was taken into custody. [@1] Allen police have released few details about what
led to the arrest, saying the investigation remains active. [@1]

He had been scheduled to headline the Country Rising Festival in Winnipeg that
night but did not appear after organizers cited a personal emergency. [@1]
Landum was released in time to travel to Des Moines and perform at the Iowa
State Fair Grandstand on Saturday night. [@1] During the show he acknowledged
having been in jail the previous day. [@1] The August 2025 Dallas arrest
followed a traffic stop after officers saw Landum driving a pickup truck without
a front license plate. [@1] Authorities said they found a firearm and a small
amount of marijuana inside the vehicle, and that case remains pending. [@1] In
February 2025 Landum had been arrested in Arlington for marijuana possession,
and those charges were later dismissed. [@2]
