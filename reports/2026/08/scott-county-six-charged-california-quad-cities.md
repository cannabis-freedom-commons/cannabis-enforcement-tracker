Six women face ongoing criminal conduct charges in Scott County over an alleged
years-long conspiracy to ship California marijuana to the Quad Cities. [@1,2,3] They
are charged with felony ongoing criminal conduct and influence enterprise. [@1]
Julia Louise Hoskinson is 38 and lives in Bettendorf. [@1] Aliyah Renita Marie
Hinton is 31 and lives in Pleasant Valley. [@1] Mariah D-yarea Anderson is 27
and lives in Milan, Illinois. [@1] Erickah Jo Jensen is 41 and lives in
Davenport. [@1] Mandy Nichole Shaw is 42 and lives in Davenport. [@1] Nakeya
Shontay Brown is 39 and lives in Davenport. [@1]

Hoskinson allegedly received marijuana packages from California at her home,
paid the source over $14,000 through Cash App and Zelle, delivered marijuana to
a co-defendant and referred customers. [@1] Hinton allegedly received drug
payments, sent money to the California source and stored 14 pounds (about 6.35
kg) of marijuana at her residence in packaging bearing a co-defendant's
fingerprint. [@1] Anderson allegedly received drug payments and sent funds to
the California supply source. [@1] Brown allegedly sent money to the California
supply source. [@1] Jensen allegedly sent $5,000 to the California marijuana
supplier and had over $25,000 in cash seized from her home, and she was already
facing eluding charges after police tried to pull her over on May 6. [@1] Shaw
allegedly sent large sums of money to the California source for a co-defendant
and allowed marijuana duffle bags and boxes to be stored in her vehicle. [@1]

Court documents show multiple warrants were executed for money transactions in
the case. [@1] The alleged operation bought marijuana in California and sold it
in the Quad Cities area. [@1] All six women were released after posting
cash-only bonds, according to court records. [@1]

Two more people were arrested in Scott County for allegedly selling and distributing marijuana, bringing the case's arrested and charged totals to six. [@3]

Eight of the nine alleged conspirators in the case have been arrested. [@4] Davenport police issued a warrant in July 2026 for the ninth alleged conspirator, a woman accused of selling and distributing marijuana. [@4]
