Bhanupratappur police arrested Subhash Koreti, 40, after acting on information about cannabis cultivation in his home garden at Bharsa Tarhul village on 26 August 2026. [@1] Haribhoomi and Janta Se Rishta also report the arrest and identify the village as the site of the cultivation. [@2,3]

Officers found the plants growing among pigeon pea and other crops and seized them with their roots and soil. [@1] Amar Ujala reports 850 plants weighing about 8.66 kilograms and an estimated value of Rs 430,000; the two other accounts describe the count as more than 850 and do not give a weight. [@1,2,3]

Police registered a case under sections 8 and 20(a) of India's Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act and presented Koreti in court. [@1]
