Gauteng police raided a property in De Deur in the Sedibeng District that had
been converted into a hydroponic dagga laboratory. [@1] Five foreign nationals
were arrested at the property during the police operation. [@1,2] Two of the five
people arrested were Jamaican nationals. [@1] The other people arrested were
from Malawi and Lesotho. [@1]

Police found and seized hydroponic dagga valued at approximately R1-million at
the property. [@1,2] Officers also seized two firearms at the property alongside
the dagga. [@1]

The five arrested people were expected to appear before the Meyerton Magistrates
Court shortly after the raid. [@1,2]

The five comprised two Jamaican nationals, two Malawian nationals and one Lesotho national. [@2] Police recovered a 303 rifle and a 9mm Glock pistol during the operation. [@2] The suspects face charges relating to contravention of Sections 3 and 5(b) of the Drug Trafficking Act 140 of 1992. [@2] Two of the five suspects face additional charges of possession of unlicensed firearms and ammunition. [@2]
