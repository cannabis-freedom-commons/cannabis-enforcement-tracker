Police in Buryatia reported on 28 August 2026 the results of the second stage of Mak-2026, held from
12 to 21 August. [@1,2] Officers surveyed more than 1.237 million hectares of land and found 254
patches of wild cannabis covering more than 745 hectares. [@1,2]

Landowners received 180 orders to destroy the plants. [@1,2] By the time of the reports 621 hectares
had been mown and burned. [@2] Officers also seized about 17.8 kilograms of drugs, mostly marijuana
but also hashish, cannabis oil and synthetic methadone. [@2] Thirty-eight people were made
administratively liable for using drugs without a medical prescription. [@1,2]
