Dakar’s regional drug squad arrested a woman after seizing a parcel sent from
the United States. [@1] Officers traced the parcel to the home of its addressee,
described as the sender’s wife, in a controlled delivery organised with the
usual courier. [@1] The woman was detained in her apartment at the moment she
received the parcel. [@1] She told investigators she acted as an intermediary
receiving parcels her husband sent from the United States. [@1] She said another
person came to collect part of each delivery while she kept the rest. [@1]

The parcel contained 10.60 kilograms of hashish, the cannabis component recorded
here. [@2] The same parcel also held 193 grams of white powder suspected to be
cocaine and 170 grams of beige sweets suspected to contain MDMA, noted only to
explain the mixed seizure and excluded from the cannabis figure. [@3] The case
began at about 5 p.m. after a New York-Dakar groupage service alerted officers
to suspect goods in a parcel entrusted to it. [@1] Investigators went to the
carrier’s shop and confirmed the suspect products inside the parcel. [@1] A
later search of her apartment found underwear and other items similar to those
in the parcel. [@1]

A GP is a private groupage parcel-forwarding service, here linking New York and
Dakar. [@3] The woman was placed in custody for alleged criminal association,
possession and international drug trafficking and money laundering. [@1] The
seized products and objects were placed under seal and inquiries were continuing
to identify others who may have been involved. [@1]
