On 7 August a man was apprehended in La Alborada in northern Guayaquil during an
operation in which police seized 87 packages of marijuana, some of them
flavoured. [@1] The National Police carried out the procedure as part of
operational strategy 3D after intelligence work by its personnel and the Armed
Forces. [@1] The suspect was travelling in a vehicle and allegedly distributed
drugs to organised-crime groups. [@1]

Officers seized 87 packages made up of 68 blocks and 19 bags, and approved tests
preliminarily identified the substance as marijuana. [@1] Zone 8 deputy
commander Colonel Christian Rengifo Dávila said between 16 and 18 packages held
flavoured drugs while the rest held unflavoured substance. [@1] Police said the
packages equalled about 87,000 doses for domestic consumption with an
approximate value of $34,000 on the national market. [@1] Police said the drugs
were possibly from Peru and intended for domestic trafficking and retail sale,
and that the detained Ecuadorian man had no criminal record. [@1] The vehicle
used to move the substance was retained, although it had no robbery complaint.
[@1]

Flavoured marijuana is a type of cannabis distributed in thermal plastic bags
and processed through a technical laboratory procedure. [@1] Producers add
aromatic or flavouring components to alter its original characteristics, and
police said it is sold mainly in nightlife centres. [@1] Through DIPA, the
police anti-drug information and prevention department, specialised staff give
preventive talks in schools and public and private units aimed at young people.
[@1]
