Charleston police arrested a North Charleston man on drug charges after a foot
chase in the early morning of 7 August 2026. [@2] When the man came back to the
vehicle they tried to speak with him, but he ran toward Savannah Highway; they
caught him on foot a short distance away. [@1] The arrested man was 27 years old
and from North Charleston. [@2]

He was charged with trafficking cocaine, trafficking cocaine base, possessing
marijuana and resisting arrest. [@1] After the arrest, officers recovered more
than 10 grams of cocaine, more than 15 grams of crack cocaine, eight grams of
marijuana hidden in the gas cap, over $1,000 in cash and a digital scale. [@1]
The marijuana was the only cannabis found, eight grams hidden in the vehicle's
gas cap, while the bulk of the seizure was cocaine and crack cocaine. [@2]

He was booked into the Al Cannon Detention Center on a $50,000 bond. [@1]
