The Edo State command of Nigeria's National Drug Law Enforcement Agency (NDLEA) destroyed two
cannabis farms at Ugboku, in the Igbanke forest of Orhionmwon local government area. [@1,3] Together
they covered 1.85 hectares, which the agency said would have yielded about 4,629 kilograms. [@1] A
40-year-old man, Agholor Elebojie, was arrested in connection with the first farm, and 12.4 kilograms
of processed cannabis were found at the second. [@1]

## Arrests in Benin City

On 13 August, agents arrested nine men aged 20 to 35 at Abuja Quarters in Benin City, a place the
agency calls a drug flashpoint. [@1] Most were found with small amounts: a few hundred grams of
cannabis or "Loud", together with tramadol, diazepam and other pills. [@1] The men come from
Nasarawa, Katsina, Kano and Benue states, among others. [@1] On 14 August, a 28-year-old from Delta
State was arrested at Oluku with about 26 kilograms of cannabis and 93 grams of methamphetamine. [@1]
The command said all of them are in custody while investigators prepare their prosecution. [@1]

## "Cannot burn its way out of poverty"

A day later, Omoyele Sowore, a human rights activist and presidential candidate for 2027, called the
destruction of cannabis farms wasteful and told the agency to stop. [@2] He asked for cannabis to be
legalised and regulated, and for research into its medical and industrial uses, saying an industry
could create jobs and government revenue. [@2]

The first plantation covered 0.699792 hectares and the second 1.151889 hectares, according to the command. [@1,4]
