Lynson Henderson Cox, a 29-year-old of Lewis Gap, St Michael, appeared before
Magistrate Gayle Scott in the District 'A' Traffic Court on Saturday, where he
pleaded guilty to trafficking and importing cannabis. [@1] The Barbados Police
Service said the offences occurred on July 13 and involved suspected cannabis
with an estimated street value of $128,000. [@1] The suspected cannabis weighed
eight kilogrammes. [@1]

The court fined Cox $30,000 over the two offences. [@1] Cox was ordered to pay
the fine within six months or spend six months at Dodds prison. [@1] The
Barbados Police Service Narcotics Unit arrested and formally charged Cox. [@1]
