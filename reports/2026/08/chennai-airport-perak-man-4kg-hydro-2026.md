Chennai Customs officers detained a 54-year-old Malaysian man from Perak at
Chennai International Airport on Saturday 8 August 2026, after finding 4 kg of
hydroponic cannabis in his luggage. [@1,2] He had arrived from Bangkok on Thai
Airways flight TG 337, and Chennai Customs officers found the cannabis concealed
inside his baggage in a thorough inspection. [@1,2]

Indian Customs held him in remand for further investigation and he faces
investigation under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act
1985 pending trial. [@1] Under that law, trafficking or possession of drugs in
commercial quantities carries a mandatory prison sentence of between 10 and 20
years and a substantial fine on conviction. [@1]

Bukit Aman Narcotics Crime Investigation Department director Comm Datuk Hussein
Omar Khan said Malaysian police obtained detailed information about the man,
including his identity and flight schedule, and believe he got the supply in
Thailand because he carried it from Bangkok. [@1] Hussein said police would
monitor whether the man was linked to a syndicate in Malaysia or was recruited
as a drug mule in Thailand or Malaysia. [@1] Initial checks showed the man had
no previous criminal record, and Malaysian police received official information
on the arrest from India's Narcotics Control Bureau. [@1,2]

The suspect was believed to have tried to leave through the Green Channel without declaring anything to customs. [@2] An X-ray scan showed a suspicious image in his checked baggage, which was then examined in detail. [@2] The inspection found four packages of greenish lumpy material with a strong, pungent smell. [@2]
