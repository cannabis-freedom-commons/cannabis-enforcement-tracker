Prosecutors from El Salvador's Fiscalía General de la República (FGR) and
National Civil Police officers carried out simultaneous pre-dawn raids on
Saturday 1 August 2026 in Lourdes, San Juan Opico and Villa Suchit, in the
department of La Libertad. [@1] Prosecutors announced the capture of five people
accused of belonging to a structure trafficking and distributing drugs in
several areas of La Libertad. [@2] Police arrested Katia María Fernández
Salazar, identified by the FGR as the network's leader and main operator,
working from colonia Las Jacarandas. [@1] Four others were arrested with her:
Wendy Yamileth Pineda López, José Mauricio Moreno Zapata, Brayan Alexander
García Martínez and Jorge Alberto Pineda Ponce. [@3] The detainees will be
prosecuted for illicit drug trafficking and unlawful association, the FGR said.
[@3] Five more members of the network were already in custody and will be
notified of the new charges, while two defendants will be tried in absentia.
[@2]

The FGR said the group began operating in early 2026, selling cocaine, marijuana
and methamphetamine brought in along routes between Guatemala and El Salvador
through small-dose street sales, known in El Salvador as narcomenudeo. [@1]
Officers seized 1,427 grams of marijuana, a further portion of the same drug,
248 grams of methamphetamine and 220 grams of cocaine. [@2] The same operation
yielded three mobile phones, a laptop, a digital scale, a revolver and $395 in
cash. [@2] El Salvador Times placed the raids in Colón district, in cantón
Lourdes, and in San Juan Opico and Sacacoyo, mainly Villa Suchit. [@3] Diario El
Salvador also listed Versalles among the search sites. [@2] Prosecutors said
they were also weighing a money-laundering charge after tracing remittances sent
to Guatemala and Mexico. [@2]

A prosecutor who led the operation said the network hauled the drugs from
Guatemala City and sold them in El Salvador under the narcomenudeo model, the
small-dose retail trade. [@2] The FGR released photographs of agents during the
raids and of the seized evidence, including containers with drugs and electronic
devices. [@1]
