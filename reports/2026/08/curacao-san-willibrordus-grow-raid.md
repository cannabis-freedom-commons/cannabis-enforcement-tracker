Police on Curaçao searched an address given as San Willibrordus z/n at about 6 a.m. on Thursday
20 August 2026 and found a large cannabis cultivation. [@1,2] Officers from the Division of
Organised Crime (DKO) seized a large quantity of marijuana plants, along with equipment and
materials used to grow them. [@1,2] The police did not disclose the number of plants, the area
cultivated or an estimated value. [@1,2] They said the search was part of an ongoing investigation
into cannabis cultivation. [@1,2]

The accounts differ on arrests. The police force said no one had been arrested as of Thursday.
[@1,2] Curaçao.nu, citing Èxtra, reported that several people who lived on the site were detained
and that officers found at least five large fields of plants. [@3] The police said the investigation
was continuing to establish who was responsible and whether others were involved. [@1,2] No charges
or court steps were reported in any of the accounts. [@1,2,3]
