Three Cuban men were detained in the parking lot of Mercado 28 in Cancún after
police saw them allegedly exchanging small bags with apparent drugs during a
surveillance patrol. [@3] The detention occurred on Avenida Xel-Há in
Supermanzana 28 in Benito Juárez while officers were on prevention and
surveillance patrols. [@1] Police officers observed three individuals exchanging
bags with green herb and decided to intervene. [@2] The detainees were
identified as Yamir Jesús “N”, Luis David “N” and Carlos Alejandro “N”. [@1]

During the search, officers found bags with substances resembling marijuana and
crystal, plus a cell phone. [@1] Yamir Jesús “N” had 39 bags of green herb and
31 bags of crystal-like fragments plus a cell phone. [@1] Luis David “N” had 37
bags of green herb and 26 bags of a crystal-like substance. [@1] Carlos
Alejandro “N” had three bags of green herb, three bags of crystal-like fragments
and three ecological cloth bags of different colours. [@1] In total, authorities
seized 139 doses of suspected drugs described as substances resembling marijuana
and crystal. [@1] Authorities seized 79 bags of green herb. [@2] Authorities
seized 60 bags of crystal-like fragments. [@2]

The three men and the seized items were handed to the Specialised Prosecutor for
Combating Narcomenudeo, which will open an investigation and determine their
legal situation. [@1] Authorities had not reported any link between the three
men detained in the Mercado 28 parking lot and a specific criminal organisation.
