Karell Deshon Allen, a man from Dallas, was arrested in Carter County, Oklahoma,
for trafficking 96 pounds of marijuana. [@1] Carter County District Attorney
Melissa Handke said Allen was sentenced to 22 years in prison. [@1] The sentence
covered three offences: drug trafficking, obstructing an officer by running
away, and possession of burglary tools. [@1]

The obstruction count arose from Allen running away from an officer, while the
remaining counts were the drug-trafficking charge and possession of burglary
tools. [@1] The 96 pounds of marijuana behind the trafficking count converts to
about 43.5 kilograms. [@1] The district attorney announced the prison term for
the Carter County case, in which Allen is the only person named. [@1] The
22-year prison term is the punishment stated for the three counts together. [@1]
