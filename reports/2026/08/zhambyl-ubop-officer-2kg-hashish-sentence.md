Officers of the Department of the National Security Committee for Zhambyl Region
detained a police lieutenant colonel who served as an operative in the
organised-crime department (UBOP). [@1] The Bayzak District Court of Zhambyl
Region found him guilty of unlawful drug trafficking. [@1] The court imposed
seven years of imprisonment for the offence. [@1] An amnesty reduced that term
by one-third. [@1] He is therefore to serve four years and eight months in a
medium-security colony. [@1] The court also stripped the officer of his police
rank. [@1] The verdict had not yet entered into force. [@1]

During a search at his workplace, officers found hashish weighing more than two
kilograms. [@1]
