Organized-crime police in Arad and DIICOT prosecutors documented two men suspected of risk-drug
trafficking after catching them on the morning of 14 August 2026 in an outdoor cannabis plot in
extravilan Lipova, on the Mureș riverbank. [@1,2]

Investigators said the pair had set up the plantation in 2026 for sale; officers found 101
cannabis plants in various stages of growth, totalling about 65 kilograms of green plant mass,
hidden among vegetables and netting with a generator, irrigation gear and a surveillance camera
nearby. [@1,2] Searches at their homes later recovered grinding and packaging equipment and
further cannabis, the communiqué said. [@1]

Prosecutors ordered both men detained on 14 August and asked a judge for 30 days' preventive
arrest; ARADON reported the Arad tribunal granted detention from 15 August 2026. [@1,2]
