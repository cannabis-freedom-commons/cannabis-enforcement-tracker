Police Regional Office Cordillera and the Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency destroyed an estimated 750,000 fully grown marijuana plants in Barangay Loccong, Tinglayan, Kalinga, on 12 August 2026. [@1]

The operation involved Kalinga police intelligence and drug-enforcement units, municipal police stations from Tinglayan, Balbalan, Lubuagan and Tanudan, two provincial mobile-force companies and PDEA-Kalinga. [@1] Officers destroyed and burned the plants at the site to keep them from entering the illegal market. [@1]

The PRO-CAR account put the standard drug price of the crop at about PHP 150 million and said the investigation and patrols would continue to identify the cultivators and other plantation sites. [@1]
