The interceptions occurred between 5 August 2026 and 14 August 2026. [@1] The
third incident occurred at Nsubane on August 5, when troops intercepted a
30-year-old Liswati man carrying 9.85kg of dagga valued at E9 850 without a
permit. [@2] The Hluthi suspect was a 52-year-old liSwati man from Ka-Mziki.
[@1] On the same day at Hluthi, troops intercepted another suspect carrying
1.85kg of dagga valued at E1 850. [@2] At Nsubane in the Lubombo Region on
August 14, troops intercepted a white Opel Corsa carrying 5.1kg of dagga valued
at E5 100. [@2] Two emaSwati men were arrested. [@2]

The UEDF also reported the seizure of 16.8kg of dagga with a combined estimated
street value of E16 800 in three separate incidents. [@2] The UEDF said all
suspects, vehicles, livestock and dagga consignments were handed over to the
relevant law enforcement agencies for further investigation. [@2]
