Gendarmes of the Saint-Pierre PSIG, the gendarmerie's surveillance and
intervention platoon, watched a suspected drug sale at a fruit and vegetable
stand in front of the Roches Maigres vocational school in Saint-Louis on
Wednesday 26 August 2026. [@1] They stopped the buyer and found 13 g of zamal,
cannabis in Réunion's Creole, on him. [@1] The seller, a fruit and vegetable
vendor, was arrested and the autonomous territorial brigade of Saint-Louis
gendarmerie opened a flagrant inquiry. [@1] LINFO reported that the gendarmes
confirmed their suspicions by checking the buyer, who held 13 g of zamal. [@4]

Under his stand officers found 210 g of packaged zamal and 2,199 euros in cash.
[@2] A search of his home turned up another 1,274 g of zamal and 14,530 euros.
[@2] Le Quotidien puts the total at 1.484 kg of zamal and 16,729 euros in cash.
[@2] Réunion La 1ère describes the haul as close to 1.5 kg of drugs and more
than 16,000 euros. [@1] Réunion La 1ère reports pouches and rolls of zamal
packed for sale beneath the fruit, along with large sums in cash. [@1] Imaz
Press said more than 16,000 euros in cash was found at the suspect's home. [@3]
The gendarmerie said selling drugs next to a school is an aggravating
circumstance that draws reinforced surveillance from its units. [@3]

The man faces judicial proceedings, and no charge, custody ruling or sentence
has been reported. [@2] The gendarmerie stressed that zamal is not a synthetic
drug and lacks the sudden effects of crack, but remains a narcotic that harms
health. [@1] It said both selling and buying or using are offences that lead to
convictions and can even cost a driving licence. [@1]
