Police stopped a car for a check at the Burnside checkpoint in Coronie district
on the evening of Sunday 23 August 2026. [@1,2] In the car they found plant
material - dried leaves, stems and seeds - that strongly resembled Cannabis
sativa, packed in several plastic bags. [@1,2]

The three people inside were arrested: a 39-year-old known by the initials N.H.,
a 42-year-old K.B. and an 18-year-old Guyanese national, A.J. [@1,2] Officers
took them to the Coronie police station. [@1]

Police seized the car and the material. [@1] After consulting the Public
Prosecution Service (Openbaar Ministerie), they held the three in custody (in
verzekering gesteld). [@1,2] The case was handed to the Narcotics Brigade for
further investigation. [@1,2]

The statement gave no weight for the plant material. [@1]
