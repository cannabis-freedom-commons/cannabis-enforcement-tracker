Fuerza Pública officers detained two men as suspects in transporting the
marijuana. [@1] One detainee is surnamed Thomson, who has judicial records for
illegal firearm carrying, vehicle theft, drug possession and aggravated robbery.
[@1] The second detainee is surnamed Torres, who has a record involving
receiving stolen goods, Fiscal Law violations, smuggling of assorted goods and
reckless driving. [@1] Both men were handed over and placed at the disposal of
the Public Ministry, which must set precautionary measures while the
investigation continues. [@1]

Agents found 524 packages of marijuana in one of the vehicles, each weighing
about 500 grams. [@1] In total the load exceeded 250 kilograms of marijuana.
[@1] The second vehicle held no drugs but was seized along with the first as
apparently linked to moving the load. [@1] Officers also seized ₡127,000 (about
USD 282) and USD 217. [@1]

The seizure occurred during a preventive road control in Macho Gaff, San Isidro
de El Guarco, Cartago, run by the Fuerza Pública with the Drug Control Police
(PCD). [@1] Officers who spotted bundles from outside one car coordinated with
the Cartago Prosecutor's Office, which called in Drug Control Police agents for
a fuller inspection. [@1] The marijuana wrappers bore images linked to Mexican
trafficking figures Rafael Caro Quintero and Pedro Avilés Pérez. [@1] The
packages also bore the image of Jesús Malverde, described as popularly known as
the so-called saint of Mexican drug traffickers. [@1] Authorities said they
still had to establish where the load came from, where it was going and what
role each detainee played. [@1]
