The Joint Task Force raid on Nadi Back Road was carried out on Wednesday by the
Fiji Police Force, the Republic of Fiji Military Forces and the Fiji Detector
Dog Unit. [@1] The raid on Nadi Back Road resulted in the arrest of one man and
the seizure of cocaine, methamphetamine and marijuana. [@1] White substances
seized during the Nadi Back Road operation tested positive for cocaine and
methamphetamine, while dried leaves were confirmed to be marijuana. [@1] The man
arrested on Nadi Back Road has been charged with one count of unlawful
possession of illicit drugs and was to appear in the Nadi Magistrates Court
today. [@1]

Two more Joint Task Force raids were carried out yesterday at a car wash in Nadi
and a home in Natabua. [@1] Those two operations resulted in the seizure of
white substances believed to be illicit drugs and the arrest of three men. [@1]
The three men remained in Police custody while the seized substances were sent
for analysis. [@1]
