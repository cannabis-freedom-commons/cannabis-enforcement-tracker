Police in Petrich and Sandanski found and destroyed about 134 cannabis plants on 26 August 2026, according to the Blagoevgrad Regional Directorate of the Interior Ministry. [@1,2] The plants tested positive for cannabis in field tests, and their combined weight was reported as 122.325 kilograms. [@1,2]

Fifty-four plants weighing about 43.2 kilograms were in a deforested area at Kukovo, in the land of Churicheni village in Petrich Municipality. [@1,2] The other 80 plants, weighing about 79.125 kilograms, were in a prepared field at Kostur Mahala, near Ilindentsi village in Strumyani Municipality. [@1,2] Both reports said the police opened pre-trial proceedings in connection with the two sites. [@1,2]

The accounts describe two discoveries made during the same day's police actions and report them together as a regional eradication result. [@1,2] They do not name anyone arrested or charged, and they do not report confirmatory laboratory testing. [@1,2] The field names identify the general areas, but the reports give no precise plot coordinates. [@1,2]
