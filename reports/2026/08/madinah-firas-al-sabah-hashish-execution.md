Saudi Arabia executed Firas bin Ahmed bin Saleh Al-Sabah, a Saudi national, in the Madinah
region on Saturday 29 August 2026 (16 Rabi al-Awwal 1448 AH). [@1,2,3]

The Interior Ministry said he had trafficked hashish for a second time by transporting it. [@1,2,3]
Security forces arrested him, charged him after investigation and referred him to the competent
court, which found him guilty and sentenced him to death as a discretionary (taazir) punishment. [@1,2,3]
The judgment became final after appeal and confirmation by the Supreme Court, and a royal order
authorised the carrying out of the sentence. [@1,2,3]

CNN Arabic said the ministry statement was carried by the official Saudi Press Agency (SPA). [@2]
The ministry warned that drug smugglers and traffickers face the prescribed penalties. [@1,3]
