Portugal's National Republican Guard (GNR) arrested seven people and seized more
than five tonnes of hashish during a major anti-drug operation along the
Guadiana river in the eastern Algarve. [@1] The operation was carried out on
Saturday 22 August 2026 in Vila Real de Santo António and Castro Marim, after
Spain’s Guardia Civil detected suspicious activity near Corte Velha, in the
Azinhal area. [@1] The GNR launched a coastal surveillance operation involving
land, sea and air resources to intercept those involved and prevent suspected
drug trafficking by sea. [@1] The movements were detected opposite Costa Esuri,
a development on the Spanish bank of the Guadiana. [@3] The drugs were found
near ruins in the Corte Velha area of Azinhal, on Portuguese territory. [@3]

Several suspects noticed the presence of police and attempted to flee, initially
on foot and later using a car and a boat. [@1] The car was intercepted with no
one inside, while maritime units caught up with the fleeing boat and arrested
seven men aged between 21 and 39, all foreign nationals. [@1] Another man
believed to be linked to the operation was identified. [@1] The GNR said
officers seized cannabis resin amounting to approximately 5.32 tonnes of
hashish. [@1] Police also seized three vehicles, two boats, a pistol with a
magazine and ammunition, six mobile phones and more than €1,000 in cash. [@1]

On Friday night, a GNR maritime patrol had tried to approach a suspicious vessel
linked to the operation; its occupants reacted with hostility and tried to ram
the patrol before fleeing towards the Canela estuary in Spanish territory. [@2]
Officers prevented the manoeuvre, and the suspects fled on foot at Ayamonte
marina, where the boat was seized. [@2]

Saturday's operation involved officers from the GNR's Coastal and Border Control
Unit, the Faro Territorial Command and the Intervention Unit, with support from
Spain's Guardia Civil. [@1] The operation was carried out by the Vila Real de
Santo António coastal control sub-detachment of the GNR's Coastal and Border
Control Unit (UCCF). [@2] On Monday 24 August, a judge at the Vila Real de Santo
António court ordered pre-trial detention, the most severe coercive measure in
Portuguese law, for all seven after a first judicial interrogation. [@3]
