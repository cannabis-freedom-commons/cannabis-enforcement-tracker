Agents of the Depincri Ate-Santa Anita, a unit of the Dirincri-PNP East
division, carried out an operation in the district of Ate against the alleged
criminal band Los Injertos Foraja. [@1] Officers detained four people in the
operation. [@1] Police preliminarily identified one detainee as 27-year-old
alleged ringleader Christian Omar Aliaga Pariona, alias Piko or Culon. [@1] The
other detainees were 28-year-old Shirley Nicole Dela Cruz Sanchez, alias
Shirley, 27-year-old Luis David Gavino Chumbimune, alias Kaluka, and a
17-year-old known as Dayana. [@1]

Officers searching a house identified during intelligence work seized three
dynamite cartridges with safety fuses. [@1] Officers also found a grenade whose
specific type will be established by expert examinations. [@1] Officers seized a
firearm during the inspection of the property. [@1] Officers seized 24 rounds of
ammunition whose calibre will be determined during expert examinations. [@1] In
the same house officers found a black plastic bag holding several Ziploc-type
bags with dry leaves, stems and seeds. [@1] Police said preliminary information
indicated the material would be cannabis sativa, known as marijuana. [@1]
Officers also seized three mobile phones for technical procedures to seek
information about the activities under investigation. [@1]

Police said intelligence and investigative work aimed at dismantling Los
Injertos Foraja motivated the operation. [@1] Police said all detainees were
taken to the specialized unit while proceedings continue to establish each
person's role and the origin of the seized items. [@1] Police said the plant
samples will be sent to laboratories to confirm their composition and any link
to other offences under investigation. [@1]
