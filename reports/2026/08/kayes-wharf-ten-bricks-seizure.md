Officers of the Goutioubé secondary customs office intercepted and held one
carrier at the Kayes river wharf. [@1] The person held was described only as a
national of an English-speaking country in the sub-region. [@1] Customs
announced seizures on 20 and 21 August in the Kayes region covering foodstuffs
without papers and ten bricks of cannabis. [@1]

The carrier was transporting ten bricks of chanvre indien, literally Indian
hemp. [@1] The authorities gave no weight for the drug and no destination. [@1]
In the separate 20 August operation at Diyala near Kayes, a Toyota Hilux was
stopped carrying 40 sacks of wheat flour totalling two tonnes and 15 cartons of
pasta weighing 150 kilograms. [@1]

Kayes in western Mali borders Senegal, Mauritania and Guinea and is a strategic
point for trade and supply. [@1] Customs linked the seizures to persistent
informal circuits on trade routes connecting Mali with neighbouring countries.
[@1]
