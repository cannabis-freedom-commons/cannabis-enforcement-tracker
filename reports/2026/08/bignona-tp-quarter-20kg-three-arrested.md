Police from the research brigade of Bignona's commissariat arrested three men in
the night of Friday 21 August 2026 on suspicion of criminal association and
trafficking in chanvre indien, the French term for cannabis. [@1] The operation
followed an anonymous tip about a large cannabis trafficking network, Ndarinfo
reported. [@1] Officers went to the TP quarter near the gas depot and arrested a
man who had come to receive the product. [@1] Police then set up a surveillance
operation and about half an hour later two more men arrived on a motorcycle and
were detained. [@1]

Searching their belongings turned up a sack holding 20 kilograms of chanvre
indien after weighing. [@2] The police also seized the cannabis and the
motorcycle, Dakaractu reported. [@3]

Ndarinfo said the operation illustrates the persistence of cannabis trafficking
networks in Bignona department in Casamance, where security forces have
multiplied operations in recent months. [@1] Ndarinfo linked the department's
trafficking to its proximity to The Gambia and Guinea-Bissau. [@1] Police said
the investigation continues to establish the network's reach and to identify
possible accomplices. [@1]
