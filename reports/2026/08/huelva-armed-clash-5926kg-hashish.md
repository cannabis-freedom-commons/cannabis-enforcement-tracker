Ten suspected drug traffickers were arrested after the crew of their narcolancha, a fast
semi-rigid smuggling boat, opened fire on a Guardia Civil patrol boat off the coast of Huelva
in the early hours of 17-18 August 2026. [@1] The joint operation paired the Tax Agency's
Customs Surveillance Service with the Civil Guard's Huelva Provincial Maritime Service. [@1]
Officers returned fire to repel the attack, but the smugglers' boat escaped after the patrol
boat broke down during the chase. [@1] During the pursuit the crew made dangerous evasive
manoeuvres and threw part of their hashish cargo into the sea to hinder the official
vessels. [@1,2]

On the morning of 20 August officers intercepted the narcolancha and a second support boat on
the high seas, arresting nine crew members and seizing 5,926 kilograms of hashish and both
vessels. [@1] A tenth suspect, wounded in the exchange of gunfire and abandoned by his
companions on the coast of Cádiz, was arrested and taken to hospital in Puerto Real. [@1] The
investigation remains open to clarify each detainee's role in the attack on the officers and
in the wider criminal organisation. [@1,2]
