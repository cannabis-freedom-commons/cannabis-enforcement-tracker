No one was detained after the motorboat that crossed the river quickly returned
to Paraguay, the country it came from. [@1] The Federal Prosecutor's Office in
Iguazú, led by federal prosecutor Marcelo José Bernachea, ordered the 13
packages moved to a Prefectura facility for further proceedings. [@1]

Prefectura officers searching among the riverside vegetation at Puerto Pique
Pescador found 13 packages containing 163.55 kilograms of marijuana. [@1] The
authorities valued the load at more than 620 million pesos. [@1]

Prefectura's criminal-intelligence and investigations unit had warned of a
possible entry of marijuana from Paraguay, which prompted the surveillance. [@2]
Officers deployed river and land units to the site to carry out the search. [@3]
The National Security Ministry presents the seizure as part of its border
anti-crime controls carried out by Prefectura Naval Argentina, the country's
coast guard. [@1] 376 reported that the boat quickly headed back towards
Paraguay after the crossing and that river and land units then swept the sector.
[@2]

376 described the load as a cargo valued under an official estimate at more than
620 million pesos. [@2]
