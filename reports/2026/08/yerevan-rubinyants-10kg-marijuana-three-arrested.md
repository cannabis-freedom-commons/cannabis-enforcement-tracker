On 31 August 2026, police acted on operational information about the
acquisition, storage and sale of a drug brought into Armenia by smuggling. [@1]
In the measures that followed that information, police detained three young
people on Rubinyants Street in Yerevan. [@1] The officers took the three
detained young people to the Kanaker-Zeytun division of the community police.
[@1] The account of the operation was provided to NEWS.am by the press service
of the republic's Interior Ministry police. [@1]

The Changan car belonging to the young people was taken to a specially guarded
lot, where it was sealed. [@1] A criminal case had been opened, and the car was
searched within those criminal proceedings. [@1] The search found a mass
weighing about 10 kg that police described as resembling marijuana. [@1]

The preliminary investigation into the case was continuing. [@1]
