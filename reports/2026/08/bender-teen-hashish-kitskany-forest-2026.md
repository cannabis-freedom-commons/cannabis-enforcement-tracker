Officers of the Criminal Investigation Department detained a 19-year-old
resident of the city of Bender in Kitskany forest while carrying out a raid in
Slobodzeya district. [@1] Officers found hashish on the young man during the
detention. [@1] He told officers that he had found a ziplock bag containing the
drug in the grass while he was walking through the forest. [@1] According to the
Interior Ministry press centre, this was not the first time the young man had
claimed to have found drugs in this way. [@1] Officers seized the banned
substance from him. [@1]

Following the incident, authorities were considering holding the Bender resident
administratively liable. [@1] For the hashish found on him, he faces a fine of
up to 6,300 Transnistrian roubles or administrative arrest for up to 30 days.
[@1]

The detention took place on 18 August, as reported from Slobodzeya district.
[@1] Police reminded the public that drug use and possession are unacceptable
and urged people not to become involved in illegal drug trafficking. [@1]
