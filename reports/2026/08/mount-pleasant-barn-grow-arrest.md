Mount Pleasant Township police and parole agents arrested Dante Sunseri on Aug.
12 at a property on Southview Road in McDonald. [@1] Officers said they were
checking Sunseri, 42, because he had failed drug tests while on parole. [@2]
Police said Sunseri is on parole for multiple felony offenses in Colorado,
including kidnapping and sexual assault. [@1] While taking him into custody,
officers said activity indicated he may have been trying to destroy evidence
inside a barn. [@1]

A compliance check by agents allegedly uncovered evidence of marijuana
manufacturing and distribution, prompting police to obtain a search warrant.
[@1] The warrant was executed at the Southview Road property with help from the
Pennsylvania State Police and the Washington County District Attorney's Drug
Task Force. [@1] Police said the search of the four-story barn, fitted with
multiple grow rooms to produce large quantities of marijuana, required three
truckloads to remove the evidence. [@1] Investigators reported hundreds of
planters, grow lamps, hydroponic systems, furnace and exhaust systems, scales
and filtration equipment, along with a large amount of marijuana and other drug
paraphernalia. [@1] Officers also recovered nine firearms of multiple calibers
and ammunition, which Sunseri is prohibited from possessing. [@1]

Sunseri was charged with felony possession of a prohibited firearm and delivery
or possession with intent to deliver, plus a misdemeanor count of
drug-paraphernalia possession. [@2] Police said the investigation remains
ongoing. [@1]
