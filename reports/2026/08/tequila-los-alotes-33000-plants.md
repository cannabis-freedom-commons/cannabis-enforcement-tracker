Jalisco state police destroyed about 33,000 plants of apparent marijuana found
at two plantations and a drying site in the municipality of Tequila, in the
Valles region. [@1] Officers of the regional police unit found the first plot
near the property known as Los Alotes, close to the village of El Olvido: about
3,000 plants up to 1.2 metres tall on roughly 1,500 square metres. [@1]

At the Los Ocotes property they found a drying site, an open area where about
6,000 plants hung on galvanised wire. [@1] A second plot in the same area, of
about 6,000 square metres, held around 24,000 plants up to 1.7 metres tall. [@1]
The state security ministry said officers asked the federal public prosecutor to
take charge, and the prosecutor ordered the sites photographed before, during
and after the destruction. [@1] After the sites were documented, the plants were
destroyed and burned on the spot under the federal prosecutor's instructions.
[@2]

Quadratín Jalisco put the black-market value of the 33,000 plants destroyed at
the three sites at about 37.1 million pesos. [@3] The reports name no arrests
and no suspects. [@3]
