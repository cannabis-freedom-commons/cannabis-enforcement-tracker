Nicaragua's Naval Force seized 710 kilograms of drugs, including cocaine and
marijuana, over the previous 12 months. [@1] The report specified no number of
detainees linked to the hauls. [@1] Between August 2025 and July 2026 the navy
seized 351 kilos of cocaine and 359 kilos of marijuana. [@1]

The navy released the figures in a report marking the 46th anniversary of its
founding. [@1] Over 46 years the Naval Force seized 67,375 kilos of drugs,
comprising 65,468 kilos of cocaine and 1,907 kilos of marijuana. [@1] The
institution put its annual average at 1,464.7 kilos, about twice the 710 kilos
seized in the last 12 months. [@1]

Authorities said they carry out a strategy called 'Muro de Contención' to keep
drugs and drug money out of population centres. [@1] Authorities said they
maintain close cooperation ties with regional countries as well as the United
States, Mexico and Russia. [@1] The army said Nicaragua has no organized groups,
gangs, hitmen or cartels and is not a drug warehouse country. [@1] Nicaragua
lies on a high-traffic corridor for drugs produced in South America and moved to
North America, where Mexican cartels operate and the main consumers live. [@1]
