The Nadymsky city court in Yamalo-Nenets Autonomous Okrug sentenced a citizen of
the Republic of Tajikistan to seven years in a strict-regime colony for
attempting to sell hashish. [@1] The court stated that his actions were stopped
during the operational-investigative measure «Наблюдение» by police officers,
who seized 22 packets of a narcotic agent. [@1]

The accused admitted the crime, described the circumstances in detail, and
apologized to citizens of Russia for his actions. [@1]
