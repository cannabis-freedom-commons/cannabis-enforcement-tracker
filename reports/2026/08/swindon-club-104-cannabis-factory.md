Three men were arrested on suspicion of cannabis cultivation. [@2,3] Police were
called at around 1pm on Thursday, 20 August, to Club 104, a members-only bar on
Cheney Manor Road in Swindon. [@2,4] Officers found a cannabis factory at the
property, with three men inside. [@2] A 52-year-old man was detained
immediately, while two others, aged 42 and 36, jumped out of an upper-floor
window, Wiltshire 999s reported. [@2] Wiltshire 999s said the men who jumped
sustained injuries believed to be serious but not life-threatening. [@2] The
Swindon Advertiser reported that the men who jumped required hospital treatment.
[@1]

Wiltshire Police said a scene was in place while the property was made safe and
further enquiries were carried out. [@2] The Swindon Advertiser later reported
that all three were bailed until November. [@1,3] Richard Hale, then the club
secretary, was among those arrested. [@1,3]

Wiltshire Police requested a review of the Club Premises Certificate, alleging
that the club had failed to uphold crime-prevention and public-safety licensing
objectives. [@1,3] The certificate, in place since August 2005, allowed the club
to supply alcohol and provide regulated entertainment to its members and their
guests. [@1]

Following an Extraordinary General Meeting, Mr Hale was replaced by Mr Fulgido Pasqualitto as secretary, providing documentation to licensing officers. [@3] The premises remains restricted due to a closure order lasting three months from September 4th, with the club unable to produce a current membership list. [@3]
