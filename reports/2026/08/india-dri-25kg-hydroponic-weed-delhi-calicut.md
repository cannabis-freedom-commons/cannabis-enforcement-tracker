Officers of the Directorate of Revenue Intelligence, India's customs
intelligence agency, intercepted two passengers in separate operations at Indira
Gandhi International Airport in New Delhi and Calicut International Airport.
[@1] Both passengers had travelled from Bangkok, and about 25 kg of hydroponic
weed was seized from them. [@1] Both passengers were arrested. [@1] One of the
two passengers was a foreign national. [@1]

The two airport arrests were part of a week of DRI operations across India in
which the agency arrested 20 people, five of them foreign nationals. [@1] Those
operations also seized foreign-origin gold, heroin, MDMA, red sanders, wildlife
products, e-cigarettes, poppy seeds and areca nuts. [@1] The release gives no
weight for each of the two weed seizures, no value for them and no date for the
interceptions. [@1]
