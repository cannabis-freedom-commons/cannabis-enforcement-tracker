Joint police units uprooted 321 cannabis plants at Sitio Talahidan in Barangay Caburacanan, Malaybalay City, on 6 August 2026; the plants were valued at about ₱64,200. [@1,2]

Bombo Radyo reported that 311 plants were burned at the site and 10 specimens were taken to the Provincial Forensic Unit for laboratory examination. [@1] Metro CDO Dev said the operation involved the 1st Bukidnon Provincial Mobile Force Company, Malaybalay City Police Station and the Bukidnon Provincial Intelligence Unit. [@2]
