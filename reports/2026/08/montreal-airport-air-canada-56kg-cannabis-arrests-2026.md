The RCMP arrested two Air Canada employees in connection with alleged cocaine
and cannabis trafficking out of Montréal-Trudeau Airport. [@1] The RCMP's
Federal Policing Eastern Region announced the arrests. [@2] The RCMP said the
two suspects held various positions related to ground operations at Air Canada.
[@2] The force alleged the two took part in a conspiracy to bring and move
luggage through secure areas of the airport to facilitate the importation of
cocaine and the exportation of cannabis. [@2]

Police executed search warrants at two residences and a vehicle in the Greater
Montreal area, and searched the employees' work base in Dorval with Air Canada's
cooperation. [@2] The RCMP said the investigation began in May 2026 after a
Canada Border Services Agency operation. [@2] That operation intercepted cocaine
concealed in backpacks on a flight from Punta Cana. [@2] Over the course of the
investigation, officers seized 56 kg of cannabis. [@2] Two suitcases containing
that cannabis were seized on 10 July. [@2]

The two employees were questioned and released pending further proceedings. [@2]
Air Canada said it had suspended both employees. [@3] The airline and ADM
Aéroports de Montréal are assisting the investigation. [@3] The RCMP said the
investigation was ongoing and that analysis of the evidence could lead to
additional arrests and charges. [@2]
