Quintana Roo security officers detained Cuban citizen Jesús Moisés “N” in Cancún
with suspected drugs and allegedly counterfeit money. [@1] The arrest occurred
on Calle 8 in the municipality of Benito Juárez. [@1] The operation involved
Grupo Orión of the Citizen Security Secretariat with personnel from the Defensa
Nacional, the Marina and the Guardia Nacional on surveillance patrols. [@3]
Officers intervened after seeing the suspect handling small bags containing
material with characteristics similar to marijuana. [@1]

Officers seized 77 doses of suspected marijuana. [@1] Officers also seized 25
wraps of a crystal-like substance, for a total of 102 wraps. [@2] Authorities
also found 1,420 Mexican pesos in allegedly counterfeit bills of different
denominations. [@1] Officers also secured a red ecological bag with the drugs
and money. [@3]

Authorities linked Jesús Moisés “N” to alleged robberies of commercial
establishments and paying with allegedly false bills. [@2] He and the seized
items were placed at the disposal of Quintana Roo’s Fiscalía Especializada en
Combate al Delito de Narcomenudeo, which will determine his legal situation.
[@3]
