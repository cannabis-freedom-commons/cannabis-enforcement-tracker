In a special operation, Kaukhali police station in Pirojpur seized 60 grams of
cannabis and five yaba tablets. [@1] Police made the arrests in the early hours
of Saturday 15 August. [@1] One account named them as Sanjib Das, 35, a night
guard at the women's degree college and the son of Niranjan Das of Aspaddi
village in Kaukhali, and Md Shahin Hossain, 34, the son of Anwar Hossain of
Saturia village in neighbouring Rajapur upazila. [@1] Sub-inspector Md Mohiuddin
of Kaukhali station led the raid. [@1]

That account said officers arrested the night guard Sanjib Das with 60 grams of
cannabis inside the women's degree college. [@1] The same account said officers
arrested Md Shahin the same night with five yaba tablets at a rented house in
the Bashri area. [@1] An account citing the station's first information report
said police learned at 2:55 a.m. on 14 August that people were buying and
selling drugs on a balcony in front of a room inside the women's college in
Kaukhali Sadar union, and raided the spot at 3:05 a.m. [@2] That account said
Sanjib Das, 35, and Md Shahin Khandaker, 34, were detained as they tried to
flee, and that 60 grams of cannabis wrapped in white paper and five pale-orange
yaba tablets were taken from the right pocket of Das's shorts. [@2] A search of
Khandaker found no drugs, that account said, though police claimed he had been
helping Das buy and sell them. [@2] The first information report estimated the
market value of the recovered drugs at 7,500 taka. [@2]

Officer-in-charge Md Anwar Hossain said cases had been filed under the narcotics
control law and the men had been sent to court. [@1] Police said separate cases
were filed against the two under the narcotics control law, and that they were
then sent to court. [@1] The first-information-report account said a case was
filed under section 36(1) of the Narcotics Control Act 2018. [@2] The station
officer said the men were sent to Pirojpur court on Saturday. [@2]
