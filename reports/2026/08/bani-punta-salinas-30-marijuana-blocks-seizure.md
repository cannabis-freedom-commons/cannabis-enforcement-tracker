Three Dominican men were detained during the air, sea and land operation off
Baní. [@1] Acento identified them as Luis Enríquez Díaz Sánchez, Michael Antonio
Herrera Guerrero and Víctor Alfonso Morel Díaz. [@2] The detainees were placed
under the control of the Public Ministry for legal proceedings. [@2] The DNCD
and the Public Ministry keep an investigation open to identify and detain other
possible members of the network linked to the load. [@1]

Authorities seized 1,360 packages of cocaine with a preliminary weight of 1.3
tonnes, plus 30 blocks of a substance appearing to be marijuana. [@1] Officers
found the drugs inside 55 bales aboard the boat, for a reported total of 1,390
packages. [@1] The 32-foot boat had no name or registration and carried two
250-horsepower outboard motors. [@1] Agents also seized a satellite
communications radio, 12 tanks and a fuel jug, plus tarps, ropes, water, energy
drinks and food among other evidence. [@1] The seized packages were sent under
chain of custody to the National Institute of Forensic Sciences, which will
determine the exact type and weight through analysis. [@1]

The operation began after authorities detected a boat moving through Caribbean
waters with a drug load. [@1] Authorities maintained surveillance for more than
15 hours until intercepting the launch several nautical miles south of Punta
Salinas. [@1] The DNCD said the operation off the municipality of Baní had
support from the United States Drug Enforcement Administration and the Colombian
Air Force. [@1] The operation was carried out on 21 August 2026 in coordination
with the Dominican Navy, the Dominican Air Force, the Public Ministry, state
intelligence bodies, the DEA and the Colombian Air Force. [@2] Authorities said
the network's mode of operation consists of introducing drugs from South America
using fast boats. [@1]
