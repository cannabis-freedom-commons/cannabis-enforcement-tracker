Officers of Squadron 71 “Aguilares” carried out operations on National Route 40
near the Tucumán localities of Quilmes and Colalao del Valle. [@1] The drug was
seized in three separate procedures on National Route 40. [@1] In the first two
checks, Sections “Núcleo” and “Montero” stopped two buses travelling from Salta
province toward localities in Catamarca province. [@1]

Officers seized a total of 17 kilos 035 grams of marijuana across the
operations. [@1] Part of the drug was hidden in parcels in the luggage holds of
two passenger buses, while the rest was found in a backpack in the overhead rack
of a third bus. [@1] Parcels opened from the luggage holds contained a
greenish-brown substance that field Narcotest testing confirmed as marijuana,
weighing 15 kilos 935 grams. [@1] A package found in the unclaimed backpack
tested positive for cannabis sativa and weighed 1 kilo 100 grams. [@1]

In the hold inspections, gendarmes found anomalies in postal pieces and used a
portable scanner that showed images consistent with organic material in bundles
and a cooler. [@1] Officers requested the intervening magistrate’s authorisation
and opened the parcels in the presence of witnesses. [@1] Federal Courts of
Tucumán Nos. 1 and 3 intervened and ordered the seizure of all the drug and
items of interest to the case. [@1]
