Three Indian nationals were arrested at Tribhuvan International Airport in
Kathmandu with 11 kg of marijuana brought from Thailand. [@1] The three were
arrested by a team from the Narcotics Control Bureau office in Koteshwar,
Kathmandu. [@1] The arrested men were 30-year-old Sonu and 23-year-old Jagroop
Singh, both from Punjab, India, and 22-year-old Sahil from Haryana. [@1] The
Bureau identifies the man from Punjab as 30-year-old Soni, which Ratopati's
English translation renders as Sony. [@3]

On the night of Saturday 1 August 2026, two of the men arrived at the airport on
a Cathay Pacific Airways flight from Thailand that transited through Hong Kong.
[@1] The Bureau told Ratopati that the 11 kg was recovered from the two
passengers who landed in Kathmandu that night. [@2] The same account dates the
landing to the night of Saun 16 in the Nepali calendar, which corresponds to 1
August 2026. [@3] The third man had come to the airport to receive the marijuana
the other two had brought. [@1]

Police arrested all three and began an investigation into drug-related charges.
[@1] The Bureau said the accused brought the marijuana from Thailand through
Hong Kong and that it was investigating them. [@2]
