El Salvador's National Civil Police detained two people transporting marijuana
in colonia Costa Azul in Chiltiupán, La Libertad Costa. [@1] The detainees were
identified as Salvadoran Erick Alfredo Valles Centeno, 19, and Guatemalan
Emiltze Eileen Hasse, 31. [@1] Officers intercepted them while they were
travelling in a car registered in Guatemala. [@1]

The operation was carried out with the support of the Anti-Narcotics Division.
[@2] Police seized marijuana and the Guatemalan-plated vehicle used to move it.
[@1] Police said the action was part of efforts to curb the flow of illegal
drugs and keep corridors used by trafficking groups under control. [@1]

Authorities said both detainees will be placed at the disposal of the competent
bodies to continue the process established by law. [@2] Police did not
immediately say whether the two belonged to a criminal network operating in the
area or had entered the country with the drugs. [@1] Investigations continue to
determine whether the detainees belong to a wider trafficking network with links
between El Salvador and Guatemala, and the case will go to specialised courts.
[@1]
