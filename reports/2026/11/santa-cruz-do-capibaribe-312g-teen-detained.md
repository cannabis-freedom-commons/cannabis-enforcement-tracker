Military police of Pernambuco acted in Santa Cruz do Capibaribe on the night of
4 November 2026 during two operations carried out in different regions that
night. [@1] In Santa Cruz do Capibaribe, officers of the 24th Military Police
Battalion found 83 small portions of marijuana and two larger quantities
weighing about 312 grams. [@1] The occurrence involved an adolescent suspected
of selling drugs in the municipality. [@1]

Officers approached the youth after seeing him try to hide four marijuana
portions in an abandoned building. [@1] The adolescent admitted selling drugs
and said he kept more of the drug at his nearby residence. [@1] At the indicated
address officers found 79 packaged portions known as bigs and the two larger
portions. [@1]

Officers also seized cannabis seeds, three cell phones and two precision scales
linked to the suspected sale. [@1] The adolescent was taken to the Civil Police
station with his mother for procedures over the investigated infraction. [@1]
The Santa Cruz occurrence was sent to the Civil Police for investigative
procedures and examination of the seized materials. [@1]
