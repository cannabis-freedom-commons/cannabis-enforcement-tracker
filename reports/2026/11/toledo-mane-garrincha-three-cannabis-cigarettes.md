Military police patrolling Rua Mané Garrincha in Toledo, Paraná, at about 21h on
Friday 6 November found two groups of people in a square in poorly lit spots
during Operation Omnis, a Military Police patrol campaign in the 19th
Battalion's area. [@1]

During the search officers found one of those approached with a lit cannabis
cigarette and two more cigarettes containing a substance similar to cannabis,
kept in a plastic container. [@1] The man said the drug was his and was taken to
the 19th Battalion's headquarters to record the incident. [@1]

The cigarettes were seized and the man signed a termo de compromisso de
comparecimento, an undertaking to appear before the authorities. [@1] The other
people searched were released after officers found no illegal material with
them. [@1]

The incident was one entry in the 19th Battalion's report of occurrences handled
between 7h on Friday 6 November and 7h on Saturday 7 November, which also
covered a traffic injury, domestic violence, a home robbery, assaults and
traffic offences in Toledo, São Pedro do Iguaçu, Marechal Cândido Rondon, Santa
Helena and Diamante d'Oeste. [@1]
