Gurgaon Traffic Police personnel intercepted suspected individuals carrying
cannabis at Nainwal Cut in Manesar during an operation against drug trafficking.
[@1] A search of the suspects led to the recovery of 12kg of cannabis from their
possession. [@1] Both individuals were handed over to the concerned police
station for further investigation and legal proceedings. [@1]

The Nainwal Cut seizure was reported as part of crime prevention and public
safety work that Gurgaon Traffic Police personnel carried out alongside their
regular traffic management duties over the past 30 days. [@1] Police
spokesperson Sandeep Turan said these actions reflect the role of traffic
personnel beyond regulating vehicles and managing congestion, adding that their
vigilance also contributes to crime prevention, action against drug-related
activities and assistance to citizens during road emergencies. [@1]

Police appealed to residents to promptly inform authorities about suspicious
activities or road accidents and to cooperate with law enforcement agencies to
help maintain public safety and ensure a secure environment across Gurgaon. [@1]
