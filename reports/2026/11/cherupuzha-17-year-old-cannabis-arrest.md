A 17-year-old boy was found carrying cannabis at Cherupuzha in Kannur district,
Kerala. [@1] Local residents stopped the boy, who seemed suspicious, and
searched him, finding cannabis in a packet he was carrying. [@1] The residents
informed the police, who took the boy into custody along with the packet. [@1]
Only a small quantity of cannabis was found. [@1]

The boy told the residents that he does not use cannabis and had brought it to
hand over to someone else. [@1] Because he is a minor, police prepared a social
background report and released him temporarily. [@1] Police said the report
would be submitted to the court promptly and that further steps would follow the
court's directions. [@1]

Officials said police had information about the places that supplied the boy
with cannabis and had stepped up a search for them. [@1]
