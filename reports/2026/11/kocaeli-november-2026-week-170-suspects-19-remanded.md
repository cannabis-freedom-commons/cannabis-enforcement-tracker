Police and gendarmerie teams in Kocaeli carried out anti-drug operations across
the province between 2 and 8 November 2026, targeting people who use, hold, sell
and ship drugs. [@2]

Provincial police narcotics officers ran 131 separate operations, took judicial
action against 143 suspects, released 114, freed 11 more under judicial control
conditions and remanded 18. [@1] The police searches seized 1 kilogram 327 grams
of esrar, the Turkish term for cannabis resin and bud, and 5 grams of skunk.
[@1] The same searches found 6,915 synthetic pharmaceutical pills, 618 grams of
bonzai and 9 grams of bonzai raw material said to be enough for about 900 grams
of the drug; bonzai is a synthetic cannabinoid and none of these are counted as
cannabis here. [@1] Officers also seized 198 grams of methamphetamine, 15 grams
of amphetamine, 2 grams of cocaine and 1 gram of heroin, which are not counted
as cannabis. [@1] Police also seized 5 precision scales, 2 handguns, 268
cartridges and 48,095 Turkish lira judged to be proceeds of drug crime. [@1]

Gendarmerie teams carried out 20 separate operations in the same period, took
judicial action against 27 suspects, released 24, freed 2 under judicial control
and remanded 1. [@1] The gendarmerie searches seized 16 grams of esrar and 9
grams of skunk, which together with the police haul make the 1.357 kilograms of
cannabis recorded here. [@1] The gendarmerie also seized 5 grams of bonzai, 85
grams of cocaine, 14 synthetic pharmaceutical items and 1 drug pill, none of
which are counted as cannabis. [@1]

The provincial police directorate said its narcotics units caught 143 suspects
in the past week and seized 1 kilogram 327 grams of esrar, 618 grams of
synthetic cannabinoid, 230 grams of assorted drugs, 6,915 synthetic
pharmaceutical items, 2 handguns, 268 cartridges, 5 precision scales and 48,095
lira. [@3,4] The authorities said the police and gendarmerie fight against drugs
across the province would continue with determination. [@1]
