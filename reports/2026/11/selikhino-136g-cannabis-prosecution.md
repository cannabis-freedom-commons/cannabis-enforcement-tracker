In August, traffic police officers in the village of Selikhino stopped a moped
ridden by a 39-year-old local man to check his documents. [@1] During the check,
officers found a blue bag in his possession containing a packet of plant
material. [@1] The investigation into his case has now been completed and the
case materials have been sent to court for trial. [@1] The accused remains under
a signed undertaking not to leave his place of residence and to behave properly
while awaiting court. [@1]

Forensic examination identified the seized material as cannabis, with a dried
weight of 136 grams. [@1] The man told police that he had personally gathered
wild hemp in the forest and kept it for his own use. [@1] Authorities opened a
criminal case for illegal acquisition, storage, transportation, making or
processing of drugs on a large scale without intent to sell, under part 2 of
Article 228 of the Russian Criminal Code. [@1] That charge carries a maximum
penalty of up to 10 years of imprisonment. [@1]

Police are treating the case as possession without intent to sell. [@1] The case
update is attributed to the press service of the Russian Interior Ministry for
Khabarovsk Krai. [@1]
