Military police arrested a 34-year-old man on suspicion of drug trafficking on
the night of Tuesday 10 November 2026 in Piraúba, in the Zona da Mata of Minas
Gerais. [@1] The incident was recorded at about 21h on Rua Pedro Vieira da
Silva, in the bairro Santa Terezinha neighbourhood. [@1]

The operation began after an anonymous tip about suspicious activity, and
officers kept watch on a property from which they saw the suspect leave carrying
a bag and walk towards an abandoned house. [@3] When officers stopped the man in
the street he no longer had the bag, and a search of the route he had taken
found it hidden under a staircase, holding one bar of a marijuana-like substance
and a package of cocaine-like powder. [@1] A search of the property the suspect
had left found two precision scales, empty vials, plastic packaging and another
portion of a cocaine-like substance. [@3]

Searches extended to the ground in front of the house and the surrounding area,
partly with a lawyer present, and the operation was supported by Military Police
teams from Tocantins and by the Ronda Ostensiva com Cães (ROCCA), the military
police dog unit. [@1] A sniffer dog helped locate further material in the
outside area, named Ogum in the regional reports and Ogun by Primeiro a Saber.
[@2]

In total officers seized two bars and 20 buchas of a substance resembling
marijuana, five packages and two vials of a cocaine-like substance, two
precision scales, a mobile phone and various packaging materials. [@1] After the
arrest the man was taken to the municipal hospital in Piraúba for a medical
assessment and then presented to the on-duty Civil Police station in Ubá, where
he remained at the disposal of the courts together with the seized material.
[@3] The case was passed to the Civil Police for the appropriate legal steps.
[@1] The regional account of the operation was credited to the 21st Military
Police Battalion. [@3]
