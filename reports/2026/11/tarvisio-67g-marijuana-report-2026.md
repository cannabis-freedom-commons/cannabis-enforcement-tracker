Carabinieri from the Chiusaforte station stopped a saloon car on the SS13
Pontebbana state road on Friday afternoon during a service against drug
trafficking and dealing. [@1,2,5] The driver was a 58-year-old man resident in the
Tarvisio area, and officers smelled a strong odour of marijuana when the car
door was opened. [@1,2,5] Colleagues from the radiomobile unit of the Tarvisio
company joined the checks. [@1,5]

A search of the driver and the vehicle found a cigarette containing the drug.
[@1,2,5] A search of his home found 67 grams of marijuana, which officers seized.
[@1,2,5] The man was reported to the judicial authorities for possession for the
purpose of dealing drugs. [@1,2,5]

In the same afternoon, carabinieri in Pontebba arrested a 42-year-old local man
over an aggravated theft committed in 2018, an unrelated case. [@1,5]

The stop took place on the afternoon of Friday 6 November 2026. [@3] The drug recovered was sequestered by the carabinieri at the end of the operation. [@4]
