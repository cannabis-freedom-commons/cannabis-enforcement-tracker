On 12 November 2026 at around 7:30 a.m., the Wellington County OPP Traffic
Management Unit ran a proactive traffic enforcement initiative that led to a
traffic stop on Wellington Road 7 in Centre Wellington Township. [@1] As a
result of the investigation, a 49-year-old from Dundalk is charged under the
Highway Traffic Act, the Compulsory Automobile Insurance Act and the Cannabis
Control Act. [@1]

Among the charges, listed under the Cannabis Control Act, is driving a vehicle
or boat with cannabis readily available. [@1] The accused is scheduled to appear
in the Provincial Offences Court in Guelph at a later date. [@1]
