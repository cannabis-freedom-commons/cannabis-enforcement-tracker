Antony Tawain Hull, 41, was arrested following a joint operation by the Escambia
County Sheriff's Office Narcotics Unit and SWAT team. [@1] The 41-year-old
Pensacola man was booked into the Escambia County Jail at 11 a.m. on Wednesday,
4 November 2026. [@2] He remained in the Escambia County Jail with his bond
amount totalling $511,000. [@1] He was charged with cocaine trafficking, two
counts of drug trafficking, methamphetamine trafficking, drug distribution,
possession of a weapon and possession of drug equipment. [@2]

Sheriff Chip Simmons said deputies searched a home during the narcotics
investigation. [@2] Simmons said investigators recovered over 200 grams of
fentanyl, 77 grams of cocaine, 89 grams of methamphetamine and 46 grams of
oxycodone. [@1] Investigators also seized marijuana alongside those drugs during
the same search. [@1] Deputies also seized a firearm equipped with an extended
magazine. [@1]

Sheriff Chip Simmons said the Narcotics Unit, assisted by the SWAT team, did a
good job on the case. [@1] Simmons announced the arrest and detailed the items
seized during the operation. [@1]
