Nine suspected members of a criminal group were arrested in Playa del Carmen,
Quintana Roo, in two separate operations where authorities seized doses of
marijuana, crystal methamphetamine, and mobile phones. [@1] The first arrest
took place on 120 Avenida Norte in the Ejido neighborhood after a pedestrian
reported that several men had threatened him and tried to sell him drugs. [@1]
Officers responded to the call and detained six men identified as Robert
Adalberto “N”, Julio “N”, Gerardo “N”, Edgar Daniel “N”, Antonio “N”, and
Nicanor “N”. [@1] A routine search uncovered packages of green plant material
similar to marijuana, crystal methamphetamine fragments, and cell phones in
their possession. [@1]

In a second operation on Avenida Sur in the Villas del Sol subdivision, police
arrested three more men: Jonathan de Jesús “N”, Eriberto “N”, and Antonio “N”.
[@1] Officers caught them in flagrante while one of the men was distributing
drug doses from a pouch to the other two suspects. [@1] Narcotics and
communication equipment were also seized at the scene of the second arrest. [@1]

Authorities said the arrested men belonged to a local retail drug trafficking
gang linked to recent violent incidents in the municipality. [@1] The joint
operation was conducted by the Municipal Police and the Quintana Roo state
attorney general's office. [@1] All nine men were brought before a control
judge, who ordered justified pretrial detention while their legal and medical
status is resolved. [@1]
