A man was arrested in flagrante for drug trafficking on the night of Thursday 12
November in Narandiba (SP), after police attended a domestic-violence call. [@1]
Military police were called after reports that the suspect had threatened his
ex-partner and others and damaged a residential door. [@1]

During the search, police found portions of marijuana and crack, a precision
scale, cash and notes related to drug sales. [@1] Military police reported
seizing 126 grams of marijuana and 41 grams of crack, along with the other
materials tied to the incident. [@1]

The man was taken to the Civil Police station, where he remained in custody at
the disposal of the justice system. [@2]
