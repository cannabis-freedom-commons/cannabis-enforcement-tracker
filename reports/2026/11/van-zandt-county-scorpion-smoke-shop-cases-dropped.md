Nine people arrested during a series of East Texas smoke shop raids will no
longer face criminal charges after prosecutors declined to try the cases. [@1]
Van Zandt County District Attorney Tonda Curry explained in a letter that
dismissing the cases was the best option for all parties when considering the
risk, time and cost of a trial. [@1] The decision resolves criminal cases
connected to the investigation, which included the seizure of thousands of
suspected illegal THC products. [@1]

The Van Zandt County Sheriff's Office announced the conclusion of a widescale
operation targeting more than a dozen East Texas smoke shops, resulting in the
arrests of nine people, the seizure of hundreds of pounds of drugs and nearly $5
million in U.S. currency and other assets. [@2] On 22 September 2026 the Texas
Department of Public Safety investigated and executed search warrants at 13
Scorpion Smoke Shop locations, targeting the illegal sale and distribution of
THC and hemp products. [@2] Officers seized 11,468 suspected THC products, 5,475
grams of marijuana, 33,455 grams of suspected psilocybin mushrooms, 23 vehicles
and nearly $5 million in currency and other assets. [@2] The $5 million in
seized assets is being returned to their owners. [@1]

According to jail records, the nine were arrested on warrants charging them with
money laundering valued at more than $150,000 but less than $30,000. [@2] They
were also charged with engaging in organized criminal activity and with
manufacture and delivery of a controlled substance in penalty group 2 weighing
less than 400 grams. [@2] David Sergi, an attorney for the individuals arrested,
said potential changes to state law could have assisted the defense if the
prosecution had moved forward. [@1] The dismissals coincide with a reported
change in state leadership, with Democrat Vikki Goodwin, who opposes an outright
ban on hemp-derived THC products, due to take office as lieutenant governor in
January. [@1] Sergi said the state is now at a policy point where it need not
rely on technical definitions and can look to the legislature to resolve the
question. [@1]
