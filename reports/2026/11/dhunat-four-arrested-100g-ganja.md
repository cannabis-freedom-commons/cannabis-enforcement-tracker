Police in Dhunat upazila in Bogura district arrested four people, one of them a
suspect named in an arrest warrant, in several cases. [@1,3] Among those held was
Shahadat Hossain, 29, a former vice-president of the Dhunat upazila unit of the
Swayamsebak League. [@1] He was arrested at Dhunat bazaar. [@1]

The others were Mostafizur Rahman, known as Firoz, 60, from southern Officerpara
in Dhunat; Samrat Babu, 31, from Gazaria village; and Shariful Islam, from
Tarakandi village. [@1] Police said Samrat Babu was detained in front of a
grocery shop by the Herringbond road in Gazaria village, in Chikashi union of
Dhunat upazila. [@1] Officers recovered 100 grams of ganja, the local word for
cannabis, wrapped in white polythene from him. [@1,3]

Atiqul Islam, officer-in-charge of Dhunat police station, said the four were
sent to jail through a court on Thursday after legal proceedings against them
were completed. [@1] He said police raids against crime were continuing. [@1]
Amar Bangla 24 reported the same arrests, saying police arrested four people in
several cases in Dhunat upazila, one of them named in an arrest warrant. [@2] It
also reported that Shahadat Hossain was arrested at Dhunat bazaar and that 100
grams of ganja was recovered from one of the arrested. [@2]
