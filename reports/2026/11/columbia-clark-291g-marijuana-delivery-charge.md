A Columbia man, already charged over an Oct. 31 downtown shooting, faces another
charge: on Nov. 12, prosecutors charged Kelvin Clark, 22, with Deliver
Controlled Substance. [@1] Nine days earlier, Clark had been charged with First
Degree Assault, Armed Criminal Action, and Unlawful Use of a Weapon. [@1] On
Nov. 3, Columbia police saw Clark in a car on Washington Street and arrested him
as part of the investigation into the Oct. 31 shooting. [@1] The baggies held
291.36 grams (0.64 of a pound) of marijuana. [@1] Clark was being held without
bond in the Boone County Jail. [@1] The prosecutor filed the charge of delivery
of a controlled substance on Thursday in Boone County. [@2] An arraignment was
held on Thursday afternoon for the new case, and a preliminary hearing is
scheduled for Dec. 2 in his assault case. [@2] The probable cause statement says
Clark was found with more than half-a-pound of marijuana when he was arrested on
Nov. 3 for his alleged role in the shooting. [@2]
