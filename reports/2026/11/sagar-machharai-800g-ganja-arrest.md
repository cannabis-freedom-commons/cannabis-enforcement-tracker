Motinagar police in Sagar, Madhya Pradesh, arrested a 65-year-old man with 800 g
of ganja, a cannabis preparation, police said. [@1] Police identified him as
Suresh Shakya, a resident of the Machharai area near the Sheetla Mata temple.
[@2] Police said a tip had come in that he was standing outside his house with
the ganja in a white bag for sale. [@1] The police team went to the spot near
the temple, cordoned off the area and caught him. [@1]

Officers searched him, seized the ganja and took him to the police station. [@1]
Police put the estimated value of the ganja at 8,000 rupees. [@4]

A case was registered at Motinagar police station under sections 8/20 of the
Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act. [@3] The station house
officer said police were questioning him about where he got the ganja and whom
he planned to sell it to. [@1] Police said the arrest was part of a special
district drive ordered by Superintendent of Police Sanjeev Sinha against illegal
arms, liquor and drug trafficking. [@3]
