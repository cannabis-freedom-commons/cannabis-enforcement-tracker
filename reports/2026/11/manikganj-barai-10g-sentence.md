Officers on an anti-drug mobile-court drive detained 60-year-old Sukumar Saha
with 10 grams of ganja in the Barai area of Manikganj Sadar. [@1] A mobile
court, an executive magistrate-led court that tries offences caught on the spot,
convicted him for consuming drugs and for creating social disorder through that
consumption. [@1] The court sentenced him to 15 days of imprisonment and fined
him 100 taka. [@1]

The operation took place on Tuesday 3 November 2026 at Saha's own house in Barai
under Sadar police station, organised by the Manikganj district office of the
Department of Narcotics Control, the national anti-drug agency. [@1] Officers
recovered and seized 10 grams of ganja, the local term for cannabis, from Saha
during the raid. [@1] Officers then filed a mobile-court case for drug
consumption and disorderly conduct, and the court imposed the sentence after
hearing the case. [@1]

The convicted man is a resident of Barai in Manikganj Sadar, the son of the late
Sudhir Saha and Maya Rani Saha. [@1] Local observers were quoted as saying such
departmental operations will play an important role in preventing drug abuse and
social disorder. [@1]
