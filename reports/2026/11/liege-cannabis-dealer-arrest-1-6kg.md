Police in Liège arrested a 27-year-old man on the afternoon of Wednesday 11
November 2026 after neighbours complained of comings and goings linked to drug
dealing in their street. [@1] Officers stopped him in his car, where a passenger
was sitting. [@1] The passenger told police that the man had been supplying him
with cannabis and ketamine since 2018. [@1] La DH reports that the passenger
said the man had been his dealer for 18 months. [@2]

Police reported finding 100 grams of cannabis on him and 1.5 kg at his home.
[@1] La DH reports that ketamine was also found at the man's home. [@2] A search
of the passenger's home found nothing. [@1] He refused to cooperate with the
police and to let them examine his mobile phone. [@1]

Sudinfo reports that both men were presented to the Liège prosecutor's office on
the morning of Thursday 12 November 2026. [@1] The suspect's file was placed
under judicial investigation with a request for an arrest warrant. [@1] La DH
describes him as already known to the courts for driving offences. [@2]
