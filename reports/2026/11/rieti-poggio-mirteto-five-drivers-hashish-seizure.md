Carabinieri of the Poggio Mirteto company reported five people at liberty to the
Public Prosecutor of Rieti after evening and night checks over the weekend into
Monday morning. [@1] The checks were part of the Rieti Provincial Command's
road-traffic monitoring to prevent dangerous driving linked to alcohol and drug
use. [@1]

On S.R. 313, officers stopped a 20-year-old driver whose breath test was well
over the legal alcohol limit, reporting him under Article 186(2)(c) of the
Highway Code and withdrawing his licence while seizing the vehicle. [@1] On
State Road 3 Flaminia, officers checked an 18-year-old driver whose preliminary
tests indicated drug-related impairment, reporting him under Article 187(8) and
withdrawing his licence. [@1] On S.S. 4 Salaria, a 40-year-old driver showed
drug-use symptoms and a search found 0.35 grams of cocaine on him, leading to a
drug-driving referral, licence withdrawal, vehicle seizure and a personal-use
report to the prefecture. [@1] On S.R. 313 Ternana, a 29-year-old local driver
who was weaving and clearly drunk had about 15 grams of hashish found in his
vehicle, which officers seized, and he was reported with his licence withdrawn.
[@1] On S.R. 657 Sabina, a 50-year-old local driver stopped for speeding failed
a roadside alcohol test and was found with 1 gram of cocaine and 2.5 grams of
hashish, leading to a drink-driving report, licence withdrawal and a prefecture
referral. [@1]

The operation covered roads across Rieti province with evening and night checks
intended to raise road-safety standards against alcohol- and drug-related
dangerous driving. [@1] The criminal proceedings were still in the
preliminary-investigation phase, with any criminal liability to be assessed by
the judicial authority. [@2]
