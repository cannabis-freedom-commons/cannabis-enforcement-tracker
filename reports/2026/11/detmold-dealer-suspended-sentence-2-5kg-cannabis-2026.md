A court sentenced a 24-year-old drug dealer from Detmold to a suspended sentence
on 10 November 2026. [@1] The young man had no previous convictions and had made
a good impression. [@1]

He had stockpiled almost one and a half kilograms of marijuana in his flat. [@1]
He had also stockpiled one kilogram of hashish in the same flat, bringing the
combined amount to about two and a half kilograms. [@1]

The court said the suspended sentence was also linked to the significant
reduction of penalties following the legalisation of cannabis in Germany. [@1]
