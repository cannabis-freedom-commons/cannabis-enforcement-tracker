Customs posts at Saudi Arabia's land, sea and air crossings recorded 1,205
seizures of prohibited goods in one week, the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority
(ZATCA) said. [@1]

The seized goods included 36 types of narcotics, among them hashish, cocaine,
heroin, crystal meth and Captagon pills. [@1] The ports also intercepted 303
other prohibited substances, 879 tobacco products and 31 illegally transported
currency consignments. [@3]

ZATCA said the seizures were part of its continuing inspections to protect
society and stop the smuggling of prohibited goods. [@1] The authority said
imports and exports would stay subject to strict customs control and that it had
stepped up work with other security agencies. [@2] ZATCA asked the public to
report suspicious activity on its 1910 hotline, by email or on its international
number, promising confidentiality and a reward for accurate information. [@4]

The tally was announced on 7 November 2026, two days before the submitted
report. [@2]
