Agash Wade, a St Catherine man, was on Tuesday sentenced to six months in prison
and fined a total of $215,000 over the discovery of 25 pounds of ganja at a
premises in Spanish Town. [@1] He appeared before Senior Parish Court Judge
Desiree Alleyne. [@1]

Wade was charged with possession of ganja, dealing in ganja, storage of ganja
and using his premises to conceal ganja. [@1] The concealment charge was dropped
by the prosecution. [@1] He was sentenced to six months' imprisonment for
storage of ganja, fined $200,000 or three months' imprisonment for dealing in
ganja, and $15,000 or 30 days' imprisonment for possession of ganja. [@1] The
judge ruled that the sentences are to run consecutively until the fines are
paid. [@1]

On September 6, police personnel conducted an operation in the community of De
la Vega City in Spanish Town, during which a search was carried out at Wade's
premises. [@1] During the search, vegetable matter resembling ganja, weighing 25
pounds, about 11.3 kilograms, was discovered. [@1] He was subsequently arrested
and charged. [@1]
