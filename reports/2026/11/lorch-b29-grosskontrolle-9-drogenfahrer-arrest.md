On Thursday 12 November 2026, Aalen police district officers ran a multi-hour
road control on the B 29 at the Lorch-Waldhausen parking lot, heading toward
Schwäbisch Gmünd, just past the Rems-Murr to Ostalbkreis border. [@1] Between 4
pm and 11:30 pm officers checked 589 vehicles and 688 people. [@2]

Nine drivers were under the influence of narcotics and three more drove without
a valid licence; two weapons-law offences were also registered during the check,
alongside nine cargo-security violations, two child-safety-restraint failures
and five seatbelt violations. [@1]

The Aalen police district statement confirms the same counts: two weapons-law
offences, nine cargo-security, two child-safety and five seatbelt violations,
three drivers without a valid licence, twelve defect reports, six vehicles whose
operating licence had lapsed, nine drivers driving under the influence of
narcotics, twelve people barred from continuing, one arrest and one security
deposit. [@2] Twelve people in total were stopped from continuing their journey
at the checkpoint: the police banned them from driving on, one was arrested and
one had to post a security deposit. [@1]
