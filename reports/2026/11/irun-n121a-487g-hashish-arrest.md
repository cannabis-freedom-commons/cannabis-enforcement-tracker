Guardia Civil officers detained two men at a traffic checkpoint in Irun after
stopping the car in which they were travelling on the N-121A road. [@1] The two
occupants of the vehicle were French nationals aged 47 and 48. [@1] Both men
were arrested accused of drug trafficking. [@1] The arrest took place on the
previous Sunday, when agents of the Citizen Security Unit, known as USECIC,
intercepted the vehicle. [@1] The Guardia Civil reported the arrest through its
social networks. [@1]

Officers found 487.5 grams of hashish in the vehicle, an amount described as
half a kilo. [@1] Officers also seized six methadone tablets from the same
vehicle. [@1] A Guardia Civil handout photo shows the methadone pills hidden in
the car's lighter. [@1]

The two men were travelling toward France when they were stopped in the Sunday
control by the Guardia Civil. [@2] The Guardia Civil account says that amounts
over 25 grams are treated as intended for trafficking rather than personal use.
[@2]
