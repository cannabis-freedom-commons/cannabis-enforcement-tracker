Carabineros of the Tenencia El Manzano, a unit of Chile's national police,
detained one man during preventive patrols on the Carretera de la Fruta, the
route H-66, after finding drugs inside his car. [@1]

While officers were carrying out their patrols along the route, they detected a
car travelling with its lights off and with a single occupant inside. [@1] The
driver tried to flee when he noticed the police, but Carabineros intercepted
him. [@1] During the search of the vehicle, officers found a bag under the
driver's seat that contained cocaine and marijuana, both in individual doses and
in bulk. [@1]

The man was arrested and taken to the police station, then placed at the
disposal of the court for the control de detención, the hearing at which a judge
reviews an arrest, and for the formalisation for the crime of drug trafficking,
the stage at which the prosecutor sets out the charges against him. [@1]
