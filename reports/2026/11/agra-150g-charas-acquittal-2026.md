An Agra court acquitted the accused in the 17-year-old case after police failed
to prove the recovery. [@1] Special NDPS Judge Harshvardhan acquitted Umrao
alias Dabar, a resident of Rajgarh in Madhya Pradesh, giving him the benefit of
doubt. [@2]

According to the Tajganj police-station complaint, Sub-Inspector Dinesh Pal
reported that a police team caught Umrao near Pushpanjali Phase-4 on the night
of 15 December 2009. [@2] Police claimed that 150 grams of charas were recovered
from his possession, after which he was produced in court and sent to jail. [@2]
The forensic laboratory weighed the charas at 136 grams, 14 grams less than the
150 grams the police had claimed. [@2]

At trial the officer gave one station-departure time in his testimony while the
recovery memo recorded another, and no general-diary copy showing the police
team's departure for the spot was produced. [@2] Police never made the person
who supplied the scales and weights used to weigh the charas a witness. [@2]
Police produced no sample-seal record and no malkhana property-store register in
court, so the court acquitted the accused on the contradictions and gaps in the
evidence. [@2]
