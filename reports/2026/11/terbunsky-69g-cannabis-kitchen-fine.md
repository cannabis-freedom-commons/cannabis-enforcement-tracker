Terbunsky District Court of Lipetsk Oblast sentenced a 56-year-old local
resident accused of unlawfully acquiring and storing narcotics, and found him
guilty. [@1] In Terbunsky okrug the same 56-year-old man was fined 30,000 rubles
for storing marijuana. [@2]

In the autumn of 2023 he picked a bush of wild-growing cannabis not far from his
home. [@1] He crushed the plant, dried it and put it in a container that had
held coffee, which he kept on a kitchen shelf. [@1] Police seized the substance
and sent it for examination. [@1] A specialist concluded that it was cannabis,
also called marijuana, weighing 6.9 grams, a significant amount under the law.
[@1] The joint press service of the Lipetsk Oblast court system said he had
picked the wild bush near his house, dried it and left the cannabis in a
container in the kitchen. [@2]

He admitted guilt and expressed remorse. [@1] Taking mitigating circumstances
and what was known about him into account, the court imposed a fine of 30,000
rubles. [@1] The court press service said Terbunsky District Court set that fine
after weighing mitigating circumstances and information about him. [@2]
