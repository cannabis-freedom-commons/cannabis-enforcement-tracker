The head of the investigating judges at the Tunis Court of First Instance
notified six defendants accused of forming an international drug-trafficking
network that their pre-trial detention would be extended by four more months.
[@1]

The defendants include a young woman, a public employee and a company agent.
[@1] The Public Prosecution at the Tunis Court of First Instance had ordered the
anti-drug sub-directorate at El Gorjani to hold the six. [@1] The case began
from information that the six had joined one of the most dangerous international
networks trafficking drugs smuggled from a European country into Tunisia. [@1]

A series of raids by the same anti-drug unit seized more than 2,000 slabs of
cannabis weighing more than 200 kilograms. [@1] The raids also seized more than
400,000 narcotic tablets of different kinds. [@1] Officers also seized a very
large sum of money and luxury, expensive cars and motorcycles. [@1]
