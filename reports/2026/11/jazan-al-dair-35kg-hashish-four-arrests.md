Saudi Border Guard land patrols in the Al-Dair sector of Jazan region arrested
four violators of the border security law who were trying to smuggle 35
kilograms of hashish. [@2,5] All four were Ethiopian nationals, according to Ajel
and Sraha. [@3] The same interception also turned up 74,400 tablets subject to
medical-trade controls, which are not counted as cannabis. [@1,5]

The Border Guard published the case on its own X account on 5 November 2026,
without naming the sector. [@4] The directorate said initial legal procedures
had been completed against the four and that they had been handed, with the
seized items, to the competent authority. [@2]

In the same batch of operations, patrols in the Al-Raboah sector of Asir region
arrested two Yemeni border-security violators over 40 kilograms of khat plant,
which is not cannabis. [@1] A separate Jazan interception in the same
announcement stopped eight border-security violators accused of smuggling 150
kilograms of khat plant, also outside the cannabis figures. [@1] The Border
Guard said it had completed initial procedures for everyone detained across the
Asir and Jazan operations and handed them with the seizures to the competent
authority for the penalties set by law. [@1]

The security agencies urged citizens and residents to report any information on
drug smuggling or promotion through the approved channels. [@1]
