Manjeri's NDPS court sentenced two men to six years of rigorous imprisonment
each and a fine of 25,000 rupees each. [@1] Judge T.G. Varghese sentenced
Muhammad Ashif, 30, of Puzhakkattiri near Perinthalmanna, and Aneesh, 34, of
Karuvarakundu. [@1]

The cannabis was being brought by car from Mysore to sell to young people in
Manjeri and nearby areas. [@1]

Excise Inspector P.V. Radhakrishnan investigated the case and filed the charge
sheet, and Special Public Prosecutor P. Suresh examined 15 witnesses. [@1] The
court was shown 28 documents and five material objects, and the two men were
sent to Thavanur Central Jail. [@1]
