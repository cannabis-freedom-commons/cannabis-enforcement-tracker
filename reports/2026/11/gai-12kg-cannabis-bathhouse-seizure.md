Criminal-investigation officers of the Gaysky interior ministry division
searched the private household of a 35-year-old local man in Gai, Orenburg
Oblast. [@2] They found the cannabis in the household's bathhouse. [@3] Officers
seized more than a kilogram of marijuana and about 200 grams of cannabis plant
parts and seeds. [@3] The regional interior ministry's press service reported
the seizure. [@3] Police said the search took place during the anti-drug
operation Kanal – Kaspiysky Mayak. [@4]

The 35-year-old owner told officers he had grown the seized material at his
household and kept it for personal consumption. [@1] The ORT television channel
reported that he said he had gathered the wild cannabis himself. [@3] A criminal
case was opened over the find under part 2 of article 228 of the Criminal Code.
[@2] The case was classified as storage of drugs in a large amount. [@4] The
offence carries a maximum of 10 years in prison. [@2] The suspect was placed
under a written undertaking not to leave the area; no detention was reported.
[@2]

The 200 grams covered seeds and plant parts together. [@4]
