Military police officers from the RAIO battalion arrested a man suspected of
drug trafficking and involvement with a criminal organisation in the Residencial
Maria Tereza neighbourhood of Rondonópolis, Mato Grosso, on the night of Friday
6 November 2026. [@1] The arrest was made by a team of the RAIO battalion of the
14th Independent Military Police Company during Operation Tolerância Zero às
Facções Criminosas, a state campaign against criminal factions. [@1]

Officers were on motorcycle patrol in the area when residents reported a house
with frequent movement of drug users and a man selling drugs there. [@1] The man
was at the gate of the house and threw a package on the ground before running to
the back of the property, where officers caught him. [@1] He was wearing an
electronic ankle monitor and, according to the Military Police, had several
previous police records for drug trafficking. [@1]

Officers found a piece of marijuana-like substance on the kitchen sink next to a
precision scale, and the drugs seized weighed about 915 grams in total. [@1] The
suspect was arrested and taken with the seized material to the 1st Civil Police
Station, and officers asked for the seized mobile phone to be examined. [@1] A
second account put the two portions of marijuana at about one kilogram and
reported that the suspect was already under electronic monitoring for the same
drug-trafficking offence, which police treated as reoffending. [@2] The suspect
had already been given an ankle monitor for the same drug-trafficking offence,
which police described as reoffending. [@2]
