Officers of Varna's First District Police Station checked a 31-year-old man from
Staro Oryahovo village, whom the Interior Ministry already knew. [@1] Marijuana
was found on him, an expert report confirmed. [@1] He was detained under police
measures for up to 24 hours. [@1]

A fast-track proceeding was opened against him. [@1] The check took place a few
days before the Varna regional police directorate published its information
bulletin for 5 November 2026. [@2] The police bulletin that carried the case is
dated 5 November 2026. [@2]

Local outlet Varnadnes.bg repeated the police account on 6 November 2026. [@3]
