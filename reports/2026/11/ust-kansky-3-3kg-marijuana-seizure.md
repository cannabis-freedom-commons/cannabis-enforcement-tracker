Police officers seized marijuana from a previously convicted 43-year-old
resident of Ust-Kansky District. [@1] According to the republican Interior
Ministry, the man gathered wild-growing cannabis on the territory of the
livestock farm where he was temporarily living in October of the current year.
[@1] He packed the collected plants into two nylon sacks and hid the sacks in an
ail. [@1]

Officers sent the seized substance for expert examination to determine its
nature and weight. [@1] Experts confirmed that the seized substance was
marijuana with a total dried weight of 3.3 kilograms. [@1] The republican
Interior Ministry described the seizure as more than 3 kilograms of marijuana.
[@1]

The villager admitted that he had prepared the intoxicating plants for his own
personal consumption. [@1] Police opened a criminal case under Part 2 of Article
228 of the Russian Criminal Code. [@1] The cited provision covers illegal
acquisition and storage of narcotic drugs without purpose of sale when committed
on a large scale. [@1]
