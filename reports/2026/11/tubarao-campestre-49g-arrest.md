Military Police officers identified two adolescents on a public street in
Campestre at about 11h50 during a police operation. [@1] The two fled toward a
wooded area as officers approached, and officers located and detained one of
them. [@1] The detained adolescent suffered scrapes on his arms and legs during
the flight, apparently from contact with the vegetation. [@1] He was taken to
hospital for medical care and then presented to the Civil Police station. [@1]

Police seized 49.2 grams of marijuana divided into individual portions from the
detained adolescent, along with 150 reais in cash. [@1] In São João Margem
Esquerda in Tubarão on the evening of Tuesday 3, police seized 208 pins of
cocaine totalling 188 grams near a residential complex. [@1] No suspect was
found in the São João seizure. [@1] In Oficinas at about 17h, two adolescents
were approached during police monitoring, 10 units of crack were found on the
ground, and a sniffer dog located 19 more units hidden under a brick at the back
of a property. [@1] Officers seized 29 units of crack weighing 4.5 grams in
Oficinas and detained the two adolescents for an infraction analogous to drug
trafficking. [@1]

The Military Police carried out the Campestre and Oficinas drug actions on the
same day in Tubarão. [@1] The occurrences in São João, Campestre and Oficinas
were reported as happening on Tuesday day 3 and involved cocaine, crack,
marijuana and money. [@1] Police used a sniffer dog to locate the São João drugs
hidden in vegetation. [@1]
