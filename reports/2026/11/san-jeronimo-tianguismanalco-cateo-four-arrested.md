Puebla state prosecutors searched a property in San Jerónimo Tianguismanalco, an
auxiliary board of the municipality of San Martín Texmelucan, and detained four
people. [@1] The state prosecutor's office (Fiscalía General del Estado, FGE)
carried out the operation with soldiers from the Secretariat of National Defence
(Defensa), the National Guard and the Puebla state police. [@1] Prosecutors said
the search followed intelligence work, field investigation, checks of anonymous
complaints and surveillance that identified a property linked to the sale and
distribution of drugs. [@1]

During the search officers detained Alejandro N., José Luis N., Erika N. and
Irene N. [@1] Officers seized 47 small bags holding a granular substance with
characteristics similar to crystal and a gram scale. [@1]

Prosecutors said the four detained people were handed to the Public Ministry
agent, who will continue the investigation and decide their legal situation.
[@1] Reto Diario likewise reported the detention of the same four people in a
search of a property in San Jerónimo Tianguismanalco, with 47 bags of a
substance similar to crystal and a gram scale seized. [@3] Two separate outlets
that reproduced the prosecutor's statement, Carlos Martin Huerta and Reto
Diario, described only crystal in this search, with no marijuana listed among
the items seized. [@2]
