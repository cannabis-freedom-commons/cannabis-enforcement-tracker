Two men aged 20 and 25 appeared before the tribunal in Carpentras on Monday 2
November 2026. [@1] The court sentenced the dealer to 24 months in prison and
his client to eight months. [@1] Carpentras police had caught them in the act at
the end of last summer while watching a suspected dealing point in Carpentras,
described as the capital of the Comtat. [@1] Officers saw a transaction between
the two men unfold before their eyes. [@1] Lounes Sefrani, 20, was then checked
in possession of two 100-gram bars of cannabis he had just bought. [@1]

A search that followed the street check recovered 13,470 euros in cash and
nearly two kilos of cannabis. [@1] The court treated one defendant as the
trafficker and the other as the buyer. [@1]

Carpentras police services carried out the surveillance of the suspected dealing
point. [@1] One of the two men had drawn his income from drug trafficking for a
year. [@1]
