Nevşehir anti-narcotics police placed 14 people under judicial proceedings after
a week of anti-drug work. [@1,2] Twelve were reported as people who used narcotic
substances and two were processed for violating Law No. 2313. [@1,2]

Officers seized one assembled setup for growing Indian hemp, with its apparatus.
[@1,2] They also seized 11 cannabis seeds and 1.62 grams of kubar esrar. [@1] The
same searches found 4.64 grams of methamphetamine, 0.83 grams of synthetic
cannabinoid bonzai, 0.95 grams of liquid synthetic cannabinoid bonzai and 58
synthetic pills. [@1] Officers also found a large number of implements for using
narcotic substances. [@1,2] The 1.62 grams of kubar esrar is the separately stated
cannabis weight in this mixed haul, while the methamphetamine, bonzai and pills
are not counted as cannabis. [@1]

The work was carried out by the narcotics branch of the Nevşehir Provincial
Police Directorate during the week before 10 November 2026 and targeted both
drug use and production materials. [@1] Police said their anti-drug efforts
would continue with determination. [@1,2]
