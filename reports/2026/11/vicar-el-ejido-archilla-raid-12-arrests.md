The Guardia Civil arrested at least twelve people on suspicion of growing and
distributing marijuana in Vícar and El Ejido. [@1] Five of the detained were
arrested in El Ejido and the rest were linked to the searches in Vícar. [@1] The
operation began before dawn on Wednesday 4 November 2026 and ran through the
morning, with dozens of officers deployed at points across El Ejido and Vícar.
[@1] Officers carried out up to ten entries and house searches in El Ejido and
Vícar, several of them in Barrio Archilla in Vícar. [@1]

The searches targeted homes used for growing and trafficking drugs, and officers
seized marijuana and various amounts of cash. [@1] La Voz de Almería reported
that the plants taken formed part of an indoor growing network in duplex homes
in Barrio Archilla. [@2] Officers also seized firearms with ammunition ready for
use and several thousand euros in cash, and the detainees were all Spanish
nationals, according to La Voz de Almería. [@2]

The raid was carried out by Guardia Civil judicial police with the GAR, its
rapid-action unit for high-risk interventions, and the USECIC, the provincial
command's public-security reserve unit. [@1] Vícar's Local Police sealed off
Barrio Archilla to secure the operation, and the town council said it was in
permanent contact with the Guardia Civil. [@1] The case file drawn up during the
searches, carried out with judicial staff present, went to the competent court,
which must decide on the detainees' situation while the investigation
establishes the structure's reach. [@1]
