Police arrested Abdul Rehman Najar, a resident of Chandigam, during routine naka
checking at Chandigam in the Sogam area of Kupwara district, Jammu and Kashmir.
[@1] The team was from Police Station Sogam, and the search of the man found
about 1.016 kilograms of a charas powder-like substance. [@1] Kashmir Images
named the man as Ab Rehman Najar of Chandigam and gave the same weight of about
1.016 kg of a charas powder-like substance. [@2]

Police took the man into custody and seized the suspected contraband under the
prescribed legal procedure. [@1] Police registered a case, FIR No. 50/2026, at
Police Station Sogam under sections 8 and 20 of the Narcotic Drugs and
Psychotropic Substances Act. [@1]

Officials said the investigation would establish the source of the substance and
identify other people possibly linked to the supply network. [@1] Kupwara police
said action against people involved in drug trafficking would continue across
the district and asked the public to pass on information, promising that
informants' identities would stay confidential. [@1]
