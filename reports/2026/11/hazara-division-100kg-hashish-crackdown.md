Hazara Police recovered more than 100 kilograms of hashish during a special
15-day crackdown across all districts of Hazara Division. [@1] Officers seized
more than two and a half maunds of charas, equivalent to over 100 kilograms, and
arrested members of drug-trafficking groups. [@2] The campaign was ordered by
Regional Police Officer Hazara Jahanzaib Nazir Khan and targeted
drug-trafficking networks alongside illegal weapons and vehicle-theft gangs.
[@1]

Police recovered dozens of illegal Kalashnikovs, rifles and shotguns and
thousands of rounds of ammunition during search and strike operations. [@1]
Officers traced and arrested several vehicle and motorcycle theft gangs and
recovered dozens of stolen vehicles and motorcycles for return to their owners.
[@1] Police seized illegally transported timber in separate operations and
handed it to the Forest Department after legal action against the smugglers.
[@1] Hundreds of proclaimed offenders and court absconders in murder, attempted
murder and other cases were arrested during the drive. [@2]

Jahanzaib Nazir Khan said all available resources were being used to maintain
law and order and eliminate crime across Hazara Division. [@1] He said action
against drug dealers would be intensified and protecting young people from drugs
remained a top priority. [@1]
