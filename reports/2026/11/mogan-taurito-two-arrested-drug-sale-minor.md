Mogan Local Police arrested two men in Taurito over the alleged sale of drugs to
a 14-year-old boy. [@1] Officers said they watched one man hand the buyer a
wrapper of apparent marijuana while the second man took part in the deal and
received the money. [@1] After confirming the buyer was 14, officers located his
legal guardian so the procedures could be carried out in the guardian's
presence. [@1] A search recovered the bag of suspected marijuana and a grinder
holding a brown resinous substance believed to be hashish or pollen. [@1]

The intervention took place on 6 November, and a canine-unit dog later helped
locate more substances hidden in a palm tree. [@1] In the palm tree officers
found five heat-sealed wraps of suspected cocaine, four pieces of apparent
hashish or pollen and two zip bags of suspected marijuana. [@1] Officers also
seized 260 euros in cash from one of the detainees. [@1] Both men were arrested
as alleged perpetrators of a crime against public health. [@1] After the reports
were prepared, they were transferred to the Guardia Civil post in Puerto Rico.
[@1]

Mogan Local Police said it maintains such surveillance operations to prevent
street-level dealing and protect residents and visitors. [@1]

The two outlets agree the scene was a sale in Taurito on 6 November where
officers surprised the suspects during the transaction. [@2]

The second outlet independently confirms that 260 euros in cash was seized from
one of those arrested. [@2]
