A 33-year-old man was arrested in Montpellier on the evening of Monday 9
November 2026 after cannabis and 6,800 euros in small notes were found during a
check on his vehicle. [@1,2] At about 22:00, officers on surveillance in the
Marels neighbourhood of Montpellier decided to stop a motorist they had just
watched leave the area. [@1,2] The driver complied with their order to stop. [@1]
A strong smell of cannabis had drawn the officers’ attention as they approached
the car. [@1] The man was arrested and held in police custody in an
investigation into drug trafficking. [@1]

At the foot of the passenger seat, officers found a black plastic bag holding
211 grams of cannabis resin and 42 grams of herbal cannabis, according to
figures released by police. [@1,2] A precision scale and several empty transparent
bags were also found inside the vehicle. [@1,2] On the driver, police found 6,800
euros in small denominations, made up of 98 ten-euro notes and 291 twenty-euro
notes. [@1,2]

During questioning, the man reportedly admitted buying and then reselling drugs
for several months. [@1,2] During questioning, the man declared earning about 150 euros per evening and 300 euros per weekend. [@2] Police said he also admitted making several deliveries.
[@1] Checks also showed his vehicle was uninsured and had not passed its
roadworthiness test. [@1,2] After his time in custody, the suspect was brought
before the court on 11 November for a guilty-plea hearing, known in France as a
CRPC. [@1,2]
