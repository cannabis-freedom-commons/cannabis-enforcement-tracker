Royal Gendarmerie officers of the Ait Amira territorial centre arrested two
suspected drug dealers in Hay Toudli on the evening of Wednesday 4 November
2026. [@1] Both suspects had previously been linked to drug-trafficking cases
and had criminal records in the field, the Chtouka Press source said. [@1]

The gendarmerie described the arrests as part of field work by its Ait Amira
units against drug dealers, with stepped-up operations targeting suspects at
spots residents had complained about. [@1] The two were placed in garde à vue,
Morocco's police-custody measure, pending an investigation supervised by the
public prosecutor's office at the Biougra first-instance court. [@1]
