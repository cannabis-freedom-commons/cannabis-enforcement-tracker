Military Police arrested a man for drug trafficking at around 23h on Saturday 7
November 2026 in the Perequê neighbourhood of Porto Belo, Santa Catarina. [@1]
Officers on patrol near a petrol station saw him throw an object away when he
noticed the patrol car and stopped him. [@1] The approach turned up six portions
of cocaine totalling about 5 grams. [@2]

During the stop officers saw notifications on his phone with drug delivery
orders and questions about prices. [@1] With the resident's permission, the
patrol went to a house he indicated in the Morretes neighbourhood of Itapema,
where officers found about 200 grams of marijuana and a precision scale. [@3]
The man acknowledged that the drugs and the scale were his and said he made
deliveries in Porto Belo and Itapema. [@2]

He was arrested and taken with the seized material to the Civil Police station
for the legal procedures. [@4] The details came from the press office of the
31st Military Police Battalion (BPM), as credited by the regional outlets. [@2]
