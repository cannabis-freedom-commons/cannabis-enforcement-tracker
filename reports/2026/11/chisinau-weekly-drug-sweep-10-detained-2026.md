Capital Police in Chișinău detained 10 people in the past week during actions
against drug consumption and trafficking. [@1] Separately, officers of the
Patrol and Rapid Reaction Battalion (BPRO) caught in the act 13 people
possessing prohibited substances and consumption devices. [@1]

In the operations officers seized several types of narcotics, including PVP
(salt), hashish, marijuana and mephedrone. [@1] Police have 19 criminal cases
concerning drug offences under management. [@1] Officers drew up 27 reports and
carried out 26 prevention activities. [@1] In the BPRO cases officers drew up
another 26 contravention reports. [@1]
