Civil police searched a property in Pelourinho, in the historic centre of
Salvador, on the afternoon of Tuesday 10 November 2026. [@1] Two men fled
through a vegetated area as the officers entered. [@1] The Bahia Civil Police
described the house as a weapons and drugs hideout of a criminal group active in
the area. [@1] Teams from the 1st Territorial Police Station (DT/Barris) carried
out the search. [@1] After the arrest of the man police called the group's
leader, intelligence teams identified members moving toward the Pelourinho
property. [@1] Investigators kept watch near the house before the officers went
in. [@1]

Officers found eight portions of pressed marijuana and two portions of Wolf-type
marijuana. [@2] The same search found 11 portions of cocaine and six crack
tablets, which are not counted as cannabis. [@2] Officers also seized two 5.56
mm rifles with filed serial numbers, 84 rounds of 5.56 mm ammunition and 224
rounds of 7.62 mm ammunition. [@2] Thirteen rifle magazines were seized with the
drugs and weapons. [@2] One mobile phone was seized with the drugs and weapons.
[@2]

Bahia Notícias reported that the man police called the group's leader was
arrested in Rio de Janeiro on Tuesday morning during Operação Sandstorm,
launched by the Bahia Civil Police. [@2] g1 reported that the seizure came a day
after that arrest in Rio de Janeiro. [@1] Police said the suspect had been
monitored for about three months. [@1] Investigations continued to identify the
two men who fled and other members of the group. [@1] The Civil Police were also
tracing the origin and destination of the weapons, ammunition and drugs. [@1]

The seized material was to be sent to the Technical Police Department for
forensic examination. [@1]
