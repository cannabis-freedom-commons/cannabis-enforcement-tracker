A Texas Department of Public Safety trooper stopped a vehicle travelling south
on East Loop 289 near Southeast Drive in Lubbock at about 10:35 p.m. on Saturday
7 November 2026. [@1] The trooper identified the driver as Larry Ramey, 65, and
the front passenger as Ronald Fritz, 32, who gave conflicting accounts of their
destination and showed signs of being on narcotics, according to court records.
[@1] Ramey refused consent to search, so a K-9 unit sniffed the vehicle and
alerted for narcotics, after which troopers carried out a probable-cause search.
[@1]

Troopers found 19 large plastic bags of a green leafy substance identified as
marijuana and a large number of containers of a yellow-brown waxy substance
identified as THC. [@1] The marijuana weighed about 21.2 pounds (9.6 kilograms)
and the THC about 1,088 grams, as weighed by authorities. [@1] Two handguns, two
shotguns and one rifle belonging to Ramey were also found in the vehicle. [@1]

Both men were taken to the Lubbock County Detention Center on charges of
possessing marijuana over 5 pounds but under 50 pounds and of possessing a
controlled substance in Penalty Group 2, 400 grams or more, the latter referring
to THC products. [@1] Ramey additionally faced a charge of unlawfully carrying a
weapon. [@1]

By Monday afternoon Fritz was held on bonds totalling $150,000 and Ramey on
bonds totalling $151,000. [@1]
