Street officers of the La Rioja federal operational division were conducting a
midday crime-prevention operation. [@1] They stopped a man who had been handling
an object with his hands and then put it into a waist pouch. [@1] The stop
happened on Avenida Ramón Navarro in the Mis Montañas neighbourhood, near
Provincial School No. 13, a secondary school, and Papa Francisco School No. 412,
a primary school. [@1] A second La Rioja outlet confirms the seizure happened
during a Thursday midday prevention operation in the same neighbourhood on the
same avenue. [@2]

Officers carried out a personal search and found marijuana among the man's
clothing. [@1] They seized the substance and placed the man at the disposal of
the Federal Justice system. [@1] The case is before substitute Judge Dr. Mario
Martínez, through the Criminal Secretariat headed by Dr. Liliam Chumbita. [@1]
That outlet likewise reports that the officers found marijuana on the man after
searching him. [@2]
