Police in the Zambezi region are searching for a suspect who fled during a
routine vehicle search at the Bukalo roadblock, abandoning cannabis, a vehicle
and cash. [@1] The suspect fled on foot after police intercepted the
consignment, leaving the drugs, vehicle and money behind. [@1] The suspect is
wanted on six counts including dealing in cannabis, possession of cannabis,
money laundering under the Prevention of Organised Crime Act, common assault,
assault by threatening and obstructing police officers. [@1]

The incident occurred on 4 November at around 18h00, when police intercepted the
consignment during a random search. [@1] Police valued the cannabis seized at
the Bukalo roadblock at N$177,000. [@2] Zambezi regional police commander
Commissioner Julia Sakuwa-Neo appealed to residents to join law enforcement
against drug trafficking and abuse, warning that drugs destroy young people
physically and mentally. [@1] The money-laundering count is laid under the
Prevention of Organised Crime Act 29 of 2004. [@2] The obstruction count is laid
under the Police Act 19 of 1990. [@2]
