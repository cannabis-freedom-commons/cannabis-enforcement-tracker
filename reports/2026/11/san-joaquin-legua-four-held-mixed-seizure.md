Chilean PDI detectives detained four people from a gang that sold illicit
substances in the commune of San Joaquín in the Metropolitan Region. [@1,3] Those
detained were three men and a woman aged 28 to 57 from the Legua Emergencia and
Antigua Legua populations. [@1] The operation raided four homes where the group
operated, identified as its sales points. [@2,3] The four detainees were placed at
the disposal of the competent authorities. [@2]

Officers seized more than a kilo of drugs comprising coca base, cocaine
hydrochloride and cannabis, together valued at six million pesos. [@1,3] Officers
seized more than 700,000 pesos in cash and five cell phones to be examined by
the PDI criminalistics laboratory. [@1,3] Officers also seized dosing elements,
digital scales, a car, an unbranded revolver, a Glock 40 pistol with fire
selector, an extended Glock magazine and 18 calibre-40 cartridges. [@1,3]

Investigators said intelligence and criminal analysis identified the homes as
gang sales points, and entry and search orders were obtained and executed to
detain the suspects and seize evidence. [@1]
