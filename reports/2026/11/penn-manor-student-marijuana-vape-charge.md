A student at Penn Manor High School in Lancaster County, Pennsylvania, was
charged with disorderly conduct after a vape containing marijuana was found on
them at school. [@1] Police said a vape detection alert came from the boys'
bathroom at the school on 4 November 2026. [@1] After reviewing security
footage, officers identified the student and took them to the office. [@1]

The student surrendered the vaping device, which police say contained marijuana.
[@1] Millersville Borough Police Department recorded the incident at the school
on East Cottage Avenue in Millersville, and said the device was found to contain
marijuana, leading to the disorderly conduct charge. [@2] The department listed
the case as a disorderly conduct incident dated 4 November and created on 6
November 2026. [@2]
