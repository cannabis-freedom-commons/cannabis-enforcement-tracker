Dhaka Metropolitan Police, the force for the capital, arrested 544 people during
a 24-hour drive across Dhaka. [@1] Police filed 62 cases during the same 24-hour
period. [@1]

During the drive police seized 540 grammes of ganja, the local term for
cannabis, along with 6,258 yaba pills, 4.2 grammes of heroin in four packets, 37
pethidine injections and two knives and two cutters. [@1]

Police said further legal action against those arrested was under way. [@1]

The drive is one of DMP's regular 24-hour operations in the capital, which it
announces day by day. [@1]
