Police searched a garage belonging to a 43-year-old Saransk resident on Lodygina Street in late August 2026. [@1,2,3] Info RM dated the search to 26 August. [@1] Officers found three marijuana packets and a plastic container marked by fire in the garage, then recovered ten cannabis bushes on abandoned ground nearby. [@1,2,3] The regional Interior Ministry reported about 200 grams of seized material in total. [@1,2,3]

The man told officers he had found wild cannabis and pulled some of it up. [@2,3] AiF Mordovia reported that he dried and packaged some of it for his own use. [@2] Info RM reported that he was subject to a written undertaking not to leave. [@1]

On 5 November, local outlets reported that the investigation into alleged unlawful drug storage was complete and the file had been sent to court. [@1,3]
