Finnish Customs, Tulli, Finland's customs authority, counts 14 suspects in the
case, with 12 still held on remand. [@1] Those with key roles are mainly
citizens of Albania, Kosovo and Italy. [@1] Some suspects came to Finland only
to help import and distribute drugs, while others are Finns or residents
recruited through social media. [@1] The investigation is due to finish in
November 2026 before going to Southern Finland's prosecution district for a
charging decision. [@2]

Customs says the group allegedly brought more than 170 kilograms of cocaine into
Finland and about 80 kilograms was seized. [@1] The group is also suspected of
distributing about 92 kilograms of cocaine and four kilograms of marijuana. [@2]
A house search also found more cocaine, a fairly large quantity of marijuana and
more than 60,000 euros in cash, some hidden inside a computer. [@2] The inquiry
began in May 2026 after a break-in to a sea container in Vuosaari Harbour in
Helsinki. [@2] Officers found the suspects' car in a parking lot in Vantaa and
seized about 50 kilograms of cocaine from the apartment they used. [@1] Officers
also apprehended two people after a backpack handover and seized about 10
kilograms of cocaine from the backpack. [@1] Customs seized a total of about 20
kilograms of cocaine from five express parcels sent from Italy under false
sender details. [@1]

Customs says the organisation had a clear hierarchy with different roles and was
directed from abroad. [@1] Some suspects only collected parcels from parcel
lockers and handed them to another person who distributed the drugs onward. [@1]
Investigators traced nearly 590,000 euros in criminal proceeds converted to
virtual currency, frozen at the National Bureau of Investigation's request. [@1]
The inquiry involved several Finnish police units, foreign customs and police
authorities and Europol. [@1]
