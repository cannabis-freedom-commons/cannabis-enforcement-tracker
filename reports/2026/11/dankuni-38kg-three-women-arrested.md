Belur Government Railway Police officers under the Howrah GRP District arrested
three women at Dankuni railway station and seized about 38 kilograms of
suspected ganja from them. [@1,2] The accused were identified as Ritu Sarkar (35),
Unnati Roy (34) and Champa Bhowmik Das (33). [@1] A case under the Narcotic
Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act was registered against the three at Belur
GRP police station. [@1] Further investigations were under way to trace the
wider procurement network and identify others in the trafficking syndicate. [@1]

Officers recovered 32 packets of the drug concealed in the women's luggage
during a search conducted under standard legal protocols. [@1,2] Police said the
consignment was allegedly being moved for clandestine delivery and local sale in
the area. [@1] Preliminary questioning indicated the trio had procured the
narcotics from North Bengal to set up an illegal supply chain in Dankuni. [@1,2]

According to GRP officials, the arrests followed a targeted operation based on
specific inputs about drug smuggling on Train No. 12504 DN SMVT Bangalore
Humsafar Express. [@1] Police intercepted the three passengers upon their
arrival. [@1]

Police acted on a prior tip that ganja was being brought for trafficking on a train from Assam to Bengaluru. [@2] Plainclothes Belur GRP officers waited under the Dankuni overbridge and took the three backpack-carrying women to Belur GRP station for a search. [@2] The three women were Siliguri residents who had travelled from New Jalpaiguri to Dankuni on the SMVT Bengaluru Express to deliver ganja in Dankuni and parts of Howrah and Hooghly. [@2] The 32 brown-tape-wrapped packets were hidden in backpacks and a ladies handbag. [@2] The women tried to leave their bags and flee when they saw police activity and were detained with the help of a woman constable. [@2] The women said they were hired for money and that more companions escaped in the station crowd, for whom police began a search. [@2] Police put the approximate market value of the seized ganja at about 76 lakh rupees. [@2]
