Police in Schrobenhausen, the local police station for the area, stopped a
23-year-old driver on the Schrobenhausener Straße in Pobenhausen, part of the
municipality of Karlskron in Bavaria. [@2] The check came at about 21:40 on
Friday 6 November 2026. [@1] Officers found signs typical of drug use, and the
driver told them he consumed cannabis regularly. [@1] On the second outlet's
account, the man came from the municipality of Geisenfeld, and his journey was
ended where he was stopped. [@2]

Officers barred him from continuing to drive and had a blood sample taken from
him. [@1] Police Schrobenhausen said he now faces a fine and a driving ban, and
the case is being handled as a regulatory offence procedure rather than a
criminal case. [@1] Pfaffenhofen Today reported that if the laboratory test
confirms drug driving, he faces a driving ban, a fine notice and penalty points
on his driving record. [@2]
