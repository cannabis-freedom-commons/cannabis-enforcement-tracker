The Dhaka Metropolitan Police arrested 577 people and filed 55 cases during 24
hours of raids across the capital. [@1] Police said the arrests were made in
different police-station areas of the capital on Wednesday, 4 November. [@2]
Police said further legal proceedings against the arrested people were under
way. [@1]

During the operations police recovered 150 grams of cannabis, the amount counted
here. [@1] The same haul included 2,539 yaba pills and 1.85 grams of heroin,
neither of which is counted as cannabis. [@2] Police also recovered three
motorcycles, eight mobile phones and Tk 1,500 in cash. [@2] Police also rescued
one victim during the drives. [@2]

Mirpur division accounted for the most arrests with 141, followed by Wari with
94, Tejgaon with 90, Motijheel with 85, Gulshan with 46, Lalbagh with 42, Uttara
with 41, Ramna with 37 and one by the Detective Branch. [@1]
