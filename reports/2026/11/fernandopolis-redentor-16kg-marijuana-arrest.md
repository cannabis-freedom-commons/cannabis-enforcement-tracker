Two men were arrested in flagrante for drug trafficking on the afternoon of 3
November 2026 during a Military Police action in the Redentor neighbourhood of
Fernandópolis. [@2] The case was registered on Rua Benjamin Reis at a property
police described as the target of complaints about drug sales. [@2] Officers
approached the pair after one discarded a portion resembling marijuana on seeing
the patrol car and the other threw a bag into a vacant lot behind the property.
[@2]

In the discarded bag officers found bricks appearing to be marijuana as well as
crack and cocaine portions and a precision scale. [@2] Officers found eight more
marijuana portions on a bed during a search of one suspect's house. [@2] In
total officers seized about 1.6 kilograms of marijuana along with 107 grams of
cocaine and 235 grams of crack plus the other objects. [@1] Officers also seized
cash, a precision scale and a mobile phone, reported as R$70 in cash and a
mobile phone taken from the man. [@2]

The two men were taken to the Plantão Policial, where they remained held at the
disposal of the courts. [@1] After presentation of the case, the police
authority ordered a trafficking report to be drawn up. [@2]
