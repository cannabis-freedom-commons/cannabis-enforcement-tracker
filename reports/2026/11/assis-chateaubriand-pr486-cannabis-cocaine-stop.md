Military police in western Paraná stopped a vehicle on the PR-486 highway in
Assis Chateaubriand at about 23h20 on Friday 6 November 2026 and found portions
of cannabis and cocaine. [@1] The teams carried out the stop in an operation
involving ROTAM, the rural patrol and the battalion's 2nd Section, under the
operations Protetor and Omnis. [@2]

Police said in the incident report that the drugs were intended for the personal
use of the vehicle's occupants. [@2] Officers issued a Termo Circunstanciado,
the procedural summons used in Brazil for minor offences, against those
involved, and seized the drugs for the legal procedures. [@1] The occurrence was
recorded as drugs for personal consumption. [@2]

The police did not report the quantity of drug seized or the number of people
stopped in the vehicle. [@1]
