Carabinieri of the Fiuggi station reported a local man at liberty after a road
check in Fiuggi developed into a home search. [@1] The man was already known to
law enforcement because of previous judicial and police matters. [@1]
Investigators said field checks clarified his alleged unlawful possession of
drugs intended for sale. [@1]

Officers seized about thirty grams of marijuana during the home search. [@1]
They also seized various materials described as unequivocally linked to
drug-dealing activity. [@1]

The operation was carried out on the afternoon of the day before the 13 November
2026 report by carabinieri of the local Fiuggi station. [@1] The carabinieri
were engaged in routine territorial control and crime-prevention duties when the
roadside check produced the suspicion that led to the search. [@1] The account
presents the episode as part of constant local attention against widespread
illegality and drug dealing in the area. [@1]
