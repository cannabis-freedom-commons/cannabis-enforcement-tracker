Udaynagar police, in Dewas district of Madhya Pradesh, uprooted more than 30
cannabis plants growing among a pigeon pea crop in a field at Kavtiapani
village. [@1] Acting on a tip-off, police identified the plants in a field
belonging to a person named Poonam Mujalde, near a forest. [@1] The plants stood
about four feet high among the pigeon pea. [@1] Officers used a drone to check
the field and the condition of the plants before going in. [@1] The operation
began on Saturday afternoon and ran for several hours. [@1]

Station house officer Abhinav Shukla said the plants were uprooted and weighed
at 65 kg. [@1] Police put the value of the cannabis at about two lakh rupees.
[@1] A case was registered against Poonam under the Narcotic Drugs and
Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act. [@1] The team included sub-inspector Rakesh
Singh and other officers of the station. [@1] The report described the raid as
the third cannabis enforcement action in about 22 days, after plants were seized
at Garaghati and Borpadaw villages of Mahigaon panchayat. [@1]
