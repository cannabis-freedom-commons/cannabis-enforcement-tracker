On 13 November 2026, state police officers stopped two men and a woman in
Cancún's Zona Hotelera after spotting them allegedly swapping small drug bags
around kilometre 14.5 of bulevar Kukulcán. [@2] The three detainees were
identified as Marvin Arbey “N”, Emmanuel Enrique “N” and Kenia Yurami “N”. [@1]

Officers of the state police's Grupo Orión Zona Norte unit made the stop during
patrols in the tourist corridor after receiving a support request for the
sector. [@1] The intervention yielded 168 small bags with green herb, apparently
marijuana, and 54 bags with crystalline fragments resembling crystal, 222
wrappers of possible illicit substances in total. [@1] Police also seized three
backpacks (brown, green-turquoise checkered and dark blue) and an unquantified
amount of cash. [@1]

The suspects, the drugs and the other objects were turned over to the
prosecutor's office specialised in street-level drug trafficking (narcomenudeo),
which will determine their legal status. [@1] Authorities did not publish the
total weight of the seized substances nor whether the detainees are linked to a
drug-distribution organisation in the hotel zone; lab tests will establish the
nature of the substances. [@1]
