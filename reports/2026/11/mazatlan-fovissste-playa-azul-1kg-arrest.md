Municipal police in Mazatlán detained a 51-year-old man from La Cruz de Elota
after allegedly finding about one kilogram of marijuana inside a backpack he was
carrying in the Fovissste Playa Azul neighborhood. [@1] The detained man was
identified as Raúl, and officers stopped him during a prevention and
surveillance patrol on the streets Ahome and Sinaloa de Leyva in that
settlement. [@1] Police said they approached him for a preventive intervention
after observing him allegedly consuming alcoholic drinks in public. [@1]

During the inspection, officers searched his black backpack and found two bags
containing a plant substance with characteristics similar to marijuana, with an
approximate weight estimated at one kilogram. [@1] When questioned about the
backpack's contents, Raúl said he was waiting for a person he knew by the
nickname "El Pelón" and claimed he did not know what was inside the luggage he
was carrying. [@1] Following the discovery of the suspected drug, police
detained him and seized the substance before transferring him to the
corresponding authorities. [@1]

He was placed at the disposition of the South Region Agency of the Public
Prosecutor Specialized in Health Crimes, in its small-scale dealing modality,
which will investigate and determine his legal situation for alleged crimes
against health. [@1] The ministerial authority will determine through the
corresponding procedures the nature of the seized substance and any resulting
legal responsibilities. [@1]
