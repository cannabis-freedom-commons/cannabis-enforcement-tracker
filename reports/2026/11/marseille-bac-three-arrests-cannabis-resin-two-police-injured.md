Anti-crime squad officers arrested two men selling drugs in the Escourtines
neighbourhood of Marseille's 11th arrondissement shortly before 7 pm on Sunday 8
November 2026. [@1] The two men, aged 40 and 24, were carrying 13 sachets of
cocaine and 5 of cannabis resin for sale. [@1] A search of one of their homes
found a revolver and a 7.65 pistol with its magazine. [@1] Both were taken into
police custody. [@1]

About an hour later, around 8 pm, a third man selling drugs in the
Campagne-Lévêque housing estate drew the attention of night officers of the
northern anti-crime squad patrolling Marseille's 15th arrondissement. [@1] He
ran, dropping several sachets as he fled, and resisted when officers caught him.
[@1] Two officers were injured in the arrest, one in the right thumb and knees,
the other in the ribs, left hand, right elbow and knees. [@1] Police seized 110
grams of cocaine and took the 25-year-old into police custody. [@1]

All three were held for selling cocaine and cannabis resin from the street, two
in the 11th arrondissement and one in the 15th. [@1]
