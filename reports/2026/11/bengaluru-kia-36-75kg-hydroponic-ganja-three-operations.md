Bengaluru Customs officials seized 36.75 kg of hydroponic ganja from three
passengers arriving from Bangkok in separate interceptions at Kempegowda
International Airport on 6 and 8 November 2026. [@1] Customs valued the haul at
approximately ₹12.86 crore. [@1]

In the first two seizures on 6 November, officers intercepted passengers
arriving from Bangkok and recovered 15.75 kg and 12 kg of hydroponic ganja,
valued at ₹5.51 crore and ₹4.2 crore. [@1] The contraband in those two cases was
concealed in the passengers' checked-in baggage. [@1] In a third seizure on 8
November, officers intercepted another passenger arriving from Bangkok and
recovered 9 kg of hydroponic ganja valued at ₹3.15 crore. [@1]

The accused in the 6 November seizure of 12 kg of ganja was arrested under the
Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act, 1985. [@1] Further
investigation is under way to ascertain the source of the narcotics and identify
the network involved. [@1]
