The court in Turnhout sentenced 43-year-old E.M. from Geel to nine months in
prison, suspended, for repeated drug possession. [@1] The court ordered him to
enter guidance and to look for work. [@1] E.M. had already accumulated 36
convictions in the past. [@1]

On 15 May 2025 Geel-Laakdal-Meerhout police held a nuisance control in Geel's
Stadspark, where E.M. ignored an order to stop on his scooter and then fell over
uneven ground and injured his knee. [@1] Police saw him hide something in the
bushes, where they found a tobacco pouch holding 9 grams of cannabis, 4.5 grams
of hashish and 0.75 grams of cocaine. [@1] About a month later he told
investigators the drugs were not his, while saying he smoked two joints a day to
be able to sleep. [@1] On 4 July 2025 police saw E.M. among nine suspicious
people in the Stadspark and found him with a bag containing 2.5 grams of
cannabis he had just bought. [@1]

On 31 October 2025 E.M. fell off his scooter again while trying to escape
another police control. [@1] That check found him with 5 grams of cocaine and an
imitation firearm, which he said he carried only to show off. [@1] The man had
been caught three times in possession of cannabis and cocaine. [@1]
