Michoacán's state attorney general's office (Fiscalía General del Estado, FGE)
carried out three raids on properties in the municipality of Los Reyes, seizing
assets from a criminal group. [@1] The raids were carried out simultaneously in
the Monte Verde housing development and the Villa Oriente neighbourhood of Los
Reyes. [@1]

Officers seized about 1.7 kilograms of marijuana at the properties. [@2] The
properties were identified as operating sites of a group attributed to Fausto
Alejandro "N", known as "El Bolillo". [@2] The operation was led by the state
prosecutor's office with the Guardia Civil's specialised investigation
undersecretariat, the federal security ministry, the National Guard and the army
(Defensa). [@2] Officers also seized 45 doses of methamphetamine and marijuana,
15 THC vapes, a filling machine, bill counters, gram scales, seven mobile
phones, nine SIM cards and two birds. [@2]

The seizures also included a .308-calibre rifle, 20 magazines, 227 rounds
reserved for the armed forces, 14 tactical vests, a drone and a Can-Am vehicle.
[@1] Quadratín Michoacán added that the 227 cartridges were of a type reserved
for the armed forces and that officers also found 20 magazines at the
properties. [@2]

The seized properties and evidence were handed to the public prosecutor's office
to continue expert examinations and the investigation into the cell. [@1]
Michoacán's attorney general said the operations formed part of efforts to
dismantle extortion and drug-dealing networks in the region. [@2]
