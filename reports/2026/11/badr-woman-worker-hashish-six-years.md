The New Cairo Criminal Court, sitting in the Fifth Settlement, sentenced a woman
described as a worker to six years of aggravated imprisonment for holding
hashish for sale in the Badr police district. [@1] Presiding judge Wagih Hamza
Shaqweir led the panel, with judges Yasser Mohamed Youssef, Sameh Abdel Ghani
al-Antabli and Sherif Sami Ali al-Saeed. [@1] The referral order said she held
the hashish, without legal authorisation, on 22 April 2026 in the same district.
[@1]

A detective from the Badr police station was on duty at a security checkpoint in
the district when he saw her looking suspicious and asked to search her
belongings; she allowed him to. [@1] He found 125 brown pieces of hashish, a sum
of money and a mobile phone. [@1] She admitted holding the drugs for sale, said
the money came from her dealing and that she used the phone to contact
customers, the officer told the court. [@2]

A laboratory report examined 30 of the 125 pieces and confirmed they were
hashish resin listed in the schedules of Egypt's anti-drug law. [@2] Al-Masry
Al-Youm puts the weight of the 125 pieces at 9 kg 350 g. [@2]
