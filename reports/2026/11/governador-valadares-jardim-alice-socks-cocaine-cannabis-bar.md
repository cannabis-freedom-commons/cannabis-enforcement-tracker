Military Police arrested a man in Governador Valadares on the night of Thursday
5 November 2026 after stopping him with cocaine hidden in his pocket and socks.
[@1] Officers had received a tip that the man used a car to move drugs, watched
a vehicle parked near a house, and approached him when he left the property
towards the car. [@1] During the personal search officers found one portion of
cocaine in his pocket and another 10 packets hidden in his socks. [@1]

The woman in the house let the officers in, and on a table in the bedroom they
found 30 more cocaine packets, cling film, a card machine and cash. [@1] In a
cardboard box the team also found 25 tablets with the characteristics of
ecstasy, one bar of material similar to cannabis, and a bag of a substance
preliminarily identified as boric acid. [@1] The police record said the boric
acid would be used to adulterate or refine cocaine, not cannabis. [@1] The haul
counted in total 40 cocaine packets, one portion of the same drug, 25 ecstasy
tablets, one cannabis bar, the suspected boric acid, a card machine and R$ 94 in
coins. [@1]

The man admitted the drugs were his, according to the Military Police, and was
taken to a municipal hospital for a medical assessment before being handed to
the Civil Police. [@1]
