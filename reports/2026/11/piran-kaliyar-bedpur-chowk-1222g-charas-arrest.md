Piran Kaliyar police in Haridwar district, Uttarakhand, arrested a 26-year-old
man after stopping and searching a car near Bedpur Chowk. [@1] The checking took
place on 9 November under a team led by station house officer Kamal Mohan
Bhandari. [@1]

Officers seized 1 kg 222 g of charas, a cannabis resin, from him. [@1] Police
put the international market value of the charas at about four lakh rupees. [@1]
Police also seized a Renault Kwid car registered as UK08BL3527 and an Oppo F-27
Pro mobile phone said to have been used in the smuggling. [@1]

Police said the man told them he collected small quantities of charas from drug
users in his village and nearby villages and then sold it at high prices in the
Kaliyar area. [@1] Police said he works as a driver and joined the drug trade to
cover his expenses and earn more money. [@1]

A case was registered against him at Piran Kaliyar police station under sections
8, 20 and 60 of the NDPS Act, India's Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances
Act. [@1] He was being produced in court and police were gathering information
about his criminal history. [@1] Police described the arrest as part of
Operation Prahar and the Nasha Mukt Devbhoomi-2026 campaign, district drives
against drug trafficking ordered by the Haridwar senior superintendent of
police. [@1]
