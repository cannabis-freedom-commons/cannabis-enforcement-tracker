Police on night patrol stopped a 22-year-old man in the streets of Périgueux,
Dordogne, in the night of Thursday 5 to Friday 6 November 2026. [@1] The driver
tried to hide objects inside his tracksuit and, when asked to hand them over,
produced several sachets of cannabis resin and cocaine. [@1]

Dordogne Libre reports the same find as several sachets of cannabis resin and
two cocottes, small units of cocaine. [@2] He was arrested immediately and
placed in garde à vue, the French police custody used for questioning. [@1]
Dordogne Libre reports that the officers had stopped a Renault in poor condition
during the patrol. [@2] The man was driving to Coulounieix-Chamiers to deliver
to a customer, Dordogne Libre reports. [@2]

Questioned by judicial police officers, he admitted that he resold narcotics and
said he had been about to make a delivery. [@1] A search of his home found more
cannabis resin and a sachet of cocaine. [@1] He is due to appear before the
court on 2 February 2027. [@1]
