A patrol of the anti-crime squad, the brigade anticriminalité, was watching a
car park behind a residence on rue du Refuge in Besançon at about 17:10 on
Sunday 8 November 2026 after spotting a youth making repeated hand-to-hand deals
with parked motorists. [@1] When the officers walked onto the car park, the
17-year-old came towards them and offered three bags of cannabis resin bars for
sale. [@1] The officers declined his offer and arrested him. [@1] He struggled
and resisted physically and was handcuffed before being taken to his home for a
search. [@1]

The search found 1,470 euros in cash, 315.6 grams of cannabis resin and 45.3
grams of cannabis herb, as well as 12 grams of cocaine, 37 grams of ketamine and
50 ecstasy tablets. [@1] Questioned in custody, he admitted selling drugs for
payment and admitted trying to get away, waving his arms to stop officers
handcuffing him. [@1]

He was brought before the judicial court in Vesoul on 10 November 2026. [@1] The
juvenile judge ordered him held in a judicial educational unit in Auxerre,
banned him from Besançon and required him to undergo treatment. [@1] He is due
to appear before the Vesoul children's court in February 2027. [@1]
