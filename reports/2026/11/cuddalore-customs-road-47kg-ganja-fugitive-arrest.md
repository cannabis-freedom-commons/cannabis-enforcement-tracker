Tamil Nadu special police arrested Yuvaraj, who had been absconding in a
ganja-smuggling case, about a month after the original interception. [@1]
Investigators identified the fugitive as 23-year-old Yuvaraj, the son of Muthu
of Puthupettai in Panruti. [@1] One of the two youths stopped during the
roadside check managed to flee from the officers at the scene. [@1] Officers
arrested the other youth at the scene, 24-year-old Rakesh, the son of Isaac of
Avadi in Chennai, and seized the ganja he was carrying. [@1]

The Superintendent of Police's special team was patrolling Customs Road in
Cuddalore on 3 October when the case began. [@1] The patrol officers stopped
youths who were walking along that road and searched the bag they were carrying.
[@1] The bag held 4.7 kg of ganja, the local term for cannabis, which the police
valued at Rs 2.5 lakh. [@1]

Police said Yuvaraj had remained in hiding after fleeing, and a prolonged search
followed to trace him. [@1] Special-team officers then arrested the fugitive
Yuvaraj and lodged him in jail. [@1]
