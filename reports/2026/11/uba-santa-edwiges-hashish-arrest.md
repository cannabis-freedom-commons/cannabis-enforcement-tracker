Military police arrested a 25-year-old man on suspicion of drug trafficking and
resisting arrest on the afternoon of Thursday 5 November 2026 in the Santa
Edwiges neighbourhood of Ubá, Minas Gerais. [@1] The incident was recorded at
about 16:35 on Avenida Elídia da Silva Fagundes. [@1]

Officers approached him during searches for those responsible for a robbery,
after reports of drug sales near the neighbourhood square and images from social
media suggesting drugs were being offered. [@1] A personal search near his home
found a portion of a hashish-like substance, R$10 and a mobile phone. [@1]

According to the police record, the man said he was a drug user and gave written
authorisation to search the home. [@1] On the way to the house he tried to flee
and resisted, and officers said they used physical force and handcuffs to
restrain him. [@1] The search of the house found another hashish-like portion,
five buchas, two portions of marijuana-like material, a precision scale and
further cash. [@1]

In total officers seized two portions of hashish-like substance, five buchas and
two portions of marijuana-like material, plus the scale, cash and phone. [@1]
The man and the seized material were taken to the on-duty Civil Police station
in Ubá. [@1] RCWTV reported the arrest as made during the searches linked to the
two robberies on the same day. [@2]
