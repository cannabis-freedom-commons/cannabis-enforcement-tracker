A South Carolina man was arrested Monday after deputies said he sold suspected
drugs to an undercover agent in Altamonte Springs. [@1] Yeriel Fortunato, 35,
was arrested on 9 November following an undercover drug operation in Altamonte
Springs, according to the Seminole County Sheriff's Office. [@1] Fortunato told
investigators he attended all three days of the Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) in
Orlando, which took place at Tinker Field over the weekend. [@1] He faces 18
charges, including methamphetamine trafficking, possession of several controlled
substances with intent to sell and conspiracy offenses, according to a Seminole
County Sheriff's Office arrest report. [@1]

Investigators said a confidential informant arranged a drug purchase through
electronic messages. [@1] Fortunato arrived at the meeting location in a rented
Nissan Sentra and invited the undercover agent into the vehicle, according to
the report. [@1] The agent paid Fortunato $1,100 for a bag containing a white
crystalline substance. [@1] Agents then arrested him without incident. [@1] A
search of the vehicle uncovered substances that field-tested presumptively
positive for cocaine, heroin, methamphetamine, ketamine, LSD, cannabis and
psilocybin mushrooms. [@1] The substance purchased by the undercover agent
weighed 36.9 grams with packaging and field-tested presumptively positive for
amphetamines, according to the report. [@1]

Investigators used a noncertified scale, and the arrest report included no
confirmatory laboratory results. [@1] Agents also recovered the $1,100 used in
the transaction, investigators said. [@1] Fortunato told investigators he lives
in Greenville, South Carolina, and had been staying at a rental property in
Kissimmee for the festival. [@1] He declined to disclose how he obtained the
drugs, according to the report. [@1] Fortunato was taken to the John E. Polk
Correctional Facility. [@1]
