Anjuna Police arrested a 27-year-old man from Himachal Pradesh during a raid
near the Football Ground at Siolim, Bardez, on 10 November 2026. [@1] The
accused was identified as Manish, 27, son of Karnail Singh, a resident of Ward
No. 1, Village Dhimla, Post Bakani, Tehsil Chamba, Himachal Pradesh. [@1] The
raid was conducted between 8.30 pm and 10.30 pm following specific information
received by the police. [@1] The accused was placed under arrest at 11.51 pm on
10 November 2026. [@1]

During the raid, police allegedly found 67 grams of suspected charas in his
illegal possession, valued at approximately Rs 67,000. [@1] Anjuna Police
Station registered Crime No. 135/2026 under Section 20(b)(ii)(A) of the Narcotic
Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act at 11.51 pm on 10 November 2026.
[@1] The operation was conducted by PSI Sahil V. Warang of Anjuna Police
Station, who also lodged the complaint in the matter. [@1]

Police said further investigation was underway to establish the source of the
drugs and determine whether others were involved. [@2]
