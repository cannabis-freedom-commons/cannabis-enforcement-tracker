District officers of the Tomaszow Lubelski County Police traced a 39-year-old
man wanted to serve a prison sentence and detained him in Gmina Tomaszow
Lubelski on Thursday. [@1] The man had previously been sentenced as a repeat
offender for driving under the influence of alcohol and had ignored a
court-imposed lifetime driving ban. [@1] For failing to comply with the court's
judgment, the 39-year-old was to serve six months' imprisonment. [@1] After the
necessary procedures, the 39-year-old was sent to prison to serve that
previously imposed six-month term, while he will separately face proceedings for
the substances found on him and at his home. [@1]

During the detention procedures, officers found several dealer portions of
psychotropic substances on the detained man. [@1] Officers also searched the
39-year-old's residence, where they found further prohibited substances,
including several portions of marijuana as well as psychotropic substances. [@1]
In connection with the drugs found, the detainee was charged with violating the
law on counteracting drug addiction. [@1] Illegal possession of the substances
carries a penalty of up to three years' imprisonment. [@1]
