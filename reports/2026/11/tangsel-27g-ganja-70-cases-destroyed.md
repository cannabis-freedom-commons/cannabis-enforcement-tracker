The South Tangerang district prosecutor's office (Kejari Tangsel) destroyed
confiscated evidence from 70 general criminal cases with final, binding
judgments on Monday, 9 November 2026. [@1] Its head, Aditya Rakatama, said the
exercise carried out court rulings ordering that the evidence be confiscated for
destruction and could not be used again. [@1]

Of the 70 cases, 46 involved narcotics, six the health law, five child
protection and five theft. [@1] The rest covered firearms and bladed weapons,
assault, consumer protection and other offences. [@1] The destroyed narcotics
included 26.6792 grams of ganja, the Indonesian term for cannabis. [@1] The
total also included 240.8887 grams of sabu, crystal methamphetamine, 917.2894
grams of sinte, a synthetic drug, six ecstasy pills and 254 etomidate items
weighing 717.43 grams. [@1] Officers also destroyed 75,613 illegal pills, 12
mobile phones and four bladed weapons. [@1]

The ganja, sinte and etomidate were burned, while the sabu and ecstasy pills
were dissolved in water, blended and sent into a drain. [@1] The phones were
smashed with hammers and the bladed weapons cut with a machine. [@1] Aditya said
destroying the drugs was meant to stop them being misused or circulating again
in the community. [@1]

Ronny Bona Tua Hutagalung, the office's intelligence chief, said it would keep
carrying out every court ruling, including on confiscated property with final
legal force. [@1]
