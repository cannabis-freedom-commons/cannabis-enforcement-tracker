Investigators had received tips some time earlier that a 56-year-old man in
Groß-Gerau was growing a larger quantity of cannabis plants at his home and
garden plot. [@1,3] Initial inquiries corroborated the suspicion against him,
after which the Darmstadt public prosecutor's office applied for search warrants
and a judge issued them. [@1,3] Acting on those warrants, criminal police officers
searched the man's flat on Reichenberger Straße and his separate parcel in a
Kleingartenanlage, an allotment garden, both in Groß-Gerau, on Tuesday, 3
November. [@1,3] On the balcony of the flat and in a grow tent at the allotment
plot, the officers found a total of 113 cannabis plants and seized them. [@2,3]

The searches and seizure were announced as a joint press release by the
Darmstadt prosecutor's office and the South Hesse police headquarters. [@1]
Investigators said further inquiries and laboratory examinations still have to
clarify whether and how much of the psychoactive substance THC the seized plants
contain. [@1,3] The 56-year-old is now under investigation on suspicion of
violating the Konsumcannabisgesetz, the consumer cannabis law. [@2,3]

Under the law in force since 1 April 2024, only three cannabis plants per person are allowed. [@3]
