On 11 November 2026, a police operation in barrio Confluencia in Neuquén ended
with the seizure of 45 grams of cannabis after a neighbour's alert identified
three people circulating in a car. [@1] The operation began during a preventive
patrol by officers of the División Seguridad y Operativa Bancaria, who
intercepted the vehicle on calle Copahue near Obreros Argentinos after receiving
the information. [@1]

During the intervention the occupants voluntarily produced items inside the car,
among them bags with a plant substance, a precision scale and two grinders. [@1]
On the finding the police requested the intervention of the División
Antinarcóticos to determine what the substance was. [@1] After the corresponding
tests the specialised staff established that it was 45 grams of cannabis sativa,
and the material was seized as part of the procedure. [@1]

So far, the official information did not specify whether there were any
detainees nor what judicial measures were adopted regarding the three occupants
of the vehicle. [@1]
