Two men were detained on suspicion of carrying 96 small bags of apparent
marijuana in the Mexico City borough of Gustavo A. Madero. [@1] Officers
carrying out inspections in the area as an investigative method against crime
saw the two men making an exchange for cash. [@1]

One of the two men had entered the Mexico City prison system four times, for
robbery in different forms and for crimes against health. [@1]

The detention was reported in connection with the streets Norte 82 and Oriente
107. [@1]
