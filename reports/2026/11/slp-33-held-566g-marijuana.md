San Luis Potosí's Guardia Civil Estatal, the state civil guard, detained 33
people in recent hours during intensified operations across the state's four
regions. [@1] The operations were described as part of the Integral Security
Strategy and permanent surveillance work. [@1] The force said the detentions
were for probable involvement in offences including drug possession, family
violence, robbery, carrying a prohibited weapon, sexual abuse and offences
against traffic safety. [@1]

During the same operations, officers seized 566 grams of marijuana. [@1] The
same haul included 80 doses of the drug known as “cristal”, which is excluded
from the cannabis figures here. [@1] Officers also secured eight vehicles,
including one with a theft report. [@1]

The state Secretariat of Security and Citizen Protection said it would maintain
operational deployment and surveillance in the four regions through a strategy
combining prevention, intelligence work and response capacity to address the
population's security needs. [@1]
