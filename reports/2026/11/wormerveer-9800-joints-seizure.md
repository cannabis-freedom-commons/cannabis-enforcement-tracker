Police officers in Wormerveer arrested one suspect after discovering a total of
almost ten thousand joints during searches of a house in the town and of a car
they also examined last Monday. [@1] The suspect was taken into custody at the
time but has since been released, and police say the investigation into the case
is still ongoing. [@1]

The operation began with a report to Meld Misdaad Anoniem, the Dutch anonymous
crime-reporting line, in which the caller suspected that one of the residents of
the Wormerveer house was dealing drugs. [@1] Officers who went to the house to
take a look arrived just as the suspect drove away in a car, so they followed
the vehicle and stopped it at a motorway exit. [@1]

A search of the stopped car uncovered almost 8,800 ready-rolled joints. [@1,2]
Officers then examined the house and found about another 1,000 joints there,
accounting for the reported total of almost ten thousand. [@1] Police seized all
of the softdrugs, the ready-rolled joints recovered in the car and the house.
[@1]

The boxes were seen being delivered to, stored at and collected from a residential care complex for older people and people with care needs. [@3] The complex was not publicly identified and it was not made public for whom the joints were intended. [@3] Cannabis news site CNNBS said the seized joints came from the stash of a coffeeshop and criticised the action. [@4]
