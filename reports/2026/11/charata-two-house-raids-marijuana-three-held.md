Officers of the Charata Drug Operations Division (División Operaciones Drogas de
Charata) ran investigative work to dismantle retail drug-selling and consumption
points in the town. [@1] After patrols and surveillance posts detected the
activity in two homes, the officers asked the prosecutor on duty for search
warrants. [@1] The searches were carried out on the night of 6 November, with
the court orders in hand and a witness present, starting at a house on Urquiza
street in Charata. [@1]

Inside one part of that house officers found the bunker where drugs were being
prepared for sale. [@1] There they seized six packages of cocaine weighing 36
grams in total, packages of marijuana, polyethylene cuttings used for wrapping,
a precision scale and 24,000 pesos in cash. [@1] Three men aged 36, 40 and 41
were notified of their arrest (aprehensión) at that house and placed at the
disposal of the Sáenz Peña anti-drug prosecutor's office. [@1] The assistant
prosecutor considered that everything found at the first house was linked to
drug dealing. [@1]

The second home searched, nearby on Blas Parera street, held 17 plastic packages
called bochitas with 15 grams of cocaine in total and 18,000 pesos in cash, all
seized. [@1] A 65-year-old woman was notified of the judicial proceedings at the
second home; the article names no arrest there. [@1]
