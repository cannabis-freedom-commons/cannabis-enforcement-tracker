Macomb police arrested two men over an alleged attempt to steal plants that were
being used in Spoon River College's cannabis processing program. [@1] The two
arrested men were identified by police as 38-year-old Sean Hayes and 36-year-old
Jacob Allen. [@1] Both Hayes and Allen were described by police as being from
Macomb. [@1] Each of the two arrested men faces multiple charges over the
alleged incident. [@1] The listed charges include alleged unauthorized control
over property worth more than $500. [@1] The listed charges also include alleged
criminal trespass to state-supported land. [@1]

Police say the two men uprooted THCa plants during the alleged incident. [@1]
Police put the estimated value of those uprooted THCa plants at $23,000. [@1]
Those same plants had been growing outside the college's campus. [@1] That
outdoor growing site outside the college campus in Macomb was identified as 2500
E. Jackson St. [@1] The targeted plants were plants used in Spoon River
College's cannabis processing program. [@1]
