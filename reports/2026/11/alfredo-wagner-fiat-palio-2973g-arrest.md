Military Police arrested the driver of a Fiat Palio on suspicion of drug
trafficking in the early hours of Tuesday 10 November 2026 in Alfredo Wagner, in
Santa Catarina's Serra Catarinense. [@1] The operation began after the Agência
de Inteligência, the Military Police's intelligence wing, reported that a Fiat
Palio was carrying drugs, and officers located and stopped the vehicle. [@1]
Rádio Sintonia FM, crediting the Military Police, gave the time of the approach
as about 3h28. [@2]

During the search officers found 2.973 kg of a substance resembling marijuana
hidden under a towel on the passenger seat. [@1] Rádio Sintonia FM described the
same find as about two kilograms and 970 grams under a towel on the passenger
seat. [@2] Police said the driver showed altered behaviour during the approach
and had to be restrained so the safety procedures could be carried out. [@1]

The driver received voz de prisão, an arrest in flagrante, on suspicion of drug
trafficking. [@1] The car was removed to an accredited impound lot, and the
driver and the seized drug were taken to the Civil Police station in Lages for
the legal procedures. [@1]
