The National Prosecutor's Office and the National Police carried out a dawn
operation in La Troncal, Canar province, seizing more than a kilogram of
marijuana and 30 grams of cocaine on the morning of 13 November 2026. [@1] The
seizure took place during a preliminary investigation into alleged organised
criminal activity dedicated to drug trafficking. [@1] Elements of the National
Police took part in the operation. [@1] A total of eight searches and seven
arrest warrants were executed in La Troncal. [@1]

Among the detainees are two women and three men. [@2] Once extended beyond La
Troncal, 10 arrest warrants and 11 searches had been executed in total. [@2] The
Prosecutor's Office said it would file charges within the coming hours. [@2]
