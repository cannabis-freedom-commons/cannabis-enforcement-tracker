A man was arrested in flagrante for drug trafficking on the night of Thursday 12
November in the Morro do Mocotó, in the centre of Florianópolis, by officers of
the Bope special operations battalion of the Military Police, during Operação
Lei e Ordem. [@1]

According to the Military Police, the teams spotted the suspect and approached
him during a tactical incursion and monitoring of strategic points in the
community and found with him several portions of drugs ready for sale. [@1]

Officers seized 395 grams of hashish and 56 grams of marijuana, as well as 90
grams of crack and 3 grams of cocaine. [@1]

The man was taken with all the seized material to the Capital's Central de
Plantão Policial, where the case was recorded. [@1]
