On the night of 11 November 2026, Federal Highway Police (PRF) officers stopped
a car during a routine check on the BR-116 in Teresópolis, in the Região Serrana
of Rio de Janeiro state. [@1] When the driver saw the officers approaching, he
sped off and a pursuit began; while the chase continued, the car's occupants
threw a white package out the window, part of which fell into a drain and the
rest scattered across the road. [@1]

Officers then intercepted the car; the second occupant got out and fled on foot
into a wooded area and was not located, while the driver was detained at the
scene. [@1] The police seized five packages and 318 small wrappers of a
substance known as PAK, a type of hashish, along with one kilogram of cocaine
paste. [@2] The incident was referred to the 110th police station (110ª DP) in
Teresópolis, where the case was recorded. [@1]
