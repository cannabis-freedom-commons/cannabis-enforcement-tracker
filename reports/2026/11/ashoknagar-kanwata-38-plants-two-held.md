Chanderi police seized 38 cannabis plants from two houses in Kanwata village in
Ashoknagar district. [@2] Police arrested Vikesh Yadav, 20, and Sachin alias
Bablu Yadav, 25. [@2] The plants had been grown in a house courtyard and in a
khera, an open plot behind a house. [@1] A case was registered against both men
under the NDPS Act, India's main drug law. [@2]

Police said the uprooted plants together weighed 54 kg 860 g. [@2] Police put
their estimated total value at Rs 24,000. [@2] Vikesh Yadav had grown 13 small
and large plants among other plants in his house courtyard. [@2] Officers
uprooted those plants and weighed them. [@2] That lot weighed 18 kg 800 g and
was valued at about Rs 8,000. [@2] Sachin Yadav had 25 small and large plants in
the khera behind his house. [@2] That lot weighed 36 kg 60 g and was valued at
about Rs 16,000. [@2]

The district police chief said action against illegal cultivation, storage,
transport and sale of narcotics would continue across the district. [@2] He
appealed to residents to pass information about such activity to police. [@2] He
said the identities of people who give information would be kept secret. [@2]
