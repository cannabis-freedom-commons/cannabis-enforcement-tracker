Dehri police searched a joint field at Chongdiapura in Tol village after an
informant said Tol residents Suku alias Suka and Subhan Chaungad had planted
cannabis among cotton. [@2] The field was on government land and in the
possession of the two accused. [@2] The two accused farmers fled during the
action and remained absconding. [@2] Police registered a case under the NDPS
Act, said the absconding accused were being sought, and said they would be
questioned about cannabis supply after arrest. [@2]

Officers seized 1,592 green cannabis plants growing among the cotton plants.
[@2] The seized cannabis weighed 406.23 kg in all. [@1] Police put the estimated
value of the seizure at 60.90 lakh rupees. [@1]

The action was carried out as part of Operation Prahar, the police campaign
against narcotics trafficking and illegal cultivation. [@2] The team was led by
Bag station house officer Hirusinh Rawat with Dehri outpost in-charge Navalsinh
Baghel and the Bag police team. [@2]
