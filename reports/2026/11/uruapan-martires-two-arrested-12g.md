Armed men fired on Mexican Army personnel and later on state police officers in
Uruapan, Michoacán, and two of those implicated were detained in a joint
federal-state operation. [@1] Michoacán's public security ministry said the two
men were detained through coordinated action by Guardia Civil agents and Defence
Ministry personnel over a gun attack on soldiers in the early hours of Monday in
Uruapan. [@1] The attack happened on Calzada Benito Juárez in the Mártires de
Uruapan neighbourhood, where soldiers on surveillance patrols were shot at from
inside a house and called for support. [@1] Officers of the Guardia Civil's
Special Operations Group (GOE) and Motorized Immediate Reaction Group (GRIM)
went to the address, where more shots were fired from the house. [@1]

Officers entered the building and detained the two men, who resisted during the
police action. [@1] At the scene the authorities seized a short 9 mm firearm, 22
live cartridges, one fired cartridge case, about 12 grams of green herb with
marijuana characteristics, and two mobile phones. [@1] A second account lists
the same seizure, including approximately 12 grams of green herb with marijuana
characteristics. [@2]

The two detainees and all seized items were handed over to the competent
authorities for the investigation into the attack on military personnel. [@1]
The published official information covers only the detentions and the items
seized, with no additional charges detailed against the two men. [@2]
