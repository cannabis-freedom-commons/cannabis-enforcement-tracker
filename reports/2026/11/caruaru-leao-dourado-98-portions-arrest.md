Teams of the 1º Batalhão Integrado Especializado (1º Biesp), a specialised
policing battalion of Pernambuco's military police, arrested a young man
suspected of drug trafficking in Caruaru in the early evening of Monday, 9
November. [@1] Police recorded the incident on Avenida Leão Dourado, near the
entrance to Vila Kennedy. [@1] The suspect is 19 years old and lives in the
Xique-Xique neighbourhood. [@1]

According to the police, officers caught him at the moment he was preparing the
drug for sale. [@1] Officers seized 98 small marijuana portions, known locally
as big bigs, along with 105 grams of pressed marijuana and a precision scale.
[@1]

After the arrest the young man was taken to the Caruaru on-duty police station
for the legal procedures, where he was expected to be charged in flagrante with
drug trafficking. [@1]
