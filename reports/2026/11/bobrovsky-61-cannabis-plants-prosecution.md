A 58-year-old local man in Bobrovsky District, Voronezh Oblast, came under
investigation after law-enforcement officers found 61 cannabis bushes in the
yard of his house. [@1] He is accused of unlawfully cultivating narcotic plants
on a large scale. [@1] According to investigators, he bought cannabis seeds in
May 2026 and prepared a plot in the yard of his house for growing them. [@1] In
the end he grew 61 cannabis bushes, which investigators say he planned to turn
into marijuana for his own use. [@1]

Officers uncovered the plantation during a search and seized all the plants.
[@1] The Bobrovsky District prosecutor's office approved the indictment in the
case. [@1] The criminal case was sent to the district court, which has yet to
hear it on the merits. [@1]
