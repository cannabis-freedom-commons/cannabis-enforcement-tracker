Police in Derry seized suspected Class B controlled drugs from an empty property
in the city centre area. [@1] Officers were carrying out enquiries and checking
an address in connection with an unrelated matter on the morning of 11 November
2026. [@2] They discovered a number of suspected cannabis plants and associated
paraphernalia in the empty property. [@1] Highland Radio reported that the
discovery was made by police carrying out enquiries at the address on the
morning of 11 November 2026 in connection with an unrelated matter. [@3] The
Derry Journal reported that the suspected Class B drugs were discovered during a
call to an address in the city centre area. [@2]

City Centre Neighbourhood Policing Team Inspector Paul McDonald said enquiries
were ongoing and appealed for information about drug supply or dealing. [@1]
Inspector Paul McDonald said police remained focused on disrupting drug-related
crime and keeping communities safe. [@3] Inspector Paul McDonald appealed for
information about drug supply or dealing, asking people to call police on 101.
[@1] Police said information could also be given anonymously to Crimestoppers on
0800 555 111. [@1]
