Chopda Rural police raided a cultivation site at Khanayapadaw shivar in Chopda
taluka on 11 November 2026 after learning that Jumma R C Pavra had grown
ganja-like narcotic plants on Forest Department compartment No. 240 land. [@1]
The field held green plants four to six feet tall with leaves, branches and
roots. [@1] Suspect Jumma R C Pavra fled when the police party reached the
field, so no arrest had been made in the case. [@1] Police filed a case at
Chopda Rural police station, where Inspector Sanjay Sonawane is continuing the
investigation, including whether a wider network was behind the cultivation.
[@1]

Officers placed the whole stock in a Swaraj tractor-trolley and weighed it on
the Agricultural Produce Market Committee scale at 6,260 kg gross and 4,390 kg
for the empty tractor. [@1] The net weight of the seized plants was therefore
fixed at 1,870 kg. [@1] Police valued the stock at 46,750,000 rupees at 25,000
rupees per kg. [@1]

The cultivation was on Forest Department land rather than a private farm. [@1]
Samples of the plants with green leaves, branches and seeds, along with soil
from the field, were kept for chemical testing. [@1] The exact nature of the
seized plants will be clear only after the laboratory report. [@1]
