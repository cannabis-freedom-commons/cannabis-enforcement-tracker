Military Police arrested a man for drug trafficking on the afternoon of Friday 6
November 2026 in Lucas do Rio Verde, 332 km from Cuiabá. [@1] The arrest
happened at about 17h during an ostensive patrol on Avenida Ênio Rospierski,
near the Mercado Primavera 3. [@1] Officers said they saw the man acting
suspiciously and that he tried to flee when he noticed them approaching. [@1]

During the approach, officers found one portion of maconha in the suspect's
pocket and another where he had thrown the package. [@1] Questioned, he
confessed that he bought the drugs to sell and said there were more drugs at his
home. [@1] He said he had bought about R$1,000 in drugs to resell and obtained
them from members of the Comando Vermelho criminal faction. [@1] With the
resident's authorization, officers went to the house, where the suspect's wife
handed over another eight portions of maconha and a precision scale. [@1] In
total, officers seized 10 portions of the drug, a precision scale, a damaged
light-blue Xiaomi cell phone and an identity document. [@1]

The man was arrested and taken with the seized material to the Civil Police
station. [@1] The police record stated that he had no bodily injuries. [@1]
