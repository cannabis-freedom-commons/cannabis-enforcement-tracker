West Sharira police arrested one accused in Kaushambi on Friday afternoon at
around 1 pm under Operation Savera. [@1] Police identified the arrested man as
Virendra Singh alias Dadu, son of Shivprasad and a resident of Barainsa under
Paschim Sarira police station. [@2]

Officers recovered 7 kg of illegal ganja and one electronic scale from his
possession. [@1] Virendra Singh alias Dadu told police that all the recovered
packets were ganja and that he weighed it on the same scale to sell to people.
[@2] He said he brought the ganja from Sirathu and Manjhanpur but did not name
the people who supplied it. [@2]

Police registered a case at Paschim Sarira police station on the basis of the
arrest and seizure. [@2] The accused already had two earlier cases registered
against him. [@1]

The arrest was part of the district-wide Operation Savera anti-narcotics drive
conducted on instructions from the police headquarters in Lucknow. [@2]
