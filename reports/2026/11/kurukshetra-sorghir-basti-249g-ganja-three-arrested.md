Kurukshetra police said they unravelled a network that stored, ordered in and
supplied ganja in Sorghir Basti and arrested three people, including a woman.
[@1] Police seized 249 grams of ganja from the woman, named in the report as
Saroj, a resident of Indra Colony in Sorghir Basti. [@1] The other two arrested
were identified as Tosan and Yashpal of Darshan Sarai in Bihar's Samastipur
district. [@1]

Police spokesperson Naresh said a team from the Subhash Mandi police post was
checking vehicles at a barricade near Gol Bank Chowk on 4 November when the
woman turned back on seeing the police. [@1] Officers caught her with the help
of the head constable and found 249 grams of ganja on her. [@1] The
investigation found that Tosan had ordered the ganja and Yashpal supplied it,
and police arrested Tosan and then Yashpal on 5 November. [@1] Tosan was living
in shanties near the government hospital, the report said. [@1]
