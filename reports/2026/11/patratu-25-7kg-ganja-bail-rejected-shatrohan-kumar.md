The Jharkhand High Court rejected the bail plea of accused Shatrohan Kumar alias
Shatrodhan Kumar in a ganja-smuggling case. [@1] The court ordered the state
government to produce the remaining witnesses on 20 November 2026 or on an
earlier short date fixed by the lower court. [@1] It said the petitioner may
seek bail again in February 2027 if the witnesses are still not produced. [@1]

The 26-year-old Shatrohan Kumar lived in Patratu in Ramgarh district. [@1] He
has been in judicial custody since 23 May 2025. [@1] The case was registered as
Patratu police station case number 131/2025 under sections 20(b)(ii)(C), 25 and
29 of the NDPS Act. [@1] It is pending before the Additional Sessions Judge-I in
Ramgarh. [@1]

Special public prosecutor Vishwanath Rai opposed bail and said two people had
been arrested together. [@1] He said 11.801 kg of ganja was recovered from one
accused and 13.908 kg from the other, a total of 25.709 kg. [@1] The chargesheet
names seven witnesses. [@1] Witnesses were produced on 25 February and 6 June
2026 but could not be examined for want of time, with the next date fixed for 20
November 2026. [@1] The petitioner's counsel said bail had already been rejected
on 10 September 2025 and 12 March 2026, and only three witnesses have been
examined so far. [@1] The court said the plea had twice been rejected on merits,
witnesses had been brought but not examined, and the case involved ganja
recovery, so bail could not be granted. [@1]
