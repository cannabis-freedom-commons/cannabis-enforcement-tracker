On 6 November 2026, Malaysian police announced the arrests of a 17-year-old
schoolmate and an 18-year-old who was no longer in school on suspicion of
supplying a drug-laced vape to a secondary-school student in Nilai, Negeri
Sembilan. [@1] The two suspects were alleged to have supplied the vape device to
the student victim. [@2] The student at the centre of the case, a 17-year-old
boy, had preliminarily tested positive for ketamine, benzodiazepines and THC
after a video showed him apparently in a dazed state. [@4]

Police believed the video of the boy hunched over in a classroom was filmed on
28 October 2026 at a secondary school in Nilai. [@1] Police analysis of the vape
liquid used by the student detected ketamine, benzodiazepines and cannabinoids,
the generic term for components found in cannabis. [@1] The Star's police
account likewise lists ketamine, benzodiazepines and cannabinoids among the
substances indicated in the vape liquid. [@2]

The student had bought the vape from a friend, who had bought it through social
media with delivery to the home. [@1] Police said they were tracking another
individual believed to have supplied the vape to the two suspects. [@3]
Preliminary urine tests on both arrested teenagers also returned positive
results for drugs. [@3] Police referred all three teenagers to the National
Anti-Drugs Agency for further action under the Drug Dependants (Treatment and
Rehabilitation) Act 1983. [@3]
