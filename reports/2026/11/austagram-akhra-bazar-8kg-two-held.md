Austagram police detained one man and one woman with 8 kg of ganja in Austagram,
Kishoreganj. [@1] The two were identified as 23-year-old Md. Ujjwal Mia of
Gonkipar village and 30-year-old Razia Khatun of Ikortoli village, both of
Gazipur Union in Chunarughat, Habiganj. [@1] Officers caught the pair as they
tried to run away on seeing the police. [@1]

Police searched the detainees in front of witnesses and recovered a total of 8
kg of ganja from them. [@1] The raid happened at about 4:30 pm on Sunday, 1
November, on the paved road in front of the Jalam BADC pump between Purba
Austagram and Ikordia Ghat, near Akhra Bazar. [@1] Police said they had received
a tip at about 4:20 pm that drugs were being bought and sold in the Akhra Bazar
area of Purba Austagram Union. [@1]

The police team was led by Austagram police station officer-in-charge Md.
Rokonuzzaman. [@1] Police described the action as a special operation launched
on secret information about drug buying and selling. [@1]
