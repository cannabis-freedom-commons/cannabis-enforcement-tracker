Rawalpindi police arrested seven alleged drug suppliers, including a woman, in
separate operations across the district. [@1] Police said the arrests were made
on the directions of City Police Officer Hassan Mushtaq Sukhera as part of
efforts to eliminate drugs. [@1]

Ratta Amral police arrested a woman drug supplier and recovered 1.3 kg of
hashish from her possession. [@1] Pirwadhai police arrested two alleged
suppliers and recovered 3.6 kg of hashish. [@1] Kallar Syedan police recovered
2.4 kg of hashish from another suspect. [@1]

Waris Khan police arrested a supplier and seized 1.9 kg of hashish, while R.A.
Bazaar police recovered 1.8 kg from another suspect. [@1] Gujar Khan police
arrested an alleged supplier and recovered 1.64 kg of hashish. [@1] The
station-level recoveries add up to 12.54 kg of hashish, which police described
as more than 12.5 kg. [@1]

The CPO said all possible measures were being taken to eliminate drugs from
society. [@1] The Nation, the English daily of the Nawaiwaqt group, carried the
same APP account of the seven arrests a day later, with identical station
totals. [@2]
