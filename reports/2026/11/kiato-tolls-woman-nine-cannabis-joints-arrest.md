Officers of the Sikyona police station's motorcycle patrol unit, known by its
Greek initials DIAS, arrested a 28-year-old Greek woman at the Kiato toll
station on the New Athens–Patras national road on the afternoon of 5 November
2026. [@1] The arrest was made during a police check on the New Athens–Patras
national road near Corinthia, with the motorcycle patrol from the Sikyona police
station carrying it out. [@2]

A search of the woman found a small quantity of cannabis and nine cannabis
joints, which police seized. [@1] The nine joints were hand-rolled, according to
the local news report, and the cannabis and the joints were confiscated. [@2] No
weight was published for the loose cannabis or for the cannabis inside the
joints. [@2]

The Sikyona police station is conducting the preliminary investigation into the
possession case. [@1]
