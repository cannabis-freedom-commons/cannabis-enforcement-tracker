The Federal Highway Police and the Receita Federal, Brazil's federal customs and
tax agency, seized 2,337 kg of marijuana on Thursday 5 November 2026 in Guaíra,
western Paraná. [@2] Officers stopped a truck on BR-163 and the driver told them
he was carrying cassava starch. [@2] A search of the load found blocks of
marijuana hidden among the starch. [@3] Asked about the trip, the driver told
officers, according to the police, that he had taken on the drug in Mundo Novo,
Mato Grosso do Sul, and was to deliver it in Nova Santa Rita, Rio Grande do Sul.
[@1]

The driver was arrested and, together with the drug, the truck and the starch
load, taken to the Federal Police station in Guaíra for the legal procedures.
[@3] The inspection took place in front of the Federal Highway Police post at
the Ponte Ayrton Senna, the bridge between Guaíra and the Paraguayan border,
where the driver gave contradictory information about his load. [@3] The Federal
Highway Police, known in Brazil as the PRF, published the seizure the same day
with a total of 2.337 quilos. [@2]
