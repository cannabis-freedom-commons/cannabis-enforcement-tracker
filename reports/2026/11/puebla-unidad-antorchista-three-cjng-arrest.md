On 2 November 2026, officers of Puebla's public-security secretariat with the
Navy and the state prosecutor's office detained three men in the Unidad
Antorchista neighbourhood of the city of Puebla. [@1] The detained men were
identified as Carlos N., aged 30, Ivan N., known as El Tartas, aged 28, and
Francisco N., aged 27. [@1] Investigators alleged that the three coordinated
drug distribution, located properties to use as meeting points and took part in
violent acts. [@1] Two of the detained men have criminal records for health,
firearms and property offences. [@1]

Officers seized more than 280 packets containing substances resembling
methamphetamine, cocaine, heroin and marijuana. [@1] Officers seized more than
280 packets containing substances resembling methamphetamine, cocaine, heroin
and marijuana. [@2] They also seized a firearm, two magazines, 39 usable
cartridges and a vehicle with Guerrero plates. [@1] They also seized a firearm,
two magazines, 39 usable cartridges and a vehicle with Guerrero plates. [@2]

Authorities described the three as alleged operational members of a CJNG-linked
criminal cell working in the state capital. [@2] The three men were placed at
the disposal of the competent authorities for investigation of their alleged
responsibility. [@1]
