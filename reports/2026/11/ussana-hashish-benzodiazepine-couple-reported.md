Carabinieri from the Donori station stopped a car on the outskirts of Ussana
carrying two unemployed 20-year-olds from the area, both already known to
police. [@1] The officers found a small amount of hashish and a packet of
benzodiazepine medicines that the pair had no medical prescription for, and
seized both. [@1]

The two young men were reported to the administrative authority rather than
arrested. [@2] The driver's driving licence was withdrawn after the
Carabinieri's road-traffic checks. [@1] The check took place on Sunday 8
November 2026, during a day of checks by the Carabinieri provincial command of
Cagliari in several towns around the city. [@3]
