During the Moinho Seguro operation on Avenida Jurumirim in Cuiabá, a man and a
woman were arrested on suspicion of drug trafficking on Thursday 12 November.
[@1] The woman, who was driving a white Honda Biz motorcycle, was found with 25
portions of a substance similar to marijuana and five portions of cocaine paste
base. [@1]

She had no driver's licence or driving permit. [@1] She said the material was
hers. [@2]

The couple was taken to the Verdão Central de Flagrantes and placed at the
disposal of the judiciary. [@3]
