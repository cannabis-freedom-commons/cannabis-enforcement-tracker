Khaknar police seized 50 cannabis plants from a field in Usarni village,
Burhanpur district, Madhya Pradesh, where they had been grown among a cotton
crop. [@1] Police said the seized plants weighed 107.200 kg and put their value
at about 200,000 rupees; ganja is dried cannabis. [@1]

The report says the accused had fled, and it gives no arrest. [@1] The seizure
was the third cannabis cultivation case in Burhanpur district in four days. [@1]
