A 30-year-old man was arrested on the afternoon of 10 November 2026 in Sao
Carlos do Ivai after being caught with portions of marijuana and crack near a
school. [@1] The Military Police team was called at about 15h53 after a report
that a man wearing an electronic ankle bracelet was near a school in the central
area of the city. [@1]

As the patrol car approached, the man dropped two bags on the ground and tried
to leave the area on a bicycle before officers stopped him. [@1] Officers
checking the discarded material found 104 grams of marijuana and 24 grams of
crack. [@1] According to the Military Police, the substances were portioned, a
characteristic associated with the sale of narcotics. [@1]

The man was arrested and taken with the seized drugs to the Civil Police station
in Paraiso do Norte, where further procedures were carried out. [@1] The
Military Police did not disclose the identity of the suspect. [@1]
