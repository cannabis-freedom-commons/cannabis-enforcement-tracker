Police arrested nine people in Maloney between Tuesday and Thursday during a
special-operations crackdown that followed a shooting in the community. [@1]
Four of those arrested were held in connection with a shooting on Monday after
five searches across 329 apartments in ten buildings, during which a pistol was
also seized. [@1] Five more people were arrested on Thursday night on
outstanding warrants, when police with Canine Unit support seized packets of
marijuana. [@1]

The Trinidad and Tobago Police Service said officers executed a series of
intelligence-led initiatives aimed at enhancing safety while disrupting criminal
activity. [@1] Police issued 181 curfew permits to residents and authorised
people seeking access to the closed area. [@1] The release said the closed area
remained secured with officers monitoring all entry and exit points while
soldiers and other units conducted reassurance patrols across the operational
sectors. [@1]
