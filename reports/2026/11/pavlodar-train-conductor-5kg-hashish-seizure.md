Officers of Kazakhstan Interior Ministry's Transport Police Department detained
a 39-year-old conductor. [@1] The conductor worked on the Turkestan–Pavlodar
passenger train. [@1] Officers searching his flat seized a large batch of
hashish. [@1]

The seized batch weighed more than 5 kilograms in total. [@1] The department's
press service said preliminary data put the black-market worth of the seized
drugs at more than 18 million tenge. [@1] Officers later traced the alleged
supplier, a 33-year-old native of Zhambyl region. [@1]

The supplier allegedly handed the prohibited substances to the conductor for a
monetary reward. [@1] A criminal case was opened under Article 297 of
Kazakhstan's criminal code, covering illicit manufacture, processing,
acquisition, storage, transport for sale, transfer or sale of narcotics. [@1]
Investigators are establishing all the details of the crime and checking whether
other people are involved in the drug trafficking. [@1]
