Officers of the first police station in Słupsk and district officers from the
Potęgowo police post detained two men and a 32-year-old woman in three separate
interventions, and police said the three would answer for possessing drugs. [@2]
Detectives from the first police station in Słupsk searched the flat of a
47-year-old resident of the city, found amphetamine and detained him. [@2]
Almost at the same time, detectives searched the flat of a 32-year-old woman who
had hidden more than 30 grams of marijuana in a plastic bag. [@2] The three
interventions were carried out by detectives of Słupsk's first police station
and by district officers of the Potęgowo police post. [@2]

The third detention took place in Potęgowo shortly before noon, after district
officers were told that a 31-year-old resident of Słupsk county was harassing
his former partner despite a prosecutor's no-contact order. [@2] The 31-year-old
became visibly nervous at the sight of the officers because he was hiding drugs
in a pouch he carried, officers said. [@1] Police seized several dozen grams of
drugs divided into portions from him: amphetamine, marijuana and a substance
officers called crystal. [@2] A search also turned up three knives and an
electronic scale. [@2] Possessing drugs in a quantity deemed significant is
punishable by a prison term, the police said. [@3] The police accounts name no
charges or court steps; officers said only that the three would answer for
possessing drugs. [@2]
