Police arrested two kiosk operators in the Maranchi police-station area of
Hathidah, Patna, during Sunday-night raids on two gumtis, small roadside kiosks.
[@2] Police identified the arrested men as Rudal Mahto of Rajesh Nagar and Pawan
Singh of Sonua Tola, both residents under Maranchi police station in Patna
district. [@2] Police said legal proceedings are under way against both men.
[@2]

Police seized a total of 330 grams of ganja-like substance in the two raids.
[@1] The first raid at a kiosk in Rajesh Nagar village recovered about 123 grams
of ganja-like substance. [@2] The second raid at a kiosk at Tower Chowk in
Maranchi village recovered about 207 grams of ganja-like substance along with
28.47 grams of opium-like substance. [@2] Both seized materials were described
as resembling ganja and opium rather than laboratory-confirmed drugs. [@2]

Maranchi station house officer Raushan Kumar said the raids followed tip-offs
that narcotics were being sold from behind the kiosks. [@2] Police said teams
raided the two locations separately based on the information received for each
site. [@2]
