A 37-year-old cook from Salzburg appeared before the Salzburg Regional Court on
Thursday to answer drug charges. [@1] The court convicted him of drug
trafficking. [@1]

Investigators said he had been dismissed by his employer in autumn 2025. [@1]
Investigators said financial problems after the dismissal led him to seek new
income by buying and selling drugs. [@1] He tried to have a parcel containing
6.3 kilograms of drugs sent to himself from the United States. [@1]

The drug was cannabis that he had probably ordered on the darknet. [@1] A police
investigative unit in the United States seized the parcel while it was still
there, so it never reached Austria. [@1]
