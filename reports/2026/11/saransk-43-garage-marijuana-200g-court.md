Police in Saransk, the capital of Mordovia, finished investigating a 43-year-old
local man accused of unlawfully storing drugs. [@1] The regional interior
ministry said officers found three parcels of marijuana in the man's garage in
late August. [@1] Nearby they also found 10 cannabis bushes that belonged to
him. [@1]

The man told police he had come across wild cannabis growing some time earlier,
pulled up part of it and kept it. [@1] The total mass of what was seized was 200
grams. [@1]

The criminal case has now been sent to court. [@1]
