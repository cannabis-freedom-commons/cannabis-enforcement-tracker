Five defendants charged over the seizure of more than 230kg of drugs in Nicosia
in February pleaded not guilty to all 30 charges before the Nicosia criminal
court on 6 November 2026. [@1] The trial was adjourned to 17 December, amid the
prospect that four more suspects will be added to the indictment. [@1] All five
have been held in custody at the central prisons since their arrest and remain
there. [@1]

The adjournment followed a request from defence lawyer Dimitris Tsolakidis, who
told the court the case may be connected to the arrests made on 5 November under
Operation Plegma, when four people were remanded in custody. [@1] He argued that
as the allegations relate to the same events, the four could be charged and
joined to the existing indictment, which would bring the number of defendants to
nine. [@1] The lawyer for the fourth defendant alone asked that his client be
required to respond to the charges, and he then entered a denial. [@1]

The case dates from 19 February 2026, when officers stopped a vehicle in Nicosia
and found 11,480 firecrackers together with cannabis and cocaine. [@1] Searches
connected to the driver then uncovered 167kg of cannabis, 62kg of cocaine and
4.5kg of cannabis resin, and police also seized firearms, explosive devices and
cash. [@1] Christos Andreou, head of the organised crime directorate, said
assets worth €750,000 have been frozen, comprising property valued at about
€400,000 and €350,000 in cash and bank accounts. [@1] Police said on 6 November
that the network behind the operation had been largely dismantled, and Andreou
said that could not be confirmed until inquiries were complete. [@1]

The 5 November operation involved 27 searches across the island and the arrest
of 26 people, with officers seizing more than €25,000 in cash, 37 mobile phones,
two firearms and ammunition. [@1] Twenty-two of those arrested were released
after questioning, while four were remanded for five days, and police believe
all 26 are associates of the original five. [@1]

The prosecution read out the 30 charges one by one, and the defendants pleaded
not guilty to each. [@2] Most of the 30 counts on the charge sheet concern all
of the defendants. [@2] The Assize Court in Nicosia set the case for hearing on
17 December. [@2] Cyprus Inform reported the same denials of all 30 charges, the
same adjournment and the same February quantities. [@3]
