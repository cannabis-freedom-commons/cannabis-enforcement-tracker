Officers of the provincial PDI's Microtráfico division carried out two raids on
houses on Avenida Los Plátanos, at numbers 30 and 800, in Remanso Valerio,
Granadero Baigorria. [@2] The raids targeted two homes on Avenida Los Plátanos,
at numbers 30 and 800. [@2] Three people were transferred to police stations as
a result of the two procedures. [@2] In one house officers identified a
21-year-old man and a 17-year-old adolescent. [@2] Prosecutor Diego Giro, of the
specialized microtrafficking unit in Rosario, attributed sales of cocaine and
marijuana from the house at Los Plátanos 800 to the 21-year-old. [@1] Judge
Chiabrera formalized the charge and ordered 90 days of effective preventive
prison, automatically extended if the prosecution files the accusation. [@1]

Officers seized 107.3 grams of plant material that tested positive for cannabis
and 0.5 grams of cocaine. [@2] They also seized six cell phones, cannabis seeds,
scissors, sale notes, elastic bands and four rounds of ammunition of different
calibres. [@2] Police seized 558,430 pesos in cash across the two searches. [@2]
Investigators allege the selling activity ran from 6 October until his detention
on 6 November 2026. [@1] The case was built on field surveillance and video
recordings documenting hand-to-hand exchanges near the houses and in public
areas. [@2]

In the second house officers found a 52-year-old man with an active capture
order sought by the sexual-integrity crimes prosecution unit. [@2] The
adolescent's situation was referred to the juvenile criminal responsibility
prosecution office, which also ordered her transfer. [@2] The investigation
remains open under the relevant prosecution office. [@2]
