Police in Station Ghanpur, Jangaon district, Telangana, arrested four men
accused of selling dried ganja, including a man accused of buying the drug. [@1]
Three of the men were accused of selling the ganja and the fourth of buying it.
[@1]

The arrested included Jatothu Rajesh alias Nithin, 19, from Durga Tanda in
Zaffergadh mandal, and Jadi Srikant, 25, from Station Ghanpur. [@1] The other
two were Munigala Rajesh, 19, and Kalakola Dileep, 32, of Pamnur village. [@1]
Officers seized 40 grams of dried ganja, one mobile phone and 1,000 rupees in
cash from them. [@1]

Circle Inspector S. Srinivas said a case was registered against the four and
they were sent to judicial remand. [@1]
