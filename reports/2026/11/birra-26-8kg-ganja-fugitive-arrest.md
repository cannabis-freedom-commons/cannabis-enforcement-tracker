Inquiry into the case implicated Odisha resident Narayan Rana, 27, son of
Lakshman Rana, in supplying ganja, the local term for cannabis. [@1] Birra
police arrested Rana at his residence after continuous tracing. [@1] The
arrested accused was sent to judicial remand on Wednesday. [@1] The case began
on 1 August 2026, when police received a tip that ganja was being moved toward
Champa by car and motorcycle. [@1] A Birra team led by station chief Rajiv
Srivastava then cordoned off the area near Basantpur village on the Mahanadi
bank. [@1] Officers detained four accused there and recovered 26.8 kg of ganja
from their possession. [@1]

Police valued the seized ganja at about Rs 13.40 lakh. [@1] Naidunia's account
of the August interception also reports 26.8 kg of ganja in separate packets
worth more than Rs 13 lakh. [@2] Police also seized the car used to transport
the ganja and the motorcycle used to pilot it. [@2] The four first arrested were
Vijay Baghel, 22, Uttam Chandra, 41, Bhuribai Baghel, 55, and Savya Sharma, 28.
[@2] Two of the four arrested in the August interception were women. [@2]

Birra police registered the matter as Crime No. 99/2026 under Sections 20(B) and
29 of the NDPS Act. [@1] District superintendent Vijay Kumar Pandey directed the
continuing drive against narcotics in the district. [@1] Birra station staff
with Karhi assistance-centre staff played a key role in the operation. [@1]
