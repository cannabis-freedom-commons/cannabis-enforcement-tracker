Military Police in Mossoró arrested a 26-year-old man in flagrante on suspicion
of drug trafficking during an approach in the Malvinas neighbourhood. [@1]
Officers on routine patrol noticed a vehicle whose driver tried to drive away
when he saw the police car approaching, and they followed it and carried out the
approach. [@1] A search found a quantity of drugs hidden under the suspect's
sandal. [@1] Inside the vehicle officers seized more than 100 grams of
marijuana, a precision scale, plastic packaging and more than R$400 in cash.
[@1]

Delegate Luiz Antônio said the man claimed the drug was for his own use. [@1]
The scale and packaging led police to suspect the drug was intended for sale.
[@1] He was taken to the Delegacia de Plantão, the police station handling
flagrant arrests, booked for drug trafficking and sent to the prison system at
the disposal of the courts. [@1]

The same delegate said the man had earlier been arrested in March for robbery,
held about three months and released provisionally. [@1]
