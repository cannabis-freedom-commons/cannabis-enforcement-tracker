Iredell County Sheriff Darren Campbell announced the arrest of 35-year-old
Charles Smith Jr. of East Broad Street in Statesville. [@1] Smith was arrested
on Saturday, November 7, 2026, following a traffic stop at Motel 7 on Greenland
Drive in Statesville. [@2] Deputy T.M. Sharpe of the sheriff's Aggressive
Criminal Enforcement Team stopped the 2011 BMW Smith was driving as it pulled
into the motel. [@1]

Sharpe found Smith was driving with a revoked driver's license and a revoked
registration plate because the vehicle lacked insurance. [@1] Smith, who was
free on bond on earlier charges, tried to walk away from the vehicle to avoid
contact with the deputy. [@1] Smith was arrested for the driving violations, and
a search of his person found methamphetamine and drug paraphernalia. [@1] A
later search of the vehicle found additional methamphetamine and marijuana. [@1]

Smith was taken to the Iredell County Detention Center and charged with felony
methamphetamine possession, felony maintaining a vehicle for a controlled
substance, misdemeanor paraphernalia and marijuana possession, driving while
license revoked and displaying a revoked plate. [@1] The sheriff said Smith had
an extensive criminal history and 22 pending charges. [@1]
