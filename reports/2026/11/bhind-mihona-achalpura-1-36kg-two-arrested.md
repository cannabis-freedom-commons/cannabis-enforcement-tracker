Mihona police in Bhind district arrested two alleged marijuana smugglers at two
separate locations in Achalpura village after acting on an informer's tip. [@1]
Mihona station house officer Inspector Rohit Gupta received the tip about
illegal narcotics sales in Achalpura village. [@1] Officers cordoned off the
identified locations, raided them and caught the men red-handed. [@1] Police
registered separate cases against both accused at Mihona police station under
the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, India's drug law. [@1]

The first accused was found with 1 kilogram and 108 grams of marijuana, which
police valued at about Rs 40,000. [@1] The second accused was found with 250
grams of marijuana, which police valued at about Rs 10,000. [@1] In total,
police seized 1 kilogram and 358 grams of marijuana with a combined
police-estimated market value of about Rs 50,000. [@1]

Police said they are investigating to identify other contacts and sources and to
establish the marijuana's origin and intended distribution points. [@1] The
police administration appealed to the public to report such illegal activity
promptly. [@1]
