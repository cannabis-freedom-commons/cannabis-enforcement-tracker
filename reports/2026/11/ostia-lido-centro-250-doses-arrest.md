On 9 November 2026 in Ostia, officers of the X Gruppo Mare of the Local Police
of Rome arrested a 27-year-old Romanian man. [@1] Officers on patrol noticed
suspicious behaviour and watched the man approach a hole in the ground from
which he was taking small packets. [@1] The hole was a hiding place dug in the
ground in the area in front of Lido Centro station in Ostia. [@1]

A search of the hiding place found about 250 doses of hashish and marijuana,
which officers seized. [@1] A second account also reports 250 doses of hashish
and marijuana found in the refuge and seized by the officers. [@2]

The man was arrested and placed at the disposal of the judicial authority for
further steps, and the arrest was later validated by that authority. [@1] The
intervention took place during a patrol in an area that had already been subject
to previous police operations. [@1] The 27-year-old was taken into custody and
placed at the disposal of the magistracy, whose order validating the police
action followed. [@2]
