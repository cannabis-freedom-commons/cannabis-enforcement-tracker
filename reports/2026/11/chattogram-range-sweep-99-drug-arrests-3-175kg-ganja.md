Chattogram Range police detained 1,172 people across 10 districts in a 24-hour
special operation against teen gangs, mugging, extortion, terrorist activity and
drugs. [@1] The operation ran from Tuesday 10 November to the morning of
Wednesday 11 November 2026. [@1] Police counted 65 people arrested in drug cases
and 34 more held as drug traders in regular and other cases. [@1] Mobile courts
jailed 28 people during the anti-drug drive. [@1]

Police collected Tk 12,405 in fines and filed 23 drug cases during the
operation. [@1] Officers seized 3 kg 175 grams of ganja in the operation. [@1]
The same recovery also included 13,711 yaba pills, 10 litres of distilled
liquor, 11 bottles of foreign liquor and 28 bottles of scuff syrup, which are
excluded from the cannabis figures. [@1]

Range DIG Abdul Quddus Chowdhury announced the results of the operation. [@1]
Officers also detained 470 teenagers in the anti-teen-gang part of the drive,
releasing 273 of them to guardians on bond. [@1] Police said the special
operation would continue in the public interest. [@1]
