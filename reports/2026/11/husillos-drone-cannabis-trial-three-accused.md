The criminal section of Palencia's provincial court holds a hearing on 13
November against three accused, two women and a man. [@1] Prosecutors seek a
year and nine months in prison for each of them for drug trafficking in a form
that does not cause serious harm to health. [@1] The prosecution also asks for
an 1,800-euro fine for one accused woman and 3,100 euros each for the other
woman and the man, who were a couple. [@1]

According to the prosecution's indictment, the Guardia Civil, Spain's
armed-status national police force, ran an operation at two homes in the village
of Husillos where consumers of narcotics were coming and going. [@1] The force
flew drones over the properties, saw cannabis pots in the patios, the yards of
the houses, and asked for search warrants for each home. [@1]

After a court gave authorisation, officers found marijuana plants, hashish resin
and dried marijuana at the home where one of the accused women lived. [@1]
Prosecutors put that haul at 400 grams of drugs with a value of 1,750 euros.
[@1] At the second home, where the couple lived, officers seized a precision
scale, marijuana plants, dried marijuana and buds. [@1] That seizure totalled
800 grams of drugs valued at 3,000 euros. [@1]
