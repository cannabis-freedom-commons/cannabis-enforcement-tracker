State police in Lentini arrested a young man accused of possessing drugs with
intent to deal. [@1] The arrest followed a targeted home search assisted by
drug-sniffer dogs. [@3] Officers placed the young man at the disposal of the
judicial authority. [@1]

During the search officers seized 43.9 grams of marijuana divided into eight
packets. [@1] In the same house officers also seized 16.4 grams of ecstasy
already divided into 46 doses and one gram of cocaine. [@2] Officers also seized
material used to package drugs and 5,330 euros in cash that investigators
considered likely proceeds of dealing. [@2] Police also found material used for
weighing and preparing doses in the home. [@4] Police describe ecstasy, known as
the droga dello sballo, as a synthetic psychoactive substance with
hallucinogenic properties known for its short- and long-term danger. [@3]

The operation was carried out by the Siracusa Mobile Squad and the Lentini
police station with support from State Police canine units from Catania. [@1]
Police said the operation formed part of services countering the sale and
consumption of drugs. [@3]
