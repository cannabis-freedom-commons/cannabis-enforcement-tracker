The driver of the pickup carrying the drugs disobeyed stop signals during a
vehicle check and fled on foot into dense woodland. [@1] Officers tracked the
pickup to Campo Grande but could not detain its driver, who used the area's
vegetation to escape on foot. [@2] The driver remains at large. [@2] Authorities
have published no official information on his identity or on the load's origin
and destination. [@2]

National Gendarmerie officers seized 514 kilos 700 grams of marijuana in
Misiones province. [@1] Inside the Toyota Hilux, officers found 12 bundles
holding 628 packages of marijuana spread across the rear seats and cargo box.
[@1] Officers also seized two Brazilian vehicle identification plates found
during the inspection. [@1] Field Narcotest tests confirmed the seized substance
was cannabis sativa, weighing 514 kilos 700 grams in total. [@1]

The operation was carried out by members of the Aristóbulo del Valle Section
under Squadron 9 Oberá. [@1] The surveillance covered provincial routes 7 and 8.
[@2] When a Toyota Hilux approached, the gendarmes signalled the driver to stop,
but he accelerated and fled. [@1] The decentralized fiscal office in Oberá
ordered the confiscation of the drugs and other items and the opening of
proceedings for suspected violation of National Law 23.737. [@1]
