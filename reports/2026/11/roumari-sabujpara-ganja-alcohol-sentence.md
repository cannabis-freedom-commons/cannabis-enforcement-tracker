Police in Roumari upazila, Kurigram district, detained 28-year-old Sohail Mia
during a special anti-drug operation. [@1]

A special team from Roumari police station carried out the raid at about 9pm on
Monday, 2 November 2026, in the upazila's Sabujpara area. [@1]

The operation, led by sub-inspector Billal Hossain, found a small amount of
alcohol and a small packet of ganja at Mia's own home, and he was detained. [@1]
Officers also found equipment for smoking yaba, a methamphetamine tablet; it is
paraphernalia rather than the drug, so no synthetic quantities are counted in
this report. [@1]

An executive magistrate then held a mobile court, a magistrate-led court that
decides minor offences on the spot. [@1]

The court sentenced Mia to six months' simple imprisonment and a fine of 100
taka. [@1]

If he does not pay the fine he must serve an additional three days in prison.
[@1]

Police described the detention as part of a special anti-drug drive in Roumari
and urged residents to help them with information about drugs. [@1]
