Vemulawada Town police arrested two men who were selling ganja on Thursday, 12
November 2026, town inspector Veera Prasad said. [@2] Police identified them as
Banoth Suresh, 33, of Mahabubabad district, and Rampelli Mahender, 28, of
Jagtial district. [@1] Acting on what police described as credible information,
sub-inspector S. Premanandam and his staff kept special watch and detained the
pair, who had arrived on a two-wheeler, near Pedamma temple on Chekkapalli Road.
[@1]

Police said they seized 160 grams of ganja, a Honda Dio scooter, two Vivo mobile
phones and ₹400 in cash from the men. [@1] Officers also recovered 72 liquor
bottles connected with a theft at Vigneshwara Wines in Karimnagar. [@1]

Veera Prasad commended Premanandam, police constables Imran and Narender and
other staff for their work in arresting the suspects and solving the case. [@1]
The inspector said police were maintaining continuous surveillance to control
crime and would take strict action against anyone involved in anti-social
activities. [@1]
