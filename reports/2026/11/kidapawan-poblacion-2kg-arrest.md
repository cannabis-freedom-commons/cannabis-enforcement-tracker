Police authorities arrested a high-value drug suspect during the implementation
of a search warrant in Kidapawan City, North Cotabato. [@1] The suspect is a
39-year-old man known by the alias “Eco,” a resident of Perez Street, Barangay
Poblacion. [@1] Joint personnel of the Kidapawan City Police Office, the
Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency, and the North Cotabato Police Provincial
Office served the search warrant at about 4 a.m. on Tuesday. [@1] The operation
served a search warrant issued by a local court for alleged violation of
Republic Act 9165, the Comprehensive Dangerous Drugs Act of 2002. [@1]

The man did not resist arrest. [@1] During the search, authorities recovered two
compressed blocks and four self-sealing plastic packages containing over 2,000
grams of suspected dried marijuana leaves with fruiting tops estimated to cost
P432,180. [@1] Police are verifying reports that the suspect had been
distributing the marijuana to students in the city and nearby municipalities.
[@1] Regional police director Brig. Gen. Alan Manibog said the seizure
underscored the importance of collective efforts to protect communities,
commending the police and anti-narcotics agents. [@1]
