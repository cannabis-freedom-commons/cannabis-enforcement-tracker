Two men, aged 22 and 34, were caught with portions of cannabis and oxi, along
with money, during an approach in a beco in the Raiz neighbourhood of Manaus'
South Zone. [@1] Two men, aged 22 and 34, were arrested on suspicion of
involvement in drug trafficking on the night of Friday 13 November 2026 during a
ROCAM operation in the Raiz neighbourhood of Manaus' South Zone. [@1] The
approach took place on rua Passo Real, where the officers were carrying out
ostensive patrol, and during a move into a beco in the area the military police
found the two men acting suspiciously. [@1] In the personal search officers
found portions of narcotics, including maconha and oxi, along with an amount of
cash, and the materials were seized and the suspects sent to the 1st Integrated
Police District (DIP). [@1] According to the report released during the
presentation of the incident, the two were already known to residents of the
region, and one of them also had police records. [@1] The suspects remained at
the police unit, where the applicable procedures were carried out, and they are
to go through a custody hearing at which the courts will decide whether the
arrests are maintained. [@1]
