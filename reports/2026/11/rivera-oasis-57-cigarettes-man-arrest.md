A 35-year-old man was captured on suspicion of trafficking, manufacturing or
carrying narcotics during a National Police procedure in the Oasis neighborhood
of Rivera, Huila. [@1] The operation took place at night under the Plan Cazador
strategy while uniformed officers were carrying out registration and control
activities. [@1]

During the search, officers found a bag holding 57 marijuana cigarettes inside a
backpack he was carrying, and they seized the cigarettes. [@1] The detainee was
placed at the disposal of the competent authority together with the seized
substance to answer for the alleged offense. [@1]

The commander of the Neiva Metropolitan Police, Colonel Alex Andrés Muñoz
Ausecha, said controls will continue to combat local drug trafficking. [@1] He
invited residents to report activities related to drug dealing in the area as
part of those continued controls against street-level sales. [@1]
