Specialized officers of the Investigative Police broke up a family
drug-trafficking group operating in La Pincoya, in the commune of Huechuraba.
[@1] The Huechuraba BICRIM Modelo Territorial Cero unit raided several houses in
La Pincoya used to store drugs for sale after an exhaustive investigative
process. [@1,2] Officers detained six Chilean citizens in an operation against a
family drug-trafficking group in La Pincoya, Huechuraba. [@1,2] Two of those
detained had prior records, and one was wanted on an arrest warrant for homicide
and had additional police records for robbery of public-use property, robbery in
an inhabited place and forgery. [@1,2]

Officers seized almost four kilograms of drugs comprising cocaine base, cocaine
hydrochloride and cannabis. [@1] Police valued that mixed drug seizure at more
than 19 million pesos. [@1] Officers also seized two vehicles valued at more
than 30 million pesos, dosing elements, more than 1 million pesos in cash,
walkie-talkies for communicating among themselves and cell phones. [@1,2]

The 261 grams of cannabis were seized alongside 3,434 grams of cocaine base and 272 grams of cocaine hydrochloride, which the PDI valued together at about 19 million pesos. [@2] A detective from the Huechuraba BICRIM, subprefect Bernardo Veloso, confirmed that the six detained were all adults of Chilean nationality. [@3]
