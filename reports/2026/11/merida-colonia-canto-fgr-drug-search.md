Federal prosecutors from the Fiscalía General de la República, Mexico's attorney
general's office, executed a judge-issued search warrant at a property in
colonia Canto, in southern Mérida, Yucatán, on the morning of 5 November 2026.
[@1] The operation began shortly before 5 a.m. on the Thursday. [@1]

The National Guard, the army, the navy (Semar) and state public security
officers (SSP) took part, sealing off the area with a security perimeter while
ministerial staff went into the building. [@1] With the help of K-9 dog teams,
the agents searched the house and found various quantities of drugs, among them
marijuana, cristal and cocaine. [@1]

By 2 p.m. that day authorities reported no one detained at the site. [@1]

The search in colonia Canto followed one the day before in Xoclán Canto, also in
Mérida, where security forces detained a woman and seized two vehicles. [@1]

A video post by local outlet Yucatán Ahora placed the searched property at calle
111 between calles 40 and 42 and described the deployment as linked to
narcomenudeo investigations, with nearby streets closed. [@2]
