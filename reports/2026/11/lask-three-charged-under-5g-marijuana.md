On Friday, 6 November 2026, officers of the Criminal Department of the Łask
County Police Headquarters were carrying out checks aimed at drug crime. [@1,5] At
about 20:50 they stopped a Mercedes on Leśników Polskich street in Łask, after
noticing that the people in the car were nervous while officers checked their
identities. [@1,4,5] They found zip-lock bags of a white crystalline substance on a
45-year-old resident of Łask County and detained him. [@1,4,5] A search of his flat
and outbuildings then turned up more drugs. [@1,3,4,5] Preliminary tests identified
the seized substances as mephedrone and marijuana. [@1,4,5] Police said they seized
more than 32 grams of drugs in that case, including more than 27 grams of
mephedrone and just under 5 grams of marijuana. [@2,5]

The same evening, at about 21:10, another patrol from the criminal department
was in a forest complex in Dąbrówka and saw an Opel with four men inside. [@1,4,5]
One of the men handed officers a bag of white powder, and a search of the car
found more drugs that another man admitted possessing. [@1,3,4,5] Tests identified
that substance as mephedrone, and officers detained two residents of Łask
County, aged 26 and 47. [@1,5]

All three detained men were taken to a police room for detained persons. [@1,3,4,5]
The next day they were charged with possession of drugs. [@1,3,4,5] Police said they
face a penalty of up to three years in prison. [@1,3,5] The account was signed by
junior aspirant Bartłomiej Kozłowski. [@1,5]
