On Tuesday 3 November 2026, Tirunelveli Range police burned about 174 kg of
seized ganja at a private facility near Nanguneri in southern Tamil Nadu. [@2,3]
The destroyed stock weighed 174.054 kg and had been seized across 124 separate
cases. [@1,3] Officers carried out the burning at a private company in
Pappankulam, within the Nanguneri police subdivision, in the presence of senior
officers. [@1] They destroyed the accumulated ganja by setting it on fire under
official supervision. [@2] Range Deputy Inspector General R. Thirunavukkarasu,
the range-level police chief, led the disposal operation. [@1,3] Tirunelveli
district Superintendent of Police Visvesh B. Shastri, the district police chief,
attended together with other police officers. [@1,3]

The destroyed substance was ganja, a cannabis product. [@1] The destroyed stock
came from 124 accumulated cases held for disposal. [@2]

Range police said they continue to work intensively to prevent the trafficking
and sale of narcotic drugs. [@1] They said they also continue to destroy seized
narcotic drugs in accordance with the law. [@1]
