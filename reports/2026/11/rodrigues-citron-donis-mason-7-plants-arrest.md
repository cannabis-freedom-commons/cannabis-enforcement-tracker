On Monday 2 November 2026, at around 10:15, officers of the ADSU carried out a
search at the home in Citron Donis, Rodrigues, of a 43-year-old man who works as
a mason. [@1] The case was presented as an arrest for both cannabis cultivation
and cannabis possession involving that mason. [@1] The suspect was arrested
immediately during the home search and was then placed in police detention. [@1]

During the same home search, the police seized a piece of paper containing 0.72
grams of cannabis. [@1] Officers also seized six cannabis seedlings found at the
mason's home during that search. [@1] They also seized seven cannabis seeds
found together with the seedlings and the loose cannabis. [@1] Officers also
seized a cannabis plant measuring about 70 centimetres in height. [@1]

The seizure combined loose cannabis with growing material, namely six seedlings,
seven seeds and a plant about 70 centimetres tall, all found at the same home.
[@1] The investigation was still continuing after the arrest and detention. [@1]
