A police patrol conducting traffic surveillance in Kemijärvi stopped a vehicle
whose driver blew a reading of zero per mille in the breath test. [@1] According
to police, the smell of cannabis was coming from inside the car. [@1] The stop
was one of many tasks for the Lapland police department over a busy weekend,
alongside a pizzeria incident in Tornio and other matters. [@1]

The driver admitted he had smoked cannabis before setting off. [@1] A roadside
drug test that police gave the driver came back positive for cannabis. [@1]
Police suspect the driver of drug-impaired driving and of a drug-use offence.
[@1] The car's passenger is also suspected of a drug-use offence because police
found a small amount of cannabis in his possession. [@1]

The Lapland police department's own weekend statement records a separate
suspected drug-impaired driving case from the same weekend in Keminmaa, not in
Kemijärvi. [@2]
