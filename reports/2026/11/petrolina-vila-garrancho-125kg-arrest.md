Military police from the 5th BPM seized more than 1 kg of marijuana during
Operation Sao Francisco II in Petrolina in the early hours of Tuesday 10
November 2026. [@1] The operation in Vila Garrancho followed a complaint about
drug dealing and threats with firearms against local residents. [@1] A man
carrying a machete disobeyed an order to stop and fled into a house when
officers approached. [@1]

The man and an adolescent tried to escape over the roof but were surrounded,
intercepted and detained. [@1] Officers seized 1.25 kg of marijuana at the
house. [@1] Officers also seized two precision scales, scissors, a vest cover,
two mobile phones and a machete. [@2]

The two people and all seized material were taken to the Civil Police station in
Petrolina, where the applicable measures were taken. [@2] The Vila Garrancho
seizure occurred in Petrolina in the Sertao of Pernambuco during the same
Tuesday early-morning operation. [@3]
