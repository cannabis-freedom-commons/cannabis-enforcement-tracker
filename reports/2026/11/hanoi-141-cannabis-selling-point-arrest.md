Hanoi police's Y5/141 task force caught a man holding drugs and weapons at his
home and broke up a cannabis-selling point in an ATV news bulletin on 6 November
2026. [@1,2] The bulletin also links to a video of the Y5/141 task force catching
a man in the act of keeping drugs and weapons at his house. [@1]

Y5/141 is one of the numbered patrol teams that the Hanoi City Police fields.
[@1] The case was carried in the ATV bulletin, An ninh Thủ đô's television news
programme, on 6 November 2026. [@1]

The task force was on duty on Trường Chinh street when its officers spotted two young men showing signs of drug involvement. [@3] Working from that stop, the officers broke up a group that sold cannabis and kept weapons, and arrested one more person who had bought drugs. [@3] The Hanoi On television account says the patrol was a night operation that found and broke up a group trading illegal narcotics. [@2]
