Two men aged 18 and 19 were detained on foot in the El Resumen sector of Cúcuta,
near the San Jorge military canton. [@2] Soldiers of the National Army stopped
them during registration and control activities and called for police support.
[@1] Officers from CAI San Rafael, the Immediate Attention Command, took over
the procedure. [@2] The two detainees and what they were carrying were taken to
the Immediate Reaction Unit (URI) of the Attorney General's Office. [@1] There
they were placed at the disposal of the competent authority while their judicial
situation is decided. [@1]

They were found carrying a package holding about 500 grams of plant material
appearing to be marijuana. [@1] The case was announced by Col. Pedro José
Saavedra Pinzón, commander of the Cúcuta Metropolitan Police (Mecuc). [@1]
According to the commander, the package held plant material with characteristics
similar to marijuana and it was seized, with an approximate weight of 500 grams.
[@2]

Saavedra Pinzón said operations and controls continue in coordination with other
institutions to counter drug trafficking and keep the substances from reaching
communities. [@1] He said the procedure forms part of the Urban Security
Operational Block, a strategy to strengthen security and citizen coexistence.
[@1] He asked residents to report suspicious situations or crimes on emergency
line 123. [@1]
