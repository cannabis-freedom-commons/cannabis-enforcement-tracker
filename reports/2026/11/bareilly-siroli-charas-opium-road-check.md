Siroli police in Bareilly district, Uttar Pradesh, arrested two men on a
motorcycle carrying opium and charas during a patrol and vehicle check. [@1] The
second report dates the check to 4 November 2026 and says officers stopped the
two riders at about 8pm on an informant's tip. [@2]

The second report gave the bike's number as RJ13 SB 5365. [@2]

Police registered a case against both at Siroli police station under the NDPS
Act, India's main anti-drug law; the second report recorded it as case number
379/2026 under sections 8, 18 and 20. [@2] The second report said Subhash Singh
had an earlier criminal record and named the arresting team, which also included
sub-inspector Satir Singh and head constable Rahul Gautam. [@2]
