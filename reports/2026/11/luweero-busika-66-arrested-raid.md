Security agencies arrested 66 suspected criminals in a joint pre-dawn operation
in Busika Town Council, Luweero District. [@1] The sweep ran from midnight until
5am on Monday and targeted the villages of Kalibongo, Vumba and Kibanyi. [@1]
Security forces detained 56 men and 10 women during the raid. [@1] Police are
screening the detainees to establish direct links to specific offences, and say
those found culpable will be formally charged and presented in court. [@1]

According to police, the three villages were gathering spots for criminal
networks whose members allegedly traded illicit drugs, including marijuana and
opium, and planned burglaries and thefts. [@1] Some of the suspects were found
in possession of narcotic drugs. [@1] The operation was executed by a joint
force comprising the Uganda Police Force, the Uganda People's Defence Force and
the Internal Security Organisation, working alongside local residents. [@1]

Police said the operation followed intelligence reports indicating that
suspected criminals were using the areas as meeting points before spreading to
other parts of the district to commit crimes. [@2] Police said more security
operations would be conducted across Luweero District to contain criminal
activities ahead of the festive season. [@2] The crackdown follows the 2025
police annual crime report in which Luweero registered the highest number of
reported crimes nationwide. [@1]
