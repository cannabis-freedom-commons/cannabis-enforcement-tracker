Police in Patna's Agamkuan police station area stopped a car near Zero Mile and
found 70 kilograms of ganja, the local name for cannabis, inside it. [@1] The
car was a Brezza, and officers seized it during the vehicle check while
arresting one occupant. [@2] Police identified the arrested man as Shravan
Paswan, 25, a resident of Pathra ward 6 in the Pipra police station area of
Supaul district. [@2]

The second man in the car ran into the crowd and escaped. [@2] The tip reached
Agamkuan police on 4 November that two men were bringing ganja from Supaul to
Patna by car. [@2] A police team led by Agamkuan station house officer Rajesh
Kumar Jha ran intensive vehicle checks at Zero Mile near Badi Pahadi on a tip
that a large consignment of prohibited ganja was coming by vehicle. [@1] Prabhat
Khabar named the other man as Sanjay Kumar, a resident of Saryagadh in the
Bhaptiyahi police station area of Supaul, and said he owned the car. [@1]

Police registered a first information report at Agamkuan police station under
the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act, India's narcotics
law. [@2] Police were questioning the arrested man about where the consignment
came from and where it was to be supplied. [@1] Assistant superintendent of
police Rajkishore Singh gave a press conference on the operations. [@1] The same
press conference covered three separate cases, including a solved burglary and a
man held with a country-made pistol, and police said legal proceedings were
under way in all three. [@1]
