Santoña Local Police detained a minor who was carrying approximately 40 grams of
a substance described as apparently hashish. [@1] The substance was hidden
inside a vaping device that was made to look like an ordinary vape. [@2] Police
said the minor was detained for an alleged offence against public health. [@1]

Police announced the detention in a statement describing several actions carried
out in recent days during surveillance, prevention and protection work. [@2] The
published accounts identify the detained person only as a minor and give no
name, age or other personal details. [@1] The case was reported in Santoña, in
the autonomous community of Cantabria in northern Spain. [@1]

In the same police roundup, officers recovered a vehicle that was listed as
stolen and subject to a Schengen Information System alert, and returned it to
its rightful owner. [@1] Police also opened proceedings for two alleged
road-safety offences, one involving driving after losing all licence points and
another involving driving by a person who had never held a licence. [@1]
