Royal Gendarmerie officers at the Sidi Bibi territorial centre in Chtouka Ait
Baha province stopped the activity of a person suspected of links to the
trafficking of drugs and psychotropic substances. [@1] The search the officers
carried out found quantities of chira, cannabis resin, and of cannabis herb,
known locally as kif. [@1]

Officers also seized a large sword and two motorcycles, which they suspect were
used to move around and to facilitate drug-trafficking activity. [@1] The
suspect was placed in garde à vue, Morocco's police-custody measure, while a
judicial investigation continues under the supervision of the competent public
prosecutor. [@1] The gendarmerie said the investigation was meant to uncover all
the circumstances of the case and to establish whether other parties were
connected to the activity. [@1]

The gendarmerie presented the arrest as part of its operations against
drug-related crime across the province and its pursuit of suspects in drug
trafficking. [@1]
