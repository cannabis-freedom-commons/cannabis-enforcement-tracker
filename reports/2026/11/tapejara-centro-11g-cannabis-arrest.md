A joint Civil and Military Police anti-trafficking operation seized crack,
marijuana and hashish on Tuesday 3 November in Tapejara in northwest Paraná.
[@1,3] The operation targeted a house in the town centre suspected of serving as a
drug-selling point. [@2,3] Investigators of the 17th Regional Police Station of
Cruzeiro do Oeste carried out the action with support from the 7th Military
Police Battalion after monitoring the address following trafficking complaints.
[@2] During surveillance officers stopped a man seen leaving the house and found
a 0.70-gram portion of crack with him. [@2,3] Teams then entered the investigated
residence and found the 24-year-old suspect with two adolescents. [@1,3] The
operation ended with the arrest of the 24-year-old, the apprehension of an
adolescent and the referral of the man stopped outside. [@2]

Inside the house police seized 11.17 grams of marijuana and 0.31 gram of
hashish, alongside 1.89 grams of crack, R$474 in broken-down cash and a mobile
phone. [@2] Officers said the 1.89 grams of crack were in the adult suspect's
shorts pocket. [@1] At an adolescent's home officers found another 64 crack
stones totalling 32 grams, described as packed for sale, plus a roll of
aluminium foil and two knives with drug-preparation traces. [@2,3] A search of
another adolescent's home found no illicit material and that teenager was handed
over to family. [@1]

The 24-year-old was booked in the act for drug trafficking, criminal association
and corruption of minors, with no bail set and a request for preventive
detention. [@2,3] After the procedures he was taken to the Umuarama public jail to
remain at the disposal of the courts. [@2] An adolescent received a
circumstantial report for acts analogous to trafficking and criminal association
and was released to guardians after referral to the Public Prosecution Service.
[@2,3] The man stopped outside signed a circumstantial statement for possession
for personal use, and the other adolescent approached was released after a home
search without irregularities. [@2]
