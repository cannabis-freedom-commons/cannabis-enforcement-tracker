Two men aged 29 and 34 were arrested for drug trafficking during a Highway
Military Police operation on the MG-188 in Paracatu. [@1] The two men were from
Uberlândia and were travelling toward Paracatu when stopped. [@1] After the
drugs were found, both received arrest orders for drug trafficking. [@1] The
suspects were taken for medical evaluation and then to the Civil Police station,
while the car used to carry the drugs was removed. [@1]

Officers stopped a Hyundai HB20 during an anti-drug operation and found the
drugs inside a suitcase in the trunk. [@1] The seizure comprised 10 bars of
marijuana plus one bar of cocaine found together in the trunk suitcase. [@2] The
stop occurred at km 185 of the MG-188 in Paracatu, recorded at about 23h30. [@2]
The second account dates the overnight operation to the early hours of Thursday
5 November. [@2]
