Officers of the Guardia Civil Municipal of Soledad de Graciano Sanchez detained
six men, one woman and two 17-year-olds for probable participation in offences
against health. [@1] Two 17-year-olds were secured in the Santo Tomas and Villas
de Foresta neighbourhoods with a bag of suspected marijuana and a dose of
methamphetamine. [@1]

Guadalupe N, 36, Alejandro N, 36, Jenifer N, 25, and Angel N, 20, were detained
in San Francisco, Rivas Guillen and San Antonio with doses of suspected
methamphetamine. [@1] Oscar N, 42, Jesus N, 26, and Andres N, 18, were detained
in Benito Juarez, Villas Foresta and the municipal centre with bags of suspected
marijuana. [@1]

After being told the reason for their detention, all nine were transferred to
the State Prosecutor with the seized substances. [@1]
