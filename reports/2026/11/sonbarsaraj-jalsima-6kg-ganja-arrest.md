Sonbarsaraj police in Saharsa district arrested a suspected ganja smuggler with
6 kg of ganja on Monday afternoon. [@1] Police said they had received
information that an illegal ganja trade was going on in Jalsima village. [@1]
During a vehicle check on Monday in the Sonvarsha area, local station police
arrested one person in a ganja recovery case. [@2] Police recovered about six
kilograms of ganja during the Monday vehicle check in the Sonvarsha area. [@2] A
bike rider was coming from the Jalsima side toward Sonvarsha Raj market when
police stopped him. [@2]

On seeing the police he tried to leave and flee, but the policemen acted
promptly and caught him. [@2] When police searched the bag hanging on his back,
they found ganja inside it. [@2] Police seized about six kilograms of ganja from
the arrested person. [@2] The recovered ganja was seized in the presence of
magistrate-cum-Circle Officer Lalan Choudhary. [@2]

The arrested accused was identified as Mukesh Kumar, a resident of Bhada
village. [@2] Station chief Nagmani Kumar said the bike rider coming from
Jalsima toward Sonvarsha Raj market tried to flee after seeing the police during
the vehicle check. [@2] After the incident, police started registering a case
against the arrested accused. [@2] The process of sending the accused to
judicial custody was under way. [@2]
