Military Police officers arrested a man and a woman for drug trafficking in Dois
Vizinhos, in southwestern Paraná, in the early afternoon of Sunday 8 November
2026, at about 1 pm. [@1,3] The approach took place in the Nossa Senhora de
Lourdes neighbourhood, where the Rádio Patrulha (RPA) patrol-car team stopped a
vehicle that seemed suspicious during a routine patrol. [@2] The team belonged
to the 2nd Company of the 21st Military Police Battalion, the Paraná Military
Police unit covering the region. [@2]

A search of the car found a backpack in the boot holding nine and a half bars of
marijuana weighing about 4.8 kilograms. [@1,3] The second account describes the
contents of the nine and a half bars as a substance analogous to marijuana. [@2]

The stop was made by the 2nd Company of the 21st Military Police Battalion after anonymous tips that passengers coming from Francisco Beltrão were carrying drugs. [@3] The app driver was released after police found he had no part in transporting the drug. [@3] According to the Military Police, the seizure caused an estimated R$ 96,000 loss to the traffickers.
