Three people were named as suspects after security forces searched along Kanal
Yolu in the Serintepe neighbourhood of Malatya on 4 November 2026. [@1] A
judicial investigation was opened against the three suspects in connection with
the seizure. [@1,4]

Officers seized 6,050 narcotic pills packed in 121 boxes together with 12 grams
of esrar, the Turkish name for cannabis. [@1,3,4,5] Malatya governor Seddar Yavuz
announced the seizure, not a police directorate statement. [@1]

Yavuz said the checks on 4 November 2026 found 6,050 narcotic pills in 121 boxes
and 12 grams of esrar, and that the investigation into the three suspects had
begun. [@2] The governor said the fight against drugs would continue without
pause. [@1] The second account says the security forces were carrying out field
checks as part of continuing controls on drug production and trade across
Malatya. [@2] Kahta Haber Postası reported that the seized material was taken
into custody. [@2]

Manşet Haber described the 6,050 pills as sentetik ecza, or synthetic pharmaceutical pills. [@3] The operation was carried out by Battalgazi District Police, part of the Malatya Provincial Police Directorate. [@3,4] Police searched the suspects’ addresses as part of the operation. [@3] Police detained three suspects and took them to the station, where their processing was still continuing. [@3,6]

Battalgazi District Police teams carried out physical surveillance of street dealers described as torbacı and then searched a vehicle stopped on suspicion. [@5,8]

A separate Anadolu Agency account said the 6,050 synthetic pharmaceutical pills and 12 grams of esrar were found in searches at the suspects' addresses. [@7]

Three suspects were caught in the operation. [@8] An investigation into the incident was opened. [@8]
