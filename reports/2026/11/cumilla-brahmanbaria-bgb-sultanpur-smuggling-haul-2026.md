Border Guard Bangladesh put the estimated value of goods seized in separate
drives at 2,090,000 taka, about 20 lakh 90 thousand. [@1] One person was
detained in the drives. [@1] The drives were separate, and the force described
the material seized as illegal smuggled goods. [@1] The force said its
operations and intelligence work against smuggling, drugs, illegal entry and
trafficking of women and children would continue. [@1]

Neither account gives a weight for the ganja seized in the drives. [@1] Both
accounts describe the seizure as one result of a day's drives across several
border outposts, not a single raid. [@1] The haul also held fireworks, cattle,
rice, soft drinks, syrups, alcohol and beer alongside the ganja. [@1]
