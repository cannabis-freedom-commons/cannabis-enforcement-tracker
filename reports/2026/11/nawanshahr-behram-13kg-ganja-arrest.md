Behram police arrested people with 13 kg 170 g of ganja. [@1] A police team
under Sub-Inspector Shinderpal was conducting a special hi-tech blockade at
Mahli to check suspicious people and vehicles for the Diwali festival. [@1]

The team saw an auto-rickshaw coming from the Phagwara side, whose driver
panicked on seeing the police checkpoint, braked and tried to turn the vehicle
back. [@1] The police team stopped the auto-rickshaw with the help of other
officers. [@1] A search of a plastic bag found inside the vehicle recovered 13
kg 170 g of ganja. [@1]

The arrested people were identified as Vijay, son of Mangat Ram of Ludhiana, and
Navneet Pathak alias Raja, son of Roop Kant Pathak of Krishna Nagar, Bhamia
Khurd, Mundian Kalan, Jamalpur police station, Ludhiana district. [@1] A case
was registered against the accused under the NDPS Act and further legal
proceedings were started. [@1]
