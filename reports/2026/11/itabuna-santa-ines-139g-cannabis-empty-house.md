Military police of the 15th Battalion inspected an uninhabited building on
Avenida Bionor Rebouças in the Santa Inês neighbourhood on the night of Friday 6
November 2026 after receiving information it was being used to store and sell
drugs. [@1] Itabuna lies in southern Bahia and its Santa Inês neighbourhood was
the site of the seizure. [@2] No suspects were found at the property and no one
was arrested. [@1]

Officers seized 85 grams of pressed marijuana, 10 grams of gold marijuana and 44
grams of hashish. [@1] The same seizure also yielded 122 wraps of cocaine and 32
crack rocks, which are not counted in the cannabis weight. [@1] Police also
seized three mobile phones, three precision scales, a notebook, an annotation
book, an identity card, one .45 round of ammunition and a bag of empty pins.
[@1]

All seized material was taken to the regional police station for registration of
the case and further legal measures. [@1]
