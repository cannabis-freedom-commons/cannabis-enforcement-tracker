A 33-year-old man was captured in the Juan Pablo Segundo neighbourhood of
Floridablanca, Santander, after attempting to escape from a police operation,
with three kilograms of marijuana and a pistol with a magazine and one 9 mm
cartridge found on him. [@1] The procedure was carried out by members of the
Special Operations Group (GOES) during patrol and control work on calle 141C
with carrera 47. [@1]

When he noticed the authorities, the man fled into a building, but officers
immediately and continuously tracked him without losing sight until they
intercepted him. [@1] During the search, officers found three blocks of
marijuana, each weighing approximately one kilogram and wrapped in brown tape.
[@1]

Brigadier General Miguel Andrés Camelo Sánchez, commander of the Bucaramanga
Metropolitan Police, said that operational interventions in the neighbourhoods
are being strengthened to combat drug trafficking and to remove from circulation
firearms that may pose a danger to the community. [@1] The captured man and the
seized items were placed at the disposal of the Attorney General's Office, the
Fiscalía General de la Nación, which will run the corresponding judicial process
and present him before a judge to define his legal situation. [@1]
