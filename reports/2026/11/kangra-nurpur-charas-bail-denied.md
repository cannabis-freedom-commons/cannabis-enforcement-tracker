The court of the Special Judge (First) in Kangra, at Dharamshala, rejected the
regular bail application of a 19-year-old woman arrested with 1.006 kg of
charas. [@1] The court held that the recovered charas fell in the commercial
quantity category, so the twin conditions laid down in Section 37 of the NDPS
Act had to be met. [@1] It rejected her bail plea on 6 November 2026. [@1] The
court considered her young age, her lack of a criminal record and the completion
of the investigation, but was not satisfied on both conditions. [@1]

The case began on the night of 27-28 June 2026, when the CIA team of Nurpur
police stopped an HRTC bus at Bodh Chowk in Jasur after a tip that a young woman
was carrying charas towards Pathankot-Amritsar. [@1] At about 12:56 am, officers
searched the bus and recovered 1.006 kg of charas from the bag of the woman
sitting in seat 34. [@1] The State Forensic Science Laboratory at Junga
confirmed that the sample was charas. [@1] Police said she would be produced in
court for police remand on the following Monday. [@3] Police registered a case
at Nurpur police station under Sections 20 and 29 of the NDPS Act. [@2]

Defence counsel argued that she was only 19, had no criminal record and that the
investigation was complete, so custody was no longer needed. [@1] The
prosecution opposed bail, calling the recovery a commercial quantity. [@1] The
court said it had to be satisfied, before granting bail under Section 37, that
there were reasonable grounds to believe she was not guilty and that she was not
likely to commit an offence after release. [@1]
