The Goud report describes the target as a dangerous network promoting hashish,
the local term for cannabis resin, around Laayoune. [@1] Royal Gendarmerie
judicial-centre officers in Laayoune, backed by anti-gang units under the direct
supervision of the company commander, raided a secret hideout used by the
network at Taslba in Boukraa commune, about 40 kilometres from Laayoune. [@1]
The operation followed precise exploitation of intelligence from the regional
intelligence service and modern technical means, which located the hideout and
allowed officers to surprise the network. [@1]

Officers arrested the gang leader during the raid. [@1] Officers seized a
four-wheel-drive vehicle and significant quantities of various drugs described
as ready for promotion and distribution, without giving a separate cannabis
weight. [@2] Searches of the hideout found a firearm with ammunition and bladed
weapons suspected of use in the network's criminal activity. [@1]

At the same site officers also found and dismantled a secret workshop making
rubber boats intended for irregular-migration trips, showing the site combined
drug dealing with people-smuggling logistics. [@1] The Laayoune regional
gendarmerie presents the raid as part of its continuing proactive campaign
against organised crime to protect people and property. [@2]
