Local people detained two youths in Dumki carrying a stolen goat and handed them
to Dumki police on 4 November 2026. [@1] The detained men were identified as
Rabbi Munshi, son of Kalam Munshi, and Oli, son of Shahjahan, both from
Madhabpur in Adabaria Union of Bauphal. [@1] They were caught around 2 pm on
Wednesday on the road on the eastern side of the market next to the Board Office
bridge. [@1]

Locals said the pair had stolen a female goat belonging to Bellal Tailors in the
Muradia area and were carrying it away in an auto-rickshaw. [@1] Those holding
them recovered a sharp knife, scissors, a cutter, 20 grams of ganja, the goat
and the auto-rickshaw used to carry it. [@1] The goat, auto-rickshaw and
recovered items were handed to Dumki police station along with the two
detainees. [@1]

Dumki police station officer-in-charge Md Selim Uddin said police were sent
after receiving the information through village police. [@1] He said the men
were brought to the station and legal action would be taken. [@1]
