On the afternoon of 11 November 2026, officers of the Z5 Prevention and
Mediation Team (O.P.DI.) of Corinthia arrested a 27-year-old Greek man on the
New Athens–Patras National Road. [@1] The man was held because he possessed
cannabis weighing 93.01 grams, which officers seized. [@1] The outlet identifies
Kiato as the place of the arrest. [@1]

In a subsequent search of his home, officers found and seized a small amount of
cannabis and a scale. [@1] The preliminary investigation is being conducted by
the Sikyona Police Department. [@1]
