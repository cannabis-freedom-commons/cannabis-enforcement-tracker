Border police found drugs on two Munich–Salzburg passenger trains and opened
investigations against two men. [@1] The checks took place on Wednesday, 11
November, near Teisendorf and Rosenheim, the Piding border police inspectorate
said. [@1] Around 14:00, investigators checked a 25-year-old Hungarian near
Teisendorf. [@1] They found about five grams of cocaine and about 100 grams of
marijuana on him. [@1] Police then searched his flat and found various banned
medicines. [@1] A criminal case is being pursued against him for various
offences. [@2]

Earlier, around midday, officers checked a 37-year-old Austrian near Rosenheim
without a concrete suspicion. [@1] In a leather pouch in his rucksack they found
a tin of marijuana in the single-digit gram range. [@1] The officers seized the
cannabis. [@1] He is being investigated on suspicion of having tried to export
cannabis. [@1] Officers finished dealing with his case on the train. [@1]

The Piding border police inspectorate gave the account of both checks. [@1] The
trains were travelling from Munich to Salzburg. [@1] Both were held for the
police measures; the Hungarian was then released and the Austrian allowed to
continue his journey. [@1] The reports name only the men's nationalities and
ages. [@1]
