Handwara police stopped a tipper bearing registration number JK05B-5479 at Batagund during checkpoint checks on the night of 6–7 November 2026. [@1,2,3,4] Officers found a charas-like substance concealed inside the dashboard. [@1,2,3,4] The driver, Irshad Ahmad Dar of Shanoo Langate, was travelling from Handwara towards Qalamabad and was arrested at the checkpoint. [@1,2,3,4] Police seized the tipper as part of their investigation. [@1]

Police registered FIR No. 78/2026 at Police Station Qalamabad under sections 8 and 20 of the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act. [@1] The investigation was continuing to establish the source and intended destination of the substance. [@1] Kashmir Life, Brighter Kashmir and Kashmir News Observer included the arrest in their coverage of several police operations announced together on 7 November. [@2,3,4]
