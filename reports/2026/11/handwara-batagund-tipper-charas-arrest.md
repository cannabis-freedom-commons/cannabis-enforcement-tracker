Handwara police stopped a tipper bearing registration number JK05B-5479 at
Batagund during a naka checking operation in the night between 6 and 7 November
2026. [@1] A search of the vehicle found a charas-like substance concealed
inside its dashboard. [@1]

The driver, Irshad Ahmad Dar of Shanoo Langate, was travelling from Handwara
towards Qalamabad and was arrested at the scene, while the tipper was seized.
[@1] Police said the accused was arrested on the spot and the vehicle was seized
as part of the investigation. [@1]

Police registered FIR No. 78/2026 at Police Station Qalamabad under Sections
8/20 of the NDPS Act, the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, which
punishes possession of cannabis products such as charas. [@1] Police said
further investigation was under way to establish the source and intended
destination of the substance. [@1]

Handwara Police restated its commitment to fighting drug trafficking and asked
residents to pass on information about drug-related activity. [@1] Police said
public cooperation was needed to protect young people from narcotics. [@1]
