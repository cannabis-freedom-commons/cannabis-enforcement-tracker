A routine check at a home in Licata, meant to verify that a woman was observing
the terms of house arrest, ended with the arrest of her daughter, a 22-year-old
woman already known to the police. [@1] Officers of the Licata police station in
via Campobello and their colleagues from the Agrigento flying squad made the
arrest in the "Bronx" district of Licata. [@1]

During the check the officers' attention moved to a car parked outside, and a
search of the vehicle found about 1.7 kilograms of marijuana hidden in a bag.
[@1] Police seized the drug for laboratory analysis. [@1]

After formal procedures at the police station, the woman was taken to the
Pasquale Di Lorenzo prison in Agrigento, where she was held at the disposal of
the judicial authorities. [@1]
