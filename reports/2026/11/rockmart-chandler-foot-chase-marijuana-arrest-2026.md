Chad Andrew Chandler, 42, of Rockmart was arrested after allegedly trying to
flee from a Polk County officer during an investigation on New Prospect Road.
[@1] After a brief foot chase and struggle, he was taken into custody. [@1] He
was later found in possession of marijuana after lying to police. [@1] He is
charged with possession of marijuana, crossing the guard line with drugs, giving
false information to police, obstruction and a bicycle equipment violation. [@1]

An officer patrolling New Prospect Road at about 10:43 p.m. saw Chandler riding
a bicycle in the roadway. [@1] The bicycle’s headlight was initially on but was
turned off as the officer approached, and Chandler turned into Wolfe’s Store at
150 New Prospect Road. [@1] The officer left her patrol vehicle to investigate,
and Chandler allegedly fled on foot during the encounter. [@1]
