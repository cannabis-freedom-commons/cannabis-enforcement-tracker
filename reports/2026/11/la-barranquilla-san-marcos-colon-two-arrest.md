Military police detained two alleged members of the Mara Salvatrucha (MS-13)
during an intelligence operation in the village of La Barranquilla, in the
municipality of San Marcos de Colón, Choluteca, seizing suspected drugs, a
firearm and ammunition. [@1] The report gave no identities for the two
detainees. [@1] They were preliminarily accused of terrorist association, drug
trafficking and illegal possession of a firearm. [@1]

During the search the soldiers seized a package of about one pound of suspected
marijuana, a bag with about five grams of the same substance and 39 wraps also
believed to contain that drug. [@1] They also found 33 transparent tips with
white powder alleged to be cocaine hydrochloride and a grinder holding presumed
marijuana inside. [@1] The evidence also included a pistol with a round in the
chamber, 42 .25-calibre rounds and two 9 mm rounds, all unfired, as well as nine
mobile phones of different makes and models. [@1] Agents also found cash in
different denominations, plus 60 córdobas and five quetzals, without authorities
detailing the total amount seized in lempiras. [@1]

The operation was carried out by the Fourth Battalion of the PMOP, under the
operational control of the Lenca Sumpul Task Force and in support of the
National Agency Against Crime (ANCC). [@1] The two suspects and the seized
objects were transferred to the Directorate of Special and Tactical Affairs
(Daet), where investigations will continue into their alleged links to criminal
structures and other crimes. [@1]
