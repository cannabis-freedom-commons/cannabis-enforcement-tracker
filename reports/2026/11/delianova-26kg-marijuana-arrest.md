The event took place in Delianova. [@1] Carabinieri of the Delianova station,
under the Palmi company and supported by provincial, helicopter-borne hunter,
dog and SIO reinforcements, launched an operation at dawn across several homes
in the area. [@1] The operation was aimed at tracing illegally held weapons.
[@1] Officers arrested a local young man found with about 2.6 kg of marijuana
that was still drying. [@1]

A precision scale and material for packaging drugs found in the search
contributed to the arrest. [@1] Officers also found and seized a carbine and a
hunting rifle, both with erased serial numbers, along with various ammunition.
[@1] A 60-year-old local was reported at liberty in connection with possession
of material for making ammunition. [@1]

After the operation the young man was taken to the Palmi prison at the disposal
of the judicial authority. [@1] The Carabinieri said the activity was part of
broader territorial control focused on clandestine weapons and on drug
possession and distribution. [@1] The proceedings were in the
preliminary-investigation phase, with the presumption of innocence applying
until a final judgment. [@1]
