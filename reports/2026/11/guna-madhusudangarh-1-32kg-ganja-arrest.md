Madhusudangarh police in Madhya Pradesh's Guna district arrested a motorcycle
rider who had 1.32 kg of ganja during a vehicle check. [@1] The rider told
police in questioning that he had bought the ganja from Lekhraj Lodhi, a
resident of Ukavad village. [@1] Police also named Lekhraj in the case. [@1]

The rider tried to flee after seeing the police, and named his supplier during
questioning. [@1] Police seized the ganja from the rider in the Madhusudangarh
police station area of Guna district. [@1] The ganja seized from the rider
weighed 1.32 kg, according to the report's headline. [@1]

The vehicle check took place on 4 November near a warehouse on the Lateri bypass. [@2] He was coming on a black Hero Splendor motorcycle from the direction of Ukawad village. [@2] The rider tried to get away when police tried to stop him, and the team surrounded and caught him. [@2] The ganja, 1 kg 323 g, was found in a bag hanging on the motorcycle. [@2] The man told police he bought the ganja to make it into small packets and sell it retail. [@2] Police put the value of the ganja and the motorcycle used to carry it at about 90,000 rupees. [@2]
