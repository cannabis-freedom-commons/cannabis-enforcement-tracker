On 3 November 2026, Phuc Loi ward police held a conference to review grassroots
public-order work and the responsibility of community police officers in 2026.
[@1] Police reported that political security and social order in the ward
remained stable during the first 10 months of 2026 with no complex or unexpected
incidents. [@1] In those 10 months, ward police investigated 21 drug cases
involving 25 suspects. [@1] The highlighted case was illegal trading of
cannabis, known locally as cần sa, with 39,544 grams (nearly 39.5 kg) seized.
[@1] Police described that cannabis case as serious with a large seizure showing
proactive and resolute drug-crime enforcement. [@1]

In the same period, police received and resolved 34 public-order crimes. [@1]
They also handled three cases of spreading false information online and removed
three cases of joining reactionary groups online. [@1] Police advised the ward
authority on managing conditional businesses, ran a campaign on weapons and
explosives, and introduced neighbourhood and school security models. [@1]
Delegates praised grassroots police, especially community officers, and raised
issues about traffic safety and fire prevention in boarding houses and apartment
blocks. [@1] Ward police chief Lieutenant Colonel Do Huu Phuc answered the
comments and asked local authorities and residents to keep working with police
to protect security. [@1]
