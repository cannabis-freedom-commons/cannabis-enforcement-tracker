Police arrested 54-year-old Chain Singh Gond in Alampur village in the Uchehra
police area of Satna district after finding cannabis growing in his pigeon-pea
field. [@2] Officers said he had planted cannabis plants among the pigeon-pea
plants in the field. [@1] The Parsamania outpost police raided the field after
receiving information from an informer. [@1] Police said the accused tried to
run when he saw the team but was caught after officers surrounded him. [@1]
Police registered a case under the NDPS Act at Uchehra station, produced him in
court, and the court sent him to jail. [@1]

Officers seized 60 green cannabis plants from the field. [@1] Police described
the plants as 5 to 6 feet tall. [@1] Police gave the weight of the seized plants
as 18 kg. [@2] Police valued the seized drug at 180,000 rupees. [@1]

The second account records the case as crime number 428/26 under the NDPS Act.
[@2] It says the operation was led by Uchehra station chief Satish Mishra and
Parsamania outpost chief Santosh Singh Dhurve. [@2] It places the raided plot in
the Chhirhan Bav area of Alampur village in the Parsamania hill area. [@2]
