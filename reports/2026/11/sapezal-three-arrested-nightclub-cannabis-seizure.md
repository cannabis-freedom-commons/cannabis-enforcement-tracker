Two women aged 24 and 31 and a 26-year-old man were arrested for drug
trafficking in Sapezal on the night of 3 November 2026. [@1,3] Police identified
the three as members of a criminal faction. [@1] The Military Police said they
were held with about 100 portions of substances analogous to cocaine and
cannabis. [@1,2] The case began during a patrol by the cavalry unit in the city
centre, where soldiers received complaints of drug sales inside a nightclub.
[@1]

At the nightclub a woman ran inside when she saw the Military Police, and
soldiers followed her to a room where she was caught trying to flush drugs down
a toilet. [@2] Another part of the material, containing 20 portions of cocaine
ready for sale, was seized and the woman was detained. [@2] Questioned, she said
she had bought the material hours earlier at another woman's house near a bar.
[@1]

Officers went to that address and found the other two suspects; the woman swore
at the soldiers and tried to block them from entering, while the man ran inside
and was caught trying to get rid of a bag of drugs. [@2] In searches of the
house police found the rest of the cocaine and cannabis portions, cocaine paste
rocks, R$300 in cash and materials used in trafficking. [@2] The three suspects
were taken to the Sapezal police station with the seized material and handed to
the Polícia Judiciária Civil, the civilian investigative police, for further
steps. [@1]

Jornal Oeste reported the case with information from the Mato Grosso Military
Police and gave the same account of the arrests and the two addresses. [@2]

Folhamax reported that the three were identified as members of Comando Vermelho (CV). [@3]
