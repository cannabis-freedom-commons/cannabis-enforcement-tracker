Military police from the Specialised Policing Company (CPE) Alpha team detained
a man suspected of drug trafficking in Catalão, in southeastern Goiás, on the
night of Friday 6 November 2026. [@1] The officers were on patrol when they saw
the man throw two objects to the ground as the patrol car approached, and the
objects were portions of a cannabis-like substance. [@1] According to the
Military Police, the man said he had more drugs at his home and that he bought
drugs in Uberlândia, Minas Gerais, travelling with the ride-sharing app
BlaBlaCar, and resold them in Catalão. [@1]

Officers went to the address he gave, where they found more drugs and materials
linked to drug trafficking, including a precision scale. [@1] In total police
seized 200 grams of marijuana and 50 grams of hashish, which is 0.25 kg of
cannabis. [@1] The same seizure included several portions of cocaine and 100
grams of cocaine known as escama de peixe, which are not counted as cannabis.
[@1] The man was taken to the competent police authority with all the seized
material for the applicable legal procedures. [@1] No charge, custody decision
or court outcome for the man was reported. [@1]
