A court found a 34-year-old resident of Okhtyrka guilty of illegally selling
narcotic drugs and sentenced him to eight years in prison with confiscation of
property. [@1] The Okhtyrka District Prosecutor's Office supported the public
prosecution against the man. [@2] The defendant denied his guilt in court, but
the prosecutor proved his involvement in the crime. [@1]

The Sumy Oblast Prosecutor's Office said the man organised cannabis sales in
Okhtyrka through in-person meetings. [@1] He kept the drugs in his car and at
home. [@1] Police detained the man during one of the sales under Article 208 of
Ukraine's Code of Criminal Procedure. [@2] A search of the basement of his house
found more than two kilograms of cannabis, which officers seized. [@2]

The sentence was imposed under part 2 of Article 307 of Ukraine's Criminal Code.
[@2] The pre-trial investigation was carried out by investigators of the
Okhtyrka district police department under the procedural supervision of the
district prosecutor's office. [@1]
