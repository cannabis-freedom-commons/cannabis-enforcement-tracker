Narsingdi District Detective Branch officers and thana police carried out
separate special operations across the district on 12 November 2026 to keep law
and order stable, recover drugs and suppress crime. [@1] Police said the
operations arrested 36 people in all, including drug traders held with 14 kg of
ganja and 50 yaba tablets as well as suspects named in case records, men with
warrants outstanding and people involved in extortion. [@1]

Acting on secret information, the Detective Branch moved on the paved road in
front of the Al Amin Bike Service shop near Pakuria Bazar in Amdia Union,
Madhabdi, and caught drug trader Abdul Karim Pagla, 62, red-handed with 12 kg of
ganja. [@1] Karim, a resident of the Changao area under Madhabdi police station,
already faced 26 cases, 24 of them for ganja and two for yaba, and police said
legal action was taken over this incident. [@1] At a checkpoint on the paved
road in front of the Liza Zarda Factory at Mahmudabad under Raipura police
station, officers detained a man named Dulal Robi Das and, on searching him,
recovered 2 kg of ganja from a box used for shoe polish. [@1] In the same sweep
police arrested Asif alias Rabbi, 23, of the Purandiya area under Shibpur police
station, with 50 yaba tablets, a methamphetamine count that is not included in
the cannabis figures. [@1]

Palash thana police arrested four people in their own operation, one on a GR
warrant, one on a CR warrant and two under section 34 of the Police Act, and
sent them before the court. [@1] In a joint midday operation by the district
Detective Branch and Shibpur Model Thana police, officers arrested Md Shafiqul
Islam, 40, named in a Shibpur extortion case, and Hadikul Islam, a fugitive
sentenced to five years by a Kushtia court. [@1] District police said operations
of this kind and other anti-crime activity would go on to keep law and order
normal in Narsingdi, eliminate drugs and bring offenders under the law. [@1]
