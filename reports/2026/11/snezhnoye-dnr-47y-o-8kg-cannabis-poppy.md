Police in the Snezhnoye urban district of the Donetsk People's Republic searched
the home of a 47-year-old previously convicted man and seized about 8 kg of
drugs, including cannabis and poppy. [@2] Officers found more than 50 dried
cannabis plants, seven containers with their fragments and a ground substance,
and nearly 150 packets of cannabis. [@2] They also seized dried poppy fragments
containing narcotically active alkaloids. [@2]

The man was detained under article 91 of the Criminal Procedure Code. [@2] A
criminal case was opened against him for attempted unlawful sale of narcotics in
a large size, under part 3 of article 30 and part 4 of article 228.1 of the
Criminal Code. [@2] He faces up to 20 years of imprisonment with a fine of up to
one million rubles. [@2]

Police were checking his possible links with other participants in the illegal
drug trade in the DNR. [@1] Criminal investigation officers of the Snezhnoye
Interior Ministry division shut down the man's activity, the DNR Interior
Ministry said. [@1]
