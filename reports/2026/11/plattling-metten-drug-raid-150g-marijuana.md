Criminal police searched two flats in Metten and Plattling in the early hours of
10 November 2026 under several court-issued search warrants. [@1] The searches
were aimed at two 27-year-old German men, one from Metten and one from
Plattling, suspected of illegal drug dealing. [@1] In the Metten flat officers
found a small quantity of marijuana, various doping substances and drug-use
accessories, and the 27-year-old resident was released after the police measures
ended. [@1]

In the Plattling flat police found about three kilograms of amphetamine, 80
grams of cocaine and 150 grams of marijuana alongside packaging material. [@1]
The 27-year-old tenant of the Plattling flat was arrested provisionally in the
Munich area. [@1] At a court hearing on Wednesday, a judge issued the arrest
warrant requested by the Deggendorf public prosecutor's office against the
Plattling man and it was executed. [@1] The Plattling suspect is now held in a
Bavarian prison. [@1]

Officers of the Kriminalpolizeistation Deggendorf carried out the searches in
the two towns. [@3,4] The two suspects were suspected of possessing narcotics and
trading in them. [@2] Police found about three kilograms of amphetamine, about
80 grams of cocaine and about 150 grams of marijuana in the Plattling flat. [@2]
The Metten suspect was released once the police measures were concluded. [@2]
The warrant against the Plattling man was applied for by the Deggendorf
prosecutor's office and executed at the judicial presentation, after which he
was taken to a Bavarian prison. [@2] The raid was reported from a press release
of the Polizeipräsidium Niederbayern. [@2] The searches concerned flats in
Metten and Plattling in the district of Deggendorf. [@3] The Plattling suspect
was arrested provisionally near Munich before being remanded in custody. [@3] At
the judicial presentation on Wednesday the court issued the warrant requested by
the Deggendorf prosecutor's office and the man was taken to a Bavarian prison.
[@3] The Passauer Neue Presse published a police photograph of the drugs and
packaging material seized in the Plattling flat. [@3]
