Płock police detained a 37-year-old city resident. [@1,3,4,5,6] The detention took place
on 2 November 2026. [@2,3,4,5,6] Officers of the municipal police headquarters'
life-and-health crime unit conducted operational work concerning a man suspected
of possessing drugs. [@1] Police established his whereabouts and carried out the
detention. [@1]

A data check showed the man was wanted by the justice system. [@1] He was to
serve a five-month prison term. [@2,3,4,5,6] He was taken to prison after the new
arrest. [@2]

During the intervention officers found nearly 40 grams of marijuana, about 0.04
kg, on him. [@1,3,4,5,6] He was charged the same day with possession of intoxicants, the
Polish legal term for drugs, and admitted guilt. [@2,3,4,5,6] Police said he now faces
up to three more years in prison for the possession charge. [@2,4,6]

Police said the detained 37-year-old had been convicted many times before for
various offences. [@1] Police spokeswoman Monika Jakubowska reported that he
faces three more years in prison. [@1]
