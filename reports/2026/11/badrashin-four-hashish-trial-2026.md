The Giza Criminal Court on Monday heard the trial of four defendants in case No.
19560 of 2025 for Badrashin felonies on charges of drug trafficking and
unlicensed possession of weapons and ammunition. [@1] Prosecutors say the four
were seized on 10 December 2025 in the Badrashin district. [@1]

Officers found about 15 kilograms of hashish divided into four exhibits: 70
pieces weighing 7 kilograms, 30 pieces weighing 3 kilograms, and two further
exhibits weighing 2.3 and 2.7 kilograms. [@1] Alongside the hashish, officers
found a quantity of a narcotic phenethylamine derivative and a sensitive digital
scale. [@1] The fourth defendant allegedly held two 12-gauge shotgun firearms
and ammunition, while the first defendant allegedly held a shotgun and three
cartridges. [@1] The prosecution also attributes to the second defendant
possession of the same weapon and ammunition held by the first defendant. [@1]

Three General Administration for Drug Control officers said they arrested the
defendants under prosecution permission with the drugs and firearms, stating the
drugs were meant for trafficking and the weapons for protecting that activity.
[@1] Forensic reports confirmed the seized weapons were functional, and
laboratory examination found hashish essence and phenethylamine derivatives
listed in Schedule 1 of the drug-control law. [@1] Prosecutors charged the
defendants with trafficking in hashish essence and phenethylamine derivatives
and with drug use, plus possession of unrifled firearms and ammunition without a
licence. [@1]

On Monday, the Giza Criminal Court sentenced the four defendants in case No. 19560 of 2025 for Badrashin felonies to 15 years of rigorous imprisonment. [@2] The court imposed the sentence for drug trafficking and possession of unlicensed firearms and ammunition. [@2]
