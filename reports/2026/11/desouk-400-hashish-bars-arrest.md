Two men were arrested in Kafr El-Sheikh governorate, Egypt, carrying 400 bars of
hashish before they could distribute them. [@1] The General Administration for
Drug Control in West Delta made the arrest in coordination with detectives from
Desouk police station. [@1] Police described one of the men as a registered
dangerous offender. [@1]

The operation followed information received by Major Ahmed El-Demerdash, an
inspector with the West Delta drug administration, that the two suspects, aged
25 and 23 and resident in Kafr El-Zayat district in Gharbia governorate, were
dealing hashish and travelling to Desouk to distribute it. [@1] A check of their
security records showed the first suspect had previously been accused in two
drug cases. [@1] Officers set an ambush and arrested the pair in a village on
the boundary between Desouk district and Gharbia governorate. [@2] With them
police found 400 hashish bars, a sum of money and a motorcycle. [@1]

Questioned, the two admitted possessing the hashish to trade and distribute to
customers, and said the money was sales proceeds and the motorcycle was used in
their illegal activity. [@1] A police report was filed and the Public
Prosecution was notified to take over the investigation. [@1]
