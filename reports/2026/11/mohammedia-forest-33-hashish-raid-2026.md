On the morning of Tuesday 3 November 2026, security forces mounted an intensive
deployment in Forest 33 near Mohammedia after two young men were found dead.
[@2] The two had been shot with a hunting rifle, in an incident that raised
suspicions about links to drug-promotion activity in the area. [@2] Bouznika
judicial investigators attributed the double killing to two men described as
members of dealer Er-Rifi's gang, suspected of killing the youths and mutilating
their bodies. [@1] The two suspects were placed under custody on prosecution
orders while the search continued for the rest of the gang. [@1] The wider sweep
had by that point detained about five people suspected of involvement in drug
promotion. [@2]

Gendarmerie officers backed by Auxiliary Forces cordoned the entrances and
tracks into Forest 33 to closely monitor points suspected of use by
drug-trafficking networks. [@2] Officers raided many makeshift dens built of
wood and plastic sheeting that had been used to sell and hide hashish and for
other unlawful activity away from view. [@1] The operation demolished a number
of the wood-and-plastic shelters suspected of serving as hideouts and venues for
unlawful activity. [@2] Officers seized what was described as important
quantities of kif and shira, cannabis products, alongside hallucinogenic pills.
[@2] Searches also recovered suspect motorbikes and cars, some believed to have
come from thefts. [@2]

Forest 33, also called Al Massira forest, lies near Mohammedia. [@1] Combing
operations and inquiries were expected to continue to establish how the two
youths were killed, verify their alleged link to trafficking networks and assign
legal responsibility. [@2]
