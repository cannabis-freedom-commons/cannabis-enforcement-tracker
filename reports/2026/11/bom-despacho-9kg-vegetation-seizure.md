Military Police officers seized about 9 kilograms of a marijuana-like substance
on the morning of Monday, 2 November, in the Vila Gontijo neighbourhood of Bom
Despacho, in the Center-West of Minas Gerais. [@1] The drugs had been hidden in
an area of vegetation and were located after the officers received information
pointing to the site. [@1] Acting on information about drugs possibly hidden
there, the officers travelled to the indicated area and carried out searches.
[@1]

During that search they found nine blocks of marijuana-like substance, each
weighing about 1 kilogram, making a total of about 9 kilograms. [@1] Officers
seized all of the material they found at the site. [@1]

After the seizure, the officers continued inquiries with the stated aim of
identifying the people responsible for the drugs. [@1] The police presented the
seizure as part of their ongoing anti-drug-trafficking actions in the
municipality of Bom Despacho. [@1] The account names no arrests and identifies
no suspects, saying only that inquiries to find those responsible continued.
[@1]
