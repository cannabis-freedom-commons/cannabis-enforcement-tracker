In July 2026, drug-control officers from the Interior Ministry's Volzhsky
district department received information about the possible involvement of a
43-year-old local resident in illegal trafficking of banned substances. [@1]
Officers visited the man and inspected his home. [@1] Police detained the
homeowner after the inspection. [@1] The man told police that he uses banned
substances and had therefore cultivated cannabis and prepared it for his own
use. [@1] He said he had not intended to sell the marijuana. [@1]

Officers found glass jars filled with dried plant parts inside his dwelling.
[@1] A forensic examination later identified the material as marijuana with a
total weight of 170.74 grams. [@1] Investigators opened a criminal case against
him for illegal acquisition and storage of narcotics without intent to sell,
committed on a large scale. [@1]

The investigation has now been completed and the case materials have been sent
to court, as reported by the Samara regional police headquarters on 4 November.
[@1]
