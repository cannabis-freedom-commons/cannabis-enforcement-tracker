Municipal police in the capital of San Luis Potosí detained two young men in the
Julián Carrillo and El Oasis neighbourhoods for possession of drugs. [@1]
Officers identified the man held in Julián Carrillo as Oscar Eduardo “N”, aged
26. [@1] Officers identified the man held in El Oasis as Fernando “N”, aged 26.
[@1] Both men were taken to Civic Justice for medical certification and then to
the State Attorney General's Office on health-related offences. [@1]

The first stop came during preventive patrols on Lanzagorta street in Julián
Carrillo. [@1] Police said officers there saw a man inhaling intoxicants through
an object. [@1] A preventive search found a total of ten Ziploc-type bags
containing cristal. [@1] The second stop came on Oasis street in the same-named
neighbourhood after officers saw a man drinking alcohol in public. [@1] Police
said the man took an evasive attitude and tried to flee running. [@1] Officers
caught him metres ahead and saw him trying to get rid of an object. [@1] A
subsequent search found a package of marijuana weighing 180 grams, the cannabis
quantity recorded here. [@1]

The municipal force said both detentions resulted from the BOMI operation with
the 12th Military Zone and the National Guard. [@1] It described the actions as
preventive patrols carried out with other security agencies. [@1]
