Sûreté du Québec officers searched a residence on the route de St-Philippe in
Val-d'Or on 11 November 2026 at the end of a drug-trafficking investigation.
[@2] A 27-year-old man was arrested during the search. [@1] Mon Abitibi
identified the arrested man as Kevin St-Pierre. [@2]

Officers seized cocaine, cannabis and an imitation firearm during the search.
[@1]

He made a first court appearance on charges including trafficking, possession
for the purpose of trafficking, possession of a weapon for a dangerous purpose
and conspiracy to traffic drugs. [@1] He also faces accusations of breaching
release-order conditions on curfew, weapons and drug possession or consumption.
[@1] He remained detained after that appearance. [@1]
