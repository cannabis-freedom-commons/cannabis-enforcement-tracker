Gendarmerie officers stopped a private car carrying a suspect identified only as
R.A. at the control point at Ikisu village on the Gumushane-Trabzon highway.
[@1] Intelligence work had indicated that R.A., travelling by private car on the
highway, would bring narcotic drugs into Gumushane province across its borders.
[@1] On the public prosecutor's instructions, officers opened a judicial
investigation against R.A. for using and possessing narcotic or stimulant drugs
under Article 191 of the Turkish Penal Code. [@1]

Officers searching R.A. and the car found 27 grams of cannabis described in the
reports as kubar esrar. [@2] The reports say the 27 grams were intended to be
brought into the city and were taken into safekeeping. [@1]

The operation was carried out by teams of the Narcotic Crimes Branch Directorate
and the Merkez District Gendarmerie Command under the Gumushane Provincial
Gendarmerie Command. [@1] The provincial command described the work as part of
continuous field efforts to prevent the manufacture, trade and use of drugs
across the province. [@1] After evaluating the intelligence, the teams increased
security measures on the identified routes before stopping the car at Ikisu.
[@1]
