Jamalpur Sadar police arrested 50-year-old Khokon Bhutro with 2 kg of ganja,
described in the report as ganja, in the Ranaganj Bazar area of Jamalpur Sadar
upazila. [@1] The report says the operation was carried out at midday on
Wednesday 4 November 2026 on the basis of secret information received by police.
[@1] Police said a police team led by Sadar police station sub-inspector Rassel,
acting under the direction of officer-in-charge Nazmus Sakib, conducted a
special operation in the Ranaganj Bazar area. [@1] Police said the team searched
the area during the special operation and caught Bhutro red-handed with 2 kg of
ganja. [@1]

Bhutro is the son of the late Azizur Rahman of the Shutki Potti area of Jamalpur
town. [@1] Officer-in-charge Nazmus Sakib confirmed the incident and said a
regular case has been filed against the detained man under the Narcotics Control
Act and that he will be produced in court after completion of the legal process.
[@1] Sakib also said police operations against drugs will continue. [@1]
