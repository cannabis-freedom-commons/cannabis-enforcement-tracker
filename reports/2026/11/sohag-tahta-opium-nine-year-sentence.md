The Sohag Criminal Court sentenced a 33-year-old unemployed man to six years of
aggravated imprisonment and a 100,000-pound fine for trafficking shabu,
methamphetamine essence and opium. [@1] The same ruling gave him a further three
years of aggravated imprisonment for consuming narcotic substances in the Tahta
police district. [@1] The panel was headed by Judge Hafni Abdel Fattah, with
judges Khaled Mohamed El-Sheikh and Ahmed Ali Saqr, and court clerks Salah
Tammam and Badr El-Din Kamal. [@1]

The case dates to 2026 in the Tahta police district, when detectives were told
the man was trafficking shabu, methamphetamine essence and opium, and a secret
source confirmed the tip. [@1] He was arrested with a quantity of drugs prepared
for sale and another quantity for his own use. [@1]

Confronted by officers, he admitted trafficking drugs and consuming opium, and
was referred to the criminal court, which issued the ruling. [@1]
