Vinnytsia police detained a man there on 3 November 2026 after he chose a
20-year-old woman as his victim. [@1] Police identified him as a 43-year-old
Vinnytsia resident who stole 450 dollars from the woman while she was riding a
trolleybus. [@1] After getting off the trolleybus, the woman noticed that her
money was missing and contacted law enforcement. [@2] Police identified and
detained the suspected thief within a few hours by checking the woman's route
and the passengers who had been on the trolleybus. [@2] The suspect had
previously been convicted of similar crimes. [@2]

During investigative actions, officers recovered the stolen money and seized
cannabis and methadone from him, according to police. [@1] He is being held in a
temporary detention facility while investigators prepare a notice of suspicion
and consider a preventive measure. [@2]

The theft case is being investigated under Part 4 of Article 185 of Ukraine's
Criminal Code, which carries a prison term of up to eight years. [@2]
