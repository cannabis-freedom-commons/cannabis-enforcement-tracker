25-year-old Daniel Kelleher of 44 Mourne Avenue, The Glen, Cork pleaded guilty
at Cork Circuit Criminal Court to possession of cannabis for sale or supply.
[@1] The charge relates to January 22 at Arderrin Way, The Glen, Cork. [@1] His
guilty plea vacated a trial date of November 23. [@1] Judge Helen Boyle remanded
him in custody until February 19, 2027 for sentencing. [@1] Defence senior
counsel asked for a probation report before sentencing. [@1]

Gardai watched Kelleher arrive at Imaal Court in a Volkswagen Passat and carry a
cardboard box with another person to Arderrin Way. [@1] Cardboard boxes were
moved into a grey Toyota Auris parked in the driveway at Arderrin Way. [@1] A
search of the Toyota Auris found a rubbish bag holding 273 kilograms of cannabis
with a street value of 546,000 euros. [@2] The cannabis was packed in
vacuum-packs, with vacuum-packed packaging seen in a box on the passenger seat.
[@1] Forensic Science Ireland has provided a certificate of analysis documenting
the weight of the cannabis seized. [@1]

Earlier in the same surveillance, gardai stopped a Volkswagen Golf at North
Monastery Road and found cannabis worth 62,000 euros linked to another man. [@1]
The charge specifies a value over 13,000 euros, the threshold allowing a minimum
10-year sentence unless there are exceptional circumstances. [@1] Detective
Garda Rory Fogarty arrested and charged Kelleher and objected to bail. [@2] An
earlier hearing ordered a book of evidence to be served on October 12 as the
case moved toward trial. [@3]
