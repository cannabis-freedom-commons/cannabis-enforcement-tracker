The investigative department of the Vyselkovsky district police completed a
criminal case against a 52-year-old local man accused under part 2 of article
228 of the Russian Criminal Code. [@2] The case with an approved indictment was
sent to court for a hearing on the merits. [@2] The offence carries a maximum
penalty of up to 10 years in prison. [@2] The man was additionally held
administratively liable for unlawfully cultivating banned plants under article
10.5.1 of the Administrative Code. [@2]

Investigators say the man found wild cannabis on a riverbank, collected its
seeds and sowed them on land by his home. [@2] He grew the plants on his own
plot and later made marijuana from the harvest for personal use. [@1] Officers
seized 800 grams of the banned substance from the man. [@1] Police say the man
had planted the collected seeds on his own plot after finding the wild plants by
the river. [@1]

The criminal case was opened under part 2 of article 228 of the Criminal Code
and has been referred to court. [@1] The regional Interior Ministry press
service for Krasnodar Krai is named as the source of the police account. [@1]
