The Guardia Civil arrested five people in a drug operation in Jimena and in El
Secadero, a hamlet of Casares in Málaga province. [@1] The operation was
directed against the retail supply of hashish and cocaine. [@1] Officers
deployed from first thing in the morning on Wednesday 4 November 2026. [@1]

About 70 agents of the Guardia Civil, the instituto armado, took part. [@1] The
deployment included three searches of separate properties. [@1] Two of the
searches were in El Secadero and the remaining one in Jimena. [@1] The action
formed part of an investigation into drug trafficking in the area. [@1]

More details on what the searches produced and on the situation of the detainees
had not yet been released. [@1]
