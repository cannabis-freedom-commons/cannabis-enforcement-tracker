Police in Adilabad district carried out an early-morning drive against ganja and
other intoxicants, testing 90 people and finding 57 who had used ganja, under
Superintendent of Police Akhil Mahajan. [@1] From 5 am, teams searched the homes
of suspects in the Adilabad and Utnoor police sub-divisions. [@2] Officers said
the goal was a drug-free district. [@2]

Police said that in the previous year the district registered ganja cases
against 221 people and seized 23 kg of ganja worth Rs 1.68 crore. [@1] Officers
also identified and seized 1,632 ganja plants grown illegally in fields in the
district. [@1] Officers filed cases against more than 100 farmers for illegal
cultivation and suspended Rythu Bharosa welfare funds on about 250 acres. [@1]
