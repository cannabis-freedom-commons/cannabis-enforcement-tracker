On the night of 9 to 10 November, patrol officers stopped a Skoda Octavia for a
road check on ul. Bolesława Chrobrego in Szamotuły. [@1] The 48-year-old driver
gave officers false personal details and only admitted it after they found his
identity card with his photograph in the car. [@1] He was fined for misleading
officers about his identity. [@1] A check showed he held an active court ban on
driving and should not have been behind the wheel. [@1]

Officers found prohibited substances in the car attributed in preliminary
findings to the 48-year-old and his 28-year-old female passenger. [@1] Police
secured marijuana and more than 90 grams of mephedrone. [@1] Radio Poznań
reported that marijuana and more than 90 grams of mephedrone were found during
the car check. [@2]

After evidence was secured, both people were questioned; the man was charged
with driving despite the ban and drug possession, and the woman with drug
possession. [@3] Two people were detained and charged over drug possession. [@4]
Officers took blood from the driver to check whether he had been under the
influence of drugs, with results pending. [@1]
