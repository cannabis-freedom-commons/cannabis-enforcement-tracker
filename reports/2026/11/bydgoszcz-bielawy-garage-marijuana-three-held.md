Police in Bydgoszcz detained three men aged 17, 18 and 19. [@2] The detainees
were two Bydgoszcz residents aged 18 and 19 and a 17-year-old from Grudziądz,
described as a football hooligan, all taken to a detention facility. [@1] The
case was handled by the Bydgoszcz city police's drug and football-hooligan crime
unit. [@1] The trail led the unit to a garage used by the 18-year-old Bydgoszcz
resident on the Bielawy housing estate. [@1] They detained him as he poured the
contents of a shopping bag into other bags. [@1] Two other men stood nearby as
lookouts; when police cars arrived they got into the car the three had come in
and tried to drive off. [@1] Both were detained after a short chase. [@1]

A search of the garage found plastic containers, plastic and vacuum bags and
other packaging holding crystal and dried plant material. [@1] Police said
preliminary tests identified more than 1.3 kilograms of marijuana, alongside
more than 9.8 kilograms of 2C-B and more than 300 grams of cocaine. [@1] Police
put the total weight of all the drugs at nearly 11.5 kilograms and their
black-market value at about 600,000 złoty. [@1] The car search found an
unlicensed firearm and 48 rounds of ammunition. [@1] Police also secured a
significant amount of cash in the 18-year-old's flat and in the car. [@1]

All three were charged with possessing a firearm without the required permit and
possessing significant quantities of drugs. [@1] At the request of police and
the prosecutor, a court ordered them held in pre-trial detention for three
months. [@1] They face up to 10 years in prison. [@1]
