Police on Friday arrested Sheikh Khalil, described as a habitual ganja
trafficker, and sent him directly to Jabalpur Central Jail. [@1] Police said it
was the first time the district had taken action under the strict PIT NDPS Act.
[@1] Rahatgaon station officer Manoj Dubey said the arrested accused is
56-year-old Sheikh Khalil, son of Sheikh Rashid, who lives in Mohanpur village.
[@1] Khalil had been involved in illegal ganja supply in the area for several
years. [@1] The action was reported from Rahatgaon in Harda district. [@1]

About a year and a half ago, police had caught him red-handed with a ganja
consignment near Temagaon. [@1] After serving jail time in that case, he came
out on bail only 15 days earlier and immediately became active in the drug trade
again. [@1] Given his continuing criminal activity, police prepared a proposal
for action against him under the PIT NDPS Act. [@1] Police records show eight
separate narcotics-trafficking cases were already registered against Sheikh
Khalil. [@1]
