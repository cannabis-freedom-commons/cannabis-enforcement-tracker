A mobile court in Joka area of Doulatpur upazila, Manikganj district, sentenced
a 30-year-old man to one month of simple imprisonment and a 100-taka fine for
consuming drugs and for creating social disorder by that consumption. [@1] The
court also seized 5 grams of ganja, the local term for cannabis, that officers
recovered from the man's own house. [@1]

The drive was run on Sunday 8 November 2026 by the district office of the
Department of Narcotics Control, the national anti-drug agency, in Joka under
Doulatpur upazila. [@1] A mobile-court case was filed for drug consumption and
for the social disorder caused by it, and the court ordered the 5 grams of ganja
confiscated. [@1]

One government statement quoted said such drives would continue to stop drug
abuse and build a drug-free society, and asked for public cooperation. [@1]
