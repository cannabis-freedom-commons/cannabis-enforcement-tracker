Police in the Philippines' CALABARZON region arrested 27 drug suspects in 19
anti-illegal drug operations during a 24-hour anti-criminality campaign. [@1]
The operations seized about 65.56 grams of suspected shabu, the local name for
crystal methamphetamine, and 1.54 grams of marijuana. [@2]

PRO 4A put the estimated value of the seized illegal drugs at 608,530.40 pesos,
without giving a separate figure for the cannabis. [@1]

The campaign ran from 6 a.m. on 8 November to 6 a.m. on 9 November 2026. [@1]
Over the same 24 hours officers arrested 153 wanted persons across the region,
45 of them classified as most wanted. [@1] The campaign covered the provinces of
Cavite, Laguna, Batangas, Rizal and Quezon. [@2]

In the same period police arrested 311 people in 150 illegal gambling operations
and 1,277 people were fined for local ordinance violations. [@1] Regional
director Brig. Gen. Dominic L. Baccay said the operations formed part of the
regional command's B.E.S.T. initiative, which stands for Be Visible, Engage
Immediately, Serve Together, and Transform Trust. [@1]
