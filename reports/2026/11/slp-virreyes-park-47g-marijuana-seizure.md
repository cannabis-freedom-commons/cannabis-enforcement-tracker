Officers of the capital's Guardia Municipal seized marijuana that had been
abandoned in the Juan Ruiz de Alarcón park in colonia Virreyes. [@1] The seizure
came during preventive security and surveillance patrols in the city's western
sector. [@2] Near the place popularly known as the Jardín del Salesiano,
officers detected an individual allegedly consuming the drug, who fled on seeing
the police and left behind a mariconera-style bag. [@1]

Inside the bag officers found several plastic bags containing dry green herb
with characteristics similar to marijuana. [@1] The seized herb weighed
approximately 47.3 grams. [@1]

Officers secured and packaged the drug to present it as evidence to the State
Attorney General's Office, which is to continue the corresponding
investigations. [@1] The officers who acted were agents of the capital's Guardia
Municipal, part of the municipal Secretariat of Security and Citizen Protection.
[@3] A second outlet described the same packaging of the drug for presentation
to the state prosecutor's office, which continues the investigations. [@4]
