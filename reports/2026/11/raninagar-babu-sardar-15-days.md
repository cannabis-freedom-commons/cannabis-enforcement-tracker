A mobile court in Raninagar in Naogaon sentenced 27-year-old Babu Sardar to 15
days of simple imprisonment for allegedly consuming cannabis. [@1] The sentence
was imposed around noon on Monday, 2 November, during an operation in
Madhyarajapur village in the same upazila. [@1] Mobile-court judge and Raninagar
Upazila Nirbahi Officer Rakibul Hasan passed the sentence. [@1]

Officials said the midday operation in Madhyarajapur followed a secret tip-off
about the village. [@1] During the operation officers caught Sardar while he was
consuming ganja, the local term for cannabis. [@1] The mobile court then gave
him 15 days of simple imprisonment through summary proceedings. [@1] Sardar is
the son of Ansar Sardar and a resident of the same Madhyarajapur village. [@1]
Madhyarajapur village lies within Raninagar Upazila where the mobile court
conducted the drive. [@1]

Naogaon Department of Narcotics Control inspector Saiful Islam was present
during the operation. [@1] Deputy administrative officer Raihan Ali of the
Upazila Nirbahi Officer’s office was also present during the operation. [@1]
