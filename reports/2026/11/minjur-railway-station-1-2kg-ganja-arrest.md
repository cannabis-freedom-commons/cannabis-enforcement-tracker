Police in Minjur, Thiruvallur district, arrested Vinoth, 25, on 11 November 2026
on suspicion of selling cannabis near the Minjur railway station. [@1] Officers
on patrol near the station stopped a man they found suspicious and questioned
him. [@1] The questioning established that he was Vinoth, 25, of Nandiyambakkam,
and that he had been selling cannabis. [@1]

Police arrested him and seized 1.200 kg of ganja, the local term for cannabis,
in packets from him. [@1]

Police in the Minjur area said they were maintaining intensive surveillance to
stop the illegal sale of cannabis. [@1]
