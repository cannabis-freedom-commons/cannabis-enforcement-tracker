Nineteen defendants are being tried at Liège's criminal court over a cocaine and
cannabis network that ran in the city for several years. [@1] Investigators
described the network as an Albanian criminal organisation whose leader, called
Totila in court, was said to be a corpulent bald man of Albanian origin. [@1]
The organisation was allegedly run from a ground-floor restaurant, Le Bella Baro
da Vito, at 10 rue Grétry in Liège. [@2]

On 7 March 2025 Miri's 12-year-old stepson called police, saying he had found
drugs at his home in Chaudfontaine and was afraid. [@1] At that house police
found 4,193 kg of cannabis hidden in black bags in the cellar. [@1] A March 2025
L'Avenir report on the same discovery said officers found 4 kg of cannabis and
detained the boy's parents. [@4]

On 31 March 2025 police dismantled a cannabis plantation on the ground floor of
the pizzeria, and a man called Marko was caught after trying to flee. [@1]
Investigators seized 715 cannabis plants there and estimated that the plantation
had generated at least 80,000 euros, with an earlier harvest already taken. [@1]
On 22 April 2025, more than 80 officers carried out 16 search warrants issued by
the investigating judge in the Liège area. [@3]

At the trial, the defendants presented as the most involved denied the facts,
with Totila telling the court that he had done nothing wrong and had only helped
people. [@1] Two further hearings were scheduled for the prosecution's
submissions and the defence pleadings, so no verdict has been given. [@1]
