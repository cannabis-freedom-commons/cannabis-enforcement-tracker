Peru's National Police (PNP) destroyed 8,983 marijuana seedlings and seized six
kilograms of the substance in a process of drying during an operation in Huaura
province, Lima, the Interior Ministry (Mininter) reported. [@1] The illegal
plantation was discovered during a police intervention in the Llacas ravine, in
the peasant community of Parán, Leoncio Prado district, where officers
identified four cultivation plots covering 4,000 square metres. [@2] The plants
were between 80 centimetres and 1.20 metres tall. [@1]

Police investigations link the illegal crops to the criminal organisation known
as 'Los Paisas de Huamboy', and work continues to identify and establish the
responsibility of those involved. [@1] After the count and the extraction of
samples, and in coordination with the Public Ministry, the agents burned the
8,983 plants at the site of the intervention. [@2] Officers also seized six
kilograms of marijuana being dried, known as 'tipo paleta', distributed in two
strings inside a rudimentary structure of poles and plastic. [@1] Four marijuana
plants, one from each plot, were extracted for forensic analysis, and the
samples and the seized drug were taken to a specialised unit. [@1]

The operation was carried out by agents of the Brigada Especial contra el
Crimen, part of the Criminal Investigation Division (Divincri) of the Lima Norte
police region. [@1]
