On 5 November 2026 a 34-year-old delivery driver was tried at the court in La
Roche-sur-Yon for driving after testing positive for drugs. [@1] Gendarmes
stopped the company vehicle he was driving at Montaigu on 22 February 2025. [@1]
The driver told the court he had smoked a joint at a birthday party three weeks
before he was checked. [@1]

He was a repeat offender, having already been convicted of similar offences, and
told the court that was in the past and he had stopped. [@1] The public
prosecutor asked for 100 day-fines of 4 euros each, a total of 400 euros. [@1]
The prosecutor also sought cancellation of his driving licence with a four-month
ban on taking the test again. [@1] The court followed the prosecutor's
submissions in full, with the day-fine sentence and licence withdrawal, and his
lawyer said he had stopped using cannabis and understood the charges. [@1]

The prosecutor told the court that road-safety figures in Vendée were
catastrophic and that many accidents stemmed from driving under alcohol or
drugs. [@1]
