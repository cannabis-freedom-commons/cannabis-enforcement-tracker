Police in Kathmandu Valley registered 444 drug-related cases and arrested 378
people in the first three months of the fiscal year, from mid-July to
mid-October, the Kathmandu Valley Police Office in Ranipokhari reported. [@1]
The account does not give the weight of cannabis seized in the quarter, and
reports it alongside heroin, pollen and nearly 296,000 ampoules and tablets of
other drugs. [@1]

The drug arrests formed one part of a wider Valley law-and-order campaign whose
headline result was the arrest of 1,162 absconding convicts. [@1]
