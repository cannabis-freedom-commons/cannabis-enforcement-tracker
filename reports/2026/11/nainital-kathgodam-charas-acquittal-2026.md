Munawwar Hussain was acquitted by Nainital's special NDPS court in a nearly
seven-year-old case involving charas, a cannabis resin. [@1] Additional District
and Sessions Judge Sanjeev Kumar delivered the verdict on 3 November. [@1] The
court acquitted Hussain of the charge under sections 8/20 of the NDPS Act. [@1]
After acquitting him, the court cancelled his personal and bail bonds. [@3]

Police said they caught motorcycle riders Munawwar Hussain and Mohammed Kamil
during a check on Haidakhan Road in the Kathgodam police-station area on 12
February 2019. [@1] Police claimed charas was recovered from both men. [@1] A
second account put the claimed recovery at about 1 kilogram. [@2] Kamil died
during the trial, and proceedings against him were closed on 6 December 2022.
[@2]

The court found no general-diary entry in the records showing that the police
team had left the station. [@1] Recovery and arrest documents contradicted one
another; the investigator had not signed the checklist, and another document
bore the signature of an officer who was not present at the scene. [@1] No
independent witness was included at the time of the alleged recovery. [@1] The
court also considered failure to follow mandatory safeguards under section 50 of
the NDPS Act significant. [@1] It treated the recovery as doubtful and found the
prosecution had failed to prove the allegations beyond reasonable doubt. [@1]
