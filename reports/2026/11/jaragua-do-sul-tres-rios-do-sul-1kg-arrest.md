Military police arrested a 26-year-old man for drug trafficking on the night of
Thursday 5 November 2026 on Rua Ovídio Spezia in the Três Rios do Sul
neighbourhood of Jaraguá do Sul, Santa Catarina. [@1] The incident was recorded
at about 19h30. [@1]

The action began after a passer-by complained of a strong smell of marijuana
coming from a house. [@1] The man police approached handed over voluntarily
about 60 grams of marijuana divided into portions from his clothing pocket, and
one portion of cocaine kept in his wallet. [@1] Searches at the house found more
than 1 kg of marijuana stored in a rucksack and a precision scale. [@1]

Police said the man's wife was shown not to have known about the crime; she
stayed at the house with the couple's 5-month-old son. [@1] The man was taken to
the police station, and no charge or court decision was reported. [@1]
