North Maluku police's narcotics directorate, Ditresnarkoba Polda Malut, detained
a 30-year-old man identified as FP alias Ical in Gosoma village, Tobelo
district, North Halmahera regency. [@2] Police said the case began with a tip
about a suspicious parcel suspected of containing narcotics at one of the
delivery services. [@1] Officers of Unit 1, Subdit I of the directorate ran the
investigation that identified the parcel and then detained Ical as he carried
it. [@1]

The parcel held one black plastic wrap of ganja, the Indonesian word for
cannabis, weighing 55.26 grams gross, police said. [@1] Ical told officers he
had bought the cannabis through a Facebook account registered in Sukabumi, West
Java, for Rp 700,000. [@1] Police also seized a light-blue Oppo mobile phone
suspected of being used in the transaction. [@2]

The suspect and all the evidence were then taken to the North Maluku
Ditresnarkoba office for examination and further legal process. [@3] North
Maluku police spokesman Kombes Pol Hadi Poerwanto said the force would also
develop the investigation to uncover the supplier network and distribution
route. [@1]
