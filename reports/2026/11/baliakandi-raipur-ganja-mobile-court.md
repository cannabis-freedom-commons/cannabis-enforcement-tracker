Nur Mohammad Sheikh, 37, was detained with ganja, the local term for cannabis,
in Raipur in Baliakandi Upazila on 3 November 2026. [@1] A mobile court, an
executive magistrate-led court that tries offences caught on the spot, sentenced
him to three months of imprisonment without labour and a 500-taka fine under the
Narcotics Control Act, 2018. [@2] Upazila Nirbahi Officer and Executive
Magistrate Syed Murad Islam, the chief executive of the subdistrict, led the
mobile court and imposed the sentence. [@1]

Officers caught Sheikh red-handed after searching his body during an anti-drug
mobile-court drive in the Raipur area. [@1] The drive ran in the morning to
midday as part of separate Department of Narcotics Control operations in
Baliakandi, Rajbari District on the same day. [@1] Sheikh is the son of Siraj
Sheikh of Raipur. [@1]

The Department of Narcotics Control, the national anti-drug agency, said Sheikh
was sent to Rajbari District Jail after the mobile-court verdict. [@2] Members
of the department's Rajbari district office conducted the day's operations. [@2]
