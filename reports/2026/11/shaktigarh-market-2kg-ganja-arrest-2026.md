Police in Shaktigarh market, in West Bengal's Purba Bardhaman district, arrested
one man and seized more than 2 kg of ganja in a raid on the night before the
report was published. [@1] Officers acted on a tip-off and cordoned off areas
around Dalkhola and Shaktigarh before searching the main suspect. [@1] Along
with the ganja, police recovered an electronic weighing machine from the
suspect. [@1] The report says the machine indicated that goods were supplied
retail and wholesale from the spot. [@1]

The arrested man told police he had been in the trade for the past 8 to 10
years. [@1] He said he brought the ganja in through Dalkhola, a town on the
border with Bihar in Uttar Dinajpur district, and that consignments came via
Odisha. [@1] Initial inquiries found he bought ganja at about 18,000 rupees a
kilogram and sold it at higher prices for a profit in the local market. [@1]

Police said they had seized all the goods and were trying to identify the other
people in the network and its main leader. [@1] The report says investigators
suspect a larger drug-trafficking gang stands behind the trade. [@1]
