Onda police station officers in Bankura district arrested one man after
recovering about 6.35 kilograms of ganja, the term widely used in India for
cannabis, from his possession. [@1] The enforcement action followed secret
information received by the police, which prompted a special targeted
enforcement operation on Monday night in the Baliguma Road area of Onda. [@1]
During that special operation, officers stopped a motorbike rider moving through
the local Baliguma Road area and subjected him to a detailed personal search.
[@1] That search led to the recovery of about 6.35 kilograms of ganja from the
rider, which officers identified as the narcotic substance forming the basis of
the case. [@1]

Officers seized the recovered ganja after observing the required legal procedure
and formally arrested the motorbike rider as the accused in the case. [@1]
Following the seizure and arrest, police produced the accused before the Bankura
court to face further legal proceedings in the matter. [@1]
