Panipat district police's CIA-3 unit arrested a man from the Sector 13-17 area
of Panipat, Haryana, on 7 November 2026 and recovered 1.6 kg of ganja from him.
[@1] The arrested man was presented in court and remanded to five days of police
custody. [@1]

On the same day, teams of Panipat's Anti-Narcotics Cell and its dog squad ran a
special checking drive in the Samalkha area, searching slums, the area around
the railway crossing on Manana Road and the homes of people earlier involved in
drug trafficking. [@1] No drugs were found in the Samalkha drive, and no arrests
were reported from it. [@1]

District police urged residents to report suspected drug activity to police or
the Manas narcotics helpline 1933, promising to keep informants' identities
secret. [@1]
