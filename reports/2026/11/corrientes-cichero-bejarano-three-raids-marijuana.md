Four adults, two men and two women, were detained during three searches in the
Cichero and Bejarano neighbourhoods of Corrientes on 6 November 2026. [@2]

In the third search, at a house in barrio Bejarano near the Virgen de
Schoenstatt and San Miguel streets, officers found marijuana buds and seized 7.5
grams in total. [@1] The officers also seized 50,000 pesos in cash in Bejarano.
[@1] The Bejarano search ended without detentions, according to case sources.
[@1]

The searches were carried out by the General Directorate of Dangerous Drugs and
Organised Crime with the High Risk Police Division and the Canes dog unit, in an
investigation run with the prosecutor's office. [@3] In the two Cichero
searches, on Gobernador Gallino street and near Guido Spano street, officers
seized 69 doses of cocaine, a precision scale, phones and 283,500 pesos in cash.
[@1] The four detained people, all held in the Cichero searches, were placed at
the disposal of the Corrientes justice system under Argentina's drug law, Law
23,737. [@1]
