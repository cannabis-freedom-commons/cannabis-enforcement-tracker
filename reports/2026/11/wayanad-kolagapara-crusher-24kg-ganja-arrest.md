Police in Wayanad arrested a granite crusher unit manager described as a key
player in the local ganja trade together with his associate and seized 24.164 kg
of ganja from the manager's office near Sulthan Bathery. [@1] The prime accused
is 35-year-old Ebi Elias of Pelakkudy House, Puthankunnu, Makkutti, near Sulthan
Bathery, and his alleged associate is 35-year-old T R Nidhun, alias Puttu, of
Thekkumkattil House, Puthankunnu. [@1] Ebi was produced before the Judicial
First-Class Magistrate Court in Sulthan Bathery and remanded to judicial
custody. [@1]

According to a police communique issued on Tuesday, Ebi allegedly sourced ganja
from Odisha and distributed it through a network of local retailers. [@1] Police
said Ebi had earlier been arrested with more than 1 kg of ganja during a vehicle
inspection near Makkutti Bridge. [@1] His interrogation reportedly led
investigators to a larger stock concealed in his office at a granite crusher
unit in Kolagapara. [@1] A police team raided the crusher premises on Monday
evening and recovered 12 packets containing 24.164 kg of ganja concealed in a
trolley bag inside the manager's office. [@1] During a subsequent raid at
Nidhun's residence in Makkutti, police recovered another 7 grams of ganja. [@1]
Police said Ebi had maintained a network of retailers to distribute the
contraband brought from Odisha across Wayanad and that more arrests are likely
as the investigation progresses. [@1]

The operation was jointly conducted by special squads attached to the Sulthan
Bathery Deputy Superintendent of Police's office, the District Anti-Narcotics
Wing and the Sulthan Bathery police station. [@1] The operation was monitored by
Narcotics and Gender Justice Deputy Superintendent of Police T Manoharan and
Sulthan Bathery DySP M Abdul Karim, with Circle Inspector Biju Antony and other
personnel taking part. [@1]
