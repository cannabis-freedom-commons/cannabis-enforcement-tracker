Carabinieri arrested three young men in flagrante during personal and house
searches in an extraordinary anti-drug operation in Val di Susa. [@1] A
25-year-old resident of Giaveno was found with about 89 grams of hashish and
packaging material. [@1] The judicial authority placed him under house arrest.
[@1] In Bussoleno, two 19-year-olds were arrested jointly and ordered
transferred to the Lorusso e Cutugno prison in Turin. [@1] Three other men found
with small amounts for personal use were referred to the Prefecture of Turin,
the local government office. [@1]

At Bussoleno, officers seized 346 grams of hashish, alongside 17 grams of
cocaine, packaging material and 120 euros in cash described as proceeds of the
activity. [@1] The operation was run in the preceding hours by the Radiomobile
Section and the Borgone Susa station to counter drug trafficking and dealing.
[@1] In total, officers checked 17 vehicles and 21 people and reported seizing
more than 430 grams of drugs. [@1]

The measures were issued during the preliminary-investigation phase, and all
those under investigation retain the presumption of innocence until any final
conviction. [@1]
