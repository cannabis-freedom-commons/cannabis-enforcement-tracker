Carabineros officers and the Maipú local prosecutor's office broke up a drug
sales point operating inside the Población Los Héroes housing estate in Maipú
and detained two people. [@1] The investigation began with neighbours'
complaints about drug sales in a building in the area, and officers of the
Carabineros OS7 unit then carried out surveillance and tailing over several
days. [@1] With the evidence gathered, the Juzgado de Garantía authorized a
search warrant, and the raid was carried out with officers from Control de Orden
Público (COP) and the GOPE special operations group, who detained the main
suspect and a second person. [@1]

During the search officers seized more than two kilos of coca paste, 970 grams
of cocaine hydrochloride and 600 grams of marijuana. [@1] They also found
122,000 pesos in cash, mostly in low-denomination notes, along with scales and
strainers used to measure out drugs, and a firearm magazine with unspent
ammunition. [@1]

Captain María Belén Galaz of the Carabineros OS7 unit said the raid established
a home dedicated to microtrafficking, the local retail sale of drugs, and that
the two Chilean detainees were held with the drugs and the money presumed to be
their proceeds. [@1] The two detainees were handed to the Public Prosecutor's
Office for a detention control hearing over their alleged responsibility for
offences under the Drugs Law and the Firearms Control Law. [@1]
