Cairo security forces arrested three people in the Marg police station district,
a district in eastern Cairo, on suspicion of trading in narcotic drugs and using
the area as a base for their activity in a security campaign. [@1]

Marg investigations officer Hossam Reda received a tip that three people were
trading in drugs and had made the station district a den for the activity. [@1]
Alaa Beshendy, director of the Cairo criminal investigation department, was
notified, and his deputy Taha Fouda ordered investigations and arrests to
proceed quickly. [@1]

After legal procedures were completed, investigations officer Asem Ashraf
detained the three suspects. [@1] Officers found a large amount of hashish and
tramadol pills with the suspects, along with a shotgun and a single-barrel
shotgun, cash and mobile phones. [@1]

Confronted before Marg police station chief Karim Matawe, the suspects admitted
holding the drugs for trade and said the cash was proceeds of their sales. [@1]
Police filed the incident report, took legal measures against the suspects and
notified the Public Prosecution, which took over the investigation. [@1]
