Officers and staff of the National Guard post in Douar Hicher arrested three
people suspected of involvement in drug possession and trafficking cases after
consulting the public prosecution. [@1] Field investigations found that two of
those arrested were considered dangerous and were wanted by security and
judicial units, with the first sought in 11 search notices and the second in 8.
[@1]

The operation seized a significant quantity of cannabis and the drug known as
Irica, along with an amount of money suspected to be proceeds of the criminal
activity and mobile phones. [@1] Officers also seized bladed weapons and a
carbine-type firearm. [@1]
