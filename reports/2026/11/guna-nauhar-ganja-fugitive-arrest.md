Bhind's Mehgaon police arrested Satyadev, also called Sarvesh and Bajrangdas
Baba, after 21 years evading an NDPS case. [@1,3] Officers caught the
permanent-warrant holder in an ashram in Village Nauhar in Aron town, Guna
district, Madhya Pradesh. [@1,3] Satyadev, originally from Village Birkhadi, had
been living there disguised as a sadhu, a Hindu ascetic, to avoid arrest. [@1]

Mehgaon police had first arrested him with ganja, cannabis, at Gormi crossing on
26 August 2002. [@2,3] Police registered that case under Sections 8/20 of the NDPS
Act, India's drug law. [@2] After coming out on bail he fled during the court
hearing and did not appear again. [@2] The court issued a permanent warrant
against him on 7 June 2005. [@1,3]

On 2 November, with help from Bajrangarh police, officers cordoned off the
ashram. [@1,3] The ashram was then hosting a bhandara, a community feast, with a
large gathering present. [@1,3] Police waited patiently for the right moment and
arrested him as he tried to use the crowd to his advantage. [@1] He was to be
produced before the court on Tuesday. [@2]
