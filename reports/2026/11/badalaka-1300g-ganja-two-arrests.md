Badalaka thana police in Moulvibazar district arrested two listed drug dealers
in a counter-drug operation. [@1] The arrest was made on Wednesday evening from
the Daserbazar South Lhagati area. [@1] The two arrested were Abdul Kayum, 40,
son of late Falu Mia from Kaluota, Chabartala Union, Naicharnagar Upazila,
Brahmanbaria district, and Ali Hossain, 43, son of Abdul Hossain from Sanga,
Dasura Bazar Union, Biani Bazar thana, Sylhet district. [@1]

Police sources said that as part of the ongoing counter-drug operation, 2 listed
dealers were arrested from Daserbazar South Lhagati on Wednesday evening with 1
kg 300 grams of ganja. [@1] A case has been filed at Badalaka thana under
Narcotics Control Act, 2018, Schedule 36(1), Sections 19(a)/41. [@1]
