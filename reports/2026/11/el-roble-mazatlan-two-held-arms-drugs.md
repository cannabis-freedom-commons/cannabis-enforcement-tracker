Soldiers of the Mexican Army and state police detained two people in the village
of El Roble, south of Mazatlán, during a security operation. [@1] The two people
were detained and placed at the disposal of the corresponding authorities to
investigate their possible participation in a crime. [@1]

Authorities seized two long guns, eight magazines and 210 usable cartridges
during the intervention. [@1] They also seized various doses of substances with
characteristics similar to methamphetamine and marijuana. [@1] Officers also
found and seized a tactical vest and two ballistic plates allegedly used for
protection. [@1] The weapons, ammunition, substances and tactical equipment were
placed under guard with the competent bodies for the corresponding legal and
forensic procedures. [@1]

The operation was part of surveillance and crime-prevention actions carried out
by security forces in the rural area of the municipality of Mazatlán. [@1] No
further details were provided on the identities of the detainees or the specific
circumstances of their capture. [@1] The corresponding authorities are to
determine the detainees' legal situation as investigations advance. [@1]
