Police carried out the raid at about 11:30 on the morning of Thursday 12
November at East Ilisha launch ghat in Bhola Sadar. [@1] The operation was led
by Ilisha investigation centre in-charge Bashir Alam, with sub-inspector Saiful
Islam assisting, acting on a secret tip-off. [@1]

Officers searched a suspect and recovered two kilograms of ganja from his
possession. [@1] He was arrested for involvement in drug dealing. [@1] Police
said the recovered ganja was seized through legal process. [@1]

The arrested man is Alamin, a resident of ward 3 of Nazrul Nagar union under
Dakkhin Aicha police station. [@2]
