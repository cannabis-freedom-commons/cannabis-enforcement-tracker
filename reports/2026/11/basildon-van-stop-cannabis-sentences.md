Three drug dealers have been sentenced for peddling cannabis in south Essex
after they were pulled over for a suspected driving offence. [@1] They appeared
at Basildon Crown Court on Monday (November 2), where two were jailed but one
walked free. [@1] All three had been charged with possessing a Class B drug with
intent to supply, to which they initially pleaded not guilty. [@1] Judge Richard
Conley jailed Back's two co-defendants. [@1] Trung, 45, of no fixed abode in
Norwich, was sentenced to 36 months. [@1] Vo-Trong, 38, also of no fixed abode
in Norwich, was handed 25 months. [@1] He was sentenced to 14 months in prison,
suspended for 18 months. [@1]

Lewis Back, Pham Trung and Hieu Vo-Trong were travelling in a white Ford Transit
which Essex Police spotted at junction 29 of the M25, entering the A127 towards
Southend on February 24. [@1] Officers believed it was being driven without
insurance. [@1] They followed it to Gardiners Lane North in Basildon, where it
was pulled over and searched. [@1] The occupants were found to be carrying
cannabis. [@1]

Back and Vo-Trong later changed their pleas to guilty while Trung took his
chance with a jury and was convicted, meaning he lost any deduction to his
sentence. [@1] Back, 32, of London Road in Sevenoaks, Kent, who had been driving
the van, also pleaded guilty to simple possession of cannabis resin. [@1] The
driving offence for which he was initially pulled over was ultimately abandoned.
