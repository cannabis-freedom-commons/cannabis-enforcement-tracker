Police in Dresden-Loebtau arrested a 29-year-old German man after a witness tip
led officers to his VW Polo and then to his flat. [@1] Officers followed the tip
on Sunday morning to the Polo on Tharandter Strasse in Loebtau, a district of
Dresden. [@2] The man was identified as the holder of the Polo and the resident
of the searched flat, and he was taken into police custody. [@2]

In the car officers found several packs of cannabis alongside crystal, several
tablets, stolen number plates, about 10,000 euros in cash and a hunting rifle.
[@1] A later search of the flat recovered further narcotics and firearms, and
officers seized the drugs, the money and the weapons. [@1] Police said the exact
quantity of the seized narcotics was still being established and the
classification of the weapons was still pending. [@1]

Investigators are now pursuing the man for alleged unauthorised trafficking in
narcotics while carrying weapons. [@2] The overall find is described as larger
quantities of narcotics and weapons found in a house on Tharandter Strasse. [@3]
