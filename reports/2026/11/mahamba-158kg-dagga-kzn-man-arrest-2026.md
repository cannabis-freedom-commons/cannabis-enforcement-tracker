A 39-year-old man travelling from KwaZulu-Natal was arrested at Mahamba in
Mpumalanga after police found 158.2 kg of compressed dagga in his vehicle. [@1]
He was stopped at about 01:00 on Tuesday, 3 November 2026, in the Mahamba
policing precinct. [@2] Police said the stop followed information about
suspected drug trafficking and officers went to the identified area in the
Mahamba policing precinct. [@1]

Officers spotted a white Nissan NP200 bakkie, stopped and searched it, and found
30 rolls of compressed dagga wrapped in greyish sellotape. [@1] The dagga
weighed 158.2 kg and police estimated its street value at R1 million. [@1] The
man was arrested on a charge of dealing in dagga and the Nissan NP200 was seized
for the investigation. [@1] A case of dealing in dagga was opened at the Mahamba
Police Station. [@1]

He was expected to appear at the Mkhondo Magistrate’s Court on Thursday, 05
November 2026. [@2] Mpumalanga Provincial Commissioner Lieutenant General
Apaphia Modise commended the officers and said police would intensify operations
against drug trafficking in the province. [@1]
