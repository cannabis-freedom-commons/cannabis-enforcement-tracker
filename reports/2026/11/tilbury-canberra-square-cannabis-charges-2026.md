Essex Police executed a warrant at an address in Tilbury on 2 November 2026 as
part of an investigation into the sale of cannabis. [@1,3,4,5] Officers found a large
quantity of cannabis and a four-figure sum of cash at the address. [@1,3,4,5]

Mollie McKenzie, 32, and Mason Russell, 31, both of Canberra Square in Tilbury,
were arrested at the address and later charged. [@1,3,4,5] Russell was charged with
possession with intent to supply a Class B drug, being concerned in the supply
of a Class B drug, and acquiring, using or possessing criminal property. [@1,3,5]
McKenzie was charged with permitting the use of a premises for the supply of a
Class B drug and with acquiring, using or possessing criminal property. [@2,3,5]
