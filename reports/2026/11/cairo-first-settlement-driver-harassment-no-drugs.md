A woman employee who lives in the Marj police district complained to Cairo's
First Settlement police station that a private-car driver had harassed her as
she walked along a street in a residential compound. [@1] She filed the
complaint on 6 November 2026. [@1] In the video post that circulated on social
media she said the driver was using drugs in his car while harassing her. [@1]

The Interior Ministry said its investigation took up the circulated video post.
[@2] Police identified the car and its driver, a driver resident in Alexandria.
[@1] Questioned, he admitted the harassment. [@2] The Interior Ministry said
checks showed he neither possessed nor used any drugs, contrary to the
circulated post. [@3] The ministry's statement, published by Youm7, gave the
same finding. [@2]

The prosecution had him held on remand pending investigation and told police to
speed up inquiries into the case. [@4] Prosecutors asked for the circulated
video to be examined. [@4] They also ordered the car used in the incident
examined and any surveillance cameras around the scene checked. [@4] The public
prosecution took over the investigation after the legal procedures were
completed. [@1]
