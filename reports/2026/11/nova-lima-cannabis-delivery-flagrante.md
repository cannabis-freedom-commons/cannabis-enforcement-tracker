Two teenagers, aged 15 and 17, were apprehended in the Nova Lima district of
Campo Grande on the night of Thursday 12 November 2026. [@1] The teenagers were
using an app-hailing car to carry six cannabis blocks and were to split R$500
for the delivery. [@1] The cannabis had left the Parque do Lageado district for
delivery to Nova Lima. [@1] The Força Tática special unit of the 11th Military
Police Company intercepted the vehicle and stopped the delivery. [@1]

An investigation led police to the supposed head of the operation, and an
18-year-old man was arrested in Parque do Lageado as the one directing the
teenagers. [@1] At a commercial establishment linked to the incident, the police
found a further 208 cannabis portions, 10 cocaine vials and cash, which the
suspect denied owning. [@1] The incident ended with two teenagers apprehended
and one adult arrested. [@1]
