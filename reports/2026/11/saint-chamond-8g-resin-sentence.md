Officers of the Gier district proximity-security group arrested a 28-year-old
motorist at Saint-Chamond. [@1] The patrol spotted her on Sunday 1 November at
about 15:00 as she committed repeated driving offences. [@1] Loire
national-police officers stopped her Volkswagen Polo after she failed to signal
on rue du Crêt de l’œillet between Saint-Chamond and L’Horme. [@1] The driver,
who lives in Saint-Etienne, admitted in custody that she delivered narcotics.
[@1]

Officers found on her 19 cocaine wraps totalling about 20 grams, one 8-gram
capsule of cannabis resin and 1,550 euros in cash. [@1] A search of her home
then yielded another 1,500 euros in cash and 24 more cocaine wraps. [@1] After
custody she was presented to a Saint-Etienne prosecutor, who sent her for
immediate trial before the Saint-Etienne judicial court. [@1]

On 2 November she was sentenced to six months’ imprisonment with full
suspension. [@1] The cannabis component recorded here is the single 8-gram resin
capsule, while cocaine weights and cash are noted only to explain the mixed
seizure. [@1]
