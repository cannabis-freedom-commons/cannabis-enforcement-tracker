The Nausori Magistrate’s Court acquitted Samuela Bisa Vakaca and Apisalome
Matetani of illicit drug possession. [@1] The judgment concluded the criminal
case involving Samuela Bisa Vakaca, Tevita Ramana, Apisalome Matetani and Unaisi
Lewamoce. [@1] Tevita Ramana and Unaisi Lewamoce entered guilty pleas and were
dealt with by the court, while Vakaca and Matetani maintained their innocence.
[@1]

All four were initially charged with one count of being found in possession of
illicit drugs following a snap police roadblock at Manoca, Nausori on December
6, 2024. [@1] Police searched a hybrid vehicle driven by Matetani during the
operation. [@1] Police discovered 14 branches of marijuana wrapped inside a
baby’s onesie suit on the back left passenger seat. [@1]

The prosecution relied on Section 32 of the Illicit Drugs Control Act, which
presumes any illicit drug found inside a vehicle is in the possession of the
person controlling the vehicle. [@1] Matetani called Ramana as a defence witness
and Ramana testified that the marijuana belonged entirely to him and that
Matetani was merely the driver. [@1] Prosecution witnesses confirmed that the
drugs were located directly adjacent to Ramana’s seating position in the back
seat. [@1] Magistrate Vaniqi held that Matetani successfully rebutted the
statutory presumption of possession, calling him just the driver caught in the
wrong place at the wrong time. [@1] Applying the same rationale to Vakaca, who
was also a passenger, the court found both Vakaca and Matetani not guilty and
acquitted them accordingly. [@1]
