Police in Shopian district, Jammu and Kashmir, arrested a suspected drug peddler
on Saturday 7 November 2026 and recovered 221 grams of a charas-like powder and
₹31,500 in cash from him. [@1] A police team from Police Post Turkwangam stopped
him during naka checking, a roadside checkpoint search, at Maldera Link Road
after he was seen moving under suspicious circumstances. [@1] Kashmir Life
reported the same arrest, putting the recovery at 221 grams of a powder-like
substance suspected to be charas along with ₹31,500 in cash at Maldera Link
Road. [@2]

The man police identified as the accused is a resident of Bijnor district in
Uttar Pradesh who had been living at Turkwangam. [@1] Police registered FIR No.
137/2026 at Police Station Zainapora and said they were investigating where the
substance came from and where it was going. [@1] Kashmir Life said the Shopian
police repeated their commitment to fighting drug trafficking and asked
residents to report drug peddling, illegal cultivation of narcotic plants and
substance abuse. [@2]
