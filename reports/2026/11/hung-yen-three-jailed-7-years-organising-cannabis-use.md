On 11 November 2026, the People's Court of Region 6 in Hung Yen held a
first-instance trial and sentenced three defendants to a total of 21 years in
prison for organising illegal use of narcotic substances. [@1] The court gave
Dau Thanh Tung, Dau Van Trung and Luong Nhat Tan seven years in prison each.
[@1] Tung was born in 1995 and Trung and Tan were both born in 1999, and all
three lived in Dong Cao hamlet, Ai Quoc commune, Hung Yen Province. [@1]

According to the indictment, on 9 June 2026 the three agreed to pool money to
buy cannabis for personal use. [@1] They then jointly contributed money to
obtain the cannabis, used it together, played games and continued spending time
together. [@1] On 14 June 2026, Ai Quoc commune police, acting on a tip from
residents, summoned Tung, Trung and Tan to the station for questioning. [@1]
During questioning, all three confessed to organising illegal use of narcotic
substances on the night of 9 June 2026. [@1] Investigators seized implements
used for consuming cannabis, and confiscated the defendants' three mobile phones
for the state budget. [@1]

Forensic testing found substances that are metabolic products of cannabis in the
urine samples of all three men. [@1] At trial, the defendants admitted their own
and their accomplices' criminal acts as charged in the indictment. [@1] The
panel said the offence was very serious and harmed society, infringing the
State's monopoly on narcotics control as well as public order and safety. [@1]
It noted that a defendant had previously served a prison term for storing
narcotic substances yet reoffended, showing disregard for the law. [@1]
