A joint Crime Branch and Belbagh police team cordoned off the area near Janjira
Bridge in Jabalpur on Thursday night after an informant tip. [@1] The youth
tried to flee on seeing police but was caught. [@1] He identified himself as
Manish Chaudhary, a resident of Babatola. [@1]

Police searched his backpack and found two packets of ganja. [@1] Weighing
showed a total of 5 kg 272 g of ganja. [@1] Police put the value of the seized
ganja at about Rs 266,600. [@1]

According to police, he had brought the ganja from Kalahandi district in Odisha
to Jabalpur for delivery to a local contact. [@1] He allegedly told police he
had gone to Kalahandi at the instance of Kalu alias Sunny Sonkar of Sindhi Camp.
[@1] He allegedly collected it from Deepu Kumar of Narla and was waiting near
Janjira Bridge to hand it over. [@1] He said he was to be paid Rs 1,000 as a
daily wage for the delivery work. [@1]

Police said the roles of Sonkar and the reported Odisha supplier could be
confirmed only after further investigation. [@1]
