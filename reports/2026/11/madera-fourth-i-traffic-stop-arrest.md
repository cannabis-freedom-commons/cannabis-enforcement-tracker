A Monday-morning traffic stop in Madera County ended with a driver under arrest
after a California Highway Patrol officer reported smelling cannabis coming from
the car. [@1] On Monday the patrol's Madera unit said its officer made the
enforcement stop around 10 AM at Fourth and I Street after seeing the driver
using a cellphone while driving. [@1] The driver was taken into custody and
booked into the Madera County Jail. [@1]

When the officer first contacted the driver during the Monday stop, the officer
reported being met with an overpowering scent of cannabis coming from inside the
vehicle. [@1] The overpowering scent reported during the contact prompted a
deeper investigation. [@1] The follow-up investigation that followed the
cannabis scent led the officer to discover that the driver had a controlled
substance with the intent to sell. [@1] The enforcement stop that led to the
arrest occurred at Fourth and I Street in Madera. [@1] California Highway Patrol
officers in Madera handled the Monday enforcement action. [@1]
