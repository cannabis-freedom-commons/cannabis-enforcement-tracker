Tamil Nadu South Zone police arrested 49 people and registered 35 cases under
the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act during a coordinated one-day
anti-drug operation across all districts and cities in the zone on Tuesday, 3
November 2026. [@3] Teams carried out simultaneous checks, targeted searches and
enforcement activities at identified drug hotspots, railway stations and other
vulnerable locations. [@2] The operation was aimed at curbing drug trafficking
and abuse. [@1]

Police seized 43 kg of narcotic substances, including ganja and hashish, during
the operation. [@2] Authorities also organised 518 awareness programmes across
the South Zone to educate the public, particularly youngsters, about the dangers
of drug abuse and encourage community participation in prevention. [@2]

Inspector General of Police, South Zone, Vijayendra S Bidari said enforcement
had intensified this year, with police registering 3,146 NDPS cases and
arresting 4,636 accused in 2026 and seizing 2,853 kg of narcotic substances so
far. [@2] Investigation and prosecution efforts had resulted in convictions in
939 NDPS cases, with 1,189 people convicted. [@2] Personnel had been directed to
maintain continuous surveillance in vulnerable areas, monitor known drug
offenders, strengthen intelligence gathering and dismantle drug distribution
networks. [@1] The synchronised operation reflected the police's firm commitment
to disrupting drug networks, protecting youth and building drug-free villages,
with police maintaining zero tolerance towards drug abuse and trafficking. [@1]
