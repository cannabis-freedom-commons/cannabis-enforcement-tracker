Police in Vriddhachalam arrested four men engaged in selling ganja, the local
term for cannabis, and seized 236 grams of it from them. [@1] Those held near
the Manalur railway overbridge were 21-year-old Aswinkumar, son of Ayyasamy of
Mukundanallur colony, and 20-year-old Ilayaraja, son of Balasubramanian of Raman
Street, Ramachandranpettai. [@1] The other two arrested men were 21-year-old
Ashok, son of Arumugam, and 27-year-old Yogesh, son of Pandian, caught with
cannabis behind the Melapalaiyur direct paddy procurement centre. [@1]

Officers recovered 100 grams from Aswinkumar and 120 grams from Ilayaraja,
together 220 grams, while 16 grams were recovered from Ashok and Yogesh, making
up the 236 grams the police reported. [@1] Vriddhachalam police registered a
case against the first two men and arrested them. [@1] The arrests came during
night patrols on 3 November ordered by Vriddhachalam Deputy Superintendent of
Police Balakrishnan and carried out by Inspector Chandru, Sub-Inspectors Bharath
Nehru and Gandhi and other officers. [@1]

A second patrol team under Karuveppilankurichi Sub-Inspector Bhagyaraj made the
Melapalaiyur catch during an evening patrol near the tamarind grove behind the
procurement centre. [@2] Police seized the cannabis from Ashok and Yogesh and
are continuing to question them. [@2]
