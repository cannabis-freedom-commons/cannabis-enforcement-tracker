Border Guard Bangladesh detained 21-year-old Maruf Hossain, also called Suruj,
on suspicion of smuggling involvement. [@1] Suruj is the son of the late Nur
Hossain from the Jhalbazar area of South Dhaldanga in Shilkhuri Union of
Bhurungamari upazila. [@1] After a case was filed against him, he was handed to
police on Sunday, 1 November. [@2] The officer-in-charge of Bhurungamari police
station, Azim Uddin, said BGB handed Maruf Hossain alias Suruj to police after
filing a case. [@1]

Over the previous 24 hours BGB carried out anti-smuggling and anti-drug
operations in Diyadanga, Moydan, Shaljhor and North Dhaldanga in Bhurungamari
and the Madarganj border-post area in Nageshwari. [@1] The operation seized 3 kg
of ganja together with 3,555 medicine pills, 80 cosmetics, two mobile phones and
a cow. [@1] Officers also seized a battery-powered auto-rickshaw used for ganja
smuggling. [@2] BGB set the seizure value of all the detained goods at 322,210
taka. [@1]

The seizures were announced in a press release signed by Kurigram Battalion (22
BGB) commander Lieutenant Colonel Mohammad Mahbub-ul-Haq. [@2] The commander
said BGB remains firmly committed to implementing a zero-tolerance policy to
ensure sovereignty and security in the border area, prevent drugs and smuggling
and curb illegal infiltration. [@1] He said such operations will continue. [@2]
