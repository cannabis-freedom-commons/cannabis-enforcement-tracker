Ujjain police arrested five accused with 2.655 kilograms of illegal narcotics in
separate Sunday actions across the Nanakheda, Nagjhiri, Panwasa, Mahakal and
Khachrod station areas. [@1] Panwasa police arrested 21-year-old Ankit Mandor
under the Pandya Khedi bridge for standing with ganja. [@1] Mahakal police
caught Moin, a Panchkua Shakarpur resident, near the steps of Maulana Mauj's
dargah. [@1] Nagjhiri police detained 42-year-old Kamal Singh Chandawat of
Jamalpur with MD in pill form rather than powder. [@1] Nanakheda police caught
35-year-old Ishwar Barwasi on the Engineering College campus. [@1] Khachrod
police caught 85-year-old Chhotu Shah near the Bhatkhedi Road railway culvert.
[@1]

Panwasa officers seized 1.298 kilograms of ganja from Ankit, which police valued
at 15,000 rupees, and also seized his motorcycle. [@1] Officers found 122 grams
of ganja on Moin. [@1] Officers seized 130 grams of ganja from Ishwar. [@1]
Officers recovered 1.100 kilograms of ganja from Chhotu. [@1] The four ganja
seizures total 2.65 kilograms of cannabis, derived from the individual weights
against the reported 2.655-kilogram combined narcotics total. [@1] The Nagjhiri
MD totalled 4.69 grams in pills weighing 0.42 grams each, which police valued at
46,000 rupees, and this amount is not counted in the cannabis total. [@1]

Police headquarters started Operation Prahar against drug sellers and Ujjain
police carried out these actions under that drive. [@1] Ganja and MD were
reported as separate seizures in the five-station-area action, with ganja as the
cannabis component covered here. [@1]
