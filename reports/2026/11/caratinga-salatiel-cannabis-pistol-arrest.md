Military police in Caratinga arrested a 33-year-old man on suspicion of drug
trafficking on Tuesday, 3 November 2026, in the Salatiel neighbourhood. [@1] The
operation began after an anonymous tip said the suspect had been involved in
drug trafficking for some time, and teams then split between the area near his
workplace and his home. [@1] At his home, the suspect's mother let the officers
enter, and searches of the bedroom and other rooms found drugs and a magazine
with two intact .380 rounds. [@1]

Officers posted near his workplace located and approached the man and, searching
his vehicle, found a small portion of maconha, or cannabis, near the console and
a loaded .380 pistol, model HC58 Plus, with 18 intact rounds. [@1] In total,
officers seized maconha bars and portions, the .380 pistol, a magazine with two
rounds plus 18 rounds that were in the weapon, a mobile phone, two precision
scales and materials used to prepare and sell drugs, plus a knife and other
objects shown in the seizure photograph. [@1] Police checks indicated the man
had been stopped before and that an arrest warrant against him had been executed
in mid-2024. [@1]

He was arrested and taken for legal procedures, and the case was registered by
the Minas Gerais Military Police through the 62nd Battalion. [@1]
