Rajshahi Metropolitan Police detained 31 people, including seven alleged drug
traders and one drug user, in separate drives by several city police stations.
[@1] The drives took place on Tuesday 3 November in the metropolitan area. [@1]
The drives spanned the Chandrima, Paba, Belpukur, Boalia, Karnahar and Motihar
thanas. [@1]

Police put the seizure in those drives at 80 tapentadol tablets, 200 grams of
ganja and Tk 16,500 said to be drug-sale proceeds. [@1] In Chandrima's Balanagar
area officers held 49-year-old Anisur Rahman with 70 tapentadol tablets and the
Tk 16,500, while his wife fled after sensing the police presence. [@1] In Paba,
Suman Ali and Al-Amin were held with 10 tapentadol tablets. [@1] Belpukur police
held Noyon Ali with 100 grams of ganja, and Boalia's Shiroil outpost held Monir
and Liton with 50 grams of ganja. [@1] Karnahar police held Foysal Rahman with
20 grams of ganja, and Motihar police held Ruman on an allegation of consuming
ganja and tapentadol. [@1]

City police spokesperson Deputy Commissioner (Sadar) Md Gaziur Rahman confirmed
the account on Wednesday afternoon. [@1] Those held were later sent to jail
through the court. [@1] Police said an operation was under way to arrest the
woman who had fled. [@1]

A separate set of drives the same day detained 23 more people in total. [@1]
