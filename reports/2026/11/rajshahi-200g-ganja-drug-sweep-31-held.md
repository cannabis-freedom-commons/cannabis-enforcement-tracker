Rajshahi Metropolitan Police detained 31 people, including seven alleged drug
traders and one drug user, in separate drives by several city police stations.
[@1,2] The drives took place on Tuesday 3 November in the metropolitan area. [@1]
The drives spanned the Chandrima, Paba, Belpukur, Boalia, Karnahar and Motihar
thanas. [@1,2]

Police put the seizure in those drives at 80 tapentadol tablets, 200 grams of
ganja and Tk 16,500 said to be drug-sale proceeds. [@1,2] In Chandrima's Balanagar
area officers held 49-year-old Anisur Rahman with 70 tapentadol tablets and the
Tk 16,500, while his wife fled after sensing the police presence. [@1] In Paba,
Suman Ali and Al-Amin were held with 10 tapentadol tablets. [@1] Belpukur police
held Noyon Ali with 100 grams of ganja, and Boalia's Shiroil outpost held Monir
and Liton with 50 grams of ganja. [@1] Karnahar police held Foysal Rahman with
20 grams of ganja, and Motihar police held Ruman on an allegation of consuming
ganja and tapentadol. [@1]

City police spokesperson Deputy Commissioner (Sadar) Md Gaziur Rahman confirmed
the account on Wednesday afternoon. [@1,2] Those held were later sent to jail
through the court. [@1,2] Police said an operation was under way to arrest the
woman who had fled. [@1]

A separate set of drives the same day detained 23 more people in total. [@1]

The police statement dates the Chandrima drive in Balanagar to about 9 pm on 3 November 2026 and gives the age of the wife who fled, Shirina Begum, as 44. [@3] The Chandrima detainee and his fugitive wife had been running a drug business together for a long time, the statement said after questioning him. [@3] The statement says Noyon Ali, held in Belpukur with 100 grams of ganja, already faced three drug cases at Belpukur police station. [@3] Liton, held with Monir at the Shiroil outpost, had long worked as Monir's partner in drug dealing, the statement said. [@3] Ruman, held in Motihar for consuming ganja and tapentadol, already faced three drug cases and two Penal Code cases at Motihar police station. [@3] Ruman was taken to Rajshahi Medical College Hospital for a health check before a case was filed against him at Motihar police station on the doctors' opinion. [@3] The 23 people held in the same day's separate thana and detective-police drives comprised six wanted on warrants, six in drug cases and 11 in other cases, the police force said in its own release. [@4] Prokash Kal put the total ganja recovery in the drives at 170 grams rather than the 200 grams police reported. [@5] Prokash Kal placed the Boalia Shiroil outpost drive in the Shalbagan area and said the 50 grams of ganja were found on Monir alone. [@5] Prokash Kal placed the Paba drive at Duari village under Nauhata municipality. [@5]
