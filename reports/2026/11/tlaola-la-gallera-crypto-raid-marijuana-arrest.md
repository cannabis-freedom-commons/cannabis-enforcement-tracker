Two cryptocurrency farms were found in less than two months in Tlaola in the
Sierra Norte de Puebla, with the most recent find coming during a raid on 5
November. [@1] The raided property was identified as La Gallera, where officers
detained one man who had a bag of presumed marijuana and a cell phone. [@1] The
search was carried out by the Secretariat of Security and Citizen Protection,
the Attorney General's Office, the Mexican Army and the National Guard. [@1]

Officers also seized 50 mining boxes, 1,031 cryptoassets, a high-speed modem, an
industrial three-phase transformer, two phone chips and a video screen. [@1] The
operation was part of simultaneous actions in Chihuahua, Sinaloa, Puebla and
Veracruz, whose financial impact the Security Cabinet estimated at 4,132 million
pesos. [@1] The site lies about 4.3 km from El Beneficio, another property
raided in September where about 300 specialized computers, a transformer, about
80 medium-voltage terminals and eight satellite antennas were seized. [@1]

Authorities investigated the possible irregular use of electricity and the
origin of the funds used to run the installation. [@1] Cryptocurrency mining
itself is not prohibited in Mexico but can lead to criminal investigation when
clandestine electricity connections are used or funds are linked to money
laundering. [@1] Available information does not establish that both sites had
the same operators or belonged to a single criminal structure. [@1]
