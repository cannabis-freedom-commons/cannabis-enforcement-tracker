A police patrol stopped and checked an e-scooter rider on the street An Fronte
Karl in Germersheim on Sunday 8 November 2026. [@1] During the check the
officers found signs of drug impairment in the rider. [@1] The rider told them
he had consumed cannabis the evening before. [@1]

He was then taken to the police station, where a blood sample was taken from
him. [@1] A criminal case was opened against him. [@1] The statement was issued
by the Germersheim inspectorate of the Landau police headquarters, which handles
the area's press work. [@2]

The rider was not named publicly and no figure for the amount of cannabis was
published. [@2]
