An investigation and arrest unit of the Sabratha Security Directorate detained a
man on Sunday 8 November 2026 on suspicion of dealing cannabis. [@1] Officers
had watched him standing beside a public school in the town, where the
directorate said he sold drugs. [@1] The arrest followed surveillance and
verification of information, and a raid caught him holding pieces of hashish
prepared for sale. [@1] He also had a sum of money that the directorate said
came from his drug trade. [@1] The directorate said the detainee had prior
records and was wanted by the anti-narcotics office in connection with other
drug-trafficking cases. [@1]

The Sabratha Security Directorate announced the arrest in a statement on its
official Facebook page. [@1] It said the legal procedures required for the case
had been taken and the detainee had been placed in custody. [@1]
