Two men identified as A.G.C. and J.V.R.F. accepted prison sentences on 11
November 2026 at a preliminary hearing before Salamanca's Audiencia Provincial
for jointly trafficking drugs from two homes in the city. [@2] La Gaceta de
Salamanca reported the agreed terms as six years and one day for A.G.C. and
three years and one day for J.V.R.F., after prosecutors had sought seven and a
half years and seven years and three months. [@2] Salamanca24horas reported that
A.G.C. also accepted a fine of 198,000 euros, cut from a requested fine of
746,000 euros. [@4] The parties asked the court to suspend the prison terms if
the men undergo detoxification and do not reoffend, and the prosecutor did not
oppose that, though the decision was still pending. [@2] A.G.C. has been in
pre-trial detention since 20 June 2025. [@1] J.V.R.F. has not entered prison,
and under the agreement reported by La Gaceta he will not do so if he notifies
any change of address, undergoes detoxification and does not reoffend. [@2]

The prosecution arose from Operación Fortaleza, in which the National Police
drugs and organised crime unit, UDYCO, searched homes on Avenida de Portugal and
Calle Gran Capitán on 19 June 2025. [@3] At the Portugal home officers seized
8,433.33 grams of cannabis resin and 148.46 grams of cannabis, along with
cocaine, MDMA, ketamine, amphetamine, cash, two precision scales and material
for preparing doses. [@1] At the Gran Capitán home, where J.V.R.F. was present,
they seized 22,008.84 grams of cannabis resin and 6.28 grams of cannabis, plus
cocaine, ketamine, MDMA and weighing equipment. [@1] La Gaceta de Salamanca said
the Portugal home was used to distribute the drugs and the Gran Capitán home to
store them. [@2] Officers also seized precision scales and 845 euros in cash at
the Portugal flat. [@3]

Prosecutors treated the facts as a public-health offence of trafficking
substances that cause serious harm to health, aggravated by the notorious
importance of the amounts seized. [@1] The prosecutor's written qualification
valued the whole mixed seizure, calculated for sale by dose, at more than
370,000 euros. [@2]
