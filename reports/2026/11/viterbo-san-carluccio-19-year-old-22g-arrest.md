Officers of the Squadra Mobile, the Viterbo Questura's investigative unit, made
the arrest after an information-led anti-drug service in the historic centre of
Viterbo. [@1] Police arrested a 19-year-old non-EU citizen on a charge of
holding drugs for sale. [@1] Officers spotted the young man in Piazza San
Carluccio holding several doses of cocaine shortly before a handover. [@1] A
check found three more cocaine doses hidden on his body. [@1]

A search of his home then seized three transparent bags holding 22 grams of
marijuana. [@1] The same search seized 4,900 euros, described by police as
proceeds of dealing. [@1] The cocaine was described only as doses, with no
weight given, so only the marijuana is counted in cannabis figures. [@1]

Police arrested the man and held him in the Questura's security cells pending a
validation hearing and a fast-track trial (giudizio direttissimo). [@1] The
Questura of Viterbo confirmed the operation. [@2] Italian law presumes a person
innocent until a final conviction. [@2]
