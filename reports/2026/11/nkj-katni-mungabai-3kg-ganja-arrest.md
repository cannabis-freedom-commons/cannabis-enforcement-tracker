NKJ police in Katni, Madhya Pradesh, arrested one man and seized 3 kg of ganja
from him. [@1] Police valued the seized ganja at about 60,000 rupees. [@1]

Officers patrolling near Mungabai Colony, Chhapawah Tirtha, Amal Road, spotted a
man sitting with a white bag and a blue cloth and surrounded and caught him when
he tried to run. [@1] On interrogation the man identified himself as Shani,
alias Kuldeep Kushwaha, resident of Prem Nagar. [@1]

The patrol was on 10 November. [@2] Police registered a case against him under
sections 8 and 20 of the NDPS Act, India's narcotic drugs law. [@2] After his
arrest he was presented to the court, from which he was sent to Katni district
jail. [@2]
