Detectives from the Sunamganj district Detective Branch arrested 20-year-old
Bijoy Robi Das in Krishnanagar village under Chhatak police station in Sunamganj
district. [@1,2] Krishnanagar is in No. 12 Chhaula Afzalabad Union of Chhatak.
[@1,2] The arrest took place at about 3:30 pm on Wednesday, 4 November 2026,
during a special operation acting on secret information. [@1,2] Bijoy Robi Das is
the son of Shirish Robi Das of Krishnanagar village. [@1,2] Another accused,
Shirish Robidas, fled from the scene and could not be arrested. [@1,2]

Officers seized 1 kg of ganja, meaning cannabis, from the accused, along with
1,104 taka in cash described as proceeds from drug sales. [@1,2]

The operation team included Sub-Inspector Abdur Rahim Jiban with Assistant
Sub-Inspector Ismail Hossain and constables Dipak Munda, Sohan Mia and Kawser
Ahmad Antar. [@1] The same article also reports a separate Tuesday-evening
operation in which detectives arrested one man with 200 yaba tablets. [@1] Yaba
tablets are methamphetamine, not cannabis, so the reported 200 tablets, their
20-gram weight and 70,000-taka estimated price are excluded from the cannabis
figures here. [@1]

The ganja was wrapped in a blue polythene bag. [@2] Police put the market value of the seized ganja at about 25,000 taka. [@2] Detectives said they were filing regular cases under the Narcotics Control Act at the relevant police stations and that the arrested men were being taken to court. [@2]
