Anantnag police arrested two alleged drug peddlers on 4 November 2026 in
separate operations in the district's Pahalgam and Mattan areas, part of an
anti-narcotics campaign. [@1] A team from Pahalgam police station stopped Nazir
Ahmed Chechi, a resident of Gujran Batkote, during naka checking, a roadside
check, in the Hokhar Batkote area. [@1] A search recovered about three kilograms
of bhanga (bhang) powder from Chechi, Hindi reports say, while English accounts
from the police statement call the material about 3 kg of Bung Boosa. [@1]
Police registered FIR No. 102/2026 under Sections 8/20 of the NDPS Act at
Pahalgam police station, the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act.
[@2]

In the second operation, a Mattan police station team arrested Burhan Sheraz
Bhat, a resident of Akura, during checking. [@1] About 19.6 grams of charas, a
hand-pressed cannabis resin, was recovered from Bhat. [@1] Police registered FIR
No. 127/2026 under the NDPS Act at Mattan police station and began
investigating. [@1] A police spokesperson said further investigations were under
way in both cases. [@3]
