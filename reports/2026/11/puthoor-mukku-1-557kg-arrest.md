In Kottarakkara, a joint team of Puthoor police and the Kollam Rural DANSAF
squad arrested 31-year-old youth Sherin for allegedly carrying cannabis meant
for sale. [@1] Sherin is described as a resident of Palavila house at Gurumuttam
in Kottarakkara. [@1] Officers intercepted him while he was walking toward the
Poovattur ITI side shortly after he got off a KSRTC bus at Puthoor Mukku
junction. [@1]

During the check of his belongings, the team recovered 1.557 kilograms of
cannabis, locally called kanjavu, from his possession. [@1] Police said the
consignment had been brought to the area for sale. [@1]

The arrest was made by a team including Puthoor station house officer Vimal
Ranganath, sub-inspectors Madhusudhanan, Saju and Arul, senior civil police
officer Rahul and members of the Kollam Rural DANSAF unit. [@1] Kollam Rural
district police chief Shaji Sugunan said stringent checks and enforcement
measures against the drug mafia will continue under Operation Toofan. [@1]
