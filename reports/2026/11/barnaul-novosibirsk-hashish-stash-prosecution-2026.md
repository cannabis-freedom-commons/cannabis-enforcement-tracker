A 19-year-old resident of Novosibirsk is to stand trial in Barnaul on a charge
of attempted drug sale. [@1,3] The criminal case was sent to the Industrialny
district court of Barnaul for consideration on the merits, while the case
against the accomplice was separated into different proceedings. [@1,3] He is
accused of attempted large-scale sale of drugs using an information network by a
group acting in prior conspiracy. [@1,3]

He came to Barnaul with accomplices in August 2026, according to the transport
prosecutor's office. [@1] In Barnaul he bought 17 grams of cocaine and 2.9 grams
of hashish over the internet. [@1,3] Investigators say he arrived in Barnaul with
an accomplice and bought cocaine weighing more than 17 grams and 2.9 grams of
hashish. [@2] On an accomplice's instructions he was to place the drugs in more
than 50 stashes. [@2,3]

Transport police detained the 19-year-old beside the railway tracks on the
Barnaul–Shtabka stretch. [@1,3] The drugs found on him were removed from illegal
circulation. [@1]
