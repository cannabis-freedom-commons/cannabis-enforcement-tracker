Carabinieri of the Radiomobile section of the Aversa company arrested a
48-year-old resident of Caserta province in Lusciano during a routine patrol on
the evening of 5 November 2026. [@1]

The search turned up 21 doses of hashish already packed into single portions for
retail sale, weighing about 74 grams. [@1] A second outlet put the weight of the
21 doses at 73.65 grams. [@2] The hashish was seized and held pending deposit
with the court's evidence office. [@2]

The man was arrested in flagrante, and after the judicial authority was informed
he was placed under house arrest at his home awaiting a fast-track trial (rito
direttissimo), the expedited hearing for offences caught in the act. [@2]
Edizione Caserta notes that the case is still at the preliminary investigation
stage and that the suspect is presumed innocent until a final conviction. [@2]
