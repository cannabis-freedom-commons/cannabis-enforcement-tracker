The Policía de Investigaciones (PDI), Santa Fe's investigations police,
destroyed drugs seized in separate investigations in the city of San Justo on
Tuesday 10 November 2026. [@1] The procedure took place at the municipal
waste-treatment plant, on orders from the Ministerio Público de la Acusación,
the provincial public prosecution service. [@1] Fiscal Unit No. 154 of San
Justo, led by prosecutor Daniela Soledad Montegrosso, supervised it. [@1]

The destroyed material came from 27 seizure records linked to different cases
under National Law No. 23.737, Argentina's narcotics statute. [@1] What was
burned included cocaine, cannabis sativa, cannabis plants, and materials used to
divide and package drugs. [@1] Before the destruction, officers checked the
material and ran new preliminary identification tests, known as PIPH, to verify
the substances. [@1] Two qualified witnesses attended, and officers of the PDI
forensic science division photographed the procedure. [@1]

Volunteer firefighters from San Justo started and controlled the fire at the
municipal site. [@1] Mayor Nicolás Cuesta, government secretary Diego Palmier
and local media representatives watched. [@1] The PDI said the burn fulfilled
prosecution orders on the final disposal of drugs that had remained in police
custody. [@2]
