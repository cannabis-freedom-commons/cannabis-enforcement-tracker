Emilio Leon, 22, of Romeoville, is charged with being a felon in possession of a
machine gun, possession of a stolen gun and unlawful possession of a gun by a
felon, according to DuPage County court records. [@1,2] Leon had no valid Firearm Owner's Identification Card or Concealed Carry License and police said he is a felon. [@2] He was arrested at 11:11
p.m. at Washington Street and Aurora Avenue in Naperville. [@1,2] A Naperville
police officer pulled Leon over on allegations that the Dodge Challenger he was
driving did not have a front license plate, had illegally tinted windows and
squealed its tires. [@1] The officer smelled fresh cannabis and saw small cigars
in the car. [@1,2]

A loaded Glock 17 with a modified conversion device, three loaded extended
magazines and 25 grams of cannabis were found in the glove box, according to the
petition. [@1,2] The firearm was equipped with a laser attachment. [@2] Cannabis wax and ammunition were found in the center console,
prosecutors said. [@1,2] The gun had been reported stolen in May 2025 in Will
County, officials said. [@1] Leon also is charged with unlawful possession of
cannabis by a driver and traffic offenses, including an unsafe equipment charge.
[@1]

DuPage County Judge Todd Scalzo approved the pretrial detention request on
Wednesday. [@1] Leon’s next court date is Thursday. [@1]
