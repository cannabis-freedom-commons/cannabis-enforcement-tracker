Rajauli police arrested grocery shop owner Rajendra Choudhury for allegedly
running an illegal ganja trade behind his shop. [@1] Officers searched the
grocery shop inside Choudhury's house at Kalali Gali, Takiya Mohalla in Rajauli
Bazar, Nawada district, after verifying a secret tip-off. [@1] Choudhury, a
resident of the same Kalali Gali neighbourhood, was arrested at the spot. [@1]
Sub-Inspector Sachin Kumar led the raid. [@1]

Police recovered 136 grams of ganja in the raid. [@1] Dainik Bhaskar reported
the ganja was found in the shop desk's second drawer after police acted on
information that ganja was being sold at the shop near the river. [@2] It
identified the shopkeeper as Rajendra Choudhury, son of late Tunnu Choudhury of
Kalali Gali, Takiya Mohalla. [@2]

Police registered a case and produced the detained shopkeeper in court after
completing legal formalities. [@1] A first information report was registered
against him under the NDPS Act, and he was sent to judicial custody after a
medical examination on Thursday. [@2] Station chief Ranjit Kumar said the drive
against the drug trade in the area would continue. [@1]
