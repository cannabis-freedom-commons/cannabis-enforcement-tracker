The Salatiga district prosecutor's office, Kejari Salatiga, destroyed evidence
from 33 general criminal cases whose court decisions had become final. [@1] The
destruction took place at SMK Negeri 3 Salatiga, a state vocational school in
the city, on Tuesday 10 November 2026, during the commemoration of the 81st
Heroes' Day. [@1] The event began at 10am local time and was attended by the
city's regional leadership forum, Forkopimda, and thousands of students. [@2]
The Forkopimda brings together the local government, police, military and
prosecutors. [@2]

Erik Meza Nusantara, the head of Kejari Salatiga, listed the narcotics destroyed
as 587.16 grams of ganja, the Indonesian term for cannabis, 7.49 grams of sabu
(crystal methamphetamine) and 1.22 grams of tembakau gorila. [@3] Only the ganja
is counted as cannabis here; the sabu and the tembakau gorila are excluded. [@1]
The same batch included 1,193 tablets of illegal pharmaceutical drugs, 17 mobile
phones, 1,435 grams of firecracker explosives and 233 other items. [@3] The
cases whose evidence was destroyed were handled between July and November 2026.
[@3]

Prosecutors destroyed the material with a blender, a grinder and a hammer so
that no convict or anyone else could use or resell it. [@3] Awan Prastyo Luhur,
who heads the office's asset recovery and evidence management section, said the
destruction was the execution of court decisions against the 33 cases that had
become final. [@1]

Awan said the office destroyed the evidence to continue the heroes' struggle by
building a law-aware society. [@2] Erik said the event also offered public
consultation stands staffed by city agencies and a student exhibition. [@3]
