Police searched an apartment on the Carel van Manderstraat in Heemskerk after
reports of suspicious activity. [@1] The municipality said officers found a
commercial quantity of drugs in the home and seized it. [@2]

The mayor closed the apartment for three months on 10 November 2026. [@2] The
residents were informed in advance. [@2] Residents and others may not enter the
home while it is closed. [@2] The closure rests on the Opiumwet, the Dutch drugs
law, and the Damocles policy, the municipality said. [@2] The Damocles policy
lets mayors close premises linked to drug dealing. [@4] The municipality said
the measure was meant to end the violation, prevent repetition and restore
public order and safety around the home. [@2]

The municipality did not say which drugs were found or in what quantity. [@3] A
neighbour said she was shocked and worried about the many children in the
neighbourhood. [@3] The reports name no suspect and report no arrest or charge.
