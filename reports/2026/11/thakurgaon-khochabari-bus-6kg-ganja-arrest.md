Officers of the police's Special Response Battalion-13 (SRB-13) set up a
checkpoint on the Dinajpur–Thakurgaon highway in front of the Sonalli filling
station near Khochabari Bazar in Nargun union, Thakurgaon Sadar, on the night of
9 November 2026. [@2] The operation was carried out by CPC-1 of the battalion's
Dinajpur camp. [@2] They searched a Nabil Paribahan bus. [@2] In the bag of
passenger Jilani Mia, 30, they found 6 kg of ganja and arrested him. [@2] Mia
was from Kalipur village in Bhairab thana of Kishoreganj district. [@2]

SRB-13 released the information in a press release on 10 November. [@3] The
seized ganja had an estimated market value of 120,000 taka. [@1] SRB-13 filed a
case against Mia under the Narcotics Control Act. [@3] The battalion handed him
and the ganja to the local police station for further legal action. [@3] SRB-13
said its intelligence work and anti-drug operations would continue under the
government's zero-tolerance policy on drugs. [@3]
