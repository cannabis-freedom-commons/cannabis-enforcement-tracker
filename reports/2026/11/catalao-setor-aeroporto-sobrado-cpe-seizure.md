Military police took a man to the police station after officers of the
Specialised Policing Company (CPE) found drugs and materials used to portion
drugs in a two-storey house in the Setor Aeroporto of Catalão, in southeastern
Goiás, on the night of Saturday 7 November 2026. [@1] The action took place on
Avenida Guanabara in the Setor Aeroporto. [@2]

According to the Military Police, the CPE 90 team was patrolling the area when
it smelled a strong smell of cannabis coming from the balcony of the property,
and the suspect went quickly into the residence when he noticed the officers.
[@1] The officers entered the house and found the man on the second floor. [@1]
In the search they seized one piece of a substance similar to cannabis, ten
portions already divided up, a precision scale and a knife. [@1]

The suspect and the material were taken to the police authority for the legal
procedures. [@1] The response was handled by the CPE, which is attached to the
9th Regional Command of the Military Police. [@1] The same account says the
suspect and the seized material were presented to the police authority for the
legal procedures. [@2]
