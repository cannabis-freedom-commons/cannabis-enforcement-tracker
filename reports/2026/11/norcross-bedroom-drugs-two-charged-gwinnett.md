Two adults who lived with two children near Norcross face charges after officers
found cocaine in the children's bedroom. [@1] Paris Taylor, 34, is charged with
reckless conduct over drugs allegedly kept easily accessible to her underage
daughters. [@1] Collin Spencer, 45, faces the same reckless-conduct charge plus
felony counts of possessing cocaine, ecstasy, marijuana and THC vape pens with
intent to distribute. [@1] Spencer is also charged with possessing a stolen 9mm
Ruger pistol and with possessing a gun as a convicted felon. [@1] A judge denied
Spencer bond on the cocaine, ecstasy, THC and gun charges. [@1]

Officers searched the home on Jimmy Carter Boulevard on Nov. 4 as part of a drug
investigation that started in October. [@1] Gwinnett County police called
finding drugs or controlled substances in a children's bedroom uncommon and an
anomaly. [@1]

Police said keeping narcotics in close proximity to juveniles is reckless and
puts children in danger. [@1]
