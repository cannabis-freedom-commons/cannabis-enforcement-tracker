Police in Vienna-Penzing arrested a 34-year-old Slovak man, a 45-year-old
Hungarian man and a 32-year-old Hungarian woman on the evening of 7 November
2026. [@1] Officers from the Albert-Schweitzer-Gasse police station stopped the
three in the Märzstraße at about 19:30 while the trio was pushing a bicycle
along the street and they behaved conspicuously nervously on seeing the patrol.
[@3]

The two men were wearing work gloves that did not match their clothing and were
carrying a broken padlock. [@2] During the check officers seized suspected
burglary tools, several bunches of keys, cannabis and crystal meth, along with a
forged 200-euro banknote. [@2]

The 32-year-old woman told officers the two men had just stolen the bicycle from
a cellar, and her statement also raised suspicion that she had taken part in the
burglary and may have kept watch during it. [@2] All three suspects were then
arrested, and police said the investigation was continuing, including whether
the trio had committed further offences. [@2]
