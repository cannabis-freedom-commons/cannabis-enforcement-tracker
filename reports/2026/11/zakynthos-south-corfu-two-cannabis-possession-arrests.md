Police arrested two people for possessing small amounts of unprocessed cannabis
over the weekend of 7 to 9 November 2026, one in Zakynthos and one in South
Corfu. [@2]

In Zakynthos, officers of the DIAS rapid-response unit arrested a 20-year-old
Greek man in the early hours of Monday 9 November 2026 for possessing a small
amount of unprocessed cannabis. [@2] In South Corfu, members of the Crime
Prevention and Suppression Team, the Hellenic Police unit known as OPKE,
arrested a 34-year-old foreign national on the evening of Saturday 7 November
2026, also for possessing a small amount of unprocessed cannabis. [@2]

The two detentions were recorded in the offences and incidents bulletin of the
General Regional Police Directorate of the Ionian Islands. [@1]
