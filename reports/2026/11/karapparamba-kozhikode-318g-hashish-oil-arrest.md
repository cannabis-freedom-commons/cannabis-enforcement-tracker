Kozhikode excise officers arrested 23-year-old Ananthu of Panarukandi house at
Vengeri Karapparamba. [@1,2] The arrest came during a flash inspection by the
Excise Inspection team at Karapparamba in Kozhikode. [@1] The inspection team
found 318 grams of hashish oil stored in the house. [@1,2] The inspection was
based on secret information from the Kozhikode Excise Intelligence and
Investigation Bureau. [@1] Excise Circle Inspector A. Prajeeth led the
inspection at the Karapparamba house on the evening of Monday 2 November 2026.
[@1]

Excise officials said the accused had stored the hashish oil for retail sale in
the area. [@1,2] Officials registered a case against him under the NDPS Act,
India's Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances law. [@1,2] Preventive Officers
T. V. Rishith Kumar, M. L. Ashkumar, K. P. Rajesh and K. Jubish were part of the
excise team. [@1] Woman Preventive Officer Shiny and Civil Excise Officers T.
Rajul and M. O. Rajin were also in the team. [@1]
