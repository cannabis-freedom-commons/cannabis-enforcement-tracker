Tiruvallur Town Sub-Inspector Gunasekar and other police officers were
conducting a vehicle check near the Tiruvallur district SP office and the
medical college bus stop. [@1] They stopped people passing by on a motorbike,
searched them and found ganja in their possession. [@1] Police then arrested
both men, identified as 21-year-old Surya of Kanchipuram district and
24-year-old Karthikeyan of Chengalpattu district. [@1] Police said questioning
established that the pair were carrying the ganja on the bike for sale. [@1]

Officers seized 250 grams of ganja from them. [@1] Police also seized the
motorbike used for the transport. [@1] Police registered a case against Surya
and Karthikeyan and are continuing the investigation. [@1]

The report describes banned intoxicants including ganja and gutka as widely
circulating in Tiruvallur district despite police vehicle, shop and tip-based
checks. [@1]
