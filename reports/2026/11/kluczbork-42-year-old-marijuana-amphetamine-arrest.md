Kluczbork plainclothes officers detained a 42-year-old man during a night
identity check in Kluczbork. [@2] The man had drawn attention by waving his
arms, stepping into the roadway and glancing nervously to the sides. [@1]
Officers approached him in an unmarked patrol car, and he first gestured for
them to drive on. [@1] When they stopped, introduced themselves and began the
check, he immediately threw the packet he was holding onto a nearby property.
[@2] He claimed he had thrown away only a pipe and said he had previously been
detained for marijuana possession and wanted to avoid further trouble with the
law. [@1]

Officers recovered the packet and found almost 200 portions of amphetamine
together with marijuana. [@1] Police secured the seized drugs for further
laboratory testing. [@1] The man was arrested and was later charged with drug
possession. [@1]

That offence carries up to three years imprisonment, and the court will decide
his eventual penalty. [@1] Police remind that drug possession is a crime in
Poland and warn that drugs threaten health and life and can lead to addiction
and unpredictable behaviour. [@1]
