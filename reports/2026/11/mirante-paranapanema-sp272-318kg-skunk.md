Three people were arrested in the act on 4 November 2026 after a car was stopped
at km 27 of the Rodovia Olímpio Ferreira da Silva (SP-272) in Mirante do
Paranapanema, São Paulo, with 31.8 kg of skunk. [@1] The three were two men,
aged 22 and 67, and a woman aged 38. [@2]

Police officers in an unmarked car stopped the vehicle after seeing it overtake
dangerously, including on stretches of continuous yellow line. [@1] During the
stop the officers noticed a strong smell of marijuana and found packages
totalling 31.8 kg of the drug. [@1] The packages were in the car's boot. [@2]

Investigators found the group had set out from Ponta Porã, Mato Grosso do Sul,
for the city of São Paulo, where the drug was to be delivered to third parties.
[@1] Police found signs of coordinated action and logistical planning among
those involved, who possibly had scouts, and a tracking device was found with
the seized drug, indicating the load was being monitored. [@1] Police located a
tracking device next to the seized drug, indicating that the shipment of
narcotics was being monitored. [@1]

The three were arrested in the act for drug trafficking, and G1 reported that
they were held for drug trafficking and criminal association. [@2] The incident
was recorded at the police station in Mirante do Paranapanema. [@2]
Investigations continue to identify other people involved and to establish
whether there was an association for trafficking. [@1]
