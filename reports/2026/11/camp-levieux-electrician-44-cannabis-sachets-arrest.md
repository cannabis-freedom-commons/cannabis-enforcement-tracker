On Friday 6 November, officers of the ADSU arrested a 25-year-old electrician
along Rue Madras at Camp-Levieux. [@1] The suspect is a resident of the
Rose-Hill locality. [@1]

Investigators of the anti-drug brigade searched him in the street and found 44
wrappings made of cellophane. [@1] Each of the 44 cellophane wrappings contained
cannabis. [@1] Officers seized the cannabis packets together with Rs 6,650 in
cash found with the suspect. [@1] Police described the cash as suspected
proceeds from selling drugs. [@1]

The suspect was placed in detention after the seizure. [@1] The investigation
was still continuing. [@1]
