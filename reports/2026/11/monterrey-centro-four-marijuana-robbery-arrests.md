Monterrey police detained four people, including two women, in possession of 28
packets of suspected marijuana. [@1] Officers detained Olga "N", 45, and
Mauricio "N", 56, both from Jalisco, and César "N", 44, from Ecatepec, State of
Mexico, at Isaac Garza and Juan Álvarez at 16:25. [@1] Minutes later, at 16:30,
officers detained Victoria Berenice "N", 40, from Tabasco, at Venustiano
Carranza and Madero. [@1] Authorities are investigating the four as suspected
members of a gang that robs bank customers across the city. [@1]

The first stop followed a C4 report about a silver-grey Chevrolet Aveo with
Jalisco plates linked to criminal acts. [@1] After intercepting its occupants,
officers reported finding 23 packets of green herb similar to marijuana. [@1]
The woman was travelling in a black Geely Emgrand with San Luis Potosí plates
when officers found five more packets of a marijuana-like substance in her
possession. [@3] C4 staff had detected the Aveo through surveillance cameras
before officers located and intercepted its occupants. [@3]

Police said the group watched people withdrawing cash from banks and placed
sharp objects in their vehicle tyres to cause punctures. [@3] While members
pretended to help change the tyre, the women opened one of the doors to take the
money and escape. [@1] Investigators link the group to at least three robberies
in Coahuila in March, September and October 2026 and another robbery in Colonia
Reforma in eastern Monterrey. [@1] Officers seized tyre inflators, screwdrivers,
pliers, Michoacán plates and plate covers during the stops. [@1] The four were
placed at the disposal of the corresponding authorities, which will determine
their possible participation and legal situation. [@2]
