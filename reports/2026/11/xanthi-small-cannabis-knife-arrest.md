Police arrested a man in the wider area of Xanthi in the early-morning hours of
Wednesday, 4 November 2026. [@1] The officers who spotted the man belonged to
the Prevention and Mediation Group of the Xanthi Department for the Prosecution
and Solving of Crimes. [@1] That Prevention and Mediation Group operates as part
of the Xanthi investigative department named in the police account. [@1]
According to police, officers found the man holding a nylon package containing a
small amount of raw, unprocessed cannabis. [@1] During the same check, officers
also found him holding a knife. [@1]

Police seized both objects, the package of cannabis and the knife. [@1] The
preliminary inquiry into the case is being conducted by the Xanthi Department
for the Prosecution and Solving of Crimes. [@1] The seized cannabis was
identified specifically as raw, unprocessed cannabis in a single nylon package.
[@1] The Department for the Prosecution and Solving of Crimes is the Xanthi
criminal-investigation department now handling that preliminary inquiry. [@1]
The wider area of Xanthi was the stated place of the early-morning arrest. [@1]
