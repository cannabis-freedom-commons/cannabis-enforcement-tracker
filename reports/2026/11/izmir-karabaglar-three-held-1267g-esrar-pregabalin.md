Police in Karabağlar, a district of İzmir, detained three suspects in a raid on
homes and businesses where drugs and unlicensed weapons were said to be kept.
[@1]

The searches found two unlicensed handguns, 12 9 mm cartridges, 32 grams of
pregabalin, 12.67 grams of esrar and eight drug pills. [@1] Pregabalin is a
prescription medicine used as a drug, not a cannabis product, and its 32 grams
are left out of the cannabis figures. [@2] Esrar is the Turkish term for
cannabis. [@1]

The operation followed information that drugs and unlicensed weapons were being
sold from the addresses, and police acted on the addresses the previous day.
[@1] The raid was carried out by teams of the Karabağlar District Security
Directorate, the district police command. [@2] The three suspects were
identified only by initials, aged 21, 20 and 18. [@1]

Police said the suspects' processing at the police station was still under way.
[@3]
