Local youths detained two men while cannabis was being bought and sold in
Ramanandapur village, Ward 6 of Daganbhuiyan municipality in Feni. [@1] The
detained men were identified as 20-year-old Sohel and 23-year-old Hanif from
Mindipanda village in Durgapur, Netrokona. [@1] Daganbhuiyan Thana police
Sub-Inspector Hafiz reached the scene and took the two detained men into custody
after locals informed the station. [@1] The two men told interrogators they
worked as brick-kiln labourers and had bought the cannabis from Ghori Babul.
[@1]

Acting on their information, police searched Ghori Babul's house at Nur Mohammad
Haji Bari in Ramanandapur in the presence of police, local BNP leaders and
villagers. [@1] Officers recovered about 2 kg of cannabis together with cannabis
plants from the house. [@1] The accused seller, Nurul Alam alias Ghori Babul,
fled after sensing the police presence and could not be arrested. [@1]

The incident occurred on Tuesday, 10 November 2026. [@1] A local leader said
residents had long complained that Babul was selling cannabis. [@1] Daganbhuiyan
station chief Faizul Azim Noman said a case was under process and efforts to
arrest the absconding accused were continuing. [@1]
