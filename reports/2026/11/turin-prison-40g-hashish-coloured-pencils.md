Penitentiary police intercepted an attempt to bring drugs into the Lorusso e
Cutugno district prison in Turin during checks of incoming mail at Pavilion C,
on via Pianezza. [@1] The operation began while officers were inspecting
incoming correspondence, and they examined a registered postal item addressed to
an Italian detainee. [@1]

Inside the parcel they found a box of twelve Giotto coloured pencils whose
wooden shafts had been hollowed out to hide several packets of hashish. [@1] A
drug test confirmed the substance, which weighed about 40 grams gross. [@1] ANSA
described the pencil seizure as a second attempt to get drugs into the Turin
prison, after officers had in recent days found cocaine packed into the cores of
marker pens in a parcel addressed to a detainee. [@2]

No details were published about who sent the registered letter or who was meant
to receive the hashish. [@3] Leo Beneduci, general secretary of Osapp, the
autonomous penitentiary-police union, said the seizure showed the
professionalism of the officers, who were working with a severe shortage of
staff and equipment and with overcrowding that was wearing the staff down. [@2]
Beneduci said that, despite the shortage of staff and equipment and the
overcrowding, the officers continued to maintain security, and that the
institutions now had to intervene in concrete terms. [@2]
