During the fifth episode of the RTL4 police documentary series Bureau Groningen,
presenter Ewout Genemans and officers Yorick and Sezgin stopped a car in the
city after the driver changed direction when he saw the patrol car. [@1] A
saliva test on the driver came back positive; the driver had been in contact
with the police in 2025 over drug dealing and driving under the influence of
drugs. [@1]

The driver said he had hashish in his pocket and handed a bag of drugs to
officer Sezgin, who put him in handcuffs. [@1] Officers found an Action plastic
bag full of drugs in the car and arrested the passenger as well. [@1] The
officers also found a stack of 10-euro and 20-euro banknotes in the car. [@1]

Bureau Groningen airs every Thursday at 20:30 on RTL4. [@1]
