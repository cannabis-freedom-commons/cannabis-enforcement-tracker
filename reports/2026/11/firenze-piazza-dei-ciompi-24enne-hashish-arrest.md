Carabinieri of the Florence mobile radio unit arrested a 24-year-old man,
described as de facto homeless in Italy, in flagrante for evasion. [@1] The man
was under an alternative semi-liberty regime and had failed to return to prison
by 10 p.m. the previous evening, making himself untraceable. [@1] He was the
subject of an urgent search notice when officers spotted him with other people
near Piazza dei Ciompi. [@1] The square is in central Florence, in the
Sant'Ambrogio area, and the stop happened on Sunday afternoon. [@1] The stop
took place on the afternoon of Sunday 8 November 2026 during a territorial
control patrol. [@3] During the check the young man appeared uncooperative and
disoriented, and he was taken to the barracks for photo-identification
procedures. [@2]

A personal search found two packets of hashish weighing 1.10 grams in total on
him. [@1] The drug was placed under administrative seizure and the young man was
reported to the prefecture as a drug user. [@4] The referral was made as a user
of narcotics under article 75 of Presidential Decree 309/1990. [@3]

After a fast-track validation hearing, the Florence judge validated the
Carabinieri's arrest and ordered the immediate transfer of the accused to La
Dogaia prison in Prato to resume detention. [@1]
