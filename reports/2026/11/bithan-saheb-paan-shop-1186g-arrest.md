Bithan police in Samastipur, Bihar, raided the Saheb paan shop in Bithan Bazar
after learning that ganja was kept there. [@1] Officers said they verified the
information before entering and searching the shop. [@1] Officers arrested one
dealer during the shop raid. [@1] Police identified the arrested man as Ramdular
Mukhiya, also called Ramudgar Mukhiya. [@1] He was described as the son of late
Shivshankar Mukhiya. [@1]

The search recovered 1 kilogram 186 grams of ganja, equal to 1.186 kilograms.
[@1] Police seized the ganja and took it into custody with the arrest. [@1]

Bithan station house officer Raj Kishore Rai said the action at the Saheb paan
shop followed secret information. [@1] Rai said police in the station area are
running a continuous campaign against the trade and consumption of illegal
narcotics. [@1] Police added that legal proceedings against the arrested man
were being completed. [@1]
