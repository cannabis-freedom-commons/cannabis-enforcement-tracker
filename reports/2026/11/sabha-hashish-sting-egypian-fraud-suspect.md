Sabha Security Directorate announced the arrest of an Egyptian national wanted
by Sabha City police station on a fraud charge. [@1] A statement from the
directorate said the man, born in 1993, identified only by the initials A.M.A.,
works for a Hajj and Umrah company and is Egyptian. [@1]

While he was at the investigation office, another man called his personal phone
and, asked about the call, he said the caller buys hashish from him. [@1]
Detectives from the station set an ambush for the caller, and the Egyptian man
phoned him to order hashish and fixed a delivery place. [@1] Officers detained
the caller during the handover and took him to the station, where he appeared
visibly drunk and was found carrying a bag of alcohol. [@1] The second detainee
is a Libyan, born in 2000, identified only by the initials A.S.A.S., and
unemployed. [@1] After investigation the two men were referred to the Public
Prosecution on charges of drug trafficking, drinking alcohol, drug use and
fraud. [@1]
