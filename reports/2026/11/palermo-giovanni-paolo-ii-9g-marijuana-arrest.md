Police in Palermo arrested a 30-year-old man identified as G. S., stopped in
Piazza Giovanni Paolo II, a short distance from the stadium. [@1] Officers of
the city police headquarters' general prevention office spotted the man, already
known to them, riding an electric bicycle along Via Ausonia. [@1] When officers
ordered him to stop he ignored the order, rode on pavements and the wrong way
down several streets, and abandoned the bicycle before being blocked. [@1]

A search found eight wraps of marijuana, confirmed by laboratory tests, weighing
almost 9 grams in total. [@1] Officers also seized about 70 euros in cash, which
police considered proceeds of drug dealing, and a mobile phone. [@1] The
electric bicycle was also seized. [@1]

The man was previously convicted of a bank robbery and had been on probation
with the social services until a few weeks before the arrest. [@1] He was
arrested and placed under house arrest pending a validation hearing before the
investigating judge. [@1]
