A fast-track court under India's Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act
in Panipat sentenced Kullu resident Sundar Singh to four years of rigorous
imprisonment for smuggling 400 grams of charas, a cannabis resin. [@1] The court
also imposed a fine of 60,000 rupees on him. [@1] He faces one additional year
in prison if he does not pay the fine. [@1] Singh is a resident of Tandi village
in Kullu district, Himachal Pradesh. [@1]

Police caught Singh at the Panipat bus stand during a patrol on 16 December
2020. [@1] A search recovered 400 grams of charas from his possession. [@1] He
told police he had brought the charas from Himachal Pradesh to sell in Panipat.
[@1] Sector-29 police station police then registered a case against him under
the NDPS Act. [@1]

The case was heard in the fast-track court of Additional District and Sessions
Judge Yogesh Chaudhary. [@1] His defence sought leniency because Singh has no
prior criminal record, is his family's sole earner, has ailing elderly parents
and supports two daughters. [@1] The government lawyer sought a strict sentence,
citing the seriousness of the offence and the harmful effects of drugs on
society. [@1] After hearing both sides, the court imposed the four-year prison
term and the 60,000-rupee fine. [@1]
