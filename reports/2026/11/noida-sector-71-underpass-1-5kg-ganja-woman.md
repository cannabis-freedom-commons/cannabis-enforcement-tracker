Sector-113 police station in Noida arrested a woman on 6 November 2026 on
suspicion of ganja trafficking. [@1,2] She was held beside the service road of the
Sector-71 underpass, in Noida in Uttar Pradesh's Gautam Buddh Nagar district.
[@1] Police said a search of her found 1 kg 500 g of illegal ganja, which
officers seized. [@1,2]

Police identified the arrested woman as Rafsana, 25, a native of Kisamat
Mukorari village in Dinhata police station area of Cooch Behar district, West
Bengal. [@1] Police said she was living in Sarfabad village in Sector 75 of
Gautam Buddh Nagar at the time. [@1]

The team had been carrying out checks and surveillance in the area with the help
of local intelligence before the arrest. [@1] Police registered case 496/2026 at
Sector-113 station under sections 8 and 20 of the NDPS Act, India's main drug
law, and began the required legal action against her. [@1] The same police
statement also mentions prior case 441/2022 under sections 8 and 20 of the NDPS
Act. [@1] Police said the campaign against drug trafficking and the illegal
trade in the district would continue. [@1]

The article states that case 441/2022, also registered under sections 8/20 of the NDPS Act, is on record against the woman at Sector-113 station. [@3] The team laid a cordon near the service road of the Sector-71 underpass after a local intelligence tip before stopping and searching the woman there. [@3]
