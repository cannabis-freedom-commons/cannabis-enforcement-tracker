Cannabis was being grown among cotton and pigeon pea, known locally as tuvar, in
a field at Nimbol village in Dhar district, Madhya Pradesh. [@1] Police from the
Nisarpur outpost acted on a tip that cannabis plants had been planted in a field
near a pond in Nimbol, searched the field and seized 120 green plants. [@1]

The plants weighed 78.15 kg and police put their estimated value at 11.72 lakh
rupees. [@1] Kamal Bamaniya, aged 50, was found in his field when police carried
out the raid. [@2] Police said the accused had occupied the government grazing
land on which the cannabis was being grown. [@2] The 120 green plants were found
hidden among the cotton, police said. [@2]

Police pulled up the plants in the presence of panch witnesses and seized them.
[@2] A case was registered against him under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic
Substances Act. [@2] Police said they were investigating where the cannabis was
to be supplied and who else was involved. [@2]

The raid was part of Operation Prahar, the Dhar district drive against narcotics
run on the instructions of superintendent of police Sachin Kumar Sharma. [@2]
Madhya Pradesh police headquarters listed the Nimbol seizure in its Kukshi
police area among actions in Dhar, Khargone, Barwani and Khandwa districts over
three days that it said netted 11,669 kg of cannabis plants worth more than 30
crore rupees. [@3]
