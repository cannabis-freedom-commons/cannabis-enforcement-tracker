Border Guard land patrols and the General Directorate of Narcotics Control,
Saudi Arabia's anti-narcotics agency, carried out four separate security
operations in Jazan, Qassim, Riyadh and Jeddah. [@1]

In Jazan region, Border Guard land patrols arrested three violators of the
border security law accused of smuggling 90 kg of khat, a stimulant leaf that is
not cannabis. [@1,3] In Qassim region, the directorate arrested two people accused
of promoting hashish, a cannabis resin, and 2,837 tablets of amphetamine. [@1,3]
Sobra, a Saudi news outlet, reported the Qassim arrests of two citizens accused
of promoting hashish and 2,837 amphetamine tablets. [@2] In Riyadh region, the
directorate arrested two residents accused of promoting methamphetamine, known
locally as shabu. [@1] In Jeddah governorate, the directorate arrested two
residents accused of promoting cocaine. [@1]

The four operations added up to nine people detained: three in Jazan, two in
Qassim, two in Riyadh and two in Jeddah. [@1,3] The security agencies said all the
suspects were detained, initial legal procedures were taken against them, and
they were referred with the seizures to the competent authority for the
penalties set by law. [@1]

The agencies called on citizens and residents to report drug smuggling or
promotion to the Border Guard on 994, the directorate on 995 or 995@gdnc.gov.sa,
911 in Mecca, Medina, Riyadh and the Eastern Province, and 999 elsewhere, saying
all reports would be treated in strict confidence. [@1]
