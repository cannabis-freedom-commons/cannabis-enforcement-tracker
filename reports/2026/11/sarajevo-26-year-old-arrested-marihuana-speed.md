Officers of the narcotics department of the Sarajevo Canton interior ministry
arrested a 26-year-old Sarajevo man at 14:00 on 11 November 2026. [@1] The
suspect is identified only by his initials, H.K., born in 2000 in Sarajevo,
where he lives. [@1]

He was arrested on suspicion of the unauthorised production and placing into
circulation of narcotic drugs under article 238(1) of the criminal code of the
Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina. [@1] A search found and seized an
unspecified quantity of material resembling the drugs marihuana, the local term
for cannabis, and speed, together with a digital scale and a pistol with a
magazine and ammunition. [@1] ALFA TV carries the same account of the arrest,
search and seizure, crediting the Sarajevo Canton interior ministry. [@2]

The duty prosecutor of the Sarajevo Canton Prosecutor's Office was notified and
the man was handed over to the ministry's detention facility. [@1] Police said
they were continuing the criminal processing of the suspect. [@1]
