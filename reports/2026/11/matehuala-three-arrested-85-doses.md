San Luis Potosí's state civil guard detained three people and seized 85 doses of suspected marijuana in Matehuala. [@1,2,3,4]

Officers of the Guardia Civil Estatal (GCE), the state civil guard, detained two
men and a woman in Matehuala during intelligence operations in San Luis Potosí's
Altiplano region. [@4] The detainees were identified as Francisco “N”, aged 20,
Ildelfonso “N”, aged 63, and Mariana “N”, aged 23. [@4] The three detainees were
placed at the disposal of the competent authorities, which must determine their
legal situation and continue the corresponding investigations. [@3]

In the same intervention, officers seized 85 doses of suspected marijuana
described by the authorities as presunta marihuana. [@3] Officers also seized
two motorcycles in the same actions. [@4]

The Secretariat of Security and Citizen Protection said the interventions formed
part of the strategic deployments the corporation maintains across the four
regions of the state. [@3] Intelligence information described the three
detainees as possibly linked to a drug-distribution network operating in the
Altiplano, with investigators still to establish any responsibility. [@4] The
Secretariat said it will maintain intelligence work and security operations
throughout the state territory. [@3]
