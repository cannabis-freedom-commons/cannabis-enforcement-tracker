Guardia Civil Estatal officers detained three people suspected of drug
distribution in the municipality of Matehuala, San Luis Potosí, as part of
reinforced security and surveillance in the Altiplano region. [@1] The three
were identified only as Francisco “N”, aged 20, Ildelfonso “N”, aged 63, and
Mariana “N”, aged 23. [@1] State agents made the detentions and seized various
doses of marijuana and the two motorcycles on which the three were travelling.
[@1] Intelligence information described the detainees as possibly linked to a
narcotics-distribution network. [@1]

The detainees and the seized items were placed at the disposal of the competent
authorities for the corresponding investigations. [@1] The operation was
presented as the result of preventive and intelligence work by the Guardia Civil
Estatal (GCE). [@1] The published summary totals the seizure at 85 doses of
marijuana, alongside the three detentions and two motorcycles. [@1] The force
framed the Matehuala action as part of its wider reinforcement of security and
vigilance in the Altiplano region. [@1]
