Federal Highway Police officers seized 512.55 kg of drugs hidden inside a
manganese ore load on the BR-262 at about 10am on 11 November 2026 in Três
Lagoas. [@1] The Federal Highway Police found 512.55 kg of drugs during the
inspection, arrested three men and seized two trucks. [@2] The cannabis
component of the seizure was 300.35 kg of skunk in 413 bales and 118.60 kg of
marijuana in 110 blocks, together with 27 kg of hashish. [@1] The first truck
stopped, an Iveco Stralis HD carrying manganese ore from Corumbá with a declared
destination of Itapeva in São Paulo state, was found to have its driver nervous
and with a prior drug-trafficking record. [@1]

A second truck, a Volvo FH12 heading from Três Lagoas toward Campo Grande, was
suspected of acting as a lookout vehicle for the drug transport and was also
brought in for unloading. [@1] During unloading the officers found numerous
bales of drugs hidden under the ore, and the weighing done with the support of
the Civil Police confirmed the different drug types. [@1]

The driver of the truck where the drugs were found confessed he had been
recruited in Corumbá to transport the drugs to the state of São Paulo. [@1]
Three men in the two trucks were arrested in flagrante for drug trafficking and
sent to the Três Lagoas DEPAC police station with the trucks, mobile phones and
all seized material. [@1] The police described it as the second large seizure of
drugs in ore loads by the PRF in Três Lagoas in 15 days, the first being about
487 kg found in an iron ore load on 3 November, totalling roughly a tonne. [@1]
