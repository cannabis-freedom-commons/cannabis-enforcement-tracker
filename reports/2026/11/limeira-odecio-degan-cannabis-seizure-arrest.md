Military police detained a man in the Odécio Degan neighbourhood of Limeira, São
Paulo, on the night of Tuesday 10 November 2026. [@2] Rápido no Ar reported that
the man ran when he saw a Força Tática patrol car and abandoned a bag with drugs
during the chase. [@2] Officers had been patrolling Rua Lauro Camargo da
Silveira when they saw him; he fled toward the green area, where the team set up
a cordon. [@2] They caught him after a few minutes. [@2] The abandoned bag held
portions of marijuana, cocaine and crack, but no quantities were disclosed. [@2]
He was taken to the Limeira police station while the case continued. [@2]

Policial Padrão reported that Military Police officers on patrol near a
children's school saw three suspects acting suspiciously and seized more than 1
kg of marijuana along with portions of crack and cocaine. [@1] Policial Padrão
described the action as reinforcing the fight against drug trafficking in the
Piracicaba region. [@1]
