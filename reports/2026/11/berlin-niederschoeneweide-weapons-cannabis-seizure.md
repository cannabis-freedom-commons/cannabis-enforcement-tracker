Police searched a flat in Berlin-Niederschöneweide and seized several weapons
and suspected cannabis. [@1] The visit was initially only to take comparison
photographs in the flat of a 39-year-old man as part of an investigation into
gang drug trafficking. [@1] Berlin police shared details of the operation on
Instagram. [@1] Officers first met the man in the hallway in the morning and he
opened the flat door only reluctantly. [@1] He opened the flat door only
reluctantly. [@1]

The subsequent search found a non-operational AK-47 rifle and a presumably
operational Russian military weapon with drum magazines and 108 rounds of
ammunition. [@1] Officers also discovered a blackjack, two telescopic batons, a
baseball bat and a concealed knife. [@1] They also found suspected cannabis in a
not-small quantity, the German legal term for an amount above the threshold for
serious trafficking cases. [@1]

During the search the 39-year-old threw his mobile phone over the balcony
railing. [@1] Officers were able to recover it undamaged. [@1] The criminal
police and the Berlin public prosecutor’s office investigations are ongoing.
