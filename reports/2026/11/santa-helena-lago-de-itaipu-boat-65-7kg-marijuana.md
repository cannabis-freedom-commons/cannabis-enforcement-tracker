Federal Police and Paraná Military Police seized a boat carrying 65.7 kg of
pressed marijuana in Lago de Itaipu, in the rural area of Santa Helena, in
western Paraná, on the morning of Wednesday 11 November 2026. [@1] The action
was carried out jointly by the Federal Police through its Special Maritime
Police Unit (NEPOM) and the Military Police through its Border Police Battalion
(BPFRON). [@1]

The incident was recorded at about 10:30 during a boat-borne patrol of the lake.
[@1] The teams spotted a boat of roughly five metres crossing the lake from
Paraguay towards Brazil, heading for one of the reservoir's arms. [@1] On
noticing the police approaching, the boat's operator abandoned the craft and
fled into a woodland area, and was not located. [@1] A search of the boat found
seven bales of pressed marijuana totalling 65.7 kg of the drug. [@1]

The craft and the drug were seized and sent to the Federal Police station in Foz
do Iguaçu for the legal procedures. [@1] The Federal Police's own statement
confirms the boat and the drug were sent to its Foz do Iguaçu station for the
legal procedures, after the operator fled and was not located. [@2]
