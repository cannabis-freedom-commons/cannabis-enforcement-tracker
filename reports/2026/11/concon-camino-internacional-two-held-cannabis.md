Officers from Concón's 4th station, the 4ª Comisaría de Concón, carried out a
vehicular fiscalización, a roadside vehicle inspection, on Camino Internacional
in Concón. [@1] Officers surprised the car's occupants smoking handmade
marijuana cigarettes, called cigarrillos artesanales de marihuana in the local
accounts. [@2] Officers detained two men of foreign nationality following the
vehicle search. [@3] The two men were placed at the disposition of the tribunal,
the court that decides custody, the same day. [@2]

Officers searched the car and found additional cigarettes containing the same
marijuana substance. [@1] Inside the same vehicle officers found three payment
machines for processing card payments with tarjetas. [@3] Officers also seized a
bladed weapon, an arma blanca, and a large sum of cash in the same search. [@2]

The Camino Internacional stop was the cannabis-related procedure among two
police procedures reported in Concón that day. [@1] Officers linked those finds
to the decision to detain the two foreign men and send them to the court. [@1]
