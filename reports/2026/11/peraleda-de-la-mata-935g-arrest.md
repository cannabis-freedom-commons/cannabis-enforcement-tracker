Guardia Civil officers patrolling a service area in Peraleda de la Mata, in the
province of Cáceres, observed a 38-year-old man circulating on a bicycle. [@1]
He was travelling with a bicycle and a backpack. [@1] When the cyclist noticed
the police presence, he tried to escape cross-country but managed only a few
metres before officers intercepted him. [@1]

Officers then searched his belongings and located two plastic bags holding 935
grams of cannabis buds. [@1] The intervention ended with his detention as a
presumed perpetrator of an offence against public health for drug trafficking.
[@1] During the subsequent identity check, officers discovered he had a pending
judicial requisition for search, detention and appearance issued by a court in
Algeciras, in Cádiz. [@1]

The force presented the episode as the most recent in a series of actions
developed over recent months by the Guardia Civil at different points across the
province. [@1] Those different cases responded to different circumstances and
did not form part of the same operation. [@1]
