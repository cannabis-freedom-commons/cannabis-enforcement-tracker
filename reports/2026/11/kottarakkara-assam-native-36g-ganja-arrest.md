Police in Kottarakkara, a town in Kerala's Kollam district, arrested a native of
Assam during a narcotics enforcement check. [@1] Police carried out the
inspection around midnight on Tuesday, 10 November 2026, and seized narcotics
from his possession. [@2] Officers recovered 36 grams of ganja, the Indian term
for herbal cannabis, from the arrested man. [@1]

Alongside the cannabis, police seized a consignment of brown sugar valued at Rs
30 lakh (3 million rupees, as one lakh is 100,000). [@2] The brown sugar had
been packed inside plastic covers and kept in eight plastic boxes. [@1] Police
found that the accused had brought the narcotics into Kerala from the
northeastern state of Assam. [@2] According to information received by
investigators, the drugs held in one of his plastic covers had already been sold
in Kochi before his arrest. [@1]

Police stated that officers were questioning the arrested man in detail
following the midnight seizure. [@1] The operation in Kottarakkara came days
after local police seized a large quantity of MDMA in the same town. [@3]
