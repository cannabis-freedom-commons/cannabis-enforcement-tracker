Rockingham police arrested and charged Aric Lamont Ray Jr. with one felony count
each of possession with intent to manufacture, sell or deliver marijuana,
possession of marijuana and possession of a firearm by a felon. [@1] Warrants
allege that on 10 November 2026 the 22-year-old from Rockingham had five
individual bags of marijuana totaling 188 grams (6.63 ounces), a digital scale,
a rolling paper dispenser and a pack of cigarillos. [@1] Ray was also allegedly
carrying a Smith & Wesson .38 Special revolver. [@1] He is accused of running
from a Rockingham Police officer during a traffic stop. [@1] He also faces one
misdemeanor count each of possession of marijuana paraphernalia, carrying a
concealed gun and resisting a public officer. [@1] Ray was booked into the
Richmond County Jail, where he was held on a combined $65,000 secured bond. [@1]
He is scheduled to appear in court on 19 November. [@1]

Online court records show Ray was convicted in 2024 of conspiracy to commit
discharging a firearm into an occupied property. [@1] Four other charges in that
case were dismissed as part of a plea agreement. [@1] On 22 July he was charged
with a probation violation for not paying court fees, and records show he still
owes $1,495.50. [@1]
