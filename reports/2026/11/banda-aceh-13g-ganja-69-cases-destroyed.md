The Banda Aceh district prosecutor's office, known as Kejari Banda Aceh,
destroyed crime evidence and state-confiscated goods from cases that had
obtained final and binding court judgments. [@1] The destruction took place in
the courtyard of the Kejari Banda Aceh office in Banda Aceh on Tuesday 10
November 2026. [@1] District attorney Bobbi Sandri led the exercise, attended by
members of the Banda Aceh regional leadership coordination forum and other
guests. [@1] Asset recovery section head Rajeskana said the destroyed goods were
evidence from 69 cases handled from April to October 2026 and that the
destruction carried out court judgments with final legal force. [@1]

The destroyed evidence included 13.27 grams of ganja, alongside 41.84 grams of
methamphetamine and implements such as bongs, pipes, glass and lighters. [@1] Of
the 69 cases, 35 concerned public security and order and other general crimes,
the largest group. [@1] Rajeskana said 25 of the cases were narcotics cases and
nine concerned crimes against persons and property. [@1] Officers destroyed the
goods by burning and crushing them so they could no longer be used. [@1]

Rajeskana said the evidence came from dozens of general criminal cases and qanun
jinayah cases decided by final court rulings. [@1] The Sharia court's account
says the exercise began at 09.00 and was held by the Banda Aceh prosecutor's
office under an invitation dated 3 November 2026. [@2] The same account says the
destroyed evidence covered general criminal cases from April to October 2026.
