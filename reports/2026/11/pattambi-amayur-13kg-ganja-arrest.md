Pattambi police broke up cannabis sales run from rented quarters housing a
family in Pattambi in Palakkad district. [@2] Mandal lived with his family in
rented quarters at Amayur East Vallur. [@2] A joint Pattambi police and DANSAF
squad search acting on secret information caught the suspect. [@1] The team led
by Pattambi Sub-Inspector K. Fazil Rahman took him into custody. [@1]

Police found 13 kilograms of cannabis from the man. [@1] The cannabis had been
stored in the quarters for sale. [@1] Live Palakkad likewise reported 13
kilograms recovered from the rented quarters. [@3] Police said the seized
cannabis was worth lakhs on the market. [@1]

Mandal worked in local concrete construction and allegedly used that work as
cover for bringing in cannabis from other states for sale. [@2] He told police
that his friend Anoop Mandal supplied the cannabis from Coimbatore. [@1] Police
are investigating whether a larger drug-mafia network is behind him. [@1] Police
said anti-drug checks in the Pattambi area would be intensified. [@1]
