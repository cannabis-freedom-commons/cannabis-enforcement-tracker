Muzaffarpur Sadar police raided an old hut-house near Mehta Field in Yaduvanshi
Nagar, adjacent to Yadav Nagar, based on a confidential tip-off about illegal
cannabis being hidden and sold there. [@1] The search seized 78 grams of illegal
ganja and 67 small empty plastic packets kept for making puriya, the local term
for small retail cannabis packets. [@1]

The main accused, Sushil Kumar, a resident of Bahadur Nagar, escaped when police
arrived, using darkness and narrow lanes to flee the scene. [@1]

A case was registered at Sadar Thana against Sushil Kumar under the applicable
sections of the NDPS Act, and police said they were conducting searches at his
likely locations with a view to arresting him. [@1]
