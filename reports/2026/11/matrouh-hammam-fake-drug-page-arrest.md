Egypt's security services arrested a man in Matrouh Governorate for running a
social-media page that promoted drug sales for money. [@1] The General
Administration for Combating Information Technology Crimes, part of the
communications and information technology sector, spotted the page together with
the Public Security sector. [@1] Investigations identified the page's
administrator as a fuel-station attendant with criminal information who lives in
the El Hammam police district of Matrouh. [@1]

Three accounts say the man held no drugs at all when he was arrested. [@3]
Officers found a mobile phone with him, and an examination showed it held
evidence confirming his criminal activity. [@1] Faced with the findings, the man
confessed to creating and running the page to defraud people who wanted to buy
drugs, asking them to transfer money to him and then switching off his phone.
[@2]

Police took the necessary legal measures in the case. [@1]
