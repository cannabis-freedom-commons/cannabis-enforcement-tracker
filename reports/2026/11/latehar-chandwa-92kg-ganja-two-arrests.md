Chandwa police station police in Latehar arrested two absconding accused in a
narcotics-smuggling case. [@1,2] Both men were sent to jail in judicial custody.
[@1] Police identified them as Sumant Kumar Chandravanshi and Sawan Kumar,
residents of New Sidhauli in Dalmianagar, Rohtas district, Bihar. [@1,2]

Police registered the case as Chandwa police station case number 47/26 on 11
March 2026. [@1] The case began after two vehicles collided near Sikni. [@1]
Officers searched the vehicles and recovered a total of 92 kilograms of ganja
kept in 87 packets from vehicle number JH05AS-5465. [@1] Both accused had been
absconding in this case. [@1]

Police arrested them about eight months later. [@1]
