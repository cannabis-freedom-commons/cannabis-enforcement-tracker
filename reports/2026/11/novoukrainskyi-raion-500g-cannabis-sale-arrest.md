Police in Kirovohrad Oblast detained a 31-year-old local resident of
Novoukrainskyi Raion at his home and notified him of suspicion. [@1]

The account came from the Main Directorate of the National Police in Kirovohrad
Oblast. [@1]

During the search, detectives found and seized 500 grams of cannabis, which the
man was keeping for further sale. [@1] Afisha Kropyvnytskyi reported that
officers from the criminal police sector together with investigators of police
department No. 2 in Dobrovelychkivka, under the procedural guidance of the
district prosecutor's office, searched the man's home. [@2] Afisha Kropyvnytskyi
put the seized amount at half a kilogram of cannabis. [@2]

Investigators notified him of suspicion under part 2 of article 307 of Ukraine's
Criminal Code, covering unlawful storage or sale of narcotic drugs. [@1] He was
detained under article 208 of Ukraine's Criminal Procedure Code. [@2] That part
of the article carries 6 to 10 years in prison with confiscation of property,
and the pretrial investigation continues. [@2]

Zlatopil named the officers as detectives and investigators of the
Novoukrainskyi district police department, based in the village of
Dobrovelychkivka. [@3]
