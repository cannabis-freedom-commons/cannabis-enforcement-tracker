Guardia Civil Estatal officers detained four people in two operations in
Matehuala, San Luis Potosí. [@1] In the first operation they detained Sarahí
“N”, 22, and Ricardo “N”, 19, who is from Tamaulipas, and seized bags of
marijuana and doses of a substance similar to crystal. [@1] Officers also seized
a vehicle and seven ponchallantas, metal spikes used to puncture vehicle tyres,
in that operation. [@1]

In the second operation, officers patrolling the community of La Lagunita saw
two men who reportedly fired a gun and tried to escape on foot. [@1] After
catching them, officers detained Miguel “N”, 29, and Óscar “N”, 21, and seized
two firearms. [@1]

The Guardia Civil Estatal described the two interventions as blows against
presumed criminal activity in the municipality. [@2] It said the operations came
from intelligence, prevention and surveillance work it maintains across San Luis
Potosí’s four regions. [@2] The state Secretariat of Security and Citizen
Protection said they formed part of its Integral Security Strategy. [@2] All
four detainees and the seized items were placed at the disposal of the competent
authority for investigation. [@2]
