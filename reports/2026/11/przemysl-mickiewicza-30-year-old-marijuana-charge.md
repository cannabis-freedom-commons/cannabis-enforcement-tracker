Officers from the intelligence-intervention unit of the Przemysl police stopped
two men on Mickiewicza Street in Przemysl on Tuesday after suspecting that a
Przemysl resident might hold banned substances. [@1] Officers detained both men,
aged 30 and 42, who are both residents of Przemysl. [@1] During the inquiry
police established that the seized drugs belonged to the 30-year-old, focusing
the case on him alone. [@1]

During the intervention officers secured narcotics in the form of dried plant
material. [@1] Preliminary testing of the seized substance showed that it was
marijuana. [@1] The Przemysl police account says the 30-year-old was detained to
answer for possession of narcotics. [@2] The account is attributed to KMP
Przemysl, the Przemysl city police. [@1]

The man was questioned and was charged with possession of narcotics. [@1] The
30-year-old admitted the alleged act. [@1] He faces up to three years of
imprisonment for the possession charge. [@1] The investigation in the case is
continuing under the supervision of the District Prosecutor's Office in
Przemysl. [@1]
