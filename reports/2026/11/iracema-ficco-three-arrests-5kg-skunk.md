Three men were caught in the act and arrested during an operation in the
municipality of Iracema, Roraima, on Tuesday 10 November 2026. [@1] The
operation was carried out by Roraima's integrated task force against organised
crime, FICCO/RR, which brings together the Federal Police, Civil Police,
Military Police, state penal police and the public security secretariat. [@1]

The action began with intelligence on the whereabouts of a suspect who had
escaped during Operation Just in Time, an earlier FICCO sweep that arrested 11
people in Boa Vista in October. [@1] Officers tracked the fugitive to a bar near
a municipal school in Iracema, where surveillance revealed rapid visits typical
of retail drug sales. [@1] When officers approached, two men tried to flee while
a third remained inside, and one fleeing suspect threw away a wrap of skunk that
police recovered. [@1] Searches inside the bar and an adjoining room yielded
portions of skunk, crack and cocaine, together with more than R$ 6,000 in cash.
[@1]

At a neighbouring residence belonging to one of the suspects, officers
discovered about 5 kg of skunk hidden inside an old washing machine. [@1]
Further searches at a farm near the Rio Branco uncovered about 4 kg of crack and
three precision scales. [@1] The three men were taken to the Federal Police
regional superintendency in Roraima to formalise their arrests in flagrante.
[@1] The three suspects remained in custody at the disposal of the courts as
investigations continued to identify other people involved and map the drug
distribution network. [@2]
