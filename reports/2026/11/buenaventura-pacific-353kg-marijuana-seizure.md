Five Colombian men were captured during two maritime operations carried out by
the Navy in Pacific waters, with the loads hidden aboard two boats. [@1,2,4,5] The
first boarding was of the langostera-type boat Bendecido 100%, carrying three
men, which was taken to the Coast Guard facilities in Buenaventura after
suspected breaches of maritime rules were detected. [@1,4,6] The second operation
was against a viento y marea-type boat crewed by two Colombian citizens. [@1,4,5]
The five detainees and the seized loads were placed at the disposal of the
competent authorities for judicial proceedings. [@1,3,4,6]

A specialised inspection supported by an antinarcotics canine found
purpose-built compartments in the bench seating, where 500 rectangular packages
of illicit substances were hidden. [@1,4,5] Aboard the second boat officers found 13
bundles containing 359 packages with characteristics similar to those of
narcotic substances. [@1,4,6] Preliminary tests by the National Police confirmed the
seized material was 500.5 kilograms of cocaine hydrochloride and 353.6 kilograms
of marijuana, for a combined total of 854.1 kilograms. [@1,3,4,5]

The Navy said the two Pacific interdictions represented an economic impact of
more than 26 million dollars on drug-trafficking structures, a combined-drugs
estimate not recorded as a cannabis-only value. [@1,4,5] The Navy said the seizures
kept about 1.3 million doses of these combined substances from reaching illegal
markets. [@1,3,4,5] The operations were carried out by units of the Buenaventura Coast
Guard Station. [@1,4,6]

Captain Daniel Gutiérrez, commander of the Buenaventura Coast Guard Station, said the operations hurt the structures that run drug-trafficking routes along the coast. [@5] The Navy said the results complemented its commitment to protecting maritime areas and contributing to the safety of people along Colombia's Pacific coast. [@6]
