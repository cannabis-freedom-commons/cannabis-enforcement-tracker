At the Posadas Federal Court, a couple pleaded guilty through an abbreviated
trial and received prison sentences of four years and four years and one month
for transporting 216.196 kilograms of marijuana and disobeying a stop signal
during a Gendarmería Nacional operation in Dos de Mayo. [@1] Nicolas Ivan Pieka,
a 33-year-old mechanic, agreed to a four years and one month prison sentence,
while Karen Antonela De Cuadra, also 33, agreed to four years. [@1] The couple
had been charged with drug trafficking in real concurrence with disobedience to
authority, Pieka for two acts and De Cuadra for one. [@1]

The underlying interception dates to 25 November 2025 at 7:25 pm, when gendarmes
of the Aristobulo del Valle section were running a vehicle check on provincial
route 11 in the Las Campanas district of Dos de Mayo. [@1] A black Volkswagen
Bora driven by Pieka with De Cuadra as passenger ignored the stop signal and
kept going. [@1] After a pursuit, officers found the vehicle hidden among the
vegetation on an access road to a grotto, where the occupants were unloading
bags wrapped in black sacks, and the pair ran off but were caught about ten
metres away. [@1] With the sniffer dog Batron, gendarmes located six bundles
holding 299 marijuana packages weighing a total of 216.196 kilograms. [@1]

The two lived in Obera at the time of the offence; the woman is from that city
and the man from the capital. [@1] The court set De Cuadra to serve her
four-year term on home detention because she has three minor children in her
care and is their main provider, with an electronic monitoring device and
monthly checks by the Posadas execution authority. [@1] Pieka, currently held at
Gendarmería National squad 9 in Obera, must serve four years and one month in a
federal prison, with the judge asking for his transfer to a facility with
capacity, prioritising the Unit 17 in Candelaria for family and personal
reasons. [@1] The ruling also ordered the forfeiture of the Volkswagen Bora,
owned by Pieka and used to carry the drugs. [@1]

Gendarmeria confirmed the interception, finding 299 packages of cannabis sativa
weighing 216.196 kilograms and detaining the driver and passenger with the Obera
federal prosecutor and court ordering the drug and vehicle seized. [@2]

The abbreviated-trial agreement at the Posadas Federal Court fixing the
four-year and four-years-and-one-month sentences was corroborated by a separate
account describing the November 2025 discovery of the load. [@3]
