An administrative team led by Sadar circle officer Rahul Shankar raided a
kirana, or grocery, shop in Boksa village in Buxar district, Bihar, on a tip-off
and found ganja, the local name for cannabis. [@1] During a search of the shop,
the police and administrative team recovered 1 kilogram of ganja. [@1] The
shop's owner was named as Amarnath Prasad. [@1] The administration seized the
ganja and began legal proceedings against the shop owner, Amarnath Prasad, as
the rules require. [@1] Rahul Shankar confirmed the action and said the
administration was running a sustained campaign against illegal activity and the
drug trade in the area. [@1] The tip-off said ganja was being traded illegally
from the shop in Boksa village. [@1] Officials warned that anyone involved in
such illegal trades in the area would not be spared and that similar raids would
continue. [@1]
