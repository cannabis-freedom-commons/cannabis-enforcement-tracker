Local police in Eturunagaram mandal centre in Mulugu district arrested a young
man who was carrying ganja. [@1] Officers seized 360 grams of ganja from him, a
sub-inspector named Mahesh said. [@1] The man is from Rohiru village and had got
off an autorickshaw and was walking near a checkpost. [@1] He tried to run when
he saw officers conducting checks there, and police chased and caught him. [@1]

A search of his bag found the 360 grams of ganja. [@1] Mahesh said questioning
showed the man, himself a ganja user, was selling it to earn money easily. [@1]
Police seized the ganja and registered a case against him, and the investigation
was continuing, Mahesh said. [@1]
