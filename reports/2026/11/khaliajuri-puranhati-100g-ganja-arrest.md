Khaliajuri thana police detained three men in an anti-drug raid in Khaliajuri of
Netrokona. [@1] The raid took place at around 9 pm on Saturday 7 November in the
Puranhati area of the upazila headquarters. [@1] The detained men were Hanif,
55, of Puranhati, Sagar, 38, of Puranhati, and Shahin, 19, of Chhothati. [@1]

A police team led by Sub-Inspector Abdul Karim of Khaliajuri police station
raided Hanif's house on secret information. [@1] Officers found the three
allegedly consuming ganja and detained them. [@1] Police recovered 100 grams of
ganja from them. [@1] Sagar was also shown arrested in an earlier case in which
an arrest warrant was pending against him. [@1]

Khaliajuri police chief Md Nasir Uddin confirmed the raid and detentions and
said legal action had been taken against the three. [@1] A case was filed at
Khaliajuri police station on 8 November 2026 under Table 19(a) of section 36(1)
of the Narcotics Control Act, 2018. [@1] The accused were sent to jail custody
through the court. [@1]
