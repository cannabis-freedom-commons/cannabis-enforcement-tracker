Security forces in Beheira Governorate arrested a man they described as a
dangerous criminal on 2 November, in Kom Hamada police district, on accusations
of trafficking drugs, firearms and unlicensed ammunition. [@1,3,4] The man had
earlier been sentenced to six years in prison in a drug case. [@1] The arrest
came after his brother posted a video claiming that an armed police force from
Kom Hamada had stormed and searched his home, smashed its contents and taken
cash without right. [@1]

Officers seized three firearms described as single-barrel shotguns, known in
Egypt as fard khartoush, and 4.5 kilograms of hashish. [@1,3,4] They also found a
quantity of ice, a methamphetamine-type drug that is not counted as cannabis,
and the man admitted holding the items for trafficking. [@1,3] A second account
attributed to the Interior Ministry carries the same seizure and the same
admission. [@2]

Police summoned the brother who posted the video, a worker living in the same
district, and he admitted staging the damage to his furniture and filming it so
his brother could use the claims in court. [@1,3,4] Police said the legal measures
required in the case were taken. [@2,3]
