Bemowo intelligence officers followed up information that a man was storing
drugs in his flat. [@1,3] After entering the 27-year-old's flat, officers
immediately noticed a characteristic smell and found banned substances in
several places, including the fridge. [@1] The 27-year-old was detained at the
flat while investigators inspected the scene. [@1,3]

Officers seized a combined total of more than 200 grams of amphetamine,
marijuana and NEP, with no separate weight reported for the marijuana alone.
[@1,3] A check of police databases confirmed that he had previously been punished
for a similar drug offence. [@1]

At the Warszawa-Wola district prosecutor's office he was charged with possessing
a significant quantity of intoxicants as a repeat offender. [@1,3] At the
prosecutor's request, a court remanded the suspect in custody for three months.
[@1,3] The proceedings in the case are being conducted under the supervision of
the Warszawa-Wola district prosecutor's office. [@2]
