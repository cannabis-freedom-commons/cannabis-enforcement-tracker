Military Police officers raided a house in the Dom Miguel neighbourhood of Rio
Verde, Goiás, on Sunday 1 November 2026 after information indicated it was used
to store and distribute large quantities of drugs. [@1] Força Tática, the
tactical unit of the Military Police, led the operation with support from the
CPU team. [@1] Officers arrested one woman accused of guarding the drug stock
and took her with the seized material to the competent police authority for
legal steps. [@1]

Inside they found 35 and a half tablets of a substance described as maconha and
six tablets of a substance described as skunk, a potent cannabis form known as
supermaconha for its higher concentration of psychoactive components. [@2] From
the same depot officers also seized 18 portions of a crack-like substance and 51
portions of a cocaine-like substance. [@2] They also collected two precision
scales and two rolls of plastic film that could be used to weigh and wrap drugs.
[@2] Police estimated the combined haul was worth approximately R$380,000. [@2]

The Força Tática team was named as Sergeant Gomes, Sergeant Artur and Corporal
Câmara. [@1] Police did not disclose the identity of the arrested woman. [@2]
The Civil Police were expected to investigate where the drugs came from and
whether other people took part in the scheme. [@2]
