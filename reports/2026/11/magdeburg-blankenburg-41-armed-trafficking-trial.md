A man now 41 years old, who last lived in Blankenburg, is to answer before the
Landgericht Magdeburg, the regional court in the state capital, for armed drug
trafficking. [@1] Prosecutors accuse him of selling amphetamine, methamphetamine
and cannabis. [@1]

The court lists one defendant, two expert witnesses and five witnesses for the
case, which is being heard by its 5th criminal chamber. [@1] The trial opens at
the Landgericht Magdeburg, in room 5, on Thursday, 12 November 2026, with
hearings also set for 13 and 30 November and 4 December. [@1] The charge covers
drug dealing in Blankenburg from the start of September 2023 until 6 November
2023, the court says. [@1]

To protect the trade, the accused is said to have kept various knives within
reach in his flat, which is why the trial asks whether the knives were weapons.
[@2]

The court's November 2026 schedule names the accused as a 41-year-old man from
Wernigerode and sets his trial to open on 12 November 2026 in room 5. [@1]
