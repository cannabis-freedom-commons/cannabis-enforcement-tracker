Military police apprehended a 17-year-old on Sunday 8 November 2026 on suspicion
of drug trafficking in the Santo Antônio neighbourhood of Barra do Garças, Mato
Grosso. [@1]

Officers of the Grupo de Apoio (GAP), a support group of the 2nd Battalion, went
to a residence after anonymous reports of heavy foot traffic at a house said to
be used by drug users. [@1] The team found only the teenager at the address and
during the approach located a bag with five portions of marijuana. [@1]

Questioned by the officers, the youth pointed to another hiding place next to a
water tank, where police found 17 more portions of marijuana and two larger
portions of the same drug. [@1] The same hiding place held six portions of
cocaine base paste and one portion of a substance similar to ecstasy. [@1]
Police also seized a precision scale, 27 plastic jars, a fanny pack and various
materials used to pack drugs. [@1]

According to the Military Police, the teenager was a member of a criminal
faction and already had police records over suspected involvement in drug
trafficking, robbery, torture and theft. [@1] He was taken to the police station
in Barra do Garças, where the incident was registered and the appropriate steps
were taken. [@1]
