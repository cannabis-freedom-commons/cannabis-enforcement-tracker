The Navy, in coordination with the Excise Department, arrested one suspect
during a search operation in the Kalmunaikudi area on 3 November 2026. [@1] The
suspect is a 61-year-old resident of the Kalmunaikudi area in Kalmunai. [@1] The
suspect and the seized substance were handed to the Excise Department office in
Kalmunai for onward legal proceedings. [@1]

Personnel from SLNS Deegayu of the Southeastern Naval Command conducted the raid
alongside personnel of the Excise Department office in Kalmunai. [@1] The raid
was carried out on receipt of information and involved searching for the
suspect. [@1] The raiding team found over 6kg of illegal substance stuffed in 94
packets. [@1] The Navy describes the seized material as a cannabis mixed tobacco
substance known as Mukku Thool. [@1] The Navy describes the 94 packets as made
ready for sale. [@1]

Mukku Thool is the local name given in the Navy account for the cannabis-mixed
tobacco product seized in Kalmunaikudi. [@1] After the seizure, the Excise
Department office in Kalmunai took over the suspect and the substance for
further legal action. [@1]
