A sub-inspector in Marpalli mandal, Mahabubnagar district, Telangana, seized 20
grams of ganja from a man found carrying it in a plastic cover at the fields on
the outskirts of Bhooc hanpalli village. [@1] Police said the search followed a
tip-off that a man was moving suspiciously and carrying ganja near farmland in
the village on Thursday afternoon at about 1 pm. [@1]

Officers identified the man caught with the ganja as Jaipal, a resident of the
same village. [@1] Questioning showed that Jaipal had bought the ganja from
Malreddy, also from the village. [@1] Police registered a case against both
Jaipal, who held the ganja, and Malreddy, who is accused of selling it, and
began an investigation. [@1] Sub-inspector Abdul Rauf led the check, with
constables Elayya and Narsimha taking part. [@1] Police said surveillance would
continue against the smuggling and sale of intoxicants such as ganja in
villages. [@1]
