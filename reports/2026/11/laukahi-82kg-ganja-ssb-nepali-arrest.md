A joint party of the Sashastra Seema Bal, India's border-guard force, and Bihar
police seized 82.780 kilograms of ganja on the India-Nepal border in Madhubani
district, Bihar. [@1]

The operation was carried out on the morning of Monday, 9 November 2026 by the
Laukahi company of the SSB's 18th Battalion at Rajnagar, together with Bihar
police. [@1] The SSB said it had received a tip-off about narcotics being
smuggled across the border, and the joint team was formed and led by assistant
commandant Gurdeep Singh. [@1]

Officers arrested a Nepali citizen at the scene on suspicion that the
consignment was being brought into India from Nepal. [@1] The arrested man was
identified as Arvind Kumar Yadav, a resident of Malhaniya village in Nepal's
Saptari district. [@1]

The seized ganja and the arrested man were handed over to local police station
officers for further legal action. [@1] Police are investigating where the
consignment was to be delivered and who else is part of the smuggling network.
[@1] The SSB said the 18th Battalion, commanded by Kojaram Lamrod, keeps up
continuous surveillance of the India-Nepal border against narcotics and other
contraband, in coordination with Bihar police and other agencies. [@1]
