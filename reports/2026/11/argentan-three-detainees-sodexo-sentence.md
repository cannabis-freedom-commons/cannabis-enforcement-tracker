Three detainees held at the Argentan detention centre in the Orne department
were tried before the Argentan court on 6 October 2026 on charges of corruption
and drug trafficking. [@1] The three detainees of the Argentan detention centre
were convicted on 3 November 2026. [@1] Their case concerned corrupting a
private contractor so the detainees could obtain drugs inside the prison. [@1]
On 3 November the court sentenced them to prison terms of twelve and eighteen
months. [@1]

The convictions followed a trafficking scheme that had developed inside the
walls of the Argentan detention centre from January 2025 to mid-March 2026. [@1]
A private maintenance team leader employed by the company Sodexo Justice acted
as the person who brought contraband into the facility. [@1] The contraband he
introduced included cannabis, alongside electronic chips, clothing, salmon,
alcohol and games consoles. [@1]

The agent, aged 37, introduced between 17 and 35 kg of stupefiants, the French
term for narcotics used here for the mixed drug total rather than a
cannabis-only amount, and the operation earned him 18,000 euros. [@1] He also
faced intimidation linked to the trafficking that reached his home. [@1]
