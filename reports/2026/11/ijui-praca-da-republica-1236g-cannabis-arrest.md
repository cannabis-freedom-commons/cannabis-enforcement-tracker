Brigada Militar officers arrested a man in flagrante, the Brazilian term for
being caught in the act, for drug trafficking in the centre of Ijuí, Rio Grande
do Sul, on 11 November 2026. [@1] Officers had received information that the
suspect would receive drugs at Praça da República, a public square in the city's
Centro district, and approached him there. [@1]

A search of the backpack he was carrying found two bricks of marijuana weighing
816 grams. [@1] Officers also found four further portions of the same drug,
weighing 420 grams together, and a precision scale. [@1] The marijuana seized
came to 1.236 kilograms in total, 816 grams in the two bricks and 420 grams in
the four portions. [@1]

The photograph published with the report is credited to the social
communications office of the 29th Military Police Battalion, the Brigada Militar
unit covering Ijuí. [@1]
