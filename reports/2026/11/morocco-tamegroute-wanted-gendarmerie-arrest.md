On Thursday early morning, the judicial centre of the Royal Gendarmerie in
Tamegroute (Marrakech) carried out a security operation that arrested a person
suspected of involvement in narcotics trafficking. [@1] The man was a national
fugitive who was the subject of more than 20 national search warrants. [@1]

The gendarmerie acted on information that he was inside a nightclub on Abdelkrim
Al-Khatibi street in Marrakech, where officers intervened and stopped his escape
to detain him. [@1]

He was taken to the judicial centre of the Royal Gendarmerie in Tamegroute to go
through the necessary legal procedures and to verify the cases linked to the
search warrants issued against him. [@1]

The operation was part of security actions aimed at pursuing people suspected of
involvement in the illicit trade in narcotics and intensifying efforts to combat
crime in the area. [@1]
