Railway police detained a 15-year-old boy with 4 kg of ganja on a platform of
the airport railway station in Dhaka. [@1]

Officers were searching passengers at the platform gate when the
Chattogram–Dhaka Turna Nishitha Express pulled into platform 2 early on Sunday,
1 November 2026. [@1] A search of his bag turned up a total of 4 kg of ganja in
polythene, wrapped inside two packets of black tape. [@1] Police estimated the
market value of the seized ganja at 100,000 taka. [@1]

Police named the boy, said he was 15, and placed his family home in Chhagalnaiya
police area of Feni district, south-eastern Bangladesh. [@1]
