On Thursday 5 November 2026, Toruń criminal police, with support from
investigators, carried out an action aimed at crime linked to the pseudo-fan
milieu. [@1] Officers detained a 50-year-old resident of Chełmińskie
Przedmieście and searched his home, seizing over 800 grams of drugs dominated by
amphetamine, plus mefedron (4-ethyl-1-methyl-N,4-dimethylphenethylamine, a
synthetic stimulant, out of tracker scope) and marijuana. [@1] In the same
search police secured a Mosin rifle and black-powder firearms for which the man
held no permits. [@1]

That same day officers detained a 41-year-old resident of the Mokre estate in
Toruń, at whom they found significant quantities of amphetamine, plus marijuana
and mefedron, almost 700 grams of prohibited substances in total. [@1] Officers
established the second man had previously been convicted of drug offences, so
this time he would answer as a recidivist. [@1] By a court order both men are in
temporary custody for the next three months. [@1]
