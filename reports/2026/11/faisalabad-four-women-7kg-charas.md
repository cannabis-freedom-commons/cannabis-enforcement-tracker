Police in Faisalabad arrested four women accused of trafficking drugs, breaking
up what officers described as a female drug-pusher network in the city. [@1] The
four were named as Amina Bibi, Shakeela Bibi, Aneela Bibi and Nabeela Bibi. [@1]
Police said a special team set up on the orders of City Police Officer Tanveer
Hussain carried out the raids. [@1]

The police said they recovered more than 7 kilograms of charas, the cannabis
resin also known as hashish, from the women. [@1] The Urdu-language APP report
described the seized charas as worth millions of rupees. [@2]

Police said the women had been supplying narcotics to young people, especially
near educational institutions. [@1] Officers began an investigation to find
their accomplices, police added. [@1]

The operations that led to the arrests were carried out in different areas of
Faisalabad, the Punjab Post reported. [@3] Police registered cases against the
women and began legal proceedings, according to the Urdu-language APP report.
[@2] The Punjab Post reported that Faisalabad police operations would continue
until the drug problem was eliminated. [@3]
