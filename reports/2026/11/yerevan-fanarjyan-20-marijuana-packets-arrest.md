Yerevan police detained two young men who planned to distribute drugs through
dead-drop caches. [@1] A tip that two youths were placing drugs on the road
section from Fanarjyan Street toward Lepsius Street reached the Kanaker-Zeytun
community police department. [@1] Community officers went immediately to the
stated location. [@2] When the officers appeared, the youths tried to flee, and
one of them threw packets from his pocket while running. [@2] Both were detained
on suspicion of drug sale and taken to the station, where they were identified
as 19- and 20-year-old Yerevan residents. [@1]

Through a Telegram channel, they had bought 20 packets of marijuana for 250
dollars to place in the caches. [@1] Part of the packets was found during the
personal search of the 20-year-old, and the rest on the Fanarjyan Street section
where the 19-year-old had thrown them while fleeing. [@1] The placement attempt
did not succeed. [@2]

A criminal case was opened over the incident. [@1] Both young men were remanded
in custody. [@2]
