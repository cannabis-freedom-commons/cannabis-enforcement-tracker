Guardia di Finanza officers of the Rome Provincial Command stopped a car at
Castelverde in the east of Rome during a standing economic-control operation
after the Italian driver resident in Rome appeared immediately nervous. [@1]

Officers found two hidden compartments under the car's front seats, opened by a
remote control, containing ten packets of cocaine, two packets of hashish
weighing 200 grams in total, and a substantial amount of cash. [@1] A search of
the man's apartment found further bundles of 50-euro notes, bringing the total
cash seized to 12,800 euros. [@1] On the orders of the Rome public prosecutor's
office, the man, aged about sixty, was arrested in flagrante and taken to Regina
Coeli prison. [@1]

The Guardia di Finanza estimated the drugs, had they been sold in the dealing
squares of the Rome metropolitan city, would have guaranteed the criminal
organisation illicit profits of over 2 million euros. [@1] RomaToday reports the
driver was carrying 11 kilograms of cocaine alongside 200 grams of hashish and
almost 13,000 euros in cash. [@2]

Rai News confirms the 60-year-old was stopped for an ordinary road control,
became nervous, and the hidden compartments reachable only by remote control
held ten packets of cocaine, two of hashish and cash. [@3]
