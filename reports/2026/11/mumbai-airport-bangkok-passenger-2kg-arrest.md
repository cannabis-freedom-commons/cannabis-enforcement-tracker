Mumbai Airport Customs arrested 29-year-old carrier P. Goswami and seized
approximately two kilograms of high-grade marijuana in a cross-border smuggling
case. [@1] The contraband was intercepted at Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj
International (CSMI) Airport on Sunday. [@1] The suspect, identified as P.
Goswami, had just landed in Mumbai on a flight from Bangkok, Thailand. [@1]
Senior Customs officials said the operation was based on rigorous spot profiling
of arriving passengers. [@1] Upon pulling Goswami aside for a detailed
inspection, officials scanned and searched his check-in luggage. [@1] A thorough
examination of his trolley bag revealed four plastic packets containing the
contraband, concealed inside commercial food packets and hidden beneath layers
of used clothing to evade detection. [@1] The seized marijuana, weighing around
2 kg, was confiscated under the provisions of the Narcotic Drugs and
Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act. [@1]

Goswami was subsequently placed under formal arrest. [@1] Investigators are
decoding the syndicate's operational methods, including the exact source of
procurement in Bangkok and the intended destination of the delivery inside
India. [@1] Customs officers said they are thoroughly examining the statement of
the accused along with available digital and material evidence. [@1] A dedicated
team is analyzing the suspect’s phone records, encrypted communication channels,
and financial transactions to identify the financiers and kingpins backing the
illicit trade. [@1]
