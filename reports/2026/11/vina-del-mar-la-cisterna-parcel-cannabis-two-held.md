The PDI's Valparaíso unit said detectives in San Antonio detained two people for
offences under Law 20,000. [@3] The operation took place in the communes of Viña
del Mar and La Cisterna. [@1] It was coordinated with the criminal-analysis
system (SAC) of the Valparaíso regional prosecutor's office. [@1]

According to the investigation, the drugs had been sent from Santiago by parcel
to Viña del Mar for distribution. [@1] After a controlled delivery, detectives
arrested the recipient in Viña del Mar and later the supplier in the
Metropolitan Region. [@1] PDI commissioner Rodrigo Guzmán Villegas said the
suspects used parcel transport to supply microtraffickers in other regions of
the country. [@1]

The force said it seized five kilograms of drugs — cannabis, ketamine and
cannabis resin — plus equipment used to prepare doses. [@1] Both suspects were
handed to the Juzgado de Garantía, Chile's guarantee court, for a
detention-control hearing. [@1]

Panorama Noticioso reported that the court would decide the precautionary
measures based on the evidence presented by the prosecutor's office. [@2]
Panorama Noticioso reported that the operation interrupted a supply route
linking the Metropolitan Region with the Valparaíso region and other parts of
the country. [@2]
