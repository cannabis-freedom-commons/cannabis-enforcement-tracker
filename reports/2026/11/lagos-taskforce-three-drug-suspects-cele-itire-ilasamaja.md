The Lagos State Taskforce arrested three suspected drug peddlers during an
early-hours operation targeting black spots in the Cele, Itire and Ilasamaja
areas. [@1]

The suspects were allegedly involved in the sale and distribution of substances,
including marijuana, Colorado and Indian hemp, to adults, minors and street
urchins around Cele Bus Stop, Itire and Ilasamaja. [@1] The suspects were
identified as Bello Musa, 18, and Abubakar Musa, 27, of Adebayo Street in
Cele-Ijesha, and Agboola Fuad, 24, of Sewage Street in Abaranje, Ijegun. [@1]

The three were arraigned before a court on three counts covering possession and
sale of illicit drugs, inciting the public against officers performing lawful
duties, and conduct likely to cause a breach of the peace. [@1] All three
pleaded not guilty and the case was adjourned until 9 December 2026 for further
proceedings. [@1]
