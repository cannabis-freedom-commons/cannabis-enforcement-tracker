Bistan police detained 34-year-old cultivator Tersingh, son of Juvansingh and
resident of Dol Fata, Mahumandali, for allegedly growing cannabis on his field.
[@1] Police took him into custody and registered a case against him under the
NDPS Act, India's drug law. [@1] He was produced before the Mandleshwar court on
Monday. [@1] Police records show an earlier NDPS case against him at the local
police station, which a senior officer also confirmed. [@1]

Acting on a tip that cannabis had been planted in Mahumandali, station in-charge
Gulabsingh Rawat reached the accused's field east of the Bijasan Mata temple
with a police force. [@1] Police said about one acre was under planned cannabis
cultivation using drip irrigation to grow the plants faster and better. [@1]
Officers seized 2,230 green cannabis plants weighing 12 quintals 69 kg from the
spot. [@1] A second account corroborates the seizure of 2,230 green plants
weighing 12 quintals 69 kg from the same Mahumandali field. [@2] Police said the
seized plants were five to eight feet tall, indicating the cultivation had been
running for a long time. [@1] The accused tried to flee on seeing the police
team but was caught after officers surrounded him. [@2]

Bistan police presented the action as part of its drive against illegal
narcotics, with station staff including ASI Sudama More taking part. [@1] After
the seizure, local residents questioned why the police intelligence system had
not detected a one-acre crop with five-to-eight-foot plants earlier. [@1]
