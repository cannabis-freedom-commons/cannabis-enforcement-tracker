Assam Rifles, under the Spear Corps, and the Excise and Narcotics Department
seized heroin and cannabis in a joint intelligence-based operation in Siaha
district of Mizoram. [@1] The operation recovered 330.81 grams of heroin No. 4
packed in 25 packets and 350 grams of cannabis. [@1] The authorities valued the
whole recovery of heroin and cannabis at about ₹66.34 lakh (₹6.634 million).
[@1]

The seizure followed intelligence inputs about narcotics-related activities in
the area. [@1] Assam Rifles said the joint operation reflected coordinated
efforts by security forces and civil enforcement agencies to identify and
dismantle drug-trafficking networks. [@1] Assam Rifles said it has been running
sustained operations against the narcotics trade, which it described as a
serious threat to society, particularly the younger generation. [@1] No arrests
or charges in the operation were reported. [@1]
