Bandipora Police on Monday arrested an alleged drug peddler after recovering
over 10 kilograms of suspected charas in Hajin on 2 November 2026. [@1,5] The man
was identified as Tariq Ahmad Mir and was arrested during naka checking at the
Hakbara-Chandergair crossing in the Hajin area. [@2,5] Police identified the
accused as Tariq Ahmad Mir, son of Mohammad Sabir Mir, a resident of Hakbara.
[@1]

Officers recovered 10 kilograms and 130 grams of a charas-like substance from
the suspected peddler's possession during the check. [@1,5] A second account gave
the same recovery as 10.130 kg of charas-like substance seized during the Monday
naka checking in the Hajin area. [@3] Police registered FIR No. 120/2026 under
Sections 8/20 of the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act at Police
Station Hajin, and further investigation was underway. [@1]

Police said the operation was part of its ongoing drive against drug trafficking
across Bandipora district. [@1] Police said they would continue action against
drug trafficking and appealed to the public to cooperate in efforts to curb the
menace. [@4]
