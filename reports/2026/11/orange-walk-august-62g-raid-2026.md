Hubert Edgar August is a 35-year-old Orange Walk Town electrician who was
charged with possession of controlled drugs after a police raid on his home.
[@1,2] August, of Rio Grande Street, was charged after police allegedly found 16.1
grams of suspected cannabis in his pocket. [@1] August was formally charged the
same day for the cannabis found in his pocket and remained silent when
cautioned. [@1]

Officers from the Trial Farm Sub Station and the San Roman Joint Patrol Team
executed a search warrant at the residence at around 11:00am on Wednesday,
November 4. [@1,2] A woman who lives at the house was seated in the yard and was
shown the warrant. [@1] Inside the house, officers found August sitting on a
couch in the living room. [@1] A search of his right pocket turned up six small
plastic bags containing suspected cannabis. [@1] Nothing else illegal was found
inside the house. [@1]

Along the outside edge of the fence under a cement block, officers found a
plastic bag with 12.2 grams of an off-white substance suspected to be crack
cocaine. [@1,2] In a tied yellow bag lodged in trees about a foot above the fence,
officers found nine wrapped bags of suspected cannabis weighing 46.2 grams. [@1,2]
The drugs found outside the fence were recorded as found property. [@1,2]
