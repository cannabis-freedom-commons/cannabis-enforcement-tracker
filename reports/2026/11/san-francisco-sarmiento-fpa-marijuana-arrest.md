In San Francisco, officers detained a man during a check on the 1000 block of
Boulevard Sáenz Peña after seizing several doses of marijuana, cash and items of
interest. [@2] Local outlet Radiocanal reported that the stop on the 1000 block
of Boulevard Sáenz Peña ended with the arrest of an adult man. [@3] According to
the official report, the stop took place in Barrio Sarmiento along a busy
commercial stretch of Boulevard Sáenz Peña. [@1] The procedure was carried out
by personnel of the Special Interventions Company of the Fuerza Policial
Antinarcotráfico (FPA), the anti-narcotics police force. [@3]

During the preventive check, officers intercepted an adult and searched him,
seizing several doses of marijuana, cash and various items of interest to the
case. [@1] Radiocanal likewise reported that officers confiscated several doses
of marijuana, cash and other items considered relevant to the investigation.
[@3]

The arrest formed part of sustained preventive checks and targeted patrols
deployed across the province against narcomenudeo, small-scale drug retail. [@1]
After the arrest, officers notified the judicial authorities, and the
prosecutor's office ordered the detainee and all seized items transferred to
local judicial headquarters to continue the investigation. [@1] The detained man
and the confiscated items were transferred to judicial headquarters in San
Francisco. [@3]
