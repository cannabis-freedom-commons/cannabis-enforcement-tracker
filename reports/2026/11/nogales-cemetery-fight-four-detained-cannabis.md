Municipal police detained four males at the Jardines del Buen Pastor cemetery in
colonia Esperanza, Nogales, after responding to a report of a fight. [@1] The
group behaved aggressively when police arrived, insulting and threatening
officers before resisting and assaulting them until they were secured. [@1]
Officers identified the two adults as Christian “N”, aged 29, and Jesús “N”,
aged 46, who resisted and assaulted officers when approached. [@1] The other two
detainees were 17-year-olds who also took part in the incident. [@1]

Officers found the teenagers with a bag containing marijuana and a beer bottle
containing a similar substance, while the group was drinking alcohol. [@1] The
second account describes the find as a bag with green dry herb and a beer bottle
containing a substance of similar characteristics, noting that the report does
not give test results identifying the material. [@2] The incident occurred at
16:56 on Monday 2 November at the cemetery on Periférico Luis Donaldo Colosio in
colonia Esperanza. [@1] Municipal officers had attended the cemetery after a
report of a fight and aggressive people. [@2]

The two adults were placed at the disposal of Sonora’s Fiscalía General de
Justicia for alleged responsibility for corruption of minors. [@1] The teenagers
were placed at the disposal of the Specialized Agency for Justice for
Adolescents, which will decide their situation under applicable law. [@2]
