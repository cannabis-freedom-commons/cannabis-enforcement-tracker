The investigating judge at the Tunis Court of First Instance issued committal
orders jailing ten people for forming an international network to promote drugs.
[@1] Those jailed include public employees, cattle traders and the owner of a
car-rental agency. [@1] The judge released four others while barring them from
travelling. [@1]

The National Guard's central anti-drug brigade in Ben Arous carried out the
arrests that broke the network, which smuggled drugs in from a foreign country
for sale in Tunisia. [@1] The investigation began from information that a
restaurant worker and a café waitress were dealing drugs to the café's and
restaurant's customers. [@1]

Further inquiries identified the rest of the network and brought the number of
people detained to 14, among them a cattle-breeding agency employee, cattle
traders, a car broker and the car-rental agency owner. [@1]

Officers seized significant quantities of cocaine, cannabis and narcotic
tablets, along with substantial sums of money and luxury cars. [@1]
Investigators found that the drugs were smuggled from a foreign country into
Tunisia to be sold there. [@1]
