Laredo police arrested Joshua David Ford, 25, in the early morning of 8 November
2026 at a convenience store in the 3700 block of US 83, after officers responded
to a report of a man asleep inside a grey vehicle. [@1]

While officers attempted to detain Ford, he resisted and bit an officer on the
arm; officers then used force to subdue and arrest him. [@1]

During the vehicle search, officers found two THC vape pens in the grey car Ford
was driving. [@1]

The vehicle was also linked to a hit-and-run incident on Wooster Street. [@1]

Ford was charged with assaulting a law enforcement officer, possession of a
controlled substance, resisting arrest, DUI, and possession of contraband in a
correctional facility. [@1]
