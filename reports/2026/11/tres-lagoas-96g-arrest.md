A 21-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of drug trafficking on the night of
Monday 2 November 2026 in Três Lagoas. [@1,2] Força Tática officers stopped him in
the Jardim das Violetas neighbourhood. [@1,2] The encounter took place on Rua dos
Trabalhadores during a police patrol. [@1,2] He was riding a motorcycle when the
officers approached him. [@1,2] A check found that he held no Carteira Nacional de
Habilitação (CNH), Brazil's national driver's licence. [@1,2] He was given voice
of arrest and taken to the police station for presentation to the police
authority and further legal measures. [@1,2] His motorcycle was collected and
taken to the yard of Detran, the state traffic department, because of
administrative irregularities found during the stop. [@1,2]

A personal search found a tablet of green plant material described as resembling
maconha, as cannabis is known in Brazil, weighing about 96 grams (0.096 kg).
[@1,2] Officers also found a mobile phone on him. [@1,2]

According to the police account, he said when questioned that he intended to
sell the drug to get money. [@1,2] He also said he had bought it about two days
earlier from a man who, as he understood it, lived in the area known as
Predinhos. [@1,2]
