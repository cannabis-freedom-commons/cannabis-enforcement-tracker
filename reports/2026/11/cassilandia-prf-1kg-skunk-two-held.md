Two men were arrested in flagrante at the scene and taken for judicial police
procedures. [@1] During the approach to a suspect vehicle, highway officers
found and seized 1 kg of crack and 1 kg of skunk, described as a marijuana
variety with high THC content. [@1] The interception was reported on Rádio
Patriarca's Rotativa no Ar programme on Thursday 12 November. [@1]

A coordinated inspection by the Federal Highway Police (PRF) intercepted the
load, described as having high commercial value, on highways accessing the
Bolsão region. [@1] Preliminary investigations and data cross-checks confirmed
the seized drugs were destined for final delivery in the municipality of
Cassilândia. [@1] The confirmation came in an official statement on the
programme from Dr. Rodrigo de Freitas, the head of the Cassilândia Civil Police
station, on the importance of the seizure for the municipality's security. [@1]
De Freitas said the PRF seizure kept two kilos of highly destructive substances
from reaching the city's streets to feed local drug trafficking. [@1]
