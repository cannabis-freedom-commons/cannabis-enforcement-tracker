Detectives with the Odessa Police Department were called on 4 November 2026 to
assist with a welfare check for a girl at a hotel. [@1] The welfare check
occurred at the Baymont Inn in Odessa, Texas. [@1] Upon arrival, detectives
found an officer who had detained 42-year-old Tina Mendes and a 16-year-old girl
inside a hotel room. [@1] Mendes was advised of her Miranda rights, which she
waived, and admitted during an interview that she had used methamphetamine with
the teenager as recently as the night before. [@1] Mendes also told Odessa
Police Department officials that she had offered marijuana to the girl on 3
November. [@1]

Investigators reported that a green leafy substance identified as marijuana,
based on their training and experience, was sitting on top of white paper in the
room. [@1] Officers also observed two plastic baggies in plain view containing a
white, crystal-like substance determined to be methamphetamine. [@1] Officials
separately interviewed two other people who stated that Mendes had used
methamphetamine with the girl that morning just before police arrived. [@1]

Mendes was arrested and charged with delivery of a controlled substance or
marijuana to a child, a second-degree felony. [@1] She was booked into the Ector
County Law Enforcement Center and held on a $55,000 bond. [@1]
