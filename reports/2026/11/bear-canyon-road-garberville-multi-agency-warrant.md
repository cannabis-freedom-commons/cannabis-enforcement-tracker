The California Highway Patrol's Northern Division Warrant Service Team served a
search warrant on Monday 9 November 2026 at a residential property in the 400
block of Bear Canyon Road, northwest of Garberville in Humboldt County. [@1] The
CHP said the warrant was served on behalf of allied agencies in a multi-agency
narcotics investigation and that it was not leading the investigation. [@1] The
CHP initially identified itself as the lead agency and then withdrew that,
saying its role was limited to warrant service. [@2] Participating agencies
included the Drug Enforcement Administration, Homeland Security Investigations,
the Federal Bureau of Investigation, the Sacramento County Sheriff's Office and
several unnamed local police departments from across California. [@1] The
Humboldt County Sheriff's Office said it was not involved in the operation. [@1]

The CHP did not clarify whether the investigation specifically targeted
cannabis, involved cannabis alongside other drugs, or focused on other
narcotics. [@1] The CHP has not released the specific narcotics involved,
whether arrests were made, or the identities of the additional police
departments. [@1] Officers were seen shovelling what appeared to be cannabis
material from totes into holes on the property. [@1] Asked whether cannabis was
involved, the CHP confirmed only that it classifies cannabis as a narcotic and
would not confirm that the material seen was cannabis. [@1]

At least five marked CHP vehicles and four unmarked vehicles were at the
property on Monday morning, and the journalist reported seeing officers put what
appeared to be cannabis flower or trim into holes in the ground. [@2] The CHP
said trenches on the property predated the operation and were part of septic
system repairs, and that equipment at the scene belonged to the property owner.
[@1] The CHP Public Information Unit said anything discovered during the search
was still being investigated and referred further questions to its federal
partners. [@1]
