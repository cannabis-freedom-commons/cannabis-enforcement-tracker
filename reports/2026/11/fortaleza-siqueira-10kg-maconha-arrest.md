Civil Police of Ceará (PCCE), through the organised-crime unit Draco together
with the narcotics unit Denarc, arrested a 20-year-old man in flagrante on
Thursday 12 November 2026 in the Siqueira neighbourhood of Fortaleza. [@1] The
arrest was made while officers served a search-and-seizure warrant on a property
in the neighbourhood. [@1] Police said the suspect was already under
investigation for membership in a criminal organisation. [@1]

Officers seized approximately 10 kilograms of a substance similar to marijuana,
divided into 10 tablets. [@1] Three precision scales, ten .40-calibre rounds and
a portion of empty pins used to package and sell drugs were also seized. [@1]

The suspect was taken to Draco and booked in flagrante for illicit drug
trafficking and illegal possession or carrying of a restricted-use firearm. [@1]
After the flagrante arrest report was drawn up, he was placed at the disposal of
the courts. [@1] The Civil Police of Ceará said the courts had already converted
the flagrante arrest into pre-trial detention. [@2]
