Municipal police officers in Charleville-Mézières arrested a man on the evening
of 4 November 2026 while patrolling the Ronde Couture sector. [@1] L'Ardennais
names the day of the arrest as Wednesday 4 November 2026, with municipal
officers patrolling the Ronde Couture area. [@2] The man was already known to
the police for drug-related offences. [@1]

Officers seized half a "pain" of cannabis resin, a French street measure,
weighing about 125 grams. [@1] They also seized 30 sachets already packaged for
resale. [@2] The Charleville-Mézières municipal police announced the seizure on
social media on 5 November. [@1]

The arrested man was placed in garde à vue, French police custody, for use,
possession and supplying of drugs. [@1] The custody was held at the
Charleville-Mézières national police station. [@2] National police said he was
released at the end of that custody and will be summoned to appear before the
Charleville-Mézières judicial court. [@2]

The Ronde Couture is a Charleville-Mézières neighbourhood that municipal
officers patrol for the region's regular anti-drug work. [@1]
