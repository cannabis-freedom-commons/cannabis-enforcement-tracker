Wesley Igor Pereira Duré, 22, was living in the house of an Operation Ifrit
target in Vila Ipiranga. [@1] He was staying at the address of another suspect,
Renan Braian Garcia Presotto, who was not found at the scene. [@1] The arrest
took place on the morning of Wednesday 4 November 2026 during the execution of
Operation Ifrit search warrants in Campo Grande. [@1]

A preliminary expert report listed 125.3 grams of hashish and 9.36 grams of
crumble, a concentrated cannabis form known as gourmet. [@1] The same report
also found tablets testing positive for 3,4-MDA, a prohibited synthetic
substance excluded from the cannabis total. [@1] Some of the drugs were in the
kitchen refrigerator and another portion was in the property's gourmet area.
[@1] The team also collected a precision scale, an iPhone 17 Pro Max, an Acer
notebook and image-recording equipment. [@1]

Duré told the Federal Police he uses drugs and admitted owning part of the
seized material. [@1] He said the substance found in the refrigerator was
already in the house when he came to live there and did not belong to him. [@1]
He also denied knowing the other drugs, which were reportedly found in two
locked bedrooms. [@1] The Federal Police treated the situation as flagrante for
storing narcotics. [@1] He was taken to the Federal Police regional
superintendency in Mato Grosso do Sul, where he was booked. [@1]

The arrest occurred during Operation Ifrit, launched by the Federal Police and
the Federal Revenue service against alleged international drug and arms
trafficking, money laundering and other crimes. [@1] The Federal Court issued 26
arrest warrants in seven states. [@1] At least 12 people were arrested in Mato
Grosso do Sul, including suspected members of the investigated group and police
officers suspected of collaborating with it. [@1]
