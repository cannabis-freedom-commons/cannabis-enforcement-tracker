Amlai police arrested two people on Sunday accused of supplying ganja near the
Jamuna nala bridge in Vidhiya village, in Madhya Pradesh's Shahdol district.
[@1] Officers seized 1 kg 430 g of ganja and a mobile phone from the two,
property police valued at 22,300 rupees in all. [@1]

The police action was a cordon around the area near the bridge, where the two
were accused of handing over ganja for sale. [@1]
