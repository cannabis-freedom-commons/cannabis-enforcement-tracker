Military police arrested a monitored apenado, a person serving a criminal
sentence, in the late afternoon of Sunday 1 November 2026 on Rua Raquel de
Queiroz in the Nova Esperança neighbourhood in the north zone of Porto Velho.
[@2,4] A Força Tática team of the 1st Battalion under Sergeant Shokness went to
the address after receiving denunciations that a man was selling drugs there.
[@2] When the officers approached, the suspect ran and jumped over the walls of
neighbouring houses to evade detention. [@1,3] The officers set up a perimeter
cordon, intercepted and detained the apenado. [@1,3,4]

During the search officers seized several drug portions including maconha, the
Portuguese word for cannabis, alongside cocaine and ecstasy. [@1,3,4] The second
outlet described the haul as portions of maconha, cocaine, crack and synthetic
drugs, plus objects of doubtful provenance. [@2] PVH Notícias presented the
location as a boca de fumo, an open-air drug-selling point, and its headline put
the total haul at more than 100 drug portions. [@2]

Police treated the case as trafficking and took the detainee with the seized
material to the Civil Police flagrante department. [@1]

Police listed the bag's contents as 11 crack stones, 40 maconha portions, 52 cocaine portions, 22 ecstasy units, 12 LSD stamps and nine hashish portions, plus R$ 196 in cash and a mobile phone. [@4] Checks showed the suspect wore an electronic ankle bracelet and had an open arrest warrant issued by the courts. [@4] The homeowner said the man fell from the roof into the bathroom while fleeing and complained of rib pain with abrasions on his body. [@4]
