Judge Mark Williams sentenced Jade-Cooper Mackenzie in the Invercargill District
Court on charges of cannabis dealing, selling methamphetamine, selling cocaine
and selling ecstasy, along with assault, possession of a taser and breaching a
community work sentence. [@1]

Mackenzie had handled 16kg of cannabis, held $35,000 in cash and had two people
working for him, but argued he sold only to fund his own addiction. [@1] The
judge rejected that explanation, holding Mackenzie to account for the dealing
rather than accepting addiction as the driver. [@1]
