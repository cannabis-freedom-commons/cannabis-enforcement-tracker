In Lomonosovsky District, police detained a young married couple who lived in a
gardening community on suspicion of unlawful drug trafficking. [@1] Both members
of the couple were remanded in custody. [@1] A criminal case for attempted
illegal production, sale or dispatch of narcotic drugs was opened against them
under articles 30 and 228.1 of the Criminal Code. [@2]

According to police, the man had fitted out both buildings for cultivating
cannabis and planned to sell the finished product. [@1] Officers found 51 bushes
and a sack of plant parts in one house. [@1] In the second building they found
another 173 bushes and three sacks of plant mass. [@1] In total, police seized
224 cannabis bushes and about 17 kg of plant material. [@1] Police also seized
cultivation equipment and packing materials. [@2]

The account was attributed to Irina Volk, an official representative of the
Russian Interior Ministry. [@2] Police said the exact mass of the substance will
be established by expert examination. [@1] Police are working to establish
possible accomplices. [@1]
