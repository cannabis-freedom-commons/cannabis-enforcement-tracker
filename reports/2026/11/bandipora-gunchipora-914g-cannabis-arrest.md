Police in Bandipora district arrested two alleged drug peddlers on Wednesday 11
November 2026. [@1] The two were identified as Shujaat Abbas, son of Shaukat Ali
and a resident of Sonam Pattan, and Tanveer Ahmed, son of Nisar Ahmed Yatoo and
a resident of Yal Kunzer. [@1] Officials said a police party intercepted them at
Gunchipora near Bakshi Bund, under the jurisdiction of Police Post Nowgam, while
they were travelling on a scooty with registration number JK05Q-3288. [@1]

During checking, police recovered two polythene bags containing 471 grams and
443 grams of a suspected cannabis powder-like substance, 914 grams in total.
[@1] The scooty used by the two was seized. [@1] Police registered a case, FIR
No. 244/2026, at Police Station Sumbal under sections 8, 20 and 29 of the
Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act. [@1]

Police said an investigation had been started to determine the source of the
suspected contraband and identify possible links in the drug supply network.
[@1] Police said further investigation would establish the case's forward and
backward linkages. [@2] Police appealed to the public to share information that
could help identify those involved in drug trafficking. [@1]
