Officers of the Houègbo arrondissement police station broke up a suspected
network running counterfeit money and several illegal activities in Agbotagon
and arrested five people, including one woman. [@1] The operation ran over three
days, from 3 to 6 November 2026. [@1]

On the evening of Tuesday 3 November police raided a house suspected of being a
meeting place for the group; the house belonged to a suspect who is still at
large. [@1] There police detained a hairdresser and two other people presented
as her accomplices. [@1] The search turned up 1,780,000 CFA francs in
counterfeit 10,000-franc notes, paper cut to the size of banknotes, 28 sachets
of chanvre indien, the French term for cannabis, and several motorcycle parts.
[@1] Police also seized SIM cards, a bottle and amulets described as objects of
a mystical nature. [@1]

According to the operation's information, the main suspect admitted the facts to
investigators and named suppliers and other network members. [@2] At dawn on
Friday 6 November police searched the home of two brothers suspected of
belonging to the network. [@1] In a shop belonging to one of them they found
about 1.5 kg of chanvre indien and two kilograms of smuggled medicines. [@1] In
the room of his brother, presented as one of the network's main organisers,
agents found printing plates and new blank notes. [@1] Both men were detained
and taken to the police station, and were reported to have admitted the facts
during questioning. [@1]

Le Plateau d'Abomey places Agbotagon in the commune of Allada and says the sweep
was part of an operation called "Houègbo zéro escroc". [@2] It reports that
several suspected members of the network were in police custody and that the
inquiry continued to find others who fled. [@2]
