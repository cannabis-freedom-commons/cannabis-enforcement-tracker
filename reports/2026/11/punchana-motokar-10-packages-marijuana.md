Agents of DIRANDRO PNP Iquitos, Peru's anti-drug police unit, stopped two people
travelling in a blue motokar at Pasaje 28 de Setiembre in the Asentamiento
Humano Amazonas, Punchana district, after information from the DIVINESP. [@1]
During the search of the motokar, licence plate 7780-CD, officers found a
backpack holding ten packages of material that a field test indicated was
Cannabis Sativa, marijuana. [@1] The two detained were Frank Jhoel Mosquera
Nolorbe, 33, who was driving, and Mayte Caterin Pinto Yahuarcani, 33, a
passenger. [@1] Both were detained and are being investigated for the alleged
crime of illicit drug trafficking. [@1]

The operation was carried out with the Iquitos specialised drug-trafficking
prosecutor, José Francisco Malaverry Pinchi. [@1] The pair, the suspected drug
and other seized items were taken to the DIRANDRO premises in Iquitos to
continue proceedings. [@1] The exact quantity of the drug was still to be
determined, and authorities must establish where the substance came from and
where it was going. [@1] The case is being investigated as an offence against
public health in the form of illicit drug trafficking, against the Peruvian
state. [@1]
