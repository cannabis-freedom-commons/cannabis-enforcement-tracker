Two men pleaded not guilty in Malta on Monday to drug charges including
trafficking in cocaine and cannabis. [@1] Solomon Haile, 32, from Eritrea and
Mohammed Eisa, 25, from Somalia were arraigned before Magistrate Kevan
Azzopardi. [@1] Both were accused of cocaine possession and trafficking within
100 metres of a youth-gathering space. [@1] Haile alone was also charged with
cannabis possession in amounts indicating it was not for personal use, selling
cannabis without a proper licence, and recidivism. [@1]

Inspector Francesco Mizzi told the court that drug squad officers were watching
people in St Julian’s who were interacting suspiciously with each other. [@1]
Police moved in when they saw Haile and Eisa interacting with a third person,
and told the two men their rights. [@1] At the moment of arrest Eisa was caught
discarding a sachet containing a white substance, the court was told. [@1]
Police searched the two men, seized their mobile phones, and found text messages
suggesting drug trafficking. [@1] The defence lawyers did not request bail. [@1]
Police said the arrests were made late on Saturday night and placed them in
Paceville, the nightlife district of St Julian’s. [@2] The men were identified
as a 25-year-old Somali man living in Marsa and a 32-year-old Eritrean man
living in Pietà. [@2] The police said the 25-year-old threw a packet containing
a substance suspected to be cocaine onto the ground after noticing the officers.
[@3]

Julian Scicluna of the Attorney General’s office and Inspector Francesco Mizzi
prosecuted, while legal aid lawyers Nadia Fiott and Martin Farrugia represented
the accused. [@1]
