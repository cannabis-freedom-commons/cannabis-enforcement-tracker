Military police seized 24 cannabis-like plants at a house in the Jardim Santa
Clara neighbourhood of Mogi Mirim, São Paulo state, on the morning of Wednesday
4 November 2026. [@1] Officers went to the address after an anonymous tip
reported cannabis cultivation on the property and described the structure used
for it. [@2] Police said they saw the plants through a gap in the gate before
approaching two men on the premises. [@1]

During the search of the house officers found an improvised growing structure
fitted with its own lighting. [@1] Officers also seized fertilisers, growing
materials, a precision scale and a replica firearm. [@1] Other people were in
the house, including two adolescents, and the municipal guardianship council
(Conselho Tutelar) was called, with a councillor present as the incident was
recorded. [@1] Technical forensic examiners also attended the property. [@2]

The case was taken to the judicially designated police station, the material was
held, and the people involved were questioned and released after the incident
report was filed. [@1] The nature of the plants was to be confirmed by forensic
examination, so the plants are described only as resembling cannabis. [@1]
