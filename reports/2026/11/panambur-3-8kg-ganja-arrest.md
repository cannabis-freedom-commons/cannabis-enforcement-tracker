Panambur police arrested a 23-year-old man near the Jokatte Cross railway bridge
on 5 November 2026 for allegedly possessing and selling ganja. [@1,3] The man was
identified as Mohammed Nisar, a resident of Paddayoor House, Padnoor Post,
Puttur taluk in Dakshina Kannada district. [@1,3] A team led by the North
Sub-Division ACP with Panambur police officers and staff raided the spot after
receiving information that ganja was being brought there for sale. [@1]

During the operation police seized approximately 3.8 kg of ganja valued at about
Rs 1.9 lakh. [@1,3] Officers also seized a mobile phone valued at about Rs 4,000
and a car bearing registration number KL 14 1662 and valued at Rs 2 lakh,
allegedly used to transport the contraband. [@1,3] The total value of the seized
ganja, phone and car was estimated at Rs 3.94 lakh. [@1,3] Police said the accused
had been selling ganja in Surathkal, Panambur, Kavoor and other parts of
Mangaluru city and had admitted selling it there on several earlier occasions.
[@1]

Panambur police registered a case as Crime No. 137/2026 under Sections 8(C) and
20(b)(ii)(A) of the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, and further
investigation is under way. [@2,3]
