A federal judge sentenced Brayan Mauricio Miranda, alias El Pozoles, to five
years in prison for possession of narcotics for commercial purposes. [@1] The
Fiscalía General de la República prosecuted the case and presented sufficient
evidence at the oral trial. [@1] The FGR identifies Miranda as one of the
leaders of the criminal organization La Unión Tepito. [@2] The court also
imposed the corresponding fine and ordered forfeiture of the seized drugs. [@2]

Mexico City citizen-security officers arrested Miranda in October 2023 while he
was driving a vehicle in Colonia Roma Norte in Mexico City. [@1] The vehicle
search found 496.7 grams of marijuana. [@1] The same search also found 538.3
grams of methamphetamine and 18.6 grams of cocaine, which are excluded from the
cannabis figures. [@2] The drugs were packed in transparent plastic bags,
evidence pointing to sale. [@1]

The case began under local jurisdiction and passed to federal jurisdiction in
January 2024, when the FGR received the declination of competence for the
complementary investigation. [@1] The Federal Prosecutor's Office in Mexico City
then carried the proceedings through to the oral trial that produced the
sentence. [@1]
