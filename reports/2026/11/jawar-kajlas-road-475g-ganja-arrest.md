Police in Jawar, in the Ashta block of Sehore district, Madhya Pradesh, arrested
a young man with 475 grams of ganja. [@1] Police put the estimated value of the
seized ganja at 2,500 rupees. [@1] The arrest was made on the Kajlas road in
Jawar, the block next to Ashta town. [@1]

Officers registered a case against him under the NDPS Act, India's Narcotic
Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, and began an investigation. [@1]
