Police detained three men in raids on alleged drug dealers in Sofia's Mladost and Botunets districts.
[@1,2,7,9] Sofia police director Nikolay Peltekov announced the operation at a briefing with prosecutor's
office spokesman Nikolay Nikolaev. [@9] Police seized more than 5 kg of marijuana in all. [@1,2,5,7,9]

## Mladost

On 3 November officers of the Sofia directorate's Specialised Police Forces checked a 26-year-old man in
Mladost 2 [@1,3,7,9], who has a criminal record and a conviction for drug dealing and is serving a
suspended sentence. [@1,9] At his address they found electronic scales and a jar of plant matter, about
60 grams of marijuana. [@1,5,9]

A second raid, with the directorate's narcotics section, detained a 33-year-old man who rented a home in
the district [@1,9] using a fake identity document. [@6] There officers found about €1,700, two
electronic scales and a vacuum machine [@1,5,9], many bags of plant matter, a slab of resinous
substance, a zip bag of white lumpy substance, three mobile phones and the fake ID. [@1,9] One account
gives 4.3 kg of marijuana and 150 grams of hashish oil there. [@4] The two Mladost addresses together
held about 5 kg of marijuana and 0.5 grams of cocaine. [@1,5,7,9] Investigators say the 33-year-old sold
large amounts of drugs in Sofia, has a conviction for possessing drugs for sale and is known for
offences under Articles 216, 195 and 354a. [@1,5,9]

## Botunets

In Botunets at about 10.30pm, patrol officers of the Fifth District Police Department stopped a man on
an electric scooter. [@1,4,7,9] They found plastic bags of green plant matter on him and in a hollow in
the handlebar, which he said was marijuana [@1,5,7,9], and 35 more bags and electronic scales at his
home. [@1,5,9] He was known to police for drug offences but had not served a prison sentence. [@6]

## Charges

The two Mladost men were held for up to 24 hours and then for up to 72 hours after police reported to
the Sofia City Prosecutor's Office. [@1,5,7,9] They were charged with possession for distribution, the
second also with a document offence [@3,6,8], which carries one to six years in prison and a fine of
5,000 to 20,000 leva; a court was to decide on permanent custody the next day. [@6,8] The prosecutor's
office said it had sent 37 people to court for drug dealing in the past month, in cases with drugs
worth over €1,113,000. [@6,8]
