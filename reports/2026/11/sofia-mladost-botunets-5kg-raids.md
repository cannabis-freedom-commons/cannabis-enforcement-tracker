Police detained three men in raids on alleged drug dealers in Sofia's Mladost
and Botunets districts. [@1,2,7,9] On 3 November, officers from SDVR's Specialised
Police Forces unit checked a 26-year-old man in Mladost 2. [@1,3,7,9] The 26-year-old
has a criminal record and a conviction for drug dealing and is serving a
suspended sentence. [@1,9] A second raid in Mladost was carried out by the same
unit together with SDVR's Narcotics section, which detained a 33-year-old man
who rents a home in the district. [@1,9] In Botunets at about 10.30pm, patrol
officers from the Fifth District Police Department stopped a man on an electric
scooter. [@1,4,7,9] Officers found plastic bags of green plant matter on the man and
in a hollow in the scooter's handlebar, which he said was marijuana. [@1,5,7,9] Both
Mladost men were first held for up to 24 hours and then ordered held in custody
for up to 72 hours after police reported to the Sofia City Prosecutor's Office.
[@1,5,7,9]

Police seized more than 5 kg of marijuana in the raids. [@1,2,5,7,9] Searches at two
addresses in Mladost turned up about 5 kg of marijuana and 0.5 grams of cocaine.
[@1,5,7,9] A search at one Mladost address turned up electronic scales and a jar of
plant matter, with the marijuana there weighing about 60 grams. [@1,5,9] At the
second Mladost home, officers found about €1,700, two working electronic scales
and a vacuum machine. [@1,5,9] They also found many bags of plant matter, a slab of
resinous substance and a zip bag of white lumpy substance, plus three mobile
phones and a fake ID document. [@1,9] At the Botunets man's home, police seized 35
plastic bags of green plant matter and electronic scales. [@1,5,9]

Investigators say the 33-year-old sold large amounts of drugs in Sofia, has a
conviction for possessing drugs for sale and is known to the Interior Ministry
for offences under Articles 216, 195 and 354a. [@1,5,9]

The first two detained men have been formally charged with possession for distribution, the second also with a document offence. [@3,6,8] Police found 4.3 kg of marijuana plus 150 grams of hashish prepared as oil with a marijuana smell in the second Mladost flat. [@4]

The 33-year-old rented the flat using the fake ID document. [@6] Sofia City Prosecutor's Office spokesman Nikolay Nikolaev said the two charged men face one to six years in prison and a fine of 5,000 to 20,000 Bulgarian leva. [@6] The two Mladost men were held for 72 hours and a Sofia court was due to decide the next day on a permanent custody order. [@6,8] The third man, from Botunets, was known to police for drug offences but had not served a prison sentence. [@6] The Sofia City Prosecutor's Office had sent 37 people to court for drug dealing in the past month, with the drugs in those cases valued at over €1,113,000. [@6,8]

Sofia police director Nikolay Peltekov, a chief commissioner, announced the operation at a briefing at the directorate's headquarters, joined by prosecutor's office spokesman Nikolay Nikolaev. [@9]
