Kuwait's General Department for Drug Control arrested six suspects in separate
cases involving trafficking in and use of narcotics and psychotropic substances.
[@1] The Interior Ministry said officers detained two suspects in the Farwaniya
and Abdali areas, one in Hawalli, two in Jleeb Al-Shuyoukh and one in Salwa.
[@1] The detainees and the seized items were referred to the competent authority
and legal measures were taken against them. [@1]

Officers seized 10 grams of hashish, the only cannabis product named in the
haul. [@1] The same operations seized 585 sheets of "chemical" and 500 grams of
shabu, which are not counted as cannabis here. [@1] A quarter-kilogram of heroin
and four precision scales were also seized. [@1]
