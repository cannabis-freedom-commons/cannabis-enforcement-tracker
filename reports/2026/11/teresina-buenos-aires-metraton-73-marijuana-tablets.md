Military police in Teresina seized 73 tablets of marijuana and a motorcycle from
a delivery bag in the Buenos Aires neighbourhood on the morning of Thursday 12
November 2026. [@1] The motorcyclist suspected of carrying the drugs escaped
during a police chase. [@1]

Officers on visible patrol noticed a motorcyclist behaving suspiciously and
began following him. [@1] As he fled, the man turned into an alleyway, abandoned
a bag containing the drugs and got away. [@1]

The Secretaria de Segurança Pública do Piauí said the load corresponded to about
80 kilograms of drugs. [@2]

The commander of the 9th Military Police Battalion, Major Tales, said the
tablets and other drugs had been found in the bag of a man who would have been
distributing them. [@5] The motorcyclist had not been located when the reports
were published. [@4] Police suspect the material was to supply drug distribution
in the area, and the investigation will establish where the drugs came from.
[@4]

All the seized material, including the drugs and the motorcycle, was taken to
the Departamento Estadual de Repressão ao Narcotráfico (Denarc), the state
narcotics repression department. [@1]

Despite searches of the area, the man managed to escape. [@3]

Police valued the drugs at about R$39,000, and the 73 tablets together with
other drugs weighed about 80 kilograms. [@6]
