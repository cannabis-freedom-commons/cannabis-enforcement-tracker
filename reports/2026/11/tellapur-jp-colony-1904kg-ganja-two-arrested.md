Kollur police arrested two young men accused of buying ganja in Bihar, bringing
it to Hyderabad by train and trying to sell it to workers at labour camps. [@1]
Officers seized 1.904 kg of ganja and two mobile phones from them. [@1]

Acting on a tip-off that ganja was being moved through the J.P. Colony area of
Tellapur, police set up checks there on 6 November. [@1] During the checks they
detained Irfan Alam, 22, and Azharuddin Ansari, 22. [@1] A search of the black
bag they carried found 1.904 kg of ganja in two packets. [@1]

Questioning showed the two bought about 2 kg of ganja around 2 November from an
unidentified man in a Bihar border area. [@1] They told police they took it by
train to Hyderabad and kept it at their home in Tellapur. [@1] Police said the
pair were carrying it out to sell to labourers at labour camps for profit when
they were caught. [@1]

Police seized the ganja and two mobile phones and sent both accused to judicial
remand. [@1]
