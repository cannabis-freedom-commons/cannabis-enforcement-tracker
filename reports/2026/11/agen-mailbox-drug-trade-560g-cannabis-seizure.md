In Agen, police carried out several arrests for drug trafficking, the first on 8
November. [@1] The traffic ran via the mailbox of an Agen resident born in 1979,
who deposited the cannabis inside and collected the money his clients brought.
[@2]

During the search, officers seized 300 g of cannabis resin, 260 g of cannabis
herb, cocaine and about 3,000 euros. [@1] Police also apprehended several drug
users, who admitted the facts, and a second arrest was made on 11 November. [@1]
At the suspect's home, officers found 300 g of cannabis resin, 260 g of cannabis
herb, cocaine, about 3,000 euros and ledgers. [@2] The mailbox owner was placed
in police custody. [@2]
