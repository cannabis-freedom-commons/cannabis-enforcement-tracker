A joint team of the Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB), India's border guard, and
Phulkaha police arrested a young man early Tuesday morning in the border area of
Narpatganj block in Araria district. [@1] Acting on a tip-off at about 2:40 am
on 10 November 2026, officers set up a checkpoint near Manikpur village roughly
500 metres inside the border from pillar 188/03. [@2] When the rider approached
from Nepal and was signalled to stop, he tried to flee, but officers chased and
caught him. [@2] Officers identified the arrested man as 20-year-old Neeraj
Kumar, son of Om Prakash Yadav and a resident of Laxmipur village. [@2]

During a search, officers recovered 55 kilograms of ganja from a plastic sack
tied to the back of the motorcycle. [@1] The team also seized a Bajaj Pulsar 220
motorcycle and a Redmi mobile phone from the scene. [@1] The seized motorcycle
carried registration number BR-07-AN-9705, and the phone was a Redmi Note 15 5G
model. [@2]

According to the SSB, Kumar said during questioning that he had collected the
ganja from a man named Ranjit Das in Dewanganj, Nepal, to deliver to an
unidentified recipient on the Narpatganj highway. [@1] Initial interrogation
indicated that Kumar was acting as a courier in the smuggling operation. [@2]
Phulkaha station house officer Vikas Kumar Maurya said a first information
report was registered against the accused and he was sent to judicial custody.
