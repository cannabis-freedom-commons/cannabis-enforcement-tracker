Police arrested two 28-year-old twin brothers in the Municipality of
Mylopotamos, Rethymno, on Crete on 5 November 2026. [@1] Officers of the
Mylopotamos Police Operations Department and the Rethymno Criminal Investigation
Sub-Directorate searched the men's home, their sheepfold and the surrounding
area. [@1] The coordinated operation was mounted for the morning of 5 November
2026 after information reached the two units. [@1] The two detained men are twin
brothers, Nea Kriti reported, and they were expected to be taken before the
competent prosecutor. [@2] Creta24 also described the men as twin brothers and
dated the coordinated operation to the morning of Thursday. [@3]

Officers found and seized 280 grams of cannabis, along with dried plant
fragments and a mobile phone. [@1] The brothers face charges under the drug laws
and under the police ordinance on livestock theft. [@1] A case file on livestock
theft was opened after the men said they could not gather their flock during the
check. [@1]

The case is being handled as possession of cannabis, alongside the
livestock-theft file. [@1]
