Police near Karimangalam in Tamil Nadu's Dharmapuri district arrested two young
men on Tuesday for allegedly selling ganja, the local name for cannabis. [@1]
The arrests followed information received by police that ganja was being sold in
areas around Karimangalam, and officers on patrol acted on that tip. [@1]
Officers stopped two men who were standing with a bag near the burial ground at
Hanumanthapuram and questioned them at the spot. [@1]

A search of the bag they were carrying found 100 grams of ganja tied into small
packets. [@1] Police said the small packets had been kept ready for sale. [@1]
Officers seized the ganja from the two men. [@1]

Police then arrested both men and were continuing their investigation after the
arrest. [@1] Police described the seized drug as ganja. [@1]
