Customs officers of the Kontrolleinheit Verkehrswege, the mobile traffic control
unit of the Heilbronn main customs office, stopped a car with Czech plates on
the A6 and directed its 29-year-old driver to the Eichhäuser Hof rest area for a
check. [@2] Asked at the start of the check about goods that had to be declared
or were banned, the man told the officers about four packets of cigarettes with
Czech tax stamps, which were in order, a knife with a blade springing out of the
side, and marijuana. [@2] The officers seized just under three grams of
marijuana from him. [@2]

When they then searched the car, they found a second knife with a 9.5-centimetre
blade that shoots out forwards, a metal knuckleduster and four throwing knives
in the glove compartment and in a bag on the passenger seat. [@2] Because he had
illegally brought the marijuana and the banned weapons into the country, customs
officers opened a criminal investigation against him at the scene and seized the
items as evidence. [@2] A roadside rapid drug test gave indications that he had
been under the influence of intoxicants at the time of the check, so the customs
officers called the Weinsberg traffic police inspectorate, which took charge of
him. [@2]

The criminal investigation was still ongoing when the report was published. [@2]
The officials' own account and the local report described the same stop near Bad
Rappenau. [@1] The photo published with the customs statement shows the weapons
and marijuana seized in the check. [@3]
