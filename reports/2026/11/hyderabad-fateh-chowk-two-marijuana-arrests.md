Police in Hyderabad, Sindh, arrested two alleged drug suppliers in separate
operations and said marijuana was recovered from their possession. [@1] Hali
Road Police Station Inspector Asad Pathan arrested Muhammad Amin Khan Bhurgri
during a raid at Fateh Chowk on Mirpur Khas Road. [@1] City Police Station
Inspector Tahir Mughal, accompanied by his team, arrested alleged drug peddler
Muhammad Faheem Ansari near St Mary School street. [@1]

The two arrest locations were Fateh Chowk on Mirpur Khas Road and the area near
St Mary School in Hyderabad. [@1] Police registered a case against Bhurgri under
the law identified in the report as the Substance of Narcotics Control Act. [@1]
Police registered a separate case against Ansari under the same
narcotics-control law after the reported recovery from his possession. [@1]
Police presented the two operations as part of a continued drive against
narcotics and antisocial elements. [@1]
