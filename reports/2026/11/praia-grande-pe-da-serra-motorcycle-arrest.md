A Military Police patrol on rounds at about 18h50 on Thursday 5 November 2026
spotted two suspects riding a blue Honda CG 125 Titan motorcycle with its number
plate raised on the Estrada Geral Pé da Serra in Praia Grande. [@1] The
20-year-old driver obeyed the order to stop, but the 19-year-old passenger tried
to flee on foot and was caught by the officers. [@1] Police described the driver
as already known to them, with several records linked to drug trafficking, and
as an alleged member of the criminal faction known as “Manos”. [@1]

A search of the passenger's jacket pocket found 28 individually wrapped cocaine
portions weighing 16.2 grams and two chunks of marijuana weighing 202.4 grams.
[@1] The stop took place near a place known for drug dealing, and given the
passenger's flight attempt and the quantity and packaging of the drugs, the
patrol recorded indicia of drug trafficking. [@1]

Both men were taken to the Civil Police station in Araranguá together with all
the seized drugs. [@1]
