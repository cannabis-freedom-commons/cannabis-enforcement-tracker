Military Police officers arrested a man suspected of drug trafficking during a
patrol in Village Campestre, Maceió, Alagoas. [@1] Officers spotted him riding a
motorcycle in suspicious circumstances, and when he noticed the patrol
approaching he tried to flee before being caught and stopped. [@1]

A search of the backpack he was carrying found about one kilogram of marijuana,
a precision scale and materials used to sell drugs. [@1] The motorcycle he was
riding was also found to have been reported stolen. [@1]

The suspect was arrested and taken to the Central de Flagrantes, the station
where arrests in the act are booked, for the formal procedures. [@1]
