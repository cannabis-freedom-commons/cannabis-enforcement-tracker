Carabineros of the Diego Portales sub-commission held six people in a wide
operation in eastern Rancagua, in coordination with the police investigation
section of the Rancagua Oriente Third Station and the Seventh Station, and
seized 984 grams of cocaína base (pasta base), 45 grams of marijuana and cash.
[@2] Four of the six were detained for the crime of drug trafficking, a fifth
for holding a valid judicial order, and a sixth for damage; it was also
established that one of the four trafficking detainees had an outstanding arrest
warrant. [@1]

Five of the detained are of Chilean nationality and one of Colombian
nationality. [@1]
