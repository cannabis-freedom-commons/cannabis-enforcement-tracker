Hazara Town Police in Quetta arrested a suspected drug dealer during a routine
snap-checking operation. [@1] The arrested man was identified as Janzaib, son of
Muhammad Qasim, belonging to the Noorzai tribe and a resident of Pashtoonabad.
[@1] Police said officers stopped and searched him as a suspicious individual
during routine checking in the Hazara Town area. [@2] Police registered a case
against him, took the recovered narcotics into custody and handed him over to
the relevant investigation team for further legal proceedings and interrogation.
[@1]

Officers recovered four kilograms of cannabis, described as hashish in the
Associated Press of Pakistan account, from his possession. [@2] Station House
Officer Tanveer Ahmed Bajwa said the operation was conducted under the
supervision of SSP Operations Zia Mandokhail and the guidance of the DSP/SDPO of
Sariab Circle as part of ongoing efforts to curb drug trafficking. [@1] Bajwa
said police operations would continue without discrimination to eliminate drug
trafficking, curb criminal activities and maintain law and order in the area.
[@1] He added that law enforcement agencies remain committed to eliminating the
narcotics trade and maintaining law and order across the city. [@2]
