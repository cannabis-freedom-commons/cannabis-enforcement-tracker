On 9 November 2026 at about 17:06, two resealable plastic packets were noticed
in the stairwell of an apartment building on Vejo Street in Birzai, according to
Panevezys County police. [@1] The packets were suspected to contain a prohibited
plant-origin narcotic substance, marijuana. [@1]

The packets were seized and handed over to experts for examination. [@1]
Officers opened a pre-trial investigation for unlawful possession of narcotic or
psychotropic substances without intent to distribute, under Article 259 of the
Lithuanian Criminal Code. [@1] The offence carries a possible fine, arrest or
imprisonment of up to two years. [@1]
