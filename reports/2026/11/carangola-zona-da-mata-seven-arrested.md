Seven people were arrested during a Minas Gerais Military Police operation in
the Zona da Mata on Tuesday 3 November 2026. [@1] The integrated action covered
Carangola, Fervedouro, Divino and Orizânia and executed six search-and-seizure
warrants. [@1] Everyone detained and all the seized materials were forwarded to
the Civil Police. [@1] A 62-year-old man was arrested in flagrante in rural
Fervedouro for illegal possession of a permitted firearm. [@1] In the Santo
Onofre neighbourhood in Carangola, officers caught two people buying drugs and
arrested them. [@1] Police said four other individuals were arrested in the same
action for involvement in drug trafficking and association with trafficking.
[@1]

In Lacerdina district at Morro do Cruzeiro in Carangola, officers with the Rocca
canine unit and sniffer dog Percy found a marijuana bar, a cocaine portion, a
crack stone and eight .32 cartridges. [@1] Searches of properties linked to
suspected dealers found various portions of cocaine, marijuana, crack and
hashish, plus precision scales, phones, cash, a radio and preparation and
packaging materials. [@1] The final tally listed three small marijuana
fragments, one marijuana tablet and five packets containing marijuana, plus a
small hashish portion. [@1] The same tally listed cocaine and crack items that
explain the mixed operation but are not counted as cannabis: 37 cocaine wraps,
five large cocaine stones, one larger cocaine portion, four small stones
resembling cocaine, and one large, six small and one bag of assorted crack
stones. [@1] Officers also seized R$419.60 in coins and R$1,850 in notes. [@1]
In rural Córrego Cachoeira Comprida officers found a Taurus .32 revolver loaded
with six cartridges, plus other ammunition of the same calibre and a mobile
phone. [@1]

Officers seized two motorcycles with signs of adulteration during another
judicial warrant in the same municipality. [@1] In rural Córrego Carangolinha in
Divino officers located a Toyota Hilux 4x4 with signs of identifier tampering
suggesting cloning, which was seized for legal and forensic measures. [@1] The
PMMG is the Polícia Militar de Minas Gerais, the state military police, and
Rocca is its Rondas Ostensivas Com Cães canine-patrol unit. [@1]
