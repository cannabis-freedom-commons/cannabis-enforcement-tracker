At about 22:20 on 12 November, officers of the Altenkirchen police division
stopped a 29-year-old car driver in Hamm (Sieg). [@1] The driver had no valid
driving licence, which had been revoked through administrative proceedings and
had not yet been surrendered, so officers seized it. [@1]

A strong smell of cannabis was detectable in the car, and both the driver and
his 22-year-old passenger were carrying cannabis in a permitted amount. [@1] The
driver's drug test was positive, indicating current influence of narcotics, so a
blood sample was taken. [@1]

The passenger was also carrying pepper spray and a single-handled/folding knife,
which officers seized. [@1] Investigations were opened against both men. [@1]
