Officers of Grupo Orión, a unit of Quintana Roo's state Citizen Security
Secretariat, arrested a mototaxi driver suspected of narcomenudeo, small-scale
drug dealing, in Cancún. [@1] The arrest took place on avenida Galaxias del Sol
in the municipality of Benito Juárez, which includes Cancún. [@1] During
prevention patrols, officers saw the man behind a mototaxi handling several
small bags of green plant material inside a waist bag. [@1] Seeing the police,
he tried to board the mototaxi and drive off, but the officers caught him. [@1]

The officers identified themselves and explained the reason for the stop, and
the man agreed to be searched. [@1] The search found 79 small bags of dry green
plant material described as apparently marijuana. [@1] Officers also found 15
small bags of white solid fragments that appeared to be crack, a form of
cocaine. [@1] In total, authorities secured 94 small bags of suspected drugs.
[@1] They seized the black waist bag that held the bags and the grey Piaggio Ape
City mototaxi with a white roof the man was using. [@1]

Officers handed the detainee and the seized items to the Quintana Roo Attorney
General's Office (Fiscalía General del Estado, FGE), which will investigate and
determine his legal situation. [@1] The detainee was identified as Deivy “N” and
was held over his probable involvement in health offences. [@2]

A second Cancún outlet confirms the same Grupo Orión arrest of a mototaxi driver
on avenida Galaxias del Sol with 79 bags of suspected marijuana and 15 of
apparent crack. [@2]
