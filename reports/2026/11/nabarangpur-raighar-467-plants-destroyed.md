Police destroyed 467 cannabis plants grown illegally at Binayipur village in
Timanpur panchayat of Raighar block, Nabarangpur district, Odisha. [@1] A
resident of Binayipur village, Tilachand Majhi, was growing the cannabis in the
compound of his own house. [@1]

Police arrested Majhi and seized the equipment he used to grow the plants. [@1]
Sub-divisional police officer Shubhendu Sabar gave the account at a press
conference on Monday. [@1] Raighar police station, acting on intelligence about
illegal cultivation, carried out the operation at village Binayapur. [@2]

A case numbered 293/2026 was registered under the NDPS Act and police said the
investigation was under way. [@2]
