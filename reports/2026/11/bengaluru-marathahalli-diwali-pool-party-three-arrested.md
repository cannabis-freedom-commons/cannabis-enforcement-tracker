The Central Crime Branch police raided an allegedly illegal high-tech pool party
held in the name of Diwali celebrations at a private villa in Bengaluru's
Kariyammana Agrahara area near Marathahalli. [@1] The villa was on Kariyammana
Agrahara Road in Yamalur, and the party ran from 9 pm to 5 am. [@1] Officers
arrested three organisers, identified as Arihant Pandey, Abhinav Pandey and
Partha. [@1] KannadaPrabha reported that CCB police raided the illegal high-tech
pool party in the private villa and arrested three accused. [@2] Police
registered a case at Marathahalli Police Station and opened an investigation
into the event and the alleged supply and consumption of drugs. [@1]

Police said the event was being conducted without the necessary permissions from
the concerned authorities. [@1] According to preliminary information, the event
was promoted through online platforms and applications, including FV Social 404
and Hopzyx. [@1] Organisers allegedly attracted young participants by
circulating photographs and videos of women through WhatsApp and Instagram. [@1]
Entry was reportedly restricted to around 50 couples. [@1] Male participants
were charged Rs 1,710, female participants Rs 1,080, while couples were
reportedly asked to pay Rs 2,400. [@1] Investigators suspect the organisers
withheld the exact location from participants, sharing only approximate details
and giving the final venue information over phone calls. [@1]

During the raid, CCB officials reportedly discovered ganja, suspected MDMA,
foreign liquor and prohibited hookah equipment at the villa. [@1] KannadaPrabha
reported that the operation found MDMA, ganja, liquor and prohibited hookah use.
[@2] The seized substances and other materials were taken for examination as
part of the investigation. [@1] Police are now examining the organisers' online
networks, payment arrangements and possible drug supply channels to establish
whether similar parties were conducted elsewhere. [@1]
