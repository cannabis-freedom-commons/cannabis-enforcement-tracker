During the previous night, State Police arrested a 21-year-old in Sant'Antimo
for unlawful possession of drugs intended for dealing. [@1,2] Officers from the
Frattamaggiore police station stopped him while passing through via Svizzera in
Sant'Antimo after noticing a car carrying a person acting suspiciously. [@1,2] The
officers said their investigative instinct was confirmed when the check found
drugs in the young man's possession. [@1]

He was carrying 18 sachets of marijuana with a total weight of 41 grams. [@1,2] He
was also carrying 6 sachets containing 30 grams of hashish. [@1,2] Officers also
seized 2,000 euros in banknotes of various denominations from him. [@1,2] They
also seized a knife with an overall length of 12 cm and a telescopic baton. [@1,2]

For these reasons, the suspect was arrested by the officers on duty. [@1] He was
also reported for unlawful carrying of weapons or objects capable of offending.
