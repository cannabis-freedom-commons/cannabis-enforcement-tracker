Adhurin Rama, 22, of no fixed address, was seen driving a grey Hyundai in
Abingdon Road, Oxford, on 4 September 2026. [@1] He pleaded guilty to dangerous
driving, driving without third-party insurance, driving without a licence and
failing to stop when ordered to do so by a police officer. [@1] He also pleaded
guilty to possessing a class B drug, namely cannabis, on the same occasion. [@1]
He was sentenced at Oxford Crown Court on 6 November, assisted throughout by an
Albanian interpreter. [@1]

His barrister, Gordana Austin, said he had spent two months in custody, had no
previous convictions and intended to work once his immigration status was
settled. [@1] The judge, Recorder Alexander McGregor, told him his status in the
country was precarious and that his conduct showed disregard for the rules of
the road and created danger for other people. [@1]

Rama was given a 13-month prison sentence, suspended for 18 months, and banned
from driving for 12 months with six penalty points. [@1] The court ordered the
cannabis forfeited and destroyed, and made no order for costs. [@1]
