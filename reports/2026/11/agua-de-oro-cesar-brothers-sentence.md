Córdoba’s Criminal and Correctional Chamber sentenced brothers Iván Gabriel
César and Pablo Nicolás César to four years of effective prison each for selling
drugs in Agua de Oro. [@1] Both men, aged 35, had been detained in 2025 during a
Fuerza Policial Antinarcotráfico investigation. [@1] The Jesús María
anti-narcotics prosecution office intervened in the case and ordered the
detainees transferred and the seized items sent to the court. [@1]

During the procedures that led to the detentions, investigators seized 530 doses
of marijuana, 1,476,000 pesos, a motorcycle, a digital scale and items used to
portion substances. [@1] Officers first detained one suspect at Hipólito
Yrigoyen and Filiberto Antún, finding several doses of cocaine and 176,000 pesos
on him. [@1] FPA tactical groups then raided two houses on Guillermo Marconi
street with no street number and detained the second 35-year-old suspect. [@1]
In those houses officers seized the 530 marijuana doses, 1,300,000 pesos, a
digital scale, a 190cc Honda motorcycle and other items linked to the
investigation. [@1] Córdoba’s Public Prosecutor’s Ministry confirms the same
2025 raids yielded 530 marijuana doses, 1,476,000 pesos, a scale, a motorcycle
and fractioning-related items. [@2]

The investigation lasted six months under the Public Prosecutor’s Ministry. [@1]
The brothers sold from their homes and also delivered drugs in different sectors
of the area, with delivery accounting for much of their sales. [@1] The
prosecutor’s office says the operation dismantled a drug-delivery network in
Agua de Oro. [@2] El Diario de Carlos Paz confirms both men received four years
of effective prison for drug dealing in Agua de Oro after the FPA investigation.
[@3]
