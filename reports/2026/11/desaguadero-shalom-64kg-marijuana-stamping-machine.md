Police in the Peruvian border city of Desaguadero detained a 29-year-old
Bolivian woman during an interdiction operation carried out at the facilities of
the Shalom transport company. [@1,2] The detainee was identified by authorities as
Gisela Marín Mamani, aged 29. [@1] Police reported that she had arrived at the
transport agency to collect the commercial consignment with the intention of
transferring it across the international frontier into Bolivia. [@1]

Officers seized more than 64 kilograms of marijuana during the operation at the
premises of transport company Shalom. [@1,2] According to the official police
incident report, the narcotics were conditioned and hidden inside a secret
compartment built into the base of a stamping machine that had been dispatched
as freight. [@1] Police found 42 packages of suspected Cannabis sativa inside the machine. [@2]

The interdiction at the transport company was led by the local police
commissioner, Darwin Chinguel. [@1] Public prosecutor Amalia Vega Mamani also
participated in the proceedings to conduct the seizure and oversee the
preliminary investigation. [@1] A QuickChek test gave a presumptive positive for Cannabis sativa, and a prosecutor ordered the 42 packages seized and sealed for the investigation. [@2] The packages were opened, extracted and verified at the SECINCRI PNP Ilave facilities in Chucuito province. [@2]
