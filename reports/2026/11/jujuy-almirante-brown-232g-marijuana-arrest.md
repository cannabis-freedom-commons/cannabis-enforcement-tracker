Police in San Salvador de Jujuy detained a man on Saturday after his partner
told officers he had intended to attack her and might be carrying a firearm.
[@1] Motorised patrols were passing the barrios Almirante Brown and San
Francisco de Álava, near calle Telui, when the 911 tower sent them to a report
of gender violence. [@1] The man was inside a vehicle when officers took him
into custody. [@1]

With the duty prosecutor's assistant's authorisation, police searched a Renault
Duster truck. [@1] The search found 232 grams of marijuana and 796 grams of
pasta base, a cocaine derivative. [@1] Officers also seized 282,800 pesos in
cash and a firearm. [@1]

The truck was seized as evidence and the case went to the courts. [@1] The man
was taken to a police station and held at the disposal of the courts pending
further proceedings. [@1]
