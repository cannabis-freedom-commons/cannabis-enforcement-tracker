Belpukur police arrested Abdul Matin, 40, in Bara Dhadas village in Rajshahi
city for possessing cannabis. [@1] Matin, the son of Abdul Majid Ali, was held
with 20 grams of ganja, the local term for cannabis. [@1] After he admitted the
offence in court, he was sentenced to six months' imprisonment and fined 1,000
Taka. [@1]

The arrest was part of 24-hour drives by Damkura, Belpukur and Katakhali station
police. [@1] Police said five people were arrested in those drives for drug
possession and use and received jail terms and fines after admitting guilt in
court. [@1] In Damkura, officers arrested 27-year-old Belal Hossain at his home
with yaba and drug-use equipment, and a court gave him one year's rigorous
imprisonment and a 5,000-Taka fine. [@1] In Katakhali, officers arrested Tamim,
23, Hasibul Islam, 28, and Mehedi Hasan Antar, 20, while using drugs, and each
received 20 days' rigorous imprisonment and a 500-Taka fine. [@1]

Separately, Rajshahi Metropolitan Police stations and detectives arrested 18
other people, including two warrant accused, nine drug-case accused and seven
others. [@1] City police spokesperson Deputy Police Commissioner (Sadar) Md.
Gaziur Rahman confirmed the results on Monday morning. [@1]
