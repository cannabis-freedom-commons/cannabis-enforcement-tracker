Municipal guards in Itu, São Paulo state, detained a man known in police circles
by the nickname "Pitolinha" and described as active in drug trafficking in the
city, after he tried to flee an approach on Saturday 7 November 2026. [@1] The
approach took place on Avenida Caetano Ruggieri, near the CDHU roundabout in the
Vila Lucinda neighbourhood of Itu. [@1] A Guarda Civil Municipal (GCM) patrol
was carrying out overt policing in the area when officers saw the man acting
suspiciously with a backpack. [@1] As the patrol vehicle came closer he tried to
break away from the officers and ran toward a wooded area inside the housing
estate. [@1] The guards chased him on foot and gave successive orders to stop,
which he disobeyed. [@1] The flight ended when he lost his balance and fell, and
the team restrained and searched him. [@1]

A personal search and a check of the backpack he was carrying found illicit
substances already broken down and packaged for retail sale. [@1] In total the
guards seized more than 600 portions of illicit substances. [@1] The haul
included 172 portions of marijuana. [@1] It also included 78 portions of ice,
described as a type of cannabis concentrate. [@1] A further 48 portions were of
dry, described as a hashish variety. [@1] The rest of the portions were 280 of
cocaine and 30 of crack, which are not cannabis. [@1] One mobile phone was also
seized with the drugs. [@1] g1 reported the same count of portions from the
police record. [@2] g1's account, however, attributed the approach to a Military
Police team rather than to the municipal guards. [@2] TVS+ reported that the man
was wearing a beige shirt and carrying a black backpack. [@3]

Because of the fall during the escape, the man was taken in custody to a Unidade
de Pronto Atendimento (UPA), an emergency care unit, for medical treatment. [@1]
He was then taken to the city's Plantão Policial, where the occurrence was
registered and the seized material remained held at the disposal of the courts.
