On 1 November 2026 the Graz traffic inspectorate Graz 2 carried out priority
traffic controls across districts in Graz with a civil patrol focused on drink-
and drug-driving. [@2] Officers presented four drivers suspected of impairment
by intoxicants to the police medical service, and the doctor on duty found all
four unfit to drive. [@2] A further driver recorded just under 1.5 per mille on
a calibrated breath test. [@2] Officers provisionally seized three driving
licences, prohibited five people from continuing their journeys and collected a
1,200 euro security deposit from a driver from Kosovo. [@2] A passer-by who
showed officers an extended middle finger was reported to the district court for
insult. [@2]

During the same checks officers seized a small amount of cannabis herb. [@1]
Police filed one report under Austria's Narcotics Act for the seized cannabis.
[@3] In total the operation recorded nine traffic reports, 13 preliminary breath
tests, four medical presentations, three licence seizures and five driving bans.
[@3] The checks followed separate Halloween-night controls that had already
netted numerous drink- and drug-drivers, a different operation left out of this
report. [@1]
