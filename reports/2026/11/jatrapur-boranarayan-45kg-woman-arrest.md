A joint team of Jatrapur police and the 81st Battalion of the BSF arrested a
woman named Safura Khatun. [@1] Officers detained Khatun at the scene of the
raid. [@1] Jatrapur officer-in-charge Parthanath Bhowmik said she would be
produced before the Sonamura court on Wednesday. [@1]

The team seized 45 kg 900 g, or 45.9 kg, of dried cannabis in the operation.
[@1] The raided house is under Boranarayan gram panchayat, a village-council
area, in the Jatrapur police station area. [@1] Officers recovered the dried
cannabis from the house after a prolonged search. [@1] The joint force entered
the house late at night. [@1] It took until sunrise to finish the overnight
operation and bring the detained woman to the police station. [@1]

Police said the raid followed information from a secret source. [@1] Bhowmik led
the operation with Sub-Inspector Bijay Chakraborty, other police officers, TSR
personnel and women police officers. [@1]
