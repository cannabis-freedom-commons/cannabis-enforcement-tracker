La diligencia se desarrolló desde el pasado miércoles 11 de noviembre de 2026,
mediante auto motivado, con la participación de detectives de la Dirección de
Lucha Contra el Narcotráfico (DLCN), Regional Litoral Atlántico, en conjunto con
la Fuerza de Tarea Conjunta Xatruch (FTC-XATRUCH) y C2. [@1] During the
intervention, the teams located an area of roughly one manzana of land where
about 700 plants of suspected marijuana were growing, with variable heights of
up to 1.5 metres. [@1]

They also counted around 2,000 plants that had already been cut and were ready
for the next stages of processing and packaging. [@1] As part of the
inspections, an artisanally-built structure was also found that was allegedly
used to process and pack the drug. Both the plantation and the structure were
the subject of the destruction and incineration proceedings ordered by the
prosecutorial authority. [@1]
