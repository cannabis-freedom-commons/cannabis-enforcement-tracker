A Military Police Shock Platoon team was patrolling in Loteamento Quebec, in the
14 de Novembro area of Cascavel, when officers spotted a cannabis plant inside a
plot near a residence. [@1] The officers entered the lot to check the plant and
found a white pickup, partially dismantled, in the same yard. [@1] No one was
arrested during the operation and those responsible for the vehicle and the
plants had not been identified. [@1,5]

During the inspection of the ground, officers located three cannabis plants,
known locally as maconha. [@1,3,4,5] The second account identifies the seized vehicle
as a Volkswagen Amarok found in the yard with cloned plates. [@2] A records
check showed the pickup had a theft-or-robbery record in Rio Grande do Sul,
while its plates belonged to a different, black pickup. [@1,5] The pickup was
stripped, missing its engine, bumpers and tailgate, with other parts also
removed. [@1]

The three plants and the pickup were seized and sent to the 15th Police
Subdivision of Cascavel for the applicable legal procedures. [@2,3,4,5] The occurrence
was recorded in the late afternoon of Monday, 2 November 2026. [@2,3,4]

The discovery occurred at around 17h during a Shock Platoon patrol on Rua Canadá. [@5] The Amarok was found without plates, with its engine removed and with differing paint between the bodywork and the engine compartment. [@5] Officers found two plates inside the pickup showing signs of forgery. [@5] The military police said the property showed no signs of being inhabited. [@5]
