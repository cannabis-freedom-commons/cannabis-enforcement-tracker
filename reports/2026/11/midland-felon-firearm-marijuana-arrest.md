A Midland traffic stop led to the arrest of a convicted felon accused of
possessing a gun and marijuana. [@1] On Wednesday, 4 November 2026, TAG Center
Permian Basin members conducted a traffic stop in the 2300 block of East Front
Street in Midland. [@1] The driver was identified as Julian Jesus Medrano, whom
the Midland County Sheriff's Office said is a convicted felon. [@1] Authorities
found Medrano in possession of marijuana and a firearm. [@1] He was arrested on
a charge of unlawful possession of a firearm by a felon and was booked into the
Midland County Detention Center. [@1]

Jail detainee records list Medrano on a federal detainer by the U.S. Marshals
Service, with no bond set. [@1] KOSA reported that driver Julian Medrano, a
convicted felon, was found in possession of marijuana and a gun. [@2] KOSA
reported that Medrano was arrested for unlawful possession of a firearm by a
felon and booked into the Midland County Detention Center. [@2]

The TAG Center Permian Basin includes personnel from the Midland County
Sheriff's Office, the Ector County Sheriff's Office, the Midland Police
Department, the Texas Department of Public Safety, the Midland County District
Attorney's Office, the FBI, Homeland Security Investigations, the DEA, HIDTA and
the U.S. Marshals Service's Western District of Texas, El Paso Division. [@1]
