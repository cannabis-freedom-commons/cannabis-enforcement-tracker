Revenue officers seized contraband with an estimated total value of over
€210,000 in various operations in Dublin, the Midlands and Rosslare Europort in
the week commencing 26 October 2026. [@2] Revenue officers seized over 8.3 kg of
herbal cannabis, valued by Revenue at over €116,000, alongside various other
illicit drugs valued at over €22,500. [@1] Revenue said investigations into all
of the seizures are ongoing and no arrests, charges or court outcomes were
reported for this operation. [@2]

The other drugs included cannabis oil, cannabis resin, edibles and cannabinoid
vape liquids alongside tablets, MDPHP HCI, 5-MEO-DMT, LSD and diazepam. [@1] The
herbal cannabis and other drugs were found in parcels that originated in Spain,
Belgium, the Netherlands, the UK and India and were destined for various
addresses nationwide. [@2] The herbal cannabis and other drug seizures were
detected through risk profiling with the assistance of detector dogs Daithi, Sam
and Ciara. [@2]

Officers also seized more than 4,118 litres of alcohol worth over €51,000,
representing a potential loss to the Exchequer of over €27,500. [@1] They also
seized tobacco products valued at over €6,600, representing a potential
Exchequer loss of over €5,100. [@1] Officers confiscated 11 counterfeit items
worth over €13,900 in the same weekly operations. [@1] Officers also seized
1,228 weapons accessories in the same weekly operations. [@1] Revenue described
the detections as part of its ongoing operations targeting smuggling and shadow
economy activity. [@2]
