Jade Hopkins, 35, was spared jail after admitting two offences of conveying a
controlled drug into La Moye prison, Jersey's only prison. [@1] Relief
Magistrate David Le Cornu accepted that the only explanation for the offences
was that Hopkins had been intimidated by her ex-partner. [@1]

Crown Advocate Harrison Gollop, prosecuting, said Hopkins had carried 1.6 g of
cannabis inside her mouth during a visit to La Moye on 19 December last year.
[@1] Officers then searched her home and found a further 1.5 g of cannabis
intended for the same partner in prison. [@1]

Defence advocate Adam Harrison said the offending arose from a controlling and
coercive relationship set out in a probation report, in which her ex-partner
intimidated her and her family at her home and affected her ability to make
decisions freely. [@1] Harrison said Hopkins had worked in nursing and social
care but had been suspended from her studies to become a qualified nurse pending
the outcome of the case. [@1]

Le Cornu said the offences passed the custody threshold but the case was so
exceptional as to justify departing from standard sentencing policy, and bound
her over on each charge for 12 months to be of good behaviour. [@1]
