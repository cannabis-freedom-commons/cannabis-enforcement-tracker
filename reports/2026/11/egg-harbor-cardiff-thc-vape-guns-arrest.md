Egg Harbor Township police arrested 19-year-old Hebereli Mejia of the Cardiff
section after a long-term narcotics investigation. [@1,3] He was lodged at the
Atlantic County Justice Facility pending a detention hearing. [@1,3] He faces 40
counts for allegedly running a drug business out of his home and a storage
facility. [@2] He appeared briefly in court by video on Thursday and learned his
detention hearing would be Tuesday. [@2]

On 4 November, members of the township police Criminal Investigation Bureau
executed multiple court-authorized search warrants. [@1] The searches covered
his Cardiff home, a storage unit and a 2022 Tesla registered to him. [@2] The
searches yielded more than a pound of marijuana flower and vape products
containing more than 2,000 grams of Delta-9 THC liquid. [@2] Officers seized two
firearms, multiple large-capacity magazines and a silencer fitted to one gun.
[@1] Officers also found 99 amphetamine pills and five MDMA pills alongside the
cannabis and guns. [@2] Police said Mejia faces additional weapons charges
because of a final restraining order against him. [@2]

Police said the action was part of an ongoing effort to disrupt the sale and
distribution of THC vape products marketed to young people. [@1] Investigators
said he had been under surveillance for more than a year since September 2025.
[@2] The township police were assisted by the Atlantic County Regional SWAT Team
and the Prosecutor’s Office. [@1] Charges are accusations and he is presumed
innocent until proven guilty. [@1]
