Officers of the Hannover main customs office found three cannabis seedlings in a
parcel from Austria during a parcel check. [@1] The parcel was addressed to a
man from Hannover and was checked on 3 November 2026. [@1] Customs officers
opened criminal proceedings for attempted unlawful import, termed Bannbruch,
over the shipment. [@2] The recipient will receive mail from customs instead of
the seedlings. [@1]

The seedlings already count as cannabis plants, unlike propagation material such
as seeds, so they fall under the import ban. [@1] Customs can check parcels on a
random basis even within the European Union. [@1]

Customs spokeswoman Joline Kassner said online buyers must pay close attention
because the plant's actual stage decides the case, not whether a website calls
it a cutting or a seedling. [@1] No arrest was reported in the case. [@2]
