The Constanța Court of Appeal finally sentenced DZ to seven years in prison on
20 December 2023 for bringing drugs into Romania. [@1] DZ had challenged the
Romanian conviction through appeal and cassation appeal. [@1] The case raised
whether a court in another member state could refuse to surrender him because
the penalty seemed disproportionate. [@1]

DZ and his wife had brought into Romania three grams of cannabis and four
tablets containing MDMA. [@1] DZ told the Dutch court the substances were for
his wife's personal use to ease pain. [@1] The Amsterdam tribunal described the
quantities as small and found no intent to traffic. [@1] Dutch prosecution
explanations at the CJEU hearing said bringing up to ten grams of the drug
category at issue in the Netherlands could draw one to three weeks in prison.
[@1]

The Grand Chamber of the EU Court of Justice held that a Dutch court's view that
the Romanian penalty is too severe does not by itself justify refusing
surrender. [@1] It decided on 3 November 2026 that such a refusal needs evidence
of serious deficiencies in the issuing state's legislation or practice together
with the lack of an effective remedy. [@2] It found Romanian law allowed courts
to tailor the penalty to the concrete circumstances and to consider mitigating
circumstances. [@1] The ruling did not treat any seven-year term for small
quantities as proportionate, did not generally validate Romanian penalties, and
did not annul the conviction. [@1]
