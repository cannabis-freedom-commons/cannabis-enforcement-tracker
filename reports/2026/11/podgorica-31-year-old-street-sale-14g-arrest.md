Podgorica police arrested 31-year-old N.B. on suspicion of street sale of
narcotic drugs. [@1] Officers of the Podgorica Security Department obtained a
search warrant from the investigating judge of the Higher Court in Podgorica, in
coordination with the Higher State Prosecutor's Office in Podgorica, and
searched the flat and other premises used by N.B. in Podgorica. [@1]

The search found about 14 grams of material suspected to be marijuana, alongside
Xalol and Buprenorphine tablets, a precision scale and bags for packing drugs,
according to the Police Directorate. [@1] In a living-room dresser, police found
a metal box with Xalol and Buprenorphine tablets listed as narcotic drugs, a
precision scale, a PVC package with dark-green plant material suspected to be
marijuana weighing about 14 grams, small empty zip bags for packing drugs, more
transparent packing bags and a hand-rolled joint. [@1] Officers also seized
N.B.'s mobile phone for expert examination. [@1]

N.B. was deprived of liberty on the order of the state prosecutor at the Higher
State Prosecutor's Office in Podgorica on suspicion of unauthorised production,
possession and distribution of narcotic drugs. [@1] Police suspect he procured
and possessed the drugs for distribution. [@1] He was to be brought to the
Higher State Prosecutor's Office in Podgorica with a criminal complaint within
the legal deadline. [@1]
