On Tuesday 10 November, late in the afternoon, officers made a significant
cannabis seizure in building MAH2 of Riom prison. [@1] At about 3:30 p.m., after
an exercise-yard round, supervisors ordered pat-down searches at the exits from
walks and movements following suspicions. [@1] One detainee tried to get rid of
something, but officers directed him to a search room. [@1] A full body search
found just under 450 grams of cannabis on him. [@1]

The union says it was one of the largest seizures since the facility opened in
2016. [@1] The union puts total drug seizures at the prison at 7.1 kg up to 30
September 2026 and 11.5 kg in 2025. [@1] The local FO Justice union called on
the prison management to impose a dissuasive sanction. [@1] It also called on
the prosecutor’s office for a penalty matching the trafficking involved. [@1]
