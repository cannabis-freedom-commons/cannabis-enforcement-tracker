A joint team of the Government Railway Police (GRP) and the Railway Protection
Force (RPF) arrested a man and a woman during a check at Rampur Junction railway
station. [@1] The pair were stopped on platforms 2 and 3, near a signboard
pointing toward Bareilly. [@1] The checking took place on the night of Wednesday
4 November 2026, the day the Moradabad railway police posted the arrests. [@3]

Officers found 210 g of charas on the man and 208 g on the woman, 418 g in all,
and police estimated the seized charas at about ₹42,000. [@1] GRP registered
case number 26/2026 against both under sections 8 and 20 of the Narcotic Drugs
and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act and was investigating the case. [@1] The
Moradabad railway police, which oversees GRP Rampur, said the joint team
arrested one man and one woman and recovered 418 g of charas, estimated at
₹42,000, in an ongoing crime-prevention drive. [@3]

Dainik Bhaskar reported that the checking was part of a campaign directed by
Moradabad railway superintendent of police Ashutosh Shukla against crime and the
illegal drug trade at railway stations. [@2] The action team included GRP
station head Ishwar Chand, sub-inspector Sandeep Kumar and RPF sub-inspector
Vijay Prakash, Bhaskar reported. [@2]
