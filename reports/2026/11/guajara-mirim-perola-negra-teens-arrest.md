Military Police officers of the 6th Battalion apprehended two 17-year-old
adolescents late on the night of Monday 2 November 2026 near the Pérola Negra
clandestine port in the Triângulo neighbourhood of Guajará-Mirim, Rondônia. [@1,3]
The patrol was operating in the area when it spotted two young people acting
suspiciously. [@1] The pair were travelling on a silver Honda Biz motorcycle
carrying two occupants. [@2] One of them got off and stayed at the spot while
the driver headed toward the commercial centre. [@1,3]

Officers stopped the teenager who stayed and found the drugs during a search of
his backpack. [@1] Officers found 12 capsules of a substance described as having
synthetic-drug characteristics, popularly known as bala. [@1,3] Officers also
found three portions of plant material described as similar to maconha, the
Portuguese term for cannabis. [@2,3]

The second adolescent returned shortly afterwards, was also stopped, claimed
ownership of the drugs and said he had bought the material in Bolivia. [@1,3] He
said part of the drugs had already been sold in Brazilian territory and the rest
would be sold at a party in the neighbouring country. [@1,3] Officers took the two
teenagers, the motorcycle, the drugs and mobile phones to the Regional Civil
Police station, where a child-welfare counsellor accompanied the case. [@1,3] The
teenagers’ legal guardians were called to follow the procedures, which were also
accompanied by a child-welfare counsellor. [@2] He also said he intended to
return to Bolivia to attend a party and sell the remainder. [@2]
