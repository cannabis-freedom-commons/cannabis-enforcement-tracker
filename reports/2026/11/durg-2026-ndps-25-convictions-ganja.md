Durg police said strong investigation and solid evidence had led courts to
sentence the accused in 25 NDPS cases in the district so far in 2026. [@1] Those
cases had been registered in the Mohan Nagar, Supela, Kumhari, Nevai, Patan,
Utai, Durg, Amleshwar, Chhawani and Nandini Nagar station areas, and courts
found the accused guilty after trial. [@1] Superintendent of Police Vijay
Agrawal said action against the illegal drug trade in the district was
continuing. [@1]

Police said the 25 convictions included several ganja-trafficking cases. [@1] In
some of those cases the accused were found with from 1 kg to more than 5 kg of
ganja. [@1] In different cases courts imposed imprisonment and fines, with some
accused given 10 to 15 years of rigorous imprisonment and fines of up to Rs
100,000. [@1] Police said seizure actions during investigation, witness
statements and documentary evidence were placed strongly before the courts,
producing convictions in the 25 cases. [@1]

Besides ganja, the 25 cases covered illegal sale and transport of restricted
narcotic medicines and prohibited intoxicants. [@1] Agrawal said the aim was not
only to arrest accused but to have courts punish them. [@1] Police said emphasis
was being placed on investigation quality, solid evidence and effective
prosecution in court. [@1] He said no one involved in the drug trade would be
spared and strict action would continue to protect young people and build a
drug-free society. [@1]
