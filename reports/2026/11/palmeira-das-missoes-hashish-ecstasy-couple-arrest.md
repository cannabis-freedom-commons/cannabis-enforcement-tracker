Military police in Palmeira das Missões stopped a VW Saveiro pickup on the night
of Sunday 8 November 2026 and arrested the two men inside, aged 24 and 25, for
drug trafficking. [@1]

A search of the men and the vehicle found seven ecstasy tablets, 36 portions of
hashish, cash and mobile phones. [@1] The officers arrested both men in the act
and took them to have the incident recorded, then to the state prison. [@1]

The drugs and the pickup were seized. [@1] The account came from the Military
Brigade, Rio Grande do Sul's state military police force. [@1]
