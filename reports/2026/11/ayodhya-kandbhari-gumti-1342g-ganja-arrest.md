Ayodhya's Kotwali Nagar police arrested Jafar Khan on the accusation that he was
selling ganja from a roadside kiosk, a gumti, near the Kandbhari crossing. [@1]
On questioning, he identified himself as Jafar Khan, son of Fakhruddin Khan and
a resident of Mangalpura Takia Reedganj in the Kotwali Nagar station area of
Ayodhya. [@1] He was reported to be 20 years old. [@2] Police registered a case against him under the NDPS Act and began
the process of producing him before a court. [@1] Police were trying to find where he had brought the consignment from and whom in the district he had been going to supply. [@2]

Station in-charge Santosh Kumar Mishra said sub-inspector Vijay Sen Yadav was on
patrol with head constable Ramesh Yadav and constable Vipin Pandey on 10
November. [@1] The operation was directed by Superintendent of Police Martand Prakash Singh and supervised by Additional Superintendent Vishal Pandey and Circle Officer (City) Abhishek Singh, with constable Sanjay Gupta also on the team led by inspector Santosh Kumar. [@2] During the patrol, an informer told the team that a man was
selling ganja from the gumti beside the road near the Kandbhari crossing. [@1]
Police said the youth inside tried to flee and hide a polythene bag kept on the
counter when he saw them, and the team caught him. [@1]

A search recovered 1.342 kg of ganja, a silver Nokia mobile phone and 1,180
rupees in cash from his possession. [@1] Police said the ganja was held in 103 packets of different sizes, and they also seized three bundles of polythene kept for packing it. [@2] Police videographed the recovery and
sealed a sample of the seized ganja. [@1]
