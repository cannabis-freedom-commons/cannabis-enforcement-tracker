Ayodhya's Kotwali Nagar police arrested Jafar Khan on the accusation that he was
selling ganja from a roadside kiosk, a gumti, near the Kandbhari crossing. [@1]
On questioning, he identified himself as Jafar Khan, son of Fakhruddin Khan and
a resident of Mangalpura Takia Reedganj in the Kotwali Nagar station area of
Ayodhya. [@1] Police registered a case against him under the NDPS Act and began
the process of producing him before a court. [@1]

Station in-charge Santosh Kumar Mishra said sub-inspector Vijay Sen Yadav was on
patrol with head constable Ramesh Yadav and constable Vipin Pandey on 10
November. [@1] During the patrol, an informer told the team that a man was
selling ganja from the gumti beside the road near the Kandbhari crossing. [@1]
Police said the youth inside tried to flee and hide a polythene bag kept on the
counter when he saw them, and the team caught him. [@1]

A search recovered 1.342 kg of ganja, a silver Nokia mobile phone and 1,180
rupees in cash from his possession. [@1] Police videographed the recovery and
sealed a sample of the seized ganja. [@1]
