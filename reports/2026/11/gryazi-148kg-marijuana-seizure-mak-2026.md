Police officers acting during the preventive operation Mak-2026 seized marijuana
from a 58-year-old resident of Gryazi. [@1] Gryazi police acted on operational
information about possible unlawful drug storage, according to the regional
interior ministry. [@2] The man said he had found the prohibited plants and kept
them for his own use. [@2]

Officers found a plant substance at the suspect's home and seized it. [@1]
Testing identified the seized substance as cannabis (marijuana) weighing 1
kilogram 480 grams. [@1] The second account confirms the total seized weight as
1 kg 480 grams. [@2]

Investigators classified his actions under part 2 of article 228 of the Criminal
Code, covering unlawful acquisition and storage of plants containing narcotic
drugs or their parts on a large scale. [@1] That charge carries three to ten
years in prison. [@1] He was placed under a written undertaking not to leave
while the investigation continues. [@1]
