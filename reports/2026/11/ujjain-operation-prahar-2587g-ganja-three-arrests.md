Ujjain police arrested three men with a combined 2.587 kilograms of ganja, the
Hindi word for cannabis, in three separate stops under Operation Prahar. [@1]
Khachraud police detained 35-year-old Mangal near the railway culvert on
Bhatkhedi Road and seized 752 grams of ganja from him. [@1] Bhatpachlana police
caught 41-year-old Rakesh near the railway gate with 549 grams. [@1] Nanakheda
police caught 38-year-old Monu, also known as Kushal Singh, near the Engineering
College Ground with 1.286 kilograms. [@1]

The three weights add up to the 2.587 kilograms police reported seizing in the
three cases. [@1] The stops were at different places in Ujjain district, and
police said they registered NDPS cases in all three. [@1]

Police said Mangal, a resident of Nayapara in the Sarangi police station area of
Jhabua district, was running a ganja trade from Station Road in Khachraud. [@1]
Rakesh is a resident of Maulana in Badnagar, and Monu of Patel Nagar in the
Chimanganj Mandi station area. [@1] The arrests were part of Operation Prahar, a
continuing police drive against drug traffickers. [@1]
