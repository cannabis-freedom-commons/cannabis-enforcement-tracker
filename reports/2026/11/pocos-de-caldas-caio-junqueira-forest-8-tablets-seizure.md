Civil Police seized eight marijuana tablets on the night of 12 November 2026 in
a forest in the east zone of Poços de Caldas. [@1] The eight tablets were buried
in two containers hidden in the forest. [@1]

Teams from the 1st Civil Police Station carried out an operation to investigate
trafficking complaints in the Caio Junqueira neighbourhood. [@1] The complaints
said a forest in the neighbourhood was being used as a drug depot. [@1] Officers
carried out an intensive sweep through the vegetation in the rain to find the
drugs. [@1]

The seized material was taken to the Civil Police station. [@1] First-station
chief Juliane Emiko said investigations continue to identify the origin of the
material. [@1]
