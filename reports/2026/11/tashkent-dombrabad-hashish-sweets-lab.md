Police in Tashkent shut down an underground laboratory that produced
drug-containing sweets, cookies and marmalade with added hashish oil, in a
rented flat in the Dombrabad mahalla of Chilanzar district, as reported by the
Interior Ministry press service. [@1] The alleged online distributor detained in
the operation is described as a 23-year-old. [@1] The operation began when
officers detained that 23-year-old in Yashnabad district for placing drug dead
drops through Telegram and online shops. [@1] Officers found 74 grams of hashish
oil on the Yashnabad detainee. [@1] On the basis of his testimony and
operational work, officers identified the wholesale supplier and his laboratory
in Chilanzar district. [@1]

In the flat officers found and seized about 6 kilograms of hashish and its oils,
described as more than 19,200 drug doses. [@1] The detailed inventory lists 3.5
kg of hashish oil, 350 grams prepared for sale in 361 separate wraps, mixtures
of hashish and hashish oil, and 600 grams of pure hashish. [@1] The laboratory
also held cookies baked using 1.5 kg of hashish oil and four packs of hashish
marmalade. [@1] As physical evidence officers seized medical syringes,
electronic scales, zip bags, tape and 500 empty vials used for dosing and
packing drugs. [@1]

A criminal case was opened under point (a) of part 6 of Article 273 of
Uzbekistan's Criminal Code for illegal large-scale manufacture and sale for the
purpose of sale. [@1] Conviction under that provision carries up to 20 years'
imprisonment, and investigators continue work to identify other network members.
