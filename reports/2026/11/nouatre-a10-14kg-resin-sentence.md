The motorist stopped by customs officers was identified as Pablo C., driving a
Renault Mégane checked on the A10 at Nouâtre on 30 October 2026. [@1] He was a
54-year-old Spanish national. [@1] He was coming from Spain with a large load of
drugs. [@1] He told the court that he acted out of despair. [@1] He was
presented in comparution immédiate before the tribunal correctionnel de Tours on
Monday 2 November. [@1] The Spanish driver caught with 14 kg of cannabis resin
hidden in his car at Nouâtre was sentenced to two years in prison. [@1]

A detection dog marked the hiding places without a single hesitation. [@1]
Officers found more than 5 kg in the right rear door, more than 5 kg in the left
rear door and nearly 4 kg in the engine block. [@1] In total, after the motorway
check, customs officers seized more than 14 kg of cannabis resin from the car.
[@1] The cargo was estimated at 113,440 euros. [@1]

The operation was carried out by customs officers after a highway check on the
A10 at the level of Nouâtre. [@1]
