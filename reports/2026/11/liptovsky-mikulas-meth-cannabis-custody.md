Police in the Liptovský Mikuláš district in north-western Slovakia detained two
people suspected of drug-related crime during an operation in the past week.
[@1] The Žilina regional police spokesperson Daniela Kocková told TASR that an
investigator had filed a motion for custodial prosecution of both, which a judge
accepted. [@2]

During the operation, officers stopped a Škoda Octavia. [@3] A search of the
vehicle secured suspicious material, which was sent for expert examination to
establish its origin. [@1] A preliminary expert examination confirmed the
investigators' suspicion that the seized substances included methamphetamine in
a larger quantity. [@1] A personal search of the suspect woman found dried plant
material of the genus Cannabis. [@1]

An investigator at the Liptovský Mikuláš district police directorate charged the
40-year-old woman and the 46-year-old man with the misdemeanour of unauthorised
possession of a narcotic and psychotropic substance and with the crime of
unauthorised production of and trading in narcotic and psychotropic substances.
[@1] A judge accepted the investigator's motion for custodial prosecution
(väzobné stíhanie), the Slovak procedure under which a charged suspect is held
in custody. [@1]
