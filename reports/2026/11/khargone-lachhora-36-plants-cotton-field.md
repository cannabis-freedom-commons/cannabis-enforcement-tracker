Bhikangaon police raided a field in Lachhora village under Bhikangaon police
station limits after a tip-off about suspected cannabis cultivation. [@1,2] The
raided field belonged to a person identified as Mehtap. [@1,2] The accused fled
from the spot during the police operation and remains absconding. [@1,2] Police
registered a case under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS)
Act and launched a search for the accused. [@1,2]

During the search, police found cannabis plants growing among the cotton crop.
[@1,2] Police seized all 36 cannabis plants from the cotton field. [@1,2] The seized
plants had a combined weight recorded at 32 kg. [@1,2] Police put the estimated
value of the seized cannabis at Rs 3.20 lakh. [@1,2]

The seizure was carried out as part of a state-level anti-narcotics campaign
being conducted from October 27 to November 10 on the instructions of the state
police headquarters. [@1,2] The operation was undertaken under the guidance of
Khargone Superintendent of Police Ravindra Verma and on the directions of SDOP
Ketan Adlak. [@1,2] Further investigation is underway to establish the
circumstances of the cultivation and determine whether anyone else was involved.
[@1,2] The police account does not specify whether the accused had been identified
beyond the name associated with the field. [@1,2]

The raid was carried out by a police team led by Bhikangaon station house officer Balram Singh Rathore. [@2]
