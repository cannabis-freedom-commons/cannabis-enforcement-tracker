Officers of the Vasto-Arenaccia police station in Naples stopped a 19-year-old
in vicoletto Santa Maria della Fede during an afternoon patrol on Sunday 8
November 2026. [@1] The officers found six bags of marijuana, seven wraps of
hashish and 170 euros in cash in notes of various denominations on him. [@1]
Cronache della Campania gave the same street find: six bags of marijuana, seven
wraps of hashish and 170 euros in notes of various denominations. [@2]

A search of the home the teenager used found another 17 bags of marijuana, 15
wraps of hashish and material for packaging the drug. [@1] In total police
recovered 23 bags of marijuana and 22 wraps of hashish, 45 packages altogether,
between the street check and the house search. [@1] The drug was already divided
into doses and accompanied by packaging material, which investigators assessed
in their checks on the suspected dealing. [@1]

The teenager was arrested for unlawful possession of narcotics. [@1] Cronache
della Campania reported that police described the operation as a patrol check in
the piazza Nazionale area of Naples. [@2] Teleclubitalia also placed the arrest
on the afternoon of the previous day in the piazza Nazionale area of Naples.
[@3]
