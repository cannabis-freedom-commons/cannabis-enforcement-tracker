Guerrero State Police and federal forces detained three people in three separate
interventions in Acapulco on 7 November 2026. [@1]

State police, the Mexican Army, the Navy and the National Guard detained one
person during preventive patrols in the Granjas del Marqués housing estate,
where officers seized 35 small bags of suspected marijuana, 16 doses of a
substance called piedra and four doses of cristal. [@1] The same forces detained
another person in the Unidad Habitacional Vicente Guerrero 200 housing complex
and seized 29 small bags of suspected marijuana and 17 doses of suspected
piedra. [@2] State police alone detained a third person in the La Postal
neighbourhood, where 27 small bags of suspected marijuana and 10 doses of
cristal were found. [@2]

Across the three interventions the authorities counted 91 small bags of
suspected marijuana, alongside 33 doses of piedra and 14 doses of cristal. [@1]
No names, ages, charges or court dates were released, and the Guerrero Public
Security Secretariat said those detained are presumed innocent until a court
sentence establishes their responsibility. [@1]
