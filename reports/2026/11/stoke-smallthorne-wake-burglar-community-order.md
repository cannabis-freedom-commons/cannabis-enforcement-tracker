A 42-year-old Stoke-on-Trent woman was given an 18-month community order at
Stoke-on-Trent Crown Court after breaking into a neighbour's home over missing
cannabis. [@1] The court heard that she and the householder were both at the
funeral and wake on 8 April 2025, and that she went to his house that evening
after drinking at the wake. [@1]

The prosecutor said she returned, got in through the back door and was drunk.
[@1] The prosecutor said she took two bicycles, fell and smashed a fish tank,
and that replacing the tank cost £300 and the water damage to the floor £400.
[@1] She was arrested and interviewed and said she meant to take the bicycles
only temporarily and that the damage was accidental. [@1]

The householder's cannabis did not come into the case as a charge or a seized
quantity; the only offence was burglary. [@1] Her lawyer said she had shown
"fulsome remorse" to the probation service and was trying to cut down on drugs
and alcohol with structured help. [@1]

The community order carries a probation requirement and 60 hours of unpaid work,
and her progress is to be reviewed at a hearing on 10 December. [@1] The judge
also made a two-year restraining order barring her from contacting the
householder and his family or going to their address. [@1] The judge said her
alcohol and cannabis use had blighted her life and that the offence did not
cross the custody threshold. [@1]
