Tarkhanwala police arrested two men, Aslam and Gulzar, during raids on Sunday 1
November 2026 and recovered 1.8 kilograms of hashish from them. [@1] Hashish is
cannabis resin. [@1] A police spokesperson said the raids took place in
different localities in the area, and the pair were detained as suspected drug
pushers. [@1] The hashish was recovered from the custody of the arrested men
during the operation, the spokesperson said. [@1] Police said further
investigation was under way. [@1]

The arrests were made in Tarkhanwala, a police station area in Sargodha district
in Pakistan's Punjab province. [@1] Javed Kalyar holds the rank of station house
officer, the officer in charge of a police station, and he led the teams that
made the arrests. [@1] More than one police team took part in the raids, which
covered different localities in the area. [@1]
