Ruston police arrested 23-year-old Dmitry Payne of Ruston at 1:15 p.m. on 4
November 2026 at West Barnett Springs Avenue and South Maple Street. [@1] A
lieutenant stopped Payne's gray Toyota Camry after his in-car radar measured it
at 47 mph in a 25 mph zone. [@1] Payne got out keeping the door closed behind
him, and the lieutenant smelled marijuana on him and saw him shaking and
stumbling over his words. [@1]

When Payne opened the door for his insurance information, the lieutenant smelled
raw marijuana strongly from inside the car and waited for a second unit. [@1]
Asked how much marijuana was in the vehicle, Payne said about 13 or 14 grams.
[@1] A search found a glass mason jar behind the front passenger seat holding
six individually packaged baggies of suspected marijuana weighing 37.1 grams in
total. [@1]

Officers took Payne to the Lincoln Parish Narcotics Enforcement Team office and
then to the Lincoln Parish Detention Center, where he was booked on possession
with intent to distribute a Schedule I drug. [@1] The account comes from an
arrest affidavit that the Lincoln Parish Journal published as law-enforcement
public information. [@1]
