Córdoba's Fuerza Policial Antinarcotráfico detained three men, including two
brothers, in an operation against drug selling in Barrio Villa El Libertador in
Córdoba city. [@1] The force carried out four raids and dismantled three alleged
drug sale points that also operated by delivery. [@1] The Second-Turn
anti-narcotics prosecutor supervised the raids and ordered the three detainees
transferred to judicial headquarters. [@1] The case proceeds as an alleged
breach of National Drug Law No. 23,737. [@1]

In the searches officers seized 92 doses of marijuana and one cannabis plant
alongside 173 doses of cocaine. [@1] Officers also seized 2,613,300 pesos in
cash and five digital scales plus materials allegedly used to portion drugs
before sale. [@1] Officers also found 46 cartridges of various calibres and a
metal firearm magazine. [@1]

The procedures formed part of a narcomenudeo investigation into small-scale drug
dealing. [@1] The investigation found the suspects also made home deliveries
rather than selling only from those premises. [@1] The sale points stood near a
kindergarten and a neighbourhood community centre, a detail investigators
highlighted. [@1] The prosecutor ordered the drugs, money, ammunition and other
seized items sent on for further proceedings. [@1]
