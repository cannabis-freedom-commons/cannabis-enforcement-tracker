Sohag centre detectives carried out intensive security campaigns in villages and
areas under the centre's jurisdiction. [@1] In Qalfaw and El-Omra, officers
detained two people found among crops with automatic rifles, live ammunition,
quantities of hashish and cash. [@1] In another move in the same area, officers
detained one person holding quantities of shabu and hashish, a firearm and cash.
[@1] In Wanina Gharbia, officers detained two people holding a large quantity of
drugs, a firearm and cash. [@1] In Edfa, officers detained one person holding 70
Tamol tablets, cash, a bladed weapon and a reported stolen motorcycle. [@1] In
El-Ghawanem, officers detained two people holding a firearm, live ammunition and
quantities of hashish. [@1]

The sweeps netted firearms, ammunition, drug quantities, tablets, cash and a
reported stolen motorcycle. [@1]

The moves came on the Sohag security director's orders to intensify security
presence and pursue suspects in arms possession and drug trafficking. [@1] The
head of the centre's investigations led the campaigns with investigation
officers. [@1] Officers completed the required legal procedures for those
detained in preparation for presenting them to the competent investigation
authorities. [@1]
