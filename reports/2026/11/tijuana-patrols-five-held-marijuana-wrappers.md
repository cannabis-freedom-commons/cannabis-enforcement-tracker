Officers of Tijuana's municipal police detained five people on suspicion of
crimes against health during preventive patrols in different parts of the city.
[@1] The detentions took place on 10 November 2026 in five neighbourhoods and
housing estates of the city. [@2] Wilber “N”, 27, was held in the Hacienda Las
Delicias III housing estate for an administrative offence, with 21 wrappers of a
substance resembling marijuana and 13 resembling cristal. [@1]

Cristian Adrián “N”, 27, was stopped in Terrazas del Valle after breaching the
municipal police and government code, with 31 wrappers of green, dry herb
resembling marijuana. [@1] Lauriano “N”, 56, stopped in colonia Del Río for an
administrative offence, had 20 marijuana-like wrappers. [@1] Omar “N”, 32,
stopped in Delicias third section for an administrative infraction, had another
20 marijuana-like wrappers. [@1] The four marijuana cases accounted for 92 of
the 147 wrappers reported by the municipal police. [@1] Emiliano “N”, 58, was
detained in colonia Mariano Matamoros with 42 wrappers of a cristal-like
substance. [@1] The other 13 cristal-like wrappers were found on Wilber “N”, who
also carried the marijuana-like wrappers. [@1]

The five were handed to the state prosecutor's office (FGE), which will follow
the investigation and decide their legal situation. [@1] The municipal
secretariat urged residents to report drug sales anonymously on 089 and to use
the emergency button for police help. [@1]
