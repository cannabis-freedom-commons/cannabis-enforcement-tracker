Zaragoza’s Audiencia Provincial, the provincial court, sentenced Kamel Ait Aissi
to four and a half years in prison for drug trafficking and acquitted the three
other accused men. [@1] The four had been detained in 2023 after a National
Police investigation found they formed a group distributing drugs in Zaragoza
obtained beforehand in Catalonia. [@1] At the June 2026 trial, three of the
accused denied any role in selling or distributing drugs, while K. A. A.
exercised his right not to testify. [@2] The hearing tried four men accused of
belonging to a criminal group trafficking drugs between Catalonia and Aragón.
[@2]

Searches in the case recovered about three kilos of hashish, the cannabis
component recorded here, along with cocaine, heroin, preparation tools,
precision scales and cash. [@2] Officers found more than 16,000 euros hidden in
a wardrobe in one searched home. [@2] Investigators also found a butane cylinder
in a warehouse in Pastriz fitted with a sophisticated hidden opening mechanism
in its base. [@2] The cylinder was empty when seized, but investigators said it
could have been used repeatedly to transport and conceal drugs. [@2]

Prosecutors said the network brought cocaine, heroin and hashish from Barcelona
for distribution in Zaragoza and surrounding municipalities. [@2] Police said
the investigation began in April 2023 and led to several entries and searches in
December that year. [@2] The case valuation put all the seized substances
together at more than 52,000 euros on the illicit market, so no cannabis-only
value is recorded. [@2]
