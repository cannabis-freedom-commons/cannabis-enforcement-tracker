Tochigi Prefectural Police re-arrested Pham Dinh Ngoc, a 33-year-old unemployed
Vietnamese resident of Ashikaga, on 25 September 2026 on suspicion of cultivating
marijuana for profit. [@1,3] Investigators said he grew 207 cannabis plants at his home
in Yamakawacho. [@1,3]

Officers first searched the house in July 2026 during another investigation and seized
about 2,900 grams of dried marijuana, which police valued at roughly 14.5 million yen.
[@1] Ngoc had already been arrested for possession with intent to profit and was later
held again for overstaying his visa. [@1]

Ngoc admitted tending the plants but told investigators he did not know they were
marijuana and denied seeking profit. [@1,2] Police maintained he was running a large
cultivation setup for illicit sale and continued to investigate. [@1] JP-NewsFocus noted
television reports that Gunma Prefectural Police carried out the July search in Ashikaga
during a separate case. [@2]

The Maebashi District Public Prosecutors Office indicted the man at the Maebashi District Court by 7 November 2026 on charges of cultivating and possessing cannabis for profit. [@3] The indictment says he worked with others to grow the plants at his Ashikaga home from about 16 March to 1 July 2026. [@3] He is also accused of staying in Japan illegally after arriving in January 2024 on a technical intern trainee status and having his later residence status revoked. [@3]
