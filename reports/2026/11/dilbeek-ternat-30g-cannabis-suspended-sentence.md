A young man from Dilbeek was convicted of cannabis possession and given six
months in prison, suspended. [@1] He was convicted in his absence after failing
to appear in court. [@1]

Police stopped the man near Ternat because he was driving too fast. [@1] As soon
as he opened his car window, the officers were met by a strong smell of
cannabis. [@1] Officers then found a bag containing 30 grams of cannabis in his
car. [@1]

The subsequent investigation showed that the man was not dealing. [@1] He was
therefore offered a 400-euro out-of-court settlement for drug possession. [@1]
He did not pay that settlement, after which he was summoned to court. [@1] The
court then imposed the six-month suspended prison sentence in absentia. [@1]
