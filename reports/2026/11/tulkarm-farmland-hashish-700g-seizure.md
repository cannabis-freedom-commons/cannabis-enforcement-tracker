Palestinian police said they seized drugs hidden in farmland in Tulkarm
Governorate that were meant to be promoted and sold. [@1] Police said the
operation followed information to the anti-narcotics branch that people had
hidden drugs in farmland ahead of distributing and selling them in Tulkarm city.
[@1] After tracking the information and monitoring the suspects' movements, an
anti-narcotics force moved to the site and seized seven slabs of hashish
weighing 700 grams in total, plus 399 ecstasy pills. [@1]

The police said the seizure was part of its efforts to combat drugs and limit
their promotion and circulation. [@1] The Tulkarm governorate anti-narcotics
branch made the seizure on Thursday 5 November 2026, according to a police
statement. [@2] The police statement said the 700 grams of hashish were in seven
slabs, alongside 399 ecstasy pills. [@2]
