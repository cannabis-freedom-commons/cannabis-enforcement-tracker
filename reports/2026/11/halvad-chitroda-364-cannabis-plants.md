Morbi's Anti-Narcotics Wing raided a field on the outskirts of Chitroda village
in Halvad taluka after a tip-off about large-scale illegal cannabis cultivation.
[@1] Officers found 364 small and large cannabis plants grown illegally among a
castor crop in a field held by accused Vipulbhai alias Roko Dhirubhai Paraliya.
[@1] Police arrested one man, 30-year-old Vipulbhai alias Roko Dhirubhai
Paraliya, who lives at Hanifbhai's farm in Chitroda, Halvad, at the scene. [@2]
The accused admitted he had sharecropped Naimbhai Hanifbhai Kagthara's farm for
ten years and had grown cannabis for about four months to sell to local users.
[@1]

Forensic officers attended the raid and the uprooted plants with roots were
weighed at 33 kg 50 g. [@1] Police estimated the cannabis value at Rs 1652500.
[@1] Including a mobile phone, officers seized total property worth Rs 1657500.
[@1] The second outlet confirms the wing raided the field on the previous
evening. [@2]

The wing combed the field for hours during the operation. [@1] Police registered
a case at Halvad police station under Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances
Act sections 8(c), 20(a) and 20(b)(2-C) and formally detained the accused. [@1]
Police are still checking whether the accused previously grew cannabis. [@2]
