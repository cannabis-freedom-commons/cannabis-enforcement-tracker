Police in the Mandav police station area of Dhar district uncovered illegal
ganja cultivation in Ramgarh village on Sunday. [@1] A 66-year-old accused
person was arrested. [@1]

Officers seized 315 green ganja plants that had been planted in the field among
cotton and tuar crops. [@1] Police put the total weight of the seized plants at
265 kilograms. [@1] The seized plants were valued at 39.75 lakh rupees. [@1]
