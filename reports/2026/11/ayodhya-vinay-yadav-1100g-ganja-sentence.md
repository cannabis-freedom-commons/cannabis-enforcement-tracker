A court in Ayodhya sentenced Vinay Yadav, caught with 1,100 grams of ganja, to
three months of rigorous imprisonment. [@1] The court also imposed a fine of
8,000 rupees, with 20 days of additional imprisonment if the fine is not paid.
[@1] Special NDPS Judge Pradeep Kumar Singh delivered the verdict. [@1]

According to the prosecution, the incident dates to 17 August, when
Sub-Inspector Praveen Mishra arrested Vinay Yadav, a resident of Ahiran Purva in
Jaidpur police station area of Barabanki district, near a kiosk by the Saket
bypass. [@1] A search recovered 1,100 grams of ganja kept in black polythene and
200 rupees from his possession, and a report was registered at Ayodhya Kotwali.
[@1]

Police filed a charge sheet in court after investigation. [@1] During the
hearing, the accused admitted his guilt and submitted a plea stating that he had
spent two months and 25 days in jail. [@1] The plea stated that he is the sole
earner of his household and is economically weak. [@1] The court then sentenced
him to three months of rigorous imprisonment and the 8,000-rupee fine. [@1]
