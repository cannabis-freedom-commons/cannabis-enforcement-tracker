Fuerza Civil officers, in coordination with Defensa personnel, detained
36-year-old Oscar “N” in Colonia Valle del Norte in Salinas Victoria. [@1] The
arrest took place at the crossing of Paseo de Castilla and Punta Castilla
avenues during an operation based on intelligence work and investigation into
the suspect's activities. [@1]

Officers spotted the man storing a bag in the trunk of a Ford Fusion SE and
stopped him for inspection after finding that he matched the person under
investigation. [@1] Officers seized around 15 kilograms of a substance with the
characteristics of marijuana, distributed in bags and plastic-wrapped packages.
[@2] The marijuana was found inside the vehicle after officers inspected the
car. [@3]

After the seizure, he was placed at the disposal of the Federal Public
Prosecutor's Office, which will determine his legal situation. [@1]
