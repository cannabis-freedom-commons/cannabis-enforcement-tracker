Drug officers in Mytilene arrested a 39-year-old Greek man on Saturday 7 November 2026 on suspicion of
possessing and trafficking narcotic substances and of breaking the weapons laws. [@1,2,3,4,5,7]

Officers stopped him as he drove a light truck on the Mytilene–Kalloni road and found a nylon package
holding about 1,035 grams of unprocessed cannabis, called hashish in the official statement, in a space
inside the vehicle. [@1,3,4,5] A search of his home in the wider Mytilene area, in the presence of a
judicial officer, found seven more nylon packages of cannabis weighing 7,493 grams in all and ten packages
with cannabis residues. [@1,3,4,5] LesvosPost placed that search at Halikas, a district of Mytilene. [@2] In
a second house in the wider Plomari area, officers seized about 4 grams of cannabis and a precision
electronic scale. [@1,3,4,5,6,7]

Officers also seized a knife and a mobile phone he had with him, and the light truck as the means used in
the offences. [@1,2,3,4,5] Police said their investigation indicated he held the drugs to supply them to
others for money, and a criminal case file was opened against him. [@1,3,4] The investigation was carried
out by the Drug Prosecution Department of the Mytilene Subdivision for Prosecution and Clearance of
Crimes. [@1,3,4,5]
