Tripura Police, the Border Security Force and Tripura State Rifles jointly
destroyed illegal cannabis plants spread across nearly 650 plantations in the
Boxanagar area of Sepahijala district over the past two days. [@1] The destroyed
crop numbered nearly 19 lakh cannabis plants. [@2]

Police sources said the operation covered forest areas, remote locations and
sensitive stretches along the Bangladesh border. [@2] The plants, allegedly
grown for illegal trafficking and cross-border smuggling, were uprooted and
destroyed. [@2] The crackdown followed recent attacks on BSF personnel in the
Kalamchoura-Boxanagar area, which raised concerns about border security and
organised illegal activity. [@2]

The scale of the find raised the question of how about 650 plantations could
have been cultivated without earlier effective action by the authorities. [@1]
Allegations of political protection and local influence behind the illegal
cultivation were also reported. [@1] The authorities now face the task of
identifying the cultivators, financiers and trafficking networks behind the
illegal trade. [@2]
