Como provincial Guardia di Finanza officers intensified patrols against dealing
in the Lario Intelvese area and identified five young people, one of whom was
reported for drug-law violations. [@1] Menaggio officers followed a speeding car
that turned into a side road until it stopped. [@2] One of the five was already
known to the officers for previous drug-law violations. [@1] When the officers
approached, that youth threw two small plastic packets to the ground, which the
officers saw and recovered. [@1] After the checks, the youth was reported to the
judicial authority. [@1] The report was for possession of drugs for dealing
purposes. [@3]

The two packets held 21.2 grams of hashish and 0.6 grams of cocaine, identified
by a rapid test carried out on the spot. [@2] All the drugs found were seized.
[@1]

The check was part of the Como Guardia di Finanza’s territorial economic-control
activity against dealing and retail drug sales. [@3] The reports stress that the
suspect remains presumed innocent unless a final conviction establishes
responsibility. [@3]
