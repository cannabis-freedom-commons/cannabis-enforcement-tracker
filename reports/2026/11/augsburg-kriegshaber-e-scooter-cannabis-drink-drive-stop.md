Police in the Kriegshaber district of Augsburg stopped a 27-year-old man riding
an e-scooter on the street Bürgermeister-Ackermann-Straße at about 01:20 on
Wednesday 11 November 2026. [@1] During the check officers noticed a smell of
alcohol and signs of impairment, and a breath alcohol test gave a reading of
about 1.4 per mille. [@2] The man admitted to officers that he had consumed
cannabis and that he did not hold a driving licence. [@2]

Police had a blood sample taken from the man and stopped him from continuing on
his way. [@2]

The 27-year-old holds German citizenship. [@1] Police are now investigating the
man for drink-driving as a result of alcohol and cannabis and for driving
without a licence. [@3]

The statement was issued by the Schwaben Nord police headquarters, the force
that covers Augsburg, and was carried by two local outlets. [@2]
