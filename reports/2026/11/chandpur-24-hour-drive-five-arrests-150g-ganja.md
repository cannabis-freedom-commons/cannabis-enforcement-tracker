Chandpur district police arrested five people in anti-drug operations and
recovered 150 grams of ganja and 22 pieces of yaba, a methamphetamine tablet.
[@2] The special operations ran across the district's police station areas on
Monday 9 November 2026 on the instruction of Police Superintendent Md Mizanur
Rahman to keep law and order normal and control crime. [@1] The drive covered a
24-hour period across different parts of the district. [@5] The figures were
released on the morning of Tuesday 10 November in a press statement sent by the
media cell of the superintendent of police's office. [@1]

In the anti-drug drive, Matlab Uttar police station arrested two people and
seized 150 grams of ganja and seven pieces of yaba. [@1] Additional
Superintendent of Police (Administration and Finance) Nadira Nur said legal
action had been taken against those arrested and that such drives to curb crime
would continue. [@2]

In the wider operation, police arrested 28 people in total, comprising 16
leaders and activists of the banned Awami League and its affiliate
organisations, six warrant and convicted suspects, one extortion suspect and
five people held in drug-related offences. [@1] Chandpur Sadar Model police
arrested the extortion suspect and separately detained eight more people in the
same station area as drug users and suspects. [@3] Officers also detained 19
alleged teenage gang members across Matlab Uttar, Faridganj, Chandpur Sadar
Model, Hajiganj and Haimchar stations and later handed them to their guardians
after verification on bond not to reoffend. [@4]
