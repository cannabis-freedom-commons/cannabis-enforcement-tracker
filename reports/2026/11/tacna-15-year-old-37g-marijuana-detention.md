Officers of the Grupo Terna of the National Police detained a 15-year-old
schoolboy during a night patrol on block six of the prolongation of Avenida
Vigil, in the cercado of Tacna. [@1] The schoolboy, identified only as J. F. G.
T., had shown a suspicious attitude on noticing the police presence. [@1]

A personal search found a plastic bag with stems and dry leaves which, after a
field test and weighing, amounted to 37 grams of marijuana. [@1] Officers also
found among his belongings a high-end black Apple iPhone 13 cell phone. [@1] The
case was referred to provincial family prosecutor Víctor Nina, who ordered the
adolescent and the seized items transferred to the National Police Antidrug Area
for the legal proceedings. [@1]

The outlet describes this as the third intervention of minors with drugs
recorded between October and November of this year. [@1] In total, five
schoolchildren were involved in cases of unlawful substance possession, with two
detained at Francisco Antonio de Zela school, two at Modesto Basadre school and
the latest case detected on a public street. [@1]
