Peruvian National Police officers detained two people, aged 23 and 74, in an
operation in the Santa Isabel neighbourhood of Chachapoyas, Amazonas, on 4
November 2026. [@1] Police and prosecutors are investigating the two for the
illegal practice of dentistry and for possible illicit drug trafficking. [@1]
The raid was carried out by National Police officers with the Provincial
Prosecutor's Office for Crime Prevention (Fiscalía de Prevención del Delito) and
representatives of the Amazonas dental college. [@1]

According to the police report, officers found dental equipment, resins,
anaesthetics and other supplies during the raid. [@1] Officers also found mobile
phones, cash and a sealed bag containing seeds that, by their characteristics,
were thought to be cannabis sativa, marijuana. [@1] The two were taken to the
criminal investigation division of the National Police in Chachapoyas (DIVINCRI)
to continue the proceedings. [@1]

Authorities said they still had to determine where the seized materials came
from, check the two people's professional status and clarify the circumstances
of the seeds. [@1] The case is at the investigation stage, and the article notes
the presumption of innocence of those involved. [@1]
