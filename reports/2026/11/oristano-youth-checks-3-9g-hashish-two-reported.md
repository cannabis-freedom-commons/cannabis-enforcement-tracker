Oristano police identified 129 people, including 88 minors, during an
extraordinary high-impact control operation carried out in recent days. [@1]
Officers additionally stopped 41 adults for checks and inspected 11 vehicles.
[@1] Officers carried out one home search and two personal searches, with one
personal search of a minor uncovering hashish. [@1] Police pursued two
administrative drug violations, one involving a minor, with accompanying drug
seizures. [@1]

Supported by the Questura dog unit and the Sardinia Crime Prevention Department,
officers found and seized 3 grams of hashish in the outside area of the Don
Deodato Meloni high school. [@1] Police put the total drugs recovered during the
checks at 3.9 grams. [@1] The operation was coordinated by the Mobile Squad of
the Oristano Questura and covered gathering places, squares and central areas,
with attention to places frequented by young people and schools. [@1]

The checks formed part of a national plan to prevent youth crime, intended to
prevent illegality and protect minors, especially in school settings. [@1] The
Questura said people reported in such cases may face driving-licence
consequences, with possible temporary withdrawal or suspension for an adult and
a ban on obtaining a licence until age 21 for a minor. [@1] Police said
monitoring would continue in the coming weeks with constant oversight of the
area. [@1]
