On the night of 11 November at 9:25 pm, a temporary checkpost on the
Dhaka-Chattogram highway in front of the Sultanatun filling station in Old
Muhuriganj, Chhagalnaiya, caught a 28-year-old Rohingya woman, Hamida Begum,
with 8 kg of ganja. [@1] SRBI-7, the Chattogram wing of the Border Security
Force, made the arrest on a tip. [@1]

She is the daughter of Kamal Mia, from Kutupalong in Ukhia sub-district of Cox's
Bazar. [@1] SRBI-7 said the woman admitted to collecting ganja from the Feni
border area and selling it to users and dealers in Cox's Bazar. [@1]

At the checkpost, a woman carrying a bag tried to flee from a CDAM Travels bus
bound for Cox's Bazar and was caught; her black bag held 8 kg of ganja wrapped
in blue polythene and scotch tape. [@1] SRBI-7 valued the ganja at about 80,000
taka. [@1]

SRBI-7 handed over the woman and the drug to Chhagalnaiya Thana police for legal
action. [@1]
