Military police arrested a man suspected of drug trafficking in Itapoã, in the
Federal District, on Thursday 5 November 2026. [@1] The arrest was made by teams
of the Tactical Operations Group of the 20th Battalion, GTOP 40 of the Federal
District Military Police (PMDF). [@2]

The incident began during a patrol in Quadra 01 of Fazendinha, where residents
told officers that drugs were being sold and gave the physical features and
clothing of a man said to be involved. [@1] Officers found a man matching the
description riding a bicycle and, in the approach, seized four portions of a
marijuana-like substance from him. [@1]

The man then authorised officers, on video recording, to enter a property on QL
05 in Itapoã. [@1] In the house, which had few furnishings and appeared
unoccupied, officers found three bars, 13 medium portions and 13 small portions
of the same material, plus a precision scale, two knives with residues of the
substance and a mobile phone. [@1] Counting the street seizure and the house
search, police recorded three bars and 30 portions of material with marijuana
characteristics in total. [@1]

The man was placed under arrest and taken with all the seized material to the
6th Police Station for the legal procedures. [@2]
