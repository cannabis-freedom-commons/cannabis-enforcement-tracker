Polda Lampung, Lampung's regional police, uncovered five narcotics cases during
October 2026. [@1] Police detained 10 suspects in the operation. [@1] Provincial
police chief Brig. Gen. Yustan Alpiani presented the cases at a press conference
in South Lampung on 4 November 2026. [@1]

The cannabis component recorded here was 33.1 kilograms of ganja. [@1] The same
seizure also included 61.9 kilograms of sabu, 19,696 ecstasy pills and 2,143
etomidate pills, which are not counted as cannabis. [@1] Police put the total
economic value of all the seized narcotics at Rp125 billion. [@1] Police
estimated the seized narcotics could save about 429,683 people from drug abuse.
[@1] Yustan called narcotics crime an extraordinary crime because of its harm to
health, lives and the future of the community, especially the younger
generation. [@1]

Police said the cases revealed an inter-provincial distribution network using
Lampung as a delivery route. [@1] One network sent narcotics from Medan, North
Sumatra, to Bandung, West Java, via the Bakauheni crossing in South Lampung.
[@1] Police described Bakauheni as the main Sumatra-Java transport link under
watch for drug trafficking. [@1] Police said traffickers exploit high
inter-island vehicle and passenger movement to smuggle contraband. [@1]
