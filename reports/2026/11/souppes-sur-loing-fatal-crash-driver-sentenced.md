The criminal court in Fontainebleau sentenced the driver to five years in
prison, including two years to be served, for a fatal road crash at
Souppes-sur-Loing. [@1] The crash happened on 18 July 2021, when the driver,
then aged 21, took the wheel of his powerful Audi A3 for drives around his
sister's birthday. [@1] He had drunk several glasses of alcohol and smoked
cannabis before losing control of the vehicle. [@1] The car struck a pole and
the front-seat passenger, his 20-year-old childhood friend, was thrown seven
metres. [@1] The passenger died later that evening. [@1]

Investigators found the driver was travelling at 135 km/h in a zone limited to
30 km/h. [@1] The sentence carries provisional execution, meaning he goes to
prison even if he appeals. [@1]

Earlier reporting on the same case says the car was uninsured and the passenger,
who was not wearing a seatbelt, was ejected. [@2] That reporting says the
passenger died while being transferred to hospital. [@2] It also says the driver
was notified of a driving ban. [@2]
