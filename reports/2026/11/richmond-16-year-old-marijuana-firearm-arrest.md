A report of masked people allegedly damaging property near 9th and Main Streets
in Richmond led officers to five juveniles on Monday night, and a 16-year-old
boy among them was arrested after police said he had a firearm in his
possession. [@1] He faces preliminary misdemeanor charges of child in possession
of a firearm and possession of marijuana. [@1] The 16-year-old was transported
to a juvenile detention facility. [@1] The other four juveniles were taken to
their parents or guardians. [@3]

Richmond police said the response began at about 6:45 p.m. after a report of
suspicious activity involving multiple masked individuals. [@2] The initial
report said five or six people in ski masks were damaging property near Ninth
and Main streets. [@2] Responding officers found five juveniles in the 300 block
of the alley between South Seventh and South Eighth streets. [@2] Officers said
the 16-year-old boy had a firearm. [@2]

He faces a Class A misdemeanor charge of child in possession of a firearm. [@2]
He also faces a Class B misdemeanor charge of possession of marijuana. [@2] The
department said officers have reported more frequent encounters with armed
juveniles. [@2] It urged parents and guardians to know where their children are,
