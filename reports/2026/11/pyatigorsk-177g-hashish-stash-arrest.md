Police detained a 22-year-old Pyatigorsk resident on suspicion of attempted drug
sale. [@1] Drug-control officers checked his mobile phone and found coordinates
of stash points. [@1] Police said he planned to sell banned substances through a
messenger by placing stash drops across the resort city. [@1] The Pyatigorsk
Interior Ministry investigative department opened a criminal case on attempted
illegal sale of narcotics on a large scale. [@1] At the investigator's request,
a court remanded the suspect in custody. [@1]

The Stavropol Krai Interior Ministry directorate said more than 177 grams of
hashish was seized from him. [@1] At those locations officers recovered ten
packets prepared for distribution with marked weights. [@1] An expert
examination confirmed the substance was hashish totalling more than 177 grams.
[@1] Pobeda 26, citing the same regional police, said the phone held data on 10
drug stashes in the resort city. [@2]

The investigation was still under way. [@1] Pobeda 26 reported the offence could
carry 7 to 15 years in prison. [@2] Regional police said the man was detained
during operational-search measures. [@2]
