Carabinieri from the Anzio station, with the Radiomobile unit, arrested three
people and reported a fourth at liberty in two separate operations in Anzio
Colonia a day apart. [@2] In Largo Venezia a 20-year-old from the area, already
known to police, tried to avoid a stop by headbutting a carabiniere in the chest
and running off, and was caught. [@2] A search of the 20-year-old found a block
of hashish of about 100 grams, a precision scale and 50 euros in cash. [@2] In
the home of his father, a 60-year-old who resisted entry and threatened the
officers, police seized another 2 grams of hashish, a second scale and a knife
coated in drugs. [@2] The young man was arrested for possession of drugs for
sale, resistance and injuring a public officer, his father for resistance, and
both were taken to the Velletri prison. [@2]

The next day, in via Ardea, officers saw a 19-year-old taking drugs in a
ground-floor garden; he fled through a window and tried to hide a package by the
front wheel of a parked small car. [@2] In the room he shared with his
18-year-old partner, officers found a small amount of hashish, packaging
material, a scale and 305 euros in cash. [@2] A search of the car recovered 16
grams of cocaine and 9 grams of hashish hidden in the front wheel-arch
compartment. [@2] The 19-year-old was arrested and put under house arrest ahead
of a fast-track hearing, while his partner was reported at liberty for alleged
complicity in possession for sale. [@2]

A day after publication, the Carabinieri put the two operations together at
three arrests and one report, with hashish, cocaine, cash and packaging material
seized. [@1] The Carabinieri statement says the proceedings remain at the
preliminary-investigations stage. [@3] The suspects are identified only by age,
and the article says the 20-year-old's hashish was about 100 grams because
officers first thought he had 20. [@1]
