Police arrested four alleged drug peddlers during four separate operations in
different areas of Nowshera Virkan. [@1] Police identified the four men as
Muhammad Yaseen, Muhammad Imran, Muhammad Asghar and Rana Afzal Ali. [@1]

Officers seized a total of 5.66kg of charas, a hand-made cannabis resin, across
the four operations. [@1] Sub-Inspector Shafqat Abbas recovered 1,520 grams from
Muhammad Yaseen at Babar Chowk, while ASI Muhammad Nawaz seized 820 grams from
Muhammad Imran at Chand Colony Chowk. [@1] ASI Waheed Ahmad recovered 1,680
grams from Muhammad Asghar at Khand Peer Chowk, while Sub-Inspector Waheed Abbas
seized 1,640 grams from Rana Afzal Ali at Adda Jagowal. [@1]

Police said the crackdown was conducted on Tuesday under the supervision of DSP
Anser Farooq Mann and SHO Ziafat Bhath. [@1] Police registered separate cases
against all four suspects and launched further investigations. [@1]
