Transport police in the North Caucasus Federal District named a 53-year-old
Nevinnomyssk resident as a suspect in unlawful drug trafficking. [@1] Acting on
operational information, officers found a packet with a gray-green substance in
the man's home. [@1] An expert examination showed the substance was marijuana
weighing more than 40 grams. [@1]

The man had found the cannabis near the Barsuchki river. [@1] He dried the
drug-containing plant and kept it for his own use. [@1] A criminal case was
opened against him. [@1]

Wild cannabis in the region is routinely destroyed under the preventive
operation Mak. [@1] Residents can report growing sites to the Interior Ministry,
after which the plants are mowed and burned. [@1]
