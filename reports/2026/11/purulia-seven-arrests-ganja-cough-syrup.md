Purulia district police arrested seven people in separate raids against drug
traffickers across the district. [@1] Those arrested were named as Apurba Gope,
Subodh Kumar, Bablu, Alam Ansari, Laba Kumar, Sheikh Zakir alias Kota and
Priyanka Das. [@1] They were held in separate cases at the Hura, Arsha, Joypur,
Tamna, Purulia Sadar and Purulia Mufassal police station areas. [@1]

Raj said most of the banned cough syrup recovered in the raids came from the
Purulia Mufassal police station area. [@1] The superintendent said traffickers
of the banned syrup never carried more than small quantities at a time. [@1]

The seized cough syrup amounted to 152 litres in more than 1,200 bottles, the outlet reported. [@2]
