Sector-58 police arrested Lavkush, 26, near the gate of Chhota D Park in Sector-62, Noida, on 6 November 2026. [@1,2,3] Officers found 1.7 kilograms of ganja in his bag. [@1,2,3] Do Took Media reported that police acted on local intelligence during a district campaign against drug trafficking. [@2]

Police alleged that he brought cannabis from Delhi and supplied it to workers and residents around factories, companies and informal settlements in Noida. [@1] A senior police officer said a first information report had been registered and police were seeking information about others linked to the alleged trade. [@1]
