Police presented the sweep as a dawn operation against alleged drug dealers
across Antalya, with 62 detentions reported. [@1] Antalya Chief Public
Prosecutor's Office coordinated province-wide anti-drug work that identified 80
suspects and designated 68 addresses for a simultaneous operation. [@1] Officers
have so far caught and detained 62 of those suspects. [@1] Of the detained
suspects, 13 were processed on suspicion of trafficking drugs and 49 on
suspicion of using drugs. [@1] The operation was supported by an unmanned aerial
vehicle, and efforts were reported to be continuing with further anti-drug
operations to follow decisively. [@1]

Searches of the suspects' addresses seized 150 grams of esrar, the Turkish word
for cannabis. [@1] The same searches also seized 330 grams of cocaine, 154
narcotic pills and 2 grams of methamphetamine, which are excluded from the
cannabis figure. [@1] Officers also seized one unlicensed pistol, one precision
scale and drug-use equipment during the address searches. [@1] The simultaneous
raids struck 68 addresses across Antalya province as part of the fight against
drugs. [@1]
