West Kowloon Station Customs intercepted an inbound male traveller who entered
the customs control area without declaring anything and behaved unusually at the
checkpoint. [@1] The traveller kept playing with a mobile phone in his right
hand while fumbling in his pocket with his left hand as he passed through
customs. [@1]

Officers found two suspected cannabis e-cigarettes in his right trouser pocket
and one each in his left trouser pocket and his toiletry bag, four in total.
[@1] Customs recorded the four e-cigarettes as weighing 67 grams gross. [@1] A
specialist agency later confirmed that tetrahydrocannabinol (THC) was detected
in all four e-cigarettes. [@1]

The case was transferred to the customs anti-smuggling department for further
handling. [@1] Customs noted that Chinese law lists cannabis among narcotics and
that smuggling, selling, transporting or manufacturing drugs carries criminal
liability regardless of quantity. [@1]
