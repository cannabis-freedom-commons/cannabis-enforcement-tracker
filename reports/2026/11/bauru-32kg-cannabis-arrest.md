A man was arrested for drug trafficking on the night of Friday 6 November on Rua
Halim Aidar, block 4, in Vila Industrial, Bauru. [@1] Military police officers
found 32 kilos of maconha (cannabis) and portions of cocaine in a Chevrolet
Tracker during the stop. [@1]

The team was on tactical patrol when it noticed the driver start breaking his
own mobile phone as the patrol car approached. [@1] When questioned, he said the
phone held information related to drug trafficking and that he had broken it out
of fear of the stop. [@1]

Searching the vehicle, police found four portions of cocaine, R$3,248 in cash, a
precision scale and two backpacks holding about 32 kilos of maconha. [@1] The
man was arrested and taken to the police station, where he remained at the
disposal of the courts. [@1]
