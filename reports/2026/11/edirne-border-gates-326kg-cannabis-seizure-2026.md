Trade Ministry Customs Enforcement teams carried out four separate operations at
the Kapıkule, Hamzabeyli and İpsala border gates and Istanbul Airport. [@1] The
accounts say only that investigations are continuing before the Edirne,
Gaziosmanpaşa and İpsala chief public prosecutor's offices, without naming any
suspects. [@1] The reports call the cannabis seized in the three border
operations 'esrar'. [@1]

At Kapıkule, a detailed search of a vehicle's fuel tank after an X-ray found
118.658 kg of cocaine in 109 packages and 25.744 kg of esrar in 32 packages.
[@1] At Hamzabeyli, detector dogs Yappa and Atlas reacted to a truck's fuel
tank, where officers found 81.134 kg of esrar in 85 packages. [@2] At İpsala,
controls with detector dog İda after suspicious density on the X-ray led to a
detailed search that found 219.063 kg of esrar in 200 packages. [@1] At Istanbul
Airport, controls of the luggage of three incoming passengers found a total of
15 kg of heroin. [@2] The ministry put all drugs seized in the four operations
at 459.599 kg, with the cannabis component stated as 325.941 kg of esrar. [@2]

Officers selected the vehicles through risk-analysis and targeting work and
acted on suspicious density seen in fuel-tank X-rays. [@1] The ministry said its
fight against smuggling continues around the clock on land, at sea and in the
air with technology, risk analysis and specialist staff. [@1]
