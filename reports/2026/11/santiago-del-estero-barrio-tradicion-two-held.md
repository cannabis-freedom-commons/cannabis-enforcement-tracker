Federal police detained two men in an afternoon operation against retail drug
dealing in the Tradición neighbourhood of Santiago del Estero city. [@1,2,3]
Officers from the Federal Operational Unit of Santiago del Estero carried out
the operation in that part of the city during the afternoon. [@1,2,3] The two
detainees and everything seized were placed at the disposal of the judicial
authorities. [@1,2,3]

Officers seized 60 packets of cocaine and 20 packets of marijuana, which
investigators said appeared ready for sale. [@1,2,3] Officers also seized cash that
police described as the suspected proceeds of prohibited-substance sales. [@1,2,3]

The case is led by prosecutor Natalia Saavedra, with court measures under the
control-and-guarantees judge Dr. Amestegui. [@1,3] The Federal Police said the
operation followed the guidelines of the national Security Ministry as part of
its actions against drug retailing, known locally as narcomenudeo. [@1,3]

The procedure took place at Avenida Colón y Japón. [@3] The 60 cocaine packets were described as bagullos. [@3]
