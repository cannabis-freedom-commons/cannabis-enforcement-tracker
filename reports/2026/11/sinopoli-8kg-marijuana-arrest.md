State Police arrested a man in Sinopoli on suspicion of possessing drugs for the
purpose of dealing. [@1] Officers arrested him in the act during a home search
and then detained him in Palmi prison. [@1] The operation involved officers from
the Palmi police station, supported by Crime Prevention Department crews and two
canine units specialised in searching for weapons, explosives and drugs. [@1]

During the home search in Sinopoli, police found 8 kg and 120 g of marijuana
inside a dwelling available to the man. [@1] Part of the marijuana was kept in a
large plastic sack and part was spread on a sheet on the floor and left to dry
ahead of distribution. [@1]

The seizure formed part of an extraordinary territorial-control operation
covering two pre-Aspromonte municipalities in the Metropolitan City of Reggio
Calabria. [@1] During that operation, police set up 10 checkpoints, identified
205 people and checked 144 vehicles, while also carrying out several searches
and recording four road-traffic violations. [@1]
