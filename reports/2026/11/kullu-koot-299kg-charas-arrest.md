Police in Ani sub-division in Kullu district raided a house and arrested one
woman after recovering a large consignment of charas. [@1,2] The arrested woman
was identified as Jovandasi, wife of Gopal Singh and resident of Koot village,
Ani. [@1,2] The Ani sub-divisional detection cell acted after receiving
intelligence that charas was stored in a house in Koot village under Ani police
station. [@1]

Police said they recovered 2.990 kg of charas from the woman in the house,
seized the drug and took her into custody. [@1,2] The headline describes the haul
as charas worth lakhs of rupees, but the report gives no exact value. [@1] A
case against the woman was registered at Ani police station under Section 20 of
the NDPS Act, India's narcotics law. [@1,2]

Police are questioning her to establish where the charas came from, why it was
kept in the house and to whom it was to be supplied. [@1] Investigators are
examining whether the case links to a larger trafficking network. [@1] Kullu
district police said they act on secret information against trafficking and
illegal sale and try to trace people involved in supply and distribution. [@1]
