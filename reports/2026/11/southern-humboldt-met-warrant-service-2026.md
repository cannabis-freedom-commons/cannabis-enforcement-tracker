Humboldt County Sheriff's Office public information officer Summer Hansen
confirmed that the office's Marijuana Eradication Team was in the Southern
Humboldt area on 12 November 2026 serving warrants on suspected illicit cannabis
cultivation sites. [@1] Hansen said the sheriff's office would release
additional details. [@1]

On the morning of 12 November 2026, readers reported multiple sheriff's vehicles
travelling south on Highway 101 through Southern Humboldt near the Eel River
confluence by Humboldt Redwoods State Park from about 7:40 a.m. [@1] Additional
social-media reports placed at least part of the convoy at the southbound Salmon
Creek exit just north of Miranda shortly after 8 a.m. [@1] The convoy was
reported to include a wood chipper. [@1] Before the sheriff's update, the
purpose and destination of the convoy had not been confirmed, though the
reported chipper suggested a cultivation site where such equipment has been used
to destroy seized plants. [@1]

The sheriff's office has maintained that illegal cannabis cultivation can
involve environmental damage, unsafe working conditions and labour exploitation.
[@1] Cannabis enforcement remains contentious in Southern Humboldt, where some
residents have questioned using law-enforcement resources on marijuana
cultivation and argued deputies should instead prioritise fentanyl,
methamphetamine and other serious crimes. [@1]
