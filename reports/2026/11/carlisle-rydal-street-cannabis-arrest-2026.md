A 60-year-old man from Carlisle was arrested on suspicion of possessing cannabis
with intent to supply. [@1] The arrest came during simultaneous search warrants
at two houses on Rydal Street and one on Sybil Street in Carlisle on 6 November
2026. [@2] At one Rydal Street address officers recovered cannabis and more than
£2,000 in cash. [@1] The 60-year-old was released under investigation while
enquiries continued. [@3] At the second Rydal Street address, officers found a
small quantity of cannabis and cocaine. [@1] Two men aged 31 and 32, both from
Carlisle, were given police cautions. [@3]

More than 20 specialist officers from departments across Cumbria took part. [@2]
A chainsaw was used to force entry to two of the homes. [@1] The operation
focused on the St Aidan's Triangle, the name police use for the area near
Melbourne Park where they say drug dealing, anti-social behaviour and
acquisitive crime have caused problems for the community. [@2] Detective
Inspector Lee Brumpton, who led the operation, said drugs were 'a blight on our
communities'. [@3] He said residents in Rydal Street thanked officers as they
carried out the operation. [@1] Brumpton said officers had been targeting Class
A supply. [@2] The action formed part of the latest drugs action funded by
Cumbria's police, fire and crime commissioner, David Allen. [@1] At the Sybil
Street address, officers seized heroin with a street value of £2,250 alongside a
quantity of cash. [@1]
