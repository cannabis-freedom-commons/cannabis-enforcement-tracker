The District Court in Domažlice sent 43-year-old Miroslav H. to prison for four
years for distributing pervitin and marijuana in Domažlice and the surrounding
area, where so-called mushrooms and ecstasy were also found on him. [@1]
Miroslav H. had been convicted four times before and had served prison terms
including for drug distribution. [@1] From March to August 2025, while
conditionally released from prison, he sold drugs in Domažlice and elsewhere in
the district, mainly pervitin, the Czech name for methamphetamine, and sometimes
marijuana. [@1] Eleven buyers bought drugs from him under the verdict, in some
cases up to twenty times and in other cases only a few times, sometimes
receiving drugs as gifts or exchanging them for items such as a pump he never
received. [@1] A smaller stock of drugs was also found on him during a house
search. [@1] In pre-trial proceedings he confessed to everything and sought a
guilt-and-punishment agreement that was never concluded, but at trial he denied
guilt and refused to testify until the final hearing when he admitted selling
drugs to someone. [@1]

The court punished him with a four-year unconditional term to be served in a
guarded prison, and ordered forfeiture to the state of all drugs found on him
and CZK 27,000 found by police. [@1]

He appealed to the Regional Court in Plzeň, which annulled the lower court's
judgment and returned the case for retrial because an important witness who had
been abroad during the main hearings must be heard. [@1] His lawyer Vladimír
Volný said one ground of appeal was the failure to examine witness Petr K., and
the defence seeks a suspended sentence instead of prison. [@1] The defence aim
is for Miroslav H. to avoid lengthy imprisonment with a suspended sentence. [@1]
The earlier account of the first-instance verdict said the judgment was not
final and that the judge concluded at least one and possibly two witnesses had
clearly lied, leaving possible perjury proceedings open. [@2]
