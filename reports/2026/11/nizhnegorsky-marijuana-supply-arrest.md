In Crimea, police placed a 49-year-old resident of Nizhnegorsky district in
custody on suspicion of supplying a large batch of marijuana. [@1,2] According to
the republic's Interior Ministry, the man passed the drugs to an acquaintance.
[@1] Officers of the ministry's drug-control directorate identified him in the
course of further operational-search measures. [@1] The ministry said results of
a DNA examination of a previously seized batch of marijuana were among the
grounds for concluding he was involved. [@1,2]

During a search at the suspect's place of residence, police seized 1.3 million
roubles. [@1,2] A criminal case was opened against the detainee under Part 4 of
Article 228.1 of the Russian Criminal Code, which covers illegal production,
sale or forwarding of narcotic drugs. [@1,2] The provision carries a maximum
punishment of 20 years' imprisonment. [@1] Investigators are continuing
procedural actions with the suspect while establishing the circumstances of the
supply and checking for other possible participants in the drug trafficking.
[@1]
