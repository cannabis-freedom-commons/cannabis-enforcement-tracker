Penitentiary police at the Casa Circondariale di Siracusa, the provincial prison
known as Cavadonna, arrested a man and a woman in flagrante on the evening of 4
November 2026 after they were found beside the prison with a drone and hashish.
[@1] Officers also reported a third person at liberty, the only member of the
group not taken into custody. [@5] The 4 November date comes from an account
that states it outright; the other reports say only that the arrests happened
the previous evening and were published on 5 November. [@1]

The officers were on duty at the institute when its video-surveillance system
picked out a suspicious car in the streets right beside the prison. [@1]
Officers led by the prison’s unit commander, with deputy commissioners and
inspectors, intervened on their own initiative and stopped the car before the
load could be flown over the walls. [@1] A search of the car found about 1.4
kilograms of hashish, a cannabis resin product, and a drone ready for take-off,
and both were seized. [@1] The outlet that named the date gives the time of the
intervention as shortly after 9.20 pm. [@3]

The prison officers’ union Osapp, which made the operation public, tied it to a
recent case at the same prison in which another two people were arrested over 2
kg of drugs. [@1] Osapp’s provincial secretary, Giuseppe Argentino, said
repeated seizures suggested the Siracusa prison risked being treated as a drug
market. [@1] The union asked the Justice Ministry to install electronic
anti-drone shielding at the prison. [@2] No charges, custody decision or court
step was reported for the three people involved. [@4]
