Military Police arrested a man for suspected drug trafficking in the Martins
neighbourhood of Rio Verde, Goiás, in the early hours of Thursday 12 November
2026. [@1] The incident began at about 3 a.m., after a Military Police team
spotted the driver of a motorcycle during routine patrol. [@1]

According to the police record, the man tried to flee at high speed as soon as
he noticed the police vehicle approaching and, while escaping, made dangerous
manoeuvres and discarded a bag in the public road. [@1]

Police intercepted and searched the driver; during the body search they found
portions of a substance similar to maconha, and the team returned to where the
bag had been abandoned and found a larger quantity of the same substance. [@1]
In total, approximately 135 grams of material similar to maconha, including
divided portions, were seized, together with a precision scale; the police
record said the scale reinforced the suspicion that the drug was destined for
sale. [@1]

The man was detained and taken to the Civil Police station; the seized
substances, the precision scale and the motorcycle used during the escape
attempt were also brought to the police station. [@1] The case will be
investigated by the Civil Police, which is responsible for the applicable legal
procedures and the definition of the suitable measures. [@1]
