Court No. 2 on 3 November 2026 sentenced 29-year-old Aleksandr Nerush for
unlawful transport and storage of drugs with intent to sell. [@1,2,3] Officers
detained Nerush together with the punching bag containing the banned substances
before he could complete the plan. [@1,2,3] Nerush partly admitted guilt, saying he
had meant to keep the drugs for personal use and had no intent to distribute
them. [@1,2,3]

The court found that in August 2026 Nerush contacted an unidentified person
online and offered his services as a courier who leaves stashes. [@1,4] After
receiving instructions, he travelled to Almaty to buy drugs and deliver them to
Kostanay in exchange for a promised 500,000 tenge. [@1,4] On 9 August he bought
the drug in three plastic packets from a person not identified by the
investigation. [@1,4] On his handler's instructions he bought a punching bag at an
Almaty market, hid the marijuana packets inside it, transported it to Kostanay
and continued to store the drug there. [@1,4]

The prosecutor asked for eight years' imprisonment. [@1,4] The court convicted him
under part 3 of Article 297 of Kazakhstan's Criminal Code, which carries seven
to 12 years with confiscation of property. [@1,4] It treated his young child as
mitigation and found no aggravating circumstances. [@1,2,4] It imposed seven years
in a medium-security penal institution. [@1,2,4] The verdict had not entered into
force. [@1,2,4]

The charge described one kilogram of marijuana for sale in an especially large amount by a group acting in prior conspiracy. [@2,3]

Police said an expert examination found the substance in the punching bag to be marijuana with a total weight of 1,130 grams, and that a criminal case was opened. [@5] Nga.kz reports that officers found the punching bag with 1,130 grams of marijuana in early September and that the Kostanay Regional Court's press service gave the details of the case heard in Court No. 2.
