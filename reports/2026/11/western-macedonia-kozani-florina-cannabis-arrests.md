Police in Western Macedonia arrested two people on cannabis charges in checks
over two days in early November 2026. [@2] In Kozani, officers of the DIAS
motorcycle policing team of the Kozani police station arrested a 22-year-old
Greek man in the morning of 7 November 2026 and seized 1.3 grams of unprocessed
cannabis from him. [@1] In Florina, DIAS officers arrested a 27-year-old Greek
man in the early hours of 6 November 2026 after finding an improvised cannabis
cigarette mixed with tobacco weighing 0.7 grams in his possession. [@1]

The two cannabis cases were among five arrests in Kozani, Florina, Ptolemaida
and Kastoria over 48 hours, of four Greek nationals and one foreign national.
[@2] A third man, 37, was arrested in the Florina area on 7 November and 33
narcotic tablets were seized from him; no cannabis was reported in his case.
[@2]

Case files were drawn up against the arrested men on charges of drug possession
and, as the case might be, of violations of the road traffic code (KOK), and
were to be sent to the competent prosecutors. [@2] The General Regional Police
Directorate of Western Macedonia said the targeted checks would continue across
the region to combat drugs and cut road accidents. [@1]
