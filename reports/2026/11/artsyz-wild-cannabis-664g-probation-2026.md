The Artsyz district court in Odesa Oblast found a local man guilty of illegally
making and storing narcotic drugs without intent to sell. [@1] The court
sentenced him to two years of probationary supervision. [@1]

Investigators found that in March 2026 he gathered the tops of wild cannabis
plants in the yard of an abandoned house. [@1] He took the plants to his own
home, where he dried and crushed them for his own use. [@1] During searches,
officers seized a paper packet of cannabis from the kitchen of the house. [@1]
The seized material weighed 6.64 grams in dried form, which the report says
exceeds the amount allowed by law. [@1]

At the hearing he fully admitted his guilt and expressed sincere remorse. [@1]
The court lifted the seizure of his property and ordered the physical evidence
destroyed. [@1] It also ordered him to pay the state 3,565.60 hryvnias for the
expert examinations. [@1]
