The federal police published the results of a multidisciplinary check operation
held on Wednesday 4 November at Tournai railway station and its surroundings.
[@1] The operation brought together the railway police, the dog-handling unit,
Securail, the Tournaisis local police zone and the Walloon public transport
operator TEC. [@1] Officers checked passengers in the transport areas, both
inside the station and around it. [@1] Police also ran 27 consultations of the
general national database, the BNG. [@2]

The operation led to one judicial arrest and seven immediate-settlement
proposals, offers to pay a sum instead of facing prosecution, totalling 725
euros. [@1,3] Officers also drew up several official reports, including for drug
offences and for carrying a prohibited weapon. [@1] The services seized 122.41
grams of cannabis, five ecstasy pills and an incapacitant aerosol. [@1,3]

The federal police presented the operation as the first joint action by the
services working at the site, intended to strengthen coordination between them.
[@2]
