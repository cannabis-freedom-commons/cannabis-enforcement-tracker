Samarinda police narcotics unit arrested five suspects in a series of raids on 5
and 6 November 2026 at multiple locations in Samarinda, East Kalimantan. [@1,2,3,4,5]
Police seized 20 kilograms of cannabis (ganja), 19.99 grams of ecstasy, 15
packets of synthetic tobacco and 20 pills of etomidate from the five suspects.
[@1,2,3,5] The chili powder was added to fool officers and sniffer dogs. [@3,4] The network used a goods-delivery service, hiding narcotics in cat-food packaging dusted with chili powder. [@3,5] From FB officers seized 22.1 grams of cannabis and 15.37 grams of synthetic tobacco. [@3,5] At the second location officers seized 40 packs of cannabis totalling 19.192 kilograms disguised in cat-food packaging. [@3,5,6]

The operations began on Thursday 5 November 2026 at around 20:00 WITA with the
seizure of ecstasy, followed by the cannabis seizure the next day. [@1] The third raid detained FB alias E on Jalan MT Haryono in Samarinda the same evening at about 23.00 WITA. [@3] The second raid detained RP, MA and JF on Jalan Pangeran Suryanata in Samarinda on 6 November 2026 at about 21.00 WITA. [@3,6] From MH officers seized a car, cat-food packaging, 1,999 ecstasy pills, 20 etomidate cartridges and 253.75 grams net of cannabis. [@3,5] The first raid detained MH on Jalan Sungai Kapih in Samarinda on 6 November 2026 at about 20.00 WITA. [@3] A search of his house on Jalan KH Wahid Hasyim found four packs of ecstasy totalling 1,999 pills with a net weight of 934.50 grams. [@4] Hanif was using an online taxi when detained. [@4] The first suspect was identified as 25-year-old MHF alias Hanif, detained on Jalan Sungai Kapih. [@4]

All five suspects were brought to the Samapolresta audit hall for ongoing
interrogation and further network development. [@1] Investigators were still tracing assets and money flows and hunting fugitive controller AR alias RR. [@3,5] The cannabis was suspected to have been prepared as stock for circulation ahead of New Year 2027. [@6] The three suspects were in a four-member WhatsApp group including fugitive AR alias RR, and police found the parcel delivery receipt on a suspect's phone. [@6] Police put the total sale value at Rp1,858,430,000 and said the seizure could save about 6,000 people in Samarinda from drug abuse. [@3,5] Police traced the narcotics to northern Sumatra, sent under an anonymous sender name to a fictitious address pointing to a Samarinda pet shop. [@3,4,5,6] Samarinda police
spokesperson Ipda Arie Soeharyadi confirmed that the case would be formally
presented at a press conference led by the chief of police on 13 November 2026.
[@1] Kasat Resnarkoba Kompol Bangkit Dananjaya also attended the press conference. [@4] Kapolresta Samarinda Kombes Pol Eko Novan Prasetyo Puspito addressed the 13 November press conference in the Aula Rupatama of Polresta Samarinda. [@4] Police said the network was controlled by fugitive AR alias RR and had three operational legs in Samarinda. [@3,5,6] RP and MA were tasked with monitoring the parcel's arrival, ensuring security and finding a Maxim driver to carry it to JF, while JF acted as temporary storage before distribution on the controller's orders.
