On 3 November the judicial court in Cherbourg-en-Cotentin tried a 35-year-old
man in his absence for driving without insurance, while positive for cannabis
and without a technical inspection. [@1] The hearing took place at the Cherbourg
court on Tuesday 3 November for various road offences. [@1] He was in detention
at the time of the hearing. [@1] His criminal record contains five entries for
driving without a licence and without insurance and for drug consumption. [@1]

On 25 April 2026 in Cherbourg police saw a car with no front registration plate
and checked its driver. [@1] Officers found the vehicle was uninsured and its
technical inspection had not been done. [@1] He said he had consumed CBD the day
before, while his saliva test was positive for cannabis. [@1]

The outlet noted he had previously received firm prison terms and regularly
consumes cannabis. [@1] The court sentenced David Cadenat to five months of firm
prison, a 500-euro fine increased by 50 percent plus two 135-euro fines. [@1] It
also cancelled his driving licence for one month. [@1]
