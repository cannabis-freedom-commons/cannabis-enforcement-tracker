The Paraná Military Police arrested two men and seized about 5.2 kg of marijuana
in Curitiba on the night of 9 November 2026. [@1] The operation was carried out
by ROTAM, the Military Police's tactical mobile patrol unit, from the 23rd
Battalion, after officers approached two men on Rua Major França Gomes in the
Santa Quitéria neighbourhood. [@1] The PMPR described the action as an operation
against drug trafficking. [@1]

Officers found portions of marijuana with one of the suspects during the initial
search. [@2] A search of one suspect's house found about 5.1 kg of marijuana,
four precision scales, a mobile phone and a notebook with notes on the illegal
sale. [@2] Police also collected utensils used to divide the drug. [@2]

The two men were taken with the drugs and seized material to the Civil Police's
Central de Flagrantes for legal procedures. [@1]
