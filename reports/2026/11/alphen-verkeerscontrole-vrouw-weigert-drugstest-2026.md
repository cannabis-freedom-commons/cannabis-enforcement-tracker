Officers stopped a car with two occupants at a general traffic checkpoint on
Saturday at about 16:40. [@1] When the woman driver handed over her driving
licence, officers smelled a strong hemp odour in the vehicle. [@1] The male
passenger said he had used cannabis shortly before. [@1] At the officers'
request, he produced a joint and a small bag of hemp from his clothing. [@1]
Police said the woman driver had bloodshot eyes and also smelled of hemp. [@1]

Officers wanted to take a saliva test to check whether she was driving under the
influence of drugs, but the woman refused to cooperate. [@1] She maintained her
refusal after officers asked her again and warned her about the consequences of
refusing. [@1] She was then arrested on suspicion of driving under the influence
of drugs. [@1] She was taken to the police station in Alphen aan den Rijn and
brought before an assistant prosecutor. [@1]

At the station she again refused to cooperate, this time with a blood test. [@1]
During questioning she declined to answer the questions put to her. [@1] Police
drew up an official report for refusing the drug tests. [@1] Her driving licence
was seized immediately. [@1] The case will have a legal follow-up at a later
date. [@1]
