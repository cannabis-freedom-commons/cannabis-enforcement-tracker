Police seized 2,820.5 kilograms of marijuana hidden in a field in Udayapur, one
of the largest recent drug recoveries in the district, and launched an
investigation to identify those behind the consignment. [@1] Police said no
suspects were found at the site and authorities have yet to determine who stored
the marijuana in the field. [@1] No suspects have been identified or arrested so
far. [@1]

The marijuana was packed in 85 sacks found at Tilitle in Belaka Municipality-7
on Thursday morning after a special tip-off. [@1] A team from the Area Police
Office in Rampur recovered the consignment at around 10am during a search
operation, as stated by Superintendent of Police Rajan Chapagain. [@1]
Investigators suspect the consignment had been hidden in the field for
transportation or smuggling. [@1]

The seized marijuana was taken into custody and stored at the Area Police Office
in Rampur while the investigation continues. [@1] The recovery came only days
after police seized a truck carrying marijuana in the same area, amid ongoing
concerns about narcotics trafficking routes through the district. [@1]
