The Yamaguchi Shimbun brief headlined as a Hagi police arrest of a 24-year-old
man on suspicion of cannabis possession was published on 13 November 2026. [@1]
The arrested man is 24 years old, unemployed, and lives in Yokohama in Kanagawa
Prefecture. [@2] Officers made a regular arrest of the man for an alleged
violation of the Cannabis Control Act. [@2]

The police entry classes the suspicion as joint possession of cannabis for
profit, an allegation that the drug was held together with at least one other
person for gain. [@2] The alleged conduct occurred on 13 October 2024 in the
city of Yokohama, and the substance involved was dried cannabis. [@2]

The prefectural police blotter carries the arrest in its reporting window
running from the afternoon of 11 November to the afternoon of 12 November 2026.
