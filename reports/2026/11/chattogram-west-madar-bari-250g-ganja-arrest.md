Officers of the 9th Armed Police Battalion detained a 35-year-old man described
by police as a drug trader in an operation on the railway line in front of a
scrap dealer's shop at West Madar Bari, under the railway police station in
Chattogram. [@1] Police said they found 250 grams of ganja and 155 yaba tablets
on him, along with Tk 1,430 in cash that they described as proceeds from drug
sales. [@1]

Mohammad Samsul Islam Akondo, commander of the Armed Police Battalion, confirmed
the operation in a statement sent to the media on Thursday 5 November. [@1] The
operation was led by Assistant Superintendent of Police Tareq Ahmad, with
support from Superintendent Arefin Jewel and Additional Superintendent Ashraful
Islam of the 9th Armed Police Battalion, police said. [@1] Police filed a
drug-control case against him at the Chattogram railway police station. [@1]
