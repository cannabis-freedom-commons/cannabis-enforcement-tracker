Gardaí from the Galway County East Community Engagement Area led a multi-agency
day of action on Monday as part of Operation Intercept on busy routes in east
Galway between 8am and 4pm. [@1] The driver stopped on the N17 near Milltown
tested positive for cannabis and was arrested and processed for drug-impaired
driving. [@1] A second driver stopped on the N83 in Headford was also arrested
and processed for drug-impaired driving. [@1] Both drivers are to face
prosecution for illegal drug possession and drug-impaired driving. [@2]

A Garda-trained dog found cannabis with an approximate value of between €800 and
€1,000 in a car on the N17 near Milltown. [@1] Gardaí seized the car in the
townland of Gortnagunned after the dog discovered the concealed cannabis. [@1]
Officers seized four vehicles during the day, two for lack of insurance and two
for marked gas oil and VRT offences. [@2] Officers found 28 technical offences
on 29 heavy goods vehicles stopped at checkpoints. [@1] Immigration officers
issued five notices under the Immigration Act. [@1]

Local Gardaí were assisted by immigration officers, the Regional Garda Dog Unit,
divisional crime and roads-policing units, the Road Safety Authority and Customs
officials. [@1] Gardaí targeted major commuter arteries in east Galway between
8am and 4pm during the operation. [@2]
