The Anti-Narcotics Force arrested 15 suspected drug traffickers in 10 operations
in cities across Pakistan. [@1,2,3,4] An ANF spokesperson said the operations targeted
international trafficking networks, domestic distribution points and
inter-provincial smuggling routes. [@1] The agency put the total haul at 50.702
kilogrammes of narcotics, including heroin, hashish, opium, ice and cannabis.
[@1,2]

In DHA Phase II, Islamabad, officers recovered 500 grams of cannabis from a
suspect. [@1] Near Humrahi Bus Stop on GT Road in Rawalpindi, officers seized
2.4 kilogrammes of hashish from a vehicle and arrested the accused. [@1] In
Mianwali, officers recovered 18 kilogrammes of hashish and six kilogrammes of
opium from a suspect near the Railway Crossing on Kalabagh Road, the drugs
hidden in an iron box. [@1]

Near Burhan Interchange on the M-1 Motorway in Attock district, officers
recovered 3.6 kilogrammes of hashish from a vehicle and arrested two suspects.
[@1] Near Kala Shah Kaku Interchange on the M-2 Motorway in Sheikhupura,
officers recovered 4.8 kilogrammes of opium and 1.2 kilogrammes of hashish from
a vehicle and arrested two suspects. [@1] At Multan International Airport,
officers found 70 heroin-filled capsules weighing 402 grams on three passengers
travelling to Madinah for Umrah and took them into custody. [@1] At Bacha Khan
International Airport in Peshawar, officers recovered 700 grams of ice from the
baggage of a passenger travelling to Bahrain and arrested him. [@1] At a private
courier office on Airport Road in Karachi, officers recovered 700 grams of
heroin concealed in leather welding gloves from a parcel bound for the United
Kingdom. [@1]

Cases were registered against the arrested suspects under the Control of
Narcotic Substances Act, 1997, and further investigation was under way. [@1,2]
