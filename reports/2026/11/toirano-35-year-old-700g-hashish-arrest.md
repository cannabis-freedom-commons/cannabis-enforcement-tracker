On the evening of 12 November 2026, Carabinieri from the Borghetto Santo Spirito
station arrested a 35-year-old resident of the Savona hinterland in flagrante
for alleged possession of drugs for dealing. [@1] The Carabinieri focused their
attention on the square in front of the elementary school in Piazza Corrado,
Toirano. [@2] There, at about 17:25, they noticed two men in a parked car who
showed evident nervousness at the sight of the officers, prompting a closer
check. [@2] The officers reconstructed a suspected drug handover said to have
just occurred: a 36-year-old from Savona spontaneously handed over about 0.64
grams of cocaine, saying he had just bought it from the 35-year-old for 50
euros. [@2] A personal search of the suspected dealer found 775 euros in cash in
small-denomination notes, considered proceeds of the illegal activity. [@2] On
the order of the Public Prosecutor's Office of the Court of Savona, the
35-year-old was placed under house arrest pending a validation hearing scheduled
for the same day. [@2] The buyer was instead reported to the local Prefecture
for personal drug use. [@2]

The searches were then extended to the 35-year-old's home and the condominium
landing, where the Carabinieri found a sports bag hidden under a small cabinet.
[@2] Inside the bag and the apartment, officers seized about 75 grams of
cocaine, over 105 grams of hashish, 6,700 euros in cash kept in a cardboard box,
three digital precision scales with drug residue, a knife with hashish traces
and a telescopic baton. [@2] A search of a second car belonging to the suspect,
parked in the same piazza, found about 598 grams of hashish divided into six
packs wrapped in aluminium foil. [@2] All the drugs tested positive on rapid
narcotests, and the drugs, cash and other items found were seized. [@2]

The operation was presented as part of the Carabinieri's daily territorial
control work, with attention to predatory crime, drug trafficking and the
protection of sensitive areas and places frequented by young people. [@2] The
case was still in the preliminary-investigation phase, with the suspect presumed
innocent until a final conviction. [@2]
