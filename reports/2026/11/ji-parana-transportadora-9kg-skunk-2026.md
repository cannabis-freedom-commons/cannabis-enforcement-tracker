Denarc, Rondônia's Department of Narcotics, and the Receita Federal, Brazil's
federal customs and tax agency, seized about nine kilograms of skunk at a
transport company in Ji-Paraná, Rondônia, on Friday 6 November 2026. [@2] The
drug was hidden in cardboard boxes among parcels that were to be sent by the
company to states in south-eastern Brazil. [@1] The parcels were intercepted
before the load left the transport company and could reach the recipients. [@4]
The officers found the drug while inspecting parcels that would be despatched by
the company. [@2]

The inspection was carried out under operations Narke and Protetor das
Fronteiras, which the reports describe as drives against drug trafficking. [@2]
No one was arrested and no suspect was found. [@2] The other accounts say the
officers searched Ji-Paraná for those responsible for posting the parcels and
also found no one. [@1] Denarc is continuing the investigation to identify the
senders and the intended recipients, and whether the parcels belong to an
inter-state distribution network. [@1] The seized material was sent to the legal
procedures, and a report published the same afternoon as the operation said
there was no information about arrests at that point. [@1]

A Diário da Amazônia report credited to Portal SGC gives the same account of
about nine kilograms found in cardboard boxes in a Ji-Paraná transport company.
