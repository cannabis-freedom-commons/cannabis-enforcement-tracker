The Tarija departmental prosecutor's office together with the Bolivian police
seized marijuana and cocaine in the city of Bermejo in a case involving four
people. [@1] Two of the four were ordered into preventive detention and the
other two into house arrest. [@1] The prosecution appealed the judicial decision
that granted house arrest to two of the accused. [@1] It said the ruling was not
in accordance with the law and filed an incidental appeal while investigations
continue. [@1]

It requested preventive detention for those under house arrest because it feared
they would flee and lose information that could help clarify the case. [@1]
Officers had stopped a vehicle after it tried to evade a routine police
checkpoint, which alerted them and led them to intervene and find controlled
substances. [@1] Inside the vehicle officers found 42.2 kilograms of marijuana,
described as 42 kilos plus 200 grams. [@1] They also found 4 grams of cocaine
alongside the marijuana, and the four suspects were charged and placed under
personal precautionary measures. [@1]

One of the suspects tried to escape on foot at the scene and the group resisted
handing over the objects they carried. [@1] Departmental prosecutor Aníbal
Cabezas Flores gave the details of the cases before the prosecution service.
[@1] He said such offences involve more than one person, including whoever hired
or delivered the substance and where it came from, all of which must be
clarified in the investigation. [@1]
