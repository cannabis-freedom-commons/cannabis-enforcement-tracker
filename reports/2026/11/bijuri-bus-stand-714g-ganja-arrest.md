Bijuri police arrested a 43-year-old man at the waiting room of the New Bus
Stand on the evening of 4 November 2026 and seized 714 grams of ganja, the Hindi
term for cannabis, from his bag. [@1,4] Police said he had gone there to sell the
ganja in the local market. [@1]

Police valued the 714 grams at about 10,000 rupees. [@2,4] Station house officer
Vikas Singh said a police team reached the bus stand on a mobile patrol and saw
a suspicious man in the waiting room who ran when he saw the officers. [@1,4]
Officers in the patrol vehicle chased and caught him. [@1] A search of his
yellow and black plastic bag found dried green leaves, stalks and seeds that
police identified as ganja. [@3]

The accused told police he had bought the ganja from Rajendra Dwivedi of
Devgawan, in Kotma police station area, and had to sell it to dealers in the
local market. [@2,4] Police registered a case under section 8/20(B) of the NDPS
Act, India's main drug law, against the accused and the supplier and took up the
investigation. [@1,4] Jansarovar Prime reported that the action followed
directions from district police chief Ankit Soni and was supervised by
additional superintendent of police Jagnnath Markam and Kotma sub-divisional
police officer Aarti Shakya. [@3]

Police said the arrest was part of a district anti-narcotics campaign that had
produced 17 ganja cases in the past week, with about two and a half quintals of
ganja valued at roughly 1.25 crore rupees seized. [@1,4]
