Police in Unterhaching stopped a driving school car on Wednesday 11 November
2026; the 21-year-old pupil was at the wheel and his driving instructor was in
the passenger seat. [@1] Officers observed drug-related impairments in the pupil
and banned him from continuing to drive; a quick drug test was positive for
cannabis. [@1]

The pupil admitted to having consumed cannabis and said he is a cannabis
patient, but that he had consumed more than medically prescribed. [@1] An
administrative offense procedure for suspected driving under the influence of
drugs was opened against the 21-year-old. [@1]
