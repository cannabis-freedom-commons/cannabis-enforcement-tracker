Department of Narcotics Control officers arrested 28-year-old Amena alias Lima
with 4 kg of ganja and a mobile phone in Akhaura. [@1] She lives in the Azampur
rail colony area of Akhaura upazila. [@1]

A taskforce team of the department's Brahmanbaria district office arrested her
on the Bypass-to-Sultanpur road on the east side of the Akhaura Bypass rail
crossing on Wednesday. [@1] Officers recovered 4 kg of ganja and one mobile
phone from her, seized the drugs and took legal action. [@1]

Assistant Sub-Inspector Muhammad Aminul Islam filed a regular case against her
at Akhaura Police Station. [@1] The department's Brahmanbaria deputy director
Sajedul Hasan confirmed the operation. [@1]
