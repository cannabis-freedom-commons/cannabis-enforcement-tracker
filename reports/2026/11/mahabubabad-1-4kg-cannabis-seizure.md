District excise officials carried out special checks against cannabis transport
and country-made liquor in Mahabubabad district under District Prohibition and
Excise Officer Karanchand. [@1,2] Around Mahabubabad railway station and near
Kuravi Gate, officers found 1.4 kg of dried cannabis hidden in bushes beside the
railway track on Anantharam road and seized it. [@1,2] No one was reported as
arrested with the cannabis. [@1]

Separately in the same operation, officers raided Bhangya Thanda, Rayini Patnam
and Islavath Thanda villages in Kuravi mandal, a subdistrict, over illicit
country liquor. [@1] Two women were caught with natusara, a locally distilled
illicit liquor, while officers seized 10 litres of that liquor along with 50 kg
of black jaggery and 20 kg of alum. [@1] Cases were registered over the liquor,
and three persons arrested in those natusara cases were produced before the
tahsildars of Kuravi and Bayyaram, local revenue officers, where bind-over
proceedings were started. [@1]

Excise Circle Inspector Ram Prasad warned of strict action over cannabis and
liquor sale or transport and said surveillance of illicit liquor making and
narcotics transport would continue. [@1] Excise Sub-Inspector Srinivas, trainee
Sub-Inspector Dhanalakshmi and constables Nagaraju, Gopi and John Pasha took
part in the raids. [@1]
