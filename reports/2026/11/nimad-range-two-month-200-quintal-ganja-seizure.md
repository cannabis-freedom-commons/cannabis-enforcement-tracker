Over the past two months, in a special operation run across the four Nimad Range
districts of Khargone, Barwani, Khandwa and Burhanpur, police say they have
carried out major action against the illegal drug trade and seized about 200
quintal of ganja, roughly 20,000 kg, valued at about 8 crore rupees. [@1] Police
registered 70 cases during the two-month operation and say they are
investigating the network behind the illegal cultivation. [@1]
