Two Nottinghamshire Police response officers were at a petrol station on
Camberley Road in Bulwell on November 10 at about 11.40pm when they noticed a
man fuelling up his car. [@1] They smelled a strong stench of cannabis coming
from the vehicle and decided to investigate. [@1] After a vehicle search,
officers found a large bundle of cash totalling £905 and a large quantity of
cannabis stuffed in a blue tube. [@1] A 32-year-old man was arrested on
suspicion of possession with intent to supply a class B drug (cannabis), concern
in the supply of class A drugs (cocaine), possession of criminal property and
drug driving. [@1]

The man's mobile phone also revealed a cannabis menu with a price list. [@1]
Police said they looked forward to progressing the investigation and uncovering
the full scale of the operation. [@1]
