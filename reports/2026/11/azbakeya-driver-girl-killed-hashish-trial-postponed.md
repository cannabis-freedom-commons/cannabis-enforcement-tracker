Cairo's criminal court postponed to 15 November 2026 the trial of a driver
charged with running over and killing a girl as she crossed the road in the
Azbakeya district. [@1] The Public Prosecution charged him with negligent
homicide, reckless and negligent driving under the influence of narcotic
substances, and disregarding the laws and regulations governing traffic. [@1] A
report by the Forensic Medicine Authority's chemical laboratory found that urine
and blood samples taken from the accused tested positive for the narcotics ice
and hashish. [@1]

Cairo police's operations room received a report from residents of a road
accident with a fatality on the road in the Azbakeya district. [@1] On
inspection, police found the body of a girl who had been hit by an unidentified
car as she crossed the road, which killed her on the spot, while the driver had
fled. [@1]

Police seized the vehicle and took the necessary legal action against the
accused. [@1]
