Military Police ROTAM officers, supported by a rural patrol team and the sniffer
dog Koby, arrested three men and one woman in Palmas, Paraná, on the night of
Friday 6 November 2026. [@1] The operation began around 22:15 after information
that a mechanic's workshop in the Aeroporto neighbourhood was being used for
drug trafficking. [@1]

At the workshop officers caught two men trying to flee through the back of the
property and, with the dog's help, found more than a kilogram of marijuana and
R$ 2,756 in cash with one suspect. [@1] One of those involved confirmed to
police that the workshop was used to divide the drugs into portions, the
Military Police said. [@1]

Officers then went to a house in the central area, described as a supply point,
and with the owner's permission found about five kilograms of marijuana in nine
and a half tablets hidden under a red blanket where a dog slept, plus a
precision scale. [@1] The man at the house resisted arrest, punching and kicking
officers, and police said they used moderate force to restrain him. [@1]

In total officers seized 6.079 kilograms of marijuana, two precision scales, R$
2,968 in cash, five mobile phones and two notebooks with notes on the criminal
activity. [@1] The four suspects and the seized material were taken to the
Central de Flagrantes, the police station where arrests in the act are
processed, for the applicable procedures. [@1]
