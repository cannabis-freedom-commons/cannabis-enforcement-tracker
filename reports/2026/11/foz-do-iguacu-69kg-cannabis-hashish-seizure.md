On Friday 6 November 2026, teams of the Federal Police and the Military Police
seized approximately 69.2 kg of drugs in Foz do Iguaçu, Paraná. [@1] The action
joined the Border Police Battalion of the Paraná Military Police with the
Federal Police. [@2] Teams spotted a boat crossing the Rio Paraná from Paraguay
to Brazil. [@1] The suspects abandoned the load and fled on foot into woodland
as the teams closed in. [@2] Searches found no suspects. [@2]

Officers searched the area and found the volumes inside an abandoned building.
[@1] The inspection found 36.5 kg of capulho-type marijuana and 32.7 kg of
Moroccan hashish. [@1] A second account described the same split as 35.5 kg of
capulho marijuana and 32.7 kg of hashish-like substance, totalling 68.2 kg. [@2]

The seized drugs were taken to the Federal Police station in Foz do Iguaçu for
legal procedures. [@1]
