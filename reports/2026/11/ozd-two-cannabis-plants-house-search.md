Officers from Ozd checked a property on Liszt Ferenc Street on 11 November after
noticing suspicious ventilation equipment on the building's wall and smelling a
strong odour suggesting drugs, as the Borsod County police reported on Thursday
on police.hu. [@1] Officers first noticed the smell on the street outside the
property. [@1] The man was questioned as a suspect and made a confessional
statement. [@1] He will defend himself at liberty as the proceedings continue.
[@1]

Behind a blackout curtain inside the property, officers found two flowering
cannabis plants grown in pots. [@1] A ventilation unit supplied the air exchange
needed for the plants to develop. [@1]

After searching the house, officers also found dried plant debris suspected to
be drugs in a preserving jar and in a wooden storage container, together with
equipment needed for cultivation, and seized those items. [@1]
