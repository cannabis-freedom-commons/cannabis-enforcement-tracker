The Northwest Regional Directorate of the National Police reported that it
seized 92 portions of plant material presumed to be marijuana and detained two
people during a targeted operation against controlled-substance distribution
points in the municipality of Esperanza. [@1] The two detained people were taken
away for investigation purposes. [@1] Several other people fled the scene when
they noticed the police presence. [@1]

Those who fled left behind an abandoned box containing the 92 portions of the
plant material, weighing about 75.5 grams. [@1]

According to a press note, the intervention took place at a suspected
drug-distribution point on Calle Primera in the El Nueve sector. [@1] The seized
substances are to be sent to the relevant department for analysis and the
established legal procedures. [@1] The National Police said it will continue
carrying out targeted operations and intelligence actions in the municipalities
of the Northwest region to combat drug trafficking and strengthen citizen
security. [@1]
