Officers of the Padova police headquarters' mobile squad arrested a 24-year-old
man who was born in Morocco and lives in Padova on suspicion of possessing drugs
for dealing and resisting a public official. [@2] The arrest came on the evening
of Saturday 7 November 2026, at a garage in a residential block in a side street
off via Bembo, after residents and shopkeepers reported a stream of young people
making brief, repeated visits. [@3] Plain-clothes officers of the mobile squad's
unit for widespread crime waited for the man to come out of the garage and
searched him and the lock-up. [@2]

Police found more than 320 grams of hashish, split into blocks and ready-made
doses, and reported it at that figure throughout their statement, despite a
summary line giving 350 grams. [@2] They also seized a precision scale,
packaging material, a knife used to cut the hashish and more than 8,000 euros in
cash that investigators considered proceeds of dealing. [@1]

The man has previous convictions for property crimes, assault and resisting a
public official, including a 2022 sentence of five months and 10 days for
aggravated theft. [@2] He was taken to Padova's Due Palazzi prison and held at
the disposal of the Padova public prosecutor's office, pending a validation
hearing. [@3]
