Lahore police arrested two men described as drug peddlers, one of them an
employee of the Special Protection Unit, a provincial police body that guards
protected people, and recovered 1,460 grammes of hashish. [@1] A spokesperson
for the DIG Operations identified the arrested men as Zeeshan and Azeem Rasheed,
the SPU employee. [@1] Police also seized a 30-bore pistol and three live
bullets from Azeem Rasheed. [@1]

Cases under drug and illegal weapons laws were registered against both men at
Badami Bagh police station in Lahore. [@1] DIG Operations Faisal Kamran said
indiscriminate accountability was under way against employees of the police
department involved in crime. [@1] He said wearing a uniform or holding a
government post did not place anyone above the law, and that police personnel
involved in crime would also face legal action. [@1]
