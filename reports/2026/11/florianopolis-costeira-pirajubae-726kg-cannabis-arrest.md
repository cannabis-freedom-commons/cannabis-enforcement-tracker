Officers of the BOPE special operations battalion of the Military Police of
Santa Catarina arrested two men in Costeira do Pirajubaé, in Florianópolis. [@1]
The arrest took place during a police operation on Monday 9 November 2026. [@1]
The CATE team of BOPE went to a house on Servidão Maria Laureana Machado after
receiving anonymous reports. [@2] Through the glass door, officers saw the two
men handling and packaging drugs. [@2] According to the PMSC, the pair did not
resist and admitted selling drugs at the address. [@2] One of the men said he
had lived in the house for about a month and sold drugs there and by
tele-entrega delivery with the help of a motorcycle courier. [@2] The other man
said he was the courier who collected the drugs and made the deliveries to the
addresses indicated. [@2]

The seizure totalled about 7.1 kg of marijuana plus 160 grams of skank, a potent
form of cannabis, counted here as 7.26 kg of cannabis. [@1] Officers also seized
135 grams of cocaine, 45 grams of ice, 45 grams of MDMA and 120 grams of
mushrooms, which are not counted as cannabis. [@1] They also seized 150 ecstasy
tablets weighing 115 grams and 16 vials of anaesthetic used as an input. [@1]
Searching the house, officers found more drugs inside an organiser box and a
motorcycle top box built with a false bottom. [@2] The BOPE team found eight
.38-calibre rounds, five precision scales and three vacuum-sealing machines.
[@1] The seized materials also included a radio communicator, three rolls of
plastic packaging and two mobile phones. [@1]

The case was classified as drug trafficking, criminal association for
trafficking and illegal possession of permitted ammunition. [@1] The two men
were taken into custody with the seized materials for the applicable legal
procedures. [@1]
