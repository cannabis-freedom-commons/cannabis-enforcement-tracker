Police in Pavlovsky District, Voronezh Oblast, seized more than 346 grams of
cannabis from a 19-year-old resident of the village of Vorontsovka. [@1]
Criminal-investigation officers found a bag of dried plant parts in the attic of
his house during a search of the property. [@1] The young man told police that
he had gathered wild bushes near the village for personal use. [@1]

Officers sent the contents for examination to the forensic centre of the
regional police headquarters. [@1] Experts confirmed that the seized plants were
narcotic cannabis with a total weight of more than 346 grams. [@1]

Police opened a criminal case under part 2 of article 228 of the Criminal Code,
covering unlawful acquisition and storage of drugs. [@2] The case was classified
as large-scale storage, which carries a maximum penalty of 10 years in prison.
[@1] While the investigation continues, the suspect was placed under a written
pledge not to leave the area. [@1] The regional police headquarters said the
young man faces up to 10 years for unlawfully storing the prohibited substances.
