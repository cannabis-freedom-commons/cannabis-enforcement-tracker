Civil Police officers arrested two men and one woman in flagrante on the night
of Sunday 8 November 2026 in an operation against drug trafficking in São
Francisco de Assis and Cacequi, in Rio Grande do Sul. [@1] Officers seized about
50 kilograms of marijuana and a .38 revolver with its serial number filed off.
[@1] The three were charged in flagrante with drug trafficking and association
for trafficking, the Brazilian rule for caught in the act. [@2] Police sent the
three to the prison system, where they remained at the disposal of the courts.
[@1]

Investigators had watched an address in Cacequi for several days and suspected
it served as a depot where drugs were stored and fed sales points in nearby
municipalities. [@2] The São Francisco de Assis police station coordinated the
operation, with teams from Jaguari, São Vicente do Sul and Mata taking part.
[@2] Police estimated the damage to the group at more than one million reais.
[@1]

Investigations continued to identify other people involved and to establish
where the drug came from and where it was going. [@1]
