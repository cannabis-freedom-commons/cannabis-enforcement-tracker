Mallarpur police arrested a woman after finding 996 grams of ganja in a shop in
Birbhum district, West Bengal, on the morning of Thursday 5 November 2026. [@1]
The woman was identified as Jhunu Mal, and the report says she lives in the same
area. [@1] Officers searched the Radharani Store on a tip-off at about 9:35 a.m.
[@1]

Mallarpur police station registered a first information report under the
Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act. [@1] The arrested woman
was produced in the Rampurhat subdivisional court the same day. [@1] Police said
they were investigating who else was involved in the ganja trade and where the
drug supply came from. [@1]

The raid was carried out under Birbhum district's campaign against narcotics.
[@1]
