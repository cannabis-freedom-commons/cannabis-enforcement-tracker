Jamrud police arrested two suspects and recovered 10 kilograms of charas during
a special snap-checking operation, officials said. [@1] Officers stopped and
searched the two men at a check post under the supervision of the Jamrud station
house officer Adnan Afridi. [@1] Five kilograms of charas was recovered from
each of the two men, the police said. [@1]

Police registered a case against both men and said further investigation was
under way. [@1] District Police Officer Waqar Ahmed said the crackdown in the
district would continue until drug dealers and smugglers were eliminated. [@1]
