Military Police officers arrested a man in the act of drug trafficking on the
night of Sunday 8 November 2026 in the Morro do Horácio, in Florianópolis. [@1]
The arrest came during Operação Lei e Ordem (Operation Law and Order), with
officers from the 4th Military Police Battalion and the BOPE special operations
battalion. [@1] According to the Military Police, the teams were patrolling a
spot known for drug dealing when they approached the man and found a range of
drugs already divided into portions, plus equipment used in trafficking. [@1]

Officers seized 260 grams of marijuana and 4 grams of hashish. [@1] The rest of
the haul was 40 grams of cocaine, 27 grams of crack, 7 grams of MD, two ecstasy
tablets, a two-way radio, two precision scales, 529 reais in cash and a mobile
phone. [@1] The man and the seized material were taken to the Capital's on-duty
police station, which handles arrests. [@1]
