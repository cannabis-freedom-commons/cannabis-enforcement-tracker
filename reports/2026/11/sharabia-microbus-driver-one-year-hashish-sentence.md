The Cairo Criminal Court of Appeal upheld the first-instance verdict against a
microbus driver in the Al-Sharabia drug-use case. [@1] The court punished him
with one year of imprisonment with labour and a fine of 10,000 Egyptian pounds.
[@1] The Public Prosecution had referred the 26-year-old defendant, identified
as Ahmed.Kh, to criminal trial for possessing amphetamine, hashish and
methamphetamine for use outside legally permitted circumstances. [@1]

A Sharabia investigations officer on patrol saw the defendant holding a
cigarette giving off dense smoke, smelled a narcotic odour on approaching,
seized the cigarette from his hand, and the defendant admitted possessing the
drugs for use. [@1] The arrest took place within the Sharabia police district in
Cairo. [@2]

The chemical laboratory report found that the seized tobacco roll contained
hashish, a narcotic listed in Schedule 1 of the drug law. [@1] Analysis of a
sample taken from him was also positive for amphetamine and methamphetamine,
likewise listed in Schedule 1, and those non-cannabis substances are excluded
from the cannabis figures for this mixed case. [@1]
