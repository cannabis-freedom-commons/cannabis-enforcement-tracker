Sangvi police raided forest land in Jamnyapani, on the Madhya Pradesh border in
Shirpur taluka of Dhule district, at about 4.45 pm on 5 November 2026. [@1]
Police Inspector Jaypal Hire of Sangvi police station had received information
that cannabis was being grown on the forest land and packed there for sale. [@1]
At the site officers found three people packing cannabis. [@1] All three fled
when they spotted the police; officers chased them for some distance but they
disappeared among hills in the remote terrain. [@1]

None was arrested, and police registered a case against three suspects at Sangvi
police station. [@1] Police seized wet and dried cannabis, a two-wheeler and
three mobile phones from the scene, property they valued at about ₹14.39 lakh
(1,439,000 rupees). [@1]

Police identified as the main suspect in the cultivation the sarpanch, the
elected head of a village council, of Phaliya Dugani village on the Sendhwa road
in Barwani district, Madhya Pradesh. [@1] Police Inspector Jaypal Hire is
investigating the case. [@1]
