Military Police arrested a 31-year-old man on suspicion of drug trafficking on
the afternoon of Monday 9 November 2026 in the Canafístula neighbourhood of
Arapiraca, in Alagoas's Agreste region. [@1] According to the Military Police,
officers seized 35 portions of marijuana, 90 rocks of crack and 47 pins of
cocaine during the operation. [@1]

The incident was recorded at around 4pm during a patrol conducted by teams from
the Pelotão de Operações Policiais Especiais (Pelopes) special operations
platoon and the Canil dog unit of the 3º BPM. [@1] Two police patrols entered a
vacant lot while a third patrol received information that objects had been
thrown over a wall towards the back of a neighbouring residence. [@1] Officers
called out to the occupants of the house, who came outside and informed police
that a man was in the backyard. [@1] The suspect then came out of the yard to be
approached and searched by the officers. [@1] During searches of the ground
where the objects had been thrown, military police found a bag containing a
sweet potato and the portions of drugs. [@1]

The man found in the backyard was taken along with all the seized items to the
Central de Flagrantes police station in Arapiraca. [@1] After being questioned
by the Civil Police, he remained detained under arrest for drug trafficking.
