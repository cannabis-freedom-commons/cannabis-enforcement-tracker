Carabinieri from the Gricignano di Aversa station carried out a targeted check
against drug trafficking and use that led to a minor being reported at liberty
in Carinaro. [@1] The intervention took place on the afternoon of 5 November as
a specific prevention and repression service linked to drug trafficking. [@1]
Officers carried out a personal and home search of a 17-year-old resident of
Carinaro, recovering drugs in different packaging as well as ammunition. [@1]
The boy was reported at liberty to the competent juvenile judicial authority.
[@1] The proceedings were at the preliminary-investigation stage, with his
responsibility to be established in court under the presumption of innocence
until any final conviction. [@1]

Officers found 16 plastic sachets containing hashish weighing 34 grams in total,
alongside three heat-sealed packs of cocaine weighing 1.3 grams in total. [@1]
They also found three projectiles, one of calibre 9 and two of calibre 6.65.
[@1] All the material was placed under penal seizure and kept pending later
deposit with the exhibits office. [@1]
