Purbadhala Thana police arrested 35-year-old Md Jamal Mia in an anti-drug
operation in Bikunia village, Hogla Union, on 2 November 2026. [@1] Jamal Mia is
the son of the late Shahid Ali of the same village. [@1] A mobile court
sentenced him to 10 days of rigorous imprisonment and a fine of 500 taka. [@1]
The court ordered five more days of imprisonment if the fine is not paid. [@1]

Officers recovered one small packet, a puria, of ganja, the local term for
cannabis, from his possession. [@1] Police said he was detained in an
intoxicated state with the ganja. [@1] Executive Magistrate and Assistant
Commissioner (Land) Salah Uddin Mahmud conducted the mobile court on the
instructions of Upazila Nirbahi Officer Shafiqul Islam. [@1] Purbadhala police
station chief Mohammad Abdullah Al Mamun confirmed the case and said the
station's anti-drug operations are continuing. [@1]
