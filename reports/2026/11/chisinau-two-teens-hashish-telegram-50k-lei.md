Two 17-year-olds in Chisinau are suspected of involvement in a network selling
drugs through the Telegram app. [@2] Police from the National Inspectorate of
Investigations and PCCOCS prosecutors seized hashish and placed the two
17-year-old minors under judicial control for alleged drug trafficking via
Telegram. [@1] According to police, their roles were as couriers and
storekeepers of the narcotic substances. [@2]

Officers of the National Inspectorate of Investigations, with PCCOCS prosecutors
and Fulger brigade specialists, executed search warrants in Chisinau in a
drug-trafficking case. [@1] During searches at the suspects' homes in the
capital, officers found and seized hashish that was to be placed in stashes
around Chisinau municipality. [@2] Police valued the seized hashish at 50,000
lei. [@1]

The narcotics were discovered at the young people's homes during the searches,
and if convicted they face between three and seven years in prison. [@1] They
remain presumed innocent until a court ruling, but face three to seven years in
prison if found guilty. [@2]
