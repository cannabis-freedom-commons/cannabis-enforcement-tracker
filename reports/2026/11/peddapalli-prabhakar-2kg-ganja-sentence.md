Police arrested Muthunuri Prabhakar, known as Nagesh, near the Ellamma temple in
Peddapalli in 2021 and filed a charge sheet in court after finishing their
investigation. [@1] He was 19 and lived in Tilak Nagar, Peddapalli town. [@1]
Then-sub-inspector K. Rajesh and his staff checked him after finding him
wandering suspiciously near the temple. [@1] Officers found two kilograms of
ganja in his possession, seized it and registered a case. [@1]

Peddapalli District Court found the offence proved, and judge K. Sunitha
sentenced him to three years of rigorous imprisonment and fined him Rs 10,000.
[@1] Officials of the Ramagundam Police Commissionerate announced the outcome.
[@1] Public prosecutor Donkana Ravi played a key role in securing the sentence.
[@1]

Peddapalli DCP B. Ram Reddy and ACP G. Krishna supervised the case team. [@1]
Commissioner Amber Kishore Jha congratulated the prosecution team. [@1]
