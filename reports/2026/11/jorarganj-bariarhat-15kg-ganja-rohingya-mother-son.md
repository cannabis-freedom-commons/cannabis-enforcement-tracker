Officers of SRB-7, Chattogram, detained two Rohingya residents of a registered camp in Ukhiya with 15
kilograms of ganja during a drive in Jorarganj, Mirsharai, on the morning of Sunday 8 November 2026.
[@1,4,5] They were stopped at about 8.25 am on the Chattogram-bound side of the Dhaka–Chattogram highway
at Bariyarhat Bazar, waiting for a vehicle to take the ganja to Cox's Bazar. [@4] The ganja was packed
in three bundles wrapped in polythene and tape inside two shoulder bags. [@4] SRB-7 put its market value
at 150,000 taka. [@1,2,3,4,5]

In preliminary questioning the two said they had long bought ganja cheaply in border areas of Feni and
Chattogram and sold it at higher prices to traffickers and users in Cox's Bazar. [@2,3]
