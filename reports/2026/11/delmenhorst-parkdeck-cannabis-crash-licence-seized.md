On Saturday evening, a traffic crash occurred on the parking deck of a
supermarket in Stedinger Straße in Delmenhorst. [@1] A 20-year-old man from
Delmenhorst drove a high-powered Mercedes across the parking deck at what police
called inappropriate speed and against the designated traffic routing. [@1] His
car collided with another driver's car. [@1] Both vehicles sustained high
material damage. [@1]

The driver who caused the crash was under the influence of cannabis, police
said. [@1] Officers had a blood sample taken from him and seized his driving
licence. [@1] The account was issued as original content of the Delmenhorst /
Oldenburg-Land / Wesermarsch police inspection, the force that covers
Delmenhorst in Lower Saxony. [@1]
