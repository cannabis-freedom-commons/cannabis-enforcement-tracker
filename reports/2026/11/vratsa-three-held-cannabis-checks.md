Police in Vratsa detained three men in two separate checks on 4 November 2026,
the Vratsa regional police directorate said. [@1] At about 22:20 on 4 November,
officers stopped a car in Vratsa driven by a 20-year-old local man, whose
roadside drug test came back positive for cannabis. [@1] Officers also found
cannabis on a passenger in the car, held both men for up to 24 hours and seized
the vehicle. [@1] Police opened fast-track proceedings over the car stop under a
traffic article of the Criminal Code covering driving after drug use. [@2]

Earlier the same day, officers of the criminal crime suppression unit at the
Vratsa regional police checked a 22-year-old city resident with prior criminal
record and convictions. [@1] A search of the man found folded paper packets
containing cannabis, Konkurent.bg reported. [@3] A search of his home then found
more cannabis and ketamine. [@1] A pre-trial proceeding was opened over the home
search, while the car stop was handled as a fast-track case. [@1]
