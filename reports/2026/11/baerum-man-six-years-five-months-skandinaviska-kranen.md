A 30-year-old man from Bærum was sentenced in Oslo District Court earlier this
week to six years and five months in prison. [@1] The court held that he had a
central role in the Swedish-origin narcotics network «Den skandinaviska kranen»,
which has taken root in Norway. [@1] The court found he had acted as a runner, a
depot keeper and a distributor, moving and storing narcotics for the network.
[@1]

He admitted working for the network and was convicted of fetching and selling
more than 34 kilograms of cannabis, amphetamine and cocaine over nine days in
April last year. [@1] He was also convicted of storing 4.8 kilograms of hashish
and 487 grams of cocaine. [@1] He was convicted of securing proceeds from
narcotics sales, receiving and handing on more than 530,000 kroner in one day in
April last year. [@1]

Police lawyer Miriam Kaya Breistein, who led the case for the prosecution, said
the charge rested largely on messaging correspondence rather than on seizures of
drugs or money. [@1] The Signal username «Joe», described in court as the
network's top leader, appeared in a conversation group on his phone that was
said to have been used to organise drug sales. [@1]

He came to police attention in April last year when he was stopped in a traffic
control and appeared, in the court's words, heavily intoxicated. [@1] Cocaine
and a softgun were found in the car, for which he was convicted of breaching the
weapons law and drug-impaired driving. [@1] Police also found a bunch of keys
marked with the address of a flat in Oslo, where they found cocaine, hashish, a
scale and packaging equipment. [@1] Some of the seized drugs were found hidden
inside a pizza box, and ten hashish plants of 100 grams each were found in a
Norwegian postal package. [@1]

The sentence was lower than the prosecution's demand of seven years in prison,
and was imposed under the so-called mafia paragraph, which allows stricter
penalties for organised criminal groups. [@1] His defence lawyer, Chamkor Singh
Nagra, said the sentence would be appealed. [@1]
