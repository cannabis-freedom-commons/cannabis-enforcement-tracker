Officers of the Department of Narcotics Control in Bogura arrested three people,
including a listed drug dealer, during an anti-drug operation in Nandigram. [@1]
The three were Md Farizul Islam, 42, son of Habibur Dhali of Binerpota village
in Satkhira Sadar upazila; Md Alam, 30, son of Md Shafiqul Islam of Damgara
village in Nandigram upazila; and Md Siddik Hossain, 70, son of the late Aji
Muddin of Chakrampur village. [@1] Police information described Siddik Hossain
as a listed drug dealer. [@1]

Officers recovered five pieces of yaba and 200 grams of ganja, the local name
for cannabis, from them. [@1] A mobile court sentenced all three to one month of
simple imprisonment, without labour, and a fine of 100 taka each. [@1] The
executive magistrate destroyed the recovered yaba and ganja on the spot. [@1]

The drive ran at about 3:20 p.m. on Wednesday 11 November 2026 across various
areas of Nandigram upazila in Bogura. [@1] An operational team from the Kha
circle of DNC Bogura carried it out, led by Nandigram upazila nirbahi officer
and executive magistrate M Abdullah Ibne Masud Ahmed. [@1] The Department of
Narcotics Control supplied the account of the operation. [@1]
