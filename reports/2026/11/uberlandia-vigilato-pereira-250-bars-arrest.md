Military Police seized about 250 bars of marijuana and three .380-calibre
pistols from a house in the Vigilato Pereira neighbourhood of Uberlândia. [@1]
The house had been rented less than a month earlier and was being used as a
depot for drugs and weapons. [@1] A 25-year-old man was arrested in flagrante at
the house. [@1] Teams acted after receiving a tip about drugs and weapons stored
in the house. [@1] The tip indicated a load would leave the house that day,
prompting an operation supported by the Pégasus helicopter. [@1] During the
operation, an officer used a ladder to look over the wall and spotted the
suspect. [@1] Officers interpreted his movement toward the back as an attempted
escape. [@1] The team at the front broke open the gate, entered and immobilised
him. [@1]

Inside, officers found boxes of marijuana, little furniture and a mattress on
the floor. [@1] The refrigerator was off, there was little food in the kitchen
and coffee powder was scattered on the floor. [@1] Police said the coffee had
been used to try to mask the smell of the drugs. [@1] The young man told police
he was guarding the house. [@1] The owner said he had rented the house to him.
[@1] The suspect indicated weapons were in another room. [@1] In a bag, officers
found three pistols and eight magazines. [@1] All the weapons had their serial
numbers suppressed. [@1]

An opening in a bedroom wall led to a small room under construction. [@1]
Officers suspect the space would also be used to store drugs. [@1] Officers
found cardboard boxes and materials indicating preparation of the bars for
transport. [@1] Police believe the house served as a temporary point for storing
and packing marijuana before shipment to another city. [@1] The load's
destination and any link between the suspect and a criminal organisation remain
under investigation. [@1] The young man's role and that of possible other
participants will be examined by the Civil Police. [@1]
