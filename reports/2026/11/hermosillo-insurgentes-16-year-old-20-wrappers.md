Municipal police in Hermosillo detained a 16-year-old boy who was carrying 20
wrappers of green, dry herb that appeared to be marijuana and a replica handgun.
[@1] The detention happened at 18:15 on Wednesday 4 November 2026 at the corner
of Mazatán and Héroes de Caborca streets in the colonia Insurgentes, in the
north of the city. [@1] The officers acted after the teenager behaved evasively
when he saw them, so they stopped him for a check. [@2]

The wrappers of suspected marijuana and the replica pistol were found among his
belongings. [@2] The boy was handed over to the investigating authority. [@2]
