Gurugram Crime Branch's Sector-17 team arrested an alleged charas supplier from
Chandigarh. [@1] The accused was identified as Vedram alias Vedu, a resident of
Chauki village in Kullu district of Himachal Pradesh. [@1] The same case
includes the 31 October arrest of Sohna residents Ankit Jain and Tapan Parashar,
who were caught with 203.71 grams of charas. [@1] Police said the two men told
interrogators during questioning that they had purchased the charas in Himachal
Pradesh. [@1] They said they had brought the consignment to Gurugram intending
to sell it there. [@1]

Police valued the 203.71 grams of charas recovered from the two men's possession
at 100,000 rupees. [@1] Investigators said further questioning established that
Vedram had planted ganja plants in his own village. [@1] Police said he prepared
narcotics from those plants and supplied them onward. [@1] Police added that a
separate case under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act was
already registered against Vedram in Chandigarh. [@1]
