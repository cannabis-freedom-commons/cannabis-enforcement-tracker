Police near Siswaniya village in the Ramgarhwa police station area of East
Champaran district, Bihar, seized 5 kg of ganja and arrested two men. [@1,2]
Police acted after receiving a secret tip about ganja smuggling. [@1,2]

The action was taken under a special operation ordered by the police superintendent, acting on a secret tip on Thursday evening. [@2] The two arrested men were identified as Rajkishore Bhatta of Siswaniya and Ram Kumar Mahato of Chikani, Ramgarhwa police station area, East Champaran district, Bihar. [@2] Police lawfully seized the five kilograms of Nepali ganja and, on the station head's statement, registered a case against both arrestees under the NDPS Act and sent them to judicial custody on Friday. [@2]
