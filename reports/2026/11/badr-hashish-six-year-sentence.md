The New Cairo Criminal Court, sitting in the Fifth Settlement, sentenced three
defendants to six years of aggravated imprisonment for holding hashish for sale
in the Badr police district. [@1] The court issued the verdict on Sunday 1
November 2026. [@2] The referral order said the three held hashish without legal
authorisation on 1 March 2026 in the same district. [@1]

A Roads and Ports Security officer posted in the district saw the first
defendant driving a car against traffic with the other two beside him, stopped
them and searched the vehicle. [@1] In a cardboard box inside the car, the
officer found 125 brown pieces of hashish, a cannabis resin. [@1]

The defendants admitted holding the drugs for sale, and investigators said the
money seized from them came from their dealing and that they used mobile phones
to contact customers. [@1]
