Civil Police officers in Águas Belas, in the Agreste of Pernambuco, arrested an
18-year-old man on Thursday 5 November 2026 while serving a search for an
assault on his brother. [@1] The suspect was identified as Alan Vitor Martins de
Menezes, 18, and was wanted on suspicion of physically assaulting his own
14-year-old brother. [@1]

The operation was coordinated by delegate Alexciana Farias. [@1] During the
searches officers found and seized 105 sachets of cocaine, 56 portions of
marijuana, a precision scale and two mobile phones. [@1] According to the Civil
Police, the seized material was thought to be linked to suspected drug dealing.
[@1]

The man was taken to the Águas Belas police station and booked in flagrante for
the offence. [@1] After the legal procedures he remained at the disposal of the
courts and was due to appear at a custody hearing. [@1]
