The Nozha prosecution ordered a foreign national detained for four days while it investigated cannabis oil allegedly found in a parcel from the United States, Youm7 reported on 10 October 2026. [@1] On 2 November the same outlet reported that a Nozha misdemeanour court judge had renewed the detention for 15 days pending investigation. [@2]

Postal customs officer Badour Hamdy Abdel Aziz flagged the shipment, which contained six hair- and hand-care containers and weighed six kilograms in total. [@1,2,3] The Egyptian Post Customs director referred the parcel to the anti-narcotics branch at Cairo International Airport. [@1,2,3] Youm7 reported that the branch identified cannabis oil and put its value at about 12 million Egyptian pounds, while Al-Masry Al-Youm reported that officials suspected cannabis oil and were awaiting further technical procedures. [@1,2,3] Customs recorded the seizure under report No. 5 of 2026 and sealed the parcel with customs wire and lead for referral to the authorities. [@1,2,3]
