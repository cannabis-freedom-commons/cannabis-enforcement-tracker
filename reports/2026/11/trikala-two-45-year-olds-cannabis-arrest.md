Officers of the Trikala Subdirectorate for the Prosecution and Investigation of
Crimes arrested two 45-year-old Greek nationals in Trikala at midday on 9
November 2026. [@1,2] During a check, officers found in their possession a nylon
package containing a small quantity of unprocessed cannabis. [@1,2] Officers also
found a hand-rolled cigarette containing a mixture of cannabis and tobacco. [@1,2]
Police seized the nylon package and the cigarette at the scene. [@1,2]

Separately that evening in Trikala, officers of the Trikala DI.AS.
motorcycle-policing unit arrested a 24-year-old Greek national. [@1] The
24-year-old was driving a private car at high speed and endangered other users
of the road network, and no cannabis was reported in his case. [@1] The article
presents the two cannabis arrests and the driving arrest together as three
Trikala arrests. [@1]
