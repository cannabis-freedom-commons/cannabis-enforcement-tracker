Police in Zheleznogorsk, Kursk Oblast, detained a 38-year-old man who tried to
slip past a stationary check post set up under the KTO legal regime, Russia's
counter-terrorism operation framework. [@1] Officers stopped him for a check
because he was nervous, answered vaguely and struggled to find words, which led
them to search him. [@2]

The search found about 30 grams of marijuana on him, which officers seized. [@1]

The man told police he had earlier gathered wild cannabis growing in a field and
made a drug from it for his own use. [@1] Police said they were working to
establish where he gathered the plant so that the spot could be cleared. [@1]

A criminal case was opened against him under part 1 of article 228 of the
Criminal Code, which covers unlawful acquisition and storage of drugs without
intent to sell. [@1] The accounts came from the Zheleznogorsk interior ministry
division, whose statement the local outlets published. [@3]
