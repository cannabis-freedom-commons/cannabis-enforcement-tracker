A joint team of the Crime Branch and Gohalpur police arrested one man with ganja
in Jabalpur during the district's campaign against illegal drugs. [@1] The
arrested man was identified as 32-year-old Kishore alias Kissu Tiwari, a
resident of Kudwari near the Kali Mata temple in Gohalpur. [@1]

Police said a search of his bag found a total of 4 kg 72 g of ganja kept in two
packets. [@1] Police put the estimated value of the seized ganja at Rs 203,600.
[@1] Police seized the ganja and registered a case against the accused under
Section 8/20 of the NDPS Act. [@1]

According to Gohalpur station in-charge Ritesh Pandey, the Crime Branch received
an informer's tip on 1 November 2026 that a man was standing with ganja in a bag
near the Kali Mata temple in Kudwari. [@1] The police team reached the spot,
cordoned it off and caught the suspect. [@1] Police said they are now
questioning him about where he brought the ganja from and who else is involved
in the trade. [@1] Police described the arrest as part of continuous action
against drug and illicit-liquor suspects on the orders of Superintendent of
Police Sampat Upadhyay. [@1]
