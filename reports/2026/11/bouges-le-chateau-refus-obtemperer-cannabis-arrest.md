Gendarmes from the motorised platoon in Châteauroux arrested a driver at
Bouges-le-Château in the Indre department on Thursday 5 November 2026, after he
refused to stop. [@1] The driver had crashed his vehicle and then tried to flee
on foot. [@1] A gendarmerie patrol sent to seal off the area arrested him about
ten minutes later. [@1] The gendarmes placed him in garde à vue, French police
custody. [@1]

The gendarmes said he was accused of rebellion, driving while his licence was
suspended, driving under cannabis, driving without insurance, failing to stop at
stop signs, excessive speed and having no valid vehicle inspection. [@1]

The arrest was one result of road checks the Indre gendarmerie ran from Monday 2
to Sunday 8 November 2026, which included a driver caught at 146 km/h on a
departmental road limited to 80 km/h near Écueillé. [@1] Officers also recorded
seven cases of driving without insurance in the department, and stopped one man
who tested positive for cocaine, had no vehicle inspection and was driving with
a suspended licence. [@1]
