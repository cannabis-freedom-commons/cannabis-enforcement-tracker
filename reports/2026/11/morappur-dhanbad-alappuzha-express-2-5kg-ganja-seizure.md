On 5 November 2026, railway special-team police boarded the Dhanbad-Alappuzha
Express at Morappur and searched the train during the check. [@1] During the
check of the general compartment, officers found a bag lying unclaimed near the
toilet and picked it up to examine its contents. [@1] The examination showed the
bag contained 2.5 kg of ganja. [@1]

The special team seized the 2.5 kg of ganja found in the unclaimed bag near the
toilet. [@1] The team then handed the seized ganja to Salem Railway Police, who
received the recovered drug for further action. [@1]

Police inquiries are continuing to trace the people who had carried the ganja
and left the bag unclaimed on the express train. [@1] No suspect was named and
no arrest was reported for this train seizure, which was described only as the
recovery of an unclaimed bag. [@1] The 2.5 kg weight is the police team's
reported quantity for the ganja recovered from the toilet-area bag. [@1]
