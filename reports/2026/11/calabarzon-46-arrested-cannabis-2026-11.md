Police in the Philippines' CALABARZON region arrested 46 people in 34 separate
anti-illegal drug operations in the 24 hours from 6 a.m. on 4 November to 6 a.m.
on 5 November 2026. [@1] CALABARZON is the police region covering Cavite,
Laguna, Batangas, Rizal and Quezon provinces south of Manila. [@1] According to
the regional police's Daily Accomplishment Report, officers seized 36.46 grams
of suspected shabu, the local name for crystal methamphetamine. [@1]

The same report listed the cannabis seizure as 10.18 grams of marijuana and 2.01
grams of marijuana kush. [@1] PRO 4A put the combined estimated value of all
three substances at ₱252,164.60, without giving a separate figure for the
cannabis. [@1]

The releases reporting the arrests were signed under Police Regional Office 4A
director Brigadier General Dominic L. Baccay. [@1]
