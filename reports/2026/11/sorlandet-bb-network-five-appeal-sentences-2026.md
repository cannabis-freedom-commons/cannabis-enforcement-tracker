Five of the seven men convicted in the large narcotics case uncovered in
southern Norway in 2024 were fighting their long sentences in the appeals court
in November. [@1]

The case began with one of the largest police operations Agder had seen, when 19
people were arrested on 15 October 2024. [@2] The prosecution said the network
handled about 52 kilos of cannabis alongside 6 kilos of amphetamine, 2.7 kilos
of cocaine, 10,000 alprazolam tablets and 375 grams of MDMA. [@2] Police
believed the group, known in court as BB, was led by a 38-year-old music artist
from Kristiansand. [@2]

Agder district court convicted seven people in the case, six of them under the
so-called mafia paragraph, section 79c, for acting as part of an organised
criminal group. [@3] The man identified as the main figure, a music artist from
Søgne, received 12 years in prison. [@3] Three of the other defendants were
sentenced to ten years, and the remaining sentences ranged from eight years down
to two years and ten months. [@3] The artist was also ordered to forfeit 3.4
million kroner, which the court treated as the total income he had from the
operation, including credited debt. [@3] The judgment followed a trial that ran
over 23 court days. [@3]

Six of the seven appealed against their sentences in the appeals court. [@4]
