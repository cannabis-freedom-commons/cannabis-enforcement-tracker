At about 4:45 am on Thursday 12 November 2026, police stopped and searched a
Noah microbus on the paved road in front of the police checkpoint by the
Lebukhali toll plaza in Dumki. [@1] Police searched a Noah microbus at the
checkpoint in front of the Lebukhali toll plaza in Dumki and recovered four
kilograms of ganja. [@1] Police arrested the two men found in the microbus on
suspicion of transporting the drugs. [@1]

Police identified one as 46-year-old Mizanur Rahman Howlader of Kawarchar in
Bandar, Barishal. [@1] Police identified the other as 32-year-old Rubel Shikder
of Ayron in Kaukhali Sadar Union, Pirojpur. [@1] Dumki police assistant
sub-inspectors Shahbaz and Shahriar Alam Rakib, with accompanying force, were
staffing the checkpoint and carried out the anti-drug operation. [@1]

Dumki police station officer-in-charge Md Selim Uddin confirmed the case at
Dumki police station under the Narcotics Control Act, 2018. [@1] Dhaka TV
records the case as No. 11 dated 12 November 2026. [@2]
