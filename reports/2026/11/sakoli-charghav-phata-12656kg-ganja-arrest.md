Sakoli police laid a trap outside the Highway Point dhaba on National Highway 53
at Charghav Phata after a tip-off that two men were coming there to sell ganja.
[@1] Officers took the two suspects into custody as they arrived, thwarting an
attempt to smuggle ganja along the highway. [@1]

Police seized 12 kg 656 g of ganja from two trolley bags; the two men taken into
custody are Sahil Sanjay Pawar, 29, and Ram Maruti Sasane, 35, both from Thane.
[@1] Police valued the ganja at Rs 6,32,800. [@1]

A case was registered at Sakoli police station under the relevant provisions of
the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act. [@1] Police said they were
investigating where the ganja came from, who it was meant to be sold to and who
else was involved in the deal. [@1] Sakoli is in Bhandara district in eastern
Maharashtra, where Sakoli police have carried out similar highway operations
before. [@1]

The operation was carried out under the guidance of district police chief Nurul
Hasan, additional superintendent of police Nilesh More and Pawni subdivision
police officer Jotika Ingle, who also holds additional charge of Sakoli. [@1]
