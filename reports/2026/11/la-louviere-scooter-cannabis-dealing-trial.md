On 16 October 2024, a resident of rue Keramis in La Louviere went to the police
station. [@1] In his building, visitors came at all hours of the day and night,
and he told police he suspected cannabis trafficking. [@1] He reported that the
supplier moved around on an electric scooter. [@1] Police searches confirmed his
suspicions, and four men were sent to the correctional court in Mons. [@1] Only
two of the four appeared at the hearing. [@1]

The first defendant admitted holding and selling cannabis. [@1] To deliver to
his clients, he rode through the streets of La Louviere on a scooter. [@1] He
already had eight convictions since 2013, including an 18-month prison term in
2022. [@1] He also owed 120 hours of community service, of which he had
completed only 50. [@1] He told the court he no longer uses anything, works
self-employed in construction, and spends his days working, preparing quotes and
walking his dog. [@1] The second defendant, from Trivieres, admitted laundering
500 euros from drug trafficking. [@1] He knew exactly where the money came from.
[@1]

The prosecutor requested four years in prison for the scooter dealer, plus
confiscation of his vehicle. [@1] Four and three years of firm prison were
requested against the two absent defendants. [@1] For the defendant prosecuted
for money laundering, the prosecution favoured a work penalty. [@1] The defence
said his cannabis use had been heavy at the time, that he had since changed his
life to set a good example for his son, and that he respects his conditions and
attends every meeting with his probation officer. [@1] The court will give its
judgment in early December. [@1]
