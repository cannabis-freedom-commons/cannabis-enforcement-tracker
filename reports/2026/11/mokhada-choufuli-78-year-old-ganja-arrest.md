Mokhada police arrested a 78-year-old man in a drive against the illegal sale of
narcotics within Mokhada police station limits in Palghar district, Maharashtra,
on 11 November 2026. [@1] Acting on information received at about 12:04 am that
night, officers laid a trap on the road towards Khodala at Choufuli. [@1] Police
said the man they stopped was Soma Sakarya Jabar, 78, of Koulale in Jawhar
taluka, Palghar district, and that he was illegally carrying a ganja-like
narcotic substance for the purpose of sale. [@1]

Police seized 2 kg 40 grams, recorded as 2.040 kg, of the substance from him.
[@1] Police valued the seized substance at about Rs 1 lakh, or roughly 100,000
rupees. [@1] A case was registered against him at Mokhada police station under
the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act and he was arrested.
[@1] The operation was carried out under the guidance of the Superintendent of
Police and the Deputy Superintendent of Police, Jawhar, by Assistant Police
Inspector Ajit Sabale, Police Sub-Inspector Pratik Pokale, Head Constable Gore
and constables Marad and Nagare. [@1] Police Sub-Inspector Ravindra Bhagwat is
conducting the further investigation. [@1]
