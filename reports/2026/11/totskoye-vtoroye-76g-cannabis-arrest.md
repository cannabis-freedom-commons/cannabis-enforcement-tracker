Police in the village of Totskoye Vtoroye detained a 35-year-old local man
carrying a bag of plant material. [@1] A local police officer spotted him on
Kobina Street and reported him to the duty desk of the district interior
ministry department. [@1]

Forensic examination confirmed that the seized substance was cannabis, weighing
76.40 grams in total. [@1] The man said he had gathered the marijuana for his
own use. [@1]

An investigator opened a criminal case for unlawful storage of drugs under part
1 of article 228 of the Criminal Code, which carries a maximum of three years in
prison. [@1]
