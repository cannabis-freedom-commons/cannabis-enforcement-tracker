Royal Gendarmerie officers from the Sidi Bibi centre in Chichaoua province
arrested one person in the Hassi El Baqar neighbourhood on 8 November 2026 on
suspicion of involvement in drug trafficking. [@1]

The officers seized quantities of shira, cannabis resin known as hashish, and
kif, cannabis herb. [@1] They also seized a large bladed weapon found in the
detainee's possession. [@1] The operation also seized two motorcycles said to
have been used in the suspect's unlawful activities. [@1]

The gendarmerie presented the operation as part of its continuing efforts to
fight crime and dry up the sources of drug promotion in the area. [@1] The
suspect was placed in police custody for a judicial investigation under the
supervision of the competent public prosecution. [@1] The investigation aims to
establish the circumstances of the case, identify possible extensions of the
activity and any other linked parties, before the suspect is brought before the
courts. [@1]
