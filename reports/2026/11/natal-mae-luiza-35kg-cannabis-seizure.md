Military police officers from the Battalion of Ostensive Patrols with Motorcycle
Support (ROCAM) seized approximately 37 kilograms of drugs on the afternoon of
Monday 9 November in the Mae Luiza neighbourhood of Natal. [@1] A ROCAM team on
patrol near 12a Travessa Joao XXIII saw several individuals who fled toward a
wooded area when they noticed the officers. [@1] Officers searched the area to
locate the suspects and found a quantity of drugs at the site. [@1] Mae Luiza is
in the eastern zone of the city of Natal. [@1]

Officers seized 27 kilograms of maconha (cannabis), eight kilograms of skunk and
two kilograms of crack, for a total of about 37 kilograms. [@1] The cannabis
component of the seizure was 35 kilograms, comprising the 27 kilograms of
maconha and eight kilograms of skunk, while the two kilograms of crack were
excluded from the cannabis total. [@1] All the seized material was taken to the
Central de Flagrantes for the applicable legal procedures. [@1]

ROCAM is the Batalhao de Rondas Ostensivas com Apoio de Motocicletas, a
motorcycle-supported ostensive patrol unit of the military police. [@1]
