Roy Hickman, 42, was arrested after police pulled over a BMW at Princes Dock in
Liverpool. [@1] He was the front passenger when police stopped the grey BMW on
William Jessop Way on 6 July 2026. [@1] Officers seized a quantity of cannabis
from the car and searched his home on the same street. [@1] The search found
three phones, two in the bedroom and one in the living room, and a fourth was
taken from him at the custody suite. [@1] Having more than one phone breached a
serious crime prevention order, known as an SCPO, imposed to limit his return to
crime. [@1]

Hickman, originally from Dovecot, had been sentenced at Carlisle Crown Court to
172 months in prison for conspiracy to supply class A drugs, and the order runs
until 2029. [@1] He had been released on licence from that sentence in November
2024. [@3] He was recalled to prison the day after his arrest and held in
custody until sentence. [@1] The court heard he had 34 convictions for 89
offences, including dishonesty, robbery and drug dealing. [@1]

He denied the breach at Wirral Magistrates' Court in July. [@2] He pleaded
guilty at Liverpool Crown Court on 4 August. [@4] The prosecution accepted the
plea and a charge of cannabis possession was ordered to lie on the file. [@1] On
19 August, Judge Brian Cummings KC adjourned sentence so police could examine
the phones. [@3] The judge said he was "amazed" that officers had seized phones
and not thought to have them examined. [@3] Three months later, the court heard
the phones had been sent to the police digital unit and "added to the pile"
before it was determined they could not be downloaded. [@1] His barrister, John
Rowan, said he had served the equivalent of a 12-month prison sentence waiting
for the tests and was suffering from psychosis. [@1]

Recorder Harriet Johnson sentenced him on the basis that the phones contained no
evidence of criminality. [@1] She gave him a 12-month conditional discharge and
told him: "You will receive no further punishment provided you commit no further
offence in the next 12 months." [@1] The judge ordered the three phones found at
his home and the cannabis forfeited, and the phone found on him returned. [@1]
Hickman, who appeared by video link from HMP Altcourse, is due at a parole
hearing next month. [@1]
