Police in Dupnitsa detained a 30-year-old man who lives in a flat in the town
after searching it and finding both hashish and cannabis. [@1] The officers
seized a plastic bag, called a space bag, holding a resinous lump of hashish,
and another bag with dry green leafy material that reacted to cannabis. [@1] The
resident was held under a police order for up to 24 hours. [@1]

A field drug test established what the banned substances were. [@1] A fast-track
prosecution for possession of narcotic substances was opened at the Dupnitsa
district police department at the end of the previous week. [@1] The duty
prosecutor at the district prosecution was notified at once, and the Kyustendil
regional police directorate said work to document the crime was continuing. [@1]

Pirinsko and the Dupnitsa-based Вяра News published the same account,
distributed by the Kyustendil regional police directorate. [@2] The resident of
the Dupnitsa flat was detained under a police order and the duty prosecutor was
notified. [@3]
