Kolkata Police's Special Task Force (STF) arrested three young men who were
travelling on a bus from another state as it reached Kolkata. [@1] The STF
stopped the three men near Dalhousie Club on Mayo Road in central Kolkata after
they allegedly got off the bus carrying sacks of rice. [@2] The men were
carrying two sacks of rice. [@2]

Kolkata Police's Special Task Force broke up an unusual scheme for smuggling
drugs concealed in rice sacks. [@1] Officers broke the sand-cement casting and
found packets of ganja inside, alongside the aluminium plate and wire. [@1]
Taaza TV said the sacks were searched at the spot, leading to the suspected
narcotics being detected. [@2]

Two of the three arrested men were reported to be residents of West Bengal. [@2]
The STF was investigating where the drugs came from and where they were to be
delivered, and whether a larger network was involved. [@2]
