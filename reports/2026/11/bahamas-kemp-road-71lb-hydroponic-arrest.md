A 54-year-old man was arrested after Drug Enforcement Unit officers searched a
residence on St. James Road, off Kemp Road, in Nassau. [@2]

The search warrant was executed shortly after 3 p.m. on Thursday 5 November
2026. [@2] Officers searched the occupant and the premises and found suspected
hydroponic marijuana. [@2]

The drugs weighed 71.1 pounds (about 32.25 kg), police estimated. [@2] Police
valued the cannabis at about $142,000. [@2]

The man remained in custody while investigations continued. [@2] Bahamas Latest
reported that no further details about the suspect had been released and that
police had not said whether more arrests were expected. [@2]

Eye Witness News reported the seizure of more than 71 pounds of marijuana worth
$142,000 in the same bust. [@1]
