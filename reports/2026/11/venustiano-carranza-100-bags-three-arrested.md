Officers of Mexico City's Secretariat of Citizen Security (SSC) detained three
men during a police operation in the Zona Centro neighbourhood of Venustiano
Carranza borough in Mexico City. [@1] The detention took place at the junction
of Anillo de Circunvalación and José Joaquín Herrera street, where officers saw
a group of people handling plastic bags apparently used to distribute narcotics.
[@1] The detained men are aged 19, 27 and 32. [@1] Officers told the three men
of their constitutional rights and took them before the corresponding Public
Prosecutor's Office agent. [@1]

Officers seized 100 small bags of suspected marijuana from the men. [@1] As well
as the suspected marijuana, officers seized three mobile phones and cash. [@1]
The seized items were placed at the disposal of the Public Prosecutor's Office,
which will determine the men's legal situation. [@1]

A data cross-check indicated the three men may be linked to a street robbery
recorded in Cuauhtémoc borough. [@1] Authorities said the 27-year-old has a 2018
entry in the Mexico City prison system for aggravated robbery. [@1]
