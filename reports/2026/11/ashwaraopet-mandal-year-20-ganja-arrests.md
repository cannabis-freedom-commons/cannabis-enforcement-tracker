Ashwaraopet station house officer Sai Kishore Reddy told mandal residents in a
statement that police would crack down hard on anti-social activities. [@1] He
said 20 people had been arrested in ganja cases across the Ashwaraopet mandal
area over the past year. [@1] He said those arrested included people who used
ganja at home, those who acted as middlemen and those who sold it. [@1] He said
ganja plants had also been grown in Pakalagudem village in the mandal, and that
those growers were arrested and sent to jail. [@1]

He said villages were being cordoned off and checked on a war footing under the
orders of district police superintendent Rohit Raj. [@1] He said
cordon-and-search checks would henceforth be held in four or five villages every
month to suppress anti-social activities. [@1] He said the drive would target
ganja as well as cockfights and people who play, organise or take part in card
games. [@1]
