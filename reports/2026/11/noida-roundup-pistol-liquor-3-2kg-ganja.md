Gautam Buddh Nagar police arrested four people in Noida, a woman among them, in
a drive against illegal weapons and drug trafficking. [@1] Police said those
four were found with a .315 bore country-made pistol, a live cartridge, 110
bottles of illegal country liquor and a total of 3 kg 200 g of ganja. [@1]

Two of the four detentions were for a pistol and one live cartridge on the
Eastern Peripheral Expressway, and for 110 bottles of illegal country liquor
near the Bhumi Road cut and Dadha roundabout, with no cannabis reported in
either. [@1]

Sector-58 station police arrested a man near Chhota D Park in Sector-62 with 1
kg 700 g of ganja. [@1] Sector-113 station police arrested a woman near the
service road of the Sector-71 underpass on a ganja trafficking charge and found
1 kg 500 g of ganja on her. [@1]

Police said they had completed legal action against all four accused under the
relevant sections. [@1]
