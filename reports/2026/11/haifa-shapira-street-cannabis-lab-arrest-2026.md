Police from the Zevulun station in Haifa arrested Daniel Romanov, a 24-year-old
from Kiryat Yam, on suspicion of involvement in a drugs laboratory at 11 Shapira
Street, Haifa, on 5 November. [@1] Police accuse him of cultivating and
producing drugs, possessing drugs not for personal use, conspiracy to commit a
crime and obstructing a police officer in the performance of his duty. [@1]

The police asked Haifa Magistrate's Court to extend his detention by eight days,
and a second suspect is involved in the case. [@1] His lawyer, Gal Dahan, agreed
with the police representative on an extension of four days only. [@1]

Judge Or Lerner gave the agreement effect and extended his detention until 9
November. [@1]
