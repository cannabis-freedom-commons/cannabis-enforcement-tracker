Police in Septemvri detained a 30-year-old local man as a suspected drug dealer,
alongside two other men held for possessing drugs, all in the 24 hours before
the police statement. [@1] Officers from the criminal police group at the
Septemvri police directorate searched the 30-year-old's home and found four
polythene bags of dry green leaf mass. [@1] A field drug test identified the
material as marijuana weighing more than 205 grams in total, and police said it
had been prepared for sale. [@1] The man was held in custody and fast-track
proceedings are being conducted against him under the supervision of the
district prosecution service in Pazardzhik. [@1]

The three arrests came in separate police drug checks in the Pazardzhik region,
one suspect for dealing and the other two for possessing drugs. [@2] In a
separate case, police from the Belovo station spotted a 37-year-old man from
Pernik and searched a home he occupies in the town, finding small quantities of
amphetamine and cannabis. [@1]

On 2 November, criminal police stopped a 19-year-old from Septemvri for a check
on the road between Pazardzhik and Belovo and found a small bag of green leaf
mass and part of a cigarette containing a synthetic cannabinoid. [@1] Fast-track
proceedings are also under way against the Belovo suspect and the 19-year-old,
supervised by the district prosecution service in Pazardzhik. [@3]

The accounts came from the press centre of the Pazardzhik regional Interior
Ministry directorate. [@1] The largest amount of drug was found at the Septemvri
home of the 30-year-old, with more than 205 grams of marijuana prepared for
distribution. [@4]
