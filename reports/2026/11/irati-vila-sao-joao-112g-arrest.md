Military police in Irati, Paraná, arrested a man for drug trafficking and a
traffic offence on the night of Thursday 5 November 2026. [@1] At about 9:30 pm,
on patrol, officers saw a motorcycle whose rider tried to flee when he noticed
the police car and ignored the order to stop. [@1] After he was stopped, the man
said he had no driver's licence (CNH, the Brazilian national driving licence),
and irregularities were found in the motorcycle, which was taken to the yard of
the 8th Independent Military Police Company, the 8ª CIPM. [@1]

Police then received reports that the same man had returned to the place of the
first stop and was looking for something in the vegetation. [@1] During further
checks officers found him near Parque da Vila São João, a park in the Vila São
João neighbourhood. [@1] In the searches, officers found a portion of a
marijuana-like substance weighing 112.2 grams. [@1] The man was arrested and
taken to the 41st Regional Police Station in Irati for judicial police
procedures. [@1] The account came from the Paraná Military Police, which
reported the case to local outlets. [@1]

Portal Irati published the same police account, naming the 8th Independent
Military Police Company and saying the man was taken to the 41st Regional Police
Station. [@2] The man was not named, and no statement on his custody or any
court outcome was published. [@2]
