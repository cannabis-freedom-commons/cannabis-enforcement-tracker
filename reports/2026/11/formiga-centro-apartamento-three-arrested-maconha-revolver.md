Military Police of Minas Gerais arrested three men on the night of Tuesday 10
November 2026 in central Formiga on suspicion of drug trafficking and illegal
possession of a firearm. [@1] The operation was run by teams of the 63rd
Military Police Battalion (63º BPM) on the basis of strategic intelligence work.
[@1] A second account reports that the intelligence work pointed to unusual
movement and suspected drug dealing in a residential building on the street.
[@2] Officers approached two men, aged 29 and 37, who had just left an apartment
on Rua Marechal Deodoro da Fonseca. [@1]

A personal search found a portion of maconha with the two men, who said they had
bought the drug in the apartment they had just left. [@1] Acting on that
information, officers went to the apartment, which belonged to a 19-year-old,
and searched it. [@1]

The Military Police seized a .38 calibre revolver and six intact rounds of the
same calibre. [@1] They also seized six large portions of maconha, four medium
and smaller wrappers of maconha and three jars holding additional portions of
the drug. [@1] The seizure also included six packets of a substance known as
dry, a substance derived from maconha. [@1] Two precision scales, R$ 335 in
small change and two mobile phones were also seized. [@1]

All three men were arrested in flagrante: the 19-year-old occupant for drug
trafficking and irregular possession of a firearm, and the men of 29 and 37 for
drug trafficking. [@1] The three suspects and all the seized material were taken
to the police station to await the legal procedures. [@1]
