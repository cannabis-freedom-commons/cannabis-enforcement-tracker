Vau i Dejës police identified and detained Shkodra resident N. N., 57, as a
suspected accomplice in the operation codenamed Vila. [@1] Bota Sot identifies
the detained 57-year-old as Ndue Ndikaj and says he was arrested in Shkodra over
the same cannabis cultivation case. [@2] The Vau i Dejës commissariat finalized
Vila after several months of intensive investigative work with special methods
under the direction of the Shkodra prosecution. [@1]

Investigators suspect him as an accomplice in the cultivation and production of
24 kg and 240 grams of suspected Cannabis Sativa. [@1] Investigators also
suspect him in the cultivation of 78 suspected Cannabis Sativa plants. [@1]
Police say the 24 kg and 240 grams and the 78 plants were found and seized on 12
October 2026 in Vila village in the Temal administrative unit. [@1] Bota Sot
confirms the narcotics and plants were discovered in October in Vile village in
Temal during the Vila operation after months of special-methods investigation.
[@2]

The procedural materials were referred to the Shkodra prosecution for production
and sale of narcotics and cultivation of narcotic plants committed in
collaboration. [@1] Police say investigations continue to fully document the
activity and identify other citizens involved. [@1]
