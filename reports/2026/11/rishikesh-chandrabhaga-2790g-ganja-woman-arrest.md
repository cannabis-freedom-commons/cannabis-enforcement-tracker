Rishikesh Kotwali police arrested a 40-year-old woman, Sabri Devi, during a
checking drive near the Chandrabhaga river in Rishikesh on 5 November 2026. [@3]
Police in Dehradun district said they arrested two people, a woman among them,
in separate operations and recovered 2 kg 790 g of ganja and 464 g of charas in
all. [@1]

Sub-Inspector Jyoti Sharma and Constable Vineet Kumar were patrolling and
checking the slum area by the river when they saw her standing by a peepal tree
with a white plastic bag and turn back towards a lane when she noticed the
police team. [@2] Officers stopped and searched her and found two packets
wrapped in brown tape in the bag, containing 2 kg 790 g of ganja. [@2] Police
said she made up sachets of ganja and sold them, and that in questioning she
said she collected the drug from another woman living in Rishikesh. [@2] She
refused to say where the ganja had come from, police said. [@2]

Police registered a case against her at Rishikesh police station under sections
8 and 20 of the NDPS Act, India's Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances
Act, as case 580/2026, and said they were checking her criminal history. [@3]
She was being produced before a court as required, Punjab Kesari reported. [@2]
The arrest was one of two operations that Dehradun district police reported on
the same day, the other being a charas seizure in Vikasnagar, carried out under
a district campaign against drug trafficking and sale ordered by the Dehradun
senior superintendent of police. [@3]
