Military Police arrested an 18-year-old man for drug trafficking in the São João
Margem Esquerda neighbourhood of Tubarão at about 5:21 pm on Wednesday 4
November 2026. [@2] Officers had monitored the area and saw the young man using
a motorcycle to carry drugs between hiding places. [@1]

Searches produced 104 cocaine pins in two different spots, 92 grams in all,
which are not counted as cannabis. [@1] Police also seized a mobile phone, 74
reais in cash and the motorcycle. [@1]

In the Pouso Alto neighbourhood of Gravatal, Military Police arrested a
33-year-old man for drug trafficking at about 9:27 pm the same night, after
reports that he was selling drugs through tele-entrega, an order-by-phone
delivery service. [@2] Officers found 1 kg of marijuana in the car he was
driving, and searches at his home turned up another 78.8 grams of marijuana and
a tube holding 18.1 grams of the same drug including its packaging. [@2] The
home search also found 2 grams of cocaine and five ecstasy tablets, and the car
was seized along with that of the Tubarão suspect, two vehicles in all. [@2]

Both men were taken to the Civil Police station with the seized material for
legal procedures. [@3] The two arrests were among three relevant occurrences in
the region that the Military Police reported in its 24-hour operations roundup.
[@2]

Officers searched the identified hiding places with a police dog and split the
cocaine between two spots, 52 pins weighing 45.7 grams in one and 52 pins
weighing 46.3 grams in another. [@4]
