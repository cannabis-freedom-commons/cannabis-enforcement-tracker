Police arrested five men for alleged ganja smuggling and possession in
Uthukkottai taluk, Thiruvallur district, on 4 November 2026. [@1] Officers of
the Uthukkottai police station were checking vehicles at the checkpost on the
Tamil Nadu–Andhra Pradesh border. [@1] They stopped a man who was walking past
suspiciously and found 100 grams of ganja on him. [@1] He was identified as
Sathish, 25, of Annai Teresa Nagar, Palavakkam. [@1] At Kannigaipair bazaar,
Periyapalayam police seized another 100 grams of ganja from Sankar, 25, of the
same area, and arrested him. [@1]

Checks at Karikalavakkam bus stand and Anaikattu led to the arrests of two
24-year-old men, both named Akash, one from Karikalavakkam and one from
Ammanambakkam, who had ganja with them. [@1] Kadambathur police patrolling near
Ekattur railway station arrested Manikandan, 28, of Indira Nagar, Ekattur, for
possessing ganja. [@2]
