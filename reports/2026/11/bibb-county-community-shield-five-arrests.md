Bibb County sheriff's deputies arrested five people on 4 November 2026 during
Operation Community Shield, an operation against illegal drugs and weapons in
Macon. [@1,3] The sheriff's office's Special Investigations Unit, with the Gang
Unit and SWAT Team, carried out five search warrants on Wednesday, 4 November.
[@1,3] Investigators said they recovered marijuana, ecstasy, firearms, digital
scales and drug packaging materials from several homes. [@1]

Deambrose Butler, 33, was arrested at a home in the 900 block of A Street after
deputies found marijuana, a firearm, a scale, baggies and packaging materials.
[@1] Butler was charged with marijuana possession and distribution, including an
offence involving drugs near a park or housing project, and with possessing a
firearm during the commission of certain felonies. [@1] Christian Person, 30,
and Jessica Person, 30, were arrested after a search at a home in the 800 block
of A Street, where deputies found 24 grams of ecstasy, 346 grams of marijuana
and 458 grams of marijuana packaged for distribution along with three firearms.
[@1]

Christian Person was charged with possession of marijuana with intent to
distribute and with possession of a Schedule I or II controlled substance with
intent to distribute, as well as weapons counts. [@1] Jessica Person was charged
with conspiracy to violate Georgia's Controlled Substances Act, marijuana
offences and weapons charges, and with driving on a suspended, cancelled or
revoked registration and making a false statement about motor vehicle insurance.
[@1] Kenterrious Coker Sr., 37, was arrested at a home in the 1100 block of N.
Beddingfield Drive, where deputies found four firearms, about 1.8 pounds (0.82
kg) of marijuana, a scale, packaging materials and currency. [@1] Melvin Dunlap
Jr., 41, was arrested at a home in the 1400 block of Bailey Avenue, where
deputies found two firearms and about 20 individually packaged bags of suspected
marijuana weighing about 3.5 grams each. [@1]

The sheriff's office reported seizing 10 firearms in total, along with what it
called a large amount of marijuana and a large quantity of ecstasy. [@1]

WGXA reported that all five were booked at the Bibb County Law Enforcement
Center and listed the specific counts filed against each, including multiple
firearm counts and charges of possessing marijuana near a park or housing
project. [@2]

Bibb County Sheriff David Davis said the operations send a message that the
office will not allow drug trafficking and the violence it often brings to take
root in the community. [@1]

Deputies seized more than 800 grams of marijuana at the 800 block of A Street, which they believed was partly packaged for distribution. [@3]
