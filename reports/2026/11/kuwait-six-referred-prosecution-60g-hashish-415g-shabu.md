Kuwait's General Department for Drug Control referred six suspects, one of them
an expatriate woman, to the drugs and alcohol prosecution after detaining them
in four separate cases. [@1]

The Interior Ministry said the detentions followed investigations by
drug-control officers and a Public Prosecution warrant to detain the suspects
and search their homes. [@1]

The cases concerned trafficking in and possession of narcotics and psychotropic
substances, including hashish. [@1]

The searches found 60 grams of hashish, the only cannabis product named in the
haul. [@1] The same seizures included 415 grams of shabu, a stimulant that is
not cannabis, and 175 sheets of paper soaked in 'chemical', along with equipment
used to divide drugs into portions. [@1] Officers also found 540 Kuwaiti dinars
in drug-sale proceeds among the seized items. [@1]

The article gives no separate date for the detentions, no places of arrest and
no court hearing date. [@1]
