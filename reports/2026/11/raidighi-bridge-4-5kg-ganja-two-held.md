On Monday night, police raided a shop under the Raidighi bridge after a secret
tip-off. [@2] Officers first detained Rupkumar Mallik at the scene. [@2] Police
then arrested Naskar as well. [@2] Police were preparing to produce both
detained men before a court. [@2]

Police said they recovered about 4.5 kg of ganja from the bridge-area shop. [@1]
The raid came during a Sundarban police district anti-drug drive week that
police said had begun on 30 October. [@1]

Naskar had at one time served as a Trinamool Congress booth president. [@1]
Local Bharatiya Janata Party leaders and some residents praised the prompt
police action. [@2] Police said the exact allegation against each man and each
man's role in the ganja recovery would become clear only after investigation.
[@2]
