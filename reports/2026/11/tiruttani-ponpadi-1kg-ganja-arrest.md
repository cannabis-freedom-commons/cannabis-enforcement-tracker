Tiruttani police led by Inspector Sivakumar were conducting vehicle checks at
the Ponpadi checkpost near Tiruttani on Sunday. [@1] During the checks they
stopped a two-wheeler travelling from the Nagari area of Andhra Pradesh toward
Tiruttani on suspicion and searched it. [@1,3] The search revealed 1 kg of ganja,
the local term for cannabis, being smuggled on the vehicle. [@1] Police seized
the ganja and questioned the rider, who was identified as Boopathy, aged 21, of
Saraswati Nagar in Tiruttani. [@1,3] Officers then arrested Boopathy and are
continuing to question him, with no other rider or occupant reported. [@1]

Dinamalar confirms the same seizure and arrest, reporting that 1 kg of ganja was
seized and that Boopathy, 21, of Saraswati Nagar was arrested. [@2] Dinamalar
reports the checks followed a tip-off that ganja was being smuggled from Andhra
Pradesh into Tamil Nadu through Tiruttani. [@2]
