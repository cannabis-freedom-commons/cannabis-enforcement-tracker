Two men from Baia Mare, aged 35 and 46, were placed in 30 days' preventive
arrest over suspected risk-drug trafficking after one of them collected a parcel
sent from Spain by courier. [@2,4] The 46-year-old was caught in the act in Baia
Mare after allegedly taking delivery of a parcel containing more than 5
kilograms of cannabis, brought into Romania from Spain through a courier firm
for sale. [@1,4] In a follow-up operation investigators found the second man, who
had allegedly received about 3 kilograms of the cannabis. [@1,4]

The case was documented by officers of the Maramureș organised-crime service
with DIICOT prosecutors from its Maramureș territorial office, the department
that investigates organised crime and terrorism. [@1,4] On 5 November 2026 DIICOT
prosecutors ordered both men detained, and a judge for rights and freedoms at
the Maramureș Tribunal granted their preventive arrest for 30 days. [@1,4] The
46-year-old is also investigated for aiding the introduction of risk drugs into
the country in addition to risk-drug trafficking. [@1]

The police statement stressed that both men keep their procedural rights and
guarantees and the presumption of innocence throughout the criminal proceedings.
[@3] Both men are investigated in a risk-drug trafficking case handled by
Maramureș police and DIICOT prosecutors. [@1]
