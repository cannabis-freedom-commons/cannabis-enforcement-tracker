Officers executed nearly 20 search warrants across Fort Wayne, Huntertown, and
Noblesville. [@2] In total, 12 individuals were arrested on felony conspiracy
and narcotics dealing charges. [@2] Investigators described Jermaine Norris as a
known large-scale cocaine trafficker in Fort Wayne. [@1] Investigators conducted
multiple controlled buys from Norris between October 2025 and June 2026. [@1]
The Fort Wayne log shows officers set out to serve the warrants between 5:53 and
6:34 a.m. across all four city quadrants. [@1]

Across the arrests, agencies seized nearly 1,000 grams of suspected marijuana.
[@1] Indiana State Police separately listed nearly 1,000 grams of marijuana
among other controlled substances seized in the operation. [@2] The same totals
listed nearly 18,000 grams of suspected cocaine alongside the marijuana. [@1] At
Marque Weeks’ home in Fort Wayne, officers located a jar of marijuana and two
bags of pills that tested positive for methamphetamine. [@1]

Weeks was charged with conspiracy to commit dealing in cocaine. [@1] Weeks was
also charged with possession of methamphetamine. [@1] The operation involved the
Indiana State Police, Fort Wayne Police Department, Allen County Sheriff’s
Department and federal agencies including the ATF, DEA and FBI. [@2] Officials
had not yet filed charges against Norris. [@1]
