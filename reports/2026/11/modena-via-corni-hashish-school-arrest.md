A young man just over 18 was arrested by the local police for dealing hashish
after being caught handing doses to another youth steps from a school and a gym
frequented by many minors. [@1] The episode dates to the evening of Tuesday 4
November, during a control operation in youth gathering areas and near schools.
[@1] The intervention took place near Istituto Comprensivo 2 in via Corni and
the adjoining gym. [@1]

Officers saw the young man handing several doses of hashish to another youth who
had arrived by car. [@1] A personal search found further doses of the same
substance, already packaged and ready for sale. [@1] Checks were extended to the
young man's home, where officers found a further quantity of cannabis resin.
[@1] All the drugs, together with money deemed proceeds of the activity, were
placed under criminal seizure. [@1]

The young man, who had a clean record, was arrested and then released pending
further judicial developments. [@1] The buyer was also stopped while driving his
own car. [@1] He was cited for the administrative offence of possessing
narcotics for personal use under article 75 of Presidential Decree 309/90, with
immediate withdrawal of his driving licence. [@3] The driver will be reported to
the Modena prefecture as a drug user for evaluation of a licence suspension.
[@2]
