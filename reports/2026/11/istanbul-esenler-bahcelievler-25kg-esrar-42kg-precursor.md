Istanbul police narcotics officers detained two suspects in two separate operations in the Esenler and
Bahçelievler districts of Istanbul within 24 hours. [@1,5,6,7,8,9,10,11,14,15,16] Officers searched a
vehicle in Esenler and a home in Bahçelievler at the same time, going through both in detail.
[@1,6,7,8,11,12,13,14,15,16]

The searches found 25 kilos 90 grams of esrar, the Turkish name for cannabis.
[@1,4,6,7,8,9,10,11,13,14,15,16] They also found 42 kilograms of a chemical the provincial police called
A-M 2201 [@2,4,13,14,15,16], which police said would have been enough to make 4 tonnes 200 kilograms of
synthetic cannabinoid and was stopped before it could be used. [@1,7]

Police said the operations aimed to stop drug production and trade, block street sales and catch those
involved. [@1] They opened proceedings against the two for manufacturing and trading narcotic or
stimulant substances. [@2,5,9,11,16] Anadolu reported that both were sent to court after processing at
the police station [@3]; other accounts said they were still to be referred. [@12,13]
