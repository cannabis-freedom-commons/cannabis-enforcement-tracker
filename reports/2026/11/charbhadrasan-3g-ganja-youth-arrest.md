Charbhadrasan police detained 20-year-old Swadhin Mridha with 3 grams of ganja
on a dirt road behind the old jail at Balia Dangi on the evening of 3 November
2026. [@1] Officers detained him at about 6:45 pm on Tuesday on the unpaved road
behind the old jail in the Balia Dangi Hindu locality of the upazila. [@1] The
detained youth is the son of the late Shakhayat of the Balia Dangi area in
Charbhadrasan upazila. [@1]

Sub-Inspector Mozammel Haque Biswas of Charbhadrasan police station led the
evening operation. [@1] By torchlight, officers recovered one paper-wrapped
packet of ganja from the right pocket of the full pants he was wearing and
seized it under a seizure list. [@1] Police said the recovered ganja weighed 3
grams including the paper and was worth about 200 taka. [@1]

Police said proceedings for legal action against the detained youth were under
way. [@1] Charbhadrasan station chief Md. Safar Ali said police operations
against drugs would continue and no one involved with drugs would be spared.
[@1] He appealed to local people to cooperate with police to build a drug-free
society. [@1]
