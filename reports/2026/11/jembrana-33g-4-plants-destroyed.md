The Jembrana District Attorney's Office, Kejari Jembrana, destroyed evidence
from 39 general-crime cases that had final and binding court judgments on
Monday, 2 November 2026. [@1] Twenty-four of the 39 cases were narcotics cases,
more than half of the total and the largest category destroyed. [@1] The
destruction was carried out under Evidence Destruction Order Number
PRINT-1180/N.1.16.BPA/BPApa.1/10/2026 as execution of final court decisions
under Article 270 of the Criminal Procedure Code. [@1]

The cannabis component destroyed was 33.04 grams net of cannabis and four
cannabis plants. [@1]

Jembrana District Attorney chief Muhammad Ruslan said narcotics cases were the
most numerous among the destroyed case evidence. [@1] Ruslan said the
destruction showed the office carrying out court decisions professionally,
transparently and accountably and was meant to keep the confiscated evidence
from being misused. [@2]
