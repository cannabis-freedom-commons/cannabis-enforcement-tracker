Attock police arrested three alleged drug peddlers in separate operations and
recovered more than five kilograms of hashish from their possession. [@1]
Haseebullah, son of Muazzam Khan, is a resident of Swabi. [@1] Muhammad Yasir,
son of Abdul Malik, is a resident of Attock city. [@1] Qaiser Shah, son of Abdul
Latif, is a resident of Hassan Abdal. [@1] Police registered separate cases
against the accused. [@1]

ASI Shafqat Khan of Rango Police Station and his team arrested Haseebullah
during checking and recovered 2,400 grams of hashish from his possession. [@1]
ASI Asif Ali of Attock City Police Station and his team recovered 1,400 grams of
hashish from Muhammad Yasir, who was subsequently taken into custody. [@1] ASI
Fayyaz Khan of Hassan Abdal Sadar Police Station and his team arrested Qaiser
Shah and seized 1,300 grams of hashish from his possession. [@1] Together the
three amounts total 5,100 grams (5.1 kilograms) of hashish. [@1]

The three operations took place in Attock, Attock city and Hassan Abdal
respectively. [@1] The arrests were reported on 3 November 2026. [@1]
