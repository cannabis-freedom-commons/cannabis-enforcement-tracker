Officers of the Anti Drug and Smuggling Unit arrested 21-year-old Kelen Presley
Marie of Providence and 20-year-old Jean Juliano Magon of Vieux-Grand-Port on 9
November in Bois-des-Amourettes on suspicion of possessing cannabis for sale.
[@1] A 15-year-old from Bois-des-Amourettes who was with them fled when police
arrived. [@1]

Officers stopped the three at about 11:40 on route Royale in Bois-des-Amourettes
while they were in a maroon Toyota Vitz, detaining the two older youths as the
teenager escaped. [@1] A search found two translucent plastic sachets holding a
total of 30.88 grams of cannabis and a Samsung mobile phone. [@1] Officers
secured the vehicle used by the three for the needs of the investigation. [@1]

Police then searched the homes of both young men and of the teenager, the latter
in his mother’s presence, but found no other substance or incriminating exhibit.
[@1] The two young men were held for possession of cannabis with intent to sell
while investigators still had to establish the exact role of the 15-year-old who
escaped. [@1]
