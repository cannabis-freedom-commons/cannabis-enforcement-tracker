Police searching a house on the Sint Martinusstraat in the centre of Beek
arrested a 31-year-old and a 68-year-old man from Colombia on suspicion of
violating the Opiumwet, the Dutch drugs law, on 3 November 2026. [@1] Officers
entered the house in the course of a continuing investigation and found several
jerrycans holding liquids that were at first unidentified. [@3] The two men were
held in custody and questioned, and were due before an investigating judge on
Friday 6 November. [@2]

Fire officers measured the house and found no danger to the surroundings when
the drums were discovered. [@3] Specialists of the Landelijke Faciliteit
Ontmantelen, the national agency that dismantles drug laboratories, then
examined the contents of the containers. [@2] Police said on Thursday 5 November
that the jerrycans held 900 litres of chemicals used in the production of drugs.
[@1] All the jerrycans have been taken away and the house has been declared
safe. [@1]

Police said storing so much of these substances in a residential area risks fire
and explosion for neighbours. [@2] Police did not name the substances or the
kind of drugs they could make, and MijnStreek Vandaag reported that production
of drugs in the house itself had not been established. [@4] The raid was part of
a continuing investigation, but police did not say which case it belongs to.
[@1]
