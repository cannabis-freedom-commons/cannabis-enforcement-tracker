Police in Santana, in Brazil's Amapá state, arrested a 32-year-old man on
suspicion of drug trafficking on Wednesday afternoon, 4 November 2026. [@1] The
arrest was made by the 1st Police Station of Santana during Operation Protetor,
the federal border and state-boundary security programme. [@1] Officers were
looking for suspects in a homicide recorded the day before and surrounded a
villa of small rented rooms on Alameda Líbano in the Fonte Nova neighbourhood.
[@1]

Seeing the police arrive, the suspect threw a bag over the wall into the back of
the property, which he later gave up, and he was caught. [@1] Searches of the
bag, the room and the suspect found a large portion of cannabis plus 135
ready-packaged portions for sale. [@1] Officers also seized a medium cocaine
portion, smaller cocaine portions, 2,044 reais in cash, a precision scale and
plastic bags for packaging the drug. [@1]

The man admitted the drug was his and was arrested in the act for drug
trafficking, with a flagrante record opened at the station. [@1] He was taken to
the 1st Police Station of Santana with the seized material and remained at the
disposition of the courts, which would decide whether to keep him in custody.
[@1] The Civil Police said the homicide investigation behind the operation
continued. [@1]

The Civil Police statement put the cash seized at 2,044 reais and did not say
what quantity of cannabis was found. [@2]
