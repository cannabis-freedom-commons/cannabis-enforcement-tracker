Criminal-investigation officers of the Leningradsky police division, with the
support of Rosgvardia troops, detained three men in the vicinity of Stepnoye
village in the Jewish Autonomous Oblast and seized more than 53 kilograms of
marijuana and parts of cannabis plants from them. [@1,3] An expert examination
found that the seized material contained 25.235 kilograms of marijuana and 27.87
kilograms of parts of cannabis plants, totalling 53.105 kilograms. [@1,3]

Police found and seized six bundles with parts of plants, five bags, and a tarp
with plant material at the scene. [@1,3] Preliminary data indicated that the three
men did not know each other and had arrived in the vicinity of the village
independently, to collect the banned plants. [@2,3]

An investigator at the Leningradsky police division opened a criminal case under
part 2 of article 228 of the Russian Criminal Code, which covers the illegal
acquisition and storage of narcotic drugs in a large amount; the maximum penalty
is up to ten years in prison. [@1,3] The investigation was still under way at the
time of the reports. [@1]
