Faridabad police arrested 116 accused in various cases during a three-day 12th
special weekend campaign. [@1] Police also caught 27 proclaimed offenders (POs)
described as absconding for a long time. [@1] Police traced 22 missing people at
various places and reunited them with their families. [@1] Police presented the
reunions as the most positive aspect of the campaign. [@1]

Police said the drive seized 22.720 kilograms of ganja, the Hindi term for
cannabis. [@1] The same police account lists 183 bottles of country liquor, 5
bottles of English liquor, 4 locally made desi katta pistols and 1 cartridge
among the recoveries. [@1] In action against gambling, police seized Rs 147,450
in cash from 11 accused. [@1]

Police described the campaign as a measure to strengthen law and order and
tighten action against criminals in the district. [@1] Police framed the
seizures as strict action against smugglers and anti-social elements. [@1]
Officials said the campaign against criminals would continue. [@1]
