Meenachigondanandhanambakkam police arrested a youth who was in possession of
ganja near the Nandhambakkam railway station. [@1] Officers found a man standing
suspiciously and took him to the Meenachigondanandhanambakkam police station for
questioning. [@1]

The man was identified as Vinodh, 25, of Anna Nagar, Nandhambakkam. [@1] 1 kg
200 g (1.2 kg) of ganja, the local term for cannabis, was found on him. [@1]

Police registered a case and arrested Vinodh. [@1]
