Civil police from the Draco unit seized 3.6 kilograms of skunk, a high-THC
variety of cannabis, and a firearm while serving a search warrant at a house in
the Progresso neighbourhood of Santa Cruz do Sul on the afternoon of 11 November
2026. [@1] The investigation indicated the house was being used to store drugs
for a criminal faction operating in the region, and officers found 16 packages
plus an additional quantity of skunk, a cannabis variety with a higher
concentration of THC. [@1]

The drugs were hidden at different points of the property, including inside a
buried barrel. [@1] Officers also seized an Argentine-made 9mm pistol with seven
rounds and its serial number erased. [@1] Police also found a portion of white
powder, which a preliminary test did not identify as cocaine; it was sent to the
state forensics institute for analysis. [@1]

The man responsible for the house, aged 29, was not present during the search
and was identified by police; no one was arrested in flagrante. [@3] The
identified man is to answer for drug trafficking and illegal possession of a
restricted-use firearm, aggravated by the erased serial number. [@1] Police said
the investigation would continue to identify others involved in storing and
distributing the drugs. [@1]

Police valued the 3.6 kilograms at at least R$180,000, based on a street price
of R$50 to R$100 per gram. [@1] O Bairrista reported that the operation began at
about 3pm and that police found 17 packages of skunk, as Portal Arauto also
reported. [@2] Portal Arauto reported that the operation began at about 3pm and
that the gun was an Argentine Bersa pistol. [@3] Portal Arauto reported that
officers found 17 packages of skunk, one more than the 16 packages reported by
GAZ. [@3]
