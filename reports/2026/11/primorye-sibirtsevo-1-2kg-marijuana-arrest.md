Police in Sibirtsevo settlement in Chernigovsky district of Primorsky Krai
stopped a woman who was storing cannabis-group narcotics on a large scale. [@1]
The Primorye police press service reported that officers detained the woman
during operational-search measures in the settlement. [@2] The detainee gave
confessional testimony and told police that she had kept the drug for personal
consumption. [@1] Police placed her under a signed pledge not to leave the area
and to behave properly while the investigation continues. [@2]

Officers of the Chernigovsky department of the Russian Interior Ministry found
dried parts of a plant during an examination of premises and the surrounding
area and seized the plant-origin substance. [@2] An expert examination
identified the seized dried material as cannabis (marijuana) with a total weight
of 1.2 kg. [@1] Police treat the 1.2 kg amount as a large scale under the
criminal case. [@2] Investigators opened a criminal case for illegal acquisition
and storage of plants containing narcotic drugs on a large scale without intent
to sell. [@1] The case was opened under Part 2 of Article 228 of the Russian
Criminal Code. [@2]
