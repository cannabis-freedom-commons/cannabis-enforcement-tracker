A 42-year-old man was captured in flagrante by the National Police during a
search and control procedure in the barrio Venezuela neighbourhood in the
municipality of Uribia, La Guajira. [@1] The intervention took place at diagonal
8 with transversal 8, as part of prevention actions carried out by the officers.
[@1]

Police inspected a bag the man was carrying and found inside it 42 grams of
cocaine hydrochloride and 60 grams of marijuana. [@1] The substances were
seized, removing from circulation more than 100 grams of narcotics that were
allegedly intended for sale. [@1]

The man was detained and placed at the disposal of the URI Prosecutor's Office
in Maicao, where he must answer for the alleged offence of drug trafficking,
manufacture or possession. [@1] The National Police said it will continue
surveillance, search and control operations across La Guajira to combat local
drug trafficking and strengthen public safety. [@1]
