El Salvador's Attorney General's Office (FGR) and National Civil Police (PNC)
carried out five raids in San Salvador Centro in an investigation into the use
of minors to sell drugs. [@1] Two people were arrested, and a third arrest
warrant was issued against people accused of forcing teenagers to distribute
illegal substances. [@1] The two held are a man, José Gabriel Cruz Aragón, and a
woman, Zuleyma Beatriz Vásquez Garay, who police said is 36 and was detained in
the barrio Candelaria. [@2] Police said Vásquez Garay was detained in the barrio
Candelaria, a neighbourhood of San Salvador Centro. [@2]

The two are to be prosecuted for use of people in a vulnerable situation for
crime, ill-treatment of children and trafficking in persons, and for possession
with intent to traffic. [@1] The case began in October 2026, when officers
caught a 15-year-old boy selling drugs and he said adults beat him and forced
him to distribute the substances. [@1] A San Salvador prosecutor's office
representative said the searches covered properties in different parts of the
capital. [@1] During the raids the FGR seized cash, portions of drugs, mobile
phones and other items. [@1]

The FGR said the seized items would support the case it will present to the
courts. [@1] According to El Diario de Hoy, both will be brought before court,
and the FGR is still investigating whether there are more suspects or more
children affected. [@3]
