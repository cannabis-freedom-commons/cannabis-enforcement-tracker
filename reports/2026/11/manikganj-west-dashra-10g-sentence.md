A mobile court in the West Dashra area of Manikganj Sadar sentenced 38-year-old
Md. Al-Amin to 15 days of imprisonment and a 100-taka fine for consuming drugs
and creating social disorder through that consumption. [@1]

Officers recovered and seized 10 grams of ganja, the local term for cannabis,
from his own house during the anti-drug mobile-court drive. [@1] The operation
took place on Wednesday 11 November 2026 at the man's own house in the West
Dashra area, organised by the Manikganj district office of the Department of
Narcotics Control, the national anti-drug agency. [@1]

The convicted man, Md. Al-Amin, is the son of the late Abul Hossain and Aleya
Akter of the West Dashra area. [@1]
