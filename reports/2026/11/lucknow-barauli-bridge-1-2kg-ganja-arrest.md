Police in Lucknow arrested a 20-year-old man from Mohanlalganj during a check of
suspicious people and vehicles under the Barauli bridge in the PGI police
station area. [@1,4] The man was identified as Amit Yadav, 20, a resident of Dadha
village in Mohanlalganj. [@1,4] Police said they seized 1.2 kg of ganja, the Hindi
term for cannabis, from his pocket and the e-scooter's footwell. [@1,4]

Vrindavan Chowki officer-in-charge Sumit Bansal was leading the police team that
made the arrest on an informer's tip-off. [@1] The ganja was packed in black
polythene in his pocket and in the footwell of the scooter. [@1] Police also
seized a green e-scooter that carried no number plate. [@1] Amit Yadav told
police he was addicted to drugs and sold ganja to passers-by to pay for his own
use. [@1] Police records showed an earlier NDPS Act case registered against him
at the PGI station in 2025. [@1] A case was registered against him under the
NDPS Act and he was produced before a court. [@1]

Swatantra Prabhat gave the same seized quantity as 1.2 kilograms in black
polythene. [@2] Swatantra Prabhat named the arrested man as Amit Yadav, 20, son
of Suryabali Yadav of Dada Sikandarpur village under Mohanlalganj police
station. [@2]

Triangle News Lucknow, citing the Lucknow police press note, gave the case
number as 575/2026 under sections 8 and 20 of the NDPS Act. [@3] Triangle News
Lucknow said the arrest was part of a campaign against the illegal drug trade in
the capital. [@3] The same press note referred to an earlier case, number
618/2025 under sections 20, 29 and 8 of the NDPS Act, registered against him at
the PGI station. [@3]
