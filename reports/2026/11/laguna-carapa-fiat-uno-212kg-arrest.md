Military Police officers in Laguna Carapã, a municipality in southern Mato
Grosso do Sul, seized 199 tablets of marijuana weighing about 212 kg during a
traffic check on Avenida Brasil on the morning of Thursday 5 November 2026. [@1]
Police valued the load at about R$ 424,000, a preliminary estimate. [@1] The
officers stopped a blue Fiat Uno because of apparent irregularities in the
vehicle's structure and found that the 20-year-old driver had no driver's
licence. [@1] The driver was arrested in the act for drug trafficking. [@1]

The young man told police he had picked up the already-loaded car at a petrol
station in Ponta Porã, a city on the border with Paraguay, and was to deliver it
to Dourados for a payment of R$ 20,000. [@1] Police said they found the tablets
packed inside the car during a search of the vehicle. [@1]

The driver and the drug were taken to the Depac police station in Dourados,
where the arrest was formally registered. [@1] The car stayed seized at the
Civil Police station in Laguna Carapã for forensic and traffic procedures. [@1]
The Laguna Carapã unit is subordinate to the 2nd Independent Military Police
Company, based in Caarapó. [@1] The suspect's name was not released. [@1]
