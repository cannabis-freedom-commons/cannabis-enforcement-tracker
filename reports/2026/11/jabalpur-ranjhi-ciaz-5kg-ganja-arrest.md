Ranjhi police in Jabalpur signalled a suspicious Ciaz car coming from the
Amarkantak and Dindori direction to stop. [@1] Officers carried out a detailed
search of the car and recovered packets of ganja from inside it. [@1] Police
arrested two smugglers and seized the ganja, reported as worth around two and a
half lakh rupees. [@1] Police registered a case against all three people in the
car. [@1]

The weighed ganja totalled 5 kg 131 g, with an estimated value of Rs 256,500.
[@1] The driver was identified as 36-year-old Balkishan Nagvanshi of Pipariya,
Narmadapuram. [@1] The other two occupants were identified as 25-year-old Manish
Kumar Rajak and 24-year-old Anand Srivas, both of Gohalpur, Jabalpur. [@1]

The driver claimed he was returning after dropping tourists at Amarkantak and
said the two men took a lift to Jabalpur for Rs 500 after missing their bus.
[@1] Police described that account as fabricated and said they were questioning
the accused to establish the real facts. [@1] Police said they were
investigating where the ganja had been brought from and to whom it was to be
supplied. [@1]
