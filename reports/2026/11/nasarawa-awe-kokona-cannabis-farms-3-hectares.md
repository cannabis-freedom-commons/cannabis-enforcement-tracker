The Nasarawa State command of Nigeria's National Drug Law Enforcement Agency
(NDLEA) destroyed cannabis farms in Awe and Kokona Local Government Areas, the
command's new head said on 3 November 2026. [@1] The commander, CN Saidu Daniel,
put each farm at about 1.5 hectares, giving roughly three hectares in all across
the two areas. [@1] Daniel said the farms were destroyed because the growers
would not be allowed to harvest, saying the crop would otherwise reach the
market. [@2]

Nasarawa State Governor Abdullahi Sule directed the agency to search out and
destroy cannabis farms on the stretches of ungoverned land in parts of the
state, where he said people set up farms deep in the forests. [@1] At the same
meeting Sule urged the agency to identify and destroy more illegal farms so that
cannabis is not cultivated and distributed in the state. [@2] The mandate was
given during Daniel's familiarisation visit to Government House in Lafia, the
state capital, where he asked the state to help set up a rehabilitation centre
for drug and alcohol users in the city. [@1] Daniel thanked the governor for
land given to the agency at Azuba, where it destroys seized drugs and other
exhibits. [@3]

Sule backed the awareness campaign against drug abuse among children and the
proposed rehabilitation centre, and asked Daniel to submit a detailed proposal
for the centre. [@3]

Daniel asked the state government to back a rehabilitation centre for drug and
alcohol users in Lafia. [@3]
