The Sofia City Prosecutor's Office has sent an indictment to court against a man
accused of holding drugs for distribution and selling part of them. [@1,4]
Prosecutors say he sold part of the drugs to two people. [@1,2,5] The case has been
transferred to the Sofia City Court, which has not yet set a date for a
preliminary hearing. [@1,2,3,6]

According to the prosecution, on 14 June 2024 the man held 3,145.67 grams of
marijuana, 53.82 grams of hashish and 60.50 grams of cocaine in a flat in the
capital's Hipodruma district. [@1,2,3,6] The reported weight is net weight. [@1,6] The
indictment values all the substances together at 45,825.04 euros, the
prosecution's figure for the mixed seizure. [@1,3,6]

The cannabis component recorded here is about 3.2 kilograms of marijuana and
hashish combined, while the cocaine is noted only to explain the same mixed
case. [@1] No court outcome has been reported yet, with the preliminary hearing
still unscheduled. [@1]

The accused is identified by the initials Й. А. [@3,7] On the same date he sold nearly 5 grams of marijuana to one person and two quantities of cocaine totalling 1.32 grams to another person. [@3,7] The investigation was conducted by officers of 06 RU-SDVR under the supervision of the Sofia City Prosecutor's Office. [@3] The prosecution qualifies the conduct as a continuing offence with three separate acts under Article 354a(2) in connection with Article 354a(1) and Article 26(1) of the Criminal Code. [@3]
