On the night of Thursday 12 November 2026, Military Police dismantled a
clandestine greenhouse used to cultivate cannabis in a house in the Carianos
neighbourhood of Florianópolis. [@1] One man was arrested in flagrante for drug
trafficking. [@1]

According to the 4th Military Police Battalion, the action began after a report
that the property was being used to grow the drug, and with a neighbour's
authorisation officers checked over the wall and confirmed the planting in the
backyard. [@1] The property's responsible person let the officers in, and inside
they found dozens of plants in cultivation, a complete greenhouse structure,
portions of already-harvested drug and bottles of oil extracted from the plant.
[@1]

What was seized: 55 cannabis plants; 245 grams of skunk; 2 bottles of oil
extracted from the plant; materials used in the assembly and maintenance of the
greenhouse. [@1] The man was taken, along with the plants, the drugs and the
equipment, to the Capital's on-duty police station (CPP), where the case was
registered. [@1]
