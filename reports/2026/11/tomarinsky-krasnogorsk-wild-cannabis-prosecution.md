A man from Tomarinsky district goes on trial over wild cannabis he found and
kept for himself. [@1] The Tomarinsky district prosecutor approved the
indictment in the case against the local man. [@2] He is accused under part 1 of
article 228 of the Russian Criminal Code, which covers obtaining and storing
drugs without intent to sell in a significant amount. [@1]

Prosecutors say that in August 2026 the man found a wild drug-containing plant
in the village of Krasnogorsk, Tomarinsky district. [@2] He carried it off and
kept it in a shed on the land of his partner, where it stayed until police
seized it. [@2] The seized plant was taken by officers of the Tomarinsky police
department, ОМВД России «Томаринский». [@3]

The case has been sent to the Uglegorsk city court for trial at its permanent
session in Tomari. [@2] The offence carries up to three years in prison, and he
has not yet been sentenced. [@1] An earlier release from the same prosecutor's
office, on 30 October, described the man as finding the cannabis in a forest and
keeping it at his home address. [@4]
