Police arrested a man travelling as a passenger on a public bus from Medellín to
Andes in the municipality of Amagá, Antioquia, and found about 500 doses of
marijuana in his hand luggage. [@1] The arrest happened at a police checkpoint
on the La Mansa–Primavera road, at the La Siria roundabout, with officers
searching the passenger's hand luggage. [@1] Minuto30 placed the same checkpoint
on the La Mansa–Primavera road at the La Siria roundabout and reported the same
arrest and quantity. [@2] The Antioquia Police Department said the operation was
carried out under Plan Cosecha, a seasonal security campaign run during harvest
periods. [@1]

The arrested man and the seized marijuana were handed to the Attorney General's
Office on suspicion of trafficking, manufacturing or carrying narcotics. [@1]
Colonel Luis Fernando Serna Zapata, commander of the Antioquia Police
Department, announced the result in Amagá. [@1] No weight in grams or kilograms
was published for the marijuana. [@1]
