Two people were arrested during an anti-drug raid in the Che la Reina
neighborhood of Ciudad del Este on the night of Thursday 12 November 2026, as
part of the Plan SUMAR. [@1] The raid, carried out by agents of the Alto Paraná
Departmental Division of the specialized anti-drug-trafficking department, found
cocaine, marijuana, dosing equipment and G. 1,841,000 in cash in the searched
home. [@1] The arrested couple were identified as Jorge Antonio Trinidad Báez,
30, and Dahiana Beatriz Larrea Leite, 31. [@1] Police seized 8.7 grams of
suspected marijuana, equivalent to about eight doses, and 26.7 grams of
suspected cocaine in two portions. [@1]

Police also found two precision scales, mobile phones, polyethylene bags, pipes,
a marijuana grinder and other items used for preparing and consuming drugs. [@1]
Police put the value of the drug and related evidence at G. 15,336,000, and the
case went to prosecutor Luis Fernando Escobar of the specialized
anti-drug-trafficking unit. [@1] The raid was carried out by agents of the Alto
Paraná Departmental Division of the specialized anti-drug-trafficking
department, and the case was assigned to prosecutor Luis Fernando Escobar of the
specialized anti-drug-trafficking unit. [@1]
