Units of the Córdoba Police Department (Departamento de Policía Córdoba)
arrested seven people implicated in drug trafficking during operations across
the municipalities of Lorica, Planeta Rica, Montelíbano and Tierralta. [@1] The
detainees were placed at the disposal of the Attorney General's Office (Fiscalía
General de la Nación) while their legal status is decided. [@1,2] Police carried
out the operations through surveillance, search, control and criminal
investigation work aimed at striking drug trafficking that authorities say also
finances illegal armed groups. [@1]

The largest seizure in flagrante took place in the corregimiento of La Doctrina,
a rural subdivision of the municipality of Lorica, where officers intercepted
one man. [@1] He was carrying a transparent plastic container with a red lid
holding 12 marijuana cigarettes individually wrapped in aluminium foil, three
medium blue zip-lock plastic bags of cocaine hydrochloride, and 13 small
zip-lock bags of bazuco, a smokable coca paste. [@1] Officers also seized
631,500 Colombian pesos in cash from him, which police attributed to drug sales.
[@1]

In Planeta Rica, police detained four men in the 12 de Enero, La Inmaculada,
Santander and Gonzalo Mejía neighbourhoods and seized a combined 12.6 grams of
marijuana, 3.8 grams of bazuco and 1.9 grams of coca base. [@1,2] In the Villa
Olímpica sector of Montelíbano, the Basic Criminal Investigation Unit (Unidad
Básica de Investigación Criminal) arrested one man and seized eight grams of
marijuana. [@1,2] In the El Prado neighbourhood of Tierralta, Judicial Police
(Policía Judicial) officers executed an arrest warrant against another man
wanted for trafficking, manufacture or possession of narcotics. [@1,2]

The Córdoba Police Department reported that so far in 2026 it had made 647
arrests for drug possession, a 50 percent increase over the same period the
previous year, and had seized more than 755 kilograms of marijuana across the
department. [@1,2]
