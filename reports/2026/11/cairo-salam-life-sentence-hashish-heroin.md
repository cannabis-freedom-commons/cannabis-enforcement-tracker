Cairo Criminal Court on Sunday sentenced a defendant to life imprisonment for
possessing half a kilo of heroin, a large quantity of hashish, a firearm,
cartridges and ammunition in the Al-Salam area. [@1] The prosecution had charged
him with possessing and trafficking narcotics and possessing an unlicensed
firearm outside the cases permitted by law. [@1] A second account identifies the
convicted man as an unemployed person and says the possession was for
trafficking in the Al-Salam police district. [@2]

The chemical laboratory report said the seized items included a transparent
plastic bag containing brown pieces proven to be hashish listed in Schedule 1 of
the drug-control law. [@1] The same report confirmed seized heroin powder with
positive samples for narcotic derivatives. [@1]

Cairo security forces acted on information that a person was possessing and
trafficking drugs, investigated, set the necessary ambushes and arrested an
unemployed man with a criminal record. [@1] A search found a large quantity of
drugs, and the man admitted possessing them for profit and trafficking, with
money from sales and a mobile phone for contacting clients, after which legal
measures were taken. [@1]
