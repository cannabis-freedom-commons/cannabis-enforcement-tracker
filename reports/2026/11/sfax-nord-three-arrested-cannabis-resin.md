Police officers of the National Security district of Sfax Nord arrested three
people on the evening of 5 November 2026 on suspicion of belonging to a
drug-trafficking network. [@1] Two of the three were already wanted by different
security units over suspected involvement in similar cases. [@1]

The operation followed intelligence work and field surveillance, a security
source told Radio Diwan FM. [@1] Tunisie Now reported that the surveillance let
officers locate the suspects before they moved in to arrest them. [@2]

Officers seized a plaque and a half of cannabis resin, known in Tunisia as
zattla, along with an electronic scale and tools suspected of being used for
dealing. [@1] Police also took a large sum of money suspected to be proceeds of
the trafficking. [@1]
