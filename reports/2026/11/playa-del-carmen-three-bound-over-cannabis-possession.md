Three men were bound over for trial in Playa del Carmen, Quintana Roo, on
suspicion of simple possession of cannabis, an offence against health. [@1] A
judge ordered the three held in justified pretrial detention while the criminal
proceedings continue. [@1]

According to the Quintana Roo state prosecutor's office, investigation agents
found the men by the roadside on the Playa Paraíso highway in the municipality
of Playa del Carmen, allegedly handling substances with the characteristics of
possible narcotics. [@1] After the intervention the three were handed to the
public prosecutor's office, which assembled the evidence and brought them before
a control judge. [@1]

The three stay in custody while the investigation continues and the case goes to
trial. [@1]
