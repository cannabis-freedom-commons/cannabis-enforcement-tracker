Military Police officers arrested a man suspected of drug trafficking in
Conjunto Jardim, in Nossa Senhora do Socorro, Greater Aracaju, on the afternoon
of Sunday 8 November 2026. [@1] The approach was made by officers of the Radio
Patrol Police Battalion (BPRp) during a patrol of the area. [@1] The officers
saw two men beside a garage in what looked like the handover of a package. [@1]
One of the men ran off across an empty lot and was not found, while the other
was stopped and was carrying portions of marijuana. [@1]

The man told officers he sold the drug and said there was more inside the
garage. [@1] In the garage officers found about 610 grams of marijuana, a
precision scale, a roll of cling film and R$9 in cash. [@1] The suspect and all
the seized material were taken to the Central de Flagrantes, the station where
arrests in the act are registered, for the legal steps to be taken. [@1]
