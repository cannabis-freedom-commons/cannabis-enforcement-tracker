Police in the Thuthibari police-station area of Maharajganj district, on the Indo-Nepal border,
stopped a Scorpio near Lohrauli village and arrested a smuggler at the scene. [@1,2,4,14] The first
reports put the ganja found at about one quintal, or 100 kg [@1,2], weighed on the spot at about one
and a quarter quintals. [@4] Acting on an informer's tip-off during a campaign against trafficking
along the border, the team threw a cordon round the area. [@3] Thuthibari police worked with ATS
units from Siddharthnagar and Gorakhpur near Naubari village, under Superintendent of Police Shakti
Mohan Avasthy. [@9,11,13,15,17,19,20,21] The operation was led by Circle Officer Nichlaul Ravindra
Kumar Singh and overseen by Additional Superintendent Siddharth [@14]; the station team was led by
station house officer Amit Kumar Singh. [@20] One account places it on the eve of Diwali, 8 November.
[@14] Police announced the case on Thursday 5 November 2026. [@9]

## The seizure

The ganja was in red bags in the Scorpio, number UP32 LB 9957 [@4], registered in Lucknow. [@20]
Officers weighed it at 1 quintal 22 kg 975 g [@18,19], seized the 122.975 kg and took the vehicle into
custody. [@9,11,13,14,15,16,17,20,21] One account calls the vehicle a car. [@12] Police put the
international-market value at about Rs 60 lakh. [@10,11,15,16,21] They said the smugglers took a woman
along to avoid suspicion and used different stickers, banners and flags to make the vehicle harder
to identify [@18,19], and that the ganja had come from Nepal and was to go via Kushinagar to Bihar.
[@8,19]

## The accused

Early reports said a woman was arrested at the scene [@2,5,6,7], that three people had been in the
vehicle, and that the roles of the other two were not confirmed. [@2,4] The two arrested were Naimun
Nisha alias Jahantara, 49, of Chainpatti village, and Rajendra Gaur alias Ravindra, 50, of Azad
Nagar, both in Kushinagar district. [@9,10,11,14,15,16,17,19,20,21] Gaur already faced NDPS cases
registered in 2020 at Vishunpura police station in Kushinagar and in 2023 at Rudhauli in Basti
district. [@9,11,13,15,16,17,20]

Police registered case 150/2026 at Thuthibari police station under sections 8, 20 and 29 of the NDPS
Act and produced both before a court [@9,10,11,12,14,15,21], which sent them to jail. [@16] They were
investigating where the ganja came from and the wider network. [@2,4,6,8]
