Police in the Thuthibari police-station area of Maharajganj district, on the
Indo-Nepal border, stopped a Scorpio near Lohrauli village and arrested a
smuggler at the scene. [@1,2,4,14] Officers found about one quintal, or 100 kg, of
ganja — cannabis in dried form — inside the vehicle during the search. [@1,2]

One woman smuggler was reported arrested at the scene. [@2,5,6,7] Three people were reported to have been in the vehicle allegedly used for the smuggling. [@2,4] There was still no official confirmation about the roles of the other two people or whether they were arrested. [@2,4] Police were investigating the source of the seized ganja and the full smuggling network, including where it was brought from and where it was to be supplied. [@2,4,6,8]

Amar Ujala reported that a police team acting on a tip-off from an informer threw a cordon around the area near Lohrauli and stopped a suspicious Scorpio. [@3] The newspaper said the interception came during a campaign against drug trafficking along the India-Nepal border. [@3]

Police stopped Scorpio number UP32 LB 9957 and found the ganja in red bags inside it. [@4] Officers weighed the ganja on an electronic scale at the scene at about one-and-a-quarter quintals. [@4]

Officers seized 122.975 kg of ganja from the Scorpio and took the vehicle into custody. [@9,11,13,14,15,16,17,20,21] The arrested pair were identified as 49-year-old Naimun Nisha alias Jahantara of Chainpatti village and 50-year-old Rajendra Gaur alias Ravindra of Azad Nagar, both in Kushinagar district. [@9,10,11,14,15,16,17,19,20,21] Thuthibari police acted jointly with ATS units from Siddharthnagar and Gorakhpur near Naubari village after a tip-off, under Superintendent of Police Shakti Mohan Avasthy. [@9,11,13,15,17,19,20,21] Police put the international-market value of the seized ganja at about Rs 60 lakh. [@10,11,15,16,21] Police registered a case at Thuthibari police station under Sections 8, 20 and 29 of the NDPS Act and produced the accused before a court. [@9,11,14,15,21] Gaur already faced NDPS cases from 2020 in Kushinagar and 2023 in Basti district. [@9,11,13,15,17,20] Investigators said the consignment had been brought from Nepal and was to be taken via Kushinagar to Bihar. [@8,19]

A case was registered against both accused at Thuthibari police station under the NDPS Act, and both were being produced in court. [@10,12] The vehicle stopped near the village was reported as a car, not a Scorpio. [@12]

The case was filed at Thuthibari police station as crime number 150/2026, a file reference the earlier reports did not give. [@14] Police said the operation was led by Circle Officer Nichlaul Ravindra Kumar Singh and overseen by Additional Superintendent of Police Siddharth, with Thuthibari police and ATS teams from Siddharthnagar and Gorakhpur. [@14] The report places the interception on the eve of Diwali, which fell on 8 November 2026. [@14]

The same Oneindia report's second operation was a 4 November heroin seizure in Sonauli, a separate event not part of this cannabis case. [@15] PTI, republished by Rediff.com, reported the police statement made on Thursday 5 November 2026, two days before Oneindia's account. [@9]

Nisha lives in Chainpatti under Taryan Sujan police station and Gaur lives in Ward No 7, Azad Nagar under Sevarhi police station in Kushinagar district. [@16] The court sent both accused to jail after police produced them. [@16] Gaur's earlier cases were registered at Vishunpura police station in 2020 and Rudhauli police station in Basti district in 2023. [@16]

Police said the suspects took a woman along in the Scorpio to avoid suspicion and used different stickers, banners and flags on the vehicle to make it harder to identify. [@18,19] The team stopped the Scorpio and weighed the ganja inside it at 1 quintal 22 kg 975 g. [@18,19]

The Scorpio recovered at the scene was registered in Lucknow, a vehicle detail other accounts of the seizure did not publish. [@20] The Thuthibari police team that stopped the Scorpio was led by station house officer Amit Kumar Singh, who has not been named in earlier accounts of the case. [@20]
