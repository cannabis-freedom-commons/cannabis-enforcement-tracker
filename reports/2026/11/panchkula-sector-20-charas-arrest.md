Panchkula's Anti-Narcotic Cell team arrested a youth at the Sector-20 grain
market after receiving information that drugs were being supplied there. [@1]
Police identified the arrested youth as Anil Kumar, a resident of New Indira
Colony in Manimajra, Chandigarh. [@1] Police registered a case against Kumar at
Sector-20 police station in Panchkula. [@1]

Police said they had received information on 2 November that Kumar supplied
drugs at the Sector-20 grain market. [@1] Acting on that information, a police
team raided the market to catch the alleged supplier. [@1] Officers caught the
accused at about 2:10 pm while he was standing near a wall beside the water tank
in the market. [@1] A search of the accused found charas kept in the pocket of
his trousers. [@1] Police put the recovered amount at 401 grams of charas. [@1]
Police took the recovered charas into custody and arrested Kumar at the spot.
[@1]

Further investigation of the case was continuing. [@1]
