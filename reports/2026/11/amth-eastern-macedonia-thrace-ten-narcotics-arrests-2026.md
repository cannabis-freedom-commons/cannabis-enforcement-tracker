Police in Eastern Macedonia and Thrace arrested ten people, six Greeks and four
foreign nationals, between 5 and 11 November 2026 in targeted anti-drug
operations, opening nine separate case files on drug-law violations. [@1]

On the morning of 5 November 2026 in a settlement of Kavala, Kavala's narcotics
squad arrested a Greek man who kept a small quantity of cannabis in a storage
room near his home. [@1] With a police drug-detection dog, officers then found
and seized six cannabis plants about one metre tall, which he had cultivated in
a fenced area near the house. [@1]

On the afternoon of 10 November 2026 at the Drama detention facility for
foreigners, officers arrested a foreign national after finding 25.98 grams of
the synthetic drug type called BONZAI in a parcel addressed to him. [@1]

On 11 November 2026 in Drama, following information about drug dealing in the
area, officers arrested a Greek man and seized three nylon packages of raw
cannabis weighing 646 grams hidden in his home, six more packages weighing 37.3
grams found in his car, a precision scale and a sum of cash. [@1]

In further checks over the same period, officers arrested one foreigner in the
Evros area, two Greeks in Komotini, one Greek in the Thessaloniki area and one
Greek in Kavala, seizing small quantities of raw cannabis, amphetamine and an
anaesthetic substance, one cannabis cigarette, one precision scale and one drug
tablet. [@1]

The case files were submitted to the competent prosecutors, and the preliminary
inquiries were carried out by the police departments in Alexandroupoli, Drama,
Kavala and Komotini. [@1]

The Drama seizure is confirmed by a second account: the same three packages of
646 grams of raw cannabis in the home and six packages of 37.3 grams in the car,
along with a precision scale and cash. [@2]
