Nawabpet police detained one man at the Nawabpet village bus stand after finding
100 grams of ganja in his possession. [@1] The man was identified as 26-year-old
Mohammed Mustafa of Natraj Nagar, Gangabowli in Jeedimetla, Hyderabad, son of
M.D. Pasha. [@1] Sub-Inspector K. Giri led the police team that reached the spot
and questioned the suspicious-looking man. [@1]

Police said they acted on information received about a person holding ganja at
the bus stand on Monday afternoon at about 5:00. [@1] The 100 grams of ganja was
found in a plastic cover the man was carrying. [@1] The report calls the seized
drug ganja. [@1] Police took Mustafa and the seized ganja to the station and
registered a case. [@1]

SI Giri warned that illegal transport, sale or use of banned intoxicants such as
ganja will draw strict legal action. [@1] He urged youth to stay away from
intoxicants and asked people in rural areas to inform police about any such
sales. [@1]
