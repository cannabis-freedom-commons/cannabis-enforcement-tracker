San Luis Potosí's Guardia Civil Estatal (GCE), the state civil guard, detained
32 people in operations carried out in the last few hours. [@1] The detentions
were part of the force's permanent security and crime-prevention operations
deployed across the four regions of the state. [@1] The force described those
detained as suspects in probable criminal acts that undermine public order and
tranquillity. [@1]

During the same operations, officers seized 160.6 grams of marijuana. [@1] The
same interventions also yielded 53 doses of the drug known locally as “cristal”.
[@1] Only the separately stated 160.6 grams of marijuana is counted as cannabis
here, while the cristal doses are excluded from the cannabis total. [@1]

The operations were reported from San Luis Potosí, with the force referring only
to the state's four regions and naming no municipality for the detentions or
seizures. [@1] The seizure totals reported for these operations were the 53
cristal doses and the 160.6 grams of marijuana. [@1]
