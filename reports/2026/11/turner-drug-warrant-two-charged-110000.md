Two men were charged with drug offences after ACT Policing searched a home in
Turner, an inner Canberra suburb, at about 1pm on Saturday 7 November. [@1] The
resident, a 35-year-old Turner man, was charged with one count of trafficking a
trafficable quantity of cannabis. [@1]

He also faces four counts of knowingly being concerned in trafficking controlled
drugs other than cannabis, two counts of possessing an unauthorised prohibited
weapon and two counts of possessing property suspected of being proceeds of
crime. [@1] Police say they seized clip-seal bags of dried cannabis along with
methylamphetamine, ketamine, cocaine, MDMA and heroin. [@1]

The second man, who was at the house, was arrested on an outstanding warrant,
and a small amount of methamphetamine was found when his bag was searched at the
ACT Watch House. [@1] He was subsequently charged with trafficking a controlled
drug other than cannabis. [@1]

Officers also seized about $110,000 in cash, a laptop, three mobile phones, a
cash-counting machine, a taser, two gun-shaped lighters, and a white Audi RSQ3
and a BMW X5 that police suspect were bought with the proceeds of crime. [@1]

The second man also applied for bail, and the court was told he had been charged
with trafficking methamphetamine and joint commission theft. [@2]
