Detectives from Polkowice, in Lower Silesia, went to a flat in Kłodzko County on
5 November 2026 to arrest a 42-year-old man wanted on an arrest warrant. [@1] At
the flat they found two men, one of them the wanted man, who was to serve 98
days in prison. [@1] The officers noticed the smell of marijuana and drying
plants hanging in the flat. [@1] Marijuana and a cannabis bush were found in the
flat, and the plants were sent for laboratory tests. [@1]

Police detained two men in the operation. [@1] A 44-year-old was detained and
charged with possessing intoxicating substances and cultivating cannabis other
than fibre hemp. [@1] The man admitted the offence, and the charge carries up to
three years in prison. [@1] The account was issued as a statement of the
Polkowice County Police Headquarters, signed by sub-inspector Magdalena
Wiaderek. [@1]
