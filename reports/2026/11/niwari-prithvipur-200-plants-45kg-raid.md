Prithvipur police arrested a 62-year-old man during a raid on an illegal
cannabis plot in Naktena Khirka, near Biraura hill, in Niwari district of Madhya
Pradesh. [@1] Police said the man admitted in questioning that he grew and
traded cannabis together with the field's owner. [@1] The field's registered
owner is a fugitive co-accused, and police said teams had been sent to arrest
him. [@2]

Officers seized about 200 green cannabis plants, dried cannabis and seeds with a
combined weight of 45 kg 450 g. [@1] According to Dainik Bhaskar, the haul came
from a one-bigha field and included about 1 kg of dried cannabis and 7 kg 920 g
of cannabis seeds. [@2] A bigha is a local land measure of roughly 0.25
hectares, the unit Bhaskar used to describe the plot. [@2]

Police valued the seized cannabis at about 40,000 to 50,000 rupees. [@1] The
raid followed a tip-off to the Prithvipur station house officer about cannabis
being grown and dried cannabis being sold at the spot. [@2] The operation was
carried out on the directions of Niwari superintendent of police Rai Singh
Narvariya, and police registered a case under the Narcotic Drugs and
Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act. [@1] Police were preparing to produce the
arrested man before a court and said the investigation would establish who else
was involved in the trade and how long it had run. [@2]
