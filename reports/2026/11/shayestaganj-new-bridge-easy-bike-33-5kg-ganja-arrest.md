SRB-9, a police Special Response Battalion, arrested Vishnu Chandra Kar, 33,
with 33.5 kg of ganja in Chunarughat upazila, Habiganj district. [@1,3,4,5,6] The detention took place at 10:20 p.m. on Monday 9 November at a checkpost on the paved road beside the new Shayestaganj bridge in Ubahata Union. [@3] The checkpost was on the old Dhaka–Sylhet highway in No. 7 Ubahata Union. [@4] Rising
Sylhet, a second Bengali outlet, gave the same suspect's name and age. [@2]

SRB-9 media officer and additional superintendent of police K M Shahidul Islam
Sohag gave the account in a press statement. [@1,3,6] He is a resident of the
Pakuria area of Chunarughat upazila. [@1,3,4] He was identified as the son of the late Upendra Kar of Pakuria village. [@4,5,6] The team from the Shayestaganj camp of
SRB-9's CPC-3 acted on a tip and signalled a battery-run easy bike coming from
Chunarughat bazaar to stop; a passenger got out, tried to flee and was caught
after a chase. [@1,6] Officers recovered the 33.5 kg of ganja wrapped in scotch tape from a plastic sack on the passenger seat and seized the easy bike used to carry it. [@3,6]

SRB-9 said that in preliminary questioning the detained man stated he had collected the ganja from a border area and kept it to sell. [@3,4] The seized consignment was described as Indian ganja, and he also told interrogators he had been trafficking drugs to different parts of the country for a long time. [@4] A case was filed under the Narcotics Control Act, and the detained man, recovered ganja and seized easy bike were handed over to Chunarughat police station. [@3,5,6] Chunarughat police station officer-in-charge Mohammad Saiful Islam confirmed the incident. [@4]
