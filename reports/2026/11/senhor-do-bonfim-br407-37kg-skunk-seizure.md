Federal Highway Police officers arrested two men in the early hours of Friday 6
November 2026 on the BR-407 in Senhor do Bonfim, Bahia, for drug trafficking and
association for trafficking. [@1] The approach took place at about 00:50 at
kilometre 117 of the BR-407, where officers stopped two passenger cars
travelling together. [@1] The officers smelled a strong smell of marijuana when
the windows of one car were opened. [@1]

Inside one car they found about 20 kg of marijuana in 35 packages and 17 kg of
skunk in 31 plastic bags. [@1] Using its official valuation method, the Federal
Highway Police put the loss to organised crime at R$ 121,600, of which R$ 40,000
for the marijuana and R$ 81,600 for the skunk. [@1] The drivers said they
collected the drugs in the Petrolina, Pernambuco, and Juazeiro, Bahia, area and
were taking them to Salvador, where they would be paid for the transport. [@1]

One of the drivers said he worked as a lookout (batedor), driving ahead to warn
the other car about police checks. [@1] The men, the drugs and the two cars were
taken to the Civil Police station in Senhor do Bonfim, where the arrest in the
act was formalised. [@1]

Bahia Notícias reported the seizure as 37 kilos of marijuana with skunk, without
separating the two substances. [@2]
