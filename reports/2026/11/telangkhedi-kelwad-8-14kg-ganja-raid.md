Nagpur Rural Police's NDPS team busted an alleged ganja trafficking racket in
Telangkhedi village under Kelwad police station jurisdiction. [@1,2] The operation
was conducted on November 4, 2026. [@1] The accused has been identified as Bandu
Krishnaji Kshirsagar, a resident of Telangkhedi village. [@1,2] The team acted
after receiving a tip-off that Kshirsagar had allegedly stocked ganja at his
residence and was selling it. [@1] The team raided his house and recovered 5.088
kg of ganja valued at Rs 1,27,200. [@1,2] During questioning, the accused
allegedly disclosed that he had also cultivated ganja plants in his agricultural
field. [@1]

Following the disclosure, police searched the field and discovered two ganja
plants weighing 3.052 kg with an estimated value of Rs 76,300. [@1,2] The total
seizure of ganja from the residence and agricultural field stood at 8.140 kg
besides the other articles. [@1,2] The seizure also included a Lava keypad mobile
phone worth Rs 2,500, a chillum valued at Rs 50 and plastic bags allegedly used
for storing the narcotic substance. [@1] The ganja and other materials were
collectively valued at Rs 2,06,130 in an action against a 64-year-old man
allegedly involved in storing and selling the contraband. [@1]

The operation was carried out under the guidance of Nagpur Rural Superintendent
of Police Harssh Poddar, Additional SP Anil Mhaske, Sub-Divisional Police
Officer Sagar Kharde and Local Crime Branch Police Inspector Ankush Mane. [@1]
Kelwad police personnel including API Ashish Singh Thakur, PSI Arun Rathod,
Shivaji Kadam, Assistant Sub-Inspector Sandeep Nagre and Nitesh Parkhad also
assisted in the operation. [@1] The seized contraband and relevant documents
have been handed over to Kelwad police station for further investigation while
police probe the extent of the alleged trafficking and other possible links.
