Coatzacoalcos municipal police detained two men in separate incidents less than
24 hours apart in the Villas de San Martín housing complex, seizing suspected
marijuana and bladed weapons from each. [@1] The interventions happened on the
night of Wednesday 11 November and in the early hours of Thursday 12 November
during preventive patrols, when both men allegedly reacted aggressively to
police. [@1] On 12 November, officers intervened after a citizen asked for help,
saying a man was disturbing public order, harassing passers-by and had tried to
physically attack him. [@1] The man allegedly reacted violently and pulled a
bladed weapon from his clothing. [@1] A preventive inspection found several
transparent ziploc baggies containing green herb with characteristics similar to
marijuana, as well as the blade he was carrying. [@1]

Neighbours had also accused that man of alleged drug selling and house
burglaries in the complex, accusations ministerial authorities must investigate.
[@1] On 11 November, officers saw the other man leave an apparently abandoned
apartment in the same complex and caught him after he tried to flee the patrols.
[@1] He allegedly reacted aggressively and pulled a hook-shaped sharp object
with which he tried to intimidate the officers. [@1] Officers found a waist bag
holding several transparent wrappers with herb similar to marijuana, plus the
sharp object. [@1]

Both men were taken to municipal police facilities for medical assessment and
then placed at the disposal of the Coatzacoalcos Comprehensive Justice
Prosecution Unit with the seized items. [@1] A second outlet confirms the same
two detentions on the night of 11 November and early 12 November in the same
housing complex during preventive patrols. [@2] The State Prosecutor's Office
will determine both men's legal situation and whether they can be linked to
other crimes in the sector. [@1]
