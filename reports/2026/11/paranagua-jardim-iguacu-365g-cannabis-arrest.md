Military Police officers arrested a 24-year-old man in flagrante for drug
trafficking in the early hours of Tuesday 10 November 2026 in Paranaguá. [@1]
The arrest happened at about 01h00 on Rua das Copaíbas, in the Jardim Iguaçu
neighbourhood, during a Military Police patrol. [@1]

The team approached him after judging his attitude suspicious and searched him.
[@1] During the search they found 365 grams of a substance described as
analogous to cannabis, divided into portions and packaged for sale. [@1] The
drug was being carried on a scale at his waist, with the portions ready for
sale. [@2]

At the scene he admitted holding the drug to sell it and said he already had a
record for the same crime. [@1] He was arrested and taken to the 1st Police
Subdivision in Paranaguá with the seized drug and a mobile phone. [@1] Officers
also seized the mobile phone as potentially relevant to the investigation. [@3]
