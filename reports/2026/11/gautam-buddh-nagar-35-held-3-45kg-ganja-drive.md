Gautam Buddh Nagar police ran a special drive against the sale of intoxicants
and illegal weapons across the district's police-station areas. [@1] The drive
led to 35 accused being arrested with illegal liquor, ganja and knives. [@1]
Police said different station teams carried out the action the previous night on
the basis of specific informer tips. [@1]

In Sector-63, in an action against drug trafficking, police arrested Shiv Kumar
with 600 grams of ganja and Sunil alias Kasim with 700 grams of ganja. [@1] In
Sector-49, police caught Sujit Kumar with 900 grams of ganja. [@1] In Sector-24,
police caught a juvenile with sava kilo of ganja, about 1.25 kilograms. [@1]
Here ganja means cannabis flower, thana means police station and bal apchari
means a juvenile, and the four itemised cannabis seizures total about 3.45
kilograms. [@1]

Apart from the ganja cases, the same drive reported recoveries of large
quantities of country and English liquor, measured in pavve, pouches and
bottles, and of illegal knives from those arrested. [@1] Police said required
legal action was being taken against all the accused in the relevant cases. [@1]
