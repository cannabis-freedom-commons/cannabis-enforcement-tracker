Police detained a 41-year-old man and immediately seized a batch of marijuana
found beside shoe boxes and home preserves. [@1] Police say the batch belonged
to a resident of Novonikolsk village in Ussuriysky district but was kept in a
shed in the yard of a house on Tsentralnaya Street in Talovy settlement in
Pogranichny municipal district. [@2] An investigator of the Interior Ministry
department for Pogranichny district opened a criminal case under part 3 of
Article 228 of the Russian Criminal Code. [@2]

Officers found the substance packed in vegetable mesh bags and seized it on the
spot. [@1] An expert examination identified the seized substance as cannabis
(marijuana). [@2] Police put its weight at more than 136 kilograms, which
qualifies as an especially large amount. [@2] The article under investigation
carries up to 15 years' imprisonment. [@2]

Drug-control officers had received information that a local resident was storing
drugs, according to police. [@3] The case was classified as especially grave.
