El Salvador's National Civil Police detained two men during a procedure on
kilometre 52.5 of the Longitudinal del Norte highway in the Tejutla district of
the Chalatenango Centro municipality, after officers found drugs on them. [@1]
The detainees were identified as Jonathan Alexander Aguilar Rivera, aged 28, and
Juan Carlos Rodríguez Ramírez, aged 33. [@1] Procedure images showed that one of
the detained men was using crutches when the officers stopped him. [@1]

During the procedure the authorities seized marijuana, cash, a mobile phone and
a vehicle. [@1] The seized items were kept in custody as evidence linked to the
case. [@1] According to the police information, both men will be referred to the
competent authorities for the offence of illicit trafficking, to answer for the
acts attributed to them. [@1] The PNC said it will continue security and
anti-drug-trafficking procedures at different points of the country. [@1] It
said the aim is to detect and intervene in activities linked to the distribution
and sale of drugs. [@1]
