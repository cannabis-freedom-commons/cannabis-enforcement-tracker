Egypt's Interior Ministry said its traffic campaigns recorded 104,237 violations
in 24 hours on roads across the country. [@1] The main offences were driving
without a licence, speeding, parking at random, using a mobile phone at the
wheel and breaching licence conditions. [@1] The same campaigns tested 1,045
drivers and 44 were positive for narcotics use. [@1] Al-Masry Al-Youm said the
figures came from an Interior Ministry statement issued on the Tuesday. [@2]
Cairo 24 reported the same nationwide figures and said security bodies had
tested 1,045 drivers, with 44 positive for narcotics. [@3] Dar El-Hilal reported
the same 104,000 and 237 violations and 44 positive drug-use cases among the
drivers tested. [@4] Sawt gave the same nationwide totals and said 1,045 drivers
had been tested with 44 positive for narcotics. [@5]

A parallel campaign on work zones along the Regional Ring Road recorded 472
violations in the same 24 hours. [@1] Those violations covered carrying
passengers against the rules, licence conditions and vehicle safety
requirements. [@1] Officers tested 156 drivers there and seven were positive for
narcotics. [@1] Seven people wanted under nine court rulings were detained. [@1]
Five vehicles were held for traffic offences. [@1] Cairo 24 reported the same
156 drivers tested, seven positive tests and seven detainees held under nine
rulings. [@3] Dar El-Hilal reported the same Regional Ring Road figures,
including five vehicles held for traffic offences. [@4] Sawt reported the same
Regional Ring Road figures of 156 drivers tested and seven positives. [@5] The
ministry said the required legal measures had been taken in the recorded cases.
[@1]

The accounts describe the positive tests only as use of narcotic substances and
do not name which drug was involved. [@2] The campaign formed part of the
Interior Ministry's continuing traffic enforcement on roads and highways across
Egypt. [@1]
