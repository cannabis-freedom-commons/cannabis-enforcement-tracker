Grey Bruce Ontario Provincial Police charged a Mississauga resident with
drug-impaired driving after a traffic stop in the Township of Southgate on
Sunday, Nov. 8. [@1] An officer on patrol saw a traffic violation on Nixon
Street around 12:38 p.m. and pulled the vehicle over. [@1] While speaking with
the driver, the officer saw evidence of cannabis and began an impaired driving
investigation. [@1] The driver was arrested. [@1] The driver was identified as
Muhammad Ishfaq, 37, of Mississauga. [@1]

He was charged with operation while impaired by drugs under the Criminal Code.
[@1] He was also charged with having care or control of a motor vehicle with
cannabis readily available under Ontario's Cannabis Control Act. [@1] He was
also charged with using plates not authorized for the vehicle under the Highway
Traffic Act. [@1] The allegations have not been proven in court. [@1] Ishfaq was
released from custody and is scheduled to appear in the Ontario Court of Justice
in Owen Sound on Thursday, Dec. 17. [@1] He also received an automatic 90-day
licence suspension and a seven-day vehicle impoundment. [@1]
