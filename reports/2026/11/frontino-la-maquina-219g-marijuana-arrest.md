A man was arrested in flagrante in the municipality of Frontino, in western
Antioquia, after officers found several narcotic substances in his possession.
[@1] The arrest took place on Monday, 9 November 2026, in the municipality's La
Máquina sector. [@1] During the procedure, authorities seized 219 grams of
marijuana along with 60 grams of coca base and 21 grams of bazuco, an unrefined
paste derived from coca. [@1]

The arrest was part of broader operational actions conducted across municipal
jurisdictions and transport corridors by the Antioquia Police Department on 9
November. [@2] The departmental police reported multiple arrests, drug seizures
and the recovery of stolen property during the day's operations. [@2] Police
reported that detained persons and seized items were placed at the disposal of
regional prosecutor offices for legal proceedings. [@2]

The detained man was handed over to the 129th Seccional Prosecutor's Office in
Frontino to face charges for the offence of trafficking, manufacturing or
carrying narcotics. [@1] That offence is codified under article 376 of the
Colombian Penal Code, which penalises unauthorised possession and distribution
of illegal drugs. [@1] The confiscated drugs were likewise placed at the orders
of judicial authorities as physical evidence. [@1] Under Colombian law, the
suspect is presumed innocent until a court establishes otherwise. [@1]
