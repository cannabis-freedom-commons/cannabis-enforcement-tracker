A man was arrested on Wednesday 4 November 2026 after Military Police officers
went to his home in the Laranjeiras neighbourhood of Uberlândia on a tip to the
Disque Denúncia Unificado. [@1] The Military Police said the action began from a
report received on the Disque Denúncia Unificado (DDU), the state's unified
anonymous tip line. [@1] With that information, the corporation went to the
Laranjeiras neighbourhood and found the man inside his residence. [@1]

In the suspect's house the officers found a .32-calibre revolver and 13 rounds
of ammunition. [@1] The Military Police seized 36 bars and 28 tablets of
marijuana, along with hundreds of buchas of the same drug and packets of
cocaine. [@1] A bucha is a small retail wrap of marijuana. [@1] The man's
vehicle was also seized, and all the material was sent to the police station
with him. [@1]

The company said the man was found with bars, tablets and buchas of marijuana,
packets of cocaine and a revolver. [@1] Diário de Uberlândia published the
account on 5 November 2026 under the byline of its own newsroom, and it names no
charges, cash amount or weight for the drugs. [@1]
