National Police patrols arrested 34-year-old Adrián García Ocampo in the barrio
Fátima of Almaguer, in Cauca department. [@1] Officers who were keeping watch in
the neighbourhood said the man grew nervous when he saw them and searched him
personally. [@1] He was carrying a white bag holding a glass jar with a lid, and
inside were 77 handmade marijuana cigarettes weighing about 190 grams together.
[@1] Police put the preliminary commercial value of the marijuana at about
400,000 pesos. [@1]

García Ocampo was handed to the on-call prosecutor of the Cauca Immediate
Reaction Unit (URI, the duty office that receives detainees caught in the act)
on suspicion of trafficking, manufacturing or carrying narcotics. [@1] The
report says the capture and seizure do not by themselves establish guilt and
that a court will decide his legal position. [@1] Diario del Cauca, a regional
outlet of the same publisher, carried the same account and figures under the
same headline. [@2]

Lieutenant Gerson Bedoya, acting commander of the Cauca Police Department, said
such operations form part of institutional efforts against drug trafficking and
for security in the department's municipalities. [@1]
