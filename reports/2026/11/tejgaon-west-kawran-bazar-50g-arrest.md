Dhaka Metropolitan Police's Intelligence Analysis Division, SRU, arrested a man
while he was allegedly attempting a mugging during a special operation to
prevent mugging and control crime in Dhaka. [@1] The arrested man was identified
as 35-year-old Md Manik Hossain Molla. [@1] The arrest was carried out by the
Intelligence Analysis Division, SRU, of the DMP as part of that anti-mugging
drive. [@1]

Officers detained him at about 1:30 a.m. on Monday, 2 November 2026, from the
footpath in front of West Kawran Bazar in Tejgaon while he was allegedly
attempting a mugging. [@1] At the same time, officers recovered 50 grams of
ganja from his custody. [@1] Ganja is the local Bengali term for cannabis, so
the 50-gram seizure is recorded as 0.05 kg of cannabis. [@1]

He was handed over to Tejgaon police station for further legal action. [@1]
Legal proceedings against him were reported to be under way. [@1] The published
account places the street detention and the small cannabis recovery as parts of
one overnight enforcement action in the Tejgaon area. [@1]
