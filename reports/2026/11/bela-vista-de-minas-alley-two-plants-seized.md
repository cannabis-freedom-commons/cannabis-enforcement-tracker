Military Police officers seized two cannabis plants in Bela Vista de Minas, in
the Médio Piracicaba region of Minas Gerais, on the night of Sunday 8 November
2026. [@1] The plants were growing in an alley on public ground near a house in
the Bandeirantes neighbourhood. [@1] The incident was recorded at about 6.30pm
on Avenida José Modesto de Ávila, after officers received an anonymous tip that
cannabis was being grown near the address. [@1]

Officers searched the area and found the two plants in the alley. [@1] Police
said the man accused of growing the plants is 40 years old, and they did not
find him during the operation. [@2] The two plants were taken to the Civil
Police station for the measures the case requires. [@1]

The operation was carried out by the 17th Independent Military Police Company.
[@2] In a separate incident on the same avenue early the following morning,
police found cocaine with a 31-year-old man, who signed an undertaking to appear
and was released. [@2]
