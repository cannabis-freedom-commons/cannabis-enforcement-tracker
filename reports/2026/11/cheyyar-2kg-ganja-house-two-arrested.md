Cheyyar police searched two premises in Cheyyar town in Tiruvannamalai district
on 8 November 2026. [@1] At the house of Sunil, 32, of Kanniyam Street, officers
found 2 kg of ganja packed in sachets and stored for sale. [@1] Police arrested
Sunil and Gopi, and said they were questioning the two. [@1]

At the petty shop of Gopi, 46, the search also turned up gutka products for
sale. [@1] The lead of the report gives the total gutka quantity as 93 kg, while
the body text gives 98 kg. [@1]
