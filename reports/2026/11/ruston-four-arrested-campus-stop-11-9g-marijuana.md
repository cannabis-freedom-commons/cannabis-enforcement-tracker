Four individuals were arrested after a traffic stop on the Louisiana Tech
University campus in which deputies found suspected methamphetamine, marijuana
and psilocybin mushrooms in a vehicle. [@1] Carl Keiffer Jr., 46, of Lafayette,
Charles Strange, 53, of Columbia, Tyler Messerschmidt, 32, of Monroe, and
Lindsey Brunet, 40, of Spearsville, were arrested at 1:07 a.m. on Nov. 10 by the
Lincoln Parish Sheriff’s Office at Everett Street and West Mississippi Avenue.
[@1] A deputy on patrol saw a white Chevrolet Tahoe traveling south on Tech
Drive. [@1] Dispatch told him the vehicle wasn’t insured, and he stopped it
after it turned west on West Alabama Avenue. [@1] Keiffer, the driver, showed an
insurance policy on his phone that had been canceled. [@1]

The deputy saw a baggie of suspected marijuana in the front passenger seat, and
a baggie of suspected methamphetamine fell out of the vehicle where
Messerschmidt was trying to discard items. [@1] The two baggies weighed 1.1
grams and 3.3 grams. [@1] Deputies took from Strange’s pocket a bag of suspected
marijuana weighing 10.8 grams, four individually packaged baggies of suspected
methamphetamine weighing about one gram each, and a glass smoking pipe with
suspected methamphetamine residue. [@1] A search of the vehicle turned up a
black bag in the rear area between Brunet and Strange. [@1] It held a baggie
with 44.5 grams of suspected methamphetamine, a bag with 10.9 grams of suspected
psilocybin mushrooms, a digital scale and numerous clear baggies. [@1] Under the
center console cup holders, a deputy found a loaded syringe containing suspected
methamphetamine, a used syringe, and three baggies holding a total of 5.8 grams
of suspected methamphetamine. [@1]

Strange was booked on charges of possession with intent to distribute a Schedule
I drug (marijuana), possession with intent to distribute a Schedule II drug
(methamphetamine), possession with intent to distribute a Schedule I drug
(psilocybin mushrooms), possession of drug paraphernalia and a drug-free zone
violation. [@1] Messerschmidt was booked on charges of possession of a Schedule
I drug (marijuana), possession of a Schedule II drug (methamphetamine),
possession of drug paraphernalia and a drug-free zone violation. [@1] Keiffer
was booked on charges of no insurance, possession of a Schedule II drug
(methamphetamine), possession of drug paraphernalia and violation of the uniform
controlled dangerous substances law in a drug-free zone. [@1] All four were
taken to Lincoln Parish Detention Center and booked. [@1] A warrant check showed
Brunet had an active Ouachita Parish warrant for probation violation. [@1]
Brunet was booked as a fugitive from Ouachita Parish. [@1]

Those named have not been convicted of any criminal offense and are presumed
innocent until proven guilty in a court of law. [@1]
