On 12 November 2026 detectives of the Modelo Territorial Cero team of PDI's
Puerto Montt Criminal Investigation Brigade detained an 18-year-old Chilean man
for microtrafficking, seizing 54 doses of cannabis resin that the PDI valued at
540,000 pesos. [@1] The operation, run with the public prosecutor's office,
caught the man in flagrante violation of article 4 of Law 20.000, the provision
punishing small-scale drug trafficking. [@1]

Officers seized 54 'Clear' devices containing cannabis resin, weighing 852 grams
in total, valued at 540,000 pesos. [@1] Acting BICR chief comisario Antonio
Retamal confirmed the arrest of one 18-year-old man and the seizure of the 54
resin devices. [@1] On the prosecutor's instruction the man was to be brought
before Puerto Montt's Juzgado de Garantía on Friday 13 November for his control
de detención, the hearing where a judge reviews the arrest. [@1]
