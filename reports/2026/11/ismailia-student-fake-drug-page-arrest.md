Security services arrested a student living in the Second Ismailia police
district for running a social-media page that advertised drugs for sale. [@2]
The student lived in the Second Ismailia police district, a qism, the
district-level police division covering part of Ismailia city. [@1] The Interior
Ministry said in a statement on Wednesday 4 November 2026 that its officers
spotted the page, which promoted drug sales for money. [@2] Police found no
drugs in the student's possession, a Youm7 report on the same ministry statement
said. [@3]

Officers seized a mobile phone that they said held evidence confirming his
criminal activity. [@1] Faced with the findings, the student confessed to
creating and running the page to defraud people who wanted to buy drugs, asking
them to transfer money to him and then switching off his phone. [@2] The page
offered only narcotic substances in general terms, and no cannabis product is
named in the accounts. [@3]

Police took the necessary legal measures and referred the student to the public
prosecution, the Ekhbary24 account said. [@1]
