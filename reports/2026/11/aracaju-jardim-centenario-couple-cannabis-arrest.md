Sergipe Military Police arrested a couple on Friday 6 November 2026 in the
Jardim Centenário neighbourhood in the North Zone of Aracaju on suspicion of
involvement in drug trafficking. [@1] After the on-site procedures, the
suspects, the seized motorcycle and all the confiscated material were taken to
the Central de Flagrantes, where the case was registered for legal measures.
[@1]

The operation seized portions of cocaine, marijuana and hashish, plus equipment
used to sell drugs such as a precision scale and change. [@1] Officers listed 12
cocaine papelotes, one hashish portion, one precision scale and R$67 in cash
among the seized items. [@1] Officers found marijuana portions and a mobile
phone on the woman during the personal search. [@1]

The operation began after anonymous reports of drug dealing and threats by a man
in a vila on Avenida Matadouro. [@1] When officers arrived, the suspect tried to
flee into a residence, throwing objects along the way. [@1] A woman then tried
to escape the scene on a motorcycle and was quickly intercepted by the teams.
[@1] With sniffer-dog support, officers then searched a second property
indicated by the suspect, where more drug portions were found. [@1] A second
account confirms the couple were arrested on Friday 6 in Jardim Centenário in
Aracaju's North Zone on suspicion of drug trafficking. [@2] A separate account
confirms that sniffer-dog teams searched another property indicated by the woman
and found more drug portions there. [@3]
