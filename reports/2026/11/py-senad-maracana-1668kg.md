Special agents of SENAD, from Regional Office No. 4 in Salto del Guaira,
searched two rural properties around Maracana, Canindeyu, after intelligence
work identified them as marijuana stash points. [@1] The stash and processing
centre stood in a wooded area. [@2] Anti-drug prosecutor Juan Benegas
accompanied the team. [@1]

Officers eliminated 1,668 kilos of marijuana, including 1,516 kilos of chopped
marijuana in 85 bags and 172 kilos of pressed marijuana in 25 packs. [@1] They
also found a camp holding presses, sprayers, hydraulic jacks and other items
used to process and condition the drug. [@1] The seized load was placed under
the custody of the Public Ministry. [@1]

Investigators said the load was likely bound for the Brazilian market, linking
the stash points to cross-border trafficking logistics in the frontier zone.
[@1] SENAD described the action as part of its standing work to disrupt the
infrastructure used to store, process and move marijuana to outside markets.
