Bajaria police arrested Shabnam Begum, also called Chacha, near her house in
Kanghi Mohal late on Tuesday night. [@1] She is a resident of Kanghi Mohal in
Kanpur. [@1] Women police helped surround her near her house after a precise
late-night tip. [@1,2]

Police put the international-market value of the seized charas at about Rs 1.5
crore. [@1,2] Officers said they had been receiving information for several days
that charas was being sold in Kanghi Mohal. [@1] The roadside detention and
search caused alarm among people nearby. [@1]

Police Commissioner Raghuveer Lal said Shabnam's husband Raees Ahmed had died
some time earlier. [@1] After his death, she lived with her partner Riyaz
Khatola. [@1] Riyaz had earlier been sent to jail for allegedly selling charas
while carrying an illegal pistol and had not been released. [@1,2] Police said
Shabnam took over his charas-selling business after he went to jail. [@1,2]
Besides selling, she also smuggled charas, police said. [@1] Women police were
questioning her to trace her associates. [@1]
