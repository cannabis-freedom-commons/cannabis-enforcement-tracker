On Saturday 7 November 2026 the head of the Aceh provincial narcotics agency
(BNNP Aceh), Brigadier General Dedy Tabrani, visited Gampong Agusen in Gayo Lues
regency, Aceh, to inspect a coffee-growing project rather than to raid a
cannabis field. [@1] Agusen, previously known as one of the cannabis-planting
areas of Aceh's interior, now grows arabica coffee on the slopes. [@1] He was
accompanied by the head of the Gayo Lues district BNN office, the BNNK Gayo
Lues, its staff and the Agusen village head, or keuchik, and he walked through
the BNN-supported coffee garden and talked with farmers who once depended on the
illegal crop. [@1]

The visit reviewed the Grand Design Alternative Development (GDAD) programme,
BNN's drive to move cannabis farmers to legal crops, and covered a
coffee-processing unit using a red-cherry fermentation system and a second
coffee factory in Desa Porang run by the BNN-supported Koperasi Kopi Gayo Lues
Bersinar cooperative. [@1] The account says the visit aimed to see how far the
programme had changed livelihoods of residents who previously depended on
illegal cannabis planting. [@2] BNNP Aceh said the project began with
trust-building, with BNNK Gayo Lues officers approaching residents persuasively
and offering coffee as a replacement for cannabis, and continued with help on
garden care, crop quality, processing and finding buyers. [@1]

According to BNNP Aceh data, the BNN-supported coffee garden in Agusen grew from
about 70 hectares in 2016 to 250 hectares. [@1] Across Gayo Lues, BNNP Aceh said
the programme had reached 3,346 farmers with 3,554.6 hectares of coffee garden
spread over 11 districts. [@1] The agency put the farm-gate coffee price at
about Rp18,600 a kilogram before the switch and about Rp68,000 a kilogram now,
with fermented wine coffee reaching Rp185,000 a kilogram. [@1]

BNN said the cooperative had partnerships with PT Ujang Jaya International and
Starbucks as buyers of the farmers' coffee. [@1] Dedy said moving farmers off
cannabis required economic alternatives, mentoring and market access, not only
handcuffs and the destruction of evidence. [@1] The Oposisi News 86 account,
attributed to a BNNP Aceh press statement, said the biggest challenge was not
only converting cannabis growers to coffee but keeping them in the new business,
with stable prices, quality, capital and buyers. [@3]
