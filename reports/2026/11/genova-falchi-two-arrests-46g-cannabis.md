Officers of the Falchi unit of Genoa's Mobile Squad arrested a 39-year-old
Italian man at his home on suspicion of possessing drugs for dealing. [@1] The
39-year-old was taken to Marassi prison pending validation of the arrest. [@1]
In the historic centre, in Piazza Santo Sepolcro, officers arrested a
19-year-old Gambian man after finding him with 44 grams of cannabis and
banknotes. [@2] After a fast-track hearing, the 19-year-old was ordered to
report daily to the judicial police. [@1] A 42-year-old Italian stopped in the
San Martino hospital car park was reported after police found 13 grams of
cocaine at his home. [@1] A woman stopped with the 19-year-old was found with 2
grams of cannabis and given an administrative sanction. [@1] Another man found
in the 39-year-old's flat with a dose of hashish after handing over 120 euros
was reported for personal possession. [@1]

Together the two cannabis seizures amount to 46 grams, comprising the 44 grams
held by the young man and the 2 grams held by the woman. [@1] Officers smelled a
strong odour of hashish outside the 39-year-old's flat and seized a precision
scale, a knife and wrapping material from the home. [@1] Police also seized
2,000 euros in cash found in an envelope in a bedroom wardrobe, which is
recorded as confiscated cash rather than a valuation. [@1] The cocaine is
excluded from the cannabis figures because the sources give it as a separate
13-gram seizure. [@1]

The three operations took place over recent days in a home, the San Martino
hospital car park and the historic centre. [@1] The first case dates to Friday
and the third to Tuesday afternoon in Piazza Santo Sepolcro during special
checks in the historic centre. [@2]
