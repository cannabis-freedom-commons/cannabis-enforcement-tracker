Polizia di Stato officers from the Chioggia police station arrested a
20-year-old man in Sottomarina during patrols targeting drug dealing in youth
gathering spots. [@1] Officers patrolling near the Sanctuary of Our Lady of the
Navicella with the support of plainclothes personnel spotted two young men
sitting close together. [@3] When the patrol approached, one of the two men
threw small packages onto the ground. [@1] Police stopped the young man and
immediately recovered four discarded wraps containing hashish. [@2]

Investigators then searched the youth's home and discovered a block of hashish
weighing approximately 100 grams hidden inside the garage. [@1] Officers also
seized a precision electronic scale believed to have been used to package
individual doses of drugs. [@2] Police arrested the young man on charges of
possession of narcotics with intent to sell and placed him at the disposal of
the judicial authorities. [@1] The operation formed part of extraordinary
territorial controls ordered by the Venice police headquarters to curb drug
distribution among young people. [@1] The criminal proceedings remain at the
preliminary investigation stage, and the suspect is presumed innocent until any
final conviction. [@3]
