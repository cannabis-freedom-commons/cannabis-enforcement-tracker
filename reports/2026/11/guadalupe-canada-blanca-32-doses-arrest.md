Officers of the Grupo Especial Táctico, Guadalupe’s special tactical unit,
detained 15-year-old Jorge Alberto in Colonia Cañada Blanca in Guadalupe, Nuevo
León. [@1] The deployment followed a citizen report that warned of a youth
acting suspiciously. [@1] Officers then deployed a search operation as part of
the Guadalupe Seguro strategy and located the adolescent at the crossing of
Nieves and Moyahua streets. [@1] Personnel of Fuerza Civil, the state police
force, also took part in the intervention. [@1]

When he noticed the police presence, the youth tried to escape and threw a black
bag onto the sidewalk, but officers caught him metres further on. [@1] Inside
the bag, officers found 32 packets with the suspected drug, described as small
bags of green herb with characteristics similar to marijuana. [@1] Officers
detained him for probable responsibility for delitos contra la salud, Mexico’s
drug-related health-crimes offence. [@1]

The adolescent, identified as Jorge Alberto, aged 15, was placed at the disposal
of the Ministerio Público, the prosecutor’s office, which will investigate and
determine his legal situation. [@1]
