Royal Gendarmerie officers of the Kalaat Sraghna territorial centre, under the
direct supervision of the squadron commander, carried out an operation on the
night of Saturday 7 November 2026, targeting an olive grove bordering Marchouh
commune. [@1]

The operation led to the seizure of significant quantities of shira, Morocco's
pressed cannabis resin; quantities of cannabis ears, kif, the Moroccan name for
the plant; and traditional home-made spirit known locally as mahia. [@1] Field
interventions also secured two motorcycles and bladed weapons that were in the
possession of those involved. [@1]

The operation ended with one suspect arrested, while field research and
investigations continue under the competent public prosecutor's office to
identify and arrest the other people involved in these banned activities. [@1]

The gendarmerie said the operation formed part of the stricter security strategy
of the new command of its Kalaat Sraghna squadron, aimed at stepping up patrols
and preventive field interventions against trafficking. [@1]
