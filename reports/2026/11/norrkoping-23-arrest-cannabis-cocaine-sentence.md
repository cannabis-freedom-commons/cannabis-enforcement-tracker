Norrköping District Court sentenced a 23-year-old man from Norrköping for a
gross drug offence. [@1] The court convicted him of storing and possessing just
over 116 grams of cocaine and 218 grams of cannabis. [@1] The man admitted the
offence. [@1]

He told investigators he was given the drugs, ziplock bags and a scale. [@1]
During the investigation he also gave information about the people he owed money
to and for whom he had agreed to store narcotics. [@1]

He had hoped that his cooperation would bring a lighter sentence, but the
district court found that the information he gave had no bearing on the penalty.
[@1] The sentence is two years and three months in prison. [@1]
