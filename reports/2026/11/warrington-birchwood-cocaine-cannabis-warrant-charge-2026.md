Two men were charged following an investigation into the online supply of crack
cocaine and heroin. [@1] The investigation targeted the Newton-le-Willows area
and led to the execution of a warrant in Warrington. [@1] Project Medusa
officers from Merseyside Police seized large quantities of cocaine and cannabis,
£2,000 in cash, drug supply paraphernalia and a phone. [@1] Christopher Lee, 36,
of Chaffinch Close, Birchwood, was charged with being concerned in the supply of
Class A and B drugs, possession of controlled Class A and B drugs, and
possession of criminal property. [@1] Leighton Watson, 18, also of Chaffinch
Close, Birchwood, was charged with possession with intent to supply Class A and
B drugs, as well as possession of criminal property. [@1] Both men appeared at
Wirral Adult Remand Court on 12 November 2026. [@1]
