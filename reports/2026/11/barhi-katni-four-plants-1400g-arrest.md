Barhi police arrested Arvind Yadav over cannabis plants. [@1] Yadav, 27, lives
in Kuthiya Mahgawan, in the Barhi police station area of Katni district. [@1]
Officers were on a crime-check patrol towards village Kuan when he ran from a
hut. [@1] Behind and beside the hut, they found four leafy cannabis plants. [@1]
Yadav told them he had no licence papers for the plants. [@1] Police uprooted
the plants and weighed them on an electronic scale; they came to 1 kg 400 g.
[@1] Police valued them at 14,000 rupees. [@1] Officers seized the plants and
booked Yadav under sections 8 and 20 of the NDPS Act, India's narcotics law.
[@1] He was arrested and presented before a court. [@1]

Sub-inspector Akhilesh Mishra, head of the Khitoli police outpost, led the
patrol with staff. [@1] The operation was part of a continuing Barhi police
campaign against illegal narcotics. [@1] Station house officer Arvind Kumar
Chaube and several constables were credited with supporting roles. [@1]
