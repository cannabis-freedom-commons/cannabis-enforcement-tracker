Asansol South police station arrested two men with about 1 kilogram of ganja, or
cannabis, in Asansol, West Bengal. [@1] Police named the two arrested men as
Suvam Sharma and Shamsher Sheikh. [@1] Police said the two had come to the area
to traffic ganja and were caught red-handed on a tip-off before they could act.
[@1]

The two were produced in the Asansol district court on Sunday, 8 November 2026.
[@1] The investigating officer asked the court for police remand, citing a
special inquiry including the arrest of other accused in the case. [@1] The
court rejected their bail plea and sent them to police custody for five days.
[@1]

The arrest came during a week of raids against drug trafficking across
Shilpanchal, the industrial belt around Asansol and Durgapur, in which police
said dozens of people were arrested with narcotics. [@1]
