Lecco State Police referred two people to the local Prefecture for possession
after officers seized doses of hashish and marijuana during checks on youth
crime. [@1] Officers arrested a young person accused of aggravated theft against
an elderly woman deceived through the so-called fake-carabiniere trick. [@1]
Another young person was reported at liberty for breaching a mandatory order
banning return to the Municipality of Lecco, while two other people received the
same ban on returning to the city. [@1]

Officers identified 579 people in total, 456 adults and 123 minors, after
intensifying controls in recent days in the city's main gathering places,
including squares, parks, shopping centres and the railway station. [@2] The
Mobile Squad carried out the work with other Questura offices, supported by the
Lombardy Crime Prevention Department from Milan and a dog unit from the Milan
Questura. [@1] The gathering places were described as areas considered sensitive
for crimes against persons and property as well as drug dealing and use. [@1]

The Lecco operation formed part of a broad national police operation against
youth crime coordinated by the central operational service and also directed at
groups of minors involved in unlawful acts. [@2] The Questura said prevention
and control activity would continue with a constant presence across the
territory to counter unlawful activity and protect residents. [@1]
