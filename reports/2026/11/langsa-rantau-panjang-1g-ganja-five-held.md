The Langsa city police drug unit arrested five suspects in five narcotics cases
between 31 October and 5 November 2026, of which one case involved both
methamphetamine and ganja. [@1,2] The only cannabis in the five cases was 1.10
grams of ganja, which police said was seized on 1 November 2026 from a suspected
dealer. [@1,2]

The suspect was detained in Gampong Rantau Panjang, Rantau Selamat district,
East Aceh regency, a gampong being an Acehnese village. [@1,2] Police classed the
man as a suspected bandar, or dealer, linked to a wanted list, and seized 8.19
grams of methamphetamine with the ganja. [@1,2] Of the five suspects held across
the cases, police described two as bandar linked to wanted lists and three as
pengedar, or street dealers. [@1,2]

The other cases involved methamphetamine, including 153.80 grams seized in a
house in Sungai Pauh Tanjung, West Langsa, on 5 November, and 9.2 grams of
etomidate, a sedative sold in vape cartridges known locally as vape getar, found
in a rice stall in Birem Puntong, Langsa Baro, on 2 November. [@1,2] Langsa police
chief AKBP Hyrowo, speaking through drug unit head Iptu Sirya Iqbal on 8
November 2026, said officers would keep developing the cases to dismantle the
networks behind them. [@1,2]

Police said the five cases were uncovered through Operasi Sarang Narkoba 2026 and a drugs enforcement drive they linked to the national police chief's Quick Wins programme. [@2]
