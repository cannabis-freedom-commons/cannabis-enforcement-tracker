Tiruchirappalli police arrested six people for selling ganja, the local term for
cannabis, in separate actions across the city. [@1] Police said they reached the
Srirangam station area after receiving information that ganja was being sold
there. [@1] In Srirangam, officers arrested 19-year-old Prasad of J J Nagar
while he was selling ganja. [@1] In areas under the Edamalai Pudur,
Ariyamangalam, Kottai and Uraiyur stations, police arrested 50-year-old
Arulkumar of Gandhinagar, 43-year-old Madhan of Malaiyappa Nagar, 25-year-old
Guru of Chinthamani and 25-year-old Vinodkumar of Gandhi Market Jailpettai for
selling ganja. [@1] In Ramji Nagar, prohibition enforcement police arrested
30-year-old Syed Ibrahim Shah of Kailasapuram, Thiruverumbur, for selling ganja.
[@1]

Officers seized 200 grams of ganja from Prasad. [@1] Officers seized 146 grams
of ganja in total from the other arrested people, bringing the reported ganja
seizures in the drive to 346 grams. [@1]

The same city roundup also reported separate tobacco-product sales cases in
Cantonment, Edamalai Pudur, K K Nagar and Thillai Nagar, which are not counted
in the cannabis figures. [@1] It also reported separate intoxicant-pill sales
cases in Ariyamangalam and Uraiyur, which are likewise excluded here. [@1]
