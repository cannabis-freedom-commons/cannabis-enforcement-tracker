On 10 November 2026 in Bahcelievler, police saw two people arrive by motorcycle
to meet a third person on the same street and approached them for an identity
check after finding their movements suspicious. [@2] Bahcelievler district
police detained three suspects identified as T.A. (23), I.C.C. (24) and Y.E.S.
(18). [@1] After police processing the three were sent to the courthouse, where
Y.E.S. and T.A. were released and I.C.C. was remanded to prison. [@2]

Material thrown to the ground during the stop was found to be esrar ready for
sale, with no weight given. [@2] A detailed check of the motorcycle found 13.32
grams of an unspecified drug hidden in a special compartment by the handlebars.
[@2] Searches at the suspects' homes found 25.68 grams of esrar alongside 18
pills, 0.93 grams of heroin and 31,580 lira, 101 dollars and 50 euros in cash.
[@1]

The operation was carried out by Bahcelievler District Police as part of work to
prevent drug trading and catch offenders. [@1]
