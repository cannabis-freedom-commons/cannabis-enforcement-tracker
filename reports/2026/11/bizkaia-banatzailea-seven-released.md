The Ertzaintza, the Basque autonomous police, arrested seven people in an
operation in several Bizkaia municipalities. [@2] Six were men and one was a
woman, aged 36 to 56, suspected of drug trafficking. [@2] Investigators are also
examining possible money laundering. [@2] El Correo reported that the group
included the man considered the organisation's ringleader, known in
drug-trafficking circles. [@3] Several of the detainees had police records. [@2]

The searches turned up about 3 kg of hashish pollen and 2.5 kg of cocaine, more
than 400 one-gram doses of which were ready for sale, the Basque security
department said. [@2] They found 200 g of crystal, Nexus pills and pink powder,
along with scales, packaging machines and wrappings. [@2] The Ertzaintza said
the network bought, prepared, distributed and sold drugs, mainly cocaine. [@2]
The investigation, run by its Bizkaia criminal investigation service, began in
May. [@2]

All seven were brought before a court after the police interviews. [@2] They
were later released, El Correo reported on 1 November. [@1] The release caused
discontent in the Ertzaintza, which had expected at least the alleged ringleader
to be remanded in custody. [@1]
