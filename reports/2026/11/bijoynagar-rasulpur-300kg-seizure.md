SRB officers raided the Rasulpur area in Bijoynagar in the early hours of
Wednesday. [@1] The team acted on a tip that several people were in the area to
buy and sell drugs. [@1] Between 15 and 16 suspected drug traders fled after
sensing the SRB team. [@1]

Officers then searched a crop field beside the road and recovered 300 kg of
ganja. [@1] The seizure was announced in a midday press release by SRB-9, Sylhet
camp. [@1] The release was signed by media officer, Additional Police
Superintendent K. M. Shahidul Islam Sohag. [@1]

The battalion said necessary legal measures had been taken. [@1]
