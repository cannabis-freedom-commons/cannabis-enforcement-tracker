Fargo police arrested Joel Patrick Rodriguez Jr. after finding the fleeing
pickup parked behind a home and overcoming his resistance. [@1] Prosecutors
filed seven charges against him on Tuesday in Cass County District Court. [@1]
The charges include possession with intent to manufacture or deliver marijuana,
a Class C felony. [@1] He was also charged with possession with intent to
manufacture or deliver methamphetamine and fentanyl, each a Class B felony, plus
paraphernalia, preventing arrest, repeat fleeing in a vehicle and carrying a
concealed weapon. [@1]

An officer tried to stop the green pickup for lacking current registration
around 4:50 p.m. Monday at 21st Street South and 13th Avenue South. [@1] The
truck fled at about 70 mph in a 25 mph zone, swerving and running a stop sign
while other vehicles, pedestrians and children were outside. [@1] A sergeant
later saw the truck again without trying to stop it, and officers found it
parked behind a home after a witness identified Rodriguez as the driver. [@1]
Officers arrested Rodriguez after he resisted, and one officer used a Taser.
[@1]

Officers reported finding what they believed was more than an ounce of marijuana
in the center console, alongside suspected methamphetamine, a scale, a realistic
BB gun and two cell phones. [@1] Rodriguez denied driving, while two people who
had been in the truck earlier told police he was driving. [@1] A police dog
alerted on a tan Silverado parked beside the truck, where officers found 6.25
grams of suspected fentanyl and paperwork in a vehicle police said was stolen
from West Fargo in mid-October. [@1] Rodriguez was taken to the Cass County
Jail, and the docket listed no charge tied to the Silverado. [@1] Court records
show he had previously pleaded guilty to fleeing a peace officer in a vehicle in
a separate Cass County case and was sentenced on March 10, 2025. [@1]
