Dhaka Metropolitan Police arrested 484 people and filed 52 cases during 24 hours
of raids across Dhaka. [@1] The arrests were made during operations in different
police-station areas of the capital on Wednesday 11 November. [@1] The figures
were disclosed on Thursday 12 November by DMP Deputy Commissioner (Media and
Public Relations) Md Akter Hossain. [@2] Mirpur division accounted for the most
arrests with 127, followed by Tejgaon with 76, Uttara with 51, Motijheel with
59, Lalbagh with 49, Gulshan with 45, Wari with 40 and Ramna with 29. [@1] Three
people were arrested by the Special Reserve Unit and five by the Detective
Branch. [@3]

During the same drives police seized 10.62 kilograms (10 kilograms 620 grams) of
cannabis. [@1] Police also rescued one victim during the operations. [@1] Police
said legal proceedings against those arrested were under way. [@1]

The figures are the police's own counts from a regular anti-crime sweep across
the capital. [@1]
