A young man who carried 50 grams of cannabis on a bike came into excise custody
along with the vehicle [@1] The arrested man is Ashwin A.V., 27, who lives in
Kandathil house, Parashinikadavu [@1]

The Alakode Excise Range Office's excise inspector, Nasib C.H., and his party
were conducting a vehicle inspection in the Naduvil and Tavukkunnu areas when
they caught him [@1] During the inspection on the Naduvil-Tavukkunnu slope, 50
grams of cannabis were found [@1] The Honda Unicorn bike, registration
KL.13.AA.5726, was also taken into custody [@1]

Earlier drug cases have already been reported against the accused at the
Thaliparamba range excise office and the police station [@1] Officers from the
party included two assistant excise inspectors, an excise officer, a central
excise officer and a driver [@1]
