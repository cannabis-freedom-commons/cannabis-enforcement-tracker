Police and gendarmerie teams took judicial action against 59 suspects in
Eskişehir during the week. [@1] The courts remanded six of the suspects in
pre-trial detention, and 51 were released. [@1] Police and gendarmerie teams
handled 44 drug incidents in Eskişehir between 2 and 8 November 2026. [@1]

The searches found 994.24 grams of esrar, the Turkish name for cannabis. [@1]
Officers also seized 4,868 synthetic pharmaceutical pills, 27.51 grams of
methamphetamine, 11.22 grams of bonzai and 0.24 grams of cocaine; bonzai is a
synthetic cannabinoid and none of these are counted as cannabis here. [@1]

The provincial governorate published the figures together with the provincial
police directorate and the provincial gendarmerie command. [@1] The tally covers
every drug in the week's work, and the sources give no breakdown of which
incidents or suspects involved cannabis. [@1] A second account of the same
governorate statement gives the same 44 incidents and 59 suspects, with six
remanded and 51 released. [@2] A SonDakika.com account of the same governorate
statement gives the same 44 incidents, 59 suspects, six detentions and 51
releases. [@3]
