A gendarmerie brigade from Quetigny stopped a car with two occupants near
Saint-Apollinaire, in Côte-d'Or, on 17 April 2026, after a tip-off about drug
trafficking. [@1] The search turned up 56 grams of cannabis resin, and also 600
grams of heroin, 36 grams of cocaine and about 100 euros in cash. [@1] The
driver was detained, tried and sentenced to four months in prison. [@1]

The second occupant fled the check and disappeared, and gendarmes found and
detained him on 26 October 2026. [@1] The justice system has now sentenced him
to one year in prison for drug trafficking and for fleeing a gendarmerie check.
[@1]

One of the two suspected traffickers who escaped at Saint-Apollinaire has been
detained and sentenced. [@1]
