Six people have been charged over suspected drug dealing run from an apartment
in central Norrkoping. [@1] Five men are alleged to have run the dealing from an
apartment on Repslagaregatan between 15 May and 25 June, in an indictment filed
with Norrkoping District Court. [@1] The apartment allegedly served as a base
where young men weighed drugs, handled customer contacts and delivered
narcotics. [@1]

Police seized 1,399 grams of cannabis resin in the case. [@1] According to the
indictment, amphetamine, cannabis and thousands of classified tablets were
stored and packaged in the apartment. [@1]

An 18-year-old who says he worked on deliveries told investigators he met
customers, handed over drugs and took payment, for 2,000 kronor per week. [@1] A
17-year-old is said in the same account to have weighed and divided the drugs,
for about 1,500 kronor per week. [@1]
