Anti-crime officers of the brigade anticriminalité (BAC) in Saint-Ouen, in
Seine-Saint-Denis, stopped a car on the evening of 2 November 2026 after seeing
its driver commit traffic offences. [@1] During the checks the officers found a
slab of cannabis resin in the car and arrested the driver, aged 20, and his
passenger, aged 21. [@1] While the two men were held at the police station,
officers learned that a man had gone to one suspect's home to clear it of drugs,
and they searched that home, where the drugs had been moved. [@1] The search
seized just over 17 kilograms of cannabis resin and nearly two kilograms of
herbal cannabis. [@1] Officers also found about 300 grams of cocaine at the
home. [@1] The 23-year-old man whose home was searched was arrested in turn.
[@1]

The three men were tried in immediate-appearance proceedings before the judicial
court of Bobigny on 6 November 2026. [@1] The driver was sentenced to three
years in prison, 18 months of it suspended subject to probation, and a detention
warrant was issued against him. [@1] The 23-year-old was sentenced to one year
in prison and jailed. [@1] The passenger was acquitted. [@1]
