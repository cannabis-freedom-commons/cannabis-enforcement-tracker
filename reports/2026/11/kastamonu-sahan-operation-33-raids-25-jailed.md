Police in Kastamonu detained 35 people in the Şahan operation, a sweep against
people accused of drug trading and drug use. [@1] A court remanded 25 suspects
in pre-trial detention and sent them to prison; nine were released under
judicial control and one was freed after giving a statement. [@2]

The operation, begun on Tuesday 3 November 2026, was coordinated by the
Kastamonu public prosecutor's office and carried out by the provincial police
directorate's narcotics branch. [@1] Officers raided 33 addresses at the same
time: 28 in the Kastamonu city centre and one each in Hanönü, İnebolu, Daday,
Çankırı and Samsun. [@2] The sweep was named after Ahmet Şahan, an officer
killed in an armed attack by an armed drug user on 1 August 2024 while serving
in the narcotics branch. [@1]

The searches found 254.73 grams of methamphetamine, 250.84 grams of bonzai, 127
synthetic pills, 5.74 grams of cocaine and 2.85 grams of esrar, the Turkish name
for cannabis. [@1] Bonzai is a synthetic cannabinoid and is not counted as
cannabis, so the 2.85 grams of esrar is the only cannabis quantity in the haul.
[@1] Officers also found three precision scales, 49 items of drug-use equipment,
napkins soaked in bonzai and 68,600 lira in cash assessed as proceeds of crime.
[@1] The searches also turned up one unlicensed handgun, a magazine, 52 rounds
of 7.65 millimetre ammunition, two unlicensed rifles and 62 rifle cartridges.
[@1]
