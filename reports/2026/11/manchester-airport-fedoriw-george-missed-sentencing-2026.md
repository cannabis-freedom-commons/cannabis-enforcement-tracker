Sam Fedoriw, 36, of Woodbine Road, and Leah George, 38, of Coultate Street, both
of Burnley, were convicted of being concerned in the fraudulent evasion of a
prohibition on the importation of a Class B drug. [@1] Fedoriw and George were
due to appear before Manchester Crown Court for sentencing but neither appeared.
[@1] Judge Peter Horgan issued a bench warrant for both defendants, who will be
sentenced at a later date. [@1]

The offences relate to an incident on September 11, when the pair brought 69.9kg
of cannabis through Manchester Airport. [@1] The haul was reported as having an
estimated street value of almost £700,000. [@1]

George's lawyer said she lives in Burnley, had spent her last £20 paying a
friend for a lift to court, the lift let her down, and she had no money left to
get to court. [@1] George's lawyer said that if a bench warrant was issued, she
would hand herself in at the police station. [@1] Fedoriw's lawyer said he did
not have a phone and, despite the probation service making contact with him, a
pre-sentence report had not been completed. [@1] The judge made a note of the
reasons given for George's absence. [@1]
