Federal prosecutors in Puebla searched a home in the municipality of Tehuacán
and detained a woman identified only as María “N”. [@1] The search was carried
out under a warrant granted by a federal district judge, after an anonymous
tip-off opened the investigation. [@1]

Officers seized four wraps of marijuana weighing about one kilogram. [@1] They
also seized seven wraps holding about 21 grams of a crystalline substance
similar to “crystal”, the street name for methamphetamine. [@3] Army, National
Guard, state police and Tehuacán municipal police officers provided perimeter
security during the operation. [@1] The federal prosecutors’ office said its
Federal Ministerial Police took part in the search. [@2]

The woman and the seized items were placed at the disposal of the federal Public
Ministry, which continued the investigation. [@1]
