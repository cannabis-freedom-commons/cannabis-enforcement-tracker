Saudi security agencies said operations across six regions of the Kingdom foiled
drug smuggling and promotion attempts and led to the detention of 71 suspects of
different nationalities. [@1] The General Directorate of Border Guard said it
foiled smuggling attempts in Jazan, Asir, the Eastern Province and the Northern
Borders and arrested 66 Ethiopian border-security violators. [@1] In Al-Jawf
region, the General Directorate of Narcotics Control arrested two citizens for
allegedly promoting hashish and 1,755 pills subject to medical-trade controls.
[@1] In Jazan, narcotics officers arrested a Yemeni resident over 50,280 pills
subject to medical-trade controls, while General Directorate of Mujahideen
patrols arrested an Ethiopian border violator for allegedly promoting hashish.
[@1] In Qassim region, security patrols arrested a Pakistani resident for
allegedly promoting methamphetamine (shabu). [@1] The agencies said the
detainees were stopped, legal procedures were taken against them and they were
referred to the competent authorities. [@1]

The agencies put the combined seizures at 1.3 tonnes of khat plant, 164
kilograms of hashish, 5 kilograms of heroin and 2.5 kilograms of
methamphetamine, plus 327,067 narcotic and medically regulated pills. [@1] The
Border Guard said its seizures accounted for the 1.3 tonnes of khat, the 164
kilograms of hashish, the 5 kilograms of heroin and the 2.5 kilograms of
methamphetamine, plus 40,582 amphetamine pills and 234,450 pills subject to
medical-trade controls. [@1] Sabq, reporting the Border Guard operation, gave
the same figures of 164 kilograms of hashish, 40,582 amphetamine pills and
234,450 regulated pills. [@2] The published pill total comprised 40,582
amphetamine pills and 286,485 pills subject to medical-trade controls. [@1]

Sabq described the interceptions as part of the Border Guard's continuing
efforts to confront smuggling, strengthen border security and curb the flow of
drugs through border crossings. [@2]
