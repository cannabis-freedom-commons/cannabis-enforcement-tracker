The Anti-Narcotics Force arrested eight suspects, including three women, during
nine operations across Pakistan. [@1] Two suspects held near colleges in Attock
confessed to selling drugs to students of educational institutions. [@1] Cases
were registered against the arrested suspects under the Control of Narcotic
Substances Act, 1997, and further investigation was under way. [@2]

In Attock, officers recovered 200 grams of hashish from a motorcyclist near a
college and 125 grams of hashish from a suspect near a college on Fateh Jang
Road. [@1] Near Lilliani Toll Plaza in Kasur, officers recovered 36 kilograms of
hashish alongside 12 kilograms of opium and two kilograms of ice from a
rickshaw, arresting two suspects. [@1] At the Motorway Toll Plaza in Islamabad,
officers seized one kilogram of hashish from two female passengers travelling on
public transport. [@1] Near Terkai Toll Plaza in Sohawa, Jhelum, officers
recovered 1.2 kilograms of hashish from a female suspect. [@1] In Karachi,
officers recovered three kilograms of weed from an abandoned bag near a bus
terminal on Shahrah-e-Pakistan Road. [@1]

The ANF put the nine operations' combined seizure at 152.997 kilograms of
narcotics, with the separately stated hashish and weed seizures totalling 41.525
kilograms of cannabis. [@1] At Bacha Khan International Airport in Peshawar,
officers recovered five kilograms of ice concealed in clothes in the luggage of
a passenger travelling to Jeddah for Umrah and arrested the suspect. [@1] In
Lahore's Gulberg area, 90 kilograms of ice soaked into 75 women's suits were
recovered from a parcel destined for Australia at a courier office. [@1] In
Quetta, 162 grams of ice were seized from a parcel bound for New Zealand at a
private courier office on Spinny Road. [@1]
