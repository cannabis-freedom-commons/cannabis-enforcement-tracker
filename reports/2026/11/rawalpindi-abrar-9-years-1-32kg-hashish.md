A court in Rawalpindi sentenced drug supplier Abrar to nine years in prison and
fined him Rs100,000 in a narcotics case. [@1] The case involved 1.320 kilograms
of hashish recovered by Morgah Police in March 2025, according to a police
spokesman. [@1]

Morgah Police arrested Abrar in March 2025 after recovering the hashish from his
possession. [@1] Officers recovered 1.320 kilograms of hashish from his
possession at that arrest. [@1]

The court awarded the sentence after solid evidence was presented and the case
was effectively prosecuted. [@1] A police spokesman provided the account of the
arrest and the prosecution. [@1] The sentencing was reported from Rawalpindi on
7 November 2026. [@1]
