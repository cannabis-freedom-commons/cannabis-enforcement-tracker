Officers of the ADSU, Mauritius's Anti-Drug and Smuggling Unit, searched the
home of a 34-year-old mason living at Camp-Levieux, Rose-Hill, at about 10 h 40
on Sunday 8 November 2026. [@1]

During the search, investigators found and seized a resealable plastic bag
containing 13 grams of cannabis. [@1]

The man was arrested after the discovery; he was released on parole after being
questioned. [@1] The investigation was continuing. [@1]
