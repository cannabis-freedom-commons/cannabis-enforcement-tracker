A cannabis grow was dismantled on the morning of 11 November in an apartment on
Provincialeweg N203 in Zaandam. [@1] The grow was on the second floor of the Aan
de Vaart apartment complex, opposite Zaandam station, and held about seventy
cannabis plants. [@1] No one was arrested during the dismantling. [@1]

The plants were destroyed on site and went into a shredder. [@1] Grow lamps and
other equipment were taken from the home and removed in a container. [@1]

The Aan de Vaart complex is still relatively new. [@1] It is not known how long
the grow had been in the home or how it was discovered. [@1] The value of the
cannabis grow found there is also unknown. [@1]
