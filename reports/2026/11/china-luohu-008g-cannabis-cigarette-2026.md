Luohu Customs seized half a cannabis cigarette, with a net tobacco weight of
0.08 grams, from the right trouser pocket of an inbound traveler. [@1,2] Officers
supervising inbound passengers stopped a man who had entered the customs
supervision zone without making a declaration and who gave off a strong smell
they suspected was cannabis. [@1,2] A further search found a previously lit
suspected cannabis cigarette in the right pocket of his trousers. [@1,2] A field
test showed the cigarette was positive for tetrahydrocannabinol (THC). [@1,2] A
professional appraisal later detected tetrahydrocannabinol in the half cigarette
and confirmed it as cannabis. [@1,2]

Customs said the half cigarette weighed 0.22 grams and the tobacco inside
weighed 0.08 grams net. [@1,2] The case was transferred to the customs
anti-smuggling department for handling. [@1,2]

Customs reminded travelers that, under China's criminal law and anti-drug law,
cannabis is listed with opium, heroin, methamphetamine, morphine and cocaine
among narcotic and psychotropic drugs that can cause addiction and are under
state control. [@1,2] It said that smuggling, selling, transporting or
manufacturing narcotics, whatever the quantity, must be pursued as a criminal
offence and punished. [@1,2]
