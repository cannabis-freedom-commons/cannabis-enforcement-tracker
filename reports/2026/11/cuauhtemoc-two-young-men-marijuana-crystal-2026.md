Investigators from Chihuahua's state investigation agency and Mexican Army
soldiers arrested two men at separate points in Cuauhtémoc during a joint
operation against street-level drug dealing. [@1]

The first detention took place at the crossing of Río Tula and Río Oaxaca
streets in Barrio Santa María, where officers asked Joel R. M., 21, to submit to
a search. [@1] Officers found a plastic bag with 93 grams of marijuana among his
belongings, plus a clear wrapper holding 10 grams of a granular, crystalline
substance with the characteristics of the drug known as cristal, or
methamphetamine. [@1] In a second intervention at the crossing of avenida
Ferrocarril and Fernando Suárez street in the city centre, officers detained
Luis Yair P. M., 19, who was carrying six plastic wrappers holding 80 grams of
marijuana in total. [@1]

Both men and the seized drug were taken to the Fiscalía de Distrito Zona
Occidente, the western-district prosecutor's office, where they were placed at
the disposal of the Public Ministry's Unit for Investigation of Street-Level
Drug Dealing. [@1] Entre Líneas, reporting the same operation, said the two men
are presumed innocent until a judicial authority declares them responsible by
sentence. [@2]
