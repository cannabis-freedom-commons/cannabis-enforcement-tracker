Civil Police officers and the Força Tática unit of the Três Lagoas Military
Police carried out a joint operation in Brasilândia, Mato Grosso do Sul, on
Thursday 5 November 2026. [@1] Officers served three search and seizure warrants
issued during investigations into drug trafficking and suspected links between
the suspects and a criminal organisation. [@1] The operation targeted addresses
under investigation for suspected drug trafficking and the possible involvement
of those investigated with a criminal organisation. [@2]

Two men, aged 23 and 18, were arrested in flagrante on suspicion of drug
trafficking and criminal association. [@1] The searches found portions of
marijuana and crack, a precision scale and other objects linked to the sale of
drugs. [@1] The detainees were taken in flagrante and were suspected of
involvement in the crimes of drug trafficking and association for trafficking.
[@2]

Police said the investigation had also indicated possible links between the
suspects and rival criminal organisations, a circumstance still under
investigation. [@1] The operation was part of integrated action by security
forces against drug trafficking and to investigate criminal groups in the
region. [@2] The state Civil Police said the suspects were arrested in flagrante
on suspicion of drug trafficking and criminal association. [@3]
