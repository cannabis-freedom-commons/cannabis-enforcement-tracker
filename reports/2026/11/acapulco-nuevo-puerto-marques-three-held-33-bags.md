State and federal forces detained three men in separate security operations in
Acapulco, Guerrero, on 13 November 2026. [@1] The detentions were carried out by
the Army, the Navy, the National Guard, the state police and the state
prosecutor's office. [@1]

In the Nuevo Puerto Marqués neighbourhood officers detained a man and seized
eight small bags of marijuana and four doses of a drug known as cristal. [@1] In
the Plan de los Amates neighbourhood the forces detained another person and
seized three doses of cristal, one bag of marijuana and a motorcycle linked to a
probable crime. [@1] In a third detention, in the Moctezuma neighbourhood, a
person was detained and 24 small bags of marijuana and 20 doses of cristal were
seized. [@1] In total officers seized 33 small bags of marijuana, 27 doses of
cristal and one motorcycle. [@1]

The participating forces also included the federal Secretariat of Security and
Citizen Protection. [@2] Those detained are presumed innocent until a court
establishes their responsibility. [@2]
