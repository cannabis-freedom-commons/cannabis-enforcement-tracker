Police in Mersin carried out raids on 7 and 8 November 2026 in different
districts of the province, coordinated by the Mersin Chief Public Prosecutor's
Office, against people they suspected of trading drugs. [@1] The raids targeted
suspected drug traders. [@2] Seven suspects were detained. [@1]

All seven were jailed in pre-trial detention on drug-trafficking charges under
article 188 of the Turkish Penal Code, which covers the manufacture and trade of
narcotics. [@1]

Searches of the suspects' persons and homes found 28.2 grams of esrar, the
Turkish word for cannabis, the only cannabis reported in the case. [@1] The
operations targeted street sellers known in Turkish as torbacı, the lowest rung
of the drug trade. [@3] Officers also seized 1 kg 780 g of methamphetamine and
39 grams of cocaine from two other suspects. [@1]

The searches also found 2.62 grams of cocaine, 3.10 grams of heroin, and various
amounts of synthetic pills and other drugs. [@1] Police also seized 20,370
Turkish lira in cash they believed came from drug sales. [@1]
