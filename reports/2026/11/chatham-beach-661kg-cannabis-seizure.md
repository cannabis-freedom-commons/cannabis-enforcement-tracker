Police and Coast Guard officers went to South Beach Road, Chatham, at about 6:15
am, acting on intelligence about a vessel heading for the beach. [@1] The
operation at Chatham Beach, Cedros, took place on Wednesday 4 November 2026.
[@1] Several people were seen transferring large items from the vessel to the
shoreline. [@1] They boarded a pirogue and escaped out to sea when the officers
approached. [@1] The release reports no arrests, and PC Cummings is continuing
enquiries into the matter. [@1]

A search of the beach found 17 bales containing packets of cannabis. [@1] The
seizure comprised 1,155 packets weighing 661.9 kilogrammes, valued at
TT$19,833,171.60. [@1]
