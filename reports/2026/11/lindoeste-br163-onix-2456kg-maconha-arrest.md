Federal Highway Police officers stopped a white Chevrolet Onix that had fled an
inspection on the BR-163 in Lindoeste, western Paraná, and seized 245.6
kilograms of marijuana from it. [@1] The driver ignored the stop order and fled
along the highway, and a tactical pursuit followed into the urban area of
Lindoeste, where officers intercepted the car. [@1]

Two people were in the car: the driver, aged 25, and a 16-year-old adolescent in
the passenger seat. [@3] Officers found bundles of marijuana spread through the
car, part on the rear seat and the rest in the boot, which weighed a total of
245.6 kilograms. [@2]

Both occupants were taken with the car and the drug to the Civil Police station
in Cascavel for the legal procedures. [@1] The driver was taken on suspicion of
drug trafficking, while the adolescent was sent for the procedures provided for
minors. [@1]

The occurrence was registered at about 21:00 during a roadside inspection by
federal highway police officers. [@1]

The Federal Highway Police gave no origin for the marijuana, no destination for
the load and said nothing about previous records of those involved. [@1]
