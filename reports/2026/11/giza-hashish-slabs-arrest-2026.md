Detectives of the Giza Criminal Investigation Department arrested an unemployed
man with a previous criminal record on suspicion of trafficking narcotic drugs
in Giza. [@1] Police said information had indicated that he was promoting
hashish widely across Giza. [@1] After completing legal procedures and preparing
ambushes, investigators detained the suspect. [@1] When confronted with the
seized drugs, he admitted in detail that he possessed them for the purpose of
trafficking. [@1]

Officers found a number of hashish slabs in his possession at the time of the
arrest. [@1] They also seized cash described as proceeds of sale and a mobile
phone said to be used to contact clients. [@1] He told police that he prepared
the slabs for sale and cut them into small pieces to make distribution easier.
[@1] He added that he bought quantities at low prices and resold them at higher
prices to earn illicit profits. [@1]

He said he had made criminal activity his means of quick earning and he
identified the source from which he obtained the drugs. [@1] Police completed
the required legal measures, filed a report on the incident, and the competent
prosecution took over the investigation and ordered him detained pending
investigation. [@1]
