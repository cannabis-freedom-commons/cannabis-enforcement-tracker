Four men were taken into custody after Indiana State Police stopped a truck
reported stolen on the Indiana Toll Road in St. Joseph County on the night of 8
November 2026. [@1] State police were alerted to a stolen vehicle from Ohio just
after 9 p.m. [@1] Troopers pulled the truck over near mile marker 73, and a
felony stop was carried out once it was confirmed stolen. [@3] The truck was a
white 2024 Dodge Ram that Blendon Township police had reported stolen earlier
that evening. [@3]

A Drug Recognition Expert concluded that the driver, 31-year-old Manuel Flores
of Toledo, Ohio, was under the influence of a controlled substance, which police
did not identify. [@2] Troopers searched the vehicle and found suspected
marijuana inside. [@1] Flores was taken to a hospital for an intoxication test
and then to the St. Joseph County Jail. [@1] Flores faces charges of criminal
conversion of a motor vehicle, two counts of operating while intoxicated and
possession of marijuana. [@1]

His passengers were booked into the same jail: Raul Martinez, 26, and Jose
Hernandez, 40, both of Toledo, and Marcos Perez-Velasco, 39, of Aurora,
Illinois. [@2] All four men face a charge of criminal conversion of a motor
vehicle, a level 6 felony. [@3] Martinez and Perez-Velasco were also arrested on
suspicion of possessing marijuana, and Perez-Velasco additionally of possessing
paraphernalia. [@2]
