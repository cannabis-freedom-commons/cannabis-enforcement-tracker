At about 16:25, traffic police stopped a Volkswagen passenger car for a check on
the Struma motorway. [@1] The driver was a 31-year-old man from Blagoevgrad.
[@1] Officers of the Second District Police Department, Vtoro RU, were
investigating two separate cases of driving after the use of narcotic substances
and alcohol, both established within less than an hour. [@1]

A test with a technical device recorded a positive result for amphetamine,
methamphetamine and cannabis. [@1] The driver gave a blood sample for chemical
analysis and was detained for up to 24 hours. [@1]

Police said the car was not owned by the man who was checked and was not seized.
[@1] Work on both cases continues under the supervision of the prosecutor's
office. [@1]

About half an hour later, an Opel driven by a 51-year-old resident of the
village of Yardzhilovtsi was checked at the junction of Yuri Gagarin Street and
Vladaysko Vastanie Street. [@1]
