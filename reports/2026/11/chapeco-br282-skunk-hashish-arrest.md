On the afternoon of 3 November 2026, Brazil's Federal Highway Police (PRF)
arrested a driver on the BR-282 highway in Chapecó, in western Santa Catarina.
[@1] The driver was 24 years old and from the state of Paraná. [@2] Officers
took the man to the Federal Police to face suspected drug trafficking. [@2]

Officers found 10 kg of skunk and 2.24 kg of hashish hidden in the boot of his
car, a combined 12.24 kg of cannabis. [@2] Skunk is a cannabis variety with a
higher concentration of psychoactive substances, while hashish is a resin
derived from the cannabis plant. [@1] Officers also found 453 ecstasy tablets
and 77 LSD doses in the same boot, synthetic drugs recorded here only to explain
the mixed seizure. [@1]

The stop began after the driver swerved abruptly into a fuel station on seeing a
PRF patrol travelling in the opposite direction. [@1] Officers then saw him
trying to use his mobile phone quickly. [@1] During the inspection, officers
smelled a strong odour of cannabis coming from the car. [@2] The driver told
officers he was close to his delivery destination in Chapecó. [@1] Brazilian law
provides 5 to 15 years' imprisonment plus a fine for drug trafficking. [@1]
