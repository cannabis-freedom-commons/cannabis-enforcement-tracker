The FPA's preliminary report stated that at least 11 people had been detained so
far. [@1] Thirteen adults were detained: eight men and five women. [@2]

The raids seized 460 doses of marijuana and seven cannabis plants. [@2] Six cars
and five motorcycles were also seized. [@2] The seizure included $12,671,350 in
cash and 212 US dollars. [@2] The authorities valued the total seized items at
$211,514,690. [@2]

The investigation is led by prosecutor Milagros Rivas of the First Turn Drug
Trafficking Prosecutor's Office. [@2] The detentions and evidence were handed
over to the judicial authorities. [@2]
