A court in Kishanganj sentenced two men to 15 years of rigorous imprisonment
each in a ganja-smuggling case. [@1] The court fined each of them Rs 150,000,
with three more months in prison if the fine is not paid. [@1] The convicts are
Avdhesh Kumar Mahto of Amanour in Saran district and Rohit Kumar Mahto of the
Pradhan Nagar police-station area in Darjeeling district. [@1] A second report
names the same two men from Saran and Darjeeling as the convicted men. [@2]
Additional Sessions Judge-III Abhay Srivastava delivered the verdict on
Wednesday in Special NDPS Case No. 37/2023. [@1] Public prosecutor Pranav Kumar
conducted the prosecution case. [@1]

The case arises from Kochadhaman police-station case No. 144/23 over an incident
on 14 June 2023. [@1] On that day Kochadhaman police were checking vehicles at
the Chardharia checkpoint on NH-327E on the Bahadurganj-Araria road. [@1]
Officers stopped a Maruti Suzuki car and searched it. [@1] The search of the
car's dickey recovered 23 packets of ganja wrapped in cellotape. [@1] Police
weighed the consignment at a total of 24 kg 400 g of ganja. [@1] The second
report likewise says the dickey held 23 cellotape-wrapped packets weighing 24 kg
400 g in total. [@2]

The two accused told police they had loaded the ganja in Siliguri to deliver it
in Muzaffarpur. [@1] They were to receive Rs 10,000 per trip for the work. [@1]
The prosecution examined five witnesses, and the court convicted both accused
under the NDPS Act on the evidence and witness testimony. [@1]
