Officers stopped a white transit van on Wilton Street in Middlesbrough after
finding it was being driven without insurance. [@1] The 59-year-old passenger
was detained and searched with his property, and officers found drugs and drug
paraphernalia. [@1] He was arrested on suspicion of possession with intent to
supply class A and class B drugs. [@1] A search of the 42-year-old driver found
cannabis, and he was arrested on suspicion of conspiring to supply a class A
drug and driving without insurance. [@2]

The stop led to a reported discovery of cash, cannabis and drug bags. [@1]
Cleveland Police photographs show the drug paraphernalia discovered during the
search. [@1] Cleveland Police confirmed both men were released on bail while
enquiries are ongoing. [@1]

Sergeant Sproson of Middlesbrough’s proactive team said the stop was part of
continuing work against drug dealing. [@1] He said drug dealing often
contributes to serious violence and other crime such as robbery. [@1] He asked
people who believe drug dealing is taking place in their neighbourhood to report
it by calling 101 or via the force website. [@1]
