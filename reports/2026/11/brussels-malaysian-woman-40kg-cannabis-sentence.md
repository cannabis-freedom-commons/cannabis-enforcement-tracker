Customs arrested a woman from Malaysia at Brussels Airport in Zaventem after she
arrived from Bangkok on 7 April 2026. [@2] The Brussels correctional court
sentenced her to 40 months in prison for drug trafficking. [@1] Customs found 20
packages weighing 40 kilograms in the traveller's luggage. [@1]

The prosecutor said phone analyses showed she knowingly made the transport and
that this was not her first such trip. [@1] Her lawyer sought acquittal, arguing
she believed she was carrying clothes and was shocked when customs opened the
suitcases and found drugs. [@2] Her lawyer also said she hoped to return to her
family and that her mother had recently been diagnosed with breast cancer. [@1]

Customs closely monitored the route from Bangkok, which had been heavily used by
cannabis traffickers for several years. [@1]
