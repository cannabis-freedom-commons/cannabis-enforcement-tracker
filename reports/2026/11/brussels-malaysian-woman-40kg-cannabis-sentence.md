A woman from Malaysia arrived at Brussels Airport in Zaventem from Bangkok on
7 April 2026. [@1,2,3] Customs arrested her there. [@2] The Brussels criminal court
sentenced her on Thursday 5 November 2026 to 40 months in prison for drug
trafficking. [@1,2,3] Customs found 20 packages weighing 40 kilograms in the
traveller's two suitcases. [@1,3]

The prosecutor said she claimed an unknown man had approached her before
departure and asked her to carry the two suitcases for payment on arrival in
Brussels, and that she had not known what was inside. [@1,2] The prosecutor
said that account was implausible: phone analyses showed she knowingly made the
transport and that this was not her first such trip. [@1,2] Her lawyer sought
acquittal, arguing she believed she was carrying clothes and was shocked when
customs opened the suitcases and found drugs. [@1,2] Her lawyer also said she
hoped to return to her family and that her mother had recently been diagnosed
with breast cancer. [@1,2] Those arguments did not convince the court. [@1,2]

Customs closely monitored the route from Bangkok, which had been heavily used by
cannabis traffickers for several years. [@1,2]
