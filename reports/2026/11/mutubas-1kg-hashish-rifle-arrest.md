Police in Mutubas in Kafr El-Sheikh governorate arrested a 59-year-old man
described as a dangerous repeat offender. [@1] The suspect was identified only
as “Kh. M.” and had previously been accused in several drugs and weapons cases.
[@1] Investigators said confidential information indicated he was trading in
drugs and holding unlicensed firearms. [@1] Officers detained him at prepared
fixed and mobile ambushes while he was driving his private pickup truck, which
police said he used in criminal activity. [@1]

A search of the man and the vehicle found an automatic rifle, a number of
rounds, about one kilogram of hashish and cash described as proceeds of sale.
[@1] The seizures were impounded and the case was recorded as Mutubas Centre
Felony No. 14229 of 2026. [@1] It was referred to the Public Prosecution to
conduct the investigations. [@1]

The operation was carried out by the Mutubas police investigations unit led by
Major Ahmed Yousry under the supervision of criminal-investigation director
Major General Mohamed El-Husseini. [@1]
