Police in Kozhikode arrested a man described as a notorious criminal who had
been selling ganja in retail in the city, including to children. [@1] The
arrested man was identified as 36-year-old Muhammed Faisal of Kallayi, who was
caught with 27 grams of ganja. [@1] A joint team of DANSAF led by Narcotic Cell
ACP Jeevan George and the Town police caught him at the Palayam Kammath Line
Road junction. [@1]

He allegedly sold ganja centred on Palayam to school students and children,
buying 50-gram and 100-gram quantities and repacking them into small packets for
sale. [@1] He had earlier been remanded in a theft case and, after release on
bail, was absconding without appearing in court when he was caught with ganja.
[@1]

Police said several drug cases and theft cases are pending against him. [@1] The
investigation team included Town police Sub-Inspector Vishnu, Senior Civil
Police Officer Niras, Civil Police Officer Ranjith, DANSAF Assistant
Sub-Inspector K. Akhilesh and Civil Police Officers Athul Abhijith, Dinesh and
Muhammed Mashoor. [@1]
