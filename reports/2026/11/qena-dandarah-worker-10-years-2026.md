The Qena Criminal Court sentenced a worker to 10 years of rigorous imprisonment
and fined him 100,000 Egyptian pounds for possessing hashish and a foreign-made
automatic weapon with ammunition. [@1] The same account is confirmed by Sada
Elbalad, which reports the same 10-year sentence and 100,000-pound fine for
possessing hashish and an automatic weapon in Qena. [@2] The verdict was issued
under counsellor Islam Mohamed Hamza, with counsellors Tamer al-Amir al-Taher
and Ahmed Aboul-Qassem al-Sharif, prosecutor Mostafa Abdel-Samad and court
secretaries Salah Farraj, Ahmed Abdel-Sattar Abdel-Hamid and Alaa Slouk present.
[@1]

The case dates to January 2026, when security forces arrested a worker accused
of trading in drugs in Dandarah village in the Qena district. [@1] Officers
found the defendant in possession of hashish, methamphetamine, an automatic
weapon and a shotgun cartridge from a foreign-made weapon. [@1]

During the arrest, the defendant tried to assault the investigations officer.
[@1] The case was referred to the Qena felonies court and registered as No. 747
Kolli Qena, which imposed the 10-year sentence and fine for drug possession.
[@1]
