Military police arrested a 31-year-old woman on the afternoon of Sunday 8
November 2026 in the Batingas neighbourhood of Arapiraca, in Alagoas's Agreste
region, on suspicion of selling drugs. [@2] The action followed a tip that drugs
were being sold at a party at a chácara, a small rural property with a
farmhouse. [@1] They went to the property after a denunciation about drug sales
at the event, and the suspect tried to hide in a bathroom. [@3] Officers from
the Canil dog unit, the Pelopes special operations platoon and the 3rd Military
Police Battalion's intelligence unit carried out the arrest. [@2]

The woman was carrying a bag with vials of cocaine and cash when officers
stopped her. [@2] She told officers she kept more drugs at her home on Rua
Princesa Isabel, in the same area. [@2] At the house the sniffer dog K9 Beretta
indicated drugs hidden above the ceiling lining, as well as cocaine portions in
the kitchen. [@2] The front door had been forced, and police said a man may have
entered the house before they arrived. [@1] In all, police seized 1.039 kg of
marijuana, 182 g of crack and 37 g of cocaine. [@2] Officers also took R$135 in
cash, a precision scale, plastic packaging, two mobile phones and a notebook
recording drug accounts. [@2]

The woman identified herself as the owner of the drugs and was taken with the
material to the Central de Polícia of Arapiraca. [@2] She was booked there for
drug trafficking. [@3] The Alagoas Military Police announced the arrest in a
single statement that also covered a separate motorcycle stop in the Cacimbas
neighbourhood, where officers seized 92 marijuana portions weighing about 110 g
and arrested two men, one of them a minor. [@1] That Cacimbas stop is a separate
occurrence from another report and its figures are not counted here. [@1]
