Police carried out two searches in the El Porvenir neighborhood of Formosa city
on the last Thursday, around 11:30, within an investigation opened from a report
of threats with a bladed weapon. [@1] During the first search, officers seized
machetes and a rubber baton (gomera), and also detained a 15-year-old boy who is
reportedly linked to a case of injury and threats in populated areas and in a
gang; the minor was taken to the police station accompanied by his mother. [@1]

At the second property, officers seized five machetes, a homemade gun known as a
"tumbera", one projectile, two rubber batons, and a bag with a plant substance.
[@1] Personnel from the General Directorate of Dangerous Drugs (Dirección
General de Drogas Peligrosas) carried out orientation tests and confirmed the
substance was Cannabis sativa (marijuana). [@1]

Separate judicial proceedings were opened for this event, with the Anti-Drug
Crime Court (Juzgado contra el Narcocrimen) intervening. [@1]
