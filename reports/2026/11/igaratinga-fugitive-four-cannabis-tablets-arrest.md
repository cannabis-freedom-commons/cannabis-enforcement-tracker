Military police in Igaratinga arrested a 24-year-old man on Wednesday 4 November
2026. [@1] Officers approached him during a routine patrol on Rua Antônio Mendes
in the town centre. [@1]

He was already on record for earlier arrests for robbery, theft, receiving
stolen goods and drug trafficking. [@1] A check of police systems showed an open
final arrest warrant against him, issued after a conviction with no further
appeals left. [@1]

During the approach the man told officers he kept drugs at his home. [@1]
Officers went to the property and seized four bars of marijuana. [@1] They took
him to the Civil Police station in Pará de Minas and left him at the disposal of
the courts. [@1]
