Jalisco regional police officers found the cannabis plot while on ground
reconnaissance patrols near the El Salvador delegation of Tequila municipality.
[@2] The plot held approximately 83,200 marijuana plants described as cannabis
sativa. [@1] After confirming the crop, officers destroyed it on the site by
uprooting the plants and burning them. [@3] Police collected representative
samples of the plants and handed them to the federal prosecutor as evidence.
[@4]

The Federal Attorney General's Office (FGR) opened an investigation file for
crimes against health over the find. [@1] The regional police notified the FGR's
regional-control prosecution unit (FECOR), which is handling the case follow-up.
[@2] Authorities reported no detentions during the operation and named no
suspects. [@4]

The find was reported at Tequila, Jalisco on 6 November 2026. [@1] The federal
investigation continued in order to clarify the circumstances of the planting
and to identify those responsible. [@2] The detailed account cites FGR official
release DPE/5056/2026 as the source of the circumstances. [@2]

The regional-control prosecution unit of the federal Attorney General's Office will continue the investigation to determine where the crop came from and to identify possible responsible people. [@5]
