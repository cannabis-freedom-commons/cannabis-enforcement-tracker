Military police officers from Rotam, the tactical patrol battalion, arrested a
suspected member of a criminal faction on Wednesday 11 November 2026 in the Dom
Aquino neighbourhood of Cuiabá. [@1] Officers had gone to a house on Travessa
Armando Cândia after a resident reported suspicious movement there. [@1] The
suspect tried to flee on foot by jumping the walls at the back, but officers
caught and detained him. [@1] He was taken to the police station for the case to
be registered. [@1]

Police reported 25 portions of drugs, identified as maconha. [@1] In the
apparently abandoned residence, officers seized a pouch with R$192.50 in cash
and several portions of maconha, as well as a cell phone. [@1] They also found a
PlayStation videogame with two controllers, a watch, knives and scissors of
alleged illicit origin and other materials for preparing and selling drugs. [@1]
