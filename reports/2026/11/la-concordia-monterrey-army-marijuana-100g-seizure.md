Ecuadorian army troops searched a house at the entrance to Monterrey, in the La
Concordia canton of Santo Domingo de los Tsáchilas province, on Monday 9
November 2026. [@1]

The soldiers found one bag holding a greenish substance believed to be
marijuana, weighing about 100 grams. [@1] The army put the marijuana weight at
approximately 100 grams in its own statement on the search. [@2] In the same
house the troops found eight bags of a whitish substance believed to be cocaine,
weighing about 800 grams; those cocaine figures are not counted in the cannabis
figures here. [@1] They also seized 140 rounds of 9 mm ammunition and a revolver
of homemade manufacture. [@2]

The army said no one was arrested in the operation and did not identify the
owners of the house. [@1] The seized material was handed over to the competent
authorities, and forensic tests are to confirm what the substances are. [@1] The
army said it runs permanent military operations to strengthen territorial
control in La Concordia. [@2]
