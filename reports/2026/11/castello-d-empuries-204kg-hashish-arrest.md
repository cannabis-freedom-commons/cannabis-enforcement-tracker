Mossos d'Esquadra traffic officers running a check at kilometre point 28 of the
AP-7 flagged a driver travelling towards France without a seatbelt. [@1] He
ignored the patrol's acoustic and light signals and fled at high speed, driving
recklessly for about 23 kilometres. [@1] Officers finally stopped him on the
C-260 road at kilometre point 39, in the municipality of Castelló d'Empúries in
Alt Empordà. [@3] The driver was arrested as the suspected author of an offence
against public health through drug trafficking and a road-safety offence of
reckless driving. [@1]

The driver was the vehicle's only occupant. [@4] A search of the vehicle found
four cardboard boxes, three in the boot and one on the rear seat, packed with
slabs of hashish. [@1] Investigators determined the total weight of the drug in
the vehicle was 204 kilograms, with a value of 413,570 euros once introduced
into the illegal market. [@1] The Mossos d'Esquadra described the man stopped
with more than 200 kilos of hashish as a 25-year-old arrested for an alleged
offence against public health. [@2]

The detainee, a 25-year-old Spanish national of Moroccan origin with a criminal
record, appeared before the on-duty investigating court in Figueres on 7
November 2026. [@1] The court ordered him remanded in prison. [@1]
