Palakurthi police in Jangaon district detained two youths who were caught
consuming ganja in Palakurthi mandal. [@1] The two were identified as Sohail of
Valmidi village and Kaushik of Palakurthi. [@1] Police gave both youths ganja
confirmation tests, and both results were positive. [@1] After the positive
results, police produced the pair before a magistrate and shifted them to the
Jangaon de-addiction centre for 15 days. [@1] Palakurthi Sub-Inspector Srujan
Kumar disclosed the detention and the magistrate's direction. [@1]

Srujan Kumar warned young people to stay away from bad habits and unlawful
activities. [@1] He urged anyone with a ganja habit to give it up immediately.
[@1] He made clear that strict action would follow any violation of the rules.
[@1] He said members of the public could report anyone selling or consuming
intoxicants to local police directly or by phone, with informants' identities
kept fully confidential. [@1]
