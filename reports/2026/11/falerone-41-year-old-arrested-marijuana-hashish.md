Carabinieri of the Falerone station arrested a 41-year-old local woman in
flagrante delicto in Falerone, in the province of Fermo. [@1] The home search
followed investigative information about a dealing activity and was carried out
with the support of a dog unit. [@1]

When the officers in uniform arrived, the woman closed the door of the flat to
obstruct the check. [@1] A carabiniere who blocked the door frame was wounded in
a finger of his hand. [@1]

The search, supported by the drug dog, seized over 95 grams of marijuana, over
66 grams of hashish and 22 marijuana branches of various sizes. [@1] Officers
also seized a precision scale with drug residue, a pocket knife and material for
packaging doses. [@1]

The woman was reported for possessing 93 counterfeit ten-euro banknotes worth a
total of 930 euros. [@1] Her personal effects included a partly used bottle of
methadone and a spoon holding a dose of heroin ready for use. [@1]

She must answer for drug possession for trafficking, resisting a public official
and personal injury, and was taken to the prison in Pesaro. [@1] The woman was a
resident of Falerone. [@3] The search was carried out by the Carabinieri of the
Falerone station with the support of a Guardia di Finanza dog unit. [@2]

Cronache Fermane describes the arrested woman as being about 40 years old. [@2]

The methadone bottle and the spoon of heroin were placed under administrative
seizure. [@4]
