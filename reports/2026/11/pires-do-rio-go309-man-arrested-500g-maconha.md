A man was arrested on suspicion of drug trafficking during a Companhia de
Policiamento Especializado (CPE) action in Pires do Rio on Friday 6 November
2026. [@1] The action followed complaints about repeated drug transport on the
stretch between Caldas Novas and Pires do Rio. [@1]

The CPE 90 team intensified tactical patrols on highway GO-309 and stopped a man
behaving suspiciously during the operation. [@1] Officers found 44 grams of a
maconha-like substance on the man and another 370 grams at his residence,
reported as a total of approximately 500 grams of maconha. [@1]

At the residence officers also seized a precision scale and a knife with drug
residue that police said was used to divide the drug for sale. [@1] In total,
the operation led to one arrest and the seizure of approximately 500 grams of
maconha, a precision scale and a motorcycle. [@1] The suspect was taken to the
competent authorities for the applicable legal procedures. [@1]
