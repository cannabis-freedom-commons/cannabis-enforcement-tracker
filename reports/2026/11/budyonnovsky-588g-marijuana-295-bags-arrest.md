Police for the North Caucasus Federal District detained a 55-year-old Stavropol
Krai resident after operational checks showed he was involved in illegal drug
dealing. [@1] Officers of the 1st operational-search unit of the regional
directorate carried out the checks. [@2]

During a search of his home, officers found and seized 295 plastic bags of plant
material with a total weight of more than half a kilogram. [@1] Rambler's
version of the release put the weight at more than 588 grams and said expert
examination identified the substance as cannabis (marijuana). [@2] Several hemp
bushes were growing on the man's household plot. [@1] Police also found
electronic scales and packaging materials at his home and said he had made the
drug himself for sale, which officers prevented. [@1]

The investigation department of the Russian Interior Ministry's Budyonnovsky
division opened a criminal case on attempted large-scale drug sale, part 3 of
article 30 and part 4 of article 228.1 of the Criminal Code. [@1] A court placed
him under house arrest, and the investigation continues. [@1]
