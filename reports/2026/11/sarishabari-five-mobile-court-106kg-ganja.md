Five people were detained in a special anti-drug drive in Sarishabari, Jamalpur
district, and a mobile court gave them prison terms and fines. [@1] The drive
ran from midday to about 4:30 pm on Tuesday, 3 November, at Bausi Bangalipara,
Bausi Rishipara and the Sarishabari railway station colony. [@2] The taskforce
was made up of the Department of Narcotics Control, Bangladesh Ansar, a
uniformed auxiliary force, and police, and was led by the upazila executive
officer. [@2] The mobile court sat that evening, conducted by Upazila Nirbahi
Officer and executive magistrate Afroza Afsana, the subdistrict's chief
executive. [@1]

Officers seized 1 kg 60 g of ganja, or cannabis, and two tablets of tapentadol,
an opioid painkiller, and detained five people. [@1] One kilogram of the ganja
came from a 45-year-old woman; a 45-year-old man had 20 grams, two men in a
railway colony had 20 grams each, and a 27-year-old man was found with the two
tapentadol tablets. [@2]

The court gave one year of imprisonment without labour to the 45-year-old man
and the 45-year-old woman, six months each to the two men in the railway colony,
and three months to the 27-year-old man. [@1] Each of the five was also fined
100 taka. [@1] Afroza Afsana said the administration takes a zero-tolerance
position on drugs and that such drives would continue. [@1] The seized drugs
were destroyed at the scene in the magistrate's presence. [@2]
