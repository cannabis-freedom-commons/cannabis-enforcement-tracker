From Friday 30 October to Sunday 1 November 2026, the Parcelles Assainies
arrondissement police station carried out a large anti-drug operation across its
jurisdiction in Dakar, Senegal. [@1] Officers concentrated their searches on
cité Mixta and Gueule Tapée market, two locations where they targeted several
suspected drug-trafficking and drug-consumption hubs. [@1] Over the three days
of the operation, officers arrested nine people in total. [@1] The nine suspects
face allegations including trafficking, possession and use of cannabis,
possession and use of hashish, and possession of Alpraz and tramadol tablets.
[@1]

During the operation investigators seized 69 small cones of cannabis, described
as cornets de chanvre indien, together with 5 grams of hashish. [@1] Officers
also seized 12 tablets of tramadol and Alpraz, medicines whose possession and
circulation outside the authorised channel are strictly controlled by law. [@1]

After being held in police custody following their arrests, all nine suspects
were referred to the public prosecutor on Tuesday 3 November 2026. [@1]
