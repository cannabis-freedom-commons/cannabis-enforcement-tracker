Patrolling police in Dharmaram mandal detained a minor boy from
Esalathakkalapalli after spotting him behaving suspiciously near Mallannagutta
on the outskirts of Dharmaram village. [@1] Officers took the boy into custody
at the spot near Mallannagutta and questioned him about his presence there. [@1]
During questioning, the boy said he had become habituated to smoking ganja, the
local name for cannabis. [@1] He told officers he had come to the Mallannagutta
area to sell ganja because he believed selling it would bring him more money.
[@1]

Officers searching the boy seized 54 grams of ganja from his possession during
the same encounter. [@1] Police described the patrol that found the boy as part
of ganja-eradication patrolling being carried out across Dharmaram mandal. [@1]
Following the seizure, police registered a criminal case in connection with the
ganja recovery. [@1] Dharmaram Sub-Inspector M. Srikanth is identified as the
officer who reported the detention, the 54-gram seizure and the registration of
the case. [@1]
