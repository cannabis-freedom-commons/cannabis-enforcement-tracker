Police in Baikunthpur, in Chhattisgarh's Koriya district, stopped a man on the
Dodi-Bahra road near Dodi village at about 8 am on 7 November 2026 and seized 2
kilograms of ganja, the Hindi name for cannabis, from him. [@1] The police acted
on a tip that a man was coming along the Dodi-Bahra road carrying ganja in a bag
to sell it, and the station party surrounded the spot the informer named. [@1]

The arrested man is 38 years old and lives in Dodi-Bahra village under the
Baikunthpur police station. [@1] Police registered crime number 330/2026 under
section 20(b) of the NDPS Act, India's narcotics law. [@1] The man was produced
before a court and sent to jail on judicial remand. [@1] The page's headline
values the seized ganja at 50,000 rupees, which police did not break down in the
text. [@1]

The operation was carried out under Superintendent of Police Harish Rathore, the
additional superintendent and the Baikunthpur sub-divisional police officer, and
the account names the cyber cell and station staff, including station house
officer Dinesh Chandra, among those involved. [@1]
