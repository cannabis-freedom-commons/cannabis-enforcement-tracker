Civil Police in Alagoas carried out a preventive detention warrant on Friday 6
November 2026 against a 19-year-old man investigated for drug trafficking in
Marechal Deodoro, in the metropolitan region of Maceió. [@2] The warrant came
from an incident at the start of 2026, when the same suspect was arrested in
flagrante in the Taperaguá neighbourhood. [@1]

In that earlier arrest officers seized about 150 grams of cocaine and 58 grams
of marijuana. [@1] The 17th Police District's head, delegate Daniel Aquino, said
the earlier flagrant arrest in Taperaguá was the occurrence that started the
investigation. [@1]

The 1st Criminal Court of Marechal Deodoro granted the preventive detention
after the police inquiry and court procedures advanced. [@1] The man was taken
to the police unit and remains at the disposal of the courts in Alagoas for the
legal procedures. [@2]
