Officers of the Department of Narcotics Control (DNC), Bangladesh's drug
enforcement agency, arrested a woman and her companion in Noakhali Sadar upazila
while they were moving ganja in a CNG auto-rickshaw. [@1] The two were arrested
during a raid on the evening of Tuesday 3 November 2026, in front of Zia
Pharmacy at Wabdar Pole Bazar in Ashwadia union, under Sudharam model police
station. [@1] Those arrested were Fatema Afrin Moni, 35, a resident of Sahapur
in Noakhali municipality, and Md. Sumon, 34, of Madhabsingh village in Rajganj
union of Begumganj upazila. [@1]

Officers seized the 3 kilograms of ganja, two button mobile phones and the CNG
auto-rickshaw used to carry the drug. [@1] Subrata Sarkar Shuvo, deputy director
of the DNC's Noakhali district office, said the raid was carried out on a
tip-off and that a regular case was being filed under section 19(a)/41 of the
table in section 36(1) of the Narcotics Control Act 2018 (amended 2020). [@1]
Noakhali Media places the raid on the evening of the same Tuesday, between 8 pm
and 9 pm. [@2]

The DNC deputy director said such drives against drugs would continue. [@1]
