A court in Hưng Yên Province sentenced a 37-year-old man to three years in
prison and a fine of 2 million đồng for illegally trading narcotic substances,
after he sold a bag of dried cannabis for 200,000 đồng. [@1] The first-instance
trial was held by the People's Court of Region 6, one of the regional courts
that replaced the district courts in the merged Hưng Yên Province. [@1]

The sale happened on the evening of 15 July 2026, when young men contacted the
man, a resident of Trần Lãm ward, and asked to buy cannabis. [@1] At his home in
Trần Lãm he handed over a red-clipped nylon bag holding dried herb described as
cannabis. [@1] The buyer asked the price, was told "của em hai trăm" (200,000
đồng) and paid by bank transfer. [@1] The buyer passed the bag to friends on the
way back, and they put the cannabis into cigarettes and smoked it all. [@1]

The man admitted trading narcotics for profit on the evening of 15 July 2026
when he appeared before the judges. [@1] He told the court he bought the
cannabis in mid-June 2026 from a man of about 40 near Phúc Khánh bridge in Vũ
Phúc ward, and investigators said they had not established that seller's
identity. [@1] The provincial police's investigation agency had prosecuted him
under clause 1 of Article 251 of the Penal Code, which covers illegal drug
trading. [@1]
