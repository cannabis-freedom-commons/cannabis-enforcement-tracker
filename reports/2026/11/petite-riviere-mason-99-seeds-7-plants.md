On Sunday 1 November, ADSU officers searched the home of a 34-year-old mason at
Petite-Rivière. [@1] During the search, officers discovered and seized a
transparent plastic sachet containing 99 cannabis seeds and 7 cannabis plants
measuring 20 to 40 cm tall. [@1] The man was arrested and placed in police
detention. [@1]

The investigation is continuing. [@1]
