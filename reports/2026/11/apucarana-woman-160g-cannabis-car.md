An anonymous tip to emergency line 190 said a woman was using a Chevrolet Prisma
to transport drugs around Apucarana, prompting police patrols that found the car
in transit. [@1] Military Police stopped the car on Rua Professor Erasto
Gaertner on the afternoon of Tuesday 10 November 2026, at about 15:10. [@1] A
personal search of the driver found nothing illicit. [@2]

Inside the vehicle, officers found a backpack holding about 160 grams of
cannabis divided into six packets. [@1] Officers also seized cash, a black
mobile phone with a purple cover and other belongings from the car. [@1] A
sniffer-dog team called for a complementary search of the car found no other
illicit material. [@1]

The car was taken to the Detran impound lot. [@1] The woman was taken with the
seized drug to the 17th Police Subdivision (SDP) of Apucarana for the applicable
procedures. [@3]
