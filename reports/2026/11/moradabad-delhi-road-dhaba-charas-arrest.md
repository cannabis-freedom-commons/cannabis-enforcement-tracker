Police in Moradabad, Uttar Pradesh, arrested a dhaba operator identified as
Faheem, a resident of Didauri in the Majhola area, late on Monday night in Naya
Moradabad. [@1,2] Officers seized 1.157 kilograms of charas from him during the
arrest. [@1,2] Following the arrest, police locked and sealed Mehfil Dhaba, which
he operated near Zero Point on Delhi Road in the Majhola police station area.
[@1,2] Police took strict legal action against the operator under the NDPS Act. [@2] On Tuesday, police presented the accused in court, which remanded him to
jail. [@1]

The illicit drug sales came to light after Abdullah, the 19-year-old son of a
utensil trader, was shot dead at the dhaba on Sunday night around 9:30 p.m. [@1,2]
Abdullah had been drinking tea with three companions when the shooting occurred,
prompting his father to file a murder complaint against two men from Mundapande.
[@1,2] City Superintendent of Police Kumar Ranvijay Singh stated that Faheem fled
after the shooting, which prompted police to investigate the late-night crowds
of youths at the eatery. [@1,2]

During interrogation, Faheem admitted to police that he sold charas and other
intoxicants under the cover of serving tea. [@1] Police found that charas and smack were supplied to young customers at the dhaba under the cover of tea. [@2] He confessed that he mixed
intoxicants directly into the tea to make customers addicted and keep them
returning late at night. [@1] Police reported that youths travelled from
surrounding areas, including Pakbara, Majhola, Mundapande and neighbouring
districts, to drink tea at the late-night roadside dhaba. [@1] Police were also identifying other suspect dhabas in the city that stayed open late without permission and served illicit substances. [@2]
