Sherrazade H., aged 26 and native of Bois-Bernard in Pas-de-Calais, was arrested
in Cagnes-sur-Mer on 29 October. [@1] The mother of three, separated from her
partner, said she took the work to pay a monthly rent of 3,000 euros. [@1] Tried
in immediate proceedings before the Grasse judicial court, she admitted
accepting drug deliveries to cover her expenses and keep her children. [@1] The
court sentenced her to 18 months in prison with suspended probation for two
years and suspended her driving licence for six months. [@1]

She herself handed to the officers the bag she was supposed to deliver to an
unknown contact. [@1] That bag contained half a kilo of cannabis and 22 grams of
cocaine. [@1] It was her third day of deliveries when she was caught without
offering any resistance. [@1] She was paid 300 euros per day for the deliveries
in addition to her cleaning hours. [@1]

The arrest followed a refusal to stop for a road check, a brief chase with
municipal police and a crash that set off the airbags but left her unhurt. [@1]
The prosecutor noted grey areas in her account, including her presence in Cagnes
while living in Ardèche and her earlier custody claim to have stolen the drugs.
[@1] Her lawyer stressed that she spontaneously gave the bag to the authorities
and described her as a struggling mother of children aged three, five and seven.
