Police officers arrested one suspect in Kanduyi, Bungoma County, during an
intelligence-led operation, the National Police Service said. [@1] Officers
searched a residential property and found several packages of suspected drugs
hidden in different sections of the house. [@1] The suspect was taken to Bungoma
Police Station and was held in custody pending arraignment in court. [@1] Police
did not disclose the suspect's identity, age or gender. [@2]

KBC reported that officers recovered 15.3 kilogrammes of suspected narcotic
drugs. [@3] TV47 reported the same weight and identified the material as bhang.
[@4] Citizen Digital put the weight at 5.3 kilogrammes. [@1] The Star valued the
haul at Sh459,000. [@2] Police also recovered Ksh5,911 in cash, suspected to be
proceeds of crime, and materials believed to have been used to package and
distribute the substances. [@5] The National Police Service commended the
officers and thanked the public for the information that supported the
operation. [@4]
