In the province of Trento, police identified 58 people, arrested four and placed
eight more under investigation at liberty during an operation against youth
crime. [@2] The Trento checks were part of a State Police judicial operation
carried out over the previous two weeks across Italy and coordinated by the
Servizio Centrale Operativo, targeting young people allegedly involved in crimes
against people and property and in weapons or drug offences. [@1]

In Trento, officers seized 2,201.47 grams of hashish. [@1] The same Trento
operation also seized 1,344.81 grams of cocaine, 41.6 grams of ketamine and
15,745 euros in cash. [@1]

Officers from the 106 Squadre Mobili, supported by Crime Prevention units and
other State Police offices, focused checks mainly on drug-dealing areas and
nightlife (“movida”) areas. [@1] Police linked the targeted conduct to offences
including attempted murder, assault, affray, criminal damage, extortion, theft,
robbery, drug dealing and unlawful possession of weapons. [@1] Nationally, the
operation monitored 1,115 social-media profiles, seeking removal for three in
Turin over violent content and weapons displays. [@1]
