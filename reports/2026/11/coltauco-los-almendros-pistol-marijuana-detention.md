Officers of the Coltauco tenencia of Carabineros, Chile's national police,
detained a man during a vehicle check in the Los Almendros sector after finding
a firearm and marijuana inside his car. [@1] The search of the vehicle found a
pistol holding 9 mm ammunition in its magazine, together with marijuana. [@1]
The driver was detained for offences against Chile's weapons law and Law 20.000,
the country's anti-drug law, and was handed over to the public prosecutor's
office for the control de detención, the hearing at which a judge reviews the
arrest. [@1] On the prosecutor's instruction, officers of Carabineros'
Criminalistic Laboratory, known as Labocar, were to carry out the expert
examination of the seized weapon. [@1]
