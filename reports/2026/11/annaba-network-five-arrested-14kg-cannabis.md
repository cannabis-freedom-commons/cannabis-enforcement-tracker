Officers of the Regional Service for Combating Illicit Drug Trafficking in
Annaba, a specialised police branch targeting unlawful drug trading, dismantled
an organised criminal network of five people involved in dealing and seized a
considerable quantity of Indian hemp together with property suspected of links
to the criminal activity. [@1,4] Algeria’s police statement on the same Annaba
operation says its officers toppled an organised network that specialised in
dealing drugs. [@2] A second outlet likewise says the regional service toppled
the five-member organised network specialising in drug dealing. [@3]

According to the available information, the operation seized 14 kilograms and
175 grams of drugs identified as Indian hemp, which is cannabis, in addition to
recovering a sum of money and seizing two motorbikes and three vehicles. [@1,4]
The police statement gives the identical haul of 14 kilograms and 175 grams of
Indian hemp, together with the recovered money, the two motorbikes and the three
vehicles. [@2]

The accounts present the operation as part of the continuing security efforts to
combat illicit drug trafficking and to confront the organised criminal networks
active in that trade. [@1] Indian hemp is the reports’ term for the cannabis
seized in this Annaba case. [@3]
