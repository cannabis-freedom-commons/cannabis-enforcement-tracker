Paulínia's Guarda Civil Municipal arrested a man in flagrante for drug
trafficking on Monday 2 November 2026 during an operation in the Ouro Negro
neighbourhood. [@1] The suspect tried to flee through vegetation when officers
approached and was then located and arrested in flagrante. [@1] A woman who was
accompanying the suspect was also taken to the police station for investigation.
[@1] Two buyers signed terms for drug possession and all those involved were
taken to the Plantão Policial de Paulínia, where the case was registered. [@1]

The operation seized 263 drug portions, described as cocaine, crack, marijuana,
skunk and hashish. [@1] The discarded bag held 100 cocaine pinos, 40 crack
portions, 57 marijuana portions, 52 skunk portions and 14 hashish portions. [@1]
The cannabis component recorded here is the 57 marijuana, 52 skunk and 14
hashish portions, with the cocaine and crack portions explaining the same
seizure but not counted as cannabis. [@1] Supported by other patrol cars, the
GCM had set a cordon in a nearby wooded area. [@1]

The incident began on Rua Pedro Pigatto Filho when guards stopped a vehicle
leaving a spot known for drug dealing. [@1] A search found a cocaine microtube
with the driver, who admitted buying it there and gave information about the
seller. [@1]
