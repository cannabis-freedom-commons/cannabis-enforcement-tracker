Fui Ashida, an unemployed man with no fixed address, is standing trial over
alleged drug dealing in Kochi City. [@1,3,4] His lay-judge trial opened at the Kochi
District Court on 2 November 2026, and he admitted the charges at the first
hearing. [@2,3,4]

Prosecutors accuse him of repeatedly selling cannabis, cocaine and other drugs
to a large number of customers in Kochi City from late March to September last
year. [@2,4] They also accuse him of fraud for using another person's credit-card
information obtained illicitly to receive sex-shop services at a hotel in Kochi
City. [@2]

Questioned about his motive, Ashida said a local senior invited him while he was
looking for work, the offer turned out to involve a drug-dealing organization,
and having decided himself to do it he felt unable to refuse. [@2] He said he
tried credit-card information obtained through a highly anonymous social-media
service and found that it worked. [@2] The next hearing in the case is scheduled
for 4 November. [@2,4]

The Kochi District Court sentenced Ashida to five years in prison and a 1 million yen fine on 6 November 2026, with no suspension of the term. [@5] Presiding judge Yoshitake Tanaka said Ashida, as a member of an illegal-drug dealing group, sold to as many as 17 customers at least 33 times within the prefecture, calling the offences malicious. [@5] Prosecutors had sought seven years in prison, arguing he dealt as a profession, while the defence asked for a suspended sentence on the grounds that he had sincerely reflected. [@5] The court took into account that he had shown intent to reform, had paid compensation for the fraud and had donated 100,000 yen to a fund for crime victims. [@5] Ashida's lawyer said it had not yet been decided whether to appeal. [@5]
