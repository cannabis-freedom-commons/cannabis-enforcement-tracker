A driver was arrested after failing a roadside drug test and allegedly
assaulting a police officer outside a school in Bournemouth. [@1] Dorset Roads
Policing officers attended Hadow Road on the morning of November 3 to support
schools with enforcement of the School Street Scheme. [@1] The scheme creates a
traffic-free zone outside schools at key times to make journeys safer for
children. [@1] During the operation, officers dealt with several road traffic
offences. [@1]

One driver became frustrated after being prevented from driving through the road
closure to get closer to the school. [@1] The driver was stopped by officers and
failed a roadside drug test for cannabis. [@1] Further checks revealed they were
driving on a revoked licence. [@1] The driver was arrested and subsequently
further arrested on suspicion of assaulting a police officer. [@1]

A Dorset Roads Policing spokesperson said road safety is everyone's
responsibility. [@1] The spokesperson said restrictions around schools are there
to protect children, not inconvenience motorists. [@1] The spokesperson asked
all road users to respect the measures and play their part in keeping school
communities safe. [@1] Enquiries into the incident are ongoing. [@1]
