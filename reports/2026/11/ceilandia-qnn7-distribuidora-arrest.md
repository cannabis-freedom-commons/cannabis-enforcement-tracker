Military police arrested a 20-year-old man in the early hours of Sunday 8
November 2026 on suspicion of using a drinks distributor on QNN 7 in Ceilândia
Norte, in the Federal District, as a place to sell and use drugs. [@1] A
17-year-old boy was also taken to a police station after officers found him with
a portion of marijuana. [@1] Officers went to the business after receiving
complaints. [@1]

The arrest followed intelligence work by the 10th Battalion, which passed
information to the radio patrol teams. [@1] A search inside the business found
portions of cocaine, marijuana, crack and ice and a piece of dry hashish, and
police also seized a precision scale, a mobile phone, packaging material and
about 3,036 reais in cash. [@1]

Officers also found receipts of financial transactions. [@1] The owner of the
distributor was taken to the 15th Police Station and the teenager to the Child
and Adolescent Police Station II (DCA II). [@1]
