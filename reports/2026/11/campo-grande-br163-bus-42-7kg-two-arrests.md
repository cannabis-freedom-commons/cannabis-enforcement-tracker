Military Police in Campo Grande arrested 19-year-old Bolivian Rider Salguero
Garcia and 28-year-old Brazilian Isabella Silva Garcia de Feo on Sunday 8
November 2026 as they tried to take cannabis to São Paulo. [@1] The inspection
took place on the BR-163 highway in the Jardim Centro-Oeste area of the city.
[@1] The bus was bound for Brasília, and officers were acting on an anonymous
tip that a passenger was carrying drugs. [@1] In the luggage hold, officers
found a blue suitcase smelling strongly of cannabis. [@1] A second regional
outlet reported that the Força Tática of the 6th Independent Military Police
Company stopped the bus in the early hours of Sunday. [@2]

The suitcase belonged to Isabella, whom officers identified from the passenger
list, and she confirmed she was carrying cannabis. [@1,3] She said she had
collected the drug in Ponta Porã, on the border with Paraguay, and was to hand
it to a person at the bus station in Bauru, São Paulo state, whose name she did
not know. [@1,3] The regional outlet reported she said she would be paid R$1,000
for the trip. [@2]

In a later inspection during the same check, officers noticed Rider was nervous
and trying to hide his face, and he admitted he was also carrying drugs. [@1]
Eight portions of cannabis weighing 8.5 kg were found in his suitcase; he said
he had collected the drug in Campo Grande and did not yet know the delivery
address in São Paulo. [@1,3] The regional outlet reported the second suitcase held
8.514 kg and that he said he would be paid R$500 to carry it. [@2]

The two seizures totalled 42.7 kg of cannabis, whose value was not reported.
[@1] Both were arrested for drug trafficking and taken to a Depac, a police
station handling arrests brought in for immediate processing, with the drug and
seized items. [@1,3] The regional outlet said the material was weighed at Denar,
the specialised anti-narcotics police station, and that investigators would
check for links between the two seizures and for other participants. [@2]

The arrest was made by the Força Tática of the 6th Independent Military Police Company. [@1]
