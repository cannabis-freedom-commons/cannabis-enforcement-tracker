Police in the Haskovo region detained three people within a day for drug
possession, according to the regional police directorate. [@1] Officers checked
a 15-year-old boy at 9 a.m. in a park on D. Blagoev Street in Svilengrad. [@1]
Officers found 9.7 grams of dry green leafy matter and 0.47 grams of white
crystalline substance in the boy's clothing. [@1] A field drug test confirmed
the two substances as marijuana and methamphetamine. [@1]

In the same daily roundup in Dimitrovgrad, a 28-year-old man was caught with
methamphetamine, with one gram of white crystalline substance and 30 tablets of
the drug seized from him. [@1] In Haskovo, detectives searching a 46-year-old
man on Yastreb Street found 17 wraps of green-brown leafy matter weighing 4
grams with a specific mint smell. [@1] The third detainee was held in the
custody of the directorate in Haskovo. [@1]

All three detainees were held for up to 24 hours. [@1] A second outlet
attributes the same account of the three detentions to the press centre of the
Haskovo regional police directorate. [@3] A third account independently confirms
the morning check of the 15-year-old in the Svilengrad park on Blagoev Street.
[@2]
