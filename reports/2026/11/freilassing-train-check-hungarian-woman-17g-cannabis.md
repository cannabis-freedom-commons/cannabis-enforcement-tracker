Border police from the Piding border police inspectorate found and seized just
under 17 grams of cannabis during a check on a passenger train from Munich to
Salzburg on Wednesday. [@1] Officers checked the train in the Freilassing area
and their attention fell on a 30-year-old Hungarian national. [@1] Police first
saw indications that the woman might be under the influence of cannabis. [@1]

A search of the luggage she was carrying turned up the cannabis, which officers
seized. [@1] Police opened a criminal investigation against the 30-year-old on
suspicion of attempted export of cannabis. [@1] After the police measures were
completed she was released and allowed to continue her journey to Austria. [@1]
