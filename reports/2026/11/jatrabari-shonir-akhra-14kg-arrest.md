Detectives of the Detective Motijheel division of the Dhaka Metropolitan Police,
the police force for the capital, arrested one drug dealer in Dhaka's Jatrabari
area with 14 kg of ganja, the local term for cannabis. [@1] The arrested man was
identified by police as 21-year-old Md Sajjad Hossain Naushad. [@2] The
operation took place at about 10 pm on Sunday in the Shonir Akhra area, a
neighbourhood within Jatrabari Police Station limits in southern Dhaka. [@1]

Officers recovered 14 kg of ganja and one mobile phone from his possession
during the search. [@2] Police filed a case against the arrested man at
Jatrabari Police Station under the Narcotics Control Act. [@1] Police then sent
the arrested man to court. [@1]

During preliminary questioning, he admitted that he was a professional drug
dealer. [@1] He said he collected drugs from different places across the
country. [@1] He sold those drugs in the Jatrabari police-station area and in
other parts of the capital. [@2]
