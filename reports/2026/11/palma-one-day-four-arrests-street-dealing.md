National Police arrested four men in Palma in a single day as suspected drug
traffickers, in three interventions in the La Soledat, Es Puntiró and Son
Forteza neighbourhoods. [@1] The three operations took place the previous Sunday
and turned up tusi, cocaine, marijuana, hashish and pills described as suspected
MDMA. [@1] The Baleares police headquarters announced the arrests on Wednesday
and placed them in a reinforced citizen-security drive against drug distribution
in the Balearic capital. [@1]

The first intervention came at about 05:30 on Sunday in La Soledat, where
officers of the Citizen Attention Group saw two men apparently exchanging
something in the open street. [@1] Officers searching the alleged seller found
four small bags of tusi, one portion of hashish and another of marijuana, plus a
20 euro note. [@1] They arrested the alleged seller for an offence against
public health. [@1]

About three hours later a patrol was sent to Es Puntiró after a 091 alert about
a possible confrontation between several people linked to drug trafficking. [@1]
Officers stopped a car carrying two men and two women who tried to leave the
scene. [@1] One man carried several doses of tusi and cash in different notes,
and the second, who had a previous record for similar offences, carried the same
substance in an identical bag. [@1] Both men were arrested as suspected authors
of an offence against public health. [@1] The third intervention came in the
afternoon of the same Sunday in Son Forteza, where officers of the motorcycle
unit were on preventive patrol. [@1] A search found a clear plastic bag that one
of the men had hidden in his private parts, holding eight single doses of tusi,
five of cocaine and thirteen pills described as suspected MDMA. [@1] He also
carried 80 euros in notes and coins, and the find led to his arrest for an
offence against public health. [@1]

National Police said they keep special surveillance at several points in Palma
because of neighbours' complaints about drug sales. [@1] The deployment includes
checks near areas considered conflictive, more uniformed patrols in cars and on
motorcycles, and unmarked police vehicles. [@1]
