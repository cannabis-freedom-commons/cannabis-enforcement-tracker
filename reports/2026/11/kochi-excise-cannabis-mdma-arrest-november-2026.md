Excise officers in Kochi arrested a man from Nettur with MDMA and cannabis that
he had bought in Bengaluru. [@2] The Ernakulam excise special squad had kept the
man under observation for a week before detaining him. [@2]

The arrest was reported together with a separate police operation in which two
men were held with 185 grams of MDMA at a lodge in Aluva, while the excise
arrested another man in Kochi city with MDMA and cannabis. [@1] Police said the
two men had carried the MDMA from Tamil Nadu as passengers on a tourist bus and
had hidden it inside bunches of plantains. [@2]
