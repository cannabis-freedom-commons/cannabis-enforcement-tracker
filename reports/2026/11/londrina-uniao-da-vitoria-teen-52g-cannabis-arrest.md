Military police apprehended a 15-year-old boy on suspicion of involvement in
drug trafficking on the night of Thursday 5 November 2026 in the União da
Vitória area of the southern zone of Londrina, Paraná, with the incident
recorded on Avenida Guilherme de Almeida. [@1] According to the Military Police,
a ROCAM team was patrolling a spot known for drug dealing when it saw the boy,
who discarded an object as the officers approached. [@1]

During the approach officers found marijuana portions in the boy's clothing and
R$10 in cash, and searches of the ground and the discarded object led them to
further drugs. [@1] In all, officers seized 15 portions of marijuana weighing 46
grams and one portion of hashish weighing 6 grams. [@1] The same seizure
included 64 Eppendorf tubes of cocaine totalling 70 grams and 21 portions of
crack weighing 9 grams, recorded here only to explain the mixed seizure and
excluded from the cannabis figures. [@1]

The boy was apprehended for an infraction analogous to drug trafficking, taken
to the police station, and the Conselho Tutelar, Brazil's child-protection
council, was also called in. [@1]
