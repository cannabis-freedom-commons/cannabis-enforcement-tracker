Staffordshire Police notified the Stoke-on-Trent City Council empty homes team
of 149 properties used as cannabis factories over the past four years, and the
council brought 99 of them back into use as homes. [@1] The Sentinel confirmed
the council's figure: 149 houses had been used as cannabis factories, and 99 are
now occupied again. [@2] The council's housing, regeneration and operations
scrutiny committee received an update on the partnership between its empty homes
team and the police. [@1] Tomos Jones, strategic manager for housing, told the
committee that the council's programme of returning properties to use is 'very
active'. [@1]

Council chiefs recently created a £1 million capital fund to support the empty
homes team's work over the next two years. [@1] The team usually brings around
100 properties back into use annually, but with the additional resource it is
expected to reach 150 this year, potentially 200 a year in future. [@1]

Councillor Duncan Walker, cabinet member for housing, praised the team as
'unsung heroes' and described the additional funding as positive. [@1]
