The operational team of the Tapanuli Tengah district police narcotics unit
arrested a 20-year-old man identified as SP, alias Gelleng, as an alleged
cannabis dealer. [@1,2,5,6] Officers stopped the man at about 17.30 WIB beside a house
in a residential area at Simpang Idola. [@1,5,6] The arrest took place in Hajoran
ward, Pandan district, Tapanuli Tengah, on the afternoon of Saturday 7 November
2026. [@1,2,5,6] District narcotics chief AKP Johannes Munthe said the case began with
a public tip that cannabis dealing and abuse were frequent at Simpang Idola.
[@1] The detained man said he is SP, alias Gelleng, aged 20, a resident of
Lopian ward in Badiri district. [@1,5]

A search found one package of cannabis wrapped in black plastic with a gross
weight of 80 grams and a black Redmi mobile phone. [@1,2,5,6] Under questioning at the
scene, SP said he had obtained the package from a man identified as MHP, alias
Uccok. [@1,3,4,5] Officers detained MHP later that same night in Lopian, Badiri. [@1,2,3,5,6] Uccok was arrested at a stall in Lingkungan IV, Lopian ward, Badiri at about 18.00 WIB on the same day. [@4] MHP alias Uccok is 41 and was allegedly the supplier. [@4] With this additional arrest, the total cannabis secured from the network in the one-day operation reached 200 grams, 80 grams from SP alias Gelleng and 120 grams from MHP alias Uccok. [@3,4] The search of Uccok found two packages of cannabis wrapped in black plastic with a total gross weight of 120 grams and a cream-coloured Realme mobile phone. [@3,4]
Both suspects and all the evidence were taken to Tapanuli Tengah police
headquarters for proceedings under Law Number 35 of 2009 on Narcotics. [@1,3,5]
