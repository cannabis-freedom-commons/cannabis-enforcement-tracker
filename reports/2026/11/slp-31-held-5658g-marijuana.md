San Luis Potosí's Guardia Civil Estatal detained 31 people in operations and
patrols across the state's four regions. [@1] The force described those detained
as suspected of involvement in diverse criminal acts. [@3] The state security
secretariat did not say what period the interventions covered or which offences
were attributed to those detained. [@1]

In the same balance, officers seized 565.8 grams of marijuana. [@1] The haul
also included 60 doses of a substance the force identified as cristal, which is
excluded from the cannabis figures here. [@1] Officers also reported two
firearms and 15 metal artefacts known as ponchallantas, spikes used to puncture
vehicle tyres. [@2] The statement was a statewide balance that did not break
down how many detentions or seizures belonged to each region. [@1]

The state security secretariat said the detentions came from permanent security
and prevention patrols in the four regions. [@2] The secretariat said it would
keep strengthening prevention, surveillance and anti-crime strategies. [@2]
