Seferihisar District Police officers searched an address in Ulamış Mahallesi in
Seferihisar, İzmir, belonging to a suspect identified only as F.Z.E. [@1] Crime
Prevention and Investigation Bureau officers raided the residence after
detecting suspicious activity there. [@2] Police said the search was part of
efforts against drug use and trafficking and the possession of unlicensed
weapons. [@2] After the search, judicial proceedings were initiated against the
suspect and the investigation continued. [@2]

Officers seized one Hint keneviri plant, the Turkish term for cannabis, from the
address. [@1] According to the police account, officers also seized one
unlicensed pistol and two unlicensed hunting rifles from the same residence.
[@1] Police said they also seized 147 rounds of 9x19 mm pistol ammunition and 23
shotgun cartridges. [@2]

Police confiscated the ammunition along with the weapons and the plant. [@2] The
district police described the wider activity as public-order work to ensure
peace and security across Seferihisar. [@2]
