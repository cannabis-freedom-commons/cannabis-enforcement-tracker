Military police detained two men, aged 19 and 21, during a stop on the BR-277 in
Guarapuava in the early hours of Wednesday 4 November 2026. [@1] The officers
found a white Volkswagen Gol and its driver stopped as ordered. [@1] A personal
search of the occupants found nothing, but officers inspecting the car found
several blocks in the boot weighing 45.3 kilograms in total and described as
similar to marijuana. [@1]

Officers also seized about one kilogram of a substance recorded in the police
report as dry cannabis. [@1] The article describes dry as a cannabis concentrate
produced by separating the trichomes, the plant structures that hold
cannabinoids including THC. [@1]

Brazil's Drug Law, Federal Law 11.343/2006, sets a penalty of five to 15 years
in prison plus a fine for transporting or storing drugs without authorisation.
