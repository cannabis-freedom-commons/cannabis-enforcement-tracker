Giza criminal court sentenced a worker to 10 years of aggravated imprisonment
for trafficking in hashish and for possessing a firearm without a licence in the
Manshiyat al-Qanatir police district. [@1] A second Arabic account of the ruling
names the same court, offence, term and police district. [@2]

The prosecution charged him with holding hashish for sale and promotion, and
with possessing a firearm without a permit from the competent authorities,
contrary to the weapons and ammunition law. [@1] The public prosecution referred
him to the criminal court after finishing its investigation and asked for the
penalty the law prescribes. [@1] The court reached its verdict after hearing the
case and the accusations set out in its papers. [@2]
