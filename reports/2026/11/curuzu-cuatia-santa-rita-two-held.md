Officers of the Curuzú Cuatiá Investigations Division (DIC) raided a house
suspected of hosting a drug-selling point, acting with the intervening
prosecutor's office. [@1] The procedure took place at about 18:00 on Tuesday at
a home in barrio Santa Rita, on calle 2 in Curuzú Cuatiá. [@1] Police said the
operation dismantled an alleged drug-selling point in Curuzú Cuatiá as a
continuation of work carried out over the weekend. [@1] Officers detained a
37-year-old woman and a 39-year-old man, who were taken to the police station.
[@1]

During the search, officers found 31 packets containing marijuana, 200,000 pesos
in cash and four mobile phones. [@1] The report put the estimated value of the
seized substance at about 2,682,000 pesos. [@1] The case was presented as
another drug “kiosco”, the term used in the area for a small neighbourhood
drug-selling point, found in Curuzú Cuatiá. [@1]

Police linked the raid to other raids carried out on 30 October and 1 November
within an investigation that remains under way. [@1]
