The Sohag Criminal Court on Sunday sentenced a 38-year-old worker to life
imprisonment for possessing nine automatic rifles, a shotgun and other firearms
for sale. [@1] In the same ruling the court gave him 10 years of aggravated
imprisonment for trafficking hashish in the Tahta markaz, a district of Sohag
Governorate in Upper Egypt. [@1]

The ruling panel was headed by Judge Hafni Abdel Fattah with judges Khaled
Mohamed El-Sheikh and Ahmed Ali Saqr. [@1] Detectives in the Tahta district
received information in 2026 that the man was trafficking firearms and drugs
there, and confirmed it through a secret informant before arresting him. [@1] He
was arrested with a quantity of hashish and several automatic and other
firearms, which the account says were intended for sale. [@1] He admitted
trafficking drugs and firearms when questioned and was referred to the criminal
court, which issued this ruling. [@1]
