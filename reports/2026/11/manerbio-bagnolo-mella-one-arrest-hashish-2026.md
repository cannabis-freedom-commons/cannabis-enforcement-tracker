Carabinieri arrested one man during a day of extraordinary checks in the towns
of Manerbio and Bagnolo Mella, in the Bassa Bresciana, and reported three other
people at liberty for alleged drug dealing. [@1] The operation ran from 1pm to
10pm in the two town centres and some commercial areas, focused on places
residents had flagged, and was staffed by the Manerbio, Bagnolo Mella, Gambara
and Ghedi stations with the Verolanuova company's patrol car unit. [@1] Officers
identified 35 people and checked 26 vehicles during the day. [@1]

In Piazza Aldo Moro in Manerbio, Carabinieri made personal and home searches and
arrested a Gambian man born in 1994 accused of possessing drugs for sale. [@1]
Police said the arrested man had 20 grams of hashish and 6 grams of cocaine in
11 doses, plus 655 euros in cash that investigators considered likely dealing
proceeds. [@1] A 27-year-old Moroccan man and a 17-year-old girl of Indian
origin, both known to police, were reported at liberty and police found 8 grams
of cocaine in nine doses, 4 grams of hashish and 100 euros in cash in their
possession, all seized. [@2] Prima Brescia identifies the younger of the two
reported in Manerbio as a 2009-born Indian woman resident in Brescia and
domiciled in Manerbio. [@3]

In Bagnolo Mella a 26-year-old Italian resident in the town, already known to
police, was reported at liberty after officers found 196 grams of hashish
divided into two blocks, which they seized. [@1] The Ghedi station separately
reported a 26-year-old Moroccan man to the prefecture as a drug user after he
was found with 0.8 grams of hashish, a measure distinct from the criminal
reports. [@1]

A judge at the Brescia court validated the arrest but has not convicted the man;
after his lawyer asked for time to prepare a defence, the case was postponed to
the second ten days of January 2027. [@1]
