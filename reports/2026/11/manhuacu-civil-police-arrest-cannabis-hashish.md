Civil Police in Manhuaçu, a municipality in Minas Gerais' Zona da Mata, arrested
one suspect in flagrante for drug trafficking on Wednesday 4 November 2026. [@1]
Police said the suspect bought and stored drugs to sell in the city, and that
the investigation mapped how he operated before officers approached him. [@1]

The operation began at the man's workplace, where officers found a small amount
of drugs, then moved to his home, where drugs were hidden in several rooms
including the refrigerator. [@1] The searches ended at the home of the suspect's
grandmother, on the upper floor of the same building where he lived, where Civil
Police officers seized most of the drugs. [@1]

In total, Civil Police took about 10 kilograms of mixed drugs out of
circulation, among them marijuana, hashish, ecstasy tablets and LSD, and seized
items and packaging used to divide the material. [@1] The suspect was arrested
in flagrante for drug trafficking, taken to the police station for legal
procedures, and then transferred to the prison system, where he was held at the
disposal of the court. [@1]
