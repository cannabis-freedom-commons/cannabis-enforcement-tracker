A Nozha misdemeanour-court judge renewed the pretrial detention of a foreign
national for 15 days pending investigation. [@1] The case involves six bottles
of hashish oil allegedly hidden in a shampoo-bottle parcel arriving from the
United States at the cargo village of Cairo International Airport. [@1]

The General Administration of Parcel Customs and the Egyptian Post customs
intercepted the United States-origin parcel in cooperation with the competent
security authorities at Cairo International Airport and the cargo village. [@1]
The inspected parcel weighed 6 kilograms in total and contained six containers
described as hair- and hand-care products. [@1] Airport anti-narcotics officers
examined the parcel and identified the seized material as cannabis oil, which
customs valued at about 12 million Egyptian pounds. [@1] A second account
likewise describes a United States-origin postal parcel of hair- and hand-care
products suspected of containing schedule-listed cannabis oil with an indicated
value of about 12 million Egyptian pounds. [@2]

Officers preserved the parcel, drew up customs report No. 5 of 2026, and sealed
it with customs wire and lead for referral to the central administration and the
competent authorities for legal procedures. [@1] The earlier account said the
exact nature of the material still required completion of technical and legal
procedures. [@2]
