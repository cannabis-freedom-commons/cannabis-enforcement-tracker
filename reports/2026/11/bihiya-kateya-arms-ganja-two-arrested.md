During the Wednesday raid near the temple, officers caught both youths with
illegal arms, cartridges and ganja. [@1,2,3,4,5,7] Police had received a tip that
offenders had gathered in the area planning a major criminal act. [@1,2]

Police suspect the arrested youths were preparing to carry out a major criminal
incident. [@1] Police took both youths into custody and brought them to the
station after the raid. [@1,2,3]

Police are now questioning both accused closely to find where the arms came from
and who is linked to the ganja trade. [@1,2] Officers are also trying to identify
other people involved in the same arms and ganja network. [@1,2] Police said they
will register a case under the relevant sections and send both accused to
judicial custody after questioning is complete. [@1,3]

Police recovered a country-made pistol, four cartridges and a total of 175.75 grams of ganja, according to a press note from superintendent of police Awadhesh Dixit. [@3,10,11] The other arrested suspect was identified as Awadh Bihari Prasad of Kateya village. [@3,10] Yadav told police he had bought the ganja from Prasad, and a raid on Prasad's shop recovered 170 grams of ganja; Prasad admitted his involvement. [@3,10]

Police registered Bihiya police station case 382/26 on 4 November 2026 under the Arms Act and the NDPS Act. [@6,7]

Police said Sanjit disclosed that he purchased the contraband from Awadh Bihari Prasad, also a resident of Kateya. [@7] Acting on this information, the police raided Prasad's shop and recovered another 170.7 grams of suspected cannabis. Prasad was subsequently arrested. [@7] According to the police, Sanjit has a criminal history, with four cases registered against him at Bihiya police station in 2018, 2019 and 2025. [@7] A search also led to the recovery of 5.75 grams of a substance suspected to be cannabis from his possession. [@7,12] An FIR has been registered at Bihiya police station in connection with the latest operation as Case No. 382/26, dated November 4, 2026, under relevant provisions of the Arms Act and the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act. [@7]

The main accused was identified as 28-year-old Sanjeet Yadav alias Kariya Yadav. [@8] Police also seized a weighing scale used for drug sales from Prasad's shop. [@9] Jagdishpur SDPO Ravi Kumar described the operation at a press conference at the Bihiya police station on Thursday. [@9]

The operation followed a tip that Sanjeet Yadav was waiting near a temple on the Kateya–Roopbandh road with illegal arms, and he threw the weapon from his waist into a paddy field as officers arrived. [@11] The team was led by Bihiya station house officer Bipin Bihari and was backed by the armed force when it caught Yadav. [@11] Sanjeet Yadav is the son of Rajnath Yadav and Awadh Bihari Prasad the son of Phudena Prasad, both of Kateya village. [@11]
