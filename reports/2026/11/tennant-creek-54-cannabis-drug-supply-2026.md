Around 7pm on 12 November 2026, detectives from the Tennant Creek Criminal
Investigation Branch apprehended a grey Toyota sedan 10 kilometres south of
Tennant Creek on the Stuart Highway. [@2] The 54-year-old driver of the vehicle
tested positive for cannabis. [@2]

He was refused police bail and was to appear before court on 13 November 2026.
[@2]

NT News reported that police seized $9,000 in cash and drugs during the Stuart
Highway stop. [@1]

ABC News confirmed the man was arrested on drug charges in Tennant Creek, found
with $9,000 in cash and cannabis in his car, charged, and refused bail. [@3]

The same police release was republished by Mirage News, confirming the charges
and seizure details. [@4]
