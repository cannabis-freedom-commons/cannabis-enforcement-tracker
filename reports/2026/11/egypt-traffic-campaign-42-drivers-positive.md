Egypt's General Traffic Directorate continued intensive traffic campaigns on all
roads and axes across the country to monitor the traffic situation, record
violations and achieve discipline in the streets. [@1] The campaigns recorded
111,813 traffic violations in 24 hours. [@1] The main offences were driving
without a licence, exceeding the speed limit, parking at random, using a mobile
phone at the wheel and breaching licence conditions. [@1]

Officers tested 1,069 drivers and 42 were positive for narcotics use. [@1]

A parallel campaign by security bodies on work zones along the Regional Ring
Road recorded 488 traffic violations in 24 hours. [@1] Those violations covered
carrying passengers against the rules, breaching licence conditions and failing
to meet safety and durability requirements. [@1] Officers tested 153 drivers on
the Regional Ring Road and six were positive for narcotics use. [@1] Six people
wanted under a total of 10 court rulings were detained, and one vehicle was held
for breaching traffic laws. [@1]
