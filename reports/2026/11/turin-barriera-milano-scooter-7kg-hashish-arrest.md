State police in Turin arrested a 25-year-old Moroccan man on suspicion of
possessing drugs for sale, and took him to the Lorusso e Cutugno prison on the
judicial authority's order. [@2]

A personal search found 14 round and 6 rectangular blocks of hashish weighing
more than 2 kg in total, and two sets of keys to two separate flats in the area.
[@2] In one of the flats officers found and seized about 5 kg of hashish in
blocks, 56 grams of pure cocaine hidden in a chest in the living room, and 2,370
euros in cash. [@2] The police statement describes the cash as proceeds of the
illicit activity. [@2]

The man was not legally resident in Italy and was arrested for drug possession
for sale by order of the judicial authority. [@1] He was then associated with
the Lorusso e Cutugno prison in Turin, the city's main pre-trial detention
house. [@1] Torino Oggi reported that the 20 hashish blocks carried by the man
weighed more than two kilograms and that one bunch of keys led officers to the
two flats. [@3] The Lorusso e Cutugno is the Turin prison where he was held, as
the reporters put it, behind the prison doors. [@3] Il Giornale Popolare
reproduced the police statement, describing the flat as the dealer's drug depot
of 5 kg of hashish, 56 grams of cocaine and 2,370 euros in cash. [@4]

The Turin police headquarters said the case is at the preliminary investigation
stage and the suspect is presumed innocent until a final judgment. [@2]
