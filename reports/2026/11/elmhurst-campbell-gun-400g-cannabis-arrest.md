Elmhurst police arrested Terrence D. Campbell, a 33-year-old from Chicago, after
officers found him with a loaded semiautomatic handgun and marijuana during a
traffic stop this week. [@1] Shortly before 1 a.m. on Monday, an officer spotted
Campbell driving a white Lincoln MKX and breaking traffic rules on Butterfield
Road near Illinois Route 83. [@1] According to police, Campbell had a prior
felony conviction that legally barred him from possessing guns. [@1] After the
stop, officers took Campbell to the DuPage County Jail and impounded his car.
[@1]

During the stop, officers found Campbell with a loaded Ruger 9mm semi-automatic
handgun. [@1] Officers also found Campbell with multiple bags containing more
than 400 grams of marijuana. [@1] Campbell was charged with unlawful possession
of a firearm by a felon, aggravated unlawful possession of a weapon and unlawful
possession of more than 100 but less than 500 grams of cannabis. [@1] Police
said Campbell was also cited for several traffic offenses arising from the stop.
[@1]

Police identified the driver as Terrence D. Campbell, 33, of Chicago, and
presented the encounter as a traffic stop that escalated into felony firearm and
cannabis charges. [@1]
