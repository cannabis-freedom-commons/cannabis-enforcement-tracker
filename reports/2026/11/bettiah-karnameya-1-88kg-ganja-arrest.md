Mufassil police in Bettiah raided the house of Karnameya resident Angad Mahto on
Wednesday night after receiving a secret tip-off. [@1] Officers arrested Angad
Mahto's son Vicky Kumar at the house during the raid. [@1] Angad Mahto himself
was not found at home when officers searched the house. [@1] Police registered a
first information report against both Angad Mahto and his son Vicky Kumar in
connection with the seizure. [@1] After questioning, police sent Vicky Kumar to
judicial custody. [@1]

Officers recovered 1 kg 880 g of ganja from Angad Mahto's house during the
search. [@1] Police said the ganja had been concealed in the house for sale.
[@1] Mufassil station house officer Kanchan Bhaskar confirmed that 1 kg 880 g of
ganja was recovered from Vicky Kumar's house. [@1]

Mufassil station sub-inspectors Girjanand and Amit Kumar were among the officers
who searched the house and recovered the ganja. [@1] Police said they had
received specific information that ganja was stored in Angad Mahto's house
before mounting the raid. [@1] The seized ganja was weighed in the presence of
the Chanpatia circle officer. [@1]
