Civil Police officers in Bauru arrested a 25-year-old man for drug trafficking
on the afternoon of Wednesday 11 November 2026. [@1] The arrest came during a
search-and-seizure warrant at a house in the Jardim Vitória neighbourhood. [@1]
The operation was run by the General Investigations Sector (SIG) of the Judicial
Police Centre (CPJ). [@1] It followed an investigation into reports of illegal
firearm possession, possible drug sales and handling stolen goods at the
address. [@1]

Police said their inquiries found movement typical of drug dealing, which
supported their request for the court warrant. [@1] Officers found the suspect
in front of the property. [@1] After being told about the warrant, he admitted
there were drugs inside. [@1] The search found several portions of marijuana and
a precision scale. [@1]

The man was arrested in flagrante, meaning caught in the act. [@1] He was taken
to the CPJ in Bauru. [@1] He remained at the disposal of the courts, awaiting a
custody hearing. [@1]
