Military police apprehended an adolescent on the night of Sunday 8 November 2026
on Servidão Tupi in the Tapera neighbourhood of Florianópolis for an act
analogous to drug trafficking. [@1] A Military Police patrol said it spotted the
youth because he matched the description of a person who had fled another police
team moments earlier. [@1] When he saw the patrol car, he tried to escape and
resisted the approach. [@1]

A search found small change and portions of marijuana and hashish on him. [@1] A
sweep of the immediate surroundings found the rest of the drugs hidden under a
pile of construction sand. [@1] In total, officers seized 99 portions of cocaine
weighing about 25 grams, seven portions of hashish weighing about 10 grams, five
portions of marijuana and R$597 in cash. [@1]

Officers took the adolescent to the Central de Polícia de Plantão da Capital,
the capital's on-duty central police station. [@1] The Polícia Militar is the
uniformed state military police that carried out the patrol. [@1]
