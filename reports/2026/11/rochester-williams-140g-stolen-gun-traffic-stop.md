Just after 9 p.m. on 10 November 2026, Rochester patrol officers stopped a
driver with a suspended license near the 1600 block of Civic Center Drive NW.
[@3] Officers identified the driver as 29-year-old Edward Williams of Rochester
and arrested him. [@3] Police say officers found a stolen handgun and suspected
drugs with him during the traffic stop. [@1]

Officers said they had probable cause to search the vehicle. [@3] The search
found a stolen handgun and approximately 140 grams of suspected marijuana in a
baggie. [@3] The complaint describes the gun as a Glock with an extended
magazine that had been reported stolen out of Roseville. [@5] The stop took
place on Civic Center Drive Northwest in Rochester. [@2]

A criminal complaint filed in Olmsted County District Court charged Williams
with illegal possession of a firearm by a person convicted of a crime of
violence, receiving stolen property, and a misdemeanor involving possession of
an open cannabis package in a motor vehicle. [@5] The Rochester Police
Department handled the stop and arrest. [@4]
