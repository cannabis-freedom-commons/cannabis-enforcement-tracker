On the afternoon of 3 November 2026, Novara’s Squadra Mobile arrested a
60-year-old Italian man living in Novara who had previous police records. [@2]
Officers watched him hand over drugs inside his car in Via Poerio and caught him
in the act after he tried to hide a packaged 0.6-gram dose of cocaine under the
car mat. [@2] The buyer received an administrative sanction for purchase for
personal use. [@1]

A search of his home found hashish divided into a block and several packets
ready for sale, with a total weight of almost 100 grams. [@2] Officers also
seized packaging material, a precision scale and 710 euros in cash described as
proceeds of the dealing. [@1] After the formalities, the man was taken to
Novara’s district prison at the disposal of the judicial authority. [@2]

The arrest was made during targeted observation and control operations against
drug dealing. [@3] Police said the measure rested on the evidence gathered so
far and that the presumption of innocence applies until a final judgment. [@2]
