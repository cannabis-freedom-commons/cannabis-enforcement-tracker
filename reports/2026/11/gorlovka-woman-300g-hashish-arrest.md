In the Donetsk People's Republic, officers of a separate patrol-police (PPS)
battalion together with drug-control officers of Russia's Gorlovka city police
identified a 37-year-old local woman as a distributor of banned substances. [@1]
Police detained her in Gorlovka as she was retrieving a master-klade, a
wholesale stash, from a hiding place. [@2] An investigator opened an attempted
drug-sale case against her and she was placed in a temporary detention facility
pending a decision on a restraint measure. [@1]

A personal search recovered about 300 grams of hashish, the cannabis component
recorded here, alongside about half a kilogram of clephedrone and alpha-PVP.
[@1] The hashish figure is the police's approximate weight and only that portion
is counted as cannabis here. [@1] Examination of her mobile phone led operatives
to coordinates of retail dead drops she had allegedly prepared. [@1] Officers
recovered seven more wrapped packets at those addresses, whose contents by
substance were not broken down and are not counted as cannabis. [@1]

Operatives are now working to establish the channels that brought the drugs into
the region. [@1]
