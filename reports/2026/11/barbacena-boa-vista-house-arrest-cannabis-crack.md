Military Police arrested a 32-year-old man in flagrante for drug trafficking and
association for trafficking in Barbacena, Minas Gerais, on the morning of Monday
9 November 2026. [@1] The operation involved actions across the São José and Boa
Vista neighbourhoods and resulted in the seizure of marijuana and crack. [@1]
Police teams carried out a court-ordered search and seizure warrant at a
residential property in bairro São José, entering after knocks went unanswered
to find the house unoccupied. [@2]

At the same time, another police team tracked the suspect's Mitsubishi ASX and
observed a man step out and hide an object on the pavement in bairro Boa Vista.
[@1] A search of the hiding spot yielded a bag containing a portion of marijuana
alongside 16 crack rocks. [@1]

Officers located and stopped the vehicle during tracking, and the driver
acknowledged ownership of the seized narcotics. [@3] The Military Police noted
that the man was already serving house arrest connected to a drug trafficking
investigation. [@1] The suspect was arrested in flagrante and taken with the
seized material to the Civil Police station for formal booking. [@2]
