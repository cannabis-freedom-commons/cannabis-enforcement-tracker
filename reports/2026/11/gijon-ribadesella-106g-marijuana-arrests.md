Two people aged 28 and 48 were arrested in Gijón for an alleged offence against
public health after officers surprised them with cocaine and marijuana in a
hospitality venue in the Laviada neighbourhood. [@1] The events took place on
the previous Tuesday, when Local Police officers went to the venue after
receiving a report of an altercation between several customers. [@1] During the
intervention officers found cocaine among the belongings of one of the people
involved and detained him. [@1]

The police investigation then identified a second man linked to the activity,
ending with the detention of both suspects and two entries and searches in homes
in the city. [@1] The pair possessed 286 grams of cocaine in three bags, 106
grams of marijuana buds and a precision scale for small-scale retail sale. [@1]
The first intervention took place on Calle Ribadesella in a joint operation
between the National Police and the Local Police. [@1] Both men were detained as
alleged perpetrators of an offence against public health in an action that
dismantled small-scale distribution of narcotic substances in the city. [@1]

Once police procedures were finished, the case report was sent to the court.
