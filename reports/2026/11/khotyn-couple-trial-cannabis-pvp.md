Prosecutors have sent a 23-year-old resident of Khotyn and his 20-year-old wife
for trial in Bukovyna, accusing them of illegal trafficking in narcotics and
psychotropic substances. [@1] The Chernivtsi Regional Prosecutor's Office filed
an indictment for illegal acquisition, storage, transportation and sale of
narcotics and psychotropic substances, including in especially large amounts,
under parts 2 and 3 of article 307 of the Criminal Code of Ukraine. [@2]
National Police investigators said the husband is charged under parts 2 and 3 of
article 307 and his wife under part 2. [@3] If their guilt is proved, the couple
faces up to 12 years in prison. [@1]

Investigators say the couple accepted orders through a Telegram channel and sent
banned substances to different regions of Ukraine. [@1] They posted the
substances using accounts opened in other people's names, or left them in hiding
places prepared in advance. [@1] Police documented 25 parcels of Alpha-PVP sent
to different regions of Ukraine. [@3] The couple also sold drugs in Bukovyna by
placing hiding places and sending customers the coordinates and photographs of
those spots. [@3]

During a search at the home of the accused, officers found and seized PVP,
amphetamine and cannabis. [@1] The man also handed officers a postal item
containing another batch of PVP. [@1] In all, officers removed more than 9.5 kg
of narcotic and psychoactive substances from illegal circulation, and experts
said more than 7 kg of that mass was PVP. [@1] Prosecutors said the black-market
price of what was seized exceeds 5 million hryvnias. [@2]

Chernivtsi police said they had detained a man, then described as a 22-year-old
resident of Khotyn district, who had organised the buying and selling of banned
substances through a Telegram channel. [@4] That police notice said officers had
detained a suspect in Chernivtsi Oblast who had set up a system for buying and
selling banned substances. [@4] The same notice said searches had found
Alpha-PVP, amphetamine and cannabis with a combined weight of about 9.5 kg,
valued at about 5.7 million hryvnias on the black market. [@4] The November
prosecutor's statement dated that home search to June of the previous year. [@2]
