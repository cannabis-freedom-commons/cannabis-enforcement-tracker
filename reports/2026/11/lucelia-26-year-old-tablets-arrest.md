Military police in Lucélia, in the interior of São Paulo state, arrested a
26-year-old man in the street outside his own home on the afternoon of Sunday 8
November 2026, accusing him of drug trafficking. [@1] Officers on patrol said
they saw the man apparently smoking marijuana in front of his house and stopped
him as he tried to go inside when the patrol car approached. [@1]

A search of his person found a marijuana cigarette and 40 reais in cash. [@1]
The man told officers there were more drugs inside the house, and a search of
the property found eight blocks (tabletes) of marijuana, one portion of cocaine
and 14 portions of crack, plus 80 reais more in cash, a knife and a pair of
scissors with marijuana residue. [@1]

Siga Mais reported that civilian police at the on-duty station in Adamantina, a
neighbouring town, confirmed the arrest in flagrante. [@2] Siga Mais reported
that the arrest was made under article 33 of Brazil's drug law 11.343/2006,
which covers trafficking. [@2] The man remained held at the disposal of the
courts, and the drugs were seized. [@1]
