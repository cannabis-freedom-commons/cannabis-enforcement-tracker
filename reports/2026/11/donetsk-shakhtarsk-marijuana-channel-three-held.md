Criminal-investigation officers of the Kalininsky district police department in
the Russian Interior Ministry's Donetsk directorate stopped the activity of
three local residents who police said sold marijuana in the Donetsk People's
Republic. [@1] In late October officers detained a 61-year-old Donetsk resident.
[@1]

A search of his home found four packets and 15 paper wraps of plant material
weighing 58 grams in total, which an examination identified as cannabis. [@1]
The man denied intending to sell drugs, but electronic scales and packaging
material found in the search were used as evidence against him. [@1]
Investigators opened an attempted drug-sale case against him under part 3 of
article 30 and point b of part 3 of article 228.1 of Russia's Criminal Code.
[@1]

Further operational work led police to his supplier, a previously convicted
resident of Shakhtarsk born in 1975. [@1] Officers searched the accomplice's
flat and a storeroom in the stairwell, finding nine paper wraps of plant
material weighing more than 119 grams. [@1] The Shakhtarsk suspect told police
he had bought the drug from an acquaintance for personal use. [@1]

Police then identified a third member of the chain, a local resident born in
1980 who kept plant material in a plastic bag and eight glass jars of one to
three litres on open ground near his home. [@1] A chemical test confirmed that
the jars held more than 1.1 kilograms of marijuana. [@1] That suspect said he
had found cannabis seeds by chance, grown the plants and prepared the drug
solely for his own consumption. [@1]

Police dismissed the suspects' explanations, citing the large batch packed into
single doses and the packaging material and high-precision scales found in the
searches. [@1] A district police investigator has opened cases against two of
the accomplices under part 3 of article 30 and point g of part 4 of article
228.1 of the Criminal Code, which carry up to 20 years in prison. [@1] The same
account was published on the interior ministry's own Telegram channel for the
Donetsk People's Republic. [@2]
