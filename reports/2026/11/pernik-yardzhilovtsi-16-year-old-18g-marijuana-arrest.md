Police in Pernik Province detained a 16-year-old boy in the village of
Yardzhilovtsi and a 20-year-old woman near the central city park in Pernik in
two separate checks for drug possession. [@2] Officers of Pernik's First
District Police Department (Първо районно управление) carried out both checks.
[@1] Shortly before 16:00 on 4 November 2026, officers searched the teenager,
who is from the village of Cherna Gora, in Yardzhilovtsi and found a bag of
about 18 grams of dry green leaf matter and a cigarette butt. [@2] A field drug
test on the substance reacted for marijuana. [@2] The teenager was detained for
up to 24 hours. [@1]

At about 40 minutes after midnight, officers checked the 20-year-old woman in
the area of Pernik's central city park. [@1] A search of the woman found a bag
of dry green leaf matter that also tested positive for marijuana in the field
test. [@2] The woman was detained for up to 24 hours in police custody. [@1]

Fast-track criminal proceedings were opened in both cases, and investigations
continue under the supervision of the prosecutor's office. [@1] The Pernik
regional police directorate described the same two detentions in its bulletin
for 5 November, calling the two detained people a boy and a girl. [@2]
