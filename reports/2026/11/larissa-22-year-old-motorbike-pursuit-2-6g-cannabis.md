On the afternoon of 11 November 2026 in Larissa, police of the two-wheeled
police unit (ΔΙ.ΑΣ.) arrested a 22-year-old foreign national, against whom a
criminal file was opened for violation of narcotics legislation, defiance and
dangerous driving. [@1]

While riding a motorcycle, he failed to stop at a police check, accelerated and
drove dangerously, including against the flow on one-way streets. [@1]

When officers immobilised him he dropped a nylon packet containing 2.6 grams of
raw cannabis, which was seized. [@1]
