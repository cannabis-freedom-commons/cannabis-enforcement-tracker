A special NDPS court in Aligarh convicted a man named Jitendra, known as Jhagdu,
and sentenced him to six months' rigorous imprisonment for possessing ganja.
[@2] The court also fined him 5,000 rupees, with one month's additional simple
imprisonment if the fine is not paid. [@1] The trial was heard by Special Judge
(NDPS Act) and Additional District and Sessions Judge Ghulam Mustafa in Court
No. 5. [@2]

The court ordered that the time he had already spent in jail be set off against
the sentence. [@2] He was convicted under section 20(b)(ii)(A) of the NDPS Act,
India's narcotics law. [@2] Police had arrested him around 11:10 pm on 20
September 2024 under the Chirauliya bridge with 1.012 grams of ganja. [@2] He
lived in Hardaspur village in the Lodha police station area, and the case was
registered at Madrak police station, both in Aligarh district. [@2] Police filed
the case under sections 8 and 20 of the NDPS Act. [@2]
