Five men aged 20, 24, 39, 45 and 52 were detained in Varna after police checks
found methamphetamine and marijuana on them, the press centre of the Varna
regional police directorate said. [@1] The officers came from the municipal
police and the city's first, second, third and fourth district police stations.
[@1]

Expert reports established the exact type of the narcotics found. [@1] The men
were taken into police custody for up to 24 hours. [@1]

One fast-track and four pre-trial proceedings were opened, and the files were
reported to the prosecutor's office. [@1]
