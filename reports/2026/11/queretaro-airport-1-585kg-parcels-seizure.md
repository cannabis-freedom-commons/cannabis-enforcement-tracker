National Guard officers found about 1 kg 585 g of herb with characteristics
similar to marijuana while inspecting parcels at Querétaro International
Airport, in El Marqués, state prosecutors said. [@1] The inspection happened
during a routine check by Guardia Nacional officers in the parcel area of a
private courier company at the airport. [@1]

X-ray equipment showed irregularities in seven cardboard boxes and four plastic
bags. [@1] Inside the packages officers also found 56 vape devices with apparent
cannabis oil, three cigarettes and 15 gummies with possible cannabis extract.
[@1]

The packages also contained an object with the characteristics of a firearm,
according to the federal prosecutor's office. [@1] No one was reported arrested
or charged in the case. [@1] The items went to the federal public prosecutor's
office attached to the Specialised Regional Control Prosecutor's Office (FECOR),
which opened an investigation into offences against health and the transport of
a firearm. [@1] The Attorney General's Office (FGR) asked the Federal
Ministerial Police to take part and ordered expert examinations to establish the
composition of the substances and the nature of the object seized. [@1] ADN
Informativo, a Querétaro outlet, gave the same account on the same day. [@2]
