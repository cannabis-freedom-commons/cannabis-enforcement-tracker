Jamner police raided a field in Maladbadi shivar, in Jalgaon district's Jamner
taluka, at about 12.15 pm on Thursday, 5 November 2026, after a tip-off that
cannabis was being grown there. [@1,5] Police said the field held fresh, green
ganja plants 7 to 8 feet tall carrying leaves, flowers and buds. [@1]

Officers uprooted the plants and seized them; the material weighed 13 kg 430 g.
[@1,3,4,5] Police valued the seized plants at ₹3,35,750 using a market rate of ₹25,000
per kg. [@1,3,4,5] The suspect, farmer Anil Dagdu Patil of Maladbadi, fled the field
when he saw the police party arriving. [@1,3,4,6]

He is accused of growing the cannabis illegally on his own land. [@2,5] Jamner
police registered crime No. 552/2026 against him under sections 8(b) and 20(a)
of the NDPS Act, the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, on the
complaint of constable Rajesh Vilas Lahase. [@1,3,4] Inspector Nivrutti Borhade and
sub-inspector Krishna Solunke visited and inspected the site, and Solunke is
continuing the investigation under Borhade's guidance. [@1] The cannabis was
intercropped among crops in the field, Jalgaon Live News reported. [@2,3] Jalgaon
Live reported that police were searching for the suspect who fled, and that he
had not been caught in the raid. [@2]

The plants were green and wet when officers seized them, Breaking Maharashtra reported. [@6] The raid party named by police included sub-inspectors Ashok Kokate and Krishna Solunke, constables including Sahil Tadvi, Umesh Chaudhari, Raju Tayde and Chandrakant Patil, and driver Gyaneshwar Badgujar, eSakal reported. [@5] The raid was carried out under the guidance of Jalgaon superintendent of police Shrikant Dhivare and additional superintendents Sandeep Gavit and Ashok Nakhate, Breaking Maharashtra reported.
