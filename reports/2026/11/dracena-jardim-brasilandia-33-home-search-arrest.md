Civil Police in Dracena, in the interior of São Paulo state, arrested a
33-year-old man for drug trafficking in flagrante on Friday 6 November 2026.
[@1] The arrest came during the execution of a search and seizure warrant at a
house on Rua Mário Quintana, in the Jardim Brasilândia neighbourhood. [@1]

The warrant was issued by the courts after a request from the police and a
favourable opinion from the Public Prosecutor's Office, following an
investigation by the specialised General Investigations Unit (DIG) and the Drugs
Investigations Unit (Dise). [@1] In the house, officers found portions of crack
and marijuana, a sum of money in notes of different values and a mobile phone,
and also seized a motorcycle. [@1] Police said the warrant was meant to deepen
the investigation into the storing and selling of drugs. [@1]

The man was arrested on suspicion of drug trafficking and remained in custody at
the disposal of the courts, awaiting a custody hearing. [@1] O Imparcial, an
outlet based in Presidente Prudente, reported the same Friday arrest of a
33-year-old man in a home search in Jardim Brasilândia, with crack, marijuana,
money, a phone and a motorcycle seized. [@2]
