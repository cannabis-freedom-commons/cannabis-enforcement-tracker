Milos Miletic, a 38-year-old Serbian man, was sentenced to 12 months in prison
and fined 3,000 euros after admitting to all nine charges against him before
Magistrate Caroline Farrugia Frendo. [@1] The charges included possession of
cannabis and cannabis resin, along with cocaine, MDMA, ketamine, 2C-B and other
psychoactive substances in circumstances indicating the drugs were not for his
personal use. [@1] He was also charged with driving without insurance and
driving carelessly, negligently or dangerously in Mosta. [@1]

The case began on 29 September 2026, when Miletic was in a motorcycle accident
in Mosta that left him seriously injured. [@1] Police investigations after the
accident led them to the drugs concealed in a compartment of his motorcycle.
[@1] He had first pleaded not guilty at his arraignment the previous month and
reversed that plea on Thursday. [@1]

The court took account of his clean criminal record and relatively early guilty
plea, and noted that the offences carry a mandatory effective prison sentence.
[@1] The court ordered the destruction of the drugs once the judgment becomes
final and told him to pay the expenses of court-appointed experts. [@1] He was
also disqualified from driving for 12 months. [@1] A copy of the judgment was
ordered sent to Home Affairs Minister Glenn Bedingfield should deportation be
required. [@1]
