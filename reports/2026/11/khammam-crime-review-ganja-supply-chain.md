Khammam police commissioner Sunil Dutt held a crime review meeting at the police
commissionerate on 12 November 2026. [@2] He told officers to investigate the
ganja supply chain thoroughly, identify major suppliers and trace the sources of
illegal transport and distribution. [@2] The commissioner said officers should
go beyond users and question the people who supplied them ganja. [@1] All the
accused should face strict legal action, he said. [@1]

He told officers to review trial proceedings regularly. [@1] He also asked them
to identify mistakes made during investigations and correct them. [@1] He told
them to record case evidence in the e-Sakshya application as prescribed and to
file quality charge sheets in court within the set deadlines. [@2]

He ordered officers to raise public awareness of the PM RAHAT scheme, under
which the government provides up to ₹1.5 lakh in cashless medical treatment for
road accident victims. [@1] He called for special surveillance on illegal sand
transport from neighbouring states and continuous checks at border checkposts.
[@1] He ordered special surveillance on repeat offenders. [@1] Officers were
also told to check second-hand vehicle dealers' registration, ownership transfer
and other verification documents. [@1]

Additional DCP Ramanujam and several ACPs and inspectors attended the meeting.
