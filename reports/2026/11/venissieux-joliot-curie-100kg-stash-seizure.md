After a long investigation, the police drug squad seized the drug stock held in
a so-called nourrice stash flat in the Joliot-Curie sector of Vénissieux, in the
Lyon metropolitan area. [@1] The flat served as a storage place supplying
dealing points in the commune and probably elsewhere in the urban area. [@1] The
stash stood near the dealing points known as the 103 and the 129, not far from
the T4 tram stop. [@1]

Officers recovered about 100 kilos of drugs, described as essentially cannabis
with a small quantity of cocaine. [@1] Lyon Capitale, citing Le Progrès,
likewise described the find as about 100 kilos, mainly cannabis with a small
quantity of cocaine. [@2] The premises had been under investigation by the
brigade des stupéfiants before the seizure. [@2] The same boulevard had
previously seen large police deployments after several shootings in March and
April. [@1]
