Falakata police in Alipurduar district, West Bengal, used drone cameras to spot
cannabis, called ganja locally, being grown secretly in household courtyards.
[@1] Teams then went door to door, raiding about 50 houses and cutting the
cannabis plants growing there, which they burned on the spot. [@1] The operation
on Friday, 6 November 2026, caused a stir across the area. [@1] After the
operation police issued a strong warning to the owners of the houses concerned.
[@1]

The action followed orders from the Alipurduar district superintendent of police
for a continuing surveillance campaign against narcotics in the area. [@1]
Several police teams deployed with drone cameras in the Mayradanga, Shalkumar,
Devgaon, Jateshwar, Guabarnagar and Falakata-II gram panchayat areas of Falakata
block. [@1] The drone cameras captured images of cannabis plants growing in the
courtyards of several houses, and police then went to those houses to act. [@1]
Inspector-in-charge Tamal Das of Falakata police station led the campaign. [@1]

Police sources said cannabis cultivation was found in the yards of an average of
eight to ten houses in each gram panchayat area. [@1] No case of cannabis grown
in the open was found. [@1] Police said anyone found illegally growing cannabis
in a house yard or compound in future would face strict legal action. [@1]
