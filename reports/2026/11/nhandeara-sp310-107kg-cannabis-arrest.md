Highway police arrested a man on suspicion of drug trafficking on the afternoon
of Thursday 5 November 2026 on the Rodovia Feliciano Sales Cunha (SP-310) in
Nhandeara, north-west São Paulo state. [@1] He was driving a grey car carrying
107.7 kg of cannabis in flower and skunk, the drugs packed into the trunk and
the back seat. [@1]

The car was stopped at kilometre 512 during Operação Fluminis Custos, after the
FICCO task force alerted that it might be carrying an illegal load. [@2] The
stop was made by the Tático Ostensivo Rodoviário, the highway patrol unit of the
Military Police, from its 3rd Highway Battalion, and the operation also involved
FICCO. [@2] Officers found 87 blocks of pressed cannabis weighing 87.150 kg and
34 packets of skunk, a more potent form of the drug, weighing 20.550 kg. [@1]
The Military Police said the officers found 87 blocks and three pieces of
pressed cannabis, alongside the 34 packets of skunk. [@2] The driver already had
a criminal record for robbery, and officers also seized the car and a mobile
phone. [@1]

He was arrested in the act and taken with the car and the drugs to the Federal
Police in São José do Rio Preto, where he remained in custody. [@2] The state
Military Police estimated the seizure dealt organised crime a loss of R$380,000.
[@2] The arrest came less than 48 hours after TOR and FICCO found 549.6 kg of
cannabis in Ouroeste on Wednesday 4 November, a separate case. [@1]
