Coimbatore police recorded two ganja arrests in Saravanampatti and Race Course
cases that were grouped together as two arrests for ganja sale. [@1] In
Saravanampatti, officers patrolling the Kunninayakkar Thottam area stopped a man
who was standing there in a suspicious manner and searched him. [@1] The search
showed that the man was holding ganja, the local term for cannabis, for sale.
[@1] Questioning identified him as 28-year-old Rahim Batcha from Nalbari in
Assam, who was staying at Chinnavedampatti and working in a laser-cutting
company. [@1]

Officers lodged Rahim Batcha in jail and seized 1.130 kg of ganja from him in
that Saravanampatti catch. [@1] In the other catch, Race Course police lodged
29-year-old Sayith from Palakkad in Kerala in jail for possessing ganja. [@1]
Both catches were reported in Coimbatore, with the quantified seizure coming
from Kunninayakkar Thottam in Saravanampatti and the second arrest handled by
Race Course police. [@1]
