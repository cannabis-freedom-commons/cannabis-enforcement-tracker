The Ninth Circuit of the Asyut Criminal Court on 7 November 2026 sentenced a
defendant to 15 years of rigorous imprisonment after convicting him of
trafficking opium and hashish weighing more than five kilograms in total and of
holding an unlicensed firearm and ammunition. [@1] The convicted man was a
38-year-old worker identified as Ahmed A., who lived in Maragha centre in Sohag
Governorate and was said to trade wholesale and semi-wholesale drugs across
villages and centres of southern Asyut. [@1] Asyut Security Directorate officers
working with the General Department for Drug Control had arrested him in May
2026. [@1]

Investigators said secret sources reported he planned to deliver drugs to a
client near the Al-Nakhila crossing on the western road in Abu Tig district.
[@1] A force led by Major Ahmed Thamarat with Major Fathi Tawson set a mobile
ambush there and caught him riding an unlicensed tuk-tuk, a three-wheeled
motorbike. [@1] His personal search yielded one piece of hashish and a bag with
four wraps of opium, plus a locally made shotgun with one cartridge loaded and
two more cartridges of the same calibre, 1,200 Egyptian pounds and a mobile
phone. [@1] Searching the tuk-tuk found a plastic sack hidden under his feet
with 29 large wraps of opium and another bag with 15 large pieces of hashish.
[@1] The Asyut chemical laboratory weighed the hashish at a net 473.90 grams for
the 15 pieces plus 99.80 grams for the single piece, together 573.7 grams. [@1]
The same laboratory weighed the opium at 4,275 grams for the 29 wraps plus 55.60
grams for the four wraps. [@1] Confronted with the seizures, he admitted holding
the drugs for trafficking and the firearm to protect that trade. [@1] He added
that the money was proceeds of the trade, the phone was for contacting clients
and the motorbike was for moving and promoting drugs. [@1]

The criminal evidence department found the firearm and three cartridges complete
and fit for use. [@1] After the investigation the First Attorney General for
South Asyut referred the detained man to the criminal court, which imposed the
15-year term. [@1] The bench was headed by Counselor Ahmed Mohamed Halawa with
members Mohamed Ayman Saddah, Mohamed Samir Al-Tamawy and Ramy Mohamed Samir.
