Police arrested eleven people in Eastern Macedonia and Thrace between 31 October
and 4 November 2026 during targeted anti-drug operations. [@1] Those arrested
were ten Greeks and one foreign national, and police opened ten separate case
files on drug-law violations. [@1]

In Drama, drug-squad officers arrested two Greek men on the night of 4 November
2026 during a drug handover. [@2] Officers found two packages of raw cannabis
weighing 8.14 grams on the first of them, which police said he had bought from
the second. [@1] A search of a house and a storage room the second man used to
hide drugs found three more packages of raw cannabis weighing 1 kg and 312.16 g.
[@1] That search also turned up a precision scale, a sum of cash and a mobile
phone. [@1]

In Drama on the afternoon of 2 November 2026, officers arrested a Greek man
after finding two packages holding 65.3 grams of raw cannabis and a precision
scale inside his car. [@2] Officers at the Drama pre-departure detention centre
for foreigners arrested a foreign national on the afternoon of 31 October 2026
after finding 4.82 grams of dried plant material of the synthetic drug type
called BONZAI in a parcel addressed to him. [@1] In checks in Evros, Komotini
and Xanthi, officers arrested two Greek men in each area, six in all. [@2] Those
six checks yielded small amounts of raw cannabis, six cannabis cigarettes, two
grinders and seven drug tablets, depending on the case. [@1]

The case files went to the competent prosecutors, and police departments in
Orestiada, Alexandroupoli, Drama, Xanthi and Komotini carried out the
preliminary inquiries. [@1] The operations were part of Greek Police targeted
drives against the spread of drugs in Eastern Macedonia and Thrace. [@1]
