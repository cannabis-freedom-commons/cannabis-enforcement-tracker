Military police arrested two men aged 23 and 26 on the afternoon of Tuesday 3
November in Barracão on suspicion of drug trafficking. [@1] As patrol cars
arrived at the indicated address, one suspect fled out the back while the other
two were detained. [@1] The two detained men were taken with the seized
materials to the Civil Police station in Santo Antônio do Sudoeste for
procedures. [@1]

A personal search found 52 crack rocks packaged ready for sale plus money in
foreign currency. [@1] A house search with the Santa Catarina Military Police
canine unit seized about 287 grams of marijuana, 19 grams of crack, 392 grams of
marijuana seeds and coca leaves. [@1] Officers also found nine plants with
marijuana characteristics behind the house. [@1] Two mobile phones were seized
during the operation. [@1]

The operation was carried out by the Barracão Military Police team of the 21st
Battalion with support from the Santa Catarina Military Police. [@1] The case
began with a complaint that a residence in Bairro Alvorada was being used as a
drug-selling point. [@1] The complainant also reported being threatened by one
suspect, who allegedly fired two gunshots toward him. [@1]
