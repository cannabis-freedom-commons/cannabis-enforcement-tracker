Mexican Army, National Guard, Fuerza de Reacción Inmediata Zacatecas and state
prosecutor's investigative police personnel arrested four people during security
and surveillance patrols in different parts of Moyahua municipality. [@1,3]
AlkimiaTV, citing the state Peace and Security Construction Board, reported the
same four arrests in Moyahua and the same seizure figures. [@2] When the
officers appeared, one of the men got on a motorcycle and tried to flee, and
when they caught him they found the bike had been reported stolen on 24 May
2026. [@1,3]

Officers searching the area and the other people seized 27 wrappers of green,
dry herb with characteristics of marijuana. [@1,3] The same search produced 33
doses of a granulated substance similar to methamphetamine and 11 wrappers of
white powder similar to cocaine, which are recorded here only as part of the
same event. [@1] Officers also seized 26 metal objects known as ponchallantas,
used to damage tyres and block traffic. [@1] AlkimiaTV put the operation's haul
at 71 doses of suspected drugs, counting the 27 marijuana wrappers, the
methamphetamine doses and the cocaine wrappers together. [@2]

The detainees were identified as Alejandro N., 26; César Alfredo N., 21, a
Mexico City native; Miguel Ángel N., 25; and Guadalupe N., 24. [@1] The four
were handed to the competent authority with the drugs, the metal objects and the
stolen Italika FT150 motorcycle, a 2026 model, for the corresponding
investigations. [@1]
