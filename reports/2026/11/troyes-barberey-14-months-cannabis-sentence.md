Ramzi Nefzaoui, 47, was sentenced by the criminal court in Troyes on 9 November
to 14 months in firm prison. [@1] The case was heard at the Troyes court on
Monday 9 November 2026. [@1] He was well known to the justice system, with 16
entries on his criminal record. [@1] The court issued an arrest warrant against
him. [@1]

On the evening of 6 December 2025, gendarmes were carrying out a roadside check
at Barberey-Saint-Sulpice. [@1] During the operation, officers arrested a man
travelling in a Renault Laguna. [@1] A very characteristic smell of cannabis was
coming from the vehicle. [@1] Officers found sachets of resin and herb inside
the vehicle, along with 80 euros in cash and a scale. [@1] The driver tested
positive for cannabis use. [@1]

Examination of his mobile phone later confirmed suspicions that he was involved
in drug trafficking. [@1]
