Egypt's Interior Ministry said a video circulating on social media showed a man
using drugs as he sat on a tricycle, a three-wheeled vehicle, in a street in
Giza. [@1,3,5,6,7,10] The ministry said investigators identified the man in the video and
arrested him. [@1,3,5,6,8,10] It said he was a driver who lived in the Kerdasa
police-station district. [@2,3,5,6,7,10] Questioned, he admitted the offence as shown in
the video and said he had used drugs on 5 November while stopped in a street in
the Boulak El-Dakrour police district. [@1,3,5,6,7,10]

Al Masry Al Youm attributed the account to an Interior Ministry statement issued
that day. [@2] Cairo 24 reported that police identified and arrested the man
after the video spread. [@3] The ministry said the legal measures had been taken
against the accused. [@2,3,5,6,7,10] The accounts describe the drug only as narcotics,
without naming a substance. [@1,10] Masrawy headlined its report with the driver "using al-keif", the Egyptian term for hashish, on a tuk-tuk. [@13] They ordered any seized items held until the investigation ends. [@12] They asked security services to trace where the drugs came from and whether he had obtained them from someone or was linked to others who held or sold them. [@12] Prosecutors sent a sample of the seized items to the state chemical laboratory to establish what it was and whether it contained narcotics, and asked for a technical report. [@12] The reports give no quantity of the drug and do
not say whether any was seized with him. [@3] No charge or court appearance has
been reported. [@1] Giza's public prosecution ordered him held for four days pending investigation on a charge of drug use. [@4,11] The accused gave his confession to the investigating authorities in Giza, confirming that he was the man in the video. [@9]
