The judicial police brigade of Sousse city arrested one man suspected of
trafficking narcotics after a week of surveillance and investigation. [@1]
Several wanted notices for drug trafficking had been issued against the suspect.
[@1]

The suspect came from a neighbouring governorate and dealt drugs between Tunisia
and a number of Arab and European countries. [@1] Officers seized a quantity of
cocaine and a quantity of cannabis resin known locally as zatla during a raid on
a house in the Khezama area. [@1]
