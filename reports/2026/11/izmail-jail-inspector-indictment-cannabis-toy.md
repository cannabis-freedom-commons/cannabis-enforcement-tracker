Prosecutors of the Odesa Region Prosecutor's Office sent to court the case
against a former junior inspector of the Izmail pre-trial detention centre in
Odesa Oblast. [@2] The jail was the Izmail pre-trial detention centre, according
to Suspilne Odesa and Informer. [@3] Investigators say the 25-year-old bought
methadone, cannabis and the psychotropic substance PVP in July 2026 to sell to
people held in custody. [@2] According to the State Bureau of Investigation, he
agreed with one of the people held there to deliver the drugs onto the jail's
grounds. [@3]

He put the drugs in a plastic wrap and hid it inside a soft SpongeBob toy, which
he carried into the institution in a bag among his personal belongings. [@1]
During a search of his belongings at the checkpoint, staff found and seized
0.067 g of methadone, more than 73 g of cannabis and 4.5 g of PVP; the tracker
records only the cannabis weight. [@1,5] Suspilne reported that the jail's
internal control staff made the find on a subsequent attempt to bring the banned
substances in. [@3] Informer reported that the detection was carried out with
the assistance of the SBU, Ukraine's security service. [@4]

Prosecutors charged him under part 2 of article 307 of Ukraine's Criminal Code,
and the investigation was carried out by the State Bureau of Investigation's
territorial unit in Mykolaiv, based in Odesa. [@1] The charge is unlawful
acquisition and transport of drugs and an especially dangerous psychotropic
substance with intent to sell, under part 2 of article 307. [@2] The pre-trial
investigation is complete and the indictment has been sent to court, and the man
remains in custody, facing up to 10 years in prison with confiscation of
property if convicted. [@3] He was notified of the suspicion in September 2026.
[@1] Informer reported that the case was investigated as part of the nationwide
Stop Traffik special operation. [@4]
