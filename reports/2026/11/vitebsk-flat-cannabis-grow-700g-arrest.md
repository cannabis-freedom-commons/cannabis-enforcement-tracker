Narcotics officers in the Vitebsk region detained a 39-year-old resident of
Vitebsk, the regional centre, on suspicion of illegal drug trafficking, the
Interior Ministry's press service said. [@2] The officers serve in the region's
drug control unit, part of the internal affairs directorate of the Vitebsk
Regional Executive Committee. [@1]

The man had fitted out a room in his own flat for growing cannabis. [@1] At his
place of residence, officers found and seized more than 700 grams of marijuana,
which the ministry described as a dangerous narcotic. [@1] They also took four
pots with cannabis bushes, specialised equipment for growing narcotic-containing
plants and a device for consuming drugs. [@2] Andrei Zyabko, the unit's acting
head, gave the account of the search and seizure. [@1]

The detained man told police he had grown the cannabis for his own use and not
for sale. [@1] A criminal case was opened against him for illegal drug
trafficking. [@1] He faces up to five years in prison. [@1]
