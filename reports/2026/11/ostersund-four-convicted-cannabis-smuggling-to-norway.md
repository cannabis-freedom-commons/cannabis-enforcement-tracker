Four men had to answer at Östersund District Court for transporting drugs to
northern Norway. [@1] Prosecutors charged four men from Värmland with smuggling
after suspected smuggling trips between Gothenburg, Värmland and Norway. [@2]

The prosecutor said the cannabis was bought in Gothenburg and transported
through the county to Norway, where it was sold on twice in April. [@2] The case
began on the morning of 29 April 2026, when police stopped a car on Stuguvägen
in Östersund on its way from the Lillängerondellen roundabout. [@3] Three people
were in the car, and a search found narcotics-classed medicines and just under 2
kg of suspected cannabis. [@3] The indictment covered possession and transport
of several kilos of cannabis and suspected smuggling across the Norwegian
border. [@2]

The investigation showed no gang link to the operation, the prosecutor said.
[@2] A fifth person, a woman who had been held in custody, remained suspected of
a drug offence but was not charged with taking part in the men's operation. [@2]
