A 39-year-old man from Bergisch Gladbach had passed his driving test and was
said to have celebrated with cannabis, cocaine and amphetamine. [@2] Traffic
officers of the Rheinisch-Bergischer Kreis police were driving along
Duckterather Weg at about 10:00 on Wednesday 11 November 2026. [@1] A Volkswagen
ahead of them had a rear left side door that appeared not properly closed, and
the inspection sticker was not correctly attached. [@1] They stopped the driver
for a traffic check at the junction with Hermann-Löns-Straße. [@1]

The 39-year-old proudly showed officers a provisional driving licence that had
been issued only the day before. [@1] During the check he showed clear signs of
impairment. [@1] He told police he had celebrated passing the test the evening
before and had used various intoxicants. [@1] A voluntary preliminary drug test
was positive for cannabis, cocaine and amphetamine. [@1]

Officers took him to a nearby hospital for a blood sample and temporarily banned
him from driving a motor vehicle. [@1] Police said he might have to give the
licence back after less than 24 hours. [@1]
