Officers from the Batalhão de Força Tática went to an events venue on Rua
Leopoldo Machado in the Jesus de Nazaré neighbourhood in central Macapá after a
report of possible drug dealing during a party. [@1] A man approached the
officers during patrolling and said drugs were being sold inside the venue, and
police brought in another team before entering to search people at the party.
[@1] No one was arrested because officers could not identify who discarded the
drugs. [@1]

During the searches, someone threw a plastic tube containing substances
described as resembling cocaine and marijuana onto the hall floor. [@1] Officers
said the large crowd and insufficient lighting made it impossible to identify
who had thrown the tube. [@1] The person in charge of the venue said he had only
rented the property for the party and did not know of any illegal activity. [@1]
Police seized the material and sent it to Ciosp, and the person responsible for
the drugs was not identified during the operation. [@1]

The occurrence was recorded at about 2:24 a.m. on Sunday, 1 November 2026. [@1]
