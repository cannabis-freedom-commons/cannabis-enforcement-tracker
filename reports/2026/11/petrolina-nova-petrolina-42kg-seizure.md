Civil police officers from the 12th Drug Trafficking Repression Precinct seized
42 kilograms of marijuana at Residencial Nova Petrolina in Petrolina in the
early hours of Thursday, 5 November 2026. [@1] The investigation sought to
identify a property that was being used to store drugs before they were
distributed to supply retail points of sale. [@1] After locating the property,
the team kept it under surveillance but saw no suspects at the site. [@1]

The officers then entered the property and found about 42 kilograms of marijuana
stored there. [@2] Police described the property as a drug depot from which the
marijuana would be distributed to supply points of sale in the area. [@2] No one
was arrested during the operation because no one was found at the property when
police moved in. [@2] The seized marijuana was taken to the police station, and
investigations continue to identify those who stored and distributed the drugs.
[@1]
