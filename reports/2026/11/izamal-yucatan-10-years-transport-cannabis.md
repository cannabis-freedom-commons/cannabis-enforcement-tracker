A federal control judge sentenced Samuel “N” to 10 years in prison for a health
crime in the form of transporting narcotics. [@1] The judge also imposed a fine
equivalent to 100 days. [@1]

The case began on 1 November 2024, when state public security officers stopped
him for speeding at a checkpoint on the Tekit de Regil–Dzoyaxché road in the
municipality of Izamal. [@1] Searching the car, officers found 13 packages
wrapped in clear plastic holding substances with the characteristics of
marijuana, cocaine and crystal, the street name for methamphetamine. [@1]
Laboratory tests put the cannabis sativa at eight kilos and 975 grams, alongside
five kilos and 992 grams of methamphetamine hydrochloride and 253 grams and five
milligrams of cocaine. [@1] The total load came to 15 kilos, 220 grams and five
milligrams, the figure the authorities cite for all three drugs together rather
than for the cannabis alone. [@1] Officers also seized a compact car with
Yucatán licence plates. [@1]

The federal public prosecutor presented its evidence to a control judge, who
entered the conviction. [@2] He was identified as Samuel “N” and held
responsible for a health crime in the form of transporting narcotics and
psychotropics. [@2] Moto Informa likewise reported that the 13 packages were
found in a compact car flagged down for speeding at the Izamal checkpoint. [@3]
