Runando Edghill, a Port Charlotte man, was arrested on 10 November 2026 and
transported to the Charlotte County Jail. [@1] He was arrested on more than 20
drug-related charges after repeatedly selling various drugs to undercover
detectives. [@1] The charges included possession of cocaine with intent to sell,
sale and delivery of cocaine, trafficking in cocaine, possession of oxycodone
with intent to sell, sale and delivery of oxycodone, and possession of marijuana
over 20 grams. [@1] He also faced six counts of possession of drug
paraphernalia, a charge of using a two-way communication device in commission of
a felony, resisting without violence and tampering with evidence. [@1] Edghill
was being held on a $277,000 bond. [@1]

Detectives in the Charlotte County Sheriff's Office Narcotics Unit completed
several undercover purchases from Edghill over the previous two months. [@1]
During those operations, a total of 72.7 grams of cocaine, 121 oxycodone pills
and 45.9 grams of marijuana were obtained. [@1]

Sheriff Bill Prummell said he was angry that people continued to sell harmful
and addictive drugs in Charlotte County. [@1] He said anyone pushing drugs in
the county should leave, because deputies would find and arrest them. [@1]
