Federal Police arrested a female passenger in flagrante at Belém airport for
transporting about 13 kilograms of a substance with the characteristics of skunk
in her luggage. [@1] The woman had just disembarked from a flight coming from
Manaus, in Amazonas, whose final destination was the capital of Pará. [@1]

The arrest took place on Thursday the 12th during a routine inspection at the
airport terminal. [@1] During the inspection of her checked suitcase, officers
found 13 bricks of the drug, each weighing approximately one kilogram. [@1]

After the seizure, the suspect was arrested and taken to the Federal Police,
where the legal procedures were adopted. [@1] She is being investigated for
possible involvement in interstate drug trafficking. [@1] The seized material
was collected for forensic examination, and the investigation is to establish
the origin of the drug and the possible destination of the load. [@1]
