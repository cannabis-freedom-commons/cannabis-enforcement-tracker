A 26-year-old man was arrested and charged following a police drugs recovery in
Glasgow. [@1] The charge followed a search-warrant raid on an address on Ruby
Street on Sunday 1 November 2026. [@1] The man is due to appear at Glasgow
Sheriff Court on Tuesday 3 November 2026. [@2]

Officers found quantities of cocaine, cannabis and MDMA inside the Ruby Street
property in Dalmarnock. [@4] Police put the estimated street value of the three
drugs together at around £9,200. [@1] Officers also recovered a five-figure sum
of cash at the same address. [@4] The Glasgow Times reports that officers turned
up with a search warrant to make the seizure on 1 November 2026. [@3]

Detective Sergeant Poppy Ford said drugs cause misery in communities and the
recovery showed the force's commitment to removing them from the streets. [@1]
Ford said information from the public is vital and asked anyone with information
to report it. [@1] She said people with concerns can contact Police Scotland on
101 or Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111. [@1]
