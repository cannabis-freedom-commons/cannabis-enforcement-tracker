Drug-control officers of the Central District police in Kaliningrad received
intelligence that a 34-year-old local resident might be involved in drug
dealing. [@1] During a search at the suspect's home in Guryevsk, police seized
44.66 grams of cannabis and 1.65 grams of cannabis-plant parts, along with 0.48
grams of heroin and 11.23 grams of amphetamine. [@1] Police said preliminary
information indicated that the man was keeping those substances for sale through
the shadow segment of the internet. [@1]

Officers found photographs with the coordinates of a wholesale drug cache on the
suspect's mobile phone. [@1] At the indicated location, officers seized 992.03
grams of a crystalline substance described as ephedrone. [@1] Investigators say
the man planned to collect that wholesale batch for later contactless sale. [@1]
Police also established that he had set up a separate dead-drop stash containing
1.86 grams of amphetamine intended for sale through an online store. [@1] An
investigator opened criminal cases under parts of Articles 30 and 228.1 of the
Russian Criminal Code. [@1]
