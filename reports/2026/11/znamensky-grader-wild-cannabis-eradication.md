During the third stage of the interdepartmental operation Mak-2026, officers
found and eliminated a site where narcotic-containing plants were growing in
Znamensky District. [@1] Law-enforcement bodies reported the discovery. [@1] The
plants were wild cannabis discovered during raid measures. [@1]

To destroy the plants completely, specialists used heavy equipment, a grader,
and then cleared the area by hand. [@1] Operation Mak-2026 targets the
detection, prevention and suppression of offences involving trafficking in drugs
of natural origin. [@1]

One of its key tasks is finding and destroying illegal crops and wild growths of
plants containing narcotic substances and their precursors. [@1] The
comprehensive operation runs from July to October of this year and includes
three stages. [@1] Taking part are drug-control officers of the regional
Interior Ministry department, district police officers and officers of Interior
Ministry units. [@1] The joint work is carried out in close cooperation with
municipal authorities and the public. [@1]
