Sabah police recorded 14,238 drug arrests between January and October 2026,
including 9,894 Malaysians and 4,344 foreigners, according to state police
commissioner Datuk Jauteh Dikun. [@1] Jauteh gave the figures at the November
monthly assembly of the Sabah police contingent at Dataran Kawad in Kepayan,
Kota Kinabalu, on Thursday. [@1]

The state's narcotics investigation department put the drugs seized in its major
cases from January to October at an estimated RM287.14 million, cases in which
10 kg or more was seized. [@1] The haul in those cases included 4,051.18 kg of
syabu (methamphetamine), 451.8 kg of ecstasy powder, 70.9 kg of ecstasy pills,
51.68 kg of ketamine and 44.3 kg of ganja, the Malay word for cannabis. [@1]
Thirty-three suspects, 26 men and seven women, were arrested in those
ten-kilogram-plus cases. [@2]

The department opened 4,870 investigation papers and charged 4,431 of them in
court, a charge rate of 90.99 percent. [@1] Jauteh said 12,171 of 13,385
investigation papers opened for case settlement were resolved, a rate of 90.93
percent. [@1] Both rates passed the department's key performance targets of 80
percent for charging and 65 percent for case settlement. [@1]
