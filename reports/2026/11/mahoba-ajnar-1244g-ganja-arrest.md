Ajnar police in Mahoba district, Uttar Pradesh, arrested a man after finding 1
kilogram 244 grams of cannabis on him near an old kiosk by the bus stand. [@1]
The arrest followed information from an informant and officers cordoned off the
area after spotting the man standing there in a suspicious manner. [@1]

The man, aged 42 and resident in the Ajnar police station area, was arrested and
later sent to jail after legal procedures were completed. [@1] Police registered
case crime number 211/2026 at Ajnar police station under sections 8 and 20 of
the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, India's main drug law. [@1]

The operation ran under the directions of Mahoba superintendent of police
Shashank Singh, with additional superintendent Vandana Singh and circle officer
Anuruddh Kumar Singh supervising and station house officer Vinod Kumar leading
the team. [@1]
