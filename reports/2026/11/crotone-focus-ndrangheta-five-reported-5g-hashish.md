Five people in Crotone were formally reported to the prefect for possessing
drugs for their own use after a day of joint inter-force checks in the city on
Friday 6 November 2026. [@1] The same tally included 1.01 grams of cocaine,
which is not a cannabis product and is excluded from the hashish figure. [@1]

Officers of the State Police, Carabinieri, Guardia di Finanza, harbour master’s
office and municipal police checked businesses, people and vehicles on the
city’s main roads. [@1] Across the day officers checked 476 people, 110 of them
with previous records, and 242 vehicles. [@3]

The same operation produced three highway code fines worth about 5,000 euros and
the administrative seizure of one vehicle, along with environmental requirements
imposed on a tyre shop. [@3] The day’s checks also brought the reporting of a
bar owner for an unauthorised 17-square-metre outdoor seating structure. [@3]

The Crotone police headquarters published the results, calling them part of a
wider territory-control plan. [@2]
