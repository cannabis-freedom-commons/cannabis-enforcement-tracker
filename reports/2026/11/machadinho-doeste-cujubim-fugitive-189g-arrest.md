A joint Civil and Military Police operation through the Cujubim station took
place in the rural zone of Machadinho d’Oeste on the morning of Friday 6
November 2026. [@1] The operation combined the arrest of a fugitive from justice
with checking information related to drug trafficking in the region. [@1] During
the effort officers located L.W.F.N., who had an open definitive arrest warrant
after a final criminal conviction. [@1] He was arrested on the court order and
taken with the seized materials to the Civil Police station for the applicable
legal procedures. [@1]

Searches seized about 53 grams of a marijuana-like substance and 136 grams of
hashish. [@1] Officers also seized two 12-gauge rounds, cash, three mobile
phones and materials used to package drugs. [@1]

The Rondônia Civil Police said continued action against organized crime and
enforcement of court orders matters for public safety. [@1]
