Two men from Hasselt, aged 44 and 49, were convicted on Thursday in Hasselt of
selling cannabis along with cocaine, speed and ecstasy. [@1] The two had long
struggled with addiction, met in a care centre for addicts, and then moved in
together and began dealing drugs. [@1] The older man was already sentenced in
Hasselt in March 2026 for selling narcotics, and dealt again with his housemate
between late May and 5 July 2026. [@1]

On 4 July the 49-year-old was caught in a police check and tried to swallow a
dose of cocaine, and messages on his phone implicated the other defendant. [@1]
A house search found 72 ecstasy pills, 28 grams of speed and just under 8 grams
of cocaine, and the two men carried a flick knife and two hunting knives
respectively. [@1]

The older man admitted dealing but said the drugs were mainly for his own use,
that he occasionally gave some away and that he earned nothing from it. [@1] The
prosecutor said the man had been given a suspended prison term with conditions
in March and had done nothing with it, and that a firm signal was needed. [@1]
The 44-year-old denied any part in the selling and said they had met at
Katarsis, a care centre for addicts, and that he was asked to provide an address
and needed help paying his rent. [@1]

The 49-year-old was sentenced to 20 months in prison, suspended except for the
time he had already spent in pretrial detention, and to a 10,000 euro fine,
suspended, with conditions including treatment for his addiction, avoiding the
drug scene and a meaningful daily activity. [@1] His 44-year-old housemate was
sentenced to 150 hours of community service, with a one-year prison term if he
does not complete it, and to a 10,000 euro fine, suspended. [@1] The court took
into account that both men were making efforts to stop using drugs when it set
the sentences. [@1]
