Officers from the Porto Metropolitan Command of the Public Security Police
arrested 12 people and identified three other suspects during anti-trafficking
operations between 1 and 8 November 2026. [@1] The operations were conducted in
areas mapped by police as active drug-selling spots in Porto and Vila Nova de
Gaia. [@2] In one overnight arrest in Mafamude, officers detained a 26-year-old
man caught in flagrante at 1:55 a.m. on 6 November with 107 daily doses of
hashish and 30 euros. [@2]

Across all interventions, police seized 694 daily doses of hashish, 308 doses of
cocaine and 83 doses of heroin. [@2] Officers also confiscated 713.78 euros in
cash believed to be proceeds of illegal drug sales. [@2]

The 26-year-old detained in Mafamude was released under an identity and
residence term while the criminal inquiry continued. [@2] Police said the
operations aimed to curb urban drug sales and address community concerns
regarding public safety. [@2]
