A Dhanotu police team was conducting traffic checks at a blockade near the Tarot
fourlane in Sundarnagar subdivision of Mandi district. [@1] The checking party
detained two young men travelling on a passing bus and then arrested them
according to procedure. [@1]

At about 10:30 on Monday morning, officers searched Punjab Roadways bus number
PB 65 BB 2836 travelling from Manali to Delhi. [@1] During that search, police
recovered a total of 164 grams of charas, a hand-made cannabis resin also known
as hashish, from the two passengers' possession. [@1] The wider Dhanotu team
account says officers recovered 164 grams of charas from two youths aboard a
Punjab Roadways bus during the Tarot fourlane traffic check. [@1]

Police identified the accused as Umair, 29, son of Galib, and Sadik, 25, son of
Yakub, residents of Dudhgarh village, Chilkhana post office, Saharanpur tehsil
and district in Uttar Pradesh. [@1] Sundarnagar DSP Bharat Bhushan confirmed the
case and the arrests. [@1] Police said further investigation is continuing, with
no charge, seizure value or court outcome reported. [@1]
