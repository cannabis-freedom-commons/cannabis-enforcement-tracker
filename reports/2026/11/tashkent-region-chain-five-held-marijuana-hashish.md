Interior Ministry officers carried out a series of special operations that
uncovered a chain of drug dealers in Tashkent Region. [@1] In Urta-Chirchik
district, officers found a man born in 1997 who was growing 20 cannabis bushes
in a rented house. [@1]

The same man stored 5 kilograms of marijuana. [@1] He had worked for an online
drug shop for a year, distributing drugs through stashes, and police seized zip
packets, electronic scales and wrapped doses prepared for sale. [@2] Acting on
his testimony, officers continued in Tashkent district and detained a man born
in 1991, finding 30 grams of hashish on him. [@1] Searches of a man born in 1994
and his accomplice born in 1984 and of their homes yielded 30 grams of opium and
50 grams of hashish. [@1] The operation then continued in Kibray district, where
a large dealer born in 1992 was detained with 150 grams of hashish, a pneumatic
pistol and homemade drug-use devices. [@1]

Repost, citing the Interior Ministry, reported that the Urta-Chirchik suspect
grew the 20 bushes in pots and kept 5 kg of dried marijuana. [@2] A criminal
case was opened under part 6 of article 273 of the Criminal Code for
manufacture, acquisition, storage and sale of drugs with intent to sell, which
can carry up to 20 years in prison. [@1]
