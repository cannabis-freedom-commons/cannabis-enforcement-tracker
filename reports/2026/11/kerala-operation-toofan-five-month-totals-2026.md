Kerala police say they arrested 15,515 people in the first five months of
Operation Toofan, the state's anti-drug campaign. [@1,4] The same period produced
14,369 registered drug cases. [@1,2,4] Of those arrested, 1,173 have been remanded
in judicial custody. [@1,4] Inspector General Putta Vimaladitya, the campaign's
state nodal officer, gave the figures at a press conference in
Thiruvananthapuram. [@2,4]

Officers seized 1,960.139 kg of ganja, the Malayalam name for cannabis, during
the campaign. [@1,4] Police found 10,341 cannabis plants and 8,682 grams of
cannabis beedis, or cannabis cigarettes. [@1,4] The haul also included 28.659 kg
of MDMA, 1,226.678 grams of methamphetamine, 673.076 grams of heroin, 606.81
grams of charas and 27.634 grams of cocaine. [@1,4]

Police registered 193 cases for commercial-quantity trafficking and 638 for
intermediate quantities. [@2,4] Another 4,651 cases involved small quantities of
drugs. [@2,4] Action was initiated in 8,887 cases under the Narcotic Drugs and
Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act and in 8,048 cases under the tobacco-control
COTPA law. [@2,4] The police also initiated action against 137 people under the
Kerala Anti-Social Activities (Prevention) Act, KAAPA. [@2] Proceedings under
the Prevention of Illicit Traffic in NDPS Act were initiated against 134 people,
of whom 25 were placed in preventive detention. [@2,4] Red Corner Notices were
issued against four people who had gone abroad, with a Blue Corner Notice
against one person and Look Out Circulars against 10. [@1]

Police froze 148 bank accounts and began steps to freeze illegal assets acquired
through drug trafficking. [@1,4] The police said they acted on information from
8,125 phone calls and 5,668 WhatsApp messages from the public. [@1,4] The
operation combines enforcement with prevention and rehabilitation, including a
Toofan Local Government 2.0 programme to find drug users and offer counselling,
treatment and rehabilitation. [@1,4] Police said surveillance cameras linked to
police stations would be installed in government schools under a Toofan ICE
project. [@1] Inspector General Putta Vimaladitya said the drive was launched on
2 June. [@3]

The campaign also led to seizures of an undisclosed amount of hashish, LSD
stamps, nitrazepam and opium, and officers found 8,682 grams of cannabis beedis.
[@2]

Police said the campaign had also seized 7,261.352 grams of hashish oil, 5.85 grams of hashish and 3.650 grams of cannabis oil. [@1] Officers seized 186 vehicles and 801 mobile phones from accused people in various cases. [@1] Police said 2,534 people had been profiled and that magistrate bond conditions were imposed in 1,584 cases under Section 34 of the NDPS Act. [@1]
