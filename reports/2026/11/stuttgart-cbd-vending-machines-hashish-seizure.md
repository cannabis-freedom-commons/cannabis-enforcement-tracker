Police officers investigated a 64-year-old vending machine operator on suspicion
of illicit narcotics trafficking in Baden-Württemberg. [@1] Officers searched
the residential and commercial premises of the operator on Thursday, 5 November
2026. [@1] Prior police investigations indicated that goods sold from the
vending machines allegedly contained THC, contrary to their labelling. [@1]

During the coordinated operation on Thursday, the officers inspected more than
one hundred CBD vending machines across the state. [@2] During the searches of
the suspect's premises, officers seized slightly more than one kilogram of
hashish. [@2] Following the completion of the measures, officers released the
64-year-old German national without holding him in custody. [@2]

The Stuttgart police headquarters stated that the investigation remains ongoing.
[@2] The investigation was conducted by the Stuttgart police headquarters, who
published details of the operation on 10 November 2026. [@1]
