Officers of the Doboj 1 police station arrested a man from the Usora
municipality, identified only by the initials R.K., on 5 November 2026 on
suspicion of stopping an official in the performance of his duties and of
unauthorised production and trade in narcotic drugs. [@1]

The Doboj police administration said the man was stopped for an identity check
in the Doboj area at about 17:40, ignored the officers' lawful order, tried to
run and, while resisting, physically attacked an officer and injured him. [@1]
Police in Doboj said the arrested man discarded two PVC wrappings as he fled,
and that officers found and seized plant material resembling marijuana and a
black lumpy substance resembling hashish in them, alongside substances
resembling speed and cocaine. [@2] The Vijesti.ba account names the substances
as green plant material resembling marijuana, a white powder resembling
amphetamine, known locally as speed, a black lumpy substance resembling hashish
and small white lumps resembling cocaine, all taken from the wrappings. [@1]

The Doboj District Public Prosecutor's Office was informed, and a report on the
offences will be filed against the man once the case file is complete. [@1] The
local portal Dobojski.info dates the arrest to 5 November 2026 and notes that
the police statement did not confirm the composition of the substances. [@3]
