Additional Sessions Judge Pradeep Kumar's court in Narnaul granted regular bail
to Mangal Chauhan alias Chirag in an NDPS case. [@1] The court said the 1 kg 193
grams of ganja recovered from him was an intermediate quantity, so the strict
bar in section 37 of the NDPS Act did not apply. [@1] The defence lawyer sought
bail, arguing that he had been falsely implicated and that the investigation was
complete, while the public prosecutor opposed bail by citing his earlier
criminal record. [@1] After hearing both sides, the court found that the
investigation was complete and that the trial could take time, and it granted
bail on a personal bond of 50,000 rupees and a surety of the same amount without
commenting on the merits. [@1]

On 6 October, City police received a tip during patrol that he sold narcotics,
raided near Chhota-Bada Talab and caught him with a bag. [@1] A search of the
bag recovered 1 kg 193 grams of ganja, the local word for cannabis, after which
police registered a case and sent him to judicial custody. [@1]

Dainik Bhaskar reported that CIA Narnaul, the local crime investigation agency,
arrested a young man with 1 kg 193 grams of ganja. [@2] Police said he had been
standing near Chhota-Bada Talab waiting to sell ganja. [@2] Police identified
him as Mangal Chauhan alias Chirag, son of Subhashchand, a resident of Mohalla
Franskhana in Narnaul. [@2] On the evening of 6 October, ASI Kuldeep of CIA
Narnaul was patrolling at Hero Honda Chowk with his team when an informer said
Chauhan was selling ganja. [@2] A search before naib tehsildar Arjun Singh found
ganja leaves in a waxed polythene inside a white-and-pink bag, and police
weighed the recovery at 1,193 grams including the bag and polythene. [@2] Police
registered a case at City police station, Narnaul, under sections 20(b)(ii)(B),
61 and 85 of the NDPS Act. [@2]
