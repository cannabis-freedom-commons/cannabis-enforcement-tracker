Police in Ernavur, Chennai arrested a married couple for allegedly selling
cannabis, known locally as ganja. [@1] The couple were identified as 23-year-old
Venkatesan and 21-year-old Yuvarani, residents of Moolakothalam in
Vannarappettai in north Chennai. [@1] According to the report, the pair had been
selling ganja at the tsunami housing colony in Ernavur, where police arrested
them on 4 November 2026. [@1] Dinamalar published the report under its Chennai
local news section on the evening of 4 November 2026, dating the arrest to that
same day. [@1]

Officers seized 1.2 kg of ganja from the couple at the time of the arrest, which
police reported as the full quantity recovered in the operation. [@1] Police
said the couple had smuggled the ganja from the neighbouring state of Andhra
Pradesh to Chennai by train. [@1] Police said the couple then sold that smuggled
ganja to young men around the Ernavur housing area. [@1] After the arrest and
seizure, police lodged both the husband and wife in jail. [@1]
