Assam Police seized 102.28 kg of ganja and detained four people in a night
operation along the Assam–Arunachal Pradesh border on 8 November 2026. [@1] The
raid was carried out under Mazbat police station in Udalguri district, where a
police team set up checkpoints on intelligence about drug transit across state
lines and stopped the traffickers. [@1] Police said the four detained were
suspected of being key links in the regional distribution chain. [@1]

The Chief Minister's Office said in a release that police seized 102.28 kg of
ganja and apprehended four persons in the operation under Mazbat police station,
carried out while people across Assam celebrated Diwali. [@2]

The identities of the four detained were not disclosed, and police said they
were interrogating them to trace the supply network, the source of the cannabis
and local handlers. [@1] Officials described the seizure as part of the state's
ongoing #AssamAgainstDrugs campaign against narcotics. [@1]
