Police officers of the Tripoli Crime Investigation and Prosecution Department
arrested a 53-year-old Greek man at the entrance to the Theodoros Kolokotronis
stadium in Tripoli on the afternoon of 8 November 2026. [@1] The check took
place as he entered the stadium, the ground of the Asteras Tripolis football
club. [@1]

Two cannabis joints were found in his possession and were confiscated. [@1] The
arrest was made in the evening and reported by the Arcadia Police Directorate
the next day. [@2]

The local radio station headlined the case as possession of a small quantity of
cannabis. [@3]
