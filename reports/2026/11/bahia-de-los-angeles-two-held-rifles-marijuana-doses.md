Navy personnel detained two heavily armed men as they travelled on rural roads
around Bahía de los Ángeles, in the south of the Baja California peninsula. [@1]
The stop happened at about 19:00 on Monday 9 November, when military personnel
intercepted the pair in a grey 2004 Honda Accord with California plates. [@1]
Authorities identified them as Silvano, 42, from Sinaloa, and César Omar, 32,
from Durango. [@1]

A search of the car turned up an AM-15-type semi-automatic rifle with a magazine
and 30 rounds and an AK-47 automatic rifle with a magazine and 37 rounds. [@1]
Officers also reported two 9mm pistols with 13 and 30 rounds respectively, four
5.56x45mm magazines with 102 usable rounds, and two green and brown ballistic
vests with metal plates. [@1] SEMAR personnel also reported 49 doses of crystal
methamphetamine, 24 doses of cocaine and nine doses of marijuana. [@1]

Because the case involved several federal offences, both men were handed to the
Attorney General's Office (FGR) for prosecution. [@1] Authorities link both men
to a cell of operators for Óscar Parra Aispuro, known as El Parra or El Tekolín,
described as a lieutenant of the Chapiza in the port of San Felipe. [@1]
