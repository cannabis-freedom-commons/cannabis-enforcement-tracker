Guards of the Guarda Civil Municipal (GCM) de Santos, the municipal guard force,
seized 369 drug portions during a preventive patrol in the Aparecida
neighbourhood on the morning of Thursday 5 November 2026. [@1] The action was
carried out by the Romu Oswaldo Justo team, the municipal guard's ostensive
patrol unit, on Avenida Almirante Ernesto de Mello Júnior. [@1] The incident
began around 10h30 near the Praça do Conjunto BNH, where the guards saw a man
riding a bicycle who sped up and fled when he noticed the patrol car. [@1]

During the flight the man threw a bag onto the ground; teams searched and swept
the stretch, but the suspect was not found. [@1] In the bag the Romu guards
counted 369 portions ready for sale, of which 92 containers held green herb
identical to marijuana. [@1] The same bag also held 156 wraps of a yellowish
substance similar to crack and 121 plastic pins filled with white powder
resembling cocaine, noted only to explain the mixed seizure and excluded from
cannabis figures. [@1]

All the material collected in the public street was seized and taken to the 3º
Distrito Policial (DP) de Santos, the city's 3rd police district. [@1]
