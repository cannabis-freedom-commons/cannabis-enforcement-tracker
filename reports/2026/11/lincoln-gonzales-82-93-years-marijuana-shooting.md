Kaeylab Gonzales, a 22-year-old man from Denton, Nebraska, was sentenced on 12
November 2026 to 82 to 93 years in prison for manslaughter, using a firearm to
commit a felony, assault by a confined person without a weapon and a
controlled-substance charge. [@1] Gonzales had pleaded guilty the previous month
to manslaughter and using a firearm to commit a felony, having originally been
charged with first-degree murder, use of a firearm to commit a felony,
possession of a firearm by a prohibited person and unlawful discharge of a
firearm. [@1]

First responders found Alvis with three gunshot wounds about a mile from where
the shots were reportedly fired; he was taken to Bryan West medical center,
where he died. [@1]

Police arrested Crayton on 6 July after connecting him to the scene, and
arrested Gonzales on 12 August after an arrest warrant was executed in Lincoln.
