Two men aged 38 and 25 were charged with producing a Class B drug after police
uncovered a cannabis farm in Teesside. [@1] Cleveland Police said the two men
fled from the property when officers arrived at the location. [@1] A police
drone was deployed to track the pair down before they were arrested. [@1] Both
men appeared in court and were remanded in custody until their next court
appearance. [@1]

Officers raided a property in Thorpe Thewles, near Stockton, at around 4pm on
Thursday, 5 November. [@1] Police discovered almost 400 cannabis plants inside
the raided property. [@1] Police estimated that the uncovered cannabis farm was
worth up to £335,000. [@1]

Inspector Paul Edmonds from Stockton CID said illicit drugs cause significant
harm in local communities and officers would continue removing them from the
streets. [@1] Edmonds asked members of the public to keep reporting information
and drug activity to the force. [@1] Cleveland Police said anyone with
information about suspected drug activity can contact the force on 101 or via
its website. [@1]
