Military Police arrested a 36-year-old man and a 30-year-old woman on suspicion
of drug trafficking in Ponte Nova on the morning of Tuesday 10 November 2026.
[@1] Officers from the Tático Móvel tactical team of the 21st Independent
Military Police Company carried out the operation at around 8:00 a.m. at a
residence on Rua Minas Gerais in the Vila Alvarenga neighbourhood following
reports of drug dealing at the property. [@1] Police monitored the address,
identified movements consistent with drug sales, and witnessed a 39-year-old
woman buying narcotics directly from the couple. [@1]

Officers stopped the buyer and found three crack rocks in her possession. [@1]
During searches inside the residence, officers seized two tablets of marijuana,
37 crack rocks, one wrap of cocaine, three mobile phones, R$1,690 in banknotes
and R$784 in coins. [@1] Police also seized notebooks containing annotations
linked to drug trafficking accounting. [@2] Police placed the couple under
arrest for drug trafficking and booked the 39-year-old woman for drug use and
consumption. [@2] Officers transferred all three individuals and the seized
material to the Civil Police station for legal procedures. [@1]
