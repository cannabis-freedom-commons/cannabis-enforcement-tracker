During the raids, law enforcement officers seized drugs and psychotropic
substances that had been distributed through dead drops. [@1] Uzbekistan's
Interior Ministry stopped a large drug network that had been operating in the
Yangihayot and Yashnabad districts of Tashkent. [@1] The press service of the
Interior Ministry reported the operation. [@1] During the operation officers
detained a previously convicted man and his accomplice, who had been
distributing drugs through dead drops. [@1] A personal search found and seized
55 grams of hashish and 24 units of Regapen. [@1] A search of the suspects'
homes found 430 grams of hashish, zip bags and electronic scales. [@1]

The raids continued in Kokand, in Fergana region. [@1] A previously convicted
major drug dealer and his accomplices were detained as they sold a buyer 1
kilogram of hashish. [@1] Officers said they had prevented prohibited substances
equal to 7,415 single doses from being spread among the population. [@1]

A criminal case was opened under point b of part 6 of Article 273 of the
Criminal Code. [@1] The charge covers illegal manufacture, processing,
acquisition, storage and other actions with narcotic drugs, psychotropic
substances or their analogues for sale, and their illegal sale. [@1]
