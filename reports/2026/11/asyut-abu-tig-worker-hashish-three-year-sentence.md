The Ninth Circuit of the Asyut Criminal Court sentenced a defendant to three
years of rigorous imprisonment after convicting him of possessing hashish for
trafficking contrary to the law. [@1] The defendant was a 29-year-old day
labourer who lived on Al-Nadi Al-Riyadi street in the Qibli Al-Balad area of Abu
Tig city in Asyut Governorate. [@1] The case began in June 2026 when an
assistant investigator at the Abu Tig police station, acting on information from
a secret source that the man was promoting drugs, obtained the public
prosecution's permission to arrest and search him. [@1] A security force of
plainclothes officers rode hired tuk-tuks, three-wheeled motorcycles, to avoid
drawing attention, and detained him at the spot. [@1]

In his left pocket officers found a matchbox holding six dark pieces suspected
to be hashish, together with 170 Egyptian pounds. [@1] He admitted holding the
drugs for sale and said the money was proceeds of that trade. [@1] The forensic
chemical laboratory of the Justice Ministry reported that the pieces, weighing
about 2.95 grams, contained cannabis resin. [@1]

The First Attorney General for the South Asyut prosecutions referred him in
custody to the criminal court. [@1] The bench was headed by Counselor Ahmed
Mohamed Halawa with members Mohamed Ayman Saddah, Mohamed Samir Al-Tamawy and
Ramy Mohamed Samir. [@1]
