New Hampshire State Police arrested a 39-year-old Massachusetts man in Hooksett
early on Nov. 9, 2026 after he drove the wrong way on Interstate 293 south. [@1]
State Police received a report shortly before midnight of a white truck driving
north at high speed in the southbound lanes, the state Department of Safety said
in a news release. [@1] The wrong-way driver was first reported near Exit 2 in
Manchester, and a state trooper stopped the vehicle about 10 miles away near
Exit 11 in Hooksett after seeing it coming toward him. [@1]

The driver was taken into custody on charges of felony reckless conduct and
reckless driving. [@1] He was also charged with aggravated driving while
intoxicated, driving while intoxicated and driving with an open container of
marijuana, police said. [@1] He is scheduled to appear in Hooksett district
court on Dec. 3, 2026. [@1]
