Wałbrzych and Wałbrzych County police detained four people for possessing drugs
between Saturday evening, 7 November 2026, and the early hours of 9 November.
[@1] On 7 November before 6 p.m., officers from the Głuszyca police station saw
an 18-year-old get out of a taxi on Kamienna Street in Jedlina-Zdrój and found
two wraps on him, with white powder and dried plant material. [@1] A forensic
technician's test confirmed the wraps contained amphetamine and marijuana. [@1]
The 18-year-old, a resident of Wałbrzych County, was detained, held in a police
cell and charged with drug possession the next day. [@1]

On the morning of Sunday 8 November, preventive officers patrolling Podgórze
stopped a 37-year-old on one of the district's main streets and found a plastic
bag of dried plant material in his cigarette packet. [@1] A field test confirmed
the material was marijuana, and the Wałbrzych man was questioned and charged the
same morning. [@1] That evening after 8 p.m., district officers and preventive
officers responded to a noise complaint at a flat on Piaskowa Street in Biały
Kamień and detained a 46-year-old carrying methamphetamine. [@1] The 46-year-old
spent the night in a police cell and was questioned and charged the next morning
under the law on counteracting drug addiction. [@1] At about 1 a.m. on Monday 9
November, officers from the second police station went to a noise complaint on
Batorego Street in Stary Zdrój and found dried plant material on a 37-year-old
Wałbrzych man. [@1] A field test showed the material was marijuana, and the
37-year-old was taken to a police cell and charged with drug possession on
Monday morning. [@1] Three of the four detentions involved marijuana, and the
fourth, the 46-year-old's case, involved methamphetamine. [@1]

Police said drug offences under the law on counteracting drug addiction carry up
to three years in prison. [@1] The statement was issued by the press officer of
the Wałbrzych city police commander, Superintendent Marcin Świeży. [@1]
