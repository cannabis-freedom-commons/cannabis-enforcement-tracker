Gwent Police officers detained a 28-year-old man from Newport on Chepstow Road
at about 1.20am on Thursday, 5 November, after spotting him acting suspiciously.
[@1] The arrest was made by plain-clothes officers as part of a drugs operation.
[@1]

He was arrested on suspicion of possessing cocaine and cannabis with intent to
supply. [@1] Officers said they found suspected cocaine, cannabis, two mobile
phones and cash when they searched him. [@1] The 28-year-old remained in custody
while enquiries continued, and no charge was reported. [@1]

Gwent Police described the arrest as part of its crackdown on drug-related crime
in Newport and urged residents to report suspected drug activity. [@1] The force
said it had also stepped up patrols in Newport in recent weeks. [@1]
