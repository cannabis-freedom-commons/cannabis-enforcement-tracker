Narpatganj police arrested a young man near Narpatganj market in Araria district
on Tuesday after acting on a tip-off that ganja was being moved in an auto. [@1]
Police identified him as Chandrahas Yadav, a resident of Rampur Tika in Munger
district and the son of Yogendra Yadav. [@1] He was sent to judicial custody in
Araria after the arrest. [@1]

Officers stopped the hired auto near Narpatganj market, searched it and
recovered nine kilograms of ganja. [@1] Police said Yadav had boarded the hired
auto in Badhara village with the ganja and was travelling toward Narpatganj
market. [@1] During questioning, officers learned that Pankaj Yadav, the son of
Tarachand Yadav and a resident of Badhara, had loaded the ganja into the auto.
[@1] Police said Chandrahas Yadav planned to take the consignment to Munger to
traffic it. [@1]

Narpatganj station house officer Sanjay Kumar said one accused was arrested in
the recovery and sent to judicial custody in Araria. [@1] The operation began
after police received a tip that a youth from Badhara had been given ganja for
smuggling. [@1]
