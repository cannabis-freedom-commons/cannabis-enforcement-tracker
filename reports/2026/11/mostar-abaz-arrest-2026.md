Police in Bosnia and Herzegovina's Herzegovina-Neretva Canton arrested Adis
Abaz, a man from Zenica, in the Mostar area on 5 November 2026 on the order of
the Court of Bosnia and Herzegovina. [@1] The canton's police arrested him and
he was to be transferred to Sarajevo. [@1] The arrest was confirmed by the duty
prosecutor of the Herzegovina-Neretva Canton prosecutor's office. [@2]

Abaz had been convicted of organised crime linked to international drug
trafficking; the group he founded trafficked marijuana and amphetamines from
Bosnia and Herzegovina to Western European countries, according to the court's
ruling. [@3] The Court of Bosnia and Herzegovina sentenced him to six years in
prison on 21 February 2022, and the appellate panel confirmed the sentence on 12
May that year. [@1] Time he spent in custody from 20 January to 18 May 2020 was
counted toward the six-year term. [@3]

According to unofficial information, Abaz had been registered in Mostar under a
false name. [@2] Interpol had issued a warrant for him in 2019 at the request of
the Court of Bosnia and Herzegovina over organised crime and drug trafficking.
[@1] His then-lawyer said Abaz was acquitted in Belgium of all charges of
supporting terrorists and of links to weapons sales. [@2] Belgium extradited him
to Bosnia and Herzegovina in January 2020 over the drug-trafficking case. [@1]
The Court of Bosnia and Herzegovina lifted his custody in May 2020, and he
defended himself from liberty until the arrest. [@4]
