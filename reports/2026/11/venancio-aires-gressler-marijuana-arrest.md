A Brigada Militar patrol arrested a man on the night of Monday, 2 November 2026,
in Bairro Gressler in Venâncio Aires, Rio Grande do Sul. [@1] Officers detained
the man for the crime of drug trafficking. [@1] After arresting him in
flagrante, officers took him to the Delegacia de Polícia de Pronto Atendimento,
the ready-response police station, where the case was formally registered. [@2]

During the operation in the Gressler neighbourhood, officers located and seized
five portions of marijuana and 15 portions of cocaine, as well as cash. [@1] The
second account of the same night describes the haul as five portions of
marijuana, 15 buchas of cocaine, a mobile telephone, material for packaging
drugs and a sum of cash. [@2]

Officers had approached and searched the suspect near a place in Bairro Gressler
described as known for the sale of narcotics. [@2] The arresting unit was a
Brigada Militar patrol from Venâncio Aires operating that night in the Gressler
neighbourhood. [@2] Officers found all of the drugs on the suspect himself
during that same street stop. [@2]
