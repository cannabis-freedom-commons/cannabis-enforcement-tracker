In the Slaveykov residential complex, police detained a 37-year-old driver on
suspicion of driving after consuming drugs. [@1] According to police, the man
had taken the wheel after using cannabis. [@1] Police also allege that he had
used amphetamine and methamphetamine in addition to cannabis. [@1]

At the time of the encounter, the driver was travelling in a Daihatsu Sirion
passenger car that officers had stopped for a check. [@1] Once stopped, the
driver refused to provide a sample for chemical analysis. [@1] Officers then
held the driver in detention for a period of up to 24 hours. [@1] Following the
detention, police opened fast-track proceedings in connection with the case.
