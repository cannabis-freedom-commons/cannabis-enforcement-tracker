Staff seized contraband and unauthorized items at Cowansville Institution on
November 1, 2026. [@2] Cowansville Institution is a medium-security federal
prison. [@1] The seizure was announced for Cowansville, Quebec, on November 6,
2026. [@2] Staff vigilance was credited for the discovery. [@1]

The seizure included cannabis concentrate, marijuana, cocaine, tobacco, rolling
papers, cell phones and accessories. [@2] The total institutional value of the
seized items is estimated at $121,655. [@2] The dollar figure is CSC’s estimate
of the items’ value inside the prison. [@1] It should not be read as their
street value outside the institution. [@1] The release did not provide drug
weights or cellphone counts, explain how the items reached the institution or
announce arrests or charges. [@1]

These tools include ion scanners and drug-detector dogs to search buildings,
personal property, inmates, and visitors. [@2] CSC is heightening measures to
prevent contraband from entering its institutions in order to help ensure a safe
and secure environment for everyone. [@2] CSC also works in partnership with the
police to take action against those who attempt to introduce contraband into
correctional institutions. [@2]
