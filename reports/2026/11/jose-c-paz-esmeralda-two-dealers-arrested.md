Police in the northwest of the conurbano bonaerense, the ring of municipalities
around the city of Buenos Aires, arrested two suspected dealers accused of
selling cocaine and marijuana. [@1] Judicial sources told cronica.com.ar that
the two are 27 and 25 years old. [@1]

The operation was carried out some days earlier by officers of the second police
station of the district, in Esmeralda al 2300, close to the junction with Viena,
in the area known as Barrio Frino. [@1]

Police seized 152 doses of cocaine and four wrappings of marijuana from the two.
[@1] The criminal case, provisionally classified as "possession of narcotics for
sale", is handled by Verónica Mabel Ghirardi of thematic unit No. 16 of the
local courts. [@1]
