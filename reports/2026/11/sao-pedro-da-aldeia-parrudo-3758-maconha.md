Military police arrested a man known as "Parrudo" and seized more than 3,700
blocks of marijuana in São Pedro da Aldeia, Rio de Janeiro state, on the morning
of Wednesday 11 November 2026. [@1] The police counted 3,758 marijuana blocks
and also seized 440 capsules of cocaine in the Porto do Carro neighbourhood.
[@1]

Officers from the Tactical Actions Group (GAT) and the Serviço Reservado, a
plainclothes intelligence unit, went to the address after reports that the
suspect supplied drug-selling points in the Morro do Limão. [@1] The Military
Police said the drugs were found inside the suspect's house. [@1]

The suspect admitted owning the drugs and said he held a position in the
hierarchy of the criminal faction the police blame for trafficking in the
region. [@1] The case was registered at the 125th Police Station, where an
arrest in flagrante was drawn up for drug trafficking and criminal association.
[@1]

A second outlet carried the same account of the arrest and of the suspect
admitting ownership of the drugs and claiming a place in the faction's
hierarchy. [@2]
