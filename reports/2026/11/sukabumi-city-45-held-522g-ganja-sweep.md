Sukabumi City Police narcotics investigators uncovered 41 drug and
restricted-medicine cases over the previous three months. [@1] Police detained
45 suspects who mostly acted as couriers and dealers. [@1] The 45 suspects face
layered charges carrying a minimum of five years in prison up to life
imprisonment. [@1] Police presented the results at Sukabumi City Police
headquarters on 12 November 2026 alongside local leaders, where officers also
destroyed seized illegal liquor. [@1]

Only one of the 41 cases involved ganja, alongside 27 methamphetamine cases, one
ecstasy case, one psychotropic case and 11 restricted-medicine cases. [@1]
Police said they seized 522 grams of ganja as part of the wider haul. [@1]
Officers also seized 18 digital scales, 45 mobile phones and Rp 690,000 in
transaction cash. [@1] Police estimated the combined seized narcotics were worth
about Rp 930 million on the illicit market. [@1] Police claimed disrupting the
trade could save about 56,550 people, a figure given for all drugs together, not
cannabis alone. [@1] Officers destroyed 2,135 bottles of seized illegal liquor
of various brands. [@1]

Police said Cikole and Citamiang districts had the most cases, with seven each.
[@1] Suspects told investigators they entered the trade because of economic
pressure and the prospect of quick profit. [@1] Dealers used drop-off placements
and direct transactions to evade officers. [@1] The dealing had run for about
three to four months, and in some cases up to a year. [@1]
