Combined detachments of Algeria's National People's Army (ANP), working with the
different security services, intercepted 28 drug traffickers. [@1] The
operations ran from 28 October to 3 November 2026. [@1] The detachments foiled
attempts to introduce 8 quintals and 44 kilograms (844 kg) of kif traité,
treated cannabis, coming from the borders with Morocco. [@1] The army said the
interceptions and seizures took place during operations carried out across the
military regions. [@1]

The same drug operations also yielded 75,526 psychotropic tablets alongside the
cannabis. [@1] The army presented the results in its weekly operational bilan as
part of what it described as sustained efforts against terrorism and
multifaceted organised crime. [@1] It said the operations reflected the high
professionalism, vigilance and permanent readiness of the armed forces across
the national territory. [@1]

The army framed the cannabis seizures as a continuation of efforts to counter
the scourge of drug trafficking in the country. [@1] The figures cover a week of
operations by army units and detachments rather than a single raid. [@1]
