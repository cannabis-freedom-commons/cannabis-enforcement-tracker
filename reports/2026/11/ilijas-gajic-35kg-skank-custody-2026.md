The Municipal Court in Sarajevo ordered one month of custody for Nemanja Gajić,
in whose truck about 35 kilograms of the drug cannabis were found. [@1] The
court ordered the custody because of the danger that he could repeat the
criminal offence if left at liberty. [@1]

SIPA, Bosnia and Herzegovina's State Agency for Investigation and Protection,
found the cannabis when its investigators searched his truck. [@3] A SIPA
spokesperson confirmed that the drug was found and temporarily seized during a
search of a freight motor vehicle. [@4] The operation was carried out under the
code name MC, on a warrant from the Municipal Court in Sarajevo and under the
supervision of the Sarajevo Canton Prosecutor's Office. [@2]

The Sarajevo Canton Prosecutor's Office said Gajić was arrested on 6 November in
the Ilijaš area and that the drug was intended for street sale. [@3] The
prosecution proposed the one-month custody and Gajić, after being handed over
and questioned, was brought before the Municipal Court in Sarajevo. [@3]

He is suspected of the illegal trade in narcotic drugs. [@1] In its first
account on 6 November, SIPA's statement said two people had been deprived of
liberty in operation MC over the roughly 35 kilograms of skunk found in the
freight vehicle. [@2]
