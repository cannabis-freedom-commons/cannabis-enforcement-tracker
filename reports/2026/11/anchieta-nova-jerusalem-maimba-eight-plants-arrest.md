The Military Police carried out two seizures of cannabis plants in Anchieta.
[@1] The first seizure happened on the afternoon of Friday the 6th in the Nova
Jerusalém neighbourhood, after intelligence reported that a man with an arrest
warrant was hiding in a house there. [@1] During searches in the backyard of a
residence, officers found one cannabis plant about 2 metres tall. [@1] The man
approached there had an arrest warrant for homicide issued by the criminal court
of Vila Velha. [@1] The man was arrested and taken to the Guarapari judicial
police station. [@1]

At night, another Military Police team found seven more cannabis plants in the
Maimbá neighbourhood, also in Anchieta. [@1] In that house, officers also seized
seeds, capsules and packaging that may be linked to growing the drug. [@1]
Officers searched the area but found no one responsible for that material. [@1]

The police asked residents to report information through the 181 hotline without
having to identify themselves. [@1]
