Police raided a grocery shop in Barni village in Bhojpur's Charpokhari
police-station area and recovered brown sugar, ganja, chillums, an electronic
scale, a mobile phone and cash. [@1] Charpokhari police had received information
that Barni resident Sonu Singh alias Rohit Ranjan Singh was buying and selling
narcotics from his grocery shop. [@1] Officers arrested the shopkeeper,
identified as Sonu Singh alias Rohit Ranjan Singh, son of Kamakhya Narayan Singh
of Barni village. [@1] Police registered a case against him at Charpokhari
police station under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act. [@1]

The action in Barni village took place on Tuesday night. [@1] The search of the
shop recovered 46 grams of ganja, the cannabis component recorded here. [@1] The
same search also found 3.57 grams of brown sugar, six chillums, an electronic
weighing scale, a chakla, a mobile phone and an aluminium box. [@1] Officers
also seized 7,196 rupees in cash from the shop. [@1]

The details were released in a press statement issued on Wednesday on behalf of
Superintendent of Police Avdhesh Dixit. [@1]
