The Oral Criminal Court of La Serena convicted three people for drug trafficking
and related offences tied to homes they used in Coquimbo and Andacollo. [@1] The
case was led by the Coquimbo High Complexity Prosecutor's Office through its
criminal-analysis system together with the PDI's anti-narcotics and
organised-crime brigade. [@1] Prosecutors said that between 2024 and March 2025
the group used homes in Tierras Blancas and two parcels in El Manzano,
Andacollo, to store and sell drugs. [@1]

Investigators found the main defendant holding more than six kilos of cocaine,
more than 69 kilos of cannabis and a cannabis crop inside one of the properties
he used. [@1] Officers also found three pistols, a revolver and three carbines
or shotguns, along with ammunition of various calibres. [@1] Seized items
included live cannabis plants, plants being dried and bulk cannabis, as well as
cocaine and cocaine base, with indoor growing systems present. [@1] Police also
seized mobile phones, a bulletproof vest, shotgun cartridges, weapon parts, five
air rifles, a drone and a press used for THC extraction. [@1]

Two defendants each received eight years of imprisonment for drug trafficking
and cannabis cultivation. [@1] The third defendant received more than 28 years
of effective imprisonment for drug trafficking, cannabis cultivation and
firearms and ammunition offences, which the court ordered to be served
effectively. [@2]
