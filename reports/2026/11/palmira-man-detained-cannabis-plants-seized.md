On Saturday night, police detained a 32-year-old man in a house on Patricias
Mendocinas street in the Palmira district of the San Martín department over a
suspected case of gender-based violence. [@1] A 911 call had warned that a
possible gender-violence situation was occurring inside that house. [@1] Police
officers who arrived at the house detained the 32-year-old man there. [@1]

During the subsequent inspection of the same house, officers seized cannabis
plants. [@1] Officers also seized a box containing marijuana found in the same
inspection. [@1] Officers also seized a digital scale found in the same
inspection. [@1] Officers also seized six nylon wrappers containing a white
substance described as suspected cocaine. [@1]

Personnel from Lucha contra el Narcotráfico, the counter-narcotics unit,
intervened because of the drug finds. [@1] That unit carried out the seizure of
the plants, the box, the scale and the wrappers. [@1] The 32-year-old man was
placed under apprehension in connection with the seizure. [@1]
