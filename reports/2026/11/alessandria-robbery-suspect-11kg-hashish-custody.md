Police in Alessandria jailed a 27-year-old Italian man in pre-trial custody on
suspicion of the aggravated robbery of a Chinese woman in her home,
investigators at the city's Squadra Mobile, the police flying squad, said. [@1]
The robbery happened in March 2026, when the man came to the woman's flat posing
as a client of the sexual services she offered there. [@1] Once inside he beat
her, demanded cash and valuables and wounded her with a knife, then took two
mobile phones and a few dozen euros. [@1] The officers who searched his flat for
evidence in the robbery found about 11 kg of hashish divided into packets of
roughly 100 grams each, and arrested him at the scene on drug charges, which the
reports treat as a separate case. [@1]

At the time of the robbery he was serving house arrest for other offences but
was allowed to leave home for set hours each day, which investigators said gave
him the chance to reach the woman's flat. [@1] He had previous convictions for
similar offences. [@1]

The custody measure concerns the robbery; the drug case is a separate
investigation, and the man is still under investigation with no final ruling on
either accusation. [@1]
