Penitentiary police found 16 grams of hashish hidden in a freezer during an
extraordinary search at the Turin district prison early on 4 November 2026. [@1,2]
The search took place at about 5:30 a.m. on the first floor of Pavilion B of the
Casa circondariale di Torino, the city's district prison. [@1,3] Officers found
the hashish inside a freezer in a shared room used by detainees, where a total
of 16 grams had been concealed. [@1,3]

In the same space officers also found rudimentary weapons, including knives.
[@1,2] Officers also found a substantial quantity of fruit that initial
assessments said may have been intended for maceration to produce alcohol. [@1,3]
The seizure was announced by Donato Capece, general secretary of the autonomous
penitentiary-police union, and Vicente Santilli, the union's secretary for
Piedmont. [@1,3] Capece and Santilli praised the officers' work and said attention
to security in prisons must stay high. [@1] Investigators started inquiries to
reconstruct the circumstances of the find, identify those responsible and verify
the destination of the seized material. [@1,2]
