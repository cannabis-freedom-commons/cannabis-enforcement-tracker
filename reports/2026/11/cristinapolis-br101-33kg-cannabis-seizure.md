Federal highway police stopped a car near the Cristinápolis operational unit on
the BR-101 in Sergipe, and the driver fled at high speed instead of stopping.
[@2] Police pursued the car for about 20 minutes and 38 kilometres along the
highway, with the driver reaching 180 km/h and performing dangerous manoeuvres.
[@3] The chase ended in the urban area of Cristinápolis when the driver lost
control after a speed bump, and the car hit a kerb and a street light pole. [@2]
The two occupants left the car and ran into vegetation, pointing objects
resembling firearms at the officers, and were not found that night. [@3]

Inside the vehicle, officers found boxes holding 59 bags of a substance
resembling marijuana weighing 31.5 kg, two tablets of a cannabis derivative
weighing 2.09 kg, and 1,015 pills resembling ecstasy. [@2] The Federal Highway
Police put the total cannabis seizure at about 33.6 kg, the 31.5 kg of marijuana
plus the 2.09 kg of derivative. [@3] Television news in Sergipe reported the
seizure as more than 30 kg of marijuana and 1,000 ecstasy pills on the BR-101 in
Cristinápolis. [@1] The car and the material were taken to the state civil
police station, and the incident was recorded as suspected drug trafficking.
[@2]

One of the two occupants was arrested by the Federal Highway Police the next
day, 6 November, in Estância, together with another man involved in drug
trafficking. [@2] The Federal Highway Police recorded the case, in principle, as
drug trafficking. [@3]
