A months-long, multiagency investigation into alleged illegal sales of
marijuana, psilocybin products and contraband tobacco through a local smoke-shop
enterprise led to felony charges against nine people. [@1] The grand jury
alleges that the defendants conspired during April, May and June 2026 to sell
unlicensed marijuana products, psilocybin-containing products and contraband
tobacco products to the public. [@1] All nine defendants pleaded not guilty and
are scheduled to appear in court again on December 3, 2026. [@1]

Authorities said they seized more than 185 pounds of marijuana, 86 pounds of
psilocybin products, several thousand packages of illegal tobacco products, five
firearms and thousands of dollars in cash and gold. [@1] The seizures were made
at all seven Spot Smoke Shop locations, which are owned by Mohamed Hamood
Alhalemni. [@1] The article lists only potential custodial exposure, including
21 years in state prison for one defendant, rather than imposed sentences. [@1]

The investigation, known as Operation No Smoke Zone, was spearheaded by the
California Department of Cannabis Control and the Fresno Police Department and
involved 10 local, state and federal agencies. [@1] Two defendants appeared in
court on November 2nd before the joint not-guilty pleas. [@1]
