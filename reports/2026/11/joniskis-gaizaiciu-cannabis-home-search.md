Police in Joniškis district searched the home on Gaižaičių Street of a man born
in 1985. [@1] Officers searched the man's residential house as part of the
operation. [@1] They also searched outbuildings belonging to the property. [@1]
They also searched a greenhouse on the property. [@1] The police account
identifies the resident as a man born in 1985. [@1] A pre-trial investigation
was opened into the find for possession of narcotics without the purpose of
distributing them. [@1]

During the searches officers found cannabis plants at the property. [@1] They
also found parts of cannabis plants. [@1] They also found dried cannabis. [@1]
Police said the quantity of the narcotics found was still being clarified. [@1]

Police announced the discovery on Wednesday. [@1] An ikiteisminis tyrimas, the
term used for the investigation, is the Lithuanian name for a formal pre-trial
criminal investigation that comes before any decision on charges or a trial.
[@1] The suspected offence reported at this stage is possession without intent
to distribute, rather than distribution or trafficking. [@1]
