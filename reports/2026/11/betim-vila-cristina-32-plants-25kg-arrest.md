Two men were arrested on suspicion of drug trafficking during a Military Police
operation in the Vila Cristina neighbourhood of Betim, Minas Gerais, on
Wednesday 4 November 2026. [@1] Officers from Rotam, the military police's
tactical patrol unit, seized 32 cannabis plants and about 25 kg of cannabis
ready for sale. [@1] Police said the operation was aimed at organised and
violent crime in the region. [@1]

The 25 kg was made up of 21 bars of cannabis weighing about 21 kg and eight
portions of the same drug adding up to about 4 kg. [@1] Officers also seized
five portions of dry and ice, cannabis resin concentrates prepared in small
units for sale, weighing about 500 g. [@1] Police found two precision scales and
materials used to assemble grow tents and prepare the drugs. [@1]

The two arrested men and all the seized material were taken to the duty police
station in Betim. [@1]
