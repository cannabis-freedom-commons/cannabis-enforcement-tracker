A Narcotics Control Force team in Faisalabad arrested a police constable
red-handed on suspicion of carrying 5 kg of hashish to a drug seller in a
station house officer's official vehicle. [@1] The constable, Kashif, was posted
at Gulberg police station in Faisalabad. [@1]

The force acted on a tip-off and, with officers already deployed at the
identified spot, stopped him as he arrived. [@1] Officers recovered five packets
from the vehicle, each containing one kilogram of hashish. [@1] The team also
impounded the official vehicle that had been used for the drug supply. [@1]

City Police Officer Tanweer Hussain Tanvio appointed Malik Imran, senior
superintendent of police for the operations wing, as inquiry officer. [@1] On
the initial inquiry, the city police officer dismissed Kashif from service with
immediate effect. [@1] He also suspended Gulberg station house officer Abdul
Ghaffar, assistant sub-inspector Suhail Ahmad, muharrar Tasneem Ahmad and
another constable, Mahboob Ali, over their alleged involvement in supplying and
selling the drug. [@1]
