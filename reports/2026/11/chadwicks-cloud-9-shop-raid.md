New Hartford Police arrested the owner after a raid at a store in Chadwicks, New
York. [@1] Police executed a search warrant at the Cloud 9 Smoke Shop on Oneida
Street in Chadwicks on 3 November. [@1] The shop was owned by 29-year-old Raouf
Alamri and was not licensed as a marijuana dispensary under New York State law.
[@1] Police said the warrant formed part of an ongoing investigation into
alleged illegal sales of cigarettes and various cannabis products. [@1] Alamri
was charged with New York State Tax Law violations for unlawful possession and
sale of untaxed cigarettes, and issued an appearance ticket to answer the
charges in New Hartford Town Court on 23 November. [@1] New Hartford Police said
additional charges were pending as their investigation continued. [@1]

During the search, police found unknown quantities of marijuana products and
flavored vaping products. [@1] Members of the Oneida County Department of Health
seized those products as part of a separate investigation into the business.
[@1] Police also reported finding and seizing more than 60 packages of untaxed
Seneca cigarettes during the raid. [@1] Police said officers had made multiple
undercover purchases of untaxed cigarettes over an unspecified period. [@1]

Chief Ron Fontaine said the Department of Health would pursue further
enforcement actions against Cloud 9 Smoke Shop. [@1] Fontaine said the
investigation involved local, county and state agencies addressing commercial
activity he described as posing public-health and safety risks. [@1]
