Secretaría de Seguridad Ciudadana officers detained Luis Francisco “N” in
Supermanzana 63 in Cancún, Benito Juárez, on suspicion of crimes against health.
[@1] The detention occurred during a Grupo Orión Zona Norte operation after
officers saw him handling several small plastic bags. [@1] Officers had observed
Luis Francisco “N” manipulating several small plastic bags before carrying out
the inspection. [@1]

Officers found a black waist bag containing 27 transparent Ziploc bags holding
green, dry plant material with characteristics similar to marijuana. [@1] The
plant material inside was green and dry with characteristics similar to
marijuana. [@1] Officers also found 11 more bags with crystalline solid
fragments in the right front pocket of his trousers. [@1] In total, authorities
secured 38 bags of suspected illicit substances. [@1]

Officers transferred Luis Francisco “N” to the Fiscalía General del Estado de
Quintana Roo. [@1] Both the man and the seized items were placed at the disposal
of the prosecutor specialized in combating narcomenudeo, small-scale drug
dealing. [@1] The detainee remains presumed innocent until the competent
authorities determine his legal situation. [@1] The characteristics of the
substances are to be confirmed by the corresponding analyses. [@1]
