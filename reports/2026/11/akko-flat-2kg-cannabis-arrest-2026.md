A 27-year-old resident of Acre was arrested on suspicion of drug trafficking and
drug possession. [@1] Detectives from the Acre police station raided an
apartment in the city. [@1]

During the raid, officers found about 2 kilograms of material suspected to be
cannabis in the apartment. [@1] Police said the material was located in the
apartment with the assistance of a dog unit and was suspected to have been
intended for trade and distribution. [@1]

The suspect was questioned at the Acre station and was jailed when the
questioning ended. [@1] A police spokesperson said police would ask the court to
extend the suspect's detention in accordance with the needs and findings of the
investigation. [@1]
