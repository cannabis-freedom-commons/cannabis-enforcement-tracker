Five men were arrested in Pristina on suspicion of involvement in drug
trafficking. [@1] Police describe all five suspects as Kosovar men. [@1]
Investigators from the Pristina unit of the Directorate for the Investigation of
Narcotics Trafficking made the arrests after searching two vehicles and an
apartment used by the suspects. [@1] After interviews, four of the men were
taken into custody by decision of the prosecutor, while the fifth was released
with the case continuing in regular proceedings. [@1]

During the searches, police seized 517.5 grams of a substance suspected to be
marijuana, alongside 52.93 grams of a substance suspected to be cocaine. [@1] As
material evidence, officers also seized a scale for measuring narcotics, two
packages suspected of being used for packaging drugs, three mobile phones and
440 euros in cash suspected of coming from drug sales. [@1] The operation and
the seizure figures come from a Kosovo Police statement as reported by the
outlet. [@1]
