The Amambay sentencing court sentenced Orlando Ramón Balbuena Saavedra and Bruno
de Barros da Silva to five years in prison for possession of marijuana. [@1]
Judges Librada Peralta, Mario Peralta and Marcelina Quintana formed the
sentencing panel. [@1] Anti-drug prosecutor Rossana Coronel proved the case
against both men. [@1]

The case stemmed from a December 2024 operation in Capitán Bado district,
Amambay department, where the two were detained. [@1] Special agents of Senad's
Regional No. 1 in Pedro Juan Caballero detained them after two simultaneous
raids on a house and a rural property in Colonia Cadete Boquerón. [@1] At the
house, agents found two black pickups, a Fiat Toro and a Ford F250. [@1] In the
body of the Ford F250, agents found 208 kilos of pressed marijuana. [@1]
Officers also seized a Sig Sauer .40-calibre pistol with two magazines and a 9mm
Sarsilmaz pistol with its serial number erased, also with two magazines. [@1] At
the rural property, officers found about 9,800 kilos of pressed marijuana with
five wooden presses, two makeshift camps, two forrajeras, scales and other
processing items. [@1]

Authorities said the operation seized almost 10 tonnes of marijuana, plus arms,
vehicles and processing equipment. [@1] Senad is Paraguay's Secretaría Nacional
Antidrogas, the national anti-drug agency. [@1]
