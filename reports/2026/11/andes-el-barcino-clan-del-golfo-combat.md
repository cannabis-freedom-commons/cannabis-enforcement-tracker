Troops of the 11th Light Infantry Battalion Cacique Nutibara, part of the Fourth
Brigade, fought suspected members of the Clan del Golfo's Edwin Román Velásquez
Valle substructure in the vereda El Barcino, rural Andes, Antioquia. [@1] One
suspect, known as alias Saltarín, was killed in the fighting. [@1] The army said
Saltarín had criminal influence in the municipalities of Tarso and Pueblorrico.
[@1] Troops captured another suspected member, alias Marlon. [@1]

Marlon already faced a judicial order and was handed to prosecutors, who were to
define his legal situation. [@2] During the operation, soldiers seized a pistol,
12 cartridges, a magazine and a motorcycle, with no drugs reported. [@1] They
also found and destroyed an improvised explosive device at the scene. [@1] The
army said the result damaged the structure's armed and logistical capacity in
south-western Antioquia. [@1]

The Clan del Golfo has a presence in several regions of Colombia, mainly
Antioquia, Chocó, Córdoba, Bolívar and Urabá, and is dedicated to drug
trafficking, extortion, illegal mining and territorial control. [@1] The army
said it would continue operations against illegal armed groups in south-western
Antioquia. [@3]
