Military Police arrested a man on the night of Tuesday 3 November 2026 on
suspicion of drug trafficking in the Campo d’Una neighbourhood, in the area near
the border between Imbituba and Garopaba. [@1] The incident was recorded at
about 20h20, after the Military Police received information about a possible
drug-selling scheme conducted through deliveries. [@1] Officers monitored the
suspect’s movements and stopped him while he was driving a car. [@1] After the
stop, the officers went to the guesthouse where the man was staying in order to
search it. [@1] Faced with the materials found, the suspect was formally
arrested and taken with the seized items to the Central de Plantão Policial for
legal procedures. [@1] The man’s identity was not disclosed. [@1]

The search of the vehicle found 0.6 gram of cocaine and 57.8 grams of maconha,
the Portuguese word for marijuana. [@1] In the guesthouse room he was using,
officers found approximately 1.9 kilos of maconha, 19.3 grams of cocaine, 17.3
grams of haxixe (hashish) and 2.6 grams of skunk, a potent form of cannabis.
[@1] The team also seized a precision scale, a knife, packaging used to pack
drugs, four mobile phones and R$389 in cash. [@1]

The operation began with tip-offs indicating possible drug distribution by
tele-entrega, a delivery-based dealing method in which orders are taken and
drugs are delivered to buyers. [@1]
