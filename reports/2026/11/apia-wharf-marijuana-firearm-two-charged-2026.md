Customs officers at Apia Wharf intercepted two Samoan men aged 25 and 42,
seizing suspected marijuana and an undeclared firearm. [@1] The separate
discoveries were made in the early hours of Saturday 7 November 2026 during
routine passenger screening after the arrival of the MV Lady Samoa IV from
American Samoa. [@1] According to a Ministry of Customs statement, officers
first found suspected marijuana concealed in the pocket of the 25-year-old
passenger. [@1] Shortly afterwards, a firearm was found hidden inside the
luggage of the separate 42-year-old passenger. [@1]

The 25-year-old was charged with importing prohibited goods, false declaration
and possession of narcotics under the Customs Act 2014 and the Narcotics Act
1967. [@1] The 42-year-old was charged with importing a firearm without the
required permit and making a false declaration under the Customs Act 2014 and
the Arms Ordinance 1960. [@1] Both men were handed over to the Samoa Police,
Prisons and Corrections Services for further investigation and legal
proceedings. [@1]

Ministry of Customs chief executive Mulipola Lemalu Maletino Teofilo commended
the officers for their vigilance and professionalism in protecting Samoa's
borders. [@1] The Ministry said it remained committed to strengthening border
security and working with police and other agencies against imports of illicit
drugs, firearms and other prohibited goods. [@1]
