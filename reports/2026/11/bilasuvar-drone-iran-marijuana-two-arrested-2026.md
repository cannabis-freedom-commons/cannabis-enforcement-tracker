Azerbaijan's State Security Service arrested two Azerbaijani citizens, Rüfət
Bəyram oğlu Badalov and Rüstəm Əli oğlu Muxtarov, at the scene near the Jabrayil
internally displaced persons settlement in Bilasuvar District as they came to
collect a drone-delivered drug consignment for onward sale. [@1] The service
said an Iranian national organised the smuggling of an especially large
consignment of drugs by drone near the settlement for refugees from Jabrayil in
Bilasuvar District. [@1]

Officers found and seized 14 kg 158 g of marijuana, along with 979 g of
methamphetamine and 23.1 g of opium, on the two detained men. [@1] APA reported
the marijuana as dried and gave the opium weight as 23.1 grammes. [@2] BR.az
summarised the haul as more than 15 kilograms of narcotic substances seized from
the two Azerbaijani citizens. [@3]

Vesti.az, citing Lent.az, placed the drop in the area of the settlement for
forcibly displaced persons from Jabrayil in Bilasuvar District and said the
operation was run by the State Security Service. [@4]
