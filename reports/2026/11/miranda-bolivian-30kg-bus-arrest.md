Federal Highway Police arrested 25-year-old Bolivian national Belmar Melgar
Gualoa in Mato Grosso do Sul for trying to transport two suitcases full of
cannabis. [@1] The arrest took place during the weekend in Miranda, about 200
kilometres from Campo Grande. [@1] He was travelling on an interstate bus on the
Corumbá-to-São Paulo route. [@1,2]

The inspection took place a little over 200 kilometres after he boarded the bus.
[@1] During the luggage-hold inspection, two suitcases attracted officers
because of their heavy weight and characteristic cannabis smell. [@1,2] Officers
checked the identification on the tags and called the passenger responsible for
the luggage. [@1] He confirmed that the suitcases were his and admitted that he
was carrying drugs. [@1] Weighing showed he was carrying 30.2 kilograms of
cannabis divided into plastic packaging hidden inside the suitcases. [@1,2]

He told police he would receive a sum of money to take the drugs to São Paulo.
[@1] He said he did not know who was supposed to receive the drugs at the
destination. [@1] He went before a custody hearing in the capital on Monday (2).
[@1,2]

He told police he would receive R$2,000 to carry the drugs from Corumbá to São Paulo. [@2]
