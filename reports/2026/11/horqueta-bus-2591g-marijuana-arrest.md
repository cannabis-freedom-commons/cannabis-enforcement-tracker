A 20-year-old man was arrested after he arrived in Horqueta by bus from Santa
Rosa del Aguaray, San Pedro, carrying cocaine and marijuana in a suitcase. [@1]
Officers identified him as Bautista Diego Martínez Morel. [@1] SENAD Regional
Department No. 7 mounted surveillance after intelligence that drugs would be
delivered in Horqueta, Concepción. [@1]

The interception took place at about 21:20 on Wednesday 11 November at the
junction of Ruta Bernardino Caballero and Avenida Mariscal Francisco Solano
López. [@2] When officers checked his luggage as he got off the long-distance
bus, they found rectangular packages wrapped in adhesive tape. [@1] The
marijuana consisted of a 2.565 kg package wrapped in plastic film and 26 grams
divided into five portions, totalling 2.591 kg. [@1] Officers also seized 2.29
kg of suspected cocaine. [@2] They also seized a mobile phone and 220,000
guaraníes in cash. [@2]

Martínez Morel said that he was transporting the drugs from Santa Rosa del
Aguaray. [@1] He had a 2026 aggravated theft record and an outstanding 2026
arrest warrant for coercion. [@1] SENAD special forces supported Regional
Department No. 7, with acting anti-drug prosecutor Arnaldo Argüello and
assistant prosecutor Hernán Huerta accompanying the procedure. [@2]
