Egypt's Interior Ministry said its traffic campaigns tested 892 drivers in 24
hours and 37 were positive for narcotics use. [@1] The same campaigns recorded
102,369 traffic violations in 24 hours on roads across the country. [@3] The
main offences were driving without a licence, speeding, parking at random, using
a mobile phone at the wheel and breaching licence conditions. [@4]

A parallel campaign on work zones along the Regional Ring Road recorded 474
violations in the same 24 hours. [@4] Those violations covered carrying
passengers against the rules, licence conditions and vehicle safety
requirements. [@4] Officers tested 155 drivers there and five were positive for
narcotics. [@4] Five people wanted under 10 court rulings were detained. [@4]
The ministry said the required legal measures had been taken and the competent
authorities were investigating. [@2]

The checks were part of measures to combat driving under the influence of
narcotics, which the ministry described as a factor that threatens road safety
and increases the likelihood of accidents. [@1] The ministry said it continues
periodic campaigns to test drivers and confirm their fitness for safe driving.
