Jayapura district police destroyed 77.41 g of cannabis-type narcotics evidence
on 13 November 2026 at the Jayapura police headquarters. [@1] The destruction
was witnessed by the Jayapura prosecutors' chief, Dewintha Faradilah Ismail, and
the suspects' legal adviser Takwa. [@1] The cannabis had come from two smuggling
cases uncovered in October 2026 at the departure SCP X-ray screening of Sentani
International Airport. [@1] In the first case, opened by police report
LP/A/23/X/RES.4.2/2026 on 6 October 2026, officers seized 42.1 g of ganja from a
33-year-old man, K.D.M. [@1] In the second case, opened by report
LP/A/28/X/RES.4.2/2026 on 19 October 2026, officers seized 35.31 g of ganja from
a 35-year-old man, M.F. [@1] The suspects hid the ganja in the pockets of their
trousers to deceive airport security officers. [@1]

The entire 77.41 g of evidence was destroyed by being burned. [@1] The narcotics
unit's chief, Iptu Daniel Ohei, said the police would tighten surveillance at
strategic transport hubs such as Sentani airport, which is frequently targeted
for cross-region narcotics smuggling. [@1]
