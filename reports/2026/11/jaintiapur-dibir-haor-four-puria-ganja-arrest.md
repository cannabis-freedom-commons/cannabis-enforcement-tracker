Police took the owner of the house, 45-year-old Ramzan Ali, into custody after a
search in Dibir Haor, Jaintiapur upazila, Sylhet district. [@1] The operation
ran in Dibir Haor area of Nijpat Union from 8pm to 10.30pm on Saturday 7
November. [@1]

Police searching his bedroom recovered four puria of cannabis, a local loose
measure, from two separate places under his mattress. [@1] Villagers had earlier
found 11 tablets described as yaba in his trouser pocket under his lungi, a wrap
he was wearing, and called the police. [@1] A police team went to the house and,
with local people's help, seized drug-taking equipment. [@1]

Two other people who were with Ramzan Ali were held by residents and handed to
police. [@1] They were later released after no evidence of drug use was found
against them. [@1] Ramzan Ali admitted smoking cannabis and that the recovered
cannabis was in his room, but denied knowing about the 11 tablets or the
drug-taking equipment. [@1]

Jaintiapur Upazila Executive Officer Mohammad Abul Hasnat Khan, an executive
magistrate, came to the scene around 10.30pm and inspected the drugs and
questioned him. [@1] He said the four puria of cannabis and 11 tablets described
as yaba were presented before him. [@1] He said the tablets must be
laboratory-tested before they can be confirmed as yaba. [@1] The officer said
the case fell outside the mobile court's jurisdiction and that he would order
the officer in charge of Jaintiapur Model Police Station to investigate and take
legal action. [@1]

Local sources said a 2019 drug case filed by 48 Border Guard Bangladesh is
pending against Ramzan Ali at Jaintiapur Model Police Station, case number 27.
