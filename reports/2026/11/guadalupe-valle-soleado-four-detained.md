State investigators and Mexican Army soldiers detained four people during three
simultaneous house searches in colonia Valle Soleado, Guadalupe, Nuevo León, on
the morning of Thursday 5 November 2026. [@2] The searches were at three homes
in colonia Valle Soleado Fomerrey 107, and officers seized bags of suspected
marijuana and three mobile phones. [@3] The Agencia Estatal de Investigaciones
led the operation with Mexican Army personnel and entered the homes before
06:00. [@1] The searches were on the streets Valle de México, Valle Morelia and
Valle de los Pinos. [@1]

Agents of the narcotics division, helped by drug-detection dog teams, took part
in order to locate possible illicit substances. [@1] Those held were Ángel “N”,
aged 28, Jaime “N”, aged 23, María “N”, aged 57, and Yuliana “N”, aged 28. [@1]
During the searches they seized several clear plastic bags containing green, dry
herb with characteristics similar to marijuana, and three mobile phones. [@1]
Homicide investigators from the Agencia Estatal de Investigaciones also took
part. [@3]

The state prosecutor said the searches came from an investigation meant to find
narcotics and other items that could help clarify the violent killing of a man
in the same colonia. [@1] The investigation file remains open, and the
prosecutor secured one of the searched properties under guard. [@1] The Fiscalía
has not said whether the four people are directly linked to the killing or were
detained because of what the searches found. [@3] MVS Noticias reported that it
was not yet clear whether the three searches belonged to one investigation file
or to separate files. [@1]
