Military police detained a man in Corbélia, western Paraná, in the early
afternoon of Friday 6 November 2026. [@1] The stop was recorded at about 13h35
on Rua Orquídea during preventive patrolling. [@1] Officers approached him and a
woman with him after he showed nervousness and tried to grab a black bag she was
carrying. [@1]

The search of the man found a ready-to-use cannabis cigarette and another
portion of cannabis in the left pocket of his shirt. [@1] He told police that
the drugs were his, and said a further amount in the woman's bag was also his.
[@1] The bag held a blue plastic bag with a larger amount of cannabis, and the
woman also carried an electronic smoking device. [@1] The portion found on the
man weighed 4.16 grams and the portion in the bag 77.05 grams, a total of 81.21
grams of cannabis. [@1]

During the paperwork at the Company base, officers found that the woman was a
minor, aged 17, and called in guardianship counsellors to accompany the case.
[@1] The man denied selling the drug and said he would take the larger portion
to work to steep it in a bottle of alcohol, using the smaller portions himself
through the day. [@1] Police gave the man a formal arrest order, guaranteeing
his constitutional rights. [@1] The incident paperwork was drawn up and
presented to the civil police chief in Corbélia for further measures. [@1]
