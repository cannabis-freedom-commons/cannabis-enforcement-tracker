A 66-year-old man and his 29-year-old son stood trial for violence against a
minor under 15. [@1] The assault dates to 27 April 2026 at about 21:00 in
Saint-André, where Joseph F. was watching his cannabis crop after repeated
thefts. [@1] His son Bryan, 29, caught one of the two boys while the other fled.
[@1] The father tied the 14-year-old from head to foot and struck him about
thirty times with a wooden stick, while his son filmed the scene and published
it on Facebook. [@1] The boy's family filed a complaint after the video spread
on social media. [@1] The boy was left with trauma and two days of total work
incapacity. [@1]

Police officers also found 7.7 kg of cannabis herb on the property. [@1] The
father told the court the cannabis grew by itself and was for his own
consumption. [@1] The prosecutor described the assault as a settling of scores
committed for a futile and lucrative motive, with no remorse from the
defendants. [@1]

Neither man was defended by a lawyer. [@1] The tribunal found both men guilty,
giving the father 18 months in prison with probationary suspension and the son
12 months with simple suspension. [@1] Both prison terms were suspended, so no
firm custodial time was ordered. [@2] Four people were placed in police custody
over the incident. [@4] The Saint-Denis prosecutor confirmed the two men would
be summoned before the criminal court for aggravated violence. [@3] The two
minors were to be summoned before the children's court over the attempted theft.
[@3]
