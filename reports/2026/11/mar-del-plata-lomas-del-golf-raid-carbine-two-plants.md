A 39-year-old woman's complaint said a 21-year-old man from the Lomas del Golf
neighbourhood walked around armed and fired shots in the street near her home in
an intimidating manner. [@1] A 21-year-old man was apprehended and faces
proceedings for firearm abuse and illegal possession of a civilian firearm. [@1]
The UFIJE No. 1 under Dr. Salas intervened and later released him under article
161 of the Criminal Procedure Code while the judicial investigation continues.
[@1]

During the search police seized a Bersa R430 semi-automatic carbine, 18
.22-calibre rounds, another 19 .38-calibre rounds and two marijuana plants. [@1]
The search took place in a home on block 4 of the Lomas del Golf neighbourhood
with support from the Grupo de Apoyo Departamental. [@1]

Comisaría Quinta personnel clarified two incidents of firearm abuse and illegal
possession of a civilian firearm following the woman's complaint. [@1] Officers
of the Grupo Técnico Operativo carried out investigative tasks and gathered
evidence that led to a search-warrant request to the Ministerio Público Fiscal.
[@1]
