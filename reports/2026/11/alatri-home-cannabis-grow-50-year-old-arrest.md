Carabinieri of Alatri found a cannabis cultivation inside a home during a house
search on the afternoon of Monday 9 November 2026. [@1] A 50-year-old man,
originally from Frosinone and living in Alatri, was arrested in flagrante at the
end of the operation. [@1] Officers found and seized several Cannabis indica
plants, some still in cultivation and others already dried. [@1] Boxes
containing dried inflorescences and dried foliage were also found. [@1] The home
also held fans, precision scales and LED lamps, equipment described as
compatible with an indoor cultivation activity. [@1]

During the operation the Carabinieri found 350 euros in banknotes of different
denominations. [@1] Cards with handwritten names and signatures were also found,
and investigators consider the notes may be the accounts of a possible dealing
activity. [@1]

The 50-year-old was arrested and taken back to his home, where he was placed
under house arrest. [@1] The public prosecutor, Dr Destro, validated the seizure
of the drug substance and of the material found. [@1] The fixing of the hearing
to validate the arrest is awaited before the Frosinone court. [@1] The man is
defended by his own lawyer, Dario Simonelli. [@1]

For the offence as charged, given the type of substance seized, the law provides
a prison sentence of two to six years and a fine of 5,164 to 77,468 euros. [@1]
The case is at the preliminary investigation stage and the man is presumed
innocent until any final conviction. [@1]
