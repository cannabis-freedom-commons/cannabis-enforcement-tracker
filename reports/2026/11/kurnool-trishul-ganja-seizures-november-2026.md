The Kurnool district administration has intensified its fight against drug
trafficking and consumption. [@1] Collector A. Siri directed officials to take
strict action against those involved in the illegal trade. [@1] At a Narcotics
Coordination Centre meeting held at the Collectorate on Wednesday, Collector A.
Siri and Superintendent of Police Vikrant Patil called for coordinated efforts
by the police, education, excise, medical and welfare departments to make the
district drug-free. [@1] The collector urged education officials to conduct
awareness programmes in all government and private schools, junior colleges and
higher educational institutions. [@1] Students identified as drug users were to
be given confidential counselling and rehabilitation support. [@1] Officials
were also told to identify school dropouts and prevent them from falling into
drug abuse. [@1]

Superintendent of Police Vikrant Patil said police had conducted nine cordon and
search operations and surprise checks under Operation Trishul. [@1] Three
drug-related cases were registered this month. [@1] Police seized 1.5 kg of
ganja in Taluka limits and another 1.5 kg of ganja during an excise operation.
[@1] Police seized 20 grams of MDMA in Veldurthi. [@1]

The administration has identified 30 dark spots in Kurnool city for the
installation of streetlights and CCTV cameras. [@1] Joharapuram Road, Fish Area
and Bangarupeta have been identified as sensitive locations requiring special
surveillance. [@1]
