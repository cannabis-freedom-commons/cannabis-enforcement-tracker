Police stopped a 61-year-old man in the settlement of Akkermanovka near
Novotroitsk, in Orenburg Oblast, on suspicion of growing drugs, regional
interior ministry (UMVD) press officers said. [@1] While searching the
outbuildings on his property, officers found and seized 64 cannabis plants and
the equipment used to grow them, including lamps, fans, watering cans, seeds and
fertiliser. [@1] They also found and seized 991 grams of marijuana, and an
examination confirmed that the seized plant material was a narcotic drug of
plant origin. [@1]

The man told investigators, according to police, that he grew the cannabis to
sell it to people who use drugs and to smoke part of it himself. [@3]
Investigators opened criminal cases against him for attempted large-scale sale
of narcotics and for illegal cultivation of plants containing narcotic
substances. [@1] Under the sale charge he faces up to 20 years in prison, and
the cultivation charge carries up to two years, Kommersant reported. [@3]

The arrest was made during "Kanal – Kaspiysky Mayak", a preventive anti-drug
operation, in a search involving officers of the Orenburg Oblast drug-control
directorate and police station No 3 of the Orsk interior ministry directorate.
[@2] The local news outlet 1743.ru reported on the case under the headline that
the man could face 15 years. [@2] Ak Kermanovka lies just outside Novotroitsk, a
city in Orenburg Oblast in Russia's southern Urals. [@4]
