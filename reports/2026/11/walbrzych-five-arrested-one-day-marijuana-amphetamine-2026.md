Wałbrzych police detained five people within one day on suspicion of possessing
drugs, four of them with marijuana. [@1] Criminal officers saw a 22-year-old man
near the market square in Śródmieściu after 4 p.m. and found a wrap of plant
material that a forensic field test confirmed was marijuana. [@1]

At about 6 p.m. district officers detained a 39-year-old in a stairwell of a
building on Traktorzystów Street in Szczawienko; he carried plastic wraps of
plant material and crystals that tested as marijuana and methamphetamine. [@1]
The same officers then searched a flat in the same building and detained a
49-year-old woman, finding a blue box with larger amounts of plant material,
crystals and white powder that tested as marijuana, methamphetamine and
amphetamine. [@1] After 10 p.m. officers stopped a car travelling through
Lubomin in Wałbrzych County and found a metal box with two plastic bags of
crystals that proved to be methamphetamine. [@1] The 41-year-old driver was held
in a police cell overnight and was taken to the District Prosecutor's Office in
Wałbrzych, where he was charged with possessing a significant quantity of drugs;
that methamphetamine-only case is not counted as cannabis. [@1] The next day at
about 6 a.m. preventive officers detained a 27-year-old walking on Piłsudskiego
Street in Nowe Miasto with a plastic wrap of white powder that tested as
amphetamine, a case with no cannabis component. [@1] At the same time
intelligence-unit officers went to Klonowa Street in Szczawienko and found a
23-year-old man sitting in a car with plant material that tested as marijuana,
and a search of his flat produced crystals that tested as methamphetamine. [@1]

All five suspects were questioned and charged under Poland's law on
counteracting drug addiction. [@1]

Police said four of them face up to three years in prison. [@1] The man found
with the larger amount of methamphetamine faces up to ten years in prison. [@1]
