SRB-11 officers searching a Dhaka-bound passenger bus arrested two suspected
drug traders with 14 kg of ganja in Bandar, Narayanganj. [@1] The two were
detained at about 2:45 am on Tuesday, 3 November 2026, in front of Rafi Filling
Station at Madanpur under Bandar Police Station, on the Dhaka-Chattogram
highway. [@1]

The arrested were 24-year-old Shahin Sardar, from Sardarkandi in Naria,
Shariatpur district, and 25-year-old Mehedi Hasan, from Mirzapur Charkhali in
Nazirpur, Pirojpur district. [@1] SRB-11 naik subedar Bilpob Molla filed the
case as complainant against the two at Bandar Police Station under the Narcotics
Control Act. [@1] The two men were sent to court in that case at midday on
Wednesday, 4 November. [@1]
