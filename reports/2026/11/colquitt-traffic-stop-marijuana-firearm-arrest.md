A Georgia State Patrol trooper stopped a vehicle on Highway 27 near MLK Street
in Colquitt at about 7:44 p.m. on Thursday, Nov. 5. [@1,3] During the stop,
troopers found a large amount of marijuana and a firearm inside the vehicle,
according to the Colquitt Police Department. [@1,3] The Georgia State Patrol asked the Colquitt Police Department's Investigations Division to take over the case. [@3]

One person was arrested and taken to the Miller County Jail without incident.
[@1,3] Officials also thanked the Georgia State Patrol for its vigilance and the Miller County Sheriff's Office for its assistance. [@3] The arrested person's identity and the charges they face had not been
released when the article was published. [@1]
