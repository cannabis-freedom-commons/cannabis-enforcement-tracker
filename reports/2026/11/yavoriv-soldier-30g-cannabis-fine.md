A court in Yavoriv, Lviv Oblast, fined a serving soldier 17,000 hryvnia on 4
November 2026 for acquiring and keeping cannabis for his own use. [@1] The
Yavoriv District Court convicted him under part 1 of article 309 of the Criminal
Code, a misdemeanour covering unlawful acquisition and storage of drugs without
intent to sell. [@1] The fine must be paid within a month of the verdict taking
legal force. [@1] The court also ordered him to pay 2,888.16 hryvnia for an
expert examination into the state budget. [@1] The court ordered the plant
material taken from him destroyed. [@1]

At the start of January 2026 the soldier found a cannabis plant while walking in
a forest near the city of Karlivka in Poltava Oblast, tore off the leaves and
took them with him for his own use. [@1] The verdict can be appealed to the Lviv
Court of Appeal through the Yavoriv District Court within 30 days, and it takes
legal force if no appeal is filed. [@1] On 31 August 2026 police inspecting the
scene on the grounds of his military unit took a bag of cannabis weighing 30.29
grams from him. [@1] He admitted acquiring and keeping the drug without intent
to sell. [@1]
