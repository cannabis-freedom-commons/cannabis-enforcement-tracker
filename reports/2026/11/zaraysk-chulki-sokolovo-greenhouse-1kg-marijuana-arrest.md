Police drug-control officers in Zaraysk detained a 41-year-old local man on
suspicion of illegal drug trafficking. [@1] He had organized cannabis
cultivation on his private plot in the village of Chulki-Sokolovo, planting the
plants in a greenhouse in May and later harvesting, drying and grinding the
crop. [@1] According to police, he planted the cannabis in the greenhouse in May
of this year and gathered the harvest several months later. [@1]

The suspect was detained at his place of residence during operational-search
measures. [@1] During an inspection of the plot, officers found four boxes of
plant material in the greenhouse. [@1] A third account likewise says police
detained him at his residence and found four boxes of plant material in the
greenhouse on the plot. [@3] A forensic examination confirmed the seized
substance was marijuana with a total mass of more than one kilogram. [@1] A
second account likewise reports four boxes containing more than one kilogram of
marijuana. [@2]

Investigators opened a criminal case over the seizure under part 2 of article
228 of the Criminal Code. [@1] The Zaraysk city court imposed a restraint
measure on him in the form of a ban on certain actions. [@1] Investigators are
continuing to establish possible distribution channels and to check the detainee
for involvement in other drug-trafficking episodes. [@1]
