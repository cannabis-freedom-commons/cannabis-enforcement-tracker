Cairo's appeals criminal court rejected the appeal of an unemployed man against
his 10-year prison sentence for possessing firearms and hashish in Dar El Salam
and upheld the first-instance ruling. [@1] Cairo security directorate officers
arrested the man in the city for possessing hashish, firearms, bladed weapons
and ammunition without a licence with intent to trade in them. [@1]

Detectives of the Dar El Salam police station had confirmed that a man living in
the district held drugs, firearms and bladed weapons without a licence for
trafficking. [@1] After legal procedures were completed, officers arrested him
at his home area and found 90 grams of hashish, a modified single-barrel
shotgun, ammunition of different calibres, two bladed weapons and a sum of
money. [@1] Confronted with the findings, he admitted holding the drugs for
trafficking, the firearms and bladed weapons for the same trade and the money as
proceeds of his criminal activity. [@1] A report was drawn up and the Public
Prosecution took over the investigation. [@1]
