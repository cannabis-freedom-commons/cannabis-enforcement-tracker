Polresta Pekanbaru ran an anti-drug operation from 8 October to 3 November 2026
targeting drug-prone kampungs and distribution sites. [@1] Officers detained 46
people at several locations during the operation. [@1] Of the 46 detained,
police classified 28 as users and 18 as dealers or couriers, separating the two
groups for different legal handling. [@1]

Officers seized 90.32 grams of sabu, or methamphetamine, as evidence in the
operation. [@1] Police said the operation also seized ganja, sabu, vape
containing etomidate liquid and other goods, including bladed weapons taken at
raid sites. [@1] Officers also found a suspected narcotics seedling from one
suspect, estimated to be 7 days old. [@1] The narcotics chief confirmed the find
was a suspected cannabis plant about 7 days old that would be examined at the
forensic laboratory to confirm its contents. [@1]

The main targets were Jalan Pangeran Hidayat, Kampung Dalam, Kampung Terendam
and other vulnerable locations, including nightlife venues. [@1] Police chief
Kombes Pol Ronny Riantoko Eppang announced the results at a release at
Mapolresta on Jalan Ahmad Yani, where police put the combined evidence value at
Rp185 million and claimed 1,200 lives saved. [@1]
