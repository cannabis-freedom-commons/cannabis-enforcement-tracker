Officers of the Motorised Operations Corps of the Chaco police stopped three men
in a white Renault Clio at about 03:40 on Friday 6 November 2026, at the
junction of avenida Lonardi and calle Borrini in Resistencia, during a
preventive check. [@3] The three detained men were 21, 23 and 31 years old. [@2]
One of the occupants was carrying a jar with a substance that raised suspicion,
and another was carrying a firearm. [@1]

Personnel of the Problematic Consumption division carried out a field test that
was positive for marijuana, with a total weight of six grams. [@1] Officers also
seized a .22 long-calibre revolver, identified as a Doberman, with three unfired
cartridges and one spent casing in its cylinder. [@2] The man carrying the
revolver could not prove its ownership or where it came from. [@1]

The three men were taken to the Ninth Metropolitan police station for the
corresponding legal formalities. [@3] The case was recorded as proceedings for a
suspected infringement of article 189 bis of the Penal Code. [@1]
