Detectives of the Zefta police station in Gharbia Governorate arrested a
38-year-old woman suspected of trading drugs and selling them to clients in
Nahtay village and neighbouring villages after security investigations pointed
to her activity. [@1] The suspect, identified only as E.H., is 38 years old and
lives in Nahtay village in Zefta district. [@1] Police said secret
investigations indicated she used Nahtay and the surrounding area to promote
drugs to people dealing with her. [@1]

Gharbia criminal-investigation chief Mohamed Assem ordered a team to examine and
verify the information and take the necessary legal measures, under the
supervision of Zefta–Santa investigation head Mohamed Saqr and with Zefta
detectives and their force. [@1] After legal procedures were completed, officers
set a tight security ambush that caught her. [@1] A search found a large
quantity of hashish in her possession, along with money suspected to be proceeds
of her trafficking and a mobile phone suspected of being used to contact clients
and facilitate her activity. [@1]

Officers took her to Zefta police station, where she admitted possessing the
drugs for the purpose of trafficking, as recorded in the incident report. [@1]
Police held the seized items, filed the necessary report and notified the Public
Prosecution, which took over the investigation to establish the circumstances
and complete the legal procedures. [@1]
