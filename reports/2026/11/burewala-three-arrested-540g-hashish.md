Model Town police in Burewala arrested three suspects, identified as Arshad,
Sarfaraz and Saleem, in separate operations. [@1] Arshad was held on an
allegation of selling liquor, Sarfaraz on an allegation of possessing an illegal
weapon, and Saleem on an allegation of drug peddling. [@1]

Police recovered 22 litres of liquor from Arshad's possession. [@1] Police
apprehended Sarfaraz for allegedly possessing an illegal 30-bore pistol. [@1] In
a separate operation, police arrested Saleem and recovered 540 grammes of
hashish, the cannabis resin, from his possession. [@1] Police described the
combined seizure from the operations as 540 grammes of hashish, 22 litres of
liquor and a 30-bore pistol. [@1]

SHO Model Town Khalid Mahmood Chauhan registered separate cases against the
suspects and initiated further legal proceedings. [@1] Police said the
operations were conducted on the directions of Vehari District Police Officer
Tassawar Iqbal. [@1]
