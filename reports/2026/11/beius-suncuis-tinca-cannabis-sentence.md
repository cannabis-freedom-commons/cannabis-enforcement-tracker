Police from Rural Police Section 2 in Beiuș detained Cosmin Tinca, a 44-year-old
man from Șuncuiș, and handed him to Oradea Penitentiary after a court sentenced
him to 3 years and 4 months in prison. [@1] The Beiuș Court gave that sentence
in October 2026 and it was not appealed. It combined 6 months for escape with
the conviction imposed in April 2024 for selling prohibited substances. [@1] He
had been under house arrest in the drug case when, on the evening of 21 October
2023, he went to a shop in Șuncuiș to drink a beer with two friends. [@1] The
chief of the Finiș police post saw him there while checking on him. [@1] Tinca
said he had not thought that going to the bar broke the house-arrest conditions.
[@1] The breach brought another 6 months in custody, during which he painted the
entire Bihor Police lock-up. [@1]

Investigators first looked at him because in January 2023 he ordered half a
kilogram of cannabis from Spain. [@1] He was caught when he collected the parcel
from a transport company, and 93 grams of a prohibited substance were found at
his home. [@1] Anti-drug police and DIICOT Oradea, the organised-crime
prosecutor's office, had followed him for some time. [@1] In November 2022 an
authorised collaborator of the investigators received cannabis from him for
free. [@1] He later brokered the sale of 13 grams of cannabis for 650 lei. [@1]
Intercepted calls showed that Tinca sent money to an unknown person in Spain who
promised to send him a parcel of drugs. [@1]

After he was caught collecting the parcel he spent more than half a year in
jail, and the court then replaced detention with house arrest, which he observed
for two months before the bar visit. [@1] In April 2024 he was sentenced to 3
years and 2 months in prison, to be served, for drug trafficking, and he was
released on parole in June 2025 after 871 days in detention. [@1] That time was
subtracted from the recent Beiuș sentence, so he was expected to serve about 11
months more. [@1]
