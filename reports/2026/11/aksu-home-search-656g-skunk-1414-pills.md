Antalya Provincial Gendarmerie Command teams conducted work to combat drugs on
the instructions of the Antalya Chief Public Prosecutor's Office in Antalya
province. [@1] As part of that work, officers carried out a search at the
residence of one suspect in Aksu district. [@1] After the search, the
authorities said judicial proceedings related to the incident had been started.
[@1]

In the home search, officers seized 656 grams of skunk. [@1] In the same search,
officers seized 1,414 synthetic pills alongside the skunk. [@1] Officers also
seized one precision scale from the same residence during the address search.
[@1] The synthetic pills were reported as a separate seizure item from the
skunk, so only the 656 grams of skunk is counted as cannabis here. [@1]

The search was presented as part of the gendarmerie's ongoing anti-drug efforts
in the province. [@1] In a statement after the operation, the Antalya Provincial
Gendarmerie Command said its work to combat drugs would continue without
interruption. [@1]
