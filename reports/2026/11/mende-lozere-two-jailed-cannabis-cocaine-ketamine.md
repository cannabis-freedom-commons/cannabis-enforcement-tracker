Police in Mende arrested two men on 13 November 2026 after an investigation by
the town's police station. [@1] The first man, aged 45, was suspected of acting
as a "nourrice", storing drugs on behalf of a trafficker. [@1] He admitted guilt
through the CRPC guilty-plea procedure and was sentenced to 14 months in prison,
five of them suspended on probation, plus a 1,000-euro fine. [@1] The second
man, aged 20, was also arrested in the same inquiry. [@1] He was sentenced to
six months in firm prison for repeat drug use. [@1]

The older man stored cannabis together with cocaine and ketamine. [@1] Police
seized cannabis resin along with cocaine and ketamine. [@1] Both arrests and
both sentences took place on the same day. [@1]

The Mende police station led the investigation that produced the two arrests.
