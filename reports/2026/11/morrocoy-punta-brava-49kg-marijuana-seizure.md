Officers of the First Company of Detachment 133 of the Bolivarian National Guard
found the packages while on a foot patrol along the beachfront when they spotted
the wrappers dragged by the waves. [@1] Three panels of marijuana weighing a
total of 4.9 kilograms were found floating at the shoreline of Punta Brava
beach, inside Morrocoy National Park in Silva municipality, Falcón state. [@1]
The seizure occurred at 6:30 in the evening on the previous Thursday during
coastal surveillance work. [@1]

After securing the perimeter and collecting the packages to verify their
contents, officers confirmed the illicit substance and seized it under
chain-of-custody protocols. [@1] A second outlet confirmed the same seizure of
three marijuana panels weighing four kilos and 900 grams, seized under
chain-of-custody procedures. [@2]

The military component formally notified the procedure and referred the case to
the 21st Prosecutor's Office of the Public Ministry, which will lead the
investigation into the origin of the drugs and those responsible. [@1] Available
information does not say how the packages reached the Punta Brava coast or
whether anyone has been detained in the case. [@2]
