A mobile court in Islampur upazila of Jamalpur district sentenced 24-year-old
Sajib Mia to three months of simple imprisonment and a 100-taka fine under the
Narcotics Control Act on 9 November 2026. [@1] Executive magistrate Shah
Mohammad Zobayer, the upazila's chief government officer (UNO), gave the
sentence in the afternoon and had him jailed after he pleaded guilty. [@2]

Officers of the Department of Narcotics Control, the national anti-drug agency
known as DNC, detained him during a raid on Bepari Para in Islampur
municipality, acting on a tip-off. [@1] They seized 20 grams of ganja, the local
term for cannabis, from him at the scene. [@1] He is the son of Ataur Rahman of
Moradubri village in Parthashi union of the upazila. [@1] After the sentence was
pronounced the court issued a warrant and sent him to the district jail. [@3]

The upazila chief executive said the district and upazila administrations'
special monitoring and mobile-court drives would continue to build a drug-free
society. [@3]
