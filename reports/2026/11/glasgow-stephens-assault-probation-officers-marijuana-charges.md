Around 11:30 a.m. on Thursday, Nov. 12, Glasgow police officers were dispatched
to assist Probation and Parole with a reported combative subject. [@1] On
arrival police saw probation and parole officers struggling with the subject,
identified as Kenneth Stephens of Glasgow. [@1] Barrenside reported the man as
45-year-old Kenneth V. Stephens. [@2] Stephens was detained after allegedly
physically assaulting multiple officers, scratching one and pushing another into
a set of large mirrors. [@1] Authorities said Stephens was reported to have a
strong odor of marijuana. [@1]

Stephens admitted to probation and parole that he possessed marijuana at his
home. [@1] Police later obtained a search warrant for his residence. [@2]
Executing the warrant, probation and parole located marijuana, scales, a grinder
and an unidentified powder, along with a large amount of cash. [@1] The powder
is described as an unidentified tan powder substance. [@2]

Stephens was arrested and charged with second-degree criminal mischief,
menacing, third-degree assault of a police officer or probation officer and
public intoxication involving a controlled substance, excluding alcohol. [@1] He
faces additional charges of trafficking in marijuana (less than 8 ounces), first
offense, possession of drug paraphernalia and first-degree possession of a
controlled substance, first offense, with the drug unspecified. [@1] He was
transported to the Barren County Detention Center. [@1] He remained jailed at
the Barren County Detention Center. [@2]
