Police in Sitamarhi district, Bihar, arrested a man on Thursday evening near
Pataura Bazar in the Bajpatti police station area and seized 15 kg of ganja from
the battery e-rickshaw he was riding. [@1] The team was from ALTF-3 Pupri, a
special anti-liquor and narcotics police unit, led by sub-inspector Vijay
Shankar Singh. [@1] Officers were checking vehicles on the Radhaur–Pathrahi road
about 100 metres south of Pataura Bazar when they stopped the e-rickshaw coming
from the Radhaur side. [@2]

A search of the e-rickshaw found three large packets of ganja hidden behind the
driver's seat, totalling 15 kg. [@1] The arrested man is from Manpaur village in
the Bela police station area of Sitamarhi district. [@1] Police seized the
battery e-rickshaw, which Bhaskar described as having no registration number,
and an Android mobile phone from the suspect. [@2]

Police said they were examining the phone's call records to trace the network's
leaders and local buyers. [@1] In initial questioning the suspect admitted he
had brought the consignment from neighbouring Nepal to sell at high prices in
local markets in India. [@1]

Officers filed a first information report against him under sections of the
Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, 1985, and prepared to send him
to judicial custody. [@1] The arrest came during a special police campaign
against drug trafficking in Sitamarhi district. [@1]
