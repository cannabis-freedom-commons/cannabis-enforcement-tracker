A Greek man was arrested in Lamia on the evening of 10 November 2026 by police
officers of the OP.DI. prevention and mediation team of the Lamia crime
investigation sub-directorate, for a breach of the drugs legislation. [@1]
Police found the man driving a car he owned, and seized a nylon bag containing
105 grams of raw cannabis. [@1]

The Lamia crime investigation sub-directorate carried out the preliminary
investigation, and the arrested man was to be taken to the Lamia first-instance
prosecutor's office. [@1] eReportaz reported that police in Lamia arrested a man
on the evening of Tuesday 10 November 2026 for a breach of the drugs
legislation. [@2]

iparnassos reported that during a police check a package with 105 grams of raw
cannabis was found in the man's possession and confiscated. [@3] The man was
expected to be taken to the Lamia first-instance prosecutor's office while the
competent police service handled the preliminary investigation. [@3]
