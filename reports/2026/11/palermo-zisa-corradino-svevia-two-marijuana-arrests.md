Officers of the mobile squad arrested two young men after two separate
interventions on Via Corradino di Svevia, in Palermo's Zisa district, where they
documented several sales of marijuana. [@1] In the first, they watched a
19-year-old from Bergamo province take a packet from a parked car and hand it to
a buyer for cash. [@1] They stopped him, seized 190 euros treated as proceeds of
dealing, and found 31 packets totalling 70 grams of marijuana hidden under the
passenger seat. [@1] He was placed under house arrest pending a validation
hearing. [@1]

On another occasion on the same street, the Falchi unit of the mobile squad saw
a 26-year-old Palermo man with a police record hand over a dose of marijuana and
stopped both him and the buyer. [@1] A search of the parked car where the drug
was kept found a bag of 960 grams of marijuana and 37 ready-made doses weighing
99 grams. [@1] On the order of the investigating judge of the Palermo court, the
26-year-old was taken to a cell at Pagliarelli prison to await a validation
hearing, after which the judge will decide whether to impose a precautionary
measure. [@1]
