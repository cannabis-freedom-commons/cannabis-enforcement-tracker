Bathery police arrested 35-year-old Abi Elias near Makkutti bridge and seized 1.08 kilograms of ganja. [@1,2,3,4,5,6,7]

Police in Sultan Bathery arrested 35-year-old Aby Elias during a vehicle check
near Makkutty bridge. [@7] Elias is described as a resident of Pelakkudi house
at Puthankunnu, Makkutty in Bathery. [@7] Bathery sub-inspector Noufal C made
the arrest together with Bathery police and the district anti-drug squad. [@7]
The operation was conducted under the supervision of Wayanad district police
chief S. Deva Manohar and led by Narcotic Cell and Gender Justice DySP Manoharan
Thachampath. [@7]

Officers seized 1 kg 80 g of ganja, or 1.08 kg, from the accused. [@7] Ganja is
the Malayalam term used in the report for cannabis. [@7] They also seized an
electronic scale used for weighing and plastic covers kept for packing the drug
for sale. [@7]

Police said the ganja had been smuggled from Mysuru intended for sale. [@7] The
accused was intercepted at the Makkutty bridge checkpoint while the joint team
was conducting vehicle checks. [@7] The drug, scale and packing material were
all recovered from him at that interception. [@7]
