Selaiyur police went to the Puthuvancheri cremation ground in Tambaram, Tamil
Nadu, after a secret tip that ganja, or cannabis, was being sold inside their
police station limits. [@1] Officers rushed to the spot, and when they tried to
catch the man selling the drug there, he ran as soon as he saw them. [@1]

He tried to escape by jumping over the cremation ground's compound wall. [@1] He
fell and fractured his left leg. [@1] Police picked him up as he lay in pain and
took him to the Tambaram district government headquarters hospital. [@1] There
his leg was put in a plaster cast as first aid. [@1]

The investigation identified him as Martin and found that 22 criminal cases,
including murder and robbery, were pending against him. [@1] Police seized the
21 kg of ganja that he had kept with him for sale. [@1] They arrested him,
produced him before a court and jailed him. [@1]
