Manickam, 36, son of Arumugam of Alanthalai Tsunami Colony near Tiruchendur in
Thoothukudi district, was arrested by Kurumbur police for alleged sale of ganja,
the local term for cannabis. [@1] He was accused of unlawful ganja sale in his
own area and had already been arrested and lodged in jail by Kurumbur police
station officers. [@2] Police said he persisted in ganja sale, after which
District Superintendent of Police Sivaprasad recommended his detention under the
Goondas Act, Tamil Nadu's preventive-detention law for habitual offenders. [@2]
Acting on that recommendation, District Collector Vishu Mahajan ordered Manickam
to be jailed for one year under the Goondas Act. [@2]

Kurumbur police served a copy of the Collector's order at Palayamkottai Central
Jail to bring the detention into effect. [@3] The reports place this detention
in a wider district drive, stating that 129 people have been detained under the
Goondas Act in Thoothukudi district so far this year in cases including murder,
attempted murder and ganja sale. [@1]
