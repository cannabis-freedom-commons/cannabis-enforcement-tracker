In the province of Rome, police arrested 17 people in the act for possession of
drugs for dealing, six Italians and eleven foreigners. [@1] Three Italian
citizens were instead reported for unlawful carrying of weapons or objects
capable of offending. [@2] During the Rome services officers identified 152
people, 25 minors and 127 adults, of whom 50 were Italian and 102 foreign, with
nine adult foreigners found to be irregular on national territory. [@1] The
checks focused on the capital's main nightlife areas, from San Lorenzo to
Trastevere via Ponte Milvio, Termini and Colle Oppio, as well as public
demonstrations. [@1]

Officers carried out 26 personal searches and two home checks, seizing about
five kilos of drugs between cocaine and hashish and 5,500 euros in cash. [@2]
During the checks officers also found three knives, pepper spray and two
balaclavas, while 37 vehicles were checked with 27 administrative penalties
imposed. [@1]

The Rome checks were part of an activity coordinated by the Servizio centrale
operativo involving the Mobile Squads across Italy that led to 644 arrests
nationally, 575 adults and 69 minors. [@1] Nationally in the same operation
police reported 69.4 kilos of cocaine, 174.8 kilos of cannabinoids and 328.44
grams of heroin, plus smaller amounts of other drugs. [@1] A separate line of
inquiry into serious disorder on 1 July in largo Gaetana Agnesi led to searches
in Pomezia seizing drugs, jewellery believed to be stolen, nunchaku and an air
rifle. [@1]
