State police in Vibo Valentia reported one arrest, several denunciations, drug
and weapon seizures and the identification of those responsible for an assault
in the nightlife area. [@1] Investigators arrested a local young man during a
targeted home search. [@1] A second person was denounced at liberty after being
found with about 100 grams of marijuana. [@1] Three other people were reported
administratively to the Prefecture as users. [@1] Investigators also identified
those responsible for an assault near a venue in the city centre. [@1]

Officers found and seized about half a kilo of narcotics divided among
marijuana, cocaine and hashish, plus a cartridge, in the searched home. [@1]
Officers seized three knives and a telescopic baton from people carrying them in
public without justification, who were referred at liberty for unlawful carrying
of weapons. [@1] A second outlet confirms the arrested youth was found with
about half a kilo of substances including marijuana, cocaine and hashish. [@2]
The same outlet confirms three people were reported for personal use while a
fourth found with about 100 grams of marijuana was denounced. [@2]

The local Squadra Mobile led the checks with other Questura units and support
from the Calabria Crime Prevention Department, focusing on youth gathering and
evening nightlife areas. [@1] The Vibo checks were part of a national
youth-crime operation coordinated by the Central Operational Service involving
Italy's 106 mobile squads. [@2]
