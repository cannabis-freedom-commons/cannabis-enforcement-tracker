The Anti-Narcotics Task Force of Davangere district police registered cases
against 23 people in a week-long crackdown on the sale, transport and
consumption of narcotic substances. [@1]

One of the operations was a raid on a public place in Manome Layout, under
K.T.J. Nagar police station. [@1] There the team arrested Mohammed Sahil, 23, of
Davangere, for allegedly selling ganja illegally. [@1] Police seized 300 grams
of ganja (0.3 kg) and ₹1,100 in cash from him. [@1]

Sahil had earlier been booked in two cases under Section 27(b) of the Narcotic
Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act, one at K.T.J. Nagar police station
and another at R.M.C. police station. [@1] In a separate drive during the week,
the task force registered cases against 22 people for allegedly consuming
narcotic substances in different police station limits. [@1] The police listed
eight of them in Badaavane police station limits, four in K.T.J. Nagar, one in
Gandhinagar, three in Vidyanagar and six in Santhebennur. [@1]

The operations were conducted on the directions of the district Superintendent
of Police and under the guidance of the Additional Superintendent of Police.
[@1] The district police said the task force would continue its drive against
the sale, transport and consumption of narcotic substances. [@1]
