A man found at a house in Crawley Hill, West Wellow was arrested at the scene
during the police warrant. [@1] Mentor Beluli, 25, who was listed as of no fixed
abode, was charged with producing cannabis, a Class B controlled drug. [@1] He
was due to appear at Basingstoke Magistrates Court on 3 November. [@1]

Hampshire Constabulary officers carried out the warrant at the house in Crawley
Hill on November 2. [@1] Police said the warrant at the Crawley Hill address
took place on Monday 2 November. [@1]

Inside the house, officers found around 160 cannabis plants. [@1] Officers also
found a pile of recently harvested cannabis at the same address. [@1] Police
described the site as a suspected cannabis factory at a residential address in
West Wellow. [@1]
