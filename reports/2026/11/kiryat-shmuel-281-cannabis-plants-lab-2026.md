Detectives from the Zevulun police station exposed a drugs laboratory in an
apartment in Kiryat Shmuel and seized 281 seedlings they suspected were
cannabis. [@1] The station put the seedlings' combined weight at about 125
kilograms. [@1] Haifa and Krayot News, citing the Israel Police spokesperson,
described the apartment as a laboratory for growing plants suspected to be
cannabis. [@2]

Officers searched the flat as part of police activity against offences of
growing drugs and trafficking in them, and found the seedlings during that
search. [@1] Two suspects were arrested, taken to the Zevulun station for
questioning, and remanded in custody when that questioning ended. [@1]

Coastal District commander Yechiel Bohadana then signed an administrative
closure order for the apartment where the laboratory had been exposed. [@1]
Haifa and Krayot News said the police spokesperson was the source of the
closure-order account. [@2] No further details of the investigation, and no
identities of the suspects, had been released. [@2]
