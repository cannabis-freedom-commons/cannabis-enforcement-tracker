In Pristina on 2 November 2026, a woman reported to Kosovo Police that a man had
threatened her with a knife. [@1] Police arrested the suspect and searched him.
[@2] The search found a knife and 0.36 grams of a substance suspected to be
marijuana on him. [@1] By decision of the prosecutor, the man was taken into
custody. [@4]

The account comes from the police's 24-hour incident report, which that day
listed seven reports of domestic violence or threats across five municipalities.
[@1] According to that report, cases were registered in Pristina, Peja, Klina,
Gjakova and Vushtrri, and in some of them suspects were arrested and taken into
custody by decision of the prosecutor. [@1]

The outlets that reported the roundup from the police statement published no
charge over the marijuana and did not say what proceedings followed. [@3] The
material is described only as a substance suspected to be marijuana, with no
testing result published. [@2]
