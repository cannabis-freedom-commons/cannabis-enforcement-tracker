Federal Police dismantled a suspected drug-sales premises (kiosco narco) in the
Pocito department of San Juan. [@1] The Federal Police Investigations Department
ran the procedure that began in early November from a report filed with the San
Juan Federal Prosecutor's Unit led by prosecutor Carolina Menéndez. [@1] The
Federal Warrants Court under Judge Leopoldo Rago Gallo authorised the search of
the premises on calle Lemos in Pocito. [@1]

The Federal Police seized more than 100 doses of cocaine and marijuana prepared
for sale, along with psychotropic substances, one Cannabis sativa plant, two
tumbero-style knives, a marijuana grinder, a precision scale and mobile phones.
[@1] Investigators ran intelligence and surveillance that indicated the property
on calle Lemos in Pocito was being used to sell cocaine and marijuana. [@1]
During the raid, officers seized more than 100 doses of cocaine, marijuana and
psychotropic substances, and one adult man was detained. [@1] As a result of the
procedure, officers detained one adult Argentine man, the presumed person
involved in the illicit activity. [@1]

The detainee and the seized items were placed at the disposal of the presiding
judge in a case for breach of the narcotics law. [@1]
