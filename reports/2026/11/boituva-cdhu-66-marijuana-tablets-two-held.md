Municipal guards in Boituva arrested a man and took a teenager into custody on
the afternoon of Friday 6 November 2026 over drug trafficking in a flat in a
CDHU public housing estate in the Novo Mundo neighbourhood. [@1] The Guarda
Civil Municipal, the municipal guard force, said a patrol of its GPO Alfa unit
spotted a car at about 14h30 and the driver tried to flee before stopping near
the estate. [@1] The two occupants ran into one of the blocks and were caught by
the guards in a third-floor flat. [@1]

In the flat the guards seized 66 tablets of marijuana, seven packets of dry, two
of skunk, two more packets of marijuana, 155 portions of cocaine and 26
marijuana and six crack portions ready for sale. [@1] The guards also took two
precision scales, a blender with powder residue, a sieve, a notebook, four boxes
of antibiotics, two mobile phones, a compressed-air carbine and six .38 rounds.
[@1] The GCM also seized R$1,885 in cash. [@1]

The suspects and the material were taken to the Tatuí flagrante police centre,
where delegate Mateus Benavides confirmed the adult's arrest in flagrante and
booked him for drug trafficking and corruption of a minor. [@1] The teenager was
held for an offence analogous to drug trafficking. [@1] g1 reported that the
guards found the flat after following the suspects to the third floor and
described the place as a casa bomba, a base flat used for drug dealing. [@2] g1
added that the seized notebook held notes on the trafficking accounts. [@2]
