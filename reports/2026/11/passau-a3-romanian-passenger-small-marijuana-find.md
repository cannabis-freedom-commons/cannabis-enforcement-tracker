Two Romanian women were driving through Germany from the Netherlands towards
Romania when border-investigation officers stopped their car on the A 3 motorway
near Passau on Sunday afternoon. [@1]

The officers found a small amount of marijuana on the 23-year-old passenger.
[@1] Officers seized the cannabis, which the Passau border police account
describes as a narcotic, because taking cannabis into and out of Germany remains
a punishable offence. [@1]

An investigation was opened against the woman. [@1] Neither woman was detained,
and both were allowed to continue their journey after the check. [@1]

The account was published by the Passau border police inspectorate. [@1] The
check was carried out by Schleierfahnder, the border police officers who run
dragnet checks on routes near the border. [@1]
