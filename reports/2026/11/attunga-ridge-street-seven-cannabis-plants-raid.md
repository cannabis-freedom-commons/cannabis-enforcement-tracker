Nathan Singh, 54, was taken into custody after police surrounded a property in
Ridge Street, Attunga, on Wednesday 4 November 2026. [@2] Officers of the Oxley
Police District Proactive Crime Team, backed by the Western Operation Support
Group, searched the home and allegedly found a rifle, a rifle scope, a Glock
pistol, magazines, a slingshot, nunchucks, methamphetamine, seven cannabis
plants and five packets of cannabis seeds. [@2] He was arrested and taken to
Tamworth Police Station, where he was charged with firearm offences, including
possession of an unauthorised pistol, and with possessing and cultivating a
prohibited drug. [@2]

Police refused him bail and he spent the night in custody before appearing in a
Sydney bail court the next day. [@2] The prosecutor said the firearms were gel
blasters that looked real. [@2] The prosecutor also said Singh had denied
knowledge of the firearms, which were found in a bedroom. [@2] His solicitor
said he had epilepsy and had been using cannabis for that and other conditions.
[@2] She said he was trying to obtain a prescription for medicinal cannabis.
[@2] His solicitor proposed that he live at home, comply with a nightly curfew
between 8pm and 8am, and abstain from drugs and alcohol. [@2] Judge Miranda
Moody granted bail, saying she did not think he would get a full-time custodial
sentence even if convicted. [@2] She told him he could not grow his own cannabis
and would need a prescription if he was relying on it. [@2] Under the bail,
police may check that he is home during curfew hours no more than once a day and
may test him for drugs and alcohol. [@2] He must also abstain from alcohol and
drugs unless a doctor prescribes them. [@2]

He was ordered to appear in Tamworth Local Court the following week. [@2] Police
were later ordered to compile a brief of evidence against him over the alleged
seizure of firearms, cannabis and methamphetamine in the Attunga raid. [@1]
