Military police of the 34th Interior Military Police Battalion arrested a
25-year-old man for drug trafficking in Amparo, São Paulo, on the afternoon of
Wednesday 4 November 2026. [@1] Officers on patrol on Avenida Dr. Carlos Burgos,
near a supermarket, stopped a white Citroën C3 with two men inside after
noticing suspicious behaviour. [@2] In the car officers found R$600 in cash,
eight units of pressed marijuana, a portion of cannabis flower and four packets
of cocaine of the escama type. [@1]

Questioned by the officers, the driver said the drugs were his and that there
was more at his home. [@1] With his authorisation officers searched the house
and an adjoining plot of land, finding more drugs, money and materials used to
prepare and package drugs. [@1] In total police seized R$11,782 in cash, 1.938
kg of pressed marijuana, 1.298 kg of cannabis flower described as Colombian,
368.82 g of dry and more than 1,600 packaging units. [@1]

The man was taken to the police station in Serra Negra, where he remained in
custody at the disposal of the courts. [@1]
