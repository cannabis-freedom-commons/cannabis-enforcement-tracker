Two people were taken to the 134th Police Station in Campos on Tuesday the 3rd
after a police action seized drugs, money and a mobile phone. [@1] One of the
two is a minor. [@1] Police on patrol in an area known for drug trafficking saw
the two sitting at a bus stop, and one fled as the patrol cars approached while
the other stayed and was approached. [@1]

With the first person approached, officers found one cannabis wrap, R$5 in cash
and a Motorola phone. [@1] Officers found another wrap of the same drug on the
bench where he had been sitting and R$30 inside the phone case. [@1] The person
who fled was identified as an adolescent and was found on Rua Olegário Gomes
trying to hide behind a parked vehicle. [@1] Nearby, police found a plastic pot
containing six cannabis wraps and eight cocaine pins, bringing the reported
cannabis total to eight wraps. [@1] The action recovered eight cocaine pins and
eight cannabis wraps in total. [@1]

Both of those approached admitted ownership of the respective drugs in their
possession. [@1] The case was presented at the 134th Police Station, and the
adolescent's legal guardian was called and attended the station to accompany
him. [@1]
