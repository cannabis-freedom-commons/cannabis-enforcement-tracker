On 9 November 2026, prosecutors of the DIICOT Mehedinti Territorial Bureau
ordered the detention of two defendants aged 20 and 40 investigated for
risk-drug trafficking. [@1] One of the defendants was additionally accused of
high-risk drug trafficking. [@1]

Investigators said the 20-year-old had, since May 2026, procured, possessed,
given away for free and sold cocaine in Drobeta-Turnu Severin. [@1] The same
20-year-old was found near his home possessing an unquantified quantity of
cannabis. [@1] The 40-year-old defendant was found in Drobeta-Turnu Severin
while possessing and transporting about 100 grams of cannabis intended for sale.
[@1] Prosecutors said the risk and high-risk drugs held and traded were seized
and searches across Mehedinti County recovered evidentiary items. [@1]

The judge for rights and freedoms at the Mehedinti Tribunal was asked to order
30 days of preventive arrest for both defendants. [@1] The judicial actions were
carried out with officers of the Mehedinti Organized Crime Combat Service. [@1]
The operation was supported by Craiova organized-crime police, Mehedinti County
police and Mehedinti gendarmes. [@1] The suspects retain procedural rights and
the presumption of innocence. [@1]
