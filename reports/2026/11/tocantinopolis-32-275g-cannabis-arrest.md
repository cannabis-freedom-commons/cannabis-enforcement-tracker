Military Police in Tocantinópolis, in the Bico do Papagaio region of Tocantins,
arrested a 32-year-old man suspected of drug trafficking on the afternoon of
Wednesday 4 November 2026. [@1]

A team from the 15th Military Police Battalion spotted him riding a motorcycle
during a patrol, followed it and stopped him. [@2] In a personal search,
officers found a portion of a marijuana-like substance, money and a mobile phone
in the pocket of his shorts. [@1]

The man said he was a drug user, but police said earlier information indicated
his home could be used as a drug sales point. [@1] He let the officers into the
house, where searches turned up about 275 grams of a marijuana-like substance,
106 portions of a crack-like substance and four portions of a cocaine-like
substance. [@1] Folha do Bico reported that the rest of the marijuana was found
during the approach itself, alongside the 106 crack portions and four cocaine
portions. [@2]

Police arrested him and took him with the drugs to the Civil Police station,
where a flagrant-arrest report was drawn up. [@1] The 15th Battalion said it
keeps up visible policing and drug-trafficking enforcement in Tocantinópolis and
the region's other municipalities. [@1]
