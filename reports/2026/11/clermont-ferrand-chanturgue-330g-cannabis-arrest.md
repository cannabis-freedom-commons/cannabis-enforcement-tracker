A 31-year-old man was arrested in Clermont-Ferrand on Friday 6 November 2026,
shortly before 10:50 p.m., near a drug-selling point on the rue des Hauts de
Chanturgue. [@1] Police said he was carrying more than 330 grams of cannabis, 48
grams of cocaine and 32 grams of heroin. [@1] He also had 1,200 euros in cash on
him. [@1]

The man was due before the Clermont-Ferrand prosecutor's office on Monday 9
November for an immediate appearance that afternoon on charges of drug
possession and unjustified resources. [@1]
