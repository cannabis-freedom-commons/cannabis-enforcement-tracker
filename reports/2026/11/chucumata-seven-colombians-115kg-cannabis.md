Detectives of the PDI's Iquique anti-narcotics brigade, working with Chile's
national customs service and the Tarapacá regional prosecutor's office, arrested
seven Colombian nationals, six men and one woman, near the Chucumata toll
station outside Iquique. [@1] The seven were stopped in two vehicles on their
way out of the region towards the Metropolitan Region. [@1]

The prosecutor's office said the two vehicles carried 115 kg 180 g of cannabis,
34 kg 440 g of coca base and more than 14.5 kg of cocaine hydrochloride, 164 kg
160 g of drugs in all. [@3] Officers also seized the two vehicles used for the
journey and seven mobile phones. [@2] One of the detainees already had an arrest
warrant for drug trafficking issued by the Antofagasta guarantee court, and
three others had records for other offences. [@2]

The investigation grew out of information left over from earlier cases, Tarapacá
regional prosecutor Virginia Aravena said, and she considered it very likely the
drug came from foreign producers. [@1] Aravena said the group had travelled to
Alto Hospicio, where PDI officers watched them before they were found with the
drugs as they tried to take them out of the region. [@1]

The seven were charged at a hearing on Friday 6 November 2026 with illicit drug
trafficking; the adults were remanded in preventive custody and a minor was held
in provisional internment, with a 120-day investigation period. [@1]

The customs service's acting regional director, Narciso Donoso, put the number
of people detained at eight. [@1]

Radio Paulina reported that the seven were arrested in flagrante for drug
trafficking and that officers seized seven mobile phones as well as the two
vehicles. [@4]
