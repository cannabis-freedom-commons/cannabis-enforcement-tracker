UMOPAR officers from Puerto Quijarro seized marijuana and detained two people in
a border operation carried out under Bolivia's Frontera Segura 2.0 plan. [@1]

Officers set up static checkpoints on the Motacucito–Puerto Suárez road and
identified a person transporting a bag of marijuana and six hand-rolled
cigarettes made from the same substance. [@1] Acting in coordination with the
Public Ministry, officers then searched two premises in the San Francisco and El
Carmen neighbourhoods, where they found 146 packages of marijuana. [@1]

Two people were detained, and a motorcycle, a vehicle, a property, a .22 calibre
firearm and two mobile phones were seized. [@1] FELCN said it would continue to
reinforce interdiction operations in the country's border zones as part of its
effort to combat trafficking of controlled substances. [@1]
