Police at Katghar police station in Moradabad district, Uttar Pradesh, arrested
three men on Sunday on charges of smuggling charas. [@1] Police seized 869 grams
of charas from the three men. [@1]

The same lead paragraph, giving the arrest of three accused and the 869-gram
seizure, was carried by the Hindusthan Samachar news agency, which the Vocal TV
page credits as its source. [@2] City superintendent of police Kumar Ranvijay
Singh said the three arrested were residents of the Katghar station area, from
Shivpuri in Ambedkar Nagar, Pirgaib in Mugalpura and Sanjay Nagar lane three at
Double Phatak. [@1] A case report against all three was being registered under
the NDPS Act, India's Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, the 1985
law that criminalises possessing and trading cannabis. [@1]

The three accused were to be produced before a court that evening, and police
said legal proceedings against them were under way. [@1]
