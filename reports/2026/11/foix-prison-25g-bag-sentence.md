Ingrid, a 31-year-old mother living in Lavelanet in Ariège, appeared before the
criminal court in Foix over conduct on 17 June 2026, with no previous
convictions. [@1] The court sentenced her to 105 hours of community service and
banned her from appearing at the Foix jail for one year. [@1] The sentence
spared her the suspended prison term requested by the prosecution. [@1]

On 17 June 2026 she tried to send 25 grams of cannabis to a detainee at the Foix
prison by preparing pieces of the drug, hiding them carefully in clothing and
packing them into a bag that she was not to carry to the jail herself. [@1] In
exchange she was to keep 4 grams for herself, telling the court she had acted
without thinking. [@1]

She said she had agreed to do it as a favour for a female friend to help get the
bag to the detainee. [@1] She had come to Ariège to rebuild her life but
described herself as isolated, with her children in care and her partner in
prison. [@1]
