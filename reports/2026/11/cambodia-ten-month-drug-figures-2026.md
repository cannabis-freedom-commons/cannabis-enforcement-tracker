Cambodia's Anti-Drug Department reported on 1 November 2026 that authorities
detained 25,360 drug suspects between January and October 2026. [@1] The number
of people detained was 16.3 per cent higher than the 21,800 recorded in the same
period of 2025. [@1] Foreign nationals made up 1,248 of the detainees, from 25
countries. [@1] The department classified 48.3 per cent of the suspects as
traffickers, manufacturers, possessors or transporters, and 51.7 per cent as
users. [@1]

Authorities confiscated 4.26 tonnes of illicit drugs and 537 kilograms of dry
cannabis between 1 January and 31 October 2026. [@1] That was more cannabis than
the 516 kilograms of dry marijuana seized in the same months of 2025, though
total narcotics seizures fell from 5.48 tonnes. [@1] Seized evidence also
included 73 pistols and 27 homemade rifles. [@1] The confiscated narcotics were
largely ketamine, ecstasy, heroin, crystal methamphetamine, methamphetamine
pills, cocaine and cathinone, the department said; the cannabis was listed
separately. [@1] Khmer Times reported that the Anti-Drug Department counted
1,248 foreign nationals among the suspects, from 25 nationalities. [@2]

Cambodia does not impose the death penalty for drug offences; trafficking more
than 80 grammes of illicit drugs can carry life imprisonment. [@1]
