Special NDPS Judge Arvind Mishra in Deoria granted bail to Vishal Kumar, a
resident of Saran district in Bihar, in a case over ganja recovered at the
railway station. [@1] The court treated the absence of any independent witness
during the recovery and the fact that the quantity was below the commercial
limit as significant to bail. [@1]

The prosecution said Government Railway Police officers found Kumar and
co-accused Anil Ram sitting together at the eastern end of platforms two and
three at Deoria railway station in Uttar Pradesh on 15 October. [@1] Officers
searched the two men and recovered five kilograms and 642 grams of ganja, or
5.642 kilograms, from their possession. [@1]

Kumar's lawyer argued that police had falsely implicated him and had not
followed the prescribed procedure for searching a suspect. [@1] The defence also
said there was no independent witness to the recovery, while the prosecution
opposed any bail. [@1] After hearing both sides, the court granted bail to Kumar
on 5 November. [@1] It ordered his release on a personal bond of 100,000 rupees,
with two sureties for the same amount. [@1]
