Military police arrested a man for drug trafficking on the night of Thursday 5
November 2026 in the São Cristóvão neighbourhood of Três Barras, in Santa
Catarina's Planalto Norte. [@2] Officers kept the property under watch during
the day after reports that drugs were being sold there, and stopped people
leaving it with portions of crack. [@3]

With the selling confirmed, the teams went into the house and found portions of
crack, cocaine and marijuana prepared for sale. [@2] Police also seized
small-denomination banknotes, packing and portioning material, and seven mobile
phones whose origin will be investigated. [@1]

The man identified as responsible for the house and the drug-selling operation
was arrested in flagrante and taken with the seized material to the Civil Police
station, where the arrest and procedures were formalised. [@2] The account came
from the Santa Catarina Military Police (PMSC), whose statement the local
outlets published. [@1]
