A first corporal of Carabineros was struck by a taxi colectivo while directing
traffic for an approaching emergency vehicle in central Osorno on the night of
Saturday 7 November. [@1] The collision happened at the junction of Manuel
Antonio Matta street and Juan Mackenna avenue. [@1] The officer, from Osorno's
First Police Station, suffered a serious fracture of his right wrist and
remained off duty to recover. [@1] Captain Juan Castro of the First Police
Station said the officer wore a reflective vest and that cones were in place to
clear the way for the emergency vehicle. [@1]

The colectivo driver did not stop in time and hit the officer, causing the
fracture, the captain said. [@2] Bío Bío reported that witnesses were present
and that SIAT traffic investigators advised on the inquiry, which established
that the driver was drugged. [@3] During the procedure the driver was given drug
tests, which came back positive for cocaine and marijuana. [@1] Radio Sago
reported that the driver was then detained by Carabineros. [@2]

Captain Castro said the duty prosecutor, the Ministerio Público official on
call, ordered that the driver be brought before a judge for a detention review.
[@1] The driver remained detained at the time of reporting, with his appearance
before the competent court to decide his legal situation still pending. [@2] T13
quoted Castro saying the respiratory test and narcotest found the driver under
the influence of alcohol and positive for marijuana and cocaine. [@4] T13
reported the officer was directing traffic outside a bank as part of Teletón,
Chile's annual charity fund-raising broadcast. [@4]

The Los Lagos regional security ministry condemned the incident and said it
expected the maximum civil and criminal penalties against the driver. [@4]
