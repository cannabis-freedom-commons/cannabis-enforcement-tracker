Police in Bihar's Kosi division arrested 2,717 suspected traffickers during an
ongoing anti-narcotics campaign running across Saharsa, Supaul and Madhepura
districts since February 2026. [@1] During the campaign, police recovered
8,254.794 kilograms of ganja across the three districts alongside illicit
liquor, cough syrup, smack and injectable narcotics. [@1] Police figures
indicated that following the state's alcohol prohibition, the regional illicit
drug trade shifted increasingly toward ganja, smack, codeine syrup and narcotic
injections. [@1]

In the week from 31 October to 6 November 2026, police operations in Saharsa,
Supaul and Madhepura seized 52.104 kilograms of ganja, 1,788.35 litres of
illicit liquor, 173.69 grams of smack and 25.9 litres of codeine syrup. [@1]
Officers arrested 71 people in illicit liquor cases and 13 people on
dry-narcotics offences during that single week. [@1] Across the entire campaign
since February, police also confiscated four pistols, ten country-made firearms,
47 cartridges, 110 mobile phones, 43 four-wheeled vehicles, five heavy vehicles
and boats, and cash in Indian and Nepalese currency. [@1]

Deputy Inspector General of Police for the Kosi range Dr Kumar Ashish
established a public helpline to receive confidential tips on narcotics dealers,
trade locations, photos and videos. [@1] Residents in Saharsa reported that
dealers at the Shankar Chowk market were openly selling intoxicants using
digital payment scanners to avoid cash handling. [@1] Local residents asked
police to conduct intensive raids and inspections around Shankar Chowk, where
authorities had previously seized ganja and cough syrup and arrested
traffickers. [@1]
