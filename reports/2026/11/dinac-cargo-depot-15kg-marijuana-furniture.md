On 13 November 2026 Paraguay's Dirección Nacional de Ingresos Tributarios (DNIT)
detected two suspicious parcels during controls of air express parcels. [@1] The
parcels were found during checks of express-remittance cargo in the cargo depot
of the Dirección Nacional de Aeronáutica Civil (DINAC). [@1] The checks were
supported by the Secretaría Nacional Antidrogas (Senad), Paraguay's anti-drug
agency, and the Dirección General de Material Bélico (Digemabel). [@1]

One parcel from abroad consisted of a wooden shoe-cabinet-style piece of
furniture with a padded top. [@1] Inside a false bottom in that furniture
officers found approximately 15 kilograms of suspected marijuana. [@1]

In a separate parcel from Miami, United States, officers found firearm
components lacking the legal permits required in Paraguay. [@1] Those items were
telescopic sights and a press with accessories for reloading ammunition. [@1]
That weapons-accessories find was referred to Digemabel for administrative and
legal proceedings. [@1] The DNIT's own statement confirms it detected the
suspected-marijuana load during express-cargo verification in the DINAC cargo
depot area. [@2]
