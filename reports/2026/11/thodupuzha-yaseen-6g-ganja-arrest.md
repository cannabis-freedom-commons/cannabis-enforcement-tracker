At Thodupuzha, a youth was caught with dried cannabis. [@1] The detained youth
was identified as 19-year-old Muhammed Yaseen, also known as Karakkunnel Pappan,
of the Karikode Kumbamkallu Matte Colony kara. [@1] Thodupuzha Range Excise
Inspector Lijo Umman and his party caught the accused with six grams of cannabis
during a Monday inspection in the Kumbamkallu Matte Colony area. [@1]

Assistant Excise Inspector Grade Joby P. Chacko, O.H. Mansoor, K.P. Jayaraj,
Civil Excise Officers Asif Ali and M.V. Denny, and Woman Preventive Officer K.
Sindhu took part in the raid. [@1]
