Military police arrested two men and a woman in Palmas, Tocantins, on the
morning of Wednesday 4 November 2026 on suspicion of drug trafficking. [@1] The
three were in a white Fiat Punto stopped in Setor Santa Fé, in the south of the
city, shortly after 8:30am. [@1] Tocantins em Voz places the stop in Taquaralto,
in front of a department store, at about 8:30am. [@2]

The operation began after Goiás Military Police teams shared information with
the intelligence unit of the Tocantins Military Police's 6th Battalion that a
bus was carrying drugs. [@1] Tocantins em Voz names the Goiás team as the 3rd
Battalion of the 12th Regional Military Police Company and says the bus was
carrying a substance similar to cannabis. [@2] Officers found the bus, watched a
man take packages from it and get into the white car, then followed and
intercepted the car. [@1] A search of the car found two pieces of a substance
resembling cannabis weighing 1.738 kg together. [@1] Tocantins em Voz reports
the same weight, 1,738 kg or 1,738 grams, in two pieces. [@2] Questioned about
where the drug came from and where it was going, the three said it was for their
own use. [@1]

Despite that claim, all three were booked in the act for drug trafficking under
article 33 of Brazil's Drug Law, Law 11.343/2006. [@1] Tocantins em Voz reports
that the military police said all three were formally recorded in the act for
trafficking. [@2] Officers also seized three mobile phones and the white Fiat
Punto the three were using, and sent the material for forensic examination. [@1]
The three were taken with the seized drug, phones and car to the 2nd Civil
Police Service Centre in Palmas, which took over the case. [@2]
