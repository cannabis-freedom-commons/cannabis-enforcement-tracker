Excise State Task Force officers arrested a man named Aluri Deepak, aged 25, in
Balaji Nagar, Kukatpally, for selling ganja from a motorbike. [@1] The State
Task Force, shortened to STF, is a special unit of the Telangana Excise
Department, and the arresting team was led by inspector Venkateswarlu and
sub-inspector Manju. [@1]

Officers seized 530 grams of ganja and a two-wheeler from the man. [@1]
Inspector Venkateswarlu said the seized items were handed over to Balanagar
Excise Station. [@1]

The article names no charge or court date, and the excise station continued the
investigation after the handover. [@1]
