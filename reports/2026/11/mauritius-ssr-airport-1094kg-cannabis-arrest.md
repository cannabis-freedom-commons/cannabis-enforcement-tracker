Officers of the Anti-Drug and Smuggling Unit, working with customs officers,
arrested a 30-year-old man arriving at Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam International
Airport on a flight from Dubai. [@1] The interception took place at the airport
on Monday 2 November at about 14:35. [@1] The suspect was detained as part of an
investigation into drug importation with aggravating circumstances and a
presumption of trafficking. [@1]

Authorities estimated the market value of the 10.94 kilos of intercepted
cannabis at Rs 16.41 million. [@1] During the intervention the authorities found
ten heat-sealed black plastic sachets containing cannabis. [@1] Investigators
also seized a Malaysian passport, three boarding passes and a baggage tag from
the case. [@1] Among several foreign currencies seized were 355 pounds sterling
and 250 United States dollars. [@1]

Following the arrest, investigators launched a controlled-delivery operation on
the same day. [@1] The operation was then interrupted on Tuesday 3 November.
[@1] The police report gave no reasons for stopping the controlled delivery.
