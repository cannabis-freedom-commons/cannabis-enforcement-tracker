A 16-year-old was found with 1.7 grams of cannabis during a police check in
Silistra, as reported by the regional directorate of the Ministry of the
Interior for Silistra. [@1] The check was carried out on 11 November 2026 in the
provincial town. [@1]

During it, police officers found that the minor was holding a high-risk narcotic
substance – cannabis – weighing 1.7 grams. [@1]

The narcotic was seized, and the 16-year-old was detained for a period of up to
24 hours. [@1,2]

A pre-trial procedure was opened for the unlawful keeping of a high-risk
narcotic at the regional directorate of the Ministry of the Interior for
Silistra. [@1] An investigating officer is working the case. [@2]
