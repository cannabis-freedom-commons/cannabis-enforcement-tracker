Department of Narcotics Control officers arrested Md Saju Miah, 54, at his home
in Nauhati Chachia village, Sundarganj, Gaibandha, on the night of 4 November
2026. [@1] Officers seized 4.2 kg of ganja, the local word for cannabis. [@1]
The Department of Narcotics Control put the street value of the ganja at 1 lakh
26 thousand taka. [@1]

A team from the department's Rangpur district office acted on a tip-off and
searched the bedroom of Saju Miah's own house. [@1] The drug was found hidden
behind a sofa in a bedroom of his five-room semi-pucca house. [@1] The ganja was
packed in a plastic sack as four one-kilogram bundles and one bundle of 200
grams. [@1]

Sub-Inspector Md Tahmudar Rahman of the department's Rangpur office filed the
case and handed the man and the seized ganja to Sundarganj police. [@2]
