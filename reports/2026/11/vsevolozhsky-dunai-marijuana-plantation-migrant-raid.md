Police, the Federal Security Service's regional directorate, the Main
Directorate's special regiment, dog handlers and Rosgvardia troops raided the
Dunai and Ogonyok gardening settlements to check foreigners breaking the rules
on stay in Russia. [@1] The operation took place on the territory next to the
Morozov powder works in Vsevolozhsky District, Leningrad Oblast, the regional
police press service said. [@1]

Officers checked two car services, 894 houses and 197 people, among them 18
foreign citizens. [@1] Four people were found in breach of migration law, and
their expulsion from Russia was being considered. [@2]

In one of the private houses police found marijuana being grown. [@1] An
administrative protocol was drawn up against the man who grew it, and an inquiry
is under way. [@1] The protocol was drawn up under article 6.8 of the
Administrative Code, which covers unlawful trafficking in drugs, and the papers
were registered at a police station. [@2]

Road police officers joined the raid and drew up five administrative protocols
against drivers. [@1]
