On Sunday 1 November 2026 at about 6:30 pm, Chhagalnaiya thana police and Ghopal
Investigation Centre officers conducted a joint operation in Uttar Adharmanik
village of Radhanagar union in Chhagalnaiya, Feni. [@1,2] The target was the area
behind the tin-shed house of expatriate Belal, next to the Dakshinpara Jame
mosque. [@1] Police said they acted on secret information. [@1,2]

At the spot, officers found cannabis left abandoned in two sacks. [@1,2] Each sack
contained six packets, making 12 packets in total. [@1] Police weighed each
packet at 2 kg, putting the total seizure at 24 kg of ganja. [@1] In the
presence of mosque worshippers and nearby villagers, officers prepared a seizure
list, seized the cannabis and took it into police custody. [@1]

No one was arrested at the scene because the sacks were found abandoned. [@1]
Police said work was under way to identify those involved in storing, supplying
and transporting the drugs and to arrest them through legal action. [@1]
Chhagalnaiya officer-in-charge Mohammad Abu Taher said anti-drug operations
would continue and those involved in storing, selling and trafficking would be
brought under the law, seeking local cooperation. [@1]
