Madrid municipal police dismantled an alleged drug sales point that operated
behind the appearance of a cannabis association, known locally as a 'soci', in
the Chamberí district. [@1,2,3] One person was arrested as the alleged perpetrator
of an offence against public health, while two other people are under
investigation for possible links to the case. [@1,2,3,4] Officers entered and searched
the premises after gathering evidence and obtaining the required judicial
authorisation. [@1,3]

During the search they found different quantities of drugs and tools allegedly
used to prepare, store and distribute them. [@1,2,4] Police seized 2.7 kilos of
marijuana and hashish, 174 cigarettes already prepared for consumption and 505
euros in cash. [@1,3,4] They also seized more than 1,300 items linked to drug use,
possession and transport, along with two precision scales. [@1,3]

Police said the investigation showed the venue went beyond the purposes of a
cannabis association and had allegedly become a drug distribution point using
its associative appearance as cover. [@1,3,4] Police consider the alleged Chamberí
sales point deactivated, saying investigators believe the seized drugs were
intended for distribution and consumption. [@1]

The seized utensils included pipes, bongs and ashtrays. [@4]
