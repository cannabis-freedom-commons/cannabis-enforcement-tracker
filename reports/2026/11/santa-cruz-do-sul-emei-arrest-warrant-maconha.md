Guarda Municipal officers in Santa Cruz do Sul arrested a 24-year-old man on the
morning of Thursday, 5 November 2026. [@1] The 2ª Vara Criminal court in Santa
Cruz do Sul had issued an open arrest warrant for him over drug trafficking and
illegal gun possession. [@2]

The city's video surveillance system identified him at the Raio de Sol municipal
early-childhood school (Emei), an Emei being a municipal pre-school centre, on
Rua Lindolfo Grawunder in the Belvedere neighbourhood. [@1] The information was
passed to a Guarda Municipal patrol, which went to the scene and stopped him
near the school, confirming the warrant. [@2]

A search found a portion of marijuana packed inside a cigarette packet. [@1]
Officers took him to the Central UPA for a medical check and then presented him
at the DPPA, the ready-response police station, before he was taken to the Santa
Cruz do Sul regional prison. [@1]

The Guarda Municipal said the case showed the value of combining video
surveillance with patrol work to identify people with court orders outstanding.
[@2]
