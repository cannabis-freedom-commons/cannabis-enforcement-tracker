Jaithari police station officers arrested two people on Saturday evening, 7
November 2026, on suspicion of ganja trafficking, the Hindi term for cannabis.
[@1] Officers seized 5.078 kg of ganja, a motorcycle and material used in the
trade from the two men. [@1] Police put the value of the seized ganja at about
60,000 rupees. [@1] The second man was arrested after the first told police he
had bought the ganja from him in Shahdol district. [@1]
