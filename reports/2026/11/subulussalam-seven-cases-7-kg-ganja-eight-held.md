Subulussalam police presented seven drug cases and eight suspects at a press
conference at the police headquarters on Friday 6 November 2026. [@3]
Subulussalam police chief AKBP Muhammad Yusuf and drug unit head IPTU Hermansyah
presented the haul at the press conference. [@3] The drug unit seized 7,271.63
grams of ganja, about 7.27 kg, alongside 11.84 grams of methamphetamine. [@1]
The officers displayed 11.84 grams of sabu at the conference. [@1] The raids
targeted several locations in Subulussalam city suspected of drug trafficking.
[@1]

The seven cases came from October 2026 case work, police said. [@3] The cases
were still under investigation and were to be sent to prosecutors. [@3] Police
said they were still working to identify other members of the network in
Subulussalam. [@2]

The city police presented the results of an operation nicknamed Membongkar
Sarang Narkoba. [@1] The police chief said there would be no room for dealers or
users in the Subulussalam police jurisdiction. [@1] The seized items were held
as part of the further investigation into the network behind them. [@1]
