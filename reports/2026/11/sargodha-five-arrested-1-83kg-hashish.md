Sargodha police arrested five suspected drug peddlers during raids on Friday 6
November 2026. [@1] The five, named by police as Sabir, Bakhtawar, Shoaib, Nawaz
and Qasim, were held as part of a continuing drive against drug pushers. [@1]

Teams from different police stations carried out the raids at various places in
their jurisdictions in Sargodha district. [@1] Officers recovered 1.830
kilograms of hashish, the cannabis resin, from the men. [@1] The same recovery
included 120 litres of liquor, which is not counted in the cannabis figures.
[@1]

Separate cases were registered over the arrests. [@1]
