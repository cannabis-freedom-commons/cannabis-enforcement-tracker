Two men aged 19 and 33 were detained by the Guarda Civil Municipal de Vitória on
suspicion of drug trafficking on the morning of Thursday 5 November 2026 in Vila
Rubim, Vitória. [@1] During patrol in the area, officers spotted two individuals
trying to hide objects. [@1] One of them threw a bag into a rubbish bin and both
grew more nervous as the officers approached. [@3] The pair tried to run but
were contained after backup teams arrived. [@1] Both men and all the seized
material were taken to the Delegacia Regional de Vitória. [@1] There they were
presented to the duty police authority and booked for the crime of drug
trafficking. [@1]

Guards found an infant shoe inside a plastic rubbish bag near a post, holding
cocaine and crack pins. [@1] With the 33-year-old suspect, officers found four
marijuana portions, R$22 in cash and one hashish portion. [@1] A personal search
found four marijuana portions with the 19-year-old and some crack pins. [@1] In
total, officers seized 13 cocaine pins, 12 crack pins, eight marijuana portions
and one hashish portion. [@1]

A second outlet gives the same totals and says the infant shoe held cocaine and
crack pins while the 33-year-old held four marijuana portions, one hashish
portion and R$22. [@2] Guard De Souza said the fractioned quantity indicated the
material was being sold at the location. [@2] A third account says officers
found four marijuana portions and some crack pins with the 19-year-old during
the approach. [@3]
