Córdoba's Fuerza Policial Antinarcotráfico, the provincial anti-drug police,
detained two men in Barrio Los Eucaliptos in the city of Córdoba during an
operation against narcomenudeo, small-scale drug dealing. [@1] The force raided
two homes and dismantled an alleged drug sales point plus a second property
allegedly used to store drugs. [@2] The procedures were carried out within an
investigation into the sale of narcotics. [@1]

During the searches officers found several doses of marijuana and seized other
items deemed of interest to the case. [@1] Investigators said the two suspects
allegedly belonged to an organisation selling drugs as a kiosco, a fixed sales
point. [@1] Investigators also said the two men allegedly acted as partners in
the suspected illegal activity. [@1]

After the raids the Fiscalía de Lucha contra el Narcotráfico del Primer Turno,
the First-Turn anti-narcotics prosecutor, ordered both men transferred to
judicial headquarters. [@1] It also ordered the seized substances and other
items sent on to be incorporated into the investigation. [@1] The case continues
as an alleged breach of National Drug Law No. 23.737. [@1]
