Civil police officers from Sergipe's Department of Narcotics Investigations
(Denarc) arrested a man on Saturday 7 November in Conjunto Santo Antônio, in the
inland municipality of Propriá. [@1] During the operation officers seized about
250 grams of marijuana and cocaine. [@1]

The delegate of Propriá, Albene Júnior, confirmed the arrest. [@1]
