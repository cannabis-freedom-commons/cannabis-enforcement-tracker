The “Falchi” unit of Cagliari’s Squadra Mobile arrested two young people from
Monserrato in an anti-drug operation. [@1,2] Squadra Mobile investigators
identified the two through observation services and reconstructed a possible
circuit for holding and distributing drugs. [@1] After the operation, the two
were taken to the Ettore Scalas prison in Uta at the disposal of the judicial
authority. [@1] After the validation hearing, the judicial authority placed the
older under house arrest and ordered the younger to comply with a signing
obligation. [@1,2]

A house search found and seized 7.1 kg of hashish divided into numerous loaves.
[@1,2] Officers also seized about 300 grams of marijuana, described as a
significant quantity potentially intended to supply the local drug market. [@1,2]

Among the seized material was a kit in packaging marked “La famille Frero
Sardegna” containing a hashish loaf and a small knife bearing the same
engraving. [@1,2] Investigators are examining whether the drugs were intended not
only for street dealing but also for transfer to others for onward distribution
through a common recognisable brand, described as a sort of “franchising dello
spaccio”. [@1] That remains an unverified investigative hypothesis that does not
establish the existence of a structured distribution network, though it makes
the branded kit particularly significant. [@1]
