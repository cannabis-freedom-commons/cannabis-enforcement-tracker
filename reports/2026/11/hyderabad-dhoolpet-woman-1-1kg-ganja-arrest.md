Prohibition and Excise officials in Hyderabad arrested a 36-year-old woman
during a raid in Dhoolpet. [@1] The raid took place in the Gangabowli area of
the locality. [@1] The arrested woman was identified as Mounika. [@1] Mounika
was allegedly involved in selling ganja in the locality. [@1] Ganja is the local
name for cannabis. [@1] During the operation, the excise team detected the
alleged illegal activity in the area. [@1] The team seized 1.1 kg of ganja from
her possession. [@1]

Officials registered cases against two other women, Jayabai and Shivani Singh,
in connection with the ganja sale. [@1] The cases against the two women concern
the same ganja sale. [@1] The two women were not arrested and both are
absconding. [@1] Efforts are under way to trace and apprehend them. [@1]

Excise Circle Inspectors Praveen Kumar, Viplav Kumar Reddy and Venkanna
confirmed the arrest and seizure. [@1] They said further action was being taken
in connection with the case. [@1] The officials said action would continue
against those involved in the illegal sale of narcotic substances. [@1]
