Nine people were arrested for possessing illegal drugs in a Central Police News
Room briefing on operations in seven districts, reported on 9 November 2026.
[@1] The police statement lists one arrest in Kathmandu Metropolitan City-16,
two in Morang's Belbari Municipality, one at Balan Khola in Bhagwanpur Rural
Municipality-4 in Siraha, one in Shuklaphanta Municipality-12 in Kanchanpur, one
in Gulariya Municipality-11 in Bardiya, two in Birtamod Municipality-5 in Jhapa
and one in Waling Municipality-8 in Syangja. [@2]

The largest cannabis seizure was in Siraha, where Lahan area police arrested
55-year-old Devsharan Yadav with about 80 kg 51 g of material suspected to be
ganja, the Nepali word for marijuana. [@2] In Jhapa, two men were arrested at
Suryadal Tol in Birtamod Municipality-5 with 5 g 40 mg of brown heroin. [@2] The
remaining six arrests involved brown heroin or prescription medicines: 19 g 80
mg of heroin in Shuklaphanta Municipality-12 in Kanchanpur, 1 g 220 mg of brown
sugar in Gulariya Municipality-11 in Bardiya, and heroin or tramadol tablets in
Kathmandu and Belbari. [@2] In Syangja, police from the Waling ward office
stopped a bus travelling from Butwal to Pokhara at Jaisipadhera in Waling
Municipality-8 and arrested a 22-year-old man with 67 ampoules each of
buprenorphine and phenergan and 86 ampoules of diazepam. [@2] Nepal News reports
that marijuana and brown heroin were seized along with prescription-only
tramadol, spaspen, buprenorphine, diazepam and phenargan. [@1]

Nepal Police said the necessary investigation was under way in the cases, with
no charges or court outcome reported. [@2]
