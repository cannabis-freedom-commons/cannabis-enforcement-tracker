Police in Vellore district, Tamil Nadu, seized 6.5 kg of unclaimed ganja lying
at Gudiyatham railway station on 4 November 2026. [@1] The same operation
detained Mujahit, who was found keeping 500 g of ganja at a house. [@1]

Police said they were questioning Mujahit and investigating the case. [@1]

The two seizures together amounted to 7 kg of ganja, ganja being the Tamil and
wider Indian term for herbal cannabis. [@1]

The two seizures were reported in a roundup of 20 events that Tamil-language
outlet ABP Nadu said took place on 4 November across Tamil Nadu's districts.
