Police arrested two women accused of ganja smuggling in the Warisaliganj police
station area of Nawada district. [@1] Police seized 1 kg 750 g of ganja from the
two women. [@1]

The Warisaliganj police team carried out the raid on the superintendent of
police's instructions. [@1] The women tried to flee on seeing the police but
were caught with the help of the armed force. [@1] The arrested women were
identified as Ganga Devi, wife of Suresh Manjhi of Hetampur village under
Mufassil police station area, and Chandania Devi, wife of Vinod Choudhary of
Tarapur village under Kashichak police station area. [@1]

Police registered Warisaliganj police station case number 814/26 against both
women under sections 8/20(A)/25 of the NDPS Act. [@1] The raiding party included
Warisaliganj police officers Pankaj Kumar Saini, Kanhaiya Singh, Dharmendra
Prasad and Dharmendra Kumar along with police force personnel. [@1] Police said
further action in the case had begun. [@1]
