Border Belt Drug Task Force investigators and Robeson County Sheriff's Office
SWAT operators executed a search warrant on Thursday 12 November 2026 at a home
on the 400 block of Stanton Road, southeast of Maxton, North Carolina. [@2]
Tommy Dale Barnes, 40, of Maxton, surrendered to investigators the following
afternoon. [@1]

Investigators seized cocaine, Suboxone strips, prescription pills, marijuana, a
firearm and drug paraphernalia during the search. [@1] The sheriff's office did
not specify the quantities of drugs seized. [@1]

Barnes was charged with possession with intent to sell and deliver marijuana and
with maintaining a vehicle or dwelling for controlled substances. [@1] The other
charges name trafficking in opium or heroin, offences involving cocaine and
Schedule II and Schedule III controlled substances, and possession of a firearm
by a convicted felon. [@2] The sheriff's office described Barnes as having prior
drug convictions. [@1]

Barnes is being held at the Robeson County Detention Center under a $1.1 million
secured bond. [@1] Sheriff Burnis Wilkins said Barnes apparently believed his
surrender to the task force was an opportunity to make light of a very serious
situation on social media. [@1] The sheriff's office said it does not set bond
amounts, which a judicial official decides under North Carolina law, and that
Barnes is presumed innocent unless proven guilty in court. [@2]
