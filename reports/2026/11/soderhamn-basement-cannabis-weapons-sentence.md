In Söderhamn, a man was convicted of narcotics offences and weapons offences
after police found an extensive cannabis cultivation, weapons and ammunition in
his residence. [@1] He explained that the cannabis was for his own medical use.
[@1]

Police carried out a house search at the now-convicted man’s home in September
2024. [@1] When officers entered the residence, they found an extensive indoor
cannabis cultivation. [@1] The cultivation was kept in the basement of his home.
[@1] In total, police seized almost 3.3 kilos of cannabis plants in the search.
[@1] Officers also seized a smaller amount of hashish alongside the cannabis
plants. [@1]

The weapons conviction covers weapons found in the same residence during the
same investigation. [@1] The weapons conviction also covers ammunition found in
the same residence during the same investigation. [@1] Police investigation
photos published with the case show the basement cannabis grow. [@1] The
published photo of the grow is credited to the police preliminary investigation
protocol. [@1]
