Ahmedabad Rural SOG arrested one man, Khodabhai Modjibhai Thakor, at his house
in Bavla. [@1] Acting on a tip-off, SOG searched the house with Bavla police at
Bordiwalu Jin behind the municipality. [@2] Officers found ganja packed in
plastic pouches under a pillow and inside a bedroom dressing-table drawer. [@2]
An FSL officer at the spot conducted a preliminary check and confirmed the
substance was ganja. [@2]

Police seized 3 kg 555 g of ganja along with a mobile phone, a weighing scale
and packing material. [@2] Police put the total value of all the seized property
at Rs 1,87,950. [@2] At Rs 50,000 per kg, police calculated the ganja alone at
about Rs 1.77 lakh. [@2]

During questioning, Khodabhai said he had ordered the ganja from Rambhai of
Mahemdavad over a WhatsApp call. [@2] He said he took delivery of the ganja
under the Kheda bridge. [@2] Police declared Rambhai wanted and registered a
case at Bavla police station under NDPS Act sections 8(c), 20(b)(2)(b) and 29
for further investigation. [@2]
