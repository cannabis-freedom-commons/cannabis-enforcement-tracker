Military Police arrested a man and a woman in Timbo on the afternoon of 10
November 2026 on suspicion of drug trafficking. [@1] The stop took place at
about 3:14 p.m. on Rua Aristiliano Ramos in Timbo during a Military Police
patrol. [@2] Officers stopped the occupants of a ride-hailing vehicle on Rua
Aristiliano Ramos after observing suspicious behaviour. [@1]

Police seized approximately 500 grams of a substance described as similar to
hashish. [@1] Officers found the drug divided into five wraps inside a bag
carried by the woman, totalling about half a kilo. [@2] Questioned by police,
one of the two admitted owning the drug, and both were placed under arrest. [@1]
The two were taken to the Civil Police station with the seized material for the
applicable legal procedures. [@1]

The Military Police recorded the case as drug trafficking. [@1]
