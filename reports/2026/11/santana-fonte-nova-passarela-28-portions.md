Officers of Patamo, the Amapá military police's independent motorcycle tactical
patrol company, detained a 15-year-old girl and a woman on suspicion of drug
trafficking on the afternoon of Wednesday, 4 November 2026. [@1] The officers
were patrolling Avenida das Nações near the entrance of the Passarela Dom Pedro,
a footbridge the report describes as known for heavy drug selling. [@1]

When the two women saw the patrol vehicle they ran into the footbridge, and the
officers followed and stopped the girl, who was carrying a black backpack. [@1]
A search of the backpack found six medium portions of cannabis and a credit
card, and the girl, identified as 15 years old, admitted the drug was hers. [@1]
The other woman had gone into a nearby property, where police found 22 small
drug portions kept in a green pot, packaging material and a precision scale.
[@1] The woman confirmed the material was hers and that the house belonged to
her. [@1]

The girl was apprehended for an infraction analogous to drug trafficking under
the Statute of the Child and Adolescent (ECA), Brazil's juvenile justice code.
[@1] The second suspect was also taken to the police authority for the
applicable procedures. [@1]
