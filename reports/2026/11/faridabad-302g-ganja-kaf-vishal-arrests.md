Faridabad Crime Branch teams arrested two accused at two different places over
drug-trafficking charges. [@1] Faridabad Crime Branch Sector-48 arrested Kaf
near the power house in Nehru Colony and recovered 8.05 g of smack from him in
the presence of a gazetted officer. [@1] Faridabad Crime Branch DLAF arrested
Vishal near Palla power house on Bypass Road and recovered 302 g of ganja from
his possession. [@1] Police registered NDPS Act FIRs against both accused at
Dabua and Sarai Khwaja police stations. [@1]
