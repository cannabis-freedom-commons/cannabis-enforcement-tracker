Rajkot Rural LCB caught Gadhada-based ganja supplier Sajid alias Bhikho near
Ashapura Chowkdi in Gondal. [@1] The detained man is 33-year-old Sajid alias
Bhikho Rahimbhai alias Bobalbhai Tarkwadiya, who lives at Khatkivas near Jinnaka
bridge in Gadhada. [@1] He had gone underground after being named as the
supplier in two separate NDPS Act cases registered at Gondal B Division and
Dhoraji Taluka police stations. [@2]

An informant told the LCB that the absconding accused was near the Mamadev
temple at Ashapura Chowkdi in Gondal. [@1] The LCB team rushed to Ashapura
Chowkdi, detained him and handed him to Gondal B Division police for further
legal action. [@1] DivyaBhaskar likewise reports that he was handed to Gondal B
Division police station after being caught near the Mamadev temple. [@2] The
operation was part of the drive against absconding accused under the guidance of
Rajkot district SP Vijaysinh Gurjar and Rural LCB PI J P Rao. [@1]

In July, Rajkot Rural SOG had separately raided quarters on Vorakotda Road in
Gondal and seized five and a half kilos of ganja valued at Rs 2.79 lakh,
arresting peddler Imtiaz alias Lala. [@1] Imtiaz allegedly told police during
questioning that Sajid alias Bhikho of Swamina Gadhada had delivered that ganja
stock to him. [@1]
