Gendarmes stopped a businessman driving his Porsche at a roadside check in
Igoville on Sunday 1 November 2026. [@1] The officers found that his driving
licence was no longer valid, its extension having expired at the end of
September 2025. [@1] A saliva test carried out at the roadside was also positive
for cannabis. [@1]

The judicial court in Évreux convicted the businessman and gave him nine months
in prison suspended with probation. [@1] He was tried at the judicial court in
Évreux in immediate-appearance proceedings, a fast-track hearing for cases where
the accused is brought before the court in person. [@1]
