Military police in Santa Catarina arrested a 27-year-old man from Pernambuco on
suspicion of drug trafficking during a patrol in the Tapera neighbourhood of
Florianópolis. [@1] Officers of the 4th Military Police Battalion, the 4º BPM,
recorded the incident on Rua Heleno Peixoto Costa after they saw suspicious
movement in a vacant lot. [@1] The force said the patrol spotted the man in a
situation that suggested he might be selling drugs. [@1] They stopped him and
found drugs and cash. [@1]

A personal search found portions of a substance similar to cocaine, already
divided and apparently prepared for sale. [@1] Police said he was wearing
several pieces of clothing, which made the drugs harder to locate. [@1] Officers
also seized 189 reais in cash. [@1] They took his mobile phone, which they said
might contain information for the trafficking investigation. [@1]

With support from the Military Police central dog unit, the Canil Central, a
sniffer dog found packages containing substances similar to marijuana and
hashish. [@1] Police said the find strengthened the suspicion that the vacant
lot was being used for drug selling. [@1] The Military Police did not give the
exact quantity of each substance seized. [@1]

The man was placed under arrest and taken to a police station without injuries
for the legal procedures. [@1] The force said the stop was part of overt
policing by the 4º BPM in the state capital. [@1]
