Military Police officers arrested a 30-year-old prisoner in flagrante for drug
trafficking in Sagres, a municipality in São Paulo state, on 9 November 2026.
[@1] The man was among prisoners working on public works in the municipality,
and officers acted on a tip that drugs were among their belongings. [@1] The
Military Police called in a city hall inspector to gather the prisoners in the
day shelter of the municipal swimming pool, asked the Civil Police for support
and requested the Military Police dog unit. [@1]

The sniffer dog inspected the prisoners' bags and signalled on the belongings of
the man later accused. [@1] In that search, officers found blocks of marijuana
and vials of cocaine. [@1] A body search then found two more wraps of marijuana
hidden in the man's sock, along with a mobile phone. [@1]

The prisoner, who had been working outside the prison under a service-work
arrangement, was taken to the Civil Police station in Osvaldo Cruz and held at
the disposal of the courts. [@1] The Civil Police and the state prison police
force also took part in the operation. [@1]
