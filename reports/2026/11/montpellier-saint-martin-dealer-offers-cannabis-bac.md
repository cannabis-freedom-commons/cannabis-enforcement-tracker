A 24-year-old man was arrested on Sunday 8 November 2026 after offering to sell
cannabis to an officer of the BAC, the anti-crime brigade, in Montpellier. [@1]
Police had spotted him in the Saint-Martin neighbourhood making suspicious
transactions with potential customers before the officer approached him
discreetly. [@1]

He struggled when the officer tried to arrest him, and three officers were
needed to detain him and place him in police custody. [@1] He bit one of the
officers, and a complaint was filed. [@1] Questioned in custody, he denied any
involvement in drug trafficking. [@1] Officers found 21 g of cannabis, a small
quantity of cocaine and some cash in a box near the place where he was arrested.
[@1] He is due to be tried in an immediate appearance hearing, the French
fast-track procedure for suspects held in custody, on Tuesday 10 November. [@1]
