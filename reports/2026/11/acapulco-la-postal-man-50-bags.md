Guerrero State Police officers detained a man during preventive security patrols
in the La Postal neighbourhood of the port city of Acapulco, Guerrero. [@1]
Personnel of the Citizen Reaction and Attention Unit, supporting the Acapulco
Operational Region, carried out the intervention on Calle 15. [@1] The detained
man was identified as Luis “N”. [@1] He is presumed innocent until a competent
judicial authority issues a sentence establishing his criminal responsibility.
[@1]

The Guerrero Public Security Secretariat placed the preventive operation in the
municipality of Acapulco on 5 November 2026. [@2] Officers seized 50 small bags
containing green, dry herb with characteristics similar to marijuana. [@1] The
secretariat said the seizure formed part of the surveillance and
crime-prevention work the force carries out in the municipality’s
neighbourhoods. [@1] Only one detention was reported in this intervention. [@1]
No other people were reported as detained in the operation. [@1]

The secretariat invited residents to report emergencies to the 911 number and
possible criminal acts to the anonymous tip service 089. [@1]
