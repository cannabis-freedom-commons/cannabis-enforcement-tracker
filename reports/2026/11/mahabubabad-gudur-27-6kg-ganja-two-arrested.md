Gudur police in Mahabubabad district arrested two people who were transporting
dried ganja in a car and seized 27.6 kg of the drug from them. [@1] Police
valued the seized ganja at about Rs 13.82 lakh. [@1] Officers also seized the
Maruti car used to carry the ganja and two mobile phones. [@1]

On the morning of 5 November, Gudur Sub-Inspector Chirra Ramesh Babu and his
staff ran vehicle checks at the Kobal Thanda crossroad. [@1] They stopped a
Maruti car and found 27.6 kg of dried ganja in its boot. [@1] Questioning showed
the two had collected the ganja from the Chintoor area of Andhra Pradesh and
were taking it to be handed to a person in Hyderabad. [@1] Janavaani reported
that Gudur police were checking vehicles on Thursday when a white Maruti car
with the registration TS 21 T 7453 carrying a man and a woman caught their
attention. [@2]

Police arrested a woman from Bhupathipet village in Gudur mandal, 36, and a man
from Aminabad village in Chennaraopet mandal of Warangal district, 24, in the
case. [@2] A case was registered against them and further investigation began.
[@1] Janavaani reported that police were making arrangements to produce the two
before a court. [@2]

Police warned of strict action over the transport, sale, purchase, use and
storage of ganja and asked the public to give Gudur police information about
such activity. [@1] They said informants' identities would be kept confidential
and that a suitable cash reward would be paid. [@1]
