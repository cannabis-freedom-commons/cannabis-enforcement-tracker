Police arrested Linder Antonio Quiñónez Gil, known as alias “Titán,” during
operation Atlas 069 in Santo Domingo de los Tsáchilas. [@1] The National Police
said the intervention took place at the La Cadena cooperative in Santo Domingo
Este during the early hours of 4 November 2026. [@2] The National Police's
Anti-Crime Brigades Unit (UNBAC) carried out the operation under the
ATLAS-069-SZI-SDT designation. [@2] Police described him as a suspected member
of organized criminal group “R” and linked him to distributing firearms and
controlled substances in Santo Domingo. [@2]

Police said investigators suspected he was using a taxi to transport firearms,
ammunition and controlled substances to La Cadena, Cristo Vive and El Paraíso.
[@2]

Police said the detainee was handed to the competent authority and the evidence
entered the chain of custody for further legal proceedings. [@2]
