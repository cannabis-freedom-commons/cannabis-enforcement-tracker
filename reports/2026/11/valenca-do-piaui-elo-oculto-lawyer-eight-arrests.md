Civil police in Piauí arrested eight people, among them a lawyer, in the
Operation Elo Oculto sweep against a drug-trafficking network with links to the
Piauí capital and São Paulo. [@2] The lawyer, Francisco Batista de França
Júnior, known as Júnior França, was held under a preventive detention order in
the town of Valença do Piauí, in Piauí's interior. [@1] Teams enforced 10
judicial orders, a mix of arrest and search warrants, in Teresina, Valença do
Piauí and São Paulo state. [@2]

Alongside the arrest order, officers searched law offices linked to the lawyer.
[@1] Denarc coordinator Samuel Silveira said the lawyer had gone beyond the
limits of legal practice and kept a direct link to the criminal group rather
than to a lawyer's role. [@2]

Silveira said the trail led from São Paulo to Valença, where the drugs were
stored and distributed, and that part of the supply also reached Teresina. [@2]
In the searches officers took phones, computers and other electronic equipment
for forensic analysis. [@1] Police said properties were used to hide drug loads,
move money and support the group, and that they are still tracing its finances
and other participants. [@2]

Denarc opened the case after a March 2025 seizure in Valença do Piauí of 293
cannabis tablets, later found with another 184 buried in a wooded area, with
four people arrested and two cars and a motorcycle taken. [@1] Piauí Hoje
reported that the 2025 haul, which it called the largest ever recorded in the
region, was valued at about R$3 million on the illegal market. [@1]
