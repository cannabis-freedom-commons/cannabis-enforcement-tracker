Galachipa Thana police carried out three separate anti-drug raids on the evening
and night of 8 November 2026 in Panpatti and Dakua unions and Galachipa
municipality. [@1] Police arrested four people, including a married couple, in
the raids. [@1] Officers seized a total of 27 yaba tablets and 30 grams of
ganja. [@1]

At about 7:40 pm another police team raided the College Road area of Ward 8 in
Galachipa municipality. [@1] Led by Sub-Inspector Suman Chandra Majumder,
officers arrested 36-year-old Md Bashir Madbar with 30 grams of ganja from the
paved road in front of Palash Saha's tea shop in the Bara Saha Bari area. [@1]

At about 9 pm in Tularam, Ward 7 of Panpatti Union, officers searched Md Saiful
Sikder's shop beside the Boalia-Panpatti launch-ghat road and recovered 7 yaba
tablets. [@1] Officers arrested the shopkeeper Saiful Sikder (28) and his wife
Halima Begum (26) at the shop. [@1] At about 11:25 pm in Phulkhali, Ward 9 of
Dakua Union, officers arrested 25-year-old Md Rajib Sardar with 20 yaba tablets
from the paved road in front of Jafar Pedar's house. [@1]

Police filed case No. 18 dated 8 November against Madbar over the ganja seizure
under section 36(1), Table 19(a)/41 of the Narcotics Control Act, 2018. [@1]
Police filed case No. 20 dated 9 November against Sikder and his wife over the 7
yaba tablets under section 36(1), Table 10(a) of the same Act, and case No. 23
dated 9 November against Sardar under the same yaba provision. [@1] Galachipa
Thana police said anti-drug drives would continue across the upazila to
eliminate drugs. [@1]
