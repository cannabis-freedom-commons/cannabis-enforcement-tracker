Istanbul narcotics police stopped a moving box van on the highway in Pendik,
Istanbul, during work against drug trafficking. [@2] Officers detained the van's
driver, identified only as Ü.A., catching him in the act. [@1] Police took Ü.A.
to the Istanbul Provincial Police Directorate for questioning, and the second
report said he was expected to be referred to the courthouse after his statement
procedures. [@2]

A detailed search of the van's cargo hold found 336 kilograms 550 grams of
skunk, packed vacuum-sealed in transparent packets and in large bags. [@1]
Police seized the drugs after following the closed-box van and stopping it on
the highway. [@2]

The operation was carried out by the Narcotics Crimes Division of the Istanbul
Police Department, the Narkotik Suçlarla Mücadele Şube Müdürlüğü. [@1] It took
place on the evening before the 2 November 2026 reports, after officers tracking
the van during an investigation stopped it while it was moving. [@2] Police said
the investigation into the shipment was continuing. [@1]
