Officers of the Volanti squad stopped a car carrying five men in via di Grotta
di Gregna, in the Colli Aniene district of eastern Rome. [@3] The occupants
tried to get away when the car was stopped, according to the police account.
[@3] Police described the load as the biggest of their recent operations,
travelling on the Tiburtina road hidden in shopping bags. [@2]

Between the seats, in two shopping bags, officers found ten blocks of hashish
weighing about one kilogram and more than 6,000 euros in cash. [@1] A further
535 euros was recovered from three of the occupants, bringing the cash seized to
7,110 euros. [@1]

All five were arrested for possession of drugs for the purpose of dealing, in
complicity. [@2] The Questura di Roma said the arrests were validated by the
judicial authority. [@2] The police headquarters said the case is at the
preliminary investigation stage. [@2]
