The court of Additional Sessions Judge Pradeep Kumar in Narnaul granted regular
bail to Karamvir, an accused in a ganja-recovery case. [@1] The court ordered
Karamvir released on a bond of 50,000 rupees with a surety of the same amount.
[@1] Karamvir's lawyer told the court that no narcotic substance was recovered
from Karamvir and that he was named only on the statements of co-accused. [@1]
The lawyer also said the other accused in the case had already been granted
bail. [@1] The public prosecutor opposed bail, saying five other criminal cases
were registered against Karamvir. [@1] After hearing both sides, the court
accepted the bail plea and ordered Karamvir's release on conditions. [@1]

The case began with an FIR at City Kanina police station, where police stopped
and searched a Swift car on 30 May on a secret tip. [@1] Officers recovered
17.04 grams of smack from Vikas alias Chotiwala and Manish Kumar alias Nishu,
residents of Malda Vas village. [@1] Police found 2 kg 60 grams of ganja, the
local word for cannabis, in the car's boot. [@1] Karamvir was arrested
afterwards on the basis of disclosures by the co-accused. [@1]

Only the separately weighed 2.06 kg of ganja is counted as cannabis here; the
17.04 grams of smack is named to explain the mixed seizure and left out of the
cannabis figure. [@1]
