The Odisha Excise Department arrested 3,764 people and seized contraband,
including ganja, worth Rs 45.57 crore during a month-long special enforcement
drive across the state. [@1] The drive ran from 10 October to 10 November 2026
and targeted the illegal manufacture, sale and transportation of liquor and
narcotic substances during the festival season. [@1] The department registered
7,826 cases during the drive. [@1]

Seizures in the period included 3,201 kg of ganja, 6,366 grams of heroin, 150.5
litres of cough syrup, 14,193 kg of mahua flowers, 1,045 litres of duplicate
foreign liquor and 4,28,585 bulk litres of illicitly distilled liquor. [@1]
Enforcement teams destroyed more than 40.6 lakh bulk litres of wash and seized
442 vehicles allegedly used in the commission of the offences. [@1]

The Rs 45.57 crore total covers the contraband seized in the drive as a whole,
including illicit liquor and other narcotics as well as ganja. [@1] The
department said it would continue vigilance and enforcement measures to prevent
the circulation of contraband and strengthen the fight against substance abuse
across the state. [@1]
