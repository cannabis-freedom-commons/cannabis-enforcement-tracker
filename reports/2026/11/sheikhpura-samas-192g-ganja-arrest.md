Police raided a paan gumti, a small roadside kiosk, in Samas village in
Sheikhpura district. [@1] The raid recovered 192 grams of ganja, the local name
for cannabis flower. [@2] Police arrested Sunil Kumar Singh in connection with
the seizure. [@1] The arrested man is a 58-year-old resident of Samas Buzurg
village, identified as the son of the late Nandkishore Singh. [@2]

Barbigha police station chief Gaurav Kumar said the accused sold ganja under the
guise of a grocery shop. [@3] Police registered a case against him under the
Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act and other relevant provisions.
[@3] Barbigha police registered the case as case number 233/26. [@2] The case
was registered under Sections 8(c) and 20(b)(ii)(A) of the NDPS Act, 1985, the
provision for small-quantity cannabis offences. [@2] After completing legal
formalities, police sent Singh to jail in judicial custody at about 5 pm on
Thursday evening. [@2]

The station chief said a special team raided the kiosk, which stood within 500
metres of an educational institution. [@3] The team included Barbigha circle
officer Gaurav Kumar and Sub-Inspector Vidya Sagar. [@3] Police said Singh had
previously been arrested with narcotics in 2014 and had gone to jail in that
case. [@3] Police said the action was part of an intensified district drive
against narcotics trading following a district Anti-Narcotics Task Force
meeting. [@3]
