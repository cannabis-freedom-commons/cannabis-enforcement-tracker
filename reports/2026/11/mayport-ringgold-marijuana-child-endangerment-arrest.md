Pennsylvania State Police charged a 42-year-old Mayport woman with child
endangerment after they say she left a bag of marijuana within reach of two
children during a midnight arrest. [@1,2] Troopers from the Punxsutawney barracks
charged Nicole M. Steele with two counts of endangering the welfare of children,
both first-degree misdemeanors. [@1,2] She was also charged with resisting arrest,
disorderly conduct, small-amount marijuana possession, drug paraphernalia
possession and a summary harassment count. [@1,2]

Steele called Jefferson County 911 from a home on Pansy Ringgold Road in
Ringgold Township about 12:20 a.m. on 12 November. [@1,2] Dispatchers heard a man
and Steele yelling before the call ended. [@1,2] Troopers arrived at the residence
about 12:27 a.m. and met Steele on the front porch. [@1,2] Trooper Joseph DiPietro
wrote in the affidavit that Steele was talking fast and rambling and showed
multiple signs of being heavily under the influence of a controlled substance.
[@1,2]

Trooper David Blazavich and Corporal Robert Means went inside to check on two
juveniles and, police said, saw a plastic bag of marijuana in plain view next to
Steele’s wallet, easily accessible to the children. [@1,2] The complaint says
troopers noted Steele was unable to properly care for the juveniles because of
her impairment. [@1,2] When DiPietro tried to handcuff her, police said, she
screamed and pulled her arms away, and troopers placed her on the ground before
taking her into custody. [@1,2]

Magisterial District Judge Jacqueline J. Mizerock arraigned Steele the same day
and set bail at 10 percent of $20,000 monetary. [@1,2] She could not post the
amount and remains in the Jefferson County Jail. [@1,2] A preliminary hearing is
scheduled for 23 November. [@1,2]
