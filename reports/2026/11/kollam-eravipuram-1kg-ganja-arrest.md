In Kerala's Kollam district, an Excise enforcement team conducting checks across
the coastal belt detained 25-year-old Sufiyan. [@1] The youth is identified as a
resident of Puliyara at Valathungal in Eravipuram in Kollam. [@1]

During the check, the team recovered one kilogram of cannabis, known locally in
Kerala as kanjavu or ganja, from his possession. [@1] Excise officers said the
seized cannabis had been brought into Kerala from another Indian state
specifically for retail sale. [@1] Preliminary information indicated that his
plan was to distribute the stock within the local area by selling it in small
quantities. [@1]

The coastal-area inspection that led to the detention was conducted by a team
working under the leadership of Excise Inspector Sajan. [@1] Excise said further
legal proceedings against the detained youth were already under way following
the seizure and arrest. [@1] The agency added that investigators would trace the
source of the narcotic drug and examine other possible links connected with its
smuggling. [@1]
