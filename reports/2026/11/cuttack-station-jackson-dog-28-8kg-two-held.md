At Cuttack railway station, the Railway Protection Force arrested two young men
from West Bengal under Operation Narcos, foiling an alleged interstate ganja
smuggling attempt. [@1] The check took place at about 11:05 am on 10 November
during a drive against suspicious activity at the station. [@1] RPF officers of
Cuttack post with the Mancheswar dog squad saw the two in suspicious
circumstances at the Bhadrak end of platforms two and three. [@1] The pair were
waiting to board a Kerala-bound train with three new grey, purple and maroon
bags bearing an Ayesha logo. [@1] They tried to flee on seeing the RPF team but
were chased and caught, the bags gave off a strong ganja smell, and trained
narcotics detection dog Jackson also indicated cannabis. [@1]

A search of the three bags found 30 packets wrapped in brown cello tape, which
an NDPS test kit and weighing confirmed as 28.8 kg of ganja. [@1] The RPF put
the estimated value of the seized 28.8 kg of ganja at 1,444,000 rupees. [@1] The
two were identified as 26-year-old Nirash Sheikh and 19-year-old Tanmay Sheikh
of Natun Bamanabad, Kazipara under Sagarpara police station in Murshidabad
district, who said they work at a marble factory in Aluva, Kerala. [@1]

They allegedly were engaged by a man named Suman Sheikh alias Tony to carry the
consignment from Cuttack to Aluva, with each promised 20,000 rupees. [@1]
According to the accused, the bags were handed to them by an unidentified man
who came in a red car near the Clock Tower-Ranihat Chhak in Cuttack. [@1] The
RPF informed the Cuttack GRP station in-charge and an NCB officer in
Bhubaneswar, then handed the accused and the seized ganja to the Cuttack Sadar
excise department for further action. [@1] Excise inspector K. L. Paikaray
registered a case under section 20(b)(ii)(C) of the NDPS Act, 1985 on 10
November, and both accused were produced in court on 11 November and sent to 14
days of judicial custody. [@1]
