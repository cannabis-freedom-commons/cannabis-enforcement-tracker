Gendarmerie teams in Şırnak detained five suspects in narcotics and smuggling
operations carried out across the city centre and its districts between 2 and 8
November 2026. [@2]

The searches found 228 kenevir roots, the Turkish word for cannabis plants, and
68 skunk plants. [@1] Officers also seized 2 kilograms 100 grams of kubar esrar
and 120 grams of pregabalin, a nerve-pain medicine that Turkey treats as a
controlled substance. [@1] Şırnak Haber reports the gendarmerie recorded four
narcotics incidents in the week and that officers also took 41 Xanax and 22
Cedrina psychiatric pills. [@3]

Three of the five suspects were remanded in custody by the court after being
referred to the judicial authorities, and proceedings were opened against the
other two. [@2]

The operation was announced by the Şırnak governor's office and carried out by
the provincial gendarmerie's anti-smuggling and organised crime branch,
according to the Son Dakika account attributed to the Anadolu Agency. [@1] DHA
and Şırnak Haber name the anti-narcotics branch of the provincial gendarmerie
command as the unit that ran the operations. [@2] Kelkit Gazetesi, carrying the
DHA text, reported that the operations targeted addresses identified in advance
in the city centre and the districts. [@4]
