A UP patrol stopped the driver on rv. 5 Florovegen in Kinn at 10.48 on Thursday
morning. [@1] The driver was stopped at Storoeysundet and is suspected of
drugged driving. [@1] Police said the driver is a man in his 30s who was stopped
during a random average-speed measurement. [@1] The measurement showed an
average speed of 96 km/h in an 80 zone, which led to the drugged-driving
suspicion. [@1]

A roadside drug test, a WipeAlyser, showed a positive result for cannabis, but
the level was not yet known. [@1] The driver was therefore taken for blood
sampling on suspicion of drugged driving. [@1] Operations leader Knut
Dahl-Michelsen said a positive result does not guarantee conviction because
cannabis can remain in the blood for a while, so blood tests are needed to
establish impairment. [@1]

Firda reported that no decision had yet been made on seizing the driving
licence. [@1] Fjordenes Tidende corroborated that UP stopped a car at
Storoeysundet at 10.48 and took the driver to secure samples on suspicion of
drugged driving. [@2] Fjordenes Tidende added that police secured samples and
opened a case. [@2]
