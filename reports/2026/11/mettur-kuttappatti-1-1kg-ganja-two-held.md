Police near Mettur arrested two young men who were moving ganja on a motorcycle.
[@1] The two are 21-year-old Murali Krishnan, son of Venkatachalam, and
20-year-old Mahendran, son of Rathnavel. [@1] Both men are from Kuttappatti.
[@1] Police said the pair were carrying the ganja on the motorcycle in order to
sell it. [@1] Officers took both into custody and produced them before a court.
[@1] The court sent them to jail. [@1]

Nangavalli Sub-Inspector Gopal was conducting a vehicle check at the Kuttappatti
four-road junction on Sunday. [@1] He stopped a motorcycle that came through at
speed and searched it. [@1] The search showed the motorcycle was carrying 1.100
kg of ganja. [@1]

Ganja is the Tamil term for cannabis, and the reported 1.100 kg equals 1.1 kg.
[@1] The case is a road-checkpoint seizure in Salem district that ended in
arrest and judicial remand. [@1]
