Police raided a cotton field on Thursday at Dangi Falia in Piprikhedi, in the
Bedia area of Khargone district. [@1] Station-in-charge Rameshwar Thakur said
officers received information about cannabis cultivation at the field and
cordoned it before catching Ram Lal, also known as Ramu Dawar. [@1] Police
arrested the man guarding the field. [@2] A search of the field found cannabis
plants growing among the cotton crop. [@1]

Officers seized all 97 green plants from the field. [@1] The plants stood about
2 to 3 feet tall and weighed 9 kg 260 g (9.26 kg) in total. [@1] Police
estimated their value at 74,000 rupees (Rs 74,000). [@1] Officers registered a
case against Dawar under sections 8/20 of India's Narcotic Drugs and
Psychotropic Substances Act. [@1] At the time of the account, he was being
produced in court. [@1]

Additional Superintendent of Police Shakuntala Ruhal said the investigation into
the case was continuing. [@1] The raid occurred during a special campaign by the
police headquarters' narcotics branch, scheduled to run through 10 November.
