A special NDPS court in Jhansi convicted a man and sentenced him to two years of
rigorous imprisonment and a Rs 5,000 fine for possessing 600 grams of ganja.
[@1] The court said he must serve two more months of simple imprisonment if he
does not pay the fine. [@1]

Ulldan police recovered the 600 grams of ganja from the accused near Dr Ram
Manohar Lohia Inter College in Bangra, within the Todi Fatehpur police-station
area of Jhansi district. [@1] Police arrested the accused, produced him in court
and sent him to jail. [@1] A charge sheet was later filed in court. [@1]

Ganja is the Hindi name for cannabis in India, and the NDPS Act is the country's
narcotics law. [@1] The same court digest also reports on unrelated matters of
gangster charges, several road-accident cases, knife possession, electricity
theft, damage to public property and liquor possession. [@1]
