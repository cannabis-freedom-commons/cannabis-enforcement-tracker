Suriname's Narcotica Brigade arrested a 53-year-old man known by the initials
L.A. at an address on the Bealaan in Paramaribo on Tuesday 3 November 2026. [@2]
Officers went to two neighbouring addresses at the same time after information
developed by the Narcotics Intelligence Unit, the police's drug intelligence
unit, with support from the Arrestation Team and the Quick Response Team. [@2]
No drugs or other items were found at the first address. [@2]

When L.A. saw the police he ran inside and tried to flush a quantity of
marijuana down the toilet, but did not succeed. [@3] Officers seized 56 grams of
marijuana in all. [@2]

Police took him to the police station to be brought before an officer. [@4]
After consulting the Public Prosecution Service (Openbaar Ministerie), police
held him in custody in verzekering, the Surinamese pre-trial detention during an
investigation, pending the inquiry. [@4] The police repeated that they will keep
working against the sale and use of drugs. [@1]
