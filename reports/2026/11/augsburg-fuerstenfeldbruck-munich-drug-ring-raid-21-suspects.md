Police arrested several suspects in a coordinated drugs raid on the morning of
Thursday 12 November 2026. [@1,5] The arrested suspects hold German, Bosnian, Croatian and Afghan nationality. [@5] All 15 arrested men and women are now in pretrial detention. [@5] Police arrested 15 suspects aged 20 to 38, comprising 13 men and two women, across the three sites. [@5] Criminal police from Augsburg, Fürstenfeldbruck
and Munich searched flats and other buildings in the city of Augsburg, in the
Fürstenfeldbruck area and in Munich, executing numerous search warrants and
several arrest warrants. [@3,5] Six of the arrests were in the Augsburg area. [@5] The searches began in the early morning hours in
the Augsburg city area, with simultaneous searches started in the
Fürstenfeldbruck area and in Munich. [@1] The operation was carried out in
agreement with the Augsburg public prosecutor's office, and riot police and dog
handlers took part. [@3] Police said the operation followed months of
investigations. [@4,5]

The searches were directed against 21 suspects suspected of dealing in narcotics
in quantities that are not insignificant. [@2,3] Not all suspects are assigned to the drug ring, as the group also included buyers who were supposed to resell the drugs. [@5] The Augsburg criminal police were
reported to have broken up a drug ring. [@1] Police said officers found
narcotics in kilogram quantities, cash in a high five-figure range and further
evidence during the searches. [@1,4] Officers found cash in a low six-figure amount and seized valuables and two vehicles belonging to the group. [@5] In the raids officers seized narcotics in the lower three-figure kilogram range, including cannabis products in the upper two-figure kilogram range, cocaine in the upper single-figure kilogram range and amphetamine in the lower two-figure kilogram range. [@5] The Augsburger Allgemeine reported that the
drugs in question were mainly cocaine and marijuana, in larger quantities. [@1,5]
Police said the investigations and the evaluation of the seized evidence are
continuing. [@2] Already before the Thursday operation officers had seized cannabis products in the mid two-figure kilogram range and cocaine in the mid three-figure gram range that investigators attribute to the group. [@5]
