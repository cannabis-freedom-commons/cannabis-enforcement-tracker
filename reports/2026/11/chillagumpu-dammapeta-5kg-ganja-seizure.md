Police seized five kilograms of ganja at Chillagumpu in Dammapeta mandal. [@2]
Ganja cases detected in Aswaraopet and Dammapeta mandals had been increasing for
several years. [@1] Police registered numerous cases involving ganja transport
and use over the previous two years. [@1]

Ganja was allegedly being sold in several colonies and along main roads, while
chain stores were becoming drug outlets. [@1] Police faced the challenge of
identifying the gangs selling drugs and tracing the networks connected to their
supply operations. [@2] Some students were reportedly becoming sellers for easy
money, while some staff at educational institutions were also said to be selling
drugs after being tempted by quick earnings. [@2]

Circle Inspector Nagaraju Reddy said police regularly held awareness programmes
through social activities to discourage young people from addiction to ganja and
other drugs. [@1] He invited information about transport or use and promised to
keep informants' names confidential. [@1] He said police would act firmly
against ganja transport and sales and expand patrolling. [@1]
