State civil-guard officers detained three people in operations in El Naranjo and
Ciudad Valles. [@1] In El Naranjo, an operation led to a pursuit that ended in
the locality of Sabino de Arriba. [@1] There officers detained Alfredo “N”, aged
26, from Matamoros, Tamaulipas, and Martha “N”, aged 33. [@1] In a second action
in Ciudad Valles, officers detained José “N”, aged 38. [@1]

In the El Naranjo stop officers seized 55 doses of a substance identified as
cristal, 17 doses of marijuana, 26 devices known as ponchallantas and five
video-surveillance cameras. [@1] From José “N” in Ciudad Valles officers seized
further doses described as marijuana, with no number given. [@2]

The detentions resulted from preventive and intelligence actions by the Guardia
Civil. [@2] The three detainees and the seized objects were placed at the
disposal of the corresponding authorities to investigate their possible
responsibility. [@1] The state Secretariat of Security and Citizen Protection
reported both operations as part of the force's preventive and intelligence
work. [@1]
