Police in Sargodha arrested two suspected drug peddlers in raids on Friday 6
November 2026. [@1] Police identified the two suspects as Imtiaz Ahmad and
Parwaiz Akhtar. [@1]

The raids by teams from different police stations in Sargodha were conducted as
part of a continued drive against drug pushers and criminals. [@1] The police
statement says the raids recovered 2.8 kg of hashish, the cannabis resin. [@1]
Separate cases were registered against the two suspects. [@1]

The account was published by the state news agency APP and carried by UrduPoint.
