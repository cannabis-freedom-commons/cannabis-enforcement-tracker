Police in Kamieniec Ząbkowicki located and detained a 35-year-old Slovak
resident shortly after a shop theft. [@1] He still had the stolen products on
him, along with a small amount of marijuana. [@1]

The incident occurred on 10 November at about 7:30 a.m. [@1] The man entered a
shop in Kamieniec Ząbkowicki, took alcohol and a drink from the shelves and left
without paying. [@1] The alcohol and drink together were worth about PLN 120.
[@1] Shop staff notified the police and passed on information about the
perpetrator. [@1] Officers started a search that quickly produced a result. [@1]

Polish criminal law punishes particularly audacious theft with 6 months to 8
years of imprisonment. [@1] Whether the man's behaviour will be qualified that
way is to be decided by the competent authorities. [@1] Ząbkowice Śląskie police
spokeswoman Katarzyna Mazurek said shoplifting does not always end with a fine
and that the concrete circumstances, not the value alone, decide the legal
qualification. [@1]
