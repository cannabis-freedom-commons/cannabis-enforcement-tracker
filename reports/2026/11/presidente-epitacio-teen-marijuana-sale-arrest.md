Military Police officers from the Companhia de Força Tática of the 42nd Military
Police Battalion (42º BPM/I) arrested a man in flagrante for drug trafficking on
the morning of Wednesday 11 November 2026 in Presidente Epitácio, São Paulo.
[@3] The officers were on tactical patrol after information about drug sales in
the area when they approached a teenager carrying two portions of marijuana.
[@2] The teenager said he had bought the drug from a man who lived on Rua Alziro
Baltazar. [@2]

The teams went to the address and found the suspect. [@1] The suspect authorised
the officers to enter the house, and they searched it. [@3] The search found
several portions of marijuana already divided for sale and a larger piece of the
same substance. [@1] Officers also seized R$200 in cash and a mobile phone. [@1]

The man was taken to the Civil Police station, where he remained in custody at
the disposal of the courts. [@1] The teenager was also presented to the Civil
Police for the appropriate measures. [@1]
