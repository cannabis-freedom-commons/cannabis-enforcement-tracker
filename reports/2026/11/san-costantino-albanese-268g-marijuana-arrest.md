Carabinieri arrested a 43-year-old man in San Costantino Albanese, in the
province of Potenza. [@1,2,3] Officers found 268 grams of marijuana and 109 grams of
cocaine in his possession, the cocaine noted only to explain the mixed seizure.
[@1,2,3]

Inside the man's sports bag, officers found two precision scales and five
packets containing drugs. [@1,2,3]

The investigating judge of the Lagonegro court later validated the arrest and
ordered the suspect held in pretrial custody in prison. [@1,2,3]

The operation was conducted by officers of the Terranova di Pollino station during a territorial control service arranged by the Senise Carabinieri company. [@2,3] Officers also found small-denomination banknotes in the bag with the drugs, scales and wrappers. [@2] On the order of the Lagonegro prosecutor, the man was taken to Potenza prison. [@2,3]
