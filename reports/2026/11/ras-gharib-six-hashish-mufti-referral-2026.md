The first chamber of the Red Sea Criminal Court referred the papers of six
defendants to the Grand Mufti on 7 November 2026 to obtain a religious opinion
on executing them. [@1] The court set a hearing next month to pronounce sentence
in light of the Mufti's opinion and the case file. [@1]

Prosecutors accused the six of forming a gang that specialised in trading and
smuggling hashish in Ras Gharib district, Red Sea Governorate. [@1] An operation
by the Red Sea anti-narcotics office targeting the desert areas around Ras
Gharib city seized about a tonne and a half of hashish, along with a number of
firearms and ammunition. [@1] Investigators said the first and second defendants
ran the group's finances and divided roles among its members, while the gang
moved drugs by sea from the South Sinai coast and Suez Gulf to the Red Sea coast
and stored them in prepared sites in desert valleys and mountain tracks. [@1]

The charges included possessing, carrying and transporting hashish and dried
cannabis plant for trafficking without a licence, and possessing machine guns
and ammunition. [@1] Prosecutors also accused the defendants, by incitement and
agreement, of assaulting anti-narcotics officers and resisting them during their
duties. [@1] The case is registered as number 1363 of 2025 for the Red Sea
prosecution office, and the alleged offences are dated 11 January 2025 in Ras
Gharib police district. [@1] The Public Prosecution referred the defendants to
the first-instance criminal court, ordering their arrest and detention, service
of the charges and evidence list, and the attachment of their criminal records.
[@1]
