Judge Brijesh Kumar sentenced each convict to 12 years of rigorous imprisonment.
[@1,4] The convicts are Keshwar Das, son of Lakshman Das, and Lalji Das, son of
Daroga Das, both residents of Bharat Mahi in Raxaul. [@1,4] Special public
prosecutor Prabhash Tripathi presented seven witnesses before the verdict. [@1,4]

Police stopped the pair near Siswaniya Pokhra Dhangartoli on NH-28 in Ramgarhwa
after a tip to station chief Indrajeet Paswan. [@1,4] Their two bags held 2.5 kg
of charas in one and 2.48 kg in the other, 4.98 kg in total. [@1,4] The seizure
was recorded as 4 kg 980 g of charas. [@1,4] Police registered Ramgarhwa case
389/24 under the NDPS Act and the court registered NDPS case 25/24. [@1] It
names the arrested men as Lalji Das and Keshwar Das of Raxaul and says an FIR
was lodged at Ramgarhwa police station. [@2] They were produced in court the
next day and remanded to judicial custody. [@2]

A second sentence report confirms two convicts received 12 years each plus Rs 1
lakh fine in the East Champaran charas case. [@3]

If the fine goes unpaid, each convict faces six more months of imprisonment. [@4]
