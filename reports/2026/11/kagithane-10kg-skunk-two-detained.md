Istanbul Provincial Gendarmerie Command teams started work to catch suspects
accused of manufacturing narcotic substances and preparing to release them onto
the market. [@1] After technical and physical surveillance, the teams raided a
workplace in Kağıthane, Istanbul. [@2] Officers detained two suspects during the
raid on the Kağıthane workplace. [@1]

Officers searching the workplace seized 10 kilograms of skunk. [@1] The same
search also seized three mobile phones alongside the drugs. [@2]
