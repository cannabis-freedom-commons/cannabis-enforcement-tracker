On 3 November 2026, following information received by Army Intelligence, a joint
raid by Army personnel and the Department of Wildlife Conservation in Kantale
uncovered a large-scale illegal cannabis cultivation inside the Somawathiya
Reserve in Agbopura, Divulgaswewa. [@1] One suspect was arrested at the scene
during the operation, the only detention reported in the account. [@1] The
Department of Wildlife Conservation in Kantale is conducting further legal
proceedings regarding the incident. [@1]

The raided plantation covered three acres, about 1.21 hectares, and contained
more than 35,000 cannabis plants on that plot. [@1] Officers also discovered an
additional two acres of land at the same reserve site being prepared for further
cultivation. [@1]

The raid followed the Army Intelligence tip-off and was conducted jointly with
wildlife conservation officers. [@1] The cultivation site lay inside the
Somawathiya Reserve in the Kantale area. [@1] The available account names no
other arrested people and gives no further personal details about the person
held. [@1]
