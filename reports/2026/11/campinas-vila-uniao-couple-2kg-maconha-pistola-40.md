Police in the Vila Union neighbourhood of Campinas, São Paulo, arrested a man
and a woman for drug trafficking. [@1] Officers had been carrying out an arrest
warrant against the woman when they stopped her partner in a condominium parking
lot. [@1]

In the man's car officers found portions of drugs and a packet of about 500
grams of marijuana. [@1] A search of the couple's home yielded more marijuana,
bringing the total to about 2 kilograms, together with a concentrated cannabis
derivative, two precision scales, materials for preparing drugs, and a
.40-calibre pistol with its serial number removed. [@1]

The man told police the drugs were for sale and that each portion of about 500
grams had been bought for R$ 25,000, with a planned resale price of R$ 50,000.
[@1] Both were arrested in flagrante for drug trafficking with a firearm and
left at the disposal of the judiciary. [@1]
