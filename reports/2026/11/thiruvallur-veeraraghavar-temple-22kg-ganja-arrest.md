Thiruvallur Town police arrested three men, including a local rowdy, during an
evening vehicular check near the Veeraraghavar temple in Thiruvallur. [@1]
Officers conducting the vehicle checkpoint intercepted and questioned three men
after observing them walking past in a suspicious manner. [@1] Police identified
the suspects as 40-year-old rowdy Anbazhagan from the Gummidipoondi area
alongside his associates 31-year-old Rajkumar and 25-year-old Satya. [@1]

Upon inspecting bags carried by the men, officers discovered and seized 22
kilograms of ganja. [@1] Officers also found and confiscated pattakkathis, heavy
cutting billhook knives, packed inside the same bags. [@1]

Thiruvallur Town police registered a criminal case following the weapon and drug
seizure and detained the trio for interrogation. [@1] Police subsequently
produced all three accused before the local Thiruvallur court. [@1] Following
their court appearance, the three men were remanded in judicial custody and
lodged in Puzhal Central Prison in Chennai. [@1]
