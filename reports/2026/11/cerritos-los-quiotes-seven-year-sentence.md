A federal judge sentenced José “N” and Pedro “N” to seven years in prison for
firearm possession and drug transport after the Federal Public Ministry proved
the charges. [@1] The two men are serving the sentence in La Pila prison. [@1]
The district judge in the federal criminal justice centre in San Luis Potosí
imposed seven years in prison and a fine of 50 Units of Measurement and Update,
equivalent to 5,187 pesos. [@1]

Officers searching the men's vehicle found the firearm and 17 live cartridges,
plus various doses of marijuana and crystal methamphetamine. [@1] Prosecutors
treated the case as possession of an exclusive-use Army firearm and possession
of drugs for transport. [@1] The marijuana doses are the cannabis component
recorded here, while the crystal, firearm and cartridges are noted only to
explain the mixed case. [@1]

Guardia Civil Estatal officers detained the men in March 2023 after spotting
their vehicle on the free road to Cerritos at Los Quiotes in Cerritos
municipality. [@1] The co-pilot aimed a squad-type pistol at the patrol vehicle,
then both men left the vehicle and fled into brush before being captured. [@1]
