Military Police teams from the 25th Military Police Battalion recorded two more
drug-trafficking cases in Feira de Santana on Friday 6 November 2026, in the
Parque Ipê and Conceição neighbourhoods. [@1]

At about 10:50, in Conjunto Renascer in Parque Ipê, an app driver told officers
he had seen a teenager selling drugs, and the patrol found and stopped the
youth. [@1] The search found 60 wraps of marijuana, 10 wraps of cocaine and 42
reais in cash. [@1] The teenager was taken to the Delegacia para o Adolescente
Infrator (DAI), the police station for underage offenders, with the seized
material. [@1]

At about 20:40, officers of Ceto I went to the Conceição Ville condominium in
the Conceição neighbourhood after the Cicom police operations centre reported
armed people involved in drug trafficking there. [@1] Police said the team was
received with gunfire and fired back, and that one of those fleeing tried to
jump a wall, fell and was caught. [@1] The man resisted and was restrained with
moderate force, and with him officers found two large packages and 27 wraps of
marijuana, 39 rocks of crack cocaine and a Xiaomi Redmi phone. [@1]

The 25th Battalion said the man had earlier records for drug trafficking, in
June 2026 and June 2025. [@1] He was first taken to the UPA emergency unit in
Parque Ipê for a medical assessment and then presented at the Central de
Flagrantes (Cenflag) with the seized material. [@1]
