Police teams from Karşıyaka district's security directorate raided three
addresses at the same time in Örnekköy Mahallesi, Karşıyaka, Izmir, after
determining that drugs were kept there. [@2] Seven suspects were taken into
custody in the operation. [@2] A 16-year-old suspect who was already the subject
of an arrest warrant was also caught during the raids. [@2] The suspects'
procedures at the police station were continuing and the final charges and any
judicial measures against them had not been set. [@1]

Searches of the addresses found cannabis, reported as esrar, the Turkish term
for herbal cannabis, alongside synthetic drugs and 1,320 synthetic drug pills.
[@2] Officers also seized two pump-action shotguns, two loaded cartridges,
12,125 Turkish lira in cash and two mobile phones. [@2]
