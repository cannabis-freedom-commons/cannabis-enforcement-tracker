Civil Police in Itapetininga, São Paulo, arrested one man in flagrante for drug
trafficking on Tuesday 10 November 2026. [@1] Police said the man used social
networks to sell drugs and a delivery service to make the deliveries. [@1] The
arrest came during an investigation into drug sales carried out using the
internet. [@1]

Officers serving a court-issued search and seizure warrant found 10 ecstasy
tablets, portions of dry and marijuana flower and more than R$7,000 in cash.
[@1] Police also seized a precision scale, two mobile phones and the motorcycle
used for the deliveries. [@1] The man was arrested in flagrante and remained at
the disposal of the courts. [@1] The operation was Operação Black Hat, launched
by the Delegacia de Investigações sobre Entorpecentes (DISE) of Itapetininga,
the local narcotics police station. [@2] The local outlet gave the cash seized
as R$7,600 and said the motorcycle was used, according to the investigation, to
carry out the deliveries. [@2]
