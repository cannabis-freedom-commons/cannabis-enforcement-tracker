Deputies arrested 34-year-old Quinton Marcellous Milbourne of Salisbury on
criminal charges and traffic violations, in addition to serving him with three
outstanding warrants and one detainer. [@1] According to the sheriff's office,
those charges included identity theft to avoid prosecution and controlled
dangerous substance offenses. [@2] According to court documents obtained by
WMDT, one of the warrants was for charges including first- and second-degree
assault stemming from an incident in Princess Anne on Sept. 22. [@1] Milbourne
is being held without bond at the Wicomico County Detention Center. [@1]

On Nov. 7, deputies with the Wicomico County Sheriff’s Office conducted a
traffic stop in the 100 block of Kendall Street in Salisbury. [@1] During the
investigation, the driver, identified as Milbourne, provided inconsistent
identifying information and repeatedly refused deputies’ commands to exit the
vehicle. [@1] A records check revealed that Milbourne had multiple outstanding
warrants through several agencies, and his Maryland driver’s license was
suspended. [@1] A Salisbury Police Department K-9 unit also conducted an
open-air sniff of the vehicle, and positively alerted to the presence of a
controlled dangerous substance. [@1] During a subsequent search, deputies
recovered suspected marijuana, a suspected MDMA pill, and other items. [@1] The
case was handled by Wicomico County deputies, with help from the Salisbury
Police Department. [@2]
