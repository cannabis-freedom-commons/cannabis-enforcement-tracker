Mexico City police detained a man known as "El Piri" after reports of gunfire at
a cemetery in the Álvaro Obregón borough, and seized a firearm and drugs from
him. [@1] The city's Public Security Secretariat (SSC) said the man may belong
to a cell dedicated to selling narcotics in the colonia Jalalpa, in the same
borough. [@1]
