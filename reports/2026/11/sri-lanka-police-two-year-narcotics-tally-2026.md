Sri Lanka Police said that 4,056 firearms and weapons were seized across the
country during the past two years, in a national two-year enforcement tally.
[@1] The police breakdown listed 97 T-56 rifles and one AK-47 assault rifle
among the weapons seized. [@1] The same breakdown listed 143 pistols, 105
revolvers and 397 12-bore shotguns. [@4]

In the same two-year period police conducted 597,964 drug-related raids and
operations across the country, in a nationwide effort covering all drugs rather
than cannabis alone. [@4] Police said those operations involved 596,591 suspects
and recovered 43,726 kilograms of narcotics in total. [@1] Within that total,
police reported 28,441 kilograms of cannabis, alongside 4,009 kilograms of
heroin and 6,526 kilograms of crystal methamphetamine, which the sources call
Ice. [@2] Police estimated the value of the whole narcotics haul, not the
cannabis alone, at around Rs. 224 billion. [@3]
