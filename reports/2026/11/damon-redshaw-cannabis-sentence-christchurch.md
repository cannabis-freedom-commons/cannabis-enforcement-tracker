Christchurch District Court Judge Quentin Hix sentenced 54-year-old Damon
Redshaw on 11 November 2026, describing him during sentencing as a man whose
mind was running at a hundred kilometres per hour. [@1] Redshaw avoided jail
over the cannabis, the sawn-off shotgun and drug driving. [@1]

Redshaw told the court the right front tyre of his vehicle blew out on his way
to Homebase in Shirley, and that he collided with another vehicle before pulling
over. [@1] Police found a briefcase in his vehicle containing six zip-locked
bags with 234 grams of cannabis. [@1] In the vehicle's boot they found a
sawn-off bolt-action .22 rifle in a computer bag and 126 rounds of ammunition.
[@1] Police also found a vape containing methamphetamine, which Redshaw said he
had no knowledge of. [@1]
