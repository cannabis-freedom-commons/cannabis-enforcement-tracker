Police in Besançon detained a 20-year-old motorist during the night from Sunday
to Monday on boulevard Winston-Churchill in the city. [@1,2] Officers had stopped
the driver for excessive speed, and a subsequent test showed him positive for
cannabis. [@1,2]

A search of the vehicle uncovered 67 grams of cannabis concealed in a hiding
place fitted inside the car. [@1,2] Officers also seized 1,190 euros in cash found
with the cannabis during the same vehicle check. [@1,2] A follow-up search of the
driver's home recovered a further 870 euros, bringing the total amount of cash
seized in the case to 2,060 euros. [@1,2] The young driver was placed in police
custody after the seizures and admitted the facts. [@1,2]

He is now to answer for his actions before the justice system through
comparution sur reconnaissance préalable de culpabilité, the French guilty-plea
procedure. [@1,2] The case therefore links a roadside traffic stop in Besançon to
a home search and a forthcoming court hearing. [@1]

The interception happened at about 02:45. [@2] The 67 grams comprised 9 grams of cannabis resin and 58 grams of cannabis herb. [@2] The hiding place was a cache fitted under the central armrest. [@2] Officers of the judicial support group came to the scene to search the vehicle. [@2] The 1,190 euros were in the driver's satchel and the 870 euros found at his home were in his bedroom. [@2] His custody ended on 3 November 2026 and he was summoned by a police officer for the guilty-plea hearing. [@2]
