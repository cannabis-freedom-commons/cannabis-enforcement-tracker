In West Champaran district of Bihar, Kangli police station police arrested Madan
Patel in Bahuarwa village of Sikta block after recovering 130 grams of ganja
from him. [@1] Police identified the arrested man as Madan Patel, son of late
Harishchandra Patel and a resident of Bahuarwa village under Kangli police
station in West Champaran district. [@1]

According to police, the arrest was made during a raid campaign against
narcotics after officers received information that a man in the area was holding
ganja. [@1] Acting on that tip, a police team carried out an operation in
Bahuarwa village of Sikta block and took the suspect into custody. [@1] During
the Bahuarwa operation, officers seized 130 grams of ganja from his possession
for this case. [@1]

Police said legal proceedings have been started against Patel following the
seizure and arrest. [@1] Police said the continuing investigation is trying to
establish where the ganja was brought from, what its source was and whether any
other person is involved in the matter. [@1]
