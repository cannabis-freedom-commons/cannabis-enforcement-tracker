Carabinieri in Sezze arrested two local residents aged 23 and 22 on suspicion of
possessing drugs with intent to deal. [@1,3,4,5,6] The operation began on the afternoon
of 4 November during a territorial control patrol in the town centre. [@1,3]
Officers stopped the two after noticing their suspicious behaviour near a public
business. [@2,5,6]

The street check found one youth with a small amount of hashish and the other
with about 25 grams in 11 doses plus an 18 cm knife with hashish residue. [@1,3,4,5,6] A
bedroom search in the 23-year-old's home found ten hashish blocks totalling
about 960 grams plus just under 70 grams of marijuana. [@1,3,4,5,6] Officers also seized
about 165 euros in cash thought to be proceeds, two precision scales and
packaging material. [@1,3,4,5,6] Officers also found an electronic identity card whose
microchip had been materially removed. [@1,3,4] A search in the 22-year-old's home
found another roughly 25 grams of marijuana kept in two glass containers. [@1,3,4,5,6]
All the recovered material was seized. [@1,3]

Both were arrested in flagrancy for possession with intent to deal. [@1,3] After
formalities the 23-year-old was transferred to Latina prison while the
22-year-old was placed under house arrest. [@1,3,4,5,6] The 23-year-old was also
reported at liberty for material forgery over the identity card. [@1,3,4,6] The
22-year-old must also answer for carrying weapons or objects capable of offence
over the knife. [@1,3,6] Both remain available to the Latina public prosecutor
pending the validation hearing. [@1,3,6] The case is in the
preliminary-investigation phase and both youths are presumed innocent until a
final sentence. [@1,3]

The hashish found on the younger man was packed as 11 ready doses of about 25 grams in total. [@3]
