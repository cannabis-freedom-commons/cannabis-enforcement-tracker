A 30-year-old man was arrested in flagrante for drug trafficking on the night of
Thursday 5 November 2026 in Povoado Ipojuca, in the municipality of Craíbas, in
the Agreste region of Alagoas. [@1] Teams from the 3rd Military Police Battalion
(3º BPM) carried out the action with the Special Operations Platoon, the Canil
canine unit and the unit's supervisor. [@1] Military police said residents had
reported intense movement of motorcyclists at a house, riders who arrived,
stayed a few minutes and left again. [@1]

When officers reached the address the man ran inside the house, and police at
the side of the property saw a bag thrown over the wall towards the back of the
land. [@1] Inside the house officers found a quantity of a substance resembling
crack, and in the wooded area the Canil's sniffer dog K9 Bereta found the
discarded bag with portions of marijuana and cocaine. [@1] Questioned, the
suspect admitted the drugs were his. [@1]

Officers seized 25 portions of marijuana weighing 96 grams, and in the arrest
report the Cannabis sativa plant is listed as the drug seized. [@1,2] The same
seizure took 98 bags of cocaine weighing about 100 grams and one crack stone of
77 grams; these weights are not counted as cannabis. [@1,3] The man and all the
material were taken to the Central de Polícia, where an arrest in flagrante
(APF) for drug trafficking was recorded. [@1]

The man was left at the disposal of the courts after being charged in flagrante at the Central de Polícia, TNH1 reported. [@4] Police said the owner of the house and his partner had already been arrested on suspicion of drug trafficking. [@3]
