Police at Netivot station arrested a resident of the town, a man in his 20s, on
suspicion of drug trafficking. [@1] Officers detained him in an operation based
on intelligence information. [@1] The suspect was taken for investigation at the
Netivot station. [@1]

Police searched his body and the bag he carried, then searched his home. [@1]
Hadashot 08, a second local outlet, said the bag was a pouch he was carrying and
that what officers found in it led them to continue the investigation. [@2] The
searches found about 166 grams of oil suspected to contain cannabis and about 20
grams of a substance suspected to be cannabis, about 186 grams of cannabis in
all. [@1]

Police also seized about 132.5 grams of a drug they call 'Doctor' (MMC, a
synthetic stimulant), about 20 grams of crystal (methamphetamine) and about 8
grams of 'dosa' (2C-B, a party drug), none of them cannabis. [@1] Officers also
seized two knives, mobile phones and about 8,300 shekels in cash. [@1] Hadashot
08 described the knives as one folding knife and one with a fixed blade. [@2]

Sgt. Maj. Beni Okanin, head of the investigation team at Netivot station, was
quoted saying the activity was part of the station's effort to find trafficking
suspects and seize suspected drugs. [@1] A fuller quotation in Hadashot 08 has
him calling the operation part of the station's continuing fight against drug
dealers and crime in the town. [@2] Police had not reported filing an
indictment, and the seized material was still described as suspected drugs. [@2]
