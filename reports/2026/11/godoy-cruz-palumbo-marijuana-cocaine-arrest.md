Police of Mendoza, through the Unidad de Accion Preventiva, deployed preventive
patrols in Godoy Cruz and Guaymallen that led to several apprehensions and
seizures. [@2] In Barrio Palumbo, a man tried to flee officers who were carrying
out a preventive patrol round. [@1] Officers pursued him on foot and intercepted
him at the intersection of Pringles and General Paz streets. [@1]

During the subsequent search, officers found wrappers containing cocaine and
marijuana on him, plus 65,000 pesos in cash. [@1] The man was apprehended and
the drugs and cash were seized. [@1]

The procedures were carried out by the motorized units of the Unidad de Accion
Preventiva. [@2] The operations used preventive rounds and coordinated movements
based on alerts from the Centro Estrategico de Operaciones. [@2]
