The Air Intelligence Unit of Airport Customs at Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj
International Airport arrested 27-year-old Karnataka resident Sheikh Mohammed
Shimman for allegedly smuggling premium-grade hydroponic weed from Bangkok into
India. [@1] Shimman was apprehended in the early hours of Wednesday after
customs officials identified him through spot profiling. [@1] Senior customs
authorities said he was intercepted at approximately 12:10 am near the exit gate
of the international arrival hall. [@1] Advocates Arun Gupta and Ashish Singh
argued for the accused in court, after which he was granted bail. [@1]

Acting on suspicion, officers subjected him to a baggage examination and found a
concealed airtight pouch stashed alongside clothes inside his luggage. [@1] The
pouch held 845 grams of high-quality hydroponic weed regulated under the
Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act. [@1]

During interrogation, Shimman confessed to the crime and admitted he was fully
aware of the legal consequences. [@1] He said he knew smuggling ganja or
hydroponic weed into India attracts stringent punishment but did it to earn
quick and easy money. [@1] Customs officials placed Shimman under arrest and are
probing deeper into the international supply chain. [@1] Authorities suspect
that he is part of a larger, well-entrenched drug trafficking syndicate
operating across borders. [@1] A customs officer said the preliminary
investigation and the accused statement disclose other unidentified persons and
that efforts are under way to identify and apprehend his associates and key
players. [@1]
