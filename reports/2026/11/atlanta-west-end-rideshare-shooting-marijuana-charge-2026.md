Atlanta police arrested 20-year-old Manderis Seagraves after a passenger shot a
42-year-old rideshare driver during an argument in southwest Atlanta on 7
November 2026. [@1] Officers were flagged down just before 7 p.m. at 884 York
Ave. SW and found the driver, who was alert and breathing, with an apparent
gunshot wound. [@1]

Police said the passenger fired at the vehicle as the driver tried to leave
after the argument, and charged Seagraves with possession of less than 1 ounce
of marijuana. [@2] The three other charges were aggravated assault, possession
of a firearm during the commission of a felony and second-degree criminal damage
to property. [@1]

Officers recovered a firearm from Seagraves and took him to the Fulton County
Jail, and the investigation continued. [@1] The information came from an Atlanta
Police Department statement, which FOX 5 Atlanta reported it reproduced. [@2]
