The Guardia Civil detained a young Spanish national in Santanyí, on the island
of Mallorca, for alleged drug trafficking after intercepting him with a kilo of
marijuana inside his car. [@1,2,4,6] The driver was detained for drug trafficking and
placed at the disposal of the competent judicial authority following the
seizure. [@1,2,3,4,5]

According to the Guardia Civil statement issued as the Instituto Armado, the
events occurred at 19.00 on Monday when officers were conducting a control as
part of their citizen-security functions. [@1,2,3] When officers signalled a vehicle
to stop during that control, they noticed a strong smell of marijuana coming
from inside the car. [@1,2,3,4,5] Officers then inspected the interior of the car and
found a large-sized bag in the rear that contained a kilo of cannabis. [@1,2,3,4,5]

Following the discovery in the rear of the car, officers seized the substance
containing the kilo of cannabis. [@1] The stop and arrest took place in
Santanyí, a town on the island of Mallorca. [@1,6]

The driver was 22 years old. [@3,4,5] The control was carried out by officers of the Guardia Civil Puesto Principal in Santanyí. [@3,4]

The cannabis was vacuum-packed in a bag of the kind normally used to store clothing between seasons. [@5]
