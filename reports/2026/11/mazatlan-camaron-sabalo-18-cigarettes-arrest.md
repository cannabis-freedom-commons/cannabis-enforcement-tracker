Naval personnel of Mexico's Secretariat of the Navy detained a man on Avenida
Camarón Sábalo in Mazatlán, Sinaloa, during a surveillance patrol. [@1] He was
carrying 161 bags of suspected drugs, including 18 cigarettes of suspected
marijuana. [@1] The Navy said he had 116 bags of white powder similar to
cocaine, 27 bags of a granulated crystalline substance alleged to be
methamphetamine, and 18 further bags, each holding one cigarette made of green
herb that appeared to be marijuana. [@1] Those 18 cigarettes are the cannabis
portion of the seizure; the cocaine and methamphetamine are not counted as
cannabis. [@1]

Noroeste said the two patrols were on Avenida Camarón Sábalo in Mazatlán and in
the sindicatura of Villa Unión, and that the Navy reported suspected cocaine,
methamphetamine and marijuana. [@1] The detainee was told of his rights and,
with the seized substances, was placed at the disposal of the competent
authorities so they could open the corresponding investigation file. [@1]

Extraoficial, citing the Fourth Naval Region, reported the same seizure of 116
bags similar to cocaine, 27 bags of a crystalline substance apparently
methamphetamine, and 18 bags each containing a cigarette of green herb,
allegedly marijuana. [@2] That account also said he was informed of his rights
and handed over with the substances for the corresponding investigations. [@2]
No weight was given for the marijuana cigarettes, and neither account named the
man or said a court had charged or sentenced him. [@2]
