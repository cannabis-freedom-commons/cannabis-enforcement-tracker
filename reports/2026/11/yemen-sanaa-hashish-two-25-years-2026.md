The Specialized Criminal Court of First Instance for the Capital Secretariat in
Sanaa convicted two men of trafficking and promoting narcotics and sentenced
them to 25 years in prison. [@1] The court convicted Yahya Saleh Mohammed and
Turki Shaye' Naji of trafficking and promoting cannabis resin and pregabalin
pills. [@1] The session was presided over by Judge Abdullah al-Hamzi, with
prosecutor Judge Mujahed al-Shabibi and court clerk Abdulmajeed al-Dhafiri
present. [@1] The court delivered the verdict on Sunday, 8 November 2026,
according to a republication of the account the next day. [@2]

The court ordered the confiscation of two pickup trucks belonging to the
convicted men, which were used to move and receive the drugs in al-Jawf, Amran
and Saada governorates. [@1] The court also ordered the seized drugs destroyed
and placed the two men in rehabilitation and reform programmes. [@1]

Prosecutors said the first defendant, with unidentified co-accused, received,
transported and delivered 594 blocks of cannabis resin for trafficking between
2023 and 2025. [@1] Prosecutors also said he promoted large quantities of
cannabis resin and amphetamine. [@1] The first defendant gave assistance to the
second in transporting two consignments of cannabis resin, and both men jointly
received, transported and delivered three cartons of pregabalin pills. [@1]

In a separate case, the same court reviewed the exhibits in the trial of 25
defendants accused of trafficking, promoting and using drugs, including samples
of methamphetamine (shabu) and cannabis resin, pregabalin ampoules and weighing
and consumption equipment. [@1]
