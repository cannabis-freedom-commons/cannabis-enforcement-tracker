A woman from Tamil Nadu was arrested after smuggling ganja on a flight from
Thailand to Chennai. [@1]

A private passenger flight from Bangkok, the capital of Thailand, reached
Chennai on 11 November 2026. [@1] Airport customs officers were carrying out
routine checks of the passengers who arrived on that flight. [@1] The woman had
gone to Bangkok on a tourist trip and was on her way back. [@1] Officers grew
suspicious of her movements. [@1] They stopped her and questioned her. [@1] She
did not cooperate properly. [@1] Female officers then took her for questioning.
[@1]

They scanned the luggage she had brought with her. [@1] Seven packets looked
suspicious. [@1] The seven packets had been concealed in the luggage. [@2] When
the packets were opened, they held processed high-grade ganja. [@1] The cannabis
weighed 1.7 kilograms. [@1] Its international value was Rs 1.70 crore. [@1]

Customs officers seized the cannabis and were continuing to question her. [@1]
Officers were investigating whom the ganja was meant for, whether an
international smuggling gang was involved, and whether anyone else was
connected. [@2]
