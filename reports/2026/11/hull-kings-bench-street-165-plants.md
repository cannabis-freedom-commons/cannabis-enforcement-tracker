The Kinetic Intervention Team forced entry to a residence on Kings Bench Street
in Hull at approximately 7.20am on 2 November. [@1,2] Officers had executed a
drugs warrant at the Hull property. [@1] Inside the property, officers
discovered suspected cannabis plants spread across three floors of the building.
[@1,2] Officers estimated the number of suspected plants at 165 in total. [@1,2] The
suspected plants were confiscated from the Hull property. [@1] After the raid,
enquiries were continuing to identify the individuals connected to the suspected
cannabis operation, with no arrests named. [@1,2]

The operation was conducted under the Misuse of Drugs Act. [@1,2] Police continued
to encourage community members to report any concerns or suspicions about
drug-related crime, emphasising the importance of public information in aiding
evidence collection and enforcement actions such as warrants. [@1] Residents
with information were asked to contact their local police officers or call the
non-emergency number 101, and to call 999 in the event of an ongoing crime. [@1]
