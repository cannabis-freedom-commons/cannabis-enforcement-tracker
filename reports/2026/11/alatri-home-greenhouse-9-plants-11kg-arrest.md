Carabinieri arrested a man from Alatri in flagrante on suspicion of cultivating
and possessing drugs for dealing. [@1] After formalities at the barracks he was
taken home and placed under house arrest as ordered by the public prosecutor at
the Frosinone court, which is coordinating the investigation. [@1] The case is
at the preliminary-investigation stage and the suspect is presumed not guilty
until any final conviction. [@1]

In his home officers found a greenhouse with nine marijuana plants of various
heights. [@1] Officers seized 11 kilograms of dried marijuana inflorescences
kept in many vases and boxes, plus 4.60 grams of hashish. [@1] The greenhouse
held four high-yield heat lamps, two fans, an automated irrigation system,
dehumidifiers and specific fertilizers to speed vegetative growth. [@2] Officers
also seized a precision electronic scale, material for dividing and packaging
doses and 360 euros in cash believed to be dealing proceeds. [@1] Officers also
seized handwritten notes with figures and names now under investigator review,
and placed the drugs, cash and the whole cultivation structure under criminal
seizure. [@1]

The operation was run by the operational section of the N.O.R.M. of the Alatri
company with specialist support from the Carabinieri aircraft unit at Roma Urbe
as part of drug-traffic prevention and suppression work. [@1] Joint action by
ground patrols and helicopter aerial reconnaissance led to an immediate house
search on the officers' initiative. [@1] The man was already listed in police
databases. [@1] The raid took place on the afternoon of 11 November, run by the
operational section of the N.O.R.M. of the Alatri company with support from the
Carabinieri aircraft unit at Roma Urbe. [@3]
