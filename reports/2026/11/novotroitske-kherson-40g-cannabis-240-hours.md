The Novotroitske Inter-District Court of Kherson Oblast convicted a 37-year-old
resident of the Novotroitske district under part 1 of article 228 of the Russian
Criminal Code. [@2] The regional prosecutor's press service said he was found
guilty of possessing banned substances in significant amount without intent to
sell. [@1] The court, taking into account the state prosecutor's opinion,
sentenced him to 240 hours of compulsory labour, unpaid work ordered as a
criminal punishment. [@2] The verdict has entered into legal force. [@1]

The court found that in early June 2026 the man discovered six bushes of wild
cannabis containing narcotics on his property in Novotroitske, an urban-type
settlement in Kherson Oblast. [@2] He cut the plants, dried them and hid them in
the attic of his house for his own use. [@1]

Police stopped his activity in August 2026 and seized about 40 grams of
narcotics. [@2] Officers found and seized the drug during a search. [@1]
