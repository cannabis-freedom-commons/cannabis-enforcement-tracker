Chennai police stopped a woman and a young man on a moped during a vehicle check
near the junction of Pachai Salai, the Greenways Road, and Box Road in
Abiramapuram on Tuesday, 3 November 2026, and questioned them. [@1] Officers
searched the moped and the bag they were carrying. [@1] The search found 400
narcotic pills and one kilogram of ganja, which police seized. [@1]

Police identified the pair as Manikandan, 31, of Paruva Nagar in Teynampet, and
Subbulakshmi, also called Suppu, 29, of Raja Annamalai Puram. [@1] Police
arrested the two and registered a case. [@1] Subbulakshmi has three pending
cases, one of them a narcotic drugs case. [@1]
