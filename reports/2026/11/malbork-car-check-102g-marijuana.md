Detectives of the Malbork district police criminal department stopped a Skoda
driven by a 43-year-old woman for a road check. [@1] The check established that
the woman had revoked driving rights. [@1] The stop uncovered two violations of
the law in the same car. [@2] Officers detained the 36-year-old male passenger
and brought him to the police unit. [@1] The Malbork district prosecutor charged
the 36-year-old with possessing significant quantities of intoxicating
substances. [@1] The prosecutor placed the man under police supervision and set
property bail of 5,000 zloty. [@1]

The passenger carried 102 grams of marijuana together with more than 105 grams
of amphetamine. [@1] Together the drugs in his possession exceeded 200 grams.
[@1] Officers sent the seized substances for further testing. [@1]

The 43-year-old woman driving the car will also answer before a court. [@2] She
drove despite revoked rights to drive mechanical vehicles, an offence punishable
by up to two years of imprisonment. [@1] Possessing significant quantities of
intoxicating substances is punishable by up to 10 years of imprisonment. [@1] A
court will decide the detained man's further fate. [@1]
