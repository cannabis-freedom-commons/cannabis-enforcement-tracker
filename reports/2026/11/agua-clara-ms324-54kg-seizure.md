An enforcement operation was recorded on Wednesday 4 November 2026 in Água Clara
after an anonymous tip triggered a police operation. [@1] The case began with
the stop of a driver suspected of trafficking in the Jardim das Palmeiras
neighbourhood and led to a hideout with about 54 kilograms of cannabis. [@1]
Military Police had received information that a man was selling drugs using a
white VW Gol. [@1] Officers located the car, but the driver tried to flee when
he saw the patrol vehicle approach. [@1] The suspect was caught after a pursuit
lasting several blocks. [@1]

Inside the car officers found a portion of a cocaine-like substance, which the
driver said he had bought for R$250 to divide into portions and resell. [@1] A
search of the suspect's home found portions of cannabis, a precision scale,
drug-packaging materials and R$220 in cash. [@1] After being taken to the police
station, the suspect provided information about another hidden quantity of
drugs. [@1]

A joint Military Police and Civil Police task force then found two other people
involved at a snack bar. [@1] Those two people pointed to a wooded area near
highway MS-324, where officers found 59 tablets of a cannabis-like substance
weighing about 54 kilograms. [@1] The driver and the two other people were taken
for legal procedures and presented to the Água Clara Civil Police station. [@1]
