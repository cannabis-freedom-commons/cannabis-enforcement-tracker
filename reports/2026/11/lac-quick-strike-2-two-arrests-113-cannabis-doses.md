Lezha police arrested 23-year-old Vanes Ruçi and 36-year-old Agron Gjetdoçaj,
both residents of Laç in Kurbin, on suspicion of selling narcotics in doses
mainly near schools. [@2] Officers caught the two men red-handed near a school
in the city of Laç. [@1]

During physical searches of the men and searches of their homes, police found
and seized 126 suspected doses of narcotics. [@2] Police broke the total down as
113 doses of suspected cannabis and 13 doses of suspected cocaine. [@1] Officers
also seized combat ammunition and two mobile phones as material evidence. [@1]

Police said the arrests completed the operation codenamed Quick Strike 2,
finalized within the Clean Schools operational plan to prevent and crack down on
drug distribution in the security perimeters of educational institutions. [@1]
Preliminary investigations suspect the two men were selling narcotics in the
city of Laç, mainly near the school security perimeter. [@1] The procedural
materials were referred to the Prosecutor's Office at the Court of General
Jurisdiction of Lezha for further action. [@1]
