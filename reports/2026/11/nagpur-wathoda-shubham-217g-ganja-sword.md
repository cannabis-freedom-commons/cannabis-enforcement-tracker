Nagpur's Crime Branch Unit-1 arrested an externed criminal who was barred from
entering the city, in a rented house in Azad Nagar under Wathoda Police Station
limits. [@1] Police identified him as Shubham alias Limbu Limbudkar, 27, a
resident of Vijaynagar, Pakhkhide Layout, Hingna Road. [@1] He had been externed
under the Maharashtra Police Act and was wanted in a serious offence registered
at MIDC Police Station. [@1] The team acted on a tip-off that the externed man
wanted in the MIDC case was staying in the rented house in Azad Nagar. [@1]
Police Inspector Prashant Thavre guided the team that raided the premises and
found Shubham Limbudkar there. [@2]

Officers recovered 217 grams of ganja, which police valued at about ₹10,850.
[@1] Police also seized a packet of plastic wrappers, an electronic weighing
scale, a sword and a mobile phone. [@1]

He was wanted in cases involving assault, rioting, offences under the Arms Act
and other serious charges registered at MIDC Police Station. [@1] Crime Branch
Unit-1 detained him with the seized materials and handed him over to Wathoda
Police for further legal action. [@1]
