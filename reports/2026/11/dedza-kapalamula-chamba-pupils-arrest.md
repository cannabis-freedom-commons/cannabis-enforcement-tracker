Police in Dedza arrested a 46-year-old man on suspicion of supplying chamba to
pupils at Kapalamula Primary School. [@1] The suspect was identified as Mayamiko
Empraim, who is alleged to have supplied the drug to three learners aged between
13 and 15. [@1] The drug was described as cannabis sativa, the botanical name
for chamba. [@1]

Empraim was in police custody and was expected to appear in court soon to answer
a charge of being found in possession of cannabis sativa. [@1] Dedza Police
Station public relations officer Sergeant Beatrice Jefita said the matter came
to light after a community policing outreach programme at the school, where
officers taught learners about the dangers of drug and substance abuse. [@1]
After the programme, one learner told the head teacher that some pupils were
smoking the drug. [@1] The head teacher reported the matter to the police. [@1]
Police investigated the report and made the arrest. [@1]

Empraim comes from Kapalamula Village in Traditional Authority Kachere in Dedza
District. [@1]
