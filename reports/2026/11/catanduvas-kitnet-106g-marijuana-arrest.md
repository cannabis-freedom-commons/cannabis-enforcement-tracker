A 40-year-old man was arrested for drug trafficking after military police
searched a kitnet on Avenida Paraná in Catanduvas, western Paraná, on the night
of Tuesday 10 November 2026. [@1] The operation began at about 8:08 pm, when a
military police team working with the ROTAM team of the 2nd Company received
reports of drug sales from kitnets on the avenue. [@4]

Officers approached the man at the corner of Avenida Paraná and Rua 25 de Julho
after seeing him leave one of the units. [@3] A personal search found 4.7 grams
of cocaine and a key. [@3] He said the key belonged to the kitnet he had left
and allowed the officers inside. [@1] Two other men who arrived at the property
were searched and released after nothing illegal was found with them. [@1]

In the empty unit, officers found a further 78.4 grams of cocaine and 106 grams
of marijuana, along with two precision scales, foil, plastic film and three HT
radios. [@1] They also seized seven .22-calibre rounds, R$446.50 in cash, a
notebook with notes on trafficking and a replica firearm. [@1] In total, police
seized 83.1 grams of cocaine and 106 grams of marijuana. [@3] The man was
arrested and taken to the Central de Flagrantes, the police station that handles
arrests in the act, for formal procedures. [@1] The operation was handled by
teams of the 5th Regional Military Police Command through the 6th Military
Police Battalion. [@2]
