Oskar Benes is a Czech man who set up a network of cannabis grow houses across
Czechia. [@1] Europol lists him as wanted by the Czech Republic for illicit
trafficking in narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances. [@2] For these
activities he was sentenced to 12 years in prison. [@2] In 2023 he escaped from
custody in Portugal and remains a wanted fugitive. [@2]

As the organizer, he provided real estate in the Czech Republic where cannabis
plant nurseries were established and operated at least since the beginning of
2013. [@2] He supplied vehicles for marijuana transport, cannabis seedlings and
grow-house equipment, and coordinated marijuana deliveries and transfers of
small cannabis plants, including shipments to couriers abroad. [@1] He accepted
payments for the marijuana sold and in some cases personally delivered marijuana
to buyers. [@2]

TN.cz counted him in November 2026 among 16 Czechs sought through Interpol and
Europol, with Europol seeking one Czech. [@1] In December 2025 Novinky described
him as newly listed by Europol among Europe's most wanted as the organizer of
large-scale marijuana production and sale. [@3]
