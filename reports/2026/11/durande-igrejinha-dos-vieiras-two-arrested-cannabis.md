Military Police arrested two men, aged 25 and 30, for drug trafficking on Sunday
8 November 2026 in Córrego Igrejinha dos Vieiras, in the rural zone of Durandé,
Minas Gerais. [@1] Police said the operation followed information that drugs
were being sold in the area. [@1] Officers stopped a car that the information
said was used to carry drugs, and found drugs and money in the vehicle. [@1]

The team also searched the home of another man involved in the case, where they
found more drugs and a round of ammunition. [@1] In all, officers seized 24
cocaine packets and three portions of cannabis, along with a .40-calibre round
of ammunition. [@1] Police also took 196 reais in cash, three mobile phones and
four pots used to store drugs. [@1]

The suspects' car was seized and taken to a licensed impound yard. [@1] Both men
were arrested and taken with the seized material to the Civil Police station in
Manhuaçu. [@1] The regional outlet Tribuna do Leste carried the same account,
with the same ages, quantities and the same Manhuaçu police station as the
destination. [@2]
