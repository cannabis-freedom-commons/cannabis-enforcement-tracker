In Faridabad, the Crime Branch Sector-16 team arrested Sameer Khan, a young man
living at Ashiana Apartments in Sector-56, with 715 grams of ganja, the local
term for cannabis. [@1] According to police, the team acted on secret
information on 2 November, caught Khan near the Ashiana flats in Sector-56 and
recovered 715 grams of ganja from his possession. [@1]

Police registered a case over the recovery under the Narcotic Drugs and
Psychotropic Substances Act, generally called the NDPS Act and India's main
anti-drug law, at Sector-58 police station. [@1] Police said Khan already faced
earlier cases registered against him under the same NDPS law before this arrest.
[@1] After the arrest, officers produced him before a court, where further
action was taken under the rules in the case. [@1]

During questioning, Khan told police that he had brought one kilogram of ganja
from Kosi. [@1] He said he had bought that consignment in Kosi for 5,000 rupees.
