Military police of the 2nd Integrated Specialized Battalion (BIEsp) received
information that narcotics were being sold in the locality. [@1] When they
reached the address given in the tip, they saw an individual who managed to flee
on noticing their approach. [@1] A second account says police went to the
indicated address after information about drug sales and saw an individual who
fled on their approach. [@2] No one was arrested. [@1]

More than 15 kg of marijuana was seized at night on Monday (2) near Projeto N4
in the rural zone of Petrolina, in the Sertão of Pernambuco. [@1] During a sweep
of the area, officers found a bag containing 15,590kg of the herb and a
.22-calibre rifle. [@1] A second outlet reports the same sweep found a bag with
15,590 quilos of marijuana and a .22-calibre rifle. [@2] That account adds that
the suspect was not located. [@2] The seized material was taken to Petrolina's
duty police station for the applicable legal measures. [@2]

The material was located by 2nd BIEsp teams during an action against the
commercialization of drugs in the region. [@2]
