Metro Nashville police say two men involved in a mid-October shootout on Hickory
Hollow Terrace are now in custody, with a third man also charged when officers
located one shooting suspect. [@1] Police say Calvin Howse Jr., 22, met Terrance
Martin, 24, to buy marijuana on Hickory Hollow Terrace. [@1] The two allegedly
argued and then exchanged gunfire. [@1] Howse was shot in the mouth and
hospitalized, while Martin fled. [@1] On Tuesday, police located Martin at a
Bell Road gas station, where he was a passenger in a vehicle driven by Paul
Cowling, 22. [@1] Cowling attempted to run and discarded a firearm but was
quickly arrested. [@1] Howse was arrested after his hospital discharge this week
on firearm and marijuana charges. [@1]

Officers recovered the discarded gun, a pistol allegedly possessed by Martin and
more than 200 grams of marijuana from the vehicle. [@1] Martin is charged with
attempted criminal homicide and firearm and drug offenses, plus violating bond
conditions tied to domestic-violence charges. [@1] Martin remains jailed on a
$278,000 bond. [@1] Cowling faces firearm and drug charges, with bond set at
$20,000. [@1] Howse remains jailed on an $80,000 bond. [@1] The shooting victim
was found with a gunshot wound to the mouth and taken to a local hospital. [@2]
Police said the wounded man is expected to survive. [@2]

Police say the shooting happened in a parking lot in the Hickory Hollow Parkway
area after the victim and another man argued. [@2] Two women were previously
charged with tampering with evidence in connection with the shooting. [@1] The
two women facing evidence-tampering charges are Karma Day and Braylee McDonald,
who investigators say were in the area during the shooting. [@2] McDonald later
admitted picking up a black firearm magazine and throwing it into a wooded area
that detectives searched without finding it. [@2] Warrants had been issued for
the hospitalized victim's arrest on weapon allegations once released from
hospital. [@2]
