The Jayawijaya district prosecutor's office in Papua Pegunungan destroyed case
evidence with final court judgments, including 868.75 grams of cannabis, at its
office in Wamena on Thursday 5 November 2026. [@1] The destroyed cannabis
weighed 868.75 grams. [@1] The same destruction also covered 44.12 grams of
sabu, crystal methamphetamine, which is not counted in the cannabis figure. [@1]
Officers also destroyed alcohol, sharp weapons, clothing and cosmetics seized in
other criminal cases. [@1]

Head prosecutor Ahmad Latupono said the narcotics evidence came from 28 cases
handled to date. [@1] He said the evidence came from cases handled by police in
Jayawijaya, Nduga, Yalimo, Mamberamo Tengah and Tolikara, all in Papua
Pegunungan. [@1] He said the destroyed evidence covered cases from 2025 to
October 2026 that had obtained final legal force. [@1] He said every item
destroyed came from a case with a final court decision whose defendant had
already been sentenced. [@1]

Latupono said this was the office's first evidence destruction this year. [@1]
He said cases were collected and executed together rather than one by one to
save costs. [@1] Police, local government, Wamena Class IIB prison and other
stakeholders attended as officials burned the evidence in the office courtyard.
[@1] Jayawijaya regional government expert staffer Natalis Mumpu thanked the
prosecutors, police and courts, saying firm handling of narcotics and other
crimes gives residents safety and peace. [@1]
