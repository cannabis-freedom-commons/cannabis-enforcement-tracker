Nigel White was sentenced on 9 November 2026 to 18 years in prison for his role
in the marijuana deal on Berner Street in Green Bay that ended in the shooting
death of Brandon Ramirez. [@2] Brown County Judge Beau Liegeois also placed
White, 24, on 10 years of extended supervision, a form of supervision after
release from prison. [@2] WBAY reported that the 18 years is initial
confinement, followed by 10 years of extended supervision, with credit for 777
days already served. [@1]

Ramirez was killed on 7 August 2024, and prosecutors said White drove a getaway
vehicle, while co-defendant Dion Richardson, the shooter, was sentenced to 25
years. [@2] Richardson told police he had arranged to buy a half-pound of
marijuana from Ramirez for $430. [@2] Ramirez died of a gunshot wound to the
head, according to the autopsy cited in the criminal complaint. [@2]

White pleaded no contest in August 2026 to felony murder, obstruction and bail
jumping, and four other counts were dismissed. [@3] White is one of five people
charged in the case; Darian Pinchart was sentenced to 15 years and Elijah White
to 12 years, while Rosemary Williams has a plea hearing set for 7 December. [@2]
Before sentencing White apologised to Ramirez's family, and statements from
Ramirez's mother and other relatives were read in court. [@2]
