Soldiers of the Mexican Army, National Guard members and municipal police
officers detained a suspected street-level drug dealer during a joint operation
in the locality of San Vicente, in the municipality of Bahía de Banderas,
Nayarit. [@1] State police officers also took part in the surveillance patrol
alongside the municipal and federal forces. [@2] Officers spotted a man wearing
navy blue jeans, a black shirt and sandals who was acting in a way they
considered suspicious. [@1] The man tried to flee when he saw the authorities,
but officers caught up with him. [@2] Seeing his evasive behaviour, the officers
ordered him to stop and carried out a preventive inspection. [@2]

Searching his belongings, officers found a white bag containing six doses of
marijuana and 15 packets of a substance with characteristics similar to crystal,
a methamphetamine known in Mexico as cristal, along with a blue mobile phone.
[@1] The six cannabis wrappers held dry green plant material resembling
marijuana, while the other 15 packets contained a granulated substance, making
21 doses in total. [@2] Authorities said the six doses of marijuana and 15
wrappers of crystal were allegedly intended for sale. [@1]

Following the discovery, the man was arrested and handed over to a public
prosecutor (Ministerio Público) to determine his legal status for probable
liability in offences against health. [@1] He and the seized substances were
transferred immediately to the Attorney General's Office (FGR), the federal
prosecutor's office tasked with conducting the investigation. [@2] The
participating forces said such coordinated operations are part of security
measures in Bahía de Banderas to combat narcomenudeo, or street-level drug
dealing, and keep drugs off the municipality's streets. [@1] Authorities
described the operation as part of ongoing prevention and surveillance patrols
carried out by municipal, state and federal agencies across different parts of
the municipality. [@2]
