Bahía Blanca's Criminal Court No. 3 sentenced María José Dupak to 4 years and 2
months in prison for selling cocaine and marijuana in split doses, with
deliveries arranged in advance at various points in the city. [@1] The guilty
verdict was handed down in a plea bargain proceeding that proved the drug sales
took place between 7 November 2024 and 7 February 2025. [@1]

The case began when Bahía Blanca's monitoring centre (CeÚM) alerted the patrol
command about suspicious movements in the first block of Montevideo street. [@1]
Cameras had recorded a woman in a jacket and jeans handing an object to a man in
a pink T-shirt, who afterwards showed her his phone. [@1] Officers seized from
the buyer a nylon wrapper with cocaine and a mobile phone, while Dupak (36) had
marijuana and also a mobile phone that was confiscated. [@1] One of the
operations that advanced the case occurred on 7 February 2025, when officers
found marijuana inside a metal box the accused was carrying in her handbag, in
the first block of Montevideo street. [@2]

The investigation was led by the drugs prosecutor Mauricio Del Cero, heading
Functional Unit No. 19. [@1] The prosecution proved earlier sales and
established that on 7 February 2025 the woman carried marijuana for sale. [@1]
In 2019 Dupak had been detained for the same offence. [@1]
