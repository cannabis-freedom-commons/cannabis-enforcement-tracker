Between January and September 2026, seizures in the Apucarana region reached
10.3 tonnes of marijuana, up from 4.9 tonnes in the same period of 2025. [@1]
The region's marijuana seizure figures rose 109.8% year on year, according to
the Cape statistics centre of the Parana public security secretariat (Sesp).
[@1]

Most of the year's total came from a Federal Highway Police (Polícia Rodoviária
Federal, PRF) interception of 6.8 tonnes of marijuana on the BR-376 in Apucarana
on 23 September 2026. [@1] The PRF said the drug was found while officers
checked the semi-trailers and weighed at 6.8 tonnes. [@2] The driver was taken
with the load to the Civil Police station in Apucarana. [@2]

In the 17th regional security division, which covers the region, seizures of
marijuana plants fell from 28 units in 2025 to five, with one plant unit found
in Apucarana municipality against ten the year before. [@1] The same balance
shows hashish seizures of 14 kg in the region in 2025 and none in the period
analysed this year. [@1] Cocaine paste seizures in the region rose from zero to
49 kg, while crack fell from 88 kg to 2 kg and ready-to-use cocaine from 32 kg
to 8 kg, all outside the cannabis figures. [@1]
