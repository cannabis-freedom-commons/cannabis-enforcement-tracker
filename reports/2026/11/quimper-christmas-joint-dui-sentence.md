On 4 November 2026 the judicial court in Quimper sentenced a 28-year-old man
from Quimper to two months of firm prison for repeat driving under the influence
of cannabis. [@1] He had first denied the offence and then admitted it at the 4
November hearing. [@1] Prosecutor Philippe Lemoine had requested two months of
firm prison and cancellation of the driving licence with a 12-month wait before
retaking it, and presiding judge Noélie Carval followed those requests. [@1]

Gendarmes had stopped the man at 4:20 a.m. on 1 January 2026 at a toll in the
commune of Voreppe in Isère while he was on holiday with his family. [@1] His
licence was immediately retained because he tested positive for cannabis. [@1]

He first said he had only used CBD and then admitted that he had smoked a joint
for Christmas. [@1] He told the court he had stopped cannabis with the help of
CBD in 2022 but relapsed at Christmas by smoking a joint with friends, which
ruined his holidays, and he expressed regret for his behaviour. [@1]
