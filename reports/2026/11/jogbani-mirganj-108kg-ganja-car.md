A joint team of the Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB), India's border guard, and Bihar
Police found an overturned, abandoned Toyota car near Mirganj bridge in the
Mirganj area of Jogbani, close to the India-Nepal border. [@1]

Officers searched the vehicle and recovered 108 kilograms of ganja packed inside
it. [@2] A testing kit was used to confirm that the material was a narcotic
substance. [@2] The seized ganja and the car, registration number BR 33 PA 9038,
were handed over to Jogbani police station. [@2] The Araria district police gave
the same figure, saying 108 kilograms of ganja were seized from a vehicle on the
Mirganj–Kochgama road during the joint operation. [@3]

Nitin Kumar Gupta, second-in-command of the SSB's 56th Battalion, said the team
acted on information received at about 9 am on Thursday, 5 November, that a
Toyota had overturned near border pillar 179/02 with drugs inside. [@2] The
officers found no one with the car, and neither account reports any arrest. [@1]
The SSB post at Jogbani and local police carried out the operation within the
SSB's 56th Battalion, based at Bathnaha. [@2]
