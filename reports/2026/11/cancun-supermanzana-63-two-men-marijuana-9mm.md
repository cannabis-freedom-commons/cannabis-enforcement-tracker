Cancún municipal police arrested Carlos Manuel “N”, 23, and Gerardo “N”, 50, in
Supermanzana 63 on 5 November 2026 on suspicion of drug dealing on a small
scale, which in Mexico is prosecuted as narcomenudeo. [@1] The arrests happened
on 5 November during the Blindaje Cancún operation, according to the police
statement published that day. [@2] The operation was run in coordination with
the Secretariat of National Defence, the Secretariat of the Navy, the National
Guard and the Quintana Roo state prosecutor's office. [@1]

Officers seized several small bags of green, dry plant material with
characteristics similar to marijuana. [@1] They also seized several small bags
of a granular substance with characteristics of crystal, a methamphetamine,
which is not counted here as cannabis. [@2] Agents found a short 9 mm firearm
with a magazine and five cartridges. [@3] The police report gave no weight or
dose count for the suspected marijuana, and no price for it. [@4]

Both men and the seized items were handed to the state prosecutor's office,
which will decide their legal situation. [@2]
