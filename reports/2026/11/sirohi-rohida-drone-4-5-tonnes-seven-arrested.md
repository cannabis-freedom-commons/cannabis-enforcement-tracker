Police in Sirohi district used drones to find cannabis cultivation hidden in
remote hills and forests, and seized a large number of plants after reaching the
fields on the drones' GPS coordinates. [@1] Growers had planted the cannabis
among maize, castor and other standing crops to conceal it. [@1]

Police seized about 4.5 tonnes of cannabis plants under Operation Trinetra and
arrested seven suspects. [@2] Police said they had been receiving continuing
information that ganja was being supplied in Sirohi, but could not find where it
was grown. [@2]

Drone teams were formed under Pindwara circle officer Rahul Meena, and they
began searching the hills for the fields on informers' tip-offs. [@2] Police
used a high-range drone to search the distant hills and forests and small drones
to watch suspect spots closely, then sent teams to the GPS locations to identify
the growers. [@2] Police said traffickers had turned the Rohida hills into a
safe site for cannabis cultivation. [@2] In the Rohida hills the plants were
grown among maize, castor, fennel and bariali, a local fodder crop. [@2]

Police said they would question the arrested suspects about the cultivation and
supply network and look for other people involved and where the ganja was sent.
[@2]
