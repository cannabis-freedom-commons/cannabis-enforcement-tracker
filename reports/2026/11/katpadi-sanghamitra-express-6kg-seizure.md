Officers of the Vellore district anti-drug unit searched the front and rear
general coaches of the Sanghamitra Express after it arrived at Katpadi railway
station. [@1] The team was led by Sub-Inspector Karthik Selvam and moved after
receiving information that drugs were being smuggled on that train. [@1] In the
rear coach, officers found a bag lying unclaimed under a seat and took
possession of it after passengers in the coach said they knew nothing about it.
[@1] No owner of the bag was identified and no arrest was reported at the scene.
[@1]

On opening the bag, police found packets holding 6 kilos of ganja, the term used
in Tamil Nadu for cannabis, and seized the consignment. [@1] The 6-kilo figure
is the police count from the packets in the single recovered bag. [@1]

Police said the train runs from Danapur in Bihar to Bengaluru in Karnataka. [@1]
The train searched had arrived at Katpadi two days before the report. [@1]
Police registered a case over the seizure and were investigating further. [@1]
