Police sources told Pilar de Todos that officers of the Grupo Táctico Operativo
(GTO), a police tactical unit, arrested a 34-year-old woman at a house on Calle
Lima in José C. Paz, a municipality in the northwest of Greater Buenos Aires.
[@2] The woman is Argentine and was imputed with tenencia de estupefacientes
para su comercialización, possession of narcotics for sale. [@1]

Officers seized 30 wrappings in all. [@1] Fourteen black nylon wrappings held a
vegetable substance compatible with marijuana, 32 g in total. [@1] The other 16,
of green nylon, held 3.66 g of a substance compatible with cocaine, a weight the
cannabis figure excludes. [@1] Officers also seized a TCL mobile phone and
28,800 pesos in cash. [@2]

The prosecutor of UFI No. 16 of the San Martín judicial department ordered the
case file opened, the woman's arrest and the seizure of the substances and other
items, after being briefed on the procedure. [@1] The case remained at the
imputation stage. [@1]
