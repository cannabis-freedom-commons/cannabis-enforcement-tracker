Balıkesir İl Jandarma Komutanlığı, the provincial gendarmerie command, carried
out narcotics operations across 12 districts of Balıkesir province targeting
drug manufacturing and trade. [@1] Officers linked the sweep to 62 narcotics
incidents and caught 75 suspects. [@1] Judicial proceedings were started against
all 75 suspects, with no detention or judicial-control outcome reported. [@1]

Officers seized 21 kilograms 919 grams of esrar, the term the source uses for
cannabis. [@1] They also seized 620 grams of skunk, six rooted skunk plants and
189 grams of cannabis seeds. [@1] The same searches also found 70 grams of
bonzai, 112 grams of methamphetamine, 5 grams of cocaine and 272 synthetic drug
pills, which are not counted in the cannabis total. [@1] Officers also seized
three precision scales, 19 drug-use implements, two mobile phones and 57,000
Turkish lira described as suspected crime proceeds. [@1]

The 12 districts were Altıeylül, Ayvalık, Bandırma, Burhaniye, Dursunbey,
Edremit, Gönen, Havran, İvrindi, Karesi, Sındırgı and Susurluk. [@1] Searches
targeted suspects’ homes, outbuildings and vehicles identified through
intelligence work. [@1] The account presents the sweep as covering a two-week
period and gives no breakdown of suspects or seizures by district or address.
[@1] The gendarmerie said intelligence work and operations against drug offences
would continue. [@1]
