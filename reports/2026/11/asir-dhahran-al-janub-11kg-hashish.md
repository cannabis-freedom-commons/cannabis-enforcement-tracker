Saudi Border Guard land patrols in the Dhahran al-Janub sector of Asir region
foiled an attempt to smuggle 11 kg of hashish. [@1] The same interception
stopped 14,800 tablets under medical-trade controls and 2,950 amphetamine
tablets. [@1] The Border Guard's announcement was published on 6 November 2026.
[@1]

Sabq reported the same Dhahran al-Janub operation, giving the hashish as 11
kilograms, with 14,800 tablets under medical-trade controls and 2,950
amphetamine tablets. [@2] In a second operation announced at the same time,
patrols in Asir region arrested four violators of the border security law
accused of smuggling 80 kg of khat plant, a stimulant leaf that is not cannabis.
[@2]

Sabq said initial legal procedures were completed against those detained and
they were handed with the seizures to the competent authority for the penalties
set by law. [@2] The Border Guard asked citizens and residents to report
smuggling through its 994 line, the anti-narcotics directorate's 995 line and
995@gdnc.gov.sa, 911 in Mecca, Medina, Riyadh and the Eastern Province, and 999
elsewhere, promising confidentiality. [@2]
