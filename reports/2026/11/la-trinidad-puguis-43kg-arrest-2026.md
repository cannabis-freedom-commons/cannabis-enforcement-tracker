In Puguis, La Trinidad, Benguet, police arrested a 21-year-old construction
worker on his birthday on the evening of 5 November 2026 after catching him
transporting marijuana. [@1,2,3] The suspect is a native of Santo Cristo, Guagua,
Pampanga. [@1,3] He attempted to flee upon noticing the police but was
apprehended, while the passengers of the larger vehicle managed to escape. [@1,2,3]
He remains in police custody and is expected to face charges for violating
Republic Act 9165, the Comprehensive Dangerous Drugs Act of 2002. [@1,3]

Patrolling officers from the La Trinidad Municipal Police Station spotted two
parked vehicles and noticed a man transferring boxes from a larger vehicle into
the trunk of a car. [@1,3] The officers saw a tubular-shaped object inside the
containers. [@1] Authorities confiscated approximately 43 kilograms of marijuana
valued at more than ₱5 million. [@1,2,3] Officers also seized two vehicles, vehicle
keys and a wallet containing various identification cards from the suspect. [@1,3]

The larger vehicle’s passengers managed to escape while the suspect was
apprehended at the scene. [@1]
