Police in Lesozavodsk, in Russia's Primorsky Krai, found a cache of marijuana in
a forest near the village of Innokentyevka and detained the 48-year-old local
man who owned it. [@1,4] The man is a resident of Lesozavodsk who had a previous
criminal record. [@1,4] Officers of the drug-control unit of the Lesozavodsk
police department, МО МВД России "Лесозаводский", ran the operation after
checking operational information. [@3,4]

More than 10 kg of marijuana was taken out of circulation, police said. [@1]
Investigators opened a criminal case under part 2 of article 228 of the Russian
Criminal Code, which covers obtaining and storing drugs in a large amount. [@1,4]
The suspect was detained under article 91 of the Russian Criminal Procedure
Code, the provision for holding a suspect in police custody. [@1,4] The offence
carries up to ten years in prison and a possible fine of up to 500,000 roubles.
[@3]

Primpress reported that the man had long been suspected of drug trafficking and
that the cache was found during a search. [@2]
