A joint team of Rajpur police and the Anti Narcotics Task Force (ANTF),
Uttarakhand's anti-drug task force, arrested a 19-year-old man with charas in
Dehradun on the night of 4 November 2026. [@1] The team was checking the Johri
Road area and stopped the man near an old shrine on a tip-off, and a search
found 1 kg 110 g of charas, the cannabis resin, on him. [@1]

Police identified the arrested man as Ashad, also known as Shubhan Khan, 19, a
resident of Mothorowala on Dun University Road in Nehru Colony. [@1] In
questioning he told police he is studying for a degree at an open university and
bought the charas from a drug trafficker living near Rispana bridge to sell at
high prices to local drug users. [@1] Police said he had been jailed before in
drug-trafficking and other criminal cases and were gathering details of his
criminal history. [@1] A first information report was registered against him at
Rajpur police station, and police are also seeking information about the
trafficker who supplied the charas. [@1]
