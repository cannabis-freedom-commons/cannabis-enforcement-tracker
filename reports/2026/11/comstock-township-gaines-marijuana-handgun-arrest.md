A Kalamazoo County man was arrested on Wednesday in connection with an illegal
drug dealing operation, police said. [@1] Officers from the Kalamazoo Department
of Public Safety arrested 30-year-old Keylonte Rahmaine-Anthoiny Gaines near
Lake Crest Circle in Comstock Township on 4 November 2026. [@1] Members of the
Crime Reduction Team executed a search warrant on Lake Crest Circle as part of
an investigation into the distribution of illegal narcotics. [@2] Investigators
contacted Gaines in front of the residence after he exited his vehicle and took
him into custody without incident. [@2]

During a search of Gaines' car, officers found a loaded gun, prescription pills
and marijuana packaged for sale. [@1] During a search of his home, police found
powder cocaine, crack cocaine and other evidence of drug dealing. [@1]
Investigators also located U.S. currency inside the residence that was believed
to be proceeds of illegal narcotic sales. [@2]

Gaines was lodged at the Kalamazoo County Jail on weapons and illegal drug
distribution offenses. [@2] Police said Gaines was being held on a $250,000
bond. [@1] Authorities asked anyone with a tip about illegal drugs or firearms
to contact the Kalamazoo Valley Enforcement Team or Silent Observer. [@1]
