State police detained 38-year-old Ángel “N” during a surveillance operation in
Colonia El Chabacano, Tenancingo. [@1] The arresting officers were elements of
the Secretaría de Seguridad del Estado de México (SSEM), the State of Mexico
police. [@1] They held him for his probable participation in delitos contra la
salud in the narcomenudeo modality, Mexico's term for street-level drug dealing.
[@1]

Officers were carrying out surveillance rounds in Colonia El Chabacano as part
of Operativo ARGOS, an operation focused on preventing and combating crime. [@1]
They said they approached a man who showed an evasive attitude on seeing them,
and he agreed to a security inspection. [@1] During the search, they found 10
transparent resealable plastic bags containing green, dry herb with
characteristics similar to marijuana among his belongings. [@1]

Officers informed him of the reason for his detention and his rights, then
transferred him to the Justice Centre in Tenancingo together with the seized
evidence. [@1] He was placed at the disposal of the Ministerio Público, the
prosecutor's office responsible for continuing the investigation and determining
his legal situation. [@1]
