Six men aged 23–28 stand trial at the first chamber of the Marburg regional
court, accused of gang-style cannabis trafficking from two indoor plantations;
on the second hearing day, 13 November 2026, police officers testified about the
investigation. [@1]

The prosecution alleges the men ran seven room plantations in an abandoned
building in Rastenberg, Thuringia, where 219 harvest-ready plants, 249
mid-growth plants and 336 seedlings were found, and four room plantations in a
building in Biedenkopf's old town, where 117 harvest-ready plants and 125
seedlings were seized. [@2] Police found 16 kg of packaged cannabis buds and
€23,700 in cash (€7,700 and €16,000) at the residences of two of the men who had
bought the buildings. [@2]

All six defendants have been in pre-trial detention since January 2026, when the
plantations were raided. [@2] Two of the accused, through their lawyers,
admitted they had scouted and prepared the buildings for cultivation but said
they did not know how many plants were ultimately grown or of any specific
trafficking arrangements. [@2] The trial is presided over by judge Wieland
Stötzel, who gave the defendants the word after public prosecutor Christopher
Riedel read out the charges. [@2]
