The Narcotics unit of the Korps Politie Aruba, the island's police force,
carried out the operation. [@1] It was a court-authorised raid on a plot in the
Papilon neighbourhood. [@1] The raid took place on Tuesday 3 November 2026. [@1]
Officers arrested two suspects during the raid. [@1] The arrests were made on
suspicion of importing narcotics and possessing weapons. [@1]

One of those arrested was a 28-year-old man identified only by the initials
L.J.V.P. [@1] The man was born in Colombia. [@1] The other person arrested was a
38-year-old woman identified only by the initials R.W.S.H. [@1] The woman was
born in Suriname. [@1] During the search of the plot, officers seized marijuana
and hashish. [@1] The KPA Arrest Team and its K9 unit took part in the operation
alongside the Narcotics unit. [@1]
