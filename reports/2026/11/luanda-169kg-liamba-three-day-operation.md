Police in Luanda ran crime-prevention and crime-repression operations across the
province from 6 to 8 November 2026. [@2] Luanda provincial police command
spokesman Nestor Goubel gave the figures to O País. [@2]

The National Police seized 169 kilograms of cannabis sativa, known in Angola as
liamba. [@2] The same operations also produced 40 pieces of liamba and 37.45
grams of cocaine, in crack form. [@2] Officers also seized two firearms, an AKM
rifle magazine with ammunition and a machete. [@2] In the same operations in
Luanda province they seized 169 kilograms of cannabis and 40 pieces of a
cannabis product, along with two firearms, an AKM magazine with ammunition and a
machete. [@1]

No arrests, charges or suspects were reported in the operations. [@2]
