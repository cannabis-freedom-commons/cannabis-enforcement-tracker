Panvel taluka police arrested Subhash Dattu Mali with ganja while he was staying
in Panvel taluka despite an externment order against him. [@1,2] Police said Mali
had previously been externed for one year from Thane city, Mumbai, Brihanmumbai,
Navi Mumbai, Raigad and Palghar areas. [@1] Despite that order, he was still
living in Panvel taluka at the time of the arrest. [@1]

The operation took place in the Haji Malang Road area of Bavanje village. [@1]
Police said they acted after receiving information about suspicious movements in
the Haji Malang Road area of Bavanje. [@1] A search found ganja and related
paraphernalia in the accused's possession. [@1] Officers seized 623 grams of
ganja, three chillims and one matchbox from him. [@1,2] Police put the total value
of the seized property at Rs 32,150. [@1]

During questioning, the accused told police that he had brought the seized ganja
from a man named Narayan in Odisha. [@1] The arrest therefore combines an
alleged drug seizure with an alleged violation of the externment order by
remaining in Panvel taluka. [@1]

Police laid a trap at around 7.30 pm on October 30 outside the house of Kachru Katkari in Wavanje village under the guidance of Senior Police Inspector Gajanan Ghadge. [@2]
