Officers of the Department of Narcotics Control arrested 40-year-old Abdul Kader
in Jibannagar with 1 kg of ganja. [@1] The arrest took place in the evening of
Saturday 7 November at Monoharpur Bazar, beside the Monoharpur-Khayerhuda road
in front of Sanjay Pal's grocery shop. [@1] The second account names him as the
son of the late Samir Molla from Mahanagar Uttarpara in Jibannagar town. [@2]

Officers recovered 1 kg of ganja from his possession. [@1] They also seized an
unregistered 125cc motorcycle and a key from him. [@1] The second account
describes the motorcycle as a red 125cc Ignitor used for carrying drugs. [@2]
The first account valued the ganja at about 40,000 taka and the motorcycle at
150,000 taka. [@1] The second account gave the combined estimated value of the
ganja and motorcycle as 190,000 taka. [@2]

Inspector Md Moniruzzaman of the narcotics control office filed a case at
Jibannagar police station over the seizure. [@1] The officer-in-charge of
Jibannagar police station confirmed the incident. [@2] The detainee was sent to
the Chuadanga court on the afternoon of Sunday 8 November. [@2]
