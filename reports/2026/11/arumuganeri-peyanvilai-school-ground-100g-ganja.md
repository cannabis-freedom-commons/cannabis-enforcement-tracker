Arumuganeri police arrested two young men, Tahir Athil, 20, son of Mustaq, and
Jasim Imran, 19, son of Jamal Hajit, both of Kayalpattinam's Kaattu Thaikka
Street, on suspicion of selling ganja near a school. [@1] Officers on routine
patrol saw the two men standing on the school ground at Peyanvilai and chased
them when they tried to run away. [@1]

Police seized 50 grams of ganja from each of them, 100 grams in total, and found
20 ziplock covers on each of the two men. [@1] Arumuganeri police registered a
case and remanded the two men to Perurani jail after producing them before a
court. [@1]
