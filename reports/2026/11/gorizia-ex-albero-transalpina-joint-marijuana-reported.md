Carabinieri of the Gorizia company checked the disused Albero alla Transalpina
building in via Giuseppe Caprin, in Gorizia, on the afternoon of Tuesday 10
November 2026. [@1] The check was part of the force's inspections of abandoned
buildings in the city, run to prevent urban decay and drug use. [@1] Officers of
the Sezione Radiomobile, drawn by a strong pungent smell from the upper floors,
reached the second floor, where they found a 37-year-old man lying on an
improvised mattress and smoking a joint. [@1]

Analyses confirmed that the substance beside him was marijuana, in a quantity
exceeding two grams, in addition to the joint he already held. [@1] The drug was
placed under administrative seizure and the man was reported to the Prefecture
of Gorizia for possession of drugs for personal use. [@1] The man was taken to
the company command for checks, and both the marijuana and the joint were seized
administratively. [@2] Il Piccolo reports the total of more than two grams as
covering the package and the hand-rolled cigarette together. [@3]
