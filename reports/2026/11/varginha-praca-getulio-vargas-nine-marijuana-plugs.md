Military police arrested a 23-year-old man in flagrante on the late afternoon of
Tuesday 3 November 2026, suspected of selling drugs in Praça Getúlio Vargas in
central Varginha. [@1] An anonymous tip indicated that an individual was selling
narcotics in the square and using the local tree planters as a depot to hide the
illicit material. [@1] Officers went to the square on the basis of the
description in the tip and found the young man there. [@1]

A personal search found R$ 55.00 in cash and a mobile phone on the suspect. [@1]
Searching vegetation around the approach, officers found nine plugs of marijuana
and six small packets of cocaine in the planter of a nearby tree. [@2] The young
man was arrested at the scene, taken for medical care and then taken to the
Varginha Civil Police station, where the incident and the seized material were
presented for legal measures. [@1]
