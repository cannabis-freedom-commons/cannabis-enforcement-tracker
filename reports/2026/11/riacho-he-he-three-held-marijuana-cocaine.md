Officers of the Comisaría Agente Daniel Juárez in Riacho He Hé, Formosa
province, detained three people and seized marijuana, cocaine, precision scales,
mobile phones and other items of interest. [@1] The case began days earlier when
police learned of a couple who had reportedly come from the city of Formosa to
sell marijuana and cocaine in small doses. [@1] Officers of the unit's
investigative brigade went to a call about a disturbance and arrested one man
there. [@1] At the scene they identified the couple under investigation among
the other people present. [@1]

A search of the belongings of a woman aged 23 and a man aged 25 found marijuana
divided into small bags, a hand-rolled cigarette, cocaine, a precision scale,
four mobile phones and items used to divide the substances. [@1] Orientation
tests by specialists from the Delegación Drogas Peligrosas Laguna Blanca were
positive for marijuana and cocaine. [@1] Officers of the Delegación de Policía
Científica documented the scene, and the investigation continued with a
court-ordered search of the property where the couple was staying, where more
drugs and a second precision scale were seized. [@1]

The three people remained detained in the judicial case, and the couple was
charged under National Law No. 23.737, Argentina's narcotics statute. [@1]
Separate cases are under way over theft, threats and injuries caused with a
bladed weapon. [@1] Diario Exprés carried the same police account under the
headline Narcomenudeo: detenidos y drogas secuestradas tras un operativo. [@2]
