Military police stopped a 26-year-old man in Bairro José Corrêa Machado in
Montes Claros, in the Norte de Minas region, on the afternoon of Wednesday 4
November 2026. [@1] The stop happened during preventive patrolling when officers
saw the suspect coming out of a vegetation area situated behind the
neighbourhood. [@1]

Police records describe that vegetation area as a place used for hiding and
storing drugs intended for illegal trade. [@1] The officers considered the
behaviour suspicious and carried out the approach. [@1] During a personal
search, officers found a small tablet of a marijuana-like substance in the left
pocket of the clothing of the person stopped. [@1]

A check of security systems showed the young man already had previous criminal
records for drug trafficking, robbery and receptação, the offence of receiving
stolen goods. [@1] The illicit material was seized and sent with the recorded
incident report to the competent authorities for the applicable legal measures.
[@1] The seizure was carried out by the Polícia Militar, the uniformed state
military police. [@1]
