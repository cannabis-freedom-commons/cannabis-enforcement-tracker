Military Police officers arrested a 27-year-old man in the afternoon of Saturday
7 November 2026 in the Lagoa Grande neighbourhood of Patos de Minas, Minas
Gerais, on suspicion of illegal firearm possession and drug trafficking. [@2]
The operation began after an anonymous call to the 190 hotline reported an armed
man riding a white Honda Biz motorcycle near the bus terminal. [@1]

A 31-year-old woman in the flat said she had not been a victim of domestic
violence and allowed the officers to enter and search the apartment. [@1] In a
wardrobe officers found a .380 pistol loaded with four rounds, and in a wooden
box on the television rack a 9 mm pistol with two magazines. [@1] With the help
of a police sniffer dog, the team found two bars of cannabis hidden in a room
off the garage. [@1] One of the pistols, the .380, had been reported stolen in
the state of Goiás. [@2] The man, who police said has previous records for drug
trafficking and illegal firearm possession, took ownership of all the material
seized. [@1]

The man was arrested in the act for illegal possession and carrying of firearms
of permitted and restricted use, receiving stolen property for the gun taken in
theft, and drug trafficking. [@2] He was taken with the seized material to the
Civil Police duty station in Patos de Minas. [@1]
