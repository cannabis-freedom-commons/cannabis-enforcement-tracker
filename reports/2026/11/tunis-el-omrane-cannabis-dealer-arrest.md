Judicial police in El Omrane, a district of Tunis, arrested a suspected cannabis
dealer and seized significant quantities of the drug, known locally as zatla.
[@1] Police said the cannabis was prepared for sale, particularly to young
people and in schools. [@1]

Investigators started from a man found carrying a piece of cannabis, who told
them he had bought it from a dealer operating in the same area. [@1] Babnet's
account, carried by Daily8, describes the same operation by the El Omrane
brigade against a dealer selling to young people and in schools, and places the
activity in El Jbal al-Ahmar, a neighbouring district of Tunis. [@2]

With the public prosecutor at the Tunis Court of First Instance, investigators
raided the suspect's home. [@1] The raid found a large quantity of cannabis
rolls prepared for sale, an electronic scale and a canister of incapacitating
gas. [@1] Babnet identifies the rolls explicitly as cannabis and says the scale
was used to weigh the forbidden goods. [@2]

After consulting the prosecutor, police were authorised to hold all those
involved while the investigation continued into the rest of the network. [@1]
