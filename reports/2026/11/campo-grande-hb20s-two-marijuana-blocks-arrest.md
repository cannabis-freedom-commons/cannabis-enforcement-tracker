Municipal guards in Campo Grande detained a driver on Tuesday 10 November 2026
after he fled a traffic inspection and abandoned a stolen Hyundai HB20S. [@1]
The pursuit began around 12:39 when a traffic-enforcement team at Avenida José
Nogueira Vieira and Rua Marquês de Pombal checked the car's theft alert and
ordered him to stop. [@1] He drove along Rua Marquês de Pombal, crossed the
BR-163 and returned along Avenida João Garcia Carvalho Filho before abandoning
the car on Rua Rogério Cavalari in Loteamento Cristo Redentor and running into a
wooded area. [@1]

Guards found him after a search and seized a mobile phone they said he had
hidden under a stone. [@2] In the car they found two blocks of a substance
similar to marijuana. [@2] Questioned, the driver said he was to leave the car
at a petrol station on the road out to Cuiabá on the instructions of someone who
had called him, and said he did not know the car was stolen; he gave no reason
for fleeing. [@1]

He was taken to Depac Cepol, the community emergency police station, with
support from another GCM team. [@1] The drug went to Denar, the specialised
narcotics unit, and the car to Defurv, the vehicle theft unit. [@1] The theft
report for the car had been filed in Recife, Pernambuco. [@1]
