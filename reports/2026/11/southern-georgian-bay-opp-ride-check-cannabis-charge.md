On Wednesday, Nov. 11, 2026, Southern Georgian Bay OPP officers ran a RIDE
check, stopping vehicles to check for impaired-driving and traffic-law
compliance. [@1] One driver was stopped and issued a Highway Traffic Act Part
III summons under s. 57(19) for driving a vehicle not equipped with an ignition
interlock, and the vehicle was impounded for seven days. [@1] A second driver,
found with cannabis readily available in the vehicle, was charged under
Ontario's Cannabis Control Act. [@1]
