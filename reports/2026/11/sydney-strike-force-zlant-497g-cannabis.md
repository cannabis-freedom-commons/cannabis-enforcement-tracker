Seven people have been charged after police raids across Sydney targeting an
alleged criminal group that supplied drugs and firearms. [@1] Strike Force Zlant
was established in June 2026 by the Central Metropolitan Region Enforcement
Squad South to investigate the group. [@2]

On 11 November 2026, officers executed six search warrants at properties in
Auburn, Bossley Park, Prairiewood, Wakeley and Greenfield Park. [@3] During the
searches, police seized 497 g of cannabis, along with 741 g of cocaine, 103 g of
methamphetamine and $23,000 in cash. [@2] A 23-year-old man and a 25-year-old
woman were also arrested at Newtown. [@2] Police seized another $7,000 in cash
and 429 g of methamphetamine during those arrests. [@2] Four more men aged
between 21 and 27 were arrested at Greenfield Park, Prairiewood and Bossley
Park. [@1]

Police had arrested a 41-year-old man on Coward Street in Mascot on 31 October
2026. [@1] Officers then searched a shipping container on the same street. [@1]
Inside an Audi station wagon in the container, they found two firearms, 194
rounds of ammunition and 1.3 kg of methamphetamine. [@1] A Holden Commodore
parked nearby was also seized as suspected stolen. [@1]

The man and woman arrested at Newtown were refused bail to appear before a bail
court on 12 November 2026. [@2] The man was charged with 19 offences. [@2] The
woman was charged with five counts of drug supply and participating in a
criminal group. [@1] The four other men were also refused bail and were due
before Fairfield Local Court the same day. [@2] The 41-year-old was charged with
17 offences. [@2] He was refused bail and is due before Sutherland Local Court
on 11 January 2027. [@1] Police have not alleged that the seven were involved in
every part of the operation, and investigations continue. [@1]
