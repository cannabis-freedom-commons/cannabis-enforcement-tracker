On 3 November 2026 the tribunal judiciaire d'Argentan, the trial court in
Argentan in Orne, convicted two people for attempting to deliver telephones and
drugs by air into the Argentan detention centre. [@1] Police had arrested the
pair in the act in May. [@1] On 13 May 2026 officers on patrol near the
detention centre spotted a drone at about 3 a.m. [@1] Officers caught its
hanging string and arrested the two operators at the scene. [@1]

Officers seized several packages wrapped in socks at the time of the arrest.
[@1] The packages held cigarettes, mobile phones and batteries. [@1] The same
packages also held 17 grams of crack and nearly 200 grams of cannabis. [@1]

The attempt targeted the centre de détention d’Argentan, the prison at Argentan
in Orne. [@1] The two defendants had attempted the drone delivery to the
Argentan detention centre at 3 a.m. on 13 May 2026. [@1] The cannabis weighed
nearly 200 grams, the cannabis component recorded here, while the crack amount
is not counted as cannabis. [@1]
