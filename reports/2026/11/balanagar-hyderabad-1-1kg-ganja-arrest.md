Balanagar Excise officials seized 1.1 kg of ganja from a man during special
inspections in several areas aimed at curbing the sale and possession of
narcotic substances. [@1] A Balanagar Excise Station team conducting checks at
various locations stopped S. Srikanth, a resident of Bollaram, after noticing
him behaving suspiciously. [@1] A search of Srikanth's possession led to the
recovery of 1.1 kg of ganja, which staff seized before taking him into custody
for further action. [@1]

A case was registered against him at the Balanagar Excise Police Station under
the relevant provisions of the law. [@1] Quthbullapur Excise Superintendent R.
Praveen said the department was taking steps to prevent the circulation of ganja
in the region, with excise teams conducting inspections in different localities
to identify people involved in possession and sale. [@1]

Officials said surveillance and checks would continue in areas under their
jurisdiction to curb drug-related activities. [@1] They also urged the public to
share information about the illegal sale or transportation of ganja with the
Excise Department to help prevent the drug menace. [@1]
