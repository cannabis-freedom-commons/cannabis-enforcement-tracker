Baban Shadi, 26, of London, admitted driving along Alfred Street,
Weston-super-Mare without insurance or a licence and to possessing 0.6g of
cannabis on 16 April 2026. [@1] The possession and the two motoring offences
were admitted together in the same hearing. [@1] The cannabis was possessed in
Weston-super-Mare, in North Somerset. [@1]

Shadi received eight penalty points and was ordered to pay £85 in costs and a
fine of £120. [@1] The court also ordered the forfeiture and destruction of the
0.6g of cannabis seized from him. [@1]

The case was listed among the latest Weston and wider North Somerset offenders
