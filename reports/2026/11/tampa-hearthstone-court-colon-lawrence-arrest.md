Two 44-year-old men face felony charges after Hillsborough County deputies
raided a Tampa home and uncovered narcotics, weapons and cash. [@1] Hillsborough
County deputies arrived at a home on the 8000 block of Hearthstone Court in
Tampa around 3 p.m. on 10 November 2026 to execute a court-authorized search
warrant. [@1] The investigation centered on 44-year-old Juan Colon Jr.,
described as an eight-time convicted felon suspected of dealing drugs in
northwestern Hillsborough County. [@1] As deputies approached the house, they
spotted Colon getting into a car driven by 44-year-old Charles Lawrence Jr. [@1]
Both men were taken into custody at the scene. [@1]

A search of the vehicle found more than $2,000 in cash alongside individually
packaged cocaine, crack cocaine, Xanax, oxycodone, amphetamine and
buprenorphine. [@1] Inside the Hearthstone Court home, investigators turned up
even larger quantities of those same drugs, as well as marijuana products,
another large sum of cash, brass knuckles, and a hatchet. [@1] Deputies
discovered a large quantity of narcotics in the search, including Xanax pills,
cocaine, crack cocaine, amphetamine, oxycodone, buprenorphine and marijuana
products. [@2]

Colon was booked on two counts of being a felon in possession of a firearm,
armed possession of a controlled substance, possession of a controlled substance
in excess of 10 grams, possession of drug paraphernalia, and keeping a public
nuisance structure for drug activity. [@1] Lawrence faces three counts of
possession of a controlled substance, possession of cocaine with intent to sell
or deliver, possession of a Schedule IV controlled substance with intent to sell
or deliver, possession of a controlled substance in excess of 10 grams, and
possession of drug paraphernalia. [@1] Sheriff Chad Chronister said illegal
narcotics threaten the safety of neighborhoods and that those who profit from
illegal drug distribution will not be tolerated. [@1] The sheriff’s office said
the investigation remains active. [@1]
