Police in Naugarh, Chandauli district, arrested a man carrying 33 kg 500 g of
ganja in a Magic pickup on the night of 5 November 2026. [@3] Officers stopped
the pickup near the Jaymohani forest range office on the Naugarh-Madhupur road
after a tip-off that a large ganja load was being moved by four-wheeler. [@3]
According to Hindustan, the driver sped up and fled when signalled to stop, and
officers chased the vehicle and took it to the police station. [@3]

The ganja was packed in 33 bundles wrapped in plastic and tape and hidden in a
concealed chamber under an iron plate on the pickup's cargo bed. [@2] The load
was weighed in the presence of the Naugarh tehsildar, a sub-district revenue
officer, and came to 33 kg 500 g. [@2] The suspect told police he picked up the
load in Ambikapur and was taking it to Chandmari in Varanasi. [@3]

Hindustan reported that police registered a case against the suspect and sent
him to jail. [@3] Police registered the case at Naugarh police station, case
crime number 99/26, under sections 8/20/60 of the NDPS Act, and seized the
pickup, registered UP 65 JT 8607. [@2] Dainik Bhaskar reported that police
seized the pickup along with 33 kg 500 g of ganja found in a secret chamber in
its cargo bed. [@1]
