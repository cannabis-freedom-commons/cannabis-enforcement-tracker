Brazil's Receita Federal ran Operation Fronteira in Sant'Ana do Livramento to
intensify action against smuggling, customs evasion, drug trafficking and other
irregularities on the Brazil-Uruguay border. [@1] Intelligence work by the
Receita Federal's Repression Division together with the Rio Grande do Sul Civil
Police narcotics department, Denarc, identified a suspect parcel. [@1] After
monitoring a parcel from western Paraná, the agencies made a controlled delivery
that ended with the seizure of approximately 16 kilograms of hashish valued at
about R$480,000. [@1] Two people were arrested when they came to collect the
parcel. [@1] The joint anti-drug action took place on 5 November, when the
R$480,000 in seizures and the two arrests were recorded. [@1]

In a balance released on Monday the 9th, the agency put the operation's first
week at eight arrests and seizures valued at about R$3 million in total. [@1]
The work involved customs inspection, parcel monitoring, vehicle checks and
joint action with public-security bodies. [@1] Other items retained included
goods, vehicles, foreign coins of numismatic interest, collectors' plates,
cosmetics, mobile phones and other products showing signs of irregularity. [@1]
Sant'Ana do Livramento shares an integrated urban area with Rivera in Uruguay,
which demands constant attention from the customs-control bodies. [@1]
