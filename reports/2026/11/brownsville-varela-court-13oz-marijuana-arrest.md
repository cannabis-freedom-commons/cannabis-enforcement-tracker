Cameron County deputies arrested Brownsville man Farid Sadek Khouane after a
search warrant at his home on Varela Court in Brownsville. [@1] Khouane
allegedly attempted to run away as officers entered the property. [@1] He
allegedly admitted to deputies that he owned and sold the drugs found. [@1]

A search of his house and vehicle found felony quantities of suspected cocaine,
49 THC pens and just over 13 ounces of marijuana. [@1] Deputies also found three
weapons and two stacks of cash at the home. [@1]

The warrant was executed by the Cameron County Sheriff’s Special Investigations
Unit and SWAT. [@1] Khouane was charged with Manufacture or Delivery of a
Controlled Substance (Penalty Groups 1 and 2) and Possession of Marijuana. [@1]
