Village police in Linga, in Balaghat district of Madhya Pradesh, seized about 1
kg 133 g of ganja from the roof space of a house during a search. [@1] Village
police stations, thana in Hindi, handle such local searches, and the report
places the find in the house of a resident of the Linga area. [@1]

Police arrested two accused in the case. [@1] The arrests were made on Tuesday,
the day police gave for the search, in the Balaghat district of Madhya Pradesh,
a district in the state's eastern tribal belt. [@1]
