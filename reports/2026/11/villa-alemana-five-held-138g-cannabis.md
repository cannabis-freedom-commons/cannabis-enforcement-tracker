Detectives from Chile's Investigative Police (PDI) detained five people and
raided four homes in Villa Alemana in an anti-drug operation. [@1] Police
describe the suspects as members of a family group dedicated to selling drugs in
the commune. [@1] All five detainees are Chilean citizens and adults. [@1] On
the prosecution's instructions, the five were to be brought before the Villa
Alemana Guarantee Court for a detention-control hearing. [@1]

Officers seized 138 grams of cannabis in the searches. [@1] They also seized 551
grams of cocaine hydrochloride and 245 grams of caffeine, which police describe
as a substance commonly used to cut drugs. [@1] The PDI put the combined
seizure's approximate value at 17 million pesos. [@1] Detectives also seized a
Mercedes Benz A250, six mobile phones, a laptop, four security cameras and a
digital recording device. [@1] Cash was also seized, but the amount was not
disclosed. [@1]

The operation was carried out by the Modelo Territorial Cero (MT-0) unit in
Villa Alemana under the direction of the Valparaíso ECOH-SAC prosecutor's
office. [@1] Officers entered the four homes under court-authorized entry and
search warrants. [@1] Police said the detainees have prior records for drug-law
offences, less-serious injuries and robbery with violence, without specifying
which record belongs to whom. [@1] No result of the detention-control hearing or
any precautionary measures was reported. [@1]
