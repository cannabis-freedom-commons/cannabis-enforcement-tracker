Kent Police officers entered 27 properties and made 32 arrests during the raids.
[@2] The raids in Gravesham ran from 2 to 6 November 2026 and followed months of
planning. [@1] The searches found cocaine, cannabis, illicit tobacco and an air
rifle. [@1] They also seized almost £40,000 in cash. [@1] The raids disrupted 12
county lines networks and led to 34 charges. [@1] Police also took action on
seven occasions to protect children and vulnerable adults. [@1]

Two men charged with drug offences were remanded in custody ahead of further
court hearings. [@1] Amarildo Hebeja, 23, of Wrotham Road, Gravesend, was
arrested in a raid on 2 November, when officers seized cash and cannabis at the
property. [@1] Hebeja admitted being concerned in the supply of cocaine but
denied possession with intent to supply cannabis and possession of criminal
property. [@1] His next hearing is at Maidstone Crown Court on 30 November. [@1]
Ryelee Yardley, 24, of Kendall Gardens, Gravesend, was arrested after a raid on
5 November uncovered cocaine, cannabis and cash. [@1] He denied possession with
intent to supply cocaine and possession of cannabis. [@1] His next hearing is at
Maidstone Crown Court on 4 December. [@1]

Kent Police said county lines gangs bring drugs into areas including Kent, often
exploiting young or vulnerable people as dealers or buyers. [@1] Chief Inspector
Sarah Rivett said the operation would be pivotal in the force's effort to combat
county lines. [@1]
