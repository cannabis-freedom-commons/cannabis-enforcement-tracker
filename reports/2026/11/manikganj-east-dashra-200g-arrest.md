Officers of the Manikganj district office of the Department of Narcotics Control
arrested 55-year-old Abdul Kader with 200 grams of ganja in a special anti-drug
operation. [@1,2] The team raided his own house in Purba Dashra village under
Manikganj Sadar police station at about 12:15 pm on Sunday, 1 November, and
arrested him there. [@1] The operation was led by Sub-Inspector Md. Morshed Alam
under the overall supervision of Deputy Director Humayun Kabir Khandaker. [@1]

Officers recovered 200 grams of ganja from Abdul Kader’s house. [@1,3] The
department said information indicated that the arrested man had long been
involved in drug dealing in the area. [@1] He was identified as the son of the
late Hafez Uddin and Joygan of Purba Dashra village. [@1]

A case under the Narcotics Control Act was filed against him at Manikganj Sadar
police station. [@1] The department’s sources said operations to eliminate drugs
and against drug dealers would continue. [@1]
