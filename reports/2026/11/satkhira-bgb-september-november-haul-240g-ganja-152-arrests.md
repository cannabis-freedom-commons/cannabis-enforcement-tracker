Bangladesh's Border Guard Bangladesh (BGB) said it seized drugs and smuggled
goods worth more than Tk 82.35 crore in drives along the border from 1 September
to November 2026. [@1] The force said it arrested 152 people in those cases.
[@1]

Among the drugs, officers seized 240 grams of ganja, the Bengali term for
cannabis, alongside 3.13 kg of heroin, 4,762 bottles of liquor, 64,031 yaba
tablets and 31,016 bottles of Phensidyl cough syrup. [@1]

The battalion said the Jashore region's roughly 600 km of border is covered by
113 Border Observation Posts under two sectors and seven units, with 452 patrols
a day. [@1]
