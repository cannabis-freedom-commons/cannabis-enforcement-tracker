Pakistan Customs arrested a truck driver and seized 51 kg of hashish hidden in
the truck at its Digital Enforcement Station in Khurkhera on the RCD Highway on
8 November 2026. [@1,2] The truck, carrying a commercial consignment of carrots,
was stopped after a tip-off about a drug shipment on its way from Quetta. [@1]

A search found 51 kg of hashish in specially built hidden compartments. [@1,2] The
driver, from Qilla Abdullah district in Balochistan, was arrested at the scene.
[@1] Preliminary investigation suggested the hashish was loaded in the Gulistan
area of Balochistan and was headed for distribution in Karachi. [@1,2]

Customs put the total value of the hashish and the vehicle at Rs239.5 million.
[@1,2] A first information report was registered and investigators were looking
for other members of the network, including its financial facilitators and
intended recipients. [@1,2]

Customs officials stopped the truck, registration number TKT-358, which was reportedly carrying a commercial consignment of carrots. [@1]
