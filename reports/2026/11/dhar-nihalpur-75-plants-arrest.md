Dasai police, acting under the anti-narcotics drive Operation Prahar, raided a
field in Nihalpur village (Hanumantya Padampura) in Dhar district. [@1] Officers
seized 75 small and large green ganja plants from the field. [@1] Police
arrested 40-year-old Rajaram, also named as Raju and Radhu, son of Champalal
Ninama of Nihalpur, at the spot. [@1]

The cannabis had been grown hidden among chilli, pigeon-pea and sugarcane crops.
[@1] Police put the estimated value of the seized plants at about Rs 400,000.
[@1] Dasai post in-charge Dinesh Singh Yadav said police received a tip on 30
October that Nihalpur resident Rajaram alias Raju had grown ganja illegally in
his field and informed senior officers. [@1]

The raid team was formed under the direction of Dhar SP Sachin Sharma and the
guidance of ASP Parul Belapurkar, SDOP Vishwadeep Singh Parihar and Amjhera
station in-charge Raju Makwana. [@1] Police registered a case against the
accused under the NDPS Act and said further legal proceedings were under way.
[@1]
