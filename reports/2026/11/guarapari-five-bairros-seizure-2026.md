Military Police detained a man in Perocao after a systems check found an open
robbery arrest warrant against him and took him to the DPJ in Guarapari. [@1]
Officers apprehended one adolescent during a monitoring action in Portal Clube.
[@1] The adolescent was taken to the Guarapari Regional station. [@2] He signed
a circumstantial report for an act analogous to drug trafficking and was
released to family after a relative agreed to appear before the prosecution when
asked. [@2] No one was arrested in the Village do Sol seizure, and the seized
material was sent to the police station. [@1]

In Village do Sol, officers seized about 198 g of cocaine, 10 units of hashish
(haxixe), 14 bundles of marijuana (buchas de maconha) and R$5. [@1] In Portal
Clube, officers seized 13 cocaine pins, 39 hashish units, about 42 g of the same
substance, 14 crack rocks, R$830 and a precision scale. [@1] The second account
lists the same Portal Clube seizure as 39 hashish units, a portion of about 42
grams of the same drug, 14 crack rocks, 13 cocaine pins, a precision scale and
cash. [@2] In Cubatao, officers found a .38-calibre revolver with five rounds,
one of them fired, and in a separate encounter stopped a man near a school with
14 crack rocks. [@1] In Perocao, officers had stopped the detained man with 42
crack rocks and R$486 in cash before finding the warrant. [@1] In Santa Monica,
an adolescent fleeing police dropped a bag holding crack rocks, cocaine pins,
marijuana and money, while three other people were stopped with crack rocks,
cocaine pins and money. [@1]

The occurrences took place during an extended holiday across Cubatao, Perocao,
Village do Sol, Portal Clube and Santa Monica in Guarapari. [@1] Police stressed
the importance of residents participation in combating trafficking. [@1]
