Hammond police responded to East Thomas Street and Morris Road on 7 November
2026 after reports of shots fired. [@1] Officers traced Tradarius Johnson, 25,
and Nicholas Johnson, 23, to a home along 2nd Avenue in connection with the

marijuana, a large amount of psilocybin mushrooms, a large amount of THC vapes
and two loaded handguns, along with a large amount of cash. [@1]

The brothers were arrested on multiple narcotics and firearm charges; Tradarius
