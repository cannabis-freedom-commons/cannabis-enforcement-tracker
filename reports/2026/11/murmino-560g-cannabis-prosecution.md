Police detained a 45-year-old resident of the settlement of Murmino on suspicion
of illegally storing drugs. [@2] Prosecutors accuse him of illegally making and
storing a large batch of drugs. [@1] His criminal case was sent for
consideration to Ryazansky District Court. [@1] He is charged under part 2 of
Article 228 of the Criminal Code for illegal acquisition and storage of drugs on
a large scale without intent to sell. [@3] He faces up to ten years in prison if
convicted. [@1] He told police the drug was intended for his own use. [@2]

Investigators say that in September 2026 the man collected parts of wild
cannabis plants growing in the district. [@1] He used those plant parts to make
cannabis weighing more than 560 grams, described by police as marijuana. [@3] He
kept the substance at his home in Murmino. [@3] Police found the substance in a
container in his outbuilding, identified as 560 grams of marijuana. [@2]
Officers found and seized the prohibited substance during operational search
measures. [@1]

The detention was carried out by the regional Interior Ministry department
together with Ryazansky district police. [@2] The case was sent to court with an
approved indictment from the Ryazan district prosecutor's office. [@3]
