Seguranca Presente officers detained a 27-year-old man on the afternoon of
Tuesday 3 November at Praca Barao do Rio Branco in central Campos dos
Goytacazes. [@1] A systems check during the stop found an open arrest warrant
against him for child-support debt. [@1] Officers took him to the 134th Police
Station in Centro, where he remained detained at the disposal of the courts.
[@1]

Officers on patrol saw the man sitting in the square smoking a handmade
cigarette that gave off a strong odour characteristic of marijuana. [@1] When he
noticed the patrol car he tried to move quickly away from the spot, apparently
to avoid being approached, but officers caught him. [@1] A personal search
seized one handmade cigarette with characteristics similar to marijuana. [@1]

The square is on Rua Baronesa da Lagoa Dourada, near a school, in the central
area of Campos dos Goytacazes in the Norte Fluminense region. [@1] The patrol
account is attributed to the Seguranca Presente programme. [@1]
