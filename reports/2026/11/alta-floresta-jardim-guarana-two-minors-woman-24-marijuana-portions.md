Military Police RAIO officers apprehended two adolescents and took an adult
woman to the Civil Police station over suspected drug trafficking on 12 November
2026 in the Jardim Guaraná neighbourhood of Alta Floresta. [@2] The patrol first
stopped an adolescent on Rua NEG after he discarded packaging on seeing the
officers approach. [@3] During the personal search officers found three
marijuana-like portions on him. [@3] Questioned, the adolescent pointed officers
to a nearby residence where they found the adult woman and the second
adolescent. [@4] The Jornal da Cidade account described the detained woman as a
19-year-old. [@1]

Inside the house officers found 24 portions of a substance analogous to
marijuana, 19 portions analogous to cocaine hydrochloride and 15 portions
analogous to coca paste base, 58 portions in total. [@4] Officers also seized
two precision scales, R$2,606 in cash, a mobile phone, a roll of plastic film
and several zip-lock bags. [@4]

The two adolescents and the woman were taken with all the seized material to the
Civil Police station in Alta Floresta, arriving uninjured and without needing
handcuffs. [@3] Police recorded the case as suspected drug trafficking,
association for trafficking and corruption of minors. [@2]
