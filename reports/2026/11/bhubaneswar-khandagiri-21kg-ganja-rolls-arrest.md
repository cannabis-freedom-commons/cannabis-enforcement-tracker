Maitri Vihar police in Bhubaneswar arrested fast-food stall operator Harekrushna
Badatia for allegedly selling ganja rolls disguised as chicken rolls. [@1]
Badatia, a native of the Ganjam area, had opened a fast-food stall at Khandagiri
Square where he prepared and sold different kinds of fast food. [@1] A huge
crowd also used to gather at his food stall. [@1] He was allegedly selling ganja
rolls under the guise of chicken rolls. [@1]

A Maitri Vihar police team intercepted Badatia while he was transporting ganja
on a scooty. [@1] During the inspection, officers found 21 kg of ganja and
arrested him along with the cannabis. [@1] Police said Badatia procured the
cannabis from Kandhamal district and sold it at his fast-food stall in the
capital city. [@1] He sold it using what police called a unique, deceptive
technique. [@1]

The ganja he carried was cannabis. [@1] The case took place in Bhubaneswar,
described as the state capital city. [@1] A further probe into the matter was
under way. [@1]
