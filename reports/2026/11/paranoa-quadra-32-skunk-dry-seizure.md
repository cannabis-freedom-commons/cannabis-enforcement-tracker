Military police officers from the Grupo Tático Operacional of the 20th
Battalion, Gtop 40, carried out an operation in Quadra 32 in Paranoá after
anonymous reports pointed to drug trafficking in a residence there. [@1] The
team monitored the address and reported an atypical, constant flow of people
approaching the building, making quick exchanges at the entrance and leaving
immediately, a pattern police described as typical of illegal drug sales. [@1]
On Sunday, officers moved in to approach and check the premises. [@1] Inside the
dwelling, they found portions of marijuana-like substances laid out on a
mattress. [@1] No resident or person responsible for the site was found during
the action. [@1]

The operation seized R$718 in cash, four portions of cocaine, three portions of
skunk, one portion of dry, three precision scales, a container of lança-perfume,
two mobile phones and a vehicle key. [@1] All the collected material was taken
to the 6th Police Station in Paranoá for registration and further investigation.
[@1]

A second account confirms the action took place last Sunday after anonymous
reports of drug trafficking in a residence in the area. [@2] The Federal
District Civil Police will continue the investigation to identify those
responsible for the site. [@2]
