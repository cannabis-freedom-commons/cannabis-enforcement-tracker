Military Police officers arrested a man in flagrante on 1 November 2026 in the
Bosque neighbourhood of Araguari, in the Triângulo Mineiro, on suspicion of drug
trafficking. [@1] The officers seized one bar of maconha, the Brazilian
Portuguese word for marijuana, and 21 individual buchas, or wraps, of the same
substance. [@1] They also seized a precision scale, a mobile phone and R$368 in
cash. [@1]

The police action followed an anonymous tip about suspected trafficking on Rua
Inhambus on Sunday the 1st. [@1] When the suspect saw the officers, he tried to
enter a residence and discarded a bag. [@1] Officers searching during the stop
found the drugs and the other seized materials. [@1]

The Military Police said the case began with a report to the Disque-Denúncia 181
tip line about possible drug sales around Rua Inhambus. [@1] Troops from the
Operations Patrol went to the address to check the information. [@1] After the
arrest, the man was taken to the Civil Police station with the seized items for
the applicable procedures. [@1]
