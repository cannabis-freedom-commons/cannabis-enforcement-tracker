Municipal Guardia Civil officers detained seven men and a 17-year-old in Soledad
de Graciano Sanchez during the previous 24 hours for probable delitos contra la
salud, offences against health. [@1] The corporation said the interventions
happened during preventive patrols in different colonias, neighbourhoods, after
officers found people allegedly disturbing public order or committing
administrative faults. [@1] In Hogares Ferrocarrileros, Praderas del Maurel and
Rancho Blanco officers detained Eduardo N, 38, Alberto N, 28, and Cesar N, 23,
finding doses of a substance with characteristics of methamphetamine. [@1] In
colonia Las Flores officers detained a 17-year-old for alleged possession of
cristal doses and said his parents were notified under the applicable protocol.
[@1] In La Virgen, San Francisco de Asis, La Foresta and Hacienda Bonita
officers detained Ricardo N, 32, Homero N, 46, Fermin N, 18, and Juan N, 20, for
alleged possession of doses of a substance with characteristics of marijuana.
[@1]

After officers told them the reason for detention, all eight people were
transferred to the Fiscalia General del Estado, the state prosecutor, with the
seized substances for investigation. [@1] In Ricardo N's case the corporation
said a records check found prior detentions for alleged robbery and weapons
possession and various prison admissions linked to metropolitan-area criminal
incidents. [@1] Authorities did not say whether the seized substances underwent
expert analysis or report the outcome of the ministerial investigation, so the
detained people must be considered innocent until responsibility is determined
by judicial ruling. [@1] A second account reports the same roundup as seven men
and a 17-year-old detained during the previous 24 hours after being found during
preventive rounds. [@2] That account names the same four marijuana detainees as
Ricardo N, 32, Homero N, 46, Fermin N, 18, and Juan N, 20, in La Virgen, San
Francisco de Asis, La Foresta and Hacienda Bonita. [@2]
