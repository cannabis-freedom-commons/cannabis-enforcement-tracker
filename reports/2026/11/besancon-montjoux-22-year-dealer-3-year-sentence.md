A 22-year-old dealer was sentenced to three years of imprisonment at the
Besançon court on 6 November 2026, for drug trafficking as a recidivist. [@1]
The storage space was a cellar in a building that belonged to a woman the man
had hired as a mule. [@1]

Police found cannabis, cocaine, heroin and ecstasy in the cellar, and the woman
said she had been paid €80 to keep the drugs there. [@1] Wearing an electronic
ankle bracelet from an earlier conviction, he had not stopped trafficking; a
search at his home seized drugs, phones and €3,500 in cash. [@1]

The man did not wish to speak at his trial, and besides the three years he was
ordered to pay a €2,000 fine and was banned from the Doubs for three years. [@1]
