On the afternoon of Monday 9 November 2026, two police stops in Vitória, the
capital of Espírito Santo, ended with three adolescents detained. [@1] Officers
then took all three to Deacle, the specialised station for adolescents in
conflict with the law. [@1]

Around 15:30, officers of the Patrulha de Aglomerados, a foot patrol for crowded
areas, were in the Romão neighbourhood when they stopped a 14-year-old. [@1] His
backpack held 17 units of maconha, the Brazilian term for cannabis, seven wraps
of hashish, 10 wraps of cocaine, 10 wraps of crack and R$ 495.80. [@1] They also
seized a radio through which the team heard other people asking about police
movements in the area. [@1] They then stopped a 17-year-old carrying 23 units of
maconha, 12 wraps of hashish, 21 wraps of cocaine, 10 wraps of crack and R$ 175
in small notes. [@1]

About two hours later, around 17:30, Tactical Force officers in another part of
Vitória stopped a second 17-year-old whose behaviour they found suspicious. [@1]
During that stop they found 14 units of maconha and R$ 180. [@1] He told them he
sold drugs there and kept more hidden in his bedroom. [@1] With his mother's
permission they entered the house and searched the room he indicated. [@1] They
found five tablets of Pakistani hashish, 342 units of hashish, 112 units of
skunk, 19 units of maconha, 50 rocks of crack and a precision scale. [@1]

Civil Police registered a circumstantial report for each adolescent for an
infraction equivalent to drug trafficking. [@1] The families agreed to present
them to the Public Prosecutor's Office when asked. [@1] After the legal
procedures, Civil Police released the three to their guardians. [@1]
