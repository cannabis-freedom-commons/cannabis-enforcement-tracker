In Barhara in Bhojpur district, Bihar, officers of Babura police station
arrested a man on a charge of possessing ganja, the cannabis product at issue in
this case. [@1] The arrested accused was identified as Ramnath Singh. [@1] Singh
is described as a resident of Semra village, a settlement served by the same
Babura police station that carried out the arrest. [@1]

Police said the search recovered 2.30 grams of ganja from his possession, a
small cannabis seizure equivalent to 0.0023 kilograms. [@1] The available police
account attributes the case information to Sanjay Kumar, the station head of
Babura police station. [@1] The incident therefore consists of one arrest and
one small-quantity ganja seizure in the Babura policing area of Bhojpur. [@1]
