Officers of the National Police's Drugs and Organised Crime Unit (Udyco)
arrested at least three people on Mallorca who were storing almost 50 kilos of
hashish for sale. [@1] The suspects received the hashish from the Spanish
mainland in courier parcels, according to the newspaper's case sources. [@1] The
arrests took place on Wednesday 4 November. [@1] Officers searched a home in
Marratxí, a municipality east of Palma, and another in a town in es Raiguer, the
district around the town of Inca. [@1] An investigating court in Palma declared
the case secret. [@1] Investigators had followed the suspects for some time;
over the previous months they had collected parcels of the drug from courier
companies and kept them at home for sale through others in the network. [@1]

In the Marratxí search, police found a large quantity of hashish in a chest
freezer and an air rifle. [@1] A woman was arrested there over her alleged link
to the criminal organisation and was released at the police station hours later.
[@1] Two more people were arrested the same day, and all the suspects are
Spanish nationals. [@1]

The investigation is still open, further arrests are not ruled out, and the
suspects were due to appear before a Palma court in the coming hours. [@1]
