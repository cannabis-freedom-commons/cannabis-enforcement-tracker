Sofia police detained twelve people in a specialised operation against drug
dealing in the capital's Hristo Botev neighbourhood. [@1] The operation was
carried out on 4 November by officers of the First District Department, the SDVR
Narcotics section, the Specialised Police Forces unit and the Cobra section.
[@1] Officers checked thirteen addresses and detained twelve people during the
operation. [@1]

The searches turned up 75 grams of marijuana, along with about 60 grams of a
crystalline substance, 23 grams of amphetamine, 16 grams of cocaine and about 5
grams of a herbal drug. [@1] A further 190 grams of ecstasy in more than 500
tablets was found in a car parked outside the home of one of those detained.
[@1]

Police also seized three electronic scales, about 130 bottles of alcohol with
broken excise stamps and no documents of origin, 20 bicycles and three electric
scooters. [@1] Seven pre-trial proceedings were opened over the cases. [@1]
Three of the proceedings were reported to the Sofia City Prosecutor's Office and
the other four to the Sofia Regional Prosecutor's Office. [@1] One of those
detained has been charged as an accused person. [@1]

The Interior Ministry said police work on the case was continuing. [@2]
