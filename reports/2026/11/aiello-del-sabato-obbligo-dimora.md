Avellino's investigating judge ordered three men under investigation for alleged
drug dealing to observe a residence requirement in their home municipalities.
[@1] The order requires them not to leave home from 19:00 to 07:00 the next day.
[@1] Finance police officers of Avellino's provincial command executed the
order. [@1] The three are seriously suspected of possessing drugs for the
purpose of dealing. [@1] Two recipients are brothers with prior convictions for
similar offences who allegedly called drugs melanzane, meaning aubergines, to
make references less explicit. [@1] One of the two brothers remains in prison,
with no explanation published of how that detention relates to the new residence
measure. [@1] The allegations remain provisional and those concerned are
presumed innocent until any final conviction. [@3]

Investigators say the inquiry began with hashish seizures from several buyers.
[@1] Avellino Guardia di Finanza's Mobile Unit then continued with technical
checks and further drug finds. [@1] In February 2025 investigators seized about
350 grams of cocaine, 2 kilos of hashish and 650 grams of marijuana, plus
packaging material. [@3] Part of the drugs had been hidden in a buried hatch and
inside a disused vehicle near the two brothers' home in Aiello del Sabato. [@2]
The find led to the arrest in flagrante of the two brothers, both now among the
recipients of the residence order. [@1] A dog unit from Capodichino with two
handlers and the dog Lyan took part in the search. [@1]

Investigators describe the evidence as pointing to intense dealing in the
Avellino hinterland involving several people. [@1] The Avellino prosecution
presents the operation as joint work with the Guardia di Finanza against dealing
and related criminal proceeds. [@2]
