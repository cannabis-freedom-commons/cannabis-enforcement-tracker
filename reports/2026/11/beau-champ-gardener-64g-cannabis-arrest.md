On Wednesday 11 November, ADSU officers arrested a 45-year-old gardener living
in Deux-Freres while he was riding a motorcycle in the Beau-Champ area. [@1] A
search found him carrying a plastic bag holding 10 rolled paper wraps containing
cannabis, a piece of cellophane wrapping cannabis, and another transparent
plastic bag holding four similar rolled paper wraps also containing cannabis.
[@1] The total weight of the seized drug was 64.23 grams. [@1]

The suspect’s motorcycle was seized for the needs of the investigation. [@1] A
later search of the suspect’s home found no incriminating object. [@1] The
investigation was continuing. [@1]
