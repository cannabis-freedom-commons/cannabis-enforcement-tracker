Dhaka Metropolitan Police arrested 465 people and filed 51 cases during raids
across Dhaka over a 24-hour period. [@1] Police filed 51 cases during the same
period, according to a DMP press release. [@1] Ramna accounted for 22 arrests,
Lalbagh for 59, Wari for 43, Motijheel for 59, Tejgaon for 56, Mirpur for 149,
Gulshan for 38 and Uttara for 38, with one more by the Detective Branch. [@1]
The arrests were made during operations in different police-station areas of
Dhaka on Monday 9 November. [@1] Police said further legal action against those
arrested was under way. [@1]

During the same operations, police seized 200 grams of cannabis and a
cannabis-cutting machine. [@1] The sweep also yielded 669 yaba pills, an
unexploded cocktail and parts of another, and 11 mobile phones. [@1]

These are the police's own counts from a regular anti-crime sweep in which the
cannabis was recorded separately from the other drugs seized. [@1]
