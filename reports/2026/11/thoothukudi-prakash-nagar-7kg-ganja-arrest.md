In a police search, officers arrested Balamurugan, a 26-year-old man and the son
of Saravanan, who is from Rajiv Nagar in Pudukottai, after they found ganja he
had been keeping for sale. [@1] Officers seized 7 kg of ganja from him. [@1] The
ganja was being kept for illegal sale in the Prakash Nagar area of Pudukottai.
[@1] The Pudukottai police limits named in the seizure are in Thoothukudi
district. [@1]

Pudukottai police station inspector Ramakrishnan led the officers who searched
the area. [@1] Police acted on secret information that the ganja was stored
there for sale. [@1] To prevent ganja and other intoxicants being sold inside
the Pudukottai police limits of Thoothukudi district, officers had been carrying
out intensive patrols and searches. [@1]

Inspector Ramakrishnan registered a case and the investigation was continuing.
