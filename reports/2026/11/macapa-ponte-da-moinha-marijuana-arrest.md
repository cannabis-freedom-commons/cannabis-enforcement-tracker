Military police officers deployed on Operação Protetor arrested a man in
flagrante on the afternoon of Wednesday, 4 November 2026, at Ponte da Moinha in
the Novo Horizonte neighbourhood in Macapá's north zone. [@2] Patamo officers
surrounded and arrested the suspect after he tried to evade a police approach by
entering a house and leaving through the back. [@1] The man became nervous when
he saw the patrol vehicle approach and fled on foot. [@2] He entered a house
through the front door and tried to slip away out the back, but officers chased
him and restrained him in the backyard. [@1] Officers handcuffed him strictly
for safety to prevent another escape attempt and to protect him and the team.
[@2]

A search of his clothing found portions of marijuana and a significant amount of
cash. [@1] Officers searched back along his escape route and found more portions
of the same suspected substance. [@1] Inside the property perimeter they seized
several mobile phones of unproven origin and a large amount of plastic material
used to divide, package and wrap drugs for sale. [@2]

On signs of involvement in drug trafficking, the suspect was formally arrested.
[@1] He was presented with all the seized material and money at the competent
police station for flagrante formalities and further legal procedures. [@2]
