A 41-year-old man wanted over the theft of medicines from a pharmacy was
arrested in the centre of Seabra, in Bahia's Chapada Diamantina region, on
Friday 6 November 2026 under a warrant for a crime committed in February. [@1]
Officers found about five kilograms of marijuana in a property the suspect used,
and the operation also seized a scale and a mobile phone. [@1]

The man had been on the run since June, according to the Civil Police. [@1] The
property search also produced a weighing scale and a mobile phone alongside the
drug. [@1]

Because of the seized material he was also arrested in the act for drug
trafficking, and he remains at the disposal of the courts. [@1] The robbery
under investigation took place in February, when units of weight-loss medicines
known as slimming pens were taken from a pharmacy in the municipality. [@1]

The arrest was carried out by the Seabra Territorial Police Station and the
investigation service, with support from the GATTI/Diamantina technical and
tactical investigation group and the 29th Independent Military Police Company.
[@1] The arrest was carried out by the Civil Police through the Seabra station
and investigation service, supported by the GATTI, with the 29th Independent
Military Police Company, units linked to the 13th Regional Interior Police
Coordinating Office in Seabra. [@2]
