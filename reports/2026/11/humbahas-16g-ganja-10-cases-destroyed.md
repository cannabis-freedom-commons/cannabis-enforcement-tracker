The Humbang Hasundutan district prosecutor's office, known as Kejari Humbahas,
destroyed evidence from 10 general criminal cases whose court decisions had
become final. [@1] The destruction took place on Tuesday 10 November 2026, in
Doloksanggul, the seat of the Humbang Hasundutan district. [@1] District
attorney Donald T.J. Situmorang led the destruction exercise, accompanied by
PAPBB head Surya Ramadhany Harahap. [@3] The exercise was run through the
office's asset recovery and evidence management section, PAPBB, and covered
cases from April to October 2026. [@1] The 10 cases comprised four property
offences, two state-security and public-order cases, and four narcotics and
other addictive-substance cases. [@1]

Officers destroyed 25 items of evidence, including dried ganja, the Indonesian
term for cannabis, and sabu, crystal methamphetamine. [@1] The dried ganja
weighed 16.96 grams gross and 16.44 grams net. [@1] The sabu weighs 3.34 grams
gross and 1.32 grams net; only the ganja is counted as cannabis here. [@1] The
other items included a machete, a spear, a bong, lighters, plastic clips, a hoe,
a sack and other tools. [@2]

Situmorang said the destruction executed final court judgments and demonstrated
the prosecutor's transparency and accountability to the public. [@1]
Representatives of the police, the detention centre, the health agency, the
subdistrict, the military command, the Tarutung court and a religious leader
witnessed the destruction. [@1]
