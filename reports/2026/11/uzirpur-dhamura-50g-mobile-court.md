Officers on an anti-drug drive in Uzirpur detained 34-year-old Mamun Hawlader
with 50 grams of ganja, the local term for cannabis. [@1] A mobile court held at
the scene gave him one month of imprisonment without labour and a 100-taka fine.
[@1] Upazila Nirbahi Officer Md Ali Suja, the chief executive of the
subdistrict, conducted the mobile court and imposed the one-month term and
100-taka penalty. [@1]

The operation took place on the morning of Tuesday 3 November in the Dhamura
area of Uzirpur, and the mobile court was held at the same place immediately
after the detention. [@1] The convicted man is the son of Shah Alam of the
Dhamura area. [@1]

Barishal Department of Narcotics Control inspector Md Jahidul Islam confirmed
the operation and the punishment through the mobile court. [@1] The drive was
presented as part of the administration's regular operations to control
narcotics. [@1] The authorities emphasised continuing such drives to check the
spread of drugs and drug-related crime in the area. [@1]
