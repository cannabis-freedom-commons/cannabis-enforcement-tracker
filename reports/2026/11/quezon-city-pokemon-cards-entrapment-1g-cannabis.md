Plainclothes officers of the Quezon City Police District arrested two Pasay City
men in an entrapment operation in Barangay Bagumbayan, Quezon City, on the
evening of 9 November 2026. [@1] The two are identified only by the aliases Jun,
25, and Vin, 26, both residents of Pasay City. [@1] The police report said the
victims found their bag of belongings missing while they were inside a mall in
Barangay Ugong Norte on 8 November 2026. [@1] The next day they learned the
belongings were being offered for sale and reported it to Eastwood Police
Station, known as Police Station 12. [@1]

Officers from Police Station 12 set up the entrapment, and the two suspects
arrived at the meeting place along E. Rodriguez Avenue in Barangay Bagumbayan at
about 10 p.m. on 9 November 2026 carrying the stolen items, the police said.
[@2] Officers recovered Pokémon collectible cards valued at P24,000 and a
Pidgeot EX Great Ball League Stamp Card worth P62,000. [@1] They also took a
Pokémon card binder holding 546 cards, a Redmi mobile phone and an eco bag. [@1]

A search incidental to the arrest found about one gram of suspected dried
marijuana worth an estimated P1,500 on Jun. [@1] Police said Jun also had a
previous record in a case under Republic Act 9165, the Comprehensive Dangerous
Drugs Act of 2002, in April 2023. [@1] Both men face charges under Presidential
Decree No. 1612, the Anti-Fencing Law, which covers dealing in goods taken in a
robbery, and Jun faces a separate charge under Republic Act 9165. [@1] The
Filipino-language report by Peoples Taliba gives the same aliases, the same
meeting place and the same one gram of suspected marijuana valued at P1,500.
[@2]
