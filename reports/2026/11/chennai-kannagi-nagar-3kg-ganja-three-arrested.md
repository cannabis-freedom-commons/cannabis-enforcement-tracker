Kannagi Nagar police in Chennai arrested a 27-year-old resident of the area's
tsunami housing colony who had been selling cannabis, known locally as ganja,
behind the municipal gym in the same neighbourhood. [@1] The arrest took place
on 7 November 2026 and officers seized 3 kg of ganja. [@1]

Police also arrested a 32-year-old man and a 22-year-old woman from Kannagi
Nagar who had been at large in a separate ganja case, bringing the total held to
three. [@1] The account ran in Dinamalar's Chennai district news roundup on the
evening of 7 November 2026, alongside unrelated reports on an assault and a
gutkha tobacco seizure. [@1]
