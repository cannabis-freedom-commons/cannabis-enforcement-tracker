Police arrested Akhilesh Kumar on a ganja-smuggling allegation. [@1] Kumar is a
resident of Raghopur in Vaishali district, and ganja is the local term for
cannabis. [@1] Police said they are questioning Kumar to trace other members of
the smuggling network. [@1] Police said the inquiry is also tracing the sources
of the liquor and ganja and links connected to Muzaffarpur. [@1]

Officers stopped a Mahindra Thar coming from the Hajipur side at the Fakuli
checkpost and searched it. [@1] The search found 60 kg of ganja in the Thar.
[@1] Police seized the Thar and a mobile phone with the ganja. [@1] In a
separate raid officers seized 4,010 litres of illicit foreign liquor with the
truck, pickup and car used to move it. [@1] Police listed the combined haul as
4,010 litres of foreign liquor, 60 kg of ganja, one truck, one pickup, one car
and one mobile phone. [@1] A corroborating account counts four seized vehicles
as one truck, one pickup, one car and one Mahindra Thar. [@3]

Rural Superintendent of Police Rajesh Singh Prabhakar said the actions were part
of a drive against liquor-prohibition violations and narcotics smuggling in the
Fakuli thana, the local police-station area. [@1] A second Bhaskar account says
the liquor raid followed a Tuesday tip that foreign liquor was stored in a
litchi orchard near Dhondhi bridge and was being moved. [@2] Samaj Jagran dates
the two Fakuli actions to 3 November 2026. [@3] Police said further legal action
and investigation of the smuggling network are under way. [@2]
