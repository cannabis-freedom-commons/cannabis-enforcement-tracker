Police in Kalia Upazila of Narail detained a youth identified as Sumon Molla,
also called Shahidul, with ganja and later produced him before a mobile court
where the judge imposed jail and a fine. [@1] The convicted youth is the son of
Mikail Molla of Kathadura village under Naragati police station. [@1] Kalia
upazila executive officer and mobile-court judge Md. Jinnatul Islam sentenced
him to one year's non-rigorous imprisonment and a 100-taka fine. [@1]

Acting on secret information on Sunday noon, members of Kalia police station's
Baronal investigation centre caught him at his maternal uncle's house in Rajapur
with 11 puria, or small packets, of ganja. [@1] Members of the upazila's Baronal
police investigation centre made the detention around noon on Sunday, 1
November. [@1]

Kalia police station officer-in-charge Idris Ali said the youth was produced
before the mobile court after being arrested with ganja and the judge imposed
jail and a fine. [@1] He said the youth was afterwards sent to the district
jail. [@1]
