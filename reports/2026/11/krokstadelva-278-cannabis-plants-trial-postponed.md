The trial of two men charged over the cannabis plantation in Krokstadelva has
been postponed for a third time. [@1] The two men were charged in September
2025. [@2] The man nicknamed «the gardener» has already been convicted in the
case. [@1] A defence lawyer said a client was in a difficult situation, and
attempts to find the defendant failed. [@1]

Police found 278 cannabis plants in a home in Krokstadelva. [@2] The discovery
was made when officers entered a detached house in Krokstadelva in the run-up to
Christmas 2024. [@1] The plantation was in a residential area. [@1] The plants
had an estimated street value of up to 2.8 million Norwegian kroner. [@2] Crime
scene technicians worked at the house. [@3] Two people were arrested and charged
with aggravated narcotics crime. [@3] Four people were arrested and remanded in
the case. [@2]
