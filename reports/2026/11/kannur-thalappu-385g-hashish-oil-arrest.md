Kannur Town police arrested 37-year-old Muhammed Aslam, of Puthanoor in
Kasaragod district, during a night patrol on the Thalappu stretch of the
national highway in Kannur city. [@1] He was caught with hashish oil. [@1]

Officers seized 3.850 grams of hashish oil, which Aslam was carrying in a
bottle. [@1] Town sub-inspector T.K. Akhil, senior civil police officer Nishanth
and civil police officer Kiran made the seizure on the instructions of Town
inspector Mahesh Kandambeethil. [@1]

Aslam was questioned and then produced before the Kannur court. [@1]
