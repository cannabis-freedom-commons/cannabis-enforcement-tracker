Police in Koothanallur, in Tamil Nadu's Thiruvarur district, arrested two men
after a check on the Kunukkudi main road turned up ganja, and were searching for
a third man who fled. [@1] Acting on a tip that ganja was being sold in the
Kunukkudi area, officers carried out an intensive check and surveillance there
on Thursday. [@1] Investigators identified the three as aged 18, 20 and 27,
residents respectively of Vakkottai, Mannargudi and Kunukkudi, and police
arrested the first two while the 27-year-old from Kunukkudi escaped. [@1]

Police registered a case, seized 1.100 kg of ganja and the two-wheeler the men
were standing beside, and said they were searching for the man who fled. [@1]

The same report says a 74-year-old man in Koothanallur was separately arrested
for selling Tamil Nadu state liquor near his home, with 18 bottles seized; no
cannabis was involved in that case. [@1]
