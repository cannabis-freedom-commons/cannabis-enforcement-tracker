Carabinieri of the Riccione NOR section arrested a 46-year-old man during a road
check in Misano Adriatico after he assaulted officers. [@1,3] The man lives in
Forlì-Cesena province and was already known to law enforcement. [@2,3] When
officers tried to continue their checks, he suddenly reacted violently to avoid
further examination, striking patrol members with kicks and punches. [@1,3]
Officers managed to restrain and secure him before conducting a personal search.
[@1] He was arrested on suspected resistance to a public official, aggravated
intentional injury, unlawful carrying of an offensive weapon and possession of
drugs for dealing. [@1] The next morning he appeared before the judicial
authority for an immediate trial. [@1] The judge validated the arrest and
imposed mandatory reporting to judicial police. [@2,3]

The personal search found 20 grams of cocaine and 25 grams of hashish hidden in
his intimate parts. [@1,3] He had initially handed over a calibre-22 blank
scacciacani-type pistol with a box of ammunition. [@1] A later search of his car
found a precision scale that investigators considered consistent with dealing
activity. [@1,3] The blank pistol and the drugs were seized. [@1] Officers
involved in the struggle sustained minor injuries that were later treated at the
emergency department. [@1]

The episode occurred during daily territorial control duties aimed at combating
drug dealing. [@1] Officers had stopped the man while he was in his car and
begun routine checks. [@1]
