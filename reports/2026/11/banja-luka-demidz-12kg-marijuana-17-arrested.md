Banja Luka police in Operation Demidz broke up an organized drug group that had
sold drugs around Banja Luka, Laktaši and Odžak for almost two years. [@1,2]
Police arrested 17 people, and ten of them were handed over to the Banja Luka
District Prosecution. [@1,2] Police named 36-year-old Slovenian Mladen Samardžija
among those brought before the prosecutor. [@1] The others taken to the
prosecutor were men from Banja Luka, Odžak and Mrkonjić Grad. [@1] Investigators
described Samardžija and fugitive Predrag Petković as the main suppliers, with
Boban Kantar as a key distributor, while the others allegedly acted as dealers
and couriers. [@1,2] The group allegedly sold drugs from early 2025 until the
arrests on 4 November 2026. [@1]

Police searched 20 locations and seized almost 13 kg of drugs and about 100,000
convertible marks in cash. [@1] The seizure included about 12.5 kg of marijuana,
about 500 g of white powder resembling cocaine and a small amount of hashish.
[@1] Officers also seized a car, digital scales, mobile phones, cocaine-purity
tests, two pistols and more than 100,000 marks in cash. [@1] Some members
allegedly weighed, repackaged and stored kilograms of marijuana and cocaine in
rented flats and houses in Trn, Motike and central Banja Luka, while couriers
moved cocaine from Sanski Most to Laktaše and Banja Luka. [@1] Cocaine allegedly
sold for 150 marks up to 7,400 euros, including 400 g sold for 7,400 euros,
while cannabis sold for 50, 100 or 200 marks depending on quantity. [@1]

The district prosecution said its report also covered further suspects, of whom
four were unavailable and four were released to defend themselves from liberty.
[@1] People identified only by initials from Banja Luka and Laktaši were
released, and suspect Predrag Petković remained at large. [@1]
