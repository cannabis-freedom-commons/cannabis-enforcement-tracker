Mandi police station officers in Saharanpur, Uttar Pradesh, arrested a man they
identified as Gulbahar alias Minda on 1 November 2026 with 120 grams of charas,
a hand-made cannabis resin. [@1] He was detained on an empty plot on Chand
Colony Road, in front of the government fairground, while officers patrolled and
checked the area. [@1] Police identified him as the son of Yameen, living in
Chand Colony near Shakuri mosque in the Mandi station area of Saharanpur
district. [@1] A search recovered the 120 grams of charas from him, police said.
[@1]

The station team was checking and patrolling after a tip from an informer, the
report said. [@1] The operation was carried out under the supervision of the
city's first circle officer and led by Mandi station house officer Virendra
Singh Rana, police said. [@1] A case, number 543/26, was registered at Mandi
police station under sections 8 and 20 of the NDPS Act, India's main anti-drug
law. [@1] Police said he was being produced before a court. [@1]
