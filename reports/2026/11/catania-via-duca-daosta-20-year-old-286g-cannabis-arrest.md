Flying-squad and dog-unit officers carrying out checks near Via Duca D'Aosta in
Catania were led to a home when the police dog Rex signalled the presence of
drugs. [@1] A young woman opened the door and appeared very surprised when
officers rang the bell. [@1] The drugs had been hidden in a room of the home
without the knowledge of the woman living there. [@1] She telephoned her
partner, a 20-year-old man from Catania with no criminal record, and had him
return home. [@1] The man admitted responsibility after Rex went straight to the
room where the drugs were hidden. [@1]

The search recovered 252 grams of marijuana and 34 grams of hashish, along with
a precision scale, packaging sachets and a vacuum-sealing machine. [@1]
CataniaToday corroborates the seizure of 252 grams of marijuana and 34 grams of
hashish with a scale, packaging sachets and a vacuum-sealing machine. [@2]

Officers of the general prevention and public rescue office arrested the
20-year-old for possessing drugs with intent to deal, with the presumption of
innocence applying until a final conviction. [@1] After the duty prosecutor at
the Catania court was informed, the man was held in the questura's security
cells to await a fast-track trial. [@1]
