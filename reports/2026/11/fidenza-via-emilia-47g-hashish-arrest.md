Late on the morning of 10 November, Carabinieri officers stopped a car driven by
a 50-year-old Italian resident of the province during a routine check on the Via
Emilia. [@1] The Carabinieri's Fidenza mobile unit, supported by the operational
section, arrested the man after the check led to an anti-drug operation. [@2] On
the magistrate's orders he was held in the Fidenza Company security cells. [@1]
A judge validated the arrest at a fast-track hearing the next morning. [@1] The
reports stress that he remains only seriously suspected and is presumed innocent
pending a final conviction. [@1]

Officers said a search of his pouch found two small cellophane wraps holding
about half a gram of cocaine and about half a gram of heroin. [@3] After his
record showed specific prior drug offences, officers extended the search to his
home, where he voluntarily handed over a kitchen container holding about half a
gram of hashish and said it was all he had. [@1] In a cabinet in a passage
between the bathroom and kitchen, officers found a metal box containing a
hashish slab weighing about 47 grams, two electronic precision scales and a
knife with hashish residue still on the blade. [@1] They also found a
transparent cellophane bag holding three envelopes with 2,200 euros in cash in
mixed denominations, whose origin the man could not explain. [@1]

The Carabinieri said the man had shown nervousness from the first moments,
acting irritated and reluctant to cooperate, which prompted the personal search.
