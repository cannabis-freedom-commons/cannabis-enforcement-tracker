Officers of the Michurinsky intermunicipal police department summed up the
annual complex preventive operation Mak-2026. [@1] The operation aimed to
identify and destroy illegal plantings of drugs of natural origin and to cut
channels supplying drugs to the region. [@1] Following the checks, 13 people
were brought to administrative responsibility. [@1] Police opened three criminal
cases over cultivation and storage of the narcotic-containing cannabis plant and
one case over sale of a potent substance. [@1]

During the raids police seized more than 100 grams of plant-origin drugs and 2.5
grams of a potent substance from illegal circulation. [@1] The operation found
and destroyed 12 wild-cannabis sites with a combined area of more than 18,000
square metres. [@1] Officers issued 10 orders to officials and members of the
public to destroy narcotic-containing plants. [@1] Officers checked all farm
machinery and land plots for possible use in unlawfully growing prohibited
crops. [@1]

Headquarters staff member Anna Izmailova said officers conduct explanatory work
aimed at preventing drug addiction and encouraging vigilance about unlawful
cultivation of narcotic-containing plants. [@1] Police asked people to report
use, sale or cultivation of drugs to the Michurinsky duty unit or by calling
112. [@1]
