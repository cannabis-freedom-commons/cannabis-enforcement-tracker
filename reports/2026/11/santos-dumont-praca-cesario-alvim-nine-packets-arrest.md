Military police arrested an 18-year-old man on suspicion of drug trafficking in
the centre of Santos Dumont, Minas Gerais, in the early hours of Tuesday 10
November 2026. [@1] The arrest came after the police video-monitoring system
showed three people acting suspiciously in a square in the city centre, one of
them handing drugs to the others. [@2] Using the descriptions from the footage,
officers tracked the group down and stopped the 18-year-old, identified as the
person distributing the drugs. [@1]

A personal search found nine buchas of marijuana — small wrapped portions — R$60
in cash and a mobile phone with him. [@1] He was arrested in flagrante, in the
act, for drug trafficking. [@1] Officers took him and the seized material to the
duty Civil Police station in Barbacena for the judicial police procedures. [@2]

The Military Police's 13th Regional Command (13ª RPM) provided the account of
the arrest. [@2] The square, Praça Cesário Alvim, lies in the centre of Santos
Dumont, where the arrest was made. [@1]
