Criminal officers detained two men in a locality in Luzino commune in the early
morning of Monday, 2 November. [@1] The 28-year-old was wanted to be brought to
a prison to serve a six-month prison sentence. [@1] His 40-year-old companion
was in the same place when officers checked them. [@1] Both men were taken to
the Wejherowo County police station and placed in the detention room. [@1] The
collected evidence allowed officers to charge both men with drug possession.
[@1]

Officers found marijuana and mephedrone on the 40-year-old, with the disclosed
substances weighing nearly 20 grams in total. [@1] On a bed in the 28-year-old's
flat, officers found nearly 90 grams of amphetamine, about 70 grams of
mephedrone and 25 grams of marijuana. [@1] Police said they seized nearly 200
grams of various substances in total. [@1] All the substances were secured and
sent to an expert to determine their type and properties. [@1]

The account was attributed to Staff Sergeant Anetta Potrykus, press officer of
the Wejherowo County Police. [@1] Possession of narcotic drugs or psychotropic
substances carries up to three years' imprisonment. [@1] Police said the
28-year-old could face a more serious offence if an expert opinion confirms that
the substances in his flat were a significant quantity. [@1]
