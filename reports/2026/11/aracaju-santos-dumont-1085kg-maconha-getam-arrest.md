Sergipe Military Police Getam officers seized approximately 10.85 kg of
suspected marijuana in the Santos Dumont neighbourhood in the north zone of
Aracaju on Wednesday 11 November 2026, and arrested a man for suspected drug
trafficking. [@1] Getam officers were on routine patrol in the area when they
received a tip that several suspects were using drugs in front of a residence.
[@1]

As officers approached the reported address they noticed a strong characteristic
odour of marijuana and a man fled into the house when he saw the police vehicle.
[@1] Officers then searched the property and found an open cardboard box
containing two scales — one large and one small — and a significant quantity of
suspected marijuana in plastic packaging; the total seized material was
approximately 10.85 kg. [@1]

The suspect told officers he had bought the drugs for R$ 12,000 and intended to
sell them. [@1] The man was taken to the police station with the seized material
for the adoption of legal measures. [@1]
