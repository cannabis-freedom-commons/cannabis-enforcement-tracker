The Kerch city court found a local man guilty of illegal storage of narcotics
and maintenance of a drug den. [@2] The court imposed two years of imprisonment,
suspended, with a two-year probation period. [@1] The verdict had not yet
entered into legal force when the decisions were reported. [@1]

In March 2026 the man bought cannabis seeds from an unknown person at the
Central market in Kerch. [@2] He planted the seeds in a flowerpot in order to
grow a narcotic-containing plant. [@1] Police stopped his criminal activity
during operative-search measures. [@2] He also provided his home and a special
device for drug use to three local residents. [@1]

Authorities qualified his actions under Part 1 of Article 228 for illegal
storage of narcotics and Part 1 of Article 232 for maintaining a den. [@2] The
case was reported by the prosecutor's office of Crimea, and Russian law
prohibits organising or maintaining a drug den and systematically providing
premises for drug use under Article 232. [@1]
