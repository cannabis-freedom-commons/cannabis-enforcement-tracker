Federal revenue officers searched a transport company's depot in Pavuna, in the
north of Rio de Janeiro, on Friday 6 November 2026. [@1] Four parcels held drugs
and a controlled medicine, and the agency estimated the retained goods at R$
356,000. [@1]

Sniffer dogs of the agency's K9 team signalled parcels with drugs hidden inside
helmets, a method the agency said was meant to hinder inspection. [@2] Officers
found 1.070 kg of skunk, described as cannabis in the agency's account. [@1] The
parcels also held about 3,600 ecstasy tablets weighing about 2 kg and 20 grams
of MDMA, which are not counted as cannabis here. [@1] The drug parcels were
addressed to Bahia, Minas Gerais and Paraíba. [@1]

A separate parcel of 246 units of tirzepatide, a controlled weight-loss
medicine, was also retained; it had been sent from Barueri, São Paulo, to Rio de
Janeiro. [@1]

O POVO republished the Agência Brasil report by Douglas Corrêa with the same
account and figures. [@3]

The drugs were handed to the authorities for criminal investigation procedures,
and no arrest was reported. [@2]
