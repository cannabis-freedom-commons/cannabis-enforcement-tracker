Carabinieri at the Cerveteri station arrested a 35-year-old local man, already
known to police, on 5 November 2026. [@1] He was held for breaking an order to
leave the family home and a ban on going near the places his mother, his victim
in a mistreatment case, frequents. [@1] Officers caught him inside his elderly
mother's home during a night patrol, although he was also barred from living in
the municipality. [@1]

A search of his bedroom found small quantities of hashish and a security cash
box belonging to a vending machine, hidden under the bed. [@1] For the hashish
he was reported to the Prefecture of Rome, the provincial prefect's office, for
personal use of drugs rather than charged with a cannabis offence. [@1]
Investigators established that the cash box had been stolen on 23 October from a
business on via Settevene Palo and returned it in full to its owner. [@1]
Further checks linked him to an attempted theft of a coin purse from another
business on the same road in the preceding days. [@1]

He had been under a precautionary order issued on 23 October by the preliminary
investigations judge of the Civitavecchia court after earlier mistreatment of
his mother, with whom he lived. [@1] Besides the arrest for breaching the court
measures, he was reported at liberty for aggravated theft and attempted
aggravated theft. [@1] The case is at the preliminary investigation stage, and
the suspect is presumed innocent until a final conviction. [@1]

The Carabinieri statement said a targeted patrol in the early hours of the
morning found the 35-year-old inside the woman's home in clear breach of the
restrictions on him. [@2]

CCTV from public and private cameras in the area showed the cash box had been
taken from the business on 23 October, which let officers recover it in full.
[@2]

Quotidiano La Voce reported that he had left the family housing unit where he
had been placed before going back to his mother's home. [@3]
