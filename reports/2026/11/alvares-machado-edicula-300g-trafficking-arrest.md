Military Police teams arrested a man in flagrante in Álvares Machado in São
Paulo state after finding marijuana portions at the back of an outbuilding, an
edícula, in the yard of a residence. [@1] The Military Police released the
information on Saturday 7 November 2026, reporting that a sniffer dog assisted
the teams during the search of the yard. [@1]

With the dog's support, teams found 11 portions of marijuana in the backyard of
the residence, behind the outbuilding. [@2] Police said the 11 portions together
weighed 300 grams. [@2] Alongside the drugs, officers seized R$247 in cash and a
mobile phone. [@1] Officers also seized materials used to prepare and package
narcotics. [@1]

The man was detained for drug trafficking. [@1] He had been arrested in
flagrante and remained in custody at the disposal of the courts. [@1]
