Military police arrested a 24-year-old man in Coxim, Mato Grosso do Sul, on the
morning of Saturday 7 November 2026 on suspicion of taking part in a homicide
committed in the municipality. [@1] Officers of the Força Tática, the Military
Police's tactical unit of the 5th Battalion, stopped him on Rua Rosa de Saron in
the city centre as he left a house. [@2]

Seeing the patrol car, he tried to go back inside and threw away a package he
was carrying at his waist, in which officers found a small amount of a substance
similar to marijuana. [@1] Police said he told them he had been meant to deliver
the drug to another person. [@2]

The arrest took place during enquiries into the homicide, and police said they
already had information linking the man to those involved in it and suspecting
him of driving the motorcycle used in the crime. [@2]

The man said he had driven a motorcycle with another person on the back to the
address where the victim was, and that the passenger fired the shots that killed
the victim. [@4] He said he had been pressured by a debt of about R$1,500 in
drugs and had been threatened with death. [@3] He told officers where he had
left the firearm used in the homicide, but searches did not find the weapon.
[@2]

Officers seized the marijuana portion, a mobile phone and a red Honda Pop
motorcycle, and took the man and the material to the Civil Police station. [@1]

The Civil Police will continue investigating the homicide and the participation
of other people in it. [@3]
