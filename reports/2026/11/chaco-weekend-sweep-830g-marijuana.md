Between Friday and Sunday, officers from the different Zone Directorates ran 287
prevention and security operations across the province. [@1] The operations left
85 people detained for various causes. [@1] In the Metropolitan Area, officers
held 51 operations, detained 31 people and recorded five people with capture
orders checked through Si.Ge.Bi. [@1]

Across the sweep, officers seized 436 motorcycles, three cars, five firearms and
14 bladed weapons, plus 829.6 grams of marijuana. [@1] The Metropolitan Area
accounted for 116.6 grams of that marijuana, with 17 motorcycles, a car, two
firearms and two bladed weapons. [@1] In Presidencia Roque Sáenz Peña, 12
operations ended with 23 people detained and seizures of 713 grams of marijuana,
69 motorcycles, two cars, two firearms and four bladed weapons. [@1]

In Castelli, 62 operations ended with five people detained and seizures of 45
motorcycles and a bladed weapon. [@2] In Charata, 28 operations ended with seven
people detained and 50 motorcycles seized. [@2] In Villa Ángela, 74 operations
ended with eight people detained and seizures of 100 motorcycles and a bladed
weapon. [@2] In General San Martín, 60 operations ended with 11 people detained
and seizures of 155 motorcycles, a firearm and six bladed weapons. [@2]
