Kotwali police officers chased several alleged ganja traders in Patchhara
village during a raid on illegal cannabis cultivation. [@1] During the raid, the
officers tried to catch and detain the people they were pursuing through the
village. [@1] After the police caught them, the accused were seen pleading with
the officers. [@1]

The operation in Patchhara village in Cooch Behar-I block was launched on
allegations of illegal ganja cultivation. [@1] Kotwali police station led the
operation and was identified as the agency behind both the raid and the
subsequent destruction. [@1] During the same operation, police destroyed the
alleged illegal ganja cultivation found in the village. [@1]

Patchhara was named as the only site of the action, within Block Number One of
Cooch Behar. [@1] The enforcement action concerned cannabis grown in the village
as a crop, which the report calls ganja. [@1] No formal arrest total was
confirmed in the account, which describes only a chase, a catch and pleading by
the accused. [@1]
