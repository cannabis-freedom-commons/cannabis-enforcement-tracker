Carabineros detained 189 people across the Araucanía region in a mass sweep, the
Ronda de Búsqueda de Prófugos y Control Migratorio, targeting people with
outstanding judicial orders and migrants. [@1] Most of the detentions, 137, were
in the Prefectura Cautín, while the Carabineros prefectures of Villarrica and
Malleco recorded 52 between them. [@1] Colonel Juan Pablo Cofré, head of
operations for the Zona Araucanía, said those captured are suspected of serious
crimes including robbery with violence, and that the operation also took
firearms, bladed weapons and ammunition of various calibres out of circulation.
[@1] Colonel Cofré said the detainees included people wanted over drug
trafficking offences. [@1]

The drug component of the sweep was 2 kilograms 680 grams of prepared marijuana,
which Cofré highlighted as the result of the anti-drug work. [@1] Across the
round, Carabineros carried out 4,227 vehicle and identity checks and issued 190
citations. [@1]

ADN Radio dated its account of the same regional round to 5 November 2026,
before SoyChile's 8 November article, which credits El Austral. [@2]
