Carabinieri from the Vico nel Lazio station arrested a local man caught in the
act of selling hashish near his home in an operation coordinated by the Alatri
company. [@1] Officers watching the area around 2 p.m. on 9 November 2026
noticed suspicious movements and saw him hand a package containing 4.25 grams of
hashish to another local resident in exchange for a 50-euro banknote. [@1] The
patrol intervened immediately, stopping both individuals and recovering the
drugs and the cash. [@1]

A subsequent search of the suspect's residence uncovered an additional 0.74
grams of hashish along with materials for packaging and consuming doses. [@1]
During identification procedures, the man presented an electronic identity card
that had its microchip removed. [@1] Carabinieri seized the tampered identity
document for further criminal investigation. [@1]

The man was placed under house arrest at his home at the disposal of the Public
Prosecutor's Office of Frosinone. [@1] He was held on suspicion of supplying
narcotic drugs. [@2] The case is in the preliminary investigation phase, and the
suspect is presumed innocent until any final conviction. [@1]
