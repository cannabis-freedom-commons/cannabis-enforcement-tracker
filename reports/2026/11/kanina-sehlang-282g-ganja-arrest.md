Sadar Kanina police detained a man with narcotic substances in Sehlang village
during a drive against drug trafficking and illegal sale. [@1] The detained man
was identified as Rajbir, a resident of Sehlang village. [@1,2] PSI Sahil of Sadar
Kanina police station was present with his police team in the Bus Adda Kheri
area for patrolling and crime prevention. [@1] The team received information
that Sehlang resident Rajbir was illegally selling narcotics in front of his Raj
Mishthan Bhandar shop at the Sehlang bus stand. [@1,2] Police went to Sehlang
village and detained the suspect. [@1] The search was carried out in the
presence of a duty magistrate. [@1]

The search recovered a small white packet containing suspected chitta from the
jacket pocket worn by the accused. [@1] Checking a plastic crate kept in front
of the shop found ganja in another packet. [@1] Police weighed the two recovered
substances separately on an electronic scale. [@1] The weighing showed 2.55
grams of suspected chitta including polythene and 282 grams of ganja. [@1,2] The
accused could produce no valid licence or permit for the substances, and police
separately sealed and took possession of the recovered substances. [@1]

A case was registered against the accused at Sadar Kanina police station under
the relevant sections of the NDPS Act. [@1,2] Further investigation and legal
action in the case were continuing. [@1] This report counts only the separately
weighed 282 grams of ganja as cannabis and excludes the 2.55 grams of suspected
chitta as the non-cannabis component of this mixed seizure. [@1]
