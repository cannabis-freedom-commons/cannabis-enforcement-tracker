Five drivers in Salzburg lost their driving licences after police stops. [@1]
Police stopped five drivers on 10 and 11 November 2026 who had alcohol or
illegal drugs in their blood. [@1] All five were reported to the competent
authorities for the violations found. [@3]

During checks with a focus on alcohol and drugs in Bischofshofen, officers
stopped a 22-year-old Croatian citizen. [@2] His alcohol pre-test was negative
but his drug test reacted to cannabis, and a doctor confirmed he was unfit to
drive. [@3] On Wednesday morning in the Zell am See district, police stopped a
21-year-old and a 42-year-old driver from Pinzgau, both showing signs of
impairment. [@2] Neither had drunk any alcohol, but their drug tests reacted,
one to cannabis and one to cocaine, and a doctor found both unfit to drive. [@2]

The series began early on Tuesday morning in the city of Salzburg, shortly
before 6 a.m. with a crash on the Schallmooser Hauptstraße. [@1] A 27-year-old
woman from Flachgau left her lane and knocked down two traffic signs in the
middle of the road. [@1] She was unhurt, and her breathalyser showed 1.38 per
mille. [@1] Shortly before 8 p.m., a 32-year-old Flachgau driver was stopped in
the municipality of Kuchl and his breathalyser showed 1.1 per mille. [@2]
