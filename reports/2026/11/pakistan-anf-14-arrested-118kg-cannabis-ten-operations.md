The Anti-Narcotics Force arrested 14 suspects, including a woman, and seized
approximately 158 kilogrammes of narcotics during 10 operations in different
cities across the country. [@1] Six of the operations were carried out under
domestic drug interdiction, two under inter-provincial interdiction and two as
part of international drug interdiction efforts. [@3] The force said the
operations targeted domestic, inter-provincial and international drug
trafficking networks. [@1]

In Quetta, 60 kilograms of hashish was recovered from a vehicle during an
intelligence-based operation near Kuchlak Bypass, and one suspect was arrested.
[@1] In Peshawar, 10.8 kilograms of hashish was recovered from a truck near
Motorway Chowk on Ring Road, where the drugs had been concealed beneath the
driving seat, and one suspect was arrested. [@1] In Mansehra, three individuals
were arrested after 12 kilograms of hashish was recovered from a vehicle at
Kotkay Bridge on Balakot Road. [@1] At the Islamabad Motorway Toll Plaza, 13.2
kilograms of hashish was recovered from wooden planks loaded on a truck, and one
suspect was arrested. [@1] In another operation at the same toll plaza, 12
kilograms of hashish was recovered from a vehicle's spare tyre, and one suspect
was arrested. [@1] In Karachi, 10.4 kilograms of cannabis was seized from a
vehicle near Simon Chowrangi in the SITE area, and two suspects were arrested.
[@1] The force valued the total haul at Rs 28.2 million. [@1]

In Peshawar, 22.8 kilograms of opium was seized from a vehicle near Gul’s Garden
on University Road and two suspects were arrested. [@1] In Gojra, Toba Tek Singh
district, one suspect was arrested with 7 kilograms of crystal methamphetamine
(ice) recovered from a truck on Mochi Wala Road. [@2] At the cargo office of
Jinnah International Airport in Karachi, 7.6 kilograms of crystal
methamphetamine concealed in decorative lamps was recovered from a parcel
destined for Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, and a woman suspect was arrested. [@1] At
Allama Iqbal International Airport in Lahore, 2.220 kilograms of heroin was
seized from the luggage of a passenger travelling to Malaysia. [@1] Cases were
registered against the arrested suspects under the Control of Narcotic
Substances Act, 1997, and further investigations are under way. [@1]
