A 50-year-old farmer was arrested in Badegaon Kekdiya for allegedly growing
cannabis among vegetable crops. [@2] Police identified the farmer as Shriram,
son of Manik Dongardiye, and searched a field beside his home in Badegaon
Kekdiya. [@1] On 4 November, Multai police received an informant's tip that
Shriram was growing cannabis among vegetables in his field. [@1] Police teams
went to the location and searched the field beside Shriram's home. [@1] The
cannabis plants were growing between brinjal and okra crops. [@1]

Police seized the green, moist cannabis plants found in the field. [@2] The
seized plants weighed 26 kg 50 g (26.05 kg) in total. [@2] Police estimated the
plants' value at 220,000 rupees. [@1] The Multai police station registered the
case as crime No. 913/26. [@2]

Station in-charge Vikas Patel, sub-inspector Nitin Uike and other officers took
part in the operation. [@1] Police said they informed Shriram of the reasons for
his arrest and seized the material under legal procedure. [@1] Officers were
questioning Shriram and further legal proceedings were continuing. [@2]
