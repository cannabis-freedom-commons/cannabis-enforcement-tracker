Sargodha police arrested five alleged drug dealers during a crackdown against
drug peddlers and illegal weapons holders in different areas on Friday 13
November 2026. [@1] Raiding teams from different police stations conducted
operations in various localities to make the arrests. [@1] Police identified the
five suspects as Tahir, Bashir, Rasheed, Waqas and Khuda Yar. [@1]

Police recovered four kilograms of hashish from their possession. [@1] The same
recovery also included three kilograms of opium along with poppy and ice, which
are not counted in the cannabis figures. [@1] Police also recovered two pistols,
one gun, one revolver, 88 bullets, 102 litres of liquor and 13 litres of wine in
the same operations. [@1]

Further investigation was underway. [@1]
