Officers of the Western Province North Crime Division arrested a 25-year-old man
in Madiwela, Kotte, during a night raid on 6 November 2026 and found heroin,
crystal methamphetamine and Kush in his possession. [@1]

Police said officers seized 558 grams of Kush from the suspect, alongside the
heroin and methamphetamine taken in the same raid. [@1]

The suspect is a resident of Madiwela in Kotte, and the raid was carried out by
a team of officers acting on information received during the night. [@1]

The Western Province North Crime Division, the police unit that carried out the
raid, said it was continuing its investigations into the case. [@1]

Kush is a cannabis product, and its 558 grams are the only cannabis in the haul;
the heroin and methamphetamine found in the same raid are not counted as
cannabis figures here. [@1]
