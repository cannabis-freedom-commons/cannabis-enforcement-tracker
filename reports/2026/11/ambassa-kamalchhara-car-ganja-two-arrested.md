Ambassa police station officers stopped an unregistered new luxury car in the
Kamalchhara area of Ambassa, Dhalai district, Tripura, on 9 November 2026 and
found a large quantity of ganja, the local name for cannabis, inside. [@1,2]
Police detained two people in the case, including the driver of the car. [@1,2]

The two detained were identified as Ihan Debbarma and Sanjay Debbarma, both
residents of Hezamara under Sidhai Mohanpur. [@1,2] Police said the traffickers
planned to take the consignment out of Tripura on a side route, avoiding the
regular police check post at Bedpagar in Ambassa, and suspected they were trying
to pass through Kamalchhara. [@1,2] Officers moved on a tip-off from an
undisclosed source and searched the car. [@1,2] Police said their initial
assessment was that the ganja was being taken out of the state to be smuggled
onward. [@1]

Police said they had begun an investigation into whether others were part of the
network, where the ganja came from and where it was meant to be sent. [@1,2]

Tripura Times reported that the cannabis was recovered from a newly purchased luxury vehicle, an account that also describes the intercepted car as running without a registration number plate. [@2]
