Dhaka Metropolitan Police arrested 534 people and filed 53 cases during 24 hours
of regular drives across the capital on November 12, 2026. [@1] Mirpur division
accounted for the most arrests with 147, followed by Tejgaon with 91, Uttara
with 55, Lalbagh with 60, Motijheel with 52, Gulshan with 50, Wari with 48 and
Ramna with 26. [@1] One person was arrested by the Special Reserve Unit and four
by the Detective Branch. [@1]

Police recovered 6 kilograms of cannabis, 207 yaba pills, 10 bottles of
phensedyl, 925 counterfeit Tk 1,000 notes, seven mobile phones, three crude
bombs, three fragments of exploded crude bombs, and rescued one victim. [@1]

The arrests were made during drives in different areas of the capital on
Thursday (November 12). [@2]

DMP said further legal proceedings against the arrested individuals were
underway. [@2]
