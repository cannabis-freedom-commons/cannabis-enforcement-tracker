Egyptian police killed two men known as Abu Yasin and Mahdi, both wanted for
drug and weapons cases, during an operation against criminal hotspots in Nasr
El-Nuba, Aswan. [@1] Al-Masry Al-Youm reported the same killing of the two
wanted men in Nasr El-Nuba. [@2]

The security confrontation left police holding a Grengov machine gun and two
automatic weapons, along with quantities of hashish and shabu, Egypt's name for
crystal meth, and a sum of money, the Aswan security directorate said. [@1]
El-Balad reported the same seizure of a Grengov machine gun, two automatic
weapons, hashish, shabu and cash. [@3] No weight was published for either drug,
and the reports name no other arrests, saying only that legal measures were
taken. [@1]

Aswan governor Amr Lashin praised the Aswan security directorate, led by Major
General Abdullah Jalal, an assistant interior minister, for chasing dangerous
criminal elements and confronting the trade in drugs and unlicensed weapons.
[@1] He thanked the security bodies for their continuing pursuit of criminal
elements and their confrontation of drug trafficking. [@3]
