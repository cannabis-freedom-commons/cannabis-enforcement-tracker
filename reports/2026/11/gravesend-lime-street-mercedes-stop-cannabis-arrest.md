Kent Police officers patrolling Lime Street in Gravesend stopped a Mercedes at
around 7pm on Thursday, 5 November, after it looked suspicious to them. [@1] The
roadside search of the car recovered four bags of white powder and six bags of
cannabis. [@1]

Officers then searched a nearby private property as part of their investigation
into suspected drug supply, seizing mobile phones, cash, drug-related
paraphernalia and a black bin bag containing a large quantity of cannabis. [@1]
A 43-year-old Albanian man was arrested on suspicion of possession with intent
to supply Class A and Class B drugs and on suspicion of driving while
disqualified. [@1]

The man has been released on conditional bail while the police investigation
continues. [@1] Police have not disclosed the full amount of drugs seized or
their estimated street value, and have not confirmed what the white powder was.
[@1]
