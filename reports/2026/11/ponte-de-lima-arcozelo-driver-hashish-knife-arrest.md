Guarda Nacional Republicana officers detained a 52-year-old man on 6 November
during a road check in Arcozelo, in the municipality of Ponte de Lima. [@1] The
officers stopped his vehicle and noticed he showed signs of nervousness, the
Guarda's Viana do Castelo territorial command said. [@1] In the car they found a
bladed weapon and drugs, the Guarda said. [@1] O MINHO reported that the
challenge happened in what it called an “operação stop”, a roadside stop
operation. [@3]

The suspects' weapon was a bladed weapon; O MINHO described it as a folding
knife. [@3] Semanário Alto Minho reported the same seizure of a bladed weapon,
25.75 doses of cocaine (crack) and ten doses of hashish. [@4]

The suspect was detained and made an arguido, a formal suspect in Portuguese
proceedings. [@1] The facts were sent to the Judicial Court of Ponte de Lima.
[@1] The Guarda announced the case on 9 November, three days after the stop.
