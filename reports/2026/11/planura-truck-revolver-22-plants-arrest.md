Military Police detained two suspects in Planura on Monday 9 November 2026
during an operation that seized a .38-calibre revolver, ammunition, marijuana
portions and cannabis-like plants. [@1] The Polícia Militar, the state's
uniformed military police, began the operation at a ponto-base, a stationary
police checkpoint, where officers spotted a truck whose occupants behaved
suspiciously. [@1] Officers stopped the truck and found a .38-calibre revolver
in the driver's waistband. [@1] The weapon held six cartridges, had no
identifiable serial number, and the driver presented no firearm documentation.
[@1] Inside the truck, officers found one portion of a substance resembling
marijuana, and the driver claimed ownership of the seized materials. [@1]

After obtaining the suspect's consent, officers searched his residence. [@1]
Inside the house, officers found 83 intact rounds and 13 spent rounds of
.38-calibre ammunition, along with seeds and flowers of suspected marijuana and
a scale. [@1] In another room of the house, officers found three greenhouses
containing two plants with cannabis-like characteristics and 20 germinated
seeds. [@1]

In total, officers seized two large and 20 small marijuana plants, 40 flowers,
three portions and 50 seeds, along with the revolver, the 83 rounds and the
scale. [@1] Both suspects were taken with the seized materials to the Civil
Police station for the applicable legal procedures. [@1]
