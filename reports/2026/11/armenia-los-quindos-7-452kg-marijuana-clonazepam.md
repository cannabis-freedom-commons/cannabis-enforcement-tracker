National Police units of the Seccional de Investigación Criminal, the criminal
investigation branch known as SIJIN, seized 7,452 grams of marijuana in the Los
Quindos neighbourhood of Armenia, the capital of Colombia's Quindío department.
[@1] The same operation found 98 boxes of clonazepam, a sedative medicine under
special control, in the neighbourhood. [@1]

The seizure came during judicial police work that located the marijuana and the
controlled medicines. [@1] According to the investigation, the marijuana and
pills were to be distributed at sales points around the city and sold in minimum
doses. [@1] The police described the seizure as part of actions to identify and
hit networks that store and distribute illegal substances in Armenia. [@1]

The marijuana and boxes of clonazepam were placed at the disposal of the
Fiscalía URI of Armenia, the prosecutor's office that will handle the judicial
steps in the investigation. [@1]
