Military police of Mato Grosso's 19th Battalion apprehended a 17-year-old girl
suspected of involvement in drug trafficking in Tangará da Serra in the early
hours of Thursday 5 November 2026. [@1,3,4] The operation seized two tablets and 33
portions of a substance analogous to marijuana, a police formulation used for
suspected cannabis before laboratory confirmation. [@1,4]

The action began with a tip that a woman was keeping drugs at a house and
repeatedly leaving it to deliver narcotics to other parts of the city. [@1,4]
Officers monitored the address, found a girl matching the description and
stopped her in front of a house carrying a bag with three marijuana portions.
[@1,3,4] Police then searched the girl's home and found another 30 portions and two
tablets of the same drug, a precision scale and other materials used in
trafficking. [@1,4] The girl told officers nothing about the origin of the drug
and was taken to the city's police station to record the case and take further
legal steps. [@1,4]

Military Police did not report the total weight of the drug or give details of
the additional material seized. [@2] No information was released about other
people possibly involved or about the origin of the seized drug. [@2]
