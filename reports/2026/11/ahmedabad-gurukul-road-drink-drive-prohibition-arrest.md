Vastrapur police arrested Mihir Niranjan Sharma, a manager at an Axis Bank
branch on Drive-In Road, after a hit-and-run crash on Gurukul Road. [@1]
Vastrapur police registered a case against Sharma for drink-driving and under
the prohibition law, and arrested him. [@1]

A car driven at high speed struck an Activa scooter near Gurukul Crossroads,
throwing its rider off, and the scooter lodged in the front of the car. [@1]
Instead of stopping, the driver kept going and dragged the scooter about one
kilometre from Gurukul Crossroads to Doordarshan Crossroads. [@1] Residents near
Doordarshan Crossroads stopped the car and pulled the driver out, and police
found he was drunk. [@1]

Vastrapur police seized a half-filled liquor bottle from the car. [@1]

Traffic police inspector A B Mori said the scooter rider filed no complaint of
his vehicle being hit by a drunk driver, and the rider's home in Gurukul was
found locked when officers went there. [@2] Gujarat Samachar reported that the
Activa was parked with no one riding it when the car hit it. [@3] The crash came
days after a drunk-driving crash in Nikol killed three people. [@2]
