An operational team of Special Response Battalion-14 arrested a couple with 06
kg of ganja at Ashekpur Bypass Mor on the Dhaka-Tangail highway under Tangail
Sadar at about 04:05 on 2 November 2026. [@1] Those arrested were 35-year-old
Farhad Mia and 21-year-old Mosammat Tanzina, both of Cumilla district. [@1] The
operation followed a tip that drugs were being smuggled by passengers disguised
as travellers on a Chattogram-to-Dinajpur Shyamoli NR bus. [@1] On that
information, CPC-3 of SRB-14 Tangail set up a checkpoint in the Ashekpur Bypass
area of the Dhaka-Tangail highway. [@2] Officers stopped the bus late at night,
searched it, and detained the couple travelling as passengers. [@1]

In front of witnesses, officers recovered 6 kg of ganja concealed in a special
way inside the bag carried by the couple. [@1] Officers also seized one mobile
phone allegedly used for buying and selling drugs. [@2]

The SRB accounts say the husband and wife had long collected illegal ganja from
border areas and other parts of the country and sold it in different places.
[@1] A case was filed at Tangail Sadar Police Station and the detainees were
handed over there with the seized material for legal action. [@1]
