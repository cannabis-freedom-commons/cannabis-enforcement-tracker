Officers of Burkina Faso's National Police seized about 350 kilograms of
cannabis on Route nationale 4, at the Ouagadougou bypass. [@1] The Saaba
district police commissariat and the Ouagadougou republican security company
made the stop during Operation WIBGA 3, and arrested the main suspect in the
traffic. [@1] Teams had been deployed at several junctions and in areas the
police called crime-prone in the commune of Saaba. [@1] During a control they
intercepted a tricycle carrying three and a half sacks of cannabis, which the
police estimated at 350 kilograms. [@1] Police described the load as three and a
half sacks of 100 kilograms each, about 350 kilograms, and placed the stop on
the Ouagadougou-Fada axis of Route nationale 4. [@2]

According to the first elements of the inquiry, the cannabis had come from a
foreign country and was brought into Burkina Faso already packed. [@1] Truck
drivers hid the goods in their vehicles to avoid security checks. [@1] Once in
Ouagadougou the cannabis was collected and sold wholesale to customers. [@1]
Customers paid in cash, including through Orange Money and other transfer
services. [@2] People involved in the circuit took care not to leave traces that
would identify them. [@1]

The National Police thanked citizens who had helped the operation and asked the
public to report suspicious behaviour on the toll-free numbers 17, 16 and 1010.
[@1]
