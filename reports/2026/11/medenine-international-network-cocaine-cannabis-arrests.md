The National Guard's Research and Inspection Brigade in Medenine dismantled an
international drug-trafficking network after a series of raids targeting the
city of Medenine and its outskirts during the past week. [@1] Officers arrested
three people who were active in the network, which extended its activity to
neighbouring countries, and seized 250 grams of raw cocaine during the
operation. [@1]

In the same security campaigns, officers arrested a person who was wanted under
ten search notices and seized a quantity of cannabis held in his possession.
[@1] Officers also arrested a second person who was wanted under three search
notices and seized narcotic pills found in his possession. [@1] All of the
detained people, five in total across these operations, were referred to the
judiciary for further legal action. [@1]

A security source said the seized raw cocaine was considered especially
dangerous because its quantity could triple in size after the preparation stage.
[@1] The same raids also seized varying and unspecified quantities of cannabis
and narcotic pills in addition to the cocaine, with no separate weights
published for the cannabis component. [@1]
