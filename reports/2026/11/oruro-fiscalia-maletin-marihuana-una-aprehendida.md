Oruro's Fiscalía Especializada de Sustancias Controladas seized marijuana
concealed in a black suitcase in an operation. [@1]

The seizure took place on Monday 9 November 2026 during an investigation by the
Public Ministry. [@1] Officers found three bags of greenish substance inside the
black suitcase, and a field test confirmed it was marijuana. [@1] One person was
detained and is awaiting a custody hearing. [@1]

The departmental prosecutor said the exact location of the finding is part of
the investigation's preparatory stage. [@1]
