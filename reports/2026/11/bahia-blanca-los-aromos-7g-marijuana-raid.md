Police in Bahía Blanca searched two homes as part of an investigation into an
armed robbery. [@1] A court authorised the searches at addresses on Los Aromos
at number 2800, and the Grupo de Apoyo Departamental (GAD) and the Comando de
Patrullas took part. [@1] In the second home officers found nothing linked to
the robbery but seized a wrapper holding 7.2 grams of marijuana. [@1]

Three people were detained: a 17-year-old described as one of the robbery
suspects, a 22-year-old woman and a 19-year-old man. [@1] At the first home
police seized a .32-calibre revolver and detained the 17-year-old for
notification. [@1] In one room they found 10 grams of cocaine and a precision
scale, and detained the 22-year-old woman over that find. [@1] At the second
home the 19-year-old was detained over the marijuana find. [@1] He and the
22-year-old woman were linked to proceedings under Law 23.737, Argentina's drug
law. [@1]

The case began with a complaint from a man who said armed men had intercepted
him and a group of friends in the street and stolen a yellow Topmega bicycle
with 29-inch wheels. [@1] The UFIJ No. 19, a prosecutor's investigation unit,
the juvenile criminal responsibility prosecutor's office No. 1 and the juvenile
guarantee court No. 1 of the Bahía Blanca judicial department are handling the
case. [@1]
