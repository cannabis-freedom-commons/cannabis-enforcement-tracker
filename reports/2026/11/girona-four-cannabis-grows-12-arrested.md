Mossos d'Esquadra officers arrested eight men and four women between 3 and 5
November 2026 over four cannabis grows in the province of Girona. [@1,4,5,6,8] Four of the twelve arrested had previous police records. [@4,8] The twelve, aged between 20 and 48, were held over drug trafficking, electricity fraud and an offence against public health. [@4,5,7,8] The grows
were dismantled in three homes and an annex of an industrial warehouse in Lloret
de Mar, Anglès, Llagostera and Maçanet de la Selva, the Catalan police said in a
statement. [@2,4,5,8] Police treated the four sites as independent investigations
rather than one network. [@1] Investigators from the units of three Mossos d'Esquadra stations took part, and the inquiry began in summer 2026, when officers suspected a home in an urbanisation in Lloret de Mar was being used to grow cannabis. [@4,8]

At Lloret de Mar officers seized 410 plants and 775 grams of buds, and arrested
two people. [@1,3,4,6,7,8] In the Lloret de Mar house, the 410 plants were found in the garage and a bag holding the 775 grams of buds was found in a nearby storage room. [@8] The two arrested in Lloret de Mar were a 43-year-old man and a 37-year-old woman with no previous police records, who were brought before the duty court in Blanes the next morning. [@8] The searches in Anglès on 4 November seized 200 plants in the
growth phase. [@2,7,8] A woman was arrested at Anglès and a second man was arrested
there the same day, with support from the Arro and officers of the Citizen
Security Unit (USC). [@1,7,8] Officers from the Santa Coloma de Farners station carried out the Anglès operation, arresting a 23-year-old woman during the search and a 31-year-old man with a previous record that afternoon. [@8] The Llagostera operation on 5 November seized 524
cannabis cuttings and 66.5 kilograms of buds, and four people were arrested.
[@1,3,6,7,8] The Llagostera raid was carried out by the investigative unit of the Girona station, and those arrested were three men aged 24, 25 and 29 and a 32-year-old woman. [@8]

In Maçanet de la Selva, suspicions raised by the local police prompted a search
hours later, in which 245 plants and 2.9 kilograms of buds were seized and four
people arrested. [@1,3,6,7,8] After smelling a strong odour of marijuana from outside, Santa Coloma de Farners Mossos officers obtained a judicial warrant to enter the Maçanet de la Selva home, where the 245 plants were 1.5 metres tall and the buds weighed 2,939 grams. [@8] The four people arrested in Maçanet de la Selva were three men aged 20, 23 and 48 and a 23-year-old woman, all of whom except the 20-year-old man had previous records. [@8] The electricity company put the unbilled energy fraud at the Maçanet de la Selva house at 8,358 euros. [@5,7,8]
