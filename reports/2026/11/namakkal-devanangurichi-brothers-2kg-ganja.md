Police received information that ganja and intoxicating tablets were being
stored and sold in Devanangurichi near Tiruchengode. [@1] A police team led by
Prohibition Enforcement Wing Inspector Selvalakshmi searched Kandasamy Nagar in
Devanangurichi following that information. [@1] During the search of 35-year-old
Somasundaram's house, officers found 2 kg of ganja concealed in a car parked at
the house. [@1] Police seized the car and arrested Somasundaram in connection
with the stored ganja. [@1]

In the same operation at Kandasamy Nagar, police separately seized 60
intoxicating tablets from Somasundaram's 33-year-old brother Suresh and arrested
him. [@1] Ganja is the Tamil term used for cannabis in this case, while the 60
tablets were described only as intoxicating tablets and are not counted in the
cannabis quantity. [@1]

Police said the two arrested men are brothers and that both already face various
cases in Salem, Namakkal and Erode districts. [@1] The ganja seizure, the tablet
seizure and the two arrests were reported together as one local enforcement
operation in Devanangurichi. [@1]
