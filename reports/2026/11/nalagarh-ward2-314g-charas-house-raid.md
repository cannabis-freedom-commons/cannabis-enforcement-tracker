Police raided a house in Ward-2 of Nalagarh in Solan and registered a case under
the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act against the house
owner, Jaswinder Kumar, before starting an investigation. [@1] Superintendent of
Police Vinod Dhiman confirmed the case and said police are investigating it
thoroughly. [@1] Police said they are now investigating who else, along with the
accused, was involved in the illegal trade. [@1]

Officers seized 314 grams of charas during the raid on the house and took the
narcotic into custody. [@1] The charas was found in a cupboard as nine sticks,
21 small balls and eight flat cakes. [@1] Police also recovered from the spot a
weighing scale allegedly used in the illegal trade. [@1] All the recovered items
were taken into custody and a case against the accused was registered at
Nalagarh police station. [@1]

Police searched Jaswinder Kumar's house on the basis of secret information. [@1]
The combined weight of the three forms of charas recovered was found to be 314
grams. [@1] After taking possession of the narcotic, police registered the case
and began the investigation. [@1]
