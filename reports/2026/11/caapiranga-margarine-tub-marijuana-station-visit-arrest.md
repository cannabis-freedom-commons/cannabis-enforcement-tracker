A 24-year-old woman was arrested in the act on suspicion of drug trafficking at
the police station in Caapiranga, a municipality 134 kilometres from Manaus,
after she tried to deliver marijuana hidden in a margarine tub during a visit to
a prisoner. [@1] The arrest was made on Thursday, 5 November, during the
inspection of food and hygiene products brought for detainees. [@1] The
operation was carried out by the Amazonas Civil Police through the 32nd
Interactive Police Station (DIP) in Caapiranga, with support from the municipal
civil guard. [@2] The arrest took place inside the station in the town centre,
during a visit to a detainee. [@4]

Delegate Claudenor Medeiros said officers were inspecting the items brought by
relatives when they found a portion of marijuana inside the margarine container.
[@1] Before the search, the woman told officers she had bought and organised all
the products she was carrying herself. [@1] The woman had brought food and
personal hygiene products for a relative in custody, and officers also seized
her mobile phone. [@3]

She was taken through the legal procedures and was due to appear at a custody
hearing, accused of aggravated drug trafficking, and remains at the disposal of
the courts. [@2] The amount of marijuana seized and the identity of the prisoner
who was to receive the products were not disclosed. [@1]
