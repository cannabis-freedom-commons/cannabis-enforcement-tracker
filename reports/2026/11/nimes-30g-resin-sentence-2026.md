On patrol in Nîmes, national police officers came across Fabrice as he
approached them as if to offer them drugs. [@1] Officers grew suspicious because
of a plastic bag hidden under his sweatshirt. [@1] The man was arrested in
possession of cocaine, cannabis and a small amount of cash. [@1] When he
realised the men facing him were police officers, he fled and fell a few metres
further on with his load. [@1] His drug-trafficking case brought Fabrice before
the Nîmes criminal court. [@1] Surrounded by police officers, he was taken to
the Nîmes remand prison to serve the sentence. [@1]

Officers seized a mobile phone and many small pouches from him. [@1] The haul
totalled 30 grams of cannabis resin and just over 15 grams of encapsulated
cocaine. [@1] From the dock, he refused to speak and chose to remain silent.
[@1] The prosecutor, describing a man with a heavy criminal record, requested
five years in prison. [@1]

The 50-year-old had already served a one-year custodial term in a
drug-trafficking case. [@1] His previous sentence had ended only days before the
3 November offence, as he had been released from detention on 24 October. [@1]
