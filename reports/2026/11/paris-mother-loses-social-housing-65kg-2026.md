A Paris mother lost her social-housing lease after the discovery of drug
trafficking involving her son. [@1] The justice system nevertheless accepts that
she knew nothing about his activities. [@1] She paid her rent regularly, had
never drawn complaints from neighbours and did not know about her son's illegal
activity. [@1] That clean record was not enough to let her keep her social
housing. [@1]

On Tuesday 3 November 2026 the Paris judicial court ordered termination of her
lease after several kilos of cannabis and thousands of euros in cash were found
in her son's bedroom. [@1] The haul was put at 6.5 kg of cannabis hidden in her
son's bedroom, which she did not know about. [@1] Her son had hidden the 6.5 kg
stash in his own bedroom inside the family home in Paris. [@1]
