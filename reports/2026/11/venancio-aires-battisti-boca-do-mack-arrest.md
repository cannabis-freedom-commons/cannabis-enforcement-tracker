Brigada Militar officers from Venâncio Aires arrested a man and a woman on the
morning of Wednesday, 4 November 2026, in Bairro Battisti, Venâncio Aires, Rio
Grande do Sul. [@1] One arrest account describes the two as a couple and the
arrests as for alleged drug trafficking. [@1] Officers arrested the two in
flagrante at about 7:30 a.m.; the pair are 21 and 29 years old. [@2] Military
police said they detained the pair while they were restocking a drug-selling
point known as the “Boca do Mack”. [@2] That sales point is named after a man
nicknamed Macdonald, who is on the run and is also suspected in a September
homicide in Bairro Battisti. [@2]

Officers seized 49 portions of marijuana and 18 portions of cocaine during the
operation, as well as mobile phones. [@1] The second account gives the same
counts of 49 marijuana portions and 18 cocaine wraps and specifies that three
cellphones were taken. [@2]

Both detainees were taken to the Delegacia de Polícia, the police station, for
the required legal procedures. [@1] The police said the two were being presented
at the DPPA, the ready-response police station. [@2] Police delegate Paulo César
Schirrmann ordered that both be formally booked for drug trafficking and sent to
the Venâncio Aires State Penitentiary, known as the Peva. [@2]
