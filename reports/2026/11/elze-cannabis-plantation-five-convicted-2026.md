A court in Hildesheim convicted all five defendants over a cannabis plantation
in Elze. [@1] The five defendants stood trial at the Hildesheim regional court
on the charge that they built and operated a cannabis plantation in Elze. [@1]

At the opening of the trial in mid-October, four of the five defendants admitted
taking part in building and running the plantation, while one insisted he was
innocent. [@1] The newspaper's heading for the verdict reads
"Bewährungsstrafen", meaning suspended prison sentences. [@1]

The five on trial were three men and two women. [@2] Prosecutors accuse them of
growing about eight kilograms of marijuana on the plantation and selling it.
[@2] The plantation had already drawn the attention of investigators in March
2020. [@2] The submitted report says the defendants wanted to increase their own
wealth by selling cannabis they grew themselves. [@1]
