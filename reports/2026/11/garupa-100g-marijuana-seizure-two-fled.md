During Misiones police's province-wide security operation, officers of the
Investigaciones Division of Regional Unit X carried out preventive and
investigative work on property-related crimes in Garupá. [@1] At the
intersection of 9 de Julio and El Fortín streets in Santa Clara barrio, officers
observed two men who fled into scrubland and abandoned a polyethylene bag. [@1]

Testing by the Dangerous Drugs Division of Candelaria confirmed the bag's plant
material was marijuana, weighing 100 grams and estimated at 400,000 pesos. [@1]
By order of the Federal Court the marijuana was seized and the case file sent to
the Prosecutor's Office; police continue the search for the two men. [@1]
