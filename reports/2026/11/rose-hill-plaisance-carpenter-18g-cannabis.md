Officers of the ADSU, Mauritius's Anti-Drug and Smuggling Unit, searched the
home of a 38-year-old carpenter living at Plaisance, Rose-Hill, on Sunday 8
November 2026. The search began at about 09 h 20. [@1] During the search,
investigators found and seized two glass jars containing a total of 18 grams of
cannabis. [@1] One jar had a red lid and held a transparent plastic bag tied at
the end; the other had a grey lid and also contained some of the same substance.
[@1]

The carpenter was arrested after the seizure. [@1] He was released on parole
after being questioned, and the investigation was continuing. [@1] The account
names no charge against him and gives no court date. [@1]
