Special-team officers led by Prabhakaran and Tamilselvan carried out the
previous day's railway checks. [@1] The team inspected the Dhanbad–Alappuzha
Express. [@1] Officers boarded at Karuppur and checked the train as far as
Salem. [@1] In the rear general coach an unattended bag near the toilet held 5
kg of ganja. [@1] The special team seized the 5 kg of ganja. [@1] No suspect was
named or arrested for the railway recoveries, which were described as unclaimed.
[@1] Salem Railway Inspector Thilagavathi registered a case and is
investigating. [@1]

In the same drive the team checked the Dibrugarh–Kanyakumari Express and found 6
kg of tobacco products in an unattended plastic cover in the rear unreserved
coach. [@1] The officers handed the seized ganja and tobacco products to the
Salem Railway Police. [@1] The 6 kg tobacco haul is not cannabis and is excluded
from the cannabis quantity in this report. [@1]

Railway police formed the special team to stop narcotics moving by train from
northern states via Salem to Kerala. [@1] The checks happened on 2 November
2026. [@1]
