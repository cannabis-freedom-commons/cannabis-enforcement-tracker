Chhindwara Dehat police arrested Rohan Kanojia, 24, of Shivnagar Colony, as he
waited by a water tank at BTI Ground to sell ganja, a Hindi term for cannabis.
[@1] Officers seized 1.075 kg (1 kg 75 g) of ganja from him, which police valued
at about 45,000 rupees. [@1]

The arrest was made under a campaign against illegal drug buying and selling,
with Dehat station officers acting on the instructions of district police chief
Ajay Pandey and additional superintendent Ashish Khare, the Navabharat account
reported. [@2] Police said Kanojia had been booked for ganja trafficking at
Kotwali police station about a year earlier and faces theft and assault cases at
Kundipura station. [@2] Police said they were looking for other people connected
to the ganja trade on the basis of Kanojia's statement. [@1]

The Dehat team that made the arrest included sub-inspectors Sandeep Singh
Rajpoot and Surendra Pratap Yadav, constables Saurabh, Suraj Chauhan, Brijesh
Pal and Shersingh, and staff of the cyber cell. [@1]
