Cairo detectives arrested two suspects in the Marg district of Cairo. [@1]
Officers seized 37 slabs of hashish, a shotgun and cartridges from the two men.
[@1]

Marg investigations chief Mohamed El-Madawy received information that the two
men were distributing hashish and notified Cairo investigations director Alaa
Beshendy. [@1] When confronted before deputy investigations director Mahmoud
Hegazy, the two admitted holding the drugs to sell them. [@1] Police described
the two suspects as unemployed men. [@2]
