The Third Section of Córdoba’s Audiencia Provincial, the provincial court,
sentenced two people to three years in prison each for a public-health offence
involving substances causing serious harm to health. [@1] The sentence resulted
from a conformity agreement between the prosecution and the defence reached
before trial. [@1] Guardia Civil units from the Córdoba command, the Montilla
company and the Baena post uncovered retail drug activity run by the two from
two homes in Montilla. [@1]

Between May and November 2021 officers maintained surveillance around the homes
and repeatedly intercepted buyers leaving with sachets of narcotics. [@1]
Searches recovered up to seven types of drugs and medicines, including
heroin-cocaine mixtures, cannabis/marijuana with an indoor grow included,
methadone, lormetazepam, mirtazapina and alprazolam, with a combined weight of
about 300 grams. [@1] Officers also seized precision scales, plastic cuttings
for making sachets, handwritten account notes and 195 euros in cash allegedly
from sales. [@1]

The court applied a drug-addiction mitigating circumstance only to the man after
it was shown he was in detox treatment at the Provincial Social Welfare
Institute of the Córdoba provincial council. [@1] With that mitigation he may
seek suspension of his imprisonment as long as he continues treatment. [@1] The
court also imposed a 304.27-euro fine equivalent to the official valuation of
the seized drugs. [@1]
