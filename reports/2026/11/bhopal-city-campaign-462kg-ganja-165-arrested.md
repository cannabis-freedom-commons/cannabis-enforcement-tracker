Police in Bhopal city registered 130 ganja trafficking cases between 1 January
and 3 November 2026. [@1] Police seized 462 kg 736 g of ganja in those cases.
[@1] Police arrested 165 accused people, including 43 women. [@1]

The Crime Branch arrested 60-year-old Kiran Ahirwar in the Govindpura area with
10 kg 100 g of ganja, which police valued at about Rs 3 lakh. [@1]

Ganja seizures were also reported in the Ashoka Garden, Kamla Nagar and Talaiya
police-station areas of Bhopal. [@1]
