A special judge under the NDPS Act, Nalin Kumar Pandey, convicted Amjad Khan in
Bettiah, West Champaran district, Bihar, of smuggling ganja. [@1] The court
sentenced him to three years of rigorous imprisonment and fined him 75,000
rupees. [@1] If he does not pay the fine he must serve three more months in
prison. [@1]

The conviction stems from a 15 September 2023 case in which Srinagar police
station officers stopped him at Kadir Chowk and found 1.77 kg of ganja, or
marijuana, in a yellow bag in his motorcycle's storage box. [@1] Police acted on
a tip that a smuggler was bringing ganja by motorcycle, and he tried to run when
he saw them before officers chased and caught him. [@1] A first information
report was registered at Srinagar police station and the special public
prosecutor under the NDPS Act, Santosh Kumar Sharma, gave the account of the
case and its outcome. [@1]
