The Chornomorske district court in Crimea, known in Russian as the Chernomorsky
district court, convicted a 44-year-old local resident of unlawfully acquiring
and storing parts of plants containing a narcotic drug in a significant
quantity. [@1] The charge was part 1 of article 228 of the Russian Criminal
Code, which covers unlawful acquisition and storage of plant parts containing
narcotic drugs without intent to sell. [@2] The court gave him one year of
imprisonment, suspended, with a probation period of the same length. [@3]

In mid-August 2026 the man was grazing cattle in a field near the village of
Vnukovo when he found a wild-growing cannabis bush. [@1] He pulled several
branches with leaves from the bush and carried them to a farm outbuilding to dry
them for his own later use. [@1] The court found the drug was stored for
personal use, without intent to sell. [@2]

The verdict was published in the name of the prosecutor's office of Crimea,
which announced the case through its press service. [@3]
