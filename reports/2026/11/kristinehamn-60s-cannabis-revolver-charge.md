A man in his 60s from Kristinehamn, a town in Värmland in western Sweden, has
been charged with growing cannabis. [@1] He is also charged over a pistol that
he had. [@1] The cannabis charge is cannabistillverkning, the Swedish term for
cannabis cultivation. [@2]

The newspaper's own summary of the case says the man had half a kilo of
cannabis. [@3] It also says the pistol was a revolver and that he had no licence
for it. [@3] The half-kilo is the only quantity the reports give. [@3]

The charge was made public on 12 November 2026. [@1] The story ran under the
headline Vapenägande 60-åring odlade cannabis. [@1] The Kristinehamn title Nya
Kristinehamns-Posten published it too. [@2] Both are Bonnier News local papers.
[@1]

The case is at the charge stage; no trial date, plea or court outcome has been
reported. [@1] The cannabis allegation and the weapons allegation are part of
the same prosecution. [@1] The reports give no plant count, growing site or
value. [@3]
