Police in Conegliano arrested an unemployed man born in 1995. [@1] Officers of
the Conegliano Public Security Commissariat, coordinated by the Treviso
prosecutor, carried out the operation as part of intensified checks ahead of the
Halloween weekend. [@3] With help from the Local Police dog unit, they located
the 1995-born man's home in the Lourdes district and seized the material. [@2]
The 31-year-old, originally from the Vittoriese area but living in central
Conegliano, was placed under house arrest. [@2]

Inside the apartment police found about 500 grams of hashish, 35 grams of
cocaine, 16 grams of marijuana and 171 grams of ketamine in several bags. [@3]
The home also held precision scales, pans believed to have been used to prepare
drugs and material for packaging individual doses. [@1] Officers recovered about
22,000 euros in cash hidden in several hiding places. [@1] The money was seized
because investigators considered it proceeds of dealing. [@1]

Investigators believe the drugs were intended for sale during the Halloween
weekend, particularly in raves and nightclubs. [@1] Police announced further
specific anti-trafficking services in the coming days. [@1] The investigation
continues under the coordination of the Treviso prosecutor. [@1] The arrest is
an investigative measure and does not establish definitive responsibility. [@1]
