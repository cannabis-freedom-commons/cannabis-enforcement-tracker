Police in Kyiv detained a 38-year-old city resident when he was carrying 21
parcels of amphetamine to a post office, according to investigators. [@2] Police
said the parcels were addressed to recipients in different Ukrainian cities and
contained 100 grams of amphetamine in total. [@2] The apartment search found
about 2.5 kilograms of prohibited substances, and police listed cannabis and
cookies containing cannabis extract among them. [@1] Cocaine, amphetamine,
mephedrone, ecstasy and psilocybin mushrooms were also listed, and all the
seized material was sent for examination. [@1]

The man had previously been convicted of drug offences and was detained under
article 208 of Ukraine's Criminal Procedure Code. [@1] Under the supervision of
the Podilska district prosecutor's office, he was notified of suspicion under
part 3 of article 307 of the Criminal Code. [@1] The article carries nine to 12
years in prison. [@1] A court ordered him held in custody without bail for the
duration of the pre-trial investigation. [@3]

During a personal search, officers found amphetamine, mephedrone and pills on
him, and the substances were packaged for sending to customers in different
regions, the prosecutor's office said. [@4] Officers also found more than 400
zip bags in his apartment, which investigators suspect were used to package
drugs for sale. [@4]
