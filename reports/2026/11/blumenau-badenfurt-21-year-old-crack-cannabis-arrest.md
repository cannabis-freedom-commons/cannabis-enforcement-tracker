Civil police arrested a 21-year-old man in flagrante for drug trafficking on the
morning of Friday 6 November 2026 in the Badenfurt neighbourhood of Blumenau.
[@1] Officers from Blumenau's Delegacia de Combate às Drogas acted at about
10:40am after an anonymous report said the suspect was storing drugs in his
home. [@2] The information said the suspect was responsible for storing
narcotics in his residence. [@1] Police went to the indicated address and found
a significant quantity of drugs inside the property. [@1]

Officers seized about 1 kg of crack together with portions of maconha and
hashish. [@1] Officers also seized precision scales and radio communicators,
which police said pointed to drug trafficking. [@2]

Police said the man already had several adolescent infraction records linked to
drug trafficking. [@1] After the arrest he was taken to DECOD headquarters,
where officers formalised the flagrante arrest. [@3] He was then sent to the
Regional Prison, where he remained at the disposal of justice. [@1] He was
awaiting a custody hearing. [@1]
