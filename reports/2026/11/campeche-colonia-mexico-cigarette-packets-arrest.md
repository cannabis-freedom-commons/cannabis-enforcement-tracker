Officers of Campeche's Secretaría de Protección y Seguridad Ciudadana (SPSC)
detained a man in the Colonia México neighbourhood of Campeche city, the state
security secretariat said. [@2] During a check, the officers found eight
cigarette packets with a plant substance that, by its characteristics, could be
cannabis, the secretariat said. [@2] State police made the arrest after a check
in a public street, according to the submitted account. [@1] The suspect and the
packets were secured to build the investigation file, the secretariat said. [@1]
The officers took the man into custody after the find, Por Esto! reported. [@3]

He was later taken to the Fiscalía General del Estado (FGE), Campeche's state
prosecutor's office, which is handling the investigation. [@2] Forensic tests
will determine the nature of the substance and whether a crime was committed.
[@2] The authorities did not release the man's identity, the weight of the
substance or its possible destination. [@2] The state prosecutor's office will
determine his legal situation as the investigation advances. [@2]
