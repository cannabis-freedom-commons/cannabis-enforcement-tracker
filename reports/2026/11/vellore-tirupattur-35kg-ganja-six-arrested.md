District police in Vellore and Tirupattur arrested six drug peddlers in a
special drive searching various public places on Wednesday. [@1] Police
identified the arrested men as 32-year-old Rajesh Kumar, 52-year-old Ali Khan,
37-year-old Suresh, 53-year-old Akbar, 35-year-old Harish and 65-year-old
Balaraman, who belonged to Vellore and Tirupattur. [@1] Police registered cases
against the six and lodged them in prison, with a further probe under way. [@1]

Teams seized 35 kg of ganja, the cannabis quantity recorded here, along with 37
kg of gukta in the two districts. [@1] Police described the combined haul as 72
kilograms of ganja and gukta seized in various public places. [@1] Sniffer dogs
were roped in for searches at railway stations, bus termini and other public
places during the day-long drive. [@1] Special teams conducted the surprise
checks on the orders of Superintendents of Police N.U. Sivaraman of Vellore and
Akshay Anil Wakhare of Tirupattur. [@1]

Police said most of the seized contraband had been sourced from agents in Andhra
Pradesh for sale in industrial regions such as Ranipet, Sriperambadur,
Vaniyambadi, Ambur, Vellore and Cheyyar. [@1]
