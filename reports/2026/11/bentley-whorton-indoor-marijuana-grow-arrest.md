Bentley police arrested a 52-year-old man on 10 November 2026 on suspicion of
aggravated child endangerment and multiple drug offences. [@1] The man was
Nathan Whorton, and he was booked into the Sedgwick County Jail. [@2] He was
booked on suspicion of marijuana cultivation, distribution of a controlled
substance, possession of a controlled substance, possession of drug
paraphernalia, aggravated child endangerment and violating the Kansas Drug Tax
Stamp Act. [@2] An infant and a 17-year-old from the home were referred to the
Kansas Department for Children and Families. [@1]

Officers served a search warrant at a home in the 10400 block of North 151st
Street West near Bentley on the morning of 10 November. [@2] Officers seized 350
items, including evidence of a suspected marijuana growing operation. [@1] KAKE
reported that the suspected marijuana growing operation was indoors. [@2] The
search of the home lasted until 9:30 p.m. [@2] Police said they recovered about
7 pounds (3.2 kg) of processed marijuana at the home. [@2] They also recovered
suspected liquid THC oil and marijuana resin, psilocybin mushrooms, poppy
plants, small amounts of LSD and fentanyl, and drug paraphernalia. [@2]
Additional charges were possible pending laboratory results, police said. [@1]

The case will be presented to the Sedgwick County District Attorney’s Office for
consideration of formal charges. [@1] The Sedgwick County Sheriff's Office,
Sedgwick County Animal Control and Andale Police Department are assisting with
the case. [@1]
