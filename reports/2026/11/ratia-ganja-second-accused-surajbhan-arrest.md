Ratia City police station in Fatehabad district, Haryana, arrested a second
accused, Surajbhan of Samalkha in Panipat district, in a case over the recovery
of 25 kg 940 g of ganja and an illegal firearm. [@1] He was produced before a
court, which sent him to judicial custody on its orders. [@1]

The arrest follows that of Amarjeet alias Bhagat, a resident of Palsar village
in Fatehabad district, who was caught on the Ratia-Lali road near the main canal
on a tip-off. [@1] Officers found 25 kg 940 g of ganja in a plastic sack on him
and seized a loaded country-made gun that he could not show a licence for. [@1]
Police put the seized ganja at about 14 lakh rupees at international market
prices. [@2] Ratia Deputy Superintendent of Police Kulwant Singh said the
recovered cannabis had been seized by a CIA Ratia police team led by
Sub-Inspector Pradeep while it was on patrol in the old bus stand and Sanjay
Gandhi Chowk areas. [@2]

Ratia City police registered case 210 on 2 October 2026 against Amarjeet under
sections 20C, 61 and 85 of the NDPS Act, the country's main drug law, and also
cited firearm offences. [@1] Police said they were still investigating the
accused over other facts linked to drug trafficking and the illegal firearm.
[@1]
