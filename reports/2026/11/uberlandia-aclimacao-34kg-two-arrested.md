Military Police seized 34.59 kilograms of cannabis-like material and arrested
two men aged 35 and 38 in a house in Aclimação, Uberlândia, on the night of
Tuesday 3 November 2026. [@2] Officers reached the house on Rua Napoleão Luiz da
Costa while searching for people possibly involved in a case registered in
Araguari. [@2] Alô Uberlândia timed the arrests at about 18:20 that night in
Aclimação, Uberlândia. [@3] Police found three men aged 35, 37 and 38 beside a
truck used to carry about 600 basic food baskets. [@2] One of the men tried to
enter the house quickly but was stopped, and police did not say which of the
three did so. [@2]

Police said the 35-year-old was held on suspicion of drug trafficking and the
38-year-old on suspicion of association for trafficking. [@2] Both men and the
seized materials were taken to the on-duty Civil Police station. [@3]

Inside the house officers found about 100 tablets and two sacks of cannabis-like
material totalling 34.59 kilograms. [@2] Officers also found 139 pins of
cocaine-like substance, a precision scale and packaging materials. [@2] Officers
also seized R$650 in cash and a mobile phone. [@2] The cocaine pins are
mentioned only as context and excluded from the cannabis figure, which counts
only the 34.59 kilograms of cannabis. [@1]
