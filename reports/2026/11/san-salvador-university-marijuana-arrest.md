Agents of the Policía Nacional Civil, El Salvador's national civil police,
arrested César Danilo López Hernández, 34, inside a higher-education centre in
San Salvador on Friday 6 November 2026. [@1] Police said he was carrying
marijuana and cash hidden in a backpack. [@1]

According to the police report, officers located him while he was acting in a
way they called suspicious, and he tried to flee when he noticed them but was
detained. [@1]

The man will be referred for the alleged offence of possession and holding with
intent to traffic, the initial stage of the case. [@1] The institution did not
publicly identify the university, nor state how much marijuana or money was
seized. [@1] Police did not say whether the detainee is a student, employee or
visitor of the institution. [@1]

El Mundo reported that police also seized a mobile phone from him. [@2] The
arrest was made inside the grounds of a university in the San Salvador district,
the police said. [@2] The detainee was to be placed at the disposal of the
courts for possession and holding with intent to traffic. [@2]

According to El Mundo, the university arrest came earlier the same morning as two other marijuana arrests in the capital, at a home in Santa Tecla and in the case of the couple accused of using minors. [@3]
