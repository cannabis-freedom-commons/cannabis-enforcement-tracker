Military Police officers approached two men during a patrol in Praça Duque de
Caxias in Toledo, Paraná. [@1] According to the police team, the two men were
carrying small bags close to their bodies and were consuming alcoholic drinks at
the time. [@1] During the subsequent personal search, officers found 0.26 gram
of cannabis sativa in the bag carried by one of the two men. [@1]

When questioned, the man carrying the bag said the substance belonged to his
friend, and the friend confirmed that he was its owner. [@1] Officers weighed
the seized drug and sealed it for the record. [@1] Both men were then taken to
the 4th Company of the Military Police, the local PM post, for the applicable
legal procedures. [@1] The police record assessed that the circumstances and the
small quantity seized initially indicated that the cannabis was intended for
personal consumption. [@1]

Officers then issued a termo de compromisso de comparecimento, a written
undertaking to appear before the authorities. [@1] After that formality, both
men were released. [@1]
