Police in Varese arrested one man for possessing drugs for sale and reported a
second man at liberty for complicity in the same offence. [@2] The investigation
began with a report about an Instagram story in which a man whose face was
hidden handled a small-calibre pistol alongside large sums of cash and
threatening phrases. [@2] Investigators of the Squadra Mobile, the police
force's mobile squad, identified the user of the profile and searched his home,
finding only a small amount of hashish. [@2]

Analysis of the location data in photographs taken with the man's phone led
officers to a second flat linked to another man. [@2] A search of the second
flat found drugs and materials for packing doses inside a wardrobe. [@2] During
questioning both men said the drugs and seized material belonged to the first
suspect, who had left them temporarily in the other man's custody. [@2] The
operation seized 35 grams of hashish, the cannabis component of the case. [@2]

Il Giorno reported the arrested man as about 30 years old and said some of the
video's threats were aimed at law-enforcement officers. [@3] Il Tradate, a local
outlet, described the second flat as a drug depot and said the owner of the
drugs was taken to Varese's prison while the other man was reported at liberty.
[@4]

Telegraf, a Slovenian outlet citing Il Giorno, reported that the police
initially found only a small amount of hashish at the suspect's home. [@1]
