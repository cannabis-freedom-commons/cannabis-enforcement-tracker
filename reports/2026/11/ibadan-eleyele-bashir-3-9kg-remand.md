The National Drug Law Enforcement Agency in Oyo State arraigned Damilola Bashir
on Tuesday, 10 November 2026, for allegedly possessing 3.945 kilograms of
cannabis sativa. [@1] Bashir pleaded guilty before the court to a two-count
charge of unlawful dealing in narcotics brought against him. [@1] Following his
guilty plea, Justice Nkeonye Maha ordered that he be remanded at the Agodi
Correctional Facility. [@1] The judge adjourned the case to 18 January for a
review of the facts and judgment. [@1]

Earlier in the proceedings, prosecutor Mrs A. A. Balogun told the court that
Bashir had committed the offence on 2 September in the Eleyele area of Ibadan,
Oyo State. [@1] Balogun said NDLEA operatives acted on intelligence indicating
that the defendant was involved in illicit drug trafficking. [@1] She alleged
that officers arrested him in possession of a sack containing 3.945 kilograms of
cannabis sativa. [@1] The prosecutor stated that the alleged offence contravened
section 11(C) of Nigeria's NDLEA Act. [@1]

After Bashir pleaded guilty to both counts, the prosecutor applied to withdraw
the second charge. [@1] Justice Maha subsequently struck out the second count as
requested by the prosecution. [@1]
