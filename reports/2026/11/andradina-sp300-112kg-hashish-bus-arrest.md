Highway police arrested a man with hashish during a bus inspection at about 0h20
on 13 November 2026 at kilometre 644 of the Marechal Rondon highway (SP-300) in
Andradina. [@1] Investigators linked the drugs to the passenger occupying seat
45. [@1] A records check showed the man was wanted by the courts for unpaid
child support. [@1] The case was taken to the Central de Polícia Judiciária in
Andradina, where the man remained in custody. [@1]

Officers found two backpacks in the bus's internal luggage compartment holding
103 packets of hashish totalling 11.2 kilograms. [@1] Highway police estimated
the seizure cost drug traffickers about R$112,000. [@1]

The inspection took place during Operação Impacto. [@1] The bus was travelling
from Campo Grande in Mato Grosso do Sul to São Paulo. [@2]
