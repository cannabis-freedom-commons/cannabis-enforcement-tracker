During a PROA-actie on 7 November, Zaventem local police pulled a car over for a
defective number-plate light, and officers immediately smelled cannabis. [@1] A
check of the vehicle and its occupants found 38 grams of cannabis and 745 euros
in cash. [@1] The stop took place during a PROA action in Zaventem on 7
November. [@2]

A follow-up house search yielded a further 245 grams of cannabis and hashish,
together with packaging material and a scale. [@1] Both occupants were arrested
and questioned; police seized the car, the money and several mobile phones. [@1]
