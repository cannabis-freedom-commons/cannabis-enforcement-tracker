Military Police officers arrested a 25-year-old man on drug-trafficking
suspicion on Saturday, 7 November 2026, in Córrego Cava Alta, in the rural zone
of Abre Campo, Minas Gerais. [@1] Officers went to the address after receiving
information that drugs were being sold in the area. [@1]

When he noticed the police, the man tried to run into a house and threw an
object over the wall. [@1] Officers recovered the object and found it was a bar
of cannabis. [@1] Further searches turned up a precision scale, scissors, a .22
calibre round of ammunition and mobile phones. [@2]

The man was arrested and taken to the Civil Police station with the seized
material. [@2] Neither outlet gave a weight for the cannabis bar or named the
specific trafficking charge filed against him. [@1]
