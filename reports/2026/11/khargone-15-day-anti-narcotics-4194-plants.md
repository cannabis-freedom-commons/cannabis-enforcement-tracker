Khargone police registered 17 cases under the NDPS Act, India's narcotics law,
and arrested 18 accused during a 15-day state-level anti-narcotics campaign.
[@1] The campaign ran from 27 October to 10 November 2026 under Superintendent
of Police Ravindra Verma. [@1]

Acting on reports of illegal ganja cultivation across the district, officers
seized 4,194 cannabis plants weighing 2,750 kilograms, which police valued at Rs
4,12,50,000. [@1] Separate actions against ganja traffickers and sellers
produced 20.600 kilograms of dried ganja from the accused, which police valued
at about Rs 5,15,125. [@1]

Teams used drones to search suspected fields and the surrounding area. [@1] In
several places officers had to cross rivers, hills and difficult tracks to reach
the ganja fields. [@1] Cases were registered under the NDPS Act against people
involved in the illegal cultivation. [@1] Police also arrested six people who
were absconding in older NDPS Act cases or against whom warrants were pending.
[@1] A report was submitted to act against two accused under the PIT NDPS Act,
the preventive detention law. [@1]

The campaign was overseen by Indore zone DIG Anurag and Nimar range IG Simala
Prasad, with additional superintendents of police Bittu Sahgal and Shakuntala
Ruhal guiding teams from every sub-division and police station in the district.
[@1] Police said action against those involved in illegal cultivation,
trafficking and sale of narcotics would continue. [@1]
