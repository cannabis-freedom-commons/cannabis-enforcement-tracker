Guardia di Finanza officers arrested an Albanian citizen at Ciampino. [@1,3,4] The
operation was run by officers of the Guardia di Finanza provincial command in
Rome after the man was seen leaving a basement room under the stairs of a
dwelling and getting into a car registered to a heavily convicted person. [@2,4]
Officers stopped the car for a thorough check. [@1,3,4] On the order of the on-duty
prosecutor of the Velletri public prosecutor’s office, the man was arrested and
taken to Velletri prison at the disposal of the judiciary. [@1,3,4]

A detailed inspection found 300 grams of marijuana hidden in a double bottom
built into the passenger-side airbag compartment. [@1,3,4] Officers then extended
the search to the basement room the man had been seen leaving shortly before.
[@1,4] Inside a refrigerator kept in that room, officers found another 1.3 kilos
of hashish and 1 kilo of marijuana, together with precision scales and material
for packaging doses. [@1,3,4] The total seizure was reported as about 2.6 kilos of
marijuana and hashish. [@2] Officers also seized the car because it had been
specially modified to transport and conceal drugs. [@1,3,4]

The man was held on accusation of possession and trafficking of narcotic
substances. [@2,4] The operation began in the Ciampino locality when officers saw
the man leave the basement and board the car. [@1]
