Five people were charged after officers intercepted a package containing
marijuana, tobacco and a cellphone being brought into the Warren County jail.
[@1] Four of those charged were already being held at the jail at 1000 Grove
Street. [@1] A fifth person who allegedly helped facilitate the scheme from
outside the jail was also arrested. [@1] Avante White, 18, was charged with
conspiracy to introduce contraband into a correctional facility and possession
of contraband in a correctional facility. [@1] Three other people already in
custody were charged with conspiracy to introduce contraband into a correctional
facility. [@1] Wanda Harrington, 52, was charged with conspiracy and given a
$25,000 bond. [@1]

Warren County Sheriff Martin Pace said the incident occurred on November 5. [@1]
A citizen reported suspicious activity outside the jail that morning, and the
package and its contents were recovered. [@1] Investigators said the package
containing contraband had been hung at a window of the jail. [@2] White had been
jailed on drive-by shooting and aggravated assault charges, and his bond
increased from $200,000 to $400,000. [@1] Isaiah Smith, 24, had been jailed on
armed robbery, aggravated assault and felony firearm possession charges, and his
bond is now $325,000. [@1] Jamarius McDaniel, 25, had been jailed on charges of
shooting into an occupied dwelling and possession of a firearm by a convicted
felon, and his bond is $375,000. [@1] Aaron Wynn Jr., 33, had been jailed on
aggravated assault and arson charges, and his bond is now $150,000. [@1]

Pace described Harrington as the mother of one of the people charged and said
she helped facilitate the alleged scheme by communicating information over a
cellphone. [@1] All five suspects appeared before Warren County Justice Court
Judge Walter Armstrong, who set their bonds. [@2] Additional arrests are
expected as the investigation continues. [@1]
