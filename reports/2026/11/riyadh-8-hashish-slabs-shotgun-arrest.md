Detectives of the El Riyadh police centre in Kafr El Sheikh arrested a man
accused of drug trafficking and possessing unlicensed firearms. [@1,2] The
suspect, identified only as A.B., is 36 years old and lives in Village 74
Maazour in the centre's jurisdiction. [@1,3] Detectives acted on a tip about his
alleged trafficking, verified it, obtained Public Prosecution permission, and a
force led by Major Mohamed Fathi Ismail raided his location and detained him.
[@1]

Police said they found 8 slabs of hashish in his possession. [@1,2] They also
found a 12-gauge shotgun with 6 cartridges of the same calibre. [@1,2] The search
also recovered two cylinders of a powder suspected of being narcotic, a
sensitive digital scale, a mobile phone and cash. [@1]

When confronted, the suspect admitted possessing the drugs to sell and the
firearm and cartridges to defend himself. [@1] He also admitted using the phone
to contact customers, the scale to weigh drugs, and that the cash came from his
drug sales. [@1] Police held the suspect and the seized items, filed case 10033
of El Riyadh centre, and referred him to the Public Prosecution for
investigation. [@1,2]

A police force led by Major Mohamed Fathi Ismail, head of the centre's investigations, raided the suspect's location after obtaining the Public Prosecution's permission. [@2]
