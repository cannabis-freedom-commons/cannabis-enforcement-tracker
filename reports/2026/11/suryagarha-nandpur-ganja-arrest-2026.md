Police in the Suryagarha police-station area of Lakhisarai district raided a
marked compound near the Shiv temple at Nandpur Dhala and arrested a man with
ganja. [@1] Police identified the arrested man as Santosh Kumar, 30, son of the
late Shambhu Singh, a resident of Nandpur village. [@1] Officers said they
recovered 1.40 kg of ganja, a mobile phone and 11 chilams from him. [@1]

According to a press release issued by Lakhisarai police, the raid was part of a
special campaign against illegal narcotic drugs ordered by Superintendent of
Police Prerana Kumari ahead of Diwali, the festival of lights. [@1] Police said
a tip-off came that a man was illegally selling ganja and other narcotic drugs
near the Shiv temple at Nandpur Dhala. [@1] After verifying the information, a
police team led by Suryagarha station house officer Rohit Kumar Singh carried
out the raid along with a magistrate. [@1] The search found 1.40 kg of ganja
divided among three plastic packets. [@1]

A first information report was registered as Suryagarha police-station case
266/26, dated 7 November 2026, under sections 8(c), 21(b), 22(b) and 25 of the
NDPS Act, India's main narcotics law. [@1] Police are questioning the arrested
man to learn where the ganja came from and who else was involved in the trade.
[@1] Officers said they were tracing where the ganja was brought from and where
it was to be sold, and that anyone else found involved would also be prosecuted.
[@1] The raid team included a sub-inspector named Ravishankar Kumar, another
sub-inspector named Khurshid Alam and armed police. [@1]
