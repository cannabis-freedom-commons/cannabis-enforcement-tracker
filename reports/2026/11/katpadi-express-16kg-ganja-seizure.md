The Government Railway Police seized a consignment of 16 kg of ganja on an
express train at Katpadi railway station in Vellore on Wednesday. [@1] Initial
inquiry revealed that two commuters alighted the express train and fled the spot
after they noticed a police team checking the train. [@1] The police also
analysed CCTV camera footage to nab the gang that was involved in ganja
smuggling. [@1] A probe is under way. [@1]

The police said that as part of a special drive against illegal transportation
of narcotic substances and inflammable articles on trains under Operation
Narcos, a team of GRP police personnel was conducting surprise checks at the
railway station. [@1] The team found an abandoned bag with bundles of ganja in
the luggage carrier of the general compartment on an express train bound to
Bengaluru. [@1] The express train on which the bag was found was bound to
Bengaluru. [@1]

A case has been registered by GRP Katpadi. [@1] Police gave the weight of the
seized ganja consignment as 16 kg. [@1]
