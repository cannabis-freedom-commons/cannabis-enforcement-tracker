Military Police arrested a 31-year-old man in Formiga, Minas Gerais, on the
night of Tuesday 3 November 2026 on suspicion of drug trafficking. [@1] The
arrest was recorded around 21:00 during an operation in the Cidade Nova
neighbourhood. [@1] The Military Police of Minas Gerais, through the 63rd
Military Police Battalion (63º BPM), made the arrest for drug trafficking. [@2]
The action took place on Rua Bento Damasceno Gomes and followed monitoring and
information shared between teams of the 63rd Battalion. [@1] During the patrol,
officers approached a motorcycle ridden by the suspect on Rua Doutor Teixeira
Soares. [@1]

Officers found a portion of maconha and three ecstasy tablets in the man's
backpack. [@1] A search of his home found four bars of maconha, two cannabis
plants grown in pots, 147 ecstasy tablets, two precision scales and R$ 450 in
cash. [@1] Portal G37 reports that, added to the three tablets found in the
backpack, officers collected 150 ecstasy tablets during the operation. [@3] The
man was arrested in flagrante and taken to the police station with all the
seized material. [@1] The motorcycle he used was removed to a licensed impound
yard. [@1]

The Military Police said the operation formed part of their actions against drug
trafficking in Formiga and the surrounding region. [@1] Portal G37 describes a
separate earlier operation that evening at about 18:02 on Rua dos Professores in
the Santa Tereza neighbourhood. [@3]
