Syria's General Authority for Border Crossings and Customs said its officers
foiled an attempt to smuggle a quantity of narcotic substances through the Qamar
border crossing with Lebanon. [@1] The discovery came during routine inspection
operations on the luggage of travellers arriving through the crossing. [@1] The
inspection seized 2,600 grams of hashish along with 17 grams of heroin. [@1]
Both drugs were hidden hermetically inside a loudspeaker in a camouflage attempt
aimed at evading customs control procedures. [@1]

Officers drew up a customs-infraction report over the seizure and took the
required legal measures. [@1] The authority said the action formed part of
continuing efforts to combat smuggling of narcotic and prohibited substances.
[@1] It also confirmed that surveillance and control would be reinforced at
border crossings. [@1]

The Qamar crossing is located in the Wadi Khaled area of Akkar in northern
Lebanon. [@1] It is the only border crossing open in northern Lebanon with
Syria, and it is reserved exclusively for pedestrian transit. [@1]
