Two people were detained during a vehicle control on Ruta 5 Norte at the height
of the commune of Nogales after Carabineros detected that they were transporting
doses of cocaine and marijuana. [@1] The inspection was conducted by officers of
the Tenencia de Carreteras Hijuelas, according to information published by
Carabineros of the Valparaíso Region on its social networks. [@1] After the
detention, both detainees were placed at the disposal of the Ministerio Público,
Chile's public prosecution service, in line with information released by the
police institution. [@1]

During the procedure officers seized the cocaine and marijuana doses found in
the vehicle. [@1] Officers also seized a digital gram scale described as a pesa
gramera digital during the same roadside procedure. [@1] Officers also seized
cash in the same procedure alongside the drugs and the scale. [@1] The procedure
was carried out by Carabineros of the Tenencia de Carreteras Hijuelas, which
also seized the digital scale and the cash. [@1] This was a mixed case involving
both cocaine doses and marijuana doses transported together, with the marijuana
forming the separately supported cannabis component. [@1]
