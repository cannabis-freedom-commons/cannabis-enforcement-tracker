A man was detained in San Vicente, Bahía de Banderas, during a joint operation
by municipal police, the Mexican Army and the National Guard after officers
allegedly found 13 packets of a substance similar to crystal and seven more
packets of marijuana on him. [@1] The officers were on routine surveillance
patrols when they spotted the man carrying a green backpack whose behaviour
struck them as suspicious. [@1]

During the search, officers found the 20 packets of the substances inside the
backpack, distributed in different sizes, which according to the police report
may have been destined for sale and distribution. [@1] The man and the seized
items were handed to the public prosecutor (Ministerio Público), who will
determine his legal situation for the probable offence against health and
whatever else results. [@1] This case is the second recent arrest reported in
San Vicente of people found with suspected drugs for possible distribution and
sale, in the context of anti-narcomenudeo operations in Bahía de Banderas. [@1]
