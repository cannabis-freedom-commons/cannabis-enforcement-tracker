On the afternoon of Tuesday the 10th, the Federal Highway Police arrested a
couple and seized more than 120 kg of marijuana. [@1] The action took place on
the BR-386 highway in Lajeado, in the Vale do Taquari. [@1] Federal highway
officers stopped a Strada pickup with Belo Horizonte plates during an anti-crime
operation on the highway. [@1]

Officers found marijuana tablets in the bed of the vehicle, which weighed 123 kg
after weighing. [@1] The 24-year-old driver, a resident of Sapucaia do Sul, had
a prior record for making threats. [@1] The 30-year-old passenger had records
for threats, theft and assault. [@2]

The couple were arrested and taken to the Lajeado Civil Police station with the
seized drugs and the vehicle. [@2] The Federal Highway Police, the Policia
Rodoviaria Federal, is the force credited with the arrest and seizure. [@3]
