Nepal's Narcotics Control Bureau arrested two Indian nationals carrying six
kilograms of ganja at Tribhuvan International Airport in Kathmandu. [@1] The
arrested pair were identified as 46-year-old Sunita Devi of Ghaziabad and
35-year-old Mohammad Santosh Nadaf of Madhubani. [@1] The bureau said both had
flown from Thailand and brought the ganja by air into Nepal. [@1]

Officers detained the pair during screening at the airport. [@1] A search of
their belongings recovered six kilograms of ganja. [@1] Officers also seized two
passports and four mobile phones from them. [@1]

The Narcotics Control Bureau disclosed the case at a news conference on
Wednesday. [@1] Its co-spokesperson, DSP Jeevan Niraula, said the two Indian
nationals who had come from Thailand were arrested during the airport check.
[@1] The bureau took the recovered drugs and other items into custody, was
questioning both suspects, and said further investigation was continuing. [@1]
