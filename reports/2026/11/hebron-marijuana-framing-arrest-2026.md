Palestinian anti-narcotics police arrested a suspect on Tuesday 10 November 2026
for deliberately planting narcotic substances inside a citizen's home in the
city of Hebron. [@1] The suspect intended to entrap the homeowner and fabricate
a drug case against him because of prior disputes between the two. [@1] Police
explained that they had initially received information indicating that a
resident possessed narcotic substances inside his home. [@1]

Acting on that tip, officers obtained a search warrant to enter and search the
citizen's house. [@2] During the search of the residence, officers found a
quantity of the narcotic substance marijuana. [@2] During subsequent search,
investigation, and information gathering, police established a suspicion that
another person had placed the drugs inside the house to entrap the owner over
prior disagreements. [@3]

Police tracked the movements of the suspect accused of planting the marijuana
and placed him under arrest. [@3] The suspect was held in detention to complete
the required legal procedures against him in accordance with due process. [@2]
