Deputies from the St. Charles Parish Sheriff's Office arrested three family
members on 3 November 2026 after executing arrest and search warrants during a
narcotics investigation at a home in Luling, Louisiana. [@1] Deputies served the
warrants at 213 Bethany Drive, where they encountered the residents and searched
the property. [@1] The three arrested residents were identified as Chad
Bettencourt, Charles Bettencourt and Patricia Bettencourt. [@1]

During the search of the residence, authorities recovered 13.71 grams of
marijuana and 2.06 grams of fentanyl. [@1] Deputies also confiscated a digital
scale and a Raven Arms .25-caliber semi-automatic handgun from the home. [@1]

Chad Bettencourt, born on 12 March 1992, was charged with possession of
marijuana, possession of a controlled dangerous substance within 2,000 feet of a
school, possession of drug paraphernalia, distribution of fentanyl, possession
with intent to distribute fentanyl, and resisting arrest. [@1] Charles
Bettencourt, born on 30 November 1957, and Patricia Bettencourt, born on 15
March 1957, were each charged with possession of marijuana, possession of a
controlled dangerous substance within 2,000 feet of a school, possession of drug
paraphernalia, possession with intent to distribute fentanyl, and possession of
a firearm in the presence of a controlled dangerous substance. [@1] The
Sheriff's Office noted that the Bethany Drive residence was in close proximity
to an elementary school. [@1]
