Hampshire police seized cannabis, a mobile phone and almost £400 in cash from
the passenger of a vehicle in Southampton during a stop for suspected
drug-related activity. [@1] Two more phones were recovered during a vehicle
search, one of which had been deliberately damaged. [@1] A spokesperson for
Hampshire police said officers were able to recover key components from the
damaged phone, including the SIM card and battery, for further examination. [@1]

Officers seized two illegal e-scooters and reported the riders for driving
without insurance and a licence. [@1] Two cars being driven without valid
insurance were also seized and a stolen van was recovered. [@1] Officers from
teams across the north and west of the city were out the previous day carrying
out proactive patrols and enforcement activity. [@1] They were joined by
specialist motorcycle officers and the proactive Roads Policing Unit. [@1]

Yahoo News UK's syndicated account records the same cannabis, e-scooter and
vehicle seizures during the Southampton operation. [@2] The work forms part of
Hampshire police's Operation Enduro, which aims to tackle vehicle-related
anti-social behaviour and target illegal vehicle use. [@1] The spokesperson said
the operation will continue in Southampton in the coming weeks. [@1]
