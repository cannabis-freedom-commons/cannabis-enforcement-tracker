Bajpe police arrested two young men on Tuesday for allegedly consuming ganja in
public places in Edapadavu village. [@1] Police sub-inspector Kumareshan of the
Bajpe station was on patrol near Kuppepadavu Road when he saw a young man
behaving suspiciously and apparently under the influence of an intoxicating
substance. [@1] Questioned by the officer, the man reportedly admitted to
consuming ganja and was identified as Rohit Dolai, 19, a native of West Bengal
living near Ganjimath. [@1]

In a second incident on Tuesday afternoon, police noticed another young man in
an intoxicated state near Padarangi in Edapadavu village. [@1] He was identified
as Abhishek Kumar, 22, a native of Bihar also living near Ganjimath, and police
said he admitted to consuming ganja during questioning. [@1] Bajpe police
registered cases against both young men over the two incidents. [@1] Police
described both arrests as cases of consuming ganja in public places. [@1]
