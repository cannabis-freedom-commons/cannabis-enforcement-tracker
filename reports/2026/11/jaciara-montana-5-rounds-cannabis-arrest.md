Military Police arrested a 32-year-old driver in Jaciara in the early hours of 8
November 2026 after finding five intact 9mm rounds and a portion of maconha in a
bag in the bed of the red Chevrolet Montana he was driving. [@1]

Intelligence from the 28th Battalion local intelligence agency said a white
Chevrolet Onix and the red Montana were travelling from Cuiabá toward Jaciara
and might be involved in drug trafficking and cargo robbery. [@1] Officers set a
roadblock at the Dom Aquino access junction and stopped the Onix first, finding
nothing illicit with its driver and passenger, who were released. [@1]

During that check the Montana passenger got out of the still-moving pickup and
fled on foot into roadside vegetation. [@1]

Police later found the Montana near Praça Tamoios in central Jaciara. [@2] After
a pursuit of about two kilometres the pickup was stopped in front of the Hotel
Glória. [@2] Inside a bag in the pickup bed officers found the five intact 9mm
rounds and the maconha portion. [@2]

Questioned, the driver said the ammunition and maconha belonged to the passenger
who had fled. [@1]

He said he had been paid R$1,600 to drive the passenger from Cuiabá to
Rondonópolis via Chapada dos Guimarães, Campo Verde, Dom Aquino and Jaciara.
[@1] The detained driver was taken with the seized material to the Civil Police
station in Jaciara with no apparent bodily injuries. [@2] The case was recorded
as alleged illegal possession or carrying of a restricted-use firearm and
illicit drug use. [@2] The passenger who fled had not been located when the
police report was closed. [@2]
