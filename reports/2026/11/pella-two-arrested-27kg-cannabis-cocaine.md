Police in Pella arrested two men on the afternoon of 3 November 2026 for drug
trafficking and possession. [@1] Officers located the two men after coordinated
action in areas of Pella. [@1] The detainees face a case file and are to be led
to the Giannitsa Court of First Instance prosecutor. [@1]

Searches of three homes seized more than 2.7 kg of raw cannabis, a small amount
of processed cannabis, six precision scales, knives, a blank pistol and 10,200
euros in cash, alongside 275 grams of cocaine. [@1] In two houses belonging to
the first man, officers found 13 packages of raw cannabis weighing 2 kg and 672
grams. [@1] In the same two houses, officers found five packages of cocaine
weighing 275.17 grams, which is noted only to explain the mixed case and is
excluded from cannabis figures. [@1] Officers also found one package of
processed cannabis weighing 6.3 grams. [@1] Officers also found 11 improvised
cannabis cigarettes. [@1] In the second man's house, officers found three
packages of raw cannabis weighing 67 grams, a knife and 500 euros. [@1]

Officers also seized one detainee's vehicle, two mobile phones and 200 euros
found in the men's possession. [@1] Officers also seized a metal grinder. [@1]
