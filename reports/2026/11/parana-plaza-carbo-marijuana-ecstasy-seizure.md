Officers of the División Antidrogas of the Policía de Entre Ríos stopped two
people on the night of 5 November 2026 beside Plaza Enrique Carbó in Paraná,
Entre Ríos. [@1] The checks were part of the Plazas Seguras programme, a
provincial drive to prevent and control drug dealing in public spaces, run
through the Dirección General de Drogas Peligrosas of the Entre Ríos police.
[@2]

At the scene police seized 11 ecstasy tablets and four bags of the same
substance, and separately a piece of compressed marijuana with other items of
interest to the investigation. [@1] Among the other items, officers reported
seizing the dose bags and wrapping holding the drugs. [@2]

The police said no one was detained; the two people stopped were identified and
the case went to the provincial prosecutor. [@2] The file was opened under Entre
Ríos Provincial Law No. 10.566 on narcomenudeo, the provincial statute that
gives Entre Ríos courts and prosecutors jurisdiction over small-scale drug
dealing. [@2] The Unidad Fiscal de Investigación y Litigación, headed by
prosecutor Carolina Guzmán, took note of the operation and of the items seized.
[@1]

The accounts were published by the Entre Ríos police and carried by local
outlets on 6 and 8 November 2026. [@3]
