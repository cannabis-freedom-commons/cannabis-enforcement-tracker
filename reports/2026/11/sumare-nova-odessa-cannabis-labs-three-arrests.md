Three men were arrested in an operation that found two drug laboratories in
Sumaré (SP) and Nova Odessa (SP) on Monday the 9th. [@1] According to the
Federal Police, the premises were used to produce marijuana derivatives known as
dry and ice. [@1]

The suspects, aged 28, 29 and 52, were found in a house in Jardim Novo Paraná in
Sumaré, where police seized cocaine, marijuana, drug-production machines and 91
gas cylinders. [@1] The operation began after the Military Police received
information about drug manufacturing at the residence. [@1] At the address on
Rua Benedito Ferreira Quental, officers saw a man in the kitchen wearing gloves
with traces of a marijuana-like substance. [@1] In the house, police listed 369
grams of cocaine among the drugs found during the search. [@1] Police also
listed 474 grams of marijuana, plus other portions of the drug. [@1] Police also
found 2 kilograms of marijuana kept in a cooler box. [@1] Police listed 780
grams of drugs that were being prepared. [@1] Police seized 91 isobutane gas
cylinders. [@1] They also seized machines, scales, sieves and silicone moulds
used to make drugs, plus mobile phones. [@1] The 52-year-old suspect said he was
preparing marijuana for his own use, the 28-year-old said he was there to buy
drugs, and the 29-year-old, who said he owned the property, denied knowing where
the materials came from. [@1]

After the arrests, officers went to an apartment linked to the 29-year-old in
Jardim Dona Maria Azenha in Nova Odessa. [@1] There, with a sniffer dog, teams
found three grow tents with their own lighting and ventilation for growing
marijuana. [@1] In the apartment, 1.128 kilograms of drugs were seized,
including 240 grams of a substance known as ice, plus portions of marijuana
stored in containers and in the grow tents. [@1]

The three men were taken to the Federal Police station in Campinas and arrested
in flagrante for drug trafficking and association with drug trafficking,
remaining at the disposal of the courts. [@1] The operation was carried out by
the Military Police together with the Campinas Integrated Force to Combat
Organized Crime, a task force coordinated by the Federal Police. [@1] Besides
the Federal Police and Military Police, the force includes the Federal Highway
Police, São Paulo Civil Police through Deinter-2, the Paulínia municipal guard
and Gaeco prosecutors. [@1] Investigations continue to identify other possible
participants. [@1]
