Units of the National Guard's intelligence and investigation directorate
arrested a 45-year-old woman in Kasserine, described as an important drug
trafficker considered particularly dangerous. [@1]

The suspect was wanted under several search notices issued by different security
units and judicial authorities, in particular for forging documents certifying
the lifting of search warrants. [@1]

During the operation, officers seized large sums of money, wanted vehicles, a
large quantity of jewellery, and falsified documents and stamps used in
counterfeiting operations. [@1]

The suspect was placed in custody on the instructions of the Kasserine public
prosecutor's office. [@1]
