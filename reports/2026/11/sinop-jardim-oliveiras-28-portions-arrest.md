A 20-year-old man was arrested for drug trafficking and association with drug
trafficking in the Jardim das Oliveiras neighbourhood of Sinop, 500 km from
Cuiabá, on the night of Thursday 5 November 2026. [@1] Officers of the Military
Police's cavalry unit seized 28 portions of a cannabis-like substance and a
precision scale. [@1]

According to the police incident report, the team was patrolling the area around
18h10 when the suspect tried to run on noticing the officers. [@1] He was
stopped and a search found four portions of the drug on him. [@1]

Questioned, he said he was making a delivery and declared he belonged to the
Comando Vermelho criminal faction, in which he said he worked as a "lojista",
the member responsible for selling drugs. [@1] After the suspect authorised
officers to enter his home, they found another 24 portions of the same substance
and a precision scale. [@1]

Officers also seized R$ 60 in cash and an iPhone 13 mobile phone. [@1] The man,
who had no previous criminal record noted in the incident report, was arrested
and taken to the Sinop Civil Police station. [@1]
