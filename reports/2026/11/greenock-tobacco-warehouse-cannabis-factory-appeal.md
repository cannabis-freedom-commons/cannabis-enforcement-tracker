Ariel Ruko, 24, was one of three men caught after police raided the former
Tobacco Warehouse building in Clarence Street, Greenock, on 5 June 2025. [@1]
Ruko, Giap Ba Thai, 51, and Ervin Guri, 38, were sentenced at the High Court in
Glasgow after admitting being concerned in the supply of cannabis. [@1] Each man
received four years in prison, cut from five years to reflect their guilty
pleas. [@1] Newly released court documents show Ruko has appealed against his
jail term. [@1] A hearing before two appeal judges is due at the High Court in
Edinburgh on 17 November. [@1]

Inside the disused warehouse, police found a large-scale cultivation with
sophisticated equipment growing more than 1,000 plants, plus dozens of
vacuum-sealed bags of cannabis. [@2] The court heard the haul had a potential
value of around £1.3 million. [@2] The factory came to light after two officers
smelled cannabis while walking in the area. [@1]

Prosecutors said it was apparent that people had also been living inside the
building. [@1] When police arrived, fans could be heard whirring and several
doors were locked from the inside. [@1] All three men were first offenders; Ruko
and Guri are originally from Albania, while Thai is from Vietnam. [@1] Ruko's
lawyer told the sentencing hearing his client had been in the UK for four years
but had no immigration status. [@1] The judge called claims that at least one of
the men had not known what they were involved in absolutely ridiculous, noting
police could smell cannabis from the street. [@1]
