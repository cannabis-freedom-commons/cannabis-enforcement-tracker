Federal Highway Police officers arrested a man in Jardim, Mato Grosso do Sul,
after finding 865 kilograms of marijuana in the Toyota Hilux SW4 he was driving.
[@1] The Federal Highway Police said the seizure took place on Saturday 7
November 2026 in Jardim. [@2] Officers were checking traffic on the BR-060 when
they ordered the vehicle to stop. [@1]

The driver disobeyed the order and officers pursued the pickup in a tactical
escort until they intercepted it. [@1] Officers found many packages of marijuana
in the luggage compartment of the Hilux, totalling 865 kilograms. [@1] Inside
the vehicle officers found a large quantity of marijuana tablets. [@2]

The driver said he had collected the load in Bela Vista, about 90 kilometres
from Jardim, and was taking it to São Paulo. [@1] Checks showed the Hilux wore
false plates and had a theft record since October 2026 in Minas Gerais. [@2] The
man, the vehicle and the drug were taken to the Civil Police in Jardim. [@1]
