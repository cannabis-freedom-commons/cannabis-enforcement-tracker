West Kowloon station customs seized 7 hand-rolled cannabis cigarettes weighing
4.2 grams gross from an inbound traveller, according to a General Administration
of Customs announcement dated Thursday the 12th. [@1] Officers watching inbound
passengers found one who entered the customs control zone without declaring; as
the traveller looked around nervously and behaved abnormally at the checkpoint,
officers stopped and searched him. [@2]

On closer inspection, officers found 7 suspected hand-rolled cannabis cigarettes
with a distinct smell inside a cigarette pack in the traveller's left trouser
pocket, weighing 4.2 grams gross. [@2] A specialist institution confirmed that
THC, tetrahydrocannabinol, was detected in the cigarettes, and the case has been
referred to the customs anti-smuggling department for handling. [@2]
