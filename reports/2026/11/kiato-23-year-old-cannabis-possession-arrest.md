Officers of the DIAS motorcycle patrol unit of the Sikyona Police Department
arrested a 23-year-old Greek man in Kiato, Corinthia, on the evening of Thursday
12 November 2026 after stopping him for a police check in the town. [@1] The
arrest in Kiato was carried out by officers of that same DIAS unit of the
Sikyona Police Department. [@2]

During the check the officers established that the 23-year-old was carrying a
small amount of cannabis on his person. [@1] The officers seized the small
amount of cannabis found in his possession at the time of the check. [@1]

Following the arrest and seizure, the case rests with the Sikyona Police
Department for further handling. [@1] That department is conducting the
preliminary investigation into the cannabis possession case. [@1]
