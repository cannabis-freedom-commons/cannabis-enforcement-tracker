Varsinais-Suomen käräjäoikeus, the district court of Southwest Finland,
sentenced a 32-year-old man of foreign background to 70 days of suspended
imprisonment for offering compensation for a sexual act targeting a young person
and for a drug offence. [@1] The man gave a 16-year-old 50 euros for sex and
handed cannabis to the teenager in Turku. [@1] The district court convicted him
of those acts while dismissing an aggravated rape charge in the same case. [@1]

The prosecutor sought punishment for events that took place in the summer of
2024. [@1] The 16-year-old told the court of refusing sex at first and agreeing
later after the man repeatedly asked for sex and linked the promised money to
it. [@1] The man denied forcing the young person, claimed he had paid for
housework, and also denied giving the teenager cannabis. [@1] After reviewing
the accounts of both parties, their messages and a phone recording, the court
found the evidence did not show that the man's conduct had deprived the young
person of the ability or freedom to decide on participating, leaving reasonable
doubt on the aggravated rape charge. [@1] However, the court found the housework
explanation implausible, ruled that the messages and testimony showed the first
50-euro payment was compensation for sex, dismissed three other alleged counts
of offering compensation for lack of evidence, and held that the handover of
cannabis had been proven. [@1]

The probation period for the 70-day suspended sentence runs until 4 November
2028. [@1] The court ordered the man to pay the complainant 1,000 euros in
compensation for suffering, with penalty interest starting from 4 July 2024, and
dismissed the remaining compensation claims. [@1] He was also ordered to pay the
state an 80-euro victim surcharge, 25 euros in evidence costs, 849.96 euros
toward his defence costs, and 1,506 euros with penalty interest for the
complainant's legal assistance. [@1]
