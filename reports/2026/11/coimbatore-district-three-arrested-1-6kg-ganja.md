Coimbatore district police ran a special operation on the orders of District
Superintendent of Police Pavan K. Reddy from Tuesday until 5 am on Wednesday.
[@1,3] The rural and city police continued the special drive on Wednesday, 11 November 2026. [@2] Police took 18 persons with criminal records to police stations for
questioning during the drive. [@1,2,3]

Officers separately searched 16 persons identified through previous drug-related
offences and intelligence inputs. [@1,2,3] Three of those searched were booked and
arrested over alleged ganja sales. [@1,3] Police seized 1.6 kg of ganja from the
three arrested men. [@1,2,3] The three ganja cases were registered by the Coimbatore District (Rural) Police. [@2]

During vehicle checks in the same drive, police registered 60 cases for drunken
driving and nine cases for overspeeding and rash driving, and seized 14
vehicles. [@1,3] Police also arrested 19 persons for consuming alcohol in public
places and executed six pending warrants during the operation. [@1,2,3]
