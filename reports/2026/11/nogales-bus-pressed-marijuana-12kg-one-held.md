A woman was detained on suspicion of drug trafficking after Carabineros searched
an interprovincial bus on Ruta 5 Norte at the height of Nogales. [@1,2]

During the inspection officers seized 11 kilos and 875 grams of pressed
marijuana. [@1,2,3] The officers found ten packages holding the drug in the bus's
luggage. [@1,2,3]

Officers from the Tenencia de Carreteras Hijuelas, a highway police unit,
carried out the check, according to Carabineros of the Valparaíso Region. [@1]

The officers were drawn to the luggage by a strong smell of marijuana coming
from it. [@1,2,3]

The woman was placed at the disposal of the Ministerio Público, Chile's public
prosecutor's office. [@1,2,3] Carabineros did not state the bus's origin or
destination. [@1]
