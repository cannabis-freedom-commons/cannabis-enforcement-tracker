The Coutances court in Manche tried a 28-year-old driver on 10 November 2026
over a crash committed while he was still under the influence of cannabis. [@1]
The crash happened at 4:30 p.m. on 2 January 2026 in a street in
Percy-en-Normandie, Manche, when vehicles slowed and a woman driver braked
sharply. [@1]

The 28-year-old man following her was inattentive, braked too late and hit the
car ahead from behind. [@1] The collision injured the woman driver whose car was
struck from behind. [@1] After the crash the driver tested positive for the drug
in a saliva screening. [@1]

He had smoked a joint on New Year’s Eve. [@1] The court in Coutances sentenced
him to six months in prison with a suspended term. [@1] His driving licence was
cancelled. [@1]
