Military Police officers arrested a 27-year-old man on suspicion of drug
trafficking in São Sebastião do Sacramento, a district of Manhuaçu in Minas
Gerais, on Tuesday 10 November 2026. [@1] The action began when officers
approached two people in the district and found portions of marijuana. [@2]

The information led the officers to search a property on Rua Santa Cruz, where
they found more of the drug and material used in trafficking. [@1] In all, they
seized 15 marijuana tablets of different sizes and three other portions of the
drug. [@3] They also took R$1,757 in cash, three mobile phones, a precision
scale, a card machine and three surveillance cameras. [@1]

The man was arrested and taken to the Civil Police station in Manhuaçu with the
seized material. [@3] No weight was published for the 15 marijuana tablets or
the three other portions of the drug. [@1] No charge sheet, custody decision or
court outcome in the case was reported. [@1]
