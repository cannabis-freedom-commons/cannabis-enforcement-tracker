A judge in Sidrolândia, Mato Grosso do Sul, convicted C.G.L. of drug trafficking
for storing about 1,010 kg of marijuana at a rural property. [@1] Judge Bruce
Henrique dos Santos Bueno Silva, of the criminal court of the Sidrolândia
district, recognised the privileged form of trafficking, reduced the sentence to
2 years and 9 months in prison and allowed the defendant to appeal at liberty.
[@1] Under Brazil's drug law, privileged trafficking applies to a first-time
defendant with a good record who is not devoted to crime or part of a criminal
organisation. [@1]

The judge revoked the defendant's preventive detention and ordered his immediate
release unless another reason existed to keep him jailed. [@1] The defendant had
been held since 19 February 2026, when military police of the Força Tática, a
tactical unit, arrested him in the act at lot 601 of the Assentamento Eldorado,
a rural settlement. [@1] The marijuana had been found in tablets behind a
counter and under a tarpaulin in a space attached to the house, next to stored
tyres. [@1] The defendant told the court he had been offered R$ 1,000 through
WhatsApp by a man nicknamed "Sedex" to store the load temporarily, and that R$
835 found on the property was part of that payment. [@1]

The judge set the initial prison regime as semi-open and refused to replace the
prison term with restrictions of rights because of the large quantity of
marijuana seized. [@1] The final penalty was 2 years and 9 months in prison and
166 daily fines, each set at one thirtieth of the minimum wage in force at the
time of the facts. [@1]

Military police seized 1,010 kg of marijuana in 1,063 tablets stored in the
house of a farmstead at the Assentamento Eldorado, in the Capão Seco district.
[@2] The February operation also found eight bales of smuggled tyres, cash,
mobile phones and other materials; one man was arrested in the act and a second
suspect was taken as a witness. [@2]
