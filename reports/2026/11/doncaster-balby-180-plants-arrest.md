South Yorkshire Police officers executed a warrant at a property on St John's
Road in Doncaster at 8.40am on Tuesday 3 November. [@1] Officers descended on
the property in Balby shortly before 9am that same morning. [@1] A 41-year-old
man was arrested at the property on suspicion of production of cannabis. [@1] He
remained in police custody after the arrest. [@1]

Inside the property, officers discovered approximately 180 cannabis plants. [@1]
Police said the cannabis plants had been seized after officers raided the
Doncaster house. [@1] Officers kept a scene at the property during the day to
allow the cultivation to be dismantled. [@1]

The raided Doncaster house was described as a cannabis factory raided by police
officers. [@1] South Yorkshire Police, through a spokesperson, gave the 8.40am
timing for the St John's Road warrant and the figure of approximately 180 plants
for the cultivation found inside. [@1] The operation was attributed to South
Yorkshire Police, the force that executed the morning warrant in Doncaster. [@1]
