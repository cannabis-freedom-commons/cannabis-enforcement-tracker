During a patrol on a street in Grajewo, police noticed two men who hurried out
of a stairwell and got into a parked Ford. [@2] When asked for identification,
the men acted very nervously and insisted they had no prohibited substances.
[@1] Officers searched the car and found two packets of green dried plant
material in aluminium foil under the front passenger seat. [@2] A preliminary
drug test confirmed the material was marijuana weighing about 10 grams. [@2] The
40-year-old admitted that the dried material found in the car was his. [@2]
Police then searched the 37-year-old's flat and secured further intoxicants
there. [@2]

Both men were detained and taken to the police station. [@2] Both were charged
with possession of narcotic drugs. [@2] Under the drug-prevention law, the
possession charge carries up to three years' imprisonment. [@2] The secured
substances were placed in the police depot. [@1]
