Gendarmes arrested four men in Châtellerault, Monthoiron, Vouneuil-sur-Vienne
and La Puye on Tuesday 3 November 2026. [@1] The four are suspected of taking
part in a cocaine and cannabis trafficking operation. [@1] The gendarmerie had
been investigating the case since spring 2026. [@1]

The four were handed over to the Poitiers prosecutor's office on Friday 6
November and brought straight to an immediate-appearance hearing. [@1] Their
trial was postponed after the defence asked for time to prepare. [@1] The
hearing before the Poitiers criminal court therefore did not address the
substance of the case. [@1] They are to be tried in January 2027. [@1]
