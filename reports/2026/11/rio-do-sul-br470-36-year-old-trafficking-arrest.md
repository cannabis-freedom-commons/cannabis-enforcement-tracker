Military Police arrested a 36-year-old man for drug trafficking on the evening
of Friday 13 November 2026 in Rio do Sul, in the Alto Vale do Itajai. [@1] The
arrest happened at about 6pm in the patio of a petrol station on the BR-470.
[@1] The second account places that petrol station in the Pamplona
neighbourhood. [@2] Police said the patrol had received complaints that the
suspect was selling drugs in the municipality. [@1] During patrols officers saw
the man smoking marijuana under a tree and approached him. [@1]

A search of the vehicle he was driving found a portion of cocaine on the
passenger seat and R$3,000 in cash. [@1] During the stop the man said the drug
was his and told officers he kept more drugs at his home. [@1] Officers went to
the house and found another portion of cocaine, a glass container holding
marijuana and another R$361 in cash. [@1] The second account places that
residence in the Canoas neighbourhood. [@2] In total officers seized R$3,361 in
cash, the drugs, a mobile phone and the car used by the suspect. [@1]

The man was arrested and taken with the seized material to the Civil Police
station for the applicable legal procedures. [@1] The second account attributes
its information to the 13th Military Police Battalion in Rio do Sul. [@2]
