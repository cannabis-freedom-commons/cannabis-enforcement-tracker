Agents of the DNCD, the Dominican drug-control agency, working with the Public
Ministry and police officers, arrested 169 people during a week of operations
against microtrafficking in the country's Norte and Nordeste regions. [@1,2,3] Of
the 169 detained, 168 were men and one was a woman. [@1] The authorities carried
out 1,468 operations and 17 searches in the provinces of Santiago, Espaillat,
Puerto Plata, María Trinidad Sánchez, Samaná and Duarte, targeting drug
distribution and sales points. [@1,2,3]

Officers seized 29,787.3 grams of suspected drugs in all, of which 2,077.1 grams
was marijuana. [@1,2,3] In Santiago, including Navarrete and Santiago Oeste, 321
operations and four searches led to 44 arrests and the seizure of 868.5 grams of
marijuana. [@1,2] In San Francisco de Macorís, Duarte, 315 operations and three
searches produced 29 arrests and 386.1 grams of marijuana. [@1] In María
Trinidad Sánchez, 409 operations and three searches led to 28 arrests, including
one woman, and the seizure of 461.8 grams of marijuana. [@1] In Samaná, 200
interventions led to 25 arrests and the seizure of 158.3 grams of marijuana.
[@1] In Puerto Plata, 98 operations and seven searches led to 25 arrests and the
seizure of 120.5 grams of marijuana. [@1]

In Espaillat, 125 operations led to 18 arrests and the seizure of portions of
marijuana alongside 1,359.6 grams of suspected cocaine. [@1] Officers also
seized RD$45,265 in cash, four US dollars, euro notes, 36 mobile phones, seven
radios, five digital scales, three motorcycles, cutting tools and a bulletproof
vest. [@1] The detainees and the evidence were handed to the Public Ministry in
the corresponding jurisdictions for legal proceedings. [@1,2] The DNCD said the
operations were part of its joint work with the Public Ministry to combat
microtrafficking and dismantle drug distribution points in communities around
the country. [@1]

In María Trinidad Sánchez, mainly in Nagua, 409 operations and three searches led to 28 arrests, including 27 men and one woman, and the seizure of 461.8 grams of marijuana. [@2]
