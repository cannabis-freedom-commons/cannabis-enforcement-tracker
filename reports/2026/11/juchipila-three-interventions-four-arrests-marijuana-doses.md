Security forces in Zacatecas's southern region arrested four people and seized
suspected drugs in three separate interventions in Juchipila. [@1] The first
intervention involved Mexican Army, National Guard, FRIZ and FGJEZ investigative
police personnel, who caught a woman and a man after they tried to flee in
different directions and were chased on foot. [@1] The second intervention on
Calle Narciso González detained a woman who tried to flee when she saw the
police units. [@1] The third intervention in colonia Centro stopped the driver
of a taxi who was allegedly photographing the police vehicles and who tried to
evade them while throwing metal objects known as ponchallantas. [@1]

Together the three interventions yielded 106 doses of suspected drugs, of which
31 were substances with characteristics of marijuana, 43 probable
methamphetamine and 32 probable cocaine. [@1] Officers also seized 35 metal
tire-puncturing spikes known as ponchallantas, two communication radios, a
motorcycle and a Nissan Tsuru III used as a public taxi. [@1] The four
detainees, two men and two women, were handed to the competent authority with
the drugs, objects and vehicles while investigations determine their legal
situation. [@1] The three interventions took place in the municipality of
Juchipila, in southern Zacatecas, according to the state Peace and Security
Construction Board cited by AlkimiaTV. [@2]

The state government said the operations formed part of a coordinated security
deployment against criminal activity and to reinforce surveillance in the
southern region of the state. [@2]
