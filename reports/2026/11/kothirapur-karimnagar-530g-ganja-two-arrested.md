Excise department officials in Karimnagar town, Telangana, arrested two people
over ganja, the local name for cannabis, and seized 530 grams (0.53 kg) of the
drug. [@1] The two suspects were identified as Ghasiram and Lakshmi. [@1] The
arrests were part of the department's crackdown on illegal ganja activity in the
town. [@1]

The raid was carried out in the Kothirapur area of Karimnagar town on a
Thursday, as a surprise check on credible information received by excise staff.
[@3] It was ordered by the Assistant Commissioner of Enforcement and led by
Circle Inspector Lobanand. [@1] Assistant sub-inspector Abhishek, head constable
Srikant and constables Suman, Arif, Parushuram and Divya took part in the
operation. [@1] Officers questioned the two suspects and recovered the ganja
before arresting them. [@2] Both were described as people involved in selling
the ganja. [@3]

Excise officials said their department keeps a continuous watch on the illegal
transport, sale and use of ganja and will take stern legal action against those
involved in such activities. [@1]
