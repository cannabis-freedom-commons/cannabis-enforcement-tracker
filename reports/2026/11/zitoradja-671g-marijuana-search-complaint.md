Officers of the Interior Ministry in Nis searched the flat and other premises
used by a man from the surroundings of Zitoradja. [@1] Police identified the man
as Z. P., born in 1969, from the surroundings of Zitoradja. [@1] The search
found 671 grams of material suspected to be marijuana and a precision scale.
[@1]

By order of the Higher Public Prosecutor's Office in Prokuplje, a criminal
complaint will be filed against him on suspicion of unauthorised production of
narcotic drugs and placing them into circulation. [@1] The complaint will be
filed in the regular procedure, with no detention reported. [@1]
