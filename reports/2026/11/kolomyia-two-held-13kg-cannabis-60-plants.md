Police in Ivano-Frankivsk region exposed a 48-year-old Kolomyia resident and his
29-year-old accomplice from Kolomyia district over a full cycle of cannabis
production. [@1,4,7,8,11,13] The older man sowed cannabis on open ground in the Horodenka
community at the end of spring 2026 and tended the crop to maturity. [@1,9,10,13] He
illegally grew more than 60 cannabis plants. [@1,6,13] Both men work in the railway
industry, and the organizer later brought in the younger man to harvest, process
and move the crop. [@1,4,11,13]

In September 2026 they jointly collected the cannabis harvest into prepared
bags. [@1,6,11,13] They drove the raw material in the older man's Mercedes-Benz to
Kolomyia and to Sheparivtsi, where they dried it and stored it for sale. [@1,4,9,11,13] In
Kolomyia police seized 7 buckets of plant material, and in Sheparivtsi, at the
younger man's home, plastic wraps, packets and a container of crushed plant
mass. [@1,9,11,13] Those searches recovered more than 13.5 kg of dried cannabis and 60
cannabis plants. [@3,5,8] Police also seized the car used to transport the cannabis. [@9,11] The searches also recovered seeds, cash and mobile phones. [@10] Officers carried out 13 authorised searches at the suspects' and their relatives' homes in Kolomyia and Kolomyia district. [@5,10] The searches also covered a garage, a dacha building and the suspects' car, where police found equipment for growing cannabis. [@9] Police put the black-market value at no less than 8
million hryvnias and said the amount is an especially large amount. [@1,4,8,9,12,13]

Police said the men planned to sell the bulk wholesale to other regions of
Ukraine. [@1,9,11,13] They also sold hand-to-hand in Kolomyia district. [@1,13] Both men
were detained on 9 November 2026. [@3,5]

On 10 November prosecutors notified both men of suspicion of group manufacture,
storage and transportation for sale under Part 3 of Article 307. [@3,5] The
48-year-old organizer was additionally notified of illegal sowing and
cultivation under Part 2 of Article 310. [@1,12] A court ordered both held in
custody without bail. [@2,4,5,6,8,9,11,13] The men face up to twelve years in prison with confiscation of property. [@5,10] The pre-trial investigation continues, with procedural guidance provided by the Ivano-Frankivsk Regional Prosecutor's Office. [@11,13]
