A court in Amur Oblast convicted a 20-year-old resident of the village of
Petropavlovka, in Ivanovsky District, of buying a large consignment of cannabis.
[@1] The conviction replaced an earlier suspended sentence with a real prison
term. [@1]

Law enforcement officers detained the man, who was already serving a suspended
sentence, over the drug he bought illegally in June. [@1] The defendant pleaded
guilty in full at the court hearing. [@1]

The court revoked the suspended sentence and imposed a final penalty of two and
a half years in a general-regime penal colony. [@1]
