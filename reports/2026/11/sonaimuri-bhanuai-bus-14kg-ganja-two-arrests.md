Officers of the Department of Narcotics Control and SRB-11 searched a Sagarika
passenger bus bound from Sylhet to Noakhali at Bhanuai, in Noakhali's Sonaimuri
upazila, on the evening of 8 November 2026. [@1,3,4,5] The arrested were Alamgir
Hossain, 35, son of Md Dulal, and Md Jibon, 22, son of Harun, both of ward 7 of
Bajra union in Sonaimuri upazila. [@2,5]

Officers seized 14 kilograms of ganja from inside the bus and arrested the two
men. [@1,3,4,5] The Department of Narcotics Control said the two had brought the ganja
from Brahmanbaria. [@1,3,5] The operation followed a tip-off and was carried out by
the narcotics department's Noakhali district office together with SRB-11, the
Special Response Battalion. [@1]

Noakhali Media reported that the two were proceeded against under sections
19(b)/41 of the table in section 36(1) of the Narcotics Control Act 2018, with a
narcotics department sub-inspector filing the case at Sonaimuri police station.
[@2,4] Subrata Sarkar Shuvo, deputy director of the narcotics department's
Noakhali district office, said a case had been filed against both men under the
Narcotics Control Act. [@1,4]

Officers also seized a button mobile phone used to carry the drug, the report said. [@3]

The operation followed a tip-off and ran from about 7:30 pm to about 8:30 pm beside the Darur Rashad Islamia madrasa in Bhanuai, where officers stopped the bus on the Cumilla-Noakhali regional highway, a detail earlier accounts left out. [@4]
