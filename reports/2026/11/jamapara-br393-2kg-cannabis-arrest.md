Military police apprehended a 17-year-old boy on Wednesday night, 4 November,
aboard a Viação Rio Doce bus on the Rodovia Lúcio Meira (BR-393), near Jamapará,
a district of Sapucaia. [@1] The stop occurred during a Military Police
inspection of the bus. [@1] The Military Police said officers noticed a strong
odor resembling marijuana coming from a backpack the teenager had with him. [@1]
Questioned by officers, the boy said he was transporting the drugs from Nova
Holanda, in the northern zone of Rio de Janeiro, to Ipanema in Minas Gerais.
[@1]

A search found approximately two kilograms of marijuana and three tablets of
hashish. [@1] Officers also found about one kilogram of a substance similar to
cocaine, 500 grams of material similar to crack and 19 packets of a substance
identified as ICE. [@1] They also seized two packages containing about 100
zip-type plastic bags commonly used to package drugs. [@1]

Police took the teenager to the 109th Police Precinct accompanied by the
Conselho Tutelar, Brazil's child-protection council. [@1] After review by the
police authority, he was booked for conduct analogous to drug trafficking and
remained detained at the disposal of the courts. [@1]
