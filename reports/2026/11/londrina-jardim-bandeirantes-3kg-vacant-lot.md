On the night of Tuesday 10 November 2026, at about 19:40, a team from the 5th
Military Police Battalion of the Paraná Military Police seized approximately 3
kilograms of maconha from a vacant lot on Rua Serra de Roraima, in the Jardim
Bandeirantes neighbourhood of Londrina. [@1] The Paraná Military Police carried
out the seizure through a team of the 5th Military Police Battalion, the 5º BPM.
[@1] The action began after a tip that individuals had left a black bag hidden
on the lot. [@1] With that information, the officers went to the address and
searched the place. [@1]

During the search the team found a plastic bag containing three tablets of a
substance analogous to maconha. [@1] Police described the contents as a
substance analogous to maconha, packed as three tablets. [@1] The three tablets
together totalled approximately 3 kilograms. [@1] The drug was seized and sent
to the duty police station, the Delegacia de Plantão, for the legal procedures
that follow such a seizure. [@1]

After the seizure, the officers patrolled the surrounding area in an attempt to
locate the people responsible for the material. [@1] No suspect was found. [@1]
The patrol around the vacant lot did not locate the people responsible for the
hidden material. [@1]
