Gwent Police Special officers were out on patrol in Newport city centre, working
to keep the city safe. [@1] During the patrol, officers conducted a stop search
in the city centre. [@1,2]

The search was carried out under Section 23 of the Misuse of Drugs Act. [@1] The
search resulted in the seizure of a quantity of cannabis. [@1,2] The patrol, the
search and the seizure all took place in Newport city centre. [@1]

Gwent Police said proactive policing such as this is crucial to help tackle
drug-related activity. [@1] The force said proactive policing like this helps
tackle drug-related activity and makes communities safer. [@1] A Gwent Police
spokesperson praised the Special officers for excellent work. [@1]
