A local court in Rawalpindi sentenced a drug supplier to 14 years of rigorous
imprisonment and fined him Rs400,000. [@1] The convicted man, identified by
police as Zulfiqar, was arrested by Chauntra Police in May 2025. [@1] Officers
recovered 5.360 kilograms of hashish from him. [@1]

After the investigation, police filed the charge sheet and the prosecution
presented forensic evidence and witness testimony. [@1] The conviction was
secured under sections of the Control of Narcotic Substances Act, the law
Pakistan uses to prosecute drug offences. [@1]

Rawalpindi Police said enforcement operations would continue across the district
to break up drug supply chains and protect young people from narcotics abuse.
