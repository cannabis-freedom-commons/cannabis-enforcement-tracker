Addis Ababa police arrested a man for growing cannabis at his home in the city's Akaki-Kality
district after officers found 16 plants hidden among what looked like backyard vegetables. [@1]

The suspect, named as Nahom Aynalem, was taken in the 5,000 Houses neighbourhood of Woreda 5
after residents tipped off the Addis Ababa Police Commission. [@1] Meried Debele, deputy
commander and head of special crimes investigation at the Akaki-Kality police division, said
officers searched the property on that tip and found the plants in 16 growing containers. [@1]

Police said a body search while he was being taken into custody found him trying to hide an
unspecified item in a sock. [@1] He was detained while the investigation continued. [@1] The
commission urged residents to report suspected crime in their neighbourhoods. [@1]
