Police operations in Guarapuava and Pinhão resulted in the seizure of 22.39 kg
of drugs and the arrest in flagrante of three men on drug-trafficking suspicion
on Thursday 12 November. [@1] According to the 16th Battalion of the Paraná
Military Police, 21.6 kg of cannabis was seized in Pinhão and 794.14 grams of
cannabis and cocaine in the Boqueirão district of Guarapuava. [@1]

The joint operation of the military and civil police took place at 12h26, after
an intelligence review indicated that a 21-year-old man monitored by electronic
ankle brace was selling drugs from home. [@1,7] During the approach near the
property, the officers found two portions of ice-type cannabis in the suspect's
clothes. [@1,7] Searches of the two properties, authorized by residents, located
521.06 grams of pressed cannabis, 194.49 grams of ice-type cannabis and 78.59
grams of cocaine. [@1] The report estimates the value of the drugs seized in
Boqueirão at more than R$10,700. [@1,7]

At around 22h20, rural patrol teams found a crashed VW Gol on the shoulder of
kilometre 6 of the PR-459 in Pinhão. [@1] During the search, the officers found
one cannabis brick under the driver's seat and 40 more bricks in a package about
three metres from the car; the police recorded 21.6 kg as the total weight of
the seizure. [@1] According to the report, the driver said he picked up the load
in Chapecó (SC), was receiving instructions by phone and would earn R$4,000 for
the transport; he said he did not know the final destination of the delivery.
The man was arrested in flagrante and taken to the Pinhão Civil Police station,
with the drugs and the vehicle. [@1]

The first incident was registered at around 12h26 in the Boqueirão district, in
an integrated operation between the Military Police and the Civil Police. Two
men were arrested in flagrante on drug-trafficking suspicion. The seized
material was valued at more than R$10,700. [@4] The total of the seizure in
Guarapuava was 521.06 grams of pressed cannabis, 194.49 grams of ice-type
cannabis and 78.59 grams of cocaine. [@4]

A 31-year-old man was arrested on drug-trafficking suspicion on the night of
Thursday (12), after rural-patrol teams found a crashed vehicle on the shoulder
of the PR-459 at kilometre 6 in Pinhão. According to the 2nd Company of the
Military Police, 21.6 kilos of a substance similar to cannabis was seized during
the incident, registered at around 22h20. [@2] Questioned about the origin of
the load, the suspect told the officers that he had picked up the material in
Chapecó, in Santa Catarina. He also reported that he was receiving instructions
from a third person by phone messages during the trip. Still according to the
Military Police, the man claimed not to know the final destination of the
delivery and said he would receive R$4,000 for the transport. [@2] Under the
driver's seat, one brick of a substance analogous to cannabis was found, with a
weight later measured at 0.514 grams. About three metres from the car, the
officers located a package with 40 more bricks of the same substance, which
totalled 21.086 kilos. In total, 21.600 kilos of narcotic was seized. [@3]

According to the Military Police, the operation was triggered after intelligence
investigations pointed to a 21-year-old man, a recent releasee from the prison
system and monitored by electronic ankle brace, who would have resumed the sale
of drugs from his residence. [@5] During the procedures, the person responsible
for the property arrived at the scene and confirmed that he was storing the
drugs at the request of the first suspect. Given the situation, the two men were
arrested and taken to the 14th Police Subdivision (SDP), together with a mobile
phone and all the seized material. [@5]

When approaching the 31-year-old driver, the officers noticed contradictions in
the information presented and carried out searches of the vehicle and the
surroundings. One cannabis brick was found under the driver's seat and 40 more
in a package located a few metres from the car. Questioned, the man reported
that he had picked up the load in Chapecó (SC) and would receive R$4,000 for the
transport. He was arrested in flagrante and taken, together with the drugs and
the vehicle, to the Pinhão Civil Police station. [@6]
