On Sunday, Sadar police in Muzaffarpur raided a shop in Patahi and arrested one
man after finding ganja on him. [@1] Police identified the arrested man as
Vishwanath Giri, a resident of Vaishali. [@1] Officers searched Giri at the shop
and recovered the ganja from his possession. [@1]

The search yielded 48 grams of ganja, equal to 0.048 kilograms. [@1] The Patahi
shop lies within the Sadar police station area of Muzaffarpur. [@1] The raid,
the 48-gram seizure and the single arrest form one enforcement action at that
shop. [@1]

After the recovery, police registered a First Information Report, or FIR, the
written entry that opens a criminal case, against the accused. [@1] The FIR
names Giri as the accused and moves the matter into further investigation. [@1]
Police said further action was under way following the FIR, with no court
outcome reported at that point. [@1]
