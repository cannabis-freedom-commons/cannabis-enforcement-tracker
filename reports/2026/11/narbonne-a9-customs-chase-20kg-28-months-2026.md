A 41-year-old father living in Montpellier was driving up the A9 with close to
20 kg of cannabis herb and resin on the morning of 7 November 2026. [@1] He
tried to shake off Catalan customs officers before losing control of his car at
the entrance to Narbonne. [@1] The Narbonne prosecutor said the man took every
risk and that it was a miracle no accident happened on the A9 because of him.
[@1]

The defendant, a 41-year-old Moroccan national living in Montpellier, was coming
up the A9 from Barcelona and carrying two bags of cannabis in a saloon car that
was not his. [@1]

The Catalan customs officers arrested him at Narbonne. [@1] The sentence
includes a five-year ban on French territory. [@1]
