Ho Chi Minh City drug-investigation police and commune police handled 6,401 drug
cases involving 17,756 alleged violators in the first 10 months of 2026. [@1] In
those operations they seized 518 kg of cannabis, which police call cần sa, along
with more than 640 kg of synthetic drugs and nearly 15 kg of heroin. [@1] Police
said many large-scale interprovincial and transnational rings and dens were
detected and dismantled. [@1] The totals were presented on the morning of 3
November 2026 when a Central Propaganda Department delegation surveyed
implementation of Politburo Directive 07-CT/TW at the Ho Chi Minh City Police,
known as CATP. [@1]

A city report on the same 10-month totals said units had broken up 75 organized,
transnational drug cases involving 780 suspects and handled 565 cases exploiting
cyberspace. [@2] In a related 45-day campaign to review addicts and illegal
users, police found 7,672 positive cases, prosecuted 4,669 suspects, placed more
than 8,400 people under management and questioned or deterred nearly 18,000
people. [@1] Police said the new approach traced supply from users to local sale
points and used a heat map and online data to identify complex areas and assign
officers. [@1]

City police leaders said the goal of building a drug-free Ho Chi Minh City by
2030 must start from each commune and ward, with responsibility placed on local
heads and participation by the whole political system. [@1]
