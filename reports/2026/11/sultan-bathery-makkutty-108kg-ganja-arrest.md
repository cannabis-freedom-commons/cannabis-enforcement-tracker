Police in Sultan Bathery arrested 35-year-old Aby Elias during a vehicle check
near Makkutty bridge. [@1] Elias is described as a resident of Pelakkudi house
at Puthankunnu, Makkutty in Bathery. [@1] Bathery sub-inspector Noufal C made
the arrest together with Bathery police and the district anti-drug squad. [@1]
The operation was conducted under the supervision of Wayanad district police
chief S. Deva Manohar and led by Narcotic Cell and Gender Justice DySP Manoharan
Thachampath. [@1]

Officers seized 1 kg 80 g of ganja, or 1.08 kg, from the accused. [@1] Ganja is
the Malayalam term used in the report for cannabis. [@1] They also seized an
electronic scale used for weighing and plastic covers kept for packing the drug
for sale. [@1]

Police said the ganja had been smuggled from Mysuru intended for sale. [@1] The
accused was intercepted at the Makkutty bridge checkpoint while the joint team
was conducting vehicle checks. [@1] The drug, scale and packing material were
all recovered from him at that interception. [@1]
