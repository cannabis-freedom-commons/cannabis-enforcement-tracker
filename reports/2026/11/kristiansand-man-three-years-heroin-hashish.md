A 57-year-old man from Kristiansand was sentenced to three years in prison after
being caught with large quantities of heroin and other narcotics on several
occasions. [@1] Police also seized more than 190,000 kroner in cash, which the
man has now forfeited, and the judgment is his 49th. [@1] He was arrested with
narcotics five times between July 2023 and September 2026, with seizures made at
several places in Kristiansand city centre. [@1] In total he stored about 149
grams of heroin, 128 grams of amphetamine and 84 grams of methamphetamine, as
well as hashish and several hundred narcotic tablets. [@1]

One of the incidents took place in March 2025, when a bus driver called police
about a passenger he could not wake, and officers found the man unconscious at
the bus station in Kristiansand. [@1] In his pockets were 61.73 grams of heroin,
56.75 grams of amphetamine and a number of narcotic tablets, and he was taken to
hospital and given an antidote. [@1]

At three of the drug seizures police found a total of 191,450 kroner in cash.
[@1] The man said the money came from his disability pension and had been saved
for a deposit on a home, but Agder tingrett did not believe the explanation and
confiscated the whole amount. [@1] The man has 48 previous convictions,
including a number of narcotics cases, and said he had been addicted since he
was 13 or 14 and that the narcotics were intended for his own use. [@1] The
court found that the storage was in principle connected to his own addiction but
stressed that the quantities carried a considerable risk of distribution. [@1]

The court found that all the offences in the man's 49th criminal case warranted
a combined sentence of five years, reduced because of his confessions and the
long processing time. [@1] Agder tingrett found that the case had had around 14
months of delay, which it said amounted to a breach of the European Convention
on Human Rights. [@1] The sentence was therefore set at three years in prison.
