Police in Kahramanmaraş reported that teams across the province intervened in 64
narcotics incidents between 2 and 8 November 2026, caught 74 people and jailed
12 of them. [@1] Public-order officers questioned 29,527 people during the same
week, caught 92 and jailed 19 of them, part of the 34 people jailed across the
provincial police directorate's branches. [@1] The smuggling and organised crime
branch took judicial action against 15 people and jailed three of them. [@1] The
provincial police directorate said the 34 people jailed after the week were held
for different crimes. [@3]

The week's narcotics seizures included 61.33 grams of esrar, the Turkish term
for cannabis, and 75.34 grams of skunk, a cannabis strain, a cannabis total of
136.67 grams. [@1] The same searches found 629.62 grams of methamphetamine, 639
synthetic pharmaceutical pills and 27.5 Ecstasy-Captagon pills, none of which is
counted as cannabis. [@1] Officers also seized 118.96 grams of bonzai and 30.06
grams of bonzai raw material; bonzai is a synthetic cannabinoid and is not
counted as cannabis here. [@1] Officers also seized two precision scales and
2,800 Turkish lira in cash. [@1] The public-order searches also turned up nine
unlicensed pistols, three blank-firing pistols, five hunting rifles, 56 pistol
cartridges, 30 shotgun cartridges, five knives and 19 blank cartridges. [@1] The
smuggling branch reported that its seizures included 24,452 road permit
documents and 30 kilograms of hookah tobacco. [@1]

MaraşBugün reported the same narcotics results from the province's narcotics
branch: 64 incidents, 74 people caught and 12 jailed. [@2] That account said the
suspects were sent to the judicial authorities and that 12 of them were jailed
after that transfer. [@2] Aksu TV Haber described the 2,800 lira as cash judged
to have been obtained from crime. [@3] Kahramanmaraş police said checks and
operations would continue with determination to keep the province peaceful and
secure. [@1]
