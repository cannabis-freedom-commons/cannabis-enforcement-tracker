Border investigators from the Passau border police inspectorate stopped the
driver of a VW Polo with German registration at the Passau Süd junction on a
Friday evening. [@1] The man behind the wheel was identified as the car's
27-year-old Afghan owner. [@1] Officers opened criminal proceedings against him
under the Konsumcannabisgesetz, Germany's Consumer Cannabis Act. [@1] After the
police measures were finished, officers allowed him to continue his journey.
[@1]

A search of the vehicle during the check found a small amount of hashish in the
glove compartment. [@1] Officers said the driver had intended to take that
hashish from Germany to Austria. [@1] Officers seized the hashish as part of the
proceedings. [@1]

The check was carried out by Schleierfahnder, the border-investigation officers
who conduct dragnet checks in the area. [@1] The stop took place at the Passau
Süd motorway junction in Passau. [@1] The published account names the Passau
border police inspectorate as the source of the information. [@1] The car model
was reported as a VW Polo. [@1]
