A suspect was arrested for allegedly clearing protected forest land and
cultivating cannabis at two locations inside Somawathiya National Park. [@1] Two
other suspects fled the area during the operation. [@1] The arrested suspect was
produced before the Hingurakgoda Court on November 04 and remanded until
November 13. [@1]

Department of Wildlife Conservation officers discovered the plantations during a
joint raid conducted on November 02 in a protected area above Dozer Pitiya in
the national park. [@1] The two plantations together covered approximately 1.5
acres, with half an acre cleared at one location and one acre at another. [@1]

The raid was carried out jointly by officers from the Kantale Wildlife Office,
Kaudulla National Park and Sri Lanka Army Intelligence. [@1] The Department of
Wildlife Conservation said further investigations are underway. [@1]
