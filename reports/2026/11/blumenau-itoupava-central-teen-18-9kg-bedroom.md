Military police apprehended a 17-year-old girl on suspicion of involvement in
drug trafficking in the Itoupava Central neighbourhood of Blumenau, Santa
Catarina, on the night of Tuesday 10 November 2026. [@1] The Military Police
registered the case at about 21:01 that night, after information that drugs
might be in the room. [@2] In the bedroom, officers found about 18.9 kilograms
of marijuana, according to the Military Police. [@2] They also seized a
precision scale and a notebook with notes that police said were related to drug
dealing. [@2] A personal document belonging to a man described as the possible
owner of the marijuana, said to be the girl's boyfriend, was also found. [@2]

The girl was taken to the Central de Plantão Policial, the city's on-duty police
station, with all the seized material. [@3] The case was registered as an ato
infracional análogo ao crime de tráfico de drogas, the juvenile equivalent of a
drug-trafficking charge in Brazil. [@2] Blumenau lies in the Vale do Itajaí
region of Santa Catarina. [@3]
