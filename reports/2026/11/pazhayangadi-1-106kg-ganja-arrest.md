Police in Pazhayangadi, in Kerala's Kannur district, arrested a 25-year-old man
from West Bengal and seized 1.106 kilograms of ganja from him. [@1] Police
identified him as Biswajit Mandal, a native of Bangsar in Murshidabad, and
described him as a labourer from another state. [@1] The arrest was made in the
early hours of 12 November 2026, in front of the Amayi level crossing under the
Pazhayangadi railway under bridge. [@1]

A DANSAF team working under the control of the Kannur City Police Commissioner
made the arrest together with the Pazhayangadi station house officer, a grade
sub-inspector and a civil police officer. [@1] A second Malayalam news outlet
published the same account of the arrest. [@2]
