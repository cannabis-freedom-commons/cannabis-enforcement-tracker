Tikari and Chandauti police jointly arrested four accused in Gaya district,
Bihar. [@1] Rural Superintendent of Police Divyanjali Jaiswal announced the
arrests at a press conference on Thursday, saying police caught the four while
raiding for named accused in Tikari police station case 423/26. [@1] The four
were identified as Sanjit Kumar, 38; Ajit Kumar, 32; Rohit Kumar, 24; and Kundan
Kumar, 24. [@1] All four lived in Bengali Bigaha village in the Chandauti
police-station area. [@1]

Information from Sanjit during questioning led police to search his home, where
they recovered 357 grams (0.357 kg) of ganja (cannabis), a country-made pistol
and two live cartridges. [@1] Police seized all the items recovered from the
home. [@1] The joint team included Deputy Superintendent of Police Ravi Prakash,
Tikari sub-divisional police officer Sushant Kumar Thayal, Chandauti station
officer Anil Chaudhary, Tikari sub-inspector Kanhaiya Kumar and other officers.
[@1]

Police said they had also registered a first information report concerning the
firearm and ganja recovery. [@1] Investigators were checking where the items
came from and what role others might have had in their use or supply. [@1]
Police were processing the four accused for judicial custody. [@1] Jaiswal said
the arrests followed a planned effort to detain named accused amid an ongoing
police campaign to control crime. [@1]
