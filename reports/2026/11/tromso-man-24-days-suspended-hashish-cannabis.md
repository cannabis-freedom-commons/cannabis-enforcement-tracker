A man in his 20s has been sentenced to 24 days' suspended imprisonment with a
two-year probation period, and must pay 3,000 kroner in legal costs. [@1] The 24
days and the 3,000 kroner were the outcome of the case covering his outburst,
his possession of a knife and his possession of hashish and cannabis. [@2] In
the summer of 2025 he was found carrying a folding knife in an inner pocket at a
petrol station in Tromsdalen, and he also had 2.1 grams of cannabis on him. [@1]
He was convicted of illegally carrying the knife in a public place. [@1]

On several later occasions police caught him with smaller quantities of hashish
and cannabis. [@1] In May 2026 he was stopped twice on the same evening: police
found about one gram of hashish in his pocket and another 4.2 grams in his
jacket pocket at midnight. [@1]

The events that led to the sentence took place over two days in October 2025,
when he behaved aggressively, verbally and physically, during a search of him at
the police station in Tromsø. [@1] When he was to be placed in custody he tried
to bite one detention officer in the arm, and the officer blocked the bite with
his elbow. [@1] The next day he threatened a second detention officer who had
taken part in the search, saying on the way to an interview room: «Jeg skal
finne deg og ta deg». [@1]
