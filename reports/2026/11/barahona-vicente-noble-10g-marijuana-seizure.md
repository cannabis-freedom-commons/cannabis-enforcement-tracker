The National Police, in coordination with the Public Prosecutor's Office,
carried out several searches and operations in homes in different sectors of
Barahona and the municipality of Vicente Noble. [@1] Agents from the
Investigations and Preventive Police departments and the Rapid Action Unit took
part alongside representatives of the Public Prosecutor's Office. [@1] The
account reported no detentions and gave no details about any people linked to
the seized objects. [@1]

Officers seized five hand-made firearms known locally as chilenas, four 9 mm
cartridges, several capsules of the same calibre and five communication radios.
[@1] They also seized approximately 10 grams of a green plant material presumed
to be marijuana. [@1] Authorities also confiscated 29 bottles of allegedly
adulterated alcohol known as clerén, a set of handcuffs and 18 packs of
contraband cigarettes, among other items. [@1]

Police said the operations are part of preventive and crime-prosecution actions
in the Enriquillo region under regional director Rigoberto de los Santos
Guerrero to remove illegal weapons from circulation and strengthen citizen
security. [@1]
