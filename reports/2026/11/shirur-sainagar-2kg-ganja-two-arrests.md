Shirur police raided the Sainagar area behind the Shirur market yard at about
half past ten on the night of Thursday 5 November 2026 and arrested two men
accused of selling ganja. [@1] Those arrested were Utkarsh Jalindar Dhere, 21,
of Babhulsar Khurd in Shirur taluka, and Sai Santosh Sasane, 20, of Hudco
Colony, Shirur. [@1] Police named Ashutosh Milind Kale of Sainagar, Shirur, as
the main accused in the case and said he had fled and was being hunted. [@1]
According to police, Kale had stored the ganja, the local name for cannabis, at
his home and had passed some of it to Dhere and Sasane to sell. [@1]

The seizure totalled 2 kg 414 g of ganja, made up of 1 kg 809 g taken from
Kale's house, 122 g from Dhere, 80 g from Sasane and 403 g from the boot of an
Activa scooter. [@1] Officers also seized 99 empty plastic bags used for packing
ganja, a weighing scale, two iPhones, a OnePlus mobile phone and the scooter.
[@1] Police put the total value of the ganja and other items at 2,71,000 rupees.
[@1] A case was registered at Shirur police station under various sections. [@1]

The raid was carried out by a team led by Shirur police station's officer in
charge, Inspector Vinod Patil, and Assistant Inspector Shivajirao Bhosale, and
Constable A. S. Mane filed the complaint. [@1] The two arrested men were being
questioned while officers searched for Kale. [@1]
