Rawalpindi police arrested five suspected drug suppliers in separate operations
across the district on Friday, 13 November 2026, and recovered more than 8
kilograms of hashish from their possession. [@1]

Pirwadhai police held two suppliers and recovered 3.8 kilograms of hashish,
Ganjmandi police apprehended two and seized 3.14 kilograms, and Kahuta police
arrested another supplier and recovered 1.1 kilograms, together 8.04 kilograms.
[@1]

Separate cases were registered against the five arrested suspects, and the
police spokesperson said all possible measures were being taken to eradicate the
menace of drugs from society. [@1]
