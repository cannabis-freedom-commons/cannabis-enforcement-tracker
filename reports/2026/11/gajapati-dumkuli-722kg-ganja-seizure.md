Police in Gajapati district, Odisha, are conducting intensive checks to make the
district ganja-free. [@1] On Friday, SP Kaushik Sethi of Odayagiri police
station, with a team, was conducting checks in the Dumkuli village area when
they learned a person was preparing to illegally transport ganja bags, and
carried out a raid. [@1]

The raid found 722.400 kg of ganja in about 24 bags. [@1] The accused was
identified as Jajya Mandal, 59, a resident of the same village. [@1]

A case was registered and the accused was sent to court. [@1] SP Lakshan Besara
said a full investigation is continuing into the incident. [@1]

A corroborating account from Argus News confirms the seizure of 722 kg of ganja
and the arrest of Yaksha Mandal of Dumukuli village by R Udayagiri police in
Mohana, Gajapati district. [@2]
