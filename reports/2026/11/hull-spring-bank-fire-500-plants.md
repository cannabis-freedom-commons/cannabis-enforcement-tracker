Officers were alerted at around 8am on Saturday 7 November 2026 to reports of a
fire in a building above a shop on Spring Bank in Hull, near the junction with
Peel Street. [@1] After the fire was put out, two floors in the upper
residential area of the building were found to contain more than 500 suspected
cannabis plants, which the BBC put at about 500. [@1] Humberside Police said a
scene guard remained at the property and that officers were continuing their
enquiries to identify those involved, asking anyone with information to call 101
quoting reference 26/157260. [@1] No arrests were reported in either account.
[@1]

The road was closed in both directions for an hour and a half while firefighters
tackled the blaze close to Spring Bank's junction with Peel Street. [@1]
Humberside Fire and Rescue advised motorists on Saturday to avoid the area,
which it described as very busy, while it dealt with the incident. [@1] The BBC
reported that emergency services were called to the fire above a shop on Spring
Bank at about 08:00 GMT on Saturday. [@2]
