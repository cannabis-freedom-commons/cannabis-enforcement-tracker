In Sant Kabir Nagar, Uttar Pradesh, Dhanghata police arrested one accused with
900 grams (0.9 kg) of illegal ganja on Tuesday as part of the ongoing campaign
against trafficking of narcotic substances. [@1] The accused was identified as
Amitabh Bachchan, son of Ramnath, who is a resident of Nathnagar within the
limits of Mahuli police station. [@1] Police registered a first information
report against him under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act at
Dhanghata police station and completed the required legal formalities. [@1]
Officers produced him before the concerned court, which remanded him to judicial
custody in the district jail. [@1]

The operation was led by station in-charge Ajay Kumar Singh, whose team raided
the area near Ranjeet Baba's place in Rampur Dakshini village. [@1] During the
raid the team caught Amitabh Bachchan at the spot and detained him for checking.
[@1] A search of his possession then recovered 900 grams of illegal ganja, which
police recorded as the seized quantity in this case. [@1] Rampur Dakshini
village is the named arrest site, falling under Dhanghata police jurisdiction in
Sant Kabir Nagar district. [@1]
