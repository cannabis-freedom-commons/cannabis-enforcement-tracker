On 2 November 2026 the Giza Criminal Court heard the trial of seven defendants
accused of drug trafficking and unlicensed possession of firearms and ammunition
in the Badrashin case No. 19525 of 2025. [@1] The prosecution charges all seven
with possessing cocaine, phenethylamine, hashish and tramadol for trafficking
outside legally permitted circumstances. [@1] A second outlet confirms the same
seven defendants, the same Badrashin case numbers and the same trafficking and
firearms allegations. [@2] A third outlet describes the case as an alleged
criminal trafficking activity with unlicensed firearms and ammunition in
Badrashin, Giza. [@3]

The chemical laboratory found 27 bags of dark substance containing the active
ingredient of hashish, weighing 1,683.10 grams. [@1] It also found 500 plastic
bags of green plant parts weighing 300 grams, plus other hashish-active plant
parts weighing 2 grams and 15 grams. [@1] Another exhibit of 181 transparent
bags of dry green plant parts containing the active ingredient of hashish
weighed 120.29 grams, bringing the stated hashish total to 2,120.39 grams. [@1]
The reports separately list 499 tramadol tablets, crystalline powder containing
a phenethylamine derivative and tablets containing cocaine, which are not
counted in the cannabis weight. [@1]

The prosecution also charges possession of two rifled rapid-fire automatic
rifles, 90 rounds for them and 13 shotgun cartridges, allegedly with another
deceased person. [@1] A narcotics-control officer testified that he investigated
the defendants, obtained prosecution permission to arrest and search them, and
seized drugs, weapons and ammunition with a security force, an account supported
by three accompanying officers. [@1]

On 7 November 2026 the Giza Criminal Court sentenced all seven defendants in Badrashin case No. 19525 of 2025 over drug trafficking and unlicensed possession of firearms and ammunition. [@4] The first, second and fourth defendants received life imprisonment, the fifth, sixth and seventh 15 years, and the third defendant 10 years. [@4] The verdict came on the back of the seizure of about 2 kilograms of hashish and 500 tramadol tablets from the defendants. [@4] The chemical laboratory report put the hashish exhibits at a total of 2,120.39 grams, about 2 kilograms and 120 grams of hashish. [@4] The laboratory report separately listed 499 tramadol tablets, crystalline powder containing a phenethylamine derivative and tablets containing cocaine, none of which is counted in the hashish weight. [@4]
