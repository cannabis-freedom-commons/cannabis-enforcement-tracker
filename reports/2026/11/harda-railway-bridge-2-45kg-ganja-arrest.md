City Kotwali police in Harda arrested a 29-year-old man with 2.45 kg of ganja on
the night of 6 November 2026. [@1,4] Police put the market value of the seized
ganja at 50,000 rupees. [@1,3,4] The arrest was made by the city Kotwali station in
Harda, the police station that covers the town centre. [@1] He was held with
ganja, the Hindi name for cannabis, a preparation banned for sale and possession
in India. [@1] The report says officers found the ganja when they searched him
and the bag he was carrying. [@1]

The arrest was made by the city Kotwali station under crime number 742/2026 for an offence under section 8/20 of the NDPS Act, the section of the law that covers possession of cannabis. [@2,4] Station officer Roshanlal Bharti said the patrol was on Chhipaner Road when officers saw the man sitting near the service road by the Uda railway bridge with a plastic bag, surrounded him and searched the bag. [@2,4] The operation involved sub-inspector Manvendra Bhadoria, assistant sub-inspector Bhagwandas Yadav, head constables Harprasad Patel and Manish Patharia, and constables Pradeep Malviya and Ramnaresh, along with a woman constable. [@2,4] According to police, a case for assault, threats and other offences had already been registered against the suspect as crime number 390/2026, and the station officer called him a habitual offender. [@2,4] The station officer said cases under the NDPS Act had also been registered earlier against the suspect's relatives. [@2]

The accused was sent to jail. [@3]

Police said the arrest was made during a special statewide drive against narcotics and illicit liquor ordered by police headquarters. [@4]
