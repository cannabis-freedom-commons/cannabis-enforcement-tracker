Kandhamal police arrested 11 people and seized 750 kilograms of ganja in a raid
in the Gochhapada police station area of Odisha's Kandhamal district. [@1]
Police valued the seized cannabis at about Rs 75 lakh. [@1] Officers found the
ganja packed in 25 plastic sacks. [@1]

The raid followed intelligence gathered on 3 November about cannabis grown and
stored near forest streams in Salaguda and Katringia panchayats. [@1] A joint
enforcement team led by DSP Sunil Arun Kumar Nayak, SDPO Subhrajit Biswal and
Inspector Subhrajit Samantaray carried out the raid in the presence of an
executive magistrate. [@1] Preliminary investigations indicated the load was to
be sent to Madhya Pradesh by rail through neighbouring Boudh district. [@1]

Using drone surveillance, the team also found and destroyed cannabis crops
across nearly 25 acres of forest land. [@1] A case was registered at Gochhapada
police station under the NDPS Act, 1985. [@1]
