A 39-year-old man from Bretteville-sur-Laize, in the Calvados department of
Normandy, was convicted by the Caen judicial court on Wednesday 4 November 2026
of drug trafficking and tobacco smuggling. [@1] Gendarmes of the Moult brigade
community searched his home in Bretteville-sur-Laize on 31 October 2026. [@1]

Officers found more than two kilos of cannabis resin and cannabis herb at the
house. [@1] The search also turned up €4,140 in cash. [@1] The sentencing
hearing at the Caen judicial court, the Calvados court of first instance,
followed the search by four days. [@1]
