The mobile judicial-police brigade of Oued El Alleug, under the district
security service in Blida province, arrested three people suspected of selling
drugs and psychotropic substances in a neighbourhood of the town during the
week. [@1] The operation was launched after confirmed security intelligence was
exploited. [@1]

Police recovered 14 prohibited sixth-category bladed weapons of various sizes
and types. [@1] They also seized half a tablet of cannabis resin weighing 36.2
grams. [@1] They also seized a quantity of psychotropic substances and a sum of
money said by investigators to come from the alleged dealing. [@1]

In coordination with the prosecutor at the El Affroun court, investigators
obtained the warrants needed to search the suspects’ homes and continue the
investigation lawfully. [@1] Once the criminal case files were completed, the
three suspects were brought before the competent courts over unlawful possession
of drugs and psychotropics for sale and unlawful possession of prohibited
sixth-category bladed weapons without legal grounds. [@1] The operation was part
of national police operations against drug and psychotropic trafficking and
crime affecting public safety. [@1]
