Police arrested a 30-year-old and a 28-year-old man on suspicion of handling
large quantities of narcotic substances in Tartu and Tartu County in October and
November 2026. [@1] The two were detained on the evening of 3 November in Harju
County. [@1]

Prosecutor Ingmar Laas said that, on current information, the men bought and
resold highly potent and dangerous alpha-PVP as well as amphetamine, MDMA,
cannabis and oxycodone. [@1] During the investigation police searched the
suspects' vehicles and the 30-year-old man's home in Tartu, finding various
substances and tablets believed to be narcotics and items used for handling or
taking drugs. [@1] Tartu Postimees reported that officers found a weapon as well
as drugs of several kinds in the men's homes and cars. [@2]

Vosman said police had received information from several sources about possible
dealers operating in Tartu and detained the two when the right moment came. [@1]
Laas said the 28-year-old was due to start serving a previously imposed prison
sentence in November, and that the activity was not isolated but lasted a longer
period. [@1] The South Prefecture is conducting the pre-trial investigation
under the South District Prosecutor's Office. [@1]
