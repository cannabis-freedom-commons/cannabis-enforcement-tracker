Military Police officers from the Tactical team of the 15th Military Police
Battalion arrested a man in Belo Jardim, in Pernambuco's Agreste region, on the
afternoon of Wednesday 4 November 2026. [@1] Officers spotted the man running
down the street, which they considered suspicious behaviour, and he quickly went
into a house when he saw the team approaching. [@1] Police said the man was
wearing an electronic ankle monitor, the device used by Brazilian courts to
track people awaiting trial or serving alternative measures outside prison. [@1]
A second outlet, Na Boca do Povo, reported that the officers were on patrol at
about 3.30pm when they saw him. [@2]

During the approach officers found a portion of cannabis and 20 reais in cash
with him. [@1] The search continued in a nearby unoccupied plot of land, where
officers found about 170 grams of cannabis, a precision scale and plastic
packaging material. [@1]

The man was taken with the seized material to the Civil Police station for the
legal procedures. [@1] According to Na Boca do Povo, the case led to the opening
of a Verificação Preliminar de Informação, a preliminary police inquiry used to
check a reported offence before a full investigation is opened. [@2] Neither
account published the man's name or age. [@2]
