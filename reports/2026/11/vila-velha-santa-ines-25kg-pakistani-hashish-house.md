Military police of the Força Tática of the 4th Battalion found about 28 kg of
drugs in a rented house in Bairro Santa Inês, Vila Velha, on the night of
Thursday 5 November 2026. [@1] The haul included about 25 kg of Pakistani
hashish, marijuana, skunk and methamphetamine. [@1] No one was arrested. [@1]

The officers were on patrol when they received information that a man was using
the property to store drugs. [@1] A relative of the owner told the officers the
house was rented and that a large movement of people had been noticed there.
[@1] Looking through a window into one of the bedrooms, the officers saw
marijuana tablets and hashish packages spread across the floor between the
curtain gaps, and smelled a strong drug odour at the door. [@1] Officers broke
the padlock to enter and seize the material. [@1]

The hashish consisted of 51 sausages of Pakistani hashish weighing about 25 kg,
plus 12 smaller pieces of the same substance weighing 675 g. [@1] Officers also
seized three marijuana tablets weighing about 1.6 kg and two packets of skunk
weighing about 579 g. [@1] The rest of the haul was 220 g of crumble, 215 THC
pods, 22 packets of tobacco, 43 silicone jars, two precision scales, 82 lighters
and a ballistic vest. [@1]

The owner of the property, a 72-year-old man, was found nearby, confirmed he had
rented the house to the suspect and followed the operation. [@1] All the seized
material was taken to the 2nd Regional Police Station, and no information on the
whereabouts of the man who rented the house had been published. [@1] The second
outlet reported that the case is to be investigated to identify those
responsible for the drugs. [@2]
