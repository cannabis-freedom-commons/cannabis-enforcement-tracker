Crime Branch officers in Indore arrested a man with 4.503 kg of ganja, a form of
cannabis, near Bhandari Bridge on MR-4 Road, police said. [@1] Officers on
patrol spotted him sitting with a white plastic bag and caught him when he tried
to run, police said. [@2] Police identified the man as Aakash Chilegiya, a
resident of the Vijay Nagar area. [@2] Police said he had studied up to Class 10
and sold flowers. [@2]

Police estimated the seized ganja's value at about 45,000 rupees on the
international market. [@2] Police said he sold ganja under the cover of his
flower business. [@1] He already had eight criminal cases registered against
him, including assault, extortion and gambling, police said. [@1]

Additional Deputy Commissioner of Police Rajesh Dandotiya said a case was
registered against him under sections 8/20 of the NDPS Act. [@1] Police took him
on remand and were questioning him about where he obtained the ganja and who
else was involved in the network, they said. [@2] Police were investigating whom
he supplied the ganja to in the city, they said. [@1] Police were also
investigating how his flower business was used as cover for trafficking, they
said. [@1]
