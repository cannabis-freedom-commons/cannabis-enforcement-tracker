The cour d’assises de la Gironde, the Gironde criminal assize court, sentenced a
resident of Saint-Médard-en-Jalles to 18 years of réclusion criminelle for
attempted murder on Wednesday 4 November 2026. [@2] The court imposed the
sentence the advocate-general had requested. [@2] His lawyer said he does not
intend to appeal. [@2]

Some hours before the shooting, he took two savonnettes — slabs of cannabis
resin — from a dealing network in the Grand-Caillou estate in Eysines. [@2] The
two slabs were estimated at 1,000 euros. [@2] Ouest-France, citing Sud Ouest,
reports that an armed group came to the young man’s door to recover the two
slabs he had stolen from them hours earlier. [@1] On 18 May 2022, about fifteen
masked people came to settle a drug debt at his parents’ house in a residential
estate of Saint-Médard-en-Jalles, and he handed the drugs back through his
bedroom window. [@2] The assailants also wanted him punished, forced their way
in, and he came out with a pump-action shotgun and fired a first time. [@2]

He went back inside for a .22 long rifle and fired again, gravely wounding a
young man of 18 outside the neighbouring house, who has since lost the use of an
arm. [@1] The victim was hit in the right arm and then in the abdomen, and keeps
a functional deficit above 40%, the lawyer for the civil party said. [@2]

Six members of the group were identified and are soon to appear before other
courts, Ouest-France reports. [@1]
