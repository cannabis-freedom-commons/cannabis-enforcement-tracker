Officers of the Interior Ministry's service for combating illicit drug
trafficking in Bishkek shut down an internet shop that had allegedly sold
narcotics and psychotropic substances for several years. [@1,2] They detained one
suspected organizer during operational measures while the stash location was
being identified. [@1,2] The Interior Ministry says the detained man was one of
the organizers of a shop that distributed drugs in Bishkek and Chuy Region. [@1,2]

Officers found a packet containing a white powdery substance in the suspect's
car and a second packet at the suspected stash site. [@1,2] A search of his home
found equipment for growing plants, liquids stored in various containers,
packing materials and scales. [@1] Officers seized marijuana, hashish and heroin
that had been prepared for sale. [@1,2] According to preliminary estimates, the
seized substances could have fetched 4.5 million som if sold. [@1,2]

The suspect's phones contained a Telegram profile for internet shop "A" and
other accounts allegedly linked to purchases of large batches of drugs,
psychotropic substances and marijuana seeds. [@1,2]
