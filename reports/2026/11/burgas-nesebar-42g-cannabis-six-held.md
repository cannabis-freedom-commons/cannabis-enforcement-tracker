Six people were detained in police actions against drug distribution and
production in Burgas Province on the day before the 5 November reports. [@1] In
Prosenik, officers checked a private home lived in by a 34-year-old man with
previous convictions and found items and precursors linked to drug production.
[@1] Three other people were found in that Prosenik house, men aged 36 and 45
from Prosenik and Burgas and a 29-year-old woman from Kazanlak, and all four
were held for up to 24 hours. [@1] In Sredets, officers searched the home of a
40-year-old man already known to police. [@1,4] That Sredets man was detained for
up to 24 hours. [@1]

Officers also searched a flat in Nesebar's Pyasatsite quarter in an operation on
the same day and seized cannabis there. [@1,3,4,5,7,8] The Nesebar seizure was 42 grams of
cannabis, found in a flat lived in by a 42-year-old man from Sliven. [@2,3,4,5,6,7,8] The
Nesebar man was kept in custody and fast-track proceedings were opened against
him. [@2,3,4,6,8,9]

The same operation also yielded methamphetamine, chemicals, precursors and
production equipment. [@1] Shortly after release, the 45-year-old from Burgas
found at the Prosenik house was stopped in Prosenik driving under the influence
of methamphetamine and was detained again. [@2]
