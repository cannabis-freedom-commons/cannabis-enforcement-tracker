State Police arrested a 33-year-old man on suspicion of drug dealing after
catching him handing over a dose of hashish in the Gad district by Ferrara
railway station. [@1] Squadra Mobile investigators ran the operation on the
previous day as an observation and control service around the railway station as
part of work against drug trafficking and dealing. [@1] A second outlet dates
the Gad anti-dealing operation to Monday 2 November. [@3]

Officers documented a handover of drugs to a buyer who was stopped immediately.
[@1] The stopped buyer admitted that he had just bought a ball of hashish from
the suspect for personal use. [@2] Police then stopped the suspected dealer and
seized the drug. [@1] Following checks, the 33-year-old was arrested for the
offence under Article 73 of Presidential Decree 309/1990 on illicit production,
trafficking and possession of drugs. [@1]

On the order of the judicial authority, the man was held in the security cells
of Ferrara police headquarters pending a hearing to validate the arrest. [@2]
The intervention was part of Squadra Mobile control activity in the Gad areas,
focused on the railway-station zone, where prevention and repression services
continue. [@1]
