Security forces in Ibagué, Tolima, arrested 89 people in at least 30
search-and-seizure operations carried out under the Bloque Operativo de
Seguridad Urbana, or BOSUR, with 51 detained in flagrancia and 38 under judicial
warrants. [@1] Of the detainees, 23 were linked to drug trafficking, 25 to
homicide, 17 to theft, three to personal injury, two to illegal weapons
possession, one to femicide, one to conspiracy to commit crimes, one to
extortion and 16 to other offences. [@1]

The authorities reported the seizure of 18,441 doses of marijuana, alongside
8,485 doses of other substances. [@1] The other substances taken out of
circulation were 3,700 doses of cocaine, 4,720 doses of bazuco and 65 doses of
tussi, none of them cannabis. [@1] Officers also seized three firearms, four
traumatic pistols and 12 cartridges, plus a car, a motorcycle, 36 mobile phones
and 14 million pesos in cash, and recovered six motorcycles. [@1]

More than 500 National Police officers took part in the BOSUR intervention, with
support from the Attorney General's Office, the armed forces, Migración
Colombia, the Public Ministry and Ibagué's city hall. [@2] Mayor Johana Aranda
said the operations were closing the net on criminality, striking its structures
and attacking its sources of financing. [@1] Presenting the balance, the mayor
told offenders directly that in Ibagué the one who does it pays for it. [@3]
Municipal authorities said the interventions would continue in the city sectors
hit by microtrafficking and high-impact crime. [@4]
