Officers of the Anti-Drug and Smuggling Unit's Abercrombie branch searched the
home of Marie Noemy Chedy, a 30-year-old who lives at Colville Deverelle in Cité
La Cure. [@1] The operation was conducted under the supervision of Inspector
Conhyedoss. [@1] After the search, Chedy was placed in police detention. [@1]
She was put under a provisional charge of drug trafficking. [@1]

During the search, officers seized Rs 260,875 that police suspect came from the
sale of drugs. [@1] Officers also seized at the home a parcel containing 25.33
grams of cannabis. [@1] The cash and the cannabis parcel were discovered during
the home search at Colville Deverelle. [@1]

The Cité La Cure search was one of two Anti-Drug and Smuggling Unit operations
carried out on Tuesday at La Gaulette and Cité La Cure. [@1] ADSU stands for the
Anti-Drug and Smuggling Unit, the agency that conducted the Tuesday operations.
