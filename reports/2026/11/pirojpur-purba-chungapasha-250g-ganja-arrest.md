Pirojpur Sadar police arrested 52-year-old Ali Hossain Molla with 250 grams of
ganja, the local term for cannabis, while his 38-year-old wife Jesmin Begum
fled. [@1] The raid took place on Monday evening, 9 November 2026, in the Purba
Chungapasha area of Durgapur Union. [@1] Acting on secret information, Sadar
police raided Molla's house in Purba Chungapasha at about 6 pm. [@1] Both people
inside tried to flee on sensing the police, and officers detained Molla while a
woman escaped. [@1] Officers later identified the woman as his wife, Jesmin
Begum, from information Molla provided. [@1]

Police said they recovered the 250 grams of ganja from a shopping bag in Molla's
hand. [@1] They also seized 5,485 taka from his trouser pocket as proceeds from
drug sales. [@1] Police estimated the approximate market value of the seized
ganja at 7,500 taka. [@1]

The police first information report alleged that Molla and his wife had long
been involved in buying and selling drugs. [@1] Police filed a case against both
Molla and Begum under relevant sections of the Narcotics Control Act, 2018. [@1]
Pirojpur Sadar Police Station officer-in-charge Md. Shariful Islam confirmed the
incident on Tuesday and said Molla had been sent to court after completing legal
procedures in the case. [@1]
