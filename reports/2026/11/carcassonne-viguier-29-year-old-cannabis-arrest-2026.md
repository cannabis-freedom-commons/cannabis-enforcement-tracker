Police officers from the anti-crime brigade (BAC) in Carcassonne were watching
the drug-dealing point in the Viguier neighbourhood when they noticed a man
behaving suspiciously at about 5 pm on Tuesday 10 November 2026. [@1] They
stopped him for a check. [@1] The man, aged 29, was arrested and placed in
custody at the police station. [@1] His custody was extended. [@1]

The officers found that he was carrying a little cash. [@1] They also found
about 20 grams of cannabis resin and less than five grams of cannabis herb on
him, roughly 25 grams of cannabis altogether. [@1] Nearly five grams of cocaine
were found as well. [@1] The cocaine is weighed separately from the cannabis.
[@1]

The Viguier is a neighbourhood of Carcassonne with a street-level drug-dealing
point that the BAC was monitoring. [@1] The suspect is identified only by his
age, 29; no name was given. [@1] No charge or court date was reported, and the
case was still at the custody stage. [@1]
