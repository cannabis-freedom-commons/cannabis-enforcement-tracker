Mulavukad police detained Sharath, aged 28 and described as a native of Fort
Vypin Panikkarupadi, at the Panikkarupadi bus stop in Vypin. [@1] Officers had
seen Sharath in suspicious circumstances at the Panikkarupadi bus stop on the
previous day before detaining him and conducting a body search. [@1] The body
search led officers to find ganja in the possession of the detained youth. [@1]

The quantity found in his possession during that search was two grams of ganja.
[@1] Following the seizure, police registered a case against the youth in
connection with the possession of the two grams. [@1] After registering the
case, police released the youth on bail. [@1]

Ganja is the local term for cannabis seized from the youth at the bus stand.
[@1] The enforcement action thus combined detention at a public bus stop, a body
search, a two-gram seizure, registration of a case and release on bail. [@1]
Panikkarupadi is in the Vypin area where Mulavukad police carried out the
detention and search. [@1]
