Civil Lines Police in Rewa arrested a habitual offender for allegedly selling
illegal ganja. [@1] The accused was caught while waiting for customers near
Padmadhar Colony. [@1] The accused was identified as Lalu Patel, also known as
Ashish. [@1] He is originally from Chhiboura in Rampur Baghelan and was living
in Dhekaha in Rewa. [@1]

Police said they received a tip-off that a suspicious person was standing in
front of Rajput Gun Factory near the Padmadhar Colony crossing waiting to sell a
ganja consignment. [@1] A police team reached the spot and surrounded the area,
and caught the accused after he tried to escape on seeing the police. [@1]
During the search, police recovered a packet of ganja from his possession and
seized it. [@1]

Police said more than a dozen criminal cases are registered against him at
different police stations and he had earlier been externed from the district.
[@1] Civil Lines Police registered a fresh case against him under the NDPS Act.
[@1] Police were preparing to produce him before the court and investigate the
source of the seized ganja and the people he planned to supply. [@1]
