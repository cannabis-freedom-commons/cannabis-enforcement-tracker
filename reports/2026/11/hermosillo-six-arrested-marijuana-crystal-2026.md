Municipal police in Hermosillo detained six people in separate operations across
the city on Monday 9 November 2026 for suspected possession of crystal and
marijuana. [@1] At 07:33 on Oaxaca Street between Garmendia and Guerrero in
colonia Centro, officers detained 21-year-old Rodolfo Emanuel “N” carrying four
wrappers of green dry herb resembling marijuana. [@1] At 09:00 on Baricua and
Colosio streets in colonia La Manga, officers detained Manuel “N”, aged 38, and
Ramón Anselmo “N”, aged 31, with four units of suspected marijuana. [@1]

At 08:30 on Lázaro Cárdenas and Mineral El Batamote streets in colonia El
Apache, officers detained 27-year-old Hugo César “N” after he discarded five
doses of possible crystal. [@1] At 10:30 on Santa Rosa and Américo Vespucio
streets in colonia Solidaridad, officers detained 28-year-old Leonel Rolando “N”
after he discarded five wrappers with a granulated substance resembling crystal.
[@1] At 16:00 on Paseo Basalto and Agustín Zamora in colonia Paseos del
Pedregal, officers detained 37-year-old Aldo “N” after he threw away five units
of possible crystal. [@1]

The report headlines the operation as 24 wrappers seized in all. [@1]
