Four persons were arrested in the Manawar police station area of Dhar district,
where nearly 6,700 kg of cannabis plants valued at Rs 25 crore was detected.
[@1,2,3,5,6] Two persons were arrested in Khandwa district after police seized 295.70 kg
of cannabis plants worth Rs 29.57 lakh from two locations under Jawar police
station. [@1,2,3,5,6] Police also reported smaller seizures from Bag, Dharampuri,
Gandhwani, Mandav, Badnawar, Dahi and Kukshi police stations, with several
suspects arrested under the NDPS Act. [@1,2] Overall, Madhya Pradesh Police seized
and identified 11,669 kilograms of cannabis plants valued at more than Rs 30.10
crore across the four districts over the past four days. [@1,2,3,4,6,7]

Under Operation Prahar led by SP Sachin Sharma, police in Dhar seized or
identified 8,577.01 kg of cannabis plants worth over Rs 27.47 crore. [@1,2,3,4,5,6] In
Khargone, teams working under SP Ravindra Verma seized 2,367.85 kg of cannabis
plants worth Rs 1.91 crore across five police station jurisdictions. [@1,2,3,4,5,6,7] The
Khargone seizures included remote villages under Helapadava outpost and areas of
Maheshwar, Gogawan, Balakwada and Bistan. [@1,2] In Barwani, police recovered
428.84 kg of cannabis plants, or 1,753 plants, valued at nearly Rs 42 lakh from
Borpadwa and Kajalmata villages under Operation Green Strike. [@1,2,3,6,7] Dhar district
accounted for the largest haul. [@1,2]

The drive forms part of the state’s sustained campaign for a drug-free Madhya
Pradesh. [@1,2] Police teams used drone surveillance, informant networks and
intensive searches to reach remote riverbanks, hills, forests and areas without
mobile coverage. [@1,2,3,4] In several places, the plants were found hidden among
cotton and pigeon pea (tur) crops. [@1,2,3] Officials said the campaign goes beyond
seizures and aims to dismantle networks involved in illegal cultivation,
production and trafficking. [@1] Police urged citizens to report information
about narcotic cultivation or trade in their areas and said operations will be
further intensified to curb the drug menace. [@1]

At Bag's Dehri outpost in Dhar, police recovered 406.23 kg of cannabis plants valued at Rs 60.93 lakh. [@3,7] In Dharampuri's Surani and Soliyapura villages, police seized 225 green cannabis plants weighing 389.29 kg and valued at Rs 46,71,480. [@3] In Mandav police seized 265 kg of cannabis plants valued at Rs 39.75 lakh and arrested one accused. [@3] In Khargone's Maheshwar, Gogawan, Balkwada and Bistan areas police seized 444.60 kg, 92.40 kg, 102.250 kg and 1,269 kg of cannabis plants respectively. [@3,7]

In Dhar's Gandhwani police station area, police recovered 371.25 kg of cannabis plants valued at Rs 44.55 lakh. [@4] At Tilgaripada Borda in the Badnawar police station area, police seized 177 green cannabis plants weighing 228.60 kg and valued at Rs 22.86 lakh, grown among cotton and pigeon pea crops. [@4,7] At Temaria village in the Dahi police station area, 59 green cannabis plants weighing 138.49 kg and valued at Rs 20.70 lakh were found beside a drain among a cotton crop. [@4] At Nimbole in the Kukshi police station area, police seized 120 green cannabis plants weighing 78.15 kg and valued at Rs 11.72 lakh, grown among cotton and pigeon pea. [@4,7]

In the Dahi police station area's Temaria village, police arrested the man accused of growing the 59 cannabis plants found beside a drain among a cotton crop and registered a case under the NDPS Act. [@5] Police arrested two accused in two NDPS Act cases in the Badnawar police station area. [@5]
