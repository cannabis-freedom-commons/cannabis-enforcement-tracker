Hanoi police said officers of Co Do commune had detected and detained two people
over alleged illegal narcotics trading. [@1] Police found Tran Tuan Anh, born in
1999 and living in Hoang Liet ward in Hanoi, acting suspiciously at Mo
crossroads in Mai Trai village, Co Do commune. [@1] An administrative check
found messages between Tuan Anh and a Telegram account named Lao Gia linked to
alleged illegal narcotics trading. [@1] Tuan Anh admitted that he was packing
and selling cannabis on that account's instructions. [@1] Extended investigation
identified the holder of the Lao Gia account as Ngo Duy Anh Dung, born in 2002
and residing in Tien Du ward, Bac Ninh province. [@1] Dung admitted acting
together with Tuan Anh in the alleged illegal narcotics trading. [@1] Hanoi's
criminal investigation agency criminally detained both men to investigate them
for illegal trading of narcotics under Article 251 of the Penal Code. [@1]

An emergency search of Tuan Anh's home seized 57.412 grams of cannabis along
with two drug-tainted electronic scales, a vacuum sealer, plastic bags and a
mobile phone. [@1]

Police presented the case as a result of a year-end peak campaign against drug
crime in late 2026. [@1]
