Military police from the 37th Military Police Battalion arrested a 35-year-old
man on suspicion of drug trafficking in Erval Seco on the night of Friday 6
November 2026. [@1] Officers found about 27 grams of marijuana with him. [@1]

The same search turned up two precision scales, 2,820 reais in cash and a mobile
phone alongside the marijuana. [@1] Police also seized cocaine, some of it in 30
bucha portions ready for sale and some as three larger portions totalling 25.1
grams, none of which is counted as cannabis. [@1]

The man was taken to the police station, where officers registered the arrest in
flagrante, and then to the Frederico Westphalen State Prison. [@1] A second
account, by Jornal O Alto Uruguai, gives the same date and the same battalion,
citing the 37th BPM. [@2]

The 37th Battalion's release describes the marijuana as a single portion of
about 27 grams. [@2]
