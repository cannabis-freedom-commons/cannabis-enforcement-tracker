Railway Protection Force officers arrested a 19-year-old West Bengal native at
Thrissur railway station on Friday, 6 November 2026, and seized about 6 kg of
ganja from him. [@1] The young man is 19 and comes from Kashinagar in
Murshidabad district, West Bengal. [@2] He alighted from train number 12660, the
Gurudev Superfast Express, on platform 1 at about 1.58 p.m. on 6 November. [@2]
A search of his shoulder bag found 5.966 kg of ganja hidden in six packets
wrapped in cellotape. [@2] Officials put the value of the seized ganja at ₹3
lakh. [@1] He had boarded the train at Bhubaneswar in Odisha with a general
ticket. [@1]

The seizure was made by the RPF Crime Intelligence Branch, Thiruvananthapuram,
during a special inspection to curb drug trafficking through trains. [@1] During
questioning the man said he had been told to bring the ganja from Bengal and
deliver it in Thrissur in exchange for ₹20,000. [@2] The Hindu reported that he
said he had been instructed to hand the bag to someone at Aluva, in Ernakulam
district, for a ₹20,000 commission. [@1] The operation was carried out under RPF
Thiruvananthapuram Senior Divisional Security Commissioner Mohammed Haneefa and
Crime Intelligence Branch Inspector Jipin A.J. [@1] Deshabhimani named the team
that stopped him as Thrissur railway station station house officer T.D. Bijan
with assistant sub-inspector Murali and civil police officer Rajesh. [@2]

The accused and the seized ganja were handed to the Railway Police in Thrissur
for further legal action, and a case was registered under the Narcotic Drugs and
Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act, India's drug law. [@1] The accused and the
seized material were produced before a court. [@2] The force said inspections
would be intensified for the Deepavali season. [@1]
