Athmallik police in Odisha's Angul district stopped a pick-up vehicle in night
patrolling and vehicle checking on 5 November 2026 and seized 134.400 kg of
contraband ganja from it. [@3] One person was apprehended and police seized the
vehicle along with other articles, the district police said. [@3] The police
account says the interception was near Dhalpur Bridge in Angul district and that
the ganja was being carried in six sacks in a Bolero. [@2]

The arrested man is Jagan Pradhan, a resident of Veeranarasinghpur village in
Boudh district, according to the Prameya report. [@2] Prameya reported that the
ganja was being taken from Boudh towards Rairakhol when police intercepted the
vehicle. [@2]

The district police said Athmallik police station case 378/2026 was registered
under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, 1985, and that further
investigation was under way. [@3] Prameya reported that police registered a case
over the seizure and sent the accused to court for further legal proceedings.
[@2] The story reached readers through the Prameya English feed on Dailyhunt,
which published it with the same account of the seizure. [@1]
