Two men were arrested on Tuesday 10 November 2026 during an intervention by the
Puerto Rico Police Division of Drugs, Narcotics, Vice Control and Illegal
Weapons in the Antonio Roig housing estate in Humacao. [@1] Police seized 17
bags of marijuana, 30 bags of cocaine, 97 bags of crack and $67 in cash during
the operation. [@1] The two men arrested were 51 and 57 years old and were
summoned for Thursday 12 November 2026 for the possible filing of charges. [@1]
El Vocero reported that the operation was a surveillance and intervention plan
by Puerto Rico Police in the Antonio Roig housing estate. [@2] Police named the
men as Richard Olmeda Berríos, 51, of Humacao, and Radamés Sánchez Rivera, 57,
of Las Piedras. [@2]
