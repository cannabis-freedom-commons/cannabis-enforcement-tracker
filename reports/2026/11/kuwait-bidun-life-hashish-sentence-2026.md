Kuwait's Criminal Court sentenced a Bidun defendant to life imprisonment for
drug trafficking after drug-control officers caught him in an ambush with
quantities of hashish and psychotropic substances in his possession. [@1] The
verdict was delivered by a panel headed by Counsellor Nasser Al-Badr, with
judges Abdulaziz Al-Maadadi and Abdulhadi Al-Hajri and clerk Yaqoub Al-Ballam.
[@1] The defendant was identified only as Bidun. [@1]

Prosecutors charged that he possessed a narcotic substance, cannabis resin
(hashish), for both personal use and trafficking. [@1] They further charged that
he possessed two psychotropic substances, amphetamine and pregabalin, for use
and trafficking. [@1] They also accused him of consuming the psychotropic
substance tetrahydrocannabinol. [@1] The cannabis resin is the cannabis
component recorded here, while the amphetamine, pregabalin and
tetrahydrocannabinol are left out of cannabis quantities as non-cannabis
psychotropics. [@1]

The court confirmed that the arrest and search conducted by detectives were
valid, rejecting the defence claim that those procedures were invalid. [@1]
