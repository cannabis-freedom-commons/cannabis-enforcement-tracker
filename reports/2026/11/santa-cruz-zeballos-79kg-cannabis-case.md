Bolivian police arrested former Santa Cruz departmental court president Aldo Quezada on 11 November 2026 after searching his home. [@7,12,13] Prosecutors were investigating alleged drug trafficking, criminal association and conspiracy in the case of more than 30 suitcases that arrived at Viru Viru airport from the United States on 29 November 2025. [@7,8,9,12,21] Former deputy Laura Rojas collected 31 pieces of luggage the following day. [@9,21,22] Accounts differ on the airport the pilot was allegedly asked to divert to and whether the flight originated in Miami or California. [@9,14,15,18,21,22]

## Warehouse seizure and detention

Police searched a warehouse linked to former judge Hebert Zeballos on 20 January 2026 and reported seizing 79 kg of cannabis. [@2] Interior Minister Marco Antonio Oviedo put the seizure at 70 kg, Visión 360 reported 79.65 kg in 38 packages and EL PAÍS reported 80 kg. [@1,7,21] Accounts describe processing equipment at the warehouse, but authorities did not report recovering the suitcases there. [@1,7,21,23,24] A court ordered Zeballos held for 120 days in January, and he was moved to house arrest in August after roughly eight months in preventive detention. [@2,3,5,8] On 31 October a court revoked his house arrest after he was detained at a clinic, and he returned to Palmasola prison. [@4,5,6] Zeballos blamed missed check-ins on faults in the electronic monitoring system and denied deliberately breaching the order. [@4]

## Statements about the suitcases

On 5 November Rojas told prosecutors the luggage belonged to Rómer Saucedo and said she did not know what it held. [@8,9,10,21] Zeballos said it held California marijuana and alleged that then attorney general Roger Mariaca had protected the shipment. [@8,11,25,26] Oviedo described the marijuana as altered and said its eventual destination was unknown, though it could have fetched a high price in Brazil. [@21,22,23,24,27,28,29] Quezada said Saucedo had told him in Mexico that he owned 32 suitcases containing modified marijuana. [@7,14,15,16,18,19,20] Quezada also alleged a plan to process the cannabis for vape products and sell it in Brazil and other markets. [@7,14,15,17,19,20]
