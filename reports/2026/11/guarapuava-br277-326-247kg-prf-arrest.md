Federal Highway Police (PRF) officers detained the driver of a car with 247.6
kilograms of cannabis in the afternoon of Sunday 8 November 2026 at kilometre
326 of the BR-277, in Guarapuava, in central Paraná. [@1] The driver was a
40-year-old man. [@2]

Officers approached the vehicle at a restaurant beside the highway. [@1]
According to the PRF, the driver became nervous as the officers approached and
first denied carrying the drug. [@1] He then admitted he was carrying the load
and said he would be paid to take it from Foz do Iguaçu to Curitiba. [@1] The
SOT account says he would receive a sum of money for the job. [@2]

The man was taken with the drugs and the vehicle to the Civil Police station in
Guarapuava. [@1] The SOT account names that station as the Delegacia da Polícia
Civil de Guarapuava, where the usual procedures were carried out. [@2] The SOT
report credits its information to the PRF and its photograph as Divulgação, the
outlet's label for agency handout material. [@2]
