A 26-year-old man identified as Dinesh stood with a knife near the Chengalpattu
bus stand in Chennai, within the area covered by the Chengalpattu police
station, and attacked two people waiting at the stand, leaving them injured.
[@1] Hearing their screams, a sub-inspector from the nearby police station ran
over and tried to overpower the man. [@1]

The man, described as heavily intoxicated, turned on the officer and lunged at
him with the weapon. [@1] The sub-inspector pulled off his belt and swung it to
fend off the attack, escaping harm. [@1] More police reached the scene and,
after a struggle, surrounded the man, restrained him and took him to the police
station. [@1]

The article presents the attack as evidence of how widely ganja and other drugs
are sold on the state's streets. [@1] The column argues that the state
government should act under tough legal provisions against drug gangs and stop
Tamil Nadu from becoming a haven for them. [@1]
