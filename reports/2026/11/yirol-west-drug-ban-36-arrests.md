Yirol West County commissioner Paul Chabiet Ayang has banned the sale and
consumption of illicit drugs, including marijuana, in Yirol town in Lakes State
to address rising drug abuse. [@1] Ayang announced the ban as part of efforts to
tackle drug abuse and alcohol consumption, particularly among young people. [@1]
He said the decision followed concerns raised by health officials about the
harmful effects of drug abuse. [@1] He also linked the substances to violence,
theft and public disorder in the town. [@1]

On Thursday, Ayang led an operation to inspect shops and confiscate prohibited
substances, including crystal methamphetamine locally known as ice, flash,
tramadol, shisha and marijuana. [@1] Ayang ruled out compensation for traders
whose goods were confiscated and destroyed, saying the county government would
not compensate anyone for selling substances considered harmful to the
community. [@1]

The commissioner said 36 young people had so far been arrested over alleged
involvement in drug-related activities. [@1] Some of those arrested are
reportedly below the age of 18. [@1] He defended the detention of minors, saying
the authorities were seeking to protect them from further harm. [@1]
