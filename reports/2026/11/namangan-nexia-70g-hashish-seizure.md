In Namangan region, law-enforcement officers discovered a packet containing
drugs with the driver of a Nexia car, with laboratory examination later
confirming the substance as hashish. [@1] The operation was carried out by
officers of the Namangan regional Interior Affairs Directorate together with
officers of the State Security Service. [@1] The measure was presented as part
of work against illegal trafficking in drugs and psychotropic substances and to
identify people involved in their distribution. [@1]

During the inspection of the driver, officers found a plastic bag holding a
dark-coloured substance with a sharp smell. [@1] Officers sent what had been
seized for forensic examination. [@1] According to the conclusion of the
forensic centre, the substance was found to contain hashish, and its total
weight was 70 grams. [@1]

The investigative unit opened a criminal case under the article establishing
liability for illegal drug trafficking. [@1] The investigation was still under
way. [@1] Police added that tackling illegal drug trafficking requires not only
the work of specialist services but also participation by citizens, and they
asked residents not to remain indifferent to suspicious situations and to report
possible violations. [@1]
