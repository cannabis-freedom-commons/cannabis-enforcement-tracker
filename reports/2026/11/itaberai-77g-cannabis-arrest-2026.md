A Military Police CPU patrol team located and stopped a suspected drug dealer
while patrolling the Itaberaí region. [@1] During the stop, he allegedly
confirmed that he sold drugs and gave the officers his home address. [@1] With
the stopped man's father's authorization, officers searched the suspect's
bedroom. [@1] The man was arrested in flagrante, in tese, for drug trafficking
under Article 33 of Law No. 11.343/2006. [@1] He was taken to the Itaberaí
Municipal Hospital for medical evaluation and then presented to the on-duty
authority at the Central de Flagrantes with all the seized material. [@1]

In the bedroom, officers found about 56 grams of a substance described as
analogous to maconha. [@1] They also found 10 portions of greenish material
identified as analogous to skank, totalling about 21 grams, bringing the
separately stated cannabis-type total to about 77 grams. [@1] Officers also
seized 10 portions of a cocaine-like substance, plus a precision scale and a
mobile telephone. [@1] The cocaine portions are not counted in the cannabis
weight above. [@1]

The account attributes its information to the Goiás Military Police. [@1] The
outlet labels the case as Itaberaí and published it on 01/11/2026 at 18:48. [@1]
