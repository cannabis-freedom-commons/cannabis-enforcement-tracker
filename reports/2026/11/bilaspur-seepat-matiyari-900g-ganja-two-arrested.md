Seepat police and the Anti Crime and Cyber Unit arrested two men near the Baba
Dham Shiva temple in Matiyari village and seized a total of 900 grams of ganja,
which police valued at 45,000 rupees. [@1]

Police said on 12 November an informer told them a man was near the Baba Dham
Shiva temple in Matiyari, with ganja in a white plastic bag ready to sell; a
joint Seepat police and ACCU team raided the spot. [@1] Police arrested Kavi
Shikari, 32, of Matiyari, and Bhakoli Vanjkar, 52, of Bandhavpara Imlibhata
under the Sarkanda police station. [@1]

A search recovered 500 grams of ganja in a white plastic bag from Kavi Shikari
and 400 grams in a blue plastic cover from Bhakoli Vanjkar; police seized the
900 grams in total. [@1] Police registered a case against both under section
20(B) of the NDPS Act and sent them to jail on judicial remand. [@1]

The action was carried out at the order of senior police commissioner Rajnish
Singh, as part of a continuing drive against the illegal dry-drugs trade in the
district. [@2]
