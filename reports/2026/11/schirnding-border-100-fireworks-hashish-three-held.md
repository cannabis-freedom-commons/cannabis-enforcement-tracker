Federal police officers from the Selb border police inspectorate stopped three
young men bringing drugs, weapons and illegal fireworks from the Czech Republic
into Germany at the Schirnding border crossing. [@2] The two 19-year-olds and a
17-year-old now face proceedings under the weapons act, the explosives act, the
narcotics act and the law on handling consumer cannabis. [@1]

Officers checked the trio at about 15:00 on Saturday, 7 November, after they
entered from the Czech Republic. [@1] The search found hashish, which the
officers seized along with the other prohibited items. [@1] The officers also
found an electroshock device, a telescopic baton, two flick knives and two
knuckledusters. [@2]

Police seized the fireworks and the prohibited objects, and the suspects must
also pay the cost of destroying the pyrotechnics. [@1] A rapid drug test on the
driver, who comes from the Miltenberg district, was positive for THC, and a
complaint was filed against him for driving under the influence of cannabis.
[@2] The police statement was released on Monday. [@1]
