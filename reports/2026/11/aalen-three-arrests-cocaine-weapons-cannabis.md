Criminal police officers in the Ostalbkreis arrested three suspected drug
dealers on Thursday, 5 November 2026, with the support of special units. [@2]
The three taken into preliminary custody were a 23-year-old Albanian national, a
27-year-old German-Russian national and a 20-year-old Albanian national. [@2]
They are held on suspicion of unauthorised trafficking in narcotics in a not
insignificant quantity. [@1] On Friday they were brought before a remand judge
at the request of the Ellwangen public prosecutor's office, who issued the
arrest warrants, and they were then taken to prisons. [@2]

Officers searched several buildings and rooms in Aalen, Böbingen an der Rems,
Mögglingen and Schwäbisch Gmünd, all in the Ostalbkreis, and in Kirchheim unter
Teck in the Esslingen district. [@2] The searches turned up about 1.5 kilograms
of cocaine, cannabis and a five-figure sum of cash in euros. [@2] Officers also
confiscated various stabbing and blank-firing weapons, ammunition and further
evidence. [@2]

The first indications of the alleged crimes had emerged in October 2025, and the
suspicion hardened during the investigation. [@1]

The Stuttgart regional council area, where the searched towns lie, covers the
Ostalbkreis and the Esslingen district. [@3]

The investigation by the Aalen criminal investigation department and the public
prosecutor's office in Ellwangen, the authority responsible for the Ostalbkreis,
is continuing. [@2]
