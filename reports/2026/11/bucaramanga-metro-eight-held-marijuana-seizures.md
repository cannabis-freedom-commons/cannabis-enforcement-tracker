The Metropolitan Police of Bucaramanga detained eight people in six procedures
in Bucaramanga, Floridablanca and Girón. [@1] Officers of the GOES, the police
Special Operations Group, captured a 21-year-old man in Villalcázar in La
Cumbre, Floridablanca. [@1] Officers captured a 31-year-old man in La
Universidad in Bucaramanga. [@1] Officers captured a 22-year-old man at
kilometre 6 of the Bucaramanga-Girón road. [@1] Officers captured a man and a
woman on the Novena viaduct in Bucaramanga for receptación, receiving stolen
goods, and recovered a motorcycle reported stolen days earlier. [@1] Officers
captured another 22-year-old man in Aguas Claras in La Cumbre after finding a
revolver and three .38 cartridges in a bag without a carry permit. [@1] Officers
captured two men aged 33 in Bellavista Sector C in Floridablanca after the
community reported shots in an establishment open to the public. [@1] All eight
detainees were placed at the disposal of the competent authorities to answer for
the attributed offences. [@1]

In Villalcázar police found 210 artisanal cigarettes, 210 sealed bags of
portioned marijuana and another sealed bag of marijuana. [@2] In La Universidad
police found three sealed bags of marijuana and 300 artisanal cigarettes of the
substance. [@1] At kilometre 6 police found four sealed bags holding more than
two kilos of marijuana on a motorcycle passenger. [@1] Across the sweep police
reported seizures of marijuana, two firearms, ammunition and the recovered
stolen motorcycle. [@1]

Officers approached the Villalcázar suspect during patrol, registration and
control work after seeing him in a wooded area and searched him. [@2] In
Bellavista officers found no weapons on the men but found a .38 revolver with
five cartridges, four of them fired, hidden on a wall above the establishment
bathroom. [@1] One Bellavista detainee known as Robotina has entries in the
SPOA, the accusatory criminal records system, for theft, drug trafficking,
illegal weapons, injuries and domestic violence, which are not convictions. [@1]
The man held on the viaduct wore an Inpec, the prison authority, electronic
bracelet while serving house detention and was also held for prisoner escape
after a guard recognised the motorcycle as his. [@1]
