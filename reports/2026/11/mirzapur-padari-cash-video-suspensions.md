Padari police went to Devahi village to examine Mithu Prasad after receiving a
complaint accusing him of selling ganja, the Hindi word for cannabis. [@2]
Officers searched his shop and home and questioned him there. [@1] Police said
they found about two lakh rupees in cash during the check. [@1] One constable
counted the notes openly in front of everyone while another recorded the
counting on a mobile phone to avoid later claims that police had taken money.
[@1] Police then returned the full amount to Prasad and his family, a return
Prasad confirmed in a video statement. [@1]

A clip of the counting later circulated on social media with claims that Padari
officers had taken money from the alleged ganja smuggler. [@1,3] Superintendent of
Police Aparna Rajat Kaushik had the video examined and said it showed only the
counting of cash found during the raid. [@1] She suspended Padari constables
Himanku Mishra and Sudhir Kumar for making the video and sharing it
irresponsibly. [@1] Hindustan spelled the first constable's first name as
Himanshu. [@2]

She said the out-of-context circulation had tarnished the police image and
handed further inquiry to Additional Superintendent of Police City Yogendra
Singh. [@1] Police later summoned Prasad to the station for detailed
questioning. [@1] Amar Ujala reported that a trafficking first information
report, the police complaint that opens a criminal case, was then registered.
[@1] Hindustan instead reported that he was released after questioning at the
station. [@2] Hindustan dates the cash-counting video to 23 October. [@2]

The video was said to show bundles of 500-rupee notes. [@3]
