Nicaraguan Army troops detained 22-year-old Erling Manuel Cordoba Alfaro during
a fluvial control operation on the Rio Coco in the Plis community, Ayapal
microregion, Jinotega. [@1] He was travelling as a passenger aboard a pipante, a
small river boat, when military forces intercepted him on the river. [@1] The
detained man and the seized drug were handed over to the corresponding
authorities for judicial proceedings. [@1]

During the inspection the authorities seized from him a plastic bucket
containing five black bags of marijuana. [@1] The marijuana weighed 1,975.3
grams in total, just under two kilograms, according to the authorities. [@1] The
Army described the action as a fluvial control operation carried out by its
personnel on the waters of the Rio Coco. [@1]
