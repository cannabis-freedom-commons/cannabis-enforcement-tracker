Bhopal Crime Branch arrested 60-year-old Kiran Ahirwar in the Govindpura area on
Tuesday night on suspicion of ganja trafficking. [@1] She was sitting with a bag
near the Hanuman temple in front of Kasturba Hospital. [@1] Police said they had
received a tip that the bag contained ganja and that she was waiting for a
customer to sell it. [@1] After watching her from a distance and matching her to
the informer's description, the Crime Branch team cordoned off the spot and
caught her. [@1]

Searching the bag, officers found ganja wrapped in transparent plastic, weighing
10 kg 100 grams in total. [@1] Police put the value of the seized ganja at about
Rs 3 lakh. [@1] Police identified the woman as Kiran Ahirwar (60), a resident of
Kainchi Chhola in the Chhola Mandir police-station area. [@1] Police said she
works as a labourer and is illiterate. [@1]

Police registered a case against her under sections 8 and 20 of the NDPS Act.
[@1] Police said they were now tracing the ganja supply chain and other people
linked to the network. [@1]
