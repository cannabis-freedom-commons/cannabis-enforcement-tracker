Matsusaka Police Station arrested a 22-year-old Filipino beautician living in
Kubo-cho, Matsusaka, on 1 November on suspicion of possessing cannabis classed
as a narcotic under the drug-control law. [@1] Officers identified the suspect
in katakana as Taroi Beruni Marushisu. [@1] The arrest was an emergency arrest.
[@1] The suspect has admitted the allegation, telling investigators there was no
doubt he had possessed cannabis. [@2] The arresting unit was Matsusaka Police
Station of the Mie Prefectural Police. [@2]

Police allege that at about 11:10 p.m. on 31 October he possessed cannabis in
the parking lot of a convenience store in Okamoto-cho, Matsusaka. [@2] Officers
patrolling the city spotted the man in a parked car appearing concerned about
the patrol car. [@1] They questioned him and inspected the car, where they found
fragments resembling a cannabis plant. [@2] A forensic test confirmed the
fragments were cannabis, leading to the arrest. [@2]

Police say they are continuing to investigate the case, including the route by
which he obtained the cannabis. [@2]
