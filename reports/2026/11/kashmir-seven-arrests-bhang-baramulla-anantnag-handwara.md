Police in Baramulla intercepted a Sumo passenger vehicle, registration JK05N-9646, during a checkpoint operation at Cheradari in Jammu and Kashmir. [@1,2,3] The vehicle was travelling from Baramulla towards Chandoosa. [@1,2,3] During the check, officers found a bag carried by passenger Ubair Ahmad Mochi, a resident of Kalantara Bala, containing two polythene packets of a substance police described as bhang-like. [@1,2,3] Police put the total weight at approximately 822 grams. [@1,2,3]

The police called a magistrate and independent witnesses to the checkpoint and said the seizure proceedings were completed in accordance with the prescribed procedure. [@1,2,3] They arrested Mochi and said the investigation was continuing. [@1,2,3] The stop was reported on 7 November alongside separate arrests in Anantnag and Handwara, which involved different suspects and seizures. [@1,2,3]
