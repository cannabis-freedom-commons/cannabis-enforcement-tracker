Three men were arrested over the beating of a youth who was attacked on the
allegation that he had given the excise department information about cannabis
sales. [@1] The man attacked was 46-year-old A K Muhammed Musthafa of Aakkool
House in Karikkulam, Pappinisseri West, Kannur district. [@1] Those arrested are
35-year-old Vijil of Vadi House in Narath Onapparamba, 36-year-old Saneem who
lives in the Kattampalli quarters, and 35-year-old Afsal of Mukkannan House in
Kattampalli. [@1] Sub-inspector T M Vipin made the arrests in an investigation
overseen by Valapattanam inspector P Vijesh. [@1]

The attack took place at 2 pm on 2 November. [@1] Vijil is an accused in several
narcotics cases and had most recently been caught with drugs by the excise
department. [@1] After three months in jail, Vijil came out and attacked
Muhammed Musthafa together with friends. [@1]

The three took Muhammed Musthafa to the house of a man named Rajesh at
Balankinar, where Vijil tied stones in cloth and struck his head and face,
injuring him. [@1] That same night at about 9.30 pm they entered Muhammed
Musthafa's house and assaulted him again, and the arrests were made in these
cases. [@1]
