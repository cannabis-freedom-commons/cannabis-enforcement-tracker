Majorganj police in Sitamarhi district arrested a smuggler near Balua Mor on
Thursday evening after acting on a tip and seized 1.156 kg of charas, a cannabis
resin. [@1] The arrested man was identified as Abhishek Kumar, also called
Suddu, a resident of Kuari Madan tola in the Majorganj police station area, and
a search recovered the 1.156 kg of charas and an unnumbered Honda bike from him.
[@1]

Police put the estimated value of the seized charas in the international market
at about 40 to 45 lakh rupees. [@1] Police registered a new case against him
under the NDPS Act, India's narcotics law, and sent him to jail in judicial
custody on Friday. [@1]

A second account says a police team led by the station in-charge raided Balua
village on 12 November and arrested Abhishek Kumar, also called Suddu, of Kuari
Madan tola. [@2] That account describes the seizure as 1,156 grams of a
charas-like substance. [@2] It records case number 434/26 for this charas
matter. [@2]
