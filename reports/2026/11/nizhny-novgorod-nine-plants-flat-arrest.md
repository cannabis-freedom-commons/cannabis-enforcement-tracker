Police in Nizhny Novgorod detained a 37-year-old man suspected of cultivating
drug-containing plants, the regional interior ministry's press service said.
[@1] He had grown nine cannabis plants in his flat, according to the regional
headquarters of the Interior Ministry. [@1]

During a search officers found specialist lamps, grow boxes, electronic scales,
a vacuum sealer and packaging material. [@1] They also found 142 grams of
ready-to-use drug in a glass jar. [@1]

A criminal case was opened and the man admitted his guilt. [@1] He faces a
maximum of 15 years in prison. [@1] The report was drawn from material by the
Nizhny Novgorod news agency Vremya N. [@1]
