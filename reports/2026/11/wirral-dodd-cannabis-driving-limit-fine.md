A Wirral man has admitted being in charge of a car while over the drug-driving
limit for cannabis. [@1] Kieran Dodd, 23, pleaded guilty to the offence at
Wirral Magistrates Court on Monday 9 November. [@1] Dodd, of Harvest Lane in
Moreton, entered his plea at the hearing. [@1] The offence relates to being in
charge of the vehicle, not driving it. [@1]

Magistrates heard that Dodd was in a grey Nissan on Eleanor Road in Bidston on 5
April 2026. [@1] He gave a reading of 5.8 micrograms per litre of
Delta-9-Tetrahydrocannabinol, known as Delta-9-THC, the cannabis compound the
test measures. [@1] The legal limit is 2 micrograms per litre. [@1] The reading
was almost three times that limit. [@1] The hearing came more than seven months
after the reading. [@1]

Magistrates fined Dodd £200. [@1] They also put 10 penalty points on his driving
licence. [@1] He will have to pay £85 in court costs and a victim surcharge of
£80. [@1] The penalties were imposed for the single drug-driving offence. [@1]
