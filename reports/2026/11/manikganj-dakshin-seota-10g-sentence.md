Officers on an anti-drug mobile-court drive detained a youth with ganja in
Manikganj. [@1] The convicted youth is 24-year-old Sadib Ahmed Sadman. [@1] He
is the son of the late Ikbal Ahmed of Paschim Bandutia in Manikganj Sadar, and
his mother is Ayesha Akter. [@1] A mobile court convicted him for consuming
drugs and for creating social disorder through that consumption. [@1] The court
sentenced him to three months of imprisonment without labour and fined him 100
taka. [@1]

The operation took place on Wednesday 4 November 2026 in the Dakshin Seota area
under Sadar police station, organised by the Manikganj district office of the
Department of Narcotics Control. [@1] Officers searched Sadman's own house
during the drive and recovered and seized 10 grams of ganja, the local term for
cannabis. [@1]

The district narcotics office presented the operation as another demonstration
of the administration's tough stance against drugs. [@1] Related observers
expect such operations to continue to build a drug-free society and curb the
spread of drugs. [@1]
