Six drug traffickers were sentenced to a combined 37 years in federal prison for
selling fentanyl, cocaine and methamphetamine in the Eastern Panhandle of West
Virginia. [@1] Kenneth Robert Eversole, 48, of Keyser, was sentenced to 168
months in federal prison for having more than 400 grams of fentanyl in Mineral
County. [@1] Brenton Daniel Berens, 38, was sentenced to 60 months and Shannon
Lynn Smith, 40, was sentenced to 60 months for selling fentanyl and cocaine in
Berkeley County. [@1] Berens and Smith, both of Martinsburg, maintained a
residence to store and package drugs as a distribution hub, where officers
seized nearly one pound of fentanyl. [@1] Malachi Brian Maurice Younger, 23, was
sentenced to 41 months for trafficking fentanyl and cocaine in Berkeley County,
selling in West Virginia and Maryland across state lines. [@1] Shawn Douglas
Kile, 29, of Keyser, was sentenced to 120 months for distributing 50 grams or
more of methamphetamine after selling a little more than 93 grams in Mineral
County. [@1]

Officers searching Eversole's duplex seized 150 pounds of marijuana alongside
nearly three pounds of fentanyl, almost two pounds of methamphetamine and a
small amount of cocaine. [@1] Officers also seized five firearms, cash, body
armor and drug paraphernalia with those drugs. [@1] Officers searching Younger's
vehicle during a traffic stop seized a quarter pound of marijuana alongside more
than half a pound of cocaine and more than 50 grams of fentanyl. [@1]

Criminal Division Chief Lara Omps-Botteicher prosecuted the cases for the
government. [@1] U.S. District Judge Gina M. Groh presided. [@1] The cases were
investigated by agencies including the Drug Enforcement Administration, the
Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms and Explosives and other Maryland and West
Virginia task forces and police departments. [@2] The prosecution advances the
office's Four Corners of Public Safety priority of stopping illegal drugs
flowing into the Northern District of West Virginia. [@1]
