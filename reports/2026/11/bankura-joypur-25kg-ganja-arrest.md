Acting on a tip-off about ganja being illegally stored and traded, Jaipur police
raided the Jaipur Hattala area of Bankura district on Sunday night. [@2] Police
arrested Devashish De, a resident of Jaipur Hattala, on allegations of illegally
stocking and selling ganja. [@1] A second account likewise says police arrested
a man named Devashish De for allegedly stocking and selling ganja illegally.
[@2] Police took action against him under the NDPS Act. [@1] He was produced in
the Bankura court on Monday. [@1]

Police recovered about two and a half kilograms of illegal ganja from him. [@1]
The second account says more than 2 kg of ganja was recovered from the
detainee's house. [@2] Police are now investigating where he sourced the
consignment, where he sold it and who else is involved in the trade. [@1]

The report presents the arrest as part of a continuing Bankura police crackdown
on drug traffickers. [@1] It notes the chief minister's directive to officials
to follow a complete 'zero tolerance' policy in rape and narcotics cases, after
which police across the state took an aggressive stance against illegal drugs.
[@1]
