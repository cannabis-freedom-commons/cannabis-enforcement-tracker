Sohag Criminal Court sentenced a 60-year-old farm worker to life imprisonment
for drug trafficking and unlicensed possession of a firearm and ammunition in
Al-Asirat district. [@1] The defendant was identified only as H.M., a
60-year-old agricultural worker. [@1] The court passed the life term on 3
November 2026 after examining the case file referred by the prosecution. [@1]

Prosecutors charged him with possessing methamphetamine, hashish and tramadol
for trafficking outside the cases permitted by law. [@1] The narcotics charge
also covered an unlicensed rifled automatic rifle and eight rounds for use in
that weapon. [@1] A third account confirms the rifle was an automatic weapon
held with eight rounds without a licence. [@2]

The case began in April 2026, when the Public Prosecution opened the
investigation and then referred the defendant to the Sohag Criminal Court. [@2]
The same account confirms the court convicted the 60-year-old farm worker and
imposed life imprisonment for trafficking and unlicensed weapons possession in
Al-Asirat. [@2]
