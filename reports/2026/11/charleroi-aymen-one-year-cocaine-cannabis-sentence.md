A 27-year-old Charleroi resident identified as Aymen had been sentenced in his
absence on 18 June to one year in prison and a 10,000-euro fine for possession
of cocaine and cannabis and sale of cocaine. [@1] On retrial after his
opposition, the Charleroi court confirmed that judgment on Friday 13 November
2026. [@1] The same default sentence is also reported as one year in prison for
possession of cocaine and cannabis and sale of cocaine, which the Charleroi
court did not overturn. [@2]

On 2 April in Charleroi, surveillance video captured a transaction between a man
on a scooter and a drug-addicted woman, after which officers arriving at the
scene arrested Aymen. [@1] A search of the suspect's base led to the seizure of
cannabis, with no amount given. [@1] The scooter transaction and the cannabis
seizure at the suspect's base are confirmed in the second account of the same
hearing. [@2]

At the retrial Aymen denied the sale and said he had only helped by giving one
euro to someone asking for money to eat. [@1] He added that had he sold cocaine
he would never have been released by police. [@1] He was not able to convince
the Charleroi court, and he is currently under an arrest warrant for cocaine
sale. [@2]
