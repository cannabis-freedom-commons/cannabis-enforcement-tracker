Madhupur thana police in Tangail district arrested three people and seized a
total of 8 kilograms of ganja in two separate anti-drug raids. [@1] The raids
were carried out on Saturday 7 November in the Motrabazar area of Chunia and
Aushnara unions. [@1]

The first raid was led by sub-inspector Rafiqul Islam on a tip-off, under the
overall direction of Madhupur police station officer-in-charge AKM Fazlul Haque,
and officers recovered 6 kilograms of ganja in Chunia. [@1] Atikur Rahman
Rippon, of Chunia Sarkarpara, was arrested in that raid. [@1] In the second
raid, led by sub-inspector Kamrul Islam and assistant sub-inspector Pintu
Talukder, officers arrested Anchar Ali, 40, and Joynal Abedin, 29, with 2
kilograms of ganja in the Motrabazar area of Aushnara union. [@1]

Two separate cases were filed at Madhupur police station over the two raids.
[@1] On the morning of Sunday 8 November the arrested men were sent to a court.
[@1] The officer-in-charge said police would continue such anti-drug operations.
