Police in Patras arrested three men within a few hours after street checks
uncovered quantities of narcotics. [@1] The three arrests took place in separate
incidents during the night and the early morning hours. [@1] All three arrests
took place in Patras rather than in a single raid on one premises. [@1]

Officers of the Patras Immediate Action Department and the OPKE unit of the
Patras crime-investigation subdivision made the arrests. [@1] The checks that
led to the arrests were carried out late in the previous evening and in the
early hours of the morning of 12 November 2026. [@1] During the checks officers
found and seized small quantities of cocaine and heroin alongside the cannabis.
[@1]

Officers also found and seized 3.4 grams of cannabis, the only cannabis weight
the report gives. [@1] No weights were published for the cocaine or the heroin,
which were described only as small quantities. [@1] Police formed separate case
files against each of the three arrested men for violation of drugs legislation.
[@1]
