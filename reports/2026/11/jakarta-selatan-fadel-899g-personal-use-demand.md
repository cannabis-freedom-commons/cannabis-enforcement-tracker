Prosecutor Cosman Oktaniel Girsang of the South Jakarta district prosecutor's
office asked the South Jakarta District Court on Tuesday 3 November 2026 to
sentence Fadel Muhamad, son of Pujiono, to two years in prison, less the time he
has spent in detention, for possessing cannabis. [@1] The prosecutor charged him
under article 127(1)(a) of the 2009 narcotics law, read with the 2026
criminal-sentencing alignment law, which covers people who use class I narcotics
themselves rather than sellers. [@1]

The cannabis found on him weighed 8.99 grams in total, a gross figure that
includes seeds; the prosecutor accepted it as personal consumption, which kept
the case away from the far heavier dealing and trafficking articles. [@1] The
evidence listed in court consisted of three brown paper packets holding a
combined 6.22 grams of cannabis, a large clear clip bag of 2.55 grams of
cannabis seeds, and a rolled paper with 0.22 grams of used cannabis. [@1] The
cannabis was hidden in an empty Sampoerna Mild Menthol cigarette pack, and
officers also took a black iPhone 12 with an XL Axiata SIM card. [@1]

The prosecutor asked the court to order all the cannabis destroyed and to make
Fadel pay Rp5,000 in court costs. [@1] The next hearing was set for the defence
plea, and no verdict had been given when the account was published. [@1]
