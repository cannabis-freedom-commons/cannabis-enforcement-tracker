Military police arrested two young men, aged 19 and 22, on suspicion of drug
trafficking during an operation on the night of Thursday 5 November 2026 on Rua
Joaquim Moreira in Bairro São José, Itacarambi, in the Norte de Minas region.
[@1]

According to the police, the operation followed anonymous complaints about drug
sales at a house in the neighbourhood, and officers who watched the address saw
a steady flow of users going in and out. [@2] When the patrol cars arrived, a
third suspect, a 28-year-old described in the complaints as the man running the
drug point, ran into the house, and officers heard people jumping walls into
neighbouring properties. [@1]

After the block was surrounded, the 19-year-old was caught outside the house
with 15 tubes of a cocaine-like substance, R$ 50 in cash and a key that police
said opened the padlock of the gate of the house named in the complaints. [@1]
The 22-year-old was found on the roof of a neighbouring house trying to escape,
with one bar and 25 bags of a marijuana-like substance, 21 tubes and nine
sachets of a cocaine-like substance, 32 crack rocks, a phone, a precision scale
and R$ 3. [@1]

The 28-year-old got away, and while searching nearby yards officers found a bag
he had left behind containing seven more bags of marijuana, 21 cocaine tubes, 17
crack rocks, two phones and a notebook the PM said held records of the drug
trade's accounts. [@1] Police also seized a Honda XRE motorcycle suspected of a
link to the trade, because the engine number showed signs of tampering, and sent
it for forensic examination. [@1] The two arrested young men were taken to the
Civil Police station together with the seized material for the arrest in
flagrante to be recorded. [@2]
