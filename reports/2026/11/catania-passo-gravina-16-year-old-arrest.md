State Police arrested a 16-year-old boy found with marijuana in Via Passo
Gravina in Catania. [@1,3,4,5] An off-duty officer from the Librino commissariat
noticed the boy in the evening while passing through the area. [@1,3,6,7] The boy,
dressed in black, was standing beside a small car with its engine running and
driven by a woman, which made the officer suspicious. [@1,6,7] The boy then climbed
over the gate of a house while the car drove away. [@1,6,7]

The officer identified himself and called for help from a Flying Squad patrol.
[@1,3,7] Despite a brief attempt to evade the check, the boy was stopped and found
with five small bags of marijuana weighing 40 grams in total. [@1,3,4,5,6,7] He was
arrested for possession of narcotics for the purpose of dealing. [@1,6,7]

On the order of the public prosecutor at the Catania Juvenile Court, he was
taken to the First Reception Centre. [@2,3,4,5,6,7] The second account states that the
presumption of innocence applies until a final conviction. [@2]
