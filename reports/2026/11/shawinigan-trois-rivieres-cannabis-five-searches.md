Quebec provincial police arrested a 37-year-old man and a 32-year-old woman
during a drug operation in Shawinigan on 5 November 2026. [@1] Both were
released afterwards and are due to appear at a later date at the Shawinigan
courthouse. [@1] They could face charges under the Cannabis Act, drug offences,
money laundering and offences under the Tobacco Tax Act. [@1]

Officers carried out five searches, at three addresses in Shawinigan and two
warehouses in Trois-Rivières. [@1] The searches yielded more than 100 kilograms
of edible cannabis, more than 64 kilograms of dried cannabis and more than 129
kilograms of concentrated cannabis. [@1] Police also seized more than 9
kilograms of hashish and more than 125 electronic cigarettes containing THC.
[@1] The same search also produced more than 126,000 methamphetamine tablets,
more than 280 grams of cocaine, more than 116,000 contraband cigarettes and more
than 113,000 prescription pills. [@1]

Police seized more than C$70,000 in Canadian cash, two conducted-energy weapons
and two vehicles as offence-related property. [@1] The operation was run by the
Sûreté du Québec's contraband investigations service, with officers from the
SQ's Shawinigan detachment and the Trois-Rivières police service. [@1]
Radio-Canada reported that the operation targeted smuggling of illicit cannabis.
[@2]

Zone911 and Radio-Canada reported that the investigation began after police
received information from the public. [@2] Zone911 reported the operation fell
under ACCES-Cannabis, a 2018 Quebec programme against cannabis contraband
coordinated by the public security ministry. [@3]

Mon La Tuque described the operation as a crackdown on illicit cannabis
contraband in Shawinigan. [@4]
