The Kharkiv Court of Appeal left in force the verdict against a former policeman
for drug sale. [@1] At the time of the offence the man worked as an inspector of
the training centre of the Main Directorate of the National Police in Kharkiv
Oblast. [@1] Officers detained the policeman and notified him of suspicion, and
he was afterwards dismissed from his post. [@1] The Kyiv District Court of
Kharkiv had found the former law-enforcement officer guilty of illegal drug
trafficking and imposed seven years of imprisonment with confiscation of
property. [@2]

At trial the accused did not admit guilt. [@1] He denied selling prohibited
substances and explained money found during his personal search as repayment of
a debt by a friend. [@1] The court examined the prosecution evidence and found
it sufficient to confirm the former officer's guilt. [@1] On prosecution by
Kharkiv Oblast Prosecutor's Office prosecutors, he was found guilty of repeated
illegal purchase, storage, transportation and sale of an especially dangerous
narcotic drug and psychotropic substance under Part 2 of Article 307 of the
Criminal Code of Ukraine. [@1] He was given seven years of imprisonment with
confiscation of property and was also stripped of the special rank of senior
police lieutenant. [@1] The defence appealed, seeking cancellation of the
verdict and a new trial, but the Kharkiv Court of Appeal refused the complaint
and left the first-instance decision unchanged. [@1]

He had bought cannabis and amphetamine, kept them at his residence and later
sold them. [@1] One dose cost from 1,500 to 2,500 hryvnias depending on the
substance. [@1] A search of his home found packaged amphetamine and cannabis,
LSD and MDMA pills, a homemade drug-use device and cash obtained through crime.
