Brian O'Connell, 51, of Pairc Ui Mhuirithe, Oakwood, Macroom, pleaded guilty at
Macroom District Court to possession of cannabis and cultivation of cannabis
plants. [@1] Gardaí, Ireland's police, searched his premises on the evening of
31 December 2025 and recovered cannabis valued at €240 along with two cannabis
plants. [@1] O'Connell admitted the drugs and plants were his and said they were
for personal use. [@1] The court heard he had 12 previous convictions, including
one for possession of drugs and one for cultivation. [@1]

Defence solicitor Sean Cahill said O'Connell grew the plants because he did not
want to buy drugs from “undesirable elements”. [@1] Cahill said there was no
suggestion that O'Connell was supplying drugs to others. [@1] Cahill said
O'Connell had five children aged between 13 and 29 and worked as a truck driver.
[@1] He said O'Connell had stopped using cannabis in January. [@1] The court
heard O'Connell had used cannabis in the past because of medical issues and only
smoked it in the evening. [@1]

Judge Joanne Carroll said it was a serious matter for a professional driver to
use cannabis. [@1] O'Connell said he had never had an accident and had only
smoked cannabis in the evenings. [@1] Judge Carroll said she needed to be
satisfied that, given his previous cultivation conviction, O'Connell was now
drug-free. [@1] She directed the Probation Service to prepare a report and
ordered two random drug tests. [@1] O'Connell was remanded on continuing bail to
appear again on 20 January 2027 for the report and test results. [@1]
