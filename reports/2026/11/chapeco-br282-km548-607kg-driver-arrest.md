A driver was arrested after trying to flee a police stop on the BR-282 highway
in Chapecó, in western Santa Catarina, on Saturday 7 November 2026. [@1]

Officers found 60.7 kilograms of cannabis hidden in the boot of a Renault Scenic
during the inspection. [@1] The driver tried to run away on foot but was caught
by the officers. [@1] He was arrested and taken with the drug and the car to the
Civil Police in Chapecó for legal procedures. [@1]

The stop was logged at about 12:20 at km 548 of the highway. [@1]
