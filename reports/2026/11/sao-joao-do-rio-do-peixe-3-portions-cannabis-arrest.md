Military Police responded on the night of Saturday at about 22h20 to a report of
drug possession in the square in front of Escola José Dantas Pinheiro in São
João do Rio do Peixe, Paraíba. [@1] Residents asked the RP 05 patrol to come
because a motorcycle with a modified exhaust was causing noise that disturbed
the neighbourhood. [@1] On arrival officers found a group of young people, among
them the owner of a black Honda Pop 100 with a modified exhaust. [@1] While
checking the motorcycle, officers found a quantity of a substance with
characteristics similar to marijuana. [@1]

The young man was taken with the seized material to the Civil Police station in
Cajazeiras and presented to the police authority for legal measures. [@1] Police
seized three portions of a marijuana-like substance and the motorcycle. [@1] The
occurrence report said the young man did not have a driver's licence (CNH, the
Brazilian national driving licence) and did not carry the vehicle's documents.
