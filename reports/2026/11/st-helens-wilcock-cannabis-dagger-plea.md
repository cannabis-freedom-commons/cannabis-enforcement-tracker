A man was due to be sentenced for offences including possessing a push dagger
and possession of cannabis. [@1] Daniel Wilcock, of Summerdale Close,
Ashton-in-Makerfield, was due to appear before Liverpool, Knowsley and St Helens
Magistrates’ Court on November 3. [@1] Wilcock, 31, pleaded guilty to five
charges during a previous hearing on September 22. [@1] The case was adjourned
until November 3 for a pre-sentence report to be prepared. [@1]

The court was due to decide the sentence at proceedings listed for Tuesday,
November 3. [@1] The five admitted charges arose from offences that happened on
August 25 this year in St Helens and Sefton. [@1] He was also accused of
possessing a quantity of cannabis, a Class B controlled drug, in St Helens. [@1]

A push dagger was found in his possession in a public place, according to the
charge. [@1] Court documents state he failed to provide a blood specimen for
analysis in Sefton. [@1] He also admitted driving a Fiat Panda on Kirkstone
Avenue without a licence and without third-party insurance. [@1]
