Saudi security agencies said a day of intensified operations against drug
smuggling and promotion in several regions ended with 31 people detained, among
them citizens, residents and violators of the border security law. [@1] The
operations seized hashish, amphetamine, methamphetamine, khat plant and more
than 143,000 tablets of narcotics and medicines under medical-trade controls.
[@1]

In Jeddah, patrols of the General Directorate of Mujahideen, a security force,
arrested five Ethiopian border-security violators accused of promoting hashish
and amphetamine as well as regulated tablets. [@1] Slaati, a Saudi news outlet,
reported the same Jeddah arrest of five Ethiopians held over promoting hashish,
amphetamine and regulated tablets, and said they were stopped and referred to
the competent authority. [@2]

In Jazan region, Border Guard land patrols in the Al-Aridah sector arrested
eight Yemeni violators accused of smuggling 150 kg of khat plant. [@1] In the
same region's Al-Dair sector, patrols arrested four Ethiopian violators accused
of smuggling 35 kg of hashish and 74,400 tablets under medical-trade controls.
[@1] Border Guard patrols in the Al-Raboah sector of Asir region arrested two
Yemeni violators accused of smuggling 40 kg of khat plant. [@1]

In Riyadh, two Pakistani residents were arrested for allegedly promoting 5 kg of
methamphetamine, a substance outside this report's cannabis figures. [@1] In
Al-Jawf region, two citizens were arrested for allegedly promoting hashish and
1,755 tablets under medical-trade controls. [@1] In Asir, two citizens were
detained over 7,461 tablets under medical-trade controls, and two other suspects
were arrested over 9,950 amphetamine tablets. [@1] A Yemeni resident in Jazan
region was arrested for allegedly promoting 50,280 tablets under medical-trade
controls. [@1]

In the Eastern Province, security agencies arrested three Ethiopian
border-security violators and one citizen accused of promoting 1.1 kg of
hashish. [@1] Legal measures were taken against those detained and they were
referred to the competent authorities, while initial procedures in the smuggling
cases were completed and the suspects and seizures handed over. [@1]
