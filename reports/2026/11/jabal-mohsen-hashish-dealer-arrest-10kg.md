Lebanese Army intelligence detained a suspected drug dealer in Jabal Mohsen
after mounting a carefully prepared ambush in the area. [@1] The operation
targeted one person suspected of involvement in promoting drugs, and officers
took him into custody at the scene. [@1] The detainee was held together with the
confiscated material so that the necessary legal measures could be taken against
him in accordance with due process. [@1]

Officers seized a quantity of hashish found in the suspect's possession,
estimated at more than 10 kilograms. [@1] The substance seized is identified in
the report as hashish, given in Arabic as hashisha. [@1] The published figure is
a lower bound of over 10 kilograms rather than a precise weighed total. [@1]

The report identifies the acting force as the Lebanese Army intelligence service
and places the ambush in the Jabal Mohsen locality. [@1] It describes the
suspect only in general terms as a person suspected of drug dealing and
publishes no name, age or other identifying details. [@1] No court charge, trial
