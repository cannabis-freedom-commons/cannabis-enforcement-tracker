Police in Tiruvannamalai arrested seven people, including three women, and were
continuing questioning over alleged ganja and intoxicant sales. [@1] More than
100 police officers led by SP (Superintendent of Police) Udayakumar joined
sudden surprise searches in areas suspected of storing ganja and intoxicants in
houses for sale. [@1] Officers used drone cameras to search Kalnagar, Samuthiram
Nagar, Manalurpet Road, Poomanthakulam and several other places in and around
Tiruvannamalai. [@1]

In houses and crematorium-ground hiding places, officers seized about 1,500
grams (1.5 kg) of ganja, the Tamil term for cannabis. [@1] Officers also seized
200 liquor bottles that had been stored for sale on the black market. [@1]

Police said the drone-assisted searches were part of work to control ganja and
intoxicant sales in Tiruvannamalai. [@1] The famous Karthigai Deepam festival
would soon be held in Tiruvannamalai, and security arrangements were being
intensified for it. [@1] Police were carrying out sudden checks in
Tiruvannamalai and surrounding areas while monitoring the activities of old
offenders. [@1]
