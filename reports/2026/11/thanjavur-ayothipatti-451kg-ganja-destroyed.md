Thanjavur range Deputy Inspector General Ziaul Haque and Trichy range Deputy
Inspector General M R Sibi Chakravarthi oversaw the court-ordered destruction of
seized ganja near Thanjavur on Tuesday. [@1] The stock destroyed near Thanjavur
on Tuesday totalled around 451kg of ganja seized in 232 cases registered across
nine districts. [@1] The destruction initiative was taken as part of the
‘Drug-Free Tamil Nadu’ campaign. [@1]

Officers incinerated the contraband at high temperature inside a private
facility at Ayothipatti near Sengipatti. [@1] The private facility at
Ayothipatti near Sengipatti was used as the high-temperature incineration site
for the seized stock. [@1] The two range officers acted on the directions of
central zone Inspector General of Police V Balakrishnan. [@1] Police carried out
the disposal following court orders for destruction of the seized ganja. [@1]
Other senior officers from the central zone were also present at the facility
while the contraband was being incinerated. [@1] A police photograph of the
operation shows officials destroying the seized cannabis in the incinerator on
Tuesday. [@1]
