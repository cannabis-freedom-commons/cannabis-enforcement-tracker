Carabineros officers searched the La Cruz municipal offices on the morning of 11
November 2026 on the instructions of the La Calera prosecutor's office. [@1,4]
Officers of Carabineros' OS-9 unit seized electronic equipment and documents
from the municipality, which were handed over voluntarily. [@1] The recording
captured mayor Filomena Navia Hevia of the Socialist Party reacting angrily
after a municipal employee casually discovered a marijuana plantation. [@1,4] The
employee, named by local media as Jorge Torres Cid, had found the plants on 21
January while flying a drone over the 28 de Mayo neighbourhood to locate water
wells as a fire-season reserve. [@1,4] He photographed several large plants
growing in a house yard and sent the pictures to his superior. [@1,4]

In the recording the mayor tells him to do nothing about the find, saying she
must protect herself because the PDI investigative police would seize the plants
with great fanfare and people would call her an informer, adding that pursuing
crime or a person with a cannabis plant was not her role. [@1] She later said
she had told the employee to do what fell to him under the administrative
statute, placing the duty to report on him rather than on his superiors. [@1]
Torres said he went back the next day to gather more images for a complaint, but
the plants were already gone. [@1] A second account dates the drone flight to 21
January over the 28 de Marzo neighbourhood. [@2] It says the employee found
three planters with plants he identified as marijuana and sent the photographs
directly to the mayor. [@2] The local outlet that first published the audio
places the well inspection in late February 2026. [@3]

An investigation was opened for failure to report an offence under the drug law.
[@1] The La Calera guarantee court set the hearing to formally charge the mayor
for 27 March 2027. [@1] The comptroller's office called the failure to report
inexcusable. [@1] On 2 November 2026 the Valparaíso electoral tribunal admitted
a removal petition against the mayor filed by three opposition councillors over
this case and other matters. [@1] The petition cites a comptroller report of 16
October 2026 finding no record of municipal steps to notify the authorities
until the case became public in August. [@2] It also notes that the prosecutor's
office opened its own investigation while a related complaint was declared
admissible on 12 August 2026. [@2]
