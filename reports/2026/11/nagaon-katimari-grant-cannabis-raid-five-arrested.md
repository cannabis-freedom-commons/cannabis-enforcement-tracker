Police from the Haibargaon police outpost conducted a raid at Katimari Grant, on
the outskirts of Nagaon town in Assam, on Wednesday afternoon. [@1] The account
presents the raid as part of a major crackdown on the illegal cannabis business.
[@1] Officers also seized what the account calls a huge quantity of cannabis
during the operation. [@1]

The arrested people were identified as Farijul Haque, Achinur, Moinul Haque,
Arun Bora and Manoj Bora. [@1] Acting on a specific tip-off, the police team
launched the operation at the residence of Farijul Haque at Katimari Grant
around noon on Wednesday. [@1] Police apprehended the five individuals during
the operation, and a thorough search of the house led to the recovery of a
substantial quantity of cannabis. [@1]

The account attributes the tip-off and timing details to sources rather than a
named police statement. [@1] The house that was searched was the residence of
Farijul Haque at Katimari Grant. [@1]
