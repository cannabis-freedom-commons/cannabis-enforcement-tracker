Dera police arrested 10 proclaimed offenders and three other suspects in
separate operations within the jurisdiction of Paharpur police station. [@1] The
10 proclaimed offenders were identified as Javed Akbar Kamrani, Nawaz Khan,
Ghazi Khan, Sanaullah, Sabir Khan, Muhammad Ali, Gul Zaman, Naseeruddin, Faheem
Abbas and Muhammad Ashfaq, who were wanted in various criminal cases. [@1] In
separate actions, police arrested Ahmad son of Gohar Rehman and Amir Khan alias
Faqiro son of Haider in connection with a case involving negligent and reckless
motorcycle riding. [@1] Another suspect, Muhammad Iqbal son of Hamad Hassan, was
also arrested. [@1]

Police said the operations recovered hashish and illegal weapons from the
suspects' possession. [@1] Police recovered 596 grams of hashish from Amir Khan.
[@1] From Muhammad Iqbal, police seized a 12-bore repeater gun, seven magazines
and 35 cartridges. [@1]

The operations were conducted under the supervision of SP Paharpur Iqbal Khan
Baloch and led by DSP Sharifullah Khan and SHO Cadet Zafar Abbas. [@1] DSP
Sharifullah Khan said action against criminal elements would continue without
discrimination. [@1]
