Michael Wilson, 45, was charged with possession with intent to supply cannabis.
[@1] The offences were committed in Warrington on 17 December 2024, prosecutor
Heather Bell told the court. [@1] Wilson also had snap bags to sell cannabis in
and scales to weigh the class B drug. [@1] He was sentenced at Warrington
Magistrates' Court, having earlier pleaded guilty. [@1] Wilson, of O'Leary
Street in Orford, received eight months in prison, suspended for the next 12
months. [@1]

Wilson admitted possessing a quantity of cannabis with the intention of
supplying it to people in the town. [@1] Magistrates found the custody threshold
had been crossed because of the severity of the offence. [@1] The custodial
sentence was suspended, taking into account his guilty plea and his 'realistic
prospect of rehabilitation'. [@1] The court noted his 'good record in terms of
no previous history of non-compliance with court orders' and that he was not
assessed as posing a risk to others. [@1]

He was ordered to pay a £160 fine and a £187 victim surcharge, with an
application approved for the funds to be deducted from his benefits. [@1] The
court ordered the forfeiture and destruction of the cannabis, snap bags and
scales seized by police during the investigation. [@1]
