The incident dates to Thursday 5 November 2026. [@1] A 59-year-old Italian man
employed in the nautical sector was arrested by Carabinieri of the Orbetello
radiomobile unit. [@1] He was driving along the Giannella road toward Porto
Santo Stefano when he noticed a patrol and reversed direction, apparently hoping
to avoid attracting attention. [@1] The Carabinieri noticed the U-turn, followed
him to a car park in the town, and stopped him and searched the car. [@1]

Hidden in the car boot were 200 hashish ovals weighing altogether more than two
kilograms. [@1] Officers also found a gram of cocaine in a door pocket and a
similar wrap in the driver’s trouser pocket. [@1] Extended searches of his
garage and flat turned up another 90 grams of cocaine in the garage, plus 10
grams of hashish and a precision scale in the flat. [@1] Investigators believe
he had invested about 18,000 euros in buying the drugs to resell them at retail,
including to acquaintances in Porto Santo Stefano. [@1] The alleged dealing was
meant to add a second income alongside his nautical work, through the margin
between the purchase price and the price charged to consumers. [@1]

The next day he appeared before judge Marco Mezzaluna for the validation
hearing, assisted by lawyer Giulio Parenti. [@1] On Friday 6 November the judge
validated the arrest and ordered pretrial detention in prison, where the man
remains. [@1] His lawyer will seek a review of the detention order. [@1]
Investigators have not yet established who supplied him or how wide his alleged
customer network was. [@1]
