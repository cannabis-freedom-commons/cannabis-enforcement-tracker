Police in Chimalhuacán, Estado de México, arrested two men for their probable
participation in acts with the appearance of crimes against health. [@1] The
intervention took place during the Estrategia Mando Unificado Zona Oriente, the
joint state and federal security strategy for the east of the state. [@1]

Officers of the two forces were on patrol in the municipal seat of Chimalhuacán
when they saw two men openly handling transparent bags of apparent green herb
with the characteristics of marijuana. [@1] The men were identified as José “N”,
aged 37, and Miguel “N”, aged 43, and were asked to undergo an inspection in
line with police protocols. [@1]

The inspection found eight resealable bags with the probable herb. [@1] Both men
were informed of the rights the law gives them and were taken and presented
before the Public Prosecutor's Office based in Nezahualcóyotl, which will
determine their legal situation. [@1]
