Windsor police arrested a 20-year-old man without incident on 3 November 2026
during a follow-up investigation into an earlier criminal offence. [@1] Officers
from the Emergency Services Unit and the Drugs and Guns Enforcement Unit found
and arrested the man on Tuesday. [@2] The suspect was charged with six counts of
possession of a Schedule I substance with intent to sell, one count of
possession of a Schedule IV substance with intent to sell, possession of
cannabis with intent to sell and possession of criminal proceeds over $5,000.
[@2] The charges have not been proven in court. [@2]

After the arrest, officers executed a search warrant at a home in the 3100 block
of Meadowbrook Lane. [@1] In the home officers seized 417.6 grams of cannabis.
[@1] Officers also seized 100 cannabis vape pens. [@1] The same search also
seized crystal methamphetamine, crack cocaine, fentanyl and cocaine. [@2]
Officers also found hydromorphone, oxycodone and clonazepam pills. [@2] Police
also collected packaging materials, scales and a cell phone. [@2] Officers also
seized about $11,780 in Canadian cash and $100 in U.S. cash. [@2] Police put the
value of all seized drugs at about $21,780 and the entire seizure including cash
at about $33,660. [@1]

Police shared no further details about the initial investigation that led to the
follow-up. [@1]
