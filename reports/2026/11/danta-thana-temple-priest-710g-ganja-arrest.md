Banaskantha Anti-Narcotics Wing officers arrested 57-year-old priest Niranjandas
VirMaharaj, who performed worship at the Dotaliya VirMaharaj temple in Thana
village in Danta, for allegedly keeping ganja in his room and selling it. [@1]
VirMaharaj is originally from Begusarai district in Bihar and was living in the
outskirts of Thana village. [@1] Police registered a case against him and began
legal proceedings. [@1]

The wing raided the priest's room in the temple on the outskirts of Thana
village and seized 710 grams of ganja valued by police at Rs 35,500. [@1]
Officers found crushed green leaves and twigs in a plastic box and in a black
bundle kept under a cooking pan. [@1] Forensic Science Laboratory officer P. D.
Rathvi's preliminary test was positive for the active components of cannabis,
and weighing confirmed 710 grams of ganja. [@1]

The Banaskantha Anti-Narcotics Wing team was on patrol in the Danta police
station area on Monday when it acted on a privately received tip. [@1] Acting on
that tip, police caught VirMaharaj allegedly storing and selling ganja from his
room. [@1]
