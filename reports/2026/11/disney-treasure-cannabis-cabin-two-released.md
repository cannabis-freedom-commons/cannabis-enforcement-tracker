Police in the British Virgin Islands were summoned to Tortola Pier Park after
authorities aboard the Disney Treasure cruise ship discovered cannabis in a
cabin occupied by two passengers. [@1]

The female passenger, a practising physician, said the cannabis had been
prescribed by her doctor and that she held a permit for its use issued by her
home state of Florida. [@1] She admitted that her partner had been smoking
cannabis inside their cabin. [@1] Police seized the remaining cannabis and other
items. [@1]

Both passengers were warned that possessing cannabis without lawful
authorisation could result in criminal charges. [@1] BVI News understands that
the two passengers were later released without charges. [@1]
