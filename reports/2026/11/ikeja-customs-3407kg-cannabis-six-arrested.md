Nigeria Customs Service Federal Operations Unit Zone 'A' arrested six suspects
and intercepted prohibited goods including cannabis, cocaine and methamphetamine
over four weeks. [@1,3] Acting Comptroller Oladapo Afeni announced the results on
Tuesday at his maiden press briefing in Ikeja, Lagos, saying 195 interception
operations produced the seizures. [@1,3] Afeni said the seized narcotics and the
suspects would be handed over to the National Drug Law Enforcement Agency for
further investigation and prosecution. [@1,3]

The cannabis seizure totalled 5,986 parcels weighing 3,407.83kg. [@1,3] Officers
also seized 400 grammes of cocaine and 100 grammes of methamphetamine alongside
the cannabis. [@1] Customs put the combined duty-paid value of all the
prohibited goods at about N6.19 billion. [@2,3] Other seized items included 23,220
new machetes, 1,250 rounds of 12-calibre cartridges, 21 foreign-used vehicles,
second-hand clothing, used tyres, premium motor spirit and eight live pangolins.
[@2]

Afeni said the volume of cannabis was a grave concern because the drug poses
serious threats to public health, particularly among young people. [@1] He
warned that the machetes, allegedly smuggled without the required certificates
and permits, could be used to disrupt peaceful elections in the coming months.
[@1,3] He said the unit carried out the operations through intelligence gathering
and sustained collaboration with the NDLEA, police, State Services, Immigration,
Army, NAFDAC and other agencies. [@1]

The operations ran between Sept. 30 and Nov. 3, 2026, and the unit generated N683.66 million through compliance checks in that period. [@3] The seizures also included 57 cartons, 37 packs and six pieces of codeine syrup. [@3]
