Nigeria Customs Service Federal Operations Unit Zone 'A' arrested six suspects
and intercepted prohibited goods including cannabis, cocaine and methamphetamine
over four weeks. [@1,3] Acting Comptroller Oladapo Afeni announced the results on
Tuesday at his maiden press briefing in Ikeja, Lagos, saying 195 interception
operations produced the seizures. [@1,3,4,5] Afeni said the seized narcotics and the
suspects would be handed over to the National Drug Law Enforcement Agency for
further investigation and prosecution. [@1,3,4,5]

The cannabis seizure totalled 5,986 parcels weighing 3,407.83kg. [@1,3,4,5] Officers
also seized 400 grammes of cocaine and 100 grammes of methamphetamine alongside
the cannabis. [@1] Customs put the combined duty-paid value of all the
prohibited goods at about N6.19 billion. [@2,3,4,5] Other seized items included 23,220
new machetes, 1,250 rounds of 12-calibre cartridges, 21 foreign-used vehicles,
second-hand clothing, used tyres, premium motor spirit and eight live pangolins.
[@2]

Afeni said the volume of cannabis was a grave concern because the drug poses
serious threats to public health, particularly among young people. [@1,5] He
warned that the machetes, allegedly smuggled without the required certificates
and permits, could be used to disrupt peaceful elections in the coming months.
[@1,3,4,5] He said the unit carried out the operations through intelligence gathering
and sustained collaboration with the NDLEA, police, State Services, Immigration,
Army, NAFDAC and other agencies. [@1]

The operations ran between Sept. 30 and Nov. 3, 2026, and the unit generated N683.66 million through compliance checks in that period. [@3] The seizures also included 57 cartons, 37 packs and six pieces of codeine syrup. [@3,4]

Officers also seized 3,028 bags of 50kg rice, 995 bags of 25kg rice, 104 bags of 20kg rice and 207 bags of 5kg rice, along with 173 cartons of frozen poultry, 6,949 cartons of spaghetti and 1,236 used tyres. [@4] Officers also intercepted a 40-foot container holding 2,760 office chairs in Completely Knocked Down form. [@4]

The unit also intercepted a 40-foot container numbered SEGU 5347239 alongside 11 means of conveyance. [@5] The seizures included 250 cartons of sardines without National Agency for Food and Drug Administration and Control registration numbers. [@5] The seizures included 8,025 litres of premium motor spirit and 459 bales and 39 jumbo sacks of second-hand clothing. [@5]
