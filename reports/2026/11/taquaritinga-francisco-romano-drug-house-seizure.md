Officers of the Força Tática, the Military Police's tactical unit, of
Taquaritinga searched a disused house in the bairro Francisco Romano after an
anonymous tip that the property was being used for drug trafficking. [@2] The
operation took place on the afternoon of Wednesday 4 November 2026, and nobody
was found inside the house. [@2] Subtenente Durval, who took part in the action,
said the suspects had left the property before the patrol cars arrived,
abandoning the drugs and other material. [@2] Inside, officers found more than
two kilos of drugs, among them marijuana, crack, hashish and cocaine. [@1]

The marijuana included a block weighing close to one kilo and portions of
marijuana flower. [@2] Officers also collected R$824 in cash, a precision scale
and plastic packaging used to hold drugs. [@2] Durval told the radio station
Canal Um FM that the drugs could be worth about R$10,000. [@2] The material was
sent for the legal procedures and the Civil Police will continue the
investigation to identify those responsible. [@2]

Officers took no one into custody at the house, and no charge or arrest was
reported. [@3]
