Joint forces detained two people in Colonia El Paraíso in Iguala for their
alleged connection to crimes against health. [@1] Quadratín Guerrero reported
that the two detainees were men. [@2]

Officers seized 3 kilograms 372 grams of suspected marijuana and a precision
scale. [@1] Bajo Palabra likewise reported 3 kilos 372 grams of suspected
marijuana and a precision scale seized in the operation. [@3]

The operation brought together the state prosecutor's office, the Mexican Army,
the federal security ministry, the National Guard and state police during
crime-prevention patrols. [@1] The two people were placed at the disposal of the
competent authorities, which will determine their legal situation. [@3]
Authorities did not release the detainees' identities. [@3] La Plaza Diario
reported that their responsibility must still be determined by the competent
judicial authority. [@4]
