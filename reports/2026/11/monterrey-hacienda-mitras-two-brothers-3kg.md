Monterrey municipal police detained two brothers, Luis Alberto, 27, and Jared
Alejandro, 18, in a house search in Monterrey. [@1,3] El Porvenir names the two as
half-brothers Luis Alberto “N” and Jared Alejandro “N”. [@2] The search was
conducted in an apartment on San Sebastián street in Colonia Hacienda Mitras
with personnel of the state prosecution service present. [@2,3] The detainees and
the seized SUV were placed at the disposal of the Public Prosecutor for
investigation. [@2,3]

At capture officers found seven packets of green herb with marijuana
characteristics and a digital scale on the detainees. [@2,3] Inside the apartment
officers found a firearm with a loaded magazine, boxes of electronic cigarettes
and several bags of herb similar to marijuana weighing about three kilograms.
[@2,3] El Norte summarised the seizure as three kilograms of marijuana, a firearm
with a loaded magazine, a box of electronic cigarettes and a pickup truck. [@1]

The Hacienda Mitras search was carried out under a judicial order in an inquiry
into 26 September gunfire and property damage on Avenida Pedro Infante in the
Cumbres sector involving a white VW Tiguan, plate SFT 205C. [@2] Investigators
traced the plate through C4 cameras and recovered the vehicle inside the
searched property. [@2] The operation was supported by the Monterrey police
Aguilas drone-division team. [@2]
