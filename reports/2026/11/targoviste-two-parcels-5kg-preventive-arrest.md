Prosecutors of DIICOT's Dâmbovița territorial office detained two defendants,
aged 28 and 48, on 4 November 2026 in two separate criminal cases. [@1] Both are
under investigation for international and domestic trafficking in risk drugs,
the category cannabis falls under in Romanian law. [@1] The two men were caught
in flagrante in the city of Târgoviște on 3 November 2026 in two separate
operations. [@1] Each was caught immediately after collecting a parcel for sale,
one holding more than 2 kilograms of cannabis and the other more than 3
kilograms. [@1] DIICOT said the men obtained the cannabis in Spain and brought
it into Romania through international courier firms. [@1]

On 5 November 2026 judges at the Dâmbovița Tribunal ordered both men held in
preventive arrest for 30 days. [@1] The judicial activities were carried out
with officers of the Dâmbovița organised-crime service. [@1] DIICOT noted that
the two accused keep their procedural rights and the presumption of innocence
throughout the criminal proceedings. [@1]
