Two women were arrested in flagrante for drug trafficking and association for
trafficking on the morning of Wednesday 11 November 2026 in Arapiraca, in the
Agreste of Alagoas. [@1] The action was carried out by teams of the 3rd Military
Police Battalion during an integrated operation coordinated by the Alagoas
Public Security Secretariat. [@1] It began at about 11:30 after an anonymous tip
that a passenger on a bus from Bahia was carrying drugs to the Arapiraca bus
terminal in the Jardim Tropical neighbourhood. [@1] The tip said the drugs would
later be delivered to a house in the municipality. [@1]

Officers from Pelopes 01, Pelopes 02 and the Canil dog unit went to the terminal
and found a woman with two travel bags. [@1] During the search they found a
tablet of a substance similar to cocaine and a quantity of cannabis. [@1] She
said she had been hired to carry the drugs from Bahia to a house in the Jardim
Esperança neighbourhood, where she was to make the delivery. [@1] At that
address officers found another woman at the door, who ran inside when she saw
them, and cannabis was visible on a sofa. [@1] After they were allowed to search
the house, they found further portions of cannabis and crack. [@1] The second
woman said the material belonged to her brother, known by the nickname Cicinho,
who had left the drugs and asked her to receive the delivery. [@1]

Both women were taken with the seized material to the Central de Polícia, where
they were booked in flagrante for drug trafficking and association for
trafficking. [@1] The Military Police reported seizing 471 grams of pressed
cannabis. [@1] They also seized 1,018 grams of cocaine paste, which is not
counted as cannabis. [@1] Officers also seized 101 grams of crack, which is not
counted as cannabis. [@1] They seized 6 grams of cocaine as well, which is not
counted as cannabis. [@1] Alagoas 24 Horas reported that officers seized R$158
in cash. [@2] Officers also took four mobile phones. [@1] They seized three
precision scales and packaging used to store drugs. [@1] Alagoas 24 Horas said a
personal search of the passenger found nothing, and that the cocaine tablet and
cannabis were found in the bags. [@2]

That account described the operation as a joint action of the Public Security
Intelligence Centre, known as CHEGII, and the 3rd Battalion. [@2]
