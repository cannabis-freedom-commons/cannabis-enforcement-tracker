A tip about drug trafficking led the Military Police to seize a large quantity
of marijuana hidden under a mattress in a house in the Catumbi neighbourhood of
Três Pontas, in southern Minas Gerais, on the night of Monday 9 November 2026.
[@1] A 16-year-old named in the incident was wanted by the force. [@1] The
adolescent was not in the property at the time of the approach and had not been
located. [@1] The Military Police said it was still searching for the young
person. [@1]

Officers went to the address after being told the young person was selling drugs
in the area and using a vehicle to help distribute them. [@1] Patrol of the
neighbourhood did not find the vehicle. [@1] The adolescent's legal guardian
authorised the officers to enter the house and carry out the search. [@1] In the
bedroom the officers found a bag of marijuana under the mattress, and in the
same room they seized cannabis seeds and a precision scale. [@1] All of the
collected material was sent to the Civil Police station for the applicable
procedures. [@1]

A motorcycle patrol received information that an adolescent was selling drugs
using a pickup, and the tip also gave the vehicle's plate. [@2] The pickup was
not found, but checks by the team identified the address where the suspect
lived. [@2] At the property the officers met the adolescent's sister, and he was
not at home. [@2] Under the mattress they found a package of marijuana, and in
another package a large quantity of cannabis seeds, together with a precision
scale. [@2] The seized material was taken and the case was sent to the Civil
Police, while the search for the suspect continued. [@2]
