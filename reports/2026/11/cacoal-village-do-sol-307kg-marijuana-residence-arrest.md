A joint operation of the Military and Civil Police forces resulted in the
seizure of approximately 307.5 kilograms of marijuana in a residence in the
Village do Sol neighbourhood of Cacoal (Rondônia state), on the night of
Tuesday, 11 November 2026, ending with the arrest of a man identified by the
initials G.B.F., known by the nickname Neblina. [@1] Military police of the 4th
Battalion, acting jointly with the Civil Police, seized about 307.5 kilograms of
substance with characteristics similar to marijuana at a residence in the
Village do Sol neighbourhood of Cacoal on 11 November 2026. [@2] A man
identified by the initials G.B.F., known by the nickname Neblina, was arrested.
[@2]

Officers found six black bundles of the substance in one bedroom and 30 more
packages wrapped in a sheet. [@2] A black and blue backpack with two more
packages was found inside a living-room sofa, and a bag with another package, a
precision scale and packaging material apparently used for the storage and
fractional sale of drugs were also seized on the premises. [@2]

He was arrested and taken with the drugs and the other seized material to the
Civil Police station, where he was presented to the police authority for the
appropriate measures. [@2]
