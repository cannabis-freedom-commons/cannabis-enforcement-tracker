Thuringia's state police inspection in Gera reported that officers stopped a
woman driver in Greiz town centre on 6 November 2026. [@2] During the traffic
check the woman admitted to police that she had consumed marijuana and alcohol.
[@2] A drug screening test came back positive. [@2]

She refused a breath alcohol test. [@1] Police ended her journey for the time
being. [@1]

The account was issued by the Gera state police inspection, which covers Greiz
in eastern Thuringia, at 10:00 on 8 November 2026. [@1]
