A Florence, South Carolina man is facing methamphetamine and marijuana charges,
according to the Florence County Sheriff's Office. [@1] Jack Randall Newsome,
48, was arrested Thursday after narcotics investigators and a SWAT team searched
a residence on Ossie Lane. [@2]

The search followed an undercover operation and resulted in the seizure of an
unspecified quantity of illegal drugs. [@2] Newsome was charged with
distribution of methamphetamine, trafficking in methamphetamine, possession of a
controlled substance, and possession with intent to distribute flunitrazepam or
an analogue. [@2]

Newsome is being held at the Florence County Detention Center while awaiting a
bond hearing. [@2]
