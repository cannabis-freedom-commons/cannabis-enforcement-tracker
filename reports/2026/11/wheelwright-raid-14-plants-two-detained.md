Officers of the PDI's Melincué district raided a house in Wheelwright, General
López department, during the night of Wednesday 11 November, detaining two men
and seizing cannabis plants and other drug-investigation material. [@2] The
raided house stands at the intersection of Buenos Aires and Uruguay streets.
[@1] The PDI operation was supported by officers from Wheelwright's Comisaría 9ª
and by the PAT tactical-entry group from Rosario. [@1] The two detained people,
identified only by the initials L. N. and F. L., were taken to Comisaría 9ª in
Wheelwright. [@1]

During the search investigators found 14 cannabis seedlings, which field tests
confirmed as positive for marijuana. [@1] They also seized 211 grams of plant
material, a precision scale, two marijuana grinders, trimmings and cannabis
seeds. [@1] The 14 seedlings tested positive for marijuana and their stripped
leaves weighed 211 grams in total. [@3] Officers also seized six mobile phones,
a memory card and six cartridges of different calibres. [@1]

On prosecutor Luciana del Grecco's order, both detainees were notified that a
simple-possession case had been opened, with the investigation continuing under
the Melincué prosecutor's office. [@1] The case is classified as simple
possession of narcotics, an offence under National Law 23,737. [@2]
