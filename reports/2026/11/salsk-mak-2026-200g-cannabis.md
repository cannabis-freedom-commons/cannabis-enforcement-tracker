Officers of the Salsk linear department of the Russian Interior Ministry on
transport detained a previously convicted 67-year-old resident of Salsk during
the interdepartmental operation Mak-2026. [@1] Mak-2026 is an interdepartmental
complex preventive operation aimed at stopping the spread of plant-origin drugs.
[@1] During the operation, transport police working with a handler and a service
dog found green plant material on a metal sieve at the suspect's residence. [@1]
An expert examination identified the seized material as cannabis, or marijuana,
with a total weight of about 200 grams. [@1]

According to preliminary information, the man grew wild cannabis on his
household plot, gathered parts of the plants, then dried and ground them for
personal use without intent to sell. [@1]

Investigators of the same Salsk transport police unit opened a criminal case
under Part 2 of Article 228 of the Russian Criminal Code for unlawful
acquisition and storage of drugs without intent to sell in a large amount. [@1]
The provision as charged carries a maximum penalty of up to ten years'
imprisonment. [@1] The suspect was placed under the procedural measure of an
obligation to appear. [@1]
