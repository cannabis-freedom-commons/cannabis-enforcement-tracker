Immigration police raided the warehouse on 12 November 2026. [@2] The warehouse
is at 191/1-2 in Naraphirom subdistrict, Bang Len district, Nakhon Pathom. [@3]
Officers found 16 Vietnamese nationals working inside. [@1] Six had stayed 1,460
days beyond their permits, about four years, and were charged with overstaying.
[@1] The other 10 were charged with working outside the jobs their permits
allowed. [@5]

Police charged all 16 and sent them to investigators at Bang Len police station.
[@1] The raid followed information that the building was used for illegal
cannabis cultivation and to employ foreign workers without papers. [@1] The site
was described as a factory used to grow cannabis. [@2]

The operation was ordered by Immigration Division 3 commander Pol. Maj. Gen.
Songprod Sirisukha. [@4] He said immigration units would tighten checks on
businesses that employ foreign workers and on premises used for other offences.
[@4] Police described the raid as part of a wider crackdown on businesses
suspected of using undocumented foreign workers or other illegal activity. [@6]
