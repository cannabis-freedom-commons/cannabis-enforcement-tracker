A special NDPS court in Chandigarh gave a lenient sentence to a 34-year-old man
convicted of keeping 200 grams of charas, a cannabis resin. [@1] The convict, a
resident of Hamirpur in Himachal Pradesh, had the one month he had already spent
in jail counted as his sentence and was fined 7,500 rupees. [@1] He will not
have to go to jail again. [@1]

The court said he should be given a meaningful chance to return to the
mainstream of society and live as a law-abiding citizen. [@1] Arguing on
sentence, the convict said he regretted his mistake and had fallen into bad
company. [@1] A driver by profession, he is the only earning member of a family
that depends on him: his wife, two minor children and elderly parents. [@1] He
had faced the trial process for about five years. [@1]

Special court judge Raman Gokalani said in the judgment that the 200 grams of
charas recovered did not fall in the commercial category but in the intermediate
quantity category under the NDPS Act, India's narcotics law. [@1] The convict
had no earlier criminal record and was not found involved in any other criminal
activity after the first information report was registered. [@1] Police had
arrested him five years earlier near Industrial Area, Chandigarh. [@1]
