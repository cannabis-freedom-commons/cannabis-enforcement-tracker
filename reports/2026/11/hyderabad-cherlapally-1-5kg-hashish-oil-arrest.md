SOT Uppal and Cherlapally police arrested a drug peddler near Cherlapally
railway station on 12 November 2026. [@1] Officers seized 1.5 kg of hashish oil
worth ₹18 lakh and a mobile phone from the accused. [@1]

Police said the accused had brought the hashish oil from Visakhapatnam to
Hyderabad after an absconding supplier instructed him to transport it. [@1]
Police are trying to trace the absconding supplier and investigating links to
the wider drug supply network. [@1]

Malkajgiri Commissioner of Police B Sumathi, IPS, supervised the operation with
senior police officers. [@1]
