Damien Coulange, 34, appeared before the Nevers criminal court on 4 November
2026 from his remand prison by video link. [@1] Police had searched his flat in
central Nevers in the early morning of 3 September 2025 as part of an
investigation into an armed robbery, and the search led to a separate drug case
against him. [@1] He was already serving five years in prison after being
convicted at first instance and on appeal over the armed robbery. [@1]

Officers found 245 g of cannabis herb and 44 g of cannabis resin at his home.
[@1] The search also turned up 640 euros in cash, phones, a precision scale, a
knife stained with cannabis and packaging bags. [@1] He admitted dealing to
settle a drug debt, pay for his own future use and cover his daily expenses.
[@1]
