A man was arrested in flagrante by the Military Police in Tupaciguara after
threatening his own mother. [@1] Officers went to the residence to answer a
domestic-violence call and, during that attendance, received an anonymous tip
that he kept a cannabis plantation in the yard. [@1] With the family's
authorisation, the officers entered the property and found seven cannabis
plants. [@1]

The Military Police said the arrest was on 8 November in Tupaciguara and that
seven plants similar to cannabis were seized from the yard of the residence.
[@2] During the call, officers were told by a complainant that plants similar to
cannabis were at the property. [@2] After contact with the people involved, and
only after permission to enter the house, the team confirmed the plants were
there. [@2] During the intervention the man spontaneously said that he
cultivated the plants and sold drugs to supplement the family's income. [@2]

Questioned, he confessed that he cultivated the drug and also sold it. [@1] He
said the sales were meant to supplement the family's income. [@1] He was
arrested for the threats against his mother and for the illegal cultivation of
cannabis. [@1] The Military Police said he was given an arrest notice, in their
wording in thesis, for threat in a domestic-violence context and for cultivating
plants intended for the production of drugs. [@2]

Seven plants were seized, and after a medical check he was presented to the
Civil Police. [@2] The plants were seized and he was left at the disposal of the
courts. [@1]
