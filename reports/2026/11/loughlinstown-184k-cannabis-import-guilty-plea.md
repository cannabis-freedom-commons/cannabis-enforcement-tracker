Duane Costello, aged 38, pleaded guilty to the importation of cannabis. [@1] The
importation offence was dated January 6, 2025 and was located at his address in
Loughlinstown Wood, Loughlinstown, County Dublin. [@1] He was handed a sentence of three years and nine months. [@3] The judge said Costello’s early guilty plea allowed the court to depart from the presumptive mandatory minimum sentence of ten years. [@3] Dublin Circuit Criminal
Court was told of the case. [@1,2]

He had paid the import duties on cannabis valued at over €180,000. [@1,2] Garda Sean Savage told the court that a package containing over 9kg of cannabis, valued at €184,000, was intercepted by customs officers on January 6, 2025. [@3] He used his Revolut account to pay the import duties. [@2] Costello has 44 previous convictions, including some drugs offences, all at the District Court. [@3] The garda said his view is that Costello was importing the drugs on behalf of another and had an awareness of what was in the package. [@3] He
refused to accept the drugs when gardai carried out a controlled delivery. [@1,2]
