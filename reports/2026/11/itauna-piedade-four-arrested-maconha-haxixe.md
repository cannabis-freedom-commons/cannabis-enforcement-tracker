Military police carried out an anti-trafficking operation in Itaúna on Thursday
12 November 2026. [@1] The action began with the execution of a
search-and-seizure warrant issued by the 2nd Criminal and Youth Court at a
property in the Piedade neighbourhood. [@1]

Inside the home officers found a 9mm Israeli-made pistol with an attached laser
sight and an extended magazine holding 34 live rounds hidden in a boot in a
25-year-old woman's closet, along with portions of crack in the same
compartment. [@1] With sniffer-dog support from the ROCCA unit, officers found a
larger pressed portion of crack in the front of the same shoe. [@1] In a room
belonging to a 31-year-old man, officers seized seven ecstasy tablets, a portion
of hashish, a portion of marijuana, cash and a firearm-maintenance kit. [@1]
Officers seized additional portions of marijuana in the hallway of the house and
inside a Chevrolet Astra belonging to a 69-year-old man. [@1]

The 25-year-old woman was arrested for illegal possession of a restricted-use
firearm and drug trafficking, the 31-year-old man for drug trafficking, and the
69-year-old man was taken in on suspicion of complicity and concealment. [@1] A
fourth person, aged 43, was arrested for slander after loudly accusing the
police team of planting the seized materials. [@1] The detainees and all seized
materials were taken to the Civil Police on-duty station in Divinópolis for
confirmation of the flagrant arrests and further legal steps. [@1]
