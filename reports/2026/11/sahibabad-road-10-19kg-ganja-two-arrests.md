Crossing Republik police station and the city-zone special weapons and tactics
(SWAT) team in Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh, arrested two men in a joint operation
against illegal ganja trafficking under Operation Prahar, a district anti-drug
drive. [@1] Officers seized 10 kg 190 g of illegal ganja and a motorcycle
without a number plate that police said was used in trafficking. [@1] Police
said the two were caught on 8 November 2026 during a checking drive on the
unmetalled path by the madrasa on Sahibabad Road. [@1]

Police identified the arrested men as Mohammad Aqil, 25, son of Israt Ali, and
Mujammil, 19, son of Zarif, both residents of Mubarakpur village in the Asmoli
police station area of Sambhal district, Uttar Pradesh. [@1] Police said the two
allegedly told them during questioning that they bought ganja cheaply in Delhi,
made it into small packets and sold them at high prices in the National Capital
Region. [@1]

A case was registered against both men at Crossing Republik police station under
sections 8 and 20 of the NDPS Act, India's Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic
Substances Act. [@1] Police said they were gathering information on the men's
other criminal history, and further legal proceedings against them were under
way. [@1]
