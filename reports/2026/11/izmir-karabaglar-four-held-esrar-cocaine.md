Police in İzmir's Karabağlar district raided an address identified as a place
where drugs were being sold and detained four suspects. [@1] The four suspects
were identified only by initials, with ages of 31, 22, 23 and 30. [@1]

Police found 0.93 grams of esrar, the Turkish term for cannabis, on three people
identified as buyers. [@1] Officers then raided the suspects' homes, where
searches found 61.09 grams of esrar. [@1] The same searches turned up 71.52
grams of cocaine, 364 drug pills and a precision scale. [@1] Officers also found
19,000 Turkish lira, 130 US dollars and 2,110 euros that police believed came
from crime. [@1]

Legal proceedings were opened against the four, and they were to be taken to the
chief public prosecutor's office after processing. [@1] The account was
distributed by the İhlas Haber Ajansı (İHA) news agency. [@2]
