Carabinieri of the Roma Eur company stopped a Cuban man while he was handing two
grams of marijuana to another man in the Corviale, Trullo and Magliana districts
on the south-western edge of Rome. [@1] The Cuban was reported to prosecutors
for selling marijuana but was not arrested, the Carabinieri said. [@2]

The operation, run by the Carabinieri company of Roma Eur with the helicopter
unit of Roma Urbe and motorcyclists of the Radiomobile unit, produced five
arrests and eleven reports to prosecutors. [@2] Officers identified 458 people
and checked 312 vehicles, and road-code fines amounted to 10,879 euros. [@2] Two
of the arrests were of an Egyptian owner and an Egyptian employee of a car wash
where officers found 247 grams of hashish, packaging material and 605 euros in
cash. [@2] Two Cubans aged 28 and 49 were arrested on prison orders from the
Prosecutor General's Office at the Rome Court of Appeal to serve one year and
ten months for a robbery and personal injuries committed in Tivoli in September
2024. [@2] Among the other reports to prosecutors were a Filipino man found with
doses of shaboo, a methamphetamine-type stimulant, and three Peruvians accused
of occupying an INPS housing apartment with an illegal electricity connection.
[@2]

The Carabinieri described the sweep as one of their extraordinary
territory-control services meant to curb illegality and strengthen security in
Rome's neighbourhoods. [@2]
