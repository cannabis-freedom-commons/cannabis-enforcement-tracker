Carabinieri arrested eight people at dawn on Friday in a blitz against drug
dealing in the woods of the provinces of Milan, Como and Varese. [@3] Six of
them were found in a flat in Limido Comasco, where the special intervention
group GIS blew open four doors with small explosive charges and flash-bang
devices. [@3] The other two were detained in Sesto Calende, Varese province, and
in Corsico, outside Milan. [@2]

Searches of two wooded areas at Pogliano Milanese and Arluno turned up an AK47
Kalashnikov rifle similar to the one fired in Parabiago, other weapons including
pistols, ammunition, cash, phones and quantities of hashish and cocaine. [@1,7]
The operation was ordered by Milan's district anti-mafia directorate (DDA) in an
investigation into hashish and cocaine trafficking that police say produced
shootings, attempted murders and kidnappings between rival dealer groups of
mainly Moroccan origin. [@4,7]

Some of those detained are accused of the armed raid on the night of 15 October
at Villapia di Parabiago, where, according to the arrest decree signed by
anti-mafia prosecutor Paolo Storari, a group of ten men, at least four of them
armed with Kalashnikov-AK47 rifles, fired 19 rounds of 7.62 calibre at a rival
faction to take over the dealing ground known as "Ristorante il Pioppeto". [@1]
Private surveillance cameras in the Parabiago woods were decisive in identifying
the assault group. [@1] Other footage showed the group's methods running without
interruption between 31 October and 4 November 2026, including a suspect at
Pogliano Milanese opening a car boot to let out an armed man. [@5] According to
the same account, the group ran the woods dealing ground in shifts, collecting
hashish and Kalashnikovs from a hiding place at Pogliano Milanese and changing
into camouflage clothing before heading to the spot it controlled. [@2] The
investigation, coordinated by prosecutor Storari, is at its opening stage and
must establish whether the group also includes those who killed 41-year-old Ait
Omar Adil, whose body was found in the Rugareto woods in the night of 29 to 30
August. [@2]

The Carabinieri released footage of the operation showing patrols filmed from
above, the GIS raid on the Limido Comasco flat and the weapons, ammunition and
drugs seized in the woods. [@6]
