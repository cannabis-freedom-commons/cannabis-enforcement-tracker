Motorcyclist Akshaykumar P. Gohel was seriously injured when his motorcycle
GJ-38-AC-5892 crashed near the petrol pump at Vadvasa village on National
Highway 48 near Prantij. [@1] He was taken with his luggage by 108 ambulance to
Prantij Civil Hospital. [@1] He died during treatment after only brief care for
the crash injuries. [@1]

Police searched his maroon college bag and black plastic bag in the hospital
compound and found ganja inside. [@2] The ganja weighed 2.928 kg and police
valued it at Rs 146,400. [@1] Police also seized Rs 400 in cash, his driving
licence, RC book and the bags with other belongings. [@2] The rider lived
opposite Sahajanand Flats at Ashokwadi in Saraspur, Ahmedabad. [@2]

Prantij police registered a case under Sections 8(C) and 20(B) of the NDPS Act.
[@1] Police said they are investigating where he had brought the ganja from and
to whom he was to supply it. [@1]
