State Police officers from the Milazzo commissariat arrested a 38-year-old man
in flagrante during extraordinary territorial checks. [@1] The 38-year-old was
placed in pre-trial custody in prison on the order of the judicial authority.
[@1] At the prosecutor's request, the investigating judge of the Barcellona
court validated the arrest and ordered continued pretrial detention in prison.
[@1] In the same checks officers also took a 45-year-old man into custody who
was subject to a final imprisonment order for earlier crimes against the person
and property. [@1]

Officers first stopped the younger man on streets in Milazzo town centre holding
doses already packaged for retail sale in the Milazzo area. [@1] A later search
of a garage available to him recovered further drugs believed intended for sale.
[@1] With the drugs officers found cutting and packaging material for single
doses and several precision scales. [@1] Police listed 140 grams of cannabinoids
among the drugs held by the 38-year-old. [@1] Officers found the 45-year-old
with about 15 grams of cannabinoids already divided into single doses. [@1] The
same haul included 152 grams of cocaine and 4 grams of synthetic drugs, which
are not counted in the cannabis figure. [@2]

The 45-year-old was reported to the judicial authority for unlawful possession
of his doses. [@2] Police described the operation as part of intensified
territorial control activity in Milazzo. [@1]
