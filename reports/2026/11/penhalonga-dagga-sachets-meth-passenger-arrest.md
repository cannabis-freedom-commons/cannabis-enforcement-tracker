Zimbabwe Republic Police arrested 27-year-old Lancelot Nehanda at the St
Augustine's Turn-off in Penhalonga on 5 November 2026 for unlawful possession of
dangerous drugs. [@2] Nehanda was a passenger in a Honda Fit travelling from
Mutare towards Penhalonga that officers intercepted at about 2am after a tip-off
that a passenger was carrying dangerous drugs. [@1]

National police spokesperson Commissioner Paul Nyathi said the team searched the
vehicle and recovered 139 small sachets of dagga, the local name for cannabis,
and two big crystals of methamphetamine from Nehanda. [@1] The drugs were all in
a black plastic bag, and Nehanda was arrested pending further investigation.
[@1]

The police's own account of the arrest describes only the methamphetamine,
saying a black plastic bag with 141 sachets of crystal methamphetamine belonged
to the suspect. [@2] A second outlet reported that Nehanda faces charges of
unlawful possession of dangerous drugs and that police recovered the sachets
from the vehicle in which he was travelling. [@3]

The Herald's account names the driver as 24-year-old Jetro Matiafa of Muchena
Village under Chief Mutasa, and says the Honda Fit carried registration number
ADT 9825. [@1]
