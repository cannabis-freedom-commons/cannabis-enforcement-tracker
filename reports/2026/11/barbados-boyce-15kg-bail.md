Natalie Jacinta Boyce, a 43-year-old woman of Cutting Road, Haggatt Hall in St
Michael, appeared before acting Magistrate Keitha Ellis in the District ‘B’
Magistrates’ Court on Monday, November 2. [@1] She pleaded not guilty to four
drug-related charges involving cannabis valued at an estimated $121,600. [@1]
The charges are possession, trafficking, possession with intent to supply and
importation of cannabis. [@1] The court granted her $45,000 bail. [@1] She is
scheduled to return to court on February 23, 2027. [@1]

The Barbados Police Service Narcotics Unit arrested and formally charged Boyce
with the four cannabis-related offences. [@2] Police allege the offences were
committed on October 30, 2026. [@2]
