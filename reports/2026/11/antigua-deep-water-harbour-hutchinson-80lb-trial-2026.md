Kimmesha Hutchinson, who is in her 30s, is facing High Court proceedings
stemming from the April 2023 discovery when she visited the port to clear a
shipment. [@1] Hutchinson had recently arrived in Antigua from the United States
when she went to Deep Water Harbour on April 26, 2023, to collect a barrel
addressed to her. [@1] During clearance police reportedly discovered
approximately 80 pounds of marijuana inside the barrel, with an estimated street
value of $480,000. [@1] A second account confirms she faces charges relating to
the marijuana, valued at $480,000, found in a barrel shipped to Antigua in her
name. [@2]

After the port discovery police obtained a search warrant for Hutchinson's
residence on Old Parham Road. [@1] On April 27, 2023, officers accompanied
Hutchinson to the property, where narcotics officers conducted a search. [@1]
During that search narcotics officers reportedly found a black laptop bag on top
of a bedroom closet at about 10:30 a.m. [@2] Inside the bag was an Amazon
package containing a white powdery substance suspected to be cocaine. [@2] That
substance reportedly weighed two pounds and was valued at $31,000. [@2]

Following that discovery Hutchinson was arrested and charged with unlawful
possession of cocaine, being concerned with its supply, and possession with
intent to transfer. [@1] She denied all three cocaine charges. [@1] The current
status of the separate cocaine proceedings remains unclear. [@1] On May 8, 2023,
Hutchinson appeared before Chief Magistrate Joanne Walsh in the St. John's
Magistrates' Court on a charge of possession of 80 pounds of marijuana. [@3]
Police also arrested and charged her with possession with intent to transfer,
being concerned in supplying cannabis, importation of cannabis and drug
trafficking. [@3] Those cannabis offences reportedly occurred on April 26 at
Deep Water Harbour. [@3] After the second drug charge she was remanded to
prison, with bail available only in the High Court. [@3]
