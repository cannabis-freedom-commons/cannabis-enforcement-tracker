The court in Turnhout sentenced 34-year-old K.S. from Geel to 20 months in
prison for dealing cannabis. [@1] He received 20 months in prison, but suspended
on condition that he comply with a series of imposed conditions. [@1] The man
had asked for a community-service order, but the court rejected that request.
[@1] He stated that he had ordered cannabis several times for his own use and
had then acted as a courier a few times because of financial need. [@1]

Kempenland police inspectors checked K.S. in Peer on 15 November of last year.
[@1] He was in the car together with his girlfriend and two minor children. [@1]
In his wallet police found just under 1,200 euros in cash, an unpackaged user
quantity of cannabis in the centre console and a small quantity of cannabis in
an empty cigarette pack. [@1] A saliva test was positive for cannabis use. [@1]
Police also seized his car because he was driving despite a driving ban. [@1]
When the car was towed, police found an Action shopping bag containing several
bags of cannabis of 12 to 25 grams. [@1]

Later, another batch of cannabis and a large sum of cash were found in his home.
[@1] Messages found on his mobile phone also pointed to drug dealing. [@1]
