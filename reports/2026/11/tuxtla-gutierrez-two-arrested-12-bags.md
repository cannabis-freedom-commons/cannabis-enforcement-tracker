The Chiapas State Attorney General's Office, the Fiscalía General del Estado,
reported the detention of two men identified as César “N” and Luis “N”. [@1] The
two men were described as alleged perpetrators of an offence against health, in
the form of simple possession of marijuana. [@1] Police detained them during a
surveillance patrol in Colonia Caleras Maciel, a neighbourhood in Tuxtla
Gutiérrez, the capital of Chiapas state. [@1]

During that patrol, officers found 12 small plastic bags holding dry herb with
characteristics similar to marijuana in their possession. [@1] The 12 bags were
described in Spanish as bolsitas de plástico with hierba seca, small bags of dry
herb with characteristics similar to marijuana. [@1]

Both detainees and the seized bags were placed at the disposal of the Ministerio
Público, the public prosecutor's office. [@1] A colonia is a neighbourhood, so
Caleras Maciel is the neighbourhood where the patrol made the detention. [@1]
That prosecutor's office will determine their legal situation and decide the
next step on the simple-possession allegation. [@1]
