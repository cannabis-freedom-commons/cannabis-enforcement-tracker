A 29-year-old man from Tamworth was arrested by officers, who also seized the
Vauxhall. [@1] Officers had pulled over the red Vauxhall Astra on Glascote Road
in Tamworth at around 9.30pm on Thursday, 5 November 2026. [@1] A Staffordshire
Police spokesperson said officers from the force's road crime team made the
roadside stop. [@1] Upon closer inspection, checks showed the vehicle was being
driven without insurance while the driver was also disqualified. [@1]

A subsequent roadside drugs test found the driver tested positive for both
cannabis and cocaine. [@1] Officers conducted a stop-search and found the driver
in possession of cash, cannabis and cocaine. [@1] Officers located a further
amount of cocaine inside the vehicle. [@1] Officers seized the vehicle for
having no insurance. [@1]

The man was detained on suspicion of driving whilst under the influence and
possession with intent to supply a Class A drug. [@1] He was also dealt with for
driving and documents offences, police said. [@1] He has since been released
under investigation. [@1]
