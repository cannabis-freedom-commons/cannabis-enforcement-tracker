Nine men were charged after a drug bust at a Memphis home, the Shelby County
Sheriff's Office said, with the charges announced on Friday 6 November 2026.
[@1] The arrests came from a search warrant executed at an address along Bethel
Avenue near Thomas Street in Uptown, a neighbourhood on the north edge of
downtown Memphis. [@1] The sheriff's office said the warrant was part of a
narcotics investigation. [@1]

Detectives seized 2.5 pounds (about 1.13 kg) of marijuana and 434 grams (about
0.43 kg) of THC wax inside the home. [@1] The same search turned up 320
Alprazolam, 190 Oxycodone, 57 Ecstasy, 48 Hydrocodone and 13 amphetamine pills,
18 Suboxone strips and five handguns. [@1]

Each of the nine men faced possession of a controlled substance with intent to
manufacture, deliver or sell, the sheriff's office said. [@1] The sheriff's
office also charged them with possessing or employing a firearm during a
dangerous felony, and several with firearm theft or possession of a handgun as a
convicted felon. [@1] Two of the men, Tremayne Scales, 41, and Torrey Thomas,
35, also faced possession of ecstasy with intent to manufacture, deliver or
sell. [@1]
