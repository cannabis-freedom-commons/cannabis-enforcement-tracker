Officers from the Quincy Police Department, the Florida Department of Law
Enforcement, the Florida Department of Corrections probation service and the
Florida Highway Patrol executed simultaneous search warrants at two Quincy
addresses on Thursday, Nov. 5, 2026. [@1] The operation targeted drug
trafficking in Quincy and grew out of an investigation opened in the summer of
2026 into quality-of-life concerns and high-crime areas of the city. [@1]

Police said they had established probable cause to arrest two men, Kirby Wimes
and Travis Lanier, for distributing cocaine, methamphetamine and marijuana. [@1]
The cannabis allegation against Wimes is possession of cannabis with intent to
sell, listed alongside cocaine trafficking, cocaine possession near a place of
worship and near a childcare facility, and probation violation. [@1] Lanier was
not at either address when the warrants were served, and his listed allegations
involve cocaine and ammunition, not cannabis. [@1] Police did not confirm
whether either man had been taken into custody. [@1]

Police did not disclose the quantities of any drugs or evidence seized in the
searches. [@1] Chief Brian Alexander called the raids "a great example of the
cooperation law enforcement agencies are forging in the effort to reduce crime
and keep the City of Quincy safe." [@1]
