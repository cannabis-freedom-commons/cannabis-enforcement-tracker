Minas Gerais Civil Police carried out Operation Smurfs against drug trafficking
in Poços de Caldas on 5 November 2026. [@1] The operation executed two
search-and-seizure warrants in the Conjunto Habitacional area in the city's
South Zone. [@2] Four people were taken to the police station, with two
adolescents handed to their mother and two 19-year-old men booked in flagrante.
[@3]

Searches seized tablets and portions of substances resembling marijuana, crack
and hashish, including 172 crack stones already packaged for sale. [@1] Officers
also found a .40-calibre pistol, three magazines, ammunition, eight mobile
phones, drug-packaging material, cash and a vehicle. [@2] A television set was
also seized so investigators could check its origin. [@1]

Police said the seized materials corroborated earlier findings that one of the
inspected homes was used as a drug depot. [@3] Investigations are continuing to
clarify the involvement of those taken in and the origin of the seized
materials. [@2]
