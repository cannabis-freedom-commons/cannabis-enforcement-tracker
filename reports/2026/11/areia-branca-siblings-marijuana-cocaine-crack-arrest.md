Two siblings, a woman and a man, were caught in the act and arrested for drug
trafficking on the night of Tuesday 10 November 2026 in Areia Branca, in the
western region of Rio Grande do Norte. [@1] Military Police carried out the
arrests after an anonymous tip. [@1] During the operation the officers found
cocaine, marijuana and crack inside the suspects' home. [@1] The siblings and
the seized material were then taken to the duty police station in Mossoró. [@1]

At that station the woman said she had been keeping the drugs for another
person. [@1] Delegate Roberto said that one of the siblings was already the
subject of a police procedure. [@1] The two were booked for drug trafficking and
for association for the purpose of trafficking. [@1] After the booking
procedures they were taken into the prison system. [@1]

Areia Branca is in the Oeste potiguar, the local name for the west of Rio Grande
do Norte. [@1] Officers found the marijuana in the suspects' home together with
cocaine and crack. [@1] Both siblings were taken into custody in the prison
system after the station procedures. [@1]
