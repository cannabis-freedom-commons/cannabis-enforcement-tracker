Government Railway Police officers at Bapatla railway station in Andhra Pradesh
found and seized a suitcase containing 18 kilograms of ganja on 5 November 2026.
[@1]

The search was made on the Bilaspur–Tirupati Express, train number 17481, after
the train's travelling ticket examiner passed officers information that some
suspected passengers were carrying ganja in a suitcase. [@1] Bapatla GRP
sub-inspector Satyavathi and sub-inspector in charge Subhani carried out the
inspection with their staff and identified the suitcase holding the ganja. [@1]
The suspects whose suitcase was found are described as having fled as it was
being checked, and no arrest is reported. [@1]

Police registered a case over the whole suitcase of ganja found and said they
were investigating. [@1] The Telugu report gives no value for the ganja, no
origin or destination for the consignment and no figure for the number of
suspects who fled. [@2]
