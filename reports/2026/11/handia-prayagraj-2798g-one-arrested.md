Handia police in the Prayagraj Commissionerate arrested Arjun Singh, a man aged
about 42, near the Anjana canal in the Handia police-station area. [@1] Police
identified him as the son of late Sehat Bahadur Singh and as a resident of
Sangrampatti village under Handia police station, and named him as the sole
accused in the action. [@1] The arrest was presented as an action by the Handia
police team against illegal ganja in its jurisdiction. [@1]

At the time of the arrest, officers recovered 2.798 kilograms of illegal ganja
from his possession. [@1] Police treated that arrest and recovery as the basis
for registering a criminal case at Handia police station. [@1] The case was
registered as Crime No. 545 of 2026 under Sections 8 and 20 of the NDPS Act.
[@1]

Police said further legal proceedings were initiated in accordance with the
rules, with the investigation to proceed from the registered case. [@1]
