A court in Aznakayevo convicted a 60-year-old resident of Aznakayevsky district
of illegally acquiring and storing narcotics without intent to sell, in a large
amount. [@1] The Aznakayevskaya city prosecutor's office supported the state
prosecution in the case. [@1] Taking the prosecutor's position into account, the
court sentenced him to three years' imprisonment, suspended. [@1] The verdict
has not entered into legal force. [@1]

Case materials established that in August 2026 he gathered a narcotic-containing
plant near the village of Ursaevo and kept it on his private household until
police seized it. [@1] Once dried, the plant's mass exceeded 6 kilograms. [@1]
The republican newspaper Respublika Tatarstan reported that the plant he
gathered near Ursaevo was cannabis. [@2] Newsroom24 said the cannabis found in
his yard was growing wild, and that he picked it near Ursaevo and dried it at
home. [@3] That account also said the proceedings established no sale of the
drug. [@3]

He admitted guilt, expressed remorse, and said that because of his age he
suffers severe pain and used the drug to ease his condition. [@1]
