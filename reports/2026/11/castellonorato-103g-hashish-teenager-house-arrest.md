An 18-year-old had three doses of cocaine in his pocket and said the possession
was strictly for his own use. [@1] Officers of the Formia group of the Guardia
di Finanza did not believe the account given by G.D.S., an 18-year-old from
Formia. [@1] They searched his home in Castellonorato, a hill hamlet of Formia,
and said they had found a sort of small drug market. [@1]

In the house they found 78 grams of cocaine and a further 12 grams of the same
drug already divided into 20 doses ready for sale. [@1] They also found 103
grams of hashish formed into a circular block, a precision scale, a mobile phone
and 1,825 euros in cash, described as the supposed proof of dealing. [@1]

The 18-year-old from Castellonorato was arrested, but on Monday he was allowed
to return to his home in the hamlet. [@1] Maria Rosaria Santoni, the preliminary
investigations judge at the Court of Cassino, validated the arrest at the
request of deputy prosecutor De Angelis. [@1] The defence conducted by his
lawyer, Gianni Bove, was accepted, and the teenager was granted house arrest.
[@1]
