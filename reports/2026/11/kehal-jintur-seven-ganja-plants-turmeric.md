Jintur police seized seven cannabis plants that a 35-year-old farmer from Kehal
had grown without a licence among the turmeric in his field in Kehal shivar,
Parbhani district. [@1] The seven plants weighed 14 kg 150 g, and police put
their market value at ₹7,07,500. [@1] The raid was carried out on the field
bearing survey number 98/1, by sub-inspector Ingole and constables Nasib Pathan,
Chaudhari and Kamble. [@1] Jintur police station registered a case over the
cultivation. [@1] The raid was reported in a daily published on 6 November 2026,
which dated the action to Thursday, 5 November. [@1]

The case is one of several cannabis-in-field cases reported in Parbhani district
in the previous month, in the Gangakhed, Jintur and Purna talukas. [@1] Among
them, police found 153 kg of cannabis worth ₹73 lakh in Undegaon shivar in the
Daithana police station area, seized plants worth ₹1.21 crore at Mohkhed in
Jintur taluka, seized plants worth ₹3.88 lakh from a farmer's field at Avai in
Purna taluka, and seized plants worth ₹8.25 lakh from a turmeric crop at Kanha
shivar in Jintur taluka. [@1] The district cases show cannabis being planted
among turmeric, cotton and sugarcane crops. [@1]
