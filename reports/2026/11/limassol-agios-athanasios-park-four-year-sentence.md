Limassol Criminal Court on Thursday jailed a 24-year-old man for four years
after he admitted possessing cocaine and cannabis with intent to supply. [@1]
The man admitted possessing 76 grammes of cocaine and 523 grammes of cannabis
for supply to others. [@1] He received concurrent prison sentences, the longest
being four years for possessing more than half a kilo of cannabis with intent to
supply. [@1] The four-year sentence runs from 29 July 2025, when he was taken
into custody. [@1]

The case came to light on 29 July 2025, when the Anti-Drugs Unit (YKAN) in
Limassol received information about drug dealing at a park in Agios Athanasios.
[@1] Drug squad officers and the Limassol Crime Prevention Team found three
people at the park, who ran off in the dark and escaped. [@1] Officers seized a
plastic bag holding packages of cannabis with a gross weight of more than two
kilos, along with 79 grammes of cocaine, 100 ecstasy pills, 1,790 euros, a
precision scale, adhesive tape and empty plastic bags. [@1] His 29-year-old
co-defendant was jailed for seven years in February. [@1]

The defendant sentenced in February 2026 received concurrent terms of four and
three years, the longest seven years for possessing 2 kilograms and 355 grams of
cannabis for supply to others. [@2] That defendant also admitted possessing 76
grams of cocaine and ecstasy tablets weighing a total of 42 grams with the
intention of supplying them to others. [@2] The defence told the court that the
24-year-old had acted recklessly and without much planning, while in a
vulnerable position and seeking to secure drugs for his own use. [@1] In
mitigation the court took into account his guilty plea, his remorse, his clean
criminal record and his personal circumstances. [@1]
