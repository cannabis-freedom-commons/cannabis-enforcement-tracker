Badnapur police seized three cannabis plants weighing 5.65 kg from a cotton
field at Gevrai Bazar in Badnapur tehsil, Jalna district, on Thursday. [@1] One
person was arrested in connection with the case. [@1]

Police put the value of the seized plants at ₹2.80 lakh. [@1] Police Inspector
MT Survase received a confidential tip that a resident of Gevrai Bazar had
cultivated cannabis in his field, Survey No. 90, and was watering and
fertilising the plants regularly. [@1]

A police team led by Police Sub-Inspector Santosh Kuklare carried out a surprise
raid on the farm, accompanied by Naib Tehsildar Tayde and Deputy Agriculture
Officer Mohan Auti. [@1] The team found the cannabis growing among the cotton in
the accused's field and seized the plants. [@1] Police arrested the man and
began legal proceedings against him. [@1]
