Police in Akola arrested one suspect and obtained two days of police custody
from a court following a narcotics raid in the Old City area. [@1] Officers from
the Old City police station seized 1.494 kilograms of ganja during the raid.
[@1]

The seizure was carried out as part of Operation Prahar-Narcostrike, a
district-wide anti-narcotics campaign conducted by the Akola police force. [@1]
