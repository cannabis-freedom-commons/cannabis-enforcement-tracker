Louisiana Tech University police arrested 21-year-old Elijah Brooks of
Shreveport at 10:29 a.m. on Nov. 6 after a report of a man smoking marijuana in
a red car in a campus parking lot. [@1] An officer was dispatched at about 10:10
a.m. to a campus parking lot, found Brooks as the only person inside the red
car, and noted an odor he immediately recognized as marijuana. [@1]

Brooks handed the officer a partially consumed blunt and pointed out more
marijuana in a bag next to the driver's seat. [@1] The marijuana was seized and
weighed at South Hall, with a total weight of 28.6 grams. [@1] Brooks was
arrested on a charge of possession of marijuana on a university campus. [@1]

The account comes from an arrest affidavit released by a law enforcement agency
as public information, and Brooks is presumed innocent until proven guilty in
court. [@1]
