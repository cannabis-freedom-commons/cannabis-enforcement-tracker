Police Scotland arrested and charged a 26-year-old man after recovering cannabis and other drugs from Ruby Street in Glasgow. [@1,2,3,4]

Police in Glasgow arrested and charged a 26-year-old man following a police bust
in the city. [@1] The 26-year-old man is due to appear at Glasgow Sheriff Court
on Tuesday, 3 November. [@1,5]

Officers executed a search warrant and raided an address on Ruby Street on
Sunday, 1 November. [@1,5] The Ruby Street raid recovered cocaine, cannabis and
MDMA along with a five-figure sum of cash. [@1,5] Police gave the seized cocaine,
cannabis and MDMA an estimated combined street value of around £9,200. [@1,5] The
combined £9,200 valuation covers all three seized drugs together, not cannabis
alone. [@1]

Detective Sergeant Poppy Ford said drugs cause misery in communities and the
recovery highlights police commitment to remove drugs from the streets. [@1]
Ford said information from the public is vital and asked anyone with information
to report it to police. [@1] She said anyone with concerns can contact Police
Scotland on 101 or call Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111. [@1] Police
Scotland is the force appealing for information about drug activity after the
Ruby Street recovery. [@1]
