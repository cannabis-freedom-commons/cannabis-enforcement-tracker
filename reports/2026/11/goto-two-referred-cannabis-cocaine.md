Police additionally referred two men to the Nagasaki District Public Prosecutors
Office Goto branch by 2 November 2026 on suspicion of cannabis and cocaine
possession and use. [@2] The two are a 30-year-old self-employed man from Kiyama
town in Saga Prefecture and a 24-year-old construction worker from Onojo City in
Fukuoka Prefecture. [@2]

The August case cited suspected violation of the Narcotics Control Law for
possessing dried cannabis in Goto City. [@1] Police say the pair were arrested
red-handed at about 5 p.m. on 13 August in Goto City for possessing about 2.9
grams of dried cannabis. [@2] They had been visiting Goto on a trip and were
travelling by car when officers conducting traffic enforcement questioned them,
which led to discovery of the cannabis. [@2] Further investigation established
suspected additional possession and use of dried cannabis and cocaine, bringing
the referrals to five counts in total by 2 November. [@2]

Both men have admitted the allegations. [@2] They reportedly told investigators
the drugs were for their own smoking. [@2] Police say they are continuing to
investigate the supply route. [@2]
