Agents of the Vicenza railway police section arrested a 27-year-old Italian man
resident in Veneto at the city's railway station on suspicion of possessing
drugs for sale. [@1] Officers stopped him for a routine passenger identification
check just after he stepped off a train, and he showed signs of irritation and
nervousness and tried to avoid the check with implausible excuses. [@1] The
officers took him to the railway police offices, where a personal search found
more than 50 grams of hashish, a precision scale, cash and a mobile phone in his
clothing pockets. [@1] Police seized all the material as they considered it
clearly intended for selling the drug. [@1]

Checks showed the man already had previous drug offences on his record, as well
as offences against property and against the person. [@1] He was arrested for
drug possession and trafficking and taken to the Casa Circondariale, Vicenza's
prison, at the disposal of the judicial authority in Vicenza. [@1] The police
said the arrest was made at the city's railway station during territorial
patrols arranged in the previous week. [@2]
