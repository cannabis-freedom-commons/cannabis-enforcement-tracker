Royal Gendarmerie officers arrested two suspects at Douar Mahir in the commune
of Bni Garfat, Larache province, on suspicion of holding and processing kif,
Morocco's cannabis product. [@2] The gendarmes attached to the territorial
centre of Bni Garfat caught the two in the act of crushing and breaking up
cannabis, in what L'Opinion described as a clandestine kif-processing unit. [@1]
The arrest was made in the morning of Wednesday 4 November 2026 by officers of
the Bni Garfat territorial centre. [@4]

A search carried out on the public prosecutor's instructions found 1,360
kilograms of kif in the form of stems and 900 kilograms of already crushed
cannabis, 2,260 kilograms in all. [@2] Le360 reported that the material was
found in a house in the Bni Garfat area and that the whole quantity was
destroyed on the spot. [@3] L'Opinion reported that gendarmes had intercepted a
2.26-tonne kif shipment in Bni Garfat commune on Wednesday morning, exposing
significant grinding and processing activity in the province. [@1]

The two were placed in police custody for a judicial inquiry supervised by the
competent public prosecutor, which is to establish the circumstances of the
activity and any possible ramifications. [@2] A mixed commission supervised the
destruction of the seized quantities under the legal procedures in force. [@1]
