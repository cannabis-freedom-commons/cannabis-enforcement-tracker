During a check of a suspicious vehicle in the Pendik district of Istanbul,
Turkish police found 336 kilos and 550 grams of skunk, described as a strong
cannabis variant. [@1,3,4] The drugs were in the car and were fully seized, and
officers arrested one person. [@1,3]

A second account of the same check likewise reports 336 kilos and 550 grams of
the strong cannabis variant found in Pendik and one person arrested. [@2] Police
had not disclosed where the load came from, where it was headed, or whether the
suspect was part of a larger drug network. [@1] It was also unclear what
specific suspicion applied to the arrested person, including whether he acted
alone or was involved in a larger drug system. [@2]

In Türkiye, the production, trade and transport of cannabis are prohibited. [@1]
Anyone who sells, transports, stores or possesses drugs for trade purposes
without a licence faces at least ten years' imprisonment under the law. [@1]
Possession for own use is also punishable, although prosecution may be deferred
under conditions with treatment or supervision. [@1]

The detained driver was identified only as Ü.A. [@4] The suspect was expected to be referred to the courthouse after statement procedures at the police station. [@4]
