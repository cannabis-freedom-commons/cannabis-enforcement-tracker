Fuerza Pública officers seized suspected drugs and cash during a police
intervention at the Miraflores dike in Taras, Cartago. [@1] When officers
entered the area, they saw three people dressed in black who fled through the
brush toward the river. [@1] The report names no arrests, saying officers
instead searched the area and found a green bag. [@1]

Officers seized nine wrappers of apparent marijuana weighing about 14 grams
each, about 126 grams in total. [@1] They also seized 158 doses of marijuana
whose weight was not reported. [@1] The same bag held 342 doses of apparent
crack, 10 doses of apparent cocaine, 52 doses of apparent tusi and three
apparent ecstasy pills, which are mentioned only to explain the mixed seizure.
[@1] Officers also seized ₡69,000 in cash described as alleged proceeds from
psychotropic sales. [@1]

The whole seizure was placed at the disposal of the judicial authorities, with
no court outcome reported. [@1]
