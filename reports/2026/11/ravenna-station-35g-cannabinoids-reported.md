State Police in Ravenna reported a young Italian for possession of a narcotic
for the purpose of dealing. [@1] The Polizia Ferroviaria, the railway police,
carried out the check and are regularly assigned to watch Ravenna station and
protect safety there. [@1] He was reported at liberty for possession of a
narcotic for the purpose of dealing and for violating two removal orders, and
the substance was seized. [@1]

The Questura said the check took place in recent days, not on a named date. [@1]
In the middle of the afternoon a strong smell in the central hall of Ravenna
railway station drew Polfer officers, who intervened at once to find where it
came from. [@1] Their search identified an 18-year-old on whom the first
searches found suspected substances. [@1] Analysis at the railway police post in
Ravenna confirmed the material was illicit, about 35 grams of cannabinoids in
all. [@1] What he was carrying was also described as several doses of marijuana,
and the smell in the central hall is what prompted the Polfer officers. [@2] A
strong smell in the central hall of the station is what drew the Polfer officers
to him. [@2]

Further checks showed he was already known for previous drug offences. [@1] The
local account likewise said about 35 grams of cannabinoids were found on the
18-year-old, who was then reported at liberty. [@2]
