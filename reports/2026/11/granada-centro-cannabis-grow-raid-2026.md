National Police arrested two people during the operation. [@1] The operation ran
through the morning of Thursday, 5 November 2026. [@1] The target indoor
cannabis grow was in a flat on Calle Doctor Buenaventura Carreras, near Camino
de Ronda. [@1] Ideal separately reported about 1,500 cannabis plants and more
than twice as many cuttings. [@1]

The grow occupied a unit of about 400 square metres on the ground floor of a
residential building, accessed through the garage. [@2] The operation began at
noon in a busy part of the city, according to Granada Hoy. [@2] Endesa
technicians joined at around 15:30 to check whether illegal power connections
supplied the grow. [@2] Residents told Granada Hoy that the unit had been rented
to a new tenant only about two months earlier. [@2] Ideal explained that the
cuttings were pieces of a healthy mother plant that developed their own roots
and had the same legal status as plants. [@1]
