Pabna District Detective Branch police arrested Md Abdul Majid, 45, a
professional drug dealer, during a special anti-narcotics operation. [@1] The
operation was carried out at about 3:15 pm on Wednesday 11 November 2026 at
Jalalpur Satpukur Mor, an area under Pabna Sadar police station. [@1]

A detective team acting on secret information learned that a man was standing on
the paved road in front of a straw store at Jalalpur Satpukur Mor to sell ganja,
and raided the spot immediately. [@1] Abdul Majid tried to run away when he
noticed the police and was caught in a physical struggle. [@1] Police searched
his body in front of local witnesses and recovered 500 grams of ganja, the local
term for cannabis. [@1]

Abdul Majid is the son of the late Abdul Jabbar Sardar of Jalalpur Satpukur Mor
in Pabna Sadar upazila. [@1] During initial questioning he said he had collected
the ganja from various places under Pabna Sadar police station and had been
selling it to drug dealers and users in different parts of the district. [@1]
The district detective branch in-charge, Officer-in-Charge Md Abdus Salam, said
a regular case had been filed against Abdul Majid under the Narcotics Control
Act and he was sent to court. [@1] Salam said the zero-tolerance policy on drugs
remains in force and that such special detective branch operations would
continue across the district. [@1]
