Dhaka Metropolitan Police (DMP), the police force for the capital, arrested 405
people and filed 62 cases during raids across Dhaka over a 24-hour period. [@1]
The arrests were made during operations in different police station areas of
Dhaka on Sunday, according to a DMP press release issued on the day of
publication. [@1] The Mirpur division accounted for the most arrests with 147,
followed by Tejgaon with 59, Gulshan with 43, Lalbagh with 40, Motijheel with
36, Wari with 34, Uttara with 20 and Ramna with 17. [@1] The Detective Branch
arrested six people while the Special Reserve Unit arrested three. [@1] Police
said further legal action against those arrested was under way. [@1]

During the same operations, police seized 5,992 yaba tablets, 41.5 kilograms of
cannabis and 22 packets of heroin. [@1] They also recovered four mobile phones,
3,112 SIM cards, a truck, a bus, a key, 26 ceiling fans and 57,685 taka in cash.
[@1] Police also rescued one victim during the raids. [@1]

The figures are the police's own counts from the 24-hour sweep as reported
through BSS, with the cannabis component standing separately at 41.5 kilograms
alongside the yaba and heroin seizures. [@1]
