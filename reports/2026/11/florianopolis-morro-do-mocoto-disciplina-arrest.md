Military Police officers policing the Morro do Mocotó in Florianópolis on
Thursday 5 November arrested a 33-year-old man the force describes as the hill's
"disciplina", the gang member who enforces its rules. [@1] The arrest came on
the first day of Operação Lei e Ordem (Operation Law and Order), an operation
run jointly by the 4th Military Police Battalion, the Riot Battalion and the
BOPE special operations battalion to suppress organised crime in the state
capital. [@1] Police said the man resisted arrest by throwing his two mobile
phones to the ground, and he was detained for drug trafficking and resisting
arrest. [@2]

There was already a warrant out for him for drug trafficking, association for
trafficking, robbery and illegal possession of a firearm. [@1] According to the
Military Police he belongs to the Primeiro Grupo Catarinense (PGC), the largest
criminal organisation in Santa Catarina, and has 20 recorded incident reports in
the state, most of them for trafficking and illegal possession of firearms. [@2]
The operation's cannabis seizure weighed 845 grams, as stated in the case
summary. [@1]

Officers seized 845 grams of cannabis and the two phones, which were to be
examined by forensic experts. [@2] The man was taken to the Civil Police
station, which handles the applicable legal procedures. [@3]
