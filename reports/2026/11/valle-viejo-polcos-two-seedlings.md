Around 12:30 on 2 November 2026, clerks from Judicial Unit No. 10, assisted by
members of the Motorized Action Group (GAM-Pampa), were carrying out a judicial
measure at a dwelling in the La Playa neighbourhood of Polcos, in the Valle
Viejo department. [@1] The dwelling is described as being in the La Playa
neighbourhood of the locality of Polcos, in the Valle Viejo department. [@1]
While at the property, the officers observed plants similar to the Cannabis
sativa variety being cultivated there. [@1]

According to police sources, the officers who were carrying out the measure
immediately gave intervention to their peers in the Dangerous Drugs Directorate
to examine the suspected plants. [@1] After performing tasks within their
speciality, those drugs officers seized two marijuana seedlings that were being
cultivated inside the La Playa property. [@1] Police described the seized plants
as two marijuana seedlings under cultivation, making seedlings the reported form
of the cannabis found. [@1]

The Federal Court, under Judge Miguel Ángel Contreras, took cognizance of the
matter, and further directives in the case were issued from that court. [@1]
