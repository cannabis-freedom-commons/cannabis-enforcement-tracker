311.3 kg of ganja in a truck at the Suryamandal check post in Barachatti
police-station area, Gaya district, on Thursday 5 November 2026. [@1] One
smuggler was arrested and the truck was seized. [@1]

The excise department put the seized ganja's estimated value at about one crore
