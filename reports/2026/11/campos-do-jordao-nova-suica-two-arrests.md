Civil Police arrested two people for drug trafficking on the afternoon of 4
November 2026 in the Nova Suíça neighbourhood of Campos do Jordão. [@1]
Investigators say the two belong to a criminal group responsible for
distributing drugs in the municipality. [@1] The suspects were inside a vehicle
allegedly negotiating Dry, which T7 News describes as a concentrated cannabis
derivative, when officers approached them. [@2] Officers seized a significant
quantity of Dry, portions of marijuana and a notebook with notes linked by
police to the group's financial movements. [@1] Both men resisted arrest, with
one trying to flee and the other fighting officers, so officers used force to
restrain them. [@1] Both were taken to the Civil Police station and remain at
the disposal of the courts. [@1]

Police link the arrests to a 20 October operation in which a woman was arrested
in Céu Azul on suspicion of being responsible for storing the group's drugs.
[@1] That earlier operation had also seized pressed marijuana, Dry and Ice
derivatives, cultivated marijuana plants, scales, phones, packaging material and
trafficking ledgers. [@1] The woman from the first operation remains detained
while police continue investigating to identify other people involved in selling
and distributing drugs in Campos do Jordão. [@1]
