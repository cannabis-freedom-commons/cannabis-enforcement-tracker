Saudi Arabia's General Directorate of Border Guard said it had foiled attempts
to smuggle 76 kg of hashish. [@1] The same statement said the smugglers of the
hashish were arrested, without giving a number. [@1]

The Border Guard's own post put the haul at 76 kg of hashish alongside 366,280
amphetamine tablets. [@2] It also listed 163,750 tablets under medical-trade
controls, a separate group of medicines that is not cannabis. [@2] The Border
Guard published the announcement with a video clip on its official account on 9
November 2026. [@2] The outlet carried the statement as it appeared, so the two
accounts are not independent. [@1]
