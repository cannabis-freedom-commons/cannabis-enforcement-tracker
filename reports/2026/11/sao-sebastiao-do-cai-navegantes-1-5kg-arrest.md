Military Police officers arrested a 21-year-old man for drug trafficking in the
Navegantes neighbourhood of São Sebastião do Caí, Rio Grande do Sul, on the
afternoon of Saturday 7 November 2026. [@1] The arrest was made by a team of the
Brigada Militar's Força Tática, the state military police's tactical unit, as
officers approached him. [@1]

In the approach officers found three portions of marijuana weighing 83 grams and
one portion of cocaine weighing 6 grams. [@1] Officers then found two bricks of
marijuana weighing about 1.5 kilograms, a roll of cling film and a notebook with
notes. [@1]

He was taken to the emergency police station in Montenegro, where a flagrante
arrest record was drawn up, and he was held at the disposal of the courts in the
prison system. [@1]
