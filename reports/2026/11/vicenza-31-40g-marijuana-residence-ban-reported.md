Carabinieri from the Radiomobile Section of the operational and mobile unit
(N.O.R.) in Vicenza reported a 31-year-old Gambian man who had no fixed address
and previous drug-related police records, accusing him of unlawful possession of
drugs for sale and of breaching a residence ban. [@1] The officers reached the
room the man occupied in the late morning, after monitoring him, and found him
trying to hide a packet in his jacket pocket. [@1]

A personal search, extended to the room, led to the criminal seizure of two
packets containing marijuana weighing a total of about 40 grams. [@1] The checks
confirmed that the man, already the subject of an oral warning (avviso orale, a
police warning issued to a person considered socially dangerous), was in the
municipality of Vicenza in breach of a residence ban imposed on him. [@1] The
man was reported at liberty rather than arrested, and the Carabinieri informed
the judicial authority, which will decide on the accusations. [@2] Tviweb
reported that the operation was carried out on the late morning of Friday 6
November as part of territory checks aimed at drug dealing and at compliance
with judicial orders. [@2]

The accounts come from a press release by the provincial Carabinieri command in
Vicenza, which recalled that, under the presumption of innocence, the man's
guilt will be established only by a final conviction. [@1]
