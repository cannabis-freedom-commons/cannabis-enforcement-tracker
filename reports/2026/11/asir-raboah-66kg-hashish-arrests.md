Saudi Border Guard land patrols in the Al-Raboah sector of the Asir region
arrested four Ethiopian nationals who were accused of smuggling 66 kilograms of
hashish through the sector. [@1] The four were described as violators of the
kingdom's border security law. [@2]

The Border Guard said it had completed the initial legal procedures against the
four and handed them, together with the seized hashish, to the competent
authority to take the necessary measures. [@1] The same announcement covered a
separate operation in the same sector in which four other Ethiopians were
arrested with 80 kilograms of khat, a plant that is not cannabis. [@2]

The Saudi security authorities renewed their appeal to citizens and residents to
report drug smuggling and trafficking through the emergency numbers 911, 999 and
994, the anti-narcotics directorate's 995 line and an email address. [@1] They
said all reports are handled in strict confidence and that the person reporting
bears no liability. [@2]
