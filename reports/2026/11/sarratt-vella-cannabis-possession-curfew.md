Claire Vella, 50, of Ash Meadow Farm, Deadmans Ash Lane, Sarratt, Hertfordshire,
admitted possession of cannabis at High Wycombe Magistrates' Court. [@1] In the
same hearing she admitted driving a motor vehicle above the specified controlled
drug limit and driving while disqualified. [@1] The round-up does not say that
the drug in the driving offence was cannabis. [@1]

The court ordered the cannabis forfeited. [@1] She was given a curfew as part of
a community order for a four-month period. [@1] She was ordered to pay £85. [@1]
