Saligao police arrested a 49-year-old Mumbai resident during a narcotics raid at
Marra, Pilerne, in Bardez taluka, Goa. [@1,4] Police identified him as Danny Frank
Braganza, 49, a resident of Andheri East, Mumbai. [@2,4] A team led by police
sub-inspector Akshay Fatarpekar acted on specific information, accompanied by
two panch witnesses, local residents who witness police searches. [@2] Braganza
was placed under arrest at 9.50pm after police said they observed the Supreme
Court's prescribed guidelines. [@2]

Police allegedly found a greenish leafy substance with fruiting and flowering
tops, suspected to be ganja. [@1] A case was registered under the NDPS Act,
India's Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act. [@3,4] Further
investigation is in progress with Fatarpekar at Saligao police station. [@1]

The Mapusa court granted Braganza bail in the ganja possession case. [@4] Defence counsel Advocate Salil Lawande said the seized quantity was within the legally defined small-quantity limit of one kilogram. [@4] Lawande argued that with the seizure completed and the evidence in police custody, continued detention was no longer necessary. [@4] The prosecution objected, citing his residence outside Goa and apprehending he could evade investigation, influence witnesses or interfere with identifying the drug source. [@4] The court observed that the restrictive bail provisions under Section 37 of the NDPS Act were not attracted in a small-quantity case. [@4] It also noted he was in judicial custody and was not required for further custodial questioning. [@4] The court allowed the bail application, holding prosecution concerns could be addressed through safeguards. [@4] He must execute a personal bond of ₹15,000 and furnish one local surety for the same amount. [@4] He must attend Saligao Police Station for six consecutive days, assist the investigation and obtain court permission before travelling outside Goa or India. [@4]
