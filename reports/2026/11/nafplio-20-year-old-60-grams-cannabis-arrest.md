Officers of the Nafplio Subdirectorate for the Suppression and Investigation of
Crimes arrested a 20-year-old Greek man in Nafplio on the evening of 9 November
2026. [@1] The Argolida police directorate recorded the incident as one drug
arrest. [@2]

The man possessed cannabis described as chocolate-type cannabis, the term Greek
police use for processed cannabis, with a total weight of 60 grams. [@1] Police
seized the 60 grams of cannabis. [@1]

The arrest took place in Nafplio and was made by officers of the Nafplio
investigative unit. [@1]
