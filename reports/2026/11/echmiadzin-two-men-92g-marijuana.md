Officers of the Vagharshapat department of the Community Police received
operational information on 6 November 2026 that two residents of the city of
Echmiadzin, a 54-year-old and a 63-year-old, stored and sold drugs and also
intended that day to sell drugs to a particular person. [@1] The police press
service of the Ministry of Internal Affairs said that, as a result of measures
taken by law enforcement, those men were found on Zoravar Andranik Street,
detained and taken with a Lexus to the Vagharshapat department. [@1] Patrol
officers of the Armavir Province battalion took part in the operation that found
the men on Zoravar Andranik Street and detained them with the Lexus. [@2]

Two packages containing a mass resembling a narcotic were in plain view inside
the car. [@1] The Interior Ministry said the two packages of drug-like mass were
in a clearly visible part of the car. [@2] The Lexus was moved to a specially
guarded area, and the detained men were handed over to the preliminary
investigation body. [@1] A criminal proceeding was opened, and an inspection of
the car in that proceeding found 92 grams of a mass resembling marijuana. [@1]
The Interior Ministry said the same inspection found 92 grams of a mass
resembling marijuana and that the preliminary investigation was continuing. [@2]
The preliminary investigation was still under way when the police account was
published. [@1]
