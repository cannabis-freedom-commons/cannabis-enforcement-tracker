Cairo's First Criminal Court circuit sitting at New Cairo sentenced two
defendants to three years in prison for drug trafficking, hashish possession for
use and unlicensed possession of a bladed weapon in First Al-Salam. [@1] The
prosecution referred a 36-year-old worker and a 16-year-old worker to the
criminal court in case 2853 of 2026 for First Al-Salam felonies. [@1] The
referral alleged that on 20 February 2026 in the First Al-Salam police-station
district they held an indazole-carboxamide-derivative drug for trafficking and
hashish for use, plus cutter blades without a licence. [@1]

A First Al-Salam detective said he received information that the pair were
trafficking drugs, went to where they were and saw them sharing a tobacco
cigarette smelling of drugs. [@1] He said he arrested both, took the cigarette
from the first defendant's hand and a bladed weapon from the second, and found
bags of drugs on the first and other drug bags, cash and another weapon on the
second. [@1] The central chemical laboratories reported that the seized material
contained an indazole-carboxamide derivative listed in Schedule 1 of the drug
law, in weights of 101.34 grams and 78.50 grams. [@1] The same report found that
the seized tobacco roll with burn marks held the same drug. [@1] Urine-sample
reports were positive for a hashish metabolite and phenethylamine derivatives
for the first defendant and for a phenethylamine derivative for the second. [@1]
The prosecution additionally charged the first defendant with possessing a
phenethylamine-derivative drug for use. [@1]

After investigation and evidence, the prosecution sent both to the criminal
court with continued pretrial detention, and the court sentenced them to three
years. [@1] The verdict was issued under presiding judge Khaled Mohamed Radwan
with judges Mahmoud Abdoh El-Qarmouti, Samir Salah El-Din and Tamer Ahmed Reda.
