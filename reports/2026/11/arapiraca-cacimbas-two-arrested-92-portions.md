Two men were arrested on suspicion of drug trafficking on the afternoon of 8
November 2026 in the Cacimbas neighbourhood of Arapiraca. [@1] A Rocam team
carried out the approach. [@1] The two suspects were on a red Honda Start
motorcycle and appeared uneasy when they noticed the officers approaching. [@1]
Rocam is the Ronda Ostensiva com Apoio de Motocicletas, a motorcycle-supported
patrol unit. [@1]

Officers found 92 portions of marijuana weighing about 110 grams, plus a cell
phone and R$64 in cash. [@1] During observation the officers noticed the
occupants were uneasy and were carrying a white plastic bag. [@2] The red Honda
Start motorcycle the men were riding was also seized. [@1]

The occurrence was recorded at about 15h30 on Rua Costa Gama. [@2] The team
called for support from another patrol to take the suspects to the Central de
Polícia. [@2] Both men were taken to the Central de Polícia and booked for drug
trafficking under article 33 of Law 11.343/2006. [@1]
