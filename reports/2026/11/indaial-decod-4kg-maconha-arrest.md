On Thursday, 12 November, a man was arrested in flagrante for drug trafficking
in Indaial, Santa Catarina. [@1] The arrest was made by civil police officers of
the Anti-Drug Police Station of Blumenau (DECOD), at a vehicle-wash in the city.
[@1] The operation began after the Civil Police received an anonymous tip about
possible drug trafficking at the establishment. [@1]

During the investigation officers found a man in possession of about 11 grams of
marijuana, along with messages suggesting involvement in selling narcotics. [@1]
In the search of the suspect's residence officers seized about 4.3 kilograms of
marijuana and 21 grams of cocaine. [@1] Officers also found a precision scale,
materials used to package the drugs and a notebook with notes related to the
criminal activity. [@1]

The man was arrested in flagrante and taken to the police station, where the
applicable legal procedures were carried out. [@1]
