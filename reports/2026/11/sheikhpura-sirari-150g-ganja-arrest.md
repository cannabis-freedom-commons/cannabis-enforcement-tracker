Police in Sirari bazaar, in Sheikhpura district of Bihar, seized 150 grams of
ganja. [@1] The ganja was found at a paan gumti, a small roadside kiosk. [@1]
Officers arrested Doman Singh, also known as Prabhakar Singh, in connection with
the case. [@1]

The kiosk stands on Purana Station Road. [@2] Police said the raid was carried
out within 500 metres of an educational institution. [@2] Police said the
accused sold ganja under the cover of the paan shop. [@2]

The raid was led by Sirari police station chief Jayaprakash Tiwari and police
sub-inspector Nikhil Bhaskar, police said. [@2] Tiwari said police registered a
first information report under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances
Act, India's main drug law. [@2] The report was registered under that law and
other relevant sections, he said. [@2] Police said the arrested man lives in
Barma village, in the Sirari police station area. [@2]

Police said they had started further action in the case. [@2]
