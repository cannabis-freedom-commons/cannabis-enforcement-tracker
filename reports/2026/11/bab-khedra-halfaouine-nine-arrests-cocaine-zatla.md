The Bab Souika judicial police brigade broke up a drug-dealing network operating
between the Bab Khedra and Halfaouine areas of Tunis. [@1] The operation
followed detailed investigations and continuous field surveillance of the
movements of several suspected individuals. [@1] In simultaneous
Saturday-evening raids on several monitored points, officers arrested two drug
dealers and detained seven of their customers who were in the process of buying
drugs. [@1]

The operation seized significant quantities of cocaine and cannabis, known
locally as zatla, that had been prepared for sale. [@1] Officers also seized two
electronic scales used to weigh and divide the drugs. [@1] Officers seized
significant sums of money suspected to be proceeds of the dealing. [@1] Officers
seized a delivery car that was being used to distribute drugs to customers
contacted through social media. [@1]

After consulting the public prosecutor at the Court of First Instance, all those
detained were held pending investigation while inquiries continue to uncover the
rest of the network and its possible extensions. [@1]
