Gendarmes of the Mbalmayo company arrested Issa A., a 26-year-old known as
'Anaconda', at around 2 pm on 3 November 2026 in a restaurant in Mbalmayo where
he was eating. [@1] The gendarmes had been looking for him since August 2026,
when an informant reported him as a drug seller in the town, and a search notice
was issued in his name. [@1]

A body search found four grams of crack, called 'caillou' locally, on him. [@1]
Gendarmes then searched his home in the Haoussa quarter and found what they
described as a packaging laboratory. [@1] The search turned up a large quantity
of Indian hemp, some of it loose and some already rolled into balls ready for
sale, as well as tramadol, diazepam, two gas cylinders and packaging equipment.
[@1] Lebledparle reported that he avoided a fixed sales point and used young
motorcycle couriers to distribute his products. [@2]

Two gas cylinders and a large sum of money were also seized, but the exact
amount of the money and the quantities of the substances were not published.
[@2] Lebledparle said investigations were continuing to establish the extent of
his activities and to identify possible accomplices, including the people who
distributed the products. [@2] The Cameroon Tribune said he was about to be
jailed for the fifth time for drug trafficking. [@1]
