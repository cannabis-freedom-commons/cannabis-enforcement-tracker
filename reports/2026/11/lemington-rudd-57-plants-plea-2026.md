Police attended Benjamin Rudd's then address on Northumberland Road in Lemington
in October last year and found he had been cultivating cannabis. [@1] Officers
seized 57 cannabis plants found within the premises and arrested the 39-year-old
Rudd. [@1] Rudd, now of Beacon Rise in Gateshead, appeared at Newcastle
Magistrates' Court this week and pleaded guilty to producing a class B drug on
the basis that it was for his own personal use. [@1]

Prosecutor Jade Houston said the Crown did not accept that basis and maintained
Rudd must have been growing some of the cannabis for onward sale. [@1] Houston
told the court the Crown disputed the personal-use claim because of the amount
involved. [@1] District Judge Paul Currer ordered a Newton hearing to determine
why Rudd was growing the cannabis, saying the finding would make a material
difference to sentence. [@1]

The judge said the court would decide whether the cannabis was for onward sale
or personal use as the basis for sentence. [@1] Rudd's Newton hearing was listed
for February 2 at North Tyneside Magistrates' Court. [@1] He was released on
unconditional bail in the meantime. [@1]
