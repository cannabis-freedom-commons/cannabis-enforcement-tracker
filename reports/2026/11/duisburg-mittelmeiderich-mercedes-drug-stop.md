A witness alerted Duisburg police on the afternoon of 9 November 2026 after
observing suspected drug dealing at Schwarzwaldstrasse/Brueckelstrasse. [@2]
Officers found a parked vehicle there occupied by two men. [@2] Officers checked
a person walking away from the car, who confirmed that he had bought drugs from
the car and handed over a press-seal baggie containing cannabis. [@2] Police
then checked the two occupants of the Mercedes, aged 38 and 59, and found
cocaine, cannabis and smartphones inside the car. [@2]

Officers found cash and cocaine on the 59-year-old. [@2] Officers found cash in
scene-typical denominations on the 38-year-old. [@2] Police seized the items and
obtained court search warrants for both suspects' homes in Meiderich. [@2] The
search of the 59-year-old's home found nothing. [@2] The search of the
38-year-old's home found a high four-figure cash sum and press-seal baggies with
green contents, which officers also seized. [@2]

Both men now face criminal proceedings for illegal trafficking in narcotics in
not small quantity. [@2] Investigations by Criminal Commissariat 24 are
continuing. [@2] The WAZ summarized the case as a 38-year-old and a 59-year-old
dealing drugs from a Mercedes in Duisburg, with officers intervening and finding
much cash. [@1]
