A 49-year-old man was arrested on the night of 3 November 2026 during a Comando
de Operações de Divisas operation on the GO-184 in Jataí in southwestern Goiás.
[@1] Officers found an open arrest warrant against the driver and 17 portions of
a hashish-like substance hidden in the fuel-tank compartment of his vehicle.
[@1] The man, who is from Brasília, was driving a Volkswagen Virtus and said he
had left Serranópolis in Goiás for the federal capital. [@1] Officers identified
the warrant as an order issued by the 3rd Family and Orphans and Successions
Court of Taguatinga in the Federal District. [@1] The police statement did not
specify the reason for the warrant. [@1]

A specialised inspection found six volumes hidden around the fuel-tank area.
[@1] The material was packed in a transparent plastic bag and protected by black
rubber balloons. [@1] Officers examining the packaging identified 17
individualised portions of a substance with characteristics similar to hashish.
[@1] Each portion was wrapped in white latex gloves. [@1] The police statement
gave no total weight for the seizure. [@1] The Comando de Operações de Divisas
made the seizure on the GO-184 and arrested the driver. [@2]

The driver allegedly said he would receive R$10,000 to carry the drug from Ponta
Porã in Mato Grosso do Sul to Brasília in the Federal District. [@1] Although he
had initially said he left Serranópolis, he later pointed to Ponta Porã as the
origin of the paid transport. [@1] After the find the man was taken to the
Federal Police Department in Jataí. [@1] Officers also handed over the
Volkswagen Virtus, two mobile phones and R$1,212 in cash alongside the 17
suspected portions. [@1] The Federal Police took over the legal procedures
arising from the presentation of the case. [@1] No details were released on any
flagrante drug-trafficking charge, forensic result for the substance or later
court decision. [@1]
