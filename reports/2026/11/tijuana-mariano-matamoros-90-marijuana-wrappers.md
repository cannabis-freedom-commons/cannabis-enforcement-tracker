Municipal police arrested 22-year-old Jazmín “N” in Tijuana’s Mariano Matamoros
neighbourhood on 10 November 2026 after finding 90 suspected marijuana wrappers
in her backpack. [@2] Officers from the Secretariat of Security and Municipal
Citizen Protection (SSPCM) stopped her for an alleged violation of the municipal
police and government code, then conducted a precautionary inspection. [@2]

The backpack held three plastic bags containing 30 wrappers each, for a total of
90 packages of green, dry herb with characteristics similar to marijuana,
according to the municipal account. [@1] Police handed the woman and the seized
substance to the state prosecutor’s office (FGE), which was to continue the
investigation and determine her legal situation. [@2]

The SSPCM asked residents to report possible drug sale or distribution points
anonymously through the 089 telephone line. [@1] It also reminded the public
that its emergency button is available to request timely police assistance in
any situation of risk. [@1]
