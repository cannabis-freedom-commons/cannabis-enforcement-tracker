Two men were arrested for allegedly maintaining a cannabis plantation in the
Viannos area of Crete. [@1] One suspect is a 52-year-old local man and the other
is a 28-year-old man from Albania. [@1] They were arrested on the morning of
Thursday 29 October over alleged hashish cultivation inside a greenhouse at
Psari Forada in Viannos. [@1] After being brought before the prosecutor the
previous Friday, both men face felony-level prosecution. [@1] They were then
referred to the investigating judge, received a postponement to give their
statements, and remained in custody until then. [@1]

Police said 135 cannabis seedlings were found near the entrance and around the
greenhouse perimeter, carefully covered with natural vegetation. [@1] The
seedlings were up to 15 centimetres tall and were placed in plastic seedling
pots. [@1]

The seized cannabis plants were expected to be sent for laboratory examination
to the Forensic Sciences and Toxicology Unit laboratory of PAGNH. [@1]
Authorities treated the 135 plants as seized evidence pending those laboratory
examinations. [@1]
