Cuddalore district police arrested 502 people in 286 cases registered for the
trafficking and sale of narcotics in 2026, up to the end of October. [@2]
Officers seized 292 kg of ganja, the local term for cannabis, from those
arrested, along with 4,923 kg of gutkha, a banned chewing tobacco, and 4,328
intoxicating tablets. [@2] The district police said 292 kg of ganja had been
seized in the district so far this year. [@1]

The district police also seized 75 vehicles used to move the drugs, the Dinamani
account of the same statement reported. [@2] The figures were released in a
press statement by Cuddalore District Superintendent of Police Vivekanand Shukla
on Friday, 6 November 2026. [@2]

The same statement reported 60 people jailed under the Goondas Act, Tamil Nadu's
preventive-detention law, and life sentences given in murder and POCSO cases,
figures that are not drug seizures. [@1]
