The Guardia Civil, working with Jumilla's Local Police, dismantled a suspected
family clan accused of controlling three drug sale points in Jumilla's old town.
[@1,2,3,4] The operation, called Swart, led to the detention of nine people, five men
and four women. [@1,2,3,4] Those detained are under investigation for alleged drug
trafficking, money laundering and membership of a criminal organisation. [@1,2,4]
Officers carried out simultaneous searches in four homes and two industrial
warehouses in the municipality. [@1,2,3,4]

Officers seized 86 doses of hashish, the cannabis component recorded here. [@1,2,3,4]
The same searches recovered more than 700 doses of cocaine prepared for
distribution. [@1,2,3,4] Officers also seized nearly 51,000 euros in cash, along with
mobile phones, jewellery and documents, a high-end vehicle, simulated firearms
and precision scales and preparation materials. [@1,3,4]

The investigation began about two years ago after officers detected suspicious
drug movements in streets in Jumilla's old town in 2024. [@1,4] Investigators said
sellers used a self-service system that left doses at points on building facades
for buyers to collect while throwing payment inside. [@1,4] Officers also found
the group offered a home-delivery drug service. [@1,2,4] The investigation remains
open to establish the full scope of the group's activity. [@1,2,4] The nine
detainees retain the presumption of innocence absent a final judicial ruling.
[@1]

Evidence was passed to a court, whose warrants authorised the searches. [@4] Officers counted several properties and high-end vehicles in the suspects' possession. [@4]
