The arrest took place on the night of Friday 6 November 2026, at around 10pm, in
Comunidade Leonel Brizola in the Vila Luizão area of São Luís. [@2] Officers of
the 40th Battalion of the Military Police, deployed on Operation Muralha
Metropolitana, spotted a man on a motorcycle whose behaviour seemed suspicious.
[@1] He tried to escape but was overpowered and searched. [@1] The suspect
entered a house in the community, where officers contained him. [@2] Police
identified the rider as Émerson Lucas. [@2] On him, officers found R$1,000 in
cash and small portions of a marijuana-like substance. [@2] He was also carrying
an iPhone 14. [@2] A records check showed the phone was flagged as stolen,
prompting an initial arrest for receiving stolen goods. [@2] He was taken with
all the seized material to the Civil Police central duty station at Cohatrac for
the formal record. [@2] He remained in custody for drug trafficking. [@1]

A search of the house turned up eight pressed bricks of marijuana. [@2] Officers
also found portions of hashish, skank and cocaine. [@2] The seizure further
included a packet of hallucinogenic mushrooms, cocaine-like substances and five
packets of ecstasy. [@1] Police seized a Taurus G2C pistol in 9mm calibre. [@2]
They also seized two precision scales, rolls of plastic wrap and ziplock bags.
[@2] The grey Honda CG 160 motorcycle, which had no plates, was seized as well.
[@2]

The operation was part of Operation Muralha Metropolitana, an effort against
drug trafficking and irregular vehicles in the metropolitan region. [@2] The
40th Battalion's command coordinated the action as part of its preventive and
repressive work in the capital. [@2]
