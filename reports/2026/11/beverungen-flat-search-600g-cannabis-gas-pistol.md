Officers of the Höxter district police authority (Kreispolizeibehörde Höxter)
searched a flat in Beverungen, North Rhine-Westphalia, and seized drugs, untaxed
cigarettes, cash and a gas pistol with ammunition. [@1] The search took place on
the morning of Friday, 6 November 2026. [@3] In the flat officers found about
600 grams of cannabis flowers packaged ready for sale and about 450 grams of
amphetamine. [@2]

The haul also included 112 cartons of untaxed cigarettes and 60 untaxed liquids
for e-cigarettes. [@1] WDR reported that the untaxed cigarettes and e-cigarette
liquids had been illegally imported. [@3] Officers also found cash in a higher
four-digit amount whose origin is not known. [@1]

Police are examining the seized items and calculating a possible street sale
value. [@2] The flat's 51-year-old resident was recorded for identification and
then released, because he had not previously come to police attention and no
further grounds for detention existed. [@2] The proceedings against him are not
yet concluded. [@1]
