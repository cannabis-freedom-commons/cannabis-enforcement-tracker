On the afternoon of Wednesday 11 November, officers of the Federal departmental
headquarters' Dangerous Drugs division searched a house on Alicia Moreau de
Justo street in Barrio El Silbido in Federal. [@1] Police described the
operation as a microtrafficking raid in which they seized narcotics and a
firearm. [@1] The investigation began after a procedure the previous day at
Hospital Dr. Raul Camino, where officers detected a man trying to bring
narcotics in for a person interned there. [@2] The search was ordered by the
acting guarantees court, with the local prosecutor's office intervening. [@2]

In the house officers seized mobile phones, cutting and portioning tools,
handmade cigarettes and wrappers containing cannabis (marijuana), more than 20
cannabis sativa seedlings, a digital scale and a large-calibre shotgun found in
working order, among other items of interest. [@2]

Six people were in the house when officers arrived. [@2] A 26-year-old man was
detained and held in the lockup of the Federal police headquarters, while the
five others, including two women, were identified by order of the judiciary.
[@2]
