Detectives of the Modelo Territorial Cero team of Chile's Investigative Police
(PDI) raided four properties in the Población Carol Urzúa, a housing estate in
the southern Santiago commune of Puente Alto. [@1] Seven people were detained on
Sunday in the anti-drug operation in the Población Carol Urzúa. [@1] Three of
those detained were linked to a family group known as Los Chócolos, which police
said sold drugs in the estate. [@1]

The PDI said it seized cocaine, ketamine, cannabis and controlled medicines in
the raids. [@1]

Authorities classify the Población Carol Urzúa as a priority neighbourhood. [@1]
