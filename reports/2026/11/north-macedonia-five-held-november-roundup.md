Police in North Macedonia detained five people at various locations on 31
October and 1 November 2026, the Interior Ministry said, after marijuana,
cocaine and brown powder were found and seized. [@1] At the Tabanovce border
crossing, officers detained D.P.H., 43, from Germany, after marijuana was found
in his personal luggage as he entered the country at 1:10 PM on 31 October. [@1]
S.M., 37, also from Germany, was detained at the same crossing at 4:00 PM after
marijuana was found in the Opel camper van with German plates he arrived in.
[@1] Both were handed over to a police station for further proceedings. [@1]

In Skopje's Centar area, police detained T.I., 35, from Štip, after marijuana
was found on him. [@2] At Idrizovo prison, officers searching four convicted men
found and seized 54 packets of brown powdered substance. [@2] On the local road
between Bogovinje and Selce Kec, police found cocaine on E.A., 37, from
Bogovinje, who was detained and held at a police station. [@2] In Ohrid, C.V.,
18, from a village near Struga, was detained at 2:40 AM on 1 November after
items with traces of marijuana were found on him. [@2]

The ministry said appropriate charges would be filed against the individuals
once the cases were fully documented. [@3]
