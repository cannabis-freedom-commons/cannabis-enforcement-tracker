A 26-year-old Namibian man, Elrico Kenda, was arrested in Walvis Bay following a
police operation that led to the seizure of suspected cannabis. [@2] Erongo
police spokesperson Senior Inspector Judith Shomongula said the incident
occurred on Friday, 6 November, at around 20:00 in Brandberg Street, Sea Point,
Kuisebmund. [@2] He appeared before the Walvis Bay Magistrate's Court on 9
November, remains in custody after being denied bail, and his case was postponed
to 15 April 2027. [@2]

Police confiscated two parcels of suspected cannabis weighing a combined 2 018
grams, with an estimated street value of N$100 900. [@2] Police said the
cannabis, valued at N$100 900, was found in his possession. [@1]

He was arrested following a controlled delivery operation that was carried out
in Seapoint, Walvis Bay. [@1] Police were conducting a roadblock in Swakopmund,
searched a seven-seater vehicle and discovered the suspected drugs, then carried
out a controlled delivery in Walvis Bay. [@1] He is facing a charge of dealing
in, alternatively possessing, prohibited dependence-producing substances
suspected to be cannabis, under section 2(a) and (b) of the Abuse of
Dependence-Producing Substances and Rehabilitation Centres Act 41 of 1971. [@2]
