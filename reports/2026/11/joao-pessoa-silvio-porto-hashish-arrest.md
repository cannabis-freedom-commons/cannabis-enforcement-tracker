Two women were arrested in flagrante on the morning of Thursday 5 November 2026
while trying to enter the Desembargador Sílvio Porto penitentiary, in the
Mangabeira neighbourhood of João Pessoa, with drugs and illicit objects. [@1]
The material was hidden inside ointment tubes and was detected by penal police
officers during inspection of the visitors' supplies. [@1]

The attempt to introduce the illicit items into the prison unit was stopped when
the packages passed through the food-scanner equipment used by the inspection.
[@1] When they analysed the scanner images, the agents identified suspicious
densities in the structure of the product. [@1] In the physical check of the
tubes, the Paraíba Penal Police team found portions of cocaine, hashish and
silver chains hidden in place of the ointment. [@1]

Faced with the flagrant offence, the two visitors were placed under arrest on
the spot. [@1] They were taken, together with all the seized material, to the
Central de Polícia Civil in the Paraíba capital, where they underwent the
applicable legal procedures and will answer for drug trafficking and attempting
to introduce illicit items into a prison establishment. [@1]
