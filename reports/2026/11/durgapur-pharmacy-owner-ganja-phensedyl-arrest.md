New Township police in Durgapur arrested pharmacy owner Sudip Mukhopadhyay after
recovering cough syrup, banned tablets and ganja packets. [@1] Officers raided
his house in the Steel Park More area under New Township police station on
Monday night. [@1] A second report describes the same overnight operation at the
house between Fuljhor More and Steel Park More and names the same detained shop
owner. [@2]

Officers found 285 bottles of Phensedyl syrup, a large number of banned tablets
and several packets of ganja, the local name for cannabis, in the house. [@1]
Deputy Commissioner (East) Raspreet Singh said about 285 bottles of
codeine-phosphate Phensedyl syrup and banned tablets were seized in the Fuljhor
operation and the detainee would be taken into police custody for investigation.
[@1] The second report lists the haul as 285 bottles of codeine-phosphate
Phensedyl syrup, many banned tablets and a few ganja packets. [@2]

Investigators are examining where the stock came from, where it was to be
supplied and whether others are involved. [@2] Police connect the Bidhan Nagar
and Fuljhor area, home to many private colleges and students from other states,
to complaints of evening intoxication among students and local youths and
suspect a larger network. [@1]
