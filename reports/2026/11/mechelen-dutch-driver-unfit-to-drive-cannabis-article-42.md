The Mechelen police court declared a young Dutch man unfit to drive after he
appeared in court and admitted he still used drugs. [@1] He had been stopped in
a WODCA traffic operation earlier in the year and tested positive for drugs.
[@1] He had been driving home from a visit to a cousin and in statements after
the test said he smoked cannabis every three days. [@1] Because he lives in the
Netherlands he had to pay a fine immediately, but he was also summoned to court.
[@1]

The prosecutor asked him directly whether he still used drugs, and after he said
yes argued that only a declaration of unfitness to drive could be imposed. [@1]
He was told he had to hand in his driving licence immediately and could only ask
for it back once he had shown six months without drug use. [@1] He said he had
already received a substantial fine and had been without his licence for two
weeks, and the court heard that he works in the Netherlands as a traffic
marshal. [@1]

The court fined him 1,600 euros, half of it suspended, and gave him a driving
ban of 45 days. [@1] The judge also applied article 42 on fitness to drive,
telling him he would have to show he no longer used drugs before he could drive
in Belgium again. [@1] The court gave its judgment immediately at the hearing.
[@1]
