Barwaha police arrested Ajay Gohar with 855 grams of ganja at the Narmada Ghat
in Navghat Khedi on Sunday. [@2] Station in-charge Rajendra Barman said police
had received a tip-off that a man in a T-shirt and capri was standing at the
ghat with a bag of ganja to deliver it. [@2] A police team led by Barman reached
the spot and detained the suspect after cordoning the area. [@2] On questioning,
he gave his name as Ajay, son of Pappu Gohar, aged 39 and a resident of Tower
Bedi in Barwaha, Khargone district. [@2] Police said they informed him of his
legal rights under Section 50 of the NDPS Act before searching him. [@2]

The bag held moist green-brown ganja packed in transparent polythene. [@2]
Weighed on an electronic scale, it came to 855 grams. [@2] Police put the
estimated value of the seized ganja at Rs 8,555. [@1]

Police registered a case against him at Barwaha police station under Section
8/20 of the NDPS Act. [@2]
