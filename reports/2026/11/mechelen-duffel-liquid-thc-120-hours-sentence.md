A 24-year-old man from Duffel has been sentenced to 120 hours of community
service for selling drugs, specifically liquid THC. [@1] He told the court he
started dealing because he had built up large debts. [@1] If he does not carry
out the community service, or does it badly, he faces 12 months in prison
instead. [@1] The Mechelen criminal court also ordered the confiscation of
almost 3,000 euros. [@1]

Earlier this year police carried out a check in the Plankstraat in Mechelen, the
street where the man was selling, after information that drugs were being traded
by a bearded person. [@1] Officers saw him leave a house three times for a short
time and each time have a brief interaction with another person, prosecutor
Margot Kennis said. [@1] One of the suspected buyers was stopped and admitted he
had bought THC liquid from the man, a liquid form of cannabis used through an
e-cigarette. [@1] Orders were placed through Snapchat, and the investigation
found a whole organisation behind the trade, with telephone operators taking
orders and dispatching couriers. [@1] A search of the house found a quantity of
cannabis, 134 bottles containing synthetic cannabinoid and 235 unused bottles.
[@1] Nearly 3,000 euros in cash was also found at the house. [@1]

He was interviewed and admitted that he was steered through Snapchat, that he
had been trading for only about a week and a half, and that he had already sold
fifty bottles of liquid THC in that time. [@1] His lawyer, Lennart Perdu, said
he had confessed from his first interview and had taken responsibility. [@1] The
lawyer said his client was out of work, was himself a drug user, and was
approached by people who suggested he sell liquid THC, which he then began
selling from his grandparents' home in Mechelen. [@1] He has since found work,
agreed an instalment plan with his insurer and is about to move in with his
girlfriend, his lawyer said. [@1]
