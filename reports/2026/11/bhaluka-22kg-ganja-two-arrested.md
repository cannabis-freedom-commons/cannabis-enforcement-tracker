Officers of Special Response Battalion-14 (SRB-14) arrested two men with 22
kilograms of ganja (cannabis) during a special operation in Bhaluka, Mymensingh.
[@1] The arrests were made at about 2:20 pm on Tuesday, 10 November, on a paved
road in Purura village under Bhaluka police station. [@1] The two detainees were
identified as 47-year-old Md Helal Mia, described as a driver by profession, and
40-year-old Rimon Bhuiya, both from Brahmanbaria district. [@1]

According to SRB-14's Mymensingh CPSC unit, an operational team stationed in
front of the Bhaluka Upazila Parishad (sub-district council) received
confidential information about drug buying and selling in the Purura area. [@1]
The team moved quickly to the area and detained the two suspects. [@1] When
officers searched the mini pickup, they recovered 22 kilograms of ganja hidden
by a special method in the rear of the vehicle. [@1] Officers also seized the
mini pickup used to transport the drugs, along with two mobile phones and cash.
[@1]

Sources linked to the operation alleged that the arrested men had long collected
ganja from border areas and supplied and sold it across different parts of the
country. [@1] The two men, together with the recovered cannabis and seized
items, were handed over to Bhaluka police station for further legal action. [@1]
