A Brazos County sheriff's deputy stopped a vehicle for a traffic violation at
about 12:34 p.m. on 8 November 2026 in the 1500 block of Graham Drive in Brazos
County, Texas. [@1] Further investigation led the deputies to search the
vehicle. [@1]

The search turned up 1.575 ounces of marijuana, about 45 grams, alongside 869.7
grams of cocaine and $617 in cash. [@1]

The driver, Lawrence Blue, was arrested and charged with possession of less than
two ounces of marijuana. [@1] He was also charged with manufacture or delivery
of a controlled substance in Penalty Group 1, 400 grams or more, a charge
concerning the cocaine rather than the marijuana. [@1] The sheriff's office
praised the deputies involved and said the seizure reflected their work to
address illegal activity in the community. [@1]
