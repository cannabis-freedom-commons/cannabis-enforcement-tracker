Jalalpur police in Jaunpur district, Uttar Pradesh, arrested Ranjeet Singh alias
Gappu, son of the late Shivpujan Singh and a resident of Tusauri village under
Kerakat police station, near the highway underpass bridge at Chhatari village.
[@1] The arrest came during a campaign run by the senior superintendent of
police to catch criminals and people wanted or facing warrants. [@1] A search of
his possession recovered 63 small packets of illegal ganja, the local name for
cannabis, with a total weight of 513 grams (0.513 kg). [@1]

Jalalpur police station registered a case against him under Sections 8 and 20 of
the NDPS Act, India's main narcotics law, and said further legal proceedings
were under way. [@2] Dainik Bhaskar reported that several cases were already
registered against him at Madiyahun and Line Bazar police stations. [@2] Police
said they were investigating where the ganja had been brought from and who was
involved in supplying it. [@2]

The team that made the arrest and the seizure consisted of senior sub-inspector
Rajkumar Tiwari, head constable Babul Yadav and constable Abhishek Tiwari of
Jalalpur station. [@2]
