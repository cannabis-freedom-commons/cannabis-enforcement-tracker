Military police arrested one passenger on suspicion of drug trafficking in Dois
Vizinhos, in southwestern Paraná, on Wednesday afternoon, 4 November 2026. [@1]
The arrest took place at about noon after police received an anonymous tip that
a vehicle was carrying illicit products. [@1] The officers were patrolling when
they stopped a car used for app-based rides in the town. [@1]

The stopping team was Alpha, assigned to the 2nd Company of the 21st Military
Police Battalion. [@1] The search found a backpack with approximately 2.75
kilograms of a substance described as analogous to maconha, the Portuguese word
for marijuana. [@1] That weight is 2.75 kilograms, with the amount written in
Brazilian decimal-comma notation as 2,750 quilos. [@1] Police also found a
mobile phone. [@1]

The driver of the app-based car was released, while the passenger received an
order of arrest. [@1] Officers seized the substance and the phone and forwarded
them, along with the passenger, for the appropriate legal procedures. [@1]
