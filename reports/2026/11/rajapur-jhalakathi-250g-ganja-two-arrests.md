Two separate anti-drug operations in Rajapur in Jhalakathi detained two people
with ganja and yaba. [@1] One of them was given one year and three months of
simple imprisonment and a fine by a mobile court. [@1] A case was filed against
the other person and he was sent to court. [@1]

Around 11am on Wednesday 11 November, the Department of Narcotics Control,
Jhalakathi conducted a raid on the west side of Rajapur Government College in
Rajapur Sadar Union. [@1,2] Officers detained 32-year-old Md. Bappy Akan there
with 250 grams of ganja and one piece of yaba. [@1,2] Upazila executive officer
Rifat Ara Mouri conducted the mobile court at the scene. [@1,2] The court
sentenced Bappy Akan to one year and three months of simple imprisonment and a
fine of 2,000 taka. [@1,2] Non-payment of the fine carries a further 15 days of
simple imprisonment. [@1,2] Rifat Ara Mouri confirmed the operation and sentence and said the upazila administration was applying a zero-tolerance policy against drugs in Rajapur and would continue such operations. [@2]

In a separate police raid in the Barbakpur area, officers arrested 38-year-old
Abdul Hakim from the unpaved road in front of his house with five pieces of
yaba. [@1] Rajapur police inspector (investigation) Firoz Kamal said a case
under the Narcotics Control Act was filed against Abdul Hakim after the yaba
seizure. [@1]
