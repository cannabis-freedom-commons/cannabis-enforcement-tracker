A special NDPS court in Jhansi sentenced a man convicted in an 11-year-old
charas trafficking case to three months and five days of rigorous imprisonment.
[@1] The court, presided over by Special Judge (NDPS) Ompal Singh, also fined
him 20,000 rupees. [@1]

Prosecutors said police caught a resident of Subhashganj in Jhansi with 500
grams of charas on 14 September 2015. [@1] A case was registered under the NDPS
Act, India's Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, and a chargesheet
was filed in court. [@1] During the trial the special public prosecutor (NDPS)
presented evidence and arguments to prove the charges. [@1]

After the hearing concluded, the judge convicted him on the basis of that
evidence and the prosecutor's arguments and pronounced the sentence and fine on
Friday. [@1] The man admitted his offence through his lawyer. [@1] The court
ordered the time he spent in jail, from 14 September 2015 to 19 December 2015,
be set off against the sentence. [@1] He will therefore receive credit for the
period already served behind bars. [@1]
