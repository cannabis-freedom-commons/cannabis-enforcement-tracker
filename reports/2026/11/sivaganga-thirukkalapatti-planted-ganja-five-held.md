Police in Sivaganga arrested five people, including a bar owner, in the case
over ganja planted at a grocery shop to take revenge on a social activist. [@1]
The five were identified as bar owner Periya Sokkanathan aged 27, Sundar aged 19
of Manakudi, Jayamurthy aged 65 of Maruthipatti, Rajapandi aged 65 of
Karaiyendalpatti and a 17-year-old boy. [@1] Police allege that they placed
ganja in front of Rajkumar's shop and then gave the police false information.
[@1]

Rajkumar, from Thirukkalapatti near Thirupputhur in Sivaganga district, runs a
grocery shop and an e-service centre. [@1] He had been continuously protesting
to demand the removal of a TASMAC shop that disturbed local residents,
schoolgirls and women. [@1] Two days earlier, two people who came to his shop on
a two-wheeler placed a packet of ganja and tobacco under the staircase and left.
[@1] Soon after, two men who arrived in a vehicle with a police sticker claimed
to be prohibition police, searched the shop, displayed the ganja packet and
threatened Rajkumar, and CCTV footage of the episode emerged and caused a stir.
[@1]

The episode drew strong condemnation over the claim that police and the bar
owner acted because of participation in the anti-TASMAC protest. [@1] Rajkumar,
along with village residents, submitted a complaint petition at the Sivaganga
superintendent of police office. [@1]
