Aceh Besar district police destroyed a cannabis field found in the hills of Desa
Pulo, Mukim Lamteuba, Seulimeum, on Sunday 1 November 2026. [@1] The destruction
operation ran from 12.00 to 14.30 Western Indonesian Time. [@1]

Police estimated the field at about one hectare and the uprooted cannabis plants
at about 200 kilograms. [@1] The hillside plants were between one and two metres
tall. [@2] Officers reached the forest hillside site after travelling about
seven kilometres from their departure point. [@2]

Aceh Besar police chief AKBP Chairul Ikhsan, through narcotics unit head Iptu
Syahrul Akhyar, said the site was far from settlements and difficult to reach.
[@2] Syahrul said officers pulled up all the cannabis plants they found and
destroyed them on the spot. [@2] Police described the operation as part of
efforts against narcotics abuse and trafficking, especially cannabis cultivation
in its jurisdiction. [@2] Police asked residents to report any cannabis planting
or drug trafficking in their neighbourhoods. [@2]
