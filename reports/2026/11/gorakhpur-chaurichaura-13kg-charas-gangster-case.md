Chauri Chaura police registered a new FIR under the Gangster Act against the two
smugglers earlier caught bringing charas from Nepal for supply in Gorakhpur and
Deoria. [@1] Police described the pair as members of an organised gang and
registered the case after the district magistrate approved the gang chart. [@1]
Police identified the accused as 26-year-old Ranjit Vishwakarma of Chorbharia
Bakuchi Mishra in Deoria's Khukhundu area, described as the gang leader, and
21-year-old Kishan Maddeshiya of Dhanauti Khurd in Deoria Sadar, described as
his associate. [@1] Both were accused of illegally bringing charas from Nepal
for financial gain and supplying it in Gorakhpur, Deoria and other areas. [@1]

An NTF Gorakhpur team arrested both men with a motorcycle near Chauri Badurhiya
village on 7 September. [@1] A search of their backpacks recovered 13 kg 85 g of
charas. [@1] Chauri Chaura station registered an NDPS Act case in that seizure,
and a chargesheet was filed on 22 September after the investigation was
completed. [@1]

Station chief Ved Prakash Sharma said the gang chart prepared against both
accused received the district magistrate's approval, after which the Gangster
Act FIR was registered. [@1] Both accused are currently in jail. [@1]
