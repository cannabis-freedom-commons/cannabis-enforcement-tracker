A special court in Mandi convicted Kahan Singh and Nagendra in a charas case and
sentenced each of them to ten years of rigorous imprisonment. [@1] The court
also imposed a fine of Rs 1 lakh on each convict, with an additional year of
simple imprisonment if the fine is not paid. [@1] In fixing the punishment, the
court considered their age, the absence of any prior criminal conviction and
their family responsibilities, calling ten years the minimum term. [@1]

The case began on 24 May 2023, when an Anti-Narcotics Task Force team from Kullu
led by Inspector Inder Singh was on patrol and blockade duty in the
Jogindernagar area. [@1] At about 8:35 pm, about 100 metres beyond Syuri bridge,
the team stopped on suspicion two youths walking from the Syuri side, one of
whom was carrying a yellow bag. [@1] A search of the bag found black
stick-shaped charas wrapped in green polythene, which weighed 1.188 kg on an
electronic scale. [@1] Police seized the charas on the spot and registered a
first information report at Jogindernagar police station. [@1]

Police later filed a charge sheet against Kahan Singh, resident of Ghadoun, and
Nagendra, resident of Chhaprot, under Sections 20 and 29 of the NDPS Act. [@1]
At trial the prosecution examined 17 witnesses, although both independent
witnesses turned hostile. [@1] The court treated the testimony of police
personnel, photographs of the scene and other documentary evidence as decisive.
[@1] It rejected the defence argument that the recovery was tampered with and
the accused were falsely implicated, holding the prosecution case proved beyond
reasonable doubt. [@1]
