National Police officers in Valladolid arrested a man whose car carried 1,550
grams of hashish packed in 15 packages. [@1] The stop came on the night of 4
November 2026 at about 21:10, when the driver made a sudden manoeuvre to park as
a marked police van approached. [@3]

Officers checked his identity, searched the car and found a black backpack on
the back seat holding three vacuum-sealed plastic bags, each package bearing a
sticker with an identifying hologram. [@1] Police valued the 1,550 grams at
€10,943 on the illegal market using the national drug office's price tables for
the second half of 2026. [@2] The driver was arrested on suspicion of a crime
against public health, and the car was taken to police premises for a fuller
search. [@1]

In the second search, with the canine unit, officers found €1,500 hidden in a
compartment of the vehicle and seized it. [@2] A drug test on the substance gave
a positive result for cannabis resin, commonly known as hashish. [@1] The man
was handed over to the courts, which ordered his release. [@2]
