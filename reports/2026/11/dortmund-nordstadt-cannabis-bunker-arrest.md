Police officers of the Dortmund force, working under the presence concept
"Fokus", ran intensified checks in the city centre and the Nordstadt over
several days in November 2026. [@1] On Tuesday 10 November, police video
observation caught a 25-year-old man from Hamm apparently dealing narcotics in
the Nordstadt. [@1]

A check of the 25-year-old turned up cash, medicines and what police described
as suspected cannabis. [@1] The video recordings allowed officers to link the
suspect to hidden drug stashes, described as bunkers, which held further
quantities of drugs. [@1] The 25-year-old was arrested and taken into police
custody, and the narcotics and the cash were seized. [@1]

During a check of a cyclist in the city centre, officers found and seized
several suspected crack stones in a tin. [@1] A 23-year-old man from Soest who
was subject to a spatial restriction fled on foot near Münsterstraße and
Heroldstraße, throwing away a starting pistol and suspected drugs, and was
arrested after a short chase. [@1] A search of the 23-year-old at the police
station found 44 tablets of a prescription medicine and a three-figure sum of
euros in dealer-typical denominations, which was secured because he could not
explain its origin. [@1] Because the conditions for pre-trial detention were not
met, the 23-year-old was released once all police measures were complete, while
criminal proceedings were opened. [@1]

On Monday 9 November, officers on Martinstraße stopped a man carrying 20 as-new
items of clothing who could not produce plausible proof of ownership; the goods
were secured and returned to their rightful owner, and the man was taken into
police custody. [@1] In the Stadtgarten on Wednesday 11 November, officers
secured a larger amount of cash from a man who could not credibly prove its
origin. [@1] An eight-person group checked in the Nordstadt area was given a
banning order after no suspicious or prohibited items were found. [@1] Police
said they would continue the presence and control measures under the "Fokus"
concept. [@1]
