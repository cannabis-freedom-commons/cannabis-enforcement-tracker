Police in Isperih, Razgrad Province, detained a 45-year-old man from the
Silistra village of Kalipetrovo who they said had a criminal record, after
finding drugs on him. [@1] The arrest followed an evening police check on Road
III-205, a third-class road, at the turn-off for the village of Sveshtari on 6
November 2026. [@1] Officers stopped a Volkswagen Transporter driven by a
42-year-old man, also from Kalipetrovo. [@2]

The passenger in the front right seat was holding a dry herbal mass weighing
3.38 grams. [@1] A field test of the material reacted for cannabis. [@2] The man
was detained at the building of the Isperih regional police station, not in the
car, and the driver was not arrested. [@1]

A pre-trial investigation was opened under article 354a, paragraph 3, item 1, of
the Criminal Code, which covers drug offences. [@3] The accounts came from the
Razgrad regional police directorate's press bulletin, which the Silistra and BTA
outlets reproduced. [@3]
