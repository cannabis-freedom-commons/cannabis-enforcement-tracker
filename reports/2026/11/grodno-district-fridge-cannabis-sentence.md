A Grodno resident stood trial for growing cannabis on his dacha plot in Grodno
District. [@1] The accused, aged 37, had equipped a greenhouse for the
prohibited plants, ordering cannabis seeds from Russia and converting an old
refrigerator in an outbuilding to create the required microclimate. [@1] He
admitted guilt in full at his trial. [@1]

Between June and August 2026 he sowed and grew Cannabis plants on the plot to
obtain marijuana, using seeds previously bought in Russia, gardening materials
and the specially equipped refrigerator. [@1] No vegetable crops appeared on the
beds because the land was used for growing cannabis. [@1] He dried part of the
leaves, producing marijuana that he stored for personal consumption. [@1] He had
already smoked part of the harvest. [@1] Officers seized the remaining about 3.5
grams of marijuana that he had kept on the plot. [@1]

The Grodno District Court found him guilty under Part 1 of Article 329 and Part
1 of Article 328 of Belarus's Criminal Code. [@1] It imposed two years'
restriction of liberty without referral to an open-type correctional
institution, a non-custodial sentence in Belarus. [@1] The verdict had not
entered into force and could still be appealed. [@1]
