A 30-year-old driver was arrested in flagrante. [@1] The arrest took place on
the afternoon of Tuesday, 3 November 2026, in the municipality of Sabáudia in
the Arapongas region. [@1] After the seizure, officers took the driver with the
seized drug to the Civil Police station in Arapongas for legal procedures. [@2]

Officers stopped a Fiat Argo at km 245 of the PR-218 highway during the roadside
approach. [@1] Police found 47 kg of maconha, the Portuguese word for cannabis,
being transported in the car's trunk. [@1] Police seized the 47 kg during an
inspection at km 245 of the PR-218 in Sabáudia on the afternoon of Tuesday (3).
[@2] The drug was being transported in the trunk of a Fiat Argo. [@2]

The 47 kg figure and the stop details come from information released by the
Federal Highway Police. [@1] The police did not report the origin of the drug or
its intended destination. [@1] The driver was described only as a 30-year-old
man. [@2]
