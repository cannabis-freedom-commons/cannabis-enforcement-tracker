North Kordofan state's anti-narcotics administration, working with related
forces, seized a large shipment of hashish in El Obeid totalling 8,738 qandul
and detained three suspects. [@1] A qandul is a head or ball of cannabis resin,
the form in which hashish is usually described in Sudanese police reports. [@2]

The seizure followed field surveillance based on confirmed information and
turned up a Kamaz tipper truck carrying the shipment, which was divided among 12
sacks. [@1] Officers also found a Kalashnikov rifle and 28 rounds of ammunition
in the vehicle. [@1]

The three suspects were taken to the central El Obeid police station, where a
case was registered under number 970 under article 15 (a) of Sudan's narcotics
and psychotropic substances law. [@1] The seized material was entered as
exhibits in the case while investigation continued. [@2]

The acting state police director, Brigadier Jamal Ezzeldin, the head of the
criminal investigations department and the police chief of Sheikan locality
attended the seizure, alongside officers and units against negative phenomena.
[@1] Ezzeldin praised the executing force for its efficiency and coordination in
completing the operation. [@1]
