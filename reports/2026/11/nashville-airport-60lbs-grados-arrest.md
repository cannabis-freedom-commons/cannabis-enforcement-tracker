A woman was arrested for allegedly attempting to smuggle more than 50 lbs. of
marijuana through Nashville International Airport (BNA) on Tuesday afternoon.
[@1,3] According to an arrest affidavit, a K9 narcotics officer was conducting a
sweep of an arriving Southwest Airlines flight from San Jose, California, when
“Peggy” signaled that she had picked up the scent of drugs in some luggage. [@1,3]
The affidavit states that the dog pinged on two pieces of luggage for the
officer, who identified 21-year-old Isah Cristina Grados as the traveler from
the baggage tags. [@1,2,3] Detectives located Grados in the terminal as she was
reportedly heading to her connecting flight. [@1,4]

She permitted them to search her luggage, which produced multiple vacuum-sealed
bundles of marijuana, totaling 60 lbs. (about 27.22 kg), according to the
affidavit. [@1,3] Grados was arrested and charged with felony drug possession with
the intent to sell. [@1,3] Her bond was set at $25,000, and she posted bail the
same day. [@1,3]

Marijuana containing a certain level of THC is illegal in Tennessee, but large
amounts of the drug continue to flood into the state, often on out-of-state
flights. [@1,3] Narcotics officers at BNA often sweep incoming luggage from
California airports, including San Diego, Los Angeles, San Francisco, and
Sacramento. [@1,3]

Court documents reported nearly 60 pounds, or 27,215 grams, across the two bags. [@2,4] Court documents place the Southwest flight from San Jose at about 3:15 p.m. [@2,4]
