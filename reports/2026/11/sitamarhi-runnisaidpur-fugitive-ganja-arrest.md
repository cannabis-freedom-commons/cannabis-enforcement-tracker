Sitamarhi town police, acting with the Bihar STF, arrested a man who had been
evading arrest for a long time in a ganja trafficking case registered under the
NDPS Act. [@1] Police named the arrested man as Vimal Kumar, son of Radheshyam
Sah, a resident of Kishanpur village in the Bathnaha police station area of
Sitamarhi district. [@1]

He was caught late on Wednesday night at an auto stand in the Runnisaidpur
police station area on a tip-off that he was there. [@1] Officers searched him
and recovered 119,000 rupees in cash and a mobile phone. [@1]

Sitamarhi town station house officer Ashok Kumar said the team verified the
tip-off and then raided the spot without delay. [@1] After completing the legal
formalities, police sent Vimal Kumar to jail in judicial custody. [@1] Police
said they were still investigating other aspects of the case and its contacts.
