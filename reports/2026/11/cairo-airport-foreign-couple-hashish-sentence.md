The Cairo Criminal Court sentenced a foreign woman to three years in prison and
her husband to one year of imprisonment with work, fining each of them 10,000
Egyptian pounds. [@1] The court acquitted the two other defendants. [@1] The
panel was headed by Counsellor Sobhy Abdel Meguid, with Counsellors Walid Anwar,
Mohamed Hosny and Ibrahim Mahmoud Helmy as members. [@1]

The public prosecution referred four foreign defendants, aged 36, 31, 26 and 30,
to the criminal court. [@1] The couple were charged with attempting to smuggle
cocaine and amphetamine without a written permit and with possessing cocaine,
amphetamine, methamphetamine and hashish for consumption. [@1] A chemical
laboratory analysis of samples taken from the couple showed they had used
cocaine, amphetamine, methamphetamine and hashish. [@1]

Security officers at Cairo airport searched the woman as she entered the airport
hall after suspecting her. [@1] The search found two rolls of cocaine and 178
amphetamine tablets in her possession, along with four mobile phones. [@1] The
chemical laboratory found the two rolls weighed 184 grams of cocaine and
confirmed that the 178 tablets were amphetamine. [@1] Confronted with the seized
items, the couple admitted possessing the drugs for consumption. [@1]
Investigations said the couple had obtained the drugs from the third and fourth
defendants. [@1] The prosecution charged the third and fourth defendants with
possessing cocaine and amphetamine for trafficking. [@1]
