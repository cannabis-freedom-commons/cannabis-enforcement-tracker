Three Malaysian nationals who arrived on flight EK 709 from Dubai were searched
at Plaisance airport. [@1,19] The three passengers were two men and a woman. [@1,8]
The three suspects were placed in police detention. [@1,5]

The search by the Anti-Drug and Smuggling Unit and the Customs Anti-Narcotics
Section found 70 packets of cannabis weighing 76 kilos hidden in suitcases. [@1,6,7,8,12,16,19]
Foreign currency was also seized from the three passengers. [@1] The two units
then carried out a controlled delivery operation to identify the recipients of
the drugs. [@1] Those recipients did not come forward. [@1]

The seizure was carried out by the Anti-Drug and Smuggling Unit and the Customs
Anti-Narcotics Section at Plaisance airport on Tuesday. [@1,14] Inspectors Gopala
and Lepois are leading the investigation under Assistant Superintendent of
Police Goinden and Superintendent of Police Ghoora. [@1]

The three suspects were two men aged 22 and 30 and a woman aged 18. [@2,6,8,10,11,14,15,16,17] The Mauritius Revenue Authority valued the haul at 114.21 million Mauritian rupees, about RM9.87 million, at local market prices. [@2,6,7,8,10,12,13,14,15,16,17,18] Le Mauricien put the market value of the seized cannabis at Rs 114.2 million. [@3] The three suspects carried an exact total of 76.14 kg of compressed cannabis. [@3,6,10,15] The 30-year-old suspect, a security officer living in Singapore, carried 10.9 kg of cannabis. [@4,9,14] The 18-year-old suspect carried 32.62 kg of cannabis in 30 vacuum-packed packets. [@4] Mauritian reporting said preliminary findings indicated the cannabis came from Thailand. [@3,9,13,18] The route used by these couriers was Thailand, then Dubai, then Mauritius. [@3,7,8] Harian Metro, quoting Mauritian media, said one suspect was believed to have passed through Thailand before flying on to Dubai and Mauritius. [@2,6] The customs anti-narcotics section inspected the passengers' checked baggage after profiling and questioning. [@2] The three passengers were arrested at Plaisance airport with the 76.14 kg haul valued at Rs 114.2 million. [@4,18] The three suspects travelled on the same Emirates flight EK709 from Dubai. [@5,6,10,15,16] The controlled delivery was interrupted on Tuesday 3 November, without identifying the intended recipients. [@5]

The 30-year-old suspect lived and worked in Singapore as a security guard, while the other two suspects lived in Penang. [@2,6,17]

The Mauritius Revenue Authority said the discovery was made after its officers stopped the three for questioning and inspected their checked baggage. [@7,9,14] The 30-year-old suspect was living and working in Singapore as a security guard, the Mauritius Revenue Authority said. [@7]

The three Malaysians were detained at Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam International Airport on 2 November 2026. [@9,14]

The 22-year-old man and the 18-year-old woman, reported to live together in Penang, carried about 32.5 kg and 32.6 kg of cannabis respectively. [@2,10,14]

Kwong Wah said the three landed at Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam International Airport on Emirates flight EK709 from Dubai and drew the attention of officers for their movements and passenger records. [@11] The Mauritius Revenue Authority said its Customs Anti-Narcotics Section decided to inspect the three passengers' checked baggage after assessing their records and questioning them. [@11,13] Officers found 70 vacuum-packed black rectangular plastic parcels of green plant matter, suspected to be cannabis, and the Anti-Drug and Smuggling Unit took over the investigation. [@11,14,15] Kwong Wah said the three were arrested on 2 November 2026, the day of the seizure. [@11] Kwong Wah said the Mauritius Revenue Authority issued its statement on a Wednesday and that the three arrived at the airport 'recently' on flight EK709 from Dubai. [@11]

The three suspects—two men aged 30 and 22 and an 18-year-old woman—remain in police custody facing provisional charges related to the importation of dangerous drugs for the purpose of distribution. [@12]

Mauritius media reported that preliminary investigations linked the drugs to Thailand, with one of the suspects believed to have transited through the country before flying to Dubai and then Mauritius. [@14] A similar modus operandi was detected on Oct 23, when a 55-year-old Malaysian cook was arrested at the same airport with 17.8kg of cannabis in his luggage. [@14]

The 55-year-old Malaysian cook arrested at the same airport on 23 October had arrived from Dubai on Emirates flight EK709, the same flight the three took before their 2 November arrest. [@15]

Three Malaysians were arrested upon arrival in Mauritius on Nov 2 after authorities found 76.14kg of cannabis in their luggage. [@14] The Mauritius Revenue Authority said the Malaysians arrived at Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam International Airport on Nov 2 before being flagged for further checks. [@14] The 30-year-old man, who reportedly lives and works as a security officer in Singapore, was found carrying about 10.9kg of the drug. [@14] The three suspects travelled on Emirates flight EK709 from Dubai and were held at Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam International Airport. [@10]

The three arrived in Mauritius on an Emirates flight from Dubai on 2 November 2026. [@17]
