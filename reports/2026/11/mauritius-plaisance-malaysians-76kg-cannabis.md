Three Malaysian nationals who arrived on Emirates flight EK709 from Dubai were searched at Plaisance,
the Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam International Airport, and detained on 2 November 2026.
[@1,5,6,9,10,11,14,15,16,17,19,20,23] They were two men aged 22 and 30 and an 18-year-old woman.
[@1,2,6,8,10,11,14,15,16,17,23] The 30-year-old lived and worked in Singapore as a security guard; the
other two lived together in Penang. [@2,4,6,7,9,10,14,17,20]

Officers of the Customs Anti-Narcotics Section stopped them for questioning after assessing their
passenger records, and inspected their checked baggage. [@2,7,9,11,13,14,23] With the police Anti-Drug
and Smuggling Unit they found 70 vacuum-packed black packets of compressed cannabis, 76.14 kg in all,
hidden in the suitcases. [@1,3,6,7,8,10,11,12,14,15,16,19,23] The 22-year-old man and the woman carried
about 32.5 kg and 32.6 kg [@2,10,14,23], and the 30-year-old about 10.9 kg [@4,9,14,23]; one account gives
the woman's share as 32.62 kg in 30 packets. [@4] Foreign currency was also seized. [@1] The Mauritius
Revenue Authority valued the haul at 114.21 million Mauritian rupees, about RM9.87 million, at local
market prices. [@2,3,6,7,8,10,12,13,14,15,16,17,18,20,23]

## Investigation

The Anti-Drug and Smuggling Unit took over the case. [@11,14,15,23] With customs it ran a controlled
delivery to identify the recipients, who did not come forward [@1], and it was ended on Tuesday 3
November. [@5] Inspectors Gopala and Lepois lead the investigation under Assistant Superintendent
Goinden and Superintendent Ghoora. [@1] Preliminary findings indicated the cannabis came from
Thailand, with the couriers flying on via Dubai. [@2,3,6,7,8,9,13,14,18,23] The three remain in police
custody on provisional charges of importing dangerous drugs for distribution. [@12]

The 30-year-old suspect lived and worked in Singapore as a security guard, while the other two suspects lived in Penang. [@2,6,17,20]

The Mauritius Revenue Authority said the discovery was made after its officers stopped the three for questioning and inspected their checked baggage. [@7,9,14] The 30-year-old suspect was living and working in Singapore as a security guard, the Mauritius Revenue Authority said. [@7]

The three Malaysians were detained at Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam International Airport on 2 November 2026. [@9,14,20]

The 22-year-old man and the 18-year-old woman, reported to live together in Penang, carried about 32.5 kg and 32.6 kg of cannabis respectively. [@2,10,14]

Kwong Wah said the three landed at Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam International Airport on Emirates flight EK709 from Dubai and drew the attention of officers for their movements and passenger records. [@11] The Mauritius Revenue Authority said its Customs Anti-Narcotics Section decided to inspect the three passengers' checked baggage after assessing their records and questioning them. [@11,13] Officers found 70 vacuum-packed black rectangular plastic parcels of green plant matter, suspected to be cannabis, and the Anti-Drug and Smuggling Unit took over the investigation. [@11,14,15] Kwong Wah said the three were arrested on 2 November 2026, the day of the seizure. [@11] Kwong Wah said the Mauritius Revenue Authority issued its statement on a Wednesday and that the three arrived at the airport 'recently' on flight EK709 from Dubai. [@11]

The three suspects—two men aged 30 and 22 and an 18-year-old woman—remain in police custody facing provisional charges related to the importation of dangerous drugs for the purpose of distribution. [@12]

Mauritius media reported that preliminary investigations linked the drugs to Thailand, with one of the suspects believed to have transited through the country before flying to Dubai and then Mauritius. [@14] A similar modus operandi was detected on Oct 23, when a 55-year-old Malaysian cook was arrested at the same airport with 17.8kg of cannabis in his luggage. [@14]

The 55-year-old Malaysian cook arrested at the same airport on 23 October had arrived from Dubai on Emirates flight EK709, the same flight the three took before their 2 November arrest. [@15,23]

Three Malaysians were arrested upon arrival in Mauritius on Nov 2 after authorities found 76.14kg of cannabis in their luggage. [@14] The Mauritius Revenue Authority said the Malaysians arrived at Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam International Airport on Nov 2 before being flagged for further checks. [@14] The 30-year-old man, who reportedly lives and works as a security officer in Singapore, was found carrying about 10.9kg of the drug. [@14] The three suspects travelled on Emirates flight EK709 from Dubai and were held at Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam International Airport. [@10]

The three arrived in Mauritius on an Emirates flight from Dubai on 2 November 2026. [@17]

Bukit Aman Narcotics Criminal Investigation Department director Datuk Hussein Omar Khan said the 22-year-old man and the 18-year-old woman, both from Penang, were recruited after making contact with an individual on the RedNote app and then being reached on Telegram. [@21,25,26] The recruiter offered RM1,000 for each suitcase delivered to Mauritius and arranged and paid for the couple's flight tickets and hotel stay. [@21,25,26] The couple agreed to carry four suitcases, two each, and were told to collect them in Bangkok, Thailand, before flying on to Mauritius via Dubai, Hussein said. [@21,26] They were also told to install the Life360 location app so the person coordinating the delivery could monitor their movements, and they sent copies of their passports over Telegram. [@21,26] Checks on the couple's luggage found 60 plastic packets of suspected cannabis with a gross weight of about 65 kg, and Apple tracking devices were found inside each suitcase carried by one of the suspects. [@21,26] The third suspect, a 30-year-old man from Sabah who works in Singapore, was arrested with 10 plastic packets, and police said he had found a job advertisement on Facebook in Singapore before being contacted on Telegram. [@21,26] Hussein said none of the three suspects had previous criminal records, and that the couple had booked five days at the Domaine de Grand Baie Hotel for a holiday. [@21] The three Malaysians were arrested at 2.35pm on 2 November 2026, shortly after arriving on Emirates flight EK709 from Dubai, in an operation by the Anti-Drug and Smuggling Unit with Mauritius Customs, Hussein said. [@21,25,26] The couple found after their arrest that conversations in their Telegram group had been deleted or that the person believed to be the syndicate leader had left the group, Hussein said. [@21,26]

Malaysia's Bukit Aman narcotics director said the Penang couple, 22 and 18, were recruited on RedNote and Telegram to carry four suitcases for RM1,000 a bag, with tickets and hotel paid, collection in Bangkok, a route through Dubai, the Life360 tracker installed to watch their movements and a five-day Grand Baie holiday as cover, while the 30-year-old from Sabah was recruited through a Facebook job advertisement. [@22,24]
