Harur prohibition-enforcement police searched the
Secunderabad–Thiruvananthapuram Sabari Express as it was coming toward Morappur
railway station on the morning of 3 November 2026. [@1] Sub-Inspector
Saravanakumar led the search with head constables Arun and Chandran of the Harur
prohibition-enforcement unit. [@1] The officers carried out the check at about
5:30 a.m. on the Jolarpettai–Salem railway stretch in Dharmapuri district. [@1]
Inside the general compartment, the team found a bag lying near the toilet. [@1]

Officers found 5 kg of ganja inside the bag and seized it. [@1] No suspect was
named and no arrest was reported for the seizure. [@1] The officers continued
inquiries to establish who had carried the ganja on the train. [@1]

The Sabari Express was operating from Secunderabad toward Thiruvananthapuram on
the checked stretch. [@1] Morappur is the railway station named as the
approaching stop for the check in Dharmapuri district. [@1] Police described the
recovered substance as ganja and continued investigation to trace the carrier.
