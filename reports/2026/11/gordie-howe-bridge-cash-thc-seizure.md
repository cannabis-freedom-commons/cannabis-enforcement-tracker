U.S. Customs and Border Protection officers pulled over a vehicle at the Gordie
Howe International Bridge on Nov. 5, 2026. [@3] The traveller was crossing the
Gordie Howe International Bridge. [@2] The vehicle was searched at the bridge by
a K9 detector dog. [@1] The search revealed about $180,000 in undeclared cash of
questionable origin. [@4] The money was in a suitcase in the vehicle. [@2] The
cash was in U.S. dollars. [@3] The traveller, a U.S. citizen, refused to
disclose the nature and source of the money. [@4] Officers seized the cash. [@3]
The Gordie Howe International Bridge is a Windsor-Detroit border crossing. [@3]

A search of the driver's vehicle also produced a variety of THC products. [@2]
Marty C. Raybon of U.S. Customs and Border Protection announced the seizure on
social media. [@2] Raybon is the agency's director of field operations. [@2] His
post included a photo of a detection dog with the seized money. [@2] HSI Detroit
is continuing to investigate, according to the agency's post. [@4]
