The Anti-Narcotics Cell of Ambala police arrested a third suspect in an ongoing
ganja smuggling investigation. [@1] Police identified the arrested man as Manish
Kumar, originally from Patori village in the Madhepura district of Bihar and
residing in New Tagore Garden, Ambala. [@1] His arrest followed the earlier
apprehension of two women involved in the same trafficking network. [@1]

The case began under the Mahesh Nagar police station jurisdiction, where
anti-narcotics officers had intercepted the two women near Guru Nanak Nagar and
seized 1.207 kilograms of ganja. [@2] Amar Ujala reported that the earlier
operation was initiated after Sub-Inspector Kulwinder Singh received information
that narcotics were being sold in the Guru Nanak Nagar locality. [@3]
Interrogation of the detained women disclosed Kumar's role in supplying and
distributing the cannabis. [@2]

On 9 November 2026, a raiding team led by Sub-Inspector Naresh Kumar and
Assistant Sub-Inspector Jasbir Singh tracked down and arrested Kumar. [@2]
Officers produced him before a local court, which remanded him into police
custody for further questioning regarding his supply chain. [@2]
