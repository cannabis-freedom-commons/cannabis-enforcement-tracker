Police of Aguilares ran a security operation on Thursday night 12 November 2026
covering the cities of Aguilares and Concepción, with officers of the Aguilares
police station. [@1] The young man was accompanied by three other people, two of
them adolescents, and during the search officers found a marijuana package under
the passenger seat. [@1] Field tests by the southern Dangerous Drugs Directorate
(Didrop Sur) weighed the vegetable substance at 456 grams. [@1]

The operation was carried out at Rivadavia and Avellaneda streets, with officers
of the Aguilares motorized patrol and the Santa Ana, La Madrid and Trinidad
police stations helping. [@1] Authorities of the Retail Narcotics Prosecution
Unit (UFINAR) of the Concepción Judicial Centre ordered the seizure of three
mobile phones, cash and the Fiat Palio car under the narcotics law, National Law
23.737, and the arrest of one of the young men, 20 years old, with the
adolescents to be handed over to their families. [@1]

Comisario General Marcos Barros, chief of the southern regional unit, supervised
the operation. [@1]

The provincial government's own account describes the operation and the seizure,
and says the Aguilares police officers detained the young man driving the car
with nearly half a kilogram of marijuana. [@2]
