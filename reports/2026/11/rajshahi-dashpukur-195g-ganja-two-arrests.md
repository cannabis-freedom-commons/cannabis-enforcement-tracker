Detective Branch police and Rajpara and Kashiadanga thana police, thana being
the local term for a police-station jurisdiction, carried out separate anti-drug
drives in Rajshahi city on Monday 2 November 2026. [@1] Rajpara police arrested
36-year-old Rumman Ali and 45-year-old Khondakar Shakil Hossain Palash at the
Dashpukur Bypass area at about 6 pm that day. [@1] Officers seized 195 grams of
ganja, the local term for cannabis, from the two men. [@1] Police said Rumman
already faced one drug case and Palash faced seven drug cases. [@1] Metropolitan
police spokesperson Deputy Police Commissioner (Sadar) Md. Gaziur Rahman
confirmed the account on Tuesday afternoon. [@1]

Police described the ganja seizure as part of a set of drives that together held
four people with heroin, tapentadol tablets and ganja. [@1] Police also said
they seized drug-sale cash and three mobile phones during the drives. [@1] In a
separate heroin action the same evening, Kashiadanga police arrested 32-year-old
Robiul Islam Minal with 300 grams of heroin and three mobile phones at
Kashiadanga crossing. [@1] Cases were filed against those arrested under the
Narcotics Control Act at the concerned stations, and they were sent to jail
through the court on Tuesday morning. [@1]
