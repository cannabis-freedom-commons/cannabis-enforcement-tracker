Military police from Rotam, the Mato Grosso tactical patrol battalion, arrested
three men aged 19, 20 and 40 in the Tijucal neighbourhood of Cuiabá, the capital
of Mato Grosso, on Friday 6 November 2026. [@2]

Residents told the officers that a man was selling drugs from a barbershop in
the neighbourhood where he also worked as a barber, and that the shop had a
heavy flow of apparent drug users. [@1] Officers went to the shop and saw the
owner handing a package to another man, and the two fled through the back of the
premises when they noticed the police. [@1] One man was caught, while the
barbershop owner tried to climb a laundry tank to jump the wall, fell, and
resisted arrest until officers restrained him. [@1]

A search of one suspect found portions of substances similar to cocaine paste,
marijuana and ice hidden in his underwear, and he said he had bought part of
them from the barbershop owner and the ice-like substance from another man at a
nearby address. [@1] The barbershop owner was found with ten portions of cocaine
paste, and a search of the shop found more drugs stored in a plastic container
on top of a fridge, along with cash and materials used to divide and package
drugs. [@1]

The officers then went to a house the suspect indicated, where the third man
lived, and his father let them in. [@1] Two portions of marijuana were found in
the third suspect's pocket, and he pointed officers to a dresser drawer holding
more drugs, cash and materials for selling the substances. [@1] The three
suspects were taken to the police station with all the seized material to record
the case and take further legal steps. [@1]
