Four young men in their twenties were tried for drug trafficking at the Bayonne
judicial court on 3 November 2026, with two of them also facing weapons charges.
[@1] The court sentenced all four to firm prison terms ranging from eight months
to five years and ordered their continued detention. [@1]

Police arrested the group on 25 September 2025, ending about a year and a half
of trafficking. [@1] Three of the defendants, aged 22 to 23, dealt mainly in
cocaine but also sold cannabis. [@1] They sold cannabis and cocaine locally in
the Pyrénées-Atlantiques and the Landes. [@1] A search recovered 12,000 euros in
cash, a pump-action shotgun and a pistol, reportedly bought in a Traveller camp.
[@1]

The network was run from the ZUP district of Bayonne. [@1] The drugs were cut
and packaged in a rented room at the Mer et golf loisirs hotel in Anglet. [@1]
That room was guarded around the clock and held a pistol inside. [@1] Two of the
defendants each supplied 50 to 100 clients. [@1] WhatsApp messages mentioned
daily takings of about 1,200 euros. [@1] Written messages, indiscreet phone
calls and easily traced movements gave investigators the evidence for the case.
[@1]
