A 48-year-old local resident of Kursk was found guilty of unlawfully cultivating
plants containing narcotic drugs on a large scale. [@1] The conviction was
entered under part 1 of article 231 of the Russian Criminal Code. [@1]
Prosecutors of the Central Administrative District of Kursk supported the state
prosecution in his criminal case. [@1]

The court found that the man had planted several cannabis seeds on the grounds
of his own house. [@1] He then regularly tended the plants in the subsequent
period, watering them, weeding the beds and clearing the plot of weeds. [@1]
Law-enforcement officers discovered the unlawful planting during
operative-search measures. [@1] An expert examination then confirmed that the
seized plants were cannabis containing a narcotic drug. [@1]
