Police in Rajbari Sadar detained four men with yaba and ganja in a special
operation, and a court later jailed each of them for a different term after a
summary trial. [@1]

Md Azizul Islam, 27, son of Sirajul Islam of Arenda village in Mulghor Union,
Rajbari Sadar upazila, was arrested with three yaba tablets and jailed for 14
days for drug use. [@1] Hasirul Molla, 40, son of the late Afsar Molla of the
same village, was arrested with one yaba tablet and jailed for seven days. [@1]
Md Sohag Mia, 26, son of Abul Hossain of Podmadi village in Nababpur Union,
Baliakandi upazila, was arrested with one puri of ganja and jailed for 12 days.
[@1]

The officer-in-charge said the four were produced before a court through a
summary trial, after which the court gave each of them a different jail term.
[@1] Uttam Kumar Ghosh said police operations would continue to prevent the
abuse and spread of drugs, control crime and ensure public security. [@1]
