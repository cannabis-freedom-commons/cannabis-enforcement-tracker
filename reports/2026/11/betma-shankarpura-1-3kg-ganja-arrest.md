Betma police arrested Shyam, also known as Bhaiyyu Masaniya, near the Raj
Rajeshwari Shiv temple at Shankarpura in the Betma police-station area of Indore
district after a tip-off that a man was standing there trying to sell illegal
ganja. [@1] Officers cordoned off the spot in the presence of independent panch
witnesses and detained him. [@1]

On questioning he identified himself as Shyam, a resident of Purni village in
Khandwa district who was then living in Dharamkunj Colony, Betma. [@1] A formal
search of his possession found 1 kg 300 g of illegal ganja, which police
estimated to be worth about 35,000 rupees. [@1]

Police booked him at Betma police station as case 653/2026 under sections 8/20
and 29 of the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, and named a
co-accused in the case. [@1] Police said the investigation was continuing and
that they were gathering information about other people linked to the drug
trade. [@1] The operation was carried out by Betma police station under the
direction of Indore Rural superintendent of police Rajendra Kumar Verma, with
additional superintendent Raj Krishna and Depalpur sub-divisional police officer
Priya Samrat supervising. [@1]
