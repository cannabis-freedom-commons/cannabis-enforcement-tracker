A woman was intercepted with a package of cannabis on her body as she tried to
enter the Penitenciária de Junqueirópolis in São Paulo state to visit her
imprisoned husband over the weekend. [@1] Penal police officers carrying out the
body-scanner search identified an irregular image in the pelvic region of the
inmate's wife. [@1]

When officers questioned her about the anomaly, the woman spontaneously handed
them a package containing a portion of cannabis. [@1] She was taken to the
Central de Flagrantes of the Civil Police, where the applicable measures were
adopted. [@1]

The prison opened an internal procedure to establish what happened. [@1] The
details were released on Monday 9 November 2026 by Ceproeste, the Penal
Enforcement Coordination for the Western Region. [@1]
