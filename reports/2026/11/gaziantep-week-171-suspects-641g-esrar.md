Gaziantep Chief Public Prosecutor’s Office coordinated a week of narcotics
operations carried out citywide by the provincial police’s anti-narcotics branch
from 26 October to 1 November 2026. [@1] Officers raided designated addresses
and carried out searches as part of the anti-drug effort. [@1] Legal proceedings
were initiated against a total of 171 suspects as a result of the week’s
operations. [@1] The operations were aimed at preventing drug trafficking and
use in Gaziantep. [@1]

The searches yielded 641 grams of esrar, the cannabis preparation known in
Turkey, alongside 3 kilograms 204 grams of methamphetamine, 218 grams of
synthetic cannabinoid and 4 grams of cocaine. [@1] Officers also seized 148 drug
pills and 3 precision scales believed to have been used in drug dealing. [@1]
The seized methamphetamine was the largest amount among the drugs recovered.
[@1]

The weekly results were published as a single tally for the coordinated
operations. [@1] Police said anti-drug operations remain a priority in Gaziantep
and that efforts to combat trafficking and use are continuing. [@1]
