The Pesaro court sentenced a man to 4 years and 6 months in prison after a trial
in which he was found guilty of aggravated sexual violence against a minor,
aggravated by the fact that he had drugged her with marijuana, and for supplying
cannabis to her on several occasions. [@1] The complainant, then 17, reported
the abuse in 2022 after her older step-brother had offered her marijuana to
smoke; the charges were brought by prosecutor Irene Lilliu. [@1]

The court also imposed a ban from public offices and from any role involving
minors, and awarded the victim, now an adult, a provisional sum of 30,000 euros
and 5,000 euros to her mother. [@1]
