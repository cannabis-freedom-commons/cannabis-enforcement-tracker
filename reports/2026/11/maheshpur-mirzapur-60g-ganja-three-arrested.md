Maheshpur Thana police arrested three alleged drug traders in a special
anti-drug operation in Jhenaidah district. [@1] A fourth person sensed the
police presence and fled from the scene. [@1] The arrests took place on the
night of 3 November 2026 on the paved road in front of Momin's house at Mirzapur
Kumillapara in Mandarbariya Union. [@1] Sub-Inspector Md Tipu Sultan with
officers and force was on special duty for drugs, weapons recovery and warrant
execution when a secret tip said several people had gathered there to sell
drugs. [@1]

Officers reached the spot immediately and caught the three red-handed as they
tried to flee. [@1] A search found 20 grams of cannabis in polythene in each
man's possession, totalling 60 grams of ganja, the local term for cannabis, with
an estimated market value of 1,800 taka. [@1] The arrested men were identified
as Roni Das, Raban Kumar and Md Sohel Rana, all residents of Mandarbariya Union
in Maheshpur. [@1] Police listed one more accused as absconding. [@1]

Maheshpur police filed regular case number 6 dated 4 November 2026 under section
36(1), table 19(a)/41 of the Narcotics Control Act 2018. [@1] Legal proceedings
to produce the arrested men before a court were under way, and police said such
operations would continue to maintain order and curb drugs. [@1]
