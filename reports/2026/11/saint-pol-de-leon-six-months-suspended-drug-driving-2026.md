A 27-year-old man was stopped by gendarmes at a routine roadside check in
Saint-Pol-de-Léon, Finistère, on 20 September 2026 at about 4.40 pm. [@1] He
appeared at the Brest criminal court on 9 November 2026 accused of driving under
the influence of drugs, possession and transport of drugs, and refusing to hand
his phone to police. [@1]

The prosecutor asked for eight months in prison, suspended. [@2]

In court he said he had never used alcohol or drugs and that the cocaine had
contaminated him when he handled it. [@2] He attended the hearing by video link
from Angers prison, where he was held on remand in a separate drug case, and had
no previous convictions. [@2]

The saliva test at the roadside came back positive for cocaine, while the
alcohol test was negative. [@2]

The gendarmes found nine grams of cannabis and six grams of cocaine on him, a
further five grams of cocaine in his car, and nothing at his home, according to
Le Télégramme. [@2]
