Valence municipal police intercepted a drone in the Fontbarlettes district
during the night of 3 to 4 November 2026. [@1] Video operators spotted the woman
preparing the drone for takeoff near rue Gounod before officers moved in and
detained her. [@2] The woman, described as homeless, was arrested at the scene.
[@1] She was described as being in an irregular situation and was placed in
custody at the Valence police station. [@1] She was then handed over to national
police officers. [@2]

Officers found a bundle on a cord attached to the drone holding two mobile
phones, 150 grams of cannabis resin and 15 grams of herbal cannabis. [@1] The
City of Valence gave the same inventory for the sock attached to the drone,
listing two mobile phones, 150 grams of cannabis resin and 15 grams of herbal
cannabis. [@2] The device was apparently about to take off for Valence prison
with phones and drugs. [@1] The drone was described as clearly intended for
delivery inside the Valence penitentiary centre. [@2]

The operation followed three other drone interceptions in the previous month by
the municipal police, all said to be bound for the Valence penitentiary centre.
[@1] The woman had already been arrested for similar acts in September and was
again placed in custody. [@2]
