The joint action was carried out on 1 November by the Anti-Crime and Cyber Unit,
the Anti-Narcotic Task Force and Kotwali police. [@2] Police arrested two women
traffickers from Nagpur in Raipur with ganja worth about Rs 98,000. [@1] The
women were identified as 60-year-old Ranjana alias Rajna Patre and 45-year-old
Seema alias Neelam Patre, residents of Nagpur in Maharashtra. [@2] They were
found near Babar Bungalow in Ahmed Ji Colony carrying ganja and looking for
buyers under a tamarind tree. [@2] When police tried to question them, both
started to flee, but the team surrounded and caught them. [@2]

Police put the estimated value of the seized ganja at Rs 98,600. [@3]

Police registered Crime No. 415/26 at Kotwali police station and invoked Section
20(B) of the NDPS Act against both women. [@3] Police said both women had
previously gone to jail in theft cases. [@2] Police said they were questioning
the women to find where they got the ganja and to whom it was to be supplied in
Raipur. [@2]
