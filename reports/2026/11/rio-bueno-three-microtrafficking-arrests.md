Carabineros on patrol in the centre of Río Bueno, Los Ríos Region, detected
people conducting a drug sale from a vehicle. [@1] Officers identity-checked
those involved and detained two men and a woman for alleged microtrafficking.
[@1] Police said all three detainees are adults and that two of them have prior
criminal records. [@1] The prosecution service directed that two of the three
accused go to detention-control hearings for microtrafficking. [@1] The third
person was left apercibido, meaning released pending a summons to the
prosecution service. [@1]

The drug seized in the procedure was marijuana, already portioned into
papelillos, small paper wraps. [@1] Officers also seized a bladed weapon, cash,
mobile phones and the vehicle the group was using. [@2] The local account adds
that officers found a digital scale and implements for portioning drugs. [@3]

The operation was an extraordinary preventive patrol by the SIP investigative
section of Río Bueno's Fourth Commissary under Major Juan Cáceres. [@3] Those
detained were held in flagrante delicto and the case file was placed at the
disposal of the Ministerio Público. [@3]
