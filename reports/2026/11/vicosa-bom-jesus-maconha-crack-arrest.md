A man was arrested in flagrante for illegal drug trafficking on the morning of
Thursday 5 November 2026 on Rua São Pedro in the Bom Jesus neighbourhood of
Viçosa, Minas Gerais. [@1]

Military police with the Tactical Mobile Platoon were on preventive patrol in an
area known for complaints about drug dealing when they saw a man repeatedly
entering a stairway and crouching among bushes and rubble. [@2] A personal
search found a mobile phone and money, with no illicit substance found on the
man at that point. [@2] Officers then searched the spot where the suspect had
been seen and found drugs packed for sale, including 17 buchas of a substance
resembling marijuana. [@2] The same search turned up cocaine and crack portions
and nine MDMA (ecstasy) tablets, alongside the cannabis, a phone and R$150 in
cash. [@1]

The man was told he was under arrest for drug trafficking, taken for medical
care and then handed to the Civil Police station with the seized material. [@1]
The Military Police said the action was part of its work against drug
trafficking and crime prevention in the municipality. [@2]
