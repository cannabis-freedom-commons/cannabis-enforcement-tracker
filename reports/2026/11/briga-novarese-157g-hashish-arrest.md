Italy's Carabinieri police arrested a 23-year-old man in the woods at Briga
Novarese on Wednesday afternoon, 4 November, during a patrol against drug
dealing. [@1,3] The operation took place at about 14:15 on Via San Colombano, in a
wooded area already known to law enforcement as a drug-dealing location. [@1,3]
The officers were from the Arona operational section of the Carabinieri's
operational and mobile radio unit. [@1] When the young man saw the Carabinieri,
they stopped him and checked him. [@1]

A search found about 157 grams (0.157 kg) of hashish, 14 grams of cocaine and 6
grams of heroin, 177 grams in total. [@1,2,3] Officers also seized two precision
scales and material used to package drug doses. [@1,3] They also seized two mobile
phones and five power banks. [@1]

Officers found €1,005 in cash, which they considered proceeds of drug dealing.
[@1,2,3] After the formalities, the 23-year-old was transferred to Novara prison.
[@1,2,3] He remained there at the disposal of judicial authorities. [@1]
