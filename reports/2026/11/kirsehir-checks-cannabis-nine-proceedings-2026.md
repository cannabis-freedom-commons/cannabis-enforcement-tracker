Kırşehir Provincial Public Order Branch officers with Kaman District police
carried out searches and inspections in Kırşehir. [@1] The checks seized
unlicensed weapons, cartridges, narcotic pills and cannabis described as esrar,
with nine people placed under judicial proceedings. [@1]

Officers found four unlicensed pistols with four magazines and 128 rounds, two
blank-firing pistols with two magazines and 11 blank rounds, and one unlicensed
hunting rifle with two cartridges. [@1] Officers separately seized 300 pills and
narcotic material. [@1] The nine people placed under proceedings were identified
only by the initials H.Ö., Ö.B., A.O., Ş.Y., K.Y., F.B., M.G., F.Ş. and B.A.
[@1]

The page was published on 6 November 2026 and gives no separate date for the
searches and inspections. [@1]
