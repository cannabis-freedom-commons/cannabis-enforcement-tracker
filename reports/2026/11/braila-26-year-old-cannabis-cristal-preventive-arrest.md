Brăila organised-crime police and prosecutors of DIICOT's Brăila territorial
office, Romania's directorate for organised crime and terrorism, investigated a
26-year-old person for continued trafficking in risk and high-risk drugs. [@1,2]
Prosecutors' case papers state that during 2026 the person obtained, held and
sold various quantities of cannabis and 3-CMC, a synthetic stimulant sold in
Romania under the generic name cristal, for sums between 400 and 1,300 lei. [@1,2]
Drugs the person allegedly traded were taken out of circulation during the
investigation. [@1,3]

On 5 November 2026 police carried out two searches in the municipality of
Brăila, finding and taking cannabis, a grinder, sums of money in lei and euros,
and other evidence. [@1,2]

DIICOT Brăila prosecutors ordered the person detained for 24 hours on Thursday,
5 November 2026. [@1,3] On Friday, 6 November, prosecutors asked the
rights-and-freedoms judge of the Brăila Tribunal to order the person's
preventive arrest for 30 days. [@1,2] Brăila county gendarmes supported the
operation. [@1,2]

Police stated that those under investigation keep their procedural rights and
the presumption of innocence throughout the criminal process. [@1,2]
