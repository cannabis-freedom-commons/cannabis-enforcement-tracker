Police arrested a 34-year-old history-sheeter near S P Pattinam in
Ramanathapuram district on suspicion of smuggling 19.8kg of hashish to Sri
Lanka. [@1,2] The arrested man was identified as Mano of Thiruvadanai, who was
involved in two narcotics cases earlier. [@1,2] A special team led by inspector
David Ravirajan had mounted surveillance near Pasiyamman temple beach at
Pasipattinam following a tip-off. [@1,2] Around 12.50pm, police intercepted Mano
and recovered 2kg of hashish from his possession. [@1,2]

After questioning him, the team searched his residence and seized another
17.8kg. [@1,2] The superintendent said the contraband is worth Rs 2 lakh per kg.
[@1] Police described the 19.8kg haul as worth nearly Rs 40 lakh. [@1] The
superintendent also said it would value around 10 lakh Sri Lankan rupees. [@1]

Police said Mano was booked earlier this year in connection with the seizure of
15kg of ganja by S P Pattinam police. [@1,2] He had been absconding in that case.
[@1] A fresh case has been registered under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic
Substances (NDPS) Act. [@1,2]
