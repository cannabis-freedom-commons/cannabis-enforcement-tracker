Kohat police arrested four alleged narcotics dealers in separate operations
carried out by City Police Station officers in Kohat district, Khyber
Pakhtunkhwa. [@1] Officers seized 16,880 grams (about 16.9 kg) of hashish and
1,903 grams of crystal meth, known locally as ice, from the four suspects. [@1]

The arrested men were identified as Basiruddin, son of Nizamuddin and a resident
of Garhi Mawaz Khan, Nauman, son of Shaukat and a resident of Lachi who lives in
Hafizabad, Shahid, son of Mir Zaman and a resident of Muhammadzai, and Daud, son
of Zahoor and a resident of Hafizabad. [@1] Separate cases were registered
against the four and they were handed to investigation staff for further legal
proceedings. [@1]

The operations followed directives from District Police Officer Kohat Rai Mazhar
Iqbal, a senior superintendent of police, to act against drug traffickers across
the district. [@1] The article reports the narcotics arrests alongside other
separate operations in the same district crackdown, including a search at Mills
police station and an intervention in a land dispute near Jarma police station.
[@1]

The figures are police totals for the four arrests and no split by suspect, or
street value for the drugs, was published. [@1]
