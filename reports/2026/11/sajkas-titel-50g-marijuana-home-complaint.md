Police in Titel will file a criminal complaint against a 21-year-old man from
Šajkaš together with the Basic Public Prosecutor's Office in Novi Sad, on
suspicion that he unlawfully kept drugs. [@1] In a search of the house where the
suspect was, police found about 50 grams of a plant substance suspected to be
marijuana and a digital scale for precise weighing. [@1] The Interior Ministry's
Novi Sad police administration, which covers Titel, issued the account of the
search and the planned complaint. [@3]

The ministry named the suspected offence as the unauthorised keeping of narcotic
drugs. [@2]

The Dnevnik daily, published in Novi Sad, gave the suspect's year of birth as
2005, which matches the age of 21 reported by Novosti. [@2]

The criminal complaint will be filed in the regular procedure, so no charge or
court stage followed the police search. [@1]
