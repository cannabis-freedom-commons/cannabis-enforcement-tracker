Rawalpindi police arrested four alleged drug suppliers in separate operations
across the district and recovered more than eight kilograms of hashish. [@2]
Westridge, Civil Lines, Pirwadhai and Taxila police each arrested one of the
four suspects in separate operations. [@2] Police registered separate cases
against all four arrested suspects. [@1]

Westridge police arrested a drug supplier. [@2] Westridge police recovered 2.6
kilograms of hashish from his possession. [@2] Civil Lines police apprehended
another suspect with hashish. [@2] The Civil Lines seizure was 2.2 kilograms of
hashish. [@2] Pir Wadhai police arrested a drug dealer. [@1] The Pir Wadhai
seizure was 2 kilograms and 100 grams of hashish. [@1] Taxila police arrested a
drug supplier. [@2] Taxila police recovered 1.2 kilograms of hashish. [@2]
Together the four station-level recoveries total 8.1 kilograms of hashish,
described by police as more than eight kilograms. [@1]

Police said further action was being taken to curb drug trafficking in the
district. [@2,3] The four operations were reported across Rawalpindi district.
[@2]

Rawalpindi Police also said operations would aim to protect young people from narcotics abuse. [@3]
