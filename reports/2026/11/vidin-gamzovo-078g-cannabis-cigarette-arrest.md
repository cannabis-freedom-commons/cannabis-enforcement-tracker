Police in Vidin detained a 20-year-old man from the village of Gamzovo after
patrol officers found a hand-rolled cigarette on him during a check. [@1] The
check took place in the Krum Bachvarov residential quarter of Vidin in the late
hours of 10 November 2026. [@1] Officers seized the cigarette and took the man
to the arrest facility at RU-Vidin, the regional police department for the town.
[@1]

The cigarette held about 0.78 grams of green, dry and leafy material. [@1]
According to the police account, the material resembled cannabis in appearance
and smell. [@1]

A file was opened in the case under article 354a of the Criminal Code, the
provision that covers drug offences. [@1] The Vidin police did not name the man,
giving only his age and home village. [@1]
