Two people who had travelled from Odisha were arrested by Asansol-Durgapur
police on Wednesday night, 4 November 2026, after officers found ganja, or
cannabis, in their possession. [@1] Mirror Media reported the seizure as about
28 kilograms of ganja. [@1] Prabhat Khabar said the two people had been bringing
ganja from Odisha when they were arrested on Wednesday. [@2] Prabhat Khabar
reported the seizure as 27.5 kilograms of ganja. [@2]

The arrests took place during a week-long special anti-drug campaign running
from 31 October to 7 November under instructions from India's Union Home
Ministry. [@1] The Police Commissioner's Detective Department led the campaign
across all police-station areas. [@2] Officers said they were carrying out
intelligence-led raids at different locations. [@1] Deputy Commissioner of
Police Sana Akhtar outlined the enforcement at a Thursday press conference. [@1]

Police said they were monitoring networks involved in illegal drug buying,
selling and supply. [@1] Officials said they were coordinating action across the
commissionerate to reach the supply chains and financial networks behind the
drug trade. [@1] Police said intelligence-based action would continue even after
the scheduled campaign period ended. [@1]
