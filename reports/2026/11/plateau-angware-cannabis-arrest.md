Troops of Operation Enduring Peace, known as OPEP, arrested a 25-year-old
suspected drug peddler at Angware in Jos East Local Government Area of Plateau
State. [@2] The suspect was identified as Samana Ayuba, aged 25. [@1] Security
sources said troops of Sector 1, Sub-Sector 12 made the arrest at about 9:30
p.m. on 1 November 2026 during a patrol in the area. [@2]

The troops intercepted Ayuba during the operation and recovered a quantity of
substances suspected to be cannabis. [@1] Following the arrest, the suspect and
the recovered items were handed over to Keystone Division at Angware in Jos East
Local Government Area. [@2] The handover was for further investigation and
necessary action. [@1]

The operation was described as part of ongoing efforts by Operation Enduring
Peace troops to combat criminal activities, including illicit drug trafficking,
and to enhance security across Plateau State. [@1] Both the interception and the
handover took place in the Angware area of Jos East. [@1]
