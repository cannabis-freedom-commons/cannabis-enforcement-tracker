Police from the Puthupettai station near Panruti, a town and taluk headquarters
in Tamil Nadu's Cuddalore district, went out on patrol under Sub-Inspector
Saranya and other officers. [@1]

Near the Karumahari mandapam at Thorappadi, by the Mariamman temple street, the
officers found two men selling ganja, the Tamil word for cannabis, and arrested
both of them. [@1] Police named the two men as Parasuraman, aged 25, son of
Pakkiri of Thorappadi Mariamman Kovil Street, and Madan, aged 21, son of
Marudhavanan. [@1]

Officers seized 80 grams of ganja, about 0.08 kilograms, from the two men, given
as one combined figure with no split between them. [@1] The account names no
charge, remand or court date for either man, and gives no value for the ganja
seized. [@1]
