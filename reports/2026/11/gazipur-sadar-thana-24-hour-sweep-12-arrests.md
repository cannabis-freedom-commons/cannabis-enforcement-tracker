Police of the Sadar Thana of Gazipur Metropolitan Police arrested 12 people in a
special sweep over 24 hours and reported seizing a large quantity of drugs. [@1]
The operation was carried out on the orders of Gazipur's new police commissioner
soon after he took up the post. [@1]

Several teams led by Sadar Thana officer-in-charge Md Aminul Islam and
investigation inspector Mehedi Hasan raided different parts of the city. [@1]

Two people were arrested with 1 kg of ganja, two with 6 grams of heroin and four
more with 183 yaba tablets, and the remaining four were held on regular cases
and arrest warrants. [@1]

Five separate drug cases were opened against those arrested in the drug
recoveries. [@1] The Sadar Thana said sweeps of this kind against crime, drugs
and wanted accused would continue. [@1]
