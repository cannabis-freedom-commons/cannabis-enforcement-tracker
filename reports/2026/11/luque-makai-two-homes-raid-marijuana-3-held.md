Two adjoining homes in Barrio Maka’i in Luque were raided on Thursday in an
operation against microtrafficking. [@1] The operation took place at about 15:30
on Calle Tomás Ozuna, with the accompaniment of officers from the Comisaría 3ª
Central de Luque. [@1] Police apprehended 30-year-old Juan Bautista Mendieta
Ricardo and a 17-year-old adolescent. [@1] The teenager’s mother later came to
the police station and was also taken into custody after a records check showed
she had a pending detention order. [@1]

Anti-narcotics agents seized suspected crack cocaine, marijuana, a revolver,
cash and other items allegedly used to sell drugs. [@1] In the first home
officers found 16 doses of suspected crack cocaine weighing 5.52 grams in total,
plus 2.86 grams of suspected marijuana. [@1] Officers also seized a revolver
with two unfired 22-calibre cartridges, a mobile phone and G. 155,000 in cash in
the first home. [@1] In the second home officers found two lots of suspected
crack: 25 grams described as about 150 doses and 10 grams described as about 60
doses. [@1] From the second home they also took G. 138,000, a mobile phone, a
blue Kenton Star motorcycle and alleged portioning items including polyethylene
bags, a knife and a cutter. [@1]

Comisaría 3ª chief Gabriel Rivas said the operation was coordinated with the
Anti-Narcotics Department and added that since taking office his unit had
already carried out eight to ten raids in different parts of the city. [@1] The
woman’s pending order concerned an alleged breach of Law 1.340/88 on narcotics
trafficking and had been issued by prosecutor Andrea Soledad Ríos of the
specialised anti-drug unit. [@1]
