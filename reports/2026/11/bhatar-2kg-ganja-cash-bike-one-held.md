Bhatar police station officers in Purba Bardhaman, West Bengal, recovered about
2 kg of ganja that police valued at four lakh rupees on Friday. [@1] Police
detained one person in the case, named as Udaychand Roy, a resident of the
Banpash Kamarpada area. [@1]

The same report says separate drives were also carried out in Shaktigarh and
Madhabdihi. [@1]

The seizure took place in Purba Bardhaman district, where the Bhatar police
station is responsible for the area. [@1]
