Police provisionally arrested one 36-year-old man from Hanau in the night to
Saturday after he allegedly caused a disturbance in the city. [@1,3] Officers
searching him and the items he was carrying found small amounts of drugs. [@1,3]
At the request of the Hanau public prosecutor's office, the suspect was brought
before an investigating judge at Hanau Local Court on Sunday. [@1,3] The judge
issued an arrest warrant over suspected armed trafficking of drugs and cannabis
in a not insignificant quantity and ordered pretrial detention. [@1,3] He was
taken directly to a prison after the hearing. [@1,3]

A court-ordered search of his flat seized about 1.4 kg of marijuana and about
900 grams of hashish, together about 2.3 kg of cannabis. [@1,3] The same search
also seized about 1.4 kg of cocaine. [@2,3] Officers also seized small amounts of
ketamine and ecstasy, a ready-to-access blank-firing gun and a telescopic baton,
plus a money-counting machine, several mobile phones and packaging material.
[@1,3]

The account is a joint press release by the Hanau prosecutor's office and the
South-East Hesse police presidium. [@2,3] Police said investigations are
continuing. [@2,3]
