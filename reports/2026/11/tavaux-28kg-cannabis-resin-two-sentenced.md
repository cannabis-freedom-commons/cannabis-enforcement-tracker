Jura customs officers arrested two Moroccan nationals resident in Spain on 9
March 2026 at Tavaux, in the Jura department of eastern France. [@1] The
officers found 28 kilos of cannabis resin in a compartment hidden inside their
car. [@1] The two men had left Catalonia in Spain and were driving to Germany.
[@1]

They were tried on 3 November 2026 before the tribunal judiciaire of
Lons-le-Saunier, the court for the Jura. [@1] One of the two men was absent,
hospitalised in Barcelona, while the other appeared with an interpreter. [@1]
The prosecution asked for 18 months in prison for each man and the court
followed those requests. [@1] Both men were sentenced to 18 months in prison,
suspended, and banned from French territory for ten years. [@1]

The defence failed to persuade the judges, who were met with the laconic answers
of the defendant present. [@1] Officers found photos and videos on his phone,
filmed two days before the trip and showing him with large quantities of
cannabis. [@1] He maintained that he knew nothing and was travelling to Germany
for work, blaming the other defendant, his "ami", for organising everything.
[@1]
