A 43-year-old man appeared before the Bruges police court after attracting
police attention with conspicuous driving in Zedelgem. [@1] The offence dated to
4 August 2026 in Zedelgem, where the driver stood out for his sporty driving
style. [@1] He allegedly took bends without using the brakes and turned off
three times without using his indicators. [@1] At a roadside check he tested
positive for THC, the active substance in cannabis. [@1]

The defendant produced several negative urine tests and said he had largely left
cannabis use behind him. [@1] He admitted, however, that he had smoked one joint
on his birthday at the end of October. [@1] The judge noted that the man was no
stranger to the courts because he had already been convicted twice for drugs.
[@1] The man said he needed his car for work and had already had to buy a
bicycle to get around. [@1]

The Bruges police court fined the 43-year-old man 3,250 euros and imposed a
four-month driving ban. [@1] The court also ordered a medical and psychological
examination. [@1] Under Article 42 of the Traffic Act, he can apply to be
declared fit to drive again only after six months, with favourable medical
results and negative drug tests. [@1]
