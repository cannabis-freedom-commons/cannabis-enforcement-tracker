Members of 10 South African Infantry Battalion operating in the Musina area
under Operation CORONA intercepted a consignment containing 78 kg of dagga. [@1]
The interception took place during border security operations near Zimbabwe as
the battalion stepped up patrols along the Limpopo River. [@1] The accounts
describe the seized cannabis as dagga. [@1]

The SANDF said the dagga was allegedly intended to be smuggled into South
Africa. [@1] The SANDF account republished by Arrive Alive likewise says the
battalion intercepted illicit goods that included 78 kg of dagga intended to be
smuggled into South Africa. [@2] Soldiers estimated its street value at R473
000. [@1]

The seizure formed part of a series of day and night operations conducted after
10 South African Infantry Battalion took over border duties from 4 Artillery
Regiment in Musina. [@1] The repositioning between 4 Artillery Regiment and 10
South African Infantry Battalion in Musina took place under Operation CORONA,
after which the incoming battalion began operations. [@2] The incoming battalion
immediately began conducting area domination operations along the Limpopo River,
which forms part of the border between South Africa and Zimbabwe. [@1] Operation
CORONA forms part of the military's ongoing border safeguarding
responsibilities, with soldiers conducting patrols and monitoring vulnerable
crossing points. [@1] The SANDF said the operations demonstrate its continued
commitment to safeguarding South Africa's borders and protecting the country's
territorial integrity. [@1]
