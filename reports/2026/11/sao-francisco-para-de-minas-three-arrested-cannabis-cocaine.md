Military police arrested three men, aged 30, 31 and 32, during an operation
against drug trafficking in the São Francisco neighbourhood of Pará de Minas in
Minas Gerais on the night of Wednesday 4 November 2026. [@1] JC Notícias
reported the same raid: the Military Police arrested the three men in Pará de
Minas on the night of Wednesday 4 November during an operation against drug
trafficking. [@2] Teams of the Military Police's Specialised Policing Company,
known as GER, went to a house in the neighbourhood after intelligence reported
it was being used to sell drugs. [@1] Approaching the property, the officers saw
a precision scale and a portion of a substance resembling cocaine on the porch.
[@1] The 30-year-old ran out through the back of the house and was chased and
caught, and a check showed an open arrest warrant against him issued by the
court of Ribeirão das Neves. [@1] Two other men, aged 31 and 32, were found in
the house, where officers located more drugs and a loaded .38 revolver with six
intact cartridges on the route the younger man had taken to flee. [@1]

Police listed the haul as 33 packets and two larger portions of cocaine, a
portion of cannabis, a precision scale, two mobile phones, a revolver and six
cartridges. [@1] JC Notícias reported the cannabis taken separately as "uma
bucha de maconha", a single bud. [@2] The 32-year-old is to face a
drug-trafficking charge, the 31-year-old was held for association for
trafficking, and the 30-year-old was held on the warrant and also for illegal
possession of a firearm and association for trafficking. [@1] All three were
taken with the seized material to the Pará de Minas Civil Police station. [@1]
