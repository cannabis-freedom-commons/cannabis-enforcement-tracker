The seccional court of Trujillo, Colón, coordinated on 11 November 2026 the
official incineration of a load of marijuana held in 16 sacks, seized earlier in
anti-drug-trafficking operations on Honduras's Atlantic coast. [@1] The Public Prosecutor said the 16 sacks of supposed marijuana were burned at the Trujillo naval station, with judicial, prosecutor and police authorities present. [@4] The whole
load was destroyed in the judicial act, carried out under strict security and
transparency measures. [@1] Prosecutors of the Trujillo local prosecutor's
office and specialists of the Forensic Medicine Directorate took part, after
field tests and toxicological analysis to certify the purity and exact weight of
the drug. [@1,4] Firefighters controlled the fire and the environmental and safety
conditions of the combustion, while personnel of the Puerto Castilla naval base
kept the perimeter guard during the transfer and the execution of the court
order. [@1] The act complied with Honduras's Code of Criminal Procedure and its
law on the misuse of and illicit traffic in drugs and psychotropic substances.
[@1]

The Judiciary reported that in the Trujillo, Colón, court of first instance the
incineration of a considerable quantity of marijuana in 16 sacks was
coordinated, with the local prosecutor's office, Forensic Medicine, the fire
service and the Puerto Castilla naval base taking part. [@2] The Public
Prosecutor's Office said the drug had been seized by the anti-drug-trafficking
directorate's Atlantic Littoral regional office together with the Xatruch joint
task force in a mountainous area of Trujillo, Colón. [@3]
