Carabineros carried out five extraordinary rounds across the Los Ríos Region
during the last week, conducting 937 identity checks and detaining 14 people.
[@1] The operation was disclosed in the regional Police Committee balance headed
by presidential regional delegate Vicky Carrasco. [@1] That balance also
reported an 11 percent decrease in serious crimes in the area during the period
evaluated. [@1]

In those rounds, officers seized 22 kilograms of drugs and 237 cannabis plants.
[@1] That combined seizure was valued at 220 million pesos in the reported
balance. [@1] The same balance put total seizures of illicit substances in Los
Ríos at more than one tonne so far in 2026. [@1]

In the breakdown, the PDI reported 300 kilograms of drugs seized during the
year, while Carabineros reported more than 700 kilograms taken off the informal
market. [@1] The 237 cannabis plants are the separately supported cannabis
component of this mixed seizure, while the 22-kilogram and annual weights cover
unspecified drugs. [@1]
