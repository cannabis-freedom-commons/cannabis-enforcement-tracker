Chengalpattu district police reported seizing 9 kilograms of cannabis and 90
kilograms of banned gutka tobacco products during a nine-day enforcement drive,
and said shops that violated the rules were sealed. [@1] Chengalpattu district
police said they began the anti-drug enforcement drive on the 2nd of the month
across the district police area. [@1] Across the following nine days, police
registered five cases relating to cannabis sales, seizing 9 kilograms of
cannabis described as ganja and arresting 11 people. [@1]

In the same period, officers registered 19 cases concerning sales of banned
gutka tobacco products and seized 90 kilograms of those tobacco products. [@1]
Shops found selling the banned tobacco were sealed through the food safety
department, and nine people connected with those tobacco cases were arrested.
[@1]

The enforcement area covers 20 police stations under the control of the
Chengalpattu police supervisory office. [@1] Police said they had received
repeated complaints that cannabis and intoxicant sales kept increasing in the
areas within those station limits, which prompted the special drive. [@1]
