Officers from the Cedry Wielkie police station stopped a Volkswagen driver for a
road check after seeing him drive uncertainly on a road in Cedry Wielkie
municipality after 10 p.m. on Tuesday 10 November. [@1] The man drove into an
intersection and then stopped, as if unsure which way to turn, prompting the
road check. [@1] The driver was a 28-year-old resident of Pruszcz Gdanski
municipality who had no driving licence. [@1] During the conversation, he spoke
incoherently and behaved nervously. [@1] A drug test indicated that the
28-year-old was under the influence of amphetamine and marijuana. [@1]

Officers searched the Volkswagen driver and found two packets hidden in a
cigarette pack. [@1] One packet held a white powder and the other held plant
material. [@1] Preliminary findings identified the substances as amphetamine and
marijuana. [@1] Officers detained the man and sent the seized drugs to an expert
for further testing. [@1] The specialist confirmed the officers' preliminary
findings. [@1]

Officers also secured a sample of his blood for analysis of intoxicant content.
[@1] The gathered evidence allowed officers to charge the detainee with drug
possession. [@1] A further charge for driving under the influence was not
excluded. [@1] He will also answer in court for driving despite having no
licence. [@1] Both possession and driving under the influence carry up to 3
years' imprisonment. [@1] Driving without a licence is an offence punishable by
a fine and a court driving ban. [@1]
