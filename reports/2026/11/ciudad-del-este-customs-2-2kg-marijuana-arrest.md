Customs officers in the primary customs zone of Ciudad del Este detained a
26-year-old Brazilian man who was carrying 2.2 kilograms of marijuana in a
backpack. [@1] The routine check was carried out by officials of the Dirección
Nacional de Ingresos Tributarios (DNIT), Paraguay's tax and customs authority,
on 10 November 2026 at about 19:50, according to the Public Ministry. [@1]
During the inspection the officers found four packages of a suspicious substance
and three mobile phones in the young man's possession. [@1]

Specialists of the Secretaría Nacional Antidrogas (Senad), Paraguay's anti-drug
agency, confirmed with a field narcotest that the four packages contained
marijuana, and the weighing put the seized amount at 2.2 kilograms. [@1] The
evidence was taken to Senad's headquarters. [@1] Ahora CDE reported that
officers of DNIT's customs guard division at the Friendship Bridge, with the
Paraguayan Navy's Eastern Naval Area, found four polyethylene packages in the
backpack as the man entered Paraguarian territory. [@2] Ahora CDE identified the
man as Matheus Henrique Alves Paulin, resident in Foz de Iguaçu, Brazil, who was
carrying the backpack as he entered the country. [@2] Ahora CDE reported that
the case was referred to prosecutor Manuel Rojas of the Specialized Fiscal Unit
in the Fight against Drug Trafficking of Alto Paraná, who took intervention.
[@2]

Prosecutor Luis Fernando Escobar, of the Specialized Unit for the Fight against
Drug Trafficking No. 1 of Alto Paraná, charged the man with possession of
narcotic substances and asked that he be held in preventive detention. [@1] The
prosecution also asked for four months to deepen the investigation, during which
it plans to run laboratory analyses, arrange the destruction of the drug and
take witness statements. [@1] The Brazilian remained in custody at Senad's
headquarters, at the disposal of the Penal Court of Guarantees. [@1]
