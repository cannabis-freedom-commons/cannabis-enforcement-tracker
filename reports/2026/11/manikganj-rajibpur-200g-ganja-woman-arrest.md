Officers of the Department of Narcotics Control arrested 27-year-old Tahmina
Akhtar with 200 grams of ganja during an operation in the Rajibpur Bazar area of
Krishnapur Union, Manikganj Sadar upazila. [@1] The team searched the house she
rents from a man named Siddique, where she lives, at about 1:15 pm on Monday, 9
November. [@1]

The raid was led by Sub-Inspector Md. Jamal Hossain under the overall
supervision of Humayun Kabir Khandaker, deputy director of the department's
Manikganj district office. [@1] Officers recovered 200 grams of ganja from the
room and arrested Tahmina Akhtar on the spot. [@1]
