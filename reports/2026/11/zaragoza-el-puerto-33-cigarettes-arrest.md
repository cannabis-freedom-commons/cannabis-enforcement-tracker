National Police officers arrested a man caught in the act in the sector El
Puerto of Zaragoza, in Antioquia's Bajo Cauca subregion, after a patrol,
stop-and-search and background check. [@1] Police found 33 handmade marijuana
cigarettes on him, which they seized. [@1]

The Antioquia Police Department announced the arrest in a post on its official
account. [@3] The police said the arrest was for the crime of trafficking,
manufacturing or carrying narcotics, article 376 of the Criminal Code. [@1]

The man and the seized items were handed to the 142nd Sectional Prosecutor's
Office in Zaragoza for the judicial process. [@1]

Minuto30, which published on 6 November, said the arrest had happened according
to unofficial accounts and described the man as not convicted. [@2]
