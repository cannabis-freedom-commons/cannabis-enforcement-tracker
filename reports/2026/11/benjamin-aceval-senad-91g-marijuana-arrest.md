Special agents of the Dirección de Operaciones Urbanas of Paraguay's anti-drug
secretariat, SENAD, raided a house in the Cerrito neighbourhood of Benjamín
Aceval that was used as a marijuana and cocaine sales point. [@1] The woman held
was Dorca Arza Benítez, aged 36, who used her own home for the drug sales. [@1]

Officers seized 91 grams of marijuana and 30 doses of cocaine from the house.
[@1] Prosecutor Norma Paredes accompanied the operation. [@1] SENAD's own
account confirms the arrest of the 36-year-old woman and the seizure of the 91
grams of marijuana and 30 doses of cocaine at the house. [@2]

The agency described the raid as part of its standing work to identify and
intervene in neighbourhood drug-distribution points. [@2]
