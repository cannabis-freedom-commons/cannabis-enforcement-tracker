Thanjavur District Collector Revathi ordered Rasikumar of Thiruvonam Nattuchalai
detained under the Goondas Act on Monday, and he was lodged in jail. [@1] The
order followed the recommendation of District Superintendent of Police K.
Sundaravadhanam, after the Pattukottai sub-division Assistant Superintendent of
Police proposed Goondas Act action against him. [@1] Police describe Rasikumar
as a history-sheeted offender linked to the ganja case, with ganja the local
term for cannabis and the Goondas Act Tamil Nadu's preventive-detention law for
habitual offenders. [@1]

The underlying case dates to 20 September 2026 within Pattukottai Taluk police
station limits. [@1] Near Ponnavarayankottai Kudikadu, police arrested eight
people including Rasikumar for allegedly stashing ganja in a car. [@1] Officers
seized 6.226 kg of ganja from the group, along with banned sharp weapons. [@1]
Police registered a case over the seizure and all eight arrested people were
jailed. [@1]

Only Rasikumar among the eight was placed under Goondas Act detention in the
Monday action. [@1] The detention is a Collector-ordered preventive detention
and not a court-imposed prison sentence. [@1]
