On Monday 9 November 2026, officers of the Maritime Intelligence Cell and the
Divisional Crime Intelligence Unit dismantled a cannabis crop hidden in a
sugarcane field at Petite-Retraite. [@1] The intervention was carried out by DPS
Rungapen of the Maritime Intelligence Cell with members of the Divisional Crime
Intelligence Unit Northern. [@2] The operation took place at about 9:30 a.m.
after police gathered intelligence. [@2] The cultivators had not been identified
when the accounts appeared. [@2] An investigation was opened to find those
responsible for the cannabis cultivation. [@2]

Officers uprooted and seized 347 plants of cannabis, called gandia, from a
sugarcane field at Petite-Retraite. [@1] The plants measured between 6 and 39
centimetres tall and were uprooted and secured. [@2] Police said they acted on
precise intelligence to break up the cultivation concealed among the sugarcane.
