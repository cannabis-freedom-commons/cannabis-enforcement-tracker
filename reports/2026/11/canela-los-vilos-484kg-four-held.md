Carabineros detained four people in the first of two weekend operations on Route
5 Norte in Canela. [@1,3,8,10] The first Canela checks were carried out at kilometres 309 and 282 of Route 5 Norte during Friday dawn. [@2,4,6,7,8,11] The four were an Ecuadorian aged 43, two Dominicans aged
28 and 37, and a Chilean aged 26. [@1,2,3,4,5,6,8,10,11] Police said some of them acted as punta
de lanza lookouts, driving in another vehicle to warn of checkpoints and ease
the drug load through. [@1,6,8] One of the four had a criminal record for drug
trafficking. [@1,8] On the prosecutor's orders, the four faced a detention-control
hearing on Saturday. [@1,2,8] The court set a 70-day period for the investigation, with the four held in preventive prison during that stage. [@9] The four were formalized as authors of illicit drug trafficking under Law 20,000. [@9] On 7 November the Los Vilos court declared the detentions legal and ordered preventive prison for the four accused. [@9] The four suspects arrested in Canela remain in pre-trial custody as prosecutors build their case. [@11]

In Canela officers seized 83 packages of processed marijuana weighing 86 kilos
625 grams, along with a Suzuki Swift car, a Chevrolet N300 van, six mobile
phones and more than $360,000 in cash. [@1,2,4,5,6,7,8,11] Hours later, about a hundred
kilometres south at kilometre 198 in Los Vilos, officers found a pickup carrying
377 packages weighing 398 kilos 360 grams, with a pistol and two cartridges
inside. [@1,4,10,11] The pickup found in Los Vilos had no outstanding theft report. [@2,8] Investigations into the Los Vilos pickup truck, including a weapons charge connected to the recovered firearm, are ongoing. [@11] Together the two seizures totalled 484 kilos 985 grams, equal to
969,970 doses, which police valued at $3,879,880,000. [@1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8,10]

The operations were carried out by the specialised OS7 Limarí unit with
Carabineros highway units deployed on the road. [@1,8] The operations were carried out in the framework of the 53rd anniversary of the OS7 anti-narcotics department. [@10] Coquimbo zone chief General
Christian Brebi said the deployments came from analysis work rather than random
checks on the country's main overland route. [@1,8] He said the nearly 500 kilos
were bound for central Chile, calling the result a blow to drug trafficking and
organised crime. [@1,8]
