Police arrested five people in the Chyandanda area of Dharan Sub-Metropolitan City-20, Sunsari, after finding them loading about 210 kg of cannabis packed in eight bundles into two Scorpio SUVs. [@1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8] Officers reached the spot at about 3 a.m. on Monday, 2 November 2026, on a tip-off, and fired two warning shots into the air when the suspects allegedly tried to flee with the cannabis. [@1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8]

Those arrested were Ramsagar Kumar Yadav, 24, of Dakneshwori Municipality-10 in Saptari, who was driving vehicle Ba 1 Cha 5038; Rajendraprasad Mandal, 45, of Rajgadh Rural Municipality-1 in Saptari; Santosh Thakur, 38, of Madhubani district in India's Bihar state; Rajkumar Shah, 59, of Chhinnamasta Rural Municipality-6 in Saptari, who was driving vehicle Ko 1 Cha 8182; and Amrit Bishungkhe, 21, of Dharan Sub-Metropolitan City-11 in Sunsari. [@1,3,4,5,6,7,8] Police said further investigation into the case is under way. [@1,3,5,6,7,8]

Police reported the seized marijuana as 209 kilograms and 420 grams. [@3]
