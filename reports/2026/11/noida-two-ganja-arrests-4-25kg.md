Police in Noida arrested two men accused of selling ganja illegally, one from
the Phase-1 police station and one from Sector-126 police station. [@1] Officers
found a total of 4.25 kilograms of ganja on the two men, police said. [@1]
Police said the accused made the ganja into small packets and sold them to
people. [@1] Police were gathering information about other people linked to the
accused men's network. [@1] Additional Deputy Commissioner of Police Abhay Kumar Mishra later said an FIR had been registered and the accused arrested. [@2]

Phase-1 station police stopped Ganesh Ghosh near the Sector-10 substation during
a checking drive. [@1,2] Seeing the police, he ran toward a ruined room and was caught after officers surrounded him. [@2] That action was around midnight on the night of 4 November, in a park near the Sector-10 power house. [@2] A search found 1,100 grams of ganja on Ghosh. [@1]
Assistant Commissioner of Police Mayank Tiwari said Ghosh is originally from
Malda district in West Bengal and currently rents a home in Harola, a Noida
locality. [@1] Ghosh is 24 and lives in Sharma Market, Harola. [@2] Questioning showed that Ghosh sold packets of ganja around
factories and slums, police said. [@1,2] He brought the ganja from Delhi. [@2]

Sector-126 station police arrested Jitendra, also known by his aliases Jeetu and
Machhar, near the Asgarpur cut in Sector 128. [@1] Officers recovered 3,150
grams of ganja from Jitendra. [@1] Station house officer Sumnesh Kumar said
Jitendra bought ganja cheaply in Delhi and brought it to Noida, where he made it
into packets and sold them at high prices. [@1]
