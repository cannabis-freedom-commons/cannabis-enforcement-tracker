Two Graettinger residents were arrested on drug charges on the morning of Monday
9 November 2026. [@1] The Palo Alto County Sheriff's Department was called to a
disturbance at 307 South Cameron Avenue shortly before 10 a.m. [@1] The
department identified them as 37-year-old Baley Dietrich and 18-year-old Landen
Dietrich, both of Graettinger. [@1] KILR, a local radio station, reported that
the disturbance was called in shortly before 9:55 a.m. [@2]

The two were at the address when deputies responded, according to the sheriff's
department. [@1] After investigating, deputies took both into custody and
charged them with first-offense possession of marijuana and possession of drug
paraphernalia. [@1] KILR described the marijuana charge as first-offense
possession of a controlled substance, marijuana. [@2] Both were booked into the
Palo Alto County Jail to await initial appearances before a magistrate, the
station reported. [@2]
