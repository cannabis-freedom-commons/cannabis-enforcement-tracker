Officers of the Bizerte regional anti-drug brigade dismantled a gang of three
young men, all from the Bizerte region, who specialised in dealing drugs among
young people and schoolchildren. [@1]

After a series of simultaneous raids the brigade seized 52 pieces of Indian
hemp, locally known as zatla, and 35 narcotics tablets. [@1] Officers also
seized a sizeable amount of cash and a motorcycle from the three. [@1]

The public prosecutor ordered the three to be held and the legal steps to be
completed before they are brought before it. [@1]
