Two men were arrested in flagrante on the main Zapatero-Jalapa highway, in the
community of San Felipe in the municipality of Jonuta, Tabasco. [@1] The two
were allegedly caught carrying out exchanges of narcotics. [@1]

Officers seized a .223-calibre long gun and various doses of suspected marijuana
from one of the men. [@1] The other man was found with a .45-calibre handgun,
various doses of marijuana, 260 pesos in cash and a mobile phone. [@1]

Both men were detained and handed to the authorities with the seized weapons and
objects, to determine their legal situation. [@1]
