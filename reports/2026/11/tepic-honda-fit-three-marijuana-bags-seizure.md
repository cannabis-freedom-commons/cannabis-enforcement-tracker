The Nayarit State Prosecutor's Office secured a Honda Fit during an operation in
the Prieto Crispín neighbourhood of Tepic. [@1]

Inside the car officers found 109 bags with a crystal-like substance, 50 with
white powder resembling cocaine, 30 with brown solid resembling base paste and
three medium bags with apparently marijuana. [@1] A second account likewise
reports three medium bags with green, dry plant material resembling marijuana,
along with 21 crystal pipes. [@2]

The vehicle and all the seized items were secured and placed at the disposal of
the Public Prosecutor, which will investigate the likely offence against health.
[@3] The Prosecutor's Office did not say whether anyone was detained during the
operation. [@1]
