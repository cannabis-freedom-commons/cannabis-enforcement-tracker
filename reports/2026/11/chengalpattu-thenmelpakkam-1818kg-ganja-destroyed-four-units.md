Police burned 1,818 kg of ganja seized from cases in the Tambaram and Avadi
police commissionerates and in Kanchipuram and Tiruvallur districts at a private
medical-waste incineration company in Thenmelpakkam, Chengalpattu district. [@1]

Of the total, 810 kg came from 227 cases registered at 25 police stations under
the Tambaram Police Commissionerate. [@1] The Avadi Police Commissionerate
accounted for 906 kg, Kanchipuram district for 37 kg and Tiruvallur district for
65 kg. [@1]

The ganja was burned in front of senior officers after a court order, on the day
before the report was published. [@1] Police said the drugs destroyed were worth
up to 10 crore rupees (about 100 million rupees). [@1] Ganja and other drugs
seized by police in Chennai and Chengalpattu district areas are routinely burned
at the G.J. Multi Cleave private company in Thenmelpakkam on court orders. [@1]
