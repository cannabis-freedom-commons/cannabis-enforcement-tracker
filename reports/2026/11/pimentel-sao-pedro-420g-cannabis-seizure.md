A Patrulhamento Tatico Movel team from the 30th Military Police Battalion was
patrolling Rua Manoel Carreiro de Melo in Pimentel. [@1] The team spotted a
group of people who fled when they noticed the approaching patrol vehicle. [@1]
Officers searched the surroundings and found narcotics hidden at different
points of an alleyway. [@1] No suspect was located or arrested. [@1]

Officers seized 420 grams of maconha. [@1] The same seizure included 240 grams
of cocaine and 68 grams of crack. [@1] Police put the combined total at 728
grams of narcotics. [@1] The material was taken to the 110th Civil Police
station, where the incident was registered. [@1]

Pimentel is one of the communities in the upper part of the populous Sao Pedro
neighbourhood. [@1] The incident occurred late on Saturday night. [@1] The
action was part of the 30th Battalion's daily confrontation with the local
drug-trafficking movement. [@1]
