Joint raids by the Koraput district police and the district excise department
destroyed 30,000 ganja plants in Koraput district, according to Eenadu. [@1] The
district superintendent of police said special teams led by district Excise
Superintendent Arun Kumar Padi carried out the raids and destroyed large
quantities of the crop. [@1]

12,000 plants were destroyed at Atal Guda in the Boipariguda block. [@1] 18,000
plants were destroyed in the Nandapur block. [@1]

Koraput police said the Nandapur police station raided hilly areas near Kasandi
and Dabuguda, found illegal cannabis cultivation on about nine acres of land and
uprooted and destroyed around 18,000 plants. [@2]
