A court in Iași sentenced a man from the city to two years and ten months in
prison for continued trafficking of risk drugs. [@1] The judgment is criminal
decision no. 374/2026. [@1] The court found him guilty of five material acts of
drug trafficking. [@1] The judges suspended execution of the prison term under
supervision for four years. [@1] During that period he must report to the Iași
Probation Service, keep to the supervision schedule and announce any change of
home or workplace or any trip longer than five days. [@1] He must also complete
a social reintegration programme and 100 working days of unpaid community work
at the Iași Clinical Recovery Hospital or the Iași General Directorate for
Social Assistance and Child Protection. [@1]

He sold cannabis, including to undercover investigators. [@1] The court ordered
the confiscation of 1,800 lei earned from sales to undercover investigators, 210
lei from transactions with two witnesses and 920 lei seized from the defendant.
[@1] It also ordered the confiscation of 28.37 grams of cannabis left after
laboratory analyses, stored at the Iași county police evidence room. [@1] Judges
ordered the confiscation of a plastic box containing THC and two mobile phones
used in the offence. [@1]

The defendant was held in detention, preventive arrest and then house arrest
between 20 May and 11 November 2026. [@1] The court found that the house arrest
had ended by law. [@1] The cannabis is to be destroyed after the judgment
becomes final, with counter-samples kept. [@1]
