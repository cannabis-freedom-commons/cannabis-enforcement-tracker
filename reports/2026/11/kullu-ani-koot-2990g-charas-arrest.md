The Ani sub-divisional detection-cell team was patrolling the area and
conducting intelligence surveillance. [@1] The team received a tip-off about
narcotics being stored in Koot village in the Ani police-station area. [@1]
Acting on the tip, the police team promptly raided the relevant house and began
a search. [@1]

Police identified the house as belonging to Jovandasi, wife of Gopal Singh, a
resident of Koot village, post office Lagauti, Ani tehsil. [@1] During the house
search, police recovered 2.990 kilograms of charas from inside. [@1] Police
seized the charas and arrested the woman on the spot. [@1]

Following the recovery, Ani police station registered a case against the accused
woman under Section 20 of the NDPS Act. [@1] Police said legal proceedings have
started and the accused woman is being questioned to gather further information
about the seized charas. [@1]
