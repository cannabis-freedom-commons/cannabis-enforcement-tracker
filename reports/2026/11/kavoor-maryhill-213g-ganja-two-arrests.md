Kavoor police arrested two men near Maryhill Junction in Mangaluru on 5 November
2026 on suspicion of possessing 213 grams of ganja to sell to young people. [@1]
The arrests were made by a patrol team led by Assistant Sub-Inspector Lokanath K
inside the Kavoor police station limits. [@1]

The officers noticed the two men standing suspiciously beside a scooter while
travelling from Mullakadu towards Maryhill, and the pair tried to flee when
approached before being caught. [@2] Police seized 213 grams of ganja, nine
transparent zip-lock covers, two mobile phones and a scooter. [@1] Preliminary
investigation indicated the two men were waiting to sell the ganja to young
people. [@1]
