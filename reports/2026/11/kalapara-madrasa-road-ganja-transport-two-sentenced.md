A mobile court in Kalapara, Patuakhali, sentenced two men to 21 days of rigorous
imprisonment each for transporting cannabis. [@1] Each was also fined 100 taka,
with five more days of rigorous imprisonment if the fine went unpaid. [@1]

The two men were arrested during a raid late at night in the Madrasa Road
culvert area. [@1]

Executive Magistrate and Upazila Assistant Commissioner (Land) Md Yasin Sadeque
handed down the sentence under section 42(1) of the Narcotics Control Act, 2018.
[@1] A police team led by Sub-Inspector Hamid of Kalapara police station
assisted in the operation. [@1] Yasin Sadeque said the campaign against drugs
and illegal activity would continue. [@1]

The ruling was given at midday on Friday 6 November, the day after the
late-night raid, Patuakhali Times reported. [@2]
