A federal judge sentenced Christopher S. Robinson, 31, to 15 years in prison for
possessing methamphetamine and fentanyl with intent to distribute, the charges
he pleaded guilty to in June 2026. [@1] WCIA reported that the 15-year term was
the minimum available for the offences he admitted. [@4]

Jacksonville police, the department's Special Response Team and the Central
Illinois Enforcement Group searched a house in the 600 block of East State
Street on 13 May 2024. [@1] The Central Illinois Enforcement Group coordinates
narcotics work for Illinois State Police, the Jacksonville, Pana, Lincoln and
Springfield police departments and sheriffs in Morgan, Cass, Logan, Christian
and Sangamon counties. [@2] Police reported finding 0.58 of a pound of
methamphetamine, 0.36 of a pound of fentanyl, 1.8 pounds (about 816 grams) of
cannabis, 76 Xanax pills, other prescription pills, 13 pint bottles of
Promethazine cough syrup and a small amount of psilocybin mushrooms. [@2]

Federal prosecutors put the search at 263 grams of methamphetamine and 166 grams
of a substance first believed to be heroin that tested positive for fentanyl.
[@1] Robinson, then 28 and living in Jacksonville, was charged with possession
of methamphetamine with intent to deliver, delivery of methamphetamine and
manufacture or delivery of fentanyl, all Class X felonies, and with felony
possession of cannabis with intent to deliver. [@2] He was charged with felony
cannabis possession with intent to deliver after the 2024 raid. [@2] Court
records showed Robinson had been released on his own recognizance on 2 April
2024 while awaiting trial on an earlier possession charge, the Journal-Courier
reported at the time. [@2] He was indicted in federal court in June 2024 and
pleaded guilty in June 2026. [@3]

The Drug Enforcement Administration, Illinois State Police and Jacksonville
police investigated, and Assistant U.S. Attorney Matthew Weir prosecuted the
case under the federal Project Safe Neighborhoods programme. [@1]
