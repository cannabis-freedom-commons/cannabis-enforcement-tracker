Carabinieri arrested a 50-year-old hotel manager and a 21-year-old man in a
hotel in Aprilia on suspicion of possessing drugs for dealing. [@1] The search
followed an investigation that had documented dealing inside the accommodation
and was ordered by the Latina public prosecutor's office. [@1]

In the manager's room officers found almost 60 grams of marijuana, a small
unquantified amount of hashish, one gram of crack, packaging material, a
precision scale and about 400 euros in cash seen as possible dealing proceeds.
[@1] In another room, occupied by the 21-year-old who was not registered as a
guest, officers seized about 15 grams of cocaine and cash. [@1] Police said both
men were already known to police for specific prior offences. [@1]

The manager was also reported for facilitating drug use, and a procedure was
started to ask the Latina police headquarters to suspend the hotel's licence.
[@1] All drugs and items were seized, and both suspects remain presumed innocent
until any final conviction. [@1]
