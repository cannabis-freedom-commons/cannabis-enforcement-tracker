The Carabinieri of the Poggioreale company carried out a high-impact operation
across the eastern Naples districts of Poggioreale, San Giovanni a Teduccio and
Barra, uncovering illegal power connections, drugs hidden in cellars, road
offences and administrative irregularities. [@1] During searches, officers
discovered a hashish depot inside the Taverna del Ferro residential complex at
San Giovanni a Teduccio. [@1] The hashish was hidden in rooms used as cellars.
[@1]

Officers seized 40 hashish blocks with a combined weight of about four
kilograms. [@1] Officers also seized 20 doses already packaged for retail sale,
weighing another 50 grams. [@1] A second local report gives the same 40 blocks
and 20 doses and describes the doses as presumably intended for dealing. [@2]

The seizure came during wider checks carried out with E-Distribuzione
technicians that found ten illegal connections to the power grid. [@1] The
overall operation identified 132 people, including 57 with police records, and
checked 80 vehicles. [@1] Seven people were reported to the Prefecture as drug
users without the reports specifying the substance. [@1]
