Police in Tiruchirappalli arrested a 25-year-old man from Kurumbapatti in
Dindigul district on Thursday, 5 November 2026, on suspicion of selling narcotic
pills. [@1] Officers from the Ponmalai police station area acted on a tip-off
received that Thursday afternoon that narcotic pills were being sold in their
jurisdiction. [@1]

A police party led by inspector Mahendran carried out a check and questioned the
man as he stood looking suspicious at Asath Nagar in Senthaneerpuram. [@1] The
questioning established that he is S. Aravind, 25, a resident of Kurumbapatti in
Dindigul district, and that he was selling narcotic pills. [@1]

Police then arrested him and seized 380 narcotic pills, ganja and a knife from
him. [@1]
