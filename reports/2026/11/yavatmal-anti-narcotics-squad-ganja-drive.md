Yavatmal district police chief Kumar Chinta has set up a district-level
Anti-Narcotics Squad. [@1] The squad works under the Local Crime Branch and was
placed in the charge of assistant police inspector Srikanth Jindamwar. [@1]
Police said its job is to investigate the district's drug-selling racket,
uncover the whole network, find the sources of supply and act against everyone
involved. [@1]

Over the past month the police chief also ran a crackdown on people selling
ganja and on those growing it secretly. [@1] Officers raided places in the
district after information that cannabis was being cultivated illegally in
fields. [@1] Investigators said the district was supplied with drugs from
neighbouring Madhya Pradesh and Rajasthan. [@1]

The problem of drug sale and use, once confined to the city, has now reached
rural parts of the district. [@1] Over the past year police also carried out
large-scale action against MD, a synthetic drug not counted in the cannabis
figures. [@1] Police said forming the special squad would speed up action
against the supply chain. [@1] Chinta appealed to residents to inform police if
drugs were being sold in their area. [@1] Parents were advised to watch for
changes in their children's behaviour, their friends and daily routine, and for
sudden spending or repeated requests for money. [@1]
