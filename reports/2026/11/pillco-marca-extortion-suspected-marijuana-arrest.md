National Police officers detained a 33-year-old man in the district of Pillco
Marca, Huánuco, after he was reported for allegedly demanding money under death
threats. [@1] The detained man was identified by the initials Leonardo E.C.P.
(33) and was located on jirón Casuarinas after a person reported being a victim
of extortion. [@1] After his detention he was transferred to the Huánuco
Criminal Investigation Department (Depincri), where he remained at the disposal
of the investigators in charge of the case. [@1]

During a personal search officers found a knife and a white bag containing dry
leaves with characteristics similar to cannabis sativa, commonly known as
marijuana. [@1] The nature of the substance was still to be determined through
the corresponding examinations, while investigators examined whether the items
found were related to the reported threats. [@1] The detained man retains the
presumption of innocence until a court decides otherwise. [@1]
