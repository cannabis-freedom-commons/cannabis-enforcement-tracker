Four adults, men and women, were detained and placed at the disposal of the
federal justice system after a series of simultaneous raids in the Capital and
Valle Viejo. [@1,3] After the home searches, officers detained and held
incommunicado the four adults and took them to police facilities pending
appointment of defence counsel and formal questioning before the federal courts.
[@1]

During the procedures, police broke up alleged drug retail points, seizing
portioned marijuana doses, anxiolytics without prescription, precision scales
and a motor vehicle. [@1] Officers seized several plastic wrappers and a
homemade cigarette containing herbal substance. [@1] A chemical-reagent field
test confirmed that the herbal substance was marijuana. [@1] Officers also found
more than 72 anxiolytic tablets allegedly used for cutting or unauthorized
resale, along with two digital precision gram scales for cutting and portioning.
[@1] Officers seized five cell phones of different brands and models for digital
forensic examination and a 150 cc Honda CG Titan motorcycle. [@1] Radio Valle
Viejo lists the same seizure of marijuana wrappers and cigarette, more than 72
anxiolytic tablets, two gram scales, five cell phones and a 150 cc Honda CG
Titan. [@2]

The searches were carried out on Buenos Aires street, Gaspar Guzman street and
Santa Marta neighbourhood in the capital, and at a private home in Don Felix
neighbourhood in Valle Viejo department. [@1] The judicial measures were
executed under express directives of the Federal Court of Catamarca after a
prior investigation that identified the homes where illicit substances were
stored and sold. [@1] The raids were led by the Dangerous Drugs Directorate of
the provincial police with tactical support from its Intelligence and
Investigations Division and investigators from the Judicial Investigations
Department. [@1,3]

The measures were carried out under directives of the federal court handling the case, and the four detainees remained at the disposal of the court, which issued the corresponding measures. [@3]
