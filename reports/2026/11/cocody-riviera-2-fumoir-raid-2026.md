Abidjan's Special Anti-Drug Group (GRAD) raided a large drug den at Riviera 2 in
Cocody on 2 November 2026 and arrested six people. [@1] The operation was
conducted under Operation Epervier 12 after a resident alerted police that the
site was used to sell and consume drugs. [@1] Officers found the site installed
in hard-to-reach ground surrounded by sewers filled with wastewater. [@1] From
their positions they watched several people, some consuming drugs while others
appeared to handle supply. [@1] After several hours of discreet observation,
officers launched the intervention at about 14:00. [@1]

A search of the site found six blocks of cannabis and 29 sachets containing 290
small wraps of cannabis. [@1] Police then completely destroyed the installation.
[@1] No injuries or material damage were reported. [@2]

GRAD is the Groupe de répression anti-drogues, Abidjan's anti-drug unit, and a
fumoir is a space used for drug sales and consumption. [@1]
