State Police patrol officers in Pavia stopped two young people after a chase and
reported them at liberty for joint possession of drugs. [@1] Officers on
territorial patrol had spotted three suspects near Piazza Salvo D'Acquisto, who
fled toward the porticoes of Via Zanella when they saw the patrol. [@1] Officers
caught and identified two of them after the chase. [@2] Checks showed that one
of the two had breached the residence-order precautionary measure imposed on
him. [@2]

During the chase, the two allegedly threw a smartphone and a cellophane wrap
containing a brown, malleable substance later confirmed as hashish onto the
grass of a residential complex. [@1] A later search of the check area recovered
a second wrap containing drugs. [@1] Analyses confirmed the material was hashish
totalling about 20 grams in two pieces. [@1] The drugs and recovered objects
were seized. [@1]

Police describe the Piazza Salvo D'Acquisto area as already known for
drug-dealing episodes. [@1] The two were taken to police headquarters for
identification and personal searches. [@1] Police say checks are continuing
across the area with particular attention to places considered sensitive for
preventing drug dealing. [@1]
