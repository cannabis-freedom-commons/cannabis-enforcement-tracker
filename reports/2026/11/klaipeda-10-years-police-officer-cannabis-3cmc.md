Klaipėda District Court sentenced a former officer of the Klaipėda Regional
Police Directorate to 10 years in prison for possession and transport of a very
large quantity of narcotic and psychotropic substances, the General Prosecutor's
Office announced. [@1] The 30-year-old officer was stopped by police on 9 May
2025 in the Klaipėda district while travelling from Vilnius to the Klaipėda
district in his Audi. [@1] During a search of the Audi, officers found a very
large quantity – 165 grams – of the psychotropic substances
3-chlormethylcatinone (3-CMC), MDMA and 4-bromomethylcatinone (4-BMC), as well
as cannabis. [@1] The same evening, a second car belonging to him, a BMW parked
in Kretinga, was searched on 10 May 2025 and powder containing 3-CMC was found
inside. [@1]

The court found that he had admitted buying the drugs several times, the last
time when he was detained by police. [@1] Information recovered from his mobile
phone confirmed that he may have distributed the drugs to other people and
earned income from it. [@1] The court stated that these actions not only
violated the law but also discredited the police, damaged its reputation and
diminished public trust in officers. [@1]

When imposing the sentence, the court considered the officer's status as a
long-serving police officer who should have ensured the prevention and detection
of offences, and noted that he had unlawfully dealt in new synthetic
psychotropic substances, 3-CMC and 4-BMC, which pose a serious threat to
people's health. [@1] The Criminal Code prescribes 10 to 15 years of
imprisonment for the unlawful handling of a very large quantity of narcotic or
psychotropic substances, and the 10-year sentence is the lowest limit of that
sanction. [@1] The pre-trial investigation was carried out by the Police
Department's Integrity Directorate, led by a prosecutor of the Organised Crime
and Corruption Investigation Division of the Klaipėda District Prosecutor's
Office; when the case was transferred to the court, the prosecutor already
called him a former officer. [@1]

The judgment has not yet entered into force and may be appealed to the
Lithuanian Court of Appeal. [@1] LRT's account of the verdict reports the same
10-year sentence, the 165-gram seizure of 3-CMC, MDMA, 4-BMC and cannabis in the
Audi and the 3-CMC powder found in the BMW parked in Kretinga, all announced by
the General Prosecutor's Office on Friday. [@2] The prosecutor's announcement
gave the exact date the substances in the car in the Klaipėda district were
found: 9 May 2025, when they were seized during a search carried out by police.
