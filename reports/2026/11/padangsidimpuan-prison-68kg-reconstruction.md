Padangsidimpuan police brought eight suspects to a reconstruction at the
district police headquarters on 3 November 2026 over ganja found inside the
prison. [@1] The reconstruction took place at Mapolres Padangsidimpuan under
police chief AKBP Noval N.G. Desky with the narcotics chief and public
prosecutors present. [@1] Officers acted out 46 scenes to fix the sequence of
events and each suspect's role. [@1] The suspect total grew from four to eight
after further examination by investigators. [@1] Police said roles differed,
including carrying ganja from a room to the rear of the prison and storing it
under a chicken coop. [@1] Police identified the eight suspects only by the
initials Z, A, A, M, A, M, M and H, and named another examined suspect initial U
as the alleged owner whose role still needed clarification. [@1]

Officers first checked Blocks E and F, covering about 20 residential rooms, and
found items suspected of drug abuse before moving to the maximum-security block.
[@1] In the electrical installation room they found one white sack holding two
bales of ganja and one red plastic bag holding four bales. [@1] Police seized
six bales weighing about 6,800 grams, or 6.8 kilograms. [@1] Officers also
collected meth-smoking tools and other items including two bongs, five small
glass pipes, empty transparent clips, pipettes, cables, charger heads and
powerbanks. [@1] At the first disclosure police detained four suspects aged 32
to 46 from Padang Lawas and Padangsidimpuan, then took all suspects and evidence
to Mapolres Padangsidimpuan for further investigation. [@1]

The police chief said the reconstruction should give a clear factual and legal
picture, especially for prosecutors. [@1] Police said they hoped the
reconstruction would let the prosecutor's office declare the dossier complete at
the P21 stage and described the work as part of combating narcotics in
Padangsidimpuan. [@1]
