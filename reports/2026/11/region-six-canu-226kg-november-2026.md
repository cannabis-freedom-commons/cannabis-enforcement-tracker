On 4 November 2026, Customs Anti-Narcotics Unit ranks stopped a motor vehicle at
High Dam, Angoy's Avenue, New Amsterdam, and arrested one man after finding
1.586 kilograms of cannabis in three yellow bags. [@1] Three people were
arrested on 8 November 2026 at Crabwood Creek, Corentyne, after CANU stopped a
boat and found 21.358 kilograms of marijuana in white salt bags. [@1] The people
held remained in custody while investigators tried to trace the origin and
intended destination of the cannabis and the weapon. [@1] CANU reported that the
Region Six series yielded more than 226 kilograms of cannabis, a shotgun and
more than 100 rounds of ammunition. [@1]

On 5 November, acting on intelligence, officers searched several properties at
Sand Hills on the Berbice River and found 186.8 kilograms of ganja in bags, a
shotgun and 110 12-gauge cartridges. [@1] No one was arrested during the Sand
Hills search. [@1] On 7 November a resident of Islington, East Bank Berbice, was
stopped with 17 kilograms of cannabis in his vehicle. [@3]

CANU said the boat stopped at Crabwood Creek was carrying several people. [@2]
The cannabis on that boat was packed in several white salt bags. [@2]
