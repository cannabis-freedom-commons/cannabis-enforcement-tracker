Detectives of the PDI's organised-crime investigation brigade, the Brico, raided
three properties in Cerro Navia, Estación Central and Renca in the Santiago
Metropolitan Region on 4 November 2026. [@1] Four people were detained in the
operation, three men and one woman. [@1] After a formalisation hearing, the
court ordered pretrial detention for all four, finding their release a danger to
society, and set a 90-day investigation period. [@1]

The PDI named the case operation Vector and said it dismantled a network selling
ammunition to criminal organisations in the Metropolitan Region. [@1]
Investigators said the main suspect bought 17,250 cartridges of various calibres
from 2025, using his registration as a sport shooter to obtain authorisations
from the national mobilisation directorate, the DGMN. [@1] He held six firearms
registered in his name and belonged to a shooting club, but investigators found
no record of attendance or practice at the club's premises. [@1] Detectives
followed him collecting cartridges from a Santiago gun shop and later making
transactions in Cerro Navia, where the PDI says the ammunition was exchanged for
cash and for drugs. [@1]

In the three properties the PDI seized 1,034 cartridges, three handguns
including a firearm adapted to fire live rounds, seven magazines and documents
related to firearms control. [@1] Officers also seized 43 bags of marijuana and
containers holding cocaine hydrochloride. [@1] Prosecutor Pablo Sabaj said the
main suspect was arrested with ammunition and a significant quantity of cocaine
hydrochloride and marijuana. [@1]

The main suspect was charged with trafficking in ammunition and trafficking in
drugs, and a hearing ordered pretrial detention for all four detainees. [@1]
