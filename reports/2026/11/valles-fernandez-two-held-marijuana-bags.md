San Luis Potosí's Guardia Civil Estatal detained two men in separate
interventions in Ciudad Valles and Ciudad Fernández. [@1] In the centre of
Ciudad Valles, officers on the Escudo Huasteco operation detained Daniel “N”,
aged 28 and originally from Tamaulipas. [@1] Police said Daniel “N” was carrying
multiple doses of methamphetamine. [@1]

In Ciudad Fernández, Guardia Civil officers and Mexican Army soldiers detained
Pedro “N”. [@1] In that stop officers seized methamphetamine doses, bags of
marijuana and a foreign Chevrolet pickup truck. [@1] The guard's 10 November
report gave a combined total of 78 methamphetamine and marijuana doses for the
two operations. [@1]

Both men, with the substances and the vehicle, were placed at the disposal of
the competent authorities for investigation. [@1] The seizures comprised
methamphetamine doses, bags of marijuana and a Chevrolet pickup. [@2] The state
Secretariat of Security and Citizen Protection said it maintains operational
actions against crime across the state's four regions. [@2]
