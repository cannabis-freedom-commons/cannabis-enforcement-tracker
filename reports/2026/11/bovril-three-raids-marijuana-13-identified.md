Police carried out three searches in Bovril on Sunday 1 November 2026 over
alleged drug selling, seizing marijuana, cash and 14 mobile phones as the first
reported results. [@1,2] Officers identified 13 people during the procedures
carried out that Sunday. [@1,2] Those identified were six women and seven men, and
the first account reported no detentions and gave no procedural status for them.
[@1,2]

Officers also seized cannabis plants, five security cameras, a 5.5-calibre
compressed-air gun and assorted cuttings whose characteristics were not
detailed. [@1,2] The marijuana was found in a nylon wrapper. [@1,2] The initial
information gave no weight for the marijuana, no number of cannabis plants and
no amount for the seized cash. [@1]

The operation was led by the Dangerous Drugs and Special Groups police
divisions. [@1,2] It formed part of proceedings for alleged breach of provincial
Law 10.566 on micro-trafficking, the provincial term for small-scale drug
dealing. [@1] The outlet described these findings as the first results of the
procedures. [@1]
