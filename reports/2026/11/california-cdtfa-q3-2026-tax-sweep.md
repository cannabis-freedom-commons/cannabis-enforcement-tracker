CDTFA investigators and inspectors conducted more than 1,700 operations in the
third quarter of 2026. [@2] In July, August and September 2026 they seized over
$26 million in illicit cannabis and tobacco products and completed cases with
nearly $6.4 million in court-ordered restitution for tax crimes. [@2] The
quarter included more than 100 joint enforcement operations with local, state
and federal agencies. [@1]

The agency reported $2.6 million in illicit cannabis products removed from
retail locations. [@1] It reported $1.3 million in restitution tied to a
cannabis case prosecuted with the San Bernardino County District Attorney's
Office. [@1] The largest single haul was $13.3 million in illicit cannabis and
tobacco products recovered from an unlicensed Los Angeles County warehouse. [@1]
The Governor's Office said that August warehouse seizure included 280,072
illegally labeled cannabis packages, 107,441 packages of cannabis edibles,
84,463 THC vape pens, 9,303 grams of cannabis flower and nearly $3 million in
illicit tobacco products. [@3]

Fourteen felony sales tax and cannabis excise tax prosecutions produced $1.7
million in court-ordered restitution. [@1] A Riverside County dealership owner
who pleaded guilty to felony sales tax evasion was ordered to pay $3 million in
restitution and will serve between two and four years in prison. [@1] An
out-of-state tobacco distributor was indicted for excise tax evasion, with an
arrest warrant issued and bail set at $23 million. [@1]
