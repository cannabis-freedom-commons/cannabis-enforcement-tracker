The Ninth Circuit of the Asyut Criminal Court sentenced three defendants to six
years of rigorous imprisonment each after convicting them of forming and running
a drug-trafficking gang. [@1] Counselor Ahmed Mohamed Halawa presided over the
bench with judges Mohamed Ayman Saddah, Mohamed Samir El-Tamawy and Rami Mohamed
Samir, and secretaries Khamis Mahmoud and Mohamed El-Arabi. [@1] The case began
in January 2026, when investigators found that three defendants from Dronka
village in Asyut Center had formed a network to sell narcotics. [@1]

Inquiries by the Asyut Security Directorate's Criminal Investigation Department
indicated that the defendants used their homes and surroundings in Dronka to
store and sell drugs wholesale and retail. [@1] After obtaining a Public
Prosecution warrant, security forces prepared an operation in the Al-Suwaiqah
area of Dronka where two of them were present. [@1] Officers arrested the first
and second defendants and found six bags of methamphetamine known locally as
shabu, four heroin wraps, an electronic scale, a scalpel, 180 Egyptian pounds
and a phone on the first. [@1] Police seized a bag of methamphetamine, a heroin
wrap, a scalpel, 370 Egyptian pounds and a mobile phone from the second
defendant. [@1] Forces then went to the third defendant's home in Dronka and
arrested him with 21 pieces of hashish, 15 tramadol tablets, iron scissors, 430
Egyptian pounds and a phone. [@1]

Asyut forensic laboratories confirmed that the first defendant's items included
8.75 grams of methamphetamine and 1.45 grams of heroin, with traces of
methamphetamine and hashish on his scale. [@1] The lab weighed the third
defendant's hashish at 19.30 grams and confirmed 9.40 grams of methamphetamine
and 2.96 grams of heroin held by the second defendant, plus tramadol in the
tablets. [@1] During questioning, the defendants admitted possessing the drugs
for trafficking and profit, saying the cash came from their drug proceeds. [@1]
They also confessed to using the phones to contact customers and users and
keeping bladed weapons to protect themselves while dividing and selling drugs.
[@1] North Asyut Public Prosecution referred the three defendants to the
criminal court based on police inquiries, forensic reports and confessions. [@1]
