Ostrów Wielkopolski police made several detentions over three days, holding men
aged 24 to 36 and seizing amphetamine, marijuana and methamphetamine. [@1] The
detentions for drug possession in Ostrów Wielkopolski were carried out by
officers of the Criminal Department and the Prevention Department. [@1] On 30
October officers stopped a nervous 36-year-old resident of Ostrów Wielkopolski
and found a foil bag with a white substance on him. [@1] Officers then checked
his residence and found additional banned substances identified as amphetamine,
marijuana and methamphetamine. [@1] On 31 October officers intervened at an
address in Ostrów Wielkopolski where a group of people were present and found
drugs on two men. [@1]

Preliminary tests identified the substances seized on 31 October as marijuana,
amphetamine and methamphetamine, with police putting the combined total at
nearly 60 grams of drugs. [@1] On the evening of 1 November a Prevention
Department patrol stopped a car carrying several people. [@1] During that check
officers found banned substances on two men aged 24, who together held more than
30 grams of drugs. [@1]

All the revealed substances were secured and the cases will be subject to
further proceedings. [@1] Ostrów police reminded that possessing intoxicants and
psychotropic substances is a crime carrying criminal liability. [@1]
