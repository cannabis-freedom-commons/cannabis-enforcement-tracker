A joint Border Police Battalion (BPFron) and Federal Police team intercepted
367.04 kg of cannabis on Monday 2 November 2026 at Profilurb II in Foz do Iguaçu
after watching a boat arrive from Paraguay and moor on the Brazilian bank. [@1]
The officers saw the boat cross the frontier and its occupants unload bundles
into a car on the Brazilian side. [@1] When officers moved in to stop them, the
men abandoned the load and fled into nearby woodland. [@1] Searches found no
suspects. [@1] The official police statement confirms the patrol in Profilurb II
saw a craft leave the Paraguayan bank, moor in Brazil, and have its volumes
transferred to a car. [@2]

Officers searching the car found bundles containing material with the
characteristics of cannabis. [@1] After weighing, the load totalled 367.04 kg.
[@1] Police seized the drug and the car and took them to the Federal Police
station in Foz do Iguaçu for legal procedures and investigation. [@1] BPFron put
the estimated loss to traffickers at R$764,480. [@1]

The action was carried out by BPFron teams with the Federal Police's Special
Maritime Police Nucleus (Nepom), which patrols drug trafficking and other
frontier crime. [@1] The identities of those involved and the final destination
of the drug were not disclosed. [@1]
