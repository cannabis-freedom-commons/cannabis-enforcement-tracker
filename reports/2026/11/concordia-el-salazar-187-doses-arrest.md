National Police officers arrested a man in flagrante in the El Salazar
neighbourhood of Concordia, Antioquia, after finding drugs in his possession.
[@1] The Antioquia Police Department said uniformed officers carried out the
procedure on Saturday 7 November for the offence of trafficking, manufacturing
or carrying narcotics under article 376 of Colombia's Penal Code. [@1] The
detained man was placed at the disposal of Fiscalía 50 Local de Concordia, which
is handling the judicial process. [@1]

Officers found 187 doses of marijuana on him, together with 142 doses of cocaine
hydrochloride and 500 doses of coca base, for a total of 829 doses of the three
substances. [@1] Minuto30 corroborated the same breakdown of 187 doses of
marijuana, 142 of cocaine and 500 of coca base, saying the drugs would allegedly
have been distributed in the area. [@2] Colonel Luis Fernando Serna Zapata,
commander of the Antioquia Police Department, announced the result of the
procedure in El Salazar. [@1]

The authorities did not release the man's identity, and no custody ruling or
later court outcome was reported. [@1] Minuto30 notes that every detained person
is presumed innocent until a court rules otherwise. [@2]
