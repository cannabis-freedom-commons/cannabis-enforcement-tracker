Officers of the ADSU, the Anti-Drug and Smuggling Unit of the Mauritius Police
Force, searched the home of an unemployed 26-year-old man living in Canot at
around 21:20 on Monday 9 November 2026. [@1] The unit is the specialised branch
of the Mauritius Police Force responsible for tackling drug trafficking and
smuggling. [@2] Canot is a coastal locality in the Riviere Noire (Black River)
district on Mauritius's northwest coast. [@2]

During the search of the property, investigators found and seized a knotted
transparent plastic bag holding 38 grams of cannabis. [@1] Police also seized Rs
15,200 in cash, which they suspected came from drug trafficking. [@1]

Following the seizure, the suspect was arrested, questioned by police and
released on parole while the investigation continued. [@1] The English account
on MauritiusNews.com described his release after questioning as release on bail
and stated that no charges had been confirmed publicly. [@2]
