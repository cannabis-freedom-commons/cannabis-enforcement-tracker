Military Police officers arrested a 53-year-old man for drug trafficking on the
afternoon of Sunday 1 November in the Caramuru neighbourhood of Patos de Minas.
[@1,3] The man was held in flagrante and taken to the Civil Police station
together with the seized drugs, money and mobile phone. [@1,2,3] The Military
Police, the uniformed patrol force, said the action followed complaints that a
residence was being used to sell drugs, mainly crack and cannabis, with payment
in cash and by Pix transfers. [@1]

Officers on patrol received a further tip about possible users moving at the
house and saw the suspect digging a hole by the back wall. [@1,2,3] When he saw the
officers he fled inside and threw crack stones onto a veranda sofa before being
caught, and officers found two crack stones and a portion of cannabis on the
spot. [@1,2] A personal search seized R$150 in cash and a mobile phone. [@1,3] A
continued search behind the house found a plastic bag containing crushed
cannabis and another portion wrapped in plastic film. [@1,3]

A sniffer dog indicated drugs by the first house's sofa, where officers found
another crack stone, and signalled a backpack in a bedroom of the neighbouring
house, where they found another portion of cannabis. [@1,2] Two women aged 34 and
32 found in the neighbouring house said they were drug users. [@1,2,3]
