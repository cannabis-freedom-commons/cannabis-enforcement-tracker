Carabinieri checks over the previous weekend led to the arrest of a 16-year-old
in Gela. [@1] The boy had several doses of marijuana on him, some hidden in his
underwear. [@1] He was stopped near Gela's railway station. [@1]

The juvenile prosecutor's office of Caltanissetta ordered that he remain at his
own home. [@1] At the validation hearing before the judge for preliminary
investigations, assisted by his lawyer, the boy said the marijuana was for
personal use and not for dealing, as investigators maintain. [@1]

The arrest was validated, but the order to stay at home was lifted. [@1] He may
return to school provided he meets specific conditions, monitored also by social
services, for at least the next two months. [@1]
