Military Police detained five people — four men and one woman — when they
stopped a vehicle in Timbó in the early hours of 4 November 2026. [@1] The five
occupants were taken to the Central de Plantão Policial, the on-duty police
station, in Blumenau with all the seized drugs and objects. [@1] The material
was presented to the police authority for the applicable legal procedures, and
the case was recorded as drug trafficking for investigation by the competent
authorities. [@1]

Officers seized approximately 13.2 kilos of marijuana, the cannabis component
recorded here, together with 21 grams of hashish. [@1] The same vehicle search
also found crack, cocaine and tablets described as an ecstasy analogue, which
are left out of the cannabis total. [@1] Officers also collected six mobile
phones, two notebooks and a scale during the operation. [@1]

The stop took place at about 1:30 a.m. on Rua Blumenau and mobilized teams from
different municipalities in the region, including Timbó, Blumenau and Indaial.
[@1] The action was carried out by the Military Police through Timbó's
Radiopatrulha, the routine patrol unit, with support from Timbó's ROCAM
motorcycle patrol, Blumenau's Tático tactical unit and Indaial's Radiopatrulha.
[@1]
