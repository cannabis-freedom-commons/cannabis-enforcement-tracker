More than 20 kg of narcotics was seized in separate incidents in the Kashmir
Valley and four alleged smugglers were arrested. [@1,2] Police announced the
seizures and arrests on Wednesday. [@1,2] The arrests were made during naka checks
in Anantnag, Shopian and Baramulla districts. [@1,2] In Anantnag, Pahalgam police
stopped Taufiq Ahmad Rather at Yanner and recovered 13.30 kg of bhang husk from
him during a search. [@1,2] The Bhung Bhosa recovered was described as crushed stems and stalks of the cannabis plant. [@2] Rather was identified as the son of Gulzar Ahmad Rather and a resident of Movera. [@3]

In Anantnag, Aishmuqam police stopped a suspect at the Haptmar-Nagbal crossing
and recovered about 6 grams of charas from him during a search. [@1,2] In Shopian,
Keller police arrested a suspect during checking at a naka at Padha Behuk
Shalidar Keller. [@1,2] A search recovered 742 grams of charas-powder-like
substance from his possession. [@1,2] The accused was identified as Mudassir Ahmad
Katari. [@1,2] Katari was identified as the son of Munshad Ahmad Katari and a resident of Guluwali Behak Mujpathri. [@3]

In Pattan, Baramulla, police arrested drug smuggler Sajid Ahmad Mir at the
Janwani Pora checkpoint and recovered 7.69 kg of charas-powder-like substance
from him. [@1,2] Cases under relevant sections of law were registered at the respective police stations in all four arrests, and police said investigations had been initiated to ascertain the source of the contraband. [@3] A case under relevant provisions of the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act was registered at Police Station Pattan, and further investigation was underway to establish the forward and backward linkages of the drug trafficking network. [@4] The substance was seized and sealed as per legal procedure. [@4] Mir, son of Bashir Ahmad Mir and a resident of Janwani Pora, was carrying a nylon bag on his shoulder when a police party intercepted him at Janwani Pora, Kripal Pora Payeen. [@4]
