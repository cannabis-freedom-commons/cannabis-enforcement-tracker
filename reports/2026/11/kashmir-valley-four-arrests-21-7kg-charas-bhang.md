More than 20 kg of narcotics was seized in separate incidents in the Kashmir
Valley and four alleged smugglers were arrested. [@1] Police announced the
seizures and arrests on Wednesday. [@1] The arrests were made during naka checks
in Anantnag, Shopian and Baramulla districts. [@1] In Anantnag, Pahalgam police
stopped Taufiq Ahmad Rather at Yanner and recovered 13.30 kg of bhang husk from
him during a search. [@1]

In Anantnag, Aishmuqam police stopped a suspect at the Haptmar-Nagbal crossing
and recovered about 6 grams of charas from him during a search. [@1] In Shopian,
Keller police arrested a suspect during checking at a naka at Padha Behuk
Shalidar Keller. [@1] A search recovered 742 grams of charas-powder-like
substance from his possession. [@1] The accused was identified as Mudassir Ahmad
Katari. [@1]

In Pattan, Baramulla, police arrested drug smuggler Sajid Ahmad Mir at the
Janwani Pora checkpoint and recovered 7.69 kg of charas-powder-like substance
from him. [@1]
