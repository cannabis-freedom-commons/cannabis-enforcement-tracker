Dumki police arrested a 20-year-old youth with 20 grams of ganja during a
special drive in Patuakhali's Dumki Upazila. [@1] The arrest occurred around
12:30 am on Wednesday, 11 November 2026, on the paved road in front of Sujon
Jomaddar's house at the head of the Satani-Atharogachia road in Ward 9 of
Atharogachia area, under Lebukhali Union. [@1]

An operation led by Dumki police Assistant Sub-Inspector Mosharraf Hossain and
accompanying personnel seized the 20 grams of cannabis and prepared a formal
seizure list. [@1] The arrested youth was identified as Md Towhid Talukder, a
resident of Ward 9 in Atharogachia and son of Md Nazrul Islam Talukder. [@1]
Dumki police station officer-in-charge Selim Uddin stated that a case was
registered under Section 36(1) table 19(ka) of the Narcotics Control Act, 2018,
recorded as Case No. 09 on 11 November 2026. [@1]
