Officers of DENARC, the Department of Narcotics, arrested 21-year-old David
Silva de Oliveira Filho in Porto Velho's south zone on suspicion of drug
trafficking. [@1] The case was recorded under operations Narke and Protetor das
Fronteiras. [@1] An anonymous tip said the suspect was selling drugs and
supplying different sale points across the region. [@1] DENARC teams
investigated and monitored the suspect, stopping him as he left his apartment to
make a delivery and finding three portions of powdered cocaine in his clothing.
[@2]

Officers then searched the apartment on Rua Baobá in the Cohab neighbourhood and
found the remaining drugs hidden inside a refrigerator. [@1] In total officers
seized 102 portions of skunk amounting to about half a kilogram of that drug.
[@1] The same seizure included 23 portions of cocaine and four larger portions
of that drug weighing about 300 grams, which are not counted in the cannabis
total. [@2] Officers also collected about R$500 in cash and a motorcycle
investigators said was used in trafficking. [@1]

The detainee was arrested and taken to DENARC headquarters, where legal
procedures were carried out. [@1] Both reports were published on 3 November
2026. [@1]
