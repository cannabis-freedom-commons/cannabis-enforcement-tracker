Montgomery County police officers from the 4th District Community Action Team
stopped a vehicle near the intersection of East Randolph Road and Kara Lane in
the Glenmont area on Thursday, November 5, during proactive enforcement. [@1]
Five people were inside the vehicle. [@1] A K-9 officer's exterior scan of the
vehicle alerted to controlled dangerous substances, and a search followed. [@1]
The search found four handguns and, in addition to several THC vape cartridges,
cannabis packaged for distribution. [@1]

Two juvenile males and an adult male passenger were charged with handgun-related
offenses. [@1] The juvenile males were placed in a juvenile detention facility,
and the adult passenger was processed at the county's central processing unit.
[@1] Police said the adult male driver will be charged with possession of
cannabis with intent to distribute. [@1] An adult female occupant was released
without charges, and police said the investigation remained active. [@1]
