Officers of the Service de police de Granby (SPG) searched an apartment on rue
Neil in Granby on the morning of 10 November 2026 in an investigation into
alleged cannabis sales. [@1] No arrests had been made, and police said steps to
link the case to one or more individuals were continuing. [@1]

During the search, officers seized more than 100 grams of dried cannabis, along
with THC concentrate and cannabis edibles. [@1] Police also placed under seal
about ten tablets believed to be methamphetamine, about ten grams of psilocybin,
a pistol, ammunition, a bladed weapon and trafficking equipment. [@1]

The narcotics inquiry by the ACCES Cannabis and Crimes initiés squads began in
August 2026 after tips from the public about alleged cannabis sales. [@2]
Investigators found the information plausible enough to open an inquiry into
suspicious activity at the address. [@1] Weeks later, after obtaining a search
warrant, officers forced entry into the targeted apartment. [@1] Police said
charges related to drug trafficking and the seized weapons could be laid later.
[@1] The investigation remained under way. [@2]
