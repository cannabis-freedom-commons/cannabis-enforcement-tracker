Police in Sochi detained a 33-year-old local man after he crossed a road where
crossing was not allowed and appeared drunk. [@1] He had no documents with him,
so officers took him to a police station. [@1] There, according to the police,
he admitted that he used banned substances and kept them at home. [@1]

A search of his home found 3.5 kg of marijuana in five packets. [@2] Officers
also seized packaging materials, scales and equipment for sorting the drug. [@1]
The Krasnodar Krai main directorate of the Interior Ministry said the man grew
the drug himself and sold it. [@1]

A criminal case has been opened against the Sochi man. [@2] Police said the
charge carries up to 20 years in prison. [@1]
