Two suspected bhang sellers were arrested in Bondeni Dip village in Kachumba,
Uasin Gishu County, after a violent clash that left a police officer seriously
injured. [@1] Officers acted on a tip-off and raided the village to arrest
individuals suspected of selling bhang from a residential house. [@1] When
police reached the house, they discovered a sack of bhang and moved to arrest
the suspects. [@1] Accomplices intervened and launched an attack on the
officers, with four people emerging armed with crude weapons. [@1]

During the encounter, one suspect slashed an officer's hand with a panga in an
attempt to frighten the remaining officers. [@1] Turbo Sub-County Police
Commander Patrick Wekesa said the officers called for reinforcements from nearby
police stations as tension mounted in the village. [@1]

Officers managed to detain two suspects, including the assailant who slashed the
officer's hand, while two others escaped and remain at large. [@1] The wounded
officer was rushed to Moi Teaching and Referral Hospital for treatment. [@1]
Police stated that the two detained suspects would be arraigned in court once
investigations are complete. [@1]
