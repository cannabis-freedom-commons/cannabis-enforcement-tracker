Excise officials booked farmer Kodapa Jyotiram of Kolamguda after finding
cannabis plants growing in his field on the village outskirts. [@1] Officers
registered a case against Jyotiram and produced him before the court. [@1]

A joint team of excise officials and the task force searched agricultural fields
at Kolamguda, which falls under Waghapur panchayat in Mavala mandal of Adilabad
district, on Tuesday. [@1] The team found cannabis, locally called ganja,
growing as an intercrop among cotton and kandi crops in Jyotiram's field. [@1]
Adilabad excise station house officer Akula Srinivas said officers seized 62
cannabis plants from the field. [@1]

The search party included trainee circle inspector Devender alongside excise
officials Sravan Kumar, Thanaji, Krishna, Shivakumar, Madhu, Naresh, Gautham and
Swapna, among others. [@1] The holding was Jyotiram's own field at the village
edge, where standing cotton and kandi crops had concealed the cannabis
intercrop. [@1] A panchayat is a village-level council and a mandal is a
block-level administrative unit, the tiers that place Kolamguda within Waghapur
and Mavala. [@1]
