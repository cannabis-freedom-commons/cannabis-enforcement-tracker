Excise officials in Similiguda detained a two-wheeler rider carrying illegally
transported ganja, arresting Sheikh Nazir of Jagannath Pur in the Jeypore area
and sending him to court. [@1] The operation was carried out by Nandapur excise
staff, whose inspector Raju K T later described how the team acted after
receiving information about ongoing ganja smuggling. [@1] Acting on that
information, the staff organized vehicle checks near Malipetta village on Sunday
morning to intercept the suspected movement. [@1]

During those checks, officers stopped a single two-wheeler for inspection as
part of the targeted screening of passing vehicles. [@1] A search of the stopped
vehicle revealed about 25 kilograms of ganja being transported on it. [@1] The
excise team seized the recovered consignment at the checkpoint and took the
rider moving it into custody. [@1] Officials identified the detained rider as
Sheikh Nazir and said he was arrested and produced before the court for further
legal proceedings. [@1]
