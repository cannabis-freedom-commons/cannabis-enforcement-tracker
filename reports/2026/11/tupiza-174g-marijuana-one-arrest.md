Officers of the Fuerza Especial de Lucha Contra el Narcotráfico (FELCN) carried
out two anti-drug operations in the department of Potosí. [@1] In the second
intervention, in the municipality of Tupiza, officers seized 174 grams of
marijuana. [@1] Officers apprehended one person in that Tupiza intervention.
[@1]

FELCN departmental director Lieutenant Colonel Martín Stolzel reported the
operations. [@1] He said the first operation took place in the Huancarani
sector, Antonio Quijarro province, where 40 kilos and 600 grams of cocaine were
seized. [@1] That cocaine was seized after a police pursuit that ended when
traffickers dropped jute sacks and fled. [@1] The account put the loss to drug
trafficking from that cocaine seizure at 73,100 dollars. [@1]
