Sergipe Military Police arrested a man suspected of drug trafficking in the
Bugio neighbourhood of Aracaju on Wednesday 4 November 2026. [@1] Officers
seized marijuana, a mobile phone and a precision scale with him. [@1]

The suspect was riding a motorcycle against the flow of traffic when officers of
the Mechanised Cavalry Motorcycle Patrol Regiment, GETAM, stopped him. [@1]
During the search, the officers found the marijuana wrapped in cling film at the
man's waist. [@1]

The suspect, the motorcycle and the items found were taken to a police station.
