Police at the Vasavappapuram checkpost on the Thoothukudi–Tirunelveli national
highway signalled a white car to stop for a vehicle check on the night of 6
November 2026. [@1] The car did not stop; it had followed a container lorry past
the post to stay out of the officers' sight and drove away at speed when police
tried to stop it. [@1] The Vasavappapuram team alerted the nearby Seydunganallur
police, whose officers chased the car to round it up. [@1] The occupants stopped
the car near the Vasavappapuram pond and ran off before police could catch them.
[@1]

Seydunganallur police searched the abandoned car and found 7 kilograms of ganja
in packets, Rs 4.40 lakh in cash, five mobile phones, a knife and a sickle. [@1]
Police had acted on a tip-off that the men were carrying ganja by car from
Meenakshipatti near Thoothukudi. [@1] No one had been arrested; Seydunganallur
police registered a case and were investigating who the fleeing men were and
where the consignment came from. [@1]
