Police in Kuznetsk, Penza Oblast, detained a 48-year-old local man suspected of
illegally storing a large quantity of drugs. [@4] The Kuznetsk district police
said they had checked a report that the man was involved in trafficking
narcotics. [@1] During operational-search measures, officers found and seized a
box of plant material from the man and sent it for expert examination. [@3] An
expert examination identified the contents of the box as marijuana weighing
almost 498 grams. [@1] The man admitted guilt and said he had collected parts of
the plant during a walk by the river the day before, for his own use. [@2] He
described the plants as wild and said he had not intended to sell them. [@4]

The regional Interior Ministry's press service confirmed that a criminal case
had been opened. [@1] It was brought under part 2 of article 228 of the Criminal
Code, which covers illegal storage of drugs on a large scale. [@4] The offence
carries up to 10 years in prison. [@1]
