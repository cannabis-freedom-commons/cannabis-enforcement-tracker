The 22-year-old woman told officers her partner had attacked her and stopped her
leaving the property where the couple was staying. [@1] The COI, the Intelligent
Operations Centre of the Guarda Civil Municipal (GCM), the municipal guard
force, sent patrols responding in the Vila do Povo neighbourhood on the morning
of Thursday 6 November 2026. [@1]

While registering the incident at the property, the officers found a pot holding
one cannabis plant and portions of the drug. [@1] The Portuguese-language
version of the same report describes the find as a vase with a cannabis plant
and portions of the drug. [@2] The seized drug and the people involved were
taken to the Civil Police duty station. [@1] The Portuguese version states the
drug and those involved were taken to the plantão, the duty station of the Civil
Police. [@2]
