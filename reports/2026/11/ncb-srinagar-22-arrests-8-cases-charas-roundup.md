The Narcotics Control Bureau in Srinagar arrested 22 people in eight cases
registered during 2025 and 2026 so far, widening its net against narcotics
networks in Srinagar and other parts of Jammu and Kashmir. [@1] The bureau
registered five of the cases in 2025 and three in 2026. [@1]

The agency put the total weight of narcotics seized in the eight cases at 57.470
kg, of which 39.230 kg was poppy straw. [@1] Charas accounted for 18.240 kg of
the seizures, the cannabis figure in the bureau's totals. [@1] Case-wise figures
give the largest single haul as 10.735 kg of charas, seized in August 2025,
followed by 2.730 kg in November and 2.010 kg in December that year. [@1] Charas
seizures continued in 2026, with 1.210 kg in one case and 0.635 kg in another in
January and 0.920 kg in a case registered in February. [@1]

The action also reached beyond Jammu and Kashmir, with an inter-state charas
smuggling network operating between Kashmir and Kolkata busted during the
period. [@1] Five accused were arrested in that case, two of them from Kolkata
and three from Kashmir. [@1]

NCB Srinagar officials said the agency regularly conducts awareness programmes
in schools and colleges on the harmful effects of drug abuse. [@1] They appealed
to the local population to support the agency, saying collective efforts are
essential to eliminate the drug menace from society. [@1]
