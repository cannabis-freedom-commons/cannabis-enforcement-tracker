Officers of the drug-control unit detained a 34-year-old man on Gubkina Street
in Kazan, the city's interior ministry directorate said. [@1] Police suspect him
of illegally distributing drugs. [@1]

During a personal search officers found a plastic case holding 17 wraps of foil.
[@1] A laboratory examination showed the wraps contained hashish with a total
mass of more than 13 grams, police said. [@1]

The man fully admitted his guilt, according to the police. [@1] A criminal case
was opened against him under point "b" of part 3 of article 228.1 of the Russian
Criminal Code. [@1] He was placed in custody for the duration of the preliminary
investigation. [@1] A second Kazan outlet carried the same directorate statement
and published photographs credited to the press service of the Tatarstan
interior ministry. [@2]
