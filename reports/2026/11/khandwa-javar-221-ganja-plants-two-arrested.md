Javar police raided two separate fields in Debri Falya Kaisun village and found
ganja being grown illegally. [@1,3,8] Police said they acted after an informer
reported that two farmers in the village were growing ganja. [@1,2,3] Police
searched the field near the house of Dariyavsingh, son of Savarn Ningwal, aged
48. [@1,3] Police arrested the owner of that first field. [@1] Police searched the
field of Bhuru alias Rajesh, son of Sagar Bhilala, aged 40. [@1] Police arrested
field owner Bhuru alias Rajesh. [@1] Police registered cases against both men
and started an investigation. [@1,2,3]

Officers uprooted and seized a total of 221 green ganja plants with a combined
weight of 295.700 kilograms. [@1,2,5,6,8] Police put the estimated value of the seized
plants at 29 lakh 57 thousand rupees. [@1,3,4,5,6] The plants were growing among cotton
and tuar crops in both fields. [@1,2,3,8] The first field yielded about 110 plants
weighing 195.361 kilograms. [@1,2] The second field yielded 111 plants weighing
about 100.339 kilograms. [@1]

Police said they are investigating how long the cultivation had continued and
where the seized plants were to be sold or used. [@1] Police said the action
against narcotics will continue. [@1]

Khandwa SP Agam Jain said police verified information that two farmers were cultivating cannabis before conducting the raids. [@2]

The 110 plants from Dariyavsingh's field were valued at 19.54 lakh rupees. [@3] The 111 plants from Bhuru alias Rajesh's field weighed 1 quintal 700 grams and were valued at about 10 lakh rupees. [@3] The operation was led by Javar station in-charge Inspector Sulochana Gahlod with Sub-Inspector Rajendra Rathore, Assistant Sub-Inspector Ranjit Singh Rajput, Head Constables Munna Barde and Prakash, Constable Suresh Chauhan and others. [@3]
