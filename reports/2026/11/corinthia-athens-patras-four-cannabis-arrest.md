The four were two Greek nationals aged 21 and 24 respectively and two foreign
nationals aged 22 and 38 respectively. [@1]

Officers of the Epirus Crime Prevention and Suppression Team, known as O.P.K.E.,
searched the vehicle and seized a total of 7.89 grams of cannabis. [@1] The
reported 7.89 grams was the combined quantity found in the vehicle for all four
occupants rather than separate amounts linked to any individual. [@2]

The arresting force was the Epirus O.P.K.E., the Crime Prevention and
Suppression Team of the Hellenic Police. [@2] The preliminary investigation into
the possession case is being conducted by the Sikyonians Police Station. [@2]
