General Directorate of Mujahideen patrols in the Riyadh region arrested two
Ethiopian nationals recorded as violators of the border-security system. [@1]
The directorate's own account said the two were held for promoting hashish and
amphetamine drugs along with tablets subject to medical-circulation regulation.
[@2] The hashish named by the directorate is the cannabis component of this
mixed case, with the reported amphetamine and regulated tablets excluded from
cannabis quantities. [@2]

Officers stopped the accused men and completed the statutory procedures against
them. [@1] The directorate then referred both men to the competent authority for
further action. [@1]

Security agencies asked the public to report drug smugglers and dealers on 911
in Makkah, Madinah, Riyadh and the Eastern Province and on 999 in the rest of
the kingdom. [@1] They also listed the General Directorate of Narcotics Control
number 995 and the email 995@gdnc.gov.sa for such reports. [@1] The authorities
said all reports are handled with complete confidentiality and without any
liability for the informant. [@1]
