Military police from the 37th Military Police Battalion and Civil Police
officers served a search and seizure warrant in Taquaruçu do Sul on the
afternoon of Thursday 5 November 2026, finding about 708 grams of marijuana and
53 grams of cocaine. [@1] Officers also collected four mobile phones, a laptop
and 1,300 reais in cash. [@1]

No one was arrested during the operation. [@1] All the material was taken to the
police station for legal procedures. [@1] A second account, from Grupo Chiru,
reports the same quantities, the same support role for the 37th Battalion and no
arrests. [@2]
