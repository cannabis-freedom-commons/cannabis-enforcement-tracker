Provincial police in Dryden stopped a motorist at a RIDE check over the weekend
and laid charges. [@1] The check happened on a Sunday evening at around 9 p.m.
local time on Highway 594 near Gordon Road in Dryden. [@1] The driver was a man
from Fort Frances. [@1] He was charged under the Fish and Wildlife Conservation
Act with unlawfully possessing a firearm at night. [@1] He was also charged
under the province's Cannabis Control Act with having cannabis readily available
in a vehicle. [@1]

Officers said the shotgun was not stored in accordance with legal requirements
after hunting hours. [@1] Police said numerous cannabis products were also
readily accessible in the vehicle. [@1] Police said the cannabis products were
seized. [@1] The accused allegedly had the weapon outside its case more than
half an hour after sunset in an area where wildlife was expected. [@1]

The Dryden OPP reminded hunters and motorists to ensure firearms are transported
and stored safely in compliance with provincial regulations. [@1] Police added
that cannabis must be stored appropriately and kept out of reach while operating
or travelling in a motor vehicle. [@1]
