Military Police officers in Lajinha arrested a 23-year-old man for drug
trafficking on Saturday, 7 November 2026, during an operation against crime in
the town. [@1] Officers stopped the young man on Avenida Natal Rodrigues Pereira
and found three cocaine packets on him. [@1] Officers then searched nearby and
the flat where the man lived and found more drugs. [@1]

In total, police seized eight cocaine packets, a larger portion of the same
drug, two buchas and one portion of marijuana, along with a precision scale and
a mobile phone. [@1] Officers also took 85.50 reais in cash, a cordão and a
pulseira. [@2] A Honda CG 160 motorcycle and a Honda City car were also seized
during the operation. [@1]

The man was arrested in the act and taken to the Civil Police station in
Manhuaçu with the seized material. [@1] The account came from the Military
Police of Minas Gerais, whose handout photograph the outlets published. [@2] The
Diário de Ipatinga published the same account, crediting Portal Caparaó as its
source. [@3]
