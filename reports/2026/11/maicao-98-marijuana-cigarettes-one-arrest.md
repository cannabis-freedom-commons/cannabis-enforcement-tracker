National Police officers captured a 39-year-old man in Maicao, La Guajira, after
finding 98 marijuana cigarettes during a personal search. [@1] Officers on
patrol said the man appeared nervous when he noticed them and then searched him.
[@1]

The 98 cigarettes weighed about 220 grams. [@1] Officers found the cigarettes
inside a glass container with a lid kept in a white bag. [@1] Police estimated
the commercial value of the cigarettes at 450,000 pesos. [@1]

The arrest took place at Calle 9 with Carrera 40 in the Las Américas
neighbourhood as part of Operation 360 Todos Unidos por La Guajira Segura. [@1]
The man was detained on suspicion of drug trafficking, manufacture or possession
and was placed with the seized substance at the disposal of the on-duty
prosecutor of the Immediate Reaction Unit in Maicao. [@1] The acting commander
of the La Guajira Police Department said the operations aim to combat drug
trafficking and strengthen security and citizen coexistence in the department's
municipalities. [@1]
