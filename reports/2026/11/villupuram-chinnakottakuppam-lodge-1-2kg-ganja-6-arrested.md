Police in Kottakuppam, Villupuram district, searched a private lodging house in
Chinnakottakuppam on the night of Thursday, 5 November 2026, and found six
people in one room with 1 kg 200 g of ganja. [@1] The raid followed a tip-off to
the Kottakuppam police station, and Inspector Babu led the officers who carried
it out. [@1]

Police registered a case against the six and arrested them, seizing the ganja.
[@1] The six held are men aged 19 to 25, five from Puducherry addresses in
Muthialpet, Samipillaithottam and Karuvadikuppam, and one from Kilsevarayanpet.
[@1]

Ganja is the name used in India for cannabis, which police in Tamil Nadu seize
in cases filed under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act. [@1]
