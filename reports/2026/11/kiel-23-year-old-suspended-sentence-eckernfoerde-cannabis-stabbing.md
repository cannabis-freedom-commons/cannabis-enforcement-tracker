The Landgericht Kiel, the regional court in Kiel, sentenced a 23-year-old man
from Bremen on 6 November 2026 to one year and six months in prison, suspended,
for aiding and abetting the sale of cannabis. [@1] The court was convinced that
his use of the knife against a 15-year-old was justified as self-defence. [@1]
The court found the man acted only as the driver in the alleged drug deal. [@1]

The presiding judge, Philipp Wirth, said in his oral reasoning that the youth
opened the car door on the evening of 11 March and attacked the accused with a
knife, and that the accused stabbed without aiming because he feared for his
life. [@1] He had originally stood trial at the regional court for manslaughter
over the dispute in a car park in Eckernförde. [@1] He spent more than half a
year in pre-trial custody, and the probation period was set at four years. [@1]

Prosecutors had also dropped the manslaughter charge during the trial and asked
for three years in prison for selling cannabis. [@1] The judgment is not yet
final; a revision can be filed within one week. [@1]

According to the court, the 15-year-old and his brother did not intend to pay in
the alleged drug deal, had brought counterfeit money and were armed with a knife
and a blank-firing pistol. [@2] The 15-year-old was injured so severely that he
had to be resuscitated and died shortly afterwards on the way to hospital. [@2]

The accused was released from pre-trial custody the previous Friday after more
than half a year. [@2] A lawyer for the victim's parents had asked for a
conviction for manslaughter, and after the verdict a woman in the courtroom
confronted the accused loudly. [@2]
