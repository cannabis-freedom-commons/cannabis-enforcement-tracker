The hotel-room search took place during a large police control in Frankfurt's
Bahnhofsviertel held on Wednesday as part of the so-called Innenstadtoffensive,
which was directed in part against drug dealing. [@1] Plainclothes investigators
searched a hotel room in the station area and found just under 3,000 capsules of
the medicine pregabalin and just over 300 grams of hashish. [@1] The suspect was
arrested shortly afterwards in the station district. [@1]

Across the wider control, officers checked more than 420 people and opened 46
investigations. [@1] Twelve people were taken to the cells at police
headquarters and two arrest warrants were executed. [@1] Police recorded 36
residence-law violations and opened five criminal proceedings on suspicion of
unlawful drug dealing. [@1] Police said narcotics, cannabis products and
prescription medicines were seized during the operation. [@1]

The checks covered the drug scene around the station area, Konstablerwache and
Allerheiligenviertel as well as several bars and restaurants. [@1] Officers also
carried out traffic checks, including checks on e-scooter use on the Zeil
shopping street. [@1] Hesse's interior minister said street crime in the
Bahnhofsviertel had recently fallen by 6.9 percent and that control pressure
would not ease. [@1]
