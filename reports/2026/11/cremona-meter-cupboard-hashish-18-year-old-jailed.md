A judge of the Cremona court on 6 November 2026 refused to release the
18-year-old Tunisian held since September on hashish dealing charges, rejecting
the defence request for house arrest that the honorary prosecutor had backed.
[@1] The youth had a work contract ready for him, but was kept in the prison
measure because the judge said he had continued to commit offences. [@1] The
judge said there was nothing to show the accused had understood the seriousness
of his conduct or gained self-control during the two months he had spent in
prison, citing an earlier arrest for resisting a public official and assault and
other reports of drug possession. [@1] The youth will be tried with the
abbreviated procedure, a fast-track trial without the investigative hearing, on
4 December 2026 while the analysis of the seized drug is awaited. [@1]

The case began on 24 September 2026, when Carabinieri of the Cremona operational
section watching via Lungastretta saw the 18-year-old hand over a 100-gram slab
of hashish to a 23-year-old who paid him 320 euros in cash. [@1] Officers
stopped both men and seized the drug and the money, then searched the residence
in a reception-centre network on via Giordano, where the youth lived. [@1] In
the electricity-meter cupboard beside his flat, used as a safe store reachable
before each sale, Carabinieri found another 900 grams of hashish, bringing the
total to one kilogram. [@1] The 18-year-old was arrested for drug possession and
dealing, while the 23-year-old buyer was reported at liberty for possession for
the purpose of dealing. [@1]

The 18-year-old was already under the residence restriction measure when he was
caught, and he had belonged as a minor to a youth gang broken up in 2024. [@1]

La Provincia reported that the youth arrested on 24 September was held and that
the 900 grams were hidden in the gas-meter space of the reception centre on via
Giordano. [@2]

The same hearing scheduled the trial for 4 December 2026 and noted that the drug
analysis was ordered for 5 November, with the defence still awaiting its result.
[@2]
