The cour d'assises de Fort-de-France, the criminal assize court of the island's
capital, sentenced Joachim Henry to 25 years of réclusion criminelle on Friday 6
November 2026 after finding him guilty of assassinat, murder with premeditation.
[@1] The court imposed a security period of two-thirds of the term and a
permanent ban from French territory. [@1] The court followed the submissions of
the advocate-general, the prosecution's senior representative at the trial. [@1]

The victim was Jean-Mathieu R., a 23-year-old from Le Lamentin, who was shot
twice in the head and died at the scene. [@1] Henry, a 39-year-old Saint Lucian,
had fired several times at him on 26 December 2019 on the chemin de
l'Anse-Moustique in Sainte-Anne, in the south of Martinique. [@1]

He had already been convicted of trafficking cannabis between Sainte-Lucie
(Saint Lucia) and Martinique. [@1] Henry told the court that a dispute over drug
trafficking had set the two men against each other, and he acknowledged having
killed the man he considered a friend. [@1] He denied that the killing was
premeditated. [@1]

Henry had been on trial before the same court since Thursday 5 November, and the
prosecution began its case on Wednesday 4 November. [@2] The law reports give
the scene of the shooting as both the chemin de l'Anse-Moustique and the Anse
Meunier in Sainte-Anne. [@2] A psychological expert told the court that he had
grown up in a deprived neighbourhood of Castries, Saint Lucia's capital, where
illegal livelihoods were normalised. [@2] The trial was to run until Friday 6
November, and the court had to rule on the circumstances of the killing and on
the accused's criminal responsibility. [@3]
