Railway police in Salem seized 3 kg of ganja from two express trains passing
through the city on Sunday, 1 November 2026. [@1] Officers found 2 kg in an
unclaimed bag near the toilet of the front general compartment on the
Patna–Ernakulam Express. [@1] A bag checked near the toilet of the New
Delhi–Thiruvananthapuram Kerala Express held 1 kg of ganja. [@1]

The checks also seized 44 kg of gutka. [@1] The Kerala Express bag held 35 kg of
intoxicating tobacco products, including gutka and pan masala. [@1] Officers
found 9 kg of gutka on the Dibrugarh–Kanyakumari Express. [@1] The 44 kg of
gutka was separate from the 3 kg of ganja. [@1]

Cannabis and tobacco are smuggled by train from northern states into Tamil Nadu
and Kerala, and railway police have formed special teams to stop it. [@1] The
Salem sub-division special team conducted the checks. [@1] The seized ganja and
gutka were handed to the Salem Railway Police. [@1]
