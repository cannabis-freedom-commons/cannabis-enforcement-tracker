Mandau police arrested three men on the evening of 2 November 2026 in two linked searches in Pematang Pudu, Mandau, in Bengkalis, Riau. [@1,3] At about 8:10 p.m., police detained the first man near Jalan Giam and found six packets of cannabis weighing 17.89 grams in the right dashboard compartment of his white Honda Vario motorcycle. [@1] He told police he got the cannabis from a man identified as BKA. [@1,2,3]

Police then went to BKA’s house near Gang Abdul Rahman at about 8:30 p.m. and arrested him and another man. [@1,2] Officers found 19 packets weighing 95.44 grams in a cupboard and two more weighing 15.74 grams in the other man’s Torch sling bag. [@1,2] The two arrests and 21 packets reported by Tribrata TV describe this second search. [@1,2] Across both locations, police seized 27 packets with a net weight of 129.07 grams, three phones and three motorcycles. [@1]

Bengkalis police chief Eko Rubiyanto presented the operation on 4 November. [@1,4] Riau24 identified the men as Yudha Saputra Pratama, Bambang Kurnia Azlansyah and Hardhi Putra, citing the police briefing. [@4] Police said they were seeking a fourth person identified as AM and cited Articles 114(1) and 111(1) of Indonesia’s Narcotics Law. [@1]
