Mandau Police's operational team arrested two men identified as BKA (34) and AP
(31) in Duri on the evening of 2 November 2026. [@1,3] The arrests took place at a
house on Jalan Asrama Tribrata, Gang Abdul Rahman, in Pematang Pudu, Mandau,
Bengkalis. [@1,3] Both men are residents of Pematang Pudu and were taken with the
evidence to Mandau Police Station for examination and further investigation.
[@1]

Police seized a total of 21 packets of cannabis-type narcotics in the operation.
[@1,2,3] Officers found 19 packets kept by BKA inside a wardrobe, comprising one
large packet, one medium packet and 17 small packets. [@1,3] Two further packets
were found in a Torch-brand sling bag in AP's possession. [@1,3] Alongside the
cannabis, officers seized two mobile phones and two motorcycles, a black Yamaha
NMAX and a white Yamaha Jupiter MX. [@1,2]

The operation followed a statement by detained suspect YS that the cannabis had
come from BKA. [@1,2] Acting on that information, the Mandau operational team
investigated and went to the house at the scene. [@1] Police said the case is
being pursued as alleged cannabis trafficking under Article 111 of Indonesia's
Narcotics Law No. 35 of 2009 in conjunction with Article 609 on penalty
adjustment. [@1] Investigators said they are still examining the case to trace
the origin of the drugs and the involvement of other parties. [@1]
