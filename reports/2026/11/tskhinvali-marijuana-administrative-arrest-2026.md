South Ossetia's Interior Ministry detained a resident of Tskhinvali for unlawful
use and storage of narcotics. [@1] During a search of his household,
drug-control officers seized marijuana, and traces of the substance were also
found during a medical examination. [@1]

A court imposed one month of administrative arrest on the man. [@1] He also
faces punishment for drug storage, with the inquiry conducted by the Drug
Control Directorate of the Interior Ministry. [@1]
