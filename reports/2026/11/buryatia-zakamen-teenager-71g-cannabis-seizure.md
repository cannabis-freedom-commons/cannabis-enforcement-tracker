Police in Zakamensky district seized a narcotic substance from a minor during an
interdepartmental raid, as reported by the Buryatia Interior Ministry. [@1] The
raid involved juvenile-affairs officers of the district police department,
members of the juvenile commission, the youth department and the district penal
inspection. [@1] A 17-year-old villager was in the house when the substance was
found. [@1] He was taken to a medical facility for an examination for drug
intoxication, but refused to comply with the officer's lawful demand to undergo
the procedure. [@1] The detained teenager explained that he had gathered the
prohibited plant himself solely for personal use. [@1]

During the check of one address, officers noticed a sharp characteristic smell
of acetone coming from the household grounds. [@1] In a metal wagon in the yard,
officers found a packet of dry plant material that smelled distinctly of
wild-growing cannabis. [@1] The total weight of the drug in dried form was more
than 71 grams, confirmed by examination. [@1]

For refusing the examination, an administrative report was drawn up against him
under Part 1 of Article 6.9 of the Code of Administrative Offences. [@2] A
criminal case was opened over the discovery under Part 1 of Article 228 of the
Criminal Code for unlawful acquisition and storage of narcotics without intent
to sell in a significant amount. [@2]
