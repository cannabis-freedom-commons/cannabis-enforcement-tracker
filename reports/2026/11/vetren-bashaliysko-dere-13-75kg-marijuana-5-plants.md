Officers from the criminal police group at the Septemvri police directorate
caught a 49-year-old man from Septemvri in the Bashaliysko dere locality near
the town of Vetren. [@1] The man was carrying the drug in two polythene sacks as
dry leaf mass of heads and inflorescences. [@1] Field drug tests identified the
material as dry marijuana with a total weight of 13.75 kilograms. [@1]

In a neighbouring locality officers found five planted cannabis plants between
0.5 and 1.3 metres tall. [@1] The growing site had been fitted with a drip
irrigation system. [@1] Two electronic scales were found in the man's home. [@1]
All the drug and equipment were seized and the man was held in custody at the
Septemvri police directorate for up to 24 hours. [@1]

The operation was run as a drive against the production and distribution of
drugs. [@1] The article presents the arrest as the failure of an attempt to use
the election day, when more than 600 Interior Ministry officers were deployed in
the region, as cover. [@1] Pre-trial proceedings are being conducted under the
supervision of the Pazardzhik district prosecution service. [@1]
