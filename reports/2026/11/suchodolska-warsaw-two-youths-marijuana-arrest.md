Officers of Warsaw's VII Territorial municipal-guard unit went to Suchodolska
Street in Praga-Południe after residents reported noisy behaviour by a group of
young people. [@1] They found five young people gathered at a bench in a housing
estate. [@1] The guards found marijuana on two of the participants after
noticing suspicious behaviour. [@1] One had hidden something behind his trouser
elastic while the other had dropped a small packet under the bench. [@1] The
packet and a cigarette pack hidden behind a belt contained marijuana, which the
municipal guard confirmed. [@1]

The incident happened on Monday, 2 November, after a report at about 18:05 that
youths were drinking alcohol and shouting vulgar words. [@1] The patrol arrived
about 15 minutes later and located the group without difficulty. [@1] The guards
saw no one drinking alcohol despite the initial report. [@1] Police were called
to the scene and took the two young people carrying marijuana away. [@1]
Officers will establish where the seized substance came from. [@1]

One of the youths also produced a sealed bottle of vodka that he said he was
saving for his 18th birthday the next day. [@1] The guards confirmed that he was
still one day short of adulthood. [@1] His mother was called to the scene and
took the alcohol from him. [@1] The guards held an educational talk with the
remaining participants. [@1]
