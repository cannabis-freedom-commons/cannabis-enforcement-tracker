On 12 November 2026, authorities of the three levels of government held a
destruction and incineration of seized narcotics and controlled substances in
Tijuana, including marijuana, cocaine, methamphetamine and controlled medicines.
[@1]

Among the destroyed quantities were 991 kilograms, 284 grams and 645 milligrams
of active cannabis. [@1] Also incinerated were 560 kilograms, 417 grams and 600
milligrams of cocaine hydrochloride, 7 kilograms and 711 grams of
diacetylmorphine hydrochloride, and 2 kilograms, 3 grams and 400 milligrams of
cocaine. [@1] In cannabis-derived products, the destruction included 61 grams
and 300 milligrams of CBD (cannabidiol), as well as 30 litres of the same
substance. [@1]

The ceremony was held at the Second Military Zone of the National Defence
Secretariat (Sedena) on the morning of Thursday 12 November, and more than
twelve tonnes of drugs were incinerated in total, of which 4.7 tonnes came from
the FGR delegation and 8.4 tonnes from the FEMDO. [@2] The FGR delegation's list
of destroyed substances also included 4 kilograms, 874 grams and 100 milligrams
of marijuana and 9 kilograms, 376 grams and 600 milligrams of cannabis sativa
seeds. [@2]
