A man and a woman went into a flat in Seinäjoki and demanded 1,000 euros. [@1]
The man threatened the occupant with a knife and beat, kicked and strangled him
while the woman collected coats, handbags and jewellery. [@1] Police caught the
pair in the stairwell with the stolen property. [@1] The stolen property was
returned to the victim. [@1] The robbery happened on 16 August 2026. [@1]

The victim was attacked again as he lay defenceless on the floor, pushed onto
broken glass on the balcony and dragged back inside. [@1] He managed to message
a friend, who called the police. [@1] The defendants denied aggravated robbery
and breach of domestic peace. [@1] The man argued self-defence and said he had
taken only his own clothes, while the woman admitted theft but denied acting
jointly. [@1] The court found the victim's account credible, supported by his
injuries, photographs and messages. [@1] It held that the woman knew about the
violence, also demanded money and was therefore guilty as a perpetrator although
she did not use violence herself. [@1] Both were convicted of aggravated robbery
and breach of domestic peace. [@1] The court called the robbery particularly
cruel and carried out with a bladed weapon, made more serious by taking place in
the victim's home. [@1]

The 20-year-old man was also convicted of criminal damage, two drug offences,
possession of an object suitable for causing serious injury and a minor firearms
offence after breaking a television and a light strip, keeping and selling drugs
and carrying a knife and pepper spray in public without a licence. [@1] He was
sentenced to a total of three years and four months in prison, including a
627-day remainder and an earlier suspended term, and was ordered held in
custody. [@1] The 35-year-old woman was also convicted of a drug-use offence and
had 1.2 grams of cannabis in her possession. [@1] Her joint sentence was two
years and two months in prison. [@1] The pair must pay the victim 700 euros for
temporary harm and 4,500 euros for suffering, with interest. [@1] The younger
defendant must also pay 220 euros for the broken television and light strip and
forfeit 665 euros in drug-trade profit to the state. [@1] Each was ordered to
pay a 120-euro victim surcharge. [@1]
