Rawalpindi police arrested five drug suppliers in separate operations across the
city on Monday, 2 November 2026. [@1] Rawat Police held one supplier, Civil
Lines Police arrested two suppliers, Dhamial Police arrested one supplier and
Pirwadhai Police apprehended one supplier. [@1]

Rawat Police recovered 2.5kg of drugs from its held supplier without the account
specifying the drug type. [@1] Civil Lines Police seized 1.6kg of hashish and
250 grams of ice from the two suppliers it arrested. [@1] Dhamial Police
recovered 1.3kg of hashish from its arrested supplier, while Pirwadhai Police
seized 1.06 kg of hashish from its apprehended supplier. [@1] The separately
specified hashish amounts total 3.96kg, while the 250 grams of ice and the
unspecified 2.5kg are excluded from the cannabis figure. [@1]

Police registered separate cases against all the accused. [@1] A police
spokesperson said all possible measures were being taken to eradicate the menace
of drugs from society. [@1]
