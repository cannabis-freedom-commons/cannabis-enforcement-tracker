Military Police arrested a man for drug trafficking on Rua Independência in the
Getal neighborhood of Lages after he tried to flee an approach. [@1] The
incident was recorded at around 22h30. [@1] During patrol, officers noticed the
man's suspicious behavior, and when he saw the officers he abandoned his bicycle
and fled on foot before being pursued and stopped. [@1]

With a sniffer dog, officers retraced the escape route and found two portions of
a substance resembling marijuana weighing about 20 grams. [@1] During the stop,
the suspect allegedly admitted that he sold small quantities of the drug. [@1]
Based on that information, officers went to the residence where he was living.
[@1] With the authorization of the person responsible for the property, the team
searched the room occupied by the man. [@1] The sniffer dog found a bag
containing another 10 portions of marijuana, already packaged and separated for
sale, totaling about 180 grams. [@1] In total, 12 portions weighing about 200
grams were seized. [@1]

The man was arrested and taken to the Polícia Científica for a forensic
examination, then to the Central de Polícia Civil with the seized material for
legal procedures. [@1] The account attributes the information to the Santa
Catarina Military Police. [@1]
