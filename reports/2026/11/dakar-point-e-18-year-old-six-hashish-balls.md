Police from the Point-E district police station's research unit stopped an
18-year-old man, identified as O. Wade, on the night of 12 November 2026 in
Point-E, Dakar. [@1] At around 3 a.m. officers on patrol found the young man
near the Le Relais bar-restaurant and, after an identity check, frisked him.
[@1] The search found six balls of hashish, a string of the same substance and a
pack of Rizla rolling papers in his possession. [@1] Taken to the police station
for questioning, the suspect reportedly admitted owning the drugs and said they
were for his personal consumption. [@1] He was placed in custody for possession
of a hard drug and police opened an investigation into the exact circumstances
of the case. [@1]
