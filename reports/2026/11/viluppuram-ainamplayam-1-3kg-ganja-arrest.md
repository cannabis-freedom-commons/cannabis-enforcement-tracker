Officers of the Viluppuram taluk police were on patrol in Ainamplayam village,
near Viluppuram, on 11 November 2026. [@1] They stopped a young man who was
standing in the village in a suspicious manner, and searched him. [@1] The
search found 1.300 kg of ganja, which the officers seized before questioning
him. [@1]

The inquiry established that he was Rajeshkumar, 22, son of Nagappan, from the
same village. [@1] Police found that he had kept the ganja for sale. [@1] They
registered a case against him and arrested him. [@1] Ganja is the name used in
Tamil Nadu for cannabis. [@1]
