Officers of the Anti-Drug and Smuggling Unit (ADSU), the Mauritian police's drug
squad, searched the home of a 24-year-old mason living in Grand-Bois at 6.10 am
on Sunday 8 November 2026. [@1]

During the search officers found a piece of printed paper holding 0.9 grams of
cannabis. [@1] The officers also found an illegal cannabis crop at the property
and uprooted 186 plants measuring between 3 cm and 40 cm in height, with a total
weight of 36 grams. [@1]

The man was arrested after the seizure and placed in police detention. [@1] The
investigation was continuing. [@1]
