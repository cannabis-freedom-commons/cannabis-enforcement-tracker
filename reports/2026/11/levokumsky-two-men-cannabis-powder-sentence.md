Two men from Levokumsky District in Stavropol Krai were convicted of illegally
storing narcotics and explosive substances, according to the region's FSB
directorate and prosecutor's office. [@2] The court sentenced the 30-year-old to
two years and the 67-year-old to three years and three months in a
general-regime penal colony. [@2] The older man was also ordered to pay a fine
of 5,000 roubles. [@1] The sentence has not yet taken legal effect. [@1]

In December 2025 the two men received cannabis from acquaintances to store for
them. [@2] A search of the men's home found and seized more than 1.4 kg of the
drug and more than 217 grams of explosive material. [@3] The older man had found
a metal jar of smokeless powder, a propellant explosive, while grazing livestock
in November 2023 and had not handed it to the authorities. [@2] He kept the jar
at his livestock point, and more than 0.7 kg of marijuana was stored next to it.
[@1]

The court found the man guilty on both counts, the narcotics and the explosives.
[@1] Cases against the people who handed the cannabis to the two men are being
considered separately. [@1]
