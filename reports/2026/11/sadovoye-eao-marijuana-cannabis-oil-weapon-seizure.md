Police found drugs, a smoothbore weapon and ammunition at the home of a local
resident in the village of Sadovoye in Oktyabrsky District. [@3] A criminal case
was opened over the unlawful storage of drugs. [@3] The search covered the
homestead of an unemployed man born in 1980, whom police did not name. [@2] The
search followed a tip to the criminal investigation unit at the Amurzet police
station of the Leninsky interior ministry division. [@2] Officers seized more
than 730 grams of marijuana and 120.65 grams of cannabis oil. [@1]

Officers also found an object resembling a smoothbore shotgun and 16 cartridges
in a room and in the kitchen. [@1] An examination confirmed that the weapon and
ammunition were fit for use. [@1] The man told police he had grown the marijuana
himself and kept it for his own use. [@1] He said the gun and cartridges had
belonged to his late father. [@1]

A criminal case was opened under part 2 of article 228 of the Russian Criminal
Code, which covers acquiring, storing, transporting, making or processing drugs
without intent to sell on a large scale. [@2] Officers also drew up
administrative papers over the unregistered weapon and cartridges under article
20.10 of the administrative code. [@2] Police in the Jewish Autonomous Oblast
reported the case publicly through the regional interior ministry press service.
[@2]
