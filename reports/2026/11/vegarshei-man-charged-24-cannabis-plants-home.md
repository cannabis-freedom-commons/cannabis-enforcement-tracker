Police in Agder, southern Norway, found that a man had tried to grow cannabis
plants in a residential house in Vegårshei, a rural municipality inland from
Arendal, in a case that has now reached the courts. [@1] The man is charged and
his case is before Agder tingrett, the district court for the Agder region, so
it has passed the investigation stage and awaits a court hearing. [@1] Local
police stopped the cultivation, and the newspaper Tvedestrandsposten, which
covers the region, reported the charge on 7 November 2026. [@1]

The newspaper gives no weight for the plants, no street value, and no report of
anyone being arrested or held in custody over the grow. [@1] The charge is
attempted cultivation rather than a completed harvest, meaning the accounts
describe an attempt to grow the plants rather than a finished crop. [@1]
