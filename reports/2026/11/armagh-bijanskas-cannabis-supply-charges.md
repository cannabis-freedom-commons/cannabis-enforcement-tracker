A 34-year-old man appeared before the courts in connection with a series of
alleged drugs, weapons and criminal property offences. [@1] The defendant is
Tadas Bijanskas, of Navan Court, Armagh. [@1] His case was before Armagh
Magistrates' Court, where the charges were formally put to him. [@1]

Bijanskas is accused of possessing cannabis, conspiring to possess cannabis and
supplying the drug. [@1] He is also charged with possessing a firearm and an
incapacitant spray. [@1] He further faces allegations of possessing and
transferring criminal property, said to represent the proceeds of crime. [@1]
The offences are alleged to have occurred on various dates between March 1 and
September 29, 2025. [@1]

A detective constable aware of the facts of the case outlined the allegations
and told the court that the charges could be connected. [@1] The case remains at
an early point in the court process and the allegations have not been tested
before a court. [@1] No evidence was heard in relation to the charges, and no
findings have been made regarding the accusations. [@1] Deputy District Judge
Austin Kennedy remanded Bijanskas on continuing bail. [@1] The case was
adjourned and is due to return before Armagh Magistrates' Court on December 1
for further consideration. [@1] The charges remain allegations at this stage,
and Bijanskas is entitled to the presumption of innocence unless and until the
offences are proved before the court. [@1]
