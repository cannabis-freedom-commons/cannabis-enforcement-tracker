On 3 November the Cherbourg-en-Cotentin court tried a 29-year-old man in his
absence for driving without a licence and while positive for drugs. [@1] He was
under curatelle, a court-appointed guardianship, and was in prison at the time
of the hearing. [@1] He refused to appear by video link. [@1]

On 11 March 2024 police stopped him at the wheel in Cherbourg, where he had no
licence, and his saliva test was positive for cannabis. [@1] His criminal record
held 34 entries for road offences and drug use. [@1] His lawyer told the court
he has bipolar disorder and had bought the car on 10 March 2024, the day before
the stop. [@1] She said he had left detention in November 2023 and had been back
in prison since April 2024, and that his disorder led to repeated offences. [@1]

The prosecutor called the record damning and sought six months of firm prison.
[@1] The court sentenced him to five months of firm prison. [@1]
