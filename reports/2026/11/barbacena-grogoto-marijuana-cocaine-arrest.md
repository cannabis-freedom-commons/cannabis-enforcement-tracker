Military Police in Barbacena, Minas Gerais, arrested a 38-year-old man on
suspicion of drug trafficking in a condominium in the Bairro Grogotó early on
Friday 6 November 2026. [@1] The operation began after officers received an
anonymous tip about drug sales at the location. [@1]

During the check, police saw the suspect at a window of the property smoking
crack and he ran inside the house when he noticed the officers. [@1] With the
occupant's authorisation, the officers entered the home and searched the man's
bedroom. [@1]

In the room they seized one bar and eight wraps of marijuana, a raw lump of
cocaine and two precision scales. [@1] The suspect was arrested in the act and
taken with the seized material to the on-duty Civil Police station. [@1]
