A Military Police patrol arrested a man in flagrante for drug trafficking on the
night of Saturday 7 November 2026 in Vila Nova, a neighbourhood of Bento
Gonçalves in Rio Grande do Sul. [@1] The arrest was made by a patrol from the
43rd Military Police Battalion (43º BPM) during routine visible policing in the
area. [@1] Officers spotted a motorcycle driving suspiciously, and its rider
tried to flee when he noticed the patrol car before being intercepted and
stopped shortly afterwards. [@1]

A personal search found 29 grams of marijuana, 13 portions of cocaine, R$ 223 in
banknotes and three mobile phones. [@1] Police also seized the motorcycle the
suspect had ridden during the escape attempt. [@1] The man was told he was under
arrest and taken to the Bento Gonçalves on-duty police station (DPPA) so that
the arrest in flagrante report could be drawn up. [@1]
