A joint operation by the 33º BPM and the Civil Police in Sapinhatuba II, in
Angra dos Reis, ended with one man arrested and one adolescent apprehended. [@1]
Angranews places the action in Angra dos Reis, Rio de Janeiro, Brazil. [@1] The
action was publicized on Wednesday, 4 November. [@1] According to the 33º BPM,
the operation was carried out to combat territorial disputes between criminals
and to weaken the activity of criminal organizations in the locality. [@1]

Officers collected 101 strips of marijuana and two tubes containing marijuana.
[@1] Officers also collected 31 units of hashish, the drug listed as haxixe.
[@1] The same seizure also included 208 cocaine pins, 151 crack rocks and three
ecstasy tablets, listed here only to explain the mixed seizure. [@1] During the
operation, agents seized a Taurus G2C .40 pistol with a suppressed serial
number, three magazines and 26 .40 CBC rounds. [@1]

The force also seized three communication radios, one radio-charger base and two
notebooks with the group's notes. [@1] Angranews cites the 33º BPM as the source
for the account. [@1]
