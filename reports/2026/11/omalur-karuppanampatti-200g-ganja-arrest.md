Omalur police arrested Bharath, a 25-year-old local resident, and seized 200
grams of ganja, the local term for cannabis. [@1] A special police team
initiated the case while conducting surveillance at Karuppanampatti near Omalur,
where officers stopped and searched a man who came by. [@1]

The search revealed that the man was keeping the 200 grams of ganja for sale,
and questioning identified him as Bharath from the same area. [@1] The special
police team handed Bharath over to Omalur police for the arrest and seizure.
