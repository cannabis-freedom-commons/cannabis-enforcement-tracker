On Monday afternoon, personnel from Alipurduar police station reached Bamanpara,
a village under the Salkumar-II panchayat of Alipurduar district, to act against
illegal cannabis cultivation. [@1] Inspector-in-charge Anirban Bhattacharjee led
the operation at the village plots. [@1] Police identified the cultivation plots
as belonging to landowners Parimal Karjee and Tapas Karjee. [@1] Both men evaded
the police during the raid, managing to give officers the slip before they could
be detained. [@1] Police said a search for the two landowners was under way
after the clearance. [@1]

Police found the illegal cannabis crop spread across 3 bighas of land, about
0.40 hectares on the West Bengal measure where one bigha is about 0.1338
hectares. [@1] Officers destroyed the standing cannabis cultivation on that land
during the operation. [@1] The operation treated the 3-bigha cannabis crop as
illegal cultivation to be cleared from the village plots. [@1]

A panchayat is the local rural administrative area, and Salkumar-II is the
panchayat area named for Bamanpara in the police account. [@1]
