National Police officers detained a 33-year-old Venezuelan man at the Puquio
crossing in the district of Vista Alegre, in Nasca, after finding several bags
of suspected illicit drugs in his possession during an identity-control
operation. [@1] The intervention took place on Wednesday 11 November between
1:00 and 2:00 in the afternoon as part of the police operation called Control de
Identidad-2026, carried out by personnel from the Vista Alegre police station.
[@1] The detained man was identified as Yojan Wesslis Diaz Sanchez, aged 33 and
of Venezuelan nationality, held on suspicion of the public-health offence of
promotion and facilitation of illicit drug trafficking against the Peruvian
state. [@1]

During the search, officers found four transparent chupetera-type bags
containing dry leaves, stems and greenish seeds, apparently cannabis sativa
(marijuana). [@1] Officers also found another eight bags of the same type
containing a creamy white powdery substance with characteristics similar to
cocaine hydrochloride. [@1]

Police also seized three hair clippers, a wooden-handled comb, black scissors, a
ZTE mobile phone and a black jacket with a Nike logo. [@1] Police also impounded
a Barsha scooter-type motorcycle with licence plate No. 84096Y. [@1]
