Special Response Battalion-6 detained 58-year-old Ashraf Matubbar in Muksudpur,
Gopalganj, as an alleged drug dealer. [@1] The raid took place at about 11:30 pm
on Friday 6 November 2026 in the Dakshin Panchbari area of Ward 4, Maharajpur
Union, Muksudpur. [@1] Acting on a tip-off, an SRB-6 operational team from the
Sadar Company at Bhatiapara Camp carried out the operation. [@1]

Officers recovered 785 yaba tablets and 300 grams of ganja from under a pile of
firewood beside a water tank on the south side of Matubbar's house, as pointed
out by him. [@1] The SRB Bhatiapara camp in-charge said the operation was part
of regular drives to implement a zero-tolerance policy against drugs. [@1] The
seized drugs were confiscated and a regular case under the Narcotics Control Act
was filed at Muksudpur Police Station. [@1]

The detainee and the recovered items were handed to Muksudpur police on Saturday
morning, and a case was filed over the seizure. [@1] After the case was filed he
was shown arrested and sent to jail through the court on Saturday, according to
the Muksudpur police station chief. [@1]
