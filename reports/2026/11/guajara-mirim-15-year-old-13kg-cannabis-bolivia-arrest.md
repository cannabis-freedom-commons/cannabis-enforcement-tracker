Military Police in Guajará-Mirim detained a 15-year-old boy carrying about 13
kilos of cannabis during a stop on the night of Monday 9 November 2026. [@1] The
cannabis had been brought from Bolivia and was to be carried on to Porto Velho.
[@1] The stop was made under Operation Madeira-Mamoré II, after officers learned
that a white Volkswagen Gol had picked up a passenger carrying a backpack near
the Pérola Negra clandestine port in the Triângulo neighbourhood. [@1] Police
intercepted the car at the crossing of Constitution and Princesa Isabel avenues
and found the boy in the back seat with a backpack holding 24 tablets of a
substance resembling cannabis, along with a mobile phone, 15 hand-rolled joints
and one portion of the drug. [@1]

The boy told officers he would be paid 1,000 reais to fetch the drug in
Guayaramerín, Bolivia, cross the Mamoré river and carry it to Porto Velho. [@1]
The 22-year-old driver said she works for an app and did not know the backpack's
contents. [@1] The boy was detained for an act amounting to drug trafficking and
taken to the Civil Police station with his mother, with the seized material
handed to the authorities. [@1] The same account is published by Portal SGC,
which states the 15-year-old was detained with about 13 kilos of cannabis
brought from Bolivia that was to be carried to Porto Velho, confirming the
incident. [@2]
