The fire occurred on Saturday 7 November 2026. [@5] Around 3:30 that morning, a
fire broke out in a house on Bergstraat in Tessenderlo and spread to the
adjoining house. [@3] When firefighters from the South-West Limburg emergency
zone arrived, the blaze was already well developed. [@3] One female resident
managed to get out in time. [@3] No one was injured, but the upper floors
suffered heavy damage. [@3] TVL reported that nobody was home at the moment of
the fire. [@4]

During the firefighting, emergency workers discovered a cannabis plantation in
the house. [@4] The Limburg prosecutor's office confirmed that a cannabis
plantation stood in one of the houses. [@2] The scale and capacity of the
plantation are not yet known. [@3] A laboratory is now examining exactly how
large the plantation is. [@4] Initial findings indicate that electricity in the
house was tapped illegally. [@5] Police from the Regio West-Limburg force went
to the scene, made initial findings and opened an investigation. [@3]

A cannabis plantation was discovered during a severe fire in Tessenderlo that
left two houses uninhabitable. [@1] Both homes were declared temporarily
uninhabitable because of the fire damage. [@3] No arrests had been made at the
time of the initial reporting. [@2]
