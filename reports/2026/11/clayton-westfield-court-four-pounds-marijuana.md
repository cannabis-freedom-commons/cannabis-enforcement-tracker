Johnston County sheriff's deputies arrested two people near Clayton after
investigating a disturbance. [@1] Deputies responded to the disturbance on
Westfield Court, off Covered Bridge Road, around 10:30 p.m. on Friday, 6
November 2026. [@1] According to the Johnston County Sheriff's Office,
responding deputies arrived and identified two individuals allegedly involved in
the incident. [@1] Officers took 22-year-old Micha Deangelo Walton-Smith of
Laurel, Maryland, and 20-year-old Jazmin Saintlien of Durham into custody. [@1]

While conducting the disturbance investigation, officers noticed a strong odor
of marijuana coming from a vehicle. [@1] A subsequent search of the vehicle
resulted in the seizure of approximately four pounds (about 1.81 kg) of
suspected marijuana along with an undisclosed amount of cash. [@1]

Both were charged with possession with intent to sell or deliver marijuana,
maintaining a vehicle or dwelling for a controlled substance, and conspiracy to
sell or deliver marijuana. [@1] Walton-Smith was also charged with felony
possession of marijuana. [@1] Walton-Smith was held under a $30,000 bond, while
Saintlien received a $20,000 bond. [@1]
