Federal Highway Police officers and other security forces stopped a suspected
vehicle as it reached the urban perimeter of Cassilândia in Mato Grosso do Sul
on the eve of the holiday. [@1]

The search found more than 100 kilograms of drugs, among them pressed marijuana
and skunk, and officers arrested the car's two occupants in the act. [@1] The
car used to carry the load had been reported stolen in another municipality and
was taken in by police. [@1] Further checks in cooperation with police forces in
neighbouring Goiás state led to a second vehicle, another drug seizure and the
arrest of more accomplices. [@1]

The details come from Cassilândia's police chief, Rodrigo de Freitas, speaking
on Rádio Patriarca's Rotativa no Ar programme. [@1] The police chief gave the
interview on Wednesday 4 November 2026 and presented it as a tally of recent
anti-trafficking operations. [@2] Cassilândia Notícias described the intercepted
load as more than 100 kilos of drugs that included traditional pressed marijuana
and skunk, a high-THC variety with a high market value. [@2]
