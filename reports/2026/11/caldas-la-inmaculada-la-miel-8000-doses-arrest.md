National Police officers arrested a man known as alias 'El Negro' in the
Antioquia municipality of Caldas, describing him as an alleged member of the
local organised crime group 'La Miel'. [@1] The arrest came during two search
and raid procedures in the sector La Inmaculada. [@1] Hora 13 reported that the
two raids were carried out at a house in Caldas. [@3] Police said the house was
used to store, dose and sell drugs. [@1] According to the investigation, he was
linked to selling marijuana and cocaine hydrochloride in the sector known as 'El
Hueco', in Caldas. [@1] During the searches, officers found and seized about
8,000 doses of the two substances. [@1] Hora 13 gave the same quantity as about
8,000 doses of marijuana and cocaine hydrochloride. [@3] Nación Paisa reported
the same arrest and the seizure of about 8,000 doses of the two substances. [@2]
Officers also found accounting notes that, according to the investigation, were
linked to drug sales. [@1] The official report did not say how many of the doses
were marijuana and how many were cocaine hydrochloride. [@1]

He was caught in the act and handed to the Attorney General's Office on
suspicion of trafficking, manufacturing or carrying narcotics. [@1] A judge of
control of guarantees then imposed a preventive measure of detention in a prison
centre while the case proceeds. [@1] MiOriente reported that a judge ordered his
reclusion in a prison centre. [@4] Nación Paisa headlined that the suspect had
been sent to prison. [@2] Lieutenant Colonel Darwin Arango, commander of the 7th
South District of the Aburrá Valley Metropolitan Police, confirmed the arrest.
[@3] The police statement gave neither his identity nor his age, and did not
give the date of the searches or the total weight of the drugs. [@1]
