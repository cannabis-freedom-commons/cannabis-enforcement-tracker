officers believed he was driving without insurance. [@1] Officers detected a
strong smell of suspected cannabis during the stop. [@1] The man was arrested on
suspicion of drug driving, possession of cannabis and driving without insurance.
[@1]

a positive result for cannabis. [@1] The arrest was for allegedly driving under
the influence of cannabis on a busy road near a fireworks display. [@1]

including many children, were gathered nearby for the town rugby club's
fireworks display. [@1] Police added that drug and drink driving can have
devastating consequences and that decisions behind the wheel can cost lives,
