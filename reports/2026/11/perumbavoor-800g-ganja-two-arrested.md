Police arrested two migrant workers from Assam on Wednesday at a plywood-company
room near Perumbavoor. [@1] The arrested men are Shahjahan Basha, 34, and Julhas
Ali, 27, both of Nagaon. [@1] A special investigation team led by the Ernakulam
Rural district police chief made the arrests together with Perumbavoor police.
[@1] The Mangalam account identifies the two as Assam Nagaon natives Shahjahan
Basha and Julhas Ali held by the same Rural special team and Perumbavoor police.
[@2]

The room was in a plywood company at Allapra Kuttippadam, where the men lived.
[@2] Police inspected the room after a tip-off that the pair were selling liquor
and ganja from it. [@1] Police said the pair had been under surveillance for
some time before the inspection. [@1] According to police, the room had been
specially arranged for drug consumption and liquor sales. [@1]

Their customers were primarily migrant workers employed in the area. [@1] The
duo allegedly used their jobs at the plywood company as cover while selling
liquor and ganja among interstate workers. [@2] Officers seized around 50 litres
of beer and 800 grams of ganja in the operation, with the 800 grams as the
separate cannabis component. [@1] The investigation was under way. [@1]
