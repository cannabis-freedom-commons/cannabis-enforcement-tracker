San Pedro municipal police detained 34-year-old Eliézer “N” for his alleged
involvement in crimes against health. [@1] Officers had gone to a home at the
crossing of Río Ródano and Río Tajo in the Del Valle neighbourhood after a
report of a man allegedly trying to enter a dwelling. [@2] At the scene they
located a man matching the description in the report. [@1]

During an inspection officers allegedly found several wrappers containing dry
herb with characteristics similar to marijuana. [@2]

The intervention resulted from preventive patrols and citizen reports attended
by municipal officers in different sectors of the municipality. [@1] The
municipal Public Security Ministry said it maintained preventive patrols across
the municipality during the festivities. [@1] The Technological Canine Squadron
and the K9 unit took part, with a presence in streets and public spaces. [@1]
Police said the same Halloween sweep also involved alleged document forgery and
family-violence cases. [@1] He was placed at the disposal of the corresponding
authorities, who will determine his legal situation. [@1] He must be presumed
innocent absent a final conviction. [@1]
