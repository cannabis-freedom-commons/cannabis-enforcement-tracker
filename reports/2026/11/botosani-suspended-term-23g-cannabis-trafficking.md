The Botoșani Tribunal convicted a man of continued trafficking of risk drugs.
[@1] The case concerned two acts of trafficking risk drugs without right. [@1]
The judgment was pronounced on 6 November 2026. [@1] The court imposed two years
and three months in prison. [@1] Execution of the prison term was suspended
under supervision, so he will not go behind bars for now. [@1]

The court set a three-year supervision period. [@1] During supervision he must
report to the Botoșani Probation Service, keep meetings with his designated
counsellor and report any change of home or workplace. [@1] He must also follow
a social reintegration programme and perform 80 days of unpaid community work.
[@1] The community work is to be carried out at Botoșani City Hall or the
Directorate for Public Services, Sport and Leisure. [@1] The judges also barred
him for two years from being elected to public authorities or holding a position
involving state authority. [@1]

In the same case the court ordered the confiscation and destruction of 23.21
grams of cannabis left over after laboratory analyses. [@1] The man was ordered
to pay 3,464 lei in court costs advanced by the state. [@1] He had been detained
and held in pre-trial detention from 24 March to 10 May 2026. [@1] That
detention period is to be deducted from the sentence if its enforcement is ever
ordered. [@1] The sentence is not final and can be appealed within 10 days of
notification. [@1]
