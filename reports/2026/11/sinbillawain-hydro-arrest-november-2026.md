Detectives at Sinbillawain police station in Dakahlia Governorate arrested a
young man said to have been carrying a large quantity of drugs for sale to users
in the town. [@1] Police said the man, identified only by the initials Kh. M.
and in his twenties, was dealing drugs in the town centre area. [@1]

Prosecutors authorised a search, and an operation led by the head of the
station's criminal investigation unit arrested him and seized a large quantity
of hydro, an Egyptian cannabis product made from treated hemp, along with a roll
of shabu (methamphetamine), some beige powder and cash. [@1] Police said he
admitted holding the drugs for sale and that the money came from his sales. [@1]

A felony case file was opened at Sinbillawain police station for 2026, and
prosecutors ordered him held four days pending investigation. [@1]
