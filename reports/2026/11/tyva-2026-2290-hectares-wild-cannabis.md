Tyvan regional authorities reported that 2,290.9 hectares of wild cannabis was
destroyed in the Republic of Tyva during 2026, equal to 99.4% of the plan. [@1]
The work was part of the Republic of Tyva's anti-alcohol and anti-drug state
programme, which runs from the destruction of drug-plants to social
rehabilitation. [@1]

The cannabis was destroyed by chemical treatment, mechanical and manual mowing
and weeding. [@1] Crops were sown on 230 hectares of the treated land. [@1] The
Tyvan government spent 2.93 million roubles from the regional budget on
destroying wild cannabis, including herbicides and container disposal. [@1]
