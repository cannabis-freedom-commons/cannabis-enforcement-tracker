On 4 November 2026, members of the Laois Drugs Unit and the Laois Roads Policing
Unit stopped and searched a car on the M7 at Clondouglas, Portlaoise. [@3,4,5] The
woman, who is aged in her early 40s, was arrested and taken to Abbeyleix Garda
Station for questioning. [@1] She appeared before a sitting of Portlaoise
District Court on Thursday, November 5, having been charged with an offence
under Section 15 of the Misuse of Drugs Act 1977/84. [@3,4] A woman in her 20s has
appeared in court after a cannabis seizure valued at €280,000. [@2]

Officers found about 20kg of cannabis inside the vehicle during the search. [@3,4]
Gardaí valued the cannabis at about €280,000. [@1,5] The stop happened at around
8.15pm on Wednesday evening between the toll plaza and Junction 18 on the M7 at
Portlaoise. [@1] The search was conducted under Section 23 of the Misuse of
Drugs Act 1977/84. [@3] Gardaí said no further comment would be made because the
matter is now before the courts. [@3,4]

The accused was named as 39-year-old Tracy Norton of The Burrow, Knockrigg, Grange Con, County Wicklow. [@5] She was charged with possessing drugs for her own use as well as possessing drugs for sale or supply. [@5] When the charges were put to her, she made no reply. [@5] Judge Andrew Cody heard there was no Garda objection to bail. [@5] Judge Cody remanded Ms Norton on her own bail to the 22 February sitting for directions from the Director of Public Prosecutions, on conditions that she sign on twice weekly at her local Garda station, provide a mobile contact number and have no contact with two unnamed individuals. [@5] Free legal aid was assigned to solicitor Barry Fitzgerald. [@5]
