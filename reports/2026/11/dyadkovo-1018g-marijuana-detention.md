Police detained a 39-year-old resident of the village of Dyadkovo who had made
marijuana at home. [@1] Officers seized 13 packs of marijuana with a total
weight of 1,018 grams from his one-room flat. [@2] The marijuana had been
vacuum-sealed in polyethylene bags with a vacuum sealer. [@2]

Officers also found a growbox and containers in the flat in which the man had
previously grown cannabis and made it into marijuana. [@2] At the moment of
detention the man was under the influence of drugs. [@1] Because his behaviour
showed signs of a mental disorder, police handed him over to medical workers.
[@1] He is now undergoing examination in a psychiatric hospital. [@1]

An investigator opened a criminal case under part 2 of Article 228 for illegal
acquisition, manufacture and storage of drugs on a large scale. [@2] The offence
carries up to 10 years' imprisonment. [@3]
