Civil Police officers arrested a 19-year-old man in flagrante for drug
trafficking on the morning of Wednesday 4 November in Arraial d'Ajuda, a
district of Porto Seguro in Bahia's far south. [@1] The arrest was made by the
2nd Territorial Police Station of Arraial d'Ajuda, with Military Police support,
at a kitnet on Rua Estrela Cadente, which the FIRO31 account places in the
Arraial d'Ajuda neighbourhood. [@2] Police said the suspect was being monitored
over suspected drug selling at Praça do Hippie and deliveries made by
motorcycle. [@2]

Searching the flat, officers found 82 cocaine pins and 32 portions of a
substance similar to marijuana. [@1] They also seized one portion that appeared
to be hashish, a shock weapon described by Furo31 as a taser, a precision scale,
a mobile phone, R$ 126.55 in cash and the motorcycle police said was used for
the deliveries. [@2] The seized motorbike was a red Honda CG 160 Start. [@2]

Civil Police said the young man is investigated for the homicide of a
20-year-old woman, Cassiele Costa de Jesus, killed with knife blows in 2025 with
her body found near Vale Verde in Arraial d'Ajuda. [@3] He is also investigated
over the August 2024 kidnapping and attempted murder of a woman and her baby
daughter near Trancoso, when military police found the victim being dragged and
beaten by two armed men and the child abandoned in a public street. [@3] The
suspect was taken to the 2nd Territorial Police Station with the seized material
and will answer for drug trafficking under article 33 of Brazil's drug law, Law
11,343 of 2006, which carries up to 15 years in prison plus a fine. [@2] The
homicide investigation continues separately. [@1]
