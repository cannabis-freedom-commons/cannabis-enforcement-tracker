Military police raiding a building on Rua Ribeirópolis in Itabuna's Sarinha
Alcântara neighbourhood found 215 grams of pressed marijuana and 80 grams of
Gold marijuana. [@1] Officers also seized four pieces of hashish, alongside 96
cocaine vials, 100 grams of cocaine and 197 crack rocks. [@1] The cannabis in
the haul therefore weighed 295 grams by the police account, separate from the
cocaine and crack. [@2]

Teams from the 15th Military Police Battalion went to the street beside a canal
after anonymous reports of drug dealing in the area. [@1] The suspects saw the
patrol cars approaching, ran into a building and escaped. [@1] No one was
arrested in the operation. [@1]

Officers also took a remote-control drone, a precision scale, a notebook of
trafficking records, two mobile phones, jewellery, cash and packaging material.
[@1] All the material was sent to the local police station, where the incident
was logged and the Civil Police were to continue work identifying and catching
those involved. [@2] iPolítica reported the raid was logged at about 3.30pm on
Tuesday 3 November, during a push against violent crime, and that officers found
nobody in the building searches. [@3]
