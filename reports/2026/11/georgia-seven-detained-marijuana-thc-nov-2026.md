Officers of the Interior Ministry's Central Criminal Police Department detained
seven people at different times in Tbilisi on charges of illegally acquiring and
storing drugs in an especially large amount and facilitating their sale. [@2]
Those detained included both Georgian citizens and foreign nationals. [@1]
Investigators said the suspects were involved in various drug-sale schemes, in
some cases acting as organised groups. [@1] The investigation is proceeding
under Articles 260 and 260-4 of Georgia's Criminal Code, covering illegal
acquisition and storage of drugs and facilitation of their sale. [@1]

Searches of the suspects' persons, homes and vehicles recovered drugs described
as especially large batches prepared for sale. [@2] The cannabis component of
the seizure was dried marijuana and tetrahydrocannabinol (THC). [@2] Officers
also seized 352 wraps of alpha-PVP alongside mephedrone, methadone, clephedrone
and MDMA, which are not counted as cannabis. [@1] Police also seized materials
intended for packaging drugs. [@1] The Interior Ministry said the charges carry
up to 20 years in prison or life imprisonment. [@1]
