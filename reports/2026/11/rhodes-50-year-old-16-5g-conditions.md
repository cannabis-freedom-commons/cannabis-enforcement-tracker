On 29 October 2026, shortly before 9 p.m., officers of the Rhodes Crime
Prosecution and Detection Subdivision stopped a 50-year-old man in Rhodes and
arrested him after a check. [@1] Prosecutors brought a felony charge of drug
trafficking against the 50-year-old and referred him to an investigating judge.
[@1] Before the Rhodes investigating judge, the man denied the charge as
attributed to him. [@1] With the agreement of the Rhodes first-instance
prosecutor, he was then released with restrictive conditions. [@1] The
conditions ban him from leaving the country and require him to appear once
during the first five days of each month at his local police station. [@1]

Police seized all the items found, 16.5 grams of cannabis in total, and filed a
case against him that was referred to the competent authority. [@1]
