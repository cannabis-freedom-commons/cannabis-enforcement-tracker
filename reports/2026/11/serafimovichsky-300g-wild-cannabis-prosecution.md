Police investigators in Volgograd Oblast sent the criminal case against a
61-year-old resident of Serafimovichsky district to court over the unlawful
storage of cannabis. [@1] He is charged under part 2 of article 228 of the
Russian Criminal Code, which covers acquiring and storing narcotic-containing
plant parts in a large amount without intent to sell. [@1] The charge carries up
to 10 years in prison. [@1]

In July 2026 the man picked wild cannabis on a wasteland to prepare a drug for
his own use. [@1] Police officers detained him on 29 July 2026. [@1] A search of
an outbuilding found a plastic jar in which parts of cannabis weighing about 300
grams were drying. [@1] The detained man admitted what he had done. [@1]

After gathering the evidence, the investigator sent the case to the
Serafimovichsky district court in Volgograd Oblast for trial. [@1] A second
outlet confirms that he had dried the plant and hidden it in the jar in the
outbuilding. [@2] No verdict has been reported, and the court has yet to decide
on his guilt and punishment. [@2]
