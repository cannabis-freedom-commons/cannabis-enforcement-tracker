Carabinieri from the Conselve station arrested a 23-year-old Moroccan man
without a fixed address on suspicion of possessing drugs for dealing, after
residents near the town's public swimming pools reported his movements. [@1]
Residents said the young man arrived on an electric bicycle for several days,
stopped for a few minutes in the car park while talking on the phone and then
left quickly, and police suspected an itinerant dealing spot. [@1] On the
morning of 4 November the officers watched the area, picked up the suspect and
followed him to a flat in Via Talpe in Tribano, a town neighbouring Conselve.
[@2]

Inside the flat police identified two other Moroccan nationals, both legally
resident in Italy, and searched the 23-year-old's bedroom. [@1] A precision
scale was found in a bedside-table drawer, and a block of hashish weighing 504
grams was hidden among clothes in a suitcase. [@1] The hashish and the scale
were seized, and on the order of the Padova public prosecutor's office the man
was taken to the Due Palazzi prison in Padua. [@1]

The operation was run by the Conselve station under the Abano Terme Carabinieri
company and coordinated by lieutenant Marco Cestaro, PadovaOggi reported. [@2]
TgPadova reported that the prosecutor ordered the man held at the Due Palazzi
prison while the judicial authority decides the case. [@3]
