Carabinieri in Fano went to the home of a 20-year-old man living in the city to
look for a pistol. [@1] The search followed a report from a relative of the
young man, who said he had threatened her and suggested he had a firearm. [@1]
The only weapon in the flat was a replica with the classic red cap on its
muzzle, a toy blank-firing pistol. [@1]

A closer search of the bedroom found nine wraps containing hashish, 34 grams in
total, and a precision scale. [@1] The 20-year-old was arrested for possession
of drugs for the purpose of dealing. [@1] A court validated the arrest, while
his lawyer asked for time to consider an alternative procedure or community
service. [@1] The young man was ordered to sign in with the police three times a
week while the case continues. [@1]
