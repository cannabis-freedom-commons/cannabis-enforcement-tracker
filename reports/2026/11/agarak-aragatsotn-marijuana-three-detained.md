Aragatsotn regional criminal police received operational information that a
51-year-old resident of Agarak village used and sold drugs. [@1] Officers
visited the 51-year-old man's home to check the tip, where in plain view they
noticed a marijuana-like mass and a pipette. [@1] Two other residents of the
same village, men aged 39 and 54, were in the house. [@1] All three were
detained and taken to the regional department. [@2] Police established that the
51-year-old man had sold the drugs to the other two men. [@2]

A personal search of the 39-year-old man found 5.41 grams of marijuana and a
pipette. [@2] A personal search of the 54-year-old man found 269 grams of
marijuana and three pipettes, two of them containing drug mass. [@1] A criminal
proceeding was opened over the episode. [@1] A search of the 54-year-old man's
land found cannabis plants, while a basement search found 604.18 grams of
marijuana-like mass. [@2] A search of the 51-year-old suspect's home found 3.66
grams of marijuana-like mass. [@1]

The 51-year-old man detained on suspicion of drug sale was remanded in custody.
[@2] The preliminary investigation was still under way, with no further court
outcome reported. [@1]
