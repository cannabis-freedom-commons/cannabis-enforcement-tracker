A man was sentenced to 96 months, or eight years, in prison in Panama on 11
November 2026 for aggravated drug possession. [@2] The sentence followed a plea
agreement that prosecutors said had been validated. [@1] He was also ordered to
pay a fine of USD 100 as an additional penalty attached to the prison term. [@1]

The case began on 3 November 2026 with a search in the barriada Arboleda, a
neighbourhood in La Chorrera district, Panamá Oeste province. [@2] The search
seized 83.20 grams of marijuana. [@1] Officers also seized 469 packages of
suspected cocaine. [@2] The operation was a diligencia de allanamiento y
registro, Panama's term for a search-and-registration procedure. [@2]

The sentence, numbered 321, was dated 11 November 2026. [@2] The case was
handled by the Specialised Prosecutor's Office for Drug-Related Offences of
Panamá Oeste, which depends on the national Public Prosecutor's Office. [@1] The
conviction was for posesión agravada de drogas, or aggravated drug possession.
[@2]
