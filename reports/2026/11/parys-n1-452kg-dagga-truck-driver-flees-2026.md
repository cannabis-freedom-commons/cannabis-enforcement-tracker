Free State police are searching for a truck driver who jumped out of his moving
vehicle and fled on foot near Parys police station after officers stopped his
truck on the N1 south, allegedly carrying dagga from Cape Town to Johannesburg.
[@1] The Trucking and Narcotics Team stopped the truck at about noon on Saturday
7 November 2026 and escorted it towards Parys SAPS so that it could be searched
in a safe place. [@2] About 100 metres from the police station the driver jumped
out of the moving truck and ran away, and police found no one in the truck when
they searched it. [@2] Police had monitored the truck over several weeks and
opened an enquiry docket before the interception. [@2]

In the truck officers found 452 bales of dagga, each weighing about one
kilogram, and seven packs of Mandrax, a methaqualone sedative, each also about
one kilogram. [@1] Police put the street value of the whole load, dagga and
Mandrax together, at R1.5 million. [@1] The truck's owner confirmed owning the
vehicle but denied knowing about or having any link to the drugs inside it. [@2]
Police seized the drugs and impounded the truck, and no one had been arrested
for the load while the search for the driver continued. [@2] Lieutenant Colonel
Kelello Patsa, a Free State police spokesperson, said an enquiry had been opened
and investigations were under way. [@2]
