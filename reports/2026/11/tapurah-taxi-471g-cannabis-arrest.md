Military Police in Tapurah, Mato Grosso, arrested a 25-year-old man on suspicion
of drug trafficking on the night of Sunday 8 November 2026, in the rural zone
near the road to the municipal landfill. [@2] The patrol had been called by an
anonymous tip about a motorcyclist stopped at the roadside, apparently searching
for something in the vegetation with his phone flashlight. [@1] Officers
searched the area but did not find the motorcycle, and on their way back came
across a taxi with the suspect in the passenger seat. [@1] Seeing the patrol car
approach, the man tried to hide inside the vehicle. [@2]

A search of the passenger found eight portions of marijuana weighing about 68
grams, a precision scale and a mobile phone. [@1] A black bag on the floor by
the passenger seat held two larger marijuana portions totalling about 403 grams.
[@1] The driver said the bag belonged to the passenger and that he had been
hired for a ride, receiving two different locations by phone. [@2]

At the first address the officers found about 26 grams of cocaine base paste,
and at the second no illicit material. [@1] In total, the Military Police
reported seizing about 471 grams of marijuana and 26 grams of cocaine base
paste, with the phone and scale. [@2] The suspect and the seized material were
taken to the Civil Police station for the appropriate procedures. [@1]
