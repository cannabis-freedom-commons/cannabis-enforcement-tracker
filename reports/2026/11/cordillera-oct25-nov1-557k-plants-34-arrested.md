The Police Regional Office-Cordillera Administrative Region (PRO-CAR), the
Cordillera regional police, arrested six suspected drug personalities from 25
October to 1 November, including one high-value target in Apayao and five
street-level offenders. [@1] Officers arrested 28 wanted individuals in the same
week, including six top-ranked fugitives. [@1] Together the drug and manhunt
arrests brought the week's total reported arrests to 34, including several
high-profile fugitives. [@1] Among those captured were a provincial-level drug
suspect and municipal-level suspects wanted for statutory rape, domestic
violence, child abuse, and attempted murder. [@1] The Kalinga haul led to the
arrest of one suspect. [@1] Benguet police led the manhunt with 14 arrests,
followed closely by Baguio City police with 13. [@1]

The bulk of the seizures came from 14 targeted operations which netted 548,130
fully grown marijuana plants, 9,000 seedlings and 1,060 marijuana leaves and
fruiting tops, plus 7.47 grams of suspected methamphetamine locally known as
shabu. [@1] PRO-CAR put the week's total haul at more than P110 million worth of
illegal drugs. [@1] The Kalinga Police Provincial Office accounted for the
largest share at P108.8 million worth of illicit drugs. [@1] Benguet police
eradicated P1.18 million worth of marijuana plants, while units in Baguio City,
Ifugao, and Apayao seized the remainder of the contraband. [@1]

PRO-CAR regional director Brig. Gen. Noel Vallo said the accomplishment was part
of continued collaboration with local governments and communities to maintain
public safety across the region. [@1] Police attributed the absence of reported
crime in 54 municipalities to heightened visibility and community coordination,
including 25 in Abra, nine in Mountain Province, eight in Ifugao, seven in
Benguet, four in Apayao, and one in Kalinga. [@1] Regional police gave crime
clearance and solution rates of 85.14% and 74.32% for the period. [@1] Shabu is
methamphetamine, not cannabis, and its 7.47 grams are excluded from the cannabis
figures, which count only the marijuana plants and seedlings. [@1]
