Anantnag police arrested four alleged drug peddlers in four separate operations
in Anantnag district on 5 November 2026 and said the recoveries totalled about
11.86 kg. [@2,3] A Mattan police team searched the home of Zakir Hussain Khan,
son of late Muzaffar Khan, in Satrimaidan, Gopalpora, after obtaining
authorisation under Section 41(2) of the NDPS Act, and recovered about 11 kg of
ground charas powder. [@1,2,3] Police registered FIR No. 128/2026 under
sections 8/20 of the NDPS Act. [@2,3]

Personnel from Police Post Sangam set up a checkpoint at Semthan and recovered
about 500 grams of ganja from Fayaz Ahmad Sheikh, son of Mohd Maqbool Sheikh of
Semthan. [@1,2,3] A police team from Police Post Aishmuqam apprehended Shabir
Ahmad Shah, son of Abdul Rashid Shah of Halwani, Panchalpora, during a checkpoint
at Rangkadal and recovered about 10 grams of charas. [@1,2,3] Police
registered FIR No. 104/2026 under sections 8/20 of the NDPS Act in that case.
[@2,3] Personnel from Anantnag Police Station apprehended Bilal Ahmad Sheikh,
son of Abdul Ahad of Zadipora, Anantnag, at Nandpora and recovered about 350
grams of charas. [@1,2,3]

Police said the four recoveries together comprised about 11 kg of ground charas
powder, 500 grams of ganja and 360 grams of charas. [@1,2,3] Police put the
total at about 11.86 kg. [@2,3] Cases had been registered and further investigations had been
initiated. [@1,2,3] Police urged the public to share credible information
about drug-related activities. [@1,3]
