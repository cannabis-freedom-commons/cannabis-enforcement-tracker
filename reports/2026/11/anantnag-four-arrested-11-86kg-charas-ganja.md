Police arrested four alleged drug peddlers in four separate operations in
Anantnag district on Thursday. [@1] A Mattan police team searched the home of
Zakir Hussain Khan, son of late Muzaffar Khan, in Satrimaidan, Gopalpora, after
obtaining authorisation under Section 41(2) of the NDPS Act, and recovered about
11 kg of ground charas powder. [@1] Personnel from Police Post Sangam
intercepted Fayaz Ahmad Sheikh, son of Mohd Maqbool Sheikh of Semthan, at a
checkpoint in Semthan and recovered about 500 grams of ganja from him. [@1] A
police team from Police Post Aishmuqam apprehended Shabir Ahmad Shah, son of
Abdul Rashid Shah of Halwani, Panchalpora, at Rangkadal and recovered about 10
grams of charas from him. [@1] Personnel from Anantnag Police Station
apprehended Bilal Ahmad Sheikh, son of Abdul Ahad of Zadipora, Anantnag, at
Nandpora and recovered about 350 grams of charas from him. [@1]

Police said the four recoveries together comprised about 11 kg of ground charas
powder, 500 grams of ganja and 360 grams of charas, totalling about 11.86 kg.
[@1] Police said cases had been registered under the relevant provisions of law
at the respective police stations and further investigations had been initiated.
[@1] Police urged the public to share credible information about drug-related
activities to help curb the narcotics trade. [@1]
