Guardia Civil officers arrested a 51-year-old man in sa Pobla, Mallorca, on
suspicion of drug trafficking. [@3] The Guardia Civil said officers found the
drugs while the man was driving through the municipality of sa Pobla on
Mallorca. [@2] The stop happened at about 9pm on 9 November 2026, when officers
were operating a checkpoint for people and vehicles. [@3]

Officers decided to stop the car after the driver's evasive behaviour aroused
their suspicions. [@1] After identifying the driver, officers searched the
inside of the vehicle. [@3] They found a piece of hashish resin in the car. [@2]
They also found a bag containing cocaine divided into numerous doses, which the
Guardia Civil said were prepared for sale. [@2]

The arrest was carried out by officers from the Guardia Civil post in sa Pobla.
[@3] The man was held on suspicion of an offence against public health, the
formulation used by officers for the alleged drug trafficking. [@3] Once
officers had prepared the necessary case papers, the man was handed over to the
competent judicial authority. [@3]
