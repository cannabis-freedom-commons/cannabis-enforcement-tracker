Police seized 110 kg of ganja from a car that broke down and was being towed
along National Highway 65 in Suryapet. [@1] When the car stopped, the people
with it arranged for a tow truck, described in the Telugu account as a Boeing
vehicle, to move it. [@1] Acting on advance information, police reached the spot
while the vehicle was being moved, and the people in the car abandoned it there
and fled when they saw the officers. [@1]

Officers searched the car and found 110 kg of ganja inside. [@1] Police seized
the ganja together with the car and registered a case. [@1] A search was under
way for the suspects who fled. [@1] The Telugu account describes the case as a
plan to smuggle ganja that fell apart when the car broke down on the highway.
