Officers of the Criminal Investigation Division (DIC) of Curuzú Cuatiá carried
out two search raids in the Primera Sección Chacra Sur area of the town under
orders from prosecutor Oscar Cañete's office. [@2] The raids formed part of an
investigation into small-scale drug dealing run with the Public Prosecution
Ministry. [@2] Radio Sudamericana reported that the Operational Tasks Group
(GTO) of Regional Unit III took part in the raids. [@2]

At the first property officers seized a long firearm, a precision digital scale
and a 110 cc motorcycle that police said was used to divide and distribute
drugs. [@1] At the second property, a remote storage place, officers found 4.688
kilograms of plant material that tested positive for marijuana. [@1]

A 40-year-old man accused of storing and selling the drug was detained there and
taken to the holding cell of the First Police Station of Curuzú Cuatiá. [@1] He
was formally notified of a case under Argentina's drug law, Law 23.737. [@1]

The Corrientes police said the raids continued an investigation behind the 30
October raid in the town, in which cocaine, cannabis, cash and vehicles were
seized and two people were held. [@1]
