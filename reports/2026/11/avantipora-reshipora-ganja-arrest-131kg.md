Avantipora police arrested Imtiyaz Ahmad Naiku in the Reshipora area of Pulwama
district on a charge of drug trafficking, seizing a bag he was carrying. [@1,2]
The arrest was made by a team from the Reshipora police chowki, a local police
post, acting under the leadership of the chowki in-charge in the Ghat Tokina
area. [@1,2] Police identified the arrested man as a resident of Reshi Mohalla
near the mosque at Tral Payeen. [@1] Officers searched the black-coloured bag
found in his possession during the operation. [@1]

From the bag, police recovered 1.20 kilograms of semi-dried ganja as the largest
of three separately weighed cannabis quantities. [@1,2] Police also recovered
109.97 grams of dark-brown pressed ganja wrapped in maize husk and 4.96 grams of
round-shaped ganja from the same bag. [@1] Together, the three stated quantities
amount to about 1.315 kilograms of seized cannabis, which police described as a
heavy quantity of narcotic substance found in his possession. [@1]

Following the recovery, police registered a First Information Report under the
NDPS Act at Avantipora police station. [@1,3]
