Municipal Guardia Civil officers detained four men during preventive patrols in
different colonias of Soledad, San Luis Potosí. [@1] Officers arrested Filiberto
N and Jorge N, both aged 41, and Ricardo N, aged 26, for disturbing public order
and possession of presumed methamphetamine. [@1] In a separate case, officers
detained Flavio N, aged 28, in colonia Nueva Foresta with bags containing
alleged marijuana. [@1]

San Luis Hoy reports the same roundup as interventions in Genovevo Rivas Guillén
Sur, San Francisco and Villa de los Cactus that detained the same three men for
disorder and possession of doses of presumed methamphetamine. [@2] San Luis Hoy
likewise reports that Flavio N, aged 28, was detained in colonia Nueva Foresta
with bags of alleged marijuana. [@2]

All those detained were taken to the municipal command for medical certification
and then placed at the disposal of the state prosecutor. [@2] The Soledad
municipal Guardia Civil restated its commitment to maintain an active presence
in the streets and avenues of the municipality to prevent crime and safeguard
residents. [@1]
