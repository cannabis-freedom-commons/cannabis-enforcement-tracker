A long-time cannabis user who became a small-scale dealer assured a judge in the
Timaru District Court that she was never going to offend again. [@1] The
defendant, a 58-year-old woman from Pleasant Point, near Timaru, was told by
Judge Maurice Casey that the evidence told him she was not just a casual
cannabis user. [@1] Judge Casey said there was an aspect of growing and that it
was not just for her own use. [@1]

Police found 500g of dried cannabis bud when they searched the Pleasant Point
property where the 58-year-old woman lived. [@1]

Two other charges, supplying and procuring meth, were alleged to have occurred
in October and November 2025. [@2]
