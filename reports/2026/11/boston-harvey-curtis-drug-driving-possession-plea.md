Mablethorpe resident Harvey Curtis appeared at Boston Magistrates’ Court on
November 4, 2026. [@1] He entered not guilty pleas to the charges. [@1] He is 57
years old. [@1] He lives on Wellington Road in Mablethorpe. [@1]

He is accused of driving a Mercedes Sprinter van with 3.9 micrograms of
delta-9-tetrahydrocannabinol per litre of blood, above the 2-microgram legal
limit. [@1] He is also accused of possessing a quantity of cannabis, a Class B
drug. [@1] Both alleged offences took place in Boston on April 23. [@1]

He will face trial at the Boston court after a case management hearing on
February 17. [@1]
