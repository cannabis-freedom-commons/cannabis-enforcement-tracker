Nine people were detained in an operation against alleged drug trafficking
carried out in Antonio Ante, in Imbabura province. [@1] The arrests were made
with judicial warrants, after an investigation into possible distribution of
substances subject to control. [@1] The operation was carried out by the
Anti-Drug Investigation Directorate of the Imbabura subzone, under what the
police call the 3D Operational Strategy. [@2]

Officers found 332.59 grams of cocaine and 393.20 grams of marijuana in the
procedure, police reported. [@1] Police also reported the seizure of nine mobile
phones, a vehicle, a scale and a blender glass. [@1] The police report listed
USD 151 in cash and one retained vehicle among the items taken. [@2]

Police said one of those detained, a 49-year-old man, was classified as an
Individuo de Interés Penal Relevante, a priority target in their investigations,
with drug-trafficking records from 2020 and 2022. [@1] The police said the IIPR
classification and the prior records do not establish responsibility in the
current case. [@1]

The interventions took place in different sectors of Antonio Ante, without exact
locations or a number of premises given, and it was not stated whether the nine
formed one group. [@1] The detained people and the evidence were placed at the
disposal of the competent authorities to continue the legal process. [@1]
