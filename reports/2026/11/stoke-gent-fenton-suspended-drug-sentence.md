27-year-old Jonathan Gent, of Tunstall, Stoke-on-Trent, was given a
two-year-seven-month prison sentence suspended for three years at Stoke-on-Trent
Crown Court on 5 November 2026. [@1,2] Recorder Samuel Skinner suspended the sentence and warned Gent it would be activated if he committed any more offences in the next three years. [@3] Recorder Samuel Skinner also ordered him to complete 100 hours unpaid work. [@3] He had pleaded guilty at an earlier hearing
to being concerned in the supply of heroin and of crack cocaine and to
possessing monkey dust and cannabis with intent to supply. [@1,2] Muldoon said Gent had not smoked cannabis for two weeks and hoped to start a gardening business. [@3] Stuart Muldoon, mitigating, said Gent had a difficult childhood, was thrown out at 16 and lost a child to a heart condition in 2024. [@3] Monkey dust is a
street name for the synthetic drug MDPHP, and the sentence covered four separate
offences. [@1]

In June 2023 police forced their way into a home in Fenton after information
linked it to drug dealing in the local community. [@1] The raid and arrest took place at an address in Victoria Place, Fenton, on 20 June 2023. [@3] Gent tried to leave
through the back door as officers entered but was caught before he could escape.
[@1] Gent armed himself with a motorcycle helmet, swung it and hit a female officer in the face, leaving her bruised, before officers subdued him with pepper spray. [@3] A search of Gent and the house found deals of monkey dust and cannabis.
[@1] Gent told police he had been smoking a lot of cannabis and began dealing to fund the habit. [@3] Cannabis, crack cocaine, monkey dust and diamorphine cut with paracetamol were recovered, and his fingerprints were found on the deals. [@3] Officers seized 1.3 grams of monkey dust, £130 cash and an iPhone from him, then found more snap bags in black plastic tubs inside the house. [@3] His phone showed he was advertising the drugs for sale along with heroin
and crack cocaine. [@1,3] The seized iPhone also held pictures of Gent and his email address among other personal information. [@3] He was taken into custody for questioning and later
charged with the offences. [@1]

Detective Sergeant Marc Proctor of the City Proactive Team said officers were
carrying out warrants against drug dealers across Stoke-on-Trent every day. [@1]
