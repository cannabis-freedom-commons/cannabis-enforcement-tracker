27-year-old Jonathan Gent, of Tunstall, Stoke-on-Trent, was given a
two-year-seven-month prison sentence suspended for three years at Stoke-on-Trent
Crown Court on 5 November 2026. [@1] He had pleaded guilty at an earlier hearing
to being concerned in the supply of heroin and of crack cocaine and to
possessing monkey dust and cannabis with intent to supply. [@1] Monkey dust is a
street name for the synthetic drug MDPHP, and the sentence covered four separate
offences. [@1]

In June 2023 police forced their way into a home in Fenton after information
linked it to drug dealing in the local community. [@1] Gent tried to leave
through the back door as officers entered but was caught before he could escape.
[@1] A search of Gent and the house found deals of monkey dust and cannabis.
[@1] His phone showed he was advertising the drugs for sale along with heroin
and crack cocaine. [@1] He was taken into custody for questioning and later
charged with the offences. [@1]

Detective Sergeant Marc Proctor of the City Proactive Team said officers were
carrying out warrants against drug dealers across Stoke-on-Trent every day. [@1]
