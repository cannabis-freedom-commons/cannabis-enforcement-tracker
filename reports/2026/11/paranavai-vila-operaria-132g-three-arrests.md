Military Police officers on patrol in Vila Operária, in Paranavai, stopped a
scooter carrying two 22-year-old men and a 23-year-old woman. [@1] The routine
patrol stop ended with three people arrested and drugs plus materials that
police said may be linked to preparing and packaging drugs seized. [@1] The
three were arrested and taken with the seized items to the Civil Police station,
where they were presented to the police authority for the applicable legal
procedures. [@1] Police did not release the names of those involved. [@1]

Officers found 132 grams of maconha during the search. [@1] They also seized 50
zip-lock plastic bags, a scale and a knife. [@1] Police said the presence of
those packaging-related materials prompted the referral to the Civil Police
station. [@1] The stop took place at about 18:35 on Tuesday 10 November 2026 in
Vila Operária, Paranavai. [@1]

Any criminal liability will depend on investigation and analysis of the facts by
the competent authorities, and no charge or court outcome was reported. [@1]
