Bielsk Podlaski patrol officers detained a 27-year-old resident of Orla commune
after finding drugs in his car at the town marketplace late in the evening. [@1]
He was taken to a police cell after officers also found a jeweller's scale in
the car and a machete in its boot. [@1] A prosecutor has charged him with
possessing a significant quantity of narcotic drugs, an offence punishable by up
to 10 years in prison. [@2]

Officers said they noticed the parked car on the targowica, the marketplace,
because the man had been sitting inside it for a long time. [@1] When he got out
on their order, a pipe containing white powder fell out. [@2] A search found a
bag with a white substance in his trouser pocket, later identified as
mephedrone. [@1] In a backpack on the car's back seat, officers secured over 63
grams of plant material that a preliminary narcotest indicated was marijuana.
[@1]

Polish drug law punishes possession of a significant quantity with up to 10
years' imprisonment. [@2] Investigators are now examining where the drugs came
from, whether they were intended for sale, and why the man was carrying the
machete. [@2]
