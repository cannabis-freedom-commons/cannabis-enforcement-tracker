Brigada Militar officers arrested a couple for drug trafficking on the afternoon
of Friday 6 November 2026 on Avenida Dr. Mário Lopes in the Centenário
neighbourhood of Caxias do Sul, in Brazil's Rio Grande do Sul state. [@1] The
arrest came during a check of the couple's vehicle by the 12th Military Police
Battalion (12º BPM), known as the Brigada Militar, the state's military police.
[@3]

In the car officers found 616 grams of marijuana divided into six portions. [@1]
The same search found 49 grams of cocaine in 56 portions and 32 grams of crack
in 34 portions, which are outside this tracker's cannabis figures. [@1] Officers
also seized R$ 242 in cash, one cannabis plant, zip-lock bags, a precision scale
and the couple's vehicle. [@1]

The couple was taken to the police station to register the incident, with no
charges or court step reported yet. [@1] Leouve reported cocaine and crack
weights slightly higher, at 49.5 and 32.5 grams. [@2] The accounts came from the
Brigada Militar's social communication office for the 12º BPM, whose handout
photo the outlets published. [@2]
