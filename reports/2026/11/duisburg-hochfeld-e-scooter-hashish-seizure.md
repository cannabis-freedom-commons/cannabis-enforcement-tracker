A motorcycle police officer spotted a 17-year-old riding an e-scooter through a
red light on Wanheimer Straße in Hochfeld on the evening of 2 November 2026 at
about 19:00. [@1] The motorcycle officer immediately took up the pursuit and
stopped the young man after the attempted flight. [@2] Additional patrol
officers alerted to the check brought the youth to the police station for
further measures. [@1] After the police measures were completed, officers handed
him over to his mother. [@2] Following his handover to his mother, he must now
face criminal proceedings over the incident. [@1]

During the subsequent check, police found hashish, a prohibited one-hand knife
and cash in denominations typical of the drug scene in the 17-year-old's
shoulder bag. [@1] He also carried dealer utensils, including a precision scale
and two crushers. [@1] Officers seized all the items found in the bag as well as
the youth's mobile phone at the station. [@2]

The stop took place on Wanheimer Straße in Hochfeld, a district of Duisburg.
[@1] The motorcycle officer had directly observed the red-light violation that
prompted the pursuit. [@1]
