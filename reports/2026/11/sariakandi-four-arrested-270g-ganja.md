Police in Sariakandi, Bogura arrested four alleged drug traders. [@1] Officers
seized 270 grams of ganja, the Bangla term for cannabis, from the four, along
with 12 tapentadol tablets. [@1] The Sarikandi police station operation took
place on the night of Wednesday 4 November 2026, and the four were sent to a
Bogura district court on Thursday 5 November. [@1]

Officers conducted anti-drug operations in several areas of the upazila, an
administrative subdivision. [@1]

Asaduzzaman said operations of this kind would continue to keep the upazila free
of drugs. [@1]
