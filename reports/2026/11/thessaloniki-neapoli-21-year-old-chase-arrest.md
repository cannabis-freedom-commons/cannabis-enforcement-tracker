Officers arrested a 21-year-old Greek man in Neapoli in the early hours of 10
November 2026 after a chase that began in Ampelokipoi. [@1] Officers spotted him
travelling with an accomplice in a car in Ampelokipoi and he sped off when they
signalled him to stop. [@1] During the pursuit he made dangerous manoeuvres and
damaged the Oraiokastro toll barrier. [@1] The car was stopped in Neapoli after
hitting a police vehicle and two parked vehicles. [@1]

Officers found a small quantity of cannabis and cocaine in his possession. [@1]
Voria likewise reports that the 21-year-old arrested at dawn on Tuesday 10
November in Neapoli had a small quantity of cannabis and cocaine. [@2] Police
found the car had been stolen in Ampelokipoi the previous month and carried
visibly forged plates. [@1]

The Ampelokipoi-Menemeni crime-detection department opened a case file against
him for theft, forgery, dangerous driving, damage to another person's property,
disobedience and violation of the drugs law. [@1] He was to be taken to the
competent prosecutor. [@1] Law & Order confirms the 21-year-old was to be taken
to the competent prosecutor. [@3]
