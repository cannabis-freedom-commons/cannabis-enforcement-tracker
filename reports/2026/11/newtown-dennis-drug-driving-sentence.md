Emily Louise Dennis, 33, of Falcon Court in Newtown admitted two drug-driving
offences after being stopped by police in her own street. [@1] Officers at
Welshpool Magistrates' Court said they spotted Dennis driving a Ford Fiesta in
Falcon Court in Maesyrhandir on January 14 after noticing a defective headlight.
[@1] Checks showed the vehicle was untaxed and had no valid MOT certificate.
[@1] A roadside alcohol test was negative, but Dennis tested positive for
cannabis and cocaine. [@1] Later blood tests showed 240 micrograms of
benzoylecgonine against a 50-microgram limit and 9.1 micrograms of THC, the
active ingredient in cannabis, against a 2-microgram limit. [@1]

Dennis also admitted using a vehicle without a valid MOT and two offences of
failing to attend court while on bail. [@1] The court heard she had failed to
appear at Welshpool Magistrates' Court after release on bail, leading to a
warrant. [@1] Defence solicitor Owain Jones described Dennis as a very
vulnerable person with ongoing health issues, memory problems and serious spinal
conditions. [@1] Jones said she had not tried to avoid the court process and had
attended the latest hearing voluntarily after a friend reminded her. [@1] Jones
said she had succumbed to peer pressure to drive on that occasion. [@1]

Chairman of the bench Jeremy Banks said the offences were serious enough to
warrant a community order. [@1] Dennis was given a 12-month community order with
a probation requirement. [@1] She was disqualified from driving for three years
and ordered to pay £319 in fines, costs and surcharges to be deducted from her
benefits. [@1]
