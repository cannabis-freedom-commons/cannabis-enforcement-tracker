Carabinieri of the Cetraro Marina station arrested a 30-year-old local man on 4
November 2026 on suspicion of possessing drugs for sale. [@1] The man was
already subject to special police supervision with an obligation to reside in
the area. [@1] Officers from the Paola Carabinieri company carried out a search
of the man's own home as part of their drug-enforcement checks on the Cosenza
Tyrrhenian coast. [@1]

When the officers entered, he threw several packets out of a window to get rid
of the drugs, and the Carabinieri recovered them and blocked him. [@1] The
search turned up 86 grams of marijuana and 2 grams of hashish, alongside 28
grams of cocaine, a precision scale and material for packing individual doses.
[@1] Calabria Inchieste and CosenzaChannel report the same seizure as 116 grams
in total, made up of 86 grams of marijuana, 28 grams of cocaine and 2 grams of
hashish. [@2] Calabria Inchieste reported that the officers also recovered the
packets thrown outside the flat, preventing the drug from being dispersed. [@2]

The man was first taken to his own home and later tried on the fast-track direct
procedure before the court. [@1] At the validation hearing the judge ordered
that he be held in pre-trial detention in prison. [@1] CosenzaChannel reported
that the man appeared before the court of Paola on the fast-track procedure.
[@3] Radio Azzurra said the operation by the Paola Carabinieri company was aimed
at drug dealing on the Cosenza Tyrrhenian coast. [@4]
