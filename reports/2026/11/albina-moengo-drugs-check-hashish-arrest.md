A police operation against drug dealing in eastern Suriname led to warning shots
in Albina and the arrest of a suspect in Moengo on Wednesday 4 November 2026.
[@1] A mixed police unit acted on continuing complaints and information from the
public about drug sales in public places in the district. [@1]

In Albina officers approached two men at a sales stand along the waterfront,
near a spot known as 'Beeld', on information that they sold drugs there. [@1]
One of the men became agitated and the other ran away. [@1] Police fired warning
shots, but the fleeing suspect ran on into a thicket. [@1] The man who stayed at
the stand was taken to the Albina police station; no drugs were found on him,
but police found packaging material at the stand. [@1]

In Moengo police arrested a man known by the initials P.R. at the Moengo Mall, a
spot police say is known as a drug sales point. [@1] P.R. had an unspecified
quantity of hashish with him and said it was for his own use. [@1] Police said
the man had earlier been arrested at the same location for violating the
narcotics law. [@1] P.R. was described as an old acquaintance of the police,
arrested in front of the Moengo Mall. [@2]

P.R. was handed to the Moengo police and brought before an assistant public
prosecutor, and after consultation with the Surinamese Public Prosecution
Service he was held in verzekering, the country's pre-trial custody during an
investigation. [@1]
