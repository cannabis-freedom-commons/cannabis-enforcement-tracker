The judicial police unit of the Mount Lebanon regional gendarmerie, part of
Lebanon's Internal Security Forces, arrested five people in four separate
operations in different parts of Beirut and its southern suburbs. [@2]

The first operation, acting on information about a store of stolen motorcycles
in Sabra, found six motorcycles and arrested a man born in 2004 who said he was
Syrian. [@2] In the same Sabra store police seized 23 bags of marijuana and 24
bags of hashish, along with about 400 narcotic pills, other varied pills, mobile
phones and sums of money; the ministry gave no weight for the bags. [@2]

On 7 November a patrol caught two Lebanese men, born in 1998 and 2003, stealing
a motorcycle in Tohwitat al-Ghadir and seized two motorcycles and a quantity of
hashish from them. [@2] On 6 November police arrested a Syrian man born in 2000
in Roueiss on suspicion of dealing drugs, seizing hashish, pills and materials
suspected to be narcotics, packing tools, a motorcycle and a phone holding
videos of items offered for sale with their prices. [@2] A fourth operation in
Bir Hassan arrested a man born in 2004 held on an arrest warrant for motorcycle
theft, with a mobile phone seized from him. [@2]

The ministry said the detainees and the seized items were handed to the relevant
units for legal action on the instructions of the competent judiciary. [@1]
