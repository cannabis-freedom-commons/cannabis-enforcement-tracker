Dholpur police arrested T. Srinivas, a ganja smuggler with a reward of 25,000
rupees on his head, in the city of Nagpur in Maharashtra on 6 November. [@1] He
had escaped from the Diholi police station lock-up on the night of 25 October
2026 by breaking a window. [@1] He was in police custody in a case over 261 kg
110 g of ganja seized from a car on National Highway 44 near the Bagchauli Khar
turn on 17 October. [@3]

Station house officer Shaitan Singh said the Mania police station team and
traffic police had searched the car and found the ganja in 11 plastic sacks.
[@3] Police valued the consignment at about 1.30 crore rupees, and a second man
was arrested with him in that case. [@2] A court sent the other man to jail and
kept Srinivas in police custody for further questioning. [@3]

After the escape he walked about 12 km through fields, took a tempo to Agra
paying with money sent online by his wife, then travelled by general-coach train
through Varanasi to Visakhapatnam and Tirupati. [@1] At Tirupati he shaved his
head and moustache to change his appearance. [@1] Superintendent of Police Vikas
Sangwan set up a special team under station house officer Shaitan Singh, which
searched in Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra and Andhra Pradesh for about six days
before tracing him to Nagpur. [@1]

Police said he is a hardened drug smuggler with trafficking cases registered
against him in Rajasthan and several other states. [@1] He was being questioned
about people who helped him during the escape and about the ganja network, and
police said he would be produced in court after the investigation. [@1]
