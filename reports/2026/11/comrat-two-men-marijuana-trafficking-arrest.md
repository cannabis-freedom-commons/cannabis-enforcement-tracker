Law-enforcement officers dismantled a network specialising in cultivating,
processing and selling marijuana allegedly run by two men from Comrat. [@1] The
two suspects, aged 34 and 44, were detained and placed in preventive arrest for
30 days. [@1] Officers of the Anti-Drug Directorate and criminal investigators
documented the pair's activity over about a month together with PCCOCS
prosecutors, suspecting an illegal cultivation and distribution scheme. [@1]

Investigators allege the men had set roles: one identified and vetted potential
buyers and sold drugs in small amounts, while the other grew hemp plants in his
household yard and harvested, processed and dried them. [@1] The marijuana was
then allegedly sold wholesale to intermediaries who distributed it to final
consumers. [@1]

In searches supported by masked Fulger special-police officers, officers seized
packets of marijuana, dried and growing hemp plants, hashish, a precision scale,
mobile phones and zip-lock packaging. [@1] At one suspect's household officers
also found an IJ-model hunting weapon with 59 16-calibre cartridges held without
papers. [@1] Officers also seized sums of money in lei and euros alleged to come
from the criminal activity. [@1] If found guilty, the two face 15 to 17 years in
prison. [@1] The suspects remain presumed innocent until a final court judgment.
