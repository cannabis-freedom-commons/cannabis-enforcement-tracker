Béjaïa wilaya security, through the Research and Intervention Brigade of the
wilaya judicial police, dismantled an organised network of nine people,
including the main supplier, who was the subject of three arrest warrants. [@1]
According to a Béjaïa police statement, the operation followed field information
that two people were about to complete a sale of a considerable quantity of
psychotropic substances in the commune of Oued Ghir. [@1] Officers in plain
clothes were posted to watch the entrances and exits of the commune. [@1]

The two suspects were found with a third man carrying a backpack; the three
resisted violently, tried to flee through the scrub and threw the bag into a
watercourse beside the road. [@1] Police arrested the three, aged 38, 26 and 32,
and recovered the bag, which held 1,080 capsules of pregabalin 300 mg and 20,500
dinars described as proceeds of dealing. [@1]

Investigators identified the main supplier as a 38-year-old man living in
Barbacha, wanted under three arrest warrants for similar cases, and, with the
prosecution, obtained two warrants to search apartments he used. [@1] In the
Barbacha apartment police arrested four people, aged 34, 32, 27 and 28, and
seized a piece of Indian hemp, cannabis resin, one ecstasy tablet, a precision
electronic scale with traces of cocaine, and a notebook listing the network's
customers, quantities sold and sums paid. [@1] In a second apartment in the
coastal village of Saket, police stopped the supplier and a 52-year-old
accomplice as they were leaving with a backpack; despite violent resistance, the
bag held 3,870 pregabalin 300 mg tablets, 600 ecstasy tablets, 48 million
centimes described as proceeds, and three mobile phones. [@1]

Police put the operation's seizures at 4,950 pregabalin 300 mg capsules, 601
ecstasy tablets, pieces of drugs, a precision scale bearing cocaine traces, more
than 50 million centimes, three mobile phones and what the statement called
white weapons. [@1] A criminal file was opened against the nine, who were
presented to the public prosecutor at the Béjaïa court, and the prosecutor
referred the file to the investigating judge. [@1] The investigating judge
ordered seven of them held in pre-trial detention and placed two under judicial
supervision pending trial. [@1]
