Police near Kadayam in Tenkasi district arrested 32-year-old Pirothip Sardar, a
migrant brick-kiln worker from West Bengal, for allegedly keeping ganja for
sale. [@1] Sardar is the son of Kalu Sardar and comes from Murshidabad district
in West Bengal. [@1] He had been working at a brick kiln on the road leading
from Mathapuram to Pulavanur near Kadayam. [@1] He had gone to his home town and
had come back only a few days before police stopped him. [@1]

Police said they detained him on suspicion and questioned him. [@1] After
questioning, they said they found that he had stocked 2 kg of ganja for sale.
[@1] Officers registered a case against him and seized the 2 kg of ganja
together with his motorcycle. [@1] They then arrested him and remanded him to
judicial custody and lodged him in jail. [@1]

Police said they are continuing to investigate where he bought the ganja and who
else may be connected to it. [@1] Police linked both the seized ganja and the
motorcycle to the same case. [@1]
