contraband cigarettes, valued at more than 26 million pesos, were seized in
Jardín América. [@1] A wide police operation was deployed across Misiones
province from the night of Thursday 12 November 2026, involving thousands of
officers from the regional units and special groups. [@1] In Oberá, police
detained two men linked to cable theft, one of them under a federal warrant.
[@1]
