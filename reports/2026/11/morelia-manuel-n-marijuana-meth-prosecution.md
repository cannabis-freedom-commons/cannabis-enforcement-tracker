Officers detained one man in Morelia after he disturbed public order and seized
methamphetamine and marijuana from him. [@1] Police stopped Manuel “N” on 18
October after he disturbed order on a public street in Morelia. [@2] A routine
inspection of his belongings found packets of green herb and crystalline
material that expert tests later confirmed as marijuana and methamphetamine
respectively. [@2]

Manuel “N” was detained and handed to the Public Prosecutor’s street-dealing
investigation unit, which opened an investigation file to bring him before a
court. [@2] Michoacán prosecutors then obtained a binding-over order against
Manuel “N” for alleged street-level dealing in the form of simple possession of
methamphetamine in the state capital. [@3] At the initial hearing a control
judge found the detention lawful, admitted the prosecution evidence, bound him
over and imposed precautionary measures provided by law. [@2]

The state prosecution said it remains committed to investigating and pursuing
punishable acts against public health and safety. [@2]
