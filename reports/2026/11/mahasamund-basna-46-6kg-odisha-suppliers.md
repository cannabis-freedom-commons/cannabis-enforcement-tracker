Mahasamund police arrested two alleged main suppliers from Odisha, saying they
had reached the roots of an interstate ganja-trafficking network. [@1] Police
reached the pair by analysing toll Fastag payments, mobile tower locations and
call detail records. [@1] Investigators concluded that on 31 July 2025 the two
men, from Marding village in Odisha's Kalahandi district and aged 27 and 30,
supplied ganja to smugglers from Madhya Pradesh. [@1] Police arrested both of
them on 4 November 2026. [@1]

The case dates from 1 August 2025, when Basna police were posted in front of the
station and a car coming from Padampur in Odisha sped off after its occupants
saw the blockade. [@1] Police chased the car, and the accused abandoned it near
Khemra pond on Jagdishpur road and escaped in the dark. [@1] When officers
searched the car before witnesses they found 46.6 kilograms of ganja in three
plastic sacks on the back seat, and registered a case under the NDPS Act. [@1]
Three smugglers from Madhya Pradesh were arrested earlier in the same case, and
police said the ganja and the car used to carry it together account for property
worth Rs 18.20 lakh seized so far. [@1]

Police said the arrests reached the main accused who supplied ganja to the
trafficking network. [@1] Besides the car's ownership records, toll Fastag,
tower locations and call records, officers established that the Madhya Pradesh
smugglers were buying ganja in Odisha, and that they fled to Nagpur after
abandoning the car at Basna. [@1] Police then arrested three Madhya Pradesh men
in the case, named in the report as Ankit Pal, Akash Kushwaha and Pushparaj
Singh Tomar. [@1]
