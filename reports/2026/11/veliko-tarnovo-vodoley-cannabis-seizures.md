Veliko Tarnovo road and district police officers seized narcotic substances in
checks announced by the regional police directorate. [@1] During a lorry check
on 3 November 2026, officers found on a 54-year-old passenger from Strazhitsa a
small bag of leafy matter that tested positive for cannabis in a field test.
[@1,2] Officers detained the man for up to 24 hours and opened pre-trial
proceedings in his case. [@1]

The cannabis weighed about 0.5 grams. [@1,3] Officers also found two more small
bags under the lorry's front seat containing white and yellow powder that tested
positive for amphetamine in a field test. [@1] The powder weighed about four
grams in total. [@1]

At midday the same day officers checked a 27-year-old woman from the village of
Vodoley. [@1,2] Officers found and seized two bags of leafy matter that tested
positive for marijuana in a field test, along with white powder that tested
positive for cocaine. [@1,3] Officers opened a case file over the Vodoley find.
[@1,2]
