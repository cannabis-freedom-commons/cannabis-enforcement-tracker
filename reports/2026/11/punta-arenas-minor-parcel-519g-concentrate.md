A minor was detained in Punta Arenas after going to collect a parcel containing
more than 500 grams of cannabis concentrate, in a procedure carried out on 11
November in an investigation aimed at detecting drug entry into the Magallanes
region via transport companies. [@1] Around 09:00, an anti-drug team from Punta
Arenas, accompanied by the K-9 unit Haru, checked a freight company warehouse in
the El Ovejero area. [@1] With judicial authorization to open and inspect the
parcel, officers found 35 glass bottles containing a yellow liquid with
characteristics of cannabis concentrate. [@1] A field test confirmed the
presence of THC and established a total weight of 519.74 grams. [@1]

Investigators identified the person who had gone to the transport company to pay
and collect the package, and the detention was carried out at around 18:35 on
the same day. [@1] The parcel had been sent from the Metropolitan Region to
Punta Arenas, a pattern within the investigative lines to detect illicit
transfers to Magallanes. [@1] In the case, a hearing for the abreviado procedure
was set for 17 December. [@1]
