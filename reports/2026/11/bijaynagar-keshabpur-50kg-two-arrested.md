Police in Bijaynagar, Brahmanbaria district, arrested two men in an anti-drug
operation and recovered 50 kilograms of ganja. [@1] Officers made the arrests on
a paved road north of the Sun City restaurant in East Keshabpur village, Pattan
Union, at about 4:40 pm on Thursday 5 November. [@1] The two were named as Md
Anis, 40, of Merashani in Singarbil Union, and Md Sajib, 25, of Chaora
(Daulatbari) in the same union. [@1]

Bijaynagar police Sub-Inspector Iqbal Monir led the operation with accompanying
forces and recovered the 50 kilograms of ganja. [@1] Bijaynagar police
officer-in-charge Mohammad Ahsan Hasan Ullah said cases were filed against both
men under sections 19(ga) of the 36(1) table, 38 and 41 of the Narcotics Control
Act 2018. [@1] Daily Bajroshakti carried the same account of the arrest, naming
the same two men and the same charge sections on 6 November. [@2]
