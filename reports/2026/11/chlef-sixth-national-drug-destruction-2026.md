Algeria's authorities carried out the sixth national operation to destroy drugs
and psychotropic substances on 12 November 2026 in Chlef wilaya in the First
Military Region. [@2] The operation gathered every consignment seized by
People's National Army, National Gendarmerie, National Security and Algerian
Customs units, in the presence of local and national civil, judicial and
security authorities. [@2] It was supervised by the National Committee for
destroying drugs and psychotropics, chaired by the prosecutor general at the
Chlef court. [@2] Judicial-police services weighed and inventoried the stocks
held at collection centres and escorted them to the factory designated for
burning and destruction. [@2]

The burn destroyed 9 tonnes, 802 kilograms and 861 grams of treated kif. [@2]
Treated kif is processed cannabis resin, described as Indian hemp or kif
hashish. [@1] The same operation also destroyed 1 tonne, 135 kilograms and 712
grams of hard drugs in the form of cocaine and heroin. [@2] It also covered more
than 33.5 million hallucinogenic tablets. [@1] It also covered 27 kilograms and
579 grams of psychotropic powder. [@2] The authorities valued all of the
destroyed consignments together at 52,979,400,320 Algerian dinars. [@2]

At the factory the seals were opened, the consignments inventoried and the drugs
destroyed under legal and environmental standards with safety conditions
observed. [@2] The operation was the sixth in a series of national destruction
operations begun in 2023 under the national strategy for preventing drugs and
psychotropics. [@2] At a related forum on drug use, National Gendarmerie
commander Maj. Gen. Ali Seydan described drug flows as a cross-border strategic
security challenge linked to organised crime, public health, road safety and
economic and social security. [@1]
