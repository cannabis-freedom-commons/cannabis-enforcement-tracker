Police in Sragen detained a man identified only as BW, from Jetak village in Sidoharjo, after finding
cannabis growing at his house. [@1,2,3,4,5,6,7] Acting on information from the public, they searched the
house at Dukuh Jetak Tani on Saturday 24 October 2026 at 20.30. [@2,8] The plants grew in used
mineral-water gallon containers in an alley between his house and his neighbour's, closed off at the
front and reachable only through his window [@2,5,7,8,9], and stood about 170 centimetres tall. [@5]

Police seized at least 25 cannabis stalks, four seedlings and 15.8 grams of dried cannabis
[@1,2,3,4,6,8,9]; one account counts 29 plants, four of them 4 to 10 centimetres tall. [@8] One of 26
plants had already been harvested and given or sold to someone around Ngrampal. [@5] Police calculated
the 25 plants at 5 kg worth up to Rp 250 million [@2]; another estimate put them at tens to hundreds of
millions of rupiah. [@7] Police chief AKBP Heru Antariksa Cahya displayed the plants at Sragen police
headquarters on 4 November 2026 at a press conference on October and November cases [@1,7], calling it
the first case of its kind in Sragen. [@3,5,7]

## The seeds

Narcotics chief AKP Sudarmiyanto said BW received the seeds in April 2026 from a man from Aceh named
Juan, whom he met in Mangkunegaran, Keprabon, Banjarsari, in Solo during a holiday outing. [@1,4,6,7] He
sowed them in a small basin and, at 6 to 7 centimetres, planted them around the house. [@1,2,4,5,6,7] The
plants were four to five months old, from seeds sown in April or May, and would be ready at six to seven
months. [@1,5,6,7] Police said they were meant for New Year celebrations, and that BW planned to sell part
[@1,5,6,7]; he consumed the leaves every morning and evening. [@3,5] Police are looking for the supplier.
[@7]

## Charge

BW is in custody [@1,3,5,6], charged under Article 114(2) of the 2009 Narcotics Law with the 2023
Criminal Code and the 2026 sentencing law [@1,3,5,6], or alternatively Article 111(2) [@2], for which
police said the heaviest penalty is death. [@1,2,3,5,6] The police chief said the case would be updated
as it develops. [@7]
