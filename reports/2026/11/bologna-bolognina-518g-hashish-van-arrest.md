Police in Bologna arrested a 43-year-old Tunisian man on suspicion of possessing
drugs for the purpose of dealing. [@1,5] Officers identified him as he moved
around the city in a van to meet clients and hand over doses. [@1] The inquiry
by the Bologna Flying Squad began with reports of suspected dealing at a flat in
via di Saliceto, in the Bolognina neighbourhood. [@2,5]

A search of the van found 11 ready-to-sell doses of cocaine, a baseball bat and
an electric stun device. [@1,5] BolognaToday adds that the van also held 60 euros
and a mobile phone. [@2] In a hideout built into the flat's wall police found
packets of hashish and cocaine, packaging material and 2,000 euros in cash
treated as proceeds of the activity. [@1] èTV says the hideout was built into
the kitchen wall. [@3]

In all, officers seized more than 518 grams of hashish and about 21 grams of
cocaine. [@1,5] The man had previous police records and a conviction for
drug-related offences. [@3] He was reported as well as arrested for carrying
objects capable of causing offence, over the bat and the stun device. [@2] The
van and the stun device were also seized. [@2]

The man had turned his van into a mobile drug shop, where he prepared doses and hid precision scales, hashish and an electric stun device. [@4]

The follow-up search of the flat was carried out with the support of the State Police canine unit. [@5]
