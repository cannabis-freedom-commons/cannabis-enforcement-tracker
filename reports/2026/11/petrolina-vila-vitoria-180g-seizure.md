Military police were acting against drug trafficking in Petrolina, described as
the largest city in the Sertão pernambucano, after a year with several
homicides. [@1] On Monday 2 November 2026, officers of the 2nd Integrated
Specialized Policing Battalion seized drugs in the Vila Vitória neighbourhood in
Petrolina after receiving information about possible drug trafficking there.
[@2] Acting on that complaint, police carried out checks and searches in several
shacks in the community. [@2]

In the searches they found 180 grams of marijuana described as ready for
consumption, the cannabis component recorded here. [@1] They also found 1.020 kg
of cocaine and a precision scale alongside the cannabis. [@2] No suspect was
found or detained during the operation. [@2]

Officers took all the seized material to the city's Civil Police station. [@2]
The Civil Police is investigating who is responsible for trafficking in the
area, with no arrests reported. [@1]
