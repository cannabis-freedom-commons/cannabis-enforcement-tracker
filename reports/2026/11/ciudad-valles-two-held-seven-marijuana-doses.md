Guardia Civil Estatal officers detained Marco “N”, 25, and Ramón “N”, 20, in
Ciudad Valles during security and surveillance patrols under the Huasteca Segura
operation. [@1] The detentions resulted from security devices and operational
actions deployed to strengthen security in the state. [@2] Those detained and
the seized substances were placed at the disposal of the competent authorities
to investigate and determine possible responsibility. [@1] Authorities are
examining whether the men belong to a criminal group operating in the area. [@1]

Officers seized about twenty doses of “cristal” and about seven doses of
marijuana from the two men, allegedly held for sale. [@1] El Exprés described
the marijuana as bags of green, dry herb with the characteristics of marijuana
and the other substance as similar to “cristal”. [@2]

The state Secretariat of Security and Citizen Protection, through the Guardia
Civil Estatal, maintains surveillance devices and operational actions across the
four regions to strengthen public safety. [@1] The competent authorities will
continue the investigations to determine possible responsibility. [@2]
