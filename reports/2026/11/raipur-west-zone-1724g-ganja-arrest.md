Officers of the West Zone of the Raipur Police Commissionerate arrested one
person in a ganja-trafficking case and seized 1.724 kilograms of ganja from that
person's possession. [@1] They registered one case under the NDPS Act, India's
narcotics law, for illegal trade in narcotic drugs, and the police account
headed the case as ganja trafficking. [@1] The arrest came in a special campaign
against criminals and anti-social elements, run so that peace and public order
could be kept ahead of festivals. [@1]

The campaign ran from 4 to 10 November 2026. [@1] It was directed by Deputy
Commissioner of Police (West Zone) Robinson Guria and monitored by Additional
Deputy Commissioner of Police Rahul Dev Sharma. [@1] Police teams from the
station areas of Purani Basti, DD Nagar, Amanaka, Azad Chowk, Saraswati Nagar,
Kabir Nagar, New Rajendra Nagar, Tikrapara and Mujgahan carried out the actions.
[@1] Amrit Sandesh reported the same West Zone campaign from 4 to 10 November,
in which police arrested 91 people, executed 52 warrants and took preventive
action against 135 people. [@2]

Police said the wider drive produced 91 arrests in all, of which the ganja case
was the only one they described as a narcotics offence. [@1] The other arrests
were 73 people in 22 gambling cases, nine people in nine illegal-liquor cases
and eight people under the Arms Act, and those figures are not cannabis
seizures. [@1] In the liquor cases police said they seized 75.24 litres of
liquor, and in the arms cases seven knives and one country-made pistol. [@1]
Police also said they executed 13 standing warrants and 39 arrest warrants, 52
in all, and took preventive action against 135 people suspected of disturbing
peace. [@1] West Zone police said keeping peace and public order in the city was
their first priority and that action of this kind would continue, with patrols
and checks through the festivals. [@1]
