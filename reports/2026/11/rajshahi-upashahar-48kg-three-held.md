Boalia Model Police detained three people in Rajshahi, including the
construction firm's site engineer, after recovering 48 kg of ganja from a
workers' room. [@1,6,9] The raid took place at about 3:30 pm on Sunday, 1 November,
in a workers' tin-shed room in a Sarkar Builders under-construction building in
Upashahar Sector 2. [@1,2,5,6,7] Police named the detained men as 24-year-old Sohir
Islam of Bagdhani, 20-year-old Rayhan of Palpur Dharampur, and another Rayhan of
Chanduria who worked as Sarkar Builders' site engineer. [@1]

Officers said they acted on secret information and broke the lock after those
inside first refused to open the door for a search. [@1,6] Inside, officers found
12 packets holding a total of 48 kg of ganja in two sacks. [@1,5,6,9,11] Police said the
consignment had arrived by truck in three sacks, with one sack already sold and
the remaining two intended to be moved elsewhere. [@1,6,9]

The three were taken to Boalia Model Police Station for questioning, with police
saying further legal steps would follow after investigation and interrogation.
[@1,6] Police said the site engineer was among the three and had been called from
his home. [@1,6] The site engineer was handcuffed while being taken to the
station, but police gave no detailed explanation of his suspected link to the
consignment. [@1] Attempts to reach Rajshahi Metropolitan Police Additional
Commissioner Gaziur Rahman by phone for comment were unsuccessful. [@1]

Police filed a case under the Narcotics Control Act at Boalia police station against the two arrested men and the absconders. [@2,4,5,7,8,10,12] The two arrested men were sent to jail through the court on Monday morning, while operations to arrest the absconders continue. [@2,4,7,8,10,11] Officers also seized two mobile phones used for drug sales, and Biplab Ali with two to three other suspects fled on seeing the police. [@2,4,5,8,11,12] The second account names the two arrested men as 27-year-old Sajib of Bagdhani and 20-year-old Rayhan of Palpur Dharampur and places the raid at Holding No. 49 in Sector-2. [@3,4,8,11,12]

Police said the arrested men are professional drug dealers who had long operated across Rajshahi city in collusion, and that Sajib already faces a Narcotics Control Act case at Natore Sadar police station. [@4,8,10] The arrested men are Md. Sajib (27), son of Md. Abdur Razzaq of Bagdhani village in Paba, and Md. Raihan (20), son of Md. Habibur Rahman of Palpur Dharampur village in Godagari, while the named absconder is 39-year-old Md. Biplab Ali, son of Md. Saidur Rahman of Lakshmipur Dingadoba in Rajpara. [@4,5,7,8,11]

The new account attributes the operation to members of the Rajshahi Metropolitan Police's Upashahar police outpost. [@8,11,12]

Rajshahir Somoy reports the arrest of two men with a large quantity of ganja in the city's Upashahar area, a further account of the same raid. [@2] Rajshahi Metropolitan Police spokesman and Deputy Commissioner (Sadar) Gaziur Rahman confirmed the account on Monday afternoon, the Rajshahir Somoy report says. [@2]
