Kotwali police in Sehore arrested 50-year-old Champalal Yadav at a field on
Badnagar Road. [@1] He is the son of Durga Prasad Yadav and lives in Durga
Colony in Sehore. [@2] Police registered crime number 832/26 against him under
sections 8 and 20 of the NDPS Act and took the case up for investigation. [@2]
Officers are questioning him about the source of the ganja and others involved
in its purchase, sale and network. [@1]

Police said they acted on an informant's tip received on Tuesday 3 November 2026
that illegal ganja was kept in a field on Badnagar Road, then raided the spot
and cordoned it. [@2] A search recovered 700 grams of ganja from his possession,
which officers formally seized. [@1] Police valued the seized ganja at about
6,000 rupees. [@1]

The operation was carried out on the orders of Superintendent of Police Sonakshi
Saxena, under the guidance of Additional Superintendent of Police Prashant
Chaubey and City Superintendent of Police Abhinandana Sharma, by a team led by
Kotwali station chief Girish Dubey. [@1] Police described Yadav as a habitual
offender with earlier narcotics-trafficking, excise and assault cases, including
six earlier NDPS cases listed in the second account. [@2]
