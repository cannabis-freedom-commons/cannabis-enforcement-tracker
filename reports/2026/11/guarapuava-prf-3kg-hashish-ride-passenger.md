Brazil's Federal Highway Police (PRF) carried out inspection operations in
Guarapuava on Friday, 6 November 2026. [@1] Around 6:30 a.m., officers stopped a
car carrying a driver and passengers in Guarapuava. [@3] During the inspection,
officers found approximately three kilograms of hashish on the body of a female
passenger. [@1] The woman had hired the trip through a ride-hailing app. [@1]
Officers sent the hashish case to the Civil Police station in Guarapuava for
further procedures. [@2]

Police described the hashish stop as one of three seizures in Guarapuava that
day, while the other stops involved mobile phones, foreign cigarettes and
medication. [@1]
