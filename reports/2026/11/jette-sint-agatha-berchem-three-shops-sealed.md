On 8 November 2026, Brussels West police sealed three shops in Jette and
Sint-Agatha-Berchem after finding CBD, cannabis-use items, and other contraband.
[@1]

The police action targeted commercial establishments to combat money-laundering
practices and drug trafficking, in cooperation with the prosecutor's office.
[@1]

At two shops in Sint-Agatha-Berchem, a petrol station and a grocery store,
police found two tubes of CBD, items facilitating cannabis use, and €1,923.60 in
cash. [@1] At a newspaper kiosk in Jette, police found CBD and cannabis
products, including 55 pots of Super Weedy (6 g), 53 pots of Edel Weeds (1 g)
and 59 pots of Zion (3 g), all with incorrectly applied fiscal stamps, along
with precision scales, packaging materials and €5,428.75 in cash. [@1]

All three shops were judicially sealed and all suspects were interviewed by
police; no arrests were made. [@1]
