Três Lagoas's Delegacia de Atendimento à Mulher (DAM), the women's police
station, carried out Operação Sclopetum on Wednesday 4 November 2026 to find and
seize firearms linked to domestic violence. [@2] Officers served seven search
warrants, six of them in Três Lagoas and one in Inocência, the second carried
out with the Inocência Civil Police. [@1] The operation ended with two men
detained. [@3] One was held under an open arrest warrant for breaching an
emergency protection order. [@2]

The second was the man responsible for a house where police found portions and
seeds of cannabis, as marijuana is known in Brazil, a precision scale and two
grow tents used to produce the plant. [@1] The outlet said the property held a
setup for cultivating and selling drugs. [@1] He was arrested in the act and
charged with drug trafficking. [@2]

The searches also took a loaded revolver ready for use, an air rifle and a
pellet pistol out of circulation. [@1] The Civil Police said the air rifle and
pellet pistol, though not illegal in themselves, were used as simulated weapons
to frighten and coerce victims. [@1] The Civil Police presents the precautionary
seizure of guns in domestic-violence cases as a way to avert escalation towards
femicide. [@2]
