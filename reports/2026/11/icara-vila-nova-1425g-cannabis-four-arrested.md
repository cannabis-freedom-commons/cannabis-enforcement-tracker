A reported drug deal in Vila Nova, Içara, ended with four people detained. [@1]
The incident was recorded on the afternoon of Friday the 6th in Vila Nova at
about 5:30 p.m. [@1] One of those detained, a 26-year-old man, was shot by the
Military Police. [@1] He received first aid from SAMU and was taken to São
Donato Hospital. [@1] All four people involved were taken with the seized
material to the Central de Flagrantes in Criciúma. [@1]

Officers found about one kilogram of marijuana in the vehicle stopped at the
scene. [@1] Officers then searched another property identified as a possible
drug storage site. [@2] With a police dog from the K9 unit in Criciúma, they
found additional portions of marijuana and cocaine there. [@2] Two more people
were arrested in flagrante at that property. [@2] In total, police reported
1,425 grams of marijuana and five grams of cocaine, plus R$57, five mobile
phones, a roll of plastic wrap, a replica firearm and a Corsa Sedan. [@2]

One occupant tried to flee and dropped an object resembling a pistol. [@2]
Officers fired during the intervention, in what police described as a
confrontation. [@1]
