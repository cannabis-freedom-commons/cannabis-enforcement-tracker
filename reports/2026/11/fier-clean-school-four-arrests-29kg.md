Fier police broke up a group that was distributing drugs near schools. [@1] The
Fier Court imposed prison-arrest measures on Kevin Bare, 28, Dejvi Ferko, 25,
Marjus Zhaka, 48, and Taulant Cela, 52. [@1] A fifth suspect, 38-year-old E. B.
of Fier, was still being sought under the same prison-arrest measure. [@1]

Searches in the arrested men's homes found 2.9 kg of suspected Cannabis Sativa,
2 electronic scales and 4 mobile phones, seized as evidence. [@1]

A several-month investigation using special investigative methods, by narcotics
investigators of the Fier police under the Fier prosecution, documented the
group's activity near educational institutions in the Fier district. [@1] The
Narcotics Investigation Sector, with the Eagle Special Force and the Operational
Investigation Support Section, completed the police operation codenamed Clean
School. [@1] The procedural materials were referred to the prosecution for
further investigation. [@1]
