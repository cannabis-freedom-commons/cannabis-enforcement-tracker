The Şanlıurfa provincial gendarmerie command's narcotics-fighting branch and the
Akçakale district gendarmerie command carried out a coordinated operation
against suspects accused of trading drugs and stimulants. [@1] Teams carried out
simultaneous raids on 17 addresses chosen in advance. [@1] Searches of the
addresses found 149 pills, 8.32 grams of synthetic narcotic, 2.07 grams of
heroin and 27 grams of esrar. [@1] A later local account reported that the 27
grams of esrar was made up of 8.95 grams of powdered cannabis and 18.05 grams of
cannabis, and that the 149 pills were Gerica and Captagon tablets. [@3]

DHA reported that the narcotics were hidden inside a shoe, inside walls and
between cushions. [@2] Eighteen suspects were detained and taken to the
gendarmerie command. [@1] After processing at the gendarmerie, the suspects were
sent to the courthouse, where the court jailed 13 of them and released five
under judicial supervision, a form of conditional release short of pre-trial
detention. [@1]

The Akçakale Chief Public Prosecutor's Office appealed against the release
decisions, and the five suspects were brought back before the court, which also
ordered their detention. [@3]
