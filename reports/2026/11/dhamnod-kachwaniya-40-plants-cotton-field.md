Dhamnod police found cannabis being cultivated at Kahirpura in Kachwaniya
village in Dhar district, Madhya Pradesh, during Operation Prahar, the state
campaign against illegal drugs. [@1] Officers uprooted 40 cannabis plants, known
locally as ganja, that had been grown hidden among a cotton crop. [@1] The
uprooted plants weighed 77 kg 61 g. [@1] The article's headline puts the
estimated value of the cannabis at about 16 lakh rupees. [@1]

Police registered the case as crime number 568/2026 under section 8/20 of the NDPS Act. [@2]
