Military police of the Força Tática, the tactical unit of the 7th Regional
Command, arrested a 26-year-old man for drug trafficking in Tangará da Serra on
the night of Tuesday 10 November 2026. [@2] The arrest followed intelligence
from the Military Police that the man had received a large quantity of narcotics
and was making deliveries around the Jardim Itália neighbourhood. [@1] Police
said he was carrying out those deliveries on a Suzuki Yes motorcycle. [@3]

Officers went to the address and stopped him in a quitinete, a small rented
room, where a personal search found portions of cocaine. [@2] The man confirmed
he had received the drugs and said he made the deliveries on the orders of a
criminal faction, which he said had lent him the motorcycle used to transport
them. [@1] He then led officers to a stretch of woodland near his home, where
police seized two more tablets of cocaine paste, half a bar of cocaine and
portions of marijuana and ice. [@2]

The man was arrested and taken to the city police station together with the
drugs and the motorcycle, and the case was handed over to the Civil Police. [@1]
