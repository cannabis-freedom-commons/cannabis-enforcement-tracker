Military Police officers went to the bus terminal in Nova Alvorada do Sul, Mato
Grosso do Sul, on the morning of Sunday 1 November 2026 after receiving
information that two people were carrying drugs on a Campo Grande–Maringá line
bus. [@1] The bus was on the Campo Grande to Maringá, Paraná, itinerary when it
was checked at the terminal. [@1] When the suspects saw the police they tried to
flee on foot, but officers followed and stopped them. [@1] The pair were a
21-year-old woman, who was arrested, and a 17-year-old girl, who was
apprehended, and the Conselho Tutelar, Brazil's child-protection council, was
called to assist with the minor. [@1]

Officers searched their luggage and found 19 packages of a substance described
as analogous to marijuana, weighing about 12.7 kilograms. [@2] The two told
officers they had left Campo Grande and were carrying the drugs to Presidente
Prudente in São Paulo state, and would receive R$3,000 for the transport,
R$1,500 each. [@1] After forensic examinations, both were taken to the Civil
Police station together with the seized substance for further procedures. [@2]
