Criminal investigators from Blagoevgrad's Second Regional Police Department
checked a 46-year-old resident of the city in the area of Chakalitsa Street at
about 15:15 on 5 November. [@1] About 3.5 grams of marijuana were found on him.
[@1] The investigation established that he had bought the drug from a
36-year-old man from Blagoevgrad. [@1]

The 36-year-old man was detained under a 24-hour detention order. [@1] A
pre-trial proceeding was opened over the case and work to clarify all the facts
continued. [@1] The regional police statement, also published by Pirinsko.com,
gives the same time, street, age and quantity for the check. [@2] Pirinsko.com
reports that the drug was bought from a 36-year-old man from the city who was
also detained for up to 24 hours. [@2]
