The PDI and the Fiscalía Oriente detained two people for drug trafficking at an
apartment in Estación Central. [@1] The two detainees were adult men, one a
Dominican national and one Chilean, and both were arrested on the street in
Estación Central. [@2] Officers stopped a buyer leaving the apartment, found a
package of cocaine hydrochloride in his backpack, and detained him. [@1] After
that arrest they searched four properties in Estación Central and Maipú. [@1]

The PDI seized more than eight kilos of cannabis and more than one kilo of
cocaine hydrochloride, along with more than 11 million pesos in cash and a
banknote-counting machine. [@2] The same operation was also described as a
seizure of about nine kilos of cannabis and cocaine together, plus 11 million
pesos in cash. [@1]

Inspector Karen Contreras of Briant Sur, the Metropolitan South Anti-Narcotics
Investigation Brigade, said the raids followed an inquiry of about six months.
[@1] She said investigators had identified a group linked to supplying, storing
and later selling illicit substances. [@1] The substances were brought into
Chile through unauthorised border crossings in the north and then stored and
distributed in the Metropolitan Region. [@1] Detectives said a Dominican
national authorised buyers into the Estación Central apartment and sold the
drugs himself. [@1]
