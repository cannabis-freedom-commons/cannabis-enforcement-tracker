Rawalpindi District Police arrested five suspected drug peddlers in separate
raids across the district on Monday, 9 November 2026. [@1] Rawat police
apprehended a drug supplier and recovered 2.6 kilograms of hashish from him.
[@1]

Taxila police took two peddlers into custody and recovered 2.36 kilograms of
hashish from their possession. [@1] Jatli police arrested a drug dealer and
seized 1.8 kilograms of hashish. [@1] Dhamyal police recovered 1.45 kilograms of
hashish after arresting another suspect. [@1]

The four station-level recoveries add up to 8.21 kilograms of hashish, which
police described as more than 8 kilograms. [@1] A police spokesperson said
administrative and operational measures were being carried out across the
district to eradicate drug use and dismantle supply networks. [@1]
