Security forces detained 27 people during a 20-day operation in Pasaband
district of western Ghor province. [@1] Police spokesman Mawlawi Abdul Rahman
Badri announced the results on Tuesday. [@1] The arrested individuals were
referred to court for legal proceedings. [@1]

The operation destroyed eight drug production factories. [@1] Officers seized
8,500 grammes of processed hashish, equivalent to 8.5 kilograms, as the
separately stated cannabis component. [@1] The same seizure also included 127
kilogrammes of a drug known as “F”, 17,020 kilogrammes of dried yama and 2,750
kilogrammes of raw materials used in drug production, which are not counted as
cannabis. [@1] Security forces also seized 150 motorcycles, two tractors, a
Kamaz truck, two Ford vehicles and two mills. [@1] All of the seized narcotics
were later destroyed by burning. [@1]

A second outlet corroborated the 20-day operation in Pasaband, the destruction
of eight factories, the arrest of 27 people and the seizure of 150 motorcycles
and other vehicles and equipment. [@2] That outlet described the administration
as presenting the operation as a step toward combating the drug crisis while
noting that drug trafficking persists. [@2]
