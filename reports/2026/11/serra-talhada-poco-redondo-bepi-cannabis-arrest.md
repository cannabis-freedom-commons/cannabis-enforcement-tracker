Military police in Pernambuco arrested two men suspected of drug trafficking on
Friday, 6 November 2026, in the Sítio Poço Redondo area of rural Serra Talhada.
[@1] The operation was carried out by the state's Specialised Interior Policing
Battalion (BEPI), with support from the 14th Military Police Battalion. [@1]

Police said they seized 399 grams of marijuana and 202 grams of hashish, about
601 grams of cannabis in all. [@1] The same operation yielded 1.262 kilograms of
cocaine and 427 grams of crack, which are not counted with the cannabis. [@1]
Officers also took a motorcycle, two mobile phones and 112 reais in cash. [@1]

The two men were taken to the Civil Police station in Serra Talhada with the
seized material and booked in the act. [@2] The submitted account reported the
same handover of the men, the objects and the suspected drugs to the Serra
Talhada station. [@1] A Terra/Portal de Prefeitura account put the total mixed
seizure at more than 2 kg, including cocaine, crack, marijuana and hashish. [@3]

The Military Police did not say how the suspects were located or whether they
had criminal records, and did not release their names. [@1]
