Officers from Vranov carried out a police operation on Thursday that ended the
cannabis cultivation. [@1] During the operation they seized plants of the genus
Cannabis. [@1] They also seized dried plant material containing THC. [@1]
Immediately after the seizure, officers detained a 41-year-old man from Vranov
nad Topľou. [@1]

The information about the detention and seizure comes from the Prešov regional
police. [@1] Police said the man was charged with the crime of unauthorised
possession of a narcotic and psychotropic substance. [@1] The same charging
decision also covers the offence of unauthorised cultivation of plants and fungi
containing a narcotic or psychotropic substance. [@1] The man faces one year in
prison. [@1]

Police said they could not provide any further information at the moment because
the investigation is continuing. [@1]
