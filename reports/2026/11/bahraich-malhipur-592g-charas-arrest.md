Police at Dargah Sharif station in Bahraich arrested a man with 592 grams of
charas at around 10 am on Friday. [@1] The arrest followed a check-campaign on
the road from Malhipur crossing towards Chichdi, where officers stopped the man
and searched him. [@2]

The station house officer said the checks were part of an intensified drive in
the area around the festive season. [@2]

The search recovered 592 grams of charas from him. [@2] Bahraich police said on
X that the accused was arrested with 592 grams of illegal charas on the SP
Bahraich's direction. [@3]

Police registered a case against him under the NDPS Act, India's Narcotic Drugs
and Psychotropic Substances Act. [@1] At around 2 pm on the same day, officers
sent him to court to be produced before a magistrate. [@1]
