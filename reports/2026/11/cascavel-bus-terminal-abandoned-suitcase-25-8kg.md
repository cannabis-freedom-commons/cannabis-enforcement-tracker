Guarda Municipal officers in Cascavel, western Paraná, seized a suitcase holding
several large tablets of a substance similar to marijuana on the night of
Thursday 5 November 2026. [@1] The suitcase had been left abandoned at Gate B of
the Cascavel bus terminal and drew the attention of terminal staff because of a
strong smell of coffee coming from it, possibly used to disguise the smell of
the drug. [@1] Inside, officers found several large marijuana tablets and one
smaller tablet packed separately. [@1]

The drug weighed about 25.8 kg, according to the Guarda Municipal account
published by Portal CATVE.com. [@2] The suitcase also held women's underwear, a
sanitary pad, rolling papers and a pair of children's flip-flops apparently
sized for a child of 2 to 3, plus a blanket with a Spider-Man print. [@1]

The belongings led officers to suspect the suitcase belonged to a woman, but the
identity of whoever was carrying it was not confirmed. [@1] The material was
sent to the Civil Police station for official weighing and investigation, and no
one had been located at the time of reporting. [@1]

The action began after terminal staff noticed the abandoned bag and alerted the
security teams, who came to the gate and opened the suitcase to check it. [@1]

The Guarda Municipal, Cascavel's municipal guard force, said the smaller
separately wrapped tablet appeared to be reserved for personal use. [@3] Rádio
Educadora reported that the municipal guards confirmed the presence of the drug
on the spot and that no suspect had been located. [@4]
