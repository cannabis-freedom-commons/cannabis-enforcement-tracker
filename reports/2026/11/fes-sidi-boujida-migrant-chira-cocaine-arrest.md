Officers of the anti-gang squad of the Fes security wilaya stopped a Moroccan
migrant living in Italy who was with his wife, to question both after drugs
described as a significant quantity of hard drugs alongside hashish were found
in their possession. [@1] The intervention took place late on Friday night in
the Sidi Boujida neighbourhood in the Jnan El Ward district. [@1] Police seized
the car in which the drugs were found and took the man and his wife to the
security wilaya headquarters for questioning. [@1]

Searching the car enabled the seizure of nearly 200 grams of cocaine and more
than 8 kilograms of chira in slab form. [@1] Chira is cannabis resin, so this
report records only the 8 kilograms of chira as cannabis and excludes the
cocaine. [@1]

On the instructions of the competent public prosecution, the suspect was placed
in police custody for further investigation of the case. [@1]
