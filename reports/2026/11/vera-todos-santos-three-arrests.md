Vera Local Police detained three people during the All Saints bridge week on
suspicion of drug-trafficking offences against public health. [@1] One of the
detentions involved an indoor cannabis grow and an additional allegation of
electricity fraud. [@1] Officers found an indoor cannabis cultivation containing
plastic bags with approximately 1.8 kilos of marijuana. [@1]

Officers also found illegal hookups to the electricity grid and seized a
precision digital scale plus growing materials such as pots, lamps, insulation
and fans. [@1] Two further detentions occurred in the central zone of the
municipality for the same alleged drug-trafficking offences. [@1] In those
central-zone cases officers seized more than 100 grams of hashish and cash. [@1]

The two people detained in the centre entered prison, according to Vera Local
Police. [@1] Police put the overall result at almost two kilos of marijuana,
more than 100 grams of hashish and cash, alongside administrative reports for
other infractions and antisocial conduct. [@1]
