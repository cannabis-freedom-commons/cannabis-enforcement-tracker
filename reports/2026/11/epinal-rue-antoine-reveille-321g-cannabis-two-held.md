Two men, aged 46 and 20, were arrested in Épinal on 7 November 2026 on suspicion
of drug trafficking. [@1] One of the two is suspected of being the trade's
«nourrice», the stash keeper who holds the stock, and the drugs were found at
his home. [@1] The arrests followed a police operation that uncovered a large
traffic in heroin, cannabis and cocaine in Épinal, in the Vosges department.
[@1]

In an apartment on rue Antoine-Réveillé, officers of the police nationale
carried out the search and seizure. [@1] Police found 321 g of cannabis herb,
3.427 kg of heroin and 2.2 g of cocaine at the home of the suspect. [@1] The 321
g of herb is the cannabis share of the haul; the heroin and cocaine weights are
not counted with it. [@1]

The two men were brought before the court on Tuesday 10 November for an
immediate appearance, a fast-track procedure. [@1] They asked for time to
prepare their defence. [@1] No plea was entered, and the case was adjourned.
[@1] The court kept them in provisional detention, custody before trial, until a
trial set for early December. [@1]
