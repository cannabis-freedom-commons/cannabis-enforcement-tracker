The criminal division of Tunis's first-instance court sentenced the three-member
gang to 12 years' prison plus 10 years of administrative surveillance near
schools in the Sidi Hussein delegation. [@1] The court also imposed heavy
financial fines on the gang. [@1]

The case began when specialised security units carried out in-depth
investigations that brought down the gang members as they were dealing narcotics
near school compounds. [@1] The investigation found the defendants in an
integrated network supplying drugs to minors and young people, exploiting their
proximity to schools to expand their customer base. [@1]
