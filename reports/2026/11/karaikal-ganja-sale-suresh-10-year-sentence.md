Karaikal District Judge Mohan sentenced Suresh, 44, the main accused in a
Karaikal ganja-sale case, to 10 years of rigorous imprisonment and a Rs 50,000
fine. [@1] The judge ordered Suresh to serve an additional six months in prison
if he fails to pay the fine. [@1] The court also fined three co-accused in the
same case, Sathish, Vijayson and Faisal Rahman, Rs 10,000 each. [@1]

The case began in 2023 after police received information that some people were
selling ganja to young people in the Karaikal Town police-station area. [@1]
Officers rushed to the scene and arrested Sathish of Kambam in Theni, who was
studying at a private college in Nagapattinam, along with his Karaikal friends
Vijayson and Faisal Rahman. [@1] Questioning revealed that Suresh, 44, Sathish's
father, was behind ganja sales in the Karaikal area, after which police arrested
Suresh and lodged him in jail. [@1]

The case was tried before Karaikal District Judge Mohan, and judgment was
delivered the day before the 10 November 2026 report. [@1]
