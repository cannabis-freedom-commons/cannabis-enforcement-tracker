Secretariat Colony police in Chennai arrested three men for allegedly selling
cannabis, known locally as ganja, in the Secretariat Colony area of Otteri on 9
November 2026. [@1] The three arrested were Suresh, 22, Taja, 24, and Peter, 19,
all residents of Thideer Nagar in Purasaiwalkam. [@1]

Officers seized 2.5 kg of ganja in the arrest. [@1] The account was one item in
Dinamalar's Chennai crime roundup published on 10 November 2026, which dates the
arrests to the previous day. [@1]
