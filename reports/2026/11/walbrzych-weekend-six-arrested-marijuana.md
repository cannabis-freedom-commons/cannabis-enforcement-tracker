Wałbrzych police detained six men over the weekend for possessing amphetamine,
methamphetamine and marijuana in Wałbrzych and Wałbrzych county. [@1] Officers
stopped a 35-year-old Wałbrzych man after 4 p.m. on Friday on a street in
Sobięcin and found marijuana and methamphetamine on him. [@1]

At about 8 p.m. the same day, officers detained a 34-year-old in Biały Kamień
with a wrap of dried herb that forensic testing confirmed was marijuana. [@1] At
about the same time, officers checked a 20-year-old in Boguszów-Gorce and found
amphetamine on him. [@1] At about 4:30 p.m. on Saturday, officers patrolling the
Piaskowa Góra area stopped a 26-year-old on Wrocławska Street and found a foil
bag with dried herb that was marijuana. [@1] After midday on Sunday,
Boguszów-Gorce officers on Niepodległości Street in Podgórze found marijuana on
another Wałbrzych man. [@1] Just before 3 a.m. on the day of publication,
officers detained a 39-year-old on Przemysłowa Street in Śródmieście with a wrap
of crystals that was methamphetamine. [@1]

All six detainees were questioned and charged under Poland's law on
counteracting drug addiction. [@1] Police said the six men face up to three
years in prison for possessing intoxicants and psychotropic substances and that
a court will decide their fate. [@1]
