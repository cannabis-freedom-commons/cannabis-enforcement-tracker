Police in Płońsk detained a 34-year-old resident of the town after he was
reported walking into the roadway on Grunwaldzka Street on the evening of
Thursday, 5 November 2026. [@2] The callers were members of the public who
noticed his behaviour, and officers from the patrol and intervention unit found
a man with clear difficulty keeping his balance. [@2]

Officers smelled alcohol and a characteristic odour of marijuana on him, and a
personal search found drugs, which were seized for laboratory testing. [@2] A
breath test showed more than 1.5 per mille of alcohol in his body, a reading
Polish police report in promile, or grams of alcohol per litre of blood. [@3]

He was detained, and if tests confirm the seized dried plant material is a
controlled substance he may face a charge of possessing it under Poland's
anti-drug law. [@1] Under the act on counteracting drug addiction, possessing
narcotic or psychotropic substances carries up to three years in prison. [@2]

The Płońsk county police command published the account, which its press officer,
Sgt. Eliza Koźlicka, gave to local outlets. [@2] Infoplonsk.pl also reported the
intervention, saying the pedestrian's behaviour drew the attention of a witness.
[@4]
