Military Police in Arapongas stopped a man wearing an electronic ankle monitor
during a patrol on Rua Ema in the Araponguinha neighbourhood on the morning of
Monday, 9 November 2026. [@1] Officers approached the suspect at around 10:50
while patrolling an area monitored to prevent drug trafficking. [@1] According
to the police bulletin, the man was already known to authorities for recurring
involvement in narcotics trafficking. [@2]

During a personal search, officers found a package containing 38 grams of
cannabis on the suspect. [@2] Officers seized the cannabis and took the man to
the 22nd Police Subdivision in Arapongas for proceedings related to drug
possession for personal consumption. [@1]

The cannabis seizure was one of two separate occurrences recorded by the
Arapongas Military Police on the same day, alongside the theft of a backpack in
the Aeroporto neighbourhood. [@1]
