Officers of the Vogošća police unit in Sarajevo Canton arrested two men at 00:10
on 5 November 2026. [@1] One is identified as Z.R., born in 1998 in Kasindol and
living in Vogošća; the other is F.V., born in 2002 in Sarajevo, where he lives.
[@1] The television's version of the report gives the same birth years and
places for both suspects. [@3] Police say they were arrested on suspicion of
possessing drugs and enabling their use, under article 239(3) of the criminal
code of the Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina. [@1]

A search of F.V. found and seized one packet of material resembling the drug
marihuana, the local spelling of cannabis, the reports say. [@1] According to
Vijesti.ba, Z.R. is 28 and F.V. is 24, the ages implied by the birth years the
reports give. [@2]

The duty prosecutor of the Sarajevo Canton Prosecutor's Office was notified, and
the two men were handed over to the Sarajevo Canton interior ministry's holding
facility for criminal processing. [@1]
