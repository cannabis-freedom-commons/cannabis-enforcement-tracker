The Federal Police seized 1,250 kilos of drugs in the Igarapé Lage Indigenous
Land in the Guajará-Mirim region of Rondônia. [@1] According to the Military
Police, the haul comprised 1,228.6 kilos of skunk marijuana, 10.3 kilos of
cocaine hydrochloride and 11.1 kilos of coca base paste. [@1] The drugs were
hidden amid vegetation. [@1]

According to the Federal Police, no one was arrested. [@1] No suspects or
vehicles linked to the find were reported. [@1] The work began on Monday the 9th
during patrol and territorial monitoring under Operations Madeira-Mamoré II and
Protetor das Fronteiras e Divisas. [@1] The seizure was disclosed on Tuesday the
10th and occurred during an inspection operation in the region. [@1]

Because the area is difficult to access, a Gerência de Aviação de Estado
helicopter was used to remove the material. [@1] The operation brought together
the Federal Police, Federal Highway Police, Military Police, Civil Police and
Federal Revenue Service, plus the Brazilian Army and the National Indigenous
Peoples Foundation. [@1] The drugs were taken by police vehicles to the Federal
Police station in Guajará-Mirim for legal procedures. [@1] The force is
investigating to identify those responsible, who could answer for drug
trafficking. [@1]
