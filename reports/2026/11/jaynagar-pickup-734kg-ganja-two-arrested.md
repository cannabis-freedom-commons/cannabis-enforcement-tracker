Police and excise officers in Bihar's Madhubani district seized 73.4 kilograms
of ganja, the local name for cannabis, from a pickup in Jaynagar. [@1] Two men
were arrested in connection with the seizure. [@1]

The two men arrested were described as smugglers from Patna and from Vaishali
district. [@1] Police also seized the pickup that carried the ganja. [@1]

The report was filed from Jaynagar in Madhubani district by reporter Shiv Kumar
Prasad. [@1] Police released a photograph of the seized ganja lying with the
pickup. [@1] The 73.4 kilogram weight is the figure given by the team that made
the seizure. [@1]
