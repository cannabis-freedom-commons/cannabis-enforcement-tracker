On Wednesday 11 November 2026, Chiniot police arrested three alleged drug
dealers and recovered more than 2,500 grams (2.5 kilograms) of hashish during
separate operations across Chiniot district in Punjab. [@1] The arrests were
carried out under the Drug-Free Punjab campaign, which police said had been
launched on the directives of Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif. [@1]

District Police Officer Dr Naveed Atif ordered the operations, and City Police
Station station house officer Sub-Inspector Imran Khan led the teams with
assistant sub-inspectors Ghulam Qambar, Umar Hayat and Muhammad Ashraf. [@1] The
teams carried out the raids in different areas of the district and recovered the
hashish from the suspects. [@1] Police registered cases against the three
suspects and began further legal proceedings. [@1]

Dr Naveed Atif said special operations against drug dealers were continuing
across the district, and that police were working to ensure professional
investigations and secure convictions in drug-related cases. [@1] He asked
residents to report drug-related activity in their areas through police helpline
15 or by calling 03237543444 and 03704022020, to help curb drug trafficking.
