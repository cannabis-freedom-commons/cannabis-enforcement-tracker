A patrol crew of the Elze police station checked a car in Gartenstraße in Gronau
(Leine) during the night from 13 to 14 November 2026. [@1] The driver was a
26-year-old man whom officers suspected of having previously consumed
intoxicants. [@1] An Elze patrol stopped the man in Gartenstrasse on the night
to Saturday, 14 November. [@3]

An on-site urine drug pre-test reacted positive for THC, a psychoactive
substance from the cannabis plant. [@1] Officers then took a blood sample from
the driver and prohibited him from continuing to drive. [@1] The Hildesheim
police statement likewise says a blood sample was taken and the onward journey
was stopped. [@2]

Police opened administrative offence proceedings against the driver for driving
under the influence of intoxicants under section 24a of the Road Traffic Act.
[@2] The statement says a first-time violation of this kind typically draws a
500-euro fine, two points in the driving fitness register and a one-month
driving ban. [@1] Hilde-News added that the decisive factor for the proceedings
is the THC level in blood serum, citing the 3.5 nanograms per millilitre limit.
[@3]
