Kenya's Directorate of Criminal Investigations (DCI) seized more than 582 kg of suspected
cannabis sativa and arrested five people in intelligence-led operations across Kisumu, Kisii
and Nairobi counties, the agency said in a statement on Sunday, 1 November 2026. [@1,2]
Detectives from the National Anti-Narcotics Bureau (NANIB), the Crime Research and
Intelligence Bureau (CRIB), the Rapid Deployment Unit and the K-9 Unit took part. [@1,2]

In Kisumu, officers intercepted a gold Toyota Axio, registration KCM 214Q, at the Kisumu Boys
Roundabout. [@1,2] The driver defied orders to stop, so officers shot out the car's front-left
tyre; the driver escaped in the commotion while 21-year-old co-driver Frida Night Odour was
arrested as she tried to flee. [@1,2] A search found suspected cannabis packed in seven Manila
sacks plus loose material in the car, weighing 282.55 kg with an estimated market value of
Ksh8,656,500. [@1,2] Officers also recovered 40 dozen packets of suspected uncustomed Supermatch
cigarettes valued at Ksh40,000. [@2]

In Kisii, officers acting on a tip-off from a member of the public raided a house at Keumbu
Trading Centre and recovered seven sacks of suspected cannabis weighing about 300 kg. [@1,2]
Elijah Amisi Mogire, 32, was arrested at the scene. [@1,2]

In Nairobi, a team led by the Jogoo Road OCS raided a two-bedroom house in Jericho Estate,
Buruburu, and recovered 1,002 rolls of suspected bhang hidden in a black polythene bag, with
an estimated street value of Ksh5,750. [@1,2] Three suspects, Noah James Kariuli, Calvin Malibo
and Brian Ndolo, were arrested and booked at Jogoo Road Police Station. [@1,2]

The DCI said the exhibits were secured, the scenes documented, and the five suspects remained
in custody pending arraignment. [@1,2]
