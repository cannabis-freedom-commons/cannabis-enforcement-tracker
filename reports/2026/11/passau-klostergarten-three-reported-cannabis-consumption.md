A police foot patrol in the Klostergarten in Passau found three people aged 29,
21 and 31 smoking a joint on Sunday 8 November 2026 at about 15:00. [@1] The
Klostergarten is a designated no-consumption zone, so officers recorded an
administrative offence report and ordered the three to leave the area. [@1]

The account came from the Passau police inspection, whose notices the regional
portal WAIDLER.COM publishes as its crime and police reports. [@1]
