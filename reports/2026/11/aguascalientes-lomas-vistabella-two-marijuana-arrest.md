Two 23-year-old men, later identified as Carlos N and Oscar N, were detained by
Aguascalientes municipal police after they noticed the pair pushing a car in
Lomas de Vistabella and officers smelled marijuana on them. [@1]

Officers smelled a strong odor of marijuana and both men fled; they were
intercepted at the crossing of Vista de la Montana and Vista del Anochecer
streets. [@1]

Police seized 12 packets of a granular substance, 14 bags of dried green plant
material, a brown waist pouch and the Ford Topaz the two men had been pushing.
[@1] Both men were taken to the public prosecutor's office together with the
seized items to continue the investigation into their possible involvement in
drug offences and determine their legal status. [@1]
