Drug-control officers of the Sevastopol interior ministry directorate, backed by
Rusguard fighters, detained a 43-year-old local resident whom police described
as a wholesale drug courier for an online shop. [@2] Police said he found the
work in a messenger app and that his job was to take wholesale batches, store
and pack them, and lay master stashes across Sevastopol and Crimea, which are
then split into single doses for retail sale. [@3] He was detained at the rented
house he was living in. [@2]

A search of the house found electronic scales, packaging materials and single
doses of marijuana and amphetamine. [@2] In a rented garage officers seized a
pressed briquette of cannabis weighing more than 1.3 kilograms. [@2] In woodland
near Inkerman they took a one-kilogram package of mephedrone from a stash the
man had not yet collected. [@2]

The 43-year-old had worked as a shoemaker before taking the job. [@2]
Investigators of the Gagarinsky district police division opened a criminal case
for attempted sale of drugs on a very large scale, under part 3 of article 30
and part 5 of article 228.1 of the Criminal Code. [@3] A court ordered him held
in custody and the investigation continues. [@2] MK v Krymu reported that the
charge carries 10 to 20 years in prison. [@3] The Crimean news feed that
published the police video put the haul as more than two kilograms of marijuana
and mephedrone combined. [@1]
