FPA investigators detained a man in a preventive street check in Cosquín during
the weekend before the house raid. [@1] Officers then raided a house in Cosquín
and dismantled an alleged drug-selling point as part of an investigation into
breach of the National Narcotics Law. [@1] The raided property stands on calle
Francisco Ramírez at 1300. [@1] The house operation was directly linked to the
earlier street procedure in the same town. [@1]

During the house procedure, investigators seized several doses of cocaine and
marijuana plants, with the plants forming the cannabis component recorded here.
[@1] Investigators carried out a detailed inspection of the premises and
collected key evidentiary material, including scales, wrappers and other
elements linked to cutting and portioning illicit substances. [@1]

The operation was carried out by the Fuerza Policial Antinarcotráfico (FPA), the
anti-drug police force. [@1] The collected evidence was placed at the disposal
of the Fiscalía de Lucha contra el Narcotráfico de Cosquín, the local anti-drug
prosecutor's office. [@1] That office ordered all seized items to be transferred
to judicial headquarters so the criminal case could proceed. [@1]
