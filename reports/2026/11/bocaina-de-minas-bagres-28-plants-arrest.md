Military police went to a farm in the hamlet of Bagres, in the rural zone of
Bocaina de Minas on Minas Gerais' border with Rio de Janeiro state, after
receiving information that cannabis was being grown there. [@1] When he saw the
police team, a man tried to run and to destroy some of the plants, but officers
restrained him. [@1] A man was arrested at the end of the operation and taken to
the competent authorities with the seized material. [@1]

Officers seized 28 cannabis plants at the farm. [@1] They also seized a large
jar containing cannabis seeds. [@1] The seizure included 450 reais in cash, a
mobile phone and two notebooks with notes said to relate to drug-trafficking
accounts. [@1] Police also took spent 12-gauge and .32 cartridges and eight
intact 9 mm cartridges. [@1]

The incident was recorded as illicit drug trafficking. [@1] The operation was
carried out by military police teams on the Minas Gerais–Rio de Janeiro border.
[@1] The outlet credits the information and photos in the account to the Minas
Gerais Military Police (PMMG). [@1]
