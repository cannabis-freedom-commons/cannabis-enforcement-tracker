Two 25-year-old men were arrested in the city of Concepción when they arrived to
collect parcels containing drugs, after police tracked the shipment from a
parcel company's industrial park to its Concepción branch. [@1] The two suspects
were detained as they came to pick up the packages after police watched the
delivery route, and they were left incommunicado. [@2] Both men were transferred
to the Unidad Regional Norte on the order of the intervening prosecution unit,
pending appearance before the courts. [@1]

Officers seized 10 kilos of marijuana that were being carried in a
parcel-delivery truck, alongside a separate one-kilo cocaine seizure that is not
counted in the cannabis figures. [@1] Police estimated that the 10 kilos of
marijuana could yield about 100,000 individual packets, according to the police
estimates reported. [@2] Officers also seized the bundles used to carry the
drugs and a motorcycle the two men were using. [@1]

The case began the previous Sunday when officers at the Cabo Vallejo border post
stopped the parcel transport at a checkpoint on National Route 9 at kilometre
1,371, on the Tucumán–Salta boundary. [@1] Federal Court No. 2 intervened and
ordered investigative steps to identify the shipment's intended recipients, with
the northern dangerous-drugs directorate placed in charge of tracing them. [@1]
The case is being investigated as a suspected violation of National Narcotics
Law No. 23.737. [@1]
