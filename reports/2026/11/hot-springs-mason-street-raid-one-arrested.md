Hot Springs police executed a search warrant at a residence early Thursday and
arrested one resident. [@1] Michael Ryan Art Shelby, 40, who lists an address of
331 Mason Street, was taken into custody at his listed residence around 7 a.m.
[@1]

The search seized large amounts of THC vapes and marijuana. [@1] The search also
seized psychedelic mushrooms and four pounds of suspected cocaine alongside the
cannabis products. [@1]

Shelby was charged with trafficking a controlled substance, punishable by up to
life in prison. [@1] He was also charged with possession of a controlled
substance with purpose to deliver, punishable by up to 30 years. [@1] He was
also charged with possession of marijuana with purpose to deliver and possession
of drug paraphernalia, punishable by up to 20 years. [@1] The charges include a
possible enhancement for distribution of a controlled substance near certain
facilities. [@1]
