Officers of Seguridad Urbana and Seguridad Vial detained a man during a vehicle
checkpoint in Campo Alegre, on Ruta Nacional 9 in the locality of La Caldera.
[@1] The police intervention took place at kilometre 1629 of the national route.
[@1] While inspecting the vehicle and requesting the corresponding
documentation, officers detected that the driver was transporting narcotic
substances. [@1]

Personnel from the División Investigación Narcocriminal 2 subsequently conducted
field tests and seized 70 doses of marijuana and cocaine. [@1] The 26-year-old
driver was placed at the disposal of the justice system for alleged narcotics
offences. [@1] The case was referred to the flagrancy unit of the provincial
criminal prosecution service. [@1]
