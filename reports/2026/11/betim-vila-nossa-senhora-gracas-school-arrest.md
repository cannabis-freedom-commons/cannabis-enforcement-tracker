Military Police officers in Betim, Minas Gerais, arrested an 18-year-old man on
suspicion of drug trafficking less than 50 metres from a school on the night of
Tuesday 10 November 2026. [@1] The arrest took place in the Vila Nossa Senhora
das Graças neighbourhood. [@1] Officers from the Grupo Especial para Prevenção
Motorizada Ostensiva Rápida, a Military Police rapid patrol unit, had received
information about possible drug sales in the area. [@1] During the patrol,
officers found a suspect matching the description in the tip. [@1] When he
noticed the officers approaching, the young man dropped a package containing
portions of a substance similar to skunk. [@1]

Officers then searched the area and found more portions of a substance similar
to marijuana. [@1] In all, the Military Police seized 40 portions of marijuana,
19 portions of skunk and a mobile phone. [@1] The man was arrested in flagrante,
meaning caught in the act, and taken to the police station with the seized
material. [@1]
