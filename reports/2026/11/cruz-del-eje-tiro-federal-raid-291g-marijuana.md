Officers of the Subdirección General de Investigaciones Criminales Zona Norte
carried out a search of a house in barrio Tiro Federal, Cruz del Eje, in the
investigation of a qualified robbery that occurred on 7 November. [@1] The
search took place on Thursday 12 November, around 12:00, and produced the
seizure of clothing officers considered related to the robbery under
investigation. [@1]

Officers also held a man of 20 and a woman of 21 for a presumed infringement of
National Law 23.737, Argentina's narcotics law, linked to possession of drugs.
[@1] Officers seized 15 packages of cocaine hydrochloride weighing about 12
grams and a bag of marijuana buds that reached 291 grams. [@1] They also seized
a mobile phone, two scales, 207,000 Argentine pesos in cash and a Honda 110cc
motorcycle. [@1]

The Fuerza Policial Antinarcotráfico, Argentina's anti-narcotics police force,
assisted the operation, and both the detainees and the seized items were handed
over to the competent judicial authority. [@1]
