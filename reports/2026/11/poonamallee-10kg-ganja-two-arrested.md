The Avadi City Police arrested two men from Keralam and seized 10 kg of ganja
from them near Poonamallee on Thursday. [@1] Acting on a tip-off about ganja
trafficking, a special team led by Inspector Prabhu of the Poonamallee
Prohibition Enforcement Wing conducted surveillance on the service road near the
Poonamallee Bypass bus stop. [@1] The police intercepted two persons behaving
suspiciously and identified them as Mohammed Shameer, 23, and Ashin, 23, both
natives of Keralam. [@1]

Police said a search revealed that Shameer was carrying 6 kg and Ashin 4 kg of
ganja, and both men were arrested. [@1] A case was registered at the Poonamallee
Prohibition Enforcement Wing, and the accused were to be produced before a court
for remand in judicial custody. [@1] The Avadi Police Commissionerate announced
that 10 kg of ganja had been seized near Poonamallee and two people arrested.
