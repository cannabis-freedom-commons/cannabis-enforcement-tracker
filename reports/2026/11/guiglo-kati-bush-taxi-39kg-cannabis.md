Two passengers in a bush taxi heading for Guiglo asked the driver to stop a few
metres before the Kati checkpoint at about 15:20 on 10 November 2026. [@1] They
asked for the stop saying they needed to relieve themselves. [@3] They walked
into the bush and never came back. [@3] The driver waited and then drove on to
the checkpoint, where he reported them to the anti-drug police. [@1] He also
handed over the bags the two travellers had left. [@5]

Officers of GRAD-Guiglo, the anti-drug group of Guiglo, checked the abandoned
bags in front of the driver and the other passengers. [@1] The search found a
large quantity of drugs, carefully packed and hidden in the baggage. [@2] The
haul was 39 blocks and 1,100 pochons, or small bags, of cannabis, with a total
weight of 39.643 kg. [@4] Police also found 800 doses of heroin weighing 61 g, 2
g of cocaine and 110 packaging packets. [@1]

The seizure was made under Operation Épervier 12, a police drive against drug
trafficking. [@2] Both passengers were still missing and no arrest was reported.
[@5] An investigation was opened to find them and to establish where the drugs
came from and where they were going. [@1]
