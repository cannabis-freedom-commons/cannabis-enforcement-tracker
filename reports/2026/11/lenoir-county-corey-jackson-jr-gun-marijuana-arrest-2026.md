Lenoir County sheriff's deputies stopped a black Dodge Charger near Skinner's
Bypass and Highway 11-55 in Lenoir County, North Carolina, around 3:38 a.m. on 7
November 2026 after finding that its registration had been revoked. [@2] The
driver was 26-year-old Corey Jackson Jr. of Kinston. [@2] Deputies said they
found a loaded AR-style pistol under Jackson's seat during the traffic stop.
[@2]

Jackson told deputies that he had marijuana in the car, which deputies recovered
at the scene. [@2] The sheriff's office described Jackson as a convicted felon
and said the stop led to new gun and drug charges. [@1] He was charged with
felony possession of a firearm by a convicted felon, felony carrying a concealed
gun and misdemeanor simple possession of marijuana. [@2]

The sheriff's office said Jackson was jailed without bond because he was on
pretrial release at the time of his arrest. [@2] The arrest took place near
