Police in Chattogram Range arrested 131 people on drug charges during a 24-hour
special operation. [@1] Eighty-four of them were held in drug-related cases,
including one drug carrier and 83 drug users. [@1] Forty-seven others were held
as suspected drug dealers in regular and other cases. [@1] Mobile courts
sentenced 23 people to imprisonment in the anti-drug drives. [@1] Authorities
filed 35 drug-related cases and collected Tk 10,600 in fines during the drives.
[@1]

Officers seized 30 kilograms and 435 grams of cannabis in the same operation.
[@1] The same seizure also included 17,044 yaba pills, 188 litres of locally
brewed liquor, six bottles of foreign liquor and 697 bottles of Skaf cough syrup
alongside the cannabis. [@1]

The drug operation was part of a wider 24-hour special operation across the
Chattogram Range targeting juvenile gangs, mugging, extortion, terrorist
activities and drug-related offences. [@1] The operation ran between Monday 2
November and Tuesday 3 November, according to police. [@1] Police said the
special operation would continue in the public interest. [@1]
