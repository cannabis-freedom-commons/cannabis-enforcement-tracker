Sidoarjo district police said they uncovered 147 drug cases between August and
October 2026 and detained 180 suspects, comprising 174 men and 6 women. [@1]
Antara reported the same August-to-October operation in Sidoarjo, East Java,
with the same total of 180 suspects. [@2] Police said five prominent cases
involved sabu in large amounts. [@1]

The seizures listed for the operation were 1,765.13 grams of sabu, 4.41 grams of
ganja, 2,089 ecstasy pills and 30,486 LL pills. [@1] Police estimated the
combined haul's economic value at about Rp35 billion and said the seizures had
saved about 20,000 people from drug danger. [@1]

Police said the suspects mainly used dead-drop stashing called sistem ranjau,
cash on delivery and other people as couriers. [@1] Police identified Taman,
Gedangan, Krian, Tulangan and Waru as the five subdistricts most vulnerable to
drug circulation. [@1] The suspects were charged under articles 114, 111 and/or
127 of the 2009 Narcotics Law and provisions of the 2023 Health Law. [@1] The
police chief said enforcement would not stop with the couriers and that
investigators would pursue higher networks including fugitive dealers. [@1]
