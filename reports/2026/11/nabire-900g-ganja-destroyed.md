The Nabire District Attorney's Office destroyed evidence from 24 criminal cases
that had obtained final legal force. [@1] The destruction took place at the
Nabire prosecutor's office on Thursday 5 November 2026. [@1] Ten of the cases
were narcotics cases, comprising two sabu cases and eight ganja cases. [@2] The
activity took place at Jalan Merdeka Number 50, Karang Mulia village, Nabire
District, Nabire Regency, Papua Tengah. [@3] The destruction implemented court
decisions ordering the evidence confiscated for destruction. [@1]

The D.M. case listed 30 large plastic packs of suspected ganja leaves, seeds and
dry stems with a gross weight of 374.53 grams and a net weight of 313.12 grams.
[@2] The S.K. case listed 40 large clear packs of suspected ganja totalling
511.74 grams. [@2] The B.M. case listed suspected ganja with a gross weight of
21.95 grams and a net weight of 18.70 grams. [@2] The R.G.B.M. case listed
suspected ganja with a net weight of 54.4627 grams. [@2] The R.S.M.R. and F.K.
cases each listed one gram of ganja-type narcotics. [@2] The sabu cases were J.
with 1.0494 grams and M.S.E.M. with 0.20 grams of sabu. [@2]

One pharmaceutical case held 490 Trihexyphenidyl pills and 545 Excimer pills,
1,035 pills in total. [@1] Other destroyed items included sharp weapons,
iron-cutting scissors, crowbars, stones, clothing, noken bags, lottery
paraphernalia, sacks and kerosene. [@1] The regent, the district attorney head,
the military district commander, the police chief and the district court head
attended the destruction. [@1]
