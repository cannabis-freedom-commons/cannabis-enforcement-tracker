Kherson police exposed a group of city residents suspected of selling cannabis
and psychotropic substances, and seized drugs valued at about 100,000 hryvnias.
[@1]

Investigators established that a 41-year-old Kherson resident and his
44-year-old female acquaintance bought prohibited substances from a 50-year-old
city resident, placing drug orders through a messenger app. [@1] The suspects
repackaged the substances at their homes, sold part of them directly to buyers
and posted the rest to other regions of Ukraine, with payments made in cash and
card transfers. [@1]

During the pretrial investigation police documented four cannabis sales, and
preliminary calculations suggested the group's monthly income from illegal
psychotropic sales could exceed 300,000 hryvnias. [@1]

At authorized searches police seized about 250 grams of cannabis, 3 grams of the
psychotropic A-PVP and 50 buprenorphine pills, plus packaging bags, electronic
scales, grinding devices, bank cards, mobile phones and a tablet. [@1] Police
also seized over 200,000 hryvnias and 1,500 US dollars in national and foreign
currencies, and estimated the black-market value of the seized drugs and
psychotropics at about 100,000 hryvnias. [@1]

The two men were detained and notified of suspicion under Part 2 of Article 307
of Ukraine's Criminal Code for illegal acquisition, storage with intent to sell
and sale of narcotic and psychotropic substances, facing up to ten years in
prison with confiscation of property. [@1]
