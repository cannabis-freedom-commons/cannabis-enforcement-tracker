Police in Toulouse arrested a squatter after the occupant called Police Secours
to report a home invasion. [@1] The man was presented to justice and sentenced
to three years in prison. [@1] He had moved into the victim's home while the
victim had been absent for several days. [@1] Investigators said he worked for a
network as a maker, accountant, storer and delivery man. [@1]

Officers seized 4,000 euros in cash and 10 kg of mixed narcotics in the case.
[@1] The dealer was described as a mid-level dealer handling several drugs,
listing cannabis alongside cocaine, ecstasy and LSD. [@1] The 10 kg total covers
all the drugs together, so no cannabis-only weight is recorded, and the seized
cash is not recorded as a fine or drug value. [@1]

The case began as a home-invasion response before officers uncovered the drug
