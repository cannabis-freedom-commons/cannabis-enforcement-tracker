State police in Bolzano arrested an adult Italian citizen after finding him with
1.5 kilograms of marijuana, reported as 1,500 grams. [@1] The arrest was the
cannabis component of checks in which officers searched four homes and reported
three young people in Bolzano. [@1] The three young people were reported for
offences against the person, property and drug legislation, without further
detail linking a specific cannabis amount to them. [@2]

In Bolzano the controls focused on the city centre, the station area, the Don
Bosco and Casanova districts and the WaltherPark and Twenty shopping centres.
[@1] Police checked about 250 people, including 35 foreign nationals and 23
minors, and inspected 73 vehicles in the Bolzano area. [@1]

The Bolzano checks were part of a large nationwide investigative operation by
the Polizia di Stato, Italy’s national police, against criminal activity
involving young people. [@1] The national operation was coordinated by the
police central operational service and aimed at crime involving young people and
minors, including in drug-dealing and nightlife areas. [@2]
