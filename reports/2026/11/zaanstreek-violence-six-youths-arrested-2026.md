Police arrested six suspects aged 17 to 19 on Wednesday 11 November over a
series of violent incidents in the Zaanstreek last July. [@2] Three are from
Zaanstad, two from Wormerland and one from Oostzaan. [@3] The incidents included
explosions at houses, arson, shootings and threats. [@2] Police said the
suspects' role was still under investigation. [@2]

In searches on the morning of the arrests, police seized data carriers for
examination. [@3] Police said there was no single conflict in the Zaanstreek but
several separate conflicts with different backgrounds, which could include
relationship problems, business disputes and drug-related or other criminal
conflicts. [@2] They did not rule out that some of the incidents were connected.
[@1] Police said the incidents had a big impact on residents. [@1]

The six arrests brought the number of suspects held in the connected
investigations to fifteen. [@3] Police said the young people had been approached
on social media and offered money to carry out violence. [@1] Police said some
of the young people were also put under pressure, manipulated or threatened to
keep going. [@4] Team chief Rick Janssen said that what begins as an apparently
simple assignment can end in serious criminal offences. [@2] Police said they
were also looking for whoever gave the orders and were investigating more than
the executors. [@2] No one was killed or seriously injured in the incidents,
which police called a wonder. [@3] Police urged parents to watch for signs that
their children were being approached. [@1]
