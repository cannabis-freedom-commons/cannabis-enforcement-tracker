Police operatives discovered three marijuana plantations in Benguet during
separate anti-illegal drug operations in the Cordillera Administrative Region
(CAR). [@1] The three plantation sites were reported in Barangays Kayapa, Bakun
and Sagubo in the municipality of Kapangan, Benguet. [@1] The marijuana
eradication operations in the province were conducted separately by operating
units. [@1] The account of the Benguet operations was attributed to the Police
Regional Office-Cordillera Administrative Region (PRO-CAR). [@1]

Officers confiscated a total of 2,230 fully grown marijuana plants from the
three Kapangan plantation sites. [@1] PRO-CAR, the Cordillera regional police
office, estimated the standard drug price of the confiscated plants at P446,000.
[@1] The P446,000 figure is the police estimate at the standard drug price, not
an independently verified market value. [@1]

The three sites were handled as separate eradication targets in the provincial
operations. [@1] Kapangan is the municipality in Benguet where all three
reported plantation sites were located. [@1] The confiscation account covers
only fully grown marijuana plants, with no other cannabis weight reported for
these sites. [@1]
