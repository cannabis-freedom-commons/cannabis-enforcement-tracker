Police near Mannarkkad in Palakkad arrested two people from Tamil Nadu with 24
kg of ganja. [@1] The two arrested were Kathirvel, 37, from Morappur in
Dharmapuri district, and Bharat, 50, from Kottappatti in Harur taluk. [@1]

Police led by Inspector Vivek with the anti-drug unit were conducting a night
vehicle check at Chirakkalpadi near Mannarkkad in Kerala. [@1] Officers stopped
a private bus coming from Kozhikode toward Palakkad and searched the passengers.
[@1] In four bags belonging to the two men, officers found 24 kg of ganja hidden
inside, which police valued at 2.40 lakh rupees. [@1]

Police are investigating where the two brought the ganja from, to whom they
planned to supply it, and whether they had smuggled drugs before. [@1]
