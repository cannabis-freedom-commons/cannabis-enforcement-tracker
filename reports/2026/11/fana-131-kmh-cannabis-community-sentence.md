A 21-year-old man has been sentenced in Hordaland tingrett, the district court
for the Bergen area, to samfunnsstraff, a community sentence served under
supervision, after driving at 131 km/h with cannabis in his blood. [@1] He was
stopped in Fritz C. Riebers veg in Fana, Bergen, after police measured him at
131 km/h; the E39 where the speeding took place runs through that part of the
city. [@1] A blood sample also showed that he had cannabis in his blood. [@1]

iBergen republished the Fanaposten story with the same lead and the same
photograph credited to Erik Støverud. [@2]
