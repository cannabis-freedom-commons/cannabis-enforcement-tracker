An 18-year-old man was arrested for drug trafficking in the afternoon of
Thursday 12 in the São João Margem Esquerda neighbourhood of Tubarão, Santa
Catarina. [@1] He had an outstanding arrest warrant for the same crime, and
officers executing it stopped his vehicle at about 16h45 when it came into view.
[@1] Officers found a bag on the seat of the vehicle with 14 portions of
marijuana totalling 320.3 grams, wrapped in clear plastic and ready for sale.
[@1] Questioned by the officers, the man said he had been delivering drugs by
phone and the marijuana was seized and he was taken to the police station. [@1]

A second outlet, Diário do Sul, reports the same stop and seizure, citing the
Military Police that the man said he did phone deliveries of narcotics. [@2]
