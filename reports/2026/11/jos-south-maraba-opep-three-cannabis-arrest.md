Troops of Operation Enduring Peace, known as OPEP, arrested three suspected
illicit drug dealers in Jos South Local Government Area of Plateau State. [@1]
The operation was conducted at about 1:00 a.m. on 11 November 2026 at Sabo Layin
in the Maraba Jama'a community, following intelligence on suspected drug
peddlers. [@1] Security sources said troops of Sector 6, deployed at Maraba
Jama'a, arrested three suspects identified as Yakubu Patrick, Emmanuel Chumm and
Joshua John. [@1]

The suspects were reportedly found in possession of a large quantity of
substances suspected to be cannabis (Cannabis sativa) and other illicit drugs.
[@1] They were held in military custody pending further action and possible
handover to the appropriate law enforcement agencies for investigation and
prosecution. [@1]

The operation forms part of ongoing OPEP efforts to dismantle criminal networks,
curb drug trafficking and prevent illicit substances from fuelling insecurity
and other criminal activities across Plateau State. [@1]
