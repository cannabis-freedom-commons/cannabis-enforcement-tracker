The Traffic and Transport Division of the Santander Police searched a building
in Girón that it said was used to distribute drugs. [@1] Officers arrested two
men, aged 23 and 32, in flagrante delicto at the premises. [@1] The search found
more than 500 doses of marijuana and 27 doses of bazuco. [@1] The detainees and
the seized substances were handed to the 6th Local Prosecutor's Office of Girón
on charges of trafficking, manufacture or possession of narcotics. [@1]

In a second operation on the Lizama–San Alberto road at kilometer 31+200 in the
Las Pampas sector of Sabana de Torres, officers stopped a truck carrying eight
plastic tanks of gasoline that was reportedly being moved illegally toward
Bucaramanga. [@1] The truck's occupants were arrested in flagrante delicto and
mobile phones were seized. [@1] Those involved were handed to the on-duty
prosecutor of Sabana de Torres on suspicion of receptation (receiving stolen
goods). [@1]

Colonel Luis Fernando Atuesta Zárate, commander of the Santander Police, said
the controls against illicit economies would continue. [@1] The arrests and
seizures in Girón and Sabana de Torres were reported as two separate operations
by the Traffic and Transport Division. [@2]
