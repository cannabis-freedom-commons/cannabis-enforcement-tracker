An 18-year-old man was arrested by Military Police during a stop in Jardim São
Jorge, in Paranavaí, on the night of Tuesday 10 November 2026, in an occurrence
recorded at about 19:43. [@1] He was arrested on suspicion of drug trafficking.
[@2] According to the Military Police, the team was patrolling the neighbourhood
when it stopped the young man. [@1]

Officers seized one Cannabis sativa plant, known locally as maconha, along with
cash, a scale and a mobile phone. [@1] They also found 110 portions of crack
weighing 21.4 grams in total and 27 portions of cocaine weighing 9.2 grams. [@2]
Part of the seized substances had already been divided into small portions. [@1]

The man was taken with the drugs and seized materials to the police station,
where the appropriate legal procedures were taken. [@1] The Military Police did
not release his identity. [@1] The case will follow the legal procedures with
the competent authorities. [@1]
