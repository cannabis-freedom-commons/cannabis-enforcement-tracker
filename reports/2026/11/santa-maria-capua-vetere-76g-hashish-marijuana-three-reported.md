Carabinieri of the Santa Maria Capua Vetere station, supported by the force's
Sarno dog unit, reported three people at liberty after targeted anti-dealing
checks across the town on 12 November 2026. [@1] Officers stopped an 18-year-old
from Portico di Caserta on foot in via Galatina and found 10.17 grams of hashish
on him after a personal search. [@1] A second account of the same checks gives
the same street stop and the same 10.17-gram amount of hashish. [@2] The
investigation then extended to home searches of a 20-year-old of Egyptian origin
living in the town and a 47-year-old woman resident there, where the drug dogs
pinpointed the stashes. [@1]

In the young man's bedroom, officers found 16.54 grams of hashish already
divided into 13 bars wrapped in foil inside a hidden backpack. [@1] The second
account confirms the 16.54 grams in 13 foil-wrapped bars hidden in the backpack
in the young man's bedroom. [@2] In the woman's home, officers recovered 19
grams of hashish, 30 grams of marijuana and a precision scale from the
dining-room table, items considered consistent with dealing. [@1] The second
account confirms the 19 grams of hashish, 30 grams of marijuana and precision
scale found in the woman's home. [@2]

All the material found was seized and placed at the disposal of the judicial
authority, to which the three suspects were referred at liberty. [@1] The
proceedings are in the preliminary-investigation phase, and the presumption of
innocence applies to everyone involved until any final conviction. [@1]
