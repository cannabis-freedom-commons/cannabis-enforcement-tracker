The two men held were a 46-year-old Ecuadorean national and a 36-year-old
Colombian national who had come from Spain. [@1] Both live in Spain and work as
delivery drivers for UPS, carrying the cannabis alongside their legal load. [@1]
They admitted acting as “mules” after collecting the load in Madrid for delivery
to a final destination. [@1]

Customs officers from the Territoire de Belfort found the drugs during checks at
the Fontaine toll on the A36 on Monday 9 November. [@1] The drugs were found
aboard a Spanish UPS truck carrying the two men. [@1] They were carrying 35 kg
of cannabis and 5 kg of resin, 40 kg in total. [@1] The drugs were packed in two
heat-sealed sports bags placed on the bunk at the rear of the cab. [@1] Belfort
prosecutor Paul-Édouard Lallois put the market value at €350,000 for the
cannabis and €40,000 for the resin. [@1]

The two men were first held in customs detention and then placed in police
custody. [@1] They were still being questioned by the gendarmerie on Tuesday and
were due to be brought before the Belfort prosecutor’s office on Wednesday. [@1]
