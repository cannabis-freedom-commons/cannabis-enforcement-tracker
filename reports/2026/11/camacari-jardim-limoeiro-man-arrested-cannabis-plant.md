Military police arrested a man in flagrante on the afternoon of Thursday 5
November 2026 on Rua Estrada 22 in the Jardim Limoeiro neighbourhood of
Camaçari, in the Salvador metropolitan region. [@2] The man ran down an alley
when he saw the Ceto Motos motorcycle patrols of the 12th Military Police
Battalion approaching, and the officers caught him. [@2] A search found a
plastic bag holding a replica Glock pistol, one round of ammunition, packaging
material for drugs and a damaged mobile phone. [@2]

Questioned by the officers, the man said he kept a cannabis plant (pé de
maconha) inside a house and claimed the plant belonged to his grandmother. [@1]
Police went to the house he indicated and seized the plant, which they described
as vegetation similar to cannabis. [@2] The officers also seized a drone in the
same incident. [@2] The suspect, the plant, the pistol replica and the other
material were taken to the 18th Territorial Police Station (18ª DT) in Camaçari,
where the incident was recorded and legal measures were taken. [@2]

The 12th Battalion and the Ceto Motos patrols belong to the Polícia Militar, the
uniformed state military police of Bahia. [@1]
