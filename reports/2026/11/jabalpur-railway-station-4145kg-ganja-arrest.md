Government Railway Police detained a youth who looked suspicious while checking
platforms 2 and 3 of Jabalpur railway station. [@1] Police identified the
detained man as Achin Kumar Dube, also called Kallu, a resident of Green City,
Dreamland Face-1 in the Madanmahal police station area. [@1] Police arrested him
and registered a case under Section 8/20 of the NDPS Act for investigation. [@1]

Officers recovered four packets of ganja from his backpack during the search.
[@1] The seized ganja weighed 4.145 kg on weighing. [@1] Police estimated the
seized ganja to be worth about Rs 103,000. [@2] The other account described the
seizure as ganja worth more than Rs 100,000 found in the youth's bag. [@1]

GRP said its teams were active and keeping watch at the station because of the
festival period. [@1] GRP said checking of stations and trains was continuing in
view of the festival season. [@2] During a Tuesday-night patrol the team
received information that a man was sitting suspiciously at the station outer
and might hold narcotics. [@2] Police said questioning about the source of the
ganja was continuing. [@2]
