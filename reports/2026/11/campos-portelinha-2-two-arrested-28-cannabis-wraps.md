Military Police arrested a man and apprehended an adolescent in an
anti-trafficking action in Portelinha 2, in the Lapa area of Campos dos
Goytacazes, on the morning of Wednesday 4 November 2026. [@1] Portal OZK places
the community in Parque Maciel and says the action was carried out by the 8th
Military Police Battalion. [@2] One of the two detained is a 14-year-old
adolescent. [@2]

According to the Military Police, teams of GAT I/1 and GAT I/2 were patrolling
when they noticed people behaving suspiciously. [@1] When the patrol cars
approached, those involved tried to flee into the community, but one adult and
one adolescent were caught. [@1] Portal OZK reports that the GAT teams, joined
by the operations officer, entered the community under an operational order and
that one suspect was caught on the top floor of a building carrying a bag while
another was found closing an apartment door. [@2]

Officers seized 28 wraps of cannabis (buchas de maconha) with the pair. [@1] The
same seizure included 244 cocaine pins, 12 bottles of loló, a homemade drink
whose name is also used for street drug mixtures, and four crack rocks. [@2]
Police also took a replica pistol, a mobile phone and R$ 1,041 in cash. [@2]

The two and the seized material were taken to the 134th Police Station in the
city centre. [@1] Portal OZK reports that the police authority kept both in
custody, booked for drug trafficking under article 33 of Law 11.343/2006,
Brazil's drug law. [@2] Police said body-worn operational cameras recorded the
action and the images were kept for any later analysis in the investigation.
[@2]
