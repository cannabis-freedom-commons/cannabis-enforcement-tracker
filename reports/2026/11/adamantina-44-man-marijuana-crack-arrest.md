Military Police arrested a 44-year-old man for drug trafficking in Adamantina,
in the interior of São Paulo, in the evening of Tuesday 3 November 2026. [@2]
Officers of the 25th Interior Military Police Battalion went to a house in the
city after an anonymous tip that drugs were being sold there, according to the
police account published later. [@2]

When the man saw the officers, he tried to go back inside the house but was
stopped in front of it. [@1] A search found two marijuana portions weighing 1.97
grams, along with 16 crack portions weighing 4.67 grams and a mobile phone. [@2]

The man was arrested and taken to the Adamantina police station, where he
remained held at the disposal of the courts. [@1]
