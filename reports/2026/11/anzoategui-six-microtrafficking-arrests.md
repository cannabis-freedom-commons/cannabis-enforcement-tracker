Police in Anzoátegui state detained six people across several municipalities on
suspicion of microtrafficking, the small-scale street sale of drugs. [@1]
Officers of the Criminal Investigation Service arrested a 37-year-old man in the
Las Casitas sector of Barcelona after he acted suspiciously on noticing the
patrol, and found a wrapper holding 25.9 grams of suspected cannabis in his
right armpit. [@1]

In the Los Cerezos sector of Bruzual municipality, Clarines police stopped a
60-year-old man riding a motorcycle and seized eight wrappers of suspected
cannabis from him. [@1] Other units made four more arrests in the same roundup,
three of them over suspected crack, a drug the reports call creepy, and a
24-year-old found with a wrapper of suspected cocaine. [@1] One of those
detained, a 42-year-old held in the Andrés Bello sector of El Tigre, had five
prior records for other crimes in the SIIPOL police database. [@1]

All six were handed to the Public Prosecutor's Office for the corresponding
proceedings. [@2] The arrests were reported by QBNoticias, citing the outlet
NDA, and by Noticias de Aquí on 8 November 2026. [@2]
