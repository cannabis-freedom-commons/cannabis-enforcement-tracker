Istanbul police narcotics teams carried out a large-scale anti-drug operation in
Pendik, a district of Istanbul. [@1] The operation centred on a suspicious
vehicle that narcotics officers had identified for inspection. [@1] The teams
act within the Istanbul Emniyet Müdürlüğü, the city's provincial police
directorate. [@1] Officers detained one suspect linked to the incident as the
operation continued. [@1] The force said it was maintaining a determined stance
to prevent drug trafficking in the area. [@1]

A detailed search of the vehicle recovered 336 kilos 550 grams of skunk. [@1]
Police recorded the 336.55-kilo haul as the result of the work against drugs in
the district. [@1] Investigators described the quantity found in the inspection
as a large amount of narcotic substance. [@1]

The Istanbul Provincial Police Directorate said its fight against traffickers
who endanger young people and the future of society would continue
uncompromisingly. [@1] It said anti-trafficking operations would continue with
determination and increase. [@1] Police added that they would give no
opportunity to dealers trying to poison young people and the future. [@1]
