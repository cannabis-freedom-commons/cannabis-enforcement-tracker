During the previous 24 hours, officers of the Guardia Civil Municipal of Soledad
de Graciano Sánchez detained five men and a 17-year-old for their probable
participation in delitos contra la salud, offences against health. [@1]

Following the protocols for minors, the 17-year-old was detained in the
municipal centre for possessing a bag of suspected marijuana. [@1] Héctor “N”,
19, Rafael “N”, 30, and Jair “N”, 29 were detained for being found with bags of
suspected marijuana in the colonias San Francisco and San José. [@1] Juan “N”,
38, and Carlos “N”, 20 were detained during patrols in the colonias El Polvorín
and 21 de Marzo for possessing doses of suspected methamphetamine. [@1]

After officers informed the detainees of the reason for their detention, they
were taken to the Fiscalía General del Estado with the seized substances. [@1]
