The incident happened on Wednesday 11 November just before 11pm in Forest Town.
[@1] McDonald's staff in Forest Town alerted police to a strong smell of
cannabis coming from a vehicle at the drive-thru, and officers stopped the car
shortly afterwards. [@1] Inside the car, officers found packets of cannabis, a
quantity of cash and three mobile phones on one of the teenagers. [@1] A search
of the vehicle also found cannabis stashed under the driver’s seat and in the
glove box. [@1] More cannabis and codeine syrup were also found stashed in the
back seats. [@1]

The 18-year-old driver failed a roadside drugs test and was arrested on
suspicion of drug driving and possession with intent to supply a Class B drug.
[@1] The two other teenagers, also aged 18, were arrested on suspicion of
possession with intent to supply a Class B drug. [@1] Officers then visited
their home addresses and seized a further large quantity of cannabis, deal bags
and a quantity of cash. [@1] All three were placed on strict conditional bail
while further inquiries are made. [@1]

Detective Sergeant Clint Gloyn commented on the stop and search. [@1] He said
the result came from two response officers working very closely with McDonald’s
to secure the stop and search. [@1] He said driving while under the influence of
drugs is reckless and puts other road users at risk of serious harm or death.
[@1]

A second account gives the same date and place, reporting the incident on 11
