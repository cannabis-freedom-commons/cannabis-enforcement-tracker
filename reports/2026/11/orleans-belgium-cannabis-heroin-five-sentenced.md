Five men aged twenty to twenty-five were convicted by the Orléans criminal court
for drug trafficking. [@1] In the dock the defendants sometimes exchanged smiles
and some asserted detachment from the charges against them. [@1] Four appeared
in custody at the hearing on Monday 2 November before the Orléans criminal
court, while the fifth was free. [@1] They answered charges over a major
trafficking operation centred on Orléans and extending across Loiret and the
wider region. [@1]

They were accused of importing several kilos of cannabis resin and heroin from
Belgium and the Netherlands. [@1] The alleged trafficking period ran from
February to July 2026. [@1] Orléans police received an anonymous tip in March
about a convoy of at least two vehicles making return trips between Belgium, the
Netherlands and the Orléans area. [@1] Two defendants were specifically named as
being present in those vehicles. [@1]
