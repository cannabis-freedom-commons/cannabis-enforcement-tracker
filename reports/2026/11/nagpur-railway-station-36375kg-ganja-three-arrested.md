Government Railway Police arrested three young men at Nagpur railway station
during a Tuesday afternoon patrol. [@1] The three were identified as 22-year-old
Rajkumar Bramha Giri, 20-year-old Bultakumar Rama and 19-year-old Arbaz Ansari
Sarfuddin Ansari, all residents of Singpatti Jungle in Kushinagar district,
Uttar Pradesh. [@1] Officers spotted the three suspicious youths at around 2 pm
on the platform by the station manager's office. [@1]

The youths were carrying three trolley bags and two rucksacks and could give no
satisfactory answer about their contents. [@1] A search of the bags found 32
heavy bundles bound in khaki-coloured tape giving off a strong smell of ganja,
which the detained men confirmed was ganja. [@1] Officers recovered a total of
36.375 kg of ganja from the five bags, which police valued at Rs 18,17,750. [@1]
Including three mobile phones taken from the accused, the total seizure was
valued at Rs 18,52,750. [@1]

Police recorded the seizure under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances
Act and described it as the station's largest action against ganja smuggling in
nearly two years. [@1] The GRP said it was still investigating where the ganja
had been brought from and to whom it was to be supplied. [@1]
