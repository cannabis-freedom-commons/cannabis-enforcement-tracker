Laksam railway police in Cumilla district said their special drives against
drugs and smuggling in the first eight months of the current government produced
28 cases and 19 arrests. [@1] A second account, drawn from the station's
official record of cases and drives, put the eight months from January to
October at 20 cases and 20 arrests. [@2]

Police said the eight months produced 130.7 kilograms of ganja in all, along
with 47 yaba tablets, 60 litres of chholai mod, a locally distilled liquor, and
smuggled goods. [@1] In January and February officers arrested two men in two
separate drug cases and seized 6 kilograms of ganja. [@1] In May, four drug
cases brought 10.9 kilograms of ganja and eight yaba tablets, and in June
officers seized 30.9 kilograms of ganja. [@1] July brought 7 kilograms of ganja,
30 litres of chholai mod and smuggled Indian syrup, chocolate and whisky, and
August another 52.9 kilograms of ganja and 39 yaba tablets. [@1] September
seizures included 21 kilograms of ganja and October 2 kilograms. [@1]

That account said the cases were filed under section 36(1) of the Narcotics
Control Act 2018, where schedule 19 sets the penalty band for ganja, schedule 10
for yaba and schedule 24 for chholai mod. [@2] The officer in charge, Rajib
Chandra Kar, said the drive against drugs and smuggling was continuing and that
police were working to keep the eastern railway free of crime. [@1]
