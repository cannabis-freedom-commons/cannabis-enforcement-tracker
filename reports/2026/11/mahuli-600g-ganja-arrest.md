In Sant Kabir Nagar, Mahuli police arrested one accused with 600 grams of
illegal ganja on Tuesday as part of an ongoing campaign against the trafficking
of narcotic substances. [@1] The accused was identified as Ramjiyawan Yadav, the
son of Ramnaresh Yadav. [@1] Yadav is a resident of Phoolui Semrauna village,
which falls under the limits of Dhanaghata police station. [@1]

At the time of the arrest, a police team led by station in-charge Harendra
Pathak was conducting checks of suspicious persons and vehicles in the area, and
officers detained Yadav during that checking drive. [@1] A search of his
possession then recovered 600 grams of illegal ganja. [@1]

Police registered a case against him under the NDPS Act and completed all the
required legal formalities. [@1] Officers then produced him before the court,
which remanded him to judicial custody in the district jail. [@1]
