Six people were arrested in the operation, named Packman 26, and accused of drug
trafficking, threats and membership of a criminal organisation. [@1,3] The Guardia
Civil, working with the Local Police of San Pedro del Pinatar, dismantled a
group suspected of drug trafficking and shut a retail drug sales point in Lo
Pagán. [@1] The investigation began in summer after neighbours complained about
a suspected drug sales point in a house next to Villananitos beach. [@2,3] The
group was allegedly led by the married couple living in the house, who used
collaborators, described by the Guardia Civil as suspected drug addicts, to
distribute the drugs and keep watch. [@2] Officers said some sales took place in
front of the couple's minor children, and that group members threatened
customers over debts, sometimes at their family homes. [@2,3]

A judge authorised a search of the house, where officers found quantities of
hashish, the cannabis product in this case, along with cocaine, heroin and MDA.
[@2,3] Officers also seized 250 euros in small-denomination notes, two precision
scales, plastic bag cuttings and equipment for preparing cocaine and consuming
drugs. [@2,3] The search also found numerous bladed weapons, including machetes
and folding knives, in different rooms of the house. [@2,3] Police initially
arrested the woman living in the house and two collaborators, then three more
men, bringing the total to six. [@2,3]

The operation closed the sales point and dismantled the organisation based in
San Pedro del Pinatar. [@1]
