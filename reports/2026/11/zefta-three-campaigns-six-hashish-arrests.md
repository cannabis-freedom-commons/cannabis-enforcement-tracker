Detectives at Zefta police station in Gharbia Governorate arrested six suspects
in three separate campaigns against drug trafficking. [@1] Officers found
quantities of hydro and hashish with the suspects, along with a shotgun,
bullets, bladed weapons, cash and mobile phones. [@1]

Gharbia security director Rashad Farouk was told of secret intelligence that
several people were trading drugs and using their home areas to sell to their
clients. [@1] Gharbia criminal investigation chief Mohamed Assem ordered a team
to verify the information and identify those involved, under Mohamed Saqr, head
of the Zefta-Santa investigation branch. [@1]

The first campaign at Qibli Army Street arrested two men identified as A.A. and
A.S. on suspicion of drug trafficking. [@1] A search of those two found a large
amount of hydro and hashish prepared for sale, plus money, a shotgun, bullets,
phones and a motorcycle. [@1] A second operation in the Kafr Anan area arrested
a suspect identified as K.A., who police said had an earlier criminal record.
[@1] A search of that suspect found hydro and hashish, plus money, a bladed
weapon and a phone. [@1] A third campaign in Al-Maazoun, part of Kafr Anan,
arrested three men identified as M.A., F.A. and A.M. with hydro and hashish,
cash, bladed weapons and phones, bringing the total to six. [@1]

Faced with the findings, the suspects admitted holding the drugs for trafficking
and holding the weapons to defend that trade, according to the arrest reports.
[@1] Police held the seized items, filed reports on the three incidents and
notified the Public Prosecution to investigate and take legal action against the
suspects. [@1]
