Customs officers at Pune International Airport intercepted an Indian national
arriving from Bangkok on the evening of 4 November 2026. [@2,8,10,11,12,14,15,16,18,19,20,21,23,24,25,26,28] The passenger had
arrived on Air India Express flight IX-291 and was stopped after passenger
profiling and suspicious scanning of his check-in baggage. [@2,3,7,8,12,13,14,15,22,23] The interception
was carried out by officers of the Air Intelligence Unit and the Airport Batch
of Pune Customs. [@2,3,12,15,20,23]

Officers searching his check-in trolley bag found nine rectangular,
silver-coloured packets labelled ‘Freeze Dried Monthong Durian’ hidden among
noodle packets. [@1,8,11,12,14,15,16,17,18,19,21,22,23,24,25,26,29,32] Each packet was further lined with two layers of
vacuum-sealed black polythene to mask detection. [@2,8,10,11,12,22,23,24] On opening, the packets
held yellowish-green dried floral tops emitting a strong pungent odour, which a
narcotic-drug field testing kit confirmed as marijuana/ganja hydroponic weed.
[@2,11,12,16,19,23] The consignment weighed 10.642 kilograms, which Customs valued at Rs 3.72
crore. [@2,4,5,7,8,9,10,11,12,14,15,16,17,18,19,20,21,23,25,27,30,31]

Customs seized the weed along with its packing materials under the Narcotic
Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, 1985. [@1,8,12,13,16,19,23] The passenger was arrested and
produced before a judicial magistrate, who granted him judicial custody till 18
November. [@2,4,6,8,9,10,11,12,14,15,16,18,21,23,24,25] Further investigation is under way to bust the illicit syndicate
involved in the smuggling attempt. [@3,4,18]

The arrested flyer was 45 years old. [@4]

Further investigation is under way to identify and track down the smuggling network involved. [@8,19]

The seizure was carried out under the direction of Dheeraj More, Pune Customs' Deputy Commissioner of Police. [@11,14,16,23]

Following statutory procedures under the NDPS Act, 1985, officers found the contraband hidden among noodle packets and packaged inside nine rectangular silver-coloured packets bearing 'Freeze Dried Monthong Durian' labels. [@2]

The cannabis had a market value of about Rs 35 lakh per kilogram. [@21]

Mumbai Samachar carried the report as PTI copy in Gujarati, adding that Customs officers are searching for other members of the syndicate. [@22]

The Times of India reported that the arrested flyer was 45 years old. [@4] Customs officers intercepted him after noticing his suspicious behaviour during passenger profiling. [@4] On 27 October, customs officials seized 381.27 grams of hashish from another passenger arriving on the same Air India Express flight from Bangkok, concealed in his shoes. [@4]
