Avignon police arrested a BTS commerce student from a school in Vaucluse in
October 2026 for suspected drug trafficking and violence against a police
officer. [@1] The student arrived at a suspect flat during the operation and
refused to be checked. [@1] He punched a plainclothes officer carrying out the
check in the face. [@1] He said he did not know the man was a police officer
because he was in civilian clothes with nothing to suggest he was police. [@1] A
court recently refused the student's request for release under judicial
supervision. [@1]

Avignon officers had been watching movements around a building and a flat in a
working-class district. [@1] After following a young man, officers seized nearly
3 kilos of cannabis and 20 grams of synthetic products from his bag. [@1]
Officers then searched the suspect flat and found narcotics stored in the
refrigerator. [@1] Police described the flat as one where the refrigerator was
full of drugs. [@1]

The cannabis amount recorded here is the nearly 3 kilos, while the 20 grams of
synthetic products are mentioned only to explain the mixed seizure and are
excluded from cannabis figures. [@1]
