Dera police arrested two suspects in separate operations in Dera Town and Kirri
Khaisour areas of Dera Ismail Khan. [@1] In Dera Town, officers detained Awwal
Khan, son of Azam Gul and a resident of Barki Town, during a crackdown on drug
trafficking and illegal arms possession. [@1] In Kirri Khaisour, officers
arrested Shakoor, son of Abdul Hameed and a resident of Malla Khel, for alleged
illegal possession of arms. [@1]

From Awwal Khan's possession, police recovered 2,380 grams of hashish, 250 grams
of opium and a 30-bore pistol with 20 rounds. [@1] From Shakoor, police
recovered a 30-bore pistol with five rounds. [@1] In another action in the same
Kirri Khaisour case, police recovered a second 30-bore pistol with nine rounds
while raids continued to arrest a suspect linked to that additional recovery.
[@1]

A case was registered against Awwal Khan and further investigation was under
way. [@1] Police officials said operations against drug trafficking and illegal
weapons would continue without discrimination. [@1]
