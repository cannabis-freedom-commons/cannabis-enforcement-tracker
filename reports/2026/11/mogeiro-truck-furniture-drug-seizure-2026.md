Military police found almost a tonne of drugs inside flat-pack furniture carried
in a truck in the rural zone of Mogeiro, in the interior of Paraíba, on Monday 9
November 2026. [@1] One occupant of the truck was arrested at the time of the
seizure. [@1]

According to the military police, several kinds of drugs were being carried
hidden in compartments of the furniture, such as cocaine and marijuana. [@1] Two
other suspects fled during the police action. [@1] The truck had been reported
by people complaining about irregular transport, and officers inspected the
vehicle and found the material. [@1]

There was an exchange of gunfire between the suspects and the military police,
and nobody was injured. [@1] The arrested occupant was to be taken to the
judicial police for the applicable procedures. [@1] The authorities said the
full weighing of the drugs was still to be completed to confirm the exact amount
seized. [@1]

The Repórter PB page also published the same account from G1 PB on 9 November
2026. [@2]
