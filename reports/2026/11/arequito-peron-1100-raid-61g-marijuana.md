Santa Fe's Policía de Investigaciones (PDI) carried out an operation in Arequito
as part of an aggravated-threats investigation. [@1] The judicial measure took
place on Thursday morning at a house on J. D. Perón 1100, led by PDI officers
from Distrito Casilda with tactical support from the Grupo de Operaciones
Tácticas (GOT) of Unidad Regional IV. [@1] The investigation began from a
threats complaint in which the victim reported verbal intimidation and
intimidation through social networks. [@2]

During the house search, officers seized a 16-gauge shotgun, a crossbow,
ammunition of different calibres, mobile phones, a precision scale, a Honda Wave
motorcycle and a motorcycle engine. [@1] Officers also found three packages of
plant material that preliminary field tests found positive for marijuana,
weighing 61.7 grams in total. [@2]

On the order of prosecutor Juan Pablo Oggero, a 21-year-old man was detained and
taken to the Unidad Regional IV departmental jail. [@3] A 17-year-old also
involved was transferred to the Juvenile Affairs Division in Rosario under the
protocols for minors. [@1] The proceedings were placed at the disposal of the
Ministerio Público de la Acusación (MPA) in Casilda in a case preliminarily
classified as aggravated threats. [@1]
