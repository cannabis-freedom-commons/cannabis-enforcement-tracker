Officers of the criminal brigade of the Sidi Bel Abbes wilaya police seized
225.4 kilograms of treated kif, reported as kif traité, and arrested two people
allegedly involved during an operation in the week before 2 November 2026. [@1]
The same police account, also republished with attribution to the APS press
agency, gives the same seizure of 225.4 kg and two arrests in Sidi Bel-Abbes.
[@2] The case began after police received information that two individuals in
the city of Sidi Bel-Abbes were holding a quantity of kif traité intended for
sale. [@1]

Police then put a security plan into place that led to the arrest of both
suspects and recovery of the reported drug quantity. [@1] Alongside the
cannabis, officers seized 300,000 Algerian dinars which police described as
income from the trafficking. [@1]

The investigation was conducted under the supervision of the public prosecutor
attached to the Sidi Bel-Abbes court. [@1] Once all stages of the investigation
were completed, both suspects were presented before the territorially competent
court. [@1]
