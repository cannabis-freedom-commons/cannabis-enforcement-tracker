Police in the Barhi police station area of Katni district arrested a 60-year-old
man and seized 1 kg 381 g of ganja from him. [@1] Police valued the seized ganja
at about 13,000 rupees. [@1] A case was registered against him under the NDPS
Act, India's narcotic drugs law. [@1]

Officers seized the ganja from a bag he was carrying. [@1] Police found him
sitting under a tree off the road in the Barhi police station area. [@1]
