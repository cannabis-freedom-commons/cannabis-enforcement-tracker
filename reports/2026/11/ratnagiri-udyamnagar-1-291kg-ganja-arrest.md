Ratnagiri city police detained a 27-year-old man accused of keeping a stock of
ganja for sale in the Udyamnagar area of Ratnagiri. [@1] Police identified the
suspect as Sahil Samir Solkar, 27, a resident of Konkan Nagar, Ratnagiri. [@1]
They seized 1 kg 291 g of ganja from him, along with other material, and put the
total value of the haul at 80,100 rupees. [@1]

The action was taken at around 4.33 pm on Sunday, 8 November, in the Kazi Chal
area of Udyamnagar. [@1] Officers checked him after they noticed he was
illegally holding a substance resembling ganja for sale, and found two separate
stashes. [@1] One parcel, wrapped in sticky khaki tape, held 910 g of ganja that
police valued at 60,000 rupees. [@1] A second blue plastic bag held 381 g of
ganja valued at 20,000 rupees, and a black travel bag used to carry it was
seized at 100 rupees. [@1]

A police constable, Rajesh Bhaskarrao Bhujbalrao, 55, filed the complaint at
Ratnagiri city police station. [@1] A case was registered against the suspect
under sections 8(c) and 20(b)(ii)(B) of the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic
Substances Act, 1985, as Crime No. 463/2026. [@1] The offence was recorded at
10.57 pm on 8 November, and Ratnagiri city police are investigating further.
