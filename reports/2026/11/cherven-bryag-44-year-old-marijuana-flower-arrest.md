Police in Cherven Bryag, Pleven Province, detained a 44-year-old man for drug
possession, as reported in the Interior Ministry's Pleven bulletin. [@1] On 5
November a search of his person found a plastic container holding one dried
green herbal flower. [@1]

In a follow-up search at his home officers found a plastic box with 11 more
dried herbal flowers in it. [@1] The man said the substance was marijuana for
personal use and handed it over voluntarily under a protocol. [@1] A physical
and chemical expert examination was still to be assigned to establish the
substance, and no weight was published. [@1] The examination was meant to
establish the type and composition of the seized material, which the bulletin
described as handed over rather than seized. [@3]

Police opened a fast-track criminal proceeding for an offence under article
354a, paragraph 5, in connection with paragraph 3, point 1 of the Criminal Code,
and notified the duty prosecutor at the Pleven district prosecutor's office.
[@1] An investigating officer of the Cherven Bryag district police department is
continuing the case. [@1] The accounts came from the Pleven regional directorate
of the Interior Ministry. [@2]

Some outlets headlined the case as a large quantity of drugs, though no source
gives a weight for the material. [@4]
