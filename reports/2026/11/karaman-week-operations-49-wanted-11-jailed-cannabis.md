Karaman gendarmerie carried out province-wide checks and operations between 6
and 12 November 2026. [@1]

The gendarmerie's searches seized 5 grams of esrar, 379 grams of hemp and skunk
plant material, 297 synthetic tablets and a drug-use device. [@1] In the
cultural-property smuggling probe, investigators seized 101 coins and 72
historical objects. [@1] Officers also seized 20 litres of illicit ethanol
alcohol, alcohol-making kits and various excavation tools. [@1]

JASAT and public-order teams checked 18,526 people and 8,425 vehicles during the
operations. [@1] Forty-nine people wanted for various crimes were caught and
sent to judicial authorities. [@1] Eleven of the suspects were remanded in
custody and sent to prison. [@1]
