Staff at Collins Bay Institution seized packages containing contraband and
unauthorized items between October 30 and November 4, 2026. [@2] Collins Bay
Institution is a multi-level security federal prison in Kingston, Ontario. [@2]
The packages held tobacco, marijuana, cannabis concentrate, drug paraphernalia,
an edged weapon, a cell phone and accessories. [@2]

Correctional Service Canada put the total estimated institutional value of the
seizures at $593,253. [@2] The agency describes the figure as institutional
value, its own estimate of what the items are worth inside the prison. [@1] No
arrests, charges or separate cannabis weights were announced in either account.
[@1]

The Correctional Service of Canada says it uses ion scanners and drug-detector
dogs to search buildings, personal property, inmates and visitors. [@2] The
agency says it has heightened measures against contraband entering its
institutions and works with police against those who try to introduce it. [@2]
The agency runs an anonymous toll-free tip line for information on drug use or
trafficking that threatens visitors, inmates and staff. [@1] Mirage News
republished the Correctional Service Canada release about the same seizures.
[@1]
