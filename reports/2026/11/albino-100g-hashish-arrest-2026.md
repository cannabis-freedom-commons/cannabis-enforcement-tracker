Clusone Carabinieri arrested a 27-year-old man of Moroccan origin in the wooded
area of Albino on Tuesday 3 November 2026 during an anti-drug control. [@1]
Officers noticed the man near a tent hidden among the trees and stopped him
immediately after he tried to walk away on seeing them. [@1] He was arrested on
suspicion of possessing drugs for the purpose of dealing. [@2]

The search recovered 52 grams of cocaine hidden in a jar placed inside a rubbish
bag. [@1] The search also recovered 100 grams of hashish kept in a packet of
crisps. [@1] Two bags with personal effects were found near the man, while a
knife and a pair of scissors were found inside the tent. [@1] Officers also
found 220 euros in cash on the 27-year-old. [@1]

A second account confirms the same seizure of cocaine in a jar and hashish in a
crisps packet together with the knife, scissors and cash. [@2] The control was
part of territorial action in the Albino woods aimed at combating drug dealing.
