Panvel city police arrested Siddharth Ramdas More, 41, after raiding a flat in
the MMRDA colony at Palaspe Phata in Panvel, Raigad district. [@1] Senior Police
Inspector Bala Kumbhar of Panvel city police station received secret information
that a stash of ganja was being kept in the MMRDA colony. [@1]

Officers raided flat number 531 in the R-2, C wing, and a search of the flat
found the ganja hidden in the bathroom. [@1] Officers seized 4.255 kg of ganja.
[@1] Police valued the seized ganja at Rs 2,12,700. [@1] An electronic weighing
scale, plastic pouches, a stapler and pins were also seized. [@1] Police suspect
the accused made small bundles of ganja at home and sold them. [@1]

The raid was carried out by a team under Bala Kumbhar that included Police
Inspector Hazrat Pathan, Head Constable Prakash Patil, Police Naik Samrat Daki,
constables Abhijit Gadkari and Vishal Dudh, and woman constable Sushila Pawar.
[@1] A case was registered at Panvel city police station. [@1] Police are
investigating where the accused obtained the ganja and to whom he would have
sold it. [@1] Police said the case could expose the involvement of others in the
drug supply chain. [@1]
