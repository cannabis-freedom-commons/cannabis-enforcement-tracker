Salt police arrested a youth with 11.745 kg of ganja during a vehicle check.
[@1] The check was held on 4 November at a bend about one kilometre before Jhad
village. [@3] Officers stopped an EcoSport Ford car numbered HR10-AB-4777 and
searched it. [@3] The driver was 23-year-old Shubham Singh Rawat of Gwali
village in Salt. [@3] His current address was given as Shakti Nagar, Sector 13,
Chandigarh. [@2]

Police recovered 11.745 kg of ganja from his possession. [@1] Officers also
seized 38 packets of pine-bark sawdust and 60 packets of pine-bark blocks from
the car. [@1] Police put the value of the seized ganja at about Rs 293,625. [@2]
The car used for smuggling was seized. [@2]

Police registered case number 22/2026 against him under NDPS Act sections
8/20/60 and Indian Forest Act sections 26/42. [@3] Police said they are
questioning him about the sources of the ganja trade and others involved. [@3]
