Sector-63 police in Noida arrested two men from Chhijarsi on Tuesday morning, in
separate stops under the metro line near the Sector-62 roundabout. [@1] Police
described the stops as action against ganja trafficking. [@1] Police recovered
600 grams of ganja from one man and 590 grams from the other. [@1] Additional
Deputy Commissioner of Police Abhay Kumar Mishra said an FIR had been registered
in each case and that both accused had been arrested. [@1]

The first arrest was near pillar number 39C, beneath the metro line on the road
that leads toward the Sector-62 roundabout. [@1] Officers caught Sunil, also
known as Kasim, a 35-year-old resident of Chhijarsi. [@1] A search found 600
grams of ganja on him. [@1]

A second police team made the next arrest the same morning. [@1] They arrested
Shiva Kumar, 22, also a resident of Chhijarsi, from a green belt near pillar
number 36A. [@1] That spot is on the road running from the Sector-62 roundabout
toward SJM Hospital. [@1] Police recovered 590 grams of ganja and 330 rupees
from him. [@1]
