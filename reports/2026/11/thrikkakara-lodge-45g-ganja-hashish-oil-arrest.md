Police arrested 42-year-old Noushad K from a lodge on Kannampuzha Road near
Marottichuvadu in Thrikkakara. [@1] City police said two people were arrested in
separate drug raids conducted within the Thrikkakara and Kalamassery police
station limits. [@1] The Malayalam account identifies the Thrikkakara accused as
Noushad K, aged 42, of Korattiyil house in Ottapalam, Palapuram, Palakkad. [@2]
The Kochi City narcotics cell ACP-led DANSAF team made the arrests. [@2]

He was allegedly carrying 45.29g of ganja and 2.9g of hashish oil. [@1] He was
also allegedly carrying 2.06g of MDMA, which is excluded from the cannabis
total. [@1] Police also seized Rs 90,000 allegedly earned through drug sales.
[@1]

Police said the accused would be produced at the stations and cases would be
registered. [@2]
