On 7 November 2026, Marseille police tracked two cars believed to supply the
Campagne-Lévêque street sale and observed one of them receive a suspicious box
at the square of the church in Les Chartreux, in the 4th arrondissement. [@1]
The next day, when a similar hand-off involving the second car unfolded, police
seized two men, aged 20 and 34, and a search of the stash apartment found around
10 kg of cannabis resin, 5 kg of cannabis herb and more than 1,700 euros in
cash. [@1] The tenant couple of the apartment, used as a base to store cannabis
sold at the Campagne-Lévêque estate, were ruled out of suspicion. [@1]
