Agents of the Reserved Service (P2) of the 10th Military Police Battalion
carried out an intelligence operation in the region. [@1] The seizure happened
in Casinhas Populares, in the Arcozelo district of Paty do Alferes, near Rua
Quatorze. [@2] Lookouts positioned on high ground spotted the team and warned
others, spoiling the surprise. [@1] An unidentified man fled on foot and
abandoned a plastic bag before disappearing. [@2] No suspect was arrested,
identified or taken to the police station. [@2]

Officers recovered the abandoned bag containing 38 cannabis strips and ten
cocaine packets. [@1] Police said the cannabis weighed about 209 grams, while
the cocaine totaled about 12.7 grams. [@2]

The seized material was presented at the 96th Police Station, where the case was
recorded as a drug seizure. [@1]
