Border Guard Bangladesh seized Indian liquor and ganja in special anti-drug
operations on the Parshuram and Chhagalnaiya border in Feni. [@1] Members of the
Jayantinagar, Guthuma and Debpur border outposts of Feni Battalion (4 BGB)
carried out the operations in the Parshuram and Chhagalnaiya border area. [@1]
The operations took place on Thursday, 5 November, in three separate
border-outpost operations. [@1] The operations recovered a large consignment of
Indian liquor and ganja. [@1]

BGB members maintain regular surveillance and operations in the border area to
prevent drug smuggling and to stop Indian drugs from entering illegally. [@1]
This operation was conducted on secret information as part of that continued
preventive effort. [@1] BGB put the seizure value of the recovered drugs at Tk
197,800, a combined amount for the liquor and ganja together. [@1]

The reports published no separate quantity for the ganja or the liquor. [@1]
They also gave no detailed information on whether anyone was detained during the
operations. [@1]
