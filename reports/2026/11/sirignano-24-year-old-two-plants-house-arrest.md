Carabinieri of the station in Baiano arrested a 24-year-old man from Sirignano,
holding him responsible for possessing and cultivating a narcotic substance.
[@1] The arrest followed an overnight search of the young man's home. [@2]
During that search the soldiers found and seized two cannabis indica plants,
more than a metre tall, with flowering tops they described as suitable for
producing marijuana, together with about 35 grams of the same narcotic
substance. [@1]

What was found was placed under seizure. [@1] The 24-year-old, already known to
the police, was declared under arrest and, on the order of the public
prosecutor's office in Avellino, placed under house arrest, a restrictive
measure that keeps him at home. [@1]

The case remained at the preliminary-investigation stage, and the presumption of
innocence still applied to him. [@3] Officers seized both of the two indica
plants and the roughly 35 grams of marijuana found in the home with them. [@3]
