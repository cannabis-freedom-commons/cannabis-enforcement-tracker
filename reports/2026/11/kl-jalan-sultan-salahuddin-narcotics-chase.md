A police officer from the Dang Wangi district narcotics criminal investigation
division, based in Kuala Lumpur, was seen on a viral video holding what appeared
to be a pistol while chasing a car along Jalan Sultan Salahuddin. [@1,2] The Dang
Wangi district police chief, Assistant Commissioner Ahmad Azwan Amin Nordin,
said the team first spotted a suspicious black Honda Accord at about 6.30pm.
[@1]

When officers approached the car for inspection, the driver allegedly sped off
recklessly and collided with a blue Honda City while trying to escape. [@1]
Police found the Accord again at about 6.40pm at the Jalan Sultan Salahuddin
intersection, and two men got out and fled the scene. [@1]

Ahmad Azwan said an initial inspection of the car found no illegal items, and
police were looking for the two men to assist the investigation. [@1] The video,
one minute and 13 seconds long and posted on Threads, showed traffic stopped at
a red light while a man carrying the pistol-like object chased a vehicle through
the intersection, drawing questions on social media. [@1]

Police said they detected the video the day after the chase and in effect
confirmed the incident had happened on Saturday. [@1]

Sinar Harian reported that the pursuit happened during Op Tapis Khas, a special filtering operation, mounted by officers of the Dang Wangi district police headquarters narcotics criminal investigation division. [@3] Dang Wangi district police chief Assistant Commissioner Ahmad Azwan Amin Nordin said in a statement issued on Sunday that police were still tracking down the two men who fled from the car. [@3]
