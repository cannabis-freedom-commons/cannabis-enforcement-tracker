On Monday 2 November 2026, officers assigned to the Alès police station arrested
a young adult man on Rue du Lozère, a street inside the Cévennes district of
Alès. [@1] The officers said they first noticed the young man because his
behaviour on Rue du Lozère appeared suspicious and drew their attention. [@1]
When they stopped and searched him, they discovered both cocaine and cannabis
resin in his possession. [@1]

Because of that discovery, the officers placed the young man in garde à vue.
[@1] Midi Libre presents this Monday arrest as another example of a recurring
local pattern in which young adults brought in from outside Alès are used as
expendable chair à canon to keep the wheels of the local narcotics trade
turning. [@1]

The account places both the suspicious behaviour that prompted the check and the
subsequent arrest on Rue du Lozère inside the Cévennes district. [@1] He is
identified only as a jeune majeur, a young adult just over the age of majority.
[@1] The officers involved were all assigned to the commissariat of Alès, the
town police station. [@1]
