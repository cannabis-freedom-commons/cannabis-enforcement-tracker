Military police arrested two men, identified as Vladimir and Meyzon, in the
early hours of 7 November 2026 on suspicion of using a drone to carry drugs and
mobile phones to a prison in Mato Grosso do Sul. [@1] Meyzon was to pilot the
drone while Vladimir watched police movements and supported the operation, and a
third man, Uenderson, fled when officers approached. [@1] The two detained men
said they had been hired to collect the material on Rua Guaicurus and take it
near a prison unit so the drone could make deliveries without entering the
facility. [@1] The two men were taken to the police station at about 4 a.m.,
with the car and cannabis placed under the responsibility of Denar, the
specialised narcotics-repression unit. [@1]

Officers stopped the suspects near Rua Dom João VI and Rua Jandaia after
noticing the Ford Ka driver's reaction to the patrol car and finding a
switched-on drone controller inside the vehicle. [@1] Officers then watched the
aircraft land in a nearby vacant lot, where they found two portions of cannabis,
packed mobile phones, batteries and accessories used to carry and release
objects from the air. [@1] Police seized 934 grams of cannabis, six mobile
phones, five batteries, a DJI Air 3S drone and the Ford Ka used by the suspects.
[@2] Military police put the total value of the material taken out of
circulation at R$97,000. [@1]

The occurrence record did not identify which prison was to receive the products,
who hired the service, how much would be paid or whether the group had made
other deliveries. [@1]

In 2026 the 9th Military Police Battalion seized four drones and arrested 10 people around the Campo Grande prison complex in Jardim Noroeste who were trying to launch illicit items. [@3] Between Friday 6 and Saturday 7 November 2026, three people were arrested trying to launch drugs and mobile phones into the Maximum Security Prison. [@3] Officers seized more than one kilogram of marijuana, seven mobile phones, batteries, a precision scale, adhesive tape, scissors, a drone and a Ford Ka from the suspects. [@3] A commander of the 9th Battalion's Força Tática said the unit had stepped up preventive and intelligence work near the prison to identify suspects and make arrests. [@3] In April, two men were arrested trying to launch objects into the Maximum Security Prison, with a drone, 49 kilograms of marijuana, batteries and mobile phones seized. [@3]
