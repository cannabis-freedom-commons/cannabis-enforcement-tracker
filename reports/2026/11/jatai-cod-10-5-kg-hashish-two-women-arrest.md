In the early hours of Wednesday 11 November 2026, the Border Operations Command
(COD) of the Goiás Military Police arrested two women for drug trafficking and
seized a hashish load valued at more than R$725,000. [@1] COD officers
intercepted a suspicious vehicle and, during a personal search, found 10.5 kg of
hashish carefully packed and fixed to the bodies of the two car occupants. [@1]

According to the police force, one of the women already had a criminal record,
having previously been arrested for the same crime using the same mode of
transport. [@1] The two suspects were arrested in the act (em flagrante) for
drug trafficking, and they and all the seized material were taken to the Jataí
(GO) Civil Police station, where the applicable legal procedures were carried
out. [@1]
