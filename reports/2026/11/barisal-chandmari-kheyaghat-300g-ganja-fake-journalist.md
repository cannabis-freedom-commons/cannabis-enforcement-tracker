Barisal Metropolitan Police's Detective Branch (DB) arrested Abdul Jalil Akon,
35, with 300 grams of ganja during a raid in the Chandmari Kheyaghat road area
under Kotwali Model police station. [@3] The raid was carried out in the
Chandmari Kheyaghat area of Ward 10 of Barisal city at about 9:30 am on Friday
13 November 2026. [@1] Police said the raid caught Abdul Jalil Akon, whom they
described as a drug seller, and recovered 300 grams of ganja from him. [@3]

Jalil ran a Facebook page and lived in a rented room on Baptist Mission Road,
and police said he used a journalist's identity while carrying out criminal
activities. [@3] A press card recovered from him named him as publisher and
editor of an online portal called Ajker Manobjomin. [@2] Police said he had made
the journalist identity card himself and had used it to try to gain advantages
in various places. [@1] Police said Jalil was not attached to any media
organisation. [@1] Jalil is the son of the late Moinuddin Akon of Indrakul
village in Suryamani union of Bauphal upazila, Patuakhali district. [@3] His
home is in Bauphal upazila of Patuakhali, and police said he had been running a
drug trade from rented accommodation in Barisal city. [@1] Police said several
drug-related allegations already stood against him. [@1]

A case was filed against him under the Narcotics Control Act at Kotwali Model
police station. [@3]
