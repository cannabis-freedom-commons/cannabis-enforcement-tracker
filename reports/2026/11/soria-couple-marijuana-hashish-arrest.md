National Police officers working with Soria Local Police detained a couple as
alleged perpetrators of family-violence offences and drug trafficking. [@1] The
case began on Monday 2 November, when Local Police received a call reporting an
assault at a home in the city. [@1] Officers interviewed the two people
involved, who were partners, and detained both over alleged ill-treatment in the
family setting. [@2] Both detainees were placed at the disposal of the judicial
authority after the search of their home in the Soria capital. [@2]

The court-authorised home search seized 573 grams of marijuana and 99.55 grams
of hashish. [@2] Officers also seized a precision scale. [@1] Officers also
seized a complete marijuana grow kit made up of seed trays, seeds and pots. [@1]
Officers also seized distribution and sale tools, including grinders and cutting
implements. [@1] Officers seized 2,730 euros in cash, in 50, 20 and 10 euro
notes. [@1] Police estimated the seized substances were worth 4,594 euros on the
illicit market. [@1]

During the initial intervention in the property, one officer saw a large
quantity of drugs inside the home along with items for handling, weighing and
selling them. [@1] The local officers then reported the find to officers of the
Soria Provincial Commissariat of the National Police. [@1] The national officers
requested an entry and search warrant from the courts, which the duty-court
judge authorised. [@1] The Government Subdelegation account likewise describes
the detention of the couple for alleged family-violence and drug-trafficking
offences. [@3]
