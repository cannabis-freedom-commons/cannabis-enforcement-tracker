San Luis Potosí's state civil guard, the Guardia Civil Estatal, detained five
suspected drug sellers in two operations in Ciudad Valles and Matehuala. [@1] In
Ciudad Valles officers detained four people: Héctor “N”, aged 20, Alan “N”, aged
18, Bryan “N”, aged 24, and a teenager. [@1] Both outlets said those held in
Ciudad Valles were travelling on the motorcycle police seized with them. [@2]
Plano Informativo also reported the two interventions and said officers seized
drugs, vehicles and objects allegedly used for criminal activity. [@2]

The four were seized with doses of cristal, a street term for a
methamphetamine-like stimulant, as well as doses of marijuana and five
ponchallantas, metal spikes used to puncture vehicle tyres. [@1] Pulso reported
that the four appeared to belong to a criminal group operating in Ciudad Valles,
in the Huasteca Potosina region. [@1] In a separate operation in Matehuala,
officers detained Juan “N”, aged 36, and seized doses of cocaine, marijuana and
cristal, along with three vehicles. [@1]

Intelligence information described the man held in Matehuala as a target who
generates crime in the municipality. [@1] The five detainees and more than one
hundred drug doses, together with the seized objects, were placed at the
disposal of the competent authorities for investigation. [@1]
