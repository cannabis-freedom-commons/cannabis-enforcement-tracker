Maheshwar police raided a field at Salipura in Khargone district and seized 245
cannabis plants there. [@1] The cannabis had been grown hidden among cotton and
tuvar (pigeon pea) crops. [@1] Officers seized 245 cannabis plants from the
Salipura field. [@1] The seized plants weighed 252 kg 380 g. [@1]

Police named the field owner as Banshilal Kasde, who was absconding. [@1] Police
registered a case under the NDPS Act, India's drug law. [@1] Additional
Superintendent of Police Shakuntala Ruhal gave the details of the action. [@1]
The raid was carried out on the directions of Superintendent of Police Dr
Ravindra Verma under the state-level anti-narcotics campaign. [@1]
