Bolivia's Special Force for the Fight against Drug Trafficking (FELCN) carried
out two drug interdiction operations in Santa Cruz department that resulted in
the seizure of 18 packages of marijuana at the Terminal de Buses Bimodal and 10
packages testing positive for cocaine base paste and marijuana on the highway
toward Ángel Sandoval province at San Ignacio de Velasco. [@1] The first
operation took place during checks on international passenger buses at the
Terminal Bimodal in Andrés Ibáñez province. [@1] While inspecting a bus luggage
compartment, officers found a large bag holding 18 ovoid packages wrapped in
clear plastic film containing a greenish herb. [@1] A field test confirmed that
the substance was marijuana, and FELCN officers seized the load and opened an
investigation to determine its origin and destination. [@1]

In a second operation under the "Frontera Segura 2.0" operational plan, officers
from the Mobile Rural Patrol Unit (UMOPAR) of San Ignacio de Velasco intercepted
a vehicle on the highway to Ángel Sandoval province. [@1] Searching the vehicle,
the officers found 10 packages holding a yellowish-green substance that
field-tested positive for cocaine base paste and marijuana, according to the
police report. [@1]

FELCN reported that the substances from the vehicle were seized, without stating
the total weight of the packages, the share corresponding to each drug, or
whether the driver or anyone else was detained. [@1] Both interdiction
operations formed part of the anti-drug force's ongoing enforcement work across
Santa Cruz department. [@1]
