The Guardia Civil detained a 37-year-old man who lives in Seville for alleged
drug trafficking after seizing 15.9 kilos of hashish resin at a company near
Asturias airport. [@1] The operation took place on 5 November 2026, when the man
flew from Seville to Asturias to collect a parcel he had sent to the company
through a transport firm. [@1] Employees of the company opened the parcel by
mistake, found it might hold drugs and called the Guardia Civil. [@3]

The case was handled by officers of the Unidad Orgánica de Policía Judicial of
the Asturias command and the Guardia Civil post at Piedras Blancas. [@2] The
parcel had been announced by a regular client of the firm, who said he would
pick it up in person after landing in Asturias. [@1] Officers took the parcel to
the Avilés court on duty and obtained judicial authorisation to open and inspect
it. [@2] Inside were 159 hashish resin bars of 100 grams each, 15.9 kilos in
total, and tests confirmed the substance was hashish. [@1]

With the judge's authorisation, officers prepared a similar replacement parcel
and mounted a simulated delivery to arrest the recipient. [@1] The suspect flew
in on a flight from Seville due at 19.30, and officers followed him from the
airport to the company offices and detained him when he identified himself and
claimed the parcel. [@1] He was then taken to Guardia Civil offices in Oviedo.
[@1]

The parcel had left Seville on 4 November with false sender and recipient names,
and the amount far exceeds the 2.5 kilos at which Spanish courts treat hashish
as of notorious importance. [@1] The man was brought before the court on 6
November, which ordered him held in the Centro Penitenciario de Asturias. [@1]
The hashish is to be sent to Cantabria for definitive weighing and analysis.
[@1] The investigation had not established whether the hashish was meant for
distribution in Asturias or for onward movement elsewhere. [@4]
