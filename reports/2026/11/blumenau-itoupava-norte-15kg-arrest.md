Military Police noticed a car making a sudden start at about 11:29 p.m. on 4
November 2026 in Itoupava Norte, Blumenau. [@2] The driver fled at high speed
after an order to stop, endangering other road users, and officers lost sight of
the vehicle. [@2] Officers later found the car in the bus terminal parking lot.
[@2] As officers approached, the suspect tried to flee on foot but was caught
and detained after resisting the approach. [@2]

Officers found about 1.5 kilograms of marijuana inside the car. [@2] A second
account says Military Police handled a drug-trafficking case that night in
Itoupava Norte and found about 1.5 kilograms of marijuana inside the vehicle.
[@3] The detained man was 29 years old and also had a cell phone. [@2] He was
arrested and taken to the Civil Police station with the vehicle and the seized
