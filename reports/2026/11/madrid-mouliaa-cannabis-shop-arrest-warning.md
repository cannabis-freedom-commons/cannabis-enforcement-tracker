The shop is Greenery Ponzano, which the actress runs in Madrid's Chamberí
district. [@3] The case began with a police inspection of the business that
found CBD products containing THC, the psychoactive compound of cannabis, above
the permitted level. [@4]

After the raid on her business, Madrid's municipal police also reported one of
the shop's assistants for the same offence. [@2] The investigating judge ordered
the local police to notify her to attend a forensic medical examination on 19
November 2026, warning that if she missed it without a properly documented
reason her arrest could be ordered. [@2]

The judge rejected the defence's request to close the case but admitted its
appeal, so the Provincial Court of Madrid will decide whether the proceedings
continue or are shelved. [@2] Her defence argues the suspect is contesting how
the case is being handled, arguing the rulings lack sufficient reasoning and
should be settled as an administrative matter rather than a criminal one. [@1]
The shop assistant is due to give evidence before the same court on 13 January.
