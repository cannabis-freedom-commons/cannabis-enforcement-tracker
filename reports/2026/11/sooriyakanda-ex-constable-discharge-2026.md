Embilipitiya Magistrate's Court on 2 November 2026 discharged former
Sooriyakanda police constable Susantha Hettiarachchi in connection with a
cannabis plantation raid case. [@1] Hettiarachchi was a former constable
attached to the Sooriyakanda police. [@1] Attorney-at-Law Punchihewa appeared
for the suspect and made submissions on his behalf. [@1]

The court issued the discharge order under Section 123 of the Code of Criminal
Procedure. [@1] The order was based on a complaint submitted by the Embilipitiya
Division Special Crime Investigation Unit. [@1] The discharge decision came on
the same day the court had ordered charges to be filed regarding the incident.
[@1] The investigation unit had presented facts against the suspect on three
charges. [@1] The three charges were unlawful obstruction of the land in
question, criminal force and criminal intimidation. [@1]

The case concerned a cannabis plantation raid on land reportedly belonging to a
relative of Ratnapura District National People's Power Parliamentarian Shantha
Pathma Kumara. [@1] The Embilipitiya Division Special Crime Investigation Unit
was the complainant in the case. [@1]
