Officers of the Service de police de la Ville de Saint-Jérôme, the city police
force, were deployed in the Lafontaine sector of Saint-Jérôme on 9 November
2026. [@1] The operation targeted suspected trafficking of cocaine and
distribution of illicit cannabis. [@1] In the morning, at about 9 a.m., the
officers executed two search warrants. [@1] The warrants notably targeted a
59-year-old man. [@1]

About ten officers took part in the operation, which was still under way when
the report was published. [@1] Further information, including the seizure tally
or potential charges, was to be released once the operation was completed. [@1]
The investigation began after police received a tip from the public. [@1]
