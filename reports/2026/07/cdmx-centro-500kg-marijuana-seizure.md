Mexico City investigation officers raided a suspected drug storehouse in the
historic centre in the early hours of 22 July 2026. [@2] The operation was run
by the Policía de Investigación with the city prosecution service after citizen
reports and intelligence work. [@1] No one was inside the apartment, so officers
made no arrests. [@1]

Inside a tenement apartment, officers found a whole room used only to store
dozens of bags of a substance resembling marijuana. [@1] They seized 134
packages of marijuana with a total weight of more than half a ton. [@2] Officers
also found a scale used to weigh the substances and boxes of all sizes used to
store them. [@1]

Milenio and Infobae place the apartment on República de Bolivia in colonia
Centro Histórico in Cuauhtémoc. [@3] Telediario names the same storehouse as on
República de Venezuela in colonia Centro. [@1] The storehouse was operated by
Los Herrera Segura, described as a cell of La Unión Tepito that allegedly forces
centre merchants to sell drugs. [@2] The reports describe the apartment as a
point for storing, distributing and buying drugs. [@3]
