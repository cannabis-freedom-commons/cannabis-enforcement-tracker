Police detained a 41-year-old woman from Bender in Tiraspol on suspicion of drug
trafficking. [@1] Investigators say she distributed so-called zakladki, drug
packages left in hiding places that customers collect. [@1] The Interior
Ministry press centre announced the case as a large consignment of drugs found
by a woman who allegedly used her child as cover. [@2]

Plain-clothes officers stopped her on Yunosti Street in Tiraspol on 2 July 2026.
[@1] A personal search in front of witnesses found a master stash, a bulk
package of packages for distribution, holding 200 wraps of a drug substance.
[@1] One hundred wraps were bound in red tape and 100 in yellow tape, and the
police said the wraps differed in weight. [@1] The Interior Ministry press
centre confirmed the detention and published its video of the case. [@2]

Experts found the wraps contained hashish, which the police classified as an
especially large quantity. [@1] Investigators believe that from May to June she
worked as a courier for two online shops selling drugs. [@1] Police said she had
been prosecuted for drug offences before and that her husband is serving a
sentence for a similar crime. [@1] Operatives say she repeatedly went to the
hiding places with her underage son, one of her two children, to avoid
attracting the attention of police and passers-by. [@1]

A criminal case has been opened against her for the sale of drugs in an
especially large amount, which carries up to 20 years in prison. [@1]
