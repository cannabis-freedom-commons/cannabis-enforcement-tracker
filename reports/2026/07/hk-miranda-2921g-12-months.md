Principal Magistrate Don So jailed 21-year-old Filipina tourist Czar Francine Miranda for 12 months
on 14 July 2026 after she pleaded guilty at West Kowloon Court to trafficking in cannabis. [@2]
Customs officers had arrested her on 21 February 2026 when she arrived from Bangkok and found 2,921
grams of cannabis buds in vacuum-sealed bags in her checked suitcase, a grinder with traces of
cannabis in her carry-on bag and a banned alternative smoking product in her handbag. [@1,2]

Customs valued the drugs at about HK$600,000. [@1,2] The department charged her with trafficking
under the Dangerous Drugs Ordinance and with importing the smoking product under the Import and
Export Ordinance. [@1,2] So also imposed one month's imprisonment on the tobacco-device count to
run concurrently. [@2]

Miranda had remained in custody since her arrest; The SUN HK reported that with remission she could
leave prison around December 2026. [@2]
