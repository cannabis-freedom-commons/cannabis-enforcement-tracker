Colombia's Navy detained seven people across two Pacific anti-drug operations
off Buenaventura, three Panamanian citizens and four Colombians, who were placed
at the disposal of the competent authority for judicial proceedings. [@1] A
minor travelling aboard the first boat was handed to the Childhood and
Adolescence Police for the restoration of rights and was not counted among the
seven captured. [@1]

Officers first inspected a coastal cabotage boat in the inner bay of
Buenaventura and found nine boxes holding 216 rectangular packages with 317.9
kilograms of marijuana. [@1] Acting on Naval Intelligence, rapid-reaction Coast
Guard units then intercepted a go-fast boat 45 nautical miles from Buenaventura
and found 35 bales holding 1,146 packages of suspected illicit substances. [@1]
After the go-fast boat sank during the transfer to Buenaventura, the Navy
rescued all its occupants and recovered the cargo, which CTI officials of the
Attorney General's Office confirmed as 1,143.7 kilograms of marijuana. [@1] In
total, the two operations seized 1,461.6 kilograms of marijuana. [@1]

The Navy said the result affected drug-trafficking finances by more than 15
million dollars. [@1] It said the seizures prevented more than 242,000 doses of
marijuana from reaching the illegal international market. [@1]
