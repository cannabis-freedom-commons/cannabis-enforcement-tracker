Police from the Valle traffic and transport unit stopped a JAC box van for
inspection on the Cali-Andalucía road in El Cerrito, Valle del Cauca. [@1]
Officers found 149 packages of different sizes wrapped in vinipel film hidden in
the van in what police call the kamikaze modality. [@2] The packages contained
3,870 kilograms of marijuana described as creepy-type. [@2] Investigators said
the van was travelling from Caloto, Cauca, to Pereira, Risaralda. [@1]

Police arrested the driver, a 24-year-old man from Corinto, Cauca. [@2] Officers
also seized the van used to move the load and two mobile phones. [@2] The
detainee has a judicial record for receptación, which police said does not
amount to a conviction and does not decide his responsibility in this case. [@1]
The anti-narcotics directorate valued the load at about 3,045 million pesos and
estimated it equalled about 3,870,000 doses kept off the illegal market. [@1]
Police estimated the operation kept about 3,870,000 doses from reaching
distribution circuits. [@1]

The detainee and everything seized were placed at the disposal of the
specialized anti-narcotics prosecutor to face a drug-trafficking charge. [@2]
Police described the result as one of the largest road-corridor seizures in
Valle del Cauca during the year. [@1]
