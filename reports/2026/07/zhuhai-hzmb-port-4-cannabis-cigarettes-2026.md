During routine supervision of the inbound passenger-vehicle channel, Gongbei
Customs officers stopped a Guangdong-Hong Kong dual-plate vehicle at the Hong
Kong-Zhuhai-Macao Bridge Zhuhai highway port at about 13:00 on 25 July 2026.
[@2] Officers singled out the vehicle after noticing that its driver appeared
unusually nervous while speaking with them. [@1] A follow-up search found four
suspected cannabis cigarettes hidden inside a chewing-gum tub in the driver-side
centre-console armrest box. [@1] The case was handed to the customs
anti-smuggling department for further handling. [@2]

The cigarettes held 1.1 grammes of dried plant material net, and one cigarette
showed signs of having been burnt. [@1] Testing by a professional institution
confirmed that all four cigarettes contained cannabis components. [@1]

The inspection was carried out by Hong Kong-Zhuhai-Macao Bridge Customs, a unit
under Gongbei Customs. [@2] Gongbei Customs reminded the public that cannabis is
among the state-regulated narcotics and psychotropic substances that cause
addiction. [@1] It stated that smuggling, trafficking, transporting or
manufacturing narcotics carries criminal liability and criminal penalties
regardless of quantity. [@1]
