Osh city officials said work to destroy wild cannabis took place in the Japalak and Kyzyl-Kyshtak
villages and the Manas-Ata area. [@1]

Local residents and law-enforcement personnel took part in the work. [@1] They mowed the wild
cannabis and destroyed it by burning. [@1]
