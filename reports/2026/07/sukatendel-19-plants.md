A joint team from Karo police, the Karo regency BNN and local government found 19 cannabis plants at Perladangan Tembuten near a drug-and-gambling barracks in Sukatendel, Tiganderket, on 22 July 2026. [@1,2]

Five plants were in pots, and officers also found two packets of dried cannabis and a bong. [@1,2] The team uprooted all 19 plants and secured them as evidence while police investigated who had planted them. [@1,2]
