Police and administration officials carried out a joint action at a house compound in Gazania Tola,
Bulka village, in the Ramna police-station area of Garhwa district. [@1] NewsDekho reported that
the team acted on a tip and found cannabis cultivation across about 300 square metres. [@1]

The team uprooted and seized all the plants, which weighed 118 kilograms, and destroyed the crop at
the site. [@1] The report put the count at about 1,800 cannabis plants and said the police began an
investigation into the cultivation and any wider network. [@1]
