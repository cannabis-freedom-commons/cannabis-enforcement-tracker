National Civil Police officers captured Milton Orlando Hernández Alfaro, alias
Ñaña, in flagrante after an investigation. [@2] Investigators linked him to the
same drug-distribution network as Iliana Iveth Chávez Hernández. [@3] The
operation took place in the department of San Miguel after officers followed him
from a marijuana shipment he received at the Nuevo Amanecer terminal in
Soyapango. [@2]

During the stop officers seized marijuana he was allegedly carrying to
distribute in different communities. [@2] The later search found a plastic bag
holding different portions of marijuana apparently ready for distribution, plus
a mobile phone and cash. [@4] The Third Peace Court of San Miguel validated the
prosecution evidence at the initial hearing and ordered him held in provisional
detention while the investigation continues. [@4] The Attorney General's Office
said he will remain in provisional detention during the investigation into drug
selling in the east of the country. [@4]

Villatoro also reported that Iliana Iveth Chávez Hernández, the alleged network
leader, had been detained the previous May in San Miguel while allegedly
carrying 2,500 grams of marijuana valued by authorities at $8,000. [@1] Security
Minister Gustavo Villatoro said the state holds territorial control and is
confronting the scourge head-on without stepping back. [@2] Villatoro said years
of clandestine drug selling had ended for the detainee and warned that a decade
behind bars awaited him. [@3]
