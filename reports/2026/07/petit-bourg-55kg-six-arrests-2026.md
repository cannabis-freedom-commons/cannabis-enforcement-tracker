Six men were arrested during the night of 2 to 3 July 2026 at Petit-Bourg during
a gendarmerie operation. [@1] Six people were caught in the act while the cargo
was being unloaded on the Petit-Bourg landing. [@2] After several hours of
surveillance, officers intervened at about 4 a.m. as the boat landed and
unloading began, finding a utility vehicle with a trailer intended to carry the
goods on site. [@1] All six suspects were placed in police custody in
gendarmerie premises. [@2]

On the night of Thursday 2 to Friday 3 July, a large gendarmerie deployment was
set up. [@2] The inquiry began after information reached the Guadeloupe
gendarmerie's judicial support section about an imminent boat arrival from
Dominica suspected of carrying a large contraband cargo. [@1] A large deployment
covered the landing that night, with units from the Saint-Claude and
Pointe-à-Pitre companies plus the nautical brigade, the GIGN antenna, the
Republican Guard intervention platoon, the State coastal action group and the
interministerial research group. [@1] Searches seized 55 kilograms of cannabis
herb and 56 cartons of Camel Yellow cigarettes, or 2,800 cartouches representing
almost 560 kilograms of tobacco. [@1] The market value of that cargo was put at
more than 378,000 euros. [@1] Officers also confiscated a saintoise-type boat
fitted with two 115-horsepower engines, valued at about 15,000 euros. [@1]
Several new number-plate sets, mobile phones and passports were also seized for
use in the investigation. [@1]

The investigation was entrusted to the Pointe-à-Pitre research brigade under the
prosecutor to establish each suspect's role and identify possible ramifications
of the smuggling network. [@1] Two of the suspects who waited on shore for the
boat will be summoned before the court in collegial hearing in January 2027.
[@1] The four other suspects were due to appear for immediate trial on Monday
afternoon, 6 July, to answer charges of smuggled-cigarette importation and drug
trafficking. [@1]
