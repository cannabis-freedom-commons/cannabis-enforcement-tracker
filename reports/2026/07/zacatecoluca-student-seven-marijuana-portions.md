El Salvador's National Civil Police (PNC) detained an 18-year-old student
carrying several portions of drugs outside the Instituto Nacional José Simeón
Cañas in Zacatecoluca, La Paz Este. [@1] The arrest took place in the morning,
shortly before the student went in to classes. [@1] Police identified the
detainee as Josué E., a bachillerato student at the same school; bachillerato is
the upper secondary stage taken in the two or three years before university.
[@1]

Officers seized seven portions of marijuana that he was carrying when they
stopped him. [@1] The PNC said the detention formed part of the preventive
operations it runs at schools in the area to curb drug use and distribution
among students. [@1] Police confirmed he will be sent to judicial authorities on
a charge of drug possession and holding. [@1] In its statement the PNC quoted a
warning that anyone found with illegal substances will be captured and brought
before the courts. [@1]

Under Salvadoran law, drug possession and holding can carry prison sanctions
depending on the quantity and type of substance seized, as well as reoffending
or links to other offences. [@1] The authorities gave no information on whether
the student acted alone or belonged to a distribution network operating in
schools. [@1] The PNC said it would continue operations in schools in La Paz
Este and other regions. [@1]
