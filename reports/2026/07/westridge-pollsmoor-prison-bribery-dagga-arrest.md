Police say the wife of an alleged gang leader serving a sentence at Pollsmoor
Prison was arrested on 15 July 2026 for allegedly bribing a Mitchells Plain
police officer to help smuggle drugs and other items into the prison. [@1] The
inmate allegedly asked the officer to collect a parcel from his wife and hand it
to another inmate at the Mitchells Plain Regional Court, and offered cash for
the arrangement. [@1]

At Otland Street, Westridge, the woman handed the officer a parcel that police
said contained dagga, two grams of tik, two knives, four cigarette lighters, a
pack of cigarettes and a cash bribe. [@1] Police say the officer arrested the
32-year-old woman on the spot for possession of drugs and bribery. [@1]
