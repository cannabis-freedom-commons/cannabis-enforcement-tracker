A Chisinau court sentenced the organizer of the smuggling of almost 17 kg of
hashish brought from Ukraine into Moldova in 2023 to nine years in prison. [@1]
The court also ordered the convicted organizer to be put on the wanted list.
[@2] The organizer was tried in his absence. [@1]

Investigators said the hashish was hidden in a specially equipped compartment in
the spare-wheel well, where officers found 178 packages weighing more than 16.8
kg. [@1] The car arriving from Ukraine was stopped in Lipcani on the evening of
23 February 2023, when the 32-year-old woman driving said she was carrying Botox
and was travelling with two children and her 63-year-old father. [@1] A forensic
report found that the hiding place had been made artisanally and had not been
fitted by the manufacturer. [@2]

In 2024 an Edinet court had already sentenced the woman driver to nine years in
prison and her alleged accomplice to eight and a half years, detaining both
directly in the courtroom. [@1] Prosecutors then appealed the acquittal of the
convicted woman’s father, who allegedly provided the car, and the failure to
confiscate the vehicle for the state. [@1] PCCOCS said the 2023 interception
relied on operational information from Moldova’s Intelligence and Security
Service obtained from Ukrainian law enforcement, with customs officers
investigating under PCCOCS direction. [@2]
