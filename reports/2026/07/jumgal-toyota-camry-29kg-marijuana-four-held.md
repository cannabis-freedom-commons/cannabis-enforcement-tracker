Officers of the Interior Ministry's Service for Combating Illegal Drug
Trafficking broke up a group suspected of transporting and selling
cannabis-group drugs, the ministry said. [@1] On 26 July 2026, during an
operational search, officers examined a Toyota Camry driven by a man identified
as T. Zh. M., born in 1993, and found five sacks of marijuana weighing 28
kilograms 666 grams in the trunk. [@1] Three more suspects were detained the
same day: E. D. D., born in 1994, I. U. O., born in 1995, and B. K. M., born in
1984, making four people held in all. [@1]

Searches at their homes in Jumgal district, Naryn Region, found a further 482
grams of marijuana, bringing the total seized to 29.148 kilograms. [@1] Officers
also seized gauze, a gas cylinder, a hotplate and various containers that
investigators suspect were used to make drugs. [@1]

A criminal case was opened under the Kyrgyz criminal code article on the illegal
manufacture of drugs for sale. [@1] The investigation is working to identify
other possible members of the group and its drug supply channels. [@1]
Kaktus.media reported that the case was opened under article 282 of the Kyrgyz
criminal code. [@2]

Reporter.kg, citing the ministry, gave the four suspects' ages as 33, 32, 31 and
42 rather than their birth years. [@3] Reporter.kg said the anti-drug service
detained four people on suspicion of transporting and selling drugs. [@3]
