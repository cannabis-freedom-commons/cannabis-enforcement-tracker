Police officers across Azerbaijan found and destroyed 17,782 wild-growing
cannabis plants weighing four tonnes in operations on 24 July 2026, the Interior
Ministry's press service said. [@2,5] e-huquq.az, also quoting the Interior
Ministry's press service, reported the same count and the same four-tonne
weight. [@3] The ministry said operations in this direction were continuing.
[@2,5]

Police across Azerbaijan found and seized 45.3 kg of narcotic substances over
the previous 24 hours, the Interior Ministry reported. [@1,5] In the same period,
law-enforcement bodies carried out measures to eliminate wild-growing cannabis.
[@1]

Operativmm.az, citing the same ministry press service for the previous day, put
the 24 July clearance at 28,074 cannabis plants weighing more than 19.8 tonnes,
found in separate districts of the country. [@4]
