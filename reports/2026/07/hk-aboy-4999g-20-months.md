Magistrate Jeffrey Sze sentenced 23-year-old John Ulrik Aboy to 20 months in prison on 20 July 2026
after he pleaded guilty at West Kowloon Court to trafficking in a dangerous drug. [@2] Customs had
arrested him on 20 December 2025 when he arrived from Bangkok and found 4,999 grams of cannabis
buds in vacuum-sealed bags in his checked suitcase at Hong Kong International Airport. [@1,2]

Customs put the seizure at about HK$1 million. [@1] Sze adopted a 30-month starting point and
reduced it by one third for the guilty plea. [@2] The SUN HK described Aboy as a Filipino resident
of Hong Kong who was stopped at the arrival hall baggage examination counter. [@2]

Under Hong Kong law, trafficking in a dangerous drug can carry life imprisonment and a HK$5 million
fine. [@1]
