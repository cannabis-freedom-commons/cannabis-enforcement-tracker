North West community safety MEC Wessels Morweng congratulated an elite Eagles
traffic unit after officers arrested two foreign nationals with 13 bags of dagga
on the N12 near Klerksdorp. [@1] The Eagles had received information that two
Eswatini nationals were travelling from Mpumalanga through Gauteng on the N12
heading towards Klerksdorp with drugs. [@1] Working with the South African
Police Service Flying Squad, the Eagles intercepted the vehicle smuggling the
dagga on its westward route and arrested the two men immediately. [@1] The
interception happened while the Eagles were on routine visible patrol duties
after receiving the tip about the vehicle travelling westwards. [@1]

MEC Morweng said experts were still calculating the exact value of the
confiscated drugs, describing the volume of the haul as a significant blow to
interprovincial drug trafficking networks. [@1] He commended the Eagles for
vigilance, quick response and seamless collaboration between law enforcement
officers. [@1] He said the arrest underscored a commitment to ensuring that
North West will not be used as a drug transit corridor. [@1] The two suspects
were expected to appear in court soon while investigation into the origin and
final destination of the contraband continued. [@1]
