Officers from Phuket Airport Customs with Sakhu Police made the arrest in the
international departures terminal at about 10am on 8 July. [@2] The detainee was
30-year-old Russian Sergei Chalabari, stopped before a flight to Kazakhstan.
[@1] During questioning, he admitted that the luggage and all of the seized
cannabis belonged to him. [@2] Officers charged him with attempting to smuggle
goods out of Thailand without going through customs procedures and handed him
with the seized evidence to investigators at Sakhu Police Station. [@2]

He was travelling with two blue BP WORLD wheeled suitcases weighing a combined
eight kilogrammes. [@2] A search of the luggage found 35 vacuum-sealed bags of
cannabis flowers weighing 11.4 kg including packaging and 12 bags of cannabis
resin weighing 6.2 kg including packaging. [@2] The two finds made 47
vacuum-sealed packs in total. [@1] Together they were reported as 17.6 kg of
cannabis. [@2] Officers seized the cannabis and both suitcases as evidence. [@2]
Police valued the haul at about 264,000 US dollars. [@1] The cannabis was to be
taken to Almaty. [@1] Customs officers warned tourists not to agree to carry
cannabis or anyone else's parcels, noting that unlawful export of one kilogramme
carries a 30,000-baht fine. [@1]

Thailand became the first Asian country to legalise cannabis cultivation and use
in 2022, spurring tourism and cannabis shops, but domestic liberalisation does
not cover export. [@1] The arrest was described as the third major
cannabis-export case at the airport in just over a week. [@2]
