No one came forward to collect the container, and no arrests had been made in
the case. [@1] Investigators are trying to establish who was behind the
large-scale smuggling attempt and whether it is linked to international criminal
groups. [@1] The Gdansk District Prosecutor's Office opened an investigation
into transporting 945.466 kg of cannabis herb into Poland. [@3] The prosecutor
assigned the investigation to the Pomeranian Customs and Tax Office in Gdynia,
whose stated aim is to establish all the circumstances and identify the
smugglers. [@2] Importing a significant quantity of narcotics under the
drug-prevention law carries a fine and 3 to 20 years in prison. [@3]

Pomeranian customs officers in Gdynia secured 945.466 kg of marijuana hidden in
cardboard boxes of clothing from Canada. [@2] The black-market value the
authorities gave for the marijuana was about 46 million zlotys, which the outlet
reported as about 12 million dollars. [@1] Tests confirmed marijuana in 1674
packages. [@2] On 21 July 2026 officers of the Pomeranian Customs and Tax Office
in Gdynia selected the container from Canada for inspection. [@2] The container
was first X-rayed and then examined in detail, and officers found vacuum bags of
dried plant material that looked and smelled like marijuana inside the used
clothing. [@2]

According to the customs declaration the container was supposed to contain
clothing for a Polish textile company in Mazowieckie Voivodeship. [@2] The
prosecutor recorded that the shipment entered through the customs clearance post
at the Bulgarskie Quay in Gdynia. [@3] The Pomeranian office said this was
another large-scale drug smuggling attempt it had stopped that year. [@2]
