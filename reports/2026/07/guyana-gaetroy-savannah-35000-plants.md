Guyana Police Force ranks, led by a gazetted officer, conducted a cannabis eradication exercise at Gaetroy Savannah on the Upper Berbice River on Sunday, 26 July 2026, from 07:00 to 16:30. [@1,2] They found three farms totalling about five acres, with roughly 35,000 suspected plants ranging from two inches to five feet tall. [@1,2] Officers also found one makeshift camp. [@1,2]

Police uprooted and burned the plants and camp, valuing the destroyed narcotics at GY$11,519,200. [@1,2] No arrests were made, and investigations were continuing in the two reports. [@1,2] INews reproduced a police statement, and News Room attributed its account to Police Headquarters; neither article describes an independent count or valuation. [@1,2] The reports give one combined area for the three farms, not measurements for each plot, and do not name suspected cultivators. [@1,2] News Room's image caption identifies a section of the farm before it was destroyed, not the burning itself. [@1]
