The Mount Lebanon Criminal Court sentenced Georges Hanna Dib, known as Dr Food,
and several co-defendants to life at hard labor in absentia in a drug case. [@3]
The six are Dib, Syrians Kamal Abbas Ramadan, Abdel Karim Zaher al-Salem and
Ahmad Dolf al-Youssef, and Lebanese Hassan Atiyeh Diab and Mohammad Abdullah
al-Ali. [@1] Ramadan and al-Salem were tried in custody, while Dib and
al-Youssef were sentenced in their absence. [@1] Each convict was fined 120
million Lebanese pounds and those convicted in absence were stripped of civil
rights under Article 150 of Lebanon's drug law. [@1]

The group manufactured narcotics from the hashish plant and hid them inside
company products for shipment from Lebanon abroad via Turkey. [@1] Hashish
hidden in cake packaging produced in Baalbek in eastern Lebanon was transported
to Turkiye and then shipped internationally. [@2] The sentencing followed a 2024
bust involving 820kg of hashish hidden in cake packaging. [@2] Dib allegedly had
the packaging produced at a facility in the Charawneh neighborhood of Baalbeck
in the Bekaa Valley. [@3]

The ruling was issued by a court presided over by Judge Elie al-Helou. [@3] Dib,
a widely followed food influencer, was believed to be outside Lebanon and had
not addressed the ruling on his public pages. [@2]
