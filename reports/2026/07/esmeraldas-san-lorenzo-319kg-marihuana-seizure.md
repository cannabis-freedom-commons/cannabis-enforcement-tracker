Two suspects fled after Ecuador's National Police stopped a single-cab pickup
carrying marijuana on the Esmeraldas–San Lorenzo highway in the early hours of
22 July 2026. [@3] The driver and a companion ignored the order to stop, tried
to escape and fired several shots at the officers during the chase. [@3]
Officers shot out one of the pickup's tyres to stop it, but the two occupants
abandoned the vehicle and fled in an unknown direction. [@3] No one was
arrested, and the National Police said investigative steps were under way to
identify and locate those responsible. [@2] The intervention happened during a
mobile checkpoint by the Bloque de Seguridad, Ecuador's joint police and
military security bloc. [@3]

Officers found 319.228 kilograms of marijuana inside the vehicle, which the
police reported as 319,228 grams. [@3] The National Police put the load at
approximately 638,456 doses. [@2] Police valued the marijuana at US$247,401.70
on the national market and US$1,276,912 on the international market. [@3] The
National Police's own statement recorded the load as 319.228 kilograms of
marijuana with an estimated international market value of US$1,276,912. [@2]

Preliminary field tests identified the substance as marijuana, and it was
entered into the custody chain. [@2] Police said investigations were continuing
to establish the load's origin and destination and to identify those
responsible. [@2] The operation was carried out under the Estrategia Operacional
3D, the National Police's operational strategy against criminal economies,
following intelligence work. [@2] Criminalistics specialists and the
Anti-Narcotics Investigation Headquarters collected the evidence and moved the
marijuana to the anti-narcotics warehouses of the Esmeraldas police sub-zone.
[@3] The Ministry of the Interior announced the seizure in Esmeraldas province,
which borders Colombia, on 22 July 2026. [@1] The ministry said the operation
kept more than 638,000 doses off the illegal market. [@1]
