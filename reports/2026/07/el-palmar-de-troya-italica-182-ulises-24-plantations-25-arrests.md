The Guardia Civil, the National Police and the Tax Agency's customs service
carried out a joint operation against marijuana cultivation in El Palmar de
Troya, the former district of Utrera in Seville province. [@2] Officers
simultaneously entered and searched 16 homes in the town on 7 July 2026 and
dismantled 24 marijuana plantations. [@2] The security forces reported the
definitive balance of the operation as 25 people arrested and four more under
investigation. [@2] The operation was named Itálica 182 Ulises, and the Tax
Agency statement adds the names Sudario and Aquiles. [@2]

The investigation began in May 2026 after unusually high and irregular
electricity consumption was detected at several places in El Palmar de Troya.
[@2] Police seized 2,200 marijuana plants at different stages of growth, about
70 kilograms of buds already processed and ready for sale, more than 50,000
euros in cash and a twelve-gauge shotgun. [@2] The plants were growing indoors,
and officers found a sophisticated system intended to cultivate them. [@2]
During the searches investigators caught several people cutting the plants for
imminent transport. [@2] With the electricity company, technicians safely
disconnected 21 illegal connections to the grid during the operation. [@2] The
arrested people, the drugs, the cash, the weapon and the other items seized were
placed before the courts, and the authorities did not rule out further action.
[@2]

On the day of the raid the government's deputy delegate in Seville, Francisco
Toscano, announced a provisional balance of 26 people arrested, 16 searches and
24 plantations dismantled. [@3] The raids took place early in the morning in the
former district of Utrera. [@1] In October 2019 a similar operation in the town
searched 36 homes, arrested 22 people and seized 2,313 cannabis plants and 84
kilograms of buds. [@1] ABC de Sevilla gave the weight of processed buds as 70
kilograms and reported the seizure of 2,200 plants. [@4]

Diario de Sevilla reported that the searchers found equipment for lighting,
ventilation, air conditioning and irrigation used to keep the plantations
running all year. [@5]
