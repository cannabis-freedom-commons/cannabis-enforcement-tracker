Michael Whittington was a participant in the Copiah County Drug Court Program
when he was arrested. [@1] He was arrested and charged with manufacturing
marijuana and booked into the Copiah County Jail. [@1] After appearing in court
on July 27 for drug court violations, Whittington was sentenced to 10 years in
the custody of the Mississippi Department of Corrections. [@1]

The Copiah County Sheriff's Office Narcotics Unit executed a search warrant on
July 23 at a residence on Thornton Lane in Crystal Springs. [@1] A second
account identifies the address as 1032 Thornton Lane in Crystal Springs. [@3] At
the home, officers found a marijuana indoor grow and an outdoor grow, described
as large and kept in containers ranging from plant pots to buckets. [@2]

Officials said all drug evidence was sent to the Mississippi State Crime
Laboratory for analysis. [@1] Once laboratory results return, the manufacturing
case is to be presented to the Copiah County grand jury. [@3]
