Costa Rican authorities intercepted drug boats in an operation off Bahía Ballena
de Osa in southern Costa Rica after detecting a suspicious vessel and pursuing
it. [@1] The first boat was moving drugs on 9 July when it was stopped in the
Pacific. [@1] Officers detained two Ecuadorians, aged 28 and 34, alongside a
third detainee of Colombian nationality aboard that first boat. [@1] Officers
detained three other men aboard a second boat that was also moving cocaine and
marijuana. [@1]

Officers found more than 2.5 tonnes of cocaine aboard that boat, presumed bound
for the United States. [@1] Some seized parcels bore the legend “Made in
Colombia. Lo mejor de Caro Quintero” with a photo of Mexican trafficker Rafael
Caro Quintero. [@1]

Costa Rican authorities said the seizures were possible through intelligence
shared with Colombia’s Navy and Panamanian authorities. [@1] The United States’
Joint Interagency Task Force South provided air support that helped locate the
suspected boats in Pacific waters. [@1] Costa Rica presented the operation as
part of the ‘Escudo de las Américas’ anti-drug strategy promoted by Donald
Trump. [@1]
