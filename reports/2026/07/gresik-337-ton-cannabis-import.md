On Wednesday 1 July 2026, Badan Narkotika Nasional officers with customs and East Java police
raided a warehouse in Gresik after following two container trucks from Tanjung Priok port. [@1,2]
They seized 3,371,400 grams (3.37 tonnes) of Thai cannabis buds hidden in 500 suitcases and in
latex cartons, and detained 12 suspects from Jakarta, Banten, West Java and East Java, including a
Chinese national alleged to own the warehouse. [@1,2]

The agency said intelligence pointed to containers declared as luggage and latex imports, and that
investigators used controlled delivery to the Gresik site. [@1,2] BNN believes the buds were meant
for tetrahydrocannabinol extraction to refill electronic cigarettes. [@1,2] Investigators are still
tracing financiers identified only as Malaysian and Chinese initials operating from abroad. [@1]

BNN described the case as Indonesia's first large cannabis-bud smuggling through formal customs
channels. [@1] Four trucks and four containers were held as evidence alongside the drugs. [@2]
