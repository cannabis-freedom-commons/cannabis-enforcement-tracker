Detectives from the Anti-Narcotics Unit based in Mombasa and Voi, working with
officers from Voi Police Station, intercepted a Scania truck towing a trailer in
Voi Township in an intelligence-led operation. [@1] The truck driver Fredrick
Otieno Ogode and his alleged accomplice Edwin Ochieng Otieno were arrested at
the scene. [@1] The two suspects were identified as Fredrick Otieno Ogode and
Edwin Ochieng Otieno after detectives stopped the truck in Voi Township. [@2]

A search of the trailer found 11 gunny bags containing cannabis weighing 825
kilograms in total. [@1] Police estimated the street value of the seized
cannabis at approximately Sh24.7 million. [@1] Detectives in Voi recovered 825
kilogrammes of bhang worth Ksh24.7 million and arrested two suspects. [@2]

The two suspects were taken into custody together with the truck and the
recovered narcotics while detectives processed the case for arraignment in
court. [@1] The DCI said investigations continued to establish the source and
intended destination of the cannabis and to pursue other people believed to be
linked to the consignment. [@1] The seizure was described as part of recent
anti-narcotics operations targeting trafficking networks along Kenya's transport
corridors. [@1]
