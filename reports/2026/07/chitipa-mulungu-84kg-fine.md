The Chitipa First Grade Magistrate's Court has fined 45-year-old George
Mulungu K1 million after convicting him of cultivating and possessing 84.6
kilograms of cannabis without a licence. [@1,2] Police from Chitipa Police
Station arrested Mulungu on 17 June 2026 after finding the cannabis at his
home in Chisembe Village, under Senior Chief Mwaulambya in Chitipa
District. [@1,2]

In mitigation Mulungu pleaded for leniency, telling the court he was a
first-time offender. [@1] State Prosecutor Robert Chawinga urged a deterrent
sentence, arguing cannabis is dangerous to users and the accused knowingly
broke the law. [@1]

Magistrate Billy Ngosi agreed with the State but took the first-offender
status into account, fining Mulungu K1 million or, in default, 12 months'
imprisonment with hard labour. [@1,2] The court also ordered the 84.6
kilograms of cannabis destroyed by burning. [@1,2]
