El Salvador's National Civil Police detained Jhon Anthony V. on a motorcycle
while he was posing as a delivery-platform rider in Antiguo Cuscatlán. [@1] His
full name is Jhon Anthony Valles García. [@2] He wore clothing similar to that
used by app-delivery workers and carried a delivery box intended to let him pass
unnoticed while moving illicit substances. [@1] He tried to flee when he noticed
the police presence but was intercepted after crashing at kilometre 8.5 of the
Pan-American Highway in Antiguo Cuscatlán, La Libertad. [@1] He remains in
custody while judicial steps advance and investigators determine the weight of
the seized packages and any linked distribution network. [@1]

Officers seized six packages of suspected marijuana, one mobile phone and the
motorcycle used to carry the load. [@1] Security Minister Gustavo Villatoro said
the work of removing from society people who hold, move or sell poison does not
stop. [@2] Villatoro warned that people who try to distribute drugs through
covert methods will face prosecution and prison. [@1] The seized phone will be
examined by the Anti-Narcotics Division to identify contacts and delivery routes
linked to the case. [@1]

Officers were patrolling the busy road in the San Salvador metropolitan area
when they detected the rider's evasive behaviour. [@1] Villatoro presented the
operation as part of the Salvadoran government's action against microtrafficking
and drug distribution. [@1]
