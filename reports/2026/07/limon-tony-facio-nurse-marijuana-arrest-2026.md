A nursing assistant at Limón's Tony Facio Hospital, identified by the surname
Araya, was arrested on 29 July 2026 on suspicion of trying to smuggle marijuana
to an inmate under guard at the hospital. [@1] The prisoner, identified by the
surname Gamboa, was being treated in the hospital's emergency service for an
apparent fracture of a toe on his left foot. [@1]

They sealed the bag again to preserve the evidence and reported the find to the
Limón prosecutor's office, which ordered Araya's arrest while agents of the OIJ,
the Judicial Investigation Agency, travelled to the hospital. [@1]

Infobae reported that the weight was later given as 39 grams in the judicial
proceedings. [@1] CR Hoy also reported 39 grams of marijuana in the snack bag.
[@2]

The prison police took Araya to the judicial cells, where he was placed at the
disposal of the Public Ministry for a criminal case. [@1] The ministry said the
seizure was possible because prison police inspect any food or object handed to
inmates during medical trips. [@1]
