Police in Regional Division Seven arrested a 27-year-old man of Second Avenue,
Bartica, after a patrol found suspected cannabis in a haversack he was carrying.
[@1] Ranks patrolling Bartica at about 3:50 a.m. on July 19 saw the man behaving
in a disorderly manner and warned him to desist. [@1] He ignored the warning and
continued, and officers arrested him and took him to the Bartica Police Station.
[@1]

A search of his black haversack uncovered a white plastic bag holding leaves,
seeds and stems suspected to be cannabis. [@2] The substance was weighed in his
presence at 12.4 grams and lodged as evidence. [@1] The man remained in police
custody while investigations continued. [@2] The arrest was made by ranks of
Regional Division #7 (Cuyuni-Mazaruni), the division that covers Bartica, a
mining town on the Essequibo River. [@2]
