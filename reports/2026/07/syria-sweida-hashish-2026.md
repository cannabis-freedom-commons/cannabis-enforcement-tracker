The Drug Enforcement Administration said it foiled an international smuggling attempt in the southeastern countryside of Sweida and seized nearly half a tonne of hashish. It said the drugs belonged to outlaw groups operating in the province and that the operation was run with the Internal Security Command in the Badia. The announcement gave no arrest figure. [@1,2]

The drugs were seized in a precise security operation, according to the anti-narcotics administration, and the Interior Ministry said the shipment belonged to groups it described as outside the law in Sweida province. [@2]
