Joint anti-drug teams uprooted and burned 38,400 fully grown marijuana plants worth an
estimated P7.68 million in Barangay Butbut Proper, Tinglayan, Kalinga, over 16 and 17
July 2026. [@1,2] A barangay is the smallest unit of local government in the
Philippines. [@1] The plantation covered about 3,200 square metres of mountainous
terrain. [@1,2]

The operation was led by the Cordillera office of the Philippine Drug Enforcement
Agency with the police regional office, the Kalinga provincial police office, the
Tinglayan municipal police station and the Bureau of Jail Management and Penology's
Kalinga district office. [@1] Bombo Radyo also named the police special action forces,
the drug enforcement group's Cordillera special operations unit and an RIU 14-PIT
Kalinga team. [@2]

No arrests were made. [@1,2] Investigators said they were tracking down those
responsible for cultivating the marijuana, including the landowners, and PDEA-Cordillera
said the operation forms part of its intensified campaign against cultivation and
production in the region. [@1]
