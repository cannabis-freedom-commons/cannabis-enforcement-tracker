Yao'an County's Shanying (“Mountain Eagle”) police-drone squad ran an anti-cultivation sweep across Dongchuan and Guanglu towns, the county government reported on 15 July 2026. [@1]

The campaign combined aerial surveys with ground checks because the county described its forests, scattered wasteland and field edges as difficult for foot patrols to cover. [@1] The squad planned grid routes and used drone zoom, live image transmission and location marking to find concealed, scattered sites. [@1]

The 45 hours of flight covered more than 3,600 mu (about 240 hectares) in priority areas, according to the notice. [@1] Police reported finding drug plants at 24 locations and uprooting 433 cannabis plants in total. [@1]

The release presents those figures as campaign totals but gives no breakdown by town or site. [@1] It does not name anyone responsible for the plots or report arrests. [@1]
