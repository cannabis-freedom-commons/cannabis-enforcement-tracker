Nhat Nguyen, 50, appeared from custody at Glasgow Sheriff Court and pleaded
guilty to being concerned in the supply of cannabis. [@1] He was caught with the
cannabis at a property in Glasgow's Maryhill on 17 July 2026. [@1] The court
heard that a warrant had been granted to search Nguyen's home. [@1] Prosecutor
Lauren Aitchison told the court that a number of items were recovered. [@1] They
included 243 cannabis plants, a lighting unit and thermometers. [@1]

The court was told that the value of the plants recovered could reach a maximum
of £145,800. [@1] Alison Miller, defending, told the court that Nguyen was a
first offender and was very keen for the matter to come to an end. [@1] She said
he knew he would receive a custodial sentence and understood that background
reports would be required. [@1]

Sheriff Owen Mullan deferred sentence for background reports until next month.
[@1] He continued Nguyen's remand in custody in the meantime. [@1]
