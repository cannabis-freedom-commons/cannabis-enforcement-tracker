DAET officers arrested three people accused of belonging to a structure
dedicated to transporting and distributing drugs in the central part of the
country. [@2] The operation took place in the municipality of Siguatepeque,
Comayagua, after several weeks of follow-up and investigation. [@2] The
detainees were identified as Elmer Oliver Guerra Guerra, 41, Mario Alexis
Gutiérrez Benítez, 38, and María Alba Leonor Gerónimo, 38. [@2] Police said the
three were traveling in a pickup truck allegedly used to move the illicit load
to different distribution points. [@2]

During the vehicle inspection officers found 70 packages of suspected marijuana
hidden inside a tire. [@2] Each of the 70 packages weighed about a pound, about
70 pounds in total (about 31.75 kg). [@3] Officers also seized four mobile
phones for forensic analysis of the network's communications and links. [@2]

Police linked the three suspects to a criminal gang in charge of transporting,
supplying and distributing drugs for sale in different places. [@1] Police said
the detainees held strategic roles in the organization's logistics chain of
transport, supply and distribution to regional sale points. [@3] Police said the
capture is only part of a wider investigation to identify other members,
determine trafficking routes and establish the load's origin. [@3] The case was
reported on 14 July 2026. [@1]
