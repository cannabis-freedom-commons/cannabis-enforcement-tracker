The State Security Service said a man in Shahrisabz had built a greenhouse on a 5.3-hectare plot at his home and was growing 714 cannabis plants for sale; the dried material weighed 201 kg. In a separate case in Tashkent, a previously convicted man was stopped with 1.994 kg of hashish said to have been smuggled from a neighbouring country. Criminal cases were opened in both. [@1]
