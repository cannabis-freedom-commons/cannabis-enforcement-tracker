Police carried out early-morning raids in the Juan Domingo neighborhood, leading
to three arrests. [@1] Officers took one resident into custody at the first
house for questioning in the investigation. [@1] Two men were arrested at a
second house on suspicion of drug-related offenses and illegal firearm
possession. [@1] All three suspects remained in custody while detectives
continued the investigation. [@1]

At the first residence, officers seized a motocross motorcycle after the
occupants could not explain its origin. [@1] At the second home, police found a
large quantity of marijuana packaged and ready for sale. [@1] Officers also
seized a firearm and other items considered important to the ongoing
investigation. [@1]

The raids were carried out by officers from several police units, including the
VVC and the arrest team. [@2] The three suspects were taken to the police
station as police continued the investigation. [@2] Police had not released the
identities of those arrested or said whether more arrests were expected. [@1]
