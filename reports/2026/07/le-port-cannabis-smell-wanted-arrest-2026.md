On 16 July 2026, officers from the Police-Secours brigade in Le Port intervened
in a residential building after noticing a strong smell of cannabis. [@1]
Following the smell to the stairwell it was coming from, the officers identified
a man wanted by the police and arrested him there. [@2] The man reacted
violently at the moment of arrest and physically resisted the officers. [@2]
Three police officers were injured while carrying out the arrest. [@1]

Police listed the allegations against him as drug use, rebellion and escape.
[@1] Imaz Press described the arrest as for use and possession of narcotics.
[@3] Officers told Imaz Press the man had been sought for several weeks for
failing to respect his semi-liberty conditions, an arrangement allowing a
detainee to spend part of the day outside detention. [@3]

The suspect was due to be brought before the prosecutor on Friday 17 July. [@1]
He was then scheduled to stand trial under the immediate-appearance procedure on
Monday 20 July. [@2]
