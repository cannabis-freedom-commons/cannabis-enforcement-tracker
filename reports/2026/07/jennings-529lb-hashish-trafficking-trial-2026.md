Denfield Roberts, 49, and Deshawn Lattibeaudiere, 23, both of Jennings, were
found with 52.9 pounds of hashish, about 24 kilograms. [@3] The seizure followed
a police raid in Jennings on 12 May 2024. [@1] Members of the Narcotics Squad
searched a property the two Jennings men occupied at about 3 pm on 12 May 2024.
[@3] Following the discovery, both men were taken into custody by the police.
[@4] Hashish is a concentrated form of cannabis, produced by compressing the
plant’s most potent components. [@1]

They were charged with possession and possession with intent to transfer of 52.9
pounds of hashish, and with being concerned with supplying, importation and drug
trafficking. [@3] Antigua News estimated the seized substance at $590,200. [@3]
Later accounts put the estimated street value at EC$600,415. [@1] Their lawyers
were absent when they appeared before Magistrate Ngaio Emanuel to seek bail.
[@3] The defendants were remanded in custody until the following day. [@3]

The trafficking trial of the two men, accused in connection with one of the
largest drug seizures in the country’s history, was set to begin on 23 July
2026. [@1] Both men are charged with drug trafficking. [@1] The men appeared
before Justice Ann Marie Smith and were given a new trial date. [@1] Attorney
Sherfield Bowen represents Roberts, while Lawrence Daniel represents
Lattibeaudiere. [@2]
