Officers of the anti-drug directorate of the St Petersburg and Leningrad Oblast police detained a
36-year-old resident of Mondelevo, a village in the Gatchina district. [@1,2] In two neighbouring
houses on his dacha plots they found growboxes with artificial lighting, ventilation and watering
systems. [@1,2]

The houses held 89 cannabis bushes and about 17.5 kilograms of ready plant mass, part of it already
packed into sacks; officers also seized electronic scales and packaging. [@1,2]

Tests confirmed the presence of a drug in the plants. [@1] The Gatchina police opened a criminal case
for attempted especially large-scale sale of drugs, and the man was placed in a temporary detention
centre. [@1,2] Police were looking for possible accomplices and further episodes. [@1]
