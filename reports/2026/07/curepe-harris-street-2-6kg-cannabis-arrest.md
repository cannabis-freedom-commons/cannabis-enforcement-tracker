Police arrested a 43-year-old woman after searching a house on Harris Street,
Curepe, on the night of Friday 31 July 2026. [@1] Officers of the Northern North
Division Task Force carried out the intelligence-led search between 7 p.m. and
10 p.m. after receiving information. [@1] In a bedroom they found an AR rifle,
several types of ammunition and cannabis-related items, according to the
Trinidad and Tobago Police Service (TTPS), the country's police force. [@1]

Police said they seized 2.6 kilogrammes of cannabis in five compressed packets
inside a black suitcase. [@1] The same search turned up 23 boxes of
cannabis-infused vapes and three boxes of cannabis-infused chocolates, which
police do not give a weight for. [@1] Officers also recovered 242 rounds of
ammunition in five calibres, along with an assault rifle and firearm magazines
and parts. [@1]

The woman remained in police custody while investigations continued, and the
operation was supervised by Cpl Griffith. [@1]
