State Security Service officers in Tashkent seized more than 20 kg of narcotics
from a 58-year-old native of Surkhandarya Region, the service's press office
said. [@1] While inspecting the man's temporary place of residence, officers
found 13 kg 50 g of opium and 7 kg 137 g of hashish. [@1]

Investigators were working to identify possible accomplices of the suspect and
the source of the seized drugs. [@1]
