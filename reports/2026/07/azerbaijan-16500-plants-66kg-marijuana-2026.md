On 29 July 2026, police in Azerbaijan removed more than 10.6 kilograms of
narcotic drugs from illegal circulation during operational measures, according
to the Interior Ministry. [@1] The ministry's breakdown put marijuana at about
6.6 kilograms and methamphetamine at more than 4 kilograms, making the reported
total a mixed-drug seizure rather than 10.6 kilograms of cannabis. [@1] Only the
approximately 6.6 kilograms of marijuana is recorded as cannabis by weight; the
methamphetamine is excluded from the cannabis figure. [@1]

Separately, police found and destroyed 16,500 wild-growing cannabis plants
during measures on the same date. [@2] The Interior Ministry also recorded 14
drug-related offenses detected on 29 July, as a same-day result alongside the
seizure and plant-destruction figures. [@1] The wild-plant eradication was
reported separately from the drug seizure and as a count of plants, not an
additional drug weight. [@1]
