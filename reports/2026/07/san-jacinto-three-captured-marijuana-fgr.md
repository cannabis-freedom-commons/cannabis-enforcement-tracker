El Salvador's Attorney General's Office announced the capture of three people it
accused of dedicating themselves to distributing and selling drugs in different
sectors of the country. [@1] The Fiscalía General de la República (FGR) named
them as Gerardo Borja Pineda, Jesse Cortez Rodríguez and Roxana Bejarano Rivas.
[@1] According to the investigations, the detainees had used the San Jacinto
neighbourhood in San Salvador as an operations centre for dosing and
distributing narcotics to other departments. [@1]

As part of the procedure the FGR carried out several searches in the area, where
various items were located and seized that would strengthen the investigation.
[@1] Among what was confiscated were portions of marijuana, cash, a firearm with
its magazine and ammunition, a scale, a grinder, mobile phones and a tablet.
[@1]

The Fiscalía said the three captured would be placed at the disposal of the
corresponding courts in the coming days to face judicial proceedings for the
offences attributed to them. [@1]
