Hyogo prefectural police re-arrested a Vietnamese man of no fixed address and no occupation, reported
as 31 and as 32 years old, on 9 November 2026 on suspicion of violating the Narcotics and
Psychotropics Control Act. [@1,3,5,12,13,14,16] Police suspect he sent ketamine and MDMA for profit to
a man living in Amagasaki, Hyogo Prefecture, in late April 2026, from Gunma Prefecture or nearby
[@1,4,8,10,14]; the warrant gives the period as April to May [@6], and one account says the drugs were
handed over for about 120,000 yen. [@11] He had already been indicted on offences including breaches of
the Immigration Control Act [@6], after his arrest in July 2026 for allegedly possessing cannabis plants
at a house in Honjo. [@10] He told investigators he would keep silent. [@1,4,8,16]

## The group

According to police, the group put drugs in Letter Pack envelopes, a flat-rate Japan Post mailer, and
sent them nationwide from Hokkaido to Okinawa, and also sold wholesale to dealers by hand.
[@1,4,13,16] It had seven or eight members and moved between bases in Saitama, Gunma and other
prefectures; police suspect the man was a courier working on the leader's orders, and say about
100,000 yen was paid into his bank account from a Vietnamese bank once a week. [@1,4,10]

The case began with an August 2025 robbery and assault in Kasai city: one of four Vietnamese
nationals arrested showed a drug reaction, and tracing his supplier led police to the Honjo house.
[@15] The investigation is run by the international investigation division of Hyogo police with Kasai
police station. [@15]

## The Honjo seizure

In July 2026 police entered the group's base, a house in Honjo, Saitama Prefecture, and seized a large
quantity of drugs. [@1,4,7,9] It included about 2 kg of stimulants worth about 104 million yen at
street prices and about 7.5 kg of cannabis worth about 37 million yen, with ketamine, cocaine and MDMA
valued together at about 150 million yen. [@1,4,6,8,14] Police valued the whole haul at more than 290
million yen and displayed it to reporters at Kasai police station [@1,2,3,6]; MBS put the value of the
July seizure at about 150 million yen. [@5] Police are investigating the rest of the group. [@1,3,6]
