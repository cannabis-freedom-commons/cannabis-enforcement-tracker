Authorities charged roughly 20 defendants in Morris County, New Jersey, for
allegedly selling illegal marijuana, THC vapes and flavored vapes. [@1] The
charges were announced in a press release on Friday, 10 July 2026, after a
multi-town crackdown. [@1] A Parsippany smoke shop was among the businesses
caught up in the sweep. [@1] Illegal marijuana, THC vapes and flavored vapes
were the products the defendants were accused of selling. [@1]

An employee of Green Lux Smoke Shop, located in Parsippany, was charged as part
of the sweep. [@1] The sweep also targeted businesses in Boonton, Montville,
Randolph and Roxbury Township. [@1] Parsippany-Troy Hills Police Chief Richard
Pantina joined eight other law enforcement leaders in announcing the charges.
[@1] Morris County Prosecutor Robert Carroll took part in the announcement. [@1]
Chief of Detectives Robert McNally took part in the announcement. [@1] Morris
County Sheriff James Gannon took part in the announcement. [@1] The police
chiefs of each participating town also took part in the announcement. [@1] Green
Lux Smoke Shop is located in Parsippany. [@1]
