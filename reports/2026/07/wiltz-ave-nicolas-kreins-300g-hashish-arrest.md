On Saturday evening a foot patrol of the Grand Ducal Police checked two people
in Avenue Nicolas Kreins in Wiltz who were apparently smoking a joint. [@3] The
patrol had come across the two individuals openly smoking joints in public on
Avenue Nicolas Kreinz on Saturday night. [@1] Officers searched one of the pair
and found about 300 grams of hashish on him. [@3]

The prosecutor's office then ordered the man's arrest. [@3] The officers seized
the drugs as well as the mobile phones of both people checked. [@3] RTL Today
reported that the man was arrested by order of the public prosecutor and that
both men's mobile phones were confiscated along with the drugs. [@1] The same
stop was recorded in the Grand Ducal Police press bulletin issued on Sunday.
[@3] Chronicle.lu likewise reported that officers on foot patrol in the Wiltz
street found about 300 grams of hashish on one of the two joint-smokers and
arrested him on the prosecutor's instructions. [@2]

Two individuals attracted police attention for smoking marijuana in public in
Wiltz, and one was found with 300g of hashish on his person. [@1]
