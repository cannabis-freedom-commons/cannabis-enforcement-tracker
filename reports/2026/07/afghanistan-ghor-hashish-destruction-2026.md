In western Ghor province, police burned more than 800 kilograms of narcotics
along with thousands of kilograms of precursor chemicals used in drug
production. [@1] Ghor police headquarters said the substances were destroyed on
Monday at a site away from residential areas in the presence of a designated
committee. [@1] Police said the narcotics and chemicals had been seized during
counter-narcotics operations. [@1] Police said the destruction followed relevant
regulations to prevent misuse and protect public health. [@1]

The destroyed items included 4 kilograms of hashish, the separately stated
cannabis component recorded here. [@1] The same items included 33 kilograms of
opium, 1.7 kilograms of methamphetamine, 799.2 kilograms of Type F narcotics and
two litres of alcohol. [@1] They also included 150 kilograms of processed yama,
10,000 kilograms of leafy yama, 600 litres of acetyl, 496 litres of acid, 4,405
kilograms of lime and 12,900 kilograms of caustic soda. [@1]

The Taliban administration announced the same destruction of more than 800
kilograms of narcotics with thousands of kilograms of precursor and chemical
substances in Ghor province. [@2] The Ghor Police Command's press office said
the substances were burned on Monday in the presence of an assigned delegation
at a site away from residential areas. [@2] That statement said the narcotics
had been seized during detection operations by anti-narcotics forces. [@2]
