A court in Almaty concluded the case of large-scale drug smuggling from
Thailand. [@1] The specialised inter-district criminal court of Almaty found a
22-year-old Kazakh citizen guilty of smuggling and of unlawful trafficking in
narcotic substances on an especially large scale. [@1] The court sentenced her
to 16 years in prison. [@2]

For a cash payment she had agreed to bring two suitcases containing cannabis
extract from Thailand to Kazakhstan. [@2] The cannabis extract weighed 8.8
kilograms in total. [@2] The two suitcases were found at Almaty airport after a
flight arrived from Bangkok. [@4] The smuggling was stopped immediately after
her arrival at the Almaty airport. [@2]

The prosecutor's office said the verdict had entered into legal force. [@3] The
account came from Kazakhstan's Main Transport Prosecutor's Office, the national
prosecution service for transport and border-crossing crime. [@1]

The prosecutor's office warned that offers of "easy earnings" and free trips
spread on social media are often part of international drug schemes. [@2] It
said that taking part in them carries strict criminal liability whatever a
person's role. [@2]
