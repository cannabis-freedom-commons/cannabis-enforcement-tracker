Units of the Syrian Arab Army's Border Guard Forces have foiled several attempts
in recent days to smuggle narcotics from Lebanese territory into Syria in the
countryside of Homs and Damascus. [@2] The operations resulted in the seizure of
more than 40 kilograms of hashish and over 700,000 Captagon pills. [@2] The
Syrian Ministry of Defense's Media and Communication Directorate said that units
of the Border Guard Forces of the Syrian Arab Army had foiled several attempts
in recent days to smuggle quantities of narcotics from Lebanese territory into
Syria in the countryside of Homs and Damascus. [@1]

The same directorate said the operations led to the confiscation of more than 40
kilograms of hashish in addition to over 700,000 Captagon pills. [@1] The
confiscated items were handed to the competent authorities to complete the legal
procedures; no details were given on the number of attempts, the identities of
those involved, or the timing of the operations. [@1]
