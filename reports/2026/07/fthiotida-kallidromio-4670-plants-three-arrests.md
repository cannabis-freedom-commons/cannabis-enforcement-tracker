Police arrested three people inside a large cannabis plantation in a forest area
of Fthiotida prefecture in central Greece, a case the Kalamata drug squad
handled. [@1] The operation took place in the morning of Friday 10 July 2026 on
Mount Kallidromio, with officers of the Special Suppression Anti-Terrorist Unit
(EKAM) and reinforcements from the Peloponnese and Central Greece regional
police taking part. [@2]

The three were arrested at the plot while asleep in tents among the plants: a
50-year-old Greek national and two foreign nationals aged 33 and 38, who lacked
legal residence documents. [@2] The plantation covered about 25 stremmata,
roughly 2.5 hectares, laid out in terraces in a cleared spot with dense natural
cover, and held 4,670 cannabis plants up to about 1.8 metres tall, which police
uprooted and seized. [@2]

Officers also found three makeshift drying rooms, living tents, an improvised
water tank for irrigation, a solar panel for power and 412 grams of cannabis.
[@1] Police seized a Zastava pistol, two magazines, 22 cartridges, seven mobile
phones, 440 euros in cash and cultivation tools and irrigation equipment. [@2]
Police estimated the organisation had been growing cannabis for at least three
months and that selling the plants would have earned it more than 9 million
euros, a figure based on the expected profit and not a market valuation of the
haul. [@1]

A case file was opened against the three for forming a criminal organisation,
drug-law offences and weapons offences, and against the foreign nationals for
immigration offences. [@2] The three were taken before the prosecutor, and the
Kalamata drug squad continued the investigation. [@1]
