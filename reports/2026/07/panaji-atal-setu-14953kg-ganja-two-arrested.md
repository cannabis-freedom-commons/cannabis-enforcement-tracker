Goa's Anti-Narcotic Cell raided the circle near the Kadamba (KTC) bus stand in
Panaji and exposed a drug-trafficking operation. [@1] The operation ran from
7.45 pm to 11.30 pm on 7 July in the presence of panch witnesses under
sub-inspector Girish Padloskar. [@1] Acting on information that drugs were being
brought into Panaji city for distribution, police laid a trap around Panaji
circle and detained Bhagirath Singh and Prashant Singh. [@3]

Police said Bhagirath Kumar Singh, 31, was carrying about 7.923 kg of ganja
worth about Rs 8 lakh. [@1] Police said Prashant Kumar Singh, 27, had about
7.030 kg of ganja worth about Rs 7 lakh. [@1] Together police recorded 14.953 kg
of ganja worth about Rs 15 lakh. [@1] Pudhari corroborated that the ANC seized
14.953 kg of ganja worth Rs 15 lakh at Panaji circle and arrested Bhagirath
Singh, 31, and Prashant Singh, 27. [@2] The ANC police station registered a case
under sections 20(b)(2)(B) and 29 of the NDPS Act, 1985. [@1]

After questioning, police said the two admitted they had brought the stock from
Akhilesh Gupta, 31, of Bihar. [@1] Police said Gupta was the racket's main
operator and supplier and was absconding. [@1] Pudhari said Gupta was absconding
and the ANC was searching for him. [@2] Police sent special teams to arrest the
main suspect and used technical surveillance to trace local agents and the
distribution network. [@3]

Tarun Bharat described the same seizure as 14 kg 953 grams worth about Rs 15
lakh at Panaji circle, the Atal Setu area, with two Bihar youths arrested. [@3]
