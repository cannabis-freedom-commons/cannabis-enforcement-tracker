Andrew Stephen Chard, 39, was sentenced to four years and eight months in Jersey's Royal Court on 24
July 2026 for importing 24 kg of cannabis resin. [@1,2] He had pleaded guilty when he first appeared
in court. [@1,4]

Chard arrived in Jersey on the ferry from Portsmouth on 16 March 2026 driving a UK-registered
Peugeot 3008, and customs officers selected the car for examination. [@1] They found 24 kg of
cannabis resin concealed in and around the front and rear bumpers; the Jersey Evening Post and
Bailiwick Express also reported packages in the engine bay. [@1,3,4] A customs expert put the maximum
street value in Jersey at £593,000. [@1,2]

## The cover story

Chard told officers that he had come for a trial shift at the Pomme d'Or Hotel, saying its name
translated as "Golden Apple" but not remembering the name itself; the hotel confirmed that the
account was false. [@2,4] The court heard that he had debts of more than £16,000 from cocaine and
gambling, and had been homeless and sofa-surfing for four months before his remand. [@3,4] His
advocate called him a vulnerable adult preyed upon by dealers. [@3,4] Deputy Bailiff Mark Temple said
Chard was a "trusted courier" who had booked the hire car himself and had been trusted with a
valuable quantity of drugs. [@3,4]
