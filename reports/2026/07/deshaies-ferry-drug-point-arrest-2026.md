Gendarmes arrested one suspect in the Ferry quarter of Deshaies during a
judicial operation on 8 July 2026. [@1] Officers arrested the individual at his
home without major incident and then searched the home for drugs and material
useful to the inquiry. [@1] The suspect was expected to answer for his acts
before the courts once the inquiry ended. [@1]

The home search recovered cannabis herb along with crack and cocaine. [@1]
Officers also recovered cash suspected to come from resale activity. [@1] The
seized products were to be analysed as part of the judicial proceedings. [@1]

The inquiry by Deshaies' Brigade territoriale autonome (BTA), the local
gendarmerie brigade, had begun months earlier after more interventions involving
people under the influence of drugs in the area. [@1] Investigators had
identified a suspected drug-selling point in Ferry feeding local traffic, which
justified opening a judicial operation. [@1] The 8 July operation brought
together Deshaies BTA personnel, investigators from the Détachement de
surveillance et d'intervention (DSI) of Pointe-Noire, the surveillance and
intervention detachment, and a dog team from Pointe-à-Pitre. [@1] The
gendarmerie presented the action as part of its strategy against the underground
economy, whether structured networks or local dealing. [@1]
