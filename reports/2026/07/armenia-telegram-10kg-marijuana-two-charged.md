Armenia's Investigative Committee completed the preliminary investigation into
an alleged attempt to circulate 10 kg of marijuana arranged through Telegram.
[@1] Investigators said two relatives, identified only as S.P. and A.P., went by
prior agreement to a location named by a Telegram user and collected 10 kg of
marijuana from a hiding place. [@1] National Security Service officers detained
them before they could complete the alleged offence. [@1]

A search of S.P.'s flat found marijuana allegedly kept for later sale, together
with electronic scales, adhesive tape and plastic bags, all of which were
seized. [@1] S.P. faces public prosecution for attempted especially large-scale
drug trafficking or sale and for drug trafficking for the purpose of sale. [@1]
A.P. is accused of attempted especially large-scale drug trafficking or sale.
[@1] Aurora News corroborates that the pair collected about 10 kg of marijuana
from the stash on a Telegram dealer's instruction. [@2] Radar.am likewise
reports the two collected 10 kg of marijuana from the stash and were detained by
National Security Service officers. [@3]

Both men were placed in custody as a preventive measure. [@1] The case file with
an indictment was sent to the supervising prosecutor for approval and referral
to court. [@1]
