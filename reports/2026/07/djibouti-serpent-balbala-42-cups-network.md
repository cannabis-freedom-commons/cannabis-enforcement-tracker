Djibouti's National Police narcotics brigade dismantled a suspected
cannabis-trafficking network operating in the capital's Plateau du Serpent and
Balbala commune. [@1] Investigators said the traffickers had gradually abandoned
traditional sales points and were using vehicles to transport and directly sell
the drugs. [@1] Officers deployed a rapid-interception operation and caught a
first suspect red-handed inside a vehicle with 11 cups of cannabis hidden in a
plastic bag, according to police. [@1]

Follow-up investigations identified several other people suspected of
involvement in the network. [@1] Searches at their homes found and seized 31
additional cups of cannabis. [@1]

In total, officers took 42 cups of cannabis off the streets, which police
estimated were worth more than 800,000 Djiboutian francs. [@1] The suspects were
brought before the Djibouti prosecutor's office and remanded in custody. [@1]
