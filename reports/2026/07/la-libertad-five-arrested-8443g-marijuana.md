Prosecutors from El Salvador's Fiscalía General de la República and National
Civil Police officers carried out raids on several properties in the department
of La Libertad on Sunday 26 July 2026. [@1] The operation followed a citizen
alert that drugs were being stored and distributed in the area. [@2] Officers
detained five people in total: three adults and two minors. [@2]

The FGR said the detainees would be prosecuted in the coming hours for illicit
trafficking. [@1] El Diario de Hoy reported that the detainees were to be
brought before the courts to be formally charged with illicit trafficking. [@3]

The FGR asked residents to report illegal activity, and the seized drugs were to
be used as evidence in the case. [@3]

Illicit trafficking is the most serious offence under El Salvador's drug law,
the Ley Reguladora de las Actividades Relativas a las Drogas, and carries 10 to
15 years in prison. [@1]
