Gendarmes stopped a cannabis transfer on the seafront at Bras-Panon, Réunion, on
Sunday 26 July 2026. [@2,3,5] Witnesses alerted officers after seeing two men moving
suitcases towards a boat on the shore. [@1,5] The transfer was taking place near
the Rivière du Mât on the Bras-Panon seafront. [@2,3,5] The gendarmes searched the
area after the alert. [@1] Officers found 10 sacks that had been carefully
hidden. [@1,5] They held 156 kg of cannabis herb in total. [@2] Officers arrested
the two suspects and placed them in custody. [@2,3,5] The gendarmerie said the cargo
was meant to leave by sea. [@1,5]

After custody, investigators established that the cannabis was to be transferred
to Mauritius. [@2,3] Both men were brought before the public prosecutor. [@1] A
judicial investigation was opened to trace the network and identify its
branches. [@1,5] The gendarmerie said it had deployed a large operation involving
multiple units. [@1] It thanked the witnesses, whose alert it called decisive.
[@2] It said the case showed how a simple report can take large quantities of
drugs off the market. [@1]

The Financial Crimes Commission seized two speedboats that may have served to import the Réunion zamal into Mauritius at a suspect's home on 7 September 2026. [@4] Ten people were being prosecuted over the attempted shipment by early September 2026, five in Mauritius and five in Réunion. [@4]

The gendarmerie deployed about 20 officers, a helicopter and divers for the operation. [@5] The helicopter helped spot a boat that fled, and whether it was involved had yet to be established. [@5]
