Three men were arrested at the Mile 38 checkpoint on Thursday 16 July 2026 in a
joint operation by the Sierra Leone Police and the Republic of Sierra Leone Armed
Forces. [@1] They were Hassan Mansaray and Foday Mansaray, both of Hastings, and
Mohamed Kamara, a motorcycle rider using registration AZX 348. [@1]

Police said the two Mansarays were on the motorcycle with a bale of dried leaves
suspected to be cannabis sativa, known locally as jamba. [@2] As they reached the
checkpoint the two got off and tried to carry the bale past the screening on foot,
while Kamara rode on alone. [@1] Superintendent Foday Sorie Fofanah, the officer
commanding the checkpoint, noticed them and called his team; a pursuit ended with
the three arrested and the bale recovered. [@1]

The suspects and the suspected cannabis were held in police custody while the
investigation continued. [@2] The police statement gave no weight for the bale and
named no charge or court date. [@1,2]
