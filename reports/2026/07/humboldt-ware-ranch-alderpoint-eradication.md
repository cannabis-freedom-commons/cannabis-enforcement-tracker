On 16 July 2026, deputies with the Humboldt County Sheriff's Office Marijuana Enforcement Team
served a search warrant at an illegal cannabis grow in the 200 block of Ware Ranch Road in the
Alderpoint area. [@1,2] The California Department of Fish and Wildlife and the California
Department of Cannabis Control assisted in serving the warrant. [@1,2] The sheriff's office said
the property was identified through aerial surveillance and lacked the required county permit and
state licence for commercial cultivation. [@1,2]

Deputies eradicated about 2,180 growing cannabis plants and 1,199 cannabis clone starts, 3,379
plants in total, and seized and destroyed over 2,300 pounds (about 1,043 kg) of cannabis
bud. [@1,2] On scene they found substandard living conditions for the people working the site,
conditions the office said are often a sign of labour exploitation; no one was present when the
warrant was served. [@1,2]

Assisting agencies also recorded water diversion, stream alteration and rubbish-dumping
violations, carrying civil fines of up to 8,000 dollars a day per violation, or up to 20,000
dollars a day for rubbish in or near a waterway. [@1,2] No arrests were made, and the case will
be forwarded to the District Attorney's Office for review. [@1,2]
