Police in Toulouse arrested the sender of cannabis parcels to Martinique and
placed him in police custody. [@1] He first claimed he did not know what the
parcels contained. [@1] After his presentation on 16 July, prosecutors referred
him to the Toulouse correctional court for immediate trial. [@2]

Overseas customs officers intercepted one parcel in Martinique in March 2024
with almost 900 grams of cannabis resin and a second in February 2025 with one
kilo. [@1] Both parcels held cannabis resin from mainland France that did not
escape the overseas customs officers. [@1] Officers intercepted one parcel in
Martinique then a second from the same sender. [@1] Investigators noted he
regularly bought cannabis resin and frequently went to La Poste to post parcels,
suggesting other shipments may exist. [@2]

The Rive droite local judicial police were tasked with tracing the man living in
northern Toulouse after the customs seizures. [@1] The case reflects a reverse
flow in which cannabis resin is brought into Guadeloupe and Martinique while
cocaine generally transits from the Antilles toward mainland France. [@2]
