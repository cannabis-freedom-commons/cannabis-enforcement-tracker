Nepal’s Narcotics Control Bureau arrested two Indian nationals at Tribhuvan
International Airport in Kathmandu. [@1] Both of those arrested at the airport
were women. [@1] One suspect was identified as 20-year-old Mona Devi Longjam
from Manipur. [@1] The other suspect was identified as 23-year-old Oinam Ally,
also from Manipur. [@1] The bureau took both suspects into custody at the
airport. [@1]

The pair had arrived in Kathmandu from Bangkok aboard Thai Airways flight TG
309. [@1] Bureau officers intercepted the two suspects after they raised
suspicion at the airport. [@1] Officers searching their luggage discovered four
kilograms of cannabis concealed inside their suitcases. [@1] Police seized the
cannabis contraband found in the suitcases. [@1] Officers also seized two
passports and two mobile phones from the suspects. [@1]

The cannabis had been brought from Bangkok and hidden in suitcase luggage. [@1]
A further investigation into the matter was currently under way. [@1] The two
passports and two mobile phones were held as part of that ongoing investigation.
