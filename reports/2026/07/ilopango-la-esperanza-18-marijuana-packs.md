Soldiers and the National Civil Police arrested 24-year-old Kevin Josué Galeas
Franco in the comunidad La Esperanza, in the Ilopango district of San Salvador
Este. [@4] The operation took place on the morning of Sunday 12 July 2026. [@1]
Elements of the Fuerza Armada de El Salvador, the country's armed forces,
located him during an operation in the comunidad La Esperanza. [@2] After the
intervention the suspect was handed over to the Policía Nacional Civil. [@2] He
was to be sent to the courts for the offence of illicit drug trafficking. [@4]

The authorities seized 18 packages of marijuana, two electronic scales and two
mobile phones. [@3] According to the official report, he also had a glass jar
holding an additional quantity of the same substance. [@1] No weight or market
value for the marijuana was published. [@1]

Defence Minister René Francis Merino Monroy announced the seizure on his X
account. [@3] "Seguimos golpeando el narcomenudeo y cerrando cada espacio
utilizado para contaminar nuestras comunidades con droga, protegiendo la
tranquilidad, seguridad y salud de las familias salvadoreñas," the minister
published. [@1] He said the operation formed part of the strategy to hit
narcomenudeo, street-level drug dealing, and close the spaces used to distribute
drugs. [@3]
