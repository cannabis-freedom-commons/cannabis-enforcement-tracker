In Nukus, officers of the anti-smuggling department of the Karakalpakstan
Customs Administration acted against illicit drug trafficking in a joint
operation. [@1] Officers stopped a local resident moving around Nukus during the
operation. [@1] In the presence of attesting witnesses, officers carried out a
personal search of the stopped man. [@1]

Inside his black handbag, officers found 16 matchboxes containing marijuana.
[@1] The total weight of the seized substance with its packaging was 77.56
grams. [@1] The UZA account likewise says 16 matchboxes hidden in the black
handbag held marijuana weighing 77.56 grams with its packaging. [@2]

The operation was conducted jointly with officers of the State Security Service,
internal-affairs bodies and the National Guard. [@1] The Karakalpakstan Customs
Administration opened a criminal case under the relevant articles of
Uzbekistan's Criminal Code over the seizure. [@1] Investigative actions were
continuing and no court outcome had been reported. [@1]
