Kemin District police cut and burned about 1 tonne of wild cannabis across 0.5 hectare in the Chon-Kemin rural district of Chüy Region. [@1,2]

Kabar said the destruction took place during the “Cannabis-2026” operational-preventive campaign organized by the Chüy regional police. [@1] The Kyrgyz-language account repeated the approximate amount and area but added no suspect or arrest. [@2]
