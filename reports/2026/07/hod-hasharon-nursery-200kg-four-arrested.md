An undercover investigation by the special crime unit of the Kfar Saba police
station led officers to a plant nursery in Hod Hasharon on Tuesday 21 July,
where they uncovered a warehouse suspected of serving drug division and
distribution. [@3] Officers raided the nursery and found a concealed warehouse
that served as the network's distribution center. [@2]

During the search of the premises, officers seized an enormous quantity of
marijuana and hashish with a combined weight of more than 200 kilograms. [@2] A
large share of the seized drugs had been hidden inside ordinary food products,
including chocolate bars, cookies and sweets, all of them packed and ready for
immediate distribution. [@2] Much of the haul was concealed in chocolate,
cookies and candy packaging prepared for immediate distribution. [@1]

Four suspects, all residents of central Israel, were arrested during the
operation. [@3] Officers brought the four suspects before a court, which granted
the police request and extended their detention until 27 July 2026. [@2] After
questioning, the suspects were brought before a court that extended their
detention until 27 July. [@3]
