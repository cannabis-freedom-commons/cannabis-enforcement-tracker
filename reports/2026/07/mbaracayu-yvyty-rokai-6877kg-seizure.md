Members of Paraguay's Armed Forces and National Police seized almost seven
tonnes of marijuana stored in a nature reserve administered by the Itaipu
hydroelectric plant for shipment to Brazil. [@1] Those guarding the cache fled
into dense woods when forces reached the site, so no arrests were made. [@1]
Anti-drug prosecutor Luis Escobar took charge of the case and ordered the whole
load moved to the naval base at Ciudad del Este, while the investigation
continues to identify those responsible for the stockpile. [@2]

The seizure totalled 6,877 kilograms of marijuana divided into 299 packages.
[@2] The packages were hidden in vegetation about 30 metres from the shore of
Lake Itaipu at a clandestine riverside port in the district of Mbaracayú in
northern Alto Paraná. [@2] The find was made in the area of Colonia Yvytu Rokái,
also identified as the perimeter of Colonia Guaraní, following intelligence work
by the Alto Paraná police. [@2] The drugs lay inside the protection fringe of
the Yvyty Rokai Nature Reserve, which is managed by the hydroelectric plant
shared by Brazil and Paraguay. [@1] The load of pressed marijuana was ready to
be sent by speedboats to Brazilian territory as part of a transfer planned by a
criminal group. [@2]

Officials ruled out laboratories or marijuana plantations in the reserve, saying
the difficult-to-access protected area of more than 4,800 hectares had served
only as a storage place before the planned move to Brazil. [@1] Strong winds and
dangerous waves on the lake prevented the use of small boats, so larger naval
vessels were brought in to remove the bulky load. [@2]
