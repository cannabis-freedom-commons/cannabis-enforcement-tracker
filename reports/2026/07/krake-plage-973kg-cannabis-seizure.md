The Krakè-Plage border police uncovered a clandestine cannabis depot at
Krakè-Plage on 23 July 2026 during an anti-drug-trafficking operation. [@1] The
cannabis was stored in a makeshift dwelling on the seafront and had reportedly
arrived by sea before being stored there. [@1] The people sought in the case
were not present when officers intervened. [@1] The Republican Police said it
deployed a system to identify and arrest the people involved. [@1] Officers
secured the drugs and all seized property pending judicial proceedings. [@1]

Officers recovered 25 large bags containing balls of Indian hemp. [@1] The total
weight of the consignment was 973.65 kilograms. [@1] The search, carried out in
the presence of local authorities, also seized 1,506,000 nairas in cash, twenty
40-kilogram bags of plaster powder, 21 straw buckets, two small buckets of
plaster-based products, 13 plastic tables, two outboard motors and various other
items. [@1] A second account confirms that Sèmè-Kraké Republican Police officers
dismantled the depot on 23 July 2026 and seized 25 bags of Indian hemp. [@2]

Investigators described the site as a storage point used by people who came
there only at night. [@1] Investigators believe the goods were transported by
sea to reduce the risk of detection and protect them from humidity. [@1] The
Krakè-Plage seafront is among the sectors regularly monitored by security forces
because of cross-border exchanges with Nigeria. [@1]
