Officers of the Directorate of Narcotics and Drugs Police (DPSD), Ivory Coast's
anti-drug police, arrested one person and seized drugs in the Samaké quarter of
Abobo during Operation Vacances Sécurisées XI. [@1] The intervention on that
Tuesday followed a tip that a delivery of cannabis described as "OG KUSCH" was
imminent in that part of the commune. [@1] A team from the DPSD's Tri Postal
branch was deployed to the area to carry out checks and identify those involved.
[@1]

The officers' inquiries led them to arrest an individual found in possession of
5,500 kg (5.5 kg) of cannabis described as "OG KUSCH". [@1] Police also seized a
black KTM motorcycle that the police note said was used in connection with his
activities. [@1] Abidjan.net reported the weight and the motorcycle seizure from
a police note it had consulted. [@1]

Police presented the Abobo action as part of Ivorian security services'
holiday-period campaign against drug trafficking. [@1] The campaign was
described as intended to strengthen public safety and to dismantle drug
distribution networks. [@1]
