On the night of 20 to 21 July 2026, officers of Luxembourg's Grand-Ducal Police
were called to an apartment building in Schifflange, in the south of the
country, to deal with a reported dispute. [@1] Officers entered one apartment to
restore calm and were immediately struck by a strong smell of cannabis hanging
in the air. [@1] Continuing their observations, they discovered several
containers holding the substance. [@1] Informed of the facts, the Parquet,
Luxembourg's public prosecutor's office, ordered the seizure of the drugs and
several mobile telephones found at the scene. [@1]

Initial inquiries then led investigators to a second person suspected of
involvement in drug trafficking. [@2] In a further search of that suspect's home
and vehicle, also ordered by the prosecutor, police found one cannabis plant, a
grow tent used to produce cannabis and several containers filled with cannabis.
[@2] Officers also seized a mobile telephone belonging to the suspect. [@2]

Police drew up an official report on the seizures and the investigation
continued under the authority of the prosecutor. [@1]
