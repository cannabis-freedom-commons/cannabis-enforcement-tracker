A joint team from Kodam Iskandar Muda destroyed an estimated 50 hectares of cannabis fields in
protected forest at Blang Meurandeh village, Beutong Ateuh Banggalang, Nagan Raya, Aceh, on
Wednesday 8 July 2026. [@1,2]

ThreeNews said the fields contained plants ranging from 15 days to four months old and that some
had reached harvest age. [@1] Metro TV reported that the team destroyed about 300 tonnes of wet
cannabis described as ready for harvest. [@2]

ThreeNews said the operation involved about 70 personnel and required a 70-kilometre vehicle trip
followed by a seven-kilometre walk to the site. [@1] Metro TV said the team walked for about seven
hours through difficult terrain and that investigators were still trying to identify the field's
owner and the network behind it. [@2]
