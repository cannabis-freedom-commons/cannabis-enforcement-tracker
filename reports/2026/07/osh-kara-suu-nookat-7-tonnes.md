Reports published on 10 July 2026 said that police in Osh Region had destroyed
7 tonnes of wild cannabis in the Kara-Suu and Nookat districts. [@1,2] The
reported areas were 4 sotki (0.04 hectare) and 3.5 hectares, a combined 3.54
hectares. [@1,2]

The same account said that eradication work was also taking place in Osh city,
including the Manas-Ata microdistrict and the Jalapalak and Kyzyl-Kyshtak
territorial administrations, but gave no quantity for that work. [@1] Those
unquantified activities are not included in the reported 7-tonne figure.
