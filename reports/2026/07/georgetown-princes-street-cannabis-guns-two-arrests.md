CANU officers, acting on intelligence received, conducted an operation at a
residence on Princess Street, Georgetown. [@2] The house searched was on Princes
Street, Lodge, Georgetown. [@1] CANU said two Guyanese men were arrested in
connection with the bust. [@1] CANU's own statement said several persons were
arrested and are in custody assisting with investigations. [@2] Guyana Times
reported that the individuals present at the premises were arrested and taken
into custody. [@3]

The search found 86 parcels of foreign marijuana weighing 98.32 pounds (about
44.6 kg). [@1] CANU valued the cannabis at approximately eighty million Guyana
dollars (GYD $80,000,000). [@2] Officers also found five firearms at the
location, four pistols and one rifle. [@1]

The operation took place on Wednesday at Princes Street, Georgetown, and was
carried out by the Customs Anti-Narcotics Unit (CANU). [@3]
