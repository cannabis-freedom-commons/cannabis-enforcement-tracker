The Slavyansk city court in Krasnodar Krai sentenced a 42-year-old local man to 10 years and two
months in a strict-regime penal colony for attempted large-scale drug trafficking. [@1,2] The court
found that from September 2022 to December 2025 he grew cannabis on his household plot and prepared
the harvest for sale. [@1,2]

Police seized 28 polymer packets of marijuana and more than a kilogram of hashish divided into five
paper wraps. [@1] Utrenniy Yug reported that the cannabis had grown from four bushes. [@2] The
prosecution said the drugs were intended for sale and that officers stopped the plan before a buyer
was found. [@1,2]

The prosecutors' office said the sentence followed its position; the charge was attempted
large-scale sale of drugs. [@1,2]
