On Wednesday 8 July 2026, authorities in Yei River County destroyed a quantity of seized marijuana, known locally as bangi. [@1,3] County Commissioner Samuel Henry Malimbo said the drugs had been seized the night before: "The owner dropped them and ran away." [@1] No one was named as arrested. [@1]

Malimbo said he had passed a local order banning the growing of marijuana in Yei, and that anyone caught growing, selling or distributing it would be arrested and prosecuted. [@1] "Any drugs we find in any store or in possession of anyone shall be destroyed," he said. [@1] Police Commissioner Major General Ali Abdullahtif said patrols were still seizing marijuana from people selling, smoking or growing it, and that marijuana and spirit drinks are banned in Central Equatoria State. [@1] Community elder Antony Dicko welcomed the destruction and asked leaders to extend the crackdown to all five payams of Yei. [@1]

One Citizen Daily and Access Radio, the Yei station, add a detail Radio Tamazuj does not: an unidentified person threw marijuana over the fence of Yei Prison and fled, and the search was still open. [@2,3] Neither account gives a weight. [@1,2] Juba TV's report of the same destruction carries no separate figures. [@4]
