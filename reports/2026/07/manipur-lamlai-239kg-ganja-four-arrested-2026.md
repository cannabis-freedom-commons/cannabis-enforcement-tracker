Manipur Police arrested four people and seized about 239 kg of ganja in a series
of operations carried out at different locations under Lamlai police station in
Imphal East district on 4 July 2026. [@2] Officials said the four were arrested
with 239 kg of contraband ganja, or marijuana, during the 24 hours before the
report. [@1]

Officers caught Mataimei W. Shimray of Ukhrul district while he was driving a
Mahindra Bolero pick-up truck. [@2] Two 18-year-old men, Ngangbam Dinesh and
Phuritshabam Yaiphaba Meitei, were arrested at Chingarel Tejpur together with a
TVS Apache motorcycle. [@2] In a separate but related operation, security forces
arrested Oinam Abung Meetei, 42, at his home in Pungdongbam Makha Leikai. [@2]

The same 24-hour police roundup also reported the arrest of six militants from
the KCP and UNLF and an arms recovery in Kangpokpi district, which are separate
events left out of this report. [@1]

Police took the seized contraband and the recovered vehicles into custody and
said investigation into the case was under way. [@2]
