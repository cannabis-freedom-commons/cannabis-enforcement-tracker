Gendarmes of Grupo Santo Pipó, under Squadron 11 San Ignacio, were posted on
Ruta Provincial Nº 7 at a checkpoint when the driver of a car saw them, turned
sharply and fled. [@1] After a lockdown operation alerting other units of the
force, the gendarmes following the car saw it turn into a rural road. [@1] The
car's two occupants tried to discard part of the load and escape on foot, and
were detained. [@1]

Inside the car, officers saw numerous plastic-wrapped bundles with blue packages
in the rear compartment. [@1] The search counted 312 rectangular packages
holding a compact substance that tested positive for marijuana, weighing 231
kilos 214 grams. [@1]

The Oberá federal court and federal prosecutor's office took the case and
ordered the drug and the car seized. [@1] They ordered the two people held in
detention incommunicado. [@1]
