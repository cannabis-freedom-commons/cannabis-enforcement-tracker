A joint team from the District Police Office Parsa and the Armed Police Force's
BOP Mirjapur searched an orchard in Mirjapur, Chhipaharmai Rural Municipality-2,
at around 11:30 pm on 25 July 2026. [@1] Police said the search followed a
tip-off. [@1]

Officers found 140 kilograms of marijuana in 13 packages concealed inside a pile
of grass in the orchard. [@1] The marijuana was seized and police began further
investigation. [@1]

Police did not disclose whether any suspects had been arrested over the find.
[@1] The District Police Office, Parsa, said it was investigating to identify
those involved in the case. [@1]
