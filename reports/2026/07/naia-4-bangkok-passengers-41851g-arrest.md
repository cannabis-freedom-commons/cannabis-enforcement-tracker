Four passengers arriving from Bangkok, Thailand were arrested after the Bureau
of Customs intercepted suspected marijuana in their baggage at Ninoy Aquino
International Airport on 30 July 2026. [@1] The arrest took place at Terminal 3
in Pasay City during a joint anti-drug operation. [@2] The four suspects were
turned over to the Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency for the filing of charges
for alleged importation of dangerous drugs. [@2]

Officers found approximately 41.85 kilograms of suspected marijuana concealed
inside the passengers' luggage. [@2] The drugs were packed in eight pieces of
luggage brought by the four suspects. [@1] Authorities estimated the seized
drugs' street value at P62,776,500. [@2] The eight pieces of luggage were
flagged after undergoing X-ray screening and K9 inspection. [@1] The eight
luggage items were then subjected to further inspection after suspicious
findings in the X-ray screening and K9 inspection. [@1]

The operation joined the Bureau of Customs anti-drug task force, intelligence
and X-ray units with the airport drug-interdiction task group, the drug
enforcement agency and the Bureau of Immigration. [@2] The Bureau of Customs
said the agency with the drug enforcement agency, the Bureau of Immigration and
other law enforcement agencies continued investigation and case-building on the
attempted smuggling. [@1]
