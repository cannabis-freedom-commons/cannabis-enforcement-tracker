The arrests took place at about 8:30 a.m. on 27 July 2026 in the international
departures terminal at Phuket airport. [@2] Customs officers arrested four
foreign nationals preparing to leave Thailand after finding cannabis flowers and
processed cannabis in their luggage. [@2] Two of those held were young men from
Vaud aged 18 and 19, arrested at Phuket with 16 kilos of cannabis in their
suitcases. [@3] Recruiters offered young French and Swiss free travel to
Thailand with flights, accommodation and pocket money covered. [@1] The
recruiters promised 600 francs in pocket money and 8,000 francs to bring back a
suitcase supposedly filled with iPhones. [@1]

Officials put the combined seizure at more than 33.6 kg of cannabis products.
[@2] All four were charged with attempting to take goods out of Thailand without
customs procedures and handed to investigators at Sakhu Police Station. [@2] One
Vaudois first paid a 6,300-franc fine to customs and was able to leave Thailand
quickly. [@3] In total he had to spend about 18,000 francs to leave the country
without difficulty and without a criminal record. [@3] The second Vaudois stayed
detained in Thailand awaiting trial after his mother could not raise about 4,000
francs requested for his release in time. [@3]

Thai authorities considerably reinforced controls on cannabis exports from 18
June 2026. [@1] A 30,000-baht fine per kilogram seized applies, and non-payment
can lead to custody and detention awaiting judgment. [@1] France's foreign
ministry warns that penalties run from two to ten years' imprisonment. [@1]
Thailand had decriminalised cannabis in 2022 but its export stayed prohibited.
[@1] The case was part of a wider summer pattern in which eleven French
nationals were arrested in Thai airports with several kilos of cannabis. [@1]
