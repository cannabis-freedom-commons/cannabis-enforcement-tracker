Casey Stewart, 27, was brought before the city's magistrates' court following an
incident on 7 July. [@1] A police constable saw the occupant of a Vauxhall
discard what was later found to be a small amount of cannabis out of the window
on Wigton Road. [@1] A drug wipe test gave a positive reading for cannabis, and
a blood sample showed the level of a main psychoactive ingredient of the Class B
controlled drug was above the legal driving limit. [@1]

Stewart, of Low Row near Brampton, pleaded guilty to drug-driving at the
hearing. [@1] After magistrates read a letter which she had submitted to the
hearing and contained information about Stewart's personal circumstances, they
imposed a £120 fine and a 12-month driving ban. [@1] Stewart must also pay £85
prosecution costs and a £48 mandatory surcharge. [@1]
