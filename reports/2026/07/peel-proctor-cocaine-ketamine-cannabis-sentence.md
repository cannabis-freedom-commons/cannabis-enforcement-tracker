Robert Proctor was jailed for a total of three years and 11 months. [@1] The
24-year-old is from Peel. [@1] Passing sentence at Douglas Courthouse, Deemster
Graeme Cook said Proctor played a significant role in supplying drugs which
caused families misery. [@1] Proctor admitted supplying Class A and B drugs
between September 2023 and September 2024. [@1] He also pleaded guilty to
possessing cannabis on two occasions, which he said he used daily to treat his
ADHD. [@1]

Police first searched Proctor's home on 5 September 2024 after arresting him
over a separate matter. [@1] Officers found 3.2g of cannabis and seized his
phone in that search. [@1] A second search warrant carried out in January 2025
found a further 15.9g of cannabis and another mobile phone. [@1] Phone evidence
showed Proctor was involved in the supply of cocaine, cannabis and ketamine and
collecting money from drug sales. [@1] The messages indicated he supplied
between 66g and 80g of cocaine, 31g of ketamine and 271.5g of cannabis over 12
months. [@1] Prosecutors argued he played a significant role in the operation
rather than acting only as a street-level dealer. [@1]

He was arrested again at his home address on 1 January. [@2] Deemster Cook
reduced the sentence for remorse, his early guilty plea and a change in
lifestyle. [@1] The headline term was imposed for being concerned in supplying
cocaine, with concurrent nine-month terms for ketamine and cocaine supply and
14- and 21-day terms for possession. [@2]
