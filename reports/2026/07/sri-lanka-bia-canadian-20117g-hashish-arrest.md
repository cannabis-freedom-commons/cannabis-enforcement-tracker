Sri Lanka Customs officers arrested a Canadian national at Bandaranaike
International Airport on 26 July 2026 while he was attempting to smuggle hashish
into the country. [@1] The arrested suspect is a 27-year-old Canadian national.
[@2] The 27-year-old works as a painter and had bought the drugs in Toronto
before travelling via Doha to Sri Lanka on Qatar Airways flight QR-662, arriving
at around 2:30 a.m. [@1]

Officers of the Customs Narcotics Control Unit found 20 kilograms and 117 grams
of brown-coloured hashish in 80 slabs inside his two pieces of luggage. [@1]
Officials estimated the seized hashish was worth Rs. 201.89 million. [@1]

Investigators said he had bought the narcotics in Toronto and travelled via Doha
on a connecting Qatar Airways flight to Sri Lanka. [@3] He arrived at Katunayake
airport at around 2:30 a.m. on 26 July aboard flight QR-662. [@2] The suspect
and the seized drugs were to be handed to Police Narcotics Bureau officers at
Katunayake Airport for further investigation. [@1]
