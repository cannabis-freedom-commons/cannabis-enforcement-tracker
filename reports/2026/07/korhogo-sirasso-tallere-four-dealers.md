Police in the northern city of Korhogo arrested four suspected drug dealers
during Operation Vacances sécurisées XI, the national police's school-holiday
security drive. [@1] The four, named only by their initials, are N.B., 25, C.M.,
18, D.I., 26, and Y.K., 32. [@1] The regional branch of the Direction de la
police des stupéfiants et des drogues (DPSD), the national police's anti-drug
directorate, in Korhogo carried out the operation after reports of drug sales in
Sirasso and Talléré, in the Poro region. [@1]

Officers seized four and a half blocks of cannabis and 145 cannabis balls, the
small hand-made pellets sold in the region. [@1] Police also took 301 Tramadol
tablets, a carton of OCB rolling papers and 279,000 francs CFA that they believe
came from drug sales. [@1] The General Directorate of the National Police
announced the operation in a statement issued on Saturday 18 July 2026. [@2]

The directorate said the operation formed part of its national fight against
drug trafficking and use, mounted across the country during the school holidays.
[@1]

Nordinfos listed the four arrests and the 279,000 francs CFA as parts of the
same operation against drug sales in Sirasso and Talléré. [@2]
