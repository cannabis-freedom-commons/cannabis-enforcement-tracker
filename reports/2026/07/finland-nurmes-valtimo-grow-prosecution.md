Police in eastern Finland finished their investigation into an indoor cannabis
grow found in Nurmes and Lieksa and sent the case to prosecutors on 6 July 2026.
[@1,2]

Acting on a public tip and their own inquiries, officers found a commercial
property in Valtimo, in Nurmes, that smelled strongly of cannabis. [@1,2] A
search on 11 February 2026 found 241 cannabis plants at different stages of
growth and some finished cannabis. [@1,2] Two further searches at homes in
Lieksa found 34 and 17 plants. [@1] Police seized the plants and dismantled the
grows. [@1]

The plants, just over 290 in all, would have produced an estimated 10 to 30 kg
of cannabis, police said. [@1,2] They put its street value at between €120,000
and €500,000. [@1,2]

Three men — a father and his two sons, all of foreign background — are suspected
of one aggravated drug offence and two drug offences. [@1,2] All three were
remanded in custody during the investigation. [@1]
