Guatemala's National Civil Police, through its anti-narcotics unit SGAIA,
located and eradicated cannabis plantations in the mountainous areas of the
hamlet of Ixmucané, in the municipality of Las Cruces in the department of
Petén. [@1] The National Civil Police said it acted in coordination with the
Public Ministry after locating more than 47,000 marijuana plants during security
patrols. [@1] No captures were reported in the operation. [@1]

The plants were growing in two fields concealed inside the wooded area to avoid
detection. [@1] Police put the official valuation at 23,784,000 quetzales, about
three million dollars on the illegal market. [@1] Authorities said the plants
were removed from the fields and subsequently incinerated on site. [@1]

SGAIA said the actions reaffirm its commitment to fighting drug trafficking by
reducing cultivation and trade of illicit drugs. [@2] Interior Ministry figures
cited in the coverage note that joint operations with the army in early 2026
have focused heavily on mountainous and border areas. [@1]
