Yemen's General Department for Combating Drugs and its provincial branches
reported detaining 734 suspects in 484 drug cases in the first half of 2026, in
operations against smuggling and trafficking networks. [@1] All 734 were Yemeni
nationals, 731 men and three women. [@1] The cases included 245 for use of drugs
and 190 for trafficking, and a single case of cultivating drug plants. [@2] The
department counted suspects by case type, including 385 in use cases and 286 in
trafficking cases. [@1]

The department said officers seized 613.7 kg of hashish, alongside 617.6 kg of
shabu (methamphetamine), 100 kg of heroin, 4 kg of opium, 1,000,182 Captagon
pills and 41,768 other drug pills. [@1] The department said all the seized
material was held as evidence and that the legal procedures were completed
against the suspects, whose cases were sent to the competent authorities. [@2]

The statement was published on 11 July 2026; an independent Yemeni tally that
month put the government areas' total seizure at about 1.29 tonnes of drugs for
the first half of the year. [@3]
