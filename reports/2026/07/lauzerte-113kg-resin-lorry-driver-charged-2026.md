Customs officers arrested a lorry driver carrying more than 100 kg of cannabis
resin on a country road at Lauzerte, Tarn-et-Garonne, on Wednesday 1 July 2026,
after he tried to force a customs roadblock in his lorry. [@1] The officers
deployed tyre-deflating spikes under the wheels, and the truck stopped in the
middle of the carriageway. [@1] The customs officers were lying in wait on the
country road, and a caption on the page said they had to use a stop stick to
intercept the truck. [@1]

Officers found 113 kg of cannabis resin hidden in a magnetic cache in the truck,
which had come from Spain. [@1] The resin, in a cache hidden in the structure of
the lorry, was described as worth several hundred thousand euros. [@2] The truck
had come from Malaga in Spain. [@2]

The driver was charged and placed in pre-trial detention by the Montauban court
on Friday 3 July 2026. [@2] He was charged with importing, transporting and
unlawfully holding narcotics and with customs smuggling. [@2] The court
justified holding him because he lives in Livorno, Italy, and has no address or
ties in France. [@2] The Toulouse anti-narcotics office (Ofast), France's
central drug-enforcement unit, was tasked with tracing the network behind the
shipment. [@2] The Montauban public prosecutor opened a judicial investigation
into the drug-trafficking case. [@2]
