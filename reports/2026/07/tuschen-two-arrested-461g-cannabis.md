Police in Regional Division Three arrested a 29-year-old male labourer and a
31-year-old unemployed woman at Tuschen Housing Scheme, East Bank Essequibo, on
Thursday 9 July 2026. [@2] The search was of a residence in the housing scheme
occupied by the pair. [@2] Officers went to the house at about 11:50 on 9 July
2026 and searched the premises. [@1] In a bedroom they found several plastic
bags and containers holding leaves, seeds and stems suspected to be cannabis,
hidden among clothing. [@1]

Both were taken to the Leonora Police Station, where the substance was weighed
in their presence at 461.6 grams. [@1] Both suspects remained in custody as the
investigation continued, with no charge reported at the time. [@1] Guyana Times
reported that charges were pending and described the pair as a couple. [@3]
