Five people, including a serving member of the Royal Bahamas Defence Force, were
arrested after police seized an unlicensed firearm, ammunition and more than
three pounds of suspected marijuana in a search on a Sunday morning. [@1]
Officers assigned to the Mobile Division executed a search warrant at a
residence on Trinidad Avenue shortly before 10:00 a.m. [@1]

A 38-year-old man wanted in connection with a Grievous Harm investigation was
shown the warrant, arrested and cautioned on arrival. [@1] A second man
identified himself as the homeowner and a serving member of the Royal Bahamas
Defence Force and told officers that an unlicensed firearm and ammunition found
in a closet on the northern side of the house were his. [@1] The 37-year-old
defence force officer was arrested and cautioned in connection with the firearm,
ammunition and suspected drugs. [@1] During the search officers found a black
and green camouflage bag holding twelve sealed packages of suspected marijuana.
[@1]

Police then arrested two more men, aged 35 and 38, and a 43-year-old woman in
connection with the investigation. [@1] The suspected marijuana weighed 3.2
pounds, about 1.45 kilograms, and police estimated its street value at $3,225.
[@1] Police said the investigation was continuing. [@1]
