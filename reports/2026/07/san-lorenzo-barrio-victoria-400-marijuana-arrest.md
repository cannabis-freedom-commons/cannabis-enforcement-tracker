Investigators of the Directorate of Police Investigations arrested a suspected
member of the Mara Salvatrucha (MS-13) holding more than 3,000 wrappers of
illicit substances. [@2] The operation took place in barrio Victoria in the
municipality of San Lorenzo and was led by investigators assigned to
Metropolitan Criminal Investigation Unit No. 23. [@2] The suspect is a
27-year-old man originally from Tegucigalpa in the Central District and living
there. [@2] The National Police presented the case as an MS-13 member detained
in San Lorenzo with 3,850 doses of drugs. [@1]

Officers seized 2,900 transparent plastic wrappers containing white stone
alleged to be crack. [@2] They also seized 550 transparent plastic bags
containing white powder alleged to be cocaine. [@2] The cannabis part of the
seizure was 400 transparent plastic wrappers containing dry herb alleged to be
marijuana. [@2] Police described the combined haul as 3,850 doses. [@1]

The man will face charges for alleged drug trafficking against public health.
[@2] The Directorate said the arrest aimed to take members of criminal
structures that cause alarm off the streets. [@2]
