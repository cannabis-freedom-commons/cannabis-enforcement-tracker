Gendarmes of the Agua Blanca section of the 20 "Orán" Squadron were patrolling
at the Chalanas Viejas border crossing in Aguas Blancas when, at a distance,
they spotted three women walking towards Argentine territory, two of them
carrying school-type backpacks. [@1] On seeing the force, the women fled quickly
and dropped what they were carrying. [@1]

Gendarmes inspecting the discarded backpacks found four amorphous packages
inside. [@1] Forensic staff of the Unidad Técnica Criminalística y Estudios
Forenses "Orán" ran a Narcotest field test, which came back positive for
marijuana, with a total weight of 4 kilograms 210 grams. [@1] Gente de Salta
reported that the women fled towards the Bolivian side. [@2]

Chalanas Viejas is a frequently used crossing for informal crossings between
Argentina and Bolivia, in a border corridor that Gendarmería Nacional patrols
for contraband and drug trafficking. [@2] The Orán decentralised prosecutor's
office took charge of the case, ordered the paperwork drawn up and the drug held
under preventive seizure. [@1] Gente de Salta reported that the purpose of the
investigation was to try to identify the people involved, and none of the
outlets reported an arrest. [@2] Que Pasa Salta also reported the seizure in
Aguas Blancas after the three women fled on detecting the patrol. [@3]
