A court in Almaty, sitting with a jury, convicted the organiser of a
drug-smuggling channel bringing narcotics from Thailand and sentenced him to 16
years in prison. [@1] Investigators and the court established that the convicted
man had organised the unlawful import of hashish oil through international
postal shipments. [@2] The narcotics were deliberately concealed inside ordinary
parcels and formally registered to relatives and acquaintances who had no
knowledge of the parcels' actual contents. [@1]

Law-enforcement officers intercepted several separate smuggling attempts in the
same channel and seized more than 5.7 kilograms of cannabis extract. [@1]
Officers also found additional narcotic drugs intended for further sale in the
convicted man's possession. [@2] Officers also found a round of ammunition that
the convicted man had kept in unlawful storage. [@1]

The transport prosecutor's office supported the state prosecution brought in the
Almaty criminal case. [@2] The court verdict in this Almaty case had not yet
entered into legal force. [@2]
