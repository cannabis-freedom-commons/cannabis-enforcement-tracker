Judge John Hughes imposed a 1km exclusion zone around all Irish prisons on a
woman arrested for dealing cannabis at Dublin's Mountjoy Prison. [@1] Ann
O'Rourke, 46, appeared before Judge Hughes at Dublin District Court on Saturday
11 July 2026. [@1] The court heard she made no reply when charged with
possessing cannabis herb for sale or supply at the prison the previous day,
Friday 10 July. [@1] She has not entered a plea, and the value of the alleged
cannabis seizure was not stated in court. [@1]

Garda Rachel Smith initially objected to bail, then agreed to it with strict
conditions. [@1] O'Rourke was unrepresented because solicitors have withdrawn
from District Court legal aid cases in an industrial dispute over the Minister
for Justice Jim O'Callaghan's plan for a €520 flat fee for District Court cases.
[@1] The 1km restriction applies unless the court sanctions an application
allowing her to visit someone in prison custody, and she must also sign on once
a week at her local garda station and provide a contact phone number for gardaí.
[@1] Asked whether she consented to the terms, O'Rourke replied: "I do." [@1]
The unemployed woman told the court she had recently had difficulty attending
court because of a sick relative. [@1]

She was remanded on bail to appear again on 4 September. [@1] RTÉ's separate
account of the same sitting reported the same 1km prison exclusion zone and the
same list of bail conditions. [@2]
