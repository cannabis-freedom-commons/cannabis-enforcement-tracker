Detectives with the Illegal Marijuana Enforcement Team seized about 1,300
marijuana plants and other suspected illegal drugs while serving a search
warrant Thursday at an unlicensed cannabis grow near Jacksonville. [@1] The
search was carried out at a property in the 100 block of Hogan Road in the rural
Applegate area, according to the Jackson County Sheriff's Office. [@1] No
arrests were announced after the search. [@2]

Investigators seized approximately 1,300 marijuana plants at the property. [@1]
They also seized about 3 1/2 pounds of THC extract. [@1] Authorities said the
property was not licensed for any cannabis growing, handling or processing. [@1]
A task force member drove a skid-steer through the pot plants, completely razing
them to the ground with a brush cutter. [@2]

The Illegal Marijuana Enforcement Team was assisted by the Jackson County
Sheriff's Office, the U.S. Drug Enforcement Administration and Oregon State
Police. [@1] The team is a multi-agency task force including personnel from the
sheriff's office, Oregon State Police and the Jackson County District Attorney's
Office. [@2] The Oregon Water Resources Department and Jackson County Code
Enforcement also responded to investigate potential regulatory violations. [@1]
The team said it is continuing to focus on illegal marijuana operations in the
Rogue Valley while state regulatory agencies oversee licensed cannabis
businesses. [@1]
