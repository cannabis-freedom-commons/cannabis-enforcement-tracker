Immigration officers arrested two men, one from Pakistan and one from
Afghanistan, during a raid on a shop in Grantham. [@1] Police said the two men
were liable to be removed from the UK. [@2] A civil penalty referral notice was
issued to the employer at Toor Superstore in Grantham for illegally employing
one of the men. [@2]

Lincolnshire Police, Trading Standards and Immigration Enforcement inspected
three businesses in Grantham and one in Bourne on 23 July. [@1] The names of
only one of the four businesses were made public at that stage. [@2] At one of
the Grantham shops, a delivery of cannabis plants arrived while officers were on
the site, and the plants were seized. [@1]

At a shop in Bourne, officers found suspected illegal cigarettes and cannabis
vapes hidden in a large rucksack in a nearby vehicle with blacked-out windows.
[@2] One of the shop workers had a key to the vehicle, and Trading Standards
seized the products for further investigation. [@1]

Sgt Lee Mayfield said the aim was to take action against a range of offences,
disrupt those involved and help keep communities safe. [@1] The force said the
operation was part of work against organised crime groups using some
Lincolnshire shops as a front for illegal tobacco and vape sales. [@2] Alex
McGonigle, the county council's executive councillor for community safety, said
targeted businesses were 14 times more likely to sell vapes to under-18s than
legitimate shops. [@1] McGonigle said vapes containing cannabis had also
increased, and the council was working with schools to help young people
identify them. [@1] Police said cannabis vapes were illegal and could contain
unknown ingredients, including harmful chemicals. [@1]
