In the early hours of 28 July 2024, police stopped Tse To-chiu as he opened the door of his light
goods vehicle in a Tsing Yi car park and found 422 packets containing 104.2 kilogrammes of herbal
cannabis, which the court valued at HK$19,902,200. [@1] Tse admitted under caution that he intended
to traffic the drugs and said an acquaintance called KK had offered HK$2,500 to transport them. [@1]

On 2 March 2026 he pleaded guilty in the magistrates' court and was committed to the Court of First
Instance, where he confirmed the plea on 2 July 2026. [@1] Judge William Tam set a starting point
of ten years and three months for a courier trafficking over 90 kilogrammes, then reduced the term
by one third for the early guilty plea to six years and ten months. [@1]

When police announced the arrest on 29 July 2024, they told reporters they had seized about 119
kilograms worth HK$32 million from the same vehicle during Operation Thunder 2024. [@2] The sentence
judgment and admitted facts used the lower 104.2 kilogramme figure. [@1,2]

Tse, a delivery driver with a clear record, was 34 at the time of the offence. [@1]
