Coast Guard officers, guided by a police helicopter, intercepted a boat off Grand Cayman's southern
coast just after midnight on Friday 29 May 2026. [@3] The helicopter crew had spotted the vessel
behaving suspiciously, and the Coast Guard found numerous packages on board. [@2,3] Police said the
packages held 505 pounds of ganja (about 229 kg), 10 kilograms of cocaine, less than a kilogram of
ketamine and MDMA, and an unlicensed firearm with ammunition. [@1,2]

Two men on board, aged 44 and 22, were arrested. [@1,2] On 14 July they appeared in court, where
both were charged with importing ganja; the 22-year-old was also charged with illegal landing. [@1]
They were further charged with importing cocaine, ketamine and MDMA and with possessing an
unlicensed firearm and ammunition, and were remanded in custody until 29 July. [@1,2] Cayman News
Service reported that the charges carry a minimum of 15 years in prison. [@2]

Cayman Compass reported the ganja at 550 pounds and gave the men's ages as 42 and 22; the police
statement carried by Caymanian Times and Cayman News Service gives 505 pounds and ages 44 and 22,
which this report uses. [@1,2,3]
