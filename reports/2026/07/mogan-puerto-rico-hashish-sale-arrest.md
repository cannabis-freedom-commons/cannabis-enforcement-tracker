The Guardia Civil detained one person in the municipality of Mogán for alleged
drug trafficking after officers allegedly caught the person selling hashish in
Puerto Rico. [@1] Officers were carrying out discreet surveillance in a street
in Puerto Rico when the events occurred. [@1] After following the vehicle,
officers observed an exchange of divided money for a resinous substance,
allegedly hashish, in small doses prepared for sale. [@1] The alleged buyer
voluntarily told officers that he had bought the substance from the detained
person moments before the intervention. [@1]

Officers seized approximately two grams of suspected hashish in several doses
and 110 euros in cash in different bills. [@1] The seized drug was placed at the
disposal of the judicial authority for expert analysis. [@1] The Guardia Civil
sent the proceedings, the detained person and the seized effects to the
Instruction Section of the San Bartolomé de Tirajana court, which will continue
the case. [@1]

The action formed part of a specific Guardia Civil operation to detect and
dismantle retail drug-selling points and prevent trafficking in areas with many
residents and visitors. [@1]
