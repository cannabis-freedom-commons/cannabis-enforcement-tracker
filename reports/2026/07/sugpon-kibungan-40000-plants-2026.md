Police destroyed two marijuana plantations holding about 40,000 fully grown
plants and 40 kilograms of dried leaves with stalks at the boundary of Sugpon
town in Ilocos Sur and Kibungan town in Benguet on Tuesday, 21 July 2026. [@1]
The Ilocos regional police valued the destroyed marijuana at about P12.8
million. [@1] The eradication was carried out by the Ilocos Sur Provincial Drug
Enforcement Unit and other law enforcement agencies, which burned the plants on
site and are still trying to identify the people who maintained the plantations.
[@2]

The operation was the largest of 71 anti-drug operations across the Ilocos
Region from 15 to 21 July 2026, in which police arrested 78 drug suspects. [@2]
No one was reported arrested over the two plantations, and no charges or court
proceedings against cultivators were disclosed. [@1]
