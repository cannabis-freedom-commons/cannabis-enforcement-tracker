Detectives of the Criminal Division of the Krotoszyn County Police Headquarters
arrested a 43-year-old man in a flat in Wroclaw on 1 July 2026 on suspicion of
involvement in two frauds against seniors from Krotoszyn. [@2] Officers said the
man was surprised and overpowered during the arrest. [@2] He heard three
prosecutorial charges. [@2] Two charges concern fraud by the fake ABW-officer
and fake son's-accident methods, while the third concerns possession of
intoxicating substances. [@2] He faces up to 8 years' imprisonment for the
offences. [@2]

During the search of the flat officers found a plastic box containing over 30
grams net of cannabis herb other than fibrous hemp. [@2] The same seizure is
described as over 30 grams of marijuana. [@1] Police put one senior's losses at
49,530 zlotys after her cash, cards and PINs were taken and used at ATMs in
Wroclaw. [@1] A second senior lost 8,600 zlotys after callers posing as a
policeman and his son claimed the son had caused a road accident. [@1]

Police said the case is developing and did not rule out further arrests. [@2]
