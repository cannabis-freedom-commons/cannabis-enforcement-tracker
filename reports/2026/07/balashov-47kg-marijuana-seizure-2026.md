Police in Balashov, Saratov Oblast, seized a large batch of marijuana from a
local resident, the regional headquarters of the Interior Ministry said. [@1]
Officers found the plant drug in outbuildings standing on the grounds of a
private house. [@1] The owner of the property, a 60-year-old Balashov man who
had previously faced criminal charges, said he had stored the marijuana for his
own consumption. [@1]

In total the officers seized four buckets of the plant drug weighing more than
4.7 kilograms. [@1] A criminal case was opened under part 2 of article 228 of
the Russian Criminal Code, which covers illegal acquisition, storage and
production of drugs on a large scale without intent to sell. [@1] The man was
released on a written undertaking not to leave and to behave properly, so he was
not held in custody. [@1]
