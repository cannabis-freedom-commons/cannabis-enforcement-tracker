Karaganda Region police detained a 45-year-old resident of Karaganda suspected
of storing and selling drugs in especially large quantities during the national
Karasora-2026 operation. [@1] Officers searched the man and seized drugs,
electronic scales and packaging materials, which police said indicated the drugs
were being prepared for sale. [@1] Investigators then found a second stash of
drugs and seized more material there during an authorised search of a second
address. [@1]

According to preliminary figures, police took about 15 kg of hashish and more
than 150 kg of marijuana out of circulation. [@1] The interior ministry's
statement listed the haul as about 15 kg of hashish and more than 150 kg of
marijuana. [@2] The regional police department put the total weight of the
seized drugs at about 180 kg and said it fell in the especially large quantity
category under Kazakh law. [@2] Karaganda's regional newspaper Индустриальная
Караганда reported the same figures, about 180 kg in total including 15 kg of
hashish and more than 150 kg of marijuana. [@3]

The suspect was placed in a temporary detention centre while a pre-trial
investigation continues into supply, storage and sale channels and other people
involved. [@1] The regional police department described the anti-drug work as
part of the country's Law and Order (Заң және тәртіп) policy and warned that
storing, transporting or selling drugs carries criminal liability. [@2]
