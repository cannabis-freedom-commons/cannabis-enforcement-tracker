A Tallapoosa County sheriff's deputy stopped a Honda Accord on Jefferson Street
in Alexander City on Friday, 17 July 2026, for a traffic violation. [@2] The
deputy developed probable cause to search the car and found two and a half
ounces of marijuana. [@2] The Tallapoosa County Narcotics Task Force was
notified and began investigating, and agents obtained a search warrant for an
apartment on Summer Street in Alexander City. [@2]

During the apartment search, agents seized about 11 pounds of marijuana, a Glock
pistol and $670 in cash. [@2]

Two Alexander City residents were arrested and taken to the Tallapoosa County
Jail after law enforcement searched their vehicle and home, the Tallapoosa
County Sheriff's Office said in a press release. [@1] Buckner, 23, of Alexander
City was charged with trafficking marijuana, being a person forbidden to possess
a firearm, and unlawful possession of drug paraphernalia. [@2] Taniya Davis of
Alexander City was charged with trafficking marijuana and unlawful possession of
drug paraphernalia. [@2]

The investigation was ongoing, the accounts said. [@2]
