The Judiciary Police arrested a 32-year-old Malaysian woman hired by a
drug-trafficking syndicate to smuggle cannabis via Macau airport. [@1] The
operation was conducted in collaboration with the Macao Customs Service. [@1]
Judiciary Police spokesman Lei Chi Hou and Customs spokesman Cheang Mou Kei
briefed reporters at the airport last night about the case. [@1] The suspect was
transferred to the Public Prosecutions Office for questioning on a
drug-trafficking charge. [@1]

Through intelligence analysis and risk assessment, Macau Customs officers
identified the woman as a suspicious passenger on a flight arriving from
Southeast Asia the previous afternoon. [@1] Customs officers intercepted her as
she retrieved her luggage, which contained 12 bags of cannabis weighing a total
of 6.7 kilogrammes. [@1] The Customs Service transferred the case to the
Judiciary Police for further investigation, where tests confirmed the substance
was cannabis. [@1] Police put the estimated street value of the seized drugs at
6.7 million patacas. [@1]

Officers said the woman had been recruited to smuggle the cannabis from an
unspecified Southeast Asian country to an unknown final destination via Macau.
[@1] Initial police investigations indicate she was tasked with smuggling the
drugs to a neighbouring region via Macau. [@1] At the time of the night press
briefing, police were still tracing the origin of the drugs and searching for
potential accomplices. [@1] Investigators were also still working to determine
the final destination of the shipment and the amount the syndicate promised to
pay her for successful delivery. [@1] Police stressed that drug trafficking is a
serious offence in Macau, carrying a prison sentence of up to 15 years. [@1]
