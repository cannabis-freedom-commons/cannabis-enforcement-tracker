Fiji's Northern Division Operations Sasamaki team seized more than 5,600 plants
believed to be marijuana from a farm in the Qaravutu farming area. [@1,2] The
operation followed information gathered during an investigation into alleged
unlawful possession of illicit drugs. [@1,2]

One suspect was taken into custody for questioning. [@1,2] Police did not name
the person or say whether more arrests were expected. [@1] The plants were taken
into police custody and were to undergo forensic analysis to confirm their
composition. [@1,2]

The Fiji Police Force announced the seizure on 8 July 2026. [@1,2] It said
investigations into the alleged cultivation were continuing, and no charge or
court appearance had been announced. [@1]
