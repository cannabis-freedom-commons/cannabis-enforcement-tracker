The traffickers moving cannabis on a horse-drawn cart and a motorbike abandoned
their cargo and fled under cover of darkness when they sensed the security
forces. [@1] No arrests were reported in the published tally, which lists only
the seized cannabis, motorbike and cart. [@1] The Territorial Brigade of Joal
made the seizure on 7 July 2026 at about 10:30 p.m. as part of the fight against
drug trafficking. [@1]

The operation's tally lists 360 kilograms of chanvre indien, the cannabis seized
in the operation. [@1] Officers also seized a motorbike of the Haojue brand that
was listed in the operation's tally with the cannabis. [@1] Officers also seized
a cart harnessed to a horse that was found with the same load. [@1] Officers
intercepted the cart and motorbike at about 10 p.m. on the sandy track linking
the villages of Mbodiène and Ndianda in the commune of Nguéniene. [@1]

Gendarmes had gone to Joal Fadiouth beach to set up surveillance after a tip
warned of a possible cannabis landing there. [@1] The Gendarmerie nationale said
its call centre remains available free of charge at 800 00 20 20 and 123 for
useful information. [@1]
