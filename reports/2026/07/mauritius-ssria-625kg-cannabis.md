ADSU and customs officers seized about 62.5 kg of cannabis at Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam International Airport on June 4, 2026; the reported estimated value was Rs 94.5 million. [@1,2]

## At the airport

Officers intercepted two passengers arriving on an Air Mauritius flight from London-Gatwick and found cannabis concealed in their four suitcases. [@1,2] The two passengers were arrested at the airport. [@1,2] During questioning, they identified a third passenger who had already left; ADSU and the Traffic Branch later arrested him. [@1,2]

The three suspects were detained while the investigation continued under ADSU. [@1,2] Neither report gives a later charging decision or court outcome. [@1,2]

La 1ère reported that the importers were charged and placed in detention in early June. [@3] Le Mauricien reported that investigators arrested a fourth suspect and found messages with Jayesh Gungadin on his phone that led them to arrest the sergeant. [@4] The Anti-Drug and Smuggling Unit arrested Gungadin on 1 July 2026 on suspicion of involvement in the cannabis importation. [@3] He appeared before the Port-Louis district court on 2 July and was provisionally charged with perverting the course of justice. [@4] L’Express described Gungadin as the fifth suspect in the case. [@5] The Port-Louis South District Court granted conditional bail on 16 July against two sureties of Rs 100,000 each, but a stay obtained by the Director of Public Prosecutions suspended the release decision that day. [@5] On 17 July, the DPP withdrew the stay, and Gungadin paid the court-ordered sureties and went home. [@6] L’Express said investigators were still seeking to establish Gungadin’s alleged role in the case. [@5]
