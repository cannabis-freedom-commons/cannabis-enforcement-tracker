Batken Region police surveyed 7 hectares in several settlements of the Kadamjay
and Leilek districts and cut and burned 3.4 tonnes of wild cannabis. [@1,2]
The operation was described as part of the wider eradication work in southern
Kyrgyzstan. [@1]

The reports do not name the settlements or provide a more precise location
within the two districts. [@1,2]
