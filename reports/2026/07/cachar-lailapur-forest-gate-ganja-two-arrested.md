A special team from the Lailapur Police Outpost under Dholai Police Station
raided the Bhetor Lailapur Forest Gate area in Assam's Cachar district and
arrested two suspected drug traffickers. [@1]

Police said the raid produced 341.6 grams of ganja, part of a haul that also
included heroin and Myanmar-made cigarettes. [@1] The same search found 86.81
grams of heroin hidden in five soap cases and 41 small plastic containers, 1,060
Myanmar-made cigarettes in 53 packets, and Rs 1,620 in cash. [@1]

The two suspects and the seized items were taken to Dholai Police Station. [@1]
Police registered a case under relevant provisions of the law and began an
investigation to identify and arrest other members of the trafficking network.
[@1]

Cachar Police said the crackdown on narcotics trafficking and other illegal
activities would continue with the same intensity in the coming days. [@1]
Lailapur lies in the Barak valley, close to the border with Mizoram, a route
police in Cachar commonly link to smuggled drugs and Myanmar cigarettes. [@2]
