Sheriff's deputies in Gillette, Wyoming, arrested a man on July 16, 2026 after a
search of a home on Sammye Avenue turned up dozens of THC vape products. [@1]
Campbell County Sheriff's Office deputies served the search warrant at about
11:55 a.m. on July 16. [@1]

Deputies found Bulluie Red Arm Panther Estell Twigg, a 16-year-old girl and a
16-year-old boy inside the home, and saw Twigg and the girl coming out of a
northeast bedroom. [@1] In that bedroom officers found a glass and metal straw
with suspected THC residue, a silicone container with suspected THC wax and a
glass pipe with suspected THC residue. [@1] Investigators also found seven boxes
of Muha Med THC vapes with 10 individually packaged vapes each, another box with
nine packaged vapes, three unopened vapes and one opened vape. [@1]

A deputy tested one unopened cartridge, which weighed 47.23 grams with its
packaging and was labelled as containing 2 grams of THC concentrate at 82.79%
concentration; a field test came back positive for THC. [@1] The deputy said the
quantity of THC products in the bedroom was consistent with intent to
distribute, which produced the felony charge. [@1] Twigg is charged with
possession of a controlled substance and possession with intent to deliver,
according to court documents. [@1] He was arrested at about 1:20 p.m. that day
and taken to the Campbell County Detention Center. [@1]

Court records showed Twigg had no previous convictions for misdemeanour
possession of a controlled substance. [@1] Deputies at the same Sammye Avenue
search found 10 black boxes labelled MUHA MED THC, each holding 10 unopened THC
cartridges, plus four vacuum-sealed bags of psilocybin mushrooms in the kitchen.
[@2]
