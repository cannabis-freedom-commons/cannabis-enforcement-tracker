On 8 July 2026, police stopped a car at the ninth kilometre of the
Bakanas–Topar road in Balkhash District and found 6.78 kilograms of un-dried
marijuana gathered in the steppe. [@1,2]

The 35-year-old passenger identified the collection site, where an
investigative team mowed and seized wild cannabis weighing 1.27 tonnes after
examination. [@1,2] The passenger and the 34-year-old driver were detained, and
the reports said a pre-trial investigation was opened. [@1,2]
