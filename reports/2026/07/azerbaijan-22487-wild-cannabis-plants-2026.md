Azerbaijan's Interior Ministry said that on 10 July 2026 police found and
destroyed 22,487 wild cannabis plants. [@4] The destroyed plants weighed 11
tonnes and 770 kilograms by the ministry's account. [@1] In the same day's
operations police took more than 33 kilograms of marijuana out of illegal
circulation. [@1]

1news.az, quoting the ministry, likewise put the marijuana at more than 33
kilograms of the drugs seized. [@2] The ministry put the rest of the haul at
about 2 kilograms of heroin and about 400 grams of methamphetamine. [@3]
Report.az gave the day's whole seizure as more than 36 kilograms of narcotic
substances across Azerbaijan. [@1] 1news.az reported that the plant destruction
came on top of that 36-kilogram haul. [@2]

The ministry said such operations were continuing. [@4]
