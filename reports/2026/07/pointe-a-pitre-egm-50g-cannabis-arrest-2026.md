Gendarmes on a routine patrol in the Pointe-à-Pitre company's district stopped a
suspect and found 50 grams of cannabis herb on him, packaged and ready to be
resold or consumed. [@1] The patrol was carried out by soldiers of Mobile
Gendarmerie Squadron (EGM) 31/6 from Toulouse, mainland reinforcements currently
deployed in Guadeloupe. [@1] The Guadeloupe gendarmerie said the interception
took place on a Friday. [@2]

Squadron 31/6 had been deployed to the archipelago for several weeks to back up
local gendarmerie units facing persistent criminal pressure. [@2]

The gendarmerie said that whether large networks or local flows are involved,
the aim is to disrupt the underground economy and saturate the ground. [@1] The
gendarmerie described the seizure as showing that each gram taken off the street
helps keep neighbourhoods calm. [@1]
