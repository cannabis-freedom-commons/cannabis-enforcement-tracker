Transnistrian law enforcement detained three drug couriers from Moldova — two
men and a woman — who rented a flat in Tiraspol. [@1] The Interior Ministry
press centre said the group sold mephedrone, hashish and alpha-PVP across the
republic. [@1] The woman, 36, arrived first and spent about a month laying out
drugs in Dnestrovsk. [@1] Her 22-year-old boyfriend distributed for two weeks in
Tiraspol, Bender, Slobodzeya district, Grigoriopol and Rybnitsa. [@1]

The third courier, a 24-year-old man, had been selling for only five days. [@1]
He was the first of the three the Transnistrian authorities detained. [@1]
Officers found a photograph album of stash sites and their coordinates on his
phone. [@1] Over their stay the three laid out about 500 stash drops. [@1] They
supported themselves in Chișinău this way. [@1] A Transnistrian television
report describes the 22-year-old's territory as running from Slobodzeya to
Rybnitsa. [@2]

Selling drugs carries up to 20 years in prison, the ministry said. [@1]
