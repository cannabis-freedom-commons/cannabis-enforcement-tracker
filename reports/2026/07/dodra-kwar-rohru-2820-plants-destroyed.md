Police registered two separate cases under the NDPS Act after an operation
against illegal cannabis cultivation in the Dodra Kwar area of the Rohru region.
[@1] In the first case, police found 1,550 cannabis plants growing on land
allegedly in the possession of Surya Dev of Dhandrawadi village. [@1]

Officers destroyed 1,530 of those plants and seized the remaining plants for
evidence. [@1] In the second case, officials discovered 1,310 cannabis plants on
land allegedly linked to Ujendri Devi of Dhandrawadi village. [@1]

Officials destroyed 1,290 of those plants. [@1] The operation was reported from
Shimla. [@1]
