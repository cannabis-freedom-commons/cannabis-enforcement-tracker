Prefectura Naval Argentina officers seized 319 bricks of marijuana weighing more
than 280 kilograms on the Paraná riverbank in Puerto Rico, Misiones, during the
early hours of 2 July 2026. [@2] Officers also seized the rowboat used to carry
the load across the river. [@1] When officers arrived they found the boat and
nine packages abandoned on the shore. [@1] The boat's sole occupant fled before
being identified, and no one was detained. [@3]

Prefectura intelligence work had warned of a possible entry of marijuana from
Paraguay via the Paraná River. [@2] From an observation post at Arroyo Mbopicua,
prefecture staff watched a rowboat with one occupant aboard cross toward the
Argentine bank carrying several bundles. [@1]

The Federal Court and Federal Prosecutor's Office in Oberá took charge of the
case and ordered the seizure of the drugs and the boat. [@3] The authorities
valued what was seized at more than 1,000 million pesos. [@2]
