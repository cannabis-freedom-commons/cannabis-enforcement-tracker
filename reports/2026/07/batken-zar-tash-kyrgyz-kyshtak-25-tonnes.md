On 10 July 2026, reports described two wild-cannabis destruction operations in
Batken Region. [@1,2,3] At Zar-Tash, police checked 1.5 hectares and cut and
burned about 1.7 tonnes; at Kyrgyz-Kyshtak in Kadamjay District, officers
checked 3 hectares and burned about 800 kilograms. [@1,2]

The reported quantities total about 2.5 tonnes across 4.5 hectares. [@1] The
operations were part of the wider southern Kyrgyzstan eradication work described
in the reports. [@1,3]
