Operatives in Tiraspol detained two friends from the city during the daytime on
15 July 2026 and seized hashish from them. [@1] One of the detained men was a
21-year-old who told operatives he had found a packet containing drugs at that
moment, by chance. [@1] The Interior Ministry's press centre account says the
21-year-old claimed he had "purely by chance" found a ziplock bag with the drug.
[@2]

Officers later also found a small packet containing a prohibited substance on
his 20-year-old friend. [@1] The 20-year-old said he had found a ziplock bag two
days earlier and had not told his friend so as not to share it. [@1] According
to Transnistria's Interior Ministry press centre, both detained young men use
drugs. [@1]

The Internal Affairs Ministry reported that the drugs had to be handed over to
the militia. [@2] For storing the prohibited substance the two face a fine of up
to 21,000 rubles or up to three years in prison. [@2] The Novosti Pridnestrovya
article reports the same threatened penalty, a fine of up to 21,000 rubles or up
to three years in prison. [@1]
