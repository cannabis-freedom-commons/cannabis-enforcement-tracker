Latvian State Police opened a criminal case in early 2026 into the illegal group
sale of narcotics and arrested five suspects in total. [@2] Officers from the
Rīga North police department arrested two men born in 2000 and 2001 on 8 January
2026. [@2] On 5 June they arrested a man born in 1991, on 30 June another man
born in 2001, and on 1 July a man born in 2002. [@2] The man born in 2002 had
earlier come to police attention over drug trafficking. [@2]

In several searches in Rīga, officers seized 30 kilograms of marijuana, more
than €7,000 in cash and a crypto wallet. [@2] Four of the suspects were placed
under non-custodial measures, while the man born in 1991 was remanded in
custody. [@2] The investigation continues. [@1]

The case was opened under section 253.1(2) of the Criminal Law, which covers
illegal production, purchase, storage, transport and transfer of narcotics for
sale and illegal sale by a group by prior agreement. [@2] The offence carries
three to ten years in prison, with or without confiscation of property, and
probation supervision for up to three years. [@3] Police reminded that no one is
considered guilty until their guilt is proved in the prescribed manner. [@2]
