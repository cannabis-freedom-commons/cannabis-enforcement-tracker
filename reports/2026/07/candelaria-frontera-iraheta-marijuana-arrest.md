El Salvador's National Civil Police, known as the PNC, arrested 66-year-old
Sócrates Miguel Iraheta Bonilla in Candelaria de La Frontera, Santa Ana Oeste,
for alleged marijuana commercialization. [@1] The detention followed a
judge-authorized search of the suspect's home. [@1] The operation took place at
his residence after a court issued the search order for the house. [@2] Canine
units assisted officers during the inspection to locate illegal substances and
other objects linked to illicit activity. [@1]

Officers seized more than half a kilo of marijuana, a weighing scale, an
undisclosed amount of cash and two cell phones in the house. [@1] The amount of
cash was not specified. [@2] All the seized items were retained as evidence in
the investigation into alleged drug commercialization. [@1]

Police said Iraheta Bonilla has prior criminal records for illicit commerce and
trafficking, injuries, rape and expressions of violence against women. [@1]
Police said those records will be considered in the corresponding judicial
process for this case. [@1] He was to be processed for alleged illicit
trafficking. [@2]
