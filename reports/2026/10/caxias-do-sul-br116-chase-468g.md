Military police arrested a man for drug trafficking on the night of Thursday 29
October 2026 after he fled a stop on the BR-116 in Caxias do Sul, in Brazil's
Rio Grande do Sul state. [@1] The case was handled by the 12th Military Police
Battalion (12º BPM) of the Brigada Militar, the state's military police. [@1]
According to the Brigada Militar, the driver disobeyed the order to stop and
fled at high speed. [@1] He crashed the car near the Monumento ao Imigrante and
was arrested. [@2]

Police seized 468 grams of marijuana and 22 grams of hashish, about 490 grams of
cannabis in all. [@1] They also found R$130 in cash, two mobile phones and
materials related to drug trafficking. [@1] The man was booked for drug
trafficking (autuado por tráfico de drogas in Portuguese). [@2] He was taken to
the police station with the seized material. [@3] The Pioneiro reported that the
Brigada Militar did not say whether the crash injured anyone. [@2]
