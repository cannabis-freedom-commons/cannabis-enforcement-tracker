Santa Catarina's Military Police arrested a 19-year-old man for drug trafficking
in Passo Manso, a neighbourhood of Blumenau in the Vale do Itajaí, on the night
of Thursday 29 October 2026. [@1,3] At about 9.16pm, during a patrol, officers saw
three people behaving suspiciously, and the group scattered when they noticed
the police car. [@1] Two of them were stopped in the courtyard of a condomínio,
a walled housing complex, and nothing illicit was found with them. [@1] The
third ran into an apartment where he did not live, and officers approached him
there. [@1]

A search of his backpack turned up about 98 grams of cannabis, already divided
and packaged, plus cash, a precision scale, a roll of plastic film, two mobile
phones and papers with notes that police said may be linked to trafficking. [@1]
He was arrested and taken with all the seized material to the Central de Plantão
Policial, the city's duty police station, for the legal procedures. [@2]
