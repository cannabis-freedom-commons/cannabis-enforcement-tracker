Two men were arrested and an adolescent was apprehended in Barbacena's Santa
Cecília neighbourhood on Saturday 31 October 2026, after a tip about drug
dealing. [@1] The Military Police said an anonymous tip reported that one of the
men was selling drugs from a Fiat Uno, with another man and an adolescent along.
[@2] Officers traced the car and stopped its three occupants. [@2]

During a personal search, officers found two marijuana tablets, one smaller and
one larger, and a crumbled portion of the same drug with the adolescent. [@1]
They also took R$20 in cash from him. [@1]

The two adults were formally arrested and the adolescent was apprehended. [@2]
The adolescent's guardian accompanied the police procedures. [@2] All three were
taken to the Civil Police station with the material seized. [@1] The Fiat Uno
was towed to an accredited yard. [@1]
