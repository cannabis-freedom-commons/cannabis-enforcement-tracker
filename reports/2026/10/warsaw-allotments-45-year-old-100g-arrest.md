Officers of the Criminal Department of the Mokotów police station detained a
45-year-old man as he left an observed summer house on allotment gardens in
Warsaw. [@1] Detectives had identified the allotments as his possible
whereabouts after operational findings that he might store significant
quantities of narcotics and psychotropic substances there. [@1] In the house
officers found mephedrone and, in a ground-floor room, further substances,
seizing a bag of herbal material, an electronic scale, a heat sealer and empty
plastic bags. [@1]

A presumptive tester test indicated that the herb was marijuana and the
light-coloured substance was mephedrone. [@1] After weighing, police reported
nearly 100 grams of marijuana and over 3 grams of mephedrone, valuing the drugs
together at about PLN 5,000 on the black market. [@1]

After procedural steps the man was charged with possession of narcotics and a
psychotropic substance. [@1] A check showed he was wanted by a court to serve
150 days of imprisonment, and he was taken to the Warszawa-Białołęka remand
centre. [@1] The proceedings in the drug case are supervised by the
Warszawa-Mokotów District Prosecutor's Office. [@2]
