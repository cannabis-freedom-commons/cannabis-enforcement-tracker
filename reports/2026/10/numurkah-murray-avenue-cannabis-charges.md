## The search

Cobram Crime Investigation Unit detectives were investigating an alleged firearms incident in Numurkah on 29 October 2026, in which a man allegedly discharged a firearm towards a vehicle. [@1,2] Detectives executed a search warrant at a property on Murray Avenue on 31 October, where they located a suspicious device. [@1,2] The Bomb Response Unit attended and rendered the device safe. [@1,2]

## The charges

A 66-year-old man was arrested at the scene. [@1,2] He was charged with reckless conduct endangering serious injury, possessing and making an explosive substance, cultivating cannabis, possessing cannabis, possessing Victoria Police equipment without authority and possessing a prohibited weapon without exemption, with the police account also listing possessing an imitation firearm as a prohibited person. [@1,2] He was remanded to appear at Shepparton Magistrates' Court on 2 November. [@1,2]
