Officers of the Dirección General de Drogas Peligrosas y Crimen Organizado of
the Corrientes provincial police searched a house on Rómulo Artieda street at
number 2100, in the barrio Ponce of the provincial capital, on Friday 9 October
2026. [@1] The raid broke up a drugs sales and distribution point, and police
detained a man known in the criminal milieu under the alias Sopapa. [@1] Members
of the P.A.R. division and the Canes (dog) division took part and searched the
different rooms of the property. [@1]

The search found 67 grams of marijuana, held in a glass jar and a polyethylene
wrapper, along with cocaine. [@1] Officers said they also seized a working
precision scale, five mobile phones and 1,400,000 pesos in cash believed to come
from the illegal trade. [@1] Court authorities estimated the total street value
of what was seized at 3,508,000 pesos. [@1] Corrientes al Día reported that the
search was carried out under a judicial order on a house on Rómulo Artieda
street on Friday 9 October. [@2] Police said the officers found the cocaine
divided into 65 doses and one larger wrapper, and also seized marijuana, five
mobile phones and a precision scale. [@2]

The man identified in the house was left at the disposal of the judicial
authorities, while the police report did not say whether he was formally
detained or released. [@2] The case was opened before the Juzgado de Garantías
of the First Judicial Circuit and the Unidad Fiscal de Investigación Criminal N°
5 (UFIC 5), under Argentina's drug law 23.737. [@1] The seized material was
taken to the Drogas Peligrosas y Crimen Organizado unit for further processing.
[@2]
