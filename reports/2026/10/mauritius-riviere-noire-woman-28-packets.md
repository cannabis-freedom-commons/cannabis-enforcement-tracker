Officers of the Anti-Drug and Smuggling Unit (ADSU), the Mauritian police's drug
squad, searched the home of a 24-year-old unemployed woman in Rivière Noire at
22:25 on Thursday 29 October 2026. [@1] The search took place at the property
where the woman lives. [@1]

During the search of the property, the officers found a green bag. [@1] The
green bag held a knotted plastic bag. [@1] Inside the plastic bag were 28
cellophane packets. [@1] Each packet contained a quantity of cannabis, and the
cannabis weighed 11.3 grams in total. [@1]

The officers also seized an electronic scale, a mobile phone and a sum of cash
amounting to Rs 775. [@1] Police suspected the money was linked to trafficking
in narcotics. [@1] The 24-year-old woman was arrested and placed in police
custody. [@1] The investigation is continuing. [@1]
