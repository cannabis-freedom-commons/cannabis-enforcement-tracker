The Guardia Civil dismantled a drug-trafficking organisation operating in
Collado Mediano and Cercedilla and arrested six people. [@1] The six were held
on suspicion of drug trafficking against public health and membership in a
criminal organisation. [@2] Investigators described the network as a three-tier
pyramid. [@1] A 30-year-old woman led the group, managing supply between the
homes and collecting payments. [@2] The other five members were men aged 18 to
34, with two supplying the sale points and three making final sales to
consumers. [@2] Final sales took place directly from the homes and through home
deliveries using four vehicles registered to third parties. [@1]

The seized drugs included 214 grams of heroin and various batches of hashish and
cocaine, plus precision scales, dosing tools, four private vehicles and 6.151
euros in cash. [@1] The investigation, which started in late 2025, ended with
the arrests on 27 October after neighbour complaints about homes in the La
Cobañera urbanisation in Collado Mediano with constant short-stay movements of
people and vehicles. [@2]

The case began after repeated citizen complaints in a Collado Mediano housing
estate about incessant buyers arriving by vehicle or on foot for very brief
periods. [@1] Early inquiries confirmed two flats in independent portals selling
drugs around the clock every day. [@1] Agents then found the Collado Mediano
activity directly connected to two other homes in Cercedilla. [@1]
