Officers of the Criminal Intelligence Board of the Lithuanian Prison Service and
Kaunas county police stopped a Volkswagen Golf for a check on the A1 highway at
about 17:30 on 28 October 2026. [@1] A man born in 1968, identified by the
initials G. T., was driving the car. [@2] He was carrying a very large quantity
of narcotic substances. [@1] The Prison Service announced the stop on Thursday
12 November, more than two weeks after it took place. [@2]

In the cabin and boot, officers found two travel bags holding 21 vacuum packages
of plant-based narcotic material, cannabis and parts of the plant. [@1] The
total weight of the material seized was about 21 kilograms. [@1]

The Criminal Intelligence Board opened a pre-trial investigation into unlawful
possession of a very large quantity of narcotic or psychotropic substances under
part 3 of article 260 of the Criminal Code. [@1] The offence carries 10 to 15
years in prison. [@1] On 30 October the suspect was remanded in custody for
three months, the strictest pre-trial measure. [@1] Prosecutors of the organised
crime and corruption division of the Kaunas Regional Prosecutor's Office
organise and lead the investigation. [@1]
