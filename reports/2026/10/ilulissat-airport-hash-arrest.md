As the new international airport in Ilulissat opened, Greenland Police received a report at 12:15
on 29 October 2026 of narcotics found in a suitcase belonging to a 54-year-old man, and arrested
him at 12:30 for passing on narcotics. [@1]

Police confirmed the incident to the Ritzau news agency, which reported that the find was about
half a kilogram of hash and that the man had arrived on Air Greenland's first direct flight from
Copenhagen to Ilulissat. [@2] Police said the case was under investigation with a view to a later
court decision and would not give more information. [@1]
