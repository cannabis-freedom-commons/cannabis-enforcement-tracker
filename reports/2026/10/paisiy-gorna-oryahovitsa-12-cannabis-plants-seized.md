Police from the district police department in Gorna Oryahovitsa seized 12 plants
of the hemp genus from a property in the village of Paisiy, the regional
Interior Ministry directorate in Veliko Tarnovo said. [@1] The check was made
the day before the announcement, during a specialised police operation against
crime and for public order. [@1]

Twelve planted plants were found in the yard of the property, which is lived in
by a foreign national. [@1] The check was carried out at a property lived in by
a foreign national, within a police operation against crime and for public
order. [@3] A pre-trial proceeding was opened over the case and the
investigation was continuing. [@3]

Police said the work on the opened pre-trial proceeding was continuing. [@1] The
Gorna Oryahovitsa police officers seized the 12 plants during the specialised
operation in Paisiy. [@3]

The find followed a separate case in which police found six jars of marijuana in
a home in Svishtov at the end of September. [@2]
