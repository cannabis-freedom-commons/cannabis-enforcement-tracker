Mahasamund police arrested two alleged main suppliers from Odisha in connection
with a ganja shipment bound for Uttar Pradesh. [@1] Police identified the
alleged suppliers as Dhyanendra Bishwal and Prashant Nag, from Odisha's
Kendrapara and Koraput districts, respectively. [@2] Investigators said
technical evidence, analysis of seized mobile-phone data and information from
earlier arrestees led them to the two men. [@2] Both were arrested on 27 October
2026, brought to Mahasamund and sent to judicial remand. [@2]

Police said the earlier seizure took place on 26 March 2026, after a tip
prompted a roadblock near Rehtikhol village on National Highway 53 in Mahasamund
district. [@2] Officers stopped a Hyundai Aura car and the Tata Yodha pickup
behind it. [@2] The pickup held ganja hidden in plastic sacks beneath
watermelons. [@2] Police recovered 225 kilograms of ganja and estimated its
value at Rs 1 crore 12 lakh 50 thousand. [@2] Police said the shipment was being
transported from Sambalpur, Odisha, to Chitrakoot, Uttar Pradesh. [@2] Police
said the investigation continued into the network's source, destination and
financial transactions, with further action possible if others were implicated.
