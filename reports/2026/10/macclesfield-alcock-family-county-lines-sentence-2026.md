Jamie Alcock, 37, his wife Nicola Alcock, 40, and their daughter Layla Alcock,
19, all of Princess Way in Macclesfield, appeared at Chester Crown Court on 30
October 2026 for sentencing. [@1] Jamie Alcock pleaded guilty to being concerned
in the supply of crack cocaine, being concerned in the supply of cocaine, being
concerned in the supply of cannabis, and the possession of criminal property,
and was jailed for six years and nine months. [@1] Nicola Alcock pleaded guilty
to two counts being concerned in the supply of crack cocaine, being concerned in
the supply of cocaine, and being concerned in the supply of cannabis. She was
handed a two year and six-month sentence suspended for two years and six months,
however she will remain on an electronic tag for 30 months. [@1] Layla Alcock
pleaded guilty to the acquiring of criminal property and was handed a 12-month
community order. [@1]

Officers from the Serious and Organised Crime Unit had previously identified two
mobile phone numbers operating in the Macclesfield area that were being used for
the facilitation of cannabis, cocaine and crack cocaine. [@1] Despite having no
form of legitimate income between 2022 and 2024, enquiries into the Alcock's
finances uncovered that approximately £450,000 had been transferred to them
across their three bank accounts. [@1] In September 2025, officers conducted a
warrant at the Alcock's address on Princess Way and arrested Jamie and Nicola.
[@1] Layla Alcock, who was 17 at the time of the offending, was arrested shortly
after on 1st October 2025. [@1]

Detective Inspector Higgins of the Serious and Organised Crime Unit said the
result had dismantled one of the most significant county lines groups in the
east of the county. [@1]
