Three men were arrested and two 16-year-old boys were apprehended in two
anti-drug operations in the Bairro dos Municípios, Balneário Camboriú, on
Thursday 29 October 2026. [@1] The men were 18, 31 and 33 years old. [@1] All
five were taken to the Civil Police station with the material seized. [@1] The
operations involved the battalion's tactical unit, Rocam, intelligence teams and
the Shock Battalion. [@2]

The first operation began on Rua Biguaçu after intelligence teams received
information about a possible drug deal and monitored the area. [@1] Officers
stopped a 33-year-old man with 104 grams of dry hashish. [@1] A 31-year-old man
stopped next had 20 grams of skunk. [@1] A search of his home found 368 grams of
a substance similar to ecstasy, R$1,889 in cash, a precision scale and
packaging. [@1]

The second operation was on Rua Chapecó at the corner of 6ª Avenida, in the area
known as Predinhos. [@1] There an 18-year-old was found with a portion of crack
and R$40. [@1] The two 16-year-olds had portions of cocaine and crack, small
change and a two-way radio, according to the Military Police. [@1] Searches
nearby found 21.1 grams of cocaine, 12.4 grams of crack and 5.7 grams of
marijuana, plus R$390. [@1]

Together the two operations yielded 531.2 grams of drugs and R$2,279 in cash,
the Military Police said. [@1] Officers also collected mobile phones,
trafficking material and a vehicle. [@2]
