No one was arrested during the interdiction at the Labangan delivery hub. [@1]
Operatives of the Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency-9's Zamboanga del Sur
Provincial Office, together with the PDEA Special Enforcement Service K9 Unit,
carried out the operation at around 12:05 p.m. in Barangay Bulanit. [@1] The
operation took place on Saturday, 31 October 2026, at a delivery hub in Barangay
Bulanit, Labangan, Zamboanga del Sur. [@1] The identities of the parcel's sender
and intended receiver remained subject to validation by investigators. [@1] The
confiscated items were placed under PDEA custody for documentation, laboratory
examination and further legal proceedings. [@1]

During the search, authorities found four bricks of dried leaves believed to be
marijuana concealed inside the parcel. [@1] The seized contraband weighed
approximately 4.274 kilograms. [@1] PDEA valued the 4.274 kilograms at P512,880.
[@1] The report described the interception as a parcel of over four kilograms of
suspected dried marijuana leaves worth over P500,000 at the Labangan delivery
hub. [@1]

PDEA-9 reiterated its call for public vigilance, saying community reporting and
cooperation remain crucial to the government's campaign against illegal drugs.
