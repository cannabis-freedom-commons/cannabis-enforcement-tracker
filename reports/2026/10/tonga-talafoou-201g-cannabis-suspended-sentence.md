Lord Chief Justice Bishop KC sentenced Viliami Taulafo at the Supreme Court in
Nuku'alofa on 16 October. [@1] Taulafo, aged 34, pleaded guilty to possessing
cannabis and unlawful possession of utensils. [@1] He is married with four
children aged between two and 10 years. [@1] He works as a self-employed farmer
and part-time stevedore at Faua Wharf. [@1]

Police raided his Talafo'ou residence on 26 February after receiving information
that he was supplying cannabis from there. [@1] Officers found him on the
veranda with seven packets of suspected cannabis on a wooden bed in front of
him, plus a plastic bag of cannabis leaves and a smoking pipe. [@1] In his
bedroom police found 1,260 pa'anga and scattered cannabis leaves. [@1] In total,
he was found in possession of 200.98 grams of cannabis. [@1]

The court set a starting point of two years imprisonment for the possession
count. [@1] After a six-month reduction for his early guilty plea and remorse,
the court imposed 18 months imprisonment, with a concurrent nine-month term on
the utensils count. [@1] He had previous convictions that did not involve drugs.
[@1] The defendant was sentenced to 18 months imprisonment, fully suspended for
two years. [@1] The suspension requires probation, no offence punishable by
imprisonment, and completion of a Salvation Army life-skills and drug-abuse
programme within 12 months. [@1] He must also complete 100 hours of community
service. [@1] The court ordered the cannabis and utensils to be destroyed. [@1]
