The Quimper criminal court sentenced a 40-year-old man on 27 October 2026. [@1]
He is a father of two children. [@1] One 40-year-old father of two from Quimper
was arrested on 16 September after a neighbour reported him to the Quimper
police. [@2] One man was tried in immediate proceedings for drug trafficking.
[@1]

A home search found 3 kg of cannabis resin and 120 g of herbal cannabis, a
combined 3.12 kg. [@2] Officers also found 258 g of heroin, 129 g of ketamine
and smaller amounts of cocaine and MDMA. [@1] Police seized more than 1,000
euros in cash and a precision scale in the flat. [@2] Phone analysis showed a
Snapchat-based trade that began in August 2025. [@1]

He admitted storing and reselling drugs since August or September 2025 while
still wearing an electronic bracelet from an earlier conviction. [@2] His record
holds 14 entries, several drug-related, and he said he sold at least once a day
to fund his own use. [@2] He had prior convictions and was in legal recidivism
under suspended probation. [@1]
