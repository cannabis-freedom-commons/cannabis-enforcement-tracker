## The arrests

On Thursday 29 October 2026, NDLEA officers conducted a follow-up operation on Idere Road in Igboora, Oyo State, and arrested Oguntona Nasiru, 46, and Soji Balogun, 47, while they were loading 554 kilograms of skunk into a Volvo truck for onward movement into town. [@1,2,3,4,5] The agency describes skunk as a strong strain of cannabis. [@1]

## The target who escaped

The agency said the operation targeted a notorious dealer known as Omo Oba, who escaped arrest on 25 October when officers seized 309.5 kilograms of the same drug linked to him. [@1,2,4,5] No further detail about Omo Oba has been published. [@1,2]
