Highway military police arrested a couple on 28 October on the Rodovia dos
Bandeirantes in Itupeva after finding 12.5 kg of hashish in their car. [@1] A
records check showed the man was wanted by the courts for robbery. [@1] Officers
took the couple to the Itupeva police station, where both remained in flagrante
custody for drug trafficking. [@1]

The stop occurred during an operation on the highway. [@1] Officers said they
noticed a strong smell of marijuana coming from inside the car. [@1] A search
found about 50 hashish tablets divided into 12 large portions. [@1] Officers
also found packaging bags, a precision scale and a baseball bat in the vehicle.
[@1] After weighing, police said the drug totalled 12.5 kilograms, which police
valued at about R$125,000. [@1]

The military police press account republished by Opina News describes the same
28 October arrest with 12.5 kg of hashish in Itupeva. [@2] A Jornal de Jundiaí
account adds that officers consulted the Muralha Paulista system to identify the
man wanted for robbery. [@3]
