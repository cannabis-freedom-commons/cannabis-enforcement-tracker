Małopolska police detained eight people, two of them members of the
international Bandidos motorcycle gang, in an investigation into drug dealing
and untaxed cigarettes. [@2] Seven of the eight received charges, including
possessing significant quantities of drugs and trading tobacco products without
the required excise marks. [@1]

The investigation was run by the drug-crime division of the Kraków provincial
police (KWP) under the supervision of the District Prosecutor's Office for
Kraków-Prądnik Biały. [@2] Police said the group came from Bochnia County and
worked across several districts of the Lesser Poland (Małopolska) region. [@2]

On 16 October, at the motorway toll booths in Balice, officers stopped two men
driving delivery vans. [@2] In the vans officers found almost half a million
cigarettes without Polish excise marks. [@2] The two detained drivers were a
38-year-old resident of Tarnów and a 47-year-old resident of Katowice. [@2]
Police identified both detained drivers as members of the Bandidos motorcycle
gang. [@2] The court jailed the 38-year-old for three months pending trial while
the 47-year-old was released under non-custodial measures. [@2] A search of the
38-year-old's flat found a handgun with ammunition and more than 300 grams of
mephedrone, cocaine and clephedrone, none of it reported to be cannabis. [@2]

On 22 October, officers of the provincial drug unit and Bochnia criminal
division detained six more people: two women aged 48 and 57 and four men aged
30, 33, 34 and 38. [@2] Searches of their homes found amphetamine, marijuana,
methamphetamine and mephedrone, which police said weighed almost 27 kilograms
gross together. [@2] Radio Kraków confirmed the 22 October searches found the
four drugs with a combined gross weight of almost 27 kilograms. [@3] Officers
also seized a heat sealer, electronic scales, sealing foil and zip bags that
police said served to prepare the drugs for sale. [@2] Of the six arrested on 22
October, two men were held in pre-trial custody for three months and three were
placed under police supervision, property sureties and a ban on leaving the
country. [@1] The near half-million untaxed cigarettes would have cost the state
more than 500,000 zloty in excise duty and about 100,000 zloty in VAT, according
to police. [@1]
