Eldoret human rights defender Benson Juma Akumu was arraigned before Resident Magistrate Naima Chelagat Kirui on 21 October 2026 on two trafficking counts. [@1] The first count charges him with trafficking 56 rolls of cannabis, valued at Ksh2,800 and not in medical preparation form; the second alleges 52 grams of heroin in straw tubes valued at Ksh15,600. [@1] The heroin is not cannabis and is excluded from the figures above. [@1]

Both alleged offences are dated 18 October 2026 at the Eldoret central police station in Uasin Gishu County. [@1] Detectives say they searched Akumu when he arrived at the station to help a detainee who had called him, and found the substances before booking him into the cells. [@1] He faced an alternative charge of resisting arrest by grabbing station grills while Constables Collins Rono and Walter Tekei acted in lawful duty. [@1]

Akumu denied both counts and told the court the charges were framed to silence his work for victims of police injustice, naming a dispute with the station commander James Lenaso and saying inmates had been incited against him. [@1] The prosecution did not oppose bond, and Kirui freed him on a bond of Ksh1.1 million or cash bail of Ksh800,000, with the matter to be mentioned on 5 November 2026. [@1]
