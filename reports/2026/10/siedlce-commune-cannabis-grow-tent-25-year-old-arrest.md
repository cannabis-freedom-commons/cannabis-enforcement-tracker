Officers of the criminal division of the Siedlce city police stopped a
25-year-old man driving a Fiat away from a property in Siedlce commune on
Tuesday, 6 October 2026. [@1] The man was taken to a police cell and on
Wednesday, 7 October heard charges of illegal cannabis cultivation and
possession of a significant quantity of narcotic substances, which carry up to
10 years in prison. [@1] After the procedural steps a prosecutor placed him
under police supervision rather than pre-trial detention. [@1]

In the car, officers found three plastic bags of substances initially identified
as marijuana, amphetamine and mephedrone. [@1] At his home they found a tent
fitted with growing equipment and, inside it, six cannabis plants with visible
flower heads. [@1] The house search also turned up dried plant material and
dried mushrooms. [@1] Officers also secured eight cacti grown under a red-violet
lamp; the man said they were Peruvian cacti with psychedelic properties, and
they were sent for tests to establish whether they contain controlled
substances. [@1]

Police said they seized almost 350 grams of drugs, six cannabis bushes and eight
cacti in all, and sent the plants and substances for further testing. [@2] The
cacti, and whether possessing them breaks the law, will depend on the test
results, because the man's own statement does not settle their properties. [@1]
The case is at the preparatory proceedings stage, and the final legal
classification of the secured plants and substances will depend on the test
results and the investigators' findings. [@3]
