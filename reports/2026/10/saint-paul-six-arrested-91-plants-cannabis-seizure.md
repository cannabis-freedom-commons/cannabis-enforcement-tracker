A judicial investigation led by the Saint-Paul research brigade targeted a
suspected cannabis trafficking network organised over several years around
cultivation and resale. [@1] On 6 October 2026, investigators from the
Saint-Paul gendarmerie company, supported by the Clermont-Ferrand mobile
gendarmerie squadron and the dog-investigation unit, carried out the judicial
operation. [@1] The operation led to the arrest of six people. [@1] The six
adults were placed in police custody. [@1] After being brought before the
courts, two of them were convicted, while the other accused were to be summoned
later. [@2]

Officers seized 91 cannabis plants. [@1] They seized 3,560 grams of cannabis
heads. [@1] They seized 7,300 grams of cannabis resin. [@1] They also seized
2,090 grams of crack, which is not counted in the cannabis weight. [@1] They
seized 7,820 euros in cash. [@1]

At the main home searched, officers also found a significant quantity of crack
and cannabis resin. [@1] Réunion La 1ère described the case as the dismantling
of a cannabis cultivation and resale network following the research brigade's
investigation. [@2]
