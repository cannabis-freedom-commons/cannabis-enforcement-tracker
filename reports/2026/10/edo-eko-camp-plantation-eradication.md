On 28 October 2026, NDLEA operatives supported by soldiers raided Eko Camp near Illushi in Esan South-East, Edo State, and destroyed a cannabis plantation covering 1.646 hectares. [@1,2,3,4] NDLEA estimated that the crop could yield 4,115.53 kg of cannabis; this is a projected yield, not a reported weight of plants recovered or destroyed. [@1,2]

The agency said operatives arrested Christiana Uboi, 50, at the camp with 301 kg of processed cannabis. [@1,2,3,4] It also reported recovering another 176 kg of processed cannabis at the site. [@1,2] The report gives no count of the growing plants. [@1] Channels Television repeated the NDLEA account, while the agency's own post places the plantation report in a broader roundup of operations that also included unrelated airport arrests. [@1,2] NDLEA published its account on 1 November, four days after the plantation raid. [@1] Neither report states whether Uboi or another person was charged, and neither gives a later court outcome. [@1,2]
