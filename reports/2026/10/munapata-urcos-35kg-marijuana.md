Cusco anti-drug police intervened and detained two alleged members of the
suspected criminal group 'Las Silenciosas del Sur' for alleged drug trafficking
involving the illegal transport of marijuana. [@1] The detainees were identified
as 33-year-old Nelly Roxana Paucar Quispe and 17-year-old A.T.H. [@2]
Authorities said neither had police records and the two had no direct family
relationship. [@2] They were travelling on an 'HC Universal' passenger bus bound
for Juliaca when they were stopped. [@1] After the case was placed with the
Public Ministry, the adult woman remained detained at the police station while
the minor was referred to the family prosecutor. [@2]

The operation took place in the early hours of 28 October 2026 in the Muñapata
sector of Urcos district, Quispicanchi province. [@2] A search of the bus
luggage hold found two bags holding four ovoid packages containing greenish dry
herb. [@1] Field testing of the herb with a chemical reagent returned positive
for Cannabis sativa (marijuana). [@1] Police weighed the seized cannabis at
35.190 kilograms. [@2] Officers also seized two mobile phones. [@1] The
detainees, the seized substance and the other seized items were taken to Cusco
for urgent proceedings with the Public Ministry representative. [@1]

According to preliminary accounts, an unknown man approached the woman at the
Cusco bus terminal and entrusted her with carrying the load to Juliaca, where
third parties would collect it. [@2] She was reportedly offered 400 soles for
delivering the load. [@2] Both remained silent on their defence counsel's advice
while police continued working to identify the man who handed over the load and
the intended recipients in Juliaca. [@2] The Cusco regional police chief said
investigators presumed the marijuana was produced and processed in some valley
in the Cusco department, with that line still under investigation. [@2] Police
said the drug will be destroyed once the criminal process is completed. [@3]
