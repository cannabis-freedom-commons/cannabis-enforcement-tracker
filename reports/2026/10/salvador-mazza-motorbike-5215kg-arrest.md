Officers of the Gendarmería Nacional's Squadron 61 "Salvador Mazza" were
patrolling on foot along a road called Algarrobal, in the town of Salvador
Mazza, in the northern Argentine province of Salta, when they stopped a Honda
Tornado motorcycle carrying two adult men. [@1] The patrol was part of Operación
Plan Güemes, the force's deployment on the Salta border with Bolivia. [@1]

Opening the two backpacks the men were carrying, the gendarmes found five
irregular ochre-coloured packages holding a compact greenish-brown substance.
[@1] Field tests with the force's Narcotest kit were positive for cannabis
sativa, with a total weight of 5 kilograms 215 grams. [@1]

The Federal Prosecution Office of Tartagal directed the detention of the two men
and the seizure of the drug, the motorcycle, a telephone and other items of
interest to the case. [@1]
