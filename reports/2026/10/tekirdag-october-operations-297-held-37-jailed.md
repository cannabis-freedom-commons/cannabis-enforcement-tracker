Tekirdag Provincial Gendarmerie Command announced the balance of its operations
carried out between 1 and 31 October 2026 across the province targeting drugs,
weapons smuggling and counterfeit goods. [@2] The gendarmerie caught 297
suspects during the month-long operations. [@1] Thirty-seven of the suspects
were remanded in custody. [@1]

The anti-drug operations seized 1 kilogram 49 grams of kubar esrar and 2 grams
of skunk, counted together as 1.049 kilograms of cannabis. [@1] The same haul
included 253 grams of bonzai, 168 grams of cocaine, 233 grams of methamphetamine
and 112 grams of heroin, which are not counted as cannabis. [@2]

The operations also yielded weapons, counterfeit and smuggling items, including
6 pistols, 8 hunting rifles, 11,162 counterfeit clothing items and 36
antiquities, reported alongside the drug figures. [@1] The gendarmerie said its
work to secure the peace and safety of citizens against crime would continue.
[@1]
