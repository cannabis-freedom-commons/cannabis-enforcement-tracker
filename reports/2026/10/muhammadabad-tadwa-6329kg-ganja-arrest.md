Muhammadabad police arrested 25-year-old Jaiprakash Rajbhar on Friday 30 October
2026. [@1] The arrest took place near the Tadwa railway crossing on the
Yusufpur–Kasimabad road. [@1] Police seized 6.329 kg of ganja and a Vivo mobile
phone from him. [@3] Officers were on patrol when an informer told them a man
was waiting at the crossing with cannabis, station house officer Vijay Pratap
Singh said. [@1]

Sub-inspector Lallan Ram Bind led the arresting team, ABT News reported. [@3]
Rajbhar is a resident of Husepur village in the Muhammadabad police station
area. [@1] Police registered case 344/2026 at Muhammadabad police station under
sections 8 and 20 of the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act.
[@2] Singh said another case, under the Gambling Act, was already registered
against Rajbhar. [@2]

The arrest was part of a campaign against drug trafficking in Ghazipur, ABT News
reported. [@3] It was carried out under the direction of the additional
superintendent of police (rural) and the Muhammadabad circle officer. [@3]
