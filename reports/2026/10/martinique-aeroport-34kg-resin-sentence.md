A 34-year-old man tried to bring 34 kilos of cannabis resin into Martinique.
[@1] The man is originally from Limoges in central France. [@1] He had already
been convicted 11 times before this case. [@1] Customs officers arrested him
that weekend. [@1] The case centred on Martinique’s Aimé Césaire airport. [@1]
He carried the drug in his suitcases while in a state of legal repeat offending.
[@1]

After custody and an investigation by the Office Anti-stupéfiants, the anti-drug
office, he faced trial. [@1] He was tried in immediate-appearance proceedings on
14 October 2026. [@1] The court sentenced him to 3 years and 4 months in prison
and banned him from staying in Martinique. [@1] He was jailed in Ducos prison.
[@1] He was also ordered to pay a 50,000-euro customs fine. [@1]

Cannabis resin is highly prized in Martinique, where one kilo of resin can be
exchanged for one kilo of cocaine. [@1]
