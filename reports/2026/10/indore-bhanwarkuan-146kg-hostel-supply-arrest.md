Bhanwarkuan police arrested one accused with 1.460 kg of ganja in an action
against illegal narcotics trafficking. [@1] The accused was identified as
26-year-old Yogesh Sharma, son of Ramjilal Sharma, resident of Mehona in Bhind
district. [@1] Police said he had studied up to Class 12 and worked operating a
hotel in Vijay Nagar. [@1] Police said he made small packets of ganja and
supplied them to students in hostels in the police-station area. [@1]

On the night of 30 October, a Bhanwarkuan police team was searching for suspects
and conducting checks. [@1] Officers saw a man sitting on a motorcycle with a
plastic bag in an open ground behind the public Teen Imli bus stand. [@1] He
became nervous on seeing police, and the team surrounded and caught him. [@1] On
questioning he gave his name as Yogesh Sharma. [@1] A search of the plastic sack
with him recovered 1.460 kg of ganja. [@1] Police said the seized ganja was
valued at Rs 21,000 and a motorcycle was also seized from his possession. [@1]
Police put the total value of the seized property at about Rs 1.62 lakh. [@1]
Besides the ganja, police seized an FZS-company motorcycle valued at about Rs
1.21 lakh. [@1]

A case was registered against him at Bhanwarkuan police station as crime number
1178/2026 under sections 8/20 of the NDPS Act. [@1] Police said they are now
questioning him about the source of the ganja, the supplier network and the
involvement of other people. [@1] The operation was conducted under the
leadership of station in-charge Santosh Dudhi. [@1]
