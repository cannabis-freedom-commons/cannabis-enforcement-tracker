On 2 October officers tried to stop a Jeep Compass on the Martuni-Getap road,
but the Vardenis residents inside tried to flee in the car. [@1] The flight
ended in Geghhovit village, where the two men were detained on suspicion of
illegally acquiring, storing, transporting and selling smuggled drugs and were
taken to the Martuni Community Police division while the car was sealed and sent
to a guarded area. [@2] Both men were later arrested. [@1]

A personal search of the 49-year-old passenger found a marijuana-filled
cigarette. [@2] Court-authorised searches of the 35-year-old driver’s Jeep
Compass found three packages containing 3 kg 200 g of marijuana. [@1] At the
driver’s home officers also found another marijuana-filled cigarette, 356
cartridges and three automatic-rifle magazines. [@2] At the 49-year-old man’s
residence officers found about 130 g of marijuana and three cannabis stems. [@2]

On 1 October Gegharkunik criminal police and National Security Service officers
had received operational information that two Vardenis residents had gone to
Syunik region to acquire an especially large amount of smuggled marijuana. [@2]
A criminal proceeding was opened and the subsequent searches were carried out on
the basis of court decisions. [@1] The preliminary investigation was still
continuing. [@1]
