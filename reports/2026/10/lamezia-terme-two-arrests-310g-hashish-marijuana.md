State Police arrested two foreign citizens in flagrante in Lamezia Terme on
suspicion of possessing drugs for dealing. [@1] The two were legally present in
Italy. [@1] A judge validated the arrests and ordered both men to remain
resident in a set place under obbligo di dimora. [@1]

The operation began with a roadside check in the city centre, where officers
found the two with a few grams of hashish. [@1] Follow-up searches extending to
their homes seized over 150 grams of hashish, 160 grams of marijuana, one hemp
plantlet and a precision scale. [@1]

Officers of the local Commissariat carried out the operation with support from
the Vibo Valentia Crime Prevention Unit. [@1] The operation was coordinated by
the Lamezia Terme public prosecutor's office. [@1]
