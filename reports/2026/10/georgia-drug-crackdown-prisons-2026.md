The Interior Ministry registered 5,598 drug crimes in 2024, 10,475 in 2025 and 7,533 in the first half of 2026; more than half were purchase or possession under Article 260. The Prosecutor General's Office opened 119 drug cases against Russian citizens in 2024, 196 in 2025 and 234 in the first nine months of 2026. Georgia's prisons held 12,344 people in June 2026, a 43% rise in eighteen months; in one case a woman caught with a few grams of marijuana received two years, deportation and a 20-year entry ban, while a man caught with about 60 grams was sentenced to three years. [@1]

A Russian woman arrested in Tbilisi in May 2026 after police found marijuana at her flat was sentenced to two years in prison, followed by deportation and a 20-year ban on entering Georgia. [@2] Police searching the home of a Russian man in Tbilisi in June 2026 seized about 60 grams of marijuana, several thousand lari, tape and scales, and he was accused of facilitating sales under Article 260 (4). [@2] A Tbilisi court sentenced him to three years in prison on 24 September 2026 after he rejected a prosecutor's offer of a 200,000 lari fine and expulsion. [@2]
