Police in Chandurthi circle, Rajanna Sircilla district, Telangana, detained
three people during checks on 30 October 2026, Circle Inspector K. Ravinder
said. [@1] One person from Sanugula village was stopped on the village
outskirts, and two from Marupaka village were stopped at Jammi Gadda in
Sanugula. [@2] The police circle covers the mandals, or sub-districts, of
Chandurthi, Rudrangi and Konaraopeta. [@1]

Officers tested all three with E-Z Split Key drug screen urine kits, and their
samples came back positive for THC, the main psychoactive compound in cannabis.
[@2] Police said the tests established at a preliminary stage that they had
consumed ganja, the local term for cannabis. [@1] They were taken into custody
and taken to court for further action. [@1]

Ravinder said the police aim was not only to punish ganja users but to bring a
positive change in their lives. [@1] Those caught through the tests would
receive special counselling and be moved to government-run rehabilitation
centres, he said. [@1] He warned that selling, buying, transporting or consuming
ganja would bring serious legal consequences. [@2] He asked residents to report
ganja sales or use to police and said informants' details would be kept
confidential. [@2]
