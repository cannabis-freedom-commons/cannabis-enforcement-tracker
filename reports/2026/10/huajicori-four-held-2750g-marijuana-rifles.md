Army soldiers and Nayarit state security officers arrested four people in
flagrante on Friday 30 October 2026 in the municipality of Huajicori, Nayarit,
saying they had weapons, ammunition and drugs. [@1] Huajicori is a municipality
in the northern sierra of Nayarit. [@1]

The security forces seized five long guns, 44 magazines and 1,382 cartridges
from the detainees. [@1] They also seized 2.750 kg of marijuana and 24 doses of
methamphetamine. [@1]

The four detainees and the seized items were handed to the ministerial
authority. [@1]
