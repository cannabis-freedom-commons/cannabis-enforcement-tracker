Police arrested four men late on Wednesday evening at the vegetable market in
Andar, in the Andar police-station area of Siwan district, Bihar, after a tip
that ganja and liquor were there. [@1,2] The men were Vikas Kumar, also known as
Pintu, his brother Deepak Kumar, Prakash Kumar and their cousin Akash Kumar.
[@1] Station house officer Sunil Kumar registered a first information report,
the written complaint that opens a criminal case in India, and on Thursday
afternoon sent all four into judicial custody. [@1]

The tip reached the station on Wednesday evening. [@1] Officers recovered 6.280
kilograms of ganja, the Hindi name for cannabis, from the men, together with
7.125 litres of liquor, 13,500 rupees in cash and five smartphones. [@1] The
station chief went out with a police party, caught the four and brought them to
the police station, where the required legal steps were completed. [@1] Sunil
Kumar said a first information report had been registered and the accused sent
to judicial custody. [@1] Police described what they recovered as a narcotic
substance and liquor. [@1]

Vikas Kumar alias Pintu was a BJP youth wing vice-president and a former BDC member. [@2]
