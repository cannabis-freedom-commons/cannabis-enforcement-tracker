Five people were arrested in Sainte-Adèle on Thursday 29 October 2026 in a
narcotics investigation that followed a series of searches leading to seizures
of weapons and drugs. [@1] Police conducted three searches targeting rooms in an
establishment located on rue Séraphin in order to intervene against the group.
[@1] Those apprehended were a 42-year-old man, two women aged 41, one woman aged
36 and one woman aged 31. [@1]

Authorities seized six different weapons during the searches. [@1] The
substances seized included methamphetamine tablets, cocaine, GHB, cannabis and
hashish, crack and prescription medication. [@1] Officers also seized more than
4,500 dollars in cash in connection with the same searches. [@1] The operation
stemmed from information received from members of the public. [@1]

The five suspects are expected to return before the courts at a later date. [@1]
They could face charges relating to narcotics, illicit cannabis and firearms.
[@1] The file was handled as a drug case in which illicit cannabis was listed
separately from other narcotics among the possible charges. [@1]
