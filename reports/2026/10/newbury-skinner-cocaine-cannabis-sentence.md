Simon Skinner, 33, from Berkshire, was sentenced to three years and four months
in prison after being found with cocaine, cannabis and £450 in cash. [@1] The
33-year-old, of Gaywood Drive, Newbury, pleaded guilty to possession with intent
to supply cocaine and cannabis. [@1]

On August 4, Thames Valley Police officers on proactive patrol spotted a Ford
Mondeo leaving the car park of The Swan pub. [@1] Officers stopped the vehicle
after it turned into Dorneywood Way, Newbury, where they detected a strong smell
of cannabis. [@1] An initial search found Skinner with £450 in cash and a mobile
phone. [@1] He then attempted to run away from officers but was quickly caught.
[@1] A subsequent search uncovered two bags of cocaine hidden inside one of his
socks. [@1] Skinner admitted possessing cannabis and handed officers a bag of
the drug concealed in his waistband, and further cannabis was found inside the
vehicle. [@1]

Police later searched his home address, where they discovered approximately 330g
of cocaine inside a safe under his bed. [@1] Further quantities of cannabis,
digital scales and mobile phones were also recovered. [@1] The keys to the safe
were found in Skinner's possession when he was arrested. [@1] Investigating
officer PC Simon Abbott said Skinner was actively involved in supplying illegal
drugs in the Newbury area. [@1]

Skinner was sentenced at Reading Crown Court on October 26. [@1] He was also
ordered to pay a £228 surcharge. [@1] The court authorised the forfeiture and
destruction of the drugs, phones and other items seized, as well as the
forfeiture of the £450 in cash. [@1]
