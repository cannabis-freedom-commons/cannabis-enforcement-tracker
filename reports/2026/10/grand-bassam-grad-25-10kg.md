Officers of the Groupe de répression anti-drogue (GRAD) of the Autonomous Port of Abidjan seized
25.10 kg of cannabis at Grand-Bassam on Thursday 22 October 2026. [@1,2] They arrested two people,
who were placed in garde à vue, French for police custody, for the investigation. [@1,2]

The operation was carried out under Opération Épervier Spécial, a police drive against drug
trafficking and the movement of illicit substances. [@2] Investigators were to establish where the
cannabis came from, where it was going and whether the two belonged to a wider network. [@1,2]

L'Avenir reported that the GRAD had carried out a series of operations since 1 October and had also
destroyed ten fumoirs, drug-smoking spots, that had been reoccupied after earlier raids. [@1]
