Cairo Criminal Court sentenced an unemployed man to 15 years of rigorous
imprisonment and a 200,000-Egyptian-pound fine. [@1] The court convicted him of
trafficking narcotics, possessing a bladed weapon without a legal justification
and resisting security force members while they tried to arrest him in Al-Salam.
[@1] Police found hashish, 20 tramadol tablets, a folding knife, cash and a
mobile phone in his possession. [@1]

Police reports say he was arrested in a series of ambushes after Al-Salam police
station received a tip that he was selling drugs, and that he was a repeat
offender carrying 100 grams of hashish. [@2] The chemical laboratory report
confirmed that the brown pieces in a clear plastic bag were hashish, listed in
Schedule 1 of the drug-control law. [@1] The court said his phone was used to
contact clients and the cash he held was the proceeds of sales. [@2]

The court said the intent to trade drugs is established when a defendant's will
is directed at circulating and promoting them to others. [@1]
