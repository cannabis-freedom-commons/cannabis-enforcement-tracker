Police detained 30-year-old K.T.Ç. in connection with the İlkadım drug
operation. [@2] After his procedures were completed at the Samsun anti-narcotics
branch, he was transferred to Samsun Courthouse. [@1]

The Samsun Provincial Police Directorate's narcotics teams mounted the operation
in İlkadım district as part of work against drug trade. [@1] Officers searched a
residence and adjoining land used by the suspect. [@2] In the house and land
search, officers seized 9,746 synthetic pharma pills, 7 ecstasy pills and 2.78
grams of cannabis, called esrar in Turkish. [@1] The same seizure is reported as
9,746 synthetic pills, 7 ecstasy pills and 2.78 grams of esrar, with the 2.78
grams equal to 0.00278 kilograms and the pills excluded from the cannabis
figure. [@2]

Police confiscated the seized substances when K.T.Ç. was taken into custody.
[@1] Police started judicial proceedings over the items seized in the operation.
