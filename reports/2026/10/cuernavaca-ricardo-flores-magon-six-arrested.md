On Calle 34, officers saw several men, two of them leaning on the boot of a Kia
Río while drinking alcohol. [@1] After seeing one man holding an object
resembling a firearm, officers searched the men and the vehicle. [@1] The six
were placed at the disposal of the competent authority to determine their legal
situation for alleged crimes against health. [@1] They are presumed innocent
until a judicial authority declares their responsibility. [@1]

They also seized the Kia Río, a pellet gun, two radios, a digital gram scale,
alcohol bottles and several mobile phones. [@1] The detainees were identified as
Jorge “N”, 43, Gabriel de Jesús “N”, 20, Jesús Eduardo “N”, 29, Víctor Manuel
“N”, 18, Carlos “N”, 21, and Fabián “N”, 43. [@2]

Authorities presented the intervention as part of the state “Morelos por la
Seguridad” strategy with state police, the National Guard and the Army. [@2]
