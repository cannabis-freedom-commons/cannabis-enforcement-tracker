State security officers in Tashkent stopped for a check and detained a resident
of Yunusabad District born in 1993 who had already been under observation. [@1]
Officers in Fergana District detained a local resident born in 1988 while he was
allegedly trying to sell hashish to a sham buyer. [@1] Officers in Fergana
detained a man born in 1997 after he collected hashish from a cache in Fergana
District and stopped him on his way home. [@1]

A search in the presence of witnesses found three small containers holding 257 g
of hashish oil on the Tashkent detainee. [@1] A later search at his residence
recovered another 2 kg 831 g of the same substance along with electronic scales.
[@1] The Fergana District sale case involved 982 g of hashish offered for
$10,700. [@1] The Fergana cache case involved 956 g of hashish taken from a
hiding place in Fergana District. [@1]

The Tashkent operation was carried out by the city State Security Service
department with the main internal-affairs department, while the Fergana
operations involved the regional security department with customs and
internal-affairs officers. [@1] Investigators said the Tashkent detainee worked
in an online shop selling drugs. [@1] Law-enforcement officials said the hashish
in both Fergana cases had been smuggled from a neighbouring state, in one case
left in a cache before collection. [@1] Criminal cases were opened over all
three episodes and investigative actions were continuing. [@1]
