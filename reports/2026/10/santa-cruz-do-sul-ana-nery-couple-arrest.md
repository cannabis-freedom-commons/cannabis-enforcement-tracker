The Military Brigade's Força Tática arrested a 21-year-old man and a 24-year-old
woman in flagrante in the early hours of 31 October 2026 in Santa Cruz do Sul.
[@1] The arrest happened at about 1:30am in the square in the Ana Nery
neighbourhood, on Rua Pereira da Cunha, during an anti-trafficking action. [@1]
Officers saw the pair arrive by motorcycle to make a tele-entrega drug delivery
to a user in the square. [@1] When patrol cars approached, the couple tried to
flee on the motorcycle but were caught and searched. [@1]

Officers found portions of cocaine and maconha with the couple, along with money
and a cellphone. [@1] In further searches officers seized two more maconha
bricks said to belong to the criminal group working in tele-entrega trafficking.
[@1] The drug buyer was also detained and the drug he had bought was seized.
[@1]

The pair were handcuffed, taken to the Central UPA for examinations and then to
the police station. [@1] The man was later taken to the Regional Prison of Santa
Cruz do Sul and the woman to the Women's State Prison in Rio Pardo. [@1] Police
described the woman as already having prior records for trafficking and the pair
as part of a group involved in tele-entrega drug dealing. [@1]
