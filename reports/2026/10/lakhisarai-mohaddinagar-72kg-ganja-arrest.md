Bihar's prohibition (excise) police carried out their first raid in Mohaddinagar
village in the Halsi police-station area after receiving a tip-off. [@1] The
team found 72 kg of ganja and one litre of distilled mahua liquor with Rinku
Devi, the wife of late Rajendra Choudhary. [@1] Officers arrested Devi at the
scene. [@1] In a second action at Thana Chowk in Lakhisarai, three men from
Pipariya Dih village were arrested on charges of drinking alcohol. [@1] The
three men were identified as Jitendra Kumar, Suraj Kumar and Deepak Kumar. [@1]
All four detainees were sent to judicial custody. [@1]

The Mohaddinagar haul totalled 72 kg of ganja and one litre of chulai, a
distilled mahua country liquor, which police confiscated. [@1] The excise police
report records the woman as held for selling liquor, while the three men were
held for consumption. [@1]

Police have confirmed no larger trafficking network so far, with further legal
proceedings continuing on the basis of the seizure and arrests. [@1] The size of
the haul has widened the inquiry toward tracing how the consignment reached
Mohaddinagar and where it was meant to be used. [@1]
