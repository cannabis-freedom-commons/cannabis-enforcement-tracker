Cavalry officers apprehended an adolescent girl on the night of Friday 30
October 2026 in the Nossa Senhora Aparecida neighbourhood of Campo Novo do
Parecis on suspicion of drug trafficking. [@1] The teenager was approached
during a cavalry patrol on Rua Severino de Lima. [@1] Officers had first seen
Éric Luan Lima de Souza smoking marijuana at a bus stop, and he told them that
his neighbour, identified only as M.A.A.F.S., was selling drugs in the area.
[@1] Officers went to the girl's house and spoke with her in the presence of her
parents. [@1] She admitted the dealing and said the material was in a brown bag
at the back of the property. [@1]

Officers searched the back of the property with the teenager, her brother and
her parents and found packaging utensils, a precision scale and portions of
marijuana. [@1] The seizure was listed as a half-bar of marijuana weighing 355
g, three medium wraps weighing 34 g, a precision scale, plastic film, packaging,
stickers referring to the CV faction, two bags and R$510. [@1]

The girl confirmed that she sold drugs in the area using a messaging app. [@2]
The teenager and Éric were taken to the Civil Police station, accompanied by the
teenager's parents. [@2] Police recorded that neither of the two had prior
criminal passages. [@2]
