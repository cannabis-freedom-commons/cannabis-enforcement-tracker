The prosecutor of the Gdańsk-Wrzeszcz district prosecutor's office, the local
prosecution authority for that part of Gdańsk, charged the man with
participating in trafficking a significant quantity of drugs. [@2] The man was
placed under police supervision, banned from leaving the country and required to
post bail, and he faces up to 12 years in prison. [@1]

During the search officers found plastic containers and bags holding almost 3 kg
of marijuana, together with an electronic scale, mobile phones and tens of
thousands of zlotys in cash. [@1] Police sent all the seized drugs to a court
expert, whose examination so far found the material amounted to more than 2,690
portions. [@2] Police put the black-market value of the seized marijuana at
about 130,000 zlotys. [@1]

Detectives said they obtained the tip during duty and then established the man's
full identity and address. [@2] Officers said trafficking ordinarily carries up
to 8 years in prison, rising to up to 12 years when a significant quantity is
involved. [@2]
