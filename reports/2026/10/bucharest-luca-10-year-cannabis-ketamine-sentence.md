Remus Tatian Luca received a merged sentence of 10 years, 10 months and 20 days
in prison after the court combined the penalties in a new drug case with those
from an earlier case. [@1] In decision 1309/2026 in case 5963/3/2024, the court
imposed five years and six months for continued trafficking in risk and
high-risk drugs and eight months for possession for personal use. [@1] The total
exceeded ten years because it included an earlier final conviction in which Luca
had received eight years and six months. [@1] In the same judgment, Radu Matei
Mitulescu received five years, eight months and 20 days in prison, while Tudor
Ionuț Apostol received five months in prison with enforcement postponed for a
two-year supervision period plus 60 days of community work. [@1]

On 29 December 2023, Luca and a woman later heard as a witness were stopped
while moving through an area of Bucharest. [@1] Laboratory tests found the woman
was carrying 24.98 grams of cannabis and 1.43 grams of powder containing
cocaine. [@1] The same day, investigators searched Luca's home in Sector 2 and
seized jars, a container marked COOKIES, a foil with white powder, a
self-sealing bag with plant matter, and a plate and card bearing powder traces.
[@1] The laboratory identified 483.72 grams of ketamine-containing substance,
two cannabis amounts of 57.20 and 1,466.9 grams, and two cocaine-and-creatine
powders of 4.13 and 10.30 grams, plus cocaine traces on the plate and card. [@1]
The court found that the 1,524.10 grams of cannabis and the ketamine substance
were kept for sale, while the cocaine powders totalling 14.43 grams were for
personal use. [@1] Apostol was found with 14.77 grams of cannabis during the
search of Mitulescu's home. [@1]

For Luca, the court retained four trafficking acts: two exchanges with
Mitulescu, giving cannabis to the woman witness, and holding cannabis and
ketamine for sale. [@1] The court acquitted Luca of bringing drugs into the
country and did not count the alleged resale of about 13 kilograms as
trafficking. [@1] The 15 October 2026 sentence is not final, with Luca's appeal
recorded on 23 October 2026 and other challenges also listed. [@1]
