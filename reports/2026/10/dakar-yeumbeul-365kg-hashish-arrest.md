Dakar's Regional Narcotics Brigade (BRS), a unit of the Central Office for the
Suppression of Illicit Drug Trafficking (OCRTIS), detained three men on 21
October and seized 36.5 kg of hashish, a cannabis resin. [@1] The operation
began after a tip-off that someone was urgently trying to sell a large quantity
of drugs. [@1] Officers arrested two suspects as they were about to make a
delivery. [@1] Police identified one as M. Dieng, a 52-year-old fan repairman
living in Yeumbeul Asecna and known as Afia. [@2] His accomplice was A. Ngom, a
47-year-old guard living in the same area. [@2] Dieng denied owning the drugs
and told investigators that K. Fall had given him the keys to his room. [@2]
Police arrested Fall, the third suspect, at 8:30 p.m. the same day. [@1] Fall,
aged 34, who presents himself as a Thiak-Thiak delivery rider living in Médina,
admitted the facts during questioning. [@2]

A search of the room found a bag containing 36 blocks of hashish weighing 36 kg
in total. [@1] The reported total for the operation was 36.5 kg of hashish,
which Seneweb valued at more than 300 million CFA francs. [@2] Officers also
sealed the suspects' mobile phones for the investigation. [@1]

To intercept the network, police had placed a fictitious order for one block
negotiated at 500,000 CFA francs. [@2] The handover was fixed at the
accomplice's home in Yeumbeul Asecna. [@2] Officers intervened there and
arrested both men with the ordered sample. [@2] Fall said the drugs had been
entrusted to him by his younger sister M. F. Fall, known as Mamy, who had
previously been arrested by the same unit. [@2] The three men were placed in
custody for criminal association, possession and domestic trafficking in an
organised gang, plus complicity, after being informed of their rights including
assistance by counsel. [@1] The prosecutor was informed of the operation and the
charges against the suspects. [@2]
