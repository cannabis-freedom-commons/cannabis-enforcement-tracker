Municipal and state investigators seized a total of 48 firearms in Ciudad Juárez
during October 2026, including 41 short guns and seven long guns. [@1]

The two agencies together seized a total of 103 kilograms of various drugs in
the same October period. [@1]

The municipal force said it seized 27 short guns, five long guns, 117 useful
cartridges and 19 magazines. [@1]

Municipal police detained 32 people in October for federal firearms offences.
[@1]

Municipal police said they seized 30 kilograms of marijuana, one kilogram of
cocaine and three kilograms of crystal, plus 394 doses of heroin. [@1]

Municipal operations led to 126 health-crime arrests of alleged retail dealers
known locally as narcomenudistas or puchadores. [@1]

State investigators seized 14 short guns and two long guns, plus 15 magazines
and 106 cartridges, in October. [@1]

The same state agency seized 60 kilograms of marijuana, 1.372 kilograms of
crystal, 1.639 kilograms of cocaine and 5.870 kilograms of fentanyl. [@1]

A second outlet reported that the municipal police alone seized 32 firearms and
34 kilograms of drugs in Ciudad Juárez during October. [@2]

The second account gives the same municipal weapons breakdown of 27 short and
five long guns with 19 magazines and says 32 people were detained. [@2]

It gives the same municipal drug breakdown of 30 kilos of marijuana, one of
cocaine and three of crystal with 394 heroin doses, and 126 health-crime
detentions. [@2]

The municipal total for the month was 158 detained people. [@2]

Other agencies working in the city did not publish their October seizure
information. [@1]
