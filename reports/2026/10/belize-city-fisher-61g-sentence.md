A 65-year-old Belize City man was sentenced to three months in prison after
pleading guilty to a drug trafficking charge. [@1] The man was identified as
Orlando Anthony Fisher, an unemployed resident of Kelly Street. [@1] Fisher
appeared before the Belize City Magistrate’s Court on Monday, October 26 charged
with possession of a controlled drug with intent to supply. [@1] Fisher pleaded
guilty to the charge. [@1] The court considered his extensive history of
drug-related offenses, including five previous convictions, before imposing a
custodial sentence of three months. [@1] He was subsequently sent to prison to
begin serving his sentence. [@1]

Fisher was detained around midday on Friday, October 23 while police officers
were conducting patrols in the Back-A-Town area of Belize City. [@1] Police
patrolling near Lindos Alley reported observing Fisher walking along East Collet
Canal and said he appeared uneasy upon noticing their presence. [@1] Officers
conducting a search allegedly discovered a transparent green plastic bag
containing suspected cannabis concealed in the front of his pants. [@1] The
substance was subsequently weighed and reportedly amounted to 61 grams. [@1]

Under Belize’s Misuse of Drugs Act, possession of more than 60 grams of cannabis
carries a legal presumption of intent to supply unless the contrary is proven.
[@1] The new sentence added another conviction to a criminal record that already
included five previous drug offenses. [@1]
