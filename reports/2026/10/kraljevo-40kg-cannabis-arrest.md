Police in Kraljevo arrested a 54-year-old man on suspicion of producing illegal
drugs and selling them without authorisation. [@1,2] Officers searched his flat and
other premises he uses and found about 40 kilograms of plant material suspected to
be cannabis. [@1,2] Of that amount, 21 kilograms was fresh and the rest was dried,
and police also seized equipment for growing the plant. [@2]

The Interior Ministry announced the arrest on 21 October 2026. [@1] The statement
gives the suspect's year of birth as 1972; Tanjug reported that he is 54. [@1,2]
The police did not say when the search took place. [@1]

He was held for up to 48 hours and was to be brought before the Higher Public
Prosecutor's Office in Kraljevo with a criminal complaint. [@1,2] The ministry's
statement says nothing about what the suspect told police, and no guilt has been
established. [@1] No charge or court date was reported. [@1,2]
