Officers of the Regional Brigade for Combating Drugs in Gafsa, the
governorate-level anti-drug police unit, dismantled a network suspected of
trafficking drugs between Hassi El Frid in Kasserine Governorate and Gafsa
Governorate. [@2] The Kasserine end of the route was the delegation of Hassi El
Frid, a district-level division known in Arabic as معتمدية حاسي الفريد, inside
Kasserine Governorate. [@1] During the resulting security operation, officers
arrested four people suspected of belonging to that trafficking network. [@1]

The operation seized a quantity of cannabis, identified as القنب الهندي, the
cannabis component recorded here. [@2] Officers also seized a separate quantity
of raw cocaine, reported alongside the cannabis as الكوكايين الخام. [@1] In the
same operation, officers seized an undisclosed sum of money together with two
cars and a motorcycle. [@2]

Police described the case as a continuation of the ongoing work of the
Sub-Directorate of Judicial Police at El Gorjani against drug trafficking. [@2]
That work is aimed at pursuing drug dealers and dismantling the networks active
in trafficking drugs. [@2]
