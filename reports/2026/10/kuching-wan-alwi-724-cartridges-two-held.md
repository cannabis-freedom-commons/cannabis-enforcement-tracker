Kuching police arrested two women at a residence in Jalan Wan Alwi on Oct 20,
2026. [@1] The suspects were a 29-year-old local woman and a 25-year-old foreign
woman. [@1] The arrests were made by the Narcotics Criminal Investigation
Division of the Kuching District Police Headquarters. [@2] A preliminary urine
screening found the local suspect negative for drugs while the foreign national
tested positive for ketamine. [@2] Police checks found that neither suspect had
any previous criminal record. [@2] Both women were remanded for five days from
Oct 22 to 26 under Section 117 of the Criminal Procedure Code. [@2] The case is
being investigated under Section 39B of the Dangerous Drugs Act 1952. [@1] That
section carries the death penalty or life imprisonment and whipping of not less
than 12 strokes on conviction. [@2]

Police seized 724 cartridges suspected to contain cannabis liquid in the raid.
[@2] Police valued the cartridges at RM347,520. [@1] Officers also seized
RM5,087 in cash and SGD67 believed to be proceeds from drug trafficking. [@2]

Kuching district police chief ACP Alexson Naga Chabu said the syndicate was
believed to have been active since August 2026. [@1] He said it used digital
communication applications to contact buyers, with drug supplies delivered
around Kuching. [@1] He said the operation resulted from continuous intelligence
gathering and cooperation from the public. [@2]
