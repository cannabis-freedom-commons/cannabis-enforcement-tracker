Officers of the GRAD Spécial, the special anti-drug squad of Côte d'Ivoire's
drug police (DPSD), arrested a 44-year-old man at a home in the Paillet quarter
of Adjamé, near the Amber crossroads in Abidjan, on Wednesday 7 October 2026.
[@1] The squad acted on an anonymous tip that drugs were being kept in a home in
the quarter. [@1] A team reached the address at 13:32, entered the courtyard and
found the flat concerned. [@2]

The search of the suspect's room found OG Kush cannabis packed in seven
balloons. [@1] The total weight of the seized drug was given as 7 kilograms.
[@2] The suspect was identified only by the initials S. S. [@1] He is an Ivorian
national with no declared employment. [@2]

The arrest fell within the police operations against drug trafficking in Côte
d'Ivoire. [@1]
