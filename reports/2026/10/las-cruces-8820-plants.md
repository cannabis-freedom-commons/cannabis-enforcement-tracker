On 25 October 2026, Guatemala's National Civil Police (PNC), through its Subdirectorate General for Anti-Narcotics Information Analysis (SGAIA), located and eradicated a cannabis crop in the mountainous area near Ixcoch and Ixmucané, in Las Cruces municipality, Petén. [@1] SGAIA said the plants were at a productive stage and that officers uprooted and incinerated 8,820 of them. [@1] The agency valued the plants at Q4,410,000. [@1]

TN23 reported the same location, count and official valuation, and said SGAIA coordinated the eradication and burning. [@2] The two reports describe the operation as part of police efforts against illegal cultivation, but neither gives a measured area for the plots or says how many separate plots were found. [@1,2] SGAIA's notice classed the work as a search, location and eradication operation against illicit crops and referred to continued nationwide operations. [@1] The reports do not identify who cultivated this planting or state whether anyone was arrested. [@1,2]
