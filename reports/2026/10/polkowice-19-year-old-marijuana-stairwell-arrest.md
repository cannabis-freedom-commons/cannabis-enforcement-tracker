Police from Chocianów, in Polkowice County, detained a 19-year-old woman after
they were sent to a report of noisy young people on a building stairwell on 30
October 2026 and found marijuana on her. [@1] Officers of the Chocianów police
station went to the building and smelled the characteristic odour of marijuana
as soon as they entered. [@1] While checking identities, officers noticed the
nervous behaviour of the 19-year-old and, during further measures, found
marijuana on her. [@1]

The woman is a resident of Polkowice County, was detained, and was taken to a
police holding cell. [@1] Detectives questioned her and presented her with a
charge of possessing intoxicating substances. [@1] The woman admitted the
offence she was charged with. [@1] The court will decide what happens to her
next. [@1]

The account was issued by the Polkowice County Police Headquarters and signed by
sub-inspector Magdalena Wiaderek. [@1] Kryminalki.pl also reported the
detention, attributing the account to the Polkowice County Police Headquarters.
[@2]
