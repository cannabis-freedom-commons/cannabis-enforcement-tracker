On the night of 29 October 2026, officers of the Grupo de Operaciones Tácticas
of the Municipal Police in Nogales, Sonora, stopped two people pushing a
motorcycle along the pavement of Avenida de los Nogales and blocking
pedestrians. [@1] The officers recovered a motorcycle reported stolen and seized
16 cigarettes containing herb with characteristics similar to marijuana, and
placed both people at the disposal of the Fiscalía de Sonora. [@1]

During the check, officers confirmed on Plataforma México that the motorcycle
was an Italika FT125TS with a theft report open since 15 October 2026, and they
seized it for investigation. [@1] The municipal report said the search also
found 16 cigarettes containing green and dry herb similar to marijuana. [@1]

The two people were taken for a medical certification and then left at the
Centro de Atención Temprana (CAT), the early-attention centre of the Fiscalía
General de Justicia del Estado de Sonora, which will decide their legal
position. [@1] The recovered motorcycle was taken to the Fiscalía's C5 impound.
[@1] The municipal communiqué names neither person and does not say whether they
are linked to the original theft. [@1] The account rests on a statement of the
Municipal Government of Nogales and notes that anyone accused is presumed
innocent until a court finds responsibility. [@1]
