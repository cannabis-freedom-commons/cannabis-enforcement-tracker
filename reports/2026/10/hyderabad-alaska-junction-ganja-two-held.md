Goshamahal Traffic Police intercepted two men on a scooter at Alaska Junction
late on Saturday night. [@1] The interception took place around 10:15pm on
October 31 during late-night vehicle checking. [@1] The two were travelling on
Honda Activa scooters and were stopped on suspicion. [@1] Traffic checks at
Alaska Junction late on Saturday night led to a ganja seizure. [@2]

During inspection of a black Honda Activa bearing a temporary registration
number, the staff found three sealed sachets and one opened sachet containing
ganja in the dickey. [@1] The two were identified as Mohd. Abubakar Ahmed, 23,
of Falaknuma, and Mohd. Rehan Khadri, 20, of Shalibanda. [@1] Police seized
ganja from the vehicle the men were using. [@1] The second account says officers
stopped two suspicious two-wheeler riders and found four ganja packets in the
dickey, three sealed and one opened. [@2] The accused and the seized ganja were
handed over to the Begum Bazar police station for further legal action. [@1]
Police registered a case under the NDPS Act. [@1] The second account also says
the accused and the ganja were handed to Begum Bazar police. [@2] It says a case
was registered under the NDPS Act and investigation is under way. [@2]

On questioning, they disclosed that they had purchased the ganja from an unknown
person at Mangal Basti in Habeeb Nagar for personal consumption. [@1]
