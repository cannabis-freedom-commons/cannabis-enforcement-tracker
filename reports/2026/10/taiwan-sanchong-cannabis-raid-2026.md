Police arrested five people after a 29 October search in Sanchong, including a
45-year-old man suspected of supplying drugs who was wanted by three judicial
authorities. [@2] The man fled to a fifth-floor rooftop and climbed down on a
rope, but police tracked him to nearby Zhongzheng South Road and arrested him.
[@2]

Police searched his premises on Dadong South Road under a warrant on 29 October,
with Security Police Corps special-task officers assisting. [@2] At the scene
and related locations, officers found the five suspects and seized etomidate
powder, vape oil and cartridges, heroin, amphetamine and cannabis. [@1]

The seizure also included mixing oil, propylene glycol, glycerin, syringes,
scales, beakers, electronic-cigarette devices and packaging bags. [@2] Police
said they suspected drug preparation, repackaging and supply, and that the case
remained under investigation. [@1]
