Wołomin criminal officers supported by Piaseczno prevention officers carried out
checks targeting offences committed by foreigners. [@2] The operation covered
premises occupied by foreigners across Wołomin County. [@1] In Nadma officers
searched a flat occupied by a 24-year-old Ukrainian citizen and detained him.
[@2] In Marki officers then searched premises occupied by a 30-year-old Turkish
citizen and detained him. [@1] At the same Marki address officers found a
61-year-old Polish citizen wanted to serve a prison sentence, detained him and
took him to prison. [@2] Both foreigners were then charged under Article 62 of
the law on counteracting drug addiction. [@1]

In the Nadma flat officers found dried marijuana weighing over 4 grams in a
cardboard box. [@2] In Marki officers found dried marijuana weighing over 14
grams in a jar standing in the centre of a chest of drawers. [@1] In the same
Marki flat officers also found two foil packets of white powder that testing
showed was methamphetamine weighing over 2 grams. [@1]

During the county checks officers detained two men found with intoxicants and
psychotropic substances. [@2] Possession of narcotics or psychotropic substances
in breach of the drug-addiction law is an offence under Article 62. [@1]
Possession carries up to 3 years in prison, or 1 to 10 years for a significant
quantity, while a smaller amount can instead bring a fine, restriction of
liberty or up to a year. [@1]
