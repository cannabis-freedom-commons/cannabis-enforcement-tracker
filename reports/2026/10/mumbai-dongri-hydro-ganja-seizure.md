The Mumbai Crime Branch's Anti-Narcotics Cell seized 3.563 kg of hydro ganja
worth about Rs 1.24 crore in the Dongri area on 28 October 2026. [@1] Two men
were spotted behaving suspiciously near Umar Khadi Cross Lane, allegedly
carrying two blue plastic bags, and a police team intercepted and searched
them. [@1]

The search recovered 1.823 kg from the first accused and 1.740 kg from the
second. [@1] Police put the market value of the haul at Rs 1,24,70,500. [@1]

The Azad Maidan unit registered a case against both men under Sections 8(c),
20(b) and 29 of the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, 1985, and
arrested them. [@1] The operation ran under the guidance of senior Mumbai
Police officers, including Police Commissioner Deven Bharti. [@1] Police said
the crackdown on the city's drug trade would continue. [@1]
