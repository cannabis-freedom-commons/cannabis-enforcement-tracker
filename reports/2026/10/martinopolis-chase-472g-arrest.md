Military police arrested a man in flagrante for drug trafficking on Friday 30
October in Martinópolis, São Paulo. [@1] Officers were patrolling Jardim Dona
Hilda when they met a car travelling in the opposite direction. [@1] On seeing
the patrol car, the driver made a sharp left turn, fled at high speed, and was
intercepted at the crossing of Rua Antônio Joaquim Senteio and Rua Osvaldo
Ferraz Caldas. [@1] The driver showed above-average nervousness and had to be
handcuffed. [@1] While being taken toward his house, he kicked the glass of the
prisoner-transport compartment in an attempt to break it and escape. [@1]

A search of the car glove box found portions of marijuana and dry. [@1] At his
residence, his wife received the officers, authorized entry, and accompanied the
sweep of the property. [@1] In the kitchen, officers found another portion of
marijuana and a precision scale with drug residue. [@1] In total, police seized
467 grams of marijuana, five grams of dry, the scale and a mobile phone. [@2]

The detainee was taken to the Civil Police station, where he remained in custody
at the disposal of the courts. [@1]
