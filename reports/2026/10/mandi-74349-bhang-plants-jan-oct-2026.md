Mandi police registered 274 cases under the NDPS Act and arrested 438 people,
418 men and 20 women, between 1 January and 31 October 2026. [@1] The district
totals included 143 charas cases and 20 illegal opium and bhang cultivation
cases, alongside 93 heroin, seven opium cases and 11 MDMA, consumption and other
cases. [@1] Superintendent of Police Vinod Kumar presented the figures at a
district Narco Coordination Centre review meeting chaired by Deputy Commissioner
Apurav Devgan in the Police Lines auditorium. [@2]

Police said they destroyed 39,705 bhang plants in September and 34,644 bhang
plants in October, a total of 74,349 plants; bhang is the local term for
cannabis. [@1] In October police seized 112.05 grams of heroin and 7.765 kg of
charas, while in September they seized 87.69 grams of heroin, 2.649 kg of
charas, 1.185 kg of opium doda and 937 grams of bhukki; charas is cannabis
resin. [@1] Police said property worth Rs 6,130,757 earned from the drug trade
was attached in six cases from 2025 to 31 October 2026, with about Rs 7,056,000
more awaiting confirmation and 70 financial-investigation cases pending in 2026.
[@1]

The second account says district police booked two NDPS cases for the October
eradication in which the 34,644 bhang plants were destroyed. [@2] Deputy
Commissioner Devgan said all departments, enforcement agencies and social
organisations must work together to make the Nashamukt Mandi-Swasth Mandi
campaign effective, with awareness activities and walkathons in educational
institutions. [@1]
