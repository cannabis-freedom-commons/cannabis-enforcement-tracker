Carabinieri from the Gandino station arrested a 50-year-old man on suspicion of
possessing narcotic substances. [@1] The operation took place on the afternoon
of Saturday 31 October in Peia during a territorial control. [@1] Officers
stopped his car at about 2:20 p.m. [@1] During the check the driver showed
evident signs of nervousness and admitted keeping about 15 grams of hashish at
his home. [@1]

Officers accompanied him to the residence where he voluntarily handed over a
packet containing a piece of hashish weighing 14.86 grams. [@1] A search of the
garage found two more pieces of hashish weighing 8.12 and 7.66 grams and a knife
with evident traces of the same substance on the blade. [@1] The garage also
held 24 marijuana seeds and three small tin boxes containing a further 3.66
grams. [@1] Officers then found a heat-sealed bag containing 70 grams of
marijuana hidden in a cavity in the wall. [@1] The total seizure was reported as
about 30 grams of hashish and 74 grams of marijuana, plus the seeds. [@1]

The Carabinieri treated the conduct as alleged unlawful production, trafficking
and possession under Article 73 of Presidential Decree 309/1990. [@1] After
informing the Bergamo Public Prosecutor's Office, the prosecutor ordered the
arrest at 3:10 p.m. on 31 October. [@1] The man was then held in the Clusone
Carabinieri Company lock-up pending a fast-track hearing before the Bergamo
court scheduled for 2 November. [@1]
