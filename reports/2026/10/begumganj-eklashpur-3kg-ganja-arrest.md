Police in Begumganj upazila of Noakhali district arrested Md. Azad Hossain, also
known as Sagar, 22, with three kilograms of ganja. [@1] He was detained during a
raid on a dwelling house in Purba Ekalshapur village, in Eklashpur union. [@1]

Police said the raid was carried out at the house of Kamuk Bepari in ward 4 of
the village. [@1] The dwelling house officers searched belonged to Anwar
Hossain. [@1] When they searched Azad Hossain's body, they recovered three
kilograms of ganja. [@1] The arrested man is the son of the late Sayedul Haque,
also known as Sadu, of Purba Ekalshapur village. [@1]

A case under the Narcotics Control Act was being filed against him at Begumganj
police station. [@1]
