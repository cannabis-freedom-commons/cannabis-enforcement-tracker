Four farmers were booked under Section 8/20 of the Narcotic Drugs and
Psychotropic Substances Act after police in Dhar district, Madhya Pradesh,
raided their farms in four police station areas late on Thursday 29 October
2026. [@1] Rajod police arrested Nana Lal Popandya at his house near his
Nipawali farm, and he was to be produced in court on Saturday. [@1] Sardarpur
police arrested Tolaram Pataliya, 58, after raiding his farm at Khamaliya
village, and he was also to be produced in court on Saturday. [@1] Ramesh
Solanki fled Kota village after learning of the raid and was absconding. [@1]
Rajaram Waskel could not be found after the Panjariya raid and was absconding.
[@1]

At Kota village, about 14 km from Gandhwani, police seized 400 plants weighing
693 kg and valued at Rs 75 lakh from Solanki’s farm. [@1,4] Sub-inspector Suraj
Singh Parmar said the plants there were grown among a cotton crop. [@1] At
Nipawali, Rajod police seized 30 plants weighing 27.5 kg and valued at Rs 30,000
from Popandya’s farm, opposite a Honda showroom. [@1,4] At Khamaliya, Sardarpur
police seized 240 plants weighing 385 kg and valued at Rs 46.2 lakh from
Pataliya’s farm. [@1,4] At Panjariya, Dharampuri police found 14 plants weighing
50.3 kg and worth Rs 6.04 lakh on Waskel’s farm. [@1,4]

Police described the Gandhwani, Rajod and Sardarpur actions as part of Operation Prahar, a district drive against illegal drug cultivation and trade. [@2] In those station areas, officers recovered cannabis plants growing among pigeon pea, known locally as tuar, and cotton. [@2]

Pradesh Prakash reported that Dhar police, under Operation Prahar, seized about 1,105 kg of illegal ganja from the Gandhwani, Sardarpur and Rajod police station areas and put its estimated value at 1 crore 24 lakh rupees. [@3] Of that haul, the outlet said 693 kg came from Gandhwani, 385 kg from Sardarpur and 27 kg from Rajod. [@3] Additional Superintendent of Police Vijay Dawar said the action followed tips from informers and the public during a 15-day special campaign against illegal narcotics. [@3] The same account said Gandhwani police arrested two accused and that a third was absconding, with teams still searching for him. [@3] Dawar said the drive against trafficking and the drug trade was continuing and that police were making special efforts to catch the remaining accused. [@3]
