Police in Charama, Kanker district, arrested two men on suspicion of selling
ganja. [@2] The arrests were made in Pandripani, a village under the Charama
police station. [@1] The men were Ramesh Pidda, 40, a resident of Pandripani,
and Kanhaiya Vishwakarma, 43, a resident of Bargari in neighbouring Dhamtari
district. [@2]

Police said they received a tip on 30 October 2026 that two men were keeping
illegal ganja for sale in the village. [@2] A team from Charama police station
and the cyber cell carried out a joint operation. [@2] The two were caught at
Pandripani during the operation. [@2] Officers recovered 1 kg of ganja from one
man and 10 packets from the other. [@1] The total haul was 1 kg 28 g. [@2]
Mobile phones were also seized. [@1] Police valued the seized ganja at about
₹51,000. [@2]

The two were accused of ganja smuggling and illegal sale. [@1] A case was
registered against both men, and they were arrested and produced in court. [@2]
