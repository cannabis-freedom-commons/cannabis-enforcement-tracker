Members of the special narcotics vigilance cell of Basavana Bagewadi police station raided a farm in Dindawar village, Vijayapura district, on 18 October 2026 after an alert that ganja was being grown there. [@1,2] The team inspected the farm under NDPS Act procedures and arrested the farmer, 56-year-old Sharanappa Jeer. [@1,2]

Officers found red gram growing around the edge of the plot and more than 500 cannabis plants in the middle. [@1,2] Police put the seized cannabis at 202.75 kg, valued by them at Rs 1,01,37,500, and registered a case. [@1,2] The district superintendent of police praised the raiding officers for the catch. [@2]
