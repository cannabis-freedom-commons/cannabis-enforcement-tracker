Officers from Narahiya police station in Bihar's Madhubani district arrested two
men after acting on secret information. [@1] The pair were arrested at a
passenger shelter beside the Bhaptiyahi Durga temple in Laukaha. [@1] Police
identified the two as Kaushal Kumar and Sanjeev Kumar. [@1]

About 2.5 grams of smack, a street name for heroin, and about 9 grams of ganja,
the local name for cannabis, were recovered from the men. [@1] An electronic
weighing scale was recovered in the same search. [@1]

A case was registered against both men, and they were sent to judicial custody.
