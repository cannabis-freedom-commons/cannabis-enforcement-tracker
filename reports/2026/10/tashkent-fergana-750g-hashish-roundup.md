Police detained a previously convicted man in Tashkent's Chilanzar district
during follow-up drug operations. [@1] Two men were detained in Fergana region
after obtaining hashish from a woman and her mother. [@1] The woman and her
mother were detained in Kokand. [@3] A heating-supply employee born in 2004 was
detained in Chirchik while receiving synthetic alpha-PVP meant for distribution
in Tashkent city and region. [@3] Criminal cases were opened over all the
episodes under point (a) of part six of Article 273 of the Criminal Code. [@3]
Investigators are continuing to identify other participants and distribution
channels. [@1]

A search of the Chilanzar man's home found hashish and heroin. [@3] Officers
stopped his supplier's Lacetti at the Chinor post in Okhangaron district and
found 250 grams of hashish hidden in the engine compartment. [@3] The Kokand
handover involved 500 grams of hashish. [@3] The Fergana buyers had paid 47
million soms for the hashish. [@2] Authorities put the combined seizure for the
multi-region operation at about one kilogram of drugs. [@1]

The detentions were made during an inquiry into a criminal group involved in
drug trafficking. [@3] The operations were carried out jointly by
internal-affairs officers with State Security and customs officers. [@2] The
part-six trafficking charge can carry up to 20 years' imprisonment. [@2]
