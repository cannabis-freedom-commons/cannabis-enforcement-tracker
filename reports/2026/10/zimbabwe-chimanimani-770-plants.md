Police in Chimanimani arrested Lovemore Zambasira, 73, and Liberty Maunganidze, 38, in
Ruwaka Village on 26 October 2026 for the alleged unlawful cultivation of dagga, the
Zimbabwe Republic Police said. [@1,2] Officers recovered processed dagga and 770 dagga
plants that were allegedly grown alongside maize crops, national police spokesperson
Commissioner Paul Nyathi said. [@1]

The two men are expected to face charges of unlawful cultivation of dagga. [@2] Nyathi
said police remained on high alert as they intensified efforts against the sale and use of
drugs. [@1] Photographs published with the police announcement showed the uprooted plants
piled beside a building and the two men with an officer. [@1]

Neither report gave the weight of the processed dagga, the size of the field, or said
whether the plants had been destroyed. [@1,2]
