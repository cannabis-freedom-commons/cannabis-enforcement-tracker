Munich police arrested a 22-year-old Munich man and his 30-year-old girlfriend
from Poland after an argument between them on the street led officers to the
man's flat in Waldperlach, Ramersdorf-Perlach, in the night to 16 October 2026.
[@1] A neighbour called the emergency number because of the loud quarrel shortly
before midnight, and officers found the 22-year-old lightly bleeding from the
nose and half undressed in the cold. [@2] Police said the two were arrested on
suspicion of drug trafficking and that arrest warrants were issued against both,
with the warrant against the woman suspended on conditions. [@4]

The search found about 1.8 kilograms of high-quality uncut cocaine, which the
investigators valued at an estimated EUR 140,000 on the street, as well as four
sharp pistols. [@1] Investigators also found a pressed block of 45 grams of
hashish in a lockable glass box, a quantity the Abendzeitung described as still
just within what German law allows for personal use. [@1]

The four pistols were three Glock handguns and one Croatian HS pistol, with
magazines for up to 30 rounds and a device that converts a semi-automatic pistol
into a fully automatic weapon. [@5] A search of the 22-year-old's car found
nothing of criminal relevance and the car was returned to him. [@2]

A judge issued arrest warrants against both on suspicion of drug trafficking;
the 22-year-old has been in pre-trial custody since, while his girlfriend was
released on conditions. [@1] The 22-year-old was already known to police for
drug offences, had no weapons licence and has not commented on the allegations,
and armed trafficking in a not-small quantity carries a minimum five-year prison
term. [@5] Munich's Kommissariat 83, the city drugs squad, took over the further
investigation. [@3]
