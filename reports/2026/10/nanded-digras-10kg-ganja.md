Nanded police's Local Crime Branch raided a farm in Digras village on Thursday 8 October 2026 and seized 10.5 kg of ganja valued at Rs 5.25 lakh. [@1,2]

The accused, Shrikrishna Ramrao Gutte, had allegedly planted the cannabis among cotton and pigeon-pea crops. [@1,2] Police registered a case against him under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act at Kandhar police station. [@1,2]

The reports said no arrest had been made and that the investigation was continuing. [@1,2]
