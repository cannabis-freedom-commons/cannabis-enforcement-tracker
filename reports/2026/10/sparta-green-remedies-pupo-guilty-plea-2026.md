Devin Pupo of Sparta pleaded guilty to illegal marijuana sales charges after a
lengthy investigation. [@2] His plea deal reduced the case to second-degree
unlawful possession of more than five but less than 20 pounds of marijuana with
intent to distribute. [@2] He faces up to 10 years in New Jersey State Prison.
[@1]

Warrants were executed on Green Remedies, Pupo's home, vehicles and another
Sparta shop, Zenshine. [@1] The searches recovered approximately 20 pounds of
raw marijuana and evidence of illegal drug distribution. [@1] Investigators
seized $477,135 in cash, a 2024 Chevrolet Suburban that held about $250,000, a
silver bar and 500 silver coins believed to be drug proceeds. [@2] Pupo admitted
the cash was proceeds of weed sales and that he used the vehicle to transport
drugs and facilitate sales. [@2]

The Sparta Police Department and the Sussex County Prosecutor's Office Guns,
Gangs and Narcotics Task Force investigated Green Remedies, the Sparta store
owned and run by Pupo. [@1] Green Remedies sold hemp-derived products but was
not licensed to sell marijuana. [@2] Prosecutor Daniel Perez said the plea was
an important step in holding accountable those behind large-scale illegal
marijuana distribution. [@2]
