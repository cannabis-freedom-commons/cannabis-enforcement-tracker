Ghana's IGP Special Operations Team arrested 192 suspects during
intelligence-led operations across parts of the country, recovering suspected
narcotic drugs, firearms, ammunition and other exhibits. [@1] The operations ran
between October 2 and 29 and covered New Town, Dansoman, Weija, Tudu, James
Town, Shukura, Okaishie, Lapaz, Chantan, Odawna, Teshie, Avenor and Fadama. [@1]
Police said the drive targeted suspected criminal activities, particularly
drug-related offences. [@1] The biggest sweep was at Teshie on October 22, when
102 suspects comprising 93 men and nine women were arrested. [@1] At Lapaz and
Chantan on October 15, 41 suspects including 35 men and six women were arrested.
[@1] At Okaishie Drug Lane on October 9, eight suspects were arrested. [@1]

At Teshie, police recovered quantities of compressed dried substances suspected
to be narcotic drugs, seven bottles of nitrous oxide and a bottle of Benylin
with Codeine. [@1] Eight suspects identified as key narcotics peddlers were
handed over to the Drug Law Enforcement Unit for further investigation. [@1] At
Lapaz and Chantan, quantities of dried leaves suspected to be narcotic
substances were recovered and retained for evidential and forensic examination.
[@1] At Okaishie, police recovered suspected narcotics and unregulated
pharmaceutical products such as Tramadol, Rohypnol and codeine-containing
medicines. [@1] At Odawna, 52-year-old businessman Richard Polo was arrested
following police surveillance. [@1] The team reportedly recovered quantities of
substances suspected to be cocaine and other narcotic drugs from his hideout.
[@1] Operations at Fadama and Shukura on October 28 and 29 led to the arrest of
two suspects identified as Douglas Kobby, 47, and Habib Yakubu. [@1] That
operation recovered four parcels of dried leaves suspected to be narcotic
substances, seven cartridges, a pump-action magazine, a dagger and a machete.
[@1] At Alabonu in Asutuare, three suspected members of a vigilante group were
arrested. [@1] In the Eastern Region, four suspects were arrested and police
recovered a pump-action shotgun, ammunition and substances suspected to be
Indian hemp, the Ghanaian term for cannabis, and Tramadol. [@2]

Police said all the suspects were in custody assisting with investigations. [@1]
Efforts were underway to identify and arrest others who may be linked to the
suspected criminal activities. [@1]
