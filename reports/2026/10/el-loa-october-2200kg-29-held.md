Carabineros' OS7 anti-drug section in El Loa province, Antofagasta region,
detained 29 people during October 2026, police said. [@1] The force said the 29
were held for drug trafficking and included Chilean, Bolivian, Venezuelan and
Colombian nationals. [@1]

The unit seized 2,200 kg of illegal drugs during the month, more than two
tonnes. [@1] According to Carabineros, the haul consisted mainly of marijuana,
coca paste, cocaine hydrochloride and ketamine. [@1] The 2,200 kg total covers
all of those drugs, not marijuana alone. [@1]

Most of the drugs were intercepted in the border area, the force said. [@2]
Officers also seized drugs during patrols, checks on passengers of
interprovincial buses and private vehicles, searches of homes and checks in
public streets. [@1] The figures cover a month of separate operations across El
Loa, not a single raid. [@1]
