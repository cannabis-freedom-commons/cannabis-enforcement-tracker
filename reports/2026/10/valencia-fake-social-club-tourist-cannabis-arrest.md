Spain's National Police arrested a 24-year-old man in València accused of
selling cannabis to foreign tourists at premises that posed as a social club.
[@1] According to the police, the venue advertised on social media and the
internet to attract visitors who wanted to buy marijuana and hashish. [@1]

The investigation began in late August, when officers of the Centro district
police station detected possible steady sale and distribution of cannabis and
its derivatives at the premises. [@1] Officers in plain clothes found that
membership cost a 25-euro fee, which gave access to consumption inside the venue
and to buying cannabis products in several formats. [@1] Police also established
that products were sold without any need to become a member, and that customers
could take the substances outside, with a bag prepared to avoid detection at
possible police checks. [@1]

The operation ended on 20 October with a search of the premises, where officers
seized more than one kilogram of substances of cannabis appearance, mainly
marijuana and hashish, already divided into numerous doses ready for sale. [@1]
Officers also found packaging and preparation material, a precision scale and
767 euros in cash, and seized 19 boxes of self-sealing bags and documents
related to the premises activity. [@1] The man was arrested after the search as
the alleged perpetrator of an offence against public health. [@1]
