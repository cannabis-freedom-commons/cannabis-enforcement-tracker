Police in Podgorica filed criminal complaints in two separate drug cases against
a sixteen-year-old and a 45-year-old identified as M.P. [@1] The teenager was
reported on suspicion of unauthorized production, possession and distribution of
narcotic drugs. [@1] M.P. was reported on suspicion of facilitating the
consumption of narcotics to the detriment of a woman. [@1]

Officers had checked two minors the previous afternoon at around 4 p.m. and
found on one of them a larger PVC bag holding 38 smaller PVC packets of
marijuana weighing about 50 grams in total. [@1] Police said they found and
seized marijuana in various packages from M.P. and from the residential building
he uses, without giving a weight for that seizure. [@1] Investigators suspect
M.P. gave marijuana to the woman to consume, after which she became ill and
sought medical help at the Clinical Center of Montenegro. [@1]

After gathering information and carrying out police activities, officers
informed the Higher State Prosecutor's Office in Podgorica about the minor's
case, which directed that a criminal complaint be filed. [@2] In M.P.'s case,
officers informed prosecutors at both the Higher and Basic State Prosecutor's
Offices in Podgorica, and the Basic State Prosecutor directed that a criminal
complaint be filed against him. [@2]
