Civil Lines police in Moradabad arrested the suspect near the trisection by the
football ground in Railway Harthala Colony on Saturday 31 October 2026. [@1]
Police identified him as Maujjam, son of Babu Khan, a resident of Tajpur Mafi
under Katghar police station. [@2] The case began with a complaint on 26 October
from a woman living in the Majhola police area. [@1] She said a bike-borne man
had snatched the earrings from her ears near the Kapoor Company bridge and fled.
[@1] Police said he admitted his involvement in the snatching. [@1,3]

Officers recovered 1.112 kg of charas, the looted earrings and other jewellery,
an Apache motorcycle and ₹1,600 in cash from him. [@1,3] He told officers he
bought and sold charas and had gone out on the night between Friday and Saturday
to sell more than a kilogram. [@2] Police said he admitted stealing the Apache
motorcycle near Rampur jail on 23 October. [@1] They said he used that
motorcycle in the earring snatching. [@1,3]

SP City Ranvijay Singh announced the arrest at a press conference at the police
lines. [@1] Police said theft, robbery and NDPS cases were already registered
against him at police stations in Moradabad and Rampur. [@1] Civil Lines police
added section 317(2) of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita to an existing case and sent
him to jail, Young Bharat News reported. [@2] Police said they were still
examining his criminal history and his role in other incidents. [@1]

The woman was Manju, a resident of Ekta Colony in Majhola's Mandi Samiti area, and the snatching happened around 4 pm on 26 October. [@3] Police said they reviewed about 100 CCTV recordings and established that the snatching had been carried out on a stolen motorcycle. [@3] Six cases were registered against him at the Civil Lines police stations in Rampur and Moradabad and in Kanth and Chhajlet. [@3]
