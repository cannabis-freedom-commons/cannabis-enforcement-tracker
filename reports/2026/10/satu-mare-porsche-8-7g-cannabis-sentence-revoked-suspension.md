The Satu Mare Tribunal convicted a young man at first instance on 16 October
2026 and sentenced him to two months in prison for possessing risk drugs for his
own consumption. [@1]

Road police stopped a car carrying three people in the municipality of Satu Mare
in the night of 6 to 7 March 2024. [@1] Officers found 8.7 grams of cannabis in
the car, later confirmed by the laboratory, and a grinding device with traces of
THC. [@1] The passenger on the back seat, the accused, acknowledged that the
drugs were his and said he had bought them for his own use. [@1] Police stopped
a Porsche Cayenne on Cloșca Boulevard in Satu Mare and found 8.7 grams of
cannabis in the car. [@3] The man told investigators he had bought 10 grams of
cannabis for 500 lei from a person living in Satu Mare and had already used
about a gram before the stop. [@2]

The DIICOT territorial office in Satu Mare investigated the case and sent it to
court in May 2026. [@1] The young man pleaded guilty in full and asked for the
simplified procedure, in which the accused admits the facts in the indictment
and the sentencing limits are cut by a third. [@1] He had already received a
suspended sentence of one year and six months under supervision, for aggravated
theft, by a Satu Mare court judgment that became final on 8 July 2026. [@1] The
drug offence was committed before that judgment became final, so the court
treated the two offences as concurrent and revoked the suspension. [@1]

The court added a one-third increase, 20 days, to the longer sentence, producing
a combined term of one year, six months and 20 days in prison. [@1] The court
then suspended execution of the combined term under supervision for two years.
