In coordination with the Ministerio Público, the Agencia Estatal de
Investigación (AEI), the state investigation agency, executed 437 arrest
warrants in the Zona Norte during October. [@1] The Fiscalía General del Estado
(FGE), the state prosecutor, also reported 111 people detained in flagrancia in
the same zone. [@1] Search actions in the same period located 54 people who had
absence reports. [@2]

The AEI seized more than 69 kilos of drugs in the Zona Norte during October,
according to the FGE balance. [@1] Marijuana made up most of the reported
weight, at 60.184 kilos. [@1] The prosecutor's breakdown also lists 5.870 kilos
of fentanyl, 1.639 of cocaine and 1.372 of crystal. [@1] Among 16 firearms
seized in October, the prosecutor counted 14 short guns and two long guns, plus
15 magazines and 106 cartridges. [@1] The FGE balance records 141 investigation
operations and 74 executed search orders, with 29 vehicles recovered and 65
seized during the month. [@1]

The results came from permanent investigation work against crime and impunity by
the AEI in the north zone during October. [@2] The FGE says results like these
reaffirm its commitment to act to safeguard and protect the public. [@2]
