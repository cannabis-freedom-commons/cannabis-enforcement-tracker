Police in Corrientes reported a search and seizure in barrio Esperanza, the
capital of Corrientes Province. [@1] The search took place at a residence on M.
Chervin Street as part of a judicial investigation. [@1] One woman was detained
during the operation. [@1]

During the search, officers seized two groups of packets: 19 containing a white
powder and five containing a substance of plant origin. [@1] Tests on the
substances produced positive results for cocaine and marijuana, respectively.
[@1] The tests were orientative, or preliminary, and were conducted at the
scene. [@1] The five packets of plant-origin material were the ones that tested
positive for marijuana, while the 19 white-powder packets tested positive for
cocaine. [@1]

Officers also seized cash, six mobile phones of different makes and models, and
one DVR device. [@1] Police placed the detained woman at the disposal of the
justice system. [@1] They transferred all seized items to the relevant police
station so investigative steps in the case could continue. [@1]
