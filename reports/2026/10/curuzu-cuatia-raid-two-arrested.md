Officers of the Criminal Investigation Division of the Corrientes police
searched a house in Curuzú Cuatiá on the afternoon of 30 October 2026. [@2,3] The
raid took place around 18:00 at General Ramírez 200. [@2,3] Officers acted under a
judicial order for search, seizure and arrest. [@2] Inside, they arrested a
39-year-old man and a 52-year-old woman, both from Corrientes. [@2,3] The
Corrientes police announced the operation on Saturday 31 October. [@1]

Officers found doses of a white substance and a plant substance. [@1,3]
Preliminary tests were positive for cocaine and marijuana, respectively. [@1]
They also seized cash, four mobile phones of different makes and models and a
precision scale. [@1,3] A Zanella 110 cc motorcycle was taken as well. [@1]

The two arrested people and the seized items were placed at the disposal of the
judicial authority. [@1,3] Police said they were continuing with the corresponding
proceedings at the police station. [@1] The search was carried out as part of a
judicial investigation. [@2]
