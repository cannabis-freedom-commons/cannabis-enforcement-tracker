## The raid

On Sunday 25 October 2026, NDLEA officers acting on credible intelligence raided cannabis farms in the Ijare forest in Ondo State. [@1,2,3,4,5] Officers arrested four suspects, three of them named as Bassey Okon, 24, Abraham Edet, 20, and Oto Udo, 24. [@1,4,6] The search recovered 16 bags of processed cannabis weighing 180 kg from the farms. [@1,2,3,4,6]

## The announcement

The raid was published on 1 November 2026 in the NDLEA's nationwide operations roundup issued by its Director of Media and Advocacy, Femi Babafemi. [@1,2] The roundup places the raid among several anti-cannabis operations across the country that week. [@1] The Telegraph's account repeats the agency's version of the Ijare raid. [@2] Neither account gives the size of the farms or a count of the growing plants, and neither says whether the four suspects were charged. [@1,2]
