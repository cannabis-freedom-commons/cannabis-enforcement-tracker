The court of the Special Judge in Sarkaghat, Mandi, rejected the regular bail
applications of two men accused in a 500 g charas case. [@1] Police had stopped
a car at a checkpoint near the 33 kV Bhararu substation on 28 September 2026 and
found 500 g of charas in a black bag on the dashboard. [@1] Both were arrested
and a case was registered against them at Jogindernagar police station under
sections 20, 25 and 29 of the NDPS Act. [@2] Police said the men admitted during
questioning that they had made the charas themselves and were looking for
customers to sell it. [@1] The court cited their old criminal record and the
seriousness of the charges when it rejected bail. [@1]

At the hearing it emerged that Lal Singh had already been sentenced to three
years and one month in another NDPS case on 29 March 2025. [@1] The conviction
was recorded in his bail application, but the prosecution's reply described the
first information report in that case as pending. [@1] The court questioned
whether the lapse was accidental or deliberate. [@1] It directed the
Superintendent of Police to investigate the conduct of the police officer
concerned and to take action under the rules if needed. [@1] The Superintendent
of Police must submit an action report to the court within 30 days of receiving
the reference. [@1]

The two were produced in court on 29 September, and police said they were
investigating where the charas came from and where it was to be delivered. [@2]
Superintendent of Police Vinod Kumar confirmed the arrests and said the
investigation was continuing. [@2]
