A Walton County jury convicted 17-year-old Artis Johnson of felony murder,
aggravated assault, possession of marijuana with intent to distribute, tampering
with evidence and gun charges. [@1] The conviction follows a dispute over a
one-dollar bet in a dice game that ended in the shooting death of Johnson's
15-year-old relative, Cordarius Early. [@3] The verdict was returned on 26
October 2026, more than two and a half years after the killing. [@3]

According to the Walton County District Attorney's Office, Johnson and
15-year-old Cordarius Early were walking with a group of friends along North
Madison Avenue when the shooting happened at about 3:30 p.m. on 3 February 2024.
[@2] Prosecutors said Johnson shot Early six times, killing him. [@3] Johnson
ran off from police and discarded the gun, his clothes and marijuana, but was
caught a short time later. [@1]

Johnson remains in custody awaiting sentencing, with a hearing set for 11
January 2027. [@3] Published accounts differ on the precise list of charges the
jury decided. [@3]
