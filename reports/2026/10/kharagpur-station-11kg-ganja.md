Railway police arrested a 42-year-old man during a special checking drive at
Kharagpur railway station. [@2] Police identified him as Sanjeev Rai, a resident
of Begusarai in Bihar. [@1] On Friday night, officers stopped Rai on suspicion
on platform six and searched his bag in the presence of a government official.
[@1] The search found 11.460 kg of suspected ganja in three packets inside his
bag. [@1] Rai told investigators he had brought the ganja from Cuttack railway
station and planned to take it to Bihar in exchange for money. [@1]

Police registered case No. 164/26 at the Kharagpur GRPS police station on 31
October 2026. [@1] The case cites Section 20(b)(ii)(B) of the Narcotic Drugs and
Psychotropic Substances Act, India's drug law. [@2]

Police said they would produce Rai in the NDPS court on Saturday and ask for 10
days of police custody to investigate the wider smuggling network. [@1] They
said they were also checking who else was involved and how far the ganja supply
and distribution network reached. [@1]
