Federal Police officers arrested a woman in flagrante (caught in the act) as she
disembarked at Presidente Castro Pinto International Airport in João Pessoa,
Paraíba, carrying about 3.3 kg of cannabis. [@1] The drug was packed in six
tablets, hidden in her hand luggage and fixed to her body. [@2] Officers found
it during a routine inspection at the airport aimed at interstate trafficking.
[@2] She had flown from Manaus, in Amazonas, with a stopover in Rio de Janeiro
before reaching the Paraíba capital. [@1]

Police took her to the Federal Police's regional superintendency in João Pessoa,
where the flagrant arrest was recorded. [@2] She was then sent to the state
prison system, where she remains at the disposal of the courts. [@1] The case
was registered as drug trafficking and will be investigated by the competent
authorities. [@1]

The Federal Police did not name the woman or give other details of the
investigation. [@1] The force also did not say whether it was investigating
where the cannabis came from or where it was going, or give the exact date of
the arrest. [@3]
