The Yamaguchi District Court sentenced a 31-year-old man on 30 October 2026 to
three years in prison and a fine of 500,000 yen for confining two men in
Shimonoseki and for possessing cannabis for profit, among other offences. [@1]
Prosecutors had sought five years in prison and the same 500,000-yen fine, and
the court imposed a prison term it did not suspend. [@1] He was convicted of
arrest and confinement and of violating the Narcotics and Psychotropics Control
Law. [@1] In December 2024 he conspired with another man to seat two men, then
19, in chairs in a flat in Shimonoseki and tie them with rope so they could not
escape. [@1] Police arrested three men over that confinement on 2 March 2026,
and the two victims had freed themselves on 29 December 2024 and reported it to
police two days later. [@2] Police said the episode began on 28-29 December 2024
in Hiroshima City, where the victims were put in a car, one was punched in the
face repeatedly and both were blindfolded before being tied up at the
Shimonoseki flat. [@2]

In October 2025 he held about 172 grams of cannabis for profit in a flat in
Shimonoseki, and late that month he used cannabis in the city. [@1] Presiding
judge Yasuyuki Mizota called the confinement malicious, saying the victims were
blindfolded, bound to chairs with cable ties and touched with a stun gun, and
said the cannabis carried a high risk of spreading the harm of illegal drugs
through society. [@1]
