Akwa Ibom police arrested Gideon David at about 9.30 p.m. on 14 October 2026 during a raid on suspected drug sales along Aka Itiam Street in Uyo. [@1,2] Operatives moved in on intelligence about the sale of illicit drugs at the spot. [@1,2] A search of David recovered a large quantity of weeds suspected to be Indian hemp. [@1,2]

Police spokesman DSP Timfon John disclosed the arrest in a 16 October press statement for Commissioner CP Baba Mohammed Azare. [@1,2] David stayed in custody while investigators worked to establish his role and find others connected to the sales. [@2] Earlier the same day, detectives separately arrested Godwin Paulinus over cable wires stolen from a firm at Udo Udoma Avenue near Tropicana. [@1,2]
