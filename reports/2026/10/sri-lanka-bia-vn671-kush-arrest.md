Officers of Sri Lanka Customs' Narcotics Control Unit arrested a 24-year-old
Indian national at Bandaranaike International Airport at about 1.15 a.m. on 26
October. [@1,2,3] The passenger, described as a mobile phone repair technician,
had arrived from Ho Chi Minh City on Vietnam Airlines flight VN671 and was
trying to exit through the Green Channel of the arrival terminal when officers
noticed his suspicious behaviour. [@1,2,3]

His luggage was scanned, and after suspicious items showed up, officers opened
it and found seven sealed polythene packets concealed among clothing that
proved to hold 5.1 kg of kush cannabis with an estimated market value above
Rs 51 million. [@1,2,3] Customs handed the suspect, the drugs and the other
evidence to the Police Narcotic Bureau for investigation and legal
proceedings. [@1,2,3]
