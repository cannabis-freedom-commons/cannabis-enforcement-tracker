Libya's General Department for Combating Drugs and Psychotropic Substances said
its Benghazi branch arrested a Libyan man on 31 October 2026. [@2] About 16
kilograms of hashish were found with him, the branch said. [@2] The man was
identified only by the initials M. A. L. H., born in 1963. [@1] His home is in
the Shuhada al-Zawiya neighbourhood of Benghazi, the branch said. [@2] He was
unemployed, according to the branch. [@1] It said he was suspected of dealing
drugs in the city. [@2]

The branch said its detectives had been assigned to open an evidence file to
check the information it had received about his activity before they set the
ambush. [@1] Officers caught him in the act of trafficking hashish, it said.
[@1] The branch said it took the legal steps required in the case and notified
the Public Prosecution. [@1]
