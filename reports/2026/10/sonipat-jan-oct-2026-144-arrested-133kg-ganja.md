Sonipat district police reported that in 2026 up to 31 October they had
registered 81 drug-trafficking cases and arrested 144 accused under a special
campaign against the drug trade. [@1] Recoveries from the arrested accused
included opium, charas, ganja, heroin, smack and cocaine, as well as medicines
used to get high. [@1]

Ganja was the largest single recovery, at 133 kg 646 g 99 mg (133.647 kg). [@1]
The same police figures list 24 kg 566 g 72 mg of charas among the recoveries.
[@1] The same tally lists 2 kg 500 g of opium, 75 g 36 mg of smack, 11 kg 94 g
of chura post (crushed poppy husk), 2 kg 918 g 5 mg of heroin, 34 g 43 mg of
MDMA and 84 g 86 mg of cocaine. [@1] The recoveries also included 21 sulfa-type
pills, 80 bottles of syrup and 490 tablets. [@1] Police said they were taking
legal action against the accused in these cases as the rules require. [@1]

Police Commissioner Mamta Singh said the district follows a zero-tolerance
policy against drug trafficking. [@1]

Mamta Singh asked residents to report drug trafficking and said informants'
identities would be kept confidential. [@1]
