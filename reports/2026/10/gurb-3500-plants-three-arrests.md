Spain's National Police ended its operation against an indoor marijuana grow in
Gurb with three men detained, two of whom have entered prison. [@1,2] Police
explained that two of the three detained men worked as gardeners responsible for
daily care, maintenance and control of the plantation. [@1,2] Investigators later
located and detained in Tordera a third man described as the alleged person
responsible for the plantation and its surveillance and control. [@1,2]

On 28 October officers dismantled the indoor marijuana plantation inside a house
in Gurb, in Osona, seizing more than 3,500 plants. [@1] Police valued the seized
drug at 406,311 euros on the illicit market, according to police sources cited
in the report. [@1,2] Police added that profits could have reached about 1,500,000
euros if the drug had been distributed in small quantities. [@1,2]

The Gurb dismantling was reported as one of two distinct police operations that
took down two active indoor plantations in Catalonia. [@1] The Gurb site was an
active indoor plantation housed in a residential dwelling in Osona. [@1]

The investigation began in early June after officers detected a strong smell of marijuana around a large building in the town, and the raid was carried out on Wednesday 28 October. [@2] The power company estimated the electricity fraud at about 99,000 euros after removing the illegal connection. [@2] Those responsible used false documentation to rent the property. [@2] Two of the detainees entered provisional prison while the third was released with precautionary measures. [@2]
