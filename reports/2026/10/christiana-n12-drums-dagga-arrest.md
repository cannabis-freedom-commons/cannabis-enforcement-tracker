A 21-year-old suspect was arrested near Christiana for dealing in dagga, the
South African term for cannabis, after police seized 4.654 kg of compressed
dagga on 28 October 2026. [@1] The North West police spokesperson, Captain
Majang Skalkie, said the arrest followed a tip-off about a person allegedly in
possession of a large quantity of dagga. [@1]

Police followed up the information and located the suspect, Lusanda Nhlangothi,
21, along the N12 road close to Christiana. [@1] Searching him, officers found
two 20-litre drums in his possession; inside the drums were several packages
that proved on inspection to hold compressed dagga. [@1] The dagga was
confiscated and Nhlangothi was arrested and charged with possession of dagga.
[@1]

Nhlangothi appeared briefly before the Christiana Magistrate's Court on 29
October 2026, and the case was postponed to 5 November 2026 for a bail
application while he was remanded in custody. [@1] The North West police
commissioner, Lieutenant General Arthur Adams, commended the police for acting
on the information and preventing the suspected illegal distribution of drugs.
[@1]
