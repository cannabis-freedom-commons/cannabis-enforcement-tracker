Customs officers stopped a man at Nuuk Airport at midday on Tuesday 13 October 2026 and found
5.4 kilograms of hash hidden in his suitcase, Greenland police said. [@1,2] He had just arrived
from Denmark. [@2]

The man was arrested and was to be brought before the district court at 11:00 the next day,
Wednesday 14 October, charged with smuggling hash. [@1,2] The police gave no further details of
the case. [@1]
