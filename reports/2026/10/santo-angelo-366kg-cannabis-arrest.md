The Military Brigade arrested a man in Santo Ângelo on suspicion of drug
trafficking. [@1] The arrest happened on the afternoon of Thursday, 29 October,
in Santo Ângelo in the Missões region. [@3] The operation is attributed to the
27th Military Police Battalion. [@1] The detained man and all the seized
material were taken to the Delegacia de Polícia de Pronto Atendimento in Santo
Ângelo. [@3] There the case was registered and the applicable legal procedures
were carried out. [@1]

Officers seized four bricks of marijuana weighing 3.631 kg. [@1] They also
seized two smaller portions of the same drug with a total weight of 31 grams.
[@2] Together the reported marijuana amounts to about 3.662 kg of cannabis. [@1]
In the same action officers seized 503 grams of cocaine and 511 grams of crack.
[@1] Officers also seized a mobile phone and a precision scale. [@2]
