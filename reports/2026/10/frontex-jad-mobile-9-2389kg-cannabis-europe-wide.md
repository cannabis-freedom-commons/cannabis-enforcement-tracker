In operation Joint Action Days (JAD) Mobile 9, led by Frontex across 26
countries with INTERPOL support, more than 7.4 tonnes of cocaine and 2,389 kg of
cannabis were seized. [@1] The 2,389 kg of cannabis was listed among the
operation's seizure totals alongside 7,455 kg of cocaine, 44,600 benzodiazepine
pills and 10.6 million cigarettes. [@2]

Officers carried out checks at border crossing points and ports, and 334 people
were arrested across the operation as a whole. [@1] Frontex hosted the
coordination centre at its headquarters in Warsaw from 28 September to 9 October
2026. [@1]

France acted as the operational leader for JAD Mobile 9, while Frontex was the
Action Leader and coordinator under EMPACT. [@1]
