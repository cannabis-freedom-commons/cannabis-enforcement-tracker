Four people are suspects in the investigation. [@1] Hassan Mohamed Soueid and
Casey Anne Smith were arrested and face multiple felony charges, including money
laundering, fraud and drug crimes. [@1] Smith, the company’s former chief
financial officer, was charged with money laundering, organized fraud and
selling a controlled substance after a two-year investigation. [@3] Smith, 41,
of Boynton Beach, was held on more than $20 million bond. [@3] Hassan Mohamed
Soueid, 33, of Pompano Beach, was booked into the county’s Main Detention Center
at 10:01 p.m. Oct. 28 on 11 felony counts. [@4] Soueid faces charges including
money laundering, conspiracy to commit money laundering, conspiracy to commit
organized fraud and selling and conspiring to sell a controlled substance. [@2]
Soueid’s bond was set at $1,080,000. [@2]

The company was said to have distributed highly concentrated marijuana products
and laundered some $33 million. [@1] Investigators said illegal proceeds paid
for about $2.7 million across several Florida properties. [@1] That included a
$1.2 million Boynton Beach home and a $1 million condo unit in Pompano Beach.
[@1] One listed property is 4950 Glenn Pine Lane in Boynton Beach at $1.2
million. [@1] Another is 1300 South Ocean Boulevard #702 in Pompano Beach at $1
million. [@1] The group used a series of front companies to launder money and
create the appearance of a legitimate business. [@1]

Investigators allege the company marketed hemp-derived products as legal
alternatives while selling products with intoxicating levels of THC. [@2]
Detectives say the products were sold as legal hemp but were not. [@3]
Investigators allege the company sold vapes, concentrates and other products
marketed as legal hemp that contained THC above the state limit. [@4] Six items
bought undercover from the company’s website in February 2025 all tested above
1% delta-9 THC. [@3] The affidavit lists business locations in Delray Beach,
Oklahoma and Texas and says the company repackaged hemp and THC products under
its own brand before distributing them. [@2] The affidavit also lists Michael
Carey Ives, 33, and Jonathan Enaldo Zelaya, 31, as co-defendants. [@4]
