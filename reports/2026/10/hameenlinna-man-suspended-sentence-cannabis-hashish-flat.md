Kanta-Häme District Court convicted a 19-year-old man of assault and five other
offences and gave him a joint suspended sentence of eight months. [@1] The six
offences were assault, a drug-use offence, possession of an object suitable for
injuring another person, a minor firearms offence, possession of a dangerous
object and a drug offence. [@1]

The drug offence came from a search of the man's flat in February, when police
seized 37.6 grams of cannabis and 130 grams of hashish. [@1] The court found the
cannabis had been divided into smaller bags for possible distribution. [@1] The
man admitted the possession offences and written evidence supported his
admissions. [@1] The probation period ends on 13 October 2028, and he was placed
under supervision for one year and three months. [@1] The court also ordered him
to pay a 120-euro victim surcharge. [@1]

The assault charge concerned 16 kicks to a person lying on the ground in the
Hämeenlinna railway station tunnel, recorded by a surveillance camera. [@1]
According to the prosecutor, the defendant struck the complainant three times in
the head or face and knocked him down. [@1] The complainant suffered bloody
scratches and broken skin on his head and face but left the scene on his own.
