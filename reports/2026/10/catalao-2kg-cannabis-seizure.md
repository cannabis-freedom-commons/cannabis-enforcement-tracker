Military police took a woman to the civil police station in Catalão, Goiás, on
the night of Friday 30 October 2026, during an operation against drug
trafficking by the Military Police's Specialised Policing Company (CPE). [@1]
The CPE Comando team was on tactical patrol when it approached a man who tried
to flee as the patrol car came near. [@2] A personal search found a portion of a
substance similar to cannabis, and the man said he was going to deliver it to
another person. [@1]

With the express authorisation of the woman who lived there, officers searched a
house, where they found cannabis portions prepared for sale, about one and a
half bars of the same substance and a precision scale. [@2] The Military Police
said the inquiries indicated a division of tasks between the couple: the woman
would store the drugs and the man would distribute and deliver them. [@1]

In all, the police seized about 2 kg of cannabis, nine portions of ecstasy, a
precision scale and a car. [@1] The woman was taken to the police station with
the seized material and the vehicle, which police said was used for deliveries.
[@1] The case was presented to the Civil Police for legal measures. [@2] The
case involves suspected drug trafficking and association for trafficking. [@2]
The man described as the second suspect fled and was not located. [@1]
