Prosecutors in Tula's Privokzalny district sent a criminal case against a
19-year-old local resident to court on charges of attempted drug sale and
illegal drug storage. [@1] He faces attempted unlawful sale of drugs by a group
with prior agreement using the internet on a large scale, and unlawful
acquisition and storage in a significant amount. [@2] The case was sent to the
Privokzalny District Court of Tula for consideration on the merits. [@1]

Investigators say that from July to August 2026 the young man conspired online
with another person, collected a wholesale batch of synthetic drug from a cache,
repacked it and placed it in dead-drop caches. [@1] Regional police officers
stopped him before he could carry the plan through. [@1] Police seized 5.53
grams of mephedrone from illicit trafficking in the operation. [@1] A search of
his home found five packets containing at least 10 grams of hashish, the
cannabis component recorded here. [@1]

The second account describes the hashish as five transparent polymer packets
found during the home search. [@2] No trial outcome has been reported yet, with
the court stage still to come. [@1]
