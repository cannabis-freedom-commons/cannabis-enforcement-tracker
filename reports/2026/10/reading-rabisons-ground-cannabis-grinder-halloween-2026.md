A group set off fireworks at Rabsons Recreation Ground in Reading on Halloween
night, 31 October 2026, while officers from the Reading South East and Town
Centre Neighbourhood Teams patrolled the area. [@2] The group ran off when it
saw the officers, leaving behind a selection of fireworks, a cannabis grinder
and a pair of AirPods. [@1]

No one was arrested or detained over the grinder, and no weight of cannabis,
charge or court step over it was reported. [@1]

In a separate incident on the same night, officers arrested a woman wanted on
recall to prison who tried to cycle away along Northumberland Avenue. [@2]
