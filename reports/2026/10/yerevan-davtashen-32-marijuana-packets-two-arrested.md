On 1 October 2026 at about 10:30, Davtashen community-police officers detained
two young foreign citizens in Davtashen. [@1] The two lived in different guest
houses in Yerevan. [@1] Police said the two were detained in Davtashen's 2nd
quarter on suspicion of selling drugs through dead-drop caches. [@2]

A search of one detainee, a 22-year-old foreign citizen, found 32 packages
wrapped in adhesive tape. [@1] The detainee said the packages contained
marijuana with a total weight of about 73 grams. [@1] A second outlet likewise
reported 32 tape-wrapped packages said by the detainee to contain marijuana.
[@2] Police said the young men had bought the marijuana packages from a Telegram
user the day before in order to sell them for money. [@1]

One detainee said he had already placed several caches and was willing to show
where they were. [@1] Within the opened criminal proceedings, officers recovered
three such placed packages. [@1] The second outlet likewise said the young man
reported having already placed several packages and being ready to show where.
[@2] Both suspects were placed in custody, and the preliminary investigation is
continuing. [@1]
