Two men from Fort Worth, Texas, were arrested and booked into the Love County
Jail in Oklahoma and charged with trafficking more than 25 pounds of marijuana.
[@1] They were taken into custody early on Tuesday 27 October 2026. [@1] The two
were 29-year-old Jose Pedro Saez and 26-year-old Jason Noel Villalovos. [@1]

More than 25 pounds, the threshold of the trafficking charge, is about 11.3 kg;
no weight of marijuana seized was reported. [@1] Jail booking records for Love
County, where the local sheriff runs the jail, list both men as residents of
Fort Worth, Texas, and record both as booked at 12:39 a.m. on 27 October 2026.
[@2] The booking records show both men held under the charge of trafficking
marihuana of 25 pounds or more. [@3]

Saez's booking record also lists a charge of using a firearm while committing a
felony. [@2] The reports do not say where officers stopped the men or found the
marijuana, what vehicle or container carried it, or whether any of it was seized
for evidence. [@1]
