Judicial police in the city of Tiznit arrested a man in his thirties on Saturday
31 October 2026 on suspicion of drug trafficking in Arbaa Sahel commune and the
surrounding areas. [@1] The detainee was already the subject of several national
search warrants issued by the Royal Gendarmerie in the Souss-Massa and
Guelmim-Oued Noun regions. [@1] Officers stopped him in Tiznit and, on searching
him, found a significant quantity of chira, the Moroccan term for cannabis
resin, which he was suspected of planning to carry to Arbaa Sahel for
redistribution to local dealers. [@1]

The competent public prosecution ordered him held in police custody while
investigators work to uncover the other possible accomplices and the extent of
the suspected network. [@1] Police describe him as one of the most prominent
distributors operating for years along the Tiznit coast, centred on Arbaa Sahel,
and report that he has numerous prior convictions. [@2]

The custody investigation, supervised by the prosecution, is intended to
identify his remaining partners in the long-running supply of drugs to the
coastal areas. [@2] The seized chira was being carried toward Arbaa Sahel for
redistribution to a group of dealers in the area. [@2]
