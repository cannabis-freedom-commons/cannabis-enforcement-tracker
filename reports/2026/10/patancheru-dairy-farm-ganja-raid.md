EAGLE Force and Patancheru police raided Govind Rao Dairy Farm at Kardanoor village in
Patancheru mandal on Saturday and caught two alleged peddlers selling ganja to local labourers,
along with four consumers. [@1] Officers seized 1.15 kg of ganja, and a case was registered under
the NDPS Act at Patancheru police station in Sangareddy. [@1]

The two men are Sanchit Roy, 50, a dairy worker from Bihar's Patna district, and Mahesh Kumar,
32, a security guard at a parking yard opposite the farm who assisted him. [@1] Police say Roy
bought ganja at Rs 20,000 per kg from Kishan Singh of Dhoolpet, packed it into 5 g packets and
sold each for Rs 300, serving 20 to 30 buyers a day from Bihar, Assam, West Bengal and Uttar
Pradesh. [@1] The report says owner Govind Rao visits the farm only for a few hours in the early
morning. [@1] Kishan Singh is absconding, and police say about ten more consumers were
identified. [@1]
