The Rome Floyd Metro Drug Task Force arrested Tineka Nicole Foster, 30, and
Devonier Marques Bowers, 35, both of Rome, during a search of a home on Betty
Kay Avenue. [@1]

Police said officers found marijuana, mushrooms and multiple digital scales that
contained drug residue inside the home. [@1] Officers also said they found
Bowers flushing the marijuana down the toilet during the search. [@1] The
reported items at the residence thus comprised marijuana, mushrooms and digital
scales bearing drug residue, rather than cannabis alone. [@1]

Both Foster and Bowers were charged with possession of marijuana, possession of
a Schedule I controlled substance, and possession and use of drug-related
objects. [@1] The three shared charges covered cannabis possession, another
controlled-substance possession allegation and possession and use of
drug-related objects. [@1] Bowers was additionally charged with tampering with
evidence and contempt of court. [@1] These extra charges applied only to Bowers,
while Foster and Bowers both faced the three shared drug-related charges. [@1]
