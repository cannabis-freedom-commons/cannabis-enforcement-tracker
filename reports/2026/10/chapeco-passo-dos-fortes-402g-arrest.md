Military Police in Santa Catarina arrested two men, aged 22 and 24, on suspicion
of drug trafficking and criminal association in the Passo dos Fortes
neighbourhood of Chapecó, in western Santa Catarina, on the night of 28 October
2026. [@2] The action followed reports that a blue Fiat Palio was being used to
sell drugs near Rua Inglaterra. [@2] Officers found the car at the end of Rua
Franz Wallerius and approached its two occupants. [@2]

At the 24-year-old's home, police found 272 g of cannabis in a backpack, divided
into 21 portions ready for sale, a precision scale, cling film for packaging and
R$92. [@2] Nothing illicit was found at the younger man's home. [@2]

In total, police seized about 402 g of cannabis, R$122 in cash, two mobile
phones and materials used to divide and package the drug. [@2] The two men were
taken to the Civil Police station with the car and the seized items. [@1]
