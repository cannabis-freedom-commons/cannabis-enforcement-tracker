On Saturday, 31 October 2026, Border Guard Bangladesh (BGB) seized an ownerless
cache of drugs in a special operation at Bilgathua field on the Daulatpur border
in Kushtia district. [@2] The 47th BGB Battalion's Joypur Border Outpost (BOP)
carried out the search in its area of responsibility at about 3 a.m. [@1] A BGB
patrol team acted on specific and reliable intelligence before recovering the
cache. [@2] The seizure comprised 8 kg of cannabis and 175 bottles of Indian
syrup. [@2]

BGB estimated the total value of the recovered drugs at 98,000 taka. [@2] The
47th Battalion said it would continue regular operations and intelligence
surveillance along the border to prevent drug trafficking and smuggling. [@1]
The battalion said these efforts aimed to strengthen border security and
establish a border free of drugs and smuggling. [@2] BGB announced the recovery
in a press release that Saturday afternoon, several hours after the
early-morning search. [@1] The force described its position against drug
trafficking and smuggling as uncompromising. [@2]
