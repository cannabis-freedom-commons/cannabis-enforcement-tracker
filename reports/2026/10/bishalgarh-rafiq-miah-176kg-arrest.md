Police arrested Bishalgarh resident Rafiq Miah in connection with the
ganja-recovery case registered at Bodhjungnagar police station. [@1] Officers
detained him during an evening operation at Koraimura Bazar in Bishalgarh
conducted under the leadership of Bishalgarh station officer-in-charge Babul
Das. [@1] Investigating officer Sub-Inspector Pintu Biswas said the arrest came
through investigation of that case and that police are examining Miah's link to
the incident. [@1]

Police said that on 3 September 2026 they acted on secret information and raided
the Nabachandra Choumuhani area of R.K. Nagar under Bodhjungnagar police
station. [@1] During that raid officers recovered a total of 176 kg of ganja, or
cannabis, from a Desire car bearing registration number TR-07-C-0772. [@1]
Police arrested the car's driver, Parimal Debnath, at the scene. [@1]

Bodhjungnagar police registered the seizure as case number 52/2026 under
Sections 20(b)(ii)(C), 25 and 29 of the NDPS Act. [@1]
