Military Police arrested a man in flagrante for drug trafficking on the night of
Saturday 31 October 2026 during Operação Feriado in São Carlos. [@1] A Tático
Comando patrol spotted his Citroën C3 on Rua Dr. Álvaro Câmara in Jardim Monte
Carlo after he appeared to discard an object onto the floor of the car. [@1]
During the stop officers found a plastic bag of marijuana on the floor behind
the passenger seat. [@1] The man's partner insulted the officers and was
arrested for contempt, and the couple were taken to the Central de Polícia
Judiciária (CPJ). [@1]

The man told police he had recently left prison and was delivering drugs by
“delivery”, and he led officers to more marijuana stored at his home on Rua
Aparecido Pandolfelli. [@1] In total police seized 272 grams of marijuana and a
mobile phone. [@1] The car he was using was impounded because its registration
was expired. [@1]

The man's arrest was ratified as drug trafficking under article 33 of the Drugs
Law and he was sent to the triage centre. [@1] The woman was indicted for
contempt and later released, and the case was presented to duty delegate
Reinaldo Lopes Machado. [@1]
