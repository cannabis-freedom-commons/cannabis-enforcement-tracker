Guerrero state prosecutors said soldiers, National Guard officers and state
police arrested two people in flagrante, caught in the act, in Taxco de Alarcón
on 30 October 2026. [@2] The two were Edgar “N”, known as “El 77”, and Inés “N”.
[@1] Officers stopped their pickup on the Libramiento Ahuehuepan, a bypass road,
after the occupants tried to evade a patrol and were intercepted metres further
on. [@2] Both were handed to the Ministerio Público, the prosecutor's office
that will open the investigation and decide their legal situation. [@2] Both are
presumed innocent until a judge rules. [@1]

Officers found about a kilogram of green, dry herb resembling marijuana in the
vehicle, along with 19 wrapped packets of the same herb and two tactical vests.
[@2] The pickup, a 2015 model in navy blue, had a current theft report in
Guerrero. [@1]

State prosecutors identified the pair as alleged members of La Familia
Michoacana's cell in Taxco de Alarcón. [@2] MVS Noticias described the arrests
as a targeted blow to the group's operating structure in Guerrero's northern
region. [@1] Infobae reported that the FGE gave no further details about its
investigation into the group. [@3]
