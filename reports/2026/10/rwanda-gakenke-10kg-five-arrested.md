Police in Gakenke District arrested five men in Kabutwa Village, Rukore Cell,
Kamubuga Sector, during the early hours of 22 October 2026. Officers seized
10 kg of cannabis in the operation, which followed information from local
residents. [@1,2,3]

Rwanda National Police said the men were suspected of working together to
distribute drugs, share information and arrange routes to bring cannabis into
Rwanda from neighbouring countries. [@1,2]

The men were also suspected of distributing kanyanga, an illicit gin. Umunota
reported that three suspects poured out some of the drink after learning they
were being sought, while another was found with a bottle. [@1,3]

Northern Province police spokesperson CIP Ignace Ngirabakunzi urged residents
to report drug-related activity to security agencies, saying it harms people
who distribute or use drugs, their families and the wider community. [@2,3]

Umunota said the five remained at Gakenke Police Station as investigations
continued and they awaited further legal proceedings. [@1]
