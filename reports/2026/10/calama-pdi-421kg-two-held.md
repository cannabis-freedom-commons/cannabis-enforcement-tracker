Chile's Investigative Police (PDI) arrested two men in Calama: a 41-year-old
Colombian and a 29-year-old Venezuelan. [@2] The inquiry targeted a group of
foreign citizens accused of storing drugs at a private house for distribution to
small-scale dealers and through delivery sales. [@2] Both men were brought
before the Juzgado de Garantía, Calama's guarantee court, on 23 October for a
detention-control hearing. [@2]

The PDI said the two procedures, on 22 and 24 October, seized 433 kg 428 g of
illegal substances in total. [@2] In the first, detectives found 14 kg 757.32 g
of cannabis, 7 kg 304 g of cocaine hydrochloride, 5 kg 60 g of cocaine base and
7.09 g of ketamine. [@3] They also seized a car and 1,659,300 Chilean pesos in
cash. [@3] The drugs were sent to the Antofagasta health service. [@2] On 24
October a drug find in Calama recovered another 406 kg 300 g of cannabis in 391
packages. [@1]

The PDI said the two interventions were separate, each carried out on its own
information. [@3] Prefect Inspector Freddy Castro Crespo, head of the
Antofagasta police region, said coordination with the Public Prosecutor's Office
was fundamental to clarifying the facts and dismantling the criminal dynamics
associated with drug trafficking. [@1]
