The Drug Enforcement Commission (DEC), Zambia's anti-narcotics agency, jointly
charged and arrested three drug suppliers for trafficking 410 sachets and eight
bundles of high-grade cannabis weighing 11.9 kilogrammes in Mpika District,
Muchinga Province. [@1] DEC public relations officer Allan Tamba named the three
as Makani Dion, 26, Teddy Nyondo, 24, and Castor Mutondo, 22. [@1] Tamba said an
intelligence-led operation by DEC officers in Mpika District produced the
seizures and arrests. [@1] Briefly, a legal news service, reported the same DEC
statement: three suppliers charged in Mpika and 11.9 kilogrammes of cannabis
seized. [@2]

Tamba said the commission also apprehended Kampiyao Tsitsi, a 50-year-old
Zimbabwean woman, for allegedly possessing 3.2 kilogrammes of high-grade
cannabis, among other arrests. [@1] Tamba described the arrests as part of the
commission's intensified campaign against organised drug trafficking nationwide.
[@1] The publication reported ten other arrests in all but did not name or
detail them in the accessible text. [@1]
