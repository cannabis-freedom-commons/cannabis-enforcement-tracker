Telangana's EAGLE Force arrested 75-year-old Shaik Jathen Bee for allegedly
selling marijuana from her house in LBS Nagar, Fateh Nagar, Hyderabad. [@1] The
Hyderabad Narcotics Police Station team searched the residence and seized 41
small packets containing 179.1 grams of marijuana, Rs 43,340 in cash and a
mobile phone. [@1] She sold the marijuana in small packets to customers who came
to the house. [@1] Cases were registered against 70 people who were buying ganja
from the woman arrested in Fatehnagar. [@3]

Of the 70 suspected consumers identified, 66 tested positive for THC in medical
examinations, and three were juveniles. [@1] Police handed 65 of the consumers
to rehabilitation centres: 45 to Gandhi Rehab Centre, 15 to Amangal Rehab Centre
and five to Kothur Rehab Centre. [@1] The juveniles were dealt with through the
appropriate legal process and referred for de-addiction treatment and
rehabilitation. [@1]

The case was registered as Crime No. 23/2026 under sections 8(c), 20(b)(ii)(A)
and 27(b) of the NDPS Act and section 77 of the Juvenile Justice Act. [@2]
Police said Shaik Jathen Bee had seven earlier NDPS cases between 2017 and 2025
in the Balanagar, Sanath Nagar, Begumpet and Ameerpet excise limits. [@1]
Investigators were trying to trace five suspected associates and the source of
the marijuana. [@1]
