Bureau of Customs officers discovered 7,335 grams of marijuana kush inside three unclaimed pieces
of luggage found abandoned at a Ninoy Aquino International Airport baggage carousel on 26 October
2026. [@1,2] The kush was concealed in food-labelled aluminium packaging; routine X-ray
screening, manual inspection and a PDEA canine panel flagged the bags. [@1,2]

The bags were linked to two passengers who arrived from Bangkok on 24 September, and the
inspection brought together the NAIA inter-agency drug task group and the police aviation
security group. [@1,2,3] No owner has been identified; the drugs were turned over to PDEA custody.
[@1,2]

The Bureau of Customs said the 26 October find and the 29 October arrest together yielded about 24.8 kg of suspected marijuana kush with an estimated street value of P24.79 million in four days. [@4,5]
