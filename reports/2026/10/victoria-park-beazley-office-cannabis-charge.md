Kensington Police charged a man after an incident at an electoral office in
Victoria Park on 21 October 2026. [@2] About 1.15pm police received a report of
a man causing a disturbance at the electoral office on Albany Highway. [@2]
Police say the man made verbal threats while possessing improvised nunchucks,
then was arrested nearby in Bentley with no one injured. [@2] The accused is a
56-year-old Bentley man charged with being armed in a way that may cause fear,
making an unlawful threat and possessing cannabis. [@2]

Police refused his bail and said he was due before Perth Magistrates Court on 22
October 2026. [@2] The office was Western Australia local government minister
Hannah Beazley's electorate office in Victoria Park, where the man allegedly
brandished nunchucks. [@1] Office staff reported that he carried a homemade
martial-arts implement and threatened them, and police later arrested him nearby
and charged weapon, threat and cannabis counts. [@1] The ABC account likewise
says the Bentley man was charged with making unlawful threats, being armed in a
way that may cause fear and possessing cannabis. [@3]

Beazley was not in the office at the time, said staff were shaken, said the man
had sought to see her and said his motive was unclear. [@1]
