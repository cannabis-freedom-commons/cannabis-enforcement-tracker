Federal revenue officers arrested a 19-year-old woman after finding 4.47 kg of
dry hashish strapped to her legs during an inspection on the BR-277 in Paraná.
[@1] Photographs released by the Federal Revenue show the drug packages bound to
the passenger's body with adhesive tape, found during a personal search. [@1]
The 55-year-old driver and a couple sharing the same app-arranged ride were
released after showing in-app messages indicating they did not know about the
shipment. [@2]

The car was a shared ride that had left Foz do Iguaçu, on the border with
Paraguay and Argentina, and was heading for Curitiba. [@1] Officers were
carrying out a routine inspection at kilometre 305 near the Posto do Relógio
when they found the drug during the personal inspection. [@1] The stop happened
in the early hours of Wednesday (28) near Prudentópolis. [@1] The woman told
officers she had received the material in Foz do Iguaçu and was to carry it to
the Paraná capital. [@1] The Federal Revenue identified the 4.47 kg as dry, a
highly concentrated hashish derived from cannabis. [@2]

After the seizure the young woman and the drug were taken to the Civil Police of
Paraná for legal procedures. [@1] Her name was not disclosed. [@1]
