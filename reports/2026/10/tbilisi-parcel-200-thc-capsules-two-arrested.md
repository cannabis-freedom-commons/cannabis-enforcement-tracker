Officers of the Central Criminal Police Department and the Customs Department of
the Revenue Service detained two people at different times on suspicion of
illegal purchase and storage, attempted purchase and storage, and illegal import
of an especially large quantity of narcotics. [@2] Officers carried out a
controlled delivery of a parcel sent from abroad in the name of one of the
accused as part of operational measures against him. [@2] Officers detained the
second accused at Tbilisi International Airport and seized drugs from hand
luggage during a check. [@1]

The parcel yielded an especially large quantity of narcotics as evidence,
including about 200 capsules containing tetrahydrocannabinol. [@2]
Tetrahydrocannabinol is a narcotic drug obtained from the cannabis plant. [@1]
The parcel also carried a sizable quantity of other drugs when it was opened by
the police, in addition to methamphetamine. [@1] The airport seizure consisted
of narcotics containing methadone and buprenorphine. [@2]

The investigation is proceeding under Articles 260, 19-260 and 262 of Georgia's
Criminal Code. [@2] If found guilty the person faces imprisonment for 20 years
or for the rest of life. [@1] All suspects are presumed innocent until proven
guilty in a court of law. [@1]
