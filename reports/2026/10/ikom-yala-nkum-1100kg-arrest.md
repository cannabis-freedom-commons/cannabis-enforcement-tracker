Officers of the NDLEA's Cross River State Command seized 181 bags of suspected cannabis weighing about 1,100 kg in Yala Nkum village, Ikom Local Government Area, on 4 October 2026, the command said. [@1,2,3] They arrested a 46-year-old woman, Adama Inuwa, who said she was from Ogoja Local Government Area. [@1,2,3]

The operation also recovered 17.15 kg of cannabis seeds. [@1,2] The command's media and advocacy officer, ASN1 Sebastian Lebo, signed the statement. [@1,2,3]

The NDLEA said the woman was in custody while investigators tried to establish the source of the drugs and identify other people in the trafficking network. [@1,2] She would be charged to court after the investigation and the necessary legal procedures, the agency said. [@1,2]

The command called on residents, traditional rulers, parents and youth organisations to give information on drug trafficking. [@1,2]
