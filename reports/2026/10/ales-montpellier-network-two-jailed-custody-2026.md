Two men aged 31 and 44 accused of running a drug trade between Montpellier and
Alès were jailed on Friday 9 October 2026 after appearing before the judge of
freedoms and detention (JLD), the judge who rules on pre-trial custody in
France. [@1] Searches carried out during the operation turned up €26,000 in
cash, 7 kg of cannabis and 1 kg of cocaine, according to the Alès prosecutor's
office. [@1]

Officers searched a house in the arènes district of Alès as part of the
operation. [@1] The two men were brought before the Alès courthouse on the
morning of Friday 9 October. [@1] Substitute prosecutor Quentin Larroque handled
the case before the two men, aged 31 and 44, appeared before the judge of
freedoms and detention. [@1]

Defence lawyers, including Me Florence Mendez for the suspect from Alès, sought
his release under judicial supervision at the hearing. [@1] Prosecutors firmly
sought pre-trial detention for both suspects. [@2] The two were arrested at the
start of that week, one in Montpellier and the other in Saint-Christol-lez-Alès,
a town in the Gard. [@2] The searches were carried out by officers of the drug
squad of the Gard territorial crime division (DCT, formerly the departmental
criminal investigation unit). [@2]
