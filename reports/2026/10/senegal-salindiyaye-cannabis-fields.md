On Thursday 8 October 2026 Military Zone No. 5 coordinated a security operation on Salindiyaye
island in the Karones archipelago off Casamance. [@1,2,3] The Southern naval base led the
mission with the Ziguinchor gendarmerie legion, deploying boats and troops to sweep an area
that is hard to reach by land. [@1,2,3]

The sweep found several fields of chanvre indien, the armed forces' public-relations directorate
told reporters. [@2,3] No suspect was arrested, but units continued searching for people linked
to the plots. [@1,2,3] Sunugox said the work aimed to push back criminal networks using the
islands. [@1]
