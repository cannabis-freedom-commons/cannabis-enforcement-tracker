Varis Preungprasob and Rutcha Pinitkarn, both Thai nationals, were each
sentenced to five years in prison by the Supreme Court for importing a controlled
drug. [@1,2] The offence dated to 2 September 2025, when they arrived on an Air
Seychelles flight and were stopped by customs and police officers. [@1,2]

A search found 15,943.9 g of cannabis in Preungprasob’s possession and 15,968.6 g
with Pinitkarn, the police communiqué reported. [@1,2] The force published the
sentences on 1 October 2026. [@2]
