Three police patrols searched the Hanf.com store on Theaterstraße in Landshut on
Friday afternoon and sealed it off during the operation. [@2] An officer was
posted at the entrance of the small shop in the heart of Landshut and let nobody
into the hemp shop, with three patrol cars at the scene on Friday the 9th. [@1]
Hanf.com founder Wenzel Cerveny, 65, said the shelves were empty and called the
action damaging to business. [@2] No charges were brought on the day of the
raid. [@2]

Cannabis seeds were taken from a refrigerator, cuttings were cut off, and hemp
teas, CBD flowers, other merchandise and the shop till were seized. [@2]
Officers had found about 13 to 15 plants under grow lighting there, according to
the warrant. [@2]

Cerveny directs his criticism at the Landshut public prosecutor's office, the
Staatsanwaltschaft, which has now had the shop searched for the fourth time.
[@2] The 65-year-old sells hemp in Landshut and Regensburg and is in conflict
with the Bavarian judiciary. [@1] Wenzel Cerveny is the founder of the Hanf.com
shop chain. [@1] In Landshut Hanf.com sells growing supplies alongside food,
cosmetics, CBD products and industrial-hemp products. [@2]
