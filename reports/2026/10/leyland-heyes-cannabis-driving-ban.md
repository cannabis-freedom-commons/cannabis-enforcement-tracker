A man has been banned from driving after being caught behind the wheel with
cannabis in his system. [@1] Connor Heyes, of Beechcroft Avenue in Darcy Lever,
was found to have 4.4 micrograms of cannabis per litre of blood while driving on
Southport Road in Leyland on May 23. [@1] The 22-year-old was also found to be
in possession of cannabis. [@1] Heyes appeared at Preston Magistrates' Court on
Friday, October 23, where he was banned from driving for 12 months. [@1]

He was also fined £346 and ordered to pay £85 in court costs. [@1] The cannabis
was ordered to be forfeited and destroyed. [@1] The court said the fines were
reduced by a third due to Heyes' guilty plea. [@1]

The Preston Magistrates listings for Friday, October 23 also name Connor Heyes
as a first-appearance defendant. [@2] They list his offences as possessing Class
B cannabis or cannabis resin and driving with a specified controlled drug above
the limit. [@2]
