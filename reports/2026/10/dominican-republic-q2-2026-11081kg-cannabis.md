The Dirección Nacional de Control de Drogas (DNCD), the Dominican Republic's
drug-control agency, reported seizing 16,671.16 kg of narcotic substances
between April and June 2026 in its second-quarter statistical summary based on
SAIC criminal-information data. [@1] As a result of the anti-drug operations,
the authorities reported 12,548 people detained between April and June: 10,682
Dominicans and 1,866 foreigners. [@1] June recorded the most detentions with
4,309, followed by May with 4,304 and April with 3,935. [@1] Men accounted for
12,422 of the detentions and women for 126. [@1]

Most of the seizures were marijuana, with 11,065.01 kg taken over the three
months, while cocaine came second with 5,542.47 kg. [@1] June had the largest
overall volume with 9,513.83 kg seized, against 4,372.85 kg in April and
2,784.48 kg in May. [@2] June marijuana seizures reached 9,011.14 kg, more than
81% of the quarter's marijuana total, compared with 1,253.83 kg in April and
800.04 kg in May. [@1] Within June's marijuana seizures, the report attributes
7,789.97 kg to San José de Ocoa and 1,022.69 kg to Peravia, while Santo Domingo
recorded 63.04 kg. [@1] The DNCD also reported 16.44 kg of hashish, 7.14 kg of
opium and 0.24 kg of ecstasy seized in the quarter. [@1]

The agency reported seizing 546 vehicles in the quarter, including 416
motorcycles, the most common type. [@1] Preliminary figures listed 121 firearms
confiscated in the quarter: 36 in April, 45 in May and 40 in June. [@1] Officers
seized RD$11,003,406.10 and US$36,686, plus 150 euros and smaller sums in other
foreign currencies. [@1]

A later breakdown of the first nine months of 2026 gives April-June as 11,065.01
kg of marijuana and 5,542.47 kg of cocaine, against 1,036.93 kg of marijuana in
January-March and 2,317.68 kg in July-September. [@3] Diario Libre's nine-month
tally, republished by Marca País, put July-September marijuana seizures at
2,317.68 kg. [@3]
