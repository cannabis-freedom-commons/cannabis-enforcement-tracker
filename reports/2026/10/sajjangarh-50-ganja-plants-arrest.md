Sajjangarh police in Rajasthan's Banswara district found ganja being grown
illegally and seized the plants, police said. [@1] Officers found 50 green ganja
plants that had been grown in a dilapidated hut, according to the report. [@1]

Police seized all the plants and arrested one suspect in the case. [@1] The
action was taken under the NDPS Act, India's main narcotics law, which governs
cannabis offences. [@1]

The operation was led by the Sajjangarh station house officer, a police post
commander, according to the headline. [@1] Sajjangarh is a police station area
in Banswara district, in southern Rajasthan near the Gujarat and Madhya Pradesh
borders, an area where police regularly report small seizures of ganja, the
Hindi term for cannabis. [@1]
