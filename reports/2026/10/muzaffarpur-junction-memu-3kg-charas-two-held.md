The Directorate of Revenue Intelligence Muzaffarpur Regional Unit with the Bihar
Police Special Task Force busted an interstate charas network allegedly
smuggling narcotics from Nepal into India for onward movement to Firozabad in
Uttar Pradesh. [@1] The two-day operation ending on Sunday seized approximately
3 kg of charas valued at Rs 3 crore and two persons were arrested under the
Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, 1985. [@1]

The action followed specific intelligence about cross-border charas smuggling,
after which the joint team kept remote stretches along the India-Nepal border
under surveillance for two days to identify operatives, hideouts and the modus
operandi. [@1] Investigators said the syndicate planned to evade agencies by
moving the consignment through multiple trains and deliberately delaying
transit, with transportation scheduled for Sunday morning. [@1] In the early
hours of Sunday the team learned that a suspected carrier was travelling on a
Narkatiyaganj-Muzaffarpur MEMU train from Bapudham Motihari and intercepted the
suspect at Muzaffarpur Junction. [@1]

The apprehended person allegedly said the contraband had been brought from Nepal
and was to be delivered in Firozabad. [@1] Based on that questioning the joint
team conducted a follow-up operation and apprehended the alleged supplier,
taking total arrests to two. [@1] Further investigation is underway to determine
the wider network, identify additional associates and establish the supply chain
and intended distribution route in Bihar and Uttar Pradesh. [@1] A second
account confirms that around 3 kg of charas valued at around Rs 3 crore was
seized and two persons arrested in the same Sunday joint DRI and Bihar Police
STF operation. [@2] A third account likewise reports the arrest of two persons
and seizure of around three kg of contraband worth an estimated Rs 3 crore in
Muzaffarpur on Sunday. [@3]
