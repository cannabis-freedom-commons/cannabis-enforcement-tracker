A 22-year-old man was caught by police smoking hashish beside a school in
Alverca do Ribatejo, in the municipality of Vila Franca de Xira, on Friday 30
October 2026. [@1] He had 4 grams of the drug with him, which O MIRANTE said
would supply nine individual doses. [@1]

The intervention was carried out by officers of the Escola Segura, the PSP
school-policing programme, attached to the PSP's Vila Franca de Xira police
division during a preventive patrol. [@1] Officers spotted the man smoking what
appeared to be a handmade drug cigarette, known in Portugal as a charro. [@1]
Asked whether he carried any illegal substance or object, he said yes and took a
transparent plastic package of several pieces of suspected drug from a shoulder
bag. [@1]

The Lisbon Metropolitan Command statement put the seizure at 4.58 grams of
hashish, which police equated to 9.16 individual doses, Notícias do Sorraia
reported. [@2] The青年教师: A safety search of the young man found no other illegal
object or substance. [@2]

O MIRANTE reported that police believe the drug was meant to be sold to pupils.
[@1] The man was detained but released after appearing in court to await trial.
[@1] The police statement reported by Notícias do Sorraia records only the
seizure and does not say the young man was detained. [@2]

The PSP said its Escola Segura teams would keep running prevention and
inspection operations in the surroundings of schools. [@2]
