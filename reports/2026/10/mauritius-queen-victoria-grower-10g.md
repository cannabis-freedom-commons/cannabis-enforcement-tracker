Officers of the Anti-Drug and Smuggling Unit (ADSU), the Mauritian police's drug
squad, searched the home of a 37-year-old grower in Queen Victoria at about
11:35 on Thursday 29 October 2026. [@1] The man, described as a planteur, or
grower, lived at the property in the village, which lies in the Flacq district.
[@1] The search took place in the late morning. [@1]

During the search, investigators found a paper packet holding 10 grams of
cannabis and seized it. [@1] They arrested the man. [@1] He was admitted to
hospital for health reasons and remains under police surveillance while he
receives treatment. [@1]

The authorities did not say what prompted the search or whether the cannabis was
grown at the property. [@1] No plants, cultivation equipment or other quantities
of the drug were reported among the seizures. [@1] The investigation is
continuing. [@1] No charges or court date have been announced, and the man has
not been named. [@1]
