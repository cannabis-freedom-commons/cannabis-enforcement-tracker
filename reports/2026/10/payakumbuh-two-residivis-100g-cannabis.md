Officers of the Payakumbuh drug squad arrested two men in two roadside stops on
the night of Friday 30 October 2026, seizing methamphetamine, known locally as
sabu, and dried cannabis. [@2] The first stop was at about 19:20 WIB on the
roadside in the Kelurahan Padang Sikabu neighbourhood, where officers detained
FD, 23. [@2] There they seized nine packets of dried cannabis with a gross
weight of 100 grams, one packet of methamphetamine weighing 0.62 grams gross,
Rp227,000 in cash and other items. [@1] The second stop was on the roadside in
front of the Padang Tangah Balai Nan Duo village office, where police arrested
DF, 36. [@1] A search of DF found three packets of methamphetamine with a gross
weight of 0.33 grams, Rp170,000 in cash and other items. [@3]

Police called both men repeat offenders, or residivis, in the same kind of case
and said they had been on their target list. [@1] Drug squad head AKP Gusmanto
called both men dealers in the local drug trade. [@3]

Both were held at the Payakumbuh district police headquarters for further
investigation. [@2] They were accused under Article 114(1) of the 2009 Narcotics
Law, a charge that carries a maximum of 20 years in prison. [@3] Gusmanto said
Payakumbuh's position on an inter-provincial route was one reason the area
needed watching. [@1] He asked residents to report suspected drug activity in
their neighbourhoods. [@1]
