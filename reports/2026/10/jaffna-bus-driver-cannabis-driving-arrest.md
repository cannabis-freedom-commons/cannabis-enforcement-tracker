A bus driver who was carrying passengers from Jaffna to Urkavathurai (Kayts) was
arrested last Friday on suspicion of driving after using drugs. [@1] The
allegation reported against him was driving while under the influence of
cannabis (ganja). [@1] Police produced him before the Urkavathurai court the
next day, and the court ordered him held on remand until Tuesday, 3 November
2026. [@1]

The remanded driver was produced again in court when the case was taken up for
hearing on that Tuesday. [@1] His lawyer told the court that his client had not
used drugs. [@1] The lawyer said the driver was undergoing Ayurvedic medical
treatment. [@1] To support the explanation that the treatment could have
produced signs of drug use in testing, the lawyer showed the court a medical
report, an Ayurvedic doctor's confirmation report and the medicines being used,
and applied for bail. [@1]

The court then allowed the driver to be released on two surety bails. [@1,3] The
court adjourned the case to January for the next hearing. [@1,3]

The court that first produced him ordered the driver held on remand until Tuesday, 3 November 2026. [@2]
