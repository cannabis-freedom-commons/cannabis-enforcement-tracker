Jason Donnelly, 33, pleaded guilty to five charges in Ballarat Magistrates'
Court on 27 October 2026. [@2] Defence lawyer Benjamin Yusupov applied for
summary jurisdiction, which allows the matter to be heard in the magistrates'
court, and said Donnelly had an intellectual disability. [@2] Magistrate Ronald
Saines granted the application. [@2] Saines said a significant message of
deterrence and denunciation had to be imposed by a term of imprisonment. [@2]
The matter was adjourned for a plea and sentencing hearing on 14 December 2026.
[@2]

Investigators were confronted by an "extremely belligerent" mother during the
raid in Mount Pleasant, where more than six kilograms of cannabis was seized
from a drug trafficker's bedroom, the court heard. [@1] Police had found
Donnelly slumped over the wheel of a parked car in Golden Point in the early
hours of 21 May 2026. [@2] A search of the car found $3,420 in cash and drugs
including methamphetamine, ketamine, MDMA and cannabis. [@2] He was then
detained at Ballarat police station while officers organised a search of his
Mount Pleasant home. [@2] Police searched the house and found numerous bags of
cannabis and $1,061.75 in cash in his bedroom. [@2] A safe from the bedroom,
forced open later, held cannabis, methamphetamine, MDMA capsules, ketamine, an
imitation handgun and hundreds of dollars in cash. [@2] The court heard the
total cannabis seized was 6.39 kilograms, while the amounts of the other drugs
were substantially smaller. [@2]

Yusupov also described the trafficking operation as "not complex". [@2] Crown
prosecutor Glenn Mohammed did not oppose summary jurisdiction and agreed the
operation was "not sophisticated". [@2] The court heard Donnelly had 10 pages of
prior criminal history, including "extensive" convictions for drug trafficking
and possession. [@2] Saines said the cash held alongside very large quantities
of illicit drugs indicated a significant level of organisation. [@2] He noted
that trafficking carries a maximum of 15 years in prison and that three
trafficking counts were proceeding against a repeat offender. [@2]
