A 26-year-old man was arrested in flagrante for drug trafficking on the night of
Friday 30 October 2026 in Limeira, São Paulo. [@1] Teams of the 10th Special
Actions Police Battalion, a specialized unit, acted after intelligence about
storage and distribution of drugs in the region. [@1] The arrest took place at
an apartment in the Campo Belo neighbourhood, where the suspect admitted keeping
drugs in his room. [@2] After the on-site action he was taken to the Limeira
police station, where he remained detained at the disposal of the courts for the
relevant hearings. [@1]

The official balance reported about 1.994 kg of marijuana and 365 g of THC vape
products, the 2.359 kg cannabis total recorded here. [@1] Apartment searches
found varied marijuana portions including eight portions of dry, a high-value
cannabis derivative, plus 243 ecstasy tablets, nine THC vapes and bulk cocaine.
[@2] In the suspect's vehicle parked in the condominium officers found more
marijuana, dry and nine more ecstasy tablets, including ten marijuana portions
and 36 dry portions in a pouch in the glove box. [@2] Officers also seized four
precision scales, a cutting knife and packaging materials, plus R$582 in cash
suspected to be linked to drug sales. [@1]

Police said the man told them he received R$1,300 per month to store the drugs
for another person he refused to name and that he also made deliveries in the
area. [@2] The same balance listed 221 g of ecstasy and 25 g of cocaine
alongside the cannabis, mentioned only to explain the mixed seizure and excluded
from cannabis figures. [@1] He was first taken to Limeira Santa Casa for medical
evaluation before being presented at the police station, where the trafficking
flagrante was formalized. [@2]
