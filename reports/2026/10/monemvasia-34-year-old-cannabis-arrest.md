Officers of the Laconia O.P.K.E. crime-prevention unit arrested a 34-year-old
foreign man at midday on 31 October 2026 in a local community of Monemvasia
municipality. [@1] Police found a small amount of cannabis in his possession and
seized it. [@1] A second account likewise reports the midday 31 October arrest
of a 34-year-old foreign man in Monemvasia by the same Laconia O.P.K.E. unit
with a small amount of cannabis seized. [@2]

The preliminary investigation is being handled by the Voion Police Department.
[@1] The amount was described by police only as a small amount, with no weight
published. [@2]
