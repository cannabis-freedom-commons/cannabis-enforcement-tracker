Mexico's federal prosecutor's office, the Fiscalía General de la República
(FGR), burned more than 1,813 kg of seized narcotics. [@1] The incineration was
held at the facilities of the 98th Infantry Battalion in Santa María Huatulco,
Oaxaca. [@1] In its own post on X, the FGR put the load at more than 1.8 tonnes
of narcotics tied to federal investigation files. [@2] The burned loads included
green cannabis herb as well as cocaine hydrochloride and synthetic
methamphetamine. [@1]

The drugs had been held in ministerial custody in Oaxaca in connection with
several open case files. [@1] Inspectors from the internal control body watched
each stage of the weighing, counting and chemical testing of the packages sent
to the fire. [@1] Forensic specialists from the Criminal Investigation Agency
and Federal Ministerial Police handled the chain of custody and verified each
package before it entered the incinerator. [@1] Representatives of different
levels of government attended, including delegates of the Oaxaca state
administration and officials of the federal security ministry. [@1]
