On 20 October, officers of the Federal Penitentiary Service directorate for
Rostov Oblast spotted a man trying to use a slingshot to throw several parcels
over the fence of a secure facility outside Novocherkassk. [@1] Officers
detained the man and, during a personal search, recovered the slingshot and
seven ball-shaped parcels containing plant material. [@1,2] Authorities in
Novocherkassk opened a criminal case against the man on suspicion of attempting
to deliver drugs onto the grounds of correctional colony No 14, known as IK-14.
[@1,2]

An expert examination established that the plant material inside the seven
parcels was cannabis with a total weight of 27.77 grams. [@1,2] Investigators
classified the act as attempted illegal sale of narcotic drugs on a significant
scale under Part 3 of Article 30 and point «б» of Part 3 of Article 228.1 of the
Russian Criminal Code. [@1]

IK-14 is identified as an ispravitelnaya koloniya, a correctional colony, and
the parcels were allegedly aimed across its perimeter fence when staff
intervened. [@1] The case remains at the suspicion stage, with the man described
as a suspect in attempted sale rather than as a convicted person. [@1]

The detainee was a 41-year-old local resident caught while throwing a parcel onto the outer restricted zone of the secure facility. [@2] The cannabis parcels were hidden inside electric light-bulb bodies weighted with iron coins and wrapped with insulating tape. [@2] An expert examination confirmed that the seized material was a narcotic drug. [@2] A court placed the suspect in custody as a preventive measure in the attempted-sale case under Part 3 of Article 30 and Article 228.1 of the Russian Criminal Code. [@2]
