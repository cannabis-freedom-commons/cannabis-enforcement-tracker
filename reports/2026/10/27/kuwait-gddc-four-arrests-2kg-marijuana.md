Kuwait’s Ministry of Interior said personnel from the General Department for Drug Control arrested
four men in four separate incidents involving trafficking in narcotics, psychotropic substances
and alcohol. [@1,2] The ministry press statement, reported on 26–27 October 2026, listed seizures
that included two kilograms of marijuana, one kilogram of cocaine, 1,000 Captagon tablets, 80
grams of methamphetamine and eleven A4 sheets soaked in synthetic cannabinoids. [@1,2]

One arrest also involved 97 bottles of imported alcoholic beverages and KWD 520 in cash that the
ministry linked to proceeds from sales. [@1,2] The four suspects and seized items were referred to
the competent authorities for legal action. [@1,2]
