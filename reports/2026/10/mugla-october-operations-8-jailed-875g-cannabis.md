Muğla Provincial Gendarmerie Command announced that its October anti-drug
operations caught 175 suspects and that courts remanded 8 of them in custody.
[@1] The operations recorded 173 separate incidents between 1 and 31 October
2026. [@1] Of the 175 suspects caught, 8 were remanded in custody, known in
Turkey as tutuklama, while 6 others were placed under judicial-control measures
known as adli kontrol. [@1]

Searches in those operations seized 573 grams of skunk and 302 grams of kubar
esrar, a cannabis preparation, together about 875 grams of cannabis. [@1] The
same searches also found 700 synthetic pills, 144 grams of methamphetamine and
66 grams of bonzai, described as synthetic cannabinoid, which are not counted as
cannabis. [@2] Officers also reported seizing 20 skunk seeds and 31 implements
for drug use. [@1]

The gendarmerie presented the seizures as the result of its month-long work to
prevent drug production, trafficking and use. [@1] It said its fight against
drugs would continue with determination. [@2]
