Gendarmes surprised a man in his twenties near the Lyon-Corbas remand prison
during the night of 27 to 28 October 2026 as he tried to bring prohibited goods
inside. [@1] The Saint-Priest resident tried at about 2 a.m. to throw a bag over
the prison perimeter wall from the glacis, the security zone around the
facility. [@1] Spotted by officers, he tried to flee on foot before being
quickly arrested. [@1]

Inside the parcel, gendarmes found food and cannabis. [@1] He was held overnight
in custody at the Corbas gendarmerie brigade. [@2] The next day he was released
with a summons by judicial police officer to appear before the Lyon judicial
court. [@2] He must answer charges of climbing the prison enclosure without
legitimate reason, intruding on prison grounds and irregularly delivering
objects to a detainee. [@1]

Officers of the Bron gendarmerie company say parcel throws to detainees happen
regularly but they cannot spend the night watching the prison surroundings. [@2]
Such delivery attempts are described as not rare around the Corbas prison. [@1]
