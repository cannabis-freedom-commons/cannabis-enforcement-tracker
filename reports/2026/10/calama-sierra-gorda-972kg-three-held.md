Officers detained three people, two Colombians and a Chilean, in three separate
drug operations in Calama and Sierra Gorda, and a judge remanded all three in
pre-trial custody. [@1] The prosecutor's office formalised the operation as an
anti-drug push with the Fiscalía SAC, the Antofagasta prosecutor's specialised
unit, and Carabineros against groups using the region for transit and
distribution. [@1]

In the first operation, investigators identified a marijuana storehouse in
Calama's western sector and obtained search warrants, finding 595 kilos 200
grams of marijuana packed in sacks and parcels. [@1] In a second Calama
operation, OS7 officers with a sniffer dog checked a vehicle on the CH-21
highway and found three suitcases carrying 273 kilos 100 grams of marijuana.
[@1] In Sierra Gorda, a truck tried to avoid a vehicle check at the Route 5
North toll plaza, and officers of the Baquedano post stopped it and found 100
parcels holding 104 kilos 630 grams of cannabis sativa in its cargo area. [@1]

The three operations together took 972 kilos 930 grams of marijuana off the
roads. [@1] Antofagasta regional prosecutor Juan Castro Bekios said the load
equalled about two million doses, roughly three for every resident of the
region, and put the region's seizures at 61 tonnes so far in 2026. [@1] Castro
Bekios said drug-trafficking routes are the same ones used by copper thieves and
called for pursuing the whole chain rather than "the visible piece". [@1]

General Cristian Montre Soto, head of Carabineros' Antofagasta zone, said the
seizures came from an integral strategy covering the border, roads and urban
sites such as informal settlements used to store drugs. [@1]
