Oran's wilaya security services arrested 10 people in several distinct
operations and seized hard drugs and psychotropic substances. [@1] The
operations followed information received by the research and intervention
brigades (BRI), anti-trafficking services for drugs and psychotropic substances,
the Aïn El Beïda (Es-Sénia) external urban security unit and the third urban
security service. [@1] The information concerned individuals suspected of
dealing hard drugs and psychotropic substances. [@1]

Investigations led to the seizure of more than 10,000 psychotropic tablets of
different types, 105 grams of cocaine and more than 9.2 kilograms of processed
kif (cannabis resin). [@1] Authorities also seized cash of 630,000 Algerian
dinars, which police said came from criminal activity, plus a further 175 euros.
[@1] The seized property also included a passenger car, large knives and an
electronic scale. [@1]

The ten people implicated in the Oran cases were presented before competent
judicial authorities for alleged trafficking and marketing of hard drugs and
psychotropic substances. [@1]
