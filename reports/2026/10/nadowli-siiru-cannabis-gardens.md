The Upper West Regional Command of Ghana's Narcotics Control Commission (NACOC) and officers of the Ghana Immigration Service burned several cannabis gardens at Siiru, a community near the Black Volta in Nadowli District. [@1] The Commission said Immigration officers supplied intelligence that led the team to the locations. [@1] Some of the gardens had already been harvested, while others were close to harvest. [@1]

Searches found cannabis growing in incomplete buildings, in front of houses and alongside maize crops. [@1] The plants were destroyed by burning. [@1] After the operation, the joint team spoke with community members about cannabis cultivation and substance abuse and asked for cooperation against illicit drug activity. [@1] Graphic Online reported the same details and said the information came from a NACOC statement issued on 31 October. [@2] Neither account gives a plant count, an area cleared, the date the operation took place or any arrests. [@1,2] The reports describe several gardens across multiple locations, but do not say whether the sites were connected or who cultivated them. [@1,2]
