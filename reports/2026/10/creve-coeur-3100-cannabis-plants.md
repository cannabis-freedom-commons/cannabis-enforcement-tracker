The Terre Rouge Criminal Investigation Division and the Divisional Support Unit carried out a joint operation in a sugarcane field along the Royal Road in Crève-Cœur on Friday, 9 October 2026. [@1]

Radio One's article on 10 October described the discovery as happening the previous afternoon, consistent with Ion News's date of Friday, 9 October. [@1,2]

Ion News said officers from Montagne-Longue police acted on specific intelligence, searched the field and found a cannabis plantation. [@1] They uprooted and seized 3,100 plants measuring between 10 and 56 centimetres tall. [@1,2]

Both outlets reported an estimated market value of Rs 12.4 million for the plants. [@1,2] Radio One said the cultivator or cultivators had not been identified when its report appeared on 10 October. [@2]

Ion News said the investigation was continuing and attributed the action to the district police and support unit. [@1] The reports describe an eradication in an agricultural field; they do not report a final police or court outcome. [@1,2]
