Spain's Policia Nacional arrested five men aged between 28 and 58, four Irish
nationals and one Englishman, in the towns of Estepona and Benahavís in the
province of Málaga. [@2] Two of the detained men are described as senior
associates of the Kinahan cartel, and all five remained in custody after raids
targeting the gang's day-to-day operations. [@1] The arrests came in an inquiry
into the disappearance of Darren Hoolahan, the brother of former Republic of
Ireland international Wes Hoolahan, who has been missing from the Marbella area
since early June. [@1] Police believe Hoolahan was driven to a rural part of
Estepona, forced into a second vehicle and taken to a house on an estate in the
town, where he was allegedly held. [@2] He remains missing despite searches, and
police fear he has been killed and buried near where he was last seen. [@4]

Officers from the Policia Nacional drugs and organised crime unit UDYCO carried
out raids and searches of six residences on 27 October as part of Operation
Bernabe, five in Estepona and one in Benahavís. [@2] The searches were backed by
the force's tactical unit with police dogs and a helicopter. [@2] Police
intervened three handguns, one equipped with a silencer, and two military-grade
weapons, an AK-47 and a submachine gun, in the searches. [@3] The searches also
yielded a machete, 37.8 kilograms of hashish, 61,520 euros in cash and eight
vehicles, one of them reported stolen, along with mobile phones and two
computers. [@3] All five suspects face investigation for unlawful detention,
possession of military-grade weapons and firearms, vehicle theft, drug
trafficking and membership of a criminal organisation. [@2]

The operation began after Hoolahan's partner reported his disappearance at the
Marbella police station, saying he had gone to meet an alleged friend at a café
in San Pedro de Alcántara. [@4] Police say the abduction was linked to a
settling of scores within a well-known Irish clan, and that Hoolahan had
received a warning a month earlier when vehicles he owned were set on fire
outside his Marbella home. [@4] Four of the five were remanded in custody
without bail, while the fifth was released on bail and then taken into custody
under an international arrest warrant issued by Spain's Audiencia Nacional. [@4]
