An 18-year-old identified only as B.T. was arrested during the night of 16 to 17
October 2026. [@1] The arrest was carried out by the mobile checkpoint team of
the 1008 Logements Sociaux police station. [@1] Officers stopped him at the
checkpoint near the Faladié Gendarmerie School while he was travelling in a
Toyota vehicle. [@1] He was taken to the police unit following his arrest. [@1]

Inside the vehicle, officers seized 40 packets of cannabis herb concealed in two
cartons. [@1] According to preliminary inquiry findings, he had collected the
cannabis from a man named Ousmane at the Virage chinois in Sénou. [@1] He was
allegedly instructed to hand the packets to an unidentified individual on the
left bank near the third bridge. [@1] The stated plan was for Ousmane to
telephone that recipient after crossing the third bridge so the recipient could
collect the packets from B.T., but investigators blocked the transfer in time
and seized the drugs. [@1] The youth is now in the hands of the judicial
authority. [@1] It remains unclear whether he belongs to a trafficking network,
as investigations are continuing. [@1]
