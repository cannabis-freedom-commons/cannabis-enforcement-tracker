An Anti-Narcotics Court in Rawalpindi sentenced a woman identified as Shabana
Bibi to three years on probation after finding her guilty in a
hashish-possession case. [@1] Additional District and Sessions Judge Tahir Aslam
announced the verdict in the case registered by the Counter Narcotics Force
(CNF). [@1] The conviction was based on possession of 2,175 grams, or 2.175
kilograms, of hashish attributed to Bibi. [@1]

Alongside the probation term, the court imposed a fine of Rs400,000 on the
convicted woman for the hashish offence. [@1] The court also directed Bibi to
furnish a guarantee bond of Rs500,000 as an additional condition linked to the
probation order. [@1] One local surety was required to support the guarantee
bond, meaning another local person must stand as guarantor for her compliance.
[@1]

The CNF-registered case therefore ended at the trial stage with a conviction and
a probationary custodial term rather than an acquittal. [@1] The sentence fixes
the custodial term at three years while allowing Bibi to remain on probation
subject to the fine, bond and surety requirements. [@1]
