On Saturday 17 October 2026 elements of the Senegalese army's commando battalion found and
destroyed seven fields of chanvre indien in North Sindian, south-east of the locality of
Tandine in Bignona department, Casamance. [@1,2] The Directorate of Information and Public
Relations of the Armed Forces, quoted in the press, said the action formed part of permanent
security of border zones and action against criminal networks. [@1,2]

Reporting on the communiqué, Le Soleil and PressAfrik said no arrests were mentioned. [@1,2]
PressAfrik noted separate eastern operations the same day but did not link them to these fields.
