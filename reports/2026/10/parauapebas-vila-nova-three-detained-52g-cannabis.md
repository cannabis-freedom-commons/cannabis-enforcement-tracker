Three men were taken to the Civil Police after a Military Police approach in the
area known as Invasão do Vila Nova in Parauapebas, in southeastern Pará, on the
night of Friday 30 October 2026. [@1] Police stopped Clevemberg da Silva
Conceição and found about two grams of cannabis-like material hidden in his
shoe, and police said he claimed to have bought it from Adiel Costa dos Santos.
[@1] Adiel Costa dos Santos ran into a residence when officers approached,
according to police. [@1] Fernando Santos Meireles was found lying in a hammock
in the residence with nothing illicit on him and said he was Adiel's friend and
was only resting there. [@1] The three men and all the seized material were
presented to the Civil Police for further procedures and investigation. [@1]

Inside the residence, officers found about 50 grams of cannabis-like material in
a backpack. [@1] Officers also seized materials described as having
characteristics similar to cocaine and crack, alongside the cannabis. [@1] On a
table officers found several cell phones and two precision scales, and three
bicycles were found in an area of the residence. [@1]

The 34th Military Police Battalion said officers responded after residents
reported intense suspicious movement and possible drug sales between streets 3,
4 and 5. [@1] A second account of the same operation says the police record
notes handcuffs were used but gives no details of the circumstances. [@2]
