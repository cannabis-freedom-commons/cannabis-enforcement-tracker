Palmi traffic-police patrols intercepted a car travelling south at dangerous
speed on the A2 del Mediterraneo and, after a pursuit, safely stopped it near
the Rosarno motorway exit. [@1,2] The driver was a visibly agitated young man of
Cosenza origin who lives in Alessandria, whose behaviour led officers to search
him and the vehicle. [@1,2] Under the coordination of the Palmi prosecutor's
office, the man was arrested in flagrante for possession of drugs for dealing
and taken to Palmi prison. [@1,2]

The search found more than one kilogram of hashish divided into two blocks,
packed and hidden inside the passenger compartment and the boot. [@1,2] In the
same search officers also seized more than 100 grams of cocaine and more than
2,000 euros in cash. [@1] The regional forensic police laboratory in Reggio
Calabria later confirmed the type of the seized substances. [@1]

The operation was part of continuing heightened checks and surveillance along
major Calabrian roads to ensure road safety for all users. [@1] The case was in
the preliminary-investigation phase, with the presumption of innocence applying
until a final conviction. [@1]
