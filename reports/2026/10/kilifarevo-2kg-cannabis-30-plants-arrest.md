Officers from the criminal crime counteraction sector of the Veliko Tarnovo
regional police directorate encountered a 28-year-old man from Veliko Tarnovo
during a patrol in the lands of Kilifarevo around 02:00 on Friday, 16 October
2026. [@1] Uniformed officers came across the drugs during a night patrol in the
territory of Kilifarevo, a town neighbouring the regional centre. [@2] Close to
the man, officers discovered a sack containing plant material that reacted
positively for marijuana in a field narcotics test. [@1] In the surrounding
area, police also located 30 planted hemp genus plants that tested positive for
marijuana. [@1]

Police seized the 30 cannabis plants along with more than two kilograms of
marijuana from the man. [@1] The 28-year-old man was detained for up to 24 hours
under the Interior Ministry Act. [@2] Following a report of the case materials
to the prosecutor's office, the man was charged as an accused for holding 2,256
grams of plant material reacting to marijuana. [@1] Authorities imposed a
measure of restraint of cash bail on the accused while work documenting the case
continued. [@3]
