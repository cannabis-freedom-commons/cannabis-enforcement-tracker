The Cairo Criminal Court sentenced a cafe worker (قهوجي) to one year in prison
and fined him 10,000 Egyptian pounds for trafficking hashish in Al-Matariya,
Cairo. [@1] The verdict was issued on 26 October 2026. [@2]

A chemical laboratory report found the seized items were a black plastic bag
containing pieces that tested positive for hashish, a substance listed in
Schedule 1 of Egypt's drug-control law. [@1] The Public Prosecution charged the
man with possessing hashish for trafficking outside legally permitted
circumstances. [@1] Matariya police had received information that a person was
holding and selling drugs, and investigations identified the defendant. [@1]
Cairo Security Directorate forces set up ambushes and arrested him. [@1] A
search found 30 grams of hashish, along with cash and two mobile phones. [@2]

When confronted, the man admitted possessing the drugs to trade for illicit
profit. [@1] He said the money came from drug sales and that he used the two
phones to contact customers. [@2] Authorities said the necessary legal
procedures were then taken. [@1]
