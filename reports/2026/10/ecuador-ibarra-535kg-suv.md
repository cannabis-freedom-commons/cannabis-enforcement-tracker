Ecuador's National Police arrested a man and seized 535.03 kg of marijuana
packed in 350 rectangular parcels from an SUV in San Miguel de Ibarra, Imbabura,
on 20 October 2026. [@1,2] The force reported the operation the next day; Diario
Extra said it was carried out under the "Cero Impunidad" operation and the 3D
operational strategy. [@1,2]

The packages were on the rear seats and in the boot of a red Chery SUV. [@1,2]
Officers also seized two mobile phones, the vehicle and a motorcycle. [@2] La Hora
named the suspect as Jorge M. and said police records showed earlier cases for
illegal drug possession in 2015 and robbery in 2020. [@2]

The police valued the marijuana at more than US$414,000 on the national market and
said investigations continued to establish where it came from and where it was
going. [@2] The suspect and the seized items were placed at the disposal of the
competent authorities. [@1,2]
