Police in Balaka arrested four people for allegedly possessing cannabis
sativa, known locally as chamba, without a licence. [@1] The suspects,
Chimwemwe Wilson, 27, Gift Mazoko, 24, Andrew Chigonjetso, 17, and Trevor
Kazembe, 24, were arrested on Saturday afternoon, 31 October 2026, during a
sweeping exercise within Balaka Township. [@1]

Mazoko and Wilson were found with loose cannabis sativa and 15 twists placed
in a plastic lid, while Kazembe and Chigonjetso were found with loose
cannabis sativa in glass bottles and two packets of hemp rolling papers
respectively. [@1]

The four, who come from Traditional Authority Nsamala in Balaka District,
will appear before court soon to answer charges of illegal possession of
cannabis sativa without a licence. [@1]
