Tijuana municipal police detained 863 people as alleged offenders during October
in preventive operations and surveillance across the city. [@1] Officers also
arrested 130 people with outstanding warrants for crimes including drug dealing,
robbery, vehicle robbery, homicide and unlawful deprivation of liberty. [@1] All
detainees were placed at the disposal of the investigating authorities. [@1]

In actions against street drug dealing, officers seized 1,560 doses and 1.01
kilograms of marijuana. [@1] Police also seized 58 firearms and four replica
weapons and recovered 114 vehicles with robbery reports. [@1]

The figures are those reported by Tijuana's Municipal Secretariat of Security
and Citizen Protection, which said it maintains permanent operations coordinated
with federal, state and municipal authorities against distribution and sale.
[@1] The secretariat said the actions followed strategies set in the Municipal
Security Roundtable and instructions from Mayor Ismael Burgueño Ruiz to
strengthen crime prevention in Tijuana. [@1]
