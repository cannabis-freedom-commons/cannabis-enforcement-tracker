Acting on local intelligence, Customs officers at the Air Cargo Complex Import at Indira Gandhi
International Airport held a Bangkok shipment declared as PU tubes in 19 cartons. [@1,2] Over
more than 72 hours they examined the load with the importer's representative, broke open plastic
spools and found hidden packets of compacted green material in 15 cartons. [@1,2]

Preliminary tests indicated ganja or marijuana; 120 spools held 148.67 kg of hydroponic weed,
the ministry said. [@1,2] It valued the seizure at about ₹52 crore and put the estimated street
value at ₹150 crore, and seized the drugs and packaging under the NDPS Act. [@1,2]

One person was arrested on 15 October 2026 and produced before a magistrate the same day. [@1,2]
Officers had also begun follow-up searches at five premises in three states. [@1,2]
