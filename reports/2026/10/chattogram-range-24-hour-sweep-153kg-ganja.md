Chattogram Range police arrested and detained 1,129 people in a 24-hour special
drive against juvenile gangs, mugging, extortion, militancy and drugs. [@1] The
juvenile-gang part of the drive held 653 people across 11 districts, of whom 461
were released to guardians on bond and 192 face legal action. [@1] The operation
ran from Thursday 29 October to Friday 30 October 2026. [@2] Police arrested 75
people in drug cases, comprising one drug carrier and 74 drug users, plus 42
drug traders in regular and other cases. [@1] Mobile courts jailed 33 people in
the anti-drug drive, 36 cases were filed under the Narcotics Control Act and
police collected 38,200 taka in fines. [@2]

The sweep seized 1,467 yaba tablets, 153 kg 380 g of ganja, 91 litres of cholai
liquor, four bottles of scuff syrup and 28 tapentadol tablets, according to
police. [@1]

Range DIG Abdul Quddus Chowdhury announced the results, including routine cases,
previous cases, Police Act and Code of Criminal Procedure detentions, and
warrant and sentence-warrant arrests. [@1] Cumilla accounted for the most
juvenile-gang detentions with 351, followed by Chattogram with 101, with the
rest spread across Lakshmipur, Chandpur, Noakhali, Feni, Brahmanbaria,
Rangamati, Cox's Bazar, Khagrachhari and Bandarban. [@1] Police said such
special drives conducted in the public interest will continue. [@1]
