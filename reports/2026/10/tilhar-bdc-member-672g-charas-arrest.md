Tilhar police arrested Raju Verma with 672 grams of charas on 31 October 2026.
[@3] Verma is a kshetra panchayat (BDC) member from Barha Mohabbatpur village.
[@2] Officers acting on an informer's tip caught him near the Dhakiya Ragha
bridge, with the blockade placed on the Birsinghpur–Khudaganj road. [@2] The
Hindustan account describes the same spot as near the Garra river bridge. [@1]
Police said Verma tried to flee on seeing them before he was caught. [@2] He was
produced in court and then sent to jail. [@3]

Police recovered 672 grams of charas, also called sulfa-charas, from his
possession. [@2] Officers also seized his motorcycle and a mobile phone. [@3]

Verma told police he had received the charas from an outsider who came to the
village and was to deliver it to an unidentified baba. [@3] He said he had met
the baba a day earlier at Dadia market and was waiting near the bridge for him
when police caught him. [@3] Police registered a case against him over the
recovery. [@1] Officers said they were tracing from whom he had bought the
consignment. [@2]
