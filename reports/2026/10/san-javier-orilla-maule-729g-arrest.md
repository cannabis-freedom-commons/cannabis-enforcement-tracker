Detectives of Chile's Investigative Police (PDI) detained a 38-year-old man for
alleged drug-law offences after uncovering cannabis at a home in the Orilla de
Maule sector of the commune of San Javier. [@1] Officers said the detained man
was the resident of the searched home, a 38-year-old Chilean national. [@1] On
instructions from the San Javier local prosecutor, he was placed at the disposal
of the Guarantee Court for a detention-control hearing. [@2]

The operation was handled by the Modelo Territorial Cero (MT-0) team of San
Javier's Criminal Investigation Brigade (BICRIM), the PDI's local detective unit
for territorial drug enforcement. [@1] It started from information received
through Denuncia Seguro, the anonymous-reporting programme, which warned of a
space fitted out for growing cannabis inside a dwelling. [@1] Investigators
treated the buried installation as a site intended for producing the drug. [@2]

Detectives then entered and searched the property, finding a buried container
equipped with a cannabis cultivation system. [@2] In the same search, officers
seized 729.27 grams of cannabis found loose and in portioned doses. [@2]
