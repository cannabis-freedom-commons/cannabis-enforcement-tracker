In October 2026 the services of the General Regional Police Directorate of
Thessaly carried out 465 arrests, 79 of them for offences under the drugs laws.
[@1,2] The directorate said its officers cleared 69 drugs cases during the month.
[@1,2] Police seized 129.01 grams of cannabis and 14 cannabis plants across the
region, the directorate said. [@1] The same roundup lists seizures of 14 hunting
guns, one pistol and 820 euros in cash, alongside other drugs, including cocaine
and heroin. [@1]

On 1 October 2026 police in Volos arrested a Greek man for a drugs offence and
seized 10 cultivated cannabis plants and 71.9 grams of raw cannabis. [@1,2] On 2
October police in the Trikala area arrested a Greek man and seized 3 cannabis
plants and cannabis buds and stems, plus raw cannabis, with a total weight of
142.6 grams. [@1,3] On 21 October 2026 police in Larisa arrested a Greek man,
found two purpose-built rooms running hydroponic cannabis cultivation systems,
and seized 145.2 grams of raw cannabis, among other items. [@1,3] On 27 October
2026 two Greek men were arrested in Volos in cases under the drugs and weapons
laws, with cocaine, 427 grams of raw cannabis and a folding knife seized. [@1]

The monthly case list also included a 5 October arrest in Larisa of a man for
attempted homicide, weapons-law offences and violence against officials. [@1]
The roundup gives combined monthly totals for the police directorate and
identifies no individual arrested people by name. [@1]
