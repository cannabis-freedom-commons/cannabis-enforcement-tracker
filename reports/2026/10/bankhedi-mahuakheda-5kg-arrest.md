Bankhedi police arrested two men who were carrying ganja, dried cannabis, on a
motorcycle near Mahuakheda. [@1] The men were identified as Bhagwandas Patwa,
aged 43, and Rajesh Nauriya, aged 33, both from Raisen district in Madhya
Pradesh. [@1] Police said Patwa already had a criminal record and that a case
under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, the NDPS Act, had
previously been registered against him. [@1] The arrest was reported from
Pipariya in Narmadapuram district, the district formerly called Hoshangabad.
[@1]

Officers found 5 kilograms of ganja with the two men, which police valued at
about 50,000 rupees. [@1] Police also seized the motorcycle used to carry the
ganja. [@1] The team, led by station in-charge Vijay Sans, acted on a tip from
an informant and on the directions of Pipariya subdivisional officer of police
Ashish Panwar. [@1] Police said they stopped the riders near Mahuakheda,
searched them and found the ganja, and that they gave an account of the action
on Tuesday evening. [@1]

Investigating sub-inspector M. M. Thakur said a case had been registered against
both men under sections 8 and 20 of the NDPS Act. [@1] Police said they were
questioning the two about where the ganja had come from and about any
trafficking network. [@1]
