Robert L. Carter, 53, of Helena, was arrested after a Jonesboro traffic stop on
27 October 2026 and held on a $200,000 cash or surety bond. [@3] Judge David
Boling found probable cause on 28 October. [@2]

Officer Chaffin stopped Carter's red Chevrolet Cruze in the 600 block of Poplar
Street after he turned from South Culberhouse Drive without signalling,
according to a probable cause affidavit. [@2] Police allege Carter ran after the
officer tried to detain him, and the officer used a Taser. [@2] A police dog was
deployed, and the affidavit alleges Carter fought it before its handler struck
him in the face to stop the struggle. [@2] Carter was treated at St. Bernards
before being taken to the Craighead County Detention Center. [@2]

Carter faces allegations of possessing marijuana, possessing methamphetamine
with intent to deliver and possessing drug paraphernalia, along with resisting
arrest, fleeing, driving on a suspended licence and failing to signal. [@2] The
marijuana, resisting arrest and fleeing counts are misdemeanors; the
methamphetamine and paraphernalia counts are felonies. [@2] The charges have not
been proven in court. [@1]
