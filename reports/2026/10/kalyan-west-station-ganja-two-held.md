Police arrested two Mumbai men near Kalyan West railway station and described
them as ganja smugglers. [@1] They tried to flee as officers moved to surround
them, and police caught them and searched their bags. [@1] They were Amil
Shakeel Mulla, 27, and Ahmad Raza alias Ammo Rashid Qureshi, 18, both residents
of the Backbay Depot area of Dr Ambedkar Nagar, Cuffe Parade, Mumbai. [@1]

A case was registered against both at Kalyan railway police station on the
complaint of Havildar Milind Patil of the anti-narcotics department. [@1] The
bags held pungent buds, fruit, seeds, dried leaves and dried stems, packed into
parcels. [@1] A forensic team called to the scene tested the material and found
it was ganja. [@1] Police valued the seized ganja at Rs 2.86 lakh. [@1] Police
said they were investigating where the two planned to sell the ganja. [@1]
Police Inspector Sachin Kadam and Havildar Ram Jadhav were among those taking
part, along with Ganesh Mahagavkar, Mayur Patil and forensic staff Pooja Londhe
and Anita Sabu. [@1]
