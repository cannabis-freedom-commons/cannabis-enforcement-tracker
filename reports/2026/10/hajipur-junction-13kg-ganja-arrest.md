Police in Vaishali district seized 13.440 kilograms of ganja, the local name for
cannabis, from a Swift car near Hajipur Junction. [@1] Officers also seized two
mobile phones from the car. [@1] Police put the value of the ganja at about 3
lakh rupees. [@1] The arrested man was Ritik Roshan, a resident of the Bidupur
police station area. [@2] Officers stopped and searched the car near a petrol
pump beside the junction, Prabhat Khabar reported. [@2]

The action was carried out under Operation Neelkanth, on the instructions of
Senior Superintendent of Police Shubhank Mishra. [@3] Sadar SDPO-1 Subodh Kumar
said at a press conference that the tip came from the Prohibition and State
Narcotics Control Bureau in Patna on Wednesday evening. [@2] The tip said a
Swift car was carrying a large quantity of ganja around Hajipur Junction. [@2]
Police said Roshan had lived in a rented house in Ramchandra Nagar, Ward 25,
Digghi Kala, and had been trafficking ganja for six months. [@2] He told
officers he bought the drug cheaply in Motihari and other districts and sold it
at higher prices in Vaishali, they said. [@2]

Sadar police station registered a case under the NDPS Act, the Narcotic Drugs
and Psychotropic Substances Act, and Roshan was sent to Hajipur jail in judicial
custody on 29 October. [@2] Police said they were checking the roles of other
people in the network. [@3]
