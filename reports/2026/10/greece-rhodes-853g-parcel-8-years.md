A Dodecanese appeals court sentenced a man to eight years in prison and a
10,000-euro fine on 8 October 2026 over 853 grams of unprocessed cannabis in
a parcel he was collecting from a courier company in Rhodes in September
2021. [@1]

The single-member Court of Appeal for criminal offences of the Dodecanese
found him guilty of drug trafficking in the forms of purchase and possession
by a non-addict. [@1] His appeal has a suspensive effect, so he remained free
after the verdict, under a condition banning him from leaving the country.
[@1]

The case began on 4 September 2021, when narcotics officers at a courier
company, working a parcel flagged by detection dog Schwartz, arrested him at
about 9:30 as he collected the package. [@1] The parcel listed the same man
as both sender and recipient; he told the court he had bought the cannabis in
Attica for his own use and mailed it to himself. [@1] A search of his home
found nothing, and he was released two days later with conditions. [@1] The
trial came more than five years after the arrest. [@1]
