National Police detained five people in El Puerto de Santa María in an
investigation into drug trafficking and money laundering. [@2,4] The investigation
focused on a family group allegedly dedicated to distributing cocaine in the
town. [@2,4]

On the morning of 29 October officers carried out three entries and searches in
different properties in the town. [@2,4] Police dismantled a marijuana plantation
in El Puerto during the operation. [@1] According to the police account,
officers seized about 105,25 grams of rock cocaine and four wraps holding
another 3,82 grams. [@3,4] Officers also seized about 350 grams of hashish, the
cannabis component recorded here. [@2,4] Officers also seized ecstasy tablets
weighing 39 grams of pink tablets and 19 grams of beige tablets. [@2,4]
Investigators found two precision scales, a vacuum-packing machine, packing
bags, plastic cuttings, notebooks with notes and other documents for analysis.
[@2] The operation also recovered 3,840 euros in cash and three vehicles
allegedly linked to the activity. [@2,4]

A court froze five bank accounts linked to the detainees as part of a
money-laundering inquiry. [@2,4] The operation was run jointly by the Local
Narcotics Group and the Economic and Fiscal Crime Unit of the El Puerto de Santa
María station with UDYCO I officers from the Cádiz provincial station. [@2]
