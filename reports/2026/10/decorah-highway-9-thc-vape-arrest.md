Around midday on 30 October 2026, Decorah police responded to a disabled vehicle
on Highway 9 near Montgomery Street in Decorah. [@1] Officers spoke there with
the driver, 19-year-old Kervens Kerlegrand of Postville, and his passenger about
what to do with the vehicle. [@1] While speaking with them, officers reported
detecting the odor of marijuana coming from inside the vehicle. [@1] Police said
Kerlegrand was found in possession of a THC vape. [@1] A search of the vehicle
found additional THC vapes under the driver's seat. [@1]

Officers arrested Kerlegrand and took him to the Winneshiek County Jail on a
first-offense charge of possession of a controlled substance (marijuana). [@1]
The passenger was given a ride to the Decorah police station to wait for a
licensed driver to collect him. [@1] After the search, Don's Towing removed the
vehicle from the scene. [@1]

Those arrested are presumed innocent until proven guilty in court. [@1] The
release described first-offense possession as a serious misdemeanor in Iowa
carrying up to one year in jail and a fine of $430 to $2,560. [@1] A second
local account confirms officers were sent to the disabled vehicle near Highway 9
and Montgomery Street on 30 October and that THC vape products were found in the
vehicle. [@2] It likewise says he was booked on a first-offense possession of a
controlled substance (marijuana) charge. [@2]
