Gendarmerie teams destroyed 48,579 cannabis plants across the Yenişehir, Eğil and Sur districts of Diyarbakır, Türkiye’s Interior Ministry said on 22 October 2026. [@1,2,3] It reported that 230 personnel in 24 teams took part and that drones and remote sensing helped them identify 35 suspected cultivation sites. [@1,2,3]

The Diyarbakır and Eğil prosecutors coordinated the operations with the gendarmerie’s narcotics division. [@1,2] The ministry said the operation formed part of its plan against illegal cannabis cultivation. [@1] TRT Haber published a photograph of burning vegetation, and Batman Rehber Gazetesi published video coverage of the operation. [@2,3]

The ministry estimated that the plants could have yielded 1.2 tonnes of cannabis. [@1,2,3] It separately reported the seizure of 36 kilograms of cannabis and gave no arrest count. [@1,3]
