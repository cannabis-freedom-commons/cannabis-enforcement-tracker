Police in the Pikine district of Saint-Louis stopped a yellow and black urban taxi at
about 2 am on 4 October 2026 and found 19 blocks of cannabis, one kilogram each, according
to the police account reported by Seneweb. [@1,2] Officers had watched the area around the
Daral livestock market after an anonymous tip that cannabis was being sold there. [@1,2]

The two men in the taxi were O. Ndiaye, 48, who said he was a trader, and M. Ndiaye, 43,
a taxi driver; both lived in Pikine. [@1,2] O. Ndiaye admitted owning the drug, which he
said he had bought in Mpal from a man identified as C.S. for 500,000 CFA francs. [@1,2]

The men were held in police custody and taken before the prosecutor at the Saint-Louis
court on 6 October, on charges of criminal association and offering and supplying Indian
hemp. [@1,2] The prosecutor asked for a judicial investigation, and the men were returned
to custody pending an appearance before an investigating judge, who was to decide whether
to indict them. [@1,2]
