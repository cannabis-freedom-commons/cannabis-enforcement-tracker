Officers of Tonk district's District Special Team (DST), a district police unit,
raided a site in Dhunwakala, in the Ghad police station area, after a tip-off
from an informer. [@1] The team seized 45 green cannabis plants at the site,
which police put at a total weight of 35 kg. [@1]

The plants were growing at the site, which the headline describes as a field.
[@1]

The operation led to the arrest of one person. [@1]
