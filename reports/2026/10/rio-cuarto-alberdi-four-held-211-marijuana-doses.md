Córdoba's Fuerza Policial Antinarcotráfico detained four people in Río Cuarto
after a two-month investigation directed by the provincial prosecution service.
[@2] Officers raided a house on calle Montevideo al 1200 in barrio Alberdi, Río
Cuarto. [@2] Those detained were a father, two of his children and a friend of
one of them. [@1]

Officers seized 1,012 doses of cocaine and 211 doses of marijuana, alongside
cash, a precision digital scale and drug-portioning items. [@2] The marijuana
component is recorded only as 211 doses, with no weight, plant count or value
published. [@1] Investigators said the older son and his friend sold drugs from
the house while the younger son made delivery rounds. [@2]

Investigators described the group as a family retail-drug organisation selling
from the house and by home delivery. [@1] The prosecutors said the father, who
had a criminal record, employed his sons in the drug sales. [@2] The case began
with neighbours' calls to the anonymous-complaints line 0800-888-8080. [@1] The
Río Cuarto anti-narcotics prosecutor ordered the four transferred to judicial
custody, accused of breaking National Narcotics Law 23.737. [@3]
