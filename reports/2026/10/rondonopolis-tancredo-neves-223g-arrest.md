Military police from the Força Tática, the Military Police's tactical unit,
arrested a woman on suspicion of drug trafficking and resisting arrest in the
Tancredo Neves neighbourhood of Rondonópolis, in Mato Grosso, in the late
afternoon of Friday 30 October 2026, logging the incident at about 5.55pm. [@1]
Officers were patrolling the area after reports that drugs were being sold
there, mainly after 5pm. [@1] On Rua das Codornas they saw a woman apparently
handing an object to a man on a motorcycle, and the rider fled as they
approached. [@1] The woman also tried to escape on foot, threw away a plastic
package and tried to damage her own mobile phone. [@1] During the containment
she struggled physically against a female officer, according to the police
record, and the team used control techniques and pepper spray to stop the
resistance. [@2]

The package she dropped held marijuana, and a search of the house found six more
portions wrapped individually plus a larger one, for about 223 grams in all.
[@1] A female officer's body search found 21 portions of cocaine hidden in the
suspect's bra, totalling about 20 grams. [@1] Police also seized R$38.50 in cash
from the kitchen table. [@1] A three-year-old child found inside the house was
left in the care of a tutelary counsellor from the Conselho Tutelar, the
municipal child-protection council. [@1] Three mobile phones were also seized,
one with the suspect at the time of the approach and two found inside the house.
[@3]

The woman was taken with the drugs and the money to the 1st Police Station for
the legal procedures. [@1] The motorcyclist was not found, even after officers
followed him. [@3] The case is to be investigated by the civil police. [@3]
