Gardai carried out three searches at locations within a short distance of
Mullingar town. [@1] One person was charged in connection with the first seizure
and is due to appear before Mullingar District Court in the coming weeks. [@1]

The first search on October 30 found suspected cocaine with an approximate
street value of 500 euros, pending analysis. [@1] A second search on October 31
uncovered cocaine worth about 1,200 euros along with a small quantity of
cannabis. [@1] A third search on the same date seized cannabis estimated to be
worth 400 euros and cocaine worth about 200 euros. [@1] Gardai put the combined
approximate value of the drugs seized across the three searches at 2,300 euros.
[@1]

All three searches took place in the Mullingar area during searches on October
30 and 31. [@1] The seizures form part of ongoing garda activity targeting the
possession and supply of controlled drugs in the area. [@1]
