The Nianing proximity brigade of Senegal's gendarmerie carried out a drug
operation on Nianing beach on 30 October 2026. [@1] Gendarmes moved in after a
tip-off about suspicious parcels on the beach and searched the area. [@1] The
seized cannabis was secured at the unit's premises for judicial proceedings.
[@1] Authorities opened an investigation to trace the supply chain, identify the
sponsors and clarify how the consignment arrived by sea. [@1]

Officers seized a total of 205 kg of cannabis, reported as chanvre indien. [@1]
The load comprised five 30 kg parcels, one 35 kg parcel and 20 smaller packets
together weighing 20 kg. [@1] Officers found six parcels and 20 packets in total
during the search. [@2]

The find was made on the beach immediately beside a large hotel complex,
identified as the RIU Baobab. [@2] The gendarmerie described the action as part
of its continuing drive against criminal networks and drug traffickers on the
Senegalese coast. [@1] It urged the public to stay vigilant and to share
information through its free 24-hour call centre on 800 00 20 20 and 123. [@1]
