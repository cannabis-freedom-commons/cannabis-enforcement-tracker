City Kotwali police in Muzaffarnagar arrested two alleged ganja smugglers. [@1]
The arrested men were identified as 29-year-old Abdul Mannan of Khadarwala and
25-year-old Javed alias Allu of Kassawan. [@1] Javed alias Allu already faces
several cases, including under the excise and Arms Acts. [@1]

Police recovered 5 kg 550 grams of illegal ganja from the two men's possession.
[@1] Police put the estimated value of the seized ganja at about Rs 2.75 lakh.
[@1] Officers stopped and searched the two suspects as they came from the
Niajupura side during a vehicle check near the Kali Nadi bridge on the
Charthawal–Niajupura road on 31 October. [@1]

Police are now examining the accused's network, tracing forward and backward
linkages to find where the ganja was supplied from and to whom it was sold. [@1]
City Kotwali station in-charge Brajesh Kumar Sharma and sub-inspector Shashi
Kapoor were part of the operation. [@1]
