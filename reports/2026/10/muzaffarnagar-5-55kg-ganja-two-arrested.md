City Kotwali police in Muzaffarnagar arrested two alleged ganja smugglers. [@1,2,3,4]
The arrested men were identified as 29-year-old Abdul Mannan of Khadarwala and
25-year-old Javed alias Allu of Kassawan. [@1,2,3] Javed alias Allu already faces
several cases, including under the excise and Arms Acts. [@1]

Police recovered 5 kg 550 grams of illegal ganja from the two men's possession.
[@1,2,3,4] Police put the estimated value of the seized ganja at about Rs 2.75 lakh.
[@1,4] Officers stopped and searched the two suspects as they came from the
Niajupura side during a vehicle check near the Kali Nadi bridge on the
Charthawal–Niajupura road on 31 October. [@1,2,3,4]

Police are now examining the accused's network, tracing forward and backward
linkages to find where the ganja was supplied from and to whom it was sold. [@1]
City Kotwali station in-charge Brajesh Kumar Sharma and sub-inspector Shashi
Kapoor were part of the operation. [@1]

The two men allegedly bought ganja in Meerut and supplied it in Muzaffarnagar, including to young people in contact with them. [@2] Abdul Mannan held 2 kg 50 grams and Javed alias Allu held 3 kg 500 grams of the seized ganja. [@2] Police registered a case under the NDPS Act and sent both accused to jail at about 3 p.m. on Sunday. [@2,3] Javed alias Allu had earlier excise and Arms Act cases, including two 2016 cases at Jansath police station and 2017 and 2023 Arms Act cases at City Kotwali police station. [@2]

Sub-inspector Devpal Singh was assigned the investigation of the case. [@3] Abdul Mannan is the son of Sarfaraz and Javed alias Allu is the son of Ikram. [@3,4]

Kotwali Nagar police registered Crime No. 487/2026 against the two accused under Sections 8/20 of the NDPS Act. [@4]
