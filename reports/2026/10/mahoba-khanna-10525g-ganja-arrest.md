Police at the Khanna police station in Mahoba district, Uttar Pradesh, arrested
two men during a vehicle check and recovered 10 kilograms 525 grams of dry ganja
from them. [@1] Ganja is the Hindi term for cannabis, and the material recovered
here was described as dry, the form usually sold as flower. [@1]

The outlet presents the two as traffickers smuggling cannabis to supply it in
Mahoba, according to its headline account of the arrest. [@1] It identifies the
men as bringing the ganja into Mahoba from Odisha, the eastern Indian state that
supplies much of the ganja moved into northern India. [@1] The same headline
describes the cannabis as hidden inside a trolley bag, a wheeled travel bag of
the kind used for clothing. [@1] The whole consignment weighed 10.525 kilograms,
the amount the headline rounds to more than 10 kilograms. [@1]
