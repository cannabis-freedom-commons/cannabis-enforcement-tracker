Police in Thanamalwila arrested a 57-year-old man after finding 754 kilograms of
dried cannabis in a room of his house in the Arambekema area, Hiru News and TTV
News reported on 16 and 17 October 2026. [@1,2]

The man, known by the alias "Bathalahin Mahaththaya", had prepared the stock for
sale, the police said. [@1,2] Officers searched the house on a tip-off. [@1,2]

Police valued the cannabis at nearly 100 million rupees. [@1,2] An investigation
into the suspect's assets was also under way, and several properties belonging to
him had previously been frozen, TTV News reported. [@2] Preliminary investigations
indicated he was connected to a large-scale cannabis trade, the police said. [@2]

He was to be produced before the Wellawaya Magistrate's Court, and Thanamalwila
police were continuing the investigation. [@1,2]
