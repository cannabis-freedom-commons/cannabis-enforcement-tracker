Saintes police stopped a man and the woman accompanying him on 8 October 2026
after judging their behaviour suspicious. [@1] The man was smoking a joint when
he admitted that his bag contained drugs. [@1] Placed in custody, the man
admitted to investigators that he acted as a dealer. [@1] A court sentenced him
to three years in firm prison with an immediate committal warrant. [@1] He was
also banned from appearing in Charente-Maritime. [@1]

Police seized 130 g of cocaine, 50 ecstasy tablets, 5 LSD blotters and 870 g of
heroin from his bag. [@1] The woman beside him had five ecstasy tablets on her.
[@1] A search of his car on 23 September also recovered heroin alongside 10 g of
cocaine, 230 g of cannabis resin, 200 g of herbal cannabis, crack pipes and
4,730 euros in cash. [@1] The cannabis part of the car seizure was 230 grams of
resin and 200 grams of herb. [@2]

Saintes police made two drug seizures targeting the same man within fifteen
days. [@1] Public-security officers made the 8 October stop as they crossed the
streets of Saintes. [@2] The earlier interception was made by the night brigade
while the man was driving a car. [@2] The criminal court ordered his
imprisonment for three years with immediate committal and a ban from
Charente-Maritime. [@2]
