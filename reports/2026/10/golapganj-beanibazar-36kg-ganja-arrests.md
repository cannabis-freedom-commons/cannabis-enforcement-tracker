Special Response Battalion-9 detained a man and a woman with a total of 36 kg of
ganja in separate raids in Golapganj and Beanibazar, Sylhet, on 30 October 2026.
[@1] The detainees were identified as 27-year-old Foysal Ahmed of Dakshinbhag
and 30-year-old Suhada Begum of Uttar Dubag in Beanibazar. [@1]

In Golapganj, officers acting on secret information raided a dwelling in
Dakshinbhag Talipara after a man tried to flee on seeing them, then recovered 32
kg of ganja from two white plastic sacks kept under a bed on his information.
[@1] In a separate raid in Uttar Dubag, Beanibazar, at about 3:50 pm the same
day, officers detained Suhada Begum with 4 kg of ganja. [@1]

SRB-9 media officer Additional Superintendent of Police K.M. Shahidul Islam
Sohag confirmed the raids on 31 October. [@1] The detainees allegedly told
initial interrogators that they had collected the ganja from Sylhet's border
area and kept it for sale. [@1] Cases were filed against them under the
Narcotics Control Act, and they and the seized ganja were handed over to the
concerned police stations. [@1] SRB-9 said its intelligence work and anti-drug
operations were continuing. [@1]
