On the evening of 30 October 2026, Mori police were conducting checking near
Phaphrala Khad by the Jakhol-Sankri crossing on the Mori-Sankri road. [@1]
During that checking, the police stopped 44-year-old Kishan Maurya in suspicious
circumstances, searched him and arrested one man with illegal charas. [@1] The
arrested man is Kishan Maurya, son of late Ram Dulare, a native of Lalpur
village under Binawar police station in Dataganj, Badaun district of Uttar
Pradesh, who was living in Rajpur, Dehradun. [@1] Police registered a case
against him at Mori police station under sections 8/20 of the NDPS Act. [@1]

The search recovered 1 kg 223 g of charas, cannabis resin, and police put its
estimated value at about Rs 2.40 lakh. [@1] The drive was conducted under the
direction of Superintendent of Police Kamlesh Upadhyay, with supervision by
Deputy Superintendent of Police Barkot Chanchal Sharma and leadership by Mori
station house officer Deepak Rawat. [@1] The police team comprised station house
officer Deepak Rawat, constable Ganesh Rana and constable Anil Tomar. [@1]

A second account confirms that the Mori police team arrested 44-year-old Kishan
Maurya during checking near Phaphrala Khad by the Jakhol-Sankri crossing on the
Mori-Sankri road with 1 kg 223 g of illegal charas. [@2] That account records
the same recovery of 1 kg 223 g of charas with an estimated value of Rs 2.40
lakh. [@2]
