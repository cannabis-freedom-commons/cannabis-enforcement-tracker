NDLEA operatives arrested a 30-year-old suspect at Ozoro in Isoko North Local
Government Area of Delta State on Saturday, October 31, 2026. [@1] Officers
recovered 10.54 kilograms of skunk, a potent cannabis product, and 9.4 kilograms
of cannabis seeds at the arrest. [@1] The same arrest also produced 4.3 grams of
methamphetamine, which is not cannabis and is left out of the cannabis figures.
[@1]

The arrest was one of several cannabis operations the National Drug Law
Enforcement Agency (NDLEA) reported in a statement issued by its Director of
Media and Advocacy, Femi Babafemi, on Sunday, November 8, 2026. [@1] The same
statement covered seizures and arrests at Lagos airport, at courier companies
and at cannabis farms across several states. [@1] NDLEA chairman Brigadier
General Mohamed Buba Marwa (retd.) commended officers of the Delta command among
others and urged them to keep up the agency's drug supply and demand reduction
work. [@1]

In a separate Delta State case, another suspect was arrested at Upper Agbarho in
the Ughelli area on November 4 with quantities of skunk and other drugs but no
stated weight for the skunk. [@1] The roundup's headline case, a Ugandan woman
caught at Lagos airport with 87 wraps of cocaine weighing 1.15 kilograms, is not
cannabis and is left out of this report. [@1]
