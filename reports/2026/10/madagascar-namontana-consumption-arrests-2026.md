Police arrested 19 men and seized 142 sachets of cannabis in Namontana, Antananarivo. [@1,2]

Police from Antananarivo's rapid intervention unit (UIR) arrested 19 men in Namontana on the morning
of Friday 16 October 2026 during a patrol of the Namontana and Anosibe sectors. [@1,2] The patrol
moved in after an informant told them that a group of men gathered every day in a secluded spot in
Namontana to consume cannabis. [@1] Midi Madagasikara reported that several men tried to flee but
that 19 were caught while consuming the drug. [@2]

The officers seized 142 prepared sachets of cannabis and two walkie-talkies. [@1,2] Midi
Madagasikara reported that the devices were believed to have been used by a lookout to warn the
group when police approached. [@2] Residents had complained to the police that the men were also
suspected of armed robberies and assaults in the area. [@1,2]

The 19 were taken to the Service des unités territoriales (SUT), the territorial units service, for
the investigation. [@2] Midi Madagasikara reported that the inquiry was to establish what each
suspect may have done and how the equipment was used. [@2]
