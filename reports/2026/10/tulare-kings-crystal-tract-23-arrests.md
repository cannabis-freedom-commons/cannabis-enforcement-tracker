Twenty-three people were arrested on 29 October 2026 in a coordinated takedown
across Tulare and Kings counties, California. [@2] The takedown involved 22
search-warrant locations, six federal arrest warrants and 20 state warrants
executed by local, state and federal agencies. [@1] Operation Crystal Tract
began in June 2025 when Tulare County Sheriff's Office detectives began
targeting drug traffickers in the area. [@1] The early phase focused on Fidel
Vasquez and Xaiver Medrano of Tulare. [@1] By March 2026 the focus expanded to
Juan Plascencia, also known as Bronco, of Visalia and his brother Gustavo
Plascencia. [@1] Over the following months detectives identified three separate
drug trafficking organizations, numerous suspected associates and three
potential stash-house locations across the two counties. [@1]

Officers seized 11 pounds of marijuana, about 4.99 kilograms, as the cannabis
component of the operation. [@1] The same haul also included 17 firearms, 3
pounds of meth, 1 kilogram of fentanyl, 36 grams of cocaine, 96 Xanax pills, 9
grams of mushrooms, 30 ounces of the hallucinogenic drug DMT and $100,000 in
cash. [@2]

The Tulare County Sheriff's Office led the investigation with the Drug
Enforcement Administration, Federal Bureau of Investigation, Homeland Security
Investigations, U.S. Marshals Service, California Department of Corrections and
Rehabilitation, Visalia, Porterville and Tulare police departments and the Kings
County Sheriff's Office. [@2] Detectives said the suspects had connections to
larger drug trafficking networks operating throughout the Central Valley. [@1]
