A man was arrested on the night of Monday, 26 October, on suspicion of drug
trafficking during a Military Police operation in Jardim San Rafael, Arapongas.
[@1] According to the Military Police, officers recorded the incident at about
23:27 on Rua Beija-Flor Marrom. [@1] The patrol was described as a routine
police effort to combat the trade in narcotics in the surrounding area. [@1]

Officers said they saw two individuals who behaved suspiciously and tried to
flee when they noticed the approaching police vehicle, prompting the officers to
stop them. [@1] A personal search found one of the suspects carrying 30 cannabis
portions, with a combined weight of 64 grams, already packaged and prepared for
sale. [@1]

Officers also seized R$171 in cash in addition to the cannabis. [@1] The man was
then taken to the 5th Central de Flagrantes in Arapongas, where he remained at
the disposal of the police authority for further procedures. [@1]
