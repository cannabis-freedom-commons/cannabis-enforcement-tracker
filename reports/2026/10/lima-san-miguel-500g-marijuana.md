Police arrested three suspected members of two criminal gangs in operations in
Ventanilla, in the constitutional province of Callao, and in San Miguel, Lima,
the Interior Ministry said. [@2] In Ventanilla, officers of the Depincri, the
criminal investigation division, detained Cristopher Rodríguez Palomino, 22,
alias 'El Nero Cris', and Jeanfranco Fernández Reyes, 19, alias 'Mille',
described as members of 'Los Michis del Cono Norte'. [@2] The ministry said the
two were stopped in a white car on Avenida Néstor Gambetta and that officers
found a revolver loaded with five rounds, six more rounds and three explosive
charges with safety fuses in a bag under the driver's seat. [@2]

In San Miguel, police arrested Jesús Ever Morán Villanueva, 20, alias 'Yeruza',
accused of belonging to 'Los Malditos de la Ruta'. [@2] The ministry said the
arrest took place at the intersection of Avenida La Paz and Jirón Santa Rosa,
where he was beside a motorcycle. [@2] Officers seized a pistol, a magazine,
five 9 mm rounds, a mobile phone and a bag with about 500 grams of marijuana,
the ministry said. [@2] Panamericana Televisión reported that one of the
detainees was travelling on a motorcycle that may have been used in the 20
October 2026 extortion attack on the chicken restaurant 'El Corralito'. [@1] The
ministry said the motorcycle's link to the attack rested on security-camera
images. [@2]

RPP reported that Morán Villanueva was also linked to an armed attack on Avenida
La Paz in La Perla on 28 September 2026 that wounded a minibus driver and a
passenger. [@3] The ministry said the three detainees were left with the
corresponding police units to continue the proceedings. [@2]
