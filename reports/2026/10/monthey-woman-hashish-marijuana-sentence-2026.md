The district court of Monthey in Valais sentenced a 54-year-old Portuguese woman
to three years in prison for drug trafficking, a rare prison term for a woman in
the canton's drug cases. [@1] Two of the three years are suspended over a
four-year probation period, leaving one year to serve, reduced by the slightly
more than 10 months she had already spent in pre-trial detention, and she was
also banished from Switzerland for five years. [@1]

At her trial on 27 October she accepted the public prosecutor's offer to settle
the case through a simplified procedure. [@1] Between January and December 2025
she sold 870 grams of cocaine for a profit of 42,550 Swiss francs. [@1] In the
same period the woman from the Chablais region sold between 100 and 200 grams of
hashish and 300 grams of marijuana, earning a total of 2,000 Swiss francs. [@1]
She sold the cocaine to about twenty users and the prosecution said the drug
sales financed her own consumption and other personal needs. [@2] She was
arrested and taken into custody on 17 December 2025, and the hashish and
marijuana were sold up to that point. [@2] The prosecutor, Emmanuelle Raboud,
argued that the accused knew she was endangering or could endanger a large
number of people, and the court tried her for a felony, a misdemeanour and a
contravention under the federal narcotics law. [@2] She had also consumed the
banned products herself, which brought the contravention charge under the
federal narcotics law. [@2] By accepting the simplified procedure she gave up
any possible appeal. [@2]

According to a cantonal police statistic, about 10% of the drug-trafficking
cases broken up in Valais involve women. [@1]
