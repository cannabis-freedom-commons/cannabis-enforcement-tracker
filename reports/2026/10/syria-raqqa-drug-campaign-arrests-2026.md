Syria's Interior Ministry announced a security campaign against drug dealers and
users in the al-Badu, al-Mashlab and al-Ramila neighbourhoods of Raqqa
governorate. [@1] The ministry said the campaign resulted in the arrest of
several people involved and the confiscation of equipment used to take drugs.
[@1]

The Raqqa campaign was announced as part of the ministry's October 2026 results,
in which it said it dismantled 30 drug-trafficking networks. [@1] In the same
monthly tally the ministry reported seizing 582 kilograms of hashish, the
cannabis resin tracked here. [@1] The ministry said it also foiled attempts to
smuggle 400,000 captagon pills through border crossings, a non-cannabis seizure
recorded only to explain the mixed tally. [@1]

The ministry published the Raqqa campaign among other October results, including
arrests of wanted people, weapons seizures and more than 1.77 million civil
transactions, without giving a date for the campaign. [@1]
