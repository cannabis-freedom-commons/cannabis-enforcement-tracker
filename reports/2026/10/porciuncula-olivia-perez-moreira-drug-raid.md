A joint anti-trafficking operation took place on 20 October 2026 in the Olívia
Pérez Moreira neighborhood of Porciúncula, in Rio de Janeiro state. [@2] The
operation involved Porciúncula's Municipal Secretariat of Public Order,
Municipal Civil Guard and Military Police. [@2] Municipal authorities acted on
gathered information and received support from the 29th Military Police
Battalion. [@3] Five suspects were taken to the 139th Police Precinct, where two
remained under arrest. [@3]

The drugs seized included marijuana, cocaine and hashish. [@1] Teams searched a
residence and seized precision scales and other materials alongside the drugs.
[@3] Police said the five suspects were found on a veranda in circumstances
suggesting the possible preparation of drugs for commercial sale. [@2] The two
remained at the disposal of the courts pending a custody hearing. [@2]

Porciúncula's coordinator for Public Safety Integration, Andreson Ramos Prevato,
said coordination among security forces was fundamental to combating trafficking
and improving public safety. [@2]
