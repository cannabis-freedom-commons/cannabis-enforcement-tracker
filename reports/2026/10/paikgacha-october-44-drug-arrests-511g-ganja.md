Police in Paikgacha upazila, Khulna district, say they arrested 44 suspected
drug dealers during raids across the upazila in October 2026. [@1]

Officers seized 511 grams of ganja from the people arrested. [@1] The same
operations also recovered 352 yaba tablets, two and a half litres of Bangla
cholai liquor and four pieces of drug-laced syrup. [@1] Police filed 10 regular
narcotics cases, 18 summary-trial cases and seven non-FIR prosecution cases
under the Narcotics Control Act for the month. [@1]

Twenty of those arrested were sent to court in regular cases. [@1] In the 18
summary-trial cases, a summary trial being a fast-track hearing before a
magistrate, officers detained another 24 people with drugs, produced them before
the court, and the accused received sentences of various terms. [@1] Another 20
people were sent to court in the seven non-FIR prosecution cases and were
sentenced and fined. [@1]

Officer-in-Charge Sheikh Abdul Auwal Kabir confirmed the figures. [@1] He said
the sweeps followed directions from Khulna Range Deputy Inspector General Md
Rezaul Karim and district Superintendent of Police Sarkar Omar Faruk. [@1] Kabir
said the drive against drugs, theft, extortion, gambling and other crime would
continue. [@1]

Subornovumi reported the same October figures for Paikgacha: 44 people arrested
and 511 grams of ganja recovered with yaba, liquor and syrup. [@2]

The second report restates that 20 of those arrested were sent to court in
regular cases and 24 were arrested in the 18 summary-trial cases. [@2]
