U.S. Park Police took 10 people into custody after raiding a block near Mellon
Street SE in Southeast Washington on Wednesday night. [@1] Officers executed a
search warrant in the area of Mellon Street SE at about 7:45 p.m. [@1] Officers
took all suspects into custody without incident. [@2] Police have not disclosed
the names, ages, or any charges facing the 10 people taken into custody. [@1]

Officers seized large quantities of marijuana along with cocaine, PCP, fentanyl
and MDMA. [@1] Police put the combined street value of the narcotics haul at
more than $100,000. [@2] Officers also recovered 10 firearms from the scene,
three of which had been reported stolen. [@2]

No separate weight or valuation was published for the marijuana portion of the
seizure. [@1] The reports do not indicate whether the suspects have appeared in
court or what comes next in the case. [@1]
