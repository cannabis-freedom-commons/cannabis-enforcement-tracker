The Lebanese Army command's Directorate of Orientation announced on 26
October 2026 that a unit had destroyed about 180 dunums (18 hectares) of
cannabis in the Jabab al-Hamr area of Hermel, in the Baalbek-Hermel
governorate. [@1] The army estimated the seized crop at about 60 tonnes. [@1]

The Sobhiye newsletter, summarising the announcement the next day, confirmed
the 180-dunum, 60-tonne figures for the Hermel destruction. [@2]
