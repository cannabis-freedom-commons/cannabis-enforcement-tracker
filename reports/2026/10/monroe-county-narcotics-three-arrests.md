Three people were arrested in a narcotics investigation in Monroe County,
Mississippi, in late October 2026. [@1] The Monroe County Sheriff's Department
said deputies with the Monroe County Narcotics and Major Narcotics Unit and
agents from the Mississippi Bureau of Narcotics made the arrests while
investigating the sale of illegal drugs in the county. [@1] Vadale Lashae Lucas,
33, of Aberdeen, was arrested on two felony warrants for marijuana possession.
[@1]

While serving the warrants at Lucas's residence, investigators found additional
illegal drugs. [@1] Lucas now faces two additional felony charges, for
possession of ecstasy and possession of marijuana. [@1] Investigators reported
finding nearly one pound (about 0.45 kg) of marijuana. [@1] Authorities also
seized several thousand dollars in cash from his residence. [@1] Lucas remained
in the Monroe County Jail awaiting bond. [@1]

Jennifer Latrice Wilson, 37, of Aberdeen, was arrested on a charge of selling
methamphetamine and was out on a $20,000 bond. [@1] Andrew Jamar Devauld, 30, of
Amory, was arrested on three counts of selling methamphetamine and was out on a
$25,000 bond. [@1] The investigation remained ongoing. [@1]
