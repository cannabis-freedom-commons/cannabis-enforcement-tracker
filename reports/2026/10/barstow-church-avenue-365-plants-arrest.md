San Bernardino County sheriff's deputies arrested 36-year-old Jose Luis Baez
Burgos, a resident of Mexico, on suspicion of marijuana cultivation and theft of
utilities after an investigation on October 16, 2026. [@1] Burgos was booked
into the Barstow Jail. [@1] County booking records cited by a second outlet
identify him as a 36-year-old Mexican national arrested on suspicion of theft of
utilities and say he was released on bail pending legal proceedings. [@2]

Deputies from the Barstow Station had responded at about 10:25 p.m. to the 27000
block of Church Avenue after a report of a man operating a lift on a power line
without warning lights or traffic cones. [@1] During the investigation deputies
found freshly cut electrical wire and what appeared to be an electrical bypass
from a power line on Church Street routed to the property. [@1] Inside a vacant
residence deputies saw fertilizer, air conditioning systems, lighting equipment
and electrical lines running into individual rooms, which authorities said were
consistent with a marijuana cultivation operation. [@1] Investigators also found
a tampered electrical panel and power lines running through a wall to a detached
garage with a strong odor of marijuana coming from the garage. [@1]

The sheriff's marijuana enforcement team served a search warrant at the property
and investigators located approximately 365 marijuana plants, described by the
second outlet as uprooted pot plants. [@1] Southern California Edison personnel
confirmed the electrical service had been bypassed and electricity was being
stolen. [@1] Burgos was arrested on suspicion of violating California Penal Code
498(b)(1) for theft of utilities and Health and Safety Code 11358(c) for
marijuana cultivation. [@1] Authorities gave no estimated value for the stolen
electricity or the marijuana plants and no harvested cannabis weight was
reported. [@1]
