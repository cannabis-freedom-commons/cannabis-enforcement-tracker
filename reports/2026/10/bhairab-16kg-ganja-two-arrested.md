An operational team of Special Response Battalion-14, CPC-2, Bhairab camp
arrested two women with 16 kg of ganja in Bhairab, Kishoreganj. [@1] The
arrested women were identified as 42-year-old Farida Begum and 18-year-old
Ayesha Akhtar. [@1] Both women are from Kishoreganj district. [@1] Officers
arrested them around 2:30 pm on Friday, 30 October, on the Dhaka-Sylhet highway
on the east side of the Syed Nazrul Islam Bridge. [@1]

Acting on a secret tip, the SRB-14, CPC-2, Kishoreganj operational team set up a
special checkpoint on the highway and searched a passenger leguna, detaining the
two women travelling dressed as passengers. [@1] In the presence of witnesses,
officers recovered 16 kg of ganja concealed in plastic and cloth bags hidden
under their seats. [@1] Officers also seized one mobile phone and 2,060 taka in
cash from them. [@1]

The SRB said the two women had long collected drugs from various border areas
and sold them in Kishoreganj and other places. [@1] The detainees were handed
over to Bhairab Police Station with the recovered ganja, phone and cash for
further legal action. [@1]
