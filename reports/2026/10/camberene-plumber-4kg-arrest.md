A 25-year-old plumber was arrested at his home in the Frontière neighbourhood
of Cambérène 1, Dakar, at about 10.30 at night. [@1] He lives in Cambérène and
was picked up during the night of 30 October 2026, the day Senego published
its report. [@1]

A search of his room found four kilos of Indian hemp, divided into four
yellow sachets and hidden in a red bag. [@1] Questioned by police, the
suspect admitted the facts and said he was acting for his own account,
according to the Kawtef account cited by Senego. [@1]

## Referral to the prosecutor

He was placed in custody for offering or transferring Indian hemp and was then
referred to the public prosecutor at Dakar's Tribunal de Grande Instance Hors
Classe. [@1] He named a supplier, identified only as M., living in Casamance,
and an intermediary called "N." living in Ngor. [@1]
