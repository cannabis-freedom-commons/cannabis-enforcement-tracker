Drug-control officers serving the Illichivskyi district of Mariupol, together
with colleagues from the Mariupol city police department, detained a 27-year-old
man while following up operational intelligence. [@1] The man is suspected of
attempted illegal sale of narcotic drugs. [@1] Investigators opened a criminal
case against him for attempted illegal sale of drugs on a large scale. [@1]

During an inspection of the man's home, officers seized two plastic bags holding
160 foil-paper wraps and a block with an unknown substance. [@1] Officers also
seized precision scales, a folding knife and two blades bearing traces of an
unknown substance. [@1] An expert examination found that the seized substance,
weighing about 150 grams in total, was hashish. [@1] The account notes that
circulation of hashish is prohibited in the Russian Federation. [@1]

The case was brought under Part 3 of Article 30 and item (g) of Part 4 of
Article 228.1 of the Russian Criminal Code. [@1] The offence carries a maximum
penalty of up to 20 years' imprisonment with a fine of up to 1 million roubles.
