Vizianagaram district police detained Sigili Venkatesh, 29, of Rangappavalasa
village in Therlam mandal, under the PD Act, the state's preventive-detention
law, and moved him to Visakhapatnam Central Jail on 31 October 2026. [@1]
District superintendent of police A.R. Damodar said Bobbili Rural circle
inspector Mallikarjuna Rao initiated the detention because Venkatesh had
repeatedly smuggled ganja, or cannabis, and broken the law. [@1] The
superintendent said Venkatesh was taken into custody and would be held without
bail for up to a year. [@1]

Police said two ganja cases were already registered against him, one at Therlam
police station and one within Budarayavalasa police station limits. [@1] They
said 16.570 kg of ganja was seized from him in those two cases. [@1]

Damodar warned that police would take strict legal action against people
repeatedly caught smuggling or selling ganja in the district. [@1] He said
police would not spare anyone who disturbed public order. [@1] The department
would keep acting to stop activities that threatened public safety, he said.
