A 40-year-old man, Mohammad Zakir Hossain, also known as Shaheen, was detained
after a special team from Lakshmipur's District Detective Branch (DB), the
district's detective police unit, found one cannabis plant on the roof of his
building. [@1] The officers acted on confidential information in Sonapur, Ward 1
of Ramganj municipality, at about 12:30 a.m. on Saturday, 31 October 2026. [@1]

The search took place on the third-floor roof of an old, unfinished building
described as belonging to the suspect's paternal family. [@1] The plant was
growing in a pot, had green leaves and branches, and stood about five feet tall.
[@1] Officers recovered it at the scene and detained Hossain there. [@1] Hossain
lived in Ward 1 of Ramganj and was the son of the late Sekandar Mia and Sajeda
Begum. [@1]

Lakshmipur Additional Superintendent of Police Hossain Mohammad Raihan Kazemi
said a case against the detained man was in the process of being filed. [@1]
