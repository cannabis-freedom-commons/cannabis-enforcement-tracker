Officers from the Narcotics and Anti-Smuggling Directorate searched the home of
İ.K., a 39-year-old man, in Famagusta, northern Cyprus, at about 15:00 on 29
October 2026. [@2] Narkotik ve Kaçakçılığı Önleme Müdürlüğü is the Turkish name
of the directorate. [@1] Police said they found about 2 grams each of substances
believed to be methamphetamine and cannabis, which they called hint keneviri,
the Turkish term for Indian hemp. [@1] The substances were taken as evidence,
and İ.K. was arrested. [@2]

The Police Press Office gave the account of the search and arrest. [@2]
Police said the investigation was continuing. [@2]
