Officers of the Criminal Investigations Department and Special Operations Unit searched three
locations in the Southern Division between 10 a.m. and 6 p.m. on Friday, 30 October 2026, in an
intelligence-led operation led by Snr Supt Parriman and Supt Matthos and supervised by Sgt
Hosein. [@1] A search of a home on Wellington Road, Debe, in the occupant's presence found
nothing. [@1] Officers then searched an empty lot at Ramdhani Trace, off Moruga Road, and found
nine transparent packets of cannabis weighing about 350 g, with an estimated street value of
TT$45,000. [@1] A search of an abandoned structure across the road found ten rounds of 9 mm
ammunition. [@1] Enquiries were continuing and no arrests were reported. [@1]
