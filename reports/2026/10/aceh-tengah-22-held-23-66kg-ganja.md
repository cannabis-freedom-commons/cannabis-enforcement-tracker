Police in Aceh Tengah regency, Aceh, detained 22 people in 13 narcotics cases
reported over September and October 2026. [@1] Officers seized 23,662.8 grams
(23.66 kg) of ganja in three cannabis cases. [@1] The 13 cases also included
nine methamphetamine cases and one ecstasy case, with 18.6 grams of
methamphetamine and 28 ecstasy pills weighing 12.95 grams seized. [@1] Of the 22
suspects, 19 were men and three were women; police classed seven as users, nine
as dealers and six as intermediaries (perantara). [@2]

Aceh Tengah police chief AKBP Teguh Siswoyo announced the results at a press
conference at the regency police headquarters in Takengon on Saturday 31 October
2026. [@1] He said most of the dealers came from outside the regency, naming
Medan, Bireuen and Lhokseumawe. [@2] The cases reached into Bener Meriah, North
Aceh, Lhokseumawe and North Sumatra. [@1]

Teguh said police were still investigating to trace the suppliers and the
networks bringing narcotics into Aceh Tengah. [@3] He asked residents to report
suspected drug activity, saying police could not work alone. [@3]
