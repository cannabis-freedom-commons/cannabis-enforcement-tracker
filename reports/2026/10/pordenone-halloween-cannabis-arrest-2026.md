A Carabinieri radiomobile patrol stopped a 20-year-old resident of Carbonera in
Largo San Giacomo, Pordenone, on the evening of 31 October 2026. [@1,2] The stop
happened at about 22:00 that Saturday. [@1,2] The youth was arrested on suspicion
of possessing drugs for dealing. [@1,2,4] A second 20-year-old from Mogliano Veneto
was with him when officers intervened. [@1,2,4] The second youth was found with
documents made out to other people and reported as lost. [@1,4] Carabinieri
reported him for receiving stolen goods. [@1,4] He was also referred to the
Prefecture as a drug user because he had an MDMA tablet. [@1,4] The first youth's
arrest on the morning of 1 November was put before the judicial authority. [@1]
On 3 November 2026 the investigating judge validated the arrest. [@1,3] The judge
ordered him to live in the municipalities of Carbonera and Treviso. [@1,4] The
judge barred him from leaving home from 19:00 to 7:00. [@1] The judge ordered
him to report daily to judicial police. [@1,4]

On the stopped youth officers found six MDMA tablets weighing 3.2 grams in
total. [@1] He carried five grams of marijuana. [@1,2,4] He carried 6.7 grams of
hashish. [@1,3,4] He carried four grams of ketamine. [@1,4] He carried 125 euros in
cash. [@1,4] A home search of his bedroom found drugs hidden in drawers and
wardrobes, partly already divided into doses. [@1] There officers recovered
almost 90 grams of hashish, about 30 grams of marijuana and over 32 grams of
ketamine. [@1,2,4] They also found another MDMA tablet, four LSD fragments and some
hallucinogenic mushrooms. [@1,4] The home also held a precision scale, packaging
material and almost 6,000 euros investigators saw as possible dealing proceeds.
[@1,4] All the drugs, cash and tools were seized. [@1,2,4]

The operation was part of territorial checks by law enforcement in the Pordenone
area. [@1] Investigations continue under the presumption of innocence. [@1]
