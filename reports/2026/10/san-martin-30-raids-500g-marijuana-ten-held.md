Buenos Aires provincial police arrested 10 people, including the alleged leaders
of a drug gang, in 30 simultaneous raids across the north of Greater Buenos
Aires and the City of Buenos Aires. [@1] Officers seized 500 grams of marijuana
buds of several named varieties, stored in jars, along with nine precision
scales. [@1] The same operation seized 11 kilograms of cocaine hydrochloride, 19
vehicles, firearms and cash. [@1]

The raids were carried out on 30 October, according to El Policial. [@2]
Investigators said the gang's main territory was San Martín, the northern belt
of Greater Buenos Aires and the City of Buenos Aires. [@1] Researchers found
that buyers handed over money in Villa Corea and collected the drugs in Villa
Ballester, moved in bags, backpacks and vehicles fitted out to hide the load.
[@1] Police said the alleged leader kept running the organisation while in
custody and, after his release, personally managed its largest deals. [@1] The
investigation began in March 2026 and relied on more than 25 tapped phone lines
with over 230 hours of recordings, plus drones, GPS trackers and antenna
analysis. [@1]

The ten suspects were placed at the disposal of the Federal Court of Tres de
Febrero, which is investigating offences under narcotics law 23.737 and money
laundering. [@1] Officers also seized 92,430,200 pesos and 21,550 dollars in
cash, three note-counting machines and four safes. [@1]
