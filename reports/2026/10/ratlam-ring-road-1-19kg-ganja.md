Deendayal Nagar police in Ratlam, Madhya Pradesh, arrested a 19-year-old man
with 1 kg 192.5 g (1.19 kg) of ganja. [@1] Police identified him as Shaitan
Ninama, 19, son of Lalu Ninama, of Tikhi Rundi in Rampuria, Ratlam district.
[@1]

Station in-charge Anurag Yadav said an informer had told police that a man from
Rampuria would come along Ring Road to sell ganja. [@1] The tip said he would
come on Saturday. [@2] Officers cordoned off Ring Road between the Baroth Mata
temple and Sagod village. [@1] The team hid nearby and waited for a man matching
the description, then surrounded and caught him when he arrived. [@2] A search
of the green plastic bag in his hand found the ganja. [@1] Police put its
estimated value at about ₹42,000. [@1]

Police registered a case against him under section 8/20 of the Narcotic Drugs
and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act. [@1] Police said they were questioning
him about where the ganja came from and whom he meant to sell it to, and were
gathering information about other people linked to drug trafficking. [@1]
