On 28 October 2026, police detained three residents of Yerevan on reasonable
suspicion of illegally acquiring and selling drugs. [@1] Officers brought the
three detainees to the Nor Nork community-police department in Yerevan. [@2] The
action followed operational information that especially large consignments of
drugs had been smuggled into Armenia and placed in caches for later sale. [@1]

Officers from the criminal police department for combating drug trafficking
carried out the operation jointly with the Investigative Committee and the
Yerevan patrol service. [@1] Officers discovered the drugs in three separate
caches on the territory of the capital. [@2] The caches together yielded 503
grams of heroin and 3.3 kg of crystalline methamphetamine. [@1] Marijuana was
also recovered in the same caches. [@2]

Police said the smuggled drugs had been hidden in the caches for subsequent
distribution. [@2] A criminal case was opened in the investigative department
for the Avan and Nor Nork administrative districts of Yerevan. [@1] A
preliminary investigation into the case was continuing. [@2]
