## The interceptions

On Sunday 25 October 2026, NDLEA officers acting on intelligence about skunk moving from Lagos Island to the Mainland stopped a white Volkswagen LT 28 bus on Kudirat Abiola Way in Ikeja and recovered 537 kg of the drug. [@1,2,3,4,5] The same day, a second team intercepted a brown J5 bus on the Gbagada-Oshodi Expressway and seized a further 439 kg. [@1,2,3,4,5] Together the two stops yielded 976 kg of skunk, which the agency describes as a strong strain of cannabis. [@1,2,3]

## The announcement

The seizures were published on 1 November 2026 in a nationwide operations roundup issued by the NDLEA's Director of Media and Advocacy, Femi Babafemi. [@1,2] The roundup presents the Lagos interceptions as intelligence-led stops on the Island-to-Mainland route. [@1] The Telegraph's account repeats the agency's figures and locations for both buses. [@2] Neither account names anyone arrested in the two stops, and neither gives a street value for the cannabis. [@1,2]
