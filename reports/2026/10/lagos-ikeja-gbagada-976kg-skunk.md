## The interceptions

On Sunday 25 October 2026, NDLEA officers acting on intelligence about skunk moving from Lagos Island to
the Mainland stopped a white Volkswagen LT 28 bus on Kudirat Abiola Way in Ikeja and recovered 537 kg of
the drug. [@1,2,3,4,5,6,8,9,11,12,16,18,20,21,22,23,24,25,26] The same day, a second team stopped a brown J5
bus on the Gbagada–Oshodi Expressway and seized a further 439 kg. [@1,2,3,4,5,6,8,9,10,11,13,16,18,20,21,22,23,24,26]
Together the two stops yielded 976 kg of skunk, which the agency describes as a strong strain of cannabis.
[@1,2,3,7,9,11,14,15,17,19,22,23,24,26]

The NDLEA's Director of Media and Advocacy, Femi Babafemi, published the seizures on 1 November 2026 in a
roundup of the agency's operations across the country. [@1,2]
