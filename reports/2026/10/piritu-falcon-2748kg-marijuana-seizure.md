CICPC officers attached to the Drugs Investigations Division dismantled the
organised crime group "El Clan de Los Salas" in Falcón state. [@1] The operation
ended with one man captured and a load of 274.8 kg of marijuana seized. [@1]
VTV, which published the CICPC account, called the group a Grupo Estructurado de
Delincuencia Organizada (GEDO), Venezuela's term for a structured
organised-crime group. [@2]

The procedure was carried out on the national Tucacas-Coro road, in the El
Huequito sector of Píritu municipality. [@1] During an incursion into wooded
areas, officers saw a rural house used as a collection point for sending illicit
substances to islands in the Caribbean. [@1] The men guarding the place fled
when they noticed the police presence. [@1] Officers later arrested the owner of
the property, a 59-year-old citizen, and were still searching for the other men.
[@1]

At the house officers seized 11 sacks holding 482 cakes of cannabis sativa, with
a gross weight of 274,800 kilograms, that is 274.8 kilograms. [@1] They also
held an outboard motor, a cattle-branding iron and two drums. [@1] The detainee
and the evidence were placed at the order of the 21st drugs prosecutor of the
Public Prosecutor's Office in Falcón state. [@1]

Notitarde described the seized material as suspected marijuana. [@3] Punto de
Corte reported that patrols continued in the coastal area to capture the
remaining members of the group. [@4] Primera Edición Col reported that the cakes
were wrapped in synthetic material and packed in 11 sacks with a similar
coating, a packaging the report said would be compatible with moving the load in
a boat. [@5] That outlet reported the working hypothesis that the drug had
arrived by sea on the Falcón coast and formed part of a route possibly bound for
the Caribbean islands. [@5] La Iguana TV reported that the deployment was
carried out under the direction of the sectoral vice-president for politics,
public security and peace, Captain Diosdado Cabello Rondón. [@6]
