Operatives of the Violent Crime Response Unit raided a hotel in Abraka, Ethiope East Local Government Area, that intelligence said was a hideout for drug-related activities. [@1,2] The raid took place on 23 October 2026. [@1,2]

Officers arrested six suspected cultists and recovered a bag containing a firearm and substances suspected to be Indian hemp. [@1,2] Some people at the hotel fled when the operatives arrived. [@1,2] Police spokesman SP Bright Edafe confirmed the operation in a statement in Warri. [@1,2] He said investigations were continuing into who owned the firearm and where it came from, and into others connected with the hotel. [@1,2]
