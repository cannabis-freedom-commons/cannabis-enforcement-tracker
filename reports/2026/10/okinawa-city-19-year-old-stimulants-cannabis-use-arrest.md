Tomigusuku Police Station announced on 6 November 2026 that it had arrested a
19-year-old boy who gave his occupation as a restaurant employee and lives in
Okinawa City. [@1] The arrest was made on 28 October 2026. [@1] He was held on
suspicion of violating the Stimulants Control Act and the Narcotics and
Psychotropics Control Act. [@1] Police did not disclose whether he admitted or
denied the allegations. [@1]

The allegation is that he used stimulants and cannabis somewhere in Okinawa
Prefecture between early October and about 14 October 2026. [@1] Police learned
of the case from a report by a medical institution in the police station's own
jurisdiction. [@1]
