Mexican navy personnel destroyed about 56,000 suspected marijuana plants on five plots in La Yesca,
Nayarit, during patrols on 23 and 24 October 2026. [@1,2] The plots covered more than 6,000 square
metres (0.6 hectares) near the municipality, the Tenth Naval Zone said. [@1,2]

The plants were destroyed where they were found. [@1,2] The navy said the operation was part of
permanent efforts to reduce drug production at its source. [@2]

The navy's statement did not report any arrests and did not give the exact location of each
plot. [@1]
