Inspectors of Rosselkhoznadzor, the federal agricultural and phytosanitary watchdog, found wild
cannabis over about 50 hectares in the Marksovsky, Balakovsky and Pugachevsky districts of Saratov
Oblast between May and July 2026. [@1,2]

The information was passed to the regional police headquarters. [@1] On 9 October 2026 the watchdog
received confirmation from the police that the patches had been destroyed. [@1] Sarnovosti reported
the same figure and said the plants had been found in May and June. [@2]

The reports name no suspects or arrests. [@1,2]
