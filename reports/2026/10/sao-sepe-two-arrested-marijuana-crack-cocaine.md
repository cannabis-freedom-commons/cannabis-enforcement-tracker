Civil Police in São Sepé arrested two men, aged 43 and 44, in the Bairro Centro
on the afternoon of 28 October 2026 on suspicion of drug trafficking. [@2] The
arrests followed an anonymous tip that men were selling drugs in the town using
a green Volkswagen Santana. [@2] Police monitored the vehicle and stopped it,
identifying the driver and passenger. [@2] In the central console they found
portions of cocaine, crack and marijuana, as well as cash and a mobile phone.
[@1]

Police took both to the station, where the arrests were recorded in flagrante —
in the act — for drug trafficking. [@2] Both had police records for offences
including threats, bodily harm, contempt and breach of a protective order,
police said. [@1] Both men were taken to the São Sepé State Prison (PESS) after
the judicial police procedures. [@1] The operation was coordinated by Carla
Dolores Castro de Almeida, the police chief at the São Sepé station. [@1] The
station published a phone and WhatsApp number for crime reports and said
informants' identities are kept confidential. [@1]
