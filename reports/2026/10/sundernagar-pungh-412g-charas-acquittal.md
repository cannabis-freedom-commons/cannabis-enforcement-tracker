On 29 October 2026, Special Judge Hoshiyar Singh Verma's special court at
Sundernagar acquitted three men in a 412-gram charas recovery case. [@1] The
acquitted men were Ajit Kumar, Aman Kumar and Anku Chandel, who had faced
charges under Sections 20 and 29 of the NDPS Act. [@1] The court freed all three
on the benefit of the doubt after finding material contradictions in the
evidence about the alleged recovery. [@1]

Police had claimed that they stopped an Alto car for checking during a blockade
at Pungh on 26 June 2019. [@1] Police claimed that two packets containing a
total of 412 grams of charas, the local term for cannabis resin, were recovered
from under the car's rear seat. [@1] The two independent witnesses examined
during the trial did not fully support the police account of the recovery. [@1]

Police officers also gave differing accounts of the blockade's time, place and
action during the proceedings. [@1] The court found significant contradictions
between the witnesses and police officers and held that the prosecution had
failed to prove recovery from the accused beyond reasonable doubt. [@1]
