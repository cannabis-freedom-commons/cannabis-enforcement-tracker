Police arrested a man in Jardim Fátima, Araras, at about 8:30 p.m. on 30 October
2026, during an overt and preventive patrol by officers from the 36th Battalion
of the Military Police of the Interior. [@1] According to the police, officers
had spotted him near a property on Rua Luiz Lagazi, a location their teams
already knew from earlier drug-trafficking records. [@1] He started walking
quickly as the patrol vehicle approached, prompting the officers to stop him.
[@1] A personal search found two portions of cannabis and 10 portions of
cocaine, as well as R$4 and a Samsung A02 mobile phone. [@1] The officers said
they believed the circumstances indicated drug dealing and arrested him. [@1]

He was taken to Pronto-Atendimento Mandic for medical assessment, then presented
at the Central de Polícia Judiciária with the seized items. [@1] After reviewing
the occurrence and hearing his account, duty delegate Luiz Henrique Lima Pereira
provisionally classified his conduct under Article 28 of Brazil's Drug Law,
which relates to possession for personal use, and ordered his release. [@1] The
responding team included First Lieutenant Zuffo, Corporal Covillo and Corporal
Hebert, supported by Soldiers Perissato and Lombardi. [@1]
