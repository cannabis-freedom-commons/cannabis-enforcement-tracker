Military police arrested three men for drug trafficking on 31 October 2026 at
kilometre 532 of the Prefeito Homero Severo Lins highway (SP-284) in
Martinópolis, São Paulo. [@1] Officers on patrol spotted a car with Rancharia
plates travelling at high speed with its headlights off and followed it into a
petrol station beside the highway. [@1] The three occupants were stopped in the
station forecourt, with only a mobile phone found on the driver. [@1] The man
next to the front passenger seat carried another phone and R$20, while the
rear-seat passenger carried a third phone. [@1] The two passengers said the
drugs belonged to them and that the driver had only been hired to drive. [@1]

Under the front passenger mat officers found a green plastic bag with nine
portions of cocaine and 18 large crack rocks wrapped in clear plastic, and two
marijuana (maconha) tablets wrapped in brown plastic on the rear seat. [@1]
Police also seized the car, three mobile phones and R$20, and took the drugs and
vehicle into custody. [@1]

The men said they had left Rancharia and were taking the drugs to other people
in Martinópolis. [@1] The Polícia Militar, São Paulo's uniformed patrol police,
handled the stop, and all three remained in custody at the disposal of the
courts. [@2]
