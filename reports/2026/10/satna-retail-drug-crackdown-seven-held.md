Seven people were arrested in raids on retail drug sales at separate locations.
[@1] Cases were registered against all seven under the NDPS Act, India's main
drug law, and the Drug Control Act. [@1] In Satna city, station officer Hemant
Sharma said Babbu alias Ravi, 29, of Bhatanwara, was caught on the night of
Friday 30 October in Bajraha Tola carrying 170 intoxicating tablets worth ₹4,000
on an unnumbered motorcycle. [@1] Aniket, 21, of Station Road, was arrested near
the Jaystambh Chowk liquor shop with 13 bottles of intoxicating syrup worth
₹2,900. [@1] In Singhpur, station officer Vishan Singh Maravi said Vinay, 45, of
Ward 11, was stopped near the Patparnath temple with 1 kg 367 g of ganja, a form
of cannabis, worth ₹13,500 in a sack. [@1] He was produced in court on Saturday
afternoon. [@1]

Ramnagar station officer Vijay Tripathi said Kailash alias Krishnapal, 60, of
Lehechua in Sidhi district, was caught on the Jhinna power house road with 1,300
g of ganja on a motorcycle, and police valued the ganja and motorcycle together
at ₹93,000. [@1] In Gulwar-Gujara village, Jaybhan, 56, was found with 720 g of
ganja worth ₹7,200 when police searched his house. [@1] In Maihar district's
Tala police area, Sunita Sharma, 65, was found with 760 g of ganja worth ₹7,600
in Mukundpur and named her supplier, whom police are seeking. [@1]

Dharakundi police recovered 430 g of ganja worth ₹6,450 from Anand, 38, of Amua,
after complaints of retail drug sales in rural areas. [@1]
