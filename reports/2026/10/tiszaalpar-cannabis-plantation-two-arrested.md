Police in Tiszakécske detained two middle-aged men from Kecskemét who were
harvesting a cannabis plantation hidden in woodland outside Tiszaalpár, in
Bács-Kiskun County. [@1]

Detectives at the Tiszakécske police station began investigating after
information from the National Protective Service, Hungary's internal affairs
body, that someone was growing cannabis on the outskirts of Tiszaalpár. [@1] The
force told reporters the officers mounted a wildlife camera among the trees to
identify the grower. [@2] The camera sent an alert on a day in October, when a
man appeared at the plantation carrying a plastic barrel and sheeting, so
officers went straight to the site. [@2]

Officers caught the man cutting marijuana plants with a machete and packing them
into the barrels he had brought. [@2] A second man was waiting in a
four-wheel-drive vehicle nearby, where police said he had been keeping watch and
was to carry the cut plants away. [@1] At the scene officers seized several
dozen plants and the equipment used for growing them. [@1] Searches of the two
men's homes turned up four digital scales and, at the grower's address, plant
material that a quick field test indicated was marijuana. [@2] Officers of the
Kecskemét police headquarters questioned both men as suspects for the crime of
drug possession and then took them into custody. [@2]
