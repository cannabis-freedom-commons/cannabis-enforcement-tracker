Coastguard units from Bahía Solano stopped a cabotage vessel in the northern
Pacific off Bahía Solano, in the department of Chocó, and found 627 rectangular
packages hidden inside mattresses and sacks of rice. [@1,2] A dog handler and dog
helped detect the load, and the vessel, its crew and the packages were taken to a
safe port for testing. [@1,2] The Technical Investigation Corps (CTI) of the
Attorney General's Office confirmed 303.7 kg of marijuana and 2.9 kg of cocaine.
[@1,2]

The eight crew members — seven Colombians and one Venezuelan — were handed to the
authorities, and officers seized seven mobile phones. [@1,2] The navy put the value
of the load at about US$3.3 million, roughly 10,339 million pesos, and said the
seizure prevented some 57,000 doses from reaching international markets. [@1,2]

Lieutenant Daniel Andrés Santos Contreras, commander of the Bahía Solano coastguard
station, said the suspicious cargo was found during a boarding and inspection. [@1,2]
The navy said Bahía Solano forms part of a maritime corridor toward Central America
and other destinations, and that the Attorney General's Office must establish where
the drugs came from and where the vessel was going. [@1] The reports were published
on 27 October; they do not give the day of the interception. [@1,2]
