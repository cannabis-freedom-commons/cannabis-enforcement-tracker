Police in Bangka Tengah, Bangka Belitung, arrested two men on Friday night after
finding cannabis hidden in a washing machine and a chicken coop. [@1] The
operation ran into early Saturday, 24 October 2026, and the men were identified
only as A, 24, and R, 37. [@3] Both were taken to Bangka Tengah police
headquarters with the evidence for further investigation. [@2]

The operation began at a house in Perlang village at about 22:00 on Friday 23
October. [@3] There, officers found a jar and 51 small packets of suspected
cannabis, with the packets hidden in a washing machine. [@3] The cannabis from
that house weighed about 430 grams. [@1] Officers then went at about 01:00 on
Saturday to R's home in Air Mesu village. [@3] A search there found 26 small
packets of suspected cannabis hidden in a chicken coop behind the house. [@2]
That cannabis weighed about 230 grams. [@1] Together the two finds came to about
660 grams, police said. [@2]

Police also seized Rp400,000 in cash, two mobile phones, a motorcycle, two jars,
brown paper and scissors, which they said were used for packing the drug. [@2]
Both suspects were charged under Articles 114(1) and 111(1) of Indonesia's 2009
narcotics law, in combination with Article 132(1). [@2] The head of the drug
unit, IPTU Heri Hadi Santoso, said the case came from investigation and
follow-up by his officers. [@2] He said officers would keep developing the case
to find other possible members of a network. [@2]
