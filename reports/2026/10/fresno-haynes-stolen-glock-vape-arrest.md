In Fresno, California, Fresno Police officers approached a parked vehicle after
spotting two people inside smoking marijuana. [@1] Police said the encounter
happened around 12:30 a.m. on Monday at Mildreda Avenue and North College Street
in Fresno. [@1] When officers spoke with the vehicle's occupants, they noticed a
loaded firearm magazine resting on the driver's lap. [@1] Police later
identified that driver as 20-year-old Christopher Haynes. [@1]

Officers searched the vehicle and said they found a 9 mm Glock handgun inside
the car. [@1] Officers said a records check showed that the handgun had been
stolen during a burglary in Phoenix, Arizona. [@1] Officers also said they found
evidence consistent with the sale of marijuana vape products in the vehicle.
[@1]

According to police, Haynes openly admitted that he personally possessed the
firearm in the vehicle. [@1] Haynes was arrested for weapons-related charges and
for possession of marijuana for sale. [@1] After the arrest on those weapons and
marijuana counts, Haynes was booked into the Fresno County Jail. [@1]
