Officers of the Benghazi branch of Libya's General Department for Combating
Drugs and Psychotropic Substances arrested a woman living in Al-Laithi in
Benghazi. [@1] The suspect was identified only by the initials G. H. A. [@3] The
branch said she was suspected of helping her husband to bring in narcotic
substances and to store them. [@2] The operation followed source information set
out in an evidence-collection report and corroborated by investigations. [@1]
Officers carried it out under an order to arrest and bring her in. [@2]

The search found 200 large pieces of hashish, known among dealers as
passportats, with a total estimated weight of about 20 kilograms. [@1] Officers
also found 800 narcotic pills of a type called Rouj alongside the hashish. [@3]
The cannabis quantity recorded for this event is the 20 kilograms of hashish.
[@2]

The branch presented the arrest as a later step in a case that had earlier
involved resistance to its members and injury to one of them. [@3] It said it
completed the required legal procedures and notified the Public Prosecution to
continue investigations and take the measures prescribed by law. [@1]
