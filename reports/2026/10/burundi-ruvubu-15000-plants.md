Burundian police said a cannabis field in Ruvubu National Park, Gitega Province, was destroyed
during USALAMA XII, an operation that ran from 5 to 9 October 2026. [@1,2] The field covered
1.5 hectares and contained 15,000 cannabis plants. [@1,2] Police deputy inspector general
Emmanuel Ndayiziga said that officers did not identify the owner at the site and had opened a
judicial investigation. [@1]

The police presented the operation's results on 23 October; Iwacu published its account on
24 October and Indundi published its report on 23 October. [@1,2] Both accounts attribute the
field details to the same police briefing and place it in Ruvubu National Park in Gitega
Province. [@1,2] The same briefing reported 361.5 kilograms of cannabis seized and three
arrests in Bujumbura and Burunga, separate events not included in this field report. [@1,2]

Neither article gives the exact day during the five-day operation when the field was destroyed
or reports a later outcome from the judicial investigation. [@1,2]
