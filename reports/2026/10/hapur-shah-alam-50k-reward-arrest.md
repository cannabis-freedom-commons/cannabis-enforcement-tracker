Police in Hapur arrested Shah Alam, whom they described as the leader of an
inter-state ganja smuggling gang. [@2] Shah Alam, a resident of Sival Khas
village in Meerut district, was arrested near Tatarpur Gol Chakkar, Additional
Superintendent of Police (ASP) Mahesh Kumar said. [@1] He had been on the run
for about a year, with a ₹50,000 reward on him. [@2] Meerut Range Deputy
Inspector General (DIG) Kalanidhi Naithani announced the reward. [@1]

Police said the gang brought ganja from Odisha and sold it in Uttar Pradesh and
other states. [@2] Police had been looking for Shah Alam since 30 December 2025,
when Dehat police arrested four men with a canter and a Scorpio near the road
from Kithore Road to Asaura. [@1] The four were identified as Amjad, Khushnud,
Rahul and Anuj. [@1] They were carrying about two quintals 24 kg 240 g of ganja,
or 224.24 kg, which they told police Shah Alam had arranged on Farman Pradhan's
instructions, on a road about 40 km from Malkangiri in Odisha. [@1] Farman
Pradhan is on interim bail. [@1]
