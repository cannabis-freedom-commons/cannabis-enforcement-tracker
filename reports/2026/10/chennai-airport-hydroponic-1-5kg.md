Customs officials seized about 1.5 kg of high-grade hydroponic ganja from a
passenger at Chennai airport and arrested him. [@1] The passenger was a
30-year-old man from Chennai who had returned from a tourist trip to Thailand.
[@1] He landed from Bangkok on the night of Friday 30 October 2026, on a flight
officers were monitoring. [@1] He cleared immigration, collected his suitcase
and tried to leave through the green channel, the exit for travellers with
nothing to declare. [@1] The New Indian Express reported that air intelligence
unit officers stopped him on specific intelligence. [@2] Officers grew
suspicious of his movements and questioned him. [@1] When his answers were
evasive they took him to the customs office inside the airport for a detailed
examination. [@1]

He was remanded in judicial custody, The New Indian Express reported. [@2]
During interrogation, officials found that an international drug trafficking
network had lured him with the promise of a free trip abroad. [@1] He was sent
from Chennai to Thailand and told to return the following day with a suitcase
containing the contraband. [@1] Customs registered a case and are investigating
to identify the network members who recruited him. [@1] The man had travelled
abroad expecting a free flight and now faces a drug-smuggling case. [@1]
