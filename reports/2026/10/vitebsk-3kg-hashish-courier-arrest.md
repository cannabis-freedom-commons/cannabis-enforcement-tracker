Vitebsk drug-control officers with OMON riot-police support detained a
34-year-old resident of Gomel region. [@1] The man worked as a courier for an
online shop selling drugs. [@1] In follow-up operations officers detained a
second alleged participant, a 35-year-old resident of Minsk region, and seized a
banned substance from caches he had prepared. [@1] Police said both men had
found offers of illegal earnings on the Internet and had worked as drug couriers
for less than a month. [@1]

On his curator's instructions, the first courier was to bring a banned substance
from a neighbouring country and place it in a cache in the capital region. [@1]
During the detention officers found and seized three kilograms of hashish in his
car. [@1] The Interior Ministry described the three kilograms as a dangerous
narcotic drug, hashish. [@2] A 35-year-old Minsk-region participant was also
detained with banned substance found in his caches. [@2]

Investigators opened a criminal case for illegal trafficking in narcotic drugs.
[@1] The two detainees face up to 15 years' imprisonment. [@2]
