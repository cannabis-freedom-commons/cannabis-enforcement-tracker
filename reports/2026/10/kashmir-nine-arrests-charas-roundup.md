Nine alleged drug peddlers were arrested in separate operations across
Bandipora, Anantnag, Pulwama and Awantipora districts on 31 October 2026, and
police said the operations recovered more than two kilograms of charas chura and
other suspected narcotic substances. [@1,3] The largest seizure was in Pulwama,
where police recovered 2.222 kg of charas chura and 40 grams of charas from a
suspect during naka checking, a roadside checkpoint, at Murran Sheikhpora. [@1,3]

In Bandipora, police arrested Ghulam Mohammad Bhat and Manzoor Ahmad Bhat, both
residents of Wewan, at Lal Qila Morh, Pethkoot. [@2,3] In Anantnag, four men were
arrested in separate operations by police stations Srigufwara, Bijbehara and
Uttrasoo and Police Post Sangam. [@1,3] Shabir Ahmad Khan was arrested at Hatigam
Crossing with 982 grams of charas powder, Mohd Maqbool Bhat at Kandipora with
four sticks weighing about 84 grams, Parvaiz Ahmad Shah at Sangam with 8 grams,
and Asif Ahmad Mochi at Shergund with 11 grams. [@1,3]

In a second Pulwama operation, police arrested Sabzar Ahmad Sheikh of Inder at
Tehsil Office Link Road with five lathis, or sticks, of a charas-like substance
wrapped in maize husk. [@1,3]

Police said cases were registered under relevant provisions of law at the
respective police stations and investigations were initiated. [@1,3]

In the Awantipora operation, police also seized the Auto Load Carrier bearing registration number JK13K-7629 allegedly used in the offence. [@3]
