Police reported six arrests in separate cannabis operations in Anantnag and Pulwama on 31 October 2026, as part of a wider nine-arrest roundup. [@1,2,3]

In Anantnag, Shabir Ahmad Khan of Liver Srigufwara was stopped at Hatigam Crossing with 982 grams of cannabis powder. [@1,2,3] Mohammad Maqbool Bhat was arrested at Kandipora with four sticks weighing about 84 grams. [@1,2,3] Police also arrested Parvaiz Ahmad Shah of Syedpora Devipora Mattan with 8 grams and Asif Ahmad Mochi of Sheikhpora with 11 grams. [@1,2,3]

In Pulwama, police arrested Rayees Ahmad Rather and recovered 2.222 kg of cannabis chura and 40 grams of a cannabis-like substance. [@2,3] A second Pulwama suspect, Sabzar Ahmad Sheikh of Inder, was held with five sticks of a cannabis-like substance wrapped in maize husk. [@1,2,3] Cases were registered at the respective police stations and investigations began. [@1,2,3]

Kashmir Images attributed the details of these recoveries and arrests to a police spokesperson speaking in Srinagar. [@2]
