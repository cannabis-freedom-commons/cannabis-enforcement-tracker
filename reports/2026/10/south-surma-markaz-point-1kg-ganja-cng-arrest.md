Police in Sylhet's South Surma area arrested a man carrying one kilogram of
ganja, the Bengali term for cannabis, and took away the CNG auto-rickshaw he was
using. [@1]

The police team stopped him in front of the CNG stand at Markaz Point just after
11:20 pm on 30 October. [@1] The operation was run by South Surma police station
officers led by sub-inspector Md Mamunur Rashid Mamun, according to the media
cell of Sylhet Metropolitan Police (SMP). [@1] Officers found the ganja inside
the CNG auto-rickshaw and stopped the man there. [@1]

Sylhet Metropolitan Police's deputy commissioner for crime and traffic (DC-CTSB)
Md Afzal Hossain said a case under the Narcotics Control Act was filed at South
Surma police station. [@1] Daily Sylhet reported that the case was registered at
the station on 31 October under section 19(a)/38 of the table in section 36(1)
of the Narcotics Control Act 2018. [@2] The arrested man was sent to court. [@1]
