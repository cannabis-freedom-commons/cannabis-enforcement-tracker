A patrol of the Central Narcotics Bureau arrested a Lebanese man in an ambush in
the Sin el Fil area on 21 October 2026, catching him in the act of dealing drugs
on board his Nissan taxi with public licence plates. [@1] The bulletin
identified him only by the initials B.A., born in 1961. [@1] His daughter, T.A.
born in 1996, was detained with him for cooperating with him in the dealing
operations. [@1]

Information had reached the bureau, a unit working under the Judicial Police's
plan against drugs, that a man was dealing narcotics by taxi in the Keserwan and
Metn districts. [@2]

The Internal Security Forces said they seized 12 packages of hashish weighing
483 grams in total from the two. [@2] Police also seized 8 packages of marijuana
weighing 142 grams in total. [@1] The itemised list of what was seized from them
also covered 10 Captagon tablets, 40 bottles of ketamine, 75 XTC tablets, three
bags of narcotic mushrooms weighing 24 grams, five bottles of GBL liquid,
cocaine base, pink cocaine and cocaine, and a sum of money. [@1]

Legal measures were taken against both detainees, and the investigation
continued under the supervision of the competent judiciary to arrest the others
involved. [@1]
