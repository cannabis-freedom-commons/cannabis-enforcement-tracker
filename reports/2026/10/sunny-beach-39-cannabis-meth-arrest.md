Police detained Valeri B., a 39-year-old man from Sliven known by the nickname
Valkata. [@1,5] The search was at his flat in the Sunny Beach resort. [@2] He had
previous convictions and was known to police in Sliven. [@4,5] The detention order
was for up to 24 hours. [@2] He was kept in the arrest facility of the Nesebar
district police department. [@1,6] A fast-track criminal proceeding was opened
against him. [@1,5,6]

Police found about 3 grams of methamphetamine and about 3 grams of amphetamine
in the flat. [@1,5] They also seized about 2 grams of dried plant material
believed to be cannabis and two hand-rolled cigarettes containing it. [@1,5,6] The
search began at about 08:00 in a flat a few metres from the beach and the Kakao
bus stop. [@1,5] Officers from the Burgas criminal police and the Nesebar district
police carried out the operation. [@3] A field test identified the white
crystalline substance as methamphetamine, known in Bulgaria as "piko", and the
white powder as amphetamine. [@1]

Police did not say whether the drugs were intended for sale or for personal use.
[@1,5,6] Investigators said they still had to establish where the substances came
from and how they were stored. [@1,5]
