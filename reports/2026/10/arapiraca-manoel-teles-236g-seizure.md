Officers sent all of the seized material to the Central de Polícia Civil in
Arapiraca. [@1]

Police reported seizing 165.23 grams of pressed cannabis and another 70.56 grams
divided into 40 portions, totalling 235.79 grams of cannabis. [@2] Officers also
seized 20 Rohypnol tablets, R$540 in banknotes, two mobile phones described as
an iPhone and a Redmi, and material used to package drugs. [@1]

The search began at about 1pm on Rua Manoel Caetano de Farias after a
denunciation about possible drug dealing in the two residences. [@1] The Canil
unit of the 3rd Military Police Battalion conducted the searches with sniffer
dog K9 Beretta, supported by Rádio Patrulha 01 and the supervising officer. [@1]
