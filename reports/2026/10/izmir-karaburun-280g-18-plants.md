Acting on the determination that two suspects were manufacturing and trading
drugs, provincial gendarmerie teams raided the Mordoğan Mahallesi home of A.Ö.
and N.Ö. in Karaburun district, İzmir. [@1] Both suspects, identified only by
their initials, were taken into custody, and their procedures at the gendarmerie
were continuing at the time of reporting. [@1]

The house search seized 280 grams of kubar esrar, the Turkish term for herbal
cannabis, along with 150 grams of cannabis seeds and 18 cannabis plants. [@1]
Officers also seized three unlicensed pistols, three magazines, 47 cartridges
and one unlicensed semi-automatic hunting rifle from the same address. [@2]

The most precise location named for the raid is Mordoğan Mahallesi, a
neighbourhood of Karaburun district in İzmir province. [@2] The operation was
attributed to a statement by the İzmir governorate, which said gendarmerie teams
acted after receiving information about drug manufacturing and trade in
Mordoğan. [@2]
