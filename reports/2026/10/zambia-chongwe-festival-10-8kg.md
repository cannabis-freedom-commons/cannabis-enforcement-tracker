The Drug Enforcement Commission (DEC) said an intelligence-led operation at the Summer Reggae and Afro Music Festival in Chongwe District led to ten arrests and the seizure of 10.8 kilograms of cannabis. [@1,2] DEC public relations officer Allan Tamba named Francis Habeenzu, 27, Matildah Zulu, 22, and Hellen Banda, 26, among those arrested. [@1,2]

Mwebantu reported that the ten were in lawful custody awaiting court appearances, while News Diggers described the operation as formally charging and arresting them. [@1,2] Tamba said the DEC was engaging the festival's organisers to prevent the event from being used for illegal activity. [@1,2]
