Sheriff's deputies served a narcotics search warrant in the 500 block of East
Railroad Street in Garysburg, North Carolina, on 28 October 2026 after a
seven-month investigation into the sale of illegal drugs. [@1] Officers seized
cocaine, marijuana, drug paraphernalia and a firearm in the search. [@1]

Two men from Garysburg were arrested, both members of the Ellis family and 40
and 20 years old respectively. [@1] The 40-year-old was charged with one felony
count each of possession of a firearm by a felon, possession with intent to sell
and deliver cocaine, possession of cocaine, and maintaining a vehicle, dwelling
or place for controlled substances, plus misdemeanor possession of drug
paraphernalia. [@1] The 20-year-old was charged with felony possession with
intent to sell and deliver marijuana, felony possession of marijuana, and felony
maintaining a vehicle, dwelling or place for controlled substances. [@1]

Neither man received a bond because of a hold under the state's pre-trial
integrity law, and both had a 29 October court date in Jackson, the Northampton
County seat. [@1] The sheriff's office said further charges against both men are
pending as the investigation continues. [@1]

The Northampton County Sheriff's Office credited the Down East Drug and Violent
Crime Task Force and the federal Drug Enforcement Administration with assisting
in the case. [@1] Sheriff Jack Smith commended the coordinated work of his
investigators, the task force and the DEA. [@1]
