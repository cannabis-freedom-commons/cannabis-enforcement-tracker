Military Police Força Tática officers arrested a man for suspected drug
trafficking on the night of Thursday 29 October 2026 in Votuporanga. [@2] The
patrol was on Rua Lidai Benini in the Santa Eliza neighbourhood when officers
noticed a man carrying a bag who tried to move away and hide the package by a
post among tyres. [@1] Inside the bag officers found a brick of marijuana and a
portion of cocaine. [@1]

The man said he had received the substances from another person and was to carry
them for delivery to a third person. [@1] He then said more drugs were in his
house, stored in a backpack he used for work. [@1] With his partner's
authorisation, officers entered the home and searched it. [@1] In the house they
found two more portions of cocaine inside the backpack and a precision scale
hidden under the laundry-area sink. [@1]

In total officers seized 504 grams of marijuana, alongside 28.4 grams of
cocaine, a precision scale and a mobile phone. [@2] The man was taken to
Votuporanga's Central de Flagrantes, formally arrested for drug trafficking and
held at the disposition of the justice system. [@1]
