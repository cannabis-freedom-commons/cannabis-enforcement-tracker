Federal highway police stopped a truck on the President Dutra highway (BR-116)
in Piraí, in Rio de Janeiro state, on the afternoon of 23 October 2026. [@1,2]
The driver, 36, was alone and told officers he was carrying fertiliser. [@1,2]

When the officers pulled back a tarpaulin under the load, they found marijuana
packed in tablets. [@1,2] The PRF put the haul at about two tonnes. [@1,2]

The driver said he had loaded the truck in Maringá, in Paraná, and was taking it
to Rio de Janeiro, where he would be paid for the transport. [@1,2] He was
arrested. [@1,2] Police took the drugs and the driver to the Federal Police
headquarters at Praça Mauá, in central Rio. [@1,2]

The PRF described the load as “innumerable” tablets of marijuana under the
fertiliser. [@1] The PRF did not name the driver, and g1 reported that the
police did not release his identity. [@1,2] The police did not say where in Rio
de Janeiro the load was going, and no value was given for the cannabis. [@1,2]
