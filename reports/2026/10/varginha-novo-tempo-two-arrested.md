Military police arrested two men aged 23 and 25 in flagrante for drug
trafficking on the night of Friday 30 October 2026 in the Novo Tempo
neighbourhood in Varginha. [@1] The arrest happened at about 22h09 during
routine patrol after information about sales of narcotics in an abandoned
residence in the area. [@1] Officers went to the address because of a history of
complaints saying the building was used exclusively as a drug-selling point.
[@1] On arrival they heard voices and warning shouts and noticed a strong odour
of marijuana. [@1] The 25-year-old tried to flee by jumping the rear wall of the
property, fell during the attempt, was injured and was then restrained by the
officers. [@1]

Police found nine packaged crack rocks ready for sale on the 25-year-old. [@1]
The 23-year-old was stopped inside the house. [@1] A search of the backpack he
was carrying found a large tablet of marijuana. [@1] Officers also collected
cell phones and cash at the scene alongside the seized drugs. [@1]

Both detainees were taken to the Varginha Civil Police station with all the
seized material for formal flagrante procedures. [@1]
