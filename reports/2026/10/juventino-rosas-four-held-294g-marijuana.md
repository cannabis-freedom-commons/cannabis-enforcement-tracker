Four young men from Juventino Rosas, Guanajuato, were detained on 12 October
2026 after they tried to evade a joint patrol by state police and the National
Guard. [@1] They were identified as Martín “N”, 23, José Armando “N”, 22, Juan
Martín “N”, 19, and Cristian Silverio “N”, 23. [@1] The Secretariat of Security
and Peace said all four lived in Santa Cruz de Juventino Rosas. [@2]

The stop happened on the rural road linking the communities of San Juan de la
Cruz and Dulces Nombres, during Operativo Células Mixtas, a joint
crime-prevention operation. [@1] Officers of the Fuerzas de Seguridad Pública
del Estado (FSPE), Guanajuato’s state public-security force, and the Guardia
Nacional saw a 2013 Dodge Charger whose driver was making dangerous manoeuvres.
[@1] When they signalled him to stop he accelerated, and a short pursuit ended
with the car intercepted a short distance ahead. [@1] During the check, officers
noticed that one occupant had damaged his mobile phone. [@1]

Inside the car they found 28 bags of green, dry herb with the characteristics of
marijuana. [@1] The seizure was about 294 grams, which the authorities estimated
as equivalent to 293 doses. [@1] They also seized four mobile phones, two of
them damaged, and the car, which carried plates from Texas, United States. [@1]

A records check showed that Martín “N” had an outstanding judicial warrant for
offences against health, and that was reported to the competent authority. [@1]
The four men, the suspected drug, the car and the phones were handed to the
Fiscalía General del Estado in Cortazar, which is to determine their legal
situation. [@1]
