In October 2026, three inmates of the Saint-Malo detention centre in
Ille-et-Vilaine received a drone delivery carrying cannabis, SIM cards and a
mobile phone. [@1] The delivery took place one night in October, when the three
inmates received the cannabis, SIM cards and mobile phone by drone. [@1]

After being placed in police custody, the three men were tried under the
guilty-plea procedure (comparution sur reconnaissance préalable de culpabilité).
[@1] They were convicted and received firm prison terms of 6 to 10 months. [@1]
The report sets the case against about 5,000 drone overflights or
parcel-delivery attempts recorded at French prisons in 2025. [@1]
