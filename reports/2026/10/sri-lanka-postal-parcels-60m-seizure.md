Sri Lanka Customs officers opened nine foreign postal parcels held at the
Central Mail Exchange in Colombo and seized narcotics with a street value of
Rs 60.26 million. [@1,2] The haul was 4,934 grams of kush cannabis worth
Rs 49.34 million, 1,035 grams of hashish worth Rs 10.35 million, 25 grams of
ecstasy tablets and four grams of cocaine. [@1,2]

Three parcels had arrived from Thailand and two each from Spain, the United
Kingdom and the United States, picked up during routine inspections of
incoming international mail over several months. [@1,2] Customs detained the
suspicious parcels pending collection, and examined them with Police Narcotic
Bureau officers after the recipients failed to claim them. [@1] One account
says the parcels were addressed to fictitious local addresses. [@2]

The Narcotics Control Unit and the Postal Appraising Branch ran the
examination with the Bureau's officers. [@1] Customs handed the drugs to the
Police Narcotic Bureau for investigation and legal proceedings; the reports
name no arrests. [@1,2]
