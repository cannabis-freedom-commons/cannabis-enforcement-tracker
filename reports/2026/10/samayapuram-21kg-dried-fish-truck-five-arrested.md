Five people were arrested near Samayapuram in Trichy district for carrying 21.2
kg of cannabis concealed among dried fish. [@2] On district superintendent of
police Dongare Pravin Umesh's directions, teams intensified vehicle checks on
key roads within Samayapuram limits on intelligence that a group was moving
cannabis by truck from Andhra Pradesh. [@2] A team intercepted the truck on the
service road linking the Trichy-Chennai national highway to the Samayapuram
four-road junction. [@2] The occupants gave contradictory answers, prompting a
thorough search that uncovered packets of cannabis concealed among sacks of
dried fish. [@2] The suspects were identified as S. Joel, 24, and A. Aravind,
31, of Puducherry, C. Ondimuthukumar, 46, and C. Idhayabalan, 33, of Cuddalore,
and M. Kamaraj, 29, of Trichy. [@2]

Investigation found the group had procured the cannabis from the Andhra
Pradesh-Odisha border and transported it via Visakhapatnam. [@2] The group had
already distributed some quantity and was carrying the remainder toward Trichy.
[@2] The accused admitted they intended to sell it at high profit to peddlers
who deal with school and college students in and around Samayapuram. [@2]

Officers seized 21.2 kg of cannabis, Rs 50,000 in cash from previous sales, the
truck, two two-wheelers and five mobile phones. [@2] Samayapuram police
registered cases under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act. [@2]
The five were produced before a magistrate and remanded in judicial custody.
[@2] Rockfort Times separately recorded that in October police seized about 21
kg of ganja near the Trichy-Chennai bypass by the Chidambaram branch road. [@1]
