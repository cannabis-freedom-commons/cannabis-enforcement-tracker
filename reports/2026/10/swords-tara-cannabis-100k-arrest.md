As part of Operation Tara, Gardaí searched a residential property in Swords, County Dublin, on
9 October 2026. [@1,2] They seized suspected cocaine with an estimated street value of €75,000
and suspected cannabis worth an estimated €100,000. [@1,2]

An adult male in his late teens was arrested and held in the Dublin Metropolitan Region under
section 2 of the Criminal Justice (Drug Trafficking) Act 1996. [@1,2] He was charged to appear
before Balbriggan District Court on 3 November 2026. [@1,2] The drugs were to be sent to
Forensic Science Ireland for examination while inquiries continued. [@1,2]
