National Police officers detained people in El Porvenir, Atlántida, who were
accused of carrying a load of marijuana hidden in the spare tires of a small
truck. [@2] The detainees must now answer to the authorities for alleged drug
trafficking, according to the police report. [@2] Police also described the case
as marijuana trafficked in a tire in El Porvenir, Atlántida. [@1]

Concealing marijuana or other illicit substances in vehicle tires is a smuggling
method that authorities say they frequently intercept. [@2] Distributors
apparently sought to sell the drug by taking advantage of tourist flows during
Semana Morazánica. [@2] The detainees must answer for drug trafficking. [@1]

The Policía Nacional de Honduras is the authority that reported the detention.
[@1] The operation is dated 5 October 2026. [@1]
