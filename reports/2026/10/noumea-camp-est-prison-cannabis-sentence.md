A 33-year-old woman was arrested after introducing cannabis into Nouméa's
Camp-Est prison. [@1] A search on 30 September at Camp-Est prison exposed a
cannabis trafficking operation in Nouméa. [@1] A detainee was found with 8 grams
of herb after a visiting-room meeting with his partner. [@1] The 33-year-old
woman was arrested on the spot and admitted bringing the herb into the prison
and giving the pouch to her partner. [@1] The woman, aged 33 and living in
Magenta, was arrested as she left the prison. [@2]

Investigators found 80,000 francs in cash and 536 grams of herb, with the
seizure valued at 435,000 francs. [@1] Investigators identified two addresses
where she stored cannabis totalling 536 grams plus 80,000 francs, with resale
value estimated at 435,000 francs. [@2] She had packed the seized herb at home
into 87 sachets for resale. [@1]

She admitted trafficking from 28 February 2026 until 30 September 2026. [@1] She
admitted nearly seven months of trafficking, including three deliveries to her
partner during his detention. [@2] The cannabis came from Houaïlou, and she
refused to give more detail on the exact growing and packing location. [@1] She
had prior drug-trafficking convictions on 1 July 2022 and a one-year prison term
on 19 February 2025. [@2] Because of her record and legal recidivism she was
dealt with under a simplified guilty-plea procedure. [@1]

She was presented to court on 2 October and given a new one-year immediate
prison term. [@2] She accepted 12 months' immediate imprisonment, bringing her
total sentences to be served to two years' imprisonment. [@1]

Her detained partner must answer charges of unauthorised possession of narcotics
and receiving proceeds of an offence at a 2027 hearing. [@2]
