Special Response Battalion-9 and Sarail Battalion (25 BGB) carried out separate
operations in Bijaynagar. [@1] Two people, including a woman, were arrested in
these incidents. [@1] The arrests were made on Saturday 31 October in different
areas of Bijaynagar upazila. [@1] The cannabis suspect is 25-year-old Abul
Bashar of Chhatarpur village, son of Sayed Mia. [@1] The same day at noon, a
joint SRB-9 CPC-1 and 25 BGB Sarail team raided a house in Kalachhara, Bishnupur
Union, and detained 50-year-old Rahana Begum as she tried to flee. [@1]

An SRB-9 CPC-1 team set up a checkpoint in front of Bishnupur High School on the
Bijaynagar-Chakbazar road at about 5:30 pm and stopped a green CNG auto-rickshaw
whose driver tried to U-turn and flee. [@1] After Bashar admitted cannabis was
in his custody, officers searched two jute sacks on the CNG's back seat and
recovered 20 kg of ganja wrapped in blue polythene and khaki scotch tape. [@1]
On Rahana's indication, officers recovered 197 bottles of Escaf syrup from khaki
cartons wrapped in four jute sacks under a bed in a tin-shed mud room. [@1]

An SRB-9 press release said initial questioning found both had collected the
drugs from Brahmanbaria border areas to keep for sale. [@1] Cases under the
Narcotics Control Act, 2018 were prepared against both suspects and they were
handed with the drugs to Bijaynagar police. [@1] The press release said SRB-9
intelligence work and operations continue to implement a zero-tolerance policy
against drugs. [@1]
