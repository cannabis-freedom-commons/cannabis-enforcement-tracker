Eutiquia “N”, Arcadio “N” and Carmela “N” were sentenced to four years in
prison. [@1] The federal prosecution proved their responsibility for possession
of narcotics for commercial purposes in the sale modality. [@1] On 31 January
2026, Oaxaca state prosecution officers executed a search warrant at a home in
Barrio La Soledad in Ixtlán de Juárez. [@1] Officers detained the three people
in flagrancia during that search and secured illicit substances found inside the
home. [@1] The three detainees were placed at the disposal of the federal
Ministerio Público, which investigated their responsibility for possession for
commercialization. [@1]

The court imposed the four-year sentence on each of the three people through an
abbreviated procedure. [@1] Officers found 131 bags containing 224.6 grams of
methamphetamine hydrochloride and nine grams of cocaine in the same home. [@1]
Officers also secured 24 bags holding a total of 1.612 kilograms of marijuana.
[@1] Officers also secured 12.1 grams of seeds of the same plant. [@1] Each
person received an individual fine of 80 days, equivalent to 9,384.80 pesos.
[@1]

On 23 October 2026 the federal Ministerio Público presented sufficient evidence
before a control judge. [@1] The ruling closes this stage of the criminal case
arising from the seizure in the Sierra Norte of Oaxaca. [@1]
