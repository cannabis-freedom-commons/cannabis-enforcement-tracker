York County Sheriff's Office deputies arrested Gerald S. Baugh III of Stromsburg
on Oct. 15 during a traffic stop. [@1] He was lodged on charges of possession of
a deadly weapon by a prohibited person, possession of a deadly weapon while
committing a felony, possession of a controlled substance, theft by receiving
stolen property, possession of a legend drug, possession of marijuana less than
1 oz and possession of drug paraphernalia. [@1]

The traffic stop occurred around 11 p.m. on Highway 81. [@1] A sergeant with the
K-9 unit deployed police service dog Justice during the stop, and Justice
indicated an odor of illegal narcotics. [@1] Deputies then conducted a probable
cause search and found a deadly weapon, methamphetamine, marijuana, legend drugs
and drug paraphernalia. [@1] Deputies also found a package belonging to someone
other than the driver. [@1] Investigation determined the package contained
multiple pill bottles holding a total of 390 prescription pills. [@1]

The published account gives no separate cannabis weight beyond charging the
marijuana allegation as possession of less than one ounce. [@1]
