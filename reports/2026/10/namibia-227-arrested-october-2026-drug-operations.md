Namibian police arrested 227 suspects during anti-drug operations across the
country in October 2026. [@1] The force said the operations also produced
seizures of illegal drugs including cannabis, Mandrax, cocaine powder and crack
cocaine. [@1] Deputy Commissioner Kauna Shikwambi, the police spokesperson,
issued a media release giving the figures. [@1] Of those arrested, 220 were
Namibian nationals, two Angolan, one Congolese, three Zambian and one
Zimbabwean. [@1]

The police said the seizures and arrests demonstrated their continued efforts to
combat the illegal use and possession of drugs in Namibia. [@1]
