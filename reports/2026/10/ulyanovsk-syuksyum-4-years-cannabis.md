A court in Ulyanovsk Oblast sentenced Sergey Bogdashkin, a 48-year-old resident of the village of
Syuksyum in the Inzensky district, to four years in a strict-regime penal colony. [@1,2] The
prosecutor's office said he collected wild cannabis on the edge of the village in autumn 2025 and
kept it at his home. [@1,2]

Officers detained him on 31 October 2025. [@1,2] The court convicted him of large-scale purchase and
storage of drugs under part 2 of article 228 of the criminal code. [@1,2] He had previously been
convicted of robbery. [@1,2]

The verdict has entered into legal force. [@1,2]
