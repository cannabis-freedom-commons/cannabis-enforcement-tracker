Kumbalagodu police arrested two men from another state for transporting ganja and seized
317 kg of the drug along with an Innova car on Monday 12 October 2026. [@1,2,3] Acting on an
informant's tip under a press note from the Commissioner of Police's office dated 11 October,
officers stopped the rented car the suspects were using to move narcotics from Odisha to
Kerala via Bengaluru, intercepting it near Eidgah Ground at Kambipura. [@3] A case was
registered under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act. [@3]

The seized ganja was valued at Rs 1.78 crore, with a street value of about Rs 3.17 crore.
[@1,2,3] Police said the men had travelled from Kerala to Bengaluru on 6 October, hired a
self-drive rental car in R.T. Nagar, driven to Odisha to buy the drug cheaply, and returned
to Bengaluru on 11 October, spending the night at a hotel in Bommasandra before being caught
the next morning; their stated destination was Kumaramanagalam in Kerala. [@3] The operation
was led by DCP Soumyalatha S.K. and ACP Satyavati K.S. [@1,2,3]
