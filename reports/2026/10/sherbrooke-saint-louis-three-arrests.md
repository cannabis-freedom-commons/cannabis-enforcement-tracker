Sherbrooke police searched apartment 2 at 810 rue Saint-Louis on 29 October 2026
and arrested three people: a 58-year-old woman and two men aged 48 and 58. [@1]
Police said the investigation had lasted several weeks and that information from
the public pointed investigators to the address, where they allege drugs were
being sold. [@1] The operation was carried out by the Sherbrooke police service
with the Régie de police de Memphrémagog and the regional ACCES Cannabis group,
plus Sherbrooke patrol officers, its intervention group and forensic
identification personnel. [@2]

Officers seized 12 grams of cannabis along with 74 grams of cocaine, 87
methamphetamine tablets and 403 Canadian dollars in cash. [@1] They also took
accounting records, seven scales and two knives. [@1]

The police release lists possession, possession for the purpose of trafficking
and trafficking as possible charges and does not say which person faces which
allegation or whether charges have been laid. [@2] The investigation remained
ongoing, and the ACCES Cannabis and ACCES Tabac programs target the illegal
cannabis and tobacco trades respectively. [@1]
