Officers from the criminal division of the Ryki county police, acting on
information about a 43-year-old resident of Pulawy county, searched his home on
the morning of Wednesday, 28 October 2026 with support from Lublin city police
officers. [@1] The Pulawy-county resident was detained and taken to police
custody, with the case placed under prosecutorial supervision. [@1] He was then
charged on suspicion of preparing to take part in trafficking a significant
quantity of intoxicating substances for financial gain. [@1] At the prosecutor's
request, the District Court in Ryki ordered three months of pretrial detention,
a preventive measure that does not amount to a finding of guilt. [@1]

During the search, officers secured significant quantities of intoxicating and
psychotropic substances, identified as marijuana, amphetamine and 4-CMC
(klefedron, a synthetic cathinone). [@1] Police said about 200 dealer portions
could have been prepared from the seized substances taken together, a combined
figure not broken down by drug. [@1] Officers also secured equipment and
products that could have been used to prepare the drugs for sale. [@1]

The operation followed earlier findings that the man might be holding illegal
substances. [@1] The account of the search, seizure and court decision was
attributed to Staff Sergeant Piotr Szyszka of the Ryki county police. [@1]
