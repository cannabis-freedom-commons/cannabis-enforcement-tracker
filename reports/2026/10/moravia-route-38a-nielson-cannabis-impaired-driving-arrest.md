Cayuga County sheriff's deputies arrested a 20-year-old Auburn man, Aiden C.
Nielson, on State Route 38A in the town of Moravia, New York, at about 12:32
a.m. on 14 October 2026. [@1] Deputies patrolling the highway had found his
vehicle stopped in the southbound lane at about 11:53 p.m. the previous night
and saw signs of impairment when they approached and questioned him. [@1]

He was charged with driving while ability impaired by drugs, improper use of
hazard flashers, consuming alcohol or cannabis in a motor vehicle on a public
highway, and illegally stopping or standing on a public highway. [@2] The charge
records impairment by drugs generally and does not state that cannabis was the
substance involved. [@2]

Deputies took him to Auburn Community Hospital for a blood sample to determine
what substances were in his body. [@1] He was processed at the Public Safety
Building and issued an appearance ticket returnable on 22 October to Moravia
Town Court. [@2]
