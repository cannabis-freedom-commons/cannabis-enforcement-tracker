Guardia Civil officers detained two men travelling in a car in Calasparra as
alleged perpetrators of a drug-trafficking offence against public health. [@1]
Officers stopped the car, occupied by a driver and a passenger, at a preventive
citizen-security checkpoint in the municipality's industrial estate in order to
identify the two occupants. [@1] While identifying them, officers noticed a
characteristic marijuana smell and then searched the vehicle. [@1]

In the boot they found plant material distributed among three glass jars and one
metal container, with a total reported weight of 170 grams whose identification
as marijuana remains pending laboratory analysis. [@2] Officers also seized a
digital precision scale, two jerrycans of fertiliser, two containers of
agricultural-use soap, a measuring cup and a pocketknife from the car. [@2] The
combination of finds led officers to suspect the car may have been used as a
mobile street-level drug-selling point, described as a hypothesis since the
reports record no observed sales during the checkpoint. [@2]

The checkpoint operation ended with the detention of both occupants and the
removal of the containers and the other related items. [@2] Both detainees
remain presumed innocent while the laboratory analysis of the substance is
pending. [@1]
