Municipal guards detained a 62-year-old tourist from Minas Gerais in the São
Sebastião–Ilhabela ferry boarding area on 29 October 2026. [@1] The Guarda Civil
Municipal (GCM), São Sebastião's municipal guard force, had been sent by the
Centro de Operações Integradas (COI), the integrated operations centre, after a
report of disorderly conduct and drug use in the boarding area. [@1] During the
stop the man admitted he had been smoking marijuana and said he had used drugs
for about 40 years. [@1] Officers took him to the São Sebastião police station,
handcuffing him after he resisted arrest. [@1] His mobile phone was seized for
examination and he remained in custody at the disposal of the courts. [@1]

Officers searching his backpack found one portion of marijuana weighing about 10
grams, the cannabis amount recorded here. [@1] The same search found 24 pinos,
small tubes, containing cocaine weighing about 21 grams, noted only to explain
the mixed seizure and excluded from cannabis figures. [@1] A second account says
officers at the ferry crossing stopped a 62-year-old man and found 24 cocaine
pinos and one marijuana portion on him. [@2] That account says the suspect
reported travelling from Minas Gerais toward Ilhabela. [@2]

The man told police he was visiting São Paulo, planned to continue to Ilhabela,
had bought the drugs in the state capital for personal use during the trip and
denied selling drugs. [@1] Officers nonetheless booked him in flagrante for drug
trafficking, citing the quantity and packaging of the drugs. [@1]
