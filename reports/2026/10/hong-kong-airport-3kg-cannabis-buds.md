Customs officers at Hong Kong International Airport stopped a 56-year-old
mainland Chinese man who had arrived from Bangkok on 26 October 2026. [@1,2]

In his carry-on baggage they found about 3 kg of suspected cannabis buds, a
product containing a suspected Part I poison, and seven duty-not-paid
cigarettes. [@1,2]

He was arrested. [@1,2] Customs estimated the market value of the cannabis buds
at about HK$600,000. [@1,2]

The investigation is continuing. [@1,2] Trafficking in a dangerous drug carries
a maximum penalty of a HK$5 million fine and life imprisonment under the
Dangerous Drugs Ordinance. [@1]

Customs did not name the man. [@1]
