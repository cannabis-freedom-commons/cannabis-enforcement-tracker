Kardzhali Regional Court sentenced defendant S.K., aged 29, on 9 October 2026 after he pleaded
guilty under shortened trial rules to holding marijuana for distribution. [@1,2]

The court found that on 3 February 2026, on road I-5 in the Verben area of Kirkovo municipality,
he kept 3.210 kilograms of cannabis in a suitcase in a passenger car without authorisation. [@1,2]
The drugs were valued at EUR 32,824.94. [@1,2]

The sentence is two years and eight months’ imprisonment in an ordinary regime plus a EUR 4,000
fine. [@1,2] The judgment is not final and may be appealed or reviewed by the Plovdiv appellate
court. [@1,2]
