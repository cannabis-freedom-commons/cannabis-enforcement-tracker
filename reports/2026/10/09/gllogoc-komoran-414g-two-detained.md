On 9 October 2026 at about 20:20, the Directorate for Investigation of Narcotics Trafficking in
Pristina stopped a locally registered car in Komoran, Gllogoc. [@1,2] Two Kosovar men in the
vehicle carried a cigarette with 0.71 grams of suspected marijuana and were released. [@1,2]

Continuing the operation, officers carried out court-ordered searches at two other suspects’
homes. [@1,2] In one house they seized 414.5 grams of marijuana, EUR 15, two phones, a door
monitoring camera, a small scale, a grinder and a memory card; nothing was found at the second
address. [@1,2] Two suspects and a minor were taken to the police station; a prosecutor ordered
the two men detained, while the minor was released after an interview on a separate
non-reporting charge. [@1,2]
