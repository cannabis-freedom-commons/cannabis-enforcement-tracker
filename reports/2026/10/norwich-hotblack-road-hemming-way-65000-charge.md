Norfolk police officers stopped a man in his late teens on Hotblack Road, off
Dereham Road in Norwich, on the night of Saturday 31 October 2026 and arrested
him on suspicion of possession with intent to supply. [@1] Officers then
searched a property on Hemming Way, another Norwich street, and found packaged
cocaine and cannabis. [@1] Norwich Police estimated the street value of the
drugs at more than £65,000. [@1]

Markelian Lugja, 19, of no fixed address, was charged with possession with
intent to supply Class A drugs, possession with intent to supply Class B drugs
and possession of criminal property. [@1] He appeared at Great Yarmouth
Magistrates' Court on 2 November and was remanded in custody. [@1] His next
court appearance is listed for 30 November 2026. [@1]

The arrest and the search that followed came during ongoing police patrols in
the city. [@1]
