Bengaluru Customs officers at Kempegowda International Airport on Sunday
apprehended a Myanmar national attempting to smuggle 12.1 kg of hydroponic
ganja. [@1] The apprehended Myanmar national was identified as 28-year-old Thein
Htet Aung. [@1] Sleuths intercepted Aung after suspecting him while he tried to
exit the airport's Terminal 2 after arriving from Bangkok. [@1] Customs also
intercepted Viveknagar resident Micheal Arokiyanathan with 15.5 kg of hydro
ganja while he was arriving from Bangkok. [@1]

Aung had boarded Thai Airways flight TG 325 from Bangkok at around 9.50 pm on
Saturday and landed at Bengaluru by 12 am on Sunday. [@1] The ganja was fully
concealed in his baggage to evade detection. [@1] Customs estimated the 12.1-kg
seizure to be valued at Rs 4.2 crore. [@1] The 15.5-kg seizure was valued at Rs
5.4 crore. [@1]

A customs official said this was the first time a person from Myanmar had been
detected smuggling ganja to Bengaluru. [@1] Both accused were arrested under the
NDPS Act 1985. [@1]
