Six people from Detroit were charged over an alleged armed robbery at a
marijuana facility in Warren. [@1] The six were arraigned on 15 October 2026
before Judge Steven Bieda of the 37th District Court in Warren. [@2] Each faces
armed robbery and conspiracy to commit armed robbery, which are punishable by up
to life in prison on conviction. [@1] Bond was set at $500,000 cash or surety
for each defendant, with a steel-cuff GPS tether required if bond is posted.
[@2]

At about 2:43 a.m. on 14 October 2026, Warren police were dispatched to the
commercial property after an alarm was triggered. [@2] The group allegedly
arrived in a U-Haul truck and a Chevrolet Camaro, threatened an on-site guard
with a firearm, took the guard's weapon and forced entry into the building. [@1]
Officers met both vehicles as they allegedly tried to escape, the Camaro struck
a patrol vehicle and two suspects were arrested. [@2] The U-Haul fled south on
Van Dyke Avenue until tire-deflation devices stopped it near 11 Mile Road, and
four people who ran on foot near Van Dyke and Lantz Street in Detroit were
captured. [@2] Police said a search of the U-Haul uncovered more than 200 pounds
of stolen marijuana, about 90.7 kg, plus other evidence linked to the alleged
robbery. [@2]

The defendants were identified as Marcellus Huntley, 59, Maya Mitchell, 24,
Joshua Dykes, 33, Kyle Turner, 38, James Claybrooks-Wells, 32, and Aaron
Marshall, 24. [@3] Pleas of not guilty were entered at the arraignment. [@3] All
six are scheduled for a probable cause conference on 29 October 2026. [@2]
