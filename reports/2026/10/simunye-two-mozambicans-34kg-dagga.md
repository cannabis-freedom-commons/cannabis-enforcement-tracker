Simunye Police arrested and charged two Mozambican nationals aged 22 and 24
after allegedly finding them with a combined 34.4kg of dagga. [@1] The two were
taken to Simunye Police Station and spent the night in custody before appearing
at the Siteki Magistrate's Court on Friday. [@1] The court remanded them back
into custody until Tuesday for continuation of the matter. [@1]

Police said the two loads weighed 17.8kg and 16.6kg respectively. [@1] Police
said the men held the dagga without a permit or licence. [@1] The two were
believed to be transporting the dagga for smuggling into Mozambique. [@1]

The two men had hitched a ride from Lonhlupheko to Simunye while carrying the
suspected dagga. [@1] They did not know the man who offered them a lift in his
private vehicle was a police officer attached to the Criminal Investigations
Department. [@1] The officer was travelling from Siteki to Ngomane Police Post
when he allegedly noticed a strong smell of dagga inside the vehicle. [@1]
Rather than confronting them, the officer kept driving while discreetly
communicating with police officers at Simunye. [@1] Police officers waited for
the vehicle at the Simunye Filling Station, where the two suspects were
arrested. [@1] The arrest happened on Thursday, 1 October 2026 at around 8.30pm
at the Simunye Filling Station. [@1]
