Officers from Thames Valley Police's Incident and Crime Response Team executed a
Section 23 Misuse of Drugs Act warrant at a property in Aylesbury on Thursday 29
October 2026. [@2] The search followed suspected drug dealing in and around the
address. [@1] Police arrested one unnamed man on suspicion of being in
possession of and concerned in the supply of cocaine and cannabis, as well as
possessing criminal property. [@1] The suspect remained in police custody while
enquiries continued. [@2]

The allegations were specified as possession with intent to supply Class A
cocaine and Class B cannabis, being concerned in the supply of both drugs, and
possessing or acquiring criminal property. [@2] Police described the warrant as
part of ongoing efforts to tackle drug-related crime and disrupt suspected
suppliers in local communities. [@2]

After the arrest police said they cannot keep up their vital work without the
support from the wider community and public. [@2] They asked anyone seeing
suspicious activity to report it via 999 or 101 and to keep supporting Thames
Valley Police efforts to fight and disrupt crime. [@1]
