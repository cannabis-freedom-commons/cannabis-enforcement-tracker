Police in Bihar's Madhubani district arrested two men on Thursday after stopping
their car on national highway NH-27 and finding 73 kg of ganja (cannabis) in it.
[@1] Officers from Bhairavsthan police station acted on a tip-off that a car was
carrying a large quantity of ganja from Triveniganj in Supaul district towards
Delhi. [@2] They set up a vehicle check on NH-27 near Khadrak village. [@2] A
suspected car coming from Triveniganj was signalled to stop, and when those
inside tried to flee, the team surrounded the car, stopped it and began
searching it. [@2]

The ganja was hidden under the boot and the seats. [@2] The recovered ganja was
seized and sealed. [@2] The car used to carry it was also taken into police
custody. [@2]
