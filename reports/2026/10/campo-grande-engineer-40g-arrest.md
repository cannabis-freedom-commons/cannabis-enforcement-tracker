Police arrested 41-year-old clinical engineer Marcelo Alves Namba in flagrante
in central Campo Grande on the night of 31 October 2026 after he pursued a
Military Police patrol car. [@1] Tactical Force officers on routine patrol saw a
silver Fiat Palio following their vehicle at high speed, honking and flashing
its high beams insistently. [@1] Officers said the car almost hit the rear of
the patrol car, ran a red light and kept running red lights, endangering other
drivers on the urban road. [@1] They pursued the suspect and intercepted the car
on Rua 15 de Novembro. [@2]

A search of the vehicle found 40 grams of marijuana divided among three
containers, a plastic pot, a market bag and the centre console. [@2] The driver
said he grew the herb and had authorisation, but presented no supporting
document. [@1] He admitted having drunk wine but refused the breathalyser test.
[@1] Officers drew up a finding of intoxication recording red eyes, alcoholic
breath, exaltation and altered speech. [@1] The car was removed to the Detran
impound lot. [@1]

He was taken initially without handcuffs to Denar, the specialised
anti-narcotics station, for weighing of the drug, but then acted aggressively
during transport. [@2] He kicked the structure of the patrol vehicle, threw his
body against its interior walls and shouted claiming he was being assaulted.
[@1] He swore at the team and made direct death threats against the officers and
their families, saying he would go after each one as soon as he left prison.
[@1] Officers used moderate force to handcuff him because of self-harm and the
risk of breaking open the mobile cell. [@1] He was delivered with light
abrasions to Depac Cepol, the community emergency station, where he remained
silent during police questioning. [@1] He faces proceedings for drunk driving,
threat, contempt, refusal to provide identity details and drug possession for
personal use. [@1]
