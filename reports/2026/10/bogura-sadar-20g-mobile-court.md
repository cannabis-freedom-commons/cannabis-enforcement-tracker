## The drive

Between 4:00 and 4:30 pm on Saturday 24 October 2026, an operational team of Circle Ka under the Department of Narcotics Control in Bogura ran an anti-drug drive in the Central Mosque and Sweeper Colony areas of Bogura Sadar Upazila. [@1] Officers caught two men with 20 g of cannabis during the drive, according to DNC sources. [@1] The men were Md. Shafiqul Islam, 55, son of the late Taiobor of the Malgram area, and Md. Murad Hossain, 35, son of the late Rofiq of the Colony area. [@1]

## The punishment

A mobile court sentenced both men to one month of simple imprisonment and fined each of them BDT 50. [@1] The account names the two convicts and the areas of the drive but gives no further detail on either man's case. [@1] The drive and the sentences were reported from Dhaka on 2 November 2026.
