Two Nigerian nationals were arrested by the Groupe de Lutte Anti Drogue (GLAD)
on 7 and 9 October 2026, the first individual in Grand-Yoff and the second
individual in Parcelles Assainies. [@1] The arrests were made in the framework
of the fight against the trafficking of narcotics. [@1] The Groupe de Lutte Anti
Drogue is a unit of the gendarmerie. [@2]

On 7 October 2026 the gendarmes of GLAD arrested the first individual in
Grand-Yoff, and he was pursued for suspected drug trafficking and for criminal
association. [@2] On 9 October 2026 GLAD arrested a second individual, also a
Nigerian national, in Parcelles Assainies for possession of chanvre indien. [@2]

The searches carried out by the gendarmes led to the discovery and seizure of a
few grams of cocaine and more than sixty-seven grams of chanvre indien, stated
as 67.4 grams. [@1] The two people under suspicion were placed in police custody
and were waiting there to be presented to the judicial authorities. [@1]
