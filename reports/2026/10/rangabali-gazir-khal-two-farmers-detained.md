Police detained two farmers at Gazir Khal in Char Montaz, Rangabali, after
cannabis plants were recovered from a red-spinach field. [@1] Lal shak means red
spinach, the crop growing in the plot where the plants were found. [@1] The
recovery and the detentions caused a stir in the area. [@1] The two detained
farmers say they were not involved in the matter and were victims of a
conspiracy. [@1] They say they did not know that cannabis plants were in their
field. [@1] Some local residents suspect the pair may have been framed over a
land dispute, though that claim has not been independently verified. [@1]

Local accounts say the matter came to light after cannabis plants were recovered
from the red-spinach field at Gazir Khal, and police then detained the two
farmers. [@1] It remains unclear how the plants reached the field, who planted
them, or whether the detained farmers were involved. [@1] Local residents say an
impartial investigation is needed to uncover what really happened. [@1] They say
investigators should examine whether the plants were planted deliberately and
whether a personal dispute or another motive lay behind the incident. [@1]

The specific allegations against the detained farmers have not been detailed.
[@1] The progress of the police investigation has not been detailed. [@1] Local
residents have demanded an inquiry to identify the source of the plants and
those involved in the incident. [@1] No number of plants, weight, charges, or
court outcome was reported. [@1]
