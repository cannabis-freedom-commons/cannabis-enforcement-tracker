Kyrgyzstan’s State Committee for National Security said on 23 October 2026 that four Kyrgyz citizens had been detained on suspicion of involvement in the illegal drug trade and placed in its pre-trial detention centre. [@1,2] The committee said its investigators and the State Customs Service had identified people suspected of moving, storing and distributing drugs in Kyrgyzstan in connection with shipments from Southeast Asia. [@1,2]

The announcement described investigations into several cross-border supply channels under the Criminal Code’s drug supply provision. [@1,2] The security service reported seizing marijuana, hashish and mephedrone with a combined estimated value of more than 15 million som. [@1,2] It did not give separate weights or values for the cannabis products. [@1,2]

The security service said it was still working to identify others involved. [@1] Kaktus.media published the service’s photographs of people and items connected with the operations described in the announcement. [@1]
