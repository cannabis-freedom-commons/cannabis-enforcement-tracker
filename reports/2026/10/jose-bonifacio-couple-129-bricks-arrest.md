Tactical Force officers of the Military Police arrested a man and a woman on
suspicion of drug trafficking and association for trafficking on the night of
Thursday the 29th in José Bonifácio, São Paulo. [@1] The case was formally
recorded as drug trafficking and association for trafficking. [@1] The man and
the woman were given arrest orders and taken to the on-duty police station. [@1]
The two remained at the disposition of the courts after being taken into
custody. [@1]

At a residence in the Jardim Bosque II neighbourhood, officers seized 129 bricks
of marijuana. [@1] In the same residence, officers also found approximately 2.7
kilos of cocaine, a separate non-cannabis drug that is not counted in the
cannabis figures. [@1] Officers found the drugs together with a precision scale
stored in one of the rooms of the house. [@1]

The Military Police said the team had been called after residents reported
people throwing packages of drugs onto the roofs of neighbouring houses. [@1]
During the subsequent checks, officers located one of the suspects and searched
the indicated property where the seizure was made. [@1]
