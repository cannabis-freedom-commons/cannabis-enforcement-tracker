Four Nicaraguan citizens were detained in Banco Grande village in Trojes
municipality, El Paraiso department in eastern Honduras. [@1] The operation was
carried out by DPI agents with support from the DNPSC and the Armed Forces under
Operation Shield of Honduras. [@1] The four men, aged 18 to 45 and resident in
Banco Grande, were caught in flagrante for alleged drug trafficking and illegal
possession of firearms. [@1] The four men face accusations of drug trafficking
and illegal possession of firearms. [@1]

Officers seized a brown rifle with no brand or serial number. [@1] Officers
seized three plastic bags of dry herb preliminarily described as suspected
marijuana. [@1] Officers also seized two mobile phones that will undergo expert
examination. [@1] Authorities said the nature of the suspected substance must
still be verified by technical procedures. [@1]

A police report preliminarily links the four detainees to a criminal structure
known as Los Nica. [@1] Authorities said the suspects had allegedly intimidated
local residents to stop them reporting the group's activities. [@1]
Investigators are examining a possible link between the detainees, marijuana
growing around Trojes and later transfer to Nicaragua. [@1] No plantation find
was confirmed during the operation and no related drug quantity was established.
[@1] After detention the suspects will be sent to the competent authorities for
proceedings under Honduran law. [@1]
