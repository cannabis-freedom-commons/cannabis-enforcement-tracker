A joint team of the 56th Battalion SSB Bathnaha Dumarbana border post and Ghurna
police was patrolling the India-Nepal border area in Araria district. [@2] A
joint SSB and police patrol recovered 84 kilograms of ganja concealed in sacks
near bamboo bushes by Dumarbana Yadav Tola in the Ghurna police station area on
the India-Nepal border. [@1] The smugglers fled from the spot during the
operation and no one was caught there. [@1]

The team spotted the suspicious sacks about 30 metres inside Indian territory
from border pillar number 192/5. [@2] The recovered ganja was handed to Ghurna
police for legal action. [@2] Station house officer Suman Kumari said a case was
registered over the recovery and further investigation was under way. [@2]

She said the campaign against drug smugglers in the border area would continue
and those involved would face strict action. [@2] The value of the ganja was
described only as running into lakhs of rupees. [@1]
