## Jailed at Huntingdon

Gjok Ndrevetaj, 33, of Heygate Avenue in Southend, appeared at Huntingdon Law Courts on 20 October 2026 and was jailed for three years and four months. [@1,2] He had pleaded guilty to being concerned in the supply of cannabis. [@1,2]

Road policing officers stopped his white Mercedes Sprinter box van southbound on the A1 at Norman Cross in Cambridgeshire on 2 March after noticing it was overweight. [@1,2] In the back they found eight boxes of cannabis with an estimated street value of up to £200,000. [@1,2] Officers also found plant pots, soil, clippings and heating-equipment boxes, described by police as the remnants of a cannabis factory. [@1,2]
