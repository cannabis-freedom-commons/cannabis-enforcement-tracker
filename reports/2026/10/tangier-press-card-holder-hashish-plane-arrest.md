Tangier's Anti-Gang Brigade arrested a man who holds a professional press card.
[@1] Officers detained him inside a cafe on the Mohammed VI corniche in Tangier.
[@2] The arrest was carried out under a wanted notice issued by the National
Brigade of the Royal Gendarmerie. [@1]

He was wanted on suspicion of links to an international drug-trafficking network
that used aircraft, and his name had appeared in the related investigations.
[@1] The case is described as international trafficking of hashish by aircraft.
[@1] After learning he was wanted, he had been using his brother's identity in
an apparent attempt to conceal his real identity. [@1]

He was placed under police custody, which was extended for further
investigation. [@1] He was due to be brought before the public prosecution in
Targuist on the Wednesday following the arrest. [@1] A second outlet says he was
transferred to the Targuist court for further investigation after the custody
period. [@3]

He runs a news website in Tangier, and his possession of the press card has
raised questions about the conditions and criteria for granting it. [@1]
