In the night from Monday to Tuesday, at about 2:30 a.m., police stopped a
38-year-old man in a street in central Tarbes, in the Hautes-Pyrenees, after
finding him carrying several drugs. [@1] He said the drugs found on him were
meant for his own personal use, rather than for sale. [@1] Officers held him in
garde a vue, the French police-custody detention, at the Tarbes police station
for questioning by investigators. [@2]

After his custody ended, he was summoned to answer for the facts on 18 January
under an ordonnance penale, a simplified procedure in which a judge can impose a
sentence without a public hearing. [@2]

During the street check, police found 23 grams of cannabis herb on him. [@2] The
same check also found 7 grams of cannabis resin on him, making 30 grams of
cannabis in total. [@2] Officers also found 0.5 gram of cocaine on him, which is
described here only to explain the mixed seizure and is not counted as cannabis.
[@3]
