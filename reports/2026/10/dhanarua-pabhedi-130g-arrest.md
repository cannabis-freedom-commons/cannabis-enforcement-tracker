Police in the Dhanarua police station area of Patna district, Bihar, arrested a
man at Pabhedi village on Saturday 31 October 2026 on suspicion of selling
ganja. [@1] He was identified as Omprakash Yadav, also known as Bhagwan, the son
of the late Bhuvneshwar Prasad of Pabhedi village; he was about 40 years old.
[@1]

Police recovered 64 small packets, known in Hindi as pudiya, containing a total
of 130 grams of ganja, the local name for cannabis, from his gumti, a small
roadside kiosk. [@2] Dhanarua station house officer Amit Kumar said police acted
on a tip-off that ganja was being sold in the area and checked the information
before moving. [@1] Kumar said Yadav was accused of keeping the drug for sale in
the kiosk. [@1]

The station house officer said legal proceedings in the case were continuing.
[@1] He said police were investigating where Yadav obtained the ganja and
whether other people were involved. [@1]
