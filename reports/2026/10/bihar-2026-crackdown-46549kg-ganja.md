Bihar police registered 4,232 cases under the NDPS Act, India's narcotics law,
and arrested 6,104 accused between the start of 2026 and 27 October 2026. [@1,2]
The 2026 figures were presented at a high-level review meeting chaired by Chief
Secretary Pratyaya Amrit on 4 November 2026. [@1] The prohibition and state
narcotics-control bureau presented the statewide enforcement account at that
meeting. [@1] By comparison, police recorded 2,583 cases and 3,562 arrests in
all of 2025. [@1,2]

Police said they seized 46,549.95 kg of ganja, the cannabis component recorded
here, by 27 October. [@1,2] Araria led the district figures with 10,573 kg of
ganja recovered. [@1] Supaul and Madhubani followed with 8,813 kg and 4,483 kg
of ganja respectively. [@1] The same drive also recovered 149.696 kg of heroin,
brown sugar and smack, alongside large numbers of pills, injections and codeine
syrup bottles, which are not counted as cannabis. [@1] Police also reported
1,156,380 pills and capsules, 492,870 injections and 511,988 bottles of codeine
cough syrup seized. [@1] Police said destruction of seized narcotics rose about
10.1 times over 2025 to reach 25,247 kg in 2026, a combined-drugs total not
counted as cannabis. [@1] Separately from cannabis, action against illegal opium
cultivation led to 37 cases against 198 accused and destruction of 512.235 acres
of opium crop in crop year 2025-26. [@1]

Officials said 1,652 cases had led to sentences for 2,225 accused between 2021
and August 2026 through 10 special courts. [@1,2] The MANAS portal supplied 3,718
inputs, leading to 138 first-information reports. [@1] The state ran a Nasha
Mukt Bihar Abhiyan awareness campaign from 27 October to 26 November 2026 and
ordered drug-free zones within 500 metres of educational institutions. [@1,2]

In heroin seizures, Patna and Purnea recorded the highest figures. [@2] Of the 512.235 acres of illicit opium crops destroyed, 508.755 acres were in Gayaji district. [@2]
