Basra Criminal Court sentenced a man to death for drug trafficking, Iraq’s Supreme Judicial Council announced on 27 October 2026. [@1,2] The council said the authorities had found hashish and methamphetamine in his possession and that he intended to sell both substances. [@1,2]

The council described the quantities as large but gave no weight for either drug. [@1,2] The court issued the sentence under the trafficking provision in Article 27(1) of Iraq’s 2017 narcotics and psychotropic substances law. [@1,2] The announcement did not name the man, give a date for the court hearing or say whether an appeal had been filed. [@2]
