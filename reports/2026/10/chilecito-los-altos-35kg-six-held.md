Gendarmes detained two men, aged 45 and 27, at a house in the Los Altos
neighbourhood of Chilecito, identifying the older man as the alleged ringleader
of a drug-selling network. [@1] A federal guarantees judge in Rosario, Santa Fe,
confirmed the charge and ordered 60 days of pretrial detention for six
defendants. [@1] Prosecutors from the regional NEA narcocriminal office accuse
the six of belonging to an organised structure that trafficked drugs between
February 2025 and October 2026, aggravated by the involvement of three or more
people. [@1] The 45-year-old moved between Monserrat in Buenos Aires, Rosario
and Chilecito with his 31-year-old partner, who allegedly handled sales
payments, transfers and logistics, while the 27-year-old allegedly sold the
substances in the Chilecito region. [@1] Retail sale in Rosario and Villa
Gobernador Gálvez was allegedly handled by a 29-year-old and a 21-year-old, with
the young woman the only one of the six to serve the order under house arrest.
[@1]

The Los Altos operation was one of 13 simultaneous raids carried out on 21
October by the National Gendarmerie. [@1] In that house officers seized 1,541.73
grams of buds, 1,596.91 grams of chopped cannabis and 429.55 grams of trimmings
from 31 seedlings, plus 66.46 grams of cocaine and mobile phones. [@1] Officers
also found 2,231 cannabis seeds and LED lights for an indoor greenhouse in the
Chilecito house. [@1] In a Chacarita house linked to a 62-year-old alleged
chemical supplier who pressed pills sold as QDance and Rolls Royce, gendarmes
found a climate-controlled grow with 12 LED panels and almost 10 kilos of
marijuana, plus money, computers and seven hard drives. [@1] The 62-year-old is
described as the chemical supplier in the City of Buenos Aires and Olivos who
pressed those pills. [@1]

Prosecutors say the organisation stockpiled marijuana and sold tusi, ecstasy,
ketamine and LSD to Santa Fe, Córdoba and Buenos Aires through cultivation,
transport, distribution and sale. [@1] The investigation began in early 2025
after 40 kilos of marijuana were seized in Rosario. [@1]
