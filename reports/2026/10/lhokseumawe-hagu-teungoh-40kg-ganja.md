Lhokseumawe police uncovered a cannabis case in Desa Hagu Teungoh, Banda Sakti,
Kota Lhokseumawe, on 20 October 2026. [@2] Officers detained a man identified as
Mu in the Hagu Teungoh case. [@1] Officers seized 40,000.128 grams of dried
cannabis, one motorcycle and one mobile phone in that case. [@2] Mu told
officers he sold the cannabis to friends at up to 2 kilograms a day for
Rp800,000 per kilogram. [@1] He said he only received the cannabis from another
person to resell and did not know where it was grown. [@1] He said he was tasked
with selling 40.128 kilograms for shared profit, had not yet split any proceeds
and could not recall how much he had sold. [@1]

Kapolres Lhokseumawe AKBP Ahzan presented the seizure at a press conference at
Mapolres on 5 November 2026. [@1] The case was handled by Satresnarkoba, the
Polres Lhokseumawe narcotics investigation unit. [@1] Police said the suspects
face Articles 111, 112, 114, 117 and 119 of Narcotics Law Number 35 of 2009
and/or Criminal Code Law Number 1 of 2023 in conjunction with Law Number 1 of
2026 on sentencing adjustments. [@1]
