Aurora police arrested 33-year-old Julio C. Chairez, described as an active gang
member, on 27 October 2026 after a narcotics search in Montgomery. [@2] A second
Aurora resident, 33-year-old Keena L. Walker, was also arrested and charged with
child endangerment and issued a notice to appear. [@2] Chairez was charged with
manufacture or delivery and possession counts for cocaine and cannabis, eight
counts of unlawful possession of a weapon by a felon, and one count of
possessing a firearm with a defaced serial number. [@1] He remains in custody at
the Kendall County Jail with his next court appearance scheduled for 6 November.
[@1]

Investigators with the Aurora Police Department's Special Operations Group
executed the warrant at about 7 p.m. in the 100 block of Hillstone Road in
Montgomery as part of an ongoing narcotics investigation. [@1] Police said
investigators recovered 128 grams of suspected cocaine, 585 grams of suspected
cannabis and 251 grams of brownish powder that later field-tested positive for
heroin and fentanyl, plus digital scales and packaging materials. [@1] Police
said they also recovered seven firearms, including a handgun with a defaced
serial number and a sawed-off shotgun, plus ammunition and about $1,214 in cash.
[@2]

The Kendall County state's attorney's office filed the charges, and police said
additional charges were pending over the powder that tested positive for heroin
and fentanyl. [@2] Aurora Police Chief Matt Thomas said recovering the weapons
and suspected narcotics was an important step in reducing risks from illegal
distribution, and police said the investigation remains ongoing. [@1]
