A man was arrested for drug trafficking and illegal gun possession by Brigada
Militar units in Bairro Coimbra, Encruzilhada do Sul, on the afternoon of Friday
30 October 2026. [@2] The operation was launched at about 14:30 by the 1st Shock
Police Battalion (1º BPChq) and its Special Patrols (Patres). [@1] The officers
were on patrol in the town when they saw movement that looked like drug sales.
[@1] They stopped him on suspicion of drug dealing. [@1]

A personal search found a loaded 9mm pistol whose serial number had been
removed. [@1] Officers seized a magazine and 13 rounds of 9mm ammunition. [@1]
They also seized 33 portions of cocaine, 87 portions of marijuana, a precision
scale, a mobile phone, a shoulder bag and cash. [@2]

The man was handcuffed and taken for a medical assessment before being
presented, together with the seized material, at the emergency police station
(DPPA) in Santa Cruz do Sul for the required legal procedures. [@1] He was then
taken to the prison. [@1]
