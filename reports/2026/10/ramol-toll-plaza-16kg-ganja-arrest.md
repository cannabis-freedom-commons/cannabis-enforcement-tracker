Ramol Police arrested 39-year-old Morbi resident Bhavik Dhanjibhai Kanjibhai
Mordiya near the Expressway toll plaza in Ahmedabad on the morning of October
29. [@1] Officers in plain clothes conducting vehicle checks stopped Mordiya
after noticing him walking suspiciously with two trolley bags. [@1] The Skybags
and Safari bags held 16 plastic packets of suspected ganja, which an FSL
officer's spot examination indicated appeared to be cannabis. [@1]

Police valued the 16.130 kg of ganja at ₹8,06,500 and the total seized property
at ₹8,17,700. [@1] Mordiya allegedly said he had procured the ganja for ₹80,000
from Ashwini Bag of Balangir in Odisha to deliver it to Pankaj alias Dhaval
Ganeshbhai Fefar of Mahendranagar in Morbi. [@1] Police registered a case under
the NDPS Act against the alleged Odisha supplier, the intended Morbi recipient
and others, with PI Pratikumar N. Zinzuwadia reportedly leading the further
investigation. [@1]

Investigators recovered a ₹3,499 Vishal Mega Mart bill showing Mordiya had
bought a trolley bag in Balangir, allegedly a new green Safari bag bought to
carry the ganja from Odisha to Gujarat. [@1] Mordiya allegedly travelled by
train or private bus to Vadodara and planned to change vehicles on the
Expressway toward Morbi via Ahmedabad before being intercepted on foot at the
toll plaza. [@1] DivyaBhaskar's account of the same interception adds that
Mordiya said debts from online gaming led him into carrying the ganja, and puts
his payment for the trip at ₹50,000. [@2]
