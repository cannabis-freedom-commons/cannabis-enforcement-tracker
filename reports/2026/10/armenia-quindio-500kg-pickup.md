Police in Armenia, the capital of Colombia's Quindío department, found 266
packages of marijuana inside a vehicle on a public street in the south of the
city on 27 October 2026. [@3] The vehicle was a white pickup truck. [@1] It
stood in front of a commercial premises at Carrera 18 and Calle 62. [@1] The
driver was not found. [@1] No arrests were reported in the operation. [@2]

Officers had been looking for a vehicle that police radio reported as possibly
heading for one of the roads into Armenia. [@1] They found the white pickup
while one of its wheels was being adjusted. [@1] Police said the load weighed
more than 500 kilograms. [@1]

President Abelardo de la Espriella announced the seizure on X. [@4] He said
there was no truce against drug trafficking. [@2] He said the marijuana would
not reach the streets or keep financing criminal groups. [@2] The marijuana and
the pickup were placed at the disposal of the competent authorities. [@1] NTN24
reported that they were handed to the Fiscalía General de la Nación, Colombia's
attorney general's office. [@3] The police gave no account of where the shipment
came from or where it was going, and NTN24 reported that the identities of those
behind it had not been confirmed. [@3]
