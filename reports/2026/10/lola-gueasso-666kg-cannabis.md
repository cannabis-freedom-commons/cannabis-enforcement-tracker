Guinea's territorial gendarmerie in Lola seized 666 kg of cannabis and 16 kg of
cannabis seeds in the Guéasso sub-prefecture on the night of Thursday 29 October
2026. [@1,2] The operation began at about 2am in the Dongola sector of the Zessou
district. [@1,2]

It followed a complaint to the justice of the peace in Lola and the discovery of
about one hectare of cannabis hidden inside a banana plantation. [@1,2]
Investigators identified a suspected owner, arrested in Lola on 27 October; he
admitted the facts and named other growers, according to Colonel Pépé Augustin
Kolié, commander of the Lola gendarmerie company. [@1,2]

Searches then found 66 sacks of cannabis weighing 666 kg in total, along with the
16 kg of seeds. [@1,2] Four people were arrested. [@1,2]

The results were presented to a regional delegation of the defence and security
forces led by Brigadier General Mamadi Condé, commander of the 4th military region
in N'Zérékoré. [@1,2] The gendarmerie said its investigations continue and that
individual responsibility would be established in court. [@1,2]
