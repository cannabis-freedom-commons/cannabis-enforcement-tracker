Customs officers at Azerbaijan’s South Astara checkpoint inspected a truck carrying fresh lettuce from Iran to Russia. [@1,2,3] The State Customs Committee said officers from the Astara Customs Department used a fixed X-ray scanner and a customs dog to examine the vehicle. [@1,2,3] The dog alerted to a pallet in the trailer. [@1,2,3]

Officers searched the pallet and found 90 packages hidden under the lettuce. [@1,2,3] The committee identified the contents as marijuana and gave a total net weight of 97 kilograms and 685 grams. [@1,2,3] Photographs published by Report.az show the dog inspecting the cargo, an officer holding a package and the seized packages laid out at the Astara customs office. [@1]

The customs committee described the shipment as an attempt to move the drugs through Azerbaijan in transit and said an investigation was under way. [@2,3] Its statement did not give the driver’s identity or say whether anyone had been arrested. [@2]
