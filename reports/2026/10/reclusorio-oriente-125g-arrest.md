Custodians of the Secretaría de Seguridad Ciudadana (SSC) detained a 48-year-old
woman who allegedly tried to introduce drugs into the Reclusorio Preventivo
Varonil Oriente in Iztapalapa. [@1] The detention occurred during security
screening of visitors to persons deprived of liberty at the prison in the Lomas
de San Lorenzo neighbourhood. [@1] Officers approached her after noticing
evasive behaviour and carried out a preventive inspection. [@1] During the
inspection they found a wrapper hidden among her clothing containing green, dry
herb with characteristics similar to marijuana. [@1] The wrapper held
approximately 125 grams of suspected marijuana. [@1] She was informed of her
constitutional rights and transferred with the seized material to the Ministerio
Público, the public prosecutor's office, which will determine her legal
situation. [@1]

Between January 2025 and October 2026, the prison system's undersecretariat
recorded 66 people detained for allegedly trying to bring prohibited substances
into the capital's 13 prisons. [@1] Those seized products included marijuana
alongside cocaine, crystal meth and alcohol, so the 66-person total is not a
cannabis-only figure. [@1] The report presents visitor screenings as a detection
point for drugs entering the prisons, while noting that arrests alone do not
show how much drugs enters or the scale of the supply networks. [@1] Detentions
do not by themselves amount to a finding of guilt, with responsibility to be
established by the Ministerio Público as each case is investigated. [@1]
