The drug and football-hooligan unit of the Bydgoszcz city police searched the
home of a 23-year-old man in a village in Koronowo commune, Bydgoszcz County, on
Tuesday 27 October 2026. [@2] Detectives had established that he might hold
significant amounts of dried plant material and grow cannabis. [@2] In his room
officers found several dozen grams of dried plant material divided into portions
in bags. [@3] In the cellar they found a jute sack holding a plastic package
with 1.3 kg of dried plant material. [@1]

In a utility room locked with a padlock they found two grow tents and equipment
for growing cannabis, including lamps, fans, heaters, dehumidifiers and air
filters. [@1] Eighteen cannabis bushes at various stages of growth were growing
inside. [@1] Officers seized more than half a kilogram of dried material there.
[@1] The 23-year-old was arrested and taken to a police detention facility, and
the plants and dried material went to the police forensic laboratory. [@1]
Preliminary tests confirmed that the dried material was nearly 2 kg of
marijuana, police said. [@4]

He was charged with possessing a significant quantity of drugs and cultivating
cannabis. [@1] On 28 October, at the request of police and the prosecutor, a
judge remanded him in custody for three months. [@3] The offences carry up to 10
years in prison. [@1]

The Bydgoszcz city police said its officers did not let up in the fight against
drug crime. [@5] The account was published by the Bydgoszcz city police. [@1]
