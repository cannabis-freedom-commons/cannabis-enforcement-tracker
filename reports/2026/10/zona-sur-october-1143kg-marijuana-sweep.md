The Agencia Estatal de Investigación seized more than a tonne of marijuana in
the southern zone of Chihuahua during October 2026, mainly in Parral and
Guadalupe y Calvo. [@1] Agents seized 983 kilograms of marijuana on 11 October
in Santa Rosa, municipality of Hidalgo del Parral. [@2] Agents seized another
160 kilograms of marijuana on 20 October in Baborigame, municipality of
Guadalupe y Calvo. [@2] Together the two operations took 1,143 kilograms of
marijuana out of circulation, not counting smaller amounts seized in the same
period. [@1]

The agency reported 45 arrest warrants executed and 23 flagrancia detentions for
various offences, for a total of 68 detained-person interventions. [@1] The
agency said it carried out 198 operations in southern-zone municipalities and
five searches to obtain evidence for investigation files. [@1] Agents recovered
12 vehicles with theft reports and secured 21 more units for document
irregularities or possible links to criminal acts. [@1] Officers seized four
firearms, two long guns and two short guns, and 61 cartridges of various
calibres. [@1]

The seizures also included 4.1 grams of cocaine and 4.48 grams of cristal,
besides various marijuana portions. [@1] The prosecutor reported six people with
prior reports were located, without giving details of the circumstances. [@1]
The results correspond to activities during October 2026 and form part of the
monthly balance presented by the Agencia Estatal de Investigación in the
southern zone. [@1]
