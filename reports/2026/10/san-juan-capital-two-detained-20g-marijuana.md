Police detained two young men aged 23 and 24 in Capital, San Juan, after they
tried to escape a police checkpoint while travelling in a Ford Fiesta. [@1] The
occupants had been travelling south along Mendoza street and ignored police
signals to stop for identification at 9 de Julio, continuing on until they were
intercepted. [@1] The driver was identified as Cerdera, 23, who behaved in a
hostile manner and told officers that they were carrying drugs, and his
companion was identified as Navarro, 24. [@1] At the officers' request, Cerdera
agreed to show the substances he was carrying with Navarro. [@1]

The stop and interception were handled by Bicycle Police officers at the
intersection of Entre Ríos and 9 de Julio in the city centre. [@2] Illegal Drugs
personnel carried out field tests that were positive for 9 grams of ketamine, a
one-gram ecstasy stone and 20 grams of marijuana, plus another bag of strongly
smelling plant material. [@1] The police account separately itemised the same
seizure as 9 grams of ketamine, a one-gram ecstasy stone and 20 grams of
marijuana, plus a bag of other strongly smelling plant material. [@2]

Officers seized the substances and referred the case to the Federal Prosecutor's
Unit of San Juan. [@1] Both young men remained detained while the investigation
seeks to establish the circumstances of the incident and their responsibility.
