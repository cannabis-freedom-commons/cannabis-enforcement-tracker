Federal Highway Police data for the first ten months of 2026 show skunk, a
cannabis variety with a higher THC concentration, overtaking cocaine to lead the
volume of drugs seized in the Corumbá region. [@1] Between January and October
the force recorded 193 occurrences related to drug trafficking in its area of
operation. [@1] Officers seized 5,841.092 kg of skunk in the period, about 5.8
tonnes, against 3,519.711 kg of cocaine, 368.910 kg of marijuana and 100.350 kg
of hashish. [@1]

Alexandre Luiz Zortea, institutional communication officer of the 3rd PRF
station in Corumbá, said cocaine had historically led seizures in the region but
that the numbers changed in the last three years. [@1] Zortea said skunk had
already taken first place among seized drugs in 2024 and 2025, a trend
continuing in 2026. [@1] One hypothesis he gave is that Bolivia, long a cocaine
producer, has expanded production of the cannabis the force finds coming in
across the Bolivian border, though he said no studies prove it. [@1]

The force's area lies on the border with Bolivia, which the report describes as
a corridor for drugs bound for other parts of Brazil, checked on highways
including BR-262 and the Bioceanic route by the PRF, Federal Police, Military
Police and Receita Federal. [@1]
