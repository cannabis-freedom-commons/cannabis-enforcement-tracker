Officers of the Anti-Drug and Smuggling Unit (ADSU), the Mauritian police's drug
squad, searched the home of a 47-year-old technician in Wooton at about 20:35 on
Friday 30 October 2026. [@1] The search took place at the technician's home in
Wooton, where he lives. [@1] The man was arrested after the seizure and placed
in police detention. [@1] He was held in police custody while the investigation
continued, rather than in prison. [@1]

During the search, the officers found and seized 4,264 cannabis seeds. [@1] The
seeds weighed 50.70 grams in total. [@1] Investigators also found a piece of
printed paper holding 3.47 grams of cannabis. [@1] The cannabis was found on the
paper, separately from the seeds. [@1]

The investigation is continuing. [@1] No charges or court date have been
announced. [@1] The technician has not been named. [@1]
