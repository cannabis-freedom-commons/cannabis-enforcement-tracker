Randolph Jason Victor, of Lowlands, Christ Church, reappeared before Justice
Douglas Frederick in the No. 2 Supreme Court. [@1] He had previously admitted
illegally possessing a 12-gauge shotgun and nine rounds of ammunition. [@1] He
also pleaded guilty to possessing 146.5 kilogrammes of cannabis, having a
traffickable quantity of the drug and possessing 1.04 kilogrammes of MDMA
tablets, also known as Ecstasy or Molly. [@1] On January 17, 2025, police
removed him from a flight bound for St Vincent at Grantley Adams International
Airport, arrested him on suspicion of committing the offences and took him to
Oistins Police Station. [@1]

The bedroom search uncovered 40 taped packages and a bag of orange tablets,
while the bed search revealed a black firearm, ammunition, a ski mask and a
passport-sized photograph of Victor, with another round found in the firearm's
chamber on examination. [@1] The cannabis had an estimated street value of $586
000, while the 2 239 MDMA tablets had an estimated street value of $44 500. [@1]

Victor is represented by attorney Kendrid Sargeant. [@1] A few days after his
arrest, he took police to an area in Kendall Hill, Christ Church, where he said
he had found the firearm, and to an area at Brownes Beach, where he said he had
received the drugs. [@1] The case continues on December 15. [@1]
