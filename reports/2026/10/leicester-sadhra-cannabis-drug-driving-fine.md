Police stopped warehouse worker Manjinder Sadhra, 38, on the night of 5 August
2026 because the car had no MOT. [@1] They found a cannabis grinder on the
passenger seat and could smell cannabis. [@1] He failed a roadside drug test and
was arrested, and a search of the Vauxhall Corsa found a quantity of cannabis.
[@1]

He had pleaded guilty to driving while under the influence of cannabis and
possessing cannabis. [@1] Defending himself, he said: "I was in the wrong." [@1]
He called it "just one silly mistake" and told the court he worked in a
warehouse and cared for his elderly father, with whom he lived in Fernhurst
Road, Braunstone Town. [@1]

He was fined £323 and ordered to pay £85 in court costs and a £129 victim
surcharge. [@1] The court also banned him from driving for 12 months. [@1]
