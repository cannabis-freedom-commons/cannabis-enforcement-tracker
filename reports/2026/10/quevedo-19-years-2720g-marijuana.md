A court in Quevedo, in Ecuador's Los Ríos province, sentenced David T. to 19 years
in prison for trafficking marijuana, the prosecutor's office said on 16 October
2026. [@1]

The case began on 26 September 2025, when soldiers acting on a tip searched a house
in the 7 de Octubre parish of Quevedo and found a person and several packages of
marijuana inside, according to the prosecutor's office. [@1] Police officers then
inspected the property and found wrappers containing marijuana and other evidence.
[@1]

A chemical analysis confirmed the substance was marijuana and recorded a weight of
2,720 grams, or 2.72 kilograms. [@1] The prosecutor's office charged David T. with
trafficking controlled substances at high scale under article 220(1)(c) of the
criminal code, an offence punishable by 19 to 22 years. [@1]

The Tribunal de Garantías Penales accepted the prosecution's evidence, including
testimony from the soldiers and police who carried out the search and the weighing
report, and convicted him. [@1]
