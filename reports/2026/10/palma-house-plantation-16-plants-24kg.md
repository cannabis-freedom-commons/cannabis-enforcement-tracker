Spain's National Police dismantled a marijuana plantation inside a house in
Palma and arrested the man described as responsible for the drugs for an offence
against public health. [@1] The arrest was carried out by Grupo II de
Estupefacientes, the National Police narcotics unit. [@1] Investigators said
they had learned that marijuana was being cultivated in a Palma home for later
sale and distribution. [@1] After further inquiries, they confirmed that the
occupant kept a plantation and cultivation materials in his dwelling. [@1] The
arrest took place on the previous Wednesday. [@1]

Officers who entered the property found 16 marijuana plants measuring between
three and four metres tall. [@1] They seized 21 kilograms of bud-covered
branches at the property. [@1] Investigators also seized three kilograms of buds
described as ready to distribute or consume. [@1]

After the operation, police invited the public to report drug trafficking
anonymously by email to antidroga@policia.es or through a dedicated section of
the police website. [@1] Reports can also be made directly to citizen
participation officers or by calling 091. [@1]
