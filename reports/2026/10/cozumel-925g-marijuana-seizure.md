On 28 October 2026, authorities seized a package containing 925 grams of dried
herb with characteristics similar to marijuana in Cozumel. [@2] The package was
located during prevention and surveillance patrols on the Costera Oriente
highway. [@2] The item was a compacted block wrapped in brown packing tape
containing green dried herb. [@3] The find was made by the Citizen Security
Secretariat's Investigation Directorate with the Navy, the National Guard,
Defence and Cozumel municipal police. [@3]

Officers placed the package at the disposal of the specialised narcomenudeo
prosecution for investigation. [@2] Authorities later presented the Cozumel
seizure among weekly coordinated-operation results covering 26 October to 1
November in Quintana Roo. [@1] In that broader weekly account, authorities
reported 219 detained people and 3,112 seized doses of various illicit
substances, plus 61 recovered vehicles and a seized firearm with magazines and
cartridges. [@1]

The weekly account singles out the Cozumel seizure as the marijuana case among
operations involving extortion groups and various narcotics. [@1] One
highlighted Benito Juárez detention involved 62 doses of various narcotics. [@1]
Another highlighted Puerto Morelos detention involved 32 doses of narcotics.
