On 3 October 2026, Guatemala's National Civil Police, through its Subdirectorate General for Anti-Narcotics Information Analysis (SGAIA), and the Ministry of National Defense carried out an eradication operation at Cooperativa Ixmucané in Las Cruces municipality, Petén. [@1] SGAIA said the team found a plot of about 660 square metres containing 7,920 cannabis plants, about one metre tall and at a productive stage. [@1] The police valued the plants at Q3,960,000. [@2]

TV Azteca Guatemala reported that authorities located and destroyed a marijuana planting in Petén on the same date, confirming the 7,920-plant count and estimated value. [@2] The site was described as a cooperative, rather than a specific farm or road address. [@1] SGAIA said the work formed part of joint police and defense efforts against drug production and to contribute to community security. [@1] The reports do not identify a landholder or say whether anyone was arrested. [@1,2] They also do not state how the official valuation was calculated or whether any plants were harvested before the operation. [@1,2]
