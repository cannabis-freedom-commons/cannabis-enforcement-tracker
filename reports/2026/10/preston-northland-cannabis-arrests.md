Preston police officers, with assistance from the Dog Squad and Transit
officers, targeted drug and weapons offences at Northland's retail hub in an
operation that ran on 20 and 29 October. [@1] Police arrested 20 people in the
operation, including 15 for drug possession. [@1] Police arrested 20 people in
an operation targeting drug activity and weapons in Northland's retail hub in
October. [@2]

A 28-year-old Whittlesea man was arrested after police allegedly located
weapons, cannabis and methylamphetamine. [@1] He was charged with possess
imitation firearm, possess prohibited weapon (x6), possess controlled weapon
without lawful excuse and drug offences and remanded to appear at court at a
later date. [@1] A 49-year-old Reservoir man was arrested after officers
allegedly located knuckle dusters and cannabis. [@1] He was charged with possess
a drug of dependence and a prohibited weapon and bailed to appear at court at a
later date. [@1]

Officers searched upwards of 50 people and actioned 10 outstanding matters
during the operation. [@1] Nine weapons were seized, including an imitation
firearm, a baton, knuckle dusters and knives. [@1] Passive Alert Detection (PAD)
dogs were utilised during the operation to detect illicit drugs. [@1]
