Drug-control officers detained two residents of Kamskiye Polyany with almost a
kilogram of marijuana on the Chistopol–Nizhnekamsk road in Tatarstan. [@1] The
republic's Interior Ministry directorate reported the detentions. [@1] Both
suspects have previous convictions for murder. [@1] The 43-year-old detainee
additionally has convictions for theft, robbery and unlawful drug trafficking.
[@1] Both men were questioned after their detention. [@1] They were placed under
a written undertaking not to leave pending the proceedings. [@1] Police said the
men face three to ten years of imprisonment. [@1] Investigative steps continue
to establish all the circumstances of the case. [@1]

Officers of the Chistopol drug-control unit seized a polymer bag containing
plant material from the men. [@1] An expert examination established that the
substance was marijuana. [@1] Police put the weight of the drug at about 890
grams. [@1] A criminal case was opened over the seizure. [@1]
