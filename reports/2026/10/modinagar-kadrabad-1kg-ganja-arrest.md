Kadrabad outpost police in Modinagar, Ghaziabad arrested 50-year-old Sanjay
Kumar, a resident of Kadrabad near the Rori railway crossing. [@1] Outpost
in-charge Narendra Kumar's patrol team cordoned the road to Naya Kadrabad after
an informer's tip that a man was carrying ganja home along the cremation-ground
road late Tuesday night. [@1] Officers signalled the approaching man to stop,
caught him when he tried to turn back, and searched him in the presence of ACP
Modinagar Shailendra Kumar Singh, registering a case under Sections 8 and 20 of
the NDPS Act. [@1]

The search recovered 1 kg 40 grams of illegal ganja and 100 rupees in cash from
his possession. [@1]
