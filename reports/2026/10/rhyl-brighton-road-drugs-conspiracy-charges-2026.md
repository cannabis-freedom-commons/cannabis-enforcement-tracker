Six people have now been charged in connection with an alleged drugs conspiracy
in Rhyl, after a sixth defendant appeared at Mold Crown Court on 6 November
2026. [@1] William Foulkes, 32, of Cae Derwen, Llanfair Talhaiarn, is accused of
conspiring to supply cannabis and cocaine. [@1]

The first five were charged after about ten officers and a police dog were
called to Brighton Road in Rhyl at about 8am on Wednesday, 7 October 2026. [@2]
Armed police attended a property in Rhyl that morning, and five people were
charged with drug supply offences. [@2] Four of them face conspiring to supply
cannabis and cocaine: Kieron Bentley, 31, of Brighton Road; Mathew Saunders, 34,
of Wellington Road; Grace Coxon, 23, of Rhys Avenue, Kinmel Bay; and her brother
Alex Coxon, 28, who was at HMP Featherstone in Staffordshire. [@1] Matthew
Ridyard, 29, of Bath Street, Rhyl, is charged only in relation to cannabis
supply. [@1] Saunders and Alex Coxon also face charges of conspiring to bring
drugs into a prison. [@1] Saunders is accused specifically of trying to bring
trainers with drugs hidden in them into HMP Berwyn in Wrexham. [@1]

The five were remanded into custody when they appeared at Llandudno Magistrates'
Court on 8 October 2026. [@2] All six appeared at Mold Crown Court on 6
November, but entered no pleas. [@1] They are due to enter pleas on 11 December
2026, and any trial would begin on 1 February 2027 and is expected to last four
weeks. [@1] The alleged conspiracy is said to have run from December 2024 until
October 2026. [@1] All the defendants are in custody except Grace Coxon, who is
on conditional bail. [@1]
