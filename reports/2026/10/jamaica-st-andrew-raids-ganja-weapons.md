Police arrested two men and a woman during a series of raids in St Andrew on
Friday 2 October 2026, seizing marijuana worth about $500,000 and what the force
described as an assortment of offensive weapons. [@1] The seizures were made in
the Highlight View community and the Half-Way Tree business district. [@1]

Between 4:00 a.m. and 10:00 a.m., a police team carried out snap raids targeting
suspected extortionists and gang members operating in the areas. [@1] Several
premises were searched, and the marijuana and weapons were found during those
searches. [@1] The police withheld the identities of the three people arrested
pending further investigation. [@1]

The force said the operation formed part of its efforts to disrupt criminal
networks involved in extortion, gang activity and the illicit drugs-for-guns
trade. [@1] It did not give the weight of the marijuana, describe the weapons,
announce charges or give a court date. [@1]
