Maldonado's Anti-Drug Brigade carried out 90 raids that led to 104 people being
formally charged and/or convicted. [@2] Officers also intervened in 47 drug sale
points, several of which were walled up or demolished. [@2]

The operations seized 30 kilograms of marijuana. [@2] Police also seized 50
vehicles alongside the drugs. [@2]

The results were presented to Interior Minister Carlos Negro during his visit to
Maldonado in a meeting with Police Chief Victor Trezza and his command. [@2] FM
Gente reported the same remarks as more than 100 raids and about 60 kilos of
cocaine and pasta base plus about 30 kilos of marijuana. [@1]
