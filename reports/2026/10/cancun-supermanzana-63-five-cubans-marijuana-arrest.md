Five Cuban citizens, four men and one woman, were detained on 12 October in
Supermanzana 63 in Cancún after officers saw them allegedly exchanging bags in
public. [@1] The Quintana Roo prosecutor's office named those held as
Yanisleidis N, 21, Jose Antonio N, 44, Deivys N, 42, Bryan N, 28, and Yandry N,
42. [@1] The arrest happened on Calle 12 in Supermanzana 63 during Blindaje
Cancun patrols by Cancun municipal police with Defence, Navy and National Guard
personnel. [@2]

Officers intervened near El Parian market after seeing the five allegedly
exchanging transparent plastic bags holding green herb resembling marijuana.
[@1] Officers seized green and dry herb resembling marijuana, a granular
crystal-like substance, a short 4.5 mm weapon with three cartridges and several
mobile phones. [@1] They also found 8,600 Mexican pesos in mixed-denomination
bills believed to be false. [@1] Officers impounded a grey Chrysler 200 holding
more dry herb and an unplated black Hero MotoCorp Hunk 160R motorcycle. [@1]

The five detainees and the seized items and vehicles were placed at the disposal
of the ministerial authority to continue the investigation and decide their
legal situation. [@1] Prosecutors opened an investigation file for their
probable role in health offences in the form of narcomenudeo, small-scale drug
dealing. [@1]
