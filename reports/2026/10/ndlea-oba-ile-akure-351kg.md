Officers of Nigeria's National Drug Law Enforcement Agency (NDLEA) raided an apartment at Oba-Ile, Akure, in Ondo State and arrested Tunde Adebayo, 42, with 35 bags of cannabis weighing 351 kg. [@1,2] The raid took place on Monday 5 October 2026, the same day officers recovered a separate 290 kg stash at Shasha, another part of Akure. [@1,2]

The seizure was announced by NDLEA spokesman Femi Babafemi in the agency's statement of Sunday 11 October 2026 covering a nationwide sweep across several states. [@1,2] NDLEA chairman Brig Gen Mohamed Buba Marwa (retd) commended the officers involved and urged them to sustain the agency's enforcement efforts. [@1,2]

The reports give no court appearance, charge or sentence for Adebayo. [@1,2]
