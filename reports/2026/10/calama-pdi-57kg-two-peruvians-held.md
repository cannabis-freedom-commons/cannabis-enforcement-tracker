The PDI's Brigada Antinarcóticos Calama, working with the Local Prosecutor's
Office, dismantled an organization dedicated to illicit drug trafficking and
detained two Peruvian citizens. [@1,3] The two detainees are Peruvian men aged 32
and 37, one with regular migratory status but an expulsion notice and the other
with irregular migratory status. [@1,3] Both men were formally charged with
illicit drug trafficking before Calama's guarantee court, which set a 70-day
investigation period. [@1,3]

The operation took place on 28 October in the commune of Calama, where the group
used a private home to stockpile drugs for later distribution to
microtraffickers in different parts of the city. [@1] Officers seized 55
packages of cannabis amounting to 57 kilos 120 grams. [@2,3] Officers also seized
a motorcycle and three mobile phones linked to the investigation. [@1,3]

Regional prosecutor Juan Castro Bekios highlighted the joint investigative work
of BRIANT and the Calama prosecutor's office in dismantling the
stash-and-distribution organization. [@1] Castro Bekios said this case adds to
two procedures from the prior week against alleged delivery sellers and a stash
found on a Calama plot, as evidence of a joint strategy against groups settling
in neighborhoods. [@1]
