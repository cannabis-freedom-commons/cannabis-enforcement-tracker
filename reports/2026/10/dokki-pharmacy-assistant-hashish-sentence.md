Giza Criminal Court, headed by Counsellor Abdelaziz Habib, sentenced a pharmacy
assistant to life imprisonment for possessing hashish, tramadol and other
restricted substances for trafficking in the Dokki police district. [@2] The
defendant was identified as Mahmoud A., whom investigators accused of holding
the drugs for sale. [@1] The case was registered as No. 2278 of 2026, Dokki
felonies. [@2]

Officers seized about 115 grams of hashish divided among several envelopes and
pieces. [@1] They also seized 302 tablets of the narcotic tramadol. [@1] Other
seized medicines included Xanax, Amotril and Restolam tablets, liquids
containing ketamine and Rivotril, Lyrica capsules and Midanitik and Nalufin
ampoules. [@1] The seizure also included a German-made 8 mm blank pistol,
unlicensed blank cartridges and a bladed weapon, adding unlicensed weapons
charges. [@1]

The prosecution relied on the defendant's statements during the investigation,
testimony from police officers and Drug Authority inspectors, and forensic
reports on the seizures. [@1] Prosecutors said mobile-phone chats seized from
the defendant supported the trafficking allegation and asked for the maximum
penalty before the court imposed life imprisonment. [@1] The defence argued the
prosecution warrant rested on unserious investigations, the confession was
coerced and the defendant had no link to items in closed places, seeking
acquittal or maximum leniency for his social circumstances. [@2]
