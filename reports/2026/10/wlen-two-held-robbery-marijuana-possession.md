Two residents of Lwówek county, aged 25 and 33, were placed in pretrial
detention for three months over robberies committed on 26 October in Wleń. [@1]
Officers located the suspects' hideout and detained them, during which the older
man insulted the intervening officers. [@1] Both men had multiple prior
convictions for property offences and allegedly acted in conditions of
recidivism. [@2]

During the police actions officers found 0.83 grams and 0.53 grams of marijuana
on the men, a total of 1.36 grams. [@1] Questioned by a prosecutor, both men
admitted only to possessing cannabis herb other than fibrous hemp and denied the
other acts. [@2]

Investigators say the pair jointly stopped a 30-year-old cyclist, beat him in
the face and stole his bicycle valued at 800 zloty. [@1] The same day the
33-year-old suspect, Maciej O., allegedly forced open a window to enter a flat
in Wleń, overpowered the occupant and stole an LG television valued at 1,000
zloty. [@1] The District Court in Lwówek Śląski ordered the three-month
temporary detention on the district prosecutor's request, and the alleged
offences carry from 2 years and 1 month to 15 years of imprisonment. [@2]
