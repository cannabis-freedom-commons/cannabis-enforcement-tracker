Galang police arrested 51-year-old Sarifudin Lubis, also called Udin Dono, for
carrying dried ganja allegedly meant for distribution. [@1] The arrest took
place in Galang district, Deli Serdang, on Friday 30 October 2026. [@1] Lubis is
a resident of Huta Durian village, Bintang Bayu district, Serdang Bedagai. [@1]
Police acted after receiving information about his suspected ganja-carrying
activity for distribution. [@2]

Officers seized about one ons (100 grams) of dried ganja, along with a
motorcycle, a bag and a black Redmi mobile phone. [@1] The stop occurred at
about 13.00 on Jalan Perintis Kemerdekaan in Galang Kota, where officers found
the dried ganja in a black plastic package inside his black backpack. [@3] Under
questioning, Lubis said the ganja was his, bought from a man named Robet for
Rp550,000 on Jalan Besar Galang-Pagar Merbau. [@1]

The operation is independently described as the arrest of a 51-year-old man, SL
alias UD, with about one ons of dried ganja. [@4] Galang police chief AKP Doli
Suryanto Silaban confirmed the arrest and said the suspect and all evidence were
handed to the Deli Serdang police drug investigation unit. [@1]
