On 5 and 6 October 2026, deputies with the Humboldt County Sheriff's Office Marijuana Enforcement
Team served search warrants at two illegal cannabis grows in southern Humboldt County: one in the
Rancho Sequoia Road area of Alderpoint and one in the Sawmill Road area of Garberville. [@1,2]
The California Department of Fish and Wildlife and the California Department of Cannabis Control
assisted in the operation. [@1,2] The sheriff's office said the properties were identified through
aerial surveillance and lacked the required county permits and state licences for commercial
cultivation. [@1,2]

Deputies eradicated about 3,102 cannabis plants and destroyed about 36 pounds (about 16.3 kg) of
processed cannabis while serving the warrants. [@1,2] At one property they found substandard
living conditions where workers were believed to be staying, conditions the office said can
indicate labour exploitation; no one was present when the warrants were served. [@1,2]

No arrests were made during the operation, and the investigations will be forwarded to the District
Attorney's Office for review. [@1,2] The sheriff's office asks anyone with information about these
investigations to call its tip lines. [@1]
