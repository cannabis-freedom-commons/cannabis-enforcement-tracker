Rajkot city SOG police raided a house in Yoginagar under the University police
station limits. [@1] Police arrested Pooja Bhatt after finding hybrid cannabis
inside the house during the Yoginagar raid. [@1] Police were preparing to
produce Pooja Bhatt in court and seek her remand for further questioning. [@1]
During questioning the name of Gautam Dave came up in connection with the case
and police launched a search for him. [@1]

Officers seized 680 grams of hybrid cannabis worth ₹23.80 lakh. [@1] The total
value of the seized drugs and other items was put at ₹23.91 lakh. [@1] Police
started legal action and were investigating the supply network, including the
main accused. [@1] Her questioning was expected to help investigators find out
where the cannabis came from and who it was meant to be sold to. [@1]

Ordinary cannabis costs ₹50,000 per kg under government rules, while hybrid
cannabis can cost up to ₹35 lakh per kg. [@1] Hybrid cannabis can have a much
stronger effect than ordinary cannabis, even in small amounts. [@1] Police were
investigating the source of the seized drugs and the people involved in the
network. [@1]
