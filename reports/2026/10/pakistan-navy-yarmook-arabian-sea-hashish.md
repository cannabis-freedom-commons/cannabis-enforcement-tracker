The Pakistan Navy ship PNS Yarmook intercepted two dhows in the Arabian Sea and recovered about
2,000 kg of hashish and cannabis and 850 kg of Ice, a form of methamphetamine, the military's
Inter-Services Public Relations (ISPR) said on 7 October 2026. [@1,2] The drugs were hidden in
concealed compartments inside the vessels. [@1,2]

The navy said the two operations took place at extended ranges from the Pakistani coast. [@1,2] It
valued the haul at about $96 million and said the narcotics would be handed to the authorities for
legal proceedings. [@1,2]

The statements gave no details of the dhows' crews and did not say whether anyone was arrested. [@1,2]
President Asif Ali Zardari congratulated the navy on the operations, the Express Tribune
reported. [@2]
