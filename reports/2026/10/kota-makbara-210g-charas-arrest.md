Makbara police in Kota arrested 48-year-old Peer Mohammed alias Peeru. [@2] The
arrest was made under the anti-narcotics drive Operation Garudvyuh. [@2] Peer
Mohammed is a resident of Chandraghata in the Makbara area. [@2] The Makbara
action was one of two police actions across two station areas of Kota city. [@1]

On 27 October a police patrol and checking team stopped him on an informer's
tip. [@2] Officers searching his motorcycle recovered charas. [@2] Police put
the haul at 210 grams of pure charas. [@2]

Police registered a case under the NDPS Act, took him into custody and were
questioning him. [@2] Kota City SP Tejaswini Gautam described the actions as
important for crime control in the city. [@2]
