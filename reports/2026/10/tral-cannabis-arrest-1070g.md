Awantipora police arrested one alleged drug peddler in Tral during a naka
checking operation, a roadside checkpoint check, and said they recovered more
than one kilogram of cannabis from him. [@1] Police identified the accused as
Owais Ahmed Khan, a resident of Yanganwani Aripal. [@1,2] A team from Police
Station Tral, headed by station house officer Inspector Tajamul Islam,
apprehended him during checks on the Tral-Awantipora road near Fiza Point. [@1]

Police said their search of Khan recovered two separately stated quantities:
approximately 30 grams of a cannabis-like substance and 1.070 kilograms of
ground cannabis. [@1] Police described the 1.070-kilogram quantity as ground
cannabis and the smaller 30-gram amount as a cannabis-like substance. [@1]
Officers also seized an auto load carrier bearing registration number
JK13K-7629, which they alleged had been used in the offence. [@1]

Police registered case FIR No. 126/2026 under relevant provisions of the NDPS
Act at Police Station Tral. [@1] Police said further investigation was underway.
