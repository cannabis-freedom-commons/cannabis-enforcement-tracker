Police detained ten students in a suspected cannabis case in Ambatondrazaka on 20 October 2026. [@1,2,3] L’Express de Madagascar described them as eight girls and two boys, aged 14 to 20, and said police went to the gathering place after a tip from a resident. [@1,2] The paper reported that officers detained them there and found a bag containing suspected cannabis on the ground during a search that afternoon. [@1,2]

The regional education office told Studio Sifaka that nine of the ten were secondary-school students and one was a university student, and said they were detained at the police station. [@3] Studio Sifaka reported that police had gone to a site where the young people trained in kickboxing and that the coach was also under suspicion. [@3] The outlet found no evidence that officers had caught the students taking drugs. [@3]

Police were investigating who owned the bag and where it came from. [@1,2] Studio Sifaka reported that the students were expected to be brought before a court on 21 October. [@3]
