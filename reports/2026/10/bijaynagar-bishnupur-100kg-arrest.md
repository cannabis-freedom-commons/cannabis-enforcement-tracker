Special Response Battalion-9 arrested 19-year-old Md. Sakib Mia with 100 kg of
ganja in Bijaynagar, Brahmanbaria. [@1] Officers detained him around 1:30 at
night on a paved road in the Bishnupur area of Bijaynagar. [@1]

An SRB-9 team raided Bishnupur on a tip-off that several people had gathered
there to buy and sell drugs. [@1] The group dropped several sacks and tried to
flee when officers appeared, and officers chased and detained Sakib Mia while
other suspects escaped. [@1] Officers searched 10 abandoned sacks on Sakib's
information and recovered 100 kg of ganja wrapped in blue polythene. [@1]

In questioning, Sakib said he and his absconding associates had collected the
ganja from a Brahmanbaria border area and kept it to sell. [@1] Bijaynagar
police confirmed SRB-9 handed over a drug dealer with 100 kg of ganja and said
legal action was being taken against him. [@1] SRB-9 said its intelligence work
and operations continue to enforce a zero-tolerance policy against drugs. [@1]
