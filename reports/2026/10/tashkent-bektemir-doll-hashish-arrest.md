Police detained a 56-year-old woman in Bektemir district, Tashkent, after
finding hashish hidden inside a child's doll she was carrying. [@1] The
detention took place during a raid by officers of the cybercrime department of
the Tashkent regional police and the Nurafshan police. [@1] Acting on an online
tip about drug transport, operatives stopped a Nexia 3 and searched the woman's
belongings. [@1] The driver, who was working through Yandex Taxi, told officers
he was seeing the passenger for the first time. [@2]

In one bag officers noticed a doll that felt suspiciously heavy, and after
opening it found eight packs of dark-brown hashish with a pungent smell in its
head, each pack weighing 100 to 150 grams. [@1] The total weight of the hashish
hidden in the doll was reported as up to 1.2 kg. [@1] Investigators said the
woman had obtained the drugs in Fergana region and was transporting them to
Tashkent for sale. [@2] In a video released by the Interior Ministry, the woman
said an acquaintance had promised to pay her 200 dollars to hand over the
packages. [@1]

Police opened a criminal case under part 6 of Article 273 of the Criminal Code,
which covers large-scale and organized-group sale using telecommunications or
the Internet and carries 15 to 20 years in prison. [@1] The investigation is
continuing to establish the source of the drugs and other circumstances. [@2]
