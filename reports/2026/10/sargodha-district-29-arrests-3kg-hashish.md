Sargodha police arrested 29 alleged criminals during raids in different parts of
the district on Saturday, recovering narcotics, liquor, illegal weapons and
valuables worth millions of rupees from their possession. [@1] Teams from
various police stations conducted raids in their respective jurisdictions and
apprehended the suspects. [@1] The arrested suspects were identified as Aslam,
Ghulam Rasool, Munir, Kareem, Abdul Samad, Sohaib, Sohail, Kamran, Mumtaz,
Naveed Asghar, Saleem, Mukhtar, Muzzmil, Nadeem, Shafique, Sharafat, Saqlain,
Ahmed, Ehsan, Asghar, Talal and others. [@1]

Police recovered three kilograms of hashish, which is the cannabis component
recorded here. [@1] The same recovery included two kilograms of opium, 108
liters of liquor and 23 liters of wine. [@1] Officers also recovered three
pistols, four guns, one Kalashnikov, three rounds and 70 bullets from their
possession. [@1] Police also recovered valuables worth millions of rupees during
the operations. [@1]

Police said raids across Sargodha district led to the arrests, with officers
recovering hashish, opium, liquor, wine, firearms and ammunition. [@1] Further
investigation into the cases was underway. [@1]
