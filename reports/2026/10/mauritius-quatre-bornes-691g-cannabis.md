Anti-Drug and Smuggling Unit (ADSU) officers searched a home at Résidence
Kennedy, Quatre-Bornes, on 19 October 2026 and arrested three men: a
35-year-old tattooist, a 32-year-old constable and a 30-year-old local
resident. The constable was suspended from duty, according to L’Express. [@1,2,3]

Officers seized 691.3 grams of cannabis in several packages, including five
packets wrapped with tape. [@1,2,3] The outlets differed on the estimated
street value, reporting about Rs1 million to Rs1.1 million. [@1,2,3]

Le Mauricien reported that the men appeared before the Rose-Hill court on
provisional possession and trafficking charges and were remanded in detention.
The reports do not establish what role each man allegedly played in the
possession or trafficking. [@1,2]
