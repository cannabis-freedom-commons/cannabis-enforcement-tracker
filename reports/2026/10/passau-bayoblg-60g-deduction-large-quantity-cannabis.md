Bavaria's highest regional court set aside the sentences for both possession
offences and the overall sentence of a man convicted over hashish and marijuana
kept at several homes, and sent the case back to a different criminal panel of
the Passau Regional Court. [@1] The decision of 1 October 2026 is case no. 206
StRR 264/26. [@1] It changed the verdict of the Passau Regional Court of 13 May
2026. [@2] Passau District Court convicted the defendant in August 2025 and the
Regional Court upheld the conviction on appeal in May 2026, treating the
cannabis as a large quantity possession. [@1] Hashish and marijuana were found
distributed across several homes and his habitual residence, together with
methamphetamine and driving without a licence. [@1]

Up to 50 grams of cannabis at home is legal. [@1] Possession becomes criminal
above 60 grams, while 50 to 60 grams carries an administrative fine. [@1] A
large quantity means 7.5 grams of THC, and the court held that the 60 grams
exempt from punishment must be deducted before that threshold is assessed. [@1]
Where the cannabis differed in strength, the most potent product counted towards
the allowance, in this case hashish with 26.4 percent THC. [@1] After the
deduction the remaining cannabis fell well below 7.5 grams of THC, so the
large-quantity element was dropped. [@1]

The court ruled that cannabis kept at several homes and a habitual residence at
the same time is added together, so the allowance is not granted separately for
each address. [@1] The verdict now reads possession of more than 60 grams of
cannabis, without the addition of large quantity. [@1] His conviction for
driving without a licence and the six-month individual sentence for it remain.
[@1] The court also held that weighing methamphetamine as a hard drug as an
aggravating factor, without specific justification for the particular substance,
was legally defective. [@1] The defendant had possessed the methamphetamine only
for personal consumption, so the danger of the drug could at most be weighted
lightly. [@2]
