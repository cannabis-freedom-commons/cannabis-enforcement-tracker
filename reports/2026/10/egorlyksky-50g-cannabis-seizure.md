Police detained a 42-year-old local man in Egorlyksky District, Rostov Oblast,
on suspicion of storing narcotic drugs, the Main Directorate of Russia's
Interior Ministry for Rostov Oblast said. [@1] Officers found a plant substance
in the man's home during a search. [@1] Forensic testing identified it as
cannabis and measured its weight at 50.23 grams. [@1] Police classified this as
a significant quantity. [@1] Authorities said the man had kept the cannabis for
his personal use. [@1]

The regional police press service said a criminal case had been opened against
the suspect under Part 1 of Article 228 of the Russian Criminal Code. [@1] The
quoted provision covers illegal acquisition or possession of narcotic drugs in a
significant quantity without intent to sell. [@1] The allegation is therefore
framed as possession without intent to sell rather than as a reported sale. [@1]
This links the forensic identification and significant-quantity finding to the
cannabis officers found during the search of the man's home. [@1]
