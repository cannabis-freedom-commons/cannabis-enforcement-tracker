Police in Khandwa, Madhya Pradesh, arrested three men accused of selling and
supplying ganja, a cannabis preparation, in two separate operations. [@1,2] The
two operations yielded 1.19 kg of ganja and a motorcycle. [@1] Moghat Road
police carried out one of the operations near the railway bridge on Bargaon
Bhila Road on the night of Friday 30 October. [@1] There they detained a young
man and found 110 g of ganja on him. [@1] Police identified him as Ameen Khan,
19, a resident of Rameshwar Tekra in the Moghat Road station area. [@1]

On Saturday, City Kotwali police arrested two men accused of bringing ganja by
motorcycle behind Chirakhadan Multi. [@1] One man arrived on the motorcycle and
was handing the other a cloth bag that hung from it when officers surrounded
them and made the arrests. [@1] A search found 1 kg 80 g of ganja on them, and
police seized the Bajaj Platina motorcycle as well. [@1] The two were Ramesh
Gohar, 41, of Sundarnagar, Chirakhadan, and Mohansingh Rawat, 30, of Sangvi
village in Bhikangaon, Khargone district. [@1]

Police put the estimated value of the seized ganja at about 4,800 rupees. [@1] A
case was registered against all three under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic
Substances (NDPS) Act. [@1] City Superintendent of Police Abhinav Barange said
officers were gathering information about other people involved in buying,
selling and supplying ganja. [@1] Police were also trying to establish where the
ganja came from and who was involved in supplying it. [@1]
