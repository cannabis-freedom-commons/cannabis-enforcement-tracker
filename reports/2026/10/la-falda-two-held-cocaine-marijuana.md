Two men were detained in La Falda, in Córdoba province, on Friday 30 October
2026, accused of belonging to a drug-selling organisation. [@3] Córdoba's Fuerza
Policial Antinarcotráfico (FPA), the provincial anti-drug police, carried out
three raids: two at homes in Villa Ramón Soria and one in Parque. [@1,4] One
detainee was identified as the supplier of the drugs and the other as a
deliveryman; investigators said the group had its centre of operations in La
Falda. [@1]

Officers seized 355 doses of cocaine, two doses of marijuana and 600,000
Argentine pesos in cash. [@1,5] They also found a firearm, 45 cartridges, three
digital scales and a machine for counting money. [@2,5]

Both men were charged with aggravated drug trafficking for the participation of
three or more people. [@1,2] The prosecution ordered them transferred to judicial
headquarters and the seized evidence sent on for the investigation. [@1]

The raids followed an investigation led by Nelson Lingua, the anti-drug prosecutor in Cosquín. [@5]
