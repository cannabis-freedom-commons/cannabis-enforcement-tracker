Officers of the Federal District Civil Police's 35th Precinct in Sobradinho II
detained three men during Operation King's Fall, including a suspect known as
the Rei da AR 19. [@1] Police identified the main suspect as DG or Galeguinho,
described as the alleged leader of a criminal group in the area who called
himself the Rei da AR 19. [@1] The suspect was a fugitive from justice and was
located by PCDF agents while attending a party in AR 19 in Sobradinho II. [@1]
The approach took place on Saturday 31 October, when the suspect tried to flee
by jumping over walls and climbing onto neighbouring roofs after officers
ordered him to stop. [@1] Relatives and friends of the suspect at the event
harassed the officers during the attempted capture. [@1] After the 35th Precinct
requested support from the 13th Military Police Battalion to contain the crowd,
officers found Galeguinho hidden inside the bathroom of a house in the COER
Parque housing area. [@1]

In the same property two other men were detained, and searches seized portions
of maconha, skunk and cocaine, plus a drone, a digital scale, trafficking
accounting notes and a pistol magazine. [@1] The three men were booked in
flagrante at the 35th Precinct and transferred to the custody of the Division of
Control and Custody of Prisoners, the DCCP. [@1]
