Police in Ufa detained a 39-year-old man on suspicion of drug dealing with
Rosgvardia power support. [@1] The incident occurred on 27 October at about
18:00 near a house on Yegora Sazonov Street, according to the Interior Ministry
press service for Bashkortostan. [@1]

A personal search found three packets of plant-origin substance weighing 7.25
grams in his jacket pocket, and another packet with dark contents weighing more
than 1 gram in the Renault Duster he was using. [@1] A search of his residence
recovered more than 180 grams of brown substance, and forensic examination
confirmed all the substances were hashish with a total weight of more than 188
grams. [@1]

The detainee admitted buying the drugs from an online store and selling them to
a limited circle of people. [@1] A criminal case was opened for attempted
illegal sale of drugs on a large scale. [@1]
