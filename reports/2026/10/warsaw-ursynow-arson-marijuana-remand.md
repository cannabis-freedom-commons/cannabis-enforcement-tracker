On 31 October 2026 at about 17:30 a man poured flammable liquid over an armchair
inside a restaurant premises in Warsaw's Ursynow district and set it on fire
while nine people were inside. [@1] The premises stands in a multi-family
residential building, so the fire could have directly endangered the life and
health of many people. [@1] Criminal officers from the Warsaw-Ursynow police
station quickly identified a suspect and, around midnight, stopped and detained
a man matching the description on a street in Bielany. [@1] The detained man is
a 24-year-old citizen of Rwanda who was already wanted by a court. [@1] Checks
showed he was wanted to serve 15 days of imprisonment, and he declined to
explain why he tried to set fire to the pizzeria. [@1]

Officers found plant material on the detained man, and testing confirmed that it
was marijuana. [@2] The premises was unofficially identified as a delivery
pizzeria in a housing block on Dereniowa Street. [@1]

The man heard two charges, causing a direct danger of fire threatening the lives
of many people while acting publicly for a trivial reason showing disregard for
the legal order, and possessing intoxicating substances. [@1] On the
prosecutor's motion a court ordered three months of temporary arrest for the
24-year-old, who faces up to eight years in prison, potentially more because of
the hooligan character of the act. [@1] The Warsaw-Ursynow district prosecutor's
office supervises the proceedings. [@1]
