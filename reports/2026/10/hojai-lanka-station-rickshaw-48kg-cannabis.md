A total of 4.8 kg of suspected cannabis was seized from an auto-rickshaw near
Lanka Railway Station in Assam's Hojai district. [@1] The auto-rickshaw,
registration number AS-31-C-3589, was searched following the initiative of Hojai
District Commissioner Plaban Nath. [@1] Officials recovered the 4.8 kg of
cannabis from the vehicle during that search. [@1]

A person identified as Ganesh Das, a resident of Lanka, has been named in
connection with the incident. [@1] The source of the suspected cannabis and its
intended destination were not yet known when the account was published. [@1]
