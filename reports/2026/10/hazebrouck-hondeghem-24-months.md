Gendarmes arrested several people and searched homes in Hondeghem and Hazebrouck
on 13 October 2026, with more than 20 officers and specialist units taking part.
[@3] The operation dismantled a cannabis-trafficking network that operated
between Hondeghem and Hazebrouck. [@1] Two days later, on 15 October, the
Dunkirk prosecutor's office brought two people before a CRPC, the French
guilty-plea procedure. [@1] The main defendant was sentenced to 24 months in
prison, 14 of them suspended with probation. [@4]

Officers seized 356 g of cannabis herb and 84 g of cannabis resin, as well as 69
g of cocaine, cash, motorbikes, electric bicycles and equipment. [@3] During
questioning, the main defendant admitted selling 8.5 kg of cannabis since
February 2025 for estimated revenue of €68,000 to €85,000. [@1] Additional
seizures were valued at about €9,000. [@2]

The inquiry began in August 2025 after a tip about possible cannabis sales from
a house in Hondeghem, near Hazebrouck, and the Hazebrouck research brigade
investigated under the Dunkirk prosecutor. [@1] Investigators found a regular
resale business and identified several people involved. [@3]
