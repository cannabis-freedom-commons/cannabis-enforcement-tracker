Roseville police arrested two people in connection with a probation search at a
residence on Herbert Street, booking both into the South Placer County Jail on
weapons- and narcotics-related charges. [@1] Officers had gone to the residence
on Oct. 11 to serve an arrest warrant and conduct a probation search. [@1]

During that initial search, officers found drug paraphernalia, illegal narcotics
and evidence of what police described as a significant indoor marijuana
cultivation operation. [@1] Officers secured the residence and obtained a search
warrant from a Placer County judge for a fuller search. [@1] That search
uncovered meth, fentanyl, several pounds of processed marijuana, additional drug
paraphernalia and 18 firearms, including handguns, rifles and assault-style
weapons. [@1] Investigators said the homeowner was prohibited from possessing
firearms in California because of narcotics addiction, and all 18 firearms were
seized as a result. [@1]

The Roseville Police Department announced the case as patrol officers seizing
illegal drugs, a marijuana farm and 18 firearms in a probation search earlier in
the month. [@1]
