Four men were arrested after Lahaul-Spiti police found 17.180 grams of charas, a
cannabis resin, in their car near the Tandi petrol pump on National Highway 03.
[@2] The district police said they recovered the charas during a checkpoint
there and registered a case under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances
Act. [@1] The car, registration HP29B9723, was stopped for checking. [@3] The
checkpoint was near a dumping site beside the pump, and a team led by Assistant
Sub-Inspector Hemant Kumar was deployed there on 25 October 2026. [@3]

Ashwani Rathore, 39, of Paslahar near Chauntra in Mandi district, was driving.
[@2] Rupesh Kumar, 32, was from Munger district in Bihar and was then living at
Chauntra in Mandi. [@2] Mukesh Kumar, 25, of Dhanetar, and Sandeep Kumar, 46, of
Ribl Bhatehad, were also from the Chauntra area. [@2] All four car occupants
were taken into custody. [@4]

Deputy Superintendent of Police K.D. Sharma confirmed the case and said the
district police were taking strict steps to curb the illegal drug trade. [@2]
