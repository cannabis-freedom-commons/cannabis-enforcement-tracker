Udaipur police registered nine drug cases and arrested seven people in a sweep
under the Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyan, India's campaign for a drug-free country.
[@1] The operation was a one-day special drive across the district. [@2] Teams
checked more than 416 places around highways, bus stands, railway stations and
educational institutions, Superintendent of Police Dr Amrita Duhan said. [@1]
Raja Hussain alias Shanu was arrested in Ambamata with 66.55 g of charas, or
hashish. [@1] Nasir Hussain alias Mukhtiyar was arrested behind the Government
Press in Surajpol with 38 g of charas. [@1]

Mandva police recovered 59.330 kg of ganja, a form of cannabis, and Kotda police
11.700 kg. [@1] In both cases the suspects fled and police said searches were
continuing. [@1] Vinod Kumar Meena of Madhari was arrested in Pratapnagar with
153 g of ganja. [@1] Taxi driver Akash alias Vimanlal was caught in Govardhan
Vilas with 37 g of ganja, Ishwarlal in Savina with 9.8 g of charas, and Mukesh
in Badgaon with 37 g. [@1]

Across the nine cases, the seizures came to about 71 kg of ganja and 144 g of
charas. [@2] The cases were registered under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic
Substances Act, India's main drug law. [@2] Duhan said the anti-drug drive would
continue and no one involved in the trade would be spared. [@1]
