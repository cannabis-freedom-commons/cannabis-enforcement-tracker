A Yorkville woman is accused of giving cannabis-infused gummies to children
ahead of their attendance at the Hinckley-Big Rock High School Homecoming dance
in September, according to information shared by Hinckley Police Chief Kenneth
Gettemy. [@1] Sydney A. Smith, 37, was charged with three counts of delivering
cannabis to a minor and three counts of endangering the life or health of a
child, according to a Hinckley Police news release. [@1] Smith surrendered
herself to police and was released after her charges were processed on Oct. 7,
according to Hinckley police. [@1] Hinckley police said Smith has a preliminary
court hearing at 9 a.m. on Nov. 3. [@1]

Police said on Sept. 26, four Hinckley-Big Rock High School students were
offered cannabis-infused edible gummies while they prepared for the Homecoming
dance at a Hinckley home. [@1] Four children were offered, and three accepted
and ate the edibles, according to the release. [@1]

The investigation began on Oct. 1 when Hinckley-Big Rock administrators
contacted Hinckley police to share information about an alleged distribution of
cannabis to high school students. [@1] Officers interviewed students and
parents, and investigators reviewed electronic communications before announcing
the charges. [@1] Those charges were not in online DeKalb County Court records
on Monday. [@1]
