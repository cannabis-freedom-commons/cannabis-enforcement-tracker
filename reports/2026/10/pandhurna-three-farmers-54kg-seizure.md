Police in Pandhurna arrested three farmers in a 35-day sequence involving three
cannabis fields. [@1] Police reported a combined 54 kg seizure from the three
actions and sent the three arrested people to judicial custody in Chhindwara
jail. [@2]

In Gujar Khedi, police raided farmer Manohar Patil’s field and seized 56
cannabis plants weighing about 24 kg. [@2] Police also initiated legal
proceedings against Patil. [@2] At Daddudevani on 23 October, police recovered
about 26 kg of cannabis from a plant around 13 feet tall at Surji Ram Salame’s
premises. [@2] Salame was arrested and sent to jail. [@2]

On 28 October in Neelkanth, a team led by station officer Amit Dani raided
farmer Bhau Rao Dhurve’s field and found about nine plants weighing around 4 kg.
[@2] Police arrested Dhurve under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances
Act and sent him to judicial custody. [@2] Police were investigating whether an
organized network was behind the cultivation or whether the accused had grown
cannabis independently. [@2]
