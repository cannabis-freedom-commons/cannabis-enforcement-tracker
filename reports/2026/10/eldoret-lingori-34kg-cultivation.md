## The sentence

Senior Resident Magistrate Beatrice Toroitich sentenced 58-year-old Geoffrey Lingori to 12 months in prison without the option of a fine on 21 October 2026. [@1] Lingori had pleaded guilty to cultivating cannabis on his land in Uasin Gishu County under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act. [@1,2] He told the court he cultivated and used cannabis to manage joint pain, saying a government hospital doctor had advised it. [@1] At an earlier hearing on 7 September 2026 before Principal Magistrate Kimani Mukabi, he had said he grew bhang on his small parcel as his main source of income. [@2]

## The August raid

Directorate of Criminal Investigations officers raided Lingori's land in the Malel area of Kapseret Sub-County on 15 August 2026 after receiving a tip-off. [@1,2] Police found him cultivating 34,000 grams of cannabis with an estimated street value of Sh1,032,000. [@1,2] Lingori, who holds a mechanics certificate from the Rift Valley Technical Training Institute in Eldoret, reportedly lived alone after his marriage broke down. [@1,2] He was held at Eldoret GK Remand Prison while the case proceeded. [@2]
