Police uncovered a marijuana grow in Jersika parish. [@1] On 2 October 2026
officers of the North Latgale station of the Latgale regional administration
inspected a property in Jersika parish after checking information about possible
illegal drug circulation. [@2] During a sanctioned search of a private house and
the adjacent greenhouse, officers found an equipped marijuana grow. [@4] On
suspicion in connection with the offence, officers detained a man born in 1980.
[@3] A criminal process was opened and the investigation is continuing. [@3]

Officers seized 81 dried marijuana plants. [@2] They also seized 19 growing
plants, seven plants in pots and seven more plants that had begun drying, making
114 plants in total. [@4] Officers also seized 19 cartridges during the search.
[@2] The grow was fitted with cultivation equipment, including lamps and
watering and ventilation systems. [@3]

The case was also qualified under Section 256(1) and Section 233(2) of the
Criminal Law. [@2] The lead drug charge carries two to eight years of
imprisonment, with or without property confiscation, plus up to three years of
probation supervision. [@2] Police noted that no person is considered guilty
until guilt is proven in the manner prescribed by law. [@3]
