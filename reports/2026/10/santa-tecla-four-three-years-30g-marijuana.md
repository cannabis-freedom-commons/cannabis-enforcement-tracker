The First Sentencing Court of Santa Tecla convicted four people, three men and a
woman, of possession and holding of drugs in two separate cases. [@1] Óscar
Alberto Gómez Hueso, Miguel Antonio Mancía Melgar and María Argelia Guevara
Torres were detained on 2 May 2025 during a preventive patrol in the district of
Teotepeque, La Libertad Costa. [@1] Officers searched the three after
considering their behaviour suspicious and seized portions of plant material
that chemical analysis confirmed as marijuana. [@1] Gómez Hueso and Mancía
Melgar were found with five grams of marijuana each, and Guevara Torres with
eight grams. [@1]

The court found the three responsible for possession and holding of marijuana
and sentenced each to three years in prison. [@1] The two men must serve their
sentences in a penitentiary because they have other judicial processes pending.
[@1] Guevara Torres instead received a conditional suspension of her three-year
sentence. [@1] During that period she must live at the same address, refrain
from using drugs, stay in the country and submit to the control of the
sentence-enforcement supervision court. [@1]

The second case concerned Francisco Barillas Canizalez, an elderly man arrested
on 9 December 2025 in Quezaltepeque, La Libertad Norte. [@1] Police who stopped
him found 12 grams of marijuana divided into eight portions. [@1] At the public
hearing he admitted the drug was his, which the court took into account in
reaching its verdict. [@1] The court found him guilty of possession and holding
of drugs and imposed three years in prison, replaced by rules of conduct. [@1]
His rules of conduct run for three years and ban drug and alcohol use and visits
to places where illicit substances are sold. [@1]

Article 34 of the Ley Reguladora de las Actividades Relativas a las Drogas sets
three to six years in prison for holding two grams or more of an illicit drug.
