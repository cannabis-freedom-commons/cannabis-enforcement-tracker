Military Police officers arrested a 27-year-old man in flagrante for suspected
drug trafficking on the afternoon of Friday 30 October 2026 in the JK
neighbourhood of Conselheiro Lafaiete in central Minas Gerais. [@1] The
operation was carried out by the 31st Military Police Battalion after repeated
reports that drugs were being sold from a home in the area. [@1] The battalion's
intelligence unit then monitored the house and gathered material that supported
a search-and-seizure warrant. [@1] On Friday officers saw movements consistent
with trafficking and entered the address under investigation. [@1]

During the house search officers found 21 packets of a substance resembling
cocaine, 20 of them already prepared for sale. [@1] They also seized one lump of
a substance resembling maconha, the Brazilian Portuguese word for marijuana,
plus a precision scale, R$384 in cash and ten mobile phones. [@1]

The man was taken in flagrante on suspicion of drug trafficking to the Civil
Police together with all the seized material. [@2] Military Police said the
operation combined intelligence, planning and street action to stop the sales
and take drugs out of circulation. [@1]
