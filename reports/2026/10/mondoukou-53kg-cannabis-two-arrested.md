Officers of the Groupe de répression anti-drogues of the DPSD Tri-Postal antenna
arrested two people during an anti-drug operation at Mondoukou on 15 October
2026. [@1] The intervention was conducted under Opération Épervier Spécial, a
police drive against drug trafficking and consumption. [@2] The two suspects
were placed in custody and then referred to the judicial authorities. [@1]

On the ground, officers seized 53.003 kilograms of cannabis. [@1] They also
seized 4,808 tablets of medicines classified as narcotics, called MQIF,
containing Eskazepam, TramaKing 250 mg and Tapentadol 250 mg, with a stated
total weight of 1.141 kg. [@1] Officers destroyed seven fumoirs, places used for
consuming drugs. [@2]

Police presented the same 53.003 kg, 4,808 tablets and seven fumoirs as the
cumulative result for 1 to 15 October 2026, alongside the two referrals. [@2] At
Mondoukou, the operation was presented as reinforcing action against
distribution networks and illicit consumption sites. [@1]
