On 22 October 2026, authorities announced 146 arrests in Operation Central Valley Blues, a sweep
by 38 federal, state and local agencies across 14 Northern and Central California counties
against drug and firearms dealers operating on social media. [@1,2] The sweep ran over three
days. [@2] The operation expanded the
local Folsom Blues model to the regional "last mile" of trafficking, with Meta removing more
than 800 accounts and sharing information that led to arrests. [@1,2]

Officers seized 3,286 pounds of drugs, including more than 3,184 pounds of cannabis alongside
over 91 pounds of methamphetamine, 16,136 fentanyl pills, cocaine, psilocybin, heroin and GHB, plus 43 firearms
and $338,222 in cash. [@1,2] Sacramento County District Attorney Thien Ho led the announcement
with the DEA, FBI, the US Attorney's Office and the Central Valley HIDTA partnership. [@1,2]
