Police in Bonon, a town in Côte d'Ivoire's Marahoué Region, arrested two men in
the Frakro quarter on Saturday 3 October 2026 at about 8.30 pm, with two blocks
of cannabis in their possession. [@2] The two blocks were found during an
operation by a team of the DPC unit under police lieutenant Poka Aimé Jorex, a
police cell the reports do not otherwise describe. [@2] The first suspect, a
30-year-old who said he is a planter living at Blanfla in Sinfra sub-prefecture,
was accused of bringing the two blocks from Bouaflé hidden in a black plastic
bag inside an old rice sack. [@2]

Officers also found 48,000 francs CFA on him during the search, while the second
man, a 43-year-old wood loader living in Bonon's Mossibougou quarter, was
carrying 224,500 francs CFA. [@2] Police said the older man was arrested as he
came to collect the parcel. [@1] Both suspects were taken to Bonon's police
station for the procedure. [@2]

In June 2026 several people were arrested in Bonon in a similar case and brought
before the Bouaflé court of first instance. [@2] The security forces have
stepped up operations against drug trafficking and the circulation of
substandard or falsified medicines in the department. [@2]
