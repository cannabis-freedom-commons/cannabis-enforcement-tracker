## The stop

On 20 October 2026 an officer stopped a Toyota sedan for a faulty brake light in Shreveport. [@1,2] During contact with the driver, Kenyon Gardner, the officer smelled marijuana and saw a firearm magazine protruding from Gardner's hoodie pocket. [@1,2]

## What was recovered

A search found a Glock 19 on Gardner and a Glock 23 on the passenger-side floorboard, with approximately 350 grams of suspected marijuana packaged in two separate bags. [@1,2] Police also reported that Gardner had five outstanding traffic warrants. [@1,2] The department presented the recovery as proactive policing against unlawful firearm possession and drug activity. [@1,2]
