Police Scotland community officers executed a drugs search warrant at an address
on Alford Drive in Glenrothes on Thursday. [@1] A 34-year-old man was charged
and will be reported for a number of offences in connection with the raid. [@1]

Officers found evidence of large-scale production of cannabis oil and cannabis
edibles at the property. [@1] They recovered 7kg of cannabis during the search.
[@1] Officers also seized three electric off-road bikes, a stolen motorbike and
a dog suspected to be an XL Bully. [@1]

Community Sergeant Martyn Reidie said detection and disruption of drug crime is
a priority for Police Scotland. [@1] He said drug misuse drives violent and
antisocial behaviour and that officers are pursuing those linked to supplying
controlled drugs among young people. [@1] He said it is clear some of those
individuals are using electric off-road bikes and that he was pleased more of
the bikes had been taken off the streets. [@1] He appealed for anyone with
information about drug activity to contact their local community policing team
on 101 or to report anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555111. [@1]
