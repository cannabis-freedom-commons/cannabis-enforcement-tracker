The Superior Court of Justice of Castilla-La Mancha dismissed the regional
government's appeal and confirmed the annulment of a 30,001 euro fine imposed on
the woman responsible for a public establishment for allegedly allowing drug
consumption on the premises. [@1] The Albacete contentious-administrative court
No. 1 had annulled the fine in a judgment dated 25 October 2022. [@1] The Second
Section of the Contentious-Administrative Chamber rejected the appeal filed by
the Finance and Public Administrations Department against that annulment. [@1]

Police officers had seized four cigarettes that they described in their reports
as joints. [@1] The regional administration imposed the 30,001 euro fine through
a 16 July 2021 resolution of the General Secretariat of the Finance and Public
Administrations Department. [@1] The regional government treated the conduct as
a very serious infringement under article 45.1 of Castilla-La Mancha Law 7/2011
on public shows and establishments. [@1] That provision punishes permissiveness
or tolerance of illegal activities involving drug consumption or trafficking.
[@1]

The courts found unexplained gaps and periods without control guarantees in the
handling of the four cigarettes. [@1] Those chain-of-custody irregularities
meant the samples eventually analysed could not be shown to be the same ones
officers had collected. [@1] The judges held that the officers' sensory
assessment by appearance and smell could not replace chemical analysis when that
essential test's reliability was undermined by the custody defects. [@1] The
sanctioned person had also been prevented from obtaining a counter-analysis to
challenge the chemical result. [@1] The court rejected the claim that a
different judge signing the first-instance judgment violated immediacy, noting
the case used a mainly written ordinary procedure with personal evidence
preserved on audiovisual recordings. [@1]
