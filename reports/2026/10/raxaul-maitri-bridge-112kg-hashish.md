Acting on a tip that a large quantity of drugs was being taken towards India, a joint team of police and the Sashastra Seema Bal under SDPO Manish Kumar Anand was deployed at the Maitri Bridge between Birgunj and Raxaul. [@1,2,3] On Saturday evening the team stopped a suspicious e-rickshaw coming from Birgunj towards Raxaul and checked it. [@1,2]

Inside the rickshaw the team found a sack holding 22 black packets of hashish weighing 11 kg and 246 grams. [@1,2] The carrier was identified as 39-year-old Amar Chaudhary of Chhapkaiya in Birgunj Metropolitan City. [@1,2] The SSB handed Chaudhary and the recovered hashish to Haraiya police station in Raxaul for legal action. [@1,2]

Police said preliminary findings indicated the drugs had been brought from places in Makwanpur district and were being taken towards India through Bara and Parsa. [@2] The report notes that the smuggling attempt through the bridge raised questions about the checking system on the Nepali side. [@2]

Police and the SSB tightened checks on the Maitri Bridge and along the border on Saturday evening. [@4]
