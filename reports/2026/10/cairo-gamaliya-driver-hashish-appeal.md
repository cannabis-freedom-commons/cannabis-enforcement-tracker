The Cairo criminal appeal court accepted a driver's appeal against a three-year
prison sentence and cut the term to six months for using hashish and possessing
drugs in the El Gamaliya district of Cairo. [@1,3,4,5] A court of first instance had sentenced him to three years in prison; the criminal court accepted the appeal in form and on the merits and changed the penalty to six months' detention. [@5]

Police from the El Gamaliya police station, under the Cairo Security
Directorate, stopped the driver during a security patrol after they grew
suspicious of him, and he tried to flee when they approached. [@1,3,4,5] They caught
him riding a motorcycle that had been reported stolen. [@2,3,4] A search found
cigarettes containing hashish, and he admitted he was holding them for his own
use, after which the police took the legal steps in the case. [@1,5]

A chemical laboratory report said the seized item was a cigarette containing
parts of a substance confirmed to be hashish, which Egypt's anti-drug law lists
in its first schedule. [@1,5] The prosecution based the charge on the chemical laboratory report. [@5] The case was referred to the investigating
authorities, who carried out their inquiries before the court issued the ruling.
[@2]
