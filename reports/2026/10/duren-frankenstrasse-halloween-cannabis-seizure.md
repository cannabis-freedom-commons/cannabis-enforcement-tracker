Düren's municipal public-order service ran reinforced controls across the city
during Halloween night. [@1] Early in the evening a patrol checked the
playground on Frankenstraße and found four young people there. [@2] Officers
found that one minor among them was smoking a joint. [@2] A follow-up check
found cannabis and ecstasy as well as a blank-firing pistol and fireworks on
him. [@2] Officers called in police for further criminal examination, and police
took over the young people and further measures. [@2]

Around 0:45 the service was alerted to a loud Halloween party in an apartment
building in Nord-Düren. [@2] Loud music and partying could already be heard
outside the building and night quiet in the stairwell was considerably
disturbed. [@2] The organiser was told to end the party and send home guests not
living in the flat, but he did not comply at first so police were called for
support. [@2] After police arrived and spoke again with the organiser, about 25
to 30 guests left within about 20 minutes and the party ended. [@2] The service
described the Halloween night overall as largely quiet, with officers following
public tips and intervening in two cases. [@1]
