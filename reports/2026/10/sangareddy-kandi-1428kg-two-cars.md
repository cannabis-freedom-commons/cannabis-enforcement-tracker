Sangareddy police, acting on a tip-off, were checking vehicles at Kandi Cross
Road on NH-65 in the early hours of Monday when they stopped two cars and
found 142.8 kg of dry ganja inside. [@1] The seizure was reported the same
day, 12 October 2026, with the contraband valued at Rs 71.40 lakh. [@1] A
second account describes the same Kandi intercept as 35 packets of dry ganja
in a Hyundai Accent and a Maruti Suzuki Fronx, with both cars seized. [@2]

The two men in the cars, Roshan Karni and Bibutha Rajtha, are both from
Odisha and were allegedly carrying the load from Odisha to Maharashtra when
intercepted at Kandi. [@1,2] A night photograph from the spot, stamped 01:16
and marked Kandi, shows both accused crouching beside the packed parcels with
the two white cars and the intercepting officers. [@1] Both men were arrested
as alleged peddlers, produced in court and remanded, and the two cars and the
full consignment were seized. [@1,2]
