Police from the Śródmieście district station in Bydgoszcz visited a 24-year-old
Bydgoszcz man at about 17:00 on Monday 26 October 2026 after detective work
suggested he might be linked to drugs. [@1] They found him leaving a cellar
belonging to his family in the Bartodzieje estate with a foil bag holding 88
grams of a congealed substance, and seized 886 grams of 2C-B in total from the
cellar. [@1]

Police then went with the arrested man to his flat on the Leśne estate, where
they seized 288 grams of marijuana, 22 ecstasy tablets and 31 e-cigarette
refills containing marijuana oil. [@1] All the seized drugs were sent to a
police forensic laboratory. [@1]

The man was charged with possessing a significant quantity of drugs, an offence
carrying up to 10 years in prison. [@1] On 28 October 2026, at the request of
the police and the prosecutor, a judge ordered him held in pre-trial detention
for three months. [@1]
