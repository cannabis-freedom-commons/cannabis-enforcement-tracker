Officers from the Ciechanów section of the Criminal Division and the
Investigation Division of the Mazowieckie regional police headquarters in Radom,
together with officers of the Ciechanów county police, arrested a 60-year-old
man in Chrościce in Gmina Sońsk, Ciechanów County. [@1]

During a search of a maize field, a police dog picked up the scent and led the
officers to four places where black bags had been tied to the plant stalks. [@1]
Inside the packages police found dried plant material, a white clumped substance
and bottles holding a clear liquid, and drug-tester checks confirmed their
suspicions. [@1] The search yielded almost 3 kg of marijuana, almost 0.5 kg of
mephedrone, more than 10,000 cigarettes without Polish excise markings and about
2.5 litres of clear liquid sent for further testing. [@1] Police also seized
more than 15,000 złoty to cover future penalties. [@1]

The man was taken to a police holding room for detainees and heard a charge of
possession of significant quantities of narcotic substances. [@1] At the request
of police and prosecutors, a court ordered him held in pre-trial detention for
three months, and the offence carries up to 10 years in prison. [@1]

The search of the field in Chrościce, near Sońsk, was the result of earlier
police work identifying a man suspected of storing illegal substances. [@3]

Tygodnik Poradnik Rolniczy reported that the prosecutor charged him with
possession of a significant quantity of narcotic drugs and psychotropic
substances, and that the Ciechanów district court in full accepted the
prosecutor's request and ordered three months' detention. [@3]

AgroNews placed the arrest at Chrościce in Gmina Sońsk, Ciechanów County, and
said the 60-year-old was from Ciechanów County. [@2]
