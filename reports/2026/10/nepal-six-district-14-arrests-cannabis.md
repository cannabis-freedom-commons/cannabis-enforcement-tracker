Police arrested 14 people for alleged drug possession in separate operations
across Nepal. [@1] The arrests spanned Morang, Sunsari, Parsa, Rupandehi,
Bardiya and Chitwan districts. [@1] Muskan Rai was arrested in Kanepokhari-7 in
Morang with approximately 1.018 kilograms of cannabis. [@1] David Khadka was
arrested in Ratnanagar-4 in Chitwan with 36 tablets of tramadol and 134 grams of
cannabis. [@1] The suspects remained in police custody for further
investigation. [@1]

Rajesh Kumar Ray and Radha Krishna Ray from India were arrested in Biratnagar-18
in Morang with 52.15 grams of suspected brown heroin. [@1] In Sunsari, Sunil
Sardar was held in Itahari-13 with 126 tablets of nitrazepam, while Rikesh
Chaudhary was arrested in Itahari-9 with 510 milligrams of suspected brown
heroin. [@1] Six people were arrested in Birgunj in Parsa district. [@1] Faiyaz
Ansari and one other person were apprehended in Birgunj-25 with 150 milligrams
of suspected brown sugar. [@1] In Birgunj-16, Sidhant Singh and one other person
were arrested with 610 milligrams of suspected brown sugar, and Raju Manandhar
was taken into custody at Rajatjayanti Chowk with 3.88 grams of suspected brown
sugar. [@1] In Rupandehi, a teenager from India was arrested in Rohini-5 with
500 ampoules each of diazepam, buprenorphine and phenergan. [@1] In Bardiya,
Karna Marsyangdi Magar was arrested in Gulariya-11 with 1.32 grams of suspected
brown sugar, and Dipesh Chaudhary was apprehended in Gulariya-6 with 570
milligrams of the same substance. [@1]
