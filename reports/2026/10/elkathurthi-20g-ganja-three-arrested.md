Police in Elkathurthi arrested three men with 20 grams of ganja, the local name
for cannabis, during a vehicle check on the outskirts of the village. [@1,2] The
check was near Kakatiya Vantage, and the three tried to flee when they saw the
officers before being chased down. [@1,2] Officers seized the ganja and three
mobile phones. [@1,2] The men were identified as Roddaboyina Sridhar, Matteda
Jaswanth and Puppala Ganesh, all from Damera village. [@1,2]

The three were going to sell the rest when police caught them, the officer said.
[@1,2] Police said a preliminary investigation showed the three had been using
ganja for two years. [@1,2] Police registered a case and sent all three to court
for remand, Narasimha Rao said. [@1]

Sridhar bought 30 grams of ganja for Rs 1,500 from a man named Akash in Huzurabad on Wednesday, and he and Jaswanth smoked some of it that night. [@2] The vehicle checks were conducted on Friday afternoon at around 3 p.m. [@2]
