Oak Lawn police found 20-year-old Ameer Z. Abdelrahman asleep at the wheel at 11
p.m. on 28 October 2026 at 91st Street and South Cicero Avenue. [@1] Officers
reported that Abdelrahman appeared to be under the influence of cannabis, told
them he had smoked it before driving, and refused a blood test. [@1]

Noting that Abdelrahman was under the legal age for cannabis possession, police
reported finding several packages of cannabis and a cannabis vape pen in his
vehicle. [@1] Abdelrahman was also charged with possession of cannabis in a
vehicle and illegal stopping on a roadway, with a court appearance set for 24
November 2026. [@1]
