Officers patrolling the 500 block of Maides Avenue detected the odor of burning
marijuana and found a vehicle parked partially in the roadway against the flow
of traffic where people had congregated. [@1] Officers observed the driver of
the vehicle attempting to conceal marijuana, detained him and charged him with
marijuana possession. [@1] An officer pushed another man, who had left a nearby
vehicle and retreated into a residence despite officers' request to stop, back
from the immediate perimeter and to the ground to place him in handcuffs. [@1]
That man was charged with resist, delay or obstruct an officer. [@1] No weapons
or strikes were utilized during the encounter, the police department said. [@1]
The encounter was recorded by officers' body-worn cameras as well as by
bystanders, and video of it, including an officer throwing a man to the ground,
circulated on social media, drawing criticism. [@1]

A police department review found the officers acted lawfully and in compliance
with departmental policy and training standards, with the facts presented to the
district attorney's office. [@1] The review found minor policy infractions that
are being addressed internally through standard departmental disciplinary and
retraining procedures. [@1]

The encounter happened shortly after 2:30 p.m., WECT reported. [@2] An
unholstered firearm equipped with an extended magazine was observed in plain
view on the rear seat of the second vehicle while officers were detaining the
front passenger. [@2] Officers also encountered two men in a nearby vehicle, one
of whom police said was a suspect in a firearm-related crime reported 10 days
earlier. [@3]
