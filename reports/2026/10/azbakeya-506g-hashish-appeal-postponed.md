Cairo's appellate felony court postponed until 6 December the appeal of a
defendant convicted at first instance of trafficking drugs in the Azbakeya area
of Cairo. [@1] The defendant, 25-year-old Youssef A., had been sent to criminal
trial after prosecutors charged him with possessing hashish with intent to sell
outside legally permitted cases. [@1] The first-degree verdict had punished him
with six years of rigorous imprisonment and a fine of 200,000 pounds. [@1]

The case began when an Azbakeya police officer's secret inquiries concluded the
man was dealing narcotics, leading the officer to obtain arrest permission from
the prosecution. [@1] Officers moved to the defendant's location, arrested him
and, on searching him, found a cardboard box containing five large pieces of
hashish, as well as a sum of money and a mobile telephone. [@1] A chemical
laboratory report established that the seized material was five pieces of
hashish listed in Schedule One of the narcotics law, weighing 506 grams. [@1]
