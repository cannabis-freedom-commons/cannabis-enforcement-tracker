Usinsk drug-control officers stopped the activity of two men from Udmurtia
suspected of attempted unlawful sale of narcotics. [@1] The two detained men
were both aged 65, had known each other before, and had gathered and dried wild
cannabis in central Russia. [@1] After deciding to sell the cannabis in the
north, they rented a flat in Usinsk and began looking for ways to sell the
banned substance. [@1] Police detained the men after developing operational
information. [@1]

During a search of the rented flat, officers found more than 220 grams of dried
cannabis stored in bags, flasks, jars and boxes. [@2] Officers also found scales
and packing material in the flat for dividing the material into smaller doses.
[@1]

An investigator opened criminal cases against both men for attempted unlawful
sale of narcotics on a large scale under Article 30(3) and Article 228.1(4)(g)
of the Russian Criminal Code. [@1] The charge carries up to 20 years of
imprisonment, and both suspects were remanded in custody. [@2] Police said
further investigative and search measures continue to establish all the
circumstances of the offence. [@1]
