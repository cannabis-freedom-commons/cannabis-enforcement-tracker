A 22-year-old man was remanded in custody for eight days on Friday after police
seized about 6.4kg of cannabis. [@1,6] The Nicosia district court issued the
remand order after his arrest in Engomi the previous day. [@1] He is being
investigated for illegal importation and possession of controlled drugs,
possession with intent to supply, conspiracy to commit a felony and offences
related to the proceeds of illegal activity. [@3,6,7]

The investigation concerned a parcel containing 2.56kg of cannabis that arrived
in Cyprus from abroad on 26 October. [@1,4,5] Police investigated the case with the
customs department. [@1,4,5] The parcel had been declared as Lego toys. [@3,7] It
carried the details of a sender company in the United States and a recipient
address in Engomi. [@3,7] Anti-drug police replaced the cannabis with dummies and
mounted a controlled delivery. [@3,6,7] On 29 October the parcel was left at a
parking area in Engomi under police surveillance. [@3] A 62-year-old taxi driver
then collected it and was arrested. [@2,4,5] He told police a customer had
instructed him to collect it and that he did not know what it contained. [@1,5] He
was released after questioning when no evidence emerged against him. [@1,4,5,6,7]

Further inquiries led police to the 22-year-old, and officers stopped his car
for a check. [@2,7] In the car they found three packets holding about 100g of
cannabis and further packages weighing about 3.84kg, police said. [@1] They also
seized €4,330 and five mobile phones. [@1,4,5,6,7]

The investigation was at an early stage. [@3,7] Investigators planned to take
statements and examine phone data and transactions, and asked the taxi platform
and courier companies for information. [@3] Police are also looking into
possible accomplices and earlier shipments. [@3]

Police searched the taxi driver's home with his written consent and found nothing illegal. [@5]

His lawyer, Nikos Koromias, did not object to the remand request. [@6] Surveillance officers saw the suspicious car circle the building complex and then follow the undercover officer when he drove away. [@6] In the suspect's car, officers found about 100 grams on the front passenger-side mat, about 1,836 grams elsewhere and four airtight packages containing two kilograms. [@6]
