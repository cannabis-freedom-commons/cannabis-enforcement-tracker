Criminal-investigation officers of the capital's militia in Tiraspol detained a
20-year-old local man suspected of unlawfully storing narcotic substances. [@1]
Officers found a transparent packet with a ziplock-type fastener during a
personal search of the young man. [@1] The packet was in the pocket of his
jeans. [@1] Inside the packet was a brown pliable mass that looked like hashish.
[@1] The detained man said he had supposedly found the packet while out for a
walk. [@1]

Authorities are carrying out a pre-investigation check into the incident. [@1]
The seized substance was sent for chemical-biological examination to establish
its composition. [@1] The young man could face criminal liability punishable by
up to three years' imprisonment. [@1] An administrative penalty is also provided
of a fine of up to 6,300 PMR roubles or arrest for up to 30 days. [@1]

Police reminded that unlawful consumption, storage and distribution of narcotic
substances entails liability under current legislation. [@1] They added that
identifying and suppressing crimes and offences in the sphere of illicit drug
trafficking remains one of the priority areas for the internal-affairs bodies.
[@1]
