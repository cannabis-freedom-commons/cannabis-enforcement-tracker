Police in Rajaldesar, Churu district, Rajasthan, moved against narcotics
smuggling, seizing drugs from a three-wheeled e-rickshaw and arresting the
driver. [@1]

The outlet's headline reports that Rajaldesar police seized 16.5 kg of doda post
and 8 kg of ganja. [@1] Doda post is opium poppy husk, not cannabis, so only the
8 kg of ganja counts as a cannabis quantity. [@1]

The case against the arrested driver was registered under the NDPS Act, India's
Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act. [@1]
