The Military Highway Police stopped a Fiat Palio on the SP-294 in Duartina, São
Paulo state, on Friday 30 October 2026 and arrested the driver on suspicion of
drug trafficking. [@2] The stop was at about 18:00 at kilometre 382. [@3] Three
people were in the car. [@1] The driver accepted responsibility for the load and
said the two passengers did not know about it. [@3] Police said he had a prior
drug-trafficking record. [@2]

Officers found 16.4 kg of Moroccan hashish in 30 packages and 3.4 kg of
marijuana in five blocks, hidden in false compartments in the seatback, under
the rear seat and in the dashboard. [@2] The combined load weighed 19.8 kg. [@3]
The Military Highway Police estimated the load's value at R$ 1.2 million. [@2]

The stop occurred during Operação Finados and involved the Força Integrada de
Combate ao Crime Organizado (FICCO), an organised-crime task force. [@2]
Officers took the case to the Centro de Polícia Judiciária in Bauru, and the
driver remained in custody awaiting a court decision. [@2]
