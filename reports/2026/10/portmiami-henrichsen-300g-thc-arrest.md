An orthopedic surgeon was taken into custody after he was allegedly caught with
drugs while trying to board a cruise ship to the Bahamas. [@1] Dr. Jacob Lee
Henrichsen, 38, was taken into custody in PortMiami after federal border agents
intercepted his luggage during an outbound inspection operation dubbed Operation
Temptation 2026. [@1] He had arrived at Terminal B at PortMiami to check in for
a cruise aboard Norwegian Cruise Line's Jewel, which was destined for Nassau.
[@1] Homeland Security found the total amount did not meet a threshold for
federal prosecution, and Miami-Dade Sheriff's Office detectives then took over
jurisdiction and arrested him. [@1] Henrichsen was arrested and charged with
trafficking MDMA and two felony counts of controlled substance possession. [@1]
He was taken to the Turner Guilford Knight Correction Center, where he was held
on a $40,000 bond. [@1]

During the outbound inspection, agents behind terminal X-ray screeners flagged a
Pelican case for secondary screening. [@1] After Henrichsen completed a customs
declaration confirming all items inside belonged to him, officers searched the
container and allegedly found contraband disguised as normal confectionery and
vape gear. [@1] A field test reportedly revealed approximately 12 grams of MDMA
consisting of capsules, gummies and pills stamped with the Tesla logo. [@1]
Around 160 grams of mushroom edibles were also reportedly seized and came back
positive for LSD properties. [@1] The search also allegedly found more than 300
grams of THC gummies, vape oil and mints. [@1]

The suspect reportedly practices at Mt. Graham Regional Medical Center in
Safford, Arizona. [@1] At a court hearing, his attorney argued the weights were
overrepresented because the gummies were largely sugar and water with only small
quantities of MDMA inside. [@1]
