Horizon City police stopped 39-year-old Miguel Angel Valdez for a traffic
violation just after 10 a.m. on Wednesday, Oct. 21. [@1] The stop was made by
officers of the Horizon City Police Department’s Tactical Suppression and Gang
Unit. [@1] The case was reported as an arrest for drug possession in Horizon
City. [@1] During contact with Valdez, officers detected an odor of marijuana
coming from Valdez and the vehicle. [@1] That odor led officers to conduct a
probable cause search, police said. [@1]

Police found suspected marijuana and a suspected crystal-like substance inside
the vehicle’s center console. [@1] The substances were field-tested and returned
positive results for marijuana and methamphetamine. [@1]

A man was arrested and charged with possession of methamphetamine and marijuana
on Wednesday, Oct. 21, according to the Horizon City Police Department. [@1]
Valdez was taken to the El Paso County Downtown Jail. [@1] He was charged with
possession of a controlled substance and possession of marijuana. [@1]
