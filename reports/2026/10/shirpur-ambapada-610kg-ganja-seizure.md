Police in Shirpur taluka, Dhule district, found cannabis (ganja) being grown
illegally on forest land in Ambapada shivar, the land around Ambapada village.
[@1] They seized about 610 kg of the crop, which police valued at ₹1.52 crore
(1,52,50,000 rupees). [@1,2] No one has been identified as the cultivator. [@1,2] A
case was registered against unidentified people at Shirpur police station and a
search for them is under way. [@1]

Police Inspector Kishorkumar Pardeshi received confidential information that
unidentified people were growing cannabis on remote forest land in the hills.
[@1] Officers verified the information and raided the forest land. [@1,2] They
found a large plantation and seized the whole crop. [@1]

Assistant Police Inspector Varsha Patil led the operation. [@1] Police are
investigating who cultivated the crop, whether a wider network is behind it and
how far the illegal business reached. [@1] Police said tracing the organisers
behind the plantation was now their main challenge. [@1] The discovery of
large-scale cultivation in remote forest land caused a stir in the area. [@1]
