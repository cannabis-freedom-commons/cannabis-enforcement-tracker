A man was arrested after police found what they described as a significant
quantity of suspected herbal cannabis during a vehicle search. [@1,3] The arrest
took place in Wanstead Park on the evening of Friday 30 October 2026. [@1,3] The
stop was made during patrols by the Wanstead Park Safer Neighbourhood Team as
part of the Metropolitan Police's Autumn Nights patrols. [@1,3] The search was
conducted under Section 23 of the Misuse of Drugs Act. [@2,3]

The man remained under investigation as enquiries continued, the force said.
[@1,3] A Metropolitan Police spokesman said the result highlighted the continued
commitment of local officers to tackling drug-related crime and anti-social
behaviour. [@1] The spokesman said the force was committed to tackling drug
dealing wherever it found it. [@1] He said drug dealing formed a key part of the
force's recently published ASB Action Plan. [@1]

The Met said it had put hundreds more officers on the frontlines of community
crime-fighting teams and increased visible patrols in hotspot areas to deter and
catch criminals. [@1] The force encouraged residents to report concerns about
drug dealing, drug use or anti-social behaviour, saying community intelligence
played a crucial role in supporting police action. [@1]
