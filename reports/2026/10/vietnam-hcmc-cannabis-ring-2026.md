Ho Chi Minh City police said on 22 October 2026 that they had dismantled an inter-provincial cannabis ring led by Trần Huy Long, arresting 151 people and seizing 176.2 kg of cannabis and 178 e-cigarettes containing cannabis oil. Eighty-one of those arrested faced criminal proceedings and 70 received administrative penalties. The ring stored cannabis in Đồng Tháp and Vĩnh Long and used express delivery and closed online groups to move it into the city. The investigation began with a bar inspection on 7 October. [@1]

Dawn on 7 October, drug-crime police with Cau Ong Lanh ward police and other units inspected a bar on Vo Van Kiet street. [@2] Electronic data collected during the checks led investigators to the organised cannabis ring headed by Tran Huy Long. [@2] Drug-crime police worked with Division 1 of the Ho Chi Minh City People's Procuracy from the outset to consolidate evidence. [@2]

The ring used encrypted apps and closed groups, and disguised cannabis in express-delivery parcels or dried-fish bags moved through several provinces before reaching Ho Chi Minh City for sale. [@3]
