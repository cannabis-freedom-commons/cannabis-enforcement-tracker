Nineteen people were referred to the Paris correctional court over an alleged
circuit in which cocaine imported to mainland France was paid for with cannabis
shipped to Guadeloupe. [@1] Twenty suspects were caught in the case, and
nineteen of them are initially being sent for trial. [@1] The alleged hub is
48-year-old Gilles T., officially a fruit-and-vegetable wholesaler nicknamed
Gillou or Gwada. [@1] Investigators say he allegedly received in the Antilles
cannabis sent from mainland France by the Strasbourg team in exchange for
cocaine travelling the other way. [@1] Mickaël H., 43, presented by
investigators as the head of the Strasbourg branch, spoke of five kilos of
poor-quality cocaine recovered by an accomplice, Salah B., 35. [@1] He admits
only a logistics role for 69 kilos of cocaine. [@2] Jefferson J.G., a
31-year-old Colombian, was arrested in Saint-Martin, where arms and cocaine
loaves were found in a studio, villa and warehouse allegedly made available by
Gilles T. [@1]

The case started with the April 2021 seizure at Le Havre of 112 kg of cocaine
hidden in moving containers from Guadeloupe on a tip from the Office
anti-stupéfiants (Ofast), followed by arrest waves from October 2022 under the
Paris Juridiction interrégionale spécialisée (Jirs). [@1] Investigators describe
mainly cocaine hidden in legal freight and shipped by sea from Latin America to
complicit teams in the Paris region and Alsace. [@1] The origin of the cannabis
remains unclear, although the investigation lists trips by suspects to the
Netherlands. [@1] A second account describes the same transatlantic barter as
cocaine from Latin America exchanged for cannabis shipped from Alsace. [@2]

Investigators describe a well-equipped network using jammers in containers to
block tracking, and say Gillou boasted of contacts in the secondary ports of
Montoir-de-Bretagne near Saint-Nazaire and Dunkirk. [@1] The network allegedly
lost 300 kg to a rival Le Havre gang in an episode that included shots fired at
a van chased by rivals outside the port. [@1] The twentieth suspect, 36-year-old
Parisian Marwane C., was arrested in January 2022 aboard a Brazilian-flagged
fishing boat carrying 900 kg of cocaine off the Canary Islands. [@1] He will not
appear with the nineteen because he was sentenced to ten years in Spain, where
he must serve half the term before expulsion, and a fixing hearing on 3 November
will set the calendar for the future trial. [@1] A second account confirms that
nineteen people were sent to the Paris correctional court and that a fixing
hearing on 3 November must set the trial calendar. [@2]
