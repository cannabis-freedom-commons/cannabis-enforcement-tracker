On Wednesday 14 October 2026 the Colorado Springs Police Department's Metro Narcotics Unit,
with El Paso County Sheriff's Office deputies and Drug Enforcement Administration agents,
simultaneously served search warrants at five Speedys Smoke Shop addresses in Colorado Springs,
one Speedys in El Paso County and two Los Compadres Liquor Store sites. [@1,2,3] CSPD said none
of the businesses were licensed marijuana dispensaries and that the long-running inquiry targeted
suspected illegal THC, psilocybin and 7-hydroxymitragynine sales, including to juveniles. [@1,2,3]

Officers seized large quantities of those products and cash; CSPD said inventory would take
several days before exact totals were known. [@1] A follow-up release put the seizure at
2,135.05 pounds of THC products, 469.2 pounds of 7-OH products and 4.2 pounds of psilocybin
products, noting those weights were for the whole products rather than pure drug content. [@2,3]
At one shop investigators found a 16-year-old working alone behind the counter when they arrived.
[@1,2,3]

Chief Adrian Vasquez and DEA Rocky Mountain division chief Mark Putnam said the packaging
appeared aimed at teenagers and young adults. [@1,2] CSPD said narcotics distribution charges
were expected against those responsible and that the investigation remained open. [@2]
