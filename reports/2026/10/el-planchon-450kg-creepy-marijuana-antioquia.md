National Police officers seized 450 kilos of 'creepy' marijuana during a road
control in southwestern Antioquia and captured two people transporting it in a
vehicle. [@1]

The seizure happened at El Planchón, on the road between La Pintada and
Bolombolo, and was detected by traffic police of the National Police's Ditra
directorate during a routine control on that corridor. [@1] Police said the
marijuana was being moved by the method known as kamikaze, a practice of
transporting illicit loads by road to evade authorities' checkpoints. [@1]

The vehicle in which the detainees and the marijuana were traveling reportedly
had a theft report registered in Cali, and judicial authorities would verify its
origin, any link to criminal networks and the final destination of the seized
substance. [@1]

The two captured people were handed over to the competent authorities and the
Attorney General's Office must define their legal status and determine whether
they belong to a structure dedicated to drug transport or played a specific role
in a criminal organization's logistics chain. [@1]

'Creepy' is a variety that usually has a higher concentration of
tetrahydrocannabinol, the main psychoactive component of cannabis. [@1]
