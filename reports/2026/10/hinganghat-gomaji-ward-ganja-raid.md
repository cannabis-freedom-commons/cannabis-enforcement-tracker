Wardha's Local Crime Branch raided Harshal Vinod Gimekar's house in Gomaji Ward,
Hinganghat, on 29 October 2026. [@2] Officers acted on a tip that ganja and
country liquor were being stored and sold from the house. [@3] A search of the
house found ganja and country liquor, and body searches found two mobile phones.
[@3]

Police seized 1 kg 113 g of ganja, 200 sealed bottles of country liquor and the
two phones. [@2] They valued the property at Rs 87,150. [@1] The two men told
officers the stock belonged to all three of them, and police concluded the three
were trafficking and selling ganja and liquor together. [@1] A case was
registered at Hinganghat police station against all three under the NDPS Act and
the Maharashtra prohibition law. [@3] Both men were taken into custody at the
scene. [@3]

Police named Harshal Gimekar as the main accused and said he was absconding.
[@3] Crime Branch Unit 6 arrested him in Nagpur. [@4] During a night patrol on
30 October 2026, officers were told he was moving around the Besa area on
Manevada Road. [@4] They chased him and took him into custody. [@4]
Superintendent of Police Saurabh Kumar Agrawal had ordered the branch to act
against drug and liquor sellers. [@2]
