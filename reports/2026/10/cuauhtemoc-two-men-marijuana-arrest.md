Chihuahua's State Investigation Agency (Agencia Estatal de Investigación, AEI)
and personnel from Mexico's Secretariat of National Defence (Secretaría de la
Defensa Nacional, Sedena) arrested two men in separate Cuauhtémoc interventions
during patrols described as crime-prevention and deterrence rounds. [@1] The men
were identified as Luis Raúl O. C., 33, and Francisco Iván E. E., 43. [@2]

Luis was detained at Francisco I. Madero and Parque Chapultepec in colonia
Pascual Orozco with a plastic packet containing 81 grams of a green, dry,
odorous herb described as marijuana. [@1] Francisco was detained at Quintana Roo
and Privada de Guanajuato in colonia Reforma with a packet containing 162 grams
of marijuana. [@2] The two reported packets together weighed 243 grams (0.243
kg) of marijuana. [@1]

The two men and the seized marijuana were taken to Fiscalía de Distrito Zona
Occidente, the western-district prosecutor's office, and placed before the
Public Ministry's Unit for Investigation of Street-Level Drug Dealing. [@2] The
accounts state that the men are presumed innocent unless a judicial authority
declares them responsible by sentence. [@2]
