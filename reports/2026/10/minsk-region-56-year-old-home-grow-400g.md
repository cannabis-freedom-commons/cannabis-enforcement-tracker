Officers detained a 56-year-old Minsk Region resident who had fitted out a room
in his private house for growing narcotic-containing plants. [@1] The account
came from the Minsk City police department, the GUVD Mingorispolkoma, which
covers the capital rather than the Minsk Region police. [@1]

At his home, officers found and seized two pots with soil and cannabis plants,
containers of fertilizer and equipment for growing narcotic-containing plants.
[@1] The same search turned up smoking devices and more than 400 grams of
ready-to-use marijuana. [@1] A criminal case was opened. [@1]

The man had bought the seeds at an online drug market and grew the plants in his
room. [@1] A second outlet, citing the GUVD through BelTA on the same day,
reported that police had shut down the activity of the 56-year-old Minsk Region
man, suspected of illegal drug trafficking. [@2] It said the search of the home
yielded two pots with soil and cannabis bushes, fertilizer containers, growing
equipment and more than 400 grams of ready marijuana. [@2]
