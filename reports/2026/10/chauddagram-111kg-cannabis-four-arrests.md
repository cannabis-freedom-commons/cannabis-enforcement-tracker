A joint operational team of Special Response Battalion (SRB)-7, Chattogram and
SRB-11, Narayanganj arrested four alleged drug dealers in separate operations in
Chauddagram of Cumilla, seizing cannabis and foreign liquor. [@1] The account
dates both operations to late at night on Friday (2 October) in the Chauddagram
police station area. [@1] The two men held in the cannabis case are 40-year-old
Md Azam Ali, son of Md Abu Taleb of Hazarbighi in Shibganj, Chapainawabganj, and
40-year-old Md Dablu, son of Md Khalil of Ajgobi in the same police station
area. [@1] The two men held in the liquor case are 28-year-old Sagor Hossain,
son of Nurul Islam of Shubhapur in Nangalkot, Cumilla, and 27-year-old Joynal
Abedin, son of the late Zillur Rahman of Enayetpur in Senbagh, Noakhali. [@1]

Acting on secret information, the first team stopped a suspicious Dhaka-bound
cargo truck at about 1:05 a.m. in front of the Hotel Bypass at Baburchi Bazar on
the Chattogram-Dhaka highway, where the driver and the man beside him tried to
flee and were detained. [@1] Officers then recovered 111 kg of ganja, the local
term for cannabis, from the rear part of that truck. [@1] The SRB put the
estimated market value of the recovered ganja at 11 lakh 10 thousand taka
(1,110,000 taka). [@1] In the second operation at about 4:10 a.m. at the
three-road crossing of Bijoykara Bazar, the team signalled a CNG-run
auto-rickshaw travelling from the Gangra area toward Nangalkot to stop, and
detained its driver and a rear-seat passenger after they tried to flee. [@1]
Officers recovered 54 bottles of foreign liquor from six cartons concealed
behind the auto-rickshaw passenger seat, and seized the cargo truck and the
auto-rickshaw used to carry the drugs. [@1]

SRB, the Special Response Battalion, attributes the interceptions to its own
operational teams working from prior tip-offs, and the seized truck and
auto-rickshaw were taken as vehicles used for drug transport. [@1] Further legal
action against the four detained men and the recovered drugs was reported as
pending. [@1]
