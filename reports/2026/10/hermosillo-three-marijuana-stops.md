Police in Hermosillo arrested three men in three separate stops, two of them
described as priority targets, and seized apparent marijuana from each. [@1] The
first stop was at midnight on Wednesday, on Burela and Borja streets in the Urbi
Villa del Prado subdivision. [@1] There officers subdued Jorge Antonio, known as
“El Toti”. [@1] When he saw the officers, he dropped a 20-centimetre knife and
three units of dry green herb. [@1]

At 10:10 on Thursday, officers arrested Jonathan Brayan, 23, on Leona Vicario
avenue and Lázaro Mercado street in the El Sahuaro neighbourhood. [@1] Seeing
the patrol car, he threw away five units of the drug. [@1] At 17:30 on Thursday,
officers stopped Yoandrik Guadalupe, 18, in the maroon 2026 Nissan Versa he was
driving. [@1] He was the other priority target, stopped at the crossing of Ángel
García Aburto avenue and Carlos Caturegli in the El Choyal neighbourhood, where
officers seized four wrappers of marijuana from him. [@1]

The three detainees, the knife, the vehicle and the drugs were left at the
disposal of the ministerial agent, an officer of the prosecutor's office
handling the case. [@1]
