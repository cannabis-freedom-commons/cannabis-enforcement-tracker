Three men aged 43, 37 and 21 were sentenced on 30 October 2026 by the
Saint-Martin criminal court for drug trafficking on Saint-Barthélemy. [@1] The
two older men received 36 months in prison, 12 of them suspended, and the
youngest 18 months, 6 of them suspended. [@1] Deposit warrants were issued at
the hearing and the convicted men went straight back to prison. [@1]

The case began on 9 September 2026 with a roadside gendarmerie check on a
scooter in Saint-Jean whose rider had an unfastened helmet and a faulty
headlight. [@1] The rider refused a drug test and threw a small backpack over a
fence, and gendarmes found cocaine, ecstasy, cannabis and ketamine inside. [@1]
A search of his home found more than a kilo of cannabis resin packed as ten
blocks. [@1] The searches yielded 1,044 grams of cannabis resin, along with 612
grams of ecstasy, 131 grams of cocaine, 111 grams of ketamine and 1,262.05 euros
in cash. [@3] The man was arrested and two presumed accomplices were identified,
arrested and questioned. [@1]

According to Le Pélican, the drugs came from Saint-Martin and were carried
between the port of Gustavia and the men's shared home in Saint-Barthélemy. [@2]
The prosecutor asked for firm prison terms of four years for the 43-year-old,
three years for his 37-year-old landlord and two years for the 21-year-old. [@1]
The defence lawyers argued that the men's access to a lawyer during a
gendarmerie interview had been breached and that the facts were insufficiently
dated, and the court ruled on those requests with the case. [@1] Le Pélican
reports that the youngest was acquitted of the transport charge. [@2] Karib'Info
describes the case as directed by prosecutors from the Basse-Terre public
prosecutor's detached Saint-Martin office, working with gendarmes of the
Saint-Barthélemy autonomous territorial brigade. [@3]
