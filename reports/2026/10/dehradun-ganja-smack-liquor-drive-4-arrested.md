Dehradun police intensified action against the illegal drug trade in the city.
[@1] The SSP Dehradun ordered checking campaigns across separate police-station
areas. [@1] The action stretched from Basant Vihar in Dehradun to Rishikesh,
marking the two ends of the reported drive. [@1] The drive was presented as a
drug crackdown in which ganja, smack and liquor were seized and four people were
arrested. [@1]

Police arrested four drug traffickers during the checking campaign rather than
in a single raid. [@1] The seizures from the accused included 1 kg 102 grams of
ganja. [@1] Smack and illicit liquor were recovered in the same drive alongside
the ganja. [@1] The four arrests cover the combined narcotics and liquor
recoveries rather than the cannabis seizure alone. [@1]

The drive thus combined narcotics and illicit-liquor enforcement across the
station areas under the campaign. [@1] Police counted the four accused across
the areas covered rather than at one spot. [@1]
