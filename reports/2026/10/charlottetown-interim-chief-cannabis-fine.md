Charlottetown interim police chief Darren MacDougall was fined under Prince
Edward Island's Cannabis Control Act for buying cannabis product from an
unregulated store. [@1] In July 2025 he bought cannabis product from an
unregulated store on Abegweit First Nation while the store was under
investigation by the RCMP. [@1] About a year later court records show the RCMP
fined him $200 under the provincial Act, and he pleaded guilty and paid the
fine. [@1] The fine came in July shortly after he became interim chief, and he
said he informed city officials immediately. [@1]

MacDougall said he stopped at Mniku Trading Post while driving east to play golf
and thought the First Nation location made the purchase permissible. [@1] Prince
Edward Island RCMP confirmed a Charlottetown Police member received a summary
offence ticket for the purchase as part of a larger investigation. [@1] The RCMP
warned that unregulated cannabis is often untested, can be dozens of times
stronger than legally sold cannabis, and can contain harmful or unmarked
material. [@1] MacDougall said he had believed treaty issues might allow
cannabis sales on reserves but accepted that someone in his position should have
known better. [@1]

City human resources officials said they reviewed the matter, were satisfied
with how it was addressed, and remain confident in his leadership. [@1] First
Nations across Canada have argued they have the right to sell cannabis, but no
court has agreed and RCMP have raided shops. [@1] Charlottetown has been without
a permanent police chief for more than a year. [@1] MacDougall, a member of
Charlottetown police for three decades, said he has interviewed for the
permanent job and wants it. [@1]
