Champawat police arrested five men under Operation Prahar, an anti-drug
operation. [@1] A joint team of the Special Operations Group and Chalthi police
was checking vehicles on 28 October 2026. [@1] Officers stopped car UK06 BS 2063
and searched it. [@1] At the Chalthi checkpoint gate, they found 992 g of charas
in the car's boot. [@1] Police seized the car at the scene. [@1] The five were
Narayan Nath of Kachlakot, Nainital; Jitendra Singh Rana and Pan Singh Rana of
Bamanpuri in Udham Singh Nagar; Mohan Joshi of Chalthia, Champawat; and
Kalicharan Rana of Dhusri in Udham Singh Nagar. [@2]

Two of the five are serving gram pradhans, or village heads: Jitendra Singh Rana
heads Bamanpuri and Kalicharan Rana heads Dhusri, while Mohan Joshi is a former
head of Ehta Bihi in Nanakmatta. [@3] Circle Officer Devendra Singh said two
current and one former village head were among the five arrested. [@3] The car
belonged to Pan Singh Rana. [@3] A case was registered against all five at
Kotwali Champawat under sections 8, 20, 29 and 60 of the NDPS Act. [@1] They
were produced in court and sent to jail, the Circle Officer said. [@3]

The checking was carried out on the instructions of Champawat Superintendent of
Police Rekha Yadav and under the supervision of Circle Officer Devendra Singh
Kapravan. [@1] Police described the action as part of Chief Minister Pushkar
Singh Dhami's 'Drugs Free Devbhoomi – Nasha Mukt Bharat' campaign. [@1]
