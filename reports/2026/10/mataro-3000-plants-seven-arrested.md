Police arrested seven people in an operation against an alleged cannabis cultivation and distribution network in Barcelona province. [@1,2,3,4,5] Police sources told Europa Press the raids took place on 20 October 2026 in Castellar del Vallès and the Can Canyamars housing area. [@1,4] El Debate and La Vanguardia identified National Police and Mataró local police as participants, while Capgròs also reported the involvement of Dosrius local police. [@1,2,3]

Officers seized 3,000 cannabis plants and a kilogram of buds along with growing equipment, 5,000 euros, three vehicles and a motorcycle. [@1,2,3,4,5] They also seized firearms, including an AK-47 and two pistols, ammunition, a replica pistol and other weapons. [@1,2,3,4,5] Police said the group used indoor growing sites to supply the illicit market in eastern Europe. [@1,2,3,4,5]

El Caso identified the investigation as Operation Bóveda and reported that the detainees included a Uruguayan national and an Armenian national. [@6]

The seven suspects were handed to the courts over alleged drug offences, unlawful possession of weapons, electricity fraud and membership of a criminal organisation. [@1,2,3,4,5]
