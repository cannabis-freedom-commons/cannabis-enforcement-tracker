The U.S. Coast Guard announced the offload of more than $226 million in illicit
drugs at Port Everglades, Florida, on Wednesday morning, 14 October 2026. [@1]
The haul came from six separate interdictions by the crew of Coast Guard Cutter
Tampa, with help from partner agencies, during operations in the Eastern Pacific
Ocean. [@1]

Officials estimated the offload at about 29,600 pounds of cocaine and 3,355
pounds of marijuana, or about 1,522 kilograms of cannabis. [@1] Officials put
the estimated value of the combined cocaine and marijuana haul at $226.7
million, not a marijuana-only value. [@1]

Atlantic Area commander Vice Adm. Jo-Ann F. Burdian joined Cutter Tampa
commanding officer Cmdr. Joshua M. DiPietro and Helicopter Interdiction Tactical
Squadron commanding officer Capt. Kent Reinhold for the announcement. [@1]
Burdian said the roughly 13 metric tons of cocaine seized was enough to deliver
10 million potentially fatal doses to American communities. [@1] DiPietro said
smugglers use increasingly powerful vessels, intelligence networks and
deliberate tactics such as scuttling valves that sink evidence and endanger
boarding teams. [@1]
