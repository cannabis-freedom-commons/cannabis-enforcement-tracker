Five people were arrested with dealer paraphernalia in their car parked near
Laon prison. [@1] Investigators linked the group to a detainee, whose cell was
then searched by prison officers. [@2] Two of the suspects were jailed and a
third was placed under judicial supervision. [@2] Three of them are to appear
before the court on 22 December. [@2]

In the car, police found 1,030 euros in cash, fourteen vials of cocaine and
cannabis resin, plus a drone with five batteries and its remote controls. [@1]
The cell search found 101.95 grams of cannabis resin, 16.97 grams of crack and
4.79 grams of cocaine. [@2] The prisoner also had a mobile phone and chargers
used to communicate with his accomplices. [@2] Officers also seized dealer
paraphernalia including a scale, utensils and packaging bags. [@2]

The drone delivered the drugs with an artisanal grappling hook fixed to the end
of a string. [@2]
