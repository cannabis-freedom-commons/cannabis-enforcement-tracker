Military Police stopped two men at about 10:50 p.m. on 31 October 2026 on
Avenida Doutor José Ferreira Azambuja in Vila Nova, São Carlos. [@2] Officers
found a replica pistol, described as a simulacro, and a portion of maconha, or
cannabis, held by one of the men. [@2] The man holding the objects told officers
he was on his way to a costume party. [@2] Both men were taken to the Central de
Polícia Judiciária (CPJ), the judicial police station, to give statements. [@2]

Police kept the replica pistol and the cannabis portion seized for further legal
procedures. [@2] Both men were released after questioning. [@2]

The approach was carried out by soldier Viotto and corporal Custódio during
patrol. [@2] The stop was one of four overnight cases handled by teams of the
38th Interior Military Police Battalion (38º BPM/I) in São Carlos and Ibaté
between the night of 31 October and the dawn of 1 November, with those involved
and seized materials sent to the Civil Police. [@2] Police recorded
drug-trafficking, replica-seizure and motorcycle-theft cases in São Carlos and
Ibaté. [@1]
