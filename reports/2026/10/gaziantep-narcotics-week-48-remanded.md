Police in Gaziantep detained 294 suspects in a week of narcotics operations.
[@2] After legal processing, all 294 suspects were sent to the judicial
authorities. [@1] A court remanded 48 of them in pre-trial detention. [@2] The
operations were coordinated by the Gaziantep Chief Public Prosecutor's Office
and carried out by teams from the provincial police directorate's narcotics
branch. [@1]

Officers raided numerous addresses in the city during the week. [@1] Police said
the searches turned up 789 pills, 769 grams of methamphetamine, 658 grams of
esrar — the cannabis preparation known in Turkey — 274 grams of synthetic
cannabinoids, 5 grams of cocaine and six precision scales. [@1] Anadolu Agency's
account of the week put the haul at 89 pills and 1,048 grams of synthetic drugs,
alongside the same 658 grams of esrar and six scales. [@2]
