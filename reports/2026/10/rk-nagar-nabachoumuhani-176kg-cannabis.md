A police team led by Sub-Inspector Arpan Saha was checking vehicles at Nabachoumuhani around 10 pm on Friday night when a white Maruti Dzire travelling from Ramchandra Ghat towards Manik Choumuhani was signalled to stop. [@1] The driver allegedly tried to speed away, and police intercepted the car near Nabachoumuhani market and detained him. [@1]

On questioning, the driver reportedly disclosed that the vehicle was carrying dried cannabis, and the Jirania deputy collector magistrate and a forensic team were called to the spot. [@1] A search recovered 17 packets of cannabis weighing about 10.4 kg each, 176.8 kg in total. [@1] Police seized the narcotics and the vehicle, registration number TR07C0772. [@1]

A case was registered against the driver under the NDPS Act, and police said he was being forwarded to court. [@1] The operation was carried out by Bojrangnagar police station under officer-in-charge Prasanta Kumar Dey. [@1]
