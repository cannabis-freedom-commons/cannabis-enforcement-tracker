Ateli police arrested a youth with 345 grams of ganja in the bani at Tobra
village. [@2] The accused was identified as Sudhan, a resident of Prithvipura
village in Ateli. [@2] Deputy Superintendent of Police Dr Kavita said the Ateli
police team was on patrol on 27 October for crime prevention when it received
the tip. [@3] Police had been told that Sudhan stays near the pond in Tobra
bani, sells ganja and was then apparently about to sell ganja. [@2] Officers
searched the youth in the presence of a duty magistrate following NDPS Act
provisions. [@2]

Police asked him to produce a licence or permit, which he could not, then took
the ganja into custody and sealed it. [@2]

Police reported this ganja recovery as one of four separate cases in
Mahendragarh district that week. [@1]
