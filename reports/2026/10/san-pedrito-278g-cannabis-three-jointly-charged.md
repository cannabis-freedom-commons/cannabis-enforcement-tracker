Three residents of San Pedrito, San Pedro Town, were formally arrested and
jointly charged with possession of controlled drugs with intent to supply after
the discovery of 278.1 grams of cannabis. [@1] Police identified the accused as
Sheildy E. Rodriguez Jacome, Euthrofio Oscar Lisbey and Joel Amalio Herrata.
[@1] Detective Constable Arley Castillo formally arrested and charged the three
on October 25, 2026, police said. [@1]

All three were cautioned by police and elected to remain silent. [@1] At a
police press briefing on Monday, Assistant Superintendent Stacy Smith said
Jacome was additionally charged with possession of controlled drugs with intent
to supply after 10.7 grams of cocaine was found in a search of her person. [@1]
Police have not disclosed further details of the circumstances of the arrests.
