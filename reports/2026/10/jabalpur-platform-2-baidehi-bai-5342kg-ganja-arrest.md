Government Railway Police officers arrested 35-year-old Baidehi Bai on Platform
No. 2 of Jabalpur railway station in October 2026 with 5.342 kg of weed in her
bag. [@1] The arrest took place on the morning of 7 October 2026, during
platform checks at the Itarsi end of platforms 2 and 3. [@2] Five packets
wrapped in white cloth were found inside the cloth bag she was carrying. [@2]
Police estimated the seized weed at Rs 1,33,550. [@2]

Baidehi Bai is a resident of Raisen and was living in Bargarh district of
Odisha. [@1] She is accused of smuggling weed from Odisha into Madhya Pradesh.
[@1] The GRP registered a case against her under Section 8/20 of the NDPS Act
and said it was investigating the source of the marijuana. [@2] Station House
Officer Sanjeevani Rajput led the operation. [@2]

A Bhaskar investigation found that needy girls are recruited into teams as
players, coaches and support staff and then used to transport illegal substances
to other states. [@1] A woman player who was caught and jailed said carriers are
paid Rs 20,000 to Rs 30,000 for delivering one consignment. [@1] Crime Branch
ASP Jitendra Singh said more weed smuggling cases are reported on the routes
from Odisha to Nagpur and Raipur, with smugglers travelling in general coaches.
[@1] Weed is available in Odisha for around Rs 2,000 to Rs 5,000 per kg, while
its price in Madhya Pradesh can reach Rs 20,000 per kg. [@1]
