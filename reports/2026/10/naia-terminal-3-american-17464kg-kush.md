A 62-year-old American passenger arriving from Bangkok, Thailand, was arrested
at Ninoy Aquino International Airport Terminal 3 in Pasay City on 29 October
2026 after officers found about 17.464 kilograms of suspected kush in his
luggage. [@1,2] The Bureau of Customs led the operation with the Philippine
Drug Enforcement Agency and the NAIA Inter-Agency Drug Interdiction Task Group.
[@1]

X-ray screening flagged suspicious images inside his two checked-in bags, which
a drug-sniffing dog then confirmed at the arrival secondary inspection area.
[@1,2] Inside, officers found 15 vacuum-sealed packages of suspected kush
hidden in food packaging: five packages weighing 5,122 grams in the first bag
and ten weighing 12,342 grams in the second. [@1]

The haul was valued at PHP 17,464,000 under the standard drug price. [@2] The
seized drugs were turned over to the PDEA, and the American was arrested by
aviation security personnel for legal proceedings. [@1,2]

## Officials respond

BOC-NAIA district collector Yasmin Mapa said inspections and surveillance at
the airport will keep intensifying with partner agencies so smuggled drugs are
intercepted and those responsible held accountable. [@1] Customs Commissioner
Ariel Nepomuceno commended the BOC, PDEA and task group for their prompt joint
action. [@1]
