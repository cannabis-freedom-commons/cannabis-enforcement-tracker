Military Police of Minas Gerais officers arrested a 23-year-old man for drug
trafficking on the night of 31 October 2026 in the Belvedere neighbourhood of
Ouro Branco. [@1] The officers were on preventive patrol in an area already
subject to trafficking complaints when they saw three men acting suspiciously
and approached them. [@1,3] During the search, officers found a skank-like portion
on the man, identified by the initials J.I.P.S., aged 23. [@1,3] He was arrested
and taken to the Civil Police together with the drugs, a precision scale and a
seized mobile phone. [@1,3]

Near the group, police also found a precision scale, zip-lock packaging and
wrappers bearing traces of a marijuana-like substance. [@1] Continued searches
recovered more skank portions, a marijuana tablet, a hashish tablet and three
pills described as LSD-like. [@2,3] Police treated the seized materials and the
circumstances as drug trafficking, ordering the arrest on that basis. [@1]
Police described the action as part of preventive patrols and approaches in
places with repeated complaints about illegal activity in Ouro Branco. [@1]
