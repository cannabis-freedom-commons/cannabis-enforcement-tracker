Shahrasti Model Thana police arrested 27-year-old Tarek in Shahrasti, Chandpur,
after recovering 30 grams of ganja from his possession. [@1] The raid took place
on 31 October 2026 in the Harairpar Chowkidar Bari area of Suchipara Uttar Union
under the supervision of Officer-in-Charge Mir Mahbubur Rahman and led by
Sub-Inspector Nizam Uddin. [@1] Officers detained Tarek in a bedroom of a rented
house described as owned by Fakhrul and rented by Shahab Uddin. [@1] Police
registered a case under section 36(1), table 19(a) of the Narcotics Control Act,
2018, recorded as FIR No. 1 dated 1 November 2026, and sent Tarek to court. [@1]

The report describes the seized drug as 30 grams of ganja, the Bengali term for
cannabis. [@1] The same article reports two other arrests in separate
non-cannabis matters, one involving a recovered stolen motorcycle and one
suspect held for investigation in a theft case. [@1] Police said all three
detained people were produced before the court under police guard. [@1]
