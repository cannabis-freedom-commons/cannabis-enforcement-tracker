Customs officers found about 6 kilograms of suspected cannabis buds concealed in the carry-on
suitcase of a 43-year-old mainland man who arrived from Manila on 6 October 2026, and arrested
him. [@1,2] Investigators then arrested a 52-year-old mainland man who had arrived from Bangkok the
same day and was suspected of being connected with the case. [@1,2] Officers also found 88 sticks
of duty-not-paid cigarettes in the second man's carry-on baggage. [@1,2] Customs estimated the
buds' market value at about HK$1.1 million. [@1,2]

The two men were jointly charged with one count of trafficking in a dangerous drug, while the
second man was additionally charged with possession of dutiable goods and failing to declare to
Customs; the case was to go before West Kowloon Magistrates' Courts on 8 October. [@1,2]
Trafficking carries a maximum penalty of a HK$5 million fine and life imprisonment, and the
cigarette offences carry up to a HK$2 million fine and seven years in prison. [@1,2]
