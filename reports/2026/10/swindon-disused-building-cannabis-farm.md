Police are looking for the person who ran a cannabis farm found inside a disused
building. [@1] The building was near Swindon. [@1] Officers from Thames Valley
Police's South Oxon and Vale of White Horse division discovered the grow on
Wednesday 28 October, during a busy day for the Faringdon Neighbourhood Team.
[@1] Police said enquiries were now ongoing to identify the person behind the
grow, described as a 'budding gardener'. [@2]

The discovery came after officers arrested a man on suspicion of possession with
intent to supply Class A drugs earlier that day. [@1] He has since been charged
and remanded to appear in court, and officers also seized his car. [@1]

Thames Valley Police said drug dealing 'has no place in our communities' and
that officers would continue to pursue those involved and take positive action
wherever possible. [@1] The force asked residents to report anything unusual or
suspicious, saying the information they provide could be exactly what officers
need to take action. [@1]
