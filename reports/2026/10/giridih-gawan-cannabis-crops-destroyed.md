After receiving information that cannabis was being grown in the remote
Gawan-block villages of Odpodo and Rajokhar in Giridih district, police carried
out an eradication action there. [@1] Odpodo and Rajokhar are remote villages
within Gawan block, placing the operation in a hard-to-reach part of Giridih
district in Jharkhand. [@1] The police team that went to both villages was led
by Gawan station in-charge Jaiprakash Kumar. [@1]

Police said the information was that cannabis cultivation was being carried out
secretly in remote parts of the block. [@1] In the fields, officers destroyed
the standing cannabis plants, ending the growing crop rather than seizing
harvested stocks. [@1]

Kumar said the campaign against illegal cultivation of narcotic substances and
their trade in the area would continue. [@1] He said people involved in such
activity would be identified and then face action in accordance with the law.
[@1] Ganja is the Hindi term used in this account for cannabis, so the destroyed
crop was cannabis plants under cultivation. [@1]
