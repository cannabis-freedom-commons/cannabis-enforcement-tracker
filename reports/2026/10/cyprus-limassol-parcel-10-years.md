A Limassol criminal court sentenced a 28-year-old man to 10 years in prison
on 21 October 2026 after he admitted possessing with intent to supply about
8 kg of cannabis and nearly 2 kg of cocaine in a parcel imported from Greece.
[@1,2]

The anti-drug trafficking police unit YKAN acted on a tip-off about the
parcel, which was held at a courier company's warehouse in Limassol. [@1,2]
In a controlled delivery the man presented himself as the recipient and was
arrested in the act as he took the parcel; laboratory tests showed the
cocaine was of particularly high purity. [@1,2]

The court said he had acted as a go-between, moving the drugs on a third
party's instructions for payment, and that his role in an organised
trafficking operation and the quantities made the offences serious. [@1,2] It
weighed his guilty plea, his personal and family circumstances and his efforts
to overcome addiction, along with a previous conviction for possessing a
Class B controlled drug and smoking cannabis, for which he had been fined.
[@1,2] The sentences run concurrently, and proceedings against his co-defendant
were set for a later date. [@1,2]
