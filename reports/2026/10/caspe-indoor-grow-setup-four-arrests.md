Caspe Local Police detained four people for illegally tapping electricity and
water at an abandoned farm whose installations were prepared for indoor
marijuana cultivation. [@1] The intervention took place on Tuesday 27 October
after officers received a citizen call warning of a possible break-in inside the
property. [@1] On arrival, officers caught two people unloading material while
two others had hidden inside the farmhouse on the property. [@1]

After ruling out the reported break-in offence, local officers alerted a Guardia
Civil patrol that took over the case, and the four Albanian nationals were
detained on suspicion of public-health offences and fraud over electricity and
water. [@1] Inspectors found several areas in an advanced state of preparation
for a possible indoor marijuana plantation, with rooms lined in thermal
insulation and fitted with specialist grow equipment. [@1] The equipment
included water tanks with pumps, industrial air conditioners, carbon odour
filters, thermohygrometers, LED lighting with supports, digital ballasts,
flexible tubing, growing substrate, plant pots and other work material for
supplying the plantation. [@1]

Police found that the four detainees were sleeping at the site and could not
document ownership of the farm. [@1] The detainees and the case file were placed
at the disposal of the judicial authorities. [@1]
