Garhi police in Jind arrested a woman for allegedly supplying intoxicants to
smugglers. [@1] The woman was identified as Taro Devi, a resident of Dhamtan
village. [@2] Her name emerged during questioning of men caught days earlier
with 1 kg 418 g of ganja. [@1]

Police recovered a total of 1 kg 418 g of ganja from the two men. [@3] The
driver was found with 510 g of ganja. [@3] The second man was found with 908 g
of ganja. [@3] Both men told police they had bought the intoxicant from Taro
Devi of Dhamtan village. [@2] Devi has not disclosed the identity of the person
who supplied the intoxicant to her. [@2]

Her husband died about 10 years ago, after which she left her marital home,
returned to Dhamtan Sahib, and has no children. [@2] She already faces two theft
cases and one NDPS Act case. [@2] Police said the theft cases are at Garhi and
Gohana stations and the NDPS case is at Siwan station in Kaithal district. [@2]
No convictions in those earlier cases were reported. [@2]
