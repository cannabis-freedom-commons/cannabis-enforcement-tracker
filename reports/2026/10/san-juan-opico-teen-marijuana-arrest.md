Police officers and soldiers intervened with 17-year-old Allán R. during a
surveillance patrol in San Juan Opico. [@1] The procedure occurred in colonia
Bendición de Dios in cantón Sitio del Niño. [@1] The Armed Forces located the
boy during the patrol and then handed him to the National Civil Police. [@2] He
will be referred for unlawful drug trafficking and his situation will be handled
under the rules for adolescents in conflict with criminal law. [@1]

He was carrying several portions of marijuana inside a disposable food
container. [@1] A second account describes the seizure as four portions of
marijuana in the San Juan Opico district. [@2] The first account said police did
not specify the total weight seized or the exact number of wrappers found. [@1]

Police presented the case as one of three suspected drug-trafficking captures
announced in different parts of the country. [@1] The three procedures were
reported as part of operations carried out in La Libertad and La Unión. [@1]
Salvadoran law provides six to 10 years in prison plus fines for possessing,
transporting or selling drugs for trafficking, with courts deciding
responsibility and any sentence. [@1]
