Mexico City officers executed a court-ordered search at a property on Calle
Capri in the Lomas Estrella neighbourhood of Iztapalapa said to be used for
selling and distributing narcotics. [@1] Officers detained two women aged 40 and
22 and a man aged 50 during the intervention. [@1] The three detainees were
placed at the disposal of the Public Prosecutor's Office, which will determine
their legal situation, and the property was sealed and left under police guard
while investigations continue. [@1]

During the search, officers seized 92 small plastic bags, a transparent bag,
three glass jars and a bag holding approximately 500 grams of loose dried green
plant with characteristics similar to marijuana, plus four foil-wrapped bags
with the same plant. [@1] Officers also seized two long firearms with two metal
magazines loaded with five rounds. [@1] Officers also seized capsules, packets
and bags of white solid material with cocaine-like characteristics and
crystalline solid material similar to methamphetamine, which are excluded from
the cannabis figures. [@1] Officers also seized four mobile phones, a
desktop-type CPU computer and a red vehicle. [@1]

The operation involved Mexico City SSC officers in coordination with the FGJ,
the Navy and the National Guard. [@1] Authorities said the deployment was
carried out without violence and in strict adherence to police action,
use-of-force and human-rights protocols. [@1] Milenio confirmed the same Lomas
Estrella search left three people detained and about 500 grams of possible
marijuana seized. [@2]
