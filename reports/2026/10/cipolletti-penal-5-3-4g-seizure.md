Provincial prison officers intercepted a package of cannabis that had been
thrown from outside into Execution Establishment No. 5 in Cipolletti during a
night perimeter patrol. [@2] The package was found in the outer bathroom area of
Pavilion A, at cell 4. [@1] Neither account names a suspect or reports an arrest
in the incident. [@1]

Inside the wrapper officers found a stone and several packets, including a clear
packet holding a powdered-juice sachet and two other wrappers holding plant
material with a strong smell. [@1] The Federal Prosecutor's Office for Initial
Attention ordered the seizure and screening tests after the find. [@1] A
spray-reagent field test was positive for cannabis. [@1] The official weighing
put the seized cannabis at 3.4 grams. [@1]

The account says someone approached the prison on Rimmele street and threw a
small envelope containing marijuana inside, expecting it to go unnoticed except
by whoever was waiting for it. [@1] The discovery came during a preventive round
that began at 9 p.m. covering the outer perimeter of Pavilions A, B, C and D and
the pre-entry cell area. [@2] Perimeter controls and preventive rounds are
described as work to stop drugs and other unauthorised items entering detention
units. [@2]
