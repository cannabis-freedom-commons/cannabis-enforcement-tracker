Four people were arrested after drugs and weapons were seized in dawn raids on
three properties in an East Hampshire town. [@1] East Hampshire Police said
officers searched two addresses and a business on Chalet Hill and Pine View in
Bordon. [@1] The simultaneous warrants were executed at about 5.45am on Monday 5
October 2026. [@1]

Officers found and seized Class B drugs, suspected Class A drugs, cannabis vapes
and drug-related paraphernalia. [@1] A lock knife and suspected illegal
cigarettes were also found, as well as an air weapon. [@1] A 46-year-old man and
a 28-year-old man from Bordon were arrested on suspicion of possession with
intent to supply cannabis, a Class B drug. [@1] A 59-year-old woman and a
25-year-old man from Bordon were arrested on suspicion of possession with intent
to supply cannabis resin and of acquiring, using or possessing criminal
property. [@1]

All four were bailed with conditions until 5 January while the investigation
continues. [@1] PC Ben Godwin, the dedicated neighbourhood officer for Bordon
and Whitehill, said: “This offending will not be tolerated in East Hampshire and
anyone committing drug related crime in our communities will be pursued and
dealt with robustly.” [@1] He asked residents to report suspicious behaviour or
suspected drug activity to police. [@1]
