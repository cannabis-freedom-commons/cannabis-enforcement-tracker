Begusarai police registered 8 drug cases during October 2026 and arrested 11
accused, who were sent to judicial custody. [@1] District Magistrate Richi
Pandey chaired a district-level Narco Coordination Centre (NCORD) review meeting
on Friday to review the drive. [@1] Superintendent of Police Manish and
officials of various departments took part in the meeting. [@1]

The October action seized 8 kg 656 g of ganja. [@1] The same action also seized
291.38 g of smack, 177.44 g of heroin and 7 litres of banned cough syrup, which
are not counted as cannabis. [@1] The meeting discussed curbing the illegal drug
trade in the district, breaking trafficking supply chains and protecting young
people from drugs. [@1]

The District Magistrate directed officials to share drug-trafficking
intelligence immediately with concerned departments so action could be taken in
time. [@1] He directed identification of areas at risk of illegal opium and
ganja cultivation and destruction of such cultivation. [@1] He stressed linking
farmers to alternative crops and government livelihood schemes. [@1] The meeting
directed awareness campaigns on the harms of drugs and the legal provisions of
the NDPS Act in schools, colleges and coaching institutes. [@1] It called for
regular review of major and inter-state trafficking cases and time-bound
investigation and testimony in pending cases. [@1] The District Magistrate
directed preparation of a database of identified drug peddlers and smugglers for
sharing with enforcement agencies, and a proposal to provide modern testing
equipment. [@1] He called for wide publicity of the Nasha Mukt Bharat helpline
number 14446. [@1] The Begusarai Sub-Divisional Officer was directed to run
regular checking drives around the railway station. [@1]
