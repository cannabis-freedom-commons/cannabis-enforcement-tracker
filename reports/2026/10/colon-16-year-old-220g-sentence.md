A 16-year-old girl received seven years of internment for unlawful drug
trafficking linked to storing and distributing methamphetamine and marijuana in
Colón district in La Libertad Oeste. [@1] The First Juvenile Court of San
Salvador imposed the internment measure after examining the evidence presented
in the case. [@1] Investigators found she used a house in Colón to keep and
distribute unlawful substances, and officers captured her in flagrante during a
raid last June. [@1] Prosecutors did not publish her identity, withholding it
under protection rules for minors facing trial. [@1] A second Salvadoran account
confirms that a 16-year-old was sentenced to seven years of internment for
unlawful trafficking. [@2]

During the procedure authorities seized 220.5 grams of marijuana with an
estimated value of USD 778.36. [@1] They also seized 2,695.5 grams of
methamphetamine valued at USD 94,342.50, a non-cannabis amount excluded from the
cannabis figures. [@1] The prosecution put the total commercial value of the
confiscated drugs at USD 95,120.86, most of it from the methamphetamine. [@1]
The same Salvadoran account reports she was captured in June in Colón with 220.5
grams of marijuana and 2,695.5 grams of methamphetamine at a combined value of
$95,120.86. [@2]

The official account did not say whether other people were investigated nor
where the girl will serve the measure. [@1] No exact June raid date was
published, and the case was handled in San Salvador juvenile jurisdiction as
part of anti-trafficking action in La Libertad. [@1]
