A 55-year-old Malaysian chef was arrested in Mauritius on 23 October 2026 after
authorities allegedly found 17.88 kg of suspected cannabis, worth RM2.3 million
(26.88 million Mauritian rupees), hidden in his luggage. [@1] Police detained
him at the Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam International Airport after he arrived on a
flight from Dubai. [@1,2]

The drugs were packed into 17 compressed bags concealed in the luggage. [@1]
Police also seized the suspect's mobile phone for forensic analysis, along with
8,150 Mauritian rupees (RM697) and Malaysian and Thai currency. [@1]

Hoping to catch the intended recipient, officers replaced the confiscated drugs
with dummy packages and set up a sting, but nobody came to pick them up. [@1]
The suspect was still being held by police and had been provisionally charged
with importing dangerous drugs for distribution. [@1]
