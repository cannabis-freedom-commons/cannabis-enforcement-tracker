Galachipa police in Patuakhali arrested 28-year-old Jesmin Begum during an
anti-drug drive after recovering 500 grams of ganja with her. [@1] The operation
took place at about 6:25 pm on Saturday, 31 October 2026, in the Shyamlabagh
area of Ward No. 2 in Galachipa municipality in Patuakhali district. [@1]
Officers from Galachipa police station raided the house of 51-year-old Fazilat
Begum on the north side of the Shyamlabagh High School playground. [@1] Jesmin
Begum was arrested in that house with the 500 grams of ganja, and police
described her as a resident of the same area. [@1]

Police named her husband as Md. Mizanur Rahman and her father as Md. Dulal
Bepari. [@1] Officers filed a case over the incident at Galachipa police station
on 1 November 2026. [@1] Police said the case was brought under section 36(1),
Table 19(a)/41 of the Narcotics Control Act, 2018. [@1] Police added that
anti-drug drives would continue in the area. [@1]
