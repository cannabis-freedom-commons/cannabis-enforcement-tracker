The NDLEA arrested 62-year-old Anetor Gloria Ubong, a Nigerian-American realtor
living in Richmond, Texas, who holds Nigerian and US passports, after she
arrived in Lagos aboard a United Airlines flight from Houston on Wednesday 21
October. [@1,2,3] A search of her two checked-in suitcases found 9.45 kg of
high-grade cannabis, known as loud, in 18 parcels hidden inside a box wrapped as
a gift. [@1,2,3]

Ubong reportedly told questioners that her daughter and the daughter's future
brother-in-law had delivered the box to her US home for onward delivery in
Nigeria to her daughter's fiance, identified as Jibola. [@1,2,3] The agency did
not say whether the daughter, the prospective brother-in-law or the intended
recipient had been arrested or questioned. [@1]

The 18 parcels were found during routine screening and a physical search of her luggage. [@3] The agency said the parcels were wrapped and hidden among her personal items to evade detection. [@3] The NDLEA said its investigation was continuing into the full chain of those allegedly involved in the shipment, including the intended recipients in Nigeria. [@3] She remained in NDLEA custody pending further investigation and possible prosecution. [@3]
