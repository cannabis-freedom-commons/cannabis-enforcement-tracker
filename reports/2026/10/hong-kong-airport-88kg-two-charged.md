Hong Kong Customs detected two drug trafficking cases at the airport on 16
October 2026, seizing about 8.8 kg of suspected cannabis buds and 220
duty-unpaid cigarettes. [@1]

Officers found about 3.3 kg of buds and the cigarettes in the carry-on baggage
of a 43-year-old mainland woman during an anti-narcotics operation. [@1] In
the second case, a 41-year-old mainland man arriving from Bangkok on 15
October was found with about 5.5 kg of buds in his carry-on. [@1]

The woman was charged with trafficking in a dangerous drug and possession of
dutiable goods, and the man with trafficking; both cases were to go before
West Kowloon Magistrates' Courts on 17 October. [@1]
