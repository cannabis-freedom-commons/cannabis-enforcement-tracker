A traffic stop on Interstate 40 in Johnston County led to the arrest of two
people. [@1] The arrests occurred on 28 October 2026, just east of the NC 210
interchange. [@1] According to the Johnston County Sheriff’s Office, a deputy
stopped the vehicle and a K-9 alerted to the presence of suspected narcotics.
[@1] A subsequent search uncovered 37.1 grams (0.0371 kg) of suspected
marijuana, along with additional suspected controlled substances. [@1] The other
substances were sent to the State Crime Laboratory for analysis. [@1]

Deputies also recovered a Glock 9mm handgun that had been reported stolen to the
Durham Police Department. [@1] The two occupants were identified as 45-year-old
Rashod Maurice Richmond of Henderson and 20-year-old Mea Alana Dadamo of
Roxboro. [@1] Richmond was charged with possession with intent to manufacture,
sell or deliver marijuana, possession of a stolen firearm, and possession of a
firearm by a felon. [@1] His bond was set at $100,000. [@1] Dadamo was charged
with possession with intent to manufacture, sell or deliver marijuana and
possession of a stolen firearm. [@1] Her bond was set at $50,000. [@1]

Authorities said additional charges were possible depending on the test results.
[@1] The investigation remained ongoing pending the results of laboratory
testing. [@1]
