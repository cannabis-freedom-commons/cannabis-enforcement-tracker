Special Response Battalion (SRB)-9 arrested 25-year-old Md Alam Mia with 32 kg
of ganja in Nabiganj, Habiganj. [@1,3] The operation took place at a checkpoint on
the Dhaka-Sylhet highway in Debpara Union at about 3:55 a.m. on Friday 30
October. [@1,3] Alam Mia is the son of the late Touhid Mia from Meherpur village
in Madhabpur upazila. [@1,3] Officers signalled the suspect black Noah vehicle to
stop, and detained the man after he got out and tried to flee. [@1,3]

Officers recovered 32 kg of ganja from two plastic sacks kept between the
vehicle's seats after he admitted the cannabis was in his custody. [@1,3,4] The
cannabis was wrapped in blue polythene and khaki scotch tape. [@2] Officers also
seized the black Noah vehicle used to carry the cannabis. [@1,3]

The Shayestaganj camp team set up the checkpoint after a late-night tip that
traders were moving drugs from Madhabpur toward Sylhet in a black Noah. [@1] In
initial questioning he said he had collected the ganja from a border area of
Habiganj to sell. [@1,3] SRB-9 processed a case under the Narcotics Control Act,
2018, and handed him and the seized items to Nabiganj police station. [@1,3] SRB-9
said its intelligence work and operations continue to implement the
zero-tolerance policy against drugs. [@1]
