Police arrested and jointly charged Jupul Stevenson, Osie George and Zendie
Bandnock on 17 October 2026. [@1] Stevenson, aged 23, and George, aged 29, are
farmers from Dasent Cottage, while Bandnock, aged 24, is a delivery clerk from
Old Montrose. [@1] The three were jointly charged with possession of a
controlled drug with intent to supply, drug trafficking and importation of a
controlled drug. [@1] They appeared before the Serious Offences Court on 19
October 2026 and all pleaded not guilty. [@1] The court granted each man bail of
$20,000.00 ECC with one surety. [@1]

Police alleged the men had 80,812 grammes (80.812 kg) of cannabis resin in their
possession with intent to supply it to another person. [@1] They were also
charged with possessing the same quantity of cannabis resin for drug trafficking
and importation. [@1]

The alleged offences were committed in Largo Height on 15 October 2026. [@1] As
bail conditions, they must report to the Central Police Station every Tuesday
between 6am and 6pm. [@1] They were also ordered to surrender all travel
documents, with stop notices placed at all ports of entry and exit. [@1] The
matters were adjourned to 19 November 2026. [@1]
