The deputy said she smelled marijuana coming from the vehicle. [@2] The deputy
searched the SUV before it was towed. [@2] During the search, the deputy
allegedly found 118 grams of marijuana, a digital scale and 14 Pennsylvania
identification cards with the same license number, according to court documents.

Prosecutors charged Franko with delivery of a controlled substance and with
possessing five or more forged identifications with intent to distribute. [@2]
The charges also included misdemeanor counts of speeding by 20 to 25 miles per
hour, driving while revoked and unlawful possession of drug paraphernalia, and
an infraction for failing to wear a seat belt. [@2] Franko pleaded not guilty in
court on 30 October. [@2] He was being held in the Moniteau County Jail on a
$10,000 bond. [@2]

Franko allegedly told the deputy he had picked someone up in Warrensburg and
that they were heading to Columbia. [@2]
