Investigating gardaí in Swords searched two domestic residences on Saturday 31
October 2026. [@1] The searches were raids on two homes in Swords, a town in
north Dublin, carried out on Halloween, 31 October. [@1]

Officers discovered and seized suspected cannabis and cocaine during the
searches. [@1] In their quoted statement, gardaí said they discovered and seized
€16,200 worth of suspected cannabis and cocaine. [@1] The seizure was also
described as cannabis and cocaine worth over €16,000. [@1] Because the published
value covers suspected cannabis and cocaine together, no cannabis-only weight,
plant count or value can be recorded for this mixed seizure. [@1]

A hatchet was also seized alongside the drugs during the same searches. [@1] A
published photograph captioned on the page shows the seized drugs and the
hatchet together. [@1] The seized drugs will be sent to Forensic Science Ireland
for analysis. [@1] In the quoted statement, gardaí in Swords added that they are
committed to keeping people safe. [@1]
