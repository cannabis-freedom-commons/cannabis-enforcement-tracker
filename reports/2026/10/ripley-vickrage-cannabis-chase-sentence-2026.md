Derby Crown Court jailed Jack Vickrage for 14 months on 27 October 2026. [@2]
The 28-year-old admitted dangerous driving, driving while disqualified,
drug-driving and cannabis possession. [@1] He was charged with dangerous
driving, driving while disqualified, drug-driving and two counts of cannabis
possession. [@2] The 14 months comprised 12 months for dangerous driving plus
two months for breaching a suspended sentence, with concurrent terms for
disqualified and drug-influence driving. [@3] The sentencing judge called
dangerous driving the most serious offence. [@3] He was also banned from driving
for 28 months and must pass an extended re-test before driving again. [@3]

Officers first spotted the disqualified Vickrage driving a black Ford Transit
van on Nottingham Road in Ripley just before 2pm on 17 July. [@2] He refused to
stop and sped without indicating into Porterhouse Road in the built-up area.
[@2] Trapped in the Stenson Court dead end, he reversed at speed into wooden
gates and fencing. [@2] He then appeared to drive forward toward the police car
blocking the van. [@2] Officers arrested him at the scene after a roadside drugs
test indicated cannabis. [@3] Officers found three exhibits of green herbal
substances in his car. [@3] While on bail, he was stopped again on 2 October
driving in Horsley Woodhouse. [@3] He told officers he had been waiting for
police and had cannabis in the vehicle. [@3]

On 3 November Derbyshire police uploaded a TikTok showing officers chasing the
black van through residential streets to a GTA6 soundtrack song. [@1] The edit
added GTA6 interface, a three-star wanted level and a minimap tracking the
escape until police intercepted him. [@1] Police captioned that he could have
waited weeks for GTA6 but will now spend release day in prison. [@1] Police said
it happened in a built-up area with other vehicles and pedestrians while he was
under the influence of illegal drugs. [@2]
