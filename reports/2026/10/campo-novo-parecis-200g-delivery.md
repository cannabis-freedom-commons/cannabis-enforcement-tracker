A man was arrested for drug trafficking and involvement with a criminal
organisation in the Jardim das Palmeiras neighbourhood of Campo Novo do Parecis,
402 km from Cuiabá, on the afternoon of 29 October 2026. [@1] Military Police
cavalry officers approached him on Avenida Amazonas, near Escola Padre Arlindo
Inácio de Oliveira, and found 11 portions of cannabis hidden in a plastic-film
wrapper during the search. [@1]

Police said they seized 200 grams of cannabis and 13 portions of the drug, as
well as a precision scale and money. [@1] He told officers there was more
cannabis at his home and allowed them to enter. [@1] Inside, they found a piece
of about 200 grams of cannabis, other portions and a precision scale. [@1]
Officers also seized R$ 100, a mobile phone, plastic packaging, the motorcycle
used for deliveries and a helmet. [@1]

He said he sold drugs in the region under the orders of the Comando Vermelho
criminal faction, negotiated sales through messaging apps and used a red Honda
CG 150 motorcycle for deliveries. [@1] He told officers he bought the drugs from
a man nicknamed 'Aladim'. [@1] He was taken to the police station for further
procedures. [@1]
