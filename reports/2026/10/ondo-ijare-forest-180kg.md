NDLEA operatives raided cannabis farms in Ijare forest, Ondo State, on Sunday 25
October 2026. [@1,4,5] Four suspects were arrested during the raid, among them
Bassey Okon, 24, Abraham Edet, 20, and Oto Udo, 24. [@1] A fourth suspect was
held but not named. [@2] Officers recovered 16 bags of processed cannabis
weighing 180 kg from the farms, the agency said. [@2,3,4,5] The agency reported no
plant count, no farm area and no value for the 180 kg. [@1]

NDLEA said the raid followed credible intelligence. [@2] The agency announced
the raid in a statement issued by its spokesperson, Femi Babafemi. [@1] The
statement was published on 1 November 2026, a week after the raid. [@2] It
reported drug seizures and arrests across Kano, Kaduna, Ondo, Edo and Sokoto
states in the same week. [@1] NDLEA chairman Brig. Gen. Mohamed Buba Marwa (Rtd)
commended the officers of its Ondo command among those credited with the week's
arrests and seizures. [@2]
