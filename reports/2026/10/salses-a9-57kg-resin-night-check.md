On the night of 15 to 16 October 2026, a large road-checkpoint operation took
place on the A9 motorway in the France-to-Spain direction. [@1] From 11 pm, the
motorway was blocked and all vehicles were redirected to the Salses-le-Chateau
rest area. [@1] The departmental flow-control squadron led military personnel, a
dog team and other state services including the biodiversity office, tax offices
and Urssaf. [@1]

Officers checked 250 vehicles carrying 320 people at the rest area. [@1]
Officers arrested one person found with 5.7 kg of cannabis resin. [@1] The unit
commander said the 5.7 kg of cannabis resin was travelling from Toulouse toward
Perpignan. [@2] Five other people tested positive for drugs and two for alcohol.
[@1] Three more people were caught holding about five to ten grams of drugs
each. [@1]

Officers also found two wanted people in their files, including one sought under
a court judgment with six months in prison to serve. [@2] Traffic in the
France-to-Spain direction was diverted for three hours without interruption.
[@2]
