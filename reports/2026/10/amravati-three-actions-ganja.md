Amravati’s crime branch carried out a three-site drive against illegal narcotics
sales on Police Commissioner Rakesh Ola’s orders, arresting three people. [@1]
The first action took place on 27 October near Madina Hotel on the
Navsari–Lalkhadi Ring Road, where officers detained 22-year-old Ahsan Khan
Rovangar Khan and seized mephedrone powder, electronic scales and a Honda Dio
scooter. [@1] Police registered an NDPS Act case at Nagpuri Gate Police Station.
[@1]

In the second action, officers caught 23-year-old Niyaz Khan Nawab Khan of Taj
Nagar-1 as he came to sell cannabis, seizing 4 kilograms 820 grams and a bag
with a reported combined value of Rs 241,500. [@1] Gadge Nagar Police Station
registered a case over that seizure. [@1] At Oxygen Park, officers took
44-year-old Mukesh Dnyaneshwarrao Palskar of Mahadev Khori into custody with 5
kilograms 5 grams of cannabis, valued at Rs 250,000. [@1] Police registered an
NDPS Act case at Frezarpura Police Station. [@1]

Across all three actions, seized mephedrone, cannabis and other items had a
combined value of Rs 811,000. [@1]
