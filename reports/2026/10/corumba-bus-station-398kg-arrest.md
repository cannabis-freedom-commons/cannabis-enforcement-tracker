Military police arrested a man for drug trafficking near the bus station in
Corumbá on the night of Friday 30 October 2026, during the 32nd edition of
Operação Força Total (Operation Total Force). [@3] The man was carrying 3.98 kg
of cannabis in a backpack. [@1] He was from Tangará da Serra, in Mato Grosso.
[@2] The 6th Military Police Battalion said officers stopped him after he
changed direction and quickened his pace when the patrol car came near. [@1]

He told police that a Colombian man had handed him the drug at a guesthouse in
Corumbá on the morning of 29 October, the day before his arrest. [@2] He said he
had come to the city to transport the cannabis. [@1] He also said members of a
criminal organisation had threatened to kill him. [@1]

Police found he was wearing an electronic ankle monitor because of a
drug-trafficking case in Mato Grosso. [@1] He was taken with the drug to the
Civil Police station, and the case was to go to the Federal Police. [@1]
