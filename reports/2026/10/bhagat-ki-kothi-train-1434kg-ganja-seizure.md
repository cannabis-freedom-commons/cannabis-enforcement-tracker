At Bhagat Ki Kothi station, the Railway Protection Force recovered 14.340 kg of
ganja from the Visakhapatnam–Bhagat Ki Kothi train under Operation Narcos. [@1]
The force put the estimated value of the ganja at 7.17 lakh rupees. [@1]

The ganja was in an unclaimed blue trolley bag under seat 39 in AC coach A-1,
holding seven plastic packets found when the bag was opened in front of
witnesses. [@2] The recovery was made on the night of 30 October after train
number 18573 arrived at platform number 2. [@2]

After the recovery, the RPF handed the seized ganja to Government Railway Police
Jodhpur for further legal action. [@2] Police inspector Mukta Parik is leading
the GRP investigation into the case. [@2] No owner of the bag had been
identified, and police were working to trace the owners. [@2]
