Acting on an informer's tip, Cantt police raided a field in Barkheda Dhekni
village in Guna district on Wednesday night. [@1] Cantt police said an informer
had told them that Pahalwan Singh Yadav had planted cannabis plants among
another crop in his own field. [@2] Officers found a man at the house built on
the field, who identified himself as 39-year-old Pahalwan Singh Yadav of
Barkheda Dhekni. [@2] Police arrested Yadav and registered case number 994/26
against him at Cantt police station under sections 8/20 of the NDPS Act, with
the case taken up for investigation. [@2]

A search of the field found a large number of green cannabis plants growing,
four cut dried plants lying at the spot, and dried cannabis in a plastic
polythene bag. [@2] Officers had the plants uprooted, counted and weighed: 295
green plants and four dried plants, 299 in all, weighing 131.440 kg in total.
[@2] The dried cannabis in the polythene bag weighed 7.040 kg, and police put
the estimated value of the seized cannabis at about 3 lakh rupees. [@2] All the
seized plants and the dried cannabis were formally confiscated. [@2]

The cannabis had been grown intercropped, with 1.31 quintals of cannabis plants
raised amid another standing crop in the field. [@1] The report describes the
accused as a farmer arrested with the 299 plants grown between his other crop.
