On 29 October 2026 the Iasi Court of Appeal finally closed the Vaslui
drug-trafficking case. [@1,3] Vlad-George Gavril must serve four years in prison
for continued trafficking in risk and high-risk drugs. [@2] Stefan-Danut
Mormenchi, sentenced as a repeat offender, must serve six years in prison. [@1]
Dumitru-Cristian Zlatan's initial four-year term was reduced on appeal to three
years and six months after the court counted three material acts instead of
four. [@1] Stefan Brudiu received a two-year prison term suspended under
supervision for two years with an obligation to perform 120 days of unpaid
community work, while minor Crina Badarau received a six-month non-custodial
educational supervision measure. [@1]

Investigators identified cannabis, 3-CMC, 4-CMC, MDMA and amphetamine among the
substances in the case. [@2] From Mormenchi the court ordered confiscation of
6.41 grams of cannabis, 54 tablets containing MDMA, five other MDMA tablets and
powder quantities with MDMA, amphetamine and caffeine. [@1,3] From Zlatan the
court ordered confiscation of 1,000 lei from two transactions in February and
March 2025 in which cannabis and substances containing 4-CMC were sold. [@1,3]

The file was handled by DIICOT's Vaslui Territorial Office with officers of the
Vaslui Organized Crime Combat Service, using surveillance and authorized drug
purchases. [@1] The three custodial defendants keep executable terms of four
years, six years and three years and six months, with the other two keeping the
first-instance measures. [@1]
