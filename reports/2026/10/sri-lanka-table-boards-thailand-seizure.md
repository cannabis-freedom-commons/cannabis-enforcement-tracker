Sri Lanka Customs officers seized 2,428 grams of cannabis valued at Rs 24
million and 12,372 grams of hashish valued at Rs 133 million from packages
imported as a gift from Thailand. [@1,2] The drugs had been carefully concealed
inside what appeared to be parts of wooden tables, and were discovered when
the packages were examined at a Customs warehouse in Peliyagoda. [@1,2]

A 29-year-old suspect from Ragama was taken into Customs custody over the
shipment. [@1,2] Deputy Director of Customs M.K.R. Kumara of the Passenger
Services Division identified the consignment under the Senior Director's
instructions, and officers of the division seized the drugs with the Customs
Narcotics Control Unit. [@1] The suspect and the drugs were expected to be
handed to the Police Narcotic Bureau for further investigation. [@1,2]
