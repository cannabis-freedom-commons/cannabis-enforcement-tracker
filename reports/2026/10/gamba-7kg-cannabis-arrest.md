At about 00:30 on the night of 11-12 October 2026, gendarmes of the mixed
Gendarmerie-Police-Customs post at Gamba on National Road No. 1 stopped a
travel-agency bus travelling from Ngong toward Ngaoundéré during a routine
check. [@1] On board, they detained a suspect who works as an aide-commerçant
and lives at Ngaoundéré-Beka. [@1] Camer.be identifies the detained passenger as
a 21-year-old man. [@2] Camer.be names the Gamba team leader as Adjudant-Chef
Signe Talla. [@2]

Officers found seven kilograms of cannabis, packed as 14 packets of about 0.5 kg
each. [@1] The drugs were stored in cartons hidden under his seat and in the
luggage hold. [@1] Officers also seized 20 packets of shisha charcoal and 21
packets of shisha tobacco from the same hiding places. [@1] During questioning
the suspect said he had obtained the load in Nigeria, travelled via Touroua to
Ngong, and boarded the bus there intending to sell the cargo in Ngaoundéré. [@1]

He was placed in custody at the Gamba gendarmerie post while an investigation
was opened. [@1] Camer.be adds that he is to be presented to the public
prosecutor at the Mayo-Rey lower courts for the next steps. [@2]
