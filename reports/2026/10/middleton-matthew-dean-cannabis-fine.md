Matthew Dean of Mary’s Court, Middleton, appeared at Lynn Magistrates’ Court on
Thursday and pleaded guilty to cultivating cannabis plants. [@1] Police had
attended Dean’s address on September 25 after noticing a strong smell of
cannabis. [@1] Prosecutor Jessica Neequaye said officers who entered the address
found black bags containing cannabis and arrested the 55-year-old Dean. [@1]
After he was taken into custody, Dean admitted buying cannabis seeds online with
the intention of growing them for his own use. [@1]

Magistrates ordered Dean to pay a £392 fine, £85 in court costs and a £157
victim surcharge. [@1] The court also ordered that the drugs be forfeited and
destroyed. [@1]

When interviewed, Dean made a full admission and said he uses large quantities
of the drug. [@1] The court heard Dean has been diagnosed with bipolar disorder
and finds cannabis helps him settle and sleep. [@1] Dean also has chronic back
pain from a work injury as a factory operative and finds the drug helps with
that pain. [@1] His solicitor told the court Dean has reduced but not stopped
his use and hopes to stop fully soon. [@1] Dean has three previous convictions
resulting from nine offences. [@1]
