Bolivia's Special Force for the Fight against Drug Trafficking (FELCN) seized 840 packages
of marijuana near the Ollagüe volcano, in Sud Lípez province, Potosí department, the force
reported on 31 October 2026. [@1,2] The packages were carried in 30 jute sacks. [@1,2]

The force said its officers carried out the interdiction under the "Frontera Segura 2.0"
plan. [@1,2] One person was detained and two vehicles were seized. [@1,2] Field tests on the
substance were positive for marijuana, and the case was handed to the Public Ministry
(Ministerio Público). [@1,2]

The FELCN said the operation formed part of its efforts to strengthen controls in border
areas. [@2] Neither report gave the weight or value of the shipment, named the person
detained, or said whether any charges had been filed. [@1,2]
