The Chinhoyi Magistrates' Court sentenced Aristotle Keith Mabvira, 32, of White City to an effective 30 months in prison for possessing dagga. [@1,2] The court imposed a 36-month term, suspending six months for five years on condition of good behaviour. [@1,2]

The case concerned cannabis found in Mabvira's room on 4 June 2026. [@1,2] Police recovered a transparent bag of dagga, 21 sachets and 57 prepared dagga cigarettes, which weighed 1.055 kilograms in total. [@1,2] Pindula reported that Mabvira pleaded not guilty but failed to produce a permit authorising possession. [@1]
