On 14 October 2026, NDLEA operatives patrolling the Bauchi–Gombe road recovered cannabis from an
abandoned car at Tudun Natsira village in Bauchi State. [@1,2] The load consisted of 291 blocks
weighing 265 kilograms. [@1,2]

The agency announced the find in a wider weekly roundup of seizures and arrests; neither account
names anyone connected to the vehicle or says whether investigators traced the cargo. [@1,2] No
arrest was reported for this recovery.
