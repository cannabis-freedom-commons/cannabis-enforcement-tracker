A 12-hour drug eradication exercise at Wiruni, Upper Berbice River, on Sunday 11 October 2026
led to the discovery and destruction of four cannabis fields measuring approximately seven
acres in total. [@1]

The first cultivation measured approximately two acres and contained about 15,000 cannabis
plants ranging between four and six feet in height, and a makeshift camp containing
approximately 40 pounds of dried cannabis was also discovered there. [@1] The second
cultivation measured approximately one acre and contained about 4,000 ganja plants ranging
between five inches and one foot in height. [@1] The third field measured approximately two
acres and contained about 10,000 cannabis plants between five and six feet tall, and the
fourth measured approximately two acres with about 8,000 plants between six inches and two
feet in height. [@1]

The suspected cannabis plants, dried cannabis and makeshift camp, with a combined estimated
value of $26,832,112, were destroyed by fire at the location. [@1] No arrests were made, and
investigations are ongoing. [@1]

The Guyana Chronicle headline for the same Wiruni operation matches these figures — 37,000
