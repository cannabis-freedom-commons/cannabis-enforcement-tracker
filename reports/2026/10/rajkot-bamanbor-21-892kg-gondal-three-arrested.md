Rajkot city crime branch officers seized 21 kg of ganja that had been hidden
among groundnut and peanut sacks in a truck delivering it to Rajkot from Odisha.
[@1] The truck was stopped by crime branch officers on the Rajkot-Ahmedabad
highway near Bamambor. [@1]

Officers arrested three drug peddlers from Gondal with the 21 kg of ganja. [@1]

The team acted on a tip-off and moved on the Bamanbor village patiya near
Rajkot, where police found the ganja among sacks of groundnut. [@2]

Officers stopped an Ishar truck in front of the Airport police station
checkpoint near Bamambor on the Rajkot-Ahmedabad highway and searched it. [@3]

The search found 21 kg 892 g of ganja, which police valued at about Rs 10.94
lakh. [@3] Police also seized the truck, a mobile phone and the groundnut sacks,
bringing the total value of property seized to over Rs 27 lakh. [@3]

Police named the three arrested men as Farid Bloch, Mehboob Dodhia and Hasmukh
Agravat, residents of Gondal. [@2]

Five people in all were found to be involved in the trafficking network, and the
other two, Javed Makrani and Salman Bloch, remain at large. [@2] The other two
accused, Javed Makrani and Salman Bloch, escaped the police and crime branch
officers are searching for them. [@2] Police took legal custody of the three
arrested men and continued investigating the wider network behind the
consignment. [@2]
