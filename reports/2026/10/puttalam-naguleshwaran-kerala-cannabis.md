Sri Lanka's Police Narcotics Bureau arrested Naguleshwaran, a former member of
the Puttalam Urban Council, on 18 October 2026 after stopping his motorcycle in
Thilladiya and finding Kerala cannabis concealed on it. [@1,2] Officers acted on
a tip-off that he was transporting the cannabis from Thilladiya to Puttalam
town. [@1,2]

Police said the cannabis and the motorcycle would be produced before the
Puttalam Magistrate's Court the next day. [@1,2] Naguleshwaran had also contested
an election as a candidate for the Sri Lanka Muslim Congress. [@1,3] The bureau
said investigations were continuing and that he was being questioned. [@1,2]

The statements gave no weight for the consignment, and no charge or court
outcome was reported. [@1,2] The three accounts carry the same police
information; none adds figures. [@1,2,3]
