Spain's National Police arrested a 30-year-old man accused of running a
small-scale drug sales point in the El Calero neighbourhood of Telde, Gran
Canaria, after neighbours reported the activity. [@1] The arrest was made on 30
October 2026, after several months of surveillance and tailing during which
officers watched apparent exchanges of drugs for money. [@1] The investigation
ran for several months and included numerous surveillance operations and
tailings, during which officers observed apparent exchanges of drugs for money.
[@1]

In a search of the man's home, officers seized 47.90 grams of hashish pollen and
15.60 grams of marijuana, alongside cocaine. [@1] Police also took 260 euros in
cash, three high-end watches and numerous pieces of jewellery. [@1]

The Canary Islands police command said residents had alerted officers to a man
possibly distributing and selling small quantities of drugs in the area. [@1]
During the investigation, officers stopped several buyers and seized drugs from
them, which police said corroborated the evidence. [@1]

The suspect had previous police records, including two earlier arrests in
connection with drug trafficking. [@1] He was handed over to the courts as the
alleged perpetrator of a crime against public health. [@2] Telde Actualidad
named the case Operación Fear and said it was run by the drug unit of the
judicial police brigade at the Telde police station. [@2]
