The Local Crime Branch and MIDC police conducted a joint raid on the field at
Imampur. [@1]

Officers found small and large cannabis plants growing concealed among the
marigold flower crop. [@2] Police seized 44.415 kg of cannabis plants and put
their estimated value at Rs 2,220,750. [@2] The team entered the field with
panchas, independent witnesses, acting under orders from senior officers. [@2]

The confidential tip that Timkare was growing cannabis in the marigold crop was
received on 10 October 2026. [@2] MIDC police station registered Crime No.
910/2026 under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, 1985. [@2]
The accounts place the case amid wider cannabis smuggling into the district from
Telangana and Odisha and earlier cultivation cases in several talukas. [@3]
