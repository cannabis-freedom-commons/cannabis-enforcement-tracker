A child was arrested on suspicion of possessing cannabis at a Halloween house
party in Whittle-le-Woods, near Chorley. [@1] The party was held on the night of
Friday 30 October 2026. [@3] It took place on Moss Lane, and officers closed it
for a breach of the peace. [@2] Sergeant Paul Harrison of Lancashire Police said
there was a smell of cannabis and that some cannabis was seized from an
individual. [@2]

Three children were arrested at the scene. [@1] One of them was held on
suspicion of possessing cannabis. [@1] That child was later released from
custody and referred to the Youth Offending Team for the offence of possession
of cannabis, while another child was referred to the same team for being drunk
and disorderly in a public place. [@2] A third child was de-arrested and taken
home. [@1]

Police said more than 200 children and other attendees were at the party. [@3]
Police said the decision to close it followed reports of fireworks being set off
towards a nearby house. [@2] Ten officers dealt with the gathering while
projectiles were thrown at them. [@1] One child was taken to hospital with
non-serious injuries. [@1] Police appealed on social media for parents to
collect their children. [@1] Police asked for information about anyone who
supplied drugs, alcohol or fireworks to the children. [@2]
