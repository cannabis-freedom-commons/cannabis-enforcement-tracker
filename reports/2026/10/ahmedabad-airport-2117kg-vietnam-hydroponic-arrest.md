Two people were caught with ganja worth Rs 2 crore at Ahmedabad international
airport. [@1] The seizure was made on 17 October after the Air Intelligence Unit
intercepted the male passenger on the basis of passenger profiling. [@3] The
passenger had arrived on flight VJ-1805 from Ho Chi Minh City. [@3] Officers
examining his baggage found seven vacuum-sealed packets containing a suspected
green-coloured substance concealed in a black handbag. [@3]

Seven packets held 2 kg 117 g of ganja. [@1] Preliminary forensic tests in
Ahmedabad indicated active cannabis constituents in all seven packets. [@2] The
haul was described as hydroponic ganja arriving on a Vietnam flight. [@1]

Investigators identified another person who had come to the airport to receive
the passenger. [@3] The second accused had allegedly coordinated the passenger's
travel arrangements at the direction of people based abroad. [@3]

Recovered WhatsApp chats contained communications about the passenger's travel
arrangements and baggage. [@2] Both men were arrested in connection with the
case. [@3] The suspected haul was seized under the NDPS Act. [@3] Further
investigation is under way to establish the roles of those involved and examine
alleged links with persons based overseas. [@3]
