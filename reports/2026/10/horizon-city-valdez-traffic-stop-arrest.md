Horizon City police arrested Miguel Angel Valdez after a traffic stop uncovered marijuana and methamphetamine. [@1,2,3]

Horizon City police arrested 39-year-old Miguel Angel Valdez during a traffic
stop on Wednesday, October 21, in Horizon City, Texas, on charges including
possession of marijuana. [@1] Officers with the department's Tactical
Suppression and Gang Unit stopped Valdez just after 10 a.m. for a traffic
violation. [@1,3] During the contact, officers detected an odor of marijuana
coming from Valdez and the vehicle, which led them to conduct a probable-cause
search. [@1,3]

Valdez was taken to the El Paso County Downtown Jail and was charged with
possession of a controlled substance and possession of marijuana. [@1,3] Police
said officers found suspected marijuana and a suspected crystal-like substance
inside the vehicle's center console. [@1,3] The substances were field-tested and
returned positive results for marijuana and methamphetamine. [@1,3]

El Diario de El Paso confirmed the same arrest, identifying the detainee as
39-year-old Miguel Angel Valdez, who faces charges for possession of a
controlled substance and possession of marijuana. [@2] That outlet reported that
both substances underwent preliminary field tests that returned positive results
for marijuana and methamphetamine. [@2] Police did not report the quantity of
drugs seized, so no cannabis weight is recorded and the methamphetamine
component is excluded from the cannabis figures. [@2]
