Three men convicted in the shooting death of a security guard at an unlicensed
marijuana dispensary in Spring Valley were sentenced to potential life prison
terms. [@1,2] Daveon Hicks, 36, Curry Williams, 35, and Jamal Johnson, 46, were
convicted of murder and other charges for the June 2020 killing of 59-year-old
Kenneth Love II. [@1,2] Love was the security guard who died in the Spring Valley
dispensary shooting. [@1]

All three defendants were sentenced on Friday. [@1] The report was published on
2 November 2026. [@1] Hicks was sentenced to 67 years and four months to life in
state prison, while Williams received 84 years to life and Johnson received 54
years to life. [@1,2] The three convictions arose from the same June 2020 killing.
[@1]

Prosecutors said the defendants were responsible for a string of shootings. [@1,2]
Prosecutors also said the men played roles in a broader scheme to extort
unlicensed dispensaries. [@1,2] The reported extortion scheme targeted unlicensed
dispensaries. [@1]
