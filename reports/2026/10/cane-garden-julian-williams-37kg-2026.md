Police arrested and charged Julian Williams, a 36-year-old labourer of Cane
Garden, on 21 October 2026 with possession of a controlled drug and drug
trafficking. [@1] Williams appeared before the Serious Offences Court on 22
October 2026 and pleaded not guilty to the offences. [@1]

Investigators alleged he had 37,228 grammes of cannabis in his possession with
the intent to supply it to another person. [@1] He was further charged with
having the same 37,228 grammes of cannabis in his possession for the purpose of
drug trafficking. [@1] The offences were committed in Cane Garden on 21 October
2026. [@1]

The court granted him composite bail of $10,000.00 ECC with one surety. [@1] As
a bail condition, he was ordered to report to the Central Police Station every
Tuesday between 8:00 a.m. and 8:00 p.m. [@1] He was also ordered to surrender
all travel documents, with stop notices placed at all ports of entry and exit.
[@1] The matter was adjourned to 23 November 2026. [@1]
