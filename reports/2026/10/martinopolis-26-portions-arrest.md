Military Police arrested a young man in flagrante for drug trafficking on
Saturday 31 October 2026 in Martinópolis, São Paulo, seizing portions of crack
and marijuana. [@1] Officers reached the suspect after stopping a drug user who
had just consumed marijuana at Praça Verde. [@1] When officers arrived at the
suspect's residence in Martinópolis, they found the young man standing in front
of the building. [@1] When he saw the police approach, he tried to enter an open
neighbouring plot with no obstacles, wall or gate, but officers intercepted him
there. [@1]

Officers found three marijuana portions in his pocket together with R$30 in
cash. [@1] Officers then found additional drugs inside a small jar hidden inside
a pair of sneakers left beside the property wall. [@1] In total, officers seized
26 portions of drugs, reported as crack and marijuana combined. [@1] The suspect
was taken to the Civil Police station, where he remained at the disposal of
justice after being arrested in flagrante for drug trafficking. [@1]
