The Thiès Regional Narcotics Brigade seized five packages of cannabis weighing
160.75 kg between Mbaling and Sinthiou Mbadane, in Mbour Department. [@1,2]
Police said the Mbour anti-narcotics unit acted on operational intelligence. [@1,2]

The people targeted fled as officers intercepted the consignment. Investigators
said they were continuing work to identify those involved and trace the
suspected trafficking network, but the reports do not name the people or say
how many fled. [@1,2]

Neither RTS nor Seneweb states when the operation took place. Both published
their reports on 12 October 2026, and Seneweb updated its account the following
day. [@1,2] Both attributed the operation details to police and gave no
independent account from the people who fled. [@1,2]
