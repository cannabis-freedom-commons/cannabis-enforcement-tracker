Ashta police arrested Sameer Khan, 38, after searching a house in Islampura,
Ashta, in Sehore district, Madhya Pradesh. [@1] Officers acted on a tip that a
man was selling ganja from home. [@1] They seized 864 grams of ganja, which was
in a clear plastic bag inside a white polythene bag, and weighed it on an
electronic scale. [@1]

Khan, the son of Salim Khan, is a resident of Islampura, Ashta. [@1] Police
registered a case against him under sections 8 and 20 of the Narcotic Drugs and
Psychotropic Substances Act, India's main drug law, and said the investigation
was continuing. [@1] The operation was led by station house officer Raman Thakur
under the supervision of superintendent of police Sonakshi Saxena, additional
superintendent of police Prashant Chaubey and sub-divisional police officer
Damodar Gupta. [@2]

The Sehore police said they were carrying out sustained action against the
illegal sale of narcotics in the district. [@1] The superintendent's office said
the crackdown on illegal activity would continue. [@2]
