Buenos Aires provincial police seized 575 kg of marijuana plants and buds in two simultaneous raids
in Parada Robles, Exaltación de la Cruz, on 26 October 2026. [@1,2]

The operation, called Sueño Verde, targeted two properties in the Parque Exaltación neighbourhood:
one used for indoor cultivation and the other to store drugs and weapons. [@1,2] Officers found
lighting, ventilation, irrigation and climate-control equipment, and seized 535 kg of plants from
334 plants plus 40 kg of buds in 37 bags. [@1,2,3]

Two men, aged 31 and 45, were arrested. [@1,2,3] Police also seized 35 g of cocaine, a .32 revolver,
cash and cultivation equipment. [@1,2] The investigation began on 22 June 2026. [@1,3]

The operation was carried out by officers of the Superintendence for Illicit Drug Trafficking Investigations of the Buenos Aires Province Police on directives from prosecutor Matías Sebastián Ferreirós. [@3] The two raids were conducted in Barrio Parque Exaltación at kilometre 74 of National Route 8 in Parada Robles, on a luxurious property with cabins and pools. [@3] The criminal case is provisionally classified as possession of narcotics for commercial purposes and is before Judge Julio Andrés Grassi of Guarantees Court No. 2. [@3] Officers also seized 390,250 pesos, 1,400 dollars, two scales, two heat-sealers, a tablet, a notebook, two mobile phones and seven rounds for the unbranded .32 short revolver, with bags, scissors, nylon cuttings and complete indoor-cultivation equipment. [@3]
