Thailand's Customs Department, acting through its Investigation and Suppression
Division, deployed a Customs K-9 detector-dog team to enter and examine parcels
stored in the warehouse of a private company. [@1] During that warehouse
inspection, officers identified one suspicious parcel whose stated destination
was Brazil, recording the examination date as 30 October 2026. [@1]

Upon inspection, officers found a Category 5 narcotic described as cannabis
extract, called yang kancha in Thai, in the form of a dark-brown solid that had
been deliberately hidden inside a book. [@1] Customs reported the total weight
including wrapping material at approximately 523 grams, equivalent to 0.523
kilograms, and assessed the value of the seized extract at 784,500 baht. [@1]

The department said the seizure established an attempt to export Category 5
cannabis extract out of the Kingdom without the required permission. [@1] It
classified that attempted unlicensed export as an offence under the country's
Narcotics Code in conjunction with the Customs Act. [@1]
