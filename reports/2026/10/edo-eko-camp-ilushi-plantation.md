NDLEA operatives supported by soldiers raided Eko Camp in Ilushi, in Esan South East Local Government Area of Edo State, on 28 October 2026. [@1,2,3,4,5,6,8] During the operation they destroyed a cannabis plantation measuring 1.646 hectares, which the agency said had an estimated yield of 4,115.53 kilograms. [@1,2,3,4,6,7,8]

A 50-year-old woman, Christiana Uboi, was arrested at the camp with 301 kilograms of processed cannabis, while another 176 kilograms was recovered during the raid. [@1,2,3,4,5,6,8,9] The two seizures together amount to 477 kilograms of processed cannabis. [@1,2]

The Edo raid was one of several operations across seven states announced by NDLEA's Director of Media and Advocacy, Femi Babafemi, in a statement on 1 November 2026. [@1,2] This report covers only the Eko Camp cannabis raid; the cocaine, ammunition and other-drug seizures in the same roundup are separate events left out here. [@1,2]
