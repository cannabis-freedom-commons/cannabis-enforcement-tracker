Ruston Police Department officers arrested 24-year-old Ja’laen Bonner of Ruston
at 12:15 a.m. on Oct. 28 at South Maple and Saratoga streets. [@1] An officer on
stationary radar saw Bonner driving a gray 2026 Kia about 42 mph in a 25 mph
zone before the vehicle stopped at Saratoga Street. [@1] Bonner told the officer
that his license was suspended and that he had traffic warrants. [@1] Dispatch
said his warrants included a Third Judicial District Court bench warrant for
possession with intent to distribute a Schedule I drug. [@1] Bonner told the
officer that he had a firearm under the driver’s seat and 8 grams of marijuana.
[@1]

After Bonner was handcuffed and agreed to a vehicle search, the officer found a
Glock 19x pistol, a plastic bag containing marijuana and a joint containing
marijuana, with a field weight of 11.1 grams. [@1] Dispatch advised that the
firearm was clear and that Bonner was not prohibited from carrying one. [@1] The
officer cited Bonner for speeding and driving under suspension, then took him to
the Lincoln Parish Detention Center and booked him on a charge of possession of
marijuana. [@1] He was also booked as a fugitive on the Third Judicial District
Court warrant and on two Ruston bench warrants for failure to register a vehicle
and no liability insurance. [@1]

The account comes from an arrest affidavit released as law-enforcement public
information. [@1] Bonner has not been convicted and is presumed innocent until
proven guilty in a court of law. [@1]
