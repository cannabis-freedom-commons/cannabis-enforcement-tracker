Two people were arrested in Saguenay and released with a promise to appear. [@1]
The raids took place on Friday 30 October 2026. [@2] The Sûreté du Québec, the
provincial police, said the two could face charges of cocaine trafficking and
handling stolen goods. [@2]

The operation was run jointly by the Saguenay regional mixed squad and the city
police's urban violence intervention group. [@1] About 40 officers carried out
ten searches at five residences and in five vehicles. [@1] They seized 16 kg of
cocaine and more than $335,000 in Canadian cash. [@1] Officers also found about
140 g of dried cannabis, about 100 methamphetamine tablets, a handgun, seven
mobile phones and two vehicles seized as offence-related property. [@1]

It found the group obtained its drugs from Laval. [@1] Suspects made frequent
road trips to bring the drugs to Saguenay for redistribution. [@1]
