At about 4:30 p.m. on Saturday 31 October, military police officers of the 1st
Military Police Battalion carried out an anti-drug-trafficking operation in
Ponta Grossa. [@1] The incident was recorded in the area of Rua Basílio Parabocz
and involved drugs, money and materials associated with drug dealing. [@1]
During patrol, a 1st Company team stopped a vehicle and found 81 portions of a
cocaine-like substance, plus cash. [@1]

In follow-up checks, the team found more portions of cocaine-like substance,
plus maconha, the Brazilian term for cannabis, and four precision scales. [@1]
In total, police listed 46 grams of maconha, alongside 316 grams of cocaine-like
substance. [@1] They also listed R$3,067 in cash, four precision scales, a card
machine, seven packs of ziplock packaging, two mobile phones and one vehicle.
[@1]

The man stopped was taken to the Civil Police station for further procedures.
[@1] The outlet presents the case as daily ostensive street policing by the 1st
BPM against drug trafficking in Ponta Grossa. [@1]
