Six people were detained on 31 October 2026 while smoking ganja, the local term
for cannabis, at a cremation ground on the outskirts of Arlapenta village in
Dammapeta mandal, Bhadradri Kothagudem district, Telangana. [@1] Urine drug
tests on all six were positive for THC, the main psychoactive compound in
cannabis. [@1] Questioning led police to two more men, Pottu Saikumar, also
called Sai, of Lankalapalli and Lavudia Chaitanya of Nagupalli, who were found
with cannabis. [@1] Disha reported that the two had sold the ganja to the six.
[@2] Police seized a quarter kilogram of ganja, two mobile phones and a
motorcycle from them and estimated the drug's value at about Rs 12,500. [@1]

All eight were produced before the Dammapeta court on Sunday 1 November 2026.
[@2] The court ordered the six who had smoked cannabis sent to a de-addiction
centre, while the two found with the drug were remanded to custody. [@1] Police
said they were still investigating other people involved in supplying and
selling ganja. [@1]

The detentions came during special checks the district police were running as
part of a drive against drugs. [@1] Aswaraopeta Circle Inspector Nagaraju took
part in the operation. [@1] Officers warned that anyone transporting, selling,
storing or using ganja would face strict legal action and urged young people to
stay away from drugs. [@1] The police announced the operation to the media on
Sunday 1 November, Kalam Daily reported. [@3]
