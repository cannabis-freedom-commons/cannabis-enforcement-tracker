Mossos d'Esquadra, Catalonia's police, dismantled an indoor marijuana plantation
in an illegally occupied house in Mediona, in Alt Penedes. [@1] Officers
detained the alleged keeper of the plantation, a 42-year-old man, on 29 October
for alleged drug trafficking and electricity fraud. [@1] Officers also arrested
a 39-year-old man found in the adjacent dwelling who had a pending judicial
search and arrest warrant. [@2] Both detainees were brought before the duty
court in Vilafranca del Penedes. [@2]

Officers seized 774 marijuana plants spread between the first floor and the
basement of the house. [@2] The plants were in an advanced stage of flowering
and maturation. [@2] Inside officers also found intensive cultivation equipment
including air-conditioning and ventilation systems, odour filters, high-power
lights, transformers, fertilisers and water tanks. [@2] Police estimated the
fraudulent electricity consumption caused about 223,000 euros in losses. [@1]
Checks with the distribution company confirmed the house had no supply contract
and had a clandestine electricity connection. [@1]

The investigation began after officers saw several people unloading
marijuana-growing material from a van into the house. [@1] Officers then
detected a strong marijuana smell, high heat emissions on thermal images and
air-conditioning units hidden with tarpaulins behind the house. [@1] After the
operation the house was returned to its owner to close and secure it against
further occupation. [@2] The investigation remains open to identify and locate
other people who could be linked to the plantation. [@2]
