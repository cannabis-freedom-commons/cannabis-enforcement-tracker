Hamza Abbou, 31, was arrested in the night of 13 to 14 October 2026 at
Laroque-des-Albères, in the Pyrénées-Orientales, as he rode a quad bike carrying
237 kilograms of cannabis resin, according to the gendarmes and the Perpignan
prosecutor's office. [@1,2]

He had just crossed the border from Spain, La Dépêche du Midi reported. [@2]
Abbou, nicknamed "le Botch", had been banned from France since a November 2020
conviction and had been living in Spain since his release from prison in March
2026. [@1,2]

He was brought before a judge on 16 October and placed under formal investigation
for transporting and importing narcotics, the Perpignan prosecutor's office told
L'Indépendant. [@1] He was provisionally jailed pending a hearing on his pre-trial
detention. [@1]

Abbou once ran the Varèse dealing point in the Mirail area of Toulouse, one of the
city's largest, and was arrested in 2016 after the RAID found him clinging to a
balcony with a bag containing 150,000 euros. [@1,2] He was sentenced in 2020 after
three years on the run and released in March 2026. [@1]

His elder brother, Badreddine Abbou, was sentenced to 11 years by a specially
composed court in Toulouse on 10 September 2026. [@2] Investigators have not
established Abbou's role in the new case or who the resin belonged to. [@2]
