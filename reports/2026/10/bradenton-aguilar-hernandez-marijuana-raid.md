Manatee County sheriff's deputies arrested Miguel Aguilar-Hernandez, 27, after
undercover narcotics purchases at his Bradenton home. [@2] Undercover detectives
bought drugs from him several times at a house in the 4900 block of 22nd Street
West. [@2] Deputies and the SWAT team executed a search warrant there on 23
October 2026. [@2] Deputies found Aguilar-Hernandez inside the home with a woman
and their infant. [@2]

The search turned up 15 pounds (about 6.8 kg) of marijuana. [@2] Deputies also
found cocaine, MDMA and other drugs in the home, the sheriff's office said. [@1]
They seized three firearms and $3,366 in cash. [@2] The sheriff's office valued
the seized narcotics at more than $150,000. [@1]

Aguilar-Hernandez faces trafficking in cocaine and possession of marijuana and
MDMA with intent to sell, as well as multiple counts of selling cocaine and
MDMA. [@2] The Florida Department of Children and Families removed an infant
from the home after its investigation, the sheriff's office said. [@1]
