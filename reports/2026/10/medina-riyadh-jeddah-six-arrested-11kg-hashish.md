Saudi Arabia's General Directorate of Narcotics Control arrested six people in
three security operations in Medina, Riyadh and Jeddah governorate for promoting
narcotics, stimulant tablets and tablets subject to medical controls. [@1] In
Jeddah, officers arrested three citizens accused of promoting hashish and other
tablets. [@1] In Riyadh, officers arrested one citizen accused of promoting
amphetamine and tablets subject to medical controls. [@1] In Medina, officers
arrested two Pakistani residents accused of promoting methamphetamine, known
locally as shabu. [@1] All those arrested were detained, processed under the
regulations and referred to the Public Prosecution. [@1]

The Jeddah seizure included 11 kilograms of hashish, the cannabis component
recorded here. [@2] The same Jeddah seizure also included 1,037 amphetamine
tablets plus additional medical-control tablets whose number was not published.
[@1] The Riyadh seizure comprised 7,093 amphetamine tablets and 14,774 tablets
subject to medical controls. [@2] No quantity was published for the shabu in the
Medina cases, with the security announcement limited to naming the drug type.
[@2]

The authorities urged the public to report smugglers and promoters via 995, via
911 in Mecca, Medina, Riyadh and the Eastern Province, via 999 elsewhere, and by
email, with strict confidentiality. [@1] A second roundup account places these
three operations within a wider day of additional announced security operations
in other regions involving qat and other seizures left out here. [@2]
