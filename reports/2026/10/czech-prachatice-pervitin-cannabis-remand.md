Police said he had long obtained pervitin, the Czech name for methamphetamine,
and marijuana and sold or supplied them to other people, mainly in the
Prachatice district. [@1,2] During the detention, detectives also searched his
home and other premises and land, and seized a larger quantity of both pervitin
and marijuana. [@1,2]

Police spokesperson Martina Joklová said the detention had been preceded by an
investigation that gathered enough information and evidence to bring a
prosecution against the man. [@1,2]

Detectives then asked the District Court in Prachatice to remand the accused in
custody, and the court granted the request. [@1,2] If convicted, he faces a prison
sentence of two to ten years. [@1,2]
