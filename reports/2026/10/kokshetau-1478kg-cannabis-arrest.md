Police in Kokshetau detained a 28-year-old man on suspicion of selling drugs
through zakladki, hidden stashes left for buyers. [@1] The detainee is a native
of Petropavlovsk who had arrived in Kokshetau in August specifically to sell
drugs. [@1] Police said he had previously sold drugs in the same way in
Petropavlovsk and Kostanay. [@1] In Kokshetau he rented a flat where he stored
and packaged drugs for later sale through тайники, hidden caches. [@1]

Police said he could make 100 to 200 stashes a day and was paid in
cryptocurrency. [@1] A search of the flat found 320 grams of marijuana. [@1] The
search also found about 371 grams of hashish oil. [@1] The search also found 787
grams of hashish, bringing the combined cannabis total to 1.478 kg. [@1]
Officers also found more than a kilogram of the psychotropic substance
alpha-PVP, which is not counted in the cannabis total. [@1]

Investigators said the suspect received large consignments, divided them into
small doses and prepared them for sale. [@1] He was placed in a temporary
detention facility. [@1] A pretrial investigation is continuing. [@1] He faces 7
to 12 years of imprisonment. [@1]
