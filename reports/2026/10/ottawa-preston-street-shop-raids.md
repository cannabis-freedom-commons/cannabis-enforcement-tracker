Ottawa police arrested a 25-year-old Ottawa man after searching a business in
the 200 block of Preston Street in Little Italy on 23 and 29 October 2026. [@1]
He was charged with possession of illicit cannabis, unlawful sale of cannabis,
sale of unpackaged and unstamped tobacco, possession of property obtained by
crime, and possession of a Schedule III substance for the purpose of
trafficking. [@1]

Police said the searches investigated the illegal sale of cannabis, nicotine and
other controlled substances. [@1] In the 23 October search, officers seized 4.79
kilograms of cannabis products, along with 40 vapes, 14,285 contraband
cigarettes and 95 cigars. [@1] In the 29 October search of the same location,
officers seized about two kilograms of cannabis flower, 407 grams of pre-rolled
cannabis, 38 grams of hash and 27 grams of THC products. [@1] The second search
also yielded 24 grams of psilocybin, 24 vapes, 12,120 contraband cigarettes, 70
cigars, two televisions and $335 in cash. [@1]

The two searches, a week apart at the same Little Italy business, together
produced about 7.26 kilograms of cannabis products alongside the tobacco and
other items. [@1]
