Ottawa police searched the same Preston Street business twice in October 2026 while investigating illicit cannabis sales. [@1,2,3,4,5,6]

Ottawa police arrested a 25-year-old Ottawa man during the 29 October 2026
search of a retail business on the 200 block of Preston Street. [@2,5,6] He faces
charges of possession of illicit cannabis, unlawful sale of cannabis, sale of
unpackaged, unstamped tobacco, possession of property obtained by crime and
possession of a Schedule III substance for the purpose of trafficking under the
Cannabis Act, Criminal Code and Controlled Drugs and Substances Act. [@3,6] Police
released him on an undertaking to appear in court at a later date. [@3,5,6]

Officers seized approximately 5 pounds of cannabis flower, 407 grams of
pre-rolled cannabis, 38 grams of hash and 27,800 mg of THC products. [@2,4,5,6] Police
also seized 24 grams of psilocybin products, 24 nicotine vape devices, 12,120
contraband cigarettes, 70 cigars, two televisions and approximately $335 in
Canadian currency, which are not counted as cannabis. [@3,5,6] Centretown
Neighbourhood Resource Team members with Tactical Section assistance executed
the search under a Criminal Code warrant tied to the unlawful sale of cannabis
and nicotine products. [@3,6]

Police said it was the second search warrant executed at the same Preston Street
location within the previous seven days for similar offences. [@3,4,5,6] Police said
the earlier search on 23 October 2026 followed community complaints about
illegal cannabis and nicotine sales at the same location. [@3,5,6] Police said they
remain focused on illegal storefront operations that undermine public safety,
evade regulatory requirements and contribute to the illicit cannabis and tobacco
market. [@3]

Google Maps lists an unlicensed retailer at 268 Preston called Preston Cannabis that was operating as recently as October 2026. [@5]

In the 23 October search, officers seized 4.79 [@1] kilograms of cannabis products, along with 40 vapes, 14,285 contraband [@1] cigarettes and 95 cigars. [@1]

The two searches, a week apart at the same Little Italy business, together [@1] produced about 7.26 kilograms of cannabis products alongside the tobacco and [@1] other items. [@1]
