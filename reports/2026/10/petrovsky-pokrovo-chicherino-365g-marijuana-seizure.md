On 28 October, officers of the Michurinsky municipal police department found 365
grams of a substance identified as marijuana held by a previously convicted
46-year-old resident of the village of Pokrovo-Chicherino in Petrovsky district.
[@1] The Tambov regional Interior Ministry said drug-control officers had
uncovered several cases of unlawful drug storage across three districts of
Tambov Oblast. [@2] The regional police press service was cited as the source
for the findings. [@2]

Police seized the 365 grams of marijuana found in the Petrovsky case. [@2] The
seized material was identified as marijuana and recorded as weighing 365 grams.
[@1] Officers opened a criminal case under part 2 of Article 228 of the Criminal
Code for storage of narcotics in a large amount. [@1]

The Michurinsky department of the Interior Ministry was named as the reporting
authority for the Petrovsky seizure. [@1] The suspect was described as a
previously convicted resident of the village. [@2] Police said further
investigation is continuing in the cases, including checks for possible
involvement in similar unlawful acts. [@1]
