Nashik city police commissioner Sandeep Karnik was honoured at a 'say no to
drugs' oath-taking ceremony on Wednesday 28 October 2026. [@1] Maharashtra
governor Jishnu Dev Varma felicitated Karnik for the city police's efforts
against drug trafficking and substance abuse. [@2] The ceremony took place at
Mahakavi Kalidas Kalamandir during the Sashakt Yuva, Sashakt Bharat
anti-addiction event. [@2] Varma urged young people to stay away from substance
abuse and contribute to nation-building. [@2] In 30 ganja cases, officers seized
332.088 kg of ganja and arrested 48 people. [@3]

Police valued the ganja at Rs 49.69 lakh. [@2] The same tally recorded bhang and
charas seizures in separate cases. [@2] The campaign has run since Karnik took
charge in November 2023. [@2]

Between November 2023 and October 2026, the city police registered 79 narcotics
cases. [@2] Mephedrone, a synthetic stimulant known locally as MD, was the
largest category, with 41 cases. [@2] The campaign extended to other districts
to trace trafficking networks. [@1] Karnik said the police had been taking
feedback from citizens through corner meetings, at jogging tracks, on X and on
the commissioner's WhatsApp number. [@2]
