Bhavnagar's Special Operations Group (SOG) raided Ranchhodbhai Rupabhai
Algotar's home in Patana village, Vallabhipur taluka, after a tip came in while
officers were on patrol. [@2] The address was in Bhal Ujalvav Timbo, where
Algotar lived. [@2] Officers searched the home's courtyard and the fenced yard
behind it, where cannabis plants had been grown. [@2]

The seizure included about 128 plants. [@2] The green ganja weighed 59.820 kg.
[@3] Police valued the haul at 29.91 lakh rupees (₹2,991,000), with one lakh
equal to 100,000 rupees. [@3] The seizure involved about 59 kg of green ganja
and total goods worth about ₹29 lakh. [@1] Ganja is the local term for cannabis
used in the case. [@1]
