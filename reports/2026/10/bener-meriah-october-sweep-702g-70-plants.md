Bener Meriah district police said they uncovered 10 narcotics cases and secured
11 people during Operasi Sarang Narkoba in Bener Meriah, Aceh. [@1] The 11
comprised eight adult men, one woman and two children held in the enforcement
series during the operation period. [@1] Nine of the 10 cases remained under
investigation, while the remaining case was the discovery of a cannabis field in
Kenine village, Timang Gajah. [@1] District police chief AKBP Ricky Nugraha
announced the results at a Polres Bener Meriah press conference accompanied by
narcotics, public-relations and internal-affairs officers. [@1]

The narcotics unit seized 702 grams gross of ganja, the cannabis component
recorded here, alongside 18.49 grams gross of sabu. [@1] Officers also found 70
cannabis stalks at the field in Kenine village, Timang Gajah, as part of the
same operation. [@1] The operation ran from 9 to 31 October 2026 in Bener
Meriah. [@1]

The police chief described the disclosures as part of the police commitment to
eradicate drug distribution and use in the Bener Meriah jurisdiction and to
protect the public. [@1] He urged all community elements to help police fight
illicit drug distribution and to report suspicious abuse or trafficking
activity. [@1] He said narcotics eradication could not rely on police alone and
required support from all levels of society to protect young people. [@1]
