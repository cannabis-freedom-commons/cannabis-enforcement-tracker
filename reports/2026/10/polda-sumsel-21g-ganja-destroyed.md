South Sumatra's provincial police destroyed drug evidence in the courtyard of
their headquarters in Palembang on Friday 30 October 2026. [@2,4] The evidence
came from 71 police reports (laporan polisi, the case files police open)
involving 87 suspects, all from October 2026. [@1,4] Police said the 87 detained
suspects came from cases handled by four units: the provincial narcotics
directorate and the city and district police of Palembang, Ogan Komering Ilir
and Banyuasin. [@2,5] Patar Silalahi, the provincial narcotics director, said some
of them were believed to have acted as bandar, or large-scale dealers. [@1]

By Patar's count the cases yielded 21.3 grams of ganja (cannabis), along with
36,459.93 grams of sabu (crystal methamphetamine), 118.2 grams of Happy Five
pills, 473.8 millilitres of etomidate, 379 ecstasy pills and 46.65 millilitres
of sinte. [@1] Part of the evidence was set aside for laboratory tests and for
use in court, and was not destroyed. [@1] The cannabis destroyed weighed 21
grams. [@1] Akurat.co, in its own account of the destruction, listed 21.3 grams
of ganja among the destroyed drugs. [@3] The cannabis came from Palembang city
police cases, which also yielded 123.34 grams of methamphetamine, 19.8
millilitres of etomidate and 45 ecstasy pills. [@1] Palembang city police
accounted for 51 of the 71 reports and 61 of the 87 suspects. [@1] The
provincial directorate handled 14 reports with 19 suspects. [@1]

Patar said some of the evidence came from a narcotics unit operation in Ogan
Komering Ilir, with most of the rest from routine investigation. [@1] He said
100 pieces of the evidence came from Malaysia. [@1] Patar said raids and
enforcement action against trafficking would remain routine police work. [@3] He
said the work was measured against a 100-day target under the new leadership and
would then continue as routine. [@1] Akurat.co reported that the provincial
police and the National Narcotics Agency (BNN) said they would strengthen
cooperation to curb trafficking in the province. [@3]
