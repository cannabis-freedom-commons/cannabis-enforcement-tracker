Two men aged 19 and 21 were arrested and placed in custody after gendarmes
carried out an arrest-and-search operation in Castres on 27 October 2026. [@1]
The searches took place in a flat and a cellar on rue Jean-Saulière in the Petit
Train-Laden neighbourhood of Castres. [@1] The younger man was referred to the
Castres prosecutor on 31 October and held in provisional detention pending an
immediate-appearance hearing on 2 November on drug detention, transport,
acquisition, offer, transfer and use charges plus possession of category B and C
weapons. [@1]

In the flat and cellar officers found 3.1 kg of cannabis resin which police
valued at nearly 11,000 euros. [@1] They also seized 3,530 euros in cash,
several nitrous oxide canisters, a 22 Long Rifle revolver classed as a category
B handgun, and a category C shoulder weapon with its ammunition. [@2]

The operation was carried out by the Castres gendarmerie company as part of an
inquiry by the Castres research brigade into drug trafficking and firearms
offences in the Petit Train-Laden district. [@1] Castres prosecutor Élodie
Buguel said the discovery confirmed the commitment of the investigative services
under the prosecutor’s authority to fighting drug trafficking. [@2]
