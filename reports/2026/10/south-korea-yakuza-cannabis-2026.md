Suwon District Court sentenced a 53-year-old Korean resident of Japan to ten years in prison on 8 October 2026 for smuggling 636 kilograms of cannabis and ordered the drugs forfeited. [@1,2,4,5] Prosecutors had asked for twenty years at a hearing on 29 September. [@1,3,4] Most Korean accounts identified him as A, and Chosun’s English report identified him as Kim and named the yakuza group Kudokai. [@2,4]

## Shipment and arrest

The container left Laem Chabang, Thailand, in early March and reached Incheon Port on 23 March. [@1,4,6] The defendant worked with a group based in Ho Chi Minh City to distribute some of the cannabis in South Korea and send the rest to Japan. [@1,4,5,7] The Ministry of Justice said the joint drug investigation headquarters, customs and the coast guard acted on intelligence from the National Intelligence Service. [@11] The cannabis was packed in 610 bags, and investigators estimated its retail value at 95.4 billion won. [@2,4,6,11]

April accounts reported that officers detained him when he went to collect the shipment at a Gyeonggi warehouse and announced the indictment on 10 April. [@6,7,8,9,10] Investigators identified four Vietnamese suspects and said they would seek Interpol notices. [@6] The Korea Times reported that investigators had also found plans to smuggle drugs from Colombia. [@10]

## Sentencing

The defence argued that investigators had used an informant to induce the offence. [@1,3] The court rejected the argument, saying the defendant had dealt directly with the Vietnamese group and the evidence did not show investigators had induced him to smuggle the drugs. [@1,2,4] The court took a prior six-year sentence for methamphetamine smuggling into account, alongside his cooperation and the fact that the seized cannabis had not been sold and yielded no profit. [@1,2,4]
