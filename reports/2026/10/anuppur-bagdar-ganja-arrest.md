Police in Anuppur district stopped Manoj Singh Gond at Bagdara Ghat bend as he
walked from Bagdara towards Shyamduari carrying cannabis in a white nylon sack.
[@2] Police identified Gond as a 33-year-old resident of Bagdara village under
the Karanpathar police station. [@2] Karanpathar police arrested him and seized
1.7 kg of cannabis and ₹11,400 described as sales proceeds. [@1] Police valued
the cannabis at about ₹17,000 and gave the combined value of cannabis and cash
as ₹28,400. [@2]

Police received a tip on 28 October 2026 that Gond was carrying cannabis for
sale. [@2] Police said he intended to sell the cannabis in a local market. [@1]
According to police, Gond said he had bought the cannabis from Mobin Khan, a
resident of Mediaras. [@2] Police registered case number 347/2026 under India's
Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act and said further legal action was
continuing. [@2]
