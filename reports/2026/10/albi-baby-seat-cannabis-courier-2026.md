Anti-crime police in Albi stopped the driver of a Peugeot 206 on the route de
Castres in the dark on Saturday 31 October 2026, in a routine traffic check.
[@1] The officers said they smelled cannabis strongly as they approached the car
door. [@1]

The check led to the arrest of a drug deliverer who had come from Toulouse and
was travelling with a 17-year-old girl who had run away from a care home. [@1]
Police found a hiding place built into the back of his car to conceal cannabis,
cocaine and ecstasy. [@1]

The man, 24 and banned from staying in the Tarn, was just tried in
immediate-appearance proceedings. [@1]
