Gendarmes of the Misiones investigations unit of Gendarmería Nacional, supported
by the high-complexity joint operations group GOC-NEA and Squadron 11 San
Ignacio, closed a ten-month inquiry into a drug-trafficking ring based in San
Vicente, Misiones, and intercepted a Ford Ka acting as a scout car and a large
truck. [@1] Officers took the trailer apart and found a false floor and a second
compartment behind the cab holding 2,087 bricks and 24 bags of marijuana buds.
[@1] A Narcotest field test, carried out in front of witnesses, confirmed
cannabis sativa weighing 1,644 kilos 788 grams. [@1]

The federal court of Posadas, the Federal Prosecutor's Office No. 2 of Posadas
and the drug-trafficking prosecution unit PROCUNAR ordered 13 searches at
properties in Misiones and Buenos Aires province. [@1] Those searches produced a
further 545 kilos 450 grams of marijuana found at one home. [@1] The force put
its total for the case at 2,190 kilos 238 grams of marijuana, and also seized a
firearm, two scales, six vehicles, 39,700 US dollars, 1,077,000 Argentine pesos
and electronic storage devices. [@1] Five people were detained, among them a
member the force described as an important link in the organisation, and a woman
was placed at the disposal of the case. [@1] The proceedings are for
infringement of Law 23.737, Argentina's narcotics law, in a case opened at the
end of 2025. [@1]

Gendarmería said that over the ten months of the inquiry it seized 13,294 kilos
884 grams of cannabis sativa in Misiones linked to the organisation. [@1]
Primera Edición attributed the account to the Ministry of Security and reported
five detainees and the seizure of more than 2,190 kilos of the drug. [@2]
Mirador Provincial reported that the case file began in December 2025, when
investigators seized more than seven tonnes of marijuana hidden in a truck, and
that officers continued working with the Misiones provincial police. [@3]
