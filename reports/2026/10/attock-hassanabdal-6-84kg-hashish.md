Hassanabdal police arrested five people they described as drug dealers during an
operation in Attock district on Saturday 31 October 2026. [@1] A police
spokesman said the operation was carried out against drug dealers, and that 6.84
kilograms of hashish was recovered from the five. [@1] Hashish is cannabis
resin. [@1]

Attock police said they had intensified a crackdown against anti-social
elements, drug dealers and people involved in unlawful activities, on the orders
of District Police Officer Ali bin Tariq, a retired captain. [@1] In Pakistan,
the district police officer, or DPO, commands the district force. [@1]
Hassanabdal police carried out the drug operation as part of that campaign, the
spokesman said. [@1]

Hassanabdal is a town in Attock district. [@1] The force announced the arrests
as part of its action against drug dealing in the Hassanabdal area. [@1]
