Balıkesir narcotics police detained two suspects in two separate operations in
the city centre. [@1] The operations targeted two people suspected of trading
drugs. [@2] Proceedings were started against the two detained suspects for
manufacturing and trading narcotic or stimulant substances, and the
investigation was continuing. [@1]

Police put the cannabis seizure at 1 kilo 254 grams of skunk/esrar, with esrar
the Turkish word for cannabis. [@1] The same searches also turned up liquid
synthetic cannabinoid known as bonzai, synthetic pills and metamfetamin, which
are not counted in the cannabis figure. [@2] Officers also seized one precision
scale and one cannabis grinder. [@1]

The DHA account dates the two city-centre operations to the day before its 31
October 2026 publication, fixing the event date at 30 October 2026. [@2]
