Papua Police foiled an attempt to smuggle 10 kilograms of drugs from Papua New Guinea near
the Holtekamp area of Jayapura City on Friday 9 October. [@1]

Director for Drug Crimes Investigation in Papua Police, Senior Commissioner Alfian, said
officers found the drugs abandoned during an inspection in Holtekamp: suspicious individuals
were spotted near the spot where marijuana in rice sacks was later found, but they escaped
when officers approached. [@1]

The drugs were smuggled through illegal pathways along the Indonesia–Papua New Guinea
border, Alfian said on Saturday. [@1]
