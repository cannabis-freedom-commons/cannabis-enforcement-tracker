Kanth police arrested a woman named Mithilesh at the spot during a raid on
Ashapur Bhatu colony in Mukhtyarpur Nawada, a village in Moradabad district in
the northern Indian state of Uttar Pradesh. [@1,2] The raid followed a tip from an
informer that a woman in the colony was running an illegal business selling
charas, a hand-made cannabis resin. [@1,2] Officers recovered 1 kg 46 g (1.046 kg)
of charas from her, police said. [@1,2] Sub-Inspector Vivek Kumar, Sub-Inspector
Sagar Kumar and woman Sub-Inspector Sonam, all posted at Kanth police station,
carried out the raid. [@1]

Police registered a case against the woman under India's Narcotic Drugs and
Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act, the country's main anti-drug law. [@1,2] Kanth
police registered the case under relevant sections of the Act. [@1] The woman
was produced in court after the case was registered. [@1,2] Police described the
arrest as part of their action against illegal narcotics trafficking in the
area. [@1]

The arrested woman is 38 years old, identified as the wife of late Shivshankar and a resident of Ashapur colony under Kanth police station. [@3] The arrest took place at about 6:30 a.m. on 31 October 2026, about 100 metres ahead of Ashapur colony. [@3] Police registered the case as FIR number 271/2026 under Sections 8/20 of the NDPS Act. [@3] Police said the arrested woman had a prior criminal history related to the excise Act. [@3]
