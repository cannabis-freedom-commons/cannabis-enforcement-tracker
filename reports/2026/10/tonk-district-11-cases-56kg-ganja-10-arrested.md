Police in Tonk district registered 11 cases under the NDPS Act and arrested 10
people in a one-day special drive against illegal narcotics. [@1]

The cases were made in different police station areas of the district, and the
seized drugs came to 56 kg 681 g of ganja as well as green ganja plants. [@1]

The force said the drive ran for one day under the Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyan,
India's campaign for a drug-free country, with 11 cases registered in different
police station areas of the district. [@2] Tonk Police said 10 accused were
proceeded against and arrested in those cases. [@2]

The Tonk Police account gives the ganja recovered in the operation as 56 kg 681
g, together with green ganja plants. [@3]
