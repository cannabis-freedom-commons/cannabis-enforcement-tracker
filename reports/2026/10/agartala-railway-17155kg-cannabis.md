Tripura Railway Police arrested Anup Sukla Baidya, 25, on platform no. 1 of
Agartala Railway Station at about 10 am on 29 October 2026. [@1] The operation
by the Government Railway Police and Railway Protection Force followed specific
information, police said. [@1] Baidya was sitting on a bench in front of the
parking area, between the Railway Protection Force post and the pay-and-use
toilet. [@1] A search of his trolley bag found 17.155 kg of suspected cannabis.
[@2] Baidya is from Shrigauri in the Badarpur police station area of Karimganj
district, Assam, now named Sribhumi. [@1] A case was registered at Agartala
Government Railway Police Station under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic
Substances (NDPS) Act. [@2]

Police estimated the destination black-market value of the seized cannabis at
about ₹8 lakh. [@1] Inspector Raju Bhowmik, officer in charge of Agartala
Railway Police Station, said the seizure was made on platform no. 1 and that one
person had been arrested, and said the investigation was continuing. [@3]
Officers were investigating where the cannabis came from, where it was to be
taken and whether a trafficking network was involved. [@3]
