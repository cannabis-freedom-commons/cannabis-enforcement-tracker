Brescia Mobile Squad investigators arrested two Brescia residents aged 19 and 17
and a 29-year-old Moroccan man without fixed abode for alleged possession and
dealing of narcotics. [@1] Officers identified the three during an identity
check of patrons at a bar in Brescia province and then carried out personal
searches with positive results. [@1] After formalities at police headquarters,
all three were placed under arrest and put at the disposal of the Public
Prosecutor, while the 17-year-old was placed in a juvenile community pending
trial. [@1]

The 29-year-old was carrying 22 grams of hashish described as still to be
divided, alongside 4 grams of cocaine and 700 euros in small notes described as
dealing proceeds. [@1] The 19-year-old was carrying two sealed cellophane
packets containing 40 grams of cocaine, plus several doses of hashish and 300
euros in cash described as dealing proceeds. [@1] The 17-year-old was carrying
16 grams of cocaine divided into 21 sealed packets ready for sale, plus 23 grams
of hashish and 1,300 euros in small notes hidden in his clothing. [@1] Police
immediately seized all the drugs and the money. [@1]

Brescia police chief Paolo Sartori issued an order for the 29-year-old, who was
irregularly present in Italy, to leave the country so he can be expelled with
escort to his country of origin once released from custody. [@1] Police said the
arrests came during targeted action against youth crime and drug trafficking in
Brescia province. [@1]
