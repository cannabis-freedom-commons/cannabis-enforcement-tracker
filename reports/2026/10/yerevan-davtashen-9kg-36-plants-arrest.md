Yerevan criminal-police drug officers and Davtashen community-police officers
detained a 49-year-old Davtashen resident after finding cannabis at his yard and
home on 16 October 2026. [@1] Officers took the man to the Davtashen police
department, where he said he had grown the cannabis and prepared the drug for
personal use. [@1] A public criminal prosecution was initiated against him, with
house arrest imposed as the restraint measure. [@1]

Police said they had received operational information that the man was growing
cannabis in his yard and selling marijuana made from it. [@1] Armenia's police
statement gives the same tip, saying the 49-year-old Davtashen resident grew
cannabis in his yard and sold the resulting marijuana. [@2] A subsequent
inspection at his residence recovered 36 cannabis bushes, 9 kg of dry cannabis
mass, 1 kg of cannabis seeds and five pipettes. [@2]

The inspection was conducted within criminal proceedings handled by the
investigative department for the Ajapnyak and Davtashen administrative
districts. [@3] The second outlet described the 9 kg as dried plant mass,
presumably marijuana, and reported the same totals of plants and seeds. [@3]
