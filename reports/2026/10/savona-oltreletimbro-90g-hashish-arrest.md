At night in Savona's Oltreletimbro district, Carabinieri of the Radiomobile
Section arrested a 26-year-old Turkish man in flagrante for alleged possession
of drugs for dealing. [@3] The patrol was conducting territorial control in the
district when a resident approached officers to report suspicious movements by
some young people. [@2] Officers entered the indicated area and identified
several Turkish men who looked suspicious. [@2] After carrying out personal
searches on the spot, they extended the control to a nearby dwelling belonging
to one of the men. [@2]

Inside that dwelling officers found about 90 grams of hashish, which was seized.
[@2] The 26-year-old was then held in the security cells of the Savona company
pending a fast-track hearing scheduled for later that day. [@3] The case was the
second dealing arrest in the wider Savona operation that week. [@1]

In that home search the Radiomobile Section found the 26-year-old with 90 grams
of hashish. [@1] The wider Savona operation ran in the week between 26 October
and 1 November. [@1]
