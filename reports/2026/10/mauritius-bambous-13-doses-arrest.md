ADSU officers carried out a perquisition at the domicile in Bambous of a
30-year-old unemployed man. [@1] The home search took place on Friday 30 October
2026 at around 19:10 in the evening. [@1] The property searched was the home
where the man resides in Bambous. [@1] Following the discovery during the
search, officers arrested the suspect. [@1] He was then placed in police
detention. [@1]

During the search of the premises, officers discovered thirteen folded papers
made of aluminium foil. [@1] Each of the thirteen papers held a quantity of
cannabis, together weighing 5.02 grams. [@1] Officers seized the thirteen papers
and the cannabis they contained. [@1]

The police investigation was still continuing after the arrest. [@1]
