A 36-year-old resident of Oktyabrsky District was convicted of acquiring and
possessing cannabis without intent to sell. [@2] Leninsky District Court
sentenced her to three years and six months in a general-regime correctional
colony. [@2] The court said police found a polymer bag in the yard of her home
and recorded the cannabis weight as 120.043 grams. [@2] The police account gave
the seized marijuana's weight as 122 grams. [@3] Police said the woman was
detained and investigators opened a criminal case for unlawful possession. [@3]

She said she had acquired the cannabis herself for personal use, and the court
cited witness testimony, expert findings and other case materials in support of
its finding. [@1] The Jewish Autonomous Oblast appellate court left the sentence
unchanged. [@1] The court described recidivism as an aggravating circumstance.
[@1] It said her suspended sentence for a similar offence was revoked, after
which she served a general-regime prison term and was released three years
earlier. [@1]
