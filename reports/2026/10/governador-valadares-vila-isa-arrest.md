Military Police arrested a 43-year-old man for drug trafficking at a house in
Vila Isa, Governador Valadares, in the early hours of 31 October 2026. [@1]
Officers went to the suspect's house after receiving complaints that he was
selling drugs there. [@1] The man was holding crack rocks in his hands when
officers approached him. [@1] Asked about other drugs in the house, he
authorised officers to enter the home and carry out searches. [@1] His wife and
5-year-old daughter were also inside the house at the time. [@1] He was arrested
in the act and taken to the Civil Police station. [@1]

Under the stove, officers found a container holding more crack rocks. [@1]
Officers seized 240 crack rocks in total, besides the cannabis portion and the
ammunition. [@2] A portion of maconha, the Portuguese word for cannabis, was
hidden inside a cement block on the house stairs. [@1] Officers also found a
.380-calibre round of ammunition in the kitchen cupboard. [@1]
