Tarkwa Circuit Court fined Kojo Sah Nkrumah GH¢120,000 over cannabis and tramadol offences, with three years in prison in default. [@1,2,3,4]

The Tarkwa Circuit Court fined 20-year-old labourer Kojo Sah Nkrumah GH¢120,000
after he pleaded guilty to two counts of possessing and selling narcotic drugs
without lawful authority. [@1,2] The court, presided over by Ms Bernice Mensimah
Ackon, convicted and sentenced him on his own plea and ordered three years in
prison if he fails to pay. [@1,2] The Ghana News Agency reported the sentence in a
dispatch datelined Tarkwa on 26 October 2026; the accounts do not give the day the
court imposed it. [@1]

Police arrested Nkrumah at about 01:30 on 21 January 2025 at Tarkwa Railway
Station, during a routine night patrol led by Chief Inspector Issahaque Yahaya.
[@1,2] They found him seated under a shed selling substances suspected to be
narcotic drugs. [@1,2] A search of a takeaway bag uncovered 81 pieces of wrapped
dried leaves suspected to be cannabis, seven sachets of tramadol and GH¢240 in
cash that police believed were proceeds of the sales. [@1,2]

The exhibits went to the Forensic Crime Laboratory in Accra on 25 April 2025, and
a report received on 13 May found delta-9-tetrahydrocannabinol in the plant
material, with a gross weight of 139.61 g. [@1,2] Assistant Superintendent of
Police Samuel Ahiabor prosecuted for officers of the Western Central Regional
Formed Police Unit based in Tarkwa. [@1,2]
