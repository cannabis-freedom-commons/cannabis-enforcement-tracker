Palm Beach County Sheriff's Office deputies arrested 30-year-old Boca Raton chef
Rafael Pedro Soto Ross following a high-speed pursuit on Forest Hill Boulevard
in West Palm Beach. [@1] A deputy clocked Ross driving a black Kia at 84 mph in
a 45-mph zone before he drove through a red light at South Congress Avenue. [@1]
The vehicle reached 102 mph in a 35-mph zone before slowing on southbound
Interstate 95, where deputies made the traffic stop. [@1]

Deputies reported a strong odor of alcohol and bloodshot eyes when they
approached Ross. [@1] Two later breath samples both measured 0.146, nearly
double Florida's 0.08 legal limit. [@2] During a search of the car, deputies
found about 7.58 grams of marijuana and a cooler holding 129 dissolvable
buprenorphine and naloxone strips with the prescription labels removed. [@1]
Records showed his driver's license had been canceled in 2024 with prior
suspensions. [@1]

Ross faces charges including DUI, reckless driving, excessive speeding, driving
with a suspended license, and possession of a controlled substance with intent
to deliver. [@1] He was booked into the Palm Beach County Jail and later
released on $5,000 bond. [@2]
