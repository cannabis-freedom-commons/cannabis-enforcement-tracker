A 52-year-old man from Timiș County is being investigated for trafficking in
risk and high-risk drugs after police and DIICOT Timișoara prosecutors carried
out searches. [@1] Investigators said the man had set up and developed, during
2026, an indoor cannabis crop for sale; the searches carried out on 8 October
found six cannabis plants over two metres tall. [@1]

The striking sight of the operation was cannabis plants hanging to dry in the
house's attic, suspended in a space used for drying the crop. [@1] Police took
away eight bags of plant material still under examination and found two bags of
psilocin- and psilocibin-containing magic mushrooms. [@1] According to the
Romanian police press release, 9,000 euros were also found during the searches.
[@1]

DIICOT prosecutors ordered his detention on 9 October, and a judge at the Timiș
Court then placed him in 30 days' preventive arrest. [@1] Ziua de Vest confirms
the same six large cannabis plants found at two homes in Moșnița Nouă and
Periam. [@2]

Știri de Timișoara confirms the two searches at the man's homes in Moșnița Nouă
and Periam. [@3] The operation was run by the Timișoara Organised Crime Police
Brigade together with DIICOT prosecutors, with gendarmerie support. [@1]
