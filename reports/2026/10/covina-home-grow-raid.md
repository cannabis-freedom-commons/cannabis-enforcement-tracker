The Los Angeles County Sheriff’s Department Narcotics Unit raided a home in the
Covina area on Oct. 6, 2026, on suspicion it held an illegal marijuana grow.
[@1] Deputies served a search warrant at the home while investigating an illegal
marijuana cultivation operation, officials said. [@1] Deputies assigned to the
San Dimas Community Action Team also assisted with the investigation. [@1]
Authorities did not say whether any suspects were arrested during the raid or
what charges they might face. [@1]

During the execution of the search warrant, deputies located and confiscated
numerous illegally cultivated marijuana plants, the sheriff’s department said.
[@1] The sheriff’s department said deputies also located other controlled
substances during the same search. [@1]

Photos of the raid allegedly show multiple rooms inside the home set up for
growing marijuana plants. [@1] The home is located in an unincorporated part of
Covina. [@1] The grow was indoors, spread across several rooms inside the
residence. [@1]
