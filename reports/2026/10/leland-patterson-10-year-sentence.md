A Leland man was sentenced on 29 October to 10 years in federal prison after
pleading guilty to marijuana distribution and firearm charges. [@1] The man was
28-year-old Malcolm Patterson. [@1] The sentencing was reported from Oxford.
[@1] U.S. District Judge James D. Maxwell imposed the sentence. [@1] Patterson
had previously pleaded guilty to possession of marijuana with intent to
distribute. [@1] He had also pleaded guilty to possession of a firearm by a
convicted felon. [@1] Maxwell also ordered three years of supervised release
after the prison term. [@1] Patterson was taken into U.S. Marshals Service
custody after the sentencing. [@1] The U.S. Attorney’s Office for the Northern
District of Mississippi announced the sentence. [@1]

The Greenville Police Department and the Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms
and Explosives investigated the case. [@1] Assistant U.S. Attorney Robert Mims
prosecuted the case. [@1] Greenville Police Chief Marcus R. Turner Sr. said the
sentence reflected cooperation among investigators and federal prosecutors. [@1]
Turner said his department’s responsibility does not end when the handcuffs go
on. [@1] He said the department is committed to building solid cases that result
in meaningful accountability. [@1]
