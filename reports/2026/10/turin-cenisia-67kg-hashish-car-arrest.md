State Police intervened in Turin’s Cenisia quarter, arresting a 31-year-old
Moroccan citizen and reporting a 46-year-old man for possession of drugs for
dealing. [@1] The operation was part of an investigation by officers of the Dora
Vanchiglia police station into suspected dealing linked to the 31-year-old. [@1]
The 31-year-old was arrested and taken to the Lorusso e Cutugno prison, while
the 46-year-old with him was reported for the same offence. [@1,3] The criminal
case was in the preliminary-investigation phase, with the presumption of
innocence applying to the suspects until a final judgment. [@1]

A personal search found and led to the seizure of a hashish block weighing about
100 grams, 225 euros in cash and keys to several cars, homes and garages. [@1] A
search of the parked car found more drugs hidden in the boot and partly under
the rear seats, for a total of more than 67 kilograms of hashish. [@1,3] In the
car used for travel, officers found many stickers of the same type as those used
to mark the drug blocks. [@1] Both cars used in the alleged unlawful activity
were seized. [@1]

Investigators had monitored the 31-year-old’s movements and identified a
possible drug store inside an underground car park in Via Nino Bixio. [@1,3]
Officers had repeatedly seen the 31-year-old linger only briefly by a car parked
inside that structure, leading them to suspect the vehicle was being used to
hide drugs. [@1] TorinoToday independently reports the same Dora Vanchiglia
operation as leading to the arrest of the 31-year-old Moroccan and the reporting
of the 46-year-old for possession for dealing. [@2]
