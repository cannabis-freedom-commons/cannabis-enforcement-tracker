Military Police arrested four people in two drug-trafficking cases registered in
São João del-Rei on 29 and 31 October. [@1] The first case was recorded on the
night of Thursday 29 October at about 19h40 in the Matosinhos neighbourhood.
[@1] A couple formed by a 22-year-old man and a 25-year-old woman was arrested
in flagrante in that Matosinhos action. [@1] The second case happened on the
morning of Saturday 31 October at about 11h50 in the Senhor dos Montes
neighbourhood. [@1] A 19-year-old man and a 24-year-old woman were arrested in
flagrante in that second action. [@1]

After anonymous reports of dealing in a Matosinhos residence, officers went to
the address and stopped a man who had just bought a crack stone. [@1] In that
action officers seized portions of marijuana and crack, R$164 in cash and mobile
phones. [@1] In the Senhor dos Montes house officers found a bar and a raw
portion of crack as well as already portioned drugs, a precision scale,
instruments with drug residues, R$1,364 in cash and two mobile phones. [@1] A
second roundup corroborates the Matosinhos seizure of marijuana and crack
portions with R$164 and phones leading to the couple aged 22 and 25. [@2]

Both cases were registered by the Military Police and those involved were sent
to the competent authorities. [@1]
