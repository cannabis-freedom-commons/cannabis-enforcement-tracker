The defendant, aged 21, was charged with defrauding an elderly person in Okayama
Prefecture of 1.5 million yen in cash and with possessing cannabis elsewhere in
the same prefecture. [@1] In May, the Okayama District Court sentenced him to 4
years and 6 months in prison and a 300,000-yen fine. [@1] Prosecutors appealed,
arguing that the failure to set a detention term was an error in the application
of the law. [@1]

It emerged on the 29th that the district court had imposed the fine without
declaring the period of detention in a workhouse if the fine could not be paid
in full. [@1] On the 28th, the Okayama branch of the Hiroshima High Court
vacated the first-instance judgment and issued a guilty judgment that set the
workhouse detention period. [@1]

Under the Criminal Code, a person who cannot fully pay a fine is detained in a
workhouse, and the detention period must be declared when the fine is imposed.
[@1] 労役場留置, rendered here as workhouse detention, refers to that detention in a
labour facility for non-payment of a fine. [@1]
