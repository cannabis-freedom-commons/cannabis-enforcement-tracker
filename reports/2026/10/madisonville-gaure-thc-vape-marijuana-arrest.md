Investigators arrested 25-year-old Alejandro Gaure of Madisonville and booked
him into the Hopkins County Jail. [@3] He was charged with first-offense
trafficking in marijuana of less than 8 ounces, first-offense trafficking of 8
ounces to 5 pounds, both enhanced, and purchase and possession of drug
paraphernalia. [@2] The case was described as a Madisonville man arrested and
charged after a search found THC and marijuana products. [@1]

Detectives executed a search warrant on an intercepted shipment and found about
250 pounds of suspected THC vape cartridges and about 35 pounds of suspected
marijuana leaf. [@2] Detectives then executed one search warrant in Madisonville
and another in Earlington, Kentucky. [@2] Those searches recovered 10 firearms
and more suspected THC wax and suspected marijuana leaf. [@2]

The investigation began after a Daviess County detective tipped the
Madisonville-Hopkins County Vice Narcotics Unit about a large shipment of drugs
being delivered to Hopkins County. [@3] The unit was helped by the Madisonville
Police Department, the Hopkins County Sheriff's Office, the Daviess County
Sheriff's Office and the Commonwealth Attorney's Office. [@2] Police say the
investigation remains ongoing. [@2]
