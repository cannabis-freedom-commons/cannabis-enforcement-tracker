A Força Tática operation of the 33rd Interior Military Police Battalion in
Barretos on Friday 30 October seized more than 1.4 kg of marijuana, cocaine
portions and materials used to prepare and sell drugs. [@1] The case began when
a ROCAM team stopped an individual on Avenida Cecap 5 in the Newton Siqueira
Sopa housing complex. [@1] During that stop officers recorded suspected
involvement in drug trafficking and tampering with a motor vehicle identifier.
[@1] Officers then carried out checks in the Vida Nova neighbourhood, where they
found more drugs and preparation and trafficking materials, including drugs
prepared for delivery. [@1] The person involved was taken to the Barretos police
station, where he remained in custody at the disposal of the courts. [@1]

Officers seized two marijuana bricks totalling 1.412 kg, the cannabis component
recorded here. [@1] They also seized 206 zip-lock packages containing cocaine
weighing 104 grams, mentioned only to explain the mixed seizure and excluded
from cannabis figures. [@1] Officers seized 1,030 empty zip-lock bags. [@1]
Officers seized a precision scale. [@1] Officers seized a mobile phone. [@1]
Officers seized a Honda CG 150 motorcycle. [@1] Officers seized a security
camera. [@1] Officers seized various utensils used to prepare and refine drugs.
[@1]

Three Tactical teams supported the ROCAM team during the operation. [@1]
