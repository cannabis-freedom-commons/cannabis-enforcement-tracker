Kondhwa Police arrested three men in the Kondhwa area of Pune for allegedly
selling cannabis (ganja). [@1] The men were identified as 19-year-old Danish
Javed Khan, 19-year-old Kalpesh Vishal Kadam and 21-year-old Siddiqui Shakeel
Khatik. [@1] All three work as delivery workers, live in Shivnerinagar in
Kondhwa Khurd and originally come from Jalgaon district. [@1]

Police Constable Amol Hirve received a tip-off that two individuals were selling
cannabis from black bags on a public road in front of the MSEB office, NIBM
branch, near Mayfair Eleganza Society on NIBM Road in Kondhwa Khurd. [@1]
Officers went to the location and apprehended three suspects. [@1] Police
recovered 1.045 kg of cannabis from Khan and Kadam together and 1.054 kg from
Khatik, making a total seizure of 2.099 kg. [@1]

Police registered a case against the three at Kondhwa Police Station under Crime
Register No. 850/2026 under Sections 8(c) and 20(b)(ii)(B) of the Narcotic Drugs
and Psychotropic Substances Act. [@1] The action was carried out on 30 October
when a Kondhwa Police Station crime investigation team was patrolling to curb
drug trafficking and substance abuse. [@1] The operation was conducted under the
guidance of Pune Police Commissioner Amitesh Kumar, Joint Commissioner Sanjay
Darade, Additional Commissioner Manoj Patil, Deputy Commissioner Rajlaxmi
Shivankar and Assistant Commissioner Ramdas Ingawale. [@1] The Kondhwa Police
Station investigation team including Sub-Inspectors Ravindra Gawade and Sagar
Pawar led the operation with other personnel. [@1] Senior Police Inspector
Santosh Khetmalas of Kondhwa Police Station confirmed the action. [@1]
