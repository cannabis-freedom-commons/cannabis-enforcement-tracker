Military police in Perdigão arrested a 25-year-old man and a 29-year-old woman
on suspicion of drug trafficking. [@1] The incident was recorded on 29 October
during a patrol in the central area of the city after reports of drug sales.
[@1] When the man saw the officers approach, he tried to flee but was caught in
a vacant lot. [@1] Officers recovered a pouch he had discarded that held drugs
and money. [@1] Both suspects were arrested in flagrante and taken to the Civil
Police station together with the seized materials. [@1] The operation was
handled by soldiers of the 60th Battalion. [@1]

Police seized 14 marijuana tablets as the separately supported cannabis
component. [@1] The same seizure also included 75 crack rocks and two larger
portions of crack. [@1] Officers also seized six cocaine packets, which are not
cannabis and are excluded from cannabis figures. [@1] A precision scale, a
mobile phone, a pocketknife and an unspecified amount of money were also seized.
[@1] The patrol had begun after denunciations about the sale of narcotics in the
central region. [@1]
