U.S. Park Police officers executed a search warrant on K Street NE in Northeast
Washington, D.C. around 6:18 a.m. on Tuesday, 27 October 2026. [@1] Officers
took two people into custody during the search. [@1]

Police recovered three firearms and about one pound of marijuana, a weight of
about 0.45 kilograms. [@1] A photo released by U.S. Park Police shows a long
gun, a handgun, cash, a bag of marijuana and ammunition. [@1]

No identities for the two people taken into custody, the exact property searched
or any charges had been released. [@2] Hoodline reported that the makes, models
and calibers of the recovered firearms had also not been disclosed. [@2] Under
Washington, D.C. law, adults 21 and older may possess, use, purchase or
transport up to two ounces of marijuana. [@2]
