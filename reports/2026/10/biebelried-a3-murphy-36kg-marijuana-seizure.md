On 28 October 2026 the Bamberg Traffic Routes Control Unit of the Schweinfurt
Main Customs Office seized drugs during a vehicle check on the A3 near the
Biebelried interchange. [@2,3,4,6] Officers had pulled an Austrian-registered rental
car from moving traffic for the check. [@2,3,4,5,6] The 33-year-old Austrian driver had
said he carried no prohibited items, but officers found about 200 grams of
cocaine in a backpack on the passenger seat. [@2,5,6] The 33-year-old suspect was
placed in pretrial detention. [@2] The vehicle check took place on the A3 near
the Biebelried interchange. [@1]

Service dog Murphy alerted immediately in the boot and pinpointed a bag holding
the main haul of about 3,600 grams of marijuana divided into several. [@2,3,4,5,6] Those
bags were carefully sealed in film. [@2,5] A follow-up search at the Dettelbach
customs office found just under 200 grams of hashish, 12 grams of amphetamine
and accessories for consuming and portioning drugs. [@2,3,4,5,6]

Proceedings were opened against the suspect for suspected violations of the
Narcotics Act and the Cannabis Consumption Act. [@2,5] At the request of the
Würzburg public prosecutor an investigative judge issued an arrest warrant and
the suspect is in pretrial detention. [@2,4,5,6] The Munich Customs Investigation
Office now leads the further investigation. [@1,5,6] The suspect is presumed
innocent until finally convicted. [@2]
