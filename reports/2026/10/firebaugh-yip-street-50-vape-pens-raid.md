Members of the Firebaugh Police Department's Criminal Investigations and Warrant
Service Team served a residential search warrant in the 1700 block of Yip Street
in Firebaugh at about 7 a.m. on Tuesday, Oct. 27, 2026. [@1] During that search,
investigators seized approximately 50 cannabis vape products from a juvenile
suspected of selling the products to other juveniles in the community. [@1]

Firebaugh police seized about 50 cannabis vape products during the Tuesday
morning search warrant as part of an investigation into the illegal sale and
distribution of cannabis vape pens in the community. [@1] Police said the
investigation remains ongoing as officers work to identify additional people who
may be involved in the illegal distribution of cannabis products in Firebaugh.
[@1]

The department said it remains committed to protecting youth and addressing
illegal drug activity in connection with the case. [@1] It encouraged parents
and guardians to speak with their children about the dangers associated with
vaping and the use of unregulated cannabis products. [@1]
