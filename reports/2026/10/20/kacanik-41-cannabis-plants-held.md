Police arrested a man in Kaçanik on the evening of 20 October 2026 on suspicion of cultivating
and selling narcotics. [@1,2] The case began around 20:30 when officers stopped a vehicle in
Doganaj and later searched a home in Vatë. [@1,2]

Material evidence included 16.7 grams of suspected marijuana, 41 cannabis sativa plants with a
combined weight of 2,112.3 grams, a pair of scissors and a telephone. [@1,2] After questioning,
a prosecutor ordered the suspect held in police custody. [@1,2]
