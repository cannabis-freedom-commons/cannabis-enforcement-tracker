In Pakhtaabad district in Uzbekistan's Andijan region, officers detained two men
as they sold almost 2 kg of hashish to a conditional buyer, an undercover
participant used for a staged controlled purchase, for $18,000. [@1] The
suspects, born in 1988 and 1995, were caught red-handed at the moment of the
sale. [@1] Law enforcement accounts said the men actively resisted the officers
during the detention and attempted to flee the scene. [@2]

The joint operation was conducted by officers of the Border Troops of the State
Security Service (SGB), Uzbekistan's state security agency, together with the
SGB's regional department and the internal affairs department. [@1] Officers
seized 1 kg 971 g of hashish, the exact weight recorded for the consignment,
from the suspects. [@2] The sellers had offered that consignment to the
conditional buyer for $18,000. [@2]

A criminal case was opened against the detainees under Article 273 of
Uzbekistan's Criminal Code. [@2] The authorities remanded both men in custody as
a preventive measure. [@2] Investigative actions were still under way. [@1]
