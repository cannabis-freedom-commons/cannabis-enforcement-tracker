Four passengers who landed at Kempegowda International Airport in Bengaluru were
arrested for carrying more than 50 kg of hydroponic ganja. [@1,2] Customs
officials at KIA confirmed that all four passengers travelled with separate
tickets and did not have any visible links with each other. [@1] They boarded
Thai Airways flight TG 325 from Bangkok at 10pm on Thursday and reached KIA's
Terminal 2 shortly after midnight on Friday. [@1,2] Customs estimated the value of
the seized substance at more than Rs 17 crore. [@1]

The four were Badruddin Ansari, 45, a native of West Bengal; Ketan, 22, from
Jind in Haryana; Stephen Young, 49, a resident of Kolkata; and Thanveer Khan, a
native of Nanjangud in Mysuru district. [@1] Young carried the largest amount of
ganja, 15.7 kg in his check-in baggage, which customs valued at Rs 5.5 crore.
[@1,2] Thanveer carried the smallest amount, 10 kg, worth around Rs 3.5 crore.
[@1]

On Sunday, Shyam Motiramani, 44, a native of Bhavnagar in Gujarat who arrived on
the same flight, was caught with 9 kg of weed. [@1,2]
