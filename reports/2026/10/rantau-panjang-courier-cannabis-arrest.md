The General Operations Force apprehended a 30-year-old man in the parking area of the Rantau
Panjang Immigration, Customs, Quarantine and Security complex early on 27 October 2026. [@1,2,4]
The arrest is part of the investigation into a seizure two days earlier at a Line Clear Express
courier premises in Rantau Panjang, Pasir Mas, where officers found three kilogrammes of
cannabis, 56 grammes of cannabis flowers and 10 boxes of electronic cigarettes containing
cannabis oil. [@1,2,3]

The suspect pointed out a silver Toyota Camry parked five metres away, believed to have been
used for the deliveries, and said someone had contacted him to help send packages to the courier
outlet. [@1,2] A body search found nothing illegal on him, but he tested positive for
amphetamine; the car, valued at RM50,000, was seized. [@1,2,5] He was arrested under Section 39B
of the Dangerous Drugs Act 1952 and handed to the Pasir Mas district police narcotics unit for
further investigation. [@1,2]

Both suspects, aged 27 and 30, were detained in Op Taring Wawasan on Monday and in the early hours of the next morning. [@5] The 27-year-old first suspect was detained at Taman Bakti in Rantau Panjang. [@5] The first suspect said a Thai woman asked him to help send a parcel collected at the Gergaji illegal base. [@5] The first suspect said he received RM200 for the delivery arrangement. [@5] The seizure through Op Taring Wawasan Kelantan was estimated to be worth RM13,429. [@3] Two men were detained in Op Taring Wawasan following the drug seizure. [@6]
