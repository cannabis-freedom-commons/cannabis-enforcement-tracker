Murnau Schleierfahnder, veil-search officers, checked a 32-year-old man from
Moldova on the A95 near Ohlstadt. [@1] The control took place in the night to
Saturday. [@1] Officers arrested the man at the scene, and he was then in
pretrial detention. [@1] That same Saturday he was brought before the competent
judge, a pretrial-detention warrant was issued, and he was transferred to a
prison. [@1]

During the control officers found about 300 grams of hashish and 150 grams of
marijuana. [@1] Together the hashish and marijuana amounted to about 450 grams
of cannabis products. [@1] Officers had noticed the Skoda at about 1:15 while it
was travelling toward Munich. [@1] Police also seized a deceptively realistic
assault-rifle replica, described as a so-called Softair airsoft gun. [@1]
According to the police account, the man presented a forged Moldovan driving
licence and could show no valid driving licence. [@1]

The Garmisch-Partenkirchen criminal police station is investigating the case,
and the Munich II public prosecutor's office applied to the competent Local
Court for a detention warrant against the suspect. [@1]
