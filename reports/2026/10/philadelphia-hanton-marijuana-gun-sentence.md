Deon Hanton, 35, of Philadelphia, was sentenced to 15 years in federal prison
on 27 October 2026 for marijuana trafficking and firearms offences. [@1,2]
U.S. District Judge Kai N. Scott also ordered five years of supervised release
after the prison term. [@1,2] The sentence followed a jury trial in June 2026,
when Hanton was convicted on all three counts against him. [@1,2]

A federal grand jury had indicted Hanton in December 2024. [@1] The charges
were possessing marijuana with intent to distribute it, possessing a gun to
further drug trafficking, and possessing a firearm despite a felony conviction.
[@1,2] Prosecutors said trial evidence showed that he began trafficking
marijuana after serving state prison sentences for drug and other offences.
[@1,2] He was still on state parole and armed himself with a gun he was
prohibited from possessing. [@1,2]

The Federal Bureau of Investigation and Philadelphia Police Department
investigated the case. [@1,2] U.S. Attorney David Metcalf announced the
sentence, and federal prosecutors Branwen McNabb O'Donnell and Shannon Zabel
handled the prosecution. [@1]
