A police operation against drug trafficking ended with four people detained, the
Spanish-language outlet Qué Pasa reported. [@2] The Hoke County Sheriff's Office
said its Narcotics/Vice Suppression Unit served a search warrant at a home in
the 600 block of Saunders Street in Raeford, North Carolina, on 29 October 2026.
[@1] The warrant was served without incident by the unit, the sheriff's
emergency response team and North Carolina Alcohol Law Enforcement. [@1] The
sheriff's office said the search followed a long-term investigation into illegal
substances sold from the house, where residents were possibly selling fentanyl
and the house was possibly being used to sell alcohol illegally. [@1]

Christopher Bratcher, Shashana Clegg, Michael Belton and Timothy Hobson were
arrested and taken to the Hoke County Detention Center. [@1] A juvenile found in
the home was turned over to a grandmother before the search began. [@1] Travis
Sentell Bratcher, named as a primary suspect, was not at the property when the
warrant was served. [@1] The sheriff's office also obtained arrest warrants for
him. [@1] Inside, detectives found 15 grams of marijuana laced with suspected
methamphetamine. [@1] Christopher Bratcher was carrying 15 dosage units of
suspected crack cocaine. [@1] Detectives found 10 more dosage units of crack
cocaine prepackaged for sale inside the home. [@1] They also seized three
handguns, three shotguns, a bulletproof vest, digital scales, baggies and
marijuana paraphernalia. [@1]

Bratcher and Clegg were charged with possession with intent to sell or deliver
methamphetamine. [@1] Both also faced a marijuana-paraphernalia charge. [@1]
Belton and Hobson were charged with possession of a Schedule II controlled
substance, conspiracy and drug paraphernalia. [@1] Bratcher and Clegg were held
on $50,000 secured bonds, and Belton and Hobson on $15,000 each. [@1] The
sheriff's office said the investigation was continuing and that all the accused
are innocent until proven guilty. [@1]
