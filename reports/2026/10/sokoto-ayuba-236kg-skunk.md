## The arrest

Operatives of the NDLEA Zone 7 Command arrested 68-year-old Ibrahim Ayuba in Sokoto on Saturday, 31 October 2026. [@1,2,3,4,5,7] The agency said its officers acted on credible intelligence. [@1,2] Officers recovered 236.6 kilograms of skunk from him. [@1,2,4,6,7,8] Skunk is a strong strain of cannabis. [@2]

## The roundup it was announced in

The NDLEA announced the arrest in a statement issued on Sunday, 1 November 2026 by its Director of Media and Advocacy, Femi Babafemi. [@1,2] The statement covered intelligence-led operations in seven states: Lagos, Kano, Kaduna, Sokoto, Ondo, Oyo and Edo. [@1,2] The agency said its commands also sustained War Against Drug Abuse sensitisation activities in schools, worship centres, workplaces and communities. [@1]
