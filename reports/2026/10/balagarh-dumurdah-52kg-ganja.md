Two men were arrested in Balagarh, Hooghly district, West Bengal, after police
searched the car they were travelling in. [@1] The search took place on 30
October 2026 at the Dumurdah bus stand, in the area covered by the Balagarh
police station, and yielded a total of 52.11 kg of cannabis. [@1] Officers acted
on a tip-off and stopped a blue Hyundai i10 with registration WB 06 5668. [@1]
The cannabis was seized from inside the vehicle. [@1]

A case was registered against the pair under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic
Substances Act, India's narcotics law, and they were produced in court. [@1]
Police applied to the court for 14 days' police custody of the pair to allow the
investigation to proceed. [@1]

At a press conference on Saturday, Hooghly Rural Police said the campaign
against drug trafficking and the illegal trade would continue. [@1] The force
said the police administration was committed to building a drug-free society.
