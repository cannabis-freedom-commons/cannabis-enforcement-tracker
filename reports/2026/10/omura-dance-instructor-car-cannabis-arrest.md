Nagasaki police arrested the 25-year-old dance instructor at about 8:45 a.m. on
27 October 2026 on suspicion of violating the Narcotics and Psychotropics
Control Law by possessing dried cannabis. [@2,3] He was 25 years old and worked as
a dance instructor, living in Kiba 1-chome in Omura City. [@2] Police say he
possessed 0.2 grams of dried cannabis on 18 October 2026 inside a car stopped on
a street in Nagasaki City. [@2,3]

Officers noticed the car drifting to the inside of a curve, stopped it to give
traffic guidance and questioned the occupants, then found the dried cannabis in
the bag of the man sitting in the passenger seat. [@2] Two acquaintances of the
man were also in the car. [@2]

The man admitted the allegation, telling police he had the cannabis to smoke it
himself. [@2,3] Police said they would investigate how he obtained the dried
cannabis. [@2] The arrest was reported on 6 November 2026, more than a week
after it took place. [@1]

The car was on a city road in Oura-machi, Nagasaki City, and an officer on patrol stopped it for traffic guidance, finding the dried cannabis in its trunk. [@3]
