Papua police detained ten people and seized ten kilograms of cannabis in a raid on Kampung Vietnam in Jayapura. [@1,2,3,4]

On Saturday 24 October 2026, Papua police's narcotics directorate and the provincial narcotics
agency launched Operasi Sarang Narkoba 2026 in Kampung Vietnam, Argapura, South Jayapura district,
starting around 05:00 Eastern Indonesian Time. [@3,4] Brigadier General Anang Triwidiandoko of BNN
Papua joined the sweep with Brimob, water-police and Jayapura city officers. [@3,4]

Teams detained ten people, six of them Papua New Guinean nationals, and seized ten kilograms of
cannabis along with two air rifles, an airsoft gun, bladed weapons and alcohol. [@3,4] When one
suspect ran toward the sea, officers stopped him before he got away. [@3,4] Everyone arrested was
taken to Papua police headquarters for further questioning. [@3,4]

Pantau noted that separate raids earlier in the same operation had already netted twenty kilograms
of cannabis, but the Kampung Vietnam site itself accounted for the ten kilogram seizure and ten
detainees covered here. [@4]
