The case was decided by the New Taipei District Court. [@1] The judgment is
dated 7 October 2026. [@1] It sentenced Chen Yi-hsien to three months in prison,
suspended for two years, for transferring a prohibited drug. [@1] As a condition
of the suspension, he must pay NT$60,000 into the public treasury within six
months after the judgment becomes final. [@1] He may appeal within 20 days of
being served with the judgment. [@1]

Prosecutors said that on 25 April 2025 he bought five grams of cannabis from Li
Cheng-hui. [@1] The purchase was in Yonghe District, New Taipei City, at
NT$1,200 a gram. [@2] Their messages used the codes wine money, five bottles and
1/12 for five grams at NT$1,200 a gram. [@1] Between 11 and 13 May 2025 he met
Guo Nian-cheng somewhere in Kaohsiung and transferred an unspecified amount of
cannabis without payment. [@1]

Chen told the court he gave the cannabis free of charge because Guo played video
games for him and raised his rank. [@1] The Songshan Precinct of the Taipei City
Police Department reported the case. [@1] Prosecutors charged him over the
Pharmaceutical Affairs Act offence. [@1] He named Li during police questioning,
officers then caught Li and sent him for prosecution, and the court reduced the
sentence for that. [@1]

The court said he had disregarded the legal ban and that the quantity
transferred was not large. [@1] It noted that he was a university graduate
working as a police officer, from a modestly comfortable family, and that he
confessed and identified the supplier. [@1] He confessed during the
investigation and at trial, and the court also reduced the sentence because the
cannabis was both a prohibited drug and a Category 2 narcotic. [@1]
