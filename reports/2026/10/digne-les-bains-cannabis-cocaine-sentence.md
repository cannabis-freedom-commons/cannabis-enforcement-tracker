Five people were tried in immediate proceedings before the judicial court in
Digne-les-Bains, in Alpes-de-Haute-Provence, for involvement in drug
trafficking. [@1] Four of the defendants were convicted at the end of the
hearing. [@1] The case concerned cannabis and cocaine trafficking in
Alpes-de-Haute-Provence. [@1] The court sentenced a man in his thirties to ten
months of firm imprisonment as the principal defendant. [@1] Three other
defendants received suspended prison sentences. [@1] The fifth defendant was
acquitted. [@1]

The investigation had begun after an anonymous report on 12 August described
cannabis and cocaine trafficking in Digne-les-Bains. [@1] Police then set up
surveillance, including cameras and a drone, to identify the people involved.
[@1] That investigation led officers to the apartment of a woman in her fifties,
which served as a storage place. [@1] During a search carried out on 21
September, investigators found material for packaging drugs, cash and several
travel bags belonging to different occupants. [@1]

Some of the defendants came from Marseille or the Paris region. [@1] One of the
defendants admitted that the activity brought him about 100 euros of profit per
day. [@1]
