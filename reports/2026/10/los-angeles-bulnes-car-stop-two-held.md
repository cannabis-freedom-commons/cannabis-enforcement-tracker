Carabineros officers from the Foco patrol of the First Precinct in Los Ángeles
stopped a car at the 400 block of calle Bulnes after seeing it make an improper
manoeuvre. [@1] The stop happened at night while the unit was carrying out
preventive patrols around green areas and plazas in the city centre, Radio
Camila TV reported. [@2] When the occupants lowered their windows, the officers
smelled marijuana, and a search found transparent bags of marijuana, ketamine
and cocaine hydrochloride inside a handbag. [@1]

They arrested the two occupants: J.N.S.C., a 30-year-old man with a criminal
record, and Y.E.R.O., a 25-year-old woman with police records. [@1] Police
seized cash, which they said could be related to the eventual sale of the drugs,
and the car the pair were travelling in. [@1] Both were handed to the Public
Prosecutor's Office and taken to a detention-control hearing at the Juzgado de
Garantía, the guarantee court, to continue the judicial process. [@2]
