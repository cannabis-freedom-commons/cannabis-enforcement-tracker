Officers of Bizerte's regional anti-drug brigade, part of the anti-drug
sub-directorate based at El Gorjani, arrested two young men with criminal
records in the coastal town of Menzel Jemil. [@1] The pair were aged between 20
and 27 and had previous records for drug trafficking, Radio Oxygène FM reported.
[@2] Officers seized 40 slabs of cannabis, known in Tunisia as zatla, which
Diwan FM said weighed 4 kilograms. [@1]

Cigarettes were seized alongside the cannabis: more than 2,000 boxes according
to Diwan FM and 2,300 according to Radio Oxygène FM, some of them smuggled. [@2]
Diwan FM's security source said the public prosecutor authorised holding both
men for investigation, including anyone else the inquiry identifies, before they
are brought before it. [@1] Al-Chourouk reported that the anti-drug units acted
after surveillance and follow-up work and that the pair were later to be
referred to the public prosecutor with the seized items. [@3] Al-Chourouk said
the operation was part of efforts against drug dealing around schools, aimed at
cutting off distribution. [@3] Realités Online also reported that Bizerte's
regional anti-drug brigade arrested two traffickers with previous records in
Menzel Jemil and seized 40 slabs of cannabis from them. [@4]
