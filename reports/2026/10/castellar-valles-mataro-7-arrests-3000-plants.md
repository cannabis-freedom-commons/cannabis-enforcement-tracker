Spain's National Police together with Mataró's local police detained seven
people in an anti-drug operation in Barcelona province. [@1] The seven detainees
had been handed over to the courts and are accused of crimes against public
health, illegal possession of weapons, electricity fraud and belonging to a
criminal organisation. [@1] The National Police reported the results in a
statement on Monday. [@1]

Police sources cited by Europa Press said the operation took place on 20 October
in the municipality of Castellar del Vallès and the Can Canyamars housing area
in Mataró. [@2] Officers seized 3,000 marijuana plants. [@1,3] They also seized
one kilo of marijuana buds, two 9mm pistols with semi-jacketed ammunition, a
simulated pistol and an AK-47 rifle, five frequency jammers, two
knuckle-dusters, several extendable batons and a baseball bat, as well as 5,000
euros in cash, three vehicles, a motorbike and tools used in indoor plantations.
[@1,3]

Investigators believe the group grew marijuana in indoor facilities for later
distribution on the black market in eastern Europe. [@1,3]

The investigation was codenamed operation Bóveda and police said the network was active in several localities in Barcelona province. [@3] Most of those arrested are Spanish nationals, with one Uruguayan citizen and one Armenian citizen among the seven detainees. [@3]
