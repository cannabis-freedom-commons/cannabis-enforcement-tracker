A 24-year-old man was arrested in the Caratoíra neighbourhood of Vitória,
Espírito Santo, on the afternoon of Saturday 31 October 2026, with a submachine
gun and drugs. [@1] The Military Police said the semi-industrial weapon was
loaded and ready for use. [@1] Officers of the 1st Battalion were patrolling an
area the force calls a drug sales point when they saw the man enter a house with
a gun in one hand and a bag in the other. [@1] When he noticed the police
approach, he ignored the order to stop and threw the submachine gun and the bag
into a neighbouring property's yard; the officers caught and stopped him. [@2]
With the resident's permission, the officers entered the yard and recovered the
material. [@1]

Besides the weapon, officers seized two magazines and a precision scale. [@1] He
and the seized items were taken to the 1st Regional Police Station of Vitória.
[@1]

It was the second submachine gun seized in Caratoíra in less than a week. [@1]
On Monday 26 October, the Tactical Force of the same battalion arrested a
23-year-old in the neighbourhood with a semi-industrial submachine gun, two
magazines holding 39 intact .380 rounds and two mobile phones, the Espírito
Santo public security secretariat said. [@1] The two cases involve different men
and different seizures. [@1] Brazil's drug law 11.343/2006 sets five to 15 years
in prison for trafficking, while illegal possession of a weapon carries two to
four years, or three to six if it is classed as restricted use. [@1]
