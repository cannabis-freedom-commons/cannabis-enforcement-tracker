Patrol officers from the Szczecin Pogodno station tried to stop a passenger car
for a road check, but the driver ignored their signals and kept driving, so
officers began a pursuit through Szczecin. [@1] Officers from the Pogodno
station together with the Prevention and Intelligence units of Szczecin’s city
police command stopped the car, which contained the driver and a passenger. [@2]
A check found that the car was not cleared for road use and that the driver had
no driving licence and was driving under the influence of an intoxicant. [@1]
Evidence gathered allowed officers to charge the driver with offences including
failing to stop for a road check while driving under the influence and illegally
possessing intoxicants, and he was placed under police supervision. [@2]

During further steps in the vehicle, officers found plant material that testing
confirmed was marijuana, with no weight published. [@1] Police said the driver
will answer for all the acts he committed and reminded drivers to obey orders
given during road checks. [@1]
