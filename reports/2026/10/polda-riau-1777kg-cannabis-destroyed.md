Riau provincial police destroyed drug evidence from four international-network
cases uncovered during Operasi Sarang Narkoba (OSN), its anti-drug operation.
[@1] Ten people were named as suspects in those cases. [@1] Investigators
described the suspects as alleged controllers and couriers who received or
collected narcotics in Dumai and Bengkalis for distribution in Riau. [@1]
Investigators said they were continuing to develop the cases to find other
suspects and trace the wider network. [@1]

The evidence destroyed totalled 100.55 kilograms. [@1] It comprised 82.79 kg of
sabu-sabu (methamphetamine) and 17.77 kg of ganja (cannabis). [@1] Police
estimated the combined haul was worth about Rp149.73 billion. [@1] Riau police
chief Inspector General Arman Achdiat announced the destruction in Pekanbaru on
28 October 2026, calling it a commitment to eradicating narcotics trafficking
and protecting the public. [@1]

The four cases were investigated by the provincial drug-investigation
directorate with Kampar and Kuantan Singingi district police, with support from
the military, the Riau narcotics agency, Riau customs and the public. [@1]
Before destruction the evidence was examined by the Riau police forensic
laboratory, then destroyed by machine after officers signed the destruction
record. [@1]
