Two males were arrested by police in Regional Division 6 (East Berbice-Corentyne) after
they were caught with more than two kilograms of cannabis during a mobile patrol on
Saturday 17 October 2026 at about 2pm along Crabwood Creek Public Road, Corentyne,
Berbice. [@1]

Officers saw an 18-year-old cane harvester from Grant 1806, Crabwood Creek, placing a
brown salt bag into a motor car driven by a 20-year-old technical assistant from No. 72
Village. [@1] The driver sped off when he saw the police and threw the bag onto the
shoulder of the road; officers retrieved it and intercepted the car. [@1]

A search of the bag in the presence of both males found three bulky parcels of leaves,
seeds and stems suspected to be cannabis. [@1] Both were escorted to Springlands Police
Station, where the suspected cannabis was weighed in their presence at 2.399 kilograms
before being lodged, and both remained in custody as investigations continue. [@1]
