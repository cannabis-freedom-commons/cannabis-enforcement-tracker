A 28-year-old man was arrested for drug trafficking during a police approach in
the Navegantes neighbourhood of Encantado, in Rio Grande do Sul, southern
Brazil, on the afternoon of Tuesday 27 October 2026. [@1] The Brigada Militar,
the state military police, found an open arrest warrant issued by the courts of
Ceará, a state in north-eastern Brazil, against him. [@2]

Officers seized 98 rocks of a substance similar to crack, 20 portions of cocaine
and eight portions of marijuana, 126 drug units in all according to Força do
Vale. [@2] Eight of them were marijuana; the rest were crack and cocaine. [@1]
Police also took three mobile phones and R$224 in cash. [@2]

He was taken for medical care and then presented at the police station with the
seized material. [@2] The 22nd Military Police Battalion handled the occurrence
as part of operations against drug trafficking and the service of arrest
warrants in the region. [@2]
