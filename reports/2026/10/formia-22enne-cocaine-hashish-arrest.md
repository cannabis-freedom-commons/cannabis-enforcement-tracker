Carabinieri arrested a 22-year-old man from Formia on suspicion of possessing
drugs for dealing. [@1] The arrest happened during a night territorial control
by the Radiomobile Section of the Formia Company. [@1] The young man, identified
as C.G. born in 2004, was already known to police and was under an order to
report to the judicial police. [@1]

Officers found just over two grams of cocaine divided into four wraps, a small
amount of hashish, a precision scale and plastic material for packaging doses.
[@1] All the drugs and materials were seized. [@1]

The Cassino prosecutor requested validation of the arrest and application of a
precautionary measure. [@1] The case remains preliminary and the young man is
presumed innocent until a final conviction. [@1]
