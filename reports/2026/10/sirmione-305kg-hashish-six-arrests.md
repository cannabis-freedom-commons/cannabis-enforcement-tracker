Police arrested six people, including a married couple aged 73 and 70 who had no
previous convictions, after finding hashish stored in a cellar and a flat in
Sirmione on Lake Garda. [@1] Officers of Bologna's Flying Squad, working with
the Brescia Flying Squad and the Desenzano del Garda police station, seized
about 420 kg of hashish across the whole operation. [@1] Police put the
hashish's market value at about 5 million euros. [@1]

The inquiry began in Bologna with two brothers living in Anzola dell'Emilia
suspected of dealing hashish. [@1] On 6 October officers followed one brother to
a meeting with a 21-year-old Moldovan driving a Fiat Talento, who hit police
cars while trying to flee before he was stopped. [@1] Inside the van, under a
false wooden shelf, police found 101.15 kg of hashish, and one of those stopped
carried a backpack with banknotes marked as 63,000 euros. [@1] A set of keys
with a remote control found in the van, and video from surveillance cameras
showing the vehicle had earlier entered Sirmione, led officers to an automatic
gate that opened the store where the rest was kept. [@1] The search of a cellar
and a flat in Sirmione turned up a further 305 kg of hashish, and police said
the arrested couple had acted as custodians of the store. [@1] At the brothers'
home in Anzola dell'Emilia, officers found 5.794 kg of hashish and 5,760 euros
in cash. [@1]

Police arrested the six people for joint possession of drugs intended for
dealing. [@2] All six arrested people were taken to prison to await a validation
hearing before an investigating judge. [@1] The head of Bologna police, Gaetano
Bonaccorso, said 700 kg of drugs seized in a short time should prompt reflection
on the scale of drug trafficking and dealing. [@3]
