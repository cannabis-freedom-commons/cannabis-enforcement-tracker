Officers of the Corrientes provincial police and the Gendarmería Nacional,
Argentina's military-status border police, stopped a car carrying marijuana near
Laguna Totora in San Cosme, Corrientes, and detained the three men inside. [@1]
The operation began when Gendarmes tried to identify the occupants of the car at
a roadside check at kilometre 1,065 of National Route 12, and the men refused
and fled. [@1] Police from San Cosme and Paso de la Patria joined a closing
operation, combing side roads and thick scrubland before finding the car and its
driver near the lagoon and catching the other two on foot. [@1]

At the base of the San Cosme section of Gendarmería's Escuadrón 48, a field test
came back positive for marijuana and the load weighed 15.559 kilograms. [@1] El
Litoral headlined the haul as 25 kilos, while its text and the other accounts
give 15.559 kilograms. [@1]

The three men were placed at the disposal of the federal court, which must
establish where the load came from and where it was going, and the car was
seized with it. [@2] Police said the load apparently came from Itatí, in
Corrientes province, and that the action fell under Plan Paraná, a programme to
strengthen security and fight complex crime on riverside areas and key roads.
