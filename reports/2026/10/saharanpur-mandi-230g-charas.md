Officers at Mandi police station in Saharanpur, Uttar Pradesh, arrested four men
in separate actions on Thursday 29 October 2026. [@1] Two of them, Aditya alias
Sunny and Usman, were stopped on Shaklapuri road near a mango orchard. [@2]
Police said the operation recovered 230 grams of charas, a cannabis resin. [@2]
Aditya carried 110 grams and Usman 120 grams, police said. [@2]

A third man, Mohammad Ahmad, was arrested near a cemetery on old Kalsia road
with 17 grams of smack, a street name for heroin. [@2] Hindustan reported that a
fourth man, Ravi, was arrested on Babalal Das road near the cemetery. [@1] An
illegal knife was recovered from him. [@1] A case was registered against Aditya
and Usman at Mandi police station under the NDPS Act, India's main anti-drug
law. [@2] A case was also registered against Mohammad Ahmad under the NDPS Act.
[@2]

Police said the three drug suspects already faced 16 cases between them. [@2]
Aditya had four cases, including theft and NDPS cases, Usman five under the NDPS
and Arms Act, and Mohammad Ahmad seven between 2018 and 2026, police said. [@2]
Ravi also had theft and Arms Act cases, Hindustan reported. [@1] Police said
they were investigating where the drugs came from and whom the men supplied.
[@2]
