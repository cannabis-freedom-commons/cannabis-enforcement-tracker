Two men, aged 23 and 29, were arrested for drug trafficking on the afternoon of
Saturday 31 October 2026 in Rio Verde de Mato Grosso. [@1] A Military Police
motor-patrol team made the arrests during ostensive, preventive patrolling
through the Jardim dos Estados neighbourhood. [@1] Officers spotted a man
leaving a residence on a Honda Biz motorcycle. [@1] He appeared nervous when he
noticed the police team approaching. [@1]

A search of the motorcycle turned up 100 grams of a substance described as
analogous to maconha, divided into ten portions. [@1] When asked where the drug
had come from, the man told officers he had picked it up at another person's
residence. [@1] Police searched the indicated house and found a further 555
grams of maconha-like substance pressed in tablet form, along with a precision
scale. [@1] Altogether, police seized 655 grams of maconha, one precision scale,
the Honda Biz motorcycle and one cell phone. [@1]

Both men were arrested in flagrante and taken with the seized materials to the
Civil Police station for the applicable legal steps. [@1]
