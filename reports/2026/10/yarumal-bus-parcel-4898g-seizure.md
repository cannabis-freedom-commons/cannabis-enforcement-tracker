At 8:00 in the morning of 29 October 2026, uniformed officers of the Seccional
de Tránsito y Transporte, the Traffic and Transport Section, found and seized
marijuana in Yarumal during prevention, control and registration checks on
people and vehicles. [@1] The officers were carrying out routine roadside
prevention and control work, checking people and vehicles moving through the
Yarumal area. [@1] The marijuana was being moved as an encomienda, a parcel
consignment, inside a public-service bus. [@1] That bus was covering the
scheduled route between Medellín and Caucasia. [@1]

Officers reported the seizure as 4,898 grams of marijuana, about 4.898
kilograms. [@1] Police estimated the value of that marijuana at three million
Colombian pesos. [@1] Officers then placed the seized substance at the disposal
of the Fiscalía Seccional de Yarumal, the sectional prosecutor’s office in
Yarumal, for the corresponding judicial proceedings. [@1] The officers recorded
the case as a find and seizure. [@1]
