Police in Dzerzhinsk, Nizhny Novgorod Oblast, detained a 38-year-old local man
in mid-October on suspicion of illegal drug circulation. [@2] The detention took
place near one of the city's stadiums, and the police said the man had no
previous convictions. [@2] A personal search found a paper wrap containing plant
material, which police seized. [@2]

A laboratory examination identified the contents as marijuana weighing 0.18
grams in total. [@2] The man told officers he had kept the drug for his own use
and had carried it in his clothing. [@4]

Officers drew up an administrative protocol against him under part 1 of article
6.8 of the Administrative Code, which covers acquiring, storing, transporting,
making or processing drugs without intent to sell. [@2] That offence carries a
fine of up to 5,000 roubles or administrative arrest of up to 15 days. [@2] The
drug was seized and the man was made subject to an administrative protocol, the
regional police said. [@3]

The regional police statement also said that carrying banned substances leads to
immediate detention, liability, registration at a drug-treatment dispensary and
loss of the right to drive. [@2] The case came from the press service of the
Nizhny Novgorod regional interior ministry directorate on information from the
Dzerzhinsk city police department. [@2] The Dzerzhinsk television site
attributed the account to the regional interior ministry's press service. [@1]
