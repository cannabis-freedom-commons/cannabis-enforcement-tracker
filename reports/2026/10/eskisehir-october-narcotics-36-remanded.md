Police and gendarmerie teams in Eskişehir handled 241 suspects in 183 drug
incidents between 1 and 31 October 2026, the provincial governorate said. [@1]
Judicial authorities remanded 36 of the suspects in pre-trial detention, and 205
were released, according to the governorate's monthly figures. [@2]

Officers seized 1,709.16 grams of esrar, the cannabis preparation known in
Turkey. [@1] They also found 24 cannabis roots. [@1] The same operations yielded
39,636 synthetic pharmaceutical pills, 9,056 grams of methamphetamine and 2,055
grams of cocaine. [@1] Officers further seized 728.81 grams of bonsai, a
synthetic cannabinoid, 74 grams of bonsai raw material, 22 ecstasy pills and
5.31 grams of opium gum. [@1] Nine precision scales, three drug-use implements,
a handgun, a blank-firing pistol and 64 cartridges were also confiscated. [@1]

The provincial police directorate and the provincial gendarmerie command carried
out the operations, the governorate said. [@2] The figures are for the whole
city, not a single operation. [@1] The governorate said the anti-drug work was
continuing across the city without interruption. [@1]
