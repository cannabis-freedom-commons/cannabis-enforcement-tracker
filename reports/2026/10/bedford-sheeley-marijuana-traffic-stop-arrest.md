Ryan Sheeley, 48, of Springville was arrested in Bedford on the night of 23
October 2026 after a traffic stop led to a K-9 sweep and the discovery of drugs
including marijuana. [@1] He faces preliminary charges of possession of
methamphetamine, possession of a narcotic, possession of paraphernalia and
possession of marijuana. [@1] Bedford Police Officer Sarah Haluda began the stop
at about 910 pm on Oct 23 after seeing a dark Chevrolet SUV travelling west on
16th Street from R Street with only fog lights illuminated while tailgating
another vehicle. [@1] The SUV carried a plate registered to a 2019 Kia SUV,
which Sheeley said belonged to his girlfriend's Kia and was fictitious, and the
vehicle came to rest in a Sunoco gas station parking lot near 16th Street and
Hillcrest Road. [@1]

Mitchell Police Officer Kuhlman and K-9 partner Vader then conducted a free-air
sniff around the vehicle that produced a primary alert for narcotics. [@1]
Confronted with that alert, Sheeley said officers would find a glass pipe in the
driver's side door pocket that he had used the previous day to ingest
methamphetamine. [@1] The search found a glass smoking device with residue in
the driver's door pocket, a small baggie of crystal-like substance above a torch
lighter in the centre console, a wooden container with plant-like substance, a
lockbox with marijuana nuggets and another smoking device, plus a grinder and
other smoking devices. [@1] Field tests on the crystal-like substance were
positive for methamphetamine and fentanyl, while the plant material tested
positive for marijuana. [@1]

Sheeley's juvenile son was a passenger in the vehicle and was picked up from the
scene by a relative, while the vehicle was impounded by Stone City Collision.
[@1] Sheeley was taken to the Bedford Police Department, agreed to field
sobriety tests that showed no substantial signs of impairment, and was then
booked into the Lawrence County Jail. [@1] Everyone named is presumed innocent
until proven guilty, with the account based on the probable-cause affidavit
rather than a final determination of guilt. [@1]
