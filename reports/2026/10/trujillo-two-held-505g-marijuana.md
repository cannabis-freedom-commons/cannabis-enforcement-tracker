A 73-year-old man and a 46-year-old man were arrested in Trujillo. [@1] Police
accused them of illicit drug trafficking over the apparent sale of drugs. [@1]
The two had marijuana and a digital scale with them when they were detained.
[@1] Officers of Grupo Terna, a police unit, made the arrests. [@1]

Police accuse the men of belonging to a band known as 'Los Marihuanos de Buenos
Aires', or the Buenos Aires marijuana men. [@1] Officers seized 505 grams — just
over half a kilogram — of marijuana during the intervention. [@1] Police
described the weight as approximate. [@1] Cash was among the items seized. [@1]

The force said it was running interventions in flagrante — cases in which
suspects are caught in the act — for various crimes. [@1] The operations go
through a Comando Unificado, a unified police command. [@1] It said the
operations had produced important operational results. [@1] It said they had
also contributed to a significant reduction in crime in the region. [@1]
