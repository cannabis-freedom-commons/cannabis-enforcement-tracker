Guardia Civil Estatal, San Luis Potosí's state civil guard, arrested two men
described as priority targets in Matehuala and Ciudad Fernández. [@2,3] The force
said both were suspected of criminal activity that affects families' safety and
property. [@1,4]

In Matehuala, officers detained Héctor N., 40, with about twelve small bags of
marijuana, 15 ponchallantas — metal spikes used to puncture vehicle tyres — and
a vehicle. [@2,3] Intelligence reports linked him to extortion, cobro de piso
(protection payments) and drug distribution, possibly for a criminal group,
Pulso reported. [@2]

In a separate operation in Ciudad Fernández, officers detained Ricardo N., 42,
who is from the State of Mexico and has prior police records. [@2,3] He was
carrying 320 marijuana cigarettes, the force said. [@1,3] The force called him a
priority target over suspected drug sales and distribution. [@2]

The Guardia Civil Estatal said it maintains an operational presence in all four
regions of San Luis Potosí. [@1,3] It said the detentions contributed to its fight
against extortion and narcomenudeo, the small-scale sale of drugs. [@2]

The Guardia Civil Estatal said both men and the seized items were placed at the disposal of the competent authorities, which would determine their legal situation. [@3]
