A joint team from the Sûreté du Québec and the Sécurité publique de Mashteuiatsh
searched a residence on rue Renaud in Mashteuiatsh. [@1] The operation was aimed
at combating trafficking in illegal substances in the region. [@1] One person
was questioned during the operation. [@1] Police said they were considering
bringing drug-trafficking charges in connection with the search. [@1]

During the search officers found what was described as a significant quantity of
drugs. [@1] The seized substances included cocaine, methamphetamine tablets and
cannabis in different forms, including edible cannabis. [@1] Officers also
seized magic mushrooms and cash amounting to nearly 3,000 dollars. [@1]

The operation was presented as part of a wider effort to counter the circulation
of illicit drugs in the area, an issue of concern to both the authorities and
the local community. [@1] The report presents the results as showing the
importance of cooperation between police forces for residents’ safety and for
reducing drug-related crime. [@1]
