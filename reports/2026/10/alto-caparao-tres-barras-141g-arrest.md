Military Police arrested a 21-year-old man for drug trafficking on Saturday 31
October 2026 during a patrol in Córrego Três Barras in the rural zone of Alto
Caparaó. [@1,2] Officers saw the man riding a motorcycle, and when he noticed them
he tried to flee but lost control of the vehicle and fell. [@1,2] He then tried to
hide by a river and officers found him under a bridge, where a search recovered
drugs, money and mobile phones. [@1] After the arrest he was taken to the Civil
Police station, where the duty delegate handled the procedures. [@1]

Officers seized about 126 grams of marijuana and 15 grams of hashish, the
separately supported cannabis component totalling about 141 grams. [@1,2] The same
seizure also yielded 43 ecstasy tablets and about 3 grams of cocaine, which are
not cannabis and are excluded from the cannabis figure. [@1] Police also
collected R$364 in cash and two mobile phones alongside the drugs. [@1,2]

The encounter began as a routine patrol sighting rather than a planned raid on a
house or plantation. [@1]
