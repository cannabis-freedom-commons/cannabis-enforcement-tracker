The Tarkwa Circuit Court, presided over by Ms Bernice Mensimah Ackon,
convicted 20-year-old labourer Kojo Sah Nkrumah on his guilty plea to two
counts of possessing and selling narcotic drugs without lawful authority.
[@1,2] The court fined him GH¢120,000, with three years in prison in default
of payment. [@1,2]

Nkrumah, a resident of Tarkwa Railway Station, was arrested at about 1:30 am
on 21 January 2025 during a routine night patrol by the Western Central
Regional Formed Police Unit, led by Chief Inspector Issahaque Yahaya. [@1]
Officers found him under a shed at the station selling from a takeaway bag
holding 81 wrapped portions of dried leaves suspected to be cannabis, seven
sachets of tramadol and GH¢240 in cash believed to be sales proceeds. [@1,2]

The exhibits went to the Forensic Crime Laboratory in Accra on 25 April 2025,
and its 13 May 2025 report confirmed the plant material was cannabis,
weighing 139.61 grams gross, testing positive for delta-9-tetrahydrocannabinol.
[@1] Assistant Superintendent of Police Samuel Ahiabor prosecuted the case.
