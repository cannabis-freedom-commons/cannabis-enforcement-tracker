Khalilabad Kotwali police arrested two men from Gorakhpur on the night of 8
October 2026 and seized charas from them. [@1] Hindustan reported that the
arrests were made on 8 October by a police team under Kant outpost in-charge
Prabhanshu Kumar Rai. [@2] Officers stopped a motorcycle coming from the
Gorakhpur side at the Pipra Kala turn, the riders tried to flee, and the team
surrounded and caught them. [@1] Police identified the two as Kuldeep Shahi, who
was riding, and Himanshu Singh, who was on the pillion; both live in Gorakhpur
district. [@1] The two arrested men were Kuldeep Shahi, a resident of Saade village in Gorakhpur's Gagaha police station area, and Himanshu Singh, a resident of Melandri village in the Khajni police station area. [@4]

The search found two packets of brown solid material wrapped in black tape
inside a khaki plastic bag, weighing 1 kg 45 g, along with two mobile phones and
200 rupees. [@1] Amar Ujala gives the amount in its lead as 1.045 kg of charas,
and Hindustan and Dainik Bhaskar also give 1 kg 45 g. [@1] Police took a 50 g
sample and sealed the rest of the charas. [@1] Police seized the motorcycle used
in the smuggling, which Dainik Bhaskar identifies as a Hero HF Deluxe. [@3]

A first information report was registered against both under the Narcotic Drugs
and Psychotropic Substances Act and they were sent to court, which sent them to
district jail in judicial custody. [@1,4] Hindustan reported that the court sent
both to jail. [@2] Station house officer Jaiprakash Dubey said the two told
police that Umang alias Suryadev Singh, a resident of Madrah in Gorakhpur's
Barhalganj police station area, had given them the charas. [@1,4] They said they
were taking the charas to sell to a man near Basti railway station and that the
proceeds would be split between the three. [@1] Police said they would
investigate the source of the supply and the role of others involved, and did
not say whether Umang had been arrested. [@1] Police are gathering information on Umang's role and the network behind the smuggling and have said they expect to arrest him soon. [@4] Khalilabad police have intensified the search for Umang and are raiding places he may be staying, on the basis of information from the two arrested men. [@4] Police said the charas was of the
quality sold as Malana cream or Kashmiri charas and valued it at 1 crore to 2.5
crore rupees on the international market. [@1] The police account of the search was attributed to Priyam Rajashekhar Pandey, circle officer of Khalilabad. [@4] Police were also gathering information about other people linked to the supply and sale of the charas. [@4]
