The National Police said 28 people were detained during a preventive operation
in several sectors of La Vega province on 30 October 2026. [@1] Officers
searched 510 people, 215 vehicles and 179 motorcycles, and kept one vehicle and
ten motorcycles. [@1]

The police reported seizing 108 portions of suspected marijuana and eight
knives. [@1] Officers also took RD$400 in cash, three speakers and three devices
known locally as kitipos. [@1] No weight was given for the marijuana, and the
statement reported no laboratory analysis to confirm what the substance was.
[@1] The police presented the deployment as part of its prevention and
surveillance work with the armed forces to keep order in La Vega. [@2]

The operation included checks in what the police called vulnerable sectors, in
pensions, meaning cheap guesthouses, and in lodging establishments chosen for
inspection. [@2] Police also reported identifying 11 foreign nationals, without
saying whether they were among the detainees. [@1] The statement did not say why
each of the 28 was detained, how many were released or sent to the Public
Ministry, or whether anyone was charged. [@1]
