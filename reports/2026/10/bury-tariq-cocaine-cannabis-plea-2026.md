Qasim Tariq, 38, of Gigg Lane, appeared at Manchester Crown Court on October 30.
[@1] Court records show Tariq pleaded guilty to possessing a quantity of Class A
cocaine with intent to supply and possessing a quantity of Class B cannabis with
intent to supply. [@1] The offences are alleged to have taken place in Bury on
August 20. [@1] Greater Manchester Police had earlier said Tariq, then described
as of Brandlesholme Road, Bury, had been charged with possession with intent to
supply Class A and Class B drugs. [@2]

A separate charge, brought under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002, alleges that on
August 26 he acquired, used or possessed criminal property, namely £12,500 in
cash. [@1] No plea has yet been recorded in relation to that allegation. [@1]
Tariq also faces allegations of dangerous driving, driving while disqualified
and driving without insurance. [@1] The driving charges allege he drove a grey
Nissan Qashqai on Brandlesholme Road in Bury while disqualified from holding a
driving licence and without valid insurance. [@1] A further allegation relates
to the dangerous driving of the same vehicle on Rollesby Close. [@1]

Following the hearing, Tariq was remanded in custody. [@1] The case was
adjourned until November 27 for a further case management hearing at Manchester
Crown Court. [@1] Court records state prosecutors are considering whether to
proceed with some of the remaining allegations in light of the guilty pleas
already entered. [@1] At the charging stage he had been remanded in custody for
appearance at Manchester and Salford Magistrates Court on August 28, 2026. [@2]
