Deputies arrested 27-year-old Shane Dalton Kellam of Exmore. [@1] He was charged
with possession of a handgun in a vehicle, possession of cannabis with intent to
distribute, and related charges. [@1] Kellam was arrested and transported to the
Wicomico County Detention Center for processing. [@1] He was released after
posting an unsecured personal bond of $5,000. [@1]

Deputies seized approximately 145.59 grams of suspected marijuana, digital
scales, and two cellular phones from the vehicle. [@1] During the investigation,
deputies observed drug-related paraphernalia and discovered a loaded Glock
.45-caliber handgun inside the vehicle. [@1] Maryland State Police and a WCSO
K-9 unit assisted in the search of the vehicle. [@1]

At 3:35 p.m. on Oct. 29, a Wicomico deputy observed a white Lincoln Town Car
make a left turn across multiple lanes of traffic, nearly causing a collision.
[@1] The traffic stop occurred on North Salisbury Boulevard and Dagsboro Road in
Salisbury, Maryland. [@2] Selling cannabis without a license remains against the
law. [@2]
