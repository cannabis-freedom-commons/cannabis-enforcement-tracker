Saudi Arabia's General Directorate of Narcotics Control arrested two Yemeni
residents in Jeddah governorate for promoting narcotic substances. [@1] Officers
arrested a Moroccan woman visitor in the same Jeddah operation on the same
promotion allegation. [@1] The three suspects were detained and had the
regulatory procedures taken against them. [@1] They were then referred to the
Public Prosecution. [@1]

The seizure in the case included 1.9 kilograms of hashish, the cannabis
component recorded here. [@1] The same seizure also included cocaine. [@1] It
also included methamphetamine, which the directorate parenthetically identified
as shabu. [@1] It also included tablets subject to medical circulation controls.
[@1] No separate weight or count was published for the cocaine, methamphetamine
or tablets, so only the hashish weight is recorded as cannabis. [@1]

Jeddah governorate is the named place of both the arrests and the seizure. [@1]
The directorate that made the arrests is Saudi Arabia's anti-narcotics
enforcement agency. [@1] The three suspects were processed together as one
promotion case. [@1] The alleged conduct in the case was promotion of narcotic
substances. [@1]
