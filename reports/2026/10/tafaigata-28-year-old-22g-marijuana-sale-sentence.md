Tinofili Perenise, 28, pleaded guilty to possession of marijuana and possession
of a utensil used for consuming drugs. [@1] Perenise received one year and ten
months in prison for possession of marijuana and three months for possession of
the utensil to be served concurrently, making one year and ten months in total.
[@1] Justice Leiataualesa Daryl Clarke told Perenise he was a drug dealer who
sold marijuana to fund his addiction. [@1] Perenise admitted that the proceeds
from selling marijuana supported his drug addiction. [@1] Justice Clarke warned
that anyone entering the business of selling narcotics, including marijuana,
should expect a stern response from the courts. [@1]

Police searched a property at Tafaigata in June, where Perenise was with four
other men. [@1] During the search, officers found a bag on top of a box
containing 45 marijuana cigarettes wrapped in foil weighing a total of 22.6
grams. [@1] A glass pipe was also found inside the bag. [@1] One occupant told
police the bag belonged to him, but the marijuana cigarettes belonged to
Perenise. [@1]

The court treated the quantity of 45 cigarettes or 22.6 grams and the commercial
intention behind the offending as aggravating factors. [@1] The prosecution
sought a starting point of three years imprisonment. [@1] Justice Clarke said
imprisonment was warranted to protect the community and to deter Perenise and
others from similar offending. [@1] The court took into account his early guilty
plea, personal circumstances and remorse, including the remorse he showed when
addressing the court. [@1] Justice Clarke declined defence counsel’s request for
an alternative sentence given the circumstances of the offending. [@1]
Perenise’s mother was given an opportunity to speak before sentencing and
pleaded with the court on behalf of her son. [@1]
