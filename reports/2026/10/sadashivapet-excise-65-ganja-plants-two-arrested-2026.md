Excise officers raided a private patta field on survey number 206 at Konapur, in
Sadashivapet mandal of Sangareddy district, and seized 55 cannabis plants being
cultivated there, Disha Daily reported. [@2] In a second raid the officers
searched the house of Goundla Vitthal Goud at Ankenapalli village and seized 10
cannabis plants and 150 grams of dried ganja. [@2] The two raids together
yielded 65 cannabis plants and 150 grams of dried ganja, and excise officers
registered cases against two suspects, Sakshi reported. [@3]

Sangareddy district excise officer K. Srinivasa Rao said both men were arrested
and sent to judicial remand with cases registered against them. [@2] Disha Daily
named the cultivator at Konapur as Mohammed Khadeer, and said a case was
registered against him and he was sent to remand. [@2] Medak division deputy
commissioner of excise Chandrayya confirmed that the Ankenapalli suspect was
also remanded. [@3] ClickTelugu reported that the Konapur raid took place on a
private patta plot and that after the two cases were registered the suspects
were sent to judicial remand. [@4]

Srinivasa Rao warned that anyone found growing cannabis on patta land would lose
the state's Rythu Bharosa farmer support scheme. [@2]

Eenadu, reporting the same Sadashivapet raids in a later district roundup, gave
the Ankenapalli seizure as 15 plants and 150 grams of dry ganja, named the
grower as Vitthal Goud, and said the two incidents took place on the 27th. [@1]
Excise district officer Srinivasa Rao said his department's aim was to curb
cannabis cultivation and transport, using a three-tier security system. [@1]
