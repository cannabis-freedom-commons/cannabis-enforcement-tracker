A joint municipal–Carabineros vehicle patrol in Estación Central stopped a car
on Calle Arica after spotting it speeding through a bus-only lane. [@1] The
driver produced only his driving licence when inspectors asked for the vehicle's
documents. [@1] After smelling a strong odour of marijuana, inspectors searched
the car and found large 'matutero' bags of cannabis sativa in the rear of the
vehicle. [@1] The inspectors called in Carabineros, who attended the scene and
removed the driver and the vehicle to continue the police procedure. [@1] The
man detained is an adult Dominican national identified only as M.A.V., whose
documents had expired and who was in Chile in an irregular migration situation.
[@1]

Carabineros' specialised OS7 unit confirmed by field testing that the substance
was cannabis sativa and weighed 51 kilograms and 890 grams. [@1] The cannabis
was divided and concealed in the car in 50 brick-shaped packages. [@1] Officers
also seized two mobile phones and placed them at the authorities' disposal for
the investigation. [@1]

The prosecution service ordered the arrested man to a detention-control hearing,
and the cannabis was formally handed to OS7 staff to be sent to the Health
Service. [@1] He was placed at the disposal of the Public Ministry for that
hearing and for formalisation for infringing the Drugs Act (Law 20,000). [@1]
CNN Chile likewise described a mixed-patrol procedure in which OS7 specialists
analysed and weighed the load found in Estación Central. [@2] G5 Noticias gave
the same final result of 51 kilograms and 890 grams of marijuana in 50
brick-type packages. [@3]
