On 30 October 2026, provincial police officers detained a 38-year-old Lodz man
after noticing the smell of marijuana as he walked toward garages near a
stadium. [@1,3,4] He was charged with possessing a significant quantity of drugs and
with violating fiscal-penal provisions, which carries up to 10 years'
imprisonment. [@1,3,4] The District Court for Lodz-Widzew ordered his pre-trial
detention for three months. [@2,3]

In the garage, officers watched the man place a glass jar of white powder on a
shelf, which he said was cocaine. [@1,3,4] The garage search also found other
illegal substances, dried plant material and electronic cigarettes containing
marijuana. [@2,3,4] Officers also checked the detainee's Toyota car, where they
found drugs and e-cigarettes without Polish excise stamps. [@2,4] Officers found
additional prohibited substances in his flat. [@1,3] Police weighed and
preliminarily tested the seized substances, reporting almost 875 grams in total,
plus 213 e-cigarettes without excise stamps. [@1,3,4]

The operation was carried out by the anti-football-hooligan unit of the Lodz
provincial police headquarters during reconnaissance of hooligan circles, based
on information from KWP Lodz. [@1]
