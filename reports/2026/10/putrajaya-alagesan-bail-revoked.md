A three-man Court of Appeal bench led by Justice Choo Kah Sing overturned the
High Court decision that had freed M. Alagesan, 44, on bail pending his drug
trafficking trial, and ordered him remanded in custody. [@1]

The court held that the Shah Alam High Court judge erred in using the Criminal
Procedure Code to grant bail when section 41B(1)(a) of the Dangerous Drugs Act
expressly bars bail for offences punishable by death, and ordered the
RM150,000 bail deposit returned. [@1] At the defence's request, the court
placed Alagesan at Sungai Buloh Prison until his trial ends and directed the
trial court to fix an early hearing date. [@1]

Alagesan was charged with trafficking 6.276 kg of cannabis at a house in Kg KL
Tin Kalumpang, Kuala Kubu Baharu, Hulu Selangor, on 7 February 2024. [@1] The
Shah Alam High Court had released him on RM150,000 bail with two sureties on
23 September 2024, with conditions including reporting to police, surrendering
his passport and staying home from 7pm to 7am. [@1] Trial dates are scheduled
for 17 to 19 February and 24 to 26 March 2027. [@1]
