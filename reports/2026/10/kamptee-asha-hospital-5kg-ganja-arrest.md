New Kamptee police arrested a 26-year-old man during a raid near Asha Hospital
on the Kamptee-Automotive Square road. [@2] The arrested man was identified as
Mohammed Shafik Mohammed Rafiq Ansari (26), a resident of Bunkar Colony near
Dhor Hospital in Kamptee. [@2] The raid took place at around 5.10 am on 31
October 2026. [@2] Police said the raid followed specific intelligence about the
movement of a drug consignment. [@1] During questioning, he allegedly told
police he had procured the contraband in Odisha and that it belonged to Faizan
Ansari Firoz Ansari (27) of Gol Bazaar in Old Kamptee. [@2] Police also named
Shadab of Kantabanji in Odisha as a suspect and said both suspects are
absconding and being searched for. [@2] A case was registered under Sections
8(c), 20(b)(ii)(B) and 29 of the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances
(NDPS) Act. [@2]

Officers recovered 5.084 kg of suspected ganja from the arrested man's
possession. [@2] Police valued the ganja at approximately Rs 1,01,680,
calculated at Rs 20,000 per kg. [@2] Police also seized a OnePlus 6T mobile
phone worth about Rs 10,000 and a Kantabanji-to-Nagpur railway ticket indicating
travel from Odisha. [@2] The total value of the seized property was put at Rs
1,11,680. [@2] Investigators said the ticket showed he had travelled from
Kantabanji in Odisha to bring the consignment to the city. [@1]

Police said Faizan has four previous cases for possession of ganja under the
NDPS Act. [@2] Police suspect the seizure could provide leads into an
inter-state supply network operating between Odisha and Nagpur. [@1] Efforts are
underway to trace the absconders and establish the source, intended recipients
and distribution network. [@1]
