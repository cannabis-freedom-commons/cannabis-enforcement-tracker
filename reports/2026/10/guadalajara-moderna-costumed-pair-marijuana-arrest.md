Guadalajara municipal police arrested Pablo “N”, 33, and Ana Gabriela “N”, 36,
in the colonia Moderna neighbourhood. [@2] The two were wearing Halloween
costumes, with the woman described as dressed as “La Guasona”, when officers
stopped them on Saturday afternoon for allegedly trying to rob a business. [@1]
The man’s specific costume character was not specified. [@1] The stop occurred
on Avenida Enrique Díaz de León between Fermín Riestra and Avenida Alemania,
where officers of the Grupo Lobos motorcycle squad were patrolling. [@2]
Officers on a vigilance patrol detected the pair manipulating the shop’s locks
and entry structure with industrial cutting shears. [@2]

When police approached, the pair abandoned the tools on the sidewalk and tried
to flee on foot, but the motorcycle squad intercepted them a few metres away.
[@2] A precautionary search recovered the shears and six packets containing dry
green vegetable with the characteristics of marijuana. [@2] The pair allegedly
also carried burglary tools plus several packets of marijuana. [@1] Both were
formally detained and referred to a Public Ministry agent to determine their
legal status for alleged attempted robbery and offences against health. [@2]
