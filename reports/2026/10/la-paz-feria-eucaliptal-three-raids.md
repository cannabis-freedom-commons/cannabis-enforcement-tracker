Police searched three homes in the city of La Paz in a firearms-abuse
investigation. [@1] The simultaneous raids took place on Friday 30 October
between 18:00 and 20:30 in the Feria and Eucaliptal neighbourhoods. [@1]

Officers seized several items they described as possibly linked to drug dealing.
[@1] They reported packets containing substances described as compatible with
cocaine and marijuana, along with a fired 20-calibre cartridge treated as
relevant to the firearms case. [@1] In one home where the firearms items sought
were not found, officers found a precision scale and several packets described
as compatible with cocaine and cannabis sativa. [@1] They also seized a mobile
phone placed at the authorities' disposal. [@1]

The Dangerous Drugs division and the corresponding prosecution office took over
after the substances were found. [@1] The seized items will undergo laboratory
testing to determine their nature, and the investigation continues under the La
Paz justice system. [@1]
