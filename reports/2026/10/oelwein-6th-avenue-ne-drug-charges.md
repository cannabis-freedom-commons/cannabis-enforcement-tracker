Two Oelwein residents were charged after Oelwein police searched a residence on
6th Avenue Northeast. [@1] The search warrant was executed at 27 6th Avenue NE
at about 3:27 a.m. on 31 October 2026. [@2] Ruben DeJesus, 39, was charged with
possession of methamphetamine and possession of marijuana, both third or later
offences and Class D felonies, and with possession of drug paraphernalia, a
simple misdemeanour. [@2] Candice Lynn Carrol, 42, was charged with
first-offence possession of methamphetamine and possession of marijuana, both
serious misdemeanours; unlawful possession of prescription drugs, also a serious
misdemeanour; and possession of drug paraphernalia, a simple misdemeanour. [@2]
The marijuana charge was a third or later offence for DeJesus. [@2] Carrol's
marijuana charge was her first offence. [@2]

The Fayette County Sheriff’s Office assisted Oelwein police. [@2] Both were
arrested on drug charges. [@1] The police department said all those charged are
presumed innocent unless and until proven guilty in a court of law. [@2]
