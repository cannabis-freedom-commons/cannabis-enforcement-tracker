Three people were charged with firearm, ammunition and controlled-drug offences
following the search of a residence in Bexon, Castries. [@1] Police said the
Major Crimes Unit executed a warrant to search the premises of Tashem Preudhomme
on 29 September. [@1] Thesia Preudhomme was also arrested during that search.
[@1] The following day police formally charged both Tashem and Thesia Preudhomme
with firearm, ammunition and drug possession. [@1] The two were taken to the
First District Court, where they were denied bail and remanded. [@1] Police
subsequently arrested and charged Zayne Austinay, whom they identified as a
resident of Tashem Preudhomme's household in Bexon. [@1] Austinay was formally
charged on 2 October with firearm, ammunition and drug possession. [@1]

During the search officers recovered what police described as a quantity of
cannabis resin, EC$16 310 in cash, a Glock 19 9mm pistol, a 31-round capacity
extended Glock magazine and 17 rounds of 9mm ammunition. [@1] Eight of those
rounds were 9mm Luger Norma ammunition. [@1]
