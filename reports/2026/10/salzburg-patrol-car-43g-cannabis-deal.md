In the city of Salzburg, police officers observed a narcotics handover at midday
on 31 October 2026 in the immediate vicinity of a parked police service vehicle.
[@1] The officers were able to perceive the transfer between the two men and
then intervened. [@1] The two men were a 30-year-old Tennengauer and a
67-year-old Salzburger who completed a cannabis deal in the city of Salzburg.
[@2] Officers said they watched the narcotics transaction from their own
vehicle. [@2] A second outlet likewise described police observing a suspected
cannabis transaction near their parked service vehicle and seizing drugs. [@3]

During the subsequent control, officers found about 40 grams of cannabis herb on
the 30-year-old dealer and confiscated it. [@1] During the same control,
officers found about three grams of cannabis herb on the 67-year-old alleged
buyer and also seized it. [@1] The seizure was reported as about 43 grams of
cannabis secured in total. [@1]

Despite the observed handover, officers used no handcuffs during the control.
[@1] Both men were reported for the offence while remaining at liberty. [@3]
