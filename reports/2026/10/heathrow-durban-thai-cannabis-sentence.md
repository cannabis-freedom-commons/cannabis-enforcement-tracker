Amy Jayne Durban, of Hemyock, Cullompton, Devon, was arrested by Border Force
officers at Heathrow Airport on 6 July 2026 after she arrived on a flight from
Bangkok. [@1] Her two suitcases were stuffed with cannabis that the National
Crime Agency said had a street value of at least £512,000. [@1] Durban, 45,
smuggled 51.2 kg of cannabis from Thailand. [@3]

She pleaded guilty to smuggling Class B drugs at Isleworth Crown Court in
August. [@2] She returned to court on 14 October 2026 and was sentenced to 25
months in prison. [@3]

The NCA said 976 cannabis smugglers were caught arriving by plane in 2025, up
from 801 in 2024. [@2] It said 600 air passenger couriers were arrested at UK
airports in the first six months of 2026. [@2] Mark Abbott, the NCA senior
investigating officer, said there were “really bad outcomes at home and abroad”
for couriers who are caught. [@1] He said a conviction would damage their future
career and travel prospects. [@1] The NCA said the case came after it and
partners in Thailand announced a new crackdown in the country. [@1] The agency
has also launched a social media campaign warning young people that smuggling
cannabis from Thailand could land them in a foreign jail. [@1]
