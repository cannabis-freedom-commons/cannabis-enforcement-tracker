Surendranagar's Special Operations Group (SOG), the district police's special
operations unit, searched a farm on the outskirts of Vadali village in Chotila
taluka and caught the farmer working there. [@2] The man was identified as
Vadali resident Panchabhai Nathabhai Meniya, aged about 51. [@2]

Acting on a tip-off, the SOG team seized 50 green ganja plants, the local name
for cannabis, from the field. [@1] Police valued the seized plants at Rs 804,500
(8.04 lakh). [@2]

SOG inspector Y.P. Patel searched the Vadali farm after receiving the tip-off.
[@2] The drive followed instructions from the Rajkot Range deputy inspector
general and the Surendranagar police superintendent to act against NDPS offences
and drug trafficking. [@2] Police said the circumstances of the cultivation and
whether anyone else was involved were still to be established in the
investigation. [@2]
