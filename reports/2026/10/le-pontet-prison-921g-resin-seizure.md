Officers at the Avignon-Le Pontet penitentiary centre searched the entire
establishment from 26 to 28 October 2026. [@1] The intensified checks, ordered
by the new management, covered the remand wing, the detention centre and the
visiting rooms. [@1] Only 20 cells were searched, whereas such totals are
ordinarily reached when about 50 cells are visited. [@1]

The operation seized 17 mobile phones, 921 grams of cannabis resin, two knives
and 11 chargers. [@1] The same tally also listed 186 grams of suspected cocaine,
with laboratory analyses still under way. [@1] Officers also seized a
PlayStation 4 controller and an Amazon key among other multimedia items. [@1] Of
the 921 grams of cannabis, 650 grams were found in a single detention-centre
cell, which union representative Clément Lopez said was not for personal use.
[@1] Another 50 grams was reported seized in the visiting rooms. [@1] The FO
Justice prison union account lists 921 grams of resin-type drugs in its tally of
the searches. [@2]

Lopez said trafficking continues inside the walls and blamed drones that overfly
the prison every night. [@1] The union called for more frequent sector searches,
phone detectors, new anti-projection netting and a more effective anti-drone
system. [@1]
