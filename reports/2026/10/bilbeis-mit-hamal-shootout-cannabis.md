Two men were killed in a shootout with police in Mit Hamal village, Bilbeis,
Sharqia, as officers moved to arrest them on drug and weapons charges. [@2] Both
were 25 and unemployed: Mohamed R. M., nicknamed Sonqor, and Abdel Fattah A. M.,
nicknamed Bouha, from Mit Hamal. [@1] Police said both had previous drug and
firearms cases, were wanted under court orders, and used farmland next to their
homes as a base. [@1] When officers reached them, the men opened fire and police
returned fire, killing both at the scene. [@2]

According to the police account, officers found 20 slabs of hashish weighing
about 1,500 grams and 2 kg of hydro, a hydroponic cannabis product. [@1] Beside
the second lay a shotgun with erased numbers and two cartridges. [@2] A plastic
bag of shotgun cartridges was also seized. [@1] Officers also seized cash,
mobile phones and a sensitive scale. [@3] The bodies were taken to the Bilbeis
hospital morgue. [@1]

The Sharqia Security Directorate mounted the operation as part of a campaign
against drug traffickers. [@1] The public prosecution in Bilbeis, headed by
Ahmed Shawqi, opened an investigation. [@1]
