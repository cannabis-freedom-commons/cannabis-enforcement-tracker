Two men aged 28 and 23 were in the stopped vehicle and said they were only
transporters. [@1] The two men later appeared as defendants before the court.
[@1] Their hearing at the Val de Briey court was on Monday 2 November, five days
after the road check. [@1] During the hearing, a lay judge asked them whether
they knew that possessing any quantity of cannabis was illegal. [@1]

Customs officers said they found a kit for delivering drugs to detainees in
prison consisting of cannabis, a drone and lighters. [@1] The cannabis resin was
concealed in socks, perfume bottles, soap and medicine boxes. [@1]

The road check took place on the A4 motorway at Hatrize on Wednesday 28 October.
[@1] The control happened shortly before midnight. [@1]
