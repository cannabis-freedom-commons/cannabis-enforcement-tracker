Luohu Customs officers stopped two travelers entering China after they entered the inspection area without declaring items. During the check, the woman threw a blue vape from her left trouser pocket into her handbag. [@1]

Testing found THC in the vape liquid, which customs identified as cannabis. The liquid weighed 0.28 g net, and officers transferred the case to the customs anti-smuggling department. [@1]

The report does not identify either traveler or say whether the man's companion was detained or questioned after the vape was found. [@1]
