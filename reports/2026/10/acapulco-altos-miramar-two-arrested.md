State police, Guerrero's state prosecutor's office and the Mexican Army arrested
two men during security patrols in the Altos del Miramar neighbourhood of
Acapulco on 31 October 2026. [@1] The detainees were named as Samuel “N” and
Kevin “N”, the abbreviated form Mexican police use in place of surnames. [@1]

Officers seized a pistol, three magazines and ammunition of various calibres.
[@1] The state police counted 34 small bags of dried herb with characteristics
similar to marijuana, 24 bags of white powder similar to cocaine, and 50 more
bags of various substances and pills whose composition is still to be
determined. [@1]

Quadratín Guerrero reported that both men were placed at the disposal of the
authorities. [@2] The Guerrero Public Security Secretariat said the patrols
formed part of inter-institutional work to prevent crime and respond to
citizens' reports, and it urged residents to call 911 or the 089 anonymous tip
line. [@1] The two are presumed innocent until a court rules on their
responsibility. [@1]
