Military Police arrested three people, two men aged 28 and 37 and a woman aged
26, at about 19h40 on 30 October 2026 in the Tocantins neighbourhood of
Uberlândia. [@1] Officers patrolling near the monitored house saw a man leave
the property on a motorcycle carrying a delivery bag, and he fled at high speed
and disobeyed a stop order when he saw the teams. [@1] During the pursuit he
discarded a bag and another object on a public road before officers caught and
searched him in the Dona Zulmira neighbourhood. [@1] The man told police he was
delivering drugs, had collected the material at the monitored house, and was
taking it to the Shopping Park neighbourhood. [@1] At the house another suspect
tried to flee across the roof of a neighbouring house carrying two backpacks,
which were thrown onto the roof. [@1]

Police found three bars of a substance similar to marijuana in the courier's
bag. [@1] In the two backpacks they found 26 tablets of a substance similar to
marijuana, a precision scale and notes on trafficking finances. [@1] The
reported total for the operation was 29 tablets, bars and portions of a
substance similar to marijuana, plus other trafficking-related materials. [@1]
Officers also seized three mobile phones and a motorcycle that police said was
used to transport the drugs. [@1] The three suspects were taken in flagrante to
the Civil Police station with the seized materials, and the motorcycle was
removed to an accredited impound lot. [@1]

The intervention was prompted by a report that narcotics were being stored in a
residence in the Tocantins neighbourhood. [@2] The Military Police of Minas
Gerais was identified as the source of the information on the operation. [@1]
