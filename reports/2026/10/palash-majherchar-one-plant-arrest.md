In the Palash upazila of Narsingdi district, police arrested 52-year-old Rushan
Pathan during an operation after finding him with one cannabis plant. [@1] The
arrest happened at midday on Thursday, 29 October, when officers took him from
the Majherchar area of Jinardi Union within that same upazila. [@1] Police
identified the arrested man as Rushan Pathan, the son of the late Hashem Pathan,
who belongs to the same Majherchar area where he was held. [@1]

Police said officers of Palash Thana, the local police station serving the area,
conducted the raid in Majherchar village at around 1:30 pm on that Thursday
afternoon. [@1] During that village raid, officers arrested Rushan Pathan with
one cannabis plant, described in Bengali in the case as a ganjar gach. [@1]

Palash police station officer-in-charge Md Shahed Alam, the officer in charge of
the thana, said a case was filed against the arrested man under the Narcotics
Control Act. [@1] Alam added that after the case filing, the arrested man was
sent onwards to the district court in Narsingdi for further legal proceedings.
