District and excise officials said a joint operation cleared another 195 acres of cannabis in
Gulistan tehsil, Qila Abdullah district, on 24 October 2026. [@1] Teams worked in Qadeel Segai
and Abdul Rehman Zai, with the assistant commissioner, police, the Anti-Narcotics Force (ANF),
Frontier Corps 77 Wing and excise officials taking part. [@1]

Officials reported that hand crews cleared 92 acres, tractors cleared 62 acres and weed cutters
cleared 24 acres. [@1] The article also says that on 17 acres the crop was cleared because of a
water shortage, though it does not clarify how this final area relates to the other methods.
[@1] The 195-acre operation is about 78.9 hectares; the article gives no plant count, crop
weight or value. [@1]

Atemad reported that the Anti-Cannabis Campaign 2026 had cleared 1,141 acres in total and would
continue until cannabis cultivation was eliminated in the area. [@1] The article does not name
anyone connected to the fields or report arrests. [@1]
