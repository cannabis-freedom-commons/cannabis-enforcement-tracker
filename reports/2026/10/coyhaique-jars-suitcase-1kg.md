Officers of the OS7 section of Carabineros in Aysén, working with the local prosecutor's office,
carried out a consensual entry and search of a home in Coyhaique, the regional capital. [@1,2] In a
storeroom, hidden inside a suitcase, they found more than 30 glass jars containing cannabis. [@1,2]

The head of OS7 Aysén, Captain Juan González Vásquez, said the jars held a gross weight of about one
kilogram. [@1,2] Carabineros said the amount was equivalent to about 2,000 doses taken out of
circulation. [@1,2]

The two occupants, a 38-year-old man and a 39-year-old woman, were detained on a charge of illicit
drug trafficking and taken before the Juzgado de Garantía of Coyhaique for their formalisation
hearing. [@1,2] Neither had a criminal record. [@1,2] The court remanded one of them in pre-trial
detention for the duration of the investigation; Radio Santa María identified him as the man and
reported that the court accepted the prosecutor's argument that his freedom was a danger to society.
[@1,2] The measure imposed on the woman was not reported. [@1,2]
