Police in Bemetara district, Chhattisgarh, arrested two men for smuggling ganja,
a form of cannabis, after a joint team of the Dadi police station and the cyber
cell stopped them near Birsinghi village. [@1] Officers had been tipped that two
men were riding from Nawagarh towards Birsinghi with the drug and set up a
blockade on an unpaved road at Birsinghi. [@2] They stopped motorcycle CG 25 P
5535 and arrested Pritosh Singh Thakur, 26, and Vishwanath Singh Thakur, 32,
both residents of Nawagaon Kala in the Dadi police station area. [@2]

A search found 1 kg 19 g of ganja, the motorcycle and two mobile phones. [@1]
Police put the total value of the seized property at ₹1,13,950. [@2] The ganja
accounted for ₹50,950, the bike about ₹35,000 and the two phones ₹28,000. [@2]

The men told police they had contacted a man in Mungeli by mobile phone and
bought the ganja from him. [@2] Police registered a case against both under
Section 20(b) of the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act and were
still looking for the alleged supplier. [@2] The operation included cyber cell
in-charge Mayank Mishra and Dadi station in-charge Suraj Prakash. [@2]
