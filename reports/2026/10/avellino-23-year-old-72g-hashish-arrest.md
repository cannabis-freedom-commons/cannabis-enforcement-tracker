The State Police of Avellino arrested a 23-year-old from Avellino in flagrant
delicto for possession of drugs with the aim of selling them. [@2] Avellino's
Squadra Mobile officers made the arrest during drug services coordinated by the
local prosecutor's office, with help from a police dog unit from the Naples
Questura, in a peripheral city district. [@1]

Officers found in the man's wallet two heat-sealed bags containing 0.7 g and 1.3
g of cocaine and two small pieces of hashish individually wrapped in baking
paper, weighing 2.1 g and 1.9 g. [@1]

A search of the man's flat found a cellophane bag with 37.7 g of cocaine hidden
in a jacket lining, two pieces of hashish weighing 44.7 g and 23.6 g concealed
in a coat, and 870 euros in cash of various denominations. [@1]

All the material was seized and the man was placed under house arrest on the
prosecutor's order, pending a validation hearing. [@1] Local reporting confirms
that the 870 euros seized in cash were in banknotes of different denominations.
[@4] Police noted that the charges were provisional and the man's guilt would be
established only at the conclusion of the trial. [@1] The 39.7 g of cocaine
found in the case is excluded from the cannabis figure; only the 72.3 g of
hashish is counted here as cannabis. [@3]
