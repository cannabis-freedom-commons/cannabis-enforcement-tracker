On 31 October 2026 the Federal Highway Police arrested three people in the Jataí
region of southwestern Goiás during an operation against a suspected drug convoy
on the BR-364. [@1] A 33-year-old woman who was in the Mitsubishi that carried
the drugs was detained. [@1] Two men aged 26 and 35 who were in the Volkswagen
Gol used as a scout car were also detained. [@2] The three suspects, both
vehicles and the seized drugs were taken to the Jataí Civil Police station. [@1]

PRF teams in Mato Grosso do Sul and Goiás shared information and identified
three vehicles suspected of travelling together to move drugs. [@3] One of the
vehicles was stopped in Cassilândia, Mato Grosso do Sul, while the other two
fled toward Jataí. [@1] Officers later stopped the VW Gol on the BR-364 near
Jataí and found the Mitsubishi ASX abandoned after it fled along side roads.
[@1] Officers found 65 kg of skunk and 57 kg of marijuana, 122 kg in total,
hidden in the doors and trunk of the Mitsubishi. [@2]

Police identified the Gol as a “batedor”, a scout car supporting the drug
transport. [@2] The Mitsubishi showed signs of tampering and had a theft record
in São Paulo state. [@2]
