In the Owo area of Ondo State, NDLEA operatives on 16 October 2026 trailed 53-year-old Okunebe
Ndidi to his hideout in Ameyinti and arrested him. [@1,2]

They recovered 25 bags of cannabis weighing 375 kilograms. [@1,2] The agency included the
operation in a national statement focused on other seizures the same week; it gave no court
update or detail on how Ndidi was linked to the drugs beyond the arrest at the hideout. [@1,2]
