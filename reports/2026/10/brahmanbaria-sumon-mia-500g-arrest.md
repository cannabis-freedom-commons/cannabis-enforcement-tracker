## The operation

On Friday afternoon 9 October 2026 a Department of Narcotics Control team from the Brahmanbaria district office operated on the road in front of a shop named Dayal Enterprise, on the western side of the Cumilla-Sylhet highway in the Radhika Bazar area of Sultanpur Union under Brahmanbaria Sadar upazila. [@1] The team detained Sumon Mia, 45, and recovered 500 grams of cannabis from his possession. [@1]

## The accused and the case

Sumon is the son of the late Bazlu Mia and the late Shahara Khatun, originally from Guruhit Uttarpara in Ward No. 6 of Chandibar Union under Kasba upazila, with a permanent address in Narui Brahmanhata of Kaitala Union under Nabinagar upazila. [@1] Following the operation the seized cannabis was confiscated, and Md. Tayriful Islam, a sub-inspector of the Department of Narcotics Control, filed a regular case with Brahmanbaria Sadar Model Police Station naming Sumon Mia as the sole accused. [@1]
