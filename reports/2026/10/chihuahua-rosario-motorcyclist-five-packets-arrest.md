Municipal intelligence police detained 20-year-old Óscar Ramón R. R. in the
Rosario neighbourhood of Chihuahua City during patrols under the Blindaje
Capital operation on 31 October 2026. [@1] The detention was reported as for his
probable involvement in delitos contra la salud after officers found five
packets of apparent marijuana in a backpack. [@1] Officers said they first saw
his motorcycle travelling without lights on J. J. Calvo street and, after he
ignored an order to stop at the crossing with 58th street, caught up with him at
the crossing of 58th and Samaniego streets. [@2]

During the inspection, officers found five black packages inside a backpack
containing green, dry and odorous herb with characteristics similar to
marijuana. [@3] The motorcycle he was riding was secured and sent to the
official impound lot. [@2]

He was taken to the Zona Sur Municipal Detention Centre for registration and a
medical assessment before being placed at the disposal of the Public Prosecutor.
[@1] The municipal account notes that the competent authority will determine
responsibility for the people mentioned. [@3]
