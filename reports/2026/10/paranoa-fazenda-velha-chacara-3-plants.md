On the night of Saturday 31 October 2026, around 19h, the Federal District
Military Police recorded a seizure at a chácara in Fazenda Velha, on DF-330 Km
17 in Paranoá. [@1] Officers found about eight people holding a party at the
property. [@1] One man was taken with the seized materials to the 35th Police
Station for the applicable procedures. [@1]

In the man's waist pouch, officers found one portion of a cocaine-like
substance, one hashish-like portion, two pills appearing to be MDMA and six
flowers described as Skunk cannabis. [@1] Officers then found three planted
Cannabis sativa plants at the entrance to the chácara. [@1] Inside the house,
under a bed, officers also found one replica pistol and one telescopic sight.
[@1]

The action began after an anonymous tip about a possible cannabis plantation on
the property. [@1] RPMON operational teams went to the address to verify the
information gathered from the tip. [@1] Questioned about the plants, the man
said they belonged to the absent chácara owner and that the property had been
lent for the event and was under his responsibility at the time. [@1]
