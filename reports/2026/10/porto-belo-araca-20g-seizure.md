Military Police stopped two men with previous records for drug trafficking and
theft at a police roadblock in the Araçá neighbourhood of Porto Belo on the
night of Friday 30 October. [@1] The officers found problems with the car's
paperwork and established that both occupants' driving licences were suspended.
[@1] Both men had prior police records and said they had already served a
sentence for theft. [@1]

A search of the vehicle yielded about 20 grams of maconha along with three
mobile phones, two radio communicators, two card machines, two credit cards, a
micha-type key and R$21 in cash. [@1] The two men admitted that the drug was
theirs and described themselves as users. [@1] Their explanations of where the
other items came from and who they belonged to were judged insufficient. [@1]

The seized materials were taken in for the applicable legal procedures, and the
vehicle was removed to the contracted impound lot because of the administrative
irregularities. [@1]
