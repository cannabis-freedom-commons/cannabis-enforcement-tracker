Buhl city councilman Ryan Loos, 49, is no longer facing drug-related charges
after a judge accepted a motion to dismiss the case. [@1] The motion states Loos
took a plea agreement in exchange for the charges against him being dropped.
[@1] Under the agreement he completed a marijuana education course and 16 hours
of community service. [@1] Judge Jennifer Bergin accepted the state's motion and
dismissed the case on Oct. 16. [@1]

Loos originally faced resisting arrest, drug paraphernalia and marijuana
possession charges after an incident in Buhl on April 18. [@1] A Buhl police
officer had been helping a Twin Falls sheriff's deputy with a suspected drunk
driver near Broadway Ave N and Locust St around 1:30 a.m. on a Saturday when
Loos approached and asked about the driver. [@2] The officer wrote that Loos
appeared intoxicated, refused several requests for identification and was
handcuffed. [@2] A search of Loos found a device the officer described as a THC
vape bearing a marijuana symbol in his front right pocket. [@2] Loos was booked
at the Twin Falls County Jail and cited for resisting, obstructing and delaying,
possession of drug paraphernalia and possession of marijuana. [@2]

He bonded out and entered a not guilty plea. [@2] Loos' fellow city councilman
Cortlin Runyan is due to be arraigned in district court on Monday on aggravated
battery, possession of a controlled substance and introducing contraband to a
correctional facility charges after an August 6 arrest. [@1]
