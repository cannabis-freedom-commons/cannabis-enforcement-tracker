Justice Musa Kakaki of the Federal High Court in Lagos sentenced 60-year-old
Chinese woman Ting Hong Kiong to two years in prison for importing 31
kilogrammes of cannabis sativa. [@1] She pleaded guilty to a one-count
drug-trafficking charge brought by the National Drug Law Enforcement Agency
(NDLEA), Nigeria's anti-drug agency. [@1] The court imposed the two-year term
without an option of a fine. [@2] The judge ordered that the term run from 17
May 2026, the date of her arrest. [@1]

Prosecutor Cyril Osaigbo told the court that Kiong was arrested at Murtala
Muhammed International Airport in Ikeja, Lagos, during the inward clearance of
passengers on an Emirates Airlines flight from Bangkok, Thailand, via Dubai.
[@1] He said she was found in possession of 31 kg of cannabis sativa without
lawful authority. [@1]

Defence counsel Chief Benson Ndakara urged the court to temper justice with
mercy, saying Kiong had shown remorse by promptly pleading guilty and had not
wasted the court's time. [@1] He asked for a non-custodial sentence or a fine,
offering to bear the financial consequences himself. [@1] He described her as a
'victim of circumstances', saying she had fallen under the influence of a person
who presented himself as a lover but had ulterior motives. [@1] Justice Kakaki
said the court could not ignore the seriousness of drug trafficking and its
consequences for society and Nigeria's image. [@1] He held that Kiong knew what
she was doing and that the prevalence of hard drugs made an appropriate sentence
necessary. [@1]
