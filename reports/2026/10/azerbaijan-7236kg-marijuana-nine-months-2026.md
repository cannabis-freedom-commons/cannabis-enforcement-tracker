Law-enforcement agencies detected 6,247 criminal drug cases during
January–September 2026. [@2] Of those cases, 1,682 involved illegal sale, 4,411
illegal acquisition and possession, 152 illegal cultivation of narcotic plants
and two involved other crimes. [@2] Officers held 4,352 people criminally liable
for drug trafficking, including 4,235 men and 117 women. [@3,4] Among those held liable were 50 foreign nationals, from whom officers seized a total of 566 kg 660.47 grams of various drugs, 2,778 tablets containing narcotic substances, 3 tablets of potent substances and 3 cannabis plants weighing 880 grams. [@4]

Seizures in the detected cases totalled 8 tons 406 kg 878.621 grams of narcotic
drugs and psychotropic substances. [@2,4] Marijuana accounted for 7 tons 236 kg
769.263 grams of that total. [@2] Hashish accounted for 253 kg 763.447 grams,
with the remainder comprising heroin, opium, cocaine, other narcotic drugs and
psychotropic substances. [@2] Officers also detected and seized 21,355 cannabis
plants. [@2] The Interior Ministry separately reported 6,443 kg of drugs seized
in January–September 2026. [@1] That ministry total was 1,780 kg more than in
the same period last year. [@1]

The ministry figures were announced at its board meeting on nine months of
operational performance and future tasks. [@1] The State Commission working
group estimated the combined haul's value at AZN 246 million 273 thousand. [@3,4]
