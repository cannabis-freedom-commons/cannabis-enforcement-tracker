Customs officers at Delhi's Indira Gandhi International Airport arrested an
Indian woman arriving from Bangkok with 2.99 kg of hydroponic ganja. [@1] She
arrived at Terminal 3 on 16 October 2026. [@2] Officers stopped her at the Green
Channel on the basis of passenger profiling and sent her for X-ray screening.
[@2]

A search of her baggage turned up two polythene pouches holding a green
substance weighing 2,996 grams. [@2] A diagnostic test indicated prima facie
that the substance was ganja or marijuana. [@2] Customs estimated the value of
the haul at approximately Rs 2.995 crore. [@2] The substance was seized under
Section 43(a) of the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, 1985, and
the woman was arrested under Section 43(b). [@2] Customs registered a case under
Sections 8, 20, 23 and 29 of the Act. [@2]

Customs said further investigations were under way. [@2] The same customs
statement also covered a separate seizure of five polythene pouches holding
4,918.5 grams from another Indian woman who arrived from Bangkok on 18 October.
[@2] That second woman was arrested on 19 October under the NDPS Act. [@2]
Customs put the two seizures together at 7.9145 kg of suspected marijuana with a
combined estimated value of Rs 4.795 crore. [@2]
