A traffic stop over a stop sign led to six drug-related charges for Boynton
Beach man Peter Simo, 30, who was arrested early on 29 October 2026 in Delray
Beach. [@1] The stop began when an officer watched Simo’s black Infiniti fail to
stop properly at the four-way stop at SE 1st Street and SE 4th Avenue. [@1] Simo
was alone in the car and was allegedly reaching behind the front passenger seat
as the officer walked up. [@1] The officer spotted a sheathed fixed-blade knife
between the driver’s seat and center console and reported a strong marijuana
odor while leaning in to secure it. [@1] Simo said he had been reaching for a
can, that his medical marijuana card had expired and that the smell came from
earlier use. [@1]

Police searched the car and reported finding an open, cold can of an alcoholic
beverage behind the passenger seat, plus vials, capsules, a loose orange pill
and a hand-rolled cigarette. [@1] Field tests were positive for 2.23 grams of
marijuana and 0.72 grams of marijuana wax, as well as 0.18 grams of fentanyl and
0.41 grams of ecstasy/MDMA, and the affidavit also lists one 30 mg
amphetamine/dextroamphetamine pill. [@1] Simo was booked into jail and later
released on $22,000 bond. [@1]
