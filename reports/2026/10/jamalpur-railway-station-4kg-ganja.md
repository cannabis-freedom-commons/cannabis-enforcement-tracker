Railway police found four unclaimed packets on platform 1 of Jamalpur railway
station. [@1] The bundles lay near a circular seating area built of concrete
tiles. [@2] The find was made on the night of Wednesday 28 October 2026. [@3]
Officers on patrol spotted the packets and asked passengers about them, but no
one claimed them. [@3] When opened, the bundles were found to hold a ganja-like
substance — ganja being the Hindi term for cannabis. [@3] The four bundles
weighed 4.008 kg. [@1]

Police seized the bundles and took them to the Jamalpur railway police station.
[@3] Station house officer Swaraj Kumar said sub-inspectors Monu Kumar Shishupal
and Amaresh Kumar found the substance during a check. [@2] Police did not know
who brought the consignment to the station or where it was to be delivered. [@3]
A first information report (FIR), the initial police report that begins a
criminal case, was registered against unknown people at the railway police
station. [@1]

Jamalpur station lies in the Malda railway division. [@3] Police said the
substance's official identification would be confirmed only after testing. [@2]
Police said they were investigating who owned the packets and why they were left
at the station. [@2]
