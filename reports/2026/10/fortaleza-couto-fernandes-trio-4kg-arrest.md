An investigation by the Civil Police of Ceará led to the arrest of three people
suspected of drug trafficking and membership in a criminal organization in the
Couto Fernandes neighborhood of Fortaleza. [@1] Officers from the 11th Police
District carried out the operation. [@1] Those detained were two women aged 22
and 25 and a 23-year-old man, all caught in flagrante. [@1] Police said the
group was located after investigators examined suspected drug dealing in the
area. [@1] After detention, the three were transferred to the Captures and
Polinter Division, where they remained at the disposal of justice. [@1]

During the action, officers seized approximately four kilograms of marijuana,
along with an unspecified quantity of crack. [@1] The drugs were packed in a
suitcase that investigators said was destined for municipalities in the interior
of Ceará. [@1] Officers collected the material and presented it at the police
station together with the three suspects. [@1]

Police are still investigating the group's role in the criminal activity and
whether other people were involved in the distribution scheme. [@1] The case was
treated as suspected organized drug trafficking in Couto Fernandes, in
Fortaleza, rather than simple possession. [@1]
