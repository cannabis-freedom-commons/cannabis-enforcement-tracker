Special Response Battalion-13 arrested 40-year-old Morjina Begum with 3 kg of
ganja in Gangachara, Rangpur. [@1] Officers detained her at about 4 pm on 29
October on a paved road in East Isli village in Lakshmitari Union. [@1] Acting
on a tip, an operational team from SRB-13's Rangpur Sadar Company raided the
area in front of Abul Malek's house in East Isli village. [@1] Officers
recovered 3 kg of ganja from Morjina Begum during the raid. [@1] Begum is the
wife of Md. Mokbul Hossain of Gopal village in Kaunia Upazila. [@2]

SRB-13 announced the arrest in a 30 October press release through Additional
Superintendent and senior assistant director (media) Biplab Kumar Goswami. [@1]
SRB filed a case against her as plaintiff under the Narcotics Control Act. [@2]
Officers handed her and the seized ganja to Gangachara police station for
further legal action. [@1] Gangachara Model police station chief Abdus Sabur
confirmed that the arrested woman and the seized ganja had been handed to the
station. [@1] He said a case under the Narcotics Control Act had been filed and
further legal steps were under way. [@1]
