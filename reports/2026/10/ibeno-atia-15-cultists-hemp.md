The Akwa Ibom State Police Command arrested fifteen suspected cultists in a raid on criminal hideouts and black spots at Atia Coastal Village, Ibeno Local Government Area. [@1,2] Ibeno Divisional officers carried out the operation on 20 October 2026 on intelligence about cult activity in the coastal community. [@1,2]

Those named were Ndifreke Paul, 29, known as "Black Satan" of Onna; Dominic, 35, known as "Don Sars" of Esit Eket; and Etim Oboho, 46, of Urue-Offong/Oruko, with twelve further suspects unnamed. [@1] Police said Dominic and Paul confessed to belonging to the Vikings and Iceland confraternities. [@1,2] Officers recovered wraps of suspected Indian hemp and suspected methamphetamine known as Ice, two suspected stolen eight-horsepower outboard engines, a motorcycle suspected stolen, cult berets, Vikings items, suspected charm bottles and five phones. [@1,2] Some suspects escaped into nearby creeks, and the manhunt continued. [@1,2]
