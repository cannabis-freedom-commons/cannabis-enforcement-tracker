Rajsamand police ran a one-day special drive across the district against
narcotics smuggling and the illegal drug trade under the Nasha Mukt Bharat
Abhiyan. [@1] Twenty police teams raided and checked 111 places in the drive
under SP Hemant Kalal's direction and ASP Mahendra Parik's supervision. [@2]
Three accused were arrested in NDPS Act cases during the action. [@2] Deogarh
police seized 466 grams of illegal ganja from one accused and registered a new
case. [@2] Railmagra police arrested Naresh (36), son of Heeralal Dangi of
Ghalla in Udaipur district, in a case under the NDPS Act. [@2] Deogarh police
caught Balveersingh (20), a resident of Thikadwas in Rashmi living at Kiton ka
Badia in Deogarh, with 466 grams of ganja. [@2] Police registered a case against
him under the NDPS Act at Deogarh station and the investigation was under way.
[@2] Deogarh police also arrested Prakash Powar (31), a resident of Ramnagar,
Kaidiyon ki Dhani under Kapadi police station, in another NDPS Act case. [@2]

The teams inspected 72 hotels, 74 dhabas and 21 dharamshalas during the drive.
[@2] They carried out special checks at 39 places within 500 metres of
educational institutions. [@2] They also set up blockades at 3 toll posts and
checked 21 bus stands. [@2] Police arrested 21 people in preventive action under
Section 170 of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita. [@2]
