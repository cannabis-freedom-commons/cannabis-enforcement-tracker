Guatemala’s National Civil Police reported that two men were captured on Monday
19 October 2026 during an operation in which drugs were seized. [@1] The
operation took place in Pasaco, Jutiapa, where anti-narcotics agents captured
Jaime “N”, aged 52, and Máximo “N”, aged 25, during a control and inspection
procedure. [@1] DIPAFRONT agents made the capture with support from the
Subdirección General de Análisis de Información Antinarcótica (SGAIA). [@2] The
control and inspection operation was carried out at kilometre 132.1 in the
jurisdiction of Pasaco, Jutiapa. [@2] The two men were transporting a pickup on
a tow truck, which officers decided to inspect. [@1]

A search of the vehicles found 20 packages of marijuana, two packages of crack
and two mobile phones. [@1] Officers impounded both vehicles for the
corresponding legal proceedings. [@1]

The detainees and the seized evidence were placed at the disposal of prosecutors
to continue the investigation and legal proceedings. [@2] Authorities are
continuing inquiries to establish the origin and destination of the substances
and the detainees’ possible links to trafficking networks. [@2]
