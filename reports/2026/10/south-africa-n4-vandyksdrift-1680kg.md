Officers from the Nkangala District Anti-Hijacking Task Team and the Middelburg Flying Squad stopped
a white Isuzu truck travelling at high speed on the N4 near Vandyksdrift at about 3:30 pm on Monday
19 October 2026. [@2] Under a green tarpaulin covering luggage they found 55 bags of compressed
dagga weighing 1,680 kg, which the police valued at about R12 million. [@1,2] Each bag was marked
with names and destination details. [@2]

While the officers were still at the scene, two men aged between 27 and 33 arrived in a white VW
Polo with Mpumalanga plates and negotiated for the release of the dagga and the truck driver, the
police said. [@1,2] They first offered R12,590 and, after being warned against bribery, raised the
offer by another R10,000, saying they were desperate to recover the consignment. [@1,2] The two men
were arrested for alleged bribery and possession of dagga, and the driver was also arrested. [@1,2]

All three appeared in the Middelburg Magistrate's Court and were denied bail, the Middelburg
Observer reported. [@1] The provincial police commissioner, Lieutenant General Apaphia Modise, said
the officers had stood their ground and that police would not allow anyone to corrupt them. [@2]
