Anti-narcotics police in Mersin detained H.Y. and M.E. in an investigation run
by the Mersin Chief Public Prosecutor's Office. [@1,4,6] The two were described as
torbacı, or street-level drug dealers. [@2,4,5,6] The operation followed work by the
anti-narcotics branch against drug sellers in the city. [@1] Officers identified
the pair through physical surveillance and raided their addresses, searching
their persons and homes at the same time. [@3] The pair were caught in the raid.
[@2,5]

The searches found synthetic drugs prepared for sale and a quantity of esrar,
the Turkish name for cannabis. [@1,4,5,6] After questioning at police headquarters,
the two were brought to court under heavy security. [@3] A duty court ordered
tutuklama, pre-trial detention, on drug-trafficking charges and sent both to
prison. [@3,4,6]

Police in Mersin detained five suspected street drug dealers and a court jailed four of them on 6 November 2026. [@7,9,10,13] Officers found 48 kilograms of esrar, the Turkish name for cannabis, in the vehicle used by one suspect and in that suspect's home, and about 1.5 kilograms of methamphetamine in another suspect's vehicle. [@7,10,12] İHA reported that police caught the suspects in two numbered-plate vehicles and seized about 1.5 kilograms of methamphetamine and 48 kilograms of esrar in the vehicles and at their addresses. [@8,11,13] Searches of the other three suspects found 14.40 grams of synthetic cannabinoid (bonzai), cocaine, ammonia, a precision scale and 120 lira. [@9] Of the five suspects brought to court, four were ordered held in pre-trial detention and the fifth was released under judicial supervision. [@9,11,13]

İHA reported that the Mersin provincial police directorate's anti-narcotics branch targeted people trading drugs and street-level dealers known as torbacı. [@8]

Sabah gives the plates of the two cars as 33 BFY 04, used by Z.S.A., and 03 VD 202, used by Y.E.A., and places the 48 kilograms in Y.E.A.'s vehicle. [@7] Sabah reports that police also detained S.B., A.A. and B.K., described as torbacı street dealers, as the investigation widened. [@7]

The submitted account places the whole 48 kilograms in Y.E.A.'s vehicle, while Sabah reports it in that suspect's vehicle and address. [@12] The Mersin chief public prosecutor's office said proceedings had been opened against the suspect S.B. under article 188 of the Turkish Penal Code, which covers the manufacture and trade of narcotics. [@12]
