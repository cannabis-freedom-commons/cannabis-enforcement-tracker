Anti-narcotics police in Mersin detained H.Y. and M.E. in an investigation run by the Mersin Chief
Public Prosecutor's Office. [@1,4,6] The two were described as torbacı, or street-level drug dealers.
[@2,4,5,6] The operation followed work by the anti-narcotics branch against drug sellers in the city.
[@1] Officers identified the pair through physical surveillance and raided their addresses, searching
their persons and homes at the same time, and caught them. [@2,3,5]

The searches found synthetic drugs prepared for sale and a quantity of esrar, the Turkish name for
cannabis. [@1,4,5,6] After questioning at police headquarters, the two were brought to court under
heavy security [@3], and a duty court ordered tutuklama, pre-trial detention, on drug-trafficking
charges and sent both to prison. [@3,4,6]

## Five more suspects

On 6 November 2026 police in Mersin detained five more suspected street dealers, and a court jailed
four of them. [@7,9,10,13] The operations were run under the Chief Public Prosecutor's Office by the
anti-narcotics branch against drug traders and street dealers. [@8,13] Officers stopped two cars,
plates 33 BFY 04, used by Z.S.A., and 03 VD 202, used by Y.E.A. [@7], and found about 1.5 kilograms of
methamphetamine and 48 kilograms of esrar in the vehicles and at the suspects' addresses.
[@7,8,10,11,12,13] Two accounts place all 48 kilograms in Y.E.A.'s car. [@7,12] Searches of the other
three, S.B., A.A. and B.K. [@7], found 14.40 grams of synthetic cannabinoid (bonzai), cocaine, ammonia, a
precision scale and 120 lira. [@9,13]

Four of the five were held in pre-trial detention and the fifth released under judicial supervision.
[@9,11,13] Prosecutors opened proceedings against S.B. under article 188 of the Turkish Penal Code,
which covers the manufacture and trade of narcotics. [@12]
