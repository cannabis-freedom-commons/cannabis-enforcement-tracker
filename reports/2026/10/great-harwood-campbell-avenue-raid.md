A man and a woman are under investigation for money laundering and possession of
controlled drugs after officers raided a property in Great Harwood. [@1]
Officers executed the search warrant at an address on Campbell Avenue in Great
Harwood on Thursday, October 29. [@1] The morning operation was carried out with
help from colleagues in the Neighbourhood Policing Team and officers from
Blackburn and Darwen Police. [@1]

During the search of the Campbell Avenue property, police seized a large
quantity of cash. [@1] Officers also seized a quantity of drugs found at the
same address that they believe to be cannabis. [@1] Police identified the two
people now under investigation as a local female and a local male. [@1]

Police said the raid shows that officers listen to community information and act
on what residents tell them. [@1] Officers invited anyone with concerns about
drug-related activity in their area to contact the Hyndburn Taskforce by email.
[@1] Police said people can also call 101 or reach Crimestoppers anonymously
with information about drug activity. [@1]
