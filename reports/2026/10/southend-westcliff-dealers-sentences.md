## Jailed at Basildon

Elliot Reddington, 36, of Ashingdon Road in Rochford, and Joe Mongan, 27, of Fairfax Drive in Westcliff, were sentenced at Basildon Crown Court on 5 October 2026 after pleading guilty to all charges. [@1] Reddington received nine years and nine months in prison; Mongan received eight years and eight months. [@1,2] Both admitted being concerned in the supply of cannabis alongside cocaine, firearms and other counts. [@1]

Officers arrested the pair in early-morning warrants in Westcliff in February 2026 during an Essex Police investigation into organised drug supply in south Essex. [@1,2] Police recovered large quantities of cocaine and cannabis, a handgun with ammunition, knives, mobile phones linked to dealing and thousands of pounds in cash. [@1,2] Detective Inspector Yoni Adler of the Serious and Organised Crime Unit said the pair were prepared to use deadly weapons to protect their criminal enterprise. [@1]
