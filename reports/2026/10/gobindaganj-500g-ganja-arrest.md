Detectives in Gobindaganj, Gaibandha, arrested Md. Badal Mondal, 40, with 500
grams of ganja, the local word for cannabis. [@1] A second man, Shamim, 38, fled
the scene, police said. [@1] The raid took place at 4:45 am on Thursday 29
October 2026 in Krodgacha, Gobindaganj upazila. [@2] Shamim is the son of Md.
Dudu Hazi of the same village. [@1]

The district detective branch, known as the DB, acted on a tip-off, led by its
officer in charge under the direction of the Gaibandha superintendent of police.
[@2] Officers alleged that Badal was buying and selling ganja from a room of his
south-facing tin-roofed house. [@2] The ganja was seized before witnesses at the
scene. [@2]

A case was filed at Gobindaganj police station against Badal and the fugitive
Shamim. [@2] Police gave the case number as 47, dated 29 October 2026. [@2] They
said it was brought under section 36(1), schedule 19(a), and section 41 of the
Narcotics Control Act 2018. [@2] Police said the local law-and-order situation
was normal and anti-drug operations were continuing. [@2]
