Peru's Huaytará provincial prosecutor's office won nine months of preventive
prison for Elvis Clinton García Garamendi, investigated for aggravated illicit
drug trafficking. [@1] The measure was argued by provincial prosecutor Eduardo
Enrique Galán Pisfil before the Huaytará Preparatory Investigation Court, which
accepted his grave and well-founded elements of conviction linking García to the
drug transport. [@1]

The investigation dates the events to 23 October 2026 at about 12:50, during an
interdiction operation run by DIRANDRO officers of the National Police near the
village of Libertadores, on the route towards Huaytará. [@1] Officers stopped
vehicle AZI-757, driven by García and bound for Lima, and found 22 camouflage
sacks in the cargo area holding 25 packages of Cannabis sativa (marihuana) with
a total weight of 476.70 kilograms. [@1] The same search found four brick-shaped
packages of cocaine alkaloid weighing 4.160 kilograms under the bunk mattress,
along with S/ 10,190.00 in cash and three mobile phones. [@1]

The court ordered García held in preventive custody at the Chincha penitentiary
while the investigation continues. [@1]
