Alès police arrested an 18-year-old on Friday evening in the city with nearly
500 g of cocaine and about the same amount of cannabis. [@1] A patrol from the
Alès station spotted the youth acting suspiciously on a well-known dealing spot
on Rue du Lozère in the Cévennes district. [@1] Lookouts shouted warnings, the
youth fled, and officers caught him after he entered an apartment-building hall.
[@1] Officers described him as just barely an adult when they checked him. [@1]
He was placed in custody at the police station and the custody was extended on
Saturday. [@1] He was then jailed to await his trial scheduled for Monday at the
Alès courthouse. [@1] The case was to be tried by immediate appearance and the
youth was treated as a repeat offender. [@1]

Officers found a satchel containing about one hundred euros, nearly 500 g of
cocaine and about the same amount of cannabis. [@1] The seizure was presented as
one kilo of drugs in total, of which about 0.5 kg was cannabis. [@1]

Four days earlier, the prosecutor had denounced a trafficking system using young
adults as "chair à canon" to sell drugs, and this case was presented as
illustrating his point. [@1] The youth had already been convicted for similar
acts in Montpellier and the Paris region. [@1]
