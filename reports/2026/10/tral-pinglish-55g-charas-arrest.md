Awantipora police, acting under their continuing campaign against drug
trafficking, arrested a suspected drug trafficker in the Pinglish area of Tral.
[@1] Police identified the arrested man as Arafat Ahmed Ahangar, a resident of
Rathsona village in Tral. [@1] A team from Tral police station stopped him
during naka checking, a roadside checkpoint check, in Pinglish after noticing
suspicious activity. [@1] The checkpoint operation in Pinglish was led by Tral
station house officer Inspector Tajamul Islam. [@1]

Officers searching the detained man recovered about 55 grams of charas, a
cannabis resin commonly called hashish, from his possession. [@1] The report
gave the amount as an approximate figure of about 55 grams. [@1]

After the recovery, police registered a first information report (FIR) under the
Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act at Tral police station.
[@1] Police said further investigation into the NDPS case was under way at Tral
police station. [@1] Awantipora police described the arrest as part of their
ongoing drive against drug trafficking in the Tral area. [@1]
