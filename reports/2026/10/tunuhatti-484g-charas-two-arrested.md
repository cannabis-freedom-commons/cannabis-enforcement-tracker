Police at Tunuhatti, the checkpoint at the entrance to Chamba district in
Himachal Pradesh, found 484 grams of charas in the storage box of a scooter.
[@1] The two men riding it were taken into custody. [@1] Police identified them
as Shubham Narang, 28, son of Rajan Narang, and Kunal Malli, 20, son of Rakesh
Malli, both residents of Kanak Mandi in Amritsar, Punjab. [@1]

The stop was made on Friday, 30 October 2026, by a joint team from the Bakloh
police post and the Tunuhatti checkpoint that was checking vehicles on the road.
[@1] Officers stopped a scooter travelling from Banikhet towards Punjab and
searched it after the two riders began to panic. [@1]

Police seized the charas at the spot and registered a case against both men at
Chuwadi police station under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act,
India's narcotics law. [@1] Superintendent of Police Vijay Kumar Saklani
confirmed the case and said police had begun further investigation. [@1]
