A former fugitive and felon captured in New York is back facing numerous charges
in New Hampshire, according to Nashua police. [@1] The accused is 39-year-old
Jacob Reznikow. [@1] Authorities arrested him in New York on Oct. 9 after
detectives joined the BATF and the U.S. Marshals Service to search for him. [@1]
After extradition to New Hampshire, he was charged in Nashua on Oct. 23. [@1] He
faces 20 felony and misdemeanor counts. [@1] The Nashua charges comprise
receiving stolen property, felonious use of firearms, four counts of possession
of a controlled drug with intent to sell/distribute-subsequent and nine
convicted felons charges, all felonies, as well as conduct after an accident and
four misdemeanor counts of possession of drugs. [@1] Hudson also charged him
with driving after suspension, and after being charged he was held without bail
and arraigned on Oct. 26. [@1]

The case began on June 10, when Nashua police stopped Reznikow for driving under
the influence and driving after suspension. [@1] Officers said they believed
drugs were in the vehicle and obtained a search warrant. [@1] The warrant search
found what police described as a large quantity of various controlled drugs,
including psilocybin mushrooms, crack cocaine, MDMA, and marijuana and
THC-infused products, the cannabis component recorded here. [@1] Police also
reported finding a drug scale, handwritten ledgers, six handguns, two shotguns,
a rifle and significant quantities of ammunition. [@1]

Sgt. John Cinelli, the public information officer and communications division
supervisor for the Nashua Police Department, provided the account of the stop
and seizure. [@1] Detectives continued the investigation after the June seizure
while Reznikow remained at large until the October arrest in New York. [@1]
