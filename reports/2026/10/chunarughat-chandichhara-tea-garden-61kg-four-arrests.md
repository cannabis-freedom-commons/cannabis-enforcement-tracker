A team from the Shayestaganj camp of SRB-9, the police Special Response
Battalion, arrested four men with 61 kg of ganja in Chunarughat upazila,
Habiganj district. [@1] The operation took place at about 12:55 a.m. on the
night of Wednesday 28 October in the Chandichhara tea garden area of Deorgach
Union. [@1] SRB-9 said it acted on a tip that several people were in the area to
buy and sell drugs, and the four were arrested as they tried to flee when they
sensed the security force. [@1]

Officers searched four jute sacks the men were carrying and found 61 kg of ganja
wrapped in blue polythene and khaki-coloured adhesive tape. [@1] The four
arrested were Saniraotia, 24, Paresh Munda, 28, Jay Tati, 22, and Sanjit Ralli,
24, all residents of Chandichhara tea garden in Chunarughat upazila. [@1]

SRB-9's media cell said the men told officers in initial questioning that they
had worked together to collect the ganja from border areas of Habiganj and were
holding it for sale. [@1] A case was being prepared against them under the
Narcotics Control Act, 2018, and they and the recovered ganja were handed over
to Chunarughat police station. [@1] SRB-9 additional superintendent of police
and media officer K M Shahidul Islam Sohag said intelligence work and operations
would continue under the zero-tolerance policy against drugs. [@1]
