The criminal investigation branch of Jalna district police and Jafrabad police,
acting under Jalna superintendent of police Tejaswi Singh Sandhu's instructions
against cannabis cultivation and its sale, raided a farm at Bhorkheda Shivram in
Bhokardan taluka. [@1] Twenty cannabis plants were seized at Bhorkheda Shivram,
the report states. [@1] Police found cannabis cultivation at four places in
Jalna district in two months. [@1] The crop had also been planted inside cotton,
sugarcane and pigeon pea (tur) fields, the newspaper reported. [@1] The district
has been seeing a series of people found growing cannabis in various parts of
Jalna. [@1]
