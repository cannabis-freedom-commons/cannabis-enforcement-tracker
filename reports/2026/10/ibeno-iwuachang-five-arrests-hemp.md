The Akwa Ibom State Police Command arrested five suspects in an intelligence-led raid on a suspected criminal hideout at Iwuachang Village, Ibeno Local Government Area. [@1,2] The operation took place at about 8 p.m. on 22 October 2026 as part of the command's campaign against black spots and illicit drugs. [@1,2]

Those held were Enoch Monila, 28; Joseph Dani, 42; Imoh Okon, 21; Joy Linus, 25; and Thadeus Damian, 32. [@1,2] Officers recovered substantial quantities of substances suspected to be Indian hemp, plus substances identified by the street names Captain Black, Kolos and Ice, all sent for further investigation. [@1,2] Police spokesman DSP Timfon John disclosed the raid in a 23 October press statement. [@1,2] Commissioner CP Baba Mohammed Azare restated the command's zero-tolerance stance on drug trafficking and criminal hideouts. [@1]
