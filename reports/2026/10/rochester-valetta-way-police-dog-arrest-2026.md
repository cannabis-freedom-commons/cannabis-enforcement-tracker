Two Kent Police dogs played a central role in the arrest of a suspected drug
dealer and the seizure of Class A and Class B drugs in Rochester. [@1] Patrol
officers were alerted to suspicious vehicle activity in Valetta Way, Rochester
on 30 October 2026 after a Honda Civic was seen making multiple short trips to
and from the road. [@1] When officers stopped the car the driver tried to flee
on foot and Police Dog Storm, working on only his third operational shift, moved
swiftly to detain him. [@1] A 21-year-old man from London was arrested on
suspicion of possession with intent to supply Class A drugs. [@1]

Officers recovered a discarded jacket containing suspected cocaine, cannabis and
over £1,000 in cash. [@1] Police dog Tilly was then deployed to search the
vehicle and found more than 100 wraps of suspected cocaine and additional
cannabis hidden in the glove box and beneath the driver’s seat. [@1]

The arrested man has since been released on bail while police enquiries remain
ongoing. [@1]
