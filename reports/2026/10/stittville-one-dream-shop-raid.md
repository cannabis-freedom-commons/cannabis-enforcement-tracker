Oneida County Sheriff's Office investigators seized marijuana products from the
One Dream gas station and restaurant on Route 365 in Stittville. [@1] The
business is at 9060 Route 365. [@1] Investigators executed a search warrant at
the business on Oct. 14. [@1]

Investigators seized 94 grams of cannabis flower. [@1] They seized 320 grams of
cannabis edibles. [@1] They seized 96 grams of cannabis THC vapes. [@1]

Investigators also seized assorted illegal fireworks. [@1] They also seized a
quantity of Kratom products and a large quantity of flavored vape products. [@1]
