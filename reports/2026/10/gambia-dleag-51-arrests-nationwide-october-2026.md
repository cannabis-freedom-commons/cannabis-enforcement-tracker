The Drug Law Enforcement Agency of The Gambia arrested 51 suspected drug
offenders in nationwide enforcement operations. [@3] Alagie Njie, 31, of Bakau
and Yasaye Ndure, 31, of Bijilo were arrested on 26 October 2026 at the Banjul
Post Office with five parcels of suspected cannabis ruderalis, commonly known as
skunk, concealed in a breadcrumb tin inside a carton. [@2] All 51 suspects
remained in custody while investigations continued. [@1]

Thirty suspects were arrested in the West Coast Region, 12 in Kanifing
Municipality, five in the North Bank Region, three in Banjul and one in the
Central River Region. [@1] The operations also led to the recovery of suspected
cocaine, crack cocaine, crystal methamphetamine and ecstasy, as well as various
quantities of suspected cannabis sativa, kush, hashish, molly and skunk. [@1]
The suspected drugs were recovered in various forms, including wraps, parcels,
stones and sachets. [@1] Saikou Sanyang, 28, of Tanji was arrested on 20 October
with 51 parcels of suspected cannabis sativa and 81 pieces of suspected kush.
[@2] Ousman Saidy, 34, and Ismaila Darboe, 36, both residents of Tanji, were
arrested on 28 October with 93 wraps of suspected cannabis sativa, 85 pieces of
suspected kush and additional cannabis. [@2] Three men from Pirang, Seedy Sonko,
33, Ebrima Gibba, 27, and Hadim Sonko, 24, were arrested on 14 October for
allegedly obstructing officers in the execution of their duties. [@1]

DLEAG warned the public against obstructing or interfering with its officers and
urged citizens to cooperate, advising anyone who believes an officer acted
improperly to seek redress through legal channels. [@1]
