Military Police arrested two youths aged 19 and 20 in the early afternoon of
Saturday 31 October during an operation in the district of Vista Alegre do
Abunã. [@1] The detainees were identified only by the initials C. L. de O. G.,
aged 20 and known as “Sheik”, and E. da S. S., aged 19 and known as “Tóquio”.
[@1] When officers arrived, several people nearby fled, and two suspects were
chased and caught by the military police. [@1] The two youths were arrested at
the scene and taken to the Central de Flagrantes in Porto Velho with the drugs
and other seized materials. [@1]

During the stops officers found portions of cannabis, cocaine and crack with the
suspects, then searched the property and found more drugs, precision scales,
mobile phones and money. [@1] The reported seizure included 110 cannabis
wrappers, 71 cocaine portions, 175 crack portions, one larger cannabis portion
and three small portions known as bombinhas. [@1] The count of individually
wrapped packages reached 356 drug portions, not counting the additional cannabis
portions. [@1] A second outlet gave the same breakdown of 110 cannabis wrappers,
71 cocaine portions, 175 crack portions, one larger cannabis portion and three
bombinhas. [@2]

The operation was carried out by officers of the 9th Military Police Battalion,
9º BPM, with support from the Rural Patrol. [@1] Teams began the inquiries after
receiving a report that a motorcycle stolen from an inn could be hidden in a
property described by police as a drug-selling location. [@1] The address was at
the crossing of Tiradentes and Pedro Lima streets, where police set up a cordon
around the area. [@1] Officers also found a red Honda CG 160 Titan motorcycle at
the property and seized it to check its possible use in criminal activity. [@1]
