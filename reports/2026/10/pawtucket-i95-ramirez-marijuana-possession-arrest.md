Rhode Island State Police troopers pulled over Isaiah Ramirez, a 26-year-old man
from Providence, on Interstate 95 in Pawtucket shortly after 10 a.m. on Friday
30 October 2026. [@1] Ramirez was arrested at the roadside stop and charged with
driving on a suspended licence and with possessing marijuana. [@1] The marijuana
charge was recorded as possession of more than two ounces, and the licence
charge as a fourth-and-subsequent offence. [@2]

Ramirez was processed at the Lincoln Woods Barracks and transported to the Sixth
Division District Court for arraignment. [@1] No court outcome beyond the
pending arraignment was reported in these accounts. [@1]
