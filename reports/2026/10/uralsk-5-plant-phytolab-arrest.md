Police in Uralsk detained a 39-year-old man suspected of storing and selling
drugs. [@1] The detained Uralsk resident owned an online shop allegedly involved
in selling and storing drugs in the region. [@2] Police opened a pre-trial
investigation, with further investigative steps under way to establish all the
circumstances. [@1]

At a dacha plot, officers found a room equipped as a phytolaboratory for growing
cannabis and seized five cannabis bushes there. [@1] The same equipped room held
a fan, lighting lamps, exhaust hoods, temperature sensors and fertilisers. [@2]
At his residence, officers seized five wraps of plant material and a large
quantity of seeds believed to be cannabis. [@1] Officers also seized an iPhone
13 during the home search. [@2]

Anti-drug officers of the West Kazakhstan police department carried out the
operation with Burkіt special police during the Qarasora-2026 preventive
campaign. [@2] Police said the offence is punishable by five to eight years’
imprisonment with confiscation of property. [@1]
