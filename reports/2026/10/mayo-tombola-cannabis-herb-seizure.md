Gardaí in Mayo seized cannabis herb together with a large quantity of assorted
fireworks. [@1] The seizure was made in an intelligence-led operation jointly
conducted by the MRL Divisional Drugs Unit and Mayo North CE under Operation
Tombola. [@1] The same Mayo operation was described as an intelligence-led
operation jointly conducted by the Divisional Drugs Unit. [@2] Investigations
were still ongoing after the seizure. [@2]

Gardaí valued the cannabis herb at €2,000. [@1] Gardaí valued the large number
of assorted fireworks at €4,600. [@2] Both the cannabis and the fireworks were
subject to further analysis. [@2]

Operation Tombola is the nationwide policing plan focused on the prevention and
detection of the sale of fireworks and associated public order and anti-social
behaviour issues during the Halloween period. [@1] The detections were made
during the implementation of that Halloween-period plan. [@2] Under Irish law,
it is illegal to import, sell or use any other fireworks without a licence. [@1]
The operation formed part of the Halloween-period policing effort against
fireworks sales and related anti-social behaviour. [@2]
