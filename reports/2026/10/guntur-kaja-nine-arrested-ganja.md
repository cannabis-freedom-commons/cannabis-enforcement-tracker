Nine people were arrested in a raid on a ganja-selling spot at a market centre
near the NH-16 underpass in Kaja Venkatareddypalem, Guntur district, on the
night of 30 October 2026. [@2] The nine were detained after some of them tried
to run when they saw the police. [@1]

Officers found the ganja in a blue college bag and in the dashboard of the car
the group was using. [@2] They seized about 3.1 kg of ganja and a Hyundai Exter
used to transport it. [@2] Police also took a weighing machine and packing
material and valued the ganja at about ₹62,000. [@3] Eight of the nine arrested
were customers who had come to buy from the main accused, Chadala Nandamayya.
[@2]

Investigators said Nandamayya, a painter, had bought four kilograms of ganja a
week earlier from Venigalla Padma of Vijayawada at ₹17,000 a kilogram, paying
₹68,000. [@2] He sold about a kilogram near Piduguralla to a man named Akash.
[@2] He kept some and packed another kilogram into 50-gram sachets sold at
₹1,000 each. [@2] Padma and Akash are at large and police said they were
searching for them. [@3]

District superintendent of police Vakul Jindal announced the case at a press
conference in Guntur on 31 October. [@2] Six of the nine arrested had criminal
records and three were rowdy-sheeters, police said. [@3] The sales targeted
young people in Mangalagiri, Vijayawada and Kaja. [@1] Jindal asked residents to
report drug activity and said informants' details would be kept confidential.
[@2]
