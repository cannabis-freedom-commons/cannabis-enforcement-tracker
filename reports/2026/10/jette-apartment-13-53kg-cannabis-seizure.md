Police in the Bruxelles-Ouest zone, which polices Jette and the neighbouring
municipalities of Molenbeek-Saint-Jean, Koekelberg, Ganshoren and
Berchem-Sainte-Agathe, found 13.53 kg of cannabis and 8,670 euros during a
search of an apartment in Jette on Tuesday 6 October 2026. [@1]

The case began during a preventive patrol on avenue de l'Exposition, where
officers noticed a person riding a scooter who tried to flee at their sight.
[@1] When stopped, the rider spontaneously handed them a bag holding 8.16 grams
of cannabis and was deprived of liberty and taken for questioning. [@1] Officers
then searched an apartment, where they found a second person filling bags with
cannabis; that person was also arrested. [@1] Cannabis was found in almost every
room, and in total officers found 9.55 kg of cannabis, 3.98 kg of cannabis
flowers, seven bottles of GHB and 8,670 euros, along with precision scales,
packaging material and mobile phones. [@1]

Two suspects were placed at the disposal of the Brussels prosecutor's office and
transferred to the courthouse on Wednesday. [@1] Sudinfo put the raid on Tuesday
6 October and described the first arrest as that of a man who had tried to
escape a Bruxelles-Ouest patrol on avenue de l'Exposition. [@2] Sudinfo
described the haul as nearly 14 kilos of cannabis and split it into 9.55 kg of
cannabis herb and 3.98 kg of cannabis flowers, totalling 13.53 kg. [@2] The
Brussels Times reported that the two arrested suspects were men, citing the
Brussels-West police zone's statement. [@3] The Brussels Times described the
seven bottles of GHB as a synthetic sedative known as liquid ecstasy and listed
the scales, packaging material and phones among the items confiscated. [@3]
L'Avenir reported that police found cannabis in practically every room of the
apartment and that the suspects were placed at the disposal of the Brussels
prosecutor's office. [@4] L'Avenir said the search was carried out in the
apartment of the man stopped on the scooter while he was being questioned. [@4]
