Konya Provincial Gendarmerie Command carried out anti-drug operations across
Konya from 1 to 31 October 2026 as part of its work to prevent criminal
activity. [@1,2] Officers caught 82 suspects during the month-long operations.
[@1,2] Officers released 75 people after taking their statements and remanded 7
suspects in custody, sending them to prison. [@1,2] The gendarmerie said the
investigation and similar operations were continuing. [@1]

Searches seized 2,779 grams of kubar esrar and 850 grams of skunk, reported as
part of various esrar types, which together form the 3.629 kilograms of cannabis
recorded here. [@1,2] Officers also seized 9 skunk roots, counted here as 9
cannabis plants. [@1,2] The same October tally also listed 8,418 synthetic pills,
488 ecstasy tablets, 219 grams of bonzai, 180 grams of pregabalin, 67 grams of
methamphetamine, 39 captagon tablets and 12 grams of cocaine, which are not
counted as cannabis. [@1] Officers also listed 302 grams of cannabis seeds and 4
grams of skunk seeds, which are excluded from the cannabis weight because they
are not dried cannabis, resin or oil. [@1]

The command described the seizures as the result of its October inspection and
prevention work in the region. [@1] The monthly results were reported together
as a single coordinated outcome, with the cannabis types distinguished from the
synthetic and other drugs in the same haul. [@1]
