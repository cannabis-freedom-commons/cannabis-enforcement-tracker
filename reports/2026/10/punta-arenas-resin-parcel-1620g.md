Detectives of the BRIANT, the anti-narcotics brigade of the PDI, Chile's investigative police, in
Punta Arenas seized 1,620 grams of cannabis resin in 100 containers. [@1,2,3] The resin was a highly
concentrated form of cannabis, and the PDI valued it at about 2.5 million pesos. [@1,2,3]

The drug was hidden in a parcel sent from Santiago, and the recipient was detained when he went to a
transport company in Punta Arenas to collect it. [@2] Detectives located the parcel through an
exchange of information with the prosecutor's criminal-analysis unit before it reached the recipient.
[@2]

The man, an adult Chilean with no police record, was formalised on Thursday 8 October 2026 on a
charge of illicit drug trafficking and remanded in pre-trial detention at the prosecutor's request.
[@1,2,3] The court set an initial 45-day investigation period. [@2] Subprefect Alfonso Salazar, head
of BRIANT Punta Arenas, said the man had been actively selling cannabis in the Magallanes region and
distributed it "through different methods". [@1,2,3] ITV Patagonia reported that detectives were
working to identify his suppliers, and that the prosecutor was considering asking for more time
because of inquiries outside the region. [@2]
