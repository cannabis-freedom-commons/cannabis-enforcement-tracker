Cagliari's Squadra Mobile, the city's mobile investigative police unit, was
working on anti-drug services against the spread of drugs among very young
people, with observation by investigators in the Cagliari metropolitan area.
[@1] During that service, officers focused on a 19-year-old whose behaviour led
police to intervene and stop and check him. [@1,3] The check found about 70 grams
of hashish in his possession, which in light of the circumstances was judged
intended for subsequent transfer to third parties. [@1,3]

He was arrested on suspicion of possessing drugs for dealing, and after the
validation hearing he was released. [@1,3] A second account of the police activity
dates this first Elmas intervention to 9 October, when investigators arrested
the 19-year-old with about 70 grams of hashish considered intended for dealing.
[@2]

That account describes the checks as targeted observation and
territorial-control services aimed at youth deviance, the framework in which the
Elmas stop occurred. [@2]
