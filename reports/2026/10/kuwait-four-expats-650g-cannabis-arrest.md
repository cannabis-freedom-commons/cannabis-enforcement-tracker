Kuwait's Interior Ministry announced on 27 October 2026 that the General Department for Drug Control had arrested four expatriates in three separate investigations into suspected trafficking in narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances. [@1,2] The operations seized 650 grams of cannabis together with 200 capsules of Lyrica, a medicine subject to controls, and three precision weighing scales. [@1,2] The Lyrica capsules are not cannabis and are excluded from the cannabis figure above. [@1]

Security checks found that two of the four were already wanted under earlier arrest warrants, with two absence reports also on record. [@1] All four were referred to the Public Prosecution, and the ministry said the appropriate procedures were initiated in each case. [@1]
