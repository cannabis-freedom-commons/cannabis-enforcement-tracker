Kuwait's drug-control officers detained four expatriates in three cases involving 650 grams of cannabis. [@1,2,3]

Kuwait's Interior Ministry said its General Department for Drug Control detained
four expatriates in three separate cases on suspicion of drug-trafficking
activity. [@3] The department referred the four with the seized items to the
Public Prosecution to complete the legal procedures. [@3] Kuwait's Al-Anbaa
newspaper confirmed the referral in a report published on Thursday 29 October
2026. [@3]

The ministry's security statement of Tuesday 27 October 2026 listed 650 grams of
marijuana among the seizures from the three cases. [@3] Officers also seized 200
capsules of the psychoactive substance known as Lyrica and three precision
scales. [@3] The seized items were preserved as evidence and sent to the
competent authorities for investigation of the incidents. [@3]

A security records check found prior arrest orders against two of the detainees
alongside two absence reports concerning them. [@3] The detentions occurred in
three separate cases rather than one joint case involving all four people. [@3]
No final court judgment had been issued against any of the four at the time of
reporting. [@3] Responsibility for determining charges and penalties remains
with the judiciary based on investigations and evidence presented during
litigation. [@3]
