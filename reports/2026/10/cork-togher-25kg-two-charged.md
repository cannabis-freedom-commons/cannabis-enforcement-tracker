Gardaí from the Cork City Divisional Drugs Unit, assisted by other local and
regional units, searched a domestic residence in Togher, Cork, on the night of
Monday 5 October 2026 under warrant, as part of an operation targeting the sale
and supply of drugs. [@1] Two men in their twenties were arrested and taken to
garda stations in the city. [@1] The search recovered suspected cannabis, which
gardaí said would be forwarded to Forensic Science Ireland for analysis. [@1]

The men were identified in court as Lee Crinnion, 21, of Blackwater Grove,
Togher, and Aaron Austin, 25, of Araglen Court, Togher. [@2] Detective Garda
Darren Twomey and Detective Garda Ruairi McGovern of the divisional drugs unit
made the arrests. [@2] Both were charged with possession of cannabis and
possession for sale or supply at Togher Road on 5 October, and neither made any
reply when the charges were put to them after caution. [@2]

At Cork District Court on 7 October, the court heard the seizure amounted to up
to 25 kg of cannabis with a street value of €500,000, a sum the detective said
meant the case would eventually be dealt with by a higher court. [@2] Gardaí
did not oppose bail, and Judge David Waters remanded both men on bail with
conditions including an 11pm to 7am curfew, signing on three times a week at
Togher Garda Station, surrendering their passports, and having no contact with
each other. [@2] They are due back before the court on 3 November for the
directions of the Director of Public Prosecutions. [@2]
