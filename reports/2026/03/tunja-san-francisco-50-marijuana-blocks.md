Tunja police, through the National Police's Criminal Investigation Section
(SIJIN), arrested a 32-year-old man. [@1] Boyacá 7 Días identified the man as
Luis Carlos Sánchez. [@2] The newspaper said Sijín officers stopped and
inspected his pickup on the afternoon of 5 March while he travelled through
Tunja. [@2] The police account placed the operation in the San Francisco
neighborhood and said officers apprehended him while he was driving. [@1] Police
said officers found 50 pressed blocks of marijuana in the vehicle. [@1] Boyacá 7
Días described three rectangular packages containing close to 25 kilograms of
marijuana and said this led to his arrest in the act. [@2]

The police account described the seized amount as 26,000 grams of narcotics.
[@1] Authorities estimated the narcotics' criminal-market value at more than 130
million pesos. [@1]

The police account said he and the seized items were given to Colombia's
Attorney General's Office, which it identified as the authority imposing house
arrest. [@1] Boyacá 7 Días reported that prosecutors presented him to a judge as
a suspect in drug trafficking, manufacture or possession. [@2] The newspaper
reported that a judge ordered house detention after preliminary hearings while
the case continued. [@2] It said he did not accept the charges. [@2]
