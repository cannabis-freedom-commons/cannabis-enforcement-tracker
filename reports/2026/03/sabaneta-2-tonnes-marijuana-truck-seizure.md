In a joint operation by the National Police and the Attorney General's Office,
authorities seized two tonnes of marijuana in the municipality of Sabaneta,
Antioquia. [@1] The load was carried in a cargo truck travelling departmental
roads, camouflaged in boxes to simulate legal merchandise. [@1] An intelligence
operation intercepted the marijuana-loaded truck, whose final destination was
the metropolitan area of Medellin. [@1]

According to official information, the drug belonged to the organized criminal
group Los Triana and was destined for the Valle de Aburra metropolitan area,
where it would be distributed to strengthen illegal economies and expand
drug-trafficking networks. [@1] Investigators established that the stash had
been acquired in the department of Cauca by the Dagoberto Ramos criminal
organization, one of the FARC dissidences under the command of alias Ivan
Mordisco. [@1] The seizure prevented the circulation of millions of doses in
Medellin and neighbouring municipalities and directly affected Los Triana's
finances from planned retail sale in the comunas of the Valle de Aburra. [@1]

The National Police director said the operation weakened the criminal groups'
financial and operational capacity and kept the drugs off the streets. [@1]
Authorities are investigating whether more people were involved and are
continuing controls to block the flow of drugs toward urban centres. [@1]
