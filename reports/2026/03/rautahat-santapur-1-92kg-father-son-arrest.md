Police in Rautahat arrested 50-year-old shopkeeper Tejilal Yadav and his
22-year-old son Lalbabu Yadav on Thursday afternoon in Santapur Tole, Fatuha
Vijayapur Municipality-5. [@1] Officers from the Area Police Office, Santapur
Dostia, found the cannabis during a search of the grocery store operated by
Tejilal in the same locality. [@1] The search seized 1 kilogram and 920 grams of
illegal cannabis, which police described as marijuana. [@1] Both men were
arrested at the shop, so this cannabis seizure accounts for two arrests. [@1]

The Rautahat seizure was reported alongside other arrests that brought a
nationwide roundup total to 15 people across districts including Kathmandu,
Banke, Kanchanpur and Rautahat. [@1] Other incidents in the same roundup
involved substances described as resembling brown sugar in Kathmandu, Banke and
Kanchanpur. [@1] A Makwanpur seizure involved 3 kilograms and 478 grams of a
substance described as resembling opium. [@1]

A Rupandehi seizure involved 12 ampoules of Diazepam, 16 ampoules of
Promethazine and 24 ampoules of Buprenorphine, none of which is counted as
cannabis here. [@1] Police said further investigation is underway regarding all
the incidents, and no charge or court outcome for the two Rautahat men was
reported. [@1]
