A resident alerted police to the plantation, which officers first found during routine monitoring on 16 March 2026. [@1,2] The eradication operation began at about 3 a.m. the following day in the mountainous Sitio Gunot area of Barangay Kimbutan. [@2] The site was in a remote upland area accessible only by a four-hour trek, the Philippine Information Agency reported. [@1]

Police uprooted 10,311 fully grown plants spread across about 1,200 square metres. [@1,2] The PIA put the crop's estimated street value at ₱20 million, while Bombo Radyo Cauayan reported an estimate above ₱20 million. [@1,2] The operation was led by Dupax del Sur police with the provincial mobile force company and police intelligence unit; representatives from the Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency, barangay officials and the Department of Justice were present. [@1]

Police said the investigation would continue to identify those responsible for the plantation. [@1,2] Neither report named a suspect or reported an arrest. [@1,2]
