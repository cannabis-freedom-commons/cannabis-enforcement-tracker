Niger's OCRTIS anti-narcotics office dismantled a transnational cannabis network on
31 March 2026 at Tchintabaraden, in the Tahoua region. [@1,2] Agents carrying out a
routine check on an Algerian-registered truck travelling from Tahoua toward Tamanrasset,
Algeria, found the cannabis hidden in a compartment built into the chassis and in spare
tyres. [@1,2]

The seizure came to 154 bricks and 28 balls of cannabis, which the authorities value
at more than 30 million CFA francs. [@1,2] Ten people were detained — seven Nigeriens,
two Algerians and one Malian, aged between 16 and 42. [@1,2]

Early investigations found the network brought the cannabis in from Ghana through Benin
and Nigeria, stored it in a house in Tahoua belonging to one member, and moved it on
toward Algeria. [@1,2] The ten were presented to local authorities at the Tchintabaraden
police headquarters on 5 April 2026, where the OCRTIS unit chief and the departmental
police director recounted the operation and the prefect praised the security forces.
[@1,2]
