Investigators from the government’s joint investigation headquarters for
narcotics crimes lifted artificial turf between two greenhouses in Ganghwa,
Incheon, revealing a staircase down into a concealed space. [@1] Inside the
concealed basement they found smart-farming equipment with LED lighting and
ventilation, with marijuana growing in dozens of flowerpots, totaling 134
plants. [@1] Authorities also found 2.8 kilograms of dried marijuana that had
been harvested and stored, an amount investigators estimated at about 28,000
doses. [@1]

The two suspects, middle school classmates, received government smart-farm
start-up support loans of 500 million won each in January 2024, grew basil, and
also received an electricity discount and a monthly youth start-up voucher. [@1]
Investigators believe the pair were later approached by a dealer active on the
dark web, built the underground bunker and began growing marijuana there in
September last year, while the greenhouses above ground were left largely empty.
[@1] The joint probe unit arrested one suspect and booked the other on suspicion
of violating the Act on Special Cases Concerning the Prevention of Illegal
Trafficking in Narcotics. [@1] The Korean report identifies the men as A and B,
both aged 36, and says the headquarters detained A and booked B on suspicion of
violating the narcotics special-case act. [@2] A prosecution official said the
suspects claim they only grew marijuana, but an additional 500 to 600 grams
packaged for wholesale and retail was found hidden in a nearby hillside, so
investigators are also looking at whether distribution took place. [@1] KBS
reports that one of the men acted as a drug dropper by hiding grown cannabis on
a nearby hillside and received cash or virtual assets from sellers. [@3]

Yonhap News TV confirms that 134 cannabis plants were growing in the underground
bunker. [@4] The Incheon Ilbo confirms that the group in Ganghwa, Incheon posed
as farmers to obtain 1 billion won in smart-farm start-up support and grew the
cannabis in a secret bunker beneath a vinyl greenhouse. [@5] The headquarters
presented the case among its first 100 days of work since its launch in November
2025, in which it booked 124 major supply-side suspects and arrested 56 of them.
[@1]
