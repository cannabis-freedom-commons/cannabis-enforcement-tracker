Nubian Greaux of Shadwell Housing was charged with possession of cannabis and
cultivation of cannabis with intent to supply. [@1] The operation was carried
out by the Anti-Narcotics Unit of the Royal St. Christopher and Nevis Police
Force, supported by the Joint Security Operations Tactical Team. [@1] Officers
executed the search warrant at his residence between 5:00 a.m. and 6:00 a.m. on
26 March 2026. [@1] Greaux was taken into custody and subsequently charged at
the Basseterre Police Station. [@1]

During the search, officers found 271 cannabis plants at various stages of
maturity, plus several packages of vegetable material later confirmed to be
cannabis. [@1] The Crime Scene Unit processed the scene and all items of
evidential value were seized. [@1]
