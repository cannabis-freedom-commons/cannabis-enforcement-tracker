Detectives seized hundreds of cannabis plants worth more than $650,000 in a raid
at a Hunter property. [@1] Officers attached to the Hunter Valley Police
District began investigating cannabis cultivation in the Hunter Valley earlier
in 2026. [@2] About 9.30am on Tuesday 24 March 2026, Hunter Valley detectives
and the Hunter Valley Proactive Crime Team executed a search warrant at the
Hunterview property. [@2] No arrests or charges were reported from the raid.
[@3]

During the search, police found an elaborate hydroponic cannabis cultivation
setup inside the home. [@4] Police seized 328 mature cannabis plants, which they
estimate had a potential street value of more than $650,000. [@2] Investigators
also seized 78 cannabis seedlings, dried cannabis and hydroponic equipment. [@2]
The property was found to be illegally connected to the power grid and was
disconnected with the assistance of a utility provider. [@2] Specialist police
forensically examined the scene. [@3]

The early-2026 Hunter Valley investigation led detectives to the property in
Hunterview. [@4] Hunter Valley police said inquiries were continuing and urged
anyone with information to contact Crime Stoppers on 1800 333 000. [@4]
