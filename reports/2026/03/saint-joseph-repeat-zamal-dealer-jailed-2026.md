A man already convicted of cannabis trafficking was arrested again in
Saint-Joseph after offering herb for sale on social networks. [@1] The operation
ended an illicit trade that had been supplying the neighbourhood for several
months. [@1] Brought before the Saint-Pierre prosecutor on 3 March 2026 for a
guilty-plea hearing (CRPC), he was sentenced to 12 months of firm prison with
revocation of an 18-month suspended term. [@1] He was jailed immediately, and
his seized telephone and money were confiscated. [@1]

Gendarmes of the Saint-Joseph brigade community (COB) opened an investigation
after being alerted. [@1] Their work uncovered a zamal cannabis grow in the
suspect's garden. [@1] At the arrest they seized 718 grams of cannabis herb.
[@1] They also seized 25 cannabis plants. [@1] They also seized a knuckle duster
and a tear-gas canister, along with other items linked to the trafficking. [@1]

The Saint-Pierre judicial court had already convicted him for similar acts in
March 2024. [@1] From June 2024 he was again offering cannabis for sale via
Facebook. [@1]
