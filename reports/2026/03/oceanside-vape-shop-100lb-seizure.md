On 12 March 2026 the Oceanside Police Department, DEA Carlsbad resident office agents, Naval
Criminal Investigative Service agents and San Diego County Sheriff's Department marijuana
enforcement detectives checked four vape and smoke shops in Oceanside for compliance. [@1,2]

Investigators seized more than 100 pounds (about 45 kg) of products they believed contained
controlled or regulated substances, including items suspected to contain psilocybin, THC and kratom. [@1,2]
DEA Special Agent in Charge James Nunnallee said vaping devices can conceal drugs that young people
do not recognise. [@1] Police spokesperson Gina Avalos told NBC 7 that four people would face state
fines and other administrative penalties. [@2]

Four people were found to have violated state regulations and were referred for administrative
penalties and fines from the state, rather than criminal charges described in the releases. [@1,2]
