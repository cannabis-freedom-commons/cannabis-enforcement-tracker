Between 15 and 18 March 2026 the Brigade de Recherches of the Mbour Gendarmerie
company seized a total of 840 kilograms of chanvre indien, the French term for
cannabis. [@1]

On 15 March, officers intercepted a 510-kilogram load of cannabis coming from
Casamance and arrested the owner and the piroguier, a canoe driver. [@1] On 17
March, a second raid at Nguékokh, where a house was identified as a stash,
seized a further 330 kilograms and immobilised two transport vehicles. [@1]

In total, seven people were arrested and are being prosecuted for criminal
association and drug trafficking. [@1] At the 17 March raid, five individuals
were arrested on the spot at the Nguékokh house used as a base. [@2] The
suspects are being prosecuted for criminal association and drug trafficking.
[@2] Seven people were arrested in total in this affair linked to a structured
drug-trafficking network. [@3] Altogether, the different operations removed more
than 840 kilograms of cannabis from circulation, dealing a significant blow to
the trafficking network. [@4]
