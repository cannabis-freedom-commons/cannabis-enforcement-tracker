A driver lost control of his car on the rue de Luxembourg in Bridel in the night
of 11 March 2026 at about 1.15am, left the road and hit a public street lighting
post, then drove off without stopping, committing a hit-and-run offence. [@1] A
witness' information let officers find the vehicle shortly afterwards in the rue
« Am klenge Park » in Bridel, where the suspected driver was also found. [@1]
Luxembourg's public prosecutor's office said officers found the man in a
manifest state of intoxication, and that a drug screening test came back
positive for cannabis. [@1] Checks showed he was already banned from driving and
had never held a valid driving licence. [@1] The Tageblatt reported that the
suspect was strongly intoxicated at the check and that a drug screening test
showed he was under the influence of cannabis. [@4] Virgule reported that the
driver was found drunk and had tested positive for cannabis while driving under
a ban without ever having held a valid licence. [@2]

The suspect had previous convictions for road traffic offences. [@1] Citing the
seriousness of the facts, the hit-and-run and the man's record, the prosecutor's
office ordered his arrest, and he was deprived of liberty and brought before an
investigating judge. [@1] After questioning that ran into the evening of
Wednesday 11 March, he was charged and placed under strict judicial supervision.
[@2] RTL Infos reported that the judge placed him under strict judicial
supervision on the Wednesday evening. [@3]

The prosecutor's office said road safety is a priority and that driving without
a licence while banned, intoxication, drug use and fleeing the scene cannot be
tolerated. [@1] The communiqué added that where conduct seriously endangers road
safety, coercive measures such as arrest, deprivation of liberty, being brought
before an investigating judge, charging and strict judicial control, or even a
committal warrant, can be ordered. [@1]
