The Narcotics Control Bureau arrested five people while dismantling an
international drug trafficking network operating across Nepal, India and Sri
Lanka. [@2,4] Its Chennai and Hyderabad zonal units ran the coordinated operation
after intelligence pointed to contraband moving from Nepal through India to Sri
Lanka by sea. [@2] Hyderabad officers intercepted a Tata Safari Storme at Raikal
Toll Plaza on the Bengaluru-Hyderabad highway in Telangana on 3 March 2026. [@3,4]
A search found 2 kg of charas hidden in specially built cavities, and the two
occupants, residents of Delhi and Uttar Pradesh, were taken into custody. [@3,4]
Acting on their questioning, Chennai officers held three more people in
Thoothukudi, Tamil Nadu, including a Sri Lankan refugee, and seized about 78 kg
of hashish oil. [@2,4]

The operation seized 77.60 kg of hashish oil and 2 kg of charas, with an
estimated street value of about Rs 10 crore. [@3] About 78 kg of hashish oil had
already been delivered at Thoothukudi for onward trafficking to Sri Lanka by sea
route. [@1] Officers also seized two cars, a motorbike and a fishing boat used
to move the drugs. [@2] In a coordinated effort with the Indian Coast Guard,
authorities intercepted the suspect fishing boat near the International Maritime
Boundary Line, thwarting the cross-border attempt. [@3,4]

Investigators said the contraband had been smuggled into India from Kathmandu,
Nepal, through the Sonauli border. [@3,4] The agency identified a Sri Lankan
national based in Sri Lanka who allegedly financed and coordinated the shipment
from Nepal to Sri Lanka. [@2] Investigators said work was under way to identify
other syndicate members, trace financial transactions and dismantle the wider
network. [@2]
