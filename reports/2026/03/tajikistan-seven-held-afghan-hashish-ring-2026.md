Tajik law-enforcement and security bodies announced the arrest of seven alleged
members of two transnational drug groups. [@1] The Interior Ministry reported
five alleged dealers in its MVD News programme aired on 11 March, naming
39-year-old Hamadoni resident Gholibjon Ganiev as leader who had previously
received a seven-year sentence. [@1] The other four were named as 52-year-old
Rasht residents Mahmadroib Asrorov and Hakimjon Mahmadjonov and 31-year-old
Firouz Mirzoyev and 41-year-old Mukhtor Rajabov from Hamadoni, all previously
convicted for drugs. [@2] The State Committee for National Security separately
reported the arrest of two smugglers, one an Afghan citizen. [@1] Those two were
identified as Tajik citizen Oyatullo Odinaev and Afghan citizen Mubaraksho
Shaidullo, cooperating in smuggling since 2023. [@1]

The seven are suspected of smuggling about 100 kilograms of hashish, opium and
methamphetamine from Afghanistan into Tajikistan for sale inside the country and
abroad. [@2] According to investigators, the first group sold hashish, opium and
methamphetamine in Dushanbe and Rasht district. [@1] Authorities allege the
suspects brought drugs from Afghanistan to Rasht district and Dushanbe, then hid
them for onward export. [@1] In November 2025 in two stages they received 80
parcels with hashish, opium and methamphetamine through an Afghan national named
Yusuf. [@1] Together with group member Firouz Mirzoyev they hid 41 parcels in
Mir Said Ali Hamadoni and the rest in Shohmansur, according to the Interior
Ministry. [@1] Authorities seized 20 kilograms of drugs from the two detained in
the second case. [@1] In February 2026 Odinaev and Shaidullo bought 20 packets
of hashish and moved them to Vahdat for sale. [@1]

A criminal case was opened against the detainees under Article 200 Part 4
Subparagraph e of Tajikistan's Criminal Code for especially large trafficking of
narcotics or psychotropic substances. [@1] That offence carries 12 to 20 years
in prison. [@1] Ganiev is shown admitting drug dealing on a video released by
the authorities, obtained under unclear circumstances. [@1] Both Vahdat suspects
admitted guilt in the official account, with the circumstances of those
statements unspecified, while the other suspects' positions are unknown. [@1]
