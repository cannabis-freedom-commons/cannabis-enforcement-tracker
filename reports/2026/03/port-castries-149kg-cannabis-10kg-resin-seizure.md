On 26 March 2026, officers from the Customs and Excise Department and the Gangs,
Narcotics and Firearms Unit, supported by other Royal Saint Lucia Police Force
units, seized drugs and cash from a shipment at Port Castries in Castries. [@1]
Officers found the drugs after noticing an anomaly during a routine inspection
of the shipment and carrying out a more thorough search. [@1] The search
uncovered approximately 149.69 kilograms of compressed cannabis and 10.144
kilograms of cannabis resin concealed in the shipment. [@1] One individual was
in custody assisting investigators, with no charges reported and the
investigation continuing. [@1]

Officials put the street value of the compressed cannabis at about EC$3,742,250
and the cannabis resin at EC$405,760. [@2] Officers also found USD $110,000 in
cash concealed in the same shipment. [@1] Authorities had not disclosed the
shipment's origin or intended destination, and no additional suspects or
networks had been publicly identified. [@3]
