On 9 March, Kunming Changshui Airport Customs flagged a passenger’s suitcase after screening showed an anomaly. Officers found 18 bags of brown-green plant material in the luggage. [@1,2]

Testing first returned a positive result for cannabis, and a later examination identified the material as cannabis weighing 907.4 g net. Customs said it had opened a criminal case. [@1,2]

The customs reports do not name the passenger or say whether an arrest followed. [@1,2]
