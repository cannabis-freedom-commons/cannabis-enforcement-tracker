Police Regional Office Cordillera and the Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency’s CAR office
executed a search warrant on 26 March 2026 at a marijuana greenhouse in Barangay Caponga,
Tublay, Benguet. [@1,2] The warrant followed monitoring after the greenhouse owner’s arrest in
a buy-bust operation in the same barangay on 12 March 2026, and was issued on 24 March by the
Regional Trial Court in La Trinidad. [@1,2]

Regional directors Ericson Dilag of PRO CAR and Martin Francia of PDEA-CAR led the raid, with
Benguet provincial police and media witnesses present. [@1,2] Operatives uprooted 90 fully grown
marijuana plants inside the greenhouse, valued at PHP 18,000 at the standard drug price. [@1,2]
They inventoried and destroyed the plants on site, keeping samples for the Regional Forensic
Unit–CAR. [@1,2] Dilag urged communities to report early signs of illegal drug activity. [@1,2]
