Police in the Komi Republic stopped a car driven by a 34-year-old resident of
Arkhangelsk Oblast on the outskirts of Syktyvdinsky District. [@1] Searching the
car, police found a wrap of dark-green substance hidden inside a binocular case
in the cabin. [@1] Testing identified the seized material as hashish weighing
6.5 grams. [@1]

Officers detained the driver, saying he worked as a wholesale courier-stash
maker for a shadow online drug shop. [@1] He had collected a master stash of
about 4 kilograms from a curator-directed location, divided it and placed it in
hiding places across two regions. [@1] Police located his caches in the city of
Ukhta and Syktyvdinsky District and seized about 1 kilogram of mephedrone and
more than 1.3 kilograms of hashish there. [@1] Police in Arkhangelsk Oblast
separately seized more than 1.5 kilograms of mephedrone and 50 grams of cocaine
from a forest cache he had made there. [@1]

Investigators opened a criminal case for attempted sale on an especially large
scale under part 3 of article 30 and point g of part 5 of article 228.1. [@1]
The man was remanded in custody for the investigation. [@1] Police said they
were continuing operational and investigative measures to establish the
circumstances and the supply channels. [@1]
