From 9 to 13 March 2026 the Central Narcotics Bureau (CNB) arrested 84 suspected drug
offenders in an island-wide anti-drug operation covering areas including Buangkok, Balestier,
Choa Chu Kang and Toa Payoh. [@1,2]

Officers seized about 1,497 g of cannabis, 169 g of methamphetamine, 5 g of heroin, 1 g of
ketamine, one Erimin-5 tablet and five bottles of GHB, which CNB estimated at more than
S$73,400 and enough heroin, Ice and cannabis to supply about 314 abusers for a week. [@1,2]

On the afternoon of 11 March officers arrested a 27-year-old foreign man in a Kaki Bukit
commercial building and found about 520 g of cannabis, 17 g of Ice and drug paraphernalia in
the building. [@1,2] On 12 March raids on two hotel rooms near Lorong 8 Geylang led to three
Singaporeans being held; one room held about 64 g of cannabis and a small amount of Ice, and
officers forced entry into another room after occupants refused access, restraining a man who
struggled and recovering paraphernalia the pair had thrown from the window. [@1,2]

Trafficking more than 500 g of cannabis can carry a mandatory death sentence under Singapore
law. [@1,2] CNB said inquiries into all those arrested were continuing. [@1,2]
