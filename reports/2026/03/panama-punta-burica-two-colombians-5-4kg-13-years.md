Two Colombian citizens were sentenced to 13 years in prison for international
trafficking of illicit substances and for altering a vehicle's structure to
transport illicit substances. [@1] Panama's Public Ministry announced the same
13-year sentence on 13 March 2026, saying it was imposed after the two men
accepted responsibility at a hearing. [@2] The Chiriquí drug prosecutor asked a
guarantee judge to legalise both men's apprehension and to charge them, and the
judge validated the plea agreement that fixed the term. [@2]

The case began on 12 March 2026 in international waters south of Punta Burica,
on the border between Panama and Costa Rica. [@2] During a maritime patrol,
security units found a boat carrying the two foreigners with 5,400 grams (5.4
kilograms) of marijuana and 621 grams of cocaine aboard. [@2] Infobae described
the seizure as part of operations aimed at curbing trafficking on the maritime
routes of the Panamanian Pacific. [@1]

The Public Ministry said it gathered the evidence with the security forces and
that it would keep bringing drug cases before the courts. [@2]
