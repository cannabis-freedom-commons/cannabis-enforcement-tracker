Four members of an alleged drug trafficking organisation were arrested in
Philadelphia and New Jersey after several controlled buys in Middletown
Township, Bucks County, Pennsylvania, according to township police. [@1] State
police executed simultaneous search warrants at homes in Philadelphia on 4th
Street, South Myrtlewood Street and South Dover Street, and a fourth warrant was
served at a home in New Jersey. [@2]

The searches turned up 40 pounds (about 18.1 kilograms) of marijuana, 950 grams
of cocaine, 1,000 suspected fentanyl pills, 141 Xanax pills, 200 grams of
psilocybin mushrooms and $31,044 in cash, police said. [@1] Investigators also
seized three handguns and a 2021 Ford Raptor pickup truck, according to NBC10.
[@3]

The four were charged with corrupt organisations, possession with intent to
deliver, possession of a controlled substance and criminal use of a
communication facility, and were sent to the Bucks County Correctional Facility
after failing to post bail. [@2] The three-month investigation by the Middletown
Township Police Special Investigations Unit and the Pennsylvania State Police
Southeast Strike Force began in January 2026, with help from the DEA and state
and local agencies in Pennsylvania and New Jersey. [@2] The marijuana was part
of a seizure that also included cocaine, fentanyl pills, Xanax, psilocybin
mushrooms, handguns and cash; no street value for the cannabis was published.
[@3]
