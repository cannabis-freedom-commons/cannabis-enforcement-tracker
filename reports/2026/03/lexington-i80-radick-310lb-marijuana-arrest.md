Thomas Radick, 65, of Chicago, was charged in Dawson County District Court with
possession of marijuana with intent to distribute, possession of more than a
pound of marijuana and having no drug tax stamp after a traffic stop on
Interstate 80 near Lexington. [@1] A Nebraska State Patrol trooper stopped
Radick's white 2024 Dodge pickup, which was pulling a U-Haul trailer, at about
12:30 p.m. on 14 March 2026 because a bracket was covering its license plate.
[@1] Radick pulled into a McDonald's parking lot in Lexington, where the trooper
reported smelling marijuana coming from the vehicle and searched it. [@1]

The search found approximately 310 pounds of marijuana in 16 large black trash
bags, plus another 10 grams in the cab of the pickup. [@1] Radick was arrested
and booked into the Dawson County Jail. [@1] His bond was set at $150,000, of
which he would have to post 10 percent to be released. [@1] He was due back in
court at 11 a.m. on 31 March. [@1]
