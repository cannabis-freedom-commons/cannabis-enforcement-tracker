Gendarmes stopped two young men from Lot-et-Garonne in their car during a
traffic-control operation at Bourran in the night of 7 to 8 March 2026 and found
1 kg 765 g of cannabis herb. [@1] The operation was run by the Agen gendarmerie
company with the Pont-du-Casse and Aiguillon brigades, the Agen PSIG, a
gendarmerie surveillance and intervention unit, and the Agen motorised platoon's
dog team. [@1] One of the two men tested positive for drugs at the roadside, and
the dog team helped search the car. [@2]

Searches at the men's homes the next day found a further bag of 78 grams of
cannabis herb. [@1] Sud Ouest put the weight of the extra herb found in the
searches at 74 grams. [@2] The two men were brought before the Agen public
prosecutor on Monday 9 March 2026. [@1] The man with a police record was held in
pre-trial detention, while the other was released under judicial supervision.
[@1] Both were due in the Agen court on Wednesday 11 March 2026 for an immediate
appearance hearing. [@2]
