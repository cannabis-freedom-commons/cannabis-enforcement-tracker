Joint Philippine National Police and Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency teams carried out 33
cannabis-eradication operations in the Cordillera Administrative Region from 9 to 15 March 2026.
[@1]

The operations seized 87,740 fully grown cannabis plants, 1,500 seedlings and 146,720 grams of
dried cannabis leaves and fruiting tops. [@1] The report separately records 14 grams of shabu from
the wider operation, which is excluded from this cannabis report. [@1]

Eight buy-bust operations, two police-response operations and one warrant service produced 15
arrests in Benguet, Kalinga and Baguio City. [@1] The source does not say that any of those arrests
came from the 33 cannabis-eradication operations. [@1]
