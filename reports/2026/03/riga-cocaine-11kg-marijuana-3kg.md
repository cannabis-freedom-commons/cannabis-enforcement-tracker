Neither had previously come to police attention. [@3] Police said the men were
mainly involved in distributing cocaine in the Rīga region. [@1] A sanctioned
search at the home of the man born in 2002 found about 11 kg of cocaine and 3 kg
of marijuana. [@1] At the other man's apartment, officers seized a further 10 g
of cocaine and found 20 g of marijuana on him during a personal search. [@1]

Police estimated the street value of the seized substances at about €1 million.
[@2] Police opened a criminal case for organised, large-scale drug trafficking,
an offence carrying five to 15 years in prison. [@2]

They said the investigation prevented them from disclosing more about the
suspects' roles or the circumstances. [@3]
