Police destroyed three marijuana plantation sites in Barangay Kimbutan, Dupax del Sur, on 23 March 2026. [@1,2]
The Philippine Information Agency identified the sites as Sitio Pinakdetan, Little Kapangan Site 1 and Little Kapangan Site 2. [@2]

The Manila Times reported that the destroyed crop was worth PHP12.9 million, while the PIA later gave an estimate of PHP20 million. [@1,2]
Pilipino Star Ngayon said the three operations found fertiliser and an organised irrigation system, and that no cultivator was found; police burned and destroyed the plantations. [@3]
