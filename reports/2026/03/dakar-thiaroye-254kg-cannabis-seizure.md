Dakar's Research and Surveillance Brigade (BRS), a detached unit of the Central
Office for the Repression of Illicit Drug Trafficking (OCTRIS), dismantled a
cannabis (chanvre indien) distribution network operating between Thiaroye,
Kounoune and Guediawaye on 31 March 2026. [@1] Officers arrested the driver and
the main suspect at Thiaroye-sur-Mer at 8:30 p.m. as they took possession of a
suspect vehicle. [@1] Investigators then detained a woman at Kounoune who was
waiting for part of the consignment. [@1] The three detainees were placed in
police custody with the assistance of a lawyer and face prosecution for criminal
association, domestic drug possession and trafficking, complicity and money
laundering. [@1]

Officers seized 254 kg of cannabis packed in several parcels during the search
of the vehicle. [@1] Police said the woman at Kounoune was expecting eight of
the parcels. [@1] A search of her home recovered jewellery and 1,500,000 CFA
francs. [@1] A search of the main suspect's home at Cité Alioune Sow in
Guediawaye recovered 3,000,000 CFA francs, a land document for a plot at Hamo 3
and other administrative papers. [@1]

Police said the operation began after a tip-off about an imminent delivery in
Dakar's suburbs. [@1] The suspect vehicle was spotted at Thiaroye-sur-Mer near
the town hall and tracked in real time by tailing and drones. [@1]
