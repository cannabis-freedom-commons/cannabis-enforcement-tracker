Royal Virgin Islands Police Force officers from the Virgin Gorda Police Station
were on mobile patrol on 27 March 2026 when they saw a man acting suspiciously
near a local establishment. [@1] A search of the man found a firearm on him,
police said. [@1] A further search of a nearby vehicle carried out in his
presence found green vegetable matter suspected to be cannabis, according to
police. [@1] Police identified the man as Kyle Kabel, 26, of Valley, Virgin
Gorda. [@1] He was arrested and later charged with carrying a firearm without a
licence, unlawful possession of explosives and unlawful possession of a
controlled drug. [@2] Police said the firearm was a Glock .45 handgun and that
five rounds of .45 calibre ammunition were recovered. [@1]

Officers seized 31 grams of cannabis, police reported. [@1] Kabel appeared
before the Magistrate's Court and was ordered remanded to His Majesty's Prison
until May 2026. [@1]

The RVIPF said it continues to take a zero-tolerance approach to illegal
firearms and controlled drugs, calling illegal weapons and substances a serious
offence that would be dealt with accordingly. [@1]
