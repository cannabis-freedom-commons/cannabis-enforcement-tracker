Clackamas County sheriff's deputies and their partners broke up an illegal
marijuana growing operation at properties in Oregon City and Happy Valley. [@1]
On Friday, March 13, the Clackamas County Interagency Task Force served two
search warrants, at South Holly Lane and Southeast Lenore Street. [@1]

Investigators found more than 3,500 live marijuana plants growing in several
outbuildings. [@1] They also seized equipment for manufacturing and processing
marijuana, just over $100,000 in cash, three firearms and two vehicles carrying
evidence of bulk marijuana transport. [@1]

Joe Zhou, 60, was arrested and charged with unlawful manufacturing, production
and possession of marijuana. [@1] Officials said neither location had registered
legal or permitted marijuana production. [@1]
