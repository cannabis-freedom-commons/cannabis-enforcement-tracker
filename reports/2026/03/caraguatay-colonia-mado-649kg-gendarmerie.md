Gendarmeria Nacional Argentina seized 649 kilos 800 grams of marijuana in two
operations in northern Misiones, at Caraguatay and Colonia Mado. [@2] A
motorised patrol of Squadron 10 Eldorado turned off National Route 12 onto a
dirt road known as Cementerio in Caraguatay after seeing recent vehicle tracks.
[@1]

With support from the Eldorado complex-crimes investigations unit and the
Montecarlo section, officers combed the wooded area and found four abandoned
bundles holding 216 rectangular packages. [@1] Narcotest field tests confirmed
the Caraguatay bundles held 154 kilos 400 grams of cannabis sativa. [@1]

Two hours later, officers of the Nucleo section of the same unit saw people in a
canoe trying to cross the Parana River at a spot called El Frances, in Colonia
Mado. [@1] Seeing the gendarmes, the canoe occupants threw bundles onto the
Argentine bank and fled towards Paraguay, and no arrests were reported. [@1] A
search of the riverbank found 22 bundles of different sizes and colours. [@1]
Taken to the squadron base with a magistrates authorisation and weighed, 10 of
the Colonia Mado bundles held buds and the rest pressed marijuana, 495 kilos 400
grams in total. [@1] Noticias de la Calle likewise reports that those on board
threw bundles onto the Argentine coast and escaped quickly towards the
Paraguayan side. [@2]

The Federal Court and the Federal Prosecutors Office of Eldorado authorised the
seizure of the drug and opened proceedings under drug law 23.737. [@1]
