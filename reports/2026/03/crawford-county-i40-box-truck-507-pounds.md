Arkansas State Police arrested 47-year-old Armen Grigoryan of Valley,
California, after a traffic stop on Interstate 40 in Crawford County on 2 March
2026. [@1] A trooper stopped an eastbound box truck for a traffic violation
around 8 a.m. near the 3-mile marker in Crawford County. [@2]

Troopers searched the cargo area and found 507 pounds of marijuana, about 230
kg, concealed inside three wooden crates. [@2] The agency said the truck was
travelling from California to Memphis at the time of the stop. [@2]

Officers took Grigoryan to the Crawford County Detention Center and booked him
on a felony charge of trafficking a controlled substance. [@2]
