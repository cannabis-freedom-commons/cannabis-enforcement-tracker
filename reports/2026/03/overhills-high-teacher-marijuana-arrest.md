Harnett County deputies arrested Overhills High School math teacher Jocelyn
McArthur, 33, after receiving a report that she had an illegal substance on
campus. [@1] McArthur is charged with possession of a controlled substance with
intent to sell or deliver on or near a school involving marijuana. [@1] She is
also charged with possession of drug paraphernalia involving marijuana and
simple possession of a Schedule VI controlled substance involving resin of
marijuana. [@1] She is also charged with possession of a weapon other than a gun
on educational property. [@1]

An arrest warrant states McArthur knowingly possessed marijuana with intent to
manufacture, sell or deliver it within 100 feet of the school boundary in Spring
Lake. [@1] Authorities are holding McArthur at the Harnett County Detention
Center under a secured bond of $102,000. [@1] The sheriff's office said its
investigation began March 26 after receiving information that an Overhills High
School teacher possessed an illegal substance while on campus. [@2] Sheriff
Wayne Coats said the investigation remains ongoing and additional charges may be
filed. [@2]

Harnett County Schools said it takes student and staff safety seriously and is
following established district procedures in addressing the matter. [@1] The
district spokesperson declined further comment, citing personnel privacy laws.
