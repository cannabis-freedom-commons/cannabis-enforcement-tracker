Two men aged 62 and 41 appeared at the Montauban court on Friday 3 April 2026
and were held in provisional detention until their trial, set for 5 June. [@1]
Gendarmes arrested four men on 31 March 2026 in Moissac, Castelsarrasin and
Valence-d’Agen in Tarn-et-Garonne, breaking up a network they said had been
active since 2022. [@2]

About fifty gendarmes carried out the dawn operation under the Castelsarrasin
research brigade, which had watched and tapped the suspects for several months
around the Marengo alley in Moissac. [@2] Searches of a flat and a cellar in
Moissac found 1.6 kg of drugs: 962 g of cocaine, 260 g of heroin and 415 g of
cannabis resin. [@2] Officers also found a dummy 9 mm ball-firing weapon, and
the Montauban public prosecutor said the man questioned over about 96 hours
admitted trafficking but denied owning the drugs found in his cellar. [@2] Two
suspects were released and face later proceedings for drug use, while the other
two were brought to court for immediate trial on 3 April. [@2]

The court in Montauban sentenced the 62-year-old, a repeat offender, to four
years, one of them suspended, and the 42-year-old, a repeat offender, to three
years, one suspended, keeping both in detention. [@3] At trial the court put the
drug haul's market value at €71,400. [@3]

The case began with a letter from the “Mères de Moissac” collective to the mayor
on 14 October 2025 alerting him to a network in the town, and the 62-year-old
was observed making repeated trips to a Mondial Relay parcel locker on a car
park. [@3] The second man, 41 in April and 42 by the trial, was arrested with
0.5 g of cocaine and denied storing the drugs, arguing he had handed back the
keys to the cellar. [@3] The defense lawyer called it a small-scale trade
reaching only about ten users, and argued fingerprints were never taken in the
cellar. [@3]
