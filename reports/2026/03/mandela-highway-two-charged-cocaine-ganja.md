Police charged two Westmoreland men who were held with alleged cocaine and ganja
during a traffic stop along Mandela Highway. [@1] The accused are Donald Baugh,
a farmer from New Works, and Lenroy Ferguson, a tiler from the Water Works
district. [@2] They are charged with possession of cocaine and ganja, dealing in
cocaine and ganja, and trafficking cocaine and ganja. [@1] They are scheduled to
appear before the St Catherine Parish Court on Friday, March 13. [@2]

About 5:25 p.m. on Wednesday, March 4, FNID personnel intercepted a Toyota motor
car travelling along Mandela Highway, driven by Ferguson with Baugh as
passenger. [@1] A search of the vehicle found approximately 1.2 kilogrammes of
cocaine and an unspecified quantity of loose ganja. [@2] Police gave an
estimated combined street value of $720,000 for the cocaine and ganja together.
[@2]

FNID acting director Deputy Superintendent Sheldon Coulson said police have
stepped up monitoring at the borders and across the island to capture drug
traffickers. [@2] Coulson said police are going after criminal networks
profiting from the movement of illegal drugs and that there is no safe haven for
traffickers. [@1]
