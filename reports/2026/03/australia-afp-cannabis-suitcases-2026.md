A 56-year-old South Australian man was sentenced to three years in prison on 25 March 2026 for trying to take 25 kg of cannabis on a domestic flight from Adelaide to New South Wales. Airport staff became suspicious of his suitcase in August 2024, and he pleaded guilty to a Commonwealth trafficking charge in August 2025. The District Court set a non-parole period of one year.
