Jamie Darroux of Herberts was taken into custody around 1:30 p.m. on March 23,
2026 during a Narcotics Department mobile patrol near Potters Primary School in
Potters. [@1] Officers said they observed Darroux allegedly smoking cannabis in
a public space. [@1] He was apprehended and later charged with smoking cannabis
in a public place and possession of cannabis. [@1] Darroux, 34, appeared before
Magistrate Ngaio Emanuel following his arrest in Potters. [@3] Darroux pleaded
guilty to both offences. [@3] He was granted bail on his own recognizance with
sentencing set for March 30. [@3]

A search found 21 grams of cannabis on him, six grams over the legal limit. [@3]
The 21 grams was estimated to be worth $210. [@3] On March 30 Chief Magistrate
Ngaio Emanuel reprimanded and discharged him on the public-smoking charge and
fined him $500 for cannabis possession. [@4] He faced 14 days in prison if he
failed to pay the possession fine. [@4]

Police said smoking tobacco or cannabis is prohibited in schools and other
public spaces under the Tobacco Control Act, 2018 and the Misuse of Drugs
(Amendment) Act, 2024. [@1] Police said smoking cannabis in a public place
carries a fine of up to EC$2,000 on summary conviction. [@2]
