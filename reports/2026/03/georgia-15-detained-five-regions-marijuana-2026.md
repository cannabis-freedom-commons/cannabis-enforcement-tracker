Police from the Interior Ministry's departments for Tbilisi, Adjara,
Samtskhe-Javakheti, Imereti and Kvemo Kartli detained 15 people on drug charges.
[@1] The detainees include both Georgian citizens and foreign nationals. [@1]
They are accused of unlawfully acquiring and storing drugs and of helping to
sell them in large and especially large quantities. [@2]

Officers seized drugs prepared for sale, among them dried marijuana, mephedrone,
cocaine, alpha-PVP, thebaine, methadone and buprenorphine. [@1] Sputnik Georgia
listed marijuana, mephedrone, cocaine, methadone, buprenorphine, alpha-PVP and
thebaine among the seized substances. [@2] The Interior Ministry did not name
the foreign detainees' citizenship. [@2]

The Interior Ministry said the investigation is proceeding under Criminal Code
articles 260 and 260-4. [@1] Those articles carry up to 20 years in prison or
life imprisonment. [@2]
