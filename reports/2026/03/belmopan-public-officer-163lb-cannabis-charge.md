Acting on intelligence, police searched #8 Starkey Hill in the Piccini area of
Belmopan at around 7:45 in the morning. [@2] The resident found there was
45-year-old Andrew Wade, the communications officer at the NEMO Belmopan branch.
[@2] Police formally arrested and charged Wade with possession of controlled
drugs with intent to supply to another. [@3] He appeared in Belmopan court and
was remanded because of the quantity of marijuana. [@2] He was remanded to the
Belize Central Prison pending further proceedings. [@1]

Officers found 10 parcels of cannabis wrapped in transparent plastic in the
home. [@2] The cannabis weighed 16.3 pounds (about 7.39 kg). [@2] Street sources
described the marijuana as high grade and put its wholesale street value at
upwards of 20 thousand dollars. [@3]

The arrest followed the execution of a search warrant at the residence in Wade's
presence. [@1] Asked whether Wade had been working as a trafficker over time,
ASP Stacy Smith said she would not speculate and limited her account to the
search and its discovery. [@3]
