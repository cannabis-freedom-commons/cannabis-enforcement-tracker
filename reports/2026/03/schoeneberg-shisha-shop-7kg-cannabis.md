In early March 2026, officers of the Hauptzollamt Berlin, the city's main
customs office, checked a shisha shop in the Schöneberg district and found
untaxed vapes on the sales counter. [@1] They opened a criminal case against the
owner of the shop. [@1] An employee who was there refused them access to the
storeroom. [@1] The Berlin public prosecutor's office immediately obtained a
search warrant. [@1]

In the storeroom the officers found seven kilograms of cannabis, 17 kilograms of
untaxed shisha tobacco, 24,436 untaxed vapes and 27,899 tins of banned nicotine
pouches, known as snus. [@1] The Hauptzollamt said those goods had already been
seized in Berlin by early March. [@1] With the Zollfahndungsamt
Berlin-Brandenburg, the customs investigation office for Berlin and Brandenburg,
officers then obtained a warrant for the owner's home and storage rooms. [@1]
There they seized a large quantity of untaxed vapes and banned nicotine pouches
and took them out of circulation. [@1] The banned and untaxed goods were
confiscated. [@1]

The Zollfahndungsamt Berlin-Brandenburg is continuing the investigation. [@1]
Officers found a large quantity of untaxed vapes and banned nicotine pouches in
the owner's living and storage rooms as well. [@2]
