Four men who were held at sea allegedly with over 2,000 pounds of ganja in
Alligator Pond, St Elizabeth have been charged. [@1] The four are Paul Stanley
Kelly, 57, Omar Nard, 40, Achiema McKoy, 37, and Carl Manderson, 35. [@1] Kelly
and Nard are of Rocky Point, Clarendon, McKoy is of Braes River, St Elizabeth,
and Manderson is of Hellshire, St Catherine. [@1] They were charged with
possession of ganja, dealing in ganja, trafficking ganja, taking steps
preparatory to export ganja and conspiracy to export ganja. [@2]

The seizure happened on Tuesday, March 3. [@1] The operation was a coordinated
effort between the Firearms and Narcotics Investigation Division and the Jamaica
Defence Force Coast Guard. [@1] About 12:07 am, the joint team intercepted a
canoe with four men on board in the waters near Alligator Pond. [@2] On boarding
and searching the vessel, officers found several knitted bags containing
compressed ganja. [@2] The seized ganja weighed over 2,000 pounds. [@1] Police
put the haul's estimated street value at $15.1 million. [@2]

Investigations are ongoing. [@1]
