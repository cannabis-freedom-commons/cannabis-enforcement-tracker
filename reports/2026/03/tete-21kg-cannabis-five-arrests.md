Police in Tete detained five people after finding 21 kg of cannabis in a
long-distance truck from Maputo, Miramar reported. [@1] A dog unit inspecting the
vehicle found the drug in 33 parcels hidden among beauty products and animal feed.
[@2] Rádio Voz Coop put the weight at 21.5 kg. [@2]

The five included the truck's driver and his assistant, a motorcycle taxi operator
and two people waiting in a light vehicle to collect part of the order. [@1,2]
The driver and his assistant denied knowing about the drug, saying the load was
made up of many boxes. [@2] The driver of the light vehicle said he had come only
to collect beauty products and animal feed. [@2]

A sixth suspect, a passenger of the motorcycle taxi operator and the suspected
owner of one of the parcels containing the drug, escaped during the operation.
[@2] Police said they were still looking for him. [@1,2]

No charges or court date were reported. [@1,2]
