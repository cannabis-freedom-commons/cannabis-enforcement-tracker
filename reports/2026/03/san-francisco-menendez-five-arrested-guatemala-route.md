At least five people were arrested in an early-morning joint operation by the
National Civil Police and the Attorney General's Office for moving different
types of drugs by boat and vehicle from Guatemala to El Salvador. [@1] Those
detained are accused of bringing marijuana, cocaine and cristal into the country
by vehicle through San Francisco Menéndez in Ahuachapán and other areas of
Sonsonate. [@1]

The detainees were identified as Miguel Ángel Soriano Olla, Verónica del Carmen
Ramos García, Damián Rafael Menjivar Ramírez, Marlon Osmaro Olivo Purquir and
Juan Carlos Urquilla Cortéz. [@1] Authorities carried out at least nine searches
and seized multiple items of evidence of involvement in the offences. [@1]

The Attorney General's Office said the detainees will be brought before the
courts for illicit association, illicit drug trafficking and illegal possession
and holding of drugs. [@1]
