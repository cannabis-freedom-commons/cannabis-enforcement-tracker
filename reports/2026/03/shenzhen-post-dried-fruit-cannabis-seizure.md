Shenzhen Postal Customs reported that an abnormal X-ray image led officers to open a transfer parcel declared as dried fruit. They found four packets of dried fruit and six packets with signs of resealing, with plant material concealed among them. [@1,2]

The seized material tested positive for THC and weighed 4,260.9 g gross, including plastic packaging. Customs transferred the case to its anti-smuggling department. [@1,2]

Neither report gives a net weight for the cannabis or says whether anyone was arrested or charged. [@1,2]
