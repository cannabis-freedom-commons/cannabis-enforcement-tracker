A 29-year-old man from Oshakati West was arrested at Oshakati on Friday after
allegedly attempting to smuggle cannabis into the holding cells at Oshakati
Police Station. [@1] Oshana regional police commander Commissioner Naftal
Sakaria confirmed the incident on Monday. [@1] The incident happened at around
13h15 when the suspect brought food to an accused person held in custody. [@1]
Officers searching the items found cannabis hidden underneath a lunch box. [@1]
The suspect was charged with contravening the Abuse of Dependence-Producing
Substances and Rehabilitation Centres Act over possession of prohibited
dependence-producing drugs. [@1] He was expected to appear in the Oshakati
Magistrate's Court on Tuesday. [@1]

Police confiscated 55g of cannabis in the incident. [@1] Police put the
estimated street value of the cannabis at N$2,750. [@1] Sakaria said attempts by
the public to smuggle drugs into cells by concealment are common and require
vigilance. [@1]
