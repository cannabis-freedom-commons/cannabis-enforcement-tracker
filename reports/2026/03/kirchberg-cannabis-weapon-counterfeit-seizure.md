On the night of 1 to 2 March 2026, Luxembourg's Grand-Ducal Police were alerted
to a man repeatedly moving in a vehicle near a reception facility at Kirchberg,
apparently armed. [@1] The Parquet, Luxembourg's public prosecutor's office,
ordered a search of the man's vehicle and a search of his home. [@1] Local
station officers carried out the operation with the support of the Grand-Ducal
Police special unit. [@1]

In the searches, officers found a firearm that was not covered by weapons and
ammunition legislation. [@1] They also found cannabis plants described as
illegally cultivated. [@1] Officers also found material that could be used for
drug trafficking. [@1] They also found counterfeit banknotes alongside the
weapon, the plants and the trafficking material. [@1] Police seized the illicit
and dangerous objects found during the operation. [@1]

The cannabis enforcement component therefore consisted of the illegally grown
plants and the suspected trafficking material within a wider seizure that also
included the weapon and fake notes. [@1] The prosecutor's order linked the
vehicle search and the home search as parts of the same Kirchberg operation.
