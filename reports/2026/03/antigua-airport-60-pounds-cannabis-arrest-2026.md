Police said a 38-year-old Jamaican man was arrested and taken into custody in
connection with the investigation at V.C. Bird International Airport. [@1,3] The
same accounts also described a 36-year-old Jamaican as assisting police
following the same seizure. [@1] He was expected to be charged and brought
before the court. [@2,3]

Officers attached to the Narcotics Department, with members of the K-9 Unit and
the Customs Enforcement Unit, conducted a coordinated operation at the airport.
[@1,3] Officers seized one black suitcase and one green suitcase containing a
total of 19 sealed packages of cannabis. [@1,3] The seized cannabis weighed 60
pounds, about 27.22 kg. [@2,3] Police gave the street value of the seized cannabis
as EC$360,000. [@1,3]

The joint operation took place at the airport last Sunday, two days before the 3
March 2026 publications. [@1,3] The Royal Police Force of Antigua and Barbuda said
it remains resolute in disrupting drug trafficking networks and safeguarding the
nation's borders. [@1]
