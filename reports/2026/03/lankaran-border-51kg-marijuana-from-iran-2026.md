Azerbaijan's State Border Service stopped narcotics entering from Iran into
Azerbaijan in the service area of the Lankaran border detachment, totalling 51
kilograms and 560 grams. [@1]

In border search and operational measures officers found and seized 41 kilograms
and 280 grams on 22 March and another 10 kilograms and 280 grams of marijuana on
25 March. [@1] Caliber.Az reported the seizure in English as 51.56 kg of drugs
from Iran in the Lankaran detachment. [@3]

Operational and investigative measures were continuing in both cases. [@1] The
border service report names no detained person and gives no arrest figure. [@2]

Day.Az published the account on 30 March 2026. [@1]
