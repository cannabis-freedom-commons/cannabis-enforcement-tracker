Puerto Rico Police Bureau officers from the Bayamón Intelligence and Special Arrests Division
stopped a black 2024 Toyota Tacoma on PR-26 before exit 7 in Carolina on Sunday 1 March 2026.
[@1,2,3] They requested the K-9 unit, whose dog alerted to controlled substances, and a Carolina
court issued a search warrant for the vehicle. [@1,3]

Inside, police seized 31 pounds of marijuana (about 14.06 kg), a marijuana cigarette and a jar of
the same substance, seven unidentified pills, three $100 bills believed to be counterfeit, and a
Glock 27 .40-calibre pistol with two magazines and 22 rounds. [@1,2,3] Three men were arrested; one
of them held a valid firearms licence. [@1,3] The case was referred to the prosecutor on duty for
possible charges. [@1,2,3] The police did not name the men or say why they were stopped. [@2]
