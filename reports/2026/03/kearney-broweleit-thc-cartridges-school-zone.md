Jacob Broweleit, 19, of Kearney, was charged in Buffalo County Court with four
counts of distribution of a controlled substance within or near a school zone.
[@1] According to an arrest affidavit, on three occasions on 7 January, 21
January and the preceding Thursday, Broweleit sold a total of 40 THC cartridges
to an undercover Nebraska State Patrol investigator for a total of $870. [@1]
Court records said Broweleit's residence was near Zion Lutheran School. [@1]

On 6 March, investigators with the Nebraska State Patrol, with assistance from
the Kearney Police Department and the TRIDENT Task Force, served a search
warrant at a house near 26th Street and Avenue B in Kearney. [@2] During the
search, authorities found over 5 grams of THC wax, 61 grams of marijuana, over
50 THC vape cartridges and nine firearms. [@2] The resident, Jacob Broweleit,
19, was arrested and lodged in the Buffalo County Jail. [@2]

A judge set Broweleit's bond at $350,000, 10% of which he would need to post to
be released from jail, and set his next court date for 3:30 p.m. on 1 April.
[@1] KSNB reported that Broweleit's bond was set at 10 percent of $350,000 and
that he had since requested a bond review hearing set for the following week.
