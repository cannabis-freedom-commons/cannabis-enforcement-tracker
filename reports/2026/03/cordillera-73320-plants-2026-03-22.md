The Philippine National Police carried out 24 eradication operations in the Cordillera Administrative
Region from 16 to 22 March 2026. [@1] The campaign focused on Benguet areas where illegal cultivation
had been reported. [@1]

Police said the operations destroyed around 72,900 fully grown cannabis plants, 420 seedlings and
more than 454,000 grams of dried cannabis leaves, stalks and fruiting tops. [@1] The reported value
of the seized plants and products was PHP69,172,200. [@1]

The PNP said it would continue monitoring and enforcement against cannabis cultivation in the
region. [@1]
