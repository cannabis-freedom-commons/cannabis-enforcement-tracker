Grodno customs officers detained a 46-year-old Belarusian man who had tried to
swallow a wrap of marijuana. [@1] The man was travelling from Poland as a
passenger in a Ford car. [@1] At the Kamenny Log checkpoint he declared that he
carried no goods subject to declaration or restricted from movement. [@1] A
customs service dog contradicted his statement and indicated, by its behaviour,
that his luggage clearly held a prohibited substance. [@1] Fearing that the drug
would be found, the man took a wrap from his backpack and tried to swallow it.
[@1] Customs officers stopped him immediately. [@1]

An expert of the Lida interdistrict department of the State Committee of
Forensic Examinations confirmed that the wrap contained marijuana. [@1] The
Grodno Regional Customs opened a criminal case against him under Part 1 of
Article 328-1 of the Belarusian Criminal Code. [@1] The Grodno transport
prosecutor authorised detention in custody as the preventive measure. [@1] BelTA
reported, citing the State Customs Committee, that the man was detained while
entering from Poland with the drug in his luggage. [@2] BelTA reported that the
same criminal case and custody measure were recorded against the offender. [@2]
