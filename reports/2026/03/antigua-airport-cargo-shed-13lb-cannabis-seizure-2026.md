Officers from the Narcotics Department, working alongside the Customs
Enforcement Unit, conducted a coordinated search at the LIAT Cargo Shed around
5:30 p.m. on March 5. [@1] No arrests had been made in connection with the
seizure, and investigators were working to determine the origin and intended
destination of the shipment. [@1]

The search took place during a joint drug interdiction operation at the cargo
facility of V. C. Bird International Airport. [@1] Officers searched two brown
cardboard boxes and found 13 packages of cannabis with a combined weight of
approximately 13 pounds. [@1] The approximately 13 pounds of cannabis is about
5.9 kg. [@1] Police gave the seized drugs an estimated street value of
EC$78,000. [@1]

Police said the operation formed part of continued efforts by law enforcement
agencies to disrupt the importation and trafficking of illegal drugs into
Antigua and Barbuda. [@1] Anyone with information that could assist
investigators was asked to contact the Narcotics Department at 462-5150, the
Criminal Investigations Department at 462-3913 or the Crimestoppers anonymous
hotline at 800-TIPS (8477). [@1]
