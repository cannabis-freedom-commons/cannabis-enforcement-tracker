A 19-year-old was tried at the Mulhouse criminal court on 2 November 2026 over a
refusal to stop for gendarmes committed in March. [@1] Around 23 h one night
that month, a gendarmerie crew was nearly hit by a car, and the driver refused
to stop and fled at 100 km/h through the town. [@1]

The driver ran off on foot after ending up in a dead end, and gendarmes found a
bag at the scene that linked the car to him. [@1] He had been driving without a
licence and was in possession of cannabis. [@1]

He denied driving that night, telling police in custody and the court that he
had left the keys on a wheel and someone else had borrowed the car. [@1] The
prosecutor called that account untenable. [@1]

The court gave him one year in prison, suspended with two years of probation,
for the refusal to stop. [@1]
