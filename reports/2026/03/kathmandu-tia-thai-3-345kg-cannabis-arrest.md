Nepal police arrested 39-year-old Thai national Yongyut Thuion at the arrival
tunnel gate of Tribhuvan International Airport in Kathmandu on the night of
Monday, 23 March 2026. [@1] He was stopped in a security check as he landed in
Kathmandu from Bangkok on a Thai Airways flight. [@1] Airport security officers
found the cannabis in his suitcase. [@3]

The Tribhuvan International Airport Security Office put the cannabis at 3.435
kg, a weight that includes plastic wrapping, and identified him by passport
number AC 2566356. [@2] He had flown in on Thai Airways flight TG 309. [@4]
Deputy Superintendent of Police Dhundiraj Neupane of the Airport Security Office
gave the account, and the Nepali report says officers checked the man because
his behaviour looked suspicious. [@4]

Police took him into custody at the arrival tunnel gate and sent him to the
Narcotics Control Bureau in Koteshwor for further action. [@2]
