Montenegrin police pursued further suspects in the Trnovo cannabis-plantation investigation after the August 2025 discovery. [@1,2]

Police filed supplementary criminal complaints against two men from Zeta, aged 42 and 49, on suspicion of drug production and supply linked to the Trnovo cannabis plantation.[@2] Investigators cited forensic examinations, analysis of traces and other collected evidence; three earlier suspects had already been processed.[@2] The case began with the discovery of about 13,100 plants near Bar in August 2025.[@1] [@2,1]

Police arrested a 31-year-old man from Bar, identified as N.D., on suspicion of drug production and supply in the Trnovo investigation. [@1]
