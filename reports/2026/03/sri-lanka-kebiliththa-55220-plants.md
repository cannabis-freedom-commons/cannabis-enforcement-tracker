Police Narcotics Bureau officers and Athimale Police raided a cannabis plantation in the protected
area of Kebiliththa, Kotiyagala, on 9 March 2026. [@1]

The officers seized 55,220 cannabis plants grown across approximately two acres, with the plants
nearly six feet and ten inches tall. [@1]

Police took a 34-year-old Embilipitiya resident into custody in connection with the cultivation and
presented him to Athimale Police for further legal proceedings. [@1]
