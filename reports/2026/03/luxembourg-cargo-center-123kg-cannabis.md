Luxembourg customs, after targeting work, stopped an air shipment from the United States that was bound for France and arrived at the Cargo Center at Luxembourg Airport. [@1,2,3,4] The goods were declared as decorative cushions. [@1,2,3,4] Inside, officers found drugs and seized more than 123 kilograms gross of cannabis. [@1,2,3,4]

The public prosecutor in Luxembourg said on 26 March 2026 that the French authorities were told at once. [@1,2,3] The cannabis was placed under seal and was to be destroyed under the rules in force. [@1,3] The prosecutor said a quantity of that size could only match structured, large-scale distribution and the means of organised networks. [@1,2,3] The statement names no one arrested. [@1]
