Haskovo District Court sentenced Serbian national Nihat Rahimi to four years and eight months in
prison on 24 March 2026 for attempting to smuggle 172.242 kg of marijuana through Bulgaria’s
Kapitan Andreevo border checkpoint with Turkey. [@1,2] Judges approved a plea agreement that also
requires a €36,000 fine and declared the ruling final with no further appeal. [@1,2]

Customs Agency officers and the Interior Ministry’s organised-crime directorate found the cannabis
in a concealed compartment of an empty trailer on Rahimi’s truck during an operation on 1
September 2025, after monitoring the load from the Vidin–Kalafat entry toward Kapitan Andreevo.
[@1,2] The court press service valued the shipment at €1.409 million. [@1,2]

The case opened in Haskovo on 1 September 2025, according to BTA. [@1] Novini.bg said Rahimi will
serve the term under Bulgaria’s ordinary prison regime. [@2]
