Commissioner Tyler J. Harper said agriculture investigators executed a search
warrant on Monday 26 March 2026 at a business warehouse in DeKalb County after
an investigation into wholesalers suspected of selling marijuana and illegal
hemp products. [@1,2] DeKalb Police narcotics and K-9 units assisted. [@1,2]

The search yielded 120 pounds of marijuana and roughly $4.8 million in illegal
hemp products measured at retail value, Harper’s office said. [@1,2] Multiple
people were taken into custody and face felony trafficking charges, and
officials said the inquiry was ongoing with further charges possible. [@1,2]

Harper said the department would keep pursuing operators who sell marijuana
disguised as hemp or other hemp goods that remain illegal under Georgia law.
