The encounter took place at the Port of Palm Beach Seaport in Riviera Beach,
Florida. [@1] On March 23, 2026, U.S. Customs and Border Protection officers at
the Palm Beach Seaport detained a U.S. citizen returning from Nassau, Bahamas,
after detecting the smell of marijuana during a routine inspection. [@1]
Officials with the agency's Director of Field Operations in Florida reported the
detention, which followed the odor detected during routine inspections. [@1]
Officers referred the traveler to baggage control after noticing the odor. [@1]
During the baggage examination, officers discovered a jar containing a green
leafy substance, and field testing confirmed the substance was marijuana. [@1]

A subsequent pat-down search revealed additional marijuana concealed on the
passenger. [@1] In total, officers recovered 6.4 grams of marijuana. [@1] The
seized marijuana was turned over to the Palm Beach County Sheriff's Office for
further action. [@1]

CBP officials reiterated their zero-tolerance stance on illegal drugs at U.S.
ports of entry, telling travelers “WEED like to check your bags.” [@1]
