Two store clerks faced charges after a multi-agency investigation into illegal
cannabis sales at three smoke shops on 24 March 2026. [@1] Nathaniel A. Doyle,
20, of Rome was charged with unlicensed cannabis sale, a Class A misdemeanor, at
Brooklyn Exotics. [@1] Brooklyn Exotics is at 401 Black River Blvd. in Rome, and
the charge was brought by the Rome Police Special Investigations Unit. [@1]

During the raid at Brooklyn Exotics, police found quantities of cannabis flower
and cannabis concentrate in various forms. [@1] Police seized approximately
2,937 flavored nicotine vapes from the Brooklyn Exotics location. [@1]
