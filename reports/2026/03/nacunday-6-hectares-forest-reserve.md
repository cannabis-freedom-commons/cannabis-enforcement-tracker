Agents of SENAD's Ciudad del Este regional office and anti-drug prosecutor Elvio
Aguilera Vázquez carried out an operation in a forest reserve in Ñacunday
district, destroying six hectares of marijuana plantations over the weekend.
[@1] The operation took place in Colonia Mbareté, inside the forest reserve
belonging to the company Agrotoro S.A. in the south of Alto Paraná department.
[@1] Officers destroyed two plots of marijuana in the growth stage, each
covering about three hectares, according to the prosecution's report. [@1] The
operation was handled by special agents of SENAD's Regional Department No. 2
based in Ciudad del Este, with special-forces support and the Public Ministry
headed by prosecutor Elvio Aguilera. [@2]

They also dismantled a camp used by the growers, along with personal belongings.
[@1] Agents found a homemade sieve, a Jacto-brand sprayer, two seeders, six
homemade cazabobo traps and three unstruck calibre-28 cartridges. [@1]

En Tiempo Real described the six cazabobo traps as homemade devices designed to
cause serious injury or death to intruders or agents entering the crop area.
[@3] All the items found as evidence were destroyed on site on the prosecutor's
orders. [@1]
