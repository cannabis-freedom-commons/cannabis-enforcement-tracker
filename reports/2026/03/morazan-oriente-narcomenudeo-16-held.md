El Salvador's Attorney General's Office (FGR) and National Civil Police (PNC)
detained 16 people accused of belonging to a street-dealing network that
operated in the departments of San Miguel, Morazán and La Unión. [@1] The
arrests came in 23 searches with arrest warrants carried out before dawn on
Wednesday 11 March 2026. [@2] The searches covered San Francisco Gotera,
Sensembra, Osicala, Jocoaitique and Gualococti in Morazán, Conchagua and El
Carmen in La Unión, and the cantón El Sitio in San Miguel. [@1] Prosecutors said
those held were profiled as distributors, redistributors, intermediaries and
collaborators. [@2] Prosecutors named thirteen of the detainees, among them José
Salvador Pacheco Arriaza, Wilfredo Alexander Cisneros Mejía and Nataly Esmeralda
Martínez Vásquez. [@3]

El Salvador Times reported that around 570 grams of marijuana worth an estimated
$2,012 were seized in the overnight procedure. [@4] La Prensa Gráfica described
the marijuana find as one large portion, alongside a large portion of
methamphetamine and large and small cocaine portions. [@1] Officers also seized
$13,953.15 in cash, 37 mobile phones, four vehicles, two laptops, a tablet, a
laptop, a USB stick and two SIM cards. [@3] Prosecutors said a year of
investigation had identified everyone in the network, from those who supplied
the drug to the redistributors. [@1]

The investigation began with a drug transaction at a restaurant in San Francisco
Gotera on 5 May 2025, where investigators identified a distributor and a
redistributor. [@1] Prosecutors said four of those held sold in San Miguel, five
in La Unión and 13 in Morazán. [@1] Those detained face proceedings for illicit
associations, trafficking, and preparatory acts, proposal and conspiracy to
commit illicit trafficking. [@1] Prosecutors said one more person already in
custody would be notified of a new case against them, and that they were still
looking for other accused. [@4] El Salvador Times reported that the network sold
and distributed the drug between 11 March 2025 and 10 March 2026. [@4] El Diario
de Hoy reported that police arrested Víctor R. A. and Samuel D. A. in October
2025 in El Divisadero, Morazán, with 13 and 2.5 grams of cocaine in a separate
step of the same investigation. [@2]
