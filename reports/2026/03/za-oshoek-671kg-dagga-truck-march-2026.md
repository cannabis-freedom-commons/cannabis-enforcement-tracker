A Border Management Authority, South African Revenue Service and South African
Police Service joint operation intercepted a truck entering South Africa from
Eswatini at the Oshoek Port of Entry at about 8pm on 16 March 2026. [@2]
Officers searched the vehicle and found 50 bags of dagga, the South African term
for cannabis, concealed in the trailer. [@1]

The bags weighed about 671.5 kg and the authorities estimated their street value
at R2.8 million. [@2]

The driver fled during the interception and was still at large, and a manhunt
was launched. [@1]

The same trailer carried 916 illicit tyres, a separate contraband consignment.
[@2] The dagga and the tyres were registered with Saps as evidence, Sars seized
the truck, and a criminal case was opened. [@1]

BMA commissioner Michael Masiapato commended the officials for the joint
operation. [@1] Masiapato said the Port Management Committee was committed to
strengthening border security and preventing the cross-border movement of
illicit goods, stressing inter-agency collaboration against criminal networks.
[@3] No arrest was reported, and the manhunt for the driver was under way. [@2]
