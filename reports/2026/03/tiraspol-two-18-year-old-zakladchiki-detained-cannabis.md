Criminal investigation officers in Tiraspol detained two 18-year-old friends on
suspicion of spreading drugs, the Interior Ministry press centre said. [@1]

The Interior Ministry press centre said the two had spent about a month placing
cannabis stashes, known locally as zakladki, around Tiraspol. [@1] They found
the work on the internet, and one of the pair had a previous conviction and had
been released from prison shortly before. [@1] According to the ministry, that
young man kept in contact with the curator and stayed on watch while his friend
placed stashes or collected a master stash. [@1]

An officer from the Oktyabrsky district police department said the suspect
received an image of coordinates through the Telegram messenger and the hidden
drugs were then distributed in the city without contact. [@1]

The ministry said both admitted the illegal trade once officers detained them.
[@1] The pair face up to 20 years in prison for drug dealing. [@1]
