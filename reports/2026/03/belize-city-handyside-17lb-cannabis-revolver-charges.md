Two Belize City residents were detained after police searched a home on
Handyside Street in Belize City for cannabis, a firearm and ammunition. [@1] A
joint police operation executed a search warrant at the residence just after
10:00 a.m. on Tuesday, March 24, 2026. [@2] The operation involved the
Anti-Narcotics Unit, Special Branch, Precinct 3 and the Quick Response Team, and
a K9 unit signalled drugs inside the apartment. [@2]

Officers found cannabis in several places, including a green plastic bag with
eight packages in a hall area, and more bags in a hidden compartment beneath the
floor. [@2] Police put the total weight of the suspected cannabis at 17 pounds,
about 7.7 kilograms. [@1] Officers also found a black .22 revolver with no
visible serial number hidden inside a PVC pipe, along with three live .22
rounds. [@2]

Both were jointly charged with possession of controlled drugs with intent to
supply, keeping a firearm without a gun license and keeping ammunition without a
gun license. [@3] They pleaded not guilty and, with bail denied because of the
nature of the offences, were remanded to the Belize Central Prison until May 12,
2026. [@3]
