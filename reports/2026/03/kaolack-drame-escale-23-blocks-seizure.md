Officers of the Brigade régionale des stupéfiants (BRS), the regional narcotics
brigade in Kaolack in central Senegal, seized cannabis being carried on a
Jakarta-type motorcycle on 9 March 2026 under an operation announced in a
Senegalese police statement dated 10 March 2026. [@1] The brigade's surveillance
operation identified the Jakarta-type motorcycle as it transported the two
suspect parcels. [@1] Officers recovered the motorcycle and a mobile telephone
from the traffickers during the intervention. [@1] The intervention took place
on the unpaved road linking the localities of Thioyène Hamady Mala and Dramé in
the Dramé Escale zone. [@1]

Officers seized 23 blocks of chanvre indien, the French term for Indian hemp or
cannabis, packed as parcels of 8 and 15 sub-blocks. [@1] Police described the
seizure as a major seizure in its announcement. [@1]

Police said the operation fell within the continuing fight against organised
transborder criminality. [@1]
