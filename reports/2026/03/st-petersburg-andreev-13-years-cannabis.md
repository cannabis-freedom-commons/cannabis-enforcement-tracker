The Primorsky district court in St Petersburg sentenced three people on 3 March 2026 for growing
cannabis in a flat in the city and in a building in Leningrad Oblast. [@1,2] Georgy Andreev led the
planting and care of the plants, his son Danila watered them and brought fertiliser, and Olga
Vashkevich provided the premises and also watered the bushes. [@2]

Between 13 November 2024 and the morning of 9 April 2025 the group grew 23 cannabis plants in the
Leningrad Oblast building and a similar number in the St Petersburg flat, 46 bushes in all. [@2]
Police shut the plantation down on 9 April 2025. [@1,2]

At Andreev's home officers also found 14.86 kilograms of other drugs, which he planned to sell with
the cannabis. [@2] The court convicted all three under the drug-cultivation and trafficking articles
of the criminal code. [@1,2]

Andreev was sentenced to 13 years and six months in a strict-regime penal colony and fined 800,000
roubles. [@1,2] Danila received three and a half years suspended with a three-year probation period,
and Vashkevich four and a half years in a general-regime colony. [@1,2] Georgy and Danila
admitted the charges in part; Vashkevich denied them. [@2]
