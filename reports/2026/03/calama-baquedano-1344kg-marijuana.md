Officers detained two Chilean men where the second van was stopped at Calama's
southern exit and seized 171 kilograms of marijuana there. [@1] Follow-up work
by the Fiscalía's SACFI unit with Carabineros OS7 led to the detention in the
Metropolitan Region of two other men linked to the transport. [@1] All those
detained in the recent procedures were to be formally charged by the Public
Ministry in the coming hours and days. [@1]

The two recent procedures together yielded 1,344 kilograms of marijuana and 4
kilograms of cocaine hydrochloride outside Calama and in the Baquedano sector.
[@1] The report of the two procedures covers 1.3 tonnes of drugs and says the
operations in Calama and Baquedano recovered marijuana and cocaine. [@2] The
first van, stopped on Route 5 on Sunday afternoon, carried 34 sacks holding
1,173 kilograms of marijuana plus 4 kilograms of cocaine hydrochloride. [@3] The
second van was stopped at the southern exit of Calama the previous night after
fleeing a vehicle check and hitting the road's containment barriers during a
controlled pursuit. [@3]

Regional prosecutor Juan Castro Bekios said the team had created a special
investigative focus on border routes and trafficking that seeks digital,
financial and logistical traces beyond drugs in transit. [@3] General Cristian
Montre Soto, head of the Antofagasta Carabineros zone, said the latest seizures
came from a consolidated operational strategy centred on permanent control of
certain routes. [@3]
