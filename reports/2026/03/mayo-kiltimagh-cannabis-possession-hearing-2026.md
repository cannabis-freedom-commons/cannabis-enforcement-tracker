Mark Shepherd of Aiden Street, Kiltimagh, appeared before Castlebar District
Court on a charge of section 3 possession of drugs relating to March 30 this
year. [@1] Garda Costello was on patrol on Main Street, Kiltimagh, when he saw a
Ford Focus owned by Shepherd and stopped the vehicle. [@1] The driver did not
have a licence and was identified as Shepherd. [@1] The garda detected a strong
smell of cannabis and found the drug in the vehicle. [@1] He cautioned Shepherd
and carried out a memo interview. [@1] Shepherd admitted the cannabis was for
his own use. [@1]

The court heard the cannabis was valued at EUR 551.40. [@1] The court heard
Shepherd has one previous conviction relating to a road traffic offence. [@1]

Shepherd, who represented himself, told the court that he had quit cannabis.
[@1] He said he no longer had a doctor but had new lines of enquiry towards
getting a new GP. [@1] He said he was off cannabis and wanted to prove it to the
court. [@1] Judge Vincent Deane asked how soon he could show this. [@1] Shepherd
said he was happy to give a hair sample to prove it. [@1] The judge adjourned
the case to Wednesday, March 3, for the result of a hair follicle test. [@1]
