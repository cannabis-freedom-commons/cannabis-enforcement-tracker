The Oruro provincial prosecutor's office ran an early-morning operation at 03:45
on 26 March on the road linking the Villamar community with Villa Alota, on the
border toward Potosí. [@1] FELCN officers intercepted a Mitsubishi van whose two
occupants, men aged 31 and 35, tried to flee on noticing the police and were
brought to the ground. [@1]

A search found 325 ovoid packets wrapped in beige and black packing tape and
clear nylon; a drug test confirmed the green herb inside was marijuana. [@1] The
total weighed 317 kilos 950 grams of marijuana, which the prosecutor's office
said was worth about three million bolivianos. [@1]

The prosecutor's office said it would formally charge the two men with
trafficking controlled substances in aggravated large-volume cases. [@1] The two
men exercised their right to remain silent while authorities investigated the
drug's origin and destination. [@1]
