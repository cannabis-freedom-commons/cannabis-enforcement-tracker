On 20 March 2026, the first day of Eid al-Fitr, Algerian army detachments
intercepted 10 quintals and 49 kilograms of kif traité, treated cannabis, coming
from Morocco in two separate operations at Hassi Achour in Adrar wilaya and
Kenadsa in Béchar wilaya. [@3]

A quintal is 100 kilograms, so the army's figure equals 1,049 kg, the exact
weight Bladi.net also reported. [@4]

The defence ministry said the operations were carried out in the localities of
Hassi Achour in Adrar and Kenadsa and targeted "smuggling barons". [@1]
Alongside the drugs, the army seized a vehicle and two motorcycles it said the
traffickers had used. [@3] The defence ministry's communiqué reported no arrests
at that stage of the investigation. [@1]

The ministry said the attempt during the Eid festivities showed traffickers'
intention to use festive occasions to carry out their plans. [@3] The army
announced the two operations in a defence ministry communiqué the following
Sunday, 22 March. [@2] The army said its forces formed an "impenetrable rampart"
against anyone threatening Algeria's security and stability. [@1]
