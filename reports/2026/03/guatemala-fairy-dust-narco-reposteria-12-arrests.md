Guatemala's Public Prosecutor's Office said on 24 March that 12 people had been
captured for links to a network selling pastry products made with drugs for
national distribution through apparently legal companies. [@1] The operation
called Narco Repostería carried out 15 searches in Guatemala and Sacatepéquez
and captured 12 people for money laundering and unlawful drug commerce,
trafficking and storage. [@2] On 22 March the National Civil Police reported the
detention of María, identified as the leader of the Fairy Dust or Fairy Kitchen
structure. [@1] Prosecutors said the network had co-opted National Civil Police
agents, who were captured over these acts. [@1]

Investigators said the network kept a farm in Santa María de Jesús,
Sacatepéquez, where it grew the raw material for the business. [@1] At the farm
officers found a greenhouse with marijuana seeds and marijuana in growth,
harvestable and processed stages for sale. [@1] Officers seized Q1,295,370 in
cash together with assorted drugs, drug-laced pastry products, a money-counting
machine, vehicles, mobile devices and laptop computers. [@1] Prosecutors said
officers eradicated 1,882 marijuana plants and seized 30 kilos of marijuana and
a shotgun during the procedures. [@1] The official evidence inventory for the
same operation instead lists 1,466 marijuana plants. [@2]

Prosecutors said the network sold cupcakes, cookies, sweets and gummies with
illicit substances and used the national financial system to give the proceeds
an appearance of legality. [@1] The organization made and distributed substances
hidden in cupcakes, brownies, cookies, sweets and gummies that were promoted
through various channels and delivered by courier services in the city and other
departments. [@2] The investigation grew out of earlier detentions in El
Salvador that led Guatemalan prosecutors and police to identify a network
selling pastry products prepared with different types of drugs. [@1]
