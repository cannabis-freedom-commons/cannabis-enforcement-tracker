Jamaica Defence Force Coast Guard units detained four people in connection with
a ganja seizure offshore Manchester. [@1] The four people and the seized
marijuana were handed over to the Jamaica Constabulary Force's Firearms and
Narcotics Investigation Division for processing. [@2]

The JDF's Maritime Air and Cyber Command seized over 2,286.75 pounds of
marijuana, valued at approximately $45 million, on Tuesday after intercepting a
vessel southwest of Long Bay Beach, Manchester. [@1] The Gleaner likewise
reported that the weed weighed more than 2,286.75 pounds with an estimated
street value of about $45 million and that the vessel was intercepted southwest
of Long Bay Beach. [@2]

The First and Second District Coast Guard units were conducting routine patrols
along Jamaica's southern coastline when they detected a suspicious vessel near
the Farquhars Beach area in Clarendon. [@1] A coordinated pursuit ensued and
ended with the successful interception of the vessel. [@1] A detailed search of
the vessel found 38 knitted bags containing marijuana and several 200-gallon
fuel drums. [@1]

IRIE FM reported that four people are in custody after the interception
southwest of Long Bay Beach in Manchester yesterday. [@3]
