Gendarmeria Nacional Argentina gendarmes from the Puerto Rico section of
Squadron 11 San Ignacio seized more than 309 kilograms of marijuana after
detecting a suspected smuggling move near the international border. [@1] The
officers were on foot patrol during the early morning on a tributary of the
Parana river when they saw several people unloading bundles from a boat. [@1]
When the group noticed the officers, its members boarded a launch and fled
toward Paraguay, abandoning a boat and nine large bundles on the Argentine bank.
[@1] A second account says the men were unloading the bundles from the boat
toward the Argentine bank and fled toward Paraguay when they saw the federal
force. [@2]

After securing the area, the gendarmes opened the packages and counted 352
bricks holding compacted plant material. [@1] Criminalistics and forensic staff
field-tested the material with a positive result for cannabis sativa. [@1] The
cannabis weighed 309 kilos 310 grams in total. [@2]

The Federal Court and Federal Prosecutor's Office of Obera ordered the drug
seized for infringement of Law 23.737. [@1]
