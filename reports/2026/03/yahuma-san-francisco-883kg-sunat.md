Customs and police officers found more than 883.30 kilograms of marijuana in the
native community of San Francisco de Yahuma, in Mariscal Ramón Castilla
province, Loreto, Peru's tax and customs agency Sunat said. [@2] Sunat valued
the load at more than 78,000 soles. [@2] The marijuana was in a fibreglass river
boat with a 200-horsepower outboard motor of Brazilian origin, which officers
also impounded. [@2] The operation was carried out by staff of the Iquitos
customs office, the National Police's anti-drug directorate and the Public
Ministry, the prosecutor's office. [@2] Sunat said the seizure neutralised an
important logistics structure linked to transnational drug trafficking. [@2]

According to the official report, the drug was hidden in a boat in the middle of
the Amazon. [@1] The authorities said the boat was serving within a logistics
structure tied to illegal trafficking in the border area. [@1] Infobae said such
operations are part of a strategy that prioritises control of river routes and
points near the border, where criminal organisations set up logistics hubs to
move drugs to neighbouring countries or to maritime routes for international
destinations. [@1]
