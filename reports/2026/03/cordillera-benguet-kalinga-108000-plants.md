The Philippine National Police reported 24 marijuana-eradication operations in Benguet and Kalinga
between 23 and 29 March 2026. [@1] It said the campaign destroyed more than PHP28.5 million worth
of marijuana. [@1]

In Benguet, 22 operations found more than 80,000 fully grown plants and over 57,000 grams of dried
marijuana, while two operations in Kalinga destroyed 28,000 plants. [@1]

The PNP chief described the campaign as an effort to destroy marijuana at its source and urged
communities to report suspected cultivation. [@1]
