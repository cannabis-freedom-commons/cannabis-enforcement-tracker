Military police destroyed a plantation of 45,000 cannabis plants on Monday 16
March 2026 in Abaré, in northern Bahia. [@1] The Military Police said the
cannabis was grown on an island near the district of Ibó, in the rural zone of
the municipality. [@1] Nobody was at the site when the officers arrived. [@1]

Part of the plantation was taken to the regional police station, where the case
was registered, and the rest of the plants were burned. [@1]

The Civil Police will investigate to identify who was responsible for the crop.
[@1] The photograph published with the report is credited to the 45th
Independent Military Police Company (45ª CIPM), the unit whose patrol area
covers Abaré. [@1]

Farol da Bahia published the same account a day later, also crediting the
Military Police and a photo to the PM of Bahia. [@2] The Petrolina-based site
Edenevaldo Alves also carried the report with the same figures and the 45ª CIPM
photo credit. [@3]
