Personnel from the Jardín América section of Squadron 11 San Ignacio, with
support from the Misiones complex-crimes investigations unit, were on motorized
patrol on secondary roads when they spotted a grey Volkswagen Amarok. [@1] The
occupants reversed course and fled, ignoring the gendarmes' stop orders. [@1]
They drove the vehicle into an embankment, abandoned it and fled on foot,
favoured by the hilly terrain. [@1] The find occurred during patrols on
secondary roads near the Paraná River, and the vehicle's occupants fled after
losing control and crashing into an embankment. [@2]

The seizure happened near the Paraná River in Helvecia, where the vehicle was
found holding 731 rectangular packages in 41 bundles. [@1] Officers inspecting
the vehicle saw large bundles inside with a characteristic marijuana-like smell.
[@1] Officers counted 731 rectangular packages in 41 bundles, which field
Narcotest testing confirmed as cannabis sativa weighing 719 kilos 270 grams.
[@1]

Investigators checking the vehicle's record established that the pickup had an
active seizure order for robbery. [@2] The Federal Court and Federal
Prosecutor's Office of Oberá ordered the seizure of the drug, the vehicle and
other items of interest to the case. [@1]
