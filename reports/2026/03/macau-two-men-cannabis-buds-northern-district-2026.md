Judiciary Police officers arrested two local men on suspicion of drug
trafficking and drug taking after an operation in Macau on 2 March 2026. [@2]
The two were a 29-year-old driver surnamed Fong and a 27-year-old maintenance
technician surnamed Ho, police spokesman Lei Chi Hou said. [@1] Acting on a
tip-off that the two were dealing drugs, officers stopped each of them in
Macau's northern district, Fong at about 11 am and Ho at about 8 pm. [@3]

Officers found a packet of 2.33 grams of suspected cannabis buds, drug-taking
tools and packaging equipment at Fong's flat in the northern district. [@4] A
second packet weighing 0.75 grams of suspected cannabis buds was hidden in the
helmet box of Ho's motorcycle parked in the northern district, and his flat held
an electronic scale and 100 small clear plastic bags for dividing and packing
drugs. [@3] Police also seized MOP1,000 and HK$1,000 from the two as suspected
proceeds of drug sales. [@1]

Laboratory tests confirmed the substance was cannabis, which the Judiciary
Police valued at about MOP3,000. [@2] Both men's urine tests were positive for
drugs and both said the cannabis was for their own use and for sale, police
said. [@2] The two were transferred to the Public Prosecutions Office for minor
production and trafficking, illicit drug consumption and improper possession of
utensils or equipment, while police kept tracing the source of the cannabis and
other people involved. [@2]
