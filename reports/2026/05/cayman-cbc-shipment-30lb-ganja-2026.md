Customs and Border Control Service officers found a suspicious package while
screening a shipment of imported goods during routine procedures. [@1] Officers
intercepted and secured the shipment, then examined it physically with other
agencies on Thursday 7 May 2026. [@1] The examination turned up parcels
containing suspected ganja inside the shipment. [@1] Three people have been
arrested in connection with the seizure. [@1]

CBC seized more than 30 pounds (about 13.6 kg) of ganja from the shipment. [@1]
The CBC release gave no street value for the ganja. [@1] Marlon Bodden, the CBC
deputy director responsible for investigations, said the matter remains active
and further inquiries are being pursued by the CBC. [@1]
