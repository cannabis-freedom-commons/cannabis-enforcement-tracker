RAB-9 arrested three drug traders with 52 kg of ganja in Madhabpur upazila of
Habiganj. [@2] Those arrested were Sajon Modi, 28, Rimon Kalindi, 21, and Sujon
Kalindi, 24. [@2] They were residents of different tea-garden areas of
Chunarughat upazila in Habiganj district. [@2] Three people tried to flee on
sensing RAB's presence. [@1] They were detained after the attempt. [@1] The
operation took place at about a quarter to 10 p.m. on Saturday 16 May in the
Surma tea garden gate area of Shahjahanpur Union. [@2] The team involved was
from RAB-9's CPC-3 Shayestaganj camp in Habiganj. [@1] The team was positioned
in front of the Surma tea garden gate under Madhabpur police station. [@1]

A search of two red plastic sacks in their possession found 52 kg of ganja
wrapped in khaki scotch tape. [@2] The detainees said they had collected the
ganja from a border area and kept it to sell. [@1] A case was filed under the
Narcotics Control Act, 2018, and those arrested were handed over to Madhabpur
police station. [@2] RAB said its continued operations and intelligence activity
against drugs would continue. [@2]
