Police found a cannabis plantation in the private banya of a house in Ryabovo, a village in the
Vyborg district of Leningrad Oblast. [@1,2] Officers seized more than 200 cannabis plants and five
polymer bags of dried brown leaves weighing more than six kilograms in total. [@1,2]

The detained man was a 27-year-old unemployed local resident of Ryabovo. [@1,2] The banya stands on
Polyanskoye shosse. [@2] Tests confirmed that the seized material was cannabis and products made from
it. [@1,2]

Police opened a criminal case for attempted large-scale sale of drugs. [@1,2] At the time of the
reports the question of a preventive measure was still being decided. [@1,2]
