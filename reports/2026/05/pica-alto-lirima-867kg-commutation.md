On 14 July 2021 Carabineros stopped a pickup truck on route A-97B in the Alto
Lirima sector of Pica commune, Tarapacá, in which a Bolivian man was riding as
the scout, or punta de lanza, for a drug convoy. [@1] The convoy carried 867 kg
of marijuana, 198.7 kg of cocaine hydrochloride and 25.3 kg of coca paste. [@1]
According to the Iquique oral-trial court's ruling, the operation started in
Cariquima, in the border commune of Colchane, where the group met a Bolivian
national driving a Bolivian-plated vehicle. [@1] The court valued the whole load
at more than 1,000 million pesos and noted three vehicles coordinated by radio
and what it called the operation's level of sophistication and planning. [@1]

Jhoel Ramos Antesana, a Bolivian with no address in Chile, was sentenced to five
years and one day in prison. [@1] During the case the Tarapacá presidential
delegation filed a negative report on expelling him as an alternative penalty,
and the court ruled that out because of the length of the sentence. [@1] On 12
May 2026 the Ministry of Justice cut three months from his sentence and set his
release for two days later. [@1] Cooperativa, citing the same Interferencia
investigation, reported that the 12 May decree cut his sentence by three months
and ended his term two days later. [@2]

His case has the largest drug volume among the cases Interferencia reviewed.
[@1] Interferencia reviewed 150 commutation decrees for foreign nationals issued
this year, 115 of them benefiting people convicted of drug offences, and found
the ministry opposed none of them. [@1] At least 13 of those beneficiaries were
stopped at the northern border or on the roads leading down from it, eight of
them Bolivian, three Venezuelan, one Colombian and one Argentine. [@1] The
decrees, citing a 2018 Santiago appeals-court ruling and two Supreme Court
rulings, argue that the ministry cannot deny a sentence reduction because of the
gravity of the offence. [@2]
