Officers of the Interior Ministry's anti-drug unit shut down a supply channel
moving cannabis-group drugs on an especially large scale from Kazakhstan's
southern regions to Mangystau. [@2] In late May 2026 in Aktau, they detained a
man suspected of illegal drug trafficking. [@1] The suspect was placed in a
temporary detention facility. [@1] A pre-trial investigation was opened under
part 4 of article 296 of Kazakhstan's Criminal Code. [@1]

Officers seized four packages containing hashish, and an expert examination put
the total weight at more than 4 kg. [@2] The Lada.kz account says the operation
was jointly carried out by the regional anti-drug department and the 5th
directorate of the National Security Committee's regional department,
coordinated by the Mangystau prosecutor's office, with a service dog named Kira.
[@3] That account describes the case as illegal storage of drugs on an
especially large scale without purpose of sale, with the city police
investigative unit carrying out procedural steps. [@3]
