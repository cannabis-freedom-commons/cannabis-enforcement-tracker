An electrician working nearby noticed unusual wiring, ventilation pipes and a cannabis smell coming from a warehouse on Queen Street in Ōtāhuhu. [@1,2] Police received the information on Wednesday afternoon, put a scene guard in place, then executed a search warrant on Thursday. [@1]

Police said the wiring and ventilation pipes signalled possible illegal activity before the smell confirmed a cannabis growing operation to the electrician. [@1,2]

Inside, officers found around 360 cannabis plants under 15 tents; Detective Senior Sergeant Mike Hayward of Counties Manukau West CIB said that police had destroyed the plants. [@1,2] No occupants were inside when police searched the warehouse. [@1,2]

The police release credited the electrician for reporting the suspicious setup. [@1] 1News described the site as a large commercial grow house one road from the suburb’s main street. [@2] The New Zealand Police release was published on Friday, 15 May 2026, and gives Thursday as the search date. [@1]
