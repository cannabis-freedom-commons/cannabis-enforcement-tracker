Zimbabwe's information minister, Soda Zhemu, told a post-Cabinet media briefing
in Harare on 19 May 2026 that 2,889 people accused of drug offences had been
brought before the courts and 381 convictions secured. [@1] Zhemu said
authorities dismantled 31 drug bases in selected districts of Manicaland and
Mashonaland West provinces. [@1] He said authorities also arrested 2,436
individual end-users and about 453 drug suppliers. [@1]

The drugs confiscated, valued by the government at about ZiG79,931,130, included
crystal meth, cocaine, ecstasy tablets, dagga, khat and unregistered medicines
such as cough syrups. [@1] The government said it had removed 32 children from
the streets and reunited 171 others with their families as part of
rehabilitation and reintegration programmes. [@1]

It said 615 liquor-licensed premises were inspected countrywide and 36
unlicensed outlets were fined and closed. [@1] The awareness and prevention
campaigns had reached 285,918 beneficiaries, while rehabilitation, training and
entrepreneurship programmes for young people and women had reached about 37,937
people, Zhemu said. [@1] The government has launched a US$1 million Youth
Empowerment Fund to offer economic alternatives to vulnerable young people. [@1]
