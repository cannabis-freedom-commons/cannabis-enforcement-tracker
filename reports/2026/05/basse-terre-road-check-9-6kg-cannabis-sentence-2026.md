BAC officers carried out a road check on Avenue de Saint-Claude in Basse-Terre
on Wednesday 6 May. [@1] Officers became suspicious of a driver, and a quick
search found a sachet of crack in his possession. [@1] Following the check,
investigators went to the home of an individual known to the stopped driver and
searched it. [@1]

The search seized 9.57 kg of cannabis herb. [@1] It also seized 44.69 g of
cannabis resin. [@1] The same search also found 99.57 g of cocaine alongside the
cannabis. [@1] Officers seized 12,858.30 euros in cash. [@1] Officers seized a
handgun with fourteen 9 mm cartridges. [@1]

A second man, known to the driver and occupying the searched premises, was
arrested after the search, bringing the arrests to two. [@1] The driver was
punished for the road offences found against him. [@1] The other man was
convicted, sentenced to three years of firm imprisonment and jailed. [@1]
Guadeloupe’s territorial directorate of the National Police presented the
operation as part of its action against drug trafficking and illegal weapons.
[@1]
