The seizure took place at the Zhuhai checkpoint of the Hong Kong-Zhuhai-Macao
Bridge. [@1] Gongbei Customs officers stopped a traveller using the green
channel at the arrivals hall for vehicle passengers at the Hong
Kong-Zhuhai-Macao Bridge Zhuhai checkpoint after he appeared evasive and walked
with a hurried gait. [@1] The green channel is the lane for travellers with
nothing to declare. [@1] Officers found an opened cigarette pack in the
traveller's coat pocket, and a pungent odour when they opened it led them to
five hand-rolled cigarettes. [@1]

Tests found cannabis components in all five cigarettes, with a net weight of
1.26 grams. [@1] The case was transferred to the customs anti-smuggling
department for further handling. [@1]

Gongbei Customs said cannabis is among the state-regulated narcotics that cause
addiction. [@1] Customs warned that smuggling, trafficking, transporting or
manufacturing narcotics carries criminal liability and criminal penalties
whatever the quantity. [@1]
