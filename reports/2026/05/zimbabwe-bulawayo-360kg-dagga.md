The Zimbabwe Republic Police arrested five men after stopping a Toyota Hilux single cab towing a trailer on the Bulawayo–Solusi Road in Bulawayo, police said. [@1,2,3] The trailer was loaded with 360 kg of loose dagga, which police valued at ZiG10.8 million. [@1,2,3]

Police named the suspects as Gordon Nkowu, 58; Nkosiyabo Moyo, 57; Shepherd Nkomo, 43; Beven Sibanda, 40; and Isaiah Moyo, 31. [@1,2,3] The vehicles carried South African registration plates, NewsDay reported. [@2]

National police spokesperson Commissioner Paul Nyathi said the dagga had been smuggled from South Africa through Botswana into Zimbabwe. [@1,3] "The ZRP will not allow Zimbabwe to be used as a transit route or destination for illegal drugs," police said in the statement. [@2] NewsDay reported that investigations were under way to establish the full scope of the suspected trafficking network. [@2]

HararePost reported that the five were expected to appear in court on charges of unlawful possession and transportation of dangerous drugs. [@3] No court date was published. [@1,2,3]
