Iraqi authorities arrested a suspected drug trafficker and seized 11.5 kilograms
of marijuana in a joint operation involving Diyala province and anti-narcotics
units in Sulaymaniyah and Erbil, the capital of the Kurdistan Region. [@1]
Diyala's drug affairs directorate announced the arrest and the 11.5 kg seizure
on Monday, 25 May 2026. [@2] The directorate said the man was caught after a
tight tracking and surveillance operation and that the marijuana was found in
his possession. [@2] It said the operation followed intelligence gathered by the
Diyala directorate and field movements. [@2]

In its statement, the directorate called it a qualitative security strike based
on intelligence work and field tracking. [@3] The spokesman for the General
Directorate of Narcotics, Abbas al-Bahdli, said the detainee was a local drug
dealer who had been wanted by the judiciary. [@5] He said the man had intended
to trade the marijuana. [@5] The Interior Ministry said legal measures had been
taken against him under Iraqi law. [@1]

Gulan Media, citing ministry officials, said Kurdish and Iraqi agencies shared
intelligence and tracked the suspect across several areas before his detention.
[@4] Authorities described the operation as part of a broader campaign against
trafficking routes linking the Kurdistan Region with central Iraq and
neighbouring countries. [@4] Interior Ministry figures put the drugs seized
nationwide in the first five months of 2026 at more than 1.697 tonnes and said
anti-narcotics units carried out 12 operations outside Iraq with regional and
international agencies. [@1] The ministry said Iraqi courts issued 42 death
sentences and 357 life sentences against drug traffickers in the same period.
