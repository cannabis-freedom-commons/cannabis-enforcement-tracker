Costa Rica's National Coast Guard, working with the Judicial Investigation
Agency, intercepted a suspect boat in South Pacific waters during a dawn
operation. [@1] Officers detained two Colombian crew members, identified only by
the surnames Rovira and Cordoba, and held them in police custody pending
judicial investigation. [@1]

Officers found about 60 bales holding about 20 packets each, a load authorities
estimated at more than a tonne of suspected drugs. [@1] The seized load included
apparent cocaine and marijuana. [@2] The judicial-police account described the
boat as coming from Colombia with an estimated tonne load of cocaine. [@3] The
boat also carried 23 fuel drums thought to have been for long-range travel at
sea. [@1]

Officers also blocked a second boat that approached the boarding site, which
authorities said may belong to the same criminal structure. [@1] The detainees,
the load and the seized goods were taken to Golfito for judicial processing
under judicial supervision, with the OIJ directing further inquiries. [@1] Coast
Guard director Juan Carlos Alvarado said officers' immediate response kept the
load from entering national territory. [@1]
