Akwa Ibom police recovered a locally made pistol, a large quantity of substances suspected to be Indian hemp and several wraps of suspected hard drugs in a targeted operation in the Ibeno axis. [@1,2] Operatives moved on intelligence about the concealment and distribution of illicit substances there. [@1,2] Police spokesman DSP Timfon John announced the recovery in a 31 May 2026 press statement. [@1,2]

No suspects were arrested in the raid. [@1,2] The command said the exhibits were secured, linked the firearm and drugs to criminal elements threatening residents, and intensified efforts to identify and arrest everyone connected with them. [@1,2] Commissioner CP Baba Mohammed Azare commended the operatives and warned traffickers to desist or face decisive action. [@1,2]
