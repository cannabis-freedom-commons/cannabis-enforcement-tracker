Zambia's Drug Enforcement Commission in Central Province jointly charged and
arrested five suspects after intercepting a public bus used for drug
trafficking. [@1,2] DEC Deputy Public Relations Officer Theresa Mawere said in a
statement on Saturday that the bus had earlier crossed into Zambia and was
headed to Copperbelt Province allegedly carrying high-grade cannabis and Benylin
with codeine. [@1,2]

DEC officers mounted a checkpoint near Kasanda Police Station in Kabwe where the
bus was intercepted and searched. [@1,2] Officers found three packs of compressed high-grade cannabis weighing about 3.1 kilograms, three boxes containing 143 bottles of Benylin with codeine, and 11 tablets of suspected narcotic drugs concealed in the fuel tank compartment. [@2] All five suspects were in custody and
were expected to appear in court soon. [@1,2] The commission identified those arrested as Cacious Kunda Ngoma, bus drivers David Sabinda and Moses Chisakama Hamashila, and bus conductors Kelvin Chola and Miles Silwimba. [@2]
