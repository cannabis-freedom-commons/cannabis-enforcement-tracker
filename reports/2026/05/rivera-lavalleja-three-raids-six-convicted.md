Police from the northern region of the National Police investigations
directorate carried out three searches at the same time in barrio Lavalleja,
Rivera, and detained six people, two of them under 18. [@1] Diario Norte
reported the same three simultaneous raids in barrio Lavalleja, with the same
six detainees, among them two minors. [@2]

Officers seized 237 packets of a "yellowish substance", 120 packets of pasta
base, a nylon bag of the yellowish substance, 4,820 Uruguayan pesos, 4,434
Brazilian reais, a precision scale and two mobile phones. [@1] Diario Norte
listed the same 237 and 120 packets, the nylon bag, 4,820 pesos, 4,434 reais,
the scale and the two phones. [@2] The Interior Ministry published the seized
drugs under captions reading "Marihuana y cocaína" and "Pasta base y marihuana
incautada", without giving any weight. [@1] The Rivera police's scientific
police unit processed the scene. [@1]

A 23-year-old woman was convicted as the person responsible for supplying
prohibited narcotics and given two years in prison. [@1] A 39-year-old man was
convicted on the same supply charge and given two years and two months in
prison. [@1] A 20-year-old Brazilian citizen was convicted of assisting the
agents of the criminal activity and given two years in prison. [@1] Two
adolescents were held responsible for a serious breach of the criminal law
classed as supply of prohibited narcotics and ordered into closed
socio-educational detention at INISA, Uruguay's adolescent social-inclusion
institute, for 16 months. [@1]

Diario Norte, the Rivera daily, reported the raids before the convictions were
announced, saying the six detainees had been placed at the disposal of the
courts. [@2]
