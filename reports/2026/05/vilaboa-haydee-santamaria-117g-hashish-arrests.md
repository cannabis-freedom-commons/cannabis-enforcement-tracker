On 27 May 2026 the Guardia Civil and the Policía Nacional carried out the final
phase of Operation Bordafi/Dado with seven searches in Sanxenxo, Poio and
Vilaboa. [@3] Officers detained nine people as members of a criminal group
selling and distributing cocaine, hashish, tobacco and medicines. [@2] The
investigation began in February 2025 by the Pontevedra Guardia Civil EDOA
organised-crime and anti-drug team and the UDYCO drugs and organised-crime unit.
[@2] A court ordered communicated provisional prison without bail for two
detainees, including the association leader. [@2] Police identify the
association president, of Cuban origin, as the alleged ringleader, with her son
and another accomplice also involved. [@1] Her son was also detained, but for an
earlier offence unrelated to this operation. [@3]

The most striking find was in a chalet in Vilaboa listed as the seat of the
Asociación Cubano-Gallega Haydée Santamaría. [@1] Alongside the hashish officers
found 5.1 kg of cocaine, 549 packs of contraband tobacco and more than 30,000
euros in cash. [@3] Officers found about 2,000 blisters of more than 150
different medicines, including Rivotril, Lexatin and Lyrica. [@3] Officers also
seized 15 mobile phones, two tablets, a computer and tools for handling and
adulterating drugs. [@2]

The association was founded in 2014 and has had no activity since 2020. [@1]
People close to the president said the medicines were donations for Cuba. [@1]
Police suspect the medicines were sold illegally to drug users online and at the
house, and could also be exported abroad. [@3] Investigators identified two
organised and very active cells of a single criminal group distributing cocaine,
hashish and tobacco. [@1]
