Seven people were found in possession of illicit products during the bus-control
operation on line 105 and 106 buses at Goyave and were arrested on the spot.
[@2] The seven were placed in garde à vue, French police custody, the force
said. [@2] One of those arrested was a passenger found carrying a large quantity
of cannabis. [@1] Several other seizures of bags of cannabis intended for
personal use were made during the checks. [@1]

The operation ran on Friday 29 May 2026, late in the day, when the buses carry
many passengers. [@2] Gendarmes, customs officers, a sniffer-dog team and
motorcyclists of the motorised brigade (BMO) took part, targeting public
transport. [@2] The action was led by the control service of CAR971, the
Coopérative des autocars réunis, which operates the lines as the delegated
interurban transport provider, with the Goyave gendarmerie brigade, the customs
services and a dog team trained to detect drugs. [@1] The buses serve lines 105
and 106 between Basse-Terre and Pointe-à-Pitre. [@1] Two transport inspectors
joined the checks to pick out the buses circulating in the Goyave area. [@2]

A sworn private inspector of the CAR971 interurban transport service said the
aim was to fight insecurity on public transport and the carriage of illicit
products aboard buses. [@1] The force's statement said the joint operations
showed the effectiveness of coordinated action against insecurity on public
transport, illicit-product trafficking and everyday crime. [@2] Further
operations of the same kind are planned in the coming weeks in other communes of
the archipelago. [@2]
