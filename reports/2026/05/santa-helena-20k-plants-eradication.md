Integrated forces of the Maranhão public-security system eradicated a plantation
of approximately 20,000 cannabis plants in Santa Helena, in the Baixada
Maranhense region, on Sunday 17 May 2026. [@1] Air Tactical Centre (CTA-MA)
teams located the plantation at a place known as Roça do Mundico, in the village
of Mundico. [@1]

During the operation officers also found about 200 kilograms of the drug in an
advanced stage of cultivation. [@1] The whole plantation was destroyed following
the operational protocols used by the teams involved. [@1] Police put the
estimated loss to local drug trafficking at approximately R$600,000. [@1]

The action was part of Operation Bioma/Amazônia – Brasil contra o Crime
Organizado, a national strategy coordinated under the AMAS Plan against
environmental crime and criminal organizations in the Legal Amazon. [@1] G1's
police account likewise reported about 20,000 plants eradicated in Santa Helena
and about 200 kilograms of the drug in an advanced cultivation stage. [@2] O
Imparcial confirmed the CTA-MA identification, the 20,000 plants and 200
kilograms destroyed on site, and the same R$600,000 estimated loss. [@3]
