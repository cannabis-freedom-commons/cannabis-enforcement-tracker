A 29-year-old woman and a 47-year-old man were arrested after a police operation
on Quakoo Street in Nassau seized an illegal firearm and suspected hydroponic
marijuana. [@1] Officers assigned to Operation Black Scorpion executed a search
warrant shortly after 2 p.m. on Wednesday 20 May 2026. [@1] Inside a residence,
officers reportedly found an illegal firearm and a small quantity of suspected
hydroponic marijuana. [@1]

Police also detected a strong smell of suspected marijuana from a nearby
abandoned house, where more suspected hydroponic marijuana was allegedly
concealed inside a vent. [@1] The suspected drugs recovered carried an estimated
combined street value exceeding $7,900, police said. [@1] The Drug Enforcement
Unit said its investigations were continuing. [@1]
