Colombian Army troops seized marijuana in an operation in a rural area of the
municipality of Puerto Asís, in the Amazonian department of Putumayo, the
institution reported on a Saturday. [@1] The load was 11,692 kilograms of
marijuana divided into more than 10,000 packages and was found in two houses in
the vereda (hamlet) of Monserrate. [@1] The houses were searched by troops of
the Gaula Militar of the 27th Brigade, with judicial and air support from the
Police Anti-Narcotics Directorate. [@1]

Brigadier General Sergio Armando Guzmán Jaimes said the blow was perhaps one of
the biggest dealt in the south of the country in recent times. [@1] The general
said intelligence information indicated the drug's final destination was Brazil.
[@1] The Army said the seizure struck at the finances of the FARC dissidence
Segunda Marquetalia, specifically the Comandos de Frontera group. [@1] The Army
links that group to control of drug-trafficking routes in the border region with
Ecuador. [@1]

Authorities said the marijuana moved through clandestine corridors and border
routes used by armed groups to send drugs to international markets. [@1] No
arrests of people at the two houses were reported. [@1]
