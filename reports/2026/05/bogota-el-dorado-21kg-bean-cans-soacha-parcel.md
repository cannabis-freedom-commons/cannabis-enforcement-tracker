Officers of the Airport Police Station and the antinarcotics dog Jeico flagged a
suspicious parcel during routine checks in the national-shipment warehouses at
El Dorado airport in Bogotá. [@1] Police said the parcel had been sent from Cali
and was bound for Soacha. [@1] The parcel was sent by air from Cali to the
municipality of Soacha, according to a second account of the same police report.
[@2]

Officers opened several food cans inside the parcel and found 94 cylindrical
packages wrapped in vinipel. [@1] The cans were bean cans used to conceal the
drug. [@1] Police weighed the contents at 21,570 grams of marijuana. [@1] Police
described the use of bean cans to camouflage the drug as evidence of trafficking
networks constantly innovating to evade controls. [@1]

Police said the intercepted parcel sought to pass unnoticed among the hundreds
of parcels moving daily from different regions of the country. [@1] Police
placed the seized drug at the disposal of the competent authority while
investigations continue to identify those behind the shipment. [@1] Police
reminded the public to report suspicious activity through official channels such
as line 123. [@1]
