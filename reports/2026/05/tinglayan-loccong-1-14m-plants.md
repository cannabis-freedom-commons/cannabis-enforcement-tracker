## Eradication

Cordillera police destroyed marijuana plants during a two-day operation in Tinglayan, Kalinga, on 17 and 18 May 2026. [@1,2] The teams found 10 plantation sites on Mount Chumanchil in Barangay Loccong and uprooted and burned 1.14 million fully grown plants. [@1,2]

The sites covered about 9.5 hectares, and police gave the crop an estimated standard drug price of ₱228 million. [@1,2] The operation was called Oplan Revisit Everest; the Daily Tribune said investigators were still working to identify the cultivators. [@1]

After the destruction, law enforcers held a flag-raising ceremony and planted tree seedlings on the cleared mountain land. [@1]
