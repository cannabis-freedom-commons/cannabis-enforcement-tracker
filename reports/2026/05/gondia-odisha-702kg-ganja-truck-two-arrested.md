Officers of the Narcotics Control Bureau's Mumbai unit intercepted a
Maharashtra-registered truck in Gondia district on 21 May 2026 and arrested two
men. [@2] The search found 702 kg of ganja the agency valued at Rs 3 crore,
hidden in 100 packets among legitimate grocery cargo including detergent,
toothpaste and hair dye. [@2]

The NCB said the men had regularly moved bulk quantities of ganja sourced from
Odisha, which the agency calls one of the prime sources of illicit ganja supply,
into Maharashtra. [@2] The consignment was destined for drug syndicates in
Nagpur, Gondia, Pune and Mumbai, which the agency said pooled their shipments
rather than moving smaller loads. [@2]

The agency said the ganja was to be further distributed and sold at retail to
end customers and local peddlers in Maharashtra and Goa. [@2]

The Indian Express reported that the agency valued the 702 kg at Rs 3 crore and
that the contraband had been hidden among grocery items such as detergent,
toothpaste and hair dye. [@1]

The NCB said further investigation on the network was under way. [@2] The agency
said the seizure followed a similar 210 kg ganja haul on 11 April 2026, also
sourced from Odisha and hidden among metal sheets, with four people arrested.
[@2]
