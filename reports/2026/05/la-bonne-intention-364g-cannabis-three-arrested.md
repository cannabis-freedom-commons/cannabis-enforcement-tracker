Police arrested three men after suspected cannabis was found during an operation
at La Bonne Intention, East Coast Demerara, on Tuesday night. [@1] The operation
ran between 19:50 hrs and 20:16 hrs, when officers observed several men in the
area. [@1] A fourth man who was seen running north was pursued but managed to
escape. [@1]

Officers combing the area found a bulky black plastic bag on a dam. [@1] A
search of the bag found several transparent zip-lock bags containing leaves,
seeds and stems suspected to be cannabis. [@1] The three suspects, a 29-year-old
electrical worker, a 34-year-old plumber and a 28-year-old carpenter, were
arrested and taken to the Betterverwagting Police Station. [@1]

The suspected cannabis weighed 364 grams in the suspects' presence before being
lodged. [@1] The men remained in police custody as investigations continued.
