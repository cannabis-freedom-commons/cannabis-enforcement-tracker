Seven people were detained in San Salvador and Santa Ana on suspicion of selling
drugs, El Salvador's National Civil Police reported. [@1,4] The detentions took
place during the night of Thursday, when police arrested two groups of people
accused of selling drugs. [@2] Three of the detainees were held in the colonia
Altamira, San Salvador. [@1] They were named as Noelia Guadalupe Tobar Ramos,
22, Jaime Salvador Molina Acosta, 49, and Moisés Stefan Canales, 37. [@2] Four
more were detained in the residencial Valladolid in Santa Ana, in western El
Salvador: Cristhofer Alexander Aguilar Ramírez, 21, José Jonathan Argueta
García, 27, Dylan Ari Hurtado Ramírez, 18, and Katherine Paola Márquez López,
30. [@3] The operation took place at the residencial Valladolid, in Santa Ana.
[@3]

Police said the group in Santa Ana had several packages of marijuana valued at
$24,004. [@1] The same Santa Ana group also had methamphetamine valued at
$2,821, and the Altamira group had methamphetamine worth about $34,790. [@1]
Neither methamphetamine figure is counted in the cannabis figures here. [@1]
Police said the drugs were kept buried in the patio of a house. [@1] Diario El
Mundo put the drugs seized in the two operations together at more than $60,000,
a total covering methamphetamine and marijuana. [@2]

The PNC said its investigation had established a link to a second group of
people. [@3] The arrests were carried out by the police anti-narcotics division.
[@2] All seven detainees were to be handed over for the offence of illicit drug
trafficking. [@3]
