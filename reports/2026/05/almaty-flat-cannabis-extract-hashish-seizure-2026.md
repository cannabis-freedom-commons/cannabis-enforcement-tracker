Almaty police detained a suspect during operational-search measures against the
unlawful storage and packaging of drugs. [@1] Police said the man was detained
and drugs were found on him. [@2] A search at his home turned up cannabis
extract, marijuana and hashish. [@1]

Officers also seized electronic scales, vacuum-packing equipment and packaging
materials. [@1] Police said the equipment suggested the substances were being
packaged and prepared for sale. [@3] The seized drugs weighed more than 3 kg in
total. [@1] The suspect was placed in a temporary detention facility. [@1] A
pretrial investigation is under way to establish the circumstances and possible
supply channels. [@1] The investigation is aimed at identifying all
circumstances and possible channels through which the drugs arrived. [@2]

Baturzhan Abdumasarov, head of the Almaty police department's drug-crime unit,
said the operation removed a large batch of drugs from circulation. [@1]
Abdumasarov said the presence of packaging equipment indicated possible
preparation for distribution. [@1] He said such cases are under special scrutiny
and that anti-drug work is conducted systematically. [@1]

NUR.KZ reported that the search took place in the detained man's flat, where
drugs and packaging equipment were seized. [@4]
