Two people threw bundles from a canoe toward the Argentine bank of the Paraná at
Vera Cue, in the Misiones town of Puerto Esperanza, and fled to Paraguay when
Gendarmería officers called on them to halt. [@1] Neither person was caught, and
the two accounts name no arrests. [@1]

After officers searched the area they found five bundles holding rectangular
packages wrapped in ochre tape of the kind used to carry drugs. [@1] Field tests
by the Eldorado forensic unit, done in front of witnesses, were positive for
cannabis sativa. [@1] The load was moved to the unit's base, where officers
opened the bundles and counted 149 rectangular packages weighing 131 kilos 946
grams in total. [@1] The weight is just over 131 kilograms, the figure the
federal Security Ministry used in its headline. [@2]
