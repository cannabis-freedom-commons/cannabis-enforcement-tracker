Qingdao Jiaodong Airport Customs stopped a traveler entering through the green channel after officers flagged an abnormal baggage image. They found plant material hidden in snacks, coffee powder and sanitary pads, along with a cannabis grinder and other smoking tools. [@1,2]

The seven packages of plant material weighed 115.07 g net, and an expert examination confirmed they contained cannabis. Customs transferred the case to its anti-smuggling department. [@1,2]

The reports do not identify the traveler or say whether an arrest or prosecution followed. [@1,2]
