The central anti-drug brigade of the National Guard in Ben Arous dismantled an
international cannabis-trafficking and money-laundering network after ten months
of investigation and surveillance. [@1] The operation was run with the
prosecutor's office at the Sousse 2 Court of First Instance and announced by the
Interior Ministry in a statement on Monday 4 May 2026. [@1] Officers arrested 67
Tunisians and foreign nationals and placed them in detention, while 29 others
were still being sought. [@1] A National Guard spokesman said the arrests were
spread over seven governorates. [@3] Deposit warrants, the orders placing an
accused person in pre-trial detention, were issued against 62 of those arrested.
[@2]

Investigators said they broke up the financing and logistics chains of a network
that was bringing cannabis into Tunisian territory. [@1] Officers seized 106.5
kg of cannabis, which the Tunisian outlets call zatla, the local term for
cannabis. [@1] They also seized 1.442 million Tunisian dinars in cash and
jewellery estimated by the ministry at 600,000 dinars. [@1] Twenty-six vehicles
used to carry the drugs or bought with laundered money were seized as well. [@1]
The Interior Ministry said 17 official reports, procès-verbaux, were drawn up
for illegal currency exchange linked to the activity and for money laundering.
[@1] Tuniscope, citing the National Guard, reported about 20 such reports and
said the laundering and illegal financing circuits used cryptocurrency. [@2]
