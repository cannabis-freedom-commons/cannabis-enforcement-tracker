Mustafa Hibroj, an Albanian national, was sentenced on 18 May 2026 by Ilfov Tribunal to four
years in prison for international and domestic trafficking of high-risk drugs after trying to
import 21.3425 kg of cannabis through Henri Coandă–Otopeni airport. [@1] Judges also barred him
from entering Romania for five years after he serves the term and ordered the cannabis destroyed
and €340 found on him forfeited. [@1] He appealed to the Bucharest Court of Appeal, so the
sentence is not yet final. [@1]

Border police stopped him on 17 October 2025 after he landed on a flight from Greece and tried
to leave through the “nothing to declare” channel without collecting a checked trolley later
found to hold 49 vacuum-sealed bags of cannabis. [@1,2] Romania’s General Police Inspectorate
(IGPR) said DIICOT and airport border officers caught him in flagrante with about 22 kg in the
suitcase on the Athens–Bucharest route. [@2]

Prosecutors said Hibroj first claimed he did not know the contents and had been promised €500 to
deliver the bag, but the court found he knew what he was carrying and intended to supply the
domestic market. [@1] He later admitted the facts under a simplified procedure, yet judges still
imposed the merged four-year term without extra leniency. [@1]
