Officers found a suspect Hyundai car on 19 May at 17:45 in a field near the
village of Zovuni in Kotayk region. [@1] The car's 46-year-old driver and
72-year-old passenger were detained. [@2] Both men were taken with the car to
the Davtashen department of the community police. [@2] The car was placed in a
guarded special lot. [@2]

A search found a polyethylene bag holding about 4.5 kg of marijuana on one of
the men. [@1] Armenia's Interior Ministry described the seizure as about 4.5 kg
of marijuana taken from the Hyundai. [@1]

The Criminal Police anti-drug-trafficking department had received operative
information that an especially large consignment of drugs was to be smuggled
into Armenia for further sale. [@1] The stop came in a joint operation with the
Investigative Committee's department for economic crimes and smuggling and
Kotayk patrol officers. [@1] The circumstances of the case were still being
established. [@1]
