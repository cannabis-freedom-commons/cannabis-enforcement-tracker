On Thursday 28 May 2026, Attorney General Gentner Drummond's Organized Crime Task Force
searched CN Solutions LLC at 1640 West Main Street and a house at 8300 Northwest 111th Terrace
that investigators linked to the grow. [@1,2] The attorney general's office said agents seized
nearly 3,200 marijuana plants and 440 pounds (about 200 kg) of processed marijuana from an
operation it believed was unlicensed and supplying the black market. [@1,2]

Agents arrested one person at the Main Street site and seized six firearms there, and arrested
four people and seized two firearms at the northwest house. [@1,2] KOKH reported that investigators
described the warehouse as operating as Nomadic Garden and that five people were held on drug
trafficking counts, with four also facing firearm charges. [@2] Drummond said fewer than 1,200
licensed grows remained in Oklahoma compared with more than 9,000 when he took office. [@1]

The task force worked with the Oklahoma Medical Marijuana Authority, the National Guard civil
support team, Oklahoma City police, state agriculture officials and Immigration and Customs
Enforcement. [@1,2]
