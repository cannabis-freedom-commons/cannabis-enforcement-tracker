Police Regional Office–Cordillera teams uprooted and burned marijuana across eight plantation
sites on Mount Whatong in Barangay Loccong, Tinglayan, Kalinga, on Wednesday 27 May 2026 under
the “Oplan Revisit Everest 2.0” campaign. [@2] No cultivators were arrested. [@1,2]

The Philippine National Police (PNP) said operatives with the Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency
(PDEA) used drones to find the fields, which covered 52,000 square metres (about 5.2 hectares)
in rugged terrain. [@1,2] They destroyed about 456,000 fully grown plants, 338,000 seedlings
and roughly 477,500 grams of dried stalks with leaves and fruiting tops, documenting the haul
before on-site burning. [@1,2] The PNP put the standard-price value at ₱162,020,000. [@1]

Police Col. Ledon D. Monte, the regional deputy director for operations, supervised the
day-long operation, according to Tempo. [@2] Authorities said they were pursuing leads on
who planted the fields. [@1,2]
