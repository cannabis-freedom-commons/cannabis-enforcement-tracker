Police arrested two people in a raid on a cannabis nursery in Rehovot, seizing
hundreds of plants. [@1] The police stated that the operation had come after
weeks of investigations. [@1] The two suspects were thought to have been
involved both in growing the drugs and in dealing them. [@1]

Marijuana seized during the raid on the Rehovot nursery was photographed, in an
