## Eradication

On 21 May 2026, Uzbek internal-affairs officers searched a resident's home and household plot in
Jondor district, Bukhara region. [@1,2] They found more than 600 plants identified in the reports
as a mixture of cannabis and opium poppy, with heights of 50 to 70 centimetres. [@1,2]

The reports put the combined weight of the seized plants above nine kilograms. [@1,2] Because
neither source separates the cannabis count or weight from the opium-poppy component, this report
records no cannabis figure. [@1,2]

Podrobno.uz reported that a criminal case was opened for cultivating prohibited crops and that the
resident faced up to five years in prison. [@1]
