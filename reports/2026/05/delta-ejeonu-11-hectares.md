NDLEA reported destroying 11 hectares of cannabis in Ukwuani, Delta State, on 10 May 2026. [@1,2,3,4]

On Sunday 10 May 2026 National Drug Law Enforcement Agency (NDLEA) operatives, supported by
soldiers, raided a cannabis farm at Ejeonu village in Ukwuani local government area, Delta
State. [@1,2]

Agency spokesman Femi Babafemi said in a statement reported by Punch and National Post that
the teams destroyed 27,500 kilograms of skunk cultivated on 11 hectares and recovered another
22.9 kilograms from the site. [@1,2]

In a follow-up operation at Obiaruku, officers arrested 52-year-old Ifeanyichukwu Peter in
connection with the Ejeonu farm. [@1,2]

The same release described a separate clearance at Orogun on 12 May; those figures are not
included here. [@1,2]

Neither account published a field photograph of the Ejeonu raid. [@1,2]

Delta State NDLEA Commander Halilu Hamidi told reporters in Ogwashi-Uku on 15 September 2026 [@3,4] that operatives had destroyed 11 hectares of cannabis farmland at Obiaruku in Ukwani local [@3,4] government area on 10 May 2026. [@3,4]
