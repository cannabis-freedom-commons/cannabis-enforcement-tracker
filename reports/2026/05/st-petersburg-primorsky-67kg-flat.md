Police in St Petersburg's Primorsky district searched a rented flat on Arzeulovskaya alleya and found
a growbox holding 22 cannabis plants. [@1,2] In the flat were also 15 sacks of plant material with
the smell of cannabis, weighing more than 67 kilograms in total. [@1,2]

A 34-year-old local man was detained. [@1,2] At his actual home on the reclaimed land of Vasilyevsky
Island, officers seized electronic scales, packaging materials and eight packets of a substance
weighing about 45 grams. [@1,2] Tests confirmed that the sacks held marijuana and that one of the
packets from his home held cocaine, which he said was for personal use. [@1]

The police said he had bought cannabis seeds, fertiliser and growing equipment himself and made the
drug for sale. [@1] A criminal case was opened for attempted large-scale drug production and sale.
[@1,2] He was placed in a temporary detention centre under article 91 of the criminal procedure code.
