Guyana Police Force ranks took part in a joint eradication operation at Kimbia
in the Upper Berbice River that ran between Wednesday and Thursday. [@1] Police
found the plants, between two and four feet tall, on about three acres of
farmland and destroyed them by fire. [@2] The field held approximately 5,000
cannabis plants. [@1]

Police put the street value of the destroyed narcotics at GYD 847,000. [@1] No
arrests were made during the operation, which police said was conducted without
incident. [@2] Police said investigations were continuing. [@1]

The Kimbia operation followed police eradication work in the same area,
including an earlier exercise in April that destroyed about 310,000 plants on
two fields. [@1]
