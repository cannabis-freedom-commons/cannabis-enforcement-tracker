Thirty-year-old Indian national Suraj Karwar was arrested after a search of his
luggage on arrival in the Maldives. [@1] Officers found suspected narcotics
concealed inside two tennis shoes packed in a bag in his luggage. [@1] The
Criminal Court named Karwar and ordered him held for five days while the
investigation proceeds. [@2]

The court said two packets recovered from the shoes tested positive for THC.
[@1] The order gave no weight for the drugs, while describing the suspicion as a
major drug case. [@1] The court granted the short five-day period after Karwar
asked for time to seek a state defence lawyer. [@1]

The case was reported amid an increase in narcotics smuggling by Indian
nationals arriving in the Maldives. [@1] The country's Narcotics Control Bureau
had expressed concern and issued a special advisory the previous month as those
cases intensified. [@1] The concealment inside footwear was treated as an
attempt to smuggle narcotics into the Maldives. [@2] The packets were identified
as containing THC, or tetrahydrocannabinol. [@2]
