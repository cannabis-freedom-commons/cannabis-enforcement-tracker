## Eradication

Gujarat's Anti-Narcotics Task Force of CID Crime and Railways searched cultivated land and a
residential property at Vinchi village in Poshina taluka on 17 May 2026. [@1,2]

The team recovered about 8.060 kilograms of green cannabis plants from the cultivated land and
4.700 kilograms of dry cannabis from the home. [@1,2] The reports do not give a count of the
plants or the area under cultivation. [@1,2]

Police detained 53-year-old Vinchi resident Makana Gamar, registered a case under the Narcotic
Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act and estimated the combined cannabis value at ₹638,000. [@1,2]

The Hawk published a photograph showing officials with the detained man and seized cannabis. [@1]
