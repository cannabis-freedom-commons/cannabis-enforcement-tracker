Officers of the Bolivarian National Armed Forces detained six people in
flagrancy in two operations in western Caracas against drug trafficking. [@1]
The two operations took place in Nuevo Horizonte and Propatria and were aimed at
dismantling organized-crime groups. [@1] The ministry identified the acting unit
as FANB personnel attached to CZGNB No. 43 operating in Sucre parish in Caracas.
[@2]

In Nuevo Horizonte officers captured five people after patrol work and reported
breaking up a criminal gang. [@1] In that procedure officers reported 2,892
kilograms of drugs divided into 430 wraps of marijuana, 614 wraps of crack and
222 wraps of cocaine. [@1] Officers also seized 337 rifle cartridges, four
vehicles and logistics equipment in the Nuevo Horizonte procedure. [@1]

In Propatria officers dismantled a clandestine processing centre and detained
one person, seizing 128 grams of marijuana with an electric oven for synthetic
drugs, two .22 rifles and dosing technology. [@1] The ministry said the
Propatria haul included an electric oven for preparing synthetic drugs and
dosing technology alongside the two rifles. [@1] The seized evidence and the
detainees were placed at the disposal of the Public Ministry. [@1]
