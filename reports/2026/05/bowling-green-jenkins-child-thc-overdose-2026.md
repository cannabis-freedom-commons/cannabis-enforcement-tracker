Bowling Green police arrested 31-year-old Walstechia Jenkins after a 1-year-old
girl in her care became unresponsive after reportedly eating edible THC gummies.
[@2] Jenkins was arrested on 8 May 2026 on two counts of first-degree wanton
endangerment and one count of second-degree criminal abuse. [@2] She was taken
to the Warren County Regional Jail after the arrest. [@1] Jenkins later appeared
in Warren District Court, waived her preliminary hearing and entered Kentucky's
rocket-docket programme. [@2] She pleaded guilty in Warren Circuit Court, was
placed on pretrial diversion and was released from jail. [@2]

Police were called on 8 May 2026 to Jenkins's apartment on Angora Court. [@2] At
the door, an officer met a paramedic who was carrying the unresponsive
1-year-old girl. [@2] Police found Jenkins in her bedroom appearing to be under
the influence. [@2] Jenkins told police the gummies had been on a table beside
her bed and the child must have eaten them while she briefly left the room. [@2]
One gummy package was labelled as containing D8, D9 and THC with 2,000
milligrams. [@1] Officers seized two THC edible packages, a brown paper bag and
a shooter bottle of vodka from the home. [@2] Two other children were in the
home at the time and could also have accessed the gummies. [@1]

The baby was transported to The Medical Center and treated. [@2] Kentucky's
rocket-docket programme expedites certain low-level drug-related cases toward
treatment and reduces the cost of housing jail inmates. [@2]
