Two Indian nationals, including the ambulance driver, were taken under control
after a security force seized marijuana from an ambulance at Barahchhetra
Municipality-2 in Sunsari. [@1] Superintendent of Police Keshav Kumar Thebe of
Sunsari said driver Dharmendra Kumar Yadav, 18, and Mohammad Sadam, 21, from
Bihar, India, were arrested over the contraband supply. [@1]

Thebe's account, as published that day, put the marijuana confiscated from the
ambulance at 142 kilograms. [@1] A second account citing Thebe at the District
Police Office in Sunsari also put the seizure at 142 kilograms and said the
marijuana was found during a security check at Barahchhetra Municipality-2 on
Wednesday. [@3] The contraband was discovered during a security check. [@3]

Deputy Superintendent of Police Chandra Bahadur Khadka, information officer at
the District Police Office in Sunsari, gave a different figure for the same
stop. [@2] Khadka said police seized an Indian-registered ambulance, number BR
07 PC 4765, carrying 148 kilograms of marijuana packed in plastic bags at
Pulthegauda in Barahachhetra Municipality-2 on Tuesday. [@2] He named the same
two men, both of Birpur in Supaul district, Bihar, as the people arrested on
board. [@2] Police suspected the ambulance was being used in an attempt to
transport the drugs into India. [@2] Further investigation was under way against
the two men who had been arrested. [@2]
