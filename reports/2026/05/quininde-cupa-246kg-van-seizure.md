Ecuador's National Police arrested a man in Quinindé canton, Esmeraldas
province, on 7 May 2026 for allegedly transporting marijuana. [@2] Interior
Minister John Reimberg announced the operation that Thursday night and said 235
kilos of marijuana in 235 bundles were found in the Cupa sector of Quinindé.
[@1]

Police found 246.750 grams of marijuana inside a silver van after officers
stopped and searched the vehicle. [@2] The National Police put the load's value
at about US$915,560 on the national market. [@2] Police retained a mobile phone
and the van. [@2] Primicias reported that the operation took place in Quinindé
canton and that the detained man had earlier drug-trafficking cases. [@3]

The government's night curfew under which the operation took place applied in
Esmeraldas, Guayas and other provinces. [@3] Primicias reported that officers
also confiscated a mobile phone and cash. [@3]

The man and the seized items were handed to the competent authority. [@2]
Authorities had not said where the marijuana was headed, and had released no
details on anyone else linked to the load. [@1]
