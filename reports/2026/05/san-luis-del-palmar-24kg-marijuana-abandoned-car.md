Officers of the Policía de Corrientes saw a Fiat Palio in San Luis del Palmar on
the night of 2 May 2026, and its occupants got out and ran off when they noticed
the police, leaving the car abandoned in Calle Misiones. [@1] The case was left
with the Unidad Fiscal de Investigación en turno, the prosecutor's office on
duty, under Corrientes provincial police jurisdiction. [@2] A search of the area
found none of them, and no one was arrested. [@2]

Inside the car, officers found 24 rectangular packages wrapped in polythene
holding compacted plant material. [@1] The Dirección General de Drogas
Peligrosas y Crimen Organizado, the provincial dangerous-drugs and
organised-crime directorate, tested the material and it was positive for
marijuana of maximum purity. [@1] The load weighed 24.155 kilograms, and police
seized it together with the Fiat Palio used to carry it. [@1] La Prensa
Corrientes described the packages as flat slabs of compacted marijuana. [@3]

The file is at the San Luis del Palmar district police station with the Unidad
Fiscal de Investigación en turno, the prosecutor's office on duty, and officers
were still working to identify those responsible. [@1] Corrientes al Día
reported that investigators were also trying to establish where the load came
from and where it was going. [@2] Corrientes al Día places the seizure in San
Luis del Palmar, a town in Corrientes province. [@2]
