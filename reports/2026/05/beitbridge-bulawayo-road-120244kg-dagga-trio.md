Zimbabwe Republic Police arrested three men at the 60-kilometre peg on the
Beitbridge–Bulawayo road in Matabeleland South on suspicion of drug trafficking.
[@1] The three men were aged 46, 38 and 41, and police said one of them was a
foreign national. [@1] National police spokesperson Commissioner Paul Nyathi
confirmed the arrests. [@1] The three were travelling in a Nissan Hardbody
double-cab pick-up, registration number XVD354GH, when they were stopped. [@1]

Officers found 120,244 kilograms of dagga stashed in the back of the pick-up.
[@1] Skyz Metro FM reported the same police account, with the same weight, road
location and vehicle registration. [@2] Hurumende reported that investigations
into the case were under way. [@3]
