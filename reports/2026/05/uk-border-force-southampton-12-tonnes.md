Border Force officers found about 12 tonnes of cannabis in two shipping
containers at Southampton Port on 6 May 2026. [@1,2] The cannabis came from
Canada, packed in 1,200 boxes. [@1,2] The agency called it its largest cannabis
seizure; the previous record was just under 8 tonnes (7,955 kg), also at
Southampton, in April 2017. [@1]

The Home Office put the revenue the load would have generated for organised
crime at about £139 million. [@1,2]

Home Office intelligence analysts identified the containers before they
arrived, working with Canadian partners and UK police. [@1] The Canada Border
Services Agency had stopped an earlier container of cannabis bound for the UK in
April. [@1]

On 16 June, officers from the South West Regional Organised Crime Unit arrested
three people — two men and one woman in their 30s and 40s, from Merthyr Tydfil,
Ebbw Vale and Abertillery in south Wales — on suspicion of facilitating the
importations. [@1,2] They were held in police custody. [@1,2]
