Officers of Georgia's Central Criminal Police Department arrested six foreign
nationals and accused them of growing cannabis in rented apartments in several
districts of Tbilisi. [@1,2] The Interior Ministry said the group had set up
greenhouses in the flats and cultivated a particularly large amount of cannabis
for sale. [@2]

Police searched the suspects' homes and temporary addresses and seized cocaine,
tetrahydrocannabinol, cannabis plants and dried marijuana, as well as packaging
materials and equipment used to grow and process the drug. [@1,2] The ministry did
not disclose the detainees' names or nationalities. [@1]

The six are charged with illegally buying and storing narcotics in especially
large quantities, helping to sell them and illegally cultivating plants containing
narcotics. [@2] Such offences carry up to 20 years in prison or life imprisonment
in Georgia. [@1] The investigation continues. [@1]
