Officers of Maldonado's Departmental Anti-Drug Brigade (Brigada Departamental
Antidrogas), backed by the Republican Guard (Guardia Republicana) and forensic
police, raided a residence in the Maldonado Nuevo neighbourhood on 21 May 2026
under Operation Jacobo. [@2] Maldonado Police Chief Víctor Trezza declined to
give details on possible new detainees or the people linked to the raided
apartment, saying the investigation remained at a sensitive stage. [@3] Trezza
said the raid dismantled the kitchen where the group prepared and cut cocaine,
and added that police had already charged and convicted some people while
continuing work to bring down the organisation, which operates in Maldonado and
San Carlos with branches elsewhere in Uruguay. [@1]

During the search, officers seized approximately 7.5 kilograms of marijuana and
4.5 kilograms of cocaine, along with three modified 9 mm Glock pistols with
their serial numbers removed, eight extended magazines and more than 550 rounds
of ammunition, mainly 9 mm and .38 calibre. [@2] Local coverage described the
seized cannabis as nearly eight kilograms of marijuana consisting mainly of
buds, and said the ammunition also included .32, .22 and .44 calibres alongside
small-denomination bills, bags of uncounted coins and some US dollars. [@3]
Inside the property, police also found 140,000 Uruguayan pesos in cash, four
precision scales, baking soda, syringes and electrical appliances used to
process cocaine for distribution to local drug sale points. [@2] Officers also
seized four masks depicting US actor Robert Downey Jr., Russian President
Vladimir Putin, businessman Elon Musk and Colombian drug trafficker Pablo
Escobar. [@4]

The raid followed an earlier stage of Operation Jacobo in which police arrested
and convicted a 51-year-old man with no prior criminal record and seized 50
bricks of cocaine weighing 53.432 kilograms, cash, surveillance cameras and
vehicles. [@2] That earlier April seizure took place at a storage site in the
Jardín Los 33 neighbourhood, where the man received a four-year prison sentence
and police estimated the cocaine's market value at more than half a million US
dollars. [@3] The continuing investigation remained under Maldonado's Second
Turn Prosecutor's Office (Fiscalía de 2.º Turno de Maldonado), led by prosecutor
Jessica Pereira. [@2]
