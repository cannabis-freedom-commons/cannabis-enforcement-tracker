At least 1.3 tonnes of marijuana were seized in the municipality of Chitaga,
Norte de Santander, in an operation by the Police with the DEA and the Attorney
General's Office. [@1] Officers found the load during registration, control and
verification checks on a public road when they detected a vehicle carrying the
illicit shipment. [@1] One person was captured during the procedure and the
vehicle carrying the shipment was immobilized. [@1]

The operation seized 1,300 kilograms of marijuana, captured one person and
immobilized one vehicle. [@2] Police said investigations established the drug
came from southwestern Colombia and was destined for the border, where it would
be moved toward Venezuela. [@1] Police framed the result within the Esmeralda
Plus strategy against drug-trafficking routes. [@2]

Investigators linked the shipment to a criminal structure associated with FARC
dissidents, specifically the Dagoberto Ramos structure, which used fractional
transport and vehicle changes to evade controls. [@1] Anti-Narcotics director
Brigadier General William Castaño Ramos said the result fulfilled commitments to
the country and the international community and significantly affected the
groups' finances. [@1] Radio Nacional corroborated the seizure as about 1.3
tonnes of marijuana in Norte de Santander in a coordinated action with judicial
authorities and international bodies. [@3]
