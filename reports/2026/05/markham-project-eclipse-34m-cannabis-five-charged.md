York Regional Police arrested the primary suspect and searched a Markham home on
15 May 2026. [@1] Five Markham residents were later charged: Tien Chen, 59; He
Jin Jiang, 43; Junjuang Ke, 52; Yun Qing Chen, 50; and Yongzhong Ke, 78. [@1]
All five are charged with possession of cannabis for the purpose of
distribution. [@1] Tien Chen faces an additional count of the same charge and
one count of possessing a scheduled substance. [@1]

On 21 May, officers searched two more Markham homes and a commercial shipping
unit in Toronto. [@2] The searches turned up more than 1,300 pounds (about 590
kilograms) of illicit cannabis. [@4] Officers also seized more than 30 jars of
cannabis oil extract. [@4] Police put the combined street value of the drugs at
more than $3.4 million. [@1] At the first home, officers also seized Canadian
currency and a substance believed to be crystal methamphetamine. [@1]

Police launched Project Eclipse in May after a tip that a suspect was
distributing illicit cannabis from a Markham home. [@3] Police allege the
suspects were part of a network trafficking cannabis throughout the area. [@1]
