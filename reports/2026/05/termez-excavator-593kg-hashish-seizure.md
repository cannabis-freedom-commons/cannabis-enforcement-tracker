Uzbekistan's border guards and customs officers intercepted drugs being smuggled
from Afghanistan into Uzbekistan, as announced by the State Security Service
press service. [@1,3] The drugs were discovered at the Termez-Auto border customs
checkpoint in Surkhandarya region during the inspection of a truck with a
trailer driven by an Afghan citizen. [@1,3] The vehicle was a Mercedes cargo truck
driven by an Afghan citizen that had entered Uzbekistan through the Termez
checkpoint and underwent routine inspection. [@2] Authorities said the criminal
group had planned to move the shipment across the border at dawn, hoping
military personnel would be less alert during the early morning hours. [@2] The
trailer was carrying a Doosan excavator, and a specialized scan found suspicious
objects hidden inside the excavator's lifting arm that were not part of its
original design. [@2] An endoscopic inspection of the excavator's internal
cavities then uncovered the narcotics hidden inside the machinery. [@1]

The shipment included 593 kg of hashish and 3 kg of opium. [@1] A second account
likewise reports that endoscopic examination found 593 kilograms of hashish and
3 kilograms of opium concealed inside the machinery. [@2] A criminal case was
opened under articles 246 and 273 of Uzbekistan's Criminal Code, covering
smuggling and illegal drug handling with intent to distribute. [@1,3]

The operation was carried out jointly by the SSS operational units and border
troops together with the Customs Committee. [@2] Authorities were working to
identify and detain individuals who might have been involved in the attempted
smuggling, and no arrests were named at the time of publication. [@1]
