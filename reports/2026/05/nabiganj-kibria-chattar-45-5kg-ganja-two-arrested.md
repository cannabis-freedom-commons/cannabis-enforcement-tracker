RAB-9 arrested two drug traders with 45 kg 500 g of ganja in Nabiganj upazila of
Habiganj district. [@1] The operation took place at midday on Thursday 7 May at
Kibria Chattar in Aushkandi Union under Nabiganj police station, acting on a
tip-off. [@1] Two men standing in front of Shyamoli Counter tried to flee when
they noticed RAB's presence and were detained. [@1]

A search of the pair and three sacks they carried recovered 45.500 kg of ganja.
[@1] Those held were Sohag Sarkar, 35, of Kalipur village in Bhairab upazila of
Kishoreganj, and Nabi Hossain, 30, of Ibrahimpur village in Belabo upazila of
Narsingdi. [@1] In preliminary questioning the pair admitted collecting the
ganja in a border area and carrying it for sale elsewhere in the country, RAB-9
said. [@1] A case under the Narcotics Control Act, 2018, was being processed and
the two were handed over to Nabiganj police station. [@1]

RAB-9 said its anti-drug operations and intelligence work would continue. [@1]
