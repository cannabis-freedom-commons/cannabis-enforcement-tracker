Israel Police arrested a 17-year-old and two young men aged 20 and 21 in Kafr
Kanna, a village in the Lower Galilee, in a raid on a building there. [@1] A
Border Police undercover unit and Northern District officers carried out the
raid after a tip that criminals were holding illegal weapons in the building,
and the officers searched the building and the open ground around it. [@1]

Kikar Hashabbat put the hashish at about half a kilogram and the crystal meth at
about 140 grams. [@1]

Arutz Sheva reported that the three suspects were brought before a judge at the
Nof HaGalil–Nazareth Magistrate's Court on Wednesday, which granted the request
to extend their detention. [@2] Kikar Hashabbat reported that the three were
arrested at the start of the week and that the investigation continued. [@1]
