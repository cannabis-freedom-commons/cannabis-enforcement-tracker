Bolivarian National Police officers apprehended one person in Maracaibo, Zulia,
during a citizen-security operation against the sale and distribution of drugs.
[@1] The suspect allegedly used gas cylinders as camouflage to evade police
checkpoints and controls. [@1] Police intercepted the cargo truck near Paseo Las
Ciencias in the Casco Central of Bolívar parish in Maracaibo after prior alerts
and intelligence work on its suspicious movements. [@2]

Officers seized 600 wraps of marijuana weighing 315 kilograms that had been
hidden inside domestic gas cylinders in central Maracaibo. [@2] The final
inventory listed 315 kilograms of marijuana in 600 packs, 30 modified domestic
gas cylinders, one cargo truck and one mobile phone. [@2] The suspect allegedly
used a basic-services distribution front to move the load by truck, drilling and
altering cylinders to create false bottoms that concealed the drug on public
roads. [@2]

Venezuelan reports call gas cylinders bombonas, the local term for domestic
cooking-gas containers, and say the Interior Ministry announced the arrest
through its official Telegram channel. [@2] The detainee, whose identity was not
revealed, was placed immediately at the disposal of the Public Ministry
Prosecutor's Office for criminal proceedings. [@2] Authorities described the
procedure as part of the National Anti-Drug Plan under the instructions of
Interior Minister Diosdado Cabello Rondón. [@2]

An English-language edition of the same account states that officers of the
Bolivarian National Police Corps seized 600 packages of marijuana weighing 315
kilograms in the centre of Maracaibo. [@3]
