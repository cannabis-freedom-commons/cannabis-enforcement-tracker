Honduras's National Police captured one person in possession of 126 packages of
marijuana in an operation in the northern department of Colón. [@1] Anti-drug
agents detained the 24-year-old in the municipality of Sabá on suspicion of
aggravated drug trafficking. [@1] The detainee is originally from Concepción del
Norte, in Santa Bárbara. [@2]

Officers seized 63 wrapped double bundles, equivalent to 126 compact packages of
suspected marijuana in rectangular presentations. [@1] Specialists assessed each
package as weighing more than one pound because of its packaging. [@1] Officers
also confiscated an additional plastic bag with about six pounds of the same
substance. [@1] Officers also seized two motorcycles, one of which had altered
physical characteristics because its colour did not match the police system
record. [@2]

The operation was carried out through surveillance and monitoring as part of
front-line action against structures dedicated to street-level dealing and
distribution of narcotics. [@1] The arrested man and the seized evidence were
placed at the disposal of the competent authorities to continue the legal
process for harm to public health. [@1] Specialists were to carry out exact
weighing and field chemical testing to confirm the nature of the drug. [@2]
