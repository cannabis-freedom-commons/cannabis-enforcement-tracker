The seizure took place on 20 May 2026 at Boca Ribeira in Praia Branca on São
Nicolau island. [@1] The Polícia Nacional seized more than 40 kg of hashish
packed in six volumes containing drugs. [@1]

The operation followed legal procedures and tests by the Polícia Judiciária were
positive for hashish. [@1] Police said the seized product weighed 42.3 kg in
total, in volumes of 6.3 kg, 7.5 kg, 6.5 kg and 8.2 kg. [@1] Each volume held
several soap-shaped blocks, packaging police associated with narcotic
substances. [@1]

The São Vicente department of the Polícia Judiciária and the Coast Guard command
mobilized a joint team to support the work. [@1] Search and patrol operations
ran along the island coast with maritime, air and land resources in coordination
with the Maritime Police. [@1]
