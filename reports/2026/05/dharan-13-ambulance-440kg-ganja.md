A police team from the Area Police Office in Dharan found 440 kilograms of ganja
packed in plastic sacks inside an ambulance bearing the registration number Ko 1
Cha 7359 in Dharan Sub-Metropolitan City-13. [@2] The team was checking the area
at around 12:40 am and found the sacks while inspecting the parked ambulance.
[@3] The ambulance was operated by Budhasubba Diagnostic Center Pvt. Ltd., a
private diagnostic centre in Dharan. [@2]

Police said they were searching for the driver and others involved in the
transport of the ganja. [@2] The first accounts of the seizure name no person
who was arrested. [@3] The ambulance and the 440 kilograms of ganja were taken
to the Area Police Office in Dharan for further investigation. [@3]

Nepal News reported the Dharan seizure as part of a pattern in which ambulances
were becoming a preferred vehicle for moving hundreds of kilograms of ganja from
fields in Nepal's border districts toward India. [@1] Sunsari borders the Araria
and Supaul districts of Bihar in India, and Nepal News reported that most of the
cannabis leaving the district crossed to India through the Koshi Rural
Municipality area around Bhantabari. [@1] The Superintendent of Police of
Sunsari, Keshav Kumar Thebe, said smugglers used private cars, trucks and
ambulances because the open border made the trade hard to stop. [@1]
