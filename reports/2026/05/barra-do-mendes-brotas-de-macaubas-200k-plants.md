Civil Police teams eradicated about 200,000 cannabis plants on Tuesday 12 May
2026 in a rural area between Barra do Mendes and Brotas de Macaúbas, in Bahia's
Chapada Diamantina region. [@1] Suspected growers at the site fled into the
surrounding woodland as the officers approached, and nobody had been arrested
when the accounts were published. [@1]

The plantation covered about four hectares of rural land. [@1] The eradication
was the third phase of Operação Raízes Ocultas, an operation the Civil Police of
Bahia runs against drug trafficking in the region. [@1] It was carried out by
teams from the 5th Narcotics and Psychotropic Drugs Police Station in Irecê and
the 14th Regional Interior Police Coordinating Office, also based in Irecê. [@1]

Diego Raphael Nogueira Rodrigues da Costa, head of the Irecê narcotics station,
put the loss to organised crime at an estimated R$45 million. [@1] Police said
investigations were continuing to identify and locate those responsible for the
crop. [@1]

The operation took place on Tuesday morning, according to Sertão Hoje, which
also reported that the teams came from the Irecê drug unit and the 14th regional
coordinating office, through its Chapada technical and tactical support group.
[@3]

Sudoeste Total likewise reported about 200,000 plants destroyed on about four
hectares. [@2]
