## Eradication

Karo police detained a 29-year-old man in farmland at Partibi Lama, Merek, on Wednesday 20 May
2026. [@1,2] Officers then searched the plot and uprooted eight plants believed to be cannabis,
measuring between 85 and 165 centimetres tall. [@1,2]

The wet plants weighed 8.8 kilograms net, while officers also seized 1.28 grams of leaves, twigs
and seeds and one rolling paper. [@1,2] The suspect and evidence were taken to the Karo narcotics
unit for investigation under Article 114(1) of Indonesia's Narcotics Law. [@1,2]

NusanewsTV published a photograph showing police and a detained man among the plants. [@2]
