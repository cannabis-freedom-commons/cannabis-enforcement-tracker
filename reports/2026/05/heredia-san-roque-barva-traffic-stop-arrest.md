Fuerza Pública officers arrested two men on the night of Sunday 31 May 2026 in
San Roque de Barva, Heredia, on suspicion of transporting drugs. [@1] The
detention happened while officers were carrying out security patrols and
roadside checks in the area. [@2]

The car held two men surnamed Esquivel and Ledezma, and officers noticed a
strong smell of marijuana from the vehicle. [@2] Under the driver's seat
officers found a large transparent plastic package containing a white powder
appearing to be cocaine. [@2] After field tests, the Drug Control Police
confirmed the substances were cocaine hydrochloride and THC. [@1]

Authorities seized 688 grams of cocaine, one marijuana cigarette, one dose of a
drug known as wax and ₡267,000 in cash of apparently dubious origin. [@1] The
two suspects were taken to the Heredia flagrancy prosecutor's office, where they
were placed at the disposal of the judicial authorities. [@1]
