At about 11 a.m. on 13 May 2026, officers from the Dunmaili Subdistrict Police Station found a large stand of suspected cannabis while patrolling a landscaped greenbelt in Yining. [@1] A specialist comparison confirmed the plants were wild cannabis growing naturally, rather than a cultivated crop, the Xinjiang News report said. [@1] The station organised a clearance and searched the surrounding area for additional plants. [@1] After more than two hours of work, officers reported that more than 1,000 wild cannabis plants had been cleared. [@1]
