Berastagi police carried out a cannabis-cultivation operation at about 09:35 on Wednesday 27 May
2026 at a field on Jalan Ujung Aji Gang Sampang Rukur in Rumah Berastagi, Karo. [@1]

Officers found 14 cannabis plants and detained DPS, a 65-year-old man from Gundaling I, who was at
the field. [@1] A search also found a cigarette box containing dried cannabis, and the police account
says DPS admitted that the plants belonged to him. [@1]

Police took the detainee and all the evidence to the Karo police narcotics unit for further
investigation. [@1] The opened account does not say that the plants were destroyed. [@1]
