Before dawn on Sunday 10 May 2026, West Sumatra's provincial narcotics agency halted a yellow
Toyota Agya and a silver Daihatsu Sigra travelling together on the Bukittinggi–Medan road at
Jorong Batang Palupuh, Nagari Koto Rantang, Agam regency. [@1,2] Officers searched the Sigra and
found seven flour sacks covered in blue plastic containing cannabis. [@1,2]

The agency put the seizure at about 150 kilograms on the scene and later reported 145,085.23 grams
(about 145 kg) in its nationwide Saber Bersinar tally. [@1,2] Four men with the initials MI, DR,
NLP and AF were arrested as suspected couriers escorting the shipment from Mandailing Natal in
North Sumatra toward Minangkabau distribution points. [@1,2] Investigators said the drivers were
watching the route while the drugs moved overland. [@1]

National BNN said the case was one of several uncovered under Operasi Saber Bersinar in May 2026. [@2]
The suspects face Indonesia's narcotics trafficking articles, which can carry the death penalty. [@1]
