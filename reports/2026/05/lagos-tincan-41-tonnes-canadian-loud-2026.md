In Nigeria, National Drug Law Enforcement Agency operations illustrate the scale
of trafficking flows through Lagos ports. [@1] Officers intercepted more than
4.1 tonnes of the same cannabis substance at Tincan Island port in Lagos. [@1]
The substance is Canadian Loud, which is described as a high-potency cannabis
variety. [@1] Authorities valued the Tincan Island cargo at about 7.6 million
dollars. [@1]

The cannabis cargo was hidden in vehicles imported from Canada and placed inside
a maritime container. [@1] The container had left Toronto on 28 March 2026,
travelled by rail to Montreal, then passed through the port of Tanger Med in
Morocco before arriving in Lagos on 9 May. [@1]

The operation mobilised anti-drug services, Nigerian Customs and international
partners including the United States Drug Enforcement Administration and the
Royal Canadian Mounted Police. [@1] The account places these seizures among
trafficking circuits linking Latin America, the African Atlantic coasts, the
Sahel and European markets. [@1]
