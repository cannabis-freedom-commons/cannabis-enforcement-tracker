## Field eradication

Honduran Armed Forces and agents from the Directorate for the Fight Against Drug Trafficking (DLCN) located and eradicated a crop of suspected cannabis in the village of Güiscoyol, Gualaco, Olancho. [@1,2] El País and HCH both reported that authorities counted 2,957 plants across about half a manzana of land, or approximately 0.35 hectares; HCH said the plants stood 1.5 to 2 metres high. [@1,2] HCH described the site as mountainous. [@2]

## Operation

El País said the operation involved the 115th Infantry Brigade, 16th Infantry Battalion and the Armed Forces' Institutional Support Operations Command, working with the DLCN. [@1] The plants were eradicated with the Public Ministry, and HCH reported that DLCN agents burned the crop. [@1,2] Both reports described the plants as suspected marijuana, and neither named a grower or reported an arrest. [@1,2] Neither report gives a weight, value or separate count for seedlings. [@1,2]
