Four Cuban citizens are being prosecuted by the Mexican courts after the
prosecution presented evidence accusing them of street-level dealing for the
distribution of marijuana in Cancún, in the state of Quintana Roo. [@1] A
control judge issued the criminal binding-over orders against the Cubans
identified as Osiel “N”, Enrique “N”, Rubén “N” and Luis “N”, after the Fiscalía
General del Estado, through its specialised unit for combating narcomenudeo,
presented evidence at a hearing on their alleged participation in health crimes
in the narcomenudeo modality in the municipality of Benito Juárez. [@1] The four
Cubans were detained over events in Supermanzana 23 in Cancún and will remain in
preventive detention as a precautionary measure for the duration of their
criminal proceedings. [@1]

The alleged acts occurred in Supermanzana 23, a district of Cancún. [@1] The
case was handled by the Fiscalía Especializada en Combate al Delito de
Narcomenudeo, the state prosecutor’s specialised unit against street-level
dealing. [@1] The four will stay in preventive detention during the proceedings
to ensure their presence at trial and to avoid flight risk. [@1]

The Quintana Roo prosecutor recalled that, under Article 13 of the National Code
of Criminal Procedure, the accused are presumed innocent until a judicial
authority declares their responsibility. [@1]
