The Royal Bahamas Defence Force intercepted a go-fast boat 38 nautical miles southwest of Mars Bay,
Andros, early on 4 May 2026 and seized 2,576 pounds (about 1,168 kg) of suspected hydroponic
cannabis. [@1] The operation also involved the Royal Bahamas Police Force, the U.S. Coast Guard and
Operation Bahamas Turks and Caicos, and police put the street value at $6.4 million. [@1]

## The interception

A U.S. air asset alerted the defence force at 7 p.m. on 3 May to a suspected narcotics vessel 11
miles west of Duncan Town, Ragged Island. [@1] The vessel was spotted at 12:45 a.m., and after a
chase of about an hour the crew launched a sea boat and fired warning shots to stop it, Lieutenant
Darius Adams said. [@2] It was interdicted at 1:57 a.m., 38 nautical miles southwest of Mars Bay. [@1]

Two Bahamian men, aged 37 and 53, were on board; both were taken into custody and brought to New
Providence. [@1,2] The Tribune reported 66 bales of suspected hydroponic cannabis. [@2] Officers
suspected the drug was bound for New Providence but could not confirm where it came from. [@2]
