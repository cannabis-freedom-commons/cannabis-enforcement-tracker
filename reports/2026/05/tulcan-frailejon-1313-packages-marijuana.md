Members of Ecuador's army seized 1,313 packages of marijuana hidden inside a
covered van during an operation in Tulcán, on the country's border with
Colombia, the Defence Ministry said on Friday 15 May 2026. [@1] The ministry
said the operation took place in the sector Y de Frailejón, in Tulcán, an area
it described as a border zone with Colombia. [@1] Several vehicles apparently
used as escorts tried to obstruct the military manoeuvre to help the driver
escape. [@1] The soldiers kept the van and the marijuana it carried despite that
attempt. [@1] The ministry published its account of the seizure on social media.
[@1] Its account says the driver fled during the intervention. [@1]

According to the ministry, the operation caused economic damage of 6.5 million
dollars to criminal structures. [@1] A heading above the brief reported one
tonne of drugs seized on Ecuador's northern border. [@1]
