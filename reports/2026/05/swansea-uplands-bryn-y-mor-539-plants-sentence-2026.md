Police raiding a cannabis farm found a man hiding inside a cupboard. [@1] The
man was 42-year-old Antonjo Kodheli, of no fixed abode, found at the property on
Bryn Y Môr Crescent, Uplands, Swansea. [@2] He emerged from the cupboard with
his hands up when asked and was then handcuffed. [@3] He had pleaded guilty to
one charge of being concerned in the production of a Class B controlled drug.
[@1] He appeared at Swansea Crown Court on Friday and was sentenced to eight
months in prison. [@1]

Police found a total of 539 cannabis plants across eight rooms in the property.
[@1] Sgt Luke Tucker of South Wales Police said the property had continually
been used for illegal activity including drug cultivation. [@1] Tucker said
money generated by cannabis farms is very commonly funnelled towards even more
serious criminal activity, which is why police act against them. [@1]
