New York City marshals executing a court-ordered eviction at 750 Pelham Parkway
South in the Bronx found a cannabis grow operation in apartment 1V around midday
on 12 May 2026. [@2] The marshal called the New York City Sheriff's Office task
force, which confirmed the flat was an illegal grow house, and the sheriff's
office raided and shut it down. [@4]

Officers seized 299 cannabis plants at every stage of growth, along with
marijuana and THC flowers. [@2] Telemundo 47, citing the Sheriff's Office, put
the marijuana at 147.2 pounds, about 66.7 kilograms, and other outlets described
roughly 300 pounds of plants and 147 pounds of THC flowers. [@2] Nobody was
inside the apartment, and police said no arrests were immediately made. [@1]

The NYPD said the five-room flat had special ventilation and lighting systems
and that its power supply had been illegally altered to keep the operation
running. [@1] The apartment was dangerous enough that the fire department's
hazardous materials unit and the buildings department were called and flats two
floors up were evacuated. [@2]

Neighbours said they had never seen anyone enter or leave the apartment, and the
NYPD asked for tips as it searched for whoever ran the operation. [@1]

Investigators had not located the person responsible and made no arrests as of
15 May 2026. [@3]
