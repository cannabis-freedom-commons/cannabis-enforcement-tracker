More than 10,000 pounds of marijuana were seized while in transit to England,
according to U.S. Customs and Border Protection officials in Baltimore. [@1]
U.S. Customs and Border Protection officers seized over 10,000 pounds of
marijuana on May 29 in Baltimore from a shipping container concealed for
shipment to Liverpool, England. [@2] No arrests had been made when the seizure
was announced, and special agents with Homeland Security Investigations
continued the investigation. [@3]

Officers had conducted an export inspection of a 40-foot shipping container sent
from Nassau County, N.Y. that was manifested as men’s cotton and nylon shirts.
[@2] Instead, officers discovered 238 boxes stuffed full of vacuum-sealed bags
of marijuana inside the container. [@2] CBP narcotics detector dog Letti, a
2-year-old female German shepherd, had alerted officers to the presence of
narcotics in the boxes. [@2]

The marijuana weighed a combined 4,815 kilograms, or 10,615 pounds. [@2] CBP put
its street value at about $24,000,000 in the United States and said it could
fetch twice that amount in Europe. [@2]
