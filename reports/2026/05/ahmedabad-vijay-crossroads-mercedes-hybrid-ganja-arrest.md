Ahmedabad’s Special Operations Group apprehended an alleged drug peddler
transporting cannabis in a Mercedes car near Vijay Char Rasta. [@1] The accused
was identified as 26-year-old Hardik Kalal, a resident of Naranpura. [@2] He is
originally from Rajasthan and currently lives in Naranpura. [@1] Police
registered a case under the NDPS Act at the DCB police station. [@2] Police said
a detailed investigation was launched to trace the consignment’s source and
intended recipients. [@1]

Officers seized 1.975 kg of hybrid ganja along with the Mercedes and other items
collectively valued at Rs 79.7 lakh near the municipal garden opposite Memnagar
fire station. [@2] Police valued the cannabis alone at over Rs 69 lakh. [@2] A
pink bag containing two plastic packets of suspected hybrid ganja was recovered
from the car’s back seat. [@2] The consignment involved nine packets of cannabis
in total. [@1] Seven packets were taken away by the supplier while two packets
remained inside the vehicle. [@1] Forensic examination later confirmed the
substance was hybrid ganja. [@2] Officers broke a small rear window to enter the
car because the keys were allegedly with a friend. [@2] Police also seized two
mobile phones, documents and the car. [@2]

Acting on information that a hybrid cannabis consignment had been brought from
Rajasthan to Ahmedabad for illegal sale, inspectors Gohil and Bhatt detained
Kalal. [@1] Police said Kalal and his relative Pradeep Kalal from Rajasthan were
allegedly trafficking hybrid ganja brought from abroad. [@2] Kalal had procured
the cannabis near the airport on the instructions of his relative Pradeep Kalal.
[@1] The contraband was handed over to him in a trolley bag at the airport
location. [@1] The Mercedes belonged to Bhupendra Parmar. [@1] Police quoted
Kalal as saying he had taken the vehicle over an outstanding financial liability
while the supply-chain probe continued. [@1] Archit Agarwal of Bopal and
associate Lalo Soni were also alleged to be supplying hybrid cannabis by
Mercedes, reflecting suspected use of luxury vehicles to avoid police attention.
