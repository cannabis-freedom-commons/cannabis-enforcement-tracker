Three people were arrested after a multiple police agency operation targeting an
online cannabis dispensary operating in Niagara. [@1] On 5 and 6 May 2026,
police executed search warrants at six locations in Niagara Region and Hamilton
and on five vehicles. [@2] The searches formed part of Project Diagonal, an
investigation begun in 2024 into a business trading as Harmonized Hemp and
CannaTrust. [@2] Investigators said the business offered same-day delivery
within Niagara Region and shipped cannabis products by courier to other parts of
Canada. [@2]

Officers seized illegal cannabis and cannabis products that police valued at
more than $1.1 million. [@3] They also seized a quantity of psilocybin and about
$13,000 in Canadian currency. [@2]

Those charged were Jason Muscat-Tyler, 32, of Niagara-on-the-Lake; Sean
Paul-Farr, 31, of Hamilton; and Michelle Dolbeck, 37, of St. Catharines. [@2]
All three face possession of property obtained by crime, possession of illicit
cannabis, possession for the purpose of distributing, and possession for the
purpose of selling. [@2] Paul-Farr and Dolbeck also each face a charge of
possession for the purpose of trafficking psilocybin. [@2] They were released
from custody and scheduled to appear in the Ontario Court of Justice in St.
Catharines on 8 June 2026. [@3]

Niagara Regional Police, Hamilton Police and the Royal Canadian Mounted Police
assisted the OPP-led team. [@4] OPP Chief Superintendent Mike Stoddart said
illegal online cannabis sales remain a priority for law enforcement and that
unregulated products pose risks to public safety. [@2]
