Danny Bouchard, a 35-year-old Montrealer, was arrested and faced 23 charges
related to drugs and firearms, and asked to be released on bail. [@1] A police
source first told investigators last summer that Bouchard was selling drugs "in
good quantity", and an undercover officer contacted him and three days later
bought a pound of cannabis. [@1] Because Bouchard had no vehicle, the officer
picked him up at the metro, and during the trip Bouchard told the officer he
could also sell him "pills" and cocaine. [@1]

Rather than arrest Bouchard for selling cannabis, officers kept the operation
going, buying hashish, methamphetamine and what appeared to be cocaine samples
from him. [@1] Bouchard also showed the officer photos of firearms and said he
could sell them, and later told him he kept a shotgun under a mattress at home.
[@1] According to the court documents, Bouchard told the officer during the car
ride that he had met a new contact in the firearms trade, an Algerian from
Cartierville who went by a nickname. [@1] Undercover officers obtained an SKS
rifle and seven rounds of ammunition from Bouchard for C$4,200, and he showed
them photos of 55 pistols offered for sale. [@1]

After hearing the evidence, judge Marie-Ève Landreville ordered Bouchard held in
pre-trial detention at the Joliette courthouse. [@1] Despite his requests to be
freed while awaiting trial, the judge kept him in custody, and he is due back in
court in mid-June for the next stage of proceedings. [@1]
