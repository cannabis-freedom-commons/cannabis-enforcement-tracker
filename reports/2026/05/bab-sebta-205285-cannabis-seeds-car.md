Customs and security officers at the Bab Sebta border post stopped a
Spanish-registered Hyundai Tucson on Monday 11 May 2026 as its driver prepared
to enter Morocco from Ceuta. [@1] The driver, a 41-year-old Moroccan resident in
Ceuta, drew the officers' suspicion, and a thorough search followed. [@1]

Officers found 205,285 cannabis seeds, more than 205,000, in bags inside a
hiding place built beside the car's gearbox. [@1] The Moroccan newspaper
Assabah, which El Faro de Ceuta drew on, reported that the stash was hidden
tightly in the compartment beside the gearbox. [@2]

The driver was placed under investigation under the supervision of the competent
prosecutor, who is to establish the possible extensions of the operation and
identify everyone suspected of belonging to the seed-smuggling network. [@1] The
crossing, known in Arabic as Bab Sebta and on the Spanish side as El Tarajal,
carries constant traffic both ways, and officers have repeatedly found hashish,
people, phones and hookah tobacco in vehicles there. [@1]
