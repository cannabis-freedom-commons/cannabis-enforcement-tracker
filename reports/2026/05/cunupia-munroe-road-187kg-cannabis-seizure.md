The Trinidad and Tobago Police Service detained a 31-year-old taxi driver at a
house on Rasheed Avenue, Munroe Road, Cunupia, around 8 am on Friday 15 May
2026. [@2] Police said the man led investigators to concealed crocus bags, the
woven sacks used for bulk cargo, inside an abandoned structure next to the
premises. [@2]

The search seized 187 kilograms of cannabis and 187 trafficking packets, which
police valued at TT$1,309,000. [@2] Officers also seized a Glock 19 pistol with
14 rounds of 9 mm ammunition. [@2]

The man was arrested and cautioned for possession of marijuana for the purpose
of trafficking, possession of a firearm without a firearm user's licence, and
possession of ammunition without a firearm user's licence. [@2] The operation
was carried out under the 2026 State of Emergency Regulations as part of Central
Division work against narcotics trafficking, gang violence, illegal firearms and
organised crime. [@2] It involved officers from the Cunupia Police Station,
Caroni CID, the K-9 Unit and the Central Division Task Force Area North. [@2]
Deputy Commissioner of Police (Operations) Suzette Martin led the operation.
[@2]

Police said investigations were continuing. [@2]

The Trinidad Guardian placed the operation in Cunupia on a Friday. [@1]
