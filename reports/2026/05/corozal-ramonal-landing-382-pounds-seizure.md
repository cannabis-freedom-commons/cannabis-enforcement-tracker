Police seized 382 pounds of suspected cannabis in an intelligence-led operation
in northern Belize. [@1] Assistant Superintendent Stacy Smith said Mobile
Interdiction Unit officers carried out the operation on Tuesday, May 19, 2026,
at around 8:40 p.m. [@1] The team was searching the area of Ramonal Landing in
Patchakan Village, Corozal District, when officers found eight black bags
holding the suspected cannabis. [@1]

The eight bags together weighed 382 pounds, which converts to about 173.27
kilograms. [@2] Police deposited the drugs as found property and announced no
arrests in connection with the seizure. [@1] 7NewsBelize gives the same account
of Smith's briefing: the same unit, time, Ramonal Landing search, eight bags and
382-pound total. [@2]
