Prefectura Naval Argentina criminal-intelligence officers set up strategic
observation posts near Puerto Piray in the early hours after receiving
information about a possible illegal drug crossing. [@1] Officers saw a
motorboat reach kilometre 1,790 of the Paraná River at Puerto Kimich, where
bundles were unloaded into vehicles waiting on the bank. [@1] When the officers
were detected, one pickup fled and officers followed it in a controlled pursuit
to a wooded area, where its occupants abandoned it and escaped on foot. [@1] No
detentions were reported, as the occupants abandoned the vehicle and disappeared
into the undergrowth. [@2]

Officers found 132 bundles with characteristics consistent with
illicit-substance transport where the pickup was abandoned. [@1] Prefectura
seized a total of 3,184 kilos of marijuana in different formats, which the force
estimated was worth more than eleven billion pesos. [@1] Officers also seized
three vehicles in total: two pickups and a motorcycle allegedly used to move the
load. [@3]

The operation took place in the city of Eldorado, Misiones, after officers
detected the clandestine entry of the merchandise from Paraguay. [@1] The load
had entered illegally from Paraguay and was being moved in vehicles adapted for
its transport. [@1] Primera Edición reported that the operation ran under Plan
Paraná, a national Security Ministry programme to strengthen controls on
cross-border crime in the Argentine litoral. [@3]

The Federal Prosecutor's Office and Federal Court in Eldorado, led by Pablo
Kulchar and Miguel Angel Guerrero, ordered the merchandise moved for official
counting and weighing. [@1]
