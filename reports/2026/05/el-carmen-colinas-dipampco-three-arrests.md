Honduras’s DIPAMPCO anti-gang police captured three alleged Mara Salvatrucha
members and seized a marijuana load in northern Honduras. [@1] Spokesperson Rosa
Guadalupe said the three were responsible for moving, storing and distributing
the drug. [@1] Police identified one detainee as 19-year-old Osman Edgardo
Martínez Vázquez, alias El Veloz, who allegedly moved the drug from place to
place. [@1] Police identified the second detainee as 21-year-old Michael Rápalo,
alias El Colocho, who allegedly hid the drug to avoid detection. [@1] Police
identified the third detainee as Angie Elizabeth Oliva García, alias La Yegua,
who allegedly stored the load. [@1] The three will be sent before judicial
authorities on aggravated drug-trafficking charges. [@1]

Officers intercepted a red pick-up in aldea El Carmen, colonia Colinas, finding
marijuana hidden in a false compartment built into the vehicle. [@1] Authorities
put the load’s value at more than four million lempiras (150,404.60 dollars) and
said it was meant for narcomenudeo, street-level retail sale, in different parts
of Honduras. [@1] Investigators confirmed the vehicle had an adapted double
bottom for moving drugs past checkpoints without raising suspicion. [@1]
DIPAMPCO said investigations remain open to establish the load’s exact origin
and where it was to be distributed. [@1] Authorities did not rule out more
people involved in the northern Honduras storage and distribution network. [@1]

DIPAMPCO said it will continue intelligence and surveillance work to weaken the
finances and logistics of criminal groups operating in the country. [@1]
