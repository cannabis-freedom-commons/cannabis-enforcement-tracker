Peruvian Grupo Terna officers captured 36-year-old Antonio Jesús Rivera Paniura,
known as alias Dragon, in San Juan de Lurigancho. [@1] The operation took place
in the family home in the Juan Pablo II informal settlement, where he lived with
his elderly mother. [@1]

After surveillance and verification work, officers entered the house and found
ten kilos of marijuana in vacuum-sealed packs ready for sale. [@1] Authorities
described the detainee as the area's main drug supplier, with direct links to
small-scale dealing networks across several parts of the district. [@1]
Escuadrón Verde chief Carlos Alcántara said the marijuana was ready to be
distributed to small-scale dealers in the district and that vacuum packing made
it harder for police to trace. [@1]

When officers asked whether he had anything illicit in the house, he first
denied it and later admitted responsibility, saying he had made a mistake. [@1]
His criminal history includes drug-trafficking records from 2020 and 2023 and an
aggravated-robbery warrant from 2018. [@1] His mother was found inside the
house, and police did not rule out her involvement in the criminal case because
the activity took place in her home. [@1] Rivera Paniura was taken to the San
Juan de Lurigancho criminal-investigation division, where police seized the
marijuana and opened an investigation to identify other members of the
distribution network. [@1]
