## Raids and seized precursor

Chinese authorities said they arrested 14 suspects, dismantled two drug-making sites and seized 478 kilograms of MDMB-INACA in February 2026. [@1,2] The Ministry of Public Security described MDMB-INACA as a non-scheduled precursor for synthetic cannabinoids. [@1,2] It also reported 1.15 tonnes of other drug-making materials, CNY 699,000 in cash and a further CNY 1.21 million in frozen funds. [@1,2] The Ministry said all 14 suspects were under criminal coercive measures for alleged illegal production, sale and transport of precursor materials. [@1,2]

## What the sources leave open

Neither release identifies the province or locations of the two sites, or says where the precursor was intended to go. [@1,2] The sources do not list the other chemicals seized or say whether any finished synthetic cannabinoids were recovered. [@1,2] They report no later charges or court outcomes. [@1,2] Xinhua and Beijing Daily relay the same Ministry announcement rather than independent accounts. [@1,2]
