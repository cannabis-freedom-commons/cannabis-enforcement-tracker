Police detained two 44-year-old men on the Yerevan–Ashtarak highway at about
20:50 on 26 May and took them with their Toyota Corolla to the Davtashen police
department. [@1] On 27 May, public criminal prosecution was initiated against
both men within an opened criminal case. [@1] They were placed under arrest and
transferred to a pretrial detention facility, while the preliminary
investigation continues to establish the circumstances. [@1]

During the search, officers found a plastic bag containing about 5 kg of a
substance believed to be marijuana on one of the detainees. [@1] Officers also
found a parcel containing a similar substance on the other detainee. [@1] Police
said officers of the Criminal Police anti-drug-trafficking department had
received operative information about a planned contraband import of a large drug
consignment into Armenia. [@1]

The account was published through the press service of Armenia's Interior
Ministry. [@1] It carries the standard notice that a suspect is presumed
innocent until guilt is proved by a final court judgment under Armenia's
criminal-procedure rules. [@1]
