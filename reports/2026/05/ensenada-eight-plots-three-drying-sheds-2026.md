Soldiers found and destroyed eight marijuana plantations in two rural areas of
Ensenada municipality, in Baja California, on 28 May 2026. [@1] The army said
its patrols and ground reconnaissance produced two operations that day. [@1] No
arrests were reported. [@1]

In the first operation, at Valle de la Trinidad, the soldiers destroyed four
plantations covering about 18,300 square metres. [@1] Proceso described Valle de
la Trinidad as a settlement 123 kilometres from the port of Ensenada and noted
recent clashes and organised-crime activity there. [@3] In the second operation,
in Cañada El Carrizo in the San Vicente district, the soldiers destroyed another
four plantations covering 2,575 square metres. [@1] Proceso placed Cañada El
Carrizo in the San Vicente district, in mountainous terrain that is hard to
reach. [@3] The same site held three drying sheds with about 1,200 marijuana
plants. [@1] The plantations were on about 20,875 square metres in total,
roughly 2.09 hectares. [@2]

The plantations and drying sheds were burned on the spot. [@1] The Defence
Secretariat said the soldiers acted under Mexico's national public security
strategy and within the rule of law. [@1] The results were announced by the
commands of the II Military Region and the 2nd Military Zone. [@1] Proceso
reported the findings as part of standing operations against drug production and
distribution in the region. [@3]
