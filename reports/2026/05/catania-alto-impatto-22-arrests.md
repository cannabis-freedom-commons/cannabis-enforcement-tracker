Catania's Mobile Squad, in the Servizio Centrale Operativo-coordinated "Alto impatto" drive
against street dealing and youth deviance, stepped up checks at young people's gathering places
and searched homes and people on the street across the San Cristoforo, San Giorgio, Barriera,
Picanello and San Giovanni Galermo districts. [@1] Officers also struck several indoor and
outdoor dealing spots in lightning actions that netted a string of pushers. [@1]

In total 22 people, two of them minors, were arrested for dealing or possession with intent to
deal; the report stresses they are presumed innocent until a final conviction. [@1] Police seized
about 10 kilograms of cocaine, 20 of hashish, 13 of marijuana and about 150 grams of crack,
ecstasy and wax between them. [@1] A further minor was arrested for repeatedly breaching a
robbery-related supervision order by leaving his community, and an adult and a minor found on a
stolen scooter were reported for receiving stolen goods. [@1]
