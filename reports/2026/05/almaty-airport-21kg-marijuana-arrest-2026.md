On 30 May 2026 Kazakhstan's National Security Committee, known as the KNB,
working jointly with police, customs and the Central Asia Regional Information
and Coordination Centre, stopped an attempted transfer of narcotic drugs through
Almaty airport. [@3] During a baggage check officers examined the luggage of a
foreign woman who had arrived from one of the countries of Southeast Asia. [@1]
In that luggage they found about 21 kilograms of plant material resembling
marijuana. [@2]

Officers seized the prohibited load and detained the suspected woman. [@1]
Police opened a pre-trial investigation under part 1 of article 286 of the
Criminal Code, as the committee's press service reported. [@2] That provision
covers smuggling of items withdrawn from circulation or whose circulation is
restricted. [@4]

The committee said no other information could be disclosed under article 201 of
the Criminal Procedure Code. [@2] According to the committee, officers carried
out operational measures at the city airport on 30 May as part of the joint
effort. [@2]
