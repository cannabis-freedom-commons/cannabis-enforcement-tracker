Gendarmes from the Clermont-l'Hérault brigade entered a house in the centre of
Lacoste on the evening of Friday 22 May 2026 after being alerted by the mayor,
and found an indoor cannabis plantation inside. [@1] Residents of Lacoste, a
village of barely 300 inhabitants above Clermont-l'Hérault, had noticed an
unusually persistent acrid smell over place Bellevue that day, prompting Mayor
Marc Carayon to alert law enforcement. [@1] Three people were arrested over the
operation after gendarmes intervened following several reports about the smell.
[@3]

Inside, officers found 90 cannabis plants spread across several rooms of the
dwelling converted into a genuine greenhouse. [@3] The installation used
horticultural lamps, air extractors, an irrigation system and makeshift
electrical fittings, according to the gendarmerie. [@2] Officers also seized two
kilograms of cannabis heads ready for consumption, along with several mobile
phones, a handgun and all the production equipment. [@2] On social media the
Hérault gendarmerie described the rooms as a genuine indoor greenhouse with
spa-like irrigation and makeshift wiring. [@4]

The production site was in the village of Lacoste, home to 300 inhabitants,
north of Clermont-l'Hérault. [@3] The investigation was continuing to establish
each person's responsibility and the possible scale of any trafficking. [@2]
