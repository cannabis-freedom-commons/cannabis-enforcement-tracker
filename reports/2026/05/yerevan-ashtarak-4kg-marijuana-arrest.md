On 18 May 2026, officers of the Criminal Police Main Department's
anti-drug-trafficking unit and Kotayk-region patrol officers detained two young
men on the Yerevan-Ashtarak road during joint operative-search measures. [@2]
Police announced on 19 May that the pair had been detained the previous day on
reasonable suspicion of acquiring, possessing and selling drugs. [@1] According
to the agency, one detainee was an 18-year-old resident of Yerevan and the other
was a 20-year-old resident of Vayots Dzor Province. [@1] Officers took both
young men to the Mashtots department of the community police. [@1]

A personal search of the 20-year-old young man found about 4 kg of a substance
resembling marijuana. [@1] Police described the seized material as 4 kilograms
of marijuana-type drug-like mass discovered during that personal search. [@2]

The young men and the compiled documents were presented to the
preliminary-investigation body. [@2] A preliminary investigation was under way.
[@1] The joint operation combined central criminal-police drug investigators
with Kotayk regional patrol officers. [@2]
