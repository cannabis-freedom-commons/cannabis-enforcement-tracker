Arkansas State Police arrested three people in a pair of traffic stops on
Interstate 40. [@1] Both traffic stops occurred in Pope County on Sept. 23. [@1]
Darnell Keith Russell Reed, 39, and Ashley Renee Carter, 36, both of Waynesboro,
Va., were arrested in the first vehicle and taken to the Pope County Detention
Center. [@1] Reed and Carter face charges of possession of drug paraphernalia
and possession of a controlled substance with the purpose to deliver, and Reed
was also found driving on a suspended license. [@1] Jermaine Bowers, 35, was
arrested in the second vehicle and taken to the Pope County Detention Center to
face a charge of possession of a controlled substance with purpose to deliver.
[@1]

Around 2:15 p.m., troopers stopped a Nissan Rogue traveling eastbound on I-40
near mile marker 72 for traffic violations. [@2] A search of the Rogue found
suitcases containing 30 individually packaged bundles of marijuana weighing
about 30 pounds, and troopers seized $4,510 in cash. [@1] Troopers say the
$4,510 was seized from Carter. [@2] About two hours later, troopers stopped a
Ford Bronco Sport near mile marker 77 and found 29 vacuum-sealed packages of
marijuana weighing about 29 pounds along with 100 THC vape cartridges. [@1] In
total, troopers report seizing approximately 59 pounds of marijuana, 200 grams
of THC vape products and $4,510 in cash. [@2]

The stops occurred less than two hours apart. [@2] The second stop happened
around 4:15 p.m. near mile marker 77. [@2] Police say the investigation is
ongoing and no additional information is being released. [@2]
