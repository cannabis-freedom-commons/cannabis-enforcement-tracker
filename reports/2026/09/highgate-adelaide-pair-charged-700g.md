South Australia police arrested a 30-year-old man and a 23-year-old woman after
searching a home at Highgate, Adelaide, on 25 September 2026. [@2] Police
charged the pair with two counts each of trafficking a commercial quantity and
trafficking a large commercial quantity of a controlled drug, plus a further
trafficking count. [@2] Both were refused police bail and were listed to appear
before the Adelaide Magistrates Court on Monday 28 September. [@3]

Officers allegedly found 700 grams of cannabis at the property, alongside about
3 kilograms of cocaine, 871 grams of ketamine and 385 grams of MDMA. [@2]
Investigators also allegedly found $72,595 in cash at the home. [@2] Police also
seized a Renault van, a Suzuki SUV and a large amount of jewellery and watches
in connection with the alleged offending. [@2]

The search was carried out by the Serious and Organised Crime Branch as part of
an investigation into alleged drug trafficking. [@3] Police seized the vehicles,
jewellery and watches under South Australia's Criminal Assets Confiscations Act
2005. [@2] An engaged Adelaide couple were separately reported to have had a
court win after police allegedly seized cocaine, ketamine, MDMA, cannabis, cash
and luxury assets from their home. [@1]
