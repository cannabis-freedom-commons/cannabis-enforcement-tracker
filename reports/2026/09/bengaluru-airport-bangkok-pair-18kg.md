Two passengers who arrived from Bangkok at Kempegowda International Airport were arrested for concealing hydroponic ganja valued at Rs 6.33 crore, officials said on Monday. [@1] The first passenger was intercepted after 3.12 kg of hydroponic ganja valued at Rs 1.09 crore was found concealed in checked-in baggage. [@1]

In the second instance, 14.97 kg of hydroponic ganja valued at Rs 5.24 crore, also concealed in checked-in baggage, was seized. [@1] Both accused were arrested under the Customs Act, 1962. [@1] Bengaluru Customs also shared details of further recent ganja and gold seizures on social media, as part of a broader crackdown at the airport. [@1]
