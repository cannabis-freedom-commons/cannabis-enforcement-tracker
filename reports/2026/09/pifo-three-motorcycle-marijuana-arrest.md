Ecuador's Armed Forces, acting through the Ecuadorian Army, apprehended three
citizens in the Pifo sector of Quito's metropolitan district during a military
Control of Arms, Munitions and Explosives (CAMEX) operation. [@1] Pifo is part
of Quito's metropolitan district, and the intervention was carried out as a
CAMEX registry and control operation. [@1] Uniformed officers intercepted the
three individuals while they were travelling together aboard the same motorcycle
during registry and control work in the zone. [@1] Authorities had provided no
official information on the identities or ages of the three detainees. [@1]

Inspection of the occupants and the motorcycle found two bags containing a green
substance presumed to be marijuana. [@1] Officers also found an additional bag
containing a whitish substance with characteristics similar to cocaine alongside
the suspected marijuana. [@1] Officers seized two mobile phones and a wallet
containing 20.15 dollars in cash from the group in addition to the suspected
drugs. [@1]

The three detainees and all secured evidence were placed at the disposal of the
competent judicial authorities for the corresponding legal procedure. [@1]
