## The interception

The Civil Guard spotted a high-powered speedboat around 13 nautical miles off the
Costa del Sol in the early hours of Thursday 10 September 2026 and tracked it to a
Marbella beach, where its cargo was being loaded into a van. [@1] Officers moved in
and found 26 bales of hashish in the van, each weighing around 40 kg, a haul of
1,124 kg in all. [@1,2] Two men were arrested with the van, and the speedboat's
skipper was later arrested in Fuengirola. [@1]

## The charges

All three are suspected of offences against public health, the legal classification
Spain applies to drug trafficking, as well as document forgery offences. [@1] The
Civil Guard announced the seizure on 10 September and the arrests the same day.
