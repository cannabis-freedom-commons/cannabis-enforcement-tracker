Police in Bihar's Vaishali district seized 38.6 kg of marijuana from five bags aboard a bus travelling from Jaynagar to Patna, arresting five people including three minors aged 13 to 16. [@1]

Acting on a tip-off, a joint team of Bhagwanpur Police and the Patna Prohibition Department set up a checkpoint at Bhagwanpur Adda Chowk and stopped the Patna-bound bus. [@1] Officers searching suspicious passengers found packets of marijuana concealed inside the school bags carried by the minors. [@1]

The five suspects were taken into custody under Operation Neelkanth. [@1] Police said the group was travelling to Patna to supply the drugs, legal proceedings were started against all the accused, and investigators were tracing where the consignment came from and who was to receive it in Patna.
