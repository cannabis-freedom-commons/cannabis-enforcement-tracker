On Saturday night, a Scorpio that had been heading from Itahari toward Biratnagar on the Koshi Highway approached a routine checkpoint set up by Bargachhi Police Post. [@1] On spotting the checkpoint around 10 pm, the driver made a U-turn and fled. [@1]

While fleeing, the Scorpio collided with an oncoming car near the Oil Corporation area in Biratnagar Metropolitan City-3. [@1] The Scorpio's driver then fled the scene on foot. [@1]

When police searched the crashed vehicle, they found 13 sacks of marijuana hidden inside, weighing 398 kg in total. [@1] Morang Police spokesperson DSP Mahendra Kumar Mishra confirmed the seizure and said a search is under way for the fleeing driver. [@1] Both vehicles are now in police custody. [@1]

A photograph published with the report shows officers unloading black sacks from the back of the Scorpio at night. [@1]
