Three men ran from police officers on beat patrol in Ba who were about to
question them after watching them act suspiciously. [@1] During the foot chase a
plastic bag holding several sachets of dried leaves believed to be marijuana
fell from one of the suspects. [@1] Police said they know who the three men are
and investigations into the Ba incident were continuing. [@2] No one was
reported arrested in the Ba chase, and the weight of the suspected marijuana was
not given. [@1]

Assistant Commissioner of Police Operations Kasiano Vusonilawe said arrests for
unlawful cultivation of illicit drugs were made in Savusavu and Taveuni over the
past week, with no plant or area totals published. [@1] Arrests for unlawful
possession of illicit drugs were recorded in Fiji's four other police divisions,
and the number of people held in each was not stated. [@2] Frontline officers
and a joint task force supported by the Republic of Fiji Military Forces in Nadi
and Lautoka carried out the arrests. [@1] Cash believed to be proceeds of crime
was seized during the operations, but its amount and currency were not
disclosed. [@2]

Vusonilawe said the operations target both green and white drugs, local
shorthand for marijuana and harder drugs, and that some of those arrested were
repeat offenders. [@1] The Fiji Detector Dog Unit and other law enforcement
partners were being deployed to strengthen the effort against the illicit drug
trade. [@2] Police urged the public to report suspicious or drug-related
activity by calling Crime Stoppers on 1681. [@2]
