Agents of Costa Rica's Drug Control Police (PCD) arrested a 57-year-old employee
of the Montes de Oro municipal government at his home in Miramar on suspicion of
selling drugs. [@1] Police identified him only by the surname Salas. [@2] He
worked as a garbage collector. [@4] According to the investigation, he allegedly
used his rounds through different communities. [@4] The raid took place in the
early hours of Wednesday 16 September. [@7] Officers carried it out with support
from the Fuerza Pública, the uniformed police force. [@6]

Inside the house, officers found doses of marijuana and crack, according to the
police report. [@1] They seized the doses, cash and utensils used to prepare and
measure the drugs. [@1] Repretel reported that officers also found cocaine. [@4]
The reports gave no weight for the marijuana or the crack, and no amount for the
cash. [@1]

Salas was placed at the disposal of the Puntarenas prosecutor's office, which
will continue the case and decide what to request from the courts. [@1] The
anti-narcotics prosecutor asked for preventive detention. [@3] The Puntarenas
criminal court instead released him under precautionary measures. [@3] The court
required him to live at a verified alternative address in Montes de Oro. [@3] It
barred him from leaving the country and ordered him to sign at court every 15
days. [@3] He must also stay away from the raided house. [@3] The court noted
that he had held a stable job for 14 years and that drug sales at his workplace
had not been established. [@3] It also noted that a health condition requires
daily treatment at a medical centre. [@3] He remained a suspect while the public
prosecutor's office advanced the case. [@1]

The Ministry of Public Security's press office said drug sales carry eight to 15
years in prison. [@5] The PCD is the specialised police body of the Ministry of
Public Security for investigating local and international drug trafficking. [@1]
