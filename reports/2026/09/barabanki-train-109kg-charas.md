During a joint operation by the Mandal Reserve, Lucknow, and the Crime
Intelligence Branch, Lucknow, railway officials recovered 10.9 kg of charas
from an unclaimed bag in the general coach of train number 19038 at Barabanki
on Monday. [@1] The train was heading to Mumbai from Bihar, and the
contraband was found in general coach number 255333. [@1]

Officials estimated the international market value of the charas at Rs 5.45
crore. [@1] The seized contraband was handed over to the Government Railway
Police at Barabanki for further legal action, and no owner was identified.
