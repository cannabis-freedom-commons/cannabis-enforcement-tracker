Police detained a 35-year-old man on 9 September 2026 in a flat at house 4 on the embankment of the
Dudergofsky canal in St Petersburg's Krasnoselsky district. [@2] The operation was carried out with
the support of Rosgvardia. [@1,2]

During the search officers seized 43 cannabis plants and 14 packets of ready-to-sell plant mixture.
[@1,2] The official statement put the weight at more than 1.2 kilograms; Fontanka reported almost
1.5 kilograms. [@1,2]

A criminal case was opened for attempted production and sale of drugs in an especially large amount.
[@1,2]
