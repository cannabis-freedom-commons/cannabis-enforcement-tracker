The Basra Criminal Court sentenced a foreign national to death after convicting him of drug trafficking. [@1,2,3]

Authorities seized 10 kilograms and 50 grams of hashish from the convicted man, which the court said was intended for trafficking and distribution. [@1,2,3] The judgment was reported by Iraq's Supreme Judicial Council. [@1,2]

The death sentence was issued under Article 27(First) of Iraq's Narcotics and Psychotropic Substances Law No. 50 of 2017. [@2,4] That provision carries death or life imprisonment for offences including importing, exporting or transporting narcotics with the intent to traffic. [@2] The council disclosed no name, nationality or arrest date for the defendant. [@2]
