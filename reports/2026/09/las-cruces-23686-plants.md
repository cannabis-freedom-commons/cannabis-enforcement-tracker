Joint Guatemalan security forces located and eradicated 23,686 marijuana plants
in the municipality of Las Cruces, Petén, on 13 September 2026. [@1] Officers of
the National Civil Police, the Army and the Public Prosecutor's Office carried
out the operation in mountainous areas, uprooting and burning the plants. [@1]
The anti-narcotics police unit SGAIA, in coordination with the Public
Prosecutor's Office, located the crop in a mountainous area beside Las Cruces.
[@2]

Police valued the destroyed crop at more than 11 million quetzales (1.4 million
dollars). [@1] The detailed police valuation was about Q11,843,000. [@2] The
Interior Ministry said operations would continue in different parts of the
country, especially in Petén, where areas used for drug cultivation have
historically been identified. [@1]

Anti-narcotics units said detecting and eradicating plantations is a key
strategy to weaken the finances of organisations involved in drug production and
distribution. [@1] Petén is Guatemala's largest department and shares an
extensive, porous border with Mexico, which the account describes as making it a
critical point for drug trafficking. [@1] Analysts cited in the account said the
plants were not for local consumption but were to be processed and eventually
moved as derivatives to the United States. [@1]
