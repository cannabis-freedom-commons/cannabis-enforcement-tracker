Police in Pointe-à-Pitre, Guadeloupe, ran two security operations on 28 and 29
September 2026, one in the Mortenol sector and one at the Péricarpin and Cipolin
housing estates, and arrested two people. [@1]

On Monday 28 September, 25 officers, three of them dog handlers brought from
Martinique, checked 12 people and 4 vehicles in Mortenol and arrested one person
for possession of drugs. [@1] On Tuesday 29 September, the Departmental
Intervention Company (CDI), the Anti-Crime Brigade (BAC) and three dog teams
checked 18 people and one vehicle at the Péricarpin and Cipolin estates and
arrested one person for transporting drugs. [@1] Officers found about fifty
sachets containing cocaine and cannabis herb during the second operation. [@1]
The second operation was run under the authority of the prefect of Guadeloupe
and on the requisition of the public prosecutor, as part of the departmental
plan to restore everyday security (PADRSQ). [@1]

ETV reported that the two operations led to two arrests and to the seizure of
about fifty sachets of cocaine and cannabis. [@2]
