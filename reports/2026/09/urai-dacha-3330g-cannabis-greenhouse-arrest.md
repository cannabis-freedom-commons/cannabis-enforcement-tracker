Criminal investigation officers in Urai, in Yugra, detained a 44-year-old local
man suspected of growing and processing drug-containing plants. [@1] Police
reported that the total mass of the raw material seized from him exceeded 3,330
grams. [@1]

A search of the dacha plot turned up four cannabis bushes, two cardboard boxes
of stems, a plastic bottle and two glass jars of crushed leaves. [@1] The man
grew the plants in a greenhouse and kept them on the plot, in the attic and in
the garage. [@1] At his home officers seized a glass jar of dark green liquid
from the fridge and a metal pan in which drugs were thought to have been made.
[@1]

Police said the man told them he made the plant-based drug for his own use and
had a previous criminal record. [@1] A criminal case was opened under part 2 of
article 228 of the Russian Criminal Code, which carries three to ten years in
prison. [@1] He was placed under a written undertaking not to leave the area.
[@1]
