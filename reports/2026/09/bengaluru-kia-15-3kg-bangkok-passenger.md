Customs officers at Kempegowda International Airport (KIA) in Bengaluru seized 80.1 kg of hydroponic ganja worth Rs28.01 crore from seven passengers in five cases, Deccan Herald reported on 22 September. [@1]

This report covers one of those cases. [@1] On 19 September, customs intercepted a passenger arriving from Bangkok and seized 15.3 kg of hydroponic ganja worth Rs5.35 crore. [@1] The passenger was arrested under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act. [@1]

The same roundup lists a 26.8 kg seizure from two more Bangkok passengers on the same day and a 36 kg seizure from another Bangkok pair. [@1] It also lists a 2 kg seizure from a Vietnam arrival and gold seizures from Kuala Lumpur and Abu Dhabi arrivals, which are not cannabis and are left out here. [@1]
