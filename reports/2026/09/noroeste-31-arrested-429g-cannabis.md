Agents of the Dominican drug-control agency DNCD, with the Public Ministry and
the National Police, detained 31 people in simultaneous microtrafficking
operations across Valverde, Montecristi, Dajabón and Santiago Rodríguez in the
Cibao Noroeste region. [@1] The sweep comprised more than 300 interventions and
five searches targeting neighbourhoods identified as drug sale and distribution
points. [@1] Those detained and the seized objects were handed to the Public
Ministry for judicial proceedings and coercion-measure requests. [@1]

Officers seized 2,046.20 grams of controlled substances in total, reported as
1,609.90 grams of suspected cocaine, 429.30 grams of marijuana and seven doses
of crack. [@1] In Valverde, eight people were detained in 62 operations and two
searches, with 855.10 grams seized, including 823.20 grams of suspected cocaine
and 31.90 grams of marijuana. [@2] In Montecristi, six people were detained in
121 operations and one search, with 552.90 grams seized, including 357.20 grams
of suspected cocaine and 195.70 grams of marijuana. [@2] In Dajabón, eight
people were detained with 334.10 grams seized, including 224.90 grams of
suspected cocaine and 109.20 grams of marijuana. [@2] In Santiago Rodríguez,
nine people were detained in 135 operations and two searches with 304.10 grams
seized, including 204.60 grams of suspected cocaine, 92.50 grams of marijuana
and seven crack doses. [@2]

Beyond drugs, authorities took RD$18,090 in cash, nine mobile phones, three
motorcycles, two weighing scales and a bulletproof vest across the four
provinces. [@2] The DNCD said the joint actions, through its Joint Task Force
with police and prosecutors, will continue uninterrupted against trafficking for
domestic consumption across the country. [@2]
