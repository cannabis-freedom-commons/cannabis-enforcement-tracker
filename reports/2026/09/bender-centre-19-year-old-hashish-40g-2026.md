Police detained a 19-year-old Bender resident in the city centre carrying a
10-gram packet of a banned plant substance believed to be hashish. [@1] Seeing
the officers, he threw away a blue plastic bag holding the packet. [@3] A search
of his room found three more similar packets, about 10 grams each, inside a
headphone box. [@3] The detainee said he had found all four packets several days
earlier beside the railway tracks. [@1] The state broadcaster's account likewise
says he was detained with a 10-gram packet of the banned substance and that
three similar packets were found at his home. [@2]

The seized material was sent for biological examination. [@1] If the examination
confirms the packets hold a drug, he could face criminal liability under Article
226 of Transnistria's Criminal Code, a fine of up to 21,000 Transnistrian
roubles or up to three years in prison. [@1]

The Interior Ministry press service reported the detention from Bender on 8
September. [@1]
