Agents of the Dirección Nacional de Control de Drogas, the National Police and
the Public Ministry detained 40 people during raids and searches carried out
over the previous 24 hours in Santo Domingo Este, Santo Domingo Oeste and Santo
Domingo Norte. [@1] In Santo Domingo Este officers carried out 70 operations and
arrested 30 people, including Jaime Luis De la Rosa López, Laury Jiménez, Romer
Calderón Rosado, Albert Luis Ventura Mota and Michael Yoldan De Óleo Agramonte.
[@1] In Santo Domingo Oeste officers executed six searches and arrested Baldes
Mallen Méndez, Joselito George and Rodolfo Algenis Castillo Ortiz. [@1] In Santo
Domingo Norte officers carried out 45 operations and two searches and detained
seven people. [@1] The 40 detained people were placed at the disposal of the
Public Ministry in the corresponding jurisdictions for the legal proceedings
determined by the authorities. [@1]

The DNCD reported a total of 19,674.4 grams of drugs seized, of which 1,184.5
grams was marijuana: 555.9 grams in Santo Domingo Este, 20.4 grams in Santo
Domingo Oeste and 608.2 grams in Santo Domingo Norte. [@1] Across the three
areas officers also held 11 precision scales, three radios, seven cell phones,
five video-surveillance cameras and cash, including RD$15,225 in Santo Domingo
Norte. [@1]

The DNCD said the operations were part of the Joint Task Force strategy against
the sale and distribution of narcotics, with the DNCD, the National Police and
the Public Ministry acting together. [@1] The seized substances were packed and
sent under chain of custody to the National Institute of Forensic Sciences for
analysis. [@1]
