Officers stopped three vehicles travelling between Uzbek regions and found drugs
on the men inside, in three separate cases reported by the State Security
Service. [@1]

In Toprakkala district, Khorezm region, security service, interior ministry and
customs officers stopped a Lacetti car on the Surkhandarya–Khorezm route and
found 490 g of opium with a passenger from Angor district born in 1960, who had
twice served sentences for drug offences. [@1] Investigators said the opium had
been smuggled in from a neighbouring state and was meant for sale in Khorezm
region, the security service said. [@1]

In the second case, a check of a passenger in an Onix car on the
Tashkent–Khorezm route found 119 g of hashish; the man was born in 1968 and
lives in Urgench. [@1] The third case was in Akaltyn district, Syrdarya region,
where security service officers for Jizzakh region and regional interior
ministry officers stopped a Lacetti on the Syrdarya–Jizzakh route. [@1] The
driver, a Jizzakh resident born in 1974 previously convicted of a drug offence,
had 16 g of opium hidden in his shoe, which officers seized. [@1]

Criminal cases were opened against all three detained men and investigative
measures were under way, the security service said. [@1] The driver, a Jizzakh
resident born in 1974 previously convicted of a drug offence, had 16 g of opium
hidden in his shoe, which officers seized. [@2]
