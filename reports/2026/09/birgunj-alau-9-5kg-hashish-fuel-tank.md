The Armed Police Force arrested an Indian national with about 9.5 kg of hashish from Alau in Birgunj Metropolitan City-17, Parsa. [@1] OnlineKhabar published the account on 16 September. [@1]

The arrested man was identified as 20-year-old Sandeep Chaudhary of India, held by a team from the APF Nepal Border Security Gulma in Alau. [@1] According to the APF, he was carrying the hashish on a motorcycle concealed in a false compartment inside the fuel tank. [@1]

Security personnel recovered about 9.5 kg of hashish packed in 21 packets from the tank. [@1] Chaudhary, with the hashish and the motorcycle, was handed over to the concerned authorities for further legal action. [@1]
