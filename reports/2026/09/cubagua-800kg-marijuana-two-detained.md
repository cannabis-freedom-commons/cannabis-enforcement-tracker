Officers of the Bolivarian National Police (CPNB), attached to its Criminal
Penal Intelligence Directorate (DIP) in Nueva Esparta state, carried out an
operational deployment on the island of Cubagua that ended with two people
detained. [@1] Police commissions deployed around the perimeter spotted the two
people at the moment they tried to evade the authorities by hiding in the
island's scrub vegetation. [@1]

During a search of the terrain the officers seized more than 800 kilograms of
presumed marijuana on the island. [@1] The drugs were packed in 1,529 wrappers
found inside various packages concealed among bushes. [@1] The officers also
retained logistical and maritime-navigation material linked to the case,
including three global positioning devices, mobile phones and a gasoline-powered
electricity generator. [@1]

According to an account attributed to Unión Radio, the security operation
followed forensic analysis of previously seized technological devices whose
digital contents yielded satellite coordinates. [@1] Those coordinates guided
the police commissions to an unpopulated strip of the insular territory where
the packages and the suspects were found. [@1]
