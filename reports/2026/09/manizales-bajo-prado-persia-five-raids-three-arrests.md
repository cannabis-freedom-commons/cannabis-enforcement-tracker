Police in Manizales arrested three men in flagrante during five searches in the
Bajo Prado and Persia neighbourhoods, as part of an investigation into drug
dealing. [@1] The Metropolitan Police of Manizales carried out the searches with
the Third Specialised Prosecutor's Office, after investigative work and
information from a human source. [@1]

Officers seized 256 grams of marijuana, along with 70 grams of cocaine, 45 grams
of bazuco and 15 ecstasy pills. [@1] Police also found three 12-gauge
cartridges, four digital scales, five mobile phones, packaging material and
296,000 pesos in cash. [@1] The National Police's own statement on the same
operations gives the same cannabis figure, 256 grams of marijuana seized in Bajo
Prado and Persia. [@2]

The three were placed at the disposal of the judicial authorities on suspicion
of trafficking, manufacturing or carrying drugs, and of illegal possession of
firearms and ammunition. [@1] Police said the three men, aged 44, 52 and 33,
together had more than nine entries in the SPOA, the criminal case-tracking
system, for offences including drug trafficking, conspiracy and domestic
violence, and noted that those entries are not convictions. [@1]

The National Police said 946 people had been arrested in Manizales for drug
trafficking so far in 2026. [@2]
