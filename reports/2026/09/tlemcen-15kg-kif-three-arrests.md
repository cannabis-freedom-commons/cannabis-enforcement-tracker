Police in Tlemcen province seized 15 kilograms of treated kif originating from Morocco and arrested three people. [@1,2]

The seizure and arrests were announced on Monday 14 September 2026 in a statement by the communication cell of the Sûreté de wilaya de Tlemcen, the provincial security service. [@1,2] Treated kif is processed cannabis resin. [@1,2]

No identities for the three arrested people and no charges or court outcome were published. [@1,2]
