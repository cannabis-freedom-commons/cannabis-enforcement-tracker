On 8 September 2026, France's National Police carried out a large
anti-delinquency operation in the Dillon neighbourhood of Fort-de-France,
Martinique. [@1] The police presented the action as part of the fight against
insecurity in Martinique. [@1] It mobilised officers of the national police's
territorial directorate (DTPN), its vehicle-pound team and two platoons of
gendarmes, who carried out several checks. [@1]

Officers seized 916 grammes of cannabis resin from a motorcyclist involved in
refusing to stop for police. [@1] Another young man was checked carrying a knife
and walkie-talkies, according to a National Police Facebook post. [@1] Three
motorbikes were also administratively immobilised during the operation. [@1]
