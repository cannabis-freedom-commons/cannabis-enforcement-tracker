Police in Iganga District arrested 31 suspects in an intelligence-led
operation against alleged criminal hideouts and black spots linked to drug
abuse and robbery. [@1] The operation covered Central Division, Northern
Division, Nakalama, Bunyiro and Buligo, targeting places where people were
suspected of gathering to smoke narcotics before robbing members of the
public. [@1]

According to Busoga East police spokesperson ASP Michael Kasadha, those held
comprised 30 men and one woman. [@1] Police recovered quantities of suspected
marijuana in rolled and unrolled form, as well as three pangas believed to be
dangerous weapons. [@1] Nineteen suspects were arrested around Ssaza Road and
Kaliro Road, three in Buligo where the pangas were also recovered, five in
Nakalama and four in Bunyiro. [@1]

The suspects are detained at Iganga Central Police Station, investigated for
alleged possession of narcotics and dangerous weapons while inquiries
continue. [@1] Kasadha said the operation is part of wider efforts to disrupt
criminal networks, and police credited tip-offs from the public. [@1]
