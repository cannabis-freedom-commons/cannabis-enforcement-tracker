Maranhão Military Police officers found and destroyed a plantation of about
1,200 cannabis plants in Povoado Leal, in the rural zone of Cândido Mendes, in
the interior of Maranhão. [@1] The area was located after the intelligence
service received information about drug cultivation in the region, according to
the Military Police. [@1]

During the operation officers found a hut used as shelter, and a man there was
ordered to stop but fled into vegetation and was not found. [@1] At the camp the
soldiers found hammocks, clothes, personal utensils and equipment used in the
plot, and they seized two shotguns. [@1]

The roughly 1,200 cannabis plants were pulled up and destroyed, the camp was
dismantled, and nobody had been arrested as of publication. [@1] The operation
was carried out by the Rural Area Survival Operations Command (Cosar) together
with the 31st Military Police Battalion, and police said investigations
continued to find the man who fled, identify those involved in the cultivation
and establish where the drug was to go. [@1]

Imirante reported that the operation was carried out on Friday 11 September,
with Cosar and the 31st Battalion still searching for the other people involved
in the plantation. [@2]

Imirante reported that the two seized shotguns, hammocks, clothes and
cultivation support equipment were collected and presented to the competent
authorities. [@2]
