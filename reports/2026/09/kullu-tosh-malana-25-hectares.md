Joint teams of the security forces and government departments destroyed large areas of illegally grown cannabis at Tosh and Malana in the Kasol forest range of Kullu district, Himachal Pradesh. [@1] Range officer Jog Raj Sharma of Kasol confirmed the action and said the eradication campaign would continue. [@1]

At Tosh, a special drive cleared about 10 hectares of forest land of illegally grown cannabis, uprooting close to 1.2 million plants. [@1] The operation was carried out jointly by the Narcotics Control Bureau (NCB), the Indo-Tibetan Border Police (ITBP) and the state task force (STF). [@1]

At Malana, teams ran an intensive drive over about 15 hectares of cannabis cultivation, completed jointly by the NCB, local police and forest staff. [@1] The NCB separately said its joint drive with the ITBP, Himachal Pradesh Police and the State Forest Department had destroyed more than 61 acres of illegal cannabis in remote high-altitude areas of Kullu, with every stage geo-tagged and documented. [@2]

The NCB said legal action would follow against those responsible for the illegal cultivation, and that the drive forms part of its Vision Document on Narcotics Control for 2026-29 aimed at stopping drugs at their source. [@2]
