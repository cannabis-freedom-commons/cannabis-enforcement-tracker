SGAIA anti-narcotics officers arrested 33-year-old Luis N at kilometre 243.5 in
Pajapita, San Marcos, after finding 21 bags of marijuana during the inspection
of a tractor-trailer. [@1] The driver was travelling in heavy-transport truck
C-582BQF when officers inspected it. [@4] The bags were hidden in the sleeping
compartment (camarote) of the truck. [@2]

The joint inspection took place in the jurisdiction of aldea Palmeras,
municipality of Pajapita, on 4 September. [@3] Inside the cab officers found two
cloth sacks holding 21 plastic bags of marijuana, identified by a field test.
[@3] Prosecutors detained the driver, seized the vehicle and packaged the
suspected drug for transfer. [@3] The operation was led by the drug-trafficking
prosecution office within the inter-institutional anti-narcotics centre CICNAG,
with SGAIA officers of the National Civil Police. [@3]

The same news roundup separately reported the arrest of 18-year-old Yeison N, an
alleged Mara 18 member, in colonia Maya, zone 18, after shots were allegedly
fired, with a pistol, a magazine and 10 rounds seized. [@1] It also reported
that 37-year-old Arturo N was detained in aldea Puerto Viejo, Iztapa, Escuintla,
on a homicide allegation, a separate non-cannabis case left out of this report.
[@1]
