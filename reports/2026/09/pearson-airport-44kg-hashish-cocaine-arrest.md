The Canada Border Services Agency seized suitcases packed with illicit
substances bound for Sri Lanka at Toronto Pearson International Airport. [@1]
The seizure took place on Sept. 3. [@1] The drugs were in a traveller's luggage
destined for Sri Lanka. [@2] One person was arrested and remanded to the Royal
Canadian Mounted Police. [@1]

Border officials seized a total of 44 kilograms of hashish and 13 kilograms of
cocaine. [@1] The arrested person was handed over to the RCMP with the seized
contraband. [@2]

A CBSA spokesperson said officers use a risk-management approach, intelligence,
detection tools and indicators of deception to decide whether a deeper
examination is required. [@1] No details about charges or the suspect were
immediately available. [@1]
