Officers of the Police Narcotics Bureau (PNB) airport unit seized a stock of
kush cannabis estimated to be worth about Rs320 million after it was found
inside luggage abandoned near baggage belt number 6 at Bandaranaike
International Airport (BIA) on the morning of 15 September 2026. [@1]

The narcotics had been packed into 60 airtight packets and concealed inside
two pieces of luggage. [@1]

Police suspect the luggage was brought into Sri Lanka by a passenger who
arrived at BIA the previous day on SriLankan Airlines flight UL 405 from
Bangkok, Thailand. [@1] The passenger is believed to have abandoned the two
bags and fled after fearing arrest at the airport. [@1] No arrest was
reported. [@1]

The seized stock was scheduled to be produced before the Negombo
Magistrate's Court the same day. [@1]
