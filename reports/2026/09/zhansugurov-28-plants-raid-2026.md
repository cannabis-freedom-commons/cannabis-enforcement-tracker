Police in Zhetysu Region stopped the illegal cultivation and storage of
drug-containing plants in a private house. [@1] Two natives of Aksu district
aged 60 and 64 were identified as suspects in the case. [@1] Both men are in
custody. [@1]

Officers seized 28 cannabis bushes described as weighing more than 5 kg. [@1]
They also seized 720 grams of freshly harvested marijuana and 762 grams of dried
marijuana. [@1] The search in a house in Zhansugurov found several rooms
equipped for growing plants. [@1] The rooms held pots with plants and a tent
with artificial lighting set up as a mini-greenhouse. [@1]

Aksu district police carried out the operation with the district KNB unit during
the Karasora-2026 prevention campaign. [@1] A pre-trial investigation was opened
into illegal cultivation of plants banned from cultivation that contain narcotic
substances. [@1] Kazinform reported that the search took place in a house in the
village of Zhansugurov. [@2] The police anti-drug committee said both suspects
remain in custody while the circumstances are being established. [@3]
