A TNI task force under Joint Regional Defense Command III found 1,347 cannabis plants in forest near Kampung Siminbuk, Serambakon District, Pegunungan Bintang Regency, Highland Papua, on 8 September 2026. [@1,2]

ANTARA reported that task-force personnel uprooted and secured the plants during the operation. [@1] The plants were found at cultivation sites in remote forest, and the authorities did not report an arrest at the location. [@1,2]

The evidence was handed to the Pegunungan Bintang Police Narcotics Unit for legal processing and further destruction. [@2] The reports leave the alleged ownership of the cultivation sites under investigation, so this report records the removal and handover without assigning responsibility to a named group. [@1,2]
