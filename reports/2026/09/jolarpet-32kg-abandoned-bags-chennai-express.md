The Railway Protection Force seized 32 kg of ganja from bags abandoned inside an express train at Jolarpet railway station on Thursday. [@1] The Hindu published the account on 18 September. [@1]

An RPF team was conducting surprise checks at the station as part of a special drive against illegal narcotics transport on trains. [@1] The team searched the Chennai-bound train halted on platform 2 and found two bags abandoned in its general compartment. [@1] On opening the bags, officers found bundles of ganja inside. [@1]

Initial inquiry found that three persons had abandoned the consignment and fled when they noticed the police team. [@1] The Jolarpet RPF registered a case and is analysing CCTV footage to trace the smuggling gang; an investigation is under way. [@1]
