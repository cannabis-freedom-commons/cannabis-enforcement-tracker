On Tuesday evening a team from Golakhark Police Post tried to check truck
number Na. 4 Kha 8996 at Bhanjyang in Ilam Municipality-9, and the driver fled,
leaving the vehicle behind. [@1,2] A search of the abandoned truck found about
1,504 kg and 610 grams of marijuana packed in 41 sacks. [@1,2]

Police say they are searching for the absconding driver and have opened an
investigation into the seizure. [@1,2] The reports name no arrests and give no
origin or destination for the consignment. [@1,2] The English page notes it was
automatically translated by AI and may contain errors. [@1]
