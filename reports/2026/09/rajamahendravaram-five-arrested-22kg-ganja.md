Police in Rajamahendravaram city, East Godavari district, arrested five men from
an inter-state gang on Saturday 5 September 2026. [@1] Officers seized 22
kilograms of ganja in the operation, which took place near a bar in the city.
[@1]

East Godavari Assistant Superintendent of Police Patil Devraj identified the
five accused: Sk. Sadhaq Ali of Rajamahendravaram, Rahat Ali of Uttar Pradesh,
Irfan and Sumit Kumar Misra of Haryana, and Sandeep Kumar of Bihar. [@1]

A case was registered against them under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic
Substances Act, 1985. [@1]
