A Papeete court sentenced a couple who produced cannabis on Huahine to three years in prison, including two years suspended with probation, on 22 September 2026. [@1] The court also imposed a 2.5 million CFP fine, and the one-year custodial portion could be adjusted. [@1]

Three couples appeared for trial, while a seventh defendant was absent and subject to an arrest warrant. [@1] Gendarmes had arrested the suspects on 21 April after an investigation involving telephone surveillance. [@1] Investigators seized 2 kg of cannabis, nearly 4.5 million CFP in cash and a vehicle. [@1]

The family grew cannabis on a small island at Huahine and sent it to Tahiti by air in cake boxes. [@1] The article also describes a couple distributing the cannabis and a reseller couple in Tahiti. [@1] A couple responsible for transactions received two years suspended, including 18 months with probation. [@1] The court ordered confiscation of the seized items apart from the car. [@1]
