A joint drive by Narcotics Intelligence Bureau-CID and Railway Protection Force personnel seized 54 kg of dry ganja from two north Indian passengers travelling on the Chennai Egmore–Guruvayur Express at Tirunelveli Railway Junction on Saturday night. [@1]

The accused were identified as 44-year-old M. Sardar Biswas of Kaizpara in West Bengal and 46-year-old K. Urmila Sahoo of Odisha. [@1] A team led by NIB-CID Inspector Karthikai Selvi with RPF Sub-Inspector M. Siva Raja checked an air-conditioned coach after the train arrived at Tirunelveli at about 10 p.m. [@1]

The team found the 54 kg of dry ganja concealed inside three trolley bags. [@1] The contraband was handed over to NIB-CID sleuths for further enquiry. [@1]
