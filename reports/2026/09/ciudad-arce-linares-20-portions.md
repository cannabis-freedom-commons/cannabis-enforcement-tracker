Soldiers of the Armed Forces of El Salvador located and detained Alexis Wilfredo
Linares Anaya in the Ciudad Arce district of La Libertad Centro. [@1] The
detention was announced by Defence Minister René Francis Merino Monroy, who said
soldiers seized 20 portions of marijuana and an amount of cash from him. [@1]
Linares Anaya was handed over to the National Civil Police so that he would
answer before the courts. [@1]

Merino Monroy said drug dealing in small amounts would not be tolerated, using
the word narcomenudeo, and that drugs have no place in communities. [@1]

A Diario El Salvador account confirms that soldiers found Linares Anaya in
Ciudad Arce and seized 20 portions of marijuana and cash, and that he was handed
to the National Civil Police. [@2]

That account adds that he was to answer before the courts for the facts
attributed to him. [@2]
