A three-day operation by the Clackamas County Sheriff's Office Interagency Task Force and the Oregon State Police Drug Enforcement Section ended with the destruction of 110,437 cannabis plants and the seizure of 81,460 pounds (about 36.9 tonnes) of processed marijuana, the agencies announced on 11 September 2026. [@1,2] They served search warrants at six properties in Clackamas and Marion counties and seized 86 firearms and $11,500 in cash. [@1,2] The state police estimated the cannabis's value at $20 million. [@1,2]

## Workers at the sites

Officers found 55 people living and working at the sites without adequate shelter, running water, plumbing or electricity. [@1,2] Victim advocates and service providers offered food, water, emergency shelter, safety planning and legal referrals. [@1] Investigators were trying to establish whether the workers had come through force, fraud or coercion. [@1,2]

Jaimee Virrueta-Gomez, 35, of Gerber, California, was arrested on suspicion of unlawful manufacture and unlawful possession of a marijuana item. [@1,2] The state police said more charges would be referred to district attorneys in both counties. [@1] County and state agencies also investigated possible environmental crimes involving water use, pesticide and fertiliser runoff, and unlawful solid waste disposal. [@1,2]
