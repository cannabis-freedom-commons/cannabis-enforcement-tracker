Excise Department officials seized 292 ganja plants weighing 213 kg grown inside the Koli Hatti Forest area at Dodda Sampige Reserve Forest in the BRT Tiger Reserve on 26 September. [@1] Acting on a tip-off, excise and Forest Department teams conducted a joint operation and registered cases against five people who had grown the plants illegally. [@1]

The Excise Department registered a case under the NDPS Act, while the Forest Department registered a case under the Wildlife (Protection) Act of 1972 against five residents of Nalli Katri Podu in Hanur taluk, aged from 35 to 70. [@1] The operation was guided by BRT Tiger Reserve Deputy Conservator of Forests and Director B.S. Sripathi, with Excise Deputy Superintendent of Police K.Y. Vijayakumar, Byluru Range Forest Officer D. Devaraju and Kollegal Excise Inspector C.M. Dayanand among the team. [@1]
