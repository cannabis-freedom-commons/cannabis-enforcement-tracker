Costa Rica's Drug Control Police detained 13 people, including two minors, in
Liberia, Guanacaste, during an operation against retail drug-selling structures.
[@1] Most of those detained had judicial or police records for various acts.
[@1] A woman surnamed Ruiz was among those detained and wore an electronic ankle
monitor under a prison benefit linked to a 2023 PCD drug-sale case. [@1] The two
minors were not identified. [@1] The 13 detainees were placed at the disposal of
the competent authorities to determine their legal situation. [@1]

Officers carried out 10 searches in El Capulín and one more in Corazón de Jesús.
[@2] The operation took place in the early hours of Tuesday as part of Plan
Escudo, which targets sectors identified as police-interest points for drug
sales. [@2] In the house searches officers seized 116 wrappers and 80 grams of
marijuana, alongside 130 doses of crack and 10 doses plus 15 grams of cocaine.
[@1] Officers also seized 135,000 colones and 60 US dollars in cash and a
handmade firearm. [@1] Officers also found utensils that police said were used
to prepare and dose drugs before sale. [@1] The Fuerza Pública and the Air
Surveillance Service supported the searches with security and coordination. [@1]

Police said the targeted points operated 24 hours a day, seven days a week,
distributing mainly crack, cocaine and marijuana. [@1] Authorities linked the
drug-selling activity to armed violence, robberies and public-order disturbances
in the intervened sectors. [@1] The searches targeted several homes that
investigators said had been used to sell drugs for several months. [@3]
