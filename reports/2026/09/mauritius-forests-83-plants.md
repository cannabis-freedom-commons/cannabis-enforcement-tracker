Officers of the ADSU anti-drug unit ran operations across the country on Thursday 24 September and found cannabis growing in forests. [@1] The account was published on 26 September. [@1]

At Montagne Cabris they uprooted 12 plants measuring 56 cm to 1.03 m. [@1] They then destroyed 10 more plants of 86 cm to 1.30 m at Saponaire. [@1]

In the Saint-François woods, officers first seized eight plants of 5 to 56 cm, then 53 more of 5 to 50 cm. [@1] In total 83 plants were seized. [@1]

The growers are still unknown and are actively being sought. [@1]
