Southampton Town police responded to a 911 call at about 1 a.m. on September 17
reporting a home-invasion burglary at a house on West Neck Road in North Sea.
[@1] Officers found one man inside with a laceration to his head who was
conscious and alert, and the other people at the house had fled before police
arrived. [@2]

Detectives observed a large illegal quantity of marijuana in plain sight and
called in the Suffolk County District Attorney's Office East End Drug Task Force
and office investigators to assist. [@2] A search warrant turned up
approximately 1,000 pounds (about 453.6 kilograms) of marijuana, which had been
packaged for sale and distribution. [@2] The injured occupant was taken to a
local hospital with non-life-threatening injuries. [@2]

Antonio M. Velay, 27, of Southampton, was arrested and charged with Criminal
Possession of Cannabis in the First Degree, a class D felony, and was held for
arraignment. [@2] Newsday reported that police did not confirm whether Velay was
an occupant of the house or involved in the alleged burglary, and that no one
had been charged in the burglary. [@3]

Detectives said they were continuing to investigate and asked anyone with
information to contact the Southampton Town Police detective division. [@2]
