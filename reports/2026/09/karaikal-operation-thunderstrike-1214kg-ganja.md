Karaikal police intercepted four people near the Keezhavanjore bus stop on 5
September 2026 and seized 10.84 kg of ganja, which officers valued at roughly Rs
5 lakh. [@1] The accused were Sadaiyan of Athoor, Salem, Pollam Appala Narsha,
Veerasai Santhosh Kiran and Devi Budi, all from Andhra Pradesh. [@1] They were
booked at T.R. Pattinam police station. [@1] Investigation revealed that they
were transporting the ganja to Bakiaraj, who was also involved in the
methamphetamine case. [@1]

On 8 September at Kottucherry, Inspector Ramu and his team found Mahendiran
Manthangi of Ammankovilpath, Narmugan and one minor of Karaikalmedu in
possession of 1.3 kg of ganja. [@1] The 1.3 kg was packed in 248 small packets
meant for sale in Karaikal and was worth roughly Rs 65,000. [@1] An NDPS case
was registered at Kottucherry police station. [@1] Police said 12.14 kg of ganja
were seized during the operations. [@1]

Karaikal police carried out the action under the ongoing drive Operation
Thunderstrike. [@1] Senior Superintendent of Police Vinay Kumar Gadge said the
operation involved the Anti-Narcotics Task Force and the Cyber Cell, and was
conducted by six teams involving 25 police personnel over around 30 days. [@1]
All the suspects were booked under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic
Substances Act, 1985. [@1] The same operations also seized 7.605 kg of
methamphetamine worth approximately Rs 6.08 crore from other suspects. [@1]
