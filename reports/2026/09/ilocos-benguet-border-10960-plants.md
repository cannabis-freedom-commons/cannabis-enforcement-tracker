PDEA and Philippine National Police teams uprooted and destroyed 10,960 fully grown marijuana plants during operations on 10 and 11 September 2026. [@1,2] The plantation lay between Barangay Licungan in Sugpon, Ilocos Sur, and Barangay Tacadang in Kibungan, Benguet. [@1,2]

The reported plantation covered about 1,760 square metres, or 0.176 hectares, and the Dangerous Drugs Board valuation was about 2.192 million pesos. [@1,2] No cultivator was arrested. [@1,2]

Authorities said they were verifying the land ownership and would pursue unidentified cultivators under the drug law. [@1,2]
