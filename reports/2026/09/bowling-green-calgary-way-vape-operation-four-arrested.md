The Bowling Green-Warren County Drug Task Force and the Kentucky State Police
Interdiction Team arrested four Bowling Green men for operating an illegal
marijuana vape operation. [@1] Those arrested were Javon Mitchell, Dayshawn
Mitchell, Savyon Howard and George Murray. [@1] They were each charged with
trafficking in marijuana over five pounds, described as a Class C felony with a
gun enhancement, and with engaging in organized crime. [@1] All of the
individuals were lodged at the Warren County Regional Jail. [@2]

Detectives seized 2,146 illegally manufactured marijuana vape products and
hundreds of commercial-appearing packaging boxes for the vapes. [@2] They also
recovered more than two gallons of liquid marijuana extract, which investigators
estimated to be the equivalent of approximately 10 pounds of marijuana. [@3]
Alongside the cannabis materials, officers seized 48 oxycodone tablets, two
handguns, a shotgun, $3,489 in cash and a 2015 Jeep Grand Cherokee. [@1]

After an eight-week investigation and surveillance operation, detectives
obtained a search warrant for a residence at 1407 Calgary Way. [@3] When
detectives arrived on the scene, the people in the home refused to open the door
and officers made a forcible entry, then found a clandestine marijuana vape
manufacturing operation running out of the garage. [@1] Officials said the
products were made in unsanitary, non-sterile conditions not suitable for a
consumer product. [@1] The agency states this is the first marijuana vape
manufacturing operation of its type seen by the task force in its 29-year
operation. [@2] The investigation remains ongoing and additional charges are
possible. [@1]
