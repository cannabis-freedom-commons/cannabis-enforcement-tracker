Shirak criminal police received operational information that a 36-year-old
resident of Kandilyan Street in Gyumri was illegally acquiring, storing and
selling drugs. [@1] Further work pointed to his uncle and a friend holding a
large quantity of drugs, the uncle in a building in Vagramaberd village and the
friend in a house on Kandilyan Street. [@1] Investigators opened a criminal case
over the matter. [@1] After the 28 September searches, police detained the
36-year-old man and his uncle, while the preliminary investigation continued.
[@1]

Officers presented the overall result as 1.5 kg of marijuana seized. [@1] At the
57-year-old uncle's house in Vagramaberd, officers found more than 50 grams of
marijuana, drug-making paraphernalia and 17 cannabis plants. [@2] At the
48-year-old friend's house, officers found packages of marijuana totalling 93
grams and more than 200 grams of marijuana in a plastic container. [@1] At the
36-year-old Gyumri resident's house, officers found three glass containers
holding a total of more than 700 grams of marijuana, alongside a vial of
methadone solution and other items. [@1] A shed behind that building held about
200 grams more marijuana, plus cannabis seeds and droppers. [@2] The same search
also turned up droppers, cannabis seeds and white powder-like and crystal-like
masses. [@2]

The account was attributed to the press service of the republic's Interior
Ministry police. [@1] The report lists only the 36-year-old man and his uncle as
detained. [@1]
