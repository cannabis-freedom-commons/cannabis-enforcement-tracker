On the morning of Wednesday 9 September 2026, police arrested three people in Rivera and seized 355 cannabis plants. [@1,3,4,6] A 24-year-old man had no criminal record. [@1,3,6] A 32-year-old man and a 61-year-old woman did. [@1,3,5]

The raids were Operation Manantial, run by the national police investigations directorate through its northern region in Rivera. [@1] The special operations directorate, the police aviation directorate and the rural security brigade of the Rivera police took part, under a semi-specialised prosecutor in Rivera. [@1] Investigators had concluded that the people they were following had a large cannabis planting in a rural part of the department. [@1] They carried out six searches in the city of Rivera and in the countryside. [@1,3]

Officers seized 355 cannabis plants and five nylon bags of plant material weighing 4.732 kg in all. [@1,3,4] They also took a shotgun, a pistol with its numbers filed off, a chumbera, two pickup trucks and a motorcycle. [@1,3] From the growing site they took a gas cylinder, a thermometer, a machete, three mobile phones, soil fertiliser, 110 black pots and three bags of soil. [@1,3] A judge ordered the three held and brought to the prosecutor that afternoon. [@1]

El Observador reported the same seizures and the same three people the next day, from the Interior Ministry. [@2,4] National police chief José Manuel Azambuya said the operation was in the same strategic line as a cocaine and coca-paste seizure in Artigas and Salto the week before, and that the two inquiries were separate. [@2]

A 32-year-old man with a criminal record was sentenced to four years in prison for producing raw materials in the form of negotiating prohibited narcotics, plus usurpation, handling stolen goods and carrying a firearm with its identification marks removed. [@5] A 24-year-old man was sentenced to three and a half years in prison for producing raw materials and the continued supply of prohibited narcotics, among other offences. [@5] The Interior Ministry said the 32-year-old was convicted as the perpetrator of production of raw materials under the negotiating modality, a usurpation offence, a handling offence and the firearms offence, all in real reiteration. [@6] The ministry said the 24-year-old was convicted of producing raw materials and the continued supply of prohibited narcotics, all in real reiteration, and given three years and six months. [@6] The Interior Ministry said the plants, the substance, the vehicles and the weapons seized in the operation had all been confiscated by the courts. [@6] Rivera prosecutor Alejandra Domínguez said the investigation had not established where the production was to go. [@5] The prosecutor recalled that a drug plantation had been found on the same estate years earlier, involving the same accused man. [@5] Telenoche reported that Domínguez dated that earlier planting to 2019 and 2020, in procedures that involved the same suspect. [@7] A 61-year-old woman also detained in the operation, found on a nearby smallholding, was released for lack of evidence against her. [@5] Telenoche reported that the plantation stood inside a Cofusa estate on Ruta 5, on forestry company land that the accused had invaded, and could only be reached by motorcycle. [@7]
