Three Jamaican men were arrested in Antigua during a police sting operation on
14 September 2026. [@1] The men were identified as Aljermin McLean, 25, Joshua
Newton, 21, and Elvis Smith, 54. [@1] A police sting operation in September led
to the recovery of 26.4 pounds of marijuana. [@1]

Police found them in possession of 26.4 pounds of marijuana, reported as valued
at about EC$79,000. [@1] They appeared before a magistrate's court jointly
charged with possession of a controlled substance, possession with intent to
supply and possession with intent to transfer. [@1] The three men were not
represented at court. [@1]

They were remanded to prison until 25 November for mention and a case management
report. [@1] The allegations against the three men had not been proven in court.
