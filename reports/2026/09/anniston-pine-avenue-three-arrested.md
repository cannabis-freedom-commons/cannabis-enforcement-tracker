Three people were arrested when police executed a search warrant at a residence
in Anniston, Alabama. [@1] Anniston Police Department investigators, working
with the 7th Judicial Major Crimes Unit, developed probable cause to obtain a
warrant for a residence in the 1600 block of Pine Avenue. [@1] Members of the
Anniston Police Department Special Response Team, with APD officers and
investigators and the major-crimes unit, executed the warrant on Tuesday, Sept.
8. [@1] Authorities did not release the names of the three people arrested or
information about specific charges. [@1]

Investigators reported recovering approximately five ounces of suspected
marijuana, alongside one ounce of suspected MDMA, during the operation. [@1] The
Anniston Police Department said it intends to continue working with other law
enforcement agencies to investigate suspected drug trafficking and distribution
within the city. [@1] Police encouraged members of the public with information
about suspected drug trafficking or illegal drug distribution in Anniston to
contact law enforcement, saying community information can assist investigators
in developing cases. [@1] The investigation remains active and ongoing, with
police saying no additional information was being released at that time. [@1]
