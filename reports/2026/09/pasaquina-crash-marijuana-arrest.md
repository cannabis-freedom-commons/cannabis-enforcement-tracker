El Salvador's National Civil Police said a 25-year-old man was detained after
police found a large portion of marijuana he was allegedly carrying hidden in a
vehicle. [@2] Police identified the detainee as Freddy Azael Amaya Zúñiga. [@1]
Police said the vehicle had been involved in a traffic accident at kilometre 193
of the Pan-American Highway in Pasaquina, La Unión Norte. [@1] Diario El Mundo's
roundup of Sunday detentions places the arrest on Sunday, 6 September 2026. [@3]
Agents were attending the crash when the driver's behaviour aroused their
suspicions. [@1] They searched the vehicle and found a large package that,
according to the official version, contained marijuana. [@1] Diario1 reported
that the police account said the agents found the marijuana package hidden in
the vehicle and then detained him. [@2]

The package was seized as evidence for the judicial proceedings that would
follow. [@1] Police said Amaya Zúñiga would be referred to the Attorney
General's Office so it could bring him before the corresponding court to face
illicit drug trafficking. [@2] The PNC said he would be referred for the crime
of illicit drug trafficking. [@1]

The stretch of highway where the procedure took place lies in the district of
Pasaquina, in La Unión Norte department, near El Salvador's eastern border. [@1]
Under Salvadoran law, sanctions for possessing, transporting, distributing or
selling illicit substances range from about 10 to 15 years in prison, depending
on quantity and circumstances. [@1] The official report left open whether the
25-year-old acted alone or whether others were linked to moving the package.
[@1]
