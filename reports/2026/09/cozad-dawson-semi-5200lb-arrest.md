Nebraska State Patrol troopers stopped 44-year-old Virginia driver William Gomez
on 4 September 2026 in an eastbound semi on Interstate 80 between Cozad and
Lexington for driving on the shoulder and speeding in a construction zone. [@2]
Troopers said they smelled marijuana coming from the truck before searching it.
[@1] Gomez was arrested and booked into the Dawson County Jail. [@2] He was
charged in Dawson County Court with possession of marijuana with intent to
distribute, possession of a controlled substance without a tax stamp and
possession of more than a pound of marijuana. [@2] A judge set his bond at
$100,000, with 10 percent required for release. [@2] He was due back in court
for a preliminary hearing on 22 September. [@1]

The search found 27 pallets holding 12 to 18 boxes each, with each box holding
20 sealed one-pound packages of marijuana. [@2] A later Nebraska State Patrol
inventory put the marijuana portion at approximately 5,200 pounds (about 2,359
kg). [@3] The same trailer also held about 13,600 pounds of THC products and
1,000 pounds of psilocybin products, making a combined load of roughly 19,800
pounds. [@3] The patrol estimated the whole shipment's street value at more than
$50 million. [@3]

State officials called it the agency's largest drug interdiction by volume. [@3]
Investigators had not disclosed the shipment's origin or intended destination.
[@3]
