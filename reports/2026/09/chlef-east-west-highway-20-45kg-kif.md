Chlef Customs officers, working with soldiers of the National People's Army (ANP), foiled two smuggling attempts on the East-West Highway as part of the fight against organised crime. [@1] The operations were carried out by the regional anti-fraud brigade and the mobile brigade of Chlef, according to a statement from the customs directorate's communication unit. [@1]

The checks led to the seizure of 20 kg and 450 g of treated kif (processed cannabis) hidden inside the fuel tank of a passenger car. [@1] One person was arrested in that operation. [@1]

In the second check, officers seized 95 ecstasy tablets carefully hidden inside the dashboard of another passenger car, and two people were arrested. [@1] Legal proceedings were opened in coordination with the competent judicial authorities. [@1]
