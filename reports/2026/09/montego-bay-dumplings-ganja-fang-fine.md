Dalton Fang appeared before the St James Parish Court charged with possession of
ganja. [@1] He was represented by attorney-at-law Chumu Paris and entered a
guilty plea. [@1] Senior Parish Judge Natiesha Fairclough-Hylton fined him
$5,000 or 10 days in jail. [@1]

On September 17 at about 1:10 pm, Fang visited the Montego Bay lock-up saying he
had brought food for a person in custody. [@1] Officers searched a transparent
plate containing dumplings and brown-stewed chicken. [@1] Inside some of the
dumplings officers found vegetable matter resembling ganja totalling one ounce
and wrapped in a plastic bag. [@1] Police described the find as six parcels of
ganja concealed inside the food. [@1] When cautioned, Fang said he did not know
about the ganja. [@1]

In mitigation, Paris said people showing kindness are sometimes taken advantage
of. [@1] Paris said Fang's actions were purely out of innocence. [@1]
