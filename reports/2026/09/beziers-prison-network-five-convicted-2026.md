The Béziers criminal court sentenced five members of a cannabis, cocaine,
ketamine and ecstasy network on 7 September 2026, all of them convicted. [@1]
The network was run by a 25-year-old man from his cell at the Béziers
penitentiary centre, where he was serving a three-year sentence given in 2024
for drug trafficking. [@2]

Gendarmes from the Lodève research brigade opened the inquiry on 20 January 2026
and made the arrests in an operation on 20 July 2026. [@1] Five people were
taken into police custody after the 20 July operation. [@3] Searches seized
several kilograms of cannabis resin and herbal cannabis, cocaine and synthetic
drugs, money and a vehicle. [@1] Another man, living at Argeliers in the Aude,
cleaned up the cannabis with a machine, a step the reports call "curage". [@2]

The main organiser was given six years in prison with continued detention and a
five-year ban on staying in Béziers. [@1] His partner was sentenced to three
years, half of it suspended with probation, with a deferred committal order.
[@1] A third defendant got 24 months, ten of them suspended with probation, with
a previous suspended sentence fully revoked and his detention maintained. [@1]
The last two defendants were each given two years, one of them suspended with
probation, with the prison part served under electronic tagging. [@1] All five
were also fined between 2,500 and 10,000 euros, and the court ordered the
confiscation of the seized goods including a vehicle. [@1]

The five were sentenced to terms running from two years to six years in prison,
as the prosecutor's statement summarised them. [@3]
