Chlef's mobile customs brigade, supported by the People's National Army and the National Gendarmerie, reported three operations that seized 96.3 kg of treated kif and detained eight people. [@1,2]

The first operation recovered 50 kg and led to three arrests, the second recovered 28.5 kg and led to one arrest, and the third recovered 17.8 kg and led to four arrests. [@1,2]

The detained people and the seized cannabis were handed to the competent judicial authorities. [@1,2]
