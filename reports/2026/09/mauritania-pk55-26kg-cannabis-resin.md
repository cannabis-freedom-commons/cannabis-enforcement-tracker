Gendarmes at the PK55 border post in Dakhlet Nouadhibou, on the road from
Nouadhibou to the Moroccan border, seized about 26 kg of cannabis resin on
Tuesday 29 September 2026. [@1,2]

Officers found the resin, known in Mauritania as al-hajar al-aswad, or "black
stone", during a routine check in which they used sniffer dogs. [@1] It was
carefully hidden inside a vehicle heading into the country. [@1,2]

Two foreign nationals who were in the vehicle were arrested. [@1,2] The
gendarmerie did not name them or give their nationalities. [@1]

The drug was sealed and judicial proceedings were opened against the two. [@1,2]
