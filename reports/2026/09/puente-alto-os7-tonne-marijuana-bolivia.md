Carabineros' OS7 anti-drug unit and prosecutors announced that two operations
ending in Puente Alto, a commune on Santiago's southern edge, seized more than a
tonne of Bolivian marijuana and detained eight men. [@1] The eight detainees
were seven Colombians and one Chilean; Emol reported that the Colombians were in
the country irregularly and that the Chilean was 34 and had a criminal record.
[@3]

In all, officers seized 1,258.298 kilos of marijuana, 1.690 kg of cocaine
hydrochloride, 1.235 kg of ketamine and 79 MDMA tablets, which the authorities
valued at 11,372 million pesos. [@3] Officers also took two vehicles, a 9 mm
Glock pistol, 28 rounds of ammunition and close to 4 million pesos in cash. [@2]

The larger operation began with OS7 officers in Iquique and the Tarapacá local
prosecutor's office, who traced a consignment brought into Chile through
unauthorised crossings at Colchane in the Tarapacá region and followed it in a
controlled delivery to a stash property in Puente Alto. [@4] That operation,
carried out under a search warrant, caught the group in the act and produced
1,124 kilos of marijuana found in 49 sacks. [@4] A second operation, run with
the Metropolitan South prosecutor's office, caught another transnational group
as it received a load from the north of the country in the same commune and
seized 134 kilos of marijuana there. [@2]

Tarapacá regional prosecutor Virginia Aravena said the packages carried two
kinds of label, one with a cartoon character, which the press called
cariñositos, and one with a money-transfer logo, which investigators believed
marked the group each consignment was meant for. [@1] Prosecutors said the
surveillance began on 12 September and that the investigation continues into who
supplied and received the loads, while a controlled delivery ended in the
Metropolitan Region. [@3] For the Tarapacá case prosecutors sought to extend the
detention of three foreign nationals and request preventive custody, while the
five held in the Metropolitan South case were due before Santiago's 12th
Guarantee Court, charged under the Drugs Act and the Arms Act. [@1] Carabineros
director general Marcelo Araya said the operations broke up two gangs that sold
drugs in crowded public places and that the force had seized about 68 tonnes of
drugs so far in 2026. [@4]
