On specific information, Sonamura Police with the 81st Battalion of the BSF jointly raided the Batadola border roadside at UNC Nagar under Sonamura Police Station on the night of Friday 11 September 2026 and recovered 27 kg of dry cannabis. [@1]

The ganja was seized as unclaimed after observing all legal formalities, and a case was registered at Sonamura police station under the NDPS Act, India's drug law. [@1] Inspector Tapas Das, the officer in charge of Sonamura Police Station, said such operations against illegal contraband would continue. [@1]

The report put the destination market value of the seized cannabis at about Rs2 lakh. [@1] Police started an investigation to identify the owner of the consignment. [@1]
