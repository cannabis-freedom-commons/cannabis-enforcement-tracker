Customs officers in Sorong and the Sorong City police narcotics unit detained
two people and seized about 306 grams of dried ganja sent from Jayapura to
Sorong on Wednesday 16 September 2026. [@3] The case began on Tuesday 15
September 2026, when Bea Cukai Sorong received information about a parcel from
Jayapura suspected of containing narcotics and declared with goods that did not
match. [@3] Officers coordinated with the narcotics investigation unit of
Polresta Sorong Kota to develop the information and watch the parcel's movement.
[@3]

On Wednesday 16 September 2026 the joint team secured the parcel in the
courtyard of a hotel with the initials CV in the Remu Utara area of Sorong. [@4]
Officers also detained two people suspected of being connected with the
shipment, for further examination. [@3] The parcel held 11 packs of dried ganja
weighing about 306 grams, hidden inside black clothing. [@4] The parcel had been
sent through a parcel-delivery company, a perusahaan jasa titipan, from Jayapura
to Sorong. [@3] The evidence and the two detained people were handed over to
Polresta Sorong Kota for examination and further legal process. [@3]

Customs described PATMA BERANI as a joint patrol run simultaneously and in an
integrated way to strengthen supervision of the circulation of narcotics,
psychotropics and precursors. [@3] Customs said PATMA BERANI 2026, which ran
simultaneously across Indonesia from 16 September to 16 October 2026, uncovered
140 cases of illicit circulation of narcotics, psychotropics and precursors,
with total seizures of 1,701.51 kilograms, or about 1.7 tonnes. [@1] Customs
said the operation's seizures of narcotics, psychotropics and precursors rose
101 percent on 2025, when it recorded 191 enforcement actions with 818.01
kilograms seized. [@2]
