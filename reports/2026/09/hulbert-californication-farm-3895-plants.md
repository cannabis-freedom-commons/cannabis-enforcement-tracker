Oklahoma Bureau of Narcotics agents served a search warrant at an illegal
marijuana operation in Hulbert, Oklahoma, on Tuesday, 22 September 2026. [@2]
The OBN Marijuana Enforcement Team and Cherokee County sheriff's deputies
carried out the raid at Californication Farm LLC near Highway 51 and South Coos
Thompson Road. [@2] The warrant was part of an ongoing investigation into
illegal cultivation and operating without an OBN registration. [@2]

Agents and deputies seized 3,895 marijuana plants, 69.2 pounds (about 31.4 kg)
of processed marijuana and one rifle. [@2] The Oklahoma Bureau of Narcotics said
agents seized 3,895 plants, 69.2 pounds of processed marijuana and a rifle in
the Hulbert search. [@1] No arrests were reported; the bureau said arrests were
pending as the case moved forward. [@2]

The district attorney's office, the Cherokee County commissioner's office, the
Oklahoma Department of Agriculture, Food and Forestry, U.S. Immigration and
Customs Enforcement, the Hulbert Fire Department and the Tahlequah Fire
Department assisted. [@2] OBN said its Marijuana Enforcement Team, created in
2021, targets black-market marijuana trafficking, money laundering and fraud
within Oklahoma's licensed marijuana programme. [@2] The bureau said the number
of farms with an active OBN registration fell from a peak of 8,400 in 2022 to
1,299 at the time of the raid. [@2]
