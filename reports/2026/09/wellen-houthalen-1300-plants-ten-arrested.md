Police arrested ten people in the morning in a large operation in the provinces
of Limburg and Flemish Brabant. [@1] Officers searched eighteen locations in the
operation. [@1] They found two active cannabis plantations, in Wellen and
Houthalen, with 1,300 plants between them. [@1] Police also seized 300,000 euros
in cash and eleven kilograms of cannabis. [@1]

The arrests were of eight men and two women from Hasselt, Alken, Herk-de-Stad
and Wellen. [@1] Nine of the ten were placed under arrest warrant, meaning an
investigating judge ordered them held. [@1] The local police force LRH, which
covers the Limburg regional capital Hasselt, said at a press conference on
Thursday that this was its largest operation, on the Tuesday before the
article's 24 September publication. [@1]
