Laukaha police arrested two Nepalese nationals and seized 23.2kg of ganja worth
about Rs 11.6 lakh in Madhubani district, according to a press release from the
office of Superintendent of Police Yogendra Kumar. [@1] Police said they
received specific information around 9am on Sept 2 that two men on a black
motorcycle were carrying contraband from Nepal into India along the western
embankment of the Balan river towards Ekaam. [@1]

A police team laid a trap on the embankment opposite Madhopur village, spotted
the suspects coming from the Nepal side with plastic sacks and bags, and
intercepted them as they tried to flee. [@1] The two were identified as Mohd
Rustam of Maholi in Nepal's Saptari district and Mohd Mustuf of Lahan in Siraha
district, Nepal. [@1]

Police recovered 23.2kg of ganja hidden in the bags and also seized the
motorcycle allegedly used for the smuggling and two mobile phones. [@1] Police
said the accused admitted during interrogation that they had brought the
contraband from Nepal for sale in India. [@1]

A case was registered under relevant provisions of the NDPS Act, and both
accused were sent to judicial custody while further investigation continues.
