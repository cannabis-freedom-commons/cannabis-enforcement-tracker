## Arrests and seizure

Chinese police arrested 21 suspects in coordinated operations in Hebei Province and elsewhere in mid-August, Xinhua reported, citing the Ministry of Public Security (MPS). [@2] Xinhua said officers seized a batch of non-scheduled precursor chemicals that could be used to manufacture drugs, but it gave no weight or list of sites. [@2] On 9 September, the U.S. Drug Enforcement Administration (DEA) said the MPS had arrested 21 people, seized 475 kilograms of synthetic cannabinoids and closed four illicit manufacturing sites in China. [@1] The releases describe the seized material differently: Xinhua reports precursor chemicals, while the DEA gives a synthetic-cannabinoid weight. [@1,2]

## Joint investigation

Xinhua said the case began after U.S. authorities shared information in early May 2026, which China connected to an investigation already under way. [@2] The DEA identified the two Chinese nationals as Lisa Xing, 36, and Zhanwen Song, 38, and said they were charged in a U.S. federal indictment in Texas. [@1] Xinhua names the pair only by the surnames Xing and Song and says they were among the 21 people arrested in China. [@1,2] The DEA said the investigation concerned synthetic drugs supplied from China and smuggled into U.S. prisons, and that an FBI-MPS counternarcotics working group coordinated the effort. [@1] The DEA said all defendants are presumed innocent unless convicted in court. [@1]
