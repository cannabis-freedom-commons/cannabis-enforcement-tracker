Detectives of the PDI and prosecutors in Punta Arenas detained five people on 4 September 2026
accused of running a group that sold cannabis sativa to homes in the regional capital, a method the
outlets call "delivery". [@1,2] The five — Fernanda Aguilar Cárcamo, Sergio Peña Bustos, Franco
Vásquez Vargas, Moisés Haro Nancuante and José Luis Rodríguez Barrera — were taken before the
Juzgado de Garantía of Punta Arenas. [@1,2]

In raids on the properties the group used, detectives seized about 500 grams of marijuana, indoor
cultivation equipment, a firearm with ammunition, cash and mobile phones. [@1,2] ITV Patagonia also
reported that cannabis plants and cocaine were found in some of the homes and firearms and
ammunition in another. [@2]

Prosecutors asked for the detention of four of them to be extended while forensic tests were
completed; Magistrate Franco Reyes granted the request, and their formalisation was set for Monday
7 September. [@1,2] The fifth, José Luis Rodríguez Barrera, was formalised at the same hearing by
chief prosecutor Johanna Irribarra. [@1,2] He presented a medical prescription for regulated cannabis
consumption, and the court imposed a regional residence order (arraigo regional) instead of
pre-trial detention. [@1,2]
