Gendarmes of Sección "Chalicán", part of Escuadrón 60 "San Pedro" of Gendarmería
Nacional, opened five parcels that had been seized from a courier company's
transport on Ruta Nacional Nº 34 at kilometre 1,212 in Jujuy. [@1] The Federal
Guarantees Court No. 1 of Jujuy, a Juzgado Federal de Garantías, authorised the
opening of the parcels. [@1] The gendarmes opened the parcels in front of
witnesses. [@1]

Inside they found 111 packages of cannabis sativa weighing 117 kilos 402 grams
in total. [@1] The release places the parcel operation at Paraje Chalicán, a
rural locality in Jujuy, and describes the packages as bricks of cannabis
sativa. [@1] No one was arrested in the parcel procedure; the release names no
detainee in it, and the federal prosecutor's office ordered only the drug
seized. [@1]

The same release reports a separate stop at kilometre 1,169 of the same road,
where gendarmes of Sección "Núcleo" found 66 kilos 13 grams of cocaine in a
hidden compartment of a car and the federal prosecutor's office ordered a man
and a woman detained. [@1] Because the release treats the two checks as separate
procedures, the cocaine stop is described here only to show what is excluded;
its 66 kilos 13 grams are not counted as cannabis. [@1]
