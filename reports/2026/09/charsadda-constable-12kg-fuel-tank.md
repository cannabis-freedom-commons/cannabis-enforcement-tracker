## The stop

The Excise Intelligence Squad and Excise Police Charsadda, acting on a tip-off, stopped
a car near the Prang graveyard on Nowshera Road in a joint operation on Wednesday 16
September 2026 and foiled an attempt to smuggle hashish. [@1,2] A search found 12,000
grams of hashish concealed in two specially built compartments inside the vehicle's
fuel tank. [@1,2]

## The suspect

The driver, Mehr Gul of Sadokhel in Mohmand district, was identified as a police
constable and arrested at the scene. [@1] Excise officials registered a case against
him at the excise police station in Charsadda, and an investigation and legal
proceedings were under way. [@1,2]
