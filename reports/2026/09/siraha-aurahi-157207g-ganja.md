At about 1:35 a.m. on Wednesday, 9 September 2026, a mobile patrol from Armed Police Force Nepal No. 7 Battalion Headquarters seized 157 kg and 207 grams of marijuana near Ittatar in Aurahi Rural Municipality-4, Siraha. [@1,2]

The people carrying the marijuana on their heads toward India fled after seeing the patrol, leaving the bundles behind. [@1,2]

The patrol took the abandoned marijuana into custody and began the process of handing it to the relevant authorities for investigation and legal proceedings. [@1,2]
