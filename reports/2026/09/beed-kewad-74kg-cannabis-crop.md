On Monday evening, 7 September 2026, a police team raided public grazing land at Kewad village in Kaij tehsil, Beed district, after receiving a tip about cannabis cultivation. [@1] Officers found cannabis plants concealed among tall wild grass in front of a residence. [@1]

Police uprooted 74.432 kilograms of cannabis crop valued at approximately Rs 14.88 lakh. [@1] The occupant of the house, a 22-year-old man, was arrested and booked under relevant provisions of India's Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act. [@1]

The opened account does not give a plant count, cultivated area, suspect name or later court outcome. [@1]
