On Wednesday, 9 September 2026, ADSU officers searched the home of a 40-year-old fisherman at Anse Quitor, Rodrigues. [@1,2]

The search found 32 cannabis plants measuring 20 to 55 centimetres and about 7.8 kg of cannabis in two areas of the home. [@1,2] Officers also found equipment associated with cultivation and packaging materials. [@1,2]

The fisherman and a second man from Pistache were arrested and held while the investigation continued. [@1,2]
