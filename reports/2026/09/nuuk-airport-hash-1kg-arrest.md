Customs officers found about a kilogram of hash on a 27-year-old man at Nuuk Airport on Thursday
10 September 2026. [@1,2] He had arrived from Denmark and was on his way to Sisimiut. [@1,2]

The man was arrested, and the prosecutor's office went for a straksdom, an immediate sentence,
because he was known to the police from another case, the duty officer told KNR; he would not say
what that case concerned. [@1] Sermitsiaq reported that the man was due before the court on the
Friday morning. [@2]
