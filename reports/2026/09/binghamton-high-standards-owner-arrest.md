A cannabis shop owner was arrested after reopening a location sealed by the OCM.
[@1] The sheriff's weekly arrest summary identifies him as 41-year-old Lucas J.
Rought of Endicott. [@4] He was charged with third-degree criminal trespass and
second-degree obstruction of governmental administration after a joint operation
with the Office of Cannabis Management. [@4]

The New York State Office of Cannabis Management (OCM), the state cannabis
regulator, had sealed the store after a May operation in which it seized illicit
cannabis and shut the location. [@2] On Sept. 4, agents found the locks cut and
the shop operating despite the seal orders. [@2] Agents took more illicit
cannabis from the location, then closed and resealed the business. [@2] Deputies
assisted at the scene with OCM as the primary agency. [@3] Signs posted on the
windows indicated illegal cannabis had been seized. [@3]

The shop is High Standards at 104 Broad Ave in Binghamton, which state and local
law enforcement visited on Sept. 4 for an OCM enforcement action. [@3] The
location had been part of a larger May enforcement operation in which several
shops were raided and shut down. [@3] OCM said the business was operating when
it was supposed to be closed, which led to the trespass arrest. [@2]
