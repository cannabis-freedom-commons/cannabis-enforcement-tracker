A joint anti-drug drive in several areas of Rangpur city ended in two young men
being given three months' simple imprisonment each and a fine. [@1] The
Department of Narcotics Control's Rangpur Circle Ka and the district
administration ran the drive around 6 pm on Tuesday 2 September, led by Priyanka
Abanti, an executive magistrate of the deputy commissioner's office. [@1]

Officers worked at several spots in the Alamnagar CNB Godown, Hunumantala and
Munshipara areas of Kotwali metro thana and detained the two men with the drug.
[@1] The detainees were Md Mehedi Hasan Biswas, 19, and Md Rajib, 26, and 200
grams of ganja were recovered from them in total. [@1]

A mobile court tried the two in separate cases under the Narcotics Control Act,
giving each three months' simple imprisonment and a 100 taka fine, 200 taka in
all. [@1] SM Eltas Uddin, an inspector at the DNC's Rangpur district office,
confirmed the details and said such drives against drugs would continue. [@1]
