Peru’s National Police detained nine Ecuadorian citizens in Huanchaco, a seaside
resort in Trujillo province in the north of the country, in an operation
supported by the FBI. [@1] The detainees were captured on 24 September 2026.
[@3] Police described the nine as presumed members of Los Tiguerones Trux and
said they were being investigated for alleged criminal-group membership, illegal
possession of firearms, ammunition and explosives, drug trafficking, and human
trafficking. [@1] The nine remained at Trujillo’s Criminal Investigation
Division (Divincri) as investigations continued. [@2]

At a property police treated as a bunker, officers found a mini-Uzi, a revolver,
nine dynamite cartridges and ammunition. [@2] Officers also seized 23
cellphones, three scales and close to one kilogram of marijuana. [@1] Police
said two notebooks contained notes that may correspond to alleged extortion
payments. [@1] The operation also yielded a vehicle and a cloth bearing the
words Los Tiguerones Trux. [@1] Officers found documents linked to one detainee
and other items for analysis. [@1] The US Embassy in Lima said Peru’s National
Police and the FBI were acting together in the operation. [@1]
