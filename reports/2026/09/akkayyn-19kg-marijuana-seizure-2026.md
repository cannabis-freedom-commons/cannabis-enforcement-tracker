The seizure took place in Akkayyn District in the North Kazakhstan Region and
involved a 40-year-old district resident. [@1] Police seized over 1.9 kilograms
of dried marijuana in a settlement in the district during the Karasora
preventive operation. [@1] Officers found the drug in the home of the
40-year-old local resident. [@1] Officers also seized a homemade device for
consuming drugs from him. [@1] The man said he had collected wild cannabis,
dried it and kept it for personal use, and he admitted guilt. [@1]

The storage case, treated as an especially large amount, was sent to court under
a simplified procedure. [@2] Police have recorded three illegal drug-storage
offences in Akkayyn District since the start of the year. [@1] Police said a
total of 19.6 kilograms of dried marijuana has been taken out of circulation in
the district this year. [@1]

Police reminded the public that unlawful acquisition, storage and sale of
narcotics entail criminal liability. [@1] Police said preventive and operational
work to identify storage places and distribution channels is continuing. [@1]
