Four Calgary-area men were charged with a combined 36 offences on 27 August 2026 after an RCMP
investigation into what police described as a Calgary-based criminal network centred around a
multimillion-dollar contraband tobacco trafficking operation. [@1,3] Jason Douglas Pereira, 44, of
Airdrie and Jonathan Patrick Roach, 42, of Calgary were charged with ten offences each, while
Brock Anthony MacQuarrie, 41, and David Shoa Guan, 52, of Calgary were charged with eight each.
[@1] Pereira, MacQuarrie and Guan were arrested on 1 September and Roach on 3 September, and all
four were scheduled to appear in the Alberta Court of Justice in Calgary on 19 October 2026. [@1]

Officers from the RCMP Federal Policing Northwest Region's Integrated Money Laundering
Investigative Team executed seven search warrants in Calgary and Airdrie. [@1] They seized more
than $3 million in contraband tobacco, more than 88 kilograms of unstamped illicit cannabis and
cannabis byproducts, and nearly 10 kilograms of other illicit drugs including psilocybin, MDMA,
DMT and LSD. [@1,2] Officers also seized nearly $170,000 in cash and more than $300,000 in
precious metals, luxury goods and a high-value vehicle. [@1,2]

CityNews Calgary reported the four were arrested in early September. [@2] The investigation
involved the Calgary Police Service, the Forensic Accounting Management Group, the Canada Revenue
Agency, the Financial Transactions and Reports Analysis Centre of Canada and the Alberta Gaming,
Liquor and Cannabis Commission. [@1]
