Sheriff Bobby Jones reported the arrest of three people following an ongoing
Narcotics Division investigation into suspected drug trafficking. [@1] Deputy
Noah Dishman stopped a blue Chevrolet Equinox on Highway 461 on 27 September
2026. [@1] The driver was 37-year-old James Carrender of Burnside, and the
back-seat passengers were 38-year-old Joseph Meeks and 34-year-old Mindy Meeks,
both of Somerset. [@1]

During the stop Deputy Noah Dishman smelled marijuana coming from the vehicle,
and Sgt. Dylan West's K-9 Rocky alerted to narcotics. [@1] A search of the
vehicle found multiple bags of suspected marijuana, suspected methamphetamine
and drug paraphernalia. [@1] Mindy Meeks told deputies she had narcotics in her
clothing and produced bags containing suspected methamphetamine and fentanyl.
[@1] James Carrender was charged with trafficking in marijuana of less than
eight ounces as a first offence. [@1] Joseph Meeks and Mindy Meeks were each
charged with the same marijuana trafficking count. [@1] Both charge lists
include a drug paraphernalia buy or possess count. [@1] Both charge lists
include first-degree trafficking involving methamphetamine of two grams or
greater. [@1] Both charge lists include first-degree possession involving
fentanyl. [@1] Carrender was additionally charged with having no operator's
licence and failing to have a licence in possession. [@1]

All three were lodged in the Pulaski County Detention Center. [@1] Dishman was
assisted by West and K-9 Rocky, deputies Daniel Drury and Noah Wesley, Kentucky
State Police Trooper Ben Allen and the FBI. [@1] The case remained under
investigation by Dishman. [@1]
