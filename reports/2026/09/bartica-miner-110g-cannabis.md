Police arrested a 52-year-old gold miner following the discovery of 110.6 grams
of suspected cannabis on September 25, 2026 at around 16:30h at Five Miles
Junction, Bartica. [@1] Officers conducting mobile patrol duties in the Five
Miles area intercepted a motor vehicle at Five Miles Junction. [@1] The vehicle
was occupied by a 32-year-old taxi driver and the gold miner, who was a
passenger. [@1]

A search found the 52-year-old passenger with a black bulky parcel containing
three transparent parcels of leaves, seeds and stems suspected to be cannabis.
[@1] He was escorted to the Bartica Police Station with the suspected cannabis,
which was weighed in his presence and amounted to 110.6 grams. [@1] The suspect
remained in police custody as investigations continued. [@1] A second account
confirms police intercepted the motor vehicle at Five Miles Junction and
investigated the discovery of 110.6 grams of suspected cannabis at Bartica. [@2]
