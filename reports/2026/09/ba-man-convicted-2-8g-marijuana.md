The Ba Magistrate's Court convicted Alivereti Nabulivou Kunaveli of possessing
2.8 grams of marijuana. [@1] Magistrate Namrata Mishra delivered the judgment on
September 18, finding him guilty of unlawful possession of illicit drugs at
Namau, Ba, on May 21, 2021. [@1]

Police intercepted a red carrier driven by a hired driver after receiving
information about drug transportation from Navala. [@1] Officers found Kunaveli
in the front passenger seat with a white sack and a bag containing dried
marijuana leaves between his legs while he held a cane knife. [@1]

Kunaveli claimed in his defence that the drugs belonged to the carrier driver.
[@1] The magistrate accepted the driver's unchallenged evidence that Kunaveli
had hired him and returned 25 minutes later carrying the sack. [@1] She ruled
that the prosecution had proved beyond reasonable doubt that Kunaveli had
custody and control of the drugs, rejecting the claim that the driver was an
interested witness shifting blame. [@1]
