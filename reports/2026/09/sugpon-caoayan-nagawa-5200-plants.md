Police destroyed 5,200 fully grown marijuana plants worth an estimated P1.04 million at three plantation sites in the hinterlands of Sugpon town, Ilocos Sur, on 11 September 2026. [@1]

The plantations covered a combined 550 square metres in Sitio Nagawa, Barangay Caoayan, in an operation led by the 1st Ilocos Sur Provincial Mobile Force Company, the Ilocos Sur Police Provincial Office said. [@1] Police Provincial Director Col. Agosto Asuncion said the operation targeted areas identified as conducive to marijuana cultivation as part of the province's campaign to eradicate cultivation at its source. [@1]

Police destroyed the plants at the site. [@1]
