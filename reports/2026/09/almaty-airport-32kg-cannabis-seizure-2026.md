In August 2026, officers at Almaty airport stopped three attempts to import
narcotic substances into Kazakhstan in an especially large amount. [@1]
Kazinform attributed the account to the press service of the KNB, Kazakhstan's
national-security committee. [@1] Officers discovered the drugs in the luggage
of foreign citizens who had arrived on flights from countries in Southeast Asia.
[@1]

The seized substances were listed as marijuana, cannabis resin and hashish oil.
[@1] The drugs were hidden in containers disguised as cosmetic products. [@1] An
expert examination concluded that the combined weight of the seized narcotics
exceeded 32 kilograms. [@1] The quantity was described as especially large, the
characterization applied to all three import attempts. [@1]

The three August interceptions were reported together as the month's result at
Almaty airport. [@1] All of the substances enumerated for the three
interceptions were cannabis products. [@1] Materials from the detected incidents
were transferred under jurisdictional rules to the competent authorities for a
procedural decision on further action. [@1] Further action depended on the
procedural decision to be taken by the receiving authorities. [@1]
