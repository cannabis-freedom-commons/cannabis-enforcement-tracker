Police seized more than 11,000 illegally cultivated cannabis plants in a massive operation across hilly and forested areas under Varla police station in Sendhwa subdivision. [@1]

Under Operation Green Strike, 12 teams of more than 400 police personnel searched 19 villages simultaneously on Saturday with the help of drones, finding cannabis growing in five fields. [@1] Teams trekked through forests and hills while drones monitored suspicious fields. [@1]

The seized plants weighed more than 23 quintals and were estimated to be worth over Rs 1 crore, SDOP Varla Ajay Waghmare said on Sunday. [@1] Including Saturday's operation, police registered nine cases and seized more than 34 quintals of illegally cultivated cannabis over the two days. [@1]

Barwani Police's separate 10 September account said teams found four fields in Borpadava forest and seized 6,185 plants weighing 10 quintals 71 kilograms (1,071 kg), with an estimated value of Rs 50–52 lakh; four NDPS Act cases were registered. [@2,3]

Police said about 75 personnel used a covered pickup and a drone to search the forest, while people at the fields fled when they saw the police vehicles. [@2,3] This 10 September phase is the earlier action referred to in the Free Press Journal report; its more-than-11,000-plant and more-than-23-quintal figures describe the subsequent Saturday operation.
