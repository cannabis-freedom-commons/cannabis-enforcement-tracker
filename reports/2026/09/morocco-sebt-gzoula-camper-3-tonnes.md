## The stop

Royal Gendarmerie officers from Sebt Gzoula, under the direct supervision of the
regional commander, seized three tonnes of drugs on the night of Monday 28 to Tuesday 29
September 2026 from a camper van belonging to two young French people travelling from
Tangier to Essaouira. [@1] A gendarmerie patrol on National Road 1 between El Ghiate and
Sebt Gzoula stopped the French-plated vehicle, which carried a young man and a young
woman, both French nationals, and the judicial police discovered a prohibited cargo of
hashish aboard. [@1]

## The seizure

The regional gendarmerie commander travelled to the scene to oversee the seizure and the
weighing of the cargo, the towing of the vehicle, and the transfer of the two suspects
to the gendarmerie headquarters for questioning. [@1] The pair remained in custody while
the investigation worked to establish their connection to the cargo. [@1]
