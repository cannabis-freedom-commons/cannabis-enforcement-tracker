Singapore authorities found 1,290 cannabis blocks weighing about 391 kilograms in glove boxes at Pasir Panjang. [@1,2,3,4]

On 24 September 2026, more than 391 kg of cannabis was detected in an inbound container at
Singapore's Pasir Panjang Scanning Station; the joint ICA-CNB statement followed on 2
October. [@1,2] Targeting work by the Immigration and Checkpoints Authority flagged the
container for enhanced checks, and image analysts spotted anomalies in its scan. [@1,2,4] When
officers opened it, they found suspected cannabis blocks concealed in boxes of gloves, 1,290
blocks weighing about 391 kg in total. [@1,2,5]

Preliminary investigations found the drugs were transiting through Singapore to another country,
which the agencies did not name, and that the container's origin was likewise undisclosed. [@1,2]
Investigations are ongoing. [@1,2,4] The agencies note that importing or exporting more than 500
grams of cannabis carries a mandatory death sentence under the Misuse of Drugs Act 1973, a
threshold this load exceeded almost eight hundred times over. [@1,2]

The Immigration & Checkpoints Authority published two CNB photographs showing the boxes containing cannabis and the cannabis taken from them on 24 September 2026. [@4]
