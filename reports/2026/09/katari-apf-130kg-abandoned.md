The Armed Police Force (APF) seized 130 kg of marijuana from a forest in
Katari Municipality-6 of Udayapur district early on Wednesday 16 September
2026. [@1]

According to the APF Security Base in Katari, the marijuana was recovered from
the Saudhuri Chure forest at around 2:30am during a motorcycle patrol. [@1]
The patrol team spotted suspected smugglers carrying the marijuana on foot
through the forest. [@1]

The suspects fled, leaving the contraband behind packed in plastic bags. [@1]
Ten APF personnel were conducting the patrol at the time. [@1] No arrests
were reported. [@1]

The seized marijuana was handed over to the Area Police Office in Katari for
further investigation. [@1]
