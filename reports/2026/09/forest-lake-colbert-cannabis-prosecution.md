Gregory Colbert of Forest Lake was charged with two counts of first-degree drug
possession involving more than 50 kilos of cannabis flower and more than 10
kilos of cannabis concentrate. [@1] He was charged via summons during the week
of publication, following a drug raid at his home in December 2025. [@1] The
charges state that Colbert is not licensed to sell cannabis in Minnesota. [@1]
The West Metro Drug Task Force began investigating Colbert nearly a year earlier
after an informant tipped police about his alleged operation. [@1]

The informant said Colbert sold cannabis products through a website, met
customers at a Speedway gas station and brought inventory from Michigan. [@1]
After the October 2025 tip, task force officers monitored Colbert and reported
seeing him selling cannabis at the Speedway. [@1] Colbert allegedly kept his
product list in a Google Doc and used a Facebook profile under the name Greg
Steiner. [@1] Investigators say his phone number appeared on the sales website
and on the cannabis product packaging. [@1]

In December officers executed a search warrant at Colbert's Forest Lake home and
seized nearly 200 pounds, about 90.7 kg, of cannabis flower. [@1] Alongside the
flower, officers found 8,671 THC vape cartridges, 6,733 pre-rolled cannabis
joints, 344 THC edible packages and 5,200 grams of THC concentrate or wax. [@1]
Officials estimated the total value of the seized THC products at around 754,000
dollars. [@1] Officers also found gold coins, gold bars, U.S. currency and
expensive jewelry in the house. [@1] Investigators also found scales and a
computer displaying what they described as his live product menu, indicating
home-based sales. [@1]
