Mauritius's Financial Crimes Commission seized two speedboats at the home of
Ravindra Chimajee, one of the main suspects in the alleged zamal traffic between
Réunion and Mauritius, on 7 September 2026. [@1] The two boats were taken away
on two trucks at the end of the afternoon and brought to the FCC headquarters at
Réduit Triangle. [@1] Défimedia named the owner as Ravindra Chimajee and
reported that the boats were seized at his home on Monday 7 September. [@2]

Forensic officers were to examine the cabin, the lockers and the cockpit for
cannabis traces. [@1] Documents, satellite phones and other electronic devices
were also seized during the searches and were being analysed. [@1] The FCC
searches also covered possible money laundering linked to the alleged network.
[@2]

The case began when gendarmes from Saint-Benoît watching the Rivière-du-Mât
sector of Bras-Panon arrested two men and seized 155.7 kg of cannabis valued at
about 100 kg, a haul the gendarmes later weighed in full. [@1] Investigators
from the brigade des recherches de l'Est identified three other suspects, shared
the findings with Mauritius's police, and a speedboat was seen near the shore
during the operation. [@1] Ten people were being prosecuted over the attempted
shipment, five in Mauritius and five in Réunion. [@1] Some Mauritian reports put
the Bras-Panon cargo at about 156 kg, while the Réunion account gives 155.7 kg.
[@2]
