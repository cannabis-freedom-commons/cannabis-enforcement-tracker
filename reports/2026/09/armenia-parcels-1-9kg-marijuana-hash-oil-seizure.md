Armenia's State Revenue Committee anti-smuggling department said it works to
detect illegal movement of narcotics across the state border using analytical
tools and operative-search measures. [@1] The committee said that over the past
month various narcotics hidden by smuggling inside different items were found in
shipments brought into Armenia through international carriers and seized from
illegal circulation. [@1]

The finds included about 1,800 grams of marijuana and 114 grams of hash oil,
reported alongside cocaine, methamphetamine, MDMA and carfentanil. [@1] The
prepared materials on these cases were submitted to the Main Department for the
investigation of economic crimes and smuggling of the Investigative Committee.
[@1]

Anti-smuggling officers and the Investigative Committee are taking investigative
and operative-search measures to establish all persons who committed the crimes.
[@1] The same statement separately reported a courier shipment in which a total
of 819 grams of mephedrone was found hidden in parts of a table's packaging.
[@1] It also reported a second courier shipment in which about 250 grams of
synthetic narcotic of the MDMB-022 type was found after dismantling an acoustic
speaker. [@1] The committee said the detections resulted from analysis, targeted
screening and large-scale operative-search measures by its anti-smuggling
officers. [@1]
