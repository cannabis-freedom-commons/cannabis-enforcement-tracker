The Brevard County Grand Jury indicted Christopher J. Hriciso, 18, and Caleb C.
Davis, 17, on one count each of first-degree felony murder and armed robbery
with a firearm. [@1] The indictment was returned on 22 September 2026. [@2]
Prosecutors said the charges stem from the fatal shooting of a 22-year-old man
at Patrick Woodard Memorial Park in Palm Bay on 26 August. [@2] Officers called
to reports of gunfire found the man dead inside his Ford F-450 pickup truck from
a close-range gunshot wound. [@2]

Palm Bay detectives established that the killing was tied to an illicit drug
transaction, the State Attorney's Office said. [@2] Investigators recovered text
messages between Davis's phone and the victim's from shortly before the murder.
[@2] A Snapchat video from a suspect's account showed two masked men, a handgun
and marijuana packaged in a way that matched marijuana packages later found at
the victim's home. [@2] A witness saw a masked man get into the pickup truck
about 30 seconds before the gunshot, Florida Today reported. [@3]

Police arrested Hriciso and Davis, both of Titusville, on 3 September, and both
were held without bond at the Brevard County Jail. [@2] Davis will be charged as
an adult, ClickOrlando reported. [@4] If convicted, the pair could spend the
rest of their lives in state prison, Florida Today reported. [@3]
