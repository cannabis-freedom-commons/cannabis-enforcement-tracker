Customs officers and PDEA agents found about 4.746 kilograms of cannabis in a shipment from Canada at a Pasay warehouse. [@1,2,3,4,5]

Customs officers and anti-drug agents intercepted 15 boxes from Canada holding
high-grade marijuana and methamphetamine at a warehouse in Pasay City. [@1] The
boxes had come from Toronto. [@2] They were declared as personal effects. [@2] A
customs X-ray screening flagged suspicious images in the shipment. [@1] An alert
order was issued and officers carried out a 100% physical examination of the
packages. [@3]

One box held about 4,746 grams of high-grade marijuana, known locally as kush,
which the Bureau of Customs valued at P7.119 million. [@1] Six boxes held 28
cans with about 28,324 grams of shabu, the local name for methamphetamine
hydrochloride, which customs valued at P192.603 million. [@1] Officers also
found 20 towels and 20 duvets that the bureau suspected held liquid
methamphetamine. [@1] Laboratory tests on samples of the marijuana, the shabu
and the liquid methamphetamine were positive for cannabis and methamphetamine
hydrochloride. [@3] The bureau estimated the whole shipment's value at P199.72
million. [@2]

The Bureau of Customs office at NAIA said a deeper investigation was continuing
before charges could be filed against those involved. [@2] The inspection was
carried out with PDEA, the customs intelligence and investigation service, the
customs anti-illegal drug task force, the enforcement and security service, the
X-ray project and the NAIA inter-agency drug interdiction task group. [@1]
Customs Commissioner Ariel Nepomuceno said better technology, sharper
intelligence and coordination with partner agencies were improving the bureau's
ability to stop such shipments before they reached communities. [@3]
