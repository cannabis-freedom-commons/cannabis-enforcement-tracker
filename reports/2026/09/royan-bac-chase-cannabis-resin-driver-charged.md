In the night of Wednesday 9 September 2026, officers of the anti-crime squad,
the Brigade anti criminalité or BAC, tried to stop a van travelling at very high
speed through Royan, and the driver fled. [@1] Cornered in a dead end, the
driver turned round and deliberately drove at the BAC car, damaging its front
end. [@1] The officers said the driver then threw himself onto the knees of one
of his two passengers, apparently hoping to escape responsibility, and he was
arrested and placed in police custody. [@1]

In police custody tests showed he had been driving after alcohol, cocaine and
THC, the psychoactive compound in cannabis. [@1] Police also found a lump of
cannabis resin on him. [@1] The driver admitted he had no driving licence and
denied having hit the police car on purpose, and officers in the BAC car
suffered neck pain. [@1]

He is to be tried by the correctional court of Saintes, which hears less serious
offences. [@2] The account came from a post by the Charente-Maritime police on
their Facebook page. [@1]
