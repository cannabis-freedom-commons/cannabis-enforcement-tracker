Dian Huang Jiang, also known as Danny Jiang, received 60 months in federal
prison in Dallas on 9 September 2026. [@1] U.S. District Judge Sam A. Lindsay
imposed the sentence after Jiang pleaded guilty to conspiring to use
interstate facilities to support a racketeering enterprise. [@1] Jiang
admitted leading a marijuana distribution network operating from at least
January 2020 through August 2023. [@1,2]

Court records described marijuana moving from Oklahoma to customers in Texas
and other states. [@1,2] Jiang used mobile phones to arrange deliveries,
negotiate prices and direct cash payments. [@1] Prosecutors said customers
had to buy between 20 and 300 pounds per transaction, and deliveries could
reach 1,000 pounds in a day. [@1,2] Residences in Oklahoma and Texas were used
to store marijuana, distribute it and collect proceeds. [@1,2]

Federal agents seized more than $2.8 million from linked bank accounts and
safe-deposit boxes on 8 August 2023. [@1] Prosecutors said Jiang also faced
forfeiture of residential properties and more than $4 million in traced drug
proceeds. [@1] The Drug Enforcement Administration led the investigation
with assistance from federal, Oklahoma and Mesquite police agencies. [@1]
