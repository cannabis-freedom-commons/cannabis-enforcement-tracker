The territorial gendarmerie group of Relizane ran the operation as part of its drive against organised crime and drug trafficking, its communication unit said in a statement sent to the APS press agency. [@1]

Gendarmes set up a checkpoint and search post, stopped a suspect vehicle and, searching it thoroughly, found about 22 kg of treated kif carefully concealed inside; the vehicle's owner was arrested. [@1] Further investigation identified and led to the arrest of a second suspect. [@1]

The operation also seized two passenger vehicles used in the criminal activity. [@1,2] Mobile phones and large sums of money described as proceeds of that activity were taken as well. [@1] The two suspects are to be presented to the public prosecutor at the Oued Rhiou court once the legal procedures are complete. [@1]

An Aures TV video report on the operation describes the same seizure of about 22 kg of processed kif and the arrest of two suspects. [@2]
