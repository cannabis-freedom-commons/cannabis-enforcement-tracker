National Police anti-drug agents raided a property in Comayagua under a judicial
order and arrested one man. [@1] The agency said the search was carried out on
judicial authority with support from the DLCN. [@1]

During the search the officers seized 45 wraps of dry plant material suspected
to be marijuana, alongside 299 wraps of white powder suspected to be cocaine
hydrochloride. [@1] Officers also took four mobile phones, 6,000 lempiras (about
230 US dollars) in cash and a digital video recorder for extracting video
material. [@1]

The police published the result as part of Operación Escudo, the National
Police's anti-drug operation. [@1]
