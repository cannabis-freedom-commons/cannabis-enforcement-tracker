Authorities recorded two illegal drug trafficking cases in Andijan and Fergana
regions. [@1] Officers stopped a Nexia in Bulakbashi district and seized 470
grams of hashish from two local residents aged 47 and 41. [@1] The two men were
detained and said the hashish had been brought by contraband from a neighbouring
state. [@2] Criminal cases were opened against those involved and investigative
actions were continuing. [@3]

Another account identifies the two Nexia passengers as local residents born in
1978 and 1985. [@2] In a separate case in Fergana district, officers found 482
grams of dried poppy in the home of a 58-year-old man. [@1] The authorities
described the combined haul from the operations as about one kilogram of
prohibited substances. [@1] The Fergana find was described as 482 grams of opium
poppy, koknori, kept dried. [@3]

The Andijan operation was conducted as a joint operation by Border Troops with
the regional security department and regional police under preventive measures
called Safe and healthy country. [@3] The Fergana house inspection was conducted
as a separate joint operation by Border Troops with the regional security
department and regional police administration. [@3]
