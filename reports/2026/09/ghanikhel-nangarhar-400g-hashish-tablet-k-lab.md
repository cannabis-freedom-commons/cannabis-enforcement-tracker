The Ministry of Interior says an illegal factory producing “K” tablets was
discovered and destroyed in Ghanikhel district of eastern Nangarhar province.
[@1] Anti-narcotics forces of the Nangarhar Police searched a house in Ghanikhel
district during an intelligence operation. [@1] The house was allegedly being
used as a facility for producing “K” tablets. [@1] The ministry announced the
operation in a statement posted on X. [@1]

The operation resulted in the seizure of 400 grams of hashish, alongside 15,110
“K” tablets, four kilograms of raw materials, 320 grams of crystal
methamphetamine, 2,500 litres of liquid chemicals and tablet molds. [@1] The
facility was subsequently destroyed. [@1]

The alleged operator was not present at the site when the house was searched.
[@1] Efforts to identify and arrest the person responsible for the facility are
ongoing. [@1]
