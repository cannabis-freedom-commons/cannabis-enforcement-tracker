Investigators acted on a tip about hashish trafficking in the Unité 26 sector of
Parcelles Assainies on the night of 23 September and went to the scene. [@1]
They arrested the alleged trafficker, who identified himself as A. U. Jalo, born
on 27 May 1995 in Guinea-Bissau, working as a freight forwarder and living in
Medina. [@1] The English version of the same report confirms the arrest,
identifying the man as AU Jalo, born 27 May 1995 in Guinea-Bissau and living in
the Medina district as a freight forwarder. [@2]

At the moment of arrest he was carrying a half-opened slab of hashish. [@1]
Officers took him to his home in Medina, where a room search found another
hashish slab along with 375,000 CFA francs and a 50-euro note. [@1]
Investigators said the cash probably represented proceeds from his illegal
activity. [@1] The English version likewise records the half-opened slab at
arrest and the second slab plus 375,000 CFA francs and a 50-euro note found at
his home. [@2]

Questioned about the source of the drugs, he said they had been entrusted to him
by a person identified only as E., currently in Guinea-Bissau, offering no
further detail in an account investigators found unconvincing. [@1] He was
placed in custody for offering or transferring hashish, and the seized drugs and
money were sealed. [@1] The prosecutor was notified, and the investigation
opened by the Unité 15 Parcelles Assainies district police station was
continuing. [@1]
