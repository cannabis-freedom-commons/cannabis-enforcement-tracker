Berkeley County sheriff's deputies arrested Bryan Edward Korth, 45, and Malquan
Curtis Pimentel, 38, after a three-month investigation into the sale of illegal
narcotics. [@1] The county narcotics task force opened the inquiry in June 2026
after receiving a community tip about suspected drug activity at a house on
University Drive in the College Park area. [@1] On 24 September 2026 the task
force joined the South Carolina Law Enforcement Division and the sheriff's
intelligence and K9 units in a joint operation that executed a search warrant at
the home. [@2] Investigators said Korth received a package containing marijuana
and then met with Pimentel, and deputies detained both men before Pimentel left
the property. [@1]

During the search deputies seized 22.63 pounds of marijuana, about 10.26 kg,
according to the sheriff's office. [@1] Korth was charged with trafficking
marijuana of 10 to 100 pounds, trafficking methamphetamine of 28 to 100 grams
and possession with intent to distribute psilocybin. [@1] Pimentel was charged
with trafficking marijuana of 10 to 100 pounds and possession with intent to
distribute methamphetamine. [@1]

The sheriff's office said its narcotics task force includes members of the
Hanahan, Goose Creek and Moncks Corner police departments as well as sheriff's
deputies. [@2]
