Acting on intelligence about a possible smuggling attempt through incoming Thai Airways flight TG-349 on 25 September, Pakistan Customs at Islamabad International Airport tightened surveillance and set up targeted checks. [@1,2]

A special vigilance team formed under an Assistant Collector intercepted a passenger and, searching him and his baggage under the prescribed legal procedure, recovered 35 kg gross weight of suspected weed. [@1,2] Customs took the contraband, estimated at Rs660 million, into custody and lodged a first information report against the passenger under the relevant legal provisions. [@1,2]

Customs said the operation reflects its drive against cross-border smuggling and for the protection of public health and safety. [@1] Further investigation is under way to establish the source and intended destination of the consignment. [@1,2] Both accounts were published on 27 September 2026. [@1,2]
