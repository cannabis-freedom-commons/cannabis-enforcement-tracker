## The chase

Acting on confidential information received by Excise Intelligence Bureau provincial
in-charge Aakif Nawaz Khan about a large narcotics movement, three special Excise teams
deployed in Peshawar around midnight. [@1] The teams set up a checkpoint near Jahaz
Chowk and signalled a suspicious Toyota Revo to stop; the driver allegedly tried to
escape at high speed and opened fire at the Excise personnel. [@1,2] Officers chased the
vehicle, registered SB-5069 of Sindh, to Double Road near the Peshawar Ring Road, where
the driver kept firing before abandoning the Revo and escaping under cover of
darkness. [@1,2]

## The record haul

A search of the vehicle at the Excise police station in Peshawar recovered 720 kg of
hashish and 240 kg of opium. [@1,2] Officials described the recovery as the largest
single seizure in the history of the department's Excise Intelligence Bureau. [@1,2] A
case was registered at the Excise police station in Peshawar, and the authorities are
working to arrest the escaped suspect and trace others linked to the operation. [@1]
Provincial excise minister Syed Fakhar Jehan called the seizure a major achievement and
praised the Excise Intelligence teams. [@1]
