Railway police took the man into custody on the platform at Khammam railway
station. [@1] He had reached Khammam on 21 September 2026 after boarding the
Bengaluru Express at Mansi railway junction without a ticket. [@1] The man is a
native of Bihar state who worked in a rice mill in Miryalaguda. [@1]

He had used ganja with employees and workers there. [@1] He bought ganja at a
low price in his home area of Nirpur. [@1] He hoped to turn it into a business
to make money. [@1] Officers seized 10 kg of ganja valued at Rs 5 lakh from him
and registered a case. [@1]

The detention came during joint checks by the railway police station and the
Railway Protection Force in which suspects are caught. [@1] Police say 15 cases
have been recorded in the past two years with the accused caught in the Konark
train. [@1] Smugglers mainly buy cheaply in the Bhubaneswar and Berhampur areas
and move the ganja to Mumbai. [@1]

Khammam GRP station house officer Ch. Venkateshwarlu said special drives are
being held between Khammam and Vijayawada stations with special focus on the
Konark train and regular checks. [@1]
