Zambia's Drug Enforcement Commission (DEC) arrested and jointly charged two suspected drug suppliers over 175 kg of dry cannabis carried from Malawi into Zambia through Lumezi district in Eastern Province. [@1,2] The commission named them as Moffat Mlotha, 39, a Malawian national, and Rebecca Nsomba, 53, a Zambian national. [@1,2]

DEC public relations officer Allan Tamba said the two had been on the commission's watch list for transnational organised drug trafficking. [@1,2] They were arrested after the cannabis was found concealed in sack bags and a laptop bag. [@1,2]

The statement was issued in Lusaka on Monday, 21 September 2026. [@1] Tamba said the drugs had been seized and the two were held in lawful custody awaiting court appearances. [@1,2] He described the arrests as a milestone in the commission's Operation Kufeza and asked people living near the border to keep reporting suspicious drug activity. [@1]

No court date was reported. [@1,2]
