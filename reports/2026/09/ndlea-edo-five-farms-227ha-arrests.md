The Edo State Command of the National Drug Law Enforcement Agency destroyed five cannabis farms and arrested three suspected drug traffickers in separate operations across the state. [@1,2] State commander Mitchell Ofoyeju disclosed the results in a statement to journalists in Benin City on 14 September 2026. [@1]

Two farms measuring 0.928744 hectares were at Monday Camp in Usen Forest, Ovia South-West, two covering 1.004278 hectares were at Igwe-Oke Forest in Owan East, and one of 0.336646 hectares was at Ogboha Forest in Owan West. [@1,2] The five plantations covered a combined 2.269668 hectares with an estimated yield of 5,674.17 kg of cannabis. [@1,2] Ofoyeju said the farms were gradually taking over the forests and urged local government chairmen to collaborate against illicit cultivation. [@1]

During the raid on the Ogboha Forest farm, operatives arrested 39-year-old Sunday Segun. [@1] In a separate operation at Bob-Izua Park, New Road, Okada, officers arrested Hassan Mohammed with 7.1 kg of cannabis, three knives and charms. [@1] The command also intercepted a vehicle at Auchi carrying 590 bottles of codeine syrup, 50,000 capsules of Tramadol 100 mg, 100 tablets of Tramadol 225 mg and 25,000 tablets of Diazepam, arresting 25-year-old Jamie Zubaru. [@1]
