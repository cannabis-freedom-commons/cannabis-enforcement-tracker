Algiers police dismantled an international trafficking network and seized 130 kg of treated kif that the authorities described as coming from Morocco on 8 September 2026. [@1,2]

The operation began when the third judicial-police district of El Biar intercepted a utility vehicle near Algiers; follow-up searches at suspects' homes in the capital produced additional quantities. [@1,2] The reports say the police also seized three vehicles and a motorcycle used by the network. [@1,2]

Four people were arrested between Algiers and Maghnia and presented to the prosecutor attached to the Koléa court. [@1,2] The sources do not identify the suspects or report what happened after that presentation. [@1,2]
