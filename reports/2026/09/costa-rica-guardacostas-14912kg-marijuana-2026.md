National Coast Guard director Juan Carlos Alvarado shared the service's
year-to-date maritime anti-drug balance on 26 September 2026. [@1] The National
Coast Guard seized more than 44 tonnes of drugs and 26 vessels in maritime
anti-drug operations so far in 2026. [@1] Those operations also left 66 people
captured, according to the institution's balance cited by Diario Extra. [@1]

Of 44,093 kilos reported by the authorities, 29,180 kilos were identified as
cocaine and 14,912 kilos as marijuana. [@1] CRprensa gave the split more
precisely as 29 tonnes 180 kilos 415 grams of cocaine and 14 tonnes 912 kilos
800 grams of marijuana. [@3] Diario Extra described the same balance as
including 66 people apprehended. [@2]

The 26 seized means of transport comprised 19 speedboats, 6 commercial fishing
vessels and one semi-submersible. [@1] Authorities also seized 19 speedboats,
six commercial fishing boats, one low-profile semi-submersible, 3815 litres of
fuel, 54 engines and one ultralight. [@3] The semi-submersible was intercepted
on 12 June in an operation with United States support. [@1] That vessel carried
more than three tonnes of drugs and was one of the largest seizures in the
annual balance. [@1] Authorities counted 24 interventions in the South Pacific
zone and one operation in the Caribbean. [@1] Alvarado credited the results to
joint information-sharing with agencies including the United States, Panama and
Colombia. [@1]
