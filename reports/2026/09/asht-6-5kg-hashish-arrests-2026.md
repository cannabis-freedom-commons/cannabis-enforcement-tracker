Law-enforcement officers in Tajikistan's Asht district detained members of what
the authorities described as an organised transnational group. [@1] The
investigation focused on 53-year-old Ilmiddin Amonov, a resident of the Shaydoi
jamoat who, according to the investigation, had previously been convicted of
unlawful drug trafficking. [@1] Investigators allege that Amonov organised the
transportation of the prohibited substances together with a citizen of
Afghanistan. [@1] Three brothers — Amriddin, Mukhiddin and Shakhrom Odirmatov —
were also detained by officers in connection with the same alleged trafficking
case. [@1]

Police found 6 kg 500 g of hashish in the detained people's possession,
according to the Interior Ministry. [@1] Preliminary findings indicate that the
seized drugs had been obtained in Afghanistan before entering the alleged onward
trafficking route. [@1] The plan, according to investigators, was to move the
consignment through Tajikistan to destinations outside the country. [@1]

Those detained remain in pretrial detention while the case proceeds. [@1]
Authorities have opened a criminal case over the seizure and say the
investigation is continuing. [@1]
