Shortly after 2 p.m. on a Tuesday, a crew from the territorial anti-crime
brigade was patrolling the new-build neighbourhood in the north of
Ivry-sur-Seine, checking the halls of residential buildings. [@1] Near Allée
Jacques-Laloé, not far from Chinagora, the officers smelled a strong odour of
cannabis but could not tell which flat it came from in a building of about ten
floors. [@1] The officers went back to base, called in the dog unit and set up a
second visit an hour later. [@1] The police dog stopped in front of one
particular door, identifying the suspect flat. [@1] An officer rang the bell
shouting “Police”, nobody answered, and police forced the door with a battering
ram, needing two blows to break it open. [@1] The flat was empty of any
occupant, and inside police found bags full of drugs in the passageway leading
to the living room. [@1]

Police seized almost 37 kilos of cannabis, made up of 36 kilos of resin and 1
kilo of herbal cannabis. [@1] At retail, the product was valued at more than
300,000 euros. [@2]

Nobody had been arrested in the case at the time of reporting. [@1] The
investigation was taken over by the territorial security service, whose
anti-drug brigade is described as long renowned. [@1]
