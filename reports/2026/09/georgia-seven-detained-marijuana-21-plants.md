Officers of the Interior Ministry's Central Criminal Police Department detained
seven people on drug charges in Georgia. [@1] Those detained included both
Georgian citizens and foreign nationals. [@2] They were accused of illegal
acquisition and storage of drugs and psychotropic substances in an especially
large amount, facilitation of their sale, and illegal cultivation of
drug-containing plants. [@1] Police said some of those detained were exposed
through a controlled substitution of parcels addressed to them, after which
especially large drug batches were seized as evidence. [@2]

Searches of the accused, their temporary homes and locations they named
recovered batches prepared for sale in an especially large amount. [@1] The
cannabis component was up to 4 kilograms of marijuana and 21 cannabis plants.
[@2] Officers also seized mephedrone, methadone and 323 pregabalin tablets,
which are not counted as cannabis. [@1] Investigators also seized packaging
materials and a firearm. [@1]

The investigation is proceeding under Criminal Code articles 260, 260-4, 19-260,
261, 265-1, 273 and 236. [@1] Those charges carry up to 20 years in prison or
life imprisonment. [@2]
