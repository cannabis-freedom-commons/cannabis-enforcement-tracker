Officers of the National Drug Law Enforcement Agency (NDLEA) raided and
destroyed 5,000 kg of skunk on a two-hectare cannabis farm in Ita-Ogbolu
forest, Ondo State, on Tuesday 15 September 2026. [@1,2]

Four suspects were arrested at the farm: Blessing Effiong, Saviour Peter,
David Friday Sunday and Ability Peter Sunday. [@1,2] Officers recovered 48
kg of dry and fresh cannabis from the location for the prosecution of the
suspects. [@1,2]

The raid was one of a string of operations the agency reported across Lagos,
Edo, Niger, Delta, Ondo, Gombe, Imo and the Federal Capital Territory, with
about 6,000 kg of cannabis and more than 1.3 million pills of tramadol and
other opioids seized at a combined street value above N3.4 billion. [@1,2]
