On 9 September 2026, a 27-year-old man who had missed a stop sign was found with
drugs and a large sum of money on board, and was later sentenced by the
Bourg-en-Bresse criminal court. [@1] Thoiry gendarmes stopped the man at
Saint-Genis-Pouilly for ignoring a stop sign. [@1] The car he was driving was
uninsured and belonged to a friend. [@1]

At Saint-Genis-Pouilly the driver was arrested for driving under the influence
of drugs. [@2] Follow-up checks seized more than 2,200 euros, more than 600
grams of cannabis and cocaine. [@2]

He was then brought in and placed in provisional detention pending his trial.
