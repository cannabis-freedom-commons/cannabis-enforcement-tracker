Two men, aged 29 and 30, were arrested on suspicion of drug trafficking in
Criciúma on the night of Thursday 10 September 2026. [@2] Military police
stopped their car at a traffic checkpoint on Rua Arcângelo Meller in the Santa
Augusta neighbourhood at about 21h20. [@1] During a personal search officers
found 910 reais in cash with one of the occupants. [@1] A search of the car
turned up approximately 1.5 kg of marijuana and two mobile phones. [@1]

The driver refused the breathalyser test and officers found that his driving
licence was suspended. [@1] Both men were arrested for drug trafficking and
taken to the Civil Police station with the car and the seized material. [@4]
Both were arrested in the act and were held at the disposal of the police
authority. [@2] The car, the money, the phones and the marijuana were sent to
the police station with them. [@3]
