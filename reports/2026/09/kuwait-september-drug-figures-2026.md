Kuwait's General Department for Drug Control recorded 160 drug cases involving
205 suspects between 1 and 30 September 2026 and referred them to prosecution.
[@1] The 205 detained suspects comprised 131 in use cases, 47 in trafficking
cases, 11 in import cases and 16 in drug misdemeanour cases. [@1] Authorities
deported 194 expatriates of various nationalities during the same monthly
crackdown. [@2] The agency also logged 52 addiction complaints, 43 from males
and 9 from females, as part of its security and treatment work. [@1] The cases
comprised 104 use cases, 29 trafficking cases, 15 drug misdemeanours, 8 import
cases and 4 cases against unknown persons. [@1]

Seized cannabis recorded here totalled about 18.8 kg, comprising 14 kg and 495 g
of hashish and 4 kg and 308 g of marijuana. [@1] The same operation also seized
about 724,946 pills, more than 62 kg of shabu and 5.159 kg of heroin, which are
not counted as cannabis. [@1] The same seizure account also listed 6 g of
chemical drugs and 305 g of cocaine, which are likewise not counted as cannabis.
[@1] Officers also seized a Kalashnikov, four pistols, three shotguns and an air
rifle in the same cases. [@1]

The statistics were issued under director Brigadier Mohammed Qabazard and his
deputy Brigadier Sheikh Hamad Al-Youssef. [@1] A security source said the
results reflected field work, investigations and monitoring of suspicious
activity to identify and detain those involved. [@1] A second account of the
same September statistics likewise reported 160 drug-related cases involving 205
suspects. [@2]
