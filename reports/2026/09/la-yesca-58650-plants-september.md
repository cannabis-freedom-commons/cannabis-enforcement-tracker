Mexican forces located and eradicated two cannabis fields in La Yesca municipality, Nayarit, on 23 September 2026. [@1,2] The operation also disabled a nearby drying site. [@1,2]

The fields covered about 9,750 square metres, or 0.975 hectares, and contained approximately 58,650 plants. [@1,2] Authorities destroyed the crops through manual labour and incineration; they also destroyed about 320 kilograms of cannabis found at the drying site. [@1,2]

The Navy worked with the Army, National Guard and Secretariat of Security and Citizen Protection during surveillance patrols and ground reconnaissance. [@1,2] The government published its account on 29 September, six days after the operation, and NTV reported the same figures that day. [@1,2]

Neither source reports whether anyone was arrested or gives the fields' locations beyond the municipality. [@1,2]
