Officers from the Ketou district police station investigating a reported
burglary identified a suspect and traced him to a hideout in the Obafemi quarter
of Ketou. [@2] The suspect was found in a ghetto in Obafemi and physically
resisted the officers who came to arrest him before being overpowered after an
intense confrontation. [@1] Police arrested the wanted burglary suspect during
the operation. [@2]

A search of the premises then uncovered 21.5 kg of Indian hemp, the term used
locally for cannabis. [@1] Officers also seized three motorcycles found at the
site, which Republican Police described as being of doubtful or suspect origin.
[@1] Police presented the operation as both the arrest and the dismantling of a
significant drug-dealing point. [@2]

Police said investigations were continuing to establish the origin of the
motorcycles and the wider ramifications of the network in the region. [@2] No
court outcome had been reported, and the quantity or nature of the goods stolen
in the initial burglary had not been detailed. [@1]
