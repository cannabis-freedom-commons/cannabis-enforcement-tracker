Ken Yiu, 49, of Brooklyn, New York, received a time-served prison sentence
for mortgage fraud connected to a marijuana grow house in Saint Albans,
Maine. [@1,2,3] U.S. District Judge Stacey D. Neumann sentenced him in Bangor
on 30 September 2026 to one day already served and three years of supervised
release. [@1,2] He had pleaded guilty on 10 December 2025. [@1]

Prosecutors said Yiu obtained an $80,000 residential mortgage by claiming he
intended to live at the property. [@1,2] Instead, he used it to grow and
distribute marijuana from September 2020 through January 2024. [@1,2] Neither
Yiu nor the property held a Maine cannabis licence. [@1,2,3] He admitted to
federal agents that he sold marijuana grown there to buyers in Massachusetts.
[@1,2]

Federal agents searched the house in January 2025. [@1,2] The Morning
Sentinel reported that prosecutors dismissed a bank-fraud count in exchange
for his guilty pleas. [@2] Yiu sold the house before sentencing and paid off
the mortgage, so prosecutors did not seek restitution. [@2] His lawyer did
not respond to the newspaper's request for comment. [@2]
