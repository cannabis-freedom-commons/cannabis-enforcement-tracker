According to police, Eric S. Cilluffo, 27, is a resident of Gloucester, MA. [@1]
Officer Marino said his observations led him to arrest Cilluffo for operating a
motor vehicle under the influence of liquor. [@1] Cilluffo was charged with
possess to distribute a Class D drug, identified as marijuana. [@1] He was also
charged with OUI-liquor of .08%. [@1] He was also charged with two counts of
possessing an open container of alcohol in a motor vehicle. [@1] Cilluffo was
arraigned in Lawrence District Court on Sept. 4, 2026 and released on $1,000
personal surety with a SCRAM bracelet requiring him to remain drug and alcohol
free. [@1]

On Sept. 3, 2026, at about 7:41 p.m., Andover officers were dispatched to
Dascomb and Frontage roads for a driver reported passed out behind the wheel.
[@1] A witness said the car drove slowly westbound on Dascomb Road toward
Tewksbury, ran over the median and stopped in the grass with the driver
apparently asleep. [@1] In a vehicle inventory before towing, officers found in
the trunk a black multi-camo bag holding Mason jars full of suspected marijuana
flower. [@1] The final inventory listed four XL Mason jars containing suspected
marijuana flower. [@1] It also listed one normal sized Mason jar containing
suspected marijuana flower. [@1] It also listed 29 small approximately one-gram
jars of suspected marijuana wax concentrate. [@1] It also listed two jars
labeled Blue Punch and Honey Milk suspected to contain marijuana wax
concentrate. [@1] In the driver's door officers found a small baggie of ground
marijuana and three one-gram wax concentrate jars. [@1] Officers said the amount
of marijuana, with small and broken-up packaging found in other parts of the
car, appeared not to be for personal use and was indicative of distribution.
[@1]

The lab inventory included a small baggie with pink powdery residue believed to
be MDMA. [@1] It also included a tinfoil pack with 20.6 grams of a white powdery
substance believed to be cocaine. [@1] During booking Cilluffo consented to an
evidentiary breath test and blew a 0.10% blood alcohol concentration reading.
[@1] A notice alleging violation of release conditions was later filed, with a
hearing held Oct. 15, 2026 that Cilluffo attended. [@1] His next court date was
a pretrial hearing scheduled for Nov. 2, 2026. [@1]
