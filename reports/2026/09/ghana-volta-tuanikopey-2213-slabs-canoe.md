## The interception

The Tema Regional Police Command, through its Marine Police Detachment at Ada,
intercepted an outboard-powered wooden canoe on the River Volta at Tuanikopey Island at
about 00:45 on Thursday 17 September 2026 during a routine patrol. [@1,2] The canoe's
occupants fled when they saw the patrol, abandoning the vessel and its cargo. [@1,2]

## The cargo

A search of the canoe found 29 sacks holding 2,213 slabs of dried leaves suspected to be
Indian hemp, wrapped in yellow cellophane. [@1,2] Officers also recovered fourteen
25-litre gallons, twelve of them filled with pre-mixed fuel, and two Yamaha outboard
motors rated at 40 and 30 horsepower. [@1,2] The suspected narcotics were conveyed to
the Ada District Police headquarters, and the motors and fuel were being transferred
there as evidence for the investigation. [@1,2]

## The hunt

The police command said it was working to identify and apprehend the people who abandoned
the canoe and its contents. [@1,2] It cautioned the public against trafficking and
transporting narcotics, and asked anyone with knowledge of the canoe's owners or
occupants to contact the nearest police station. [@2]
