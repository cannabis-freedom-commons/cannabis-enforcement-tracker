Customs officers in Tlemcen, working with the People's National Army (ANP),
seized 75 kg of treated kif and arrested three people. [@1]

The regional customs directorate in Tlemcen announced the operation in a
communiqué on Tuesday 15 September 2026. [@1] The operation was carried out
by the regional mobile anti-drug brigade of customs, together with the
Ghazaouet customs mobile brigade and ANP units of the 2nd Military Region,
after an ambush laid in Tlemcen wilaya. [@1]

The three arrested people were travelling in a vehicle carrying the drug.
[@1] The suspects were detained and brought before the competent judicial
authorities. [@1]
