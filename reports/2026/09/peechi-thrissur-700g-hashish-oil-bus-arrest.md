Police seized around 700 g of hashish oil allegedly being smuggled on an
interstate bus and arrested a 27-year-old man in connection with the case. [@1]
The arrested man has been identified as Shyam Shashi (27) of Ernakulam. [@1] He
was taken into custody during a vehicle inspection jointly conducted by the
DANSAF team and Peechi police as part of the ongoing 'Operation Thufan'
anti-drug drive on Saturday. [@1]

Police said Shyam had previously worked as a bus cleaner and helper in Ernakulam
city. [@1] For the past year he had reportedly been working as a cleaner with a
Bengaluru-based travel company. [@1] According to the police, he allegedly
exploited his familiarity with interstate bus operations and used the buses as a
means of transporting the contraband while taking advantage of the relative
cover provided during travel. [@1]

The police are investigating the source of the seized hashish oil and its
intended recipients. [@1] A detailed probe is also under way to identify other
links allegedly involved in the interstate drug trafficking network. [@1]
