Drug-control officers of the Interior Ministry and State Customs Committee
officers detained a 54-year-old resident of the Minsk region in a joint special
operation. [@1] Investigators say the man took part in organising supplies of
drugs into Belarus from the Republic of Lithuania. [@1] Fighters of the Almaz
special-purpose unit provided force support for the operation. [@1]

The load was moved in a hiding place fitted into the semi-trailer of a heavy
goods vehicle. [@1] Officers found and seized 38 kg of hashish from that hiding
place. [@1]

Customs opened a criminal case over the unlawful movement of drugs across the
Belarusian state border. [@1] The offence carries up to 10 years in prison, a
possible penalty rather than a sentence already handed down. [@1]
