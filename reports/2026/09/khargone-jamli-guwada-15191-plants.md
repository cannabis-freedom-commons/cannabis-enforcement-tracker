Khargone police carried out two raids against illegal cannabis cultivation in the Chainpur area of Helapadava police station on 27 September, as part of Operation Prahar. [@1] The teams used drones to find the fields in Jamli village and Guwada Thatta Phalia, both in a shadow area without mobile network coverage, then crossed rivers and mountainous terrain to reach them. [@1,2]

Officers seized 15,191 cannabis plants weighing 5,974 kg, which police valued at about Rs 2.98 crore. [@1,2] At Jamli they took 9,391 plants weighing 3,765 kg and worth about Rs 1.88 crore, and registered Crime No. 311/2026 under Section 8/20 of the NDPS Act against Dangaliya, Madhu and Sukhlal, all residents of Jamli. [@1] At Guwada Thatta Phalia they took 5,800 plants weighing 2,209 kg and worth about Rs 1.10 crore, registering an NDPS case against Jagir, a resident of Guwada. [@1]

The joint teams drew personnel from Chainpur, Bhikangaon, Bistan and Gogawan stations and the Khargone Police Line, acting on informer inputs. [@1] Officers spent several hours uprooting the plants. [@1]
