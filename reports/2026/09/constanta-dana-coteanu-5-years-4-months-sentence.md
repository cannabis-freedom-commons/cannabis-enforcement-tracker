On 4 September 2026, the Constanța Tribunal sentenced former singer Dana
Coteanu, known publicly as Dana Marijuana, to 5 years and 4 months in prison for
trafficking high-risk drugs and for driving a vehicle under the influence of
alcohol or other substances. [@3] In the same case, Cristi-Alin Neagu received 6
years and 3 months for trafficking risk drugs and for possessing risk drugs for
his own use. [@1] Mugur-Valentin Nicula was acquitted of complicity in
trafficking risk drugs, because the court found no proof he committed that act,
and was sentenced to 6 years and 4 months for trafficking high-risk drugs and
for possessing risk drugs for his own use. [@1] The tribunal's decision was not
final. [@3]

DIICOT Constanța, the organised-crime prosecutors' directorate, had sent Coteanu
for trial for trafficking risk drugs, in continued form, and for driving under
the influence of alcohol or other substances. [@4] In the tribunal's written
reasons she said she did not like people knowing she was a cannabis user,
because she was seen as a public person. [@2] She told investigators that
gatherings of about 20 people smoked marijuana more often, and that she also
took speed. [@2] She said those present sold or gave the substance to one
another. [@2] She said a product that was not hers was in her car. [@2] She said
she had used the banned substance to treat her own body. [@2]

The sentence was appealed to the Constanța Court of Appeal, which listed a first
appeal hearing for 11 November 2026. [@1] On 13 October 2026 the appeal court
replaced her house arrest with judicial supervision for 60 days, running until
11 December 2026. [@2] That ruling also covered the other two defendants. [@2]
