Soldiers of the General Botha Regiment's Platoon 1 Charlie Section, deployed
under Operation Corona in the Free State, attempted to stop a suspicious vehicle
during a night operation at Tsupane. [@1] The driver abandoned the vehicle and
fled on foot, leaving behind six black bags containing suspected marijuana. [@1]
Four suspects who later arrived at the scene to collect the drugs were arrested
after allegedly attempting to bribe the soldiers. [@1]

The suspected marijuana weighed approximately 101,65 kg. [@1] The suspects and
the seized drugs were handed over to the South African Police Service in Wepener
for further investigation. [@1] Those arrested were handed over to SAPS at
Wepener. [@2]

The incidents highlight ongoing efforts by the SANDF and SAPS to combat crime
and strengthen border security under Operation Corona. [@1]
