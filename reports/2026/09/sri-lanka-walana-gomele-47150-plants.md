On the morning of 30 September 2026, officers of the Walana Central Anti-Corruption Striking Unit
raided a cannabis cultivation at Gomele Mav-Ara in the Meegahagamuwa police division, acting on
received intelligence. [@1,2] They found about half an acre (about 0.2 hectares) planted with
47,150 cannabis plants. [@1,2]

A 53-year-old man from Udagama was arrested with the plants, and the suspect and the seized crop
were handed to Meegahagamuwa police for further legal proceedings. [@1,2] Meegahagamuwa police
are carrying out further investigations. [@1,2]
