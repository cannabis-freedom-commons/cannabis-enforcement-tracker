Chandoura police seized about Rs 1.19 crore worth of ganja from an overturned truck at Ghat Pendari on the Ambikapur-Banaras road in Surajpur district. [@1,2] The truck, registration UP 13 T-5497, had overturned, and a man named Sunil Kumar was lifting it with a crane while sacks unloaded from it were hidden in a roadside pit covered with a tarpaulin. [@1]

Acting on a 30 September tip about the suspicious activity, police reached the spot and found nine sacks holding 238 kg of ganja concealed among plastic-granule sacks. [@1] The English roundup gives the weight as 238.290 kg. [@2] Police said the ganja was being smuggled under the cover of plastic granules and would have reached its destination had the truck not overturned. [@1]

Police arrested the truck driver, produced him in court, and he was sent to jail. [@1] The case was part of a district drive against narcotics ordered by the Surajpur superintendent of police. [@1]
