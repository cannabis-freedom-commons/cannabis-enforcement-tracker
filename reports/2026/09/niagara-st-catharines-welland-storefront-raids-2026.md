Ontario Provincial Police opened an investigation that linked two unauthorized
Niagara businesses to people suspected of trafficking illegal products. [@1]
Officers used search warrants to search storefronts in St. Catharines and
Welland. [@1] The searches of the St. Catharines and Welland storefronts took
place in June and August during a summer enforcement push. [@1] Three people
were arrested during the summer months as police cracked down on illegally
operating Niagara storefronts. [@1]

At the storefronts officers seized thousands of dollars' worth of suspected
illegal cannabis alongside other controlled substances. [@1] The seized
suspected products included psilocybin. [@1] The seized suspected products also
included lysergic acid diethylamide, commonly abbreviated as LSD. [@1] The
seized suspected products also included dimethyltryptamine, commonly abbreviated
as DMT. [@1]

A summary of the operation valued the seized product at roughly $69,000 and said
cash was also seized. [@1] The detailed account valued illegal cannabis and
controlled substances together at roughly $50,000 for the summer seizures. [@1]
