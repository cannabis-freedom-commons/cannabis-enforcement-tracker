Marion County deputies arrested a man after a home confinement check found
multiple marijuana plants at his home. [@1] The man was identified as Michael
Jack, 49, of Idamay. [@1] The Marion County Sheriff’s Department says Jack is
charged with cultivation of marijuana. [@1] Jack is being held in the North
Central Regional Jail with bond set at $50,000. [@1]

During the check, a deputy entered Jack’s bedroom and found a large tent
consistent with an indoor marijuana grow. [@1] Inside the tent was a pot of soil
bearing a plant label. [@1] Behind it sat another pot containing a stem and
leaves. [@1] Deputies found a digital scale in the same bedroom. [@1] A further
search uncovered four 5-gallon buckets in an under-stairs closet fitted with
grow lights, ventilation and a fan. [@1]

According to court documents, Jack first said the plant was a tomato, then said
it was CBD, before admitting it was marijuana. [@1]
