Piaseczno criminal police identified a property in Legionowo County as possibly
hiding drugs. [@1] Officers found only the property owner at first, then two men
of interest to the criminal division arrived minutes later. [@1] One of the men
rented a room on that property. [@1] Both men were detained. [@1] The
38-year-old was charged with possessing a significant quantity of narcotics and
preparing to manufacture them, and a court remanded him for three months. [@1]
The 33-year-old was charged with possessing narcotics and placed under police
supervision. [@1]

Officers searched rooms occupied by the 38-year-old and seized over one kilogram
of amphetamine, ecstasy tablets and marijuana. [@1] At the second man's
residence officers found more drugs: marijuana, amphetamine and methamphetamine.
[@1] Officers said objects and devices in the room appeared usable for preparing
or producing drugs. [@1]

They seized a tableting machine, mixers, a dust mask, electronic scales, bags
and foil sealers. [@1] The District Prosecutor's Office in Legionowo supervises
the case. [@1] Both men were brought before the District Prosecutor's Office in
Legionowo. [@2]
