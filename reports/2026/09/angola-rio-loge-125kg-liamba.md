Police stopped a 43-year-old woman at the Rio Loge Grande inspection post in
Zaire province during a routine check. [@1]

In the load she was carrying they found 125 kg of cannabis, known in Angola as
liamba, hidden inside sacks of múcua, a cassava flour. [@1]

She was arrested. [@1] Investigators are trying to establish where the drug came
from and where it was going. [@1]

No charge or court appearance has been reported. [@1]
