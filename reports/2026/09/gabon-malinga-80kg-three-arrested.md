The Gabonese national gendarmerie seized 80 kilograms of cannabis, referred to
in the French-language report as chanvre indien, during an enforcement operation
at Malinga. [@1] Three suspects were arrested in the same operation. [@1]
Malinga is located in the southern part of Gabon, close to the border with the
Republic of Congo, where the interception took place. [@1] National radio first
reported the seizure on Tuesday. [@1] KOACI then published its detailed account
of the case on Wednesday 16 September 2026. [@1]

At the time of the interception, the suspects were travelling aboard a vehicle
that was also carrying foodstuffs. [@1] Officers reported that the seized
cannabis had been divided into 225 separate bundles. [@1] Authorities estimated
the market value of the 80-kilogram haul at nearly 10 million CFA francs,
equivalent to about 17,600 dollars. [@1]

The seized cargo was placed under seal. [@1] The public prosecutor ordered the
opening of an investigation to determine the origin of the drug and to establish
any individual responsibilities. [@1]
