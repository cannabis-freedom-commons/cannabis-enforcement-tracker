Prosecutors of Romania's DIICOT central structure, working with Bucharest airport border police,
broke up a group suspected of organized risk-drug trafficking after searches linked to indoor
cannabis cultivation in Singureni, Giurgiu county. [@1,2]

They said that between June and September 2026 four men, aged 29 to 43, acted under a
43-year-old leader to import cannabis seeds from Czechia by air courier, then set up and tended
indoor grows for sale. [@1,2] On 8 September 2026 prosecutors ordered all four detained; searches
recovered more than 90 cannabis plants, about 4 kilograms of cannabis, about 38 kilograms of
dried plant material in sacks, buds and grow equipment. [@1,2]

On 9 September a Bucharest judge placed them in 30 days' preventive arrest, the agencies
reported. [@1,2] Gendarmes supported the action, and the releases noted that suspects remain
presumed innocent pending trial. [@1,2]
