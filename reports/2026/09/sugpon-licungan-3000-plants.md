Police and anti-drug units uprooted and destroyed an estimated 600,000 pesos' worth of fully grown marijuana plants at a plantation in Sugpon on 27 September. [@1,2] The plantation stood at the boundary of Sitio Ogiyet in Barangay Licungan, Sugpon, and Barangay Tacadang in Kibungan, Benguet, according to the Ilocos Sur police. [@1]

The site covered about 1,000 square metres and held about 3,000 fully grown plants, valued at an estimated 600,000 pesos at the standard drug price. [@1,2] The operating teams came from the 2nd Provincial Mobile Force Company, PDEA Region 1, the provincial drug enforcement and intelligence units and Sugpon police. [@1] They uprooted the plants, completed an inventory, evaluation and documentation, and burned the haul at the site. [@1]

The provincial police said the operation forms part of its continuing campaign with partner agencies against marijuana cultivation in Ilocos Sur and nearby areas. [@1]
