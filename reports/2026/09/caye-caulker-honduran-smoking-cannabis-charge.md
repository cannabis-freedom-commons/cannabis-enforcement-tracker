Police on Caye Caulker charged a 28-year-old Honduran labourer with smoking
cannabis in public, police information said. [@1] Officers were conducting
mobile patrol in the Bahia area of Caye Caulker Village at around 7:30 a.m. on 9
September when they stopped a man on a bicycle smoking what appeared to be a
brown cigarette. [@1] Police said they smelled a strong aroma of cannabis and
saw smoke coming from the cigarette. [@1] The cigarette was retrieved and police
said it contained a green leafy substance suspected to be cannabis with a burnt
tip. [@1]

The man was identified as Brayan Sosa, 28, a Honduran labourer living in the
Bahia area. [@1] Sosa was taken to the Caye Caulker Police Station and formally
charged with smoking cannabis in public. [@1]

He was granted station bail and is scheduled to appear in the San Pedro
Magistrate Court on Tuesday, 15 September. [@1]
