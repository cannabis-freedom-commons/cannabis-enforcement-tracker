Anti-drugs police found 27.5 kg of ketamine, 377 g of cannabis, 776 g of
mephedrone, 269 g of MDMA and 172 g of cocaine in a flat in Limassol. [@1] The
two occupants of the flat, men aged 23 and 24, were arrested there and accused
of attacking officers during the arrest, slightly injuring one. [@1] The injured
officer, a member of YKAN, Cyprus's anti-narcotics unit, had swelling to the
lips and redness in one eye and was discharged after first aid. [@1] Searches
that followed turned up about €10,000 in cash. [@1] The police listed the
cannabis as 377 g of κάνναβη among the drugs recovered from the flat. [@2]

YKAN officers working with the Intelligence Management and Analysis Service
searched the flat at about 11.30am after a tip-off, while the two men were
there. [@1] A Cyprus court ordered the two men detained for eight days for the
investigation. [@2] Members of YKAN, the anti-narcotics unit, found the ketamine
in three bags and a travel suitcase inside the flat. [@2]
