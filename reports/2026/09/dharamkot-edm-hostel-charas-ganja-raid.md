Kangra Police raided an alleged rave party at the EDM Hostel in Upper Dharamkot
during the night between 13 and 14 September 2026. [@1] Acting on a tip-off, a
team from the McLeodganj police station searched the hostel in the early hours
of 14 September. [@1] Police registered a case under the Narcotic Drugs and
Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act against Aamir Khan, 35, a Madhya Pradesh
resident staying at the hostel, and Radhesh Ranjan Pajoria, 37, of Chittorgarh
in Rajasthan, the hostel's leaseholder and owner. [@1]

Fifteen people at the premises were medically examined as part of the
proceedings. [@1] The search recovered 1.3 grams of charas, a hand-rubbed
cannabis resin, and 1.5 grams of ganja, alongside 1 gram of heroin and 3.7 grams
of opium. [@1] Police gave the measured weights as 1.33 grams of charas and 1.54
grams of cannabis flowers. [@2] Preliminary investigation indicated that rave
parties were hosted at the hostel on 13 and 14 September and promoted through
social media platforms and closed online groups. [@1] Police said they had
collected screenshots and other digital evidence and were examining it. [@1]

Upper Dharamkot, about 3 km from McLeodganj, is known locally as Kangra's "Mini
Israel" for the number of tourists it attracts from Israel. [@1] Police officers
reportedly bought entry tickets costing Rs 2,000 per person for the events on
both days to gather evidence before moving in. [@3] DIG (Northern Range) Soumya
Sambasivam said police would continue stringent action against people involved
in the possession, supply, distribution and consumption of illicit drugs, and
against establishments used for such activity. [@3] Police said their
intervention disrupted the alleged rave party and prevented further illegal
activity at the hostel. [@4]
