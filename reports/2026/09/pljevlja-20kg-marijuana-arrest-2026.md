Pljevlja police located and arrested E.S., a 39-year-old Montenegrin citizen
from Pljevlja. [@1] The arrest was made on suspicion that he transported about
20 kilograms of marijuana from Montenegro to Serbia in February 2024. [@1] After
detention in Pljevlja, he was taken to the Higher State Prosecutor's Office in
Bijelo Polje for further proceedings on the order of the competent state
prosecutor. [@1]

Police suspect he committed unauthorised production, possession and distribution
of narcotic drugs by illegally moving the marijuana for further sale on 19
February 2024. [@1] The marijuana was divided into several vacuum-sealed
packages and hidden in a specially built space at the rear of the vehicle he was
driving, under the seat. [@2] The marijuana was found and seized when Serbian
authorities searched the vehicle. [@1] Police said the criminal proceedings in
the case will be conducted in Montenegro. [@1]

The detention was carried out on the prosecutor's order under international
legal assistance in criminal matters, after Serbian authorities asked Montenegro
to take over the prosecution. [@2] The Police Directorate said the operation
confirms the importance of efficient international police and judicial
cooperation against cross-border crime. [@1]
