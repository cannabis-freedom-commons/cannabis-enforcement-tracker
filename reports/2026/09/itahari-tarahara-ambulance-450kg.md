At about 11:30 PM on Tuesday night, police tried to stop an ambulance
belonging to Triveni Cultural Group, registration Ba. 2 Cha 9573, near the
Panipiya forest in Tarahara, Itahari Sub-Metropolitan City-20. [@1] The driver
fled after the stop attempt, and police pursued the vehicle until it was found
abandoned. [@1,2]

Inside, officers found 450 kg of marijuana, described as packed in plastic
bundles and, in the roundup account, in 15 sacks. [@1,2] Police took the
ambulance and the drugs into custody at the Area Police Office in Itahari, and
the reports name no arrests. [@1,2]
