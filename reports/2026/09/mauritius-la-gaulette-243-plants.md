## The sweep

Anti-Drug and Smuggling Unit officers carried out a sweep of abandoned land at La
Gaulette on Tuesday 15 September 2026 and found 243 cannabis plants measuring 5 to 55
cm tall. [@1] The identity of the person growing the plants is unknown, and the
inquiry continues. [@1]
