A 20-year-old man appeared before South Cumbria Magistrates' Court over
possession of cannabis and drug-related paraphernalia after he tried to run from
police officers on foot patrol. [@1] Officers pursued him on the evening of
September 11 after finding two males 'acting suspiciously'. [@1]

A search of the defendant turned up cannabis vapes and cannabis buds. [@1] A
later search of his home at Storey Square in Barrow found scales, three
grinders, zip lock bags and further cannabis buds. [@1]

He pleaded guilty at the first opportunity to possession of Class B cannabis.
[@1] Representing himself, he told the magistrates there was no point producing
weighing scales and grinders because he was not a dealer and was only there for
simple possession. [@1] The prosecutor said possessing such paraphernalia was
consistent with people who use cannabis. [@1]

The magistrates fined him £415 at band C and ordered him to pay a £166 victim
surcharge and £85 in court costs. [@1]
