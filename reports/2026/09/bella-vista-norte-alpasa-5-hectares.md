Agents of the Regional 10 office of Paraguay's National Anti-Drug Secretariat
(Senad) in Bella Vista Norte carried out rural incursions in the Alpasa area of
Bella Vista Norte, Amambay department. [@1] The operation was directed by
anti-drug prosecutor Rossana Isabel Coronel. [@1] Última Hora described Alpasa
as an area where groups of marijuana growers repeatedly enter to plant the crop
illegally. [@1]

On the Wednesday of the operation the agents eliminated about 15 tonnes of
marijuana. [@1] Tracks and paths through the forest led the teams to about five
hectares of marijuana crops. [@1] They also found a camp used by those running
the production. [@1] The eradicated area corresponded to approximately 15,000
kilograms destroyed during the procedure. [@1]

The party was to keep working in the area under the Public Prosecutor's Office,
which this time ordered everything found at the site destroyed. [@1] The raids
took place in an area the account describes as a recurring and central part of
the illegal production chain. [@1]
