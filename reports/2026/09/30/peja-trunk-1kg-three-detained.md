Kosovo Police in Peja arrested three people on the evening of 30 September 2026 after a vehicle
check. [@1,2] The suspects were two Kosovar men and an Albanian woman travelling together. [@1,2]

Officers found and confiscated about 1 kilogram of suspected marijuana-type narcotic in the
trunk of the car. [@1,2] They also seized cash, three mobile phones, two knives and the vehicle
as material evidence. [@1,2] By prosecutor’s decision all three were sent to custody while
investigations continued. [@1,2]
