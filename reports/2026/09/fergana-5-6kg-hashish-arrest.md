State Security Service officers in Fergana Region disrupted an alleged attempt
to sell 5.6 kg of hashish said to have been smuggled from Kyrgyzstan and
detained a 33-year-old man. [@1] The security service conducted the operation
jointly with the regional customs and internal-affairs departments. [@1]

The suspect collected the drug from a hiding place and drove a Cobalt car toward
Fergana. [@1] When officers tried to stop the car, the driver disobeyed their
orders. [@1] While trying to flee, he threw a household sack from the car and
attempted to escape. [@2] Officers found five bricks of a hashish-like substance
in the sack, with a total weight of 5.6 kg. [@1]

The agency said the drug had previously been smuggled from Kyrgyzstan and placed
in the cache, and treated the detention as preventing its further sale. [@1] A
criminal case was opened against the man under part 2 of Article 246 of the
Criminal Code, and he was remanded in custody. [@1] The investigation is working
to identify possible accomplices and other circumstances of the case. [@1]
