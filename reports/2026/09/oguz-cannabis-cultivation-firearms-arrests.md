Azerbaijan's Interior Ministry press service said Oğuz District Police carried
out operations against people trafficking and cultivating drugs and illegally
keeping firearms. [@2] The police account published in Azerbaijani gives the
third man's surname as T.Haşımov, while the Russian-language report spells it
T.Gaşimov. [@1]

Drugs, cannabis plants the men had cultivated and firearms were found on them.
[@2] A criminal case was opened over the findings. [@2] A court ordered all
three men held in pre-trial detention. [@1]

APA reported the operation under the headline that three people had been
detained over drug trafficking in Oğuz. [@3] The four reports were all published
on 30 September 2026. [@3] Azerbaijan's Interior Ministry is the authority that
publicised the arrests. [@4]
