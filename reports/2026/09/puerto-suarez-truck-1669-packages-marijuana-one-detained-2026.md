Over 1.2 tonnes of marijuana were seized in an anti-drug operation in the border
municipality of Puerto Suárez, Santa Cruz, after officers found the drug being
transported in a truck with a final destination of Brazil. [@1]

Officers found 1,669 packages of marijuana weighing a total of 1,240 kilograms,
hidden between bags, pallets and a tarp, in the operation carried out under the
Frontera Segura plan run by the Ministry of Government and the National Police.
[@1] Bolivian Police General Commander Adrián Álvarez said the seizure of
controlled substances, marijuana, amounted to a total of 1,240,126 grams, and
put the economic impact on drug trafficking at about US$1,624,000. [@2]

One Bolivian citizen was detained as the person transporting the load, and the
truck was seized and left at the disposal of the authorities for the criminal
investigation. [@1] The detainee is in preventive detention while investigations
continue to identify the other people who took part in moving the drug, as
authors, co-authors, accomplices and cover-ups, said Álvarez. [@1] Government
Minister Marco Antonio Oviedo said the operation took place in Puerto Quijarro,
on the border with Brazil, and as part of the Frontera Segura plan's actions.
[@2]
