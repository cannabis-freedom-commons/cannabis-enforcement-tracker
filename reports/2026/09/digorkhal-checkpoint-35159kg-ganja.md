Acting on specific source information, Assam Police intercepted a vehicle bearing registration AS 01 QC 4152 at the Digorkhal Check Point along the Assam-Meghalaya border. [@1,2]

A thorough search recovered 379 packets of suspected ganja weighing 35.159 kg. [@1,2] The packets were concealed inside courier parcel cartons, packed in polythene resembling tea packets to hide them during transport. [@1,2]

One person, Arup Kalita, a resident of Nalbari, was apprehended in connection with the seizure. [@1,2] SSP Cachar Partha Pratim Das said further investigation was under way to establish the source and destination of the contraband and identify others involved. [@2]
