Officers of the Interior Ministry's operative-search department and its regional
units, working with the State Security Service, carried out the operation as the
second stage of the Qoradori-2026 preventive campaign. [@1] The campaign
prevented the distribution of 24 kg of hashish in Uzbekistan. [@1] On the border
territory of Namangan region, officers detained a native of Andijan region born
in 1993. [@1] According to the Interior Ministry, he had carried 20 kg of
hashish hidden in a bag across the Naryn river from a neighbouring state and
tried to leave it under trees about 20 metres from the border. [@1] The
investigation established that he intended to pass the drugs to accomplices
living in Andijan and Namangan regions. [@1] Operational footage indicated he
had been promised $10,000 for delivering the bag. [@1]

On 7 September at about 02:00 in Andijan, a man born in 1976 was detained while
receiving 10 kg of hashish as officers extended the operation to catch other
group members. [@1] Officers then detained another suspect in Kokand in Fergana
region while he was attempting to sell hashish for $24,000. [@1] In Besharik
district of Fergana region, officers detained a man born in 1991 with physical
evidence as he sold 4 kg of hashish packed in four separate packages. [@1]

Criminal cases were opened against the suspects under Article 246(2) and Article
273(5)(a) of Uzbekistan's Criminal Code, and all of them were remanded in
custody. [@1] The Interior Ministry urged the public to report suspected drug
trafficking through its @stopnarko_bot Telegram bot. [@1]
