Four people were arrested at a residence at Francis Trace, Santa Flora, in
connection with enquiries into the possession of marijuana. [@1] One of the
four, a 19-year-old man whom police identified as a priority offender, allegedly
had 320 grammes of marijuana in his possession; the others were a 39-year-old
woman, a 19-year-old woman and a second 19-year-old man. [@1] A 48-year-old man
of Francis Trace was also arrested after an air rifle was seized; police said he
did not hold a permit for the firearm. [@1] A sixth man, a 21-year-old of
Francis Trace, was detained in connection with enquiries into a reported larceny
in the Erin Police District. [@1]

The arrests came during an anti-crime operation in the Siparia, Santa Flora and
Erin districts on Wednesday 9 September 2026, conducted between 4 am and 9 am.
[@2] The exercise formed part of the Trinidad and Tobago Police Service's
crime-fighting work under Operation R.E.S.O.L.U.T.E. Shield and involved the
High Performance Patrol Response Unit, the Santa Flora, Siparia and Erin police
stations, Siparia CID, the K-9 Unit and the South-Western Division Task Force.
[@1] Police said searches were carried out under the Emergency Powers
Regulations, 2026, targeting people and premises for firearms and ammunition.
[@1] A search at the home of a 34-year-old man of Francis Trace Extension, Santa
Flora, found nothing illegal. [@1]

The operation was led by Senior Superintendent Thompson and Superintendent
Jaggernauth and coordinated by Assistant Superintendents Corrie, Lewis and
Forbes. [@1]
