Police Narcotics Bureau officers arrested two Sri Lankan businesswomen at Bandaranaike International Airport in Katunayake on 9 September 2026 for allegedly smuggling kush cannabis. [@1,2] Ada Derana valued the stock at Rs. 53.72 million. [@1]

The women were aged 33 and 35 and were identified as residents of Dematagoda and Thotalanga in Colombo. [@1,2] They arrived at about 10.55 a.m. on SriLankan Airlines flight UL 403 from Bangkok, Thailand. [@1,2]

Officials found approximately 5.372 kg of kush cannabis in five airtight packages packed inside two pieces of luggage. [@1,2] The women were scheduled to be produced before the Negombo Magistrate's Court on the day of the arrests. [@1,2]

The sources do not report the result of that court appearance or where the women intended to travel next. [@1,2]
