Acting on passenger profiling, officers of the Air Intelligence Unit
intercepted a male Mumbai resident after he arrived at Sardar Vallabhbhai
Patel International Airport on Thai Airways flight TG-343 from Bangkok on 21
September 2026. [@1,2] A detailed check of his check-in baggage found a
green-coloured substance hidden among clothes in 14 airtight plastic packets.
[@1,2]

The substance tested positive for ganja on an NDPS field-testing kit, and its
weight was recorded as 8.913 kg. [@1,2] Customs officials valued the haul at
about Rs 8.9 crore, seized it under the NDPS Act, 1985, and arrested the
passenger. [@1,2] Investigators are examining his travel history and links to
establish the source and destination of the consignment. [@1,2]
