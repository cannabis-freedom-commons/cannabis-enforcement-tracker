Nine people were detained in Agua Verde, Rosario, Sinaloa, during an operation
by state and federal authorities. [@1] The detainees included four Colombian
men, one Cuban man and two women, one of them a minor. [@1] Authorities did not
give the identity, age or nationality of the other two detained people. [@1] The
FGR later obtained the binding over for trial of the nine detainees, including
the minor and five foreign nationals. [@2] A judge found the adolescent's
detention lawful and ordered preventive internment in the adolescent centre in
Culiacan. [@2]

Inside the property officers seized nine rifle-type firearms, 2,560 cartridges,
52 magazines and nine tactical vests, plus 4.282 kilograms of marijuana in 448
packets. [@2] The marijuana was found in 448 packets. [@2] The first reports put
the suspected marijuana at about five kilograms. [@1] Those first reports listed
nine AK-47 rifles, 54 magazines and 2,620 usable cartridges. [@1] Seven adults
were bound over for possession of military-use weapons, cartridges and
magazines. [@2] Three of them were also accused of dealing-related possession of
marijuana for sale. [@2]

Officers acted after spotting armed civilians during patrols, then deploying to
a property in the community. [@1] The surveillance was carried out by the
Sinaloa Special Operations Group and the Mexican Army. [@1] The detainees and
everything seized were placed at the disposal of the FGR's public prosecutor,
which investigates and decides their legal situation. [@1] Authorities had not
said whether the property was a safe house or whether the detainees belonged to
a criminal organisation. [@1]
