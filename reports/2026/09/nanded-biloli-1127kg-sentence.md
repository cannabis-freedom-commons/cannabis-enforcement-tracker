## The sentence

The Additional Sessions Judge at the special NDPS court in Biloli, Nanded, convicted Gokul Narayan Rajput and Sunil Yadav Mahajan on Tuesday, 8 September 2026. [@1,2] The court sentenced each man to 10 years of rigorous imprisonment and fined each Rs 1 lakh, with six more months of rigorous imprisonment if the fine is not paid. [@1,2] The court held that the prosecution had proved beyond reasonable doubt that both were in conscious possession of a commercial quantity of ganja. [@1,2] The court ordered the confiscation of the truck and the other seized articles. [@1,2]

## The 2021 interception

The case began on 15 November 2021, when the NCB's Mumbai Zonal Unit intercepted an Ashok Leyland truck near Manjram village in Naigaon taluka of Nanded district. [@1,2] Officers found 49 bags holding 112 packets of ganja, weighing 1,127 kg in total, concealed beneath a load of industrial iron rods. [@1,2] Rajput, the driver, and Mahajan, the co-driver, were arrested at the scene. [@1,2] Rajput, 32, is from Aurangabad district and Mahajan, 35, is from Jalgaon district. [@2] Investigators said Rajput had earlier been booked in attempted murder and kidnapping cases and that his truck had been blacklisted by the Regional Transport Office. [@2]
