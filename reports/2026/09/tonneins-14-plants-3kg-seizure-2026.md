Gendarmes from the Tonneins brigade were called late on Friday morning, 25
September 2026, to a house in Tonneins, Lot-et-Garonne, because of a strong
smell coming from it. [@1] They entered the house and found two people inside:
the owner in the middle of a transaction with a customer. [@2] A search that
followed turned up 3 kg of cannabis already packaged and 14 cannabis plants.
[@2] The officers said one plant is capable of producing 2 kg of cannabis. [@2]

The owner of the house was placed in garde à vue, French police custody, on
suspicion of drug trafficking. [@2] The customer was fined for using drugs. [@2]

The same account appeared in Le Petit Bleu d'Agen's report on petitbleu.fr. [@1]
