Railway Protection Force police arrested three people who were carrying ganja
made into chocolates on a train in September 2026 and took 2 kg of the drug into
their possession. [@1]

Kakinada Superintendent of Police Bindumadhav said police were registering cases
against those held and seizing ganja with the help of Eagle teams, technology
and drones. [@1] The newspaper said the suspects carrying ganja were mostly
young men, and that people addicted to the drug were committing crimes. [@1]
Police search the district's outskirts and abandoned houses using drones, with
Eagle teams keeping watch, while awareness programmes are held in schools, the
newspaper said. [@1] The paper reported that ganja is moved at night from the
state border areas of Odisha and the agency areas, the tribal tracts, along back
routes through Polavaram district and handed to middlemen. [@1]
