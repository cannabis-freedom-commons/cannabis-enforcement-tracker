Officers of the Zhetysu Region police department's anti-drug unit stopped
illegal drug trafficking in Taldykorgan. [@1] During operational and search
measures, police detained a 32-year-old man suspected of illegal trafficking in
narcotic drugs. [@1]

During an authorised search at the detainee's home, officers found and seized a
substance of plant origin. [@1] An expert examination established that the
substance was hashish. [@1] The total weight of the seized drug was 6 kg 550
grams. [@1] Police said the seizure removed a large batch of drugs from illegal
circulation that could have reached the city streets. [@1]

An investigation is under way against the detainee. [@1] The man has been placed
in custody. [@1] Police said they were still establishing all the circumstances
and other people possibly involved in drug trafficking. [@1]
