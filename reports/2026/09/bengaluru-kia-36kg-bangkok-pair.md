Customs officers at Kempegowda International Airport (KIA) seized 80.1 kg of hydroponic ganja worth Rs28.01 crore from seven passengers in five cases, Deccan Herald reported on 22 September. [@1]

This report covers one of those cases. Customs intercepted two passengers arriving from Bangkok and found 36 kg of hydroponic ganja in their possession. [@1,2] The consignment was valued at Rs12.57 crore. [@1,2] Both passengers were arrested under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act, 1985. [@1,2]

The article dates two other cannabis cases, 15.3 kg from one Bangkok passenger and 26.8 kg from two more, to 19 September. [@1] It dates a 2 kg case from a Vietnam arrival to the Monday of publication week. [@1] Those are separate events from this 36 kg interception. [@1] The same roundup also lists gold seizures, which are not cannabis and are left out here. [@1]
