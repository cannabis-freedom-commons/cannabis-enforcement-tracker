Police and National Guard officers destroyed a camouflaged plantation of Indian hemp in the pre-desert zone of Zhambyl Region, the region's police department said. [@1,2] The plantation had an autonomous power supply and an irrigation system. [@1,2]

Drones were used to find it. [@1,2] Officers detained a 40-year-old resident of Taraz at the site; the department said he had several previous convictions and cultivated the plants for processing and wholesale sale outside the region. [@1,2]

A search found 50 two-metre cannabis bushes, 438 kg of marijuana, packaging materials and agricultural equipment. [@1,2] Officers also seized a Makarov pistol with ammunition and filed-off serial numbers, a 12-gauge Baikal shotgun and its ammunition, and an SUV used to carry the raw material. [@1,2]

Criminal cases were opened under Articles 300 and 287 of Kazakhstan's Criminal Code, for illegal cultivation of drugs and illegal storage of weapons. [@1,2] The suspect confessed and was placed in a temporary detention centre, and police said they were working to identify other people involved. [@1,2]

The operation was part of Karasora-2026, a national operational and preventive campaign. [@2]
