No one was arrested in connection with three cannabis seizures made in
Port-of-Spain during a Trinidad and Tobago Police Service anti-crime operation.
[@2] Officers conducting Operation B.L.A.S.T. searched three locations in
Port-of-Spain on Tuesday between 11 a.m. and 4 p.m. [@2]

Around 1:45 p.m., officers searched an abandoned apartment at Harpe Place, East
Dry River, and found a black plastic bag holding three packets of plant material
resembling cannabis that weighed 862 grammes at Besson Street Police Station.
[@2] The Harpe Place material was taken to the Besson Street Police Station and
weighed 862 grammes. [@1] About 30 minutes later at People’s Mall on Henry
Street, PC Wallace noticed a black plastic bag lodged between two booths
containing several plastic bags and small zip-lock bags of similar plant
material weighing 680 grammes. [@2] Upon retrieving the bag, officers found
several plastic bags and small zip-lock bags containing plant material
resembling cannabis, and the contents weighed 680 grammes. [@1] At about 3 p.m.,
PC Subero searched a bushy area at Jackson Place, East Dry River, and found a
black plastic bag with loose plant material and several packets weighing 635
grammes. [@2] Police said the material seized at the three locations together
weighed approximately 2.2 kilogrammes. [@2]

The operation was spearheaded by senior officers including ACP Hazel and
coordinated by Inspector Edwards and Sergeant Adams, with personnel from
Charford Court, Duncan Street Post and the Canine Unit taking part. [@2] The
operation also used K9 Gino and Canine Unit members, and police said it
concluded without incident while the monitored areas were otherwise quiet and
regular. [@2]
