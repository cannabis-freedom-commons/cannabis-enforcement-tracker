National Guard units seized 110 slabs of cannabis resin, known in Tunisia as
zatla, during September 2026. [@1] They also took 338 pieces of cannabis of
different sizes in the same operations. [@1] The same month's operations seized
3 kg of cocaine and 25,000 narcotic tablets, which are counted separately from
the cannabis. [@1] Mosaique FM gave the same cannabis total of 110 plaques and
338 pieces for the month. [@2] Directinfo, citing the TAP agency, reported the
same list of 110 cannabis slabs, 3 kg of cocaine, 25,000 tablets and 338
cannabis pieces. [@3]

The National Guard's general directorate announced the results in a video on its
official page. [@1] The units detained 1,515 people during the month. [@1]
Directinfo gave the same total of 1,515 arrests for the month. [@3] One of those
detained was wanted under 52 search notices issued by different security and
judicial authorities. [@1] Another detainee was said to have escaped from prison
during the events of the Revolution and had been sentenced to 30 years in prison
for drug trafficking, with five search notices against him. [@1]
