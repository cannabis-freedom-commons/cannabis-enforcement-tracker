Belgian, Dutch and Luxembourg police forces carried out a large-scale
cross-border checkpoint operation to mark Benelux Day. [@1] The joint operation
entitled “From Knokke to Athus” ran on 4 and 5 September 2026. [@2] In total,
968 vehicles and 382 people were checked, with controls also carried out on
board international rail services. [@1] The operation resulted in 48 criminal
charges and seven arrests. [@1]

The checks led to the seizure of approximately 500 grams of drugs, mainly
cannabis, as well as 700 cartons of contraband cigarettes. [@1] Two vehicles
were also seized and police officers issued 28 fines for traffic offences,
notably relating to taxi regulations and drink-driving. [@1] Officers carried
out joint checks at Luxembourg City’s main railway station. [@1] Luxembourg and
Belgian officers conducted joint patrols in the Grand Duchy, Luxembourg. [@1]

The sweep was organised for Benelux Day 2026 to highlight intensive police
cooperation within the Benelux. [@2] It was presented as an application of the
modernised Benelux Treaty on Police Cooperation in force since 2023, enabling
cross-border pursuit, information exchange and joint operations. [@2]
