Officers of Serbia's Interior Ministry arrested 22-year-old F. P. in Belgrade on
the morning of 11 September 2026. [@2] The operation was carried out on the
order of the Higher Public Prosecutor's Office in Belgrade by officers from the
Palilula police station, working with the criminal police service and the
Belgrade police department. [@1] Authorities said the suspect was detained on
suspicion of unauthorised production and trafficking of narcotic drugs. [@1]

During a search of his apartment and other premises he used, officers discovered
marijuana, hashish, hallucinogenic mushrooms, pink cocaine and MDMA. [@1] Police
seized about nine kilograms of drugs in total across the five substances found
in the premises. [@2] Authorities did not publish a breakdown of the specific
weight of cannabis or the other substances seized. [@2]

Interior Minister Ivica Dačić said the suspect was ordered held in police
detention for up to 48 hours. [@1] Following the detention period, he was to be
brought before the Higher Public Prosecutor's Office along with a criminal
complaint. [@2]
