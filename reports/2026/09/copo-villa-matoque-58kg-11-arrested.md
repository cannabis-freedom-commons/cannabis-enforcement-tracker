Federal prosecutors were due to question the 11 people arrested in Copo
department with 58 kilograms of marijuana, after operations that ran from Sunday
night into Monday afternoon. [@1] Federal judge Sebastián Argibay ordered the
arrests of an organisation made up of people from Salta, Tucumán and one from
Santiago del Estero. [@1] The police base Muralla Norte El Mojón, in Pellegrini
department, carried out the operation, and officers from the base found the load
again in the bush. [@1]

Uniformed officers intercepted the two vehicles, a Chevrolet S10 and a Renault
Kangoo with five people on board, on Provincial Route 4 near San José de
Boquerón and Pichanal. [@2] Two men and a woman abandoned one of the vehicles
and ran into wooded ground, and a search operation followed on Argibay's orders.
[@2] Police made 11 arrests in total, took three vehicles and weighed the load
recovered in the bush at about 58 kilograms. [@2] Field tests by specialists
identified the bundles as marijuana weighing about 58 kilograms, but the
Panorama account gave a total of 57 kilograms. [@3]

Officers also held a third vehicle, a white Citroën C4 Aircross with a man and a
couple in it, whom they suspected of coming to support those who fled Sunday.
[@2] A woman who fled was found later in El Quebrachal, Salta province, and held
in a police unit there pending judicial steps. [@2]

Investigators believe the marijuana was collected in Bolivia and was headed for
Buenos Aires province. [@1] A police source suggested the receivers were
Peruvians in Villa 1-11-14, officially barrio Padre Rodolfo Ricciardelli, one of
Buenos Aires' largest informal settlements. [@1] At the time of publication the
detainees were held in different security units, and the lawyers said none of
them would testify until they knew the criminal charge. [@1]
