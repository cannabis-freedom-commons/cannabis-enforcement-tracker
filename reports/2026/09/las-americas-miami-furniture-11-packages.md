Agents of the National Drug Control Directorate (DNCD), with the Public Ministry
and the Specialized Corps for Airport Security and Civil Aviation (CESAC),
intervened a suspicious parcel in a cargo area of Las Américas International
Airport in Boca Chica, Santo Domingo province. [@1] Authorities reported no
detentions in the operation and did not disclose the listed sender or recipient
of the parcel. [@1] The Public Ministry and the DNCD opened an investigation to
identify those responsible for the shipment and to establish its final
destination. [@1]

The box had arrived from Miami and drew attention during controls at Las
Américas International Airport José Francisco Peña Gómez. [@1] Inside a bench
seat inside the box, authorities found 11 packages of suspected marijuana. [@1]
X-ray operators had observed elements that did not match the declared contents
and notified the operational units in the area. [@1] The acting prosecutor
ordered a broader inspection, and a dog trained to detect controlled substances
marked the box, leading to its opening. [@1] The packages were hidden in tubes
and plastic wraps in interior spaces of the furniture frame that had been
covered to simulate a conventional seat. [@1] The DNCD said this concealment
method exploits hollows in wooden frames, replacing padding with drug packages
before adding outer upholstery to reduce detection chances. [@1]

The plant material was packed and sent to the National Institute of Forensic
Sciences (INACIF), which must determine its composition, weight and
characteristics. [@1] Until that analysis is known, authorities treat the
material as suspected marijuana. [@1] The drugs will remain in custody while
INACIF completes the analyses requested by the Public Ministry. [@1] The seizure
occurred in a terminal that handles an important share of the Dominican
Republic's air passenger and cargo movement, where controls combine scanners,
physical inspections and canine units to detect drugs hidden in luggage, parcels
and goods. [@1]
