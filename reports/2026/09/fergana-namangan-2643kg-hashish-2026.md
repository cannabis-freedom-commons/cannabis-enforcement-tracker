Officers of the State Security Service with police and customs detained four
people in the Fergana and Namangan regions, seizing hashish in three operations.
[@2] At the Namangan traffic-police post in Pap district, officers stopped a
Cobalt travelling from Fergana to Tashkent, driven by a Chirchik resident born
in 1995. [@1] A search of the car's boot found and seized 1 kg of hashish. [@1]
In Chust district in Namangan region, officers stopped and inspected a VAZ-2103
belonging to a previously convicted resident of Yanginamangan district born in
1987 and found 995 grams of hashish in its interior. [@1] A witnessed search of
the house of a Namangan resident born in 1975 found 648 grams of hashish in the
basement. [@1] Investigators said the householder had bought that drug for
$5,000 from an acquaintance born in 1972 from Yanginamangan district, who was
also detained. [@1]

The total seized across the operations was 2.643 kg of substance described as
hashish. [@1] The operations were conducted jointly by regional State Security
Service departments with internal-affairs and customs officers. [@2] Criminal
cases were opened over all the episodes and investigations were continuing. [@1]
