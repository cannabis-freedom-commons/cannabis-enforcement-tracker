Thiruvananthapuram Rural Police seized 87.59 kg of ganja from a Tamil
Nadu-registered car in an operation under the state's Operation Toofan anti-drug
drive. [@1] The car was intercepted in the early hours between Vellarada and
Kattakada in the district. [@2] An initial search of the car found nothing, and
a special chamber built at the bottom of the vehicle was found only during a
detailed inspection. [@2] Officers found 40 packets of about 2.2 kg each in the
secret compartment. [@1]

Akhil, 32, and Subeesh, 33, both from Vellarada, were arrested first. [@3] The
team had kept the two men under surveillance for nearly a month, and the
operation began after a tip-off to Rural SP Prasanth Kani. [@3] Interrogation
led police to Joby Jose, a Thiruvananthapuram-based peddler they describe as the
consignment's intended recipient, and they arrested him as well. [@1] Jose has
been an accused in 13 cases, several of them under the Narcotic Drugs and
Psychotropic Substances Act. [@1]

Police said the ganja had been bought in Andhra Pradesh and brought to Kerala
through Tamil Nadu. [@2] Investigators suspect the consignment was meant for
distribution across Thiruvananthapuram and other districts and to be broken up
for sale at schools and in coastal and other sensitive areas. [@1] Police said
the two drivers had been involved in drug peddling before and were still being
questioned about others in the network. [@2]
