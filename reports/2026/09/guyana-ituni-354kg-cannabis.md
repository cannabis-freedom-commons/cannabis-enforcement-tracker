Police in Guyana's Region 10 seized 35.4 kg of suspected cannabis during an operation at Five Mile on Ituni Road on September 9, 2026. [@1,2] Officers also found GYD $455,180 in cash. [@1,2]

## The pursuit

Police said they acted on information and followed two motorcycles travelling without registration plates; one carried bulky parcels. [@1,2] During the pursuit, the rider with the parcels fell and was arrested, while the other rider escaped. [@1,2] Officers found leaves, seeds and stems suspected to be cannabis in the parcels, which weighed 35.4 kg. [@1,2]

The arrested man was 40 and lived in Siberia Mines, Upper Demerara River. [@1,2] News Room Guyana reported that he remained in custody as investigations continued; News Source Guyana said he had been informed of intended prosecution. [@1,2] Neither report gives a later charge or court outcome. [@1,2]
