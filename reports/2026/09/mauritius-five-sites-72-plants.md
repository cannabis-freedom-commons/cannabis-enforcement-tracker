Officers of the ADSU anti-drug unit ran a series of operations during Friday 18 September 2026, helped in some sectors by sightings from the Police Helicopter Squadron. [@1] They uprooted 72 cannabis plants from abandoned land and forest zones. [@1] The account was published on 20 September. [@1]

The first intervention came at 7.42am in a forest at Citron Donis, where 7 plants of 5 to 20 cm were seized, followed at 8.15am by 7 more plants of 15 to 25 cm in the Anse Raffin forest. [@1] Officers later uprooted 5 plants of 35 cm to 1.50 m in the Tamarin woods at 10.30am, then 29 plants of 10 to 65 cm on abandoned land at Bambous Virieux at about 11.30am. [@1]

A final sweep at 12.30pm on fallow land at Anse Jonchée recovered 24 plants of 30 cm to 1.10 m. [@1] The growers remain unknown and inquiries are ongoing. [@1]
