Gudalur North police in Theni district, Tamil Nadu, stopped two cars during a
vehicle check on the bypass road between Gudalur and Kumily and seized about 190
kg of ganja hidden in 95 packages. [@1] Officers grew suspicious because the
occupants gave inconsistent answers, and the search followed. [@1] Onmanorama
reported that police acted on a tip-off received by Gudalur North police
inspector Prabhu. [@2] Police seized the two cars used to carry the ganja, the
Tamil daily Dinakaran reported. [@3]

Four men were arrested and taken to the Gudalur North police station, where the
inquiry found they were carrying the ganja for sale in Kerala. [@1] Onmanorama
identified the four, aged 25 to 46, as residents of Ramanathapuram district,
from Muthupandi of Kadugusandai Chathiram, Mohamed Anifa and Mohamed Aliyar of
Sayalkudi and Eeswaramoorthy of Appanur near Kadaladi; The Hindu gave the second
man's name as S. Mohamed Hanifa. [@2]

The four accused were produced before the Judicial Magistrate in Uthamapalayam,
a town in Theni district, with the seized ganja and cars, and remanded in
judicial custody. [@1] Police valued the seized ganja at ₹1 crore and said they
were searching for Kaleeswaran, also from Ramanathapuram district, in connection
with the case. [@1]
