Officers of the Bilasuvar Customs Department of Azerbaijan's State Customs
Committee stopped an attempt to move a large consignment of marijuana through
the country from Iran to Russia. [@1]

The drugs were found at the Bilasuvar customs post during an inspection of a
vehicle carrying fresh onions from Iran to Russia in transit through Azerbaijan.
[@1] Officers examined the vehicle with a stationary X-ray scanner and a customs
service dog. [@1] In a hidden compartment built into the lower part of the
wooden pallets under the load, officers found 80 packages of dried marijuana.
[@1] The State Customs Committee put the net weight of the dried marijuana at 25
kg 570 g. [@1] APA reported the seizure as 80 packages with a total net weight
of 25 kilograms and 570 grams of dried marijuana, hidden from customs control.
[@2]

An investigation into the case is under way. [@2]
