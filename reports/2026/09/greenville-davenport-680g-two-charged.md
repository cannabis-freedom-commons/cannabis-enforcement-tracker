Two Greenville men were behind bars after warrants accused them of exposing a
child to marijuana. [@1] Court records identified them as Jonathan Johnson, 39,
and Maurice Johnston, 60, who allegedly conspired to sell nearly 24 ounces of
marijuana from a home on Davenport Street. [@1] Warrants said they exposed the
drug to a 9-year-old in the home. [@1] Jonathan Johnson was arrested on Sept.
17. [@4] Maurice Johnston was arrested on Sept. 17. [@3]

A magistrate's order alleged Johnson possessed about 680 grams of marijuana with
intent to sell from the Davenport Street dwelling. [@4] A magistrate's order
alleged Johnston possessed about 680 grams of marijuana and maintained the
Davenport Street home in Greenville for keeping and selling marijuana. [@3] Both
men were charged with exposing a child to a controlled substance, marijuana
possession, possession with intent to sell or deliver, paraphernalia possession,
maintaining a dwelling for a controlled substance and felony conspiracy. [@2]
Johnston was also charged with possession of a firearm by a felon and possession
of a stolen firearm. [@1] The order accused Johnston of possessing a stolen
Taurus 9mm handgun. [@3]

Johnston was jailed on a $60,000 secured bond while Johnson initially received
no bond. [@1] Johnson posted bond on Sept. 18. [@4] Johnston received a $60,000
secured bond. [@3] Johnson was held without bond and Johnston was jailed on a
$60,000 secured bond after their Thursday arrests. [@2]
