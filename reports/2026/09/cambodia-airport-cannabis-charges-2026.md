Kandal Provincial Court charged five British men with possession, trafficking and transport under Article 40 after two stops at Techo International Airport. [@1] On 6 September officers seized about 18 kg of cannabis and heroin from two of them; on 9 September they found 73.017 kg of dried cannabis and 6.219 kg of white powder suspected to be heroin in the luggage of the other three. [@1,2] The Phnom Penh Post described the 9 September haul as more than 70 kg of suspected cannabis seized at the airport. [@2] The court said the men faced 10 to 30 years if convicted, and the cases were part of a run of outbound cannabis seizures at the airport. [@1]
