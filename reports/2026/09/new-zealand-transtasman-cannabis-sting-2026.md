Police and Customs seized a mix of drugs in a coordinated Australia–New
Zealand week of action, executing 85 search warrants across New Zealand and
taking border enforcement action. [@2,1] [@3]

In Auckland, police shut grow houses across North Shore, Auckland City and
South and West Auckland linked to Vietnamese organised crime, taking more
than a quarter-tonne of loose cannabis; about 6,180 plants were seized
nationally. [@2] [@3] Customs' tally put the NZ haul at 270 kg of loose
cannabis and 6,183 plants, alongside 34 arrests, $647,690 in cash, four
firearms and three vehicles. [@3] Across both countries the AFP-led
operation charged 1,203 people and seized 716 kg of cannabis plus 8,701
plants. [@4]
