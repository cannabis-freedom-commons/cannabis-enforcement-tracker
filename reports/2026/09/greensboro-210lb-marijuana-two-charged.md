Greensboro police arrested two men in connection with a narcotics investigation.
[@1] Christopher Jarel Suggs, 35, was charged with possession with intent to
sell or deliver marijuana, felony possession of marijuana, felony maintaining a
dwelling for a controlled substance, felony conspiracy and possession of drug
paraphernalia. [@2] Jammi Theo Parker, 39, was charged with trafficking
marijuana, conspire to sell or deliver marijuana, felony maintaining a dwelling
for a controlled substance and possession of drug paraphernalia. [@2] Suggs
posted a $5,000 secured bond. [@2] Parker posted a $50,000 secured bond,
according to online court records. [@2]

Vice/narcotics detectives executed search warrants on Sept. 29 with assistance
from the Guilford County Sheriff's Office before making the arrests. [@2]
Officers seized approximately 210 pounds (about 95.25 kg) of marijuana and 19
firearms. [@3] Detectives also seized an undisclosed amount of cash. [@3] Police
released no further details about the firearms or the amount of cash seized.
[@3]

Police said the investigation remains ongoing. [@1] Additional case information
is available through the North Carolina eCourts system for Guilford County. [@3]
