Customs officers at Bandaranaike International Airport arrested a 44-year-old businessman from Weligama, Matara, for allegedly trying to smuggle kush cannabis into Sri Lanka. [@1,2] He had arrived from Bangkok, Thailand, on 12 September 2026 and was stopped while leaving through the Red Channel used by business-class passengers. [@1,2]

Officers found about 668 g of kush cannabis packed into three packets concealed in the side pockets of the trousers he was wearing. [@1,2] Customs estimated the consignment at about Rs6.68 million. [@1] VivaLanka put the estimated street value at about Rs6.8 million. [@2]

The suspect and the consignment were handed over to officers of the Police Narcotics Bureau at the airport for further investigation. [@1,2]
