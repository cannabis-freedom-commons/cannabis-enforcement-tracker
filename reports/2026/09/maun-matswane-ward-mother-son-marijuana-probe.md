A Maun nurse and her 25-year-old son are under police investigation after the
discovery of a suspected cannabis cultivation and distribution operation at
their home. [@1] During a recent raid on the Matshwane ward residence, police
officers confiscated a full bakkie load of uprooted green plants, seeds, and
packaged sachets containing dried leaves. [@1]

Acting Maun Station Commander Moutlwatsi Ikobeng confirmed that law enforcement
intervened after discovering evidence of ongoing cultivation on the property.
[@1] Ikobeng stated that the pair were found in possession of a plant suspected
to be marijuana, and that they were also found with seeds they are using to grow
the plant. [@1] All confiscated plant material and substances have been
dispatched to the police forensic laboratory for official testing. [@1] Formal
charges against the 46-year-old mother and her 25-year-old son remain pending
the outcome of the forensic analysis. [@1]

Speaking at the close of a full council meeting in Maun, North West District
Council Chairperson Itumeleng Kelebetseng highlighted the scale of the haul and
voiced distress over the impact of illegal drugs on the community. [@1]
Kelebetseng said he had been told in a monthly briefing that Boseja police found
marijuana in large sums that loaded a van full, which he called a concern. [@1]
He added that Boseja has many drinking spots and that police have confiscated
illegal alcohol, noting illicit alcohol, drug distribution, and illegal gambling
continue to threaten social stability in Maun. [@1]
