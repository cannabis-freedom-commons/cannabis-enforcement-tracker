Judicial Police officers in Quito raided two properties in the El Inca and La Morita neighbourhoods on the night of 16 September and the early hours of 17 September 2026, arresting six people and seizing 11 kilograms of cannabis. [@1,2] The operation, called Apolo 41, also found 500 grams of cocaine base, a revolver, five mobile phones, electronic equipment and three scales. [@1,2]

Police identified the detainees as Fernando E., 25; Jefferson C., 27; Inés B., 46; José F., 23; Oscar S.; and Alex E., 17. [@1,2] Fernando E., Jefferson C., Inés B. and Alex E. are Ecuadorian; José F. and Oscar S. are Venezuelan. [@1] The 17-year-old was to be handled by the juvenile justice system. [@2]

The investigation lasted about four months and focused on a group that police said stored, hid, moved and sold drugs, weapons and stolen goods. [@1,2] Police linked the structure to the gang Los Lobos and said it was led by someone using the alias "Madrina". [@1,2]

Officers also seized a pickup truck and motorcycles with reports of theft. [@1,2] El Comercio reported that the truck had been stolen in Calderón on 3 May 2026 and that one motorcycle had been taken in San Antonio de Pichincha on 14 August. [@1] The detainees were handed to the prosecutor's office. [@1,2]
