At about 20:00 on Thursday evening 10 September 2026, plainclothes officers in
Wurselen noticed a car travelling along Krefelder Strasse toward
Willy-Brandt-Ring and called for a patrol car to carry out a traffic check. [@2]
The driver ignored the patrol team's stop signals, accelerated and tried to
evade the check. [@2] Officers briefly lost sight of the car near Broichweiden
and then found it parked at the roadside on Parkstrasse. [@2] A man who ran a
few metres away toward a field path was caught after a short pursuit through
gardens and over roofs and was secured with handcuffs. [@2] Police identified
the man as a 26-year-old from Alsdorf who had previously come to police
attention and held no driving licence. [@2] The Thursday-evening attempt to
evade the traffic check led to a short chase. [@1]

A search of his trouser pocket found four-figure cash. [@2] Because the car
smelled strongly of cannabis, officers obtained a search warrant for it. [@2] A
drug-detection dog searched the car and found several containers holding a
substance that appeared to be narcotics. [@2] Near the car the dog also found a
bag containing a larger quantity of cannabis that could be attributed to the
26-year-old. [@2] A drug pre-test on the suspect was positive for cocaine and
cannabis, and officers took a blood sample from him. [@2]

The 26-year-old was provisionally arrested and taken into police custody. [@2]
Officers seized the cash, the drugs and the vehicle. [@2] Criminal police opened
investigations into driving under the influence of drugs, driving without a
licence, suspected unauthorised drug dealing and endangering road traffic. [@2]
