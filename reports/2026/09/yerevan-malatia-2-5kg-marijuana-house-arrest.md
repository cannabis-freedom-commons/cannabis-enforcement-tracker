Community police officers in Yerevan's Malatia district received operative
information that a resident of the Akhtanak quarter was illegally storing and
selling drugs. [@1] The Interior Ministry press service told Armenpress that the
tip had been checked and confirmed. [@1] The Malatia-Sebastia investigative
division opened a criminal proceeding and searched the 54-year-old man's home on
24 September under a court order. [@1] The 54-year-old homeowner was placed
under house arrest as a preventive measure. [@2]

The search found two electronic scales, tablets of unknown origin, empty
containers with yellow-green traces and containers filled with cannabis seeds, a
container with white solution and 2.5 kg of marijuana. [@1] Police separately
described the haul as jars and test tubes with yellowish-green traces, a
container with white solution and jars holding cannabis seeds. [@2]

The circumstances were still being established. [@1] The tablets were described
as of unknown origin and the white solution was not identified as cannabis,
while the 2.5 kg find was identified as marijuana. [@1]
