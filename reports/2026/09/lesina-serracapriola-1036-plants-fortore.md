Guardia di Finanza officers discovered and seized an outdoor cannabis plantation
covering about 400 square metres hidden in dense vegetation along the Fortore
river, between the municipalities of Lesina and Serracapriola in the Province of
Foggia. [@1] Officers uprooted 1,036 cannabis plants with a total weight of
approximately 330 kilograms. [@1] The cultivation comprised 556 Cannabis sativa
plants averaging about two metres in height and 480 Cannabis indica plants
averaging 60 centimetres. [@1] The plants bore luxuriant flower heads close to
harvesting, indicating that the cultivation was nearing the end of its
productive cycle. [@1]

The plantation was organized in regular rows and supplied by an irrigation
system connected to a pump submerged in the river. [@1] In addition to the
crops, officers confiscated agricultural tools and technical equipment used to
manage the plantation. [@1] The seizure was carried out under Italy's
consolidated drug law, Presidential Decree 309/1990, which provides for the
seizure of irrigation systems, pumps and production equipment alongside the
plants. [@1] Technical tests on samples confirmed the presence of
tetrahydrocannabinol, and authorities estimated the illicit market value of the
plants at more than 3 million euros. [@1]
