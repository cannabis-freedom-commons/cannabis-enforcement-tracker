Four men were arrested in two separate drug raids in Pacific Harbour and Qauia
conducted jointly by the Fiji Police Dog Unit and Fiji Detector Dog Unit. [@1]
In a media statement, police said the K9 teams detected substances believed to
be marijuana during the raids. [@1] Police linked the drug arrests from the
joint operations to alleged unlawful possession of illicit drugs. [@2]

Cash believed to be proceeds of crime was also seized during the raid in Pacific
Harbour. [@2] The seized substances were sent for analysis while investigations
into the arrests continued. [@1] Police urged members of the public to report
crime, share tips or raise concerns about police service delivery by calling the
Crime Stoppers line on 1681. [@1] Police also invited the public to send videos
or photos to the Fiji Police Force Call Centre via WhatsApp, Viber, Signal or
email. [@1]
