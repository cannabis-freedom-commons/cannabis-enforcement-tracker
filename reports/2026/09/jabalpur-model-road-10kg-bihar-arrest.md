Jabalpur Crime Branch and Madan Mahal police arrested a suspect in a joint
operation. [@1] Officers caught the suspect in front of Lloyd Gallery on Model
Road on the night of 12 September after an informant said a man carrying two
bags had got off a bus there. [@1] The accused was identified as 50-year-old
Ravi Kumar. [@1] Ravi Kumar is a resident of Ismailpur in Bihar's Arwal district
and had been living in Jharsuguda, Odisha. [@1] He told police he had travelled
to Jabalpur by bus with Malkhan, who lived near Patan Road. [@1] Madan Mahal
police registered a case against him under Sections 8/20 of the NDPS Act. [@1]
Police were searching for his absconding accomplice Malkhan. [@1]

Police searched his blue and green bags and found ten packets of cannabis in
them. [@1] The cannabis was weighed under the NDPS Act at 10 kg 168 g. [@1]
Police put the estimated value of the seized cannabis at around Rs 508,400. [@1]
An Android mobile phone was also seized. [@1]
