East Kazakhstan police anti-drug and economic-crime units said they uncovered
unlawful cannabis cultivation and storage during the Karasora-2026 operation.
[@2] In Glubokovsky District, police came with a search to the home of a
48-year-old local resident. [@1] In the house and outbuildings, officers found
wraps of plant material, dried plant parts and 40 bushes. [@1]

The main find was in the vegetable garden, where police found another 156
cannabis bushes on the household plot. [@2] Together with the earlier finds, the
total reached 196 bushes and the overall weight of what was seized reached 34.9
kg. [@1] An expert examination confirmed that the seized material were narcotic
drugs. [@1]

A 48-year-old resident of Glubokovsky District was detained over the haul. [@2]
The man was placed in a temporary detention centre, a criminal case was opened
and the investigation is continuing. [@2] Police reminded the public that
growing and storing drug-containing plants can entail criminal liability, saying
what looked like an ordinary harvest became the basis for the criminal case.
[@2]
