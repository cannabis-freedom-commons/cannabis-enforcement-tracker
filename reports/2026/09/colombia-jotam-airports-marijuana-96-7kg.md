Colombia's National Police announced on 6 October 2026 the results of Operación
Jotam, an anti-narcotics operation run during September at the country's main
airports. [@1] The police said 96.7 kilograms of marijuana were seized in the
operation, alongside 4,921.5 kilograms of cocaine. [@1] Thirty-two people were
captured. [@1] The operation was led by the Anti-Narcotics Directorate as part
of the Esmeralda strategy, and its director is Brigadier General William Castaño
Ramos. [@1]

Officers reinforced controls on passengers, baggage, air cargo, postal shipments
and aircraft, and identified 103 cases linked to drug trafficking. [@1] The
police said the networks used export products, souvenir items, luggage, courier
and postal mail, substances attached to passengers' bodies and ingestion. [@1]
Separately from the marijuana figure, the police reported 3,500 kg of cocaine
hidden in beer and 1,211 kg mixed with coffee, bound for Toledo and Barcelona in
Spain. [@1]

The operation drew on information-sharing with authorities in Spain, the United
States, Canada and Panama, and routes were identified toward Spain, the United
States, France, the Netherlands and Panama. [@1] The police statement did not
specify the airports where the seizures took place or the nationalities of the
32 people detained. [@1]
