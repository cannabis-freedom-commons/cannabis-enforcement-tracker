At about 7:30 PM on Monday, a patrol team from the Area Police Office in
Katari recovered 182 kg of marijuana from the forest area of the Shikhar
Community Forest Users Group. [@1,2] The drugs had been packed in nine sacks
and concealed in bushes near an open area of the forest. [@1]

Police said the marijuana was found unattended, and it was kept at the Area
Police Office in Katari while an investigation proceeds. [@1] The reports name
no suspects and give no origin or destination for the consignment. [@1,2]
