A 40-year-old woman and her 17-year-old son were arrested after police allegedly
found suspected cannabis on the roof of their verandah at their home in San Jose
Village, Orange Walk District. [@1] The two lived in San Jose Village in Orange
Walk District; police described the woman as a domestic and her son as a
student. [@2]

The Quick Response Team and Special Branch officers executed a search warrant
for illegal drugs at the family's home between 6:55pm and 7:30pm on Friday,
September 25. [@1] When asked whether she had anything illegal to declare, the
woman reportedly answered, “No sir.” [@1] Nothing illegal was found inside the
house. [@1]

On top of the zinc covering the verandah, officers allegedly found a clear
plastic bag containing suspected cannabis. [@1] Both mother and son were
arrested for possession of controlled drugs and taken to the Orange Walk Police
Station, where the suspected cannabis was weighed and sealed in their presence.
[@2]

A second Breaking Belize News account reports that a search of the teenager's
front trouser pocket found a clear plastic bag with small foil parcels of
suspected crack cocaine, counted at the station as 28 parcels weighing 3.99
grams. [@3] That account gives the searching team as Orange Walk Special Branch,
GI3 and the Quick Response Team, and puts the cannabis find on the rooftop. [@3]
