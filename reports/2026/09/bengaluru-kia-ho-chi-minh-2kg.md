Customs officers seized 2 kg of hydroponic ganja, valued at about Rs70
lakh, from a passenger who arrived at Kempegowda International Airport from
Ho Chi Minh City on the night of 16 September 2026. [@1]

The passenger was identified as Mookada Rasheid. [@1] He arrived from Ho Chi
Minh City in Vietnam at around 10:35pm on 16 September. [@1] During checks,
officials found 2 kg of hydroponic ganja in his possession. [@1]

Officials said further investigation is under way to establish the source of
the drug and its intended destination. [@1] The report names no arrest. [@1]
