West Sumatra's provincial narcotics agency, BNNP Sumatera Barat, stopped a red
Toyota Calya minibus in front of the Sawah Panjang petrol station on the
Bukittinggi–Padang Sidempuan trans-Sumatra road at Lubuk Sikaping, Pasaman
regency, at about 08:00 on Sunday 6 September 2026. [@1] Officers detained two
men, identified only as MR and AAZ, who were suspected of acting as couriers.
[@1] A search of the minibus found four sacks of cannabis packages, some taped
in brown and others in black plastic bags. [@1]

The agency said the net weight of the cannabis was 44,084.46 grams, about 44 kg,
which it valued as a single weight rather than one split by sack. [@1] Chief
Toton Rasyid said the case began with a public tip about a planned shipment
along the cross-Sumatra route involving a network linking North Sumatra and West
Sumatra. [@1]

From the initial interrogation of MR and AAZ, officers went on to Kuranji in
Padang city, where they detained a third man, TRAR, suspected of directing the
couriers and owning the cannabis. [@1] Officers also seized the minibus, several
mobile phones and some cash the agency linked to the network. [@1] The three
suspects were accused under Indonesia's 2009 narcotics law, with the agency
saying the articles carry a maximum penalty of death. [@1]
