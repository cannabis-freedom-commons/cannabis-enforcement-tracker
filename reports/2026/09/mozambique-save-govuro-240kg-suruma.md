Police in Mozambique's Inhambane province detained a 38-year-old transporter and seized 240 kg of cannabis, known locally as suruma, at the Save checkpoint in Govuro district. [@1] The drug was packed in seven suitcases, each holding five large parcels. [@1]

The spokesperson for the PRM command in Inhambane, Nércia Bata, said a tip-off led to a targeted inspection at the checkpoint. [@1] The man told officers he had received the load in neighbouring South Africa and was taking it to Manica province, to be sold in the "Mina dos 6 Carros" area. [@2] Miramar identified the destination as Mossurize district. [@1]

Police described the seizure as the largest of suruma in Inhambane in recent years. [@1] Notícias MZ put the weight at more than 250 kg. [@2] The man was handed to the judicial authorities, and police said they were looking for others involved in the network. [@2]

No charge or court date was reported. [@1,2]
