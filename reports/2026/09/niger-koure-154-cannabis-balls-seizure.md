On 9 September 2026, officers of the Niamey Customs Brigade intercepted 154
balls of cannabis about 22 kilometres south of the Kouré checkpoint. [@1] Niger
Customs described the haul as a record for the brigade. [@1]

The officers targeted the suspect cargo using intelligence gathered beforehand
and intervened before it could continue its journey. [@1] The brigade thus
turned a control operation into a halt to a prohibited-goods transport circuit.
[@1] The operation came amid reinforced Nigerien customs surveillance against
trafficking that could threaten people and national territory. [@1]

After the operation, Niamey-Tillabéri regional customs director Aïcha Dioffo
congratulated the officers on their commitment and professionalism and assured
them of management support. [@1] She also urged the teams to maintain high
vigilance and strengthen rigour in checks. [@1] Customs presented the seizure as
a strong signal that surveillance remains constant and intelligence remains
decisive in intercepting illicit cargoes. [@1]
