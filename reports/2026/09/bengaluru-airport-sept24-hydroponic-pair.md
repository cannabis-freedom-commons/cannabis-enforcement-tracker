Customs officers at Kempegowda International Airport (KIA) in Bengaluru reported two hydroponic-ganja interceptions from Bangkok arrivals on 24 September. [@1] Rediff published the account on 28 September, dating the announcement to Monday and these two interceptions to 24 September. [@1]

A passenger arriving from Bangkok was held after 12 kg of hydroponic ganja valued at Rs4.20 crore was found concealed in checked-in baggage. [@1] On the same day, 6.54 kg valued at Rs2.29 crore was seized from another passenger who had also arrived from Bangkok. [@1]

The same article also describes the seizure of 3.12 kg and 14.97 kg from two other Bangkok passengers, announced the following Monday; those are a separate event from these 24 September interceptions. [@1]
