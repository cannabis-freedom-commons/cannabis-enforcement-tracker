Grady County deputies and Oklahoma Medical Marijuana Authority agents searched
the Supreme Grow facility in the Alex area of Grady County and found 877 pounds
of marijuana, about 398 kg. [@1] The sheriff's office valued the marijuana at
about $745,000. [@1]

Deputies arrested four people, who were held on Immigration and Customs
Enforcement detainers. [@1]

The joint investigation targeted illegal operations around Supreme Grow and
began in September 2026. [@1] Officials said the grow kept operating after its
owner surrendered an Oklahoma Bureau of Narcotics and Dangerous Drugs marijuana
grow licence. [@1]

Officials said other suspects linked to Supreme Grow could face charges of
trafficking a controlled dangerous substance, using surveillance equipment in a
felony and conspiracy to commit a felony. [@1]
