Four people were arrested in Antigua after separate police operations that
uncovered two firearms, ammunition and quantities of cannabis. [@1] The most
significant seizure came during an early-morning raid at Green Bay Hill on
Friday, September 4, where officers executed a search warrant at a property.
[@1] The operation involved members of the Criminal Investigations Department
Task Force, the Gang and Guns Suppression Unit and the K-9 Unit. [@1] Two
firearms and several rounds of ammunition were found during the search. [@1]
Officers also seized a licence plate, quantities of Eastern Caribbean and United
States currency, several cellular phones, articles of clothing, a Nikon camera
and other items for further investigation. [@1] Three people who were at the
property were arrested on suspicion of unlawful possession of a firearm and
ammunition and taken into custody at the St. John’s Police Station. [@1]

Police conducted mobile patrols and stop-and-search exercises in Briggins,
Belmont, Bellevue Heights and Bathlodge as part of Operation Restore Confidence.
[@1] Officers searched a vehicle and discovered 55.2 grams of cannabis. [@1] A
search of the driver’s residence found an additional 1.4 pounds of cannabis and
a flare gun. [@1] The driver was arrested on suspicion of possession of cannabis
and the items were seized as investigations continue. [@1] Police impounded a
vehicle after it was found to be unlicensed and uninsured. [@1]

The Royal Police Force of Antigua and Barbuda described the seizures as the
result of operations under Operation Restore Confidence. [@2] The force said
Operation Restore Confidence will continue, with officers maintaining increased
patrols and carrying out intelligence-led searches aimed at removing illegal
firearms, ammunition and controlled substances from the streets. [@1]
