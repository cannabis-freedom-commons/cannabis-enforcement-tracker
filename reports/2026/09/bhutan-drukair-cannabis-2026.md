A Drukair employee was arrested over 22.34 kilograms of cannabis found at Paro airport in September 2026. [@1,2]

Customs officers found two abandoned carry bags hidden in a waste-bin bag inside the
cabin of a Drukair flight from Bangkok during a standard post-flight inspection at Paro
International Airport. [@2] Laboratory analysis confirmed the contents were cannabis,
weighing 22.34 kg in total. [@2]

A middle-aged Drukair employee from Samtse district, who had travelled as a passenger on
the 12 September flight via a Bagdogra stopover, was taken into custody on 14 September
after CCTV footage showed him moving suspiciously near where the bags were found. [@2]
Screening found he had used cannabis, and police set up a task force to trace the supply
chain and possible accomplices. [@2]

The suspect denies the packages were his. [@2] Investigators note he made several personal
trips to Bangkok in the preceding months, and say they had no prior intelligence about this
smuggling attempt. [@2] With the quantity far above personal-use thresholds, the case is
being pursued as trafficking under Bhutan's narcotics law. [@2]
