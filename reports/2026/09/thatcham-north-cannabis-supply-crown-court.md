Brandon North, 18, of Park Avenue in Thatcham, appeared in the dock at Reading
Magistrates' Court on Wednesday, September 30. [@1] He is charged with being
concerned in the supply of cannabis, a Class B controlled drug. [@1] Magistrates
declined further jurisdiction and sent the case to Reading Crown Court, to be
tried by a judge and, in the event of a trial, a jury on a date to be fixed.
[@1] North was released on bail. [@1]

He is also alleged to have possessed 6.98g of cocaine, a Class A drug, with
intent to supply it to others. [@1] He is charged as well with being concerned
in the supply of ketamine, another Class B drug. [@1] All three alleged offences
are said to have happened in Thatcham on March 5. [@1]
