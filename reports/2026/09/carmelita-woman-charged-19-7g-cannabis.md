Belize police executed a search warrant at a residence in Carmelita Village at
around 9:30 a.m. on Wednesday, September 16, 2026. [@1] While officers searched
the kitchen, the woman reportedly told them she had a small amount of cannabis
in the house and handed over a small blue transparent plastic bag containing a
green leafy substance suspected to be cannabis. [@1] The substance was weighed
and reportedly amounted to 19.7 grams. [@1]

Police identified her as Cristine Cristylane Wade, 35, self employed, of
Carmelita Village. [@1] She was later formally arrested and charged with
possession of controlled drugs. [@1]

Police said possessing more than 10 grams of cannabis constitutes an offence,
and placed her under arrest. [@1] Officers continued searching the residence but
reportedly found nothing else incriminating. [@1] She was escorted to the Orange
Walk Police Station, where the suspected cannabis was weighed again, sealed and
labelled in her presence. [@1]
