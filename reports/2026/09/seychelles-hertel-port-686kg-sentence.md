Nico Hertel, a 32-year-old businessman from La Gogue, Anse Etoile, was sentenced
on Friday 11 September 2026 after the Supreme Court convicted him of importing a
controlled drug. [@1] Justice Brassel Adeline imposed six years’ imprisonment.
[@1]

Police arrested Hertel at the port on 14 March 2025 when 68,648 g of cannabis
was found concealed inside car seats in an imported container. [@1] He initially
pleaded not guilty but later admitted the charge. [@1] In court he said financial
pressure on his firm Auto World (PTY) Ltd had led him to accept an offer to import
drugs to clear debts. [@1]

Time he had already spent in custody would be deducted from the six-year term,
and he had 30 days to appeal. [@1]
