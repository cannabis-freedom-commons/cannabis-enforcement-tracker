A monthlong investigation into an unlicensed marijuana operation in Coos Bay
ended with a search warrant at 91597 Newman Place after the South Coast
Interagency Narcotics Team, SCINT, applied for and was granted the warrant. [@1]
The warrant sought evidence of unlawful possession of marijuana or hemp and
unlawful production of marijuana under Oregon law. [@1] The Coos County
Emergency Response Team executed the search warrant on September 11, 2026. [@1]

Investigators said the property was set up as a commercial marijuana operation
with hundreds of thousands of dollars in equipment, and was believed to have
operated unlicensed in Coos County for several years. [@1] SCINT said it seized
about 180 pounds of processed marijuana, along with LSD and psylocibin
mushrooms, whose amounts were not separately stated. [@1] Officials said they
believed the marijuana was intended for transport and sale in a state where
marijuana remains illegal, putting its total street value at about $270,000.
[@1]

SCINT thanked the Emergency Response Team for the safe and successful execution
of the warrant and thanked community members whose reports helped lead to the
investigation. [@1] SCINT said it remains dedicated to eradicating illegal
marijuana operations in Coos County because they affect the legal market, are
not held to the same standards as licensed businesses, do not pay required
permit fees and do not report proceeds from sales. [@1]
