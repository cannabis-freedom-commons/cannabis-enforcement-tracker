Western border police from Gjakova found 9.4 kg of suspected marijuana on 21 September 2026 after
patrols traced drugs hidden the night before. [@1,2,3] While being pursued, suspects had stashed the
load and abandoned their vehicle; officers later located three bags and a sack in Popoc, in the
border belt near Gjakova. [@1,2,3]

Police said the packages held nine kilogrammes and 400 grammes of suspected marijuana. [@1,2,3] They
identified a Turkish citizen, arrested him with help from units in Pristina and south Mitrovica, and
opened an unauthorised purchase, possession and sale case with the Gjakova prosecutor. [@1,2,3] After
questioning, the man was released and the investigation continued in regular procedure. [@1,2,3]
