Agents with the West Tennessee Violent Crime and Drug Task Force raided Cali
Smokes on Dupree Street in Brownsville and a second location connected to the
shop, leaving a Brownsville man behind bars. [@1] The task force identified the
store clerk as 40-year-old Yousef Al Baadani. [@1] The case began after agents
received complaints that the shop stored illegal substances in its back room and
sold them over the counter. [@1] An undercover agent asked to buy a product
known to contain a high dose of kratom, and Al Baadani said the product was
illegal before asking how many the agent wanted and bringing it from the rear
storeroom. [@1] The agent bought the product for $30. [@1]

The task force said the operation seized more than 50 pounds of illegal kratom
and high-potency marijuana together with more than $14,000 in cash. [@1] At the
store, agents seized multiple illegal kratom and THC products as well as felony
drug paraphernalia. [@1] Probable cause from the store search led to a second
search warrant, where agents seized eight large boxes of illegal kratom and THC
products along with the cash. [@1] Investigators determined the products were
stored and packaged at the second location and taken to the store, where they
were not displayed but sold from the back storeroom and from Al Baadani's car
when requested. [@1]

Sheriff Terry Smith and Brownsville Police Chief Kelvin Evans watched a member
of the Tennessee Dangerous Drugs Task Force test the products on site. [@1] The
office of District Attorney General Frederick H. Agee will prosecute the case,
with all charges remaining allegations and everyone presumed innocent until
proven guilty in court. [@1]
