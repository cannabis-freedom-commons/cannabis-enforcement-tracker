Tunisia's Defence Minister Khaled Sehili said the armed forces recorded
operational and security results in securing the borders, combating smuggling
and confronting other threats. [@1] He said combing operations were continuing
in the country's mountains alongside work to dismantle the logistical support
networks of terrorist groups. [@1] Sehili was answering deputies' questions at a
joint plenary session held on 12 November 2026. [@1] The session brought together the Assembly of People's Representatives and the National Council of Regions and Districts to discuss the defence budget. [@2] He said the electronic
border-surveillance system had proved effective at detecting and handling
suspicious movements. [@1]

The results he presented covered the period from September 2025 to September
2026. [@1,2] For that year, he put the seizures at 516 kilograms of cannabis,
reported as qinnab hindi. [@1] The same annual tally listed 1.4 million narcotic
tablets separately from the cannabis. [@1,2]

Sehili said the navy was continuing its missions against threats and securing
the maritime approaches as part of an integrated system protecting land and sea.
[@1] He stressed developing working methods and making greater use of available
capabilities. [@1]
