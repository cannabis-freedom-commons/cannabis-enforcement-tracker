Three Mass Communication and Journalism students of Khulna University faced
expulsion plus fines after being detained with drugs. [@1,3] They were caught with
cannabis at about 7pm on 30 September on the second floor of under-construction
Academic Building 4 during a routine patrol by assistant student affairs
directors. [@2] The university Board of Discipline reviewed the investigation
report at its 28th meeting on 27 October and made the decision. [@1,3] The
decision was announced in a 28 October memo signed by Director of Student
Affairs Professor Dr Md Salahuddin. [@1,3]

SM Toufiq Alam was expelled for two terms and fined Tk20,000, Mushfiq Ul Islam
was expelled for one term and fined Tk10,000, and Kazi Riyajus Salehin was
expelled for one term and fined Tk15,000. [@2] Undertakings are to be taken from
the guardians of all three students. [@1] The student-affairs director said the
three students were caught with ganja during the routine patrol. [@1,3] They
admitted drug involvement during video-recorded questioning. [@1]

The university said information indicated prior involvement and that the
students had sourced the drug from a van driver on Hall Road. [@1] The van
driver was later detained with a significant quantity of ganja recovered from
him and was handed over to law enforcers. [@1,3] A three-member investigation
committee examined the incident and the Board acted within a month against the
three accused students. [@1,3]
