A joint team of the Railway Protection Force (RPF) and Government Railway
Police (GRP) seized 17.749 kg of ganja from the Tejas Rajdhani Express at
Gaya Junction overnight on 16-17 September 2026. [@1,2]

The operation was carried out on the night of 16 September under Operation
Narcos, the drive against narcotics smuggling on railway premises, after a
tip-off that a passenger carrying ganja would arrive at Gaya station.
[@1,2] A team led by RPF Inspector Banarasi Yadav and rail police station
chief Shivkumar kept watch at the station. [@1,2]

When train number 22811 Up Tejas Rajdhani Express reached platform number 1
at about 10:20pm, personnel stopped a passenger seated at berth 21 in coach
9 and searched his satchel, handbag and trolley bag. [@1,2] The search
recovered 17 packets of ganja weighing 17.749 kg including the packing
tape. [@1,2]

The passenger was identified as Manoj Kumar Barnwal, a resident of
Kaimashikoh in Patna district. [@1,2] A mobile phone, identity card and the
travel ticket were also recovered from him. [@1] He was arrested at about
12:50am and handed over with the seized ganja to the Gaya GRP for further
legal action. [@1,2] Officers put the estimated value of the cannabis at
Rs8,87,450. [@1,2]
