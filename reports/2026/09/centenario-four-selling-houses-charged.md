Neuquén prosecutors charged four people with selling cocaine and marijuana from
four drug-selling houses in Centenario. [@1] Case prosecutor Silvia Moreira
presented the charges at a hearing on 18 September 2026 in the Ciudad Judicial
in Neuquén city. [@1] Anti-narcotics officers of the provincial police searched
four homes on the streets México, Costa Rica, Puerto Argentino and Ricardo
Güiraldes on 12 September 2026. [@1] Four people were charged over the sales,
which prosecutors said ran from September 2024 to 12 September 2026. [@2]

The accused are three men, identified by their initials as M.N.V.A., J.P.Z.A.
and K.B., and a woman, M.A.A. [@1] Prosecutors described M.N.V.A. as the buyer
and seller of the drugs who knew the suppliers and kept drugs and firearms in
reserve. [@2] M.A.A. was accused of coordinating payments and transfers and
storing drugs and cash, J.P.Z.A. of administering transfers and custody, and
K.B. of selling permanently at the homes. [@1] Three were charged as co-authors
of aggravated drug commerce involving an organised group of three or more
people, and K.B. as a secondary participant. [@1]

The raids found drugs, precision scales, electronic devices, nylon strips used
to divide doses, and 638,230 pesos in cash. [@1] The source does not give the
weight of the marijuana seized. [@2] Prosecutors also accused M.N.V.A. of firing
shots at a house on calle Puerto Argentino in September 2025 and threatening its
owner, and of trying to run down a police officer in barrio Fonavi on 11 April
2026. [@2] During that escape he dropped a package with 137 grams of cocaine,
and the car held another 33 grams of cocaine and marijuana. [@1]

Guarantee judge Natalia Peloso accepted the charges, rejected a defence
challenge to provincial jurisdiction and set a four-month investigation period.
[@1] Peloso ordered four months of pre-trial detention for M.N.V.A., granted
house arrest for four months to M.A.A. and J.P.Z.A., and released K.B., who must
report three times a week at the Comisaría 52. [@1] The judge allowed the
seizure of bank and Mercado Pago accounts, except M.A.A.'s pension account, and
the preventive closure of the four homes used as selling points. [@1]
