In the Jewish Autonomous Oblast, officers of the Birobidzhansky drug-control
unit caught a 47-year-old resident of a village near Birobidzhan storing a large
batch of plant-based narcotic. [@1] The man had previously been convicted of a
similar offence. [@1] The second account names his village as Kazanka. [@2] The
man was detained after the search. [@1]

Officers searching his house found a drying mass of crushed cannabis parts in
the attic, which the regional forensic centre identified as marijuana weighing
about 20 kilograms. [@1] He told police that while gathering mushrooms he found
a plantation of wild cannabis, picked the top parts, packed them into bags and
carried them home. [@1] He claimed he kept the drug only for personal
consumption without intent to sell. [@1]

A city police investigator opened a criminal case against him under part 2 of
Article 228 of the Russian Criminal Code. [@1] He was released on a travel
restriction pending the case. [@1]
