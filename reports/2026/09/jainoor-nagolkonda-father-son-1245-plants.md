Asifabad police arrested a man and his son for cultivating ganja as an intercrop in a cotton field, seizing 1,245 plants that police valued at Rs 1.50 crore. [@1,2]

Pundalik was first detained with 11 grams of ganja during a vehicle check in Jainoor on Sunday night. [@1] Under questioning he confessed to growing the crop with his son's help, and police raided the farm and seized the plants before arresting his son Ramesh. [@1] A second account says Jainoor SI G. Ravikumar received the tip on 13 September, searched Pundalik on Patnapur Main Road, found the 11 grams hidden in his towel, and inspected the field with an agricultural extension officer and five witnesses before arresting both men on 14 September. [@2]

SP Nitika Pant said counting the plants took more than ten hours and that policemen had to carry them on their heads because of the valley terrain. [@1] She said the accused had grown the crop for quite a long time on the ten-acre cotton field and that police were working to identify the masterminds behind the cultivation. [@1,2]
