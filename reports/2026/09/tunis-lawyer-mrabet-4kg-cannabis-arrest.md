The Tunis public prosecutor authorised the anti-drugs sub-directorate of the
judicial police to hold lawyer Ghazi Mrabet and a wanted drug dealer, already
serving a 40-year prison sentence, on drug-dealing charges. [@1]

The two were caught in the act at the searched dealer's home, where the search
seized more than 8 kg of cocaine, 4 kg of Indian hemp and 394,000 dinars in
cash. [@1] The lawyer allegedly tried to obstruct the officers so the dealer
could flee before both were caught. [@1]
