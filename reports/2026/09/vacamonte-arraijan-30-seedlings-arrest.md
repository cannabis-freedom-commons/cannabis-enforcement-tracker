During an intelligence-led action named Operation Santander, police apprehended
a Colombian man at a residence in the corregimiento of Vacamonte, in Arraiján
district, Panamá Oeste. [@1] Police units had located the residential property
through intelligence work before executing a search-and-registration procedure
there. [@1] Officers carried out that search in coordination with the Public
Ministry. [@2]

Inside the residence, officers seized 30 seedlings of suspected marijuana. [@2]
Officers also seized a shotgun at the property. [@1] Officers also seized
ammunition found alongside the weapon and the seedlings. [@2] Officers found
several bottles containing suspected fertilizer during the same search. [@1]
Officers also collected other items at the residence. [@2]

The apprehended man and all the seized items were to be placed at the disposal
of the competent authorities. [@1] Those authorities were to continue the
investigation and carry the case through the legal process. [@2]
