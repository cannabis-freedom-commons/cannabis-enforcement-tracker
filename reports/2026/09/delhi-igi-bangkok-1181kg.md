Airport Customs officers seized suspected ganja valued at about Rs41.33 lakh
after intercepting an Indian passenger arriving from Bangkok on Wednesday 16
September 2026. [@1]

The passenger arrived in Delhi on IndiGo flight 6E 1054 and was stopped
during security checks at Terminal 3. [@1] Examination of his travel bags
revealed four polythene pouches of suspected ganja or marijuana weighing
1,181 g net. [@1]

The passenger was arrested under Section 43(B) of the NDPS Act, 1985, and the
substance was seized under Section 43(A). [@1] A case was booked under
Sections 8, 20, 23 and 29 of the Act. [@1]
