Officers of the Trinidad and Tobago Police Service seized 109 packets of cannabis weighing 57.42 kilograms from two apartment buildings in the Reform Phase 4 Residential Development in Gasparillo on 21 February 2026. [@1,2] Police estimated its street value at TT$6,316,200 and described the cannabis as high-grade Colombian. [@1,2]

The search followed intelligence that a shipment had recently been imported from South America and was being stored in Gasparillo; further intelligence identified two premises in the development. [@1,2] Officers carried out surveillance at both locations, then executed search warrants with support from the Southern Division Task Force. [@1,2]

The drugs and TT$119,900 and US$146 in cash were concealed inside one of the apartments. [@1,2] No occupants were present during the searches. [@1,2] Police said investigations were continuing and that arrests were expected; the Express reported that arrests were "imminent". [@1,2]

The police service said the operation was part of its work against the importation, storage and distribution of illegal narcotics and involved coordination between divisions. [@1]
