A criminal court in Uzun district, Surkhandom region jailed five men for 15
years each after they were detained the previous summer on suspicion of drug
trafficking, the State Security Service said. [@1] The five were convicted under
Criminal Code provisions on illegal border crossing, smuggling and the
manufacture, storage and sale of drugs. [@1] Their ages ranged from 20 to 26.
[@1]

In August 2025, Border Troops, State Security Service and internal-affairs
officers detained two local residents in Uzun district as they illegally crossed
the border and tried to bring 41.66 kg of hashish and 20.37 kg of opium in from
Tajikistan. [@1] Two other accounts gave the more precise seizure as 41 kg 668 g
of hashish and 20 kg 374 g of opium. [@2] Repost.uz put the two detainees' birth
years at 2005 and 2006. [@2] Police later arrested two more residents of the
district as suspected accomplices and a 26-year-old from Denau they regarded as
the person who ordered the shipment. [@1] UzDaily said the accomplices were born
in 2000 and 2004 and that the alleged customer, from the town of Denau, was born
in 2000. [@3]

The operation was carried out by State Security Service Border Troops, the
service's Surkhandarya regional department and the regional internal-affairs
department. [@4] Zamon.uz reported that the two border crossers were detained
with the physical evidence as they crossed and delivered the drugs in from
Tajikistan. [@5] Podrobno.uz described the two border crossers as young men aged
20 and 19 and put the intercepted load at more than 20 kg of opium and almost 42
kg of hashish. [@6] Anhor.uz described both sets of arrests as arrests of the
couriers and their suspected accomplices, the buyer being a Denau resident born
in 2000. [@7]
