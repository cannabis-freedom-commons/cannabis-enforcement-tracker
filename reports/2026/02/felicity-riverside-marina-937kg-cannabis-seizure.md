Police arrested one man after officers found several multi-coloured crocus bags
packed in the tray of a white Kia K2700 parked between two moored vessels at
Riverside Marina, Cacandee Road, Felicity, around 2 pm on Friday 20 February
2026. [@1] The Guardian reported that police acted on a tip from a confidential
informant and arrested one man over the find. [@1]

The bags held high-grade Colombian cannabis, known in Trinidad as “creepy”, and
the Guardian said the weight and value were yet to be determined. [@1] A police
release reported by the Trinidad Express put the load at 937.66 kilogrammes of
high-grade Colombian cannabis with an estimated street value of $102,942,686.
[@2] The release said the operation at the marina was carried out by the Central
Division Gang Unit, Criminal Investigations Department, Crime Scene Unit and
Task Force, led by ACP Anthony Remy, coordinated by Senior Superintendent
Rudolph Bhagwandeen and supervised by ASP Mark Hernandez. [@2]

TTT News rounded the load to just over 900 kilogrammes of cannabis worth TT$102
million and said one male suspect was arrested in connection with the find. [@3]
According to police, the Guardian reported, the drugs had recently entered
Trinidad and Tobago and were offloaded at Riverside Marina, Cacandee Road,
Felicity. [@1] The Express reported that the suspect, a 36-year-old from
Freeport, was detained for questioning. [@4]

A senior police source told the Express that investigators were exploring a
possible South American criminal network’s involvement and the role of local
businesses suspected of laundering money to pay cartels in US currency. [@4] A
second senior police source said investigators were working to establish the
shipment’s intended destination and to identify all those involved in the
trafficking operation. [@4] No charge or court appearance for the arrested man
was reported in these accounts. [@2]
