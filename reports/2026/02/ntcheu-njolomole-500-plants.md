Police in Ntcheu district uprooted about 500 cannabis plants in Njolomole Village, under Chief Njolomole, in an operation reported on 26 February 2026. [@1] A second account published the day before gives the same figure and place. [@2]

Ntcheu police spokesperson Inspector Jacob Khembo said residents provided the information that led officers to the illegal cultivation. [@1,2] Three people suspected of growing the crop fled before the operation was completed, so no arrests were reported. [@1] The second account likewise says the three suspects are on the run. [@2]

The Njolomole clearance came about five days after officers destroyed another cannabis field in Wanyemba Village, under Chief Kwataine. [@1] That earlier operation is a separate event and its plants are not counted here. [@1]

Malawi licences regulated cannabis cultivation, and the destruction prompted some farmers and community members to call for clearer communication on the licensing framework, including who may apply. [@2]
