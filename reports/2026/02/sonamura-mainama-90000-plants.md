Sonamura police, working with Tripura State Rifles and other security and forest units, destroyed about 90,000 mature cannabis plants on 5 February 2026. [@1,2]
The plants were spread across 25 plots in forest land at Mainama, Aralia and Paoramura in Sepahijala district. [@1,2]

The operation began at 9 a.m. and covered about 32 acres. [@1]
Officials estimated the destroyed crop at approximately INR135 million. [@1,2]
The reports do not name a cultivator or report an arrest. [@1,2]
