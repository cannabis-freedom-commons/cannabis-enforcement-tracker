Tunisia's National Guard burned a large quantity of seized narcotics on
Wednesday 18 February 2026, in what it presented as part of national efforts to
fight drug trafficking and cut off its supply routes. [@1] The burning took
place at a cement plant, under the supervision of the public prosecutor and in
the presence of the other authorities concerned, to ensure the legal procedures
in force were followed. [@1]

National Guard spokesman Colonel-Major Houssem Eddine Jebabli told Mosaïque FM
that the operation destroyed 538 kg of cannabis. [@1] The 538 kg came from a
National Guard operation that the report calls qualitative, run by the guard's
intelligence and investigations directorate with its intelligence
sub-directorate and the central anti-narcotics brigade. [@1] The operation
followed nearly two months of intelligence work and led to the arrest of several
people implicated, while other suspects remained on the run abroad. [@1] The
National Guard's general directorate said it oversaw the destruction of a
significant quantity of seized narcotics on Wednesday 18 February 2026 as part
of ongoing national efforts to cut off drug-trafficking supply sources. [@2]

Jebabli said the presence of the media at the burning was meant to build trust
in the security institution and clear up any doubt about what happens to seized
goods. [@1] Jebabli said drug enforcement could not be limited to a repressive
approach and called for coordination between state institutions, civil society
and the media. [@1]
