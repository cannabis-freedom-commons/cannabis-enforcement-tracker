Timothy Mc Garrell, a 24-year-old labourer of the Timehri Squatting Area, East Bank Demerara, was caught trying to smuggle cannabis into the Lusignan Prison on 17 February 2026. [@1,2,3] Prison officers intercepted him at routine checks at the prison checkpoint on the East Coast of Demerara and found cannabis concealed under the soles of his slippers. [@1,3] He was arrested and handed over to the police. [@1,3]

He was charged with introducing narcotics into prison under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (Control) Act. [@1,2] He appeared on Wednesday 18 February at the Vigilance Magistrate's Court before Senior Magistrate Sunil Scarce, admitted the offence when the charge was read and was sentenced to three years' imprisonment with a $30,000 fine. [@1,2] The second account confirms the fine as $30,000 Guyanese dollars. [@2]

Guyana Times reported that the interception happened on a Saturday morning, and a prison official said the cannabis was confiscated. [@4] Director of Prisons Nicklon Elliot commended the officers for their vigilance and warned the public that such attempts would be detected and dealt with firmly. [@3]
