Guatemala's National Civil Police (PNC) arrested a Colombian man at a security
checkpoint in zone 3 of Guatemala City, as part of operations to prevent crime
and drug trafficking. [@1] Agents of the 11th precinct (comisaría 11) stopped
him at the checkpoint on avenida Elena and 25 calle, where they were inspecting
vehicles and passers-by. [@1] Police identified him as Hernando “N”, 34,
travelling on a motorcycle with plate M-222JYJ. [@1] Soy502 identified the
detainee as Hernando Valencia Loaiza. [@2]

During the search, officers found 18 small bags containing marijuana, which they
seized as evidence. [@1] Police also described him as a drug distributor and a
collector for 'gota a gota' ('drop by drop') loans. [@2]

After the find, the man was arrested and taken before the competent authorities
to resolve his legal situation. [@1] The PNC said the checkpoints are part of
operations across the capital to prevent crime and combat small-scale drug
distribution. [@1]
