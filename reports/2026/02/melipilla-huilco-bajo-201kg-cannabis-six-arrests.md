Detectives of the Peñalolén criminal-investigation brigade detained six people
in Melipilla for drug trafficking. [@1] Five of those detained were Bolivian
nationals and one was Chilean. [@2] The group included a minor. [@1] The adult
suspects were due to appear for a detention review the following day. [@1] The
detained minor had already appeared before the Melipilla guarantee court. [@1]

The operation took place in the Huilco Bajo sector of Melipilla, where officers
found people unloading packages of doubtful origin from a truck. [@1] Neighbours
had tipped off the brigade about a truck with people around it at a rural
property, unloading packages of doubtful origin. [@1] Officers found that the
truck's ramp had been modified to conceal drugs. [@1] A Briacan sniffer dog
found more drugs hidden inside the spare tyres. [@1] Detectives seized 201 kilos
of marijuana together with cocaine-based drugs, as part of a haul first reported
as 350 kilos. [@1] Officers also seized seven mobile phones, the truck and cash.
[@1]

Police said the drugs came from Bolivia and were intended for sale and
distribution mainly in Melipilla province. [@1] The combined haul was valued at
more than 2,000 million pesos. [@1] Puranoticia likewise reported that the PDI
detained six people for drug trafficking in Melipilla. [@3]
