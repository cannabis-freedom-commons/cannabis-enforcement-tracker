Bolivia's Vice-Minister of Social Defence and Controlled Substances, Ernesto
Justiniano, announced the results of the nationwide Plan Carnaval con Seguridad
anti-drug operation. [@1] The report covers 133 operations carried out across
the country, which destroyed five drug factories and led to the apprehension of
43 people linked to drug trafficking. [@1]

Officers seized a total of 4.7 tonnes of marijuana alongside 79 kilos of cocaine
during the carnival operation. [@1] The largest marijuana cases were more than
3.2 tonnes in Oruro and 1.3 tonnes in Cochabamba, where clandestine factories
were also destroyed. [@1] In Santa Cruz officers seized more than 83 kilos of
marijuana and cocaine capsules, in Potosi 21 kilos of marijuana hidden in
parcels, in Tarija drugs were seized and plantings eradicated, while in Beni
hangars and aircraft linked to drug trafficking were sealed. [@1]

The Vice-Ministry of Social Defence said 133 operations were carried out under
Plan Carnaval con Seguridad. [@2] In Santa Cruz, 83 kilos of marijuana and
cocaine capsules were confiscated. [@2] In Beni, hangars and light aircraft
allegedly linked to drug trafficking were intervened and sealed. [@2] Justiniano
described the outcome as a result worth celebrating with joy, crediting the
sustained work of the men and women providing security and protection. [@1]
