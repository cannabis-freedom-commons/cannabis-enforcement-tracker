A young man was convicted in the criminal High Court after he was found in
possession of 0.20 grams of cannabis. [@1] After his arrest the man hired a
lawyer, gathered character references and attended a men's counselling centre.
[@1]

A Cook Islands resident, Steve Boggs, wrote to the newspaper arguing that the
arrest should never have happened because the amount fell below the threshold in
the Narcotics and Misuse of Drugs Act 2004. [@1] Boggs quoted the act's
presumption provision, which applies where a person is in possession of 250
milligrams or more of tetrahydrocannabinol. [@1] He read the 0.20 grams as 200
milligrams of tetrahydrocannabinol, below the 250 milligrams he says is required
for an arrest for possession. [@1]

Boggs said the man should have faced no more than a civic infraction and a small
fine. [@1] He argued that the cannabis laws should have been changed by now to
reflect how possession is treated in the Cook Islands. [@1]
