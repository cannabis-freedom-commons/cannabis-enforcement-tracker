Frederick County sheriff's deputies stopped a car at about 2:54 a.m. on 19
February 2026 near Taney Avenue and Homestead Avenue in Frederick, Maryland, for
traffic violations. [@1] The driver and only occupant, Jordan Perry-Walker, 31,
of Frederick, got out, refused the deputy's commands and ran; deputies caught
him after a brief pursuit. [@1]

Deputies found PCP and cannabis on him during the arrest. [@1] A search of the
car produced psilocybin mushrooms. [@1] In total deputies reported seizing about
361 grams of psychedelic mushrooms, 13.7 grams of PCP, 70.6 grams of marijuana,
seven jars of THC wax, eight jars of marijuana moon rocks, three bottles of
promethazine and cut OxyContin pills. [@1]

He was taken to the Frederick County Adult Detention Center for processing. [@1]
He was charged with misdemeanor possession of cannabis with intent to
distribute, obstruction of law enforcement, and several felony and misdemeanor
counts covering PCP, LSD or hallucinogens and other non-cannabis drugs. [@1]

The sheriff's office named no other suspects and gave no court date. [@1]
