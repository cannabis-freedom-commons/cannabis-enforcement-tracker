Cyprus's Anti-Drug Service (YKAN) raided a warehouse in Nicosia after several
days of surveillance and assessment of intelligence gathered by investigators.
[@1] Two men aged 34 and 26 were arrested in connection with the case. [@1]
Police sources said the storage facility where the drugs were found is believed
to be linked to the 34-year-old suspect. [@1]

Officers found more than 140 kilograms of cannabis and more than 50 kilograms of
cocaine inside the warehouse. [@1] Police described the haul of more than 190
kilograms of narcotics as one of the largest drug consignments seen in recent
years. [@1] Beyond the narcotics, officers recovered three pistols, one a
replica, 30 rounds of ammunition, two improvised explosive devices and more than
11,000 firecrackers. [@1] Authorities also confiscated €12,765 in cash believed
to be connected to the operation. [@1]

The drugs, explosives and firearms were located in external storage areas of a
house in Strovolos. [@2] The two suspects were taken into custody to facilitate
inquiries while YKAN investigations continued. [@2]
