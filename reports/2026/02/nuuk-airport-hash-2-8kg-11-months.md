Customs officers at Nuuk Airport stopped a passenger on Friday 20 February 2026 and found about
2.8 kilograms of hash in his baggage. [@1] The man, 29, admitted the possession without
reservation, so Sermersooq District Court was able to decide the case with a straksdom, an
immediate sentence, the next day, Saturday 21 February: 11 months in the anstalt, Greenland's
prison. [@1] After the sentence he was released so that he can serve the term later. [@1]

The report, based on the Greenland Police duty officer's account, gives no origin for the flight
and does not say how the hash was packed or what it was worth. [@1]
