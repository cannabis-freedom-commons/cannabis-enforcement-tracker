CANU said officers acted on information received and executed an operation at a
residence near Tabatinga in Central Rupununi. [@2] A search of the residence
revealed several parcels containing a quantity of cannabis & cocaine. [@2]

Laurence France and Drawn Williams were detained and transported to the CANU
Lethem Office along with the narcotics. [@2] The narcotics were also seized and,
when weighed, amounted to 45 grammes (cocaine) and 27 grammes (cannabis). [@1]
CANU said during a search by its ranks, they uncovered 45 grams of cocaine
stashed in small packets and 27 grams of marijuana that was also in small
packets. [@3]

Two residents of Tabatinga, a community west of Lethem, the capital town of
Region Nine (Upper Takatu-Upper Essequibo) on Monday night remained in custody
in connection with the alleged discovery of marijuana and cocaine at a
residence, the Customs Anti-Narcotics Unit (CANU) said Monday. [@4]
