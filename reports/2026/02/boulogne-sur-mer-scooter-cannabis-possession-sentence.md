On 17 February 2026, police officers in the lycée Mariette sector of
Boulogne-sur-Mer stopped a man riding a kick scooter the wrong way. [@1] When he
saw the officers he threw a packet over the school fence, and when recovered it
contained 195.14 grams of cannabis. [@1] The man said he had just bought the
drugs and that he consumed 100 to 200 grams a month, spending about 700 euros on
them. [@1] He also said he sometimes supplied a friend or family member. [@1]

The defendant was absent at the trial, and the prosecutor noted he had not shown
up to any earlier summons. [@1] Found guilty of illegal possession and use of
narcotics, he was sentenced to 6 months' suspended imprisonment. [@1]
