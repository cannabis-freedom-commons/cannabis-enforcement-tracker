Azerbaijan police cut off the circulation of a large drug shipment in Gakh
district. [@1] According to the Ministry of Internal Affairs press office,
previously convicted 40-year-old Elnar Allahverdiyev was detained, and 19
kilograms of marijuana and the psychotropic substance methamphetamine were found
on him. [@1] The detainee said he had bought the drugs through an Iranian
citizen whose identity is being established by the investigation. [@1]

A criminal case was opened on the matter and a court ordered arrest as the
measure of restraint against Allahverdiyev. [@1]
