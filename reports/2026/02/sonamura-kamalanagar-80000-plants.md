On 15 February 2026, Sonamura Police Station led a joint operation with the 81st Battalion of the Border Security Force, Tripura State Rifles battalions, Forest officials and other agencies in the Kamalanagar, Krishna Dola and Ghatigarh forest areas. [@1] The operation destroyed approximately 80,000 mature cannabis plants in 30 plots covering about 30 acres, or roughly 12.14 hectares. [@1]

The report names the 5th, 9th, 11th and 14th TSR battalions, the 14th Battalion Women TSR, the 35th Battalion Assam Rifles and the Sonamura Forest Division among the participating units. [@1] Inspector Tapas Das, officer in charge of Sonamura Police Station, said the destroyed crop had an estimated destination-market value of about ₹12 crore. [@1]
