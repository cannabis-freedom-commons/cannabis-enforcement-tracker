Zimbabwe Republic Police arrested a man posing as a police officer, Norest
Jingura, 35, and Alison Maredza, 43, a member of the security services, on the
Harare–Beitbridge Road near Bubi. [@1] The two were travelling towards Harare in
a Toyota Hilux, registration AGP 1412, owned by Maredza. [@1] National police
spokesperson Commissioner Paul Nyathi confirmed the arrests and said police were
investigating how Jingura obtained the police uniform. [@1] Bulawayo24 reported
that Maredza is a corporal in the Zimbabwe National Army's Presidential Guard
and that the vehicle was stopped at a National Task Force anti-smuggling
roadblock in Bubi, 77 km from the Beitbridge Border Post. [@2] According to a
police memo quoted by Bulawayo24, the two were arrested at about 7:15 am on 14
February 2026 after a tip-off that the vehicle was carrying drugs. [@2]

A national police crack team intercepted the vehicle and recovered 120 cobs of
dagga, the local name for cannabis. [@1] The memo said each of the 120 cobs
weighed about 5 kg, a combined 600 kg that police valued at US$96,000 on the
street. [@2] The memo said the two told investigators they were paid US$25 per
cob and expected US$3,000 on delivery of the load. [@2] Jingura was in full
Zimbabwe Republic Police uniform with Assistant Inspector rank badges and told
police that an officer at Mkushi Academy had given it to him, the memo said.
[@2] Investigations were continuing and no charge, court date or outcome was
reported. [@2]
