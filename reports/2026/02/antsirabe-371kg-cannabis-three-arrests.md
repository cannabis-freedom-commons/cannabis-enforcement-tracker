Madagascar's national gendarmerie announced on 17 February 2026 that it had
seized 371 kg of cannabis in Antsirabe, in the centre of the country, and arrested
three men. [@1,2]

The operation followed reports from residents about drugs hidden in bags, the
gendarmerie said. [@1] Officers found the bags and arrested the three men at the
scene. [@2]

The gendarmerie said drug trafficking is a major threat to public security and
called on people to report the cultivation, sale, consumption or transport of
drugs. [@1,2] It gave no other details of the case. [@1]
