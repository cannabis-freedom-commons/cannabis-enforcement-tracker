Eliana Y. Acosta, 30, was arrested Thursday at Miami International Airport on a
cannabis-trafficking charge. [@1,3] She was stopped at the jet bridge before she
could board a flight to Santo Domingo and acknowledged the checked luggage was
hers. [@1] Acosta is a citizen of the Dominican Republic who lives in McAdoo,
Pennsylvania. [@2] She was booked into jail and appeared before a judge on
Friday, who set her bond at $10,000. [@2]

U.S. Customs and Border Protection officers were conducting inspections at the
airport because of a high trend of narcotics being smuggled outbound from the
United States. [@1,3] Officers inspected Acosta's checked bag and found 28
vacuum-sealed packages containing a green leafy substance. [@1] The substance
tested positive for marijuana and weighed about 15.26 kilograms, or 33.64
pounds. [@1]

The incident was described as the latest of several recent drug-trafficking
arrests at the airport, including an arrest earlier that week of a woman from
California. [@2]

Acosta, who lives in Pennsylvania’s town of McAdoo, was not allowed to board her American Airlines flight to Santo Domingo. [@3] A CBP Passenger Enforcement Rover Team, which conducts targeted inspections, reported Acosta was traveling to the Dominican Republic with the marijuana. [@3] Acosta, 30, was booked shortly after 12:40 a.m. on Friday at the Turner Guilford Knight Correctional Center, faced a marijuana-trafficking charge, and her bond was $10,000. [@3]
