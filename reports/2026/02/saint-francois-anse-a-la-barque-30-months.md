On the afternoon of Monday 23 February 2026, the Pointe-à-Pitre criminal court
sentenced three defendants prosecuted over the landing of about 30 kilograms of
cannabis at Saint-François. [@1] The court gave each of the three men 30 months
of firm imprisonment and ordered that they stay in detention. [@1] One of the
two longest-standing friends among the defendants works for the conseil
départemental, Guadeloupe's departmental council. [@1] The court added a
five-year ban from French territory to the Dominican defendant's sentence. [@1]

The cannabis was landed at night on the beach of Anse à la barque, in
Saint-François, in early January 2026. [@1] OFAST, France's anti-narcotics
office, made the arrests, and the Dominican defendant told the court he had
collected the drugs in Roseau, Dominica, for money he owed and to treat his sick
mother. [@1] The two other defendants denied involvement in cannabis trafficking
and said they had come to the beach around 1 a.m. by chance. [@1] Prosecutors
said phone location data and messages about being careful, including the remark
that gendarmes were too busy with the carnival, supported the charges. [@1]

OFAST 971 officers, supported by the local RAID unit, interrupted the landing in
the night of 11 to 12 January 2026 and arrested four people. [@3] The seizure
totalled 239.82 kg of cannabis herb and 3.46 kg of cannabis resin, along with
two loaded handguns. [@3] Three of the four arrested were brought before
prosecutors and presented for immediate trial on 16 January, and one asked for
time to prepare his defence, so the case was adjourned to 23 February. [@2] RCI
reported that 2 Guadeloupeans and 1 Dominican national aged 23 to 39 were the
three defendants, held in pre-trial detention pending the hearing. [@2]
France-Antilles Guadeloupe reported the same operation and total seizure of
nearly 250 kilos. [@4]
