Officers of the Bender non-departmental guard unit detained a drug stash
distributor in Bender during the daytime on 9 February 2026. [@1] The duty desk
had received information that a young man in Druzhby Narodov Park was rummaging
under bushes, digging the ground and looking around. [@1] Officers were told he
was heading toward Yermakova Street and were given his height, build and
clothing, then saw him at the crossing of Yermakova and Kishinyovskaya streets.
[@1] The unit representative said that on noticing the officers the man threw
something from his pocket, and officers detained him. [@1]

The man initially denied involvement in distributing banned substances. [@1]
Minutes later officers found a bundle a few metres away holding 22 stash packets
with hashish. [@1] The detainee had a smartphone with its navigation map
switched on and a folding knife on him. [@1]

The stash distributor was placed under arrest. [@1] For drug distribution he
faces up to 20 years imprisonment. [@1]
