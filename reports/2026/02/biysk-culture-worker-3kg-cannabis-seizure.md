Criminal-investigation officers of Biysk's municipal interior department
identified a 43-year-old local woman, who works in cultural and
mass-entertainment leisure, as a suspect in illegal drug circulation. [@1]
Police searched her home and found a cardboard box holding four packets of plant
material and a cloth sack of dried leaves in an old cupboard on the veranda.
[@1]

The seized material had the characteristic smell of hemp, the source states.
[@1] An expert examination found that the seized substance was cannabis with a
total mass of about three kilograms. [@1]

The woman admitted collecting wild bushes of so-called dope grass near her
settlement and keeping them only for personal use and for inspiration. [@1] An
investigator of the interior bodies opened a criminal case under Part 2 of
Article 228 of the Russian Criminal Code. [@1] The suspect was placed under a
written undertaking not to leave her residence and to behave properly. [@1]
