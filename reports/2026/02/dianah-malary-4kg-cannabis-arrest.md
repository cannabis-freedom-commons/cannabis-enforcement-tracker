Law enforcement officers in the Sédhiou region made a new drug seizure during a
night patrol against narcotics trafficking. [@1] During the patrol carried out
that day at about 2:30 a.m. in the Diana Bah sector next to Dianah Malary,
officers of the local post stopped a suspicious man travelling by motorcycle.
[@1] They identified the rider as O. Baldé, a 28-year-old farmer living in Saré
in the commune of Niagha in Goudomp department. [@1] Baldé is married and the
father of three children. [@2]

He was carrying four kilogrammes of chanvre indien, the term used in Senegal for
cannabis. [@2] Investigators said he had left the Fogny area and was trying to
reach Guinea-Bissau to sell the cannabis there. [@1] Officers said his
suspicious behaviour at the anchoring point drew their attention before the
stop. [@1]

Officers arrested him in flagrant possession of the cannabis. [@2] They took him
to the gendarmerie unit for investigation and the further judicial procedure.
[@1]
