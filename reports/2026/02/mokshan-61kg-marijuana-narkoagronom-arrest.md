Officers of the drug control department of the Russian Interior Ministry for
Penza Oblast stopped the activity of a 50-year-old man from Mokshan district
with no previous convictions. [@1] The man was detained and held in custody;
local outlets reported that he was under arrest when their accounts were
published. [@2]

A search of his home found 196 bundles wrapped in black tape and 200
foil-wrapped packets holding plant material. [@1] Police said he used a
gardening cooperative to produce the drug, and found stashes in the rooms of
abandoned dachas, the Russian term for country cottages, some dug into the
ground. [@1] From the caches officers removed three 50-litre plastic containers
and two large cardboard boxes filled with dry green mass. [@1] Testing
identified the seized material as 61 kg of marijuana plus more than 16 kg of
parts of a drug-containing plant of the cannabis genus. [@1]

The man told police he grew the cannabis himself, made the drug and delivered it
by car to stashes in Penza and Moscow Oblasts. [@1] He said an online market
found his buyers and paid him a salary, which police said was his only income at
the time of his detention. [@1]

The investigation unit of the Mokshan district police department opened a
criminal case under part 4 of article 228.1 of the Russian Criminal Code, which
covers the sale of drugs in especially large amounts. [@1]
