Police arrested 35-year-old Lance Alfred, also known as Bolo, and 36-year-old
Tresha Richard after searching premises in Ti Morne, Gros Islet. [@1] Both were
charged with possession of cannabis resin and possession of cannabis resin with
intent to supply. [@1] They appeared before a magistrate on February 6, 2026,
and each was granted $1 500 bail. [@1]

Officers executed search warrants on February 5, 2026, at premises linked to the
two accused. [@1] The search was at a residence in Gros Islet and led to the
seizure of cannabis resin and approximately US$13 000 in cash. [@1] The Royal
Saint Lucia Police Force confirmed the searches in a statement issued on Monday.
[@1]

Police said the large amount of money and the circumstances in which it was
found led officers to start a cash-seizure process under Section 29(a) of the
Crime Act on suspicion the funds are proceeds of criminal conduct. [@1] The
Royal Saint Lucia Police Force said the Financial Intelligence Authority was
formally notified and took an active role in pursuing the cash seizure and
conducting further financial investigations. [@1]
