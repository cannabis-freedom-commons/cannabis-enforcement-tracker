Customs officers of Transnistria's State Customs Committee detained a man near
the republic's state border, in Bender's Solnechny neighbourhood. [@1] The
customs committee's press service said the man was carrying a drug stash, known
locally as a zakladka, that he had just picked up. [@1] During questioning he
said he had bought the substance, which he said was hashish, through an online
platform in Moldova. [@1]

An investigation team went to the scene, and the man's phone held records of an
order on an internet site that distributes narcotics and the coordinates of the
stash. [@1] Investigators seized the package and sent it for expert examination,
which confirmed it contained hashish. [@1] A criminal case was opened under part
1 of article 185-1 of Transnistria's Criminal Code, covering smuggling of
narcotic drugs, psychotropic substances or their analogues. [@1] The man is
under arrest and faces three to seven years in prison. [@1]
