Kyle Reece Wright, 25, of Douglas, was jailed for a total of four years and
eight months for cannabis dealing, assault and a knife offence. [@2] Police
searched the Douglas home where he had been staying on 14 October 2024 and found
656 g of cannabis bush valued at £3,686. [@2] The same search found 138 g of
cannabis resin worth £2,766. [@2] It also found 243 g of ketamine, valued at
between £9,724 and £12,155. [@1] Wright's girlfriend and her parents were
arrested at the address during the raid and later released without charge. [@1]
Wright told officers after his arrest that all the drugs were his. [@1]

Messages and voice notes on his phone showed he had supplied cannabis between 20
December 2023 and 4 October 2024. [@2] The court heard he directed others to
sell drugs for him, indicating he "might not be at the bottom of the pile" in
the supply chain. [@1] Some messages showed him threatening violence against
people who owed him money. [@2] He had also been using his unsuspecting former
girlfriend's bank account to receive payments for drugs. [@1] He had been given
a suspended prison sentence in January 2024 and was in breach of it when he kept
dealing. [@2]

Wright admitted supplying cannabis, possessing cannabis bush, cannabis resin and
ketamine with intent to supply, causing actual bodily harm, threatening
behaviour and possessing a knife in public. [@1] He was jailed for 20 months for
the assault, two years and four months consecutively for being concerned in
supplying cannabis, and a further eight months for breaching his suspended
sentence. [@2] Deemster Graeme Cook called Wright a "caveman" for throwing the
glass at the man's head in the Outback nightclub in Douglas on 4 May 2025. [@2]
Cook said Wright had shown no remorse and that his drug dealing had led to three
innocent people being arrested. [@1]
