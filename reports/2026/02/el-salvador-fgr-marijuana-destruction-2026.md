El Salvador's Fiscalía General de la República, the attorney general's office,
and the National Civil Police supervised the destruction of drugs seized in
separate operations across the country. [@1] The elimination took place at
controlled facilities under strict guard, with specialised prosecutors present
who certified compliance with the established legal protocols. [@1]

The authorities carried out field tests and weighed the loads before confirming
what had been eliminated. [@1] The marijuana destroyed weighed 2,658.90 grams,
about 2.66 kg. [@1] The same destruction eliminated 3,675.91 grams of cocaine.
[@1] It also eliminated 32,113 grams of methamphetamine. [@1] The FGR put the
economic value of the destroyed substances at 96,572.67 dollars, calculated on
the estimated illicit-market price. [@1]

The authorities said the seizures formed part of the national strategy to reduce
drug trafficking and consumption on Salvadoran territory. [@1] The PNC said
inter-institutional coordination strengthens controls and speeds the response to
crime linked to the sale and distribution of narcotics. [@1]
