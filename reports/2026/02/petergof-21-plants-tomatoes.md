Police detained two residents of Petergof, a town inside St Petersburg, after searching their home.
[@1,2] Officers seized 21 cannabis plants, almost seven kilograms of dried cannabis, cultivation
equipment, electronic scales and packaging material. [@1,2] The men were 31 and 38 years old. [@2]

One of them told officers that he had planted tomatoes, "but it grew, and not those". [@1,2] Tests
confirmed that one of the packets held dried parts of the cannabis plant. [@1]

A criminal case was opened for attempted large-scale drug trafficking. [@1,2] Both suspects were
remanded in custody, and police were checking other episodes and possible accomplices. [@1]
