District Court No 2 of the Enbekshikazakh District in the Almaty Region found
citizen Sh guilty of illegally planting and growing narcotic-containing plants
on an especially large scale. [@1] The court found that in spring 2025 he
illegally planted and grew cannabis plants on the land of a peasant farm in the
Enbekshikazakh District. [@2] Officers found the 233 cannabis bushes hidden
among rows of corn during a special operation by the Interior Ministry's
drug-crime department together with the National Security Committee. [@4] In
August 2025, during a special operation by National Security Committee and
Almaty Region police officers, a 36-year-old local resident was detained. [@3]
On a plot of 4.5 hectares among the corn, police found the 233 cannabis bushes
and seized 58 test tubes of marijuana seeds from a makeshift hut. [@3]

Police seized 233 narcotic-containing bushes during the investigative actions.
[@1] The police report identified the field as belonging to a 36-year-old local
resident, who was detained and taken to the Shelek police station, where a
criminal case was opened. [@4] A forensic examination identified the seized
plants as the narcotic cannabis with a total mass of more than 49 kilograms, a
specially large amount. [@2]

At trial the defendant fully admitted guilt and repented. [@1] His guilt was
confirmed by the case evidence, including records of the scene inspection,
expert findings and witness testimony. [@1] The court qualified his actions
under point 2 of part 3 of Article 300 of Kazakhstan's Criminal Code, illegal
planting and growing of prohibited narcotic-containing plants on an especially
large scale. [@1]

Taking account of the nature of the crime and mitigating circumstances, the
court sentenced him to 7 years' imprisonment in a medium-security penal
institution. [@1] The court also ordered him to pay the procedural costs of the
expert examinations and a compulsory payment to the Victims' Compensation Fund,
and ordered the physical evidence destroyed. [@1] The verdict had not entered
into legal force and could be appealed through the established procedure. [@1]
