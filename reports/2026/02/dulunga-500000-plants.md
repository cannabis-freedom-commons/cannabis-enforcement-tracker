India Today NE reported that security forces carried out a joint cannabis-eradication operation in Dulunga forest under Sonamura subdivision on 1 February 2026. [@1] The operation lasted nearly eight hours, from 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., and personnel destroyed more than 500,000 cannabis plants in 92 plots spread across about 170 acres of forest land. [@1]

Northeast Herald independently reported the destruction of more than five lakh plants worth an estimated ₹75 crore, in 92 sites covering nearly 170 acres of government forest land. [@2] It said personnel from Sonamura and Jatrapur police stations, the BSF, TSR, CRPF and Forest Department participated, and that all plants were uprooted and destroyed on site. [@2]
