## Record raids on licensed growers

Police in North Macedonia seized more than 40 tons of cannabis in a nationwide operation against companies licensed to grow cannabis for medical use. [@1,2] Interior Ministry spokesman Goce Andreevski said the operation began on 5 February 2026 and was continuing. [@1] In a factory raid near Skopje officers took nine tons of marijuana and 1,300 bottles of cannabis oil. [@1] Another 31 tons of marijuana and biomass for oil production were seized in the east of the country. [@1] Investigation sources put the split at about ten tons around Skopje and 30 tons in facilities in the Strumica region. [@2]

The searches were carried out by the police with the Organized Crime Prosecutor's Office over suspected irregularities at the licensed companies. [@2] Investigators suspect licensed producers diverted large quantities to the black market instead of legal pharmaceutical channels. [@1] Andreevski said the raided Skopje firm showed large gaps between the cannabis it recorded as held, produced, bought and sold. [@1] The firms and the people involved were not officially named. [@1]

## Reactions

The record seizures set off accusations between the ruling VMRO-DPMNE party and the opposition Social Democrats over who enabled the illicit trade. [@1]
