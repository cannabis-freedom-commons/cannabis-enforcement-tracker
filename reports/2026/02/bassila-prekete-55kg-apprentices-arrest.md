A joint customs and police check intercepted a minibus coming from Togo at
Adjiro in the Prèkètè district of Bassila commune on Sunday 15 February 2026.
[@2] When customs officers approached, the driver fled and abandoned the vehicle
with two apprentices inside. [@1] The two people left behind were Togolese
apprentice drivers aged 18 and 19 who live in Badjou in Togo. [@2] Both
apprentices were arrested and taken to the border police station for
investigation. [@2] The two apprentices were placed in custody for possession of
drugs and ammunition. [@1]

Officers searching the minibus found about 55 kg of chanvre indien, the French
term for cannabis, and 2,500 calibre-12 cartridges hidden among luggage. [@1]
The minibus was registered TG 1074 BO and had come from Togo before being
stopped at Adjiro. [@2] Customs officers sealed the cartridges while police kept
the seized cannabis. [@2]

Investigators opened an inquiry to clarify each person's role, identify the
fleeing driver and establish the cargo's intended destination and possible
accomplices. [@2] The authorities describe the border roads around Bassila as
routes regularly used by cross-border networks to move prohibited goods between
Togo and Benin. [@1]
