On the morning of 11 February, officers of the PSP's 3rd Police Division carried
out an operation in Bairro de São Tomé in Paranhos, Porto. [@1] The operation
ended with the detention of a 44-year-old man and the seizure of drugs stored in
a private residence. [@1]

A home search found that the suspect held a stock described as enough to supply
the local illicit market for an extended period. [@1] Officers seized 1,548.50
grams of hashish, identified as cannabis resin, equated in the report to 3,097
individual doses. [@1] The amount was described as more than one and a half
kilos of drugs, well above the personal-consumption threshold. [@1]

After being brought before the judicial authorities, the detainee was released.
[@1] He was placed under Termo de Identidade e Residência, described as the most
basic coercive measure in the Portuguese Code of Criminal Procedure. [@1] The
measure requires him to declare his residence and to report any change of
address lasting more than five days to the court. [@1] It also requires him to
appear before the authorities whenever summoned. [@1]
