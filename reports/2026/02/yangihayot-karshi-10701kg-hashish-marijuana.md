State Security Service officers in Tashkent's Yangihayot district stopped a
resident of the city's Almazar district, born in 1981 and previously convicted
of drug offences. [@1] A search of a bag he was carrying, in front of witnesses,
found 9 kg 829 g of hashish, seized as evidence. [@3] Gazeta.uz rounded the same
Yangihayot find to 9.8 kg of hashish. [@1]

State Security Service and internal-affairs officers then searched the home of a
Karshi resident born in 1959 in Kashkadarya region. [@1] The house held 380 g of
hashish and 492 g of marijuana, which officers said were brought from a
neighbouring state and kept for sale. [@1]

Taken together, the two cannabis finds come to 10.701 kg of hashish and
marijuana, part of a combined 20.2 kg of drugs including opium and synthetics.
[@3] According to the service, the Ohangaron detainee worked as a stash-keeper
for an online drug shop, taking large consignments from hiding places, splitting
them and delivering them to regions. [@1]

Criminal cases were opened over all three episodes under part 2 of article 246,
smuggling, and part 5 of article 273 of the Criminal Code, and the suspects were
remanded in custody. [@2]
