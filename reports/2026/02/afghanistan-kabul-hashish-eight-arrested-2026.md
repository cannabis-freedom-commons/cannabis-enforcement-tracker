Counter-narcotics police in Kabul seized 23 kilograms of hashish and detained
eight suspects, the Ministry of Interior said on Sunday, 8 February 2026. [@1]
The operations took place in areas of the capital's 4th, 15th and 17th police
districts. [@1] Police also seized about 38,000 narcotic tablets in the same
raids; the ministry did not identify the tablets and they are not counted as
cannabis. [@1]

The ministry said the eight detainees would be handed to judicial authorities
after preliminary investigations. [@1] A ministry statement carried by The Kabul
Times described coordinated operations across Kabul's 4th, 15th and 17th
districts, with the substances seized within the past day. [@2]

The Kabul Times put the tablet seizure at 38,200, all of them described as K
tablets, and reported the same eight arrests and 23 kilograms of hashish. [@2]
Pajhwok's Pashto version reported the same figures, describing the 23 kilograms
as charas, the local term for hashish. [@3]

The Kabul Times reported the ministry as saying anti-drug operations had
increased significantly across the country in recent months. [@2]
