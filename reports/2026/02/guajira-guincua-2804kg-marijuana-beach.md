Officers of the Strategic Operations Group (GOES), the Bolivarian National
Guard's National Anti-Drug Command and the Bolivarian National Police seized 107
buried bales of marijuana in the Guincua sector of Guajira municipality, Zulia.
[@2] The bales were dug up by hand on the shore of Caimare Chico beach, in
Guincua, an indigenous municipality on Zulia's border with Colombia. [@3]
Officers reached the place where the marijuana cargo was buried. [@4] Police and
military personnel made the seizure as part of a deployment Venezuela has
maintained for months against drug trafficking. [@1]

The Interior Ministry put the gross weight at 2,804 kilograms and 6 grams, in
about 5,729 wraps of plant material it described as active cannabis, commonly
called marijuana. [@2] An earlier GOES account gave the same load a gross weight
of 2,864 kilograms and 5 grams. [@3] State channel VTV said the 107 bales were
found in Guajira after intelligence work by the Strategic Operations Corps, the
National Guard anti-drug command and the Bolivarian National Police. [@1]

VTV published images of National Guard and police officers unearthing large
packages in a sandy, apparently empty area. [@1] The ministry called the seizure
a blow to drug trafficking in Zulia, carried out by popular, military and police
forces. [@2] The ministry said the operation followed the line set by the
sectoral vice-president for policy, citizen security and peace, Captain Diosdado
Cabello. [@2]
