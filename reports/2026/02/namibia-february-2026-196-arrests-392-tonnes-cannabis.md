Namibian Police spokesperson deputy commissioner Kauna Shikwambi said the police
seized various illicit drugs and arrested 196 suspects in February. [@1] She
said the seizures and arrests were recorded between 1 and 28 February. [@1] New
Era reported that Namibian Police said the arrests covered 1 to 28 February 2026
and that 200 people were arrested. [@2] Police said the 196 suspects included
187 Namibian nationals, seven Angolan nationals and one Zimbabwean national.
[@1] New Era listed the same nationality breakdown of 187 Namibians, seven
Angolans, one Zimbabwean and one Tanzanian. [@2]

The drugs seized included 391 879.86 kilograms of cannabis, about 391.9 tonnes.
[@1] New Era reported that cannabis weight as 391,879 kilograms. [@2] The same
seizures also included 28 363 mandrax tablets, 47.7 grams of cocaine powder,
412.5 units of crack cocaine and 50 abortion tablets. [@1]

Shikwambi said the recordings highlighted the police commitment to law
enforcement. [@1] She said police remained determined to combat illegal drugs
and to arrest those found in possession of them or involved in dealing in them.
[@1] The figures cover seizures and arrests recorded across that February
period, not a single named raid. [@1]
