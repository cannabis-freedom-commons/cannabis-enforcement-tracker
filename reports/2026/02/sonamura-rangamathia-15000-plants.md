## Eradication

Sonamura police, working with the 11th and 14th Battalions of the Tripura State Rifles and other state forces, ran a joint cannabis-destruction operation in the Pulamura–Rangamathia area on 3 February 2026. [@1] Teams found seven plots in forest land at Rangamathia and Bholamura, covering nearly five acres, and burned about 15,000 mature plants. [@1]

The officers also recovered 136 kg of dry cannabis in two large packets and four sacks. [@1] Police treated the processed cannabis as unclaimed, registered a case and said the investigation would identify those responsible for cultivation and storage. [@1]
