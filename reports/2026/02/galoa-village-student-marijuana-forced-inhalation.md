A group of youths in Galoa Village, Serua, tied up a 16-year-old boy, threatened
him with a cane knife and forced him to inhale marijuana, and filmed the attack.
[@1] The attack happened on the Saturday before the Fiji Sun's Wednesday 11
February 2026 report, which gives it as Saturday 7 February 2026. [@1]

At the time the minister spoke, police had not answered questions about the
case. [@4]

Police questioned several students and released them, and said investigations
were continuing. [@2] Police urged the public not to share the footage and to
let the investigation proceed without interference. [@3]
