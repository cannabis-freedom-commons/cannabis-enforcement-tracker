Mumbai's Anti-Narcotics Cell Worli unit arrested four people, including two
Nepal nationals and two Mumbai residents. [@2] Police identified the accused as
Dipak Inderbahadur Lamba and Sunder Inderbahadur Lamba of Nepal and
Aahteshamuddin Aabid Shaikh and Farah Faruq Shah of Vile Parle. [@2] The two
Nepal nationals were reported as residents of Chitwan district, and the two Vile
Parle accused as a married couple. [@1]

In total, officers seized 2.81 kg of charas valued by police at Rs 2.81 crore.
[@2] In the initial operation officers seized 1.715 kg of charas and Rs 52,000
in cash from two accused. [@2] On February 26 officers arrested a 31-year-old
peddler in Mumbai's Lower Link area and recovered another 1.103 kg of charas and
Rs 55,000 in cash. [@2] On February 27 officers arrested a 28-year-old woman
accused of assisting the financial transactions. [@2] The two couriers had
stayed at a hotel in Andheri and were apprehended from Bandra in a trap laid on
intelligence inputs while preparing to distribute the charas. [@3]

The Worli unit registered the case on February 25, 2026 under Sections 8(c),
20(c) and 29 of the NDPS Act. [@2] Police described the haul as high-quality
Afghan hashish, sold as joint hash, and the network as an international one.
[@1] Police said the network supplied drugs into India through the Nepal route
and they were probing possible Pakistan links. [@1] Further investigation was
under way to identify the wider supply chain and network. [@2]
