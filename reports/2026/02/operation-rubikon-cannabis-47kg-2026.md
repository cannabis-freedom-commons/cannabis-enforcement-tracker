Between 12 and 17 February 2026 Europol supported a joint operation by police in
Ukraine, Poland and Moldova against a criminal network producing and trafficking
drugs, mainly synthetic cathinones such as alpha-PVP. [@1] On the operation's
main day, Ukrainian National Police and Poland's Central Bureau of Investigation
carried out coordinated checks at about 510 locations. [@1] Officers dismantled
36 illegal production sites and 74 storage facilities. [@1]

Officers detained 97 people in Ukraine under the criminal procedure code and
notified 123 people that they were suspects under several articles of the
criminal code, depending on their role. [@2] Europol put the totals for the
three countries at 129 people facing criminal charges and 103 taken into
custody. [@1] A Catalan security blog's account of the Europol statement listed
47 kg of cannabis among the confiscations and 129 people investigated with 103
arrested. [@3]

Police said the network, led by a Ukrainian organiser, built a full production
chain from smuggled EU precursors to sale through marketplaces, forums and
closed chats paid in cryptocurrency. [@2] Police said the network's combined
capacity was more than 700 kg of drugs a month and its estimated profit more
than 400 million hryvnias. [@2] In a parallel phase in Poland, six people linked
to the laboratories were detained, and a courier was held in Moldova trying to
take Ukrainian-made drugs across the border. [@2] Members of the groups face up
to twelve years in prison with confiscation of property. [@2]

The submitted EU NEIGHBOURS east summary of the operation reported the same 36
production sites, 74 stores, 129 people charged and 103 detained. [@1]
