Angola's Criminal Investigation Service in Benguela detained three citizens aged
between 34 and 48 in an operation in Bairro Vimbalambi, describing them as the
alleged owners of the drug. [@1] The operation took place on a Thursday in the
municipality of Catumbela in Benguela province. [@2] During the operation three
men aged between 34 and 48 were detained on suspicion of drug trafficking. [@2]

Officers found liamba, as cannabis is known in Angola, buried inside the yard of
a residence in Catumbela. [@1] The drug had been buried under a layer of sand in
the yard of a residence in Vimbalambi in an attempt to hide it. [@2] The SIC
linked the individuals to storage and possible distribution of the substance in
the region. [@2]

The SIC said inquiries were continuing to identify other sales and storage
points connected to the case. [@1] The detainees were expected to be presented
to the Public Prosecutor's Office in the following days while the seized product
followed the procedures provided by law. [@2] No charge or court outcome beyond
the expected presentation to the prosecutor was reported. [@2]
