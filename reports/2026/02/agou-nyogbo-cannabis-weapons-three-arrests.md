Togo's national police arrested three men at Agou Nyogbo, in the Agou prefecture,
in a pre-dawn operation on 26 February 2026. [@1,2] The men were named as K. Komi,
49, A. Komi, 27, and K. Kokou, 25. [@1]

Police said the operation targeted illegal possession of weapons and trafficking in
drugs and prohibited goods. [@1] K. Komi, described as the network's main figure,
was arrested behind his house as he tried to flee. [@1,2] A mason who travels
regularly to Ghana, he and the others were suspected of buying their supplies
there. [@1,2]

Officers seized a Reck Cobra revolver and an Uma SP50 automatic pistol, both 4.5
mm, two homemade hunting rifles, 80 cartridges and 127 pellets. [@1,2] They also
seized five blocks and 34 joints of cannabis and two small boxes of cannabis
seeds. [@1,2]

A fourth man, A. Akiza, 60, a mechanic at an airport company, was implicated after
he admitted giving K. Komi the revolver and pistol, which he said he had found in
the bush four years earlier. [@1,2] Police said the three arrested men would be
presented to the public prosecutor. [@1,2]
