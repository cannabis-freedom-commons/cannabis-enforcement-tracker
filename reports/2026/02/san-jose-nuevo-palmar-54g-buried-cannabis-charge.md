Police executed a search warrant at a home on Corozo Road in San Jose Nuevo
Palmar Village, Orange Walk District, shortly after 6:00 a.m. on Friday morning.
[@1] Officers found four plastic bags containing 26 smaller transparent bags
with a green leafy substance suspected to be cannabis, buried about three inches
underground roughly ten feet from the rear of the house. [@1]

The 60-year-old resident was informed of the discovery, cautioned, and taken to
the Orange Walk Police Station along with the suspected drugs. [@1] At the
station the substance was weighed and amounted to 54 grams, and the exhibits
were sealed and labeled; the resident reportedly declined to sign the label.
[@1]

He was formally arrested and charged with possession of controlled drugs, and
the matter is expected to proceed through the court process. [@1]
