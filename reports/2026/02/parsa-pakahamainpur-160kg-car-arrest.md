A joint Armed Police Force Nepal and Nepal Police team arrested one driver,
Buddhi Lal Ghalan, with cannabis in Pakahamainpur Rural Municipality, Parsa.
[@2] Officers caught Ghalan while he was attempting to throw packets of cannabis
from the trunk of his car. [@2] Police handed Ghalan over to the Area Police
Office at Pokhariya for further legal action. [@2]

On Monday 16 February 2026, the team intercepted Ghalan’s Suzuki Swift car
coming from Madhuwani Chowk in Pakahamainpur. [@2] Officers seized 160 kilograms
of marijuana from the car at Pakhaminpur. [@1] The seized car was a Suzuki Swift
with registration Pra 3-01-002C 5432. [@1]

Officers also recovered Rs 22,500 in cash and a smartphone from the driver. [@1]
The APF Nepal 13th Battalion headquarters, through Superintendent Mohan Bahadur
Kshetri, was named as the source of the account. [@1]

The arrested driver was sent to the Area Police Office at Pokhariya for
necessary action, according to the APF 13th Battalion commander. [@3]
