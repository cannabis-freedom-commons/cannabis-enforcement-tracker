Northeast Today reported on 4 February 2026 that Assam Rifles conducted an operation against illicit cannabis cultivation in the Boxanagar area of Tripura, jointly with the Forest Department, Agartala. [@1] The account says the team identified and destroyed approximately 175,000 cannabis saplings spread across nearly 50 acres. [@1]

Sentinel Assam independently reported the same figures and said officials estimated the destroyed crop's market value at approximately ₹25.72 crore. [@2] The two accounts describe the activity as a coordinated Assam Rifles–Forest Department eradication operation in Boxanagar. [@1,2]
