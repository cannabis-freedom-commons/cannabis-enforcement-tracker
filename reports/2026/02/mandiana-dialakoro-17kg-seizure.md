In Dialakoro sub-prefecture, Mandiana, the patrol unit of the Mandiana Central
Commissariat arrested a 27-year-old woman found in possession of drugs. [@1]
Police placed her at the disposal of the competent services for further
investigation. [@1]

Guinea's National Police seized 17 kilograms of cannabis, called chanvre indien,
in the operation. [@1] A second account says the same patrol-unit operation in
Dialakoro led to the interception of a young woman with a large quantity of
drugs. [@2] It says the 27-year-old suspect held 17 kilograms of chanvre indien
at the moment of her arrest. [@2]

She was handed to the Mandiana Central Commissariat so investigators could
identify possible accomplices and determine the origin and destination of the
seized drugs. [@2] Police present the seizure as part of the national strategy
against drug trafficking and banditry. [@2] Authorities called on citizens to
stay vigilant and cooperate, recalling that the toll-free number 117 remains
operational. [@2]
