Agents of the National Drug Control Directorate (DNCD), prosecutors and airport
security officers seized 15 packages of suspected marijuana at Las Américas
International Airport in Boca Chica, Santo Domingo province. [@1] The Public
Ministry and the DNCD were investigating the shipment, and no arrests were
reported. [@1]

A canine unit alerted officers to controlled substances inside a cardboard box
that had arrived from the United States. [@1] Working under a prosecutor's
direction, the teams opened the box and found 15 packages wrapped in transparent
plastic bags. [@1] The packages had a preliminary weight of 18 pounds (about
8.16 kg), according to the DNCD statement. [@2]

The manifest listed the sender at an address in Elkhorn, Sacramento, California,
and the final destination as a supposed company in Miami, United States. [@1]
The box was in transit through the Dominican Republic, according to the
manifest. [@1] The operation was carried out with the Public Ministry and the
Specialized Airport Security Corps (CESAC). [@2] The DNCD said it was
intensifying interdiction at airports, ports, borders and other points in the
country. [@1]
