A 42-year-old businesswoman of Central Amelia's Ward, Linden and a 36-year-old
driver of Bamia, Soesdyke-Linden Highway were arrested after police searched a
motor lorry at a house in Central Amelia's Ward. [@1] Guyana Times said the
search took place at about 01:30 on Sunday in Regional Division #10, acting on
information received. [@2]

Police found 4.536 kilogrammes of marijuana in a haversack behind the driver's
seat. [@1] Guyana Times reported that the haversack held several parcels of
leaves, seeds and stems suspected to be cannabis, and that the cannabis was
weighed in the presence of the suspects. [@2] Officers also found three 9mm
pistols and nine .32 rounds of ammunition concealed among the cannabis. [@1]

Guyana Times said the businesswoman was arrested at the house and the driver was
arrested later at his residence after further investigations. [@2] The firearms,
ammunition and cannabis were lodged pending further investigation, and both
suspects remained in custody as investigations continued. [@2]
