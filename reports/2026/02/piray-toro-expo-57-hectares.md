Agents of Senad's Regional Department No. 1, with support from the agency's
Special Forces, carried out the operation named Toro Expo at Piray, in Capitán
Bado district, Amambay department. [@2] Senad is Paraguay's Secretaría Nacional
Antidrogas, the national anti-drug agency, and its head, Jalil Rachid, holds the
rank of minister. [@1]

Following earlier intelligence work and field incursions, the teams found a
mechanised cannabis production complex of 15 interconnected parcels covering
close to 60 hectares. [@2] In coordination with prosecutor Celso Morales, they
eradicated 57 hectares of cannabis in the growth stage. [@2] They also
dismantled six clandestine camps used as shelters, logistics bases and
collection centres. [@1] Rachid said the plantation was fully mechanised, on
very fertile soil, which he said showed how much the criminal organisation
behind it had invested. [@1]

Senad estimated the eradicated crop would have produced more than 170 tonnes of
marijuana, mainly for the Brazilian market. [@2] Rachid put the economic loss to
the organisation at USD 25.6 million, and said the crop would double in price
once on the Brazilian market. [@1]

Senad called the operation a decisive blow to drug-trafficking structures on the
border, sapping their production capacity and finances. [@1] No arrests were
announced in the accounts of the operation. [@3]
