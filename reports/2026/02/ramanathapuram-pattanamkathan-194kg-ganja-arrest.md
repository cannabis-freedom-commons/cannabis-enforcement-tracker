Acting on specific intelligence, Ramanathapuram Q-Branch police seized 194 kg of
ganja intended to be smuggled to Sri Lanka on Monday 2 February 2026. [@1]
Police intercepted the car during a vehicle check at the Pattanamkathan East
Coast Road junction. [@1] Officers searching the car found 194 kg of ganja
packed in 97 bundles hidden inside it. [@1] Police arrested the driver,
Manikandan, 40, of Gummidipoondi in Thiruvallur district. [@1]

Preliminary investigation found the consignment had been brought from Chennai
and was meant to be taken illegally to Sri Lanka. [@1] DT Next placed the
interception near Subbaiya Nagar on the East Coast Road junction at
Pattinamkathan and described the vehicle as a luxury car. [@2] DT Next reported
that police valued the ganja at around Rs 30 lakh and also seized the car. [@2]
Ada Derana described the detained man as a 40-year-old driver without naming
him. [@3]

The Q-Branch police handed the seized narcotics and the accused to the
Ramanathapuram Narcotic Intelligence Bureau for further legal proceedings. [@1]
Police said they were investigating others involved in the smuggling network.
