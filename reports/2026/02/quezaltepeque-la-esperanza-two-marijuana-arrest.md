El Salvador's National Civil Police arrested Guillermo Aldair Laureano Meléndez
and Héctor Wilfredo Jovel in the colonia La Esperanza of the Quezaltepeque
district, La Libertad Norte. [@2] Police said the two men were stopped on the
night of Monday 9 February 2026 while carrying drugs in a vehicle. [@2] Infobae
dates the arrests in the colonia La Esperanza, Quezaltepeque, to 9 February
2026. [@1]

During the procedure officers seized several portions of marijuana, the car the
two men were travelling in and two digital scales. [@3] Diario1 reported that
the scales were suspected of being used to sell the drug. [@3]

Police said both men would be referred to court on a charge of illicit drug
trafficking. [@2]
