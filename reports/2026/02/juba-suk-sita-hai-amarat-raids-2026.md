Central Equatoria security forces raided drug hotspots in Suk Sita and Hai
Amarat in Juba on 24 February 2026, targeting people involved in distributing
substances in the municipality. [@1] Deputy Governor Paulino Lukudu Obede led
the operation with the Juba City Council to stop the sale of substances in Juba
Municipality. [@1] The high-level operation included the Mayor of Juba City,
state ministers and the Commissioner of Juba County, and focused on Suk Sita and
Hai Amarat near Logele House. [@1] A later state health account also described a
targeted operation against drug trafficking and abuse in Juba on 24 February
2026 that seized illegal substances and warned offenders. [@2]

Authorities said they acted on intelligence reports to focus on individuals and
locations involved in selling and misusing illicit substances. [@1] The seized
items included marijuana locally known as bangi, alongside tramadol,
codeine-based syrups and non-medical oxytocin injections. [@1] The crackdown
also covered crystal methamphetamine known as ice, misused prescription
medicines and unlawful alcohol sales. [@1] City officials warned that people who
traffic or facilitate illegal substances will face the full force of the law.
[@1] The council urged residents to cooperate with law enforcement agencies and
report drug-related activity, saying collective action and vigilance would make
Juba safer and healthier. [@1]
