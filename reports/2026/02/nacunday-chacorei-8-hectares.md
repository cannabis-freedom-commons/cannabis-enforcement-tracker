On 9 February 2026 at around 06:15, a prosecutor-police team carried out a
court-ordered eradication operation under a warrant from the Iruña criminal
court and Judge Miguel Ángel Brítez. [@2] The team was led by anti-drug
prosecutor Elvio Aguilera Vázquez with other Public Ministry officials and
officers of the National Police Anti-Narcotics Department. [@1] The 8 hectares
of marijuana were on a property in the natural reserve at Chacore'i in Nacunday
district. [@1] The field lay inside the forest reserve of Nacunday National Park
in Alto Paraná. [@1]

Officers first found a 4-hectare plot of the hallucinogenic herb in its growth
stage after walking about 2,000 metres along an embankment road. [@1] After a
preliminary field test came back positive, they cut and destroyed that planting
because its volume made removal impossible. [@1] The team then found a second
trail leading to another 4-hectare plot that was also developing. [@1] Following
the same protocol, the second planting was fully destroyed. [@2] In total, the
operation eliminated 8 hectares, causing significant economic losses to the
marijuana growers. [@1]
