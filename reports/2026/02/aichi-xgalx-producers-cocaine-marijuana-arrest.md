Japanese authorities arrested two Korean music producers affiliated with the
entertainment agency XGALX on suspicion of drug possession on Monday. [@1] Simon
Jakops, whose real name is Park Jun-ho, heads XGALX and works as a producer for
the Japanese girl group XG. [@1] Fellow XGALX producer and singer Chancellor,
whose real name is Kim Jeong-seung, was arrested with Jakops. [@1] The two were
taken into custody early on Monday at a hotel room in Aichi Prefecture on
suspicion of cocaine possession. [@1]

Four people were present at the scene, including the two producers and two
employees of a local agency. [@1] During the arrest, officers seized several
packets of cocaine and one packet of marijuana. [@1] Local police had been
investigating the case after receiving an anonymous tip in March last year. [@1]
Police are investigating how the drugs were acquired and the circumstances
surrounding their acquisition. [@1]

XGALX said it takes the concern caused by the arrest very seriously and is fully
cooperating with the investigation. [@1] The arrests were reported as suspected
violations of Japan's Narcotics and Psychotropics Control Act. [@1]
