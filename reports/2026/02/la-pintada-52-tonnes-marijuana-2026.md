National Police officers seized 5.2 tonnes of marijuana in the municipality of
La Pintada, Antioquia, during search and control operations. [@1] The marijuana
was being carried in a van that was abandoned in an attempt to evade the police.
[@1]

The National Police said the operation prevented the shipment from entering
Medellín and its surrounding municipalities. [@1] Police put the criminal
profits affected at about 2 billion pesos. [@1] The force said the seizure
prevented the distribution of more than five million doses. [@1]

The National Police said it would continue permanent checks on the country's
road corridors. [@1] The force said it would not allow criminal organizations to
use the roads for drug trafficking or other illegal activity. [@1]
