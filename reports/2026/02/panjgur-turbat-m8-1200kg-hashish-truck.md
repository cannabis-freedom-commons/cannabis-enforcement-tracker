Anti-Narcotics Force officers stopped a truck on the Panjgur–Turbat section of
the M-8 highway in Balochistan on 11 February 2026 and found hashish hidden in
secret compartments. [@2] The force seized 1.2 tonnes of hashish, and an ANF
spokesperson announced the operation on Wednesday 11 February. [@2] Two
suspected smugglers travelling in the truck were arrested. [@2] The ANF
estimated the hashish at about $66.15 million on the international market. [@2]

The spokesperson said the network, allegedly run by a trafficker named Ishaq
Baloch, moved drugs from Panjgur through Turbat to Gwadar and Pasni, then
shipped them by sea to Gulf countries, Yemen and Tanzania on high-speed fishing
boats. [@2] The ANF said initial investigations suggested the hashish had been
smuggled from Afghanistan and was destined for onward trafficking abroad. [@2] A
case was registered under Pakistan's narcotics law, and officers were still
looking for the alleged ringleader and other members of the gang. [@2] Radio
Pakistan reported that the two arrested men told investigators during
questioning that the drugs had come from Afghanistan. [@3]

The Friday Times, writing about Afghanistan's drug trade, put the seizures in
the same ANF announcement at 1,353 kilograms of narcotics worth more than 77.85
million rupees (about $280,000) across seven operations, with nine people
arrested. [@1] The same ANF announcement included 1.3 kilograms of crystal
methamphetamine found in Peshawar. [@1]
