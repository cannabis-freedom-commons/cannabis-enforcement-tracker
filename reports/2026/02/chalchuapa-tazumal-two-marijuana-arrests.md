National Civil Police officers captured a man and a woman who were found
transporting several marijuana packages in Chalchuapa district, Santa Ana Oeste,
on Tuesday night and Wednesday. [@2] Alberto Fernando Escalante Salazar was
detained in Chalchuapa transporting several marijuana packages valued at
2,347.45 dollars. [@1] Officers surprised the 39-year-old driving a vehicle
carrying the packages in colonia Tazumal. [@2] Police identified the second
detainee as 59-year-old Ana Elizabeth Rodriguez Ardon, Escalante Salazar's
partner, who was arrested in Chalchuapa with four marijuana portions and a
mobile phone. [@2]

Officers also seized the vehicle and a mobile phone from Escalante Salazar. [@2]
The institution published photographs showing the marijuana packages on one of
the vehicle's seats. [@2] Police said the second procedure in colonia Tazumal
was supported by the Canine Unit. [@2]

Both detainees will be accused of illicit drug trafficking. [@2] The National
Civil Police, known as the PNC, is El Salvador's national police force, and
Chalchuapa is a district in the municipality of Santa Ana Oeste. [@2]
