A bus driver for the Compagnie des Autobus de Monaco braked on the avenue des
Spélugues on 11 February 2026 to avoid a scooter. [@1] An elderly passenger
fell inside the bus and fractured her hip. [@1,2]

Police tested the driver after the incident: the alcohol test was negative and
the drug test positive. [@1] Blood taken at Princess Grace Hospital showed THC
at 1.8 ng/mL, which the court said meant he was under the influence of cannabis
at the time, and THC-COOH at 41.8 ng/mL, which it said showed a habit of
use. [@1]

On Friday 13 February 2026 the criminal court sentenced the 45-year-old Italian,
who had worked for the company since December 2023 and had no criminal record,
to six months in prison, five of them suspended, and banned him from driving in
Monaco for five years. [@1,2]

The driver said he had bought a CBD product in Ventimiglia to relax and did not
know it contained THC; the court rejected that explanation. [@1] The court
ordered the unsuspended month to be served and he returned to the remand
prison. [@1]

It was the first conviction under Monaco's law of 14 November 2025 strengthening
road safety, which raised penalties for professional drivers. [@1,3]
Prosecutors brought no separate charge of unintentional injury, saying video
showed the bus moving at 10 to 15 km/h and braking normally. [@1]
