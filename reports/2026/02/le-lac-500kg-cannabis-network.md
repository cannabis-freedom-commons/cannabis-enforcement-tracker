The judicial police brigade of Le Lac, a district of Tunis on the city's
northern shore, broke up an international cannabis-trafficking network known as
the zattla. [@1] The network operated in the northern suburbs of the capital.
[@1] Officers arrested three people said to be active in the trafficking. [@1]
The Arabic account of the same operation says three people were detained and
others were put on a wanted list. [@2]

The operation seized 5,000 blocks of cannabis, a total of 500 kg. [@1] The
Arabic account describes the same haul as 5,000 pieces of zatla weighing close
to 500 kg. [@2] Officers also seized three luxury cars, several large
motorcycles, a hunting rifle and 24,000 dinars. [@1] The Arabic account confirms
three cars, luxury motorcycles and a sum of 24,000 dinars. [@2]

La Presse reported that the rest of the network was still being sought. [@1] The
newspaper said the operation at the Le Lac brigade was still under way. [@1]
