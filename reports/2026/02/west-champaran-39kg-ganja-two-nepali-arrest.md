Bihar Police and the Sashastra Seema Bal arrested two Nepalese nationals near
Sindhwalia Chhatiya Ghat in the Kangli police station area of West Champaran
district on the evening of 7 February 2026. [@1] The two men are residents of
Sonbarsa village in Parsa district of Nepal. [@1] A second account names the
arrested men as Rambhu Kumar Yadav and Nagendra Sahni of Sonbarsa, Bairiya,
Parsa, Nepal. [@2]

The pair were allegedly transporting 39kg of ganja concealed on a motorcycle
when the joint police and SSB team laid a trap and stopped them. [@1] Officers
seized the 39 kg of ganja-like substance together with one motorcycle. [@2]
Police registered Kangli police station case number 14/26 dated 07/02/2026 and
began legal proceedings. [@2]

Superintendent of Police Shaurya Suman said the trap followed credible
information about the movement of narcotics across the border. [@1] A case was
registered under relevant sections of the NDPS Act, and further investigation is
under way to trace the network involved in the smuggling attempt. [@1] Police
described the operation as part of intensified surveillance along the border to
curb cross-border smuggling, with further questioning continuing to establish
the source and intended destination of the seized contraband. [@1]
