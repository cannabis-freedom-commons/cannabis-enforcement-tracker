The court in Rosso, Mauritania, convicted seven people for cannabis trafficking,
ordering the expulsion of five foreigners from the country and prison terms in
the civilian prison for the other two. [@1] The Rosso gendarmerie brigade
arrested the accused on Tuesday after an investigation that began with a
Senegalese national found holding cannabis he was selling with a Guinean
national. [@1] MADAR independently reported the same outcome, describing
expulsion for five foreigners and incarceration in the penitentiary for two
others after convictions for cannabis trafficking. [@2]

Investigators said the initial arrest of the Senegalese national allowed them to
dismantle a drug-trafficking network operating in the border zone. [@1] The
investigation found that the suspects acted in an organised way, with roles
divided among the network's members. [@1] The court placed a Mauritanian citizen
under judicial supervision after his name was mentioned in the investigation
following one suspect's statement, suggesting local ramifications of the
network. [@1] MADAR added that the Mauritanian was placed under judicial control
on the basis of an accused person's testimony, as part of pursuing the
trafficking network and bringing all those concerned to justice. [@2]
