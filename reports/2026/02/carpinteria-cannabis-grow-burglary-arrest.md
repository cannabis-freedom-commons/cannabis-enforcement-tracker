Sheriff’s detectives arrested 22-year-old Dvaryae Bryant of Los Angeles in
connection with the burglary of a Carpinteria-area cannabis grow operation where
marijuana worth hundreds of thousands of dollars was stolen. [@1] Detectives
identified Bryant through follow-up investigation and coordinated with the Los
Angeles Police Department, which was conducting a parallel investigation
involving him. [@1] On February 18, 2026, Bryant was arrested on a warrant at
his residence in the 2300 block of South Victoria Avenue. [@1] During the
arrest, detectives recovered a short-barrel assault rifle that Bryant allegedly
threw from a window. [@1] Bryant was booked at the Van Nuys Jail for local
charges and, after his Los Angeles court proceedings, will be extradited to
Santa Barbara County on a no-bail felony warrant for burglary, conspiracy, grand
theft and a gang enhancement. [@1]

The investigation began on January 17, 2026, at approximately 1:47 a.m., when
deputies responded to a reported burglary in the 4000 block of Foothill Road and
the suspects had fled before deputies arrived. [@1] Detectives and LAPD later
served a search warrant in the 12000 block of Cranbrook Avenue in Hawthorne,
where they recovered approximately 15 pounds of processed cannabis, about 6.8
kg, plus additional firearms and thousands in cash. [@1] The firearms and cash
were seized by the Los Angeles Police Department as part of its investigation
and on-view charges. [@1]

Detectives are investigating potential links between this case and other similar
burglaries in Santa Barbara County, including a second burglary at the same
Foothill Road location on February 4, 2026. [@1] The investigation remains
ongoing and anyone with information is asked to contact sheriff’s detectives.
