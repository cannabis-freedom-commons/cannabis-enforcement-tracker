Argentine Gendarmería officers of the “Núcleo” section of Escuadrón 71
“Aguilares” stopped a car on Ruta Nacional 38 at Huacra, in La Cocha department,
Tucumán, on 6 February 2026. [@1] The driver was an Argentine adult travelling
from San Miguel de Tucumán to San Fernando del Valle de Catamarca. [@1]

Officers saw a bundle covered with a black burlap-type tarpaulin in the boot of
the car. [@1] In the presence of witnesses they opened it and counted 17 bricks,
called ladrillos in Spanish. [@2] The bricks tested positive for marijuana in a
Narcotest field test. [@3] The marijuana weighed 17 kilos 861 grams. [@2]

The magistrate handling the case ordered the drug and the car seized and the
driver detained. [@3] The driver was placed at the disposal of the courts for
breaking Argentina's drug law. [@1] The ministry placed the operation within the
drug-control and prevention work it promotes. [@1]
