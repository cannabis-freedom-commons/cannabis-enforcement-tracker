## Smuggling and arrest

On 27 August 2025, Zhou asked Lin, a traveler who regularly crossed between China and the United States, to bring three packs of cigarettes from the United States, according to China's national drug control office. [@1,2] Police stopped Lin at Fuzhou Changle Airport on 2 September and found three packs in his luggage; one opened cigarette packet contained 10.19 g of tobacco that tested positive for 5F-ADB at a reported concentration of 0.45%. [@1,2] The account describes 5F-ADB as a synthetic cannabinoid mixed into the tobacco. [@1,2] Police arrested Zhou in Lianjiang County that same day. [@1,2]

## Sentence

A Fujian court later convicted Zhou of smuggling drugs and sentenced him to six months in prison with a CNY 3,000 fine, the reports said. [@1,2] They do not state the date of sentence or Lin's legal outcome. [@1,2] The account gives the mass of the tobacco tested but not the net mass of 5F-ADB itself.
