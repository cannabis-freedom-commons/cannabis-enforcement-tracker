Police in Ushuaia searched three addresses on 10 February 2026 in an investigation into a robbery
reported on 7 February, under the Juzgado de Instrucción N° 1 of the Southern Judicial District.
[@1,2]

At a house on 25 de Mayo street, officers arrested Pablo Nahuel Moreno, 38, Juan Martín Saganias, 31,
and Nathanael Ariel Zubillaga, 26, for attacking and resisting the police. [@1,2] In the same house
they found 19 cannabis plants and called in officers specialising in drug offences. [@1,2]

The three were notified of their rights in relation to the robbery. [@1,2] The provincial police said
they had also referred the possible breach of the drug law to the competent authorities, and that the
case remained under investigation. [@1]
