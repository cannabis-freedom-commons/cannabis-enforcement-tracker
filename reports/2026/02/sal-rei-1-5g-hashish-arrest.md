Cape Verde's Judicial Police detained a 33-year-old man in flagrante on Tuesday
3 February on the island of Boa Vista on suspicion of drug trafficking. [@1] The
force's Criminal Investigation Department in Boa Vista announced the detention
on 5 February as part of drug-trafficking prevention work. [@2] The detention
occurred on 3 February during routine maritime trafficking prevention and
repression checks around Boa Vista. [@2]

Police said the man was in transit on a Praia-Sao Vicente sea voyage with a stop
in Boa Vista and disembarked at the port of Sal Rei with 1.50 grams of haxixe.
[@1] A second police account likewise says he was travelling between Praia and
Sao Vicente via Boa Vista and landed at Sal Rei port with 1.50 grams of haxixe.
[@2]

The detainee, a Cape Verdean national, was presented to the Boa Vista judicial
authorities on the afternoon of Wednesday 4 February. [@1] He was left subject
to scheduling of a summary trial. [@2] The Boa Vista investigation department
said it maintains continuous prevention and repression actions against this type
of criminality on the island. [@2]
