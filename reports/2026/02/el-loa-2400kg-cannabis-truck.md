The Antofagasta regional prosecutor's office and Chile's Investigative Police
detained an adult Chilean citizen in flagrante as part of their joint strategic
work against drug trafficking. [@1] The Calama prosecutor's office formalized
the detainee for drug trafficking, and at the Public Ministry's request he was
placed in preventive detention as a danger to society with a 100-day
investigation period. [@1]

The operation on routes in El Loa province recovered 2.4 tonnes of cannabis,
equivalent to 2,311 packages, with an estimated value that could reach 12,111
million pesos. [@1] The cannabis had been stockpiled in the city of Calama and
was being taken to the country's central zone for distribution, portioning and
sale when coordinated work by the Public Ministry and the PDI thwarted the
organization's plans. [@1] Officers also seized a 2015 Freightliner TRCA truck,
a semi-trailer and cash, described as items linked to the transport logistics.
[@1]

The Calama anti-narcotics and organized-crime brigade found the load hidden in a
cargo vehicle after investigative work based on criminal analysis, police
intelligence and specialized field work. [@1] The two institutions called it the
largest drug seizure so far in 2026 and one of the most important in recent
years. [@1]
