Police in Volos arrested 13 members of a drug trafficking ring in a coordinated
operation across Magnesia, including the man police described as its leader.
[@2] Fourteen people were arrested in total: the 13 alleged members and one
non-member held under the procedure for offences caught in the act. [@2] More
than 50 officers and three police dogs took part in the operation, which began
in the early hours of 11 February 2026 in areas of Magnesia. [@2]

Officers searched 18 homes and seized 1,243.32 grams of raw cannabis among
cocaine, heroin and 60 narcotic pills. [@2] The haul also included 70,846 euros,
gold coins and jewellery, three hunting guns, 852 cartridges, knives, 52 mobile
phones, seven cars, eight motorcycles and nine electric scooters. [@2] Police
put the group's illegal profit, including the seized drugs and the profit it
expected, at 275,000 euros. [@2]

Police built a case against the 13 members and two identified members who were
being sought, and the file also included 11 non-members suspected of one-off
dealing and 13 people who bought drugs. [@2] The 14 arrested people were taken
to the Volos courts under heavy guard on 12 February 2026 to appear before a
prosecutor. [@3]

The Volos drug squad said the group had run its hierarchical structure since at
least mid-October 2025, dealing cocaine, heroin and cannabis. [@2] Local
reporting put the cannabis seizure at more than one kilogram and the cash at
60,000 euros. [@1]
