Customs officers working with the border police arrested a young Comorian woman at Prince Said Ibrahim International Airport in Hahaya for importing drugs on a journey from France. [@1,2] The Interior Ministry communiqué, dated in Moroni on 14 February 2026, says officers seized 150 slabs of "shit" (chite), the local term for cannabis resin, weighing 14 kilograms in total. [@1,2] The ministry presented the seizure days after closing a training course on fighting drug trafficking at the borders, calling it proof of stronger surveillance at the country's entry points. [@1,2] The reports say she was arrested for importation; no charge or court outcome has been published. [@1,2]
