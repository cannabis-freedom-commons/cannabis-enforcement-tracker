Palmyra police arrested two people on Wednesday, 11 February 2026, over an
incident in which a student reportedly ingested LSD at Palmyra-Macedon Middle
School the previous September. [@1] Officers went to the middle school at about
11:15 a.m. on 19 September 2025 to help with a student who was taken to
hospital, and police said the child got the substance from someone they lived
with on Cuyler Street in the village. [@1]

A juvenile suspect was charged with endangering the welfare of a child and
reckless endangerment and was released to a legal guardian with appearance
tickets to probation. [@1] Andrea Allen-Ascioti, 39, was arrested and charged
with endangering the welfare of a child. [@1] Police said Allen-Ascioti also
smoked marijuana with her 11-year-old child on multiple occasions at her home on
Cuyler Street, which led to a second count of endangering the welfare of a
child. [@1] Allen-Ascioti was taken to the Wayne County Jail and released after
arraignment, and police said they would seek an order of protection against her.
[@1]

Police said the juvenile was arrested at 4:33 p.m. on 11 February and
Allen-Ascioti at 5:05 p.m. the same day, both in connection with the Cuyler
Street residence. [@2]

Police said the juvenile was also charged with reckless endangerment in the
second degree and must report to Wayne County Probation to answer the charges.
[@3] FingerLakes1 reported that police described the marijuana allegation as a
separate announcement after the LSD investigation and that Allen-Ascioti was due
at a centralized arraignment on 12 February. [@2]
