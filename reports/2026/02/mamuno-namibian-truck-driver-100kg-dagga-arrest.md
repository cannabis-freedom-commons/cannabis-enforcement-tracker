Botswana police intercepted and searched a truck driven by 31-year-old Namibian
Mbaunguraije Kaitjirokere at the Mamuno border during an operation. [@1] He
appeared in Botswana's Gantsi Magistrate's Court on Thursday on charges of
trafficking in narcotic drugs. [@1] He faces a single count of cross-border drug
trafficking and had not yet been asked to plead. [@2]

Botswana police said they recovered eight boxes of cannabis weighing over 100kg
from the truck. [@1] A later NBC account described the seizure as 100 kilograms
of dagga, the regional term for cannabis. [@2] The driver was heading to South
Africa, and police put the estimated value of the contraband at over N$600,000.
[@2]

After his first appearance, he was remanded in prison and scheduled to reappear
on 26 February. [@1] After a second brief appearance in the Gantsi court, he was
remanded in custody. [@2] He is expected back in court on 12 March and has not
been asked to plead, with police saying unresolved processes form part of
ongoing investigations. [@2]
