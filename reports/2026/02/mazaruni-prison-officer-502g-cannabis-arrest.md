A 26-year-old prison officer from Golden Grove, East Coast Demerara, was
arrested after suspected cannabis was found at Mazaruni Prison. [@2] The
discovery happened at about 6:45 a.m. on 20 February 2026 during a routine
morning briefing inside the prison compound. [@1] Officers searched the officer
in his presence after his bulletproof vest appeared unusually bulky. [@1]

Inside the vest, officers found a transparent parcel containing leaves, seeds
and stems suspected to be cannabis. [@1] The vest parcel weighed 474.6 grammes
when weighed in the presence of the suspect. [@2] A search of the officer's
living quarters in the compound found another parcel with similar suspected
cannabis on his bed, weighing 27.6 grammes. [@1] In total 502.2 grammes was
seized, and the suspect and the suspected cannabis were escorted to the Bartica
Police Station where it was lodged. [@1]

The officer remained in police custody as investigations continued. [@1]
