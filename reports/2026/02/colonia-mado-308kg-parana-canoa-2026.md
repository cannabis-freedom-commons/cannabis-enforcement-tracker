Three men in a canoe threw bundles onto the Argentine bank of the Parana at El
Frances, a sector of Colonia Mado in Misiones, and fled toward Paraguay when
Gendarmeria Nacional officers ordered them to stop. [@1] Officers of the Nucleo
Section of Gendarmerie Squadron 10 Eldorado, with the Eldorado complex-crimes
and judicial-procedures unit, the Eldorado road-safety section and the Iguazu
criminal-intelligence unit, were on a motorised patrol along a dirt road toward
the Parana riverbank when they saw them. [@1] No arrests were reported; the
three men escaped across the river into Paraguay. [@1]

A search of the area found eight bundles wrapped in black nylon bags and clear
tape, holding rectangular packages. [@1] Officers counted 150 bricks, which
tested positive for marijuana in a field Narcotest and weighed 308 kilos 400
grams. [@1] The local newspaper Primera Edicion reported the same count of 150
bricks and a weight of 308.400 kilograms of cannabis sativa, and described the
find as more than 300 kilograms. [@2] The federal court and federal prosecutor's
office in Eldorado ordered the drug seized, and it was stored in the judicial
drug depository. [@1]

Primera Edicion reported that the patrol saw the three people in the canoe last
Sunday, putting the crossing on Sunday 1 February 2026. [@2] The paper said the
sector is called El Frances de Mado and is named for the drug-trafficking
operations once run from a riverside house there by Slawomir Andrzej Polus, also
known as Thierry Andre Polus, a Polish and French citizen convicted in Misiones
and expelled to Paraguay in 2016. [@2]
