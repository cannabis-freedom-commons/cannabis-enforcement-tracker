Michigan State Police investigators said they shut down a large illegal
marijuana grow operation at three locations across metro Detroit on Wednesday 18
February 2026. [@1] The searches were carried out by the state police Marijuana
& Tobacco Investigation Section, the agency's unit for marijuana and tobacco
cases. [@2]

One of the locations was a former industrial building with multiple grow rooms,
irrigation systems, security cameras and a "clone room" from which all the
actively growing plants were derived. [@1] The state police described it as a
multi-million-dollar elaborate operation. [@1] In all, officers seized more than
11,000 marijuana plants in various stages of growth and 41 pounds (about 18.6
kilograms) of dried marijuana flower. [@1] They also seized eight firearms,
Rolex watches, high-value jewellery and $146,000 in cash. [@1]

A Metro Detroit News account repeating the state police statement listed the
same seizures and the same description of the industrial building site. [@3] The
state police said the investigation was ongoing and would be submitted to the
Attorney General for review when complete. [@2] The state police reported no
arrests in its account of the operation. [@2]
