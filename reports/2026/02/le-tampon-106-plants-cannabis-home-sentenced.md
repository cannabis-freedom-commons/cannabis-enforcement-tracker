Gendarmes of La Réunion uncovered a large-scale cannabis grow operation at the
home of a resident of Le Tampon. [@1] The man was sentenced to 26 months'
imprisonment, of which 8 months are to be served, with the remainder subject to
sentence management. [@1]

The operation led to the seizure of 2.05 kg of ready-to-consume cannabis and 106
cannabis plants still in cultivation. [@1] Officers also found €4,100 in cash
suspected to come from the illicit activity. [@1]

Equipment for the production and packaging of narcotics was seized; the narcotic
products were destroyed at the conclusion of the proceedings, and the money was
confiscated. [@1] Following his trial, the accused received a 26-month prison
sentence, with 8 months to be served and the remainder subject to sentence
management. [@1]
