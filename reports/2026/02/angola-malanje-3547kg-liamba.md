Angola's Criminal Investigation Service (SIC) arrested three men and seized 3,547.5 kilograms of liamba, as cannabis is known in Angola, in the municipality of Cambundi Catembo, Malanje province, on 15 February 2026. [@1,2] The three were aged between 39 and 54, according to O País. [@1]

The SIC said the men were travelling separately in two vehicles, a white Mitsubishi Canter and a green Toyota Hilux, carrying 176 raffia sacks of about 50 kilograms each. [@1] One of them, Vitorino dos Santos Afonso, was a member of the Civil Protection and Fire Service, O País reported. [@1] The other two were named as Nelson Filipe Pedro and Romão Valente Tomás. [@1]

## Bound for Luanda

The SIC said the load was destined for Luanda, where each sack would have sold for 80,000 kwanzas; the whole consignment could have brought in more than 14 million kwanzas. [@1,2] The service seized the two vehicles, which it said had been bought with the proceeds of the trade. [@1]

Augusto Barros André, the SIC's head of institutional communication and press in Malanje, said the suspects would be presented to the Public Prosecutor's Office. [@1,2] No charge or court date was reported. [@1,2]
