Leicestershire Police officers went to a property on Dumbleton Avenue, off
Narborough Road in Leicester, at around 14:00 BST on Sunday 5 April 2026. [@1]
Officers entered the property and found cannabis being grown inside, about 150
plants in all. [@2]

No arrests were made and the force said the property had been secured, with
enquiries ongoing. [@1] Officers broke through the front door, and a police van
and a car were pictured parked on the residential street during the raid. [@2]
