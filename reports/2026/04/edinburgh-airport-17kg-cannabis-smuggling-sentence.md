An aspiring Scottish social media influencer was jailed for 16 months after
being caught smuggling £150,000 of cannabis at Edinburgh Airport. [@1] Ellie
Crampsie admitted carrying 17kg of the Class B drug in a suitcase as she
travelled home from a holiday in Thailand in April last year. [@1] The
23-year-old, from Broomhouse in Glasgow, told Edinburgh Sheriff Court she had
been forced into carrying the drugs by an ex-boyfriend. [@1] Sheriff Graeme
Watson acknowledged she had been naive and potentially taken advantage of by her
ex. [@1] The sheriff said her role was carrying the cannabis in her luggage and
accepted there was a power imbalance in the relationship. [@1] At sentencing,
Sheriff Watson accepted Crampsie had a lesser degree of culpability but said her
decision was reckless and merited a jail sentence. [@1]

Crampsie was stopped by border control officers after arriving on an Air France
flight from Thailand via Paris. [@1] The suitcase, which Crampsie said belonged
to her ex-boyfriend, also contained female clothing. [@1] The value of the drugs
was estimated to be between £115,000 and £151,000. [@1]

Crampsie, known as a promoter on Glasgow's nightlife and events scene, has about
5,500 followers on Instagram and 6,000 on TikTok. [@1] Her lawyer, Michael
Poggi, said she had played a lesser role in transporting the drugs and there was
evidence of pressure from others. [@1] He told the court a much older person had
a controlling influence over her. [@1]
