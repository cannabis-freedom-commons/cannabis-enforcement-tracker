Police in Bhurungamari upazila, a sub-district of Kurigram district, arrested a
woman at the bus terminal as she was transporting cannabis hidden among sacks of
betel nut. [@1] Officers recovered about 3 kg of cannabis from her. [@2] The
woman was Momota Begum, 55, wife of Taher Ali of Beguntala village in
Badalgachhi upazila, Naogaon district. [@1] The operation took place at about
8:30 pm on Tuesday 21 April 2026 on a tip-off and was led by Muntasir Mamun Mun,
senior assistant superintendent of police for Bhurungamari Circle. [@1] Police
said she had collected the cannabis in the border area and hidden it in the
betel nut sacks for transport. [@1]

The auto-rickshaw was seized, and its driver fled; police said they were trying
to arrest him. [@2] Azim Uddin, officer-in-charge of Bhurungamari police
station, said a case was filed against Begum under the Narcotics Control Act and
she was sent to jail at noon on Wednesday 22 April. [@2]
