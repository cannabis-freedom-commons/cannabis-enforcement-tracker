Karo police arrested four men at a rented house in Kabanjahe on 8 April 2026 and then followed
their statements to a cannabis-growing site in protected forest at Kutarayat, Naman Teran. [@1,2]

The officers found 75 cannabis plants at the site, ranging from 35 to 150 centimetres tall and
weighing 650 grams in wet condition. [@1,2] The initial house search recovered 47 grams of
cannabis, 27 grams of cannabis powder and a mobile phone. [@1,2]

The four men, identified as FG, DK, EF alias I and YZ, were taken with the evidence to the Karo
police narcotics unit for further legal processing. [@1,2]
