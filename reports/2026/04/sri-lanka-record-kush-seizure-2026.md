On 26 April 2026 police arrested 22 Sri Lankan monks returning from Thailand at Bandaranaike International Airport with 110 kg of kush hidden in their luggage, valued at about US$3.5 million. It was reported as the country's largest single cannabis seizure. The case was an enforcement event, not a change in the law. [@1]

Customs officials said each monk carried about 5 kg of kush, a particularly potent form of the drug, hidden behind false walls inside the luggage on arrival in Colombo. [@2] The monks were mainly students returning from an all-expenses-paid four-day holiday in Thailand paid for by an unnamed sponsor. [@2] A 23rd monk, believed to have organised the trip, was later arrested in a Colombo suburb, police told BBC Sinhala. [@2] The acting police spokesman said the monk, who did not join the trip, had told the others that "these parcels are a donation" and that a van would collect the packages. [@2] The Police Narcotics Bureau found photos and videos on some of the monks' phones showing them enjoying their holiday in casual clothing. [@2] The 22 monks were remanded in custody for seven days after appearing in court on Sunday. [@2] Police told BBC Sinhala they believed the monks potentially did not know what they were carrying. [@2] The drugs were found among school supplies and sweets. [@2]

Twenty-two Sri Lankan monks returning from Thailand were arrested at Sri Lanka's main international airport with 242 pounds of cannabis, customs officials said. [@3] A Sri Lanka Customs spokesman said the group had returned from a four-day vacation in Bangkok with kush hidden in their luggage. [@3] Customs officials called it the largest single detection of kush at the airport. [@3] Each monk carried about five kilos of the drug concealed within false walls in his luggage, and the group was handed over to police. [@3] The 22 monks appeared in court on Sunday and were remanded in custody for seven days for further questioning. [@2] The Police Narcotics Bureau's airport unit apprehended the suspects in an operation on the night of 25 April acting on intelligence. [@4]
