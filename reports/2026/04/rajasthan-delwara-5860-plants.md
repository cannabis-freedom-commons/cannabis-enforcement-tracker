## Eradication

The Rajasthan Anti-Narcotics Task Force and the Jodhpur unit of the Narcotics Control Bureau found a cannabis plantation concealed among wheat on a six-bigha hillside near Delwara in Rajsamand district. [@1,2] The teams destroyed 5,860 plants on site, with police putting the crop's market value at about ₹10 crore. [@1,2]

Patrika reported that officers arrested Nattulal Gameti while he was preparing small cannabis packets for sale, and that 19 landholders connected to the field were being sought. [@1] ThePrint described the Delwara action as one part of a seven-location Rajasthan operation, so only the plants and arrest linked to this field are counted here. [@2]
