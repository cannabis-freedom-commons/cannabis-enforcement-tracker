Guatemala's anti-narcotics police and soldiers located 21,000 marijuana plants
in a mountainous area of caserío Los Laureles in Las Cruces, Petén. [@1]

The Interior Ministry put the value of this single operation at Q10.5 million,
reported as about US$1,346,153.85. [@1] The plants were found in the harvesting
stage and were burned after seizure. [@1] The joint operation by the Interior
Ministry, the Army and the National Civil Police was reported by the authorities
on Thursday through social networks. [@1]

Authorities described the eradication as part of strategic actions against
organized crime operating in remote areas of the country. [@2] The ministry said
patrols and eradications would continue in other areas identified with illegal
crops to strengthen public welfare through crime repression and citizen
protection. [@1]
