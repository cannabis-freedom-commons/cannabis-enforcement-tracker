In a coordinated crackdown led by the Central Crime Branch (CCB), Bengaluru
police arrested 16 people and seized narcotics worth Rs 36.67 crore. [@1] The
arrested comprised four foreign nationals, eight inter-state operatives and four
local accused. [@1] The operation targeted an organised syndicate believed to be
sourcing drugs from foreign and domestic markets and distributing them locally,
especially among students and young consumers. [@1] The week-long crackdown by
Bengaluru City Police with the Central Crime Branch arrested 16 people and
seized over 26 kg of various narcotics worth around Rs 36.67 crore. [@2]

The seizure included about 11.4 kg of ganja, 5.1 kg of hydro ganja and 618 grams
of charas alongside other narcotics. [@2] The precise seizure comprised 11.47 kg
of ganja, 5.195 kg of hydro ganja and 618 grams of charas, plus MDMA, LSD
strips, heroin, cocaine and drug-laced gummies. [@3] The operation was led by
the Central Crime Branch with East and West zone police stations against a
network trafficking and distributing narcotics. [@3] Cases were registered under
the NDPS Act and all 16 accused were produced before the court and remanded to
judicial custody. [@3]
