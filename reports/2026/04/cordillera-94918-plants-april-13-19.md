The Philippine National Police reported 22 marijuana eradication operations across the Cordillera Administrative Region from 13 to 19 April 2026. [@1,2]
The operations uprooted around 94,918 fully grown marijuana plants and seized about 56.6 kilograms of dried marijuana. [@1,2]

Benguet accounted for 17 operations and 14,418 plants valued at PHP9.68 million. [@2]
A Mountain Province operation accounted for 60,000 plants valued at PHP12 million, while four Kalinga operations destroyed 20,500 plants valued at PHP4.1 million. [@2]
The PNP reported the campaign's combined value as about PHP25.7 million. [@1,2]
