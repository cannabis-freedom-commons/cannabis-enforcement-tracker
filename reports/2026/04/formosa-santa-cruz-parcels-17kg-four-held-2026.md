Gendarmes of the Tatane section of Squadron 15 Bajo Paraguay stopped a
parcel-transport vehicle for a documents and physical check on National Route 11
at kilometre 1,144, in Tatane, Formosa province. [@1] A narcotics dog alerted on
two parcels and field narcotest checks confirmed 17 kilos 090 grams of cannabis
sativa. [@1]

The federal prosecutor and Federal Guarantee Court No. 1 of Formosa ordered a
controlled delivery, and investigators traced a route sending cannabis parcels
from Clorinda to Santa Cruz province. [@1] With the Rio Gallegos complex-crimes
unit and the operations group of Squadron 43 Rio Turbio, gendarmes raided two
homes at the same time, one in Clorinda and one in 28 de Noviembre - Güer Aike
in Santa Cruz, and also searched the post-office branch where the parcels were
to arrive. [@1] Four adults, three women and one man, were detained and
identified as the sender and the recipients of the cannabis. [@1]

Along with the 17 kilograms of marijuana, officers seized 078 grams of cannabis
buds, six mobile phones, a laptop, 304,000 Argentine pesos and two folders of
notes. [@1]
