The Philippine National Police said that 18 operations in the Cordillera Administrative Region from
6 to 12 April 2026 burned 71,873 fully grown cannabis plants and 22 seedlings. [@1,2,3] The same
campaign destroyed about 70,610 grams of dried cannabis stalks, leaves and fruiting tops, with a
reported value of PHP22,856,305. [@1,2,3]

Most of the plantations were in hard-to-reach areas of Benguet, while police also uprooted and
destroyed cannabis valued at about PHP4.8 million in Kalinga. [@1,2,3] One high-value suspect was
arrested in Benguet and was reported to face charges under the Comprehensive Dangerous Drugs Act.
[@1,2]
