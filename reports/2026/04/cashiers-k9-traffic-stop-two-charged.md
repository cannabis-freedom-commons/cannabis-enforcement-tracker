The Jackson County Sheriff's Office stopped a vehicle in the Cashiers area on
Monday, April 20, 2026, and its Criminal Suppression Unit dog, K9 Ziva, alerted
officers to narcotics in the vehicle. [@1] A probable cause search of the
vehicle found what the sheriff's office described as suspected marijuana
weighing approximately 4 grams, along with 42 grams of suspected fentanyl, 56
grams of suspected methamphetamine and more than 20 Xanax pills. [@1]

Deputies charged two people, Bethany Lightstar Powers and Hunter Duke Fortner,
according to the sheriff's office. [@1] Both were charged with trafficking in
fentanyl by possession and by transportation and with trafficking in
methamphetamine by possession and by transportation, as well as possession of
fentanyl, possession of methamphetamine, possession of a Schedule IV controlled
substance, maintaining a dwelling for controlled substances and possession of
drug paraphernalia. [@1] Powers was additionally charged with possession of
marijuana. [@1]

The charges were announced in a post on the sheriff's office Facebook page that
the station cited, and no court appearance or plea was reported. [@1]
