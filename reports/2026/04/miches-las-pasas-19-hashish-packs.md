Agents of the Dirección Nacional de Control de Drogas (DNCD), the Dominican
Republic's drug-control agency, and members of the Armada de República
Dominicana, the navy, seized 19 packs of hashish during an inspection on the
coast of Miches, in El Seibo province. [@1] Members of the Public Ministry
coordinated the joint operation. [@1] Intelligence information alerted the teams
to suspicious movement at Las Pasas beach. [@1] They went to the beach, searched
the area and found a sack containing the suspected narcotic. [@1] Following the
prosecutor's instructions, the agents opened the sack and seized 19 packs
containing 380 portions of hashish. [@1] The Instituto Nacional de Ciencias
Forenses (Inacif), the national forensic sciences institute, analysed the packs
and determined that they weighed 33.60 pounds in all, about 15.24 kilograms.
[@1]

After the find, the teams widened the search along the whole shoreline for
further evidence and for the people behind the failed operation. [@1] Several
people who were near the place at the time of the intervention were detained for
investigation. [@1] The DNCD said that, with other state security bodies, it was
reinforcing interdiction at airports, ports and borders and across the country
as part of the fight against drug trafficking. [@2]
