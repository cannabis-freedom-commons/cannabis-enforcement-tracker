Gendarmería officers of Escuadrón 12 “Bernardo de Irigoyen” stopped a Toyota
Hilux on Ruta Nacional 101 at the Misiones town of San Antonio at 1 a.m. on 15
April 2026. [@1] The pickup's cargo box was covered with a black tarpaulin, and
when the occupants lifted it officers found 23 boxes of different sizes that
gave off a strong smell of marijuana. [@2] The two occupants were an Argentine
and a Paraguayan national. [@2]

Officers pulled pressed packages, heat-sealed bags and waxed-paper wrappings out
of the boxes, holding a vegetable substance and a compact greenish-brown mass.
[@1] Narcotest field tests were positive for 925 kilos 400 grams of cannabis
sativa and 5 kilos 250 grams of hashish. [@1] Gendarmería described the hashish
as made from the resin stored in the marijuana flowers and later kneaded, with a
higher THC level. [@1]

The Federal Court and Federal Prosecutor's Office of Puerto Iguazú took part in
the case, and officers detained the two adult occupants, seized the drug and
took the vehicle. [@1] La Gaceta, reporting the same operation on 16 April, also
put the haul at 925 kilos 400 grams of cannabis sativa and 5 kilos 250 grams of
hashish. [@2] El Liberal published the same agency text the same day and put the
combined haul at more than 900 kilos. [@3] Two people were detained in the
operation, according to Diario Síntesis, which reported the load as more than
930 kilos of drugs. [@4]
