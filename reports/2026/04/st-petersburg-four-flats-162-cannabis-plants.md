Police in St Petersburg detained six local men aged 30 to 40 suspected of
growing and selling drug-containing plants. [@1] Three of the detained men
allegedly cultivated the plants while the other three packed and sold the banned
substances. [@2] Four criminal cases were opened against the detainees for
attempted illegal production, sale or shipment of narcotics. [@3] The men were
held for 48 hours in a temporary detention facility awaiting a restraint
decision. [@2]

The men had rented four flats on Zdorovtseva, Planernaya and Ivanovskaya streets
and Leninsky Prospekt, where they grew cannabis for production and sale. [@1]
Officers seized 162 cannabis bushes, about 16.5 kg of plant parts and
cultivation equipment from the rented flats. [@2] Officers also seized 1.6 kg of
dried plants at the men's residences and found more than 100 g of ready
substances described as cannabis, hashish and cocaine, with some powders sent
for examination. [@3] The operation was run by the Interior Ministry's
drug-control directorate for St Petersburg and Leningrad Oblast together with
Vasileostrovsky district police. [@1]
