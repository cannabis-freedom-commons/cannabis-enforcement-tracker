A joint team of the Anti-Narcotics Task Force and police arrested an alleged
drug smuggler along the Indo-Nepal border on 26 April 2026. [@1] The arrest took
place near the Indo-Nepal border in Bahraich, Uttar Pradesh. [@1] Bahraich
Superintendent of Police Vishwajeet Srivastava said the ANTF had received a
tip-off that 40-year-old Ismail of Jamunaha village in the Rupaideha area was
smuggling narcotics from Nepal into India. [@1]

The joint team raided a mobile repair shop on the road connecting Nepal to
Chakiya village, detained Ismail and recovered 6.77 kg of charas from him. [@1]
Police estimated the seized charas was worth about Rs 1.50 crore in the
international market. [@1] During interrogation Ismail confessed to procuring
the charas from Nepal's Banke district and smuggling it into India for sale.
[@1]

A case was registered against the accused under the Narcotic Drugs and
Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act, with further legal action under way. [@1] A
second account of the same operation confirms that the ANTF-police team arrested
Ismail on the Indo-Nepal border with 6.77 kg of charas valued at about Rs 1.50
crore. [@2]
