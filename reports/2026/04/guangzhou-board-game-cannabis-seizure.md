Guangzhou Baiyun Airport Customs stopped a transfer parcel declared as a “limited-edition board game” after its X-ray image appeared abnormal. Officers opened a board-game box and found a plastic-wrapped bundle of green plant material, a container of candy and several cigarettes. [@1,2]

Technical testing identified cannabis and cannabis products in the parcel, with a combined reported weight of 442.83 g. [@1,2]

Neither report names the sender or gives a later arrest, charge or court outcome. [@1,2]
