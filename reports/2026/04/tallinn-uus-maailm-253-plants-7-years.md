Northern Prefecture police searched a house in Tallinn's Uus Maailm district on 13 January 2025
and found a two-storey indoor cannabis grow with 253 plants a short walk from a gymnasium. [@1,2,3]
They seized 54 kilograms of cannabis in plants and sale-ready product, which they valued at more
than half a million euros at retail prices. [@1,2,3]

A neighbour had reported the smell of cannabis and covered windows. [@1,2,3] Prosecutors charged
men aged 39 and 48 with large-quantity drug handling and unlawful cannabis cultivation as a
group; both had been held since the arrests. [@1,2,3] A preliminary hearing was set for
13 August 2025 at Harju County Court. [@1,2,3]

Later reporting named them as Rain Viiralt, 39, and Ermo Saar, 48, and placed the grow between
a gymnasium and a nursery in a rented building marked for demolition. [@4,5] A court found both
guilty of unlawful cultivation and handling of cannabis in a group and sentenced each to seven
years in prison, counted from the 13 January 2025 detention. [@5] The court did not accept the
prosecution's large-gain valuation based on the full 54 kg and treated under 12 kg as proven for
that element, according to a write-up of the Delfi court report. [@5]
