The Arakan Army (AA) Coast Guard intercepted two boats and arrested 11
Bangladeshi nationals accused of smuggling marijuana toward junta-administered
Sittwe in Rakhine State. [@1] The seizure took place near the Rathedaung
Township coastline on April 24. [@1] The first vessel had stalled with engine
failure between Donpaik and Chenkhali and was boarded at about 1:20 p.m., when
six men and one woman, all Bangladeshi nationals, were taken into custody. [@1]
A second boat arrived at about 2:30 p.m. to tow the disabled vessel and was also
seized, with four more Bangladeshi nationals arrested on board. [@1] The Coast
Guard is holding all 11 suspects together with the contraband and both vessels,
with officials saying the detainees are being processed in accordance with
relevant laws. [@1]

The first vessel was carrying 23 packets of marijuana totalling roughly 32
kilograms. [@1] Alongside the marijuana, officers seized aphrodisiacs, birth
control pills, mobile phones and a quantity of Bangladeshi Taka. [@1]

The shipment had originated in Bangladesh. [@1] Local sources note that
methamphetamine and heroin are frequently trafficked in the opposite direction,
from junta-controlled waters in Rakhine toward Bangladesh. [@1]
