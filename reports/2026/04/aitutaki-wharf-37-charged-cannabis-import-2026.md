A 37-year-old man was charged with importing Class A and Class C controlled
drugs after Cook Islands Customs officers detected the drugs in Aitutaki. [@1]
The drugs are alleged to be methamphetamine, a Class A substance, and cannabis,
which is Class C. [@1] Cook Islands Customs and the Cook Islands Police Service
issued a joint statement on the case. [@1]

The joint statement said the detection happened on Tuesday 14 April 2026 during
targeted inspections at the Aitutaki International Wharf. [@1]
