Law enforcement raided eight locations on April 15, 2026. [@1] The raids
followed a two-year investigation prompted by a parent tip, according to the
Delaware County Sheriff's Office. [@1] The investigation began after a Delaware
County high school parent raised a concern with a school resource officer. [@1]
The 24-month investigation led to numerous traffickers, including some supply
sources in Michigan. [@1] Sheriff Jeff Balzer said the drugs were illegally sold
to buyers, illegally brought across state lines and illegally obtained by youth.
[@1]

Officers seized 148 pounds of raw marijuana. [@1] They seized 130 pounds of
marijuana candy and edibles. [@1] They seized 64,371 grams of THC wax. [@1] They
seized 23,090 THC vape pens and cartridges. [@1] They seized 1,128 milliliters
of THC liquid. [@1] They seized $417,500 in cash. [@1] They seized 13 firearms.
[@1] The task force said the products were designed and packaged to look like
candy with cartoon characters and bright colors. [@1] The haul was described as
worth nearly $2 million. [@1]

Officers also seized 11 pounds of raw mushrooms. [@1] They also seized 1,844
grams of edible mushrooms. [@1] The task force did not say who was arrested or
what its next steps are. [@1]
