Canada Border Services Agency officers intercepted a vehicle entering Canada
through the Cornwall port of entry on 25 April 2026. [@2] The RCMP released
information on the arrests and charges on behalf of the Cornwall Regional Task
Force. [@1] During the examination, officers found a significant quantity of
unstamped cannabis and vape products hidden in the vehicle. [@2] Five Bengali
nationals living in the Toronto area on valid work permits were taken into
custody without incident. [@2] The RCMP named them as Mostafizzur Rahman, 31,
Mashuk Iqbal, 30, MD Ashraful Alam, 30, Maher Afroz, 25, and Shurove Akter, 28.
[@2] Each was charged with possession of unstamped cannabis under the Excise
Act, 2001 and with possession for the purpose of distribution under the Cannabis
Act. [@2] Mashuk Iqbal faces an additional charge of failing to comply with a
release order, because he was on judicial release for two similar Toronto-area
offences at the time and remains in custody. [@2]

RCMP officers seized 32.3 kilograms of unstamped cannabis, which they estimated
was worth $142,418.62. [@2] They also seized 1,700 one-gram THC vape cartridges
valued at $34,000, two packages of THC edibles totalling 1,100 mg, $2,510 in
cash and seven cellular devices. [@2] The case went to the Cornwall Regional
Task Force, a joint unit of the Cornwall RCMP, the Ontario Provincial Police,
the CBSA and the Ontario Ministry of Finance. [@2]

StratCann reported the men were entering from the United States at the Cornwall
port of entry, which links Ontario to New York over the Three Nations Crossing
bridge. [@3] StratCann reported that the seized one-gram cartridges appeared to
be an illicit US brand called KRT. [@3] The RCMP credited the interception,
saying it reflected the strong, vigilant work of its CBSA partners. [@2] The
statement was attributed to Inspector Etienne Thauvette, officer in charge of
the RCMP's Cornwall detachment. [@2] The RCMP said the investigation was still
ongoing. [@2] CP24 reported the suspects ranged in age from 25 to 31 and lived
in the Toronto area on valid work permits. [@4]
