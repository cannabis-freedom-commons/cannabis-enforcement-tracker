Gendarmes of the security section “Paraná de las Palmas”, part of Squadron 63
“Zárate-Brazo Largo” of the Gendarmería Nacional, stopped a vehicle at night
during checks on Ruta Nacional 12 at kilometre 85.500. [@1] The procedure was
carried out under Argentina's drug law 23.737 and its arms and explosives law
20.429. [@1]

The driver carried a .22-calibre carbine with 35 rounds of ammunition and no
legal permit for it. [@1] Officers then searched further with a sniffer dog,
which reacted to the driver's backpack. [@1] Inside the backpack, opened in
front of witnesses, they found a transparent bag of green plant material, and a
Narcotest field screening was positive for marijuana. [@1] The marijuana in the
bag weighed 41 grams, and the driver was held in the case opened over the find.
[@1]

Zárate's prosecutor's unit No. 8 ordered the carbine, the 35 rounds and the
marijuana seized. [@1] The same unit left the driver at the disposal of the
investigation under the drug and arms laws. [@1]
