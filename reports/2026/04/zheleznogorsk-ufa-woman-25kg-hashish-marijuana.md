Drug-control officers in Krasnoyarsk Krai stopped the activity of a 49-year-old
Ufa woman involved in trafficking banned substances. [@1] Police detained her
red-handed near checkpoint No. 2 of the closed administrative-territorial entity
of Zheleznogorsk while she was trying to arrange a drug cache. [@1]
Investigators opened attempted large-scale drug-sale cases against her. [@1] A
court remanded her in custody at the investigator's request while the
investigation of her activity continues. [@1]

An initial search of the Audi she had arrived in found more than 1.6 kilograms
of marijuana and hashish. [@1] Police recovered with a metal detector the phone
she had managed to throw away in the adjoining woodland, and the device held
coordinates of caches she had already prepared. [@1] From those caches officers
seized 21 hashish wraps with a combined weight of 4.3 kilograms. [@2] A detailed
examination with service dog Groza uncovered a hidden cache under the lining of
the folding-roof niche holding 33 packs weighing more than 18 kilograms. [@1]
That hidden cache comprised 20 hashish wraps weighing 12.8 kilograms and 13
marijuana wraps weighing 5.3 kilograms. [@2] Police also found a half-kilogram
batch of cannabis in the rented Krasnoyarsk flat where she was temporarily
living. [@1] Police put the total weight seized at about 25 kilograms. [@1]

She had arrived in her personal Audi to sell a large batch of drugs in
Krasnoyarsk Krai. [@2] A GUFSIN dog handler with service dog Groza was brought
in to examine the car in detail after it was taken to a secure lot. [@1]
