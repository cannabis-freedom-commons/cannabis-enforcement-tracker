Curtis Aubert, 23, was sentenced by Jersey's Royal Court on 30 April 2026 for his part in importing
cannabis from Spain. [@1,2,3] In February 2024, when he was 21, Aubert met Evert Smallegange at the
Jersey Airport car park after Smallegange arrived from Spain; customs officers found that
Smallegange had swallowed 199 pellets containing 998.98 grams of cannabis resin. [@1,2] Aubert,
Smallegange and Oliver Lucas were arrested. [@1,2]

Smallegange was jailed for ten months and Lucas received a community service order. [@1,2] Aubert
initially denied the charges but later admitted being knowingly concerned in the importation of
cannabis, possession with intent to supply, and failing to disclose his phone PIN to customs
officers. [@1,2] Police found a further 446.11 grams of cannabis at his flat, along with scales and
self-sealing bags; the Jersey Evening Post estimated the drugs' street value at £20,000 to £30,000.
[@2]

Aubert was sentenced to 330 hours of community service — 180 for the importation, 150 concurrent for
possession with intent to supply, and 150 consecutive for the PIN offence — and a one-year probation
order. [@1,2] He had spent two months in custody on remand. [@1,3]
