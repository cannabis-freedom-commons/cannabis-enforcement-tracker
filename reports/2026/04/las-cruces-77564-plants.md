Guatemala's National Civil Police eradicated 77,564 marijuana plants in Las
Cruces, Petén, valued by police at 38,782,000 quetzales. [@1] The seizure took
place in a mountainous area, where specialized teams destroyed the illegal
crops, according to the PNC's official X account. [@1] The operation was
coordinated by the anti-narcotics analysis directorate SGAIA and the PNC agency
APNC, following intelligence work against trafficking networks in the north of
the country. [@1]

Authorities confirmed the official valuation, estimating the economic impact on
criminal structures at more than 38 million quetzales. [@1] The National Civil
Police said the actions strengthen the fight against drug trafficking in the
national territory. [@1]

The PNC presents this type of action as aimed at protecting and serving the
population while acting transparently. [@1] The PNC describes eradicating
illegal plantations as part of a sustained strategy including patrols, ground
operations and collaboration with state entities. [@1] Official data show
security forces intensified prevention and eradication in 2026 in border
departments such as Petén, where criminal groups use hard-to-reach areas. [@1]
