Costa Rica's Coast Guard seized 1,266 kilos of marijuana and 176 kilos of
cocaine in southern Pacific waters about 170 nautical miles off Cabo Matapalo.
[@1] Officers boarded an unflagged go-fast boat with no name or registration and
found the load on its open deck. [@2] Three suspects were detained: two Costa
Ricans and one Nicaraguan, and placed at the disposal of the judicial
authorities. [@1]

The boat held 1,266 marijuana packages of about a kilo each plus 295 additional
grams of marijuana. [@2] Authorities also found 13 drums with about 850 litres
of fuel used to move the load. [@2]

The operation was carried out by the Coast Guard's Special Operations Group with
the Drug Control Police, the Special Support Unit and the criminal intelligence
directorate, in coordination with Colombia, Panama and the United States. [@2]
The operation began after the Joint Interagency Task Force South operations
centre received information from Panama's aeronaval service, Colombia and the
DEA about a boat carrying suspected drugs toward Costa Rica. [@2] Security
Minister Mario Zamora said Costa Rica had already passed 21 tonnes of drugs
seized in 2026. [@1]
