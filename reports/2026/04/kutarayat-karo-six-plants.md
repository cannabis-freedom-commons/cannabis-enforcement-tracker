## Eradication

Karo narcotics officers detained a 46-year-old farmer identified as HS in Kutarayat, Naman Teran,
on Tuesday 28 April 2026. [@1,2] The search began after residents reported that he regularly
visited a nearby swamp to monitor cannabis plants. [@1]

Officers found six cannabis plants in the swamp, measuring 35 to 160 centimetres and weighing
about 500 grams wet. [@1,2] A follow-up search of the nearby home found 1.1 grams of damp cannabis
twigs, as well as a black plastic pot and a sack used for planting. [@2]

Police took HS and the evidence to the Karo narcotics unit and charged him under Article 114(1) of
Indonesia's Narcotics Law. [@1,2] Mistar.id published a police photograph showing the detainee and
the seized plants and evidence. [@1]
