On 15 April 2026 the French navy frigate Floréal intercepted two vessels in the
exclusive economic zone of Tromelin, an islet in the Indian Ocean, in an
operation named Baldor. [@2] The operation was coordinated by the authorities of
Réunion, Mauritius and the Seychelles and seized a drug cargo estimated by
French reporting at more than 64 million euros. [@2] Aboard the suspect ship
were ten crew members of different nationalities: four Pakistanis, three
Tanzanians, two Ethiopians and one Iranian. [@2] The search of the vessel turned
up 121 kg of cocaine, 108 kg of heroin and 42 kg of cannabis. [@2] The seizure
was carried out by the Réunion gendarmerie, which also arrested the crew
members. [@2] French reporting put the cannabis alone at about one million
euros, with the cocaine at 33 million and the heroin at nearly 30 million. [@2]
According to the first findings of the investigation, the drugs came from
Afghanistan and were destined for the Mauritian market. [@2] Le marin reported
that the Floréal took part in the joint Réunion–Mauritius–Seychelles operation,
which led to the seizure of more than 270 kg of drugs near Tromelin. [@1]
Mauritian outlet lexpress.mu reported that the Afghan-origin drugs were bound
for Mauritius and that the authorities seized 121 kg of cocaine, 108 kg of
heroin and 42 kg of cannabis from a suspect vessel. [@1]

Investigators identified two Mauritians, aged 35 and 33, suspected of recovering
the drugs at sea and said to play a key role in the network's local logistics.
[@2] On 16 April the two men were seen leaving Baie-du-Tombeau by boat for the
open sea, apparently to collect part of the cargo. [@2] The two men were
arrested the following day and brought before the courts, and one of them was
already known to the authorities from a 2024 case involving an attempt to import
cannabis between Mauritius and Réunion. [@2]

The Le Portail des sous-marins account, citing Zinfos974, states that the
Floréal intercepted the suspect ship in Tromelin's exclusive economic zone on 15
April and that searches found 121 kg of cocaine, 42 kg of cannabis and 108 kg of
heroin. [@3] The Floréal is one of the two surveillance frigates of the Réunion
naval base, alongside the Nivôse. [@2]
