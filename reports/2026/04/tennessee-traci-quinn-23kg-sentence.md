Traci Quinn, a mother and grandmother, spent two decades working as a nurse.
[@1] In 2016 she was arrested after transporting 50lb (23kg) of cannabis into
Tennessee. [@1] She was tried and convicted for trafficking even though she had
legally purchased the cannabis in California. [@1] She served three years in
prison. [@1] She set up her Pink Hats construction business in 2020 shortly
after being freed. [@1]

The first account describes Tennessee as having some of the strictest cannabis
laws in the United States. [@1] While working in hospitals Quinn had seen
cannabis help some patients, including those with seizures, which inspired her
to set up a company selling medical cannabis. [@1]

While behind bars Quinn says she felt called to set up a construction company on
release to specialise in redevelopment work in Chicago's poorest communities.
[@1] She said her mission to start the construction company came from God while
she was in prison. [@2]
