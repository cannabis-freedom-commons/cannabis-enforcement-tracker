Two young children in Waynesboro, Pennsylvania, were rushed to urgent care and
hospitalized on Tuesday after ingesting THC gummies left in their father's
vehicle. [@1] Mohammed Al Mawjari, 25, of Waynesboro, was charged with
endangering the welfare of children. [@1]

The 4-year-old daughter left the home, retrieved the gummies from the vehicle,
ate one and gave another to her 2-year-old brother. [@1] The Waynesboro Police
Department said the children showed signs consistent with an overdose and were
semi-responsive. [@1] Police responded at about 1:22 p.m. on 14 April 2026 to a
report on Abigail Avenue in Waynesboro. [@2]

The gummies were labelled 'Double Stacked Bites 2000mg Delta-8, Delta-9, and
THCP'. [@3] Officers found THC vape cartridges and multiple packages of
THC-infused gummies stored in areas accessible to the children at the home. [@1]
Al Mawjari was arrested on 16 April 2026 and charged with two counts of
endangering the welfare of a child and ten counts of possession of a controlled
substance. [@2] He remains held at Franklin County Jail on $300,000 bail. [@3]
Waynesboro Chief of Police Ryan Ramsey said there were two kids under the age of
five who overdosed at the home. [@4]
