Specialised operational units of the Sûreté nationale dismantled three criminal
narcotics-trafficking networks in three distinct operations in Blida and Tlemcen
during the previous week. [@2] Eight people were arrested and more than 2.5
million dinars were recovered in the three operations. [@1] The Directorate
General of National Security's central communication service announced the three
operations in a communiqué. [@2]

Officers seized a total of 86 kilograms of treated kif that the authorities said
had come from Morocco. [@1] The first operation in Blida yielded 36.5 kilograms
of cannabis, the sum of 2.53 million dinars and four passenger vehicles. [@1]
The second operation took place in Tlemcen, where law-enforcement officers
recovered 27 kilograms of cannabis. [@1] The third operation, also in Tlemcen
wilaya, yielded 23 kilograms of treated kif. [@1] The services also recovered
253 million centimes in the operations. [@3]

The communiqué said the dismantled networks were active in illicit trafficking
of narcotics. [@3] The French-language reports call the seized drug kif traité,
meaning treated kif. [@1]
