Brendan Fagan Self, 49, was the managing director of Hobart events company
Vandemonian Touring, which hosts live music acts at venues around Tasmania. [@1]
He appeared in Hobart Magistrates Court on 30 April 2026 charged with
trafficking cocaine and possessing cannabis. [@1] Court documents allege the
cocaine trafficking occurred between 13 and 18 September 2025. [@2] Court
documents allege he possessed cannabis in February 2026. [@1] He is yet to enter
a plea. [@1] Magistrate Reg Marron adjourned the case to July 21. [@2]

The alleged quantities of the substances were unclear and no weight or plant
count was published. [@1] Defence lawyer Pip Monk said the disclosure received
so far, including property seizure records, photographs and copies of search
warrants, was absolutely nowhere near what was required. [@1] Prosecutors said
the brief against Self was extensive and more was expected from police. [@2]
Self first appeared on the charges in February. [@2]

The company has previously hosted big-name artists, including Flume and Tones
and I, and staged the annual Tasmanian Wine Festival. [@1] Vandemonian Touring
also formerly operated Hobart Uni Bar on the University of Tasmania's Sandy Bay
campus. [@1] The venue closed in June last year. [@1] Australian Securities and
Investments Commission documents show another person was appointed director and
secretary of Vandemonian Touring in October last year. [@1]
