On 23 April 2026, National Guard anti-drug units arrested the brother of a
former international footballer in Ras Tabia, El Omrane, in Tunis. [@1] The
Bardo research-and-investigation brigade said the operation followed detailed
investigations and dismantled a criminal network active in drug trafficking in
Ras Tabia. [@2] Those arrested included the former international player's
brother alongside other alleged members of the network. [@2]

Officers seized 99 cannabis slabs weighing about 10 kilograms and said the drug
was intended for resale. [@1] Two accounts put the cannabis haul at more than 95
slabs intended for distribution in Ras Tabia and surrounding neighbourhoods.
[@2] Officers also seized an unspecified quantity of cocaine during the same
operation. [@3] After consulting the public prosecution, the suspects were
placed in custody and judicial proceedings were opened toward bringing them
before the courts. [@1] The prosecution ordered all of the suspects held and a
judicial inquiry opened before they are brought before the courts. [@2]
