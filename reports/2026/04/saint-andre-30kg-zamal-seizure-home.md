The national police in Saint-André, Réunion, carried out a large cannabis
seizure at the home of a private individual on Tuesday 14 April 2026. [@1]
During a patrol in Saint-André, officers were alerted by a strong smell of
cannabis near the individual's home, an account the outlet said came from its
own information. [@1]

Arriving at the premises, the officers found more than 30 kg of zamal, the local
name for cannabis on Réunion. [@1] An investigation was opened after the
discovery. [@1]

No arrest, charge or court step was reported in the account, which did not name
the person whose home was searched. [@1] LINFO.re published the report on 15
April 2026, the day after the operation, under the headline on the seizure of
more than 30 kg of zamal from a private individual in Saint-André. [@1]
