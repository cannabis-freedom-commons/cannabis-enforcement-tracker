Gendarmes arrested a man from Sainte-Lucie at a cabin in Le François on 9 April
2026 during a drug-trafficking investigation. [@1,3] The operation was run by the
François gendarmerie and the Marin research brigade, with the GIGN's Martinique
unit and the Marin intervention platoon. [@2] Investigators had located the
cabin, described as a place where drug users regularly came to stock up. [@2,3]
The GIGN was brought in because armed men were thought possibly to be guarding
the site. [@2] The suspect tried to flee when the GIGN arrived and was quickly
caught. [@2,3]

A search of the cabin found more than 5.7 kg of cannabis herb, three handguns
and 58 rounds of 9 mm and .380 automatic ammunition. [@1,3] The same search was
also reported as yielding more than 5.7 kilos of cannabis herb, three handguns
and 58 rounds. [@2]

He was brought before the Fort-de-France prosecutor on Saturday 11 April 2026
and placed in pretrial detention. [@1] He is to be tried in deferred
immediate-appearance proceedings with a hearing expected on 18 May. [@2,3] He was
already banned from holding a weapon and from French territory and faces charges
of drug detention and transport, category A and B weapon detention, and
breaching those bans. [@2]

The man had already been convicted before this arrest. [@3]
