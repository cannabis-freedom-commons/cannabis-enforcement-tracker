Rapid Action Battalion-1 arrested two men in the Kaliakair police-station area
of Gazipur district. [@1] The detainees were Md. Dadon Mia, 32, and Md. Shihab
Uddin, 22. [@1] The men tried to flee as officers entered the room and were
detained inside it. [@1] The arrest took place at about 3:00 a.m. on 22 April
2026. [@1]

The room was the northeast corner room of Md. Dadon Mia's rented flat. [@1] The
flat is on the second floor of Sikder Mohol behind the biking pump at
Hortokitola. [@1] The detainees produced the cannabis by hand from under the bed
in their bedroom. [@1] Officers recovered 70 kg 700 grams (70.7 kg) of cannabis
(ganja). [@1] The force valued the cannabis at Tk 2,121,000. [@1] Officers also
seized Tk 370,500 in cash described as proceeds of cannabis sales. [@1] Officers
also seized two smartphones in the presence of witnesses. [@1]

The team had received a secret tip on 21 April 2026 while on law-and-order duty
in Mirer Bazar, Pubail. [@1] The tip said a large quantity of illicit cannabis
was being stocked in the flat for buying and selling. [@1] Officers went to the
flat to verify the information and take legal action. [@1] All seized items were
recorded under a seizure list and handed to Kaliakair Police Station for further
legal action. [@1]
