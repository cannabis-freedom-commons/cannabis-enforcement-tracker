## Eradication

Unit Intel Kodim 0206/Dairi detained a 33-year-old man identified as SRC in the early hours of
Friday 24 April 2026 after a community report about cannabis plants in his maize field. [@1]

During a morning field inspection with Dairi narcotics officers and village officials, the team
found three cannabis plants about 1.5 metres tall hidden among the maize. [@1] A later Polres
Dairi summary also listed three cannabis plants at Desa Bangun Induk among its January–April
cultivation cases. [@2]

The authorities handed SRC and the evidence to the Dairi narcotics unit for further proceedings.
[@1] MedanBisnisDaily published a photograph of him holding the plants at the police station. [@1]
