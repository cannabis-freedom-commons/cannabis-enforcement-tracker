Federal Police officers, supported by Piauí Military Police units, eradicated a
cannabis plantation on a rural property in Avelino Lopes, in southern Piauí.
[@2] Three people were arrested in the act for direct involvement in the illegal
cultivation. [@2] The Piauí Public Security Secretariat named the three only by
the initials D. S. A., D. M. L. and D. F. S. [@1]

The plantation covered about three hectares and held roughly 35,000 cannabis
seedlings that were still to be extracted. [@1] Colonel James Sean, commander of
the Specialised Policing Command, said part of the plants was taken as a sample
for the investigation file and the rest was burned on the spot. [@1] Security
forces also found equipment used for planting, irrigation and processing the
drug. [@1] Officers seized three mobile phones and two handheld radios at the
site. [@1]

A10+ located the operation at Lagoa do Tuntun, a hamlet where a patch of about
35,000 cannabis plants was found. [@3] The units involved were the Specialised
Policing Command, the 4th Company of the Interior Policing Battalion based in
Bom Jesus, the 4th Company and 7th Battalion of Avelino Lopes, and the Tactical
Force. [@1] The suspects were taken to a Civil Police station and placed at the
disposal of the courts, A10+ reported. [@3]
