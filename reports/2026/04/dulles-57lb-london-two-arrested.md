Carl Caran Raashad Henson, 31, and Tilesha Kelly Theresa Coles, 31, were both
arrested on April 16. [@1] The two, a man and a woman from Baltimore, face
felony charges after being caught at Washington Dulles International Airport
trying to smuggle more than 57 pounds of marijuana on a flight to London,
according to U.S. Customs and Border Protection. [@1] The pair was turned over
to the Washington Airports Authority Police. [@1] They both face charges of
possession of controlled substances, transport into the Commonwealth with intent
to distribute greater than five pounds of marijuana, and conspiracy to commit
drug offenses. [@1]

Investigators inspecting luggage being loaded onto a London-bound flight
discovered several black, vacuum-sealed bags. [@1] Officers said the bags held
57 pounds and 6 ounces of marijuana, about 26.02 kg. [@1]

CBP has also made similar seizures at Baltimore-Washington International (BWI)
Thurgood Marshall Airport, also on London-bound flights, in a separate set of
cases not counted here. [@1] In all, 247 pounds of marijuana have been seized at
that airport. [@1]
