Investigators of the Gendarmerie nationale's search and investigation service in
Chéraga, in the Algiers region, arrested a man described as a drug trafficker on
Sunday after acting on intelligence. [@1] The national defence ministry said the
operation also broke up an organised criminal network specialising in
trafficking narcotics and psychotropic substances. [@2] The operation took place
on Sunday 26 April 2026, according to the ministry statement quoted by Algerian
media. [@3]

Officers seized a quintal and 17 kilograms of treated kif, that is, 117
kilograms of processed cannabis resin. [@1] The seizure was announced in a
communiqué of the Ministry of National Defence, the parent ministry of the
gendarmerie, which belongs to the army's 1st Military Region. [@2]

Alongside the cannabis, officers seized a quantity of money, a semi-trailer
truck and a passenger vehicle. [@1] The gendarmerie's statement described the
money as substantial and as coming from the network's criminal revenues. [@3]
The gendarmerie unit was described as falling under the 1st Military Region, and
the national gendarmerie is based in Chéraga. [@2]
