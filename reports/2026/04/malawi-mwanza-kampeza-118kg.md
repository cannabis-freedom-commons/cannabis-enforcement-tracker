A First Grade Magistrate's Court in Mwanza convicted Bertha Kampeza, 40, of importing and possessing 118 kg of cannabis without a permit and sentenced her to six years' imprisonment with hard labour, Face of Malawi reported. [@1]

State prosecutor Inspector Jane Juma told the court that on 31 March 2026 drug enforcement officers at Mwanza Border received a tip-off about a truck from South Africa suspected of carrying cannabis. [@1] Officers working with Malawi Revenue Authority officials searched the truck and found seven sealed bags of cannabis sativa. [@1] Kampeza admitted the bags were hers and was arrested. [@1]

The substance was sent to Bvumbwe Research Station, where it was confirmed as Indian hemp weighing 118 kg. [@1] Kampeza pleaded guilty when she appeared before First Grade Magistrate Gladstone Chilundu, who upheld the state's submissions and imposed the six-year term. [@1]

Kampeza comes from Namiwa Village under Traditional Authority Kapichi in Thyolo District. [@1]
