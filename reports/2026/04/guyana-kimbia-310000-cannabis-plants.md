Guyana Police Force officers conducted an eradication operation at Kimbia in the Upper Berbice River between 05:15 and 19:55 on 17 April 2026. [@1,2] Police said they found two fields spanning about seven acres (2.83 hectares), with approximately 310,000 cannabis plants between two and six feet tall. [@1,2]

Officers also found two makeshift camps and two drying areas, where they reported finding about 380 pounds (172.4 kg) of dried cannabis. [@1,2] Police said they burned the plants, dried cannabis, camps and drying areas. [@1,2] They estimated the value of the destroyed narcotics at GYD 258,729,310 and said no arrests were made. [@1,2]

The Guyana Chronicle reported that investigations continued after the operation. [@1] Kaieteur News published the same figures and details from a police statement, including that the operation ended without incident. [@2] The two reports do not break the value estimate down by item or give a separate weight for the plants. [@1,2]
