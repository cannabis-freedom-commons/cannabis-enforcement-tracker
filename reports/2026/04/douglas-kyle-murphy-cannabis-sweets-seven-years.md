Kyle Murphy, 20, was jailed for seven years after admitting cocaine possession
with intent to supply, cannabis supply offences, psilocybin possession, cannabis
possession and possessing criminal property. [@1] He was sentenced at the Court
of General Gaol Delivery, where the headline seven-year term for cocaine
possession with intent to supply ran concurrently with all other sentences. [@1]
His advocate said he had serious mental health issues, had been drawn into
dealing at 15 through exploitation, naivety and immaturity, made very little
money and had been used as a cog in the supply machine. [@1] Deemster Graeme
Cook told Murphy he had essentially run a shop selling drugs and had continued
dealing after arrest and bail, while taking his young age when drawn in into
account. [@1]

Police searched Murphy's home in Hillside Avenue, Douglas, on April 24, 2024 and
seized 11 containers of cannabis in 3g amounts along with THC sweets. [@1] That
search also yielded 450g of sweets containing THC, plus 4.4g of magic mushrooms,
928g of chocolate laced with psilocybin, other paraphernalia and £2,000 in cash.
[@1] His earlier plea hearing recorded the drugs found as 64.6 grams of
cannabis, 4.4 grams of magic mushrooms, 928 grams of chocolate and 450 grams of
cannabis sweets. [@2] At that hearing he admitted two counts of possessing
cannabis with intent to supply, two counts of possessing psilocybin, being
concerned in supplying cannabis and possessing £4,115 in criminal cash. [@2]

He was later traced to a room at the Empress Hotel in Douglas in November 2024,
where 5g of cannabis was found with ziplock bags and cash. [@1] A red holdall
there held white powder identified as 117g of cocaine worth £11,700. [@1]
Operating as Flavour Chaser, he sold psilocybin chocolate and chewy and
marshmallow sweets laced with THC, the psychoactive component in cannabis. [@1]
Phone evidence showed dealing from October 1, 2021 until November 5, 2024,
beginning when he was 15, including ounce-level cannabis sales such as three
ounces worth £1,050. [@1]
