On 2 April 2026 a Colombian Navy operation seized 1.7 tonnes of marijuana and
captured two Costa Rican citizens in the central Pacific. [@1] The action took
place in waters near Buenaventura, in the department of Valle del Cauca. [@1]
The operation began after information was received about a boat moving
suspiciously in the area. [@1] A Rapid Reaction Unit of the Buenaventura Coast
Guard station was deployed to intercept the motorboat. [@1] A second account
said the launch was intercepted very near Buenaventura with a load valued at
more than 1.8 million dollars. [@2]

During the inspection officers found the boat was carrying 71 sacks with 1,772
rectangular packages of the kind used for illicit shipments. [@1] Communications
equipment was also found among the seized items. [@1] Personnel of the
Fiscalía's Technical Investigation Corps confirmed through preliminary tests
that the load was 1,743 kilograms of marijuana. [@1] The same account said
inspection found 71 sacks holding the load. [@2] Based on Navy estimates, the
seizure prevented more than 289,000 doses of marijuana from reaching illegal
international markets. [@1] The Navy said the operation denied criminal
drug-trafficking structures income of more than 1.8 million dollars. [@1]

Adverse weather and several cracks in the hull let water enter and sank the
boat. [@1] The coast guards' rapid action rescued both crew members and
recovered the entire cargo. [@1] It added that officers rescued the crew and
recovered the drugs after the boat began to sink. [@2] The two Costa Rican
citizens and the seized material were placed at the disposal of justice while
investigations continue into the origin and final destination of the drugs. [@1]
