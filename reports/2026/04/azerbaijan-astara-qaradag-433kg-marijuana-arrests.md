Azerbaijan's Interior Ministry Drug Enforcement Main Directorate arrested three
previously convicted men, Ali Mamedov, Ravan Huseynov and Ilkin Akhmedov, during
operations in Astara and Qaradağ districts. [@2] The ministry said the three men
had been convicted earlier of premeditated murder and of theft. [@3]

Officers seized 43 kg 300 g of marijuana with harmful substances added to it
from the three men. [@1] According to the ministry, the men had entered a
criminal conspiracy with Iranian citizens whose identities investigators were
still establishing. [@3] The ministry said the three obtained the drugs in
border districts and planned to place them at various addresses in Azerbaijan
for later sale. [@2]

A criminal case was opened over the findings. [@3] A court ordered all three
detained men held in pre-trial detention. [@3]
