Cabo Verde's Polícia Judiciária dismantled two cannabis plots at the locality of
João Bernardo, in the municipality of Santa Catarina de Santiago, on the morning
of Tuesday 21 April 2026. [@1] The operation was carried out in the presence of
prosecutors of the Santa Catarina de Santiago judicial district. [@1] Officers
seized and destroyed all the plantation found there, a total of 741 kg of
cannabis that was already ready for distribution and consumption. [@1]
Investigations continue with a view to identifying the owner of the plantations.
[@1]

The operation was carried out under authorisation, seizure and destruction
warrants requested from the Public Prosecutor's Office by the Assomada criminal
investigation department and issued by the Criminal Instruction Court of the
Judicial District of Santa Catarina de Santiago. [@2] The Judicial Police said
the operation was part of its actions to prevent and combat domestic drug
trafficking. [@3] The cannabis was spread across two agricultural plots, all of
which were seized and destroyed on the spot. [@3]
