Police in North Kazakhstan Region detained a 57-year-old Petropavlovsk resident
suspected of selling drugs. [@1] According to police, the man sold prohibited
substances “from hand to hand.” [@1] Officers seized three wraps of hashish from
him at the time of detention. [@1] A search at his residence found additional
batches of drugs packaged for sale and homemade devices for using them. [@1]

The total weight of the seized hashish was over 463 grams. [@1] A medical
examination found the detainee was under narcotic intoxication caused by
cannabinoid use. [@1] His acts were qualified under Article 297, part 3 of
Kazakhstan's Criminal Code as illegal storage of narcotic substances for sale on
an especially large scale. [@1] An investigation was under way. [@2]

Since the start of the year, police in the region had stopped 96 drug offences.
[@1] In that period about 12 kilograms of drugs had been taken out of
circulation, including about 7.5 kg of synthetics. [@1]
