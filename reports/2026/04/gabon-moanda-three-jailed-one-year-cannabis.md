Officers of the Haut-Ogooué provincial branch of the Central Anti-Drug Office
(Ocad), Gabon's central anti-drug agency, arrested three men in the Commercial
district of Moanda. [@1] Those arrested were Dan Beyanga, 31, Stan Lekouma, 34,
and Rosny Poungou, 22, a student at a private university in Franceville. [@1]
They were held in pre-trial detention at Yené prison and tried in a correctional
flagrant-délit hearing. [@1] The court sentenced all three to one year of firm
imprisonment. [@1] The conviction was for unlawful possession of prohibited
substances. [@1]

At the arrest they were found with cannabis sweets (bonbons de cannabis), two
Tramadol capsules and a sachet of loose cannabis. [@1] The court treated those
items as sufficient to establish the possession offence. [@1]

Beyanga had previously been sentenced to six months' imprisonment in 2021 for
cannabis possession. [@1] Lekouma had received a similar conviction in 2023.
[@1] The one-year terms were presented as part of a judicial effort to deter
drug circulation in Haut-Ogooué despite regular enforcement operations. [@1]
