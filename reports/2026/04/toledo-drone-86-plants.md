Law enforcement officers flew a reconnaissance drone assigned to the Special Branch office
over a remote area of Toledo District, and its aerial scan led them to a corn field. [@1]
There they found cannabis plants hanging to dry beneath a small structure with zinc roofing,
and a further check of the surroundings revealed a second site where more plants were being
cultivated. [@1]

Officers went to both locations and uprooted every plant found, destroying 86 cannabis
plants, three to seven feet tall, by fire. [@1] The account names no arrests and no owners
of the sites. [@1]
