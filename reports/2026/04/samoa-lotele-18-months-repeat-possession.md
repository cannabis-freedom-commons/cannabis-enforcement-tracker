Tulolo Lotele, 32, was jailed for 18 months by Samoa's Supreme Court for a
repeat offence of possessing narcotics. [@1] He pleaded guilty to possessing
marijuana and utensils. [@1] Police searched his vehicle at Tauese on 6 January
2026. [@1] Officers found 12 marijuana cigarettes, two clear pipes and a
portable scale. [@1]

The Alcohol and Drugs Court denied him bail because of previous offences of the
same kind. [@1] Justice Leutele Mata Tuatagaloa began at two years and took off
25 per cent for his early guilty plea. [@1] The judge told him she hoped prison
would teach him a lesson and that he would become a good citizen of Samoa
afterwards. [@1] Lotele is unemployed and comes from the village of Saina. [@1]
