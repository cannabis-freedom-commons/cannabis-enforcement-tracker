Police in Belize have formally arrested and charged a 43-year-old man. [@1] The
charge is possession of controlled drugs with intent to supply to another. [@1]

On Wednesday, April 8, 2026, at about 5:00 p.m., police observed a white Ford
Fusion travelling from the direction of Orange Walk toward Belize City. [@1]
Police pulled the car over on the roadside. [@1] The driver, Godwin Samuel
Straughan, consented to a search of his person and vehicle. [@1]

During the search, police discovered a large black and transparent plastic bag
in the vehicle. [@1] The bag contained 463 grams (16.33 ounces, 1.02 pounds) of
suspected cannabis. [@1] Police formally charged Straughan with the offence on
Wednesday, April 8, the same day as the stop. [@1]
