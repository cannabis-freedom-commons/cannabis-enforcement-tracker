Police formally arrested and jointly charged 21-year-old Teja Robateau and
25-year-old Joshua Franklin, both of Hattieville Village. [@1] The two face
charges of possession of controlled drugs with intent to supply, keeping a
firearm without a gun licence, and keeping ammunition without a gun licence.
[@1]

Police executed a search warrant at a residence in Hattieville Village on 1
April 2026. [@1] The search led to the discovery of seven parcels of suspected
cannabis weighing a total of 739 grams. [@1] Officers also seized a Glock 9mm
pistol holding 13 live rounds and two extended 9mm magazines holding 13 and 17
live rounds. [@1]

Assistant Superintendent Stacy Smith, communications officer for the Belize
Police Department, said the packaging was consistent with distribution rather
than personal use. [@1] Police said the manner in which the marijuana was
packaged raised concerns about possible drug trafficking. [@1]
