Jordanian Armed Forces–Arab Army personnel intercepted ten coordinated attempts
to smuggle large quantities of drugs into the kingdom across its northern and
western frontiers early on Wednesday, 1 April 2026. [@2] On the western border,
Southern Military Zone personnel stopped a drone carrying approximately 100
blocks of hashish. [@2]

The military attributed the outcome to precise monitoring and integration
between Border Guard units, military security agencies and the Anti-Narcotics
Department, which brought the intercepted devices and their payloads down inside
Jordanian territory. [@2] The intercepted materials and equipment were handed to
the relevant authorities for legal processing. [@2]

On the northern front, authorities intercepted nine simultaneous smuggling
attempts involving electronically guided balloons. [@3] The military said
traffickers launched the balloons in a synchronised pattern to exploit
prevailing weather and overwhelm border-guard monitoring. [@2] JAF personnel
tracked and intercepted all nine balloon payloads despite the effort to distract
security units and overwhelm monitoring. [@2] The military put the combined
seizures at more than 1.5 million narcotic pills in addition to the hashish.
