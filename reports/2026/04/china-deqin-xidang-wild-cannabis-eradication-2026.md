Deqin County's public security bureau anti-drug team and officers from Yunling police station carried out a spring eradication sweep around Xidang Village, the county government said in a notice published on 16 April 2026. [@1]

The joint team searched woodland, fields, areas around homes and unused plots in what the notice described as a thorough, grid-style inspection. [@1] Officers uprooted more than 1,000 wild cannabis plants. [@1]

Police also spoke with nearby residents and distributed leaflets on the risks of drug plants, how to identify them and the relevant laws. [@1] The county presented the sweep as part of its spring effort to stop illegal cultivation at its source. [@1]

The notice gives no exact day for the fieldwork, no count of separate sites and no information about who may have grown the plants. [@1] It reports no arrest or later case outcome. [@1]
