Oklahoma Bureau of Narcotics agents seized 8,897 marijuana plants while serving
a warrant at Huang Zhewei 5858 LLC in Wynnewood, Oklahoma. [@1] The seizure
followed an OBN investigation into alleged black market marijuana trafficking.
[@1] Three people were detained after the raid. [@1]

One person was taken into ICE custody, and more arrests are expected. [@1]
Oklahoma Department of Agriculture agents wrote a separate search warrant to
seize illegal pesticides found at the grow. [@1]
