Uzbek border guards and State Security Service officers detained two men as they
crossed the state border illegally from a neighbouring country into Uzbekistan.
[@1]

Two sports bags carried by the men held 4.88 kg of opium, 19.86 kg of hashish
and 380 boxes of tobacco products, all seized as evidence. [@1] In Surkhandarya
region, officers detained three men aged 48, 45 and 22 as they crossed the
border and swam the Amu Darya using an inner tube from a car wheel. [@1] The
bags the three men carried held 2.7 kg of opium and 8.25 kg of hashish, giving
the two cases 28.11 kg of hashish and a reported total of more than 35 kg of
drugs. [@1]

The two detained border crossers face a criminal case under the criminal code's
articles 223, 246 and 273 and were placed in pre-trial detention. [@1] A
criminal case on the three detained on the Amu Darya was opened on the
illegal-crossing and smuggling charges, and they were also remanded in custody.
[@1]

The State Security Service said traffickers had promised the couriers 200 US
dollars for each kilogram of the load delivered to its destination. [@1]

The Uzbek-language version says the first pair's hashish weighed 19 kg 863 g, 3
g more than the Russian text, and that officers were investigating further. [@2]
The service's own Telegram statement described the bags as holding 4 kg 884 g of
opium and 19 kg 863 g of hashish, and described the tobacco as 380 boxes of Esse
Change cigarettes. [@3] The service said the three men on the Amu Darya were
carrying 2 kg 708 g of opium and 8 kg 257 g of hashish. [@3]

Border guards and the service's Tashkent regional office carried out the first
operation, while the second involved the service's Surkhandarya office and
border guards. [@3]
