A 29-person joint patrol operating on 10 and 11 April 2026 found cannabis plantations in Kampung
Yunabol, Oksibil District, and Kampung Siminbuk, Serambakon District, in Pegunungan Bintang. [@1,2]

The patrol removed about 226 plants from the two sites: 81 plants at Yunabol and 145 at Siminbuk.
[@1,2] The Indonesian National Police said the evidence was handed to the Pegunungan Bintang
Police Narcotics Unit for further investigation. [@1]

Officers also held LU, 57, and GU alias K, 27, but described both as witnesses rather than
suspects; initial urine tests were negative. [@1,2]
