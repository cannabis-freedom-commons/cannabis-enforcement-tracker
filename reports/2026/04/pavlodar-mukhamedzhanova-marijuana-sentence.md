Pavlodar's Interdistrict Court for criminal cases found Olga Mukhamedzhanova
guilty on 14 April under part 2 of article 297 and part 2 of article 296 of
Kazakhstan's Criminal Code. [@1] The court found her guilty of illegal storage
of psychotropic substances for the purpose of sale and storage of drugs for
personal use. [@1] In court Mukhamedzhanova fully admitted all charges,
expressed remorse and did not dispute the collected evidence. [@1] The case was
therefore considered in abbreviated procedure. [@1] For storage for sale she
received 6 years' imprisonment and for storage without purpose of sale 80 hours
of public works, with the combined final punishment set at 6 years'
imprisonment. [@1] The court deferred execution of the punishment for 5 years
under article 74 of the Criminal Code and placed supervision with the probation
service at her residence. [@1]

On 22 January 2026 she was in Pavlodar when police stopped the car she was
riding in near the Irtysh checkpoint at about 19.43. [@1] The search found 1.3
grams of a 4-methylmethcathinone analogue and 50 wraps with the synthetic
cannabinoid MDMB-4en-PINACA with a total mass of 0.1815 grams. [@1] According to
the case materials those substances were intended for further sale. [@1]
Officers also found 2.6 grams of dried marijuana in her bag, which the court
found she kept for personal use. [@1] The court took into account that she had
no prior convictions, had admitted guilt, was remorseful and was raising a
9-year-old child, and found no aggravating circumstances. [@1] The court warned
that the deferral could be revoked if she evaded raising the child or continued
to disturb public order after two written warnings. [@1] It ordered her to pay
99,612 tenge in procedural costs and 86,500 tenge to the victims' compensation
fund. [@1]

Because of the deferral she was not sent to a penal colony immediately. [@1]
