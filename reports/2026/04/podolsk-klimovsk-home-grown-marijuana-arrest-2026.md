Drug-control officers of the Interior Ministry directorate for the Podolsk urban
district detained a 49-year-old resident of the Klimovsk microdistrict on
suspicion of attempted sale of banned substances. [@1] The Podolsk City Court
ordered the man held in custody pending the case. [@1] A search of his home
found 12 plastic packets containing plant material. [@1]

The seized material went to the Moscow Oblast Expert-Criminalistic Centre, whose
specialist concluded the samples were marijuana with a total weight of more than
1.5 kilograms. [@1] The detainee said he bought cannabis seeds through the
shadow segment of the Internet the previous spring and grew them on his
household garden plot. [@1]

He said he dried and ground the harvested crop and prepared it for further sale.
[@1] An investigator of the Podolsk Interior Ministry directorate's
investigative department opened a criminal case for attempted large-scale drug
sale under part 3 of article 30 and part 4 of article 228.1 of the Russian
Criminal Code. [@1]

Moscow Oblast Interior Ministry information chief Tatyana Petrova told the
Moscow city news agency that the packets held marijuana weighing more than 1.5
kg. [@2] The Interior Ministry's own account, republished with its press
release, says the packets were marijuana with a total mass of more than one and
a half kilograms. [@3]
