French customs officers stopped a utility vehicle at the Gerzat toll plaza on
the A71 in Puy-de-Dôme, north of Clermont-Ferrand, at about 2am on the night of
Wednesday 8 to Thursday 9 April 2026. [@1] The driver of the vehicle was
arrested and placed in police custody. [@1] The prosecutor's office said an
investigation was opened and entrusted to the Clermont-Ferrand judicial police.
[@2]

The seizure involved more than one tonne of cannabis, described as cannabis
resin, found during the roadside customs check. [@1] The drug was hidden in bags
stored in the boot of the vehicle. [@1] The utility vehicle was travelling on
the A71 between Clermont-Ferrand and Orléans. [@2]

No profile of the detained driver was released. [@1] A customs account described
the A71 as one of the main routes used by traffickers to move drugs arriving
from Spain, alongside the A10 via Bordeaux and the A7 via Lyon. [@1] French
customs seized more than 67 tonnes of cannabis in France in 2025, according to
the same reporting. [@1]
