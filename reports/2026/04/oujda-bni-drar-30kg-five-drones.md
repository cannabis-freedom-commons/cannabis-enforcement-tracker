Royal Gendarmerie units under the Oujda regional command foiled an attempt to
traffic hashish toward Algeria on Thursday 16 April. [@1] Officers arrested a
drug trafficker during the operation. [@1] The arrested man was presented to the
Oujda prosecutor on Friday. [@1] The investigating judge in charge of the case
opened an inquiry. [@1]

Officers seized about 30 kilograms of chira that had been prepared for transfer
across the Morocco-Algeria border by cross-border drones. [@1] They also seized
five cross-border drones with their batteries, accessories and control systems
from the trafficker's home. [@1]

That week the gendarmerie ran stepped-up security operations in the municipality
of Bni Drar and the commune of Bni Khaled against the networks' new trafficking
strategies. [@1] Gendarmerie sources describe the two localities as recent
informal logistics bases from which hashish is flown by drone toward Algeria,
where accomplices intercept the loads. [@1] The same accounts blame the area's
size, difficult mountainous terrain and a lack of economic alternatives for
residents for hampering the fight against the trafficking. [@1]
