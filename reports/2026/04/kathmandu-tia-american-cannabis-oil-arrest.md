Police at Tribhuvan International Airport in Kathmandu arrested 46-year-old
American citizen Ognian Petrov. [@1] Airport Security Office spokesperson Raj
Kumar Silwal identified Petrov as the person arrested. [@1]

Officers grew suspicious of Petrov's luggage during screening at the
luggage-checking area and opened it for inspection. [@1] Inside they found five
bottles of liquid resembling cannabis or hash oil, each holding 1.25 litres.
[@2] Petrov was scheduled to fly from Kathmandu to Delhi on Air India flight AI
218 when the bottles were detected. [@2] Police identified him by US passport
number A83488487. [@2]

Airport police handed Petrov to the Narcotics Control Bureau police team
deployed at the airport. [@1] The bureau took him for further investigation and
legal action, which was still under way. [@2]
