Officers of the Policía de León, acting for the Secretaría de Seguridad,
Prevención y Protección Ciudadana, detained two men who were in possession of
weapons reserved for the Army and a drug. [@1] The men were identified as
Leonardo Daniel "N" and Sebastián "N", and were described as apparently
brothers. [@1] The intervention was on calle República Dominicana, almost at the
corner with Tlaxcala, in the colonia Loma Bonita in León, Guanajuato. [@1]
During an operation in the area, officers saw a white Volkswagen Jetta
circulating without a front licence plate, which breaches the police and traffic
regulations. [@1] They ordered the driver to stop by loudspeaker commands; he
accelerated, and officers intercepted the car a short distance ahead. [@1] As
they approached to point out the offence, they saw what appeared to be a firearm
and ordered both occupants to get out for a search. [@1]

In the intervention officers seized three long guns, magazines, live cartridges
and marijuana. [@1] The search found a 7.62 mm long gun of the type called a
cuerno de chivo. [@1] Officers also found a second 7.62 mm long gun. [@1] One
weapon was of .223 calibre and reserved for exclusive use by the Army. [@1] They
seized four magazines for long guns and one magazine for a handgun. [@1] A bag
held approximately a quarter of marijuana. [@1] Periódico AM, citing the same
secretariat, reported the same seizure of four long-gun magazines, one handgun
magazine, 98 live cartridges, a bag with about a quarter of marijuana and a
mobile phone. [@2]

The weapons, the drug and the two detainees were placed at the disposal of the
Fiscalía General de la República, the federal prosecutor's office, which was to
determine their legal situation. [@1] The secretariat said it was investigating
whether the men could be linked to other crimes, and that the competent
authority would follow the investigations. [@1]
