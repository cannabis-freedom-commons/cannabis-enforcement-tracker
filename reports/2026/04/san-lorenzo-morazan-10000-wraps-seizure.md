No one was arrested in the San Lorenzo house operation. [@1] Police said some of
the main targets fled after detecting the officers but have been fully
identified and could be captured in the coming hours. [@1] Police added that
other people are wanted for investigation in connection with the case. [@1]

National Police officers seized more than 10,000 wraps of suspected drugs in San
Lorenzo, Valle. [@1] Officers found the drugs hidden in stashes, known as
caletas, inside a residence in colonia Morazán, according to Subinspector
Maldonado. [@1] The Dirección Policial de Investigaciones (DPI), Honduras's
investigative police directorate, joined preventive personnel in carrying out
the seizure after intelligence work. [@1]

Police information presumptively linked the drugs to the criminal structure
known as MS-13. [@1] Police presented the operation as part of actions to
dismantle criminal structures in southern Honduras, where intelligence work and
preventive patrols have been intensified. [@1] Police said they will continue
strategies to locate and seize illicit substances and weaken groups linked to
drug trafficking. [@1]
