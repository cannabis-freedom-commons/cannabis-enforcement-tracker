Police and soldiers detained Walner Edenilson Quijada Cortez, alias Roro, in
Ciudad Arce in La Libertad Centro shortly before midnight on Wednesday and found
marijuana on him. [@1] Police describe him as a suspected member of the
Zapotitanes Criminals Salvatruchos clique of the MS13 gang. [@2]

He is to be sent to the courts on charges of illicit association and of
possession and holding of drugs. [@1] According to the police, Quijada Cortez
had been arrested in 2022 and 2023, when he was still a minor, and released both
times under the minors' law by juvenile judges. [@2] The arrest was the third in
four years for Quijada Cortez, Diario1 reported. [@3] The authorities did not
give the exact charges in the two earlier detentions, or the amount of marijuana
found this time. [@1]

Police presented the arrest as part of a joint police and armed forces strategy
against organised crime in La Libertad, a region where criminal groups have long
been present. [@1]
