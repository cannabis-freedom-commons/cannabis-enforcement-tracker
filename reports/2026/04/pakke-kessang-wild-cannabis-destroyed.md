A joint team of police, forest officials and local residents destroyed wild
cannabis plants spread over 20,000 square metres (about two hectares) in Pakke
Kessang district, Arunachal Pradesh, on 8 April. [@1] The operation took place
near Sede Natung village, in the Lemmi police station area. [@2]

Pakke Kessang superintendent of police Swati Singh said a special police team
was formed after information came in on the night of 7 April about large-scale
wild cannabis cultivation under Lemmi police station. [@1] The information was
passed to the district administration and the forest department so that the
operation could be run jointly. [@1] The team uprooted and cut the plants and
burnt them on site. [@1]

Singh said such operations would continue in the coming days to curb the illegal
cultivation of narcotic plants in the district. [@1] She urged residents to
cooperate by sharing information about such activity. [@1] The public was
advised to avoid narcotic substances because of their effect on society and on
young people. [@1] No arrests or charges were reported. [@1]
