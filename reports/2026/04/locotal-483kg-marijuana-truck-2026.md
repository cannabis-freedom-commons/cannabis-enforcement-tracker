Officers from Bolivia's Rural Mobile Patrol Unit (UMOPAR) intercepted a truck at
the Locotal vehicular checkpoint in the Cochabamba tropics on the morning of 17
April 2026. [@2] At around 07:30, police stopped the vehicle carrying
construction bricks in its cargo bed and questioned the 37-year-old driver,
identified as Hernán M. G. [@2] Officers conducted an exhaustive search of the
vehicle after the driver appeared nervous and gave contradictory answers during
the police interview. [@2]

During the inspection, officers uncovered a concealment compartment in the
front, centre and rear sections of the truck bed, where wooden boards hidden
beneath the bricks covered blue jute sacks. [@2] Inside the sacks, police found
packages of a greenish plant material that field-tested positive for marijuana.
[@2] The shipment comprised 445 packages distributed across 16 jute bags, with a
total weight of 483.6 kilograms in wet state. [@2]

Anti-narcotics agents arrested the driver on the spot and confiscated the truck
along with two mobile phones. [@2] A controlled substances prosecutor ordered
the driver, the confiscated drugs and the vehicle transferred to UMOPAR
headquarters in the Chapare region for further investigation. [@2] On 21 April
2026, FELCN national director Frans William Cabrera confirmed the Locotal
seizure of 483 kilograms of marijuana among six recent operations carried out in
the Cochabamba tropics. [@1] Authorities announced that the six operations
together netted 1,170 kilograms of controlled substances and inflicted an
estimated 2.6 million dollars in economic damage on trafficking networks. [@1]
