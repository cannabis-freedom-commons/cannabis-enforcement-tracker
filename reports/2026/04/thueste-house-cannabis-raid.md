Police searched a residential house in Thüste on Friday for a suspected cannabis
plantation in a large-scale raid in the Hameln-Pyrmont district. [@1]
Investigators had followed an anonymous tip about a suspected indoor cannabis
plantation. [@1] The Hannover public prosecution office confirmed the operation
to NDR Niedersachsen on Saturday. [@1]

Several units deployed with a large contingent on Friday. [@1] Three riot-police
lorries as well as numerous vans, personnel carriers and patrol cars were
reported at the scene, with registration plates from Hannover, Braunschweig,
Hameln and Holzminden. [@1] The multi-storey house was searched from the cellar
to the attic. [@1] Forensic technicians supported the deployed officers. [@1]

Seized material was removed in so-called Big Bags, but no figures for the number
of officers or the extent of the seized material were given. [@1] The
prosecution said further background would be announced on Monday, and the
Deister- und Weserzeitung had first reported the raid. [@1]
