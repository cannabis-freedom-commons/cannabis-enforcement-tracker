Criminal investigation officers in the Oktyabrsky district of Tiraspol detained
a local man at about 20:00 on 19 April 2026. [@1] He was carrying a packet of
brown substance suspected to be drugs, and the 20-year-old detainee said it was
hashish. [@1] The Interior Ministry's own account says officers stopped the
young man during search operations and found the packet in his bag. [@2]

He said he had found the packet near the pedestrian bridge on Odesskaya Street
and, guessing what it contained, decided to keep the find for his own use. [@1]
The substance was sent for expert examination, which will decide the legal
assessment of his actions, the Interior Ministry press centre said. [@1]
Depending on the weight established, he could face criminal liability of up to
three years in prison, a fine of up to 6,300 Transnistrian rubles or
administrative arrest of up to 30 days. [@1]

The ministry said a check was under way and reminded residents that illegal
storage and use of drugs carry legal responsibility. [@2]
