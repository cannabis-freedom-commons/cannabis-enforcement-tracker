Jammu's Anti Narcotics Task Force arrested four suspected inter-state drug
smugglers in the outskirts of Jammu city on Wednesday, 1 April 2026. [@1] The
four were travelling in a cab that officers stopped on the Jammu-Srinagar
national highway near the Indian Institute of Management at Nagrota. [@1] Police
said the ANTF team acted on specific intelligence and searched the vehicle
following due legal procedures. [@1]

The search recovered a consignment of high-grade cannabis weighing about 10 kg,
and all the occupants were taken into custody. [@1] Officials put the value of
the seized cannabis at more than Rs 30 lakh. [@1] The arrested men were
identified as Amar Chand of Ramban district, Sachin Verma and Vijay Verma of
Gonda district in Uttar Pradesh, and Aakash Ramsuhel Verma of Surat in Gujarat.
[@1]

Preliminary investigation indicated the four belonged to a trafficking syndicate
with links beyond Jammu and Kashmir, officials said. [@1] The cannabis was
suspected to have been sourced from Ramban and to be bound for distribution in
Mumbai. [@1] Police registered a case under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic
Substances (NDPS) Act, India's main drug law, at a police station in Jammu. [@1]
Officers said further investigation was under way to identify and arrest other
members of the network, including its forward and backward linkages. [@1]
