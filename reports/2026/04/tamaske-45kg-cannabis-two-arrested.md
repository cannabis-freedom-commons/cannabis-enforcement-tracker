Police in Tamaské arrested two people whose cannabis seizure was presented at
the commune police station on 22 April 2026. [@1,3] The presentation ceremony was
attended by Keita department prefect Captain Hamsatou Nomao. [@2] The seizure
was carried out by officers of the Tamaské police station. [@2]

Officers seized 45 kilograms of cannabis in the case. [@2] Tamaské delegated
administrator Adjutant-Chief Hamza Hamma congratulated the National Police
officers of Tamaské, describing the result as hard work by those who watch over
residents day and night. [@2] He said the defence and security forces had struck
a severe blow against insecurity and trafficking networks threatening households
and the future of youth. [@2]

Prefect Nomao praised the professionalism of the defence and security forces
working to ensure free movement of people and property and to fight crime across
the department. [@2] She urged residents to support the forces by reporting the
movements of suspicious people in order to stop supplies reaching terrorists and
to put traffickers out of action. [@2] Tamaské canton chief Abdoulkadre Cheffou
Touba asked young people to abandon drug trafficking. [@2] He also called on
residents to continue cooperating with the forces. [@2]
