Drug-control officers of the Kaliningrad regional Interior Ministry identified
three residents of the regional capital who were growing cannabis and selling
marijuana. [@1] A 31-year-old man had set up a cannabis plantation in a dacha
house in a gardeners' partnership, known as an SNT, in Kaliningrad, where he
grew cannabis, processed it into drugs and passed the product to two
acquaintance couriers for sale. [@1] Officers later detained two 29-year-old
Kaliningrad residents who acted as couriers. [@1] The couriers received batches
of cannabis from the grower and sold them through hidden dead-drop stashes and
hand to hand. [@1]

An inspection of the dacha premises found 82 cannabis plants, 2.7 kilograms of
dried cannabis and specialised growing equipment. [@1] Officers found another
3.4 kilograms of marijuana at the grower's residence. [@1] A search at one
courier's residence seized more than 1.6 kilograms of marijuana and 30 vapes
whose contents proved to be 27 grams of hash oil in total. [@1] The second
account totalled the marijuana linked to the grower at more than 6 kilograms.
[@2]

City investigators opened criminal cases for attempted illegal sale of drugs and
illegal cultivation of cannabis. [@1] Police said they are now establishing the
channel that supplied the region with the laced e-cigarettes. [@1]
