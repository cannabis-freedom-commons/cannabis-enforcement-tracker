Bhopal's Crime Branch carried out a night operation that exposed a hashish
smuggling network. [@1] Officers arrested one accused in the MP Nagar Zone-1
area and recovered 126 grams of hashish from him. [@1] The arrested man, Mansoor
Khan of the Kotwali area, told interrogators he procured hashish from a woman
trafficker living in Shahjahanabad. [@1] Dainik Bhaskar's Hindi account likewise
says police arrested the accused in the MP Nagar Zone-1 area and found 126 grams
of charas on him. [@2]

Acting on that information, the Crime Branch raided a house in Shahjahanabad.
[@1] During the search, police recovered around 1.5 kg of hashish hidden inside
the dryer of a washing machine in the house. [@1] After the seizure, police
arrested the woman, Naushin Khan, with her husband Javed alias Chikna. [@1] Amar
Ujala identifies the first accused as 39-year-old Manjur Mohammad Khan alias
Junaid of Hawa Mahal, Imamigate. [@3] It says his search yielded about 125 grams
of charas, a mobile phone and an Activa scooter. [@3] It says a bag hidden in
the washing-machine dryer held 1.501 kg of charas, making 1.630 kg seized at
both places together. [@3]

Additional DCP Crime Branch Shailendra Singh Chauhan put the total value of the
seized hashish at about 7 lakh rupees. [@1] Naushin admitted selling charas from
the house, while Javed brought the supply from an outside supplier and already
had more than a dozen criminal cases registered against him. [@3] A case was
registered against all three accused under the NDPS Act. [@3]

Police were investigating the source of the drugs and others in the supply
network while questioning of all three accused continued. [@1]
