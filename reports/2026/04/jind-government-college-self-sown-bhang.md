## Eradication

Students and staff at Sant Shiromani Shri Sen Bhagat Government College in Jind uprooted and
destroyed naturally growing bhang plants on the campus on Tuesday 21 April 2026. [@1]

The action was conducted under a drug-free campaign following directions from Haryana's Higher
Education Department, with the college team cleaning the campus and promoting a safer educational
environment. [@1]

Amar Ujala published an institutional photograph showing students and staff holding uprooted
plants. [@1]
