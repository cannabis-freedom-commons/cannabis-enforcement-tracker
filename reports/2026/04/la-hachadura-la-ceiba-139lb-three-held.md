Soldiers of the Comando Sumpul of the Armed Forces intercepted three Salvadoran
nationals at the unauthorized La Ceiba crossing in La Hachadura, Ahuachapán Sur,
as they tried to enter the country. [@1] The three were travelling in a private
vehicle. [@1] The detainees were Edgar Efraín Gómez Guzmán and Steven Adonay
Ortega Rodezno, both 21, and a 16-year-old. [@1] The three were placed at the
disposal of the courts. [@1] Diario1 named the same two 21-year-olds and
reported that two of the detainees were identified while a minor was added to
them. [@2]

They carried three bundles holding 60 marijuana packages weighing 139.14 pounds,
about 63.11 kg. [@1] Field tests on the packages put the exact weight at 139.14
pounds. [@1] Authorities put the value of the load at about USD 204,740. [@1]

Defence Minister René Francis Merino Monroy confirmed the seizure and the
arrests. [@1] The interception took place during border patrols and controls
against drug trafficking and other transnational crimes. [@1] The La Ceiba area
in La Hachadura is described as a vulnerable point for illegal entry of people
and goods, where military deployments have been reinforced. [@1] The minister
said the government would maintain a zero-tolerance policy toward drug
trafficking. [@1]
