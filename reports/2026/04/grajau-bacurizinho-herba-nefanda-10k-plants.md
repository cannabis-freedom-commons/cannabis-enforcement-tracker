Maranhão security forces destroyed more than 10,000 cannabis plants in four
plots in the Aldeia Bananal area of the Bacurizinho indigenous land, in Grajaú,
on 19 April 2026, during Operation Herba Nefanda. [@1] One man, from Pernambuco,
was arrested in the act. [@1]

The operation was carried out by the Maranhão Civil Police with support from
other security forces, and four cannabis plots were eradicated. [@1] The Civil
Police estimated the loss to the criminal group at about 5 million reais. [@1]
Investigators from the Regional Police Station of Barra do Corda and the Grajaú
police station found a structured cultivation site with an irrigation system and
technology aimed at large-scale production, which they said indicated an
organised trafficking scheme. [@1]

Wooden huts covered with plastic tarpaulin, used as logistical support by the
suspects, were also destroyed at the site. [@1] The operation was supported by
the Military Police's 5th Battalion, the Military Fire Brigade's 7th Independent
Company, the Grajaú municipal guard and Air Tactical Centre teams based in
Balsas and Presidente Dutra. [@1] The raid was part of Operation Forças
Integradas, a statewide drive against organised crime, and the operation's name
Herba Nefanda is Latin for "cursed herb". [@1]

The suspect was taken to the police station, where the legal procedures were
carried out. [@1]

Police described the cultivation as professional rather than rudimentary, with
irrigation and large-scale production techniques. [@2]

The man was reported to remain available to the courts after the procedural
steps at the police station. [@2]

The cultivation was described as not rudimentary, with an advanced irrigation
system, large-scale production technology and structured logistics in the form
of wooden huts covered with plastic tarpaulin. [@3]
