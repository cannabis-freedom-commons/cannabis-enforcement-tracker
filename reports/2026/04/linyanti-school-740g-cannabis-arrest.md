Six pupils from a school at Linyanti in the Zambezi region, allegedly linked to
a cannabis syndicate at the school, were brought in for questioning at Katima
Mulilo Police Station on Friday. [@1] The school was identified as Linyanti
Combined School. [@2] A police report confirms that two 19-year-old pupils from
the school, along with a 22-year-old Zambian national, are being held in police
custody on charges of possession of cannabis. [@1] Those held include a Zambian
national believed to be the main supplier, and they were expected to make their
first court appearance in the Katima Mulilo Magistrate's Court. [@1]

The discovery began when a hostel supervisor conducting routine rounds at the
community hostel noticed suspicious behaviour involving a Grade 10 pupil and a
strong smell of cannabis. [@1] A search at the community hostel first led to the
discovery of 40g of marijuana. [@2] A joint operation then led to the discovery
of 700g of marijuana from the main supplier. [@2] Two Grade 10 pupils were found
to be in possession of drugs on behalf of the supplier. [@2]

Community police and the Chinchimane sub-police station were contacted along
with the school principal and responded promptly. [@2] An amount exceeding
N$5,000, along with five Zambian kwacha, believed to be proceeds from marijuana
sales, was also recovered. [@2] More potential clients are believed to be pupils
at the school, particularly from Grades 9 and 11. [@1]
