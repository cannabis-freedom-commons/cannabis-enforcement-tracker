Troops of the Fuerza Armada de El Salvador (FAES), El Salvador's Armed Forces,
detected an illegal marijuana plantation while patrolling the northern zone of
Izalco during a Thursday operation. [@1] The patrol formed part of the
military's anti-drug-trafficking deployments in the area. [@1] After the find,
Defence Minister Rene Francis Merino Monroy warned that there is no space for
drugs in El Salvador and that anyone who persists will face the law. [@1]

The plantation held 132 marijuana plants in Cantón Las Marías, in the
municipality of Izalco, department of Sonsonate. [@1] The plants were in a rural
area where difficult access and natural cover help hide illegal crops from the
authorities. [@1] Military personnel inspected multiple cannabis plants grown in
containers and makeshift bags on rocky ground in low visibility. [@1]

El Salvador prohibits sowing, cultivating or harvesting drug-producing plants
including marijuana without state authorisation under its drug law, the Ley
Reguladora de las Actividades Relativas a las Drogas. [@1] Those found
responsible for unauthorised sowing or cultivation face 5 to 15 years in prison.
[@1] The minister said the official strategy seeks to eradicate any
drug-trafficking activity at its roots. [@1]
