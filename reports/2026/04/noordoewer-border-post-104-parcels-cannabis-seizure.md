National police spokesperson deputy commissioner Kauna Shikwambi confirmed the
Noordoewer seizure in a weekend police report released on Sunday. [@1] A
Namibian man driving the vehicle disappeared after border officials detected
hidden objects during a scanner inspection. [@1] Vehicle records traced the car
to a Tanzanian national living in Namibia, who denied ownership of the car when
questioned by police. [@1] The suspect was still at large and police
investigations were continuing. [@2]

The interception happened at around 03h00 on Friday at the Noordoewer border
post as a white Mahindra with Windhoek registration entered Namibia from South
Africa. [@2] Officers recovered 104 parcels of cannabis described as skunk
concealed in the vehicle’s compartments. [@2] Police valued the cannabis at
N$5,450,000. [@2] Officers seized the drugs and impounded the vehicle. [@1]
Police in the //Kharas region opened a case of dealing in prohibited
dependence-producing drugs over the seizure. [@2]
