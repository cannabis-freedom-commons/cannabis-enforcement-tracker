Police on Aruba dismantled a large cannabis growing operation in raids on four addresses on Friday
10 April 2026. [@1,2] Officers from the Narcotics Unit searched properties in Seroe Blanco,
Bernadetastraat, Korvetstraat and Edisonstraat, in and around Oranjestad, and found several
cultivation sites. [@1,2] They seized 354 marijuana plants and a substantial quantity of processed
cannabis, as well as equipment used to grow and tend the plants. [@1,2] The police said it was a
well-organised setup. [@1]

Four men aged between 25 and 56, all born in Aruba, were arrested. [@1,2] The K-9 unit, the Flex
Team and the Rapid Response Team took part in the operation. [@1,2] The police said more arrests
could follow and that the raids were part of a wider effort against drug dealing in residential
neighbourhoods. [@1] They did not publish the weight or value of the processed cannabis, and the
reports did not say whether the four men had been charged. [@1,2] The force asked residents to keep
reporting suspicious situations through its tip line. [@1]
