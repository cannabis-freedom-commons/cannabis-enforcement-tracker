Police identified the alleged cultivator as 45-year-old farmer alias Rico, a
resident of Barangay Manlapay in Dalaguete, Cebu. [@1] He was already in police
custody on April 9 after Dalaguete police arrested him on April 7 for a separate
offence. [@1] Officers said charges under the Comprehensive Dangerous Drugs Act
of 2002 were being prepared against him over the Badian plantation. [@1]

The operation uprooted and destroyed at least 400 fully grown marijuana plants
in the mountainous area of Badian on the afternoon of April 8. [@1] Authorities
put the plants’ estimated Dangerous Drugs Board value at P80,000. [@1] Most of
the plants were burned at the site. [@1] Officers kept 10 stalks, marked them
and set them aside for chemical examination at the Philippine National Police
Crime Laboratory. [@1]

The plantation was found in Sitio Lawaan, Barangay Sohoton, at about 4:30 p.m.
[@1] Personnel of the 703rd Major Combat Operation first spotted it during
preventive patrol in a remote mountainous area. [@1] The discovery prompted a
joint eradication effort by the Badian and Dalaguete police, Regional
Intelligence Division 7 and the Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency. [@1]
Barangay officials witnessed the eradication to document it under standard
procedures. [@1] Police said further investigation continued into the extent of
the cultivation. [@1]
