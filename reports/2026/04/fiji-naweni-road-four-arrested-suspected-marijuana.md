Authorities carried out snap checks along Naweni Main Road on 13 April 2026 and
arrested four men. [@3] The checks were carried out by a joint team from the
Fiji Police Force and the Republic of Fiji Military Forces under Operation
Sasamaki. [@2]

The men were found in possession of sachets and small plastic bags containing
dried leaves believed to be marijuana. [@1] The seized substances were sent for
analysis to confirm their composition. [@1]

Police said the joint operation focuses on both white and green drugs and is
continuing around the country. [@1]
