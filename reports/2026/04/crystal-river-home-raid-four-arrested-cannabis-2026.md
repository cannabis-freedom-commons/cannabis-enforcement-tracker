Citrus County deputies raided a home near Lake Rousseau and said they found
fentanyl, cocaine, marijuana and drug paraphernalia, along with a single round
of .22-caliber ammunition. [@1] At least four people were arrested in the raid
last week on North Autumn Oak Point, east of Inglis and just south of Lake
Rousseau in the northern part of Citrus County. [@1] The Citrus County Sheriff's
Office Special Investigations Unit and Special Weapons and Tactics team executed
the search warrant at 10710 North Autumn Oak Point in Crystal River. [@2] Four
people were found inside the home, including Charlie McGowan, Debra Sue Ann
Mischler, Sean Fedirko and Ashley Geer. [@2]

All four occupants were arrested and charged. [@2] Detectives reported finding
12.5 grams of fentanyl in the home. [@2] Detectives reported finding 1.4 grams
of cocaine in the home. [@2] Detectives also reported finding 14.9 grams of
cannabis in the home. [@2]

Sean Fedirko was arrested and charged with possession of fentanyl, cannabis and
paraphernalia. [@2] Ashley Geer was charged with trafficking fentanyl and
possession of paraphernalia. [@2] Debra Sue Ann Mischler was charged with
possession of cocaine and paraphernalia. [@2] Charlie McGowan was also charged
in the case. [@2]
