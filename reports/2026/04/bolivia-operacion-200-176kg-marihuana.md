Bolivia's anti-drug force FELCN seized more than 176 kilos of marijuana in
larger operations. [@1] The results were part of the plan Operación 200: por
nuestras familias, Bolivia y nuestro futuro, which ran from 1 to 3 April in
Santa Cruz, Cochabamba, La Paz, Tarija and Beni. [@2]

The same operations also recovered about 100 kilos of coca paste, more than 21
kilos of cocaine hydrochloride and more than 980 litres of liquid drug, which
are described only to explain the mixed seizure and are not counted as cannabis.
[@1] In the Trópico de Cochabamba officers located four cocaine factories and
burned large quantities of chemical precursors, including 2,000 litres of
petrol, sulphuric acid, sodium carbonate and cement. [@1] Authorities detected
irregularities in the weighing and incineration of controlled substances
involving two FELCN police officers with the rank of sergeant, who were caught
altering drugs due to be incinerated in Cotoca, Santa Cruz, and were ordered to
serve 180 days of preventive detention. [@1]

Officials described the consolidated result as a confiscation of 300 kilos of
drugs in simultaneous operations. [@2]
