A joint team of local police and the STF intercepted a truck on the
Sultanpur-Lucknow highway near Ravania Purab village on Tuesday evening, acting
on a tip-off. [@1] A search of the truck found 1,520 kg of ganja packed in 50
sacks, with an estimated market value of around Rs 1.5 crore. [@1] The
consignment was being smuggled from Odisha to Lucknow. [@1] To avoid detection
while entering Lucknow with the heavy vehicle, the accused had hired four
labourers to shift the contraband into a smaller pickup van. [@1]

The two arrested have been identified as Nasir, a resident of Muzaffarnagar, and
Arya, from Lucknow. [@1] The accused were booked under the Narcotic Drugs and
Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act. [@1] Circle Officer Saurabh Sawant said
police are investigating the entire network involved in the drug racket. [@1]
