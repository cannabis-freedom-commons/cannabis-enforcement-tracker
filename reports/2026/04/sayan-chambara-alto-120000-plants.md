## Operation

Peru's National Police (PNP) burned more than 120,000 cannabis plants in three plots at Chambara Alto, in the district of Sayán, Huaura province. [@1,2] Police described the plots as covering about 3.5, 1.5 and 1 hectare, a combined area of roughly 6 hectares, and said the plants were 40 to 60 centimetres tall. [@1,2] The operation followed intelligence work and surveillance, according to Andina. [@1]

## Destruction and site

Police said they burned the crop in the presence of a representative of the Public Ministry. [@1] The reports describe an irrigation system across the plots and a rustic structure with agricultural supplies and equipment; Andina also reports a small reservoir made from soil-filled sacks and plastic. [@1,2] Andina says the site also had a well used to clean the plants after cultivation and before drying. [@1] Diario Correo says the authorities planned further work to identify those responsible. [@2] Neither report identifies a cultivator or says whether anyone was arrested. [@1,2]
