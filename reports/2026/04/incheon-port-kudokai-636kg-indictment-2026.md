South Korea's Government Joint Investigation Headquarters for Drug Crimes said
on 10 April 2026 that it had arrested and indicted a 53-year-old Korean resident
of Japan, a member of the Japanese yakuza group Kudokai, under the Narcotics
Control Act. [@1] He is accused of shipping about 636 kilograms of cannabis in a
container that left Laem Chabang port in Thailand in early March and reached
Incheon Port on 23 March. [@1] Investigators said he bought the cannabis from a
Thai drug-selling group and conspired with a Vietnamese organisation based in Ho
Chi Minh City to sell part of it in South Korea through its network and
re-export the rest to yakuza groups in Japan. [@2] The headquarters announced
the arrest and indictment after taking delivery of the container, which the
account said had been moved to a warehouse on the outskirts of Gyeonggi
Province, where officers detained him when he came to collect the cannabis. [@3]
MBN reported that he was arrested the day after the cannabis arrived at Incheon
Port, when he went to a warehouse in Gyeonggi Province to collect it. [@4]

The headquarters identified four Vietnamese organisation members who supplied
the cannabis with him, requested arrest warrants for them and said it would seek
Interpol red notices. [@1] The Chosun Daily reported that the 636 kilograms were
equivalent to what 1.27 million people could smoke at once and were valued at
about 95.4 billion won. [@1] The cannabis was compressed in vacuum-sealed
packaging and sent as a full container load, in boxes hidden among clothing and
shoes. [@3] He communicated with the Vietnamese members on secure messengers
using a Chinese-registered phone and paid for the cannabis in virtual currency.
[@1] The Korea Times reported that investigators found he also had plans to
smuggle narcotics from Colombia. [@5]

The investigation began in January with intelligence from the National
Intelligence Service, and the container was tracked through cooperation between
prosecutors, police, the coast guard and customs. [@1] He served six years in
prison in 2016 for keeping about 956 grams of methamphetamine imported from
China for re-export to Japan and for importing a pistol and 19 rounds from
Japan. [@1] The Seoul Economic Daily reported that he was released in 2022 and
that a kilogram of cannabis costs about 1 million won in Thailand against about
150 million won at retail in South Korea. [@2]
