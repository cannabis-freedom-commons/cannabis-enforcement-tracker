Israel Police arrested two Bedouin cousins, aged 16 and 20, from the Negev town
of Al-Azazma on suspicion of smuggling about 45 kilograms of hashish from Egypt
into Israel by drone. [@2] The two were arrested along the Egyptian border
during a targeted operation by the Southern Border Patrol Unit and its
undercover unit against gangs that use drones to smuggle goods across the
frontier. [@2]

Working from police and IDF intelligence, officers identified a drone suspected
of making several crossings between Israel and Egypt and later watched it land
in Israeli territory. [@2] Police then watched members of the group remove items
from a vehicle, which was seized and found to hold about 45 kilograms of
hashish. [@2] The two suspects fled on foot but were quickly caught and
arrested. [@2] A search of the vehicle found a drone, its remote controls and
mobile phones, and material recovered showed the seized drone had allegedly made
several flights over the border that day. [@2]

Both suspects were taken to Israel Police's Southern District police
headquarters for questioning. [@2] Besides importing a dangerous drug and
endangering human life, prosecutors are expected to charge them under a new
offence of violating the state's sovereignty at the borders, never before used
in a Southern District case and carrying up to nine years in prison. [@2] The
