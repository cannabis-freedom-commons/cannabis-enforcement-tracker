Operation Margate is an ongoing investigation which began in 2023 and targets
commercial cannabis cultivation by Vietnamese organised crime syndicates in the
ACT. [@1] To date six offenders have been arrested, with five pleading guilty
and the sixth failing to appear. [@1] One offender was extradited into the care
of Victoria Police for a Melbourne County Court warrant after ACT sentencing.
[@1] One offender was in detention awaiting deportation. [@1] The Kaleen man was
arrested at the scene and charged with cultivating a commercial quantity,
possessing cultivation equipment, theft and unauthorised use of electricity.
[@2]

The Kaleen search found a sophisticated hydroponic set-up cultivating 156
cannabis plants. [@2] Police found extensive indoor damage and wiring used to
bypass the electricity meter at Kaleen. [@2] The Bonner search seized more than
190 cannabis plants. [@3] A Vietnamese national believed involved in large-scale
cultivation fled before Bonner police arrived. [@3] No arrests had then been
made in the Bonner case. [@3]

Four rental grow houses caught fire in 2019 to 2023 from poor wiring, with no
fires since Operation Margate began. [@1]
