A joint Czech-Polish investigation team was formed while tracing drug and
anabolic-steroid sales between groups operating in the two countries. [@2] Two
men built the organised group after finding marijuana buyers in Poland. [@2]
Five other accused people in Czechia grew the cannabis the gang bought. [@2]
Police caught 24 people in Czechia and Poland in the Operation Kardio roundup.
[@1] The Brno prosecutor indicted 13 people in Czechia for drug crime and
anabolics dealing. [@2] Eleven other people were charged in Poland. [@2] Three
accused people were in custody at the time of the announcement. [@2] Eight
defendants in Czechia face up to 18 years in prison, while five marijuana
growers face up to five years. [@1]

Detectives blame the group for exporting and selling 35 kg of marijuana. [@1]
Officers seized 20 kg of marijuana, 130 cannabis plants and growing equipment,
along with 110 g of cocaine, 165 g of pervitin, 27 g of mefedron, additional
anabolics and an artillery-shell fuse. [@2] Officers also seized a flat worth
almost three million korunas, investment gold worth about one million, half a
million in cash, luxury watches and three cars. [@1] Polish police seized 167 kg
of klefedron, almost one kilogram of MDMA, a similar amount of cocaine, 230 g of
alpha-PVP and funds worth almost half a million korunas. [@2] Polish
investigators blame the group for importing 120 kg of marijuana, 20 kg of
amphetamine, 50 kg of metamfetamin and 5 kg of alpha-PVP. [@2]

The gang bought marijuana for 30,000 to 40,000 korunas per kilogram and sold it
in Poland and Hungary for roughly double. [@1] In Poland the gang with two other
men arranged pervitin, mefedron and anabolic and hormonal substances that it
imported and distributed in the Central Bohemian, Olomouc and South Moravian
regions. [@2] The group later expanded its portfolio to include cocaine sales.
[@1]
