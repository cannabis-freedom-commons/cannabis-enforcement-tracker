Four Cuban citizens were bound over for trial in Cancún, Quintana Roo, accused
of distributing marijuana through Facebook after the state prosecutor presented
evidence to a control judge. [@1] The accused, identified as Edilberto N,
Nicolás N, Pablo N and Hans N, were detained at Boulevard Puerto Cancún during
an operation arising from investigations by the Fiscalía Especializada en
Combate al Delito de Narcomenudeo. [@1] A judge ordered justified preventive
detention for the four during the criminal proceedings because of the
seriousness of the accusations. [@1]

Edilberto N and Nicolás N face charges for supplying cannabis sativa L., while
Pablo N and Hans N were processed for possession of the same substance for
supply purposes. [@1] Investigators said the four used Facebook to coordinate
the sale and distribution of the drug, contacting clients and arranging
deliveries through private messages to hinder detection. [@1] The prosecutor
said there are lines of investigation into the possible participation of the
accused in robberies and extortion through so-called cobro de piso, principally
in Cancún's Zona Hotelera. [@1]

A report by Informado Caribe asserted the detainees belonged to a mostly Cuban
criminal organisation operating in Cancún for several months and claimed more
than 17 people linked to it had been arrested in recent weeks, which the state
prosecutor has not officially confirmed. [@1] Under the presumption of
innocence, the four remain innocent unless a final sentence establishes criminal
responsibility. [@1]
