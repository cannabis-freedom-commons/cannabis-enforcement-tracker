In the evening of 23 January 2025 a police patrol on the A4 stopped a grey car
driving with only its sidelights on. [@1] A second driver mistaken the police
stop signals for himself and followed the patrol, then told officers he had seen
the grey car's driver throw a backpack out of the window. [@1] The officers
found a plastic bag resembling cannabis under the passenger seat and two more in
the driver's jacket pockets. [@1] A black backpack found on the motorway was
full of drugs, and police seized 18 plastic bags of marijuana and hashish
weighing more than 800 grams that evening. [@1]

He answered for his actions before Luxembourg's district court in early June.
[@1] The ninth criminal chamber acquitted him of smuggling the drugs from France
into Luxembourg, finding no proof that he had crossed the border with them. [@1]
The court did find proven that he had put narcotics into circulation and
transported them. [@1] The court sentenced him to two years in prison, entirely
suspended, and banned him from driving for 18 months, also suspended. [@1] He
was also ordered to pay procedural costs of a little over 2,000 euros and a fine
of 1,500 euros. [@1]
