Belgian customs intercepted several tonnes of hashish in a container that had
come from Morocco, at the Port of Antwerp, on Thursday 18 June 2026. [@1] The
Federal Public Service Finance (SPF Finances), the Belgian federal finance
service, announced the seizure on Wednesday 24 June 2026. [@1] The authorities
described the load as several tonnes of drugs but said the exact quantity was
not yet known. [@1]

The narcotics were discovered in a container at quai 188, a berth in the Port of
Antwerp. [@1] The container had come from Morocco and also carried calcium
sulphate. [@1]

The discovery was not made public until 24 June 2026, when SPF Finances
announced it. [@1] The drugs were seized and will be destroyed. [@1] An
investigation is under way to determine the origin of the hashish and the
intended recipient of the shipment. [@1]

Hashish seizures are relatively rare in the port of Antwerp, where the great
majority of drug discoveries concern cocaine. [@1]
