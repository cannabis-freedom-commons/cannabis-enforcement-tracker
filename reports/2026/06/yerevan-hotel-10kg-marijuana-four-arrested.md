Four suspects have been arrested in an ongoing investigation into drug smuggling
allegedly involving Iranian nationals. [@1] Armenia's State Revenue Committee
said it received information that an Iranian national had been plotting to
smuggle 10 kg of marijuana across the land border into Armenia. [@1] Further
investigation identified the suspects, who were already in Armenia. [@1,2] They
were arrested by the Investigative Committee after being taken into custody at a
hotel in Yerevan. [@1] A court remanded them in pre-trial detention on drug
smuggling charges. [@1,2]

The investigation later found the alleged smugglers had intended to sell 3 kg of
the smuggled marijuana in Armenia to other accomplices. [@1] The two buyers,
Armenian citizens, were also arrested. [@1,2] Confiscations were made during
searches. [@1,2]

The account attributes the smuggling allegation to a State Revenue Committee
press release. [@1] The report describes the investigation as ongoing. [@1,2]
