Angola's Criminal Investigation Service (SIC) deactivated a 15-hectare cannabis field in Malanje province and seized about 10 tonnes of liamba, as cannabis is known in Angola, on 16 June 2026. [@1,2] The operation took place in the Seca and Bamba neighbourhoods of the Xandel and Cambundi Catembo municipalities. [@2]

The SIC detained a 50-year-old Angolan man it described as the field's owner and the person responsible for growing the drug. [@1,2] Na Mira do Crime named him as Fernando João Ndala and described him as one of the largest cannabis growers in Malanje. [@2] The service said he would be presented to the Public Prosecutor's Office. [@1,2]

O País placed the operation in Cangandala rather than Xandel municipality. [@1] Both accounts say the field covered 15 hectares and that about ten tonnes of cannabis were seized. [@1,2]

The reports gave no value for the crop. [@1,2] No charge or court date was reported. [@1,2]
