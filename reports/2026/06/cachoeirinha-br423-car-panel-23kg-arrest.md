Federal Highway Police officers detained a man and a woman on the afternoon of
Sunday 21 June 2026 after catching them carrying cannabis in a car stopped on
the BR-423 in Cachoeirinha, in the Agreste region of Pernambuco. [@1] The two
were taken with the seized drug to the Civil Police station in Belo Jardim,
where further procedures were handled. [@1] The authorities did not publish the
names of the two people involved. [@2] The couple could face a drug-trafficking
charge carrying 5 to 15 years in prison plus a fine. [@1]

Officers seized 29 pressed blocks of cannabis weighing 23.7 kilograms in total.
[@1] The cannabis was hidden in a secret compartment behind the car's dashboard
panel, opened through an electronic device linked to the multimedia unit and
released by a button. [@1] Officers had stopped the car for a routine check and
grew suspicious after a strong smell of cannabis came from inside the vehicle.
[@1] Officers then found inconsistencies in the couple's statements, which
prompted a more detailed inspection of the car. [@1]

The stop took place at kilometre 37 of the BR-423 during an Operation São João
enforcement checkpoint. [@1] Teams from the PRF's Garanhuns unit and its
motorcycle group were carrying out the checkpoint when they approached the
vehicle. [@2]
