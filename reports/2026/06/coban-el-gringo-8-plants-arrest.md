Guatemala's National Civil Police captured 78-year-old United States citizen
Richard “N”, known as “El Gringo”, after searching a house in zone 8 of Cobán,
Alta Verapaz, where officers found a marijuana plantation. [@1] The detention
was registered on Tuesday 23 June, after a house search found eight marijuana
plants in harvestable stage that he grew in his patio. [@2] He was captured and
taken to a hospital under PNC custody after complaining of health problems. [@2]

Police said the eight plants were in fase de aprovechamiento, the harvestable
stage, and valued them at Q3 mil, about 385 United States dollars. [@1] Officers
also seized 22 bags and four jars containing marijuana, along with a scale and
grinders. [@1] The inspection also found artisanal cookies made with marijuana.
[@1] The cookies were allegedly made by alias “El Gringo”. [@3]

The detention was carried out during an allanamiento, a house search, where the
eight patio plants were located. [@3] Planners from DIPANDA, the National
Division against Criminal Development of Gangs, and the Guatemalan Red Cross
assisted in the operation. [@1]
