Four people were arrested, including the main accused, Vinod Sahni. [@1]
Officers stopped the pickup on the main road between Jhitkohia and Kauaha in
Basopatti police station area, in an area close to the international border with
Nepal. [@2] Dainik Jagran reported that the operation was carried out at about 4
am on Saturday, after the Special Task Force received advance information. [@2]
Dainik Jagran reported that Sahni was caught by the STF and police near the
Indo-Nepal border at Madhwapur while trying to flee. [@2]

Among the four arrested were the pickup's driver and its helper, the deputy
superintendent of police told Dainik Jagran. [@2] Three police personnel were
suspended over the case. [@1] The disciplinary action came after the
investigation allegedly showed that the driver of a Dial 112 emergency response
vehicle had colluded with the traffickers. [@1]

The pickup used to carry the ganja was seized. [@3] The local police's Dial 112
vehicle was used to escort the smugglers' pickup safely through the district
when the STF team, already lying in wait, stopped it too. [@2] Dainik Jagran
reported a market value of about Rs 5 lakh for the haul, while Aaj Tak put it at
about Rs 1.20 crore. [@2]
