Peru's National Police identified the detained man as 48-year-old Carlos
Cabrera, known as alias Pichichi. [@1] Officers of the Grupo Terna of the
Escuadrón Verde captured the suspected small-scale drug seller in the
urbanización Las Flores in San Juan de Lurigancho. [@2] Two officers dressed as
Clutch and Maple, the official mascots of the 2026 World Cup, to approach the
suspect without raising suspicion while he watched the tournament opening at
home. [@1] Officers broke open the house door with a metal battering ram when it
became necessary and entered with colleagues to complete the arrest. [@1] Police
said intelligence work showed Cabrera was a devoted football fan who would be
home watching the World Cup opening. [@1]

Inside the house police seized 209 grams of marijuana. [@1] Officers also found
wrappers with marijuana and cash whose amount was not specified. [@3] The same
search seized 2,524 ketes of coca paste alongside the marijuana. [@2]

Police said Cabrera had two prior prison terms for the same offence totalling
about nine years. [@1] The detainee and all seized evidence were placed at the
disposal of the Depincri of San Juan de Lurigancho for preliminary
investigation. [@3] In Peru microcommercialisation is punished with three to
seven years when a person is found with 5 to 50 grams of coca paste. [@1]
