On 2 June 2026 the Sri Lanka Coast Guard, working with the Police Special Task Force unit in Kataragama, conducted an operation in the Yala Jungle area and arrested one person with more than 50,000 locally cultivated cannabis saplings. [@1] The Coast Guard said officers burned the saplings where they were found and handed the suspect to Kataragama Police for investigation and legal proceedings. [@1]

The Sinhala-language version of the Coast Guard's notice says officers acted on special information and describes the locally grown saplings as prepared for planting. [@1]
