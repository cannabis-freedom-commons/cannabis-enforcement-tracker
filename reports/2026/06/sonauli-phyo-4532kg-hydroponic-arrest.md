The accused, identified as Phyo (30), was arrested after police and SSB
personnel recovered six packets of contraband from his trolley bag. [@1] The
accused, a resident of the Mandalay region in Myanmar, was intercepted by the
SSB during a targeted operation near the international border. [@1] SSB
officials said actionable intelligence was received on Thursday evening
indicating that a narcotics carrier would attempt to cross into India near
Border Pillar No. 517/2. [@1] Within hours, personnel identified a suspicious
individual crossing from the Nepal side. [@1] He was intercepted around 30
metres inside Indian territory. [@1] He has been handed over, along with the
seized contraband, to the Narcotics Control Bureau (NCB) unit in Gorakhpur for
further investigation. [@1]

Maharajganj Police and the Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB) have seized 4.532 kg of
high-grade hydroponic weed worth around Rs 4.5 crore from a Myanmar national at
the India-Nepal border in the Sonauli area of Maharajganj district. [@1] A
search of his trolley bag led to the recovery of six packets of hydroponic weed,
weighing 4.532 kg in total. [@1]

Sources in the police said that during preliminary questioning, the accused
allegedly revealed that the consignment was handed over to him in Thailand, from
where it was moved to Kathmandu before being smuggled into India via Nepal. [@1]
Investigators believe the consignment was headed for Delhi, a major consumption
and distribution centre for premium-grade cannabis. [@2] Officials suspect the
involvement of a well-organised international syndicate operating across
multiple countries. [@1] Agencies suspect the syndicate has links spanning
Thailand, Nepal, Myanmar and India, with distribution networks extending to
Delhi, Haryana and Goa, where demand for premium cannabis is high. [@1]
Officials said traffickers lure carriers with lucrative payments, reportedly
offering up to ₹1 lakh for transporting 500 grams to the national capital. [@1]
Financial trails, travel patterns and communication data are now being analysed.
[@1] Further investigation is underway, and more arrests are expected as
agencies tighten the noose around the cross-border drug network. [@1]
