Jordan Woolmer, 28, formerly of Hawthorn Road, Burton Latimer, near Kettering,
was sentenced at Northampton Crown Court on Friday 12 June 2026 to 10 years and
six months in prison for supplying cocaine and cannabis across Northamptonshire.
[@1] A jury at the same court had unanimously found him guilty of supplying
Class A and Class B drugs earlier in the year. [@3] The court gave him a
concurrent term of one year and 10 months for breaching a suspended sentence and
a further three months for driving while disqualified, and banned him from
driving for seven years and three months from his release. [@1]

His co-defendant Kacper Burzynski pleaded guilty to supplying a Class B drug and
possessing criminal property and was given a 10-month prison sentence suspended
for two years, the Daily Mirror reported. [@2]

Woolmer was first stopped in 2022 by Northamptonshire Police in Victoria
Gardens, Northampton, when his BMW was searched and officers seized 450 grams of
cannabis, 83 grams of cocaine and £7,000 in cash. [@1] A £12,500 Rolex watch was
seized after his arrest, and police said the watch was evidence of him living
off the proceeds of crime. [@1] He was forensically linked to £14,000 recovered
at a property in Leicester and to 500 grams of cocaine found at a storage
facility in Rutland. [@1] Officers arrested him again on 24 May 2024 after
seizing a further 500 grams of cocaine, designer clothing and £16,000 in cash
from two properties in Burton Latimer, the Daily Mirror reported. [@2]

Across the investigation police recovered £57,465 in cash, a kilogram of
cocaine, 500 grams of cannabis and clothing worth £34,680. [@1] Det Con James
Roe, who led the four-year inquiry, said Woolmer could not resist boasting about
his lifestyle on Instagram. [@1] The Daily Mirror reported that his posts showed
him with Rolex watches, in a Lamborghini and Bentley, and at a meal at Salt
Bae's Nusr-Et London steakhouse that cost more than £1,200 in cash. [@2]
