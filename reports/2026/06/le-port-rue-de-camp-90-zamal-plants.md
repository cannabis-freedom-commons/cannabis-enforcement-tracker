Police in Le Port arrested five people suspected of cannabis trafficking on
Friday 19 June 2026. [@1] Officers of the neighbourhood unit, with a reservist
and the anti-crime squad (BAC), were patrolling Le Port when a strong cannabis
smell led them to a house in rue de Camp. [@1] Inside they found two indoor
cannabis growing rooms, growing zamal, the local Réunion word for cannabis. [@1]

The operation dismantled two fully equipped growing rooms with all the material
needed to produce cannabis. [@2] Officers seized 90 zamal plants and 9 kg of
cannabis herb intended for sale, the figures reported by Réunion La 1ère. [@1]
Le Quotidien de La Réunion also reported 90 plants and several kilos of packaged
cannabis herb meant for the local market. [@2] Imaz Press put the seizure at 105
plants and about 30 kg of herb, attributing that account to the police or to its
own information. [@3] LINFO reported that officers found 105 zamal plants and 30
kg of cannabis herb intended for resale. [@4] Imaz Press said the operation
followed a tip-off and involved the dog unit, the GSP400 public-security and
proximity group, the judicial action group and the BAC. [@3]

The three suspects were held in police custody before being brought before
prosecutors for an immediate-appearance hearing (comparution immédiate, a
fast-track trial). [@2] Three people aged between 18 and 30 were presented to
judges in Saint-Denis on Monday 22 June for that fast-track hearing. [@1] The
police union Alliance Police Nationale 974 publicly praised the officers
involved. [@2] Stéphane Patché, departmental delegate of Alliance Police
Nationale, said such hidden indoor crops escape drone reconnaissance and appear
to be increasing on the island. [@1]
