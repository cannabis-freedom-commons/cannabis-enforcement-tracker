Police in Famagusta seized more than seven kilograms of narcotics on 20 June 2026 after searching
land belonging to a 49-year-old man in the Famagusta district. [@1,2] Officers found 6.37 kilograms
of cannabis resin concealed inside a plastic toolbox, along with one kilogram of kief, a fine powder
police described as the resin glands of cannabis flowers. [@1,2] Police said such a large quantity
of kief had never before been detected in Cyprus. [@1,2]

During the operation officers also arrested a 28-year-old man who was residing illegally in the
Republic. [@1,2] Both men were arrested and were expected to appear before the Famagusta district
court on Sunday. [@1,2] Investigations were continuing. [@1,2]
