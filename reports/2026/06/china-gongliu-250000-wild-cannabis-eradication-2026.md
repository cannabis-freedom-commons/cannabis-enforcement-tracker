On 18 June 2026, Gongliu County's anti-drug office and forestry and grassland
bureau led forest rangers and local residents in a wild cannabis search in
Aketubieke Town. [@1] They divided the area into sectors and checked mountain
forests, waste slopes, field edges, riverbeds, irrigation ditches and unused
courtyards. [@1]

The county-media report says crews cleared plants as they found them and did
not leave untreated areas. [@1] Zhang Yongliang, head of the bureau's public
forest office, said the operation removed more than 250,000 wild cannabis
plants. [@1]

Staff also gave drug-prevention information to forest rangers and local
farmers and herders, and asked residents to report illegal cultivation or
spreading wild stands. [@1] The report, published on 22 June, says patrols
would continue after the operation. [@1] It gives no area or weight for the
plants removed and names no suspect, arrest or charge. [@1]
