Soldiers intercepted a boat carrying weapons, explosives and controlled
substances in Sucumbíos. [@1] The detention took place in the La Papaya sector
of Putumayo canton, Sucumbíos province. [@1] The operation ended with six people
detained, three Ecuadorians and three Colombians. [@1] Ecuador's Defence
Ministry said the detainees allegedly had links to the organised armed group
Comandos de la Frontera. [@1]

Officers found weapons, a hand grenade, ammunition and magazines alongside about
twelve kilograms of suspected marijuana. [@1] They also seized a canoe-type boat
and an outboard motor. [@1] The Defence Ministry put the blow to criminal
structures operating on the northern border at 120,000 dollars. [@1]
