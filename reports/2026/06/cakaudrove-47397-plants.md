Fiji Police reported that four coordinated operations in Cakaudrove, Vanua Levu,
uprooted and destroyed 47,397 marijuana plants. [@1,2] The Fiji Times listed
13,007 plants in the first wave, 20,226 in the second, 1,332 in the third and
12,832 in the fourth. [@2]

The operations were reported to have produced 77 arrests: 23 at farms linked to
drug cultivation and 54 at roadblocks. [@1,2] The roadblock operations also
seized dried marijuana leaves, ammunition and cash; the four separate Labasa
arrests for methamphetamine offences are not included in this report's arrest
figure. [@1,2]

The reported focus was the Northern Division's Golden Triangle, including the
districts of Tunuloa, Natewa, Cakaudrove Vanua, Navatu and Naweni. [@1,2]
