The Birmingham Police Department arrested 62-year-old Kevin Flowers of
Birmingham after a narcotics investigation in the city's Ensley neighborhood.
[@1] Police charged Flowers with trafficking marijuana, trafficking cocaine and
unlawful possession of a controlled substance. [@1] Officers took him into
custody without incident on the day of the search. [@1] Authorities booked
Flowers into the Jefferson County Jail, where he was held on a $325,500 bond.
[@3]

The arrest stemmed from a search warrant executed on Tuesday, 16 June 2026, by
the department's Vice/Narcotics Unit. [@1] Officers carried out the search in
the 3100 block of Avenue F in Ensley, in Birmingham, Jefferson County, Alabama.
[@2] Investigators reported recovering 1,876 grams of marijuana, about 1.88
kilograms, during the operation. [@1] Along with the cannabis, officers
recovered 226 grams of cocaine and one firearm at the Avenue F property. [@2]

Police said the marijuana trafficking and cocaine trafficking counts followed
the narcotics investigation into drug activity in Ensley. [@2] Flowers faced the
trafficking and possession charges while held in custody, with the case at the
charge stage. [@2]
