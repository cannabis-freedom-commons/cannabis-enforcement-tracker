A resident in Shihezi, Xinjiang, reported a large patch of plants suspected to
be wild cannabis, and local police followed the tip to the area. [@1,2] Officers
identified the plants as a controlled drug plant and said they found no signs
of deliberate sowing or cultivation at the site. [@1,2]

Police swept the area and uprooted more than 10,000 plants one by one, clearing
the roots and remaining seedlings, according to the CCTV report. [@1,2] The
accompanying footage shows people pulling plants by hand beside farmland, with
a police vehicle nearby. [@1]

Beijing Daily published the CCTV Quick Look item on 25 June 2026; Sina carried
the same verified CCTV account post later that day. [@1,2] Both accounts call
the discovery and removal recent but give no separate date for the operation.
[@1,2] Neither names anyone responsible for the stand or reports an arrest.
[@1,2] The article names Shihezi but gives no more precise site, acreage or
weight for the removed plants. [@1,2]
