Helen Spree, 63, the chairwoman of the Independent Monitoring Board at HMP
Liverpool, was jailed at Liverpool Crown Court for smuggling cannabis and other
prohibited items into the prison. [@1] She had pleaded guilty at an earlier
hearing to misconduct in a public office, conspiracy to supply cannabis and
conspiracy to convey prohibited items into prison. [@1] Spree joined the board
as a volunteer in 2017 and was appointed its chair in January 2021, a role that
let her move anywhere in the prison unaccompanied with her own keys. [@2] The
court heard she had become besotted with the inmate Dylan Westall, 35, who was
serving a life sentence for manslaughter. [@2]

The offences ran over a 20-month period and ended with her arrest in August
2021, when police searching her home found two custom-made pillows bearing
Westall's face. [@3] Prosecutors said her deliveries were so frequent that she
called herself the prisoners' version of Deliveroo. [@1] She also passed
prisoners details of when cell searches would take place and of prison officer
deployments. [@1] Judge Neil Flewitt KC accepted she had been manipulated to
some extent but called the offences deliberate, planned and sophisticated, and
noted she gained no money from them. [@1]

Spree was jailed for five years and three months. [@2] Sentence was passed on
Spree and four co-defendants on Tuesday 2 June 2026. [@3] The other four
defendants were the inmates Dylan Westall and Thomas Porterfield, a third inmate
who cannot be named, and Westall's brother Michael Westall, 28. [@1] Dylan
Westall, 35, serving life with a minimum of 22 years for the manslaughter of a
17-year-old, got 12 months to run alongside that term. [@1] Porterfield got 22
months suspended for 18 months, Michael Westall got 20 months suspended for 18
months, and the unnamed inmate was jailed for two years and 10 months. [@1]
