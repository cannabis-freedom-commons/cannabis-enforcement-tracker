Coast guard and police officers stopped a truck in New Taipei's Rueifang district early on 3 June 2026 and found 1,309 packages holding 766.09 kg of dried cannabis flower, along with amphetamine and mixed drugs. The coast guard valued the haul at about NT$1.62 billion and said it was its largest cannabis seizure in ten years; seven people were arrested and prosecutors indicted them on 24 August. The announcement was made on 5 October. [@1,2]

At the award ceremony on 5 November 2026 the minister again described the case as the largest cannabis seizure in Taiwan in the past ten years. [@2] She put the coast guard's year-to-date drug seizures at 12,901.6 kg, a total not separated by substance. [@2] She said that during a maritime lockdown the service had broken two drug cases and seized more than 3 tonnes of drugs, alongside two illegal border-crossing cases and more than 20 wanted fugitives.
