Costa Rican authorities detained two Nicaraguan men after intercepting a
speedboat carrying apparent marijuana in the southern Pacific. [@1] Only the two
Nicaraguan suspects were aboard, and officers arrested them and placed them at
the disposal of the Public Ministry. [@1] The two were later identified by the
surnames Bustillo and Simón, with police records showing no criminal history in
Costa Rica. [@2] Both remained in judicial custody while precautionary measures
were pending. [@2]

The fast boat was located off Playa Matapalo in Quepos, Puntarenas. [@1]
Special-operations coast guards stopped the 36-foot boat about 120 nautical
miles off Matapalo and found it fitted with two outboard motors. [@2] Officers
found 70 bundles aboard, first described as holding about 20 packs each for
roughly 1,400 packs of apparent marijuana. [@1] The Drug Control Police then
carried out the formal count and processing of the seized evidence. [@2]

The seizure came in a joint operation by Costa Rica's Coast Guard with
authorities from the United States, Colombia and Panama. [@2] Coast Guard
director Juan Carlos Alvarado said a United States aircraft first spotted the
boat 92 miles offshore. [@1] Costa Rican crews then tracked it for more than
eight hours using its speed, distance and course. [@1] The boat, the detainees
and the load were taken to port for judicial proceedings and inspection of the
drugs. [@1] The two Nicaraguans were placed at the order of the Costa Rican
prosecution, which will decide their legal situation as investigators examine
the load's origin and destination. [@1]
