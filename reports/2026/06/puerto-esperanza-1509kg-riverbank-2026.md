Gendarmería officers of Escuadrón 10 Eldorado patrolled the area known as
Vera-Cue in Puerto Esperanza by the Paraná riverbank. [@1] Among the vegetation
officers found two bundles wrapped in yellow bags and packing tape resembling
drug-transport packaging. [@1] Officers inspected the packages and a
Criminalistics Narcotest field test was positive for marijuana weighing 15 kilos
90 grams. [@1] The Federal Court and Federal Prosecutor's Office in Iguazú
ordered the drug and items of interest seized. [@1] The Vera-Cue find was
reported by Primera Edición as part of federal operations against drug
trafficking and smuggling in different points of Misiones with more than 927
kilos of marijuana seized in total. [@2] The outlet reported that the Federal
Justice of Iguazú ordered the substance seized and inquiries continued to
establish its origin and destination. [@2]

That car was later found empty on Provincial Route 20 holding 7,750 packs of
foreign cigarettes without customs approval. [@1]
