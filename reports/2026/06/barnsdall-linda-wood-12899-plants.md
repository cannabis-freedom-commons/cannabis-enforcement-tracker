The Oklahoma Bureau of Narcotics and the Osage County Sheriff's Office served a
search warrant at Linda Wood LLC near State Highway 11 and County Road 2307 in
Barnsdall over an active investigation. [@1]

Authorities said they seized 12,899 marijuana plants and 840.8 pounds (about 381
kg) of processed marijuana during the search. [@1] One person was arrested and
five other people were taken into ICE custody, according to law enforcement.
[@1]

OBN public information officer Mark Woodward said the investigation involved
illegal marijuana cultivation at the location, black market production and
distribution, and cultivation without OBN registration. [@1] Osage County
Sheriff Bart Perrier said illegal grow operations outside Oklahoma's regulatory
system had no place in the county and credited cooperation between local, state
and federal agencies. [@1]

Authorities said they expected to make more arrests as the investigation
continued. [@1]
