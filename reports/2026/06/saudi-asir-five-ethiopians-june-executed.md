## The executions

Saudi authorities executed five Ethiopian migrants on 23 June 2026 for allegedly
"smuggling hashish", according to the official statement cited by Human Rights Watch.
[@1] The statement records the sentences as discretionary death penalties carried out in
the Asir region. [@3] Reporting on detainees at the Khamis Mushait facility confirmed
that five Ethiopians were put to death on that date. [@2]

## The qat account

In one of these cases, a smuggler had forced the man to carry qat, a mild stimulant plant
native to Ethiopia and Yemen, from Yemen into Saudi Arabia as a condition of facilitating
his journey. [@1] Cathinone, the stimulant in qat, is banned in Saudi Arabia but legally
permissible and culturally consumed in parts of Ethiopia and Yemen. [@1] The man did not
know that carrying qat into and within Saudi Arabia was illegal. [@1]

## A wider pattern

The five were among at least 17 Ethiopian nationals executed on drug charges in Saudi
Arabia since the start of 2026, with dozens more at imminent risk after trials that
detainees and rights groups say denied basic due process. [@1,2] Prisoners at Khamis
Mushait said they had been forced to sign documents in Arabic without interpreters or
adequate legal representation. [@2]
