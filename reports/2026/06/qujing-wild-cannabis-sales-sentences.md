Qujing Intermediate People's Court reported that Huang Mouyi and Huang Mouzhao found wild cannabis on vacant land in the city's economic development zone. They picked and dried the leaves, used some themselves and sold some to another person. [@1]

Testing found THC in material seized in the case. The court sentenced Huang Mouyi to one year in prison and a 6,000-yuan fine, and Huang Mouzhao to nine months and a 5,000-yuan fine for drug dealing. [@1]

The report does not give the quantity of cannabis or identify when the judgments were delivered. [@1]
