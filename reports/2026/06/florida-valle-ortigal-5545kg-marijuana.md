Police stopped a cargo truck on the road between Villa Rica and Palmira, at El
Ortigal in the municipality of Florida, and the driver ignored the order to stop
and fled. [@1] After a controlled pursuit the truck crashed on an alternate
route and the driver escaped into a sugarcane field. [@1] No one was arrested,
and police said they were investigating to identify and capture the driver and
those responsible for the load. [@1]

Inside the truck, officers found 226 packages of a substance similar to
marijuana with a gross weight of 5,545 kilograms. [@2] The National Police
called the load "creepy" marijuana and estimated its street value at about 4.365
billion pesos. [@1] Police said the load amounted to more than 5.5 million doses
for the illegal market. [@1]

The seized Sinotruk truck was valued at about 238 million pesos. [@2] Colonel
Jair Alonso Parra Archila, director of the police's traffic and transport
directorate, said the seizure kept more than five million doses off the streets
and strengthened the force's campaign against trafficking organisations. [@2]
Police linked the shipment, on intelligence information, to criminal networks
operating in the region, and the case was handed to the Valle del Cauca
prosecutor's office. [@2]
