Two people were arrested on 27 June 2026 under the anti-drug grand campaign of
the Koshi Province Police. [@1] A team from the Area Police Office, Kumarkhod,
arrested 26-year-old Vivek Rai of Chulachuli Rural Municipality-3, Ilam, who was
walking on foot from India into Nepal with 2 grams 820 milligrams of brown
sugar. [@1] A joint team from the Area Police Office, Lauwahi, and the District
Police Office, Sunsari, arrested 25-year-old Nishan Karki of Itahari
Sub-Metropolitan City-17, Kanchichok, who was walking on foot from India into
Nepal with 1 gram 290 milligrams of brown sugar. [@1]

A team from the Koshi Province Police Office in Biratnagar and a joint team from
Temporary Police Post Jorsukhawa recovered 120 kg of marijuana packed in black
plastic, in an unclaimed state, from the Siran Community Forest area of Dharan
Sub-Metropolitan City-20. [@1] Police put the combined recovery at 4 grams 110
milligrams of brown sugar and 120 kilograms of marijuana. [@1]
