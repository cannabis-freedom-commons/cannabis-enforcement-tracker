San Bernardino County sheriff's deputies served a search warrant on 19 June 2026
at a home in the 7000 block of Breno Place in Rancho Cucamonga. [@1] Deputies
assigned to the Rancho Cucamonga Sheriff's Station began the investigation on 15
June after receiving information about a possible marijuana cultivation
operation at the residence. [@1]

Inside the home investigators found 1,016 marijuana plants in various stages of
cultivation, the sheriff's department said. [@1] Deputies also found about seven
pounds (about 3.2 kilograms) of processed marijuana inside the home. [@1]
Members of the department's Marijuana Enforcement Team helped with the
investigation and evidence processing. [@1]

Deputies found what they described as an electrical bypass at the property and
contacted Southern California Edison. [@1] Code Enforcement officials red-tagged
the home, a notice that it cannot be occupied, after finding multiple
unauthorised modifications. [@1]

No arrests had been made and the investigation was continuing. [@1] Deputies
asked anyone with information to contact the Rancho Cucamonga Sheriff's Station.
