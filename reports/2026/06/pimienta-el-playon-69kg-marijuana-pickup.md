Honduran anti-drug officers seized 139 packages of marijuana weighing an
estimated 69.5 kilograms from a pick-up truck abandoned after a chase in the
village of El Playón, in Pimienta, Cortés department. [@2] No one was arrested:
the occupants of the Nissan pick-up fled when anti-drug units appeared, leaving
the vehicle and its sacks behind. [@2] The same vehicle carried 344 packages of
cocaine weighing about 172 kilograms; the cocaine is outside this tracker's
scope and its weight is not counted. [@2]

Police estimated the whole load, cocaine and marijuana together, at about 40
million lempiras on the illegal market. [@1] The operation began with
intelligence about a drug shipment moving through the Pimienta area and followed
the pick-up truck until its occupants tried to escape. [@2] The National
Anti-Drug Police Directorate (DNPA), Honduras's anti-narcotics police unit,
carried out the operation with the investigations directorate DPI and the
community security directorate DNPSC, through the UMEP-12 metropolitan
prevention unit. [@2]

Officers took the load to a police station, where the sacks were opened, counted
and field-tested for purity. [@3] Police said they were working with the Special
Prosecutor's Office against Organized Crime (FESCCO) in San Pedro Sula to
identify and arrest those responsible. [@3] The police communications director
said the suspected traffickers abandoned the vehicle and fled into the cane
fields, escaping the officers for the time being. [@1] One preliminary account
said the pick-up got stuck in the cane fields after heavy rain, and that a
single driver abandoned it. [@5] Diario El Mundo, reporting the earlier police
figure, put the marijuana at 60 kilograms, alongside 172 kilograms of cocaine.
[@4]
