Oman’s Directorate for Combating Drugs and Psychotropic Substances under the
South Al Batinah Police Command arrested two Asian nationals after monitoring
and field surveillance. [@1] The Royal Oman Police said the two were held for
possessing large quantities of marijuana and crystal meth. [@2] Legal procedures
against the two were being completed. [@1]

Police described the seizure as a large quantity of marijuana and crystal meth
held together. [@1] The arrests were carried out by the Directorate for
Combating Drugs and Psychotropic Substances under the South Al Batinah Police
Command. [@2] The two had deliberately dissolved part of the drugs to conceal
them and evade detection. [@2]

The operation was presented as part of the continuing efforts by Oman’s security
agencies against drug trafficking and the illicit psychotropic trade. [@1] The
drug-combating directorate said it works around the clock to monitor
intelligence and detain those involved in such crimes. [@1] Omani law provides
deterrent penalties for drug use and trafficking under the national anti-drug
strategy aimed at protecting young people and society. [@1]
