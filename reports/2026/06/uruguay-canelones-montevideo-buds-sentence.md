A court in Minas sentenced two men and a woman for producing and supplying cannabis buds to unregistered consumers from sites in Parque del Plata, Canelones, and La Unión, Montevideo, the Interior Ministry said. [@1]

The man identified as M.J.A.O., 43, who has previous convictions, was sentenced to three years in a rehabilitation centre for supplying prohibited drugs and for internal trafficking in arms and ammunition. [@1,2] M.L.A.C., 43, and A.F.M., 44, who have no previous convictions, were sentenced to two years of effective imprisonment for supplying prohibited drugs. [@1,2]

The three were detained in Minas and appeared before the first-turn court of Dr Beatriz Protesoni. [@1,2] The raids dismantled the production and supply circuit, with support from the General Directorate for the Repression of Illicit Drug Trafficking, the rural security brigade and forensic police from Montevideo and Lavalleja. [@1,2]

The case began when officers of the 8th police station in Mariscala stopped a man on 29 December 2025 and seized a parcel containing cannabis buds and a phone. [@1,2] The Lavalleja anti-drug brigade handled the investigation. [@1,2]

Neither source gives the amount of cannabis seized in the raids or the date they took place. [@1,2]
