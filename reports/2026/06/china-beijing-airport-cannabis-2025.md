Two foreign transit passengers were sentenced to prison after cannabis was found in four checked bags at Beijing Capital International Airport. [@1,2,3]

Customs inspected four checked bags when an international flight stopped at Beijing Capital International Airport in June 2025. Officers found more than 70 kg of suspected cannabis products, and rapid tests detected THC. [@2,3]

Investigators identified two foreign transit passengers who planned to carry the cannabis through Beijing to a third country for payment. [@2,3] A later weighing identified the substance as cannabis with a net weight of 70.8309 kg. [@2,3]

Beijing’s Fourth Branch of the People’s Procuratorate brought the case, and the court sentenced the two defendants to eight and four years in prison, fined them, and ordered their deportation. [@2,3] The reports said the defendants argued they were only transiting, while prosecutors said carrying drugs across China’s customs boundary completed the smuggling offense. [@2,3]
