The Saint-Denis correctional court tried the 43-year-old British man by
immediate appearance on Wednesday 10 June 2026 and sentenced him to two years in
prison with continued detention. [@2] Customs officers at Roland Garros Airport
found 16.5 kg of cannabis herb in his luggage when he arrived from Bangkok on 2
April 2026. [@2]

Customs put the resale value of the herb at 214,500 euros, the same sum the
prosecution claimed as a customs fine. [@2]

The prosecutor had sought four years in prison, telling the court that the
defendant knew perfectly well what he was carrying. [@1] The defendant said an
intermediary he had known since 2014, when both lived in China, recruited him to
carry computer servers worth up to 20,000 pounds each for 7,000 pounds. [@1]
Investigators found messages, including in encrypted groups, in which he asked
whether what he was carrying was drugs. [@1]

Zinfos974 described the case as part of a new drug route from Thailand to
Réunion, with four travellers from Bangkok or Chennai intercepted at Gillot in
2026 carrying about 17 kg each of Thai skunk. [@2]
