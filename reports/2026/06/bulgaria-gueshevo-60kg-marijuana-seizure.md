Border Police officers detained a 28-year-old Albanian truck driver outside the
Gueshevo border checkpoint after he attempted to flee once officers found the
drugs in his truck. [@1] The driver was then held for up to 72 hours on the
instructions of the Kyustendil District Prosecutor’s Office, whose prosecutors
are seeking a court order to keep him in custody. [@1] Pre-trial proceedings
have been opened and the investigation continues under that office’s
supervision. [@2]

On 6 June 2026, customs officers selected the Albanian-registered truck arriving
from North Macedonia for a detailed inspection using risk analysis. [@1] Inside
the tractor cab, in a specially built concealed compartment, officers found six
bags holding a green plant substance with a strong odor. [@1] A field test
reacted positively for marijuana. [@1] The seizure totalled 60.920 kilograms,
which customs valued at 498,366.42 euros. [@1]

Officials said the concealment method with drugs hidden in a modified structure
in the vehicle indicated deliberate smuggling. [@1] The Gyueshevo checkpoint is
on the border with North Macedonia, where the truck was entering Bulgaria when
stopped. [@2] Authorities said they are still working to establish the
shipment’s origin and intended destination. [@1]
