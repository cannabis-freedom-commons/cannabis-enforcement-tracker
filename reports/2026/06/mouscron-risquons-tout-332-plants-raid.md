Mouscron police detained a 25-year-old man in connection with the case. [@1] On
9 June 2026, officers uncovered a large cannabis grow inside a house in the
border Risquons-Tout district of Mouscron, close to Halluin and Tourcoing on the
edge of the French Lille metropolis. [@1] The operation was conducted as part of
an inquiry by the Mouscron police zone's Enquêtes et Recherches service, its
investigations and research unit. [@2] The Mouscron local police said the
intervention followed several pieces of information gathered and used by
investigators. [@1]

During the search, officers found an indoor installation holding 332 cannabis
plants, with growing equipment including lamps, ventilation systems and
filtration devices. [@2] Officers also seized a smartphone and 120 euros in
cash. [@2]

The prosecutor's office was notified and the file was placed under judicial
investigation, and the suspect was placed under an arrest warrant for
infringement of the narcotics law. [@2] The inquiry now continues under an
investigating judge and has been entrusted to the Mons-Tournai federal judicial
police. [@2]
