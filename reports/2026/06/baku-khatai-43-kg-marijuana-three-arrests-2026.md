Police in Baku's Khatai District seized over 11 kg of opium and marijuana from
47-year-old Hafiz Guseynov, who was suspected of drug courier work. [@1] Police
seized about 21 kg of marijuana from 32-year-old Sarkhad Eminov, who had a prior
criminal record, and 22 kg of marijuana from 39-year-old Rastam Madatov. [@1]

The Interior Ministry said more than 55 kg of narcotic substances were removed
from illegal circulation across the three arrests. [@1] The three suspects told
police they planned to deliver the drugs to various addresses in Baku for onward
distribution. [@1]

Criminal cases were opened and the three were remanded in custody by court
order. [@1] Since the start of 2026, Khatai District police had seized 103 kg of
narcotic substances in operations across Baku. [@1]
