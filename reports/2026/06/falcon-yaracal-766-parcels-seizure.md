Two men were detained by the National Guard early on Saturday 27 June. [@2] The
two men were identified as Gilberto José Martínez and Yorbis José Figueroa
Figueroa. [@1] The checkpoint was the Punto de Atención al Ciudadano (Citizen
Attention Point) in Yaracal, Manaure municipality, Falcón. [@3] Soldiers of the
2nd Company of Detachment 133 in Tucacas and URIA 13 Falcón carried out the
seizure. [@1]

Officers found 766 parcels of suspected marijuana in a false bottom in the
truck's refrigerated compartment. [@1] The National Anti-Drug Command report
gave the 766 marijuana parcels a weight of 406 kilos and 30 grams (406.03 kg).
[@3] The white JAC truck carried drinking-water bottles said to be intended as
aid for communities affected by the double earthquake of 24 June. [@1] The two
occupants were travelling from Coro in Falcón toward Caracas. [@3]

Officers also seized mobile phones from the two men. [@1] Authorities called the
use of humanitarian-aid logistics for criminal purposes a reprehensible act and
sent the case to the Public Prosecutor's Office. [@1]
