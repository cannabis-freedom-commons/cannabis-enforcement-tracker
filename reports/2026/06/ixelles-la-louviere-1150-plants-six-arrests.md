A major operation by the Brussels Capitale-Ixelles police zone led to the arrest
of six people in a drug-trafficking investigation. [@1] On 23 June 2026 officers
carried out seven searches in Brussels and the La Louviere area. [@1] Officers
arrested six suspects during the 23 June searches. [@1] Two of the six were
placed under arrest warrant while the other four were released under conditions.
[@1]

Investigators found two cannabis plantations, one in Ixelles and one in La
Louviere. [@1] In total, 1,150 cannabis plants were discovered across the two
sites. [@1] Officers also seized 30 grams of cocaine, 5,200 euros in cash and a
vehicle. [@1] La DH's account of the police statement confirms the same vehicle,
30 grams of cocaine and 5,200 euros in cash. [@3]

The case began in late 2025 when a vehicle fled a police check before being
intercepted. [@1] The two occupants were arrested and police found nearly four
kilos of cannabis in the car. [@1] After months of investigation, an
investigating judge issued seven search warrants. [@1] The judicial
investigation is continuing under the direction of the investigating judge. [@1]
The Brussels-Capitale/Ixelles police zone announced the results on Monday 6
July. [@2]
