Namibian police carried out anti-drug operations across the country during June
2026, recording both seizures of illicit drugs and arrests of suspects. [@1] The
operations ran between 1 and 30 June 2026, according to a media statement issued
by national police spokesperson Deputy Commissioner Kauna Shikwambi. [@1]
Officers arrested a total of 165 suspects over the course of those month-long
operations. [@1] Those arrested comprised 162 Namibian nationals, one Chinese
national and two Congolese nationals. [@1] Shikwambi said the seizures and
arrests underscore the Namibian police's commitment to combating the illegal
use, possession and distribution of drugs in the country. [@1]

Police recorded the seizure of more than 279 kg of illicit drugs alongside the
165 arrests. [@1] Officers confiscated 279.292 kg of cannabis during the 1 to 30
June period. [@1] The same operations also yielded 3,480.6 mandrax tablets and
440.6 units of crack cocaine. [@1] Police additionally confiscated 23 grammes of
methcathinone, described as khat, and 153 sildenafil tablets. [@1]
