The Sverdlovsky district court in Krasnoyarsk sentenced a local man to seven and a half years in a
strict-regime penal colony for growing cannabis and making drugs for sale. [@1] The verdict entered
into legal force on 3 June 2026. [@1]

From March 2024 to March 2025 he fitted out two rooms of a garage on his land in the Yemelyanovsky
district with lamps, automatic watering and extractor ventilation, and grew about 48 cannabis
bushes. [@1,2] Officers of the FSB border directorate for Novosibirsk Oblast searched the site in
March 2025 and seized 26 kg of marijuana, 600 g of cannabis oil, almost 2 kg of plant parts and 50 g
of hashish. [@1,2]

The man was held in custody during the investigation. [@1,2] The case was sent to the Sverdlovsky
district court, which convicted him of illegal cultivation and attempted production and sale of
drugs. [@1,2]
