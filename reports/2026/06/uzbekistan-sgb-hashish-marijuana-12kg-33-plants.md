Uzbekistan's State Security Service reported cases involving illegal drug
trafficking. [@1] At a traffic-police post, officers stopped a Cobalt travelling
from Karshi to Tashkent and checked the hand luggage of a 60-year-old passenger
from Bulakbashi district, Andijan region. [@1] The search found five bundles
holding 5 kg 32 g of hashish. [@1]

In a second case, a 52-year-old resident of Gijduvan district, Bukhara region,
was growing 33 cannabis bushes on his household plot. [@1] At the same address
officers seized 7 kg 79 g of marijuana. [@1]

In a third raid in Baghdad district, Fergana region, officers stopped a Lacetti
travelling from Andijan to Kokand and found 1,200 units of the potent drug
Tropicamide in bags belonging to two passengers from Kokand. [@1] An accomplice
following them in a Spark was detained at the same time. [@1] Two more people
were later detained, with 345 units of Tropicamide and 15 units of Regapen
seized as evidence. [@1] Investigators said those suspects were bringing the
potent drugs from Kyrgyzstan by bypass roads for further sale. [@1] Criminal
cases were opened over the detected facts and investigations are continuing.
