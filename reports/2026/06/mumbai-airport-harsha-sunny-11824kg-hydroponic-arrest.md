Harsha Sunny, a 28-year-old model, was arrested at Mumbai airport as she flew
from Bangkok to India carrying over 11 kg of high-quality hydroponic marijuana.
[@1] The arrest came on 11 June 2026 at Mumbai's Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj
International Airport, and she is a woman from Wayanad in Kerala. [@3] She was
first runner-up in the Mrs Kerala Global 2025 pageant and also works as a
relationship manager with a private bank. [@3] She also works as a relationship
manager with a private bank. [@3] Officers of the airport's Air Intelligence
Unit intercepted her at about 4 a.m. as Thai Airways flight TG-351 arrived from
Bangkok, acting on Advance Passenger Information profiling of her travel
pattern. [@3] A personal search in the presence of a gazetted officer found no
contraband on her person. [@3]

In a checked-in trolley bag, officers found 12 vacuum-sealed packets, five
black-and-transparent and seven transparent. [@3] The packets held green
flowering and fruiting tops of cannabis, which a field test with an NDPS kit
confirmed as ganja, or hydroponic weed. [@3] The haul weighed 11.824 kg, which
Customs valued at Rs 11.82 crore in the illicit market. [@2] Under the NDPS Act,
ganja above one kilogram counts as a commercial quantity. [@3]

She was formally arrested at 9 a.m. and booked under several provisions of the
NDPS Act. [@3] A court in Mumbai's Fort area remanded her to judicial custody,
with the Chief Judicial Magistrate's court at Esplanade setting 14 days. [@3]
Her advocate said she had travelled to Bangkok for tourism and modelling work,
befriended a man there who asked her to carry gifts for his family, and was
handed the bag at the airport with no chance to check its contents. [@3]
