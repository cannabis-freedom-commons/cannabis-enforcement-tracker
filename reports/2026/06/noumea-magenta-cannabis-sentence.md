Police dismantled a drug-dealing point in the towers sector of Magenta, in
Nouméa. [@1] The Territorial Directorate of the National Police ran the
operation that week against drug trafficking, using ground-security and
anti-crime brigade officers. [@1] Officers witnessed a drug transaction in
flagrante delicto. [@1] Police first detained the buyer, then arrested the
alleged cannabis seller. [@1] Police said the seller was already known to them
and active on a neighbourhood dealing point. [@1]

The man was brought before the court in immediate appearance proceedings for
drug trafficking. [@1] The court sentenced him to one year of firm imprisonment.
[@1] He was immediately incarcerated at Camp Est. [@1]

Narcotics-brigade investigations led to the seizure of cannabis. [@1] Officers
also seized a large sum of money considered to be proceeds of the trafficking.
[@1]
