A man from Le Creusot was convicted on a Friday by the court in Chalon-sur-Saône
for shipping cannabis resin to Martinique hidden in sacks of dog kibble. [@1]
The court was told the parcels had been handed to him in Paris by a sponsor who
commissioned the shipments to Martinique. [@1] He faced the court as the
identified sender of the parcels destined for Martinique. [@1]

Customs officers at the Fort-de-France sorting centre had flagged four parcels
arriving from Saône-et-Loire. [@1] The first interception, on 11 April 2023,
concerned three packages posted a few days earlier, on 4 April, from Autun and
Le Creusot. [@1] Those packages were presented as containing vitamins and dog
kibble. [@1] Each of the three packages also contained a little more than 500
grams of cannabis resin. [@1]

The resin was hidden inside sacks of dog kibble within the mailed parcels. [@1]
The parcels moved through the postal system from Saône-et-Loire to the sorting
centre in Fort-de-France for delivery in Martinique. [@1]
