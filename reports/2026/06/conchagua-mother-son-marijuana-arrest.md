El Salvador's National Civil Police arrested a 65-year-old woman and her
24-year-old son at their home in lotificación Santa Mónica, in Conchagua, La
Unión Sur department, for alleged possession and holding of drugs. [@2] Police
say the raid was carried out under a search warrant issued by a judge as part of
a drugs investigation. [@2] Police identified the two as María Ángela Membreño
Ventura, 65, and Erick Armando Ventura Membreño, 24. [@3] El Salvador Times
described the pair as accused of keeping drugs meant for sale in local
street-level dealing, known in El Salvador as narcomenudeo. [@2]

Inside the house officers found marijuana, the cannabis component of the case,
along with cocaine, an electronic scale, two mobile phones and cash. [@2] The
police report gave no weight for the marijuana and no amount for the cash. [@1]
The cocaine and marijuana are recorded separately; the accounts state no
combined drug weight or value that could be attributed to cannabis. [@3]

Both detainees were handed to the Fiscalía General de la República, El
Salvador's general prosecutor's office, on the charge of possession and holding
of drugs. [@2] Police said they found the pair through the work of their
investigation teams. [@3] The police said the arrests follow a prior
investigation that gathered enough evidence to seek judicial authorisation for
the search. [@1] Police quoted by Infobae warned that those who sell or hide
illegal goods will face justice. [@1]
