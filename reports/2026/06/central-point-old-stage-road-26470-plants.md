Detectives with the Illegal Marijuana Enforcement Team, a multi-agency task
force in southern Oregon, served a search warrant on Tuesday, 16 June 2026 at a
cannabis grow in the 1000 block of Old Stage Road in rural Central Point,
Jackson County. [@2] The team arrested one suspect in connection with the
property, 35-year-old Jorge Alexander Ruiz of Central Point. [@2] Ruiz was
charged with three felony counts of unlawful production, manufacture and
possession of a marijuana item. [@3] Ruiz was not listed in Jackson County Jail
records on the Friday after the raid. [@3]

Detectives seized approximately 25,000 marijuana plants hidden among pear trees
in the orchard and 16,000 pounds (about 7,257 kilograms) of usable cannabis.
[@2] They also seized 1,470 plants found in five hoop-style greenhouses on the
property, bringing the plant total to about 26,470. [@1]

The sheriff's office said the farm had no licence for any cannabis growing,
handling or processing. [@2] Investigators said the farm had applied for an
Oregon Department of Agriculture licence to grow hemp while hiding cannabis
starter plants in the pear orchard. [@1]

The Illegal Marijuana Enforcement Team was assisted by the Jackson County
Sheriff's Office, the Drug Enforcement Administration, the Basin Interagency
Narcotics Enforcement Team and Oregon State Police. [@1] The team, funded by a
grant from the Oregon Criminal Justice Commission, includes personnel from the
sheriff's office, Oregon State Police and the Jackson County District Attorney's
Office, and focuses on the black-market marijuana trade in the Rogue Valley.
[@2] Medford Alert reported that the investigation was continuing and that
additional charges had not been ruled out. [@4]
