Sri Lanka Customs officers arrested a 23-year-old Dominican national studying in
Toronto, Canada, at Bandaranaike International Airport after detecting 37.639
kilograms of hashish in his luggage. [@1] The suspect had arrived in Sri Lanka
from Dubai on Emirates flight EK648 at 11.15 p.m. on Friday 19 June 2026. [@1]

Customs officers found 146 parcels of hashish hidden inside two suitcases. [@1]
Customs put the street value of the seized hashish at Rs. 376.39 million. [@1]

The suspect and the hashish were handed to the Police Narcotics Bureau in Sri
Lanka, or PNB, for further investigation. [@1]
