State Security Service officers and interior ministry officers stopped and
searched a Lacetti car near the Kuyluk bazaar in Tashkent. [@1] The car was
parked, and the 30 bundles of drugs inside it were covered with bread. [@2]
Inside the car, officers found a plastic container holding 30 packages of banned
substances. [@1]

Examination established that the seized drugs weighed 34.61 kg in total. [@1] Of
that total, 12.96 kg was opium and 21.65 kg was hashish. [@1] Gazeta.uz reported
the same hashish weight but put the total load at 34.81 kg. [@2] The State
Security Service said drugs of that type are usually brought into the country
from abroad by smuggling. [@2]

The driver was a previously convicted man born in 1968, a resident of Sariosiyo
district in Surkhandarya region, and his passenger was a man born in 1984 who
was born in Denov and lives in Tashkent. [@1] The State Security Service's
investigation department opened a criminal case against both detainees over
large-scale drug smuggling and preparation to sell drugs on a large scale. [@1]
Both suspects were remanded in custody. [@1] Investigators said they were
continuing measures to identify and detain the other accomplices in the crime.
