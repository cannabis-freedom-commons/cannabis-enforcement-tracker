Dean Robert Gould was charged in connection with a cannabis seizure and remanded
to prison after the court raised concerns that he might be a flight risk. [@1]
He appeared before Chief Magistrate Ngaio Emanuel in the St John's Magistrates'
Court. [@1] He appeared before the court on Monday, when prosecutors opposed
bail. [@2] The court became concerned that Gould was a flight risk after he
failed to show sufficient ties to Antigua and Barbuda. [@1] Apparently, he only
has a United States passport. [@1] Gould, who claims to live in Cedar Grove, has
until 29 June to provide proof of address to the court. [@1] He was described in
court as a barber and identified in official reports as a pastor. [@2]

He faces charges of possession of cannabis, possession with intent, importation,
drug trafficking and being concerned in the supply of drugs. [@1] The charges
arose from a joint operation at V.C. Bird International Airport on 13 June
involving the Narcotics Department, the K-9 Unit and the Customs Enforcement
Unit. [@1] Authorities said officers searched two suitcases after a police dog
alerted them. [@1] Sixty vacuum-sealed packages containing cannabis and weighing
59 and a half pounds, about 27 kilograms, were reportedly found. [@1] Reports
are that Gould arrived in Antigua on a flight from New York's John F. Kennedy
International Airport. [@1]

Police said the search uncovered 60 packages of suspected cannabis concealed
beneath clothing and footwear. [@2] Antigua News Room estimated the street value
at EC$357,000 for about 59.5 pounds. [@2] The Observer put the estimated street
value of the seizure at $476,000. [@1]
