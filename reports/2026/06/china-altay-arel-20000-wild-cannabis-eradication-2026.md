On 14 June 2026, officers from Arel Border Police Station of the Altay Border
Management Detachment found green plants beside a roadside farm irrigation
channel while patrolling and visiting homes in their jurisdiction. [@1] The
report locates the event in Altay but names no village or county for the site.
[@1]

The plants were growing irregularly, with no sign of deliberate planting. [@1]
Officers compared them with a drug-plant identification chart and identified
them as wild cannabis. [@1] They cut the plants with spades or pulled them up
by the roots, then burned them; the report says more than 20,000 plants were
destroyed. [@1]

After the clearance, officers carried out drug-prevention outreach in the area
and explained the dangers of cannabis and the legal responsibility for illegal
planting of drug plants. [@1] The report gives no figure for the area cleared
or the weight of the plants and does not say whether anyone was suspected,
arrested or charged. [@1] Chinanews Xinjiang published its account on 16 June,
two days after the reported discovery. [@1]
