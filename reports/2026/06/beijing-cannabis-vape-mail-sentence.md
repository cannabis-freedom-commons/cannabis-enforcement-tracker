Wang, a man whose full name was not released, had family abroad mail him cannabis e-cigarettes
hidden in bottles of a calcium-magnesium-zinc supplement between 2025 and 2026. [@1,2] In January
2026 he was arrested on the spot as he collected a parcel declared as the supplement; officers
found 10 e-cigarettes in the parcel and six more at his home, together holding 6.92 grams of a
yellow paste containing THC, the cannabis compound. [@1,2]

On 26 June 2026, International Anti-Drug Day, Beijing No. 4 Intermediate People's Court sentenced
him to six months in prison and a 5,000-yuan fine for smuggling drugs, granting leniency for his
confession and guilty plea. [@1,2] The court said the 6.92 grams fell under "other small
quantities" of drugs, which carry up to three years, and warned that any parcel containing
cannabis is treated as drugs however it is disguised. [@1]
