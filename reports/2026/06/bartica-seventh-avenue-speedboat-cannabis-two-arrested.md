Two men are in police custody after suspected cannabis was found during a mobile
patrol at Seventh Avenue, Bartica, Essequibo River. [@1] The discovery was made
between 14:40 hrs and 15:00 hrs on Thursday, June 11, 2026, while officers were
conducting patrol duties in the area. [@1] Ranks contacted the occupants of a
wooden passenger speedboat docked along the shoreline at Seventh Avenue. [@1]

A search of the belongings of a 33-year-old fisherman of North Ruimveldt,
Georgetown, led to the discovery of leaves, seeds and stems suspected to be
cannabis concealed in a blue Lays chips container. [@1] A further search of a
35-year-old miner of Vreed-en-Hoop, West Coast Demerara, uncovered another
quantity of suspected cannabis. [@1] The substances were weighed in the
suspects’ presence and amounted to 46 grams and 24 grams respectively before
being lodged. [@1] Both men were arrested and escorted to the Bartica Police
Station along with the suspected narcotics. [@1] The suspects remain in custody
pending charges as investigations are ongoing. [@1]
