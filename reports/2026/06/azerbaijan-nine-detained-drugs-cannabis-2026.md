Officers of the Main Directorate for Combating Drugs of Azerbaijan's Interior
Ministry carried out a series of operations to identify and cut off trafficking
channels in Baku and several regions of the country. [@1] During those
operations, police detained nine people suspected of involvement in distributing
prohibited substances. [@1] Police named the detainees as Muzadil Mamedov,
Kyamran Mamedov, Khanym Gardashova, Rovshan Nasirov, Farid Nazarov, Orkhan
Abbasov, Eyub Mamedov, Abdulla Mamedov and Museib Hasanov, aged 26 to 43. [@1]

Police said searches of the detainees found and seized a total of 46 kilograms
of various narcotic substances. [@1] The ministry said the haul included
marijuana with harmful impurities, heroin, opium, hashish and cocaine, which it
described as among the most dangerous highly toxic drugs. [@1] Officers also
seized 2,087 tablets of psychotropic substances, including methadone and
ecstasy. [@1] They also found electronic scales used for packaging drugs and
55,270 manats which investigators said were proceeds from selling the banned
substances. [@1]

The ministry said the preliminary investigation found the detainees had
organised drug trafficking in Azerbaijan on the instructions of foreign drug
dealers. [@1] Criminal cases were opened over the findings and a court remanded
all nine detainees in custody. [@1] The Interior Ministry said work to establish
all the circumstances, identify possible accomplices and cut off supply channels
was continuing. [@1]
