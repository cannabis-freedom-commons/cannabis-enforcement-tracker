Law-enforcement officers from Kyrgyzstan, Uzbekistan and Tajikistan ran a joint
operation that dismantled a transnational channel supplying Afghan-origin drugs.
[@1] Kyrgyzstan's Drug Control Service conducted the special operation with
Uzbek and Tajik agencies and detained previously convicted foreign national
T.Kh.K., aged 62. [@2] The operation was initiated by Kyrgyzstan's Service for
Combating Illicit Drug Trafficking. [@1] Authorities said the detained foreign
citizen had repeatedly faced criminal prosecution before. [@1]

Officers seized 51 kilograms of Afghan-origin hashish from the suspect when he
was detained. [@1] A total of 51 kilograms of Afghan-origin hashish was seized
from the suspect. [@2] Investigators said the detainee organized a group
smuggling large consignments of Afghan narcotics through Kyrgyzstan for
distribution in Uzbekistan. [@1] The network transported drugs from Tajikistan
into Kyrgyzstan before smuggling them onward to Uzbekistan for further
distribution. [@2] Podrobno described the haul as half a hundredweight of
hashish taken from the suspected organizer. [@1]

Investigators are continuing to identify all participants, trafficking routes
and other circumstances of the case. [@1] The Interior Ministry said
investigative and operational measures are ongoing to identify all members,
establish routes and determine other circumstances. [@2]
