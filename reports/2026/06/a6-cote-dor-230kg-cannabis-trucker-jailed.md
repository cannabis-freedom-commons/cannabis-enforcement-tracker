On 19 June 2026 officers of the Dijon inland surveillance brigade stopped a road
combination with a Bulgarian-registered tractor and a Belgian-registered trailer
travelling south to north on the A6 motorway. [@1] The driver first denied
knowing drugs were in the vehicle, then admitted during the proceedings that he
had accepted the illicit transport to clear a large gambling debt. [@1] At his
immediate trial before the Dijon judicial court he said he had agreed to carry
the illicit goods without knowing the exact quantity. [@1]

Inspecting the load, officers found seven black sports bags holding a total of
230 kg of cannabis herb hidden in an empty space at the back of the load. [@1]
France 3 likewise describes a Bulgarian-Belgian truck intercepted on 19 June
carrying more than 230 kg of cannabis herb concealed in the trailer. [@2] The
court sentenced him to 15 months' imprisonment with continued detention, a
five-year ban from French territory and a one-million-euro customs fine, and
ordered the truck combination and its freight returned to their owners. [@1] ICI
Bourgogne identifies the driver as a man from eastern Europe tried at the Dijon
judicial court for the 19 June A6 transport. [@3]

Dijon-Actualités says the intervention took 230 kilos of cannabis herb off the
illicit market. [@4] French Customs presents the seizure as further evidence of
its commitment against drug trafficking, saying drug trafficking feeds criminal
networks and seriously harms security and public health. [@1]
