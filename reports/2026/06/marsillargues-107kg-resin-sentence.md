On 29 September 2025 at about 7pm, customs officers were checking vehicles at
the Gallargues motorway area on the A9 in the Gard. [@2] The driver refused to
comply and fled, and officers lost the car after a chase of about ten minutes.
[@2] An hour later the abandoned car was found at Marsillargues in the Hérault,
carrying 107 kg of cannabis resin. [@1]

The inquiry went to the Hérault interdepartmental judicial police drugs unit,
working with forensics, and vehicle and telephone analysis identified the
suspected driver. [@1] A 29-year-old man from Béziers was arrested at Perpignan
airport on 1 June 2026 by the Pyrénées-Orientales anti-narcotics office. [@2] In
custody he admitted taking part in transporting the drugs and said he had been
paid for the trip. [@3]

He was brought before the Montpellier prosecutor on 3 June and held in pre-trial
detention. [@2] The Montpellier court tried him on 5 June 2026 and sentenced him
to 18 months in prison, keeping him in detention under a committal warrant. [@2]
