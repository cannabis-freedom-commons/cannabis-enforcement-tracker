A US Customs and Border Protection P-3 Orion maritime surveillance aircraft
spotted a fast boat in the Caribbean Sea, setting off an operation. [@1] CBP AMO
said the aircraft was on a routine patrol in the area on 11 June when its crew
identified a three-engine Ecuadorian-style boat carrying fuel barrels and large
packages at high speed. [@1] Its position data went to Joint Interagency Task
Force South and the US Coast Guard, which sent a helicopter and Over-the-Horizon
patrol boats to intercept the boat. [@1] After an overflight the suspect boat
stopped, and Coast Guard teams took control of it. [@1]

The operation seized 3,960 pounds of marijuana, about 1,796 kilograms, and two
suspects of Ecuadorian nationality were detained, CBP AMO said. [@1] Cuenca's
Diario El Mercurio reported that the intervention began on 11 June, when a
Jacksonville P-3 on a routine patrol in the Caribbean detected a fast boat with
the features of the so-called Ecuadorian boats. [@3] El Mercurio reported that
US authorities announced the marijuana seizure and the detention of two suspects
of Ecuadorian nationality linked to transporting the drug. [@3] CBP AMO said on
Tuesday that the Jacksonville P-3 patrol had detected a fast boat carrying cargo
in the Caribbean Sea days earlier. [@2] El Universo reported that Coast Guard
teams including a helicopter and other boats answered the call and reached the
boat. [@2]

CiberCuba described three-engine Ecuadorian-style go-fasts as a widely
documented method of moving drugs on Western Hemisphere sea routes. [@1] CBP AMO
praised what it called excellent teamwork and coordination between its
Jacksonville air security operations centre, JIATF-South and the Coast Guard.
[@1] CiberCuba noted that the case was unusual because the load was marijuana
rather than the cocaine more commonly carried on these routes. [@1]
