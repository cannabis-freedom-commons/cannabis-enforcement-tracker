Police in Sucre reported the seizure of 932 kilograms of marijuana hidden in a
container on the night of 14 June 2026 in Sampués. [@1] The driver, Víctor
Manuel Gutiérrez Rodríguez, was detained in flagrante on the Sincelejo–Sampués
road in an operation by the Seccional de Tránsito y Transporte de Sucre. [@2] He
told officers the container was empty, but the smell of the load gave it away
once they opened the doors. [@3] A judge sent him to prison at the request of
the Fiscalía, the Attorney General's Office. [@3]

The marijuana was packed in 64 packages hidden in a container travelling the
Medellín–Cartagena route. [@3] The drug travelled in a sophisticated
double-walled compartment, known in Colombia as a caleta, built inside the
tractor-trailer and was bound for Cartagena and onward shipment abroad. [@2] El
Heraldo put the load's value at more than 7,000 million pesos. [@3] The police
announcement placed the operation on the Planeta Rica–Sincelejo road and dated
it 14 June 2026. [@4]

The vehicle was stopped at a control point on the route linking Antioquia and
Bolívar, and the driver was to be prosecuted for drug trafficking. [@1] Infobae
recorded that the truck driver was captured and would be processed for the crime
of drug trafficking. [@1] Defence Minister Pedro Arnulfo Sánchez called the
seizure a blow to the illicit economies and criminal networks that seek to
finance violence and organised crime. [@1] The National Police said it would
keep strengthening operational and investigative work on the country's strategic
road corridors. [@4]
