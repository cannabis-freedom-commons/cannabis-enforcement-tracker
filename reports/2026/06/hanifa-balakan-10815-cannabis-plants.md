Balakan District Police found wild-growing cannabis in Hanifa village during the Interior Ministry’s “Hash-hash-2026” operation. [@1,2]

APA reported that police destroyed 10,815 plants with a combined weight of 9,800 kg. [@1] Operativmm.az gave the same count and weight, and said officers uprooted and incinerated the plants. [@2]

APA attributed its account to the regional press service of the Ministry of Internal Affairs. [@1] Operativmm.az cited the Operative Information Center, which relayed the ministry’s report about the Balakan District Police operation. [@2]

Operativmm.az describes Hash-hash as an annual, nationwide initiative against illegal narcotics cultivation and distribution; this report covers the discovery and destruction in Hanifa. [@2] Its attached photograph shows cannabis plants burning in a field, consistent with the account of incineration. [@2]

APA gives the combined weight as nine tonnes and 800 kilograms, while Operativmm.az expresses it as 9.8 tonnes. [@1,2] Both reports describe the plants as wild-growing in Hanifa village territory rather than naming a managed plantation. [@1,2]
