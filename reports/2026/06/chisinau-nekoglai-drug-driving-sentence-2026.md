A Chișinău court found Moldovan TikToker and streamer Nikolai Lebedev, known
online as Nekoglai, guilty of driving without a licence and refusing a
compulsory blood sample, and fined him 100,000 lei. [@3] The case rested on a
plea agreement, and the court took into account that Lebedev had not been tried
before, had admitted guilt and had repented. [@2]

Moldovan traffic police stopped him in Chișinău because he had no documents with
him and they suspected he was under the influence of drugs. [@2] Officers said
his eyes were reddened and cloudy and that a strong smell of marijuana came from
the car, and Lebedev told them he had used marijuana. [@2] He was taken to the
Republican Narcological Dispensary, where doctors found cannabinoids at a
concentration of 205 ng/ml in his urine. [@2] He refused to give a blood sample,
citing a fear of syringes. [@2] He was charged in the summer of 2025 under
Moldova's offence of driving while intoxicated or drugged, which carries up to
five years in prison. [@2] Traffic police stopped the blogger in Chișinău in an
inadequate state at the wheel in June 2025, and he refused to give blood for
testing. [@1]

An examination showed cannabinoids in his blood, and the court fined him and
barred him from leaving Moldova until he paid. [@1]
