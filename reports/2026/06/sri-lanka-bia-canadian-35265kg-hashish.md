Sri Lanka Customs Narcotics Control Unit officers arrested an 18-year-old woman
with 36 kilograms of hashish at Bandaranaike International Airport. [@1] The
suspect is an 18-year-old Canadian nursing student at a university in Toronto
who was apprehended on Monday night. [@2] She had arrived in Sri Lanka from
Toronto via Dubai on Emirates flight EK 648 at about 10:40 p.m. on 23 June. [@1]

Officials estimated the seized stock to be worth over 352 million rupees. [@1]
She had tried to leave the arrival terminal through the Green Channel for
passengers with nothing to declare. [@2] She had used the airport's Meet and
Greet facility, under which her luggage was collected from baggage claim and she
was escorted through the terminal. [@2] Investigators said she had allegedly
agreed to carry the drugs for 12,500 Canadian dollars to support her studies and
travel expenses. [@2]

Preliminary investigations were being carried out by the Customs Narcotics
Control Unit. [@1] The suspect and the seized drugs were to be handed to the
Police Narcotics Bureau for further investigation and legal proceedings. [@1]
She was due to be produced before the Negombo Magistrate's Court on 24 June.
[@2]
