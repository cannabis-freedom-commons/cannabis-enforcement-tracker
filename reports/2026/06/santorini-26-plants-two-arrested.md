The operation took place on Tuesday 9 June 2026 in Santorini as part of efforts
against drug trafficking. [@2] Two men aged 33 and 41 were arrested and face
charges related to drug trafficking and illegal possession of weapons. [@1]
According to police, simultaneous searches were carried out at the homes of both
suspects. [@1] The two arrested men were taken before the Prosecutor’s Office in
Naxos for further proceedings. [@1]

During the search of the 33-year-old's home, police found 46.5 grams of raw
cannabis, eight packages of cocaine weighing 7 grams in total and two precision
electronic scales. [@1] Authorities also seized there a handgun, 11 rounds of
ammunition, 100 euros in cash and a mobile phone. [@1] At the home of the
41-year-old foreign national, police discovered a fully equipped hydroponic
cannabis cultivation facility. [@1] The setup included two makeshift structures
with shading systems, support beams, lamps and ventilation equipment for growing
cannabis. [@1] At the second home officers seized 26 cannabis plants in the
development stage. [@2] Officers also found 248.4 grams of raw cannabis there.
[@2] Officers also seized there an air gun, eight rounds of ammunition, a
precision scale, 300 euros in cash, a laptop and a mobile phone. [@1]

The investigation also found that on 15 April the 33-year-old was driving a car
with a 20-year-old foreign national as passenger when he fled a police check and
threw away a handgun. [@1] A case file had previously been opened over that
incident for illegal weapon possession, dangerous driving and disobedience. [@1]
