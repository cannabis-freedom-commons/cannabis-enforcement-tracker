Investigators detained a man known as “Canche Loco” during a search in aldea El
Manantial, Flores Costa Cuca, Quetzaltenango, on 16 June 2026. [@2] The detainee
was identified as Julio “N”, 38, also known by the alias “Canche Loco”. [@2] He
was accused of illegal carrying of a civilian or sporting firearm. [@1] Police
said he had two previous records for aggravated robbery. [@2]

The search was carried out by investigators of the División de Información
Policial (DIP) with prosecutors of the Ministerio Público (MP) as an operation
against narcomenudeo, street-level dealing. [@1] During the search investigators
found and seized 48 bags of marijuana. [@1] In the same search they also seized
41 bags of methamphetamine, 25 crack stones and a mobile phone. [@2]

Police said the seized items would be analysed as part of inquiries into
possible retail drug distribution in the sector. [@1] After the detention, DIP
investigators transferred the detained man from Flores Costa Cuca. [@1]
