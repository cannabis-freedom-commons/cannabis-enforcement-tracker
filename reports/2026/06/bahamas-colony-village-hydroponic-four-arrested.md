Bahamas police arrested four people after Drug Enforcement Unit officers
searched a residence on Sun Glow Drive in Colony Village, Nassau, on the night
of 24 June 2026. [@1] Officers acted on information received and executed the
search warrant shortly after 10:30 p.m. [@1] The two men in the home were aged
22 and 46, and the two women were aged 20 and 43. [@1]

The warrant was presented to the occupants, who appeared to have read and
understood it, before officers searched the house. [@1] Officers found suspected
hydroponic marijuana packaged and stored in a bedroom and the kitchen area. [@1]
All four people were arrested and cautioned over the find. [@1] Police said the
marijuana weighed about one pound, roughly 0.45 kilograms. [@1] Police estimated
its street value at $2,000. [@1]

Police said the investigation was continuing, and no charge or court date was
reported. [@1]
