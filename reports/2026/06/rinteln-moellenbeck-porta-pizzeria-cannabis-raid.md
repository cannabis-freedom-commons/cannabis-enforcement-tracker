Police searched a vacant house in Rinteln-Möllenbeck, in the Schaumburg district
of Lower Saxony, early on the morning of 25 June 2026 and found a cannabis
plantation. [@2] In the same operation officers searched a former pizzeria in
Barkhausen, part of Porta Westfalica in North Rhine-Westphalia, which the
Bückeburg public prosecutor's office said had been used as a cannabis store.
[@3]

A later Mindener Tageblatt report on the case repeats that more than 50
kilograms were found in the former pizzeria. [@1] They arrested a 41-year-old
man at the plantation; police said he was caught working on the plants and was
to be brought before a judge on suspicion of flight risk. [@2] Two other men,
aged 31 and 50, from Minden and Porta Westfalica are also suspects, and police
searched their homes for evidence. [@3]

Prosecutors and police accuse the three men of trading in cannabis in not small
quantities, the wording German law uses for a serious amount, and say they ran
the plantation in the empty single-family house. [@2] The searches were carried
out by officers from Rinteln police, the Lower Saxony state criminal police
office and the Minden-Lübbecke district police, and followed lengthy
investigations by the Bückeburg prosecutors and Rinteln police. [@2] The
Bückeburg prosecutors said the operation followed meticulous planning and close
coordination with the Minden-Lübbecke police for the searches in Porta
Westfalica and Minden. [@2]

Police said the investigations were continuing at the time of the raids. [@2]
