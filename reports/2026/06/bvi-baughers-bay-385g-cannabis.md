Royal Virgin Islands Police Force officers on patrol in Baugher's Bay, Tortola, saw Milford Todman
Jr, 35, get out of a red Kia Soul on Friday 19 June 2026 and detected a strong smell of cannabis from
the vehicle. [@1,2,3] A search of Todman found suspected cannabis, and a search of the car found a
backpack with more. [@1,2,3] Police put the total at about 385 grams. [@1,2,3]

Todman was arrested and taken to the Road Town police station, where he was charged with possession
of a controlled drug with intent to supply. [@1,2,3] He was granted $20,000 bail and was due to
appear in court on 30 July 2026. [@1,2,3] The reports did not give the outcome of that hearing.
[@1,2,3] The RVIPF said it continued to target drug offences and asked the public for information.
