Police in Iganga District arrested 28 young people on Saturday 27 June 2026 in an operation against suspected drug hotspots in Iganga Municipality and Nakigo Sub-county. [@1,2,3] Officers led by the officer in charge of Iganga Central Police Station searched Bikadho Zone and Bugumba "A" in the municipality and Bulubandi Central "B" in Nakigo Sub-county. [@1]

Busoga East regional police spokesperson ASP Michael Kasadha said the operation followed complaints from residents about insecurity linked to drug abuse. [@1] Pieces of suspected marijuana were recovered and exhibited, he said. [@1] Edge.ug reported that officers also recovered quantities of suspected cannabis during the raids. [@2]

The suspects were held as detectives completed investigations; the Daily Express reported that all 28 were at Iganga Central Police Station and that case files would go to the Resident State Attorney for legal advice. [@1] Uncovered UG said charges over possession and use of illegal narcotics were expected after screening. [@3] No weight for the marijuana was given. [@1,2,3]

Kasadha said earlier investigations had linked drug abuse in the district to mugging, house break-ins, robbery and motorcycle theft. [@1] The operation was part of a wider campaign in the Busoga sub-region, with similar crackdowns recently held in Jinja and Mayuge, the Daily Express reported. [@1]

Local resident Okia David told the Daily Express that arrests alone would not solve the problem and called for sensitisation in schools and communities. [@1]
