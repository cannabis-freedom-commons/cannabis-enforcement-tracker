More than thirty mobile gendarmes and gendarmes of the Martinique gendarmerie
carried out an anti-crime operation on Friday 5 June 2026 in the communes of
Ducos and Le Saint-Esprit, in southern Martinique. [@1] Officers deployed to
three sensitive sectors of the two communes for about three hours, searching for
drugs, weapons and ammunition. [@1]

At the end of the operation, gendarmes had written ten fixed fines, known in
France as amendes forfaitaires délictuelles and issued on the spot without a
prior investigation, for holding drugs. [@1] Officers seized 60 grams of
cannabis herb, along with one 9 mm cartridge and one 12-gauge cartridge. [@1]

The operation's commander, Captain Ludovic of the mobile gendarmerie squadron,
said officers controlled people at points across the island, searched them and
used a dog to find offenders or fine them for holding drugs. [@1] The
gendarmerie placed the operation in a context of armed violence, with 14
homicides in Martinique since the start of the year, 13 of them by firearms.
[@1] Colonel Bertrand Pallot, second-in-command of the Martinique gendarmerie,
said the force has 100 more gendarmes than when he arrived three years earlier,
covering patrol, deterrence and criminal investigations. [@1] The RCI page
reports the operation in a video by Mélissa Grutus. [@1]
