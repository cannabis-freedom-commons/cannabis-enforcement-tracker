Defence and security forces in Chad's Tibesti Province seized arms, cannabis,
adulterated alcohol and other prohibited goods. [@1] The seized goods were
presented to the press after a security meeting held in the province. [@1] The
security meeting was chaired by the Government Delegate-General for Tibesti
Province, Divisional General Alifa Weddeye. [@1] The presentation linked the
seizure to wider efforts by the security forces to guarantee peace in Tibesti.
[@1]

The Tibesti operation placed the cannabis seizure alongside arms and adulterated
alcohol as prohibited goods removed in the same action. [@1] Weddeye saluted the
efforts of the defence and security forces to preserve the peace and security of
local people. [@1] He urged the forces to maintain their vigilance. [@1] Local
people also expressed satisfaction with the results obtained in the fight
against insecurity. [@1] The displayed haul combined weapons with cannabis and
adulterated alcohol, making it a mixed security seizure rather than a
cannabis-only interception. [@1]
