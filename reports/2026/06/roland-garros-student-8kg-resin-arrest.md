Customs officers at Roland-Garros Airport in Saint-Denis arrested a Réunionese
student in his twenties when he landed on Saturday 27 June 2026, stopping him as
he came through the airport with his luggage. [@1] The arrested student is
enrolled at the University of Réunion and was previously unknown to the police
services. [@1] He had been spending holidays in the Paris region and was
travelling back to Réunion when customs officers intercepted him. [@1]

Officers searching his suitcases discovered more than eight kilograms of
cannabis resin hidden in his baggage. [@1] Photographs released by Réunion
Customs show the seized resin packed across several bags. [@1] The seizure was
presented as the latest case in the drug trafficking that continues to affect
Réunion. [@1]

After the airport interception, officers placed the student in garde à vue,
where he was held for questioning before the judicial follow-up. [@1] He was
then presented to the public prosecutor in Saint-Denis and tried on Wednesday 1
July 2026 under the comparution immédiate procedure for immediate trial. [@1]
