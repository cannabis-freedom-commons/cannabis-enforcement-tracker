Officers of the Bembèrèkè district police station carried out an important drug
seizure on Sunday 28 June 2026. [@1] The alleged traffickers behind the cache
remain actively sought by the security forces, with no arrest reported in the
account. [@1] The Bembèrèkè arrondissement commissariat, the district police
station, handled the seizure and kept the goods for the judicial procedure. [@1]

Six jute sacks filled with Indian hemp, the French term for cannabis, were
discovered in scrubland near the village of Pédarou. [@1] According to police
sources, the operation followed several days of surveillance of a trafficking
network operating in the commune. [@1] Officers searching the prepared cache at
about 9 p.m. recovered the six sacks, each with an estimated capacity of 100
kilograms, together about 600 kilograms. [@1] The sacks contained many pressed
slabs of drugs, described as numerous plaquettes found inside the six bags. [@1]

The seized goods were transported to the Bembèrèkè police station and placed
under seal for the needs of the judicial procedure. [@1]
