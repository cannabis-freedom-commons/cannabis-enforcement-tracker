FELCN officers seized a suitcase holding 15 packages of marijuana at the bus
terminal of Llallagua, Potosí, during a luggage-control operation. [@1] The
interdepartmental bus had dispatched the parcel on the night of 25 June from the
municipality of Uncía, with final destination the city of Cochabamba. [@1] The
find was made while the transport unit was boarding passengers at the Llallagua
terminal. [@1]

After identifying the suspicious suitcase, anti-drug agents opened it and field
tests confirmed it positive for marijuana. [@1] Despite the discovery, no one
had been apprehended, because the traffickers had provided false data about the
sender to evade the controls. [@1] The case was handed to the Public Ministry,
which will coordinate with FELCN to identify the luggage owner and establish the
drug's origin. [@1] This operation is the second case this year in the zone
under the same modality of sending drugs through parcels. [@1]
