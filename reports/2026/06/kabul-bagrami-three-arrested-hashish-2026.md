Counter-narcotics personnel arrested three people on charges of drug trafficking
and selling narcotics in separate intelligence-based operations in Kabul. [@1]
The Interior Ministry said in a statement on its X account that the operations
took place in the 11th security district of Kabul city and in Bagrami district.
[@1]

Officers seized more than three kilograms of hashish. [@1] The operations also
seized 1,382 capsules, a quantity of "tablet K", a locally named stimulant
tablet, and an amount of heroin. [@1] The statement said the security forces
destroyed a factory used to produce alcoholic drinks and confiscated 24 litres
of alcohol. [@1]

The detained people were under investigation and would be referred to judicial
and legal institutions after preliminary inquiries. [@1] The ministry's
Dari-language account of the same statement gives the same three arrests and the
same seizure of 1,382 capsules, tablet K, more than three kilograms of hashish
and heroin. [@2]
