Police in Martinique arrested a 41-year-old man on the night of 4 June 2026
after he refused to stop at a road check and fled on foot. [@2] The check took
place on avenue Maurice Bishop in Fort-de-France and was run by the night
command service of the national police's territorial directorate for Martinique
(DTPN 972). [@2] Officers immobilised his vehicle with an interception device,
but the driver abandoned it and ran off before the anti-crime brigade (BAC)
caught and arrested him. [@1]

The man was quickly caught and arrested, and the police found a category-B
handgun on him, a Sarsilmaz pistol with a magazine holding 11 9 mm cartridges.
[@2] Police also seized cannabis resin during the arrest. [@1]

The man was placed in police custody and then brought before the courts. [@1]
The investigation continued to establish the circumstances in which he came to
hold the weapon and the drugs. [@1] France-Antilles reported that the fight
against firearms trafficking is one of the Martinique authorities' priorities,
with 46 weapons seized in the police zone since 1 January 2026. [@2]
