Soldiers of the General De La Rey Regiment deployed under Joint Tactical
Headquarters KwaZulu-Natal intercepted a vehicle carrying dagga near Nthuthuko
Village in Pongola. [@1] During a night patrol along the border with eSwatini,
soldiers from Platoon 1's Bravo Section observed a speeding white Hyundai bakkie
approaching a standing stop-and-search checkpoint. [@1] The driver accelerated
and attempted to run over members of the South African National Defence Force
when ordered to stop. [@1]

Led by section leader Lance Corporal Machete, the patrol chased the vehicle and
temporarily incapacitated it to halt its flight. [@1] The driver abandoned the
bakkie and fled on foot into the village. [@1] A search of the vehicle uncovered
bales of dagga weighing 514 kilograms, which authorities valued at an estimated
R2,950,000. [@1] The Hyundai bakkie was impounded by authorities at an estimated
value of R450,000. [@1]

The cross-border patrol formed part of ongoing military operations to prevent
international crime along South Africa's border with eSwatini. [@1] The platoon
operated under the overall command of Lieutenant Colonel Moyo, who had assumed
command of the KwaZulu-Natal operational area on 19 June 2026. [@1]
