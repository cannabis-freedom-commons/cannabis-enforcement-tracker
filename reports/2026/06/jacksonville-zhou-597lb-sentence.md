Henglie Zhou, 31, a Chinese national, was sentenced to five years in federal
prison for possessing at least 100 kilograms of marijuana with intent to
distribute it. [@1,2] Prosecutors announced the sentence in Jacksonville on
30 June 2026. [@1] U.S. District Judge Jordan E. Pratt imposed the term after
Zhou pleaded guilty on 5 March 2026. [@1,2]

The case followed a Florida Highway Patrol traffic stop on 7 October 2024,
during an investigation by the Drug Enforcement Administration. [@1,2]
Zhou was the van's only occupant. [@1,2] After a police dog alerted,
officers found 22 cardboard boxes containing vacuum-sealed bags of marijuana.
[@1,2] Prosecutors put the total at approximately 597 pounds, or 271
kilograms. [@1,2]

Zhou initially denied knowing marijuana was inside the rented van and said
he did not know who had rented it. [@1,2] Investigators said rental records
showed he had rented it that day and had made 11 earlier cargo-van rentals
in Jacksonville between March and September 2024. [@1,2] Those rentals each
lasted one or two days. [@1,2]
