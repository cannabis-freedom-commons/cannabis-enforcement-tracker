## The interception

On a Tuesday in mid-June 2026, CBP officers at the Port of Buffalo's Peace Bridge crossing encountered a commercial truck with a shipment manifested as chocolate. [@1,2] A nonintrusive examination showed irregularities, so officers escorted the truck and trailer to an inspection area and searched it by hand, finding vacuum-sealed packages in cardboard boxes. [@1,2]

## The seizure

The inventory found 56 boxes containing more than 1,600 pounds (about 726 kg) of suspected marijuana, which field-tested positive. [@1,2] The agency estimated the street value at about $4 million and quoted Buffalo's acting port director Sharon Swiatek crediting inspection technology and officer expertise. [@1,2] The Peace Bridge connects Buffalo, New York, with Fort Erie, Ontario, and is one of the busiest commercial crossings on the northern border. [@1]
