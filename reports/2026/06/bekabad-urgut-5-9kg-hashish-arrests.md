State Security Service and customs officers detained a man born in 1992 in
Bekabad who had been wanted since 2025 on suspicion of smuggling and drug
trafficking. [@1] The Bekabad detainee was placed in pre-trial detention. [@1]
In Urgut district, State Security, Border Troops and internal-affairs officers
detained two people born in 2003 while they were attempting to sell drugs. [@1]
A criminal case was opened over the Urgut episode and both suspects were also
remanded in custody. [@1] Investigations into the episodes were continuing. [@1]

A search of the Bekabad man's rented flat found 151 grams of opium and 4
kilograms 971 grams of hashish hidden in places including a refrigerator and a
ventilation shaft. [@1] Investigators said the Bekabad drugs had been brought
illegally from a neighbouring state. [@1] The Urgut attempted sale involved 932
grams of hashish and 2.5 grams of opium offered for 10,000 US dollars. [@1]

A second outlet said the Bekabad detainee was 34 years old and had been wanted
since 2025. [@2] The same outlet said officers seized 151 grams of opium from
the kitchen refrigerator and 4 kilograms 971 grams of hashish from the toilet
shaft. [@2] It reported that the Urgut pair were caught selling 932 grams of
hashish and 2.5 grams of opium to an undercover buyer for $10,000. [@2]
