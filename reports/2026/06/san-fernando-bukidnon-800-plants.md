Joint police and military forces uprooted and destroyed about 800 fully grown marijuana plants on
8 June 2026 in Sitio Salumayag, Barangay Halapitan, San Fernando, Bukidnon. [@1,2] Central Mindanao
Newswatch said the plantation covered about 350 square metres. [@1]

The plants were destroyed at the site, and The Mindanao Sentinel said they were uprooted and burned
to prevent recovery and distribution. [@1,2] Central Mindanao Newswatch reported that 10 hills of
plants were retained as evidence and sent for laboratory examination. [@1]

The operation involved the San Fernando Municipal Police Station, PDEA, military personnel and
provincial and regional police units. [@1,2] The reports said the investigation was continuing to
identify those responsible for the plantation. [@1,2]
