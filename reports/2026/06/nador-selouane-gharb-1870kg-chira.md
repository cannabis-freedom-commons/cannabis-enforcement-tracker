Judicial police in Souk El Arbaa du Gharb and Nador ran two separate
anti-narcotics operations on the evening of Wednesday into the early hours
of Thursday June 10 and 11. [@1]

The first, in the rural Selouane zone outside Nador, immobilised a utility
vehicle with falsified plates carrying 40 bales of cannabis resin weighing
1,280 kg in total; the driver fled. [@1]

The second, coordinated with DGST services at the entrance to Souk El Arbaa
du Gharb, caught a 31-year-old man red-handed holding and trafficking 590 kg
of resin in a likewise fake-plated van. [@1] Checking his identity against
the national security database showed he was nationally wanted by the
Tangier judicial police over alleged financial offences. [@1]

The suspect was placed in custody under the competent prosecutor's inquiry
to clarify the cases and identify possible accomplices. [@1]
