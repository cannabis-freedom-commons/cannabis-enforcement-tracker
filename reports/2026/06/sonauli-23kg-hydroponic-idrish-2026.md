A man was arrested near the India-Nepal border in Maharajganj district with over
2.30 kg of high-grade hydroponic weed valued at around Rs 1 crore in the
international black market, officials said on Wednesday. [@1] Acting on a
tip-off, a team of Sashastra Seema Bal personnel arrested the man in the Sonauli
border area on Tuesday evening. [@1] The Sashastra Seema Bal, India's
border-guard force on the Nepal frontier, said the man was a 34-year-old from
Ramanathapuram district in Tamil Nadu. [@2] SSB Assistant Commandant Priya Yadav
said he had just entered India on foot when he was checked and the contraband
was found in his bag. [@1] Maharajganj police and SSB personnel stopped him
during a joint check near border pillar 517/2 at Sonauli, the main border
crossing. [@3]

He told officers he had flown from Thailand to Kathmandu and then travelled by
road to Belahiya before trying to walk into India through Sonauli. [@3] The man
was questioned and then handed over to the Narcotics Control Bureau, the PTI
account says, while the Hindi accounts say the local police charged him. [@1]

Officials said investigators were trying to trace other people in the suspected
cross-border drug trafficking network. [@2] The Sonauli border crossing has seen
several significant drug seizures in recent months, and agencies have tightened
monitoring there. [@2] Hydroponic weed costs far more than ordinary ganja, and
cases of it being trafficked internationally are growing. [@3]
