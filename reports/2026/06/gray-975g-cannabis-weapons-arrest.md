Strike Force Trident searched a residence in Gray on 11 June 2026 after youths
caught stealing cat food from a Bakewell supermarket said they swapped it for
cannabis from a woman there. [@1,2]

Officers seized 975 g of cannabis, schedule 4 tablets, counterfeit currency,
eight extendable batons, two Tasers, knives and samurai swords, about $35,000
in gold and silver coins and bullion, and about A$3,850 in cash. [@1,2]

A 45-year-old woman was charged that night with commercial-quantity supply and
possession of schedule 2 drugs, possessing schedule 4 substances, nine weapons
counts, receiving tainted property and possessing counterfeit money, and was
remanded to appear in court on 12 June. [@1,2]
