Customs and Excise Division officers intercepted a shipment containing marijuana
and a firearm during an intelligence-led operation at a Customs Bonded Area in
Port of Spain on 22 June 2026. [@1] The cargo was consigned to a retired Customs
and Excise Guard. [@2]

The detailed examination found 11 packages of a green plant-like substance
believed to be marijuana, which the Ministry of Finance estimated had a street
value of $1.4 million. [@1] Officers also found a separate package holding a
black metallic object resembling a firearm, concealed inside a washer and dryer.
[@1]

Officers attached to the Customs Canine Enforcement Unit and Preventive Branch
secured and seized the prohibited items. [@1] The Firearms Trafficking
Interdiction Unit and Special Investigations Unit of the Trinidad and Tobago
Police Service helped with the investigation. [@1] Officers arrested and
formally charged one person in connection with the seizure. [@1] The accused was
expected to appear before a Master of the High Court to answer charges under the
Customs Act Chapter 78:01. [@1]

The man was due in court on 23 June 2026 after Customs officers found the drugs
and the firearm during an intelligence-led operation at a bonded warehouse in
Port of Spain the previous day. [@3] The Ministry of Finance said the seizure
demonstrated the division's efforts to prevent the importation of prohibited
goods, illegal narcotics and items that threaten national security and public
safety. [@1]
