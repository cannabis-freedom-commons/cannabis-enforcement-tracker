In the first five months of 2026, January to May, Azerbaijani agencies took 4
tonnes 263.1 kg of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances out of illegal
circulation. [@1] The figures came from the working group of the State
Commission for Combating Drug Addiction and Illegal Drug Trafficking. [@1] The
working group valued the whole haul of every drug at 111.8 million manat. [@2]

Marijuana made up 3,664 kg of the haul and hashish 214.2 kg, alongside heroin,
opium, other narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances. [@1] The
Azerbaijani-language report gives the marijuana weight more precisely as 3
tonnes 664 kg 289.559 grams and the hashish weight as 214 kg 243.147 grams. [@3]
Officers also took 3,660 hemp plants, çətənə in Azerbaijani, out of circulation.
[@2]

Officers held 2,110 people criminally liable over illegal drug trafficking in
the five months, 2,057 men and 53 women, 25 of them foreign nationals. [@2] The
working group counted 3,766 drug crimes in the period: 890 for illegal sale,
2,841 for keeping drugs, 33 for cultivation and two other crimes. [@2]
