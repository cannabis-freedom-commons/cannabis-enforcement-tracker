Moldova's anti-drug directorate and organized-crime prosecutors reported
intercepting 4 kg of drugs sent from Spain and detaining two young men accused
of drug trafficking. [@2] The drugs were found in a scheduled bus carrying
parcels from Spain, and the two were detained on suspicion of involvement in the
scheme. [@1] A 21-year-old was detained at the scene when he came to collect the
parcel concealing drugs in food products. [@2]

Investigators say the shipment held 3 kg of marijuana and 1 kg of cocaine hidden
in food parcels, including pasta and panettone. [@2] The bus crossed the Leușeni
border checkpoint under anti-drug surveillance until the parcels reached their
pickup point. [@2] Police believe the 21-year-old acted as a courier who would
store the drugs for later sale through Telegram. [@2] An 18-year-old was
detained in the same case for allegedly accompanying the shipment from Spain and
watching for any risk of exposure. [@2]

Prosecutors charged both suspects with drug trafficking, and an investigating
judge ordered 30 days of preventive detention. [@2] The investigation continues
to identify others involved, and both suspects are presumed innocent under the
law. [@2]
