Officers of the Flying Squad Falcons unit of Cagliari's Squadra Mobile seized
over seven kilograms of drugs in a flat on Corso Vittorio Emanuele that,
according to the unit, may have stocked the city's many clubs. [@1,3] Hidden in
several bags in the flat, officers found about 4 kg of marijuana, 3 kg of
hashish and just under 40 g of cocaine. [@1] The flat was in a building between a laundry and a bar on Corso Vittorio Emanuele. [@3] The flat is located just a few
steps from Largo Carlo Felice and Piazza Yenne, in the heart of Cagliari's
nightlife district. [@1] Police had received a YouPol tip in the days before the raid, naming one of the three suspects as a dealer in the city centre, according to the investigators' reconstruction. [@3]

Three young people were arrested in Uta on charges of drug possession with
intent to sell. [@1] The arrested suspects were Simone Mellano (32), Marcello
Congiu (51) and Denise Serri (24), who allegedly kept the drugs in a flat in
Stampace. [@1]

Officers first stopped the woman at the building entrance, where she began to
scream and resist; they then stormed the flat. [@1,3]

The Italian version of the report names the same three suspects, Simone Mellano
(32), Marcello Congiu (51) and Denise Serri (24), who allegedly kept the drugs
in a flat in Stampace. [@2]
