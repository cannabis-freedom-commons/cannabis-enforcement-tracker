At about 10:53 a.m. on 17 June 2026, troopers from the Trooper G Community
Stabilization Unit stopped a vehicle on Interstate 87 Northway in Malta for a
vehicle and traffic law violation. [@1,2] The driver was Tyler Thompson, 41, of
Schenectady. [@1,2] With help from U.S. Border Patrol and State Police K-9 “J,”
troopers found about 300 pounds of cannabis in the vehicle. [@1,2]

Thompson was arrested for first-degree criminal possession of cannabis, a class D
felony, and additional traffic charges. [@1,2] He was processed at State Police
Saratoga and arraigned in Malta Town Court, where he was released on his own
recognizance. [@1,2]
