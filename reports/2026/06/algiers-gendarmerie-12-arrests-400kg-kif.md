Algiers gendarmes of the National Gendarmerie's drug and psychotropic substances
service dismantled a cross-border organised-crime network on Sunday 28 June
2026, Algeria's defence ministry said. [@2] Gendarmes arrested 12 drug
traffickers in the operation, the ministry said. [@2]

Officers seized four quintaux of treated kif, roughly 400 kg of a processed
cannabis product. [@2] The same operation also took 2,966 psychotropic tablets,
which are not part of the cannabis figures. [@2] Gendarmes seized ten tourist
vehicles, a motorcycle, phones and cash estimated at more than one billion
centimes. [@2]

Ennahar reported the same operation as at least twelve arrests, nearly four
quintaux of treated kif, 2,966 psychotropic tablets, vehicles, motorcycles, cash
and phones. [@1] Ennahar attributed the operation to the gendarmerie's anti-drug
service and said the defence ministry announced it in a communiqué. [@1] The
ministry said the operation added to others that had blocked attempts to bring
such substances into the country and showed the readiness of army units of all
branches against organised crime. [@2]
