Officers detained the driver of a fuel tanker at the Ollagüe border complex, an
adult Bolivian citizen with regular migration status in Chile. [@1] The driver
had no police record or outstanding warrants. [@1] The Calama prosecutor's
office charged the detained driver with drug trafficking. [@1] He was ordered
into pretrial detention as a danger to society, with a 100-day investigation
period. [@1]

The joint operation seized 458 kilos 60 grams of cannabis hidden inside a tanker
truck entering Chile through the Ollagüe border complex. [@1] Antofagasta
customs officers had flagged a Bolivian-registered fuel tanker that declared it
was travelling empty. [@1] Using a portable scanner, officers confirmed
anomalies in its contents and found bags with unknown merchandise inside the
tank. [@1] Detectives later established that 44 packages containing cannabis
were concealed in the vehicle's structure, bringing the seizure to more than 458
kilos. [@1]

The seizure was carried out jointly by the National Customs Service, Calama
migration and international police, the Border Task Force and the PDI
anti-narcotics brigade, under the direction of the Calama prosecutor's office.
[@1] After the initial customs check the case was reported to the Public
Prosecutor's Office, which sent Calama anti-narcotics detectives to take over
the specialised procedure. [@1] The Antofagasta customs director said its
inspectors identified the load before it entered national territory, triggering
the prosecutor-led investigation carried out by the investigative police. [@1]
