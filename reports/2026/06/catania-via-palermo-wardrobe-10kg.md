During a patrol in via Palermo, Catania, dog-unit officers saw a young man throw objects from
his balcony at the sight of the police. [@1] The police dog Ares sniffed out hashish pellets the
21-year-old had tossed in an attempt to get rid of them, and officers recovered and seized the
pellets. [@1]

Back-up was called and a search of the young man's bedroom found thirteen large bags of marijuana
weighing more than 9 kilograms inside the wardrobe, plus a precision scale and small bags for
packing doses. [@1] The seized drugs were to go to Scientific Police laboratories for analysis by
specialist staff. [@1]

The 21-year-old Catania man was arrested for possession of narcotics with intent to deal; after
the duty prosecutor was informed he was held in the Questura's security cells awaiting a
fast-track hearing. [@1] The courts validated the arrest and ordered him to report regularly to
the judicial police. [@1]
