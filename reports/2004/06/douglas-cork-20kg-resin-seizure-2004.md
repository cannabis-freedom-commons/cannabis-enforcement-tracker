A young man in his early twenties was arrested after gardaí searched a house in
Grangewood Court in Douglas, County Cork, on the evening of 24 June
2004. [@1] Officers seized 20 kilos of cannabis resin with an estimated street
value of €200,000. [@1]

The man was being questioned by gardaí and could be held for up to seven days
under the Drug Trafficking Act. [@1]
