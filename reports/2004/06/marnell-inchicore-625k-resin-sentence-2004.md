Aodan Marnell, 34, of Leopardstown Avenue in Stillorgan was jailed for eight
years, with the last four suspended, by Judge Desmond Hogan at Dublin Circuit
Criminal Court. [@1] He had pleaded guilty to possession of cannabis resin worth
€625,000 for sale or supply. [@1]

Marnell was arrested at a house he was renting on Woodfield Avenue in Inchicore
on 9 September 2001. [@1] Officers found the resin, then valued at IR£490,000,
together with IR£59,000 in cash and drugs paraphernalia including weighing
scales. [@1]

## Holding it for another man

Detective Garda Jim McDermott told the court that gardaí were satisfied Marnell
was holding the drugs and cash for another individual and that they did not
belong to him. [@1] Marnell admitted he was being paid IR£50 for every kilogram
he was holding. [@1] He had no previous convictions, had co-operated with the
investigation and had made a voluntary statement to gardaí. [@1]

The man said to be behind the consignment was arrested shortly after Marnell and
was later jailed for eight years over two other drugs seizures in Dublin and
Donegal. [@1] The case against Marnell had been delayed for almost three years
through no fault of his, causing him great anxiety. [@1]
