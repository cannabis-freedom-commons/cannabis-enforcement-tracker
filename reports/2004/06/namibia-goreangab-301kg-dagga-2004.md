Officers from the Drug Law Enforcement Unit and the Serious Crime Unit
discovered 301 kilograms of dagga in twelve hessian bags that nearly filled a
house in Windhoek's Goreangab residential area on Sunday 27 June 2004,
following ongoing police operations. [@1] The unit commander, Detective
Inspector Barry de Klerk, called it the biggest single quantity of dagga
seized by Namibian police since independence, valued at about N$903,000. [@1]

A 58-year-old Namibian man and his 43-year-old girlfriend, together with a
36-year-old South African woman, were arrested at the scene and were due in
court that week on a charge of dealing in cannabis. [@1]
