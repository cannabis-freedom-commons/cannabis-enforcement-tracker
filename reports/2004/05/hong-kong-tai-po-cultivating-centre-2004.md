Customs Drug Investigation Bureau officers intercepted and searched a man in
Tai Po about 12.40pm on 4 May 2004 and found about half a gram of suspected
cannabis in his possession. [@1,2] They then searched his residence at Kam
Shan Village, where they uncovered what Customs described as the first
cannabis cultivating centre ever detected in Hong Kong. [@1,2]

Officers seized 38 cannabis plants along with about 70 grams of cannabis and
1,000 cannabis seeds, a haul Customs valued at about HK$400,000. [@1] Two men
and a woman, aged between 29 and 39, were arrested in the operation. [@1,2]

A Customs spokesman said the cannabis was believed to have been planted for
self-consumption as well as for sale. [@1] Planting cannabis carries a maximum
penalty of a HK$100,000 fine and 15 years in prison under the Dangerous Drugs
Ordinance. [@1]
