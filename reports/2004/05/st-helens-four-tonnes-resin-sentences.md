On 23 May 2003 an articulated lorry driven by Konstantinos Sevastos arrived at Harwich by ferry from the Netherlands, ostensibly carrying flowers, and customs officers monitored its movements. [@2] At St Helens the lorry was met by Brendan Robertson with a white box van, Brendan Clarke with a similar red-cab van and Stephane Hassiakos in a Ford Focus, and Steven Rooney directed the transfer of boxed resin from the lorry into the two vans at farm premises. [@2] The vans together held four metric tonnes of cannabis resin, with a retail value of £3.6 million and a street value of about £9 million; phone records showed Rooney and Hassiakos in contact on 22 and 23 May and making calls to Spain. [@2]

Police and Customs seized the load the same day, and six men appeared in court the following week on a £10.5 million haul, with one van then reported at 2.7 tonnes and the other at 800 kg; all were remanded in custody. [@1] Rooney pleaded guilty on 22 March 2004 to being knowingly concerned in evading the import ban on cannabis resin, and each co-accused pleaded guilty on re-arraignment. [@2]

On 7 May 2004 Rooney and Hassiakos were each sentenced to nine years' imprisonment, with lesser sentences for the other co-accused. [@2]
