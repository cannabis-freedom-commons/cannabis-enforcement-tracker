On 8 October 2004, Washington County Drug Task Force agents seized the
largest cannabis grow site in southern Utah's history near the Pine Valley
district of the Dixie National Forest. [@1] Three Mexican nationals were
arrested at the site and a fourth was arrested the next day near St. George.
[@1] A hiker had found the cultivation site along a stream in a secluded
area and reported it to officers. [@2]

The site held more than 1,500 cannabis plants. [@1,2] Agents subsequently
seized 814 plants growing among scrub oak trees, 764 plants in the drying
stage, and 50 pounds (about 22.7 kg) of processed marijuana. [@1,2] The
growers watered the site with a gravity-flow irrigation system running
through plastic tubing from a nearby stream. [@1,2] Investigators believed
other accomplices had left the area for Southern California or Mexico.
[@1,2]

A 2005 Forest Service account said a citizen reported a large grow the
previous year on the Pine Valley ranger district just north of St. George,
and officers confiscated 1,700 plants and charged four people with
cultivating them. [@3]
