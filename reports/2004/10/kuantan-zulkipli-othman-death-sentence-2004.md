Zulkipli Othman, 30, of Terengganu was sentenced to death by the Malaysian High Court after being found guilty of trafficking in 237.4 grams of cannabis on 16 May 2001. [@1]

The Kuantan High Court judgment for the same case records that Zulkipli was tried jointly with two others for possession of 13.7 grams of cannabis and with one co-accused for trafficking the 237.4 grams at house P 459 U, Sultan Haji Ahmad Shah Polytechnic quarters, Semambu, Kuantan; both co-accused were acquitted at the end of the prosecution case while Zulkipli was convicted on both charges and handed the mandatory death sentence by hanging. [@2]
