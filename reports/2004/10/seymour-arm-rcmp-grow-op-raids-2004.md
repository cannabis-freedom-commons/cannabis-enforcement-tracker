About 150 police officers raided marijuana grow-ops across Seymour Arm, a remote community of about 70 people at the north end of Shuswap Lake, on Tuesday 5 October 2004. [@2,3,4,5] Carrying 28 search warrants, officers searched 14 properties and seized more than 20,000 marijuana plants and about 50 weapons. [@1,2,3,4,5]

Sixteen people were arrested and released with promises to appear in court; no one resisted the raids. [@1,2,4,5] Police said the operation followed a two-year investigation opened after residents complained of violence, threats and intimidation by growers. [@1,4,5]

The RCMP's Combined Forces Special Enforcement Unit led the operation with help from neighbouring detachments, the Coordinated Marijuana Enforcement Team, Calgary police and sheriffs' officers. [@4,5] Officers found grow-ops in attics, basements, underground bunkers and outbuildings, including multi-level factory-style operations with nurseries and drying rooms, powered by commercial-grade generators. [@1,4,5] Police said the marijuana was bound for Vancouver, Calgary and the United States, and described the growers as a tightly knit group running a sophisticated distribution network. [@1,3,4]
