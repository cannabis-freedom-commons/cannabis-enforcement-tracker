Police discovered four cannabis plantations around the Navosa highlands in
October 2004, uprooting more than 8,000 plants and arresting nine
people. [@1] Acting Assistant Superintendent Kolinio Vunaki warned growers
they would be shown no leniency. [@1]

"Police operations in drugs will never end," Vunaki told the Fiji Times,
calling the raids a consistent effort toward a drug-free Fiji. [@1] The
raids continued a Navosa campaign whose July phase had seen growers sabotage
police vehicles with nails strewn on roads. [@1] The Fiji Times ran three
marijuana articles in two days, pairing the enforcement news with reports on
youth drug treatment and school drug plans. [@1]
