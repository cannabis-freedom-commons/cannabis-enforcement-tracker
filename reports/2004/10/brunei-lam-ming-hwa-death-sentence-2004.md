Brunei's High Court sentenced Malaysian national Lam Ming Hwa to death by hanging on 26
October 2004 for possessing a compressed slab of cannabis weighing 922.276 grams for the
purpose of trafficking. [@1,2] Narcotics Control Bureau officers had arrested Lam and
Sylvester Anak Kuling at a plaza in Tutong on 24 November 2002. [@1,2] Because the amount
exceeded 600 grams, the judges said death was the sentence they were obliged to pass. [@1]

The court convicted Lam after rejecting his explanation for his fingerprint on the packet
holding the cannabis, while it acquitted Sylvester after accepting that he had touched a
packet under a car seat by accident. [@1] Both men had pleaded guilty to consuming
methamphetamine and cannabis; Lam, a repeat offender, received three years in jail on each
consumption charge, running concurrently from his arrest, against three months for
Sylvester. [@1]
