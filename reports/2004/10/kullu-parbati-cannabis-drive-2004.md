Narcotics Control Bureau superintendent O.P. Sharma and Kullu deputy superintendent Kushal
Sharma led an NCB and Kullu police team into Tosh village in the early hours of 6 October
2004, acting on a tip-off with permission from the NCB zonal director in Chandigarh. [@1]
They caught 20 Nepalis in the cannabis-growing pasture and booked five villagers who had
grown cannabis on private land; Tosh, 90 km from the divisional headquarters at Mandi, had
emerged as a charas hub drawing Nepali, Jharkhand and Orissa labourers. [@1]

## Nine-day drive

The team destroyed over 500 bighas of cannabis in Tosh and surrounding forests and planned
to move on to Malander and Rasol in the Parvati valley. [@1] Over the previous nine days
the joint team reported destroying more than 1,700 bighas, about 138 hectares, across the
Malana, Magic, Waraichin, Belang and Tosh areas of the Manikaran valley. [@1] A Himachal
Pradesh High Court judgment confirms a special destruction drive was under way in Kullu
district on 6 October 2004, when a police party reached Tosh Kutla at 8 am and uprooted
cannabis on forest and private land. [@2]
