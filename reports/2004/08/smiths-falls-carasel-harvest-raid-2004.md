Smiths Falls police raided the headquarters of Carasel Harvest Supply Corporation, a medicinal marijuana company operating from a former Canadian Tire store at 41 Centre Street, on Wednesday 4 August 2004. [@1,2,4] Officers seized 221 marijuana plants with an estimated street value of about $220,000, the largest drug seizure the town's police force had ever made. [@1,2,3]

The company had set up shop in February and begun growing to supply chronically ill patients as quickly as possible, though its Health Canada licence was still pending. [@1,4] Police said they had known of the company's plans but not that growing was under way, learning of it from media reports after founder Samantha Klinck told a council committee the plants were already growing. [@1,2]

The raid came a day after 30 licensed medicinal users filed a federal court challenge asking that Carasel be licensed to supply them with Ontario-grown marijuana. [@1,2] No charges were laid immediately; police said they would meet federal Crown attorneys first, and the town council later refused the company's greenhouse rezoning request. [@2,4,5]
