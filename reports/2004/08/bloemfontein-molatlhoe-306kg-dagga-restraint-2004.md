On 18 November 2002 police in Project Big Boy, which investigated dagga smuggling in the Free State, searched various properties and seized 305.956 kilograms of dagga with a street value of R305,956. [@1] Andries Raborepe Molatlhoe was one of the people arrested, and a prosecution for dealing in dagga was later instituted against him. [@1] Police said a syndicate controlled by Molatlhoe had dealt in dagga for years, with an undercover agent buying from its runners between June and November 2002, and some of the seized dagga was found in vehicles registered to him. [@1] Officers also found an unlicensed firearm with two magazines, 26 bullets and a holster in a Corolla registered to him at the Taaibosch Street property. [@1]

On 5 August 2004 the Free State High Court confirmed the provisional restraint order of 26 February 2004 over two properties at Mojatou Street in Rocklands and Taaibosch Street in Botshabelo, household goods worth about R300,000 and 25 motor vehicles. [@1] The court found reasonable grounds to believe a confiscation order could follow conviction because his declared taxi income and tax returns could not account for the assets. [@1] It ordered the defendant to pay the outstanding costs of the application. [@1]
