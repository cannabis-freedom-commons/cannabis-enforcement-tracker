Additional Sessions Judge Sureshwar Thakur convicted Nakul, son of Ganga Ram and a
resident of Labag in Nepal, and sentenced him to two years' imprisonment with a fine of
Rs 40,000 for carrying charas in Mandi on 31 August 2004. [@1] The sentence was imposed
under Section 20 of the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act. [@1]
