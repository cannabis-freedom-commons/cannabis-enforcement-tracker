Dozens of Vancouver police officers raided the Da Kine Smoke and Beverage Shop on Commercial
Drive on the evening of 9 September 2004, arresting the six people inside on charges of
trafficking a controlled substance. [@1] Officers blocked Commercial Drive between Parker and
Napier streets for hours while a crowd of more than 200 taunted them, smoked marijuana and
kept protesting late into the night. [@1] The drug squad had obtained a search warrant after
complaints from a secondary school and the community policing centre about the shop, which
police called very blatant about breaking the law. [@1]

Da Kine had sold marijuana cafe-style for about four months before a television expose pushed
police to act. [@2] The raid did not close it: the shop was back selling marijuana the next
day while police said they were still gathering evidence inside. [@1,2] The case pulled Mayor
Larry Campbell and federal MPs into a national fight over the stalled decriminalization bill,
with the local MP saying sharing a joint counted as trafficking under federal law. [@2]
