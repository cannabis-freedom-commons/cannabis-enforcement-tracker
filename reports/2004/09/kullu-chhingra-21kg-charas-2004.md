Himachal police arrested two people and seized 21 kg of charas from their bags near
Chhingra in the Parvati valley of Kullu district on 14 September 2004. [@1] District
police chief Anand Pratap Singh said a police party had stopped the two as they were
walking toward Manikaran on foot, and a case was registered against them under the
Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act. [@1]
