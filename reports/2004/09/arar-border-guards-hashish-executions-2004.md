Saudi Arabia beheaded three security guards in the northern border city of Arar after convicting them of trafficking hashish and misusing government vehicles. [@1,2] The Interior Ministry named them as Khamis bin Mabrouk al-Sayeri, Nasser bin Mohammed al-Fahadi and Zidan al-Oqaili al-Anzi. [@1,2] They had been caught loading an undisclosed amount of hashish into Saudi border-guard vehicles. [@1,2]

The ministry's statement, carried by the official Saudi Press Agency, said the Sunday executions brought the number of people executed in the kingdom that year to 13. [@1,2] One account put the previous year's total at 52 beheadings, mostly of drug smugglers. [@2] Saudi law permits execution for drug trafficking, and beheadings are carried out in public by sword. [@2]
