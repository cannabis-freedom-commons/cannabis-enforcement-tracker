On 13 May 2003 customs officers at Poole Ferryport stopped a UK-registered truck driven by Ian John
Bridgwater, of Ellesmere Port, Cheshire, and found shrink-wrapped pallets of cannabis resin hidden
behind a legitimate load. [@1,2] Officers first valued the haul at £3 million, and Bridgwater was
arrested at the scene. [@2]

Bridgwater pleaded not guilty and insisted throughout his trial that he had no idea the drugs were in
his vehicle, but a jury at Bournemouth Crown Court convicted him on a majority verdict. [@1] The judge
said he had played a vital role in the supply chain and jailed him for 10 years for smuggling
cannabis then valued at £2.7 million. [@1] A Customs spokesman said hard drugs were the priority but
officers would keep upholding the cannabis law, calling it an excellent detection and warning that
Poole should not be seen as a soft touch. [@1]
