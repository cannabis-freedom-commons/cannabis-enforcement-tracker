New South Wales police raided a camouflaged plantation in isolated woodland near
Dubbo and seized more than 30,000 cannabis plants, which police valued at about
$50 million, for destruction in a day-long harvest.[@1] Officers arrested twelve
people shortly after 6am, among them sentries, crop keepers and three alleged
ringleaders.[@1]

The drug squad commander said most of the plants were at their most valuable
growth stage and that the growers had put in pumps and tanks and gone to an
enormous amount of work.[@1] Up to 200 officers spent the afternoon hauling
metre-high plants from the ground before poisoning them and piling them up for
burning over the weekend, once an exemption from the local total fire ban was
granted.[@1]
