Ilocos Sur police destroyed about ₱15 million worth of marijuana in separate assaults on a
3.7-hectare plantation in Barangay Caoayan, Sugpon, near the Benguet boundary. [@1] Provincial
director Senior Superintendent Mario Subagan said barangay officials and residents had tipped the
force before teams under Senior Inspectors Mario Castillo and Fabiano Sudario moved in. [@1]

On the first day of the operation, guards described as New People’s Army rebels fired on the
police before pulling back, and the raiders seized 40,000 plants. [@1] A second assault recovered
another 35,000 plants, which were burned in bulk. [@1] Subagan’s report, carried on 22 March 2004,
does not say anyone was arrested or charged. [@1]
