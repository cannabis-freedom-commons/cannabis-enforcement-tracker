Police arrested Shunsuke Tamazaki, 32, and Yohei Saito, 21, both Japanese
nationals, after officers allegedly found them with marijuana bricks at a
checkpoint in the mountain town of Bontoc on 19 March 2004.[@1] The haul
weighed 1,360 kilograms, an amount for which Philippine drug law provided
capital punishment for possession of as little as 500 grams.[@1]

The provincial police director said officers were investigating whether the
pair planned to transport the marijuana to Japan, and the Japanese Embassy
told Philippine police that Japanese customs experts planned to visit to
discuss smuggling of illegal drugs from the northern mountain regions to
Japan.[@1]
