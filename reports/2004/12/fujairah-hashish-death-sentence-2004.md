## The sentence

A Fujairah Sharia Court sentenced a Pakistani woman to death after finding her guilty of possessing and dealing in drugs.[@1] Gulf News identified her only by the initials S.M., while later accounts named her as Lisa Tray.[@1,2,3]

## The sting

Fujairah police had received a tip that the woman was selling hashish on the East Coast.[@1,2] After she set a time and place to carry out the deal, an undercover police agent was sent to meet her, and officers seized about 149 grams of hashish when she arrived.[@1,2]

## Her account

Questioned by police and prosecutors, the woman said her stepfather had given her the bag of hashish to deliver to someone and that she had not known what it contained, adding that she did not even know what hashish looked like.[@1,2] The public prosecution did not believe her, charged her with possessing and trading in drugs, and the court imposed the death sentence.[@1] Her lawyers appealed.[@2]
