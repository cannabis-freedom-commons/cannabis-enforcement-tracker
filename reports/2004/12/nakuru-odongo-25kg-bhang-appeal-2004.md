On 25 October 2004 Stesia Anyango Odongo was caught transporting 129 stones of bhang weighing 25 kilograms, valued at Kshs 25,800, in a Scania bus, registration KAP 647G, on the Eldoret-Nakuru road in Nakuru District. [@1] She was charged with trafficking in narcotic drugs under section 4(a) of the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (Control) Act 1994. [@1] Arraigned before the Senior Principal Magistrate's Court at Nakuru, she pleaded guilty and was sentenced to six years' imprisonment. [@1]

On 15 December 2004 the High Court at Nakuru allowed her appeal. [@1] Justice L. Kimaru found the plea was not taken as the law requires: the record showed only "Eng/Swa" without stating the language used, the reply was recorded as "guilty" instead of her own words, and the facts read out spoke of 109 stones while the charge sheet said 129. [@1] The state conceded the appeal, and with the exhibits already destroyed after the plea, no retrial was possible. [@1] The conviction was quashed and Odongo was discharged and set at liberty. [@1]
