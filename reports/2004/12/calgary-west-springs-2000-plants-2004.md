The Southern Alberta Marijuana Investigative Team, a joint unit of the RCMP,
Calgary police and Alberta's criminal intelligence service, broke down the
door of a house in the 100 block of West Springs Place SW on the afternoon of
Monday 6 December 2004. [@1] Officers found a sophisticated three-stage
marijuana dirt-grow operation holding nearly 2,000 plants, plus 300 clones in
a refrigerator, in what the team called its biggest bust since the previous
December. [@1,2]

Police said the crop could have earned dealers over $2.4 million once matured
and sold on the street. [@1] The raid capped a record year in which Calgary
drug squads took just under 72,000 plants off the market, worth nearly $87
million, up from about $50 million the year before. [@1]
