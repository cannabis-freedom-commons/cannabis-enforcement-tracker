Members of Operation Kingfish searched several premises on West Street in
downtown Kingston between 9:45 and 10:35 on the morning of 20 November 2004.
[@1] They found ganja weighing about 76 kilogrammes (167 lbs) in several
plastic bags and barrels, along with cash amounting to J$57,000. [@1] Seven
scales, three chillum pipes and a still camera were also confiscated. [@1] A
man and a woman were detained in connection with the seizure. [@1]
