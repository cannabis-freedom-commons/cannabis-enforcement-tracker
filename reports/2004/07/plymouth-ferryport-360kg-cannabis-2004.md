A Customs mobile team seized approximately 360 kilos of cannabis from a lorry at Plymouth Ferryport early on Friday morning, 16 July 2004. [@1,2] The lorry had arrived on a ferry from Santander in northern Spain, and officers found the drugs packed in 18 holdalls concealed under old shower trays carried as the load. [@1] The haul carried a street value of around £1 million. [@1,2]

The 37-year-old driver and a 35-year-old passenger, both from south-east London, were arrested and questioned by Customs and later released on bail pending further enquiries. [@1] Customs South West spokesman Bob Gaiger called the find an excellent detection, adding that Plymouth should not be seen as a soft touch by smugglers. [@1]
