Thirty police officers entered the Navosa highlands in early July 2004 and
uprooted 1,819 cannabis plants in one raid and 2,335 in another. [@1]
Growers fought back without direct violence, strewing nails across the roads:
a police vehicle blew a tire returning from the raids, and three more
vehicles blew tires two days later after a raid in the interior of
Korolevu. [@1]

"It is the marijuana cultivators, and we believe that it is being
deliberately done to keep us away," a police spokesman told the Fiji
Times. [@1] The paper said growers had also killed informers' cattle and
destroyed their crops, and that only some villagers assisted the
police. [@1] Peasants in the islands' interior had turned to cannabis as a
cash crop in a tough economy. [@1] The Fiji Post reported further raids days
later. [@1]
