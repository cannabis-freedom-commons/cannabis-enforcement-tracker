Zambia's Drug Enforcement Commission said on Wednesday that a leading Zambian
businessman had been arrested and charged with drug trafficking after a huge
greenhouse full of cannabis was discovered on his farm. [@1] Sydney Chileshe,
chairperson of the Zambia Export Growers Association, was arrested and detained
along with 10 of his farm workers on Monday evening, according to DEC
spokesperson Hussein Khan. [@1] A second account confirms that Chileshe and 10
farm workers were arrested and detained on Monday night. [@2]

Chileshe, 48, had been exporting roses and vegetables to Europe for many years
and was described as a leading figure in Zambian business circles. [@1] The DEC
also seized a machine that was allegedly used to compress cannabis before it was
smuggled to Europe. [@1] The report described Chileshe as the first prominent
Zambian businessman to be arrested in connection with cultivating cannabis. [@1]
