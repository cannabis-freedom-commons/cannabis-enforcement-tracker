The National Drug Law Enforcement Agency's Lagos State command seized a total of about
2,135.5 kilogrammes of marijuana, otherwise known as Indian hemp, from suspects at different
locations in Lagos State. [@1,2] Sector Commander Abiodun Adesola, addressing reporters in
Lagos, called it the biggest seizure since the command was created. [@1,2]

## Twelve suspects paraded

The agency paraded 12 suspects from whom the seizures were made. [@1,2] Adesola said the
large consignments were meant for distribution and consumption in Lagos, and said it was
painful that those bringing them in treated the trade purely as a business venture. [@1,2]
He added that since the beginning of the year the agency had redefined its strategies,
repositioned its officers and refocused its operations for greater efficiency. [@1,2]
