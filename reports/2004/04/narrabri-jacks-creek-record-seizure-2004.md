Drug squad detectives with Narrabri police raided the rural property known as Valala at
Jacks Creek at about 7 am on Thursday 8 April 2004 and arrested 16 people. [@1,2] Six were
arrested in the farmhouse, which held temporary beds for workers; three more, including a
couple in a car, were picked up nearby; and seven were found at a crop site inside the
property boundary. [@2] Police called the 2.5 tonnes of dried cannabis found there, valued
at an estimated A$35 million, the biggest haul in New South Wales history. [@1]

## Three crops and a drying shed

The property held three crop sites: Crop A of 1,300 to 1,600 plants, Crop B of 850 and
Crop C of 450 to 550, most already harvested and drying on lines, with 41 plants still in
the ground. [@2] About 30 plastic sheets each holding more than 50 kg of leaf lay across
the sites, and a camouflaged generator-powered shed held drying beds filled to capacity.
[@2] Those arrested included workers cleaning leaves and men who admitted knowing the
drying process. [@2]
