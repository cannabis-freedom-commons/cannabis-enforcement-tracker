Belfast Crown Court on 1 April 2004 jailed two South African nationals for two years each for importing cannabis through Belfast City Airport. [@1] Jacobus Frederick Engelbrecht, 24, had been stopped by Customs officers on arrival from Johannesburg via Heathrow in September 2003, and officers found over 15 kg of cannabis worth an estimated £46,000 concealed in his luggage. [@1,2] In a separate seizure Mark Human, 43, had been stopped at the same airport after arriving from Johannesburg in September 2003 with 20 kg of cannabis worth an estimated £61,000 in his luggage. [@1]

Judge Markey said the sentences should send a message to Johannesburg that smuggling cannabis into Northern Ireland would not be tolerated. [@1] Customs head of investigation for Northern Ireland Jane Salt said removing over £107,000 worth of cannabis showed Customs' commitment to the multi-agency fight against drugs. [@1] Engelbrecht had appeared at Belfast Magistrates Court in late September 2003 charged with illegally importing prohibited substances and had been remanded in custody. [@2]
