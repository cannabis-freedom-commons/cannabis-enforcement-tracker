Customs and Excise officers seized cannabis worth an estimated £35 million sterling (€52 million)
in raids on two freight containers at Seaforth docks in Liverpool on the evening of 14 April
2004. [@1] The containers were full of brick-sized slabs of the drug. [@1]

Seven people were in custody after the operation: six men and a woman, all from addresses in
Britain. [@1] The article names none of them and gives no weight for the cannabis. [@1]
