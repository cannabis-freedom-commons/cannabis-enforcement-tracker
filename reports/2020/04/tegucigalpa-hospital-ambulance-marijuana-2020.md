Officers stopped the hospital's ambulance HEU-201 at a police post at kilometre
13 on the road out of Tegucigalpa toward Olancho. [@2] A prosecution laboratory
later confirmed that the dried plant material seized was marijuana. [@3]

Mario Fu, spokesman for the National Anti-Gang Force (FNAMP), said intelligence
had tracked the pair as Barrio 18 gang members who used the ambulance to move
weapons and drugs. [@1] He said they used the ambulance and its siren to pass
roadblocks during the coronavirus curfew. [@1] Officers from the Military
Police, FNAMP and the Police Intelligence Directorate (Dipol) took part in the
interception. [@2] Police said the two were also under investigation over
vehicle theft, weapons transfers and facilitating robberies, attacks and escapes
inside the hospital. [@2]

Under a shortened procedure the prosecution obtained nine-year prison sentences
against both men on 20 October 2021, convicting them of aggravated illicit drug
trafficking and linking them to the Pandilla 18. [@4] At sentencing the
prosecution put the load at 669 packages, against the roughly 300 first reported
at their arrest. [@4] Prosecutors said the pair will serve their sentence at the
Támara prison. [@4]
