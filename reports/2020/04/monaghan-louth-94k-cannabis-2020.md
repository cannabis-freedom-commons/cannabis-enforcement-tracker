Gardaí stopped a car in the Cloughvalley Upper area of Carrickmacross at about 2.30pm on 22 April 2020 and found its two occupants, men in their 20s and 30s, in possession of €15,500 in cash believed to be the proceeds of crime. [@1] Follow-up searches at two homes in Drogheda seized cannabis plants, pending analysis, with an estimated combined value of €30,000. [@1] A further search at a house in the Collon area of County Louth seized cannabis plants estimated at €64,000. [@1] In total about €94,000 of cannabis and the cash were seized and five people were arrested. [@1]

The two men from the car were detained at Carrickmacross and Castleblayney stations, while a woman and two men, all in their 40s, were detained at Drogheda and Dundalk stations. [@1] The two men were subsequently charged and appeared before the Criminal Courts of Justice on 23 April 2020. [@2]
