A young woman was arrested at Khorixas in Namibia's Kunene Region after throwing
several bales of dagga, as cannabis is widely called in southern Africa, and a
mandrax pill into the police station's prison cells. [@1] Khorixas station
commander Teofilus Ipinge said the woman came to the police station saying she
was bringing food to a trial-awaiting inmate, then went behind the station and
threw the dagga into the cells after failing to reach a prisoner who helps
outside. [@1] A police officer living in the police barracks saw the woman and
alerted the other officers. [@1]

The woman was charged on two counts under section two and section three of
subsection (B) of Act 41 of 1971, one for possession of suspected mandrax and
one for possession of suspected cannabis. [@1] Ipinge said the woman might have
tried to communicate with an inmate before throwing the cannabis, but this could
not be established for certain. [@1] The suspect told the police she had
children at home and pleaded for leniency. [@1] The Khorixas police chief said
it was not the first time the woman had been arrested in possession of drugs and
described her as a regular. [@1]
