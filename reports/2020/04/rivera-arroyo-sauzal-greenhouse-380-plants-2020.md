Police in Rivera department found a clandestine cannabis greenhouse on rural
land at Arroyo Sauzal, about 10 km from the departmental capital, and seized the
plants inside. [@2] The raid took place on Thursday 23 April 2020, carried out
by the Rivera police division specialised in complex crimes, the División
Especializada en Materia de Delitos Complejos. [@3] The greenhouse was found
after neighbours reported strangers entering a plot of rural land in the
department. [@3]

Officers looking through a window saw the greenhouse inside and counted 380
cannabis plants. [@3] Teledoce reported 340 cannabis plants seized from a
nursery, a discrepancy with the 380 counted by the operation's own account. [@1]

Police also seized lamps, fans and air extractors, plus 57 lamps, 22 electrical
ballasts, 15 lamp holders, tools, a shotgun, cartridges, a knife, a mobile phone
and a television. [@3] Several bags of plant substrate brought from Brazil were
at the site, and police found a second structure of wooden posts that had been
started on the same land. [@1]

Nobody was arrested: Subrayado reported that three people at the property fled
when police arrived and were being sought. [@2] Teledoce reported that the owner
of the plot declared voluntarily to investigators the day before publication,
and that police said the person responsible for the crop was fully identified.
[@1] Subrayado reported that the owner of the land rented the property to third
parties. [@2]

The prosecutor on the case, Alejandra Domínguez, confirmed the seizure of the
plants to A Plateia and said investigations would continue to locate the person
responsible for the greenhouse. [@3] The person responsible for the greenhouse
was identified by police and requested by the Uruguayan courts, A Plateia
reported. [@3]
