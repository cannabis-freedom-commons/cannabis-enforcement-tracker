On 10 April police stopped a car on the Western Europe–Western China highway
near Shizhaga in Aral district during an operation. [@2] The driver was a
36-year-old resident of Zhambyl region. [@1] The suspect was detained and placed
in the detention facility of the Aral district police department. [@2] A
pre-trial investigation was opened under article 297 of Kazakhstan's Criminal
Code, covering unlawful manufacture, processing, acquisition, storage, transport
for sale, forwarding or sale of narcotics. [@1]

In the boot of the car officers found plant material with a distinctive smell
packed separately in plastic bottles: eight 2-litre bottles, twenty-six 1-litre
bottles and one 1.5-litre bottle. [@1] Officers also seized a 1-litre plastic
bottle containing a grey powder. [@1] Expert testing identified the seized
substances as 21 kg 458 grams of dried marijuana and 1 kg 345 grams of hashish.
[@2]

The stop was carried out by the Kyzylorda region police department's
drug-control department jointly with Kazakhstan's Interior Ministry. [@3] Police
described the detention as the interception of a man transporting narcotics on
an especially large scale. [@2]
