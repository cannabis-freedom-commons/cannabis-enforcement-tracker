Egyptian customs authorities in Port Said seized four tonnes of hashish
from the ship EGY CROWN on 12 April 2020. [@1,2] The vessel had come from
Syria and was headed for the port of Benghazi in Libya. [@1,2]

The hashish was packed inside milk cartons manufactured by the Syrian
company Milkman, according to the same accounts. [@1,2] Milkman was owned
by Syrian businessman Rami Makhlouf, a cousin of Syria's president who was
listed under United States and European sanctions. [@1,2]

The seizure was made public by Fathi Bashagha, interior minister of Libya's
UN-recognised Government of National Accord, in early May 2020 statements
accusing the Syrian government and Khalifa Haftar's forces of running drugs
through Mediterranean ports under their control. [@1,2] Neither account
reports any arrests or the legal fate of the shipment. [@1,2]
