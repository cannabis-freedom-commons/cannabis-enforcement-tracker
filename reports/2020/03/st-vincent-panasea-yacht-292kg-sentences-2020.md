Three men were convicted of four cannabis offences and sentenced at St Vincent's
Serious Offences Court on Tuesday 24 March 2020. [@1] Curton Allen, 42, a diver
of Clifton, Union Island, was jailed for one year and four months. [@1]

Marvin Alexander, 43, a mechanic and the yacht's captain, was jailed for two
years and eight months. [@1]

Mark Bristol, 52, a mason of McCarthy, was jailed for four years and seven
months. [@1] Time the three men had already spent in jail before the trial was
deducted from their sentences. [@1]

Lawyer Grant Connell had argued for a non-custodial or suspended sentence
because Alexander and Allen had spent 13 months in prison and Bristol 11 months
before making bail in December 2019. [@1]

Chief Magistrate Rechanne Browne convicted the three of possessing 392,376 g of
cannabis for drug trafficking, attempting to export it and conspiring to traffic
it. [@1]

The prosecution said the yacht Panasea was chased from Fancy on St Vincent's
North Windward coast into St Lucian waters on the night of 21 February 2019
before the men complied with an order to stop. [@2] Coast guard officers fired
warning shots and boarded the moving yacht from a dinghy before taking 13 nylon
sacks of cannabis from its cabin. [@2]

The prosecution put the street value of the cannabis at EC$1.73 million, a
figure the defence rejected in earlier hearings. [@3] The defence argued that
the men were in St Lucian territorial waters when arrested, but Browne held that
the pursuit began in St Vincent and the Grenadines and was covered by regional
security treaties. [@1]

Senior Prosecutor Adolphus Delplesche commended the three coast guard officers
for their bravery in the operation, saying they had risked their lives in raging
seas. [@2] Delplesche told the court that while the conversation about cannabis
was changing, the laws remained the same. [@1]
