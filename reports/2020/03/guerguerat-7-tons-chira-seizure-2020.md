Moroccan authorities foiled an attempt to smuggle 7 tons of cannabis resin, locally known as chira, through the country's southernmost border crossing at Guerguerat. [@1] The banned substance was concealed in a large truck bound for sub-Saharan markets, the Moroccan police said in a statement. [@1]

The report noted that Morocco had seized nearly 72 tons of cannabis resin in 2018, along with 252 tons of majoun, a consumable product consisting mostly of cannabis, according to the International Narcotics Control Board. [@1] In June 2019 the authorities had reported seizing 12 tons of cannabis and 800 kg of cannabis resin, with a further 600 kg found in sewage pipes in Taghbalt two weeks later. [@1]
