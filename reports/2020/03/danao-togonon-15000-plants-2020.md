Police of the Regional Mobile Force Battalion-7 destroyed a marijuana plantation
in Barangay Togonon, Danao City, Cebu, on Sunday 1 March 2020. [@1] Police
Captain Emmanuel Rabaya, who headed the operation, said a tip from one of the
residents of the area helped the team locate the plantation. [@1]

The operation was run by the battalion's 703rd Maneuver Company, joined by the
Danao City Police Station, the Cebu Provincial Police Office Mobile Force
Company and the Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency (PDEA-7). [@2] The teams
uprooted 15,000 fully grown marijuana plants and 5,000 seedlings. [@2] Police
valued the destroyed plants at about P8 million. [@2]

Because the plantation was large and lay far from the nearest road, police took
only a few plants as evidence and burned the rest on the spot. [@2] Police did
not say whether any planter had been caught, but noted that the plants were
clean and well tended and that the ground around them had been cleared. [@2]
