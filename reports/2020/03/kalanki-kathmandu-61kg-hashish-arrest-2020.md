Kathmandu police arrested an Indian national carrying 6.1 kg of hashish at
Kalanki on the night of 17 March 2020. [@1] A team from the Metropolitan
Police Circle at Kalimati stopped Anawar Ali Ansari at a hotel in Kalanki.
[@1] He had hidden the hashish in a plastic bag. [@1]

Police said Ansari would be presented before the Kathmandu district court to
extend his remand, according to Deputy Superintendent Rugam Kunwar. [@1]
