Police seized 5000 plants and 30 bags of dried leaves of marijuana in Navosa.
[@1]

Fiji Police spokesperson Ana Naisoro said the Fiji Police Force acknowledged the
assistance of Korolevu and Namoli villagers in the discovery. [@1] A 21-year-old
man from Korolevu, Navosa, was charged with one count of unlawful cultivation
and two counts of found in possession of illicit drugs. [@1]

The suspect allegedly owned the two farms where more than 5000 plants were
uprooted, and a subsequent search found more than $5500 in cash alleged to have
come from the sale of drugs. [@1] The seized plants and dried leaves later
tested positive as marijuana. [@2]

A farmer has been charged after 5,000 marijuana plants were found in his two
farms in Navosa. [@3]
