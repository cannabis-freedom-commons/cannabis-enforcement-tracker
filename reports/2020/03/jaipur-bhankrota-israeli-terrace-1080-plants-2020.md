Jaipur police arrested an Israeli national on Thursday for allegedly farming
marijuana on the 15th-floor terrace of a residential apartment at Bhankrota.
[@1] The arrested man was identified as Alon Molay, a native of Israel. [@1]
Officers raided an apartment near Mahapura Mode in the Bhankrota police station
area to make the arrest. [@1]

Police said the accused was sowing good-quality marijuana over an area of 2,750
square feet on the terrace. [@1] Police described the grow as organic farming of
marijuana. [@1] Police seized 1,080 marijuana plants from the terrace of the
apartment. [@1] The terrace grow used an automatic system in which the plants
received heat from tubelights even during the night. [@1]

Various teams of the Jaipur police commissionerate had been constituted by the
commissioner of police for the crackdown against narcotics in the city. [@1]
During the lockdown, police received specific input that a man appearing to be a
foreign national was roaming in the locality and deployed police in plain
clothes to check the activities of the suspect. [@1]
