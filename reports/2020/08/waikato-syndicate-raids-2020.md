One hundred and sixty police staff executed 23 search warrants at addresses in Taupō, Hamilton and Rotorua, ending a six-month investigation into the sale and supply of cannabis by an organised crime syndicate. [@1] Officers arrested 20 people, 12 of whom were remanded in custody, and all were due in Rotorua District Court on charges including cannabis cultivation and supply and participation in an organised criminal group. [@1]

## Plants, guns and restrained assets

Police seized about 3,450 cannabis plants with a wholesale value of about $3.2 million, along with seven firearms including three semi-automatic military-style rifles and one semi-automatic shotgun. [@1,2] The Southern Asset Recovery Unit restrained homes, vehicles including classic cars and Harley Davidson motorcycles, a jet ski, jewellery and more than $100,000 in cash. [@1,2]
