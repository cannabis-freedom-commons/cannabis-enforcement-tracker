Fiji police uprooted more than 6,700 plants confirmed to be marijuana from five
farms in Kadavu in the week before 23 August 2020. [@1] Two raids in Nacomoto
seized more than 1,000 plants. [@1] The owners of the farms had not been
identified. [@1]

Two more raids in the Lavidi Hills in Nakasaleka uprooted more than 5,000
plants. [@1] The last raid, at Togoni Settlement in Naceva, seized close to 200
marijuana plants. [@1]

Chief Operations Officer ACP Abdul Khan said drone surveillance images easily
picked up irregularities at one of the farms, and a team was deployed to search
nearby areas and found marijuana plants hidden nearby. [@1] Another farm looked
as if its crops had recently been uprooted, but after officers cleared dead
grass and branches from each mound they found marijuana plants hidden
underneath. [@1] Khan said police would not give up easily on Kadavu and were
committed to reducing cultivation of the illicit drug, while also targeting
buyers and the proceeds of the trade. [@1] Another police team was sent to
Kadavu on 22 August to reinforce officers based on the island and the Police
Special Response Unit. [@1]
