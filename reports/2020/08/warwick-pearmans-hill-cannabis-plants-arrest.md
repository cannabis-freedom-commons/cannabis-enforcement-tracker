At about 9.20am on Wednesday 19 August 2020, police officers on mobile patrol in
Warwick observed plants they believed to be cannabis growing in flowerpots at a
residence on Pearman’s Hill West. [@2] A Bermuda Police Service patrol passing
the house spotted the suspected cannabis plants in flowerpots outside. [@1]
Following inquiries, officers determined that the flowerpot plants belonged to a
22-year-old male occupant of the residence. [@1] Officers searched the
occupant’s apartment at the same residence and found a quantity of suspected
cannabis as well as drug equipment. [@1]

The 22-year-old male occupant was subsequently arrested and detained by police.
[@1] The observation, search and arrest all took place at the home on Pearman’s
Hill West in Warwick, Bermuda. [@1] Both identifications remained unconfirmed at
the time, with the plants and the indoor material described only as suspected or
believed to be cannabis. [@2]
