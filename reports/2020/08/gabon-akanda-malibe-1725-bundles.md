Oclad agents arrested 43-year-old Patrick L. Akouaraga in northern Libreville
with a dozen sacks holding 1,725 bundles of cannabis resin in total, and his
accomplices were on the run. [@1] The Oclad seized the 1,725 bundles of resin at
Malibé, in the commune of Akanda, and they most likely belonged to Akouaraga.
[@1] The daily L'Union reported that the 43-year-old had tried to hide the
bundles in the dozen sacks. [@1] L'Union reported that the cargo was in a
Mitsubishi car with registration AB 101 W, carrying two other men, Benjamin
Mintsa Minkoua, known as Akamoto, and Léonard Odzame More, known as Tata, both
on the run. [@1]

By the Oclad's estimate, the load had a market value of more than 25.8 million
CFA francs. [@1] After his arrest on 22 August 2020 he was brought before the
Libreville prosecutor and placed under an order of committal to the central
prison, suspected of possessing and selling narcotics. [@1]

Gabonreview described the seizure as a large new one in Akanda, which it said
appeared to have become one of the country's drug-trafficking hubs. [@1] The
article noted that the seizure came less than three months after 207 kg of
cannabis were found at the home of a Togolese couple. [@1]
