Gardai searched a house on Castle Road in Dundalk just after 8am on Tuesday 18 August 2020 and found a suspected cannabis grow house. [@1,3] Officers from the Dundalk drugs and detective units executed the search under warrant. [@1] Inside they found cannabis plants at varying stages of growth and about 13 kg of suspected cannabis herb. [@1]

The Gardai valued the plants and drugs at over €310,000 and seized lights, fans and an irrigation system. [@1,3] A man in his early 20s was arrested at the scene. [@1,3] He was later charged in connection with the discovery. [@2]
