Fiji police charged a young man over marijuana cultivation in Kadavu in August
2020 after a raid uprooted plants at a Togoni Settlement farm. [@1] The police
special operations team uprooted more than 170 marijuana plants at Togoni
Settlement, Naceva, in the week before he was brought to court. [@1] He was
charged with one count of unlawful cultivation of illicit drugs and was due to
be produced at the Island Court sitting in Vunisea that day. [@1]

Police placed the uprooting at Togoni Settlement last in a run of Kadavu raids
that uprooted more than 6,700 plants from five farms in the days before 23
August 2020. [@3]

Three days after his court appearance, a 23-year-old man from Qauia, Lami was
sentenced to three years and six months in prison for cultivating marijuana in
Kadavu after the same Togoni Settlement raid. [@2] fijivillage reported that he
was arrested following the raid at Togoni Settlement, Naceva, where the special
operations team uprooted more than 170 marijuana plants. [@2] He faced one count
of unlawful cultivation of marijuana in that case. [@2]
