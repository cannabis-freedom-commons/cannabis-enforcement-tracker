Drug and Firearms Squad detectives established Strike Force Harthouse in
November 2019 to investigate cannabis cultivation and supply across New South
Wales. [@1] Following that investigation, strike force detectives executed a
crime scene warrant at a property on the Golden Highway at Gungal, about 58
kilometres south-east of Muswellbrook, from 9am on Thursday 20 August
2020. [@1]

During the search, officers seized 2,628 cannabis plants with an estimated
potential street value of A$7.8 million. [@1] They also found equipment for
cannabis cultivation inside 12 large-scale industrial grow houses, which were
dismantled. [@1]

Two men aged 24 and 29 were arrested at the property and taken to Muswellbrook
Police Station. [@1] Both were charged with cultivating a prohibited plant in a
large commercial quantity, refused bail, and were due to appear at Muswellbrook
Local Court on Friday 21 August 2020. [@1] Detectives said investigations under
the strike force were continuing. [@1]
