Officers of Strike Force Riche, a joint Organised Crime Squad and NSW Crime
Commission investigation into cannabis cultivation and money laundering set up
in July 2019, carried out a search warrant at a property in Kemps Creek, in
Sydney's west, on Tuesday 25 August 2020. [@1] The property held 31
greenhouses, four of them fitted with cannabis cultivation equipment, and
officers seized 4,297 live cannabis plants with an estimated value of 12.89
million dollars. [@1]

Four men found at the scene, aged 19, 23, 26 and 41, were arrested and charged
with participating in a criminal group and cultivating a prohibited plant in a
large commercial quantity, which carries up to twenty years in prison. [@1]
All four faced Liverpool Local Court on Wednesday 26 August and were refused
bail. [@1]

Police alleged the 19-year-old was also involved in another large-scale
operation at Gibberagee, where officers had seized almost 22 million dollars
worth of cannabis in June. [@1] By the time of the Kemps Creek raid, Strike
Force Riche had arrested twelve people in total. [@1]
