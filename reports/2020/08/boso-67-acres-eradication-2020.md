A joint team of military and police personnel with Narcotics Control Commission operatives destroyed about 67 acres of cannabis farms at Boso in the Eastern Region, acting on a tip-off in the week before 27 August 2020. [@1,2] The growers had fled by the time the team arrived, so no arrests were made; officers seized farming implements and kept investigating those behind the cultivation. [@2]

NCC communications head Francis Opoku Amoah said unlicensed cultivation remained illegal under section 39 of the Narcotics Control Act (Act 1019), despite that year's new medical and industrial provisions. [@1,2] Those provisions allow only cannabis with under 0.3 per cent THC and require a licence from the interior minister; the Commission was still drafting the licensing rules. [@2]
