Jean Francois Adrienne and Terence Robin Servina were found guilty of
trafficking and conspiracy to traffic 47,345.1 grams of cannabis resin in
Supreme Court case CR 20/2014, and both were sentenced to life imprisonment on
each count on 27 July 2015. [@1] The National Drugs Enforcement Agency had
found the two men, a farmer and carpenter and a driver, in possession of 47
kilos of cannabis on 9 April 2014, and the life sentences shocked the nation.
[@3] Their appeals against conviction and sentence were dismissed on 11 August
2017. [@1]

The men then applied under the transitional review provision of the Misuse of
Drugs Act 2016, and the Sentence Review Tribunal cut the outstanding portion
of their sentences to 20 years' imprisonment. [@1] The Supreme Court had
sentenced two men to life for trafficking 47,435 grammes of cannabis, a class
B drug, and that sentence was unanimously upheld by the Court of Appeal before
being used as the precedent for later life sentences. [@2] On 21 August 2020
the Court of Appeal dismissed both men's appeals and maintained the 20-year
sentences, ending the case. [@1, 3]
