Three young men were arrested in Walvis Bay during separate operations by drug
detectives. [@1] Two of them were brothers, aged 17 and 21, who were arrested at
about 15:30 on Tuesday. [@1] The Erongo regional crime investigations
coordinator, Deputy Commissioner Erastus Iikuyu, gave the details of both cases.
[@1]

Officers found the brothers with 66 cannabis plants they were cultivating inside
their home in 7th Road and two bankies (plastic bags) of dagga seeds weighing
615 grams. [@1] Police estimated the value of the dagga and the plants at N$30
900. [@1]

In a separate incident, a 21-year-old man was arrested at a house in 6th Road
East on Monday, the day before the brothers. [@1] He was arrested for
cultivating a cannabis plant and for being in possession of 55 grams of dagga,
and police estimated the total value of the plant and the dagga at N$4 250. [@1]

The Drug Law Enforcement Subdivision of the Namibian Police handled the arrests.
[@1]
