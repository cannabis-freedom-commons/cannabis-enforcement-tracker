Senad, Paraguay's anti-drug agency, and Brazil's Federal Police concluded Operation New Alliance XXII on 24 August 2020 in the Pedro Juan Caballero border region, destroying 127 hectares of cannabis crops on the Paraguayan-Brazilian border. [@2] The Federal Police put the yield of the cleared fields at more than 650 tonnes of marijuana bound for the Brazilian market. [@2] Agents also incinerated 1,190 kilograms of cannabis seeds and destroyed 70 clandestine campsites that supported production and distribution, and they detained a Brazilian national suspected of cutting the drug with solvents and packaging it with presses. [@2]

The phase had begun on 16 August with Senad agents destroying production and storage centres in the border area with the Federal Police's assistance. [@1] On the first day they found and destroyed two narco-camps and 2,200 kilograms of marijuana and cleared 19 hectares of crops, enough for at least 57 tonnes. [@1] Aboard Federal Police helicopters, officers of both countries flew over the border area to direct the raids, and Senad said the two forces had destroyed 3,900 tonnes of cannabis in five combined eradication operations over the previous two years. [@2]
