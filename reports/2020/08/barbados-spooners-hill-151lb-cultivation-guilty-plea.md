Police executing a search warrant at his Allamby Gap, Spooner's Hill, St Michael
home caught 25-year-old Shavar Dominique Murray carrying cannabis plants,
arrested him and charged him. [@1] Around 10 a.m. on 14 August 2020 officers
found him holding several plants and he tried to throw them over a galvanised
paling in the backyard before stopping when commanded. [@1] He told officers he
had grown the plants in seven plant pots, a plastic case and half a can, and a
further search of the yard found one marijuana plant behind a fowl pen, three
inside the pen and nine more in a corner of the backyard. [@1]

On 15 August 2020 Murray pleaded guilty before Magistrate Kristie Cuffy-Sargeant
to possession, possession with intent to supply, possession with intent to
traffic and cultivation of 1.51 pounds of cannabis, offences dated to 13 August.
[@1] Prosecutor Station Sergeant Cameron Gibbons put the estimated street value
of the cannabis at 4,500 Barbados dollars. [@1] The court ordered a
pre-sentencing report, Murray, who was not known to the courts, was released on
4,000 dollars bail and is due back before the No. 2 District 'A' Magistrates'
Court on 23 October 2020 for sentencing. [@1] He was on 4,000 dollars bail in
the meantime. [@1]
