Five people were arrested on the night of Thursday 6 February 2020 when the
Walker County Sheriff's Office executed a search warrant at a home in West
Jasper, Alabama. [@1] Deputies said they seized methamphetamine, Suboxone and
marijuana at the home. [@1]

A 43-year-old Jasper woman was arrested for unlawful possession of drug
paraphernalia. [@1] A 53-year-old Jasper man was arrested for unlawful
possession of drug paraphernalia. [@1] A 55-year-old Jasper man was arrested for
unlawful possession of a controlled substance and unlawful possession of drug
paraphernalia. [@1] A 40-year-old Jasper man was held on outstanding warrants
for third-degree felony domestic violence and third-degree assault. [@1] A
38-year-old Parrish woman was held on an outstanding failure-to-appear warrant
from the Jasper Police Department and also faced felony charges of unlawful
distribution of a controlled substance. [@1]

Five people were arrested, a figure the sheriff's office released through the
local ABC affiliate. [@1]
