Customs Enforcement officers at the Kapikule border gate in Edirne searched a foreign-plated lorry travelling from Italy to Georgia after a risk analysis flagged it for an X-ray scan. [@1] The lorry was declared as carrying fertilizer, but narcotics detector dogs reacted to several sacks, and officers unpacking them found 2,070 kilograms of cannabis in 213 sacks. [@1] The lorry's foreign-national driver was detained. [@1]

Trade Minister Ruhsar Pekcan announced the seizure on 11 February 2020, calling it the largest single drug seizure at the country's land border gates, with an official estimated street value of 124,220,000 lira. [@1] The English-language press put the value at about $20.4 million and noted a companion find the same week of 306 kilograms in a trailer's hidden compartment at the Ipsala crossing from Greece. [@2] The Kapikule crossing in the northwestern province of Edirne is a key transit route for narcotics moving between Asia and Europe. [@2]
