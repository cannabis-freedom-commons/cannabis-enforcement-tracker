The Royal Navy destroyer HMS Defender, operating in support of the multinational Combined Task Force 150, launched her Wildcat helicopter to search for illegitimate marine traffic in the Indian Ocean before identifying a suspicious dhow. [@1,2] A boarding team of Royal Marine Commandos secured the dhow, and a Royal Navy team then searched it for illicit cargo. [@1]

The boarding party discovered 2,500 kg of hashish in 119 bags hidden throughout the dhow. [@1,2] The Royal Navy said the haul was worth at least 10 million pounds, while The Maritime Executive put its value at US$1.3 million. [@1,2] The commanding officer said the seizure reinforced the Royal Navy's commitment to disrupting drug smugglers and terrorists at sea, and the armed forces minister said the drugs would never reach the street. [@1]
