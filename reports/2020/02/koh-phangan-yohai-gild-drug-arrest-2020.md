Police on Koh Phangan arrested Yohai Gild, a 44-year-old man described as of
Israeli descent and British nationality, in February 2020. [@4] Officers from
Koh Phangan police station searched a luxury house in Ban Tai on the island's
south coast, where they arrested him. [@2] Gild was held with other Israelis on
suspicion of selling cocaine, ketamine and cannabis and other drugs. [@2]

The search of the bedroom turned up a digital scale and many plastic bags
prepared for dividing up drugs. [@4] The drugs seized included dried cannabis
and cannabis resin, listed in the police account as category-5 narcotics. [@4]
Police also found ecstasy, ketamine and cocaine in the bedroom. [@2] Gild
admitted at the police station that the seized drugs were his. [@2] He allegedly
offered to pay one million dollars to an officer in charge to be freed, and the
police refused. [@2]

He was charged with illegal possession of drugs and psychotropic substances with
intent to sell and handed to the investigators at Koh Phangan police station.
[@4] The police said the case would be pursued along with checks on money trails
and assets. [@4] He was later reported to have been released on bail. [@3]
Globes said sources close to the company confirmed the arrest, and that the Thai
account of cocaine, ketamine and marijuana dealing and a bribe attempt had not
been confirmed in Israel. [@3]

Gild was one of the pioneers of cannabis cultivation in Israel and founder of
Better Cannabis, which he set up after studying cannabis cultivation in the
United States. [@3] The founding of Better Cannabis came after his studies of
cannabis cultivation in the United States, and the company had a role in the
young medical cannabis market in Israel. [@1]
