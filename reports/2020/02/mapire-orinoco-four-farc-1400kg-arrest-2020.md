On 17 February 2020, officers of Border River Command No. 51, attached to the
5th Marine Infantry Brigade, captured four alleged FARC-EP members on the
Orinoco River. [@1] Those detained identified themselves as FARC members but
were presented by the authorities as members of violence-generating groups. [@1]
The reports noted that some of those detained were Venezuelan. [@1] The detained
men were named as Giovanny Calderón, Álvaro Róbinson Gutiérrez García, Wilmer
Antonio Pulido and Carlos Enrique Nieves Salazar. [@1]

They had 1,400 kilograms of suspected marijuana, as well as firearms and a large
quantity of Colombian army uniforms. [@1] The group kept a camp at Mapire, on
the banks of the Orinoco in Anzoátegui state. [@1] A second account likewise
places the group's camp at Mapire on the banks of the Orinoco. [@2]

The four were brought before the military justice tribunals inside the Bolívar
Integral Defence Operations Zone. [@1] The prosecution requested pretrial
detention against all four men and the court granted it. [@1] They were charged
with military rebellion and violation of a security zone, and the judge ordered
their detention at the Oriente Penitentiary Centre (La Pica) in Monagas state.
