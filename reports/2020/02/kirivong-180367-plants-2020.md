Police officers from 12 communes, led by district police chief Col. Yuk Sarath, chopped down marijuana plants at more than 24 locations in Preah Bat Choan Chum commune on Saturday 1 February, anti-drug chief Maj. Sok Sopha said.[@1] They cut 180,367 plants and burned them on the spot, and destroyed 15 ponds used to water the one-hectare mountainside crop.[@1,2] No suspects were arrested, as they escaped before police arrived; officers had surveyed the plantations at night and raided by day.[@1]
