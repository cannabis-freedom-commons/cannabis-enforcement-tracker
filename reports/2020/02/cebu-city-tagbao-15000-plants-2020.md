Personnel of the City Mobile Force Company of the Cebu City Police Office
uprooted fully grown marijuana plants in Sitio Mangabon, Barangay Tagbao, Cebu
City, on Tuesday, 25 February 2020. [@1] Police put the plants at 15,000 stalks
across three plantations, worth at least P6 million, the biggest marijuana haul
in Cebu City that year. [@1]

Lieutenant Colonel Randy Korret, the CMFC chief, said a tip about a plantation
in Tagbao prompted the operation. [@1] Korret said the plantations were hidden
behind bamboo trees on steep ground, and that drones flown over the area failed
to spot them. [@1] The Cebu Provincial Police Office had warned the team to be
cautious because of reports that the plantations had traps. [@1]

Police said they were still investigating who owned the plantation, and that the
cultivators would face charges under the Comprehensive Dangerous Drugs Act of
2002, the Philippines' main drug law. [@1] The uprooted plants were burned
straight away after police obtained permission from the regional Philippine Drug
Enforcement Agency office. [@2] Bombo Radyo reported that the grower was not at
the site during the operation, and gave the village as Sitio Manggabon in
Barangay Tagba-o. [@2]
