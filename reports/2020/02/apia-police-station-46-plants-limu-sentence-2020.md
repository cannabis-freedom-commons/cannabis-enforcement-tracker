Samoa's Supreme Court jailed Pauvi 'Pana' Limu of Malifa for 12 months and
ordered 12 months of rehabilitative and educational programmes for alcohol
consumption after he pleaded guilty to aggravated burglary, theft and possession
of narcotics. [@1] The 55-year-old accepted the Police Summary of Facts that he
had used a hammer to tear the screen wire of the old police training room where
the plants from the November 2019 Faleatiu raid had been kept. [@1]

Limu put 46 marijuana plants valued at $46,000 tala into a rice sack and, so
intoxicated that he swayed, left the building through the front door where a
police officer saw him. [@1] Police said all of the plants were recovered after
a brief foot pursuit. [@1]

Justice Tafaoimalo Leilani Tuala-Warren treated the break-in at the Central
police station as an aggravating feature and described it as both extremely bold
and unbelievably foolish. [@1] The judge noted Limu's previous convictions for
theft and burglary dating back to 1979 and his last offending in 2003, but took
his early guilty pleas and the recovery of all 46 plants into account. [@1] The
plants Limu took were part of the more than 10,000 marijuana plants Samoa Police
Service announced had been brought into Apia Station after the November 2019
Faleatiu raid. [@1]
