On 11 February 2020 officers of the Riverside County Sheriff's Department with the California Department of Fish and Wildlife, county code enforcement and the auto-theft detail served six marijuana-cultivation search warrants around Aguanga. [@1] About 9,946 plants were eradicated and 411 pounds (about 186 kg) of processed marijuana collected, a combined weight of roughly two tons. [@1]

Officers also found a disassembled honey-oil lab, four firearms including an SKS rifle, a tractor, an ATV and two generators, and located two stolen vehicles on one property. [@1] Four men were arrested and booked: Israel Yanez, 27, of Garden Grove, and Aguanga residents Brian Heim, 35, Jacob Buckmaster, 44, and Mitchell Rumer, 32, on cultivation counts, with Heim and Buckmaster also held on honey-oil manufacturing counts. [@1]

## Early-2020 sweeps

The February operation was one of a series of Riverside County sweeps of the Anza and Aguanga valleys that winter as the sheriff targeted unlicensed grows. [@1] Everyone serving the warrants went home safely, and no injuries were reported. [@1]
