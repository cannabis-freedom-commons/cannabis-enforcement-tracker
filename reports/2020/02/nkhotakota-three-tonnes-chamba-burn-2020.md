Nkhotakota police destroyed Indian hemp worth over three tonnes on Tuesday 25 February 2020, burning it inside the Nkhotakota Game Reserve. [@1] The hemp had been confiscated between June and December 2019 at the Nkhotakota roadblock, on uncharted routes and in homes, among other places. [@1]

The exercise was witnessed by the Nkhotakota district council, the magistrate's court and the media, and the hemp was burned using diesel and petrol. [@1] Police described it as a routine exercise carried out once confiscated hemp accumulates in their stores. [@1]
