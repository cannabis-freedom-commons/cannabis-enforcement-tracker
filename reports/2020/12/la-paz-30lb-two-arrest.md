The Local Prosecutor's Office in La Paz coordinated the capture of a man and a
woman transporting wrappers of suspected marijuana after surveillance and
monitoring. [@1] Officers first detained 25-year-old Heber Daniel Agurcia Mencía
and minutes later detained 20-year-old Brendi Marilú Bardales Argueta on El
Soldado boulevard in the city of La Paz. [@1] Both were described as alleged
drug distributors for the Mara Salvatrucha MS-13 criminal structure. [@1] The
detentions were carried out as part of Operation Safe Christmas under the
National Inter-Institutional Security Force, with the Public Ministry's
anti-microtrafficking prosecutor accompanying the operations. [@2]

From Agurcia Mencía officers seized a bag holding 30 packages of dried suspected
marijuana of about one pound each and a mobile phone. [@2] From Bardales Argueta
officers seized a black plastic bag holding one package of dried suspected
marijuana of about one pound, ten small transparent bags of suspected marijuana
and a mobile phone. [@2] The seized packages weighed more than 30 pounds (about
13.6 kg) in total. [@1] The packages were to be sent to Public Ministry
toxicology laboratories to determine purity. [@1]

The detention was supported by agents of the National Anti-Maras and Gangs
Force, known as FNAMP. [@1] Investigators said Agurcia Mencía allegedly intended
to move 30 packages from Guaymaca, Francisco Morazán, to La Paz. [@2]
Investigators said Bardales Argueta was allegedly dedicated to selling and
distributing drugs in an aldea, a village, of the municipality of Santiago de
Puringla and in La Paz. [@2] The force verified that the man had prior
drug-trafficking antecedents and that the young woman had been detained in 2017
for illegal possession of a firearm. [@1] The La Paz prosecutor was preparing a
criminal charge against the detainees for drug trafficking by possession against
public health. [@1] The detainees were to be referred to the corresponding
courts as alleged perpetrators of that offence. [@2]
