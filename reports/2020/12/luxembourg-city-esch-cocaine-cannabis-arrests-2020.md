During a routine check at Luxembourg City's central station, police officers
noticed two people involved in a drug deal. [@1] Both ran off along Avenue de la
Gare and were stopped by officers. [@1] The man police describe as the client
had a ball of cocaine on him, while the dealer had two mobile phones, cash and
several balls of cocaine. [@1] The dealer was arrested and will have to face
court proceedings. [@1]

In a separate incident in Esch-sur-Alzette, police took a drunk man to the
station because he could not show valid identification. [@1] Officers found a
small bag of cannabis on him and arrested him. [@1]
