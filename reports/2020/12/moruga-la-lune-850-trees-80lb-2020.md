Police destroyed marijuana with an estimated street value of $16 million during
an operation in the Moruga district between 7am and 2pm on Thursday 3 December
2020. [@2] According to a media release from the Trinidad and Tobago Police
Service, officers proceeded several miles into the La Lune Forest, where they
discovered and destroyed four marijuana fields located on approximately four and
a half lots of land. [@2]

The fields contained 850 fully grown trees, one seedling bed and one drying rack
with approximately 80 pounds of cured marijuana. [@2] The cured marijuana was
seized and destroyed during the eradication exercise in Moruga on Thursday 3
December 2020. [@1] The operation was led by officers of the Special
Investigation Unit, the Air Support Unit, the Trinidad and Tobago Regiment and
its Special Forces, the Southern Division Task Force and the Multi-Operational
Police Section. [@2]

No one was arrested. [@2] Investigations are continuing. [@2]
