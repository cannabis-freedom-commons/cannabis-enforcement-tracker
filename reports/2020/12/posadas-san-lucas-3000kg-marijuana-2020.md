A joint anti-drug task force led by the Prefectura Naval Argentina raided a
rural property in the San Lucas neighbourhood of Posadas, Misiones, late on 15
December 2020. [@1] The raid was carried out at a finca, a rural property, in
the San Lucas neighbourhood. [@1]

Officers detained two people at the property. [@1]

The Misiones police's Special Operations Group took part in the operation. [@1]
The Misiones provincial government provided logistical support and resources.
[@1]

Officers seized 243 bundles holding approximately 3,000 kilograms of marijuana.
[@1] The bundles were hidden inside a locked refrigerated transport container,
and officers cut the padlocks to reach the drug. [@1]

The exact weight of the marijuana had not been established by the end of the
day, so the three-tonne figure is approximate. [@1] The Federal Court of
Posadas, acting through Judge María Verónica Skanata, is handling the case. [@1]
