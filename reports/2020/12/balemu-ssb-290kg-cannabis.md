The 61st Battalion of the Sashastra Seema Bal, India's border-guard force,
seized about 290 kg of cannabis from a bus stand on the OKSRT road under Balemu
police station in West Kameng district, Arunachal Pradesh. [@1]

During the operation along the Indo-Bhutan border on Tuesday night, a team of
SSB personnel took the cannabis, packed in 13 bags, from a Bolero pick-up van.
[@1] The van was travelling from Kalaktang in Arunachal Pradesh towards Udalguri
district in Assam. [@1] The driver escaped in the darkness and no arrest was
reported. [@1]

The SSB team handed the seized cannabis to Balemu police station. [@1] The SSB
said it has been running special operations in West Kameng against illegal
activity, and has been trying to persuade local people to give up illegal
cannabis cultivation. [@1]
