Police in Antigua and Barbuda arrested a man they said was from Dominica with
marijuana weighing 90.9 pounds. [@1] The 49-year-old man was arrested together
with a 33-year-old Antiguan woman. [@1] The ONDCP intercepted the Dominican man
and the Antiguan woman, both residents of All Saints, on 15 May 2018. [@2]
Albert Baker, 49, and Kerion Friday, 33, appeared before Magistrate Conliffe
Clarke in the St. John's Magistrates' Court facing 12 charges related to 90.9
pounds of cannabis. [@3]

Officers intercepted the pair in St. Johnston's village in a vehicle containing
18.4 pounds of cannabis. [@3] Each was charged with possession, possession with
intent to transfer, being concerned in the supply of cannabis and drug
trafficking. [@3] A search warrant at a Linsey, Parham village home that Baker
frequented found another 72.5 pounds of marijuana. [@3] He was charged with four
further counts of possession, possession with intent to transfer, being
concerned in the supply of cannabis and drug trafficking. [@3] The drugs' total
weight was 90.9 pounds, with an estimated wholesale value of EC$360,360. [@2]
Officers also seized US$1,315 and EC$6,370.25. [@3]

Baker was remanded to prison after a prosecutor called him a flight risk because
he travelled often between Dominica, Antigua, Guadeloupe and St. Maarten. [@3]
The prosecutor objected to bail because Baker travelled often between Dominica,
Antigua, Guadeloupe and St. Maarten. [@3] Friday was offered bail of $146,000,
needed $14,000 to secure her release, and had to surrender her travel documents.
[@3] She also had to report to All Saints Police Station on Tuesdays, Thursdays
and Saturdays, and ask the court before changing address. [@3]

Baker pleaded guilty on 9 October 2020. [@4] On 3 December 2020 Justice Iain
Morley sentenced him to 22 months in Her Majesty's Prison for possession of
cannabis with intent to supply. [@4] The sentencing account described the
cannabis as 89 pounds worth $360,000, seized on the afternoon of 15 May 2018
while ONDCP officers were on duty in St. Johnson's Village. [@4]
