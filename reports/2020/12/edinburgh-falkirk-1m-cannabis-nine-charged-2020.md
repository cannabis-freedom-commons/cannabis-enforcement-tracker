Police in Scotland charged nine people after seizing cannabis plants with a potential value of at least £1 million from private rented properties in Edinburgh and Falkirk. [@1,2] Eight men aged 17 to 35 and a 39-year-old woman appeared at Edinburgh and Falkirk sheriff courts between 17 November and 16 December 2020. [@1,2]

Detective Inspector Paul Greig said the operation showed the force's determination to tackle drug cultivation at all levels and thanked the public for assistance, adding that enquiries were continuing. [@1] He urged private landlords to check tenants thoroughly, especially those paying in cash, and to inspect their properties regularly. [@1]
