Customs Anti-Narcotic Unit (CANU) officers searched a house at 221 Clarke
Street, Dartmouth, on the Essequibo Coast. [@4] The search took place on
Saturday, 19 December 2020. [@4] Four people were arrested, including a woman.
[@3] Officers found the cannabis in suitcases in a room, already packed into 79
parcels. [@2]

CANU said the parcels weighed 85.804 kilograms, about 189 pounds. [@4] Stabroek
News reported a street value of US$80,000, equivalent to more than $16 million.
[@1] Stabroek News reported that the cannabis was already packaged and labelled
when it was found, and that it was suspected of being prepared for sending
overseas. [@1]

CANU said it believed the cannabis was commercial “Creepie weed” trafficked from
Venezuela. [@1] Kaieteur News reported that the parcels bore what appeared to be
a British flag and that it was believed they had been smuggled to Guyana from
Venezuela. [@3] CANU head James Singh said the unit had launched a countrywide
campaign to bring traffickers to justice. [@4] The investigation was ongoing.
[@1]
