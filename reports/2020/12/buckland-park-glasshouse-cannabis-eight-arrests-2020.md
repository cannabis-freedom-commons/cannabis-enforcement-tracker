A Crime Stoppers tip sent Northern District detectives to a Nemesis Court property at Buckland Park just before 8 a.m. on 29 December 2020, where they found a sophisticated cannabis operation spread across glasshouses. [@2] Plants ranging from small to large grew in the glasshouses, others hydroponically, and a significant amount of cannabis dried on racks. [@2] Eight people tried to flee the compound and were arrested. [@1,2,3]

Detectives expected to uncover more than 10,000 plants in further searches and to remain at the property for days burning and disposing of the drugs. [@2] The eight arrested, five men and three women, were charged with trafficking a commercial quantity of a controlled drug, refused bail and were due in court the next day. [@1]
