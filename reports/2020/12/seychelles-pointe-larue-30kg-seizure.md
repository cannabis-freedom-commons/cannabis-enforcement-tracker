Anti-Narcotics Bureau officers seized about 30 kg of suspected herbal cannabis
at a warehouse in the Pointe Larue district of eastern Mahé on Saturday 26
December 2020. [@1,2] The Bureau had gone to view suspected drugs stored at
the warehouse, which one account describes as a workshop, and recovered the
cannabis together with 96 g of heroin. [@1,2,3]

No one was arrested during the seizure, and the ANB opened an investigation
instead. [@1,2] The Bureau called it the second-biggest herbal cannabis
seizure of the year, after 180 kg intercepted from a yacht in July whose
principal offender, a Cascade resident, received two prison terms. [@2]
