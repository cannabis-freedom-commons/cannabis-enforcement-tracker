Morocco's judicial police seized three tonnes and 700 kilograms of cannabis resin in the province of Azemmour, about 72 kilometres from Casablanca, on Sunday 6 September 2020. [@1]

The national police (DGSN) said the operation rested on precise information from the domestic intelligence service (DGST) with field coordination from the Royal Gendarmerie, and that searches inside a farm recovered the resin plus a large inflatable boat and two engines suspected for use in international drug trafficking by sea. [@1] Officers arrested a 37-year-old man with a criminal record for alleged involvement in an international drug-trafficking network. [@1]
