Border patrol police found 270 kg of dried marijuana abandoned on the bank of
the Mekong river in Tha Uthen district on the night of 12 September 2020.
[@1,2] Officers of Border Patrol Police Company 237 found eight black plastic
bags near Hat Sai Phe village in tambon Nong Thao. [@1,2] The bags held 270
bars of dried marijuana, each weighing a kilogram, described by police as
premium grade. [@1,2]

Police believe the bags were smuggled across the river in long-tail boats to
the Thai side and left there awaiting further delivery. [@1,2] Nobody was at
the scene when the officers arrived, and no arrests were reported. [@1,2]

Company commander Pol Lt Col Apisit Rodnoy told a press conference the next
day in Muang district that nearly two tonnes of marijuana had been seized in
the border province during the previous month. [@1,2] The seized bars were
displayed at the company headquarters in Tha Uthen for the media. [@1]
