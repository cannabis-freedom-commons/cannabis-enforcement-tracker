Officers from Singapore's Central Narcotics Bureau (CNB) arrested five men, three Malaysians and two Singaporeans aged 24 to 33, in a drug bust across several locations on 7 September 2020. [@1,2] In the morning they raided a flat near Woodlands Street 32, arresting a 25-year-old Malaysian and recovering Ice and S$15,350 in cash, while a separate team raided a unit near Pasir Ris Street 12 and arrested another 25-year-old Malaysian. [@1,2]

That evening officers watching Kranji Loop recovered about 1,000 grams of Ice and about 3,000 grams of cannabis, and later arrested a 31-year-old Malaysian at Woodlands Checkpoint. [@1,2] A follow-up raid back at Woodlands Street 32 netted two Singaporeans aged 24 and 33; the 24-year-old struggled violently before being subdued, and officers found cannabis and cash on him, more Ice in his vehicle, and a parang knife on him plus a karambit knife in the vehicle. [@1,2]

In total the operation seized about 3,001 grams of cannabis and 1,002 grams of Ice, along with offensive weapons and S$19,865 in cash, with all the drugs valued at about S$360,000. [@1,2] CNB said investigations into all the suspects were continuing. [@1]
