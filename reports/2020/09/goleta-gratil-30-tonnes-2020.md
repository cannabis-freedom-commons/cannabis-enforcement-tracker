Spain's Tax Agency and National Police seized more than 30 tonnes of hashish from four high-end sailing yachts of the same organisation in five days in the Atlantic and arrested nine Bulgarian and Russian nationals. [@1,2] Authorities called Operation Goleta-Gratil the largest operation against hashish trafficking by sea carried out in Spain. [@1]

The maritime sweep ran from 24 to 28 September with four patrol boats, a helicopter, a Customs Surveillance aircraft and the Navy vessel Meteoro, following months of investigation since October 2019. [@1] The Sweet Breeze was boarded on 24 September south-east of Fuerteventura in international waters with 10,700 kilograms on board, and the Eagle Ray was boarded the same day near the Strait of Gibraltar with 9,300 kilograms. [@1] The Amaalta was boarded on 26 September off Mauritania with an estimated 4,500 kilograms, and the Carla was boarded on 28 September south-east of Fuerteventura with an estimated 6,000 kilograms. [@1] The vessels and the arrested men were handed to the competent courts in El Puerto de Santa María, Manacor, Inca and Telde. [@1] Earlier in 2020 the same investigation had taken 4,244 kilograms from the Phase One south of Mallorca and 360 kilograms from the Hanna in the Gulf of Cádiz. [@1,2]
