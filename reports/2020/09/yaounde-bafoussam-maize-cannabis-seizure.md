National gendarmes seized an important quantity of cannabis on the
Yaoundé-Bafoussam axis in an operation that took place that weekend, as reported
by Equinoxe radio. [@1] The shipment had come from Bamenda in the North-West and
was intended to be sold in Yaoundé and other towns. [@1] Officers found the
cannabis hidden in maize inside a bus at city-entrance checkpoints, particularly
on the road leading to the West. [@1]

Members of the trafficking network were apprehended and were to answer for their
acts before the competent courts. [@1] Lieutenant Jean Bello, head of the
criminal research and investigation office, said the hemp had been buried in
maize so it could not be distinguished from the maize package. [@1] He described
the network as running from a supplier around Bamenda through a transporter
carrying the haul to Yaoundé to a buyer who collected it for sale across the
capital’s neighbourhoods. [@1]

After the roadside find, gendarmes followed inquiries until they located the
rest of the stock already dispersed throughout Yaoundé. [@1] Bello praised the
work of officers posted at city entrances, especially those covering the road to
the West, a point the second account also reports as a weekend seizure from
Bamenda for Yaoundé. [@2]
