Semiliguda police stopped two vehicles at Kokriguda Chhak, under Semiliguda
police limits in Koraput district, at about 11 am on Friday 18 September 2020
after a tip-off about ganja smuggling. [@1,2] Officers searched a white
Mahindra pickup, registration OR-10-E-6548, and a Glamour motorcycle,
registration OD-10-J-7885, and recovered 1,000 kg of ganja packed in sacks.
[@1,2]

Police arrested the five men carrying the load: Parmesh, also called Dipu, 32,
of Kunduli; Kartika Chapadi, 22, of Maliput in Pottangi; and Gopal Bhoi, 27,
Shyama Bhoi, 19, and Damu Bhoi, 18, all of Parajaburuda in Nandapur. [@2] The
seized ganja was valued at about one crore rupees. [@2]

A case, Semiliguda PS Case No. 79/2020 dated 18 September 2020, was registered
under sections 20(b)(ii)(C) and 29 of the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic
Substances Act, 1985, and the accused were forwarded to court. [@1,2]
Semiliguda inspector-in-charge Dhiren Kumar Behera said police were working to
unravel the wider network behind the shipment. [@2]
