Chatham-Kent police raided a large commercial greenhouse on Maynard Line east
of Chatham on 16 September 2020, seizing cannabis police valued at more than
$7.3 million. [@1] Officers took three days to dismantle the greenhouse,
taking hundreds of plants. [@1] A later police statement put the seizure at
7,300 plants and said a 48-year-old London man was charged with cultivating
marijuana for the purpose of selling. [@2]

At the time of the first report the investigation was still open and charges
were pending. [@1] Police said criminal organisations from outside the area
rent vacant buildings in Chatham-Kent to hide large grow operations, with
profits funding other organised crime. [@2]
