Police arrested a 42-year-old Turaga ni Koro, a village headman, after a raid at
his house yielded dried leaves in a plastic bag. [@1] The raid occurred at his
house in Loa Village in Cakaudrove and resulted in the seizure of marijuana.
[@2] Following further investigation, officers found a farm with more than 180
plants believed to be marijuana that allegedly belonged to the suspect. [@1] A
joint operations team arrested five other people and processed them at Tukavesi
Police Station, where they were charged and produced in court. [@1] In Taveuni,
a 15-year-old boy and a 26-year-old man were arrested and charged over alleged
drug-related activities, while two farmers were arrested and processed in
Savusavu for similar offences. [@2]

Across the Northern Division and Kadavu combined, police reported seizing more
than 4,000 marijuana plants. [@1] In Cakaudrove, officers uprooted more than
2,000 marijuana plants from the Loa and Naqio Hills and near Vunikura Village
during the week. [@2] In Kadavu, operations uprooted more than 2,000 marijuana
plants from three farms. [@1] The first Kadavu raid at Lavidi Village uprooted
more than 300 marijuana plants. [@1] Officers later uprooted more than 130
plants in Vacalea and more than 1,500 plants on Friday morning from a farm in
Naikorokoro Village. [@1] Police said the owners of the three Kadavu farms had
not yet been located. [@3]

During the week, police teams focused on the Tukavesi and Savusavu areas in the
Northern Division and on Kadavu in the Southern Division. [@4] The special
operations team comprised officers from the Police Special Response Unit and
officers from different divisions targeting areas known for marijuana
cultivation. [@1] Chief Operations Officer Assistant Commissioner of Police
Abdul Khan said the momentum of the anti-drug drive would increase heading into
the festive season. [@1] Khan said police were targeting both green and white
drugs, noting that intelligence-driven operations against white drugs were more
complex and took longer to prepare. [@2]
