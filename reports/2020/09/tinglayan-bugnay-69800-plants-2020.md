Local police and Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency agents in Kalinga burned at least 69,800 fully grown marijuana plants seized from a 4-hectare plantation in a three-day operation in Tinglayan. [@1] The plants, valued at about PHP 13.96 million, were found in the village of Bugnay and seized in operations from 18 to 20 September 2020. [@1]

Agents uprooted and burned the plants on site as part of "Oplan 17: Quebec Jolly Green", launched jointly by the drug agency's Cordillera and Cagayan Valley offices with Kalinga police units to clear the villages of marijuana cultivation. [@1]
