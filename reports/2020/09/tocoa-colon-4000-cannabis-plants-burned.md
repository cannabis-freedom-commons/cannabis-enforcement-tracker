Prosecutors of the Bajo Aguán regional prosecution service, with the
anti-microtrafficking drugs unit and the anti-drug directorate, carried out
inspections at four different places in Colón department, Honduras. [@1] The
plantation was found in the area known as El Portillo and Cuaquita, in the
jurisdiction of Tocoa, Colón department. [@1]

Each of the sites had plantations of a herb alleged to be marijuana, and
officers took a representative sample from each to send for toxicological
analysis. [@1] Detectives of the anti-drug directorate counted between 4,000 and
5,000 cannabis plants in total across the growing site. [@1] The rest of the
plants were burned at each of the places where the herb was found. [@1]
