The Anti-Drug Brigade of the Paysandú departmental police seized a tonne of
marijuana in Paysandú on Thursday 17 September 2020. [@2] Under an operation
named Patriarca, officers searched a rural estate in the north of the department
and detained a 40-year-old man and a 32-year-old woman. [@1]

They found exactly 1,103.2 kg of marijuana in 15 large sacks and 5 woven plastic
bags, la diaria rounding the total to 1,103 kilos. [@1] Officers also seized 40
kg of cannabis seeds, 1 kg of pressed marijuana and 68 grams of buds. [@1] The
haul included a digital scale, a .380 pistol with six cartridges, a Magnum rifle
with 45 cartridges, mobile phones, 11,400 Argentine pesos and 300 Uruguayan
pesos. [@1] Police also took a Volkswagen Saveiro pickup and a Chevrolet S10
pickup. [@2]

A second search at a home in the east of Paysandú led to the detention of a
28-year-old man, whom la diaria places in a dwelling in the same northern
locality. [@1]

The local court convicted the 28-year-old of a continuing offence of trafficking
prohibited narcotic substances in the form of dealing and sentenced him to 2
years and 10 months in prison. [@1] The 32-year-old woman received 20 months of
prison to be served under supervised release, while the 40-year-old man was
freed and remained summoned to court. [@1]
