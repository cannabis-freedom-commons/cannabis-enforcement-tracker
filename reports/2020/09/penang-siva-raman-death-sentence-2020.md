On 30 September 2020, the George Town High Court sentenced R Siva Raman, a 48-year-old coconut milk (santan) seller, to death for trafficking 208 grams of cannabis. [@1,4] He had been charged with trafficking the drug in front of the Fresh Food Court at Jalan Rozhan Alma in Bukit Mertajam at about 8.15pm on 16 January 2018. [@1]

## The sentence

Judicial Commissioner Mohd Radzi Abdul Hamid said the court had no other choice but to impose the death sentence, finding that Siva Raman had failed to raise a reasonable doubt that he was in actual possession of the drugs. [@1] When the judge asked both sides to address whether life imprisonment was possible, Deputy Public Prosecutor Lim Saw Sim asked for death, saying life was reserved for convicts who helped disrupt trafficking and there was no evidence Siva Raman had assisted any enforcement agency. [@1] Says estimated the street value of the 208 grams at about RM2,000, based on a going price of RM100 per 10 grams. [@1]

## A wider pattern

Months later, VICE World News cited the Penang food-court case as another September death sentence for selling cannabis, alongside medical-cannabis campaigners facing the same punishment. [@2] Green Flower likewise recorded that the Indian coconut milk seller caught with slightly over 200 grams in 2018 was sentenced to death in September 2020. [@3]
