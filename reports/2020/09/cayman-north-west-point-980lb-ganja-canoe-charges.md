Three Jamaican men aged 56, 44 and 31 were arrested and later charged after the
Royal Cayman Islands Police Service and the Cayman Islands Coast Guard
intercepted a drug canoe off North West Point in West Bay, Grand Cayman. [@1]

The police helicopter was on an aerial patrol at about 3:50am on Thursday, 24
September 2020 when the crew spotted the vessel in the waters off North West
Point Road in West Bay. [@2] The men were seen throwing items from the vessel
into the water after they saw the helicopter, and the coast guard was informed
of their location and intercepted the boat. [@2] Officers recovered 42 packages
of ganja with a gross weight of 980lbs, about 444.5 kilograms. [@2] Some of the
bales, wrapped in white packaging, floated to shore and were recovered by police
and members of the public on Thursday morning. [@1] Cayman News Service placed
the interception at about 3:50am on 24 September 2020. [@3]

On Friday, 25 September, all three men were charged with importation of ganja
and illegal landing. [@2] The 44-year-old was also charged with contravention of
a deportation order. [@2] The three men remained in custody and were expected to
appear in Summary Court on Monday, 28 September 2020. [@2]
