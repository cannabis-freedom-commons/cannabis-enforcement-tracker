More than a hundred suspected ganja smugglers, disguised as kanwariya pilgrims and carrying the contraband in slings, were intercepted by police near Chaulimendi under Balimela police limits on the night of 5 September. [@1] Acting on a tip-off, the police party stopped the group for a search, and the smugglers attacked the officers with sticks and axes. [@1,2]

Home guard Banabasi Maharana was grievously injured in the attack and died while under treatment at Mathili Hospital. [@1,2] Police detained 36 people at the site as the rest fled in the darkness, and seized 1,917 kilos of ganja that the detained men could show no papers for. [@2] The case was registered as Mathili police station case 125 of 2020, with murder and NDPS charges. [@2]
