Central Gondar Zone police seized 46 quintals (4,600 kg) of cannabis from a truck heading to Humera town. [@1] The consignment had been picked up in Shashemene town and was supposed to cross borders via Humera, according to the police. [@1]

The cannabis was concealed under 20 quintals of corn and 18 quintals of cattle fodder on the truck. [@1] The driver tried to avoid a checkpoint and change direction, and was caught red-handed by security forces in the area. [@1] Police arrested three people including the driver and his aide. [@1]
