Royal Virgin Islands Police Force officers arrested two men after a car chase on
Tortola on 9 May 2020. [@1] The two were Shannon John, 34, of Little Dix Hill,
and Mackebo Fahie, 24, of Greenland. [@1] The chase started in Fat Hogs Bay
around midday when Joint Patrol officers engaged the vehicle. [@2]

Both men were charged with possession of a controlled drug. [@2] Police said two
arrests were made and a quantity of cannabis was recovered when the chase ended.
[@2]

The vehicle travelled in reverse through Fat Hogs Bay onto Little Dix Hill Road
before stopping beside the Francis Lettsome Primary School. [@3] Officers
searched John and his vehicle and found 30 grams of cannabis with a $300 street
value. [@3]

John pleaded guilty to possession of a controlled drug and four traffic offences
before Magistrate Christilyn Benjamin, but denied reckless and negligent
driving. [@3] He was granted $20,000 signed bail. [@3] He was ordered to
surrender his travel documents as a bail condition. [@3] He was due to reappear
before the court on 8 July. [@3] John told police the drug and the vehicle were
his and said he fled because he was carrying weed. [@3] The passenger told
police he was just catching a ride and did not know drugs were in the car. [@3]
