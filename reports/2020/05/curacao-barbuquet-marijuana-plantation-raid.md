Police in Willemstad dismantled a marijuana plantation in the Barbuquet
neighborhood during the afternoon of 24 May 2020, the day before the local
report published on 25 May 2020 described the operation. [@1] No one was
arrested during the police operation because no one was present at the
plantation location when the officers arrived to carry out the raid. [@1] The
police nevertheless said that they expected to be able to make arrests in
connection with the dismantled plantation later during that same week. [@1]

The police raid on the Barbuquet plantation site happened after officers
received a tip that directed them to the location. [@1] During the dismantling
operation the officers seized a large number of marijuana plants found at the
plantation together with tools found at the same site. [@1]

During the same raid the police also discovered that water and electricity at
the plantation site were being tapped illegally. [@1] The plantation no longer
remained in operation after the police dismantled it. [@1]
