National Guard members on an incursion operation to locate and destroy plantings
found a plot sown with plants similar to marijuana on the outskirts of the
municipality of Cucurpe, Sonora. [@1] The plot covered about 600 square metres,
about 0.06 hectares, with an average of nine plants sown per square metre at a
height of one metre. [@1]

The guards pulled the plants out by the roots. [@1] Before burning the drug,
officers took samples and handed them to the federal public prosecutor, who will
follow up the inquiry. [@1]

The National Guard published the find as press release 430/2020 and reported no
arrests. [@1]
