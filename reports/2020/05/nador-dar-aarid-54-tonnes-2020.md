Moroccan police seized 5.4 tonnes of cannabis resin in the north-eastern city of Nador on
Saturday 23 May 2020. [@1] Officers found the shipment inside a four-wheel-drive vehicle in the
Dar Aarid neighbourhood, but its passengers managed to flee during the arrest attempt. [@1]

The national security directorate, the DGSN, said in a statement that the city prosecutor had
opened an investigation to identify the drug network behind the shipment. [@1] No arrest was
reported. [@1]
