Marine Battalion Landing Team-7 troops and PDEA operatives launched a law
enforcement operation against a drug personality identified as Sahabi at Sitio
Mangal-Mangal in Masjid Punjungan village. [@2] The plantation was believed to
be owned by Sahabi, described as a supporter of the Abu Sayyaf Group. [@1] No
one was present during the raid, so no arrests were made. [@1]

Authorities uprooted and burned around 22,000 fully grown marijuana plants in a
2,100-square-metre plantation in Sitio Mangal-Mangal, Barangay Masjid Punjungan,
Kalingalang Caluang, Sulu, on 1 May 2020. [@1] The site comprised six areas of
fully grown marijuana and two areas of newly planted marijuana, totalling about
22,000 fully grown plants across 2,100 square metres. [@2] Combined Naval Forces
Western Mindanao and PDEA forces seized marijuana plants valued at P5.1 million
from the Sulu plantation. [@1]

Joint Task Force Sulu commander Maj. Gen. Corleto Vinluan said the plantation
could be another source of funds for the Abu Sayyaf Group and its supporters.
[@1] The plantation was discovered on 1 May 2020 at Sitio Mangal-Mangal, Masjid
Punjungan village, Kalingalang Caluang, Sulu. [@2]
