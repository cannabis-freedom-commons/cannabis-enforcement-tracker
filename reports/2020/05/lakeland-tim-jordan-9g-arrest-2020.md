Tennessee Vols running back Tim Jordan was arrested on gun and marijuana charges
after a traffic stop in Lakeland, Florida. [@1,3] Jordan is a senior from Bartow,
Florida. [@1] Jordan, 20, was behind the wheel with a 17-year-old male in the
passenger seat, and both seemed extremely nervous. [@1] His teenage passenger
also was arrested. [@1,3] Jordan was booked into the Polk County Jail and released
Saturday night after bond was set at $2,000. [@1,3]

Two Lakeland police detectives spotted the vehicle speeding and swerving
repeatedly into the bicycle lane. [@1,3] The officers stopped the vehicle and
reported smelling a strong odor of cannabis from inside. [@1] Jordan told
officers he was carrying a gun in his back pocket without a concealed carry
permit. [@1] The handgun, a Ruger LCP pistol, was found in his back pocket
loaded and unholstered. [@1] Inside the vehicle officers found a small bag with
about 9.25 grams of marijuana, a scale and several small plastic bags. [@1,3]
Jordan faces charges of carrying a concealed firearm, possessing narcotic
paraphernalia and possessing 20 grams or less of cannabis. [@1,3] The
concealed-carry charge is a third-degree felony in Florida punishable by up to
five years in prison and a $5,000 fine. [@1]

A spokesman for UT athletics declined to comment. [@1] ABC News corroborated
that Jordan, 20, was released Saturday on $2,000 bond. [@2] ABC News
corroborated that a 17-year-old male passenger was in his car and also arrested.
[@2]

Citing the arrest warrant, the Knoxville News Sentinel reported that Jordan was arrested shortly before 5 a.m. ET. [@3]
