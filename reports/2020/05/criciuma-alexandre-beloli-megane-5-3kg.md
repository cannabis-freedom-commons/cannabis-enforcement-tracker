Military police seized more than five kilos of marijuana in Criciúma on the
night of Wednesday 13 May 2020 after intercepting a drug transaction on the
Alexandre Beloli road. [@1]

Tactical and Rocam motorcycle patrols were told a Renault Megane was involved in
a possible drug deal, and the driver ignored the order to stop and fled. [@1]
During the chase the occupants threw objects from the window near the Via Rápida
junction, and police found a bag with more than 4.7 kilos of marijuana. [@1] In
Linha Anta the driver lost control and crashed into an embankment, the car
caught fire and the three men escaped through dense bush. [@1] A further piece
of marijuana weighing 409 grams was found inside the Megane. [@1]

At the registered address of the car, belonging to a trafficker known to police,
the man's wife let the officers in and was arrested. [@1] At the house police
found six lumps of marijuana weighing a total of 195 grams, 650 reais in cash,
two scales, plastic bags and knives used to divide the drug, and took the woman
to the police station. [@1] The three men who fled had not been found when the
report was published. [@1]
