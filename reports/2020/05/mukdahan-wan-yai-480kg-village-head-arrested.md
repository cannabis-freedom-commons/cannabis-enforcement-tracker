Border Patrol Police Company 235 commander Pol Col Sippanan Sornkhunkaew told a media briefing on Saturday 16 May 2020 that a village head and two accomplices had been arrested with 480 kg of compressed marijuana, worth at least 10 million baht, on the bank of the Mekong River in Mukdahan province. [@1,2] The three men were Phuthorn Wongwai, 57, head of village Moo 11 in tambon Pong Kham of Wan Yai district, his assistant Yutthana Wongwai, 41, and Phoonsak Eksaphang, 34. [@1,2]

Officers spotted the men as they were about to move the drugs, smuggled across the Mekong from Laos, to a storage place. [@1] Border patrol police and soldiers later searched the suspects' houses and seized three vehicles. [@1] Phuthorn claimed it was his first involvement in the drug trade, which the authorities did not believe. [@1]

The arrest followed an investigation finding the suspects close to a trafficking network that bought marijuana in Laos and stored it before delivery to Thai agents, and the authorities said they would expand the investigation to others involved. [@1]
