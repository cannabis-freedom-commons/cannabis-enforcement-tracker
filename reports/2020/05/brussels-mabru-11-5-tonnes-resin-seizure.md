Belgian federal police seized cannabis resin at Brussels' main wholesale food
market on Monday 18 May 2020. [@1] Six people were arrested in the operation, as
announced by the Brussels prosecutor. [@1] After questioning on Monday by the
federal judicial police, the six suspects were to be heard within 48 hours by an
investigating judge ruling on detention. [@1]

The drugs were in a container intercepted at dawn at the Marché matinal de
Bruxelles (Mabru), the wholesale food platform serving traders and restaurant
owners. [@1] Special federal police units carried out the intervention under an
investigating judge in a drug-trafficking investigation. [@1]

Mabru management said the load was aboard a truck and not in the market's own
infrastructure and that the case was unrelated to market activity. [@3] The
Mabru wholesale market in Laeken operates at night into the early morning and
mainly serves professional food retailers. [@2]
