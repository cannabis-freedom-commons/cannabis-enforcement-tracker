Jagmohan Murray, a 37-year-old Guyanese national who had lived in Barbados for
more than a decade, admitted cultivating marijuana and two possession offences
in the District ‘A’ Magistrates’ Court. [@1] He was fined $5,000, payable within
three months, and faces six months in prison if he does not pay the fine at the
District ‘A’ Magistrates’ Court. [@1]

Police searched Murray’s home at 1st Avenue Accommodation Road, Bush Hall, St
Michael, on May 30 and found 867 seedlings of marijuana. [@1] Sergeant Robert
Jones of the police said the drugs weighed 600 grammes and had an estimated
street value of $3,000. [@1] Murray told police he is a herbalist who likes
marijuana herbs and said he and his wife had been in an argument and he was put
out. [@1] Murray said he had built a little shack in the bush and studied how to
make a little extra change, planting the marijuana the previous year and selling
about $500 or $400 of it. [@1]

Murray was jointly charged with his 46-year-old wife Roseann Maria Murray, who
pleaded not guilty to possession, possession with intent to supply and
trafficking of cannabis. [@1] Roseann Maria Murray was granted bail of $3,000 to
return to court on October 6. [@1] Murray was fined on the supply charge and was
convicted, reprimanded and discharged on all the other cannabis offences. [@1]
