Two unemployed youths from Kadavu, aged 19 and 20, were sentenced to seven
years' imprisonment on 4 May 2020 for unlawful cultivation of an illicit drug.
[@1] They appeared before Justice Daniel Goundar at the Suva High Court after
earlier pleading guilty to the charge. [@1] In March 2019 a team of police
officers raided a farm in Kadavu and found the two men near it; confronted, they
admitted cultivating marijuana. [@1] The Fiji Sun named the men as Viliame
Mocevakaca, 20, and Josaia Delai, 19. [@2]

Both will be eligible for parole after four years; the Fiji Sun reports the same
four-year non-parole period. [@1] After the men led police to the farm, 1,202
plants between 15 and 163 centimetres tall were uprooted and seized as exhibits,
with a total weight of 25 kilograms. [@2] Justice Goundar said cultivation of
marijuana, especially in secluded locations, was becoming too prevalent, and he
ordered the drugs destroyed forthwith. [@1]

The judge also said drug cartels were turning to ordinary unemployed villagers
and exploiting their social-economic vulnerability, and that unemployment was no
excuse for cultivation. [@2] fijivillage reported that the men had cultivated 25
kilograms' worth of marijuana plants and that the incident happened in March the
previous year. [@3]
