Members of the Jamaica Constabulary Force's Narcotics Division and the Manchester police raided two premises in Spur Tree, Manchester, at about 8:45 pm on Monday 4 May 2020. [@1,2] The raid was described as the biggest ganja bust in Jamaica's history. [@1,2]

Officers seized about 8,700 pounds (about 3,946 kg) of compressed ganja with an estimated street value of $35 million. [@1,2] Four suspects, three men and a woman, were detained pending further investigation, and a motor vehicle on the premises was also seized. [@2] Arrests were made but the suspects' identities were not released. [@1,2]
