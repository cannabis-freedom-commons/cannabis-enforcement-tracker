Members of the Fiji Police Force who are also district soccer representatives
took over the drug operations then being conducted in Kadavu, during which close
to 5,000 plants were uprooted in the past week. [@1] Chief Operations Officer
ACP Abdul Khan said the deployment of officers would continue throughout the
operational period to support the Kadavu operation. [@1] Khan said officers from
various units and divisions would be pushed forward to assist Kadavu Police with
the drug operation. [@1]

Close to 5,000 plants were uprooted in Kadavu over the past week. [@1] The raids
were conducted in Levuka, Yale, and Naioti and Nacomoto Village. [@1] Police
described the seized drugs as believed to be marijuana. [@1]

Khan said that apart from Kadavu, drug raids continued to be recorded across the
country. [@1] A police photo showed some of the district soccer representatives
who were described as instrumental in the Kadavu raid. [@1]
