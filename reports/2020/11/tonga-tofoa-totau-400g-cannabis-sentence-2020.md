Matangi Tonga reported the case under the headline that a dealer who sold
cannabis from home was jailed. [@1] Sosiua Totau, 25, was sentenced to two
years' imprisonment for possessing 400.37 grams of cannabis found at his home in
Tofoa. [@2] Lord Chief Justice Whitten sentenced him on 6 November 2020 at the
Nuku'alofa Supreme Court after he pleaded guilty to the single count in
September. [@2] The court suspended the final 12 months of the two-year term for
18 months from his release, on conditions. [@2]

On 30 March 2020, police carrying out routine Covid-19 traffic checks stopped
Totau and a co-driver in a vehicle at Tofoa and found cannabis in it, which the
court dealt with under other charges. [@2] Police then received information that
drugs were being sold at Totau's home and searched it, finding a large plastic
bag of cannabis leaves and seeds. [@2] Totau first said he knew nothing about
the cannabis, then told police after further questioning that it belonged to
him. [@2]

The Crown called the weight of the cannabis an aggravating feature, while the
mitigating features were his cooperation with police, an early guilty plea and
his status as a first offender. [@2] The court heard that Totau is married with
a child, works on his family's plantation with his wife and is not otherwise
employed. [@2] The Chief Justice said possessing some 400 grams of cannabis
carried clear commercial connotations but that it was appropriate to give him a
chance at rehabilitation by suspending part of the sentence. [@2] The court
ordered the cannabis destroyed. [@2]

Totau was charged jointly with Likamani Fa'aoso over the 165.37 grams of
cannabis found in the vehicle at the checkpoint. [@3] Their arraignment was on
30 September, when Fa'aoso pleaded guilty and Totau pleaded not guilty. [@3]
Lord Chief Justice Whitten sentenced Fa'aoso to 16 months on 30 October 2020
after he admitted possessing the 165.37 grams. [@3]
