Anti-Narcotics Unit officers arrested suspected major dealer Heralie Nyirakabanza, 64, with
6,500 pellets of cannabis in Ryambungira Village, Gakingo Cell, Shingiro Sector on Tuesday
10 November 2020. [@1,2] Her casual employee Emerence Muragijimana, 22, who helped find
customers and deliver, was arrested with her. [@1,2] A community policing partner in Kigali had
tipped off the unit, which trailed Nyirakabanza to Musanze and closed in as a disguised buyer
was to pay FRW1.2 million for the bundle. [@1]

Nyirakabanza told the police she was supplied by big dealers from Gisenyi in Rubavu District
and distributed to retailers in Kigali. [@1] Rwandan law classes cannabis as a severe narcotic
whose conviction carries life imprisonment and a fine of FRW20 to 30 million. [@1]
