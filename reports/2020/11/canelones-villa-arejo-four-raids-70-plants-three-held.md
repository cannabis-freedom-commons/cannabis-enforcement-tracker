Canelones police detained three people in the operation they called Operativo
Villa Arejo, at Villa Villarejo, a settlement on Ruta 32 about 16 kilometres
from Canelones city. [@3] Officers carried out four raids on homes in the area,
one of several simultaneous searches carried out on the morning of Monday 30
November 2020. [@1] The three detainees included the man police described as the
suspected head of the group, and one of them had a record for robbery, handling
stolen goods and theft. [@3] Police were looking for four people and were still
seeking the fourth, a young man who had left his home for work early that
morning. [@2]

Officers found cannabis in three greenhouses, with more than 70 marijuana plants
in all. [@1] Police chief Víctor Trezza put the count at about 40 plants in one
greenhouse, 20 on another property and 12 on a third, all of which he said would
be confiscated. [@4] Officers also seized a .22 pistol and a sawn-off shotgun,
ammunition, mobile phones, loose cannabis buds and 9,000 pesos in cash. [@3]

The operation followed talks with Villa Arejo residents who said drugs were
being sold locally and reported nightly shootings. [@1] Investigators worked on
the case for at least a month and a half before seeking the search warrants.
[@2] About 30 officers took part, including the tactical response group, the
investigations division of the Canelones police command and forensic police,
arriving in patrol cars, large vehicles and a helicopter. [@3]

A police scientific unit was surveying what was seized, and the detainees were
to be taken before prosecutor Mónica Castro. [@1]
