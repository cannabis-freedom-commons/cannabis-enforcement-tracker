Gendarmes of Section San José, part of Squadron 8 Alto Uruguay of the
Gendarmería Nacional, seized 10,070 kilograms of marijuana from a semi-trailer
truck at kilometre 785 of National Route 14, at Paraje Centinela in Misiones
province. [@1] The truck was on its way to Buenos Aires province when officers
stopped it. [@1] Checking the driver's papers, officers found inconsistencies
and the absence of the seals and certifications required to carry the load. [@1]

The sniffer dog Uno, trained to detect drugs, reacted to the trailer, signaling
that it carried drugs. [@1] A Nuctech scanner then showed that the load inside
was marijuana. [@1] Inside the opened semi-trailer officers found 467 bundles of
rectangular packages holding the drug. [@1]

The Gendarmería reported the find to the Federal Court and the Federal
Prosecutor's Office of Posadas, which ordered the truck's seals removed and a
narcotest carried out; the field test was positive. [@1] The court ordered the
truck driver detained incommunicado and the load weighed; the initial weighing
came to 10,070 kilograms of marijuana. [@1] The Security Ministry presented the
operation as part of Security Minister Sabina Frederic's order to federal police
and security forces to tighten surveillance at strategic points used for illegal
drug traffic. [@1]
