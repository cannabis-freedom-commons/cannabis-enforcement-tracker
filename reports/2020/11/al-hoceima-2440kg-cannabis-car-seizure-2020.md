On Saturday 7 November 2020, Moroccan security services seized 2.44 tonnes of cannabis near the northern city of Al Hoceima, as reported by the official news agency MAP. [@1,2] The banned substance was concealed in 91 packages inside a car, which was stopped at a customs checkpoint about 35 kilometres from the city. [@1,2]

## What the reports do not say

Neither report names any arrest, charge or court outcome connected with the car or its load. [@1,2] The reports also give no breakdown of the cannabis by form, and the seizure's intended destination was not published. [@1,2]
