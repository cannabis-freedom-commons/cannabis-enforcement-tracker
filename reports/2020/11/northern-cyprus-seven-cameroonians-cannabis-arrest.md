Police in northern Cyprus broke up an alleged drug ring with links in
Gemikonagi, Girne, Lefkosa, Lefke and Gazimagusa, seizing almost 17 kilos of
cannabis. [@1] Seven Cameroonian nationals were arrested in connection with the
haul. [@3] The Narcotics and Smuggling Prevention Branch seized what it called
one of the largest recent drug hauls in its four-district Pence (Claw)
operation. [@2] The overnight operation across Lefkosa, Girne, Gazimagusa and
Lefke recovered 16 kilos 750 grams of drugs and led to seven Cameroonian
nationals being detained. [@2] Those detained, said to be university students,
were brought before a court, which ordered them held for one day in the first
instance. [@2]

The case began with a tip that two suspicious packages were being sent by taxi
from Gemikonagi to Girne. [@2] The suspect who came to collect the packages at
the Girne address tried to flee on seeing the police and was caught with
reasonable force after running about 100 metres. [@2] The two taxi packages held
1 kilo 750 grams of cannabis-type drugs, and the man collecting them was taken
into custody. [@2] A search of another suspect's home found 10 kilos in 17
packages and 5 kilos in 16 packages. [@2] Police established that one of the
suspects had brought the drugs into the country from Cameroon through Ercan
Airport on 28 October 2020. [@2] He spent two weeks with the drugs in a Catalkoy
quarantine hotel before being collected from the hotel by another suspect once
isolation ended. [@2]

All seven suspects were given the floor in court and denied the charges. [@2]
The suspect who imported the drugs claimed someone in Cameroon gave him the
package, telling him it held food products, and that he did not know it
contained drugs. [@2] Police said statements were still to be taken, other
wanted persons were sought and some evidence was missing, and asked for the
suspects to be held. [@2] The investigating officer, Baris Duman, testified in
court that the seven suspects had been caught trafficking drugs. [@3]
