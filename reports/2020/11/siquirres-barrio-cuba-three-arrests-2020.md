Judicial Investigation Agency agents detained three people in raids in Siquirres
and Barrio Cuba. [@1] The operations targeted two independent groups on the
morning of that Thursday. [@1] In Siquirres, police detained two siblings, a
37-year-old woman and a 34-year-old man, who allegedly distributed drugs from
their homes. [@1] In Barrio Cuba, San Jose, Narcotics Section agents raided a
house and detained a 34-year-old man. [@1]

In Barrio Nazareth in Siquirres, officers seized a calibre 22 firearm,
marijuana-type drugs, crack-base cocaine and 320,000 colones in cash. [@1]
Investigators said many buyers came to both Siquirres houses, with the man
allegedly supplying the drugs and both siblings selling them. [@1] The two
Siquirres houses stood about 100 metres apart, and officers said the cash in
small bills appeared to be proceeds of drug sales. [@2] The two Siquirres
detainees were referred to the Public Ministry to determine their legal
situation. [@2] The Siquirres siblings were held as suspects of an offence under
Costa Rica's Psychotropic Substances Law. [@2]

In Barrio Cuba, the suspect allegedly sold marijuana-type drugs, cocaine, crack
and ketamine on the street and at the house in Mar Caribe. [@3] The Barrio Cuba
investigation began in June 2020 after confidential reports about drug sales.
[@3] The Barrio Cuba raid took place at about 8am in the Mar Caribe sector of
Barrio Cuba. [@3] The Barrio Cuba detainee was placed at the disposal of the
Public Ministry to determine his legal situation. [@3]
