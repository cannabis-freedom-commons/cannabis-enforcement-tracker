Senad, Paraguay's anti-drug agency, and Brazil's Federal Police ended Operation Nueva Alianza XXIII on 13 November 2020 after ten days of joint raids in the forests of Amambay department. [@1] They destroyed 137 hectares of marijuana crops and 116 drug camps used as operating bases. [@1]

Senad reported destroying 83.6 tonnes of processed marijuana and 100 kilograms of pressed marijuana, putting the combined result at 494.6 tonnes taken out of circulation. [@1] Two Federal Police helicopters carried agents into the forest zones, and Paraguay's Office of the Attorney General backed the operation alongside the Joint Task Force of soldiers, police and Senad agents. [@1]

## The same phase on paper

A Brazilian Federal Police study records the same phase at 137 hectares and 116 camps with 83,718 kilograms of chopped marijuana, dating it 3 to 12 November for a combined 494.7 tonnes. [@2] On 20 November the Brazilian ambassador visited the Senad base to review the results of the 23rd phase with minister Arnaldo Giuzzio. [@1]
