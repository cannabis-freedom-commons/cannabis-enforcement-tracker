Ontario Provincial Police raided a greenhouse on Mersea Road in Leamington on Wednesday, seizing more than 75,000 cannabis plants and over 5,300 pounds (about 2,400 kg) of processed cannabis. [@2,1] Officers from the Essex County detachment, its crime unit and the Regional Community Street Crime Unit carried out the Cannabis Act raid with the OPP-led Provincial Joint Forces Cannabis Enforcement Team, the Organized Crime Enforcement Bureau, the Emergency Response Team and the Tactics and Rescue Unit. [@2] A police detective inspector valued the seizure at more than C$80 million. [@2]

Ten people were arrested and charged with possession of cannabis for trafficking and growing cannabis somewhere other than a private dwelling. [@1,2] Police named the ten accused, aged 35 to 57 and mostly of no fixed address, with others listed from North York, Scarborough and Markham. [@1,2] They were due in a Leamington court at a later date, and the investigation was continuing. [@2]

The Essex County detachment commander said the seizure showed the resolve of criminal enterprise groups to profit from illegal grow operations and warned that police would keep pursuing them. [@1,2]
