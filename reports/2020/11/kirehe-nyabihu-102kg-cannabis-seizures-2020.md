Rwanda National Police operations against major dealers on 26 and 27 November
2020 netted a combined 102 kg of cannabis in Kirehe and Nyabihu districts.
[@1] In Nyabihu, officers intercepted Francois Niyonzima, 34, in Mukamira
Sector on 27 November trafficking 30 kg of cannabis on a motorcycle. [@1]
Niyonzima, a commercial motorcyclist from Kinigi Sector in Musanze District,
refused to stop at a police roadblock, threw a bundle of cannabis into the
road to cause mayhem and lead the trailing police vehicle into an accident,
and was handed to the Rwanda Investigation Bureau station at Mukamira for
further process. [@1]

In Kirehe, police foiled the trafficking of two sacks holding 72 kg of
cannabis being sneaked into Rwanda from Burundi through the Akagera River in
Gahara Sector. [@1] Four traffickers carrying the sacks abandoned them and
fled back to Burundi when they saw the officers, after local authorities in
Murehe Cell reported them. [@1] The four were said to be Burundi nationals.
