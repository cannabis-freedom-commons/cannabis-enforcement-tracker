Dineh Benally, a former San Juan River farm board president, had leased more
than 400 acres of Navajo farmland near Shiprock for what he called a hemp
enterprise, filling former cornfields with hundreds of lighted
greenhouses. [@1] Weeks before the raids, a Navajo court barred him from
growing, and tribal inspectors found much of the crop looked like
marijuana. [@2]

The FBI, the Navajo Nation Police and more than a dozen other agencies then
raided the operation, dubbed Operation Navajo Gold, after reports of
THC-rich cannabis production, interstate trafficking and labour
violations. [@2] The multi-agency sweep eradicated about 260,000 live plants
and confiscated 30 tons of evidence, including about 1,000 pounds of
high-grade marijuana ready for distribution, the US Attorney's office
said. [@3] No arrests were announced in the raid reports. [@3]
