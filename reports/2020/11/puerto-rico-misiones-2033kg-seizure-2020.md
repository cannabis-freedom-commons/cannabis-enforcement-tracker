Prefectura Naval Argentina officers seized a load of more than 2,033 kilograms
of marijuana on the coast of Puerto Rico, Misiones. [@1] A Prefectura land
patrol found an abandoned pickup truck with its doors open near the Paraná
riverbank at kilometre 1,750, loaded with many packages. [@1]

Because the cache appeared recently stockpiled, officers brought the
drug-detection dog Rango and his handler to the scene and found the rest of the
load scattered around the area. [@1] Searches recovered 87 packages holding a
total of 2,114 pressed marijuana bricks weighing more than 2,033 kilograms. [@1]
The Prefectura put the seized drug's estimated value at more than 171 million
pesos. [@1]

Elonce reported the same seizure of more than 2,033 kilograms of marijuana on
the coast of Puerto Rico. [@2] The Federal Prosecutor's Office in Oberá, led by
Viviana Vallejos with secretary Arlindo Otto Kurtz, took charge of the case
after the Prefectura notified the federal judiciary. [@1]
