Benin customs officers of the Borgou-Alibori Rapid Intervention Service seized
drugs from a bus on Wednesday 22 January 2020. [@1] The unit intercepted a bus
coming from Togo on the Djougou–N'Dali road at Wereke. [@1]

The seizure totalled 37 kg of chanvre indien, the French term for cannabis. [@1]
The operation was conducted under Borgou-Alibori customs chief Colonel Charles
Sacca Boco and led on the ground by Commander Sadeck Orou Guidou. [@1] The
traffickers had carefully concealed the prohibited goods inside the vehicle.
[@1] Customs recovered the 37 kg through vigilance and intelligence received by
Commander Orou Guidou and his officers, according to information relayed by
national radio. [@1]

Bénin Web TV says the seizure followed intelligence received and carried out the
order of the director general of customs, Comptroller General Inoussa Bocco
Sacca. [@2] Bénin Web TV says the traffickers would be placed at the disposal of
the police for possible criminal proceedings. [@2]
