On 2 January 2020 an appeal court confirmed prison terms of 1 to 3 years for the
six men arrested with 142 kg of zamal at Sainte-Rose. [@4] The six had first
been convicted in September 2019 after the 31 May 2019 interception. [@4] Those
arrested comprised two Réunionnais and four Mauritians. [@1] All six were placed
in custody and questioned after the night operation. [@2]

Gendarmes seized 142 kg of zamal, the local name for cannabis, at Anse des
Cascades in Sainte-Rose. [@1] The interception happened late on Friday 31 May
2019 at Anse des Cascades. [@1] The compressed cannabis was packed in about ten
airtight sports bags for transport by boat. [@3] The consignment was bound for
Mauritius. [@3] Authorities valued the load at 2 million euros on the Mauritian
market, about half that locally. [@2]

The boat sent from Mauritius to collect the load escaped despite the gendarmerie
helicopter intervening. [@1] Forty-five gendarmes from the Saint-Benoît company,
the research section and the GIGN carried out the operation after months of
surveillance. [@2] The operation was a joint effort by Réunion gendarmes and
Mauritian police who had monitored the suspects for weeks. [@3] The defendants
had faced up to 10 years in prison. [@4]
