Security forces raided the Akbarkhel area of Azra district in central Logar
province late on Friday, 24 January 2020, police spokesman Shahpur Ahmadzai told
Pajhwok Afghan News. [@1] Officers seized about 160 kilograms of hashish in the
raid. [@1] Ahmadzai said the hashish had been skillfully placed inside a pick-up
vehicle. [@1] No arrest was made in the operation. [@1]

Bismillah, a tribal elder from the area, said hashish was freely cultivated in
Azra district. [@2] The elder claimed 80 percent of the district's land was
cultivated with hashish. [@2] He claimed drugs from eastern provinces arrived in
the district before being smuggled to other areas. [@2]
