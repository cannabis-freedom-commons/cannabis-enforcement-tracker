Police in Manicaland intercepted two trucks ferrying a total of 6.7 tonnes of marijuana (mbanje), believed to have been smuggled from neighbouring countries. [@1] A haulage truck carrying 4.7 tonnes was stopped on the Nyanga-Nyamapanda Road, while another with over two tonnes was stopped by Zimbabwe Revenue Authority (Zimra) officers after leaving Forbes Border Post near Mutare. [@1]

The Forbes truck was coming from Malawi and headed for Zimbabwe, and its driver disappeared after declaring the container empty; officers found the marijuana hidden in a specially created false compartment. [@1] At a roadblock at the 75 km peg on the Nyanga-Nyamapanda Road, police found 53 90 kg bags of mbanje in a containerised truck and arrested Dennis Chikumba, Farai Biziki, Mario July and Aswell Marange, all of Harare, who were travelling in a Toyota Toyoace, registration AFD 5997, for drug trafficking. [@1] National police spokesperson Assistant Commissioner Paul Nyathi confirmed the arrests. [@1]
