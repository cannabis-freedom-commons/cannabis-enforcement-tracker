Egypt's interior ministry announced that police had seized more than 20 tonnes of marijuana and destroyed over 283 acres of drug farms in South Sinai province [@1]. The General Department of Narcotics Control ran the campaign with the armed forces, targeting drug farms across several areas of the province for more than ten days [@1].

## What was seized

Officers seized 20 tonnes of dried marijuana along with 20 kg of hashish, 828 kg of cannabis seeds, 243 kg of opium seeds and a cannabis rosin press machine, according to the ministry statement [@1]. The 283-acre eradication figure covers cannabis and poppy farms together and is not counted separately here [@1].

## Context

The ministry's statement notes Egypt's high rate of drug use, citing the national addiction fund's estimate that about 10.4 percent of people aged 15 to 65 take drugs [@1]. No arrests were reported in the republished account [@1].
