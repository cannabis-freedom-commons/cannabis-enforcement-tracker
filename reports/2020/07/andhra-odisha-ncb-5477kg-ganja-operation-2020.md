India's Narcotics Control Bureau said on 9 July 2020 that intensive
enforcement against ganja smuggled from the Andhra Pradesh–Odisha border had
seized 5,477 kg of the drug in five cases across four states. [@1] Acting on
intelligence of large-scale movement from the border region to other states,
the bureau ran coordinated operations over the previous week that broke up
several trafficking networks using nationally permitted trucks to ferry the
contraband across state borders. [@1] Thirteen suspects were arrested across
the five transport cases. [@1]

In the largest detailed case, the bureau's Kolkata zonal unit seized 1,133.2
kg of ganja near the Dhulagarh toll plaza on national highway 6 on 28 June,
hidden in a coconut-laden truck bearing Jharkhand registration plates, and
took three people into custody. [@1] On 2 July the Hyderabad sub-zonal unit
intercepted a 14-wheeler trailer with Andhra Pradesh plates at Vijayawada and
recovered 515 kg of ganja concealed in cavities between the vehicle's chassis,
arresting two people. [@1] The bureau said it was also investigating network
links with money laundering and other anti-state activity. [@1]
