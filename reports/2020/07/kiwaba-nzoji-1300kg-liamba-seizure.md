Municipal police in Kiwaba-Nzoji detained two people after intercepting a
vehicle carrying cannabis. [@1] The two detainees were placed in the custody of
the Serviço de Investigação Criminal (SIC), Angola's criminal-investigation
service, which was preparing the criminal case for presentation in court. [@1]
The driver was identified as Cristiano Ndaluar, accused of illegal transport and
possession of narcotics. [@1] Ndaluar said he did not know what was inside the
sacks, saying they looked like the bombó cassava-flour sacks he usually carried
and that checkpoint searches had never found anything before. [@1]

The load, described as more than one tonne, was hidden in 25-kg bombó sacks in a
truck bound for sale in Luanda. [@1] Kiwaba-Nzoji municipal commander
Subinspector André Manuel said a residents' tip led officers to intercept the
vehicle. [@1] Police called it the largest drug seizure in Malanje province
since the start of 2020. [@1]

Liamba is the local name for cannabis sativa. [@1] Authorities describe Cahombo
municipality in northeastern Malanje as part of a corridor of localities with
high levels of liamba production. [@1]
