Joint police and anti-drug operatives destroyed 63,220 fully grown marijuana plants with a Dangerous Drugs Board value of more than PHP12.6 million in the hinterlands of Kalinga and Ifugao over one weekend, the Police Regional Office Cordillera reported [@1]. In Kalinga, just over 63,200 of the plants, worth PHP12,640,000, were found uprooted from 7,900 square metres in Loccong, Tinglayan, on 4 July 2020 [@1]. The operation, Oplan 07 Hawk Pumadcha, brought together the provincial police, its intelligence and drug units, the regional mobile force, the Army-backed regional intelligence units and PDEA teams from four regions [@1].

## No arrests yet

No cultivator had been arrested in the operation, though intelligence work to arrest the growers continued, the report said [@1]. Cordillera police director Brigadier General R'win Pagkalinawan said the campaign was part of an aggressive push to end marijuana cultivation in parts of Kalinga and Benguet [@1].
