Prefectura Naval Argentina officers seized more than 550 kilograms of marijuana
on the coast of the Misiones city of Posadas, in a shipment brought by boat from
Paraguay. [@1] The operation began while officers patrolling the area to prevent
crime spotted a canoe loaded with several bundles approaching the Argentine
coast from Paraguay. [@1] The canoe reached the shore at La Cantera, at
kilometre 1568 of the Paraná River. [@1] When officers reached the spot, they
found 25 bundles abandoned on the shore. [@1] Inside the bundles, officers found
647 marijuana bricks weighing more than 550 kilos. [@1]

The ministry put the value of the seized shipment at 41,500,000 pesos. [@1] The
ministry noted that officers of the force had seized more than two tonnes of
drugs that week. [@1] The Federal Prosecutor's Office of Corrientes Province,
under Dr. Flavio Ferrini with sole secretary Dr. Juan Resoagli, intervenes in
the case. [@1]
