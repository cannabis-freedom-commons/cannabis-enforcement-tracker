Gardai stopped a vehicle in the Raheen area of County Laois at about 9:15am on Wednesday 1 July 2020 during an intelligence-led operation against the sale and supply of drugs. [@1,2] Officers searched the vehicle and seized a substantial quantity of suspected cannabis herb and a large amount of suspected cannabis products. [@1,2]

The Gardai valued the seizure at approximately €3.9 million, pending analysis. [@1,2] Four men were arrested at the scene. [@1,2] Two men in their 30s were detained at Portlaoise Garda Station and two men in their 40s at Tullamore Garda Station under drug-trafficking legislation. [@1,2]
