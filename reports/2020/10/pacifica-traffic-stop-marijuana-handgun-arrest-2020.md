Pacifica police pulled over a car on Highway 1 near Crespi Drive at about 1:20
a.m. on Friday 16 October 2020. [@1] The driver, 21-year-old Dante Dalporto of
El Granada, was on probation with a search and seizure clause, which allowed
officers to search the car. [@1] Bay City News reported that police stopped the
car for speeding. [@2] Dalporto told the officer he had drugs and a gun in the
car, police said. [@1]

The search found a loaded 9mm handgun, ammunition, marijuana, fentanyl and drug
paraphernalia. [@2] Police described the amount of marijuana as a "large
quantity" and also found items used to prepare drugs for sale. [@1] The handgun
had been stolen in a residential burglary in another county, investigators said.
[@1]

Because he is a convicted felon, Dalporto was booked into the San Mateo County
jail on weapons charges, including possessing a firearm and ammunition as a
prohibited person and carrying a concealed firearm, and on drug charges
including possession of drug paraphernalia and possession of a loaded firearm
while possessing a controlled substance. [@1] Bay City News listed one of the
charges against him as possession of cannabis for sale. [@2] A 21-year-old woman
in the car with him was briefly detained and released without any charges. [@2]
According to jail records, Dalporto was held without bail and was expected in
court on the Monday afternoon after his arrest. [@1]
