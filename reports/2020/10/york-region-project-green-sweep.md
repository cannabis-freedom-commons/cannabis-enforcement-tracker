York Regional Police seized about $150 million of illicit cannabis during
Project Green Sweep, a summer 2020 operation under its cannabis enforcement
strategy. [@1] Officers executed fifteen search warrants on indoor and outdoor
grow operations across York Region, including Markham, King, Stouffville and
East Gwillimbury. [@1] Thirty-seven people were arrested and 67 charges were
laid. [@1]

Police seized 28,964 plants and 3,990 pounds (about 1,810 kilograms) of
harvested cannabis, plus seven firearms, two crossbows and a conducted energy
weapon. [@1] At one site in Schomberg they found three kangaroos and two
zebras, and notified animal control. [@1] Police allege organised crime
exploited Health Canada's medical-grow licensing rules, pooling
designated-grow licences to run cannabis farms for the criminal market. [@1]
Chief Jim MacSween said the profits funded violence and other crime in the
community. [@1]
