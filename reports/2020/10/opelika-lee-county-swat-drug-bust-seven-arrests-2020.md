At about 1:20 p.m. on 2 October 2020, Opelika police and the Lee County SWAT
team executed a narcotics search warrant in the 1800 block of Hurst Street. [@1]
Officers retrieved a large amount of marijuana and a stolen gun during the
search. [@1]

Raven Gibson, 29, was arrested and charged with possession of marijuana. [@1]
Chezmon Jordan, 28, was charged with possession of marijuana and possession of
drug paraphernalia. [@1] Christopher Jordan, 45, was charged with possession of
marijuana and a misdemeanor warrant. [@1] Kentavious Heard, 28, was charged with
possession of marijuana. [@1] Joshua Andrews, 28, was charged with possession of
marijuana and license to carry a pistol required. [@1] Derrico Collier, 28, and
David Lilly, 25, were arrested on unrelated misdemeanor warrants. [@1]

All seven were booked into the Lee County Jail. [@1]
