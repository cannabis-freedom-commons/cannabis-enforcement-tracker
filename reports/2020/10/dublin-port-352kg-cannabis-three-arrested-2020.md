Revenue customs officers and members of the Garda National Drugs and Organised Crime Bureau seized 352 kg of herbal cannabis at Dublin Port on 23 October 2020. [@1,2] The drugs arrived in a container from Spain and were found during routine profiling with the help of Revenue's mobile X-ray scanner and detector dog Kelly. [@1,2] The authorities put the estimated street value at more than seven million euro. [@1,2]

International police also assisted the joint operation against organised crime groups. [@2] Three men aged 31, 47 and 49 were arrested and detained under section 2 of the Criminal Justice (Drug Trafficking) Act 1996 at Garda stations in Dublin. [@1,2] Searches were still under way on the afternoon of the seizure and investigations were ongoing. [@1]
