The operation, run by the Içara precinct's Civil Police with 40 officers, ended
with the seizure of two tonnes of marijuana in Criciúma, after the outlet
corrected its first version placing it in Içara. [@1] The action began at 6 am
on Friday 9 October 2020. [@1] A 27-year-old man was arrested for drug
trafficking during the operation. [@1] Notisul gave the same account of 40 Içara
Civil Police officers seizing two tonnes of marijuana in Criciúma from 6 am on
that Friday. [@4]

Officers seized about two tonnes of marijuana stored in an airy depository built
for the drugs. [@1] The seizure was made in the Primeira Linha neighbourhood of
Criciúma. [@2] The load was at a country property near a gated condominium. [@2]
The man was alone in the residence when detained. [@1] Police seized a
money-counting machine from the property. [@1]

Delegate Rafael Marin Iasco said the man had no criminal record and did not
resist arrest. [@1] The delegate said the marijuana was probably distributed
across the Sul Catarinense region, with further questioning and investigation to
determine the next steps. [@1] The operation was commanded by delegate Rafael
Iasco, with the Civil Police dog unit of the Núcleo de Operações com Cães taking
part. [@2]

The weighing had not been completed when the reports were published, and
officers estimated the marijuana at close to two tonnes. [@3] ClicSC reported
that the operation followed an investigation by the Içara precinct and that one
person was arrested. [@3]
