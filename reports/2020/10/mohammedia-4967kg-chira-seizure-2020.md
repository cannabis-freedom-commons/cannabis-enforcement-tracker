On the evening of Friday 16 October 2020, judicial police from Casablanca stopped a utility vehicle at the entrance to the port city of Mohammedia and arrested the man driving it, seizing 4,967 kilograms of cannabis resin, known locally as chira. [@1,2] The operation followed surveillance and monitoring based on information from the General Directorate of Territorial Surveillance (DGST), and the national police (DGSN) described it as foiling an international drug trafficking attempt. [@2]

## Custody and investigation

The suspect, described as the driver escorting the shipment, was placed in police custody for an investigation conducted under the supervision of the competent prosecutor's office. [@2] The investigation aimed to identify everyone involved in the criminal network and determine its ramifications at national and international level. [@1,2] No charges or court outcome were reported in these sources.
