Gendarmería Nacional detained four people after a series of raids that began at
a farm in Gobernador Roca, Misiones. [@1] Officers found 8,889 kilograms of
marijuana hidden in a truck on the farm, a load the federal prosecutor's office
valued at 1,370 million pesos. [@1] The raiding party found 8,889 kilos of
marijuana in the daylight search. [@1] The Gendarmerie said the drug was packed
in 463 bundles inside the semitrailer of a Volvo truck. [@2] The Gendarmerie's
own weighing put the load at 8,829 kilograms 87 grams. [@2] The four detainees
began giving statements before the Federal Criminal Court of Posadas the day the
prosecutor's statement was published. [@1] The prosecutor's office said two more
people were being sought and that it would push a financial investigation to
seize the organization's assets. [@1]

The magistrate ordered searches of six addresses in Posadas, Gobernador Roca,
Jardín América, Puerto Rico, Colonia Polana and Colonia Gisela. [@2] Those
searches turned up nine vehicles, two boats and three firearms, among other
property. [@2] Perfil reported separately that the money, the two boats and the
firearms were confiscated in the raids. [@3] Gendarmes had spotted the hidden
truck on the night of Monday 5 October, and carried out the searches in daylight
the next day. [@1] The farm belonged to the brother of the man identified as the
organization's leader, and the prosecutor's office said tapped calls linked him
to a Paraguayan supplier who asked for a place to hide the truck. [@1] The
investigation ran for a year and included transcribing more than 230 CDs of
tapped calls, slowed by COVID-19 health measures and a shortage of staff for the
wiretaps. [@1]

Investigators say the organization bought properties on the Paraná riverbank
with their own ports to bring the marijuana into Argentina by boat from
Paraguay, then move it overland. [@2] Prosecutors are pursuing aggravated drug
trafficking, criminal association and money-laundering charges, noting the
suspects held no registered jobs but owned high-end cars and strategic
properties. [@1]
