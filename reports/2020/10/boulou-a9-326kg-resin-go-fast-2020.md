On the night of 28 October 2020, the SPAFT Le Perthus and the CCLJN carried out
the operation that intercepted a go-fast on the A9. [@1] At about 23:00 the SUV,
which had come from the Perthus, was approaching Le Boulou when the department
was still under a 21:00 curfew. [@1] The vehicle was ultimately intercepted at
the aire du village catalan, the A9 service area on the commune of
Banyuls-dels-Aspres. [@1]

Border police then arrested the driver and his passenger and seized 326
kilograms of cannabis resin. [@1]
