Cabo Verde's Polícia Judiciária, the judicial police, dismantled several
cannabis plantations in various localities in the interior of Santiago Island on
Friday 30 October 2020. [@3] The operation was run by SCITE, the Central Section
for Investigation of Drug Trafficking. [@2] About 120 officers took part in the
field operation, including members of the Armed Forces supporting the judicial
police. [@3] The judicial police reported no information on detentions. [@1]

The judicial police reported seizing 11,728 kg of cannabis during the operation.
[@3] The cannabis was burned where it was found, on the order of the Public
Prosecutor's Office, the Ministério Público. [@2] The judicial police said the
operation took place as part of a plan to prevent and combat domestic drug
trafficking. [@2]

The 30 October 2020 operation was the first of four phases of Operação Verde
2020, which ran on 30 October and 3, 13 and 17 November 2020. [@4] Police called
the four-phase campaign the largest cannabis seizure the judicial police had
ever made. [@4]
