Fijian police uprooted close to 1,000 plants believed to be marijuana from four
different farms on Kadavu last Saturday. [@1] The raids were conducted in Vabea,
Ono, Tabugunu Settlement in Nakasaleka, Levuka, Yale and Naceva. [@1] Police
spokesperson Ana Naisoro said the plants ranged from 5cm to more than 2m. [@1]
She said they now had in custody a farmer from Levuka, Yale, who was arrested
following a raid on a farm allegedly belonging to him. [@1] She said more than
570 plants believed to be marijuana were seized from that farm. [@1] Three
hundred plants believed to be marijuana were seized from a farm in Nakasaleka,
while the rest of the alleged illicit plants were seized in Vabea and a
settlement in Naceva. [@1]

Close to 1,000 plants believed to be marijuana were uprooted from four different
