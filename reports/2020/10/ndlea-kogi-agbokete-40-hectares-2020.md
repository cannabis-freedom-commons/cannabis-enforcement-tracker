Officers of the National Drug Law Enforcement Agency (NDLEA) in Kogi State, joined by men of the Nigeria Security and Civil Defence Corps (NSCDC), stormed cannabis farms at Kara forest in Agba-Okete community, Igalamela-Odolu Local Government Area, and destroyed more than 40 hectares of cannabis sativa spreading over about four kilometres. [@1] The state commander led more than 80 officers in the operation, which he described as the largest cannabis plantation ever discovered in the state, and said the destroyed crop was worth over N1.5 billion. [@1,2]

The officers arrested 29-year-old Ameh Joseph, who admitted owning a section of the farms and said he had turned to cannabis cultivation after his legitimate business collapsed. [@1] Joseph said he had previously worked as a farm manager in charge of cannabis sativa at Ogbese in Ondo State on a yearly salary of between N85,000 and N100,000. [@1]

## A community of growers

The commander said intelligence showed an entire community in the eastern part of the state where cannabis cultivation was the major source of livelihood, a discovery that shocked him because Kogi had been assumed to be only a transit point for the drug. [@1] He said Joseph, the prime suspect and owner of one of the farms, would soon be charged to court while efforts continued to arrest the owners of the other farms, and he warned growers to desist. [@1,2]
