National Crime Agency officers raided a former nightclub on Trinity Street, Coventry, on Thursday 15 October 2020, smashing through several reinforced doors to reach the grow. [@1,2] Inside they found around 1,000 cannabis plants spread across the building's three floors, which NCA experts valued at more than £1 million. [@1,2] Officers also recovered sophisticated planting, growing and irrigation equipment valued at around £150,000. [@1,2]

## Three arrested

Three men were arrested, two of them detained with the help of a West Midlands Police drone unit as they tried to flee the building. [@1,2] All three were charged with production of a class B drug. [@1,2] Albanian national Andi Qani, 24, and Vietnamese national Cao Van Vu, 31, appeared before Coventry magistrates and were remanded until a hearing at Warwick Crown Court on 12 November. [@1,2] The third man, Albanian national Adrian Lieshaj, 28, was wanted by Germany for robbery offences under a European Arrest Warrant. [@1,2]
