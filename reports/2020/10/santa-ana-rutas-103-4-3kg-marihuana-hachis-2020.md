Gendarmes of Escuadrón Núcleo 50 “Posadas”, supported by the Grupo Operativo de
Investigaciones y Procedimientos posted on secondary roads, were checking
Provincial Routes 103 and 4 near Santa Ana, Misiones, when they spotted a
motorcycle carrying two people who fled at high speed on seeing the officers.
[@1] During the chase the two riders threw away a black backpack that gave off a
strong marijuana-like smell when the gendarmes picked it up. [@1] The two people
on the motorcycle escaped during the pursuit. [@1]

In the presence of witnesses, officers opened the bag and found four
ochre-coloured rectangular packages and a fifth package wrapped in black nylon.
[@1] After the Federal Prosecutor's Office of Posadas ordered the items moved to
the unit, criminalistics officers carried out Narcotest field tests that
identified the contents as cannabis sativa with a total weight of 3 kilos 001
grams of marijuana and 0.950 grams of hashish. [@1]

The intervening magistrate ordered the seizure of the drugs and the other items
of interest. [@1]
