The National Narcotics Board of Aceh province, with soldiers of the Indonesian military and police officers, destroyed about 3.5 hectares of cannabis fields in the hills of Mount Seulawah, in Seulimeum district of Aceh Besar, on 22 October 2020. [@1] Officers found about 15,000 ready-to-harvest stems and burned the plants on the mountain slopes. [@1] The head of BNN Aceh, Heru Pranoto, took part in the operation alongside the joint personnel. [@1]

The plantation's owner was not caught during the raid, and the report names no suspects or arrests. [@1] ANTARA's photographers documented officers piling up the uprooted plants before they were set on fire. [@1]
