Police of Bamako's 12th arrondissement arrested the driver of a semi-trailer
truck and one of his suppliers on 6 October 2020 in Titibougou, on the road to
Koulikoro, and seized 122 kg of cannabis herb. [@1] The divisionnaire, Hamadou
Ag Elmédi, led the officers who seized the drugs and the two men. [@1]

A tyre had burst under pressure and scattered cannabis along the road. [@1] The
search found 32 bricks of cannabis hidden in a second tyre and 18 kg loose on
the roadside after the first tyre exploded. [@1] Under questioning the driver
named his supplier, who was also detained. [@1]

The articles say the cannabis was bound for sale in Dakar, Senegal, and that
investigations continued into the rest of the network. [@1] The divisionnaire
credited residents with the information behind the operation and said the
suspects were not on their first run. [@1] Icimali reported that the two men
were held in custody at the station pending transfer to the courts. [@2] The
same account said the suspects were on their seventh run, estimating the load by
comparison with the earlier shipments. [@2] The articles note that Mali had no
equipment at the time to detect drugs concealed in vehicle tyres. [@1]
