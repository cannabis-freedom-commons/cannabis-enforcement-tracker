Honduras's National Police anti-drug unit eradicated an illicit plantation it
described as supposed marijuana in the village of Aguacatal in the municipality
of Ojojona. [@1] One person was detained during the operation. [@1]

The police described the plants only as supposed marijuana, without reporting
any laboratory confirmation or test result. [@1] The named site was aldea
Aguacatal, inside the municipality of Ojojona in the department of Francisco
Morazán. [@1]

The account was published by the Policía Nacional de Honduras on its official
Facebook page. [@1] The publication is timestamped Friday, 9 October 2020 at
5:20 AM, and no separate date for the field operation was given. [@1]
