Honduran prosecutors and security forces found and destroyed a marijuana crop in
a hill area next to Las Mangas, in Tocoa, Colón department. [@1] The eradication
was coordinated by the instruction section of the Bajo Aguán regional
prosecution service (FRBA) with the National Anti-Drug Police Directorate (DNPA)
and the Xatruch Joint Task Force. [@1]

At the end of the inspection the authorities counted about 2,500 marijuana
plants sown over an area of roughly one manzana, a local land measure of about
0.7 hectares. [@1] The plot lies inside the Botaderos National Park. [@1]
Besides the plants, officers found a sack holding two pounds of seeds of
suspected marijuana. [@1]

Prosecutors described the destruction as a neutralising measure against the
effects of the crime. [@1]
