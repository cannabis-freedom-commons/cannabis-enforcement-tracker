The suspected drug traffickers, all from the same family, were presented to the
press on 24 June 2020 in Douala. [@1, 2] A family cannabis-selling network supplied
from the North-West region was arrested that Wednesday in Douala. [@1, 2] Four
people aged between 20 and 40 were arrested and transferred to the Douala
Tribunal de Grande Instance. [@1, 2]

Officers of the Littoral gendarmerie legion acted after receiving information
about cannabis-selling sites in the Texaco Aéroport quarter and a telephone
number for contacting the suspected dealers. [@1] The contacts indicated the
transaction location, and the officers located the indicated premises at around
4 p.m. [@1] The operation seized 548 kg of cannabis from the family network.
[@1]

The second account says two of the men had sealed a deal with undercover
gendarmerie agents on 11 May before an intervention unit surprised them
preparing the packages and seized 29 bags. [@2] It says the two others then
arrived with a 1 million CFA franc bribe to free them and recover the stock.
[@1, 2] According to their statements, the head of the trafficking network is their
father, who was being sought by the gendarmerie. [@1] Questioning traced
supplies to the North-West region. [@1, 2] A woman identified as
Mbouta Brigitte, described as a receiver, was transferred that day to the CED in
Yaoundé. [@1] The 1 million CFA francs offered as a bribe was paid into the
public treasury. [@1] The same account says two further Littoral legion
operations that month seized another 32 kg in Douala, putting the month's total
at 581.28 kg. [@2]
