Italian and Albanian police arrested 37 members of an organised criminal group trafficking large quantities of drugs from Albania to Italy in simultaneous raids on 30 June 2020, coordinated by Eurojust. [@1,2] The operation in Italy was run by the Anti-Mafia Investigation Direction of Bari with the Carabinieri, state police and Guardia di Finanza under the Bari anti-mafia prosecutor, while Albania's special anti-corruption prosecutor and police concluded their side. [@1,2]

The suspects, of Italian and Albanian origin, had smuggled drugs by leisure boat and then overland, mainly into the Bari, Puglia and Basilicata regions. [@1,2] The investigation seized 3.5 tonnes of drugs, described as marijuana, cocaine and hashish together and estimated with other drugs at over 40 million euros, alongside assets in Albania worth over 4 million euros including factories, businesses, apartments, cars and a boat. [@1,2] The joint investigation team behind the case was set up in 2017, and some suspects had already been convicted by an Italian court in 2019. [@1,2]
