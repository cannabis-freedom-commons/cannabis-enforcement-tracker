Several people described as suspected criminals had also been arrested by the
time of the public presentation. [@1] The national gendarmerie presented seized
arms and drugs at its Klessoum Legion in N'Djamena on a Friday. [@1] Its
national spokesman, Abakar Abdramane Haggar, listed 54 weapons of different
calibres, two sacks of cannabis, several drug balls, a sack of adulterated
drinks, a Turaya satellite communication device and 189 tear-gas grenade
detonators. [@1]

The two sacks were identified as cannabis, while the additional balls were
described only as drugs. [@1] The account names an armed robber caught at night
with a homemade weapon in N'Djamena's 7th arrondissement and another man
arrested at an inn entrance. [@1]

Toumaï Web Médias says Colonel Abakar Abdraman Haggar made the presentation in
the presence of Gendarmerie Director General Djontan Marcel Hoïnati and deputy
prosecutor Wambel Assoucia Ngueli. [@2]
