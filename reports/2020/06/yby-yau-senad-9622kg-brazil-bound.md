SENAD agents raided a rural property in Yby Yaú on 19 June 2020 and destroyed
more than 9 tonnes of marijuana destined for Brazil. [@1] At the property
officers seized 122 kg of chopped marijuana in 14 bags and 500 kg of chopped
marijuana hidden underground, along with 7 kg of seeds. [@1] They also destroyed
3 hectares of cannabis crops, which the agency equated with 9,000 kg of the
drug. [@1]

SENAD said the operation amounted to the elimination of 9,622 kg of marijuana
presumably prepared for the Brazilian market. [@1] Paraguayan justice ordered
the destroyed plantations burned. [@1]

On 18 June, a joint operation of the Paraguayan Navy and SENAD detained a minor
who intended to take 65 kg of marijuana to Brazil. [@1] The minor was apparently
waiting for a vessel to move the marijuana across the Paraná river to Brazil.
[@1] Part of the 65 kg was pressed marijuana and the rest chopped cannabis
vacuum-packed. [@1] The boy was caught during a Navy patrol of the riverside
area of the San Miguel neighbourhood in Ciudad del Este. [@3] La Clave reported
that prosecutor Elvio Aguilera ordered the drugs seized and the minor detained.
[@3]

On 14 June, anti-drug officers seized a marijuana shipment at another rural
property in Yby Yaú that was also being prepared for shipment to the
neighbouring country. [@1] In a wooded area the officers saw a group of people
who immediately fired shots at the delegation and then fled. [@1] Officers found
906 kg of pressed marijuana distributed in 39 bags. [@1] The 14 June operation
was carried out by special SENAD agents based in Concepción, led by prosecutor
Celso Morales. [@2] The property was in the Villalba Kue locality of Yby Yaú
district. [@2] Officers also seized two motorcycles and a bicycle. [@2] The
prosecutor ordered the evidence moved to the Public Ministry's deposit. [@2]
