In May and June 2020 the gendarmerie unit of Antsiranana, which oversees the
Analabe Ambanja region, carried out an operation against cannabis growing and
trafficking. [@1]

The operation seized more than 16 tonnes of cannabis and 21 litres of cannabis
oil. [@1] Gendarmes destroyed more than 80,000 individual cannabis plants. [@3]
The operation led to the arrest of 80 people. [@2]

Madagascar's Gendarmerie Nationale as a whole seized close to 53 tonnes of
cannabis in 2020, including several seizures of more than one tonne. [@1] The
Analabe region, in Ambanja district, is one of Madagascar's main
cannabis-growing areas, and cultivation, sale and consumption of cannabis remain
strictly illegal in the country. [@1] A gendarmerie officer in Ambanja said
gendarmes rarely enter the forest because traffickers there are armed with
Kalashnikovs and shoot without warning. [@1]
