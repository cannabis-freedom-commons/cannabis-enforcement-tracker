A sixty-strong joint police force arrested two suspected cannabis farmers and destroyed seventeen farms covering about thirty acres in the Tome mountains between Peki and Boso. [@1] The team, drawn from the national Drug Law Enforcement Unit, SWAT, the regional CID in Ho and the Peki divisional command and led by unit director Superintendent David Selorm Hukporti, acted on gathered intelligence after picking up the first suspect and then the second. [@1]

Officers assisted by drones found an enclave of hundreds of acres in which each plantation was demarcated with rows of corn and dotted with hamlets sheltering the growers; they managed to destroy only about a fifth before dark and placed the area under surveillance for a return. [@1,2] The Gyaasehene of Boso, Nana Adu Kwaku II, disputed reports linking the farms to Boso, saying officers had passed through Boso only because no route existed from the neighbouring towns where the farms lie. [@2]
