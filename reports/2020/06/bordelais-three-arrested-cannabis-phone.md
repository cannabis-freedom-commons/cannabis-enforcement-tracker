Three men were arrested after allegedly attempting to introduce prohibited
articles into the Bordelais Correctional Facility in Dennery. [@1] Police said
the arrests took place at about 3:00 a.m. on 16 June 2020. [@1] Acting Corporal
Ann Joseph, the public relations officer of the Royal Saint Lucia Police Force,
disclosed the arrests at a press conference. [@1]

The men were in police custody and were awaiting charges at the time of the
announcement. [@1] Police said the men were being held at the Dennery police
station while still in custody awaiting charges. [@1] The prohibited articles
included a substance believed to be cannabis, a cellphone and other
paraphernalia, according to police. [@1]

Joseph said the three were caught as they were attempting to introduce those
articles into the Bordelais facility. [@1] The Royal Saint Lucia Police Force
addressed the case through its public relations officer at the press conference.
[@1] The Bordelais facility in Dennery is the correctional institution the men
allegedly targeted, and Dennery police handled their detention after the
arrests. [@1]
