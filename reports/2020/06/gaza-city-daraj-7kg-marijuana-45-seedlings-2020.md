Gaza's anti-drug police seized seven kilograms of marijuana, 45 seedlings and
1,500 seeds in an ambush in the Al-Sidra area of Al-Daraj, east of Gaza City.
[@2] The force detained three drug traders who were carrying the seedlings and
seeds, and said they were wanted by the anti-drug department over cases of drug
dealing and trafficking. [@1] The announcement was carried as a statement
distributed by the Interior Ministry in Gaza, and the detainees were described
as wanted in drug dealing and trafficking cases. [@4]

The head of the anti-drug branch in the North Gaza governorate, Lieutenant
Colonel Aayed Saleh, gave the account in a brief statement. [@1]

The case and the seized material were referred to the competent authorities to
complete legal procedures. [@1] The Interior Ministry in Gaza published the
statement and two photographs of the seized material. [@2] Palestine Now
published the same figure of seven kilograms with 45 seedlings and 1,500 seeds,
placed in Al-Sidra, Al-Daraj, east of Gaza City. [@3]
