Police in Nikšić arrested 30-year-old resident Miloš Đurković after seizing
about 30 kilograms of marijuana, firearms and ammunition during a search. [@2]
Officers from the Police Directorate carried out the search in Vitalac under an
order from an investigative judge of the High Court in Podgorica. [@5] The
search covered Đurković's house and other premises he used in the Vitalac
neighbourhood of Nikšić. [@2] Police announced they had arrested the man and
seized about 30 kilograms of marijuana, an automatic rifle and a machine gun at
his Nikšić apartment. [@1]

The police statement specified that the confiscation included two magazines with
37 rounds of rifle ammunition and drug-packaging equipment, namely a
vacuum-sealing device. [@3] In the police announcement, the suspect was
identified only by his initials, M.Đ., aged 30, from Nikšić. [@5]

The competent state prosecutor was notified and ordered Đurković arrested on
suspicion of unauthorised production, possession and distribution of narcotic
drugs. [@3] Police brought the suspect before the competent prosecutor with a
criminal complaint. [@4] Đurković was brought to the Higher Prosecutor's Office
for questioning. [@2]
