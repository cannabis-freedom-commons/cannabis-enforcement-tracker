The driver of the minibus carrying the cannabis fled the Hihéatro checkpoint and
abandoned his vehicle there after officers detected his false declaration of
goods. [@1] Togolese customs, acting through the Office Togolais des Recettes
(OTR), seized 1.5 tonnes of cannabis at the Hihéatro control and customs post
during the night of 9 to 10 June 2020. [@1]

Hihéatro, where the checkpoint stands, is a locality in the Plateaux region
about 150 km from Lomé. [@1] Officers found the illicit cannabis aboard a
15-seat minibus that had come from Kpalimé and was bound for northern Togo. [@1]
The announced quantity of 1.5 tonnes equals 1,500 kilograms of cannabis seized
at the Hihéatro post in that single night operation. [@1] Customs officers said
the minibus had been travelling under a false declaration of merchandise. [@1]

Togolese customs presented the seizure as part of its intensified fight against
drug trafficking in the country. [@1] To strengthen that fight, the OTR appealed
to the public for assistance to help dismantle trafficking networks and end
trafficking. [@1]
