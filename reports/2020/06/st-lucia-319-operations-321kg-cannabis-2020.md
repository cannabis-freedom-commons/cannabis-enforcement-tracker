Acting Corporal Ann Joseph, public relations officer of the Royal Saint Lucia
Police Force, announced the results of the force's operations at a press
conference on 16 June 2020. [@1] Joseph said 24 people had been arrested in the
operations. [@1] The Drug Unit had led 319 operations in 2020 up to that point.
[@1] Officers executed 74 warrants on various premises, 37 of them during
special operations. [@1]

Police said a total of 321 kilograms of cannabis was seized in the operations.
[@1] Officers also seized 33 grams of cannabis resin. [@1] The same operations
produced 8.827 kilograms of cocaine and 6 grams of crack. [@1] The operations
resulted in the seizure of 504 articles of ammunition. [@1] Fifty firearms had
been recovered overall, 22 of them by the Drug Unit. [@1]

Joseph described the operations as joint efforts between the Drug Unit and other
departments and stations within the force. [@1]
