Gendarmes of the Núcleo section of Squadron 50 Posadas were patrolling the
riverside of Candelaria, in Argentina's Misiones province, in the early hours
when they saw a small boat on the Paraná and, nearby, a parked vehicle with its
doors open. [@1] The patrol took place in the early hours, with the release's
'ayer' (yesterday) placing it a day before the release's 25 November 2021 date.
[@1] The officers saw one person loading bundles who fled towards National Route
12 on noticing them. [@1]

A search backed by the narcotics detector dog Gringo found five black bundles
inside a culvert, giving off a strong smell of marijuana. [@1] With
authorisation from the Federal Prosecutor's Office in Posadas, the gendarmes
took the bundles and witnesses to their unit for Narcotest field tests and
weighing. [@1] The field tests were positive for cannabis sativa and the bundles
weighed 91 kilos 900 grams in total. [@1] No one was arrested; the person
loading the bundles had fled on foot towards the route. [@1]

The prosecutor's office directed the case file and the seizure of the drug under
Argentina's drug law 23.737, known as the Narcotics law. [@1] The Misiones
outlet Iguazú Ahora carried the force's account of the same patrol under its own
headline the same day, using the same weight of 91 kilos 900 grams. [@2] The
Chaco outlet Más Contenidos also reproduced the force's account on 25 November
2021, saying the bundles had been hidden in the culvert before the gendarmes
seized them. [@3]
