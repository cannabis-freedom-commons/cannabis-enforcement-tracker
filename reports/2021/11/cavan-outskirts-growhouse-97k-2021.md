Shortly after 9pm on 29 November 2021, gardaí from Cavan Garda Station
searched a house on the outskirts of Cavan town under warrant as part of
Operation Tara. [@1] Inside they found a quantity of suspected cannabis plants
valued at €97,600, along with the heaters, lights and extractor fans used for
the grow house, all of which were seized. [@1]

One man in his early 20s was arrested at the scene and detained at Cavan Garda
Station. [@1] He was later charged and due before Monaghan District Court on
the morning of 1 December 2021. [@1]

The seizure formed part of Operation Tara, the national anti-drugs strategy
launched by Garda Commissioner Drew Harris on 2 July 2021 to disrupt drug
trafficking networks. [@1]
