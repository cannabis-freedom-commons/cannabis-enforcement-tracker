Gardaí searching a vacant dwelling in the Ballymote area of County Sligo on
the afternoon of 20 November 2021 found a growhouse built inside the building
holding 85 cannabis plants at various stages of growth, valued at about
€68,000. [@1] Officers also seized suspected cannabis herb valued at €140,000,
bringing the total estimated value to €208,000, and said all of the drugs
would be sent for analysis. [@1]

Four men were arrested at the scene: two men in their 40s detained at
Ballymote Garda Station and two men in their 30s detained at Sligo Garda
Station, all held under Section 4 of the Criminal Justice Act 1984. [@1] One
of the men in his 40s was charged and due before a special sitting of
Carrick-on-Shannon District Court on 21 November, while the two younger men
were released without charge. [@2] A second man in his 40s was then charged
and appeared before Donegal District Court on 22 November. [@2]
