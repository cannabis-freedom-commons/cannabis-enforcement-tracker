Moroccan police seized 3.21 tonnes of cannabis near the city of Guelmim on
Saturday 13 November 2021. [@1,2] The drugs were hidden on an agricultural
farm in the town of Asboya, about 30 kilometres from Guelmim. [@1]

Five people aged between 34 and 47 were arrested during the operation over
their alleged links to an international drug-trafficking network, according to
a police statement. [@1,2] Officers also seized two inflatable boats and four
outboard engines alongside the drugs. [@1]

The suspects were placed in custody while investigations continued to find
everyone involved in the criminal activity. [@1] The police described the
operation as part of Morocco's efforts against cross-border crime and
international drug trafficking. [@1]
