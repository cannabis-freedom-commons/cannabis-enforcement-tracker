On 18 November 2021 troopers of the Oregon State Police Southwest Region Drug Enforcement Section served a search warrant on five industrial-sized warehouses on Antelope Road in White City, north of Medford, and seized roughly 500,000 pounds (about 227 tonnes) of processed marijuana. [@1,2] The two-day operation, assisted by county teams, the Medford police and the Drug Enforcement Administration, also turned up a firearm. [@2]

State police called it an epic amount of illegal processed marijuana and conservatively valued it at about $500 million depending on where it would have been exported. [@1,2] More than 100 people found at the site were detained, identified, interviewed and released, including migrant workers living there in poor conditions without running water. [@2]
