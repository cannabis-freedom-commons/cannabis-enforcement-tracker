Mexican naval personnel, Manzanillo customs officers and federal prosecutors
seized 516 kilograms of marijuana inside a container at the port of Manzanillo
on 10 November 2021. [@2] The navy's Sixth Naval Region and customs staff found
the load during a routine inspection carried out with sniffer dogs. [@2] The
container was bound for the Dominican Republic. [@1] The marijuana was packed on
six pallets, ten rectangular packages per pallet, for 60 packages in all. [@2]

Mexican prosecutors, the Fiscalía General de la República (FGR), and the
Secretariat of the Navy (Semar) said the container was for the Caribbean island,
at the far end of the Pacific-facing port area. [@1] A ministerial weighing
determined the load weighed 516 kilograms. [@3] Naval personnel found the drug
working with Manzanillo customs workers and the FGR. [@1] The marijuana was
placed at the disposal of ministerial authorities. [@1]

The two agencies described the seizure as part of the Navy's surveillance
operations to curb criminal activity and maintain the rule of law in Mexico.
[@2]
