Acting on intelligence, NDLEA personnel stormed a storage facility at Uzzeba
in Owas West Local Government Area, as the reports spell it, on 23 November
2021 and evacuated 107 bags of suspected cannabis weighing 1,240 kg. [@1,2]
The next day the command found
another concealed warehouse at Uhiere village in Ovia North East Local
Government Area and removed 315 bags weighing 4,252 kg. [@1,2]

Together the two raids took 5,492 kg of suspected cannabis off the streets
within two days, which state commander Buba Wakawa called a major seizure for
the command in November. [@1,2] A 21-year-old suspect was arrested during the
operations. [@1,2]

Wakawa said November seizures across the state totalled 6,477.86 kg, with 22
suspects arrested, ten convictions secured and nine new cases filed at the
Federal High Court in Benin. [@1,2]
