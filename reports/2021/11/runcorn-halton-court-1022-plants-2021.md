Cheshire police found 1,022 cannabis plants growing inside the former Halton Magistrates' Court on Northway in Runcorn at around noon on Thursday 25 November 2021. [@1] The court closed in 2017 and stands next to Runcorn police station. [@1,2] Officers had first reported a farm at business premises in the Halton Lea area before confirming the court building. [@1]

A 20-year-old man was charged with producing a Class B drug over the grow. [@1,2] He appeared at magistrates' court on 29 November and was remanded to appear at Chester Crown Court on 23 December. [@1]
