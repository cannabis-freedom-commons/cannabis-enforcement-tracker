Police in Chimoio, the provincial capital of Manica, detained a 42-year-old man.
[@1] The detainee has dual Mozambican and Tanzanian nationality. [@1] He comes
from Palma in Cabo Delgado. [@2] Officers of the Polícia da República de
Moçambique (PRM), Mozambique's national police, made the arrest. [@1] He was
accused of the crime of drug trafficking. [@1] The arrest took place on the
preceding weekend. [@1] Police detained him after tip-offs given by his
accomplices. [@1]

He was found in possession of cocaine and cannabis sativa, known locally as
suruma. [@1] Manica police spokesperson Mateus Mindú announced the seizure but
did not specify the quantity of drugs seized. [@1] The second account likewise
says he was arrested with cocaine and cannabis sativa and that the amount was
not specified. [@2]

Police believe the drugs came from abroad and are trying to locate other
possible members of the group. [@1] The aim is to dismantle a possible organised
crime network operating in Manica. [@1] Mozambique continues to be described as
an international drug corridor carrying drugs mostly from Asia to Europe and
South Africa. [@1]
