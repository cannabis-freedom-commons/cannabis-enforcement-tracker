Mexico's navy led the seizure of almost 300 kilograms of marijuana in the port
of Manzanillo bound for Costa Rica. [@1] The find occurred during a routine
inspection in the Manzanillo port facility in Colima with customs and federal
prosecution agents taking part. [@1]

Sniffer dogs alerted officers to the container, which was embarked for Costa
Rica. [@1] Inside officers found 10 pallets holding 516 rectangular packages.
[@2] The packages were wrapped in tan tape and marked on the outside with the
words Qukis and Tiburón. [@1] A preliminary weighing put the load at about 288.8
kilograms of marijuana, which was placed at the disposal of ministerial
authorities to build the investigations. [@1]

Authorities said traffickers reused the same pallet concealment method even
though it had been detected the previous week. [@1] The navy described the
action as part of its surveillance operations to inhibit crime and uphold the
rule of law. [@2]
