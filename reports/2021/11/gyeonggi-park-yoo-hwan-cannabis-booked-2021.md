Actor Park Yoo-hwan, 30 and the younger brother of singer-actor Park Yoo-chun,
was booked without detention by the Gyeonggi Nambu Provincial Police Agency on a
charge of violating the Act on the Control of Narcotics. [@2] Police say he
smoked marijuana once with two other Koreans at a restaurant in Bangkok,
Thailand, in December 2020, while he was there to make a guest appearance at his
brother's concert. [@2] All three people admitted the allegation, and Park
Yoo-hwan was due to be summoned for questioning. [@2] A police officer said the
case was under investigation and gave no further detail on the charge. [@2]

The news that police were investigating Park Yoo-hwan for marijuana use was
reported on 3 November 2021. [@1]

On 28 January 2022 the Gyeonggi Nambu Provincial Police Agency said it had sent
Park Yoo-hwan to prosecutors without custody on the same charge. [@3] One of the
two Koreans who smoked with him was also sent to prosecutors without custody.
[@3] The other, who lives in Thailand, could not travel to Korea because of the
COVID-19 pandemic and was to be questioned by police once he arrived. [@3]
Soompi's English account of the same referral named the Narcotics Unit of the
Gyeonggi Nambu Provincial Police Agency as the body that handled it. [@4]

Park Yoo-hwan did not release music during the investigation, but his brother
put out a three-track mini album, Da Capo, on 5 November 2021 and drew online
criticism for the timing. [@1]
