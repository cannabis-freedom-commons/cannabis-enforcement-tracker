Police from the Northern Division, headed by acting Deputy Divisional Commander
Superintendent Adrian Broomes, destroyed narcotics at the Portvale Sugar Factory
in St James on Saturday 13 November 2021. [@1]

The narcotics burned included 14,110 marijuana trees, ranging in height from
seedlings to 12 feet. [@1] The load also included 1,088 grams of cocaine, which
is outside this tracker's cannabis scope and is not counted in its figures. [@1]
Police put the total weight of the destroyed narcotics at 638 kilograms, or 289
pounds. [@1]

Police said the drugs had been recovered through operations across the whole of
the island's northern districts. [@1] Members of the media and an independent
witness watched the destruction process. [@1]
