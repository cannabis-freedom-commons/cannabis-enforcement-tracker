Chilean investigators from the Policía de Investigaciones, the maritime
intelligence department of DIRECTEMAR, Chile's maritime authority, and the
National Customs Service seized 166 kilograms of "creepy" marijuana in a joint
operation announced on 19 November 2021. [@1] The same operation also found more
than six kilograms of cocaine, a separate load that is not counted in the
cannabis figure. [@1] Officers found the drugs hidden in shipping containers
whose declared cargo was bananas exported from Ecuador. [@1]

Investigators had identified Ecuadorian nationals as the people of interest,
suspected of sending drug consignments from Ecuador in containers. [@1] The
consignments were routed through the ports of San Antonio and Valparaíso, and
the final destination of the merchandise was the market of the Región
Metropolitana around Santiago. [@1] The agencies said intelligence work and
surveillance of the criminal organisation gave them the arrival and transfer
dates of the merchandise, which they used to follow it and later detain those
involved. [@1]

The Navy's own news service published the same account and a gallery of five
photographs of the operation under the same 19 November 2021 date. [@2]
Valparaíso outlet G5 Noticias republished the authorities' statement on the
evening of 19 November 2021 with the same figures and the same account of the
later detention of those involved. [@3] DIRECTEMAR heads Chile's Maritime
Authority, the body that polices and inspects shipping under the Navy, and its
maritime police department worked alongside the investigative police and customs
in this case. [@1]
