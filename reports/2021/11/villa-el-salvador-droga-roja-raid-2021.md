Officers of the Terna squad of Peru's National Police entered a property in
Villa El Salvador, a district in southern Lima, where different types of drugs
were stored. [@1] The agents moved in after residents of Avenida Jorge Chávez,
sector 26, gave them information about the property, and they blended into the
area before the intervention. [@2] Inside the building police detained Gabriel
Renzo Gómez, a 40-year-old Colombian national. [@1]

The drugs found in the property included marijuana, cocaine hydrochloride and
coca paste. [@1] Officers said the red drug was known locally as "la
coloradita". [@1]

Police said the drugs were sold by delivery on motorcycles. [@2] The detained
man remained in custody and was to answer before the courts. [@2]
