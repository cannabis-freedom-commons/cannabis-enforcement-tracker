The Sûreté Départementale, the departmental police service, received information
that an individual was producing and selling cannabis herb from a home in the
Port Blanc area of Le Gosier. [@2] Police described the individual implicated as
very unfavourably known to their services. [@2] At 10 a.m. on Tuesday 15 June
2021, officers from the narcotics brigade entered the Port Blanc residence with
assistance from elite officers of the Raid. [@2]

The search uncovered and led to the seizure of 141 cannabis plants that were
being grown at the property. [@1] The plants were cultivated in both “in door”
and “out door” settings, meaning indoor and outdoor growing at the residence.
[@1] Officers also seized the indoor growing installations found on the
premises. [@1] They also found ammunition cartridges at the property, identified
by their 9 mm and 7.65 mm calibres. [@1]

The single person implicated in the case was due to be tried before the judicial
court in Pointe-à-Pitre. [@1]
