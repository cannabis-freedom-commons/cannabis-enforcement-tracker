Prison officer Rudolph Rambaran pleaded guilty to smuggling contraband into HMP
Northward, Grand Cayman, and was due to be sentenced on 7 March 2022. [@1] He
admitted one count of smuggling contraband and two counts of possession of
ganja. [@1] Rudolph Rambaran was 39, a Jamaican officer who lives in West Bay.
[@2]

Prison staff were tipped off that he was bringing contraband into the jail, and
a sting operation was set up to catch him in the act. [@1] On the morning of 9
June 2021, he was stopped as he arrived for his shift and taken to a room to be
searched. [@1] He told officers he had two packages of ganja in the back pockets
of his trousers. [@1] Officers found two blue packages of vegetable matter
suspected to be ganja with a combined weight of 9.13 ounces, about 259 grams.
[@1] He also admitted to having another 6.29 ounces of ganja, about 178 grams,
and two mobile phones in his car. [@1] Asked who the ganja was for, he told
officers it was for a prisoner, Jeremy Parchman. [@1] He told officers he had
fallen on difficult financial times and had children to maintain in Jamaica as
well as personal expenses in Cayman. [@1]

He said he had offended in a very awful way and had brought the Prison Service,
his colleagues and himself utter disgrace and embarrassment. [@1] After his
arrest at the jail he was suspended from duty but remained on bail. [@2]
