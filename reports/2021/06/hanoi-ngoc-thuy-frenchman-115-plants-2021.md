Police in Hanoi's Long Bien District found a French national renting a 3,000-square-metre plot on a Red River islet to grow cannabis. [@1,2] Frederic Tiberghien, 55, worked for a company in the northern Yen Bai Province, according to the district police. [@1,2]

Officers discovered the plot on Saturday 5 June 2021 on the islet in the river section running through Ngoc Thuy Ward. [@1,2] They seized 115 cannabis plants weighing around 34 kilograms, along with around 40 grams of dried cannabis. [@1,2] An investigation is under way. [@1,2]
