Afghanistan's Ministry of Interior announced on Tuesday 22 June 2021 that it had
arrested a smuggler with seven kilos and 200 grams of hashish in Kabul. [@1] The
ministry said the smuggler was detained in the limits of Kabul's eighth police
district on Monday night, that is on 21 June. [@1] In the same brief statement,
the ministry said police also recovered a vehicle from the detainee. [@1]
