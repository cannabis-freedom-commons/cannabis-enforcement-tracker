Central Hastings OPP officers with the Provincial Joint Forces Cannabis
Enforcement Team executed a search warrant at a rural property on Cooke Road
in Stirling-Rawdon at about 9:45 a.m. on 3 June 2021, after a lengthy
investigation. [@1, 2] Officers seized 2,000 cannabis plants, which police
estimated were worth about $1.3 million. [@2, 3]

Police arrested Phuc Dau Mac, 66, of Markham, and Wei Kuan Lee, 59, of
Toronto. [@2] Both were charged with growing illicit cannabis, growing
cannabis somewhere other than their own dwelling, and possession of cannabis
for the purpose of distributing; they were released on bail to appear in a
Belleville court on 9 August. [@1, 2, 3]
