Wellington OPP's Community Street Crime Unit and uniformed officers, with the
OPP Organized Crime Enforcement Bureau and the Joint Forces Cannabis
Enforcement Team, executed a search warrant at a commercial building at 10
Wells Street West in Arthur on the morning of 4 June 2021. [@1] Officers
seized over 7,000 marijuana plants and a large amount of Canadian
currency. [@1, 3] County dump trucks hauled seized plants and growing
equipment from the site, with an officer noting the transport was needed
because police vehicles were too small. [@1]

Four men were arrested at the scene. [@1] They were later named as Engang Ex,
43, of Scarborough; Chenguang Li, 47, of Scarborough; Lifan Li, 23, of
Markham; and Hai Lin, 29, of Markham, charged with growing cannabis somewhere
other than their dwelling, possession for the purpose of distributing,
possession for the purpose of selling, and possession of proceeds of crime
over $5,000. [@2] They were released to appear in Guelph court at a later
date. [@2]

The building had been the subject of a March rezoning application to permit
indoor cannabis cultivation and a processing facility, opposed by neighbours
over odour and water use; police would not say whether township staff who
visited the property on 31 May were accompanied by officers. [@1, 2]
