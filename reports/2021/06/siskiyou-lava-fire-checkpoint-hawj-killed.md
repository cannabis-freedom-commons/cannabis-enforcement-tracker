Soobleej Kaub Hawj, 35, of Kansas City, Kansas, was driving a pickup loaded
with 132 pounds (about 60 kg) of cannabis and firearms when he ignored orders
to turn west onto County Road A-12 at a checkpoint near Big Springs on the
night of 24 June 2021 [@1,2]. Officers were evacuating residents as the
lightning-sparked Lava Fire threatened the rural area near the
California-Oregon border, close to thousands of unlicensed greenhouses [@1,2].

## Conflicting accounts

Officers say Hawj panicked and fired at them, and that they returned fire,
hitting him in the head, chest, arms and legs; they reported finding a loaded
.45-calibre pistol on his lap and more rifles afterwards [@1]. Witnesses gave
conflicting accounts, said more than 60 shots were fired, and noted that
dash-cam footage was not released, prompting a national outcry over possible
anti-Asian bias [@1].

## Officers cleared

District Attorney Kirk Andrus cleared the four officers, from the sheriff's
office, Etna police and Fish and Wildlife, in a nine-page letter on 14 June
2022, finding the shooting legally justified [@1,2]. He wrote that the
checkpoint existed only to move people from the flames, not to search for
marijuana, and that Hawj may wrongly have believed evacuees were being
searched [@1]. Hawj also faced an arrest warrant in Mesa County, Colorado, on
a cannabis and gun charge [@1].
