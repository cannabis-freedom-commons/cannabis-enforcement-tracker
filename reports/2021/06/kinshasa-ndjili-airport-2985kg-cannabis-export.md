Customs officers of the DGDA's Kin-Aéro brigade seized 476 cartons of cannabis
biomass being readied for export without a declaration at N'djili international
airport in Kinshasa on 11 June 2021. [@1] The lot weighed 2,985 kilograms, and
part of it was already on pallets for loading. [@1] The shipment was bound for
Macedonia, which is now North Macedonia. [@2] Le Phare reported that part of the
consignment was on pallets for loading aboard an Ethiopian Airways scheduled
flight. [@2]

According to the DGDA's seizure report, the export documents covered a larger
cargo of 169 packages weighing 21,832 kilograms, and the exporter was named as
the pharmaceutical firm Instadose Pharma DRC SARL. [@1] The DGDA said the amount
that reached the airport was only part of a larger convoy intended for export.
[@1] The firm held two authorisations from the health ministry, one for
industrial cultivation and one for industrial production and marketing of
narcotics and psychotropic substances for medical purposes. [@1] Because the
holders of the goods showed no export authorisation, customs drew up a formal
statement of customs offence. [@1] AfricaNews RDC quoted DGDA Kin-Aéro deputy
director Levy Bwana Kitoko saying the two health ministry authorisations did not
give Instadose the right to export the cannabis biomass. [@3] AfricaNews RDC
stated a price of about 17,000 US dollars per kilogram, without saying whose
estimate it was. [@3]

The seized shipment was stored in the installations of the Bolloré customs
clearance area. [@1] None of the accounts reports the arrest of an Instadose
Pharma DRC SARL official. [@2]
