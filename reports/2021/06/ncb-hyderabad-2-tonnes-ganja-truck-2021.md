Officers of the Narcotics Control Bureau's (NCB) Bengaluru zonal unit and
Hyderabad sub-zone intercepted a truck carrying ganja near the Pedda Amberpet
toll plaza in Hyderabad, acting on an intelligence tip received on Sunday.
[@1,2] They arrested four men from Osmanabad district in Maharashtra,
including the truck driver. [@1,2]

The marijuana, weighing more than 2,000 kg, was concealed between raw cashew
nut shells in about 1,080 packets of roughly 2 kg each. [@1,2] NCB officials
called it the Bengaluru zone's biggest seizure and valued the haul at about
Rs 15 crore. [@1,2]

According to the NCB, the ganja came from the Andhra-Odisha border area,
where it is grown illegally in an area affected by Naxalite insurgents, and
the consignment was bound for Pune and Mumbai. [@1,2] Officials said a wide
peddling network distributes the drug across Indian states and smuggles it
overseas to Sri Lanka. [@1,2] The seizure was made under the Narcotic Drugs
and Psychotropic Substances Act, 1985. [@1,2]
