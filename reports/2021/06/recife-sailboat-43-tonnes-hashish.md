Brazil's Federal Police, acting on intelligence shared with French Customs, MAOC-N and the British Virgin Islands authorities, learned that a sailboat coming from Portugal carried a large load of hashish. [@1] On the night of Wednesday 16 June 2021, the Navy's ocean patrol ship Araguari intercepted the vessel in international waters about 230 nautical miles off Recife, with two federal officers aboard making the arrests. [@1,2]

The vessel was escorted to Recife, arriving on the morning of Friday 18 June, and the two crew, one Russian and one Estonian, were taken to the Federal Police headquarters in the Bairro do Recife. [@1,2] Forensic weighing put the haul at 4,304 kilograms of hashish. [@1] The Federal Police called it the biggest hashish seizure ever made in the country and evidence of a transcontinental maritime trafficking route to Brazil; more than 100 officers from both forces took part in the operation. [@2,1]
