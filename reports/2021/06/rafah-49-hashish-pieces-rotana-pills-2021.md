Anti-drug police in Rafah governorate, in the southern Gaza Strip, seized 37
pieces of hashish and 3,000 Rotana narcotic pills during a search of the home of
a drug dealer. [@1] The branch force, backed by intervention and public-order
troops, detained a man identified only by the initials S. J., who was holding
the seized drugs. [@1]

In a separate case, anti-drug police in the Central Governorate detained a man
identified as S. A. and found 12 pieces of hashish when they searched his home.
[@1] The anti-drug department said it referred the files of both detainees and
the seized drugs to the public prosecution to complete the legal procedures
against them. [@1]
