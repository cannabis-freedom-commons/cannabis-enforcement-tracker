Officers of the Provincial Joint Forces Cannabis Enforcement Team with the
Central Hastings OPP Community Street Crime Unit executed a warrant at a rural
property on Springbrook Road in Stirling-Rawdon at about 8 a.m. on 22 June
2021, after an extensive investigation. [@1, 2] Officers seized more than
2,000 cannabis plants, two pounds of processed marijuana (about 0.91 kg) and
a pickup truck; police valued the haul at about $2 million. [@1, 2]

Junli Chen, 44, and Ai Min Yang, 56, both of Toronto, were charged with two
counts each of cultivating cannabis illegally. [@2, 3] Quinte News gives the
charges as cultivating illicit cannabis and cultivating at a place other than
their dwelling. [@1] Both men were released from custody to appear at the
Ontario Court of Justice in Belleville on 9 August 2021. [@1, 2]
