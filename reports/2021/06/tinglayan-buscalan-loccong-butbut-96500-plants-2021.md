A joint team of the Philippine National Police and the Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency destroyed 96,500 fully grown marijuana plants and 65,000 seedlings in Tinglayan, Kalinga from 14 to 16 June 2021. [@1] In Barangay Buscalan, officers cleared 5,000 plants worth 1 million pesos on a 500-square-metre plot. [@1] Four sites in Barangay Loccong held about 34,000 plants worth 6.8 million pesos on 4,000 square metres, while Barangay Butbut Proper held 57,000 plants and the 65,000 seedlings worth 14.1 million pesos on 6,650 square metres. [@1]

Police chief Guillermo Eleazar commended the teams and said eradication operations in the hinterlands, particularly in the Cordillera region, would be stepped up, noting that cannabis in plant, product and derivative form remained classified as dangerous drugs. [@1]
