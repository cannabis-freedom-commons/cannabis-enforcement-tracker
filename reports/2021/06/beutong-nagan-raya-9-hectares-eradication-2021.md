A joint force of 202 personnel from the National Narcotics Agency (BNN), the
Aceh Regional Police, soldiers of district military command 0101 Aceh Besar,
customs and the civil-service police mustered at a field in Pulo village,
Seulimeum, Aceh Besar, before setting out on a four-to-five-hour march to
harvest-ready cannabis fields at the feet of Mount Seulawah Agam and Mount
Leuser. [@1] The operation covered two hectares in Pulo village and seven
hectares in Beutong Ateuh village, Beutong, Nagan Raya, nine hectares in
all. [@1]

BNN estimated the crop at about 20,000 plants, four to five months old, with a
wet-plant weight of around 15 tonnes. [@1] Officers uprooted the plants one by
one and burned them on the spot. [@1] Alongside the destroyed fields,
authorities held four suspects and 529 kg of dried cannabis ready for
distribution. [@1,2]

The drugs directorate of the national police criminal investigation agency
(Bareskrim Polri) said the case broke up a national-network syndicate moving
cannabis through Aceh, Medan, Palembang, Bogor and Jakarta. [@2] Its deputy
director for narcotics, Senior Commissioner Jayadi, presented the evidence at a
press conference at the Central Aceh police headquarters on 1 July 2021, after
the seven hectares in the Beutong mountains of the Mount Leuser area were
destroyed on Wednesday 30 June. [@2]
