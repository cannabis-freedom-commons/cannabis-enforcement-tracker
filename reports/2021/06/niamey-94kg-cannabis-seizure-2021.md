Niger’s Central Office for the Suppression of Illicit Drug Trafficking (OCRTIS)
dismantled a transnational drug-trafficking network in the city of Niamey on 16
June 2021 and arrested four people. [@2] The network comprised one person of
Nigerien nationality and three others of Malian nationality. [@1] Police
described the well-structured network as one Nigerien and three foreigners. [@2]
Police presented the suspects and the seized drugs to the press on 22 June. [@2]

Officers seized 94 kilograms of cannabis in the operation. [@1] Police estimated
the market value of the seized cannabis at about 25,000,000 CFA francs. [@2]
Officers also seized a vehicle, a motorcycle, 517,000 CFA francs including
420,000 francs in counterfeit notes, and several mobile phones. [@1]

A Niamey-based member of the network received and secured cannabis brought into
Niger before it was moved onward to Mali in goods-transport trucks. [@1] Other
members who had come from Gao in Mali guarded the onward movement for the
occasion. [@1] National police spokesperson Dr Mountari Habou gave the account
on behalf of OCRTIS. [@1] Analysts called the Niger-to-Mali routing new because
cannabis usually came from Mali en route to North African countries. [@1] OCRTIS
was urged to redouble vigilance against expanding drug trafficking in Niger and
the public was asked to collaborate. [@1]
