The Koudougou gendarmerie's research brigade arrested three people for
trafficking chanvre indien in June 2021, including two students and a shop
employee. [@3] Wakat Séra explained that chanvre indien is a subspecies of
Cannabis sativa used to make marijuana. [@3] The case began with a 1 June 2021
complaint about a suspected wallet theft that led gendarmes to a first-year
modern-literature student at Université Norbert Zongo in Koudougou. [@1]
Gendarmes arrested the student on 5 June and searched his home, where they found
cannabis he was selling in Koudougou. [@1]

The student told investigators that a wholesaler in Ouagadougou supplied him and
said he would help them arrest the supplier. [@1] On 6 June, a gendarmerie team
sent to Ouagadougou arrested two wholesalers, another student and a shop
employee, and seized cannabis packed in slabs for delivery. [@1] Wakat Séra
named the Koudougou student Baba Emmanuel Sia and the Ouagadougou wholesalers
William Kientega and Moumoula Zoulka. [@3]

Koaci said Zoulka held 12.670 kg of cannabis packed for delivery. [@4] LeFaso
gave the gendarmerie's total seizure as 16.415 kg of chanvre indien. [@2]
Burkina24 reported that the three people arrested had more than 16 kg of
cannabis in their possession. [@1]

The gendarmerie referred all three to the prosecutor at Koudougou's Tribunal de
Grande Instance for further judicial proceedings. [@1]
