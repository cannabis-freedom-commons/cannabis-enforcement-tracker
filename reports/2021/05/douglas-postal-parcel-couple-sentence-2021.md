Christopher Corkill, 44, was jailed for 19 months and Lisa Barnett, 42, for 18
months at Douglas Courthouse on Friday. [@1] The couple, of Marathon Road in
Douglas, admitted arranging to have the parcel delivered to a separate address
on 28 January. [@1] They also pleaded guilty to attempting to possess the drug
with intent to supply. [@1] Deemster Graeme Cook, the judge, said Corkill's
letter expressing remorse about involving Barnett said the right things but that
he remained a supplier of drugs. [@1] The deemster rejected the argument that
Barnett had been dragged into the situation, saying she had a choice and chose
to get involved. [@1]

The couple imported more than £29,000 of cannabis to the Isle of Man through the
post. [@1] Prosecutors said the allegations at their first appearance concerned
992.1 grams of cannabis valued at £29,763. [@2] The court was told the drugs
were hidden inside a pillow in a large white box addressed to a property on
Tynwald Terrace, in Douglas, which postal staff reported as suspicious. [@1]
Police found the pair in a car outside the house and a note on the door asking
for parcels to be left in the porch. [@1] The couple were arrested after a
search of their home and phones found evidence of drug dealing. [@1]

At that first hearing Barnett pleaded guilty to attempting to possess cannabis
with intent to supply, being concerned in the production of cannabis to the
island and being concerned in the supply of cannabis, while Corkill pleaded not
guilty to the same three charges. [@2] Corkill had been released from prison in
February 2020 for previous drugs offences and was on licence at the time of the
offence. [@1] Barnett was also resentenced for attempting to send £5,000 hidden
in a box of chocolates to an address in Liverpool to pay off a drug debt for her
son, for which she had originally received a community service order. [@1]
