Tehama County investigators arrested three Cottonwood men on 18 May 2021 after
searching a property and finding evidence of drug sales, illegal guns and three
greenhouses growing marijuana without a licence. [@1] The Tehama County District
Attorney's Bureau of Investigation and the county Department of Environmental
Health found 1,730 marijuana plants in the three greenhouses. [@1]

Investigators said they also found 50 pounds (about 22.7 kilograms) of packaged
marijuana for sale on the property. [@1] The search also turned up $4,000 in
cash and four guns, including an AK-47. [@1]

The men arrested were Remigo Arcos Jimenez, 39, Leon Leopoldo Carbajal, 39, and
Leon Naranjo Cipriano, 20, all of Cottonwood. [@1] All three were arrested on
suspicion of possessing marijuana for sale, cultivating marijuana and possessing
an illegal assault weapon. [@1]
