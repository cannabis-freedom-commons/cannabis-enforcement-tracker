On the morning of 6 May 2021, a Gaza anti-narcotics police force backed by
intervention police and women's police went to search a house in the Al-Rimal
area of Gaza City after receiving information about drugs inside it and
obtaining a prosecution search warrant. [@1] As soon as the force arrived, it
came under direct gunfire from inside the house, injuring two anti-narcotics
officers, who were taken to Al-Shifa Hospital in moderate condition. [@1]

The force returned fire and brought the incident under control, arresting the
suspected 35-year-old gunman and other people found in the house, who were under
investigation. [@1] During the house search, police found and seized hashish,
marijuana and narcotic pills, and also seized weapons, hand grenades, ammunition
and explosives, while legal procedures in the case continued. [@1]

The Gaza Interior Ministry gave the same account: the backed anti-narcotics
force searched the Al-Rimal house on Thursday morning under a prosecution
warrant and two of its officers were shot and taken to Al-Shifa Hospital in
moderate condition. [@2]
