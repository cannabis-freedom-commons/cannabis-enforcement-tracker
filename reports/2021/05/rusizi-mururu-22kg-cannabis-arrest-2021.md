Rwanda National Police's Anti-Narcotics Unit seized 22 kilograms and 693 pellets of cannabis in Mururu Sector, Rusizi District, and arrested one man. [@1] The drugs were recovered from the house of Damien Bangamwabo, 65, in Cyete Village, where officers also arrested Eric Niyonkuru, 27, who was present. [@1]

Police said Bangamwabo regularly travelled to the Democratic Republic of Congo to bring in large quantities of cannabis for local retailers and used the house as a store. [@1] Bangamwabo escaped during the operation while Niyonkuru, who also tried to flee, was caught and handed to investigators in Kamembe with the exhibits. [@1]
