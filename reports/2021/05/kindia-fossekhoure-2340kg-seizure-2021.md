Gendarmes detained van occupant Abdoulaye Sylla as the main suspect in the
trafficking case and he remained in gendarmerie custody. [@3] His alleged
accomplices escaped during the seizure operation and the drugs’ owners remained
at large while investigations continued. [@3] Kindia prosecutor Amadou Diallo
asked the gendarmerie company commander to expedite the inquiry so the main
suspect could be tried quickly. [@2]

The seizure occurred during a territorial-gendarmerie patrol on Monday 17 May in
the district of Fossèkhouré in the rural commune of Mambia, Kindia prefecture.
[@2] Officers seized a total of 2,340 kg, or 2 tonnes 340 kilograms, of cannabis
concealed in 110 cartons. [@1] The cannabis was being carried in a van
travelling from Forécariah toward Conakry. [@1] The vehicle was intercepted at
Fossikhouré in the Mambia sub-prefecture. [@3]

Prosecutors weighed the load in the presence of regional gendarmerie
authorities, confirming 2,340 kilograms of cannabis. [@2] The prosecutor said
the drugs would be incinerated quickly and the van confiscated outright for the
state. [@2] The operation was carried out by Kindia’s territorial gendarmerie
and its research brigade. [@3]
