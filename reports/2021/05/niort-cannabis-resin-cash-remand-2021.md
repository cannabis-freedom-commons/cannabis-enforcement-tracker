Police arrested a 23-year-old man in Niort on 29 May 2021 for his possible
involvement in trafficking drugs. [@1] He was arrested while banned from
appearing in Deux-Sèvres, the department whose capital is Niort. [@1] He was
carrying 1,270 euros in cash and a small quantity of cannabis resin, which was
seized. [@1]

He was born in Bangui, in the Central African Republic, had no valid residence
permit since April 2017 and was unmarried and unemployed. [@1] His criminal
record carried twelve convictions, eight of them for drug offences. [@1] He was
due before the Niort criminal court on Monday 31 May under the
immediate-appearance procedure, comparution immédiate, which lets a court try a
suspect within days of arrest. [@1]

At that hearing he asked for time to prepare his defence, a postponement the
court had to grant. [@1] His lawyer, Me Nathanaël Ormillien, asked for further
investigation, and the request was granted. [@1] The court ordered him held in
provisional detention until trial. [@1] The case was set to be tried on the
merits on 5 July at 13:30. [@1] He may have been filmed shortly before his
arrest by a person who could shed light on the case. [@1]
