Karim Benkhelif, a 41-year-old French-Algerian nicknamed "Mâchoire" and
suspected of being one of France's biggest drug traffickers, was arrested on
Sunday 30 May 2021 at Lisbon Airport as he stepped off a flight from Dubai. [@1]
He was the subject of two European arrest warrants issued by France and Spain.
[@1] He was on the list of "priority targets" of the Ofast, France's
anti-narcotics office, and was wanted in an investigation by Paris investigating
judges of the national jurisdiction for organised crime, JUNALCO. [@1] The
arrest was reported by Agence France-Presse from a police source, confirming a
report by the French radio station Europe 1. [@1]
