Tajikistan's Drug Control Agency detained a 21-year-old resident of the Balkhi
district in the city of Bokhtar, Khatlon Region, during a special operation as
he met a buyer for a large batch of Afghan hashish. [@1]

Officers seized 29 kg 560 g of hashish from the detainee, the agency's press
centre said. [@1]

The agency's own English translation of its statement said more than 29 kg of
drugs were taken out of illegal circulation. [@2]

At his first interrogation the detainee said he had bought the drugs in the
Jayhun district from an Afghan citizen and taken them to Bokhtar to sell on.
[@1] The agency said he met the Afghan citizen in the border zone of the Karadum
village in the Jayhun district and took 29 kg 560 g of hashish for handing on to
another person. [@3]

A criminal case was opened under part 4, point "d" of article 200 of
Tajikistan's Criminal Code, which covers drugs in especially large amounts, and
an investigation was under way. [@3]
