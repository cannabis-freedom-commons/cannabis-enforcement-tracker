National Police officers captured Reynaldo Obando Sobalvarro, 25, at 2:20 a.m.
on Friday 14 May 2021 in comarca Amakita, a rural district in the Ayapal
micro-region of San José de Bocay, Jinotega. [@1] Commissioner General Marvin
Castro, chief of the National Police in Jinotega, said the arrest was a result
of the Muro de Contención strategy against drug trafficking and organised crime.
[@1] He also cited the Plan de Seguridad en el Campo, the force's rural security
plan. [@1] Police described the man as a drug seller in the department. [@1] The
National Police announced the seizure at a press conference at the Germán
Pomares police delegation in Jinotega. [@1]

Officers seized two MACEN sacks containing eight rectangular packages of green
herb. [@1] A field test was positive for marijuana, and police weighed the
packages at 40 pounds (about 18.1 kg). [@1] A technical investigation team
inspected the scene, investigated it and took photographs. [@1] The team took
samples of the green herb, and chemical testing concluded it was marijuana. [@1]

Castro said the man and the evidence would be sent to the competent authorities
to begin prosecution. [@1] The National Police said he had been captured for
supplying drugs in Jinotega, and that 40 pounds of marijuana had been taken from
him. [@2] Police said the detained suspects and the evidence were already before
the competent authorities for prosecution. [@2] La Nueva Radio YA reported that
he had been captured and processed for supplying drugs. [@3]
