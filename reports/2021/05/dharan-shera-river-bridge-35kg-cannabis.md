Police in Sunsari district recovered 35 kg of cannabis on the night of Sunday 23
May 2021. [@1] The sack was found at the Shera river bridge in Dharan
Sub-Metropolitan City-6. [@1]

Two people on a motorcycle threw away the sack after seeing officers from the
Panbari-based police office and fled on the motorcycle. [@1] Police found the
cannabis packed in plastic bags inside the sack. [@1]

Police said further investigation was under way and that they had begun a
manhunt for the two people who fled. [@1]
