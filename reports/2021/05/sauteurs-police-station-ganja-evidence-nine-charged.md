Police in Grenada arrested nine people, including two juveniles, over the
weekend and charged them in connection with cannabis that disappeared from the
storage facility at a police station in the northern parish of St Patrick. [@1,4,5]
The cannabis, valued at EC$750,000, was being held as evidence in ongoing court
matters. [@1,5]

The Royal Grenada Police Force said officers at the station noticed the drugs
disappearing over a period of time, and that the cannabis had been pulled out
from the rear of the building through structural deficiencies. [@1,5] The force
said those charged include a boat builder, a farmer and two fishermen. [@1,5]

NOW Grenada reported that the nine were residents of St Patrick and were charged
with handling a controlled drug and stealing cannabis valued at EC$721,224. [@2]
The Grenadian Voice reported that the drugs were taken from the Sauteurs Police
Station, which has housed officers in a building designed as a supermarket since
the original station was destroyed in 2004. [@3]

The paper reported that all nine appeared before the St David's Magistrate's
Court on Monday 17 May 2021 and were granted bail, with sureties set at
EC$30,000 for each juvenile, EC$50,000 for four accused and EC$120,000 for three
others. [@3,4] The force said the structural deficiency had since been resolved
and that investigations were continuing. [@1,5]

They were also placed on a curfew from 4:00 p.m. to 6:00 a.m. daily. [@4]
