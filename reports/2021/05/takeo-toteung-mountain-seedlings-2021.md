Takeo provincial police chief Major General Sok Samnang and Kiri Vong police
chief Yuk Sarath led a police team to Toteung mountain in Kiri Vong district.
[@1] The team found and destroyed thousands of illegally grown marijuana
seedlings on the mountain on Saturday. [@1] The growers escaped after learning
of the police arrival, so no arrests were made. [@1] Police said they were now
on the trail of the perpetrators and hoped to arrest them soon. [@1]

The seedlings were spread across eight locations covering about 2,000 square
metres, or about 0.2 hectares. [@1] Police also found six water tanks placed on
the mountain to help cultivate the illegal crop. [@1]

A police spokesman said local people are afraid to report this cultivation for
fear that retaliation might happen to them. [@1] Police have successfully
destroyed crops planted by the perpetrators since 2019, each time in a different
location. [@1] The spokesman said the perpetrators can keep planting and police
will keep destroying the crops. [@1] Authorities were now educating local people
and community leaders on the importance of alerting police to illegal marijuana
planting. [@1]
