Daniel McSween, 18, of Whitebridge Road in Onchan, had fled and thrown the bag
away when approached by police on 19 March. [@1] He admitted possession with
intent to supply, possession of criminal property and breaching the Isle of Man
lockdown. [@1] He was jailed for eight months. [@1] He was also jailed for £635
of unpaid fines. [@1]

He was reported to police by a member of the public who saw him putting the
drugs into a "man bag" while sitting on the bench at Onchan School, which had
been closed to pupils during the island's third lockdown. [@1] When police
arrived, McSween jumped over a wall and crouched behind a van. [@1] Officers
subsequently found the bag, which had cannabis worth about £500 inside, under
the vehicle. [@1] McSween also had about £300 in cash on him when he was
arrested. [@1] Prosecution advocate Hazel Carroon said McSween was arrested by
police, on Sandringham Drive in Onchan, on 19 March. [@2] When he was searched
by police officers, they discovered cannabis worth around £1,000 as well as £390
in cash. [@2]

Daniel James McSween of Whitebridge Road in Onchan appeared before Deputy High
Bailiff Christopher Arrowsmith at Douglas Courthouse via a live video link from
the Isle of Man prison. [@3] The 18 year old pleaded guilty to possessing
cannabis with intent to supply it, possessing £390 of criminal cash and being
out of his home without good reason – all offences were committed in Sandringham
Drive in Onchan on March 19. [@3] Sentencing him, Deemster Cook said McSween's
plans to relocate to the UK to live with his father to stay out of trouble upon
release were a good idea. [@1]
