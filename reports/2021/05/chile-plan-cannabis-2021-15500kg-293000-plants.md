On the morning of 6 May 2021, Chilean authorities presented the closing balance
of Plan Cannabis 2021 at the PDI department for small-quantity drug trafficking
in Cerrillos. [@1] Interior Minister Rodrigo Delgado and PDI Director General
Héctor Espinosa led the event, joined by Interior and crime-prevention
undersecretaries, Metropolitan South prosecutor Héctor Barros and Senda director
Carlos Charme. [@1] The National Anti-Narcotics and Organised Crime Headquarters
runs each summer season to eradicate Cannabis sativa crops and dismantle groups
that grow, harvest and sell them. [@1,5] Working with the Public Prosecutor's Office, investigators reached difficult mountain areas before harvest to anticipate and eradicate plantations. [@5] The 2021 season covered plantations from
La Serena to the Biobío region. [@1] The operation ran between January and April
2021. [@4]

The PDI said investigators seized more than 15.5 tonnes of processed cannabis
and 293,000 plants. [@1] The PDI put the haul at more than 162 million doses and
valued it at 225,000 million pesos. [@3] The emphasis on investigation-led work
almost quintupled inquiry counts. [@1] Detainees rose from 4 in the prior season
to 42 after producers and gangs were identified. [@2] Firearms seized rose from
1 to 17 and vehicles seized rose from 3 to 16. [@1]

The PDI said the plan started 20 years earlier and has improved year by year.
[@1] PDI chief Espinosa said the institution had never had such an important
result. [@1] Minister Delgado commended the PDI and prosecutors, saying hits
against trafficking matter because drugs, violence and arms form a triad. [@1]
The PDI chief clarified the 15.5 tonnes were cannabis sativa under Plan Cannabis
only, excluding other drugs seized separately. [@3]
