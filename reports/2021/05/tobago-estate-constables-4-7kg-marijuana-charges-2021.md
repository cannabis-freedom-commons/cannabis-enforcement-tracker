Malek Marcelle and Neilus Biggart, both Estate Constables, were charged in
separate incidents following an anti-crime exercise in Tobago over the weekend.
[@1] Marcelle was charged with possession of marijuana for trafficking as well
as possession of ammunition. [@1] Biggart was charged for possession of
marijuana. [@1] Both men appeared virtually at the Scarborough Magistrates’
Court yesterday. [@1]

According to police reports, Marcelle was arrested after officers executed a
search warrant at his home in Canoe Bay, Tobago. [@1] During the search,
officers allegedly found a white Styrofoam icebox with six rectangular objects.
[@1] Each allegedly contained a quantity of marijuana. [@1] The packages weighed
a total of 4.566 kilogrammes. [@1] Officers also allegedly found a black plastic
bag with eight rounds of ammunition and a tin of pepper spray. [@1] During the
exercise, a search warrant was also executed at Biggart’s home at Emerald Drive,
Signal Hill Development, Signal Hill. [@1] One hundred and sixteen grammes of
marijuana were allegedly found during the search. [@1] Marijuana with a weight
of 116 grammes was found during the search. [@2]

The exercise, which was conducted between 10 pm on Saturday 15th May, 2021 and
2:40 am on Sunday 16th May, 2021 was coordinated by Snr Supt Anand Ramesar. [@1]
It also included officers of the Tobago Divisional Task Force, the Special
Investigations Unit (SIU), and Canine Units. [@1] Marcelle was granted $250,000
bail with conditions. [@1] He must surrender his passport and report to the
Shirvan Road Police Station on Wednesdays and Fridays. [@1] He is scheduled to
reappear on June 14th 2021. [@1] Biggart was granted $40,000 bail and is
scheduled to reappear on August 13th 2021. [@1]
