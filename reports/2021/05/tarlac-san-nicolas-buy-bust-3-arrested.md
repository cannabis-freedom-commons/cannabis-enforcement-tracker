Police arrested three men from Mountain Province in a buy-bust operation in
Tarlac City on Sunday, 2 May 2021. [@1] Philippine National Police chief Gen.
Debold Sinas identified them as Cornelio Chumil-Ang, 33, Jomar Pallar, 24, and
Marcelino Caraowa, 40. [@2] Tarlac City police and the Tarlac provincial police
office nabbed the suspects at around 1:30 a.m. in Barangay San Nicolas, Tarlac
City. [@1] Sinas said they belonged to a group trafficking narcotics in
Pangasinan, Tarlac and Mountain Province. [@1]

Officers seized 24 bricks of dried marijuana leaves and 26 rolled dried
marijuana leaves that police valued at P4.6 million. [@1] Officers also seized
two bottles of suspected cannabis oil that police valued at P30,000. [@3] Police
also seized mobile phones and two vehicles they said were used to transport the
marijuana. [@2]

Investigators said the three belonged to the same group whose members had been
arrested with marijuana in Tarlac in February 2021. [@2]
