On 27 May 2021, officers from Xiao Mazha Border Police Station of the Ili
Border Management Detachment uprooted more than 100,000 wild cannabis plants.
[@1] They found the stand on remote wasteland beside the Sixth Company of the
61st Regiment during door-to-door visits under the “Millions of Police Entering
Millions of Homes” outreach drive. [@1]

Officers questioned residents in the surrounding area and concluded that the
plants were wild. [@1] They removed the stand and brought samples to local
residents, explaining cannabis-related harms and national drug-control laws.
[@1] The report says the outreach was intended to improve residents' awareness
and prevent the plants from entering circulation. [@1]

Chinanews Xinjiang reported the clearance that evening but gave no area for the
site, weight for the plants or number of officers involved. [@1] It did not name
a grower or report an arrest or charge. [@1]
