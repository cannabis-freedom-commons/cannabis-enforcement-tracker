Between January and 10 May 2021, the Kaduna State command of the NDLEA,
Nigeria's anti-drug agency, seized 1,721.508 kg of cannabis sativa across the
state and arrested 89 suspects, state commander Uche Iyke Samuel told
reporters in Kaduna on 11 May. [@1,2]

Samuel said four suspects were arrested in May with 328.1 kg in Kudenda and
Kasuwan Magani: one with 86.8 kg on 1 May, two with 162.8 kg in 17 bags at
Kudenda industrial layout on 7 May, one with 68.5 kg at Dutsen-Wai on 8 May
and one with 10.2 kg at Kasuwan Magani on 9 May. [@1] He added that the
command secured the conviction of eight people to various jail terms at the
Federal High Court in Kaduna during the period. [@1,2]

Samuel named manpower and vehicle shortages, along with banditry and
kidnapping on major highways, as the command's main obstacles, and warned drug
dealers to find legitimate business. [@1]
