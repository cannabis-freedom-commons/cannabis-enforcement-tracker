Customs officers of the Koungheul mobile brigade, in Kaffrine subdivision,
seized 482 kg of chanvre indien on 17 January 2021. [@2] Officers found the
cannabis in a Malian truck during a routine check at the Koungheul customs
checkpoint. [@1] The cannabis was packed in 241 sacks and hidden in a
purpose-built compartment. [@1] Customs valued the Koungheul haul at 38,560,000
CFA francs. [@1]

The truck driver was arrested and referred to the prosecutor. [@1] Over the same
weekend, maritime customs units sweeping the coast and the Saloum islands seized
another 152 kg of chanvre indien at Keur Balla. [@1] The two finds together made
the weekend's cannabis seizures 634 kg. [@1]

The figures come from a statement by the Directorate General of Customs' public
relations and communication office. [@1] The customs service also reported 8
parcels of medicines found in a pirogue at Joal, valued at 21,578,400 CFA
francs, and 17 parcels of fake medicines at Toubacouta, valued at 13,138,000 CFA
francs, with two people arrested in each case. [@1] The Saloum sweeps took place
over the weekend of 16 to 17 January 2021. [@3] Several Senegalese outlets
carried the same customs statement in the days after the seizure. [@5] The
Senego account, credited to Libration, carries the identical statement and notes
the driver was arrested and sent to the prosecutor. [@4]
