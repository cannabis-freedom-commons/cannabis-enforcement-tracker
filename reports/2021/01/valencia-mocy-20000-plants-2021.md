Spain's Civil Guard arrested 20 suspected drug traffickers in Valencia as part
of Operation Mocy, a Europe-wide crackdown on one of the continent's biggest
cannabis networks. [@1] Nineteen suspects were Bulgarian nationals and one was
Spanish, and the group included a lieutenant of the network's leader. [@1]

The investigation began in September 2019, when the Seville branch became aware
of a Malaga-based organisation moving large quantities of marijuana from
Andalusia to Poland. [@1] Officers later found the network's Polish leaders
living in a luxury estate in Estepona. [@1] In Valencia the officers found
20,000 plants hidden in industrial warehouses and chalets, including 10,000
plants on the premises of a grow-shop in l'Horta run by the Valencia branch's
Bulgarian leader. [@1]

Officers also seized a ton of cannabis buds, 300,000 euros in cash and
high-end vehicles. [@1] The investigation was coordinated with Europol and
Eurojust, and the wider operation reached 110 arrests across Spain. [@1]
