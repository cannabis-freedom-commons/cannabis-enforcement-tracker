Fiji's police drug operations team uprooted more than 1,200 green plants believed to
be marijuana from three separate farms over a weekend in January 2021. [@1] On Kadavu,
officers took more than 1,000 plants from farms at Nacomoto and Natumua, arresting a
25-year-old farmer at Natumua, where they also found loose dried leaves. [@1] The
seized plants ranged from 0.15 to 1.10 metres tall. [@1]

In a separate raid at Nabalabala in Ra, police uprooted more than 200 plants after
receiving information. [@1] One farm owner was apprehended while two others were
still to be identified. [@1]
