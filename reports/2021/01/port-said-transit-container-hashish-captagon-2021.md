Egyptian security forces seized 8.22 tonnes of hashish and more than 8 million
Captagon pills from a container transiting through Port Said on 6 January
2021. [@1] The Interior Ministry called it the biggest drug haul ever seized
in the country and one of the biggest blows to drug smugglers. [@1] It put
the combined value of the haul at 611 million Egyptian pounds. [@1]

The container's owners had provided shipping documents declaring plastic
materials, to conceal the drugs packed inside. [@1] The General Administration
for Narcotics Control found the consignment hidden in a container sent by
transit from one Arab country to another through Port Said. [@1] The shipment
had come from Lebanon, where it was loaded on 19 December in a warehouse
belonging to a Lebanese exporting firm before passing through the Port of
Beirut, and it was bound for Libya. [@2] Cartons inside were labelled "Made
in Lebanon", the narcotics control administration said. [@2]

## A Levant transit route

Middle East Monitor noted that Lebanon is believed to be the world's
third-largest supplier of hashish after Morocco and Afghanistan. [@2] The same
account describes Lebanon and neighbouring Syria as conduit routes for
Captagon, a drug popular in the Gulf. [@2]
