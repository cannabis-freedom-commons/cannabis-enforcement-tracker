District police, military police and local police raided five marijuana farms in the foothills of a mountain near the Cambodian-Vietnamese border in Takeo's Kirivong district at about 8am on Saturday 30 January 2021. [@1] The farms sat in valleys of Preah Bath Chhoum Choan commune and had been watched under police surveillance before the raid. [@1]

Officers set fire to more than 20,000 marijuana plants and confiscated materials from each of the five farms. [@1] The owners fled when police moved in, and deputy district police chief Keo Kimheng said a manhunt was under way so legal action could be taken against them. [@1]
