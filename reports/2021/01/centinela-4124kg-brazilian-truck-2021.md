Gendarmería Nacional Argentina seized 4,124 kilograms of marijuana from the
trailer of a truck stopped at the Centinela roadside checkpoint at kilometre 785
of National Route 14, in Misiones province. [@1] The driver was detained and the
drug, the driver and the truck were placed at the disposal of the Federal Court
of Posadas. [@2] El Territorio reported that the truck was a
Brazilian-registered Scania H113 driven by a Brazilian national who had crossed
into Argentina hours earlier over the Tancredo Neves international bridge at Foz
do Iguaçu. [@3]

Officers of Section San José, part of Squadron 6 Alto Uruguay of the
Gendarmería, carried out the check; El Territorio described the unit as Squadron
8 Alto Uruguay. [@1] The narcotics dog León reacted to the trailer, and a mobile
Nuctech LT 1213 LH scanner showed 175 sacks or bundles holding more than 4.1
tonnes of marijuana in bulk. [@1] After the Federal Court and Federal
Prosecutor's Office of Posadas authorised opening the seal covering the drug, in
the presence of witnesses and customs staff, a Narcotest gave a positive result
for marijuana and the load was weighed. [@1]

El Territorio reported that a loose securing rope first drew officers'
attention, that the weighing was done at the squadron's Apóstoles headquarters,
and that this was the third large drug seizure in Misiones in 72 hours. [@3] The
Security Ministry presented the seizure as part of the order given to federal
security forces under Minister Sabina Frederic to tighten checks at strategic
points against drug trafficking. [@1]
