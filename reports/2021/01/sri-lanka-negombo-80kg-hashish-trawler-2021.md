Sri Lanka Navy personnel arrested four people on a multi-day fishing trawler in
the seas off Negombo on 4 January 2021. [@2] About 80 kg of hashish was seized
from the trawler, packed in nine sacks and concealed as plastic containers and
small packets. [@2] The Navy put the street value of the whole stock held in the
operation at more than 600 million Sri Lankan rupees. [@2] The suspects were
residents of the Thoduwawa area of Chilaw, and they, the drugs and the trawler
were handed to the Police Narcotic Bureau in Colombo for legal action. [@2]

The operation was carried out with the Navy's intelligence services and the
Police Narcotics Bureau, with assistance from the Sri Lanka Air Force, and the
Navy impounded the fishing trawler used to smuggle the contraband. [@1] The
consignment also carried 100 kg of crystal methamphetamine, which is outside the
cannabis scope of this tracker and is not counted here. [@1]

An Indian Customs official said earlier large consignments of heroin came by sea
from the Makran coast in Pakistan, and that meth, as the main component this
time, may now bring smugglers more profit. [@1]

The Sri Lanka Air Force supported the operation, which the Navy said followed
COVID-19 preventive guidelines. [@2]
