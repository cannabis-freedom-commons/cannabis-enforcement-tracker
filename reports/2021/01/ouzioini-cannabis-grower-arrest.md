In Ouzioini, gendarmes arrested a man suspected of producing cannabis and placed
him in garde à vue, the initial period of police custody. [@1] The arrest was
made on Saturday by officers of the Brigade territoriale de Dembeni, the local
gendarmerie unit responsible for the area. [@1] The detained grower was expected
to be brought before the parquet, the public prosecutor's office, on the
following Monday for further proceedings. [@1]

The same Dembeni brigade said it had taken control of a cannabis plantation in
the locality of Ouzioini during Saturday's operation. [@1] Gendarmes who
examined the plantation discovered about thirty cannabis plants growing at the
site. [@1]

The gendarmerie presented the arrested man as the producer linked to that
plantation. [@1] The Gendarmerie nationale, the country's national gendarmerie
force, was identified as the authority reporting the operation. [@1] The
plantation find and the arrest were reported together as a single gendarmerie
operation in Ouzioini. [@1]
