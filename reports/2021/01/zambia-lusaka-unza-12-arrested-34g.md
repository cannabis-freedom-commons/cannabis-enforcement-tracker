Zambia's Drug Enforcement Commission arrested seven people in Lusaka, including
three University of Zambia third-year students, for trafficking in over 30
grammes of cannabis. [@1] The three UNZA students were identified as Amadu Bah
Mwansa, 22, Siddy Gadjo Mwansa, 22, and Kitonzui Mesala, 24, who were found with
12.5 grammes of special cannabis along with one suspected sachet of heroin. [@1]
The four others arrested with them were Neyma Camara, 19, Robinson Chibwe, 28,
bus conductor Cloud Kabwe and bus driver Mathias Banda, for trafficking in 21.5
grammes of cannabis. [@1]

The four were picked from UNZA while driving a Pajero IO registration number ALD
5604 and were believed to be major suppliers of drugs in the area. [@1] Two
Zambia Centre for Accountancy Studies students, Kachizya Mulenga, 20, and Choma
Chato, 19, were jointly charged with Shawa Mwale, 23, Gift Kumwala, 36, and
Samson Sinkala, 25, for trafficking in 19 sachets of special cannabis. [@1] The
DEC said the variety, also called special cannabis or cheese on the market, was
suspected to have been imported from a named country in southern Africa and was
believed to be more potent than locally grown cannabis. [@1]

Arrangements were being made for all the suspects to appear before courts of
competent jurisdiction at an appropriate time. [@2] The Lusaka operation charged
trafficking in cannabis contrary to Cap 96 of the Laws of Zambia. [@2]
