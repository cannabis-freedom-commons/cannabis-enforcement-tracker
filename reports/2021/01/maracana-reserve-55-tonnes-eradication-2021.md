On 21 January 2021, agents of Paraguay's anti-drug agency SENAD, led by prosecutor Meiji Udagawa with specialists from the Salto del Guairá regional office, entered the Maracaná Nature Reserve in the department of Canindeyú and located a marijuana cultivation and processing centre. [@1,2] Inside the protected forest they found two campsites used to store the production equipment, including presses, guillotines and sieves. [@1,2]

## Destroyed and burned

The weighed total reached 5,500 kilograms of marijuana: about 5,000 kilograms chopped and 500 kilograms pressed. [@1,2] The prosecutor ordered the evidence incinerated, and SENAD estimated the traffickers' lost income at about US$165,000. [@1,2] Trafficking groups set up production and storage bases in protected forest areas, which SENAD says it has targeted with intensified reserve operations since late 2020. [@3]
