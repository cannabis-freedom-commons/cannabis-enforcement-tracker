A 40-year-old man appeared at the Nadi Magistrates Court on 6 January 2021,
accused of growing marijuana on his farm in Malevu, Sigatoka. [@1] Police
arrested him on the morning of Monday 4 January 2021 during a drug raid on the
farm. [@1]

Officers took ten green plants that were confirmed to be marijuana. [@1] Police
spokesperson Savaira Tabua said he faced one count of unlawful cultivation of
illicit drugs. [@1]

The court remanded him in custody until a further appearance on 15 January 2021.
