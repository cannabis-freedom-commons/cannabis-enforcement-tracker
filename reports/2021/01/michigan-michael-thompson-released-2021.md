Michael Thompson, a 69-year-old Flint native, was released from prison on the morning of 28 January 2021 after serving roughly 25 years of a 42-to-60-year sentence for selling marijuana. [@2] He was one of four men whose sentences Governor Gretchen Whitmer commuted on 22 December 2020, and all four were released on the same day. [@1,3]

Thompson had been sentenced in the mid-1990s for selling three pounds of cannabis to an undercover officer, with firearms charges attached to the case. [@1] Michigan Attorney General Dana Nessel pressed for clemency after Thompson contracted the coronavirus in prison, writing that a decades-long sentence for selling marijuana was unheard of even with firearms offences, and noting the offence would carry at most four years under current law. [@1] "The 'tough on crime' and 'war on drugs' eras have fractured countless families," Lieutenant Governor Garlin Gilchrist said on the day of the release. [@3]
