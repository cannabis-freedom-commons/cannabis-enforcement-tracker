Ivorian gendarmerie officers arrested four people on Wednesday 13 January 2021
during raids on drug-smoking dens in Adjamé Abadjame, Adjamé village and Cocody
Washington in Abidjan. [@1] The operation was run by the anti-drug section of
the National Gendarmerie in the context of its fight against drug trafficking.
[@1]

The officers seized 56 blocks of cannabis, 1,043 cannabis balls and four
cannabis slabs, weighing 45 kg in total. [@1]

The same searches found 68 diazepam tablets, 19 tramadol tablets and six
Rivotril tablets on the people arrested. [@1] Officers also took 114,085 CFA
francs in cash from the suspects. [@1]

The gendarmerie said the four detained people would be brought before the
competent courts to answer for their actions. [@1]
