Police uprooted more than 2,900 plants believed to be marijuana in two separate
raids on Kadavu. [@2] In the first raid, officers uprooted 1,800 plants from a
farm located between Gasele and Daku villages on Wednesday. [@1]

During the first raid, officers also found several bags of dried leaves wrapped
in aluminium foil. [@1] In the second raid in the Nacomoto area, officers
uprooted more than 1,000 plants believed to be marijuana. [@1]

Police Chief of Operations ACP Abdul Khan said the war on drugs will continue
and thanked the public for providing information that led to the raids. [@1]
