On the evening of 26 October 2021, the Antilles-Guyane patrol vessel La
Combattante intercepted an unflagged yole south of Martinique suspected of drug
trafficking. [@2] Two men of Saint Lucian nationality were aboard the boat. [@1]
At the request of the Fort-de-France prosecutor, the two crew members, the cargo
and the boat were handed over to the Caribbean branch of the anti-narcotics
office (OFAST). [@2] Investigators immediately placed both men in police
custody. [@2] Tried in immediate proceedings on 29 October 2021, both men were
sentenced by the Fort-de-France criminal court to three years in prison. [@2]
The court also banned them from holding or carrying a weapon and from French
territory for five years. [@2]

Aboard the yole, the crew found two loaded handguns, 55 kilos of cannabis and 24
kilos of contraband tobacco. [@2] Authorities described the seized drugs as a
loss of more than one million euros in resale value for regional drug
trafficking. [@2]

The operation was the armed forces' seventh drug seizure of the year in the
Antilles and the only one involving cannabis. [@2] Since the start of the year,
maritime enforcement in the Antilles had removed more than 11 tonnes of cocaine
from the market, including 6.8 tonnes through the armed forces. [@2]
