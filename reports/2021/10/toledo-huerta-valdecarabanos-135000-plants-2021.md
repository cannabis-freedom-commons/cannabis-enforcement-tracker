Spain's Guardia Civil found 135,000 marijuana plants ready for consumption on a 12-hectare holding in Huerta de Valdecarábanos, Toledo, in what officers called the largest marijuana plantation found in Europe to date. [@1] The force announced the find on 18 October 2021, and LaSexta carried the story with video. [@1]

The find was part of a wider October crackdown in Toledo province: more than 200,000 cannabis plants were seized that month in Huerta de Valdecarábanos and the neighbouring municipalities of La Guardia and Sonseca, 420 Magazine reported, citing Civil Guard figures. [@2] The Attorney General's Office had warned of repeated attempts to pass off illegal plantations as hemp for industrial use. [@2]
