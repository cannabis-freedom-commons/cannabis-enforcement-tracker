On 6 October 2021, Customs Surveillance officers in Murcia watched a suspect
hide a refrigerated lorry near the fishing port of San Pedro del Pinatar
while a fishing vessel under investigation put to sea without fishing gear,
then kept the port under surveillance until the vessel returned a few hours
later. [@1] As the boat docked, bales of the kind typically used for hashish
resin began moving from its deck to the lorry, and the officers moved in,
arresting the three men doing the unloading: two Spanish nationals and one
Moroccan national. [@1]

Once fully unloaded, the haul totalled 130 bales weighing 4,360 kg of
hashish. [@1] The operation, codenamed Racel, grew out of a 2020 investigation
into a suspected smuggling organisation using a fishing vessel based at the
port. [@1] The detainees, the drugs, the boat and the lorry were to go before
Investigating Court No. 4 in San Javier, and the agency said further arrests
could not be ruled out. [@1]
