Police and customs officers found vacuum-sealed packages of cannabis in two
separate shipments at the V.C. Bird International Airport cargo shed on Friday 8
October 2021. [@1] The find was made during a joint operation by the police and
the Customs Department. [@1] The parcels were taken to police headquarters and
investigations were under way. [@2]

More than 120 pounds (about 54.4 kg) of cannabis were seized over the weekend.
[@1] Antigua News Room reported one parcel weighed 95 and a half pounds and the
other 25 and a quarter pounds, and put the estimated street value at $724,500.
[@2] The Observer, attributing the figures to police, said the parcels weighed
nine and a half pounds and 24 and a quarter pounds, worth more than 700,000
dollars combined. [@1]

No arrests were reported for the seizure. [@1]
