Four men were arrested and charged after police found 18.51 pounds of cannabis
at a home in Liberta. [@1] Police went to the Liberta home on 7 October 2021,
searched the property and found the drugs, worth $37,000. [@1] The four were
charged with cultivating the controlled drug and being concerned with its
supply. [@1]

Three of the men, Uroy Joseph, Zamir O'Garro and Micha O'Garro, said they worked
for the Ras Freeman Foundation, which holds a cannabis licence, and had taken
the drug home to finish cleaning it before the curfew struck. [@1] The fourth,
18-year-old Delon Edwards, was reportedly just at the house when they were
caught. [@1]

The four appeared before Chief Magistrate Joanne Walsh the day before the
article was published and asked to be bailed for the indictable offence, arguing
they had moved the drug only to avoid breaking the curfew. [@1] The prosecution
objected to bail for two of the men because their records showed a propensity to
commit the same crime. [@1] The magistrate granted all four bail of $40,000 with
a cash component of $2,500, and required them to report to the nearest police
station, provide two sureties and surrender their travel documents. [@1]

They are due back in the same court on 14 February 2022 for a committal hearing,
when the magistrate will decide whether there is enough evidence to send the
matter to the higher court. [@1]
