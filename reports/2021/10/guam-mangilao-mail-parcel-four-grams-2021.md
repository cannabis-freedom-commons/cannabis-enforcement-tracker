A package searched on 4 October 2021 on its way into Guam was found to contain
four grams of marijuana. [@1] The parcel had been sent from Citrus Heights,
California, to an address on University Drive in Mangilao, a village on Guam's
eastern coast. [@1]

The search was detailed in a federal court case that was unsealed in early July
2022, alongside a second case about a mailed package containing methamphetamine.
[@1]

No charges had been filed in connection with either case when the account was
published. [@1]
