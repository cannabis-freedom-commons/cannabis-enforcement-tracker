Detectives from the Firearms and Cannabis Team, with rural and Gatton district
officers, searched a rural property at Churchable and found more than 10,100
growing and mature cannabis plants in several structures, some with
hydroponics. [@1,2] Police put the street value at $25 million, calling it one
of the largest grows the drug squad had intercepted. [@1,2]

Four men aged 37, 37, 42 and 48 were each charged with producing dangerous
drugs and possessing property used in crime. [@1,2] The bust was part of an
ongoing operation against cannabis production and trafficking in the Darling
Downs. [@1]
