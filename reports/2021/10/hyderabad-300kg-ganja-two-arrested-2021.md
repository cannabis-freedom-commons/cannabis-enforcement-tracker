Hyderabad City police, with the task force and the east zone in a joint operation, seized 300 kg of ganja and arrested two inter-state smugglers on 16 October 2021. [@1] Police Commissioner Anjani Kumar said the ganja was worth Rs 30 lakh and that officers also seized one vehicle. [@1]

The accused, Vilas Bhausaheb Dhokane and Dnyaneshwar Mohite of Aurangabad and Ahmednagar districts in Maharashtra, sourced the ganja from the Andhra-Odisha border area in the Vizag rural belt, according to the commissioner. [@1] He said Hyderabad police had detected the whole gang and written to the Odisha and Andhra Pradesh police chiefs identifying names and persons in the network. [@1]

Both men were booked under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act and handed over with the seized drugs for further investigation. [@1]
