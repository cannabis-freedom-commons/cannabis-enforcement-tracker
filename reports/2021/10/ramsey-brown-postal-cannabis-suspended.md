Michael Brown, 30, of Bircham Avenue Close in Ramsey, agreed to receive more
than £19,500 worth of cannabis through the post so a £300 drug debt would be
cancelled. [@1] He had agreed to accept the package to pass it on to someone
else. [@1] Brown was arrested after accepting the package on 13 February 2020.
[@1] Deemster Graeme Cook at Douglas Courthouse handed Brown an 18-month jail
term suspended for two years plus a two-year supervision order. [@1]

Staff at the Ramsey postal sorting office reported the large brown box as
suspicious because it was the third package addressed to Brown that week. [@1]
The intercepted box held three sealed silver bags of cannabis. [@1] The parcel
held 1.4 kilogrammes of cannabis worth more than £19,000. [@2] Officers replaced
the drugs with the same weight in tea bags and the package was delivered to
Brown's home in Bircham Avenue Close. [@1] Brown was arrested for importing the
drug and attempting to possess it after signing for the parcel. [@1] A search of
his home found a small quantity of cannabis in a glass jar on the floor along
with a grinder. [@1]

Prosecutors said there was no evidence on his mobile phone to indicate he had
been dealing the drug. [@1] At an earlier hearing before magistrates at Douglas
Courthouse, Brown had denied attempting to possess cannabis with intent to
supply but admitted possessing cannabis. [@3] Magistrates said the case was too
serious for the summary court and committed it to the Court of General Gaol
Delivery. [@3]
