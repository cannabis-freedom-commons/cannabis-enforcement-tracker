The Barbados Coast Guard, the maritime unit of the Barbados Defence Force, said
it seized 5,895 pounds of marijuana in the period from 1 January to 13 October
2021. [@1] The force's list for the period gives 5,895 pounds of marijuana, 183
pounds of cocaine, one firearm, one AR 15 rifle and nine vessels. [@1]
Twenty-eight non-nationals and seven nationals were arrested in connection with
the maritime drug offences during the period. [@1]

The coast guard said the figures reflected an 11 per cent increase in marijuana
interdictions and a 50 per cent rise in cocaine interdictions compared with
2020. [@1] It also put the rise in non-nationals arrested in connection with
these crimes at 467 per cent. [@1] In 2020 the coast guard reported interdicting
5,277 pounds of marijuana and 162 pounds of cocaine, and arresting and charging
seven vessels, six non-nationals and seven nationals in connection with maritime
drug offences. [@1]

The force attributed the rise partly to the region's socioeconomic conditions
and local demand for the drugs. [@1] Everyone taken into custody was screened
for COVID-19, and one positive case, a non-national, was found in 2021 and
isolated under the national policy. [@1]

WIC News reported the same coast guard totals for the period, including the
5,895 pounds of marijuana and the 28 non-nationals and seven nationals held.
