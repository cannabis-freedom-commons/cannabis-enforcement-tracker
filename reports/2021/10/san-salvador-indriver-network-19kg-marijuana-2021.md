El Salvador's Attorney General's Office and National Civil Police arrested 13
people in an operation against a network accused of moving drugs from Guatemala
for delivery to clients by order in San Salvador and La Libertad. [@1] The
arrests were made on the night of Saturday 23 October and in the early hours of
Sunday 24 October 2021. [@1] Prosecutors issued 15 administrative arrest orders
against members of a structure dedicated to selling marijuana, methamphetamine
and cocaine, of which 13 detentions were carried out. [@3] Officers carried out
about 30 searches with raid authorization in San Salvador and La Libertad,
including San Marcos, Nejapa, the centre of the capital, Santa Tecla, Comasagua
and Antiguo Cuscatlán. [@2] Police identified Miguel Ernesto Iraheta, alias
Mike, as the organisation's leader, with international contacts for buying drugs
and control of its trafficking orders. [@1] Marco Antonio Landaverde, alias Oso,
described as one of the leaders under Iraheta, tried to get rid of narcotics by
throwing them into the toilet when detained. [@2]

Officers seized 19 kilos of marijuana from the network in the operation,
alongside more than a kilo and a half of cocaine and two pounds of
methamphetamine. [@4] Those detained also had nine vehicles, more than 6,000
dollars, a handgun, drugs and 23 cell phones seized. [@4]

Police said the network distributed the Guatemalan drugs through an organised
network of InDriver platform drivers for delivery to clients by order across the
San Salvador metropolitan area. [@1] Prosecutors said the group was also
organised to commit armed robberies of people in La Libertad department. [@3]
Prosecutors said the investigation with anti-narcotics agents had run for more
than a year, and attributed cumulative seizures since April 2020 of 1,173.295
grams of cocaine, 19,362.813 grams of marijuana and 816.116 grams of
methamphetamine to the structure. [@4] The detainees were to be sent to a
specialised court in the jurisdiction in the following hours. [@3]
