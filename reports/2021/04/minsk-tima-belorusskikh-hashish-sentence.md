Moscow District Court in Minsk sentenced 22-year-old singer Timofey Morozov,
known as Tima Belorusskikh, to two years of restriction of freedom without
referral to a correctional facility. [@1] His associate Anton Krivetsky was
sentenced alongside him to the same type of restriction of freedom. [@2] The
court found Morozov guilty of storing drugs without intent to sell. [@1] Morozov
admitted guilt and expressed remorse. [@1] After he was charged, Morozov was
released on bail of 8,700 Belarusian rubles while Krivetsky remained in custody.
[@1]

Police said officers seized 0.4 grams of hashish and 0.03 grams of amphetamine
from Morozov on the night of 6 January. [@1] Prosecutors said the pair had
bought more than 1.96 grams of hashish online in December 2020 and January 2021
and used part of it in a Minsk gaming club before police found the remainder.
[@2] The charge sheet listed 1.9609 grams of hashish and 0.0375 grams of
amphetamine covering purchase, use, transport and storage. [@2] The case was
charged under part 1 of article 328 of Belarus Criminal Code, which carried up
to five years in prison. [@1,4]

The two said they bought the drugs online through dead drops. [@1] In August
2020 Morozov had publicly supported Belarusians who protested after the
presidential election. [@1,4] Early media claims that Belorusskikh had been
detained with several kilograms of hashish were false. [@1,4]

Police detained Morozov on the night of 6 January in a gaming club in Minsk, which Onliner reports as the location the Investigative Committee gave. [@3,4] The head of the Investigative Committee's Minsk directorate said urgent investigative steps were taken and chemical and forensic-genetic examinations were assigned to the State Committee of Forensic Examinations. [@3]
