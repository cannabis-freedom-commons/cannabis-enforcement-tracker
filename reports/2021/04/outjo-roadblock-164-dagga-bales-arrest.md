A 32-year-old man was arrested at a roadblock at Outjo in the Kunene region
after he was allegedly found in possession of 164 bales of dagga, the Namibian
name for cannabis. [@1] Kunene police commissioner James Nderura confirmed the
arrest. [@1] The suspect was held in custody at Outjo Police Station. [@1] He
was due to appear at the Outjo Magistrate's Court the next day in connection
with possession of drugs. [@1]

Police did not give the value of the bales. [@1] Nderura said police were
exposing those dealing in cannabis and asked the public to give the police
information, saying they want a drug-free nation. [@1]
