Police arrested a 23-year-old man from Graham Street, Plaisance, on the East
Coast Demerara after a cordon and search at his home. [@1] The search ran from
21:50 to 22:30 on Tuesday, 20 April 2021. [@1] Ranks found 13 transparent
plastic parcels, each holding leaves, seeds and stems. [@2] They also found 17
bills of GYD $5,000 suspected to be counterfeit. [@1]

The suspected cannabis was weighed at Sparendaam Police Station and came to
2,075 grams. [@2] The man was held in custody pending charges. [@2]
