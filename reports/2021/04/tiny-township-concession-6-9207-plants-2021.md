Southern Georgian Bay OPP officers visited a farm on Concession Road 6 East in
Tiny Township on 19 April 2021 after residents complained of a possible
cannabis grow operation. [@1] The next day, the Provincial Joint Forces
Cannabis Enforcement Team with the Community Street Crime Unit and OPP officers
executed a search warrant on the property. [@1]

Officers seized 9,207 cannabis plants, 2,448 clones and 80 lb of cannabis bud
(about 36.29 kg), along with a loaded and insecure .22-calibre rifle. [@1]
Tiny Township's bust was confirmed months later when residents raised the
Concession Road 6 East seizure of over 9,000 plants at a virtual town hall,
their first chance to discuss it since the April raid. [@2]

No charges had been issued at the time of the April report, and the
investigation continued. [@1]
