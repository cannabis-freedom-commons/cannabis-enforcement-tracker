Senan units intercepted a go-fast boat south of Punta Mala, in Los Santos
province, and found seven henequén sacks holding 335 packages of marijuana
weighing 242.9 kilograms. [@2] Four men were detained in that operation, two
Panamanian and two Colombian, and a go-fast boat was seized. [@3] The Los Santos
drug prosecutor's office brought the four before a judge of guarantees, who was
asked to impose precautionary measures. [@3]

In a separate operation the same day, police intelligence units seized 919
packages of undetermined weight and type about ten miles off Punta Mala, in
Pedasí district, and detained two unidentified foreigners. [@1] Senan officers
and anti-drug prosecutors also seized 110 packages of undetermined type and
weight in the Archipiélago de las Perlas area and detained four unidentified
people there. [@1] The two national forces reported 1,364 packages seized and
ten people detained across the three operations. [@1]

At an intermediate hearing the following January, a court sentenced one of the
detainees from the 335-package boat, a man identified as Cándido Rodríguez, to
100 months in prison and 48 months' disqualification from public office after he
accepted a plea agreement for international drug trafficking. [@2]
