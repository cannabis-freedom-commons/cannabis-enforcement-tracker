The Drugs and Narcotics Division with area personnel executed a search at a
residence at Calle Clave #26, Urb. Victoria in Aguadilla, where it seized
controlled substances. [@1] Officers arrested 24-year-old David A. Madera Dapena
in the operation. [@1] Officers seized 44 marijuana plants, marijuana stem
leaves and growing equipment from him. [@1]

The division's agents, supervised by Sgt. Carlos Vélez, consulted prosecutor
Silda Rubio. [@1] The prosecutor ordered the detainee to remain in the holding
cell until the morning for the filing of charges. [@1]
