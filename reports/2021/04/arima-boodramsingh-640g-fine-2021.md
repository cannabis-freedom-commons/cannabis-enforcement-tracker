Clement Boodramsingh, a 32-year-old man living on Cocorite Road in Arima, was
fined $9,000 for possession of marijuana. [@2] If he failed to pay the fine, the
alternative was 30 days’ hard labour. [@1] He appeared virtually before
Magistrate Debby-Ann Bassaw at the Arima Magistrates’ Court. [@2]

The case followed a police warrant search of his home. [@2] Police said his
arrest took place during an anti-crime exercise conducted by officers of the
Area East Operations Unit, members of the Canine Unit and the Trinidad and
Tobago Air Guard (TTAG). [@2] On 22 April, between 4 p.m. and 8 p.m., officers
executed a search warrant at his home. [@2] They found marijuana weighing 640
grammes in the house. [@2] Officers then arrested him, took him to La Horquetta
Police Station and subsequently charged him. [@2]

Sergeant Caldon and Corporal Durity supervised the operation. [@2]
