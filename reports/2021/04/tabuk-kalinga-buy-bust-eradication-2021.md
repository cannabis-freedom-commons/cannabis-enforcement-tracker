Police arrested three people and destroyed 20,000 marijuana plants in separate
anti-drug operations in Kalinga on 29 April 2021. [@1,2] Cordillera police
director Brig. Gen. Ronald O. Lee named the three as Carlos Bocad Banosan, 27,
Mylene Bullay Sola-og, 25, both of San Pedro village in Rizal town, and Ninoy
Dalacnas Addawe, 30, of Tabuk. [@1,2]

Joint officers from the regional intelligence division, Quezon City police and
the national police drug enforcement group arrested Banosan and Sola-og after
they sold marijuana to an undercover buyer in Bulanao, Tabuk City. [@1,2]
Officers seized 45 marijuana bricks and eight tubular packs of dried marijuana
weighing about 49 kg in all, valued at 5.88 million pesos. [@1,2] Sola-og had
been monitored selling drugs in parts of Kalinga, according to the
operatives. [@1,2]

Addawe was caught in a separate buy-bust in Bulanao Norte, but for selling
suspected shabu rather than cannabis. [@1,2] Officers took half a gram worth
3,400 pesos, buy-bust cash, a phone and a motorcycle from him. [@1,2] All the
seized evidence was documented and inventoried on site in front of witnesses,
and all three suspects were held facing charges under the drugs law. [@1,2]

In Tinglayan town, operatives uprooted and burned 20,000 fully grown
marijuana plants at two sites in Loccong covering 2,000 square metres, worth
about 4 million pesos. [@2] No cultivator was caught there. [@2]
