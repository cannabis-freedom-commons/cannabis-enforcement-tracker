Central Narcotics Bureau officers arrested a 22-year-old Malaysian man in the late afternoon of 16 April 2021 in the vicinity of Choa Chu Kang Avenue 4 on suspicion of drug trafficking, recovering four bundles with about 3,915 grams of cannabis from him. [@1] Officers then searched his hideout in a residential unit in the same area, seizing a further 20 bundles with about 19,772 grams of cannabis from the storeroom along with heroin, Ice and Ecstasy tablets. [@1]

In total the operation recovered about 23,687 grams of cannabis, the bureau's largest cannabis seizure since 1996, as well as its largest heroin seizure since 2001. [@1,2] The drugs were estimated to be worth more than S$2.3 million altogether. [@1,2] Singapore has a zero-tolerance policy for drugs, and trafficking more than 500 grams of cannabis can carry the death penalty. [@2]
