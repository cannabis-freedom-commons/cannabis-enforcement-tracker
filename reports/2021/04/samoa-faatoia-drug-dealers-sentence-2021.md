Samoa's Supreme Court sentenced four people convicted over a December 2019
police raid at Fa'atoia, near Apia, in which police found marijuana and
methamphetamine in a bedroom. [@1] Ana Fuimaono, 12 weeks pregnant, and Stewart
Tuitama were jailed after Justice Vui Clarence Nelson drew from the trial
evidence that she dealt in marijuana and he dealt in methamphetamine, known in
Samoa as ice. [@1] A later Samoa Global News account records that Fuimaono was
arrested with four men and another woman when police found guns and narcotics in
a Faatoia house while responding to a domestic violence call on Boxing Day 2019.
[@2] That account also records that Fuimaono was convicted in February 2021 and
sentenced to two years in April 2021. [@2]

AJ Roache, whom the court described as having a drug and alcohol problem, was
jailed for two years on all the charges arising from the incident, including
possession of two grams of ice. [@1] Sanele Schuster, whose culpability the
court found the least, avoided prison and was fined $2,000 for taking part in
the smoking party by smoking marijuana. [@1] The charge of possessing
methamphetamine against Schuster was dismissed. [@1] Fuimaono, Roache and
Tuitama pleaded not guilty to the joint charges of possessing marijuana and
methamphetamine and were found guilty after the trial. [@1]

The evidence came largely from Pitoitua Aloese, a co-defendant who became the
prosecution's star witness and testified to a gathering at his Fa'atoia house on
26 December 2019. [@1] Aloese told the court that Fuimaono arrived with a bag of
marijuana and that Tuitama, half an hour later, exchanged methamphetamine for
marijuana with her. [@1] According to the police evidence reported at
sentencing, police convened a large raiding party to enter and search Aloese's
property at Fa'atoia. [@1]
