Working on the instructions of Cordillera police regional director Brig. Gen.
Ronald O. Lee, operatives of the Police Regional Office Cordillera and
PDEA-Cordillera found four marijuana plantation sites in Barangay Ngibat,
Tinglayan, and destroyed them in a three-day operation from 12 to 14 April
2021. [@1,2] The four sites covered a combined 4,300 square metres, planted
with 43,000 fully grown marijuana plants worth 8.6 million pesos at the
standard drug price. [@1,2]

Operatives also found 400 grams of dried marijuana leaves, worth 48,000 pesos,
at the first plantation site. [@2] In all, 8,648,000 pesos of plants and dried
leaves were uprooted and burned on site. [@2] Kalinga police chief Col. Davy
Vicente Limmong reported the discovery of the four sites during the operation.
