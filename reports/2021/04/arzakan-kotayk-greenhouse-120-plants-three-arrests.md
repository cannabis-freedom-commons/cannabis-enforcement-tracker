On 4 April 2021, officers searched a house in the village of Arzakan in Kotayk
as part of a criminal case handled by the Kotayk regional investigative
department, jointly with National Security Service officers. [@1] Three Yerevan
residents were detained the same day on suspicion of organising the sowing and
growing of cannabis in an especially large amount by prior agreement. [@1]

Officers seized 120 cannabis bushes grown in 97 pots as cannabis in an
especially large amount. [@2] They also seized yellowish-green material with a
cannabis smell weighing 2 grams and 32 grams. [@3] Officers also seized two
glass flasks, a syringe and a glass container with an inscription, and found
buckets holding clear liquid with sediment on the floor. [@1]

Armenia's Investigative Committee reported the find as a greenhouse operation
for growing cannabis in a Kotayk village. [@1] The preliminary investigation was
continuing. [@1]
