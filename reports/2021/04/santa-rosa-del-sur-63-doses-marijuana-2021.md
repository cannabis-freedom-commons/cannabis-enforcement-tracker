Officers of the National Community Surveillance Model by Quadrants captured a
54-year-old man known as “Feliciano” in Santa Rosa del Sur, Bolívar, for alleged
trafficking, manufacturing and carrying of narcotics. [@1] The man was caught in
the act while trying to bring marijuana camouflaged among rolls of toilet paper
into the cells of the municipal police station for detainees. [@1] Police said
63 doses of marijuana were seized from him after the duty unit detected the
drug. [@1]

The duty police unit detected the drug, seized it and read the 54-year-old his
rights as a captured person. [@1] The captured man and the seized substance were
placed at the disposal of the Attorney General’s Office, with a judge to define
his legal situation. [@1] The Magdalena Medio Police Department presented the
result as part of its work to maintain calm in the region. [@1] Police invited
the public to report drug-trafficking information through National Anti-Drug
Hotline 167. [@1]
