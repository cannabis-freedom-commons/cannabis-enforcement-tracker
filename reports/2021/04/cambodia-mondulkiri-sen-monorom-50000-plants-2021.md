Police in Mondulkiri province destroyed a marijuana plantation of more than one
hectare in Sen Monorom City on Sunday, 4 April 2021. [@1] Major Nuon Ratanak,
deputy chief of Sen Monorom City police, said district police officers and the
Mondulkiri province Anti-drugs Department raided the plantation at 10.30am in
the valley of a mountain in Pousam village, Sen Monorom commune. [@1] The
plantation owner fled before police arrived, and the officer said police were
searching for him to bring him to justice. [@1]

Police seized and destroyed about 50,000 marijuana plants described as tall as
trees and weighing nearly 400 kilogrammes, along with other drug-related
materials. [@1] The owner is accused of planting marijuana without authorisation
and faces up to five years in prison if convicted, Major Ratanak said. [@1]

Police learned of the plantation on the previous day, after a villager found it
while searching for wild bees and tree fruit. [@1] The officer called on
villagers not to plant marijuana or deal in drugs because it is against the law.
