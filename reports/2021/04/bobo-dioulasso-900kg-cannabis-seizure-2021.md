The Bobo-Dioulasso Mobile Customs Brigade seized 900 kilograms of cannabis and
five motorcycles in an ambush overnight near the city. [@1] The operation ran
between 1 and 2 a.m. in the night of Saturday 24 to Sunday 25 April 2021, about
forty kilometres from Bobo-Dioulasso. [@1] The five motorcycles were used to
carry the cannabis, the account says. [@1]

The brigade is a key unit of the subdivision of the Western Regional Customs
Directorate. [@1] After receiving a tip, the brigade's chief, Chief Inspector of
Customs Téghawendé Macaire Yameogo, sent a team to a route used by the
traffickers. [@1] The cannabis was valued at 92,500,000 CFA francs, after the
opening paragraph described the haul as worth more than 90 million. [@1] The
Director General of Customs urged residents to work with customs officers
against fraud and to report suspicious cases to the nearest customs service.
