CBP Office of Field Operations officers at the Pembina Port of Entry targeted a
commercial shipment that had arrived from Manitoba on 23 April 2021 and, on
inspection, found 2,796 pounds of marijuana (about 1,268 kg) inside a load
manifested as straw. [@1,2] The Manitoba truck had been referred to secondary
inspection before officers found the drugs concealed in the straw load. [@1,2]

CBP valued the haul at more than $4.5 million. [@1,2] The two truckers, who
were not identified, were turned over to the Pembina County Sheriff's Department
for prosecution. [@2] Homeland Security Investigations and the sheriff's
department had assisted CBP, and CBP said its investigation was continuing. [@1]
Area Port Director Jason Schmelz said the interdiction keeps narcotics from
entering the United States. [@1]
