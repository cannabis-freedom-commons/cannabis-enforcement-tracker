Constable Brian Dube, 27, a serving officer stationed at ZRP Gutu, was arrested
while carrying 50 kg of dagga by car from Chipinge to Chiredzi. [@1] He faces a
charge of unlawful possession of dangerous drugs. [@1] Dagga is the southern
African name for cannabis, which the article also calls mbanje, the Zimbabwean
term. [@1]

According to an internal police memo quoted by the outlet, officers from ZRP
Ndali were manning a roadblock on the Ngundu–Tanganda road at about 3am on 4
August 2021. [@1] They stopped a Honda Fit with two male occupants, Dube at the
wheel, who showed a police identity card and said he was stationed at ZRP
Beitbridge rather than Gutu. [@1] Sergeant Gwara let the car go but then smelled
dagga and sent three constables after it in a private vehicle; about 3 km on,
the officers found the car parked and its occupants running away. [@1]

A search of the car found a 50 kg sack of loose dagga, and the vehicle was
impounded and taken to ZRP Ndali. [@1] The case was referred to the Criminal
Investigations Department in Chiredzi, which established that Dube was stationed
at ZRP Gutu and called him in. [@1] On 5 August 2021 Dube handed himself over at
CID Chiredzi with his lawyer, Munyaradzi Vengesai of Mugiya and Muvhami law
chambers. [@2] Africa-Press published a shortened version of the same leaked
memo, naming the same officer, charge, roadblock, weight and surrender date.
[@2]
