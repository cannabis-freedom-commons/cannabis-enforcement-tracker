On 12 August 2021, the Brussels federal judicial police carried out several
searches in a federal prosecutor's drug-trafficking case. [@1] Officers found a
container holding nearly nine tonnes of cannabis in a warehouse at Wavre. [@1]
RTBF said its reporting on the find was confirmed by federal prosecution
spokesperson Wenke Roggen. [@1]

The container had previously passed through the port of Antwerp. [@1] The
cannabis was a brownish powder, an unusual form compared with the compressed
resin blocks or herbal cannabis usually seized. [@1] It was hidden in more than
2,000 bags of durum-wheat couscous semolina stacked on about 50 wooden pallets.
[@1]

Investigators carried out 11 searches in Wavre, the Brussels region, Liège and
Spa, detaining several people. [@1] Three arrest warrants were issued. [@1] An
investigating judge charged the three suspects with participation in a criminal
organisation and drug trafficking in association. [@1]
