Deputies with the Merced County Sheriff’s Office developed information about
several marijuana cultivation sites across the county and obtained search
warrants for them. [@1] The warrants resulted in the eradication of about 71,000
marijuana plants. [@1] Deputies also seized about 12,000 pounds of finished
marijuana, 29 firearms and an undisclosed amount of cash. [@1]

Sheriff Vern Warnke said all outdoor marijuana grows are illegal in the county
and would be prosecuted to the full extent of the law. [@1] He said the
sheriff’s office was enforcing cultivation laws the county put in place in 2018,
which allow only six plants grown indoors per residence. [@1]

The announcements came as apparent arrest orders that the agents had executed
against the cannabis crops destroyed thousands of plants and led to the seizure
of arms, processed drugs and an unspecified amount of cash. [@3] Univision
reported the eradication of 71,000 marijuana plants in the county over recent
months, with 12,000 pounds of processed cannabis and 29 firearms seized. [@2]
