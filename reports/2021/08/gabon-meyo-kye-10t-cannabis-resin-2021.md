Gabon's anti-drug police seized more than ten tonnes of cannabis resin at
Meyokyé, a locality in the north of the country on the border with Cameroon.
[@1] The seizure was announced in a police statement reported on Friday 13
August 2021. [@2] Officers arrested the suspected drug traffickers at Meyokyé in
the same operation. [@1]

According to the police statement, the suspects had placed the haul in the boot
of a car travelling toward Libreville. [@2] The suspects intended to sell the
cannabis products in Libreville. [@2] Officers searched the car and seized the
packages containing more than ten tonnes of resin intended for trafficking. [@1]
Police estimated the market value of the seized resin at more than 90 million
CFA francs. [@1]

Police described the operation as part of the security efforts carried out by
anti-drug police officers. [@2] Both accounts say seizures of chanvre indien,
Indian hemp, have multiplied in Gabon. [@2] According to anti-drug police
statistics, about 950 tonnes of chanvre indien were seized in the country
between 2019 and 2020. [@1]
