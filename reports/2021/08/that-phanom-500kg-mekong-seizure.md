A drug-suppression team of the Mekong Riverine Unit found ten sacks on the bank of the Mekong river near Ban Khap Phuang in tambon Nam Kam, That Phanom district, late on Tuesday night 31 August 2021. [@1,2] The sacks held 500 slabs of compressed dried marijuana weighing one kilogram each, 500 kg in total. [@1,2]

The unit's Nakhon Phanom commander, Rear Admiral Jaraskiat Chaiyaphan, said the sacks were believed to have been brought across the river by boat from Laos and left on the bank to be collected by a smuggling network. [@1,2] Nobody was arrested. [@1,2]
