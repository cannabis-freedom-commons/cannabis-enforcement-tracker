Border Security Force troops of the Guwahati Frontier arrested three
Bangladeshi nationals, a man, a woman and a girl, at around 12:45 pm on 24
August 2021 near East Digaltari village in Cooch Behar district. [@1,2] The
three were trying to cross the international boundary into India illegally.
[@1,2] They were handed over to the Border Guard Bangladesh through a flag
meeting. [@1,2]

In overnight operations on 24 to 25 August the same troops seized 19.5 kg of
cannabis and 45 bottles of phensydyl in Cooch Behar district at the
India-Bangladesh border. [@1,2] The BSF said it acted on specific information
and had put its troops on high alert because a state government programme at
Salmara in Assam was expected to draw heavy crowds. [@2] The force described
the finds as another success against cross-border smuggling and illegal
infiltration. [@1,2]
