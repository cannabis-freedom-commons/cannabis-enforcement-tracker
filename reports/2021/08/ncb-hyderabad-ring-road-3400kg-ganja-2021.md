A joint team of the NCB's Bengaluru and Hyderabad units stopped a Maharashtra-registered truck at a toll booth on Hyderabad's ring road on Saturday 28 August 2021. [@1,2] They arrested three men from Latur district in Maharashtra: D Shinde, M R Kamble and N Jogdand. [@1,2]

The truck carried 3,400 kg of high-grade ganja packed in 141 gunny bags, hidden under tarpaulin sheets and nursery saplings. [@1,2] Zonal Director Amit Ghawate valued the haul at about Rs 21 crore. [@1,2]

The NCB said the consignment came from the Andhra-Odisha border area and was arranged by a Maharashtra kingpin for syndicates in Pune, Mumbai, Thane and other states, which peddle the drug in colleges and parties. [@1,2] The agency noted it had already seized over 7,500 kg of ganja that year by the end of August. [@1]
