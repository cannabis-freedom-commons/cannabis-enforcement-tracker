Police in Humera seized about 56 quintals (5,600 kg) of hashish hidden under sugarcane in a truck, in a two-day operation conducted with the residents of the town. [@1,2] The zonal police department said the seizure took place in Humera town of the Wolkait Tegede Setit Humera Zone. [@1,2]

An unspecified number of suspects were captured and an investigation was under way to identify the origin of the consignment. [@1,2]

## Police claim

The head of the zonal police crime prevention unit, Commander Atinkut Ayalew, claimed the hashish may have been intended for use by the TPLF, which parliament had designated a terrorist entity earlier that year. [@1,2] He commended the community of Humera for its part in the operation and urged residents to keep cooperating with security checks. [@2]
