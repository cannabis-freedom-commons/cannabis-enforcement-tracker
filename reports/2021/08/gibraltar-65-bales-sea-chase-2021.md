On 18 August 2021, four men in a rigid inflatable boat powered by three Yamaha engines carried 65 bales of cannabis resin through British Gibraltar Territorial Waters toward the Spanish coast. [@2] Royal Gibraltar Police vessel Bravo 3 pursued them until they left Gibraltar waters, recovering 15 bales the crew had jettisoned; when the boat re-entered, the Customs ship HMC Searcher took up the chase. [@2] After more bales were thrown overboard and the vessels collided, officers boarded the boat and arrested all four men. [@2] The RGP put the haul at about 2.6 tonnes of cannabis worth around £13 million. [@1]

The men pleaded guilty in November 2022 to possessing the cannabis and received prison terms of between three years and two months and three years and four months; one of them admitted damaging HMC Searcher and received an additional month. [@2] They were acquitted of importing the drugs in January 2023, and the Court of Appeal dismissed the Attorney-General's challenge to those acquittals on 15 March 2024, with no retrial to follow. [@2]
