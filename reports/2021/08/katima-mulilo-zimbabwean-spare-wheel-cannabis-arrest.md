Police in the Zambezi region arrested a 28-year-old Zimbabwean man on Thursday
12 August 2021 for possession of cannabis. [@1] Zambezi region police
spokesperson inspector Kisco Sitali said the arrest happened around 09h30 in
Bebi location of Katima Mulilo. [@1] Sitali said the cannabis had been found
hidden inside a spare wheel of a vehicle. [@1]

Police put the street value of the cannabis at N$21 950. [@1] The suspect could
not be named before his appearance at the Katima Mulilo Magistrates' Court on
Monday, where he was to face the possession case. [@1]

Sitali urged residents to keep alerting police to people dealing in cannabis or
other illegal products by calling the station or leaving tips in police
suggestion boxes around town. [@1]

The article gives no weight for the cannabis seized. [@1]
