Micheal Awong, 56, of Diego Martin, had been wanted by police for about three
years on three outstanding Time Allowed Commitment warrants. [@1] Officers
arrested Awong on Oropouche Road, Sangre Grande, at about 4:45 pm during an
anti-crime exercise covering Sangre Grande and Biche. [@1] He was subsequently
charged with the cannabis offences and was due to appear at the Sangre Grande
Magistrates' Court the next day. [@1]

Police arrested him for possession of 131 grammes of cannabis and for
cultivating 38 fully grown cannabis trees. [@1]

Charges were laid by PC Nanan. [@1] The exercise ran between 4 pm and 8 pm, was
coordinated by Snr Supt Joseph Chandool and spearheaded by Ag Sgts Khan and
Narine. [@1] It involved officers of the Eastern Division Task Force and two
members of the Trinidad and Tobago Regiment. [@1]
