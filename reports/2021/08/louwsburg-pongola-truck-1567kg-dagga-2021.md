Police received intelligence on the morning of Tuesday 24 August 2021 about a
truck transporting dagga from Pongola towards Vryheid, and units of the South
African Police Service together with other law enforcement agencies mounted
an operation to intercept it. [@1] Officers spotted the truck on the R69 road
in the Louwsburg policing area, stopped it and searched it, finding 33 bales
of dry dagga weighing 1,567.5 kg. [@1]

The two men in the truck, aged 43 and 46, were arrested and were due to
appear before the Louwsburg Magistrate's Court. [@1] Police put the street
value of the seized dagga at about R3 million. [@1]
