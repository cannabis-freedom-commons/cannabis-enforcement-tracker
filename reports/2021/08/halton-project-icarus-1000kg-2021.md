The Halton Regional Police Service in Ontario concluded Project Icarus, the
largest drug seizure in the force's history, ending in 12 arrests and 44
charges. [@1,2] The investigation began with a single suspect in October 2020
and grew into a joint operation with Criminal Intelligence Service Ontario,
Peel Regional Police and the Canada Border Services Agency, executing 130
judicial warrants across the Greater Toronto Area. [@1,2]

Police seized more than 1,000 kg of cannabis in bulk, edible and resin forms,
alongside cocaine, ecstasy and opioids including fentanyl. [@1] Officers also
recovered two Glock handguns, a shotgun with 1,100 rounds of ammunition, more
than $100,000 in cash, jewellery and three vehicles. [@1] The operation
disrupted a cocaine-processing site and illegal cannabis-processing
facilities. [@1]

## Charges

Twelve people from Ontario were charged, mostly with possession of a controlled
substance for the purpose of trafficking. [@1] A full breakdown of the charges
was published on the police service's website. [@1]
