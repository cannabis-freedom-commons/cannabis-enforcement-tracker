Authorities surprised a man transporting 266 packages of creepy-type marijuana
weighing 200 kilos in a vehicle at calle 79 A con carrera 30 in the Manrique
neighbourhood of Medellín. [@1] The detained man is 31 years old. [@1] He was
described as an alleged coordinator of the common organised criminal group La
30, serving the combo La Terraza. [@1] Investigators said the seized drug came
from the department of Valle del Cauca and was to be sold and distributed in the
area known as Lovaina in northeastern Medellín. [@1]

The operational commander of the Metropolitan Police of the Valle de Aburrá said
the marijuana was intended for sale as microtrafficking in about 200,000 doses.
[@1] The captured person, the drugs and the seized vehicle were placed at the
disposal of the Attorney General's Office. [@1]

He must answer for the offence of manufacture, carrying and trafficking of
narcotics. [@1] Authorities said that so far that year 3,740 people had been
captured for drug trafficking offences in the Valle de Aburrá, 2,801 of them in
Medellín, with 4,930 kilos seized in the valley and 2,332 of those in Medellín.
[@1]
