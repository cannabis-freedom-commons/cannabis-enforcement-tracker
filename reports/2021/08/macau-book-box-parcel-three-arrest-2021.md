Three Macau men were arrested on Thursday 5 August 2021 for trafficking cannabis
concealed in a book-shipping box sent to Macau via the mainland. [@1] The
suspects were a 24-year-old air-conditioning repair worker surnamed Vong, a
25-year-old jobless man surnamed Chan and a 57-year-old chef surnamed Wu. [@1]
On 5 August at about 5 p.m. the two younger men rode motorcycles to a parcel
collection shop in Sha Li Tou, where Wong took a parcel from a 24-hour smart
locker while Chan kept watch outside. [@3]

Judiciary Police spokesman Leng Kam Lun said officers seized a total of 1,661
grammes of cannabis with an estimated street value of 1.66 million patacas. [@1]
Officers seized 668.43 grammes of cannabis hidden inside a book shipping box in
the collected parcel. [@1] Officers later found two packets of cannabis weighing
517 grammes and 38 grammes plus drug-taking paraphernalia at Chan's home in
Patane district. [@1] Officers seized 437.63 grammes of cannabis and drug-taking
paraphernalia at Wu's home in Fai Chi Kei. [@1]

Vong and Chan said they had jointly paid 140,000 patacas for 2,000 grammes of
cannabis on 28 July. [@1] Vong and Chan told police they had sold 1,000 grammes
of the cannabis to Wu. [@2] Wu told police he had resold 10-gramme packets for
8,000 patacas to customers in night entertainment venues. [@2] Leng said both
Chan and Wu were drug users and police were continuing to investigate the source
of the drugs. [@1]

The Judiciary Police were notified by the Zhuhai Public Security Bureau the
previous Monday that a parcel believed to contain drugs was being sent from
overseas to Macau via Guangxi. [@1] Zhuhai police and customs, Guangxi police,
local police and Macau Customs jointly tracked the parcel to a pickup point near
Patane district. [@1] The three suspects were transferred to the Public
Prosecutions Office on Friday facing trafficking in narcotics and psychotropic
substances charges, while Chan and Wu also faced possession of drug-taking
paraphernalia charges. [@2]
