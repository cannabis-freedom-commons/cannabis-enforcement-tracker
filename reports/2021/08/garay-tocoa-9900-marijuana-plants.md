The Dirección de Lucha Contra el Narcotráfico (DLCN), Honduras's directorate for
the fight against drug trafficking, working with the Fiscalía Regional de
Litoral Atlántico, the regional prosecutor's office for the Atlantic coast,
seized and then eradicated a marijuana plantation in the department of Colón, on
the Caribbean coast of Honduras. [@1] The Public Ministry said the eradication
was carried out on Wednesday, 4 August 2021, at a plantation that had just been
located in the department. [@2]

Detectives of the DLCN anti-drug department found the plantings in a mountainous
area of aldea Garay, a village in the municipality of Tocoa. [@3] The
cultivation was on two plots of land sown with the drug, the first of about half
a manzana and the second of two and a half manzanas. [@1]

The authorities eradicated 9,900 marijuana plants in all, 900 from one plot and
9,000 from the other. [@2] The DLCN said its investigation would continue in
order to identify the people or the criminal organisation harvesting and
trafficking the drug. [@2]

Proceso Digital reported that, so far in 2021, the authorities had seized a
total of 17,520 pounds of marijuana and had cut and incinerated 120,262
marijuana plants, figures given as a national year-to-date total rather than as
the yield of the Garay plots. [@1]
