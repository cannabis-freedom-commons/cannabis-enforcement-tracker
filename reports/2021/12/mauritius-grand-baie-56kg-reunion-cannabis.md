Mauritian officers intercepted a speedboat at Grand-Baie. [@1] Inspectors had
been waiting for the boat thanks to information from Réunion gendarmes. [@1] A
National Coast Guard patrol noticed the speedboat. [@2] The occupants abandoned
the boat near the beach opposite the Royal Palm hotel. [@2] Officers from ADSU
Northern and ADSU Central were called to the scene. [@2] Investigators have been
reviewing CCTV and Safe City camera footage to identify the fugitives, who are
still sought. [@2]

Officers found 11 sacks holding 56.1 kilos of cannabis in the speedboat. [@2]
Officers also found three plastic sacks holding 3.31 kilos of hashish in the
same boat. [@2] Officers also found 37 boxes of cigarette rolling paper in the
boat. [@2] Officers also found 20 boxes of psychotropes in the speedboat. [@2]
The load was expected to be sold easily for the end-of-year festivities. [@1]
The drugs were reported to have come from Réunion. [@2]

Réunion outlets describe the 56 kg load as zamal from Réunion. [@3] On 17
November 2021, Réunion officers had intercepted a Mauritian boat about a month
earlier. [@1] Officers had found 33 kg of zamal aboard that first boat. [@1] It
was that first case that led the sister island’s officers to intercept the
second load. [@1]
