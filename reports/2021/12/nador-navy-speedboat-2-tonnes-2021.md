On 24 December 2021 a Moroccan navy unit on Mediterranean patrol chased a suspected speedboat off the coast of Nador. [@1,2] The crew threw the drug shipment into the sea and managed to flee. [@1,2]

The navy recovered about two tonnes of cannabis from the water and handed the shipment to the Royal Gendarmerie at the port of Nador. [@1] A second account of the interception confirms the two-tonne haul, the speedboat chase and the crew's escape. [@2]
