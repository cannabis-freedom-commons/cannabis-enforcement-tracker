Two people allegedly linked to a federally classified hate group were charged
with illegally producing marijuana in Alpena County, Michigan. [@1] Police
arrested Tiffany Danielle Williams, 34, of Alpena and formerly of Tennessee, and
John Michael Clayton, 33, of Lawrenceburg, Tennessee, on 13 December 2021. [@1]
The Huron Undercover Narcotics Team said Williams and Clayton were associated
with the group Brotherhood Forever, closely associated with the Aryan Nation.
[@1] Preliminary reports indicated that mature marijuana product or the proceeds
of its sale were destined for Tennessee in support of Brotherhood Forever. [@1]
Williams was charged with manufacturing marijuana and felony firearms. [@1]
Clayton was charged with aiding and abetting the manufacturing of marijuana.
[@1] Both suspects were due back in court on 4 January 2022, and the
investigation was ongoing with possible additional charges. [@1]

The Alpena News reported that Williams and Clayton were held in the Alpena
County Jail with bonds set at $100,000. [@2]
