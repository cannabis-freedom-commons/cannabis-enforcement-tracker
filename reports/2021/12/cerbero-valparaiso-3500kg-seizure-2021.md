On 2 December 2021 Chile's Interior Ministry announced that Operation Cerbero, months of work coordinated by the Valparaíso regional prosecutor with the Navy's Directemar maritime-intelligence unit and Carabineros' OS7 anti-drug department, had broken up an international organisation moving drugs by sea between Colombia and central Chile. [@1] Run by Colombian nationals resident in Chile, the organisation supplied smaller distribution groups across Chilean neighbourhoods; the operation took 3,500 kilos of creepy marijuana off the market, valued by the authorities at more than 11,200 million pesos or about ten million doses, the biggest seizure of the last two to three years. [@1]

## Ten remanded in Viña del Mar

Ten suspects were arrested and formally charged on the Wednesday, 1 December, at the Viña del Mar Guarantee Court, and all were remanded in preventive prison for a 90-day investigation. [@1] The operation also seized four vehicles, almost two million Chilean pesos in cash and 150,000 Colombian pesos. [@1]

## Biggest seizure of 2021

BioBioChile's January 2022 review of maritime trafficking named the 3.5-tonne December seizure in Valparaíso by Directemar as the biggest of the year. [@2] Maritime authorities seized 7,353 tonnes of drugs in Chilean waters in 2021, 6,916 of them processed marijuana, a 62 percent rise on 2020, detaining 380 people in 234 operations. [@2]
