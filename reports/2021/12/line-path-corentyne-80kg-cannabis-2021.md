Customs Anti-Narcotics Unit (CANU) officers seized 80 kilogrammes (176 pounds)
of marijuana in the Corentyne area of Berbice on the evening of 10 December
2021. [@1] CANU put the street value of the cannabis at more than GY$25,000,000.
[@1]

The operation ran on 9 and 10 December between the Sandaka pump station at
Skeldon and the Goat Dam area of Line Path village, to identify routes used to
move narcotics. [@1] On the evening of 10 December officers on patrol along a
dam saw several people in an adjacent cane field, identified themselves and
tried to detain them, but the people ran off in different directions. [@1]

A search of the area found four brown salt bags holding 28 parcels of cannabis
wrapped in transparent plastic. [@1] The parcels were taken to CANU headquarters
and investigations continued. [@1] No one was arrested in the bust; Stabroek
News reported that the suspected owners of the narcotics evaded the officers.
[@2] The parcels weighed a total of 176 pounds, Stabroek News reported. [@2]

Guyana Times also reported the 80 kilograms total and the GY$25 million street
value, and placed the find at the Goat Dam area of Line Path village. [@3] The
St. Kitts-based WINN FM republished the Demerara Waves account, which identifies
CANU as a unit of the Ministry of Home Affairs. [@4]
