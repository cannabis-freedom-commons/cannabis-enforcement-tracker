CDFW said it eradicated 2.6 million illegal cannabis plants, destroyed 487,270
pounds (about 221.1 tonnes) of illegal cannabis flower, served 1,125 search
warrants, seized 794 firearms, removed 32,230 pounds of trash from public lands
and removed 404 illegal water diversions. [@1] The numbers reflect all
operations in which CDFW acted as lead agency or in a support role. [@2]

CDFW investigates illegal cannabis cultivation to curtail environmental damage
such as unauthorized streambed alterations with water diversions, habitat
destruction, illegal pesticides and poaching. [@1] David Bess, CDFW Deputy
Director and Chief of the Law Enforcement Division, said illegal operators
bypassing the legal system threaten California's fish and wildlife and praised
his officers' work with federal, state and county partners. [@2]

At the time CDFW had 68 dedicated cannabis enforcement officers working with
county, state and federal partners. [@2]
