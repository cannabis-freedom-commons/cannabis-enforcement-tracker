On 23 December 2021 the General Inspectorate of Police of the Republic of
Moldova (IGP) and the Prosecutor's Office for Combating Organized Crime and
Special Causes (PCCOCS) uncovered a criminal group specializing in drug dealing.
[@1] Law enforcers detained three people, including the leader of the group.
[@1]

At the suspects' homes, searches found and seized 1.2 kg of the synthetic drug
PVP, 600 grams of marijuana, LSD, mobile phones, bank cards, packaging bags and
scales. [@1] The police valued the seized batch of drugs at approximately 1.3
million lei. [@1] Two of the suspects were placed in preventive detention while
the third remained on release during the investigation. [@1]

The same account was republished by BasTV, a regional outlet of Bessarabia. [@2]
