Turkish gendarmerie seized 1.5 tons of skunk, a potent strain of cannabis, smuggled into the country from Albania in an operation in the Dalaman district of Muğla province. [@1] Three suspects were detained in connection with the shipment. [@1]

The investigation was led by prosecutors in Izmir on intelligence that smugglers would bring a large shipment of drugs into Turkey by sea. [@1] After surveillance by Izmir gendarmerie troops, security forces learned the shipment would arrive aboard a speedboat in Muğla. [@1] Officers captured the suspects as they stashed bags of the drug in a forested area near the coast. [@1]

Albanian President Ilir Meta reacted to the seizure of 1.5 tons of cannabis in Turkey, calling it a sign that hashish was still produced in Albania at a high rate and urging a full investigation to bring the perpetrators to justice. [@2]
