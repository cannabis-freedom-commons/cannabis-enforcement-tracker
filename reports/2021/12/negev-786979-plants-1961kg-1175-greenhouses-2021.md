The Israel Police, with the IDF and the Green Patrol, conducted an ongoing
campaign against cannabis greenhouses in military training zones and across the
Negev. [@1] Inspectors uprooted cannabis plants, piled them on wooden pallets,
doused them in benzine and burned them. [@1] Police said drugs were destroyed on
the spot under a model intended to improve the efficiency of the campaign. [@1]

Police said more than 1,175 cannabis greenhouses had been destroyed since the
start of 2021, most of them in IDF training areas. [@1] Police said they had
seized more than 1,961 kilograms of marijuana ready for distribution and more
than 786,979 marijuana plants. [@1] The street value of the confiscated drugs
was believed to be more than 1 billion shekels. [@1]

Growers irrigated the crops through networks of water pipes taken illegally from
the main water source. [@1] Greenhouses of all sizes growing marijuana were
dotted across the desert from near the Ze’elim army base to the outskirts of
Be’er Sheva and near the Ramon base. [@1] Despite the quantities destroyed, the
number of prosecutions was close to zero. [@1] The Green Patrol director said it
was almost impossible to gather evidence to prosecute an individual for a
specific patch. [@1]
