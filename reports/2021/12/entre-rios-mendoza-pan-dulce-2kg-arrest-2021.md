Gendarmería Nacional, Argentina's national gendarmerie, detained a man in Villa
Nueva, Mendoza province, who had been tasked with collecting a parcel of
cannabis sativa sent from Misiones with Mendoza as its final destination. [@2]
Officers of the force's Mendoza Complex Crimes and Judicial Procedures
Investigations Unit spotted the man near a postal parcel depot after four days
of surveillance as he prepared to pick up the guarded box. [@2] Acting on
instructions from the magistrate handling the case, the gendarmes detained the
man and seized the marijuana and cash. [@2]

The parcel had been intercepted on 11 December 2021 at kilometre 124.5 of Ruta
Nacional 14 in Entre Ríos province, where officers of Escuadrón 6 "Concepción
del Uruguay" were alerted by a narcotics-detection dog while inspecting a
parcel-transport truck. [@1] Inside the box, officers found 2 kilos 64 grams of
marijuana inside four wrappers of pan dulce, a traditional Argentine sweet
bread. [@2]

Federal Court No. 1 of Concepción del Uruguay, headed by Dr. Pablo Andres Seró
with its human rights secretariat under Dr. Mariano Gaspar Fornasari, ordered an
"entrega vigilada", a controlled delivery with tracking and preservation of the
parcel under judicial custody. [@2]
