Moroccan police foiled an attempt to smuggle 8.99 tonnes of cannabis resin hidden in large blocks of marble at a warehouse in Douar Moualin El Oued, Fedalate commune, near Casablanca. [@1,2] The operation took place on Friday 3 December 2021 against a ring planning to export the haul to an African country the police did not name. [@1,2]

Five people were arrested for alleged international drug trafficking, and large sums of money and two cars with fake licence plates were also seized. [@1,2] Two of those held were already wanted by Moroccan police for international drug trafficking, while a third was the subject of a French international arrest warrant in a voluntary manslaughter case. [@1,2] The drugs were concealed inside marble blocks of about ten tonnes each. [@1,2]
