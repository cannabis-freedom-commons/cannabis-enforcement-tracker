On Dec. 17, 2021, Tala Ameer Rattray, a jail employee of the Upson County
Sheriff’s Office, was arrested. [@1] At the time of the announcement he was
described as a former jail employee. [@1] He was charged with violating his oath
of office and with distribution of marijuana and methamphetamine. [@1] According
to Sheriff Dan Kilgore, jail staff had received information and found Rattray
attempting to smuggle tobacco, marijuana and methamphetamine into the facility
for inmates. [@1]

Sheriff Dan Kilgore said he was disappointed by the conduct. [@1] He called it a
disgrace to every honorable and hardworking employee and said he expected
Rattray to be prosecuted to the maximum extent allowable by law. [@1]

After the arrest, Rattray remained in custody with no bond at the Lamar County
Jail. [@1] The incident remained under investigation. [@1]
