Prefectura Naval Argentina officers seized a marijuana consignment in an
overnight operation in Paso de la Patria, a locality in Corrientes province.
[@1] Members of the force, Argentina's national maritime authority, carried out
the seizure under preventive anti-narcotics measures ordered by Security
Minister Sabina Frederic. [@1] The operation began in the early hours of 27 July
2021, when officers on a preventive patrol of the border zone heard the engine
of a boat. [@1]

Near kilometre 1,238 of the Paraná River the officers found several burlap sacks
scattered along the riverbank. [@1] The sacks held 213 "panes", or pressed
blocks, of marijuana weighing more than 150 kilograms. [@1] After being told of
the find, the magistrate handling the case ordered the drug to be confiscated.
[@1] The Security Ministry put the value of the seized marijuana at more than 17
million pesos. [@2]

The force said the haul came a day after its officers in Misiones province
seized another consignment of more than 256 kilos of cannabis. [@1] The
Corrientes outlet FM Gualamba carried the same account the same day, with the
same 213 blocks, the same river kilometre and the same weight of more than 150
kilos. [@3]
