Trinidad and Tobago police arrested four men on 18 July 2021 and took 138 kilos
of marijuana from the trunk of a car on the Uriah Butler Highway. [@3]

Officers of the Special Operations Response Team (SORT), the police tactical
unit, and sniffer dog Tino began Operation Red Fox at 5 am and watched a silver
Nissan Cube in the Moruga district. [@3] Around 7.15 am they intercepted the
Nissan Cube on the northbound lane near the Caroni Bridge and found a large
quantity of marijuana in the trunk. [@1] Officers took the driver, a man from
L'anse Mitan Road in Moruga, and the marijuana to the St Joseph Police Station.
[@1]

Officers then went to Grand Bazaar, Valsayn, and arrested three men police said
were waiting to collect the same marijuana. [@2] One of the three lives at La
Ruffin Road, Moruga, and drove a silver Nissan AD Wagon; another lives at
Roxborough Extension, Diego Martin, and drove a white Nissan Navarra; the third
lives at Topaz Avenue, Diamond Vale, Diego Martin. [@1] The three were also
taken to the St Joseph Police Station and police seized their two vehicles. [@1]
A video shared on social media showed heavily armed officers leading a man out
of the Xtra Foods Supermarket at Grand Bazaar. [@2]

Two days later, on 20 July 2021, Nathan Daniel of Lanse Mitan Road, Moruga
appeared in the Chaguanas Magistrates' Court charged with illegal possession of
138 kilos of marijuana and was denied bail. [@4] Three other people arrested at
Grand Bazaar in connection with the same incident were later released. [@4]
