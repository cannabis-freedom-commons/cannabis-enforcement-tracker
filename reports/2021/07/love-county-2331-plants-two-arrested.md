Officers served a felony search warrant on an unlicensed, Chinese-owned and
operated marijuana grow on the east side of Love County, seizing the site. [@1]
The grow had six structures with plants at various stages of growth. [@1]
Officers seized $3.5 million in marijuana and $65,000 in cash and cashier's
checks believed to be proceeds of illegal narcotics transactions. [@1] Love
County Sheriff Marty Grisham said the search found 2,331 plants worth about
three and a half million dollars. [@2]

Two people, Chong Chen and Zhimou Chen, were arrested on site on charges of
illegal cultivation of marijuana. [@2] The grow was illegal because it held
neither an Oklahoma Medical Marijuana Authority licence nor an Oklahoma Bureau
of Narcotics licence. [@2] The grow's owner had tried to obtain an OMMA licence
but withdrew the application for unknown reasons before growing illegally. [@1]
