Oddar Meanchey provincial anti-drug police arrested three suspects for
questioning over cannabis cultivation in Anlong Veng commune, Anlong Veng
district. [@1] Those held were a man and two women. [@2] They were arrested on
Sunday. [@2] The provincial court later charged all three with illegal planting
of addictive plants under Article 38 of the Law on Drugs Control and placed them
in prison. [@2] Police did not release their identities. [@2] If convicted, each
faces two to five years in prison. [@2] Before the charge, deputy provincial
police chief Thong Sami said he had asked the prosecutor to extend their
detention for a day or two to complete the investigation. [@1]

Officers found the cannabis concealed in a mango plantation after residents
reported a plantation in the commune. [@1] The crop covered 5,500 square metres,
about 0.55 hectares, inside a five-hectare mango farm at Ta Meng village. [@2] A
villager who entered the farm to look for fruit discovered the plants and
alerted police. [@2] Police destroyed the fresh marijuana plants. [@1] Police
seized 36kg of dried marijuana and counted 12,500 marijuana trees at the site.
[@2] Officers also seized a car, a tractor and other cultivation equipment. [@1]

Provincial police chief Major General Huoth Sothy said drug cultivation is
illegal in Cambodia and urged villagers not to plant marijuana or deal drugs.
[@2] Deputy chief Sami declined further details, citing the ongoing
investigation. [@1]
