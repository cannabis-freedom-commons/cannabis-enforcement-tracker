Gabon's anti-drug police seized more than five tonnes of cannabis resin and
arrested the suspected traffickers, the police said in a statement on Thursday 8
July 2021. [@1] According to the police statement, the suspects had come to the
port of Owendo to dispose of the haul by loading it onto ships leaving for the
West. [@1]

Police searching their car found packages containing more than five tonnes of
cannabis resin intended for trafficking. [@1] The police valued the haul at more
than 70 billion CFA francs (about 126.4 million dollars). [@1]

The operation was presented as part of the security efforts of the anti-drug
police, the police statement said. [@1] The statement said seizures of Indian
hemp had multiplied in Gabon in recent months, and that anti-drug police
statistics put about 750 tonnes seized in the country between 2018 and 2019.
[@1]

A second outlet carried the same account on 8 July 2021, reporting that the
anti-drug police of Gabon had seized more than five tonnes of cannabis resin in
a police statement issued that day. [@2]
