Officers of Gendarmería Nacional Argentina's Squadron 32 Aluminé, with the
Zapala complex-crimes investigations unit, mounted a cordon operation to find
out whether drugs were being moved through west-central Neuquén province. [@1]
The gendarmes set up a vehicle checkpoint on Provincial Route 13 at the Litrán
hamlet, where they inspected two vehicles and found sums of money, 1.7 grams of
cocaine and portable communications equipment. [@1] The Zapala federal court
issued search orders for two more vehicles parked in the street and a warrant to
search a cabin where the people involved were staying temporarily. [@1]

Inside a pickup truck at Villa Pehuenia, officers found 22 white burlap sacks,
one of them holding 27 cocaine bricks weighing 29 kilos 35 grams. [@1] The other
21 sacks held 493 marijuana bricks with a total weight of 503 kilos 968 grams,
the figure used for the cannabis seizure here. [@1]

The judge handling the case ordered the detention of the five people involved.
[@1]

The newspaper El Diario de la República, carrying a Télam report, says the
amount of cocaine came to 29.35 kilos and that the court also ordered the drug
held as evidence. [@2] That report also says the vehicle holding the sacks had
been found abandoned in Villa Pehuenia, and the Télam account puts the marijuana
at about half a tonne. [@2]
