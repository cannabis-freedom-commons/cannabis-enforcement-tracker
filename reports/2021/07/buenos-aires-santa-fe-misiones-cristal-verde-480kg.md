Gendarmería Nacional's anti-drug operations squadron carried out 14 simultaneous
searches at addresses in Buenos Aires, Santa Fe and Misiones provinces under an
operation named Cristal Verde. [@1] The searches followed four years of criminal
investigation and information gathered from an informant's confession. [@1]
Seven members of the organisation were detained during the searches. [@1] The
Morón federal criminal and correctional court, Juzgado Federal N° 2, ordered the
detentions and the seizure of the drugs, money, vehicles and weapons. [@1]

Investigative sources told the Télam news agency that the operation was ordered
by Morón federal judge Jorge Rodríguez and came out of a confession in a case
that had seized 2,900 kg of marijuana three years earlier. [@2] The network
carried drugs from Paraguay to Buenos Aires province for sale. [@1] About 500
Gendarmería Nacional officers took part in the field studies and surveillance
across the provinces. [@2] Investigators traced the band's leaders to a house in
Escobar that held its base and a kitchen for making pink and gold pills stamped
"Visa" and "Mastercard". [@2]

Officers seized 480 kilos 252 grams of cannabis sativa. [@1] A Télam account of
the same operation gives the marijuana total as 485.812 kg, valued with the
ecstasy at about 120 million pesos by judicial sources. [@2] The searches also
produced 60 grams of cocaine and 17,304 synthetic pills, which are not cannabis
and are kept out of the cannabis figures. [@1] Investigators said 14,000 pink
pills and 3,000 gold pills were found ready for sale. [@2] Cash seized included
2,775,352 pesos, 189,500 US dollars and 50 euros, in the Gendarmería's own
tally. [@1]

Officers also seized nine vehicles, four motorcycles and a quad bike. [@1] The
force took pistols in calibres 9, 22 and 25, a calibre 16 shotgun and calibre 32
ammunition. [@1] A La Nación account puts the detainees at eight, seven men, two
of them Paraguayan, and one woman, all adults with criminal records. [@3] The
detainees were to be questioned by judge Rodríguez in the following hours or the
next day, once the last procedures and the counting of the seized items were
finished. [@3] Investigators said the inquiry was continuing in search of other
members of the organisation and other places in the synthetic drug distribution
chain. [@2]
