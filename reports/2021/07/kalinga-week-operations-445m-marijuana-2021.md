Government forces in Kalinga seized and burned 44.536 million pesos worth of
marijuana in three operations between 14 and 20 July 2021. [@1] Provincial
police director Col. Davy Vicente Limmong said the haul came from two
eradication raids and one roadside seizure. [@1]

On 20 July, two teams worked simultaneously in Tinglayan town. [@1] One team
of provincial and mobile-force police with regional intelligence and drug
enforcement agents found three plantation sites in Loccong village and
destroyed 21.5 million pesos worth of marijuana. [@1] The other team found
six sites in Butbut Proper village and destroyed 15.2 million pesos worth.
[@1] Officers also destroyed 76,000 fully grown plants found on a 6,700
square metre site. [@1] The growers escaped in both raids. [@1]

Six days earlier, on 14 July, Kalinga police found three sacks of dried
marijuana leaves and stalks abandoned by the national highway at Sitio
Anonang in Liwan West, Rizal town. [@1] The sacks held 53 marijuana bricks
and 24 tubular packs weighing 65.3 kg, worth 7.836 million pesos. [@1]
Police believe the carrier dumped them after spotting intensified checkpoint
checks on vehicles coming from Tabuk City toward Tuguegarao. [@1]
