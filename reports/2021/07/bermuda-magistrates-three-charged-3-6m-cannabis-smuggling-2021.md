Three Bermuda men, aged 27, 31 and 28, appeared in Magistrates' Court on 12 July
2021 charged with conspiring to smuggle more than $3.6 million worth of
cannabis. [@1] The charge alleged the conspiracy ran between an unknown date and
21 December 2019. [@1] The court heard that about 79 pounds (35.8 kilograms) of
drugs were alleged to have been found, and the $3.6 million is the estimated
street value of the cannabis. [@1]

None of the three was required to enter a plea because the case must be heard in
the Supreme Court. [@1] Magistrate Tyrone Chin released all three on $30,000
bail and ordered them to report to Hamilton Police Station three times a week.
[@1] The men were told they must report three times a week to Hamilton Police
Station and surrender their passports. [@1] They were due back in the Supreme
Court on 1 September. [@1]
