Cabo Verde's Judicial Police announced the in-flagrante detention of five people
in the municipality of Tarrafal in an operation against drug trafficking,
including two Nigerians. [@1] Those detained were two women and three men aged
between 20 and 55, including two men of Nigerian nationality. [@1] The
detentions were carried out on Wednesday 28 July in Tarrafal de Santiago on
suspicion of drug trafficking. [@2] The arrests were made by the Assomada
Criminal Investigation Unit on Santiago island. [@3]

Officers acted on eleven search warrants to homes and commercial establishments,
plus searches of vehicles and an agricultural area in Tarrafal. [@1] The
operation was supported by the Judicial Police's Central Directorate of Criminal
Investigation in Praia. [@3] Officers seized drugs described as cannabis and
cocaine, together with money, precision scales and other goods. [@1]

The seizure included 136,806 Cape Verdean escudos, 50 dollars and 110 euros, two
precision scales, unknown powder products and documents. [@2] Those detained
were taken to the Judicial Police delegation in Santa Catarina to await
presentation to the judicial authorities for a first interrogation and coercive
measures. [@3]
