Paraguay's National Anti-Drug Secretariat (Senad) and Brazil's Federal Police dismantled 109 narco camps and eradicated 260 hectares of cannabis in Amambay department during the 10-day Operation Nueva Alianza XXVI, which ran from 6 to 15 July 2021. [@1,2] The forces seized 47,730 kilograms of processed marijuana and estimated the total taken out of circulation at more than 800 tons, counting the destroyed crop. [@1,2]

## Camps, seeds and a financial blow

Air and ground teams with helicopter support from both countries swept Capitán Bado, the María Auxiliadora colony, Pedro Juan Caballero and nearby border zones, detecting camps run by Brazilian and Paraguayan criminal groups. [@1] Officers also destroyed 842,000 marijuana seeds, and Senad said the dismantled camps were the logistics base for processing large volumes bound for Brazilian criminal factions. [@2] The agencies put the financial blow to traffickers at about 24.8 million US dollars. [@1]
