David Allen, 68, served 24 years in the army and four years as a Northumbria
Police officer. [@1] He was jailed for three years and seven months after
admitting producing cannabis. [@1] Allen grew the cannabis on his smallholding
at Calthwaite, a village about 100 metres from the primary school and near
Penrith. [@2]

Cumbria Police detectives went to the smallholding on 20 July 2020 and found
continuous commercial production of cannabis at the property. [@3] The search
found 80 plants nearing full maturity, grown with specialist equipment designed
to maximise yield. [@2] The plants were contained in a fully insulated room with
sophisticated timed lamps, heaters and a working industrial ventilation system.
[@2] A drying area next to the growing room held an insulated tent, drying racks
and vacuum-packing machinery. [@3] Police also found an assortment of knives,
air rifles and a crossbow strategically placed around the farm. [@1] One
detective described the operation as one of the most carefully constructed and
sophisticated set-ups he had ever seen. [@1] Police released body-worn camera
footage of a walk-through of the farm. [@4]

Prosecutor Alaric Walmsley told the court that seven crops could have been
produced over a 140-week period, potentially yielding up to 42kg of cannabis
with a possible wholesale value of between £50,400 and £336,000. [@2] A
financial probe of Allen's personal and business accounts identified unexplained
cash deposits totalling over £425,000 and regular transfers of money to an
ex-partner in the Philippines. [@1] Allen admitted possessing £12,000 in
criminal cash found in a set of bedroom drawers. [@1] Recorder Kate Bex QC said
she was satisfied Allen had a leading role and had directed and organised the
production of cannabis on a commercial scale. [@3] Michael Davies, defending,
said the real story was a national hero through his army service who had stooped
to commit the offence, and that none of the legally-held weapons was loaded.
[@1] Allen was arrested and remanded in custody in November 2020 after telling
an estate agent he was packing to retire in Asia. [@3]
