Dewi Mitchell, a 22-year-old from Ebbw Vale in Wales, was jailed for three years
and four months for bringing cannabis and cocaine to the Isle of Man. [@1] He
was sentenced at the Court of General Gaol Delivery at Douglas Courthouse after
admitting importing the drugs on 28 June. [@2] He had pleaded guilty to two
counts of importing the drugs and two counts of possession. [@2] The court
banned him from visiting the island for five years after his release from
prison. [@1]

Mitchell arrived on the Ben-my-Chree ferry from Heysham shortly after 06:00 BST
on 28 June, the first ferry after Covid border restrictions were eased, planning
to stay for three days of "chill time". [@1] He arrived alone with his
Volkswagen Golf and said he had no links to the island. [@2] Police arrested him
at the Sea Terminal in Douglas in an operation the local paper named Operation
Strongbox. [@2] Officers became suspicious of a Pringles tube in the boot
containing Rizla cigarette papers, which he said he believed were unavailable on
the island. [@2]

A thorough search at police headquarters found a heat-sealed package under the
spare wheel holding £160 of cocaine and £276 of cannabis. [@2] The search also
found cannabis valued at £276, the only cannabis figure recorded. [@2] Mitchell
told police he brought the drugs himself because he feared the quality on the
island would be lower and the price higher than in the United Kingdom. [@2]
Prosecutors accepted that the amounts were relatively small and that he had
brought the drugs for personal use, not to supply anyone else. [@1]

Deemster Graeme Cook said the way the drugs were concealed showed Mitchell knew
they were illegal and had chosen to import them anyway. [@1] He told Mitchell:
"It was your choice to make." [@1] An appeal found the 40-month term manifestly
excessive and cut it to six months, while upholding the five-year ban. [@3]
Deemsters Alastair Montgomerie and Alan Gough said the case involved a "very
small quantity of drugs" for personal use, a powerful mitigating factor
warranting a significant reduction. [@3]
