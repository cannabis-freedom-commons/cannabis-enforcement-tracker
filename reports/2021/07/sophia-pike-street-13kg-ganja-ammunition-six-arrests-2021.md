Police cordoned off a house on Pike Street, North Sophia, Georgetown, at about 3
am on 13 July 2021, acting on information they had received. [@2] During the
cordon and search, police said they seized 13.166 kilogrammes of marijuana and
190 live .45 bullets. [@1] As officers arrived, a bulky black plastic bag was
thrown from a window of the house and was found to contain leaves, seeds and
stems suspected to be cannabis. [@2]

Police found six people in the house: a 30-year-old man and his 22-year-old
reputed wife, a 28-year-old woman and her 53-year-old reputed husband, a
28-year-old man and another man whose age was not verified. [@2] All six denied
knowledge or ownership of the suspected cannabis and were arrested. [@2] Guyana
Times reported that the six arrested included two couples. [@3] A search of the
house found a large scale, and a further search about 30 feet from the house
found a black garbage bag holding eight parcels of suspected cannabis. [@2]

In the yard police also unearthed a white plastic bucket holding a small rice
bag with 190 rounds of live .45 ammunition. [@2] The cannabis thrown from the
window weighed 55 grams and the cannabis found in the bushes weighed 13.166
kilograms. [@2] Questioned about the other cannabis and the ammunition, the six
suspects again denied any knowledge. [@3] All six remained in custody as
investigations continued. [@2]
