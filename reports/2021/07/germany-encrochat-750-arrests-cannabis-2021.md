German security officials announced on 6 July 2021 that they had made more
than 750 arrests and seized large quantities of drugs after gaining access
to the encrypted chats of EncroChat mobile phones. [@1,2] The investigation
had started in April 2020 and focused on users of the EncroChat service
involved in the illegal drug trade, according to Frankfurt prosecutors and
the Federal Criminal Police Office (BKA). [@1,2]

Investigators seized 3.2 metric tonnes of cannabis, alongside about 320 kg
of synthetic drugs, more than 125,500 ecstasy pills, almost 400 kg of
cocaine and 10 kg of heroin. [@1,2] They also confiscated 310 weapons, more
than 12,200 rounds of ammunition and assets worth 168 million euros. [@1,2]

The EncroChat data came from a French investigation in which French and
Dutch authorities set up a joint team that decrypted the network's
communications. [@1,2] Frankfurt prosecutor Julia Bussweiler said the
investigation was far from over, with many suspects in pre-trial detention
while the seized data was checked for indictments. [@1,2]
