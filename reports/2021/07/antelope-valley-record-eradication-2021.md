Sheriff Alex Villanueva announced on 7 July 2021 that a ten-day operation in the Antelope Valley had eradicated 372,918 illegal cannabis plants and destroyed 33,480 pounds (about 15.2 tonnes) of processed marijuana, the largest illegal-grow bust in the history of Los Angeles County. [@1,2] Officers served 205 search warrants on cultivation sites from 8 June, arresting 131 people, including 19 held by water-theft enforcement teams, and seizing 33 firearms. [@1,2]

The grows were run by armed groups that stole millions of gallons of groundwater during a severe drought and poisoned streams with banned pesticides, the sheriff said. [@2] Aerial surveillance counted about 500 illegal grows in the valley, up from 150 a year earlier, so the 205 sites raided were only a fraction of the total. [@2] California wildlife officers led the eradication and evidence work on 28 of the warrants and took in 84 dogs and other animals found living in poor conditions at the sites. [@1]
