Military Police officers arrested a couple in flagrante in Criciuma on Saturday
17 July 2021. [@2] Officers first stopped the pair in a Chevrolet Celta. [@1]
The couple were taken to answer for drug trafficking. [@1] The house they had
left stands on Rua Clementina Ronchi in the Vila Cechinel subdivision, bairro
Sao Defende. [@2]

Officers found more than six kilos of marijuana, R$682 and three mobile phones
inside the car. [@1] Sniffer dog Flecha found four marijuana bricks hidden among
bricks outside the house. [@2] Officers saw another marijuana brick on a kitchen
counter through the window. [@2] Inside the house, officers found another 33.7
kilos of marijuana with the help of sniffer dog Flecha. [@1] During a personal
search at the police station, another 8.5 grams of marijuana was found on the
woman. [@1] The same house search also yielded 5.3 kilos of cocaine, 806 grams
of crack, 978 grams of caffeine for mixing with cocaine, two scales and a
pressure gun, which are not counted as cannabis. [@1]

The man told officers he had collected the marijuana from someone and was to be
paid 1,000 reais to deliver it. [@1] Police went to the house partly because the
pair had been seen leaving it and a fugitive might have been there. [@1] At the
station, a female officer found the additional marijuana hidden in the woman's
bra during the personal search. [@2]
