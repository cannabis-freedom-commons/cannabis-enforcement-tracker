Rankin Inlet RCMP confiscated 25.7 pounds of marijuana (about 11.7 kilograms),
$15,600 in cash and an all-terrain vehicle after executing a search warrant at a
residence in the community on 4 July 2021. [@1,2]

Larissa Misheralak was arrested and charged with possession of cannabis for the
purpose of selling, unlawfully importing cannabis into Nunavut, and possession of
property obtained by crime worth more than $5,000. [@1,2] Dustin MacLean was also
arrested and faces the same cannabis and property charges, plus a charge of failing
to comply with an undertaking. [@1,2] The charges had not been proven in court at
the time of the reports. [@1,2]
