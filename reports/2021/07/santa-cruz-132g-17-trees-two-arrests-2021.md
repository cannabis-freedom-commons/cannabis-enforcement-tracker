Trinidad and Tobago Police Service officers arrested a woman and a man
separately for marijuana possession during an anti-crime exercise in Santa Cruz.
[@1] The exercise ran from 10 pm on Wednesday 14 July to 2 am on Thursday 15
July 2021. [@1]

At the home of a 35-year-old woman on Pipiol Road, Spice Hill, Upper Santa Cruz,
police found 132 grammes of marijuana. [@1] Officers also found Trinidad and
Tobago Defence Force camouflage kits at her home. [@1] The items, including the
marijuana, were seized and she was arrested. [@1] The woman said the kits
belonged to the father of her child, a serving Defence Force member who had
recently moved out of the home. [@1]

In a separate incident, officers arrested a 32-year-old man of Blackford Street,
Cantaro Village, Upper Santa Cruz, for possessing a quantity of marijuana. [@1]
In a bushy area off Conrad Street, Cantaro Village, police found and seized 17
fully grown marijuana trees, which they valued at TT$20,000 on the street. [@1]

Senior Superintendent Winston Maharaj led the exercise, with ASP Pariman, and it
included the NED Gang and Intelligence Unit, the NED Task Force, the Special
Intelligence Unit and the Canine Unit. [@1] Police said investigations were
continuing. [@1]
