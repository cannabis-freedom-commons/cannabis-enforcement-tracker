Some 400 police officers carried out simultaneous raids at 42 locations in Tarragona, Barcelona, Girona and Castellon on 13 July 2021, targeting an Albanian-led clan managing industrial cannabis plantations in Spain. [@1] The action day, supported by Europol's serious organised crime centre with a virtual command post and an expert deployed to Spain, also drew in Albanian investigators and Germany's Berlin-Brandenburg customs investigation office. [@1]

The searches uncovered 51 cannabis farms and led to 107 arrests, including Albanian, Spanish, Slovak and Greek nationals, with 25,000 cannabis plants, 70,000 euros in cash and two high-end vehicles seized. [@1] The group grew the plants indoors on diverted electricity estimated to have cost utilities more than 1.6 million euros, and shipped the processed marijuana on to other European countries. [@1] A joint press conference in September added that 938 kilograms of cannabis and two firearms were seized. [@2]
