Officers of the National Drug Law Enforcement Agency in Edo State arrested seven suspected cannabis farmers at Ughieudu near Ehor in Uhunmwonde Local Government Area on Saturday. [@1,2] The suspects were caught on their farms during an operation lasting several hours, led by Principal Staff Officer for Operations Kennedy Obedeke. [@1,2] The command said the three farms had been discovered through intelligence, measured about 22.216411 hectares and held plants worth several millions of naira. [@1,2]

The operation had been running since the previous Wednesday, and officers destroyed the farms the same day. [@1] Obedeke said Edo was a cannabis-producing state but the command was working to keep the substance from end users, crediting the backing of chairman Buba Marwa. [@1] One suspect said he managed the farms for 120,000 naira a year and knew the implications of the crime, adding that the owner of the farms was on the run. [@1]
