Comoros' National Gendarmerie seized 71 kg of cannabis at the fishermen's quay
in Magoudjou, in Moroni on Grande Comore, on Tuesday 27 July 2021. [@1] Officers
detained two people described as belonging to the network behind the shipment.
[@1] HabarizaComores reported the same 71 kg seizure at the Magoudjou quay on
the same day, with two people arrested. [@2]

The gendarmerie's statement said the investigation was continuing and that the
cannabis would be handed to the prosecutor's office for incineration. [@1]

La Gazette des Comores later counted the 71 kg intercepted at Magoudjou among
992 kg of drugs seized off the archipelago's coasts in one week, alongside 846
kg taken at sea off Anjouan and 75 kg burned on Mohéli. [@3]
