On 29 July 2021 President Musa Bihi Abdi viewed more than 470 bundles of
cannabis at the Rapid Response Unit quarters beside police headquarters in
Hargeisa. [@1] The bundles had been packed in sacks and hidden among
lookalike sacks of vegetables imported from Ethiopia. [@1] Interior minister
Mohamed Kahin Ahmed said more than 1,100 people had been jailed in the
preceding six months on smuggled liquor and drug charges. [@1]

## The six-month drive

Police chief Mohamed Adan Saqadhi had said two days earlier that police seized
26,800 litres of liquor between January and June, about a tenth of what he
believed entered the country. [@2] He said officers also confiscated 1,055
rolls of hashish alongside powders, liquid injections and 1,500 bottles of
spirits, and arrested 1,001 suspects. [@2] He added that police had identified
cannabis farms on the outskirts of Hargeisa. [@2] He described smuggling
methods including cannabis hidden in trucks and stuffed in mattresses, and
farms on the city outskirts and inside homes. [@3] An earlier account said the
police chief and army officers had unveiled alcohol hidden in khat sacks, and
that two officers refused a $3,000 bribe offered to free detainees. [@4]
