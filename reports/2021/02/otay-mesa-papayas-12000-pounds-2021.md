CBP officers at the commercial facility of the Otay Mesa port of entry seized more than 12,000 pounds of marijuana hidden in a papaya shipment on 22 February 2021. [@1,2] At about 5:45 p.m. that Monday, a 50-year-old Mexican man drove in with a tractor-trailer manifested as fresh papayas. [@1,2]

An X-ray scan showed anomalies in the load, and a narcotics detector dog alerted on the shipment. [@1,2] Officers then found 873 wrapped packages of marijuana mixed among the papayas, a haul CBP valued at about $27 million. [@1,2]

The driver was handed to Homeland Security Investigations agents and taken to the Metropolitan Correction Center in San Diego, while CBP seized the truck, trailer and drugs. [@1,2] Acting Director of Field Operations Anne Maricich said the officers remain focused on stopping smugglers at the border. [@1,2]
