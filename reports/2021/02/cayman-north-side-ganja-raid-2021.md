Police arrested two people on suspicion of drug-related offences on Tuesday 9
February 2021 after a raid on a property in North Side, Grand Cayman. [@1] A
34-year-old woman from West Bay and a 58-year-old man from North Side were the
two people arrested. [@1] Police said both were held on suspicion of consuming
ganja and of possessing ganja with intent to supply. [@2]

RCIPS officers in North Side carried out what the service called a proactive
operation in the vicinity of Further Road, off North Side Road, on the morning
of 9 February. [@2] Officers searched a property there under the Misuse of Drugs
Law. [@1] During the search they recovered bags containing suspected ganja as
well as ganja plants. [@2] Cayman News Service reported that an undisclosed
quantity of ganja and plants was seized in the raid. [@3] Police photographs
published after the raid showed the bags of ganja taken on 9 February. [@1]

Police said the matter remained under investigation. [@1]
