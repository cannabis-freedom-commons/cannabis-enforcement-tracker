The anti-drug brigade of Maldonado department police raided three rural
properties in Paraje La Coronilla, near the city of Aiguá, where marijuana was
being grown without authorisation. [@1] The properties lay about 10 kilometres
from Ruta 39 at kilometre 78, in sierra country where different cannabis
varieties with different THC levels were cultivated and processed. [@1] FM Gente
reported that the investigation began about five months earlier over complaints
of an illegal cannabis crop in the sierras of Aiguá, identified from light
aircraft because the area was hard to reach by vehicle. [@2]

Officers seized 746 marijuana plants, 3.24 kg of cannabis buds and 8.431 kg of
plants in the drying phase, and detained four people there. [@1] The Interior
Ministry estimated the market value of the haul at 1,100,000 Uruguayan pesos.
[@1] Containers fitted out as greenhouses held equipment to control temperature,
humidity and light, including air conditioners, halogen lamps, humidity
regulators, dehumidifiers, fans and fertilisers. [@1]

Police also searched a house in the city of San Carlos, where they detained a
woman who had marijuana cigarettes, eight plants and 19 packets of cannabis.
[@1] Five people were detained in total, four at the properties and the woman in
San Carlos. [@1]

Two were summoned by the prosecutor's office and three were formally charged
with production of narcotic substances in real reiteration together with
possession not for consumption. [@1] Two of the three must serve 60 days of
preventive detention and the third total house arrest for 90 days. [@1] The
searches were carried out with the IRCCA, Uruguay's cannabis regulation and
control institute, and the second-shift prosecutor's office of San Carlos. [@1]
FM Gente reported that the three men were formalized at a hearing on Friday
afternoon. [@2] The prosecution had asked for 90 days of preventive detention
for all three; the court gave two of them 60 days in custody and the third house
arrest, a decision prosecutors appealed. [@2]
