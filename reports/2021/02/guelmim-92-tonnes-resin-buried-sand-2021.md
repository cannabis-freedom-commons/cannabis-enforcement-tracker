Morocco's national security service (DGSN) said nearly 9.2 tonnes of cannabis resin, hidden in bales buried in sand, were uncovered in the Guelmim region of the southwest. [@1,2] The service announced the seizure on Saturday 6 February 2021 and said an investigation had been opened. [@1,2]
