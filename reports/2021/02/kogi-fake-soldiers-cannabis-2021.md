Operatives of the Kogi State command of the NDLEA intercepted two suspected fake military
officers in a VIP-edition Toyota Hilux on the Okene-Lokoja expressway on 15 February 2021.
[@1,2] The men, identified as Mohammed Nura and Abubakar Dahiru, were stopped by vigilant
operatives and the vehicle was searched. [@1,2]

Officers found 394 compressed blocks of cannabis sativa concealed in different compartments
of the Hilux, with a total weight of 234 kg. [@1,2] Kogi commander Adewunmi Alfred disclosed
the seizure, and both suspects were arrested. [@1]
