Mozambique's Republic Police (PRM) in Manica detained a maize farmer who
allegedly kept a cannabis plantation. [@1] The suspect is a 60-year-old man
accused of producing, consuming and selling drugs in the province. [@1]
Provincial police spokesman Mateus Mindo said officers acted after learning of
the facts and detained the suspect. [@1] Police said the case file has already
been drawn up and will follow its legal course. [@1]

The alleged plantation held more than 300 cannabis sativa plants, known locally
as suruma. [@1] Suruma is the local name used in the report for cannabis sativa.
[@1] Police described this as the second case of its kind in Manica that month.
[@1]

Earlier that month, provincial police had intercepted a vehicle transporting
more than a tonne of cannabis sativa. [@1] Police said the detained man does not
act alone and has suppliers who must also be identified. [@1] Police said work
continues to identify other members of the group and its suppliers in order to
hold them responsible and combat such cases in the province. [@1]
