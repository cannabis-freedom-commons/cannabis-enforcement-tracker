Acting on a tip-off, the Ghatkopar unit of the Mumbai Crime Branch's Anti-Narcotics Cell (ANC) laid a trap and intercepted a truck at a foot-over bridge on the Eastern Express Highway in suburban Vikhroli on the evening of Friday 12 February 2021. [@1,2] Inside, officers found 1,800 kg of ganja concealed under a consignment of coconuts, smuggled from Odisha and worth Rs 3.5 crore according to the police. [@1,2]

Two men, 25-year-old Akash Yadav and Dineshkumar Saroj, were arrested at the spot; police said they worked as the truck's driver and cleaner. [@1,2] Joint Commissioner of Police (Crime) Milind Bharambe said the gang hired trucks from Mumbai under the guise of importing coconuts but used them to smuggle ganja, bringing about five tonnes into Maharashtra every month with three to four tonnes distributed in Mumbai and neighbouring districts. [@1]

The police identified the inter-state racket's kingpin as Laxmikant Pradhan of Ganjam district in Odisha, acting on information from the Odisha Police, and said the consignment's Mumbai handler was Sandeep Satpute, who allegedly ran the syndicate from Bhiwandi and owned the godown where the ganja was kept. [@1,2] A warrant was issued for Satpute, a resident of Louis Wadi in Thane, and the ANC was also probing how the smugglers crossed Naxal-affected areas in Andhra Pradesh to reach Maharashtra. [@2]
