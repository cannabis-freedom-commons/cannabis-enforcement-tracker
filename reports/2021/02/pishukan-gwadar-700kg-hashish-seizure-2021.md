The Pakistan Navy and the Anti-Narcotics Force seized about 700 kilograms of narcotics near the Pishukan coast in Balochistan in an intelligence-based joint operation on 24 February 2021. [@1] The Navy said the seized drugs were valued at about PKR 2.2 billion and were handed over to the ANF for further legal formalities. [@1]

An ANF press release identified the Pishukan haul as 700 kg of hashish recovered by its Gwadar station together with the Pakistan Navy from a pickup parked in the general area of Pishukan in Gwadar District. [@2] The release lists the Pishukan seizure as one of 23 counter-narcotics operations across the country totalling 2,698 kg of narcotics. [@2] No suspects were reported arrested at Pishukan. [@1,2]
