On 11 February 2021, officers from the Jamaica Customs Agency's Contraband
Enforcement Team, together with officers from the Jamaica Constabulary Force's
Narcotics Division and Port Security personnel, seized a shipment concealed in a
transhipment container at the Port of Kingston. [@1] The officers made no
arrests in relation to the seizure, according to the Jamaica Customs Agency.
[@1] The agency said its Border Protection Unit and the Jamaica Constabulary
Force's Narcotics Division are investigating the matter. [@1]

The agency said the shipment contained 1516.7 pounds (688 kg) of marijuana. [@1]
The agency described the marijuana as compressed marijuana. [@1] Officers found
the container during a routine examination of containers after noticing
discrepancies in the container. [@1] Inside the container were thirteen knitted
bags and one duffle bag holding 155 packages containing a substance resembling
marijuana. [@1] The Jamaica Customs Agency put the estimated street value of the
marijuana at JMD 6.1 million, or USD 41,840. [@1]
