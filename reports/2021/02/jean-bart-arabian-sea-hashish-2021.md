The French frigate Jean Bart intercepted a suspicious dhow in the Arabian Sea on 12 February
2021 and recovered 148 parcels containing 2,960 kg of cannabis resin, known locally as
hashish. [@1,2] The ship's Panther helicopter first investigated the dhow, whose crew were seen
throwing packages overboard before a search-and-recovery operation picked them up. [@1,2]

The seizure, made under Combined Maritime Forces task force CTF-150, was valued at over
US$1.5 million, and the ship's commanding officer said it showed the task force's cooperation
in waters French units know well. [@1,2] Boarding teams then searched the dhow for further
contraband under strict COVID-19 precautions, and the drugs were destroyed at sea in line with
local and regional laws. [@1]
