Togo's national police arrested Sam Adinane, a 41-year-old Togolese who
described himself as a reseller living in the Gbadago district of Lomé. [@1]
Officers of the Central Directorate of the Judicial Police (DCPJ) detained him
on 18 February 2021 in an open-air drug spot between the Canal Olympia site and
the Le Togo market. [@1] At the time of the arrest he was carrying a few
cannabis joints. [@1]

A search of his home then found 100 kg of cannabis hidden in one of his rooms.
[@1] Police also seized 1,093,000 CFA francs at the house. [@1]

Icilome reported that after the find the suspect admitted the drugs were his and
that he was part of an international network. [@2] The suspect told police he
bought the cannabis in Ghana, selling part of it retail in Togo and sending the
rest on to Nigeria. [@2] According to the police account, his group moved the
drug across borders mainly in canoes and otherwise on motorcycles at night. [@3]
Investigators said they were still working to arrest the rest of the trafficking
network. [@1] The suspect was to be brought before the Lomé public prosecutor,
the prosecutor of the Republic, to answer for the case. [@1]
