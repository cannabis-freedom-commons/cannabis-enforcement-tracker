A multi-agency team of Anti-Narcotics detectives, Administration Police officers attached to the Kedong field training camp and the Naivasha sub-county police chief raided Kedong Ranch in Naivasha on Thursday 11 February 2021 after a tip-off from members of the public through the Directorate of Criminal Investigations (DCI) toll-free line. [@1,3] The team found fourteen suspects weeding an over one-acre field of bhang and arrested them. [@1,2,3]

The officers put the plantation under surveillance and destroyed the crop, which the authorities valued at Sh27 million. [@1,2] DCI director George Kinoti said the operation was conducted jointly with the Administration Police and the Anti-Narcotics department, that the suspects were found cultivating the bhang, and that they would be charged in court the following week. [@2,3] The suspects were placed in police custody while investigations continued to bring all parties behind the illegal activity to book. [@1,3]
