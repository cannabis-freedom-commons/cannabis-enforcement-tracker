Security regiment patrols in the Jazan region caught smugglers attempting to
bring 24 kilograms of hashish over Saudi Arabia's southern border. [@1,2] The
Saudi Press Agency reported the interception on Sunday 21 February 2021.
[@1,2] A Ministry of Interior spokesperson, Col. Ahmed Al-Tuwayan, said the
smugglers were Ethiopian nationals. [@1,2]

The hashish was seized and the smugglers were arrested, with initial legal
procedures against them completed. [@1,2] Al Bawaba's account, adapted from
Arab News, carries a stock photograph unrelated to the interception. [@1]
