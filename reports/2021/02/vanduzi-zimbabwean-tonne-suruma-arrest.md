Police in Vanduzi district, Manica Province, are holding a 47-year-old
Zimbabwean man accused of trafficking and selling a tonne of cannabis sativa,
known locally as suruma. [@1] The suspect is being held at the district command
of the Republic of Mozambique Police, the PRM, the country's national police
force. [@1] The man is 47 years old. [@1] The man tried without success to bribe
traffic police on duty at the Cruzamento de Tete post with 100,000 meticais.
[@1]

Confronted with the accusation, the man denies it and says the drugs belong to a
friend living in Zimbabwe who called on him to pay the goods' customs costs.
[@1] Mário Arnaça, head of the public relations department at the PRM provincial
command in Manica, said the detention followed an intelligence operation by the
Vanduzi district command. [@1]
