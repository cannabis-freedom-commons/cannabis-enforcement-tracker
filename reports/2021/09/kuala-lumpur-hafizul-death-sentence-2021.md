The High Court in Kuala Lumpur sentenced Muhammad Hafizul Rashid Emmy, 29, a delivery man, to death by hanging on Friday 3 September 2021 after finding him guilty of trafficking in 299.09 grammes of cannabis three years earlier. [@1,2] He broke down in tears in the dock, and a woman believed to be a family member wept in the public gallery. [@1]

Judge Collin Lawrence Sequerah found that the defence had failed to raise a reasonable doubt over the prosecution's case on possession and had failed to rebut the trafficking presumption under the Dangerous Drugs Act 1952. [@1] He then imposed the death sentence as provided for the offence. [@1]

## A 2018 raid in Cheras

Hafizul had been charged with trafficking in the drug at a house in Bandar Tasik Selatan, Cheras, at 11.50 pm on 8 March 2018. [@1] The offence carries death by hanging or life imprisonment with whipping on conviction. [@1,2] Deputy public prosecutor Izalina Abdullah prosecuted, while the accused was represented by lawyer Suzana Norlihan. [@1]
