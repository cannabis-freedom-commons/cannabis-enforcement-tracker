Acting on a tip-off, Jeypore Sadar police in Koraput district intercepted a truck on 6 September 2021 and recovered 1,008 kg of cannabis going out of the state from Jalaput. [@1] The driver was arrested at the spot and two suppliers were nabbed later, and officers seized Rs 1.65 lakh in cash along with the truck, a car and a motorcycle. [@1]

In Kalahandi district, police intercepted a pumpkin-loaded truck in Kesinga town on 5 September 2021 and found 573.46 kg of cannabis in 280 polythene-wrapped packets hidden inside the pumpkin consignment. [@1] Superintendent of Police Saravana Vivek M said the truck was travelling from Padampur in Rayagada district to Ghaziabad in Uttar Pradesh, and the two arrested men from Uttar Pradesh were produced in court. [@1]

Police valued the combined 1,581.46 kg haul at Rs 1.22 crore and registered cases under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act in both incidents. [@1]
