Police seized over 2,000 marijuana plants on Wednesday 15 September 2021. [@1]
Officers carried out the operation that morning in the parishes of St John and
St Philip. [@1]

CBC News reported a major police operation spanning St Philip and St John that
had netted over 2,000 plants so far that morning. [@2] Inspector Anthony
Cadogan, attached to the Suppressing Criminal Activity Threatening Society
(SCATS) unit, gave CBC News the account of the operation. [@2]

Neither Barbados Today nor CBC News reported any arrests or charges over the
operation. [@1] Barbados Today published the first account while the operation
was still under way and promised more details later. [@1]
