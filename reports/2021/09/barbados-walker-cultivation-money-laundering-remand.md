Barry Omar Walker, a 39-year-old plumber from Buckingham Road, Bank Hall, St
Michael, was charged with drugs and money-laundering offences over a police
search of his home on August 28, 2021. [@1] Walker faced charges of possession
of cannabis, cocaine and methamphetamine, possession with intent to supply and
possession with intent to traffic the three drugs, and cultivation of cannabis.
[@1] He was also charged with laundering the proceeds of crime in connection
with BDS$5,254 and US$550. [@1]

Walker pleaded not guilty to the offences before Chief Magistrate Ian Weekes at
the No. 1 District 'A' Magistrates' Court. [@1] Sergeant Verdon Forde objected
to bail, citing the seriousness of the offences, the strength of the evidence,
the quantity of drugs and fears that Walker would interfere with the ongoing
investigation, and said Walker was known for similar offences. [@1]

Defence attorney Arthur Holder said Walker had been in custody since the Monday
before the hearing and that his earlier convictions, from 2010 and 2014, had
been expunged. [@1]

Chief Magistrate Weekes refused bail, noting that Walker faced numerous drug
charges including cultivation and that he would not exercise his discretion in
Walker's favour. [@1] Walker was remanded to Dodds prison to reappear at the No.
1 District 'A' Magistrates' Court on September 30. [@1]
