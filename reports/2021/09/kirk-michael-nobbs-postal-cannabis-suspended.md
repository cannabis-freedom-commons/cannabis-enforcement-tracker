Christopher Nobbs, 19, of Kirk Michael, was sentenced at Douglas Courthouse for
agreeing to have cannabis worth about £2,500 sent to him through the post for
someone else. [@1] Deemster Graeme Cook sentenced him to nine months in prison,
suspended for a year, and also imposed a 12-month supervision order. [@1] Nobbs
had pleaded guilty to importing the drug at an earlier hearing. [@1] The case
had been sent up from the magistrates' court to the Court of General Gaol
Delivery in November 2020, when Nobbs, then 18 and living on Lord Street in
Douglas, was bailed in the sum of £500. [@2]

Police were called to the Spring Valley postal sorting office on 3 July 2020
after staff identified a suspicious package addressed to Nobbs at his home in
Faaie Ny Cabbal in Kirk Michael. [@1] Police opened the parcel and found a red
shoe box containing five packets of cannabis. [@1] Nobbs was arrested after the
parcel was intercepted in July 2020, and the court heard he had agreed to
receive it. [@1]

After his arrest Nobbs would not say who the drugs were for, saying he feared
having his "head kicked in" and did not "want to be known as a grass". [@1]
Sentencing him, Deemster Cook said Nobbs "knew" the parcel would contain
cannabis when he agreed to receive it, but that he had not known for certain
cannabis would be sent, and that if it had held Class A drugs he could have
faced a lengthy custodial sentence. [@1]
