Kyiv-region police announced on 22 September 2021 that they had found a
cannabis plantation in a field near the village of Vovchkiv in Boryspil
district during the MAK operational-preventive campaign. [@1] Officers
counted almost 100,000 plants growing across about 3.5 hectares, valued on
the black market at more than 20 million hryvnias. [@1]

Samples were sent to the Kyiv forensic centre of the Interior Ministry for
examination, and the remaining plants were destroyed. [@1] Police opened
criminal proceedings under Part 2 of Article 310 of the Criminal Code
(sowing or growing poppy or hemp) and were working to identify those behind
the plantation; no suspects had been named. [@1]
