In the early morning hours of Thursday, 16 September 2021, the Glenn
Investigations and Narcotics Task Force (GLINTF) conducted a compliance
operation at three illegal outdoor cannabis grow sites within a few miles of
each other on County Road 8 west of Orland. [@2] Agents arrested 28-year-old
Vidal Chavez Jr. of Orland on allegations of cultivating marijuana for sale,
operating a butane honey oil (BHO) lab and child endangerment. [@2] County jail
booking records show a GLINTF agent took Chavez, an Orland cashier, into custody
at 6138 County Road 8 and booked him into the Glenn County Jail at 9:50 a.m. on
$200,000 bail on felony allegations of marijuana cultivation, possession of
marijuana for sale, manufacturing a controlled substance and child endangerment.
[@4]

Agents seized 545 marijuana plants, 145 pounds (about 65.8 kg) of processed
marijuana and two ounces (about 57 grams) of concentrated cannabis across the
separate County Road 8 properties. [@1] The task force described the plants as
mature and also located a butane honey oil lab at one of the addresses. [@3] The
Glenn County Probation Department, Orland Police Department, California Highway
Patrol and Glenn County District Attorney's Office assisted in serving the three
separate search warrants. [@3]

Although recreational cannabis use is legal in California, authorities noted
that selling marijuana or having more than six plants without a licence remains
illegal. [@1] Under local Ordinance 15.797.020, Glenn County also prohibits
outdoor marijuana cultivation across all zoning districts as well as cannabis
delivery. [@2]
