Bengaluru's Central Crime Branch arrested four men, including two Iranian
nationals, for growing and selling hydroponic ganja. [@2] Police named the
Iranians as 34-year-old Javad Rostampour Ghotb Aldin and 35-year-old Shir
Mohammadi Barogh, both from Tehran, and the other two as Mohammed Mohsin Uz
Zaman, 31, and Mohsin Khan, 30. [@2] Officers picked up the four after a tip-off
that they had come by car to sell drugs at Kaveri Nagar in DJ Halli. [@2] Police
said the two Iranians were staying illegally in the city after their visas had
expired. [@2] Aldin had previously been arrested once by Narcotics Control
Bureau sleuths and twice by Yeshwantpur police for peddling, and he continued
the racket after being granted bail. [@1]

Police said they seized 12.85 kg of hydro ganja along with LSD strips, valuing
the haul at Rs 1 crore. [@2] Officers also recovered 13 hydroponic ganja plants
and growing equipment including LED lights, heaters and temperature regulators.
[@3] The gang sold hydro ganja at Rs 4,000 to Rs 5,000 per gram. [@3] They sold
the contraband to students, businessmen and IT professionals. [@2]

The accused had ordered the seeds and equipment on the dark web and grown the
crop in a rented villa in Bidadi using UV lights and LED lamps. [@2] Aldin had
first grown hydroponic weed on a trial basis in a fish tank at his rented house
in Kammanahalli. [@1] He had learned the method from a ‘Marijuana Bible’ bought
on an e-commerce portal, police said. [@1] Aldin had come from Tehran to
Bengaluru as a 23-year-old in 2010, completed an MBA at a Banaswadi college, and
feared returning to Iran because his drug use could attract the death penalty
there. [@1]
