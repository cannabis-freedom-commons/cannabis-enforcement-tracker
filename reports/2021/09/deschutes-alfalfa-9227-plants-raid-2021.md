Detectives with the Deschutes County Illegal Marijuana Enforcement team,
backed by the sheriff's office SWAT team, executed a search warrant on
2 September 2021 at a 30-acre illegal marijuana grow in the community of
Alfalfa. [@1,2] The property held 49 greenhouses containing 9,227 marijuana
plants in various stages of growth, plus a watering system of large cisterns.
[@1,2] Officers also seized 2,800 pounds (about 1,270 kg) of processed
marijuana in what detectives called the biggest drug bust in county history.
[@1]

Officers detained about 21 people at the site and released them. [@1] A
sheriff's sergeant said none of the migrant workers were arrested and most
were paying off debts for being smuggled into the country; the workers lived
in wooden shacks and dome tents with limited clean water. [@1] Authorities
described the grow as operated by a Mexican drug cartel. [@1] Two pistols and
an AR-15 rifle were also confiscated. [@1]
