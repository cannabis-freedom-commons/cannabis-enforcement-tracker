Eight women were arrested and charged with being found in possession of dagga
and appeared in court in Nhlangano, in Eswatini's Shiselweni Region; dagga is
the local name for cannabis. [@1] The eight women had spent the long weekend in
custody before they appeared in the Nhlangano court. [@1] The women wept as they
hoped for a lenient sentence after spending the long weekend in custody. [@1]
