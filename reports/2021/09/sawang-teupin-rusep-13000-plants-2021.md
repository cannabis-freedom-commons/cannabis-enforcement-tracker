A joint operation of the National Narcotics Agency (BNN), the military (TNI)
and the police (Polri) destroyed cannabis fields in Teupin Rusep village,
Sawang, North Aceh Regency, on Wednesday 8 September 2021. [@1,2] Officers
found more than 13,000 harvest-ready stems, equivalent to about 6.5 tonnes of
marijuana, spread over two hectares of land. [@1,2] The plants were destroyed
by burning on the spot as part of the national P4GN anti-drug campaign.
[@1,2]

BNN's eradication director, Brigadier General Aldrin Hutabarat, and the North
Aceh district military commander joined officers in burning the
harvest-ready plants during the raid. [@2]
