Matthew Williams, 18, was involved in the importation of more than £60,000 of
cannabis in 2019 and 2020. [@1] Deemster Graeme Cook jailed him for three years
and three months. [@1] Gawne pleaded guilty to involvement in the importation of
both the cannabis and the ecstasy, and was sentenced to six years and three
months in prison. [@1] Both men were sentenced at Douglas Courthouse on Friday.
[@1] A court heard he also indecently assaulted a girl, who was 12 years old at
the time, after befriending her. [@1] He also admitted charges of indecent
assault, gross indecency with a child under the age of 16, and possession of
indecent images, relating to the assault of a girl on 17 May. [@1] Williams was
placed on the sex offenders register for 10 years. [@1]

The court heard a package containing more than £9,000 of cannabis addressed to
Williams at his home in May Hill in Ramsey was intercepted at the town's postal
sorting office on 23 December 2019. [@1] He was arrested after trying to collect
it. [@1] On 2 December, Williams tried to take delivery of a parcel addressed to
Michael Gawne at a house in Waterloo Road, but was told it had already been
returned to the sorting office. [@1] That package was found to contain more than
£51,000 of cannabis. [@1] Messages on Williams' phone showed he had arranged for
the importation of a large amount of the drug in the days prior via a UK
contact. [@1] He pleaded guilty to producing 1.7 kilogrammes of cannabis worth
£51,000 to the Island on 30 November. [@2] A search of his phone found
photographs of similar packages, and images on his social media accounts
suggesting the supply of the drug. [@1] He later told police he had agreed to
accept the parcel for someone else in exchange for a small amount of cannabis
for personal use. [@1] He told police he had gone to collect the parcel after
being threatened by someone else. [@1]

Gawne, 19, had already been arrested after a parcel containing almost 1,500
ecstasy tablets was reported to police as suspicious. [@1] Williams pleaded
guilty to two charges of importing cannabis and attempting to possess the drug
with intent to supply. [@1] Prosecutors said there was "clear evidence" he had
been involved in the supply of drugs. [@1]
