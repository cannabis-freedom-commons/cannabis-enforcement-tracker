Between 30 August and 2 September 2021, investigators from the San Bernardino
County Sheriff's Marijuana Enforcement Team and deputies from several patrol
stations served 13 search warrants in Hesperia, Piñon Hills, Phelan and the
Landers area. [@1,2] The raids opened week one of Operation Hammer Strike
after complaints about large outdoor marijuana cultivations. [@1,2]
Deputies arrested 12 suspects, who were cited on suspicion of cultivating
cannabis over California's six-plant maximum. [@1,2]

Investigators seized 10,105 marijuana plants and 1,334.8 pounds (about
605.5 kg) of processed marijuana, along with three guns and more than $30,000
in cash. [@1,2] They also mitigated one electrical bypass and eradicated
39 greenhouses. [@1,2] The sheriff's department said the grows violated state
cannabis law and the county's ban on commercial cultivation in unincorporated
areas. [@1,2]
