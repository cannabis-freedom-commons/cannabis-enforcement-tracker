Officers of the National Drug Law Enforcement Agency stormed remote forests in Ovia North East Local Government Area of Edo State between 8 and 16 September 2021, destroying more than 14 hectares of cannabis farms and recovering 172.6 kg of cannabis. [@1,2] Three women and a man were arrested in follow-up raids across Owan East and Akoko Edo areas, where officers recovered cannabis and local mixtures known as monkey tail. [@1,2]

The operation was part of a continued nationwide offensive in which the agency also seized drugs at airports and courier firms, NDLEA spokesman Femi Babafemi said in a statement on 19 September. [@1,2] Chairman Buba Marwa commended the Edo command and other units involved, saying traffickers would be chased in their homes, on the streets, at the ports and deep in the forests. [@1]
