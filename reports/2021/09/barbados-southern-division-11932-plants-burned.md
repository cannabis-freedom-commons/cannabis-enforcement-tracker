On Saturday 18 September 2021 at 2pm, police burned 11,932 cannabis plants that officers had harvested over the previous three months in the parishes of St John, St George and St Philip. [@1] The plants weighed 1,940 lbs in total, about 880 kg. [@1]

Assistant Superintendent Anthony Warner, head of the Criminal Investigation Department in the Southern Division, supervised the destruction with Station Sergeants David Forde of District B station and Winston Bynoe of District C station. [@1] Accredited media, reporters and photographers watched, and Warner thanked the public for its continued support as the force persisted against illegal drugs. [@1] The public-relations release was issued by Inspector Rodney Inniss. [@1]
