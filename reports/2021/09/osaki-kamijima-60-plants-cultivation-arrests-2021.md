Three men from Hiroshima city, all in their early forties, were arrested on
suspicion of cultivating cannabis on the island of Osaki Kamijima, off the
coast of Takehara in Hiroshima Prefecture. [@1] They are suspected of farming
the plants in the mountainous interior of the island between June and August
2021. [@1] Police began investigating after the landowner reported that the
plot was being cultivated without permission. [@1]

Officers seized around 60 cannabis plants from an area of about 130 square
metres and are investigating how the men obtained them. [@1] Police believe
the men were growing the plants with intent to sell. [@1] The report notes
that cannabis prosecutions are rising in Japan, especially among younger
people, which police link to misinformation spread on social media, and urges
readers to understand the risks of dependency and effects on the body. [@1]
