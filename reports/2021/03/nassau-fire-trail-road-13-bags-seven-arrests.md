Police seized 13 bags of suspected marijuana from a home on Fire Trail Road East
in Nassau early on Wednesday 10 March 2021. [@1] Officers from the Drug
Enforcement Unit and the K-9 unit searched the home with a warrant, acting on
police intelligence. [@1]

Five Bahamian men and two women, all believed to be in their late 20s to early
40s, were taken into custody to help the investigation. [@1]

Police Press Liaison Audley Peters said everyone in the house was a suspect
because the drugs were found in the residence. [@1] Peters warned people
involved in such crimes that the police intelligence network was working and
that more results like these would follow. [@1]
