Hume Police District detectives searched a farm property on Junction Point
Road at Crooked Corner, about 80 kilometres north-west of Goulburn, on the
morning of Thursday 18 March 2021 after a tip about cannabis cultivation. [@1,2]

Inside three large outdoor greenhouses they found 3,513 cannabis plants
being grown, and seized more than 100 kilos of dried cannabis leaf; police
put the combined estimated street value at up to $7.5 million. [@1,2]

Three men aged 26, 27 and 35 were arrested at the property and taken to
Goulburn Police Station. [@1,2] Each was charged with cultivating a
prohibited plant in a large commercial quantity and supplying a prohibited
drug in a large commercial quantity. [@1,2] All three were refused bail to
appear before Goulburn Local Court the next day. [@1,2]
