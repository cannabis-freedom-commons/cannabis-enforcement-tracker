The house was in the Sampene neighbourhood on the outskirts of Quelimane and
held 180 kilograms of cannabis sativa within 625.5 kilograms of drugs in total.
[@3] Sernic said one of those held used the residence as a warehouse and moved
drugs onward in very small quantities to Maputo province and South Africa. [@2]
The Tanzanian tenant denied knowledge of the drugs, saying his business was
buying fish, especially crab, to export to Tanzania for onward sale to China.
[@3]

The same residence also yielded 440 kilos of heroin intended for trafficking.
[@1] The same house also held 5.5 kilos of morphine alongside the cannabis. [@1]
Sernic said the heroin had come from Tanzania. [@2] Citing United Nations
drug-office figures for East Africa, the authorities put the goods at at least
11 million euros, noting the price varies and multiplies toward the consumer.
[@1]

Sernic said Quelimane, a city near the Indian Ocean coast and capital of
Zambezia province, was serving as a reception and distribution centre for a
trafficking network. [@1] Sernic said investigations were continuing into links
to other criminal networks. [@1] Mozambique is described by international
organisations as a corridor for international drug trafficking. [@1]
