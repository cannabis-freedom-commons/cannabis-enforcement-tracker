Moufide Bouchibi, described as France's biggest drug trafficker, was arrested in
Dubai. [@1] He was the man France's drugs squad had dreamed of arresting for
more than 20 years. [@1] He headed the main networks importing Moroccan cannabis
into Europe. [@1] He had lived outside France for more than ten years, hidden
between Algeria, Morocco and finally Dubai. [@1]

Before his arrest in Dubai, he was estimated to have brought about 50 tonnes of
cannabis into France each year. [@1] He had been convicted in his absence and
sentenced to 20 years in prison in Bordeaux in 2015. [@1] A new trial for him
was due to be held on Wednesday 1 September. [@1]

In 2002 he was first arrested while living in Mulhouse, where gendarmes seized
240 kilos of cannabis together with weapons and stolen cars. [@1] At least five
teams worked for him, communicating in coded language, leaving unsent draft
messages in mailboxes and using up to 80 telephones in a set order. [@1]
