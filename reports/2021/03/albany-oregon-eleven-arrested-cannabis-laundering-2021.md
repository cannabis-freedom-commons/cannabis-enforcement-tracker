Eleven people were arrested on 17 March 2021 during five search warrants served
in Linn, Benton and Marion counties in Oregon, police said. [@1] The warrants
covered two locations in Linn County, one in Benton County and two in Marion
County. [@2] Investigators said the group was illegally growing and processing
marijuana in Linn County and laundering the proceeds through local restaurants
in the three counties. [@2] The operation was run by the Linn Interagency
Narcotics Enforcement (LINE) Task Force, which was set up after Linn County was
designated a High Intensity Drug Trafficking Area, with help from local, state
and federal agencies. [@2]

The eleven taken into custody were lodged in the Linn County Jail on charges
including racketeering, money laundering and unlawful manufacture of marijuana.
[@2] The accused live in Salem, Albany, Lebanon, Sweet Home and Portland, as
well as Renton, Washington, and San Francisco, California. [@1]

Investigators seized more than 1,900 marijuana plants, KATU reported. [@1] They
also seized 40 pounds (about 18 kilograms) of processed marijuana, guns and
several vehicles, police said. [@2] The Albany Police Department put the cash
seized at more than $373,000; KATU reported more than $370,000. [@2] Police also
seized cocaine, which is not counted among the cannabis figures here. [@2]

Albany Police Captain Jerry Drum said the department would not name the
restaurants involved. [@3] The AP reported that some alleged money laundering
happened at the restaurants and that the arrests followed a long-running
investigation. [@3]
