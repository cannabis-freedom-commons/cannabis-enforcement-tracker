The Fiji Police Force uprooted 82,700 marijuana plants from Kadavu province
between January 2020 and March 2021. [@1] Acting Police Commissioner Rusiate
Tudravu said the haul would cost close to $100 million. [@1] Police sent 12
teams to Kadavu during that period after more marijuana farms kept being
identified. [@1]

Tudravu announced the total while opening the Kadavu Provincial Council meeting
in the past week. [@1] The council meeting opened on March 24, 2021 in Tavuki
village, Kadavu. [@1] Tudravu personally asked the council to help the police
with the drug-related issues the island has always faced. [@1] He said the
council had always helped but asked them to take it up a notch and move forward
together against the drugs. [@1] He said police would not spare any citizen
found in possession of the drugs or planting them. [@1] He called on chiefs,
district representatives and provincial council members to help eliminate the
drug on the island. [@1]
