Guyana Police Force ranks burned 7,689 cannabis plants in swamp lands behind
Yarrowkabra village on the Linden/Soesdyke Highway on 19 March 2021. [@1] The
plants were destroyed by fire and some measured between three and five feet in
height. [@1] No one was found at the farm, which the police described as large.
[@1]

The operation took the officers more than five hours to complete. [@1] Stabroek
News reported the eradication ran from 7.20 am to 12.30 pm in the swamp lands
about six miles behind the village. [@2] Three officers and eight other ranks
carried out the exercise, according to the police account cited by Stabroek
News. [@2]

The police said they could not mark off the location to establish land ownership
because the equipment for the marking off was not available. [@1] Stabroek News
said no incident was reported because no one was in the area at the time of the
raid. [@2]
