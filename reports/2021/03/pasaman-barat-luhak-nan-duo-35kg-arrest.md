Pasaman Barat district police arrested a 35-year-old labourer identified as MK
on the night of Monday 8 March 2021. [@1] The arrest took place behind the
suspect's house in Jorong Sungai Talang, Nagari Koto Baru, in Luhak Nan Duo
district. [@1] A second outlet names the detained labourer as Kodor, aged 35,
from Jorong Sungai Talang in Nagari Koto Baru, Luhak Nan Duo. [@2]

Officers seized four large packages of dried cannabis weighing about 3.5
kilograms. [@2] Police also seized an orange sitting scale, a white plastic sack
and a large white plastic bag, with the cannabis wrapped in yellow tape. [@1]
Police said the operation followed a public tip about dried-cannabis
distribution in the Sungai Talang area. [@1] The suspect and the seized goods
were taken to Pasaman Barat police headquarters for further investigation. [@1]

Police said the labourer was accused of distributing cannabis and cited Articles
114(2) and 111(2) of Law No. 35 of 2009. [@1] The second outlet reported that he
faced up to 20 years in prison. [@2]
