Police seized 11 packages of marijuana in Sea Lots on Thursday night. [@1] The
seizure was made during an anti-crime exercise in the Port of Spain Division
that ran from 7:30 pm to 10:30 pm on Thursday 4 March 2021. [@2] Officers from
the Port of Spain Task Force and the Besson Street Police Station chased a
silver Nissan Almera at around 9.35 pm. [@1] PC Harrypersad and WPC Lewis tried
to intercept the Nissan Almera, which was occupied by three men. [@2]

The car drove to Pioneer Drive, Sea Lots, and the three men inside escaped by
running through nearby dirt tracks. [@1] Officers searched the car and found a
crocus bag holding 11 packages of marijuana. [@2] The marijuana weighed nine
kilograms. [@1] The car was seized and taken to the Besson Street Police
Station. [@1] Police said they were continuing enquiries. [@1]

The exercise was co-ordinated by ACP Gaffar and led by Snr Supt Nobbee, Supt
Ramnarine and ASP Cumberbatch, with field operations by Insp Knott, Sgt
Alexander and several corporals. [@1] A second account of the police statement
named the co-ordinating officer as ACP Yussuf Gaffar and added ASP Maharaj among
the senior officers. [@2]
