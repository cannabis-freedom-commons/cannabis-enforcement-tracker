Paraguay's National Anti-Drug Secretariat (Senad) and Brazil's Federal Police finished Operation Nueva Alianza XXV on 31 March 2021 after fifteen days of joint raids in Amambay department. [@1,2] Paraguayan Air Force and Federal Police helicopters carried agents into narco sectors at Cerro Kuatiá, Alpasa and Bella Vista Norte, supported on the ground by special agents, special forces and the Joint Task Force. [@1,2]

Agents eradicated 635 hectares of marijuana crops and destroyed 23,490 kilograms of marijuana already prepared, bagged and ready for transport to consumption centres. [@1,2] They also detected and incinerated 104 narco camps used as production and collection bases. [@1,2]

## Nearly 2,000 tonnes out of circulation

Senad put the combined result at 1,928 tonnes of cannabis taken out of circulation, counting both the eradicated crops and the destroyed ready product. [@1,2] Hoy reported the damage to organised crime at around US$57 million. [@1]

Together with the preceding Operation Nueva Alianza XXIV, the two phases eradicated more than 1,000 hectares of marijuana in a single month. [@1]
