Ontario Provincial Police raided a large commercial building in the 900 block of Mackay Street in Pembroke on 3 March 2021. [@1,2] The raid brought together the provincial cannabis enforcement team with organised-crime, street-crime, asset-forfeiture, emergency-response and Upper Ottawa Valley officers. [@1,2] Officers seized more than 5,000 cannabis plants and a large quantity of growing and processing equipment. [@1,2]

Four people were charged with cultivating cannabis at a place that is not a dwelling house. [@1,2] Police released them to appear at the Ontario Court of Justice in Pembroke on 6 April 2021. [@1,2]
