CBP officers and Agriculture Specialists at the Port of Highgate Springs
selected a commercial shipment manifested as "food product" for additional
examination on 29 March 2021. [@1,2] A non-intrusive examination of the truck
and trailer showed anomalies, and a physical search found multiple vacuum-sealed
packages that field-tested positive for marijuana. [@1,2]

Officers seized 1,331 pounds (about 604 kg), which CBP valued at more than $2.6
million. [@1,2] St. Albans Area Port Director Eileen Bigelow said the officers'
vigilance led to the discovery. [@1]
