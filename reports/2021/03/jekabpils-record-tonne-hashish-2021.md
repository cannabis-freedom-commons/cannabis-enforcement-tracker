During targeted work under a previously opened criminal case, Latvian officers stopped a lorry with a semi-trailer on the Daugavpils highway near Jēkabpils on 12 March 2021 and found about 1,000 kilograms of hashish hidden inside. [@1,2] The truck carried Lithuanian registration plates. [@2]

The hashish, in black plastic bundles, lay concealed beneath artificial stone slabs in the trailer's cargo. [@1,2] Two people were detained: the truck's driver, a Lithuanian resident born in 1964, and a Latvian national born in 1972 arrested at his Riga residence in a parallel operation. [@1] The head of the organised-crime division called it the largest hashish cargo the State Police had ever seized, believed to be bound for eastern European markets rather than Latvia itself. [@1]
