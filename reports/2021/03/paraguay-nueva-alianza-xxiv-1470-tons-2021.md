Senad, Paraguay's anti-drug agency, and Brazil's Federal Police ended Operation Nueva Alianza XXIV on 8 March 2021 after fourteen days of joint raids in Amambay department. [@1] They destroyed 490 hectares of marijuana crops and 87 drug camps responsible for the early stages of the supply chain. [@1]

## Where they worked

Supported by four Federal Police helicopters, agents moved through Santa Ana, Colo'o and Colonia Piray, where they also incinerated 14.7 tonnes of chopped and ready-to-pack marijuana. [@1] Diálogo, citing Agencia IP, reported that agents found water collection systems, irrigation hoses, rubber pots and partial shade used to germinate seedlings. [@1]

## The same phase on paper

A Brazilian Federal Police study of the same phase counts 525 hectares, 97 camps and 1,592.8 tonnes between 23 February and 8 March. [@2] Senad put the combined result of the crops and the seized product at 1,470 tonnes taken out of circulation. [@1]
