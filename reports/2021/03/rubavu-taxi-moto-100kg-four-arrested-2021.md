In the wee hours of Sunday 14 March 2021, a joint team of the RNP Anti-Narcotics Unit, the
Rubavu district police and local residents arrested two taxi-moto operators, Claude Habumugisha
and Jean de Dieu Sibomana alias Fils, both 31, in Amahoro Cell, Gisenyi Sector, with 54,000
pellets concealed in 32 bundles weighing 100 kg. [@1,2] The pair were held alongside Jean Claude
Rukundo, the storekeeper of the Amahoro house where the bundles had just been delivered. [@1,2]
Police said the house was rented by major dealer Janvier Hakizimana, who was on the run. [@1,2]

The day before, officers had arrested Jonas Sebareme, 39, in Busasamana Sector with 5,000 rolls
of cannabis. [@1,2] The unit had acted on residents' word that consignments would be sneaked in
from the Democratic Republic of Congo across Lake Kivu, and trailed the motorcyclists to the
store. [@1,2] All four suspects were handed to the Rwanda Investigation Bureau at Gisenyi station
with their exhibits. [@1,2] Police named only Rafiki, resident in the Congo, as the main
supplier. [@1,2]
