Four men were arraigned in the Magistrate's Court in Nassau on drug trafficking
charges over 336 pounds of marijuana. [@1] They appeared before Deputy Chief
Magistrate Andrew Forbes. [@1] The men were charged with possession of dangerous
drugs with intent to supply, conspiracy to possess dangerous drugs, importation
of dangerous drugs and conspiracy to import dangerous drugs. [@1] All four
pleaded not guilty. [@1]

The prosecution alleged that on March 10, the men were found in possession of
some 336 pounds of marijuana, valued at $336,000. [@1] Between March 6 and March
10, the group conspired to import drugs into the country, the prosecution said.
[@1]

Three of the four were granted $7,000 bail with one surety and must report to
the Carmichael Road police station every Monday, Wednesday and Friday. [@1] They
will return to court on July 14. [@1] The prosecution objected to bail for the
fourth man because he is a Jamaican and was considered a flight risk. [@1] He
told the court he had come into the country the previous week for a holiday and
had been given two weeks' stay by the Immigration Department. [@1] He was
remanded to the Bahamas Department of Correctional Services and was to return to
court the following Monday for a bail inquiry. [@1]
