Central Narcotics Bureau officers arrested a 63-year-old Singaporean man for
suspected drug activities on 29 March 2021. [@1] The arrest took place in the
evening in the vicinity of Edgefield Plains. [@1] Edgefield Plains is in
Punggol, Singapore. [@2]

Officers recovered the drugs from inside a blue plastic bag. [@1] The bag held
one bundle with about 323g of Ice and two bundles with about 2,000g of cannabis.
[@1] Ice is methamphetamine. [@2] The bureau estimated the seized drugs together
were worth more than $72,000. [@1]

The bureau said the 323g of Ice could feed the addiction of about 184 abusers
for a week. [@1] It said the 2,000g of cannabis could feed the addiction of
about 285 abusers for a week. [@1] Investigations into the suspect's drug
activities were ongoing. [@1]
