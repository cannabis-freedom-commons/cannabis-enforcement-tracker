Gendarmería Nacional officers searched two hectares of cannabis sativa planted
in the wooded Paraje Yacutinga in Misiones province and reported destroying the
crop. [@1] The operation was ordered by the Federal Court of Eldorado after a
week of inquiries into suspected offences under Argentina's drug law, Law
23,737, near the Paraje Dos Hermanas. [@1] Officers from the Eldorado unit for
complex crimes, the 12 Bernardo de Irigoyen squadron and other Gendarmería units
took three hours to reach the site along tracks described as difficult to pass.
[@1] The warrant covered two hectares in the wooded, hilly Paraje Yacutinga, and
the officers travelled three hours along hard-to-pass trails to get there. [@1]

The officers surveyed the terrain with a drone and with the help of a narcotics
detector dog named Rex. [@1] Inside the plantation they found 1,070 marijuana
plants between 30 centimetres and 2 metres tall, at flowering stages of four to
nine months. [@1] The judge handling the case ordered the seizure of the plants
and equipment found in the search, and no arrests were reported. [@1] InfoChacú,
a Chaco news site, republished the Security Ministry account of the operation
under the headline that the Gendarmería destroyed the plantation. [@2]
