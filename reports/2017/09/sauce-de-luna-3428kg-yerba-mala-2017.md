Federal police intercepted a truck with a trailer on Ruta Nacional 127 and Ruta
Provincial 6 at Sauce de Luna, Entre Ríos, and seized 3,428 kilograms of
marijuana packed in 3,342 blocks. [@1] Simultaneous searches of 14 homes in
Misiones, Entre Ríos and Córdoba followed the seizure and left six people
detained, including the truck driver. [@1] Officers also seized 10 vehicles, 40
mobile phones, firearms, ammunition, 127,990 pesos, four laptops, four tablets
and case documents. [@1]

The marijuana travelled in burlap sacks declared as a load of yerba mate and
left Oberá, Misiones, for Río Cuarto, Córdoba, where it was to be distributed in
cities of that province. [@3]

Federal judge Sergio Pintos of Bell Ville ordered the interception, guided by
the inquiry of federal prosecutor José María Uriarte. [@3] Two cars escorting
the truck fled when police appeared. [@3]

The operation, presented as Operación Yerba mala, was detailed in Bell Ville by
Security Minister Patricia Bullrich and federal police chief Néstor Roncaglia.
[@2] Bullrich said the case began in May with an operation at a drug sales point
in Bell Ville and led investigators to Oberá, where the drug was stored. [@2]
Bullrich said the shipment was meant for distribution in Córdoba and that the
organisation had been broken up. [@2]
