Maldives police arrested four suspects in connection with the sudden and
unexplained deaths of a 22-year-old man and an 18-year-old woman in Hulhumalé.
[@1] Ahmed Haameen, a 22-year-old man from Thimarafushi in Thaa atoll, and
Fathmath Sama, an 18-year-old woman from Malé, were pronounced dead on arrival
at the Hulhumalé hospital around 8:30 pm on Thursday night. [@1] Superintendent
Ahmed Shifan, the police spokesman, said the two deaths were related. [@1]
Police said they had raided several places in Hulhumalé and found party drugs at
one place, and that a serious investigation was ongoing because of suspicious
links to drugs. [@1]

The four suspects, three men and a woman, were arrested late Thursday night and
the criminal court on Friday ordered their detention for 15 days. [@1] At the
remand hearing, police raised 12 charges against the suspects, including the
trafficking, peddling, dealing and use of cannabis and diamorphine (heroin), as
well as attempted suicide and forcing, encouraging and assisting suicide. [@1]
Local media reported that the deaths occurred while a group of friends were at
the Hulhumalé beach after renting a guesthouse room for a birthday party later
that night. [@1]
