Central Narcotics Bureau officers arrested three suspected drug traffickers on 5 September 2017 and seized about 2 kg of cannabis and 466 g of heroin estimated to be worth more than S$51,000. [@1] Officers watching a suspected local trafficker saw a motorcycle rider hand a bag to a 29-year-old Singaporean in a car at the Woodlands Town Garden carpark around 11.00 am. [@1]

The rider, a 26-year-old Malaysian, and his 21-year-old Malaysian passenger were stopped before they could leave the carpark. [@1] The Singaporean's car was stopped later at the junction of Jalan Toa Payoh and Upper Serangoon Road, where a search of the front passenger area recovered the cannabis and heroin in bundles along with more than S$4,000 in cash. [@1]

The bureau noted that trafficking more than 500 g of cannabis carries the death penalty in Singapore. [@1]
