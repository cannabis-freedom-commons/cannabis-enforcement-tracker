Police in Teuk Chhou district, Kampot province, found more than 100 marijuana
plants growing in forest at the foot of a mountain in Stoeung Keo commune on
Wednesday 6 September 2017. [@1] District police officer Chhun Chhim said
officers counted 107 plants. [@1] He said the plants had been grown inside the
forest to hide them from local authorities. [@1] The crop stood on state land at
the foot of the mountain, and villagers farming in the area found it and
reported it to police. [@1]

The plants were destroyed. [@1] No owner had been identified, and the case
stayed open because police were looking to arrest the grower. [@1] Chhun Chhim
said growing marijuana is banned under Cambodian law. [@1]
