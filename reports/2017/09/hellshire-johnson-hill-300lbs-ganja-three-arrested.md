Police detained three suspects during an anti-narcotics operation at Johnson
Hill in Hellshire, St Catherine, on 7 September 2017. [@1] Detectives were
conducting the special anti-narcotics operation at about 6:20 pm. [@1] Police
added that three suspects were taken into custody and two motor vehicles were
seized. [@1]

Officers found eight knitted bags holding 32 rectangular parcels, each wrapped
in brown masking tape and containing ganja. [@1] The police said the marijuana
seized weighed more than 300 pounds, about 136 kg. [@1] Police estimated the
ganja's street value at $1.3 million. [@1]
