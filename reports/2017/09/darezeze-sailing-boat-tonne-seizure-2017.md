Albanian and Italian law enforcement seized a sailing boat with about a tonne of cannabis on board off Darëzezë beach in central Fier county, in a joint operation mounted after border radar picked up the vessel. [@1] The boat was operated by an Albanian and an Italian, who threw cannabis packages into the sea as the Albanian and Italian police boats reached them; both were taken into custody. [@1] An Albanian wire bulletin of the same date headlines a matching seizure of one tonne of cannabis with two arrests. [@2]
