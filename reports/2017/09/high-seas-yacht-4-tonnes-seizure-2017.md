Italian authorities seized about four tonnes of marijuana from a luxury yacht
on the high seas in the first week of September 2017. [@1] Three Albanians
were caught on board with the drugs. [@1] Albanian police said the yacht had
never entered Albanian waters, and sources told local media the cannabis was
probably brought out to the yacht in international waters by another vessel
that had left the northern Albanian coast. [@1]

Tirana Times describes the haul as part of continuing massive seizures of
Albania-grown marijuana by police in neighbouring countries and in Albania
itself. [@1] The paper says traffickers appear to be shifting routes, moving
loads through Montenegrin or Croatian waters after heavier policing of the
direct Albania-Italy crossing. [@1] The find sparked a dispute between
Albania's police chief, who said navy radar at Durres was not working
properly, and the defence ministry, which said the radar systems were fully
functional. [@1]
