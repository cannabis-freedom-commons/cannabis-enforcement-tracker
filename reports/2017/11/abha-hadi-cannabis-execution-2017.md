Saudi Arabia executed Yemeni national Hassan Mohammed Ali Hadi in Abha for smuggling cannabis in November 2017. [@1,2]

Saudi Arabia executed Yemeni national Hassan Mohammed Ali Hadi in Abha, in the Asir region, after convicting him of smuggling cannabis into the kingdom. [@1] The Interior Ministry said the General Court convicted him, the Appeal and Supreme Courts endorsed the sentence, and a royal order was issued to carry it out. [@1]

An execution log for 2017 records Hadi as beheaded in Saudi Arabia on 21 November for a drugs offence. [@2] A second Saudi man was listed as beheaded for murder the same week, a separate non-cannabis case left out here. [@2]
