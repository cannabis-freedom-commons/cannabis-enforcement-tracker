A team of more than two dozen agencies led by the Grays Harbor County Drug Task Force began executing 50 search warrants on Tuesday 28 November 2017 across Grays Harbor, Thurston and King counties against suspected Chinese-run marijuana grows. [@1, 2] The raids netted 44 arrests, 26 confiscated vehicles, guns, and 32,449 marijuana plants that the sheriff's office valued at more than $80 million. [@1, 2] Investigators also found more than $400,000 in cash and gold, and say the inquiry began after residents reported grows in Elma, Aberdeen and Hoquiam. [@1, 2]

The sheriff's office believes people tied to organised crime bought homes mostly with cash and flipped them into grows, and the sheriff said many of the workers were brought from China under false pretences and held as indentured labour. [@1, 2, 3] Fifty Chinese nationals were later arraigned over the network, with the court communicating through Google Translate at first. [@3]
