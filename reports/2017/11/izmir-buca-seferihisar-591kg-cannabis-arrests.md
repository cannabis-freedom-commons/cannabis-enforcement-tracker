Izmir police anti-narcotics teams raided the Buca home of 26-year-old Y.B. after
receiving information that he was storing drugs there. [@1] Officers found 55
kilograms of esrar, the Turkish term for cannabis, hidden in the base of a bed.
[@1] Y.B. was detained, processed and sent to the courthouse, where a court
remanded him after prosecutors argued the drugs were held within 200 metres of a
school and requested an increased sentence. [@1]

In Seferihisar, officers raided a dog farm where 42-year-old U.Ş.Ö. and
38-year-old Z.U.Ö. allegedly stored drugs obtained from abroad and eastern
provinces. [@2] A search of a room housing dogs at the farm found 4 kilograms
100 grams of esrar. [@2] Both suspects were detained, processed and remanded by
the court they were brought before. [@2]

Together the Buca and Seferihisar raids yielded 59 kilograms 100 grams of esrar
and three remands in custody. [@1] The operations were carried out by the Izmir
Emniyet Mudurlugu Narkotik Suclarla Mucadele Subesi, the city police
directorate's anti-narcotics branch. [@2]
