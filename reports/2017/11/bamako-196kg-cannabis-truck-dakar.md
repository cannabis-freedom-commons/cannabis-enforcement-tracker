Mali customs officers of the Bamako Mobile Intervention Brigade intercepted a
Mali-registered container truck bound for Dakar. [@2] The officers had followed
the truck along the Kati road overnight and stopped it at the Kati camp
roundabout before searching it fully. [@2] The driver and his apprentice were
both arrested and were to be brought before the prosecutor. [@1]

Officers found 196 kilograms of cannabis in the truck, packed as 98 bricks. [@2]
The cannabis was hidden under the chassis, inside the cabin and in the truck’s
second fuel tank. [@2] VOA Afrique reported the market value at about 500
million CFA francs, while Bamada.net reported it at more than 100 million CFA
francs. [@1]

The brigade acted on advance information about a drug shipment moving by truck
near Kati. [@2] The seized bricks were presented to the press on Tuesday
afternoon. [@2] Brigade chief Abdrahamane Diakité said some of the cannabis was
below the truck’s axle and a significant quantity was hidden inside the cabin.
[@1] The brigade said it had seized 319 kg of cannabis or drugs since January
2017. [@2]
