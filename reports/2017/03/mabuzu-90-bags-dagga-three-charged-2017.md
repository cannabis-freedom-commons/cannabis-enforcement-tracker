Two men from Bulawayo and a woman from Botswana were arrested in Botswana, and
all three appeared before the Masunga Magistrate’s Court on a charge of unlawful
possession of dagga. [@1] The two Zimbabwean suspects, aged 62 and 56, live in
Pumula South and Emganwini suburbs in Bulawayo. [@1] The woman, a Motswana aged
44, is the third accused in the same case. [@2]

Botswana Police Assistant Commissioner Witness Bosija said the two men were
arrested in Botswana on a Tuesday morning. [@1] The dagga was in 90 bags of the
kind used for 5kg of mealie-meal, and the police said its weight had not
immediately been established. [@1] Police suspect the two Zimbabweans smuggled
the dagga into Botswana through an illegal crossing point with the woman’s help.
[@1] The cannabis was allegedly found hidden in the woman’s house in Mabuzu
Ward, Botswana. [@1]

The Zimbabwean reports, carried by the Bulawayo Chronicle, Herald, Bulawayo24
and ZimEye, name the three accused and the charge but report no court outcome.
[@3]
