Guinea’s National Gendarmerie presented 1,180 kilograms of cannabis to the press
after seizing it at Petit bateau in the commune of Kaloum in Conakry. [@1] A
gendarmerie patrol intercepted the pirogue Talmèssou-Coopérative as it tried to
reach a Conakry landing whose name officers withheld. [@1] Three people were
aboard the pirogue when the patrol closed in. [@1] Two of them immediately
escaped by sea, while officers detained the captain with the packages. [@1]
Officers also questioned Rouguiatou Camara, who said she knew nothing about the
drugs. [@1] Camara said her uncle was among those detained and had called her to
help him. [@1] She said relatives in Sierra Leone had raised two million for her
road trip to Guinea. [@1] She said she met her uncle at Km36 just as officers
came to arrest him, so she ended up in the wrong place at the wrong time. [@1]

Officers said they acted on information received on Friday 3 March 2017 that a
boat was carrying 47 sacks of cannabis. [@1] The load weighed 1,180 kilograms
and had come from Sierra Leone. [@1] Operational units set a patrol at about 2
a.m. Universal Time to intercept the boat. [@1] The maritime research commander
said a floating unit was sent off Boussoura at about 2 a.m. and saw the boat
coming from Kambia in Sierra Leone. [@1] Officers first took the captain and the
packages to the Fotoba surveillance post base, then moved the packages to the
HCGN store. [@1]

Communication officer Colonel Mamadou Alpha Barry presented the seizure to the
press. [@1] He said traffickers had turned to sea and river routes because road
routes were fully covered by gendarmerie units. [@1] The maritime commander said
insecurity at sea had led them to plan patrols along the coast. [@1]
