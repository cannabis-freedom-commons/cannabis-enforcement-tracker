A 50-year-old Surinamese national pleaded guilty to possessing narcotics for the
purpose of trafficking when the charge was read to him at the Springlands
Magistrates' Court on 8 March 2017. [@1] Magistrate Rabindranauth Singh
sentenced him to three years in prison. [@1]

A Surinamese national was arrested by police on Monday, 6 March 2017, on his way
back home with 242 grams of marijuana. [@1] Police arrested the man as he was
about to board a boat at the Number 78 village foreshore to travel to Suriname
along the backtrack route. [@1] At about 08:30 that day an officer carrying out
routine searches on speedboat passengers searched a man who was behaving
suspiciously. [@1] The officer found a pair of jockey shorts holding two compact
parcels wrapped in scotch tape. [@1] The parcels were opened in his presence and
were found to contain leaves, seeds and stems suspected to be marijuana. [@1]
The items were weighed at Springlands Police Station in his presence and came to
242 grams. [@1] The man, of Nickerie in Suriname, had been travelling home when
he was stopped. [@1]
