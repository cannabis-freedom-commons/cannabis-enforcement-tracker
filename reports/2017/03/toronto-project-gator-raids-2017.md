Toronto police arrested Marc Emery and his wife Jodie on the evening of 8 March 2017 at Pearson airport as they waited to board a flight to Spain, then raided their Cannabis Culture dispensaries the next morning. [@1,2] Officers executed 11 search warrants in Toronto, Hamilton and Vancouver, hitting seven shops, five in Toronto plus one each in Hamilton and Vancouver, and the Emerys' associates' homes. [@2] Five people were arrested: the Emerys, Erin and Chris Goodwin, and Britney Anne Guerra. [@2]

The Emerys were charged with conspiracy to commit an indictable offence, trafficking, possession and possession of the proceeds of crime. [@2] Police spokesman Mark Pugash said the force was merely enforcing the law, pointing to health risks from untested dispensary cannabis and a spate of armed robberies of pot shops. [@2] Federal health minister Jane Philpott answered that the government remained on track to table its legalisation proposal within months. [@2] Activists at the courthouse called the operation a waste of taxpayer money aimed at entrenching the licensed-producer model. [@2]
