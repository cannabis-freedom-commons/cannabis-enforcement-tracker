Police in North Iceland raided a private home in the fishing town of Grenivík
on 4 March 2017 on suspicion it was being used to manufacture marijuana on a
large scale. [@1] Officers found more than 50 fully grown marijuana plants
and 30 smaller plants, along with 2 kg of dried marijuana. [@1] One person
was arrested at the scene and confessed during questioning that the drugs
belonged to him. [@1]

The same house had been raided less than twelve months earlier, when police
shut a larger growing operation there in May 2016. [@1] The house was
described as specially remodelled to hold a hidden indoor greenhouse. [@1]
Police suspect growers have been moving production out of the capital area to
evade detection. [@1]
