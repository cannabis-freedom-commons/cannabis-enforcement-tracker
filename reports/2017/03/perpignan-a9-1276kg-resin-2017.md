Perpignan customs officers seized a large quantity of cannabis resin from a
heavy lorry on the A9 motorway on the evening of Sunday 26 March 2017. [@2] The
customs service announced the seizure on Tuesday in a statement. [@1] The
Perpignan customs brigade carried out the lorry check. [@1] The lorry was coming
from Spain and was heading toward the south of France. [@1] The driver was alone
on board and presented papers declaring a transport of lettuce. [@1]

During the trailer inspection, officers noticed plastic crates behind the
pallets of salads and decided to examine the load more closely. [@2] Behind the
salads, officers found 39 plastic-coated fabric bags containing loaves of
cannabis resin. [@1] A second account described the find as about forty such
bags holding 1,276 kg in total. [@2]

Customs estimated the haul at nearly 8.3 million euros on the illicit retail
market. [@2] Judicial follow-up in the case was entrusted to the Perpignan
prosecutor. [@1]
