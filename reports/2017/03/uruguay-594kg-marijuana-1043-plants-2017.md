Uruguay's Dirección General de Represión al Tráfico Ilícito de Drogas (DGRTID)
ran operations across the country that allowed 594,472 grams of marijuana to be
seized in the year so far. [@1] Between 1 January and 22 March 2017 the
directorate's units seized 594,472 grams of marijuana, 1,043 cannabis plants and
350 grams of cannabis seeds. [@1] More than 700 people were detained over
offences linked to drug trafficking and more than 100 were sent for prosecution.
[@2]

The director of the unit, commissioner general Walter Menéndez, said the volume
of operations run by its teams had led drug traffickers to change the way they
work. [@1] Menéndez said the operations were based on intelligence analysis of
the timetables, modus operandi and routes used by international trafficking
organisations. [@2] Police were permanently monitoring national routes,
especially those giving access to Uruguay across the borders with Brazil and
Argentina. [@1] Specialised staff were reinforced in the departments of Rocha
and Maldonado to track criminal organisations during the summer season. [@2]
