Police first detained M.U. and, acting on his statement, caught A.A. and H.Ç. at
their homes. [@1] After police procedures, the suspects were referred to the
courthouse. [@1] A.A. was remanded in custody by the court for trading in
narcotic substances. [@1] H.Ç. was released under judicial control, while M.U.
was released after giving a statement to the prosecutor. [@1]

Searches of the suspects' homes found 10 cannabis plants grown in pots. [@1]
Officers also seized 100 grams of cannabis prepared for sale in 15 cigarette
packs. [@1] Hint keneviri is the Turkish term for cannabis plants and esrar is
the Turkish term for cannabis. [@1]

The operation was carried out by teams of the Bodrum District Police
Directorate's Anti-Smuggling and Organized Crime unit, known as KOM, against
people identified as selling drugs. [@1] The operation took place in Bodrum
district, where the district police targeted the suspected sellers' homes. [@1]
