A Maldives man was arrested in India for alleged involvement in drug peddling.
[@1] The suspect was identified as 35-year-old Anil Ahmed, who was arrested
together with a Nigerian national. [@1] The arrests took place at a house in CMR
Layout in Chellikete, Bengaluru, according to Indian media reports cited in the
account. [@1]

Officials seized 48 grams of hashish oil, 430 grams of marijuana, three grams of
methamphetamine and five blots of LSD from the two suspects. [@1] The cannabis
component recorded here is the 48 grams of hashish oil and 430 grams of
marijuana, together 478 grams. [@1] The three grams of methamphetamine and five
blots of LSD are mentioned only to explain the same mixed seizure and are
excluded from cannabis figures. [@1]

Police alleged the two were selling drugs to local distributors. [@1] Indian
officials said Ahmed had also been involved in smuggling hashish oil through
Kempegowda International Airport in November 2016 and had been absconding since
then. [@1] Both suspects remained in judicial custody. [@1]
