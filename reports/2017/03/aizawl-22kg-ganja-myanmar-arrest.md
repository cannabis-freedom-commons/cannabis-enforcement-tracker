One person from Selam village in Champhai district, on the Mizoram-Myanmar
border, was arrested for allegedly smuggling the seized contraband from Myanmar.
[@1] The allegation against the arrested person was smuggling the contraband
from Myanmar. [@1] The accused was booked under relevant sections of the
Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, 1985, the statute named in the
case. [@1]

In Aizawl, Mizoram Excise and Narcotics department officials seized over 21.98
kg of ganja, as the department's officials reported. [@1] The seized ganja was
described parenthetically as cannabis, identifying the contraband as a cannabis
product. [@1] Department officials said the seizure was made the previous night,
making the Aizawl seizure an overnight enforcement action. [@1]

The arrested person belongs to Selam village, which lies in Champhai district on
the Mizoram-Myanmar border. [@1] The case was presented as a cross-border
smuggling case, with Myanmar named as the alleged source of the seized
contraband. [@1] The Mizoram Excise and Narcotics department, through its
officials, reported both the 21.98 kg seizure and the related arrest. [@1]
