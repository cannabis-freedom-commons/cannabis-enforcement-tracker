Sala Krao district police and village security guards raided a marijuana patch
in O'Andong commune on Tuesday morning after locals tipped them off. [@2]
Officers arrested 54-year-old Meal Pun at his home for cultivating cannabis.
[@1] Pun said he had grown the plants for a long time for personal use, and
police said young villagers sometimes smoked with him. [@2] Police sent Pun to
the Pailin provincial police station with the uprooted plants, two pipes, a
knife and a chopping board allegedly used to process the plants. [@2] Deputy
provincial police chief Un Serey Roth said Pun was released yesterday after
signing an agreement not to plant marijuana anymore and that the plants would be
destroyed. [@2] Another deputy provincial police chief said Pun had been sent to
his office and would appear in court once the relevant paperwork was complete.
[@1]

Officers found 89 marijuana plants around Pun's home, some of them not yet fully
grown. [@2] Fewer than 20 of the plants were mature, and three had already been
harvested and dried. [@2] Officers also seized two smoking pipes and a long
paring knife and cutting board used to prepare cannabis, keeping them as
evidence with the plants. [@1] The plants had been grown among citronella, herbs
and bamboo bushes before officers uprooted them. [@2]

District police said they did not know whether Pun grew the marijuana only for
personal use or intended to sell it, but his actions were illegal either way.
[@1] Police described cannabis as an addictive substance listed in the Law on
Drug Control, which bans its consumption and trafficking. [@2]
