Saudi Arabia executed two convicted drug traffickers on Thursday 23 March 2017, after convictions in the kingdom. [@1,2] The state-run Saudi Press Agency named them as Saudi national Nasser Harshan and Pakistani national Namtallah Khasta Qul. [@1,2]

SPA said Harshan was a repeat offender found guilty of dealing hashish, while Qul was found guilty of dealing heroin. [@1,2] Most Saudi executions are carried out by beheading with a sword, which the interior ministry calls a deterrent. [@2] The two executions brought the number of prisoners killed in the kingdom that year to 20. [@1,2]
