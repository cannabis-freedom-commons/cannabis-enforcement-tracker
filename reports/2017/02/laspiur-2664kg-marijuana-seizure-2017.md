Argentina's Policía Federal detained four members of a drug gang in Saturnino
María Laspiur, a town in Córdoba province, and seized 2,664 kilograms of
marijuana. [@1] Officers stopped a Toyota Hilux pickup and a truck with a
trailer in the parking area of a service station, where the load was hidden
inside the truck. [@1]

The ministry said the marijuana was divided into 2,995 packages hidden inside
the truck. [@1] The seizure took place at Avenida Dr. Lamelas 200, on Ruta
Nacional 158 at kilometre 47. [@1] The gang drew its marijuana from Paraguay and
moved it through northern Argentina towards Chile and the provinces of Mendoza,
San Juan and San Luis. [@1]

Officers of the federal police's San Luis delegation led the operation with
support from its Bell Ville and Villa Mercedes units and its intelligence
division, working with provincial authorities. [@1] The ministry said the
inquiry had begun in late 2015 and relied on intelligence work and telephone
intercepts. [@1]

The load was intercepted before dawn on Saturday 11 February 2017, in the urban
area of Saturnino María Laspiur, about 195 kilometres east of the city of
Córdoba. [@2]

Three of the four detainees lived in San Luis province and one in Formosa
province, and the suspects were taken to the court in San Francisco and then to
San Luis. [@2]

The load travelled in the chassis of a Ford Cargo 1722 truck, its trailer and
the Toyota Hilux pickup. [@2]

The prosecutor leading the inquiry was federal prosecutor Cristian Rachid, in a
case run by the courts of San Luis province. [@3]

Officers spent about 12 hours counting the load, which the outlet described as
the largest drug shipment seized in Córdoba province. [@3]

Two men were travelling in the pickup and two in the truck, whose make was given
as a Ford Cargo 1752. [@4]

Police sources gave the weight as between 2,600 and 2,800 kilograms and said the
inquiry had lasted about a year. [@4]
