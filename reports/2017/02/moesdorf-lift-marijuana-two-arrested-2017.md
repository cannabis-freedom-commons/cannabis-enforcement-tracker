A police patrol found a man in the rue d'Ettelbrück in Moesdorf late on the
evening of Wednesday 1 February 2017, very lightly clothed for the weather, and
offered him a lift to Mersch after he said he had missed his bus. [@1] When he
got into the car officers noticed the smell of marijuana and found that he was
carrying two large bags of it. [@2]

At the police station the bags were weighed at 800 grams and one kilo, about 1.8
kg in total. [@3] The man claimed he was innocent and that the bag belonged to
an acquaintance who had thrown him out of his car shortly before. [@1] Police
found and stopped that car, driven by a family member of the man, with another
man inside. [@1]

The public prosecutor ordered a house search at the shared home of the man and
the driver, carried out with a dog handler. [@1] The flat smelled of marijuana
on entry and officers seized 19 small bags holding more than 300 grams in total.
[@2] Drug utensils, a prohibited weapon and a small bag of white powder were
also confiscated. [@1]

The prosecutor's office ordered the arrest of the two residents and both were
brought before an investigating judge on Thursday morning. [@2]
