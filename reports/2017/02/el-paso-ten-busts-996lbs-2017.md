U.S. Customs and Border Protection officers working at the El Paso port of entry made
ten drug busts on Tuesday 21 February 2017, seizing a total of 996 pounds (about
452 kg) of marijuana with an estimated street value of $796,800. [@1]

The largest seizure of the day came just before 3 p.m. at the Bridge of the Americas
crossing, when a 2003 Ford F150 pickup truck arrived from Mexico. [@1] Drug-sniffing
dog Dora alerted to the vehicle, and officers scanning it with the Z-portal x-ray
system saw anomalies in the pickup bed. [@1] They found 191 marijuana-filled bundles
stashed in a false compartment in the bed. [@1]

Officers arrested the driver, a 41-year-old U.S. citizen man, and turned him over to
Homeland Security Investigations special agents. [@1] The report names no other arrests
connected with the ten marijuana seizures. [@1]
