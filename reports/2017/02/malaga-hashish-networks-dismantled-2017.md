Spanish authorities announced that in less than two days they had dismantled two international networks smuggling hashish from northern Morocco into the port of Malaga. [@1] Officers confiscated a helicopter, cars, boats and communications gear along with the drugs, and arrested 33 members of the two networks. [@1] A second account of the same operation put the haul at 3.7 metric tonnes of hashish plus the helicopter, cars, boats, cash and weapons, with Moroccan authorities cooperating in the investigation. [@2]

According to a Civil Guard statement, officers broke up the first network in the cities of Malaga and Granada, seizing three tonnes of hashish valued at about $4 million and arresting 15 Moroccan and Spanish nationals. [@1] Raids on houses used by that network turned up five speedboats worth about $600,000 with trucks to carry them to and from the beach, plus seven leisure boats, stolen cars, documents, computers and smartphones. [@1] The second network, caught in Murcia and Alicante, lost 700 kilos of hashish, three boats, 13 cars, a gun with ammunition, communications gear and large sums of money, with 18 more people arrested. [@1]
