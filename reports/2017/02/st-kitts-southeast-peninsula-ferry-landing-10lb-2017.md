Three people were detained near the ferry landing on St Kitts's Southeast
Peninsula on Sunday 5 February 2017 after police found a box of marijuana and
cocaine. [@1] The Royal St Christopher and Nevis Police Force said the three
were interviewed and later released, and that nobody had been charged over the
find. [@1] The box held about ten pounds of marijuana, some 4.54 kg, and about
two pounds of cocaine. [@1] The force estimated the street value of the
marijuana at EC$93,288, and put the cocaine separately at EC$89,100. [@1]

The operation combined the police Special Services Unit, an armed unit, the
police Anti-Drug Unit and the St Kitts and Nevis Defence Force. [@2] Three
county press outlets carried the police release on 6 February 2017, a day after
the search. [@2]

The release linked the find to an unrelated January case in which a passenger
arriving at Robert L. Bradshaw International Airport was stopped carrying
cocaine in his stomach. [@1] NevisPages and SKN PULSE carried the same release
but gave the marijuana's street value as EC$279,864. [@3]
