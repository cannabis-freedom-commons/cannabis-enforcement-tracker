Six people in western North Carolina were arrested after officers found more than a ton of marijuana in a van at a home in Asheville. [@1,2] Stacy Cox of the Asheville office of North Carolina Alcohol Law Enforcement said officers found a shipment of nearly 2,300 pounds (about 1,043 kg) in the van around 7 p.m. on Tuesday. [@1,2]

## Tip, raid and charges

Law enforcement had received information that a large shipment of marijuana was expected in the Asheville area. [@1,2] A local account says agents raided the Buncombe County home around 6.30 p.m. after an informant's tip, with a helicopter overhead and officers on the scene until 2 a.m. [@3] Investigators valued the marijuana at $3 million. [@1,2,3]

Two of the people arrested lived at the home where the marijuana was found. [@1,2] Those arrested were men and women ranging in age from 25 to 67. [@3] All six were each charged with two felony counts of conspiracy to traffic marijuana. [@1,2]
