Gaza's anti-narcotics police raided all the storage sites of a drugs consignment
in three governorates and seized 1,100 slabs of hashish. [@1] Officers arrested
a number of traders and dealers during the raids. [@1] The police said they had
followed information for more than a month that a trader intended to bring a
large quantity of drugs into the Gaza Strip, and had located where it was
stored. [@1] The head of the anti-narcotics directorate, Col. Ahmed al-Qadra,
gave the account and called on the Palestinian courts to impose harsh sentences
on drug traders. [@1] The same raids also seized 250 cartons of narcotic pills,
a non-cannabis quantity not counted here. [@1]

Al-Qadra said banjo, the local name for cannabis, was grown inside Gaza's border
areas before the police put an end to its cultivation and production after 2007.
[@1] He said the drugs found in Gaza are smuggled in across the borders and that
nothing is grown or manufactured inside the Strip. [@1] He said large quantities
of hashish had been seized on Gaza's borders in the current year before they
could be smuggled in, among the largest seizures of the past decade. [@1]

The Interior Ministry in Gaza published the same account and the same figure of
1,100 slabs in its own statement of 5 February 2017. [@2]
