Deputies in Nebraska pulled over a truck after noticing a strong smell coming
from it during a routine highway traffic stop. [@1] The occupants were Patrick
Jiron, 80, and his wife Barbara Jiron, 83, who lived in Northern California and
said they were taking the drugs to Vermont. [@1] Both were jailed in York County
on felony possession of marijuana with intent to deliver and no drug tax stamp.
[@1]

A probable cause search of the couple's Toyota Tacoma turned up the marijuana in
boxes, according to Lt. Paul Vrbka of the York County Sheriff's Department. [@1]
Large bags found inside held about 60 pounds of marijuana, roughly 27.2 kg. [@1]
The stop took place on Dec. 19, 2017, the date the sheriff's department gave for
its photograph of the load. [@1]

Vrbka said the couple told investigators the marijuana was for Christmas
presents. [@1] Vrbka estimated the marijuana's street value at $336,000. [@1]

California's law legalizing recreational marijuana did not take effect until
Jan. 1, and marijuana remained illegal in Nebraska. [@1]
