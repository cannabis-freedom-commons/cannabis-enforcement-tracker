The Drug Enforcement Commission arrested six farmers for cultivation of cannabis
contrary to the laws of Zambia. [@1] Five of the arrested farmers are from
Mulobezi District whilst one is from Sibanze village. [@1] The Drug Enforcement
Commission in Southern Province arrested five small-scale farmers of Matako
Chiefdom in Mulobezi District for unlawful cultivation of cannabis plants
weighing over six tonnes. [@1] A second outlet confirmed that five small-scale
farmers of Matako Chiefdom in Mulobezi District were arrested by the Drug
Enforcement Commission for unlawful cultivation of cannabis plants weighing over
six tonnes. [@2]

Sibanda Ntunta, 29, of Kamwatamwata Village was held for cultivation of cannabis
plants weighing 3.9 tonnes. [@1] Kapupa Eric, 37, also of Kamwatamwata Village
was held for cultivation of cannabis plants weighing 600 kilograms. [@1] Madao
Janita, a female of unknown age in Kazuzi Village was held for cultivation of
cannabis plants weighing 600 kilograms. [@1] Kapupa Limbama, 41, also of Kazuzi
Village was held for cultivation of 495 kilograms of cannabis plants. [@1]
Kapeto Ostern, 36, a small-scale farmer of Sibanze Village was held for
cultivation of cannabis plants weighing 790 kilograms. [@1]

The arrests were announced in a statement issued by DEC public relations officer
Kamufisa Manchishi. [@1] The suspects were expected to appear in court on the
day of the statement. [@1]
