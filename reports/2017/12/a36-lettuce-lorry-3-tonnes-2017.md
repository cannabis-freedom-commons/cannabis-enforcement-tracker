French anti-drug police seized about three tonnes of cannabis from a lorry
transporting lettuce at a toll station on the A36 motorway in eastern France
on Thursday 14 December 2017. [@1,2] The consignment was cannabis resin, and
officers also found more than 200 kilograms of marijuana leaves hidden among
the lettuce crates. [@1,2] Police put the street value of the haul at six
million euros. [@1,2]

The seizure was disclosed on Saturday by sources speaking to the AFP news
agency. [@1] The lorry's 60-year-old Spanish driver was detained, along with
a second driver who police allege was driving a scout car ahead of the
lorry. [@1] Both men were held at a police station in Bayonne, in the French
Basque country, and were due to appear in court the following Monday. [@1]
