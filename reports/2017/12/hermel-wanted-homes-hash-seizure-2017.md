Lebanon's army raided the homes of several wanted individuals in Hermel on the morning of 4 December 2017 and confiscated around four tonnes of unmanufactured hashish and 80 kilograms of manufactured hashish, as well as two drug manufacturing machines. [@1] Troops also destroyed around 30 tonnes of hashish plants during the raid. [@1] Everything confiscated was handed to the relevant authorities so that the necessary measures could be taken. [@1]

## Weapons alongside the drugs

In the same raided homes the army confiscated a DShK machine gun, a Browning shotgun, two rifles, one mortar, five binoculars, an amount of ammunition and various items of military equipment. [@1] The statement reports no arrests from the operation. [@1]
