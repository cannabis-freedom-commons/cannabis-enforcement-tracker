The Civil Guard dismantled an international drug-trafficking network based in
Malaga province and arrested 15 suspected members. [@1] The detainees were
accused of drug-trafficking, criminal-organisation membership, forgery, unlawful
weapons possession and money laundering. [@2] All of the detainees were remanded
in prison by court order, and two international arrest warrants were issued for
two other fugitive members of the group. [@2]

Officers seized 3,947 kg of hashish in the operation, described as nearly four
tonnes. [@2] The operation also netted 12,450 euros in cash, a sailboat, a
firearm, ten vehicles, three frequency jammers, satellite and mobile phones and
computer equipment. [@1] After bringing the hashish into Malaga province, the
network hid it in two so-called primary nurseries in Manilva and San Pedro de
Alcantara, then moved it by high-speed Go-Fast cars to a secondary stash house
in a rural part of Utrera, in Sevilla province, before trucking it on to other
European countries. [@2] In September investigators boarded an Italian-flagged
sailboat on the high seas and arrested its two Italian crew members, seizing
1,072 kg of hashish resin. [@2]

The investigation began the previous March, when officers learned of the
Malaga-based organisation's complex infrastructure for receiving large
quantities of Moroccan hashish and distributing it across Europe. [@1] To avoid
suspicion at police checkpoints, the organisation used people in their sixties
driving rental cars, including a 67-year-old caught in September with 107 kg of
hashish and a 70-year-old caught in October with seven bales weighing 272 kg.
[@2]
