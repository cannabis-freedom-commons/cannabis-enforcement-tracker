Officers of U.S. Customs and Border Protection at the Pharr International Bridge cargo
facility found 1,055 pounds (about 479 kg) of marijuana inside a commercial trailer
hauling avocados on Saturday 16 December 2017. [@1,2] Officers had referred the white
2005 Freightliner tractor-trailer for a secondary inspection and used a non-intrusive
imaging system to locate the narcotics. [@1,2]

Officers extracted 440 packages of marijuana from the trailer. [@1,2] The drugs were
valued at approximately $210,980, and the case remained under investigation by Homeland
Security Investigations special agents. [@1,2] The reports name no arrests. [@1,2]
