In Dnestrovsk, officers of the city internal-affairs department and the
Transnistrian Interior Ministry Criminal Investigation Department detained a man
suspected of drug trafficking. [@1] The detained man was a 26-year-old
unemployed local resident. [@1] He was held under Article 104 of the
Transnistrian Code of Criminal Procedure. [@1] According to the Interior
Ministry press centre, he refused to testify. [@1]

A personal search found a bag containing 11 paper wraps on him. [@1] The wraps
held crushed plant material that resembled marijuana by appearance and smell.
[@1] A search of his flat found about another fifteen wraps with similar
contents. [@2] The Interior Ministry dates this Dnestrovsk incident to 4
December. [@2]

The Dnestrovsk police opened a criminal case over the detected drug-trafficking
facts. [@1] Police said further operational-search measures were continuing and
a number of special expert examinations had been ordered. [@1]
