Police in St Catherine arrested and charged 50-year-old Maxie Thompson, of Cocoa
Ridge in Spanish Town, with breaches of the Dangerous Drugs Act after ganja was
seized at a premises in his community last Friday. [@1] He was charged with
dealing in ganja and illegal possession of ganja. [@1]

Between 5:00 am and 8:00 am, a team of officers carried out a series of
operations at four premises, where they found a total of 125 pounds of ganja,
about 56.7 kilograms. [@1] Twenty-five pounds of the drug, about 11.3 kilograms,
was found at Thompson's residence, and the rest of the haul was found at the
other premises. [@1]

Thompson was listed to appear in the St Catherine Parish Court on 30 January
2018, and police said investigations were continuing. [@1] He was the only
person police reported charging over the four premises searched. [@1]
