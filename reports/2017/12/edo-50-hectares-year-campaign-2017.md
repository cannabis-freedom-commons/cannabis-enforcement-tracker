The Edo State command of the National Drug Law Enforcement Agency destroyed 50 hectares of cannabis plantation across the state between January and December 2017. [@1] Command spokesman Mitchell Ofoyeju said the destroyed farms would have generated 240,000 kg of cannabis, valued at 2.9 billion naira. [@1] The command separately seized 50,000 kg of cannabis in different locations, including the Benin metropolis, and arrested 240 suspected dealers in the operations. [@1]

## A year of seizures

Ofoyeju called 2017 very eventful for the command, with the highest monthly seizure of 10,929 kg in November and the lowest, 90.80 kg, in March. [@1] He said cannabis cultivators had invaded farms across local government areas, particularly the government reserved forest, and decried the lack of operational facilities, including vehicles and rehabilitation bed spaces. [@1]
