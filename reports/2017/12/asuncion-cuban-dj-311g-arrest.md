Agents of SENAD's Urban Operations Department with prosecutor Carlos Alcaraz
surprised the suspect inside the apartment with friends. [@2] The raid took
place at 21:00 on the previous Friday at the apartment on Comandante Franco casi
Capitán Pedro Carpinelli in Asunción. [@2] The detained man was 39-year-old
Cuban national Alejandro Henriquez Aquino, who lived in Paraguay as a political
refugee. [@2] He worked as a DJ and co-owned Sequence Club, an electronic-music
disco in central Asunción. [@2] Officers detained Henriquez inside the house.
[@1]

Officers seized 311.4 grams of marijuana in several doses, alongside 100.5 grams
of cocaine, 63 ecstasy pills and 289 LSD tablets. [@2] They also seized three
mobile phones, a scale and G. 982,000 in cash thought to come from drug sales.
[@2] Agents later went to the Sequence disco and found only documents showing
Henriquez was one of its owners. [@2]

The raid followed a two-month investigation using wiretaps and undercover agents
to buy substances sold at a disco. [@1] The prosecutor treated the evidence and
quantity as pointing to micro-trafficking rather than mere consumption. [@1]
Henriquez said the drugs found were only for personal use. [@1] He was charged
with drug trafficking and possession. [@2]
