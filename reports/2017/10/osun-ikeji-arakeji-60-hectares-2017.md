The National Drug Law Enforcement Agency's Osun State command destroyed a 60.8-hectare cannabis farm at Ikeji Arakeji in Oriade Local Government Area. [@1,2] State commandant Samuel Egbeola disclosed the operation during an interview with journalists at the agency's office in Osogbo. [@1,2] The farm was destroyed on 5 October following a tip-off from an intelligence source. [@1,2]

Officers seized ten bags of already harvested cannabis weighing 107.4 kilograms during the operation. [@1,2] The agency also arrested two suspects found on the farm. [@1,2] Egbeola said it was the command's second such feat in two weeks. [@1,2]

Egbeola appealed to residents to keep reporting cannabis plantations to the agency, saying its office at the testing ground area in Osogbo was always open. [@1] He said the agency would not relent in its fight against illicit drugs across the state. [@1,2]
