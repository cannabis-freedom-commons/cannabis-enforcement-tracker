Guinea-Bissau's Polícia Judiciária dismantled a network selling liamba in the
Quelélé neighbourhood of Bissau and detained a young man of the male sex in that
operation. [@1] The account identifies liamba in parentheses as cânhamo,
explaining the local term for the cannabis product. [@1] A source from that
criminal-investigation police force announced the Bissau operations in the city
on that day. [@1]

Officers seized six kilograms of liamba in connection with the dismantled
Quelélé sales network. [@1] The Quelélé cannabis operation was presented as
another distinct operation alongside the airport cocaine case in Bissau. [@1]
The separate airport case involved a man of Guinean nationality detained at the
Osvaldo Vieira international airport. [@1] He was held with 49 capsules of
cocaine weighing about 600 grams. [@1]

The same source explained that the airport detainee had come from Brazil to
Morocco and then taken the connection to Bissau. [@1] Quelélé is described as a
bairro, a neighbourhood, in Bissau. [@1] Osvaldo Vieira, where the separate
cocaine detention occurred, is identified as an international airport. [@1]
