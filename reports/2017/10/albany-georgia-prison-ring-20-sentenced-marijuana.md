Twenty people were sentenced in Albany Federal Court for a wide-ranging drug
conspiracy run inside various Georgia prisons. [@1] Inmates including Antron
Miles, Anthony Moore and Rejhan Mujanovic conspired with people outside prisons
to acquire and distribute methamphetamine, heroin, cocaine and marijuana. [@1]
The inmates coordinated with outsiders using cell phones smuggled into Georgia
Department of Corrections facilities. [@1] The drugs were warehoused in Tifton
and distributed by Leslie Howard, Tonya Harp and Sarah Frost Taylor. [@1]

Drugs shipped by United States Mail to Enigma were diverted by relief postmaster
Loretta Hillman Hunt to other conspirators. [@2] Loretta Hunt of Tifton was
sentenced on August 29, 2017 to five years of probation for conspiracy to
distribute marijuana. [@1] Antron Miles of Tifton was sentenced on October 10,
2017 to 360 months imprisonment to run consecutive to his Tift County sentence.
[@1]

Agents seized multiple kilograms of high purity methamphetamine, hundreds of
grams of heroin and marijuana together, small amounts of cocaine and
prescription pills, and $30,000. [@2] The case was investigated by the Drug
Enforcement Administration Macon Division, GBI Sylvester, Ben Hill County
Sheriff's Office, Tift County Sheriff's Office and the Mid South Narcotics Task
Force. [@1]
