Police officers from the Capesterre-Belle-Eau police station were patrolling
near the flamboyants on a Tuesday when they spotted a parked scooter whose
number plate was only partly visible. [@1] The officers decided to check the
scooter's owner, a 19-year-old who had come to collect the machine. [@1] The
19-year-old was carrying more than ten packaged sachets of cannabis herb and a
sum of money whose origin he could not explain. [@1]

A search of his home found a quantity of cannabis herb, and several cannabis
plants were also discovered behind the house. [@1] The investigation was handed
to officers of the urban safety squad (brigade de sûreté urbaine) of the
Capesterre-Belle-Eau police station and was still under way. [@1]
