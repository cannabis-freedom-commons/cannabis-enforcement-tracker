Police officers from Timis county and prosecutors of the anti-organised-crime directorate DIICOT found a tonne of cannabis buds drying in a warehouse hall near the village of Nitchidorf on 10 October 2017. [@1,2] The crop belonged to a German citizen of Romanian origin who led a drug-trafficking network that investigators had watched for a long time. [@1,2]

The organised-crime police of Timis ran the operation together with the DIICOT prosecutors. [@1] Officers made several successive cannabis purchases from the network using undercover officers before moving in. [@1,2] On Tuesday they staged another purchase and caught the German citizen with six kilograms of drugs on him. [@1,2]

Together with two other members of the network, he had kept the tonne of cannabis buds to dry in the hall near Nitchidorf, as well as many cannabis plants. [@1] The six kilograms from the staged purchase were reported separately from the tonne in the warehouse. [@1,2] The three men were taken to the DIICOT headquarters in Timisoara for questioning. [@1,2]
