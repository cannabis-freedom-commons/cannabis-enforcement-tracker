A joint Civil and Military Police operation on Tuesday 17 October 2017 uprooted
about six thousand cannabis plants at Sítio Serrinha in rural Santa Filomena, in
Pernambuco's Sertão. [@1] Police reached the site after arresting Francisco José
da Cruz, 47, identified as responsible for the planting. [@1]

Santa Filomena military police were called at about 10 a.m. on 17 October by
civil police who reported the plantation, and military police from Santa Cruz
joined the teams. [@2] The blog account describes the suspect as an
endemic-disease agent who was located in Santa Filomena and confirmed the
plantation existed, and reports that the plants were uprooted at the site. [@2]

Alongside the plantation officers found 14.5 kg of cannabis ready for
consumption, two precision scales, two mobile phones, 2,000 metres of electrical
wire and R$ 201 in cash. [@1] The seized material and the arrested suspect were
taken to the Civil Police station in Ouricuri, where the case was registered.
[@2]
