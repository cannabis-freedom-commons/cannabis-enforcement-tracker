A Dili court sentenced Australian resident Tony Crean to three and a half years in prison on 20 October 2017 in a case involving suspected cannabis plants. [@2] He was convicted of possession and trafficking but acquitted of consumption after a negative urine test. [@2] His defence said it would appeal. [@2]

The case began with five suspected cannabis plants, and Crean had been held in pretrial custody since 31 January. [@1,2] Prosecutors had successfully challenged an earlier decision that let him remain outside prison with reporting requirements and a confiscated passport. [@1]
