Eight Colombians were detained in Los Santos province after police seized a boat
carrying 1,452 packages of marijuana at La Honda, in the Santa Ana
corregimiento, the weekend of 21–22 October 2017. [@1] The aeronaval service
said it found a launch carrying five Colombians at the port of La Honda during
an aerial and naval operation, and that they were placed at the disposal of the
authorities. [@2] El Siglo, citing Drug Prosecution Service data, counted 30
sacks holding 1,453 packages of marijuana and said five Colombians were arrested
trying to bring the drug ashore by boat at Puerto de La Honda. [@3]

Los Santos judge of guarantees Bosco Monterrey validated the detainees'
preventive detention and charges of international drug trafficking, and allowed
the Los Santos drug prosecutors six months to complete the investigation. [@1]
Monterrey also imposed precautionary measures on the boat and two outboard
motors the detainees had used. [@1] During the hearing, defence lawyer Boris
Quintero asked the judge to grant periodic reporting to prosecutors instead, and
the request was rejected. [@1]

El Siglo said the Senan made the seizure after a pursuit, and that it was the
ninth drug seizure that year in Los Santos province. [@3] La Prensa reported
that on the previous Saturday police intercepted another boat 60 miles off Los
Santos and detained two Spaniards and a Venezuelan aboard with 500 kilograms of
cocaine, a separate operation. [@1]
