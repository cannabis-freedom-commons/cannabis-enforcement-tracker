One suspect detained in the Lice seizure was remanded by the court after
proceedings before the judicial authorities. [@1] A second suspect was detained
over cannabis plants found within the boundaries of Kaya village in Eğil. [@2]
The governorship said judicial proceedings were continuing under the
prosecutor's instructions after the cannabis was confiscated and the plants were
destroyed on site. [@2]

Officers recovered a total of 1 ton 158 kilograms of powdered cannabis in 57
sacks hidden at four separate points in mountainous and forested terrain between
Oyuklu and Kabakaya villages in Lice. [@2] Officers found 52,000 cannabis plants
in a field in Kaya village in Eğil and destroyed them. [@1]

The Diyarbakır governorship said provincial gendarmerie acted on intelligence in
rural Lice and Eğil to seize illegally produced drugs described as a financing
source of the terrorist organisation. [@2] The statement dated that operational
activity to 17 May 2017. [@2] Haber7, crediting Anadolu Agency, described the
same Lice and Eğil operations as narcoterror operations yielding 1 ton 158
kilograms of cannabis and 52,000 cannabis roots. [@1] The governorship said work
against narcoterrorism would continue to protect public safety and eliminate the
organisation's financing sources. [@2]
