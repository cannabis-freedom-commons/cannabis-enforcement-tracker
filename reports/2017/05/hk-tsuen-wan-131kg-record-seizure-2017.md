Hong Kong Customs raided a logistics company in an industrial building on
Wang Lung Street in Tsuen Wan on 8 May 2017, capping an investigation into a
local drug trafficking syndicate that began early in the year. [@1] Officers
found a sea consignment from North America declared as personal effects and
seized about 131 kilograms of suspected cannabis buds with an estimated
market value of about HK$21.5 million, a record quantity for a Customs
cannabis-bud seizure in the past decade. [@1]

Officers kept the company under surveillance and arrested three local men,
aged 26, 41 and 60, when they came to collect the consignment the same day.
[@1] Two of the men said they were unemployed and the third said he was a
transportation worker. [@1] Trafficking in a dangerous drug carries a maximum
penalty of life imprisonment and a HK$5 million fine. [@1]
