Police stopped a car on the Diyarbakır–Şanlıurfa highway on 8 May 2017. [@2] The
Şanlıurfa Provincial Police Narcotics Branch had received information that
cannabis was to be transported from Diyarbakır to Suruç and followed two cars,
one serving as a lead vehicle, near the entrance to Hilvan. [@1] Officers
stopped the cars at the Bozova road junction and found 143 kilos 600 grams
(143.6 kg) of packaged cannabis in the vehicle carrying the consignment. [@1]
Hürriyet identifies the driver as Fehmi Ş., says officers found the cannabis in
a concealed compartment in the car's trunk, and names the stopping officers as
members of the Anti-Smuggling and Organized Crime Branch. [@2]

Police detained the driver and two passengers, İzzet B. and Hüseyin A. [@2]
After questioning at the police station, all three were sent to the courthouse.
[@2] A court remanded them on a drug-trafficking offense; Hürriyet's account
also describes the offense as trafficking and transporting narcotics. [@2]
