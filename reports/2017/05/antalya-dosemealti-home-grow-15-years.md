Antalya's 4th Heavy Penal Court sentenced Ali Rıza Ceylan and Ümit Yılmaz to 15
years in prison each for growing cannabis at home and drug trafficking. [@1] The
two attended the hearing as detained defendants, along with their relatives and
lawyers. [@1] Ceylan said he was a drug addict who grew the home cannabis for
his own use and denied trading drugs. [@1] Yılmaz said he agreed with Ceylan and
asked for acquittal. [@1]

Police raided a house in Yeniköy neighbourhood in Döşemealtı on 4 February 2017
after a tip-off and found Indian hemp grown with special lighting and heating.
[@1] Officers seized 6 kilos 800 grams (6.8 kg) of cannabis material during the
search. [@1] Both men were detained after the raid. [@1]

The court gave each man 5 years for cultivating hemp to obtain cannabis beyond
personal need and 10 years for drug trafficking. [@1] The court said it weighed
the manner of the offence, the quantity and value of the drugs, and the
instruments and methods used. [@1] It also imposed fines of varying amounts.
[@1] The report calls the plants Hint keneviri, meaning Indian hemp. [@1]
