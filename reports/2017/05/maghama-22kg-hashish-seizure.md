Officers of the Maghama commissariat, the local police station serving the town
of Maghama in Gorgol Region, arrested five Mauritanian nationals linked to the
hashish case. [@1] The five suspects remained under investigation while police
prepared to transfer them to the public prosecutor in Kaédi for the next stage
of the case. [@2] Police said the market garden where the concealed drug was
found was the property of one of the alleged traffickers behind the seized
consignment. [@1]

In the same enforcement action, the Maghama commissariat in Gorgol Region seized
a total of 22 kilograms of hashish. [@2] The seized hashish was explicitly
identified as cannabis resin, clarifying that the local term haschich refers to
resin in this case. [@1] Officers found the full consignment hidden inside a
market garden, described in French as a jardin maraicher, situated on the bank
of the river. [@2]

Maghama is the town where the seizing police station is based, located within
Gorgol Region where the enforcement action took place. [@1] Kaédi is the town
whose prosecutor's office, called the parquet in French, was designated to
receive the suspects once the police investigation allowed referral. [@2]
