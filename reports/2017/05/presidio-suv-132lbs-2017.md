Officers of U.S. Customs and Border Protection at the Presidio port of entry seized
132 pounds (about 60 kg) of marijuana from a sport utility vehicle on the morning of
Friday 26 May 2017. [@1] The drugs were concealed in nine different locations
throughout the vehicle. [@1]

The seizure was made just after 8 a.m. when a woman driving a 2004 Nissan X-Terra
applied for entry at the primary inspection station. [@1] Officers sent the vehicle to
secondary inspection after noting issues with her travel itinerary, saw anomalies
throughout the vehicle on x-ray, and had a narcotics-detector dog alert to the
narcotics. [@1] Officers extracted 123 bundles, each holding a green leafy substance
that field-tested positive for marijuana. [@1]

Officers took custody of the driver, a 44-year-old United States citizen, and turned
her over to Homeland Security Investigations agents. [@1]
