Odisha police seized about 10 quintals of ganja from Badaguda forest under
Mathili police limits in Malkangiri district on the morning of 19 May 2017.
[@1] Officers arrested 28 people in connection with the seizure. [@1]

The arrested were carrying 56 sacks of ganja on their shoulders from the
cutoff areas of Chitrakonda toward Chhattisgarh in a neighbouring state.
[@1] A police team led by the Malkangiri sub-divisional police officer acted
on a tip-off to raid the forest and seize the consignment. [@1] Police said
those held were being interrogated to establish the involvement of an
inter-state ganja racket. [@1]
