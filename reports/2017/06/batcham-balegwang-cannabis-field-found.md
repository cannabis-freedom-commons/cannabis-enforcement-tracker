Gendarmes of the Mbouda company under Commander Patrice Asseko discovered a
cannabis plantation at Balegwang in Batcham arrondissement in Cameroon's West
region. [@1] The discovery followed tips from local residents about suspicious
movements in the area. [@1] The owners had not been found, and the commander
said an investigation would be opened to arrest them. [@1] The field lay in a
difficult-to-reach mountainous area about 30 km away, which the gendarmes
reached after walking nearly 25 km on foot. [@2]

The plantation covered one hectare. [@1] The commander said the most urgent step
was destroying the plantation during a return visit planned within days. [@1]
Pending destruction, gendarmes barred access to the plantation and set up
surveillance to help catch any suspect. [@1]

The owner had not been arrested at the time of the reports. [@2] In Cameroon,
cultivating, selling and consuming cannabis is prohibited and punishable by law.
[@2]
