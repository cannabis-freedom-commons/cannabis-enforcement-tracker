The District Court of Guam sentenced Joseph Wendell Monton Develles, 23, to 21
months in federal prison on 13 June 2017 for marijuana and firearms offences.
[@1] He had pleaded guilty in November to possession of a Schedule I controlled
substance with intent to distribute and to possession of a firearm while on
probation. [@1]

Superior Court of Guam probation officers had found two 5-gallon buckets holding
3.4 pounds (about 1.5 kg) of marijuana, a Glock pistol, a rifle, a Glock
magazine with ammunition and $26,300 during a random search of his home. [@1] At
the time he was still on probation from a September 2015
driving-under-the-influence case. [@1]

Chief Judge Frances Tydingco-Gatewood read the sentence while Develles stood
silently and declined to address the court. [@1] His lawyer, David Lujan,
challenged the probation report's label of him as an addict, and the point was
later withdrawn. [@1] Tydingco-Gatewood dismissed the argument that he was a
happy user, saying the offences were serious and that he had tested positive for
marijuana and alcohol when arrested for the DUI. [@1] Prosecutors put his base
offence level at 14 under a federal law that labels unlawful drug users
prohibited persons. [@1] In his amended plea agreement he admitted smoking
marijuana since he was 16 and called himself a chronic smoker. [@1]

Tydingco-Gatewood also ordered two years of supervised release and vocational
training, and allowed him to surrender before serving his sentence. [@1] He
asked to be sent to a federal facility in California. [@1]
