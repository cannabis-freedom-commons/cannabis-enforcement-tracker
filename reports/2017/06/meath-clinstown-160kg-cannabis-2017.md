As part of a planned operation by the Meath Divisional Drugs Unit, gardaí searched a house at Clinstown, Stamullen, County Meath at about 2.45pm on Thursday 29 June 2017, assisted by the Ashbourne Detective Unit and the Garda Dog Unit. [@1,2] The search of the house, its lands and the vehicles present found 160 kilos of suspected cannabis herb, approximately 30 kilos of suspected cocaine and 2.5 kilos of suspected cannabis resin. [@1,2] The street value, subject to analysis, was estimated at €3.2 million for the cannabis herb, €50,000 for the cannabis resin and €2.1 million for the cocaine. [@1,2]

A man in his 30s was arrested at the scene and detained at Kells Garda Station under section 2 of the Criminal Justice (Drug Trafficking) Act 1996. [@1,2] No charges or later court outcome were reported in these accounts.
