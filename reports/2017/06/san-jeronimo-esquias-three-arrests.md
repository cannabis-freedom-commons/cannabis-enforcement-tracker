A combined operation by the Public Ministry, the DPI investigative police, the
National Police, the anti-extortion force and police intelligence detained three
people in the municipalities of San Jerónimo and Esquías in Comayagua. [@1]
Those detained included 52-year-old Florencia Machado López, known as “lencha”.
[@1] Authorities described Machado López as one of the main drug distributors in
the Guasistagua community. [@1] In the village of Calpules in Esquías officers
detained 32-year-old Orvin Juan Cáceres Euceda on suspected illegal storage of
commercial firearms. [@1] In the same raids officers executed an arrest warrant
against José Santos Umanzor Rosa on suspicion of receiving a stolen vehicle and
cover-up. [@1]

The detentions were carried out through six simultaneous raids aimed at
street-level drug dealing and illegal possession of weapons. [@1] Officers said
they had kept the raided locations under surveillance to locate the suspects.
[@1] From Machado López officers seized 45 small bags and about two pounds
(about 0.91 kg) of suspected marijuana. [@1] Officers also seized several litres
of allegedly adulterated liquor from her. [@1] Inside Cáceres Euceda’s home
officers found a 12-gauge shotgun and a .357 revolver with several unfired
rounds. [@1]

The Public Ministry said the actions were part of coordinated work with security
forces against common and organized crime in areas controlled by street-dealing
structures. [@1]
