Guyana Police Force ranks stopped and searched a hire car on the Aurora Public
Road on the Essequibo Coast on the morning of 12 June 2017, and arrested the
five people inside. [@2] Acting on information, officers stopped the car about
08:00hrs and found a carton box holding 25 taped parcels of cannabis. [@2] The
driver and a woman passenger were among the five taken into custody. [@2]

Police weighed the parcels at 12.3 kilograms, the equivalent of the just over 25
pounds reported by News Source Guyana. [@2] Officers took the suspects and the
drug to the Aurora Police Station, where the drug amounted to 12.3kg. [@3] News
Source Guyana described the seizure as just over 25 pounds of marijuana found in
the car on the Aurora Public Road. [@1]

Police had not released the names of any of the arrested people. [@1] The five
were expected to be charged shortly. [@3] News Source Guyana said charges were
expected to be filed before the end of that week. [@1]
