## The indictment

On 28 June 2017 Colorado Attorney General Cynthia Coffman announced the indictment of 62 people and 12 businesses in Operation Toker Poker, the largest illegal-marijuana trafficking bust since Colorado legalised the drug in 2014. [@1,2] A Denver grand jury had handed up the indictment on 9 June against a ring that grew marijuana in Colorado and sold it out of state for millions of dollars over several years. [@1] Forty-three of the 62 people charged had been arrested, with 19 still at large, and investigators seized 2,600 illegally cultivated plants and 4,000 more pounds of marijuana. [@1]

## The ring

Many of the defendants knew each other from high school and played poker together, which gave the operation its name. [@1] Former Denver Broncos players Erik Pears and Joel Dreessen were swindled after investing, believing the grow was legal. [@1] The ring operated across the Front Range as well as in Kansas, Texas, Nebraska, Ohio and Oklahoma. [@1,2] "We believe these seizures only scratch the surface of the illegal drugs produced by this organization over time," Coffman said. [@1]
