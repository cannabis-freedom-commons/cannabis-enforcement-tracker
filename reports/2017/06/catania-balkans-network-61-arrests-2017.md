Catania prosecutors coordinated a massive Italian police operation that crippled a marijuana trafficking network moving narcotics from Albania to Italy, netting 61 arrests including 37 Albanian citizens. [@1] The operation, codenamed "The Balkans", followed investigations launched in 2012 in which several police forces across Italy worked together to identify the suspects. [@1]

## Seizure

The most significant action was carried out by Ragusa police and Como's Guardia di Finanza, who seized a tonne of marijuana and firearms used by the criminal group. [@1] Investigators said their initially separate inquiries had been joined into a single file that identified the Albanian suppliers. [@1]
