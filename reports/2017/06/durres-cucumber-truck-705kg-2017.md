Albanian border police seized a large haul of marijuana hidden in a vegetable
truck at the Port of Durrës as it was waiting to board a ferry to Italy on
7 June 2017. [@1] Nearly 705 kilograms of cannabis sativa were found hidden
inside a cucumber shipment, police said. [@1]

The driver of the vehicle was arrested. [@1] Kiril Filev, 32, and the truck he
was driving were both from neighbouring Macedonia. [@1] Police said three more
people were wanted in connection with the shipment but did not release the
suspects' names. [@1]
