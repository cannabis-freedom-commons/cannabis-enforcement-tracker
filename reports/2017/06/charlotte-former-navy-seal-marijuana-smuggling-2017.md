A former US Navy SEAL, James Dennis "JD" Smith Jr., was arrested on Saturday 17
June 2017 in Charlotte, North Carolina, on federal charges of smuggling
marijuana. [@1] Carl Rye and Bryon Rye, brothers from Columbia, South Carolina,
were arrested the same day when agents raided Bryon Rye's home on Rosewood Drive
in Columbia. [@2]

All three were charged with conspiracy to distribute and possession with intent
to distribute at least 700 kilograms (over 1,540 pounds) of marijuana, according
to a criminal complaint filed the previous day. [@2] The complaint says the
three were accused of possessing and planning to distribute at least 700
kilograms (over 1,540 pounds) of marijuana. [@1]

Smith, who served 16 years as a Navy SEAL, flew marijuana into Columbia on a
twin-engine aircraft that landed several times at Jim Hamilton-L.B. Owens
Airport, and the Ryes sold it across South Carolina, according to a DEA
affidavit. [@1] Smith told agents at a 2014 document check at Manassas Airport
in Virginia that he had bought the plane, registered as The Praetorian, for
medical humanitarian missions in Central America. [@1] A search of a storage
unit linked to the Ryes found deep freezers holding vacuum-sealed bags of
marijuana, about 28 pounds of it, some labelled "Medical Cannabis", according to
the complaint. [@2]

The DEA had been investigating the three men since August 2014, and a federal
court in South Carolina set a hearing for the following Thursday. [@2] All three
were arraigned in federal court on Monday and held without bond, and each charge
carried 5 to 40 years in prison; they were charged, not sentenced. [@3]
