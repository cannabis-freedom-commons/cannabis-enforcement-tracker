Humboldt County sheriff's deputies and drug-task-force agents served three search warrants on 28 June 2017 at Visser Court in McKinleyville and at two parcels in the Mosquito Creek drainage in eastern Humboldt County. [@1,2] At a home on Visser Court they arrested Brett Visser, 58, and Anne Clarke, 32, on suspicion of illegal cultivation of marijuana, possession of marijuana for sale and maintaining a structure for distribution. [@1,2] Officers found 367 pounds of processed marijuana in vacuum-sealed one-pound bags, six firearms, 255 Xanax pills and $80,000 in cash at the residence. [@1,2] A nearby parcel under Visser's control held two unpermitted indoor grow rooms with 700 growing plants. [@1,2] At the two Mosquito Creek parcels, owned by Visser, teams found 35 greenhouses and six indoor grows, eradicated 21,389 plants and seized another 391 pounds of processed marijuana. [@1,2] Two men found at the Mosquito Creek site were detained, questioned and released. [@1] State wildlife officers recorded stream-bed alterations, possible water pollution from stored diesel and signs of poaching, while county investigators cited hazardous-material, timber-harvest and grading violations. [@1] All criminal violations were forwarded to the district attorney for review. [@1]
