Hat Yai police arrested a man from Amnat Charoen on Monday morning 12 June 2017
for allegedly trying to smuggle 306 kilograms of marijuana under a pile of
durians and corn to Songkhla province. [@1, 2] Police chief Colonel Kittichai
Sangkhathavorn said the pickup truck was stopped at a road checkpoint on the
Asia Highway in Ban Nakhuan village in Tambon Khuanlung in Hat Yai. [@1, 2]

The driver, Anant Srising, 30, was arrested. [@1, 2] Police found the drug
under a canvas with the durians and corn piled on top. [@1, 2] Anant told
police he was paid 80,000 baht to deliver the drug to a recipient located five
kilometres from the spot where he was stopped. [@1]
