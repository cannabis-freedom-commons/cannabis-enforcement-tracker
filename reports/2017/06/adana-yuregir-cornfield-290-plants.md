No one had been detained over the planting, and police teams were continuing
work to identify the person or persons who had planted the cannabis crop. [@1]
The inspections in Ali Hocalı in the central Yüreğir district of Adana were
intensified by anti-narcotics branch teams acting on the orders of provincial
police chief Osman Ak. [@1]

Counter-terrorism, special-operations and public-order teams also took part in
the inspections in Ali Hocalı Mahallesi, where officers searched a number of
vehicles. [@1] Officers also carried out General Information Screening checks,
known by the Turkish initials GBT, on suspicious people encountered during the
inspections. [@1]

A police helicopter inspection of the area determined that cannabis had been
planted in the middle of a cornfield, rather than in a separate plot. [@1]
Motorized teams dispatched to the field seized the 290 cannabis plants growing
there, described in the report as 290 roots of Hint keneviri. [@1]
