Guyana Police Force ranks arrested six people in an intelligence-led operation
in New Amsterdam, Berbice, on the morning of Saturday 8 July 2017 and seized 7.5
kilogrammes of cannabis from them. [@1] Those held included a husband, his wife,
their daughter and three other women, whom police said they caught packaging the
marijuana. [@1] In a second operation that afternoon in 'C' Division, police
arrested a 30-year-old unemployed man from First Street, Paradise, East Coast
Demerara, at his home, where they found seven kilograms of cannabis. [@1] Police
said all seven were being processed for court and that the New Amsterdam
suspects were to appear that Monday. [@1]

The six people arrested in New Amsterdam appeared in the New Amsterdam
Magistrates' Court on Monday 10 July 2017, jointly charged with possession of
narcotics for the purpose of trafficking. [@2] The charge alleged that on
Saturday 8 July 2017, at Smythfield, New Amsterdam, the six had seven and a half
kilograms of marijuana for the purpose of trafficking. [@2] Five of the six
pleaded not guilty when Magistrate Alex Moore read the charge; the sixth pleaded
guilty. [@2] The prosecutor told the court that all five women were found in the
upper flat of the house cutting and parcelling the marijuana, and that
photographs implicated them. [@2] The court granted bail of 250,000 Guyana
dollars each to three of the women and 150,000 each to the other two, and
adjourned the case to 11 August 2017. [@2]
