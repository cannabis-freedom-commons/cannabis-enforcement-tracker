Gardaí from Listowel carried out a planned search of a house in Moyvane on the afternoon of Monday 3 July 2017 and found a sophisticated indoor grow house. [@1,2] Officers seized cannabis plants at various stages of maturity together with dried cannabis, with a combined estimated street value of up to €350,000 pending analysis. [@1,2]

A man in his 60s was arrested at the scene and detained at Listowel Garda Station under section 4 of the Criminal Justice Act 1984. [@1,2] He was later released pending a file to the Director of Public Prosecutions, and the scene was preserved for technical examination. [@1,2]

## Grow house

The Moyvane find was one of several indoor grows uncovered in Kerry that year, where plants grown under lights to maturity alongside dried product point to an established production site rather than a first crop. [@1,2] No charges or later court outcome for the arrested man were reported.
