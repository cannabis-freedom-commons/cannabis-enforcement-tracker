Sakarya provincial gendarmerie teams detained four suspects aged 22 to 28,
identified only as E.E., Y.D., T.C. and T.T., after searching their vehicle and
surrounding land in the Paşalar neighbourhood of Pamukova district. [@1] Eleven
separate operations over the previous week targeted unlawful cannabis
cultivation and drug use across the Akyazı, Hendek, Geyve, Karasu and Pamukova
districts. [@1] Across all the reported Sakarya operations, 13 people were
detained. [@1]

The Paşalar search seized 300 grams of kubar esrar alongside 2 kg of bonzai, a
synthetic cannabinoid that is excluded from the cannabis total. [@1] The
week-long operations seized 530 grams of kubar esrar and 782 cannabis roots,
along with three unlicensed hunting rifles and one synthetic pill. [@1] The
report gives the combined cannabis total as 830 grams of esrar. [@1] The vehicle
found to have been used in the Paşalar incident was banned from traffic and
given a 3,526-lira penalty. [@1]

The operations were carried out by Sakarya İl Jandarma Komutanlığı, the
provincial gendarmerie command, following intelligence work, with the Paşalar
operation described as targeting synthetic cannabinoid trade. [@1] The report
uses kubar esrar for the seized cannabis and kök kenevir for the cannabis
plants. [@1]
