Rapper T.O.P. of boy band Big Bang, whose real name is Choi Seung-hyun, was
sentenced to 10 months in prison, suspended for two years, for smoking
marijuana. [@2] The Seoul Central District Court sentenced him on 20 July 2017.
[@4] Choi was 29 at the time. [@2] The court fined him 12,000 won, about $10.65.
[@1] The fine was calculated at 3,000 won for each of the four doses. [@1] The
sentence matched what prosecutors had demanded at his first hearing on 29 June.
[@1] Choi will serve no prison time unless he commits an offence during the
two-year probation. [@1]

Choi was charged with smoking marijuana four times at his home in Yongsan
District in Seoul between 9 and 14 October 2016. [@4] Two sessions were in blunt
form and two with liquid marijuana. [@1] He smoked with a 21-year-old trainee
singer. [@2] He was indicted without detention in June. [@2] A police hair
follicle test in April proved positive. [@1] He first denied smoking marijuana,
later admitted two sessions, then pleaded guilty to all charges at the first
hearing. [@1]

The court said Choi had admitted the four sessions, shown full remorse, promised
not to reoffend and was a first offender. [@1] It also said he had disappointed
fans. [@1] The judge said drug offences damage body and mind and need heavy
punishment. [@3] Choi was serving as a conscripted policeman when he was
indicted and was relieved of duty on 9 June. [@1] The court said he could
continue his service because the term was under 18 months, though his return
date was undecided. [@1] He was eligible for a police review of whether he was
fit to serve again. [@3] If he failed the review, he would finish his military
duty as a public service worker. [@3] Leaving court, Choi said he accepted the
ruling and would take it as a life lesson. [@3]
