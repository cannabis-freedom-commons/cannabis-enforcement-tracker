The case remained under investigation by local police and the DEA, and no
arrests had been made. [@1] Sheriff David W. Doak told NBC News that police were
called to a dealership about a week ago when a suspicious package was found
concealed in the trunk wheel of a new Ford Fusion. [@1] Portage County police
told NBC News that authorities had seized 400 pounds of marijuana worth $1
million hidden in cars at Ohio dealerships. [@1]

More marijuana blocks were recovered from other vehicles at that dealership, and
a neighboring county reported five other Ford Fusions with concealed narcotics.
[@1] In total, 400 pounds of marijuana were recovered from 15 vehicles at the
Ohio dealerships. [@1] The marijuana was tightly packed and hidden in the spare
tire wheel wells of the Ford Fusions. [@1]

The cars were manufactured in Mexico and crossed the Mexican border into Arizona
on their way to Trumbull County, where they were dropped off at a rail yard.
[@1] Ford has a plant in Sonora, Mexico. [@1] Silverio Balzano of the DEA's
Youngstown office said those working at the Mexican plant might not have even
been involved. [@1] Balzano said investigators did not know at what point the
marijuana was put into the vehicles. [@1] Ford denied that the incident happened
at the Mexico plant and said it was working with the FBI and customs on an
extensive investigation. [@1]
