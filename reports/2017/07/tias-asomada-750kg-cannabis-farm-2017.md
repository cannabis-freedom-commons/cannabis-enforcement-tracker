Following an anonymous tip, the Policia Nacional found a marijuana farm hidden in the Barranco de la Asomada ravine, complete with four greenhouses, powerful halogen lights and mechanised harvesting equipment. [@1] Officers dismantled the farm and seized 750 kilograms of marijuana. [@1]

Five people were detained, including a British national presumed to be the head of the operation. [@1]
