Aydın provincial gendarmerie teams raided a house belonging to H.Y. in Eğrikavak
Mahallesi in Efeler district. [@1] Officers detained H.Y. during the Efeler raid
and released him after taking his statement. [@1] Acting on a tip, police teams
in Çine district searched a car at a petrol station on Adnan Menderes Boulevard.
[@1] Three people were detained in connection with the Çine car search and their
procedures were still continuing. [@1]

In the garden of the Efeler house, officers found 1,500 cannabis plants
described as Hint keneviri standing planted. [@1] In the Çine car, officers
found 8.86 grams of cannabis described as esrar. [@1] Officers also seized an
apparatus used for consuming drugs from the same car. [@2]

Across the Efeler and Çine operations, officers reported a total of 1,500
cannabis plants, 8.86 grams of cannabis and a drug-use apparatus seized, with
four people detained in total. [@1] The Efeler operation was carried out by
provincial gendarmerie teams, while the Çine operation was carried out by police
teams. [@2]
