Albanian police seized about 15 tonnes of cannabis in the village of Llakatund in the southwestern Vlora region on the night of 13 July 2017. [@2] More than 150 officers targeted three buildings where packed cannabis was stored, including cannabis products such as chocolates, which police described as processing as well as storage centres. [@1]

Nine people were arrested, including two local police officers, Avdyl Sinanaj, 56, and Lirim Lamaj, 49, who were charged with abuse of duty and cultivation of narcotic plants. [@1] Officers also found a field of 260 cannabis plants said to belong to a regional police inspector. [@2] Local media reported that intelligence agents had tipped off the police about the drugs. [@1]

In the same week police stopped a further large cannabis load near Kavaja on the coast road toward Italy, seizing a speedboat believed to have been waiting for the drugs. [@1] The two opened reports give different weights for that Kavaja seizure, so it is kept out of this report's figures. [@1,2]
