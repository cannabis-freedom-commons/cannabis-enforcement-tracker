Moroccan police dismantled an international drug smuggling network in the northern city of Nador and seized over 12 tonnes of cannabis. [@1] Six dealers aged between 20 and 57 were captured. [@1] Officers raided three houses in the rural Ammejjaou area that the dealers used as storage sites. [@1]

The raids also netted 19 vehicles, including three vans in which drugs and nearly 300,000 euros were found. [@1] Police seized an inflatable boat used for international trafficking, electronic marine navigation equipment, high-speed boat engines and a hunting rifle with 56 bullets. [@1] A second account puts the haul at 12.5 tons of cannabis and confirms the six arrests, the 19 confiscated vehicles and the boat and marine gear. [@2]
