Bolivian police and prosecutors arrested three Paraguayan citizens at Naranjos,
on the Roboré–Puerto Suárez road in Santa Cruz department, as they guarded black
sacks holding packages of marijuana. [@2] Ximena Morales, national director of
Bolivia's control-substances prosecutor's office, said the three were caught in
flagrante with the drug and that they would be charged with trafficking
controlled substances. [@2] Morales said officers found them guarding several
black sacks of brick-shaped packages that tested positive for marijuana. [@4]

Godoy was president of a Colorado Party branch in the city of Liberación, in San
Pedro department, for the party's Honor Colorado faction of then-president
Horacio Cartes. [@1] He belonged to Honor Colorado, the faction of President
Horacio Cartes. [@1] ABC Color reported that Godoy was close to the Vázquez
family of San Pedro, including deputy Perla Acosta de Vázquez. [@3]

The load came in 470 brick-shaped packages hidden in black bags and weighed five
tonnes, 111 kilos and 300 grams. [@2] Opinión Bolivia reported that the seized
drug was valued at 12.3 million dollars. [@2] The marijuana had been brought
into Bolivia from Paraguay. [@1] Bolivia's public prosecutor's office ordered
the seizure and said it would file a formal charge against the three, while a
judge was to set a precautionary-measures hearing to decide their legal
situation. [@4] The whole load was taken to Santa Cruz de la Sierra for
presentation and later incineration, according to Morales, the national director
of control substances. [@1]
