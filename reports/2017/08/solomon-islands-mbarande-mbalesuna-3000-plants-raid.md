On 12 August 2017, a joint operation by Solomon Islands police from Honiara City
and Guadalcanal Province raided cannabis gardens in East Guadalcanal. [@1]
Officers uprooted and confiscated about 3,000 marijuana plants during the
operation. [@1] Police raided two gardens in two different locations. [@1] One
garden was at Mbarande and the other was at Mbalesuna, East Guadalcanal. [@1]

Police apprehended one alleged suspect for farming the plants. [@1] The suspect
was held in police custody pending further investigation. [@1]

Chiefs and community elders welcomed the operation and appealed for more raids
to uproot marijuana around the Guadalcanal plains. [@1] Guadalcanal Provincial
Police Commander Nixon Manetoga credited cooperation between the community and
police for the successful operation. [@1] Manetoga appealed to local communities
to contact police and report any similar cultivation so police could remove it.
