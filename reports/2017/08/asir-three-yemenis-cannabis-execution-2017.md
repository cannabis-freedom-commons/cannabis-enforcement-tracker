Saudi Arabia executed Yemeni nationals Yahya Ali Moussa A'ti, Ali Ibrahim Jibran Ahmad and Shu'ei Issa Yousef Hajaji in the Asir region on 15 August 2017. [@1,2] The Interior Ministry said the three had been convicted of smuggling a large quantity of cannabis and a quantity of narcotic qat into the kingdom. [@1]

The ministry said the General Court convicted the men, the Appeal and Supreme Courts endorsed the sentence, and a royal order was issued to carry it out. [@1] An executions log for 2017 records all three by name as beheaded in public in Saudi Arabia on 15 August for a drugs offence. [@2]
