Luxembourg customs intercepted a delivery of 77.4 kg of marijuana at the end of
August 2017 in a controlled delivery. [@1] The customs service said the drug had
arrived from South Africa by air. [@1] Luxembourg's Customs and Excise
Administration said its officers intercepted 77.4 kg of marijuana that had
travelled from South Africa by air at the end of August 2017. [@2]

According to the authorities' intelligence, the goods were to be taken to an
address in Luxembourg. [@1] The truck carrying the consignment changed route and
appeared to be heading for Britain instead. [@1] The investigation after the
interception found the drug was supposed to continue to a new destination of
Britain. [@2]

The consignment was intercepted before it could reach its destination. [@1]
Customs put the value of the seized drugs at about 650,000 euros. [@1] The load
was seized on the order of Luxembourg's public prosecutor and an investigation
was opened. [@2]
