Two men from Virgin Gorda and two from Tortola were arrested and charged over
different marijuana-related offences allegedly committed in August 2017. [@1]
Police named them as Kael Mandoza Palancares, 29-year-old Adrian Blackman,
24-year-old Kadeem O’Neal and 30-year-old Carey Dore. [@1] Blackman, a resident
of Spooner’s Estate on Tortola, also faced non-marijuana charges. [@1] He was
arrested and charged on 24 August with unlawful possession of marijuana with
intent to supply, driving without a driver’s license and driving a motor vehicle
with tinted windows. [@1]

Blackman was granted $10,000 police bail with one surety to appear in the
Magistrate’s Court on 6 October. [@1] Palancares, a resident of Joe’s Hill on
Tortola, was arrested and charged on 17 August. [@1] He was due to appear in the
Magistrate’s Court on 29 September to answer charges of unlawful possession of
marijuana, failing to comply with the traffic light and driving without driver’s
license. [@1] He was granted $7,500 bail signed by his surety for his appearance
at the Magistrate’s Court. [@1] O’Neal, a resident of Valley in Virgin Gorda,
was charged with unlawful possession of a controlled drug. [@1] He was arrested
and charged on 20 August and granted $10,000 police bail with signed surety to
appear before the Magistrate’s Court on 13 October. [@1]

Dore, who resides at the Valley on Virgin Gorda, was granted $10,000 bail with
one signed surety to appear before the Magistrate’s Court on 3 October. [@1] He
was charged with unlawful possession of a controlled drug on 20 August. [@1] The
Royal Virgin Islands Police Force published the cases in arrest blotters that
gave no details of the allegations. [@1]
