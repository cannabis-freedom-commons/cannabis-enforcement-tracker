Acting on a tip on 17 August 2017, the Mississippi Bureau of Narcotics asked the Mississippi National Guard counterdrug team to fly aerial reconnaissance near Polk Oatis Road in Jefferson Davis County. [@1] The flights found six fields covering four to six acres, fitted out with irrigation, living-quarter tents and plant processing areas. [@1] Bureau director John Dowdy called it the biggest marijuana grow operation the state had seen in probably 35 to 40 years. [@1,2]

Agents collected more than 15,000 plants on Thursday and found another 5,000 on Friday, taking the haul past 20,000 plants. [@2] The later National Guard account put the seizure at more than 20,000 plants with a street value exceeding 23 million dollars. [@1] Dowdy said a mature plant could yield about a pound of processed marijuana selling for 1,000 to 1,500 dollars on the street. [@2]

State and local agencies assisting the bureau included its helicopter pilots and crew, the Highway Patrol, the Bureau of Investigation, wildlife and forestry officers, the Transportation Department, the Jefferson Davis County sheriff's department and the Prentiss police. [@2] Neither account reported any arrests. [@1,2]
