El Monte police raided a home at 14720 Horticultural Drive in Hacienda Heights
at about 6 p.m. on Tuesday 1 August 2017 with a search warrant and found a
marijuana oil extraction lab inside. [@1] Five men in the home were arrested on
suspicion of cultivation of a controlled substance. [@1] The five were named as
David Byung Kim, 38, Michael Myungkhun Choi, 31, Andrew Euyyong Kim, 26, Aaron
Michael Corpuz, 28, and Francis Azle Ocampo, 28. [@1] All five were released
from custody on Wednesday after posting $75,000 bail each, pending their initial
court appearances. [@1]

Officers seized marijuana and concentrated marijuana known as honey oil or wax,
which El Monte police valued at $1.8 million, including tins labeled White
Walker OG with Game of Thrones characters. [@1] The raided home was a
4,300-square-foot house valued at more than $1 million. [@1] Police said the
house contained numerous types of hazardous chemicals and equipment used in the
production of concentrated cannabis. [@1] Police said most of the contraband was
packaged for the purposes of distribution. [@1] Because of the dangerous
chemicals, authorities evacuated the property while a regional narcotics lab
team and the county hazardous material team dismantled the lab and stored the
evidence. [@1]

The investigation began Monday with the theft of a Lexus LS 460 from Long Lexus
at 3530 Peck Road in El Monte. [@1] A tracking device in the stolen car was
pinging in the area of the 14700 block of Horticultural Drive in Hacienda
Heights, leading detectives to identify 14720 Horticultural Drive as the
suspected location. [@1] A man at the home spoke to detectives through a
security gate and denied them permission to search for the stolen Lexus, after
which police surrounded the home and obtained a search warrant. [@1] The stolen
Lexus was not found at the home and was ultimately recovered unoccupied within
the jurisdiction of the sheriff's Industry Station. [@1]
