The National Drug Law Enforcement Agency's Ekiti State command destroyed 246 tonnes of cannabis sativa on farms at Ode-Ekiti in Gbonyin Local Government Area. [@1] The state commander, Alhaji Bage Bamaiyi, announced the destruction in an interview in Ado-Ekiti on Tuesday 1 August 2017. [@1]

Officers acting on information had placed seventeen farms under surveillance. [@1] Three mapped farms measuring 42.66 hectares and four unmapped farms measuring 8.05 hectares were destroyed, yielding 205 tonnes of mapped cannabis and 41 tonnes of unmapped cannabis. [@1] The growers were not found in their hideouts. [@1]

Bamaiyi said the remaining ten farms could not be destroyed for lack of logistics, but would be visited once logistics were available. [@1] He appealed to all levels of government to fund, staff and equip the agency, and assured the public of the command's determination to eliminate cannabis planting and peddling in the state. [@1]
