Yong Keh Kee, a 28-year-old from Kuala Lumpur in Malaysia, was stopped by Border Force officers at Edinburgh Airport as he travelled from Madrid. [@1,2] Officers examined two of his suitcases and found ten vacuum-packed packages of cannabis leaves wrapped in towels. [@1,2]

The Home Office put the street value of the roughly ten kilos at £100,000. [@1,2] After the seizure, Border Force passed the investigation to the National Crime Agency. [@1]

Yong was sentenced to 18 months at Edinburgh Sheriff Court on Wednesday 23 August 2017 for attempting to smuggle the cannabis. [@1,2] Border Force Scotland head Murdo MacMillan said officers' vigilance had kept a significant quantity of drugs off the streets, and the NCA said it was determined to protect the public from drug trafficking. [@1]
