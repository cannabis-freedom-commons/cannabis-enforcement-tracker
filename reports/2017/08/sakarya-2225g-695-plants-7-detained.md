Sakarya İl Jandarma Komutanlığı, the Sakarya provincial gendarmerie command,
carried out seven separate operations in the Geyve, Karasu, Kocaali and Hendek
districts targeting unlawful cannabis cultivation and drug use. [@1] Gendarmerie
teams acted after intelligence work conducted across those districts during the
preceding week. [@2] Seven suspects were detained in connection with the
operations. [@1] A judicial investigation was opened against the seven suspects
in connection with the same operations. [@2]

The gendarmerie put the seized harvested cannabis, described as kubar esrar, at
2 kilograms 225 grams. [@1] The 2 kilograms 225 grams was harvested cannabis,
described as kubar esrar, and the 695 items were cannabis plants, described as
kök kenevir bitkisi. [@2] Officers also seized 695 cannabis plants, described as
kök kenevir bitkisi. [@1] Officers also seized one precision scale, described as
a hassas terazi, alongside the cannabis. [@2]

The seven operations took place during the week before 10 August 2017. [@2] The
four districts lie within Sakarya province, where the provincial gendarmerie
conducted the operation series. [@1]
