Police in Adana detained the cornfield's owner, whose name was not released.
[@2] Officers found 1,090 cannabis plants growing in the middle of his standing
corn crop. [@1] The field is in Camuzcu Mahallesi, a mahalle, or neighbourhood,
of Seyhan district, one of Adana's central districts. [@1] The man told police
he had not known that the cannabis plants were there. [@1]

Narkotim, the anti-narcotics teams of the Adana provincial police, inspected
farmland across the city by helicopter as part of a police campaign against
cannabis cultivation. [@1] Officers who went into the field on foot lost their
way among corn about two metres tall, and the helicopter crew guided them to the
plants by radio. [@1] The teams then uprooted the 1,090 plants, known in Turkish
as Hint keneviri, and destroyed them all. [@2]

Karar reported that the cannabis appeared to have been sown into the corn to
hide it. [@2] The field owner was detained, and no other suspect was named in
the case. [@1]
