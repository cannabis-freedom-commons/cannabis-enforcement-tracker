Timothy Fadgen, 47, confronted three men about an illegal marijuana growing site on the edge of his rural property in the small community of Oakhurst, and a fight broke out. [@1] He was shot during the confrontation on Monday night, 7 August 2017, and died at the scene, Madera County sheriff's lieutenant Zack Zamudio said. [@1,2]

Fadgen knew the marijuana was being grown illegally on his property, Zamudio said. [@1] Madera County lies about 20 miles north of Fresno. [@1]

The three men fled in an older-model blue or green minivan with tinted windows, and police were still searching for them the next day with no descriptions of the suspects available. [@1,2] Investigators at the scene had not yet determined how many pot plants were growing on the land. [@1,2]
