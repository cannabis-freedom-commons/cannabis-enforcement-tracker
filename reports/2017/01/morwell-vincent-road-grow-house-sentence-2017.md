A Melbourne man has been sentenced to 21 months' jail after pleading guilty to
operating an elaborate hydroponic cannabis grow house at Morwell in Victoria's
east. [@1] Police raided a double-storey house at 119 Vincent Road, Morwell
owned by Ahn Tu Vu, 46 of Caroline Springs, Melbourne, on July 16, 2016 and
seized 96 cannabis plants weighing 17 kilograms. [@1] Vu purchased the property
after arriving in Australia from Vietnam. [@1] Vu pleaded guilty to two charges
of cultivating cannabis and stealing electricity. [@1]

The Latrobe Valley Magistrates Court heard the home was fitted with $50,000
worth of highly sophisticated equipment. [@1] White tarpaulins covered the
floors, walls and windows of four bedrooms upstairs. [@1] The court heard lights
with large white shades were hanging from the ceiling in rows. [@1] Hoses
connected to a bathroom distributed water and fertilizer to the cannabis plants
in big black pots. [@1] Fans, extension cords, power boards, four mobile
telephones and bottles of fertilizer were found downstairs. [@1] The prosecutor
told the court an electricity bypass system had been set up, allowing Vu to
steal thousands of dollars' worth of power over a three-month period. [@1]
Electricity retailer SP Ausnet put the value of stolen power at $14,000. [@1]

Vu's lawyer told the court Vu had been encouraged into the operation by others.
[@1] In documents tendered in court, detectives alleged Vu was involved in an
organised crime syndicate. [@1] His arrest was part of an investigation into a
syndicate of hydroponic cannabis grow houses in the Latrobe Valley. [@1] Vu is
expected to appeal against the sentence. [@1]
