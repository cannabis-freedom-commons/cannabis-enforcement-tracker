Revenue's Customs Service and the Garda Drugs and Organised Crime Bureau seized 1,873 kg of herbal cannabis at Dublin Port on Friday in a joint intelligence-led operation targeting organised crime groups. [@1,2] The consignment had entered the port in three crates, wider than 40-foot containers carried by trucks, with the drugs expertly packed inside and disguised as machinery parts. [@1]

The haul weighed almost two tonnes and carried an estimated street value of 37.5 million euros, making it one of the biggest consignments of drugs ever found in the Republic. [@1,2] Revenue and the Garda said that despite its size, the cannabis was destined for the domestic market. [@1] The seizure was put on display at the port on Saturday, and joint investigations were continuing at home and abroad. [@1,2,3]
