A 32-year-old Barbadian from Husbands Garden, Barbados, was charged with
breaches of the Dangerous Drugs Act at Norman Manley International Airport in
Kingston on Tuesday, 17 January 2017. [@1] At about 2:15 p.m. he checked in for
an outgoing flight to Barbados. [@1] During security checks his luggage was
searched and ganja weighing approximately 6.3 pounds (about 2.86 kg) was found.
[@1]

The man was to be interviewed in the presence of his attorney and was expected
to be charged with possession of ganja, dealing in ganja and taking preparatory
steps to export ganja. [@1] He was scheduled to appear before the St Andrew
Parish Court the following Wednesday. [@1]
