As part of an intelligence-led operation targeting organised crime groups, officers from the Revenue Customs Service, the Garda Drugs and Organised Crime Bureau and the Special Crime Task Force intercepted several people and vehicles in the Ashbourne area of County Meath at about 4pm on 31 January 2017, and searched a business premises there. [@1]

Officers seized about 160 kilos of cannabis herb with an estimated street value of 3.2 million euro. [@1] Four Irish men aged between 25 and 61 were arrested for drug trafficking offences and detained at Finglas and Kells Garda stations, and investigations were continuing in Ireland and abroad into the consignment destined for the Irish market. [@1]
