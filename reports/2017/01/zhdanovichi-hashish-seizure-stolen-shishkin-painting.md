Belarusian drug police detained suspected drug couriers who were bringing drugs
from Russia into Belarus, seizing drugs and a painting. [@2] The detention took
place on 16 January, when the drugs had arrived in Belarus, with support from
Almaz special forces. [@3] Officers searched a garage rented by the alleged
organiser, a 36-year-old resident of the agrotown Zhdanovichi. [@3]

In the garage officers seized over 300 grams of hashish, reported as the
cannabis part of the haul. [@2] The Current Time account rounds the same haul to
300 grams of hashish. [@1] Alongside the hashish, officers also seized cocaine
and ecstasy tablets, which are not counted as cannabis here. [@2]

In the couriers' car officers found a painting bearing the inscription I.
Shishkin 1897. [@2] The painting was preliminarily identified as Ivan Shishkin's
Forest (Les. Eli), stolen in 2013 from the Vyazniki history and art museum in
Vladimir region. [@2] Police said the painting had been brought to Belarus to be
shown to a potential European buyer, but no deal was reached, and it was to be
taken to Moscow for sale for 100,000 euros and cocaine. [@2] The Interior
Ministry said specialists estimated the painting could reach 2 million dollars
at auction. [@2]
