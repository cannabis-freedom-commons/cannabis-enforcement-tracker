Military police uprooted and burned a cannabis plantation of about 20,000 plants
at Sítio Seriema, in the rural zone of Santa Filomena in Pernambuco's Sertão, on
Wednesday 26 April 2017. [@1] Two people were detained in the operation, one of
them Gildejânio Coelho Melo, a councillor in the neighbouring municipality of
Ouricuri who owned the land. [@1]

The operation began on Tuesday 25 April, when police received information about
the plantation, and the crop was set alight on Wednesday morning, with material
kept aside for forensic examination. [@3] The blog Fredson Paiva, citing the
Pernambuco Military Police, reported that the crop was burned at around 5pm, by
members of GATI, the Military Police's mobile tactical support group, working
with agents of NIS-4 under Major Rutênio. [@4] Officers detained a 33-year-old
farm worker who lived on the site and admitted taking part in the cultivation.
[@2] The councillor arrived at the property around 11pm without knowing about
the police operation and was detained; the Military Police said he confirmed he
was the owner of the land. [@3]

Besides the plantation, police seized 6 kg of cannabis ready for consumption and
15.2 kg of cannabis seeds, and found electrical wiring used in the operation.
[@2] The councillor said another man had left with a load of the finished drug
in a pickup truck on 17 April but did not say where it was taken. [@1]

The two men and the seized material were taken to the Civil Police station in
Ouricuri; the councillor was questioned and released shortly afterwards. [@1]
Police were investigating the involvement of a third person, Folha de Pernambuco
reported. [@2]
