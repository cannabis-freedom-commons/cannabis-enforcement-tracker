Albanian police seized about twelve tonnes of cannabis in Përmet in southern
Albania on Saturday 8 April 2017, in what the report called the biggest
operation in months. [@1] Officers found 11,650 kg of dried cannabis packed in
plastic sacks in an abandoned depot in the town. [@1] Two depot administrators
were arrested at the scene and a warrant was issued for a third. [@1]

Police also arrested the headman of Pagri village, and prosecutors were
examining whether police officers were involved in the trafficking. [@1] The
report said seizures of tonnes of cannabis were happening almost weekly as
police cracked down on growing and trafficking across the country. [@1]
