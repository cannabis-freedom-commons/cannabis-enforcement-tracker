Drug Enforcement Unit officers arrested a Bahamian man and a Jamaican woman
after finding 390 pounds (about 177 kg) of suspected marijuana at a house on
Water Street in the Big Pond community of New Providence on 26 April 2017. [@1]
Assistant Commissioner of Police Samuel Butler, the senior officer responsible
for drug investigations, said the seizure happened around 6.45am after officers
received intelligence and executed a search warrant at the house. [@1] Police
said officers found sacks and a children's backpack holding the cannabis, and a
child was also present in the home. [@1] Police put the street value of the
marijuana at $390,000, a figure the Tribune rounded up to nearly $400,000 in its
headline. [@1] The Bahama Journal reported that the search began around 7am at
number 46 Water Street, where officers met a male and female occupant and found
several nylon bags of marijuana. [@2]

Butler said police were concerned about the level of drug trafficking within
local communities and preferred to make seizures at sea before drugs reach land.
[@1] Both arrested people were expected to appear before a magistrate as soon as
possible, the Bahama Journal reported. [@2]
