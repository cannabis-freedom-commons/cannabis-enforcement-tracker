Nador's judicial police seized about 15 tonnes of hashish on Monday and arrested
one person over alleged links to an international drug-trafficking network. [@2]
Morocco's Directorate General of National Security (DGSN), the national police,
said in a statement that the suspect, aged 46, was arrested on the basis of
information supplied by its own services. [@2] The 46-year-old man was taken
into police custody by the arresting officers. [@3] He was to be brought before
the courts once the inquiry led by the Nador judicial police was completed. [@3]

Field operations and searches at Douar Oulad Aissa and in the Taourirt Boucetta
area near Nador recovered 578 bags containing 14.973 tonnes of hashish. [@2] The
seized hashish was identified as cannabis resin, the processed form of cannabis.
[@1] The same operation also seized six cars bearing false registration plates
that were used for transporting and trafficking drugs. [@1]

The seizure occurred on Monday near Nador in northeastern Morocco and was
publicly reported on 4 April 2017. [@3] Investigations were continuing in order
to arrest all the other people suspected of links to the criminal network. [@2]
The official DGSN account of the operation was relayed through Morocco's MAP
press agency, which the outlets cite as the source for the announcement. [@1]
