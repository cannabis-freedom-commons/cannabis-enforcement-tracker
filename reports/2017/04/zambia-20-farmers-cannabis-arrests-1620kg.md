Zambia's Drug Enforcement Commission said it arrested 20 small-scale farmers in
other parts of the country for unlawful cultivation and trafficking in cannabis.
[@2] The commission named Richard Chanda, 36, of Chafoma village in Muchinga
Province, as held for unlawful cultivation of 600 kg of fresh cannabis plants.
[@2] It also named Juliet Kalale, 36, a small-scale farmer of Chisenga village
in Serenje District, as held for unlawful cultivation of fresh cannabis plants
weighing 422 kg. [@2] Uganda's Observer, summarising the Zambian accounts,
separately reported that the commission had arrested 20 small-scale farmers for
unlawful cultivation and trafficking of cannabis. [@1]

The Mongu Magistrates Court convicted Sitali Likando, 46, a small-scale farmer
of Njoko area in Mulobezi District, sentencing him to five years' imprisonment
with hard labour effective 10 April 2017. [@2] The commission said Likando had
been arrested on 9 April 2017 for unlawful cultivation of 527 kg of fresh
cannabis plants and trafficking in 71 kg of loose cannabis. [@2] The cannabis
arrests were announced in the same DEC briefing as the arrest of Ugandan
businessman Isa Mutebi in Lusaka for possession of $79,500 in counterfeit notes.
[@2] Uganda's Observer reported that DEC officers picked Mutebi up at a shopping
mall in Lusaka where he operated businesses. [@1]
