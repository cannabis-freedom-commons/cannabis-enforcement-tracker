The tribunal correctionnel, Pointe-à-Pitre's criminal court, sentenced
25-year-old Danick Bourguignon to eight months in prison without suspension
after he tried to throw cannabis resin and alcohol over Baie-Mahault prison's
wall. [@1] He appeared before the court on Monday, 30 May 2016, over the
attempted throw at Baie-Mahault prison. [@1] Bourguignon admitted what he had
done and did not try to excuse himself, but his admission did not convince the
public prosecutor. [@1] He had been in prison a month earlier, and the
prosecutor said he should have done something else with his life. [@1]

To explain his conduct, Bourguignon pointed to the difficult conditions of his
imprisonment. [@1] He told the court, “J'ai vécu des pressions en prison, je
devais de l'argent” (“I experienced pressure in prison; I owed money”). [@1] The
prosecutor requested eight months of firm imprisonment for Bourguignon. [@1] The
court fully followed that request and imposed the same eight-month firm term.
