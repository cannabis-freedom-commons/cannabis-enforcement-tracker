Teams from the Milas Gendarmerie Command stopped a passenger bus on the
Söke-Milas highway and searched it together with police officers. [@1] The drug
dog Alçı took part in the searches of the bus. [@1] Officers detained S.M., a
passenger on the bus, in connection with the incident. [@1]

During the searches, officers found 40 grams of cannabis concealed in a
cigarette pack. [@1] The bus search and seizure took place in Muğla, where
officers seized the 40 grams of cannabis. [@1]
