Police in Dushanbe's Sino district detained a 52-year-old Afghan man from Kabul,
temporarily living in the Tajik capital, on suspicion of involvement in illegal
drug trafficking. [@1] The detention took place on 29 May 2016. [@2] According
to the press centre of Tajikistan's Interior Ministry, the detainee and his
accomplices had agreed in advance to bring the hashish across the state border.
[@1] They hid the load in a purpose-built cache in the Chubek jamoat of Hamadoni
district, Khatlon region; a jamoat is a local administrative unit. [@3]

In a search of the cache, officers found and seized 40 kilograms 600 grams of
the narcotic substance hashish. [@3] Police were still searching for the
suspected accomplices of the Afghan trafficker. [@1] A criminal case was opened
over the incident. [@1] Detained on suspicion of drug pushing, the man faced
criminal proceedings with an investigation under way. [@2] The Tajik-language
account said officers continued search operations to find and detain the
suspect's accomplices, with the investigation continuing. [@3]

The Russian account says the smuggled quantity exceeded 40 kilograms of hashish
and reports the seizure only as over 40 kilograms. [@1] The English account
likewise reported more than 40 kilograms of hashish smuggled into Tajikistan and
hidden in Chubek jamoat. [@2]
