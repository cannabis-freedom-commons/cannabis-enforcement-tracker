Gendarmerie officers found 1.5 kg of cannabis hidden inside a pumpkin on a
passenger bus in Ergani, Diyarbakır. [@1] The Diyarbakır governor's office said
the bus was travelling from Diyarbakır to western provinces when it was
searched. [@2] Officers from the provincial gendarmerie's anti-smuggling and
organised crime branch and the Ergani Değirmendere station carried out the
search. [@1] A sniffer dog reacted to the pumpkin in the bus's luggage hold.
[@1] Inside, officers found the cannabis in a cavity whose cut top had been
glued back on to prepare it for transport. [@1]

The person who handed the parcel to the bus as a consignment was detained. [@1]
The governor's office said the cannabis was confiscated on the public
prosecutor's orders and that judicial proceedings had begun against the suspect.
[@2] It said anti-drug work would continue with 'increasing determination and
resolve'. [@1]

Tigris Haber described the drug as 1 kilo 500 gram kubar esrar, a resinous
cannabis product. [@3] It named the sniffer dog as Haklı. [@3]
