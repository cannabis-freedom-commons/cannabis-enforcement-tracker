Four people were arrested on suspicion of cannabis possession and cultivation
after police searched a residence in Warwick. [@1] Shortly after 7pm, Bermuda
Police Service executed a warrant at the address on Henry Vale Road, just off
Keith Hall Road. [@1] Police said a few minutes after 7pm on a Wednesday they
executed a warrant under the authority of PACE, the Police and Criminal Evidence
Act, at the residence. [@2]

After officers discovered a substance believed to be cannabis, they arrested
three men and a woman. [@1] Police said an investigation was under way. [@1]
