Italian authorities dismantled a major drug trafficking network that moved large quantities of marijuana from Albania into Italy, arresting eight people including three Albanians in the south-eastern region of Apulia. [@1] The operation, codenamed Illyria, targeted the suspects on drugs and arms smuggling charges. [@1]

## Seizures

Police seized over a tonne of marijuana along with a kilogram of cocaine and heroin, 4.5 kilograms of gelignite, an AK-47 assault rifle and 170 bullets. [@1] The report presents the operation as evidence that trafficking through Albania toward the southern tip of Italy continues despite Albanian police crackdowns on growers. [@1] It notes that cannabis cultivation remains a major law-enforcement problem in Albania even after the fall of Lazarat, a village once notorious as a haven of cannabis production. [@1]
