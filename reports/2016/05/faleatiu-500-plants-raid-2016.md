Police in Samoa raided a property at Faleatiu in mid-May 2016 and uncovered a total of 500 marijuana plants. [@1] Officers of the Tactical Operation Sector took part in the raid, and five people were arrested. [@1]

Two officers were injured by booby traps allegedly set by the plantation's operators: six-inch nails drilled through pieces of wood as spikes, which went through their boots and deep into their feet. [@1] Police commissioner Egon Keil said the officers were given sedatives and had spent time in hospital recovering, adding that the team had risked their lives doing the job. [@1]

Officers also recovered ammunition in the raid, even though a gun amnesty had ended in January. [@1] Keil said the find was a sign that weapons could still be out there. [@1]
