District police in Didim, Aydın province, detained two suspects identified only
as N.S. and M.T. in a cannabis enforcement operation. [@1] After both men were
referred to the adliye, the courthouse where custody decisions are made, N.S.
was remanded in pretrial custody, known in Turkey as tutuklama, while M.T. was
released. [@1]

Anti-Smuggling and Organized Crime teams, known by the Turkish initials KOM,
stopped a car driven by N.S. on Güvenlik Street in Efeler Mahallesi after acting
on a tip-off. [@1] Officers found an unspecified amount of cannabis, called
esrar in Turkish, inside the car. [@1] A search of N.S.'s home then yielded 4 kg
900 g of cannabis. [@1] A separate search of M.T.'s home, who police said acted
together with N.S., yielded 50 g of kubar esrar, a resinous form of cannabis,
along with 4 g of an unspecified drug and an unlicensed firearm. [@1] Police
described the overall result as about 6 kg of cannabis seized. [@1]

The stops and searches were carried out by the district police's KOM teams. [@1]
