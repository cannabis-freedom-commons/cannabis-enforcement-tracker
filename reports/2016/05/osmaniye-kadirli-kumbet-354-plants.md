Gendarmerie teams detained four people in Kümbet village in Kadirli district in
connection with cultivated cannabis found in village farmland. [@1] Kümbet is a
village in Kadirli district of Osmaniye province, where the field operation took
place. [@1]

Officers found 354 planted cannabis plants, known in Turkish as Hint keneviri,
growing as a cultivated crop in the village fields. [@1] The 354 plants were
spread across two different fields that were already planted with corn. [@1]
Officers seized all 354 plants during the search of the two corn-planted fields.
[@1] The authorities later destroyed the seized cannabis plants. [@1]

The field search followed evaluation of a tip received by the gendarmerie teams.
[@1] The jandarma, the gendarmerie force responsible for rural policing in
Türkiye, conducted the operation in the village. [@1] The account described the
seized plants as planted cannabis intended for use in producing narcotic
substances. [@1] The two corn fields were identified as the places where the
drug-material cannabis had been planted and grown. [@1]
