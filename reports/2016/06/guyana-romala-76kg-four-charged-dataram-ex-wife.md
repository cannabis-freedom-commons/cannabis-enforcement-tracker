Four people were jointly charged at the Georgetown Magistrate's Court on Monday
13 June 2016 over cannabis found in a house in Romala Housing Scheme, West Coast
Demerara. [@1] They were Balkhrison Ramdass, 54, and three women: Shameza Khan,
34, the ex-wife of drug trafficker Barry Dataram, Falicia Laundry and Andrea
Purran, 40. [@1]

Agents of the Customs Anti-Narcotics Unit (CANU), Guyana's anti-drug agency,
searched the house acting on information and found 76 kilos and 110 grams of
cannabis. [@1] The cannabis was being compressed and was believed to be prepared
for shipment abroad. [@1]

All four pleaded not guilty to the joint charge before Chief Magistrate Ann
McLennan. [@1] They were denied bail and remanded until 24 July 2016. [@1]
Ramdass faced a second charge of soliciting persons to traffic narcotics and
also pleaded not guilty to it. [@1]
