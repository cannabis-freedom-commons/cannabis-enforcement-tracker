Anamur District Gendarmerie Command's special anti-drug team carried out
operations at three separate addresses after intelligence and technical work.
[@1] In forestland at Ören Mahallesi, officers seized 852 cannabis plants said
to have been planted by S.K., 44, and M.B., 32. [@1] S.K. and M.B. were caught
with two hunting rifles in a house used to watch the planted field. [@1] Acting
on the detainees' statements, officers found another 300 cannabis plants in
forestland at TOKİ Beleni, and the suspects were remanded by the court after
questioning at the gendarmerie. [@1]

In a separate operation at Tenzile in Çamlıpınaralanı Mahallesi, officers seized
70 cannabis plants grown in forestland. [@1] Officers detained M.D., 36, in that
operation and found 40 grams of kubar esrar during a search of his house. [@1]
After court referral, M.D. was released to be tried without detention. [@1] In
Hacınuhlu neighbourhood in Mut district, gendarmerie teams staged an operation
after receiving a tip that cannabis was being grown at a house. [@1]

In the garden there, officers seized 400 planted cannabis plants and 2 kilograms
of ready-to-sell kubar esrar. [@1] B.K., detained in the Mut operation, was
remanded by the court after procedures at the gendarmerie. [@1] Across the
Anamur and Mut operations, officers reported seizing a total of 1,622 cannabis
plants and 2 kilograms 40 grams of kubar esrar. [@1]
