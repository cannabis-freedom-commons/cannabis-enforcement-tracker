Detectives assigned to the Narcotics division were operating in Gazeland
district in St Elizabeth at about 5:30 a.m. on Tuesday 21 June 2016 when they
seized 2,000 lb (about 907 kg) of ganja. [@1] The police put the drug's
estimated street value at just over J$10 million. [@1] Three foreign
nationals, including two women, were taken into custody. [@1]

Police then searched another premises and seized two 9 mm rounds of
ammunition, US$2,000 and J$22,000 in cash. [@1] Three more people were taken
into custody there. [@1] The identities of all six people in custody were
withheld, and police said they were expected to be charged under the Proceeds
of Crime Act. [@1]
