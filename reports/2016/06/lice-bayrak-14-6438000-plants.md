Security forces screened 6,210 vehicles and 15,595 people in the operation area
and detained 59 suspects. [@1] Three of the 59 detainees were remanded on
charges of drug trafficking and providing premises for it. [@1] A detained
shepherd told security forces that PKK members threatened to kill those who
refused orders and forced them to stand guard over drug-planted fields. [@1] The
shepherd said he had not consented, that dozens of his animals had been seized,
and that many others were forced at gunpoint to camp near the fields, stand
guard and control and irrigate the plots. [@1]

In the operation's first three days, security forces uprooted and then destroyed
6,438,000 Indian hemp plants found across hundreds of plots. [@1] On newly
identified plots, teams later found about 2,700,000 more Indian hemp plants and
373 kilograms of powdered cannabis buried underground. [@1] Village guards,
known as korucular, supported the detection and destruction work while searches
for newly planted areas continued. [@1]

The continuing Bayrak-14 joint operation in Lice and surrounding areas was
conducted with 24 battalions under the command of the Diyarbakır 7th Corps
Command. [@1] Its stated aims were to neutralize militants, said to include
senior leaders, in mountainous and forested areas, to destroy cannabis
cultivation areas described as the organization's funding source, and to
demolish shelters and depots. [@1] The account said the operations aimed to dry
up what it described as the PKK's biggest financial source. [@1] Forces also
destroyed shelters and improvised explosives found in the area and seized
weapons including a rocket launcher, ammunition and organizational documents.
[@1]
