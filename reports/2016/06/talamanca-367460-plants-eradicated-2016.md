Drug Control Police officers cut 367,460 cannabis plants in 27 plantations in
the Talamanca mountains of Limón. [@1] The plantations were found in Finca 20,
Alto Bley, Monteverde Arriba, Arco Iris, Cerro Congo, Asunción and Valle de las
Rosas. [@2]

The Drug Control Police (PCD) worked with the Public Force, the Police
Intervention Unit, the Air Surveillance Service and the Border Police. [@1]
Officers found the growers hiding the plants under mesh and a framework of rods
overgrown with passion-fruit vines, which made the plantations almost impossible
to see. [@1] The Ministry of Public Security said in a statement that the
intelligence work still located the hidden plots. [@2]

A medical team attached to the operation, including two doctors, a pharmacist, a
pharmacy technician, two nurses, two volunteer male nurses and a cook, treated
330 people in the area. [@2] The team completed vaccination schedules and
applied influenza vaccine, and handed out soap, sanitary towels, nappies,
clothing and notebooks while running hand-washing workshops. [@1]

Officers found the 27 plantations in farm plots during an intensive mountain
operation. [@3] The national eradication total reached more than 1,126,439
plants cut in 2016, alongside more than 1.8 tonnes of cannabis seized that year.
[@1]
