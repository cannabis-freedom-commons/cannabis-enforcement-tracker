Luxembourg police arrested two suspected drug dealers in Lintgen, north of the
capital, on Thursday 16 June 2016, following an investigation. [@1] Officers of
the Service de recherche et d'enquête criminelle, the Diekirch criminal
investigation service known as the SREC, carried out the arrests during an
inquiry ordered by the Diekirch public prosecutor. [@2]

A search found 1.6 kilos of marijuana between the two men. [@1] The drugs seized
in the searches weighed 1.16 kg and were packaged in plastic bags. [@2] Officers
also seized about 500 euros in cash. [@1]

Le Quotidien reported that the SREC had seized a little over one kilogram of
marijuana in the Lintgen area in the days before its 16 June report. [@2]
