The Provincial Criminal Investigation Service (SPIC) of Cuando Cubango
incinerated 320 kilos of liamba in Menongue on 26 June 2016. [@1] The stock had
been seized recently in operations by the province's National Police. [@1]
Officers also incinerated 191 liamba plants that had been cultivated in the
province that same day. [@1]

Cuando Cubango lies on a drug-trafficking route that continues to Namibia. [@1]
Part of the drugs passing through the province originate in Kwanza Norte,
Malanje, Bié and Zaire before moving through Cuando Cubango toward Namibia. [@1]
Police name Cuangar, Calai and Dirico among the municipalities with the highest
incidence of drug trafficking. [@1] Mavinga, Cuchi and the capital Menongue
complete the police list of trafficking hotspots. [@1]

SPIC's anti-narcotics department head, Intendant Firmino António, said 72 people
had been arrested in the province for drug trafficking in the previous year.
[@1] SPIC is the province's criminal-investigation service. [@1] Liamba is the
name used in the report for the drug destroyed in Menongue. [@1]
