A joint police and military team uprooted more than 38,400 marijuana plants in
the Navosa highlands of Fiji during Operation Cavuraka. [@1] Assistant
Commissioner of Police Luke Navela, head of intelligence and investigations,
said the public had helped the operation by passing on information, which led to
arrests. [@1] Navela said police had also seized generators and household
appliances while investigating proceeds of crime linked to the unexplained
wealth of some suspects. [@1]
