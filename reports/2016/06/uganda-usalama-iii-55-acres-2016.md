Some 1,500 officers from 22 countries took part in Operation Usalama III, a two-day regional operation on 29 and 30 June 2016 against transnational crime, led by the Eastern Africa Police Chiefs Cooperation Organization and the Southern African Regional Police Chiefs Cooperation Organization with Interpol's support. [@1,2] Across the region the operation produced nearly 4,500 arrests. [@1,2]

In Uganda, police destroyed 55 acres (about 22.3 hectares) of cannabis plants. [@1,2] In the same Ugandan strand of the operation, officers arrested six suspects over wildlife crimes after recovering 20 kilograms of ivory, tortoises, geese, ostrich egg shells and leopard skins, and seized seven guns with more than 300 rounds of ammunition. [@1,2]
