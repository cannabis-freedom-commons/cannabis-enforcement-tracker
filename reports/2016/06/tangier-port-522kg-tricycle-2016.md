National security officers at the port of Tangier seized about 522 kg of
hashish, pressed into slabs, on the evening of Monday 13 June 2016. [@1] The
hashish was found aboard a tricycle parked on the port's maintenance quay. [@2]

The drug was hidden in 17 suitcases, according to a statement from the General
Directorate of National Security (DGSN), Morocco's national police. [@1] The
Tangier prefectural police service opened an investigation under the supervision
of the competent prosecutor's office to arrest everyone suspected of involvement
in the case. [@1]

The police statement as reported in English described the goods as 522 kg of
cannabis bars hidden in 17 suitcases aboard the tricycle. [@3]
