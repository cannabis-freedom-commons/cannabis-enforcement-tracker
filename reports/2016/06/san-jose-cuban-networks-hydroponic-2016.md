Costa Rica's police broke up two linked Cuban-led trafficking networks on
Wednesday 8 June 2016, the Public Security Minister Gustavo Mata Vega announced
at a press conference. [@1] One network recruited poor Costa Ricans as couriers,
flying them to Cuba with drug deliveries hidden in clothing. [@1] The other grew
cannabis in clandestine hydroponic laboratories, at least three of which were
found, and supplied the courier operation; the caretakers were arrested. [@1]

Seven searches in San José that Wednesday seized 24 grams of cannabis buds, 24.6
grams of cannabis stems and 58 cannabis plants between 60 centimetres and a
metre tall. [@1] The searches also yielded 11,500 US dollars, three revolvers,
five cars and a motorcycle, a heat sealer and five air-conditioning units. [@1]
The investigation began in September 2015 with the Drug Control Police of the
security ministry and the National Intelligence and Security Directorate of the
presidency. [@1]
