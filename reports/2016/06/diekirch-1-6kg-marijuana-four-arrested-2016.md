Four young men were arrested for drug trafficking in Diekirch after several
weeks of investigations. [@1] The four were aged between 18 and 24 and were
arrested immediately. [@1]

Several police departments and the criminal investigation centre in Diekirch,
known by its initials SREC, carried out the raid. [@1] Officers seized no less
than 1.6 kg of marijuana. [@1] Police put the street value of the marijuana at
around 16,000 euros. [@1]

Officers also seized two vehicles and about 9,000 euros in cash. [@1] A Diekirch
investigating judge, a magistrate who directs the investigation, had ordered the
surveillance that preceded the raid. [@1]
