Missouri freshman defensive tackle Terry Beckner Jr. was arrested by MUPD on
Wednesday night during a traffic stop in Columbia. [@1] An officer stopped
Beckner’s car on Conley Avenue at 9:34 p.m. after seeing it driven without
headlights on. [@2]

During the stop officers smelled marijuana coming from the vehicle and found a
clear plastic Ziploc-style bag containing marijuana after searching the car.
[@2] Beckner was taken to the University of Missouri Police Department, where he
was booked and processed before being released on summons. [@2] Police recorded
the case as possession of 35 grams or less of marijuana. [@2]

The charge was described as a misdemeanor. [@1] After the arrest Missouri coach
Barry Odom suspended Beckner indefinitely and said he will face team
disciplinary measures. [@3] In his first season at Missouri he had started five
games and recorded three sacks before tearing the ACL and MCL in his right knee.
