Officers of the Border Police Regional Center Center - Tuzi Border Police
Department controlled E.H. (23) from Bajza, Albania, after he approached border
control at the Božaj crossing on foot on 8 January. [@1] Border officers
suspected that he was carrying concealed items he was trying to smuggle into
Albania, and they inspected and searched him and his belongings jointly with
officers of the Customs Administration's Božaj outpost. [@1] The inspection and
search was carried out together with officers of the Customs Administration -
Customs Outpost Božaj. [@1] Inside his bag they found a larger package
containing a dark-green substance suspected to be marijuana and weighing about
500 grams. [@1] Police described the overall result as about half a kilogram of
marijuana found and seized from an Albanian citizen at the Božaj border
crossing. [@1]

The man and the suspected drug were handed to the Department for the fight
against drugs - Group Podgorica, which informed the Deputy High State Prosecutor
in Podgorica about the incident. [@1] The case was pursued on suspicion of
unauthorized production, possession and placing narcotic drugs on the market,
and the substance was sent to the Forensic Center for expert examination. [@1]
