Lilongwe Chief Resident Magistrate Court granted bail to Ntcheu Third Grade
Magistrate Stanford Majoni and nine other judicial and police officers accused
of theft by a public servant and conspiracy to steal. [@1] State prosecutor
Assistant Commissioner Levison Mangani told the court that the accused worked as
a magistrate, district court administrator, security guard, court clerk,
accounts assistant and court marshals. [@1] Mangani said they had 84 bags of
cannabis sativa, known in Malawi as chamba, in their custody as exhibits and
could not produce them or account for them. [@1]

Chief Resident Magistrate Ruth Chinangwa granted bail on condition that the
accused report to Ntcheu police weekly, produce one non-cash surety of 10,000
kwacha, pay 10,000 kwacha in cash, surrender travel documents and not leave
Ntcheu district without police clearance. [@1] The defence asked for the
hearings to be moved to Ntcheu for transport reasons, but Chinangwa refused and
told them the case should be finished within four sittings. [@1] The court
adjourned the case to 18 January 2016. [@1]
