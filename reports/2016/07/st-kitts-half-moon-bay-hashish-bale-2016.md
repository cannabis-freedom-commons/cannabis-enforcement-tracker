A group of swimmers saw a bale bobbing in the waters off Half Moon Bay on
Saturday 16 July 2016 and notified police. [@1] The Anti-Drug Unit of the Royal
St Christopher and Nevis Police Force said it seized the bale, which officers
described as containing a large quantity of hashish. [@2] The police statement
said the bale contained a number of compressed packages. [@1] The force called
this its first seizure of this quantity of hashish. [@1] It described hashish,
also called hash, as a very potent extract from the cannabis plant. [@1]

Commissioner Ian M. Queeley thanked the people who contacted police. [@1]
Queeley said “it is this type of cooperation and partnership that will help to
reduce crime in our federation.” [@1] He urged citizens and residents to report
any criminal activity they might be aware of. [@1] The force directed people
with information to any police station in the federation or Crime Stoppers, an
anonymous hotline. [@1] Police said the investigation into the find was ongoing.
