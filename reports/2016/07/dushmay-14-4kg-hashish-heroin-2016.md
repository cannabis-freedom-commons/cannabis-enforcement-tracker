The Drug Control Agency (AKN) of Tajikistan said that in a three-stage operation
targeting people moving drugs from Tajikistan to neighboring countries, it had
seized more than 16 kg of narcotics. [@1] Sputnik reported that, according to
the investigation, the three detained residents of Istaravshan had been engaged
in supplying drugs to neighboring countries and then to Russia. [@2]
