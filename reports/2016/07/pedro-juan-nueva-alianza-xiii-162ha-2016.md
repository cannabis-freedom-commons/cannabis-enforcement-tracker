Paraguayan and Brazilian authorities met in the border city of Pedro Juan Caballero to evaluate a joint operation that destroyed about 70 tonnes of marijuana. [@1] The force reported seizing and burning 57,810 kilograms of chopped marijuana and 9,100 kilograms of marijuana, alongside 162 hectares of crops eradicated and about 100 clandestine camps destroyed in forested parts of Amambay department on the Brazilian border. [@1,2]

The meeting brought together Brazil's justice minister Alexandre de Moraes and Federal Police director Leandro Daiello with Senad chief Hugo Vera and foreign ministry official Víctor Aquino. [@1] Operation Nueva Alianza XIII worked from Pedro Juan Caballero, the capital of Amambay, with Senad reporting 162 hectares eradicated, 350 kilograms of seeds destroyed and 103 processing camps burned. [@2] Both sides stressed bilateral cooperation against large-scale cultivation and trafficking between the two countries. [@1]
