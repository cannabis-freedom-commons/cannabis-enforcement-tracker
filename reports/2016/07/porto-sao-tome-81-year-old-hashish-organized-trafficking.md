Porto PSP officers detained a 31-year-old man and an 81-year-old woman late on
Friday 8 July 2016 in an operation in Bairro de São Tomé in Porto. [@1] Both
were held on suspicion of drug trafficking. [@1] Police said the two were
engaged in organised drug trafficking in the São Tomé neighbourhood. [@2] The
PSP metropolitan command in Porto said the 31-year-old was unemployed, the
81-year-old was retired, both lived in Porto and were related. [@2]

Officers seized more than two kilograms of hashish pollen, which police said was
enough for more than 10,200 individual doses. [@1] The operation, carried out at
about 21:30 on Friday evening, also yielded cannabis herb (liamba) enough for
about 556 individual doses. [@2] Police also seized seven doses of cocaine, 308
euros, a precision scale, untaxed tobacco packs, a mobile phone and other
material used in direct drug sales. [@1]

The two detainees were to be brought before the court so that coercive measures
could be decided. [@1] The PSP statement said they would first be presented to
the Public Prosecution Service. [@2]
