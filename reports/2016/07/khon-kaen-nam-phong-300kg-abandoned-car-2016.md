Highway police seized 300 kilograms of compressed marijuana from an abandoned car after a chase in Khon Kaen province in the early hours of Sunday. [@1] The seizure was made at about 3am after a black Bangkok-registered Toyota Vios broke through a checkpoint between kilometres 360 and 361 on Mitraphap road at tambon Muang Wan in Nam Phong district without stopping for a search. [@1]

Police gave chase and found the car abandoned about five kilometres from the checkpoint. [@1] Inside were seven bags holding 300 bars of dried marijuana weighing about 300 kilograms. [@1] The driver was not found. [@1] The commander of the Khon Kaen Highway Police, Pol Col Apichart Phochan, announced the seizure. [@1]
