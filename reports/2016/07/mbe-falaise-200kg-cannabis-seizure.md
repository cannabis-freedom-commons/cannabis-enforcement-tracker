The Mbé gendarmerie brigade and Vina prosecutor Hamadou Bouba stopped a van at the foot of the Mbé cliff in Cameroon after receiving a tip. [@1] The van had left Bafoussam for Kousseri, in the Far North, carrying cannabis hidden in jute sacks among cabbages. [@1,2,3] Officers seized more than 200 kilograms. [@1,2,3]

Gendarmes arrested the driver, his assistant and the owner of the cargo. [@1] Security sources told Camer.be all three had admitted involvement when questioned and were held on allegations of possessing, transporting and delivering narcotics. [@1] The outlet said they were expected to appear in court within days. [@1]

Bouba said the cargo might have been intended for Boko Haram, citing its destination in the Far North. [@1,2,3]

The reports give no exact weight above 200 kilograms or subsequent court result. [@1,2,3]
