National police intercepted a lorry carrying cannabis on Monday 25 July 2016 at
the Kpéta-Bèna post in Wawa Prefecture in Togo's Plateaux Region. [@1] Three
Togolese nationals travelling with the load were arrested, the driver, his
apprentice and the escort. [@2] The three detainees told police they had
believed the cargo was pagnes, lengths of wraparound cloth. [@1] Two other
people who were aboard the vehicle fled and were still being sought. [@2] The
cannabis was being conveyed from Ghana toward Benin. [@3]

Officers searching the load found 3,955 loaves and 6 balls of cannabis weighing
a total of 3,600 kg, carefully packed and concealed under firewood. [@1] The
vehicle was a 10-tonne lorry registered RT 2041 AH whose suspicious parcels drew
the attention of officers from the Kpéta-Bèna police post. [@1] Plateaux police
sector commissioner Kolani Nanangue was named as the officer giving the account
of the search and seizure. [@2] Police said the name of the intended recipient
was visible on each parcel. [@1]

The load had been covered with firewood to evade the vigilance of the security
forces. [@3] The commissioner credited the cooperation of local people for the
success of the operation. [@2]
