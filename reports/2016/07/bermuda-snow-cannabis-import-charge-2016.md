Marcus Snow, a 40-year-old from Toronto, was charged in Bermuda's Magistrates'
Court on 11 July 2016 with importing about 4kg of cannabis on 8 July and
possessing it in St George's with intent to supply. [@1] Senior Magistrate Juan
Wolffe remanded Snow in custody and listed the case for the Supreme Court's
September arraignments session. [@1]

In the same morning's list, Toby Smith, a 62-year-old New York visitor, admitted
importing two cannabis cigarettes holding 2.1g of the drug, found in the
toiletry section of his checked luggage after a search at LF Wade International
Airport on 8 July. [@1] Smith told the court he had used the drug to help his
appetite without having a prescription. [@1]

Magistrate Wolffe fined Smith $250 for importing the drug and a further $250 for
possessing it, $500 in all. [@1] Bernews reported the Crown's alleged weight for
Snow's substance as 4,000 grams and gave his age as 39. [@2]

Snow denied the charges at the Supreme Court arraignments on 1 September 2016
and was remanded in custody until a trial date in November. [@3] No sentence or
verdict from Snow's November 2016 trial was reported. [@3]
