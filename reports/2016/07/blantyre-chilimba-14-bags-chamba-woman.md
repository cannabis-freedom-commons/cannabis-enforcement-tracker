Police in Chilimba township in Blantyre arrested 23-year-old Elizabeth Mitundu
on Saturday 30 July 2016 with 14 bags of cannabis sativa, known locally as
chamba. [@1]

The cannabis was kept at a building under construction, and police sent it to
the Bvumbwe research station for testing as part of the trial process. [@2] She
will appear in court charged with being found in possession of cannabis sativa
without a licence. [@1]
