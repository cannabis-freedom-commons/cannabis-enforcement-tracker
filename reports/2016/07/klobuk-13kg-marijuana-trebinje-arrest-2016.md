Border police in Trebinje arrested a Montenegrin citizen identified only as M.Š.
on 29 July 2016 on suspicion of trying to smuggle 13 kilograms of marijuana into
Bosnia and Herzegovina. [@1] The drugs were found during a check of a passenger
Mercedes car with Montenegrin licence plates at the entrance to Bosnia and
Herzegovina at the Klobuk international border crossing. [@2] The driver was
M.Š., a citizen of Montenegro born in 1985. [@2] The marijuana was packed in 24
packages hidden in several places inside the vehicle. [@1]

The Prosecutor's Office of Bosnia and Herzegovina and the Court of Bosnia and
Herzegovina were informed of the case. [@1] Vijesti reported that further work
on the case was under way. [@1] Vijesti credited the account to the Sarajevo
portal Klix.ba. [@1]
