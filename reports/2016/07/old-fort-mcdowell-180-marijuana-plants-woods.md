Law enforcement agencies seized 180 marijuana plants from plots in the woods
between Catawba River and Mount Hebron roads near Old Fort, North Carolina. [@1]
The McDowell County Sheriff's Office, Marion Police Department, the State Bureau
of Investigation and a North Carolina Highway Patrol helicopter unit located the
plots. [@1] Officers seized 93 of the plants on 5 July and took the rest on 12
July. [@1]

The sheriff's office had received information earlier in July about patches of
marijuana being grown in the area. [@2] The plants taken on 12 July were about 2
to 7 feet tall. [@2]

Lt. Chris Taylor of the McDowell County Sheriff's Office said the investigation
was ongoing and that investigators had leads. [@2] Deputies asked anyone with
information to contact the sheriff's office or McDowell County Crimestoppers,
which offered a possible cash reward. [@2]
