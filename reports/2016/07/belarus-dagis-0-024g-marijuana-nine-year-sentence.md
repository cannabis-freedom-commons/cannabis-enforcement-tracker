Kirill Dagis was 25 years old when he was detained. [@1] He was detained in July
2016 with 0.024 grams of marijuana. [@1] He was later convicted under part 3 of
Article 328 and received nine years in a penal colony with confiscation of
property for the 0.024 grams of marijuana. [@1]

Part 3 of Article 328 covers sale committed by a previously convicted person.
[@1] About a year earlier he had been detained with almost the same dose, 0.026
grams, and given one year of home arrest. [@1] The second case followed a
statement by another detained man, in whose car boot 15 grams of marijuana had
been found, who claimed he had bought the drug from Dagis. [@1] His mother said
no direct evidence of the alleged handover was found. [@1]

His lawyer insisted there was no crime of sale because of the insignificant
amount seized, 0.024 grams. [@1] Under Article 328 of the Belarusian Criminal
Code, liability does not depend on the amount found or the substance seized.
[@1] The other accused received a suspended sentence, while Dagis received nine
years in a reinforced-regime colony with confiscation of property. [@1]

The family had a garage confiscated that his grandparents had gifted to him by
deed. [@1] There was also an attempt to confiscate the flat where he lived with
his 80-year-old grandmother, a Chernobyl invalid, but the family kept the flat.
[@1] He and his mother appealed the verdict up to the Supreme Court but received
standard replies leaving it unchanged. [@1]
