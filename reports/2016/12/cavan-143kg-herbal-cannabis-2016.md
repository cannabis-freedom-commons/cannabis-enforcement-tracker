On 16 December 2016 an intelligence-led joint operation in County Cavan by the Garda National Drugs and Organised Crime Bureau and Revenue's Customs Service seized approximately 143 kilos of herbal cannabis. [@1,2] The operation focused on people involved in drug trafficking and the sale and supply of illicit drugs. [@1] The seized cannabis, subject to analysis, had an estimated street value of €2.8 million. [@1,2]

A 36-year-old man and a 50-year-old woman were arrested and detained at Ballyconnell Garda Station under section 2 of the Criminal Justice (Drug Trafficking) Act 2006. [@1,2] In a follow-up operation a 46-year-old man was also arrested for the same offence and detained at Cavan Garda Station. [@1] No charges or later court outcomes were reported in these accounts.
