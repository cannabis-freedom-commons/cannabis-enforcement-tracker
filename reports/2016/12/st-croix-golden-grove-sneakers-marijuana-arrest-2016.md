A 28-year-old woman was arrested after she allegedly tried to bring marijuana
into the Golden Grove Correctional Facility on St. Croix for an inmate. [@1]
Officers were told the woman, Sherifa Petersen, had bought a medium-sized clear
bag of clothing and shoes for an inmate. [@1]

Police were sent to the prison at 1:38 p.m. on Friday, 9 December 2016, to
investigate a reported attempt to bring in narcotics. [@1] A corrections officer
smelled a strong odour of marijuana from a pair of black Jordan sneakers, and
the chief corrections officer also detected it. [@1] When officers pulled the
sneaker apart, two small clear plastic bags of a green leafy substance fell from
the soles. [@1]

The green leafy substance tested positive for marijuana. [@1] Petersen was
arrested and charged with introduction of narcotics into the prison, and was
remanded to the Golden Grove facility. [@1]
