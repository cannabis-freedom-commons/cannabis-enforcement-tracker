Ottawa police raided seven marijuana dispensaries across the city on 3 and 4
November 2016, arresting nine budtenders. [@1] The drug unit executed warrants
at shops on Montreal Road, Preston Street, Bank Street, Rideau Street, St
Laurent Boulevard and St Joseph Boulevard, plus a Roydon Place address on 3
November. [@1] Officers seized marijuana, hashish, hash oil, THC concentrate
known as shatter, cannabis edibles and soft drinks, cash and documents. [@1]

The nine arrested face charges including possession for the purpose of
trafficking and possession of the proceeds of crime. [@1] Staff Sergeant Rick
Carey said police had investigated the shops for a long time after community
complaints and would keep investigating dispensaries as complaints
arrived. [@1] Police said they were working with federal prosecutors under the
new medical-marijuana access rules that had taken effect on 24 August. [@1]
