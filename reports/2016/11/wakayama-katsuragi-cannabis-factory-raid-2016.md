A police raid on a factory in the small town of Katsuragi, Wakayama Prefecture, uncovered a cannabis haul with an estimated street value of 2 billion yen (about 18.3 million dollars). [@1,2] Officers seized more than 10,000 cannabis plants in the operation, which police announced on Wednesday 16 November 2016. [@1,2]

Police arrested four men from Osaka on suspicion of possessing marijuana for sale, serving fresh arrest warrants on the same day the seizure was displayed at a Nara prefectural police station. [@2] They included 54-year-old Junichi Kimura, described as a senior gang member. [@1]

## Displayed as evidence

Part of the seized haul was photographed piled at a Nara prefectural police station as evidence of the factory raid in neighbouring Wakayama. [@2]
