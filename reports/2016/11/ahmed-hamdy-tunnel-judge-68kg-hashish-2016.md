Security forces stopped an unnamed male judge in his car at the Ahmed Hamdy tunnel connecting the eastern and western banks of the Suez Canal and found 68 kg of hashish valued at about EGP 1.5 million. [@1] Prosecutors ordered his detention on 10 November 2016. [@1]

The judge's driver and a European woman travelling with them were also arrested. [@1] Judicial sources said Justice Minister Hossam Abdel-Rahim accepted the judge's resignation after the incident. [@1]
