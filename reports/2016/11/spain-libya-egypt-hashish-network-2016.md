Officers of the National Police, the Guardia Civil and the Tax Office,
coordinated by the Centre for Intelligence against Organised Crime, broke up a
criminal organisation smuggling large amounts of hashish into Spain and on to
other European countries and North Africa, arresting 25 people including its
leaders in Melilla, Málaga and Murcia. [@1] The network ran cells in Melilla,
Málaga, Lorca in Murcia, Algeciras and Alicante that handled boats, storage
and distribution of hashish bound for Spain, France, Germany and Libya. [@1]

In international waters about 100 miles off Algeria, the Guardia Civil's
Maritime Service, with air support and the Tax Office's Customs Surveillance
service, boarded two yachts. [@1] A 14-metre yacht that had sailed from the
Almerimar marina in Almería under a false German flag carried 950 kilos of
hashish, and a 17-metre yacht that had sailed from the Mazarrón marina in
Murcia under a Spanish flag carried 4,000 kilos. [@1] Officers also found over
200 kilos in a safe house in Villanueva de la Concepción, Málaga, for onward
distribution. [@1]

## More than five and a half tonnes

In total more than five and a half tonnes of hashish were confiscated, along
with cars, a yacht, a pleasure craft and five inflatable boats with their
engines, three short firearms, cash, telephones, computers and documents now
under study. [@1]
