Paul Costa, a 35-year-old go-kart worker, grew more than 100 cannabis plants in
a concealed area of the Emotion Karting centre in Ayr between February 2013 and
February 2015. [@1,2] Police raided the site and found high-powered lamps and
other growing equipment alongside the crop, which the court valued at up to
£100,000. [@1,2]

Costa denied responsibility at his trial at the High Court in Glasgow, claiming
he had found the farm and was trying to catch the culprit when detectives
raided the complex. [@1] The court heard he had trawled the internet for
growing tips, including a video titled "how to properly dry cannabis", and
police found his fingerprints in the growing area and his DNA on a juice can.
[@1,2]

## Sentence

The jury rejected his account and convicted him of producing and supplying
cannabis. [@1] He was then sentenced at the High Court in Glasgow to four and
a half years in prison. [@2]
