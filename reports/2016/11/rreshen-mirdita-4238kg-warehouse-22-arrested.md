Around 100 police forces from Lezha and Mirdita led by Lezha police director
Ermal Muca carried out the codenamed operation Befasia at a warehouse at the
former Collection at the exit of Rreshen-Mirdita. [@2] The warehouse served for
the collection and processing of cannabis. [@2] Officers arrested 22 people
inside the workshop in flagrante delicto while they were working. [@2] Nine
women were pursued at large and the warehouse owner Kastriot Reci was declared
wanted. [@2]

Weighing completed the next day found 4238 kg of cannabis-sativa in the process
of drying and processing in the warehouse. [@2] In a vast warehouse in the town
of Rreshen, tier after tier of drying cannabis was laid out on mesh shelves with
sacks around and a transit van with open back doors. [@1] In this one location
there were more than four tonnes of cannabis with a street value in Italy of
some six million euros, described by officer in charge Agron Cullhaj as the
largest ever find in this area. [@1]

The seizure came as the growing season ended and police shifted to confiscating
the prepared drug after the government said more than two million cannabis
plants had been destroyed that year. [@1] The investigative group in cooperation
with the prosecutor continued actions to fully document the activity. [@2] On
November 2, 4238 kg of cannabis was found in a warehouse in Mirdita with 22
workers arrested on site. [@3]
