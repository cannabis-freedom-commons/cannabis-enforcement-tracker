German customs officers arrested two brothers from Saarbrücken on the
Luxembourgish-German border a week before the case was reported. [@1] The men
had 7,263 grams of marijuana and 6,030 euros in cash on them when they were
stopped. [@1] The brothers were 29 and 32 years old. [@1] The German customs
office in Frankfurt am Main described the two as drug traffickers. [@1] The
public prosecution in Saarbrücken issued an arrest warrant for both brothers.
[@1]

Customs said a follow-up search of the brothers' home produced a further 521
grams of marijuana, 809 grams of hashish and 4,000 euros of suspected drug
money. [@1] The Frankfurt office announced the border arrest and the
house-search results only later. [@1]

Hans-Jürgen Schmidt of the Frankfurt customs office said investigators were
trying to establish who the drugs were being delivered to and whether more
people and customers were involved. [@1] Schmidt said customs were also checking
whether the brothers were responsible for other earlier drug flows and, if so,
on what scale. [@1]
