Customs and Border Protection officers at the Mariposa commercial facility seized approximately 17,060 pounds of marijuana on Friday 12 February 2016 from a tractor-trailer whose load of six palletized boxes, marked as aircraft parts, hid the drug. [@1,2] A narcotics detection canine had alerted officers to the rig, driven by Gerardo Cruz, 23, of Colonia La Mesa, Sonora, Mexico, and the agency valued the load at more than $8.5 million. [@1,2]

Cruz, a Mexican national, was arrested and transferred to U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement's Homeland Security Investigations. [@1,2] "Near record seizures such as this are the result of a combined effort by our officers, canine, and technology," said Nogales Port Director Guadalupe Ramirez. [@1,2] The agency called it the second-largest marijuana seizure in Arizona port history, after more than 20,000 pounds seized in November 2013. [@1,2]
