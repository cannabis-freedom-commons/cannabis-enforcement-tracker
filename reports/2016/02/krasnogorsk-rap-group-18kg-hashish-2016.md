Officers of the Interior Ministry's Main Criminal Investigation Directorate,
working with Moscow Oblast police, detained four men in Krasnogorsk district
during a sale of drugs. [@1] The police press service said two of the detainees
were sellers and two were buyers who intended to resell what they had bought.
[@3] Bloknot Moskva reported that officers caught the group in the middle of the
transaction. [@3] The Interior Ministry press service said the detainees were
members of a rap group. [@1] Lenta.ru identified one of the detainees as the
actor and rapper Nikita Ost, whose real name is Nikita Savinkin. [@2] Lenta.ru
described Rynochnye Otnosheniya as a Moscow collective that had existed for more
than ten years. [@2]

Laboratory testing established that the seized substance was 18 kg of Moroccan
hashish. [@1] Investigators opened criminal cases for unlawfully acquiring,
storing, transporting and processing drugs and for unlawfully producing, selling
or mailing them. [@1]

On 17 February the Krasnogorsk city court placed Savinkin and Yevgeny Rumyantsev
under house arrest. [@4] The same court extended the detention of Anton Setenko
and Sergei Mishko by 72 hours to give investigators time to settle the charges,
the Moscow Oblast court's press secretary said. [@4]
