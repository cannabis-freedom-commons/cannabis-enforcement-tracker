Gendarmes of the Saint-Gilles brigade found an open-air zamal plantation at the
home of a resident of Saline-les-Hauts during a search, the gendarmerie of La
Réunion announced on Thursday 4 February 2016. [@1] The gendarmes uprooted
nearly 300 plants of all sizes and destroyed them, for 640 kilos, the
gendarmerie said. [@1] The gendarmes uprooted and destroyed the plants that same
Thursday under orders from the public prosecutor's office, Réunion La 1ère
reported. [@2]

Two people were implicated in the case, the gendarmerie said. [@1] The two
alleged cultivators were to be summoned to explain themselves before the
tribunal correctionnel, the criminal court, in the following months. [@2]

LINFO.re reported the figures from the gendarmerie's Facebook page the day of
the search, crediting Imaz Press Réunion with first publishing the gendarmes'
announcement. [@1] Imaz Press Réunion reported the same day that a search at a
Saline-les-Hauts resident's home had discovered the plantation. [@3] Zamal is
the Réunion name for cannabis, whose cultivation, sale and possession are
illegal in France. [@2]
