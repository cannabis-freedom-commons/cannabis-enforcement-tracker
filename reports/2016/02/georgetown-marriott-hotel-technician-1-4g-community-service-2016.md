A 23-year-old audio-visual technician at the Guyana Marriott Hotel Georgetown
was fined GYD$20,000 and sentenced to 50 hours of community service by a
Georgetown court on 2 February 2016. [@1] The court was told that police
arrested him at about 1 AM on 28 January 2016 after 1.4 grammes of marijuana was
found in his left shirt pocket at the Marriott Hotel in Kingston, Georgetown.
[@1] He pleaded guilty to possessing the marijuana, and was ordered to serve the
community service at the Georgetown City Constabulary. [@1]

The court said that if the community service is not performed he would be jailed
for six months. [@1] Police prosecutor Deniro Jones told the court that officers
found the narcotic in his left side shirt pocket when they arrested him. [@1] He
told the court he had kept the marijuana on his person instead of in his locker
because of the hotel's policy, and asked for leniency, saying he had applied for
a job in Chicago and feared the case would harm his chances. [@1]
