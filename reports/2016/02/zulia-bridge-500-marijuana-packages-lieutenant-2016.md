Venezuelan army first lieutenant Yorjan José Ojeda Páez was detained before dawn
on 13 February 2016 at the Punta de Piedra checkpoint on the General Rafael
Urdaneta bridge over Lake Maracaibo, in Zulia state, while driving a Toyota
pickup truck with army insignia. [@1] A search of the vehicle found 15 cardboard
boxes holding 500 packages of marijuana and 10 packages of cocaine. [@2]
National Guard officers at the bridge checkpoint stopped the vehicle for a
routine inspection, El Nacional reported in its account of the case. [@4]

La Verdad, citing an official, put the marijuana at 500 bricks weighing 255 kg
and 310 g. [@1] El Nacional later reported that the 500 marijuana packages
weighed 260 kg and 110 g. [@4] El Pitazo reported the combined weight of the
marijuana and cocaine as 266 kg and 20 g, without separating the two drugs. [@3]

Venezuela's public prosecutor's office charged Ojeda Páez and army lieutenant
colonel José Antonio Picón with trafficking narcotic and psychotropic substances
in the form of transport, and with criminal association. [@3] Picón, commander
of a radio-electronic exploration unit of the Strategic Operational Command in
El Tigre, was arrested in Maracaibo on 29 February 2016 on an arrest warrant
requested by the prosecutors. [@3]

In March 2018 a court in Zulia sentenced Ojeda Páez to 20 years in prison after
he admitted the trafficking charge, and he was held at the Coro prison. [@4] El
Nacional reported that Picón, a National Guard sergeant and a police officer
were still awaiting trial in the case. [@4]
