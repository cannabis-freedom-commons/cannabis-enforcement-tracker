An investigation led Barrie police to believe that several people had been
trafficking drugs. [@1] On 17 February 2016, members of the Barrie Police Street
Crime and CERT Unit executed a search warrant at a residence on St. Vincent
Street in Barrie. [@1] Police arrested and charged three people who lived at the
residence. [@1]

A 19-year-old man was charged with three counts of possession of a controlled
substance and two counts of possession for the purpose of trafficking, and he
was to appear in bail court on Thursday morning. [@1] An 18-year-old woman was
charged with four counts of possession of a controlled substance, was released
on a promise to appear, and was to appear in a Barrie court in April. [@1] A
51-year-old woman was charged with possession of a controlled substance and two
counts of breach of probation, was released on a promise to appear, and was to
appear in a Barrie court in April. [@1]

Officers seized numerous prescription drugs, along with 3 grams of heroin and 8
grams of marijuana. [@1]
