Luxembourg Police arrested six people in connection with the illegal drugs
trade. [@1] The arrests were carried out on 16 and 17 February 2016 in
Mertert-Wasserbillig. [@1] Officers of the Grevenmacher criminal investigation
unit arrested the six suspects under the direction of an investigating judge.
[@2] According to police, one suspect was accused of selling marijuana and
chemical drugs to young consumers, mainly minors. [@1] About 60 customers of the
dealer were identified. [@1]

Police raided a property where they found 350 grams of cannabis and 570 euros in
cash, with the help of drug sniffer dogs. [@1] Officers also found an indoor
cannabis plantation there containing about 50 plants. [@1]

The swoop followed an investigation that had been running since November 2015.
[@1] The investigating judge ordered provisional detention for five suspects and
placed the sixth person under judicial supervision. [@2]
