Police arrested a foreigner carrying marijuana on Friday morning at the port in
Maya, Daanbantayan. [@1] The arrested man was 48-year-old Wille Marco, a citizen
of Liechtenstein. [@1] Marco owned a resort on Malapascua Island. [@1]
Daanbantayan police said they had received a report that Marco was transporting
marijuana. [@1] Police chief Senior Inspector Roger Quijano assembled four
officers and waited at Maya wharf for the bus to arrive. [@1] Marco was riding a
passenger bus toward Daanbantayan, planning to take a boat onward to Malapascua
Island. [@1]

When the bus arrived and Marco alighted, officers approached him. [@1] Marco
tried to run and threw away a plastic bag that was found to contain dried
marijuana leaves. [@1] Marco resisted arrest and tried to keep running before
police restrained him. [@1] Inside the bag, police found at least five bars of
marijuana hash and 500 grams of dried marijuana leaves. [@1] A second account
said police recovered a plastic bag of dried marijuana leaves weighing 500
grams. [@2] The same account said a chocolate-bar-shaped item turned out to be
processed marijuana leaves weighing another 500 grams. [@2]

Police valued the seizure at P200,000 based on Dangerous Drugs Board estimates.
[@1] Police said Marco would be charged with violating the Comprehensive
Dangerous Drugs Act of 2002 and with disobedience to an agent of a person in
authority. [@1] Investigators said they were tracking down Marco's supplier and
described Marco as uncooperative. [@2]
