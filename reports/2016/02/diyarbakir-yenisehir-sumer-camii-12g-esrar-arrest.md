Police in Diyarbakır detained a driver identified as İ.H.Ç. after intercepting a
suspicious vehicle in the city's central Yenişehir district. [@1] Officers
stationed outside the governor's residence noticed the car, bearing license
plate 56 AE 340, pass by the building twice around 00:20. [@2] Determining that
the driver was monitoring the residence, police followed the car and brought it
to a controlled stop on İstasyon Avenue in front of Sümer Mosque. [@2]

A search of the vehicle uncovered 12 grams of esrar, a cannabis preparation,
which officers confiscated alongside 21 narcotic pills. [@2] Inside the car's
boot, officers also found a firearm described as a long-barreled weapon along
with matching ammunition and a flash drive. [@1] The search additionally yielded
an organizational vest and a badge bearing the portrait of Abdullah Öcalan. [@1]

Following the vehicle search, the driver was taken into custody and transported
to police headquarters for interrogation. [@2] Security sources reported that
legal and administrative procedures regarding the suspect were continuing at the
police department. [@1]
