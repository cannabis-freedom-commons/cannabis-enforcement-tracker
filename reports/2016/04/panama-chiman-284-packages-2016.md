Panama's National Aeronaval Service, Senan, intercepted a boat named "El Compa"
carrying four Colombian crew members and found bundles of drugs on board. [@2]
Agents also detained a second, homemade-style boat crewed by one Colombian man
who had a Panamanian nationality and five 18-gallon fuel tanks with him. [@2]
The five detainees were placed at the disposal of the Second Superior
Prosecutor's Office for Drug-Related Offences, which did not release their
identities. [@2] During the operation named San Esteban de Antioquía, five
people and two boats carrying the illicit substance were detained. [@1]

Aeronaval commissioner Joe Laniado confirmed the seizure of 284 packages of
marijuana on Tuesday, 26 April. [@1] Senan found 284 packages of marijuana in
the operation, which it named San Esteban de Antioquía. [@3] The packages were
divided into 14 bultos, or bundles, according to the agency. [@3] Second
specialised prosecutor Julio Villarreal said a preliminary field test confirmed
the substance was marijuana. [@3]

The operation took place the previous Sunday within the perimeter of Isla Maje
in Chimán district. [@2]

Laniado said traffickers keep changing their methods and continue trying to
breach Panama's borders and coasts. [@1] He said Senan and other security
agencies carry out constant sea and air patrols against drug trafficking. [@1]
