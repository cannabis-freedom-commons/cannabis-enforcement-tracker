An American cruise-ship tourist arrested for buying 1.8 g of cannabis in Belize City had her charges dismissed: the judge noted the cannabis had been sold to her openly and she appeared to believe it was legal, cautioning her never to possess drugs in Belize again.[@1] [@1]

Belize City Magistrate Carlon Mendoza cautioned and discharged an American cruise-ship visitor after she was caught with cannabis on the morning of 19 April 2016. [@2] A security guard at the Belize City Tourism Village found 1.8 grams of cannabis in the visitor's handbag. [@2] The visitor said she had bought the cannabis in Belize and did not know it was illegal. [@2] Magistrate Mendoza said the cannabis had been sold to her openly and that police should find the sellers rather than buyers and users of cannabis. [@2]
