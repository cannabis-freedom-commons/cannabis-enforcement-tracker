U.S. Customs and Border Protection officers at the Mariposa Commercial
Facility in Nogales, Arizona, seized about 14,800 pounds of marijuana, worth
about 7.4 million dollars, from a Mexican national on 21 April 2016. [@1]
After a narcotics-detection dog alerted on his tractor-trailer, officers sent
the rig for a secondary inspection and found the packaged marijuana mixed
among a shipment of watermelons. [@2]

The driver, a 51-year-old man from Nogales in the Mexican state of Sonora, was
arrested. [@1] Officers seized the tractor-trailer and its shipment and turned
the driver over to U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement's Homeland
Security Investigations. [@1] Officials called it the third-largest marijuana
seizure at an Arizona port of entry. [@2]
