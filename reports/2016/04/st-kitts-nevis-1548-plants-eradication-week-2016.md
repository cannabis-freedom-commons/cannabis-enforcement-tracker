Royal St Christopher and Nevis police with law-enforcement partners uprooted and
seized marijuana plants and seedlings in a series of eradications in the week of
23 to 28 April 2016. [@1] On 23 April, Division B officers on an eradication
patrol in hills in the division's area found 450 marijuana plants under
cultivation and 150 seedlings in a nursery. [@1]

Those Division B plants ranged from one to six feet tall. [@1] On 27 April, a
joint Special Services Unit and Anti-Drug Unit operation uprooted 654 plants in
the Conaree area. [@1] On 28 April, the same two units found and uprooted
another 39 plants in Newtown. [@1] On the same day, officers in Nevis uprooted
and seized 255 plants, two to seven feet tall, from a lot in the Gingerland
area. [@1] Police put the week's total at 1,548 plants and seedlings. [@1]

Anti-Drug Unit head Inspector Travis Rogers said police had seized over 24,000
plants and seedlings so far that year in anti-drug efforts against marijuana.
[@1] Head of Operations Assistant Commissioner Terrance James said eradicating
growers was like a game of cat and mouse as growers sought more secluded areas
and better camouflage. [@1]
