Police in Fort-de-France presented the week's find as a fine catch after
cannabis plants were found at the home of a man in his thirties in the Dillon
neighbourhood. [@1] A 30-year-old resident of the Dillon neighbourhood in
Fort-de-France was stopped on Tuesday 26 April 2016 by a Fort-de-France police
team while allegedly trying to extort money by threatening with a firearm. [@1]
Officers then searched his home. [@1] The search uncovered a grow of 129
cannabis plants at his home. [@1]

Police found no evidence of genuine drug trafficking. [@1] The man admitted that
the cannabis was for his personal use and said he regularly supplied relatives
without taking any payment. [@1] He was left at liberty rather than held under
arrest. [@1] He was due to appear before the court on 27 June 2016. [@1]
