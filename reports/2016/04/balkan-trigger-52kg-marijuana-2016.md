Operation Balkan Trigger ran from 17 to 19 April 2016 across Bosnia and
Herzegovina, Croatia, the Former Yugoslav Republic of Macedonia, Montenegro,
Serbia and Slovenia. [@1] Some 5,000 officers were deployed during the 48-hour
operation. [@1] Police arrested 14 people during the operation targeting illicit
firearms in the Western Balkans. [@1] Around 43,000 people were stopped at
border crossing points, transit routes and hot spots, with more than two million
checks against INTERPOL databases. [@1] Officials from participating countries
worked from a coordination hub in Sarajevo, with a second hub at INTERPOL
headquarters in Lyon. [@1]

Police recovered nearly 40 firearms, six kg of explosives, 11 hand grenades and
1,300 pieces of ammunition. [@1] Police also recovered 52 kg of marijuana during
the operation. [@1] Police also recovered 14.5 kg of gold and EUR 250,000 in
cash and identified a stolen car. [@1]

INTERPOL said the operation was aimed at giving regional countries a base to
address weapons smuggling and providing a blueprint for other operations. [@1]
Information gathered will be analysed for links with other criminal or terrorist
activity, and seized weapons details were added to the iARMS firearms database.
