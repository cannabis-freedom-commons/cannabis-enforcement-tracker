Members of the Indonesian national police, the armed forces and the Aceh narcotics agency burned cannabis plants in the hills of Lamteuba village, Seulimeum district, Aceh Besar, on Friday 1 April. [@1] National police chief General Badrodin Haiti attended as Operation Bersinar Rencong 2016 of the Aceh police destroyed 189.5 hectares of fields spread across 23 sites in Aceh. [@1]

In a parallel action under the same operation, Lhokseumawe police officers destroyed 13 hectares of cannabis by burning in Teupin Rusep village, Sawang district, North Aceh, on the same day. [@2] The operation aimed to suppress cannabis circulation from Aceh across the archipelago. [@2]
