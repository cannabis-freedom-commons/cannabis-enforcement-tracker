On 28 April 2016, at about 9:40 a.m., a 47-year-old Mexican citizen drove a 1994 Freightliner tractor-trailer into the Otay Mesa cargo facility, declaring a shipment of furniture from Tijuana. [@1,2] Officers referred the truck for closer inspection and ran it through the port's imaging system, which showed anomalies in the load. [@1,2]

## 587 packages in boxes

Officers opened the trailer and found boxes stacked throughout, each holding large wrapped packages that field-tested positive for marijuana. [@1,2] In total they extracted 587 packages weighing 14,219 pounds (about 6.4 tonnes), which the agency valued at $7.1 million on the street. [@1,2] The truck and trailer were seized. [@2]

## Driver handed over

The driver was turned over to Homeland Security Investigations agents for further processing. [@1,2] The seizure was described as unusually large, though smaller than the record 17-tonne seizure of 2013 and a near-record 16-tonne seizure of 2015 at Southern California crossings. [@2]
