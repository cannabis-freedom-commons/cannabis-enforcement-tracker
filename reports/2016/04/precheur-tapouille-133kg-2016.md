French customs coastguards and OCRTIS Caribbean officers stopped a tapouille, a
local fishing boat, about 25 km north-west of Le Prêcheur in Martinique on 6
April 2016. [@1] The boat had left Saint Vincent and was suspected of carrying a
large consignment of cannabis herb for the Antillean market. [@1] The OCRTIS
Caribbean branch organised the operation under the Action de l'État en Mer, with
the police services of Saint Vincent and Saint Lucia and Britain's National
Crime Agency. [@1]

An aircraft of the regional coastguard directorate located the boat, then the
Pointe-à-Pitre coastguard patrol boat Pitera, the two rigid inflatables Tarpon
and Macouba from Le Marin and Fort-de-France, and the helicopter Racam made the
interception. [@1] The five people on board threw bales of the drug into the sea
as the coastguards approached. [@1] Officers recovered about twenty bales of
cannabis herb weighing 133 kg in total. [@1]

The five people on board, nationals of Saint Vincent, Grenada and Dominica, were
placed in police custody in Fort-de-France. [@1] Their custody could last up to
96 hours and the judicial investigation, followed by the specialised
interregional court, was entrusted to the OCRTIS Caribbean branch based in
Fort-de-France. [@1] La 1ère's account confirms that the recovered packages
contained cannabis herb. [@2]
