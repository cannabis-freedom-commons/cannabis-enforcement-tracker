Maldives police and customs arrested five men, two women and a 16-year-old boy
on drug-smuggling charges after four operations over a weekend that brought
olive-oil-like hashish oil and heroin from India. [@1] Police said the
operations found 24.9 kilos of hashish oil and 4.76 kilos of heroin in total.
[@1] The Criminal Court had remanded the eight suspects it counted to police
custody, although the police arrest tally includes nine people. [@1]

On a Friday, customs officers at the airport found 7.27 kilos of hashish oil and
1.09 kilos of heroin in the luggage of a 31-year-old woman who had flown in from
Trivandrum on a Maldivian airline flight. [@1] After questioning her, police
arrested a 21-year-old man and the 16-year-old boy, both of whom had criminal
records. [@1] The next day, customs found 10 kilos of hashish oil and 1.98 kilos
of heroin in the luggage of a 28-year-old man who arrived on an Air India flight
from Bangalore. [@1] Police also arrested a man who came to the airport to
collect that consignment and later a third suspect in Hulhumalé, the capital's
suburb. [@1]

Raids on two apartments where two of the suspects lived turned up a further 7.07
kilos of hashish oil and 1.09 kg of heroin, and police arrested two more people
there, including a woman. [@1] The newspaper called the four busts together the
largest haul of the year so far. [@1] Maldives Independent noted that police and
courts have a history of releasing suspected traffickers and that convictions in
high-profile trafficking cases are rare. [@1]
