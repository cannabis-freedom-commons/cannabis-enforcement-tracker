Police detained the truck driver, identified only as Ş.A. [@1] In the second
operation, officers detained five suspected dealers identified only by the
initials R.C., O.D., M.D., M.Y. and A.A. [@1] Together, the two operations led
to six detentions. [@3]

Officers of the Police Directorate stopped a wheat-loaded truck with licence
plate 34 L 6010 at a road checkpoint after finding it suspicious. [@2] A search
of the truck found 257 kilograms of esrar, the Turkish term for cannabis, hidden
inside the wheat. [@1] The truck was carrying a load of wheat used to conceal
the cannabis. [@1] In the city operation, officers seized 350 grams of esrar
described as ready for sale. [@1] That city operation was an early-morning
simultaneous action at five addresses targeting suspected sellers. [@1]

Police said the cannabis found in the truck was being carried from Diyarbakır to
Mersin. [@2] The accounts present the highway interception and the city
operation as two drug operations in Şanlıurfa. [@1]
