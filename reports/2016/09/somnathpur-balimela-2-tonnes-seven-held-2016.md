Odisha police seized about two tonnes of ganja valued at around two crore rupees near Somnathpur village under Balimela police limits in Malkangiri district on Tuesday. [@1,2] A Balimela patrol team noticed a group carrying a large consignment packed in sacks around 5 pm the previous evening and raided them on suspicion. [@1,2]

The carriers tried to flee, abandoning the sacks, but the team caught seven men and later identified the consignment as marijuana. [@1,2] Police named the arrested men as Lachhu Padhani, Mangalu Ganda, Mahadev Bhadra, Gyani Harijan, Tulasiram Tarki, Laket Bhadra and Budu Bhadra. [@1,2]
