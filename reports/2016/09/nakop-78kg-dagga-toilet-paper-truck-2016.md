Northern Cape police arrested a 46-year-old truck driver for dealing in dagga at
the Nakop Border Post. [@1] The arrest happened at about 11:00 on Tuesday, 20
September 2016. [@1] The driver was exporting toilet paper by truck from South
Africa to Namibia. [@2] The search took place at the Nakop border post near
Upington. [@3]

Officers searched a toolbox inside the truck and found 78 kg of dagga stashed
inside. [@1] Police put the dagga's estimated street value at almost R400,000.
[@1] Officers confiscated the dagga together with the truck and a cellphone.
[@3]

The suspect was expected to appear in the Upington Magistrate's Court soon. [@2]
Police said the investigation was continuing. [@1]
