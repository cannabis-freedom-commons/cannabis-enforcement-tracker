The Himachal Pradesh government ran a statewide Cannabis and Opium Eradication Campaign from 22 August to 5 September 2016, aiming to make the state free of both crops. [@1,2] Police destroyed the cannabis crop on 19,157.2 bighas of government land and 6,040.57 bighas of private land, about 25,198 bighas in total, or roughly 2,041 hectares at the Himachal measure. [@1] The state deployed the Sashastra Seema Bal paramilitary force with modern equipment for destruction in high-altitude regions. [@1]

The same release put the overall result at 18,850,152 cannabis-and-opium plants uprooted and 16,498,175 plants disposed of over 1,527.05 hectares, without separating the two crops, so only the cannabis-specific bigha tally is recorded above. [@1] One outlet's headline separately claimed 18.9 million cannabis and 16.5 million opium plants from that area, a far higher total the body of the reports does not reconcile. [@2]
