Operation Safe Streets, a police-led operation against illegal firearms,
ammunition and drugs in St Kitts and Nevis, was in its fourth day with its
marijuana eradication objective being met, according to the Royal St Christopher
and Nevis Police Force. [@1]

By 23 September, the fourth day of the operation, teams had visited 92 sites
under cultivation and uprooted and seized more than 25,000 marijuana plants
ranging from seedlings to over four feet tall. [@1] Superintendent (Ag) Travis
Rogers, Divisional Commander 'B', put the estimated street value of the seizure
at more than $11,000,000 Eastern Caribbean dollars. [@1]

Assistant Commissioner of Police Andre Mitchell, who had responsibility for
crime, said the marijuana trade of cultivation and sale contributed to gang
violence in the Federation, and that removing it was meant to reduce that
violence. [@1] The police force said the operation's other objective was to take
illegal firearms, ammunition, drugs and instruments usable in crime out of
communities. [@1]

SKN PULSE published the same 23 September police release, with the same figure
of more than 25,000 plants from 92 sites and the same EC$11 million valuation.
[@2]

In a statement dated 28 September, police said the operation's searches to that
point had found more than 31,000 marijuana plants under cultivation uprooted and
seized from over 75 fields, some plants as tall as eleven feet, along with a
very large quantity of dried and cured marijuana and a quantity ready for sale.
[@3] On Tuesday 27 September a combined search team executing a warrant in Boyds
Village seized a .380 pistol and five rounds of matching ammunition, and Kenesha
Whattley and Chadin Buchanan were arrested, charged and cautioned over the
firearm and ammunition, not cannabis. [@3]

On Friday 30 September a combined team burned more than 31,000 marijuana plants
seized in Operation Safe Streets together with a large quantity of plants from
earlier St Kitts Nevis Defence Force eradication operations. [@4] The plants on
the fire ranged in size from one foot to eleven feet, and Rogers said it was one
of the largest burns the force had done. [@4]
