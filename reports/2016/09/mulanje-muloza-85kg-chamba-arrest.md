Police in Mulanje district in southern Malawi arrested a 36-year-old man, John
Namowa, for being found in possession of Cannabis sativa, locally known as
chamba. [@1] Officers from Muloza police post arrested him at his house during a
sweeping exercise on a Tuesday. [@1]

Mulanje police public relations officer Gresham Ngwira said the officers had
received a tip that the suspect was keeping chamba at his house. [@1] A search
of the house found 17 plastic bags of Indian hemp, each weighing about 5
kilograms, the weight the headline gives as 85 kilograms. [@1] The seized
cannabis was sent to Bvumbwe Research Station for examination. [@1]

Namowa, who comes from Namani village under Traditional Authority Njema in
Mulanje district, was to be arraigned on a charge of being found in possession
of Indian hemp under the Dangerous Drugs Act. [@1]
