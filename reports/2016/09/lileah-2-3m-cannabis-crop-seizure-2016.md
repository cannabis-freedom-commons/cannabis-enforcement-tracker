Officers of the Western Drug Investigation Services executed a drug-related search warrant at a private residence at Lileah, on Tasmania's North-West Coast, at about 10.30am on 7 September 2016. [@1] They seized cannabis plants and cannabis product with an approximate street value of $2.3 million, along with a large sum of money found at the premises. [@1] A radio report the next day described the haul as a large quantity of marijuana and cash alleged to be part of a commercial drug business, with Detective Inspector Kim Steven of Tasmania Police interviewed about the bust and its estimated $2.3 million street value. [@2]

A 52-year-old man was interviewed over the seized items, and police said he would be charged with trafficking in a controlled substance and was bailed to appear in the Burnie Magistrates Court at a later date. [@1]
