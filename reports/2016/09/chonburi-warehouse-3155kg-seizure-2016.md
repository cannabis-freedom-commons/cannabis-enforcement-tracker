Thai police seized 3,155 kilograms (6,940 pounds) of marijuana from a warehouse in Chonburi, about 60 kilometres southeast of Bangkok, in the country's biggest cannabis seizure since May 2014. [@1] Border Patrol Police official Prapan Chan-em said the raid happened on Sunday after a tip-off the previous month that marijuana was being smuggled from Laos into northeastern Thailand. [@1]

## The smuggling front

Police arrested a 44-year-old Thai man and a 64-year-old Taiwanese man at the warehouse, which Prapan said had operated for nine years as a drug-smuggling front jointly run by Thais and Taiwanese. [@1] The cannabis was vacuum-sealed at the site and labelled as rubber gloves for shipment to Australia. [@1] The previous record seizure, about five tonnes, was found in May 2014 at a warehouse in Pathum Thani after the same Laos route was used for shipments to Europe and Australia. [@1]
