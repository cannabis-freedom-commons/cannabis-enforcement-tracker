London police officers arrested Rocco John Ritchie, the 16-year-old son of the
singer Madonna and the film director Guy Ritchie, after cannabis was found on
him. [@1] A Metropolitan Police spokesman said the arrest followed a stop and
search by officers from Camden borough at about 5.15pm on Wednesday, 28
September 2016, at Steeles Mews North. [@3] Neighbours who called the police
said the teenager was “smoking or using drugs” near his home in Primrose Hill, a
district of north London. [@1]

Officers saw cannabis residue on his clothes and found a quantity of the drug
when they searched him. [@1] The Metropolitan Police said the youth was arrested
for possession of cannabis. [@3] He was taken to a north London police station
and later bailed. [@2] Camden borough’s youth offending team then took over his
case. [@1]

The police spokesman said he was dealt with through Triage, a scheme the force
said uses several strands of engagement and education to prevent young people
re-offending. [@3] Madonna said the matter was private, telling the Associated
Press: “I love my son very much. I will do whatever I can to give him the
support that he needs, and I ask that you respect our privacy at this time.”
[@3] The arrest happened on 28 September but only became public in late
November. [@1] Ritchie was born on 11 August 2000, and his parents Madonna and
Guy Ritchie were together from 2000 to 2008. [@1]
