İzmir's anti-drug police acted after receiving a tip that 26-year-old M.C. and
his 46-year-old stepfather E.Ö. had brought drugs to the city and were storing
them at a workplace in Karabağlar district. [@1] Officers detained both men and
sent them to the courthouse. [@1] M.C. was remanded in custody, while E.Ö. was
released to be tried without detention. [@1]

Police searched the workplace and the truck parked in front of it with the
assistance of a narcotics dog. [@1] In bags concealed inside the truck's four
normally non-load-bearing tyres and its spare tyre, officers found 113 kilograms
of cannabis. [@1] Officers also found 2 kilograms of cannabis in a cardboard box
inside the workplace, bringing the total seizure to 115 kilograms. [@1]

The tip had identified both the shipment into İzmir and the Karabağlar workplace
as its storage point. [@1] The operation was carried out by teams of the
Anti-Drug Branch Directorate. [@1] The truck was empty at the time, so the four
tyres searched were those that do not touch the ground when unloaded, plus the
spare tyre. [@1]
