Police in Karamürsel, a district of Kocaeli province on Turkey's Marmara coast,
detained two men in an operation aimed at street sellers. [@1] The suspects were
identified by their initials as F.K., 54, and M.O., 50. [@1] Teams from the
Kocaeli provincial police department and the Karamürsel district police
department carried out the operation. [@1]

During a search of M.O.'s home, officers found 1.5 kg of powdered cannabis — 1
kilo 500 gram toz esrar — and a forged identity document. [@1] Toz esrar is the
Turkish term for cannabis in powdered form. [@1] The forged identity document
was found in the same house search as the cannabis. [@1]

The two men were held in police custody, or gözaltı, before being sent to the
courthouse after processing at the police station. [@1] An adliye is the
courthouse where Turkish prosecutors and judges handle a detained suspect's
case. [@1]
