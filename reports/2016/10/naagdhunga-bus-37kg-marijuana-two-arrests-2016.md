Police arrested two people on Saturday, 1 October 2016, who were carrying 3.7 kg
of marijuana on a bus travelling to Kathmandu from Dhading. [@1] Security
personnel found the contraband on the passenger bus registered Ba 3 Kha 827
during a security check at Naagdhunga in Chandragiri Municipality-3 of
Kathmandu. [@1] Police said they were looking into the case. [@1]

The same report counted two other young men arrested on Friday with 200 grams of
an illegal drug at Naya Baneshwor in Kathmandu Metropolitan City-34, a separate
case. [@1] It identified them as Ram Krishna Tamang, 22, from Nuwakot and
Dipendra Gamal, 23, from Makawanpur, without saying which of the two cases
either man was held in. [@1]
