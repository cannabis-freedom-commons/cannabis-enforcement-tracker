Investigators from the Los Angeles County Sheriff's Department Marijuana Dispensary Task Force served a search warrant at about 8 a.m. on 19 October 2016 at a small strip mall in the 27000 block of Sierra Highway in Canyon Country, after a civilian reported possible illegal narcotics activity. [@1,2] Inside several of the mall's units they found an elaborate indoor marijuana grow with carbon filters and air conditioning meant to hide the smell. [@1,2]

Officers seized 2,484 marijuana plants at various stages of growth, 200 pounds (about 91 kg) of dried marijuana buds and 50 pounds (about 23 kg) of THC-infused edible products such as cookies. [@1,2] The department valued the growing plants at $6.21 million and the dried buds and edibles at $500,000. [@1,2]

Three men were detained at the scene, but two were released; 28-year-old George Tunis of Los Angeles was arrested on suspicion of possessing marijuana for sale. [@1,2] He was booked at the Santa Clarita Valley station, posted $30,000 bail within about two and a half hours and was due in court in San Fernando that Friday. [@1,2]
