Police detained 61 people during the month-long Diyarbakır enforcement roundup,
and courts remanded 30 of those referred to the judicial authorities in custody.
[@1] The roundup comprised 50 separate operations conducted in Diyarbakır over
the previous month. [@1] Teams of the Provincial Police Directorate's
anti-narcotics branch carried out 30 of the operations across the province in
one month against people engaged in drug trading. [@1]

Those 30 operations seized 1 tonne 213 kilograms of kubar esrar, 15 kilograms of
toz esrar, 5,585 cannabis roots and one pistol. [@1] Twenty separate operations
targeting street dealers in the same period seized 96 kilograms of kubar esrar,
265 drug pills, two pistols and three hunting rifles. [@1] The reported combined
result for all 50 operations was 1 tonne 324 kilograms of kubar esrar and 5,585
cannabis roots. [@1]

The 5,585 cannabis roots were reported as part of the same overall seizure total
alongside the cannabis. [@1] The 265 pills were described only as drug pills,
and the pistols and hunting rifles were reported alongside the cannabis finds.
