Calaveras County sheriff's deputies raided old airport hangars outside San Andreas that had been turned into a cannabis growing and processing operation, arresting 35 workers and seizing 2.5 tons (about 2,270 kg) of marijuana worth about $10 million. [@1,2] The grow had been under investigation for about a month after residents reported increased traffic going in and out of the airport. [@1,2]

The owners held a county cultivation permit and had paid a $5,000 registration fee, but Sheriff Rick DiBasilio said the ordinance allowed growing only, not processing, manufacturing or testing. [@1,2] Ata Gonzalez of G FarmaBrands, which funded licensed growers in the county, called the bust politically motivated and said deputies destroyed 2.5 tons of legal cannabis from a harvesting centre used to dry, trim, cure and package flower without due process for the licensee. [@1,2]
