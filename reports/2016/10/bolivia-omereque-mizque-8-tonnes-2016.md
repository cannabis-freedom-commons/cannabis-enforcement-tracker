Bolivia's anti-drug force FELCN seized eight metric tons of marijuana in raids across the Cochabamba region in October 2016. [@1,2] Several campesinos were arrested and crops were burned in the fields at various locations. [@1,2]

The daily La Razon named Omereque and Mizque, on the edge where the Cochabamba plateau drops toward the Amazon, as the sites of the major raids. [@1,2] Vice Minister for Social Defense and Controlled Substances Felipe Caceres said troops from the armed forces took part in the operations alongside the FELCN, an entity of the National Police. [@1,2]

The reports describe the zone as the country's key coca-growing area, where more coca-leaf growers were turning to cannabis. [@1,2]
