Pakistan Customs and the Maritime Security Agency (PMSA) foiled a bid to smuggle
what they called their biggest-ever cache of hashish, arresting nine smugglers
including three foreigners. [@1] PMSA Director General Rear Admiral Jameel Akhtar,
speaking at a press conference at PMSA headquarters alongside Gawadar Customs
Collector Saeed Akram, said the two agencies acted on a tipoff in the international
waters on the western boundary of Pakistan. [@1]

The boarding party recovered 2,400 kilograms of high-quality hashish from a
suspicious boat and arrested the nine accused, confiscating their boat. [@1] When the
smugglers saw the PMSA fast-response boat they tried to flee, and their boat caught
fire; PMSA officials first doused the fire and then made the arrests. [@1] The
accused were presented before the media. [@1]

The PMSA chief put the value of the recovered hashish at more than Rs 1,500 million
on the international market. [@1]
