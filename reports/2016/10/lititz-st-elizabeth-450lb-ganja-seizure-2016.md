Narcotics police conducting operations in Lititz, St Elizabeth searched a
dwelling house at about 10:00 pm on 14 October 2016. [@1] Officers found
approximately 450 pounds of ganja in an unfinished section of the house. [@1] No
arrest was made in connection with the seizure. [@1]

Police put the estimated street value of the seized ganja at approximately $2.1
million. [@1] The Jamaica Gleaner corroborated that close to 450 pounds of ganja
worth $2.1 million was seized in Lititz the previous evening and that no one was
arrested. [@2]

The Gleaner likewise reported that the drugs were found in an unfinished section
of a house and that no one was arrested. [@2]
