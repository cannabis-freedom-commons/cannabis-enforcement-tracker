Gendarmerie teams in Yozgat province detained two men, C.E. and T.E., in a
cannabis operation in Sorgun district. [@1] Sorgun is one of the province's
districts. [@1] The teams acted on information that people staying temporarily
in the district centre and its villages were manufacturing drugs and selling
them. [@1]

With sniffer dogs, officers searched three addresses belonging to the two men.
[@1] The searches turned up 1 kg 50 g of kubar esrar, a form of cannabis. [@2]
Officers also seized 5 g of hemp seed, four unlicensed hunting rifles and six
cartridges for the rifles. [@1] The provincial command described the operation
as part of its anti-drug work. [@1]

C.E. and T.E. were held for processing at the gendarmerie after their detention,
known in Turkey as gözaltı. [@1] They were then referred to the courthouse, or
adliye, where prosecutors and judges decide whether a detainee stays in custody.
[@2] The jandarma is Turkey's gendarmerie, which polices rural districts and
villages. [@1]
