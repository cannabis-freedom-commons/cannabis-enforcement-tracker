Gardaí from the Drugs and Organised Crime Bureau, supported by the Special Crime Task Force, seized cannabis herb with an estimated value of €2.7 million during an operation in Co. Meath on 25 October 2016. [@1] The discovery was made when detectives searched a number of vehicles in Ashbourne shortly after 3pm that Tuesday. [@2] The operation was part of ongoing investigations targeting organised criminal activity in the Dublin and Meath regions. [@1,2]

Three men aged 58, 41 and 35 were arrested and detained at Ashbourne and Finglas Garda stations. [@2] The 58-year-old was charged in connection with the seizure and was due before Trim District Court on 27 October 2016. [@1] The 41-year-old was charged with unconnected offences, while the third man was released without charge and a file was being prepared for the Director of Public Prosecutions. [@1]
