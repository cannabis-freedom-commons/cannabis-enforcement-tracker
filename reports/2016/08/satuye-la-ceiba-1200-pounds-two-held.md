In the early hours of a Tuesday, officers stopped a vehicle at the Satuyé police
post in La Ceiba and found 12 sacks of dry plant material treated as marijuana.
[@4] The police detained Walter Javier Ávila Juárez, 36, and Yadir Fernando
Montoya, 21. [@3] Ávila Juárez was the driver and Montoya was travelling with
him as his helper. [@4] The National Police, through the DPI and the preventive
police, made the seizure that morning in the Satuye sector of La Ceiba after
intelligence and surveillance. [@3] The two men had been under investigation
through intelligence and surveillance. [@2]

The police said they were in a white truck, plate AAJ-5647, carrying 12 sacks of
supposed marijuana of about 100 pounds each, 1,200 pounds in all, about 544
kilograms. [@3] Proceso Digital called the find what appeared to be the largest
marijuana seizure of the year so far, in the Satuye sector of La Ceiba,
Atlántida. [@1] The operation was carried out in the early hours of that
Tuesday. [@1] The men and the drug were sent to the prosecutor's office, to be
brought before the courts in La Ceiba. [@1]

The same day the Special Prosecutor's Office against Organised Crime, FESCCO,
filed a fiscal request against both men for illicit possession and trafficking
of drugs, to the detriment of the health of the population of Honduras. [@4] A
criminal trial court ordered judicial detention. [@4] The initial hearing was
set for 22 August. [@4]
