The Osun State command of Nigeria's National Drug Law Enforcement Agency (NDLEA) destroyed about 21.4 hectares of cannabis sativa growing at the Onikete farm settlement in Isokan Local Government Area, state commandant Sylvia Egwunwoke said in a Channels Television interview on 15 August 2016. [@1,2]

Residents of a nearby village alerted the agency to the settlement, Egwunwoke said, and no arrests were made. [@1,2] She said landowners who lease out their fields to cannabis growers would face the law, and that other farmland in the state had been identified for destruction while the crop was still young. [@1,2]
