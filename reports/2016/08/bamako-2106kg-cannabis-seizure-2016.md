Mali's customs Mobile Intervention Brigade (BMI), the customs' mobile
intervention unit, made a historic cannabis seizure in Bamako during the night
of 15 to 16 August 2016. [@1,2] The traffickers arrested in the operation were
Malian nationals. [@1,2] The cannabis was carried by a truck adapted for the
purpose. [@1] The Mali-registered truck was coming from Fana, a locality 120 km
from Bamako, when BMI agents found the carefully hidden load. [@1]

Officers at the Zantiguila post detected an approximately one-metre-deep cache
built into the floor of the vehicle because its exterior and interior lengths
did not match. [@1,2] The seizure comprised 1,254 bricks of cannabis weighing
2,106.88 kg as weighed by BMI agents. [@1,2] Customs put the market value of the
load at 3.5 billion FCFA. [@1,2] Customs chief Ibrahima Diakité called it the
largest drug seizure yet by the Malian customs administration. [@1,2]

The cannabis had been loaded in the Bobo Dioulasso sector in Burkina Faso. [@1,2]
The load was destined notably for Senegal and Guinea. [@1,2] A stock was deposited
at Sebénicoro to supply Guinea-Conakry and Senegal. [@1,2] People familiar with
the practice said such quantities were transported once or twice a month from
Burkina Faso. [@1]

Four suspected traffickers were arrested. [@2]
