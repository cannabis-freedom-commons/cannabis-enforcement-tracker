Acting on a tip-off, teams from the district gendarmerie command searched the
garden of 37-year-old A.S. on Yukarı Bekdemir Street in Bekdemir Mahallesi in
Kavak. [@1] The 37-year-old garden holder, identified only by the initials A.S.,
was still at large after the search, and the teams began work aimed at catching
him. [@1]

During that garden search, the officers found 22 cannabis plants standing
planted. [@1] The gendarmerie account records those 22 plants as seized in
Kavak. [@1] A second report of the same Kavak incident likewise says gendarmerie
teams seized 22 cannabis plants in Kavak. [@2]

That second account places the searched garden on Yukarı Bekdemir Street in
Bekdemir Mahallesi and likewise says the teams that found the 22 planted plants
had started efforts to catch the 37-year-old. [@2] It says the district
gendarmerie command teams acted after evaluating the tip-off and searched A.S.'s
garden at Bekdemir Mahallesi Yukarı Bekdemir Sokak. [@2]
