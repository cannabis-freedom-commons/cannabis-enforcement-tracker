Manai Roberts, 25, of Smith's, appeared in Bermuda's Magistrates' Court on 29
August 2016 charged with possessing more than 900g of cannabis. [@1] He faced
charges of possessing cannabis with intent to supply and of possessing $23,885
in cash representing the proceeds of criminal activity. [@1] Both charges
related to an incident in Paget on 26 August 2016 and were said to involve
902.7g of the controlled substance. [@1]

Prosecutors said that quantity of drugs could fetch between $12,350 and $15,200
if sold on the streets of Bermuda. [@1] He was not required to enter a plea
because the matter must be heard by the Supreme Court. [@1] Magistrate Khamisi
Tokunbo released him on $20,000 bail with a like surety and ordered him to
surrender his travel documents and report to Hamilton Police Station twice a
week. [@1]

The case was expected to appear before the Supreme Court in November. [@1]
