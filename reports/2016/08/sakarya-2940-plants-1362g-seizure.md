Sakarya İl Jandarma Komutanlığı, the Sakarya provincial gendarmerie command,
mounted drug operations in the responsibility zones of its Geyve and Karasu
district commands. [@1] Gendarmerie teams acted after tip-offs were received and
intelligence information was assessed. [@1] The series comprised operations in
six separate areas carried out on different dates. [@2] The operations were
described as part of the fight against unlawful cannabis cultivation. [@1] A
judicial investigation was opened against five suspects in connection with the
incidents. [@1]

Officers seized a total of 2,940 cannabis plants, described as kök kenevir, in
the operation series. [@1] Officers also seized 1,362 grams of harvested
cannabis, described as kubar esrar, in the same operations. [@2] The reports use
kök kenevir for cannabis plants and kubar esrar for harvested herbal cannabis.
[@1]

The six operations together produced the combined 2,940-plant and 1,362-gram
totals. [@1]
