Algeria's National Gendarmerie arrested three alleged drug traffickers and seized 692.7 kilograms of cannabis resin and a car in the communes of Maghnia and Remchi, in Tlemcen province about 540 kilometres west of Algiers, on 7 August 2016. [@1] The Defence Ministry announced the operation in a statement carried by the state news agency APS as part of what it called the fight against organised crime. [@1]

Maghnia and Remchi sit in the 2nd Military Region near the Moroccan border, on the corridor traffickers use to move Moroccan hashish east into Algeria. [@1] The statement gave no names, no charges and no account of what happened to the three men after their arrest. [@1]
