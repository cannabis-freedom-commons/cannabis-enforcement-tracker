Hillsborough County sheriff's deputies shot dead Levonia Riggins, 22, during a SWAT raid
on a house at 1432 Longwood Loop in the Clair-Mel area near Tampa around 8 a.m. on
30 August 2016. [@1,2] Riggins had been the subject of a month-long drug investigation,
and police said they used the SWAT team because of his criminal record and reports that
he owned guns. [@1,2] When deputies arrived, everyone inside came out except Riggins, who
had been commanded to leave several times; Deputy Caleb Johnson found him in a bedroom
and fired one shot after perceiving him as an immediate threat. [@1] Riggins died later
at Tampa General Hospital, and the sheriff's office later said it appeared he had been
unarmed. [@1,2]

The raid followed undercover marijuana purchases from Riggins earlier that summer, and a
family friend said investigators recovered about two grams of marijuana from his body, a
figure the sheriff's office had not confirmed. [@1,2] The killing set off days of
protests in the Clair-Mel area, with demonstrators blocking intersections and five people
arrested. [@2] The Hillsborough State Attorney's Office and a sheriff's internal team
were investigating the shooting. [@1,2]
