On 17 August 2016 the Ontario Provincial Police executed a search warrant at a greenhouse operation on the 1500 block of Essex County Road 34 at Ruthven, in the municipality of Kingsville. [@1] Officers of the Essex County street-crimes unit and the Organized Crime Enforcement Bureau's drug enforcement unit seized about 1,700 marijuana plants, which police valued at $1.7 million. [@1] Two men from the Toronto area were arrested in the raid. [@1]

Essex County detachment commander Inspector Glenn Miller said the seizure at the Ruthven greenhouse complex showed officers working with the public to find unlicensed criminal grow operations across Essex County. [@1] No charges or later court outcomes for the two men were reported.

## Greenhouse grow

The plants grew inside a greenhouse complex rather than in open fields, and the published photograph shows cut plants piled beside the emptied greenhouse. [@1] Indoor and greenhouse grows of this kind need constant heat and light, which is one reason they draw police attention through utility use and tips. [@1]
