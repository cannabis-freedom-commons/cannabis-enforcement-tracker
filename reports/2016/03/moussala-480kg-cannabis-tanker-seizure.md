Officers of the Moussala customs office seized 480 kilograms of cannabis,
described as chanvre indien, from a tanker truck on Monday 28 March 2016. [@1,2]
The truck's attendant fled when officers carried out the check at the border
with Mali, and no arrest in the cannabis seizure was reported. [@1,2] Officers
seized the vehicle that had been carrying the cannabis. [@1,2]

The Moussala post covers the southern Dakar-Bamako road corridor. [@1,2] The
cannabis had been carefully hidden inside compartments normally used to hold
hydrocarbons. [@1,2]

The account was issued by the customs administration's public-relations and
communications office. [@1,3] The customs administration said its security mission
kept it committed to defending the national economy and protecting public
health. [@1,2] The same statement separately reports 70 pellets of cocaine seized
at Mpack, a different drug seizure whose weight is not counted in this cannabis
report. [@1,3]
