Officers from the Organised Financial Crime Agency of New Zealand and Wellington
District police teams executed ten search warrants on 31 July 2013 after a
three-month investigation, Operation Hive, into a drug-dealing group based in
Ascot Park, Porirua. [@1] At seven properties they found separate cannabis
growing operations, seizing more than 100 mature plants and a substantial
quantity of dried cannabis being prepared for sale. [@1]

Detectives also recovered methamphetamine, evidence of steroid importing and
selling, a shotgun and three air rifles. [@1] Six properties worth $2.5 million
were restrained under the Criminal Proceeds (Recovery) Act 2009, including a
Titahi Bay boat house police believed coordinated the operation; cannabis was
also found growing in houses the group had rented for the purpose. [@1]

## Sentences

Police later said Hive had targeted the organised distribution of
methamphetamine and the commercial cultivation and sale of cannabis across
Porirua, Wellington and the Hutt Valley. [@2] Mason Tobin, 28, of Churton Park
pleaded guilty to nine cannabis charges and was jailed for three and a half
years in the Wellington District Court on 29 January 2016. [@2]

His father Joseph Mason Tobin, described by the judge as the key player in the
family growing operation, was jailed for five and a half years on 16 March
2016 after pleading guilty to cultivating, selling and offering cannabis for
sale, alongside methamphetamine and firearm charges. [@3]
