Vanuatu police drug squad officers seized marijuana plants from a house in the
Independence Park area of Port Vila. [@1] An Australian national was arrested in
connection with the raid. [@1] The house, next to Vila East School, had been
under police surveillance for two weeks. [@1]

Police said the building was used as a marijuana nursery, with the plants in
pots left to grow to full maturity. [@1] Police found about 60 plants, grown on
coconut husks inside flower pots. [@1] The plants were taken to the Central
Police station in Port Vila and then moved to another location. [@1]

Detectives were examining whether the raid was connected to an alleged illicit
drug manufacturing lab found behind Mele Beach on the outskirts of Port Vila,
where another Australian was arrested and released on bail. [@1] The
investigation continued with help from Australian Federal Police forensic
officers. [@1]
