A man interrupted an Easter egg hunt in Washington State on Saturday, saying his
roommates were trying to shoot him. [@1] Clark County sheriff's deputies arrived
at a home in the Salmon Creek area at about 6 p.m. after disturbance calls. [@2]
Sheriff's deputies traced the disturbance to a large-scale unlicensed marijuana
operation. [@1] The operation was headquartered in suburban Salmon Creek,
Washington, just north of the Oregon state line. [@1] Three Vancouver men,
Charles J. Yorkovich, Joseph Sullivan and Jordan Long, were arrested and booked
into the Clark County Jail. [@2] Joseph E. Sullivan, 24, and Jordan M. Long, 27,
were booked on felony drug warrants out of Missouri. [@1] Charles J. Yorkovich,
24, was charged with possession of a controlled substance with intent to
deliver. [@1]

Detectives with the Clark County Regional Drug Task Force executed a search
warrant at the house and found more than 45 pounds (about 20.4 kilograms) of
processed marijuana. [@2] The sheriff's office put the street value of the
marijuana at more than $202,000. [@1] Detectives also seized more than $108,000
in cash and four vehicles. [@1]

The sheriff's office said the operation appeared to be distributing marijuana
across the country. [@1] No guns were found. [@1]
