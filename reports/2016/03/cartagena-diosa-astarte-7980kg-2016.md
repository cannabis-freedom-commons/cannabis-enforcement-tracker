Spain's Tax Agency intercepted the sailing yacht Diosa Astarte 93 miles south-east of Cartagena at 1.30 p.m. on 20 March 2016. [@1] The over-15-metre Spanish-flagged sloop was carrying 320 bales of hashish resin, which on weighing totalled 7,980 kilos. [@1] The agency's 2016 annual report lists the Diosa Astarte sloop among its key Customs Surveillance activities in international waters. [@2]

## Aerial detection, four arrests

The operation, codenamed Sea, began when the agency's aerial surveillance assets spotted the yacht sailing without a defined course in an area commonly used for loading narcotics. [@1] The patrol vessel Colimbo IV from the Cartagena maritime base boarded it, found the bales and canisters of diesel fuel, arrested the four crew members and towed the yacht to Cartagena. [@1] The detainees, two Spanish nationals, one Bolivian and one Moroccan, were brought with the yacht and the drugs before Central Investigating Court No. 2 of the National High Court. [@1] The agency estimated the black-market value of the seizure at more than 12.5 million euros. [@1]
