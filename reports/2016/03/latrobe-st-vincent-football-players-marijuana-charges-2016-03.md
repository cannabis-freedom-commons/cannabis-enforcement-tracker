Two former St. Vincent College football players waived their right to hearings
on felony marijuana charges in Latrobe, Pennsylvania, and hope to strike plea
agreements. [@1] The two are 22-year-old Renny LaRue-Holloman of Pittsburgh and
21-year-old Joey Milhomme of Hollywood, Florida. [@1] The case was reported in
Latrobe, Pennsylvania. [@1]

State police say both men fled onto a porch roof when an apartment was raided on
February 27. [@1] Police found 10 pounds of marijuana inside the apartment. [@1]
Police also found $1,370 in cash and a digital scale inside the apartment. [@1]

LaRue-Holloman has withdrawn from St. Vincent College, a private Catholic school
about 40 miles east of Pittsburgh. [@1] Milhomme remains a student of the
college but is suspended pending the outcome of the criminal case. [@1] Their
attorneys say both young men have not been in trouble before and waived the
Tuesday preliminary hearing as they try to resolve the case in a way that allows
them to have productive futures. [@1] The waiver of the Tuesday preliminary
hearing was reported on March 8, 2016. [@1]
