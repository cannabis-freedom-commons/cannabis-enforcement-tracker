In just under two weeks, the narcotics police seized two shipments of compressed ganja bound for overseas at Port Bustamante in Kingston. [@1]

About 9:15 a.m. on Thursday 25 July 2002, guards from Security Administrators Ltd. joined narcotics police searching a 40-foot container at Kingston Wharves and found 90 packages of ganja weighing 527 pounds (about 239 kilograms) hidden in a false compartment in the roof. [@1] No one was arrested. [@1]

About two weeks earlier, officers at the port's container station searched another 40-foot container and found compressed ganja concealed in tins and boxes among 1,000 cases of syrup bound for the Netherlands. [@1] Members of the Contraband Enforcement Team had been examining export documents when they noticed the name of an exporter on the Customs Department's watch list, which prompted the thorough search. [@1] Again, the police reported no arrest. [@1]

The account gives no weight for the syrup-container find and no destination for the 527-pound shipment beyond overseas. [@1]
