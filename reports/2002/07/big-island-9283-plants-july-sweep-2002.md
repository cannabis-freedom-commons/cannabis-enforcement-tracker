Hilo vice officers ripped up 9,283 marijuana plants ranging from seedlings to eight feet tall in a five-day eradication effort that ended Friday 19 July 2002. [@1] The plants grew on 609 separate plots in the Puna, Hilo, North Hilo and Hamakua districts of East Hawaii. [@1] A month later the Star-Bulletin confirmed the seizure of 9,283 plants at 609 locations in East Hawaii. [@2]
