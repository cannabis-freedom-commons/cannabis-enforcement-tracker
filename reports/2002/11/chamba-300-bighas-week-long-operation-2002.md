Chamba district police and the Chandigarh zonal unit of the Narcotics Control Bureau
destroyed about 300 bighas of cannabis plantation in the Kalhel, Jassourgarh and
Bharada-Lesuin belts of Chamba district during a week-long operation that concluded on
13 November 2002. [@1] The teams acted on joint intelligence gathered by the district
police and the NCB unit. [@1] The report names no arrests or seizures of people in the
operation. [@1]
