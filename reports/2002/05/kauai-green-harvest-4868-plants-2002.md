Kaua'i police pulled up 4,868 marijuana plants during a three-day Green Harvest operation covering areas throughout the island. [@1,2] The Advertiser dates the operation 21 to 23 May 2002. [@2] Officers from the state Department of Land and Natural Resources, the Hawaii Army National Guard and the Maui Police Department assisted the Kaua'i officers. [@1,2]
