U.S. Drug Enforcement Administration agents raided the Aiko Compassion Center near downtown Santa
Rosa on Wednesday 29 May 2002 and arrested two people. [@1] A DEA spokesman said agents searched
two addresses and seized marijuana, cash, a car and a weapon. [@1]

## Federal stance

San Francisco DEA spokesman Rich Meyer said the agency had not targeted marijuana clubs as such but
followed trafficking leads, adding that investigators would search a club if a lead took them there.
[@1] The raid came after the U.S. Supreme Court had ruled that distributing marijuana for medical
purposes remained illegal under federal law, while California voters had approved medical use in
1996. [@1]

## Arrests and local context

Authorities declined to name the arrested men because a federal judge sealed the case, but Santa
Rosa attorney Rich Ingram identified his client Edward Bierling and said Dan Nelson was also
charged; both were due in San Francisco federal court the next day. [@1] Witness Mark Nabavi, who
ran a print shop next door, said at least six agents entered around 10:45 a.m. and recorded
license plates. [@1] Ernest Knapp of the Sonoma Alliance for Medical Marijuana said the center
served more than 100 people. [@1]
