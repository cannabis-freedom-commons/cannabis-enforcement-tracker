On 30 May 2002 Narcotics Control Bureau officers led by Superintendent D.K. Ghosh intercepted a
Maruti carrying 75 kg of ganja in Hooghly district and arrested two men. [@1] The Times of India
said one of the detainees was a repeat offender who had turned to the trade after an earlier jail
term. [@1]

Bureau sources told the paper that consignments were being driven from Nagaland via Guwahati,
Siliguri and Berhampore toward receivers around Nabadwip and markets in Kolkata and the Hooghly
industrial belt. [@1] They described the cannabis as high-grade “Manipuri ganja” cultivated and
processed near Dimapur, fetching about Rs 2,500 per kg in markets they tracked. [@1] The report
does not name the suspects, the exact road location or any court filing. [@1]
