Big Island police confiscated 2,973 marijuana plants ranging from seedlings to five feet tall during a three-day operation that ended Wednesday 22 May 2002. [@1,2,3] Agents from the US Drug Enforcement Administration and personnel from the Hawaii Army National Guard counter-drug program worked alongside Big Island officers. [@1,2,3]

The sweep covered Umauma in North Hilo; Keaukaha, Panaewa and Honomu in South Hilo; Glenwood, Kurtistown, Mount View and Pahoa in Puna; and Waimea in South Kohala. [@1] It was part of the Hawaii County Police Department's continuing Counter-Cannabis Field Operation. [@1,2,3] No arrests were reported in any of the three accounts. [@1,2,3]
