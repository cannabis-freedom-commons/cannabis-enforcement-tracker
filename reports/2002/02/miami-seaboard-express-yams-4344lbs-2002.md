U.S. Customs inspectors seized 4,344 pounds (1,970 kg) of marijuana hidden in a container
of yams aboard the container ship Seaboard Express at the Port of Miami on 28 February
2002.[@1,2] A drug-sniffing dog alerted inspectors as the shipment was being taken off the
ship, which had arrived that day from Kingston, Jamaica.[@1,2]

More than 100 yam boxes held plastic-and-tape-wrapped bricks of marijuana, agents
said.[@1] The load was valued at more than $4 million wholesale.[@1,2] The Reuters account
names no arrests.[@1]
