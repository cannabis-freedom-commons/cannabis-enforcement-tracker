Lebanese police seized 2,500 kilograms of hashish in raids on warehouses in the
village of Barga in the Bekaa Valley on 1 February 2002. [@1] Canada's
Immigration and Refugee Board records the operation in a research response on
organized crime and the drug trade in Lebanon, citing Associated Press
reporting of the same day. [@1]

The response places the raid in a year when cannabis cultivation in the valley
was rising even as eradication continued, and notes that convicted drug
traffickers in Lebanon faced up to 15 years in jail. [@1]
