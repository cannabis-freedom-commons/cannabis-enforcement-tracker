Police stopped and searched a car on the A1 near Hillsborough at 11am on Thursday
morning and found what was believed to be cannabis resin with an estimated street
value of £750,000. [@1] One man was arrested and was assisting police with their
enquiries. [@1]

The report presented the stop as the latest in a series of cannabis finds in
Northern Ireland, recalling the Navan warehouse seizure and a Belfast holdall find
from April. [@1]
