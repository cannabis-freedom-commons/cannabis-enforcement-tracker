Police recovered a holdall containing cannabis resin with an estimated street value
of £200,000 in Glengall Street, Belfast, on the evening of Thursday 25 April
2002. [@1] A later report described the same-day Belfast find as worth over
£200,000. [@2]

A man and a woman were arrested over the holdall. [@1,2] The woman was released on
police bail, while the 26-year-old man was expected to appear at Laganside
Magistrates' Court on Saturday 27 April charged with possession and intent to
supply a Class B drug. [@1]
