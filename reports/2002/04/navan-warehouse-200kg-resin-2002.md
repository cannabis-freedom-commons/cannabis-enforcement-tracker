Gardaí raided a warehouse at Navan in County Meath on Thursday 25 April 2002 and
recovered two hundred kilos of cannabis resin. [@1] A later report put the haul at
over 200 kilos with an estimated value of €2.5 million (£1.5 million). [@1,2]

Two men from Belfast were among four people detained in the warehouse search. [@1]
Two men were arrested over the seizure. [@1,2]

## Cross-border case

The Navan raid was reported alongside a separate cannabis seizure in Belfast on the
same day, in which a man and a woman were arrested. [@1] That Belfast find is
recorded as its own report.
