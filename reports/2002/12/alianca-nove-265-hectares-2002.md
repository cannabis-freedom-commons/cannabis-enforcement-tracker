The Polícia Federal said Operation Aliança Nove, run with Paraguay’s Departamento de Operações
Antidrogas (Dinar), destroyed at least 265 hectares of cannabis plantations on the Paraguayan side of
the border with Ponta Porã, Mato Grosso do Sul, by 11 December 2002. [@1] Work began on 4 December
in Amambay department, and Perfil News reported it ended the day before the 12 December story was
published. [@1]

## What authorities destroyed

The Federal Police said teams cleared 141 plots, burned 74 camps and 27 presses, and seized three
crop sprayers. [@1] Agents incinerated 5,120 kg of chopped marijuana, 5,017 kg of pressed marijuana
and 640 kg of seeds at the sites. [@1] The agency estimated the eradication removed about 583 tonnes
of raw cannabis from the market, based on expected yield per hectare. [@1]

## Same operation, border controls

While Aliança Nove was under way, Brazilian police on the border announced a record 12.7-tonne
marijuana seizure in Tacuru when a wood truck was stopped on 11 December; the driver was arrested.
[@2] Ámbito described that haul as part of the same “Alianza 9” binational border operation with
Paraguayan officers. [@2] Perfil’s account of the plantation phase does not say whether cultivators
were caught in Amambay. [@1]
