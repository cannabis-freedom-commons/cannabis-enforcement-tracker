Big Island police destroyed 4,629 marijuana plants ranging from seedlings to eight feet tall in a five-day eradication effort that ended Friday 27 September 2002. [@1] The plants grew on 155 plots in Puna, North and South Hilo, Hamakua and North and South Kohala. [@1,2,3] Personnel from the Honolulu and Maui police departments, the Hawaii National Guard and the US Drug Enforcement Administration joined the Big Island officers. [@1,3] Police reported no arrests or major incidents during the operation. [@1,3]
