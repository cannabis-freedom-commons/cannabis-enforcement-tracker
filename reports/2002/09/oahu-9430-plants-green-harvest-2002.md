Honolulu police seized 9,430 marijuana plants on Oahu public lands in a five-day Green Harvest operation that ended Friday 13 September 2002. [@1,2] Detective Emilio Laganse of the Honolulu police narcotics detail called the haul "better than average" and said most of it stood on state land in remote mountainous areas reachable mainly by hikers and hunters. [@1,2] The plants ranged from seedlings to 8 feet tall. [@1]

The sweep was run by the Honolulu police Narcotics/Vice Division with the state Department of Land and Natural Resources, the Hawaii National Guard and the US Drug Enforcement Administration, joined by officers from Maui and Hawaii counties. [@1] Routine surveillance and tips from hikers and hunters led officers to the plots, and Laganse said the sweeps also protect the public after growers threatened hikers and hunters in the past. [@1] Neither account names any arrests. [@1,2]
