By Saturday 3 August 2002, more than 50,000 dunums (about 5,000 hectares) of hashish
fields had been destroyed in the Baalbek-Hermel area, on the eleventh consecutive day of
the eradication campaign's second phase. [@1] Members of the Anti-Drug Bureau, the Internal
Security Forces and the Lebanese Army carried out the operation jointly with Syrian security
forces in Lebanon. [@1] Judicial Police commander Brig. Samir Sobh, speaking to reporters in
a field in the Shleefa-Iaat area of Baalbek, said no security incidents had been reported so
far. [@1]

About 2,000 dunums of cannabis had been destroyed in the North and some 53,000 dunums in
the Bekaa, and about 5,000 dunums were being uprooted every day. [@1] Sobh said the bureau
had added 300 tractors and 200 workers to speed up the work, bringing the cost of the
campaign above LL200 million. [@1]
