Toronto drug officers from 13 and 53 Divisions battered down the door of the Toronto Compassion Centre at Bathurst and St Clair on 13 August, with their guns pointed. [@1] The club had supplied medical marijuana for five years to about 1,200 people living with AIDS, hepatitis C, multiple sclerosis and other conditions. [@1,2] Police seized the medicine for those patients and hauled the staff off to jail, according to an urgent alert the centre issued on 14 August. [@2]

In a busy week the club's operators spoke to between 500 and 600 patients, while Health Canada had granted only 806 people country-wide status at the time. [@1] Members received detailed analyses of strains from a message board rating sativa and indica content, information the writer called vital for daily health logs. [@1]

The club's two founders, Zack Noftolin and Warren Hitzig, were charged with possession and trafficking after the August 2002 raid. [@3] The charges were later dropped. [@3]
