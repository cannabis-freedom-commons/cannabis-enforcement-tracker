Iranian police seized about 4.5 tonnes of hashish in two separate raids near the border
with Afghanistan, the official IRNA news agency reported. [@1] Police intercepted a truck
carrying 3,200 kg of hashish at the Dogharun border region while it was en route to
Southeast Asia. [@1]

The second haul of more than 1,345 kg was raided at another border point in southern
Sistan-Baluchestan province. [@1] Iran is a main transit route for drugs smuggled from
Afghanistan toward Asia and Europe, with the authorities seizing close to 200 tonnes of
narcotics a year. [@1]
