Somalia's Transitional National Government burned about 1,000 kg of seized drugs in public
in front of the police academy in Mogadishu on Saturday, in an event attended by President
Abdiqassim Salad Hassan and other senior officials. [@1] Police chief Abdi Hasan Awale
Qeybdid said the drugs, mostly cannabis, had been seized in special operations over the
previous few months by anti-narcotics police. [@1] He said 280 people had so far been
detained on drugs charges. [@1]

## Where the drugs came from

Qeybdid said the drugs were found mostly in Lower Shabelle, Middle Shabelle, Lower Juba
and Middle Juba and in districts of the capital, and that officers had also seized about
40 kg of cannabis seed from India. [@1] He said the police believed some of the drugs were
in transit to other countries. [@1] He appealed to the international donor community for
help with training, drugs education and equipment. [@1]
