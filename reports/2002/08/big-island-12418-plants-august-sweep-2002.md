Hilo vice officers ripped up 12,418 marijuana plants ranging from seedlings to 10 feet tall in a five-day eradication effort that ended Friday 23 August 2002. [@1] The plants grew on 675 separate plots in the Puna, Hilo, North Hilo and Hamakua districts of East Hawaii. [@1,2,3] The Advertiser and the Star-Bulletin both confirmed the 12,418-plant, 675-plot totals the next day. [@2,3]
