Judicial Police commander Brigadier Samir Sobh said on Saturday 3 August 2002
that more than 50,000 dunums of hashish fields had been destroyed in the second
phase of Lebanon's cannabis eradication campaign, then in its eleventh day.
[@1,3] Anti-Drug Bureau officers, Internal Security Forces soldiers, the
Lebanese Army and Syrian security forces in Lebanon carried out the operation
together across the Baalbek-Hermel fields. [@1]

Sobh reported about 53,000 dunums destroyed in the Bekaa and 2,000 in the
North (5,500 hectares combined), with about 5,000 dunums uprooted daily and no
security incidents so far. [@1] The bureau added 300 tractors and 200 workers
to speed up the work after the campaign's cost passed 200 million Lebanese
pounds. [@1]

The drive had begun on 22 July with a government target of 60,000 dunums in
the Bekaa and 450 in the North, using paid local labourers and more than 200
rented trucks a day rather than the chemical spraying the planners had
rejected. [@2]
