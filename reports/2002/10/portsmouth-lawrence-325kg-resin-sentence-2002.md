A truck passenger was jailed for six years after pleading guilty to smuggling about 1 million pounds' worth of cannabis into Britain; the driver escaped punishment for lack of evidence. [@1]

Southampton Crown Court heard that Jamie Lawrence, a property developer based in Spain, arrived at Portsmouth Ferry Port in July on a ferry from St Malo as a passenger in the cab of a DAF tractor. [@1] Customs officers searched the vehicle and discovered 325 kilograms of cannabis resin under the bunk. [@1] Both men were arrested, but the driver was released on bail while Lawrence was charged. [@1]

Judge Iain Hughes told Lawrence that his attempted smuggling was on a commercial scale. [@1] A Customs spokesman said stopping Class A drugs was the service's main priority but added that it continued to uphold the law on other illegal drugs, including cannabis. [@1]
