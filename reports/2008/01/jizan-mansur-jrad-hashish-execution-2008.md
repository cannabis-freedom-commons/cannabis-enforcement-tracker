Saudi Arabia beheaded Yemeni resident Mansur Jrad in the southern province of
Jizan, which borders Yemen, after convicting him of smuggling hashish into the
kingdom. [@1,2] The Interior Ministry announced the execution by sword in
statements carried by the official Saudi Press Agency, and gave no amount for the
hashish. [@1,2]

The beheading was announced on 22 January 2008 alongside the executions of two
Saudis convicted of murder in Al-Ihsa; those are separate events left out of this
report. [@1] Saudi Arabia follows a strict interpretation of Islamic sharia law,
which provides for the death penalty for drug trafficking among other offences.
[@2]
