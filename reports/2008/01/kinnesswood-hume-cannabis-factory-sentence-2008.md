George Hume, 41, from Edinburgh, was jailed for four years for turning his
rented home in Kinnesswood, Kinross, into a cannabis factory. [@1] At Perth
Sheriff Court he admitted growing and supplying cannabis between 29 November
2006 and 15 March 2007. [@1]

The court heard Hume had acted as caretaker at the property to clear a £4,000
drug debt run up through his addiction to strong skunk cannabis, which cost him
about £40 a day while he lived on £80 a week in income support. [@1] His
solicitor said the arrangement was to last 14 weeks, with Hume directed in
watering the plants, and that he had now had a wake-up call about cannabis.
[@1]

Police raided the flat after a tip-off and found 316 plants growing in pots
plus 346 empty pots ready for mass production. [@1] The bedroom crop grew under
high-voltage lights behind thick plastic sheeting, running so hot it would have
shown as a thermal-imaging hotspot, though experts said the plants were
over-watered and poorly kept. [@1] The court heard they could have produced
cannabis worth £7,000, much more had they been healthy. [@1] Sheriff Michael
Fletcher noted Hume had previously served five years handed down by the High
Court in Edinburgh in 2001 for drug offences. [@1]
