French customs officers seized 2.4 tonnes of cannabis resin on 26 February 2008 near Hendaye, on France's border with Spain. [@1] The drug load was found on a truck registered in the Netherlands. [@1] Customs estimated its resale value at 4.96 million euros, and released photographs of the seized packages at Hendaye three days later. [@1]
