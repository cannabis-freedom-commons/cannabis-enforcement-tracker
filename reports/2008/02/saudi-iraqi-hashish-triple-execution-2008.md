Saudi Arabia beheaded Iraqi national Ahmed al-Khazali on Monday 4 February 2008 after convicting him of smuggling hashish into the kingdom, the Interior Ministry said in statements carried by the official Saudi Press Agency. [@1,2] No amount was given for the hashish. [@1,2]

Two other foreigners were beheaded the same day for trafficking opiates: Indian national Rashid Akbar Hussein Eddin and Pakistani national Wassee-Allah Rafee-Allah Khan. [@1,2] Their deaths brought the number of executions announced in Saudi Arabia that year to 23, after a record 153 people were put to death in 2007. [@2]

Drug trafficking is among the offences that can carry the death penalty in Saudi Arabia, alongside murder, rape, apostasy and armed robbery; executions are usually carried out in public by sword. [@2]
