Keith Brown, a council youth development officer, was stopped in transit at Dubai's main airport in September 2007 on his way back to England from a family visit in Ethiopia. [@1,2] Customs officers found 0.003 grams of cannabis, less than a grain of sugar, in the tread of his shoe; one account describes it as a cigarette stub stuck to the sole. [@1,2,3] Held in a cell with three other men, he was sentenced to four years in prison in February 2008; four years was then the usual sentence for possession in the emirate. [@1,3]

On 26 February 2008, the emirate's English-language paper 7Days reported that Brown, 43, had been pardoned and released early from prison. [@3]
