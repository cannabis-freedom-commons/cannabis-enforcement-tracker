The Bauchi command of Nigeria's National Drug Law Enforcement Agency arrested 14 suspected drug dealers and seized over 400 kg of cannabis in a series of raids on hideouts across the state. [@1] State commandant Sule Momodu paraded the suspects the day the report was published and said the raids had recovered more than 400 kg of the drug. [@1]

Momodu said one of the suspects had been arrested and charged in 2002 but jumped bail and returned to dealing. [@1] The report names no other suspects and gives no charges for the current arrests. [@1]
