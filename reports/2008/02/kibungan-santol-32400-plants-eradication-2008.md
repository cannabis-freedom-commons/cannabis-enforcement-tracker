Combined anti-narcotics and police operatives uprooted and destroyed at least 54 million pesos worth of marijuana plants near the boundary of Benguet and La Union in early February 2008. [@1]

Superintendent Joker Cuanso, the Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency director for Cordillera, said about 32,400 plants were destroyed: 24,000 worth 40 million pesos in Sitio Apana, Badeo, Kibungan, Benguet, and 8,400 worth 14 million pesos in Sitio Cutali, Sapda, Santol, La Union. [@1] Cuanso credited tip-offs from concerned residents for the successful operations. [@1]
