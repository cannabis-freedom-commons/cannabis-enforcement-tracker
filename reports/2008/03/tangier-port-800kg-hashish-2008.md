Moroccan police at the northern port of Tangier seized 800 kg of chira, cannabis resin,
estimated at 3 million euros, from a vehicle loaded with furniture, the Moroccan news
agency MAP reported. [@1] The drug was aimed at the French market and was found in a van
bound for the southern Spanish city of Algeciras. [@1] The 22-year-old driver and a
21-year-old passenger were arrested, MAP added, without giving their nationalities. [@1]

## A busy smuggling port

The same account notes that in 2007 dozens of traffickers of various nationalities were
arrested at the port of Tangier, where 35 tonnes of hashish were seized over the year.
[@1] The 800 kg find was reported through Panapress in an Afrik-News dispatch by Theo
Benizza. [@1]
