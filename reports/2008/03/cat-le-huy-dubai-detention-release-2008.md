Cat Le-Huy, a 31-year-old German national living in London who headed
technology for the television producer Endemol, was arrested on arrival at
Dubai International Airport on 26 January 2008 while on his way to visit a
friend. [@1] Officers first seized melatonin tablets, a legal jetlag remedy,
then ordered tests on dust in his suitcase that allegedly showed traces of
cannabis resin; Le-Huy denied carrying drugs. [@1,3] The alleged amount was
0.03 grams. [@3]

He spent weeks in airport detention cells before transfer to Al Wathba prison,
was made to sign an Arabic document feared to be a confession and to give a
urine sample, while friends campaigned for his release. [@1,2] In early March
the case was dropped completely and he was released without charge, free to
enter or leave the emirate. [@2,3]
