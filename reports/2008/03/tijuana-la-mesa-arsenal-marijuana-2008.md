Soldiers seized assault rifles, grenades, marijuana and bulletproof vests bearing police insignia after a brief overnight shootout at a house in Tijuana's La Mesa district. [@1,2] Nobody was wounded in the exchange with three suspects, army General Sergio Aponte Polito told reporters on 7 March 2008. [@1,2] Troops took 91 assault rifles, some with gold and ivory butts, with 18 grenades and the vests, plus more than 880 pounds (about 399 kg) of marijuana. [@1,2] The three suspects, aged 25 to 33, were arrested. [@1,2]

Aponte's troops were policing a trafficking corridor amid weeks of confrontations between security forces and cartel gunmen along the border. [@1,2]
