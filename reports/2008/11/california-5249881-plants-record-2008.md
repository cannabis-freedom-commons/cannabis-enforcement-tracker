On 19 November 2008 federal and state officials announced record seizures of 5,249,881 cannabis plants from public and private land across California during the CAMP season and Operation Green Acres 2. [@1,2] Of that total, 3,641,328 plants, almost 70 percent, came from state and federal public land. [@1] CAMP alone eradicated 2.9 million plants in its second straight record year, two million of them on public land. [@1] The eradication season ran from July through October. [@1]

## Raids, arrests and counties

Officers arrested 143 people and seized 142 weapons during the 2008 season, up from 53 arrests and 41 weapons in 2007. [@1,2] Lake County had the highest county total with 499,508 plants, followed by Tulare with 395,489 and Shasta with 394,375. [@1,2] Mendocino County contributed 231,802 plants and Humboldt County 145,762. [@1,2]

Operation Green Acres 2, a four-week statewide operation ending in August, seized almost 1.4 million plants that the agency valued at 4.2 billion dollars. [@1,2] The same operation brought 63 arrests and 102 seized weapons. [@1] A separate summer effort, Operation LOCUST around Sequoia National Park, took about 524,000 plants and led to 15 indictments. [@1] Officials blamed large cartel-run grows for environmental damage on public land, where caretakers often stood guard heavily armed. [@1,2]
