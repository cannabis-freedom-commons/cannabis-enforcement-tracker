At about 8 p.m. on 19 November 2008, the Cork City Divisional Drugs Unit searched a house on the northside of Cork city as part of an ongoing operation. [@1] Officers seized cannabis with an estimated street value of about 500,000 euro, subject to analysis. [@1] A man in his 30s was arrested and detained at Fermoy Garda station under Section 2 of the Drug Trafficking Act 1996. [@1]
