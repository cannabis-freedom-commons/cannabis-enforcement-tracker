A state security court in Sanaa sentenced Iranian national Ayub Mohamed
Houd, 33, to death on 15 November 2008 for leading a 13-man hashish
smuggling operation, while eleven other Iranians and a Pakistani received
25 years in prison each. [@1,2] The court found the men guilty of bringing
about one and a half tonnes of hashish from Iran into Yemeni territorial
waters, hidden in the hold of a ship; a dpa account put the load at 1,400
kilograms. [@1,2,3] The men had been arrested in March after their
Iranian-flagged boat was seized by Yemeni coast-guard patrols off the
south-eastern province of Mahra, though prosecutors said a US navy warship
had interdicted them and handed them over after destroying all but 20 kg of
the drugs. [@1,2,3]

The defendants, aged between 24 and 50, denied the charges when the trial
opened on 12 October, saying through Farsi interpreters that the US sailors
had thrown a large quantity of fish into the sea from the hold. [@1,2] All
thirteen appealed immediately after Judge Muhssin Alwan delivered the
verdict. [@1]
