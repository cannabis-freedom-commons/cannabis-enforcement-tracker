Ondo State's National Drug Law Enforcement Agency command said it arrested 34 suspects and seized 6.1 tonnes of Indian hemp in different locations across the state over the previous three months. [@1,2] Eleven suspects had been convicted for drug offences in the same period. [@1,2]

Commandant Shedow Gaura disclosed the figures at the 2008 drug-abuse awareness rally, raising alarm over rising Indian hemp cultivation and its effects on youths. [@1] Operations chief Okon Okon Ulo, who flanked Gaura, said traditional rulers had a major role in discouraging subjects from planting cannabis and must monitor what was planted on local farms. [@1,2] Ulo repeated the same 34 arrests, 6.1-tonne seizure and 11 convictions to the Daily Sun in Akure, again urging monarchs and chiefs not to give farmland to hemp cultivators. [@2]
