A Kuala Lumpur court sentenced Indonesian nationals Mohamad Idris and
Jainuddin to death for distributing 5.7 kilograms of marijuana. [@1,2] The
two were found in possession of the drug when arrested in September 2002.
[@1,2] They told the court they were innocent and did not know the contents
of a package they were delivering to a person they named as Tengku Yan, but
they could never prove that person existed. [@1,2]

The sentence was reported on 15 November 2008. [@1,2]
