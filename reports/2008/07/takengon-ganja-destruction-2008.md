Local authorities in Central Aceh burned 371 kg of dried ganja leaves along
with hundreds of bottles of alcohol in Takengon on Saturday 19 July 2008.
[@1] District head Nasaruddin called the burning proof of law enforcers'
seriousness against drugs and drink, and urged young people not to consume
goods banned by the state and Islamic sharia. [@1] Prosecutor Ahmad Darmansyah
said 251 kg had been confiscated from 42 people convicted by the Takengon
court and 120 kg collected in 2008 police operations, alongside 282 bottles of
alcohol. [@1]
