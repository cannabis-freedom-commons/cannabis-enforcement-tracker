Officers from the Riverside police and sheriff's departments and the Drug
Enforcement Administration served search warrants on the morning of 22 July
2008 at eight single-family houses in Riverside, Corona, Walnut, South El
Monte, Temple City, Los Angeles, Hesperia and Hemet. [@1] Five of the houses
held indoor grows with 5,600 marijuana plants. [@1,2] Each grow bypassed
utility meters and stole electricity, with whole houses converted, carpets
pulled up, holes cut for wiring and ventilation, and windows covered with
drywall inside. [@1] Most of the houses stood in new developments and had been
bought for $500,000 to $800,000. [@1]

Four people were arrested: Jennifer Zhang, 44, of Temple City, Sehn Nguyen,
41, of Los Angeles, Quan Vi To, 51, of El Monte, and Sang Vong Din, 47, of
Temple City. [@1,2] They were charged in a criminal complaint with conspiracy
to manufacture 1,000 or more marijuana plants, which carries a ten-year
mandatory minimum and a possible life term. [@1] The release noted that every
defendant is presumed innocent until proven guilty. [@1] The day's raids
followed a year-long task force investigation that dismantled 33 indoor grows
and seized more than 19,000 plants, with each house capable of about $3
million a year and total potential proceeds above $60 million. [@1,2]
