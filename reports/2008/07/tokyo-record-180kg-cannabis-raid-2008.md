Officers of the Metropolitan Police Department seized around 180 kilograms of cannabis
with a street value of 720 million yen from a condominium in Tokyo in July 2008. [@1,2]
Police called it the largest amount ever found in a raid in Japan. [@1,2] A 40-year-old
Tokyo company executive and a 22-year-old Chinese student were arrested on suspicion of
possessing the cannabis for sale. [@1]

The seizure came amid a wave of cannabis arrests that put marijuana on Japan's front pages
in 2008, drawing in students, suburban householders and sumo wrestlers. [@2]
