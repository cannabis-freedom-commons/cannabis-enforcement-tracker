Police stopped a jeep at the A.P. Genco check post on the morning of 29 March 2007 and
found 90 ganja packets weighing about 100 kg hidden in its roof. [@1] The two men
inside, both from Kerala, said they were carrying the packets from Orissa; officers
seized the ganja, arrested them and registered Crime No. 13 of 2007. [@1]

The First Additional Sessions Judge at Khammam convicted them in January 2008 and
imposed ten years of rigorous imprisonment each plus a fine of Rs 1,000, treating the
haul as a commercial quantity. [@1] On appeal, the High Court held that only 3.5 kg
had actually been sent to the chemical examiner and proved to be ganja, so the
commercial-quantity minimum did not apply. [@1]

## Reduced to time already spent in jail

The court set aside the ten-year terms, convicted the men instead of the lesser
intermediate-quantity offence and sentenced them to the period they had already
undergone, ordering their release. [@1] Both men had been in jail continuously since
their arrest on 29 March 2007. [@1]
