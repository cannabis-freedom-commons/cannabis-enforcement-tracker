On 5 August 2008 Senad agents raided four marijuana gathering camps near the
town of Itakyry in Alto Paraná department and seized 10,926kg of
marijuana. [@1] The traffickers controlling the camps fled before Senad
arrived. [@1] Agents also seized nine hydraulic presses used to compress
marijuana bricks, 200kg of marijuana seeds, four motorcycles and two
shotguns. [@1] No arrests were reported. [@1]
