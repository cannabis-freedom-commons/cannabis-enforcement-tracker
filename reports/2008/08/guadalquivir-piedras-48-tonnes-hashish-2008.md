Spain's Tax Agency seized 4,800 kilos of hashish in two operations off the
Andalusia coast in late August 2008, part of its CERROJO coastline monitoring
plan against drug trafficking and smuggling. [@1]

In the first operation, in the early hours of Tuesday morning, the Cadiz
combined customs vessels Gerifalte I and Milano II boarded an eight-metre black
inflatable boat at the mouth of the Guadalquivir. [@1] It carried 73 bales of
hashish of the type usually used for the drug, weighing more than 2,300 kilos;
three Moroccan nationals on board, one of them a minor, were arrested and handed
to duty Investigating Court No. 1 in Sanlucar de Barrameda with the drugs. [@1]

## The Piedras yacht

On Wednesday morning off Huelva, near the mouth of the Piedras, a patrol vessel
detected a yacht whose British registration number was not valid. [@1] The
patrol boat HJ-X boarded it and found 85 bales of hashish weighing 2,500 kilos;
the two crew members, both Spanish nationals, were arrested, the 14-metre yacht
was seized, and all were remanded in custody by the Huelva duty court. [@1]
Customs officials said their investigation was continuing and further arrests
could not be ruled out. [@1]
