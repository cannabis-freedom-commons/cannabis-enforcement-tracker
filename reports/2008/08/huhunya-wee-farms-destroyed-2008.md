The Eastern Regional Police Command destroyed several acres of wee plantation at Huhunya in the Yilo-Krobo district on Tuesday 5 August 2008. [@1] The plantation, mixed with tomatoes and other crops, sat in the mountains surrounding the Boti and Akaa waterfalls. [@1]

Led by the regional crime officer and the Koforidua district commander, police set fire to the plantations and to heaps of prepared wee ready for sale. [@1] Acting on a tip-off, officers searched the area and arrested two suspects, Padi Kwaku and Emmanuel Keteku, in a cave on the mountain; both admitted during interrogation that they owned the farms. [@1]

The men told police they had lived and farmed in the caves for 18 years but had taken up wee farming only two years earlier to pay debts from ill health, selling to customers from Accra and other areas who signalled them with an agreed whistle sound. [@1] Police said the swoop was part of a clampdown on criminals linked to the region's high crime rate, and that more than 10 acres of wee plantations had been destroyed in the region within two months. [@1] The seized bags of wee and seedling samples were sent to laboratories for testing while investigations continued. [@1]
