The Santa Barbara County Sheriff's Department, working with the Campaign Against Marijuana Production task force and the National Forest Service, completed its annual marijuana eradication in late July 2008, seizing 34,996 mature plants. [@1] A Drug Enforcement Administration evaluation put their street value at $87,490,000. [@1]

All of the grows were inside the Los Padres National Forest: two large sites near Juncal Dam yielding 13,501 plants, three sites in a wilderness area west of Lake Cachuma yielding 18,555 plants, and four sites in Bear Canyon yielding 2,940 plants. [@1] Evidence recovered at several of the sites indicated that Mexican nationals had been living near the fields and tending them. [@1] No arrests were made during the operation. [@1]
