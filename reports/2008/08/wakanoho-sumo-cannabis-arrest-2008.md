Tokyo police arrested Soslan Aleksandrovich Gagloev, a 20-year-old Russian competing
as top-division wrestler Wakanoho, on 18 August 2008 for possessing marijuana. [@1,2]
A passer-by had found his lost wallet on a Tokyo street on 24 June and handed it to a
nearby police box, where officers found it held marijuana and a document in his
name. [@2] The wallet contained about a third of a gram of the drug, an amount that
carries up to five years in prison with forced labour under Japanese law if it leads
to conviction. [@1] Questioned after his arrest, Wakanoho admitted the facts and said
he had received the cannabis from a foreigner in Roppongi, Tokyo's nightlife
district. [@2] Officers also searched his apartment and his quarters at the Magaki
stable, finding a small quantity of the drug and a smoking pipe. [@2,3]

## Expulsion

On 21 August the Japan Sumo Association expelled Wakanoho, the first active wrestler
thrown out of the sport. [@2,3] The association apologised to fans and pledged to
investigate. [@1] Speaking from his cell, Wakanoho said he regretted what he had
done. [@2]
