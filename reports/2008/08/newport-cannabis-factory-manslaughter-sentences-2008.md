Quynh Van Huynh, 51, was jailed for nine years and Thanh Van Le, 31, and Cong Van Le, 48, for
seven years each at Cardiff Crown Court on 11 August 2008 for the manslaughter of Tran Nguyen.
[@1,2] The three had denied murder but were convicted after a four-month trial that the judge
called a gangland punishment beating gone too far. [@1]

Mr Nguyen, 44, a father of two smuggled from Vietnam in the back of a lorry, had been housed in
London and put to work in a Birmingham nail bar before being sent to tend a cannabis factory in
Keynsham Avenue, Newport as a lone 'ghost' grower. [@1] The gang suspected him of involvement in
a rival raid on the factory, took him to London where he was beaten, then dumped him unconscious
at the Royal Gwent Hospital in November 2006. [@1]

Three other men were acquitted of all charges, and Gwent Police issued a warrant for Tu Minh Le,
who had fled to Vietnam and could not be extradited. [@1]
