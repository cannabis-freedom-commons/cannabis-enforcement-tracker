Spain's Tax Agency seized 2,500 kilograms of hashish in the early hours of
Monday 4 August 2008, when a helicopter and a patrol vessel of the Andalusian
Customs Surveillance unit intercepted a fishing boat heading for the port of
La Caleta de Vélez. [@1] The six crew members on board, two Moroccan nationals
and four Spanish nationals, were arrested on suspicion of smuggling and
offences against public health. [@1]

The detainees and the vessel were taken to the port of Málaga, where
Investigating Court No. 2 authorised a full search that uncovered eighty
packages of hashish resin weighing about 2,500 kilos in the holds. [@1] The
drugs, the vessel and the detainees were to be brought before the duty
investigating magistrate's court in Málaga. [@1]
