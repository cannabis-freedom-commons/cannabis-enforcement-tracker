An anonymous tip-off passed via Crimestoppers led Nottinghamshire Police to a former chicken
factory on Barrows Hill Lane in Jacksdale, raided on the afternoon of Wednesday 6 August 2008.
[@2] Inside, officers found about 3,000 cannabis plants with an estimated street value of
£3 million, plus electrical equipment worth about £250,000. [@2] Police described a
sophisticated set-up, with upstairs office space converted into living quarters complete with a
gym. [@2]

Chief Inspector Mark Wakeland said one attending officer, who had executed drugs warrants for
seven years, called it the biggest and most sophisticated set-up he had ever seen; plants grew
on an industrial scale across a factory floor roughly 50 metres long by 25 metres wide. [@2]
The force believed the haul was the biggest seized in Nottinghamshire. [@2]

## Seven arrested

Seven men aged 20 to 48 were arrested in connection with the find and remained in custody being
questioned; no names were published. [@2] The BBC's video report from the site confirmed
cannabis worth an estimated three million pounds found at the former chicken factory. [@1]
