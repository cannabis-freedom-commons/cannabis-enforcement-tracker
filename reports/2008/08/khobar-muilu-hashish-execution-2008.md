Saudi Arabia beheaded Hussein Bin Saleh Mailu in the eastern city of al-Khobar
on Sunday 31 August 2008 after he was convicted of smuggling hashish into the
kingdom. [@1,2] The Interior Ministry announced the execution in a statement
carried by the official Saudi Press Agency. [@1,2]

The beheading was the 71st execution announced in Saudi Arabia that year,
following a record 153 people put to death in 2007 against 37 beheaded in 2006. [@1]
Saudi Arabia applies a strict version of sharia, Islamic law, under which rape,
murder, apostasy, armed robbery and drug trafficking can all carry the death
penalty, and executions are usually carried out in public. [@1]

The ministry statement as reported named the man and the offence but gave no
quantity for the hashish and no account of his arrest, trial or sentencing. [@1,2]
