South Aceh police detained four suspected growers at Gunung Titi in the Pasie
Raja district on Thursday 3 April 2008 during Operasi Antik. [@1,2] Officers
found three hectares of marijuana fields and destroyed 700 ready-to-harvest
trees. [@1,2] Antara's photographers documented officers carrying the uprooted
plants through the forest and an officer guarding the seated suspects beside
the seized crop. [@1,2]
