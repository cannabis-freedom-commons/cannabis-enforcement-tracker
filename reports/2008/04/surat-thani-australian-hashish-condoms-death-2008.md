Australian John Paul Jones died in a hospital in Surat Thani, southern Thailand, on Saturday 19 April 2008, eight days after friends rushed him there with severe stomach pains. [@2] Doctors had found about 60 condoms stuffed with hashish in his intestine, three of which had burst, and recovered a total of 800 grams of hashish from him. [@1,2]

Jones, a Queenslander who had been staying on Koh Phangan, known for its Full Moon parties, told no one what he had done; his friends said he had suffered stomach pain for five days and disappeared after dropping him at the hospital. [@1] Police said he would be charged once his condition improved, and later filed narcotics charges while he remained in hospital. [@1,2] Provincial authorities believed he had swallowed the condoms to get the hashish past pier inspectors on the way to Phangan Island. [@2]

## Confirmed death

Australia's Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade confirmed that the hospitalised man died on 19 April without naming him for privacy reasons, and said consular assistance to his family continued. [@2] The Australian Broadcasting Corporation reported he died of septicaemia and liver failure. [@2]
