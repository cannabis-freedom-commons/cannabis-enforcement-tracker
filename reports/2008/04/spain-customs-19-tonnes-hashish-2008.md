Spain's Tax Agency seized about 19 tonnes of hashish in five customs operations carried out
between 23 and 25 April 2008, three times the amount seized in the same period of the
previous year.[@1]

The largest single seizure came in the early hours of 25 April, when the customs patrol
vessel Arao boarded the 16.5-metre Spanish-flagged sailing yacht Burbuja 101 miles off
Alicante in international waters.[@1] The yacht carried 200 bales of hashish weighing around
6,000 kilos; two Spaniards, a Moroccan and two Russian women were arrested.[@1]

On 23 April, officers seized a four-engined rigid-hulled boat with 143 bales (4,460 kilos)
at La Manga del Mar Menor in Murcia, detaining eight people.[@1] The same day in Les Deveses,
Valencia, police and customs seized a lorry and a boat carrying 4,000 kilos and arrested eight
people: two Cubans, a Spaniard and five Moroccans.[@1] In the early hours of 24 April, a joint
operation in Lloret de Mar, Girona, netted 3,300 kilos and a high-speed boat, with 14 arrests
so far.[@1] Also on 24 April, a vehicle and a boat with 1,000 kilos were seized on a beach in
Níjar, Almería, with no arrests reported.[@1]
