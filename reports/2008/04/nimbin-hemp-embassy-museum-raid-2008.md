About 50 police from across New South Wales stormed the Hemp Embassy and the
Nimbin Museum on April Fool's Day 2008, arresting eight people and seizing
four kilograms of cannabis. [@1] A riot-squad contingent from Sydney joined
the operation, and officers took cannabis leaf, cookies and cake. [@2]
Volunteers said police came "in strength and with heavy hands to harass the
street traders particularly the cookie sellers", calling the raid "like
busting a CWA cake stall". [@3]

Hemp law-reform supporters announced a protest at the Lismore Court House for
28 April over what they called a waste of taxpayers' money. [@3] The raid
came a month before Nimbin's 16th annual MardiGrass festival, which drew an
estimated 15,000 people despite a heavy police presence. [@2]
