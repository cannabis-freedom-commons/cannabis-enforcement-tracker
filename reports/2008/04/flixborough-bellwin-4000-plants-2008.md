Humberside Police raided a building at the Bellwin House Business Park in Flixborough after a tip-off and found more than 4,000 cannabis plants being grown.[@1] The plants, in two office suites beside legitimate businesses, were put at a street value of about £4 million.[@1] Officers were still clearing the site on the Sunday after the Wednesday raid.[@1]

Two men, Quang Van Tran, 36, and Anh Duc Ngugen, 45, both of no fixed address, appeared at North Lincolnshire Magistrates' Court on the Friday charged with cannabis cultivation.[@1] They both denied the allegation and were remanded in custody to appear at Scunthorpe court on the Thursday.[@1] Police said a major drugs operation appeared to have run at night in the two suites, secured with roller-shutter doors and a complex ventilation system to hide the smell.[@1] Pc Lee Smith, who took part in the operation, said it was an unusually large factory in an occupied first-floor office complex, and Health and Safety experts and Ministry of Defence police were brought in to help clear it safely.[@1]
