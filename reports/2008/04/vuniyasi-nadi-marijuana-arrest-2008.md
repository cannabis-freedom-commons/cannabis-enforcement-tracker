A 58-year-old man was in police custody after officers acting on information
stopped his vehicle at Vuniyasi, Nadi, on Sunday and found eleven sachets of
dried leaves believed to be marijuana inside his car. [@1]

Assistant police public relations officer Corporal Suliano Tevita said police
had sent the leaves to the government analyst and were waiting for a
report. [@1] No charge was stated in the report. [@1]
