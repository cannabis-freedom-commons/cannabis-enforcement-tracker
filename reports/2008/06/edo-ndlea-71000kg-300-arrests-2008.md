Edo State NDLEA commander Okey Ihebom told reporters in Benin that his command
impounded 71,000kg of Indian hemp and arrested 300 people between January and
June 2008, also taking 15 vehicles used to move the drugs. [@1] Courts convicted
18 suspects while 10 more awaited prosecution, and Ihebom said the drugs fell
into raw weed for local use and a processed type for export. [@1] He added that
76,000kg of cannabis had been publicly burnt in the state on 26 January 2008. [@1]

That January burning belonged to a 101,652.24kg destruction across Anambra, Delta
and Edo states, with Edo's 75,896kg the largest share against 22,700kg in Delta
and 3,056.24kg in Anambra. [@2] NDLEA chairman Ahmadu Giade said the Edo command
had also found and destroyed 27 hectares of cannabis farmland in the previous
ten months, and recalled 31,000kg destroyed in Edo in January 2007. [@2] Giade
called cannabis the biggest drug challenge in Nigeria and Africa, saying it
grows effortlessly in the country. [@2]
