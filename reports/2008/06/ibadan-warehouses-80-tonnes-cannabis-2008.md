NDLEA officers raided two warehouses storing cannabis in residential areas of Ibadan after surveillance based on a tip from the National Security and Civil Defence Corps, the agency said in a statement on Sunday 1 June 2008. [@1] The warehouses stood on Adegboyega Elufidiya Avenue off Airport Road at Alakia and at Gbagi on New Ife Road. [@1]

Officers weighed the haul at 80.53 metric tonnes, valued at about 160 million naira; the BBC rounded the seizure to 80 tonnes and put the local street value at $1.2 million. [@1,2] Eight suspects were arrested, including Victor Chukwuka, who allegedly managed the warehouses, while the suspected kingpin of the inter-state distribution syndicate, Okpor Raphael, remained at large. [@1] Officers also impounded a bullion van, a truck painted in a brewing company's colours and a Peugeot 504 saloon car used to disguise the trafficking. [@1,2]

The Oyo commander called it the largest seizure since the state command was created in 2003, and the BBC described it as one of the agency's largest hauls. [@1,2]
