Joint police, Philippine Army and Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency (PDEA) teams hit marijuana
fields across Benguet in a three-day operation that Cordillera police director Chief Superintendent
Eugene Martin said covered 22 hectares at 36 sites. [@1,2] They uprooted 150,320 fully grown plants
and 36,610 seedlings in Barangays Badeo and Takadang, Kibungan, and Barangay Kayapa, Bakun. [@1,2]

## Burn at Camp Dangwa

Martin put the Dangerous Drugs Board-priced haul at ₱31,528,400. [@1,2] The force burned the
uprooted cannabis at Camp Dangwa on Friday afternoon while vowing to keep clearing plantations in
Benguet. [@1] Neither Star account filed on 7 and 8 December 2008 reports any arrest during the
sweep. [@1,2]

PDEA-Cordillera, Benguet police, the Philippine Air Force’s Tactical Operations Group 1, the 503rd
Infantry Brigade and municipal police from Bakun and Kibungan took part. [@1]
