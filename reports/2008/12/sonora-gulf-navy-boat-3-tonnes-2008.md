The Mexican navy seized a boat with 3 tonnes of marijuana in Pacific waters off the coast
of Mexico, military officials said on Thursday 18 December 2008. [@1] The navy said three
Mexicans were arrested in the operation in the Gulf of California near the state of
Sonora. [@1] The drugs were divided into 326 packages. [@1]
