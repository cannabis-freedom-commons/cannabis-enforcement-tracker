Officers of the Oyo State command of the National Drug Law Enforcement Agency (NDLEA) raided two
clandestine warehouses in a residential area of Ibadan in an operation that ran from the morning of
29 May to the afternoon of 30 May 2008, seizing 80.53 metric tons of cannabis. [@1,2] The agency's
director-general later called it the single largest seizure ever made by the Oyo State command. [@2]
The drugs had been loaded into a bullion van, a brewery-painted truck and a saloon car for supply
to a residential part of the city. [@1,2]

Eight suspects were arrested, including Victor Chukwuka, who managed the warehouses, while the
suspected kingpin, Okpor Raphael, was still at large. [@1,2] The warehouses stood at Adegboyega
Elufidiya Avenue off Airport Road in Alakia and at Gbagi on Old Ife Road, and officers had kept both
places under surveillance after a tip from the National Security and Civil Defence Corps. [@2] The
BBC, citing the NDLEA's Oyo commander Ekaete Egwunwoke, put the local street value at $1.2 million;
the Nigerian press reported about N160 million. [@1,2]
