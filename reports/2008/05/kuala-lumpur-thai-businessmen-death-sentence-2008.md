The Kuala Lumpur High Court sentenced Thai businessmen Masoh Daloh, 35, and
Romuelee Yakoh, 46, to death on 12 May 2008 for trafficking 34,942 grams of
cannabis. [@1,2] The two men had claimed trial over 34 compressed slabs of
the drug found in a car at the Taman Tasik Titiwangsa car park at 12.10am on
20 February 2002, after a police officer acting on a tip-off checked the
vehicle. [@1,2] Justice Mohtarudin Baki found the defence had failed to
raise reasonable doubt and imposed the mandatory death sentence under
section 39B of the Dangerous Drugs Act 1952. [@1,2]

Seven prosecution witnesses testified at the trial while both accused gave
evidence in their own defence. [@1]
