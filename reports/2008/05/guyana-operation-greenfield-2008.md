Guyana's Joint Services unearthed and destroyed ten fields of cannabis sativa
holding about 14,900 plants as high as 12 feet at Coomacka, New England and
Wisroc in Region Ten (Upper Demerara/Upper Berbice) over one weekend. [@1] The
operation, codenamed Greenfield, combined air, river and ground assets, with one
of the recently acquired Bell 206 helicopters flying reconnaissance and
vectoring tasks. [@1]

The Joint Services put the estimated weight of the destroyed plants at 3,305
kg. [@1] Officers worked across a seven-acre area, where they also found 684 kg
of dried marijuana and 14 nurseries holding 5,550 seedlings, and destroyed
eleven camps. [@1]

One man was arrested, found in a house with 8 kg of marijuana near one of the
fields, and equipment was seized. [@1] River assets ferried the forces to the
sites while ground forces located and destroyed the fields and executed the
arrest. [@1]
