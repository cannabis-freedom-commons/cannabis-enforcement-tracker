Kent Police were still clearing a third cannabis factory on Church Road, Ramsgate, on 22 May 2008
after a two-day operation at three addresses. [@1] More than 450 plants were found at 20a and 20b
Church Road—where internal walls had been knocked through to join the houses—and at 68b Church Road.
[@1] Kitchens, living rooms and bedrooms were filled with plants, and electricity meters had been
bypassed. [@1]

Specialist teams helped execute a warrant under the Misuse of Drugs Act because of safety concerns
about the wiring. [@1] Sergeant Dave Knox said organised groups use factories to fund other crime and
that the three sites could potentially yield more than £50,000 every twelve weeks, the plants’
propagation cycle. [@1] Ngoc Bang Tran, 46, of Plaistow, London, was arrested in Church Road and
charged with being concerned in the production of cannabis, with a court date listed for that
Friday. [@1] Investigators were still examining whether the three properties were linked. [@1]

Superintendent Chris Hogben said the case reflected intelligence work in Thanet and noted a growing
trend of rented homes being converted for cultivation. [@1]
