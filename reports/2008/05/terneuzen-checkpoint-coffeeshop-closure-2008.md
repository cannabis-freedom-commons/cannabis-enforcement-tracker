On 20 May 2008 Dutch police again raided the Checkpoint coffeeshop in Terneuzen and
closed what was described as the biggest coffeeshop in the Netherlands. [@3]
Prosecutors later put the cannabis seized in the raid at 110 kilograms. [@1,2]
Before its closure Checkpoint had served 2,000 to 3,000 customers a day, most of
them Belgian or French. [@1,2]

The closure followed a June 2007 raid in which investigators had found 120 kilograms
at the shop. [@2] Owner Meddy Willemsen and sixteen managers and suppliers later stood
trial in Middelburg on trafficking and criminal-organisation charges. [@1,2]
