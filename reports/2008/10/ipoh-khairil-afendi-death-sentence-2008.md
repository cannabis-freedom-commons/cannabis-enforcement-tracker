The High Court on 8 October 2008 sentenced Khairil Anuar Abdul Rahman, 34, and
Afendi Adam, 28, to death by hanging for trafficking in cannabis. [@1,2]
Judicial Commissioner Ridwan Ibrahim held that the prosecution had proved its
case beyond reasonable doubt against both men. [@1,2]

Khairil Anuar, a restaurant worker, and Afendi, a painter, had been charged on
12 March 2002 with trafficking 971 grams of cannabis in Jalan Rokam, Taman
Razaki, on 28 February 2002, together with another person still at large.
[@1,2] Ridwan said the court had no choice but to pass the death sentence,
but gave no grounds for the judgement. [@1,2] Both men's lawyers were expected
to appeal against the conviction and sentence. [@1]
