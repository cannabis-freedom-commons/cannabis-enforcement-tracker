Customs officers at Rosslare Europort seized 1,450 kilograms of cannabis resin with an estimated
street value of €10 million on Thursday 2 October 2008. [@2] The Irish Examiner reported the find
the next day as one of the largest recent cannabis seizures and the biggest at Rosslare. [@1]

## Concealment and search

Officers stopped a Dutch-registered refrigerated articulated truck that had arrived by car ferry
from the Netherlands via Britain carrying potted plants consigned to a business address in Cork.
[@1,2] Revenue’s sniffer dog Dillon indicated drugs, and staff found an elaborate false floor; they
cut through steel-plated compartments beneath wooden flooring to reach the resin. [@1,2]

## Arrest

A 42-year-old Dutch national driving the truck was arrested under section 2 of the Criminal Justice
(Drug Trafficking) Act 1996 and detained at Wexford Garda Station. [@1,2] The Irish Times said
Revenue described the Cork consignee as a legitimate company whose name smugglers use as cover.
[@2] Customs and gardaí continued tracing the load’s origin and intended recipients. [@1]
