Copenhagen police had secretly investigated the ring since January 2008 and arrested nine people, seizing the gang's accounts, after what they called Denmark's biggest ever cannabis case. [@1,2] A criminal organisation had imported as much as 17 tonnes of cannabis from Spain and the Netherlands over about 18 months, distributing it in Copenhagen, including Christiania, and in Holstebro, Esbjerg, Horsens and Aarhus. [@1,2]

A 46-year-old German woman working as a money courier and a 41-year-old Danish man believed to be the drug baron had been in custody since May, police superintendent Steffen Th. Steffensen said. [@1,2] The woman was charged with ferrying DKK 53 million to the gang's foreign accounts and was caught leaving Denmark with DKK 1.7 million. [@1,2] Two suspected principals were caught south of Køge, sentences had already been passed for receiving 600 and 800 kg, and officers found a machine gun in one Jutland raid. [@1]

Police linked the ring to the previous record case, the 13-tonne Atlantic Privateer shipment, for which Claus Malmqvist was serving a 16-year sentence; a fugitive wanted in that case was suspected of handling three tonnes of its cargo and working with the new gang. [@1,2]
