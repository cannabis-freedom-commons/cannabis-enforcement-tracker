On 17 September 2008, US authorities announced that 175 people had been arrested that day and the day before in a coordinated enforcement action against the Gulf Cartel, bringing the 15-month Project Reckoning investigation to 507 arrests to date. [@1,2] The takedown seized about $60.1 million in cash, 16,711 kilograms of cocaine, 1,039 pounds of methamphetamine, 19 pounds of heroin, 51,258 pounds (about 23.25 tonnes) of marijuana, 176 vehicles and 167 weapons. [@1,2]

## A 15-month cartel investigation

Project Reckoning was a DEA-led multi-agency effort against the Mexican Gulf Cartel and its US and international distribution networks, combining operations including Dos Equis, Vertigo, Stinger and The Family. [@1,2] Three alleged cartel leaders were indicted in the District of Columbia on charges of conspiring to import drugs into the United States from Mexico. [@1,2] The cartel moved multi-ton quantities of cocaine, methamphetamine, heroin and marijuana from Colombia, Guatemala, Panama and Mexico into the United States and distributed them within the country. [@1,2]
