The Shah Alam High Court sentenced Indonesian immigrant Junaidi Nurdin, 32, to death for selling 979 grams of cannabis. [@1]

Nurdin was arrested in April 2004 after he sold the cannabis to an undercover policeman at a restaurant in Shah Alam. [@1] Two weeks before the 10 October 2008 roundup that reported it, the court imposed the death sentence, the mandatory punishment for trafficking under section 39B of the Dangerous Drugs Act 1952. [@1] His lawyers were expected to appeal against the conviction and sentence. [@1]
