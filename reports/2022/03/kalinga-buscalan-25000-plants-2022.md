No cultivators were arrested during the two-day clearing operation, the
Philippine National Police said. [@1] Police and Philippine Drug Enforcement
Agency (PDEA) operatives cleared a cannabis plantation in Buscalan, Tinglayan,
Kalinga. [@2] The clearing ran from 6 a.m. on 28 March to 10 a.m. on 29 March
2022. [@3] The team included police drug enforcement and intelligence units, the
Kalinga police provincial office, the Tinglayan municipal police station,
regional mobile force units and PDEA Kalinga. [@2]

Officers found about 25,000 fully grown marijuana plants planted in roughly
2,500 square metres (0.25 hectares) of land. [@1] The plants were burned on the
spot, and the PNP put their estimated standard drug price at 5 million pesos
(PHP 5,000,000). [@2]

PNP chief Dionardo Carlos said the discovery was part of the police force's
summer offensive against cannabis cultivation in Luzon. [@2] He commended the
combined PNP and PDEA forces and called for continued eradication in the
Cordillera mountains. [@1] He also said police would intensify efforts to track
down those behind the plantations and put them behind bars. [@1]
