Prefectura Naval Argentina officers on patrol in the San Gotardo area of Puerto
Rico, Misiones, spotted a group of people carrying bundles. [@1] Officers
recovered several bundles hidden among the undergrowth after an intensive
search. [@1] No detentions had been reported. [@3] The Federal Prosecutor's
Office of First Instance in Oberá, headed by Viviana Alejandra Vallejos, took
over the case. [@1]

Officers found that the load comprised 648 pressed marijuana bricks weighing
more than 415 kilos. [@1] The Prefectura put the total weight at 416 kilos. [@2]
The Prefectura valued the load at almost 50 million pesos. [@1]
