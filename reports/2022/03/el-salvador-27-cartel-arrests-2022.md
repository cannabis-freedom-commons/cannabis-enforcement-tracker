El Salvador's Attorney General's Office (FGR), the national prosecution
authority, ordered the capture of 27 people and the seizure of sea and land
vehicles in an operation against a cartel moving drugs from Nicaragua to
Guatemala. [@1] The case prosecutor said the structure operates nationally but
maintains international links to move drugs from Nicaragua to the Guatemalan
border. [@1] The investigation dates back more than three years and began with
the seizure of 533 kilos of cocaine and 97 pounds of marijuana (about 44 kg).
[@1] Walter Alexander Ramos Alvarenga was among those captured and was described
as the suspected leader of the organization. [@1] Prosecutors said Ramos will
face drug-trafficking prosecution over a 330-kilo cocaine seizure on 15 June
2021 and six cases of proposing and conspiring homicide. [@1]

Investigators attribute approximately 533 kilos of cocaine, 97 pounds of
marijuana (about 44 kg), cash, vehicles and boats to date to three structures
operating nationally and internationally. [@1] During the operation authorities
requested raids on at least 54 properties across the country, seizing cash, land
vehicles and boats. [@1] Officers also seized a 9mm firearm and an A15 rifle, as
well as satellite and cellular phones. [@1] Among the seized items was a
facial-recognition surveillance system taken from Ramos Alvarenga's business.
[@1]

The case prosecutor said the group had repeatedly moved drugs from Nicaragua to
the Guatemalan border, calling the operation a blow against organized crime that
had also kept Salvadorans in anxiety. [@1] The report did not specify whether
the drugs were for regional consumption or for shipment onward. [@1]
