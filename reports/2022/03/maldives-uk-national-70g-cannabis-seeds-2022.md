Maldives Customs Service seized drugs from a United Kingdom national who arrived
in the Maldives. [@1] The traveller arrived on 6 March and was flagged for
suspicious behaviour before officers searched his luggage. [@1]

Testing of the suspected substances was positive for cannabis. [@1] Customs said
officers found 30.9 grams of cannabis seeds and 40.3 grams of cannabis herbal
seeds in his luggage, with a total weight of 70.12 grams. [@1]

The suspect and the drugs were placed in police custody. [@1] Customs said it
had set up a tactical team for special operations in January to strengthen
efforts against drug imports. [@1]
