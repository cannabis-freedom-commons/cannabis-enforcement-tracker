Gendarmería Nacional Argentina reported that cannabis was being moved on a
long-distance bus travelling from Río Negro province toward Chubut province.
[@1] Gendarmes of Grupo “Gobernador Costa”, under Escuadrón 37 “José de San
Martín”, were running a check at kilometre 1,592 of Ruta Nacional 40 when they
stopped a passenger bus travelling from San Carlos de Bariloche, Río Negro, to
Comodoro Rivadavia, Chubut. [@1] During the search, with support from narcotics
detector dog “Pecky”, officers found a backpack holding plant material. [@1] The
release identifies the acting force as Gendarmería Nacional Argentina, under the
national Security Ministry. [@1]

Criminalistics and forensic staff carried out “Narcotest” field tests on the
plant material, which came back positive for “cannabis sativa”. [@1] Officers
seized a total of 559 grams of the drug. [@1]

The Federal Court and the Federal Prosecutor's Office of Esquel ordered the drug
seized and one passenger to remain tied to the case. [@1] The only measure
reported for the passenger was remaining subject to the case. [@1]
