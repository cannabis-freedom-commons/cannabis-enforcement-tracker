Officers from HM Customs, Bermuda's customs service, intercepted a shipment that
was found to contain about 58 pounds (about 26.3 kg) of cannabis. [@1] Police
put the street value of the shipment at $1.2 million in Bermuda dollars. [@1]
Four people were arrested in connection with the shipment. [@1] Police officers
searched three residences in Warwick, the parish where the arrests were made.
[@1] The Bermuda Police Service Drugs Unit was called in after the interception,
and police ran the joint operation with HM Customs under the name Operation
Mizar. [@2]

The Royal Gazette reported that Operation Mizar was carried out on a Thursday,
four people were arrested and three homes in Warwick were searched. [@2]
Detective Chief Inspector Sherwin Joseph, who heads the Specialist
Investigations Department, said the importation of cannabis is still illegal.
[@1] Joseph said drugs imported in this way involve members of organised crime
groups who are also involved in gang and gun culture. [@2] Police said they
would give no further information while the Drugs Unit continued to investigate
the incident. [@1]
