U.S. Postal Inspectors executed a search warrant on a package on March 14, 2022
and found approximately 449 grams of marijuana, according to a warrant filed in
the District Court of Guam. [@1] The package had been shipped from a Las Vegas
address to a residence in Liguan Terrace in Dededo. [@1] No charges or arrests
were reported in connection with the case. [@1] The box's description said its
contents included two hats and two shirts. [@1]

A later roundup of mailed-drug seizures in Guam listed the same case, with 449
grams of marijuana found in a package shipped from a Las Vegas address to a
residence in Liguan Terrace, Dededo, on March 14, 2022. [@2] That roundup,
published about six weeks later, said no indictments had been filed or made
public in connection with the cases it listed. [@2]

The roundup also lists a separate April 5, 2022 shipment of 375 grams of
marijuana and THC products to a Barrigada post office box, treated as a
different event and not counted in this report's figures. [@2]
