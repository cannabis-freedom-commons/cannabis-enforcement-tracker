State traffic police officers stopped a foreign car on the Tyumen–Omsk highway
early in the morning for a document check. [@1] The driver, a man behind the
wheel, showed excessive nervousness, and the officers decided to search the car.
[@1] On the back seat, inside a suitcase, officers found six packages wrapped in
food film. [@1] The driver, a Moscow resident born in 1986, did not deny it and
said he was carrying drugs from St Petersburg to Novosibirsk. [@1]

An expert examination of the seized material established that he was carrying
six kilograms of hashish, and he was taken to the police. [@1] Investigators
found that the Moscow resident had been spreading drugs for an online shop for
three years. [@1] On this trip he had first collected 10 kilograms of hashish in
St Petersburg. [@1] Along the way he laid drug stashes, and the journey was to
end in Novosibirsk with the remaining 6 kg of "goods". [@1] For the trip he was
to be paid about one million roubles. [@1]

A criminal case was opened against the previously unconvicted 36-year-old man.
[@1] He was placed in pre-trial detention as a measure of restraint, and the
investigation continued. [@1]
