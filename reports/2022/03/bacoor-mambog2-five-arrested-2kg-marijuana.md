Anti-narcotics agents arrested five drug suspects in Bacoor City, Cavite, early
on Wednesday morning. [@1] The Calabarzon police, the regional police command
for Cavite, Laguna, Batangas, Rizal and Quezon, said the arrests came around
1:30 a.m. in Barangay Mambog 2, a barangay or village, the smallest unit of
local government. [@1] Joshua Conejos, Richard Victoria and Hershey Anne Reyes
were named, while two others were identified only as “Adrian” and “Ian
Kristopher.” [@1] Police said the five were held after they sold a pack of
shabu, crystal meth, to an undercover officer. [@1]

Officers found two kilograms of marijuana that the authorities valued at
P90,000. [@1] They also found nine sachets of shabu weighing 25 grams and valued
at P170,000, which the report treats separately from the marijuana. [@1] Police
put the total value of the two drugs at P260,000. [@1]

The five were detained and faced charges under the Comprehensive Dangerous Drugs
Act of 2002, the Philippines' main drug law. [@1]
