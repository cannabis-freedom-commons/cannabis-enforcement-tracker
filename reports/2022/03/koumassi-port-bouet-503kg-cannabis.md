Customs officers made a second seizure of 356 cannabis blocks weighing 302.4 kg
on Sunday 6 March 2022. [@1] The drugs were hidden in a minibus at the Akwaba
crossroads in Port-Bouët, with two men aboard. [@1] The two men were handed over
to the DPSD, the national police drugs squad. [@1] The day before, officers of
the GIRA customs unit chased a Peugeot to the Camp Commando crossroads in
Koumassi. [@1] A search of the car found 203 blocks weighing 182.7 kg. [@1] The
transporters fled, abandoning the vehicle and the illicit goods. [@1] The two
seizures totalled 559 blocks weighing 503.1 kg. [@1]

Customs said the operations formed part of its mission to protect the population
from trafficking in arms, drugs and psychotropic substances. [@1] It said the
seizures were carried out under Law No. 88-686 of 22 July 1988 against drug
trafficking and customs code articles on the contraband import of prohibited
goods. [@1] That law makes importing, exporting, transporting and possessing
psychotropic substances an offence. [@1] Customs Director General Da Pierre
Alphonse congratulated his officers and called on the population to help by
denouncing fraud and trafficking. [@1]
