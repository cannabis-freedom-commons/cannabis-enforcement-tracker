Six men were arrested for firearm and drug-related offences during an Operation
Strike Back exercise by the Trinidad and Tobago Police Service. [@1] Four of the
men, aged 28 to 46, from the San Juan and Malick areas were held for possession
of a firearm and ammunition. [@1] A 39-year-old man of 10th Street Malick was
detained on shooting enquiries. [@1] A 36-year-old was also arrested for
cultivating marijuana, the TTPS social media page stated on Thursday. [@1]

The exercise ran from 10 p.m. on 9 March to 2:30 a.m. on 10 March 2022. [@1]
Several search warrants were executed, leading to the arrests. [@1]

The exercise was led by acting Snr Supt Winston Maharaj, with ASP Pariman, and
included the NED Gang Intelligence Unit, the NED Task Force and the Canine Unit.
