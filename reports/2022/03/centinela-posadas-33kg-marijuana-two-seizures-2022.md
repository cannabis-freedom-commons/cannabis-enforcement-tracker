On 28 March 2022, Gendarmes from Section and Vial Group 'San José', part of
Squadron 8 'Alto Uruguay', stopped a long-distance bus from Puerto Iguazú at
Paraje Centinela on Ruta Nacional 14 for a physical and documentary check. [@1]
Officers inspected the bus hold with drug-detection dog 'Boni', which reacted to
a suitcase, prompting them to ask the driver for the suitcase's key to identify
the owner. [@1] The suitcase belonged to a Paraguayan citizen; a fixed scanner
detected organic matter, and opening it revealed five nylon bags with 21 packets
inside that tested positive for Cannabis Sativa, totalling 21.3 kg. [@1] The
Federal Court and Federal Prosecutor's Office of Posadas ordered the seizure of
the drugs and the detention of the involved person. [@1]

Meanwhile, members of Squadron 50 'Posadas' were patrolling a rural area near
the provincial capital when they stopped a private vehicle to check its
documentation. [@1] During the search, gendarmes detected a characteristic
marijuana smell from burlap bags in the trunk; field tests confirmed marijuana
weighing 12.76 kg. [@1] The Federal Court of Posadas ordered the seizure and
custody of items found in the vehicle and the detention of the citizen under Law
23.737. [@1]

Those involved were detained in violation of the Psychotropic Substances Law.
