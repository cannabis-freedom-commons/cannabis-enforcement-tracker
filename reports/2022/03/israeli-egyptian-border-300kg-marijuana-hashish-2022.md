Israeli troops foiled an attempt to smuggle drugs into Israel from Egypt on the
evening of 2 March 2022. [@1] Soldiers monitoring surveillance cameras spotted
the smuggling attempt and dispatched troops to the scene. [@1] Suspects on the
Egyptian side tossed the drugs over the border fence, apparently for accomplices
on the Israeli side to collect later. [@1] No arrests were made. [@1]

Troops seized more than 300 kilograms (660 pounds) of marijuana and hashish.
[@1] The Israel Defense Forces estimated the seized contraband was worth NIS 6
million, about $1.8 million. [@1]

Israel says Egyptian smugglers operate by tossing contraband over the border to
Israelis, who then sell the drugs in Israel. [@1] The smugglers mostly traffic
in marijuana from grow houses in the Sinai Peninsula, though sometimes harder
drugs such as heroin are smuggled as well. [@1] Although the IDF is tasked with
preventing smuggling along the Israeli-Egyptian border, it typically avoids
direct confrontation with Israeli drug smugglers and leaves that to the police.
[@1] Several deadly encounters between Israeli troops and drug smugglers had
occurred the previous year. [@1]
