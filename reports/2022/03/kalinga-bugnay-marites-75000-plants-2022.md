Officers of the Police Regional Office Cordillera and the Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency destroyed 75,000 fully grown marijuana plants at eight plantation sites in Barangay Bugnay, Tinglayan, Kalinga, on 11 and 12 March 2022. [@1,2] The two-day operation, dubbed OPLAN MARITES, covered about 7,150 square metres, and the plants were uprooted and burned on site at an estimated value of 15 million pesos. [@1] No cultivators were apprehended, and an investigation to identify them was under way. [@1]

The operation followed OPLAN HERODOTUS 2 in the same province, and reports were submitted to PROCOR regional director Ronald Oliver Lee. [@1,2]
