Acting on territorial-surveillance intelligence, police raided a farm 17 km
north of Es-Semara on Saturday morning and broke up an international drug
trafficking network operating by sea. [@1] Officers seized a first shipment of
cannabis resin totalling 4,345 kg, plus three inflatable boats, two motors,
fuel canisters, a four-wheel-drive vehicle and hunting cartridges. [@1]

Two men aged 31 and 32 were arrested and taken into custody for the judicial
police investigation in Laayoune. [@1] A later search found a second stash 50
km away holding nine more bundles of chira. [@1]
