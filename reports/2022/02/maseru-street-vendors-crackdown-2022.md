Lesotho police began raiding street drug peddlers in Maseru after a tip-off that vendors were dealing drugs while pretending to sell food such as corn chips, fruit and vegetables. [@1] One vendor arrested along Kingsway Road and another at the Moshoeshoe I Statue Park had openly displayed fruit on their tables while allegedly stashing the drugs underneath. [@1]

Police arrested 24 street vendors in the operation, most of them dealers selling marijuana alongside regular goods. [@1] Twenty-two appeared before the Maseru Magistrates' Court charged with illegal possession of drugs after allegedly being found with stocks of marijuana. [@1] The other two were found with suspected cocaine, which police said they had sent to a laboratory for testing. [@1]

Deputy police spokesperson Sub-Inspector 'Mareabetsoe Mofoka said the dagga was packed in small paper packages called jointers and sold in small quantities, mostly to youths. [@1] She said more work was needed to find the suppliers behind the street vendors. [@1] The head of the Diamonds and Drugs Unit at police headquarters, Superintendent Retselisitsoe Bokopane, said the harshest sentence handed down during the crackdown was a M10,000 fine or five years' imprisonment and the lowest a M500 fine. [@1]
