Luxembourg police staged a series of drug checks in Differdange and on the A4
motorway on Thursday. [@1] At around 1am, officers investigated a vehicle at a
service station on the A4. [@1] A search of the car yielded a joint, 11 grams of
cannabis and 108 grams of hashish. [@1] One of the car's occupants was arrested
by order of the public prosecutor, while the other two occupants received a
fine. [@1] The car was seized as part of the investigation. [@1]

In Differdange, police staged a larger operation near Parc Gerlache at around
4pm. [@1] Officers searched a group of people on rue Adolphe Krieps and
confiscated a small amount of hashish as well as a kitchen knife. [@1] One
member of the group was arrested after they drunkenly threatened the officers.
[@1] Another person was taken into custody after it was discovered they had
broken the law in another country and was on a police watchlist. [@1]
