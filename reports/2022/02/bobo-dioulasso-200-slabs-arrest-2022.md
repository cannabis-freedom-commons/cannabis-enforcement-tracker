Officers of the Hauts-Bassins Regional Judicial Police Service, acting to secure
the population and combat insecurity in all its forms, arrested a suspected drug
trafficker and seized 200 slabs of cannabis. [@1] The trafficker was 61 years
old and was arrested with other consumers who had come to stock up. [@1]

The National Police arrested a drug trafficker and seized about two hundred
slabs of cannabis at Bobo-Dioulasso, announcing the operation on Tuesday 1
February 2022 on its Facebook page. [@3] The suspect lived in the city of
Bobo-Dioulasso and stored his goods in a field in a farming hamlet outside the
city, where he received all his clients. [@1]

He transported his products by motorcycle from a neighbouring country at night
along rural tracks to supply the Bobo area as far as Mali, and had done so for
four years, according to the National Police. [@2] The National Police thanked
the public for cooperation that made this result possible. [@1] It invited the
public to become more involved in fighting drug abuse in all its forms, a
growing scourge causing serious harm especially among young people in Burkina
Faso. [@1]
