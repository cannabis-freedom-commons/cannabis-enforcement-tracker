Gendarmes of Squadron 16 "Clorinda", Argentina's border gendarmerie unit based
at the city of Clorinda, deployed near the hamlet of Paraje Garcete Cué after
being alerted to people moving on paths toward the border with Paraguay. [@1]
Personnel of the National Route 11 checkpoint "Gendarme Fermín Rolón" supported
the deployment. [@1] While patrolling on foot, the gendarmes saw two people
riding a motorcycle who threw a bag into the undergrowth when they noticed the
officers. [@1] The release names no arrests, and does not say whether the two
riders were stopped or pursued. [@1]

After searching the area, the officers opened the bundle in front of witnesses
and counted 17 rectangular packages holding a brown plant substance. [@1] In
front of witnesses, members of the force's Criminalistics and Forensic Studies
unit confirmed a total of 15 kilograms 205 grams of "cannabis sativa". [@1]
Officers seized the cannabis through the Federal Prosecutor's Office No. 1 of
Formosa. [@1]

A Corrientes news site, Tu Mercedes, carried the same Gendarmería account on 28
February 2022, with the same place, the same two motorcycle riders discarding
the bag and the same 15 kilograms 205 grams of cannabis sativa. [@2]
