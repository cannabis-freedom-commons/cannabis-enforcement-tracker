Moufide Bouchibi, described as France's biggest drug trafficker, was arrested in Dubai in March 2021
after nine years on the run and transferred to France. [@1,2,3,4] The French drugs squad had sought him
for more than 20 years. [@1] He headed the main networks importing Moroccan cannabis into Europe and had
lived outside France for more than ten years, between Algeria, Morocco and finally Dubai. [@1] Before his
arrest he was estimated to bring about 50 tonnes of cannabis into France each year. [@1]

## Earlier cases

He was first arrested in 2002 in Mulhouse, where gendarmes seized 240 kilos of cannabis with weapons and
stolen cars. [@1] At least five teams worked for him, communicating in coded language, leaving unsent
drafts in mailboxes and using up to 80 telephones in a set order. [@1] In 2011 a Mercedes carrying 750 kg
of cannabis was chased in Tarn-et-Garonne, leading investigators to a courier network supplying Agen and
Toulouse. [@4] In 2015 he was sentenced in his absence to 20 years in prison in Bordeaux [@1,2,3], where
14 of his associates were also heavily sentenced. [@4]

## Retrial and appeal

A new trial was due on 1 September 2021 [@1]; the Bordeaux court sentenced him to 16 years. [@3] On
appeal he was sentenced to 18 years for a series of cannabis go-fasts in 2011 and 2012 involving several
tonnes, with a two-thirds security period, a fine of one million euros and a customs fine of 2.9 million
euros. [@3] He denied being behind the trafficking, and the defence challenged the telephone intercepts
after learning that the exhibits had been destroyed; his lawyers said they would appeal to the Court of
Cassation. [@3] He is being tried separately over alleged leadership of a trafficking network in the
2010s. [@3]
