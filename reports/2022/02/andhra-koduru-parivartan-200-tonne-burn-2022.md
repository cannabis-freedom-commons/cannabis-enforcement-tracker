Andhra Pradesh police set ablaze more than 200,000 kilograms of seized ganja in an open area at Koduru village in Anakapalli mandal on Saturday 12 February 2022, in what the force described as the largest quantity of drugs destroyed anywhere in the country in one go. [@1,2] Director General of Police Gautam Sawang lit one of the heaps to start the event, organised with the Special Enforcement Bureau and the drug disposal committee, with fire-service and disaster-response personnel on standby and the press invited to watch. [@2]

## Parivartan campaign

The stock had been seized over the previous two years across Visakhapatnam, Vizianagaram, Srikakulam and East Godavari districts under Operation Parivartan, launched on 31 October 2021 to choke the supply side of the ganja trade on the Andhra-Odisha border. [@1,2] Police said the operation had produced 1,363 cases and 1,500 arrests, including 562 accused from other states, and had destroyed 7,552 acres of cannabis plants between November 2021 and February 2022. [@1,2] Police valued the burned stock at Rs 850 crore at the event. [@1]
