On 7 February 2022, Vellore narcotics intelligence police checked the
Tatanagar–Ernakulam Express at Kadappadi railway station. [@1] The check of a
passenger's bag found 1.8 kilograms of ganja inside it. [@1]

The suspect was identified as a 25-year-old from Vidyaratanpur, Cooch Behar
district, Assam, who was carrying the ganja for sale. [@2] Police arrested him
and seized the ganja. [@1]

The case was heard in the Salem Special Court for drugs, where a judge sentenced
him to seven months in prison and a 5,000 rupee fine. [@1]
