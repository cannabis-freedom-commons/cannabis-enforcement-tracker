Tunisia's customs administration said its officers at Tunis-Carthage airport
foiled an attempt to smuggle 3,400 grams of cannabis hidden in sweet packets
carried by a foreign woman passenger. [@2] The passenger had arrived from abroad
when customs officers checked her baggage. [@1] Officers found 100 packets of
cannabis sweets weighing 3,400 grams in all, the customs statement said. [@1]

After consulting the public prosecutor, customs drew up a report and sent the
file to the competent security services to continue the investigation. [@2]
Réalités said the woman was of foreign nationality and held the full 3,400 grams
of cannabis. [@3]
