Two gendarmes of the Saint-François brigade searched a home in Saint-François
over alleged thefts, then decided to also search the neighbouring plot. [@1] On
that neighbouring plot they found 387 cannabis plants. [@1]

Two neighbours were placed in garde à vue, French police custody, and both
admitted growing and holding the plants. [@1] The two occupants said the
plantation was for their personal consumption. [@2] Despite the large number of
plants seized, the owners maintained that the plants were for their own use.
[@1]

The gendarmerie of Guadeloupe released the account with a photo credited to it,
when two local outlets reported the discovery. [@2]
