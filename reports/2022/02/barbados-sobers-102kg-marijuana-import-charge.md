A 36-year-old Prior Park, St James businessman appeared in a Bridgetown
magistrates' court accused of importing more than $800 000 worth of marijuana
into Barbados in December 2021. [@1] He was also charged with possession,
possession with intent to supply and trafficking of 101.85 kilogrammes of the
drug, which had an estimated street value of $814 800, on December 30, 2021.
[@1]

He was not required to plead to the indictable charges. [@1] There were no
objections to bail, which Magistrate Manila Renee set at $100 000, secured with
two sureties. [@1] As a bail condition he must report to the District 'D' Police
Station every Friday by 6 p.m. with valid identification, and he was ordered to
surrender his travel documents to the District 'A' Magistrates' Court. [@1] His
next court appearance was set for February 15. [@1]
