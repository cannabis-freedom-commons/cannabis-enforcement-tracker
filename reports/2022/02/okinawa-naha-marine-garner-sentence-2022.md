US Marine Cpl. Nicholas Garner pleaded guilty to violating Japan's cannabis control and customs laws after cannabis products mailed to him were intercepted. [@1,2] A half gallon of cannabis liquid and nearly a quarter pound of cannabis flower arrived in the mail at Camp Foster, Okinawa, on 7 June 2021. [@1] He had a small amount of cannabis and liquid on him when arrested. [@1]

The Naha District Court sentenced him on 18 February 2022 to two years in prison with hard labour and fined him $4,344. [@1,2] Two other Marines faced separate proceedings over a similar mailed package intercepted in April 2021. [@1]
