Nagapattinam police arrested six people near Puthur on 1 February 2022 and
seized 500 kg of ganja allegedly bound for Sri Lanka by sea. [@1] Nagapattinam
Superintendent of Police G. Jawahar said police acted on a tip-off about ganja
bundles being moved in vehicles. [@1] A police team kept watch on vehicles
coming toward Nagapattinam on Tuesday morning and stopped two cars near the
Puthur roundabout. [@2]

Officers found about 250 bundles weighing about 2 kg each, making up the 500 kg
haul. [@1] The ganja was concealed under chicken feed in a pickup truck
travelling behind the two cars. [@2] Police seized the 500 kg haul along with
the vehicles and arrested the six people. [@1] Jawahar put the market value of
the seized drugs at over Rs 1 crore. [@1]

Police said the group had smuggled the ganja from Andhra Pradesh via
Tiruvannamalai. [@2] The consignment was to be taken to Akkaraipettai and then
smuggled to Sri Lanka by fishing boat. [@2] The six suspects were produced
before a magistrate and remanded to judicial custody. [@2]
