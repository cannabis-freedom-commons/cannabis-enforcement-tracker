Luxembourg customs officers were checking vehicles at the "Centre douanier" in
Luxembourg City at around 11am on Tuesday 15 February 2022 when a car drove off
from the check. [@2] RTL Today reported that customs officers had been checking
vehicles on the route d'Esch that morning. [@1] Police officers who had been
alerted arrested the two people in the car on the orders of the public
prosecutor's office. [@2]

About 300 grams of marijuana were found and seized by police. [@2] Two house
searches turned up further drugs as well as two weapons. [@2]

The two suspects were to be brought before an investigating judge during the
morning. [@2] RTL Today reported that the two suspects would be presented to a
judge on Wednesday morning. [@1]

L'essentiel reported that customs agents began the pursuit of the car and that
the police then took over. [@3]
