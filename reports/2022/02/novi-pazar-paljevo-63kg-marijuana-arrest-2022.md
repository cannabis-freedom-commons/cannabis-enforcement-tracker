Police in Novi Pazar seized about 63 kilograms of marijuana and arrested five
people on suspicion of the unauthorised production and distribution of narcotic
drugs. [@1] Two of the suspects, a 22-year-old and a 35-year-old, are citizens
of Montenegro. [@2] The other three suspects, aged 27, 25 and 36, are all from
Tutin, a town in south-western Serbia near Novi Pazar. [@2]

Police found the five suspects at Paljevo, a village near Novi Pazar, as they
moved three bags of marijuana from one Golf car into another. [@1] The Interior
Ministry's statement said the arrests came during a handover of the drug between
the two cars. [@3] The Interior Ministry put the seizure at about 63 kilograms
of marijuana. [@4]

On the order of the competent prosecutor's office, all five were detained for up
to 48 hours. [@1] They were to be brought to the prosecutor's office with a
criminal complaint after the detention. [@2]
