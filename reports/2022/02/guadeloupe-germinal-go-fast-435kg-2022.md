Crew of the French navy frigate Germinal stopped a go-fast boat that fled and
dumped bales into the sea on the night of 11 February 2022. [@1] The five people
on board were stopped, and 11 bales recovered from the water tested positive for
cannabis and weighed 435 kg. [@1] Three of the five were French and two
Venezuelan, and all were held in detention after the seizure. [@2]

RCI reported that the investigation was handed to an investigating judge. [@2]
On the instruction of the Fort-de-France public prosecutor, the drugs, the boat
and the five suspects were handed to the Caribbean branch of OFAST, France's
anti-drug office. [@1] The operation was run under the direction of Martinique's
prefect, the government's delegate for state action at sea, and the commander of
the Antilles maritime zone. [@1]

The authorities said the seizure cost traffickers a loss of more than one
million euros, for a cargo probably intended for Caribbean consumers. [@1] It
was the armed forces' second drug seizure of 2022, after the Germinal recovered
680 kg of cocaine on 29 January. [@1]
