Nasyid singer and composer Muhammad Yasin Sulaiman was reported arrested
over drug charges on March 31, 2022. [@2] He stood accused of trafficking
about 214g of cannabis at his Kota Damansara residence on the evening of
March 24. [@1]

On July 6 the prosecution withdrew the trafficking charge, which carried the
death penalty or life imprisonment, and the magistrate granted him a discharge
not amounting to an acquittal. [@1] The same day Yasin pleaded not guilty to
ingesting a cannabis metabolite at the Petaling Jaya police narcotics office
on the night of March 24, an offence punishable by a fine or up to two years
in jail. [@1]

He also pleaded not guilty before the Sessions Court to possessing 193.7g of
cannabis at his home, a charge carrying life imprisonment or at least five
years plus caning. [@1]
