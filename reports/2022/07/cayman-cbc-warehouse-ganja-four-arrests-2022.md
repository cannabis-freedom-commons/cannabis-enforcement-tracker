Four men were arrested after Customs and Border Control officers, assisted by
police, found ganja in a consignment at the CBC transit warehouse on Owen
Roberts Drive in George Town. [@1] Three of the men, aged 23 and 52 from George
Town and 27 from West Bay, were arrested on suspicion of being concerned with
the importation of ganja. [@2] A fourth man, aged 30 and from George Town, was
arrested over CBC revenue-related offences and on suspicion of being concerned
in the importation of ganja. [@4] All four were on bail while the investigation
continued. [@1]

The operation began when CBC officers secured cargo of interest that had been
imported on Wednesday, 6 July. [@2] A CBC K-9 indicated a particular pallet, and
officers who inspected it found several large packages of ganja, each containing
smaller packets. [@2] The suspected drugs were seized and secured at the
warehouse. [@2] Police described the amount as “a significant amount” and did
not reveal its weight or value. [@3]

The RCIPS described the material as “vegetable matter resembling ganja”. [@2]
