Brunei's Narcotics Control Bureau (NCB) detained 18 people aged between 18
and 53 in a string of operations codenamed Mary Jane against marijuana
dealing syndicates. [@1] Raids were carried out over two
weeks at residences in Kampong Mata-Mata, Kampong Lambak Kiri, Kampong Panchor
Mengkubau, Kampong Tanah Jambu, Kampong Rimba, Kampong Sungai Besar and
Kampong Mentiri in Brunei-Muara District. [@1]

The NCB said the syndicate was distributing cannabis and targeting young
users. [@1] Officers seized 877.42 g of cannabis and 7.13 g of cannabis
edibles, along with 14 ecstasy pills, BND8,634 in cash, drug paraphernalia,
digital weighing scales, three cars and mobile phones. [@1] Contraband
cigarettes and alcohol found during the operation were handed to customs for
investigation. [@1]

All those arrested were under investigation under the Misuse of Drugs Act for
possession for distribution, possession, consumption and related offences, and
for money laundering. [@1]
