The Volta Regional Office of Ghana's Narcotics Control Commission (NACOC), with National Security operatives and soldiers of the 66 Artillery Regiment in Ho, intercepted 17,522 slabs of cannabis with a gross weight of 8,761 kg between Adaklu Trekpe and the Ho Sports Development Centre. [@1,2] The drugs had an estimated street value of GH¢17,522,000. [@1,2]

Acting Director of Public Affairs and International Relations Francis Opoku Amoah disclosed the seizure to the Ghanaian Times in Accra. [@1,2] The squad stopped a Renault freight truck with registration GE 8246-14 as it was leaving Ho, following several weeks of monitoring and intelligence gathering in the Ho enclave. [@2] Officers found 222 bags holding the 17,522 slabs inside the truck. [@2]

The truck driver fled after noticing the security officers, and NACOC said further inquiries were under way to find the truck's owner. [@2] The commission stressed that Ghana's Narcotics Control Commission Act 2020 (Act 1019) does not legalise cannabis, allowing only licensed industrial and medical use of plants with under 0.3 percent THC. [@2]
