Police in Búzi district, Sofala province, detained a man found with more than
150 kilograms of cannabis sativa, known locally as suruma. [@1] The detainee is
46 years old, according to the police. [@1] Police suspect him of producing the
drug and supplying it to the cities of Beira and Dondo and to Nhamatanda
district. [@1] The detention followed an anonymous tip-off, according to Duílio
Mabai, the PRM district commander in Búzi. [@1] The suspect admitted producing
and selling the suruma and said he now regretted it. [@1] The cannabis, suruma,
was in the trafficking route, the commander added. [@2]

Club of Mozambique's English account names the same 46-year-old suspect as a
cannabis farmer and a supplier to Beira, Dondo and Nhamatanda. [@2] MZNews
reported that the 46-year-old was detained in possession of more than 150 kg of
cannabis sativa, called suruma, in Búzi. [@3] According to Duílio Mabai, the
district police commander, the detainee was suspected of producing and supplying
the drug to Beira and Dondo and in Nhamatanda district. [@3] Cannabis sativa is
called suruma in Mozambique. [@1]
