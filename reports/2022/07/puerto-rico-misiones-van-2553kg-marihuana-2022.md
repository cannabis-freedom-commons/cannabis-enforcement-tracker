Gendarmerie officers stopped a Mercedes Benz Sprinter van and seized 2,611
packages totalling 2,553.206 kilograms of cannabis sativa from it in the
Misiones city of Puerto Rico. [@1] Members of Escuadrón 11 “San Ignacio”,
together with other units of the force, were patrolling Puerto Rico when they
stopped the van, driven by an Argentine citizen. [@1] The van held large
bundles, three amorphous and 93 rectangular, that gave off a strong smell of
marijuana. [@1] The gendarmes opened the bundles in the presence of witnesses
and with the authorisation of the federal court in Oberá, and field Narcotest
tests found the material was cannabis sativa. [@1] The gendarmes seized the
entire drug load and detained the driver under Law 23.737, Argentina's narcotics
law. [@1]

A separate Conclusión account reproduces the same Gendarmería statement with the
same place, quantities and detention. [@2]
