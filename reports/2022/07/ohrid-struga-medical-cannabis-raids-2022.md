In the summer of 2022 police raided two cannabis plantations near Lake Ohrid
within 48 hours. [@1] Both plantations held licences to grow cannabis for
medical use. [@1] Criminal police with the medical-cannabis commission and Ohrid
police searched a legal entity in Orovnik, Debrca municipality on a court order.
[@2] Officers then searched a facility in Drsalajca, Struga municipality,
closing its medical-cannabis factory. [@3] The action in Drsalajca took place in
a building in that village. [@3] The Ohrid farm was operated by Greengo 2018
with three owners while the Struga farm was the property of Green and Bio Farm
with two owners. [@1] Permits were revoked after the raids and Struga
prosecutors opened investigations into both firms for regulatory violations and
illegal drug production and trade. [@1]

In the Orovnik safe room officers found 343 kg of dry flower, 7 kg more than
recorded. [@2] In the attic officers found vessels used for cannabis-oil
production although the firm had no permit for such oil. [@2] At Drsalajca the
video did not cover the whole facility and the fence had holes allowing
unimpeded entry and exit. [@3] Officers found 3,442 outdoor plants without
protection against 4,304 reported. [@3] Officers found another 1,007 plants plus
seedlings in an outer parcel where the firm had only an expansion permit, not a
planting permit. [@3] Health-ministry experts said those Drsalajca plants could
have produced about 2.5 tons worth five million euros on the black market. [@3]
In the Orovnik case a prosecutor was notified and all items were seized. [@2] In
the Struga case a prosecutor was notified and all items were seized. [@3]

North Macedonia legalised medical cultivation in 2015 with a five-person
health-ministry commission tracking production and inspecting plantations twice
a year. [@1] By 2021 more than 60 firms held permits under strict
record-keeping, surveillance and fencing rules, with around half producing. [@1]
The violations uncovered suggested much of the product was heading to the
illegal market. [@1] A settlement was reached in the first case while the second
remained in regular procedure. [@1]
