Tunisia's customs administration announced on Thursday 21 July 2022 that it had
seized 60 slabs of cannabis weighing 6 kg, along with 11 capsules of cocaine
weighing 100 grams. [@1] Customs said the drugs were found at the port of La
Goulette aboard a truck that had come from the port of Marseille, driven by a
Tunisian living abroad. [@1]

The customs service said the drugs were discovered during a check carried out by
the units responsible for inspecting travellers. [@1]
