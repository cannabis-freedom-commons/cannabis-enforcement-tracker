Police in Bad Berleburg, in the rural Siegen-Wittgenstein district about 130
kilometres north of Frankfurt, harvested a hemp plantation and secured 2,500
plants. [@1] Passers-by had noticed the illegal drug field, about 300 square
metres, on a remote potato field and reported it, after which the officers
moved in and collected the mature plants. [@1,2]

The plantation, roughly 3,000 square feet in size, held about 2,500 plants.
[@2] The plants were sent for testing to determine their cannabinoid content.
[@2] The farmer who owns the field may be charged under the Narcotics Act if
the investigation shows involvement in or knowledge of the cultivation, even if
the plants turn out to be low-THC hemp. [@2]
