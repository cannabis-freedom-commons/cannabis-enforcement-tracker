Police ranks from Regional Division Five acting on information received made a
drug bust at about 12:00 hrs on 2 July 2022 in which 52,617 grams of suspected
cannabis was found and one person was arrested. [@1] The officers went to an
area on the left bank of the Abary River, about 40 miles in from the public
road, where they saw a two-storey wooden and concrete house on a plot of land.
[@2] There they found a 23-year-old labourer from Tempie Village, West Coast
Berbice, who was arrested and cautioned over the suspected cannabis. [@2]

A search of the house found four salt bags in a room on the lower flat, holding
31 wrapped parcels of leaves, seeds and stems suspected to be cannabis. [@2] The
labourer said the place belongs to "Jah Jah" and that he is just the caretaker.
[@1] Police said they were searching for the owner of the house while
investigations continued. [@1] The accounts came from a Police Headquarters
statement, which described the material as suspected cannabis; no charge or
court outcome was reported. [@2]
