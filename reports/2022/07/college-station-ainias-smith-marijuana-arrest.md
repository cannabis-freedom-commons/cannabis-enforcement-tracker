Texas A&M senior wide receiver Ainias Smith, 21, was arrested early on Wednesday
20 July 2022 by Texas A&M University police. [@1] Smith was booked into the
Brazos County Jail and released the same day after posting combined bail of
$8,000. [@1] KBTX reported that Smith was charged with driving while
intoxicated, unlawful carrying of a weapon and misdemeanor possession of less
than two ounces of marijuana. [@2]

Officers pulled him over for speeding on University Drive just after 2 a.m., and
a search of the car found a loaded pistol and a single rolled joint. [@2] Smith
consented to a breathalyzer test that twice read below the legal limit, at .066
and .061. [@2] Smith and a passenger both denied owning the gun or the
marijuana, and the passenger was released without arrest. [@1]

Texas A&M suspended Smith under athletics department policy, and he did not
attend SEC media days in Atlanta, though the suspension was lifted within nine
days. [@1]

The Brazos County attorney refused to prosecute the DWI and weapons charges, and
Smith's suspension was lifted. [@3] County attorney Earl Gray said the marijuana
weighed 0.29 ounces (about 8.2 g) and that neither occupant had claimed it. [@3]
Gray said he understood there could be an appearance of special treatment for a
high-profile player but said his decision rested on a lack of evidence. [@3]
