Officers of the Andijan regional internal affairs directorate and other
law-enforcement agencies detained A.O. and M.N., both from Pakhtaabad, during an
operation in the district on 15 July 2022, the regional police press service
said. [@1] The interior ministry's own statement named the units involved as
operative-search officers, patrol and public-safety units, special
rapid-response units and the canine service of the Andijan regional directorate,
working with the district police department. [@2] The police said the two men
had entered a criminal conspiracy in advance and tried to sell hashish to
another citizen on the district's territory. [@2]

The police press service said the parcel of hashish weighed 455.5 grams and the
men asked 5,000 dollars for it from the buyer. [@1] The Uzbek-language account
of the same statement gives the weight as 455.5 grams of hashish offered for
5,000 US dollars. [@3]

The investigating group at the Pakhtaabad district police department opened a
criminal case under part 5 of Article 273 of Uzbekistan's Criminal Code,
covering illegal sale of narcotics, and investigative actions were under way.
[@1] The Uzbek-language version says that if the suspects' guilt is proven in
court they face between 10 and 20 years in prison. [@3]
