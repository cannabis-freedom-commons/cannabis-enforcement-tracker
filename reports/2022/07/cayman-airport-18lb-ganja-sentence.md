Toni Lee Danso, a Canadian national, pleaded guilty to a single charge of
importation of ganja. [@1] She had made her initial Summary Court appearance one
week after being arrested. [@1] Magistrate Angelyn Hernandez sentenced Danso to
18 months' imprisonment, with a recommendation for deportation on completion of
the sentence. [@1]

Danso attempted to smuggle 18 pounds of ganja into Cayman in hollowed-out
compartments of her suitcase. [@1] Officers found what appeared to be packages
of ganja concealed beneath a false bottom on both sides of the suitcase. [@1]
Officers removed 18 heat-sealed packages of ganja weighing a total of 18 pounds
from the suitcase. [@1]

Danso arrived aboard a WestJet flight from Toronto, Canada, on 10 February that
year. [@1] During immigration screening, Customs and Border Control officers
noticed what they described as a strong odour of glue coming from her luggage.
[@1] Danso admitted that she had lied to officers when initially confronted.
[@1] She later told Customs and Border Control and police that she had received
the luggage two days before arriving in Cayman from a man she knew only as
Ruben. [@1] Danso said she did not know how the ganja got into her bag because
airport staff in Toronto had shown her an X-ray of the bag and cleared it. [@1]
The court ordered the luggage and the drugs to be destroyed. [@1]
