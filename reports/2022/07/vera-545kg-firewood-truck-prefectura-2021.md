Specialist officers of the Prefectura Naval seized marijuana hidden under a
firewood load in a truck and detained three people. [@1] The operation was
carried out in Vera, in Santa Fe province. [@1] Officers intercepted the suspect
truck at kilometre 722 of National Route 11. [@1] They also stopped a utility
car that was acting as a puntero, a scout meant to warn the truck's occupant to
avoid roadside checks. [@1] The Federal Prosecutor's Office of Reconquista said
the stop was made on the afternoon of 22 April 2021, when officers found a
Renault Kangoo and a Scania truck loaded with firewood parked on the shoulder in
Vera. [@3]

Investigation had indicated that a truck carrying marijuana from Resistencia, in
Chaco, would pass through Vera on its way to Rosario. [@1] Under the firewood,
officers found 23 bundles holding 413 panes, pressed blocks of the drug, a
little more than 545 kilograms. [@1] The prosecutor's office later recorded the
weight as 545,412 kilograms of marijuana hidden among the firewood. [@3]
Prefectura valued the marijuana at more than 65,400,000 pesos. [@2] Federal
judge Aldo Alurralde of Reconquista ordered the marijuana seized and the three
people detained. [@1] The Prefectura's own account said the judge ordered the
detention of two men and one woman. [@2]

El Litoral reported that Judge Alurralde then processed the three with pretrial
detention for aggravated transport of narcotics by three or more people
organised to commit it. [@4] The paper named the truck driver as Roberto García,
60, the owner as Julio Ireneo Hoyos, 39, and his companion as Olga Noemí
Ledesma, 48, all from Resistencia. [@4] The judge ordered both the Scania truck
and the Kangoo seized, and set a 500,000 peso asset embargo on each of the
three. [@4] On 13 July 2022, federal prosecutor Roberto Salum asked Judge
Alurralde to send six people to trial for transporting 545,412 kilograms of
marijuana from Chaco toward Rosario. [@3] The prosecutor said the six should
face trial as authors of aggravated transport of narcotics by three or more
people organised to commit it. [@3] In October 2021, searches in Chaco,
Corrientes and Santa Fe detained the alleged supplier of the load. [@3] Searches
at two homes in Ibarlucea seized a further 2,204 kilograms of marijuana and six
cannabis plants, a later find kept out of the truck-load figures. [@3]
