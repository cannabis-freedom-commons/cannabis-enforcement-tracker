Singapore's Central Narcotics Bureau arrested 100 suspected drug offenders in an
island-wide operation from 18 to 22 July 2022. [@1] Areas covered included Boon
Lay, Changi, Sengkang and Toa Payoh. [@1] On 19 July officers raided a
residential unit near Sumang Walk and arrested a 56-year-old Singaporean man for
suspected drug trafficking. [@1] On 21 July officers arrested a 46-year-old
Singaporean woman and a 41-year-old male foreign national in a private
residential enclave near Haig Road. [@1] On 21 July officers arrested four male
foreign nationals aged 22 to 30 near Lorong 7 Geylang, catching a 25-year-old
after he tried to evade arrest. [@1]

Officers seized about 9,339g of cannabis along with heroin, Ice, ketamine, new
psychoactive substances, Ecstasy tablets, an Erimin-5 tablet, an LSD stamp and
six bottles of liquid suspected to contain GHB, with an estimated street value
of $470,000 for all drugs together. [@1] The bureau said the 9,339g of cannabis
could feed the addiction of about 1,330 abusers for a week. [@1] In the Lorong 9
Geylang hideout officers recovered about 9,282g of cannabis plus heroin, Ice and
Ecstasy tablets, while smaller amounts of 32g and 3g of cannabis were recovered
from two men near Lorong 7. [@1] That smaller Lorong 7 recovery is reported here
to account for the cannabis split within the Geylang cases. [@1] In the Haig
Road hideout officers recovered about 18g of cannabis along with Ice, ketamine,
Ecstasy tablets, an LSD stamp and paraphernalia. [@1] Mothership's account
confirms the Lorong 9 hideout yielded about 9,282g of cannabis with heroin, Ice
and Ecstasy tablets. [@2]

Trafficking, offering to traffic or doing any act preparatory to trafficking is
an offence under Section 5 of the Misuse of Drugs Act 1973. [@1] The release
notes that trafficking more than 500g of cannabis may face the mandatory death
penalty. [@1] Investigations into all arrested suspects were ongoing. [@1]
