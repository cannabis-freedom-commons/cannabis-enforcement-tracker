Operatives of the Drug Law Enforcement Agency of The Gambia (DLEAG) arrested
eleven suspects with suspected cannabis at venues across the country between 13
and 15 July 2022. [@1,2]

Serving Gambia Armed Forces member Seedy Camara was arrested at Busumbala on 13
July around 19:10 with two bundles and ten parcels of suspected cannabis
sativa. [@1,2] Agency spokesperson Ousman Saidybah said Camara was in custody
pending handover to the Military Police under armed-forces rules, after which
he would be handed back to DLEAG for prosecution. [@1,2]

In a separate development, 27-year-old Sheikh Omar Hydara was arrested at Brufut
on 15 July with three big bundles, three parcels and further quantities he
said he got from a friend in Casamance. [@1,2] Hydara jumped from a window of
an uncompleted building to evade arrest; scans showed no fracture and he was
put on bail with his prescription medication procured. [@1,2]

Officers also apprehended 18-year-old Samba Cham with one parcel at Busumbala
on 14 July, and Cham together with Babucarr Drammeh, Basiru Fatty, Dembo Marong
and Musa Baldeh with thirteen parcels the same evening. [@1,2] Sheikh Susso was
taken at Tallinding with three wraps, Sierra Leonean Sheka Bangura at Latrikunda
German with two parcels and a wrap, and 19-year-old Modou Salieu Bah of Old
Jeshwang with one and a half parcels. [@1,2]
