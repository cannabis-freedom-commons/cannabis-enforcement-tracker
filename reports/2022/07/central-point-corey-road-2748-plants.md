Detectives from the Illegal Marijuana Enforcement Team, drawn from the Jackson
County Sheriff's Office and the Medford Police Department, served a search
warrant on a grow site in the 4100 block of Corey Road in Central Point on
Thursday 14 July 2022. [@1] An armed suspect tried to flee but was found hiding
at a property in the 3400 block of Randall Avenue and arrested. [@1] Police said
he carried a pistol and booked him into the Jackson County Jail on charges of
interfering with a peace officer, resisting arrest, second-degree criminal
trespass, unlawful use of a weapon, unlawful carrying or concealing of a firearm
and unlawful manufacture of marijuana. [@2] Eight other people found on the
property were detained, interviewed and released without charge. [@2]

Investigators said the property held about 2,748 illegal cannabis plants in
seven greenhouses, about 1,000 pounds (454 kilograms) of processed black-market
marijuana and seven firearms. [@1] The cannabis was seized and destroyed on the
site. [@3] The sheriff's office said one rifle seized was a "ghost gun" with no
serial number, and one handgun had been reported stolen in Florida. [@1]

The case followed a months-long investigation of a black-market growing
operation that found no licence for any cannabis growing, handling or processing
at the location. [@1] The sheriff's office records the case as JCSO Case
22-4007, and the team said it was focusing on the black-market marijuana trade
in the Rogue Valley. [@4] Jackson County Code Enforcement also responded and
issued citations totalling $18,000 for unapproved marijuana production,
unapproved greenhouses, solid waste and riparian corridor destruction. [@1]
