A 37-year-old woman from Liverpool was sentenced at Douglas Courthouse on 1 July
2022 to four and a half years in prison for smuggling cocaine and heroin hidden
inside her body to the Isle of Man. [@1] Deemster Graeme Cook, the island's
judge, also imposed a five-year exclusion order banning her from returning to
the island after her release. [@1] She had admitted two counts of possession of
a Class A drug with intent to supply and one count of possession of cannabis.
[@1]

Police arrested her on 22 June 2022 after finding her with a small amount of
cannabis in a UK-registered car in Port St Mary. [@1] Officers spotted her in
the passenger seat of a white Ford Fiesta at about 12:40 BST, and she admitted
to carrying about five pounds worth of cannabis. [@1] Isle of Man Today reported
that the cannabis was a small amount of resin, about one gram, which she handed
over voluntarily. [@3]

Suspicious that she was concealing more drugs, police took her to Noble's
Hospital, where the cocaine and heroin were recovered in two golf ball-sized
packages. [@1] The packages held 20.1 grams of cocaine, which police valued at
£2,010, and 7.7 grams of heroin, valued at between £962.50 and £1,925, according
to Manx Radio. [@2]

Her defence advocate said the inevitable prison sentence would be a "rude
awakening", while the deemster said the concealment gave the crime an element of
sophistication. [@1] Isle of Man police said the sentence sent a clear message
that police and courts would deal robustly with drug dealing and importation.
[@2]
