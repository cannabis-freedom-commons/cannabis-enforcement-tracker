Peruvian Rodrigo Ventocilla, a 32-year-old transgender activist studying at
Harvard University, was detained at Bali's Denpasar-Ngurah airport on 6 August
2022. [@1] His husband Sebastián Marallano arrived on a different flight and was
also detained when he tried to help him. [@1] Peru's foreign ministry said
Indonesian authorities reported that the detention followed the discovery in
their luggage of items containing traces of cannabis and various products made
from it. [@1]

Ventocilla died in police custody at a hospital in Bali on 11 August. [@1] A
Bali police spokesman said he died of failure of organs throughout his body.
[@1] The police account says he was taken to hospital after taking narcotics
that the search leading to his detention had not seized. [@1] His family denied
the official version, accused the Indonesian police of mistreating him and of
blocking access to the lawyer assigned to him, and described his arrest as an
act of racial discrimination and transphobia. [@1] The family said police in
Bali demanded exorbitant sums of money for the release of the two Peruvians;
police later denied accusations of violence. [@1]

Peru's foreign ministry rejected the family's account and said it was publicly
known that Indonesia maintains a zero-tolerance policy on possession of drugs
and drug products. [@1] Authorities seized several items suspected to contain
marijuana from Ventocilla, including an herb grinder and edibles weighing 231.65
grams, according to Bali police. [@2]
