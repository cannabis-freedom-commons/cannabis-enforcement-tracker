Livermore police arrested two people over the robbery and carjacking of cannabis
dispensary delivery drivers in the city. [@1] Zulfiqar Khan, 25, and a
17-year-old boy were arrested in connection with the thefts. [@1]

The robberies happened on 17 and 19 August 2022, and two separate ones took
place on 17 August, one on Alameda Drive and the other on Cottonwood Court. [@1]
Police said the suspects placed an order and then robbed the drivers who arrived
to make the delivery. [@1] The suspects stole large amounts of cannabis products
and hundreds of dollars in cash, according to police. [@1]
