Brest customs officers stopped a supply channel into Belarus after seizing about
160 kg of hashish from a lorry that had crossed from Poland at the Kozlovichi
border checkpoint. [@1] The drug was hidden behind the lining of the front wall
of the semi-trailer. [@1] The Belarusian customs committee said the supply
channel had been organised by citizens of Belarus and European Union countries.
[@1]

A freight Mercedes-Benz truck was travelling through the Kozlovichi checkpoint
with documents from a Polish haulier declaring a consignment of foil and
fastening elements. [@1] Officers sent the vehicle for an X-ray scan, which
showed unusual attachments behind the front wall of the refrigerated trailer.
[@1] After the lining panels were removed, officers found a cavity holding 186
packets of a light-brown substance. [@1]

Experts from the Brest Region directorate of the State Committee of Judicial
Examinations concluded that the contents of one of the packets was hashish. [@1]
The customs committee put the total weight at about 160 kg, said it would have
made more than 320,000 doses and valued it at more than 5 million US dollars on
the black market. [@1]

Brest Customs opened a criminal case against a group of people under part 2 of
article 328-1 of the Belarusian Criminal Code, on illegal movement of narcotics
across the customs border. [@1] The article carries a prison term of up to ten
years, the customs committee said. [@1] Four suspects in organising the supply
channel were detained in operations run with the drug-control department of the
Brest Regional Executive Committee's internal affairs directorate and the
Belarusian Interior Ministry. [@1] The state news agency BELTA reported the same
seizure, saying the State Customs Committee had announced the case and that the
truck was a Mercedes travelling through Kozlovichi. [@2]
