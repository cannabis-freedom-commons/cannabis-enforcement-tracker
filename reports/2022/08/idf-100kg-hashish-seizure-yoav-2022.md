On 20 August 2022, Israeli troops seized NIS 4 million worth of hashish in a
drug bust near the Egyptian border, the military announced Saturday. [@1]
According to the Israel Defense Forces, military lookouts spotted a vehicle in
the southern Yoav Regional Brigade that was suspected of attempting to smuggle
drugs. [@1]

IDF troops and Border Police operating in the area later found bags containing
some 100 kilograms of hashish, a haul the military estimated was worth NIS 4
million (over $1.2 million). [@1]

The IDF announced the arrest of a suspect later Saturday, after scouring the
area. [@1] Both the suspect and the drugs were handed over to police as part of
the investigation. [@1]

The article's caption locates the seizure near the southern border with Jordan.
