Toowoomba police with the Drug and Serious Crime Group and Proston police
executed a search warrant at a former cattle station at Boondooma, about 110
km north-west of Kingaroy, at about 6 a.m. on 5 August 2022. [@1] On the
property, bought only six months earlier for its remoteness, police found
several greenhouses measuring about 130 by 60 metres. [@1, 2]

Inside, officers seized about 15,680 cannabis plants and 50 kg of dried
cannabis, plus generators and hydroponic equipment, with an estimated street
value of $39.9 million. [@1, 2] All seized plants were destroyed and the
equipment dismantled. [@1]

Seven men aged 35 to 46 were arrested and each charged with one count of
producing and one count of possessing a dangerous drug. [@1, 2] They appeared
in Murgon Magistrates Court and were remanded in custody to reappear on 16
August. [@1, 2]

The raid followed an 18 July intercept on the Warrego Highway at Helidon, where
officers found about 200 kg of processed cannabis in a Brisbane-bound vehicle
that police allege came from the Boondooma property. [@1, 2]
