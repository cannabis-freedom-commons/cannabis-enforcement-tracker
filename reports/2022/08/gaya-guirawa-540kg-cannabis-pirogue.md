Gaya's river brigade of the gendarmerie seized drugs on the River Niger on
Wednesday 17 August 2022. [@1] The operation took place at the Guirawa post in
the rural commune of Tounouga, 23 km from the town of Gaya. [@1] Officers
presented the seized drugs to the departmental authorities on the evening of 17
August. [@1] No number or identity of the people involved in the trafficking had
been disclosed at the time of publication. [@1]

Officers seized 560 bricks weighing 540 kg of cannabis, known locally as chanvre
indien, from a motorised pirogue crossing the River Niger toward a neighbouring
country. [@1] The 540 kg were packed in seven bales of 80 bricks each. [@1]

Gaya department borders Benin to the west and Nigeria to the south and is
described as a hub for drug and other trafficking. [@1] The department lies in
southern Niger in the Dosso region. [@1]
