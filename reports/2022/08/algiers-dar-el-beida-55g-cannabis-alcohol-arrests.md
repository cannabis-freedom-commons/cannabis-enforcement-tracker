Police of the Dar El Beida administrative district in Algiers arrested two
suspects and seized 55 grams of cannabis resin and marijuana together with 935
bottles of alcoholic drink, the Directorate General of National Security (DGSN)
said in a statement. [@1]

The case began when officers of the district's judicial police brigade searched
a foreign suspect during patrols and found a bag of 20 rolled paper parcels
holding 25 grams of cannabis and marijuana. [@3] A search warrant issued by the
territorially competent public prosecutor led officers to search the suspect's
home. [@1] At the home, officers detained a second suspect, a foreign woman, and
seized the 935 bottles of alcohol. [@3]

Beyond the drugs, the two suspects were also accused of selling alcohol without
a licence and of harbouring foreigners who had entered and stayed in Algeria
illegally. [@2] After the legal procedures were completed, the two suspects were
brought before the territorially competent courts. [@1]
