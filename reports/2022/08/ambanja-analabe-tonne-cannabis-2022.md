Gendarmes from the Antsohihy territorial company, working with Madagascar
National Parks agents, seized one tonne of cannabis. [@1] L'Express de
Madagascar put the haul at nearly one tonne. [@2] L'Express de Madagascar
reported that the cannabis was concealed in 42 woven polyethylene sacks. [@2]

The operation took place at the end of the week. [@2] The deployment took place
at night. [@2] Caught by surprise, the traffickers fled and abandoned their
cargo at the site. [@2]

On inspection, the gendarmes also found that the traffickers had used the
forest's natural shelter to plant thousands of cannabis plants. [@1] The
cannabis was grown deep in the forest, in nearly impassable and
difficult-to-reach areas, and the loads were carried on men's backs through
paths hidden in the woods and at passage points. [@2] The seized tonne, called
“zamal,” was expected to be destroyed in the following days. [@1]
