On 9 August 2022, police searched three separate premises in the Bodden Town
district during a proactive operation under the Misuse of Drugs Act. [@2] Five
people from Bodden Town were arrested on drug offences, comprising men aged 28,
35, 37 and 39 and a 25-year-old woman. [@2] Three men aged 28, 37 and 39 were
charged with possession of ganja and possession of ganja with intent to supply.
[@2] The 35-year-old man was charged with possession of ganja. [@2] The four
charged men appeared in court on 12 August 2022. [@1] The 25-year-old woman was
granted bail as investigations continued. [@2]

Officers recovered over 300 pounds of suspected ganja, about 136 kg, along with
drug utensils and a large quantity of cash. [@2] Police described the cash as a
large but undisclosed quantity seized in addition to the ganja and utensils.
[@1]

The Royal Cayman Islands Police Service announced the charges on 12 August and
said the charged men were appearing in court that day. [@2]
