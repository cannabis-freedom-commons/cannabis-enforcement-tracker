Police and anti-drug agents uprooted and burned about 12,000 fully grown
marijuana plants in a plantation at Sitio Binongsay in Barangay Malin-awa, Tabuk
City, on 4 and 5 August 2022. [@1] The eradication was carried out in Sitio
(sub-village) Binongsay in the village of Malin-awa and was named Oplan Lindol
by the police. [@1]

Kalinga police spokesperson Police Major Garry Gayamos said the uprooted plants
were worth P2.4 million and came from a 1.2-hectare plantation. [@1] No
cultivators were arrested in the operation. [@1]

Police said in a statement on Saturday, 6 August, that the operation came out of
cooperation between local law enforcement partners and the public, which
provided information. [@1]
