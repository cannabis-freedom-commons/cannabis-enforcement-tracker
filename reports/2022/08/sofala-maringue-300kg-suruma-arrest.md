Police in Marínguè district, in Sofala province, detained one Mozambican citizen
found in possession of cannabis. [@1] The suspect is a farmer who, by all
available indications, dedicates himself to cultivating the drug. [@1] António
Semendes, director of the Gabinete do Combate a Droga, the drug-combat office in
Sofala, addressed the detention. [@1] Semendes said the detainee is one of those
responsible for most of the cannabis sativa that reaches Beira. [@1]

Officers seized three hundred kilograms of cannabis sativa from the suspect’s
possession. [@1] The seized drug is cannabis sativa, commonly known as suruma.
[@1] The quantity therefore amounts to 300 kg of suruma held in a single
possession case. [@1]

Maringuè is the district where both the detention and the seizure took place,
within Sofala province. [@1] According to Semendes, official steps were already
under way to have the entire seized drug consignment incinerated by the
following week. [@1] The incineration was presented as the planned next step for
the seized consignment. [@1]
