Police arrested 22-year-old Cameron Lenzini after the August 21 explosion at a
house on the 1000 block of Russell Avenue in Santa Rosa, accusing him of
illegally manufacturing butane hash oil. [@2] Cameron Lenzini was charged with
manufacturing a controlled substance, setting fire to an inhabited building and
two counts of child cruelty, and he entered a not guilty plea before Judge
Christopher Honigsberg. [@1] Judge Honigsberg granted Cameron Lenzini pretrial
release, with terms meaning he likely would wear an electronic monitor and check
in with pretrial services, and set his next court appearance for September 27.
[@1] The two additional suspects were Trisha Lenzini, the mother of Cameron
Lenzini, and her boyfriend Efrain Gomez. [@1] Trisha Lenzini, 43, and Gomez, 41,
are convicted felons, and investigators believed the guns found in the home
belonged to them. [@1] Trisha Lenzini and Gomez turned themselves in on August
24 and were jailed on suspicion of committing four felony offenses. [@1] Those
suspected offenses were possession of stolen property, unlawful possession of a
weapon, possession of a firearm by a felon and possession of ammunition by a
felon. [@1]

Police said they searched the home and found 12 guns, including three assault
rifles, one of which was stolen. [@1] Police also said they found cases of empty
butane canisters, marijuana and more than $57,000 in cash. [@1] During the
search of the home, officers found 12 firearms including three assault rifles,
among them a stolen AK-47 style rifle, along with cases of empty butane
canisters, marijuana and more than $57,000 in cash. [@2]

The August 21 blast blew the Russell Avenue home's garage off its foundation,
rendered the house uninhabitable and endangered Trisha Lenzini's two young
daughters who were inside. [@1] Four adults and two children were evacuated,
Santa Rosa firefighters put out the fire caused by the blast, and no major
injuries were reported. [@1] Hash oil, also known as honey oil, has a high
concentration of THC, making it much more potent than regular marijuana. [@1]
Butane is sometimes used to extract the concentrated oil from marijuana leaves,
a process that can fill a room with flammable, highly explosive gas. [@1]
