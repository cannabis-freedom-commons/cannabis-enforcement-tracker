Judicial police arrested a drug dealer at Ksibet El Mediouni as part of a
drug-trafficking investigation in Monastir. [@1] A raid by the Narcotics Brigade
and the judicial police, carried out in coordination with the prosecutor’s
office, led to the arrest of the main dealer. [@1] An accomplice remained at
large after the operation and was the subject of a search warrant after being
identified. [@1] The public prosecutor ordered the arrested dealer held in
detention while the investigation continued. [@1]

Officers seized 14 slabs of cannabis weighing 1.5 kg together with cocaine
during the operation. [@1] The same seizure included 22,895 euros and 78,424
Tunisian dinars in addition to other sums in foreign currency. [@1]

The Interior Ministry announced the operation on 31 August 2022. [@1] The
Interior Ministry said the at-large accomplice was also linked to an illegal
sports-betting network. [@1]
