Central West Police District detectives commenced Strike Force Burria in March
2022 to investigate commercial cannabis cultivation in the state's west. [@1]
Following that five-month investigation, strike force investigators attended a
rural property at Trundle, about 53 kilometres north-west of Parkes, on
Wednesday 3 August 2022. [@1] Several people at the property fled into nearby
bushland as officers arrived and could not be located. [@1]

Officers obtained a crime scene warrant, which was held overnight, and
returned on Thursday 4 August 2022 with specialist forensic police. [@1] They
found a number of large greenhouses being used for cannabis cultivation and
seized 27,484 cannabis plants, all of which were destroyed. [@1] Police put
the potential street value above A$80 million. [@1] Investigations under the
strike force to identify those involved were continuing. [@1]
