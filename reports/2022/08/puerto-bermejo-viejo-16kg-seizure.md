Officers of Prefectura Naval Argentina, the coast guard, seized almost 16
kilograms of marijuana on the bank of the Paraguay River at Puerto Bermejo
Viejo, in Chaco province. [@1]

The seizure followed a patrol carried out as part of crime-prevention duties at
kilometre 28 of the Paraguay River. [@1] The patrol found the marijuana inside a
burlap sack. [@1] The marijuana was packed in 17 bricks, which the Prefectura
valued at more than two million pesos. [@1] The seizure was made on the
instructions of the Federal Prosecutor's Office in Resistencia, headed by Dr.
Juan Marcelo Burella Acevedo. [@1]

The Chaco daily Diario Norte reported the same 16-kilogram seizure at Puerto
Bermejo Viejo the same day. [@2] Diario Norte also gave the location as
kilometre 28 of the river and the sack of burlap. [@2] The Chaco outlet Noticias
del Paraná republished the Prefectura's account under its own masthead a day
later. [@3]
