Fiji police destroyed more than 81,000 marijuana plants kept as exhibits from
2018 to 2021 at the Kadavu Police Station incinerator in Vunisea. [@1] The
plants were part of more than five tonnes of marijuana held as exhibits from
cases on Kadavu between 2018 and 2021. [@1] The Fiji Times reported the
destruction happened on the morning of 8 August 2022. [@3] fijivillage reported
that the destruction took place on the morning of 8 August 2022. [@2]

Police Prosecution filed 76 affidavits with the Suva Magistrates Court for
permission to dispose of the drugs, and the court granted the application the
previous Friday. [@1] Senior police officers and representatives of the Kadavu
Provincial Council office watched the burning. [@1]

Police Commissioner Brigadier General Sitiveni Qiliho, who was in Kadavu, said
destroying all hard drugs kept as exhibits followed the process of obtaining
court approval for destruction. [@1] Qiliho said tightening the process would
stop officers taking drugs kept as exhibits and make the handling of illicit
substances transparent and accountable. [@1] He said officers who work against
efforts to combat the illicit drug trade have no place in the force. [@1]
