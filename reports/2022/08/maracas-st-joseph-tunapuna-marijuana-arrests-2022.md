Northern and North-Central Divisions police arrested two people with marijuana,
according to a police media release. [@1] Three men were charged with firearm
and narcotic offences on August 24. [@1] Officers of North-Central Area West and
the Canine Branch made the arrests between 3 am and 10 am on Wednesday in the
Maracas, St Joseph and Tunapuna districts. [@1]

In a separate event the same day, police from the Maloney CID and the Northern
Division Task Force, Area North searched between 3 pm and 7 pm. [@1] A
33-year-old Princes Town man was arrested in that search. [@1]

Police said investigations were continuing. [@1]
