Kareem Tyron Eaghan, 39, and Rosita Zelaya, 37, were brought into the Belize
City Magistrate's Court charged jointly with possession of controlled drugs with
intent to supply to another. [@1] The charge concerned 371 grams of marijuana, a
little more than half a pound, that police said was found in their possession.
[@1] They were represented by attorney Leroy Banner. [@1]

Both pleaded not guilty to the charge. [@1] There was no objection to bail, so
each was granted bail of $1,500 plus one surety of the same amount. [@1] Court
records showed that Eaghan owed the court $305 and had to pay $100 before he
could post bail. [@1]

They are due back in court on March 31, 2022. [@1]
