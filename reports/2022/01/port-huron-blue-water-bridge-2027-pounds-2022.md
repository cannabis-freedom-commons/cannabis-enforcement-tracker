CBP officers at the Blue Water Bridge in Port Huron seized 2,027 pounds of cannabis from an inbound commercial shipment on the evening of Thursday 20 January 2022. [@1,2] Officers had referred the food shipment, bound for Columbus, Ohio, for an additional examination at the Michigan crossing. [@1,2]

A secondary inspection found 15 wooden crates holding the cannabis inside the trailer. [@1,2] The Detroit Field Office noted a surge in contraband since COVID-19 travel curbs began in March 2020. [@1,2]
