Officers of the St Elizabeth Police Division carried out a targeted raid on a premises in Beersheba district, New Market, between 3:30 pm and 5:30 pm on Tuesday 4 January 2022. [@1,2] They found 1,100 pounds (about 499 kilograms) of compressed ganja at the property, which police valued at an estimated $4.4 million. [@1,2] No arrest was made in connection with the seizure. [@1,2]

Investigators named Jason Cope, otherwise called Kukos, of Happy Hall district in New Market, as a person of interest in the matter. [@1,2] He was asked to report to the Black River police station by the afternoon of Thursday 6 January, and police asked anyone knowing his whereabouts to contact the Black River police or the nearest police station. [@1,2] The case still stood without an arrest when the second account was published on 6 January, two days after the raid. [@1,2]
