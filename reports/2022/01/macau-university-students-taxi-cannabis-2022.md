Two 21-year-old local male university students, surnamed Sin and Aquino, were
arrested on 11 January 2022 near Central Park in Taipa in a cannabis case and
were enrolled at two different local universities. [@1] The men became suspects
after a taxi driver handed police a bag left on the back seat the previous day,
and officers found a cannabis joint inside it. [@1] Judiciary Police arrested
the pair at about 9 pm near Central Park after they had taken the taxi from the
Barrier Gate checkpoint to Taipa, and seized 0.9 grammes of cannabis, several
yuan notes and drug-taking paraphernalia from Sin. [@2]

A search of Sin's nearby flat found another 15.15 grammes of cannabis and
drug-taking paraphernalia, bringing the total seized to 16.05 grammes. [@1]
Police put the cannabis at a market value of about 16,000 patacas. [@1] Both men
tested positive for drugs and admitted taking cannabis, and Sin said he had
given some to Aquino for his own use. [@1] Sin told police he had bought the
cannabis for 3,000 yuan at a night entertainment venue on the mainland and
smuggled it into Macau; Macao Daily News reported that he denied trafficking.
[@3]

Both were transferred to the Public Prosecutions Office on 12 January, facing
charges of taking narcotics, while Sin also faced charges of drug trafficking
and possession of drug-taking paraphernalia. [@1] Macao Daily News reported that
Sin was charged with trafficking, drug use and possession of drug-taking
equipment, and Aquino with drug use. [@3] Macau Times reported that police were
still tracing where the cannabis came from when the two were sent to
prosecutors. [@5]

Cheng Pou reported that the cannabis seized, including the joint left in the
taxi, was worth more than 16,000 patacas. [@4]
