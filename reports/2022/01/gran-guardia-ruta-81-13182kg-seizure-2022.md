Officers inspected a pickup on a secondary road off National Route 81 near Gran
Guardia, in Formosa province. [@1] The patrol combined officers of Squadron 15
“Bajo Paraguay” with the “Comandante Fontana” reconnaissance unit. [@1] The
pickup carried three adults, two of Argentine nationality and one of Paraguayan
nationality. [@1] The three were detained for infringement of Law 23.737 on
narcotics. [@1]

Searching the vehicle in the presence of witnesses, the officers found several
packages wrapped in packing tape, of the kind used to move drugs. [@1] Field
Narcotest tests confirmed the contents as cannabis sativa, totalling 13 kilos
182 grams. [@1] The officers also found 2,600 US dollars and 682,060 Argentine
pesos in the vehicle. [@1] The Federal Court and the Federal Prosecutor's Office
of Formosa ordered the drug, the pickup and other items of interest seized, and
the adults detained. [@1]
