Gendarmes of the La Rioja complex-crimes and judicial procedures investigations
unit searched a house in the Matadero neighbourhood of La Rioja city, the
provincial capital. [@1] The unit was supported by Squadron 58 of the Catamarca
grouping and by provincial police officers. [@1]

The search followed a complaint and an investigation ordered by the intervening
magistrate into drug selling in the provincial capital. [@1] Inside the house
the officers found cannabis plants more than a metre tall, 21 grams of cannabis
sativa, a precision scale and mobile phones. [@1]

A resident of the house was detained in a case under Law 23.737, Argentina's
narcotics law. [@1] The Federal Court of La Rioja ordered the detention and the
seizure of the marijuana. [@1]
