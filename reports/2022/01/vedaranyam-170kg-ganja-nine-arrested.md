Police seized 170 kg of ganja meant for smuggling to Sri Lanka near Vedaranyam,
55 km from Nagapattinam, and arrested nine people. [@1] Nagapattinam district
superintendent of police G Jawahar said police had information that Sudhakar of
Pudupalli village near Vedaranyam was going to receive bundles of ganja from
Andhra Pradesh. [@1]

Acting on the tip-off, a team led by sub-inspector Balamurugan kept watch at the
village. [@1] The team noticed two cars at the village and intercepted them.
[@1] A search yielded 85 bundles carrying 2 kg each. [@1] Officers seized the
170 kg of ganja and arrested the nine people, the district police chief said.
[@1]

Interrogation of the nine revealed the bundles were to be taken to Vedaranyam
and smuggled to Sri Lanka. [@1] Jawahar said further investigation was under
way. [@1]

The Hans India reported the same seizure of 170 kg near Vedaranyam, 55 km from
Nagapattinam, with nine people arrested, and identified the district police
chief as G Jawahar. [@2]

The Hans India said the arrested nine disclosed during interrogation that the
bundles were intended to be taken to Vedaranyam and smuggled into Sri Lanka, and
that more inquiry was under way. [@2]
