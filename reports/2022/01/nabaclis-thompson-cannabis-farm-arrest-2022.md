Agents of the Customs Anti-Narcotics Unit (CANU) arrested a Jamaican man on
Tuesday during an operation at Nabaclis backlands, East Coast Demerara. [@2] He
was identified as 46-year-old Winston Thompson. [@1] He lived in Victoria
village on the East Coast of Demerara and was seen on the camp. [@2] He was
arrested and taken to CANU headquarters with the weapons, ammunition, processed
cannabis and seeds. [@2] He was assisting with the investigation. [@2]

The operation also unearthed guns, ammunition and a ganja field. [@2] CANU
officers, acting on information, found a camp on a half-acre of land holding
about 4,000 to 5,000 cannabis plants and seedlings. [@2] They also found
processed cannabis, 1 kilogram of mixed seeds, one shotgun, one crossbow with
several arrows, and several calibres of ammunition. [@2] The ammunition was
thirty-one 9mm bullets, twelve .45 cartridges and four 12-gauge cartridges. [@2]

A Jamaican man was in custody on Wednesday after he was allegedly caught on a
half-acre marijuana farm at Nabaclis, East Coast Demerara. [@1] CANU said it
continues operations around the country aimed at dismantling networks engaged in
the cultivation, sale and distribution of narcotics. [@1]
