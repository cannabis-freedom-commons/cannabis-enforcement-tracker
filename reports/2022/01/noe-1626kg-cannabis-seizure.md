Officers of the Noé customs bureau intercepted a motorcyclist carrying cannabis
at the Côte d'Ivoire–Ghana land border during a routine check. [@1] The cannabis
was packed in a carton transported by the motorcyclist and was described as
carefully concealed. [@2] Customs detained the carrier together with his
accomplices and handed them to the mixed police of Noé for investigation. [@2]

The seizure weighed 1.626 tonne, or 1,626 kilograms, of cannabis recovered from
the carton. [@1] The seizure took place on Tuesday 11 January 2022, when customs
officers carried out that routine control at Noé. [@2] The discovery was made by
officers serving under General Alphonse Da Pierre during that routine control.
[@1]

The operation was conducted by agents of the Noé customs bureau at the land
border between Côte d'Ivoire and Ghana. [@1] Ivorian customs recalled that
beyond its well-known tax mission it also protects public health by controlling
medicine imports, fighting illicit narcotics trafficking and carrying out
sanitary and phytosanitary checks. [@1]
