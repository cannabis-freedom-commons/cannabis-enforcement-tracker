Tanah Karo drug investigators arrested Adil Abdulah Meliala and Sudi Barus, both
residents of Seberaya village in Tigapanah district. [@1] The two men were
detained at different times. [@1] The arrests led officers to two cannabis
fields in Seberaya village, Tigapanah district, Karo Regency. [@1] A second
account gives their ages as 51 and 52. [@2] Checks found both men had lately
been living on Jalan Bakti Luhur in Medan. [@2]

Police said the two fields together held 492 cannabis plants of different sizes
and growth stages. [@1] At suspect AM's site officers recorded 313 harvest-ready
plants, 21 harvested stumps and 136 sown seedlings. [@1] A second account
describes about 500 ready-to-harvest plants of various sizes secured from the
location. [@2] The Karo police chief said a search and uprooting at two
locations seized varied-size plants and two suspected owners were secured. [@2]
Officers also recovered dried cannabis and two small meth packets from the
suspects. [@2]

Police said the case developed from community information and residents had
already suspected the men. [@2] Investigators said each harvest was bartered for
meth outside Karo Regency. [@2] Police took both suspects and the evidence to
Tanah Karo police for further investigation. [@2] The uncovering and arrests
occurred at Seberaya on Thursday 6 January. [@2]
