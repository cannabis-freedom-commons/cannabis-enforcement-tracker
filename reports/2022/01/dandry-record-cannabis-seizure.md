In January 2022, Strike Force Harthouse detectives executed a crime-scene
warrant on a rural property at Dandry in western New South Wales. [@1] Police
seized more than 19,000 cannabis plants, which they valued at about A$66.8
million, calling it the biggest cannabis bust on the New South Wales
record. [@1] Six people aged 23 to 42 were arrested and taken to Dubbo and
Coonabarabran police stations. [@1]

All six were charged with cultivating a prohibited plant, taking part in
cultivating large commercial quantities of cannabis, and participating in a
criminal group, and were refused bail to appear at Coonamble Local Court on 19
January 2022. [@1] Strike Force Harthouse was set up in 2019 to investigate
cannabis cultivation and trafficking across the state, and had charged 51
people and seized almost 70,000 plants worth more than A$217 million by the
time of the report. [@1]
