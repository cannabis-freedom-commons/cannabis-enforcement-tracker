NDLEA spokesman Femi Babafemi announced on 27 November 2022 that the agency had seized 5.8
tonnes of cannabis from dealers across five states in raids run between 21 and 26 November.
[@1,2] In Edo, officers evacuated 141 bags weighing 1,884 kg from a warehouse in Okpe forest,
Akoko-Edo, on 22 November, then seized 1,512 kg in Obi Camp forest, 216.5 kg at Okada
junction, and 529.5 kg plus a Sienna bus loaded with 566.5 kg bound for Onitsha, arresting
Ismaila Abubakar and Sunday Mathias. [@1,2]

In Ogun, officers arrested Ibrahim Yusuf with 4 kg at Gasline, Ifo, on 21 November and
recovered 570 kg in Ogunmakin forest; in Ondo, Onyebuchi Chime was arrested with 88 kg, a gun
and ammunition in Oke-Ogun forest on 25 November, and 149.5 kg more was recovered in Ipele
forest. [@1,2] Officers destroyed 12.42 hectares of cannabis farms and recovered 195 kg at
Efon Alaye in Ekiti, arresting Richard Ebong and Nze Abraham, and arrested Rahmat Adebayo
with 136.3 kg at Sabo-Ilupeju in Oyo. [@1,2]
