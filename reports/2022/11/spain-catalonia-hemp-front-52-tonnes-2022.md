Spanish police announced on Friday 18 November 2022 that they had seized more than 50 tonnes of marijuana, the largest amount ever confiscated in the country. [@1,2] Officers of the Policía Nacional, the Guardia Civil and Catalonia's Mossos d'Esquadra dismantled eight farms in Catalonia between June and October. [@3,1]

The farms were disguised as supposedly legal hemp plantations, registered as owned by a trading company and sited among warehouses with legal industrial activity. [@1,2] A Policía Nacional post said seven of the eight plantations were in Barcelona province and one in Tarragona province. [@3]

## Plants, buds and detentions

In total, agents seized 52 tonnes of marijuana: more than 187,000 plants plus 19 tonnes of buds. [@1,2] Twenty people were detained on suspected electricity fraud and offences against public health. [@1,2]

Spain's Tax Agency had said in June, for a different operation, that cannabis was worth between 2,300 and 2,500 euros per kilogram; at that rate the 50 tonnes could be worth about 125 million euros. [@2]

The announcement came less than two weeks after police reported seizing 32 tonnes of marijuana buds in Operation Gardens, which had set the previous record. [@1,2] That is a separate bust from this Catalonia operation. [@1]
