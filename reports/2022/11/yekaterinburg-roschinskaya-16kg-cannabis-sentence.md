A 34-year-old director of a local commercial company was arrested on 10 February
2022 in a rented flat in a multi-storey building on Roschinskaya Street in
Yekaterinburg. [@3] When the officers arrived, the man threw boxes and cannabis
out of the window and tried to flush the rest down the toilet, but the dried
plant clogged the plumbing. [@1]

Officers of the regional drug-trafficking department seized 16 kg of cannabis in
all. [@1] The man told detectives he had grown the crop himself in the flat,
which belonged to a woman he knew, and had kept the cannabis there until his
arrest. [@2] He grew the plants in barrels in a greenhouse set up inside the
flat. [@3] Officers backed by Rosgvardiya riot police detained him, and he was
placed under arrest. [@4]

On 22 November 2022 the Chkalovsky district court of Yekaterinburg found the
businessman guilty of storing drugs on a large scale and sentenced him to six
years in prison. [@2] The case was opened under article 228 of the Criminal
Code, which covers unlawful acquisition and storage of drugs, and the press
service put the maximum penalty at 20 years. [@1]
