Shkodra police arrested three people as they attempted to traffic cannabis to
Montenegro. [@1] Police identified those arrested as 24-year-old driver R. M.,
28-year-old R. M. and 21-year-old O. S., all residents of Bajze. [@1] The
arrests were made in flagrante delicto during the police operation coded Grabom.
[@1]

Police seized three bags filled with packets of cannabis sativa narcotics in the
operation. [@1] As material evidence, police seized 21.5 kg of cannabis, the
vehicle used to transport it and three mobile devices. [@1] The driver tried to
leave and threw the bags with narcotics on the road before police neutralized
him. [@1]

The operation was run by Shkoder border and migration officers with the Grabom
Border Police and the Shkoder narcotics investigation section. [@1] Police said
they acted on intelligence that suspects were driving narcotics toward Grabom to
move them across the green border into Montenegro. [@1] Officers located the
suspect vehicle at a place called Kryqezimi i Gabomi with Shkoder narcotics
specialists. [@1] Police said the case materials were sent to the prosecution at
the Shkoder Judicial District Court for trafficking in narcotics committed in
cooperation while documentation continues. [@1]
