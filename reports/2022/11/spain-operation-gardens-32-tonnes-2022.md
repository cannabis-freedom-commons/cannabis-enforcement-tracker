Spain's Civil Guard said on Saturday 5 November 2022 that it had seized
32,370.2 kg of packaged marijuana buds, the largest such seizure in Spain
and internationally. [@1,2] The haul, equivalent to about 1.1 million whole
plants, came from raids on farms and production plants across Toledo, Ciudad
Real, Valencia and Asturias in an operation called Gardens (Jardines). [@1,2]

Twenty people aged 20 to 59, nine men and eleven women, were arrested as
members of an organisation with offices in those four provinces that
controlled the whole production and distribution process. [@1,2] Through a
network of companies the group acquired seeds, planted and tended the crop,
then dried it and vacuum-packed the buds in two warehouses in Valencia for
shipment across Spain and to Switzerland, the Netherlands, Germany and
Belgium. [@1,2] The investigation began with an inspection of
industrial-hemp plantations in Villacañas, Toledo. [@1]

In Ciudad Real police found about 37,000 plants in four greenhouses plus
four tonnes of stored marijuana, arresting three people on site. [@1] In
Valencia they seized 30,530 kg of buds, 20 kg of pollen, 21,600 drying
plants and 231,200 packs of buds with processing machinery, arresting 15
people. [@1] A final inspection in Asturias seized 4,000 psychoactive plants
and led to two arrests. [@1]
