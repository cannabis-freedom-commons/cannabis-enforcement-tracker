Gendarmería Nacional officers of the Concepción del Uruguay unit for complex
crimes and judicial proceedings, working with the toxicology division of the
Entre Ríos provincial police, searched five properties in Concepción del
Uruguay. [@1] The searches were part of a case under Argentina's national drug
law 23.737 and the Entre Ríos provincial law 10.566 on narcomenudeo, local
retail drug dealing. [@1] The Juzgado de Garantías y Transición of Concepción
del Uruguay, the provincial bail and rights court, ordered the detention of five
people, who the force said belong to a criminal drug organisation. [@1]

During the searches, officers seized wrappings holding 1 kilo 303 grams of
cannabis sativa. [@1] Officers also seized marijuana seeds and plants, cutting
and packaging materials, a precision scale, cash, and 14 and 11.25 calibre
cartridges. [@1] The same seizure included 5.7 grams of cocaine, a separate
substance recorded here only to explain the mixed case. [@1]

The investigation began in May 2022 under the direction of the Concepción del
Uruguay federal prosecutor's office, aiming to break up an organisation involved
in holding firearms and selling drugs. [@1]
