Jorge Monjaraz-Perez, a 20-year-old Mexican citizen, pleaded guilty in
Brownsville on 13 January 2022 to conspiring with a drug trafficking
organisation to move more than 4,000 pounds of marijuana. [@1,2] At the
hearing he admitted moving at least 1,886 kilos, running a marijuana stash
house in Brownsville and taking part in at least seven trafficking events;
investigators seized every load, each weighing between 91 and 528 kilos. [@1]

The case was investigated by the FBI and the Drug Enforcement
Administration with help from the Border Patrol and the Texas Department of
Public Safety. [@1] U.S. District Judge Fernando Rodriguez Jr. sentenced
Monjaraz-Perez to 200 months in federal prison on 17 November 2022, noting
he had coordinated loads for the organisation and managed its operation. [@2]

As a non-citizen, Monjaraz-Perez was expected to face removal proceedings
after his prison term. [@2]
