Gaza Police's Drug Control Directorate dismantled a drugs hub early on 9
November 2022 in Rafah, south Gaza. [@1] The operation seized a large quantity
of hashish, pills and other narcotics, in a task that ran for several hours in
Rafah. [@1]

Police arrested a number of dealers and distributors of the narcotics. [@1] Gaza
Police General Manager Maj. Gen. Mahmud Salah said the police would chase drug
dealers and distributors and would not allow anyone to seek drugs inside the
Gaza Strip. [@1]
