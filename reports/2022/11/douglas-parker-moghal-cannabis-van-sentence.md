Christopher Parker and Bilal Moghal were sentenced at Douglas Courthouse on 11
November 2022 for a cannabis trafficking operation. [@2] Parker was jailed for
five years. [@2] Moghal, 64, was jailed for four years and eight months. [@1]
Parker, 43, had been seen transferring drugs from another man's van at a petrol
station in Onchan. [@1] Moghal, of Jennison Close in Manchester, had travelled
to the island the previous day. [@1] Parker admitted two offences of possession
of cannabis with intent to supply and one of possession of criminal property.
[@2] Moghal admitted producing cannabis to the Isle of Man and possessing the
drug with intent to supply. [@2]

Parker parked his black Volkswagen Transporter near Corkills Garage in Onchan at
about 09:25 on 3 September before getting into Moghal's rented white Vauxhall
Crafter. [@1] The two men then transferred several packages from the Vauxhall to
the Volkswagen before both drove away. [@1] Officers approached Parker as he
arrived at his home on Fairfield Avenue. [@1] Moghal was stopped by police on
Douglas Promenade shortly after the handover. [@2] In Parker's van police found
four bin bags of cannabis. [@3] A later search of Parker's home found more
cannabis hidden in the kitchen and garden. [@3] Police said the drugs from
Parker's vehicle and property had a combined weight of 14,447.50 grams. [@3]
Police put the estimated street value of that cannabis at £288,948. [@3]
Officers also recovered £33,165 in criminal cash concealed in Parker's garden.
[@2]

Parker told police he had been offered £500 to pick up the drugs and look after
them to cover a veterinary bill. [@2] Moghal said he agreed to transport the
drugs for £5,000 to pay off debts and contribute to his son's wedding. [@1]
Deemster Graeme Cook said it was clear both men had become involved at the
behest of someone else. [@1] Moghal was also banned from returning to the island
for five years. [@1]
