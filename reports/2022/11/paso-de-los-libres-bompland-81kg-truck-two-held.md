Gendarmes of Escuadrón 7 Paso de los Libres "Cabo Misael Pereyra" stopped a
cargo truck on Ruta Nacional 14 at kilometre 480, by the inactive Bompland toll
booth, in the early hours of the morning. [@1] The truck was travelling from
Uruguaiana in Brazil to San Francisco in Córdoba, and two adult Argentine men
were on board. [@1] The anti-narcotics dog "Nico" signalled drugs when it passed
the cab, prompting a more thorough search. [@1]

Inside five boxes the officers found 111 rectangular packages. [@1] Narcotest
field tests by the force's criminalistics staff were positive for "cannabis
sativa", with a total weight of 81 kilos 72 grams. [@1] The Federal Court of
Paso de los Libres ordered the drug, the vehicle, the mobile phones and other
items of interest to the case seized, and both men detained. [@1]
