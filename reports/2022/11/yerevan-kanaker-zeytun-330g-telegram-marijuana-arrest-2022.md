Officers of the Kanaker-Zeytun police division detained a 23-year-old resident
of Yerevan on Paruyr Sevak Street on suspicion of possessing, using and selling
drugs. [@1] The police acted on information received on 7 November, the date the
Armenian-language accounts give for the detention. [@2]

A personal search at the police station found 162 packets of a marijuana-like
substance weighing about 267 grams and another lot of the same substance
weighing about 63 grams. [@1] Officers also seized 3 polyethylene bags, 2
electric scales, adhesive tape and 342 empty bags. [@1]

The man told police the substance was marijuana he had bought through a Telegram
channel and intended to sell in different districts of Yerevan. [@1] The account
came from the Armenian police press service, which Armenpress and Sputnik
Armenia reproduced. [@4] Oragir.news published the same police statement, dating
the arrest to 7 November. [@3]

The investigation in the criminal case was continuing, with no charge or court
decision reported. [@1] The police statement noted that a person suspected or
accused of an offence is presumed innocent until guilt is proven under Armenia's
law by a court decision in force. [@1]
