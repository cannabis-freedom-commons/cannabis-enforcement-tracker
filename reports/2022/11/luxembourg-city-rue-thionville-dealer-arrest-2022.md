Police responded around 7 pm on Thursday evening to a report of a suspected
person dealing drugs near the drug addict care structure in Rue Thionville in
Luxembourg City. [@1] The officers identified the man at the scene together with
two other suspected persons in the company of drug users. [@1] All three were
taken to a police station for further investigation, where officers found drugs
on two of them. [@1]

Officers seized two bags of hashish, a bale of cocaine and a further 14.6 grams
of hashish. [@1] The report gives no weight for the two bags of hashish, so only
the 14.6 grams is recorded as the cannabis weight. [@1] The main suspect was
arrested on the order of the public prosecutor's office and brought before an
investigating judge on Friday. [@1] The other two men were fined. [@1]

Police informed the responsible police section and the relevant administration
of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs after none of the three could show documents
proving they were in the country legally. [@1]
