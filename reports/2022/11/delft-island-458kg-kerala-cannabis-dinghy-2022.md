In the early hours of 9 November 2022, the Coast Guard craft CG 402 attached
to the Northern Naval Command stopped a suspicious dinghy in the sea southeast
of Delft Island, Jaffna. [@1,2] Officers found about 458.9 kg of Kerala
cannabis in 18 packages aboard and arrested the two suspects with the boat.
[@1,2]

The two men, aged 37 and 44 and from Mandaitivu and Nachchikuda, were to be
handed to the Delft police with the cannabis and the dinghy for legal action.
[@1,2] The Navy put the street value of the haul at more than 137 million
rupees. [@1,2]
