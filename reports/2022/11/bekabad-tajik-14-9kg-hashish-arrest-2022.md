Bekabad police detained a 29-year-old Tajik citizen, identified only as A.Sh.,
in the Uzbekistan mahalla of Bekabad, Tashkent region, on 2 November 2022. [@2]
The Tashkent region police press service announced the detention of a Tajik
citizen with a large consignment of drugs. [@1] Officers of the Bekabad police
department searched the flat where he was staying temporarily, in the presence
of witnesses. [@1]

In the bedroom officers found 14.9 kg of hashish, which was taken as material
evidence. [@1] Podrobno.uz reported that the hashish had been kept for sale.
[@3] Podrobno.uz reported that the flat was inspected under Operation Black
Poppy-2022, an anti-drug campaign. [@3]

Police opened a criminal case under part 5 of Article 273 of Uzbekistan's
Criminal Code, which covers the illegal sale of drugs. [@1] Podrobno.uz reported
that the offence carries a prison term of 10 to 20 years. [@3] UzNews.uz said
the criminal case had been opened under part 5 of Article 273 for large-scale
sale and that an investigation was under way. [@2]
