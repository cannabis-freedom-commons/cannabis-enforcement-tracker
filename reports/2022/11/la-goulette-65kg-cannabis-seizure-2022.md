Customs officers at La Goulette, in Tunis’s northern suburbs, intercepted a
Tunisian traveller arriving from Marseille. [@1] The traveller was arrested with
cannabis in his possession. [@1] The drugs were found among the passenger’s
personal effects in a vehicle with foreign registration. [@2]

Customs officers became suspicious of his belongings and sent them through an
X-ray scanner. [@2] The scan revealed foreign objects inside a television, a
fryer and a small kitchen appliance. [@2] After opening the appliances, officers
found 63 slabs of cannabis. [@2] The three appliances had been placed at the
rear of the vehicle. [@1] The seized 63 slabs weighed a total of 6.5 kg. [@1]

Customs described the seizure as a foiled drug-trafficking attempt at the port.
[@2] Customs announced the interception on Tuesday, 1 November 2022. [@1] After
a formal report was drawn up, the prosecutor ordered the case file transferred
to the competent security services for further investigation. [@2]
