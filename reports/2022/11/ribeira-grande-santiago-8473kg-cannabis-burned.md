Cabo Verde's Judicial Police announced the seizure and destruction in recent
days of almost 8.5 tons of cannabis in several locations on Santiago island.
[@3] The Judicial Police dismantled cannabis cultivation fields in Ribeira
Grande de Santiago on 28 October and 4 November 2022. [@1] The fields were in
Belém, Mosquito de Horta and Encosta de Santa Clara, all in the municipality of
Ribeira Grande de Santiago. [@1] The operation was run by the police's Central
Section for Investigation of Drug Trafficking and Organised Crime, SCITECO, 2nd
Brigade. [@1] The accounts of these two operations specify no arrests. [@3]

Police put the total seized at 8,473.5 kg of cannabis herb. [@1] On 28 October
officers seized 1,322 kg in Belém and 2,258.5 kg in Mosquito da Horta. [@2] On 4
November officers seized 3,668 kg in Belém and 1,225 kg in Encosta de Santa
Clara. [@2]

Police weighed all the seized cannabis and then incinerated it in those same
localities. [@1] Cabo Verde's Armed Forces supported the 4 November operation in
Belém and Encosta de Santa Clara. [@1]
