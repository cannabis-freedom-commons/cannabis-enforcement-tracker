John Edison Kelly Jr., 21, of Montana was stopped while driving in Bellevue,
Idaho, on Nov. 23, 2022 and charged with felony marijuana possession. [@1] He
was arraigned in Hailey on one count of felony possession of more than 3 ounces
of marijuana. [@1] He pleaded not guilty to misdemeanor possession of drug
paraphernalia the same day. [@1]

The criminal complaint filed in Fifth District Court on Nov. 23 said he had more
than half a pound of marijuana in his car during the Bellevue traffic stop. [@1]
Bellevue Marshal Cpl. Michael Shelamer reported running stationary radar at Main
Street and Broadford Road at about 3:15 a.m. when he saw a Ford F-150 allegedly
driven by Kelly going 40 mph in a posted 25 mph zone. [@1]

Shelamer made a U-turn and stopped the car near the intersection of Main and
Spruce streets. [@1]
