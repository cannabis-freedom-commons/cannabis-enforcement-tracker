Angola's Criminal Investigation Service burned a total of more than 210
kilograms of cocaine and 50,000 kilograms of liamba seized in several operations
during 2022 in almost all provinces. [@1] Liamba is the Angolan term for
cannabis, the cannabis component recorded here. [@1] The incineration acts took
place in the last week before the 28 November report. [@1] National radio
announced that the SIC would incinerate more than 200 kilos of cocaine on 24
November, from seizures made in various operations over the year. [@2] SIC
spokesman Manuel Halaiwa said simultaneous incineration acts for various drug
types would take place across the country at the same time. [@2]

Luanda had the most cocaine seizures, with about 200 kilos taken at 4 de
Fevereiro Airport, the Port of Luanda and elsewhere in the capital. [@1] In
Bengo, more than 33,000 kilograms of liamba were destroyed in Caxito at the
Sassa Cária instruction centre, in the presence of SIC prosecutor Mário Simão
and provincial director Armando Vieira. [@1] Bengo spokesman Peterson Cassule
said that drug had been seized in Dande, Ambriz and Nambuangongo and was bound
for markets in Luanda. [@1]

In Namibe, the SIC incinerated 135 kilos of liamba seized from individuals
implicated in drug trafficking. [@1] In Cunene, more than three kilos of cocaine
and 63 kilos of liamba were burned at the Okapanda-Cuanhama landfill, while in
Huíla more than 115 kilograms of liamba were destroyed at the Lubango landfill.
[@1]
