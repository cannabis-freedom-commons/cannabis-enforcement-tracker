Police in central Maidan Wardak province said they found and destroyed a drug
laboratory in Behsud district. [@1] The laboratory was demolished in the Qar
Katak area of the district, according to a statement from the provincial police
headquarters. [@1]

The raiding party seized 2,250 kilograms of hashish. [@1] The same statement
said officers seized 1,000 kilograms of other illicit drugs, a combined figure
not counted as cannabis. [@1] Police said those behind the drug trade had fled
the area and security personnel were trying to detain them. [@1] No arrest was
reported in the raid. [@1]
