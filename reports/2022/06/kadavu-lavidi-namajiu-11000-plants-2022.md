Fiji police officers from the Police Special Response Unit were deployed to
Kadavu the previous week to support drug operations on the island, Police Chief
Operations Officer ACP Abdul Khan said. [@1] Police were searching for the
owners of five different farms in Kadavu where the plants were found. [@1]

Officers from the Police Special Response Unit uprooted more than 11,000 plants
believed to be marijuana from five farms in Kadavu. [@2] The farms were found
along the border of the Lavidi and Namajiu terrain. [@1] The plants were
uprooted on 27 June 2022, the day before the 28 June reports that described the
seizure as happening yesterday. [@3]

Khan said more information had been forthcoming about the cultivation of the
illicit drugs in other parts of the island. [@1] No farm owners had been
identified when the reports were published, and police said investigations to
identify them were ongoing. [@2]
