The Nancy specialised interregional jurisdiction described the 1.3-tonne
cannabis seizure in Metz and Hettange-Grande as a record. [@1] On 8 June 2022
officers arrested five men aged 40 to 52 in Moselle and Spain. [@2] Four of
those arrested, including the alleged organiser, lived in Moselle and one lived
in Spain. [@1] They were placed under investigation for organised-gang
importation and drug trafficking. [@1] Of the five men, three were remanded in
pretrial detention and the other two were placed under judicial supervision.
[@2]

Three of the men were travelling in cars holding 757 kg of cannabis resin and 16
kg of herbal cannabis. [@2] A fourth man described as a stasher held 519 kg of
cannabis resin at his home. [@2] The driver of the Spanish refrigerated truck
that carried the load was also arrested. [@2] In total officers seized 1,292 kg
of cannabis plus 396,000 euros in criminal assets. [@2] The assets included
71,000 euros in bank accounts, over 30,000 euros in cash, about 115,000 euros in
luxury watches and about 180,000 euros in luxury leather goods and clothing.
[@2]

The inquiry began with an anonymous tip about massive regular cannabis imports
from Spain or Morocco by a Moselle resident aided by others. [@1] Investigators
found the traffickers brought the drug from Spain by Spanish truck and stored it
in a warehouse at Hettange-Grande. [@1] The cannabis was hidden among pallets of
food, unloaded at the warehouse by several people, then moved to Metz and sold
gradually. [@1] The Metz judicial police, the Metz BRI intervention unit and the
Metz interministerial research group handled the investigation. [@2]
