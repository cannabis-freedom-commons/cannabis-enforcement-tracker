Ivorian customs announced that nearly three tonnes of cannabis had been seized
in eastern Ivory Coast near the Ghana border. [@1] The operation followed an
anonymous tip reporting bags containing cannabis from Ghana. [@2] The
intervention took place in the border locality of Akaty. [@1]

Officers seized 57 packages of cannabis with a total weight of 2.88 tonnes
during the night from Tuesday to Wednesday. [@1] The Abengourou customs service
was credited with the 2.88-tonne cannabis seizure in the east of the country.
[@3] The customs operations ran for 48 hours from Tuesday 21 June to Thursday 23
June 2022. [@3] Those operations led to two people being detained. [@3]
Separately, 1.75 tonnes of inferior and falsified medicines were seized on
Thursday at a camp in a cocoa plantation at Angouakro, also near the Ghanaian
border. [@1]

In April, customs had made a record 2.06-tonne cocaine seizure in the port
cities of Abidjan and San Pedro, with an estimated market value of 41 billion
CFA francs, nearly 62 million euros. [@1] That cocaine case caused a stir with
the arrest of about thirty people. [@1] Ivory Coast has in recent years become a
favoured transit country for cocaine between Latin America and Europe. [@1]
