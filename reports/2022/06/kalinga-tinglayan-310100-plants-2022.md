Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency agents, Tinglayan municipal police and other
local anti-drug operatives uprooted about 310,100 fully grown marijuana plants
in Tinglayan, Kalinga, over three days from 24 to 26 June 2022 and burned them.
[@1] Police valued the uprooted plants at P62 million at the standard drug
price. [@1]

The plantations were found in Barangays Buscalan, Butbut Proper and Loccong in
Tinglayan town and covered a combined 25,950 square metres (about 2.6 hectares).
[@1] No suspect or cultivator was arrested during the operations. [@1] The
Philippine Star reported that the raids began at about 5:30 a.m. on Friday 24
June and ended on Sunday afternoon, 26 June. [@3] GMA News reported the
destroyed plants as about 310,000 on 26,000 square metres of land. [@2]

Police officer-in-charge Lt. Gen. Vicente Danao Jr. said the operations were
meant to hunt down those behind the planting and cultivation of the plants. [@1]
