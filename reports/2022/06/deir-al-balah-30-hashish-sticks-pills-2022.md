Gaza's anti-narcotics police seized hashish and narcotic pills during an
inspection mission in Deir al-Balah, in the Central Governorate, on Tuesday
evening. [@1] The department said it had received information that pills and
hashish were being held for sale in Deir al-Balah and had carried out
investigations over the previous days. [@1] It obtained a search warrant from
the public prosecution and searched the home of one of the dealers with a force
from the Central Governorate anti-narcotics branch, backed by intervention
police and women's police. [@1]

During the search, officers seized 30 sticks of hashish, 54 Rotana pills, 8
Atramal pills and 3 Larica pills, along with 1,880 shekels that the department
described as proceeds from drug dealing. [@1] The anti-narcotics department said
it detained the homeowner, described as a dealer with a criminal record,
confiscated the seized items and referred the case to the public prosecution.
[@1]

The anti-narcotics department issued its statement on Wednesday, the day after
the search. [@2] The department said it was continuing intensive efforts to
pursue drug traders and dealers and to protect the community from what it called
a dangerous scourge. [@1]
