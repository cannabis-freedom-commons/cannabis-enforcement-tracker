Kyle Badley, 38, of Birkenhead, was jailed for three years and two months for
importing cannabis to the Isle of Man hidden in a secret compartment in a car.
[@1] Badley was stopped by police and customs officers at the Sea Terminal in
Douglas after driving off the evening sailing from Liverpool on 22 April. [@2]
He told officers he was visiting his girlfriend, but officials found he had no
links to the island. [@1]

A sniffer dog showed interest in his black Mercedes, and officers arrested him
and seized the vehicle. [@2] A search found a hidden compartment under the
driver's seat, opened by a switch inside the car, holding 26 bags of cannabis
sealed with yellow tape. [@1] Officers found 26 vacuum-sealed packages holding
6,398 g of herbal cannabis, which the court valued at £127,098. [@2]

He pleaded guilty to importing cannabis to the island and possessing the drug
with intent to supply. [@1] Prosecutors accepted he was unlikely to be high up
in an organised gang because he had been used as a driver, but said he must have
known there were drugs in the car. [@1]

Deemster Graeme Cook called him "simply a mule" but said a deterrent sentence
was needed. [@1] The judge gave three years and two months for producing or
bringing cannabis to the island and a concurrent two-year term for possession
with intent to supply. [@2] Badley was also banned from visiting the island for
five years after his release. [@1]

Energy FM's account, which credits the BBC, reported that Badley was stopped by
customs officers after driving off the ferry on 22 April and jailed for three
years and two months. [@3]
