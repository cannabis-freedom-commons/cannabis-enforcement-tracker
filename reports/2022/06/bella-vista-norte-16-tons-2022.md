Paraguay's National Anti-Drug Secretariat (Senad) destroyed more than 16 tons of marijuana in an operation in Bella Vista Norte, Amambay department. [@1] Agents found the drugs at a rural property of about five hectares that housed several narco-camps with marijuana crops, including 1.5 tons of the drug in the drying process. [@1]

## Camps and chemicals destroyed

The agents destroyed the improvised camps and the drugs along with chemical precursors and production support equipment, Paraguayan daily ABC Color reported on 17 June. [@1] Senad's Bella Vista Norte regional office was showing a growing operational presence in Concepción and Amambay, the departments holding the country's largest marijuana plantations, Spanish daily ABC reported. [@1]

The operation took place on 16 June 2022, according to Senad's account of the destruction. [@1]
