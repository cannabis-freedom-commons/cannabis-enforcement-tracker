On the evening of 21 May 2022, police raided a house compound in Janda Baik,
Pahang, and found up to 100 plants believed to be ganja growing around the
property. [@1] The 78-year-old former diplomat living there, Datuk Zainal
Abidin Alias, was detained in the raid, and his 53-year-old son Mohamed Rizal
was detained in Shah Alam two days later. [@1,2] The head of the federal
narcotics crime department confirmed both arrests and said officers had seized
the plants and other items. [@1]

Police later put the count at 102 cannabis plants worth about RM61,200, plus
1.02 kg of dried cannabis leaves and a bottle of cannabis extract. [@3] One of
the men admitted learning to grow the plants from a YouTube video and said the
seeds came from Kuala Lumpur. [@3] Police called it the largest cannabis-plant
seizure so far, since past cases had involved plants grown in pots. [@3] The
father was released from remand on May 28 while his son stayed in custody, and
on June 2 their lawyer wrote to the attorney-general asking him not to press
charges against the father. [@3]

On June 3 the pair were charged in the Bentong Magistrate's Court with
trafficking ganja and possessing cannabis oil, and the son faces a third charge
of misusing tetrahydrocannabinol. [@2] Five days later both pleaded not guilty
in the Raub Sessions Court to planting the 102 trees, a charge carrying life
imprisonment and at least six strokes of the cane. [@2] The prosecution
offered no bail, and the judge fixed July 22 for mention of the case. [@2]
