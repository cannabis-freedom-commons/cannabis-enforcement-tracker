Argentine Naval Prefecture officers detained two men who were trying to leave
Argentina with a load of marijuana from El Soberbio, Misiones, aboard a boat.
[@1] The officers were on a night patrol on the Uruguay River when they detected
a boat with its crew and bundles inside at kilometre 1157. [@1]

Officers confirmed the men were carrying three bundles holding more than 65
kilograms of marijuana divided into 56 panes, compressed marijuana bricks. [@1]

The Federal Prosecutor's Office in Oberá, Misiones, headed by Viviana Vallejos,
and the Federal Court of the same city, headed by Marcos Gallandat Luzuriaga,
took charge of the case. [@1] The federal court ordered the seizure of the drug,
of the boat used to carry it, and the detention of those involved. [@1]

Misiones Online reported the same night patrol on the Uruguay River, the three
bundles of more than 65 kilograms of marijuana in 56 panes, and the intervention
of the Oberá federal prosecutor and court. [@2]
