Turkish security forces opened an operation against the PKK in the southeastern province of Diyarbakır, fielding 1,165 gendarmerie troops joined by village guards. [@1] On the first day, Thursday 9 June 2022, they seized one tonne of cannabis and 2.1 million cannabis plants, the Interior Ministry said. [@1] Three AK-47 rifles and 20 kilograms of ammonium nitrate for homemade explosives were also seized during the operation. [@1] Interior Minister Süleyman Soylu said the government was determined to root out drug trade and terrorism, which officials link to PKK financing; Ankara says the group plays a role in international drug trafficking. [@1] The PKK is listed as a terrorist organisation by Türkiye and has been rebelling against the government for over 30 years. [@1]
