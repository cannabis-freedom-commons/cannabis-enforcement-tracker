A 55-year-old Douglas man was jailed at Douglas Courthouse after agreeing to
take delivery of a package of cannabis hidden in wheels at the haulage firm
where he worked. [@1] John Houghton, of Peel Road in Douglas, accepted the
package in exchange for £1,000, which he said he meant to spend repairing rust
patches on his car. [@1] With his employer's permission he had arranged for the
wheels to be shipped to Trade Distribution in Douglas on 24 May 2022. [@1]
Police dogs searching the haulage premises early on 1 June found the drugs in
four shrink-wrapped wheels addressed to Houghton, and he was arrested. [@1]

Officers found 48 packages of cannabis totalling 11.6 kg inside the wheels,
which police valued at £232,914. [@2] Houghton told police he knew the shipment
contained drugs but said he did not know what they were or what they were worth.
[@1] He pleaded guilty to being concerned in the production of cannabis to the
island. [@1]

Deemster Graeme Cook called it an "appalling and disgraceful offence" but gave
Houghton full credit for his openness and frankness in admitting guilt. [@2] The
judge said Houghton was fortunate that the load was only cannabis, because Class
A drugs would have brought a far higher sentence. [@2] Cook sentenced him to 46
months in prison, with automatic release at 23 months; the BBC reported the term
as three years and ten months. [@2]
