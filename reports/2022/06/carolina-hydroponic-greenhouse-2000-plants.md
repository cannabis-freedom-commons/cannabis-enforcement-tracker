Puerto Rico police seized the largest hydroponic marijuana greenhouse ever
recorded on the island on Tuesday 28 June 2022, in the barrio Carruzo of
Carolina. [@1] Officers from the police Intelligence and Arrests Unit and the
Carolina municipal police went to the three-storey building in sector Las
Filipinas to investigate a complaint of possible human trafficking. [@1] For the
municipal force, an anonymous tip about human trafficking had secured the search
warrant that led to the discovery. [@2] They found the greenhouse inside a
structure that had previously housed a treatment and rehabilitation centre for
users of illicit substances, but no person was inside. [@1] No arrests had been
made, and Commissioner Antonio López Figueroa said the investigation would
continue until those responsible were found. [@1]

Officers found about 2,000 marijuana plants, each six feet tall and mature
enough to be cut and processed. [@1] They also found marijuana already packed in
33-gallon bags and ready for distribution. [@1] Officers also seized 9 mm and
.40 calibre ammunition. [@1] Inter News Service reported that the municipal
police seized 1,179 pounds (about 535 kg) of marijuana, 390 plants and about 319
pounds ready to be packed. [@2] Police valued the confiscated drug at more than
$943,800 on the market, and the whole find including equipment at more than $1
million. [@2]

The building held about 30 inverter air-conditioning units and a special
lighting system for hydroponic cultivation, and was powered illegally through
independent transformers. [@1] López Figueroa said the greenhouse output looked
industrial in scale and that the equipment and facilities were very expensive.
[@1] Carolina mayor José Carlos Aponte said the operation with the police was a
hard blow to drug trafficking and thanked residents for their collaboration.
[@2]
