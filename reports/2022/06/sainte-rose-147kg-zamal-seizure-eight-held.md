On the evening of Friday 17 June 2022, a local gendarmerie patrol went to the
area near the port of Sainte-Rose after a report of a suspicious presence. [@1]
Searching a wooded area there, gendarmes detained one person found with numerous
bags packaged for transporting drugs, while several other people fled when the
gendarmes arrived. [@1] The gendarmes found 19 large-volume sports bags
containing drugs, and arrested two other people who had fled by vehicle. [@2]

The seizure totalled 147 kg of zamal found near the port of Sainte-Rose, hidden
in the 19 sports bags. [@2] The total included 1 kg of cannabis resin, the rest
being zamal herbal cannabis. [@2] Eight suspects were placed in custody and
questioned in the case. [@2]

Four of them were given summonses to appear in court, and four were brought
before the liberty and custody judge on 21 June for immediate trial on 22 June.
[@2] The gendarmerie captain described a genuine zamal trafficking flow from
Réunion toward Mauritius, calling Mauritius the number-one destination. [@1]
Investigators exchanged intelligence with the Mauritian authorities during the
inquiry, which was supervised by the Saint-Denis prosecutor. [@2]
