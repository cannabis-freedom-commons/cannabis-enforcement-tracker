On 28 June 2022 the Ontario Provincial Police's Provincial Joint Forces
Cannabis Enforcement Team executed search warrants at two greenhouse
locations and two related residences near Kingsville, Ontario. [@1] Officers
seized more than 45,000 cannabis plants and eight tonnes of processed
cannabis with an estimated value of more than $61 million, plus
offence-related property worth more than $300,000. [@1]

The OPP-led joint team carried out the raids with assistance from partner
units. [@1] No charges had been laid at the time of the 7 July report, and
the investigation was ongoing. [@1]
