National Police officers arrested Julio César González Hernández, 25, on a
public street in Reparto Los Robles, in District I of Managua, at 4:30 pm on 1
June 2022. [@1] A body search of González Hernández found a pink bag holding a
rectangular package of white powder wrapped in clear tape, which field-tested
positive for cocaine and weighed 1 kg 51 g. [@1] The same search also recovered
89 grams of crack rocks, a cocaine base, and 40.6 grams of marijuana, the only
cannabis in the two cases. [@1]

At 4:20 pm the next day, 2 June 2022, police arrested Saúl Alejandro Tercero
López in the Jocote Dulce settlement, also in District I, for alleged drug
trafficking and illegal possession of a firearm. [@1] A search of his home found
a rectangular package wrapped in adhesive tape and six clear plastic bags of
white powder that field-tested positive for cocaine and weighed 1 kg 448 g. [@1]
Officers also seized a pistol, a Nissan car with plate M 287 210 and a digital
scale in that case. [@1] The National Police presented the two arrests as part
of its continuing operation against organised crime and drug trafficking. [@1]
District I chief Manuel Zambrana said the first case involved 1 kg 448 g of
cocaine valued at 36,200 United States dollars. [@1] He said the second case
involved 1 kg 51 g of cocaine valued at 26,275 United States dollars. [@1]

Forensic chemical and ballistics tests came back positive for cocaine and
marijuana and found gunpowder residues on the pistol, showing it had been fired
recently. [@1] Officers also ran the Scintrex Trace particle detector over the
car, the men's clothing and their backs and palms, and it registered cocaine and
marijuana particles. [@1] Both men and the seized evidence were handed to the
competent authorities. [@1]
