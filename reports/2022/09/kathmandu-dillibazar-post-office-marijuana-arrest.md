Nepal Police arrested three people on the charge of smuggling marijuana into
Nepal from the United States through the post office. [@1] SSP Bikas Raj Khanal
of the Nepal Police Narcotics Control Bureau said officers first arrested Manoj
Kumar Rai of Kavre, who came to the Dillibazar post office on Monday to collect
his parcel. [@1] Post office staff had alerted police to a suspicious parcel as
soon as it arrived. [@1] Acting on what Rai told them, police then arrested
Rahul Chhetri and Ankit Kumar Shrestha, both of Kathmandu. [@1]

Police said their investigation into the people smuggling marijuana was
continuing. [@1]

Smugglers have increasingly turned Nepal's post offices into a hub of drug
smuggling, importing drugs ordered through foreign online platforms. [@1]
