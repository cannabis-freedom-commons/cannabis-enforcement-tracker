Police and customs officers found 56 vacuum-sealed packages of cannabis inside
two barrels during a joint drug operation at Deep Water Harbour, Antigua's main
seaport, on Tuesday 13 September 2022. [@1] A search at the port that day
uncovered the 56 packages. [@1]

The cannabis weighed 56 pounds, about 25.4 kg, and the police gave it a street
value of EC$336,000. [@1] Police said they were conducting further
investigations into the find. [@1]
