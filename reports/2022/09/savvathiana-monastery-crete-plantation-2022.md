In an operation starting Monday 19 September 2022, Heraklion police found a
two-level plantation of 1,152 carefully tended cannabis plants up to 2.5
metres tall in rugged forest above the Savvathiana Monastery, and uprooted
them. [@1] A nearby fitted-out drying room held 27.5 kg of cannabis, and
officers estimated the growers' profits above two million euros. [@1]

The growers had cut the monastery's wire fence and built the plantation in the
ravine above it, digging a pond, laying hoses, linking the levels with tunnels
and escape exits, and camping on site. [@1,2] They reached the plots posing as
pilgrims and hung prayer ropes on the trees. [@1,2] The monastery said it knew
nothing of the plantation, and the drug squad's preliminary inquiry
continued. [@1,2]
