National Guard officers attached to the Anti-Extortion and Kidnapping Command
arrested six people in Tucupita, Delta Amacuro, on Tuesday 13 September 2022 on
suspicion of drug trafficking. [@2] Authorities described the case as the
dismantling in Tucupita of the Brownie Magicos group, which sold drug-based
desserts, and said the six people seized in the Conas operation were placed at
the order of the prosecution service. [@2] The armed forces commander, General
Domingo Hernandez Larez, said citizens associated with TANCOL groups were
detained as they tried to sell marijuana cakes. [@1] Those detained were
described as the owners of a venture that advertised the sale of happy snacks on
Instagram in the city of Tucupita. [@1]

Authorities seized 10 cannabis-based brownies with a weight of 1.2 kilograms.
[@2] Officers also confiscated a vehicle that the group used to dispatch orders.
[@2] The operation also netted six mobile phones and a Hyundai car, according to
the armed forces account. [@1] The EFE wire described the brownie detentions as
one of two armed-forces operations that day, alongside the capture of four
people allegedly crossing Bolivar state with more than 10 kilos of drugs. [@3]

The general published the report on his Twitter account at about 5:47 on
Wednesday morning. [@1] TANCOL is an acronym invented by the Venezuelan
government for armed Colombian narcotraffickers that does not refer to any
specific gang. [@3]
