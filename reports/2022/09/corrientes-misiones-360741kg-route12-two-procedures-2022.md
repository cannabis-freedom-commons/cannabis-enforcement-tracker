Gendarmería Nacional Argentina reported seizing 360 kilos 741 grams of cannabis
in two separate operations on Route 12 in Corrientes and Misiones. [@1]

In the first, Sección San Cosme of Escuadrón 48 Corrientes was alerted before
dawn to an abandoned Chevrolet Aveo at kilometre 1.069 of National Route 12 in
Ensenada Grande, Corrientes. [@1] Officers found bundles inside the car and
burlap sacks beside it holding packages wrapped in transparent nylon like those
used to move drugs. [@1] Narcotest field tests came back positive for cannabis
sativa and 98 kilos 584 grams were seized in 116 packages. [@1] The Federal
Prosecutor's Office of Corrientes ordered the drug and the car confiscated, and
no one was reported arrested in that find. [@1]

In the second operation, the Jardín América group of Escuadrón 11 San Ignacio
was running roadside prevention checks at kilometre 1.375 of Route 12 at the
Santa Ana toll booth, in Misiones, when a second Chevrolet Aveo arrived. [@1]
The driver seemed to have trouble handling the car because of mechanical faults
and appeared in a hurry to pay the toll, so officers directed him to pull over
and he drove off. [@1] Gendarmes followed the car for 3 kilometres until it
pulled onto the shoulder, where the driver got out through the passenger door
and fled into the adjoining woodland. [@1] On the back seat and in the boot
officers found 12 compact bundles, of different sizes, holding 293 packages that
gave off the characteristic smell of marijuana. [@1] Field tests were positive
for cannabis sativa, with a total weight of 262 kilos 157 grams. [@1] The
Federal Court and the Federal Prosecutor's Office of Posadas ordered the drug
and the vehicle confiscated, and the driver who fled was not caught. [@1]

The release placed both procedures under Law 23.737, Argentina's narcotics law.
[@1] Corrientes al Día republished the same account of the two seizures and the
same weights the following day. [@2]
