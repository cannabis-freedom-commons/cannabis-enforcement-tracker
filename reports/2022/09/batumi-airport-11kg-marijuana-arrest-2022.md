Georgian police detained a 37-year-old foreign woman at Batumi International
Airport after finding more than 11 kg of marijuana in her luggage. [@1] The
Interior Ministry's press service announced the detention. [@2]

Investigators established that the woman had brought the drugs into Georgia from
South Africa. [@1] A search of her luggage found 11.2 kilograms of marijuana
packed in 11 bags in a hiding place inside a travel bag. [@1] The substance was
seized as material evidence. [@1]

A criminal case was opened under two articles of Georgia's Criminal Code,
covering the illegal import of a large amount of narcotics into Georgia and the
illegal acquisition, storage and transport of an especially large amount of
marijuana. [@1] The two charges carry 15 to 20 years in prison or life
imprisonment. [@1] The Interior Ministry reported the case on 20 September 2022.
[@2] Sputnik Georgia reported the same Interior Ministry account, including the
11.2 kilograms found in a hidden compartment of a travel bag. [@3]
