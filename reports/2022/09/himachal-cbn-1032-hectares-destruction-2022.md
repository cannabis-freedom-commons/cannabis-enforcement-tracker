Officers of the Central Bureau of Narcotics (CBN) destroyed 1,032 hectares
(12,900 bighas) of illicit cannabis cultivation in Himachal Pradesh in an
operation conducted over two weeks and announced on 20 September 2022. [@1,2]
Acting on intelligence about illicit cultivation, CBN teams verified the
information through physical surveys that detected further areas under
cultivation, then began the destruction drive with the district
administration, the Forest Department and the police. [@1] Four CBN teams
worked in different areas, later joined by officers of the Directorate of
Revenue Intelligence. [@1]

The teams paired enforcement with village awareness meetings about the effects
of drugs and the penal provisions of the NDPS Act, after which villagers
passed resolutions and helped destroy plantations under CBN supervision. [@1]
Officers worked steep rainy terrain, climbing to 11,000 feet and camping in
sensitive areas during the apple and pomegranate harvest season, and used GPS
tagging and drones to detect and monitor cultivation. [@1] The Narcotics
Commissioner said the crackdown would continue with the same vigour in other
parts of the country. [@1]
