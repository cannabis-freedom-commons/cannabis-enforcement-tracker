The Marion County Sheriff's Office arrested 19-year-old Jordan Matthew Neal on
charges of second degree murder and robbery with a firearm. [@1] Deputies said
Neal and another man, both armed, went to a park to rob two others of their
marijuana. [@1] Neal's partner, 18-year-old Cameron Cole Dalzell, pulled a gun
on the driver of the car they entered for the deal, and after Neal tried to draw
and the passenger took his gun, Dalzell and the driver exchanged gunfire. [@1]
Dalzell was killed, and the driver was shot in the hand and went to the
hospital. [@1]

Neal left and went to the sheriff's office to explain what happened, where
deputies arrested him. [@1] He was held at the Marion County Jail on a $100,000
bond. [@1]
