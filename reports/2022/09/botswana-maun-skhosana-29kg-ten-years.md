Maun Magistrates Court sentenced 29-year-old South African truck driver Thapelo
Skhosana to 10 years in jail for trafficking marijuana into Botswana. [@1]
Skhosana was convicted of trafficking in narcotic drugs after police stopped his
truck at Makalamabedi veterinary gate and police checkpoint on the outskirts of
Maun in January 2022. [@1] Officers searching his Woolworths truck found five
packages of a green substance that was later confirmed to be 29.4 kilograms of
dagga. [@2] The Ngami Times reported on 26 September 2022 that Chief Magistrate
Thebeetsile Mulalu had sentenced Skhosana to five years in prison and a P20 000
fine, payable within two days, with a further five years to be added if he did
not pay. [@2]

Skhosana could not raise the P20 000 within the two days given, so the
alternative term applied and he will serve the full decade. [@1] Magistrate
Thebeetsile Mulalo said drug taking was becoming a big problem in the area, with
young people particularly affected, and that the courts must impose appropriate
sentences. [@1] The magistrate noted that trafficking in narcotic drugs carries
a fine of up to P500 000 or up to 25 years in prison, or both. [@1] The court
treated Skhosana as a first-time offender and backdated his sentence to his
arrest 220 days earlier, so he will be released in 2032. [@1]

The Ngami Times reported that Skhosana told police an unknown man had given him
the packages at Kwa Nokeng filling station in Maun to take to a man in
Francistown. [@2] The magistrate declined to believe that police had settled for
the carrier when they could have pursued the supplier, saying Skhosana had
protected the identity of the dealer. [@2] The Voice ran the story in its print
edition of 30 September 2022 under the headline A decade away for dagga, and its
subheading said the trucker paid a high price for failing to pay the P20,000
fine. [@3]
