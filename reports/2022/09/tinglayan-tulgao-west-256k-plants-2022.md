From 13 to 15 September 2022, Kalinga police and Regional Mobile Force Battalion 15 raided two
plantation sites covering 2.1 hectares in Barangay Tulgao West, Tinglayan. [@1] They uprooted
256,500 fully grown marijuana plants with a standard drug price of about PHP 51.5 million and
burned them on site. [@1] The regional police information officer said teams keep revisiting
cleared sites so that seeds left behind cannot grow into a new crop, as supply-reduction work
ordered continued from the past administration goes on. [@1] The report names no arrests. [@1]
