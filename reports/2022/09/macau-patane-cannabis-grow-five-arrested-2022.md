An air-conditioner short-circuit fire exposed a cannabis grow in an
industrial-building unit on Rua da Ribeira do Patane, where Judiciary Police
arrested jobless local man surnamed Ng. [@3] Officers found nine cultivation
tents, 25 cannabis plants and 268 grammes of cannabis flowers with growing,
weighing and packing equipment in the unit. [@3] A search of Ng's flat on Rua
Central da Areia Preta found seven packets of cannabis products weighing about
90.35 grammes. [@3] Police put the total seized in that first haul at 358.36
grammes with a street value of nearly 36,000 patacas. [@3] Ng said he bought six
seeds online and rented the industrial unit for HK$15,000 a month since April to
grow 25 plants by grafting for sale. [@3] Ng said he had sold 100 grammes for
about 20,000 patacas, admitted taking drugs and tested positive. [@3]

After follow-up investigation, Judiciary Police arrested Ho, Lei, Chang and Tin
in different districts on Tuesday. [@1] The four are local residents Ho, a
29-year-old casino PR worker, Chang, a 29-year-old saleswoman, Lei, a
28-year-old restaurant worker, and Tin, an insurance saleswoman. [@1] Officers
found two cannabis joints and 1.57 grammes of cannabis flowers in the
city-centre flat where Ho and Chang lived together, which the pair said Ng had
provided for their own use. [@1] Officers found no illicit drugs in Lei's and
Tin's flats. [@1] Ho, Lei and Chang had repeatedly helped Ng install cultivation
equipment and decorate the unit, which police said showed they were jointly
involved in growing cannabis despite denying direct trafficking. [@1] Tin had
stayed in the building for a while so police did not rule out her involvement in
trafficking, and she denied committing the crime. [@1] The four were intercepted
on 13 September over moving and installing growing equipment and staying in the
building. [@2]

The follow-up haul totalled 2.33 grammes valued at about 2,400 patacas. [@1] The
four tested negative for drugs but police believed they acted for profit. [@1]
Ng was transferred to the Public Prosecutions Office facing drug-trafficking,
drug-abuse and paraphernalia charges. [@3] Ho, Lei, Chang and Tin were
transferred to the Public Prosecutions Office facing drug-abuse and
organised-crime charges. [@1]
