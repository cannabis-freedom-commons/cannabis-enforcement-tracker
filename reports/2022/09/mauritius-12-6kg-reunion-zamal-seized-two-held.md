In Mauritius, anti-drug officers arrested two Mauritians, Georges Désiré
Lafleur, 39, and Guillano Ludovic Thomas, 42, and the pair were placed in
provisional detention for drug trafficking. [@2] Their preliminary questioning
began at the Anti-Drug and Smuggling Unit (ADSU) after the provisional
trafficking charges. [@2]

Officers seized 12.6 kilograms of zamal, described as cannabis, that had been
intended for the Mauritian market. [@2] The same seizure is also reported as
12.6 kg of cannabis, valued by the authorities at 15.1 million Mauritian rupees,
about 186,755 euros. [@1]

The consignment had travelled by sea from Réunion toward Grand-Gaube in
Mauritius, and ADSU officers followed it before arresting the two men. [@1] The
seizure was made on Thursday 8 September 2022 by the ADSU Northern Division
anti-drug brigade. [@1] The operation followed detailed field intelligence work
and exchanges between ADSU and the Réunion gendarmerie. [@2] The surveillance
and interception operation lasted about six hours. [@1]
