Officers of the Trelew road-safety section, part of the Chubut core squadron of
Gendarmería Nacional Argentina, were running checks on Ruta Nacional 3 when they
saw a vehicle carrying two people towards the city of Rawson. [@1]

Searching the car, the gendarmes found three rectangular packages on the floor
on the mat beside the passenger seat, similar to those used to move drugs. [@1]
Criminalistics and forensic officers ran Narcotest field tests that came back
positive for cannabis sativa. [@1] A total of 4 kilos 613 grams of cannabis was
seized. [@1]

The Federal Court and the Federal Prosecutor's Office of Rawson ordered the drug
confiscated and both occupants detained. [@1]
