Gendarmes of the Núcleo section of Squadron 9 Oberá detained a motorcyclist on
Ruta Provincial 39 at Paraje 18, Jardín América, after he stopped and threw away
a black rucksack that held 5 kilos 295 grams of cannabis sativa. [@1]

While officers were still working at the same spot, a Volkswagen Amarok drove
past at speed and its driver struck the gendarmes before continuing; the vehicle
was later found abandoned with 47 bundles holding 1,135 kilos 088 grams of
marijuana inside and in the cargo bed. [@1] The same squadron, with other units,
stopped a Brazilian-flagged truck on Ruta Nacional 14 at Campo Grande, and after
a narcotics dog reacted and a judge authorised moving the vehicle to the unit,
officers counted 307 bundles holding 8,952 kilos 064 grams of cannabis sativa.
[@1]

The Oberá federal court and federal prosecutor's office handled all three cases
and ordered the seizure of 10,092 kilos 447 grams of the drug, the confiscation
of the vehicles, cash, phones and other items, and the detention of the two
people arrested. [@1] Canal Doce Misiones dated the first two operations to
Saturday 14 May 2022, in the same Jardín América and Campo Grande area. [@2]
