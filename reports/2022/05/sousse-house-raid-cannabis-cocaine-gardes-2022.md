National Guard officers raided the Sousse home of a man suspected of dealing
drugs on Friday 6 May 2022 and detained him of their own. [@1] The patrol came
from the Sousse National Guard's research and inspection brigade, supported by
its rapid-intervention squad and intervention unit. [@3]

Officers seized cannabis and cocaine, a sum of money taken to be proceeds of
dealing, knives and tools used to cut the drugs. [@3] The raid on the
neighbour's home in the region was carried out after a patrol of the Sousse
National Guard research and inspection brigade detained him, the National Guard
statement said; the cannabis was Indian hemp. [@2] No weight or amount was
published for the cannabis, the cocaine or the money. [@1]

The public prosecutor's office ordered the man held in detention and instructed
that the investigation continue. [@1] The National Guard's general directorate
said the raid was part of the national fight against drug trafficking. [@1]
