Police found ten boxes of dagga, fifty dagga plants, two scales, three fans and
other cannabis production equipment on a farm near Philadelphia in the Western
Cape. [@2] Two suspects, a man aged 47 and a woman aged 56, were arrested there
for illegal possession of firearms, ammunition and dagga. [@2] Police also
seized an undisclosed amount of cash, one shotgun, two rifles, 86 rounds and
three cartridges. [@1] The two suspects were to appear at Atlantis Magistrate
Court on Monday. [@2] Police said the street value of the dagga was yet to be
established. [@2]
