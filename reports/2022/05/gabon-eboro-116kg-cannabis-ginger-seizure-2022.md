Customs officers of the central customs bureau in Bitam stopped a van at the
Eboro border post on 21 May 2022 and seized 116.6 kg of Indian hemp, the
cannabis the report describes. [@1]

The vehicle was a Mitsubishi stopped for a routine check at Eboro, and it was
carrying the two men and a load of fresh food bound for Libreville. [@2] The
cannabis had been loaded at the Marché mondial on the Cameroonian side and was
hidden among sacks of ginger in the van, which was to unload at the Venez-voir
market in Libreville. [@1] An officer opened an unidentified bundle in a ginger
sack, found cannabis, searched every sack and then weighed the load. [@2]

Bitam sits at the meeting of the Gabon, Cameroon and Equatorial Guinea borders
and is increasingly a centre of drug trafficking. [@1]
