Detectives from the Drug and Serious Crime Group, with the Ipswich Tactical Crime Squad and Laidley police, raided a rural property on Redwood Drive, Brightview, on 17 May 2022 and found four greenhouses holding 4,581 cannabis plants of varying sizes, with hydroponic chemicals, irrigation and machinery police said was used for large-scale production. [@1,2] Officers also seized 28 kg of dried cannabis from a shed on the property. [@1] A second search warrant the same afternoon at Boyces Road, Mount Tarampa, uncovered a greenhouse with a sophisticated hydroponics system and 144 more plants. [@1]

Five men, aged 27, 27, 46, 36 and 21, were charged with producing a dangerous drug, possessing a dangerous drug and possessing anything used in the commission of a crime, and were due in the Ipswich Magistrates Court on 18 May. [@1,2] Police valued the cannabis at the two properties at an estimated street value of $1.7 million and said both properties were being used by a criminal syndicate for large-scale production. [@1] The men were remanded in custody with their matters to return to court later in the month. [@2]
