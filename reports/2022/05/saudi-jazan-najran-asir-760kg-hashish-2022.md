Saudi Arabia's Border Guard foiled hashish and khat smuggling attempts in
the southern Jazan, Najran and Asir regions, seizing 760 kg of hashish. [@1,2]
The Saudi Press Agency announcement, reported on Wednesday 4 May 2022, also
records 45.6 tonnes of the narcotic plant khat taken in the same
operations. [@1,2]

Sixty-one suspects were held: 41 Saudi citizens, 11 Ethiopians, seven
Yemenis and two Pakistanis. [@1] A second account confirms 61 people held
over the smuggling of 760 kg of hashish and almost 46 tonnes of khat in the
same three regions. [@2]

The sources report no charges or court outcomes for the 61 people held. [@1,2]
