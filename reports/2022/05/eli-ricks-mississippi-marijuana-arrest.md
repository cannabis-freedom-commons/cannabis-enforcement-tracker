Alabama cornerback Eli Ricks was booked on Mississippi charges of speeding,
driving without insurance and possession of marijuana on the evening of Sunday 1
May 2022. [@1] Ricks was arrested by the Mississippi Highway Patrol. [@1] He was
booked at 5:15 p.m. that day. [@1]

According to an online database, it was his first marijuana possession charge.
[@1] His bond was set at $0. [@1]

Ricks transferred to Alabama from LSU before the 2022 season. [@1] He had
entered the transfer portal in November, about a month after head coach Ed
Orgeron was fired. [@1] The 6-foot-2 cornerback from California began his
college career at LSU, where he earned Freshman All-SEC and AP All-America
honours in his first season. [@1] He was expected to compete for a starting role
at Alabama, which had lost starting cornerback Josh Jobe to the NFL. [@1]
