Militia officers detained a 27-year-old Tiraspol resident who was smoking
marijuana in one of the capital's internet clubs. [@1] The arrest came after a
visitor told the duty desk that the man was smoking a mixture in the toilet
without hiding from other people. [@1] It happened shortly after one in the
morning on Saturday, 21 May 2022. [@1] The Interior Ministry said the man used a
special device to smoke the banned smoking mixture in the club toilet. [@2]

Officers who arrived seized a plastic bottle and sent it, with the suspect's
biological fluid, for examination. [@1] The ministry said the bottle was seized
together with the suspect's biological fluid and sent for expert testing. [@2]

If drug use is confirmed, the 27-year-old suspect faces administrative liability
carrying a fine above 2,700 Transnistrian roubles or 15 days of administrative
arrest. [@1]
