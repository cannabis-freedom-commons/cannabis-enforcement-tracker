The Customs Anti-Narcotic Unit (CANU) arrested three men on Tuesday after they
were reportedly found with 48 grams (1.6 ounces) of marijuana. [@1] The men were
detained during a CANU narcotics operation in Tabatinga, Central Rupununi, in
Lethem, Region Nine. [@2]

Guyana Times reported that the three were held with cannabis in 31 small Ziplock
bags, which tested positive for cannabis and weighed a total of 48 grammes. [@2]

CANU said the operations were part of its strategy to combat micro-trafficking
within various communities, including schools, and thanked the public for
supporting its work. [@1] Guyana Times reported that the operation followed
accounts of students selling narcotics in schools in Region Nine, and that CANU
said it was working with the Education Ministry on a programme to educate
children about drugs. [@2]

Investigations were continuing, and no charges or court outcome were reported in
either account. [@2]
