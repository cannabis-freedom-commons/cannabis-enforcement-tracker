The Unified Drug Enforcement Strike Team arrested Willie Cobb Jr., 48, of Ocala
on suspicion of drug trafficking after finding dozens of containers of drugs in
his home. [@1] Cobb was arrested on Friday 20 May 2022, booked into the Marion
County Jail and later released on bond. [@2] Records showed Cobb was selling
crystal meth, fentanyl, cocaine, marijuana and other controlled substances out
of his Ocala home. [@1]

UDEST began investigating Cobb in February 2022 after a complaint about drug
activity at his residence on NE 22nd Street. [@2] UDEST had first noticed Cobb
in January after a man was found dead at his home from fentanyl use. [@1]

The search found a loaded firearm and multiple bags of narcotics in the home.
[@1] The UDEST report listed the marijuana found at over 391 grams. [@2] Agents
also seized two pill presses, scales, packaging materials and about $6,000 in
cash sorted into pre-counted stacks. [@2]

Cobb faces multiple felony charges including armed trafficking of crystal
methamphetamine, fentanyl, cocaine and hydrocodone, possession of a firearm by a
convicted felon and possession of marijuana with intent to distribute. [@2] He
also faces a misdemeanour charge of possessing drug paraphernalia. [@2] A
criminal history check showed Cobb had felony convictions in 1996 for sale and
possession of cocaine. [@2] No court date had been scheduled when the reports
were published. [@2]
