Officers of the Bender branch of the State Customs Committee's department for
combating smuggling and customs violations detained a man who had smuggled
narcotic drugs in from neighbouring Moldova. [@1] The customs operation took
place close to the border with Moldova, in the Solnechny neighbourhood of
Bender. [@1]

Customs officers stopped a suspicious-looking man carrying a black polyethylene
bag. [@1] The search of his hand luggage turned up a green plant mass, suspected
to be marijuana, and the man was detained. [@1] The customs officers called an
investigative team to the scene of the incident. [@1]

A later expert examination classified the contents of the bag as marijuana
(cannabis) weighing more than 88 grams. [@1] A criminal case was opened for
smuggling narcotic drugs, and Transnistria's Investigative Committee is handling
the investigation. [@1]
