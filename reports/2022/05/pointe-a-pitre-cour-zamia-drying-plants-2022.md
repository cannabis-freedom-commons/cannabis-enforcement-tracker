On Tuesday afternoon, Territorial National Police officers seized several
cannabis plants in a disused house at Cour Zamia. [@1] On 31 May in the late
afternoon, acting on a requisition from the public prosecutor, DTPN officers ran
an identity-check operation in Pointe-à-Pitre. [@1] The operation covered the
sectors of Zamia, Raspail, chemin Neuf and Massabielle in Pointe-à-Pitre. [@1]
When officers arrived in Zamia, several people fled at the sight of the police.
[@1]

The police dog used in the operation is specialised in searching for narcotics.
[@1] In tall grass the dog found two sachets containing cannabis. [@1] Following
the dog into a ruined house, officers found a line holding more than ten
cannabis plants left to dry. [@1] In another room of the same abandoned house,
officers found a second line holding more than twenty cannabis herb plants. [@1]

All of the cannabis plants were seized and then destroyed. [@1] The DTPN is the
Territorial Directorate of the National Police. [@1]
