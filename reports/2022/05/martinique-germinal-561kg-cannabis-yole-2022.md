The French navy surveillance frigate Germinal spotted a yole, a fast open boat,
travelling without lights or flag on the high seas west of Martinique on 24 May
2022. [@1] Some reports described the boat as a "lancha" or "go-fast", other
names for the same fast open vessel. [@2] The recovered cannabis weighed 561 kg,
according to the Fort-de-France prosecutor's account, while the navy's own
publication put the figure at 560 kg. [@1]

The seven crew members, all stating that they were Venezuelan nationals, were
handed with the boat and the drugs to the Caribbean branch of OFAST, France's
anti-narcotics office, on 25 May 2022. [@2] The prosecutor in charge was
Clarisse Taron, the Fort-de-France public prosecutor attached to the city's
specialised inter-regional jurisdiction (JIRS). [@2]

The seven were held in custody and brought before the criminal court in
immediate appearance on 27 May 2022. [@1] The hearing was adjourned to 13 June
2022 and all seven were remanded in pre-trial detention in the meantime. [@1]
LINFO.re, a Réunion outlet drawing on France-Antilles, reported that the seven
were in pre-trial detention after the 27 May appearance. [@4]

The armed forces said the seizure cost traffickers more than one million euros
and that the cargo was destined for the islands of the Caribbean arc, and that
it was the forces' third seizure of 2022, bringing their annual total to one
tonne of cannabis and 680 kg of cocaine. [@3]
