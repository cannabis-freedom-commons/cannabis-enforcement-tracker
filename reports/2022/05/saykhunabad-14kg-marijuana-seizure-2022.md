Acting on prior information, officers stopped a Lacetti driven by
Akaltyn-district resident A.F. on the M-39 road in Sirdaryo district. [@2]
Officers found 111 grams of marijuana under the front seat in the presence of
witnesses. [@2] A.F. said he had bought the marijuana from Saykhunabad-district
resident S.Sh. for 2 million sums. [@2] Officers then went with witnesses to the
house of S.Sh. in Saykhunabad district. [@2]

In the bedroom officers found hashish packed in four matchboxes, dried marijuana
and cannabis seeds in small cellophane bags. [@1] In the living room officers
found marijuana in three cellophane bags, electronic scales and a bottomless
plastic bottle. [@1] In the basement officers found marijuana in two polythene
bags. [@1] On the plot officers found eight tended cannabis bushes 5-10 cm tall
hidden under piled tree branches. [@1] Officers recorded 13,879 grams of
marijuana and 1.68 grams of hashish seized in the operation. [@3]

The operation was run by the criminal-investigation department of the Sirdaryo
police (UVD) and the regional State Security Service (SGB). [@1] All finds were
seized as material evidence. [@2] A pre-investigation check was under way. [@1]
