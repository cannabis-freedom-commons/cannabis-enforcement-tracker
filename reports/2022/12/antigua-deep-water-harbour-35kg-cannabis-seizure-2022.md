Police and customs officers found 77½ pounds (about 35 kg) of cannabis hidden in
two barrels and a cardboard box during a joint operation at Deep Water Harbour,
Antigua's main seaport, on Monday 5 December 2022. [@1] The cannabis was valued
at EC$465,000 and was taken to the police station. [@1] The Royal Police Force
of Antigua and Barbuda said it thwarted an attempt by unknown persons to import
the drugs into the country. [@2] Police said the drugs were taken to St John's
Police Station, where an investigation began. [@2]

No arrests were made, according to the police statement. [@2]

The week before, the same two agencies found two guns, more than 100 rounds of
ammunition and about 40 pounds of cannabis in a barrel at the same port, an
earlier seizure with its own investigation. [@1] Pointville described that
earlier find as two guns, more than 100 rounds and about 40 pounds of cannabis
hidden in a barrel in vacuum-sealed packages, valued at $262,000. [@2] The
police said investigations into the matters were ongoing. [@1]
