Costa Rica's National Coast Guard intercepted two high-speed drug boats in
Pacific waters. [@1] Officers detained four Nicaraguan citizens on one boat and
three Colombians on the other, seven people in total. [@1] The crew of the first
boat ignored orders to stop and tried to flee, so officers fired on the engines
to disable it. [@2] The first interception happened at 5:30 a.m. off Estero
Grande beach in Golfo Dulce. [@2] The second happened at 8:25 a.m. about 120
kilometres off Cabo Matapalo on the Osa Peninsula, where three Colombians were
apprehended. [@2] The second boat was stopped about three hours after the first,
a few kilometres away in the same Pacific zone. [@1]

Each boat carried 1,625 kilograms of marijuana, a combined 3,250 kilograms. [@1]
Drug-control police confirmed the weights after bringing both craft into port
and completing their examinations. [@1] The Security Ministry said both loads
had come from Colombia and pointed to the identical quantities as a link between
the cases. [@2] The Colombian-crewed boat also carried five kilograms of
cocaine, which is not counted in the cannabis total. [@2]

Both boats travelled at high speed with no flag or name. [@1] Authorities
reported the two operations on Tuesday. [@1]
