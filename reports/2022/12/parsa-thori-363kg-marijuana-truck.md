Armed Police Force officers stopped a truck carrying about 363 kg of marijuana
at Thutekhola in Thori Rural Municipality-3, Parsa district. [@2] The Border
Outpost at Thori acted on a tip-off passed on by the headquarters of Armed
Police Force Nepal No. 2 Chhinnamasta Battalion in Mahottari. [@2] The marijuana
was packed in 22 cartons and hidden behind a false bottom in the truck,
registration Na 4 Kha 6555, which was heading from Hetauda in Makwanpur toward
Bijayabasti in Parsa. [@1]

The driver fled the scene as soon as he saw the officers, and no arrest was
reported. [@1] The marijuana and the truck were handed over to the Area Police
Office at Thori for investigation. [@1]

The seizure was made on the 26th day of the Nepali month of Mangsir in the year
2079, which corresponds to 12 December 2022. [@2]
