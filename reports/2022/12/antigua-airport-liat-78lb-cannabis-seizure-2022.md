Forty-three vacuum-sealed packages of cannabis were seized at V.C. Bird
International Airport, Antigua's international airport, on Wednesday 14 December
2022. [@1] The packages were found at LIAT Cargo storage, the cargo facility of
the regional airline LIAT, during a joint narcotics operation by the police and
the Customs Department. [@1]

The cannabis weighed 78 pounds, about 35.4 kg. [@1] The authorities estimated
its value at EC$468,000, the East Caribbean dollar used in Antigua and Barbuda.
[@1]

No arrest was reported in the accounts of the seizure. [@1] The police said they
were continuing their investigations into the matter. [@1] Antigua News Room
published the same account of 43 packages, 78 pounds and the EC$468,000 estimate
on the same day. [@2]
