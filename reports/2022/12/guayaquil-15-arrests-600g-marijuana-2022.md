Ecuador's National Police reported the results of its anti-drug operations in
Guayaquil on the afternoon of Monday 5 December 2022. [@1] Officers detained 15
people in those Guayaquil operations. [@1] The operations also seized 600 grams
of marijuana, the cannabis component recorded here. [@1] Alongside the drugs,
the police seized ammunition. [@1] Police said the substances were intended for
domestic consumption. [@1]

The Guayaquil result was reported the same day police announced a separate
seizure of 2.2 tonnes of cocaine hidden among bananas in a container bound for
Sweden. [@1] The container shipment was to leave from the port of Guayaquil for
Sweden. [@1] A single suspect was detained over the cocaine container, the
exporter's representative at the port. [@1]
