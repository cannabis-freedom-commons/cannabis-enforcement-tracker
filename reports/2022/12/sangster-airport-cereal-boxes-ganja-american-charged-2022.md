Narcotics officers arrested Jermain Hall Sutherland, a businessman from Fort
Worth, Texas. [@1] Police say Sutherland also resides in Duanvale, Trelawny.
[@1] Sutherland was accused of possession of, dealing in and importing ganja,
charges under Jamaica’s Dangerous Drugs Act. [@1]

Narcotics officers apprehended him after he had been on the island for a week.
[@1] Reports say Sutherland landed at the airport the previous Thursday on an
American Airlines flight from Texas, but his luggage was delayed, so he went to
the airport on Wednesday to collect it. [@1] At 12:15 p.m., the luggage was
searched at the Customs Department, when it was found to contain two cereal
boxes, each with compressed ganja, the police said. [@1] The find was at
Sangster International Airport in Montego Bay, in the parish of St James. [@1]
Narcotics detectives arrested Jermain Hall Sutherland after he had been on the
island for a week. [@2]

The American was taken into custody and was subsequently charged. [@1]
