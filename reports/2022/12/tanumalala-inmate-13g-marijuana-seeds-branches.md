Sefo Amituana'i, a man serving a sentence at Tanumalala prison, appeared in the
Supreme Court of Samoa during the week of 19 December 2022 to answer three
charges of possessing marijuana. [@1] He was accused of possessing 27 marijuana
seeds and 34 branches weighing 13.48 grams in total. [@1] Justice Fepuleai
Ameperosa Roma told the defendant, who pleaded not guilty, that the charges were
very serious, especially because he was already serving time at Tanumalala and
now faced drug-related charges. [@1]

The judge set a hearing for 19 April 2023 and told the inmate his scheduled
release on 3 April 2023 would go ahead, but that he must return to present his
defence to the new charges. [@1] Justice Roma told the inmate that 19 April
would be the day police witnesses were called and the day he could bring
evidence supporting his not guilty plea. [@2]

The case was reported as part of broader concern about how drugs reach
Tanumalala prison, where an investigation into a suspended prison officer later
heard witnesses describe marijuana moving inside the prison. [@3]
