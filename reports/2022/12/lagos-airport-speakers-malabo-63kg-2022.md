Parcels of cannabis weighing 6.3 kg, concealed in sound systems (speakers) and bound for Malabo, Equatorial Guinea, were seized through the SAHCO export shed at Lagos airport on Thursday 8 December 2022. [@1,2] The account comes from NDLEA spokesman Femi Babafemi's statement on the agency's end-of-week operations, as reported by both outlets. [@1,2]

Operatives first arrested freight agent Joseph Obiji, who was connected with the consignment, and the following day nabbed a second agent, Mbanu Ifeanyi Andrew, in a follow-up operation at ASPAMDA market in the Trade Fair Complex, Ojo area of Lagos. [@1,2]
