NDLEA officers in Lagos intercepted a truck and a bus conveying 113 jumbo bags
of cannabis sativa weighing 4,802.84 kg around the VGC estate area of Ajah on
Sunday 11 December 2022. [@1,2] Three suspects, Taofeek Yusuf, Ifeanyi Okorie
and Israel Nwachukwu, were arrested in connection with the seizure. [@1,2]

Agency spokesman Femi Babafemi announced the seizure in a Sunday statement that
mostly covered a separate haul of not less than 1,760,460 tramadol and other
opioid pills hidden in packs of Indomie noodles at Lagos airport and in Gombe
State. [@1,2] Those opioid consignments are separate events and are not counted
here.
