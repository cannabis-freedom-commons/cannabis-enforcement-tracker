Costa Rica's National Coast Guard stopped a go-fast boat in the Caribbean off
Limón on the night of Sunday 18 December 2022 and detained its five crew
members. [@1] The detainees were identified by the surnames Pino, Mercado,
Escalante, Anaez and Sánchez, all Colombian nationals. [@1] Officers seized
eight kilograms of marijuana aboard the boat, alongside one tonne of cocaine.
[@1]

A United States aircraft on a preventive overflight spotted the boat about 142
kilometres off the Limón coast and alerted the Costa Rican authorities. [@1] The
United States provided the aircraft under a bilateral agreement on joint ocean
surveillance. [@1] Coast guard officers chased and stopped the craft, which was
more than nine metres long and carried no name, registration or flag. [@1]
Aboard they found fuel containers as well as the drugs. [@1]

The coast guard took the drugs and detainees ashore, and the Security Ministry
reported the results of the operation on the Monday. [@1] The suspects face
charges of international drug trafficking, which carries a maximum of 20 years
in prison in Costa Rica. [@1]
