A district police officer in Krasnoyarsk Krai found almost 12 kg of marijuana at
the home of a 48-year-old man in the village of Brazhnoye, Kansky district,
during a routine check of people under police supervision. [@1] The man,
previously convicted of drug trafficking, had gone to the town to report to the
criminal-inspection authority, and his 67-year-old friend let the officer in.
[@1]

Inside, the officer noticed a plastic container of ground plant material and a
smoking pipe on a bedside table and waited for the owner to return. [@1] The
owner admitted the container held a banned substance, and officers called to the
scene seized it and searched the other rooms with him. [@1] In the storeroom
police found three more sacks and a metal bathtub filled with dry plant stems
with a characteristic smell. [@1] An examination established that the seized
material was marijuana weighing almost 12 kg in total. [@1]

The man said he had gathered the weed in vacant lots outside the village for his
own use and tried to collect enough to last through the New Year holidays. [@1]
Investigators at the Kansky interior ministry division opened a criminal case
under part 2 of article 228 of the Russian Criminal Code, which carries a
maximum of ten years in prison. [@1] The suspect was placed in custody as a
measure of restraint. [@1] The regional police press service said the man could
face up to ten years in a penal colony because he had previously been caught for
a similar offence. [@2]
