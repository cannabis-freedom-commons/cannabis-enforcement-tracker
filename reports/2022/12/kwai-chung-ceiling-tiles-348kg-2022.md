Hong Kong Customs found about 348 kg of suspected cannabis buds hidden inside ceiling
tiles at the Kwai Chung Customhouse Cargo Examination Compound on 9 December 2022.
[@1,2] Officers had selected a seaborne consignment declared as ceiling tiles arriving
from Canada for inspection and found 40 boxes of tiles with the buds concealed inside,
worth an estimated HK$60 million. [@1,2]

After a follow-up investigation and a controlled delivery operation, officers arrested
two men aged 32 and 34 in San Po Kong on 23 December. [@1,2] Customs said
investigations were continuing and warned it would step up enforcement before the
Christmas and New Year holidays, when it expected local drug demand to rise. [@1]
Trafficking in a dangerous drug carries a maximum penalty of a HK$5 million fine and
life imprisonment. [@1]
