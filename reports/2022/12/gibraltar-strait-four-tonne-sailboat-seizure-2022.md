Britain's National Crime Agency tipped off Spain about a sailing boat suspected of running drugs from Morocco, working with the MAOC-N maritime centre in Lisbon; Spanish customs intercepted the vessel and escorted it into Cádiz. [@1,2] The boat had got into trouble in a storm in the Strait of Gibraltar on 10 December 2022, and the search found 130 bales of cannabis resin, about four tonnes. [@1,2]

Officers detained a 49-year-old man and a 53-year-old woman, both British; they remained in custody in Spain while investigations continued, with details made public by Spanish police only weeks later. [@1,2] The NCA's regional manager said the agency had suspected the vessel of shipping drugs across the Atlantic and pledged to keep disrupting organised trafficking networks with its partners. [@1,2]
