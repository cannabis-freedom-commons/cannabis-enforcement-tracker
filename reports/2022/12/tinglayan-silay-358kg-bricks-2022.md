Kalinga police seized nine sacks holding 358 kilos of dried marijuana bricks and
tubular packs in Tinglayan town worth P42.96 million, in what the provincial
police information officer called the biggest single seizure of marijuana in
Kalinga in 2022. [@1]

Residents of Sitio Silay in Barangay Poblacion tipped the police that
unidentified men were about to move marijuana bricks from Tinglayan to Tabuk
City. [@1] Police went to the village to arrest the men, but they abandoned the
cargo and fled, and no one was arrested after a pursuit operation. [@1] The
sacks had been stockpiled beside the road when officers arrived shortly after 4
a.m., so the police positioned themselves nearby and waited, but nobody came for
them. [@2]

Officers found bricks and tubular packs of marijuana leaves and stalks in
transparent plastic, and the material was taken to the Kalinga forensic unit for
examination. [@1]
