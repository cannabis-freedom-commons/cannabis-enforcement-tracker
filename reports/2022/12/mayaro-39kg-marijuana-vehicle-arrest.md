Southern Division Task Force officers arrested a 25-year-old man during an
anti-crime exercise on December 8, 2022. [@1] At about 9:50 p.m., while on
mobile patrol along Bellevue Industrial Estate in Mayaro, officers acting on
intelligence intercepted a motor vehicle driven by a man from Champ Fleurs. [@1]
A search of the vehicle found two crocus bags containing 39 kilograms of
marijuana. [@1] The driver was arrested and the marijuana was seized. [@1]

Police put the estimated street value of the 39 kilograms at $1,115,400 in
Trinidad and Tobago dollars. [@1] The account identifies the driver only as a
25-year-old man and does not report a quantity beyond the 39 kilograms found in
the car. [@1] Constable Weekes is continuing enquiries, with no charge reported
in either account. [@2]
