on Monday, according to the newspaper's information. [@1] The wider operation

biggest cannabis-herb production and resale networks supplying local dealers out
of action. [@1]

searches also seized weapons. [@1] Officers also seized 20 million francs in
cash. [@1] The newspaper called the cash seizure a record haul for police
officers. [@1]
