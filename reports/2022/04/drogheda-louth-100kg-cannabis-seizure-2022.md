On Friday 29 April 2022 the Garda National Drugs and Organised Crime Bureau and the Drogheda Divisional Drugs Unit joined Revenue's Customs Service in an intelligence-led operation targeting an organised crime group in the Louth area. [@1,2,4] Revenue officers searched a premises in the Drogheda area under warrant and seized about 100 kg of herbal cannabis with an estimated street value of €2 million. [@1,2,4]

Two men, aged 49 and 65, were arrested at the scene and detained at Drogheda Garda station under section 2 of the Criminal Justice (Drug Trafficking) Act 1996. [@1,2,4] A Garda update on 1 May said both men had since been charged and were due before a special sitting of Drogheda District Court that morning. [@3]
