Police in Blantyre arrested Castel Malawi Limited Managing Director Herve Milhad
on the morning of Friday 8 April 2022 on allegations of cultivating chamba, the
local name for cannabis. [@1] Milhad was granted police bail later on that same
Friday. [@1] Police said he would appear in court on the following Tuesday or
Wednesday, once Bvumbwe Research Station had completed a test to verify that the
herb was cannabis. [@1]

Officers who raided his residence alleged that they found five grams of Indian
hemp, locally called chamba, in his bedroom. [@1] Police also alleged that he
was illegally cultivating chamba in his vegetable garden at Kabula Heights in
Blantyre. [@1] Officers who raided his residence on Friday 8 April 2022 uprooted
59 plants of the herb he was allegedly growing illegally. [@1]

The account was given by Beatrice Mikuwa, deputy police spokesperson for the
South West Division, describing the bedroom seizure, the garden cultivation and
the raid. [@1] At the time of the 9 April 2022 account, the laboratory
verification was still pending, so no charge, plea or court outcome had yet been
reported. [@1]
