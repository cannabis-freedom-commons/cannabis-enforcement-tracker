Officers of the Special Investigations Unit and the Maraval Criminal
Investigations Department conducted an intelligence-led exercise in the Western
Division on Monday 4 April 2022. [@1] The exercise ran between 5 pm and 8 pm.
[@1] During the exercise officers executed a search warrant at the Maraval home
of a 75-year-old female suspect. [@1] The woman who was in the house at the time
was arrested in connection with the find. [@1] The suspect was 75 years old.
[@1]

The search of the premises resulted in the seizure of 12 brown packages
containing 7.7 kilogrammes of marijuana, which officers found hidden inside a
barrel. [@1] Police put the estimated street value of the seized drug at
TT$103,950. [@1]

The Trinidad and Tobago Police Service said its various operational units
continue to engage in anti-crime exercises. [@1] It said the aim is removing
illegal guns and drugs off the streets and out of the hands of criminal
elements. [@1] Investigations into all the matters were continuing. [@1]
