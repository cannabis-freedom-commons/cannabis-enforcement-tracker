A 27-year-old man was arrested at the Jeppes Reef border post for allegedly
trafficking drugs after attempting to smuggle dagga wrapped around his belly
from Eswatini. [@1] Officers on Easter weekend border patrols spotted the man
using an illegal crossing near Jeppes Reef to enter South Africa at about
12.10pm. [@1] Mpumalanga police said the 27-year-old was intercepted on Monday.
[@1]

A search found dagga wrapped around his body weighing about 1.9 kg. [@1] Police
put the estimated street value of the dagga at about R3 000. [@1] He was
arrested and charged with possession of dagga and was expected to appear before
the Nkomazi Magistrate’s Court on Tuesday, 19 April 2022. [@1]

Mpumalanga provincial police commissioner Lieutenant General Semakaleng Daphney
Manamela commended the border policing unit that intercepted him. [@1] Manamela
said police remain vigilant to prevent criminal activity along the borders. [@1]
