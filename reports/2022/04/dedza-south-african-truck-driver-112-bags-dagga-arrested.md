A South African truck driver, Trust Hlengeni Masingi, was arrested in Dedza,
Malawi for being found in possession of 112 bags of suspected indian hemp
(cannabis). [@1] The 42-year-old Masingi carried the 112 bags of chamba, each in
50-kilogram bags, along with rice husks in his 30-tonne Scania truck. [@1] The
seized bags of marijuana were kept at Dedza police station as police prepared to
take the suspect to court. [@1] The bags were described as 112 of the illegal
drug weighing 50 kilograms each, with a further 30 bags of rice husks in the
30-tonne truck. [@2] Masingi comes from the Denniottin area in Limpopo, South
Africa. [@2]

The charge of being found with chamba usually attracts a fine of about K200,000,
or 12 months in prison if a convict fails to pay the fine. [@2]
