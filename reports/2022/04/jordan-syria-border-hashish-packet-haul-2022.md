Since the start of 2022, the Jordanian army has intercepted more than 17,000
packets of hashish on the Syrian border. [@1] In the same period the army
intercepted 17 million Captagon pills, a figure that is not a cannabis quantity.
[@1]

Jordan is largely a transit route for the region's drug trade, with the Gulf
states, particularly Saudi Arabia, the biggest market. [@1] Skirmishes between
the Jordanian military and drug traffickers were becoming more frequent, with
larger hauls being made, and a colonel estimated about 160 armed groups operate
in southern Syria. [@1] The killing of a Jordanian soldier by smugglers in
January 2022 prompted the army to adopt a shoot-to-kill policy in its rules of
engagement. [@1]
