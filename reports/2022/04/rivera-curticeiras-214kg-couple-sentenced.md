Two Paraguayan citizens, a 35-year-old man at the wheel and a 19-year-old woman,
were detained in Rivera with 214 kilograms of marijuana hidden in their pickup
truck. [@1] At about 23:00 on Sunday 3 April 2022 they entered the country at
the integrated migration control area and drew the suspicion of border officers.
[@1] At the Curticeiras checkpoint about five kilometres from the centre of
Rivera, the Rivera departmental anti-drug brigade questioned the pair while
searching the vehicle. [@1]

Officers found the marijuana packed in bricks hidden in compartments of the
truck's bodywork, and the final weighing was 214 kilograms and 680 grams. [@2]
The Rivera police chief said officers found 366 bricks of marijuana in hidden
compartments of a Paraguayan-registered Volkswagen Amarok. [@4] Telenoche put
the load's value at about three million Uruguayan pesos. [@1] Police also seized
100 US dollars, 345 reais and two mobile phones. [@2] Police said the marijuana
had set off from Paraguay, entered Brazil at the triple border and was bound for
Montevideo. [@1]

On 5 April the Rivera courts sentenced the man to three years in prison for
transporting and importing narcotics. [@3] The woman was sentenced to 12 months
in prison for assisting criminal activity. [@3]
