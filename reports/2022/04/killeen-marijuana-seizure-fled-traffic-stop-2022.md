On Tuesday, April 5, 2022, Killeen police seized more than 681 grams of
marijuana after a vehicle fled an attempted traffic stop near the intersection
of Central Texas Expressway and Willow Springs Road. [@1] The vehicle, a white
2016 Hyundai Sonata driven by Daisy Arteaga Solis with Joshua Bernard Jones as
passenger, stopped briefly before fleeing the stop. [@1]

Officers found two large clear plastic bags holding a green leafy substance in
the road, and an officer smelled the odor of marijuana and saw residue scattered
throughout the passenger side floorboard and front passenger seat. [@1] Police
held Jones at gunpoint when he reached into the passenger side window, and
driver Solis got out of the vehicle and was ordered away. [@1]

The suspected substance tested positive for THC and weighed 681.6 grams, and was
sent to the Texas Department of Public Safety lab for further testing. [@1]
Justice of the Peace Gregory Johnson arraigned Jones on a charge of possession
of marijuana 5 pounds or less but more than 4 ounces, setting his bond at
$40,000, and arraigned Solis on evading arrest or detention with a vehicle,
setting her bond at $30,000. [@1]
