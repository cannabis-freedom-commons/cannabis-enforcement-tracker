Spanish police discovered a cannabis plantation of about 415,000 plants in the
northern region of Navarre and announced the find on Wednesday 13 April
2022. [@1] The plantation spread across 11 fields with a combined area of 67
hectares (166 acres), which police called Europe's biggest cannabis
plantation. [@1,2] The growers had presented the farm as a legal
industrial-hemp operation, but police found it was growing cannabis for drug
trafficking and processing into cannabidiol (CBD) products. [@1,2]

About 50 tonnes of the plants were being dried in a warehouse for processing
into CBD, and the owner had planned to export large quantities to Italy and
Switzerland, the Guardia Civil said. [@2] In their dried state the plants
would have had a black-market value of about 30 million euros, rising to up
to 100 million US dollars if processed into CBD or other derivatives,
officers said. [@1,2] The investigation began in mid-2021 when police
discovered one of the fields. [@2]

Three people were arrested, according to Reuters, while The Nomad Today
reported five arrests. [@1,2] The Foral Police of Navarre and the Guardia
Civil carried out the operation jointly. [@1]
