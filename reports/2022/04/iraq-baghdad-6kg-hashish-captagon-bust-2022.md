Ten people were arrested in the two cases, the national security agency said.
[@1] One of the rings was broken up when an Arab national was arrested “in
possession of six kilos (13 pounds) of hashish” and two accomplices were
detained. [@1] Al Jazeera's account said three men were arrested with six
kilogrammes (13 pounds) of hashish. [@2] All 10 accused “admitted to links with
international drug trafficking networks,” the agency said. [@1]

In the other case, Iraqi forces seized “around 6.2 million pills” from a
warehouse in the southwest of the capital. [@1] The statement said the drugs
were set for distribution “in areas of Baghdad and other provinces.” [@1] Three
Iraqis and four suspects from other Arab countries were arrested in connection
with the trafficking network. [@1]

Drug trafficking convictions can be punishable by the death penalty in Iraq.
[@3] In the first three months of the year, Iraqi security forces detained 18
suspected drug traffickers in the largely desert province of Anbar, which shares
a long border with Syria. [@1] More than three million captagon pills were
seized in the same period. [@1]
