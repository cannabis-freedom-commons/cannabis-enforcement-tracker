A Florida bride, 42, and a caterer, 31, were arrested on accusations of serving
food containing marijuana to unsuspecting guests at a February wedding. [@1] The
two women were arrested on alleged violations of an anti-tampering law and
delivery of cannabis, according to the Seminole County Sheriff's Department and
court records. [@1] The pair were identified as Danya Shea Svoboda, 42, and
caterer Jocelyn Montrinice Bryant, 31, facing alleged anti-tampering, delivery
of marijuana and culpable negligence counts. [@3]

County firefighters and deputies responded to the February 19 event at a
clubhouse in Longwood after guests reported getting sick or feeling strange.
[@1] Several reception guests reported feeling extremely ill and high, with some
vomiting and asking to be taken to hospital. [@2] Some people felt so ill that
they went to hospital, where they tested positive for THC, the psychoactive
compound in marijuana. [@1] Guests said they felt heavily drugged although they
had not been warned, with one guest saying he had trouble operating a cellphone
and another saying she thought she might die. [@1]

A guest who felt stoned on the wedding day said she asked the bride whether
cannabis was in the food and the bride said yes and smiled. [@1] Officers
collected several containers of food and dishes at the scene, and a piece of
bread and a portion of what appeared to be lasagna later tested positive for
THC. [@2] The bride and caterer face possible penalties of up to five years in
prison and a $500 fine each if convicted, according to the Seminole County
Sheriff's Office. [@2]

At the time of the NBC report, a charging decision was still under consideration
by the state prosecutor's office. [@1] The wedding occurred on 19 February in
the city of Longwood in central Florida, about 12 miles northeast of Orlando.
[@4] Medical cannabis is legal in Florida, but recreational use of the drug is
not. [@4]
