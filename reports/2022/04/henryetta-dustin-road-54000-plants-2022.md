On Tuesday 26 April 2022, agents and officers from the Oklahoma Bureau of
Narcotics, the FBI, the DEA, the District 25 Drug Task Force, the Creek Nation
Lighthorse Police and the Oklahoma City Police Department served a search
warrant on a marijuana-growing operation on Dustin Road in Henryetta, in a
multi-agency investigation into criminal marijuana trafficking. [@1,2] The
bureau said 54,000 plants were seized and 28 workers were detained and
interviewed. [@1,2]

Bureau spokesman Mark Woodward said evidence showed large loads of marijuana
being moved off the property and trafficked out of state onto the illicit
market. [@1,2] He said the raid potentially took at least 81 million dollars
in drugs off the streets, and warned that operators hiding behind a licence
would be pursued aggressively. [@1] No one had been arrested when the reports
were published and the investigation was continuing. [@1,2]
