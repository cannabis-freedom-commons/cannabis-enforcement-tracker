Sayre Borough Police arrested Lea Pudiak, 26, of Sayre, and Alexandra Goodrich,
28, of San Diego, after a package of about 100 THC vape cartridges was delivered
to an employee at a local business. [@1] Police alleged that the women had
shipped THC vape cartridges from San Diego to Sayre multiple times. [@1] The
restaurant owner called police because he was upset that Pudiak had the package
delivered to his business address, according to the complaint. [@1] Affidavits
listed the cartridges at a street value of $50,000 because of the variety of
strains. [@1]

Police later went to Pudiak's apartment to meet with her and Goodrich. [@1]
Pudiak reportedly told police she did not know why officers were there despite
an officer telling her that a box of drugs had been delivered to the address.
[@1] As Pudiak was being taken into custody, Goodrich told police that she was
from California and that her parents lived in Owego. [@1] The complaints said
UPS could not deliver to her parents' address, so Goodrich bought the vapes
legally in bulk in California and shipped them to Pudiak. [@1] The affidavits
said Goodrich allegedly paid Pudiak a small fee through CashApp to collect the
vapes while Goodrich used one every day for personal use. [@1]

Goodrich told police that people in California approached her and paid her in
cash to deliver “stuff” to other people. [@1] When asked specifically about
previous THC-vape shipments to Pudiak, Goodrich declined to give further
information and said she needed a lawyer. [@1] Pudiak and Goodrich were each
charged with one count of manufacture of a controlled substance, possession of a
controlled substance and criminal use of a communication facility. [@1]
