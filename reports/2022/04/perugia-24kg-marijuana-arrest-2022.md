Officers of the Provincial Command of the Guardia di Finanza in Perugia, Italy's
finance police, arrested a citizen of Albanian origin. [@1] The arrest was made
for possession of 24 kilograms of marijuana. [@1]

Officers of the same Provincial Command seized 24 kilograms of marijuana in
Perugia in the operation that led to the arrest. [@1] The drug involved was
reported as marijuana, with the quantity given as 24 kilograms. [@2] The person
taken into custody was described as a citizen of Albanian origin. [@2]
