A Windhoek resident was arrested after police discovered a dagga plantation at
Kleine Kuppe, a residential area south of the city centre. [@1] The police said
the plantation held 5,5 kilograms of cannabis. [@1]

Police valued the cannabis at N$277 000. [@1] The suspect was charged with
planting cannabis (skunk), dealing in cannabis and racketeering. [@1]

He was held at the Windhoek Police Station and was to appear in court the
following Tuesday. [@1] Africa-Press republished the same account in its Namibia
news section on 30 April 2022. [@2]
