On Sunday 24 April 2022, officers of the Bouaké Drugs and Narcotics Police, the
Ivorian police's specialist anti-drug branch, seized nine blocks of cannabis in
the Zone quarter of Bouaké. [@1] Officers first stopped two dealers who were
found holding cannabis already packaged and conditioned for sale. [@1]
Information provided by the two dealers led officers to their supplier, who was
then arrested at his home. [@1] In total three people were arrested, listed as
BM aged 19, MY aged 18 and KO aged 21. [@2]

Bouaké is a city in central Côte d’Ivoire, about 350 km from Abidjan. [@1]
Ivorian police presented the operation as another success in its campaign
against drug trafficking. [@1] Police announced the seizure in a communiqué on
Monday. [@1]

Those arrested were to be brought before a judge to answer for their acts. [@1]
Police described fighting drug trafficking as a top priority, pursued inside the
country as well as through reinforced maritime and airport controls. [@1]
