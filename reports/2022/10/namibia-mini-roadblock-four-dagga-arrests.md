Four men were arrested for alleged dagga smuggling after police stopped them at
a mini roadblock in Namibia. [@1] The four men arrested in the roadblock
operation were aged 24, 30, 36 and 47. [@1] At the time they were stopped, the
four men were travelling together in a silver Nissan Note. [@1]

Officers searching the silver Nissan Note discovered the drugs hidden under the
bonnet of the car. [@1] The account of the discovery was attributed to a police
crime report, which put the value of the drugs at N$300,000. [@1] The seized
drugs were reported as dagga. [@1] The operation was described as a mini
roadblock stop that led directly to the four arrests and the seizure. [@1]

Police investigations into the case were continuing after the arrests, with
officers still pursuing the matter. [@1] The four men were expected to appear in
court soon, although no charges or hearing outcome were reported. [@1]
