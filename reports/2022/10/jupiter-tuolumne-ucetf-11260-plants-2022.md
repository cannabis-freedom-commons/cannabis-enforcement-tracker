Governor Gavin Newsom's Unified Cannabis Enforcement Task Force, created in
June to coordinate the state's fight against unlicensed grows, carried out its
first major operation in a rural area of Jupiter in Tuolumne County on 4
October 2022. [@1,2] Led by the Department of Cannabis Control's Law
Enforcement Division and the California Department of Fish and Wildlife,
officers served nine search warrants on unlicensed outdoor cultivation
operations. [@1,2]

Officers eradicated 11,260 illegal cannabis plants and destroyed 5,237 pounds
(about 2,375.5 kg) of processed cannabis flower, worth an estimated retail
value of more than 15 million dollars according to the cannabis control
department. [@1,2] Newsom said the task force would shut down illegal grow
sites and pursue criminal organisations undercutting the legal market. [@1]
