On 10 October 2022, the crew of the U.S. Coast Guard fast response cutter Glen
Harris seized drugs from a fishing vessel in the Gulf of Oman. [@1] The vessel
was in international waters. [@1] Glen Harris was operating as part of the
Saudi-led Combined Task Force 150, one of four task forces under the Combined
Maritime Forces. [@1]

The seizure totalled 11,023 pounds of hashish, about 5,000 kg, which is the
cannabis component recorded here. [@1] The same boarding also recovered about
1,764 pounds of methamphetamine, which is not counted in the cannabis weight.
[@1] U.S. Naval Forces Central Command and the U.S. 5th Fleet estimated the
combined drugs seized in the operation at $48 million. [@1]

Combined Task Force 150 conducts maritime security operations in the gulfs of
Oman and Aden, the Red Sea and the Indian Ocean. [@1] The seizure brought the
international maritime task force's total drug busts so far that year to more
than $300 million. [@1]
