On Saturday 1 October 2022, officers from the Tuasivi police substation on
Savai'i responded to a call about a marijuana plantation at Salelavalu. [@1]

A shooter opened fire on the officers with a 12-gauge shotgun, injuring two of
them. [@1] Both officers received only minor injuries, were taken to Tuasivi
Hospital and were released after treatment. [@1]

Police named three suspects, Manuia Lafaele, Masina Lega Taulelei and Apisaloma
Setu, and appealed for public help to find them, saying they were wanted as a
result of the Salelavalu incident. [@1]
