A cannabis dealer who was caught growing cannabis in his loft was sentenced at
Douglas Courthouse. [@1] Police raided his house on Archallagan Terrace in
Foxdale on 19 August 2021. [@2] Police also found evidence on his phone of
supplying the drug since 2018. [@1] He was handed an 18-month sentence suspended
for two years. [@1] He pleaded guilty to cultivation of cannabis, being
concerned in the supply of the drug, possession with intent to supply, and
possession of criminal property. [@1]

He was found to have £5,000 of the drug in jars at his home as well as 16 young
plants and saplings. [@1] Christian had blacked out the windows in the loft of
his home and installed an air filtration unit and a hydroponic system to
cultivate the plants. [@1] When mature the plants would have had a street value
of between £13,000 and £40,000, prosecutors said. [@1] Messages on his phone
showed evidence of drug sales and bank transfers of more than £23,000. [@1]
After being arrested Christian claimed half of the drugs were for his personal
use and only £11,000 of the money transferred to his account was payment for
drugs sold to friends. [@1]

Deemster Graeme Cook said he was prepared to suspend the sentence due to his
"clear remorse" and the fact his children were financially reliant on him. [@1]
Christian's defence advocate said he accepted the offences were serious but he
was now distancing himself from cannabis. [@1]
