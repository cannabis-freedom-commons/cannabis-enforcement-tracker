A police sergeant was arrested after he allegedly gave an envelope containing
cannabis to an offender at the Windhoek Correctional Facility when the offender
was brought to court. [@3] He was allegedly paid N$50 to deliver an envelope
containing cannabis to an offender at the Windhoek Correctional Facility. [@2]
He was to be formally charged on 13 October 2022. [@2]

Warrant officer Silas Shipandeni, the Khomas police spokesperson, confirmed the
incident and said the police officer would be formally charged that day. [@1] An
unknown person allegedly bribed the officer with N$50 to deliver the drugs to
the offender. [@1] The arrest was over a dagga envelope, the name used for the
cannabis in that account. [@1]
