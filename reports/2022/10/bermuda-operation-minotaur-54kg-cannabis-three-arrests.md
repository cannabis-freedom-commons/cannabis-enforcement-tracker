Bermuda Police Service officers and HM Customs officers carried out a joint
operation called Operation Minotaur on Monday, 24 October 2022. [@1] HM Customs
officers intercepted a shipment found to contain approximately 120 pounds of
cannabis. [@1] The Bermuda Police Service Drugs Unit was called in after the
interception. [@1] Warrants were executed at a number of locations in both
Pembroke and St George's. [@1] Three suspects were arrested in connection with
the matter. [@1]

Acting Detective Superintendent Sherwin Joseph of the Crime Division said the
cannabis had an estimated street value in excess of five million dollars. [@1]
Joseph called the seizure a major success in the combined effort by both
agencies to combat drug importation at Bermuda's borders. [@1] Police said the
Drugs Unit was continuing to investigate and that no further information would
be provided at that time. [@1] The Royal Gazette reported that the raids
followed the seizure of the shipment and led to the three arrests. [@2] The
Royal Gazette said the investigation into the drug seizure was still ongoing.
[@2]
