Police seized high-powered rifles, pistols, ammunition and marijuana in a
Central bond. [@1] Between 2:30pm and 8:15pm on Friday 14 October 2022, Central
Division officers with Customs and Excise Division officers searched a bond in
the Central Division on intelligence information. [@2] Officers said they
intercepted an international crime network, seizing a large quantity of
firearms, ammunition and narcotics. [@2] Police said months of meticulous
intelligence gathering and surveillance by a dedicated TTPS intelligence-unit
team produced the seizures. [@2] Acting Commissioner McDonald Jacob praised TTPS
and Customs officers, saying the high-powered weapons were stopped before
reaching criminals. [@2]

The search recovered 40 boxes of 7.62 ammunition. [@2] Officers also seized
several firearm component parts. [@2] Seven cans in one barrel held 3.7 kg of
marijuana hidden among grocery items. [@2] Six cans in a second barrel held 5.2
kg of marijuana among grocery items. [@2] Across the exercise, the team seized
16 high-powered rifles, five pistols, 22 rifle magazines, two pistol magazines
and several thousand rounds of 7.62 ammunition. [@2]

When weighed, the marijuana totalled 8.9 kilograms, with an estimated street
value of $2,670,000. [@2] Investigations are continuing. [@2]
