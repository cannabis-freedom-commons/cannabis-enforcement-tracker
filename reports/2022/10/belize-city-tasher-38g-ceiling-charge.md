A 37-year-old Belize City woman appeared in the Belize City Magistrate's Court
on October 27, 2022, charged with a single count of possession of a controlled
drug. [@1] She pleaded not guilty to the charge. [@1] Bail was granted at $300
plus one surety of the same amount, with no objection from the prosecution. [@1]
She is due back in court on December 20. [@1]

Police searched her home early that morning and found 38 grams of marijuana
hidden in her ceiling. [@1] She is out on bail after the charge, and the account
names no other suspect. [@1]
