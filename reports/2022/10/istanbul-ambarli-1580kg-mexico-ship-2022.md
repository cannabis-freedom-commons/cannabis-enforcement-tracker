Istanbul narcotics police seized 1,580 kilograms of marijuana from a container on a ship that had sailed from Mexico, at Ambarlı Port. [@1] The bags of marijuana were found stashed between bolts of fabric in the container. [@1]

Police acted after a two-month surveillance of the suspects, following a tip-off that a large shipment of marijuana would be sent from Mexico to Turkey. [@1] Five people were detained in connection with the shipment. [@1]
