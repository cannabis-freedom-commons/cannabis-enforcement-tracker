Cambria County District Attorney Gregory Neugebauer announced that a Johnstown
man was jailed on Thursday, 27 October 2022, after drug agents raided a home on
Cypress Avenue and seized marijuana, cocaine and cash. [@2] Task force members
and agents from the Pennsylvania Attorney General's office searched 755 Cypress
Ave., where they took 42-year-old Tarik Hobbs into custody. [@2] The Cambria
County Drug Task Force charged Hobbs with three counts each of possession with
intent to deliver a controlled substance and possession of a controlled
substance, and one count of criminal use of a communication facility. [@2] He
was held at Cambria County Prison on $150,000 bail, court records showed. [@1]

The search turned up 949.5 grams of marijuana, which prosecutors valued at
$2,000 on the street. [@2] The raid also produced $1,351 in cash. [@2]

Neugebauer said the task force and participating agencies would keep taking
"aggressive action" against people who "pollute our streets with drugs", and
tied the arrest to Red Ribbon Week, the national drug-prevention week. [@2]
