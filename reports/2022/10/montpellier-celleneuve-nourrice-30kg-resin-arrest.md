Montpellier's narcotics squad arrested a 35-year-old Moroccan man on 11 October
2022 after spotting him dealing drugs on the terrace of a bar on the place
Renaudel in the Celleneuve district. [@1]

The inquiry began with a tip-off about a cannabis and cocaine trade in the
historic heart of Celleneuve, naming a suspect who worked from the place Mansart
out of a "nourrice" flat, a store flat used to hold drugs and the traffickers'
supplies. [@1] Police kept the suspect under surveillance before stopping him,
and at his home they seized 3,000 euros in cash. [@1] In the store flat, opened
with support from the departmental public security directorate's forced-entry
team after the dog squad identified it, officers found 30 kg of cannabis resin,
50 g of cocaine, 13,200 euros in cash and two handguns, one a 7.65-calibre
automatic with a loaded magazine hidden in a printer. [@1]

The suspect, a 35-year-old Moroccan national, was questioned in garde à vue,
French police custody, and should be brought before the courts. [@1] The case
was opened by Montpellier's narcotics squad on a tip-off about drug trafficking
in the historic heart of Celleneuve, a district of the city in Hérault. [@1]
