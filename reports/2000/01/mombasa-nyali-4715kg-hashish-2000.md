On 26 January 2000, police recovered yellow sacks stored in a bedroom of a rented house on Links
Road, Nyali, acting on information Mohamed Ghani Taib had brought to officers two days earlier
about contraband from the port. [@1] The sacks were taken to Bamburi Police Station, counted and
sealed, and government analysts confirmed the contents were cannabis resin, commonly known as
hashish, weighing 4,715 kilograms in total. [@1]

Taib, who said he was a police informer following a tip about stolen port goods, was convicted of
trafficking the stash and sentenced to 15 years in prison, and the High Court dismissed his appeal
on 5 January 2007. [@1] The International Narcotics Control Board lists the seizure as one of two
Mombasa consignments totalling over six tons taken in January and March 2000. [@2]
