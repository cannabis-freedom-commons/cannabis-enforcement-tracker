On 31 January 2000, United States authorities intercepted a marine container in Newark, New Jersey,
carrying 10,635 kilograms of hashish bound for Montreal, Quebec. [@1,2] A joint Canadian-American
assessment says the container was en route to Montreal and that the 10.6-tonne interception made up
almost all of the 10.8 tonnes of hashish seized in the United States in 2000. [@2]

## Montreal pipeline

Montreal-based criminal groups specialized in the large-scale smuggling of hashish and monopolized
its distribution, with multi-tonne shipments moved in marine containers. [@2] The RCMP seizure table
records the 31 January Newark interception alongside other Montreal-bound container seizures that
winter, including 10,208 kilograms detected at the Port of Montreal and 1,227 kilograms stopped in
Indian Head, Saskatchewan, en route to Montreal. [@1]
