Ventura County sheriff's deputies raided a ranch in Lockwood Valley on 4 August 2000,
seizing 342 marijuana plants grown by four members of the Los Angeles Cannabis Resource
Cooperative. [@1,3] Those arrested were activists Lynn and Judy Osburn with Mark Davison
and Carol Jo Papac, all of whom said they were medical marijuana users growing for the
cooperative's more than 800 patients; all four were released on bail on Friday night.
[@1,2,3] The cooperative's president said the marked garden held a significant share of
its annual supply and that patients were already suffering from the loss. [@1,3]

When the four came for arraignment in September, prosecutors had not decided whether to
charge them, and a deputy district attorney said further investigation was needed before
setting a new date of 25 October. [@2] The couples also lost computers and storage disks
holding a legal-defense manuscript kept at the ranch home. [@2] Deputies additionally
seized two pistols, two revolvers, a shotgun and a rifle belonging to Lynn Osburn. [@4]
Charges were never filed over the seizure, though federal prosecutors later built a case
on evidence gathered in this and two later raids at the farm. [@4]
