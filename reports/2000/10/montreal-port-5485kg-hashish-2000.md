Canadian authorities seized 5,485 kilograms of hashish at the Port of Montreal on 19 October
2000. [@1] The hashish had come from South Asia, transiting through South Africa, and the seizure
was a joint effort by customs with Montreal Urban Community police targeting the container. [@2]

A parliamentary committee heard the shipment moved on direct shipping routes that re-manifest South
Asian cargo through intermediary ports, and that traffickers paid port insiders a share of each
container's value to move it through customs. [@2]
