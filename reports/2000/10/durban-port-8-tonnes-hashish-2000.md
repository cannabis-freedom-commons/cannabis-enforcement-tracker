South African authorities seized 8 tonnes of hashish from a marine container in the port of Durban
on 19 October 2000, a shipment the RCMP records as destined for Montreal. [@1] The country's police
commissioner later credited cooperation with the United Nations drug-control programme for the
major hashish seizure at the port. [@2]
