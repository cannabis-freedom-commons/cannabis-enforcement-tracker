A steamship from Mexico unloaded a container of napkins and detergent at the Port of Charleston in
November 2000 with 171 packages holding 2,871 pounds of marijuana hidden inside boxes of powdered
soap. [@1] Customs inspectors found the load after spotting discrepancies in the shipping manifest,
repackaged the marijuana and had an aircraft follow the container as a truck carried it to
Greensboro, North Carolina. [@1]

Guilford County sheriff's deputies arrested five people there the following weekend and seized three
vehicles and $80,000 alongside the drugs. [@1,2] The marijuana was valued at $1.2 million. [@1]
