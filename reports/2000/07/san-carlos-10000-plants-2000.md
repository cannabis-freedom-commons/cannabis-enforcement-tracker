Police in Orange Walk District shut down what they called a major marijuana plantation near San
Carlos Village on the morning of Thursday 27 July 2000. [@1] Officers said they entered the area
between 5:00 and 7:00 a.m., found a field with about 10,000 cannabis plants four to five feet
tall, destroyed the crop and arrested Evalio Campos, a 28-year-old labourer from Indian Church
Village. [@1]

The account is a verbatim transcript of the station’s evening newscast and does not say whether
others were sought or how long the plantation had been there. [@1]
