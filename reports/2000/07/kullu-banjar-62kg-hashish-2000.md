Police and Narcotics Control Bureau (NCB) personnel seized 62 kg of hashish from Faryari More
village in the Banjar valley of Kullu district on 30 July 2000 after a decoy arranged a purchase
with the sellers. [@1] They arrested Khub Ram, the panchayat pradhan of Mashiar village, and Ram
Singh Tung. [@1]

NCB Zonal Director Rakesh Goel told the reporter that the hashish would cost almost Rs 100 million
on the international market. [@1] The same account describes Khub Ram’s arrest as part of a wider
pattern in which influential locals were involved in the trade. [@1] It does not report charges,
custody or sentences for the two men. [@1]
