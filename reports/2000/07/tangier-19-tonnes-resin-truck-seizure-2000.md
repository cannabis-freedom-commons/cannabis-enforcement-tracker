Moroccan customs officials at Tangier announced a record haul of 19 tonnes of cannabis stashed in the back of a truck, in the middle of the country's hashish export trade to Europe. [@1] The report estimated Morocco's cannabis crop earned the country over $2 billion a year, with European drug squads struggling to intercept some of the 2,000 tonnes of hashish it exported annually. [@1]

The government argued that demand, not supply, was the problem, pointing out that Europe itself was beginning to ease its bans on soft drugs. [@1] Official figures admitted the area under cultivation had grown more than fourfold in a decade, reaching an estimated 80,000 hectares behind a cover of maize along the Mediterranean coast. [@1]
