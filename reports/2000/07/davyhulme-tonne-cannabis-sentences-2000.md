Ian Johnson, 28, was jailed for eight years at Minshull Street Crown Court in
Manchester after admitting conspiracy to supply drugs. [@1] His brother-in-law
William Probert, 36, of Exeter Road in Davyhulme, admitted possessing
£600,000 worth of the cannabis with intent to supply and was jailed for four
years. [@1]

Johnson, a warehouseman living in Selby Drive, Lancaster, was arrested at
Probert's Davyhulme home after a surveillance operation tracked him driving
his works van the 60 miles from Lancaster toward Manchester. [@1] Officers
recovered 232 kg of cannabis packed in boxes at Probert's address and a
further 860 kg from a locked shed at Johnson's garage in Lancaster, a haul
weighing more than one metric tonne that the paper valued at £8 million and
said was destined for Manchester streets and nightspots. [@1] The paper said
Johnson was probably close to the smugglers who had imported the cannabis
into Britain. [@1]

Both men were ordered to declare their means ahead of a proceeds-of-crime
hearing later in the year to determine whether they must surrender financial
assets. [@1]
