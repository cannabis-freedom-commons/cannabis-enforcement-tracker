French customs officers found 1.5 tonnes (3,307 lb) of cannabis resin hidden among a
consignment of kitchen knives in a lorry waiting at the Coquelles terminal to board a
train for Britain. [@1] The discovery came on 28 July 2000 during random checks on
lorries arriving to join a Britain-bound train. [@1]

The cannabis came from Morocco, and officials estimated its street value at 50 million
francs (about $7 million). [@1] A judicial source said it was the largest drugs haul yet
made at Coquelles, France's only car and lorry terminal for Channel Tunnel trains. [@1]

Three French nationals were charged with drug trafficking over the seizure. [@1] News of
the haul was held back until 5 August so that police could work on cracking the drug ring
behind it. [@1]
