Saudi Arabia beheaded Saudi national Amer al-Roueili on 1 May 2000 in the northern province of al-Jouf. [@1] An Interior Ministry statement said he had been convicted of smuggling hashish and other drugs into the kingdom and of financing drug smugglers, giving no further details. [@1]

The execution, carried out with a sword in a public square, brought the number of beheadings in the kingdom that year to 25, after at least 99 people were executed the year before. [@1] Saudi Arabia imposes the death penalty for drug trafficking under its interpretation of Islamic law, and human rights groups say defendants are often denied access to lawyers and fair trials.
