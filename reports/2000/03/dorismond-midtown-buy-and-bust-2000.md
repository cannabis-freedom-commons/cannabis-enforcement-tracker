Two plainclothes New York police detectives approached Patrick Dorismond, a 26-year-old unarmed
black security guard, on 16 March 2000 and asked him to sell them "some weed" as part of a marijuana
"buy-and-bust" operation. [@1] Dorismond rebuffed the men, a scuffle ensued, and a third officer,
Anthony Vasquez, fired a single bullet into Dorismond's chest, killing him. [@1] No drugs or other
contraband were found on his body. [@1]

Dorismond, a Haitian immigrant, was shot in Midtown Manhattan after an undercover officer stopped
him and asked where to buy marijuana. [@2] Mayor Giuliani then directed that Dorismond's sealed
juvenile arrest record be released to justify the shooting, and a grand jury chose not to indict.
[@2]
