Spanish police seized 3.25 metric tonnes of hashish in two operations against drug trafficking across the Strait of Gibraltar, according to a 31 December 2018 wire report. [@1,2] The report put the number of arrests at 42. [@1,2]

## Boats seized, arrests on both shores

Five boats and five vehicles used by the alleged gang were seized. [@1] The arrests were made in Cádiz and Málaga on Spain's southern coast and in Ceuta, Spain's North African enclave east of Tangier. [@1] The account gave no details about the nationalities of those arrested or when the operations happened. [@1]
