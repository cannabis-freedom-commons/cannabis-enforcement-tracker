A 44-year-old man was arrested after police seized 3.2 kilograms of cannabis at
Nafanua Wharf on the Tongan island of 'Eua on 13 December 2018. [@4] He remained
in custody on 17 December, the commander of the 'Eua police station, Inspector
Loluhama Fine, confirmed. [@3] Police arrested the man with the cannabis at
3:50pm and suspected he intended to take it to Tongatapu on the ferry scheduled
to depart 'Eua at 4:30pm. [@4]

Inspector Fine confirmed that the seized cannabis weighed 3.2 kilograms, not the
2 kilograms first reported. [@4] The first account, published on 14 December,
said two kilograms of cannabis found in a bag at the wharf had been seized by
Tonga Police. [@1] It said a police patrol led to the seizure and a man was
taken in for questioning, but no arrest had been made. [@2] Police believed the
bag belonged to someone who was to bring it to Tongatapu, and said an
investigation continued. [@2]

“We continue to focus on capturing and prosecuting those responsible for the
supply of these destructive drugs that cause harm to our youths, families and
our community,” Inspector Fine said. [@4]
