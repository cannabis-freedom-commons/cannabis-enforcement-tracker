Wellington County Ontario Provincial Police arrested 41-year-old Kulbir Singh
Bains of Cambridge at the carpool lot on Lake Road at Townline Road in Puslinch
at about 10:45 p.m. on 19 December 2018. [@2] Officers who spoke to the driver
of the beige sedan immediately noticed a strong smell of marijuana inside it.
[@2]

Officers found marijuana bud over 30 grams, above the Cannabis Act limit, and
placed him under arrest. [@2] He resisted as officers tried to handcuff him by
pushing away, and they gained control and put him in the rear of the cruiser.
[@2] A search of the vehicle found a large quantity of cocaine, and no weight or
value for it was published. [@2]

Police charged Bains with possession of cocaine for the purpose of trafficking,
possession of cannabis for the purpose of selling, resisting arrest and driving
while under suspension. [@2] He was due to answer the charges in Guelph criminal
court on 22 January 2019. [@2] CTV Kitchener described the cannabis charge as
possession of cannabis for the purpose of trafficking. [@1]

CityNews Kitchener reported that the arrest was made just before 11 p.m. on the
Wednesday at the same carpool lot. [@3]

CityNews Kitchener also reported that police found 30 grams of marijuana in
contravention of the Cannabis Act. [@3]
