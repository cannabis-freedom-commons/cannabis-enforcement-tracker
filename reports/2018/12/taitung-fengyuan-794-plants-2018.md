A task force led by the Coast Guard Administration's Taitung investigation team, with shore patrol, police criminal investigators and Taitung prosecutors, raided a remote work shed in the Fengyuan area of Taitung City on 3 December 2018 and found a one-hectare “marijuana forest” behind it. [@1] Officers moved in as the grower, a man surnamed Zhang, was fertilising the crop and arrested him on the spot. [@1]

The task force seized 794 cannabis plants, dried leaves and drying equipment; at an estimated 300 grams of dried leaf per plant and NT$1,000 per gram, the ministry valued the haul at more than NT$230 million, calling it the largest cannabis manufacturing case in Taiwan's history by plant count, area and plant size. [@1]

The group had rented the farmland claiming to grow betel leaves, and the crop was approaching harvest when investigators closed in under the “Secure Homes” anti-drug program. [@1]
