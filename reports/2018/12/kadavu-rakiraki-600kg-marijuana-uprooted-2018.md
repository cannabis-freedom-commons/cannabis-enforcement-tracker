Police Commissioner Sitiveni Qiliho travelled to Kadavu on 24 December 2018 to
meet 11 officers deployed to Rakiraki for two weeks on a special drug operation.
[@2] Local community members guided the officers to the plantations and helped
uproot the plants and carry them down rough terrain. [@2] Fijivillage reported
that Qiliho said several arrests had been made when the evidence was transported
back on the Monday night. [@3] Two days later police said no one had yet been
charged and the owner of the farm had yet to be determined. [@4] Police said
investigations were continuing. [@2]

Police uprooted 600 kilograms of plants believed to be marijuana near Rakiraki
village in Kadavu during a two-week special drug operation. [@3] Police put the
estimated value of the seizure at $2.3 million. [@2] The uprooted plants were
transported back to Suva on the police vessel MV Veiqaravi on the Monday night.
[@2] Qiliho said the uprooted marijuana plants would be tested first before
being destroyed. [@1] He said police would have the material tested and then
apply for a court order to have it destroyed. [@1]

Qiliho said the police focus was not only on marijuana cultivation but also on
the drug market, because growers would keep planting only while buyers existed.
[@1] He warned people involved with drugs in any form that police would get to
them as the war on drugs continued. [@1]
