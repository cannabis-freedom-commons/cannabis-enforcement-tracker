Torin Jacob Williams, 20, was living in a home in the 11000 block of Day Camp
Lane in Austin when Round Rock narcotics and SWAT officers served a search
warrant around 5:30 a.m. on Thursday, 20 December 2018. [@1] Williams was
arrested and charged with felony manufacturing of a controlled substance. [@1]
Court documents showed police found 311 grams (0.311 kg) of THC oil in the home.
[@1] Some of the oil was in individual cartridges called “Mario karts.” [@1]

Neighbor Tina Nguyen said the police arrival woke her; officers used a megaphone
outside the house, and she recalled, “We saw a bunch of police, we were like
freaking out.” [@1] Another neighbor, James Griffin, said he found it surprising
that THC oil had been found in the neighborhood. [@1] Griffin said cars would
drive quickly into the cul-de-sac and turn around before driving out, a pattern
he considered unusual. [@1] Round Rock Police said they were noticing THC oil
more often than before. [@1]
