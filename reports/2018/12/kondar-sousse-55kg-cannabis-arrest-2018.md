Judicial police officers on Monday 17 December 2018 arrested a 32-year-old man
in the Kondar area of Sousse Governorate on suspicion of drug trafficking and
smuggling, seizing 55 kg of cannabis and 171,000 Tunisian dinars. [@1] They
stopped him in a van in which he had hidden 45 kg of cannabis. [@1] The man was
already wanted under five arrest warrants for smuggling and drug trafficking and
had been sentenced in absentia to 20 years in prison. [@1]

A search of the suspect's home found 171,000 dinars, four cars, one motorcycle,
27 drug pills, seven different identity cards, two driving licences, 12 SIM
cards, a banknote counter, two electronic scales and several purchase contracts
for property in his name. [@1] Police put the total value of the seizure at 1
million Tunisian dinars. [@1]

The public prosecutor's office placed the suspect in pre-trial detention (garde
à vue) for the investigation, and police were still looking for his accomplices.
[@1]
