Belgian customs intercepted a container holding 7.4 tonnes of cannabis in the
port of Antwerp in late December 2018. [@1] The discovery was made on 20
December in the Waasland part of the port, but it was only made public in
January 2019. [@2] On that day officers inspected quay 1742 and found a
container filled with bricks and drugs that had come from Laos. [@2]

Belgium's finance ministry confirmed the seizure and described it as one of the
largest cannabis seizures in the port area in recent years. [@1] The
confirmation came from the Federal Public Service Finance. [@2] Authorities
estimated the street value of the cannabis at 59 million euros. [@2]

Drug seizures are frequent in the port of Antwerp, especially cocaine. [@1] The
seizure was therefore described as quite unusual because large quantities found
there are generally cocaine. [@2] In early 2018 customs had seized a record
7,000 kilos of cocaine hidden in a container of bananas in the same port. [@1]
