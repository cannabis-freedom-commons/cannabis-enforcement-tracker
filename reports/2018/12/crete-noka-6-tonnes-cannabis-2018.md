The Hellenic Coast Guard intercepted the Syrian-flagged freighter Noka off
the southeastern coast of Crete on 5 December 2018. [@1] The ship, with a
crew of 11, was sailing from the Syrian port of Latakia to Benghazi in
Libya. [@1,2] Officers found about six tonnes of processed cannabis hidden
under false floors in eight containers, packed between layers of coffee,
spices and wood shavings. [@1,2]

The same search also uncovered about three million Captagon amphetamine
pills alongside the cannabis. [@1,2] The Noka was escorted to Heraklion port
on 8 December, where officers unloaded its cargo for a full search. [@1,3]
The vessel and its contents were impounded, and the crew were arrested and
taken before a public prosecutor. [@1,2] The coast guard released video of
its underwater-missions officers boarding the vessel while under way. [@3]
