Merced County deputies, with federal and state agencies, served a search warrant
on the morning of Monday 5 November 2018 on a farm near Dos Palos. [@1] The farm
sits along Hutchins Road and Palm Avenue west of Dos Palos. [@1] Federal
prosecutors and the sheriff described the grow as one of the largest law
enforcement had seen in the North Valley, with the sheriff's office calling it
the largest ever found in the Central Valley. [@2]

More than 540,000 cannabis plants were removed from the property. [@1] The
plants covered nearly 200 acres of the farm. [@1] The property owner had told
investigators he planted 194 acres of hemp in two fields and had about 500,000
plants growing there. [@2] The sheriff said the rancher believed he was going to
grow hemp and that the county had warned him not to. [@1]

Four people caught raiding the fields during the week of surveillance were taken
into custody, and officers recovered two guns. [@1] On 31 October 2018 a deputy
stopped a car leaving the property with 50 pounds of marijuana, and on 1
November 2018 deputies stopped a van carrying 220 plants. [@2] All four people
inside the van carrying 220 plants were arrested on state felony charges. [@2]
After an anonymous tip in August 2018, a sheriff's sergeant visited the property
and saw what he recognised as live marijuana plants. [@2]

The county prohibits outdoor cannabis cultivation and allows six plants grown
indoors. [@1] The sheriff said the size of the grow was why his office brought
in the federal government. [@1] The sheriff's office said the case remained
under investigation. [@2]
