Prison officers at the Maximum Security Prison in Arouca seized four apples
stuffed with four packets of marijuana weighing 88 grams on the afternoon of 21
November 2018. [@1] A 22-year-old man from Kelly Village, Caroni, was arrested
and handed over to Arouca police. [@1]

Around 1 pm a prison officer scanning visitors noticed a visitor carrying a bag
of fruit, saw that the four apples were unusually large, cut them open and found
a packet of marijuana in each. [@1] The suspect was arrested after attempting to
smuggle the apples into the prison. [@1] The week before, officers had arrested
a man who tried to smuggle marijuana stuffed inside grapefruits. [@1]

Prisons Commissioner Gerard Wilson warned visitors and rogue officers that
surveillance at the prisons had been increased, saying visitors keep trying to
bring illegal items in. [@1] Wilson said the fruit concealment may be a new
trend, and that the surveillance team had been monitoring officers who may have
been involved in trafficking. [@1] He said people were coming up with creative
ways to smuggle drugs and contraband into the prison but that this would not be
tolerated. [@1]
