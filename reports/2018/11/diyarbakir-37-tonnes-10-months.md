Diyarbakır Governor Hasan Basri Güzeloğlu announced the results at a meeting of
the provincial addiction commission on 8 November 2018. [@1] He said 2,110
operations in Diyarbakır in the first ten months of 2018 caught 2,374 suspects,
of whom 460 were remanded in pre-trial detention. [@2]

The operations seized 37,293.994 kg of esrar, the cannabis preparation known in
Turkey, including 14,525.784 kg of kubar (bud) and 22,768.209 kg of toz
(powdered cannabis). [@1] Officers also seized 3,016,747 hemp plants. [@1] The
same operations yielded 89 kg 16 g of heroin, 539 g of cocaine, 9 kg 147 g of
methamphetamine and 40,122 synthetic pills. [@2] Weapons, ammunition and cash
believed to be used in the drug trade were also confiscated, the governor said.
[@1]

Güzeloğlu linked the trade to the PKK, saying terrorist organisations took most
of their income from drug smuggling. [@1] He said the fight against drugs
continued alongside the fight against militancy. [@1] He noted that the National
Board for Combating Addiction had been set up under a presidential directive
published in the Official Gazette, and that state institutions and civil society
organisations were working on the issue. [@1]
