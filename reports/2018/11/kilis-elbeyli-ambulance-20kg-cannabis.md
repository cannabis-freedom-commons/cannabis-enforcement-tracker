Customs officers stopped the private ambulance with plate 01 CGT 37, driven by
Y.Y., on suspicion as it returned to Turkey through Çobanbey Customs Gate after
leaving a Syrian patient in Syria. [@1] Officers found 20 kilograms of cannabis
hidden under the stretcher and detained the driver and N.Ç. who was travelling
with him. [@1] Acting on their statements, Çobanbey anti-smuggling teams caught
M.H. in Elbeyli district as the person due to receive the cannabis in Turkey.
[@1]

After questioning was completed, all three suspects were remanded in custody by
the judicial authorities. [@3] The search took place on 13 November 2018 as the
ambulance returned from taking a discharged Syrian patient from a Gaziantep
private hospital to Syria. [@3] The Kilis Chief Public Prosecutor's Office had
the private-hospital ambulance seized under a court order. [@3]

The Gaziantep Provincial Health Directorate said the ambulance was not owned by
the Health Ministry and announced that it had opened an investigation. [@2] A
later account described the haul as 22 kilograms. [@3]
