Fremont County sheriff's deputies stopped a 2017 Chevy Coachmen motor home on
Interstate 29 in southwest Iowa early on Friday, 9 November 2018, for several
moving violations. [@1] A sheriff's K-9 indicated drugs were inside the vehicle,
and a search found about 300 pounds (136.08 kg) of what deputies called
high-grade marijuana, police said. [@1] The sheriff's office put the street
value of the load at $2,880,000. [@2]

Deputies arrested three men from the east coast, who were charged with
possession of a controlled substance with intent to deliver more than 100
kilograms. [@1] WTKR reported that the charge is a Class B felony and that one
of the three, 44-year-old Elwood Perry Jr. of Chesapeake, Virginia, was formerly
a Chesapeake police officer from 2000 to 2011. [@4] The men were being held in
the Fremont County Jail, and a TV station reported they were held without bond
pending an initial appearance in court. [@1]

KCRG reported that the motor home was stopped at about 2:40 a.m. near the 4-mile
marker of southbound Interstate 29. [@3] The Iowa Division of Narcotic
Enforcement assisted the sheriff's office in the stop and seizure. [@1]
