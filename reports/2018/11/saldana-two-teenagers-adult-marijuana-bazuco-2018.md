Tolima department police detained two 16-year-old boys during a surveillance
patrol in the vereda La Esperanza, in the municipality of Saldaña, Tolima, and
then arrested an adult. [@1] The three were handed to the local 39 prosecutor's
office in the neighbouring municipality of Coyaima and were to answer on charges
of trafficking, manufacturing or carrying narcotics. [@1]

A search of one of the detainees found about 80 doses of marijuana and 10 doses
of bazuco, the cocaine base sold in small doses in Colombia. [@1] Police said
the detainees would be brought before the courts. [@1]
