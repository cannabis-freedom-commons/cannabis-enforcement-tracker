Guyana's police force and the Customs Anti-Narcotics Unit (Canu) burnt 994 kg of
cannabis on the Linden/Soesdyke Highway on Saturday 10 November 2018. [@2] The
cannabis went into the fire alongside 477 kg of cocaine, a combined street value
the authorities put at GYD $513,310,940. [@2]

Police Headquarters said the exercise took place at Yarrowkabra, with
representatives of the Ministry of Public Security, the National Anti-Narcotics
Agency (Nana) and the Guyana Forensic Science Laboratory (GFSL) witnessing it.
[@1] The police force handed over 132 kg of cocaine and 440 kg of cannabis, and
Canu 345 kg of cocaine and 554 kg of cannabis. [@1] Members of the GFSL tested
the drugs before they were burnt by fire. [@1]

Both agencies were disposing of narcotics held as evidence in cases that had
reached finality in the courts, and no arrests were reported at the burning.
[@2] The exercise was a joint disposal by the police force and Canu at the same
Yarrowkabra site where the agencies destroy narcotics taken out of evidence
storage. [@1]
