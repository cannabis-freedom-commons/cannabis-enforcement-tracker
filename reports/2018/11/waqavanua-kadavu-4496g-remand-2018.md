A 36-year-old man was further remanded by the High Court in Suva on a charge of
unlawful cultivation of illicit drugs. [@1] The court was told the man, Joeli
Waqavanua, unlawfully cultivated 4,496 grams of marijuana in Kadavu on 14
October 2018. [@1]

State lawyer Eoghan Samisoni asked for time to file information and disclosures.
[@1] Judge Justice Thushara Rajasinghe adjourned the case to 3 December for
mention. [@1]
