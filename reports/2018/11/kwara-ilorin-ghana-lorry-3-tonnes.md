NDLEA officers, acting on intelligence, intercepted a lorry with registration number BDG 998 XU at the Okolowo area of Ilorin at about 11.30pm on Thursday, finding compressed cannabis sativa concealed in bags of secondhand clothes. [@1,2] Two of the three occupants, Ayomide Azeez and Ismail Kabir, were arrested and paraded with the impounded lorry and its contents. [@1]

The agency estimated the consignment at about three tonnes of compressed cannabis worth at least N250 million in the open market. [@1,2] It said the drug originated from Ghana and was being moved through Ilorin to Togo and Benin Republic. [@1]

Assistant Commander Dandi Emmanuel, who announced the seizure on Friday, said taking the consignment off the market mattered and appealed for logistics support for the command. [@1] The state drug control committee chairperson Barakat Olarewaju called the seizure a good development and urged parents and young people against drug abuse. [@1]
