Anti-Narcotics Bureau officers working with customs officers arrested five Seychellois after 3 kg of compressed cannabis was found on an Air Seychelles flight from Johannesburg, with the arrests running from late Thursday into early Friday. [@1,2] A 32-year-old wholesale and retail business owner and a 19-year-old casual labourer, both from Perseverance 2, were arrested at Seychelles International Airport after travelling on flight HM060. [@1,2] Three more suspects were arrested later: a couple from Quincy Village and a 33-year-old Air Seychelles aircraft technician from Maldive. [@1] The Magistrates' court remanded all five on Friday 9 November, with their next appearance set for 19 November. [@1,2]
