A suspicious pickup was stopped by Van gendarmes on the Kırkgeçit
Mahallesi–Gürpınar road in Gürpınar district of Van, and officers searched the
vehicle on suspicion. [@2] The search involved teams from the Van Provincial
Gendarmerie Command's Jandarma KOM (anti-smuggling and organized-crime unit) and
Intelligence Branch, together with the Kırkgeçit Gendarmerie Station Command.
[@1] Narcotics dogs named Devran and Cesur also assisted the gendarmerie
officers during their search of the pickup and its cargo. [@2]

The pickup carried firewood in its cargo bed, with cannabis hidden beneath the
wood. [@1] Officers found 23 packages beneath the firewood in the pickup,
containing a total of 24 kilograms and 100 grams of cannabis (24.1 kg). [@2] Two
people riding in the pickup were detained after the cannabis was found, and the
two detainees were linked to the same vehicle seizure. [@2] The cannabis was
seized from the pickup in Gürpınar during a roadside search on the
Kırkgeçit–Gürpınar road. [@1]
