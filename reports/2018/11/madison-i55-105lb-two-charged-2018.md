Just after 1 a.m. on Monday, a Rankin/Madison Interdiction Unit officer stopped
a vehicle for careless driving on I-55 northbound near mile marker 109 in
Madison. [@2] The officer said the odor of marijuana was coming from the
vehicle. [@1] After the K-9 alert, approximately 103 pounds of marijuana was
found inside the car occupied by Lanekia Michelle Brown and Marcus Anthony
Redrick. [@2] The car was occupied by Lanekia Michelle Brown of Grenada and
Marcus Anthony Redrick of Hutchins, Texas, and both were arrested. [@1]

Redrick and Brown are both being charged in Madison with trafficking of a
controlled substance and were held without bond pending an initial appearance in
Madison Municipal Court. [@2] Madison narcotics investigators coordinated with
Grenada investigators after it was determined the marijuana was destined for
Grenada. [@1] Agents with the city of Grenada and the Mississippi Bureau of
Narcotics executed search warrants at Brown's locations, where an additional two
pounds of marijuana was found with packaging material and scales used for
distribution. [@1]
