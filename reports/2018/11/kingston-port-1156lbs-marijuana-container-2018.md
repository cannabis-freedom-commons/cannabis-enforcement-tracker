Customs officers seized more than 1,000 pounds of marijuana at the Port of
Kingston on Friday, 2 November 2018. [@1] The Jamaica Customs Agency said
officers found 15 packages containing the drug in a container during a routine
patrol of the port. [@1]

The agency said the parcels held vegetable matter that a field test identified
as marijuana weighing 1,156.2 pounds, with an approximate street value of
$5,781,000. [@1]

No arrest had been made in connection with the seizure, according to the Jamaica
Observer. [@1]

In a separate account, the Caribbean Customs Law Enforcement Council reported
that members of the Jamaica Customs Agency's Contraband Enforcement Team and the
Narcotics Division of the Jamaica Constabulary Force found the shipment while
inspecting containers and detected seal discrepancies. [@2] That account
described 11 duffle bags holding 338 packages and put the weight at 2,525.8
pounds with an estimated street value of more than J$8 million. [@2]

The matter is being investigated by the Border Protection Division of the
Jamaica Customs Agency and the Narcotics Division of the Jamaica Constabulary
Force. [@1]
