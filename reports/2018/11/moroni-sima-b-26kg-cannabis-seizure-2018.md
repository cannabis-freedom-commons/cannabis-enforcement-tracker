Customs officers arrested one crew member of the Sima B at the Port of Moroni
after it arrived from Majunga on the preceding Friday. [@1] They intercepted him
during routine checks with a bag containing 2 kg 600 g of cannabis (2.6 kg).
[@1] Customs officer Allaoui Maoulana said the regular customs staff had already
left, leaving only the duty team authorized to inspect passengers. [@1] After
the seizure, the customs anti-drug brigade took over and began its procedures.
[@1]

The man, known as Bosco, was transferred to the prosecutor’s office in Moroni,
where an immediate procédure de flagrant délit, a proceeding for an offence
caught in the act, was opened. [@1] Deputy prosecutor Abdou Ismael said the file
had reached his office and he had asked anti-drug officers and gendarmes to
identify alleged accomplices for prosecution. [@1] Ismael said authorities were
searching for those people, reasoning that the presence of a courier implied
there was a network. [@1]

Alhamid Ahmed, director of litigation at Moroni’s customs directorate, said the
anti-drug services were working to counter trafficking. [@1] Ahmed said the
customs anti-drug services were doing what they could despite lacking sniffer
dogs. [@1] He also said the duty team had carried out the seizure. [@1] At
publication, Bosco was in pre-trial detention and was expected to attend a
hearing on Thursday morning. [@1]
