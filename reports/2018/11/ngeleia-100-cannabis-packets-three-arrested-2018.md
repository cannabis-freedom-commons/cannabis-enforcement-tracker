Three people were arrested after police found 100 small packets of cannabis,
already packed for distribution, at a home in Ngele'ia on 21 November 2018. [@1]
Tonga Police's Drugs Enforcement Taskforce, with police dogs, executed a search
warrant at the home early in the morning of that day. [@2] Officers seized 75.44
grams of cannabis in the 100 packets, the police statement said. [@2] The three
were two men from Ngele'ia aged 31 and 22 and an 18-year-old woman from Fahefa.
[@2]

The two men and the woman were charged with possession of the illicit drug. [@3]
All three were in police custody to appear at the Fasi Magistrate's Court on 27
November 2018. [@2] Acting Chief Superintendent Tevita Vailea of the Central
Police Station said police would keep working with the community to prevent the
harm illicit drugs cause and asked anyone with information about drug offending
to contact the nearest police station or ring 23417 or 922. [@2]
