Security forces from the Gaoual prefecture, gendarmes and police, seized 1,400
kg of Indian hemp on the morning of Wednesday 21 February 2018 at 14:00 UTC,
according to the Guinean Press Agency (AGP), the state news agency. [@1] The
seizure was made on a Peugeot 505 estate taxi registered RC-9171-P RG that had
left Kindia prefecture for Gaoual, after members of the public alerted the
forces. [@1]

The forces set up a roadblock at the foot of the Hérama classified forest, a
district of the Gaoual urban commune, and stopped the car there half an hour
later. [@1] Three occupants of the car were detained and taken to the Gaoual
territorial gendarmerie company, where they were questioned by judicial police
officers on Thursday 22 February. [@1] AGP named the three as the driver Thierno
Abdoulaye Barry, Abdoul Camara and Thierno Mounir Barry, alias Rougeau,
described as the owner of the cargo and known by that nickname to the kaniaka,
the people of Kindia. [@1]

According to the first information gathered, the 1,400 kg cargo was bound for
the Koumbia sub-prefecture, described by AGP as a major transit and consumption
zone for drugs in Gaoual prefecture. [@1] Guinée Quotidien published the same
dispatch the day before Guinee28, crediting AGP's correspondent in the locality.
