Police in the town of Mossendjo in the Niari department in southern
Congo-Brazzaville arrested four alleged cannabis dealers on Saturday 24 February
2018. [@1] The four arrested were Bernadin Moukassa Moupouma, who was 22 years
old, Juison Mounahoumou, who was 21, Adam Santos Veldy, who was 17, and Danila
Moussounda-Bouanga, who was 41. [@1] Officers placed all four in police custody
after arresting them. [@1] The report described them as presumed dealers held in
an investigation concerning alleged possession and use of cannabis. [@1]

The arrests were carried out by the police of the town of Mossendjo. [@1] The
case concerned chanvre indien, the French term for cannabis, and was treated as
a possession and consumption matter. [@1] Mossendjo is in Niari in the south of
the country. [@1]

After their period in custody, the four were brought before the Mossendjo
prosecutor on Monday 26 February 2018. [@1] That appearance before the parquet
was the latest procedural step reported in the case. [@1]
