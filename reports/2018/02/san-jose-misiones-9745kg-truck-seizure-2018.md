Gendarmería Nacional seized 9,745 kilos of marijuana being moved in the
semi-trailer of a cargo truck in San José, Misiones, and three people were
detained. [@1] Judicial-procedure units from Buenos Aires and Misiones Group IV
intercepted an Iveco tractor with a Bonano semi-trailer at National Routes 105
and 14 in Paraje San José while investigating a possible drug movement. [@1]

The driver said the load was paper napkins from Paraguay, but officers checking
the data in SICAM in front of witnesses got a negative result. [@1] Inside the
trailer officers found several packages containing 9,745 of cannabis sativa
(marijuana). [@1] At a second checkpoint, Squadron 7 “Paso de los Libres” staff
posted on National Route 14 at kilometre 536 stopped a Ford Focus carrying two
people that served as the truck’s guide to sweep the planned route. [@1]

The operation followed research begun in October of the previous year into a
criminal group allegedly dedicated to moving and selling drugs coming from
Paraguay. [@1] The Federal Court of Eldorado intervened and ordered the seizure
of all of the drug and the detention of those involved. [@1]
