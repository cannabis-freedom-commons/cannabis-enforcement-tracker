Local police in Burao town in Togdheer province seized 904 beer bottles and 1.5 kg of
hashish during a security operation, finding the load in two trucks that drug traffickers
had abandoned. [@1] No suspects were arrested; the traffickers fled the scene after
getting wind of the operation, according to the local authority. [@1] The police
commander called the drugs a danger to the general public and against religious belief.
[@1]

## Court-ordered burning

A local administrator said the haul was burned in an event held after a court ruling: the
beer had been brought in over the border with Ethiopia, while the hashish had been grown
locally in the town. [@1] The court fined two transport vehicles used in the case, one
first-time offender 5 per cent of its value and one repeat offender caught earlier in
Odweyne 10 per cent. [@1]
