Federal Highway Police officers stopped a bus of the Santos–Três Corações line
at km 871 of BR-381 in Pouso Alegre, Minas Gerais, during a routine inspection
using sniffer dogs. [@1]

Dogs of the force's canine operations group, K9 Mister and Heros, found a bag in
the luggage hold containing four tablets and a jar of marijuana weighing about
two kilograms. [@1] The 45-year-old owner of the bag, a native of São Bernardo
do Campo in São Paulo state, was arrested in flagrante for drug trafficking and
taken to the Civil Police station in Pouso Alegre. [@1]

He confessed that he was to be paid R$ 500 to carry the drug to São Tomé das
Letras, the town it was meant to supply, in the same region. [@1]

A second account of the stop, at the PRF's Cruz Alta post in Pouso Alegre,
describes three bars and a jar belonging to the same traveller and the same R$
500 offer, but puts the load at nearly three kilograms and the man's age at 38.
[@2]
