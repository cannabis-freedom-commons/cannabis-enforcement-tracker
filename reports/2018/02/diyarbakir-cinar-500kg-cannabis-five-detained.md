Diyarbakır anti-narcotics police detained five suspects at the entrance to Çınar
district after intercepting a cannabis convoy bound for Mardin. [@2] Police
named those held only by initials: minibus driver M.E.G. and four people in the
escort cars, F.D., D.D., F.B. and R.C. [@1]

Officers searching the stopped minibus and two scout cars found about 500
kilograms of kubar esrar, the Turkish term for herbal cannabis. [@1] They also
seized one unlicensed pistol with nine cartridges loaded in its magazine. [@1]
Police said the cannabis had been collected in Lice district and was being moved
by minibus toward Mardin with two escort vehicles travelling ahead. [@1] The
teams had started work after receiving a tip that a drug shipment was about to
move. [@1]

The operation was carried out by teams of the Diyarbakır Provincial Police
Directorate's Narcotics Crimes Combat Branch, the province's anti-drug unit.
[@1] Afterward, officers arranged the seized drug sacks to spell “Afrin” to send
greetings to Turkish soldiers serving in the Olive Branch operation around Afrin
in Syria. [@1]
