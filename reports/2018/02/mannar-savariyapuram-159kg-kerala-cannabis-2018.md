Officers of the Police Narcotics Bureau's Mannar division arrested three suspects who were transporting cannabis in the Savariyapuram area on Thursday afternoon. [@1] Acting on a tip-off, the officers raided the spot and found 159 kilograms and 393 grams of Kerala cannabis packed in six gunny bags. [@1]

The three suspects, aged 38, 28 and 29, were residents of the Silawathurai area. [@1] They were to be produced before the Mannar Magistrate's Court. [@1] The Bureau's Mannar unit continued the investigation. [@1]
