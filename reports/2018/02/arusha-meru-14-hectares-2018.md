Tanzanian authorities destroyed 14 hectares of cannabis in the northern Arusha
region over several days in February 2018. [@1,2] Eight hectares were laid
waste at the Meru forest reserve on Saturday 10 February, after another six
acres were destroyed earlier in the week, in an operation overseen by Interior
Minister Mwigulu Nchemba. [@1,2] Tanzania is one of the world's largest
cannabis producers. [@1]

Arumeru administrative secretary Timotheo Mzava said during Saturday's
operation that the authorities had decided to put a stop to the cannabis
culture. [@1] The minister vowed the operation would run in every corner of
the country, with everyone implicated arrested and every vehicle carrying even
the smallest quantity of cannabis seized by the state. [@1]

Arusha is a key cannabis-producing area, and much of its crop is hidden in
large stretches of natural forest. [@1] Africa Feeds attributes its account to
the AFP wire, and the Daily New Nation roundup carries the same AFP dispatch
from Nairobi. [@1,2]
