Teams from the Osmaniye Provincial Police Directorate's narcotics-crimes branch
were carrying out a roadside vehicle check at the Kadirli toll booths on the
Tarsus-Osmaniye-Gaziantep highway. [@1] During that check, officers stopped the
minibus bearing licence plate 41 NS 201, which was being driven by C.K., because
they considered it suspicious. [@1] When officers searched the stopped minibus,
they found wrapped parcels deliberately hidden inside its ventilation
compartment. [@1] Officers described the hiding place as the overhead
ventilation section mounted above the body of the minibus. [@2] The hidden
parcels together weighed 60 kilograms in total and contained cannabis, the drug
known in Turkish as esrar. [@2]

Police detained the driver, identified only by the initials C.K., immediately
after officers discovered the concealed cannabis in the ventilation section.
[@1] After completing custody procedures at the police station, officers
transferred C.K. from police custody to the courthouse for a judicial decision.
[@1] The court then ordered C.K. to be remanded in custody, the custodial
detention outcome reported in Turkish as tutuklandı. [@2]
