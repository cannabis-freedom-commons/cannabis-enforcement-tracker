A 34-year-old fisherman from Hellshire, Portmore, in St Catherine was charged
after police seized nearly 500 pounds of ganja during an operation in Portmore
on Sunday, 3 June 2018. [@1] Police stopped a Toyota Noah at the intersection of
Grange Lane and the Portmore Municipal Boulevard, and a search of the car found
nine knitted bags holding 43 parcels of compressed ganja weighing 490 pounds,
worth an estimated J$1.9 million on the street. [@1] The constabulary's
Corporate Communications Unit said police described the man as a major player in
the illegal drug-for-guns trade. [@1]

He was charged with possession of ganja, dealing in ganja, use of conveyance,
taking steps preparatory to export ganja and conspiracy. [@1] Radio Jamaica
reported that police put the seized ganja at J$1.96 million and that he was to
appear in the St Catherine Parish Court on 12 June. [@2]

He pleaded guilty when he appeared in the St Catherine Parish Court on 12 June
and was sentenced the following day. [@3] The St Catherine Parish Court gave him
three months at hard labour for ganja possession, six months for taking steps to
export ganja and a J$800,000 fine, or six months at hard labour, for use of
conveyance. [@3] The Gleaner reported the terms as running concurrently, a total
of 16 months at hard labour. [@4]

The Narcotics Police and the Financial Investigation Division said they were
continuing the financial side of the investigation under the Proceeds of Crime
Act. [@3]
