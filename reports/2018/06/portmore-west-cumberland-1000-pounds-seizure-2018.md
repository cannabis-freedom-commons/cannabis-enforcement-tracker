Police recovered over 1,000 pounds (about 454 kg) of compressed ganja in West Cumberland, Portmore, St Catherine, on the morning of 22 June 2018. [@1] The seizure was made about 6:30 in the morning and was disclosed in a post on the police force's Twitter account. [@1] Police said more information on the seizure would be made public. [@1]
