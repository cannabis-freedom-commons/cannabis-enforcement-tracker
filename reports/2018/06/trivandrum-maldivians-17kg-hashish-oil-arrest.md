City shadow police arrested Maldivian nationals Shanees Maheer, aged 27, Ayman
Ahamed, aged 24, and Ibrahim Fauzen Saleh, aged 29, in Thiruvananthapuram. [@2,3]
The men were trying to smuggle hashish oil to the Maldives from Trivandrum
International Airport. [@2] Police described Maheer as an international drug
dealer who was on the run. [@1] Officers had tailed the smugglers for three days
while the trio kept shifting between luxury hotels in the city. [@2]

Officers seized 17 kg of hashish oil in the operation. [@2] Police found that
the men had bought around 32kg dalda sachets. [@2] Investigators believed the
plan was to smuggle the hash oil in the sachets after emptying their contents.
[@2] Police put the international-market worth of the seized hashish oil at
around 5 crore rupees. [@2]

The tracking operation followed a tip-off from the National Drugs Control Bureau
to the Kerala Anti-Narcotic Special Action Force. [@2] A shadow police team led
by control room ASP Suresh Kumar tracked down the trio. [@2] Police said Maheer
was engaged in transhipment and mid-sea transactions and that the other two had
connections across the international drug market. [@2] Police said more arrests
would be made in the coming days. [@2]

The city police have arrested another Maldivian in connection with the alleged attempt to smuggle around 17 kg of hashish oil. [@4] Amhar Rasheed, 22, was arrested from a house near Pettah where he lived as a paying guest. [@4] The three men arrested earlier were in judicial custody at the time of his arrest. [@4] Amhar Rashydh, 22, who was studying for a degree in the city, was arrested after the three Maldivians already held said during questioning that he was involved. [@3] Amhar was a member of the international racket and was instrumental in purchasing the contraband from Kerala-based sellers. [@5] He had lived in the city for five years as a paying guest at Palkulangara, where the hashish oil was kept before it was handed to the trio. [@5] Amhar came from Thinadhoo in the Maldives and was a friend of Aimen, listed as the first accused; the two knew each other before coming to Kerala. [@5] Narcotics Cell Assistant Commissioner Sheen Tharayil said Amhar was preparing to leave Kerala for the Maldives when he was arrested. [@5] Amhar was identified after police examined CCTV footage from the hotels where the other accused had stayed. [@4] Their phone records also strengthened the suspicion that he had been in close contact with the gang. [@4] He is suspected of having provided a large quantity of hashish oil to the trio for smuggling out of India to the Maldives. [@4] A senior official said Amhar had lived in a house in Palkulangara for five years, completing a course at the Cooperative Arts and Science College in Thampanoor and then pursuing an aviation programme. [@4] The police formally informed the Maldives Consulate of the arrest. [@4] The trio had been planning to smuggle the drugs to the Maldives in vegetable oil cans. [@3]
