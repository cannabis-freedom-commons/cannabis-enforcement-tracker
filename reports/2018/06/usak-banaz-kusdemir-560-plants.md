Gendarmerie teams from Banaz district searched the house and adjoining
outbuildings belonging to a person identified only as K.K. in Kuşdemir village
after receiving a tip-off. [@1] Kuşdemir is a village in Banaz district in Uşak
province, the operation's stated setting. [@1] A narcotics detection dog took
part in the search. [@1] In the search, officers found 560 rooted cannabis
plants, described as Hint keneviri, together with 20 grams of kubar hashish.
[@1] Officers also seized four unlicensed hunting rifles and one unlicensed
pistol at the same property. [@1] K.K. was first taken into custody and, once
procedures were completed, was brought before a court. [@1] The court ordered
K.K. remanded on a charge described as engaging in narcotic-substance
trafficking. [@1]

The seized cannabis plants were burned and destroyed under the supervision of
teams from the district food, agriculture and livestock directorate. [@1] Hint
keneviri is the Turkish term the report uses for cannabis plants, while kubar
esrar denotes pressed cannabis or hashish. [@1]
