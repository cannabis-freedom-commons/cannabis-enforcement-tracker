Italy's Guardia di Finanza finance police boarded the Dutch-flagged fishing
vessel Quest about 130 nautical miles south-east of Sicily on 7 June 2018
after tracking it from the air for two days. [@1,2] Officers found more than
10 tonnes of cannabis resin on board, arrested all nine crew members and
confiscated the vessel. [@1,2] The finance police said the resin was high
quality, with a market value of between 15 and 20 million euros. [@1]

The Quest had been overhauled that spring at a Valletta wharf and renamed
from Sea View shortly before the voyage, which investigators say was done to
confuse them. [@2] It had sailed from the Gulf of Oran off Morocco claiming
to be bound for Alexandria, and the patrol ship Monte Sperone watched it for
40 hours before two armed teams boarded it as it motored toward western
Sicily. [@2] Two of those detained, including the first officer, were
identified as members of a Maltese criminal organisation previously arrested
in Spain over cigarette smuggling. [@1] The raid was part of Libeccio
International, the joint anti-trafficking operation in which Italy works with
other European states. [@1,2]
