Traffic police (GAI) officers in Dubossary detained a cyclist on 28 June 2018 on
suspicion of drug use. [@1] Officers pointed to his clouded gaze, unclear speech
and aggressive behaviour as signs of intoxication. [@1] The detained man was a
42-year-old resident of Dubossary. [@1] Officers took him to the district
internal-affairs department for an inquiry and for an administrative-offence
report to be drawn up. [@1]

From a pocket of his clothing, officers seized a paper wrap containing crushed
plant material. [@1] By its appearance and smell the contents resembled
marijuana, an assessment reported through the press service of the PMR Interior
Ministry. [@1] A later examination found that the seized material was cannabis
and that the cyclist had been under its influence while riding. [@1]

A check into the matter was then under way. [@1]
