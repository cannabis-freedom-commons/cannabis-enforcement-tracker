Police arrested a 33-year-old woman at a People's National Party meeting in
Falmouth, Trelawny, on Sunday 17 June 2018. [@2] She was about to enter the
meeting when officers asked to search her handbag; she handed it over and ran,
and police chased and caught her. [@2] The bag contained a 9mm pistol with a
magazine holding ten rounds and about one pound (0.45 kg) of ganja, the Falmouth
police said. [@1] The Jamaica Star reported that the incident happened at the
entrance to the Falmouth All-Age School. [@3] The Jamaica Observer, which
published the first police account, said she was at a public event at about 7:30
pm. [@1]

She was questioned in the presence of her lawyer on Tuesday evening and charged
with illegal possession of a firearm and ammunition, and with possession of and
dealing in ganja. [@2] She was due to make her first appearance in the Western
Regional Gun Court on 26 June 2018. [@4]
