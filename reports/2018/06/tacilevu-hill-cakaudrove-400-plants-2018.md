Fiji Police uprooted 400 plants believed to be marijuana on Tacilevu hill in
Cakaudrove, on the island of Vanua Levu, on 8 June 2018. [@1] Police
spokesperson Ana Naisoro said the 400 plants were uprooted during a police
operation at the hill site. [@1] The plants were described only as believed to
be marijuana, and the account gives no laboratory confirmation, weight,
cultivated area or estimated value. [@1]

Naisoro said the Tacilevu operation was part of Operation Cavuraka 3, a police
operation she said was currently progressing around the country. [@1] Acting
chief operations Assistant Commissioner of Police Maretino Qiolevu said
Operation Cavuraka 3 was under way and officers were revisiting areas known for
marijuana cultivation. [@1] Qiolevu added that officers also had a specific
target on hard drugs alongside the marijuana cultivation revisits. [@1]
