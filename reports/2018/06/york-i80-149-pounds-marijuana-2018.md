Nebraska State Patrol troopers arrested two men and seized 149 pounds (about
67.6 kg) of marijuana during a traffic stop on Interstate 80. [@1] The patrol
identified the pair as 56-year-old Robert Rosenwasser of Florida, the driver,
and 53-year-old Milton Coore of Jamaica. [@1] Both were arrested for possessing
more than one pound of marijuana, possession with intent to deliver and missing
a Drug Tax stamp. [@2] Both men were then taken to York County Jail. [@2]

The stop occurred at approximately 10:30 p.m. on Wednesday, June 13, when a
trooper stopped an eastbound 2017 Dodge Caravan for failing to signal near York
at mile marker 353. [@1] During the stop the trooper observed a vacuum-sealed
bag of marijuana plainly visible inside the vehicle. [@1] Troopers and deputies
from the York County Sheriff’s Office then searched the vehicle and discovered
149 pounds of marijuana. [@1] The Nebraska State Patrol estimated the street
value of the seized marijuana at $447,000. [@1]
