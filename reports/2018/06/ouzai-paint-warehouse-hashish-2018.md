Lebanon's Internal Security Forces raided a paint warehouse in Ouzai, south Beirut, on 1 June
2018 and seized about 15 tons of hashish packed in plastic paint buckets. [@1,2] The drugs
were masked in brown and white blocks and were set to be exported out of the country. [@1,2]

Security forces arrested several suspected members of the trafficking gang but published
neither their number nor their identities, and sent the case to prosecutors for review.
[@1,2] The labels on the buckets belonged to a paint factory unrelated to the smuggling
operation. [@2]
