Police officers raided a house along Rewa Street in Suva on 11 June 2018, the
second raid on the same house within 14 days. [@1] The raid found 20 sachets of
marijuana, after police found 17 sachets of marijuana during a search of the
same house the previous week. [@1] A man from the house was in custody and was
to be produced in court, police commissioner Sitiveni Qiliho confirmed. [@2]

Police said the raid followed information about drug-related activity at the
house from members of the public. [@1] A man had posted a video on social media
claiming police abused their powers by cutting the locks of the house while the
owner was not present. [@1] Police spokeswoman Ana Naisoro said the officers had
produced a warrant and identified themselves, and denied they were breaking in
or abusing their authority. [@1] Naisoro said family members did not want to
cooperate, so officers took that action to carry out the search. [@1] The
fijivillage account reported that officers had a search warrant when they cut
open the padlock to enter the house and seize the drugs. [@2]

Many people living along Rewa Street had earlier raised concern about drugs
being sold from the house. [@2]
