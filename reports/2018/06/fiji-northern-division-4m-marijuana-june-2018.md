Fiji Police confiscated marijuana worth $4 million in the Northern Division in
June 2018. [@1] The force said the total was the result of drug raids conducted
on Vanua Levu for the month of June. [@1]

Assistant Commissioner of Police Maretino Qiolevu announced the figure at the
closing of the week-long Digicel Duavata Crime Prevention Carnival in Labasa on
Saturday night. [@1] Qiolevu said officers had been conducting raids throughout
Vanua Levu focusing on the illegal marijuana trade. [@1]

The raids uprooted marijuana plants. [@1] A police photograph showed an officer
holding a plant believed to be marijuana that was uprooted from a farm in
Cakaudrove. [@1]
