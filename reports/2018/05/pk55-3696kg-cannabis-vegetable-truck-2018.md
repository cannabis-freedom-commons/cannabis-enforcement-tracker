Mauritanian gendarmes seized 369.6 kg of chanvre indien, the French term for
cannabis, hidden in a truck carrying vegetables from Morocco. [@1] The gendarmes
arrested the people involved in the case. [@1]

The seizure took place at PK55, a crossing point on Mauritania's border with
Western Sahara. [@1] The Moroccan truck was travelling the trans-African highway
that links Morocco to sub-Saharan Africa through Mauritania. [@1] The cargo was
bound for Niger. [@1]

Moroccan news agency Atlasinfo published the same account, crediting the seizure
to security sources. [@2]

The account came from security sources, which said the same vigilance that
caught the drug had recently been criticised in a documentary made for Al
Jazeera by a Moroccan production company. [@1] Police and gendarmes had seized
drug loads hidden in vegetable trucks from Morocco before. [@1] Two Moroccan
drivers who regularly use the road criticised the Mauritanian stretch of the
trans-African highway. [@1]
