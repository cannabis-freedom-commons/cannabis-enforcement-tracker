Montreal police raided six Cannabis Culture dispensaries across the city on the evening of Friday 16 December 2016, a day after they opened, starting around 5.30 pm. [@1] Officers arrested ten people, including co-owner Marc Emery at the Mont-Royal Avenue shop, and seized about 18 kilograms of cannabis along with money and equipment. [@1,2] Those arrested faced charges of drug trafficking and possession for the purpose of trafficking; Emery was additionally charged with conspiracy. [@1,2] His wife Jodie was also arrested and released within hours, while Emery spent the night in jail before release on a $5,000 bond that barred him from Quebec except for court dates and from cannabis businesses and associates. [@2,3] All but one of those arrested were released on promises to appear. [@3]

## Guilty plea and fine

On Wednesday 30 May 2018 a Montreal court fined Emery $5,000 for drug trafficking after he pleaded guilty; the possession and conspiracy charges were dropped. [@3,4] The Crown said he received a lighter sentence because the federal government was on the verge of legalizing cannabis. [@3] Emery called Quebec's coming government-run sale model a disaster as he left the courthouse. [@3]
