The Humboldt County Sheriff's Office Drug Enforcement Unit served two search warrants
on 22 May 2018 to investigate illegal cannabis cultivation off Rancho Sequoia Drive
near Alderpoint, with wardens and biologists from the California Department of Fish
and Wildlife, CAL FIRE officers, county environmental health and HAZMAT staff, code
enforcement and State Water Board personnel assisting. [@1,2]

Three parcels were investigated: two had no commercial cannabis permit with the
county and the third parcel's application had been withdrawn. [@1,2] Deputies found
eight greenhouses, six of them fitted with grow lights for year-round cultivation,
and eradicated 10,609 growing marijuana plants. [@1,2]

Eight people were detained; Hector Merdrano Escalante, 37, of Redway was arrested for
felony cultivation of marijuana, conspiracy to commit a crime, streambed alteration
without a permit and depositing hazardous material in or near a waterway. [@1,2]
Wildlife biologists recorded sediment-delivery water pollution, trash deposition in a
stream, stream alteration and water diversion violations, while county officers added
grading, building, sewage, solid-waste and HAZMAT violations, including 1,900 gallons
of diesel fuel and 220 gallons of waste oil stored on one parcel. [@1,2]
