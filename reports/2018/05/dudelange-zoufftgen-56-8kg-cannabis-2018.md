Luxembourg and French customs officers carried out a joint cross-border control
that resulted in a very large drug seizure on Wednesday at the border. [@1]
Officers stationed at Dudelange-Zoufftgen at about 11 a.m. intercepted drugs
hidden aboard a Spanish-registered refrigerated truck. [@1] Officers found the
hidden drugs thanks to Luxembourg's mobile X-ray scanner. [@1] The refrigerated
truck involved in the check was registered in Spain. [@1] The interception
happened at Dudelange-Zoufftgen, where the two customs services were posted at
about 11 a.m. [@1]

The Romanian driver told officers that he was transporting vegetables from Spain
for delivery to Denmark. [@1] Behind the declared merchandise, officers
discovered large sports bags that had been detected by the scanner and that the
driver had not disclosed. [@1] Those sports bags contained a large quantity of
cannabis herb. [@1] The sports bags were concealed behind the declared
vegetables rather than placed in the open. [@1]

The cannabis herb in the bags weighed 56.8 kg. [@1] Customs put the black-market
value of the cannabis at 500,000 euros. [@1]
