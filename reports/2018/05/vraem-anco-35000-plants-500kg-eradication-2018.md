Peru's National Police and Army destroyed about 35,000 marijuana plants growing on four hectares at Hacienda Caldera in the Anco district on 3 May 2018, in a joint operation by the local Vraem front and the special Vraem Commando. [@1] Acting on intelligence, officers drove to the area in five cars and found the fields, then confiscated and destroyed 500 kilograms of dried marijuana stored in eight rustic storehouses on the same site. [@1] The drugs were subsequently sent to Lima. [@1]

The Vraem, the valley of the Apurimac, Ene and Mantaro rivers, is the country's main drug-growing region, and the reports name no arrests and no owners of the fields or the storehouses. [@1]
