Gendarmerie teams detained A.P. in Karaçalılık Mahallesi, Akyazı, after
determining that he was growing cannabis in his house garden. [@1] A.P. was sent
to the courthouse and released under judicial control. [@1] In a separate raid
on a house in the Akyazı town centre, officers detained Ç.C. for growing
cannabis at home. [@1] Ç.C. was sent to the courthouse after his detention and
likewise released under judicial control. [@1]

Officers seized 44 cannabis plants from A.P.'s garden, where pipes had been laid
to water the plants. [@1] A search of A.P.'s house also recovered 15 grams of
cannabis. [@1] The search of Ç.C.'s house recovered 110 cannabis plants grown in
pots. [@2] Together, the two seizures in Akyazı district totalled 154 cannabis
plants. [@2]

The first case was a joint effort by the provincial gendarmerie's KOM narcotics
teams and the Akyazı district gendarmerie, which found the garden plot cleared
among bushes. [@1] The second raid was carried out after officers received
information that Ç.C. was cultivating cannabis at his residence in the district
centre. [@2]
