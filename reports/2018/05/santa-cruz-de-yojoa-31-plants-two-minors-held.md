National Police agents found a marijuana plantation in the village of La Fe,
Santa Cruz de Yojoa municipality, Cortés department, on the morning of Wednesday
9 May 2018. [@1] Police detained two minors aged about 15 or 16 who were in
charge of the plantation. [@1] HCH reported that the two were brothers aged
between 15 and 16. [@2]

An officer who took part said the two were found tending and watering about 31
plants, three balls and several tops of marijuana. [@1] The officer said police
had watched the two for several days before catching them inside the plantation.
[@1] The plot was in scrubland, according to HCH, where the pair were said to
have cultivated the drug. [@2]

The two were to be handed to the duty prosecutor's office accused of possession
for drug trafficking. [@1] One of the detainees denied that the plantation was
for sale, saying they grew it for personal use. [@1]
