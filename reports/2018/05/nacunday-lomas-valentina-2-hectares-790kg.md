Argentina's Gendarmería Nacional and Paraguay's Secretaría Nacional Antidrogas,
Senad, confirmed a marijuana plantation at Lomas Valentina, in Ñacunday
district, Alto Paraná, close to the Paraná river that divides the two countries.
[@1] Officers burned two hectares of Cannabis sativa and seized 790 kilograms of
marijuana. [@1]

A criminal-guarantee judge for the Iruña district ordered the destruction of the
crop and the seizure of the marijuana, which was carried out through Senad's
special anti-drug agents. [@1] The case began with an exchange of data between
Gendarmería's Eldorado judicial investigations unit and Paraguay's anti-drug
investigations department, after drug seizures in a border strip covering the
Misiones localities of Wanda and Montecarlo. [@1] Part of the intelligence came
from the arrest of two Paraguayan citizens on 18 March 2018 in El Dorado,
Argentina, where officers found 67 kilograms 530 grams of marijuana packed
inside the body of a pickup truck. [@1]

Economis, a Misiones newspaper, reported that Gendarmería and Argentina's
federal intelligence agency, the AFI, took part in the joint operation with
Senad and that the production site in the forest was meant to supply the
Argentine market. [@2] Economis published the Senad statement, which described a
drug-growing and processing operation steps from the Argentine border and put
the amount eliminated at two hectares and more than 700 kilograms. [@2]

Patrulla Radial, a Corrientes police-news site, carried the federal Security
Ministry's release with the same figures and the same account of the March
arrests. [@3]
