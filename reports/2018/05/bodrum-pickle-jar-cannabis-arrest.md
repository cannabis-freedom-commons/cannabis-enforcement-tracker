The bus passenger at the centre of the case was identified only as M.M.T. [@1]
Gendarmerie teams detained the suspect after the search of the bag. [@1] The
suspect was released from the police station on the prosecutor's order after
giving a statement. [@1]

Teams of the Bodrum District Gendarmerie Command stopped a bus carrying
passengers between cities during a road check in Güvercinlik Mahallesi, a
neighbourhood in Bodrum. [@1] A narcotics dog reacted to M.M.T.'s bag, after
which officers carried out a search. [@1] The search found cannabis wrapped in a
black bag inside a pickle jar and hidden in a chocolate plastic container. [@1]

The road check took place in Bodrum district of Muğla province, where officers
reported a drug seizure during the control. [@1] Güvercinlik Mahallesi is the
Bodrum neighbourhood where the gendarmerie teams stopped the intercity bus. [@1]
Esrar is the Turkish term for cannabis, the substance reported as seized in the
pickle jar. [@1] Officers seized the cannabis reported as esrar during the
roadside control of the bus. [@1]
