On 28 May 2018, the departmental directorate of Niger's national police at
Abalak seized 44 bricks of cannabis weighing 44 kg at the road barrier on the
Tahoua road in Tahoua region. [@1] The people implicated were arrested, placed
in garde a vue police custody and an investigation was opened. [@1]

Officers found the cannabis stock hidden in a basket and covered with tomatoes.
[@1] It was being transported on a motorcycle travelling from Gaya towards
Tibarkatan in the Arlit area. [@1] The seizure was attributed to the Abalak
departmental police directorate operating at the Tahoua barrier, with the
account learned within the national police service. [@1]

A few hours later the same day, the regional police directorate at Agadez seized
at the Zinder road barrier a Falcon-brand PA-type firearm with its magazine and
a box of 47 cartridges hidden in a loudspeaker, along with three uniforms. [@1]
That firearm seizure included one olive-green combat uniform and two uniforms
resembling those of the anti-terrorist and public-order units, and is mentioned
here only to explain the joint police announcement. [@1]
