The Hatay Governor's Office said the provincial Gendarmerie Command opened an
investigation to detain suspects identified for manufacturing and trading
narcotics. [@1] Gendarmerie teams then carried out simultaneous operations in
Reyhanlı district and in Istanbul as part of that investigation. [@2] Officers
detained 10 suspects during those coordinated searches in the two provinces.
[@1] Courts remanded 8 of the detained suspects in custody and released the
other 2 under judicial control. [@2] Authorities recorded that one additional
suspect connected to the operation was still being sought. [@1]

Officers seized 3,242 narcotic pills during the same searches alongside the
cannabis finds. [@1] Officers also seized 17 Hint keneviri roots, the Turkish
term for cannabis plants. [@1] Officers also seized 11 grams of esrar, the
Turkish term for cannabis. [@1]

The Governor's Office said the case grew out of intelligence work conducted by
the provincial Gendarmerie Command. [@1] The Reyhanlı and Istanbul actions were
therefore treated as one joint inquiry rather than separate cases. [@2] The
separately supported cannabis component in that mixed seizure was limited to the
17 plants and the 11 grams. [@1]
