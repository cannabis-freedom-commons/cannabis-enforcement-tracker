Three men appeared in Summary Court in George Town on Tuesday, 22 May 2018,
charged with conspiracy to import ganja in connection with an incident in July
2016. [@1] The defendants were named as Jamie Justin Solomon, 25, Ivon Somoza
Bush Jr., 24, and Kevin Ashley Ebanks, 38. [@1] Their charge sheets listed
Solomon and Ebanks as having addresses in West Bay, while Bush had an address in
East End. [@1]

The three were accused of conspiring together and with others between 12 and 17
July 2016 to import 35.66 kilos, or 77.18 pounds, of ganja. [@1] No further
details of the allegations were mentioned during the court hearing. [@1] A
police press release issued the weekend of 17 July 2016 stated that three men
had been arrested on suspicion of importing ganja after a canoe came ashore in
Prospect. [@1] Reporting by Cayman News Service at the time indicated that
police had recovered a quantity of ganja and arrested three people on suspicion
of importation after a canoe came ashore in Prospect at about 2am on Saturday,
16 July 2016. [@2]

Magistrate Valdis Foldats adjourned the case until 12 June 2018 and suggested
that the defendants complete applications for legal aid. [@1]
