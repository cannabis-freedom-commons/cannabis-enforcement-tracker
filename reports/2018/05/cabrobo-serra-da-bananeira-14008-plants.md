Federal and military police officers eradicated three cannabis plantations in
the Serra da Bananeira, in the rural zone of Cabrobó, in the sertão of
Pernambuco, on Tuesday 22 May 2018. [@1] The 2nd Independent Military Police
Company in Cabrobó and Federal Police teams acted after intelligence surveys by
the NIS-3 intelligence unit. [@2]

Officers eradicated 4,704 plants in the first plot, 1,600 in the second and
7,704 in the third, totalling 14,008 plants. [@2] The G1 photo caption on the
same report is captioned with the eradication of 14,008 cannabis plants at the
site. [@1] Officers also seized 400 grams of marijuana ready for consumption.
[@1] All the material was incinerated at the planting sites, and an incident
report was drawn up and handed to the Federal Police for the necessary legal
steps. [@1] No one was arrested in the operation. [@3]

G1's own body text gave 7,704 plants in total, with 1,400 in the third plot.
