The military police detained 35 soldiers and several Israeli civilians for
questioning on Sunday on suspicion of involvement in drug trafficking and use.
[@2] Most of the suspects were soldiers from several different units, and some
were also suspected of drug offences inside IDF bases. [@2] The 35 soldiers were
to be brought before the military court for remand extensions, and several
Israeli civilians were detained on suspicion of the same offences. [@3] The
detentions were announced by the army Spokesperson's Unit and followed an
inquiry by Israel's Military Police Criminal Investigation Division, which
detained several Israeli civilians on suspicion of the same offences. [@4] The
inquiry was run by the Military Police's central unit for special
investigations, with the Military Advocate General and the Israel Police. [@2]

The IDF Spokesperson's Unit announced that several Israeli civilians and 35
soldiers had been detained on suspicion of drug trafficking and use. [@1] Days
earlier, an Engineering Corps officer had been detained on suspicion of smoking
marijuana on dozens of occasions, and close to 70 soldiers had been arrested in
various drug operations during the previous month. [@2] Middle East Monitor
reported, citing the Israel Anti-Drugs Authority, that at least 54 per cent of
Israeli soldiers had smoked cannabis in the past year. [@4]
