Jorge Nava is a former reality star of TLC's 90 Day Fiancé. [@1] Nava was
arrested in Arizona on Feb. 12 and received sentencing for possession of drugs
with the intention to sell. [@1] He will serve two-and-a-half years in prison
after his class-two felony was reduced to a class-four felony. [@1] His lawyer
said the judge handed down the maximum sentence under the plea agreement because
of the large amount of marijuana and Nava's prior record. [@2]

At the time of his February arrest, Nava was found with 293 lbs. of high-grade
marijuana in the trunk of his car. [@1] He spent two days in jail after the
arrest before being released on $25,000 bail. [@1] Nava said he had initially
faced around 24 years on the charges against him. [@1] He said his lawyer worked
out a plea agreement under which he pleaded to a class-four felony in Arizona.
[@1] Nava had previously pleaded guilty to drug trafficking. [@1]

The reduced sentence was reported to reflect his prior record and the amount of
marijuana involved. [@1] After serving the prison term, he was expected to face
up to five months of parole. [@2] In Arizona, where Nava was arrested, marijuana
was legal for medical purposes at the time. [@1]
