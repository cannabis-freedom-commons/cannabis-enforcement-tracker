Provincial police narcotics teams in Diyarbakır mounted an operation in the
Ergani district aimed at people selling drugs. [@1] The operation, directed by
Narcotics Branch Director Nadire İnanç Karaca, searched the barn-used annex of a
house owned by an agricultural engineer. [@1] Officers detained the owner of the
house, identified only by the initials M.K., during the raid. [@1] After
questioning and procedures at the police station, M.K. was taken to the
courthouse and remanded in custody by the court. [@1]

In the barn annex, officers seized 111 kilograms of kubar esrar, the pressed
cannabis named in the report. [@1] Concealed inside a chicken coop on the same
property, officers also recovered a pistol, a hunting rifle, a blank-firing
pistol, two magazines and 14 cartridges. [@1]

The operation was described as based on information received from security
sources and carried out by the provincial anti-narcotics branch. [@1] Ergani,
where the house, barn and coop were searched, is a district within Diyarbakır
province. [@1]
