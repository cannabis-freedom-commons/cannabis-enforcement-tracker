Four containers travelling through Malta from Syria to Libya were flagged as
potential high-risk containers by the Customs Container Monitoring Unit after
risk analysis. [@1] Three were declared as carrying crockery detergent and
the fourth as carrying blow-torches. [@1] Scanned twice at Malta Freeport on
Monday 17 September 2018, they showed images that were not homogenous. [@1,2]

Customs officers opened them and found bundles of a brown substance hidden
inside the detergent; a narcotics field test confirmed cannabis resin. [@1]
The four containers held 11 tonnes of resin with a street value of €28.5
million. [@1,2] Malta Customs called it the largest drug seizure ever made by
the Maltese authorities. [@2] A week later the European Anti-Fraud Office
published Malta Customs' final figures on the bust. [@3]
