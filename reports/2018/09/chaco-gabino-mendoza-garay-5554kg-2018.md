Senad, Paraguay's national anti-drug secretariat, reported that the two loads
came to 5,554 kilograms of pressed marijuana once they were weighed. [@1] The
agency called it one of the biggest marijuana seizures in the Chaco, the
sparsely populated region along the Bolivian border. [@1] Fresh marijuana from
Paraguay's departments is traded across that border for Bolivian coca paste,
which the reporting describes as the traffic the two loads fed. [@2]

The people with the pickup left the vehicle and the load when they saw the
officers coming. [@2] That first cargo weighed 2,622 kilograms. [@1] The same
operating group found the second stash on the morning of Monday 24 September in
bush at General Eugenio Garay; much of it was pressed marijuana and some sacks
held chopped marijuana. [@2] The second cargo weighed 2,932 kilograms. [@1]

Both loads were taken to Senad's base at Infante Rivarola in Boquerón
department, where officers examined them package by package. [@1] La Nación
reported that the loads only reached Infante Rivarola on Wednesday 26 September,
after some vehicles were stuck for a time in the mud. [@3]

The Senad statement said the loads were apparently bound for Bolivia. [@1]
