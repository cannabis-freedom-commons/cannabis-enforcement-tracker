Uasi Musika, 48, was convicted on two counts of possession of cannabis seeds and
plant material. [@1] Tonga Police seized the cannabis seeds and plant material
at a property in Fo'ui on Tongatapu the previous year. [@1] The news headline
put the quantity at over one kilogram of cannabis seeds. [@1]

The Supreme Court heard the case as Rex v Uasi Musika, criminal case CR 137/17,
before Justice Cato, who delivered verdicts and reasons on 28 September 2018.
[@2]
