Ninety-six packets of cannabis and cash were seized when police arrested 10
people in Malapo, eastern Tongatapu, on Saturday 22 September 2018. [@1]

Police executed a search warrant at a bush allotment in Malapo on 22 September
2018 and arrested 10 people there. [@3] The ten arrested were seven men and
three women, Superintendent Sisi Toutai Tonga, the Mu'a District Commander,
said. [@2] All the suspects were held in police custody while the investigation
continued. [@3]

The superintendent said police were committed to disrupting and detecting the
supply of drugs to prevent the harm the illegal activity causes. [@2] Police
said they rely on the public to pass on information about drug cultivation,
manufacture or dealing, or information can be given anonymously by calling 23417
or 25147. [@2] Police said it takes only one call to stop crime and the
availability of drugs in a community. [@3]
