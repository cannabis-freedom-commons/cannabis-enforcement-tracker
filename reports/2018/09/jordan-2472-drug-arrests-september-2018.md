Jordan's Drug Control administration arrested 2,472 people on drug cases in
September 2018, a spokesman for the Directorate of Public Security said. [@1] Of
those held, 508 people were involved in 281 trafficking cases. [@1] Another
1,964 people were involved in 1,550 cases of drug use and possession. [@1] The
spokesman said 1,831 cases were handed over to the prosecutor of the State
Security Court, Jordan's court for security and drug offences. [@1]

The spokesman put the seized cannabis at about 23 kilograms, counting natural
and industrial cannabis together. [@1] The same monthly count also included more
than 404,000 Captagon pills and other pills, 910 bags of Joker drug and a
variety of other drugs, whose quantities include synthetic substances. [@1] The
separate Ammon account of the same statement put the month's case total at 1,831
trafficking, possession and use cases, and said 153 of the people involved were
non-Jordanians. [@2] The Arabic-language accounts call the seized material
hashish, natural and industrial, and say the industrial form is Joker. [@3]
