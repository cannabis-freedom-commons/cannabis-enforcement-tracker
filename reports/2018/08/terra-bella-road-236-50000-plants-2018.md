Tulare County sheriff's detectives found a massive illegal marijuana grow on a property in the 7100 block of Road 236 in Terra Bella on the morning of 28 August 2018, serving a code-enforcement abatement warrant with the county Resource Management Agency. [@1] The TAGNET narcotics team assisted in the operation. [@1]

Four people found at the home and property were arrested for maintaining a residence for drug use or sales, cultivating marijuana and possessing marijuana for sale. [@1] TAGNET destroyed 50,000 marijuana plants and two pounds of processed marijuana, and detectives noted large amounts of trash at the site. [@1] The investigation into the grow sites was continuing. [@1]
