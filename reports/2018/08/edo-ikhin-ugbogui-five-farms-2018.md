The Edo State command of the National Drug Law Enforcement Agency destroyed five cannabis farms measuring 4.3864 hectares. [@1,2] Three of the farms stood in Ikhin forest in Owan East Local Government Area, and two were found in Ugbogui forest in Ovia South West Local Government Area. [@1,2] State commander Buba Wakawa confirmed the operation on Wednesday, saying drug barons had suffered serious setbacks with the farms destroyed. [@1,2]

Officers arrested two suspected cannabis farmers in connection with the farms at Ikhin forest. [@1,2] The men were named as 41-year-old Orhiowobho Isaac and 34-year-old Bayode Ohiomokhe. [@1,2] Isaac, arrested in a hut inside a cannabis farm, said he was cultivating cannabis for the first time to pay for medical treatment and his family's upkeep. [@1] Ohiomokhe, a former commercial motorcyclist, said it was his second attempt at cannabis cultivation after a fatal accident ended his riding work. [@1]

Wakawa said the arrests and the destruction were part of efforts to reduce illicit drugs in the state, adding that any attempt to jeopardise food security would be highly resisted. [@1,2] He said the two farmers would soon be charged in court. [@1,2]
