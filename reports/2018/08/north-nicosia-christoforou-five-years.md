A Turkish Cypriot criminal court in north Nicosia sentenced Greek Cypriot
Constantinos Christoforou to five years in prison for carrying half a kilo of
Blue Dream cannabis from the south into north Nicosia. [@1]

The Turkish Cypriot daily Yeni Duzen reported that the court found him guilty in
a case in which five other men had been arrested in December 2017 for illegal
drug possession. [@1] Philenews, citing Yeni Duzen, said the other five men were
arrested in Kyrenia and that Christoforou was one of six people held in the
case. [@2]

Police learned of the drug deal and put Christoforou, said to be the supplier,
under surveillance. [@1] Officers moved in when he parked his car in a hospital
car park and seized 525 grams of cannabis indica, which reports said he had
brought from the Republic of Cyprus in the south. [@1] Witnesses at the trial
said he had brought the drugs from the government-controlled areas, the part of
the island run from Nicosia by the internationally recognised Republic of
Cyprus. [@2]

The judge said this was not the first time he had transported illegal drugs into
the north, a self-declared state recognised by no other country except Turkey.
[@1] On the earlier occasion he had carried 28 grams of cannabis indica, and the
court said his aim was to supply and sell drugs. [@1]
