A man identified in court documents as Jack Nor, also known as Robert Mackson,
was charged with illegal drug possession in the Superior Court of Guam after
police said he approached a man in Tumon and tried to sell him marijuana. [@1]
The witness told police that a man in a yellow shirt, red shorts and a cap,
carrying a backpack, had tried to sell drugs to him. [@1] Officers met Nor
because he matched the description, and he agreed when they asked to search his
backpack. [@1]

When an officer pulled a grey and red shirt from the bag, an object fell to the
ground and Nor said the bag was not his. [@1] The fallen item was a tissue and a
makeshift glass pipe that appeared to contain methamphetamine, and officers also
found a resealable plastic bag with suspected marijuana in the backpack. [@1]
The items tested presumptive positive, the court documents said. [@1]

Nor was charged with possession of a Schedule I controlled substance with intent
to deliver as a first-degree felony, possession of a Schedule II controlled
substance as a third-degree felony and possession of a Schedule I controlled
substance as a violation. [@1] Court documents also record that Nor was charged
in a separate illegal drug possession case. [@1]
