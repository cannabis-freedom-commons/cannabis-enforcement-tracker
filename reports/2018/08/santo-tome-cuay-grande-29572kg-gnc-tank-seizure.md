Officers of Gendarmería Nacional's Escuadrón 57 “Santo Tomé” stopped a private
car carrying a man and a woman on Ruta Nacional 14 at Paraje “Cuay Grande” on a
Saturday. [@1] The couple said they were travelling from Puerto Iguazú in
Misiones to Concepción del Uruguay in Entre Ríos. [@1] Both occupants were
detained. [@1]

During the search officers were struck by the fresh paint on the
compressed-natural-gas cylinder fitted in the boot. [@1] The drug-detection dog
“Pucara” showed interest when passing the metal cylinder as it walked around the
vehicle. [@1] In front of witnesses, gendarmes dismantled and opened the
cylinder and removed 29 kilos 572 grams of cannabis sativa. [@1] El Litoral
places the checkpoint at kilometre 669 of Ruta Nacional 14 at Paraje Cuay
Grande. [@2] El Litoral says the private car came from Puerto Iguazú in Misiones
and was bound for Concepción del Uruguay in Entre Ríos. [@2] El Litoral says the
cylinder showed fresh paint and a small hole, and officers smelled marijuana as
they approached it. [@2] El Litoral says a field test of the loose load was
positive for cannabis sativa and put its weight at 29 kilos 572 grams. [@2]

The Federal Court of Paso de los Libres ordered the drug and the vehicle seized
and both people held. [@1] Gendarmería presented the seizure as part of the
National Security Ministry's campaign against drug trafficking. [@1]
