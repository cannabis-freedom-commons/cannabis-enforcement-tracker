Aerial surveillance units of the Tax Agency's Customs Surveillance service and the Guardia Civil, patrolling the Strait of Gibraltar, detected several semi-rigid inflatable boats sailing without lights and laden with bales of the type used for hashish, heading for the Algeciras coast where no fewer than fifteen people waited to unload them. [@1,2] The forces alerted their command centres and reinforced the operation with maritime and land patrols. [@1]

When aerial surveillance confirmed the bales were being unloaded, a helicopter intervened and the traffickers fled, abandoning what they were unloading. [@1,2] Civil Guard officers and Customs Surveillance patrols then searched the area and recovered 71 bales weighing 2,332 kilos in total. [@1,2] The difficult terrain and vegetation prevented any arrests, though the operation remained open and further arrests were not ruled out. [@1,2] The seizure was presented as part of the government's ongoing fight against drug smuggling in the Campo de Gibraltar. [@1,2]
