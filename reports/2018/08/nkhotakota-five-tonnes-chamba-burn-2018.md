Nkhotakota police burned five tonnes of Indian hemp, known locally as chamba, at a dumpsite near the Nkhotakota Wildlife Reserve on Friday 17 August 2018. [@1] Station public relations officer Williams Kaponda said the hemp had been confiscated and accumulated since the middle of the previous year. [@1]

Kaponda said chamba smuggling is rampant in the district, with police registering one or more cases every week under the Dangerous Drugs Act. [@1] He said the plant is grown on the boundary between Nkhotakota and Mzimba districts and that the lakeshore road is considered a good route by traffickers. [@1] Burning the five tonnes took 110 litres of diesel, which Kaponda cited as one reason resource shortages delay destruction exercises. [@1]

Nkhunga Police Station, which covers the northern side of the same district, had confiscated about 3.2 tonnes since January 2017 that it had yet to burn. [@1] Figures from the two stations indicate the district confiscates about eight tonnes of Indian hemp a year. [@1]
