Customs officers at the port of Radès in Tunisia carried out what was described
as an exceptional seizure of 6.7 tonnes of sunflower seeds mixed with cannabis.
[@1] The published account did not specify the number of people involved in the
case. [@1] The agency said the case file was transmitted to the customs
investigations directorate. [@1]

The suspected goods were bird feed that had been imported twice through the same
port. [@1] Border customs officers in charge of controlling the warehouses of
the Radès customs office analysed samples in a laboratory. [@1] Laboratory
results showed that the seeds were cultivable and could be used to obtain
cannabis. [@1] The Tunisian press agency TAP reported the seizure on the basis
of a communiqué issued by Customs. [@1]

The checks focused on the warehouses of the Radès customs office, where officers
took the samples for testing. [@1] Beyond transmission of the file, no charges
or court outcomes were mentioned. [@1] Both of the suspect bird-feed imports had
entered through the port of Radès. [@1]
