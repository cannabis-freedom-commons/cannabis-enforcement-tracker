Police seized thirty-seven cannabis packets and cannabis branches from a home at
Hauloto, near Liahona in Tongatapu, on October 17, 2018. [@1] A 39-year-old man
was arrested during the police search, Acting Chief Superintendent Tevita Vailea
said a week later. [@2]

Officers seized the cannabis packets, which weighed around 127.75 grams, in
addition to the cannabis branches. [@2] The man was charged with possession of
the illicit drug and was awaiting trial. [@2]
