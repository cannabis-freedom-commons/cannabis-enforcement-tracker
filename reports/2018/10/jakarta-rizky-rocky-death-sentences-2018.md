The West Jakarta District Court handed down the death penalty to Rizky Albar, 29, and Rocky Siahaan, 37, on Tuesday 2 October 2018 for their roles in smuggling 1.3 tons of cannabis to Jakarta from Aceh in December 2017. [@1,2] The verdicts were read in two separate hearings. [@1,2]

Presiding judge Agus Setiawan said there were no mitigating factors as he sentenced Rizky to death. [@2] The panel found Rizky guilty of being the right hand of a dealer named Iwan, who remained at large, and said Rizky had been involved in several other large smuggling attempts. [@2] In the separate hearing, presiding judge Avrits sentenced Rocky to death for recruiting people to smuggle and distribute the cannabis. [@2]
