Toronto police shut down eleven unlicensed marijuana dispensaries in raids carried out between Friday 19 and Sunday 21 October 2018, days after recreational cannabis became legal in Canada. [@1,2] Twenty-one people were charged and released under the province's new cannabis act. [@1] Officers changed the shops' locks and seized their cash and products. [@1]

## Post-legalization enforcement

Police shut five shops on Friday afternoon and another six over the weekend. [@1] Acting Inspector Steve Watts, who leads the Toronto drug squad, said the force would keep cracking down on unlicensed sellers, adding that stores hoping to go legitimate had been told to close by the 17 October legalization date. [@1] The raided locations included a CAFE Cannabis and Fine Edibles shop on Fort York Boulevard, the Allevi8 shop on Church Street and the Bud Station on Eglinton Avenue West. [@2] Watts said customers found inside during the raids would not be charged. [@1]
