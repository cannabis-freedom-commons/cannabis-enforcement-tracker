Lebanon's army finished days of raids around Hermel and announced on 1 October 2018 that 10 people had been arrested, 13 storage rooms containing narcotics confiscated, and 0.7 square kilometres (70 hectares) of narcotic plants destroyed. [@1] Troops also seized five hashish manufacturing machines along with weapons, ammunition, military equipment and communication devices. [@1] The arrested people and everything confiscated were handed to the relevant authorities. [@1]

## An earlier statement

The 1 October announcement followed a previous army statement on the same Hermel raids, which it said were still under way at that point. [@1] The statement also lists one gram of cocaine among the items seized alongside the narcotics. [@1]
