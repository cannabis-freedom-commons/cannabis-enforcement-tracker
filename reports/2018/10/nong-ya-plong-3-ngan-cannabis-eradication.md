At 08:30 on 8 October 2018, a joint Thai force inspected a cannabis-growing plot
at grid NQ561590 at Ban Lin Chang, Moo 3, Yang Nam Klat Nuea in Nong Ya Plong,
Phetchaburi province. [@1] The team combined Phetchaburi's drug-suppression
operations centre, Region 7 drug-suppression officers, Border Patrol Police
Company 144, soldiers, provincial investigators, forensic officers, Nong Ya
Plong police, district administrators, district health officers, forestry
officers and Phetchaburi volunteer defence personnel. [@1] The account reports
no arrests at the scene. [@1]

The plot lay in a forested valley in Nong Ya Plong connected toward Kaeng
Krachan, where forest had been encroached and cleared for hidden cannabis
growing. [@1] The planted area covered about 3 ngan, a Thai area unit of 400
square metres, or about 0.12 hectares in total. [@1] The cannabis plants stood
1-2 metres tall, and part of the crop had already been cut, leaving only a
portion standing when officers arrived. [@1] Officers jointly dismantled the
remaining plants and recovered about 300 kilograms of cannabis in total. [@1]

They sent the seized cannabis to Nong Ya Plong police investigators, who were to
investigate to identify the growers for prosecution. [@1]
