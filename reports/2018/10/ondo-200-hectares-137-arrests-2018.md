The Ondo State command of the National Drug Law Enforcement Agency (NDLEA) said
it arrested 137 people for drug offences between May and October 2018. [@1,2]
Commander Haruna Gagara announced the figures in Akure, the state capital,
where he also confirmed that his officers had destroyed 200 hectares of
cannabis sativa plantations. [@1,2]

Of the 137 suspects, 127 were described as male, while 10 were arrested for
cultivating cannabis. [@1,2] Some of those arrested had already been charged in
court, while others were still being investigated at the command's Alagbaka
office in Akure. [@2]

Gagara said Ondo State had been ranked among the states with the highest rate
of cannabis cultivation, calling it unfortunate that the state had become
fertile ground for growing the crop. [@1,2] He said the command had been
campaigning against drugs in schools, motor parks and host communities and
appealed for support from other security agencies and government at all levels
to cut cultivation to the minimum. [@1,2]
