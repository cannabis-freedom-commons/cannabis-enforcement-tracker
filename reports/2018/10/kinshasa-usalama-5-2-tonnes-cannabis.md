Congo's national police announced the seizure of more than 2 tonnes of cannabis
as part of a joint regional police operation called Usalama 5. [@1] Colonel
Pierrot Mwanamputu, spokesman for the Congolese national police, presented the
operation's results to the press in Kinshasa on Wednesday 24 October 2018. [@1]
The operation was launched on 27 September 2018 against cross-border and
transnational crime in the member countries of EAPCCO, the Eastern Africa Police
Chiefs Cooperation Organisation, and SARPCCO, the Southern African Regional
Police Chiefs Cooperation Organisation. [@1]

Judicial police units and mobile intervention groups carried out the field work
and brought suspected offenders before the public prosecutor's office, the
police said. [@1] In the Democratic Republic of the Congo the operation covered
twelve provincial police commands, in Kinshasa, Kongo-Central, Lualaba,
Haut-Katanga, Tanganyika, South Kivu, Ituri, Haut-Uele, Kasaï, Kasaï-Central,
Kwango and North Kivu. [@1] The police statement also reported the recovery in
Pointe-Noire, Republic of the Congo, of a Congolese child who had been deported
to Gabon and the discovery of a large quantity of Sim Box equipment in the home
of expatriates. [@1]
