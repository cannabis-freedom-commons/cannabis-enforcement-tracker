Police arrested 35 people during the operation; all were bailed to appear in
court on 5 December 2018. [@2] The arrests formed part of Operation Black Ice,
which ran across Port Vila from 18 to 20 October 2018. [@2]

Police charged eight male Lycee LAB students with possessing marijuana and
viewing pornography. [@1] Police apprehended them in an empty house on Bahai
Street, a few metres from their school. [@1] The eight were among 21 male
students at the house whom police searched and cautioned. [@2] Police also
searched eight young people at an empty house near Karri Shopping and arrested
five at Fresh Wota Hill for marijuana possession. [@2]

Across the operation, officers conducted 29 searches in 16 areas and on one
shipping vessel in Port Vila. [@2] They harvested 101 cannabis plants in five
different areas. [@2] Police destroyed three marijuana ‘hot spots’ at Ohlen,
Fresh Wota and Teouma, describing them as places where young people went to get
cannabis. [@2]

The operation combined search warrants, foot and mobile patrols, awareness
sessions and arrests. [@2] At each area, police gave awareness sessions about
marijuana use, its dangerous effects and the law. [@2]
