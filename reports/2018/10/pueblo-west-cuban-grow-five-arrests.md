Detectives executed search warrants at six homes in Pueblo West. [@2] Five
people were arrested in the bust. [@2] Those arrested were Olga
Alfonso-Hernandez, 49, Yuriesky Vazquez-Morales, 34, Yordan Medina Del-Sol, 31,
Oniel Torna, 50, and Rayda Reyes Martinez, 51. [@2] Each person was arrested for
possession with intent to dispense marijuana, cultivation of marijuana involving
more than 30 plants, and possession of 12 ounces or more of marijuana. [@2]
Torna was also arrested for suspected child abuse. [@2] Bail for Torna was set
at 50,000 dollars. [@1] Bail for Alfonso Hernandez was set at 40,000 dollars
while each of the other three was set at 15,000 dollars. [@1]

Detectives seized 340 plants and an estimated 70 pounds of dried marijuana
product, plus cash and grow equipment. [@2] Authorities put the value of the
illegal products at about 1.2 million dollars. [@1] Officers found illegal
growing operations in five of the six searched houses. [@2] The five houses were
rented homes belonging to a Colorado Springs man identified as Esam Demian,
whose possible involvement had not been established. [@1] The county building
department closed the five houses because of extensive electrical alterations
and carbon dioxide pumped in to boost plant growth. [@1]

The investigation began with a tip about a possible illegal-grow syndicate
operating out of the six Pueblo West homes. [@2] Detectives worked with DEA
assistance to execute the warrants. [@2] Investigators said the grows were a
coordinated operation and believed the marijuana was intended for shipment to
Florida. [@1] The sheriff's office described the bust as the largest such
operation it had conducted to date. [@2]
