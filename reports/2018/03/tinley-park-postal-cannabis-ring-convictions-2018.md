Marvin Jones and Angela Wansley, postal workers at the Tinley Park Post Office,
were convicted in a Chicago federal court in early March 2018 over a marijuana
delivery ring. [@1] Prosecutors said Jones, 51, a mail carrier, and Wansley, 44,
a sales associate, made sure packages holding significant amounts of marijuana
reached drug traffickers in Chicago's south suburbs during a five-month period
in 2016. [@1]

Jones took misaddressed parcels from the mails and delivered them to Jayson
Smith and Courtney Poindexter for cash. [@1] Jones told Smith which customers
had asked the post office to hold their mail and which post-office boxes were
unused, and Smith had parcels sent to those addresses with fake California
return addresses. [@1] Investigators seized five parcels holding more than 4
pounds of marijuana, plus hash oil, THC-infused lemonade and marijuana edibles.
[@1]

Smith and Poindexter, both 38 and both of Country Club Hills, pleaded guilty and
were waiting to be sentenced. [@1] Jones and Wansley had not been sentenced and
each faced up to 15 years in prison for bribery and up to five years on the
conspiracy and obstruction charges. [@1] The postal service's inspector general
began the case in May 2016 after an informant said Smith, 34, was paying a
Tinley Park mail carrier to bring narcotics to his home. [@1]
