Members of the Royal St. Christopher and Nevis Police Force executed a search
warrant at the Basseterre home of Keith Hicks on King Street after midnight on
Tuesday, 13 March 2018. [@1]

The search found close to 0.5 kg of cocaine along with a large portion of
compressed cannabis, and the items and Hicks were taken into police custody.
[@1]

On Wednesday, 14 March, Hicks was charged on a Warrant in the First Instance for
Possession of Cannabis, Possession of Cannabis with Intent to Supply, possession
of cocaine, possession of cocaine with intent to supply and obstruction under
the Drug Act. [@1] The police said investigations into the matter were ongoing.
[@1]

In the force's report of arrests and activities for 19-23 May 2018, Keith Hicks
of King Street was listed as bailed on charges of possession of cannabis and
possession of cannabis with intent to supply, with no court outcome reported.
[@2]
