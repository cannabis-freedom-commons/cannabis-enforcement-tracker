The owners of the marijuana plants remained unknown and police were continuing
to investigate the case. [@1] No suspects were named in connection with the
seven plots. [@1] Officers burned the plants at the scene immediately after
finding them. [@1] Police said someone had planted the cannabis illegally along
the mountainside thinking officers would not find it. [@1]

Officers found and destroyed 1,139 marijuana plants in seven different locations
in Preah Bat Choanchum commune. [@1] The seven sites were in Kirivong district
in Takeo province. [@1] Deputy district police chief Yang Dara said his officers
were following orders from provincial police during the Saturday operation. [@1]
Police described the Saturday operation as a continuation of their crackdown on
illegal marijuana plantations. [@1]

Police said the seizure brought the total they had destroyed so far in 2018 to
about 56,000 marijuana plants. [@1] A Takeo provincial police report also put
the year's police total at nearly 56,000 marijuana plants destroyed so far that
year. [@1]
