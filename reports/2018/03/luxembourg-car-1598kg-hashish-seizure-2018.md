Luxembourg customs officers stopped and searched a car registered in the
Netherlands and found hashish packages on its back seat. [@1] The packages were
wrapped in jute sacks of the kind known as "valises marocaines", or Moroccan
suitcases. [@1] Officers then searched the boot and other hiding places built
into the car and found 159.8 kg of cannabis bars in total. [@1] The customs
administration put the market value of the haul at 1.3 million euros. [@3] The
driver, an elderly man, told officers he had been tasked with taking the goods
from the Netherlands to France. [@1]

On the prosecutor's orders the man was arrested and brought before an
investigating judge. [@1] The drug, telephones and the car were confiscated.
[@1] The driver was placed in detention on the prosecutor's orders before
appearing before the investigating judge. [@2]

Luxembourg customs said its anti-drugs and sensitive-products inspection
officers had stopped the driver carrying about 159 kg of hashish in a car. [@3]
The seizure had taken place the previous week, the government announced on the
Monday. [@2] L'essentiel reported that the goods could have been resold in
Luxembourg for 1.3 million euros, according to the authorities. [@2]
