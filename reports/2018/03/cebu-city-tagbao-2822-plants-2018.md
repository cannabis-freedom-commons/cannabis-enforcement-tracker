The City Mobile Force Company of the Cebu City Police Office uprooted 2,822
marijuana stalks in Sitio Mapa, Barangay Tagbao, Cebu City, on the morning of 2
March 2018. [@2] Officers removed 1,285 fully grown and 1,537 young marijuana
stalks. [@2] ABS-CBN News reported that nearly 3,000 stalks were uprooted. [@1]
No one was arrested and it was still unknown who was growing the plants. [@1]

Superintendent Tim Alam, commander of the City Mobile Force Company, said a
concerned citizen reported a marijuana plantation in Sitio Mapa, which prompted
his team to check the area. [@2] The team raided the area at dawn on Friday.
[@2] Alam said the plants were on a government lot in a secluded area of the
village. [@1]

Police put the estimated market value of the stalks at P203,350. [@2] ABS-CBN
News estimated the stalks to be worth over P300,000. [@1] The uprooted stalks
were to be turned over to the Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency for proper
disposal. [@1] Authorities said they would conduct an investigation to determine
the owner of the plantation. [@2]
