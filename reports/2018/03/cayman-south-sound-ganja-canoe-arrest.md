Three men were arrested late Wednesday night off the coast of South Sound, Grand
Cayman, following a police air and marine patrol operation. [@1] The Royal
Cayman Islands Police Service Joint Marine Unit intercepted a canoe with three
men aboard around 11 p.m. [@1] The three men on board the canoe were Jamaican
nationals aged 34, 40 and 45. [@1] They were arrested on suspicion of carrying
an unlicensed firearm, illegal landing and various drugs-related offences. [@1]

A police search of the surrounding waters located several bags containing large
amounts of ganja. [@1] Police also recovered a revolver with five rounds of live
ammunition in the same operation. [@1] The canoe the men had been travelling in
was seized in the Wednesday operation off South Sound. [@1]

The operation combined air and marine patrol assets, with the Joint Marine Unit
carrying out the interception at sea. [@1] The suspected offences linked the
three men to the firearm, the ganja found in the surrounding waters and their
landing. [@1]
