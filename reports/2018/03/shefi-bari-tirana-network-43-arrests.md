Italy's anti-mafia department in Bari took down an Albanian-Italian drug-trafficking network on Wednesday 14 March 2018, issuing 43 arrest orders for around 20 Italians, 21 Albanians and a couple of Romanians. [@1] Four of the suspects were arrested in Albania. [@1]

The network, known under the Albanian code-name Shefi (the Boss), had been under investigation since 2016 and allegedly ferried both light and hard drugs to the Italian coast by speedboat, with seizures over the period valued at EUR 15 million. [@1] More than 200 Italian and Albanian officers took part in the operation. [@1]

Eurojust reported in December 2018 that the Italian-Albanian joint investigation team, set up in 2017 with its support, had so far led to the arrest of 43 people and the seizure of more than 2,300 kg of marijuana, cocaine and heroin worth EUR 15 million. [@2] The agency said two powerful groups of Italian and Albanian nationals operated out of Bari and its environs while production, packaging and transfer were handled from Albania, and that the investigation was ongoing. [@2]
