The search followed several months of investigation by the gendarmerie's
research brigade (brigade de recherches), reinforced by a surveillance and
intervention platoon (PSIG) and a dog team. [@1]

The gendarmes seized 178 plants from a cultivator and found equipment at the
home, including the working grow room. [@1] The plants had been tended by their
owner. [@1] The crop was described as a 178-plant cannabis culture meant "to
help out friends". [@1]

The suspect was placed in police custody after the search. [@1] The Le Moule
gendarmes called it a good seizure. [@1]
