Police in Çamlıhemşin, Rize province, detained a man identified only as E.A.
after finding 30 cannabis plants, known in Turkish as Hint keneviri, in a
private room at an apart hotel. [@2] Officers from the provincial anti-narcotics
branch and the Çamlıhemşin district police launched the operation after a tip
about drug dealing at the apart hotel. [@1] The search found that the room had
been converted into a cannabis greenhouse, the DHA news agency reported. [@3]

The 30 plants had been sown in 29 pots. [@1] Officers also found ventilation,
heating, liquid fertiliser, humidity and temperature meters, and fluorescent
lamps in the room, along with similar equipment. [@1]

The Anadolu Agency reported that his processing at the police station was still
continuing. [@1] The DHA news agency reported that he had been referred to the
courthouse. [@2]
