Gendarmería Nacional carried out the operation and detained the 15 members of
the organisation. [@1] The three timber businessmen later charged were detained
on Thursday 30 November 2017, after gendarmes searched a warehouse on Avenida El
Fundador in Eldorado. [@2]

Officers carried out 15 searches in Eldorado, Alem, Oberá, Jardín América,
Posadas and the Buenos Aires suburbs, detained 15 people and seized 3,116
kilograms of marijuana in 3,573 brick packages. [@1] Besides a truck with a
trailer, officers seized 200,000 pesos in cash, 44 vehicles used by the network
and 15 mobile phones, together with circular saws, nails, grease and packing
tape. [@1] The same day a dozen or so people were detained in searches in
Eldorado, Oberá, Posadas, Leandro N. Alem and Arroyo del Medio. [@2]

The group camouflaged the marijuana among timber, wrapped it in lithium grease
and hid it in vehicles taken toward Buenos Aires province. [@1] The ministry
identified a family boss, the drivers and necessary participants in Buenos Aires
province, Misiones and Paraguay. [@1] The inquiry began from a 2016 seizure of
2,500 kilos of marijuana hidden in a false floor of a truck from Misiones. [@1]
The ministry put the two operations together at 5,370.356 kilos of marijuana.
[@1]

Three timber businessmen from Oberá and Eldorado were indicted with pretrial
detention as leaders of a network that moved marijuana from Misiones to Buenos
Aires in timber trucks. [@2] The Federal Court of Lomas de Zamora adopted the
ruling, the Federal Chamber of La Plata upheld it, and each of the three was
ordered to face a 200,000-peso embargo. [@2] The three were formally charged
with organising the transport of narcotics, aggravated by the number of people
involved, an offence that can carry up to 20 years in prison. [@2] A fourth man,
said to have rented a warehouse in Esteban Echeverría where the drug was
unloaded, was formally accused of aggravated storage. [@2] The same ruling found
no case to answer against three other detainees, who were released at once. [@2]
The November searches were ordered by Criminal and Correctional Court No. 2 of
Lomas de Zamora, under Federico Hernán Villena, and the Federal Court of
Eldorado, under Miguel Ángel Guerrero. [@1]
