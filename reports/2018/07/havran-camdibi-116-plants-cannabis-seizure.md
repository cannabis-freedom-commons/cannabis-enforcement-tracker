Gendarmerie detained two suspects identified only as Ş.A. and İ.B. in connection
with the Havran and Gönen searches and started legal proceedings against them.
[@2] The investigation into the two detentions was still continuing. [@3]

Balıkesir provincial gendarmerie teams acted after receiving intelligence that
Indian hemp was being grown in the garden of a vineyard house in Çamdibi
Mahallesi in Havran. [@1] Officers raided the house and found 116 cannabis
plants and 1 kilo 210 grams of cannabis in the garden. [@1] The provincial
governorate recorded the same garden haul as 1210 grams of kubar esrar and 116
cannabis plants. [@2] In the linked Gönen search, officers checking a Citroën
vehicle in Hacıvelioba Mahallesi found 148 synthetic pills described as
metamfetamin. [@2]

The Havran search was carried out by teams of Balıkesir İl Jandarma Komutanlığı,
the provincial gendarmerie command. [@3] Hint keneviri, literally Indian hemp,
is the term the sources use for the cannabis plants found in the garden. [@1]
