Aksaray anti-narcotics police detained two men accused of growing cannabis at
their homes in Aksaray city in two separate operations. [@1] One suspect,
identified only as Y. E., aged 49, lived in Yavuz Sultan Selim neighbourhood,
while the other, identified as M.D., aged 40, lived in Kurtuluş neighbourhood.
[@1]

At Y. E.'s address, officers searching the garden found 166 cannabis plants
growing in four greenhouses. [@1] At the same address, officers also seized 8
kilograms of dried cannabis described by police as ready for sale. [@1] At
M.D.'s residence, officers raided the home and found 29 cannabis plants in a
search of the house garden. [@1] Together the two raids accounted for 195
cannabis plants and 8 kilograms of dried cannabis, with Hint keneviri being the
Turkish term for cannabis. [@1]

The raids were carried out by teams of the Aksaray Police Department's narcotics
branch, the unit responsible for combating narcotics crime, after receiving
tip-offs that each man was cultivating cannabis. [@1] The detained men were
expected to be referred to the courthouse after procedures at the police
station. [@1]
