Acting on intelligence, public-order teams of the Dörtyol District Police
Directorate searched a house in Çaylı Mahallesi in Dörtyol, Hatay, belonging to
a man identified as M.A. [@1] Officers detained M.A. in connection with the
incident. [@1] The Dörtyol district governor's office named the detained man as
25-year-old Mevlüt A. and said a judicial investigation into him was started on
the instructions of the duty public prosecutor. [@2]

In the search, officers seized 1,250 grams of cannabis, described as esrar,
which had been hidden inside beehives at the property. [@1] They also seized
three cannabis plants, described as Hint keneviri, planted in the garden, which
the governor's office said were 50 to 100 cm tall. [@2] Officers additionally
seized one precision scale, described as a hassas terazi, during the house
search on Aksu Sokak in Çaylı Mahallesi. [@2] A second local report of the same
operation likewise reported 1 kilo 250 grams of cannabis and one precision scale
seized from the beehive hiding place. [@3]

The governor's office said district police teams are continuing day-and-night
work to prevent narcotics trafficking and to catch suspects. [@2] No court
outcome beyond the detention and the opened judicial investigation was reported
in the cited sources. [@2]
