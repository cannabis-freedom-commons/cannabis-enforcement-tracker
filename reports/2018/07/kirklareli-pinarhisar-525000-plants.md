Kırklareli Jandarma Komutanlığı teams, the provincial gendarmerie command,
mounted an operation at Manastırdere mevkisi in the Kaynarca belde of Pınarhisar
district after a tip-off that E.Ö. had cannabis growing in a field. [@1]

Officers found 525,000 cannabis plants, described as Hint keneviri (Indian
hemp), growing on a 40-dönüm field, about 6.4 hectares. [@1]

The gendarmerie detained E.Ö. over the field. [@1] Gendarmerie teams set the
seized plants on fire and destroyed them. [@1]
