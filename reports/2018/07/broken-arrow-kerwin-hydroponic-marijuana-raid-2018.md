Wagoner County sheriff's deputies, the Tulsa Police Department's gang unit,
Broken Arrow police and the ATF served a search warrant at Jordan Kerwin's
Broken Arrow home near 31st and 209th East Avenue on 12 July 2018. [@1] Kerwin
was arrested on complaints of distributing a controlled substance, using a
firearm while committing a felony, and directing, planning, financing, managing
or transferring drug proceeds. [@1] He was booked into jail without bond. [@1]

Investigators found about a pound of hydroponic marijuana in the home. [@1] They
also found two pistols, an AR-15 rifle and $5,000 in cash. [@1] News On 6
reported that investigators found the materials needed to make a concentrated
marijuana product and that the process was under way in the home. [@2]

Sheriff Chris Elliott said the product, which he called "dab", is made by
extracting high levels of THC from marijuana and was the first such case in
Wagoner County. [@2] Neighbours told News On 6 they had no problems with the
people at the home but saw heavy foot traffic there several times a day. [@2]
