An RCMP officer on traffic patrol stopped a commercial semi-trailer on the Trans-Canada Highway at West Hawk Lake, Manitoba, on 22 July 2018 for a routine inspection of the driver's paperwork. [@1,2] When the paperwork showed discrepancies, officers examined the load and found 925 pounds (about 420 kilos) of vacuum-packed marijuana hidden among a legitimate load of food products. [@1,2] They also found 75 pounds (about 34 kilos) of cannabis shatter, oils and edibles, bringing the total haul to 1,000 pounds (about 454 kilos). [@1,2]

The driver was arrested and the drugs and the semi-trailer were seized. [@1,2] Police identified him as 46-year-old Claudiu Huber of Agassiz, British Columbia, and charged him with possession for the purpose of trafficking and trafficking a controlled substance; he was released pending a future court date. [@1,2]

Police said the load had originated in the Lower Mainland of British Columbia and was destined for southern Ontario. [@1,2] Both outlets called the seizure the largest marijuana seizure through a traffic stop in Canada since 2015. [@1,2]
