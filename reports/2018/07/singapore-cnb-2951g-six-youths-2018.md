Central Narcotics Bureau officers arrested a suspected drug trafficker, an
18-year-old Singaporean, in the evening of 11 July 2018 and recovered about
2,906 grams of cannabis from the backpack he was carrying. [@1,2] Another
team raided the home of a 20-year-old suspected trafficker seen meeting the
18-year-old earlier, arresting him plus four suspected drug abusers aged 19
to 20 inside the unit. [@1,2]

Officers then raided the 18-year-old's home and recovered about 45 more
grams of cannabis. [@1,2] In total the operation seized 2,951 grams, which
the bureau said could feed about 420 abusers for a week. [@1,2]
Investigations into all six suspects were continuing. [@1]
