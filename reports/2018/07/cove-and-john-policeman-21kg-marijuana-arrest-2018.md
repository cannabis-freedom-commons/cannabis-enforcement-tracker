A policeman was arrested on Friday 13 July 2018 after he was allegedly caught
with 21 kilogrammes of marijuana in the car he was driving, the Guyana Police
Force said. [@1] The policeman was driving a vehicle that was heading to
Georgetown when he was intercepted on Cove and John Public Road on the East
Coast Demerara at about 9 o'clock in the morning. [@1]

The force said the arrest came from intensified collaborative intelligence
between anti-narcotics police and its East Coast Demerara division. [@1] The
officer was attached to Force Headquarters for trafficking in narcotics work.
[@1] Investigators said the marijuana was packed in several taped parcels. [@1]
