French navy sailors and customs officers in Martinique seized 1.3 tonnes of
cannabis at sea between 17 June and 15 July 2018. [@1]

In the night of 17 to 18 June 2018, the frigate Germinal chased a go-fast boat
suspected of carrying drugs, with support from a C26 military aircraft based in
Barbados and a Panther helicopter. [@1] The Germinal's crew recovered 25 bales
of marijuana that the traffickers threw into the sea, weighing 497.5 kg in
total. [@1] On 25 June, customs officers chased a go-fast boat off the commune
of Le Carbet using an Alouette III helicopter and the coastguard vessel DF44.
[@1]

Customs officers estimated that at least 500 kg of cannabis had been thrown into
the sea in that chase but recovered only 272 kg. [@1] Two of the three people
aboard that go-fast were arrested a few days later by the authorities of the
island of Antigua. [@1]

In the night of 14 to 15 July 2018, the Germinal intercepted a go-fast boat
south-west of Martinique. [@1] Aboard that boat, sailors found three suspected
traffickers and thirteen bales of marijuana weighing 332 kg. [@1] Five alleged
traffickers were arrested in Martinique and at Antigua over the month. [@1]
