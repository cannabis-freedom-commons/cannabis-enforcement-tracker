Around 3:00 on the morning of Sunday 22 July 2018, Senad agents exchanged
gunfire on the Paraguay River with drug traffickers moving in boats loaded with
marijuana. [@1] The joint team of Senad, the Naval Prefecture and the Public
Ministry detained seven people operating the boats, while others reached the
bank and escaped in the darkness. [@1] Senad identified one of those held,
46-year-old Jorge Carlino Silva Aguilera, as an alleged financier of the
criminal group, which investigators described as a family clan with other
brothers still fugitives. [@1]

Officers intercepted the boats at Km 1421 of the Paraguay River, 7 km downstream
of Villa Franca and 20 km from Alberdi in Ñeembucú, as they moved the load
toward a larger vessel for river transport. [@1] After weighing, the load
totalled 6,968.9 kilograms of marijuana carried by a large launch and an
aluminium boat at the moment of the interception. [@2] Senad officers believed
the load was ready to be transshipped to a larger vessel given its tonnage. [@2]
A second account said the joint operation stopped the marijuana from crossing
from the Paraguay River bank to reach the Argentine coast. [@3]

Senad said the action followed earlier investigations linked to the 2015 seizure
of 13 tonnes of marijuana at Villeta from the same organisation. [@2]
