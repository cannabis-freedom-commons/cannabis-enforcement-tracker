Officers of the Customs Anti-Narcotics Section of the Mauritius Revenue Authority (MRA) intercepted 2.1 kg of a dark brown substance suspected to be hashish and 304 g of green leaf matter suspected to be cannabis, together worth Rs 5.4 million, in the luggage of a 43-year-old Russian woman at SSR International Airport on 6 July 2018. [@1] The drugs were concealed in organic tea and health-food containers found among her clothing after officers in the Green Channel scanned her luggage and directed it for examination. [@1]

The passenger had arrived on Kenya Airways from Nairobi and said she had bought the drugs in Goa, India, where she had stayed three months, and would take them back to Russia after her stay in Mauritius. [@1] She was informed she had committed an offence under the Dangerous Drugs Act, and the drugs and the passenger were handed over to the Anti-Drug and Smuggling Unit (ADSU) for further inquiry.
