Arisi Kaitani, a 27-year-old farmer from Kadavu, was sentenced to 14 years and
two months in prison by the High Court in Suva on 16 July 2018 for cultivating
marijuana. [@1] High Court Justice Vincent Perera convicted Kaitani of one count
of unlawful cultivation of illicit drugs. [@1] Justice Perera treated Kaitani as
a first offender who had engaged in large-scale production of illicit drugs.
[@2] The court set a non-parole period of 10 years and two months. [@1]

Police raided Kaitani's farm at his village in Kadavu on 15 September 2016 and
found him with another person uprooting marijuana plants. [@1] During the raid
police uprooted 820 marijuana plants. [@1] fijivillage reported that the judge
sentenced him for cultivating 824 marijuana plants at a farm in Kadavu. [@2]

Kaitani had been remanded in custody for one year and 10 months, and that time
was deducted from his head sentence to leave 14 years and two months to serve.
[@1]
