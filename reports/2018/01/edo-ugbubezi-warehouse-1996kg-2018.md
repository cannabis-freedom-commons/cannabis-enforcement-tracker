Officers of the NDLEA's Edo State command discovered a warehouse holding a large quantity of harvested cannabis in Ugbubezi Forest, Owan West Local Government Area, acting on an intelligence report. [@1,2] A team of more than 70 officers evacuated the drugs, which the command valued at more than N20 million. [@1,2]

The exhibits comprised 34 bags of compressed cannabis weighing 680 kilograms and 94 bags of dried weeds weighing 1,316 kilograms, 1,996 kilograms in total. [@1,2] Two suspected members of the drug cartel working at the warehouse were apprehended and questioned. [@1,2]

The suspects, identified as 45-year-old Augustine Egwutu and 38-year-old Gabriel Orji, said they were farmers who had turned to the cannabis business out of poverty after losing their food crops. [@1] The state commander, Buba Wakawa, said the command had spread its dragnet for other cartel members and warned that there was no safe haven for traffickers in the state. [@1,2]
