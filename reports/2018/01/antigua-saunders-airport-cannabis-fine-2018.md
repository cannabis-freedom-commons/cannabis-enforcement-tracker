Kittitian cricketer Akeem Saunders was arrested at V.C. Bird International
Airport in Antigua on Monday 15 January 2018 after officers found cannabis
hidden in his shoe. [@1] He was 23 years old and had flown in from Jamaica with
the Leeward Islands team, in transit to St Kitts. [@2]

Narcotics and Customs K9 officers searched his suitcase and found 13 grams of
cannabis inside his shoe, which they seized. [@2] He spent two days on remand
before appearing in the All Saints Magistrate's Court. [@3] Saunders pleaded
guilty to importing the drug through the airport, and Magistrate Ngaio Emanuel
fined him EC$300, to be paid at once or replaced by a week at Her Majesty's
Prison. [@3] The Observer put the imported amount at 11 grammes of marijuana,
while the first reports said 13 grams. [@3] He paid the fine and flew back to St
Kitts. [@4]

The Leeward Islands Cricket Association said its board would meet that weekend
to decide how to help him, and its president said the priority was the player's
wellbeing rather than cricket. [@4] The association's president, Enoch Lewis,
said Saunders was not included in the already selected Volcanoes Super50 squad.
[@4] Kittitian cricketer Akeem Saunders was sentenced at the All Saints
Magistrate's Court in Antigua after he pleaded guilty to importing cannabis
found in his shoe at V.C. Bird airport; he was fined EC$300. [@1]
