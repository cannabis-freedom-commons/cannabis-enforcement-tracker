Takeo provincial authorities launched a crackdown on nearly 70 marijuana growing
locations in Kirivong district on Tuesday 16 January 2018. [@1,2] About 70
officers took part in the operation under deputy district police chief Yang
Dara. [@1,2]

Officers found marijuana growing at 69 locations in Chroy village in Preah Bat
Choantok commune and burned a total of 16,270 plants at the scene. [@1,2] They
also found 84 irrigation wells dug to water the crop across 5,075 square
metres. [@1,2] Some plants had just been planted while others had already
flowered. [@1]

Mr Dara said the mountainous terrain made it hard for police to stop the
growing, with people continuing to plant and dig wells. [@1] Some of those
responsible had been identified and would be sent to court, while forces were
tracking other perpetrators, but nobody had been arrested yet. [@1] District
police chief Yuk Sarath said officials had burned 300,000 marijuana plants in
the commune the previous year, and that growers kept planting for sale in
Vietnam despite warnings. [@1]
