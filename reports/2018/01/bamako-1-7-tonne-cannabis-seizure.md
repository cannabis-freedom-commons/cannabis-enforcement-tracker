Mali's Office central des stupéfiants, the country's central anti-drug office,
intercepted 1.7 tonnes of cannabis in Bamako during the weekend before 23
January 2018. [@1] Two people were held in custody while searches for other
suspects continued. [@1] The office said the truck's owner had been identified
and was actively sought. [@2]

Officers counted 1,018 cannabis bricks totalling 1.7 tonnes, as reported by the
OCS in a communiqué dated 22 January. [@2] The cannabis was hidden in a truck
arriving from Ghana and was seized in the Yorodjambougou sector of Yirimadjo in
Bamako. [@2] The office described the seizure as the second largest cannabis
seizure in Mali, after a record 2.7-tonne seizure from a vehicle that had also
come from Ghana. [@2]

OCS director Colonel Adama Tounkara gave the account to the BBC's correspondent
Alou Diawara in Bamako. [@1] He said cannabis is also grown in Mali. [@1]
Clandestine cannabis fields had been discovered in the southern Sikasso region
in October 2017. [@2]
