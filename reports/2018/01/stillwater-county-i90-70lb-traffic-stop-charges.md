James Yannick Van Sluytman, 32, of Guyana, and Bing Yuan Liang, 46, were
arrested on Interstate 90 in Stillwater County, Montana, on 16 January 2018
after a trooper stopped their rented car. [@2] A Montana Highway Patrol trooper
pulled the car over at mile marker 408 after it travelled well below the speed
limit, pulled into the passing lane without signalling and swerved onto the
shoulder. [@2] Van Sluytman was driving and produced a Guyanese passport; the
trooper noticed orange peels strewn about the car. [@1]

The men gave conflicting accounts of the trip, Van Sluytman saying they were
heading to North Dakota to sell soy sauce and Liang saying Chicago to sell
beauty products. [@1] Van Sluytman consented to a search of the car, in which
the trooper found approximately 70 pounds (31.75 kilograms) of suspected
marijuana in vacuum-sealed packages and US$1,700 in cash. [@1] Both men were
charged with one felony count of possession of dangerous drugs with intent to
distribute under court documents filed by the Stillwater County Attorney's
Office. [@2] They were held at the Yellowstone County Detention Facility with
bail set at US$125,000 each. [@1]

Stillwater County News reported the case on 25 January 2018 as one of a run of
Interstate 90 traffic stops that had netted 90 pounds of marijuana and eight
pounds of methamphetamine in six weeks. [@2]
