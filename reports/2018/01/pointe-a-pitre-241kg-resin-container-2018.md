On 30 January 2018 customs officers in Guadeloupe uncovered a large cannabis
resin trafficking operation. [@1] They seized 241 kilograms of cannabis resin,
which customs valued at 1.7 million euros. [@1] RCI Guadeloupe reported the same
241 kilograms of resin and the same estimate of 1.7 million euros. [@2] RCI
described the operation as the dismantling of a drug trade between mainland
France and Guadeloupe and noted that the drug involved, unlike in earlier cases,
was cannabis resin. [@2]

Guadeloupe customs officers traced the network by analysing transfers of
vehicles between mainland France and the archipelago. [@1] A suspicious
container had been unloaded at the port of Pointe-à-Pitre. [@1] RCI reported
that analysing the vehicle transfers led officers to a suspicious container
recently unloaded at the port. [@2] The customs surveillance service's check of
the container's two vehicles found bags concealing the resin in one of the cars.
[@2]

RCI reported that the prosecutor's office of the specialised interregional court
in Fort-de-France assigned the inquiry to that OCRTIS branch. [@2]
