The Narcotics Control Board (NACOB), working with the Asesewa district police, intercepted a Kia truck with registration number ER 105-13 at Buokono, near Asesewa in the Upper Manya Krobo District. [@1,2] Officers found 124 sacks of dried leaves suspected to be cannabis packed onto the truck's bucket and covered with a tarpaulin. [@1,2]

The 124 sacks comprised 72 sacks of compressed leaves holding 4,326 slabs and 52 sacks of uncompressed leaves, with an estimated gross weight of 5 tonnes and a street value of about GH₵500,000. [@1,2] NACOB said a late-2017 tip-off had traced a boat loaded with cannabis from Dzemeni in the Volta Region, which offloaded onto the truck near Asesewa for onward transport to Ashaiman in Greater Accra. [@1,2]

The driver, Stephen Narteh Sangmortey, was arrested and later remanded in prison custody, while his accomplice fled when officers stopped the vehicle and the purported owner of the consignment, Nicholas Lartey, was still at large. [@1]
