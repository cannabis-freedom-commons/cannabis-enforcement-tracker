Two men charged with importing 692 pounds of ganja appeared in Summary Court on
Monday. [@1,2] Both were initially remanded in custody while the legal papers were prepared. [@2]

The Joint Marine Unit chased and intercepted this canoe Thursday and police
found 692 pounds of ganja on board. [@1] The two were arrested by the Joint
Marine Unit early Thursday, Jan. 25, near Uncle Bob Road in West Bay. [@1,2]

Yoandy Swaby Ebanks, 36, is from West Bay and was due to return to court that
afternoon for a bail application. [@1] The second man, Terry Wright, is a
Jamaican national whose lawyer deferred a bail application until he received the
case papers. [@1] Ebanks is also charged with human smuggling, while Wright, 39,
is charged with illegal landing. [@1,2]

Three other Jamaican men also appeared in Summary Court on Monday charged with
illegal landing after being arrested in Cayman Brac on Friday, Jan. 26. [@1,2] Customs officers arrested the three men in Cayman Brac the following day. [@2] The
three Cayman Brac men were arrested on suspicion of being concerned in the
importation of ganja, but in court their only charges were illegal landing. [@1] The Crown says the two boats are connected, but the men from the second boat have not been charged over the ganja. [@2] Robinson was also charged with possession of ganja. [@2]
The three are Andre Robinson, 33, Nicholas Maxam, 30, and O’Shane Ricketts, 29.
[@1] The three were remanded in custody as the case continued. [@2] Their defence attorney said they were not linked to the drug haul and had been forced to land on the Brac by bad weather. [@2]

Police said Joint Marine Unit officers came upon two vessels acting suspiciously
near the Big Channel entrance to North Sound around 4:30 a.m. Thursday, and
believed the vessel that made off to sea was the one later found in Cayman Brac
on Friday. [@1,2] The three other men pleaded not guilty and were due to stand trial in July. [@3] Their sentencing was set for 25 June 2018. [@3] By late May 2018, Ebanks-Swaby and Wright had admitted importing about 692 pounds of ganja. [@3] All five were expected back in court later that week. [@2]
