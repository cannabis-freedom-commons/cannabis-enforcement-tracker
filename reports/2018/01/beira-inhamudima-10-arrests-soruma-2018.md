Police in Beira detained 10 people in a house in the Inhamudima area, where they
were allegedly selling and using drugs including heroin and cannabis sativa,
known locally as soruma. [@1] The police seized small quantities of the drugs
and an unspecified sum of money. [@1]

The detainees denied involvement but admitted they were found in the house where
the drug was being sold. [@1] One detainee, the owner of the house where the
drug was seized, said he rented the house to several people and did not know
what they did. [@1]

A suspect described by police as the owner of the drug, along with other dealers
and users, had fled. [@1] PRM spokesman in Sofala Daniel Macuácua said officers
were working to neutralise the rest of the group and establish where the drug
came from. [@1]
