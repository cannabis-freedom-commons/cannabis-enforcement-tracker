Fairfield police went to a home in the 5000 block of Oakbrook Circle on
Wednesday 17 January 2018 after a report of four men fighting in the front yard,
one of them carrying a handgun. [@1] Officers detained two of the men and found
a THC extraction laboratory, also called a honey oil or hash oil lab, inside the
house. [@1]

Robert Olivieri, 30, and Nicholas Olivieri, 22, both of Fairfield, were arrested
on narcotics and firearm charges and booked into the Solano County Jail. [@1]
Officers also confiscated cocaine, methamphetamine, Xanax tablets, psilocybin
mushrooms, LSD and PCP from the home. [@1]

Nicholas Olivieri was arraigned in Solano County Superior Court on Friday 19
January and pleaded not guilty to a set of drug-related charges, including
manufacturing methamphetamine. [@2] He remained in jail on bail of $123,333 and
was due back in court on 1 February for a readiness conference, while Robert
Olivieri had been released from custody. [@2]

Police said legal medical and recreational marijuana in California had increased
the number of explosions from making marijuana concentrates. [@1] The department
said honey oil labs are prohibited and that convictions carry sentences of up to
seven years in prison. [@1]
