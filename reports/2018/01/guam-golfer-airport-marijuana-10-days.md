A local golfer was sentenced on Tuesday to spend 10 days in prison in connection
with marijuana found in his luggage. [@1] Superior Court of Guam Judge Vernon
Perez sentenced golfer Nalapon Vongjalorn to 10 days in prison. [@1] Vongjalorn
had entered a plea deal and pleaded guilty, and his lawyer said it was his first
offense. [@1] He was scheduled to turn himself in to the Department of
Corrections on 4 January to serve the sentence. [@1]

Nalapon Vongjalorn was initially charged in December 2016 after he landed at the
Guam airport and a drug detecting dog alerted a Guam Customs and Quarantine
Agency officer to him at the baggage carousel. [@1] He told Customs he had a
grinder, which officers found along with two resealable bags containing a green
leafy substance. [@1] Officers also found two more small plastic baggies on
Vongjalorn, containing leafy vegetable matter that tested presumptive positive
for marijuana. [@2] The combined gross weight of the suspected marijuana was
78.9 grams, according to the magistrate's complaint. [@2] His lawyer later said
Vongjalorn had less than two ounces of marijuana. [@1] He was charged with
importation of a Schedule I controlled substance as a first-degree felony,
possession of a Schedule I controlled substance on board an aircraft as a
first-degree felony and possession of more than one ounce of marijuana as a
petty misdemeanor. [@2]

He reportedly admitted the items were marijuana and said he used the drug
regularly. [@2] At sentencing he said that before coming to Guam he had been
using the substance a lot and had not thought to dump it before boarding his
flight. [@1] Former Guam National Golf Federation president Samuel Teker spoke
for Vongjalorn at sentencing, calling him a good ambassador for Guam. [@1]
