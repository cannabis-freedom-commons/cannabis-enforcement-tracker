Antwerp local police arrested members of an Albanian drug gang in Antwerp during
the weekend before 11 April 2018. [@1] The arrests were carried out by the
Antwerp local police's Drugs Ondersteunings Team (DOT). [@1] The searches
targeted Fitim B. in the Jezusstraat and other gang members. [@1]

The gang operated from an apartment in the Jezusstraat. [@1] The street joins
the Meir directly and the apartment was above a bankrupt snack bar. [@1] From
there, the gang distributed both hard drugs and soft drugs. [@1] House searches
seized in total more than 40,000 euros in cash, half a kilogram of cocaine and
more than 600 grams of marijuana, plus 35 mobile phones and three cars. [@1]

Fitim B. did not hide his income and posed in a photo with a large stack of
banknotes. [@1] The photo showed many green 100-euro notes. [@1] A relative
reacted to the photo saying he should now treat others. [@1] The report notes
the police photo of the seized drugs and cash showed no green notes. [@1]
