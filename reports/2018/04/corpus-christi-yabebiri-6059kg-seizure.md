Residents were not detained in the operation; officers found two abandoned boats
and, about 500 metres away, a truck loaded with wrapped bundles, with more
packages of the same kind scattered on the ground around it. [@1]

Argentina's Naval Prefecture counted 6,059 kilos 400 grams of marijuana in the
truck and the packages around it, in front of witnesses. [@1] The Ministry of
Security valued the load at more than 180 million pesos, some 9 million dollars,
and said it could yield more than 10 million doses for sale. [@1] It said the
seizure was the second largest of the year in Argentina. [@2]

An undercover Prefectura patrol watching the bush around Corpus Christi before
dawn spotted two boats entering the Yabebirí stream on thermal cameras. [@1]
Officers from the Prefectura posts of Puerto Maní, Santa Ana and San Ignacio
closed off the area in an Operativo Cerrojo, a lock-down cordon, and searched by
air, land and water. [@1] El Territorio reported the operation as a joint action
by the Misiones provincial police and the Naval Prefecture. [@2]

The Posadas federal court, the first-instance criminal and correctional court,
is handling the case. [@2] The Ministry of Security said it had seized more than
77 tonnes of marijuana nationally in 2018 by that point, and Misiones accounted
for more than 52 tonnes. [@2]
