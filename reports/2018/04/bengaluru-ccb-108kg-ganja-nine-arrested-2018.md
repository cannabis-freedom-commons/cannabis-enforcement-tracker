The Woman and Narcotics Wing of the Central Crime Branch seized 108 kg of ganja worth Rs 50 lakh on Saturday and arrested nine people of a Kerala-based gang who were moving the drug into Karnataka and Kerala. [@1] Assistant Commissioner of Police Venkatesh Prasanna and his team studied the gang's entry and exit routes and covered them before making the arrests. [@1]

The alleged mastermind, 36-year-old N. Nainesh of Thrissur in Kerala, sourced the cannabis through a Kerala-based dealer from an Odisha-based grower cultivating marijuana on unclaimed land in the Naxal-affected Gajuvaka village near Srikakulam, with at least a quintal smuggled into the city every fortnight. [@1] Nainesh operated from a rented house in Eashwar Layout, Indiranagar, whose owner had already asked him to vacate after finding his activities suspicious. [@1] The gang divided the drug into packs of eight and ten grams and sold each for Rs 300 to Rs 500 to college students and other buyers in Ejipura, Murugeshpalya, Banaswadi, Hennur, KR Puram, Ramamurthy Nagar and other areas. [@1]
