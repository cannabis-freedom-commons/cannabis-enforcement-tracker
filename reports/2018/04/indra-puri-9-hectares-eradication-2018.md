Personnel of Polda Metro Jaya, soldiers of Kodam Iskandar Muda and officers of Aceh's National Narcotics Agency (BNN) destroyed cannabis fields in the Indra Puri mountains of Aceh Besar regency on Thursday 26 April 2018. [@1,2] The joint teams destroyed 9 hectares of fields holding cannabis nearly ready for harvest. [@1,2]

ANTARA Foto's captions describe the teams handling uprooted plants for destruction in the mountain fields. [@1,2] The operation's captions report no capture of the field owners. [@1,2]
