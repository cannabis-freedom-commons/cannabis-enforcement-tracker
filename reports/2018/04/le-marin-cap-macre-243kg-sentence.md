One of the two traffickers unloading cannabis on Cap Macré beach on 18 April
2018 was arrested immediately. [@1] The suspect was arrested as he hid near the
beach and placed in police custody. [@1] The arrested man, originally from
Barbados, was tried in immediate proceedings before the Fort-de-France criminal
court on 20 April 2018 and sentenced to four years in prison with a ten-year
territorial ban and confiscation of the seals. [@1] He had been prosecuted for
drug trafficking and criminal association and was convicted of trafficking with
a ten-year territorial ban. [@2]

At about 9:50 on 18 April the Le Marin gendarmerie was alerted that two
individuals were unloading suspect bales on a beach at Cap Macré. [@1] A
dog-team search found 13 bales holding a total of 243 kg of cannabis herb, which
had to be winched by helicopter to the Le Marin company barracks. [@1] Le Marin
patrols and the Saint-Nazaire mobile gendarmerie squadron deployed with support
from the nautical brigade, the gendarmerie helicopter and two dog teams. [@1]

The man had left his island for Saint Vincent early that week to collect a
cannabis cargo meant to return to Barbados by yole, but he and his accomplice
met bad weather and lost their way. [@2] He told the court he had taken part in
the run to pay for care for his seriously ill mother after leaving prison. [@2]
The cannabis bales were destroyed, while the engine of the yole used for the
transport was seized and confiscated. [@1]

An earlier local account reported about 230 kg of cannabis herb seized by
gendarmes at Cap Macré in Le Marin, saying the bales were buried. [@3] The
second man was still being sought, with the Fort-de-France research section
leading the investigation. [@1]
