Gendarmes from South Basse-Terre lay in wait in scrub at Les Trois Pointes, at
the end of the Rivières-Sens fitness trail, on Saturday night after a tip-off.
[@1] A boat with all its lights off landed a few hours later. [@1] The officers
stormed the boat and arrested its owner, a Dominican national. [@1] The arrest
was described as caught in the act at Vieux-Fort during the previous weekend.
[@1]

The account presented the interception as one in a run of drug landings bringing
arrests. [@1] Officers found more than 10 kilograms of cannabis herb packed in
parcels aboard. [@1] The landing spot was described as rocky and poorly lit, and
the gendarmerie intercepted the delivery at the last moment. [@1]

The suspect was immediately placed in police custody and then held in
provisional detention while the duty magistrate decided how to proceed. [@1] He
was to be tried by immediate appearance in the main town. [@1] Investigators
still had to identify his alleged accomplices in Guadeloupe who were to collect
the drugs. [@1]
