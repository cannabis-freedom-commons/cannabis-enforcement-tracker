Diyarbakır police anti-narcotics teams searched a vehicle on Silvan road as part
of citywide work targeting people and organisations involved in drug
trafficking. [@1] The search was carried out by teams of the Narkotik Suçlarla
Mücadele Şubesi, the police branch responsible for combating narcotics crime.
[@2] Three people, identified only by the initials D.B., D.Ö. and E.Y., were
taken into detention in connection with the incident, with gözaltı meaning
police detention rather than a court-ordered arrest. [@1]

Officers recovered 700 kilograms of kubar esrar, the Turkish term for herbal
cannabis in unprocessed form, from inside the searched vehicle. [@1] The
cannabis was reported as found in that single vehicle on Silvan road, rather
than in fields, buildings or additional locations. [@3]

Diyarbakır is the named province and city location of the drug operation that
produced this seizure. [@3] The account attributes the search, the seizure
weight and the detentions to a statement by the Police Directorate, the Emniyet
Müdürlüğü. [@1]
