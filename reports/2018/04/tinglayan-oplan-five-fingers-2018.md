Joint police and anti-narcotics teams uprooted and burned about 860,000 marijuana plants across 15 plantation sites in Tinglayan, Kalinga, during a three-day operation from 27 to 29 April 2018. [@1] Personnel of the Kalinga police, Regional Mobile Force Battalion 15 and PDEA Cordillera ran "Oplan Five Fingers Saga" through Barangays Buscalan and Loccong, hauling out 847,000 mature plants and 13,000 seedlings and burning them on site at a Dangerous Drugs Board value of 17.48 million pesos. [@1] Only token samples were kept for evidence in Tinglayan. [@1] No cultivator was arrested in the operation; regional director Edward Carranza said identities were being validated so charges could follow. [@1]

Carranza vowed the campaign would continue until the last cultivator, financier and pusher is jailed, framing the raid as part of the president's standing orders against drugs and crime. [@1]
