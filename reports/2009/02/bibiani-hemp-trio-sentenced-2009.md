The Bibiani Circuit Court, presided over by Emmanuel Ankomah, sentenced three people to a combined 30 years' imprisonment for possessing Indian hemp on 7 February 2009. [@1] Akwasi George, a 30-year-old mason, Fani Kalim, a 26-year-old driver's mate, and Sulemana Bukari, a 35-year-old farmer, had all pleaded not guilty and were each ordered to serve ten years. [@1]

The three had been staying at Garo, a village near the Ghana-Togo border. [@1] On 12 August 2008 at about 10 p.m., police in Bibiani received information that they held large quantities of suspected Indian hemp at Sefwi-Bekwai in the Western Region, detailed officers to the town, arrested them and found five cocoa sacks full of the substance on them. [@1] They were charged after police investigations. [@1]
