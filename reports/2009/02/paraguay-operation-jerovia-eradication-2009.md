Joint Paraguayan military and police forces deployed about four weeks earlier in central and northeastern Paraguay in pursuit of an armed group destroyed 8,900 kilos of marijuana and 400 hectares of the drug, Interior Minister Rafael Filizzola announced. [@1]

President Fernando Lugo and Filizzola presented the results at a press conference on 9 February, warning drug mafias and contraband gangs that the northern provinces of San Pedro, Concepción and Canindeyú would no longer be left as ungoverned zones. [@2] The task force, launched as Operation Jerovia to establish a permanent institutional presence in the north, took on the traffickers operating among the rural population there. [@2]

The daily ABC Color's account put the destroyed crop's value at $12 million and reported a large quantity of contraband weapons and cars confiscated alongside the marijuana. [@2]
