Montenegrin police arrested 25 people suspected of smuggling marijuana from
Albania, police spokeswoman Tamara Popovic said. [@1] A police officer and a
low-ranking government official were among those arrested, according to media
reports on 27 February 2009. [@1]

Popovic said the suspects belonged to seven crime clans that moved 2.5 tonnes of
"extra-strong marijuana also known as skunk" from Albania via Montenegro to
Serbia and Bosnia. [@1] Popovic said Albanian police had recently arrested
another six people in connection with the case in a joint action. [@1] The
indictment said the groups moved the drug across Montenegro, Albania, Bosnia and
Herzegovina, Croatia and Serbia. [@2]

Prosecutors alleged that Leon Gojčaj organised a group in the Tuzi area that
bought and moved skunk from Albania into Montenegro. [@2] In May 2011 a special
panel of judge Slavka Vukčević at the Higher Court in Podgorica sentenced 36
defendants to more than 220 years in prison in total over organised crime and
trafficking of about two and a half tonnes of "skunk" marijuana. [@2] Two
defendants, Željko M. Nikolić and Goran M. Šuškavčević, were acquitted. [@2]

In December 2008, before this bust, Montenegrin agencies had arrested 19 people
and seized some 550 kilograms of narcotics. [@1] The mountainous
Montenegro-Albania border is frequently used for trafficking drugs, weapons and
people. [@1]
