The Bibiani Circuit Court, presided over by Emmanuel Ankomah, sentenced 35-year-old farmer Muntali Keyebu to five years' imprisonment for possessing dried leaves suspected to be Indian hemp on 6 February 2009. [@1] He had pleaded not guilty. [@1]

In August 2008 police in Bibiani received a tip-off that people were selling and smoking Indian hemp at Agyina, a suburb of the town, and armed officers rushed to the scene and arrested the accused. [@1] Searching his room, they found four wrappers of dried leaves concealed in his pair of shoes. [@1] Keyebu told the court he often smoked the substance for energy, and he was charged after further interrogation. [@1]
