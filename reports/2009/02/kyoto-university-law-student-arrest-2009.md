A 20-year-old Kyoto University law student was arrested on 14 February 2009 after
walking into a police station in Osaka's Minami entertainment district with marijuana
in his pocket. [@1] Police identified him as Masataka Taniguchi, a sophomore in the
Faculty of Law, arrested at the station the previous Saturday. [@1] Kyoto University
confirmed the arrest six days later, saying a student had been held on suspicion of
violating the Cannabis Control Law and warning that drug cases would be dealt with
strictly, with grave consequences for a student's academic career. [@2] The arrest
came during a wave of cannabis cases at Japanese universities that had put student
marijuana use on the front pages. [@1]
