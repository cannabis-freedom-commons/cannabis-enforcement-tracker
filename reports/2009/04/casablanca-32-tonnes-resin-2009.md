Customs at the port of Casablanca seized late on 23 April 2009 more than 32.2 tons of
cannabis resin in three 40-foot containers bound for the port of Valencia in
Spain, the local MAP news agency reported the next day. [@1] The resin, known locally as
chira with a street value of 33 million dirhams (about $3.9 million), was concealed in
aluminium cans declared as used cans sent to a Spanish drink-can company. [@1] It was the
largest drug haul at the Casablanca port in five years, after a 16-ton cargo seized in
2005. [@1]

## Arrest and record

The forwarding agent was arrested pending interrogation. [@1] A later US diplomatic cable
recording Morocco's 2009 counternarcotics results described the April seizure at the
Casablanca port as a record 34 metric tons of cannabis seized and destroyed by customs
and police. [@2]
