Kanagawa police arrested second-division sumo wrestler Wakakirin, 25, together with
30-year-old musician Tsutomu Hirano, at the office of a CD shop in Tokyo's Roppongi
district on 30 January 2009. [@1,2] Officers found 16 grams of marijuana there while
investigating a separate drug case, and sent both men to prosecutors two days
later. [@2] Wakakirin, whose real name is Shinichi Suzukawa, admitted to police that
he had smoked marijuana. [@1] He first said he had smoked it for some time, then
changed his account to having smoked only twice, both times at that shop; police
noted he had twice tested positive in the association's urine tests the previous
September. [@2]

## Dismissal and guilty plea

On 2 February the Japan Sumo Association dismissed Wakakirin, the first Japanese
wrestler arrested in a cannabis case, stopping short of the harsher penalty of
expulsion. [@1,3] In April 2009 he pleaded guilty to cannabis possession in the
Yokohama court. [@4]
