Justice Datuk Syed Ahmad Helmy Syed Ahmad of the Malaysian High Court sentenced lorry driver Mohd Shuzairil Shamsuddin, 40, to death for drug trafficking. [@1] Shuzairil was found guilty of trafficking 511 grams of cannabis in Bandar Baru Ampang on 3 July 2006. [@1] "After scrutinising the evidence of all witnesses, I'm satisfied that the prosecution has proved the case beyond reasonable doubt and there is one penalty, death", the judge said. [@1] Seven prosecution witnesses and two defence witnesses gave evidence at the trial. [@1]
