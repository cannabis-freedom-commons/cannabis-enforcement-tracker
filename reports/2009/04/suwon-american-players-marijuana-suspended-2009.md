Three American professional basketball players, Terrence Shannon and DeAngelo Collins of the Seoul SK Knights and Calvin Warner of the Anyang KT&G Kites, were sentenced by the Suwon District Court on 13 April 2009 to six months in prison suspended for one year for smoking marijuana at a downtown Seoul hotel in January.[@1] Collins, who had left his team and South Korea with a knee injury, admitted the charge, while Shannon and Warner pleaded not guilty.[@1] Shannon and Warner had been expelled from their clubs in February.[@1]

## Prosecution

Local prosecutors gave the players hair tests: Collins and Shannon tested positive for marijuana while Warner's result was unreadable.[@2] Collins was cut by his team in late January and the marijuana suspicions surfaced a day later, and Shannon and Warner were cut from their respective teams in late February.[@2] All three were indicted for marijuana use in Seoul, though none had been formally convicted when the case was reported on 2 March.[@2]

## The law

Under South Korean law, marijuana users face up to five years in prison or a fine of up to 50 million won (about $33,300).[@1]
