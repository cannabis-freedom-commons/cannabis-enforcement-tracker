A Kuwait court sentenced Gareth Rutherford, a 50-year-old British civilian defence contractor, to life in prison for smuggling cannabis from Iraq, where he worked for a Kuwaiti shipping company holding a British military contract in Basra. [@1,2] He denied trying to smuggle 49 kg of hashish into Kuwait concealed in the tyre of a car, and had faced a possible death sentence. [@1,2]

Rutherford was arrested the previous September crossing into Kuwait with the drugs, alcohol and cash. [@1] His lawyer, Hassan al-Hotiri, said he would appeal the ruling. [@1,2] A Foreign Office spokeswoman said the life sentence meant 25 years and that the appeal would be heard within a month. [@1]
