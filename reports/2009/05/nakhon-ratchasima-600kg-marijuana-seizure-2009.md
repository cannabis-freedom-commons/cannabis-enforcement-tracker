Police intercepted an attempt to smuggle 600 kilogrammes of marijuana from
Mukdahan to Ayutthaya and arrested four suspects. [@1] Officers stopped the
four at a police checkpoint at a traffic flyover in Muang district, Nakhon
Ratchasima. [@1] Deputy Police Commissioner-General Jongrak Juthanon and
Provincial Police Bureau 3 Commissioner Krissada Phankhongchuen announced the
arrest at a press conference on 21 May 2009. [@1]

The four were identified as Thawee Phosim, 43, Adulsak Khampila, 40, Thiwa
Jungjun, 25, and Tim Phosim, 47. [@1] The drug was hidden inside a Toyota
van. [@1] Thawee told police the group had been hired to deliver the drug to
a person in Ayutthaya for 15,000 baht. [@1]
