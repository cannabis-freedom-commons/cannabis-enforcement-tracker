Paraguayan anti-drug agents arrested Brazilian Robson Albert Da cruz, 21, and Paraguayan Fidel
Aquino Paéz, 38, during an operation in a precarious forest camp at Capitán Bado in the early hours
of Saturday 9 May 2009. [@1,2] The Secretaría Nacional Antidrogas said officers seized about 4,000
kilograms of pressed marijuana along with ten hydraulic presses and an electric generator. [@1,2]

## Export branding

Senad noted that the wrapped blocks carried a “Pinito” label, which officials described as a mark
Brazilian buyers use to identify product from Amambay. [@1,2] Both outlets reported that the load
was intended for the Brazilian market. [@1,2]

## Transfer to custody

The detainees were taken to Senad’s office in Pedro Juan Caballero on the border with Brazil. [@1,2]
Officials said much of Paraguay’s cannabis grown near Capitán Bado supplies Brazil, Argentina and
Chile, with the town facing Coronel Sapucaia in Brazil’s Mato Grosso do Sul. [@1,2]
