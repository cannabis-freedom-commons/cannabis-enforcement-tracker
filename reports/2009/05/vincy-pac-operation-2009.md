Operation Vincy Pac, a two-week anti-crime operation involving hundreds of
personnel from the seven Regional Security System member countries plus
Trinidad and Tobago, destroyed more than 8.5 million marijuana plants,
including 7.3 million seedlings, in the mountains of St Vincent. [@3] Thirty
people were arrested, and 12 firearms with 395 rounds of ammunition were
recovered. [@3]

Three men were killed during the operation. Barbadian Dwayne O'Brian was shot
dead in Vermont on 21 May after shots were fired at police approaching the area
where he was believed to be, and two Vincentian nationals also died in the
alleged gun battle. [@2]

The operation began at 4am on Monday 18 May with the stated aims of capturing
foreign fugitives, recovering illegal firearms and eradicating marijuana
plantations. [@1] By 29 May six foreign nationals were in custody, including a
Jamaican who had overstayed his visa, two Barbadians wanted in their homeland
over drugs and murder, a Grenadian wanted for rape and two St Lucians who had
entered illegally. [@2]

## Why it mattered

Commissioner Keith Miller said fugitives met in the mountains were found
cultivating marijuana or among its cultivators, and that the success of the
operation would restore tranquillity for Vincentians. [@1] Prime Minister Ralph
Gonsalves, after a 21 May helicopter tour over burning plants and destroyed
shanties, said most growers were part of larger criminal enterprises and
announced a funded alternative-livelihood project for young men leaving the
trade. [@2] RSS coordinator Grantley Watson called it the largest operation in
the RSS's 27 years and the first joined by a non-member country. [@3]
