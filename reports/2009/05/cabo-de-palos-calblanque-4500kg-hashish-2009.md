Customs Surveillance vessels Arao and Cormorán boarded a planing boat approaching the Murcia
coast near Cabo de Palos on the night of 7 May 2009 and seized more than 4,500 kilos of
hashish. [@1] The 13-metre boat, fitted with four 250 hp engines and carrying two high-end
vehicles, was intercepted in the rugged roadless Calblanque area, where bales must be carried out
on foot; six foreign nationals were arrested. [@1]

The Civil Guard joined the continuing operation with helicopter and ground patrols, and the
agency said further arrests were expected. [@1]
