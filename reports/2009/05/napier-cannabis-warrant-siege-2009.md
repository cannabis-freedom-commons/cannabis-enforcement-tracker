On the morning of 7 May 2009 three police officers went to Jan Molenaar's house on Chaucer
Road South in Napier to serve a routine cannabis search warrant. [@3] Molenaar grabbed a gun
and ordered them out, then shot them as they left: Senior Constable Len Snee was killed, and
Senior Constables Bruce Miller and Grant Diver were seriously injured, along with a civilian,
Lenny Holmwood. [@1,3]

## The siege

Snee had been slain on Napier Hill while carrying out a search warrant for cannabis, and
Miller and Diver were grievously wounded. [@2] Police cordoned off the area and brought in
the Armed Offenders Squad and the Special Tactics Group to contain the well-armed gunman. [@3]
What followed was a siege of about two days that ended with Molenaar shooting himself. [@1,3]

Ten years later more than 250 people, including Snee's wife Vicki, his sons and his wider
whanau, gathered at the Napier Sailing Club to mark the anniversary of the siege. [@2]
