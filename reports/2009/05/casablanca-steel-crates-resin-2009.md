Police in Morocco uncovered more than 20 tonnes of cannabis resin, described as one
of the country's largest hashish hauls, hidden in steel crates bound for France, an
official said on Saturday 23 May 2009. [@1]

## Find and investigation

The crates were concealed in a consignment of machinery at the premises of an
international transportation company in Casablanca, Morocco's largest city and main
port. [@1] Police launched an investigation after the find on Friday 22 May and issued
arrest warrants for those implicated; no arrests were reported. [@1] The official added
that the final amount seized could exceed 24 tonnes. [@1]

## Context

Police gave no street value for this haul; the 32.2 tonnes seized at the port a month
earlier had been valued at three million euros (about $4.2 million). [@1] Moroccan
authorities seized 110.8 tonnes of hashish in 2008, slightly down on the previous year,
according to official figures. [@1]
