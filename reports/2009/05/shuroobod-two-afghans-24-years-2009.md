Tajikistan’s Supreme Court sentenced two Afghan nationals to 24 years in prison
each for drug trafficking. [@1] The sentencing account names them as Khairiddin
valadi Sulaymon and Nazir valadi Imonazar. [@1] A Russian account of the verdict
gives variant transliterations of the same surnames and calls them residents of
Afghanistan’s Badakhshan province bordering Tajikistan. [@3] The presiding
judge, Anwar Ahmadov, said they were found guilty of illegal border crossing to
smuggle a large drug consignment and illegal possession of weapons. [@3] Border
guards arrested the pair on 24 December 2008 in the border village of Porvor in
Shuroobod district. [@2] Criminal proceedings were then instituted against them
while an investigation continued. [@2]

Officers confiscated about 84 kilograms of narcotics, including about 30
kilograms of cannabis, 48 kilograms of hashish and six kilograms of heroin, as
well as weapons. [@1] This report records 78 kilograms as the cannabis
component, combining the stated cannabis and hashish, and excludes the heroin
and the combined total from cannabis figures. [@1] The initial arrest account
had reported more than 100 kilograms of drugs and two Kalashnikov sub-machine
guns seized. [@2] A Russian account of the sentence reported about 84 kilograms
including six kilograms of heroin, two Kalashnikov rifles, 10 magazines and 300
cartridges. [@3]

The main border directorate within the State Committee for National Security
(GKNB), Tajikistan’s security service, said the operation was carried out by
border guards in Khatlon’s Shuroobod district. [@2] Its spokesman Khoushnoud
Rahmatulloyev said a preliminary investigation found the two were involved in
drug trafficking and kidnapping Tajik nationals to clear debts. [@2] He said the
arrest of the men would promote stabilization in the region. [@2] Shuroobod is
described as the most vulnerable part of Tajikistan’s common border with
Afghanistan. [@2]
