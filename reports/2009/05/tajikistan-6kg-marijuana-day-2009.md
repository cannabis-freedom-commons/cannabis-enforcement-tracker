The Interior Ministry recorded five drug-trafficking cases in Tajikistan on 19
May 2009 and seized about 17 kg of narcotics. [@1] The figures came from the
Interior Ministry, as reported to the Asia-Plus news agency. [@1] An Afghan
citizen was detained in Pyanj district of Khatlon region, where a personal
search found about 11 kg of raw opium. [@1] A local resident, Sirojiddin
Nazarov, was detained the same day in Hamadoni district of Khatlon region, and 4
kg of marijuana was found and seized from a bag he was carrying. [@1]

About 2 kg more marijuana was seized in three further detentions in Danghara
district, Ishkashim district and Dushanbe city, bringing the day's marijuana
seizures to about 6 kg. [@1] Only the separately stated marijuana weights are
counted as cannabis here; the raw opium and the combined 17 kg total are left
out. [@1]

The ministry's officers were described as militia, the term then used for
Tajikistan's police. [@1] More than 1.5 tonnes of narcotics had been seized from
illegal circulation in the first four months of 2009. [@1]
