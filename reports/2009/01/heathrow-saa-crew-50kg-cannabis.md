The pilot and the entire crew of South African Airways flight SA234 from Johannesburg,
which landed at Heathrow at 8:00 am on Tuesday 20 January 2009, were arrested after UK
Border Agency officers found 50 kilos of cannabis and four kilos of cocaine in three
pieces of baggage at a crew customs clearance point. [@1] HM Revenue and Customs
spokesman Bob Gaiger said the 15 crew members, including the pilot, were held in
custody and interviewed by HMRC investigators. [@1] SAA confirmed all crew on the
Johannesburg-London flight were detained and said they were being questioned to
identify the owner of the bags. [@1]

## Follow-up

The drugs were worth an estimated £150,000, and a joint investigation by SAA aviation
security and the South African police crime intelligence unit began in Johannesburg.
[@1] The crew could not be linked to individual bags and were released on unconditional
bail, ordered to report back to a Heathrow police station on 23 March. [@2] A security
guard working for an aviation protection firm and a cabin crew member were subsequently
arrested in Johannesburg; the guard, Pulane Hlahane, 43, confessed to helping a cabin
crew member smuggle the consignment out of the country. [@2,3]
