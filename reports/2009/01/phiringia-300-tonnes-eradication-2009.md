A joint team of police and excise officials destroyed nearly 300 tonnes of cannabis
plants, worth more than Rs 50 crore, cultivated across 58 villages of Phiringia block
in Kandhamal district. [@1] The crop covered about 20 sq km in three panchayats of the
block, where traders from outside the state used local tribals to grow hemp in dense
forest, according to official sources. [@1]

## Arrests and reaction

Two local sarpanchs were arrested for apparently patronising ganja cultivation in the
Kandhamal area. [@1] The echoes of the raids reached the state assembly, where
opposition members alleged the crop was being grown with a minister's knowledge. [@1]
