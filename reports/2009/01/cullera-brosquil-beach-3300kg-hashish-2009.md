Spain's Tax Agency seized 3,300 kilos of hashish on Brosquil beach in Cullera,
Valencia, on 15 January 2009, after its aerial surveillance team located a
large inflatable boat laden with hashish heading for the Valencian coast south
of Alicante the previous afternoon. [@1] Officers monitored and tracked the
vessel until it reached the beach. [@1]

The facts were reported to Sueca Magistrates' Court No. 4, and the
investigation remained open, with the agency saying arrests in the coming
hours could not be ruled out. [@1] Customs Surveillance officers had seized
about 8,000 kilos of hashish on the Levante coast since the start of 2009
under a new strategic plan for the department's resources. [@1]
