Spanish police seized 11,000 kilograms of hashish from an underground parking lot in Seville in January 2009. [@1] The US embassy in Madrid, reporting on Spain's drug year, called it what would prove the security forces' largest operation of 2009. [@1] The seizure opened a year of heavy hashish interceptions across Spain: 7,000 kilos followed in February, 2,100 in Ibiza and 3,000 more in a boat near Cadiz in April, and 13,750 kilos in five operations in June. [@1]

Spain remained the major transit point to Europe for hashish from Morocco, and its police, Civil Guard and customs service raised their operational tempo through the year even as total hashish seizures fell to about half of 2008 levels by September. [@1]
