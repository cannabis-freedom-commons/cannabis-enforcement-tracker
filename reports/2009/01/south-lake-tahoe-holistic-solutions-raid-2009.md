The Drug Enforcement Administration raided the Holistic Solutions medical cannabis dispensary in South Lake Tahoe on Thursday 22 January 2009, in the first days of the Obama administration. [@1,2] Agents seized medical cannabis and an unknown amount of cash, and made no arrests. [@1,2,3] It was the first dispensary raid of the new administration, carried out while Bush-era officials including acting DEA Administrator Michele Leonhart were still in charge. [@1,2] Obama had said repeatedly as a candidate that he would end such raids. [@2]
