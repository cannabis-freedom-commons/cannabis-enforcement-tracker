West Midlands Police's Serious and Organised Crime Unit seized three tonnes of cannabis resin from
a property on the outskirts of Coventry on Tuesday 15 July 2008, in what the force believed was
its largest cannabis seizure. [@1,2] The resin sat in six pallets, each holding 500 kg of cannabis
resin bars, with a wholesale value of £2.5 million and a potential street value of £9.5 million;
forensic teams began examining the drugs. [@2] About £20,000 in cash was also seized from the
property. [@1]

Dean Merrick, 41, of Bedworth, Warwickshire, was charged with possessing cannabis resin with
intent to supply and was remanded in custody after appearing at Birmingham Crown Court. [@1]
Merrick, a roofer of Church Street, Bulkington, later pleaded guilty and was sentenced to seven
years in prison. [@3] The court heard officers had seized the three tonnes from two lockable units
Merrick rented, where he acted as warehouseman receiving and storing drug pallets. [@3] Police
called it the biggest cannabis seizure ever made by the force and believed it was the largest
resin haul recovered by any British force that year. [@3] Sentencing him, Judge Robert Orme said
it was by any standards a very substantial amount of cannabis resin. [@3]
