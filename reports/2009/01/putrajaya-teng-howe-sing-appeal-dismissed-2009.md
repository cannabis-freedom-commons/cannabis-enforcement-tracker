The Federal Court in Putrajaya dismissed Teng Howe Sing's final appeal and
affirmed his conviction and mandatory death sentence for trafficking 371.12
grams of cannabis. [@1,2] Federal Court judge Datuk Zulkefli Ahmad
Makinudin said the court did not wish to disturb the findings of the lower
courts, which had both concluded Teng knew the drugs were in his
possession. [@1,2]

Teng, 31, a former college student, was charged over two compressed blocks
of dried leaves collected from the Skynet courier office in Sibu at about
7.50pm on 17 March 1999 and seized in front of the WWW Chat Cafe in Jalan
Kai Peng. [@1,2] Police lying in ambush shouted "Polis, jangan lari" as
Teng and another man walked toward a motorcycle; Teng threw away the parcel
and was arrested, while the other man, Moh Yong Ming, turned prosecution
witness. [@1,2] Teng had claimed the parcel with a consignment note signed
under the fictitious name Ling Hang Wei, and the chemist confirmed the
contents were cannabis. [@1,2] The courts applied the statutory presumption
of trafficking, which Teng failed to rebut with reasonable doubt. [@1,2]

Teng showed no emotion as the sentence was reaffirmed, then spoke with his
parents before officers led him to prison. [@1]
