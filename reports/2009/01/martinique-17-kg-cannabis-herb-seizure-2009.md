Seventeen kilograms of cannabis herb were seized in Martinique. [@1]
France-Antilles reported the seizure on 9 January 2009 in its Martinique and
Guadeloupe editions, describing an uninterrupted hunt for traffickers with no
truce in the chase. [@1]

The Martinique edition noted that in 2008 the drug squad had seized nearly 18
kilograms of cocaine or crack and 160 kilograms of cannabis herb. [@2]
