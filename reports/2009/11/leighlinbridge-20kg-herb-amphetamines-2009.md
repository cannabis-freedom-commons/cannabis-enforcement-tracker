Gardaí from the Organised Crime Unit and Carlow station, supported by the National Drugs Unit, searched a house near Leighlinbridge, County Carlow, on the afternoon of Saturday 28 November 2009. [@1,2] They seized 20 kg of suspected cannabis herb and 8 kg of suspected amphetamines, which gardaí said could have a street value of up to €400,000 subject to analysis. [@1,2] A 21-year-old man arrested at the scene was detained at Carlow station under section 2 of the Drug Trafficking Act, where he could be held for up to seven days. [@1,2]
