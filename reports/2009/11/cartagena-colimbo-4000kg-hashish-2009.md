Spain's Customs Surveillance service seized 4,000 kilograms of hashish from a 12-metre motor yacht about 60 nautical miles south-east of Cartagena on 22 November 2009. [@1] The Colimbo IV patrol vessel spotted the suspicious yacht at around 4 pm and boarded it, finding 128 burlap sacks of hashish piled on the deck and in the open areas of the vessel. [@1] The agency put the black-market value of the haul at 5,528,000 euros. [@1]

Officers detained six people aboard and said most of the crew and the owner already had police records for drug smuggling. [@1] The drugs, the detainees and the yacht were taken to Cartagena and handed over to the duty investigating magistrate's court. [@1] The agency said investigations were continuing to identify and arrest the remaining members of the organisation. [@1]
