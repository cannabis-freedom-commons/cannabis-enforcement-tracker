Police raided a cottage in Girton at 8am after a tip-off from a member of the
public, seizing more than 400 skunk plants with a potential street value of
£112,000. [@2] Officers found more than 70 plants growing in makeshift hanging
baskets suspended from ceilings in various rooms and in a secret bolthole built
from wood and plastic. [@1,2]

Cambridgeshire police drugs expert PC Gavin Guy called the method experimental
and unlike anything he had seen: the baskets were made from bin liners with
holes cut in the sides so that plants could grow outwards in small spaces.
[@2] The neighbourhood policing team made the find, and a 28-year-old man was
arrested. [@2]
