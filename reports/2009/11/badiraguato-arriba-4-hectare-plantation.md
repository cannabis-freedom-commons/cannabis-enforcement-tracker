Soldiers of the 42nd Infantry Battalion found a marijuana plantation of 40,000
square metres, about 4 hectares, near the village of Arriba in the municipality
of Badiraguato, Sinaloa, on 25 November 2009. [@1] The troops were carrying out
ground reconnaissance near the village when they came on the plantation. [@1]

The plants were about 1.2 metres tall and grew at a stated density of 15 plants
per square metre, which the Defence Ministry's own figures put at some 600,000
plants over the 40,000 square metres. [@1] Soldiers destroyed the plantation by
manual work and burned it where it was found. [@1] No one was arrested in the
operation. [@1]

The Secretariat of National Defence said the find was made under the Permanent
Campaign Against Drug Trafficking and the enforcement of the Federal Firearms
and Explosives Act. [@1]
