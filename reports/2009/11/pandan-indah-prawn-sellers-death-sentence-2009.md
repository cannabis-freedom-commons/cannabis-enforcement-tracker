The Shah Alam High Court sentenced prawn sellers Halim Nor, 45, and Sabri
Alias, 38, to death for trafficking 36,220 grams of cannabis. [@1] Justice
Datuk Mohd Zaki Md Yasin found both men had failed to raise any reasonable
doubt against the prosecution's case, rejecting their claim that they had not
known of the drugs' existence. [@1]

They had been charged with committing the offence at DOHC Power Auto Services
in Pandan Indah, Ampang, at about 4.15pm on 20 October 2001, eight years
before the verdict. [@1] Twelve defence witnesses gave statements during the
trial, while the defence produced two witnesses of its own. [@1] Deputy Public
Prosecutor Alfred Egin prosecuted; Ghazali Taib represented Halim and Rusli
Zain represented Sabri. [@1]
