NDLEA officers arrested Sulaiman Adebayo at his home in Odeda, Ogun State, after finding tonnes of cannabis stored behind his house. [@1,2] Reuters reported on 1 April 2009 that the agency had confiscated 6.5 tonnes in 254 sacks the previous day, quoting NDLEA chairman Ahmadu Giade as saying the quantity pointed to large-scale involvement going beyond the old man. [@1]

Adebayo, a lifelong farmer, told police he thought the sacks held rice. [@1] An AFP report described the same arrest after a tip-off, putting the haul at 100 bags, and named the Ogun commander as Chinyere Obijuru. [@2] Adebayo claimed he was 114, saying he had attended the inauguration of a hall in Abeokuta as a small boy in 1895; Reuters noted there was no independent confirmation of his age. [@1]
