Members of the Special Operation Section executed a search warrant at an
8-hectare property in Marrakai at about 10:30 am on 25 March 2009 and found a
hydroponics setup in the residential area of the property. [@1] Officers seized
118.8 grams of loose cannabis material, about 20 seedlings, growing equipment
and a large cannabis plant weighing 2.36 kilograms. [@1] They also recovered a
12-gauge double-barrel shotgun, a .308 rifle with telescopic sight, a knife, a
hand grenade, ammunition, military ordnance and a crocodile skull. [@1]

A 47-year-old man was arrested and charged with seven offences, including
cultivating a prohibited drug, possessing a trafficable quantity of cannabis,
unlicensed firearm possession, failing to meet storage requirements, carrying a
prohibited weapon, possessing stolen property and possessing protected
wildlife; he was bailed to appear in Darwin Magistrates Court on 7 April
2009. [@1] A 37-year-old man was to be summonsed to the same court at a later
date for cultivating a trafficable quantity of a prohibited drug and possessing
cannabis. [@1]
