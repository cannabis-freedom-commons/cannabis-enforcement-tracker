An unidentified Nigerian man flew into Beijing Capital International Airport
from Lagos on 2 March 2009 and abandoned a suitcase at the airport, fearing
tight security. [@1,2] When officers frisked him at the security check, they
found only a bag with clothes and $1,600 in cash. [@1] He returned the next day
to claim the suitcase and was detained. [@1,2]

Inside were 72 bricks of marijuana wrapped in black plastic bags, weighing
87.25 kg. [@1,2] Beijing Customs called it the biggest drug haul in China that
year, and said smugglers were using more sophisticated methods while pledging
continued crackdowns. [@1,2]
