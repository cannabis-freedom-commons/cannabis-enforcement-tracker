Nearly 6,000 cannabis plants were removed after police raids in the top of the South Island, the
Otago Daily Times reported on Sunday 15 March 2009. [@1] Nelson Bays officers searched by ground and
air with an Air Force Iroquois helicopter and found more than 165 plots. [@1]

Police cut down more than 5,700 plants, some almost four metres high; officers attributed the height
to good growing conditions. [@1] Sixteen people were arrested in Nelson and five in Marlborough.
[@1] Sergeant Steve Savage said large plots appeared to be run on a significant commercial scale and
that “this year we’ve noticed it more than ever.” [@1] The brief report does not list charges or
individual defendants. [@1]
