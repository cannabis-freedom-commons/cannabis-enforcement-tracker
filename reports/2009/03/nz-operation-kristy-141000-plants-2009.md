New Zealand police seized 141,000 cannabis plants and arrested 1,125 offenders during Operation
Kristy, the National Cannabis and Crime Operation that ran from December 2008 to March 2009.
[@1] Officers also seized 191 firearms, recovered $400,000 worth of stolen property and located
four methamphetamine laboratories. [@1]

The haul was the highest in ten years, up 17,000 plants on the previous season, Detective Senior
Sergeant Scott McGill told police magazine Ten One. [@2] The potential community harm was put at
$379 million, based on a conservative estimate of 226 grams of cannabis per plant, and police
searched more than 820 houses and properties during the season. [@2]

## Cultivation-led increase

The year's increase in cannabis offences was almost entirely driven by cultivation, supply and
possession offences detected through the extra resources invested in Kristy. [@1]
