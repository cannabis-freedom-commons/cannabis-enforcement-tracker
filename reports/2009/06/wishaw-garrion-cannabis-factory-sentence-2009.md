Thomas McCulloch, 51, was jailed for five years at the High Court in Glasgow
on 22 June 2009 for producing cannabis at a warehouse on the Garrion Business
Park in Wishaw. [@1] He was found guilty of acting with another in being
concerned in the production of cannabis between 1 December 2005 and 17
September 2006. [@1]

Police found 2,000 plants that could have produced £600,000 of cannabis per
crop, alongside equipment set up to grow a new crop almost six times a year, a
potential £3.6 million of herbal cannabis. [@1] Officers had uncovered the
factory after 15-year-old Dean Cannon fell 30 ft through the warehouse roof in
September 2006 and died; the 2006 account put that haul's value at up to
£500,000. [@1,2] A two-storey timber building had been constructed inside the
factory to hide its heat from police helicopter infrared cameras. [@1]

McCulloch claimed he had rented the unit as a base for a mobile car-valeting
business and sublet it to two men in March 2006, denying he had ever seen the
plants or equipment. [@1]
