On 4 June 2009, Jilin provincial police announced the roundup of 55 suspects in
a marijuana growing, processing and trafficking network spanning 19 cities in
nine provinces and regions, including Xinjiang, Chongqing and Heilongjiang. [@1,2]
Acting on a tip received in June 2008, officers found that twelve farming
households had planted 12.6 hectares of marijuana in Yongjiu township near
Songyuan, and traced a total of 600 hectares of planted marijuana, with the
earliest planting dating back to 2004. [@1]

Police confiscated 1,400 kg of refined marijuana and 6,200 kg of semi-finished
marijuana, along with 300,000 yuan in drug money, five vans and drug-making
equipment. [@1] The growers were ordinary farming families in Changling County:
Zhang You began planting hemp in the spring of 2007 on about a quarter of a
hectare, and by 2008 twelve related households across three villages were
involved, moving the crop with contacts from Xinjiang. [@2]

The key arrests came in January 2009, including Alim, a Xinjiang native, caught
in a drug deal in Changchun on 9 January, and members of Zhang You's family
detained in the days after. [@2] Zhang's daughter, a mother of two young
children, was released on bail the day after her arrest. [@2]
