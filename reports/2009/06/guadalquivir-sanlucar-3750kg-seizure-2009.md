Spain's Tax Agency seized 3,750 kilos of hashish in three operations at the
mouth of the Guadalquivir between 26 and 30 June 2009, part of a special
coastal surveillance deployment in Andalusia. [@1]

On the night of 26 June, air, naval and land units intercepted a 12-metre
inflatable boat running dark and fast toward the river mouth, then moved in as
its crew unloaded at the Bonanza dock in Sanlúcar de Barrameda, recovering 13
bales weighing 390 kilos. [@1] In the early hours of 28 June, officers
stopped a second 12-metre boat approaching the Real Fernando jetty, also in
Sanlúcar, and found 76 bales weighing about 2,280 kilos inside a trailer
towed by an off-road vehicle. [@1]

## The Chipiona recovery

In the early hours of 30 June off Punta Montijano in Chipiona, a patrol boat
boarded an inflatable under surveillance that fled, throwing bales overboard;
36 bales weighing about 1,080 kilos were recovered from the water. [@1] The
agency said investigations were continuing and arrests could not be ruled
out; the drugs were placed at the disposal of the duty Investigating
Magistrate's Court in Sanlúcar de Barrameda. [@1]
