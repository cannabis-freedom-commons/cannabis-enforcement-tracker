In June 2009 the Moroccan government seized 20 metric tons of cannabis resin
during an inspection at the port of Nador, the largest load ever seized there.
[@1] The embassy's counternarcotics report places the seizure two months after
customs and police seized and destroyed a record 34 metric tons of cannabis at
the port of Casablanca in April. [@1]

## Tighter port controls

The report says Moroccan forces were using helicopters, planes, speed boats,
mobile x-ray scanners, ultrasound equipment and satellites in the drug fight,
and that the mobile x-ray scanner had proven particularly effective. [@1]
Detection training and ultrasound equipment were critical to the success of
seizures such as Nador's, according to the report. [@1]
