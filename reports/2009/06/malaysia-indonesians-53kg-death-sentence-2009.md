Malaysia's High Court sentenced two Indonesian men, aged 28 and 30, to death by hanging after finding them guilty of trafficking 53 kilograms of cannabis carried in the boot of their car two years earlier.[@1] The judge said trafficking carried a mandatory death sentence once the prosecution proved its case beyond reasonable doubt.[@1]

The two men came from Aceh province in Indonesia.[@1] Police had asked their car to stop in September 2007, and after they failed to stop, a high-speed chase followed.[@1] Their car then crashed, and both men ran from the vehicle and dived into a nearby river before police arrested them shortly afterwards.[@1]
