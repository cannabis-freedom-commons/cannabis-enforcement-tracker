The anti-narcotics department seized a large quantity of drugs in North Gaza
Governorate on the evening of Friday 10 July 2009. [@1] First Lieutenant Munir
Musbah of the anti-narcotics department put the seizure at 200 farsh of hashish,
with farsh used as the unit for slabs of hashish. [@1]

Musbah valued the 200 slabs at 250,000 dollars. [@1] The operation began when
officers seized 51 slabs in the possession of one dealer. [@1] During
questioning that dealer guided anti-narcotics officers to two other dealers, in
whose possession officers seized the remainder of the quantity, making three
dealers caught in total. [@1]

The operation lasted four days from the arrest of the first dealer and passed
through stages of planning, surveillance and execution. [@1] The account
describes this as the first seizure of this size in the North governorate. [@1]
