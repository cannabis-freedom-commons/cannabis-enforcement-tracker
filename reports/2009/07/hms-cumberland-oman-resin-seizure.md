The Royal Navy frigate HMS Cumberland's boarding team searched a suspicious dhow off the coast of Oman on 7 July and seized 12 tonnes of cannabis resin with an estimated UK street value of more than £43 million. [@1] The ship was on a Combined Task Force 150 counter-narcotics patrol in the Gulf of Aden after sailing from Plymouth on 24 May for a six-and-a-half-month deployment against drugs and piracy. [@1]

The British Ministry of Defence put the haul at 12.4 tons of cannabis resin worth $70 million, enough for about 35 million marijuana cigarettes, and a UN drugs research officer called it the largest single seizure of cannabis resin on record. [@2] The crew found the drugs packed as large bales in a secret compartment of a cargo vessel, and the shipment was believed to be bound from the Middle East toward Europe. [@1,2] The seized drugs were later destroyed. [@2]
