Customs officers in Moroni found more than 26 kg of cannabis herb hidden in a
lorry carrying chillies. [@1] The lorry had been stopped at the customs gate so
that officers could establish who owned its load. [@1] A woman in her forties
presented herself as the owner and the vehicle and the woman were taken into the
customs warehouse for investigation. [@1] While the officers withdrew to another
office to confer before examining the parcels, the woman fled, and she was still
being sought. [@1]

Four buckets of more than six kilograms each were found to hold blocks of
cannabis mixed in with the chillies. [@1] Customs brigade chief Anfane Mohamed
said the cannabis had been loaded onto the boat Shisiwani 2, which arrived from
Dar es Salaam on 26 July 2009. [@1] Anfane Mohamed said the cannabis was valued
at 26 million Comorian francs. [@2] The drugs were destroyed by fire on the
orders of the prosecutor, the brigade chief said. [@2]

The investigating officers said the penalty for this kind of case is
confiscation of the smuggled goods, the means of transport and the objects used
to mask the fraud, a fine of twice the value of the goods and up to three months
in prison. [@1] The woman, who was identified as living in the Irungudjani
district of Moroni, had not returned home, and the gendarmes continued to search
for her. [@2]
