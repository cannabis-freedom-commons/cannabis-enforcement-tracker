Albanian police seized more than 200 kilograms of marijuana in Lazarat, a southern village described as one of the biggest marijuana producers in the country. [@1]

The operation took place on a Saturday and started from a tipoff received from within the village. [@1] The police did not manage to detain the suspected ringleaders of the drug ring, who managed to escape, and no arrests were reported. [@1]

Albania is considered a main producer of marijuana, or cannabis sativa, on the continent. [@1]
