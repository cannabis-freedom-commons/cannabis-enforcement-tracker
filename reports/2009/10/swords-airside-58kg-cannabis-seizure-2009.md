Gardaí from the Garda National Drugs Unit seized drugs with an estimated street value of €750,000 at Swords on the afternoon of 17 October 2009 as part of an intelligence-led operation. [@1] Officers intercepted two vehicles, a car and a truck, containing 49 kilos of what is believed to be cannabis pollen and 9 kilos of cannabis herb, subject to analysis, at the Airside Business Park at about 12.30pm. [@1]

Two males, aged 44 and 41, were arrested at the scene and were detained at Swords and Malahide Garda stations respectively under Section 2 of the Drug Trafficking Act 1996. [@1]
