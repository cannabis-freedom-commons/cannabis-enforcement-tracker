The head of the Crimean militia, Gennadiy Moskal, presented a marijuana harvest
he called a record find in the Kirov steppe district of Crimea. [@1] On
12 September, officers had found 673 bushes weighing more than two tons, with
an estimated value of about $2 million. [@1]

Moskal said the plants were not a local variety but came from seeds brought
from Denmark, and that growers had set up special greenhouse complexes. [@1]
The seized harvest was stored in the basement of the militia headquarters in
Simferopol under guard. [@1]
