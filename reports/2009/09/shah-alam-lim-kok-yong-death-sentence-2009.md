The Shah Alam High Court on 2 September 2009 sentenced tyre-shop assistant
Lim Kok Yong, 24, to hang for trafficking 625.7 grams of cannabis five years
earlier. [@1] Judge Su Geok Yiam said she was satisfied that the prosecution
had proved its case beyond reasonable doubt. [@1] She told the court there was
only one sentence for the offence: death by hanging. [@1]

Lim committed the offence at a house in Pandamaran, Port Klang, around
10.45pm on 4 November 2004. [@1] He was charged under section 39B(1)(a) of the
Dangerous Drugs Act 1952, which carries the mandatory death sentence on
conviction. [@1] The prosecution called seven witnesses during the trial and
the defence three. [@1] Lim stood calm in the dock as the sentence was
passed. [@1]

A campaign report against Malaysia's cannabis death sentences the following
January confirmed that Lim was sentenced to hang on 2 September for 625.7
grams from five years before, one of several Malaysians facing execution for
cannabis amounts as small as half a kilogram. [@2]
