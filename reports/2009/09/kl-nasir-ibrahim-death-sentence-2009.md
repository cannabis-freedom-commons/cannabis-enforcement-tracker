The Kuala Lumpur High Court on 30 September 2009 gave Indonesian Nasir
Ibrahim, 31, the mandatory death sentence for trafficking 868 grams of
cannabis more than five years earlier. [@1] Judicial Commissioner Zainal Azman
Ab Aziz found that the defence had failed to raise any doubt in the case.
[@1] He said Nasir's defence statement was made up and could not be accepted.
[@1]

Nasir was charged with committing the offence at a roadblock in Taman Wilayah
at 4am on 7 January 2004. [@1] A campaign report against Malaysia's cannabis
death sentences the following January confirmed that Nasir, an Indonesian,
was sentenced on 30 September for 868 grams from more than five years
before. [@2]

The same sitting saw the same judge acquit M. Muniyaretnam, 45, of a separate
charge of trafficking 255 grams of cannabis at a noodle factory on Jalan
Kasipillay in January 2007, finding the prosecution had failed to establish a
prima facie case. [@1]
