Gardaí searching business premises at Greenogue Business Park in Rathcoole, County Dublin, on the evening of 1 September 2009 found a large stock of cannabis resin. [@1,3,4] The resin had an estimated street value of over €2 million, subject to analysis. [@1,2] Officers watched the men park at a truckers' facility in the business park for a 9pm handover before moving in. [@3]

Three men aged 27, 35 and 36, with addresses in Tallaght, Newcastle and Clondalkin, were arrested and detained at Clondalkin Garda station under Section 2 of the Criminal Justice (Drug Trafficking) Act 1996. [@1,3,4] Gardaí linked the men to a major Clondalkin crime gang and said one of them had become its leader after its former head was imprisoned. [@3] The three were due in Blanchardstown Court on 4 September to be charged. [@5]

Gardaí put the seized resin on display on 2 September; a photograph was offered only on request from the Garda Press Office. [@2]
