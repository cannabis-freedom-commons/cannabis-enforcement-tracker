The High Court in Johor Bahru sentenced 25-year-old Khalil Anuar Sukirman to death
for drug trafficking on 4 September 2009. [@1,2] He had been charged with
trafficking 1,942.9 grams of cannabis at Jalan Dataran 4/5, Taman Kempas, at
around 12.30am on 4 October 2006. [@1]

The drugs were found inside a bag in the basket of the motorbike he was riding,
and the bag held two compressed slabs of ganja leaves wrapped in foil and
cellophane tape. [@1] Judicial Commissioner Datuk Mohd Zawawi Salleh held that the
defence had failed to cast doubt on the prosecution's case and sentenced him to
death. [@1,2] He had been charged under Section 39B(1)(a) of the Dangerous Drugs
Act 1952, which carries the mandatory death penalty. [@1] Deputy public prosecutor
Laila Lateh prosecuted, while lawyer Bustaman Menon represented the accused. [@1]
