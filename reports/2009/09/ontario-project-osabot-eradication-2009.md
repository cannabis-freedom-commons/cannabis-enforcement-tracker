Five police services, supported by the Department of National Defence, teamed
up to eradicate outdoor marijuana grows across southwestern Ontario between
14 and 18 September 2009 in an initiative dubbed Project OSABOT.[@1] The
Marihuana Eradication Team brought together officers from the Brantford
Police Service, the Hamilton Police Service, the Niagara Regional Police, the
Ontario Provincial Police and the Royal Canadian Mounted Police.[@1]

The team targeted outdoor grows throughout the Hamilton and Niagara regions
and Brant, Norfolk and Haldimand counties.[@1] About 4,700 marijuana plants
were seized and destroyed over the five days, with an estimated value of
close to 4.7 million dollars.[@1] Police said pooling information, people and
equipment made the seizures larger and more cost-effective, and that similar
teams had run earlier phases out of Kitchener, Kingston and London.[@1]
