The High Court in Kota Bharu sentenced Thai woman Roseedah Cheubong, 41, to death by
hanging for trafficking cannabis five years earlier.[@1] She shed tears in the courtroom
in the north-eastern state of Kelantan after the sentence was passed, the official
Bernama news agency said.[@1]

Judge Datuk Muhamad Ideres Muhamad Rapee imposed the mandatory death sentence after the
prosecution proved its case against her.[@2] She had been caught with just over 1 kg of
cannabis while waiting in front of a petrol station in February 2004.[@1] The court
recorded the trafficking weight as 1.04 kg of marijuana.[@2] Her lawyer told the court
she was a single mother trying to support herself and her teenage child and that she was
sorry, while the deputy prosecutor called for the mandated penalty because of the
gravity of the offence.[@2] She had been jailed since her arrest in February 2004, and no
appeal had been reported.[@2]
