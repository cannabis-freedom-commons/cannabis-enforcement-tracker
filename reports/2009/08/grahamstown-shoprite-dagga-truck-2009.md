A truckload of dagga with an estimated street value of R1.2 million was confiscated after police stopped the truck behind the Shoprite at the Market Square Mall in Grahamstown on the afternoon of 27 August 2009.[@1] According to the police duty officer, Superintendent Martha Mankayi, police had received information they had been following, and Crime Intelligence inspectors Dawie Barnard and Marius Herselman, working an anti-crime operation, tailed the truck and stopped it in the supermarket parking lot.[@1]

In the back of the white truck officers found 24 bags containing dagga.[@1] Police arrested one suspect found in the vehicle, a young-looking man who was guarded by two rifle-armed officers; the truck was believed to be from the Free State.[@1] A crowd of shoppers watched as a local tow company's truck proved too light for the laden dagga truck, so officers unloaded bags by hand into a police bakkie while onlookers counted aloud and photographed the scene on their cellphones.[@1]
