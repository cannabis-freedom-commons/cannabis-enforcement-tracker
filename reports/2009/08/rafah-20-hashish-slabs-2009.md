Anti-narcotics police in Rafah governorate in the southern Gaza Strip arrested a
man they described as a hashish dealer and referred him for investigation. [@1]
Police said the announcement was made on the morning of Wednesday 5 August 2009.
[@1]

Anti-narcotics sources said they had received information that a person was
holding hashish at one of the suspicious locations. [@1] A force from the
anti-narcotics police went straight to the place and raided it. [@1] Officers
found 20 slabs of hashish there. [@1] They arrested the person found holding the
hashish and handed him over to investigation. [@1]
