Police in Orissa's Rayagada district raided several villages and destroyed
nearly 20,000 cannabis plants cultivated illegally in forest areas. [@1]
Excise Inspector Siba Prasad Gantayat said officers uprooted about 20,000
hemp plants and destroyed them in the presence of an executive magistrate. [@1]
He put the approximate value of the destroyed crop at Rs 20 million (two crore
rupees). [@1] The authorities suspected Maoists of being behind the
cultivation. [@1]
