North Jakarta police announced on Monday 21 December 2009 that they had
arrested a man for alleged possession of 90 kilograms of marijuana. [@1,2]
Ra bin Daud, 30, was arrested in his rented room in Koja, North Jakarta, with
the contraband, which he allegedly got from Aceh. [@1,2]

The drugs were found wrapped and duct-taped inside his closet, with an
estimated street value of Rp 225 million (US$23,000), police chief Senior
Commissioner Rudi Sufahriadi said. [@1] Narcotics division chief Commissioner
Suparmo said a kilogram of marijuana could fetch up to Rp 2.4 million. [@1]
Daud told police this was the third batch, and that the first two batches of
98 and 140 kilos had both sold out. [@2]
