Shahrul Izani Suparman was 19 when he was stopped at a roadblock in Klang, Selangor in September 2003 after officers found 622 grams of cannabis in the basket of a borrowed motorcycle on which he was riding. [@1,2,3] Because of the amount he was presumed to be trafficking, an offence that carried the mandatory death penalty. [@2]

The Shah Alam High Court convicted him of drug trafficking and mandatorily sentenced him to death on 8 December 2009, after he had already spent six years in prison. [@1,3] His appeal was denied in 2012. [@2]

## Pardon

Shahrul's family approached Amnesty International Malaysia in 2014, and the organisation campaigned for commutation of the sentence from 2015, collecting more than 10,000 signatures worldwide. [@1,3] The Sultan of Selangor granted a pardon, and 33-year-old Shahrul and his family were told of the successful clemency application at Sungai Buloh prison in February 2017. [@1,2,3] His mother Sapenah Nawawi thanked the Sultan at a press conference on 27 February 2017. [@3]
