Zambia's Drug Enforcement Commission (DEC) arrested a priest of the New Apostolic Church in Sesheke district on Christmas Day 2009 for cultivating cannabis near the churchyard. [@1] DEC spokesperson John Nyawali said in a media statement that William Pumulo Siomboka, 37, had cultivated 2.1 tonnes of cannabis in fields about 150 metres from the church premises. [@1]

Siomboka, of the Siomboka congregation in Chief Imalenda's area, was arrested together with six women from the same area who had cultivated a combined 7.1 tonnes of cannabis. [@1] Preliminary investigations indicated the group had been contracted to grow the cannabis, which was earmarked for export to neighbouring countries. [@1] Nyawali said the DEC was disheartened by custodians of good morals joining the drug trade and urged people to uphold ethics and morals. [@1]
