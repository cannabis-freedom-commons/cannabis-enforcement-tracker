Gardaí from the National Drugs Unit and Carlow station searched a rural property near Leighlinbridge, County Carlow, on the evening of Tuesday 8 December 2009. [@1,2] They found 500 cannabis plants and growing paraphernalia at the premises, which gardaí said could have a street value of up to €200,000 subject to analysis. [@1,2] Three men in their twenties were arrested at the scene and detained at Carlow station under section 2 of the Drug Trafficking Act, where they could be held for up to seven days. [@1,2] The men were released without charge two days later, and a file was prepared for the Director of Public Prosecutions. [@2]
