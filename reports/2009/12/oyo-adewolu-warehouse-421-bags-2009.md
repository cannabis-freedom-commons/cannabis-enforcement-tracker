The Oyo State commander of the National Drug Law Enforcement Agency, Sulaiman Jadi,
announced the seizure of a bungalow used as an Indian hemp warehouse at Adewolu
village in Egbeda local government area. [@1] Officers found 421 bags of the drug,
with a street value put at more than N8 million, after a tip-off about dealers
operating in the area. [@1]

Jadi said his men mounted a three-day surveillance on the bungalow until the arrival
of the hemp dealer, who was still on the run when the seizure was announced. [@1] No
arrests were reported. [@1]
