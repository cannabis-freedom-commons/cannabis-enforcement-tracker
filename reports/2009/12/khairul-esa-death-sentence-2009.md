The Malaysian High Court sentenced factory worker Muhammad Khairul Esa Jemali, 22, to death for trafficking 316.53 grams of cannabis on 8 May 2008. [@1,2] Judicial Commissioner Kamarudin Hashim passed the mandatory death sentence on 17 December 2009 and added three years jail for a second drug charge. [@1] A European campaign report on Malaysia's December 2009 death sentences confirms that Jemali, 22, was sentenced to be hanged on 17 December for trafficking 316 grams of cannabis in 2008. [@2]
