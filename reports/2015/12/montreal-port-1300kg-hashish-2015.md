Border officers at the Port of Montréal seized more than 1,300 kg of suspected hashish on 10 December 2015 from a marine container arriving from Malawi filled with boards of floating floor and wooden handcrafted furniture. [@1] The drugs were concealed inside the laminated floor pieces and were detected by River, a seven-year-old Labrador retriever trained to find drugs and firearms. [@1,2]

A later X-ray exam showed suspicious forms inside the floor pieces, which officers broke open one by one to remove a total of 3,046 packets of suspected hashish. [@1] A later summary describes the same seizure as 3,000 packets found within the floor pieces on 10 December. [@2]

No arrests were reported, and the CBSA and the RCMP are investigating. [@2] CBSA officers in Quebec had made more than 2,700 drug seizures since the start of 2015. [@1]
