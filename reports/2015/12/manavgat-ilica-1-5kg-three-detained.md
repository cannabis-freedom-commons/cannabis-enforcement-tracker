Acting on information that A.U., A.E. and B.S. were trading drugs and holding a
large quantity, gendarmerie teams located the house where they lived in Ilıca
neighbourhood. [@2] Three suspects were taken into custody over the incident.
[@1] After questioning at the gendarmerie, all three were referred to the Public
Prosecutor and brought before a court. [@2] B.S. was remanded on a charge of
drug trafficking. [@1] B.S. was sent to Alanya L-Type Prison, while A.U. and
A.E. were released to be tried without detention. [@2]

Officers searched the house under a search warrant obtained from the Public
Prosecutor and found cannabis hidden in various places around the home wrapped
in papers. [@2] The amount seized was 1 kilo 500 grams of what the reports call
kubar esrar. [@1] In these Turkish reports, kubar esrar denotes the seized
cannabis. [@2]

The operation was carried out in Manavgat district by teams of the Kumköy
gendarmerie station attached to the Manavgat District Gendarmerie Command. [@1]
