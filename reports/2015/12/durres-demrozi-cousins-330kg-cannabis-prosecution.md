Prosecutors at Albania's Serious Crimes Prosecution asked a court on 1 December
2015 to convict three cousins, Muhamet, Bashkim and Agim Demrozi, over an
attempted cannabis shipment through the port of Durrës. [@1] Prosecutors
requested 15 years for Muhamet Demrozi and 13 years each for Bashkim and Agim
Demrozi, 41 years in all, and asked to convict a fourth defendant, Ermal Lusha,
in the same case. [@1]

According to the prosecution account, the cousins hired a truck driver and told
him to travel to Italy to collect crates of wine from a winery near Brescia, and
investigators said he did not know about the cannabis. [@1] When the case
reached court, Muhamet Demrozi was the only defendant in custody, and the
article records that Agim Demrozi had been wanted since August 2014. [@2]

The seizure was made at the port of Durrës on the night of 19 August 2014, when
border police found cannabis hidden inside the chassis of an unloaded Iveco
truck waiting to board a ferry to Bari and arrested its driver. [@3] That
account put the load at about 350 kg of marijuana; Durrës Lajm, Agim Demrozi's
Romanian profile and later police announcements put it at 330 to 332 kg, so no
kg figure is recorded. [@3]

Albanian police announced in March 2020 that Bashkim Demrozi had been extradited
from Italy, after the Serious Crimes Court gave him eight years and the Durrës
appeal court in 2018 made the sentence final at six years and eight months. [@4]

The Romanian sports outlet reported in November 2026 that the 2014 truck was
bound for Italy with a load of about 330 kg of cannabis and that prosecutors
sought 13 years for Agim Demrozi, now the president of Albanian football club
Egnatia. [@2]
