On 19 November 2015 around 15 armed police entered filmmaker Ala Eddine Slim's home in Nabeul with a search warrant for suspected terrorist activity while Slim was hosting fellow artists Fakhri El-Ghezal and Atef Maatallah. [@1,2] The officers found filming equipment and nothing pointing to terrorism, but seized joints and charged the three men with cannabis possession under Law 52, the 1992 anti-drug statute. [@1,2]

After a week in detention in Nabeul and a trial lasting about ten minutes, each man received one year in prison and a 1,000-dinar fine. [@2] They were moved to Mornag prison, where they spent about a month before the court of appeal acquitted them and dismissed the case on 21 December 2015. [@1,2]

The case drew attention to Law 52, under which drug use carried a one-year term, and the president later promised to amend it. [@1,2]
