Hakop Nagapetian, 25, and Berg Nagapetian, 23, are brothers whom Glendale police
identified as Armenian Power gang members. [@1] The brothers were arrested on 17
December 2015 at their father's residence in the 12000 block of Gilmore Street
near Laurel Canyon Boulevard in North Hollywood. [@1] They were arrested on
suspicion of possessing weapons, concentrated cannabis and the chemicals needed
to create the drug. [@1] The arrest was made by a task force that checks
compliance of lower-level offenders paroled under a law to reduce the state
prison population. [@1] Hakop Nagapetian was on formal probation in a firearms
case and remained in jail on a probation hold, while Berg Nagapetian posted
$35,000 bail and was released the night he was arrested. [@1]

A search of the Gilmore Street residence revealed a sawed-off shotgun, a
handgun, ammunition and AR-15 large-capacity magazines. [@1] Officers discovered
an elaborate butane honey oil lab, several pounds of marijuana, more than 130
bottles of butane fuel and cooking products, plus a significant amount of
concentrated cannabis, packaging and a ledger containing $21,000 in cash. [@1]
Safes found in bedroom closets contained more than 800 rounds of ammunition.
[@1]

Authorities also searched a relative's home in the 12000 block of Oxnard Street
near Laurel Canyon Boulevard in North Hollywood and seized high-powered rifles
including three assault weapons, two unregistered handguns and a sawed-off
shotgun. [@1] Both suspects have ties to Glendale and Burbank. [@1]
