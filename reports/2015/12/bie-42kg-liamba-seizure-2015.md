The National Police command in Bié province seized 42 kilograms of liamba in one
week under its programme against drugs. [@1] Police spokesman Chief
Superintendent António Hossi gave the figures to Angop. [@1] Liamba, the local
term for cannabis used in the police account, was described as the seized drug.
[@1]

Hossi said officers also destroyed 52 liamba plants besides the seized drugs.
[@1] In the same weekly results, officers destroyed two makeshift sites for
making aguardente caseira, a home-brew spirit, and dumped 400 litres of the
product. [@1] Those aguardente sites and liquid are not cannabis and are
excluded from the cannabis figures. [@1]

Police said a group called Tinex, with three members who robbed motorbikes and
other items using a firearm, was dismantled in the same period. [@1] In the same
period, the force said it carried out six micro-operations that recovered three
AKM firearms, 22 model-43 rounds, one 82-mm mortar projectile, one 60-mm mortar
projectile and 13 ZU-23 projectiles. [@1] The Tinex robbery case is a separate
non-cannabis result reported in the same period and is not counted in the
cannabis figures. [@1] The recovered firearms and ammunition are separate
non-cannabis results reported in the same period and are not counted in the
cannabis figures. [@1]
