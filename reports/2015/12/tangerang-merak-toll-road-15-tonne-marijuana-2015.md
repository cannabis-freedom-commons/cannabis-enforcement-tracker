Indonesia's National Police said they intercepted a truck on the Tangerang–Banten highway carrying 1.5 tonnes of marijuana and arrested its driver, 31-year-old YS of Bogor. [@1] The drugs were packed in 39 sacks hidden among 120 sacks of staple foods used as cover, and police said the load had come from Aceh. [@1]

The National Police narcotics director, Brig. Gen. Anjan Pramuka Putera, said the marijuana was bound for a warehouse in Bogor, which had been raided on 16 December, and then for buyers in Bandung, Purwakarta and Garut in West Java. [@1] He put the haul's value at Rp 6 billion (US$441,000) and claimed the seizure had saved 40,000 Indonesians from ruin. [@1]
