A six-month joint operation by Queensland Police, the Australian Crime
Commission and the Australian Border Force shut down an alleged Vietnamese
cannabis syndicate running grow houses across south-east Queensland. [@1,2]
In a two-day closure on 15 and 16 December 2015, 240 officers searched 27
properties, including 20 purpose-built hydroponic grow houses in Brisbane's
south. [@1,2]

Police seized 3,704 cannabis plants and 45 kg of dried cannabis along with an
unstated quantity of cocaine, putting the combined street value at about $20
million, plus a significant amount of cash. [@1,2] Homes had been remodelled
specifically for hydroponic production, and a warehouse at Acacia Ridge packed
to the rooftop with $1.3 million of growing equipment was allegedly supplying
growers across Brisbane. [@1] Police said the syndicate used Vietnamese
students and overstayers as crop-sitters and had links to Victoria and South
Australia. [@1,2]

Thirty-seven people were charged with more than 100 offences, including three
charged under Queensland's anti-association laws; the alleged Victorian-based
principal was arrested on Hamilton Island during a family holiday. [@1,2]
Fourteen unlawful non-citizens found during the raids were detained for removal
by the Border Force. [@1]
