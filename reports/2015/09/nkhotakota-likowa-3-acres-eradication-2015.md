Nkhotakota police, together with the Nkhotakota game officer, destroyed a field of Indian hemp planted inside the Nkhotakota Game Reserve along the Likowa River. [@1] According to Nkhotakota criminal investigation officer Assistant Superintendent Kadzandira, the officers uprooted about three acres of chamba and burned it. [@1] The Likowa River lies in the reserve between Traditional Authority Mwansambo and Traditional Authority Mwadzama in Nkhotakota district. [@1]

In the same operation the officers discovered further areas with large chamba fields along the river, at Mwalawamphasa and Kanjedza 1, 2 and 3. [@1] Kadzandira said the police had yet to take action on those newly found fields. [@1]
