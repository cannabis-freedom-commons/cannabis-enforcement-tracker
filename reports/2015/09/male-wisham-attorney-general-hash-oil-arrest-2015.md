Maldives police arrested Assistant Attorney General Ismail Wisham and his wife,
Hana Waheed, at 11.30pm in a late-night raid on an alcohol and drug abuse charge
after an intelligence tip-off. [@1]

The couple were transferred to five days of house arrest; Hana Waheed is a
senior public prosecutor at the Prosecutor General’s Office. [@1]

Police published photos of the seized items from Wisham’s apartment: six
unopened and two empty beer cans, and six pieces of cellophane containing hash
oil. [@1] Police refused to state the quantity of alcohol or hash oil found;
alcohol use in the Maldives carries up to three months’ jail and 40 lashes,
while drug abuse carries harsher prison sentences. [@1]

People close to Wisham, including a lawyer at the Attorney General’s office who
called him a 'pro-government, technical lawyer,' described the arrest as
'targeted,' saying there seemed to be no explicit public policy reason for
police to carry out such a raid. [@1]
