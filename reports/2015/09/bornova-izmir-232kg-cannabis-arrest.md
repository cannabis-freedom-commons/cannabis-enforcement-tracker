Izmir police narcotics teams mounted an operation in Bornova district as part of
work targeting drug trafficking and so-called torbaci street dealers. [@1]
Officers searching a house in the district seized 2 kilos 320 grams of esrar,
the Turkish term for cannabis. [@1]

Two suspects were detained in connection with the incident. [@1] One detainee
identified as H.S. was released on the instructions of the prosecutor's office.
[@1] The other detainee, identified as Y.S., was referred to the courthouse and
remanded in custody. [@1]

The published account summarised the result as 2 kilos 320 grams of esrar seized
and one of two detainees remanded. [@1] The page dates its entry and update to 5
September 2015 and attributes the report to the AA news agency. [@1]
