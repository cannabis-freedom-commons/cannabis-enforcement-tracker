Dubai Police's anti-narcotics department, tipped off about the carpenter,
assigned an undercover source to pose as a buyer. [@1] The two agreed to meet
in Al Murar, and when M R handed the drugs to the source at the surrounded
meeting point on 20 June 2014, officers arrested him immediately; he denied
knowing what he had handed over, saying a man had asked him to make the
delivery and return with the cash. [@1]

At the criminal court in October 2014 the 22-year-old Bangladeshi denied
possessing 4.78 grams of marijuana to sell and was sentenced to four years in
prison plus deportation. [@1] Prosecutors appealed, and on 30 September 2015
the Court of Appeal raised the term to life imprisonment, which the report
equates to 25 years. [@1]
