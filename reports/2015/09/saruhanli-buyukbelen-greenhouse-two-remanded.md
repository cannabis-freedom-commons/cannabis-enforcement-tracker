District gendarmerie teams raided a greenhouse belonging to A.K. in Büyükbelen
neighbourhood in Saruhanlı district of Manisa province, where olive seedlings
were being grown. [@1] Officers searching pots inside the greenhouse found
cannabis plants alongside 40 grams of kubar esrar, the Turkish term for
cannabis. [@1] A.K. and a companion identified only as İ.K. were detained during
the operation. [@2] After questioning at the prosecutor's office, both detainees
were referred to court and remanded in custody. [@1]

A corroborating account says officers from the Halitpaşa gendarmerie station
acted on a tip that A.K. was growing cannabis among olive seedlings in his
greenhouse in Büyükbelen. [@2] It describes the seizure as potted cannabis
plants plus 40 grams of cannabis left to dry, and says the plants were uprooted
to be destroyed. [@2] It identifies A.K. as a resident of Büyükbelen and says
both suspects were sent to the courthouse after gendarmerie procedures and
remanded. [@2] The two detainees were the only people reported as held in the
operation. [@2]
