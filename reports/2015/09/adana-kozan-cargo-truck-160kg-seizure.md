Adana and Mersin police directorates carried out a joint operation targeting
people involved in trafficking in narcotic substances. [@1] As part of that
joint work, officers searched a truck belonging to a cargo company on the
Adana-Kozan highway. [@1] The Adana Police Directorate disclosed the operation
and its results in a written statement. [@2] Officers detained two people in
connection with what the roadside search uncovered. [@1]

Inside the truck, officers found cannabis wrapped in transparent bags and
divided into separate packages. [@1] The search recovered 24 separate packages
containing a total of 160 kilograms and 500 grams of cannabis. [@1]

The operation brought together officers from two neighbouring provincial police
forces, Adana and Mersin, rather than a single local unit. [@1] The reported
enforcement result from the highway interception was the seizure and the
detention of the two people. [@1] The seized substance was described as esrar,
the Turkish term for cannabis, in the amount of 160 kilograms 500 grams. [@1]
