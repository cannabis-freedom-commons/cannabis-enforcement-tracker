Kasungu police stopped a Toyota Noah, registration NU 8087, at a checkpoint at Kalenga Trading Centre at about 2 a.m. on Friday 14 August 2015 after a tip from members of the public. [@2] Searching the car, officers found 13 bags of cannabis sativa inside, and arrested the three men aboard: Mbiriyakula Maliko, 32, Pilirani Chafuwa, 25, and Redson Kamangira, 28. [@2] Kasungu police spokesperson Edwin Kaunda confirmed the arrests to The Maravi Post the next day. [@2]

The three were convicted by the Kasungu Magistrate Court and each fined K50,000 for possessing cannabis sativa. [@1] Together with the court, the police then burned about two and a half tonnes of cannabis sativa confiscated from Maliko and his two friends at Kalenga Trading Centre in the area of Traditional Authority Wimbe. [@1] Kaunda said the burned pile also included a consignment found at police checkpoints at Chisazima and Bua roadblocks, whose owners were not traced. [@1]
