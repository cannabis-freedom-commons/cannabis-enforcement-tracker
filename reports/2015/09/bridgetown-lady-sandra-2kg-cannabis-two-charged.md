Two Guyanese nationals faced eight drug charges after drugs were found aboard
their vessel in Barbados. [@2] The accused were 29-year-old Coco Ibert Prophett
and 42-year-old Paul Fraser. [@1] Prophett was the cook and Fraser was a sailor
aboard the vessel Lady Sandra. [@1]

Officers searched the Guyana vessel Lady Sandra berthed at Bridgetown Port on 10
September 2015. [@3] The search discovered 14.5 kilograms of cocaine and two
kilograms of cannabis. [@1] The two men were jointly charged with possession,
possession with intent to supply, trafficking and importation of cannabis, and
the same four counts for cocaine. [@1]

Both men were arrested after the search of the docked vessel at Bridgetown Port.
