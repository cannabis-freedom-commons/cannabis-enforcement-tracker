FELCN officers seized 1,224 bricks of marijuana of Paraguayan origin weighing
more than 1.2 tonnes in a house in central Cochabamba. [@1] The seizure was
announced by Deputy Minister of Controlled Substances Felipe Cáceres, according
to the state ABI news agency. [@1]

Officers detained a 32-year-old Bolivian woman with the initials M.R.A. and a
22-year-old Bolivian man identified as E.Q. [@1] According to the deputy
minister, an investigation begun more than six months earlier established that
the two detainees were in charge of collecting the drugs at the house to then
send them to Chile. [@1] FELCN officers also seized two vehicles, four mobile
phones and about 1,223 dollars at the scene. [@1]
