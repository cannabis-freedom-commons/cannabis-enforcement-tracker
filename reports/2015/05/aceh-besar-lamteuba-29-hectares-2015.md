Banda Aceh Police found 29 hectares of cannabis plantations at five spots in
Aceh Besar and destroyed most of the plants. [@1] Aceh Besar Police chief Heru
Novianto said the plants were burned on Sunday, 24 May 2015, by 87 police
personnel and local residents. [@1]

The fields lay on the slopes of the Glee Dua mountainous area at Meurah
Kemukiman Lemteuba village in Seulimeum district, Aceh Besar. [@1] Police were
stationed in the plantations a few days before the destruction began. [@1]

Some fields were ready for harvest, while others had just been planted or
already harvested. [@1] No resident or land owner was found at the locations.
[@1] Police found the fields after reports from local residents, and Heru said
they were the largest found in the regency that year. [@1]
