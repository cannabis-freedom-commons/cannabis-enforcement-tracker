The Criminal Court sentenced 46-year-old Ali Imran to life in jail over one
kilogram of cannabis. [@1] The same court order also handed him a fine of
MVR100,000 for the cannabis offence. [@1] Imran, described as of Coconut House
in Malé, was caught with 1131.2 grams of cannabis in November 2012. [@1]

Police apprehended Imran at Malé’s commercial harbour because his behaviour had
appeared suspicious. [@1] Officers searching at the harbour found the drugs
hidden under the seat of his motorcycle. [@1] At the time of the stop, police
had received information that a cargo boat traveling from India was carrying
illicit drugs. [@1]

When officers later searched Imran’s house, they found MVR134,050 and US$2200 in
cash together with 21 diamond stones and four precious stones inside a safe.
[@1] The search also established that he held another MVR461,948.26 in an HSBC
account. [@1] The Criminal Court ordered the state to confiscate the cash, the
bank balance and the precious stones, stating that Imran had not explained how
he had obtained the money. [@1]
