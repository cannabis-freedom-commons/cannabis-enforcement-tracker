Saudi Arabia beheaded three Yemeni citizens on Wednesday 13 May 2015 after convicting them of attempting to smuggle hashish into the kingdom. [@1,2] The Interior Ministry named the men as Eisa Ali Ahmed Hajri, Mohammed Ali Saifi and Majid Gasim al-Ahdal and said they were put to death in the south-western city of Jazan, near the Yemeni border. [@1,2]

The ministry announced the executions through the official Saudi Press Agency. [@2] The beheadings brought the number of people put to death in the kingdom since the start of the year to 83, compared with 87 in all of 2014, according to press counts. [@2] The ministry has cited deterrence as its reason for using the death penalty despite criticism from human-rights watchdogs. [@2] Drug trafficking carries the death penalty under the country's strict interpretation of Islamic law. [@1]
