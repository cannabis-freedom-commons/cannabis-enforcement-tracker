Officers of Dschang's second-district police station seized six sacks of dried
cannabis during a road check at Lifock, near Dschang's bus station. [@1] The six
sacks weighed 108.1 kg in total. [@1] Police detained the driver's assistant,
Christian Toninbeng, after finding the cannabis in the bus luggage. [@1]

The bus, a Toyota Coaster run by a travel agency, had left Bamenda for Kumba in
the South-West Region. [@1] Judicial police officer Nnane Sone Moses, head of
the second-district station, led the check with his officers. [@1] The second
account places the interception near the Tradex station, upstream of the Lifock
bus station, after police acted on a tip. [@2]

The driver, 35-year-old Raymond Meland Tinjoh, pretended to go and fetch a
technician and then fled into town. [@2] The cannabis, said to be meant for a
man named Derrick in Kumba, was stored at the second-district police station
while the assistant remained in its cells. [@1] Police issued a search notice
for the driver, whose telephone line no longer answered after he slipped away.
[@1]

A second account dates the same seizure of six sacks of dried cannabis by the
same station to Saturday 4 April 2015. [@2]
