Gendarmes of the Sainte-Rose brigade intervened at the home of a resident of the
commune who was growing zamal, the Réunion name for cannabis, and seized 73
plants there. [@1] The gendarmes found the plants in the resident's garden
during the week of 20 April 2015, according to Réunion La 1ère. [@2] The plants
were juveniles about one metre tall, and the gendarmes pulled them up and said
they would be destroyed shortly. [@2] The amateur gardener was questioned and is
to be tried by the criminal court of Saint-Denis, Réunion's capital. [@2]

Announcing the seizure on its Facebook page, the gendarmerie of La Réunion said
its officers had discovered more than 2,500 zamal plants across the department
since the start of 2015. [@1] Réunion La 1ère put the island total at 2,500
plants seized since the start of 2015. [@2] Zamal is the Réunion name for
cannabis, whose cultivation, sale and possession are illegal in France. [@2]
