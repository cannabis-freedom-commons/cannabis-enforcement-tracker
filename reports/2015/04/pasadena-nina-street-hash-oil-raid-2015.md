Officers arrested two men after an anonymous tip on Monday led police to a
suspected drug lab in Pasadena. [@2] The men were Jeffrey Raber, 39, of
Whittier, and Mark Piesner, 43, of Calabasas, arrested on suspicion of
manufacturing a controlled substance. [@2] Each man posted $75,000 bail and was
released late Monday. [@1] Raber told police he had permission to produce
botanical medicine. [@1] The owners told police they were operating lawfully.
[@3]

Police received a call at about 2:30 p.m. Monday about a strong marijuana smell
and people smoking marijuana in front of a business. [@1] Police served search
warrants at two adjacent businesses in the 2500 block of Nina Street. [@1]
Officers went to The Werc Shop, which police said operates at two locations on
Nina Street, and served a search warrant at about 10 p.m. [@2] Raber was linked
to The Werc Shop, described as testing botanical products through cannabinoid
profiling to help medical marijuana patients get an accurate dosage. [@3]

Police said they seized about 500 pounds (about 227 kg) of marijuana and about
200 pint-sized Mason jars or bottles of hash oil from the lab. [@3] Police said
they found a manufacturing factory for hash oil or honey oil at the business.
[@2] Agents placed a yellow sign on the building warning that a clandestine drug
laboratory had been seized there. [@3] The Pasadena Fire Department responded
because of the extraction danger but ordered no evacuations. [@3]

Police initially detained 28 people but later released 26 workers, leaving only
the two arrested men in custody. [@2] Police said the two men had a court date
in June. [@2] Police said they did not know how long the business had operated
on Nina Street. [@2]
