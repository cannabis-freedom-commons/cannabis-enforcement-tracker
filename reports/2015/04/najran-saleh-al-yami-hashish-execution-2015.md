Saudi Arabia beheaded Saudi citizen Saleh al-Yami in the southern province of Najran on 6 April 2015. [@2] The Interior Ministry said he had been convicted of smuggling hashish and banned narcotic pills into the kingdom and of wounding several policemen during a confrontation. [@1,2]

The ministry announced the execution through the official Saudi Press Agency. [@2] The beheading brought the number of people executed in the kingdom so far that year to 57, with around half of the year's executions for drug-related offences according to Amnesty International. [@1]
