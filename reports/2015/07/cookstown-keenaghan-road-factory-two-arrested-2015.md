Police discovered a cannabis factory at a house in the Keenaghan Road area of Cookstown on Tuesday 14 July 2015. [@1] Officers seized a quantity of herbal cannabis and a number of suspected cannabis plants in various stages of growth, with an estimated street value of £200,000. [@1] Detective Sergeant Martin McAuley said the two men had been arrested on suspicion of possession of a Class B controlled drug, possession of a Class B controlled drug with intent to supply and cultivating cannabis, and that both remained in custody assisting police with their enquiries.
