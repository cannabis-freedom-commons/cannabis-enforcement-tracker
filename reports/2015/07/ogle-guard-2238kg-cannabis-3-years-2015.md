Security guard Kevin Sandy was heading to Barbados via Ogle International
Airport when anti-narcotics ranks stopped him after he was seen acting
suspiciously. [@2] The officers searched his bag and uncovered the drugs he was
carrying. [@2] He was arrested and, under caution, said someone had told him to
collect the narcotics from a garbage bin and a toilet tank at the airport. [@2]
The narcotics were concealed in a suitcase bearing his name. [@1]

The court case states that on 8 July at Ogle International Airport he had 2.238
kilogrammes of cannabis and 1.012 kilogrammes of cocaine in his possession. [@2]
Sandy, reported as a 30-year-old resident of Lot 37 East LaPenitence Housing
Scheme, pleaded guilty to the charge before Magistrate Ann McLennan at
Georgetown Magistrate’s Court 2. [@2] He was sentenced to three years in jail
for trafficking the cocaine and cannabis. [@2] He was also ordered to pay a
$6,156,900 fine, reported as three times the street value of both drugs. [@2]

Customs Anti Narcotics Unit agents made the initial arrest at the airport. [@1]
Ogle International Airport is Guyana’s smaller international airport, which
regional carriers had begun using more heavily about a year earlier. [@1]
