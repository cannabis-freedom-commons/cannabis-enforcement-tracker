Six men, five Bahamians and one Jamaican, were taken into custody after the
Royal Bahamas Police Force intercepted a 35-foot go-fast boat carrying marijuana
in waters off Kemps Bay, South Andros, around 12.50am. [@2] The Drug Enforcement
Unit, helped by the Police Marine Support Services Unit, acted on intelligence
and stopped the vessel, then found a large quantity of marijuana aboard. [@2]
The marijuana weighed 5,050 pounds, about 2,291 kg, and the U.S. Coast Guard put
its wholesale value at $4.6 million. [@1]

A Customs and Border Protection aircraft on routine patrol spotted the vessel
speeding through the dark without navigation lights, and raised the alert. [@2]
A Coast Guard MH-60 Jayhawk crew from Air Station Clearwater in Florida and a
Royal Bahamas Police Force boat crew carrying Drug Enforcement Administration
agents set off in pursuit and intercepted the boat at 12:53am. [@1] The
suspects, the vessel and the marijuana were brought to Nassau, where the drugs
were offloaded at the police Marine Support Services branch on East Bay Street.
[@2]

Operation Bahamas, Turks and Caicos, known as OPBAT, is a partnership of the
Coast Guard, the DEA, Customs and Border Protection and the Bahamian government
against drug smuggling to and from the Bahamas. [@1] Seven men were charged over
the haul, and one of them, a 31-year-old Jamaican taxi driver, pleaded guilty at
his arraignment in Magistrates Court to possession of dangerous drugs with
intent to supply, importing them and conspiring to import and possess them. [@3]
