Gardaí from Ashbourne, assisted by the Eastern Regional Support Unit, carried out a planned search of a warehouse near Ashbourne, County Meath, on the morning of Saturday 25 July 2015. [@1] The search was part of ongoing intelligence-led operations targeting the sale and supply of controlled drugs in the Dublin and Meath areas. [@1] Inside the warehouse officers found cannabis plants, still to be analysed, growing in what they described as an elaborate grow-house, with an estimated street value of up to €750,000. [@1,2]

A man in his late forties was arrested at the scene and detained under section 2 of the Criminal Justice (Drug Trafficking) Act 1996 at Kells Garda station. [@1,2] The Garda release offered a photograph of the plants to the press on request; no photograph was published with either opened source. [@1]
