Palestinian anti-narcotics police in the Middle Governorate of Gaza searched a
resident's house after receiving information that hashish and Tramal pills were
kept there. [@1] Maj. Mahmoud al-Bayoumi, the Middle Governorate anti-narcotics
chief, said his officers acted immediately after the tip about the home of a man
identified only as (W. A.). [@1] He said the force first obtained the necessary
search warrants from the Public Prosecution before entering the home. [@1]

Officers found four slabs of hashish during the search, the cannabis component
on which this report focuses. [@1] They also found 3,972 Tramal pills in the
same house; those pills are not counted as cannabis. [@1] The suspect was not
present during the search, so no arrest was made at the house. [@1] Police said
the absent suspect is now wanted by the anti-narcotics force. [@1] The force
retained the seized drugs and referred the case to an investigations officer to
complete the legal procedures. [@1]

The report was datelined Gaza and published on 30 July 2015, which is used as
the incident date because no separate raid date was given. [@1]
