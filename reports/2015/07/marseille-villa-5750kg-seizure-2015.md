French police discovered nearly six tonnes of cannabis resin, known as hashish, in a villa in the southern city of Marseille and arrested three men, the interior ministry said on 27 July 2015. [@1,2] The bust came after months of investigation and was described as one of the biggest cannabis seizures in France in recent years. [@1,2]

Interior Minister Bernard Cazeneuve said police had tracked the three men, believed to belong to a vast drug trafficking network, to Marseille and the nearby town of Vitrolles on Sunday 26 July. [@1,2] Officers found 5.6 tonnes of hashish in the villa and another 150 kg of the drug in a car. [@1,2] Cazeneuve, who visited Marseille later that Monday, congratulated the officers on their "major success". [@1,2]
