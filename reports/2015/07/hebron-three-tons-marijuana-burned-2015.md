Hebron police set fire to about three tons of marijuana on Monday morning, destroying cannabis seized in an operation that ran over the previous several months. [@1] The Hebron District Governorate called it the biggest drug destruction operation in Palestine to date, saying it resulted from months of police work. [@2]

## Potent stock and politics

Raid Dudin, who heads the Hebron police drug unit, said the destroyed marijuana was some 30 times stronger than regular marijuana, without saying how its strength was measured. [@1] District Governor Kamel Hamid praised the police and urged joint action against what he described as attempts to destabilise Palestinian society through drugs. [@1,2]
