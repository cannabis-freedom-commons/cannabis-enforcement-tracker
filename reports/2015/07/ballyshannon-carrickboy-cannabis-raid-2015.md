Gardai seized about 800 cannabis plants and 30 kilos of cannabis herb in planned searches of lands and a premises at Carrickboy, Ballyshannon, Co. Donegal on Thursday afternoon 23 July 2015. [@1] Gardai from Ballyshannon and Bundoran, assisted by the Regional Support Unit, carried out the searches at about 4pm and uncovered a large cannabis cultivation operation. [@1] The drugs carried an estimated street value of about 1.2 million euro, pending analysis. [@1]

Two men, aged 40 and 41, were arrested at the scene and detained at Ballyshannon Garda station under section 2 of the Criminal Justice (Drug Trafficking) Act 1996. [@1] The scene was preserved for technical examination. [@1]
