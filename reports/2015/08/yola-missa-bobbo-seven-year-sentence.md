Adamawa State's drug agency arrested former state assembly member Missa Bobbo in Jada town after
several months of monitoring, seizing 81.9 kilograms of cannabis sativa. [@1] NDLEA commander
Hassan Zungeru confirmed the arrest to the News Agency of Nigeria in Yola and said Bobbo, who sat
in the assembly in 1992, had been charged before the Federal High Court in Yola. [@1]

The court later sentenced Bobbo to seven years in prison for possessing the 81.9 kilograms in
2013, NDLEA spokesman Mitchell Ofoyeju said. [@2] Justice Balkisu Aliyu, who passed the sentence,
described the former legislator's part in drug trafficking as shameful. [@2] Bobbo represented the
Leko-Koma constituency between 1991 and 1993 and attended the 1995 national constitutional
conference; officers also harvested fresh cannabis plants from his farm alongside the dried
stock. [@2]
