Anti-drug police raided storage and processing sites in Phnom Penh's Russey Keo and Meanchey districts on the evening of Friday 21 August, working through to Saturday morning under a court warrant, prosecutors said.[@3] They seized 1,487 kilograms of dried marijuana and 1.5 kilograms of concentrated marijuana oil, along with drying and packing machines; the cannabis had been smuggled from Laos packed in Dao coffee bags.[@1,2,3] The head of the Interior Ministry's anti-drug office, Gen. Khieu Samon, said the haul was worth more than $7 million on Western markets and was bound for a third country, later identified as Europe, calling it the biggest seizure in 15 years of crackdowns.[@1,2,3] Three Cambodians were detained, interrogated and sent to court, though the deputy prosecutor first said four had been arrested.[@3] The haul, the suspects and the equipment were presented to the media in Phnom Penh on Tuesday 25 August.[@2]
