Kayseri police tracked two cars on the Kayseri-Malatya highway, treating one as
a suspected drug vehicle and the other as its escort. [@2] The driver of the
suspected vehicle fled on foot and abandoned the vehicle on empty land. [@1]
Officers also stopped the escort car carrying M.K. and M.B. [@1] Officers
detained both occupants of the escort car after finding 22 grams of cannabis
inside it. [@1] The two occupants were later named as Murat Kaya and Mahmut
Budunoğlu. [@3] Both men were then remanded to prison by a court for alleged
dealing in narcotic substances. [@3]

The abandoned vehicle was taken to the police directorate car park, where
officers found 145 kilograms 790 grams of cannabis hidden under the seats and in
the luggage compartment. [@1] Together with the 22 grams from the escort car,
police reported a total seizure of 145 kilograms 812 grams of cannabis. [@1] The
fleeing vehicle was described as carrying Bursa plates. [@2] The escort car was
described as carrying Balıkesir plates and as being linked to the fleeing
driver. [@2]

The operation was carried out by Anti-Smuggling and Organized Crime Branch teams
of the Kayseri police. [@2] Police said work to catch the suspect who fled on
foot was continuing. [@1]
