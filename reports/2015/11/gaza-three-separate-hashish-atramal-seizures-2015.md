Police acted on information about drugs in a merchant's house in Gaza
Governorate and obtained Public Prosecution permission to search the house of a
man identified as (A-Kh). [@1] The search of the house turned up two slabs of
hashish (farsh, the unit used for hashish slabs) and 290 Atramal pills. [@1]
Police said the second man held at the same house was carrying gold jewellery
found to have been stolen. [@1]

In Rafah Governorate, officers set up a flying checkpoint on the road two
suspects were expected to use and stopped their car. [@1] The Rafah search found
327 'happy pills' (حبوب سعادة), a street name for non-cannabis synthetic pills,
and no hashish. [@1] In Khan Younis Governorate, a flying checkpoint on one of
the governorate's streets led to the seizure of 15 cartons of Atramal from a
single merchant. [@1]

In the Gaza house case, police detained the two suspects, retained the seized
items and referred the case to an investigation inspector to complete the legal
procedures. [@1] In the Rafah case, police retained the two suspects and the
seized items and referred the case to an investigation inspector to complete the
legal procedures. [@1] In the Khan Younis case, police retained the suspect and
the seized items and referred the case to an investigation inspector to complete
the legal procedures. [@1] The roundup carried the headline note that the three
seizures happened in separate operations across Gaza governorates. [@1]
