Military Police in São Tomé das Letras, Minas Gerais, uprooted and seized 18
cannabis plants kept in various pots inside a house in the rural zone of the
town on Thursday 12 November 2015. [@1] Officers carried out the search and
seizure after an anonymous tip. [@1] The resident of the house was not found
when police arrived. [@1] No suspect had been arrested by the time the report
was published. [@1]

Police said they also seized various cannabis seeds at the same house. [@1] The
plants had been cultivated in pots in the town's rural zone. [@1]

An evidence photo published with the report, credited to EPTV, shows the potted
plants laid out on a tiled floor behind a case of seeds. [@1]
