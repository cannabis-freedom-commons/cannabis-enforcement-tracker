The Kayseri governor's office said in a written statement that provincial police
teams from the Anti-Smuggling and Organized Crime Branch stopped a passenger bus
on the Kayseri–Malatya highway. [@1] Officers searched with narcotics dog Sedef
and found cannabis inside a sports bag in the bus's baggage compartment. [@1]
The seizure weighed 3 kilograms 953 grams (3.953 kg). [@2] The cannabis was
wrapped in transparent plastic, then covered by six layers of white stretch
film, one blue plastic layer and five layers of packing tape. [@1]

One suspect, identified by the initials E.U., was taken into custody in
connection with the case and remanded on the stated drug dealing and transport
allegation. [@2] The Turkish offence cited was “Uyuşturucu Madde Ticareti Yapmak
ve Nakletmek,” meaning dealing in and transporting narcotic substances. [@2]
This operation combined a roadside stop and luggage search with a cannabis
seizure and one suspect's remand in the same case during a single police action.
