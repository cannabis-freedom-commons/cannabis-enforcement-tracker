Customs officers opened the two parcels and found nearly 33 kg of cannabis
inside, after the parcels had gone unclaimed for several days following
unloading. [@1]

The case goes back to 10 November 2015, when a boat from Dar es Salaam had
brought the cargo to the port of Moroni. [@1] Youssouf Msoury, head of the port
surveillance and customs brigade, said suspicion arose when the parcels were not
claimed for days after unloading. [@1]

Two people suspected of being the recipients of the two parcels were put in
custody, and the justice system now has to identify the real owner of the cargo.
[@1]
