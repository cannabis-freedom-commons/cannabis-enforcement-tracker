Three suspects detained during gendarmerie raids on several addresses in
Yeşilköy Mahallesi in Sur were brought before a court, which ordered them
remanded. [@1] The public prosecutor ordered the drugs found in the searches to
be seized. [@2] The detentions followed an operation targeting several addresses
in the neighbourhood. [@1]

Officers seized 741 kilograms of cannabis, reported as esrar, during the
searches. [@2] Some of the cannabis was hidden in grass and haystacks and in
gaps between trees. [@1] More of the cannabis was hidden in a tractor trailer
and in drums buried in the ground. [@2] Narcotic detector dogs named Altın, Caka
and Haklı took part in the address searches. [@2] Officers described the drugs
as stashed in separate hiding places across the searched addresses. [@1]

The operation was carried out by teams from the Diyarbakır Provincial
Gendarmerie Command's KOM branch and the Sur central gendarmerie station. [@2]
The Diyarbakır Governorship announced the operation. [@1]
