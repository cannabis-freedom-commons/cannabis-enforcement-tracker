Elazığ police detained six suspects identified only by the initials H.A., B.A.,
D.K., M.A., M.K. and Y.D. during a road interception at the entrance to the
city. [@1] After processing at the police directorate, all six were brought
before the judicial authorities and remanded in custody. [@1] They were remanded
on a charge of trafficking in narcotic substances and sent to prison. [@2]

Officers stopped three vehicles whose plates had been identified, one minibus
and two cars. [@1] Police said the drug-loaded minibus was travelling with the
two cars acting as escorts, and they mounted a simultaneous operation against
all three vehicles. [@2] Searches found 140 kilograms of kubar esrar, a Turkish
term for herbal cannabis, packed in sacks, with the load reported as being
inside the minibus. [@1]

The operation was carried out by teams of the Elazığ Provincial Police
Directorate's Anti-Smuggling and Organized Crime Branch, including its narcotics
bureau, as part of work to prevent drug trafficking. [@2] Police said they had
received intelligence that narcotics would be shipped in three vehicles from the
Diyarbakır direction toward Elazığ and onward to western provinces. [@1]
