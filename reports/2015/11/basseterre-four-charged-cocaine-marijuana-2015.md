Police charged four men on 16 November 2015 over one kilogram of cocaine and
50.7 pounds (about 23 kg) of marijuana seized during a patrol in Basseterre two
days earlier. [@1] Rafael Chapman and Merlin Murrain Matos, both Dominican
Republic nationals living on Nevis, were each charged with possession of
cocaine, possession of cocaine with intent to supply and importation of cocaine.
[@1] Bevin Taylor, of Salt Pond Alley in Irish Town, was charged with the three
cocaine counts plus possession of cannabis and possession of cannabis with
intent to supply. [@1] Dennial Rouse was charged with possession of cannabis and
possession of cannabis with intent to supply. [@1]

At about 3 p.m. on 14 November, officers of the Special Services Unit and the
Anti-Narcotics Unit on a routine patrol along the Bay Road in Irish Town watched
a blue and white boat named Pearla Negra approach close to the shoreline near
the Gardens Housing Project. [@1] Officers said a man alleged to be Taylor took
an orange and white bucket thrown to him from the vessel, ran towards the
Gardens playfield and was caught, and that one kilogram of cocaine with a street
value of EC$89,100 was found in the bucket. [@1] The boat headed out to sea but
a St Kitts and Nevis Coast Guard vessel intercepted it and took Chapman and
Matos into custody. [@1] A search warrant executed at Taylor's Salt Pond Alley
home found 50.7 pounds of marijuana with a street value of EC$380,250, and he
and Rouse, who was present, were taken into custody. [@1] The outlet's headline
total of EC$469K is the sum of the two police street-value estimates for the
cocaine and the marijuana. [@2]

Assistant Commissioner of Police Ian Queeley, quoted in the police release,
called the case an example of the collaboration the force's Six-Point Plan
intended and said the Coast Guard had been called on to intercept a small craft.
[@2] The same release noted a significant drug seizure in Nevis the previous
day, 13 November. [@2]
