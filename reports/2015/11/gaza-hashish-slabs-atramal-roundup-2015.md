Anti-narcotics police in Gaza Governorate seized 10 slabs of hashish and 47
cartons of Atramal, the report says. [@1] In the North Governorate area,
anti-narcotics police reported seizing two slabs of hashish among several
seizures, the report says. [@1] In a separate Rafah case, anti-narcotics police
seized 46 cartons of Atramal pills in the house of a dealer, the report says.
[@1]

Gaza police spokesman Ayman al-Batniji said anti-narcotics police had arrested
well-known drug traders and seized large quantities of drugs and Atramal over
the past year. [@1] Al-Batniji said intercepted drugs entering the Strip were
followed up and that more than one operation to promote them among young people
had been foiled. [@1]
