Saudi Arabia beheaded Malik bin Said al-Sayaari in the Al-Hasa district of the Eastern Province after a repeat conviction for hashish smuggling. [@1,2] The interior ministry said he was put to the sword on Thursday for promoting a large quantity of hashish for the second time. [@1,2]

He was the first person executed in 2015 in the kingdom, which had beheaded 87 people the year before. [@1,2] That tally was itself a sharp rise from the 78 executions recorded in 2013. [@2] Drug trafficking is punishable by death under Saudi Arabia's strict version of Islamic sharia law, alongside rape, murder, apostasy, homosexuality and armed robbery. [@2]
