Police raided marijuana farms across Vanua Levu on 5 and 6 January 2015 in the first week of Operation Sasamaki, a January campaign against drug cultivation in Fiji's Northern Division. [@1,2,3] On 5 January officers carried out six raids, uprooting 861 plants and seizing two parcels of suspected marijuana, and seven people were kept in custody for questioning. [@1] A day later, in the operation's second phase, officers uprooted 956 plants from two farms near Tukavesi and 265 plants from six farms near Savusavu, and confiscated dried leaves from one farm, with eight people in custody. [@2]

## New approach

Police said January would be dedicated to eradicating drug cultivation and sale, with the operation moving to other divisions in the coming weeks. [@2,3] Under a new approach, a police analyst would travel with raid teams to test seizures immediately so charges could be laid and suspects brought to court quickly instead of being released pending analysis. [@1,2] By 12 January investigators were working with crime prevention committees to identify the owners of raided farms. [@3]
