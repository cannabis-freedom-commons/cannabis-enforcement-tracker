A former Guam police officer was sentenced to eight years in prison for growing
marijuana while he was employed by the police department. [@1] The officer was
identified as Roy Pablo, who had been convicted in October on drug charges. [@1]
At the same hearing, Pablo's companion, Ah-Rum Rose Kim, received a two-year
sentence. [@1] The hearing took place in Hagatna, Guam. [@1] The sentencing
hearing was held on 15 January 2015. [@1]

Pablo had been arrested in November 2013 as part of an investigation into a
Dededo growth operation. [@1] Kim, who had been accused of selling marijuana and
other drugs, had earlier pleaded guilty to possession with intent to deliver.
[@1]

At the hearing, Pablo said he was afraid he would not be able to support his
family in prison. [@1] Judge Alberto Lamorena said Pablo's disregard for the law
he was supposed to enforce made his crimes all the more deplorable. [@1]
