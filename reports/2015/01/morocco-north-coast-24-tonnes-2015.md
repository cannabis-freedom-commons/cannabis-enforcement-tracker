Moroccan security forces seized nearly 24 tonnes of hashish from a number of boats and arrested 11 people after an overnight chase off the northern coast. [@1] The MAP news agency reported the operation on Saturday 24 January 2015. [@1]

## A cross-strait wave of raids

The Moroccan operation came hours after Spanish police said they had seized 11 tonnes of hashish and detained 55 people across Spain in raids on a Moroccan trafficking ring. [@1] The Moroccan agency gave no further detail on how far the two operations were linked. [@1] A Spanish police source said the gang moved the drug into Spain on speedboats or hidden inside the fuel tanks of trucks. [@1]
