Four people between the ages of 20 and 45 appeared in court in connection with
the two seizures and were remanded in custody while investigations and drug
analysis continued. [@1] Those arrested were believed to form part of an
importation and distribution network, and the agency said follow-up
investigations into others operating inside and outside the country were
ongoing. [@1]

The National Drugs Enforcement Agency seized 18.2 kilogrammes of compressed
cannabis in the Cascade area on Monday 26 January 2015 and 39.75 kilogrammes in
the Beoliere area on Wednesday 28 January, bringing the two-day total to almost
58 kilogrammes. [@1] The agency put the street value at almost R29 million and
said the drugs were believed to have come from Madagascar, shipped by local
operatives using privately owned boats. [@1]

One of the four, Ralph Sonny Samedi, was charged with trafficking the Beoliere
find: NDEA agents searching a Beoliere property on 28 January 2015 recovered
containers holding 32.493 kg of herbal cannabis, 1.559 kg of cannabis resin and
11.7 g of heroin, and Samedi was charged on all three counts. [@2] The court
record confirms the date, place and quantities of the Beoliere search behind the
agency's announcement. [@2]
