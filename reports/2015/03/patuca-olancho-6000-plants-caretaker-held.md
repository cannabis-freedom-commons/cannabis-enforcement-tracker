Fusina agents found a marijuana plantation at Buenos Aires, a community in
Patuca municipality, Olancho department, and took its caretaker into custody.
[@2] The detained man was named as Ferrufino Vásquez Zelaya, who was described
as the person in charge of guarding the plantation. [@2]

Fusina said officers seized 6,000 marijuana plants at the site. [@3] The agency
said the plants had not yet been weighed or valued and would be destroyed within
hours. [@3] Officers also seized a shotgun the man held without a permit, and El
Heraldo said he would be placed at the disposal of the courts of Olancho. [@2]

Fusina said the detainee would be sent to the prosecutor's office to be
prosecuted for illegal possession of a weapon and drug trafficking. [@3] Proceso
Digital reported the find as roughly 6,000 plants in the Patuca area of Olancho
and said the operation was led by Fusina, Honduras's inter-institutional
national security force. [@1] Colonel Santos Nolasco, a Fusina spokesman, cited
the find among what he called enormous plantations and said the force had seized
more than 68,000 pounds (34 tonnes) of marijuana nationwide that year. [@1]
