Waikato police recovered 6,946 cannabis plants searching 57 properties across the district over two weeks in the Waikato phase of Op Jasmine, a national cannabis and crime operation. [@1] Teams on foot, in vehicles and in fixed-wing aircraft and helicopters found a large share of the plants on the Coromandel Peninsula north of Whitianga, with other recoveries in farmers' maize crops, parks, reserves and private forestry blocks. [@1]

Officers also recovered 1.5 kilos of dried cannabis along with quantities of methamphetamine, LSD, ecstasy and GBL, plus 16 firearms of various calibres with ammunition, $18,000 in cash and about $27,000 worth of stolen property including a digger. [@1] Thirty people were arrested for a variety of offences and ten more were issued with formal warnings; three referrals went to the Asset Recovery Unit over properties with a combined estimated value above $1 million, and one child was referred to child welfare services. [@1]

Detective Senior Sergeant Nigel Keall said the results showed strong links between illicit drugs and illegal firearms, and noted that growers were increasingly hiding commercial crops on other people's land, leaving innocent landowners as victims. [@1]
