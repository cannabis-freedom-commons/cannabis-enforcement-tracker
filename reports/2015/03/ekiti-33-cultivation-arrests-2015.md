The National Drug Law Enforcement Agency (NDLEA) in Ekiti State said it arrested 33 suspects for allegedly cultivating Indian hemp between January and February 2015. [@1,2] The state commander, Suleiman Jadi, told the News Agency of Nigeria (NAN) in Ado-Ekiti that the suspects were arrested at various points. [@1,2] He said they were picked up at different farms and beer parlours within and outside the state capital. [@1,2] He said 16 suspects were arrested in January and 17 in February. [@1,2]

## Seizures and surveillance

Jadi said 7.35kg of Indian hemp was impounded in January and 93.45kg was seized in February. [@1] He said the command was putting logistics in place to arrest more suspects and had intensified surveillance across the state's 16 local government areas. [@1] He said the agency was determined to uncover more farms, adding that nine farms had been discovered in the state in the last three years. [@1] He urged residents to give useful information to help tackle illicit drug use. [@1]
