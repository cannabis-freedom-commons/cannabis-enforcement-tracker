Gros-Islet police carried out an eradication exercise in the Beausejour area at
about 3:30 a.m. on Thursday 5 March 2015. [@1] In that Beausejour exercise
officers uprooted and destroyed 1,333 fully grown cannabis plants and 100
seedlings. [@1] Also on Thursday 5 March 2015 the Drug Unit of the Royal Saint
Lucia Police Force carried out an eradication exercise in the Belle Vue area in
Dennery. [@1]

That Dennery exercise uprooted and destroyed 6,682 fully grown cannabis plants
and 3,000 seedlings. [@1] No one was arrested in either eradication exercise.
[@1]

Commissioner of Police Vernon Francois said, in a statement earlier that year,
that cannabis remains an illegal substance on Saint Lucia's law books. [@1]
Francois said the island would not be a safe haven for drug trafficking and that
police efforts would not ease. [@1] The Royal Saint Lucia Police Force said it
had carried out a number of operations against cannabis cultivation. [@1]
