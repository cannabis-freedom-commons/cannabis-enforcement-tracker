Nigeria's National Drug Law Enforcement Agency seized 34,030 kg of cannabis, locally called Indian hemp, from a warehouse in Akure on Thursday 12 March 2015, acting on a tip-off about suspicious activity at the Shagari Housing Estate in the state capital. [@1,2] The agency called it the largest single haul since its Ondo State Command was established and put the street value at 364 million naira. [@1,2]

Ondo NDLEA commander Ibrahim Abdul described the seizure as massive. [@1] Officers began evacuating the drugs at 4 a.m. with twelve vehicles, and the operation lasted more than twelve hours; Abdul said three sensitive plantation-destruction operations were running at the same time. [@1,2] Two suspects, Collins Nmor, 35, and Godday Chibuzor, 27, were arrested and were assisting investigators. [@1,2]
