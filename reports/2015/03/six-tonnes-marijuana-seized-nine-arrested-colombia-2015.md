Colombian police seized six tonnes of marijuana in an operation against an
organisation that moved the drug towards Central America and Venezuela. [@1]
Officers arrested nine members of the organisation in different cities across
Colombia. [@1] The same operation seized 392 kilograms of cocaine, which is not
counted in this report's cannabis figures. [@1]

According to a police statement, the organisation mainly operated in the
departments of Cesar and Bolívar, in northern Colombia. [@1] The police said the
organisation had links to drug-trafficking networks in Panama and the
Netherlands. [@1] The arrests followed joint investigations with the Attorney
General's Office, known in Colombia as the Fiscalía. [@1] The organisation's
leader, named by police as Hernán José Roa Ibarra, alias “Hernán”, was detained
in Barranquilla, on Colombia's Caribbean coast. [@1]

Members of the network used high-end cars to move the drugs in small quantities
to evade police checks. [@1] Listín Diario, a Dominican newspaper, carried the
same EFE agency account of the seizure and the nine arrests the following day.
[@2]
