St Elizabeth police seized ganja in an early-morning operation in Coker
district, St Elizabeth, when officers from the Siloah police searched an
abandoned premises. [@1] Officers found seven knitted bags holding ganja that
weighed about 110 pounds (50 kilograms). [@1] The Star gave the same weight as
approximately 110 pounds, or 50 kilograms. [@2] No one was arrested in
connection with the find. [@1]

Police put the street value of the ganja at J$500,000. [@1] The Star reported
that investigations were continuing. [@2]
