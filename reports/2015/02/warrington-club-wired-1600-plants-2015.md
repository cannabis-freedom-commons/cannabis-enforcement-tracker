Cheshire Police raided the disused Club Wired nightclub in Warrington at 8am on Friday 13 February 2015 and found a field of drug plants growing inside. [@1,2] Officers recovered more than 1,600 cannabis plants in varying stages of growth. [@1,2]

## Value and arrests

Inspector Richie Watts called it an extremely large cannabis factory, estimating the street value of the plants at about £1.5 million with a potential annual income of more than £6 million. [@1,2] He said the drugs could have been destined for the streets of Warrington or further afield. [@1,2] Two men aged 48 and 34 and two youths were arrested and taken into custody. [@1,2] Watts said information from the public is vital in the fight against drug-related crime and urged residents to report suspicious activity to Cheshire Police on 101. [@1]
