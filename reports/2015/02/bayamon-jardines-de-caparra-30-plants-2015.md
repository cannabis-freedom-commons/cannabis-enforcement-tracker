Police agents from the Bayamón Drugs Division searched residence AC 43 in the
Jardines de Caparra housing development in Bayamón under a warrant. [@1]
Officers seized about 30 marijuana plants and $1,072 in cash at the house. [@1]

The search took place at about 5:00 p.m. the previous afternoon, and José Melvin
Hernández Sánchez, 38, and Damaris Sánchez Colón, 37, were arrested. [@1] Judge
Rafael Villafañe had issued the search warrant on 8 October. [@1]

Prosecutor Gustavo Vélez handled the case, and a magistrate set bail of $30,000
for Hernández Sánchez, who was taken to the Bayamón regional jail. [@1] No
criminal charges were filed against Sánchez Colón. [@1]
