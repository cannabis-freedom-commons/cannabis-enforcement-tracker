On the afternoon of Sunday 28 June 2015, Spanish Customs Surveillance and Civil Guard patrol vessels intercepted the 85-metre merchant vessel Just Reema about 45 miles south of Málaga. [@1,2] The vessel flew the flag of the Democratic Republic of the Congo, was carrying about 1,500 tonnes of salt, and was sailing toward the eastern Mediterranean when a French alert via Europol prompted the search under Operation Urca. [@1,2]

## Hidden compartment

Officers towed the ship to the port of Málaga under judicial authorisation and, after unloading most of the salt, found a double bottom in the bow inside the hold hiding more than 500 bales of hashish resin, estimated at about 15,700 kg. [@1,2] All nine crew members were arrested, six Syrian nationals and three Indian nationals, and the vessel, detainees and drugs were brought before the duty investigating court in Málaga. [@1,2] Europol estimated the haul's value at about 24 million euros on the Spanish market. [@2]
