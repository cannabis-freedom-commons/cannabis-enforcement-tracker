Gunmen in Lazarat, the southern Albanian village known as a major illegal marijuana producer, shot dead RENEA special-forces officer Ibrahim Basha, 31, and wounded two colleagues on the morning of 24 June 2015. [@1,2] Basha, an Albanian armed-forces veteran who had served with the NATO mission in Afghanistan, was shot in the head and died on the way to the regional hospital. [@1]

## Checkpoint ambush

RENEA officers backing an operation against overnight shooting at police stopped a car carrying weapons at a village checkpoint around 9 a.m., seizing two rifles and an automatic rifle and arresting the driver. [@1,2] Gunmen in a nearby house then opened fire on the officers. [@1,2] The two wounded officers were flown by helicopter to Tirana for treatment. [@1]

## Siege and arrests

About 400 officers besieged the 5,000-resident village, controlling everyone entering or leaving, and raided homes to detain 14 suspected gang members. [@2] Police published a list of 21 wanted persons, and days later reported eight more arrests including brothers Arban and Arbion Aliko. [@2]
