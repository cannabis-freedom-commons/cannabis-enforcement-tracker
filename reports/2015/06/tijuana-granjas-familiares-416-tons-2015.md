On 10 June 2015 Mexican soldiers and Baja California state police stopped a
Honda CR-V in Tijuana after an anonymous tip and found 54 kg of marijuana
inside. [@1,2,3] The couple in the car, Francisco Pineda Villanueva and Blanca
Estela Robles García, said they worked as guards at a warehouse holding a much
larger stash. [@2,3]

Two days later, with a search warrant granted, troops in more than 40 vehicles
surrounded a warehouse on Laderas street in the Granjas Familiares area and
found 5,271 brown-taped packages in an underground room holding 41.6 metric
tons of marijuana. [@1,2,3] Second Military Zone commander Gabriel García
Rincón called it Baja California's biggest seizure since the record 134-ton
haul of 2010, and said the packages carried more than 15 different logos,
including the Road Runner cartoon. [@2,3]

The couple, a married pair living at the warehouse with their three children,
were placed in federal custody and the children handed to the DIF child-welfare
agency. [@1,2] The operation was run jointly with the State Preventive Police
after several weeks of intelligence work. [@1,2]
