The investigation began on Friday 5 June 2015 and the seizure of the plants concluded on Monday 8 June. [@1,2] Authorities confirmed the seizure of more than 109,000 marijuana plants from a grow operation busted that weekend in Lipscomb County. [@1,2]

No arrests had been made, and a joint local, state and federal investigation was under way. [@1,2]

The bust might be the largest of its kind in state history. [@1,2] The previous largest was 100,197 plants seized across 29 fields in Polk County the year before, a haul estimated at 175 million dollars that a deer hunter had discovered on a farm with irrigation systems and underground power generators. [@1,2]
