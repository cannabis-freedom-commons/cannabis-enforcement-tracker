Queensland Police raided a house on Littler Street in Sunnybank on the morning of
14 October 2015 after a tip from the public and found a large hydroponic cannabis
crop inside. [@1] About 450 plants in various stages of maturity were seized along
with a large amount of growing equipment, worth around $2 million by police estimate.
[@1,2]

The house had been extensively modified to hold the set-up, and power to the house
had been diverted around the meters; officers had to render it safe before they could
dismantle the crop. [@1] Acting Detective Superintendent Geoff Sheldon credited
information from the community for taking what he called a sophisticated and
well-hidden set-up off the street. [@1]

Two men were taken into custody at the scene and later charged with drug offences; a
legal account of the case gives their ages as 32 and 22 and describes both as
Vietnamese men. [@1,2] Their matters were proceeding through the Richlands and
Brisbane Magistrates courts. [@2]
