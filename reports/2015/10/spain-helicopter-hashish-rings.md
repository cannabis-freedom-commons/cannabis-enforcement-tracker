Spanish police broke up two gangs that flew hashish from Morocco into Spain by
helicopter on low night flights, arresting fifteen people. [@1] Police detained
seven French nationals, six Spaniards and two Moroccans, and seized 810 kg of
hashish and three helicopters worth €2.5 million. [@1] The gangs flew the drug
to remote farms in Málaga province on the Costa del Sol and moved it overland
to other European countries. [@1]

The smugglers used a veteran flight instructor skilled in mountain rescue and
night flying, who recruited and trained their pilots; he was caught delivering
600 kg by helicopter while training another pilot. [@1] The fifteen suspects
were charged with drug trafficking, belonging to an organised crime group and
illegal possession of arms. [@1] Police said the inquiry began in May 2014,
when a hashish-laden helicopter hit power cables at night in southern Spain,
killing its pilot. [@1]
