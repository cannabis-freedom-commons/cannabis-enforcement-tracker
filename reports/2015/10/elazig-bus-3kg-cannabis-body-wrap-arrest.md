Provincial gendarmerie teams carried out an anti-drug check at the Gülüşkür
Bridge area on the Elazığ-Bingöl highway. [@1] Officers stopped and searched a
minibus travelling from Bingöl toward Elazığ. [@1]

During the search they seized 3 kilograms of cannabis wrapped around the body of
passenger O.S. [@1] A second account says officers grew suspicious of O.S.'s
behaviour and, on a body search, found 3 kilograms of powdered cannabis wrapped
on him. [@3]

The Haber7 account, credited to the Anadolu Agency, says the same 3 kilograms
were taken off O.S. on the minibus and that a court in Elazığ remanded him on
the trafficking charge. [@2] O.S. was detained and, after questioning by the
gendarmerie, was remanded in custody by a court on a charge of drug trafficking.
[@1]
