French customs officers seized more than seven tonnes of cannabis from three
vans parked in an upmarket area of Paris on the night of 17 to 18 October
2015. [@1, 2] The 7.1 tonnes, found in big bags in the boots of the vans in the
city's 16th district, were valued at at least 15 million euros, in the biggest
cannabis haul Paris had seen. [@2] President François Hollande visited the
customs intelligence unit's headquarters to congratulate the officers, calling
the seizure one of the biggest ever made in the capital. [@1, 2]

The discovery followed a weeks-long investigation by the National Directorate of
Customs Intelligence and Investigations (DNRED). [@2] The vans were hired
vehicles with French number plates, parked on an arterial boulevard for more
than a week, and a fourth empty van stood nearby. [@2] A local resident raised
the alarm after noticing the vans sitting there. [@2] An initial police
statement said the cannabis had come from Morocco and probably transited
through Spain. [@1, 2]
