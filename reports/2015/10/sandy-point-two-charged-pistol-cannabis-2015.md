On the morning of 21 October 2015, police conducted an operation in Sandy Point
and arrested two people. [@1] Police identified the two as Davereen Brownbill
and Chavez Nital. [@1] The search of the residence they occupied was carried out
by the Special Services Unit with support from Division B officers executing a
search warrant. [@1] Both men were charged with possession of an illegal
firearm, possession of ammunition, possession of cannabis and possession of
cannabis with intent to supply. [@1]

Officers found one loaded 9mm semi-automatic pistol and three rounds of
ammunition in the house. [@2] They also found a quantity of dried marijuana in
the house. [@1] Police said the pistol was the 16th illegal firearm removed from
the Federation's streets that year, compared with 12 in 2014. [@1]

Division B commander Superintendent Cromwell Henry said the operation was part
of ongoing targeted intelligence-led operations aimed at finding and seizing
illegal firearms and drugs. [@1] Assistant Commissioner Ian Queeley said
removing illegal firearms from communities was critical under the force's
Six-Point Plan for reducing crime. [@1]
