Saudi authorities beheaded Saudi citizen Jari al-Dossari for smuggling hashish on Thursday 7 June 2007. [@1,2] He was arrested while receiving a large quantity of hashish and was executed in Al-Sulayyil in Riyadh Province. [@1]

Under Saudi Arabia's strict interpretation of Islamic law, people convicted of murder, rape, armed robbery and drug trafficking can be executed. [@1] The executions listing records the 7 June beheading of al-Dossari in Saudi Arabia for a drugs offence. [@2]
