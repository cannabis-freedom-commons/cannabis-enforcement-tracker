Hong Kong's Narcotics Bureau smashed a drug syndicate shipping cannabis into the city to meet local market demand, arresting a 36-year-old man and seizing 195 kilograms of cannabis in an operation on 1 June 2007. [@1,2] Following an intensive investigation, officers apprehended the man at an address in Tai Po and later seized 195 slabs of vacuum-packed cannabis herbs in a stone hut, each slab weighing one kilogram. [@1]

The seizure could fetch some HKD10.1 million on the retail market. [@1] The man was charged with two counts of trafficking in dangerous drugs. [@1]

The bureau published a photograph of its officers briefing the news media on the case. [@1]
