Tangier customs seized over 340 kg of cannabis resin, worth some US$4.1
million, on Wednesday 27 June 2007. [@1] The drug was concealed in a car
registered in Italy and was destined for the Italian market. [@1] The driver,
a Moroccan expatriate in Italy, was arrested for investigation. [@1]
