Immigration and Customs Enforcement agents had been watching a heavily wooded
five-acre lot in the 12000 block of Martindale Street in southeast Houston, which
officials described as an unloading and distribution point for marijuana shipments from
the southern border. [@1] On Saturday 3 March 2007 officers stopped four vehicles
leaving the property; one evaded them, the occupants of a black pickup fled, and
agents found the two school buses hidden in open-air sheds on the lot. [@1,2]

## Arrests and haul

Gerardo Valdez, 22, of Houston, and Juan Jose Villarreal, 35, of Donna, were arrested
and charged with possession with intent to distribute nearly 19,000 pounds of
marijuana, valued by authorities at nearly $13 million. [@1] Valdez was driving a
rented van holding 1,500 pounds and a pickup linked to Villarreal held 2,500 pounds;
another 1,200 pounds were found in the black pickup abandoned during the chase. [@1]
The NDIC later summarized the haul as over 9,000 kilograms taken from two school
buses and two rental vans. [@2] ICE, Customs and Border Protection, the Houston and
Pasadena police and the Harris County Sheriff's Department took part. [@1]
