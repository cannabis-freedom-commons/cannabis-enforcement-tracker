Police conducted a drug eradication exercise at Lancaster Village backlands on the
Corentyne on Thursday 1 March 2007, destroying a field of about half an acre holding
roughly 4,000 marijuana plants. [@1] Dried cannabis and seeds found at the camp were
also burnt, bringing the haul to 221 kg. [@1] Two bicycles and a spray can were lodged
in police custody, but no one was arrested. [@1]
