Bireuen police chief S. Rubianto led 150 officers in Operation ANTIK 2007 on Tuesday 27 March 2007,
finding about 20 hectares of cannabis in the Pinto Rimba hills of Desa Jaba, Peudada regency. [@1]
Personnel left Bireuen at about 08:00 and reached the fields on foot after 13:00. [@1]

## On-site destruction

Rubianto said the cannabis was split across five locations in the same forest area, with some plots
30 centimetres high and others about one metre tall. [@1] Officers destroyed about 8,000 plants on
site and took 2,000 stems to police headquarters as evidence. [@1] They also demolished two shacks
believed to belong to growers, including a high lookout structure, and seized urea fertiliser,
hoes, irrigation hose and a machete. [@1]

## Contact with growers

Police said they saw three people run into the forest and chose not to chase them. [@1] A farmer
watering vegetables nearby was questioned but not detained because he was outside the cannabis
plots. [@1]
