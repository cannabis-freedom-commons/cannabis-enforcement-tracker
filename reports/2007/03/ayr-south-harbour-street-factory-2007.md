Strathclyde Police raided a disused furniture warehouse and adjoining premises on South Harbour
Street in Ayr on Thursday 29 March 2007, seizing between 3,000 and 4,000 high-grade cannabis
plants. [@1,2] The plants, in various stages of growth across nine rooms over three floors, had a
street value of up to £1 million, and police called it the largest cannabis cultivation factory ever
found in Scotland. [@1,2]

A 16-year-old boy from south-east Asia was arrested at the warehouse and charged with producing
cannabis, supplying it and possessing it with intent to supply; he appeared at Ayr Sheriff Court and
was remanded in custody. [@1,2] A patrol officer had noticed a pungent smell coming from the
building, and inside officers found a sophisticated hydroponic set-up worth about £50,000 that had
bypassed the mains electricity supply. [@1] Police said an organised crime group ran the factory and
that two families living above it had not known what was there. [@1]

The raid was part of Operation League, a Strathclyde Police drive against home-grown cannabis that
had already seized about 8,000 plants across Ayrshire, Lanarkshire and Glasgow. [@1,2]
