In April 2007 the Moroccan navy seized three tonnes of Europe-bound hashish
off the Mediterranean port of Nador. [@1, 2] Maghreb Arabe Presse reported the
interception north of Nador in a late-June dispatch on northern customs
successes, calling it the most important of the smuggling attempts foiled that
year. [@1]
