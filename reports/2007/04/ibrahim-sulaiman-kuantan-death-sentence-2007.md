The High Court in Kuantan sentenced trader Ibrahim Sulaiman, 46, of Kelantan to death
after finding him guilty of trafficking 61.159 kg of cannabis. [@1,2] He had committed
the offence in front of the Petronas service station in Balok Makmor on 1 December
2002, more than four years before the sentence. [@1,2]

Judge Datuk Heliliah Mohd Yusoff passed the sentence on 30 April 2007, the day before
it was reported. [@1,2] Ibrahim showed no reaction, but members of his family including
his parents broke down. [@1,2] He was represented by counsel Bob Arumugam while Deputy
Public Prosecutor Rosli Mohd Sham prosecuted. [@1,2]
