About 7 am on Monday, police found 1,045 pounds (about 474 kg) of compressed and loose parcels of ganja in an unoccupied dwelling on Summit Road in Morant Bay, St Thomas. [@1] Officers valued the find at about 142.12 million Jamaican dollars. [@1] The narcotics division described it alongside two other recoveries that weekend as seizures worth a combined 377 million dollars. [@1]
