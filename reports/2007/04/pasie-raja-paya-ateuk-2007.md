Police burned ready-to-harvest marijuana on 14 hectares at two locations in the
Paya Ateuk mountains of the Pasie Raja district on Wednesday 11 April 2007
during Operasi Antik. [@1,2] Antara's photographer documented officers feeding
the uprooted plants into smoking fires and carrying bundles past a field hut.
[@1,2] The owners of the fields were not captured. [@1,2]
