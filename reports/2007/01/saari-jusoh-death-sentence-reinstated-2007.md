Malaysia's Federal Court on 30 January 2007 unanimously reinstated the death sentence imposed on former construction worker Saari Jusoh for selling 3,686 grams of cannabis. [@2] Saari and Mohd Saufi Jusoh had been jointly charged with trafficking the drug at Jalan Tun Abdul Razak in Johor Bahru on 8 September 1991; the High Court sentenced Saari to death on 11 December 1995 and acquitted Saufi. [@1,2] In August 2006 the Court of Appeal quashed the conviction, ruling no sale had occurred because police sprang their trap before money changed hands, and ordered 20 years' jail and 12 strokes for possession instead. [@1]

Five Federal Court judges allowed the prosecution's appeal, holding that a sale is complete upon transfer of the drug even if the price has not been paid, and restored the High Court's death sentence. [@2]
