On 5 January 2007, Kenya’s High Court in Nairobi upheld Mohamed Ghani Taib’s concurrent 15-year sentences on two cannabis trafficking counts. [@1,3] It overturned co-defendant Idris Yusuf Bwana’s conviction on the Shanzu count and ordered his release unless he was lawfully held for another reason. [@1,3] Nine defendants had been charged together, and the other seven were acquitted at trial. [@1,3]

## Seizures in Nyali and Shanzu

On 26 January 2000, officers recovered sacks of hashish from a bedroom in a rented house on Links Road in Nyali after Taib approached police with information about suspected contraband from the port. [@1,3] The Nyali charge specified 4,715 kilograms, and government analysts identified cannabis resin. [@1,3] Taib said he had acted as a police informer. [@1,3]

On 15 March, police acting on another tip arrested Taib after he arrived at a rented house they were watching in Shanzu. [@1,3] Officers recovered 1,540 slabs of hashish from the house and photographed the seized material before taking it for analysis. [@1,3] The International Narcotics Control Board reported that the January and March Mombasa seizures together amounted to more than six tonnes. [@2]
