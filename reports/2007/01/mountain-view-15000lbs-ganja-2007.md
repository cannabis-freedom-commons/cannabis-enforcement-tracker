During January 2007 narcotics police seized more than 15,000 pounds (about 6.8 tonnes) of
compressed ganja found in three Toyota Hiace buses at a premises in the Goodwill Avenue area of
Kingston 6, in the Mountain View district. [@1] No one was arrested for the drug find. [@1]

The seizure was recounted the next month in an Observer report on a separate Clarendon raid,
which described the Mountain View find as the narcotics police's big recent haul. [@1]
