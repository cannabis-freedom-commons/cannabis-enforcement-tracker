Police seized more than 50,000 marijuana plants and 207 kilograms of bulk
marijuana buds during Project Sabot, a one-month crackdown in the National
Capital Region. [@1] Aerial patrols targeting outdoor grow operations located
51,031 plants between 22 August and 27 September 2007, according to an RCMP
news release issued on Friday 5 October. [@1]

The operation joined the Department of National Defence, the RCMP drug
section's A Division, the Ontario and Quebec provincial police forces and the
Des Collines municipal police. [@1] The release said the seizures were meant
to cut the risks of drug use and to deny marijuana profits that financed
criminal activity. [@1]
