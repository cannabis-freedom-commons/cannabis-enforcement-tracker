The Penang High Court sentenced former painter Chuah Kok Eng, 47, to death for
trafficking 271.9 grams of cannabis. [@1,2] He was convicted at the end of the
defence case over an offence committed at a house in Jalan Jelutong at
12.45am on 3 November 2001. [@1,2]

Justice Abdul Rahim Uda held the defence had failed to raise a reasonable
doubt, and the court rejected the evidence of Chuah's wife, Ong Lean Ean, over
material contradictions in her testimony. [@1,2] Chuah shook his head as the
interpreter explained the death sentence, then put his hand around his neck to
convey the hanging to his wife in the public gallery before being led away.
[@1,2]
