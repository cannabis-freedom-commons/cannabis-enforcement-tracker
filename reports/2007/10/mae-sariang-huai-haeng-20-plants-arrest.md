Mae Sariang police detained Chan Pha-thai Phongphaew, of house No. 16 in village
11, inside a hut in the cannabis-growing area. [@1] Police accused him of
producing a category-5 narcotic, cannabis, and of possessing firearms and
ammunition without permission. [@1] Chan admitted that the seized cannabis was
his and that he had planted it. [@1] He said the 12-gauge shotgun belonged to
his father-in-law, Pha Khaew Hae, and that he had borrowed it. [@1] Officers
took the suspect and the seized items to the Mae Sariang inquiry officer for
prosecution. [@1]

The team cut down and destroyed about 20 cannabis plants. [@1] A search of the
hut found one 12-gauge shotgun, serial D635191, seven 12-gauge rounds, one
homemade gun and one set of cap ammunition. [@1]

Mae Hong Son governor Thongchai Wongrianthong ordered Mae Sariang police to cut
down and destroy an illegally planted cannabis field in Mae Sariang district.
[@1] The governor spoke as director of the provincial anti-drug operations
centre. [@1] On 15 October, police entered the field at Ban Huai Haeng, village
11, Mae Khong subdistrict, where cannabis had been planted mixed in with a rice
field. [@1]
