Police searching a car at Sonauli on the Indo-Nepal border found secret compartments holding one
quintal and 94 kilograms of hashish, about two quintals in total. [@1] Siddharthnagar
Superintendent of Police R. P. Chaturvedi said it was one of the biggest seizures ever made and
put its international-market value at about $508,582. [@1]

Police said they had stepped up security along the 1,747 km porous border India shares with
Nepal, using surprise checks and checkpoints at known crossings and other transit points. [@1]
