Former Australian airline executive Barry Wilfred Hess, 50, was arrested in August 2007 after police discovered 14.4 grams of hashish and 2.7 grams of marijuana in his home in Kuta. [@1,2] He initially faced a maximum penalty of death by firing squad on a range of charges including drug trafficking. [@1]

The Denpasar District Court threw out the more serious charges and instead found him guilty of being a drug addict who had not reported himself to authorities to undergo rehabilitation. [@1,2] He served four months in Bali's Kerobokan Prison alongside members of the Bali Nine and convicted cannabis trafficker Schapelle Corby. [@1,2]

## Release into immigration detention

Hess was released on 19 December 2007 and taken into immigration custody, with a holding cell near Bali airport, to await a decision on his fate within days. [@1] His lawyer said Hess, a Bali resident of 12 years with a local furniture business and partner, was asking to stay in Indonesia rather than face the usual deportation and blacklisting. [@1,2]
