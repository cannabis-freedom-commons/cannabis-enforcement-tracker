Between 22 November and 3 December 2007 a 240-strong force led by the National Narcotics Board (BNN), with officers of the Aceh provincial and Bireuen district police and local residents, destroyed 103.1 hectares of cannabis fields across seven districts of Bireuen regency, Aceh. [@1]

About 15 tonnes of cannabis plants and seized evidence were destroyed in total, both in the fields and at the police station. [@1] On 4 December police officials and Bireuen regency leaders burned the seized marijuana in the yard of the district police headquarters. [@1]
