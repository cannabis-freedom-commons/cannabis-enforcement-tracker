Moroccan customs officers seized 35 tonnes of hashish at Tangier in hundreds of
operations during 2007, a record haul then valued at an estimated 140 million euros on
the European market. [@1] The northern port sits a short ferry ride from Europe, and
smugglers hid resin in ever more ingenious vehicle concealments to get past its
inspectors. [@1]

## People caught

Officers arrested 437 people over the year, about half of them foreigners, including 78
Spanish, 61 French and 22 Portuguese nationals. [@1] Abdelhalek Marzouki, the customs
director for northern Morocco, said smugglers study customs methods and constantly invent
new ones. [@1] Those caught face up to 10 years in prison. [@1]

## Record confirmed

Reuters confirmed the 35-tonne Tangier total for 2007, up 25 percent on 2006, as Morocco
remained the world's biggest exporter of processed cannabis. [@2]
