Attorney General Alberto Gonzales announced in San Diego on 28 February 2007 the
culmination of Operation Imperial Emperor, a 20-month DEA-led investigation into the
Mexico-based Victor Emilio Cazares-Gastellum trafficking and money-laundering organization. [@1] More
than 400 people were arrested nationwide, including more than 66 that morning in
California, Arizona and Illinois, and charges were unsealed against 139 defendants in
several federal districts. [@1,2]

## What was seized

Agents seized about $45.2 million in cash, 27,229 pounds (about 12.4 tonnes) of
marijuana, 9,512 pounds of cocaine, 705 pounds of methamphetamine, 227 pounds of
crystal meth ("ice") and 11 pounds of heroin, plus $6.1 million in property and assets,
roughly 100 weapons and 94 vehicles. [@1,2] The organization moved multi-ton cocaine
shipments and large amounts of marijuana and methamphetamine monthly from Mexico across
the Southwest border, breaking bulk in Los Angeles and San Diego for distribution
across the United States. [@1]
