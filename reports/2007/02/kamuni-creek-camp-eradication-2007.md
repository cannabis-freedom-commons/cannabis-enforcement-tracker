Police from the Narcotics Branch destroyed a marijuana camp at Kamuni Creek on the
Demerara River on Tuesday 27 February 2007, burning three fields of about two acres
holding 2,300 plants up to six feet tall. [@1] They also burnt 13 nurseries holding
about 14,500 seedlings that weighed 240 kg. [@1] No one was arrested. [@1]
