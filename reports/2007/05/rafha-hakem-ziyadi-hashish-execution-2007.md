Iraqi national Hakem Rajouj Mutair al-Ziyadi was beheaded by sword after being convicted of
smuggling hashish into Saudi Arabia. [@1] He was caught red-handed trying to smuggle an
undisclosed amount of hashish on a motorbike, the Interior Ministry said in a statement carried
by the state SPA news agency. [@1,2] The execution took place in the northern town of Rafha, on
the border with Iraq. [@1]

The beheading was announced on 14 May 2007 alongside other executions, including a Pakistani
national beheaded for heroin smuggling. [@1,2] At least 37 people were executed in the kingdom in
2006, after 83 in 2005 and 35 the year before, according to press tallies of official
statements. [@2] The latest execution brought the announced 2007 total to 73. [@2]
