Saudi national Jalal bin Ahmed al-Marhoun was executed in the northern city of Al-Jawf after being convicted of smuggling hashish into the kingdom, the Interior Ministry said in a statement.[@1,2] The beheading was carried out with a sword, as is customary in public executions in Saudi Arabia.[@1]

The execution was announced alongside the beheading of two Ethiopians in Jeddah for a fatal armed robbery, taking the year's reported execution total in the kingdom to 70.[@1] Saudi Arabia applies the death penalty for murder, drug trafficking, rape and armed robbery under its strict interpretation of Islamic law.[@1]
