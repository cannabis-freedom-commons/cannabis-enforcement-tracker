Stephen Rochester, 60, of Cliffsend Grove, Ramsgate, admitted cultivating cannabis and was jailed for four years and nine months at Canterbury Crown Court. [@1] He had grown more than 4,000 plants in a 20,000 square-foot warehouse on an industrial estate in Ramsgate, which police gave a street value of between 1.9 million and 2.5 million pounds. [@1]

The factory was raided on 8 May 2006. [@1] A second man, 40-year-old Dennis Murphy of Frazier Street in south London, also admitted cultivating cannabis and received a nine-month suspended sentence with 120 hours of community service. [@1]

Detective Chief Inspector Rob Vinson of Kent Police called the seizure the biggest cannabis factory find in the South East and possibly the country, saying its discovery had made a major dent in the drugs supply in Thanet and possibly the wider South East. [@1]
