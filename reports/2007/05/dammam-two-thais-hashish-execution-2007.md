Two Thai nationals convicted of drug trafficking were beheaded by sword in the eastern city of Dammam on Monday 7 May 2007. [@1,2] The Interior Ministry said the men had smuggled a "large quantity of hashish" into the kingdom hidden inside a sportswear shipment. [@1,2] The English service of the state SPA news agency named the men as Shalirm Yonha Koy and Smnoq Mintcho Keet, transliterations of their Thai names. [@2] Their convictions had been upheld by the cassation court and the supreme judicial council. [@2] The beheadings brought the number of executions announced in the kingdom that year to 55, a sharp rise after 37 in all of 2006 and 83 in 2005, according to press tallies of official statements. [@1,2]
