On Monday 13 August 2007 Yosemite park rangers located and seized 7,428 mature marijuana plants from three gardens in the park, all part of the same growing operation. [@1,2,3] The plants were valued at about $22 million. [@1,2]

Monday's multi-agency operation brought together several national parks, the Mariposa County Sheriff's Office, the Army National Guard and the California Department of Justice's Campaign Against Marijuana Planting (CAMP). [@1,2] The sites bore the marks of a Mexican drug trafficking enterprise, including a sophisticated watering system and the use of fertilizers, pesticides and herbicides, and the growing operation caused considerable damage to natural resources. [@1,2] Acting Superintendent Kevin Cann said the activity would not be tolerated in the park, and Chief Ranger Steve Shackelton said the park was keeping a "full court press" on the Mexican organizations growing in the Sierra Nevada. [@1]
