Gardai raided a rented house in Cloone village in the Carrick-on-Shannon area on Tuesday night and found a large quantity of cannabis plants growing upstairs.[@1] The house had specialised plumbing and irrigation equipment installed, along with transformers used to boost the electricity supply, in what officers described as a professional operation.[@1]

More than 100 plants were found in what gardaí called a very professional-type operation capable of turning out a fresh crop every ten weeks.[@2] A separate section of the house had been converted to dry the cannabis leaves.[@2] A man in his twenties was arrested, and a file on the case was prepared for the Director of Public Prosecutions.[@1,2]
