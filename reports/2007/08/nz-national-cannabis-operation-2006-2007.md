New Zealand police arrested more than 700 people involved in commercial cannabis
cultivation and distribution during the national cannabis crime operation of the
2006-2007 growing season. [@1] Officers destroyed about 100,000 plants, seized
more than 120 firearms and recovered over $170,000 in stolen property. [@1,2]

The season also yielded more than 170 kg of dried cannabis, ten methamphetamine
laboratories and twelve proceeds-of-crime actions. [@1] Detective Senior Sergeant
Scott McGill said plant numbers were down on the previous season's 118,457, but
arrests were up by 200, dried cannabis seizures up by about 100 kg, firearms up
from 54 to 122, and the laboratory count up by one. [@1]

## A gateway operation

McGill described the cannabis operation as a "whole of crime" gateway into other
organised criminal activity, citing the firearms, stolen property, methamphetamine
laboratories and proceeds actions found alongside cannabis inquiries. [@1]
He said members of the public made a major contribution by providing information
that led to plots being located and offenders arrested. [@1]
