In August 2007, a grower was caught while waiting in the ferry lineup at
Horseshoe Bay in West Vancouver. [@1] Police had been called to the lineup after
reports of an inebriated driver in a RAV4. [@1] When officers reached the
vehicle, they found the driver with open liquor visible inside. [@1] The
officers then noticed the odour of marijuana coming from the vehicle. [@1] A
search of the vehicle uncovered 140 plants inside. [@1]

The SUV had been retrofitted as a mobile grow operation, with a transformer
boosting the electrical input, fertilizer, white sheeting and other equipment.
[@1] Investigators believed the plants had already been growing inside the SUV
for several weeks when they were found. [@1] The setup reflected a period when
illegally grown British Columbia cannabis was lucrative but still illegal to
cultivate. [@1]
