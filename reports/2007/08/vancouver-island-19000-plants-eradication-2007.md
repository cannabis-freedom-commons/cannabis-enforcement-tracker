Police destroyed 19,000 mature cannabis plants at about 350 sites across
Vancouver Island in a nine-day air-and-land operation that ended on Wednesday
29 August 2007. [@1] RCMP Corporal Greg Cox said most of the plants were found
in the northern part of the island, with concentrations north of Campbell River
and around Sayward; some sites held a few hundred plants and others a few
thousand. [@1] Officers from the RCMP and the Victoria and Saanich municipal
police flew with Canadian military Sea King helicopters to reach remote plots,
pulling in an average of 2,000 plants a day. [@1,2]

The haul beat the previous year's record of 16,500 plants. [@2] Cox said
police believed the crop was destined for island streets and possibly beyond,
with profits flowing to organised crime groups. [@2]

One arrest incident was reported near Campbell River, where two people were
found tending a crop and charges were pending. [@1] A second account describes
the same incident as two arrests. [@3]
