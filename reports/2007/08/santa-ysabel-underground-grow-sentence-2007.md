Damien Anthony Andrews, 34, of Encinitas was sentenced to five years in
federal prison on 3 August 2007 by US District Judge Dana Sabraw for the
clandestine marijuana operation beneath his log cabin in Santa Ysabel.
[@3] He had pleaded guilty in May to manufacturing marijuana and to money
laundering charges, after admitting in a plea deal that he conspired to
produce more than 100 marijuana plants. [@2,3]

Federal agents had arrested Andrews on 28 March 2007 and searched his
39-acre Santa Ysabel property, finding 454 marijuana plants in underground
rooms reached by an elevator concealed in a garage closet and a 4-foot-wide,
65-foot-long tunnel. [@2] The rooms, two 20-by-20-foot chambers with 8- and
12-foot ceilings, had irrigation, lighting, electrical and ventilation
systems with steel roof beams, while the cabin carried mounted infrared
cameras with night-vision capability. [@1,2] Andrews had bought the land
without permits for the cabin and garage, and agents said tips about the
operation had reached them as early as 2004. [@1,2]

Andrews agreed to forfeit the Santa Ysabel property, an Oceanside home and
his Encinitas-area residence to the government. [@2,3] A permanent legal
resident originally from Canada, he will be deported after serving his
sentence. [@3] Two alleged accomplices were also charged: Anthony Martin
Hillerby, 40, who tended 233 plants at an Encinitas house as Andrews'
sub-tenant and had pleaded guilty, and Sean Daniel Smith, 35, charged with
conspiracy. [@1,2,3]
