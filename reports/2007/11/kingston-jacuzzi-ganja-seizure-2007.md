Police carrying out a routine narcotics check at the Port of Kingston on Tuesday 20 November 2007 opened five luxury Jacuzzis and found 119 packets of compressed ganja weighing more than 400 lb (about 181 kg) attached to their bases. [@1] The five tubs were destined for St Lucia, and the find was the narcotics police's third seizure of ganja hidden in Jacuzzis in as many months. [@1]

In August, officers in Kingston had found 43.7 lb of ganja fixed to two Jacuzzis headed for Trinidad and Tobago, and in October Montego Bay police seized three Jacuzzis holding 196 lb at the Sangster International Airport in a shipment bound for Antigua. [@1] The head of the Narcotics Division, Senior Superintendent Carlton Wilson, called the Jacuzzi method a new trend, with traffickers seeking new ways to move drugs out ahead of Christmas. [@1]

Wilson said marijuana fetched US$4,000 a kilogram in the Eastern Caribbean; the Gleaner calculated the latest haul, for which the police gave no value, at more than J$51 million. [@1] No one had been arrested, though Wilson said investigations were well advanced and arrests were expected. [@1]
