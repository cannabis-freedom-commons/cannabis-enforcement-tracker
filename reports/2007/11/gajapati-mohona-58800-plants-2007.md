Acting on district collector D. V. Swamy's order, excise staff with police support destroyed cannabis plantations across the Mohona block of Gajapati district. [@1,2] The teams destroyed 58,800 plants of various sizes spread over 40 acres, which excise sources put at about Rs 1.5 crore in the market. [@1,2]

About 30 excise staff with two platoons of armed police raided the villages of Murdamulli, Panaguda and Juba from early morning, taking more than eight labourers to cut mostly full-grown plants. [@1,2] Not a single man was present in the villages when the squads arrived after travelling more than 15 km through deep forest and mountains. [@1,2] Excise superintendent Lokanath Mandia led the squads with excise inspector Damburu Dhara Gantayet, Mohona officer-in-charge Ram Chandra Thamba and Chandragiri outpost chief Abhimanyu Parida. [@1,2]

Officials said it was their first such raid that year despite many intelligence reports of hemp farming in Gajapati, and called the haul only the tip of the iceberg, estimating far larger cultivation elsewhere in the tribal district. [@1,2]
