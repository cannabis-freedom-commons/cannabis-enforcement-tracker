Three men who ran a Ticino hemp business were convicted of aggravated narcotics trafficking as a profession for selling, growing, transporting and holding at least 550 kilograms of dried hemp flowers between July 2002 and 5 June 2003. [@1] Prosecutors put the turnover at about 1.35 million francs and the profit at over 360,000 francs, including four documented sales totalling about 308 kilograms in early 2003. [@1] All three had spent time in pre-trial detention in June 2003. [@1]

## Suspended terms

On 8 November 2007 the Lugano criminal court sentenced them to 20, 18 and 15 months in prison respectively, minus time served, and suspended all three terms with a two-year probation period. [@1] It acquitted two of them on one minor count and ordered all seized property confiscated. [@1] The case grew out of Operation Indoor, the 2003 Ticino crackdown in which police seized about 198,000 plants and jailed about 130 people. [@2]
