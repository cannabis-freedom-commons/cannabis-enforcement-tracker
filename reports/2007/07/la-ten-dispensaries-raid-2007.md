Drug Enforcement Administration agents raided 10 medical marijuana dispensaries across
Los Angeles on Wednesday 25 July 2007, only minutes after the City Council placed a
moratorium on new dispensaries so rules could be drafted to regulate them. [@2] The
Associated Press reported that agents served search warrants at 10 locations across the
county the same day city leaders moved to restrain the crackdown on dispensaries
allowed under state law. [@3]

The largest raid hit the California Patients Group at 6208 Santa Monica Boulevard in
Hollywood, where an estimated 200 people gathered to protest while Los Angeles police
officers handled only perimeter control. [@1] The collective served more than a
thousand patients over the age of fifty and was a focal point of patient activism. [@5]
Staff and patients were reported held inside the dispensary during the raid, and agents
broke down the front door though it was unlocked. [@1]

## Council against the DEA

Councilman Dennis Zine, a former LAPD officer, called the timing deliberate
intimidation and demanded the DEA stop threatening landlords who rented to
dispensaries. [@1] Three weeks later the LAPD told the council it would keep assisting
federal raids, saying it did not want to damage its relationship with the DEA, while
the DEA spokeswoman said the agency would not reveal why it chose the ten facilities
and that the warrants remained under seal. [@4]
