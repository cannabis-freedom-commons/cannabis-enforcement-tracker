Orange County sheriff's deputies pulled over 24-year-old Al Gore III at about
2:15 a.m. on 4 July 2007 as he was driving a blue Toyota Prius at about 100 mph
on the San Diego Freeway. [@1] Deputies stated they smelled marijuana and
searched the vehicle. [@1] Officers found less than an ounce of marijuana in the
car alongside prescription medications including Xanax, Valium, Vicodin, and
Adderall. [@1] Authorities said Gore did not have a prescription for any of the
medications found in the vehicle. [@1]

Gore was arrested on suspicion of possessing marijuana and prescription drugs
after being stopped for speeding. [@1] He was booked into the men's central jail
in Santa Ana and released that afternoon after posting $20,000 bail. [@1] Gore,
the son of the former vice president, had previously been arrested for marijuana
possession in Maryland in December 2003 while attending university, later
completing substance abuse counseling under a diversion program. [@1]

On 30 July 2007, Gore pleaded guilty to two felony counts of drug possession,
two misdemeanor counts of prescription drug possession, and one misdemeanor
count of marijuana possession. [@2] He was also charged with a traffic
infraction for speeding. [@2] A judge allowed Gore to enter a drug diversion
program, ordering sentencing continued until February 2008 with the condition
that charges could be dismissed and his plea withdrawn if he completed the
program. [@2] Senior Deputy District Attorney Jaime Coulter stated that if Gore
complied with all conditions, sentencing would be continued for another year and
the charges could be dropped in 2009. [@2]
